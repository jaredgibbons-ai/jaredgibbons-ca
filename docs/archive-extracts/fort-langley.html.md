Fort Langley Houses and Townhomes for Sale

Fort Langley is a village on the Fraser River in the Township of Langley. Homes for sale range from older houses on large village lots to newer townhomes and rowhomes near the core, plus rural acreage outside the village. The village core is a heritage conservation area, so the rules for a property can matter as much as the house.

The Fort Langley sales table above uses MLS® sub-area F69 only. Detached homes that sold in the last 12 months had a median year built of 1979 and a median lot of 10,549 sq ft, which is why many houses here sit on bigger lots than in newer parts of Langley.

Parts of Fort Langley to know

Village core:
 Downtown became a heritage conservation area in 1997. Exterior changes generally need a Heritage Alteration Permit, and façade design guidelines apply to the downtown business core.

Riverfront and low areas:
 During the 2023 freshet the Township issued a flood advisory for unprotected floodplain areas including Fort Langley. Check the flood status of the exact lot.

Near the historic site:
 Fort Langley National Historic Site covers about 8.4 hectares. The act establishing the colony of British Columbia was proclaimed here on November 19, 1858.

Trails and parks:
 The Fort-to-Fort Trail links the village with Derby Reach Regional Park, and Brae Island Regional Park is across the Jacob Haldi Bridge.

School area:
 Fort Langley Elementary (K to 7) is on Bartlett Street. Langley Fine Arts School (K to 12) on Trattle Street is a district program, so ask SD35 about admission.

Parking and traffic. The village is busy with visitors.
