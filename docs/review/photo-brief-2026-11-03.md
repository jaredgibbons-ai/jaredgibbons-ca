# Photo brief: jaredgibbons.ca neighbourhood pages (2026-11-03)

**What:** 3 photos for each of 219 neighbourhoods (657 shots). Every shot is written out in `photo-shot-list-2026-11-03.csv` (area, shot, the filename to save it as). Start with the 20 busiest areas; the rest can follow.

**Use of the photos:** your own original photos replace stock images on the area pages. Real photos of the real street are a strong local signal for Google and for buyers.

## Rules for every photo
1. **Original and yours.** Taken by you or someone you hire; no stock, no AI images, no screenshots of maps or Street View.
2. **Landscape (3:2 or 16:9).** Long edge 2,400 px or more. JPEG or HEIC is fine; send the original, I'll compress.
3. **Daylight, level horizon, no heavy filters.** Soft morning or golden-hour light is best. Avoid noon glare and rainy days unless the shot calls for it.
4. **No people's faces, no licence plates, no house numbers on private homes.** Streetscapes, parks, schools and landmarks are fine. If a private home is the subject, don't send it without the owner's OK.
5. **Public places only.** Shoot from a public road, trail or park. No trespassing, no private land.
6. **Drone shots:** follow Transport Canada rules (line of sight, under 122 m / 400 ft, no flights over crowds, near airports or inside restricted zones). If you can't fly there, take the ground version.
7. **One shot, one file.** Don't stitch or collage.

## How to name and send
- Save each file as the filename in the CSV, for example `bradner_01.jpg`.
- One folder per area, or one zip per 10 areas.
- Add a one-line note per file if the real spot differs from the brief (for example: "shot from the north side instead").
- Mark `done` in the CSV, or just tell me which areas are finished.

## What I do with them
Compress to web sizes, add descriptive alt text, set the page's main image, and add them below the "streets and boundaries" section of each page. Nothing is published until you OK it.
