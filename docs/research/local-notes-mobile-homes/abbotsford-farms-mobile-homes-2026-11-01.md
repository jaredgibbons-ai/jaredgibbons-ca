### Manufactured homes on farm ground — the only route this file documents (full ALR block)

- A manufactured home on ALR farm ground is not a park purchase and not a pad tenancy. It is an ALR residence question: on ALR land one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- An additional manufactured-home residence on farm ground is dealt with only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 additional manufactured-home residence route and, where that route does not fit, a Non-Adhering Residential Use application to the ALC. A general farm-worker entitlement to manufactured housing is UNCONFIRMED — no source opened in this slug states one (and this slug's research expressly leaves farm-help dwelling rules UNVERIFIED, no City zoning-bylaw text having been opened), and none is claimed here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (research file §4.)
- **No stratification of ALR residences:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"), with the Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) position as carried program-wide (ALCA s.21(1)). A farm parcel cannot be turned into a bare-land strata manufactured-home community by assumption, and the caps above are stated as the ALC sources state them. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The stock this topic is about, in process terms only: a farm sale can combine ALR land, barns and other farm buildings, a principal residence (ALC frame: ≤500 m²) and possibly one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), plus — separately, and only if the contract states it — production quota, whose position is UNVERIFIED in this file. Farm classification is property-by-property with BC Assessment; no source classifies Abbotsford farms as a group. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (cross-referenced parks only; it does not govern a farm residence).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; no fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. How PTT apportions between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB figure at that point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. **Sibling check for this slug, stated explicitly: NO sibling of THIS slug carries $78,000 at a $3.5M input as its own figure** — its lots and acreages siblings state $93,000 at $3.5M and refer to the $78,000 error as living in townhomes siblings elsewhere in the program (the four carriers named program-wide are the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m, south-surrey-lots-land, southsurrey-foreclosures-court-ordered-sales and richmond-homes-for-sale townhomes siblings — none is a sibling of this slug). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a manufactured-home park on Abbotsford farm ground?** No — none is verified in this slug's research or siblings. This is a farm topic file with no roster; Abbotsford's verified parks (Paradise, Georgian, Maplewood) sit on other grounds and are documented in their home files. (§2.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a farm I buy?** Only through the ALR residence frame: one principal residence ≤500 m², a suite only inside it, and at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — an additional manufactured-home residence only via ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application. A general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify farm residences into a manufactured-home community?** No, not by assumption: depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission (ALC Bulletin 09, s.6; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: If I bought in a park instead, would I own land?** No. **On a rented pad you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad — that is not land ownership.** The pad tenancy is assigned on sale under MHPTA s.28 / Form 10, with the 10-day consent rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in a park?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only; no pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is quota included when I buy an Abbotsford farm, and what is it worth?** UNVERIFIED as a general rule in this file — no board source was opened in this slug's pass, and any quota price is UNVERIFIED everywhere. Read the specific contract and verify the transfer position with the applicable board before subjects are removed. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I keep farm classification across a purchase?** File the General Application for Farm Classification with BC Assessment by October 31 after purchase and keep meeting the income threshold for the operation's size ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% / $10,000 by tier). https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How is a manufactured home sale registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; the CSA label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if a park I live in is converted?** All permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with early move-out on at least 10 days' notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which schools serve a farm address?** The Ministry-listed schools in §1 are names and addresses only; catchments are address-based in SD34 and UNVERIFIED for any specific farm parcel in this slug — run the exact address in the SD34 School Locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
