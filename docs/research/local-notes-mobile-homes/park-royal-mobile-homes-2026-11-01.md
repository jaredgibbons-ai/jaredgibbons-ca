## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: **District of West Vancouver**. The Park Royal sub-area takes in the residential streets around the Park Royal shopping centre at Marine Drive and Taylor Way — West Vancouver's eastern gateway, at the foot of the Lions Gate Bridge and the mouth of the Capilano River. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/park-royal.md)
- MLS sub-area name as the board (Greater Vancouver REALTORS®) uses it: **Park Royal**. Nearby areas: Ambleside (east), Dundarave (west along Marine Drive), and — across the Capilano River in the District of North Vancouver — Norgate, Pemberton Heights and Lower Capilano. (Research file §1.)
- **The land-tenure split that defines this sub-area:** the shopping centre straddles the municipal boundary — part of it sits on **Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land**, held under the Park Royal Lease (an indenture of lease dated April 1, 1974), on which the Squamish Nation exercises land-use and zoning control. Under the arrangements documented in the Nation's zoning by-law amendments for the Park Royal leasehold lands, the Nation controls zoning on Capilano IR No. 5, while West Vancouver provides building design review and inspection services on the leasehold lands so construction meets municipal standards. The exact set of residential parcels inside the sub-area that sit on Capilano IR No. 5 versus municipal land is UNVERIFIED — title-by-title confirmation required. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- The centre: Park Royal opened in 1950 and is presented by the centre as Canada's first covered shopping mall; it operates as North and South malls divided by Marine Drive, and the District's OCP classifies it as West Vancouver's regional shopping centre. http://parkroyal.ca/
- Transit: Park Royal Exchange is the western terminus of the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat from Park Royal through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station; the exchange is also the hub for West Vancouver's Blue Bus routes, including downtown express service via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. (It does NOT govern a reserve-land leasehold interest of the Park Royal Lease kind — that tenure follows the lease and the Nation's land regime, per §2.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director," never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's own research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's settled files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. (Municipal-side Park Royal homes pay District of West Vancouver taxes — the 2026 payment was due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; homes on reserve land are under a different tax/tenure regime — tenure must be confirmed per title.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure — and, on this ground, for a reserve-land leasehold structure as well. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. At West Vancouver values the Province's additional school tax is also material: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Strata-form communities — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified at Park Royal).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / plan frame — District of West Vancouver.** Zoning Bylaw 4662 (2010); OCP Bylaw 4985 (2018), which classifies Park Royal as the District's regional shopping centre and names the shopping centre lands a Form & Character Development Permit area, with foreshore and environmental DP rules along the waterfront and watercourses. The area's zoning is in active transition: on July 21, 2025 the Province issued ministerial directives requiring West Vancouver to amend its OCP to increase density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area (with defined minimum densities) by December 31, 2025; the District missed that deadline, a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 implemented the neighbouring Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, and the Park Royal–Taylor Way density directive remains outstanding with quarterly provincial progress reports required through 2026 and 2027 — verify the current designation for any specific property. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: **District of West Vancouver servicing is UNCONFIRMED** in the verified ledger — no servicing bylaw number is asserted here. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377 and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the settled general (CRA GI-003) level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park at Park Royal?** None verified. The research file and every sibling name no park on this ground; the sub-area's real tenure story is the municipal-freehold / reserve-leasehold split (§2), and a park buyer widens the search outside West Vancouver. (Research file §5; siblings in the FILE STATUS block.)
- **Q: Is Park Royal in West Vancouver or on First Nations land?** Both, depending on the parcel. The shopping centre and surrounding area sit at West Vancouver's eastern edge; part of the centre stands on Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5, Squamish Nation land held under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, where the Nation exercises zoning control. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: Are homes near Park Royal freehold?** Homes on the municipal side are freehold; homes on reserve land are leasehold. The two can be streets apart, so tenure has to be confirmed on the individual title — never assumed from the sub-area name. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: Is a reserve leasehold the same as renting a pad in a manufactured home park?** No. A pad tenancy under the MHPTA is an assigned residential tenancy for a home the buyer owns as a chattel; a Park Royal Lease interest is a leasehold interest under a 1974 head lease on Squamish Nation land, governed by the lease and the Nation's land regime. The documents, the lender treatment and the resale mechanics are different — read the head lease before pricing. (§2 above.)
- **Q: If I buy in a pad-rental park elsewhere, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What is changing around Park Royal?** Density. A July 2025 provincial directive required West Vancouver to amend its OCP to increase density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area by the end of 2025; that directive remains outstanding with quarterly progress reports required, on top of the centre's long-standing role as the district's regional shopping centre. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Q: Which schools serve the Park Royal area?** Irwin Park and Westcot elementary schools (K–7) are the nearest SD45 schools, and West Vancouver Secondary is the east-side secondary school; the Irwin Park vs Westcot catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED — confirm with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific. Confirm for the specific home and its pad tenure before removing subjects. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide.md)
