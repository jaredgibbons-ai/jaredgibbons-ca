## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase, and the line this topic turns on.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. Land on this page is owned land; a park pad is not — never price or describe one as the other. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park-home sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file** — this slug's research documents no pad figure, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice, rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for each year. City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (research file §4)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province's newly built home exemption point (manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land, not previously occupied) is the land-side point for this topic. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (land / own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors with inputs: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M fully residential → $93,000 including the further 2% tier (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000) — follow $93,000**; the $78,000-for-$3.5M figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling (a different slug), is NOT met in this slug's own siblings, and is not followed anywhere. **Shorthand check for this slug's own siblings: the "Over $3M — 5%" compression wording IS met in this slug's townhomes sibling, where it is itself flagged as a compression of the 3% tier + further 2% tier — carried here as flagged, never as a rate; the flat "3% to $3M, 5% above" form is NOT met in this slug's own siblings — stated explicitly.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (research file §4)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a park pad a lot I can buy from this page?** No. Pad ground is NOT land: in a pad park you own the home as a chattel and rent the pad. The parcels on this page are owned land (fee-simple, or a bare-land strata lot where that form applies). (§§2–3)
- **Q: How much down payment should I budget for a lot?** Neither figure is stated as fact in this file: the live page gives two different passages (body: 35–50% for raw or unserviced land; FAQ: 25–35% for a fully serviced urban lot and 35–50% for unserviced rural/agricultural land) and both are UNVERIFIED — have a lender confirm for the specific purchase. The inconsistency itself is flagged in §5. (§§3, 5)
- **Q: Can I build a house on vacant ALR land in Abbotsford?** Under the ALC frame, generally one principal residence ≤500 m² with a 1,000 m² fill limit, a suite only inside it, and one additional residence at 90/186 m² by parcel size — plus City zoning. The page's FAQ answer omits parts of that frame; use the ALC figures. (§3)
- **Q: Can I place a manufactured home on a lot from this page?** That is an own-land purchase, not a pad tenancy — placed and affixed on your parcel and registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, with the Province's newly built home exemption expressly contemplating that structure for a new, not-previously-occupied home. Zone, servicing, ALR and floodplain status decide the specific lot — all UNVERIFIED per parcel. (§§2–3)
- **Q: How small a lot can I create?** On agricultural zones, 8.0 ha (A1) / 16.0 ha (A2) per the City's parcel-size work, with ALR subdivision also engaging the ALC. Urban zone minimums are UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in the research — confirm the specific zone with the City. (§3)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a $1.5M lot?** $28,000 fully residential with no exemption (anchors in §3); at $3.5M, $93,000 including the further 2% tier.
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up (if I buy in a park instead)?** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount (2026) / 2.2% + proportional amount (2027) — percentages only. (§3)
- **Q: Does a park have to approve a resale buyer?** In a pad park, yes — RTB Form 10 consent, 10-day rule, no fee. On owned land there is no pad assignment. (§3)
- **Q: What happens if a park closes or converts?** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (MHPTA s.42). (§3)
- **Q: How do I check a lot's zone, plan designation and permit areas?** Through the City's property information search / WebMap, per the City's Development Planning page — no feed parcel's status is verified in this file. (§3)
