## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block — the pad-tenancy law, in full (it governs the Abbotsford pad purchases cross-referenced in §2; no pad exists in Matsqui itself).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies: a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on is a tenant under the Act; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead; where both the home and the site are rented to the same person, the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. **CORE DISCIPLINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (pad tenure):** the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested on RTB Form 10; the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day after the request, silence by the end of the 10th day is consent deemed given, and no fee may be charged for consenting. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms, with pad rent and the next increase date unchanged. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY, verified live from the RTB framework (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice; the cap is the BC inflation rate plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); no rounding up, no carry-forward. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file** — the one listing-stated figure in §2 (Georgian Park) is labelled listing-stated for one home, and no official source publishes a pad rent for any Abbotsford park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules / closure / conversion (pad tenure):** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement, changeable only on 3 full months' notice (and, since April 30, 2024, only once per year), and enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. On a conversion of all or a significant part of a park (MHPTA s.42): permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' notice; the dispute window is 15 days; additional compensation of assessed value minus $20,000 may be ordered where the home could not be moved, plus a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, if the landlord does not proceed. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone else; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. On Matsqui's own parcels the tax posture is the farm/rural one: Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026, plus the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy on district properties. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **ALR BLOCK IN FULL — Matsqui Prairie is ALR farm ground, and this block governs any manufactured home sited on it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — which is the statutory reason no bare-land strata park can be created out of prairie ALR ground. Residence caps: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY within the principal residence; an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. The City's A1 zone mirrors the added-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, at the same 90 m² / 186 m² caps; one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m² is also permitted in A1. A manufactured home does not bypass any of this: whether a manufactured unit qualifies as the principal residence, the suite, or the accessory farm dwelling on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC/City question — UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until its ALR status and approvals are read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is asserted here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Abbotsford zoning frame:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs. A1 Agricultural Zone (s.210): Agricultural Use and Single Detached Dwelling are principal uses; parcels created through subdivision must be a minimum of 8.0 ha. RR Rural Residential Zone (s.310): intended for single detached dwellings and agricultural uses on large lots in a rural setting; minimum lot size 4.0 ha; a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, and a coach house or secondary suite is limited to one per lot at a maximum of 90 m² or 40% of the net floor area of the principal dwelling, whichever is less. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY. - **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Matsqui farm parcel as the main residence?** The frame allows ONE principal residence per ALR parcel at 500 m² or less, with the A1 zone listing Single Detached Dwelling as a principal use — but whether a specific manufactured unit satisfies the residence, siting and servicing requirements on a specific parcel is a parcel-level ALC/City confirmation: UNCONFIRMED until read for that parcel. The Registry/CSA rules still apply to the home itself. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Can I add a second manufactured home as a farm-worker dwelling?** Only through the A1 accessory route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). A suite is permitted only within the principal residence (A1 also permits one suite per lot to 100 m² as the research states the zone). Do not assume a second unit can be added — read the zone and the ALR status first. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide a prairie parcel into park-style lots?** No route on this record: A1 subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha (RR: 4.0 ha), subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified at all (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, CSA label attached at sale. A fee-simple parcel itself transfers in the land title system — on an own-land Matsqui purchase both systems can be in play (land title for the parcel, registry history for the home). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What holding costs are specific to the prairie?** Property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3; second 5% September 16, 2026) plus the separate Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy on district properties, and well/septic maintenance on a rural parcel. There is no pad rent and no strata fee on a fee-simple prairie parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Matsqui address?** Address-based in SD34. The settled chain, on a single-address basis: 33661 Elizabeth Avenue → Mathxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary. Any other address needs its own locator run. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
