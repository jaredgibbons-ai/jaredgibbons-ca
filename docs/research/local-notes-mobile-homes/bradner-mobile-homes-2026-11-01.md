## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Bradner; carried in full as the standing block, because Bradner buyers cross-shop Abbotsford's parks).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only Bradner form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. Plainly, where a pad tenancy does exist: **a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file — and no pad range is mentioned at all, because no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — the rule that matters for an own-lot Bradner manufactured home.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. Whether a specific farm-lot manufactured home remains Registry-registered or has been de-registered and affixed to its fee-simple land is UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and title records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **FULL ALR BLOCK (this ground is farm ground — carried in full).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. **ALR residences cannot be stratified** — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² gross floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction where the ALC housing page states it; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence for farm use or another permitted use capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions on ALR land, per the ALC housing page. Farm classification (BC Assessment), full three-tier form: $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31; a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error wherever met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — only as ALC sources support (the poultry-farms M2 treatment is the model).** Under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s.34(3), as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional residence that is a manufactured home and not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner (parent, grandparent or great-grandparent, sibling, or child, within the regulation's meaning); a manufactured home permitted as an additional residence may only be occupied as permitted, and if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use. Outside those conditions, ALC permission is the route: a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, which the Commission must not grant unless the residence is necessary for a farm use. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED — no source supports one, and none is stated here.** https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf (~/workspace/local-notes/mobile-homes/poultry-farms-mobile-homes-2026-11-01.md)

- **Q: Can I add a second house or a detached suite on a Bradner farm?** A suite is permitted only inside the principal residence (max 500 m²); a detached second home is the "additional residence," capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha) and tied to farm use or another permitted use. A suite in a detached building is not allowed under the ALC rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide a Bradner manufactured home off its parcel later?** No on ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified, and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval on top of the A1 (8.0 ha) / A2 (16.0 ha) zoning minimums. - **Q: If I buy in an Abbotsford park instead, how does pad rent work?** You own the home and rent the pad under the MHPTA; pad rent rises at most once every 12 months on three full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On a Bradner farm parcel there is no park owner; the gates are title, the ALR frame and the Registry position instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What protects a park buyer if a park is converted?** Permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
