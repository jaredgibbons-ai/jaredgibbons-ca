## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**HONEST POSITION AT THE TOP: there is NO manufactured-home park on Mission farm ground in any source in this program, and NO mobile-home market as a park market on this ground. What exists on farm ground is the possibility of a manufactured home AS A RESIDENCE on an individual farm parcel, governed by the ALR rules in §3 — a completely different purchase from a park pad. This file pads nothing.**

### Manufactured homes on farm ground — only as ALC sources support

- On ALR land, a manufactured home can serve as a residence only within the ALC's housing framework: the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, and the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route where the use does not adhere to the ALR rules. Outside those routes, placing a manufactured home as a residence on ALR land is not a permitted-as-of-right use established in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **A general entitlement to manufactured housing for farm workers on ALR land is UNCONFIRMED** — no ALC source opened in this program grants a blanket farm-worker manufactured-housing right. Each case turns on the parcel's ALR status, the residence rules below, and whether an ALC application (including a NARU application) is required and approved. Confirm with the ALC and the City/FVRD for the exact parcel before pricing any manufactured-home plan into a farm purchase.
- A manufactured home on a farm parcel the buyer OWNS is not a pad tenancy: there is no park, no pad rent, and no MHPTA site tenancy — the home is dealt with through the Manufactured Home Registry for the home itself and the land title system for the land (§3). **THE TENURE LINE, stated plainly for contrast: a home on a RENTED PAD in a park is a chattel purchase plus a pad tenancy and is NOT land ownership — that park form does not exist on this farm ground.**

### The full ALR block — residences on farm ground

- **No stratification of ALR residences:** ALC Bulletin 09 s.6 — residences on ALR land cannot be stratified; the Strata Property Act s.239 and the Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) frame the prohibition, and subdivision of ALR land requires ALC approval. A buyer cannot turn farm residences into strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Principal residence cap:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Suite position:** a secondary suite is permitted only INSIDE the principal residence — no separate suite building as of right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Additional residence:** one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, total floor area 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Mission (or FVRD, per jurisdiction) bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR status is parcel-specific:** check the parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. Mission's ALR land area or share of the municipal land base: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm classification — the full three-tier form (BC Assessment):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — and farm classification applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — stated for contrast, because it does NOT govern a home on your own farm land.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. It governs the cross-referenced Lougheed corridor parks in §2, not a farm parcel purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (park sales only).** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (park sales only).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules (parks only).** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY (parks only).** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and no pad figure from any Mission park listing is carried into this farm file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42, parks only) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry — this applies to a manufactured home placed on farm land just as it does to a park home; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a manufactured home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. A farm parcel's own tax position turns on farm classification (§2) and jurisdiction (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on the buyer's own farm land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure — and on a farm purchase, what is being transferred (land, home, or both) decides the analysis. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land transfers — the form a farm purchase takes).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier** — the $78,000 figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling, is not met in this slug's own siblings, reconciliation is OPEN, and $93,000 is followed everywhere. Farm/ALR transfers can carry their own PTT considerations — confirm the exact parcel's treatment with a lawyer/notary; nothing finer is asserted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (no bare-land strata manufactured-home community is verified in Mission, and ALR residences cannot be stratified at all — §2).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on Mission farm land?** No — none is verified on this ground in any source. Mission's parks are Lougheed Highway corridor pad-rental parks documented in the city-wide files (§2, cross-reference only). Farm-ground manufactured housing is a residence-on-a-parcel question under the ALR rules, not a park question.
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a farm I buy in Mission?** Only within the ALC framework: as a residence under the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, or through a Non-Adhering Residential Use application — subject to the 500 m² principal cap, the suite-inside-principal rule, and the additional-residence size limits (90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha), plus local bylaws. There is no blanket right. (§2/§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Q: Can I house farm workers in manufactured homes on the farm?** UNCONFIRMED as a general entitlement — no ALC source in this program grants one. A specific proposal may fit the residence routes or a NARU application, but it must be confirmed with the ALC for the exact parcel before it is priced into a deal. (§2.)
- **Q: If I place a manufactured home on my own farm land, do I pay pad rent or strata fees?** Neither. There is no pad (you own the land) and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Your costs are the land's taxes (farm classification matters, §2), the home's registration, insurance and servicing. (§2/§3.)
- **Q: How is the home itself transferred if it's manufactured?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA label must be attached at sale. The land transfers separately through the land title system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's three tiers: $2,500 (operation 0.8–4 ha); $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (operation over 4 ha); $10,000 (operation under 0.8 ha) — applications by October 31 for the following tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Which tax penalty applies to my Mission farm — 10% or 5% + 5%?** It depends on the parcel's jurisdiction, not its mailing address: inside the City of Mission, a FLAT 10% after July 2; in an FVRD electoral area, the rural regime (5% after July 2 + 5% after October 31, Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11). Confirm the parcel's jurisdiction first. (§3.)
- **Q: Can the farm's residences be stratified or subdivided later?** Residences: no — ALR residences cannot be stratified. Land: subdivision of ALR land requires ALC approval, and local minimum parcel sizes apply on top. (§2.)
- **Q: What happens to a park home if its park closes — does that affect farm buyers?** Only for the cross-referenced corridor parks: MHPTA s.42 requires permits first, 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation (§3). It does not apply to a home on your own farm land — there is no park to close. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mission farm address?** SD75 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a farm address is on record — UNVERIFIED. The rural schools named in §1 are Ministry-listed schools in the districts, not catchments for any specific parcel. Run the exact address in the SD75 locator. https://www.mpsd.ca
