## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. For both Kent-ground records, the park's own rules were not opened in this program: UNCONFIRMED — obtain them with the tenancy agreement before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute a closure notice within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. District of Kent taxes apply to a Kent park home — notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day), and diking rides on the bill as its own rate line (§1). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED); pad-resale PTT stays UNCONFIRMED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in the District of Kent — the block governs the townsite's townhouse-strata diligence instead).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District frame — OCP / zoning / servicing, numbers HELD.** The District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025 governs; it directs most growth into the Agassiz townsite, with Mount Woodside as a satellite growth village, and makes agriculture a headline priority (§4.2). **Kent zoning and servicing numbers are held UNCONFIRMED in this file, per this slug's own research position**: the research file states no Kent zoning bylaw number and no Kent servicing bylaw number that this file adopts, and the numbers carried in the condos siblings (1219, 2001 / 1737, drawn from the kent research file) are a **conforming-pass item only — flagged in FILE STATUS, never adopted here**. (The research file does document the District's SSMUH information guide position — up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots in the RS1 zone — as a District guide statement; that guide statement is carried as such, not as a bylaw number relied on in this file.) **Harrison Hot Springs OCP 1184, 2022 is Harrison's — never a Kent number.** Any parcel's actual zone and servicing: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED — confirm with the District of Kent for the specific property. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf
- **ALR / floodplain — the farm-edge frame.** Most of the District's flat land outside the townsite is farmed and in the ALR (§1); on ALR parcels, ALC rules on homes, farm-worker housing and non-farm use apply on top of District zoning, and manufactured homes on farm ground follow only the s.34(3) / Policy L-25 + Non-Adhering Residential Use route (§2). Flood and diking frame every flat-land purchase here: verify dike status, drainage, flood construction requirements and insurance for the specific parcel (OCP §5.5). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at the CRA GI-003 general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in the District of Kent?** On record, two items — and both are tenure records, not verified parks: Continental Trailer Park Ltd (40 pads in the FVRD's 2020 inventory, with no civic address in the extract checked, so its location is UNCONFIRMED), and Heath Court in the Agassiz townsite (1884 Heath Road), which is documented at home in the agassiz mobile file on listing evidence. No park in the District is verified on an operator source in this program. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home at Heath Court, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Heath Court a 55+ park?** UNCONFIRMED — no source checked states any age rule for Heath Court. Do not buy or market it as 55+ without the park's own rules and tenancy agreement in hand. (§2; agassiz mobile file §4.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). A listing's monthly figure for one specific home is listing copy for that home, not a park rate, and no dollar figure is carried as fact in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Kent farm?** Only through the ALR route — ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 plus a Non-Adhering Residential Use approval; there is no general farm-worker entitlement (UNCONFIRMED), and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Confirm with the ALC and the District before relying on any farm-placement plan. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Kent address?** SD78 Policy 7000, as the research records it: Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School takes all grade 7–12 students in the District of Kent and the Village of Harrison Hot Springs, plus the Chehalis-watershed-to-Ruby Creek strip along Highway 7; Kent Elementary takes K–6 students south of McCallum Road in the District, plus that same strip. No park-address locator run is on record, so a specific pad address's catchment is carried on that policy basis only. https://sd78.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/PDC-Meeting-Agenda-Package-2021-04-20.pdf

- **Diking is a line item on the tax bill.** The District's 2026 Tax Rates Summary carries a dedicated "Dyking" column — 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value for Class 1 Residential, 0.29356 for Class 9 Farm — charged as an assessment-based rate, not a flat per-parcel charge. A park-home buyer on the flat sees the flood system in the tax schedule every year. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **An inventory entry with no address is not a park you can drive to.** Continental Trailer Park Ltd exists in this program only as a 40-pad line in a 2020 FVRD inventory, with no civic address in the extract checked.
