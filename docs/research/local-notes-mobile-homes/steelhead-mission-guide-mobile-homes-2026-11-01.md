## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — Steelhead's documented form — is dealt with through the land title system instead, and the MHPTA pad rules below do not apply to it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified from the RTB: **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — s.42 block.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, and may dispute the notice within the 15-day dispute window; additional amounts can apply — the difference between the home's assessed value before and after the closure notice, plus $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This registry check applies to Steelhead's own-land manufactured homes as well — confirm the home's registry record and whether it is affixed to the land, per property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Property taxes — Mission frame.** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **flat 10% penalty** added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026. The 5% + 5% figures in rural-area material are the separate RURAL regime and are DISTINCT — they do not apply inside the City of Mission, where Steelhead sits. An own-land Steelhead owner carries no pad rent and no strata fee. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact; both flat page claims met in this program are UNVERIFIED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — directly relevant to Steelhead's own-land form. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Steelhead).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Mission frame.** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 (adopted April 21, 2026) and Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 govern. The rural area divides into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s; a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size. A Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity — not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. Mission adopted SSMUH by Bylaw 6299-2024; **the unit counts under it are UNCONFIRMED in this program**, and whether SSMUH counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel UNVERIFIED — Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: Development Servicing Charges Bylaw 5650-2017 (City source) in the verified ledger. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **ALR, wells and septic.** ALR status is per-parcel — check the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder; on ALR land a manufactured home is available only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + Non-Adhering Residential Use route, and a general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. The guide page's own listings show the area's servicing pattern — drilled wells, septic systems and propane heat on acreage parcels. Wells are recorded in GWELLS, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level (CRA GI-003 general level); anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (Standing program position.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
