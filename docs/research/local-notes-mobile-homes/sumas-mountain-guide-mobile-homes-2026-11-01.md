## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the pad-tenancy block IN FULL.** The Act governs pad tenancies: it applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on in a manufactured home park; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No verified pad-rental park exists on Sumas Mountain ground (§2), so this block is the governing frame for the Abbotsford parks cross-referenced in §2 and the test that keeps Straiton (strata) from being confused with a pad park (tenancy). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **CORE DISCIPLINE — a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership:** the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad; the land stays the park owner's. There is no strata lot, no Form B, and no land title for the pad. Own-land stock is different in kind: a bare-land strata purchase (Straiton) carries Form B/fees/bylaws, and a co-op purchase carries shares/membership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale — no new tenancy:** when a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is ASSIGNED to the purchaser, who becomes the park owner's tenant on the same terms from the assignment date. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park-owner consent — the 10-day rule:** assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on the approved form (RTB Form 10); the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules:** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY:** pad rent may be increased only once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) PLUS a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the Residential Tenancy Branch (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact anywhere in this file — no pad-rent figure for any park is documented in this ground's research file, and any live-page pad-rent range is UNVERIFIED site copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion — only as the Province states it:** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Anything finer for a specific park is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Age-restricted parks — the provincial general rule only:** in a designated 55-plus park, at least one tenant on the site must be 55 years of age or older. No specific park's age rule is stated from this general rule, and Straiton has NO verified age restriction (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home is factory-built to the CSA standard and registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the Province's central register of manufactured home ownership; a sale, transfer or purchase is ONLY effective if registered there, filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary) with the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does NOT include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (governs Straiton in full):** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (Straiton, at 26 lots on the City report's count, is inside the requirement); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Strata Property Regulation 7.01, including legacy-resident, live-in caregiver, and the May 1, 2023 expansion for a younger spouse/partner and defined children) — a 45+ line, wherever marketed, is VOID and there is no conversion of a 45+ bylaw into a valid one. What Straiton's fees cover specifically is in its budget/Form B: UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

- **Q: If I buy on a rented pad elsewhere in Abbotsford, what do I own?** The home only — a chattel. You rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; the sale takes effect only when registered in the Manufactured Home Registry (Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer via a lawyer or notary, CSA label attached); and the existing pad tenancy is assigned to you on the same terms, with the park owner's written consent requested on RTB Form 10 — answered by the 10th day or deemed given, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How fast can pad rent rise, and what if a park closes?** Pad rent: once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at inflation plus the proportional levy/utility amount — 2.3% + proportional for 2026, 2.2% + proportional for 2027, percentages only in this file. Closure/conversion: permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: City taxes or rural taxes — which apply to a mountain lot?** It depends which side of the line the lot sits on: City lots pay the City (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026); FVRD rural lots pay the Province's Surveyor of Taxes under the rural regime (due July 2; 5% on July 2, further 5% on October 31). Confirm the lot's government before relying on any rule — the two rule sets differ on plan, servicing and taxes alike. (§3.)
