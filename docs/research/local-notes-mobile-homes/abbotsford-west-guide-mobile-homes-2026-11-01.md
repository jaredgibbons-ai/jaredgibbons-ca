## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for every Paradise Park pad:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies in BC — the buyer owns the home and rents the site. Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice. **Pad-rent increase caps, verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount — percentages ONLY; no pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion (RTB):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give a 12-month notice (Form RTB-31), and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; a tenant who cannot relocate the home in the circumstances the RTB page sets out (including owing no tax in relation to the home) may apply for dispute resolution for additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the initial $20,000. Finer park-rule, park-sale and rule-change mechanics are UNCONFIRMED beyond the RTB forms/rules pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Resale in a park runs through the park:** the Paradise Park listing itself shows the approval step is real — one small pet is allowed only with park approval — and a purchaser-approval step applies in parks of this form (carried as the siblings carry it for Abbotsford parks). Get the park's current rules and approval requirements in writing before writing an offer. https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31313-livingstone-avenue/9
- **Home as chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** in BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains a central register of manufactured home ownership details, controls the movement of manufactured homes in BC, and improves the security of lenders financing manufactured-home purchases. A pad-park home is registered in the Registry, not on a land title — check the Registry record (owner, location, liens) before completion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **PTT on a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED at the primary-source level:** no Province PTT passage stating the transfer treatment of a pad-park chattel home was opened for this file, so no flat "PTT does / does not apply" statement is carried as fact here. The Abbotsford pad-fee page items are NOT documented for this guide slug (see §5). State the PTT position for a specific purchase in the contract on advice, not on site copy.
- **Bare-land strata block (governs every Downes Road Estates lot, via its home file):** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **PTT worked example — own-land case only (a Downes Road Estates bare-land strata lot purchase):** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Zoning/OCP for this ground:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; OCP = Abbotsforward 2050 CURRENT (adopted December 2025) — the research file's "2600-2016, as updated" wording is STALE. SSMUH rules sit in Zoning Bylaw s.140 (bylaw number UNCONFIRMED); **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it creates no right to add units on a rented pad, and a park's form is governed by its park tenure and rules, not by the SSMUH provisions for a fee-simple lot. Suite cap under 50% of the principal dwelling and coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² are Abbotsford's own-lot rules ONLY. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the level the CRA sources state:** CRA contemplates a new or substantially renovated mobile home purchased from a builder (including the manufacturer or vendor) as new housing for the GST/HST New Housing Rebate, and CRA policy material treats the sale of a previously occupied residential unit that is a residential complex under the exempting provisions — carried here only as that used-generally-exempt / new-taxable framing; the GST treatment of a specific Paradise Park or Downes Road resale is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and the home's history are established. State it in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-home-construction/new-housing-rebate.html
- **First-home programs:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — never March 12) applies to new HOUSING: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. Whether a specific manufactured-home purchase qualifies is UNCONFIRMED until the home's new/used status and agreement date are read. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. A pad tenant's tax picture differs from a lot owner's — what portion, if any, of park levies flows through is in the tenancy's proportional-amount mechanism above, not a separate land tax bill for the pad. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: In Paradise Park, what do I actually own?** The manufactured home itself — a chattel. You rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; you do not own the land, hold a strata lot, or get a Form B. The sale/transfer is only effective when registered in the Manufactured Home Registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How much can my pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least three full months' written notice, capped at 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount for increases taking effect in 2026, and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027 — percentages only; the dollar amount depends on the pad rent in your own tenancy agreement, which no source in this file states. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How secure is a pad tenancy if the park is sold or closes?** A sale alone is not a closure. For conversion of all or a significant part of a park, the RTB requires permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route where the RTB conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a home on a rented pad?** Financing a chattel is different from financing land: there is no land collateral, and the Registry exists in part to improve lender security on manufactured-home purchases — but lender terms, rates and eligibility for a specific park home are UNCONFIRMED in this file; confirm with the lender against the Registry record before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is Paradise Park really 55+?** Listings state a 55+ age restriction — carried as listing-stated. A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw, so the SPA 55+ framework does not settle it; get the park's current rules in writing. https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/31313-livingstone-avenue/9
- **Q: Can I resell my park home to anyone?** Resale runs through the park's approval process as well as the Registry transfer — the listing record shows park approval operating even for a pet. Confirm the park's purchaser-approval requirements before listing or offering. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How is Downes Road Estates different from Paradise Park?** Tenure. Downes is bare-land strata: you own the lot (LMS2380), pay a strata fee, and diligence is the Form B and registered bylaws. Paradise is pad rental: you own only the home and rent the site under the MHPTA. Its land form is documented in the lots-and-land home file. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Downes Road Estates 55+?** UNRESOLVED. Listings in the siblings state 55+; one listing record markets "45+," which cannot be a valid BC age bylaw. The registered bylaws on the Form B govern — do not rely on either marketing line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra unit to a pad home, or to a Downes lot?** SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies, so no added-unit right arises on a rented pad. On an own lot, Abbotsford's s.140 suite rules (one suite in all single detached dwellings, suite under 50%, coach/garden suite 15% of lot to 100 m² maximum) are lot rules — and in a bare-land strata the registered bylaws also apply. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Which schools serve a Paradise Park or Downes Road home?** UNCONFIRMED for those addresses — no locator run for either address is documented in the research or siblings. The west side splits (three settled chains are in §1, each single-address only); run the exact address in the SD34 School Locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
