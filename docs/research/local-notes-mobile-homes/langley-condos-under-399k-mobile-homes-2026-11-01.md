## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example at this band's level, inputs stated: a $399,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $199,000 ($3,980) = **$5,980**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is in this band).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — two-municipality Langley frame.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500 / SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (NO 6-plex tier), suite cap 120 m², coach house fee-simple s.102, servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED. City: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED), SSMUH Bylaw 3284, suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4.)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt, a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000. FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age/foundation requirements) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.
- **Foreclosure — where met.** Settled court process (Rules 21-7/13-5, PD-66, Form 60); Form B + PTT/GST on court sales UNCONFIRMED. REGISTRY LEDGER: Langley is NOT in the ledger — no registry asserted. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a condo under $399K the same purchase as a mobile home under $399K?** No. This page's stock is strata apartment condos — you buy a strata lot. A park home is a chattel on a rented pad under the MHPTA (§3). The monthly stack, the approval step and the resale route are all different. (Research file §§1, 5.)
- **Q: Are the listings on this page really all under $399,000?** No — the research documents two cards above the ceiling ($399,999 and $399,900) and one exactly at it ($399,000). Set the search ceiling explicitly rather than trusting the page's filter. (Research file §1.)
- **Q: How many Langley condos are under $399K?** That count cannot be stated from SnapStats — it publishes no band cut and no condo-only cut (699 combined Condos & Townhomes actives, September 2026). The feed's own 75-result display is a snapshot, not a band statistic. (Research file §§1–2.)
- **Q: If I buy in a Langley park instead, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me?** Yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park closes?** Permits first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a park-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; the CSA label must be attached at sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home at this price point?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level — chattel treatment and home-age/foundation rules are lender-specific. Confirm before removing subjects.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Langley address?** SD35, address-based. The one settled chain on record is the 7888 200 Street sample (§1); every other address — including this page's condo and any park address — is UNVERIFIED until run in the SD35 locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
