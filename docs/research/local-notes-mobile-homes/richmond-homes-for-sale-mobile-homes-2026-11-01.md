## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — stated in full (background law for any pad tenancy in the city; no such tenancy is verified here).** The Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site; a home on owned land goes through the land title system. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** The existing pad tenancy is assigned on sale; the seller must give the proposed purchaser any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules before asking the park owner to consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order) on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt, silence = deemed consent, NO fee/benefit for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, formula cap (BC CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' written notice; 15-day dispute window + assessed-value/$5,000 amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** Effective only if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership via a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land or on land owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home — City of Richmond 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% on balances outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source settles PTT on a home-only pad purchase; NO flat claim either way as fact (BOTH flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — for a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. Lawyer/notary confirmation required for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example: $700,000 = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — follow $93,000. **CONFORMING ITEM FLAGGED (this slug's own sibling): the townhomes sibling for THIS slug lists "$3.5M purely residential → $78,000" in its worked anchors — that figure omits the further 2% tier and is WRONG for a $3.5M input; it is flagged here precisely, never followed, and the reconciliation follows $93,000. (The M12 PTT reading discipline is respected: $78,000 at a $3,200,000 input would be correct for that input — the Richmond townhomes figure is stated at a $3.5M input, which is why it is an error.) Sibling PTT-shorthand check, stated explicitly: NO sibling for this slug carries the "3% to $3M, 5% above" form as its own rate — the townhomes sibling's "Over $3M — 5%" wording is a flagging sentence only.** (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/richmond-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md §3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park anywhere in Richmond?** None is verified in this program. Quilchena Green is a strata townhouse community in Terra Nova and Covenant Court is a strata complex in Woodwards counted in no total — neither is a park. (§2.)
- **Q: If I found a home on a rented pad here, would I own land?** No. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad**, under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Quilchena Green 45+ or 55+?** UNRESOLVED — the labels conflict (45+ vs 55+) and are carried flagged, never resolved; a 45+ strata bylaw would in any case be void under the current SPA and does not convert to 55+. Read the actual Form B / registered bylaws for the unit. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Is Covenant Court verified 55+?** No — listing-stated only; its registered bylaw wording is UNCONFIRMED, and it is counted in no total in this program. (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in BC?** 2.3% + proportional (2026); 2.2% + proportional (2027), once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice — percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park owner have to approve me?** Where the MHPTA applies, yes — Form 10, day-10 response, deemed consent on silence, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured home registered?** Manufactured Home Registry only, via lawyer/notary with signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer; CSA label at sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which school serves a Richmond address?** Names in §1 are Ministry-listed only; every catchment is UNVERIFIED in this file, runs are single-address/non-transferable, and Boyd Park's catchment is UNVERIFIED everywhere. Run the exact address in the SD38 locator. (§1.)
- **Q: Does the floodplain change a manufactured-home purchase on owned land?** Yes on the land side: Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels and dike setbacks apply to new construction city-wide — confirm for the specific lot before pricing a placement or rebuild. (§1.)
