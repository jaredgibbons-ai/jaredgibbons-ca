## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Eastern Hillsides; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (the $78,000 figure in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000; it is not met on this ground). The townhomes siblings' "3% to $3M, 5% above" shorthand is WRONG wherever met and is never carried here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Eastern Hillsides — Baker Trail Village is the Chilliwack example, in its own home).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Chilliwack frame.** OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500, with the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan as Schedule B4 — its development cells and unit allocations still regulate how many units may be developed, while land-use designations now come from the city-wide OCP. Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) minimum lot area is set by the slope table and ranges from 360 m² to 990 m² (minimum width 12 m, depth 20 m); R3 minimum lot area 360 m²; HR minimum lot area 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha otherwise (minimum width 25 m); RH minimum lot area 8 ha. The zone applying to any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — confirm per property. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH / added units.** Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a lot-zoning framework, not a park-tenancy right. (Standing Chilliwack frame.)

- **Hazards — the defining rules for this ground.** The plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 amendments exist specifically because of rock fall, landslide and earthflow risk; the City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands; Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported; and under the 2050 OCP's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5), a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Eastern Hillsides?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park inside the plan area, so there is no rented-pad stock here at all. - **Q: Can I place a manufactured home on a hillside lot I buy here?** That would be an own-land purchase, not a pad tenancy — and on this ground the gating items are the zone-and-slope lot math (SHR minimums run 360 m² to 990 m² by slope), the Hillside Development Permit Area (permit required for subdivision of land with 20%+ slopes), and the geotechnical record. - **Q: What slope and slide checks matter before I buy here?** The plan identifies steep slopes and geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 amendments exist specifically to protect development from rock fall, landslide and earthflow; the Marble Hill slide areas are identified as undevelopable lands; and an expanded Marble Hill moratorium was directed in 2017. Get the property's geotechnical reports and any covenants on title before removing subjects. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **Q: Is Marble Hill a separate neighbourhood?** No — Marble Hill is a named slope area inside the Eastern Hillsides plan area, and it carries the area's sharpest hazard history (see above). - **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a Chilliwack park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). Eastern Hillsides itself has no pad rent — there is no park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On an Eastern Hillsides freehold lot there is no park owner and no such approval. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How is a manufactured home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The 2017 plan amendment is a hazard amendment, not a growth amendment.** The Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan was amended in 2017 specifically to clarify development-approval requirements protecting future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions — a buyer reading only the adoption date (2012) misses why the current version reads the way it does. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- **The Marble Hill moratorium followed reported ground movement.** Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported, and development there requires extensive geotechnical evaluation first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2756/2017%2011%2021%20%20%2D%20%20OCP31%20RZ1130%20%20Full%20Council%20Report%20Marble%20Hill%20Area%2Epdf
- **The same zone can mean two very different lots.** In the HR zone the minimum lot area is 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases, and in SHR the minimum runs 360 m² to 990 m² by slope band — two lots with the same zone label can carry very different subdivision and build potential, which is the whole pricing question for a manufactured-home placement on owned land here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **The plan area has parks and trails but no daily services.** City listings give the area Allan Creek Park, Forest Gate Park, Marble Hill Natural Area, Allan Creek Trail, Chilliwack Community Forest and the Elk Mountain Trail — and the research records no shopping centre or grocery store inside the plan area confirmed on an official source, and no recreation centre or library branch inside it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=203
- **The historic wagon road is a protected heritage asset in the plan.** The Neighbourhood Plan identifies the historic wagon road (Browlee Road) as a heritage asset to protect, and states wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
