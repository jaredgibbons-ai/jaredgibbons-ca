## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before asking for consent the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response due by the 10th day, silence = deemed consent, NO consent fee. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file, and no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' notice; 15-day dispute window; additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the settled files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** Sale/transfer/purchase effective only if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED); the Province's express point covers only a NEW manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land. Confirm with a lawyer/notary for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a community is a bare-land strata; none verified for the hub's areas here).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); 45+ VOID, never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame (all three guide areas).** OCP 21900 (never 18020); Zoning 12000; SSMUH July 8, 2024 (3 units <280 m², 4 units >280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop); suite cap 40%; servicing 8830 (1986); STR owner-hosted primary-residence only with a business licence — **$358 / $350 conflict disclosed, resolved nowhere**; floodplain Part 7A by stream class, NO single figure. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** ALR (the Serpentine guide's ground): principal residence maximum 500 m², suite inside it only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); subdivision needs ALC permission; ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** General level: GST on new construction, not resales; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate = new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes: UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm per home/tenure. (Standing brief.)
- **Property taxes — Surrey frame.** Due July 2; 5% penalty after midnight July 2, further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure — where met.** BC process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, PD-66, Form 60). REGISTRY LEDGER: Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED). Form B + PTT/GST on court sales UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

- **Q: In a park, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. Own-land manufactured stock is the fee-simple / bare-land strata form instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me?** Yes, for a pad tenancy — prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
