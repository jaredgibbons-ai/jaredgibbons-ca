## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists on the Main corridor; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** — both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. **Conforming flag for this ground: this area's own townhomes sibling carries the PTT shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" — that shorthand is WRONG (the structure is 3% above $2M plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) and is NOT carried here; it is flagged as a conforming-pass item.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/main-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified on the corridor — the block DOES govern a strata purchase in the corridor's built condos/townhomes, and it does NOT govern the Lee Building record in §2, whose tenure is OPEN and which carries no Form B regime on the standing record).** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / plans — Vancouver frame.** The residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units; the district schedule for the Main Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED. The Broadway Plan (approved June 22, 2022; Council updates December 2024) covers the corridor's north end, providing for buildings up to 20–30 storeys near the future Mount Pleasant Station; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Subdivision / servicing — Vancouver frame.** Subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, with fees under By-law No. 14538 — the standing verified ledger position for Vancouver. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/subdividing-land.aspx (standing brief)
- **Empty Homes Tax — Vancouver only.** Owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a live cost on the corridor's older rental or vacant stock. The 3% rate is a City of Vancouver tax — it does not apply outside Vancouver (and not in the UEL). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level CRA sources support: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer for a specific manufactured home (vendor status, whether the home was previously occupied, placement on own land) is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — a March 12 statement met anywhere is a conforming-pass item, never carried): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on the Main corridor?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park, bare-land strata manufactured-home community or purchasable co-op park on the corridor. The stock is houses, walk-up rentals and built strata condos/townhomes. (Section 2.)
- **Q: The Lee Building looks cheap for its size and location — what is the catch?** Tenure — and the question is OPEN in this program. The standing record is undivided interest under a tenants-in-common agreement: listings state it cannot be financed (cash purchase only), taxes are bundled into the monthly fee, and there is no strata Form B. (Section 2; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/main-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What would I own if I bought a home on a rented pad somewhere in BC?** The home only. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Plainly, a home on a rented pad is not land ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in a BC park?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). The corridor has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. A corridor strata purchase needs no park approval. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A missing label is a red flag for financing and resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific. Confirm financing for the specific home and its pad tenure before removing subjects. (Standing brief.)
- **Q: When does the corridor's SkyTrain station open?** Mount Pleasant Station, at Main Street and Broadway, is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027 as part of the 5.7 km Broadway Subway — a dated event, not an open-ended promise. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Which schools serve a Main corridor address?** Catchments are address-based in SD39 and no locator run for a corridor address is settled in this program — the research sample (315 East 23rd Avenue) is UNVERIFIED. The corridor-area schools on the VSB list are Livingstone Elementary (315 East 23rd Avenue), Wolfe Elementary (4251 Ontario Street), Queen Alexandra Elementary (1300 East Broadway) and Vancouver Technical Secondary (2600 East Broadway) — run the exact address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
