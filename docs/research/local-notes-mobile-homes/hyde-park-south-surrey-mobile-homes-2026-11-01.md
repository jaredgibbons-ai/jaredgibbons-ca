## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) plus a proportional amount (change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window applies, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the settled program files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; BOTH flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. Pad-resale PTT: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked anchors with inputs + tier math: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). $78,000 at a $3,200,000 input is CORRECT for that input and is never flagged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; Hyde Park itself is a conventional strata townhouse complex, to which this block applies as strata law, not park law).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Surrey frame.** OCP 21900, Zoning 12000, suite cap 40%, SSMUH Jul 8, 2024; STR licence figure conflict $358/$350 disclosed in the siblings, not resolved here. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Surrey streamside setbacks are Part 7A by stream class — NO single figure.
- **GST — only at CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt, a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level (CRA GI-003 general level); anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home, age/foundation) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Hyde Park a mobile home park?** No — it is a Zenterra strata townhouse complex (156 townhomes per the developer brochure). You buy a strata lot, not a home on a pad; the Strata Property Act, not the MHPTA, governs. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Q: Is there any manufactured-home stock in Grandview?** None verified — no park, bare-land strata manufactured community or co-op is verified on this ground in the research or any sibling. (§2.)
- **Q: Where is South Surrey's manufactured stock documented?** Sea Ridge Bays' park home is the south-surrey-mobile-homes topic file — start there, not in this project file. Ocean Park Place carries a dual basis (counted in condos AND in the 55+ queue; this file takes no count), and Ocean Park Village/Grove stay UNCONFIRMED/queued. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy in a pad-rental park elsewhere, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me?** Yes — assignment needs prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, 15-day dispute window. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which strata am I buying into at Hyde Park?** Establish it first — Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner are separate stratas per the live page, with different fees and documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Hyde Park address?** UNVERIFIED — the page names Sunnyside Elementary / Grandview Heights Secondary (both Ministry-confirmed nearby by name/address), but no locator run for this complex is on record; run the exact address. (Research file §4.)
