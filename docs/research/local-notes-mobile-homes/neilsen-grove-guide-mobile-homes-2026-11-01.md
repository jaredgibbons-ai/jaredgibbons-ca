## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the pad-tenancy block IN FULL.** The Act governs pad tenancies: it applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on in a manufactured home park; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No verified pad-rental park exists on Neilsen Grove ground (§2), so this block is the governing frame a buyer needs if a pad-tenancy listing is ever marketed here — and the contrast that keeps South Pointe (strata) and a pad park (tenancy) from being confused. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **CORE DISCIPLINE — a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership:** the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad; the land stays the park owner's. There is no strata lot, no Form B, and no land title for the pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale — no new tenancy:** when a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is ASSIGNED to the purchaser, who becomes the park owner's tenant on the same terms from the assignment date. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park-owner consent — the 10-day rule:** assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on the approved form (RTB Form 10); the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules:** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY:** pad rent may be increased only once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) PLUS a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the Residential Tenancy Branch (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact anywhere in this file — no pad-rent range for any Delta park is documented in this ground's research file, and any live-page pad-rent range is UNVERIFIED site copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion — only as the Province states it:** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Anything finer for a specific park is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Age-restricted parks — the provincial general rule only:** in a designated 55-plus park, at least one tenant on the site must be 55 years of age or older. No specific Delta park's age rule is stated from this general rule — a park's own rule is read from its operator / tenancy documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home is factory-built to the CSA standard and registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the Province's central register of manufactured home ownership; a sale, transfer or purchase is ONLY effective if registered there, filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary) with the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does NOT include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (governs South Pointe in full):** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Strata Property Regulation 7.01, including legacy-resident, live-in caregiver, and the May 1, 2023 expansion for a younger spouse/partner and defined children) — a 45+ line, wherever marketed, is VOID and there is no conversion of a 45+ bylaw into a valid one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City frame — Delta:** OCP = Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400, consolidated 2024 ("Housing Our Future"), with NO lettered schedules — a "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an ERROR wherever met. Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps; the zoning BYLAW NUMBER is CONTESTED (zone sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates) — flagged, never asserted; South Pointe's own zone is the site-specific CD98A. SSMUH amendments are Bylaws 8394 + 8395 (June 24, 2024), allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a land-zoning regime for owned lots, and inside a bare-land strata the strata bylaws sit on top of the zoning. Subdivision/servicing: DSSS Bylaw 8288 adoption is UNCONFIRMED — the lots-and-land GUIDE sibling for this ground asserts Bylaw 8288, 2024 in governing terms; that assertion conflicts with the standing correction, is flagged here and in §5, and is NOT followed in this file. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — treatment stated only as official sources support it:** PTT treatment of a resale manufactured home on a RENTED PAD is UNCONFIRMED in the sources for this file — flat site-copy claims either way (taxable or exempt) are UNVERIFIED, and no flat claim is made here. What the Province's own exemption page DOES state expressly: the Newly Built Home Exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) as a newly built home — a rule for a NEW home placed on OWN land, not for a resale home on a rented pad. For an owned-land purchase in this sub-area (a fee-simple lot, or a bare-land strata lot at South Pointe), the general PTT tiers apply unless an exemption applies: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs + tier math stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential owned-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a $78,000 figure for $3.5M met in a standalone acreages sibling for this ground (carrying a townhomes-program figure) is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption

The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year.
