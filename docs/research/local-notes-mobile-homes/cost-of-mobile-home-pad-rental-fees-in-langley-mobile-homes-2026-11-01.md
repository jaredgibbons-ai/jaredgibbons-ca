## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. Landlords and tenants in a manufactured home park must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy — and the rent does not reset.** Assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; under MHPTA s.28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; and **on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase** — a transfer does not trigger a new market rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response due by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent, NO consent fee. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY (the core of this topic).** A park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit: **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** The proportional amount is exactly: the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park — each tenant pays a share of the year's increase in the park's common-property taxes and regulated utilities. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. Above-limit increases require an RTB application (Form 52) for qualifying financial-loss or substantial-repair reasons; tenants do not have to pay an above-limit increase and may deduct an overpayment from future rent or apply for dispute resolution. **No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice; 15-day dispute window; additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000 in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing); if the landlord does not proceed within a reasonable period, the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** Sale/transfer/purchase effective only if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer/notary filing the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; CSA label (A-277 modular, Z-240 mobile) must be attached — a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal, and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. A Registry transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land where the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner — the accurate version of the live page's "taxes on the structure only" line. Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED); the Province's express point covers only a NEW manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land. Confirm with a lawyer/notary for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a community is a bare-land strata; none verified for Langley in this slug's research).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); 45+ VOID, never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Township + City frames, kept separate.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 units on larger eligible lots, **NO 6-plex tier**); suite cap 120 m²; coach houses may be fee-simple under s.102; **R-CL s.403 lot area = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum — there is no 355 m² figure**; servicing Development Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED. City of Langley: Zoning Bylaw 3300 cited by number (adoption UNVERIFIED); SSMUH Bylaw 3284; suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. The manufactured-home-park zone name and rules in either bylaw: UNVERIFIED — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened in the research pass, so no zone claim for a Langley park is made. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md; research file above.)

NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much is pad rent in Langley?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated as fact in this file — and the live page's range is flagged in §5 as UNVERIFIED site copy. Budget from the specific park's written site tenancy agreement: the current rent, the last increase date, and what the rent includes. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the yearly limit: 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 — once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice; anything above the limit requires an RTB order. Ask the park for its last proportional-amount calculation, because that amount is park-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent stays the same for the new tenant and the next scheduled increase date does not reset — and the park cannot charge a fee for consenting. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: In a Langley park, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** The exception forms are different products entirely: a fee-simple lot, a bare-land strata lot, or a co-op share (Pioneer Park) — none of which carries MHPTA pad rent. (§2 above.)
- **Q: What does pad rent include?** Whatever the specific park's written agreement says — there is no verified general Langley answer. The live page's "usually includes / does not include" lists are site copy, not an official source; read the agreement for the exact site. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a park home?** Yes, on the home: assessed like other residential property, but the assessed value excludes the land value in a park (not on reserve land). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted?** Under MHPTA s.42: permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with the additional-compensation routes in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter to the monthly-cost question?** Because certification is the value gate: without the original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — a price and timeline fact on every park purchase, whatever the pad rent is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is a co-op's monthly charge the same as pad rent?** No. Pioneer Park's charge is a co-op fee set by the co-operative, not MHPTA pad rent — the provincial increase formula does not set it. Confirm the current fee, share position and occupancy rule with the co-op in writing. (§2 above.)
