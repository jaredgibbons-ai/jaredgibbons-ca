## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (carried in full as the standing block; no pad purchase is documented in this band).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — every townhome in this band's documented form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park-home sale the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the **director** — never "arbitrator"), requested on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt; silence = deemed consent; NO fee for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fairly applied, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's research, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice, rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A manufactured-home sale is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; **no flat claim either way is stated as fact** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — this band's working block (strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. **Every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers** — the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise under $1.2M. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated:** at the band ceiling, $1,200,000 fully residential, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. At $800,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**.
