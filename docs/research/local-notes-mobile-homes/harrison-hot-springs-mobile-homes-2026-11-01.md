## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: Village of Harrison Hot Springs, BC — an incorporated resort village at the south end of Harrison Lake, in the Fraser Valley Regional District, surrounded by (but separate from) the District of Kent. Esplanade Avenue is the waterfront street facing the lake; Hot Springs Road is the main road in and out, running south to Agassiz. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/harrison-hot-springs.md)
- Board position: Harrison Hot Springs is a **CADREB (Chilliwack and District) board community** with its own MLS® area name; CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only, and **no Harrison row exists in SnapStats FVR reporting** — all Harrison market figures are UNVERIFIED from that source and nothing is substituted. (Research file §§1–2.)
- The village is small: 2021 Census population 1,905 (up from 1,468 in 2016), 885 occupied private dwellings, and seniors 65+ were 32% of the 2021 population. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-02/2025.02.19---VHHS-Housing-Needs-Report.pdf
- The hot springs are the village's reason for being: two spring types — Potash at 40°C and Sulphur at 62°C — are pumped and cooled to supply the public mineral pool and the resort pools; the public indoor pool, the Harrison Mineral Baths at Hot Springs Road and Esplanade Avenue, is owned and operated by the Harrison Hot Springs Resort, and no natural outdoor hot spring is open to the public. https://tourismharrison.com/explore/harrison-river-valley/our-region/our-hot-springs/
- Nearby areas: Agassiz (District of Kent) ~10 minutes south, Harrison Mills (Kent), Sts'ailes (Chehalis) to the northwest, Chilliwack via Highway 9. Everyday services — grocery, the arena/recreation complex and the secondary school — are in Agassiz. (Research file §§1, 4.)
- Transit: BC Transit Route 71 (Agassiz–Harrison) runs from downtown Chilliwack through Agassiz to Harrison Hot Springs (terminus at St. Alice St/Esplanade Ave), administered by the FVRD with BC Transit; service is limited — check schedules per trip. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html
- Schools (SD78 Fraser-Cascade): Harrison Hot Springs Elementary (K–6) at 501 Hot Springs Road (~100 students) is the village school; students then feed to Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (7–12) in Agassiz. Catchments are district-policy based — see §4. https://sd78.bc.ca/harrison/ and https://www.new.agassizsecondary.com/school-information/principal-s-message
- Flood frame (it governs lakeshore pads as much as houses): flood construction level **14.55 m** (Bylaw No. 1179, 2022); the 1:200-year lake flood elevation is 13.95 m; Council minutes (April 22, 2025) record the existing dike crest at 13.9 m, below the 1894 flood of record at 14.1 m and the 15.1 m crest provincial standards require. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Harrison-Hot-Springs-WTP-Master-Plan.pdf and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2025-05/2025.04.22---Regular-Council-Meeting-Minutes.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. For both Harrison grounds, the park's own rules were not opened in this program: UNCONFIRMED — obtain them with the tenancy agreement before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute a closure notice within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED); pad-resale PTT stays UNCONFIRMED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Harrison Hot Springs — the block governs this ground's strata diligence generally).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Village frame — OCP / zoning / servicing / flood.** OCP **Bylaw No. 1184, 2022** (consolidated to November 3, 2025) is the plan of record; it repealed OCP Bylaw No. 864, 2007 — any page or research line citing 864 as current is out of date. - **Short-term rental / resort frame (context for the hotel-strata record, not park law).** The Village is exempt from the provincial STR principal-residence rule as a designated resort region; since Dec 15, 2025, Business Licence Bylaw No. 1128 (amended by Bylaw No. 1231, 2025) requires a separate business licence for each dwelling unit operated as tourist accommodation, and accommodation in the designated area (Village + District of Kent + FVRD Electoral Area C) carries the 3% MRDT, with a repeal/renewal date of January 1, 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023hous0060-001598 and https://tourismharrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Tourism-Harrison-One-Year-Tactical-Plan-FINAL.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Harrison Hot Springs?** Two pad grounds are on record — Glencoe Motel and Trailer Park (259 Hot Springs Rd) and Harrison Holiday Park (973 Hot Springs Rd) — but only as **tenure records** on the FVRD's 2020 housing inventory; neither is verified on an operator or primary source in this program, and neither carries any stated age rule. Treat any fuller claim about either ground as UNCONFIRMED until the park's own tenancy agreement and rules are in hand. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home in one of those parks, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is either Harrison park a 55+ park?** No source checked states any age restriction for either ground — do not describe either as 55+. The village's wider 55+ position is the same: zero verified 55+ ownership stock of any kind inside the village (55-plus sibling §2).
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: A unit at the Harrison Beach Hotel is priced like a cheap condo — what am I buying?** A tenure record, not a home: a current listing states 1/3 fractional ownership with two weeks of use per year in a strata-hotel operation — not a residential strata lot and not mobile-home stock. (§2.)
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which schools serve a Harrison address?** Harrison Hot Springs Elementary (K–6) at 501 Hot Springs Road in the village, then Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (7–12) in Agassiz, both SD78, per the district's catchment policy as the research records it. No park-address locator run is on record, so a specific pad address's catchment is carried on that policy basis only. https://sd78.bc.ca/harrison/
