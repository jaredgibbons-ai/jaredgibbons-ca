## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**No roster — stated plainly at the top: this TOPIC/LAND file counts no park and no lot. Pad ground is NOT land, and Surrey's parks are documented in their home grounds. What follows is the boundary, stated once and precisely, plus the cross-references a land buyer comparing a pad purchase against a lot purchase needs.**

### Pad ground is not land — the boundary

- **A manufactured-home park pad is not a lot for sale.** In a park, the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — **a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership**, the pad does not transfer on title, no PTT land analysis applies to it as a parcel, and it is never counted in this topic's land market or in any lots-and-land roster in this program. A park appearing in a land search is a tenure mismatch, not a land offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The land forms of manufactured housing are different products, and they ARE land:** (a) a manufactured home on the buyer's own fee-simple lot — a land purchase on title, with the home also registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; and (b) a manufactured/modular home on a bare-land strata lot — a strata-lot purchase on title, with Form B diligence. Both forms' LAND side is documented ONCE in the lots-and-land home files; this file documents neither roster and re-counts neither. (Standing program discipline; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/surrey-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (the contrast case for this land file).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — every product on this page — is dealt with through the land title system instead. That single line is the whole tenure test for this topic: if the MHPTA applies, it is not land; if title transfers, it is not a pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (pad purchases only).** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply; on assignment the pad rent stays the same and the next increase date does not reset. No assignment step exists in a land purchase. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (pad purchases only).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules (pad purchases only).** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY (pad purchases only).** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file, and no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. A lot purchase carries no pad rent — its carrying costs are taxes, servicing and (for a bare-land strata lot) strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (pad tenancies only) — only as official sources support.** All necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, and may dispute within the 15-day dispute window; additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months'-pad-rent amounts only as the provincial pages state them. Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy adds the City layer for Surrey parks (12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after all permits, with an affordable housing program required on redevelopment), and Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw 21788 does NOT apply to MHPTA property. None of this applies to a lot the buyer owns. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — the step that survives a land purchase.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale (Z-240 for a mobile home, A-277 for a modular; a lost label must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection route). On an own-land purchase BOTH systems run: the land transfers on title and the home registers in the Registry — a buyer who closes only the title step has not closed the home. A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Registry and expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner — on the buyer's OWN lot, the land IS assessed to the buyer, which is the tax-side proof of the §2 boundary. Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — directly relevant to this land topic: placing a NEW manufactured home on a lot the buyer owns is the one manufactured-home PTT point the Province states. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — the rates that govern this page's actual product (land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential lot purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. - **Strata-form lots — BC strata block (governs a bare-land strata lot purchase, Sea Ridge Bays' form).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP / subdivision — Surrey land frame.** Zoning By-law No. 12000 divides the city into zones and regulates use, density, siting and subdivision; the consolidated bylaw text is the source for any zone's minimum subdivision lot area, and zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. A complete Surrey zone-minimum table: UNVERIFIED as a table in this file (individual zone minimums exist in the program's master research but were not re-extracted in this slug's pass). Added units are not subdivision: Surrey's SSMUH framework (adopted July 8, 2024; 20 single-family/duplex zones consolidated into 9) allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer — that permission does not create new lots and does not waive floodplain, riparian or servicing requirements. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a park pad on this land page a cheap lot?** No — a pad is not a lot at all. **You would own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad; that is not land ownership**, the pad never transfers on title, and it carries pad rent under the MHPTA instead of a purchase of land. If the goal is land, shop this page's fee-simple and bare-land strata lots; if the goal is the lowest-cost detached-form home, shop the parks through the surrey-mobile-homes topic file — and do not confuse the two budgets. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I buy a Surrey lot and place a manufactured home on it?** That is the own-land form: the lot transfers on title under Bylaw No. 12000 zoning (and ALC rules if the parcel is in the reserve — principal ≤500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence ≤90 m² / ≤186 m² by parcel size), and the home itself must be registered in the Manufactured Home Registry with its CSA label attached (Z-240 mobile / A-277 modular). The Province's newly built home PTT exemption expressly includes a manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land (not previously occupied). Confirm zoning, servicing and the exemption with the City and a lawyer/notary for the exact parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in manufactured-home terms?** A strata lot that is bare land with the home on it: you own the lot and the home, pay strata fees, are bound by the bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B — Sea Ridge Bays in South Surrey is Surrey's roster example. It is own-land stock, NOT a pad tenancy, and its land form is counted once in its lots home, not here. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me turn a park into lots, or subdivide by adding units?** No on both counts. SSMUH is a lot-zoning framework for eligible residential lots (3–4 units, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop) — it does not apply to pad tenancies, does not create new lots, and subdivision still runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000 (and ALC permission on reserve land). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How much PTT do I pay on a Surrey lot?** At the general rates, with inputs stated: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000 (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $500,000); $1,000,000 → $18,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. Exemptions change the figure parcel by parcel — confirm before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
