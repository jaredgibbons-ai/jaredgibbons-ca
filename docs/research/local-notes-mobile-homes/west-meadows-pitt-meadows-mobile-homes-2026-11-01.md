### Manufactured homes on West Meadows farm ground — the ALR positions only

- West Meadows is genuine farm ground: the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve, and this sub-area's large farm parcels are zoned A-1 General Agriculture / A-2 Large Lot Agricultural under regulations consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act. A specific property's ALR status is confirmed on Agricultural Land Commission maps — per-parcel, never assumed from the sub-area name. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Additional manufactured home on a farm: family occupancy only.** Under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s. 34(3) as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional residence that is a manufactured home and not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner (parent, grandparent or great-grandparent, sibling, or child, within the regulation's meaning). ALC Policy L-25 adds that a manufactured home permitted as an additional residence may only be occupied by the property owner or the owner's immediate family, and if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use. That is an occupancy rule in the opposite direction from a park: it restricts WHO may live in the home; it creates no tenancy, no pads and no park. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf
- **Outside those conditions, ALC permission is the route.** An additional residence that does not fit the permitted conditions needs ALC permission through a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, and the Commission must not grant it unless the residence is necessary for a farm use. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED at official-source level — no source in this program states one, and none is asserted in this file.** Any farm-worker housing proposal for a specific West Meadows parcel runs through that necessity test on that parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Own-land manufactured homes — the other form, stated plainly:** a manufactured home on land the buyer owns (fee-simple, or a bare-land strata lot where Form B / strata fees / registered bylaws apply) is a land purchase, not a pad tenancy. **No manufactured home on its own fee-simple lot, and no bare-land strata manufactured-home community, is verified by name inside West Meadows** in the research file or any sibling — the sub-area's documented stock is farm houses on A-1/A-2 parcels plus detached houses in the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent pocket at the east edge. Any West Meadows listing marketed as a manufactured home on owned land needs its title, tenure, ALR status and registry record checked individually; its land rules live in this ground's lots-and-land / acreages home files. (Research file §5; ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-acreages-2026-11-01.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **ALR RULES IN FULL — the governing frame for a manufactured home on West Meadows farm ground:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately; the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. The Pitt Meadows A-1 zone runs with this frame: one farm house per lot (maximum gross floor area 500 sq m), minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha (A-2: 16 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **Additional manufactured-home residence — the mobile-specific ALR rule (carried from §2 because it decides this page type on farm ground).** ALR Use Regulation s. 34(3) as summarised in ALC Policy L-25: a non-pre-existing manufactured home as an additional residence is permitted only at 9 m or less in width and only for occupancy by the owner or specified relatives; once it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use; anything outside those conditions needs a Non-Adhering Residential Use approval the Commission must not grant unless the residence is necessary for a farm use. A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. Pitt Meadows' own 2026 rates by class underline why the class matters: Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the Farm class (Class 9) general rate at $48.6824 per $1,000 with a $1.8524 drainage levy, versus $2.8438 general and $0.1082 drainage for residential (Class 1). https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in West Meadows or anywhere verified in Pitt Meadows; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the West Meadows farm form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. Whether a specific Meadow Highlands co-op occupancy is also an MHPTA pad tenancy is a document question for the co-op's agreements — UNCONFIRMED from the public sources in this program (see its Central Meadows home file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. For a co-op park like Meadow Highlands, the equivalent question is the co-op's housing-charge history — ask the co-op, not the RTB table. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — applies to a manufactured home on farm ground too.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file**. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land (including farm land, subject to the ALR rules above), not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / farm-land transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (apportionment on a mixed farm purchase is parcel work). Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (stated for completeness; on ALR land the strata form is barred outright, §3 ALR block; no bare-land strata park is verified in West Meadows).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in West Meadows?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park inside West Meadows, in any tenure form, and no pad-rental park is verified anywhere in Pitt Meadows. The city's one verified park is Meadow Highlands, a co-op in Central Meadows, cross-referenced in Section 2 to its home file only. (Section 2; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: In Meadow Highlands, do I own the land?** Not individually. Members own shares and equity in the co-operative that holds the land, with no lease (listings-stated) — you hold a co-op membership interest under the co-op's documents, not a fee-simple lot and not a rented pad. Read the co-op's membership, occupancy and share-transfer documents before comparing its price to either a pad-rental home or an own-land home. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Is Meadow Highlands a 55+ park?** No — affirmatively not. No source states any age restriction, and listings advertise an outdoor kids play area and school proximity. It is a family/all-age co-op park. The 55+ "Meadowlands" name nearby is a different co-op of built row-homes, not a park. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3095727-19630-pinyon-lane-77-pitt-meadows-bc-v3y-1z3/brochure
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a West Meadows farm for my parent, or for farm workers?** For a parent, possibly — the ALR Use Regulation permits an additional manufactured-home residence only within its conditions: 9 m or less in width, occupied only by the owner or specified relatives (the regulation's list includes a parent), and ALC policy requires the home to be demolished, removed or converted to non-residential use once it is no longer occupied as permitted. For farm workers, there is **no general entitlement in any source in this program — UNCONFIRMED**: the sourced route is a Non-Adhering Residential Use approval, granted only where the residence is necessary for a farm use, decided parcel by parcel by the ALC. Do not buy on the assumption worker housing in a manufactured home is permitted as of right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf
- **Q: Can I stratify the farm, or split the residence off?** No, on ALR land — residences in the ALR cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I buy a park home elsewhere in BC, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Plainly, a home on a rented pad is not land ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, in a rental park?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). For a co-op park, the equivalent question is the co-op's housing-charge history. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. A co-op purchase runs on the co-op's own membership and share-transfer documents instead — a different approval, read in Meadow Highlands' home file and the co-op's documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Can I run a farm stay or short-term rental from a home on a West Meadows parcel?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows' Zoning Bylaw, full stop. Do not price any parcel on STR income. (~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-acreages-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a West Meadows address?** Catchments are address-based in SD42 and no school building sits inside the sub-area. The one locator result documented in this slug's research is single-address only: 19452 Fraser Way returns Davie Jones Elementary → Pitt Meadows Secondary. Run every other address in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
