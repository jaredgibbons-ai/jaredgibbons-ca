## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — stated in full as the governing frame for any pad tenancy an East buyer considers elsewhere (the nearest documented ground is Tsawwassen Lands / Surrey / Langley):** the Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and renting both home and site falls under the Residential Tenancy Act, not the MHPTA. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** No such tenancy is verified in East. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment and consent:** buying in a park assigns the existing pad tenancy — the seller must first give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and any written park rules; assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day or consent is deemed given; no fee or benefit for consent), and the purchaser then holds the tenancy on the same terms. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Pad-rent increase caps — verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026), percentages ONLY:** once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at inflation (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) PLUS a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional**. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules and closure/conversion/compensation — only as official sources support:** park rules form part of the tenancy agreement, must apply fairly, be clear and properly noticed, and since April 30, 2024 can change only once a year on three months' notice on an RTB-approved form. Conversion of all or a significant part of a park requires all permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation payable on or before the notice's effective date, with early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice; added and bad-faith compensation positions are as the Province's June 6, 2018 bulletin states them. Finer points for any specific park: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home sale, transfer or purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry (BC Registries); the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a lawyer or notary, and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. Tax: the owner is assessed like other residential owners, excluding the land value where the home is in a park not on reserve land or on another's land; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Bare-land strata block — the block that actually governs Beachcomber Cottages:** Form B diligence (SPA s. 59: the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a strata established on/after that date follows the new-strata first-report timeline); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, incl. legacy-resident, caregiver and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID, with no conversion route. On a presale, REDMA comes first: disclosure statement (s. 18), 7-day rescission (s. 21), deposits in trust. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **PTT / transfer treatment — ONLY as official sources support:** the Newly Built Home Exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — the own-land case. PTT treatment of a resale manufactured home on a rented pad is NOT settled by any official source read in this ground's research: **UNCONFIRMED — flat site-copy claims either way are UNVERIFIED and none is asserted here.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Delta OCP / zoning / hazard frame for this ground:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, NO lettered schedules ("Schedule D.1 of 8400" is an ERROR wherever met, including in batch 1/2/7 siblings; farm land is "Agriculture (A)"). Zoning number CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted. Beach Grove waterfront: RD2 zone (stated in the bylaw to be for waterfront lots in Beach Grove), 4,000 m² subdivision minimum; s. 6.2.3 waterfront setback on Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue (a seawall may encroach; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level). **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) names Southlands expressly:** habitable floor no lower than 2.9 m GSC datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 m GSC datum, no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of a tidal area; lot-level SD3 status needs a DeltaMap check. - **ALR block (Southlands farm edge):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31). GST on a new Southlands home follows the new-housing frame in the next bullet; GST treatment of a specific manufactured-home transaction beyond the cited official positions: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Tsawwassen East or Beach Grove?** No. No pad-rental park or other rented-pad tenure was verified anywhere in this sub-area; the shore stock is freehold houses/duplexes and the new stock is Southlands' built collections. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is a Beachcomber Cottage a mobile home?** No source read says so. It is a detached cottage on a BARE-LAND STRATA lot in Southlands: you own the strata lot and the cottage, pay strata fees, and diligence is the Form B / disclosure statement — there is no pad, no pad rent and no MHPTA tenancy. Its land form is documented once in the East lots-and-land home file. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/
- **Q: What is the difference between bare-land strata and a rented pad?** Everything that matters: on a rented pad **you own only the home (a chattel) and rent the pad — that is not land ownership**; in a bare-land strata you own the lot itself in strata form, with Form B, fees and bylaws instead of a tenancy agreement and park rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: If I buy on a rented pad elsewhere, how much can the rent rise?** Once a year, on 3 full months' notice, by inflation plus the park's proportional amount: 2.3% + proportional for 2026, 2.2% + proportional for 2027 (RTB, verified Nov 1, 2026). Percentages only — no dollar rent is stated as fact here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What flood rules touch a Southlands or shore home?** SD3 names the Southlands and the Boundary Bay foreshore: 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor, 2.3 m land elevation around construction, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — behind the Boundary Bay dike Delta maintains as diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). Confirm the lot's SD3 position with the City. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Beachcomber Cottage?** As a strata question: long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws / disclosure statement for the collection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there any 55+ mobile or manufactured stock on the east side?** None verified — and no verified 55+ ownership stock of any form in East. Tsawwassen's verified 55+ buildings (The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene) are apartment condos in Central; The Wexford in East is rental only. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/tsawwassen-east-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: How is any manufactured-home purchase registered, if I find one on my own land?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, with the CSA label attached at sale. A home on your own land is otherwise dealt with through land title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a new Southlands home?** On a new developer sale, yes — with the Bill C-4 FTHB rebate (100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000) for eligible first-time buyers and the New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300) underneath; on a resale, no GST question arises. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Southlands / Beach Grove address?** Address-based: the sample (570 Boundary Bay Rd) maps to South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary in the SD37 locator. Run the exact address before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
