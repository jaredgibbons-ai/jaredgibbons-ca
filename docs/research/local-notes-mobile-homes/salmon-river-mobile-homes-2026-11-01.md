## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: Township of Langley, BC — Salmon River is the rural district along the Salmon River in the township's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley. The district follows the Salmon River valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue, with Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side; no Township-published boundary for the sub-area was confirmed. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md)
- MLS sub-area name as the board uses it: "Salmon River" — the name used in the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board data published in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. The name is the river's name: the Salmon River is a salmon-bearing watercourse, and Metro Vancouver watershed material covers the Salmon River watershed. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park)
- Nearby areas: Fort Langley to the north, Otter District to the south, Walnut Grove to the west, Aldergrove to the southeast. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Main roads: 56 Avenue and Rawlison Crescent on the upland, 232 Street and 240 Street as the north-south edges, and River Road / Glover Road toward Fort Langley; Highway 1 access is south via the 200 Street or 232 Street interchanges; there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Transit: TransLink route 562 (Walnut Grove / Langley Centre) runs on Glover Road via 96 Avenue, 88 Avenue and 202 Street to Carvolth Exchange, serving the Fort Langley edge of the district; no TransLink route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads inside the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Flood frame, stated up front because it governs valley-floor parcels: the Salmon River valley is Fraser River floodplain land — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was originally built to help mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and has flood box gates that close as river levels rise; Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for pump station upgrades. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. That distinction decides most Salmon River purchases: the district's documented manufactured home sits on its owner's own ALR parcel, so it is a land-title purchase, not an MHPTA pad tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. No park rule document for any park in this district was obtained in this program — UNCONFIRMED at park level for both §2 records. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This registry step applies to the home itself whether it sits in a park or on the buyer's own farm parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. A home on the owner's own parcel is assessed with that parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land purchases, including a Salmon River farm parcel).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (a $78,000 townhomes-sibling figure elsewhere is OPEN — follow $93,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Salmon River, and §2 of the farms topic file explains the ALR stratification bar).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR block — in full, because ALR applies to the valley farm land in places.** Confirm a specific parcel on the ALC maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status is parcel-specific, never assumed from the district. On ALR land: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. One additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws. A strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6), and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Township of Langley frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and the Township Official Community Plan / Rural Plan govern the district. The Township states its bylaw was updated for the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules (SSMUH Bylaw 6020: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², **NO 6-plex tier**), and that on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning; Township suite cap 120 m² in the standing frame. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and on ALR land the ALC housing rules in the bullet above layer over Township zoning. Servicing: Township Development and Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **Flood / water / septic — parcel diligence on this ground.** Parcel-level floodplain mapping and watercourse setback distances for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED in the research file — the Salmon River is salmon-bearing, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit building envelopes near the river; check with the Township and in GeoSource. Wells: search GWELLS for a parcel's record; well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well. Septic: Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/salmon-river-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. For Westfield Courts and Cedar Springs specifically, no park document was obtained in this program, so their consent practice is UNCONFIRMED until their own documents are in hand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is the valley floor safe to build or place a home on?** The valley is Fraser River floodplain land protected by the pump station and flood box gates, and the river is salmon-bearing, so setbacks can limit building envelopes. Parcel-level floodplain mapping and setback distances are UNCONFIRMED in the research file — check the specific parcel with the Township before planning a build or a placement. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Can an ALR acreage here add a second dwelling?** On ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met (up to 90 m² at 40 ha or less, up to 186 m² over 40 ha), subject to Township bylaws; the Township's small-scale multi-unit rules also allow a suite or accessory unit on lots over 4,050 m² depending on zoning. Whether that additional residence may be a manufactured home for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED on any source opened here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River address?** Catchments are address-based in SD35. One settled single-address chain: for the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown.
