## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Presale process — the rules this topic actually turns on.** Under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, a developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (s.15); a purchaser may rescind a purchase agreement by serving written notice on the developer within seven days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement (s.21). None of this applies to a manufactured-home purchase in an existing park, which is an assignment of an existing pad tenancy instead (§ below). https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the **director** — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. No "arbitrator" wording appears in this slug's research; the director position governs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. The live page's presale deposit figures are presale contract terms, not pad rent, and are carried UNVERIFIED in §2 only as the research carries them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT — newly built home exemption (the presale buyer's exemption, verified in this slug's research).** Effective April 1, 2024 the full-exemption threshold is a fair market value of $1,100,000, with a partial exemption phasing out over the $50,000 range above it and complete elimination at $1,150,000; the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000). **Sibling checks for this slug, stated explicitly:** a grep of this slug's OWN siblings (55-plus / condos / townhomes / lots-and-land / acreages) finds the PTT shorthand in all its forms ("3% to $3M, 5% above" / "Over $3M — 5%" / "2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above") ONLY inside flagging sentences — no sibling carries it as its own rate, so there is NO new carrier from this slug. The $78,000 figure appears in this slug's acreages sibling ONLY as the flagged other-slug figure (the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling, a different slug) — it is not met at a $3.5M input in this slug's own siblings, and $78,000 at a $3,200,000 input would in any case be CORRECT for that input and never flagged. **March 12 check:** no sibling for this slug states March 12 as its own Bill C-4 assent date — March 13, 2026 per CRA governs (below); this slug's own research itself states March 13, 2026 correctly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; a completed presale strata unit is ordinary strata stock governed by the same block as strata law, not as park law).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Langley frames (both municipalities, since this topic spans both).** Township of Langley: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) — 3 units at 280 m² or smaller, 4 units at 280–4,050 m², **NO 6-plex tier**; suite cap 120 m²; coach house fee-simple s.102; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area; servicing Bylaw No. 5382 CONFIRMED. City of Langley: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED), SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level (CRA GI-003 general level); anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. A presale purchase is new construction for GST purposes in the ordinary case — confirm the exact treatment, and any rebate, for the specific contract.
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate; a qualifying new presale home can be — eligibility turns on the buyer's and the home's status, confirmed on the CRA page. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Langley presale a way to buy a manufactured home?** No project documented in this slug's research is a manufactured-home park or community. A presale buys a new condo or townhome under REDMA; a manufactured-home purchase buys an existing (or newly placed) home plus either a pad tenancy or owned land, under the MHPTA / Manufactured Home Registry route. (§2 above.)
- **Q: Which Langley projects are selling now, and when do they complete?** UNVERIFIED in this file: the live page's browse section named no projects in the text read, and no developer source for a named Langley project was opened in the research pass — project names, pricing and completion dates must be confirmed project-by-project from developer sources and disclosure statements. The five projects named in §2 (Mirada Estates, Towers at Latimer Heights, Unity South Langley, Archer at Latimer, Kensington Gate) are named ONLY to warn that they complete OUTSIDE 2027–2029 — never present them as in-window. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
- **Q: How long can I take to cancel a presale after signing?** Seven days: under REDMA s.21 you may rescind by written notice to the developer within seven days after the later of the purchase agreement date and the date the developer obtained your receipt for the Disclosure Statement. There is no equivalent rescission right on an in-park manufactured-home assignment — that purchase turns on park-owner consent instead. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **Q: Do I pay property transfer tax on a Langley presale?** Often not on a principal residence: BC's Newly Built Home Exemption fully exempts a qualifying purchase at a fair market value of $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — and you do not need to be a first-time buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST back on a new Langley home?** Yes: under Bill C-4 (royal assent March 13, 2026, per CRA guidance) an eligible first-time buyer receives a rebate of 100% of the GST on a newly constructed home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with no rebate at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: If I buy in a Langley park instead, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific. Confirm financing for the specific home and its pad tenure before removing subjects.
