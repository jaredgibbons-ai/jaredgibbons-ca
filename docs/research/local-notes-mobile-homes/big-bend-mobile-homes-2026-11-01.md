## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Big Bend; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the Big Bend farm form in §2 — is dealt with through the land title system and the ALR framework instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This applies to a manufactured home on own farm ground as it does to a park home — the Registry record is part of the home-by-home check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. For a Big Bend farm parcel, farm-class assessment and tax treatment are a per-property BC Assessment / tax-notice check: UNVERIFIED here (see the ALR block below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (this district IS ALR farm ground, with an ALR/farm edge on its industrial ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); residence caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); farm class runs on the three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met). Manufactured homes on farm ground sit inside this frame: the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route and the Non-Adhering Residential Use route only, as §2 states; a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. ALR status and permitted farm / non-farm uses for a specific property are checked in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder — run the ALR check first on any farm parcel, and on any industrial parcel with an ALR edge, before pricing development or non-farm value into it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/big-bend-acreages-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Big Bend?** No — no manufactured-home park in Big Bend was found in any source checked, so there is no rented-pad stock, no pad rent and no park owner here at all. (Section 2; ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/big-bend-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Big Bend farm I buy?** Only through the ALR frame, not as of right: the documented routes are the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route and the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route, inside the ALR residence caps (principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — and a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. Run the ALC check for the specific parcel and home first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Big Bend farm or stratify its residences?** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Then verify the parcel's current district under Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026) in writing — agricultural-district rules were being updated through the rewrite. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What would I own if I bought in a pad-rental park elsewhere?** The home only — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad; the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. A Big Bend farm purchase is the opposite structure: fee-simple land under ALR rules, with no pad tenancy at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, in a park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). Big Bend has no pad rent — the comparison costs here are farm-class taxes, servicing and flood insurance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is the floodplain a real cost, or a formality?** Real. The district is a roughly 800-hectare Fraser River floodplain protected by dykes, pump stations and flood boxes, with flood hazard from late-spring/early-summer snowmelt and from combined heavy rain and winter storm surge. Confirm flood construction requirements, dyke position and insurance before subjects come off — including for any manufactured-home placement. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- **Q: Does farm class change what I pay to carry the land?** It can drive the carrying cost — and it is a per-property BC Assessment / tax-notice check, not a sub-area statistic. The farm-class thresholds themselves are three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Do I pay PTT and GST like a house purchase on a farm or industrial parcel?** No assumption is safe in either direction in this file: the commercial/farm/industrial PTT position for Big Bend ground is expressly UNCONFIRMED here, and GST is handled as the business-land fact pattern at CRA GI-003 level — business-held or business-sold land can be taxable, and no farm or industrial parcel is assumed exempt. Both belong in the buyer's per-parcel tax advice before writing; the worked examples in §3 are labelled residential-basis-only and price no farm or industrial parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. That holds for a manufactured home on own farm ground as it does for a park home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
