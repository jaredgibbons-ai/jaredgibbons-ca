## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act where a pad tenancy exists.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No English Bluff purchase in §2 is a pad tenancy on a verified source, so this block is the frame a buyer takes to a park elsewhere — it does not describe an English Bluff park, and it does not describe the Tsawwassen RV Resort, for which no manufactured-home tenure is established. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, consent and park rules (park frame).** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned (not newly granted): the seller must give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and written park rules before consent is requested; assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement and may be changed only with 3 full months' notice, and only if fair, clear, properly noticed and not changing a material term. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (BC all-items CPI, 12-month average change) plus a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** **No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file** — no live-page pad-rent range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is flagged or adopted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** Conversion of all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, requires all necessary permits and approvals in place before notice, 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, with the land's value excluded from the home's assessed value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly only that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — own-land placement, not a resale pad purchase. Confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure with a lawyer or notary before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for registered land transfers where PTT does apply (a fee-simple bluff lot).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 fully residential fee-simple purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form block — theoretical on this shore (no strata verified).** If a manufactured-home community were a bare-land strata, the BC block would be: Form B diligence (fee, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC — upland ownership does not include the beach. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
