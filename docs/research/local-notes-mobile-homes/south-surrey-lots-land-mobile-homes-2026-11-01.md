## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. That distinction is this whole topic file: land on this page is owned land; a park pad is not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On sale in a park, the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province's newly built home exemption point (a manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land, not previously occupied) is the land-side point for this topic — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own lot, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (land / own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors with inputs: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M fully residential → $93,000** (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 = $93,000). **FLAGGED in this slug's own sibling set: this slug's TOWNHOMES sibling carries the anchor "$3.5M purely residential → $78,000" and an "Over $3M — 5%" compression wording — read against its stated $3.5M input, $78,000 is wrong for that input; follow $93,000, and the "5%" wording is a compression of the 3% tier plus the further 2% tier, not a flat rate (see §5).** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies to Sea Ridge Bays as a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP / land rules — Surrey frame, with White Rock kept separate.** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — NEVER 18020 as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (confirmed current; the page's old-to-new zone mapping RF→R3 / RH→R1 / RQ→R2 / one-acre→RA and its RA minimum-lot figures — 4,050 m², 50 m wide, 60 m deep, Part 12 — were not re-read from the bylaw in the research and are site copy only, UNVERIFIED beyond site copy; any lot's zone is checkable on the City's COSMOS map); suite capped at 40% of the principal dwelling (the old 90 m² cap is NOT current); coach/garden suites 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots; SSMUH Jul 8, 2024 (inside the urban containment boundary, lots under 4,050 m²: 3 units at 280 m² or less, 4 on larger, up to 6 for some lots within 400 m of frequent bus service, per the page's copy as carried in the research); STRs permitted, owner-only from the principal residence, City licence $358/yr — with the $350/yr figure disclosed as a CONFLICT; servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** White Rock lots: Zoning Bylaw No. 2506, adopted Jun 27, 2024, incorporating SSMUH (up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop); the page's RS-1 minimum-lot dimensions (742 m², 18 m wide, 30.5 m deep) are site copy only (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **ALR frame (east-side acreage on this page).** One principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); parcel-level ALR status UNVERIFIED — confirm with the ALC (research file §4). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a park pad a "lot" I can buy from this page?** No. Pad ground is NOT land: in a pad park you own the home as a chattel and rent the pad. The lots on this page are owned land (fee-simple or, at Sea Ridge Bays, a bare-land strata lot). (§§2–3)
- **Q: Where is Sea Ridge Bays documented?** Its LAND once, in this slug's lots-and-land home; its park/homes side at home in the south-surrey-mobile-homes topic mobile file. This file cross-references both and counts it nowhere. (§2)
- **Q: Can I place a manufactured home on a vacant lot from this page?** That is an own-land purchase, not a pad tenancy — the home is placed and affixed on your parcel, registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, and the Province's newly built home exemption expressly contemplates that structure for a new, not-previously-occupied home. Zone, servicing and (on the east side) ALR status decide whether the specific lot works — all UNVERIFIED per parcel. (§§2–3)
- **Q: How many homes can go on a Surrey lot?** Inside the urban containment boundary, SSMUH counts run 3 / 4 / up to 6 by lot size and bus-stop proximity as carried in §3, and a suite (40% cap) or coach/garden suite (35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3) sits inside those counts; White Rock lots answer to Bylaw No. 2506 instead. Confirm the lot's zone in COSMOS first. (§3)
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a $3.5M lot?** $93,000 fully residential, including the further 2% tier (tier math in §3). This slug's townhomes sibling shows $78,000 for that input — flagged in §5, NOT followed.
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up (if I later buy in a park instead)?** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 — percentages only; no dollar rate is published as fact. (§3)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve a resale buyer?** In a pad park, yes — RTB Form 10 consent, 10-day rule, no fee. On owned land or a bare-land strata lot there is no pad assignment at all. (§3)
- **Q: What happens if a park closes or converts?** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (MHPTA s.42). (§3)
- **Q: Is "serviced" / "subdivision potential" on a land card reliable?** No — those are listing claims, UNVERIFIED against City records in the research; prove them with City servicing records, Fraser Health testing where a well/septic is involved, and the subdivision condition list before valuing the lot on them (research file §§5–6).
- **Q: Can I get financing on land or on a manufactured home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — confirm with a lender for the specific lot, home and tenure before removing subjects. (§3)
