## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Fairview; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — and, by the same boundary, a False Creek South home on City-leased land under a ground lease, which is a leasehold interest dealt with through its lease and title documents, not a pad tenancy — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — no Fairview research or sibling documents a pad figure, so none is flagged or carried here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. This MHPTA closure frame does NOT govern a False Creek South ground lease — a leasehold building's end-of-lease position is a lease-document question, UNCONFIRMED building by building in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file**. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure — pad, freehold, strata or City leasehold — before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. **Conforming flag, met in this ground's own townhomes sibling:** that sibling states PTT as "1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above" — that shorthand is WRONG against the provincial structure above, is never carried here, and is flagged as a conforming-pass item. The $78,000-for-$3.5M figure carried elsewhere in the program is OPEN — follow $93,000; that $78,000 figure itself is NOT met in this ground's siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/fairview-vw-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Fairview — this block is the diligence frame for Fairview's actual strata stock, including Terraces on 7th in its home files).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Fairview?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park in Fairview, in any tenure form. The tenure a buyer will actually meet here is City leasehold in False Creek South, plus freehold and strata forms. (Section 2; research file §§4–5.)
- **Q: Is False Creek South leasehold the same as renting a pad?** No. A pad tenancy is a Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act tenancy for a home you own on a rented site in a park. A False Creek South home sits on City-held land under a ground lease for the building — a leasehold interest with its own lease documents, remaining term and administration, and no park owner, pad rent or MHPTA assignment process. The lease terms for a specific building are UNVERIFIED here — read them before pricing. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
- **Q: What would I own if I bought a park home elsewhere?** On a rented pad, you own the home as a chattel and rent the pad — that is not land ownership. On your own land or a bare-land strata lot, you own land and diligence runs through title and, for strata, the Form B. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only; no pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file). A False Creek South leasehold has no pad rent — its land cost is inside the lease, not this formula. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. A leasehold or strata purchase in Fairview follows its lease/strata documents instead — read any consent or assignment clause in the specific building's lease. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Is Terraces on 7th a Fairview park or a Granville building?** Neither a park nor Granville stock: Terraces on 7th (1570 West 7th Avenue) is a built 55+ retirement community filed under Fairview ONLY in this program, counted in the fairview-vw condos sibling — never imported into granville/south-granville files, and never mobile stock. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How is a park-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped — and what is the False Creek South equivalent risk?** In a park: all permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. In False Creek South the equivalent risk is the ground lease itself — its remaining term and the City's active planning for the City-held lands — which is a lease-reading exercise, not an MHPTA notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx

For an own-land or strata purchase the general rates and anchors are in Section 3 (a $700,000 purchase = $12,000; $3.5M = $93,000 including the further tier); for a City-leasehold purchase, confirm the exact PTT treatment for that structure with a lawyer or notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fairview address?** Catchments are address-based in SD39 and no locator run for a Fairview address is documented in this file's research — and no public secondary school inside the area was confirmed at all — so the catchment is UNVERIFIED: run the exact address. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Tenure is the first question in False Creek South — before price.** Homes there sit on City-held land under ground leases, so the lease structure, remaining term and administration are priced before the unit is; leasehold does not comp against freehold product a few blocks south, and none of it is pad tenure despite the "you don't own the land" surface similarity. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south-leases-on-city-land.aspx
