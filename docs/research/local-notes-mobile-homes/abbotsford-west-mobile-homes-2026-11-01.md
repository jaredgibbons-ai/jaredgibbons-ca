## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; no fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB figure at that point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. **FLAGGED — THIS slug's own condos sibling carries the wrong flat shorthand AS ITS OWN RATE: "1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above"** (`~/workspace/local-notes/condos/abbotsford-west-condos-2026-11-01.md`) — that shorthand, its "Over $3M — 5%" compression and the flat variant "2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above" are all wrong as flat rates; the tier structure stated here governs, the sibling line is flagged as a conforming-pass item and is never followed or silently overwritten. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: In Paradise Park, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Paradise Park's full entry lives in the Abbotsford West GUIDE mobile file; this plain file cross-references it. (§2; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes)
- **Q: Is Downes Road Estates the same tenure as Paradise Park?** No — opposite forms. Downes Road Estates is bare-land strata: you own the strata lot (Form B, fees and bylaws apply). Paradise Park is pad rental: you own only the home. Do not compare their monthly costs or resale rules as if they were the same tenure. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Downes Road Estates 55+?** UNRESOLVED. Listings record 55+; one listing record markets "45+", which cannot be a valid BC age bylaw. Its count and year also conflict (52/1996 vs 51/1997). - **Q: Is Paradise Park really 55+?** Listings describe it as a 55+ park — AS listing-stated. A park's age rule lives in its park rules and tenancy documents, not in a strata bylaw; get the park's current rule in writing. (§2.)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no consent fee. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Eden Court mobile-home stock?** No. Eden Court (3348 Mt Lehman Rd, BCS2347) is built freehold strata townhouses, listing-described 55+ — documented + flagged in §2 and counted NOWHERE program-wide. (§2.)
- **Q: How is a park-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; the CSA label must be attached at point of sale. A missing label is a red flag for financing and resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if Paradise Park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park: all permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. Check the City's development applications for the park address before buying. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
