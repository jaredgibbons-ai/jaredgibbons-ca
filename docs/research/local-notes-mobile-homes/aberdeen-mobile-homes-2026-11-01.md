## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Aberdeen; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only manufactured form this ground supports — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and no live-page pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. For an own-land Aberdeen home, whether a Registry record exists at all is part of the home-by-home check — a site-built home on its own lot transfers on title alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file**. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — directly relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on the buyer's own Aberdeen parcel, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — this ground's own condos sibling carries a compressed tier line ("3% to $3M, 5% above" in effect) for a $3.5M-class purchase; that shorthand is WRONG as a tier statement, is flagged in §5 as a conforming-pass item in this slug's own siblings, and is never carried here: follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Aberdeen, and the form is barred on ALR ground per the ALR block below).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (Aberdeen is farm ground throughout).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — there is NO stratification of ALR residences, and ALC approval is required for subdivision. Residence caps: a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY within the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Abbotsford bylaws also permit it. The City's A1 zone mirrors the farm-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m² in the A1 zone text; the City's secondary-suite size rule is less than 50% of the size of the principal unit. Farm class (BC Assessment) runs in the three-tier form stated in the standing program record: $2,500 of farm value on 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 + 5% of the value over 4 ha, and $10,000 on parcels under 0.8 ha, with farm-class applications by October 31. Manufactured homes on farm ground are available only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route plus the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route; a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED — there is no standing right to place a manufactured home for workers outside those routes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Aberdeen, Abbotsford?** No — no manufactured-home park, age-restricted or otherwise, was found inside Aberdeen in any source checked, and the M1 guide file for this ground records the same honest zero. - **Q: Could a bare-land strata park be built here later?** Not out of ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified, subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval, and the zone minimums are 8.0 ha (A1) / 16.0 ha (A2). No live park proposal for Aberdeen is documented — UNCONFIRMED as a prospect. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I put a second manufactured home on my Aberdeen farm parcel?** Only within the ALR/A1 frame: a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional farm dwelling at 90 m² (40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha) on a lot of at least 0.4 ha — and it cannot be stratified or sold separately. A manufactured home for farm workers has no general entitlement: the route is ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 plus a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, with the general entitlement UNCONFIRMED. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in an Abbotsford park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). An Aberdeen acreage has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent.
