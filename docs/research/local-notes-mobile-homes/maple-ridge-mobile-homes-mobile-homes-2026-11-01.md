## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. On assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not reset. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator" — the topic research's information-sheet summary uses the older "arbitrator's order" wording; the settled provincial position carried here is the director. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. The registry covers both mobile homes (CSA Z-240) and modular homes (CSA A-277); a home without its original legible CSA label may not be sold until re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical-inspection route. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier** — the $78,000 figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling; it is not met in this slug's own siblings, reconciliation is OPEN, and $93,000 is followed everywhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Maple Ridge).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Maple Ridge frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 (RMH zone at s. 626). OCP citation CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) — flagged, resolved nowhere. Servicing: Subdivision Servicing Bylaw 4800-1993, from a City source only. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** REGISTRY LEDGER: Maple Ridge's Supreme Court registry position is UNCONFIRMED in the ledger — never blended with another city's.
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** The FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — a March 12 statement met anywhere is a conforming-pass item, never carried): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year — carried per this topic's research verification status. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age/foundation requirements) are UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects. The Alouette home file's "no financing permitted" line is listing-stated for that park only and is not a city-wide financing rule.
- **Property taxes.** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Maple Ridge parks can I actually buy into?** On the settled program record: Alouette Retirement MHP in East Central (pad rental, 55+ listing-stated) and Val Maria in Southwest Maple Ridge (leasehold pad rental, 55+ listing-stated) — each documented at home in its own ground's mobile file, cross-referenced in §2. Ruskin Place in the Northeast is a tenure record only — its form and age rule are unverified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Do I own the land under a park home?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — a home on a rented pad is NOT land ownership. Own-land manufactured stock (fee-simple lot, bare-land strata lot, or a co-op share at The Maples) is a different purchase entirely. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment the pad rent stays the same for the new tenant and the next scheduled increase date does not reset. Increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me?** Yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence is deemed consent, and no fee may be charged for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Are Maple Ridge parks 55+?** Only as each park's own documents state it. Alouette and Val Maria are 55+ AS listing-stated; Ruskin Place's age is UNCONFIRMED (secondary sources conflict — NOT verified 55+); The Maples is a co-op, not a park. The live topic page's broader "many parks are 55+" wording is site copy, not a verified park-by-park position. Get the specific park's written rules. (Section 2; research file §4.)
- **Q: What pad rent will I pay?** The figure in the specific site's tenancy agreement — no park rate is published as fact in this file. The topic research documents a pad-rent range in the live page's own copy; that range is UNVERIFIED site copy only (see §5) and is not a rate for any named park. The listing-stated figures carried in the home files ($713.31 at Alouette, $850 at Val Maria) belong to those listings, not to this topic file.
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** The sale is only effective once registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, and a home may not be sold unless its original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 mobile / A-277 modular); a lost label means Technical Safety BC re-certification before sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of a park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date (MHPTA s.42, §3). Search the City's development applications for the park's address before buying. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
