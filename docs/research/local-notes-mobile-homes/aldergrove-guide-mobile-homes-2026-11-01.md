## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block, carried in full — and its limit for this ground stated plainly:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs PAD tenancies — in a pad park the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and RENTS the pad; that is not land ownership, and **Pioneer Park is a co-operative, not an MHPTA pad park, so the MHPTA pad-rent caps do not set Pioneer Park's co-op fee.** For a true pad tenancy anywhere in BC: pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice. **Caps verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount — percentages ONLY; no pad-rent dollar figure is ever stated as fact in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion (RTB, pad parks):** conversion of all or a significant part of a pad park to another use requires all necessary permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice (Form RTB-31), and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route (assessed value minus the initial $20,000) where the RTB conditions are met. How a co-operative's own winding-up/closure rules compare is a co-op-documents question — UNCONFIRMED in this file; read Pioneer Park's co-op documents, not the MHPTA, for its closure position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home as chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** in BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains a central register of ownership details, controls the movement of manufactured homes, and improves the security of lenders financing manufactured-home purchases. A Pioneer Park purchase therefore has two records to diligence: the Registry record for the home, and the co-op's share/occupancy record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Co-op occupancy rules are the co-op's own:** listings state buyer acceptance by the co-op and a share purchase as conditions of buying into Pioneer Park; the provincial strata exemptions (spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy, defined children — SPA Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) govern STRATA 55+ bylaws and do not automatically apply inside a co-operative. Current share price, monthly fee, pet and occupancy rules: confirm with the co-op in writing — the figures in §2 are listing-stated only. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Strata block (applies to Creekside Villa-type strata purchases in this community, and to any bare-land strata park a buyer cross-shops):** Form B diligence (strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum CRF 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+). An older Creekside Villa listing's "45+ rancher" line is exactly that void form — MLS data for the same complex records "Restricted Age: 55+." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/27435-29a-avenue/29
- **Township frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs; Section 110.1 ladder as read in Township council reports — R-1B/R-1C 464.5 m², R-1D 650 m², R-1E 930 m², SR-3 1,765 m², SR-1 3,716 m², SR-2 8,094 m² minimum lot areas. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier); suite cap 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and it creates no added-unit right inside a co-op park — Pioneer Park's form is governed by its co-op documents and its zoning, not SSMUH. The Aldergrove Community Plan and Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community within the Township OCP. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Servicing — ledger governs over the sibling:** the Township servicing bylaw is **Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, CONFIRMED** in the program's verified ledger. The aldergrove-GUIDE lots-and-land sibling holds Township servicing for Aldergrove parcels as UNCONFIRMED — that sibling position is flagged here: the 5382 ledger governs for the bylaw's identity, while servicing (water/sewer vs well/septic) for any single parcel remains a parcel-level confirmation with the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/aldergrove-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §§2–3)
- **ALR:** much of the Township's land is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; the Township directs applicants to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource before applying. Where a parcel IS in the ALR: subdivision requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps — principal 500 m² or less, suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). Farm class runs on the full three-tier test ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT:** for an own-land fee-simple purchase in Aldergrove, general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. The PTT treatment of a chattel home / co-op share purchase is a different question and is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no flat pad-PTT claim is carried as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the level the CRA sources state:** CRA contemplates a new or substantially renovated mobile home purchased from a builder (including the manufacturer or vendor) as new housing for the GST/HST New Housing Rebate, and CRA policy material treats a previously occupied residential unit that is a residential complex under the exempting provisions — carried only as that used-generally-exempt / new-taxable framing; a specific Pioneer Park resale's treatment is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-home-construction/new-housing-rebate.html
- **First-home programs:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — never March 12) applies to new HOUSING: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What manufactured homes can I actually buy in Aldergrove?** One verified community: Pioneer Park (27111 0 Ave), a 55+ manufactured-home co-operative. No pad-rental park was verified in Aldergrove, and Langley Grove Estates is Otter District ground, not this community. (§2.)
- **Q: How is Pioneer Park different from a normal manufactured-home park?** Tenure. A pad park rents you a site under the MHPTA; Pioneer Park is a co-operative — listings state you must be accepted by the co-op, purchase co-op shares ($9,200 in one listing, refunded upon sale), and pay a monthly co-op fee ($510 in the same listing) instead of pad rent. All figures listing-stated; confirm current figures with the co-op. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 at Pioneer Park?** UNRESOLVED on the listings: one states "Strictly 55plus (both occupants must be 55)," another states only that the park is 55+ with occupancy limited to 2 occupants who are 55+. A co-op sets its own rules and the strata exemptions do not automatically apply — get the co-op's current rule in writing before offering. (§2.)
- **Q: Does the pad-rent cap protect me at Pioneer Park?** No — the MHPTA caps (2.3% for 2026, 2.2% for 2027, plus the proportional amount) govern pad tenancies, and a co-op fee is not pad rent. Fee changes at Pioneer Park are a co-op-documents question: UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What happens on resale in a co-op park?** Two steps the pad/strata routes do not have in this form: the incoming buyer must be accepted by the co-op and purchase the shares (listing-stated as refunded to the seller upon sale), and the home's transfer must be registered in the Manufactured Home Registry to be effective. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I finance a Pioneer Park home like a house?** It is a chattel purchase plus co-op shares, with no fee-simple land collateral; the Registry exists in part to improve lender security on manufactured homes, but lender eligibility for a co-op park home specifically is UNCONFIRMED in this file — confirm with the lender and the co-op before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Pioneer Park?** Listings state 2 pets allowed with no size restrictions — listing-stated; confirm the current co-op rule for the specific home. https://murphyrealestategroup.ca/recip.html/1272399.search/SearchResults.form?_pg=3
- **Q: Can I add a suite or second unit to a rural Aldergrove manufactured home on my own lot?** On an own lot the Township frame applies: suite cap 120 m², coach house as a fee-simple second unit under s.102 (never strata), SSMUH Bylaw 6020 with no 6-plex tier — and on ALR land the ALC caps replace them (principal 500 m², suite inside only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m²). SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies or inside the co-op park. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which schools serve Pioneer Park?** UNCONFIRMED — no SD35 locator run for 27111 0 Ave is documented. The one settled Aldergrove chain (26850 29 Avenue → Parkside Centennial → Betty Gilbert → Aldergrove Community Secondary) is single-address only; run the park address in the SD35 School Locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: Is there care nearby if I buy at Pioneer Park for the long term?** Jackman Manor (27477 28 Ave) is an 88-bed long-term care home in Aldergrove, operated by the Aldergrove Lions Seniors Housing Society, accessed through Fraser Health — not purchased. https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/85553/
