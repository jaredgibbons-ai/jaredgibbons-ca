## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (the background a park-home buyer brings to any sale, court-ordered or not).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. Whether and how that assignment machinery runs inside a specific court-ordered sale is file-specific — UNCONFIRMED as a general court-sale rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** In an ordinary park sale, assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator" — no "arbitrator" wording is met in this slug's research or siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. How a court vesting order interacts with that registry record for a specific home is file-specific — confirm with the lawyer handling the file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; the Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **The court process — confirmed core (this slug's research, verbatim on BC Laws).** A foreclosure proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Rule 21-7 (1). All persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be made respondents — Rule 21-7 (2); a petitioner may also join a claim arising out of the mortgage and a person liable for the mortgage debt — Rule 21-7 (3). A person who registers an interest after a certificate of pending litigation is registered need not be served, is bound by orders made, and may file a response — Rule 21-7 (4). The petitioner must serve the Form 125 informational notice with the petition — Rule 21-7 (4.1). The court may make a final order of foreclosure, order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes (foreclosure of the equity of redemption in default), or determine / have certified the amount due — Rule 21-7 (5)(a)–(c). The Rule text states no fixed redemption-period length. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03 (research file §4)
- **The court process — settled program treatment carried, research's narrower stance disclosed.** The settled treatment for this program carries the wider frame — Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66 and Form 60 — for conduct-of-sale, court-approval and sale-confirmation steps. - **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, and no source opened settles a court-sale-specific PTT or GST rule; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption (research file §4)
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. No $78,000-at-$3.5M figure is met in this slug's siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. **Form B treatment on a court-ordered sale specifically: UNCONFIRMED** (see the court-process item above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH.** This is a regional topic file with no single-city frame — no municipal zoning position is carried. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** in any municipality.
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level. **GST treatment for a court-ordered purchase specifically: UNCONFIRMED** in this slug's research. (Research file §4.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. In this slug's siblings, March 12 appears ONLY as a superseded mention tied to the condos sibling (March 13 governs) — NOT as a sibling's own assent date, so no March-12 carrier flag is raised here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can a mobile home in a park be sold through a court-ordered sale?** A Rule 21-7 proceeding is about mortgaged property generally, with no property-type limit in the confirmed text — but how a chattel home, its registry record and its pad tenancy are handled in a specific file is file-specific and UNCONFIRMED as a general rule here. Get the file's own documents through a lawyer before assuming the ordinary park-sale steps apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court under Rule 21-7 (1), with everyone whose interest is sought to be extinguished made a respondent, and the Form 125 information notice served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the confirmed Rule text — it is the period the court fixes in its order under Rule 21-7 (5)(b). (Research file §7.)
- **Q: Can someone outbid my offer at the approval hearing?** UNVERIFIED as a stated process rule in this slug's research — the bid-rights wording in Rule 13-5 and PD-66 was held UNVERIFIED there, and the live page's courthouse competing-offer statement is the page's own account, not adopted here. Plan for the possibility and get the specific file's bid instructions in writing from the seller's counsel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/foreclosures.html (research file §7)
- **Q: Do I still need park-owner consent if I buy a park home through a court sale?** UNCONFIRMED as a general court-sale rule — the ordinary assignment route needs prior written consent on RTB Form 10 (10-day rule, §3), but whether a court order changes that for a specific file is a question for the lawyer on that file. Do not assume either way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf

The tenancy position inside a specific court file is file-specific — confirm it from that file's documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
