### Farm ground — the only route for a manufactured home here (full ALR block)

- Ladner Rural's inland ground is fee-simple ALR farm parcels under the Agriculture (A) designation and, where the zone applies, A1 zoning. A manufactured home on farm ground is NOT park stock and is NOT a general entitlement: it can appear only through the ALR routes — the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route and the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED.** Parcel ALR status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (plain ladner-rural mobile file on disk; settled ALR block, M3 brief)
- A1 residential limits (the conventional farm-house frame a manufactured-home proposal is read against): one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate); one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot, maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate, with ALC approval required for more than one; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report and written ALC approval. Accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Subdivision of farm land: in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (stated for completeness; no pad tenancy is verified on this ground).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact; both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Ladner Rural).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

Flood protection: the City operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure — dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection. Parcel-level flood construction levels: UNVERIFIED for any Ladner Rural parcel. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level (CRA GI-003 general level); anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established.
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Ladner Rural?** None verified — and none anywhere inside the City of Delta in this program. Ladner Rural's stock is ALR farm parcels plus the West River Road float-home pocket; neither is a park. (Research file §§4–5; plain ladner-rural mobile file on disk.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Ladner Rural farm?** Only through the ALR routes — ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25, or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application. There is no general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement: UNCONFIRMED. Check the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Are the Canoe Pass float homes a mobile home park?** No. They are float homes in a marina setting per their listing texts; their tenure, moorage and legal structure are UNVERIFIED, and listing text alone does not establish tenure. Diligence what is actually owned (the float, the slip, any lease or strata interest) before the lifestyle. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural-guide.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Ladner Rural?** Under the A1 zone: one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a Ladner Rural farm have a second dwelling?** Within limits: one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house; more than one needs ALC approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: If I buy in a park elsewhere, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which schools serve a Ladner Rural address?** Address-level catchments are UNVERIFIED — no locator result was run for any rural address in this pass. SD37 publishes Ladner Area catchment maps (updated October 2017) and directs families to the Baragar School Locator or their local school. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
