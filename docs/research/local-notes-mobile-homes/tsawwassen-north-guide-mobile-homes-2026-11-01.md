## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that decides everything on this ground:** Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (724+ hectares, Final Agreement); City of Delta bylaws and zoning do not apply there. Every rule below is the PROVINCIAL/CITY frame; **whether and how each applies to a site or home on Tsawwassen Lands is UNVERIFIED per property — the TFN-law position and the site's own tenancy/lease documents govern, and they must be read for the specific site.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the provincial pad-tenancy frame, stated in full:** the Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and renting both home and site falls under the Residential Tenancy Act. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** Application of this Act to a site on Tsawwassen Lands: UNVERIFIED (jurisdiction note above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment and consent (provincial frame):** a park purchase assigns the existing pad tenancy; the seller must first give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and any written park rules; assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day or consent deemed given; no fee or benefit for consent), and the purchaser then holds the tenancy on the same terms. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Pad-rent increase caps — verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026), percentages ONLY:** once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at inflation (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) PLUS a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional**. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file** — including for the Section 2 resort, whose directory rent range is directory-stated only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules and closure/conversion/compensation — only as official sources support:** park rules form part of the tenancy agreement, must apply fairly, be clear and properly noticed, and since April 30, 2024 can change only once a year on three months' notice on an RTB-approved form. Conversion of all or a significant part of a park requires all permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice; added and bad-faith positions are as the Province's June 6, 2018 bulletin states them. Finer points for any specific park: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Age rules in parks:** the Province's general rule for a designated 55-plus park is that at least one tenant on the site must be 55 or older — a park designation in the park's own documents, not a strata bylaw. The Section 2 resort is documented all-ages, so no age rule is carried for it; age rules are carried in this program ONLY from operator/Form B/registered-bylaw sources, listing-stated ages stay labelled, and conflicts stay UNRESOLVED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home sale, transfer or purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry (BC Registries); the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a lawyer or notary, and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. Tax: the owner is assessed like other residential owners, excluding the land value where the home is in a park not on reserve land or on another's land; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant. How registry/tax administration runs for a specific Tsawwassen Lands home: confirm for that property — UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Bare-land strata block (City-side strata frame; no bare-land strata park is in North):** Form B (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, incl. the May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID, no conversion route. - **Q: If I own a home on a rented site, do I own land?** No. **A home on a rented pad/site is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the site.** Own-land forms are different: fee-simple title, a bare-land strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws), or co-op shares. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the BC pad-rent cap protect me on Tsawwassen Lands?** UNVERIFIED. The provincial caps (2.3% + proportional for 2026; 2.2% + proportional for 2027, once a year on 3 months' notice) are the MHPTA frame on provincial ground; whether that framework applies to a site on Tsawwassen Lands is not established in the sources read — TFN law governs its own lands, so read the site's tenancy document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What is the difference between the RV resort sites and the Boardwalk/Seaside homes nearby?** Tenure and form: the resort is an RV resort with (per a directory) land-lease home sites — tenure record only; Boardwalk and Seaside are built townhouse communities held under documented TFN leasehold structures (99-year prepaid lease; leasehold prepaid-strata EPS4960/EPS5770). Neither is fee-simple, and neither is counted as the other. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/tsawwassen-north-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse an assignment where the MHPTA applies?** Consent is required, requested on RTB Form 10 — the owner must respond within 10 days, silence is consent deemed given, and no fee can be charged. The seller must hand you the written tenancy agreement and park rules first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park closes where the MHPTA applies?** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How would a manufactured-home sale be registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, CSA label attached at sale. For a Tsawwassen Lands home, confirm the registry/tenure handling for that property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is there any park at all on the Delta city side of North?** None found — City-side stock is detached houses, Springs condos, and a townhouse pipeline project at 4800 Springs Boulevard (QUEUE, counted nowhere, and townhouse form, not mobile). (Section 2.)
