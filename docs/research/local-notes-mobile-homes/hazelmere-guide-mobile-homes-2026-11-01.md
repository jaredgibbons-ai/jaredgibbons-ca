## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Hazelmere; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified from the RTB: **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and none is documented in this slug's research — Hazelmere has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply as the Act states, with a 15-day dispute window. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact; both flat page claims met in the program are UNVERIFIED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. **Grep-checks stated explicitly for this slug:** the PTT shorthand (all forms) appears in this slug's siblings ONLY inside flagging sentences (the townhomes guide sibling flags an "Over $3M — 5%" row as a compression, documenting no such row in this slug's research) — NOT a new carrier; the $3.5M/$78,000 error is NOT met as this slug's own sibling rate (sibling hits are flagging sentences naming a south-surrey townhomes sibling and following $93,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Hazelmere).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH / ALR — Surrey frame for this ground.** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. The plain page states A-1 General Agriculture zoning for Hazelmere Valley acreages, Part 10 minimum lot areas of 4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it for new A-1 lots, one single-family dwelling which may contain one secondary suite under A-1, and RA Acreage Residential zoning on some nearby land with a 4,050 m² minimum lot size — the RA figure is consistent with the City's SSMUH zone table (RA lots at over 4,050 m²), but the A-1 / Part 10 figures themselves were not re-read in Bylaw 12000 in the guide research pass: UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text. Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: Surrey Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED in the ledger; well/septic servicing is parcel-specific in any case (one guide feed listing is described in its own text as on city sewer and water). ALR parcels follow the ALC frame: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² without ALC application; one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a secondary suite only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict through zoning. A manufactured home on farm ground is possible only via ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 plus the Non-Adhering Residential Use route, and a general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. A separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel (the plain page states a separate coach house is not confirmed on a City source; guide feed listings here include coach-house product, e.g. at 2130 179 Street — verify the unit's status with the City and, where in the ALR, the ALC frame). Whether any specific Hazelmere parcel — or what share of the sub-area — is inside the ALR: UNVERIFIED in this file; ALR status is parcel-specific in the ALC finder. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **GST — only at cited-CRA level (GI-003 general level).** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Hazelmere?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park inside Hazelmere, so there is no rented-pad stock here at all. The stock is fee-simple acreage and estate product, and the guide 55-plus sibling records the same zero for 55+ housing of any kind on this ground. (§2; research file §§2, 5.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Hazelmere acreage?** Only through the routes the sources actually open. Off the ALR, on your own lot, it is a land purchase with Manufactured Home Registry registration and a CSA label. In the ALR, only as the ALC sources support — ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 plus the Non-Adhering Residential Use route, inside the ALC residence caps — and a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. Check the parcel's ALR status first; no Hazelmere parcel result is on record. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself in the ALC finder; no sub-area share is documented. If it is in: the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only where local bylaws allow it, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with local zoning able to be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Hazelmere acreage add the usual Surrey SSMUH units?** Generally not on the standard framing: Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer. Hazelmere's acreage lots need a parcel-level check of zone, containment boundary and servicing before any added-unit claim. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: A Hazelmere listing shows a detached coach house — is it lawful product?** Verify it, do not assume it. On ALR land an additional residence must fit the ALC size limits; on A-1 land the plain page itself flags a separate coach house as unconfirmed on a City source. Confirm the unit's status with the City and, where the parcel is in the ALR, the ALC frame before marketing or paying for it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere.html
- **Q: What would I own in a pad-rental park elsewhere in Surrey?** The home (a chattel) only — **you rent the pad and do not own the land**; the existing pad tenancy is assigned to you under the MHPTA, and the transfer is effective only once registered in the Manufactured Home Registry. Hazelmere has no such stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a Surrey park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). Hazelmere has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which school is physically in Hazelmere, and will my kids attend it?** East Kensington Elementary, 2795 184 St, is confirmed by name and address inside Hazelmere, with Xw'epiteng Elementary (18035 8 Ave) and Douglas Elementary (17325 2 Ave) also confirmed nearby — but catchments are address-based in SD36, the guide page states one rural area can be split between several catchments, and no Hazelmere locator result is on record, so catchment schools for a specific address are UNVERIFIED; run the exact address before relying on any school.
