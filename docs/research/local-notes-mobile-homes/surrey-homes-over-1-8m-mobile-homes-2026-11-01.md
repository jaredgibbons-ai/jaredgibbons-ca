## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **PTT at this band — the rule that bites hardest, with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (only the residential portion on a mixed-class property; where land is classed as farm only because it is used for an owner's or farmer's dwelling, up to 0.5 hectares is treated as residential property). PTT is based on fair market value on the day of registration at the Land Title Office and is not annual property tax. Worked, fully residential, no exemption: **$1,800,000** (the band floor) → 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**; **$2,000,000** → $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**; **$3,500,000** → $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. Lower-price anchors for scale: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000. **THE $78K FLAG:** a $78,000 PTT figure for a $3.5M purchase is carried in the townhomes program for this band family — it is an **OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000**, which is what the provincial tiers produce. (Disk note, stated plainly: this slug's own townhomes sibling, as read on disk for this file, states the tiers and reaches no $3.5M worked total, so the $78,000 is flagged here at program level and is never quoted as that sibling's line.) The shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" met elsewhere in the program is **WRONG against the provincial structure** — the tiers are 3% above $2M plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — and it is never carried in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band.** The first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, so buyers in this band budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Home owner grant phase-out, directly relevant at this band.** The grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the residential or partitioned value is over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none, even as principal residences. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Additional PTT.** A foreign national, foreign corporation or taxable trustee must also pay additional property transfer tax on the fair market value of the residential portion of a property within a specified area of BC; the rate is published on the Province's separate additional-PTT page, which was not re-read for this file, so **the rate is UNVERIFIED here**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **MHPTA — the governing Act for any park home, at any price.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** The band's price frame changes none of this for a pad purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's M1 files state them. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year — subject, at this band, to the phase-out above where the assessed value reaches it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the PTT treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file; pad-resale PTT is UNCONFIRMED**. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata — the Sea Ridge Bays form).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
