## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**TOPIC FILE — NO ROSTER, stated plainly at the top. No manufactured-home park is verified on Chilliwack farm ground, and no park of any tenure form is counted in this file. The mobile-home position for this topic is a FORM position: what a manufactured home legally is when it sits on Chilliwack farm land.**

- **The farm-ground form, stated plainly:** farm parcels in this topic are overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source), and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any case (§3). A manufactured home on a Chilliwack farm parcel is therefore **own-land stock**: the buyer owns the land in fee simple and the home on it, the transfer runs on title (with the home's Manufactured Home Registry position checked — §3), and the residence rules are the ALC caps, not park rules. **It is NOT pad stock: there is no pad, no pad rent, no park owner and no MHPTA tenancy in this form.** The land rules live in this slug's lots-and-land and acreages home files; this file documents the home-form position only. (Research file §5; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/chilliwack-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Farm types in the topic (from the official material):** valley-floor farms under the AL and AS agricultural zones, upland farms under AU, farm-residential parcels under AR, and agricultural commercial and food-processing operations under AC and AFP — the six Section 6 zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. Land base: agriculture is the city's dominant land use at 67% of the total land base on the City's own statement, governed by the Agricultural Area Plan inside the OCP and by ALC rules where a parcel is in the ALR. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Section 6 minimum lot areas (the bylaw's minimum LOT AREA for a newly created lot), carried from the research:** AL and AU — 7.5 ha where land is excluded or exempted from the ALR or a subdivision is approved within the ALR, 1 ha if created by boundary adjustment to allow the more efficient use of agricultural land, and 0.2 ha if created by homesite severance or a boundary adjustment approved by the ALC (ss. 6.01(2), 6.02(2)); AS — 2 ha where a subdivision is approved in the ALR, 1 ha by boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha by an ALC-approved boundary adjustment or homesite severance, or by subdivision of ALCA-exempt land served by a community water system (s. 6.03(2)); AR — 0.2 ha where served by a community water system, 1 ha for all other uses (s. 6.04(2)); AC — 2 ha (excluded/exempted or approved subdivision), 1 ha by boundary adjustment served by a community water system, and 0.2 ha by ALC-approved homesite severance (s. 6.05(2)); AFP — 0.4 ha for all uses (s. 6.06(2)). Minimum lot width is 25 m in all six zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Farm Home Plate figures inside Section 6, carried from the research:** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate is capped, as maximum lot coverage, at 4,000 m² for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use (ss. 6.01(5), 6.04(5), 6.05(5)); the dwelling must be sited no farther than 70 m from the front or exterior side lot line with no part of the plate more than 80 m from that line, or no farther than 50 m from the rear lot line with the plate within 60 m (ss. 6.01(8), 6.04(8), 6.05(8)); and the Farm Home Plate requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha in area (ss. 6.01(10), 6.04(10), 6.05(10)). The AU, AS and AFP sections state no Farm Home Plate figure. A Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met (OCP Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists on this topic's farm ground; carried in full as the standing block, because a farm buyer cross-shops Chilliwack's urban-side parks).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the farm form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** **Cleaner discipline, stated expressly: NO pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file, and no pad range is quoted at all — a pad figure met in a sibling for a non-farm park is listing copy for that park's homes, is not documented in this slug's own research, and is therefore not mentioned here beyond this non-documentation statement in §5.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — the rule that matters for a farm-lot manufactured home.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. Whether a specific farm-lot manufactured home remains Registry-registered or has been de-registered and affixed to its fee-simple land is UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and title records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **FULL ALR BLOCK (this topic IS farm ground — carried in full).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; the City likewise states an ALC application may be required for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, a new house, coach house or suite, placing fill or removing soil, and non-farm uses. **ALR residences cannot be stratified** — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, built without an ALC application; a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. An additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Chilliwack bylaws must also permit it (with the City's Farm Home Plate caps in §2 sitting on top). Farm classification (BC Assessment), full three-tier form: a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 for the following taxation year; a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error wherever met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — only as ALC sources support (the poultry-farms M2 treatment is the model).** Under the ALR Use Regulation's additional-residence provisions (s.34(3), as summarised in ALC Policy L-25), an additional residence that is a manufactured home and not a pre-existing structure is permitted only if it is 9 m or less in width and is used only by the owner or specified relatives of the owner (parent, grandparent or great-grandparent, sibling, or child, within the regulation's meaning); a manufactured home permitted as an additional residence may only be occupied as permitted, and if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use. Outside those conditions, ALC permission is the route: a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, which the Commission must not grant unless the residence is necessary for a farm use. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED — no source supports one, and none is stated here.** Temporary farm worker housing is permitted under conditions on ALR land per the ALC housing page — carried at exactly that general level, with no manufactured-form entitlement read into it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (~/workspace/local-notes/mobile-homes/poultry-farms-mobile-homes-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on Chilliwack farm land?** No — none was verified on any source, and the form is structurally unavailable: farm parcels are freehold, strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Chilliwack's verified 55+ park (Cottonwood Village, 7610 Evans Rd) is urban-side, is documented in the West Chilliwack guide mobile file, and carries open placement and lease conflicts — it is not farm stock. (§2.)
- **Q: I saw a manufactured home on a Chilliwack farm — what am I buying?** Own-land stock: a freehold farm parcel with a manufactured-form home on it. You own the land; the home-form questions are its Manufactured Home Registry position (still registered, or de-registered and affixed — UNCONFIRMED until the records are read) and whether the residence fits the ALC caps and the City's Farm Home Plate rules. There is no pad, no park owner and no MHPTA tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on the farm for my parent, or for farm workers?** For family: possibly, within the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 conditions — an additional manufactured-home residence 9 m or less in width, occupied only by the owner or specified relatives; when that occupancy ends, the home must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use. For workers: no general entitlement is supported — beyond the family conditions, the only sourced route is a Non-Adhering Residential Use approval, which the ALC must not grant unless the residence is necessary for a farm use, decided parcel by parcel. Anything broader is UNCONFIRMED. (§3.)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Chilliwack?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a suite is permitted only inside that principal residence; and Chilliwack also applies its Farm Home Plate rules (Development Permit Area 4 — plate caps of 4,000 m² principal / 1,000 m² additional in the AL, AR and AC zones, §2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Chilliwack farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; the City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application. If the additional residence is a manufactured home, the s.34(3) / L-25 width and family-occupancy conditions in §3 apply on top. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide the farm home off later?** Stratify: no — ALR residences cannot be stratified (a strata plan is a subdivision; ALCA s.21(1)). Subdivide: only within the Section 6 minimums for the parcel's zone (§2) and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. Price the parcel as one parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers for Chilliwack farms?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market. The SnapStats reports held for this program cover the Fraser Valley and Greater Vancouver boards only, publish no farm category, and contain no Chilliwack figures — CADREB publishes no area stats in this program, so any Chilliwack or farm-specific statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. Price from the parcel's own comparables. (Research file §§2, 7.)
- **Q: What protects the farm's tax treatment after I buy?** The farm classification itself: the operation must keep meeting BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income for its size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha, scaling above 4 ha, $10,000 under 0.8 ha), and a new owner continuing to farm must submit the farm classification application by October 31. If the plan can't meet the threshold, the classification — and its tax treatment — is at risk. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: If I buy in a Chilliwack park instead, how does pad rent work?** You own the home and rent the pad under the MHPTA; pad rent rises at most once every 12 months on three full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only — this file quotes no pad dollar figure for any park). Assignment needs the park owner's consent on RTB Form 10, response by day 10, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
