## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home is assessed/taxed like other residential property; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; the Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact, and both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000 (1% × $200,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 = $10,000); $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB figure at that point in the program anchors); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a lot-zoning framework, not a park-tenancy right. (MHPTA sources above.)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt, a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/taxes/goods-services-tax-harmonized-sales-tax.html
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA, never March 12**): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age/foundation requirements) are UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.

- **ALR housing frame (full block).** On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. No stratification of ALR residences without ALC approval (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — the only routes.** A manufactured home on farm ground is permitted only as the ALR frame allows: as a principal/additional residence within the caps above, and — for an additional manufactured-home residence that is not a pre-existing structure — only under ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) as summarised in ALC Policy L-25: 9 m or less in width, occupied only by the owner or specified relatives (parent, grandparent or great-grandparent, sibling, or child, within the regulation's meaning); if it is no longer occupied as permitted it must be demolished, removed, or converted to a non-residential use. Any other placement requires a Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application to the ALC. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED.** https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf
- **Farm classification.** Farm classification is voluntary and property-by-property: income thresholds $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of actual value over 4 ha (>4 ha), $10,000 (<0.8 ha); applications by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must file the General Application by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

- **Board facts — ONLY from this slug's condos/55-plus siblings' opened sources, named here.** From the condos sibling, sourced to the BC Chicken Marketing Board General Orders: chicken quota is the amount of chicken, in kilograms of live weight, the board allots to a grower, with mainstream and specialty classes, and transfer of quota with registered premises treated distinctly from acquisition/disposition without registered premises. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- From the same siblings: the BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission's mainstream quota is hen-based (one unit = one placeable broiler breeder hen) and specialty is hatched-chick-based, per its annual report; turkey quota under the BC Turkey Marketing Board scheme material is pounds of turkey meat per specified period; the BC Farm Industry Review Board supervises the system and hears appeals of a board's decision within 30 calendar days, per the BCFIRB appeals page as carried in the siblings. https://bcbhec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-Annual-Report.pdf (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc-condos-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I own the land in a park?** No — on a rented pad you own the home as a chattel and rent the pad; the tenancy, not land title, is your right to the site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me?** Yes — prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response by day 10, silence deemed consent, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Can the park change its rules?** Only once a year, with 3 full months' notice, and only if the rule is fair, clear, properly noticed and does not change a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Q: What if the park closes or converts?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is the sale registered?** In the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer/notary, with the CSA label attached at sale; an unregistered transfer is not effective. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I finance a chattel home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — lender treatment is home- and tenure-specific; confirm before removing subjects.
- **Q: Is a resale park home eligible for the first-time-buyer GST rebate?** No — that rebate is for new housing only (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026 per CRA). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can I just put a manufactured home on a poultry farm for a worker?** Not as a general entitlement — UNCONFIRMED. The only sourced routes are the ALR residence caps, s.34(3)/Policy L-25 family occupancy for an additional manufactured home, or a NARU application. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_-_policy_l-25_-_manufactured_homes_in_the_alr.pdf
- **Q: Is quota included, and what is it worth?** Only if the contract states it; any quota price is UNVERIFIED in this slug, and the one operating farm in the feed states quota is not included. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html
