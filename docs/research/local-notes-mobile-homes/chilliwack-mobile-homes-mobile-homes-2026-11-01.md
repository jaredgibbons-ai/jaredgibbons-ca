## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent (MHPTA s.28(3)). On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file; the live page's Chilliwack range is flagged in §5 as UNVERIFIED site copy only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice (notice on RTB-31), must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the tenant may dispute within 15 days, may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale (Z-240 series for mobile homes, A-277 series for modular homes). If the label is lost, Technical Safety BC re-certification (electrical inspection + approval label) is the official route. A Registry transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. How a specific park home is assessed depends on its tenure and registration — a per-property check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** The live page states PTT "generally does not apply" when buying only a manufactured home on a rented pad. **That flat claim is UNVERIFIED** — no primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, and no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, including a Baker Trail Village lot).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that price point in the program anchors). Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies to Baker Trail Village as a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. Note the distinction the siblings draw: inside a pad-rental park, age/pet/approval rules are PARK RULES under the MHPTA, not SPA bylaws — the Regulation 7.01 exemptions do not automatically apply inside a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Chilliwack frame.** OCP Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — no manufactured-home-park zone section was extracted in the research pass, so no City park-zone figure or park standard is stated: UNVERIFIED. SSMUH Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024; suites capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40%, 1 ADU per lot, garden suites to 100 m². **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing held UNCONFIRMED (candidate 3055 NOT adopted; the City webpage assertion flagged wherever met — see §5). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level CRA sources support: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established. The live page's financing split, First Nations lease-park framing and 55+ legality statements were not confirmed on a lender, Province or park source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements): UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects. (Standing brief.)
- **Property taxes — City of Chilliwack frame.** Notices mailed end of May, due the first working day after July 1; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between a pad-rental park home and Baker Trail Village?** Tenure. In a pad-rental park **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** under the MHPTA — no land ownership. At Baker Trail Village you own the home AND a bare-land strata lot, pay strata fees instead of pad rent, and diligence runs on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is Rainbow Estates 40+ or 45+?** UNRESOLVED. The specialist list records 40+; the feed's own two cards for 9055 Ashwell Rd state 40+ and 45+ respectively. Age rules must come from the park's current documents for the specific site, never from listing text. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: Which Chilliwack park is actually 55+?** One verified park: Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Rd — 143 units, First Nations leased land), where you own the home and rent the pad; it is documented at home in the West Chilliwack GUIDE treatment. Every other named park in §2 is below 55+, all-ages, or a tenure record only. (§2 above.)
- **Q: What do I pay each month in a park here?** Pad rent under the tenancy agreement, plus the home's property tax and insurance. No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file — the live page's range is flagged in §5 as UNVERIFIED site copy — so confirm the current figure, what it includes, and the increase history for the exact site before offering.
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** For a pad tenancy, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no consent fee (MHPTA s.28(3)). At Baker Trail Village (strata) there is no park-consent step at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A manufactured home may not be sold without the original legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular); if it is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC before it can be listed for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Do I pay PTT on a park home?** For an own-land purchase (including a Baker Trail lot), PTT applies on the general tiers (§3: $800,000 = $14,000). For a home-only purchase on a rented pad, the live page's "generally does not apply" claim is UNVERIFIED — confirm with a lawyer or notary for the exact structure before subjects come off. (§3 above.)
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with the additional-compensation routes in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which school serves a park home?** SD33 catchments are address-based and no locator result for a manufactured-home address is on record — run the exact park address; never promise a school from the park name. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
