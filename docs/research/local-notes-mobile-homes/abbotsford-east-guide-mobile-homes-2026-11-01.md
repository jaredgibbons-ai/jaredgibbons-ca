## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block, carried in full because east-side buyers cross-shop Abbotsford's parks:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies in BC — in a park, the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and RENTS the pad; that is not land ownership. Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice. **Caps verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount — percentages ONLY; no pad-rent dollar figure is ever stated as fact in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion (RTB):** conversion of all or a significant part of a park to another use requires all necessary permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice (Form RTB-31), and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route (assessed value minus the initial $20,000) where the RTB conditions are met. Finer park rules are UNCONFIRMED beyond the RTB pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home as chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — the rule that matters for an own-lot east-side manufactured home:** in BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains a central register of ownership details, controls the movement of manufactured homes, and improves lender security. Whether a specific east-side manufactured home remains Registry-registered as a chattel or has been de-registered and affixed to its fee-simple land is UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and land title records for that home are read — the two positions diligence, finance and tax differently. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **PTT:** for an own-land fee-simple purchase (land plus home as real property), general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. **The PTT treatment of a pad-park chattel purchase is a separate question and is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no flat "PTT does not apply on a pad" statement is carried as fact, and that page claim is NOT documented for this guide slug (see §5).** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks (redirect — none in this sub-area):** if an east-side buyer instead buys in a bare-land strata park elsewhere in Abbotsford, the BC strata block applies to the lot — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum CRF 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning/OCP for this ground:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs (RS3 540 m², RS5 420 m², RS6 compact lots); OCP = Abbotsforward 2050 CURRENT (adopted December 2025) — the research file's "2600-2016, as updated" wording is STALE; the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) covers the mountainside. Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (City-report criteria: 400 m² lot, 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, not on a cul-de-sac bulb); Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% and a coach/garden suite is 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford ONLY. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **ALR/flood edges:** the OCP includes an Agricultural Land Reserve map (Map 16); ALR status inside the built east-side neighbourhoods is not confirmed parcel by parcel — UNCONFIRMED per parcel. The low land at the south/southeast edge toward Sumas Prairie is floodplain by the Sumas River, and City flood notices have named properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation areas — lot-level status is checked in the City WebMap. Where a parcel IS in the ALR: subdivision requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the level the CRA sources state:** CRA contemplates a new or substantially renovated mobile home purchased from a builder (including the manufacturer or vendor) as new housing for the GST/HST New Housing Rebate, and CRA policy material treats a previously occupied residential unit that is a residential complex under the exempting provisions — carried only as that used-generally-exempt / new-taxable framing; a specific home's treatment is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-home-construction/new-housing-rebate.html
- **First-home programs:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — never March 12) applies to new HOUSING: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Abbotsford East?** No — none verified on any source checked: no pad-rental park, bare-land strata park, co-op or leasehold park with an east-side address appears in the research file or the siblings. Abbotsford's verified parks are in Abbotsford West, Central Abbotsford and Poplar. (§2.)
- **Q: I saw a manufactured home for sale on an east-side lot — what am I buying?** On the research's housing profile, that is own-land stock: a fee-simple lot with a manufactured-form home on it. You would own the land; the open diligence question is the home's Registry position — still registered as a chattel, or de-registered and affixed to the land — which is UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and title records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Does pad rent apply to an own-lot home?** No. Pad rent exists only in a pad tenancy under the MHPTA. An own-lot owner pays property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3; second 5% September 16, 2026) and their own servicing costs instead. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: If I cross-shop Paradise Park in Abbotsford West, how much can pad rent rise?** Once every 12 months, on at least three full months' notice, capped at 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027 — percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What protects a park buyer if a park closes?** Under the RTB's conversion rules: permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route where the conditions are met. That protection attaches to a pad tenancy — there is no east-side park to attach it to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I finance a manufactured home the same as a house?** Not automatically: a home that remains a registered chattel is personal property, and the Registry exists in part to improve lender security on such purchases; a home de-registered and affixed to owned land presents differently to a lender. Lender terms for a specific home are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are there age rules on east-side manufactured homes?** No park age rule can exist where there is no park, and no age-restricted manufactured-home community was verified here. A 55+ bylaw question only arises in a strata — and only 55+ bylaws are valid in BC strata since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I add a suite to an east-side home?** Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, subject to the City-report criteria (400 m², 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, no cul-de-sac bulb); Abbotsford caps a suite at under 50% and a coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum. SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies — relevant only if you are comparing a park purchase elsewhere. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Which schools serve an east-side manufactured home on its own lot?** The two settled chains are Auguston Traditional → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (36367 Stephen Leacock Drive) and Sandy Hill → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary (3836 Old Clayburn Road), each single-address only — run the exact lot address in the SD34 School Locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q: Does flood or ALR status affect an east-side lot?** At the edges, yes: the south/southeast low land is Sumas River floodplain, and ALR status is parcel-by-parcel UNCONFIRMED in the built neighbourhoods — check both in the City WebMap and on the ALC map before offering. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
