## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the pad-tenancy block IN FULL (governs Maplewood Estates directly).** The Act governs pad tenancies: it applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on in a manufactured home park; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. Maplewood Estates runs on this Act, NOT the Strata Property Act — there is no Form B, depreciation report or strata-meeting machinery in a pad purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/poplar-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Assignment on a sale — no new tenancy:** when a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is ASSIGNED to the purchaser, who becomes the park owner's tenant on the same terms from the assignment date. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. Maplewood's listings state the buyer must be approved by the park owner before purchase — this assignment consent is that approval. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park-owner consent — the 10-day rule:** assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on the approved form (RTB Form 10); the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules:** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. Maplewood's listing-stated pet position (a small pet allowed) is a park-rule item of exactly this kind — confirm it in the written park rules for the specific home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY:** pad rent may be increased only once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) PLUS a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the Residential Tenancy Branch (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file (see §2 — Maplewood's pad rent is UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion — only as the Province states it:** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Anything finer for Maplewood Estates specifically is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Age-restricted parks — the provincial general rule only:** in a designated 55-plus park, at least one tenant on the site must be 55 years of age or older. Maplewood's own 55+ position is listing-stated (§2) and is read from its tenancy documents, not from this general rule alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home is factory-built to the CSA standard and registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the Province's central register of manufactured home ownership; a sale, transfer or purchase is ONLY effective if registered there, filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary) with the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does NOT include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. Abbotsford tax itself is the BC Assessment value multiplied by a rate per $1,000 (Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- **City frame — Abbotsford:** OCP = Abbotsforward 2050, CURRENT — the research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" citation is STALE and is flagged, not followed. Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs: urban RS zones (RS3 540 m² / RS5 420 m² minimum interior lots in City reports), rural A1 at an 8.0 ha minimum subdivision lot area. Suites: Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (City-report criteria include a minimum 400 m² lot and 12.0 m frontage); Abbotsford's suite cap is UNDER 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY. SSMUH amendments were adopted in June 2024, but the SSMUH BYLAW NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" is never asserted). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** The Development Bylaw NUMBER is likewise UNCONFIRMED — process is documented in the siblings, the number is never asserted. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (Poplar's south edge is farm ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; a suite ONLY within the principal residence; an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). Farm class (BC Assessment), full three-tier form: $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31; a flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (form guidance; Poplar's park is pad-rental, not strata):** Form B diligence (fee, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with legacy, caregiver, and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions) — a 45+ line is VOID, with no conversion. This block governs Poplar's two strata complexes (§2), never the pad tenancy at Maplewood. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — treatment stated only as official sources support it:** PTT treatment of a resale manufactured home on a RENTED PAD (the Maplewood form) is UNCONFIRMED in the sources for this file — flat site-copy claims either way (taxable or exempt) are UNVERIFIED, and no flat claim is made here. What the Province's own exemption page DOES state expressly: the Newly Built Home Exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) as a newly built home — a rule for a NEW home placed on OWN land, not for a resale home on a rented pad. For an owned-land purchase in Poplar, the general tiers apply unless an exemption applies: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs + tier math stated: a $500,000 fully residential owned-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 after maximum First-Time Home Buyers' relief where it applies); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a $78,000 figure for $3.5M met in a standalone acreages sibling for this ground is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000, flag the conflict wherever met. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
