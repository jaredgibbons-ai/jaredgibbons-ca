## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block — the pad-tenancy law, in full (it governs any pad purchase a Hawthorne buyer makes elsewhere in Ladner or the region; no pad exists in this sub-area per §2).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies: a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on is a tenant under the Act; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and where both the home and the site are rented to the same person the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **CORE DISCIPLINE — a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership:** the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad. Nothing in a pad purchase conveys the land, a strata lot, or an interest registerable as real property in the park's land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale:** the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is consent deemed given, and the owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for consenting. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms, and the pad rent and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase carry over unchanged. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY, verified live from the RTB framework (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice; the cap is the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); no rounding up and no carry-forward of an unused portion. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file** — no official source publishes one for any park, and none is documented in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules:** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. Since the April 30, 2024 regulation change, park rules may be changed only once per year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days; additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the $20,000 may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home is factory-built to the CSA standard — the registry/CSA-label rules are what separate a registrable manufactured home from an unregistered unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment — UNCONFIRMED for a pad purchase, stated so:** flat site-copy claims either way (for example an Abbotsford page's "PTT does not apply on a pad" and a Chilliwack page's PTT-on-a-pad claim, as carried in other programs) are UNVERIFIED and are never stated as fact here. What official sources DO support: the Province's newly built home exemption from property transfer tax expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) as a newly built home — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on one's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. The PTT position on any specific pad purchase is UNCONFIRMED until the transfer's own facts are taken to the Province's PTT guidance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates and worked anchors (they govern an own-land purchase in this area, e.g. a fee-simple lot with a home):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 under the First-Time Home Buyers' Program); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a $78,000 figure carried in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000 and flag the $78,000 figure wherever met). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (governs the bare-land strata cross-references in §2, e.g. South Pointe, in their home files):** Form B Information Certificate diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, with exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion for spouses/partners and defined children); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Delta zoning / OCP frame:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — it contains NO lettered schedules (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the OLD Bylaw No. 3950; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"). - **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a zoning frame for owned lots, not a right inside a manufactured home park. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Hawthorne I can buy into?** No verified one. No park of any tenure form is verified inside Hawthorne in the research file or any sibling file (§2). - **Q: If I buy a manufactured home on a rented pad elsewhere in Ladner, do I own land?** No. You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act tenancy. The own-land equivalents in Delta are a bare-land strata lot (South Pointe, NWS2528, Neilsen Grove ground — you own the strata lot) or a fee-simple lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much is pad rent, and how fast can it rise?** No dollar figure is published as fact in this file — no official source publishes one for any park; get the current pad rent from the park's own written tenancy agreement. Increases are capped by formula, once every 12 months on at least 3 full months' notice: 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse my purchase?** Assignment needs the owner's prior written consent (RTB Form 10), but the owner must respond within 10 days, silence is consent deemed given, and no consent fee may be charged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if the park is sold or redeveloped?** A sale alone does not end an assigned tenancy — it continues on the same terms. A conversion of all or a significant part of the park requires permits first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date, with the additional-compensation route in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is the purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered; a lawyer or notary files the signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a home on a rented pad?** Yes — on the home; the assessed value does not include the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, and a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: Do I pay PTT on a pad purchase?** UNCONFIRMED — flat claims either way on live pages are UNVERIFIED site copy (§3). The sourced exemption point runs the other way: a NEW manufactured home placed and affixed on vacant land the buyer owns can fall inside the newly built home exemption. Take the specific transfer to the Province's PTT guidance before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a manufactured home?** Lender/insurer treatment (chattel vs land-home, age and foundation rules) is UNVERIFIED on official sources in this research — confirm it for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects, and treat a missing CSA label as a financing and resale red flag. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which schools serve a Hawthorne address?** Catchments are address-based in SD37. The settled sample: 5160 Central Avenue returns Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12). Any other address needs its own locator run — catchments for other Hawthorne addresses are UNVERIFIED. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
