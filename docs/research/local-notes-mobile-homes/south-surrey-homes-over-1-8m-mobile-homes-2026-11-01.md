## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On sale, the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers — the transfers this band actually contains).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors with inputs: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M fully residential → $93,000** (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 further-2% tier = $93,000). **FLAGGED for this slug's own sibling set: the townhomes sibling for THIS slug carries "$3.5M purely residential → $78,000" — WRONG for that input; follow $93,000. The same sibling's "Over $3M — 5%" wording is a compression of the 3% tier plus the further 2% tier, not a flat rate — flagged wherever met, never carried.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies to Sea Ridge Bays as a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — NEVER 18020 as current (the siblings for this slug name 18020 only as the replaced predecessor — an explicit negative, not an item); Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; secondary suite capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area (the old 90 m² cap is NOT current); coach/garden suites 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots; SSMUH Jul 8, 2024; STRs permitted, owner-only from the principal residence, City business licence $358/yr — with the $350/yr figure that appears in program material disclosed as a CONFLICT, unresolved here; servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** White Rock-side parcels: White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **ALR frame (band listings near Elgin/Hazelmere).** One principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); ALR status is confirmed with the ALC, not from a note on title; parcel-level ALR status UNVERIFIED (research file §4). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt, a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — no March 12 statement is met in this slug's own siblings; the townhomes and condos siblings both carry March 13): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate; at this band's prices the rebate is fully phased out in any case. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: At Sea Ridge Bays, do I own the land?** Yes — as a bare-land strata lot, with a strata fee instead of pad rent (listing-stated at $210/month including water in the listings checked; confirm in the Form B). That is the opposite tenure from a rented pad. (§2)
- **Q: On a rented pad, do I own the land?** No. **A home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad.** The tenancy is assigned on sale under the MHPTA (§3).
- **Q: What PTT do I budget at $3.5M?** **$93,000** fully residential, including the further 2% tier over $3M (tier math in §3). This slug's townhomes sibling shows $78,000 for $3.5M — flagged as wrong for that input and NOT followed.
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). (§3)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad park — prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence = deemed consent, no consent fee. At a bare-land strata like Sea Ridge Bays there is no pad assignment; listings state council approval is required there — as listing-stated, confirmed on the Form B. (§§2–3)
- **Q: Can a suite or coach house be added to a band acreage?** Under Surrey's current rules a suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling and a coach/garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots); on ALR land the ALC frame governs instead (500 m² principal cap; 90/186 m² additional residence). Parcel status decides — UNVERIFIED per parcel. (§3)
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion, the landlord needs all permits/approvals first, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date (MHPTA s.42). (§3)
