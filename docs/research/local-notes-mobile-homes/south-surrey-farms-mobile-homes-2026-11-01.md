## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

### The ALR block — full (governs a manufactured home on farm ground)

- **ALR housing frame.** On ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **No stratification of ALR residences.** Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); the Strata Property Act s.239 and the Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) frame the same prohibition: ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's approval, and ALC approval is required for subdivision of ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Manufactured homes on farm ground — only as ALC sources support.** A manufactured home as a farm residence is dealt with under ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 (manufactured-home residence) and, where the use does not adhere, the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route to the ALC. A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED — no source in this program states one, and none is claimed here. Confirm the specific home, its placement and its use with the ALC and the City for the exact parcel before relying on either route. (Standing brief position; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **BC Assessment farm classification.** Farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Parcel-level status.** Whether a specific South Surrey farm parcel is in the ALR, its zoning, and its floodplain / Flood Construction Level position are UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check a property in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder by PID or civic address and check City mapping lot by lot for flood construction levels. Floodplain in Surrey is Part 7A by stream class — NO single figure is stated for this ground; the Serpentine / Nicomekl lowland and Mud Bay parcels are exactly the parcels to check lot by lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-farms.html

No marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in this slug's research pass. Get any quota position in the contract, in writing, and verify it with the applicable marketing board directly. (Research file §4.)

### The MHPTA block — full (governs a pad purchase, if a farm buyer also buys in a park elsewhere)

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — including a farm parcel — is dealt with through the land title system instead, and on ALR land through the ALC frame above as well. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. The 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This registry step applies to a manufactured home on farm ground too — it is the home's register, separate from the land title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and any farm assets on a specific South Surrey farm purchase is likewise UNVERIFIED — no Province farm-transfer tax source was opened in this pass. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT, GST apportionment and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. The $3.5M/$78,000 error is NOT met in this slug's own siblings — and note the ALR prohibition above: a farm residence cannot be stratified to chase a strata form in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata — Sea Ridge Bays, cross-referenced in §2, is the South Surrey example; it does NOT apply to ALR farm land, where stratification needs ALC permission).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 is current (the former Bylaw No. 18020 is replaced and is cited only as predecessor, never as current); Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs, its A-1/A-2 specifics UNVERIFIED as above. Servicing: Surrey Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) in the verified ledger. - **Q: If I buy in a Surrey park instead, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA.
