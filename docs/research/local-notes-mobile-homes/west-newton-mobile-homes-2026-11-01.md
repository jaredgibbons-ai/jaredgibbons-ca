## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for the §2 tenure record's pad tenancies.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and renting both the home and the site falls under the Residential Tenancy Act, not the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the provincial pages also provide a 15-day window to dispute the notice and additional compensation routes (an assessed-value-based amount where the home cannot be relocated, and a $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amount in the circumstances those pages describe) — finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. Surrey's own Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure walks through the same steps (permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 per home); Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 (adopted Oct 20, 2025) does NOT apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked examples (inputs + tier math): $500,000 fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**; $700,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 = **$14,000** (a qualifying first-time buyer at $800,000 pays **$6,000** after the first-$500,000 relief); $900,000 = **$16,000**; $1.0M = **$18,000**; $1.1M = **$20,000**; $1.2M = **$22,000**; $1.5M = **$28,000**; $1.8M = **$34,000**; $2.0M = **$38,000**; $3.5M fully residential = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata block (governs West Newton's strata stock — Glenwood Village, Lakeview Estates — and any strata purchase; it does NOT govern the pad park).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page; fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP: Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and never current. Zoning: Bylaw No. 12000, including the RM-M Manufactured Home Residential Zone permitting manufactured home parks. Secondary suites: one per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, habitable floor area capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit, not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and no 90 m² cap was found in the current consolidated bylaw text. Coach houses / garden suites: at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with lane access required on smaller R3–R6 lots and one extra parking space. SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024): maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Streamside protection sits in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 and runs by stream class — NO single setback figure. Servicing: Bylaw 8830 (1986), VERIFIED from City pages. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (research file above)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** An STR is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating (the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed alongside). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (research file above)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level CRA sources support: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer for a specific manufactured home is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

A park home's holding cost is pad rent plus the home's own tax/insurance; a fee-simple home carries no pad rent. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (research file above)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What mobile/manufactured homes can I buy in West Newton?** One park in the program record: Newton Mobile Home Park, 6571 King George Blvd — a 55+ pad-rental park of 34 home sites (directory-stated) where you buy the manufactured home and rent the pad. Its record rests on a park directory plus MLS listing statements; no operator source was read, so park-level details are confirmed with the park, not from listings. (§2.)
- **Q: Do I own the land at Newton Mobile Home Park?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad — that is not land ownership.** There are no strata fees and no Form B; the tenancy runs under the MHPTA, the pad tenancy agreement and the park rules, and the purchase is subject to park approval. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can my pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by inflation plus the park's proportional levy/utility amount: 2.3% + proportional for 2026, 2.2% + proportional for 2027 (RTB, verified Nov 1, 2026). Percentages only — no current pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file; confirm the actual figure with the park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park refuse my purchase because of age?** In a 55+ park, in effect yes: assignment needs the park owner's consent on RTB Form 10 (response by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent), and the provincial assignment rules recognise the park's 55+ reservation (at least one of 2+ tenants aged 55+, Human Rights Code s.10(2)(b)(i) framework). The seller must give you the written tenancy agreement and park rules before consent is requested — get both. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — park vs strata difference?** In a 55+ STRATA (Glenwood Village, Lakeview Estates), a younger spouse/partner is exempt by Regulation 7.01. In a PARK, the assignment-rules test is that at least one of 2+ tenants has reached 55 — the park's own rules govern a pad tenancy, not the strata exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed at the park?** A listing states one pet is allowed, subject to park rules — LISTING-STATED only; the park rules govern, so confirm with the park before writing. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6571-king-george-boulevard/6
- **Q: What if the park is redeveloped?** The provincial frame: permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice to end tenancy, $20,000 compensation per home on or before the effective date, early move-out on 10 days' notice — and Surrey's own Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure walks through the same steps.
