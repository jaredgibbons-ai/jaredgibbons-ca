## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad park is verified in Aldergrove; the block is carried in full, with its limit for this ground stated plainly: Pioneer Park is a co-operative, not an MHPTA pad park, so the MHPTA pad-rent caps do not set Pioneer Park's co-op charge).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". A Pioneer Park purchase runs on the co-op's own acceptance and share-transfer documents instead — a different approval, read in the home file and the co-op's documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY (pad tenancies only; a co-op charge is not pad rent).** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. Fee changes at Pioneer Park are a co-op-documents question: UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support (pad parks).** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute a closure notice within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. How a co-operative's own winding-up/closure rules compare is a co-op-documents question — UNCONFIRMED in this file; read Pioneer Park's co-op documents, not the MHPTA, for its closure position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A Pioneer Park purchase therefore has two records to diligence: the Registry record for the home, and the co-op's share/occupancy record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, and the PTT treatment of a chattel home / co-op share purchase is likewise UNCONFIRMED in this file — **no flat claim either way is stated as fact** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad or a co-op share. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies to Creekside Villa-type strata purchases in this community, and to any bare-land strata park a buyer cross-shops; no bare-land strata park is verified in Aldergrove).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. **Co-op occupancy rules are the co-op's own:** the provincial strata exemptions govern STRATA 55+ bylaws and do not automatically answer a Pioneer Park occupancy question — obtain the co-op's current rule in writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH / servicing — Township of Langley frame.** Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs; Section 110.1 ladder as read in Township council reports — SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m², SR-2 8,094 m², SR-3 1,765 m², R-1E 930 m², R-1D 650 m², R-1B and R-1C 464.5 m² minimum lot areas; RU-2, C-2 and CD-192 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): **NO 6-plex tier**; Township suite cap 120 m²; a coach house is a Bylaw 2500 s.102 accessory dwelling unit on a fee-simple basis; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area (no 355 m² anywhere in the bylaw). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and it creates no added-unit right inside a co-op park — Pioneer Park's form is governed by its co-op documents and its zoning, not SSMUH. Servicing: Township Development and Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger (the aldergrove-GUIDE lots sibling's UNCONFIRMED holding is flagged in the guide mobile home file; the 5382 ledger governs for the bylaw's identity, while servicing for any single parcel remains a parcel-level confirmation). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Aldergrove's one manufactured-home community runs on a third legal system.** It is not strata (no Form B, no SPA bylaws governing the park itself) and not a pad tenancy (no MHPTA cap, no RTB pad rules): Pioneer Park is a co-operative, and its acceptance, share and occupancy documents are the whole rulebook. Buyers who diligence it as either of the other two forms will ask the wrong questions. (§§2–3; guide mobile home file §5.)
