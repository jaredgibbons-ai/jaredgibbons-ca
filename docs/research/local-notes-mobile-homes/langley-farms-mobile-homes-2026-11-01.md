### The farm-ground position, stated only as official sources support

- **No park on farm land.** The 55-plus sibling verifies ZERO 55+ ownership stock on Langley farm land or in the rural farm sub-areas, and no source in any sibling verifies a manufactured-home park of any age profile as a farm-belt product on a farm parcel. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-farms-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md; research file §5.)
- **A manufactured home on a farm parcel is a residence on that parcel, not a park home.** The ALC housing rules govern what housing ALR land may carry: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. No ALC or Township source opened in this program states a separate manufactured-home-on-farm-land permission, a park permission, or a pad-tenancy regime on a farm parcel — anything finer is UNCONFIRMED, and a specific parcel's status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **One additional residence on ALR land:** the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. Whether that additional residence may be a manufactured home for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED on any source opened here — confirm with the ALC and the Township before relying on it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **No stratification route to a "park" on ALR land.** A strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6), and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation — so neither a bare-land strata manufactured-home community nor a strata farm product arises on ALR land as of right. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. A manufactured home sitting on the buyer's own farm parcel is therefore not an MHPTA pad tenancy at all — it is dealt with as a residence on that title, layered with the ALR rules in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This registry step applies to the home itself whether it sits in a park or on a farm parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. A home on the owner's own farm parcel is assessed with that parcel — and farm classification is a separate BC Assessment position (see §5). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land (including, in principle, the buyer's own farm parcel), not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, including a farm parcel purchase).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (a $78,000 townhomes-sibling figure elsewhere is OPEN — follow $93,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified on Langley farm ground, and §2 explains the ALR bar).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Township of Langley frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township, including Rural Zones RU-1 and RU-2; a general RU-zone minimum parcel size from the direct bylaw zone text is NOT confirmed in the research file — UNCONFIRMED. SSMUH Bylaw 6020: 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², **NO 6-plex tier**, and lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning; the Township suite cap in the standing frame is 120 m², and a coach house is defined in Bylaw 2500 s.102 as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot, on a fee-simple basis in the standing frame. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and none of the SSMUH tiers creates a manufactured-home park right on a farm parcel. Servicing: Township Development and Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf (research file §4.)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer for a specific manufactured home is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED — confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a mobile home in a park on a Langley farm?** No such product was verified. No manufactured-home park sits on Langley farm land in any source checked; Langley's verified parks are in Brookswood, Aldergrove and Otter District, documented in their home files (§2). (55-plus sibling above.)
- **Q: A farm listing includes a manufactured home — what am I actually buying?** The farm parcel, on its own title, with the home as a residence on it — priced, in the one-off example the siblings document, on land value. There is no pad, no park tenancy and no park age rule; the ALR housing rules in §2 and the Manufactured Home Registry step in §3 are the checks that apply. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I place a new manufactured home on a farm parcel I buy?** Only within the ALC / Township housing permissions for that parcel — the principal-residence permission (500 m² or less, fill cap 1,000 m², suite only inside it) or the one-additional-residence permission (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), each subject to Township bylaws. No source opened states a manufactured-home-specific placement right — UNCONFIRMED for any specific home and parcel until the ALC and Township confirm. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Could I create a small manufactured-home park or bare-land strata on ALR land?** Not as of right: a strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR, and subdivision needs Commission permission after an application or a permission in the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is Langley Grove Estates a seniors farm park?** No, on both points: it is in Otter District, not on a farm parcel, and its listings state no age restrictions and all ages welcome. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/24330-fraser-highway/52
- **Q: If I do buy in a Langley park elsewhere, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** In the Pioneer Park co-operative you buy the home plus co-op shares — a different tenure again, documented in its Aldergrove home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in a park?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a manufactured home on farm land or in a park?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel vs land-home treatment and age/foundation requirements are lender-specific. Confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **235 km² is the Township's own ALR figure** — most of Langley's land base is reserve land, so the default first check for any Langley farm parcel, for housing form as much as for farm use, is the ALC map, by PID or civic address. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **The fill rule surprises farmhouse builders:** the ALC's 500 m² principal-residence permission carries a second cap — no more than 1,000 m² of fill may be placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification is an income test, not a label.** BC Assessment states a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A buyer whose plan cannot meet the threshold for the parcel's size puts the classification — and its tax treatment — at risk. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water and septic are paper-trail checks on farm ground.** Well reports must be submitted within 90 days after a well is constructed, altered or decommissioned, and older wells may not appear in the GWELLS registry; domestic groundwater use needs no licence, but non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence. For septic, only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system, and must file a record of sewerage system before work and a letter of certification after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
