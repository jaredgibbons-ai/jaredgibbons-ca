## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — stated in full (background law for any pad tenancy in Vancouver; no pad tenancy is verified on Yaletown ground).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned; the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules before asking the park owner to consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / a director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after receipt, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice, and enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and additional assessed-value / $5,000 amounts carried only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **City of Vancouver taxes + Empty Homes Tax (applies to every Yaletown condo).** Advance taxes due February 3, 2026, main taxes July 3, 2026, 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline; every residential owner must file the annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration by the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf — Standing frame: EHT rate **3%, Vancouver only**; the research file did not state the rate on the pages it read (research UNVERIFIED item 7), so 3% is carried as the standing frame only.
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (BOTH flat page claims are UNVERIFIED). The newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — for placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example: $700,000 = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — follow $93,000. **Sibling check, stated explicitly: NO sibling for this slug carries the wrong PTT shorthand as its own rate — the townhomes sibling's "Over $3M — 5%" wording is a flagging sentence only; NO sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M (the acreages sibling states $93,000 and names $78,000 as living ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling).** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Yaletown).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports 5-year cycle (5+ lots, since July 1, 2024); minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning/OCP + servicing — Vancouver frame.** Subdivision By-law No. 5208 VERIFIED (fees 14538); Yaletown's historic core is the HA-3 district schedule area (§1). SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** General level: GST applies to new construction, not resales; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.

A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. **Sibling check: the condos + townhomes siblings note secondary sources saying March 12 with March 13 governing — a disclosure note, NOT a March-12 carrier; no conforming item arises.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Yaletown?** No — none is verified in any source for this ground. Yaletown's verified stock is strata condos (10 counted in the condos sibling). (§2.)
- **Q: If I found a home on a rented pad downtown, would I own land?** No. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad**, under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes

Read title + strata plan for the exact unit. (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in BC?** 2.3% + proportional amount (2026); 2.2% + proportional amount (2027), once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice — percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park owner have to approve me?** Where the MHPTA applies, yes — Form 10, response by day 10, silence = deemed consent, no consent fee; seller hands over the tenancy agreement + park rules first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured home registered?** Manufactured Home Registry only — effective on registration, via lawyer/notary with signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer; CSA label attached at sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
