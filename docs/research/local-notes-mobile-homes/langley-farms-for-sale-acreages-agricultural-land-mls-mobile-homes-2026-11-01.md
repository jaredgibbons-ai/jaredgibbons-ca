## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

### Manufactured homes on farm ground — the ALR block (full)

- **No stratification of ALR residences.** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 is the governing reference for strata in the ALR; the Strata Property Act s.239 and the Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) frame it — ALR approval is required for subdivision, and the bulletin's detailed strata conditions were not re-read in this slug's research pass, so no condition beyond that reference is stated: detail UNVERIFIED. (Research file §4.)
- **Residence caps on ALR land (ALC housing page, verified in the research pass):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of **500 m² or less**, with no more than **1,000 m² of fill** placed for its construction. A secondary suite is permitted **only when located entirely within the principal residence** — a separate or detached dwelling is not used as a secondary suite — and local government bylaws must also permit suites. One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met: maximum **90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha**, and Township bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (research file §4.)
- **Manufactured home as a farm residence — the only routes the sources support:** a manufactured home placed as a residence on ALR land runs through **ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 (manufactured-home residence)** plus the **Non-Adhering Residential Use** application route where the use does not adhere to the ALR rules. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — no source in this program grants one, and none is stated here. (Settled program position; M15 brief.)
- **Farm classification thresholds (BC Assessment):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha — with farm classification applications due by **October 31**. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (research file §4.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (not for a farm parcel).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the farm case — is dealt with through the land title system instead, with the ALR block in §2 layered over Township zoning. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (park sales only).** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after receipt, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits and approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and additional assessed-value / $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. This registry step applies to a manufactured home placed on farm land as well as to a park home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. A farm parcel's tax position is the farm-classification position in §2, a different mechanism. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way as fact (both flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own (including farm) land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. How PTT is apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific farm purchase: UNVERIFIED in this slug's siblings — confirm per parcel with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is on this topic, and ALR strata is separately barred/conditioned per §2).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning — Township frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township; SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier); suite cap 120 m²; coach house = Bylaw 2500 s.102 accessory dwelling unit in the second storey of a detached garage, fee-simple in the standing frame; servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED. A general RU-zone minimum parcel size from direct bylaw text and any farm home plate rule: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4.)
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — used home generally exempt, new home taxable only as sourced at that general level. No GST-on-farmland rule beyond that level is stated: the research opens no farmland-GST source, so farm-specific GST treatment is UNCONFIRMED. (This slug's condos sibling.)
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000. FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home, age/foundation) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Langley farm parcel?** Only through the ALR routes: ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 for a manufactured-home residence, or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application — subject to the residence caps in §2 (principal ≤500 m²; suite only inside the principal; one additional at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) and Township bylaws. A general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide the farm to place several homes?** No stratification of ALR residences (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — subdivision needs ALC approval, and the bulletin's detailed conditions are UNVERIFIED beyond that reference in this slug. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Does the farm keep its tax break if I just live there?** Farm classification must keep meeting BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income for the operation's size (§2), with applications due October 31 — a residence-only plan puts the classification, and its tax treatment, at risk. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Does quota come with a farm on this feed?** UNVERIFIED — no marketing-board source was opened for this slug. If a listing mentions quota, verify it directly with the relevant board before pricing it in. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Is a manufactured home on my own farm land a pad tenancy?** No. You own the land; the purchase runs through the land title system, and the home's transfer still registers in the Manufactured Home Registry. The MHPTA pad rules in §3 apply only where the site is rented. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up (if I compare a park home instead)?** 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027; once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice, percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What happens if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter on a farm placement too?** The sale/transfer is only effective once registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale — placement on your own land does not remove either step. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is there a median price for Langley farms?** No — SnapStats publishes no farm category, so no farm median, price-per-acre or farm sales count exists in the permitted sources; §1's figures are residential context only. (Research file §2.)
- **Q: What water checks matter on a farm parcel?** Search GWELLS for the well record; irrigation (non-domestic) groundwater use requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
