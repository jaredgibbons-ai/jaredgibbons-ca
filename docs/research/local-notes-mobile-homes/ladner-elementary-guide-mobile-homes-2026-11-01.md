## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block — the pad-tenancy law, in full (it governs any pad purchase a buyer from this sub-area makes elsewhere; no pad exists in this sub-area per §2).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies: a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on is a tenant under the Act; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead; where both the home and the site are rented to the same person, the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **CORE DISCIPLINE — a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership:** the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad. Delta Green is the deliberate local contrast: its buyer owns a strata apartment in the land title system — real property, with a Form B — which is a different purchase from both a pad home and a bare-land strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (pad tenure):** the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested on RTB Form 10; the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day after the request, silence by the end of the 10th day is consent deemed given, and no fee may be charged for consenting. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms, with pad rent and the next increase date unchanged. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY, verified live from the RTB framework (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice; the cap is the BC inflation rate plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); no rounding up, no carry-forward. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file** — no official source publishes one for any park, and none is documented in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules / closure / conversion (pad tenure):** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement, changeable only on 3 full months' notice (and, since April 30, 2024, only once per year), and enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. On a conversion of all or a significant part of a park (MHPTA s.42): permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' notice; the dispute window is 15 days; additional compensation of assessed value minus $20,000 may be ordered where the home could not be moved, plus a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, if the landlord does not proceed. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone else; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. Delta's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Strata block (governs Delta Green and any bare-land strata purchase, in their home files):** Form B Information Certificate diligence (strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; provided within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion for spouses/partners, live-in caregivers and defined children); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert. For Delta Green specifically, the sibling record pairs its 55+ category with an adaptable-ready covenant — a bylaw and a covenant are different instruments, so a buyer confirms which are registered via the Form B and title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **PTT / transfer treatment — UNCONFIRMED for a pad purchase, stated so:** flat site-copy claims either way (an Abbotsford page's "PTT does not apply on a pad"; a Chilliwack page's PTT-on-a-pad claim, as carried in other programs) are UNVERIFIED, never fact. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 under the First-Time Home Buyers' Program); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a $78,000 figure in a townhomes sibling elsewhere is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000, flag $78,000 wherever met). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Delta Green a mobile home community?** No. It is a 24-unit freehold strata apartment building (EPS8064, built 2021) at 4686 51 Street, verified 55+ on listing data and the City's approval record. A buyer there buys a strata apartment with a Form B — not a manufactured home, not a pad, not a bare-land lot. https://en.youlive.ca/delta-building/13200-delta-green and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Q: Is there any park I can buy into in central Ladner?** No verified one in this sub-area (§2). Ladner's own-land alternatives are the bare-land strata communities in Neilsen Grove (South Pointe, NWS2528) and Delta Manor (Laburnum Park Place), documented in their home files; pad-rental parks in the wider region are documented in their own area files in this program.
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we both live at Delta Green?** On the strata rules that govern it: provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption), and defined children can also be exempt. Ownership itself is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside. Confirm Delta Green's own registered bylaws and covenant on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: If I buy on a rented pad elsewhere, do I own land?** No — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad under the MHPTA. That is the opposite end of the tenure spectrum from Delta Green (strata real property) and from a bare-land strata lot (you own the lot). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent rise?** By formula only, once every 12 months on at least 3 full months' notice: 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse my purchase?** Assignment needs prior written consent (RTB Form 10), but the owner must respond within 10 days, silence is deemed consent, and no consent fee may be charged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if a park is redeveloped?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, early departure on 10 days' notice, and the additional-compensation route in §3. A strata apartment or bare-land lot has no equivalent park-closure mechanism. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, CSA label attached at sale. A strata purchase registers in the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Does buying in this sub-area put my kids in Ladner Elementary?** Not automatically — the sub-area name is a school name, not a guarantee. SD37 catchments are address-based; the settled sample (5016 44 Avenue, the school's own address) returns Ladner Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12), and every other address needs its own locator run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Do I pay PTT on a pad purchase?** UNCONFIRMED — flat claims either way on live pages are UNVERIFIED site copy (§3). The sourced exemption point covers a NEW manufactured home placed and affixed on vacant land the buyer owns. Take the specific transfer to the Province's PTT guidance before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
