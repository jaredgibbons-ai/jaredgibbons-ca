## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — IN FULL, as it governs Newton Mobile Home Park pad tenancies:** the Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and renting both the home and the site falls under the Residential Tenancy Act, not the MHPTA. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment and consent:** buying in a park assigns the existing pad tenancy — before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment requires the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director): request on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day or consent is deemed given, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser holds the tenancy on the same terms. The assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or, for 2+ tenants, at least one who has reached 55) under the Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) framework. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Pad-rent increase caps — verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026), percentages ONLY:** once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at inflation (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) PLUS a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional**. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules and closure/conversion/compensation — only as official RTB/provincial sources support:** park rules form part of the tenancy agreement, must apply to all tenants fairly, be clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, be properly noticed, and not change a material term; since the April 30, 2024 regulation change, park rules can change only once a year on three months' notice on an RTB-approved form. A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all permits and approvals in place first, give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Added compensation where a home cannot be relocated and bad-faith closure compensation are as the Province's June 6, 2018 bulletin states them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2018MAH0066-001111
- **Surrey's own park framework:** the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure walks through the provincial steps for a park redevelopment — 12 months' notice, permits and approvals first, $20,000 compensation per home, and provincial resources for tenants. Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 (adopted October 20, 2025) does NOT apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Age rules — sources discipline:** park age rules are carried ONLY from operator/Form B/registered-bylaw sources; listing-stated ages are labelled listing-stated and conflicts stay UNRESOLVED. Newton Mobile Home Park's 55+ position is directory + listing-stated (Section 2) — UNCONFIRMED at operator level. In a STRATA, only a 55+ bylaw is valid (next bullet). The standing program example of an unresolved park-age conflict (a Cultus Lake park carried as 45+ in one listing and 55+ in another) is NOT this ground and is not imported here; it is named only as the pattern this discipline exists for. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home sale, transfer or purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership (BC Registries); the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. Property tax: the owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, excluding the land value where the home is in a park not on reserve land or on another's land; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment — ONLY as official sources support:** the Province's Newly Built Home Exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — the own-land case. No official source read for this ground settles PTT on a resale home on a rented pad: **UNCONFIRMED** — no flat claim either way is made here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Strata block (governs West Newton's strata stock — Glenwood Village, Lakeview Estates — and any strata purchase; it does NOT govern the pad park):** Form B (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (the "Rentals Not Allowed" line MLS data still records for Glenwood Village is void in law); only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, incl. legacy-resident, caregiver and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and there is no conversion route — which is why older "45+" descriptions of Glenwood Village are pre-2022 wording, not law. A strata buyer OWNS the lot/unit — the opposite tenure from Newton Mobile Home Park's rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

The seller must give you the written tenancy agreement and park rules before consent is requested — get both. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
