## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (a $78,000 townhomes-sibling figure elsewhere is OPEN — follow $93,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Willoughby Heights — Willow Park Estates is a conventional strata townhouse complex, to which this block applies as strata law, not as park law).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Township of Langley frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. The Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (Section 403) is Willoughby's compact-lot pattern: under s.403.7, R-CL lots run from a **minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum lot area of 464.5 m²**, with a minimum lot frontage of 11 m for front-loaded lots and 8.2 m for rear-loaded lots. **No 355 m² figure exists anywhere in the bylaw for R-CL** — any page stating one is stating a number the bylaw does not contain. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², **NO 6-plex tier**; lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning. Township suite cap 120 m² in the standing frame; a coach house is a Bylaw 2500 s.102 accessory dwelling unit in the second storey of a detached garage, on a fee-simple basis in the standing frame. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: Township Development and Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/willoughby-heights-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED — confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Willoughby?** None verified. The research file and every sibling name no park on this ground; the honest answer is that Willoughby's stock is strata attached plus compact-lot detached, and a park buyer widens the search to Brookswood, Aldergrove or Otter District (§2 cross-references). (Research file §5; 55-plus sibling above.)
- **Q: Is Willow Park Estates a mobile home park?** No — it is a 1987 strata townhouse complex (64 units per the building source) with a listing-stated 55+ position. You buy a strata lot, not a home on a pad, and the Strata Property Act — not the MHPTA — governs. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/willow-park-estates-6467-197-street
- **Q: What about Forest Green Estates at 9080 198 St?** Candidate only, UNCONFIRMED — and it is Walnut Grove ground, north of Highway 1, not Willoughby. No tenure, age rule or site count for it is verified in this program, and it is never counted on this ground. (§2 above.)
- **Q: If I buy in a Langley park elsewhere, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific. Confirm financing for the specific home and its pad tenure before removing subjects. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc-guide.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Willoughby address?** Catchments are address-based in SD35. One settled single-address chain: for the sample address 7888 200 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary → Peter Ewart Middle School → R.E. Mountain Secondary.
