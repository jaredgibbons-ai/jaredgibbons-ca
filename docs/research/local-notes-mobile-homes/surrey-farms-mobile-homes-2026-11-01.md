## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**Topic — no roster, stated at the top. NO manufactured-home park is verified on Surrey farm ground, and no farm parcel is listed here. Farm-ground housing is a different stock from a park: the homes sit on freehold farm parcels under the ALC frame below. For the record of what the park form means where it does exist: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad, under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (Section 3). No pad-rental park, bare-land strata manufactured-home community or co-op is verified on Surrey farm ground in any source.**

### Manufactured homes on farm ground — the ONLY route, stated exactly as the ALC sources support it

- **The route:** manufactured homes on ALR farm ground are supported in this program ONLY via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, together with the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route where the residence does not adhere to the permitted forms. Anything beyond what those sources permit requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. (Standing brief, poultry-farms M2 treatment as the model; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **What is NOT established:** a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement — a right to place a manufactured home on farm ground for farm workers as such — is **UNCONFIRMED**. No source opened in this program states it as a flat entitlement, and none is claimed here. A buyer whose plan depends on a farm-worker manufactured home must confirm the exact structure (s.34(3) / Policy L-25 conditions, or a NARU / ALC application) with the ALC and the City before subjects come off. (Standing brief.)
- **The ALC residence frame any farm-ground home — manufactured or site-built — must fit:** one principal residence per parcel without an ALC application, at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence (a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites); one additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; further residences only by ALC approval where necessary for farm use. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR, and replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (research file above)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (carried in full as the standing block; no pad purchase exists on this farm ground).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the farm-ground form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home placed on the buyer's own farm parcel still carries this registry record — registry and land title are separate checks. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **THE FULL ALR BLOCK (governs every residence on farm ground in this file).** No stratification of ALR residences: ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) — an ALR residence cannot be stratified, and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction); a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; one additional residence at 90 m² maximum on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² maximum on parcels over 40 ha, where the ALC conditions are met; anything beyond requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. Manufactured homes on farm ground ONLY via ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + the Non-Adhering Residential Use route (Section 2); a general entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. ALR short-term accommodation: whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. Farm classification (BC Assessment), three-tier form: $2,500 minimum income if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (research file above)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — directly relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on the buyer's own farm parcel, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, including a farm parcel purchase).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — this slug's acreages sibling follows $93,000 on the same tier math; no $78,000 figure is followed here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (carried for completeness; on ALR ground the ALR block above bars stratification of residences, so this block can only ever apply off-ALR).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 — never 18020 as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Urban suites (for contrast with the ALR rule): only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. SSMUH: 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — an urban-lot framework for eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary; **SSMUH is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules govern, and SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. Streamside setbacks in Part 7A by stream class — NO single figure. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (research file above)
- **Short-term rentals — two layers.** ALR layer: whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval (above). City layer: only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host, tenants cannot operate one, and a business licence is required before operating — **licence figure CONFLICT carried disclosed: $358 per year in this slug's research vs $350 per year + fees in the standing program figure; neither is settled here.** https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (research file above)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level CRA sources support: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — this slug's acreages sibling notes a March 12 date in-line in some sources with March 13 governing; that noting is carried here as the sibling states it, never as an assent date): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. On ALR land any qualifying new home must first fit the ALC caps above. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Surrey farm for my farm workers?** Not as a flat entitlement — that general entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. Manufactured homes on farm ground are supported only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route + the Non-Adhering Residential Use route, or an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use, and the residence must fit the ALC caps (principal ≤500 m²; additional 90 m² at ≤40 ha / 186 m² above 40 ha). Confirm the exact structure with the ALC before subjects. (Section 2; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on Surrey farm ground?** No verified one — farm-ground housing is freehold parcels under the ALC frame, not parks. Surrey's verified pad parks are urban-ground stock in Fleetwood and Queen Mary Park, counted in their home files. (Section 2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home on a rented pad elsewhere, do I own land?** No. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** under the MHPTA. A farm parcel purchase is the opposite form: you own the land on title, and any manufactured home on it carries its own Registry record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** One additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; anything beyond that requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide the farm residences?** No stratification of ALR residences (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. Surrey's SSMUH framework is for eligible urban lots and is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR parcels. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Surrey farm house?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR, renting out the entire principal residence for short-term stays is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval, and the City separately requires the host to be an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a business licence (figure conflict disclosed in Section 3). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What does farm classification actually require?** BC Assessment's three-tier income form: $2,500 if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a park instead?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). A farm parcel carries no pad rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are the Surrey market numbers in this file farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Section 1 figures are Surrey residential context only, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
