## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in East Delta; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only East Delta form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. This registry step applies to a manufactured home on an East Delta farm parcel as much as to one in a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. On an owned farm parcel, the land itself is taxed as land — the park-home exclusion does not apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **ALR RULES IN FULL — this file's core rule set.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond needs ALC approval. Delta's A1 zone mirrors the caps (500 m² farm house; 3,600 m² farm home plate; 90 m² / 186 m² accessory farm dwelling unit), and a new A1 lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and ALC Act approval where applicable (home site severance excepted under ALC policy); A2 likewise sets 8 ha; A3 does not permit subdivision. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s. 5, applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met; none is carried here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact here. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on the buyer's own farm land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land transfers, including an East Delta farm parcel).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program (the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes file) carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. The townhomes siblings' "3% to $3M, 5% above" PTT shorthand is WRONG and is never carried here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (no strata manufactured-home community is verified in East Delta; carried as the standing block).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. Note the ALR bar above: an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Delta frame.** OCP: Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; its Agriculture policies (OCP s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan) apply to Delta's agricultural land, designated "Agriculture (A)". The Delta ZONING bylaw number is CONTESTED in this program and is never asserted here; the parcel's own zone sheet is the read. SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 + 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) permit small-scale multi-unit housing combinations where the zoning applies — **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and it does not displace the ALR / A-zone rules on farm ground. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Servicing / subdivision — held UNCONFIRMED.** Delta DSSS 8288, 2024 adoption is UNCONFIRMED in this program; no Delta servicing standard is quoted as settled here. Wells and septic are diligence items on farm ground: search the well in the provincial GWELLS registry (wells built before the Water Sustainability Act may not be recorded), and Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer (vendor status, previous occupation, farm-sale GST treatment) is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. - **Q: Can an ALR parcel here be turned into bare-land strata lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified; strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval in any event. (§3.)

- **Q: How is a manufactured home on my own farm land registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA label must be attached at the point of sale. The land itself transfers on title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a park elsewhere in BC?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). East Delta has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On an owned East Delta farm parcel there is no park owner; the gatekeepers are the ALC frame and the City zone, not a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What water and flood checks matter on this ground?** Search the well in GWELLS (older wells may be unrecorded), ask for the Authorized Person's sewerage filings, and check flood protection requirements with the City — Delta's low farm land is dyke-protected and the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems and https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
