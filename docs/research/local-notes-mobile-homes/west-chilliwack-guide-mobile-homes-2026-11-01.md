## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — IN FULL, as it governs Cottonwood Village pad tenancies:** the Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and renting both the home and the site falls under the Residential Tenancy Act, not the MHPTA. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad; at Cottonwood the pad itself sits on leased land under a head lease whose terms are CONFLICTED and UNRESOLVED (Section 2), so the buyer is reviewing two layers — pad tenancy plus head lease — not a Form B.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment and consent:** buying in a park assigns the existing pad tenancy — before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment requires the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director): request on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day or consent is deemed given, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser holds the tenancy on the same terms. The assignment rules expressly recognise a park where every pad is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or, for 2+ tenants, at least one who has reached 55) under the Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i) framework — the mechanism by which a 55+ park rule operates, and the reason Cottonwood's 55+ rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **Pad-rent increase caps — verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026), percentages ONLY:** once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped at inflation (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) PLUS a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional**. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** Stated expressly: the Chilliwack mobile-homes page's $550–$950 pad-rent range is UNVERIFIED site copy, and **it is NOT documented in this guide's own research file, so it is not carried or relied on here.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules and closure/conversion/compensation — only as official RTB/provincial sources support:** park rules form part of the tenancy agreement, must apply to all tenants fairly, be clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, be properly noticed, and not change a material term; since the April 30, 2024 regulation change, park rules can change only once a year on three months' notice on an RTB-approved form. A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all permits and approvals in place first, give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Added compensation where a home cannot be relocated and bad-faith closure compensation are as the Province's June 6, 2018 bulletin states them. How any closure framework interacts with a head lease on leased land at a specific park: UNCONFIRMED — a lawyer question on the park's own documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2018MAH0066-001111
- **Age rules — sources discipline:** park age rules are carried ONLY from operator/Form B/registered-bylaw sources; listing-stated ages are labelled listing-stated and conflicts stay UNRESOLVED. In a STRATA, only a 55+ bylaw is valid (next bullet); a park's 45+ or 40+ marketing number (Country Park Village, Rainbow Estates — not this ground) survives, if at all, only as a park rule under the Human Rights Code 55+ assignment framework, which is written around age 55 — read the actual park rules, not the marketing number. - **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a manufactured home sale, transfer or purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership (BC Registries); the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale.
