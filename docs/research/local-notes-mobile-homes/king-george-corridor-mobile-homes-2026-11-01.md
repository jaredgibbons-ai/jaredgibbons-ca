## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (it governs the pad form in Section 2, not Sea Ridge Bays' strata form).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — Sea Ridge Bays' form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park-home sale the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the **director** — never "arbitrator"), requested on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt; silence = deemed consent; NO fee for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fairly applied to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — and no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. (The $210 Sea Ridge Bays figure in Section 2 is a listing-stated STRATA fee, not pad rent, and is never presented as a rate.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice, rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A manufactured-home sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; **no flat claim either way is stated as fact** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers — including a Sea Ridge Bays lot).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. Stated expressly: **the $78,000 figure is NOT met as a computation in this slug's own siblings** — the corridor acreages and guide-lots siblings mention it only as the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling's figure (its only home), following $93,000; reconciliation OPEN. **The shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" is NOT met as a stated rate in this slug's own siblings** — the corridor townhomes sibling flags a compressed "Over $3M — 5%" page row only as a compression, with no such row documented in its research; never carried here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (this block GOVERNS Sea Ridge Bays).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP: Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 — the corridor's designations now come from the 2050 OCP (updated by Corporate Report R246, endorsed December 2, 2024), not the 1995 concept plan; check the parcel's current designation and zone in COSMOS. Secondary suites: one per unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach/garden suite. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 by stream class — no single figure. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing

- **Q: Is Breakaway Bays on 160 Street a 55+ park?** UNCONFIRMED — the sources conflict directly: a park directory calls it age-restricted (55+, 345 sites directory-stated), while MLS listings state "NO AGE RESTRICTION" and describe a family park. - **Q: In a pad-rental park, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — a home on a rented pad is not land ownership; your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than 2.3% + proportional amount (2026) / 2.2% + proportional amount (2027) — percentages only; no dollar figure is published in this file. At Sea Ridge Bays there is no park owner to approve you; strata purchases do not need council approval to buy, though the bylaws bind you from completion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park closes or converts?** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
