## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, consent and park rules.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned (not newly granted): the seller must give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and written park rules before consent is requested; assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement and may be changed only with 3 full months' notice, and only if fair, clear, properly noticed and not changing a material term. A park's 55+ rule operates through park rules and assignment consent (the assignment rules expressly recognise a park reserved for tenants 55+, via Human Rights Code s.10(2)(b)(i) as the Surrey-wide sibling records) — not through a strata bylaw. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (BC all-items CPI, 12-month average change) plus a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** **No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file** — no live-page pad-rent range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is flagged or adopted here, and no directory lot-rent range for any Newton park is quoted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — provincial Act plus Surrey policy, only as official sources support.** Provincially: conversion of all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, requires all necessary permits and approvals in place before notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' notice, rent to the move-out date. Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy brochure states the same frame for Surrey residents (12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after all permits and approvals, $20,000 on or before the move-out date). **Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act** — park residents rely on the provincial Act and the City policy, not that bylaw. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, with the land's value excluded from the home's assessed value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly only that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — own-land placement, not a resale pad purchase. Confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure with a lawyer or notary before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for registered land transfers where PTT does apply.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form block — only if a community is a bare-land strata (none verified in East Newton).** Form B diligence (fee, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** **OCP: Surrey Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is CURRENT; Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current.** The East Newton North NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and East Newton South's designations were superseded by it, with the NCP still guiding amenities and infrastructure. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; it has a Manufactured Home Residential Zone (RM-M) that permits manufactured home parks — the municipal zoning home for park tenure, applied parcel by parcel (no park's zone in East Newton was confirmed on a City source in this program: UNCONFIRMED per park). Zone minimums in the research include R3 at 464 m² for a single-family home and 557 m² for a duplex, R4 at 250 m², R5 at 200 m² / 225 m² duplex. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/planning-reports/PLR_7915-0417-00.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites / STRs.** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH framework in place Jul 8, 2024); the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house, and Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal habitable area. SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Short-term rentals in Surrey are owner-only, with a licence at $358/yr as carried in the siblings, the City's 2024 corporate report's proposed $350/yr disclosed alongside it. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986) is VERIFIED as carried in the program ledger. Streamside setbacks are in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 by stream class — **NO single riparian figure is stated**. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the level the siblings cite from CRA: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer for a specific manufactured home is UNCONFIRMED until vendor and home status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/east-newton-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **First-home programs.** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate = new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out on a straight line to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. Verification status in this file: the East Newton condos sibling cites CRA News No. 122 directly — carried as CRA-sourced. A resale park home is not new housing for the FTHB rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
