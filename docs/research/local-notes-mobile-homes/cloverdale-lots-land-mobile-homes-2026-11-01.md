## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**Topic file — NO roster, stated plainly at the top. This file counts no park, names no park as Cloverdale land stock, and carries no pad-rent figure. A manufactured home on a rented pad is not land and creates no entry in any land roster.**

### Why there is no park here — the tenure split, stated once

- **Pad ground is NOT land.** In a manufactured home park the tenant owns the manufactured home and rents the site (pad) under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Plainly: **a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** There is no lot to roster, no land title to transfer, and no bare-land market attached to the pad-rental form. Surrey's pad-rental parks are documented at home in their own area grounds (Westwood Court → Fleetwood home; Westwood Estates → Queen Mary Park home; Newton MHP tenure record → West Newton home) — none is Cloverdale land stock, and none is counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Own-land manufactured stock is a different stock.** A manufactured home on land the buyer owns (fee-simple lot, or a bare-land strata lot) is a land purchase. Cloverdale's land that actually trades on this topic — serviced infill lots, lot-value properties, Clayton NCP assembly parcels and ALR acreage — is documented ONCE in this slug's lots-and-land home (`~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/cloverdale-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md`), cross-referenced here per document-once and never re-rostered. No bare-land strata manufactured-home community is verified on Cloverdale ground in this slug's research or siblings. (Research file above; lots-and-land sibling.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response due by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice, and enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file, and no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the dispute window is 15 days, and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the settled files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; **no flat claim either way is stated as fact** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly only that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. Confirm with a lawyer/notary for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, the form this land topic actually trades).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fully residential land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified on Cloverdale ground here).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP Bylaw 21900 (never 18020). Zoning Bylaw 12000; the July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes replaced RF-12/RF-12C with R4 and RF-SD with R6 — older land material using RF-12/RF-SD names uses pre-2024 zone names. SSMUH: maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites: one per dwelling unit, never in a coach house or garden suite, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses/garden suites: 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR. Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986). Short-term rentals: owner-hosted, primary residence only, business licence required — the licence figure carries a **$358 / $350 conflict in the sources, disclosed and resolved nowhere in this file**. Floodplain: Surrey Part 7A by stream class — NO single figure. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** ALR: subdivision requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions; principal residence maximum 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate, and bare land alone is not new housing either. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a manufactured home on this lots-and-land page?** No. The feed is vacant land only — serviced infill lots, lot-value properties, Clayton NCP assembly parcels and ALR acreage. A pad-rental park home never appears as a land listing, because **the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad — there is no land to list**. (§2 above; https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html)
- **Q: If I place a manufactured home on a Cloverdale lot I buy, what governs?** The land transfer runs through the Land Title Office with PTT on the general tiers (§3), the Registry record for the home, and the City's zone/plan position for the parcel in COSMOS. The Province's newly built home exemption expressly includes a manufactured home placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land (not previously occupied) — confirm eligibility with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Are the Cloverdale medians land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category and no manufactured-home category; the §1 medians ($1,212,500 detached; $740,000 attached) are residential community context only. Price a lot on its own zone, plan designation, services and comparable land sales. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Why does Serpentine show zero listings but farm parcels appear on the feed?** The residential table does not capture farm product: Serpentine's 0 / 0 rows are residential-split zeros, while Serpentine-labelled farm acreage trades as land. Never quote the zero as "no Serpentine market," and build no market claim on either filing of the Serpentine name. (§1 above.)
- **Q: How do I check a lot's zoning and subdivision potential?** In COSMOS — current zoning (post-July 2024 names: R4/R6 replaced RF-12/RF-SD), OCP designation, secondary plan status, servicing mains, road dedications and riparian setbacks. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html
- **Q: Can I subdivide an ALR parcel on this feed?** Only with ALC permission outside limited exceptions, and housing follows the 500 m² / 90 m² / 186 m² frame in §3. Check ALR status parcel by parcel on ALC mapping — which feed parcels are in the ALR is UNVERIFIED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What extra costs sit on a land purchase?** DCCs, Metro regional charges, latecomer agreements, frontage works and Hydro connections are named in the live page's FAQ as categories — but the rates and the GST answer for a specific parcel are UNVERIFIED in this file. Get both from the City and a CPA/lawyer before removing subjects. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-lots-land.html
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put multiple homes on a Cloverdale lot?** SSMUH density (§3) applies to owned lots, not to pad tenancies — **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — and any multi-unit build still needs the parcel's zone, plan and servicing position confirmed in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Which school serves a Cloverdale lot?** The §1 schools are confirmed by name and address in the Ministry file, but SD36 catchments are address-based and no locator result for a Cloverdale address is on record — run the exact address.
