## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — including every strata condo on the band page itself — is dealt with through the land title system instead. The band page's own stock is strata, not MHPTA stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A strata condo on the band page transfers on title, with no Registry filing — one of the cleanest differences between the two purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file**. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates and the band's worked examples — inputs + tier math stated.** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A purchase under $500,000 meets only the first two tiers and never the 3% tier or the further 2% tier. **Band anchor — $500,000, fully residential, no exemption: 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = $8,000.** Worked in-band example — $400,000, fully residential, no exemption: 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $200,000 ($4,000) = **$6,000**. Program anchors above the band, for scale: $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. The wrong PTT shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" was grep-checked in this slug's own siblings and was **NOT met there** — stated explicitly, not flagged against this slug. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions at this band — as the band research states them.** First time home buyers' program: where all conditions are met, the purchaser is exempt from PTT on the first $500,000 of the purchase price; a full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), and a partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere on this page is inside the full-exemption value range on price alone (the program's other conditions still have to be met). Newly built home exemption: full-exemption threshold $1,100,000, with complete elimination at $1,150,000 (effective April 1, 2024) — a qualifying newly built condo anywhere on this page is also inside that threshold on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (this block GOVERNS the band page's own strata stock, and any bare-land strata purchase).** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, the Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **No single city frame governs a band file.** Zoning, OCP, SSMUH, servicing, tax-penalty and registry positions are those of the community the specific unit sits in, carried in that community's own files — none is stated band-wide in this file, and SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies in any community. (Standing city frames, per-community files.)
- **Mortgage insurance at this band — CMHC, as this band's research documents it.** Mortgage loan insurance is required in Canada when a homebuyer makes a down payment of less than 20% of the purchase price, it protects the lender rather than the buyer, and premium rates range from **0.6% to 4.5%** of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** At the general level CRA sources support: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. **Sibling check material: this slug's acreages sibling notes a March 12 date in-line in some sources while stating March 13 governs — flagged here as a conforming-pass item wherever a March 12 assent statement is met, never carried; March 13, 2026 per CRA governs.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED — confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects. Chattel treatment, where a lender applies it, is a different loan from a strata-condo mortgage at the same price. (Standing brief.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why don't mobile homes show up on the condos-under-$500K page?** Form, not price: the page is a condos-only feed of apartment-style strata units, and a pad-rental manufactured home is not a strata condo — it is a chattel home on an assigned pad tenancy, sold through the Manufactured Home Registry, not on title. Park homes in this price range are found through the parks' own home files (§2 cross-references), and their band status is UNCONFIRMED per home in this file. (Section 2.)
- **Q: At the same $450K headline price, what actually differs between a band condo and a park home?** Almost everything except the number: the condo buyer owns a strata lot (Form B, strata fees, registered bylaws, PTT per §3, title transfer); the park buyer owns a home as a chattel and rents the pad (assigned tenancy, park-owner consent on Form 10, park rules, pad rent on top of any loan payment, Registry transfer, and PTT on the home-only purchase UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule). Compare total monthly cost and tenure security, not list prices. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Why is $500,000 a clean financing line?** At $500,000 or less the minimum down payment is a flat 5%; one dollar over it, the portion above $500,000 needs 10% down — and with less than 20% down, mortgage loan insurance is required, at CMHC premium rates of 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: How much PTT do I pay on a condo on this page?** With inputs + tier math: at the $500,000 ceiling, $8,000 with no exemption (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $300,000); at $400,000, $6,000. A qualifying first-time buyer is exempt on the first $500,000 and is inside the $835,000 full-exemption value range on price alone — the program's other conditions still apply. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up on a park home I compare against this page?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only; this file states no pad dollar figure). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me, and what if it closes?** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10 (response by day 10, silence = deemed consent, no fee); on a conversion, the landlord needs all permits first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date. A strata purchase needs no council approval to buy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: A card in this band says "55+ community" — can I rely on it?** No — that is listing text on one visible Langley City card. An age restriction is confirmed only from the strata's registered bylaws / Form B, and since November 24, 2022 only a 55+ bylaw is valid in a strata in any event (SPA ss.123.1/123.2). In a park, the age rule is a park rule in the tenancy documents — a different document again. https://jaredgibbons.ca/condos-under-500000.html
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home under $500K?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific, and chattel loans are not insured-mortgage equivalents of a strata purchase. Confirm financing for the specific home and its pad tenure before removing subjects. (Standing brief.)
- **Q: How many condos are under $500,000 right now?** No sourced number exists: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, combines condos with townhomes, and has no mobile-home category, and no pagination count appeared in the page text read — so no band count, share or median is stated here. Work from the specific comparable listings and the strata documents. (Research file §2/§6.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a unit on this page?** There is no band answer: catchments are address-based in every district the feed touches, and no locator run for a band address is on record in this file — run the exact address in that district's locator before relying on a school.
