## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: NONE — Barnston Island is unincorporated. Most of the island is Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A; the southeast portion is Barnston Island Indian Reserve No. 3 (Katzie First Nation). Mailing addresses use "Surrey," but no part of the island is inside the City of Surrey. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- What it is: a Fraser River island between Surrey (Port Kells) and Pitt Meadows, ringed by a dike that doubles as the island's main road, reached only by a free 5-minute ferry. Whether FVREB's official Paragon area list carries a "Barnston Island" sub-area under any parent is UNVERIFIED (board list not openly published; needs Paragon confirmation). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- Access / transit: the Barnston Island Ferry crosses Parson's Channel from 104 Ave in Port Kells; it is a free provincial inland ferry (barge + tug, capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers), runs on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m. (to 12:55 a.m. weekends). There is no bridge and no bus service on the island itself. School-bus boarding priority on school days: UNVERIFIED — not stated on the Province's ferry page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- Nearby areas: Port Kells (Surrey) at the ferry landing; Pitt Meadows across the main Fraser channel (no direct connection); Katzie's main reserve in Pitt Meadows. (Research file §1.)
- Planning: Metro Vancouver's Electoral Area A Official Community Plan (2018) sets the vision of Barnston Island as a thriving agricultural community; Metro Vancouver — not a municipality — is the planning authority for the non-reserve lands. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- Flood: the whole island is low Fraser River floodplain protected by a perimeter dike; the dike is also the main transportation route. Flood construction requirements for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- ALR: the island is predominantly farmland — Metro Vancouver's 2019 Agricultural Viability Study was commissioned precisely because agriculture is the island's land base and economy. Exact ALR percentage: UNVERIFIED. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- Schools: School District 36 (Surrey) serves Barnston Island; island children cross by ferry to their assigned Surrey schools. **Settled locator chain, cited on its single-address basis only:** for the island test address 52 Dyke Road (locator range 2–328 Dyke Rd), the SD36 School Locator (run 2026-11-01) returned **Bothwell Elementary → École Fraser Heights**. - Parks / services: Metro Vancouver's Barnston Island Regional Park provides riverfront rest areas and protects riparian habitat; the dike-top perimeter road is the island's walking/cycling loop. There are no shops, recreation facilities or medical facilities on the island — all services are in Surrey (Port Kells/Fleetwood) or Pitt Meadows, via the ferry. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists on this island; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only manufactured-home form met on the island (§2) — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — this slug's research documents no pad range, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. The 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on a buyer's own island parcel, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land transfers, the island form).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples, inputs stated (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 = **$14,000** (a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that price point: $6,000, per the program anchors); $1,500,000 = **$28,000**; $2,000,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified on this island and no strata product was identified).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Planning / servicing frame — Electoral Area A, NOT a city frame.** Planning authority for the non-reserve lands is Metro Vancouver under the Electoral Area A OCP (2018), whose vision is a thriving agricultural community; there is no municipal zoning bylaw, no municipal servicing bylaw and no city SSMUH frame to carry for this ground, and no servicing bylaw number is asserted (FVRD-style and municipal numbers are never imported here). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** in any case. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the settled general level.** At the general level CRA sources support (GI-003 level): GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED — confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects.
- **Property taxes / holding cost — rural regime.** As unincorporated Electoral Area A land, property taxes are collected under the rural (provincial) system rather than by a city, with Metro Vancouver and school levies on top; rural owners receive their notice from the province's Surveyor of Taxes Office. For rural-area properties the Province's published dates are: notice mailed May 31, deadline to pay July 2, a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance on July 3, and an additional 5% penalty on November 1. Parcel-specific tax figures and the current-year breakdown: UNVERIFIED — confirm with BC Assessment / the Surveyor of Taxes for the specific property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sale — where met.** BC court-ordered sales follow the Supreme Court process (Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). REGISTRY LEDGER: no registry position is asserted for Electoral Area A ground in this file (the ledger's Surrey position — NONE, mapping UNVERIFIED — is a City of Surrey position and is not imported onto island ground). Form B availability and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on Barnston Island?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park of any tenure form on the island, so there is no rented-pad stock here at all. (§2.)
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Surrey?** No. It uses Surrey mailing addresses, but the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A, plus the Katzie First Nation's Barnston Island IR No. 3 — services, taxes, building permits and planning run through Metro Vancouver / the Province, not Surrey City Hall. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: How do residents get on and off the island?** Only by the free Barnston Island Ferry from 104 Ave in Port Kells — about 5 minutes across Parson's Channel, on demand within operating hours (roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m., to 12:55 a.m. weekends), capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers. It does not run all night, and crossings can be interrupted by river traffic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Can I buy a small lot and build a house on Barnston Island?** Most of the island is agricultural land under an OCP vision of a farming community; building is tied to farm parcels and ALR rules, so a parcel-by-parcel check with Metro Vancouver and the ALC is essential. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Where do island kids go to school?** School District 36 (Surrey) serves the island; children ride the ferry to their assigned Surrey schools — for the island test address 52 Dyke Road (locator range 2–328 Dyke Rd), Bothwell Elementary and École Fraser Heights (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). Port Kells Elementary serves Port Kells / landing-side addresses, not island addresses. School-bus boarding priority: UNVERIFIED. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Is Barnston Island in a floodplain?** Yes — it is a low Fraser River island protected by a perimeter dike that also serves as its main road; confirm dike condition reports, flood construction levels and insurance availability early in any purchase. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a park off-island instead?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). The island itself has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On an island freehold parcel there is no park owner and no approval — title, ALR and flood checks replace it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a manufactured home on its own island parcel?** The parcel transfers on title like other land, but lender and insurer treatment of the specific home (age, construction form, foundation) — and flood/dike insurance on this ground — is UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program. Confirm both before removing subjects.
