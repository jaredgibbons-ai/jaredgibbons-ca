## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block — the pad-tenancy law, in full (it governs the Abbotsford pad purchases cross-referenced in §2; no pad exists in Matsqui itself).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies: a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on is a tenant under the Act; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead; where both the home and the site are rented to the same person, the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. **CORE DISCIPLINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (pad tenure).** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director," never "arbitrator." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — the one listing-stated figure in §2 (Georgian Park) is labelled listing-stated for one home, no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, and no range is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window applies, and additional amounts — assessed value minus $20,000 where the home could not be moved, plus $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, if the landlord does not proceed — are stated only as the program's settled files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. On Matsqui's own parcels the tax posture is the farm/rural one in the taxes bullet below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **ALR BLOCK IN FULL — Matsqui Prairie is ALR farm ground, and this block governs any manufactured home sited on it:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — which is the statutory reason no bare-land strata park can be created out of prairie ALR ground. Residence caps: generally no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY within the principal residence; an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. The City's A1 zone mirrors the added-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, at the same 90 m² / 186 m² caps; one secondary suite per lot to a maximum floor area of 100 m² is also permitted in A1. **Manufactured homes on farm ground are permitted only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route plus the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) route; a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED.** A manufactured home does not bypass any of this: whether a manufactured unit qualifies as the principal residence, the suite, or the accessory farm dwelling on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC/City question — UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until its ALR status and approvals are read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: In Paradise / Georgian / Maplewood, what am I actually buying?** The manufactured home only — a chattel — plus an assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. You do not own land in any of the three. Pad rent rises only by the formula in §3; Georgian Park's one listing-stated figure ($782/month for a specific home) is not a tariff. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes

Rent: capped by formula, once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice — 2.3% + proportional for 2026, 2.2% + proportional for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What if an Abbotsford park is redeveloped?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, early departure on 10 days' notice, and the additional-compensation route in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Matsqui farm parcel as the main residence?** The frame allows ONE principal residence per ALR parcel at 500 m² or less, with the A1 zone listing Single Detached Dwelling as a principal use — but whether a specific manufactured unit satisfies the residence, siting and servicing requirements on a specific parcel is a parcel-level ALC/City confirmation: UNCONFIRMED until read for that parcel. Manufactured homes on farm ground run only via ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / Policy L-25 + NARU (§3). The Registry/CSA rules still apply to the home itself. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Can I add a second manufactured home as a farm-worker dwelling?** Only through the A1 accessory route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). A suite is permitted only within the principal residence (A1 also permits one suite per lot to 100 m² as the research states the zone). A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED — do not assume a second unit can be added; read the zone and the ALR status first. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210

- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, CSA label attached at sale.
