## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (and why it does NOT govern the Cottages lots).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. A Cottages at Bridal Falls lot is freehold strata land the buyer owns, so the Strata Property Act — not the MHPTA — governs it (§2). The MHPTA block below is the settled program block, carried in full because a Bridal Falls buyer comparing a pad park elsewhere in the valley needs it stated correctly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator" — no "arbitrator" wording appears in this slug's research file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all; and there is no pad rent at the Cottages in any case (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. Whether a specific cottage/park-model unit on a Cottages strata lot is a registered manufactured home, an RV, or a strata improvement is a per-unit record check — UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. **Taxes on this ground follow the RURAL regime (FVRD electoral area, not a municipality): under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11, a 5% penalty applies after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31 — Chilliwack's municipal penalty dates and Mission's flat 10% do NOT apply here.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home (program standing record)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, including a Cottages strata lot).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential strata-lot purchase with no exemption = **$8,000** (math: $2,000 + $6,000). Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (this is the block that governs the Cottages at Bridal Falls).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — for a bare-land strata lot the Form B package is what establishes the fees and registered rules); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Planning / zoning / servicing — FVRD Area D frame.** The FVRD sets zoning for Electoral Area D; the specific FVRD zoning bylaw and the zone of any Bridal Falls parcel are UNVERIFIED (the Cottages lots' CD1 zoning is a listing-record statement in §2, not a bylaw citation). The guide-stated Popkum-Bridal Falls OCP (Bylaw No. 1501, January 2021) is UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record (§1). **Servicing: FVRD servicing/bylaw numbers are UNCONFIRMED per the verified ledger — no number is asserted, and Chilliwack's bylaws (including its UNCONFIRMED candidate 3055, which was NOT adopted per the ledger, and the City of Chilliwack webpage's contrary "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" assertion, flagged wherever met) are never borrowed for FVRD ground.** Servicing in an electoral-area community is parcel-specific (the Cottages lots are recorded as serviced — gas, hydro, water, sewer — per listing records; any other parcel's water/sewer service is UNVERIFIED). **SSMUH: no FVRD SSMUH position is documented in this slug's research for Area D parcels — UNCONFIRMED here; Chilliwack's SSMUH Bylaw 5395 does NOT apply and is not imported. - **ALR block (where ALR ground is met — parcel status UNVERIFIED per parcel).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area, a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, an additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), subject to local bylaws. Farm classification in the full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is the Cottages at Bridal Falls a mobile home park?** No — it is the opposite tenure. Listing records state Title to Land: Freehold Strata and subdivision name The Cottages At Bridal Falls: you own the strata lot, pay strata fees, and the Strata Property Act governs. A pad rental (own the home, rent the land, MHPTA governs) is a different purchase. (§2 above; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/bridal-falls-guide-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What exactly would I own on a Cottages lot?** The bare-land strata lot (recorded as serviced, with a concrete pad), and whatever cottage/RV is included in the specific sale — listing text alone does not establish that, and the strata plan number, lot-specific fees and registered pet/age/use rules are UNCONFIRMED in this file. Diligence is the Form B strata package, not the resort marketing. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: Is the resort a seniors community?** No source says so — it describes itself as a year-round resort ownership community with cottages, a clubhouse and a pool, and its listings record pets and rental-investment use: a recreational strata profile, not a 55+ bylaw profile. http://www.discoverbridalfalls.com/ (55-plus sibling in FILE STATUS.)
- **Q: Which government do I call about zoning or building here — Chilliwack?** No — Bridal Falls is FVRD Electoral Area D, so zoning, building and plan questions go to the FVRD; City of Chilliwack zoning does not apply, and the live page's Chilliwack-plan framing is UNVERIFIED site copy (FILE STATUS). (Research file §6.)
- **Q: If I buy in a pad park elsewhere in the valley, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up (in a pad park, not at the Cottages)?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). At the Cottages there is no pad rent; strata fees are set by the strata's budget instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. A strata-lot purchase has no park-owner consent step; its gates are the Form B package and the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a pad park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. That MHPTA closure frame does not apply to a strata lot you own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How would a manufactured-home purchase be registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. Whether a specific Cottages unit is on that registry is a per-unit check, UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which school serves a Bridal Falls address?** Rosedale Traditional Community (50850 Yale Rd Rr 1, Rosedale) is the Ministry-listed school on this side of SD33 and no school is in Bridal Falls itself; address-level catchments, including the grades 9–12 school, are UNVERIFIED — confirm the exact address in the SD33 School Locator. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
