## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **ALR block (full).** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (strata-plan deposit is a subdivision — ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Housing: one principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), suite ONLY inside the principal residence, one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (>40 ha); more/larger requires ALC approval; local bylaws may be more restrictive. Manufactured homes on farm ground: only via ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + Non-Adhering Residential Use route; general farm-worker entitlement UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (full three-tier form) + the page error:** $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. **The live page's flat "generating $2,500+ in qualifying annual farm income" line is INCOMPLETE against the regulation — flagged here as this slug's own documented defect, owned by this file.** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html

- **MHPTA — pad purchases only.** Own a manufactured home + rent the site = MHPTA; own the land = land title system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment / consent (s.28 / Form 10).** Tenancy assigned on sale; seller gives the proposed purchaser any written tenancy agreement + park rules before seeking consent; prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order) on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence = deemed consent, NO consent fee, same terms on assignment. "Director", never "arbitrator". https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, CPI + proportional formula. **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure (s.42).** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 on or before the effective date, early exit on 10 days' notice, 15-day dispute window, additional assessed-value + $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as settled files state them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **MHR + CSA.** Sale/transfer/purchase effective only if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer filed via a qualified supplier; CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed like other residential property, land value excluded in a non-reserve park / on another's land; Home Owner Grant yearly for a principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT — pad-resale UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule; both flat page claims UNVERIFIED.** Newly built exemption includes a manufactured home "placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — new home on own land only. Confirm with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land farm transfers).** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers + further 2% above $3M. Anchors with inputs + tier math: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M → $93,000 incl. the further 2% tier** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). $78,000 at $3,200,000 is CORRECT, never flagged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
