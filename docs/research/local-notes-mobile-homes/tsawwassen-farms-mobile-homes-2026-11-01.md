### Manufactured homes on the buyer's OWN farm land — only as ALC sources support

- A manufactured home placed on ALR farm land the buyer owns is an own-land residence, not a pad tenancy — the MHPTA does not govern it; the ALR residence rules do. The ALC permits a manufactured home as a residence route on ALR land only as its sources support: the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence position, and the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route where the as-of-right rules do not fit. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — no ALC source opened in this program grants one in general terms, and none is stated here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- The residence caps that govern any such home are the ALR caps in §3: principal residence ≤500 m², a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha), with Delta bylaws also required to permit it. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists on this ground; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the farm-ground form — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. This applies to a manufactured home sold on farm ground exactly as to one in a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land transfers, including a farm parcel).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. The $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling (a different slug) — reconciliation OPEN; this file follows $93,000 and does not meet the $78,000 figure on this ground. The townhomes siblings' "3% to $3M, 5% above" shorthand is WRONG wherever met and is never carried here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (no strata form exists on this ground; carried for completeness).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **FULL ALR BLOCK — the governing rules on this ground.** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision — ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction); a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha, where its conditions are met and Delta bylaws also permit it. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta farm zoning (number flagged, never asserted).** The research cites the A1 Agriculture zone: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); subdivision in A1 (s. 10.10.8) requires OCP compliance and ALCA approval where applicable, with an 8 ha minimum lot size created (home site severance exception under ALC policy); A2 sets the same 8 ha minimum and A3 does not permit subdivision. The zoning bylaw NUMBER is CONTESTED in this program and is not asserted. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm classification — full three-tier form.** BC Assessment minimum gross farm income (farm gate): $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if over 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **SSMUH / servicing — Delta frame.** SSMUH Bylaws 8394 + 8395 govern single-detached and duplex lots, not ALR farm parcels, and **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**. DSSS 8288 adoption is UNCONFIRMED (see FILE STATUS). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — a March 12 statement met anywhere is a conforming-pass item, never carried): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate; a farm parcel is not new housing either. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park on Tsawwassen farm land?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park on the Agriculture (A)-designated edges, so there is no rented-pad stock on this ground at all. (Section 2; research file.)
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Delta farm I buy?** Only as the ALR rules support: as the parcel's residence under the ALC caps (principal ≤500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 position or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application — and Delta bylaws must also permit it. A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If the home sits on my own farm land, do I pay pad rent?** No — pad rent exists only in a park tenancy. On your own land the holding costs are the parcel's property tax and insurance; the trade-off is that the ALR residence caps and farm-classification income thresholds in §3 govern what you can build and keep. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How do I check whether a Tsawwassen-edge parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the residence caps, the additional-residence size limits and most use permissions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide the farm into manufactured-home lots?** No on ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and A1 subdivision needs a minimum 8 ha lot, OCP compliance and ALCA approval where applicable; A3 does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** The full three tiers: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha, and $10,000 under 0.8 ha (farm gate prices). https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How is a manufactured home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen RV Resort farm stock or a place to buy a farm home?** Neither — it is a tenure record in its Tsawwassen North home: an all-ages RV resort on Tsawwassen First Nation Lands, not in the City of Delta, never a manufactured-home park on this evidence. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9
- **Q: Which schools serve a farm address?** SD37 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a farm address is on record, so a farm parcel's catchment is UNVERIFIED — run the exact address. The one town sample on record (1299 56 St → Cliff Drive Elementary / South Delta Secondary) is single-address only. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
