## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (and the reason pad ground is not land).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. A Ladner land buyer is in the second world — title, zoning and ALR rules — not the first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (pad sales only).** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. No park is verified on Ladner ground (§2), so this is carried for completeness for a buyer who widens the search. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator" — no "arbitrator" wording is met in this slug's research or siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. This registry step applies to the HOME even when the buyer also buys the land under it — the land transfers through the land title system and the home through the Registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home / on an own-land home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; the Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. Delta 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% on outstanding balances after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes (research file §4)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — directly relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on a Ladner lot the buyer owns, and not a rule for a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers — the Ladner land case).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. No $78,000-at-$3.5M figure is met in this slug's siblings — its lots/acreages siblings carry the correct $93,000 anchor. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (the block that governs Ladner's bare-land strata stock such as South Pointe).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Delta frame.** OCP consolidated Bylaw 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules — farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s.1.5.1); any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an error wherever met (Schedule D.1 belonged to the former Bylaw 3950). **ZONING number CONTESTED (City pages cite 7600, 2017 vs an internal "8400, 2025" title) — flagged, never asserted; a parcel's zone is read off the City's current record.** SSMUH Bylaws 8394+8395. - **ALR frame for the rural stock in this feed.** On ALR land: one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum 500 m² total floor area (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Manufactured homes on farm ground are supported only via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route and the Non-Adhering Residential Use route — a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (research file §4)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Ladner I can buy into?** None verified — no pad-rental park is named for Ladner ground in any source in this program (§2). Ladner's manufactured-adjacent stock is bare-land strata (South Pointe) and own-land homes, which are land purchases, not pad tenancies. (Sibling files for this slug.)
- **Q: Why isn't a park pad counted as a "lot" on a lots-and-land page?** Because pad ground is NOT land for the buyer: **a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad**, with no title to the site. A building lot, by contrast, transfers title through the land title system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: If I place a manufactured home on a Ladner lot I own, what governs it?** The land rules: Delta's OCP 8400 (2024) and the parcel's actual zone (zoning number contested — read the City record), plus, on ALR parcels, the ALC residence frame (500 m² principal, suite only inside it, 90 m² / 186 m² additional). The home itself still registers in the Manufactured Home Registry, and the Province's newly built exemption expressly contemplates a manufactured home placed and affixed on vacant land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I strata-split an ALR parcel to create manufactured-home lots?** Depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR under ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6 and needs ALC permission — do not assume a strata split works on farm land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I widen the search to a park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. That machinery does not apply to a titled Ladner lot purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a Ladner land purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000→$8,000; $800,000→$14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $1,500,000→$28,000; $2,000,000→$38,000; $3,500,000→$93,000 including the further 2% tier. GST on a specific lot is UNVERIFIED here — state it expressly in the contract. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (research file §4)
- **Q: What extra checks does a Ladner lot need that a park home doesn't?** Flood Construction Level, diking/floodplain position, geotechnical/preload requirements for delta soils, servicing and subdivision status with the City, and — on rural parcels — ALR status and farm classification (BC Assessment thresholds and the October 31 application deadline in the research). All are parcel-level and UNVERIFIED for any specific lot in this file. (Research file §§4, 6.)
- **Q: Which schools would serve a home built on a Ladner lot?** The Ministry-listed SD37 schools are named in §1 (including Neilson Grove Elementary, 5500 Admiral Blvd — school spelling "Neilson," area spelling "Neilsen"), but the catchment for a specific parcel is UNVERIFIED — SD37 evidence here is map-based only, not a locator run. (Research file §4.)
