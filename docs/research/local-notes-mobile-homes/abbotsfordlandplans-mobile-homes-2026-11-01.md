## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No plan in §1 changes that: an OCP designation is not a tenancy, a zoning or a park approval. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; no fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. This is where the plans layer bites in practice: a park inside a redevelopment plan area still gets the full s.42 process — the plan does not shorten it. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does an OCP designation make a parcel a manufactured-home park site?** No. The OCP sets future land use and rezoning policy; the Zoning Bylaw sets what can be built as-of-right today; neither creates a park, a pad tenancy or a tenure. Check both layers for the specific parcel in the City's property information search or WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: Which OCP bylaw number should I quote — 2500-2016, 2600-2016, or Abbotsforward 2050?** Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT. The live page's "2500-2016" citation and the older "2600-2016" framing are both stale/conflicted — that defect set is owned by this slug (§§1, 3, 5); do not copy either number into an offer or a listing. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there really a Marshall Road Corridor Plan?** UNVERIFIED as an adopted City plan — no City source opened in the research pass confirms a plan of that name, and it is not in the OCP adopted-plan list (UDistrict, City Centre, Historic Downtown, McKee). Verify the corridor's actual plan status with the City before marketing airport/industrial potential on it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsfordlandplans.html
- **Q: If I bought in a park inside a redevelopment plan area, what protects my tenancy?** The MHPTA, in full: assignment on sale (s.28 / Form 10, 10-day rule), park-rule change limits (once a year, 3 full months' notice), the pad-rent cap formula, and for a conversion the s.42 process — permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation. A plan area does not dilute any of it. (§3.)
- **Q: On a rented pad, do I own land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad — that is not land ownership**, whatever the surrounding plan designation says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026, 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can ALR parcels be planned or rezoned into urban subdivisions through the City alone?** No — exclusion needs ALC approval, and inside the ALR the residence frame and A1/A2 minimums govern. The live page's related 72%-ALR claim is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source opened in the research pass (owned defect set, §3). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How is a manufactured home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; the CSA label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What development charges apply if I assemble plan-area land?** UNVERIFIED in this file — no DCC bylaw or rate page was opened in the research pass. Get the current DCC rates for the specific proposal from the City before subjects come off. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a park address?** Catchments are address-based in SD34 and no locator run for a park address is documented in this slug — UNVERIFIED; run the exact address. School names in this program are stated only as Ministry-listed. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
