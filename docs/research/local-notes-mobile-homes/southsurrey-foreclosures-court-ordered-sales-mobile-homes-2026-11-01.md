## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

### The court process — settled treatment, with the research's confirmed core

- **Step 1 — the petition (Rule 21-7(1)).** "A proceeding for foreclosure of the equitable right to redeem mortgaged property or for redemption must be started by petition" in the Supreme Court of British Columbia — Supreme Court Civil Rules, Rule 21-7(1), re-opened on BC Laws and CONFIRMED verbatim in batch 10 of this program (research file §4). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Parties and later interests (Rule 21-7(2)–(4.1)).** All persons whose interest in or claim to the mortgaged property is sought to be extinguished, and all persons against whom relief is sought, must be made respondents — Rule 21-7(2); a collateral mortgage claim / person liable for the debt may be joined — Rule 21-7(3); a person who registers an interest after a certificate of pending litigation is registered need not be served, is bound by orders, and may file a response — Rule 21-7(4); the petitioner must serve the foreclosure informational notice, Form 125, with the petition — Rule 21-7(4.1) (research file §4). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Powers of the court (Rule 21-7(5)(a)–(c)).** The court may make a final order of foreclosure — (5)(a); order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes, with foreclosure of the equity of redemption in default — (5)(b); and determine summarily, or order an account taken and certified, the amount due — (5)(c). The redemption period has no fixed length in the Rule text read — it is the period the court fixes (research file §4). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **The settled framework beyond that core.** The program's settled court-process treatment names Rules 21-7/13-5, Practice Direction PD-66 and Form 60 (the conduct-of-sale / sale-confirmation framework, including certification) as the governing process for BC court-ordered sales. **This slug's research holds the finer mechanics under that framework UNVERIFIED program-wide (see FILE STATUS)** — so no conduct-of-sale, reserve/upset-price, competing-bid or vesting wording is published from this file; get the specific file's bid and approval instructions from the seller's counsel, confirmed by your own lawyer (research file §§4, 6).

### The MHPTA block — for any park home a court file touches

- **MHPTA scope.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park-home sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice, rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province's newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a new-home-on-own-land point, not a resale-on-a-pad point. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M fully residential → $93,000 including the further 2% tier — follow $93,000.** (This slug's townhomes sibling carries a $78,000 anchor for $3.5M and an "Over $3M — 5%" compression wording — both flagged in §5, neither followed.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition in the Supreme Court under Rule 21-7(1), with everyone whose interest is sought to be extinguished made a respondent and the Form 125 information notice served with the petition. (§3)
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** There is no fixed length in the Rule text read — the court fixes it in its order under Rule 21-7(5)(b). Ask which stage the file is at before spending on inspections. (§3)
- **Q: Can the first order already be final?** Yes — Rule 21-7(5)(a) lets the court make a final order of foreclosure; do not assume every file is at an early, reversible stage. (§3)
- **Q: Can someone outbid my accepted offer at the approval hearing?** Treat a competing bid at the approval stage as a real possibility to plan for. The bid-rights mechanics (Rule 13-5 / PD-66) are held UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — including the live page's 9:45 a.m. sealed-bid account, which is NOT adopted here — so get the specific file's instructions from the seller's counsel, confirmed by your own lawyer. (§3; research file §7 Q4)

- **Q: If the court sale is a park home, what else applies?** The MHPTA and the Registry: **you would own the home as a chattel and rent the pad — a rented pad is not land ownership** — with pad assignment/consent machinery on top of the court order, and PTT/GST/Form B treatment UNCONFIRMED for the specific purchase.
