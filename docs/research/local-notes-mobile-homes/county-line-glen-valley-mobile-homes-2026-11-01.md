## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant, assumes the rights and obligations under the Act and the agreement from the assignment date, and the agreement continues on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and this slug's research documents no pad-rent figure for this ground, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the tenant's dispute window is 15 days, and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's settled files state them. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fully residential fee-simple purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000; $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — follow $93,000; the $78,000 figure for $3.5M lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling (a different slug) and was **NOT met in this slug's own siblings**, so it is flagged nowhere here beyond this note. The "3% to $3M, 5% above" shorthand and its "Over $3M — 5%" compression variant are both wrong as flat rates and were **NOT met in this slug's own siblings** — stated explicitly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in County Line Glen Valley).** Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Zoning / OCP — Township frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible and NO 6-unit transit tier applies in the Township — most parcels in this feed exceed the ceiling by a wide margin, so SSMUH generally does not apply to them. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Secondary suites under Bylaw 2500 s.102: maximum 120 m² gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations. A coach house under s.102 is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage, held fee-simple under s.102. For completeness on the Township's residential-compact-lot zone: R-CL under s.403 = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area — no 355 m² figure exists in that section. Servicing: Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. Township short-term rentals: the Township does not issue business licences for STR accommodations; the permitted-use status under Bylaw 2500 is UNVERIFIED beyond that licensing point. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via research file above)
- **ALR housing frame + manufactured homes on farm ground — stated in full.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Residences / land in the ALR cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALCA s.21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239 is the companion strata bar on ALR subdivision). A manufactured home counts toward that frame like any residence: the only routes the ALC sources support for a manufactured home as an additional farm residence are the additional manufactured-home residence provision (ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25) and an approved Non-Adhering Residential Use application. A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. Feed text about Township-approved secondary-home plans or a mobile home as a second dwelling is a document claim to verify against this frame and Township records, not a right established by the listing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (research file above)

- **Q: A listing shows a mobile home as a second dwelling on a farm. Can I rely on that?** No — treat it as a document claim, not a right. On ALR land a manufactured home as an additional residence must fit the ALC frame (principal ≤500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) via the s.34(3) / Policy L-25 route or an approved Non-Adhering Residential Use application; a general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. Verify against ALC and Township records for the PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I did buy in a Langley pad park elsewhere, would I own the land?** No. **You would own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Plainly, a home on a rented pad is not land ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in a Langley park?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only; no pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, where a pad tenancy is involved — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, with a response due by the 10th day and silence deemed consent; no fee may be charged for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Can I put a second home or suite on a Glen Valley acreage?** Parcel by parcel. On ALR land: one principal residence to 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), with a suite only inside the principal residence; under Township Bylaw 2500 a secondary suite is capped at 120 m², one per principal unit. SSMUH does not rescue an acreage plan — eligibility ends at 4,050 m². https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What happens if a park I buy into is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which school serves a Glen Valley home?** SD35 schools confirmed in or near the sub-area by the Ministry file include Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, Wix-Brown Elementary, North Otter Elementary, Peterson Road Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary — but catchments are address-based and no rural locator result is on record, so never promise the school from the sub-area label; run the exact address. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv)
- **Q: Is a manufactured home on my own acreage taxed like a park home?** No — the park-home assessment rule (value excludes the land when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner) is a rented-ground rule. On your own land the parcel is assessed as land plus improvements in the ordinary way; the Manufactured Home Registry record and CSA label still govern the home's own transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
