## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**TOPIC FILE — NO ROSTER. No park, home or second unit is verified, counted or listed as stock in this file. A coach house is not mobile-home stock, not a park, and not strata — this §2 states the form positions a mobile-home buyer must not confuse.**

- **Coach house = fee-simple second unit, NEVER strata.** In Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only — a fee-simple second unit on the same lot as the principal house, never a strata lot, never a bare-land strata lot, never separately titled or separately saleable. Section 100's text contains no size cap for a coach house (full-text search, rules-gaps pass); whether a cap sits in the individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township suite cap 120 m² + the LIVE CONFLICT, flagged not resolved:** s.102 as amended caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap. The Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) states a suite may be located in a townhouse or rowhouse. Both documents are reported with their dates; a buyer relying on either confirms with the Township in writing. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **A manufactured home is a different form again.** It is neither a coach house nor a suite: on the buyer's own land it is own-land stock registered through the Manufactured Home Registry; on a rented pad it is pad tenure — **a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership: the buyer owns the home as a chattel and rents the pad** (THE TENURE LINE). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Coach-house rule (the topic rule, in full).** Fee-simple second unit under Bylaw 2500 s.102 — second storey of a detached garage only — NEVER strata, never separately saleable; PTT is payable once, on the whole fee-simple parcel, not per unit. Suite cap 120 m²; the townhouse-suite conflict (July 2025 s.102 vs Jan 2025 guide) is flagged in §2, not resolved. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block (for the topic's rural ground).** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); principal ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), suite only inside the principal, additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) / ≤186 m² (>40 ha). Manufactured homes on farm ground only via ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + Non-Adhering Residential Use route; general farm-worker entitlement UNCONFIRMED. Farm class three-tier: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha) / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 (under 0.8 ha), by Oct 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **MHPTA — pad purchases only.** Own a manufactured home + rent the site = MHPTA; own the land = land title system. A coach-house purchase is a fee-simple land purchase — the MHPTA does not govern it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment / consent (s.28 / Form 10).** Park tenancy assigned on sale; seller gives the proposed purchaser any written tenancy agreement + park rules first; prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order) on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence = deemed consent, NO consent fee, same terms. "Director", never "arbitrator". https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair, clear, properly noticed, not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, CPI + proportional formula. **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure (s.42).** Permits/approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 on or before the effective date, early exit on 10 days' notice, 15-day dispute window, additional assessed-value + $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as settled files state them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **MHR + CSA.** A manufactured-home sale/transfer/purchase is effective only if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer via a qualified supplier; CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed like other residential property, land value excluded in a non-reserve park / on another's land; Home Owner Grant yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT — pad-resale UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule; both flat page claims UNVERIFIED.** Newly built exemption includes a manufactured home "placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township?** A dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only, under Bylaw 2500 s.102 — a fee-simple second unit on the same lot, NEVER strata. A ground-level cottage or barn loft may be a different legal thing entirely. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I buy the coach house on its own?** No — it is an accessory unit on one fee-simple parcel, not separately titled; it transfers only with the whole parcel, and PTT is payable once on that parcel. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: How big can a Township suite be?** 120 m², no percentage cap — with the live conflict flagged: the Jan 2025 guide says no townhouse/rowhouse suites, the July 2025 s.102 says a suite may be in one. Confirm in writing. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a carriage home the same thing?** Similar building, different bylaw: Township = "coach house" (2500); City of Langley = "carriage home" (3300). The page's detailed City limits are UNVERIFIED — the City bylaw was not opened in this pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: Are the feed's listings all true coach houses?** No — only two of six cards read described the subject in its own terms; the rest were a garden suite, a second home + cabin, a barn loft and a cottage. Check each unit's permit, not its headline word. (§1.)
- **Q: How do I know a marketed coach house is permitted as one?** Ask for its building permit + final inspection and confirm the zone with the right municipality — the page itself warns a marketed coach house may have been approved as a garage, studio or workshop. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: Is a manufactured home a coach house?** No — different form and different registry: a manufactured home transfers through the Manufactured Home Registry (and, on a pad, under the MHPTA); a coach house is part of a fee-simple land title. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can an ALR additional residence sit over a garage?** Yes, on the ALC carriage-house exception on its stated conditions (§2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
