## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — stated in full (background law for any pad tenancy in Richmond; no pad tenancy is verified in Steveston).** The Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site; a home on owned land goes through the land title system. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** The existing pad tenancy is assigned on sale; the seller must give the proposed purchaser any written tenancy agreement and written park rules before asking the park owner to consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order) on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day, silence = deemed consent, NO fee/benefit for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, formula cap (BC CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' written notice; 15-day dispute window + assessed-value/$5,000 amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** Effective only if registered; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership via a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land or on land owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home — Richmond 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source settles PTT on a home-only pad purchase; NO flat claim either way as fact (BOTH flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — for a NEW home on owned land, not a resale on a rented pad. Lawyer/notary confirmation required. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example: $700,000 = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — follow $93,000. **Sibling check, stated explicitly: NO sibling for THIS slug carries a $78,000 figure for $3.5M as its own anchor (the acreages sibling states $93,000 with the tier math and names $78,000 as living ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling; the $78,000 carrier found in the RICHMOND city-wide slug's townhomes sibling belongs to that slug and is flagged in that slug's mobile file, not imported here), and NO sibling for this slug carries the PTT shorthand as its own rate (the townhomes sibling's "Over $3M — 5%" wording is a flagging sentence only).** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Steveston).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports 5-year cycle (5+ lots, since July 1, 2024); minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies. Heritage Alteration Permits govern exterior change inside the Village Heritage Conservation Area. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML

- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in BC?** 2.3% + proportional (2026); 2.2% + proportional (2027), once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice — percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

- **Q: How is a manufactured home registered?** Manufactured Home Registry only, via lawyer/notary with signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer; CSA label at sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
