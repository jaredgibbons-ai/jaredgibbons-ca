## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after receipt, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, NO fee or benefit may be charged; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms, the pad rent stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase does not change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (CPI + proportional amount = levy + regulated-utility change, divided by sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file; the card figures in §5 are individual listings' copy only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits and approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and additional assessed-value / $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (research file §4.)
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. The monthly-cost stack is pad rent + home insurance + tax on the home excluding land + utilities as the park's agreement allocates them — structure only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home (research file §4.)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way as fact (both flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is cross-referenced as such for Langley here).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Langley frame.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500 / SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (NO 6-plex tier), suite 120 m², coach house fee-simple s.102, servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED; the park-zone section of Bylaw 2500 was not opened in this research — UNVERIFIED. City: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED), SSMUH 3284, suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4.)
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — used home generally exempt, new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000. FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home, age/foundation) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects. Card tenure claims (a lease to 2072, a "co-op" park) are exactly the kind of fact that changes a financing answer and must be verified first. (Research file §5.)
- **Foreclosure — where met.** Settled court process (Rules 21-7/13-5, PD-66, Form 60); Form B + PTT/GST on court sales UNCONFIRMED. REGISTRY LEDGER: Langley is NOT in the ledger. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much is pad rent on a Langley mobile home?** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated as fact in this file. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement — current rent, last increase date, and what the rent includes. (Research file §§5, 7.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027; once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice; the proportional amount is park-specific, so ask the park for its last calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment under MHPTA s.28 the pad rent stays the same and the next increase date does not reset; assignment requires the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director) and no consent fee may be charged. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: Do I own the land in a Langley park?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** Pioneer Park is the co-op exception in form — its Aldergrove guide home file governs that tenure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is Pioneer Park really a co-op, and is there really a lease to 2072 in Aldergrove?** Both claims are listing-card copy on this page's feed — flagged in this slug's research as needing title/tenancy verification for those parks, and carried here as UNVERIFIED card claims only. (Research file §5.)
- **Q: Are the parks on this page all 55+?** Most first-page cards describe their parks as a "55+ community" — an age-restriction claim taken from listing copy only, not verified park by park. Langley Grove Estates' settled position is the opposite: "no age restrictions" listing-stated, expressly NOT 55+, at its Otter District home. Confirm each park's written rules. (Research file §5; settled roster.)
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Permits first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What should I check before anything else?** The CSA label — without the original legible CSA decal the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification; label status is a price and timeline fact on every park purchase — and the Manufactured Home Registry record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

- **Q: Which schools serve a park address?** SD35, address-based; no catchment for any specific park address is on record — UNVERIFIED until run in the SD35 locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
