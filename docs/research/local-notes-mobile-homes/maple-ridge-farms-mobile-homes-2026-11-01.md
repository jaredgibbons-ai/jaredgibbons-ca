## 2. THE PARKS AND THE HOMES

**TOPIC FILE — NO ROSTER, stated at the top: no manufactured-home park of any tenure is documented on Maple Ridge farm ground, and this file names no farm parcel as mobile stock. The farm-ground manufactured-home position is a RULES position (§3), not a roster: a manufactured home placed on a farm parcel is a residence on that parcel — on ALR land it must fit the ALC residence rules; on non-ALR rural land it is an own-land home governed by the City's zoning and the Manufactured Home Registry. Nothing in this topic converts a farm parcel into a "park", and no pad tenancy exists on farm ground in any source checked.**

### Manufactured homes on farm ground — only as ALC sources support

- On ALR land, a manufactured home can serve as a residence only within the ALC's residence framework: the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, and the Non-Adhering Residential Use (NARU) application route for a residence that does not fit the as-of-right rules. **A general entitlement to place manufactured housing for farm workers on ALR land is UNCONFIRMED** — no ALC source opened in this program states one, and no listing copy creates one. The residence caps that frame any such placement are in §3 (principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional 90 m² / 186 m²). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Tenure on farm ground, stated plainly: farm parcels in this topic are overwhelmingly **freehold** — a manufactured home on a farm parcel its buyer owns is an own-land home (a land purchase through the land title system, registered in the Manufactured Home Registry), **not a pad tenancy, and the MHPTA pad rules do not govern it**. No strata farm product is a Maple Ridge pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source). (Research file §5.)
- What is NOT farm-ground stock: a manufactured home offered "on a farm" in listing copy still needs its parcel's ALR status, zone and residence compliance checked individually — ALR status controls residences, second dwellings and most use permissions, and the City's own A1–A3 checklist flags that structures in the agricultural zones may require ALC approval. Marketing language is not an ALC approval. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **ALR BLOCK IN FULL (the governing frame for manufactured homes on farm ground).** (1) **No stratification of ALR residences:** residences on ALR land cannot be stratified — ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). A manufactured home on ALR land can never become a bare-land strata lot. (2) **Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC approval.** (3) **Residence caps:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of **500 m² or less**, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a **secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence**; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. (4) **One additional residence** is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, **90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha** — and City of Maple Ridge bylaws must also permit it. (5) **Manufactured homes specifically:** only as the ALC sources support — the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, plus the Non-Adhering Residential Use application route for anything outside the as-of-right rules; **a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED**. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross farm income requirements at farm gate prices: **$2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**; farm applications must be submitted **by October 31** for the following taxation year. A buyer who plans to continue farming must protect the classification — and a residence plan that takes land out of production can put it at risk. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **MHPTA — stated for contrast, because it does NOT govern farm-ground own-land homes.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No pad tenancy is documented on Maple Ridge farm ground, so the pad rules below (assignment, consent, rent caps, closure) apply only if a buyer cross-shops an actual park (Alouette / Val Maria) — they are carried in full so the contrast is checkable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy (pad purchases only).** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (pad purchases only).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules (pad purchases only).** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY (pad purchases only).** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (pad purchases only) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. Maple Ridge Council Policy No. 6.27 adds City-level tenant assistance on a park-redevelopment rezoning. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-627
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry (applies on farm ground too).** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home placed on a farm parcel registers here — placement does not exempt it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; on an owned farm parcel the home is assessed as an improvement on that parcel (and farm classification, above, is the parcel-level tax question). A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on the buyer's own (farm) land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land farm-parcel transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. (PTT on a mixed farm parcel follows the parcel's residential/non-residential split — confirm the exact treatment for the parcel; no farm-specific PTT rate is stated in any source here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (carried for completeness; NO strata form exists on ALR farm ground — see the ALR block's no-stratification rule).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Maple Ridge frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs across the city; the City's A1, A2, A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist is a summary handout only — **the live page's five-zone table (A-1 through A-5, minimum lots 1.8 ha to 8 ha) is UNVERIFIED site copy**: the consolidated bylaw text could not be opened in the research pass (fetch 429), so agricultural zone minimums are UNVERIFIED here and must be read from the bylaw / confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. **The OCP citation is CONTESTED — Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024 — flagged here, resolved nowhere** (this topic's research cites no OCP number itself). The Lougheed Transit Corridor (ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026) ends at 221 St — it does not reach Whonnock or any farm district, and no corridor designation should be priced into a farm parcel. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and SSMUH lot-zoning does not create ALR residence rights — the ALC rules above govern ALR parcels. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Maple Ridge farm?** Only within the residence rules. On ALR land, a manufactured home is a residence: it must fit the ALC framework (principal ≤500 m², suite inside the principal only, or the additional-residence 90 m² / 186 m² route) via the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route, or go through a Non-Adhering Residential Use application — and City bylaws must also permit it. On non-ALR rural land, the City's zoning governs. Either way, check the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. (Section 3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/)
- **Q: Can I add a manufactured home for farm workers as of right?** UNCONFIRMED — no ALC source opened in this program states a general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement. Treat any such claim in listing copy as unverified; the routes that ARE sourced are the s.34(3)/L-25 residence route and a NARU application. (Section 3.)
- **Q: If the manufactured home sits on my own farm parcel, do I rent a pad?** No. You own the land and the home — there is no pad, no pad rent and no MHPTA tenancy. The purchase runs through the land title system, the home registers in the Manufactured Home Registry, and the ALR rules (not park rules) are the constraint. (Sections 2–3.)
- **Q: Can an ALR residence be stratified or sold off separately?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and subdivision of ALR land requires ALC approval. A manufactured home on ALR land can never become a bare-land strata lot. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How big can the homes be?** Principal residence ≤500 m² (with ≤1,000 m² of fill for its construction) and its suite only inside it; one additional residence of ≤90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (parcels >40 ha) where the conditions are met and City bylaws permit. (Section 3.)
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate): $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with the application due by October 31 for the following tax year. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How is a manufactured home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A missing label is a red flag for financing and resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What if I want a park home instead of farm ground?** Then the tenure flips: at Alouette or Val Maria **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — assignment needs the park owner's consent on RTB Form 10 (10-day deemed-consent rule, no consent fee), pad rent can rise only by the capped percentage (2.3% + proportional for 2026; 2.2% + proportional for 2027), and a park conversion needs permits first, 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation. Those parks are documented in their home files, cross-referenced in Section 2. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Does the Lougheed Corridor or SSMUH give a farm parcel extra homes?** No. The corridor ends at 221 St and does not reach the farm districts; SSMUH is a lot-zoning framework that does not create ALR residence rights and does not apply to pad tenancies. ALR parcels follow the ALC rules. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a farm address?** Address-based in SD42 and UNVERIFIED for farm addresses generally — the one settled run is single-address only: 25554 Dewdney Trunk Rd → Webster's Corners Elementary → Garibaldi Secondary.
