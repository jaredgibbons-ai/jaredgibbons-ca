## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — stated in full (it governs every pad tenancy a land search might surface).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned; the seller must give the proposed purchaser any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules before asking the park owner to consent. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order) on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt, silence = deemed consent, NO fee/benefit for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, formula cap (BC CPI + proportional amount). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file — this slug's research documents none, so none is mentioned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' written notice; 15-day dispute window + assessed-value/$5,000 amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond cited provincial pages. **Topic note: a park as a commercial ASSET (the land under the pads) is a commercial purchase, not pad tenancy — the MHPTA block here governs the tenancies inside it, stated plainly; no listing data is carried.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A manufactured-home sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership via a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land or on land owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **ALR housing block (the land side that decides manufactured-home placement on farm ground).** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC. Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission apart from the limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. No stratification of ALR residences (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) applies where stratification is proposed. Manufactured-home residences on farm ground sit only within these caps and the s.34(3)/Policy L-25 + Non-Adhering Residential Use routes noted in §2 — there is no general entitlement. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source settles PTT on a home-only pad purchase; NO flat claim either way as fact (BOTH flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — for placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. Lawyer/notary confirmation required for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (land / own-land / strata-lot transfers — the rates that DO apply to a land purchase).** On fair market value (land + improvements) at registration: 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example: $700,000 = $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 including the further 2% tier** — follow $93,000. **Sibling check, stated explicitly: NO sibling for THIS slug carries $78,000 for $3.5M as its own anchor (the acreages siblings state $93,000 with tier math and name $78,000 as living ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling), and NO sibling carries the PTT shorthand as its own rate — the townhomes siblings' "Over $3M — 5%" wordings are flagging sentences only. (The condos sibling for this slug carries a finer GST framing beyond the program's GI-003-level treatment, flagged by the lots-and-land sibling as a conflict — this file follows the settled general-level GST treatment in the next bullet and carries no finer framing.)** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (research file §4)
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only where a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; its LAND is documented once in its lots home, §2).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports 5-year cycle (5+ lots, since July 1, 2024); minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions) — **a BC age restriction exists only inside a strata bylaw, a park rule or an organization's own documents, never on a raw freehold lot** (the 55-plus sibling's framing for this slug); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Servicing / zoning — NO single frame for a topic file.** No municipality's servicing bylaw, zoning bylaw or OCP is asserted for this slug: servicing and zoning are per-parcel, per-municipality confirmations (the verified ledger's city positions live in the city files). SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.
- **GST — only as CRA sources support, and parcel-specific for land.** Whether GST applies to a specific land sale depends on the seller and the parcel's history: UNVERIFIED for any specific feed parcel — confirm with the buyer's legal professional before writing (research file §4). At general manufactured-home level: GST on new construction, not resales; finer treatment UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. **The rebate applies to new housing, not bare land, and a resale park home is not new housing.** **Sibling check: NO sibling for this slug carries March 12 as its own assent date — sibling mentions are in-line source notes with March 13 governing; no conforming item arises.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The land page shows a cheap "home" in a park — is that a land purchase?** No. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** No title to land transfers; the deal runs under the MHPTA + Manufactured Home Registry (§3), however a feed labels it.
- **Q: Is this page only Fraser Valley land?** No — that is its title, not its feed: the first cards read include Quesnel, Fort St. John, Gibsons and Squamish alongside Langley, Surrey and Mission parcels. Confirm the parcel's actual municipality before treating it as a Fraser Valley option. (§1.)
- **Q: How many homes can I build/place on ALR land?** Generally one principal residence ≤500 m² (suite only inside it), plus at most one additional detached residence (≤90 m² at ≤40 ha; ≤186 m² above), where local bylaws also permit; more requires ALC approval. A manufactured home counts inside that same framework — there is no separate general entitlement. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check ALR status, zoning and servicing for a feed parcel?** ALR status on the ALC maps by PID/address; zoning + servicing from the parcel's own municipality. All three are UNVERIFIED at parcel level in this file — listing text is not a confirmation. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: What PTT applies on a land purchase?** The general tiers in §3 on fair market value at registration (worked $700,000 example = $12,000); GST for a specific parcel is UNVERIFIED until seller/parcel history is confirmed. (§3; research file §4.)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata "lot" in a manufactured-home community — land or pad?** A strata LOT: you buy the lot as a strata interest (Form B, bylaws, strata PTT treatment), documented once in that ground's lots-and-land home. It is not a rented pad and it is not fee-simple land — never price it as either. (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up in BC?** 2.3% + proportional (2026); 2.2% + proportional (2027), once per 12 months on 3 full months' notice — percentages only; no dollar rate exists to quote for an unverified park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park owner have to approve a resale buyer?** Where the MHPTA applies, yes — Form 10, day-10 response, deemed consent on silence, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What if the park under a home is sold or converted?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date; early move-out on 10 days' notice. Buying the park LAND itself is a commercial purchase — different deal, different advice. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage / the first-home GST rebate on a park home found through a land search?** Financing is UNVERIFIED at official-source level (confirm per home/tenure); the FTHB GST rebate is for new HOUSING, not bare land and not a resale park home. (§3.)
