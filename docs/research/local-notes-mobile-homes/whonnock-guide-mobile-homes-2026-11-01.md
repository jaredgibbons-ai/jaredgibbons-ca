## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Whonnock; carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only manufactured-home form met in Whonnock (§2) — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — neither Whonnock research file documents a pad range, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. The 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW manufactured home on a Whonnock buyer's own acreage lot, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land transfers, the Whonnock form).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples, inputs stated (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 = **$14,000** (a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that price point: $6,000, per the program anchors); $1,500,000 = **$28,000**; $2,000,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 fully residential = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Whonnock).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Maple Ridge frame.** Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 (zone minimums in §1). OCP citation CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) — flagged, resolved nowhere; the settled adoption positions carried in this program are OCP updates adopted November 25, 2025 and the Albion Area Plan update adopted October 28, 2025 (the guide page groups Albion with November 2025 — a guide-page error documented in this slug's research, see §5). No area plan for Whonnock was found. Suites: one secondary suite per lot in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area. Servicing: Bylaw 4800-1993 (City source). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** **Lougheed Transit Corridor: ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021), ending at 221 St — it does NOT reach Whonnock.** A contrary reading sits in this slug's research line of work (per the standing brief, the whonnock material carries it, as do the cityofmapleridge research and the north, northwest and west-central Maple Ridge guide research files) — **flagged wherever met, never carried; the ADOPTED position governs**, and this slug's siblings on disk carry the adopted / does-not-reach position. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the settled general level.** At the general level CRA sources support (GI-003 level): GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. State the GST position in the contract. (Standing brief.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED — confirm financing for the specific home and tenure before removing subjects.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Whonnock?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park of any tenure form inside Whonnock, so there is no rented-pad stock here at all. Maple Ridge's parks sit in East Central and Southwest Maple Ridge, documented in their own home files. (§2.)
- **Q: A Whonnock listing offers a modular/manufactured home on acreage — what am I buying?** Land, with the home on it: a fee-simple acreage purchase on title, not a pad tenancy. The manufactured-home diligence still applies to the home itself — Manufactured Home Registry record where one exists, CSA label, age and foundation — on top of the parcel's ALR, well, septic and flood checks. Confirm the exact structure with a lawyer/notary before subjects come off. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I place a new manufactured home on a Whonnock lot I buy?** That is the one manufactured-home scenario the Province's PTT material speaks to expressly: the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied). Zoning, servicing (well/septic), floodplain and — on ALR land — the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / Policy L-25 route all gate the placement parcel by parcel; none is confirmed for any specific Whonnock lot in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a Maple Ridge park instead?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). Whonnock itself has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On a Whonnock freehold acreage there is no park owner and no approval. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Does the Lougheed Transit Corridor change what I can build in Whonnock?** No — the corridor was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021) and ends at 221 St, west of Whonnock's working scope (which starts around 264 St). It does not designate Whonnock parcels. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Whonnock acreage have a suite or a second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, inside the single detached building, at most 40% of gross floor area and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s.32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019

(Research file §6.)
