## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules — and the park-age point that governs Lismore and Windmill.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. A park's age rule is a park rule under this framework, not a strata bylaw: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) lets a park owner refuse consent to an assignment or sublet where no proposed purchaser or subtenant meets the age requirement in a park where every site is reserved for a tenant who has reached 55 (or 2+ tenants at least one of whom has), as set out in s. 10(2)(b)(i) of the BC Human Rights Code — which is why each Hope park's exact age must come from its own written rules, and why the inventory's unstated ages stay UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute a closure notice within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. District of Hope taxes plus FVRD levies apply here (recreation is an FVRD service); utility fees ride on the tax notice with 5% + 5% late penalties under Utility Bylaw No. 1153 per the research file — and the property-tax due date/penalty could NOT be confirmed on a District page in the research, so it stays UNCONFIRMED from a District source (no other town's penalty scheme is imported). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and http://hope.ca/p/tax-rates
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED); pad-resale PTT stays UNCONFIRMED. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Hope — the block governs this ground's strata diligence generally).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District frame — OCP / zoning / SSMUH / servicing / flood.** OCP Bylaw 1617, 2025 (adopted February 9, 2026) governs; Zoning Bylaw No. 1324, 2012 (consolidated to March 20, 2025) is the zoning bylaw, and the District's 2024 SSMUH amendments (Bylaw 1580, 2024, RS-1 zone) allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones. **Servicing: no District of Hope servicing bylaw number is documented in this slug's research or the verified ledger — UNCONFIRMED, and no FVRD or other jurisdiction's servicing number is imported.** Flood: parcel-level flood construction levels are UNVERIFIED — pull the parcel's flood status against the June 2026 provincial floodplain maps and the District's Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit Area before pricing or writing. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw (research file §§4–5.)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at the CRA GI-003 general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What mobile home parks are in Hope?** Seven grounds are on record — Lismore, Windmill, Crystal River, Cariboo, Cedars, Silver Ridge Estates and Grace Estates — but all as **tenure records** on the FVRD's 2020 Hope housing inventory (Crystal River also has one listing's leasehold-tenure line). None is verified on an operator source in this program, so pad counts are the inventory's counts and every park's own rules must be obtained before a purchase is described in detail. (§2.)
- **Q: If I buy a home in a Hope park, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** (at Crystal River, the listing-stated form is a leasehold pad) — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are Lismore and Windmill 55+ parks?** The FVRD inventory calls Lismore a "Senior's mobile home park" and says Windmill "is for seniors" — but neither source states an exact minimum age, so **55+ status is UNCONFIRMED for both**. Get the park's written rules before calling either any specific age. (§2; 55-plus sibling §2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is flooding a real issue for a Hope park home?** Parts of the District are Fraser/Coquihalla floodplain, the design flood is measured at the Hope gauge, and updated provincial floodplain maps for this reach were released in June 2026 — check any pad address against the maps; a dike nearby does not remove the District's flood-hazard permit step for construction in hazard areas. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715
- **Q: Which schools serve a Hope park address?** SD78 Policy 7000, as the research records it: Coquihalla Elementary takes the old Town of Hope, east to Sumallo Lodge including Kawkawa Lake/Othello, west to Ruby Creek, plus Spuzzum and Yale; Silver Creek Elementary takes Silver Creek and west of it; Hope Secondary takes all Gr. 7–12 students west to Ruby Creek and up the Fraser Canyon to China Bar. The Flood-Hope Road parks sit on the west fringe — which elementary catchment a specific pad address falls in is carried on that policy basis only; no park-address locator run is on record, so that position is UNVERIFIED at address level. https://sd78.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/PDC-Meeting-Agenda-Package-2021-04-20.pdf
