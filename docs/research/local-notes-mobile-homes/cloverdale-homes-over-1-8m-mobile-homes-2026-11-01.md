## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (carried in full as the standing block; it would govern any pad purchase, none being documented in this band).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the **director** — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file — and no pad range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; the Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. **Band-relevant grant point:** the Province states the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; **no flat claim either way is stated as fact** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — the band's working block (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. **Worked examples for this band, inputs + tier math stated:** at the band floor, $1,800,000 fully residential, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. At $2,000,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. At $3,500,000 fully residential = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000. Stated expressly: **the $78,000 figure is NOT met as a $3.5M computation in this slug's own siblings** — the acreages sibling for this slug mentions it only to state that it appears in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling (its only home) and follows $93,000; the reconciliation is OPEN and $93,000 is followed here. **The PTT shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" is NOT met in this slug's own siblings** — this slug's research records that the live page's own PTT summary matches the Province's published tiers, and no sibling states the shorthand; it is never carried here. **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — buyers in this band budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in the Cloverdale community).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP Bylaw No. 21900; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. SSMUH: 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (July 8, 2024 changes renamed RF-12 / RF-12C to R4 and RF-SD to R6). Secondary suites: one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area. Coach houses / garden suites: 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, lane access on smaller R3–R6 lots, one extra parking space. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 by stream class — no single figure. STRs: owner-only, owner must live at the property as primary residence, business licence **$358/yr** carried with the City's 2024 **$350/yr** proposal disclosed. Servicing: Bylaw 8830 (1986) VERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales at the general level CRA sources support — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
