## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block, carried in full because Aberdeen buyers cross-shop Abbotsford's parks:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies — in a park the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and RENTS the pad; that is not land ownership. Pad rent can be increased only once every 12 months, with at least three full months' written notice. **Caps verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount — percentages ONLY; no pad-rent dollar figure is ever stated as fact in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion (RTB):** conversion of all or a significant part of a park to another use requires all necessary permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice (Form RTB-31), and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route (assessed value minus the initial $20,000) where the RTB conditions are met. Finer park rules are UNCONFIRMED beyond the RTB pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home as chattel / Manufactured Home Registry — the rule that matters for an own-lot Aberdeen manufactured home:** in BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Registry maintains a central register of ownership details, controls the movement of manufactured homes, and improves lender security. Whether a specific farm-lot manufactured home remains Registry-registered or has been de-registered and affixed to its fee-simple land is UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and title records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **ALR RULES IN FULL (Aberdeen edges are farm ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; a secondary suite ONLY within the principal residence; an additional residence 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). The City's A1 zone mirrors the farm-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, at the same 90 m² / 186 m² caps, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m² in the A1 zone text. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met; none is asserted here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT:** for an own-land fee-simple purchase, general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchors: $700K→$12,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. **The PTT treatment of a pad-park chattel purchase is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no flat "PTT does not apply on a pad" statement is carried as fact, and that page claim is NOT documented for this guide slug (see §5).** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks (redirect — none in Aberdeen, and barred on ALR ground):** where a buyer instead buys in a bare-land strata park elsewhere, the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum CRF 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning/OCP/airport for this ground:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, Section 200 agricultural zones; OCP = Abbotsforward 2050 CURRENT (adopted December 2025) — the research file's "2600-2016" wording is STALE, and there is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan. Land covered by the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations, SOR/83-253 (City drawing M-500), is subject to height limits, with NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals required before a City building permit. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies**, and SSMUH does not override the A1/A2 minimums or the ALR. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at the level the CRA sources state:** CRA contemplates a new or substantially renovated mobile home purchased from a builder (including the manufacturer or vendor) as new housing for the GST/HST New Housing Rebate, and CRA policy material treats a previously occupied residential unit that is a residential complex under the exempting provisions — carried only as that used-generally-exempt / new-taxable framing; a specific home's treatment is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-home-construction/new-housing-rebate.html
- **First-home programs:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA — never March 12) applies to new HOUSING: 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes / servicing:** Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% September 16, 2026. Rural lots outside the urban boundary use wells and onsite sewage — check the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing for any farm-lot home. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Aberdeen?** No. No manufactured-home park, age-restricted or otherwise, with an Aberdeen address was found in any source checked, and the only strata complex in the sub-area (Lantern Park townhomes) is built form with no age restriction. (§2.)
- **Q: I saw a manufactured home on a Bradner Road farm lot — what am I buying?** Own-land stock: a fee-simple farm or rural parcel with a manufactured-form home on it. You own the land; the home-form question is its Registry position — still registered as a chattel, or de-registered and affixed — UNCONFIRMED until the Registry and title records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Could a bare-land strata park be built here later?** Not out of ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified, subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval, and the zone minimums are 8.0 ha (A1) / 16.0 ha (A2). No live park proposal for Aberdeen is documented — UNCONFIRMED as a prospect. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: If I buy in Paradise Park (Abbotsford West) instead, how does pad rent work?** You own the home and rent the pad under the MHPTA; pad rent rises at most once every 12 months on three full months' notice, capped at 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What if a park I buy into is converted?** The RTB conversion rules require permits/approvals first, a 12-month notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with an additional-compensation route where the conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I finance a manufactured home on a farm lot like a house?** The Registry position drives the answer — a registered chattel is personal property (the Registry exists in part to improve lender security on such homes), while a de-registered home affixed to owned land presents as real property. Lender terms for a specific home: UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I put a second manufactured home on my Aberdeen farm parcel?** Only within the ALR/A1 frame: a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional farm dwelling at 90 m² (40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha) on a lot of at least 0.4 ha — and it cannot be stratified or sold separately. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Are there age rules for manufactured homes here?** No park exists to carry one. In BC strata the only valid age bylaw is 55+ (since November 24, 2022); on a fee-simple farm lot there is no strata bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which school serves a Bradner Road address in Aberdeen?** The settled chain for 2975 Bradner Road is Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary, single-address only — run any other farm address in the SD34 School Locator. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/
- **Q: Does the airport limit a tall manufactured-home or shop build?** Where land is inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, height may not exceed the approach, outer or transitional surfaces and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals precede a City building permit — check the parcel before planning. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
