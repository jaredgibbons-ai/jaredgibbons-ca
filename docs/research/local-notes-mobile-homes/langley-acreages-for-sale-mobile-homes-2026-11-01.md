## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **ALR block (full, governs this farm topic).** Subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (deposit of a strata plan is a subdivision — ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — the Bulletin 09 citation itself was not opened in this slug's pass and is carried as the settled program position (research file §4 flags it UNVERIFIED in-pass). Housing frame: one principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (≤1,000 m² fill), a secondary suite ONLY inside the principal residence, and one additional residence ≤90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (over 40 ha); more or larger requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Manufactured homes on ALR ground:** only via ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 + the Non-Adhering Residential Use route, as above. A general farm-worker entitlement: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Farm class (full three-tier form):** $2,500 in farm-gate sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha on larger operations; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever met. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **Board / quota discipline:** NO board source was opened in this slug — no quota, transfer or certification specific is documented in this slug's research, so every such specific is **UNVERIFIED** in this file and none is stated.
- **MHPTA — pad purchases only.** The Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site; a home on land the buyer owns goes through the land title system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent, 10-day rule (s.28 / Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit for consent; purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional.** No pad-rent dollar figure anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure (s.42).** Conversion of all or a significant part of a park requires all permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to move-out; 15-day dispute window; additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the settled files state them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the MHR; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land or on land owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT on a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule; pad-resale PTT UNCONFIRMED.** Both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. The newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. Confirm with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land transfers — the acreage case).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors with inputs + tier math: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). $78,000 at a $3,200,000 input is CORRECT and never flagged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata block (bare-land strata only).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); 45+ VOID, never converts. Note the ALR point above: ALR residences cannot be stratified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Township frame.** Zoning 2500 (feed cards cite RU-2/RU-3 — zone per parcel confirmed with the Township); SSMUH 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024): eligible lots ≤4,050 m², inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water/sewer — most acreages fail on size and servicing; NO 6-plex tier. Suite cap 120 m²; coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata; R-CL s.403 = 220 min / 464.5 max LOT area (no 355 m²). Servicing 5382 CONFIRMED. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST — CRA GI-003 general level only.** GST applies to new construction, not resales; vacant-land/farm specifics beyond that level: UNCONFIRMED in this file.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate = new HOUSING (Bill C-4 assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12); NHR 36% max $6,300; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000. A resale park home is not new housing.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a Langley farm acreage?** Only through the ALR route — ALR Use Reg s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25, or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application. There is no general farm-worker entitlement on record: UNCONFIRMED. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage or its residences?** No — depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). (§3.)
- **Q: How many homes can an ALR acreage have?** One principal ≤500 m², a suite only inside it, plus one additional residence ≤90 m² (≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (>40 ha); more needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** The three-tier thresholds in §3 — never the flat "$2,500+" line — with the BC Assessment application by October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on an acreage?** No for most — lots must be ≤4,050 m², inside the UCB, on municipal water/sewer. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: If I buy a park home instead, do I own land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad.** (§2 tenure line.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent rise?** 2.3% + proportional (2026); 2.2% + proportional (2027), once per 12 months, 3 months' notice — percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is there a quota or board fact I should rely on for a Langley farm purchase?** Not from this file — NO board source was opened in this slug, so quota specifics are UNVERIFIED here in every particular. (§3.)
