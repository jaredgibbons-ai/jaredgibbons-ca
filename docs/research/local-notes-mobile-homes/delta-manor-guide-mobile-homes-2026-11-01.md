## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act where a pad tenancy exists.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No Delta Manor purchase in §2 is a pad tenancy on a verified source, so this block is the frame a buyer takes to the nearest park outside Delta — it does not describe a Delta Manor park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, consent and park rules.** On a park sale the existing pad tenancy is assigned (not newly granted): the seller must give the proposed purchaser the written tenancy agreement and written park rules before consent is requested; assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, with a response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, and no fee for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement and may be changed only with 3 full months' notice, and only if fair, clear, properly noticed and not changing a material term. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (BC all-items CPI, 12-month average change) plus a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** **No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file** — no live-page pad-rent range is documented in this slug's own research, so none is flagged or adopted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support.** Conversion of all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, requires all necessary permits and approvals in place before notice, 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, and the assessed value does not include the land's value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The Province states expressly only that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — own-land placement, not a resale pad purchase. Confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure with a lawyer or notary before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for registered land transfers where PTT does apply (a bare-land strata lot such as Laburnum / South Pointe, or a fee-simple lot).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 fully residential bare-land strata purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item: follow $93,000 and flag the conflict wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form block — governs Laburnum Park Place and South Pointe as bare-land stratas.** Form B diligence (strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary; provided within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert. In a bare-land strata the owner owns the lot and the house on it, pays strata fees for common property, and generally insures/maintains the house itself — confirm the exact split in the bylaws and Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Zoning / OCP — Delta frame.** OCP: **consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," with NO lettered schedules** — farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; any attribution of a lettered schedule (including a Ladner schedule) to Bylaw 8400 is an error; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the OLD Bylaw No. 3950. **Zoning bylaw number: CONTESTED (No. 7600, 2017 on City zone sheets vs an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted in this file**; what governs is the parcel's own zone sheet. The urban Ladner ladder includes RS5 (550 m² subdivision minimum, or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 (390 m²), RS7 (335 m²), RD1 duplex (836 m²) and RD4 duplex (504 m²); RS1 lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites.** Delta's SSMUH zoning amendments (Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. DSSS Bylaw 8288, 2024 adoption is UNCONFIRMED — some Delta land-home siblings assert it as governing; that conflict is flagged and it is **never asserted in this file**. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Inside a bare-land strata, the strata bylaws sit on top of the zoning. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Streamside / flood.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Ladner flood diligence is lot-specific behind the dike/drainage system in §1 — confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

Anything finer for a specific manufactured home is UNCONFIRMED until vendor and home status are established. State the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/delta-manor-condos-2026-11-01.md)
