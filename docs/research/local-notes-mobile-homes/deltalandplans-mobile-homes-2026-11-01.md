## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

*The Delta plan/zoning frame comes first, because it is this topic's substance; the MHPTA block follows in full, because a plans reader buying a park home needs both — and needs to know the plans do not govern the pad tenancy.*

- **Delta OCP — Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules.** Adopted July 8, 2024; Land Use Plan at s. 1.5, designations at s. 1.5.1 on Map 2, Development Permit Areas in Appendix B (including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA and hazard DPAs SD2 English Bluff / Tsawwassen Escarpment and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands). Any citation of a Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan, or of "Schedule D.1," as part of Bylaw 8400 is the superseded Bylaw 3950 position and is an error. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Zoning — NUMBER CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted.** This slug's research states that the OCP's designations are implemented through Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, with zoning assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps hosted on maps.delta.ca. Program-wide, the number is CONTESTED (zone sheets citing No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025") — this file follows the standing correction: the number is flagged and asserted neither way; what governs a parcel in practice is its own zone sheet. Residential ladder AS SOURCED in the research (minimum lot area for subdivision): RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Agriculture zones AS SOURCED:** in the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH in Delta.** The City has introduced the provincially required zoning updates that allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots (SSMUH amendments Bylaws 8394 + 8395 in the standing frame); the City frames SSMUH as ground-oriented housing between single-detached homes and condominiums, attainable for middle-income families. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a lot-zoning framework for owned lots, not a park-tenancy right. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **ALR layer.** Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply on top of the OCP designation and zoning where land is in the ALR; the ALC generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel, with a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area and one additional detached residence permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED.** Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw 8288 adoption is UNCONFIRMED in the verified ledger. The deltalandplans lots-and-land sibling holds that position correctly (8288 not asserted); any sibling elsewhere asserting 8288 in governing terms is a conforming-pass item — none is asserted in this file. (Standing ledger; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/deltalandplans-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (not the OCP).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. A park's pad tenancy is not created, changed or ended by the OCP or the zoning bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated anywhere in this file; no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's M1 files state them. This is where the plans system and the tenancy system meet: the permits-first requirement is the s.42 gate. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact; pad-resale PTT is UNCONFIRMED, and BOTH flat page claims met in the program are UNVERIFIED.** The Province states expressly only that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a rule for a NEW home on OWN land, not a resale home on a rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — the $78,000 figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling (flagged, not followed, in this slug's acreages sibling); follow $93,000. The "3% to $3M, 5% above" shorthand is WRONG, never carried, and NOT met in this slug's siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At the general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established.
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. No March 12 statement is met in this slug's research or siblings. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender and mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age and foundation requirements) were not confirmed on an official source in this program: UNVERIFIED.

Registry ledger, unblended: Delta has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED); Abbotsford HAS one; Surrey / Mission NONE. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Delta's current Official Community Plan?** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan whose Land Use Plan (s. 1.5, Map 2) sets the designations for the whole city. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan or a Ladner Area Plan in Delta's OCP?** No. Those were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1). Bylaw No. 8400 superseded them — there is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, and citing one as part of 8400 is an error. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Which zoning bylaw number should my documents cite?** The number is CONTESTED in the sources (No. 7600, 2017 on City zone sheets vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged in this file, asserted neither way; confirm the current citation with the City and work from the parcel's own zone sheet. (Research file §4; standing correction.)
- **Q: How do I find what can be built on a Delta property?** In order: the OCP designation (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area in Appendix B — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What did SSMUH change on Delta's single-family lots?** The provincially required updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, under the framework the 2024 OCP provides — but servicing, flood and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel. SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does the OCP or zoning change my rights if I buy a home in a park?** No. A pad tenancy is governed by the MHPTA, not the plan: you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad, the tenancy assigns on sale with the park owner's consent (Form 10, 10-day rule), and pad rent is capped by the provincial formula in §3. The plans matter to a park mainly at closure/conversion, where s.42 requires all permits and approvals BEFORE notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What does the Agriculture (A) designation mean for a farm parcel with a manufactured home?** Agriculture (A) is the s. 1.5.1 designation applied on Map 2 to maintain agricultural lands; where the land is also in the ALR, ALC rules on residences apply on top of the City's A-zone zoning — the City designation does not settle what the ALC allows. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: For a subdivision question, where do I start?** From the zone's minimum: a new A1 farm lot needs 8 ha and OCP compliance (plus ALC approval where applicable); a new RS7 lot needs 335 m² — the range is why the zone lookup comes before any lot-value opinion. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I rely on the live deltalandplans page's own FAQ answers as verified facts?** No — the page is Jared's own editorial. Every plan fact in this file is sourced instead to the City of Delta, the OCP (Bylaw No. 8400) or the ALC documents cited; any live-page claim not matched by those sources (for example its Flood Construction Level wording for specific neighbourhoods) is UNVERIFIED here.
