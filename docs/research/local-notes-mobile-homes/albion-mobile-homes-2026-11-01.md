## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase is verified in Albion; the block is carried so an Albion buyer comparing Maple Ridge park product elsewhere in the city has the settled rules).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule.** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the program's M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers — the form every Albion purchase takes).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K cap → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier** — the $78,000 figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling, is not met in this slug's own siblings, reconciliation is OPEN, and $93,000 is followed everywhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (Albion's strata are built townhomes, §2; the block applies to a bare-land strata if one is ever verified — none is).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. For an Albion townhome purchase, the Form B on the townhome strata is the operative diligence — see the townhomes home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Maple Ridge frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 (RS-1 at s. 605; the RMH manufactured-home-park zone at s. 626 exists in the bylaw city-wide, but no Albion park is zoned or verified under it in this program). Suites: one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Servicing: Bylaw 4800-1993 (City source). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.

A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Albion?** No — none is verified in Albion in the research or any sibling. Albion's stock is freehold detached houses and four strata townhome complexes (§2). Maple Ridge's documented parks are in East Central, Southwest and Northeast Maple Ridge — see the city topic file.
- **Q: Are Albion Station, Ridgemont, The Warwick or Spencer Brook Estates mobile homes?** No — all four are built strata townhome complexes, counted in the Albion townhomes file. Their purchase diligence is strata diligence (Form B, bylaws, depreciation report), not park diligence (no pad, no park consent, no Manufactured Home Registry transfer for the home as a chattel). (§2/§3.)
- **Q: If I buy a park home elsewhere in Maple Ridge, do I own the land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — a home on a rented pad is NOT land ownership. In Albion, every verified purchase is a land purchase (fee-simple house lot or a strata townhome lot). (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a Maple Ridge park instead?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, capped at 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, for park product elsewhere in the city — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence is deemed consent, no fee for consent. No such consent exists for an Albion house or townhome. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: Can I put a manufactured home on a South Albion farm parcel?** Only within the ALC framework — the s.34(3) / Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route or a Non-Adhering Residential Use application, within the residence caps in §2, and only if the parcel is ALR and local bylaws allow it. No Albion case is documented, and a general farm-worker entitlement is UNCONFIRMED. (§2.)
- **Q: What happens if a park closes?** MHPTA s.42 (park product elsewhere): permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date (§3). It does not apply to an Albion fee-simple or strata purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a manufactured home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel treatment and home-age/foundation requirements are lender-specific. An Albion townhome or house is conventional land financing by comparison; confirm the specific product either way. (§3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Albion address?** Catchments are address-based in SD42 and no locator run for an Albion address is on record — UNVERIFIED. The four Ministry-listed schools in §1 are physically in or at the edge of the area, not catchments for any specific address. Run the exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Is any Albion stock 55+?** No verified 55+ ownership stock of any form exists in Albion (55-plus sibling) — and a strata 45+ bylaw would be VOID in any case (§3). Do not market anything in Albion as 55+ on this program's evidence.
