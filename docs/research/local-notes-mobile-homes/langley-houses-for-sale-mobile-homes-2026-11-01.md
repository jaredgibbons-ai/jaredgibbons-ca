## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after receipt, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, NO fee or benefit may be charged; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (CPI + proportional amount = levy + regulated-utility change, divided by sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits and approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and additional assessed-value / $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way as fact (both flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example at this page's median level, inputs stated: a $1,345,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = $2,000 + $36,000 + 2% × $0 above $2M — stated precisely: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $22,900 (2% × $1,145,000) = **$24,900**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — two-municipality frame.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) — eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water AND sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible; **NO 6-unit tier**; suite cap 120 m² (s.102, as amended incl. Bylaw 6147, July 21, 2025 — with the documented guide-vs-bylaw conflict on suites in townhouses/rowhouses carried in the research); coach house fee-simple s.102; servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED. City: SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024) — RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED); suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%, registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); carriage/garden unit capped at 100 m². **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284 (research file §4.)
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — used home generally exempt, new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000. FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home, age/foundation) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.
- **Taxes / STRs.** City of Langley 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 (penalty rate not stated on the City page read); the City penalty percentage and the Township's 2026 due date/penalty are UNVERIFIED in this research. Short-term rental rules in either municipality: UNVERIFIED — no municipal STR rule was read on an official page in this program. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes (research file §4.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this houses page include mobile homes?** Its stated feed is detached houses on their own lots, acreage and farms left off — a park home on a rented pad is not that product and is not counted here. A manufactured home on its OWN lot would be a land purchase; none is verified by name in this research. (Research file §1; §2 above.)
- **Q: Where are Langley's actual parks, then?** Brookswood (Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park), Langley (Pineridge), Aldergrove (Pioneer Park co-op) and Otter District (Langley Grove Estates) — each documented in its home ground's files, cross-referenced in §2 only. (Settled roster.)
- **Q: In a pad-rental park, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad**; your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027; once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice, percentages only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me?** Yes — prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How many units can a Langley house lot hold?** It depends on the municipality first: Township Bylaw 6020 gives 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 at 280–4,050 m² with no 6-unit tier and strict eligibility; City Bylaw 3284 gives RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies. (Research file §4.)
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — effective only once registered, filed by a lawyer/notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; CSA label attached at sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I get a mortgage on a park home?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level — chattel treatment and age/foundation rules are lender-specific. Confirm before removing subjects.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Langley house or park address?** SD35, address-based; recorded locator runs exist only for the three addresses in §1 — every other address is UNVERIFIED until run. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
