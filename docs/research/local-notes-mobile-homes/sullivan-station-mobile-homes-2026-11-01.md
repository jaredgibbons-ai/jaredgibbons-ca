## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. No verified pad-rental park exists in Sullivan Station (§2), so this block is the frame a buyer needs if a pad-tenancy listing is ever marketed under this sub-area's name — and the test that exposes a mislabelled listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Pad rent may be increased only once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** NO pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact anywhere in this file — no pad-rent figure for any park on this ground is documented in this ground's research, so none is mentioned (M6 discipline). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place BEFORE issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file (BOTH flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** (a $78,000 figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m TOWNHOMES sibling — it is not met in this file's own siblings and is not carried here; follow $93,000 everywhere; reconciliation OPEN). The townhomes siblings' PTT shorthand "3% to $3M, 5% above" is WRONG — it is NOT met in this file's own siblings (checked) and is never carried. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Sullivan Station).** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Surrey frame.** OCP = Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — never 18020 (a stale 18020 citation met in a sibling would be a DIFFERENT defect from an email item, flagged as its own item; none is met in this file's own siblings). Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; South Newton lands carry R-series residential zones, with RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook. The rescinded South Newton plan no longer governs — see §1. Servicing: Bylaw 8830 (1986). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites.** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (SSMUH in Surrey from Jul 8, 2024); the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house; Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal habitable area; properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Short-term rentals: Surrey STR licence $358/yr, with the City's 2024 $350/yr proposal disclosed alongside — the two figures are not blended. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/taxes/goods-and-services-tax-gst-housing.html
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12 — no March 12 statement is met in this file's own siblings): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Sullivan Station?** No — not on the record of this program. - **Q: If I buy into a Surrey park elsewhere, do I own land?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. Built strata townhomes (the 9 complexes in the townhomes home file) are the different, own-land strata form, with Form B diligence. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: What is Sullivan Mews, and is it an option here?** No. Sullivan Mews is a membership-tenure purchase placed in East Newton — excluded everywhere in this program, never a Sullivan Station strata and never mobile stock. (§2; townhomes sibling.)
- **Q: How does park-owner consent work on a pad purchase?** The seller must give you the written tenancy agreement and park rules first, then ask the park owner to consent on RTB Form 10; the response is due by the 10th day, silence is deemed consent, and no consent fee may be charged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Does property transfer tax apply to a home-only purchase on a rented pad?** UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule — no primary source in this program settles it, and both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED. A lawyer or notary must confirm for the exact structure. (§3.)
- **Q: Can a Sullivan Station house lot add units?** Within Surrey's SSMUH framework — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units on lots over 280 m², and 6 units on eligible lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code, capped at 40% of the principal habitable area. Confirm the lot's band and bus-stop eligibility with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Which school serves a Sullivan Station address?** Catchments shifted with Snokomish Elementary's January 2026 opening and are UNVERIFIED for any specific address in this file — run the exact address in the SD36 School Locator.
