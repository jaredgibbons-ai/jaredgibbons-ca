## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of day 10 is deemed consent, and NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator" — no "arbitrator" wording is met in this slug's research or siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file — none is documented in this slug's own research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and the additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed and taxed like other residential property; the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; the Home Owner Grant is available for a principal residence, applied for each year. Which Langley taxes the home depends on its municipality — Township and City issue separate tax notices. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home (research file §4)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**; the townhomes sibling's worked example on the combined $611,000 attached median = $10,220 — that $611,000 input is the COMBINED Condos & Townhomes median, NOT a condo-only or park-home price. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. No $78,000-at-$3.5M figure is met in this slug's siblings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; the Langley parks cross-referenced in §2 are pad-rental / co-op forms, not bare-land strata).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. This block DOES govern the 22 strata condos as strata law — including the voiding of any "rentals not allowed" directory lines for Parkway Estates / Parkway II. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-condos-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Zoning / SSMUH — two Langley frames, never blended.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500, SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², NO 6-plex tier), suite cap 120 m², coach house fee-simple s.102, R-CL s.403 = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area (no 355 m²), servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED. Langley City: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED — a live page's adoption date is expressly NOT carried), SSMUH 3284, suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** in either municipality. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4; standing frames)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only as CRA sources support.** At general level: GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established. (Settled program position.)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. In this slug's siblings, March 12 appears ONLY as a superseded secondary date in the condos sibling (March 13 governs) — NOT a sibling's own assent date, so no March-12 carrier flag is raised here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/mortgage-insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home treatment, age/foundation requirements) are UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.
- **Registry.** REGISTRY LEDGER: Langley is NOT in the ledger — no Supreme Court registry is asserted for a Langley file here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the Langley parks part of the condo market on this page?** No — the feed is condo-only, and park homes are a different tenure entirely: in Cedar Creek, Fernridge, Pineridge and Langley Grove you own a home on a rented pad; in the 22 counted condos you own a strata lot. The two are cross-referenced in §2 only so they are never confused. (Research file §1; condos sibling.)
- **Q: Do I own the land in a Brookswood park?** No. **You own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Which Langley parks are 55+?** Cedar Creek, Fernridge and Pineridge are 55+ listing-stated, and Pioneer Park is a 55+ co-op — all documented at home in Brookswood/Aldergrove files. Langley Grove Estates is expressly NOT 55+ ("no age restrictions" listing-stated). A park's age rule is a park rule, not a strata bylaw — confirm it in the park's written rules. (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. At Pioneer Park the approval is the co-op's membership acceptance instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park closes or converts?** The landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a park-home purchase registered, and how does that differ from a condo?** A manufactured home registers in the Manufactured Home Registry (signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, filed by a lawyer/notary; CSA label attached at point of sale). A condo registers as a strata lot in the land title system, with a Form B as the core diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can I finance a park home like a condo?** UNVERIFIED at official-source level in this program — chattel financing treatment, home-age and foundation requirements are lender-specific and differ from strata-lot lending. Confirm before removing subjects.
- **Q: Is there a Langley condo-only median I can price a park home against?** No — SnapStats publishes no condo-only split and no mobile category at all; the $611,000 September 2026 figure blends condos and townhomes across all of Langley and must never be quoted as a condo or park-home figure. (Research file §§2, 7.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a park or condo address?** Catchments are address-based in SD35 and no locator run for a specific address on this feed is on record — UNVERIFIED; run the exact address. Settled SD35 chains exist in the program for other sample addresses only and are not transferred here. (Research file §4.)
