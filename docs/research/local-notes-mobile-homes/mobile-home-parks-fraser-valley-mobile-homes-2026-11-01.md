## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act.** Manufactured homes are built off-site and transported for installation; BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies — a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site; a home on land the buyer owns goes through the land title system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On sale the site tenancy is assigned permanently to the buyer; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent, 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment only with the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it); requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response by the 10th day after receipt; silence = deemed consent; under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting; on assignment the pad rent and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase stay the same. The Act's word is "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, only with 3 full months' written notice, up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion. Formula: inflation (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change) + proportional amount (change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Re-confirmed in this slug's pass: **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — full block.** Converting all or a significant part of a park to another use requires all necessary government permits and approvals BEFORE issuing notice, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may end the tenancy early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to move-out; the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days; additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the $20,000 may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the MHR; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label must be attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule; pad-resale PTT UNCONFIRMED; both flat page claims in the program UNVERIFIED.** The newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a NEW home on own land, not a resale on a rented pad. Confirm with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Anchors with inputs + tier math: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; **$3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier** ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). $78,000 at a $3,200,000 input is CORRECT, never flagged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata block (bare-land strata communities only).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); 45+ VOID, never converts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / servicing — no single-city frame (regional topic).** Each park sits under its own municipality's bylaws; the verified servicing ledger is carried per home file (Township 5382 CONFIRMED; Surrey 8830 (1986); Coquitlam 3558, 2003; Maple Ridge 4800-1993 (City source); Mission DSCB 5650-2017 (City source); HELD UNCONFIRMED: Abbotsford Development, Chilliwack 3055 (NOT adopted; City webpage assertion flagged wherever met), Delta DSSS 8288 adoption, FVRD numbers). **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.**
- **GST — CRA GI-003 general level only** (new taxable, resale generally exempt); finer treatment UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate = new HOUSING (Bill C-4 assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12); NHR 36% max $6,300; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000. A resale park home is not new housing.
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many parks does this page list — and does listing verify a park?** 73 entries in page order, with Cedar Grove and Mountainview Estates each listed twice, so the distinct count is lower. No — the entries are TENURE RECORDS (site copy); no official registry exists and no park is verified by this file. (§§1–2.)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Fraser Valley parks?** No figure is stated as fact in this file: this page publishes no pad rent for any park and no official source publishes a figure, average or range. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (§5 carries this slug's own documented sibling ranges as UNVERIFIED site copy only.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent rise?** 2.3% + proportional (2026); 2.2% + proportional (2027), once per 12 months, 3 full months' notice, no rounding up, no carry-forward. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Do I own land when I buy a park home?** On a rented pad, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad**; the site right is the assigned tenancy under the MHPTA. (§2 tenure line.)
- **Q: Does the park have to approve my purchase?** Yes — prior written consent under s.28 (Form 10, 10th-day response, deemed consent on silence, no consent fee), and the pad rent + next increase date carry over unchanged. (§3.)
- **Q: What if the park is sold or converted?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 on or before the effective date, 15 days to dispute, additional compensation only through the dispute route in §3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Is "Centennial Motor Inn" really a park?** UNCONFIRMED — it is listed as one in the directory (§2 defect); whether it is an operating park or a data error is an open question in this slug's research, and it is never presented as verified here.
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Fraser Valley mobile homes?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category; the figures in §1 are all-residential community context only. (§1.)
- **Q: How is a park-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry (Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer via a lawyer/notary; CSA label attached) — the sale is effective only once registered. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
