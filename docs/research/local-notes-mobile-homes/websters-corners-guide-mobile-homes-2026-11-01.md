## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase (no pad purchase exists in Websters Corners; the block is carried in full as the standing block).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns — the only manufactured form this ground supports — is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** A landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and no pad-rent figure is documented in this slug's own research, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date, may dispute a closure notice within the 15-day dispute window, and additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts apply only as the Act and the cited provincial pages state them. Finer closure detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Maple Ridge's own park-redevelopment policy (City layer).** Council Policy No. 6.27, the Manufactured Home Park Redevelopment Tenant Assistance Policy, supplements the provincial Act: it is triggered by a rezoning application proposing redevelopment of a mobile home park, and sets compensatory measures for tenancies where the pad is rented but the dwelling is owned by the tenant. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/manufactured-home-park-redevelopment-tenant-assistance-policy-627
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. For an own-land Websters Corners home, whether a Registry record exists at all is part of the home-by-home check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so **no flat claim either way is stated as fact in this file** (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own Websters Corners acreage, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier** (tier math: $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Websters Corners).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP — Maple Ridge frame.** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs, with the rural minimums as the research states them: RS-2 at 0.4 ha, RS-3 at 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha per the zone text, A-1 at 2.0 ha, A-2 at 4.0 ha and A-3 at 8.0 ha; confirm any parcel's zone per property. **The OCP citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — flagged here, resolved nowhere.** Settled plan positions carried: Council adopted updates to the OCP on November 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan update on October 28, 2025; no area plan for Websters Corners was found. The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan (OCP amendments covering both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue, about 500 acres per the City release) establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction. The Lougheed Transit Corridor position is settled as ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021), ending at 221 St — far west of this ground and not reached by it; this slug's guide research documents no Lougheed adoption claim on the guide page, so no correction is needed here. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic (research file §4)
- **Suites and added units.** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels, the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: it permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31) and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019

Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. Parcel-level ALR, floodplain, well and septic status: UNVERIFIED. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Websters Corners?** No — no source found for any manufactured-home park inside Websters Corners, so there is no rented-pad stock here at all. The manufactured-form homes here sit on their own acreage lots. Maple Ridge's verified pad park (Alouette, East Central) is cross-referenced in §2, counted only in its home file. (§2.)
- **Q: If I place a manufactured home on a Websters Corners acreage, what governs it?** Title and zoning, not the MHPTA — you own the land. The checks are the parcel's zone (RS-2/RS-3/A-1–A-3 minimums in §3), ALR status and the ALR Use Regulation where the parcel is in the reserve, well and septic records, floodplain position per parcel, and the home's own registry/CSA/foundation record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up, if I buy in a Maple Ridge park elsewhere?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). A Websters Corners acreage has no pad rent at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** In a pad-rental park, yes — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. On an own-land acreage purchase there is no park owner to approve you. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of a park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; in Maple Ridge, Council Policy 6.27 adds City-level tenant assistance triggered by a rezoning application to redevelop a mobile home park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3 if it sits inside the single detached building, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the ALR Use Regulation also applies, and an additional separate residence is allowed only under its s.32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Which schools serve a Websters Corners address?** SD42, address-based, with no middle tier. The one settled sample on record is single-address only and is never generalised: **25554 Dewdney Trunk Road → Webster's Corners Elementary (K–7) / Garibaldi Secondary (8–12)**; the French Immersion schools the locator shows for that address are choice programs, not catchments. Run any other exact address in the locator. Blue Mountain Elementary (12453 248 St, Ministry file) is physically in the area but its catchment position for any given address is UNVERIFIED. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Can I price a manufactured home off the 25% sales ratio?** No. Detached recorded 8 active listings, 2 solds and a 25% sales ratio — the highest detached ratio in Maple Ridge that month on a very small base, so one sale either way swings it by double digits — while Condos & Townhomes recorded 0 listings and 0 solds.
