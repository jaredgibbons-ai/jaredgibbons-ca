## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on — the tenure form at Cultus Lake Village on the east-classified ground cross-referenced in §2. A home on a Park Board LEASE lot (the north shore's form) is not a pad tenancy: its deal runs on the Park Board lease, Park bylaws and Board consent rules, not the MHPTA pad block below; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. The block is carried here in full because a north-end buyer who widens the search to the lake's one park meets exactly this block. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation expressly recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows a landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — which is why Cultus Lake Village's own written rule, not a listing label, decides who can buy in there. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and the park owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for giving consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. On this ground specifically: Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill (the current lease fee schedule and per-lot levies are UNVERIFIED), and non-Park Area H land sits under the **RURAL tax regime — Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11: 5% penalty after Jul 2 and a further 5% after Oct 31** — not any City of Chilliwack schedule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/cultus-lake-north-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. PTT and registration handling for a Park Board leasehold interest is likewise a structure-specific question: a lawyer or notary must confirm both for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,000,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$18,000** ($2,000 on the first $200,000 + $16,000 at 2% on the next $800,000). The research file's own heading frames the area as "Chilliwack / FVRD Electoral Area H" and the lake's live pages carry Chilliwack framing — both are flagged here: Chilliwack bylaws, Chilliwack taxes and Chilliwack stats never apply to this ground, the rural Taxation (Rural Area) Act regime does, and CADREB publishes no area stats for it. A buyer reading a "Chilliwack" page for a north-end lot is reading the wrong municipality's rules. (Research file §1; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/cultus-lake-north-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Start with the lease, not the house.** North-end homes are on Park Board leased lots, so lease terms, Park bylaws and Board consent rules drive the deal — and the current lease fee schedule and per-lot levies are UNVERIFIED from an official source in this program, so they have to be pulled for the specific lot before pricing. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/
- **The lake's only park is not on this shore, and its age rule is unsettled.** Cultus Lake Village sits on east-classified ground (3942 Columbia Valley Road); the same home there was marketed as both 45+ and 55+ within one listing cycle. A north-end buyer searching "Cultus Lake mobile home park" is searching east-shore stock with an UNCONFIRMED age rule — only the park's written rules settle it. (§2 above.)
- **The hazard list here is longer than a buyer's first read.** The FVRD Area H Evacuation Guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding among local hazards — read it and price insurance accordingly before writing on a north-end home. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **Year-round living here means driving.** Route 56 runs in summer only, the Village Centre is the Park's only commercial area (its grocery's street address is UNVERIFIED on an official source), and the Village Centre redevelopment is a Park Board RFP process with a preferred proponent in negotiations — not a verified presale a buyer can price. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56 (research file §§4–6.)
