## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA block — the pad-tenancy law, in full (it governs any pad purchase a Holly buyer makes elsewhere; no pad exists in this sub-area per §2).** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs pad tenancies: a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on is a tenant under the Act; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and where both the home and the site are rented to the same person the Residential Tenancy Act applies instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **CORE DISCIPLINE — a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership:** the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad. Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green are the deliberate contrast in this file: their buyers own a strata LOT — that is land ownership in strata form, with strata fees instead of pad rent, and it is why the MHPTA does not govern them and the strata block below does. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (pad tenure):** the buyer does not get a new tenancy — the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), requested on RTB Form 10; the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day after the request, silence by the end of the 10th day is consent deemed given, and no fee may be charged for consenting. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms, with pad rent and the next increase date unchanged. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY, verified live from the RTB framework (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice; the cap is the BC inflation rate plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); no rounding up, no carry-forward. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact in this file** — no official source publishes one for any park, and none is documented in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules / closure / conversion (pad tenure):** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement, changeable only on 3 full months' notice (and, since April 30, 2024, only once per year), and enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. On a conversion of all or a significant part of a park (MHPTA s.42): permits and approvals first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on at least 10 days' notice; the dispute window is 15 days; additional compensation of assessed value minus $20,000 may be ordered where the home could not be moved, plus a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, if the landlord does not proceed. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land or on land privately owned by someone else; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for yearly. Delta's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. A bare-land strata lot (Hollyview, Ashley Green) is taxed as strata real property on the lot and home together — a different assessment posture from a pad home, confirmed on the Form B / tax notice for the specific lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (governs Hollyview Estates and Ashley Green directly):** Form B Information Certificate diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; provided within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page) — in a bare-land strata the Form B is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain, because the house itself is the owner's responsibility. Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (Hollyview's 13 lots are inside this rule); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **PTT / transfer treatment — UNCONFIRMED for a pad purchase, stated so:** flat site-copy claims either way (an Abbotsford page's "PTT does not apply on a pad"; a Chilliwack page's PTT-on-a-pad claim, as carried in other programs) are UNVERIFIED, never fact. For Holly's actual stock the question mostly does not arise in pad form: a bare-land strata lot purchase is a strata real-property purchase at the general PTT rates below. The sourced pad-adjacent point is narrow: the Province's newly built home exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a new home on one's own land, not a resale on a rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates and worked anchors (they govern a Hollyview / Ashley Green lot purchase and any fee-simple lot purchase in Holly):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fully residential purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Anchor checks: $500K→$8,000; $700K→$12,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 under the First-Time Home Buyers' Program); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a $78,000 figure in a townhomes sibling elsewhere is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000, flag $78,000 wherever met). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
