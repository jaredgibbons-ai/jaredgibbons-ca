## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** On sale, the existing pad tenancy is assigned; before asking the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent / director's order), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after the request is received; silence by end of day 10 = deemed consent; NO fee or benefit may be charged for consent; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms. The decision-maker is the "director", never "arbitrator". https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (BC all-items CPI 12-month average change + proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utilities, divided by number of sites). Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file** — the research for this slug documents no pad figure, so none is mentioned at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits/approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice, rent to the move-out date. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale/transfer/purchase is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); CSA certification label attached at point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for each year. City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (research file §4)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way is stated as fact (both flat page claims in the program are UNVERIFIED). The Province's newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — a new-home-on-own-land point, not a resale-on-a-pad point. A lawyer/notary must confirm for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a purchase under $1,800,000 meets only the first two tiers. Worked example, inputs stated: $1,800,000 (this band's ceiling), fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. Anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier — follow $93,000; the $78,000-for-$3.5M figure lives ONLY in the south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m townhomes sibling (a different slug), is NOT met in this slug's own siblings, and is not followed anywhere. **Shorthand check for this slug's own siblings: the "Over $3M — 5%" compression wording IS met in this slug's townhomes sibling, where it is itself flagged as a compression of the 3% tier + further 2% tier — carried here as flagged, never as a rate; the flat "3% to $3M, 5% above" form is NOT met in this slug's own siblings — stated explicitly.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (research file §4)
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (bare-land strata communities).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, exemptions incl. Development servicing bylaw number: UNCONFIRMED. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/2025-12-16%20-%20SSMUH%20Application%20Guide_1.pdf (research file §4)
