## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. Manufactured-home parks in Chilliwack run on this tenancy law, not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director," never an "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. In a designated 55-plus park, the Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) recognises refusal of an assignment where the purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — the Cottonwood Village position in the 55-plus sibling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000 and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. This is where the plan stack in §1 bites: a conversion needs the City's permits and approvals first, checked against the parcel's OCP designation and zone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. City of Chilliwack taxes: notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1, with a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies to a bare-land strata community such as Baker Trail Village / Braeside Estates, cross-referenced in §2).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / OCP / SSMUH — Chilliwack frame.** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets what can be built as-of-right today; the 2050 OCP is Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (the live page's stated adoption date of December 16, 2025 is UNVERIFIED — the City's OCP page read states no adoption date). SSMUH Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) amends Bylaw 5000; within that frame a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling (s.2), only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot (s.4.06(1)), and garden suites cap at 100 m². **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies** — it is a lot-zoning framework, and a park's parcels are the park owner's lots, not the tenants'. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, read 2026-11-01.)
- **Servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED, 3055 conflict carried openly.** No Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. The candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted per the verified ledger and is never presented as verified; the City's own subdivisions webpage names a "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055," and this slug's lots-and-land sibling documents several Chilliwack lots siblings asserting 3055 in governing terms (the asserting set for this topic per the M13 brief is chilliwack-proper-west + chilliwack-farms lots files) — both positions are documented as a CONFLICT against the ledger, the ledger governs, and the documented route is the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Assessment process, confirmed per parcel. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/chilliwacklanduseplans-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/chilliwacklanduseplans-acreages-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR / floodplain — the plan layers a park parcel can sit under.** Inside the ALR the residence frame governs regardless of the Agricultural Area Plan (Schedule B1): one principal residence at a maximum 500 m² total floor area, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require Agricultural Land Commission approval. A manufactured home counts as a residence under that frame. Floodplain: the live page's "Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519" citation and its Flood Construction Level mechanics are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — no City floodplain bylaw or FCL table was opened — so FCLs are confirmed with the City per parcel, never from the page. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (research file §§4, 6.)
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/chilliwacklanduseplans-condos-2026-11-01.md)

GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this plans page list Chilliwack's mobile home parks?** No — it is an editorial plans page with no listing cards, and this file takes no roster from it. Chilliwack's parks are documented in their home grounds (§2 cross-references): Cottonwood Village in the West Chilliwack guide ground, Baker Trail Village in the chilliwack-mobile-homes topic file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html (research file §1.)
- **Q: What do the plans actually decide for a park purchase?** The park home itself is a tenancy question (MHPTA, §3); the plans decide the ground under it — the parcel's zone under Bylaw 5000, its OCP designation under Bylaw 5500, its Schedule D permit areas and its floodplain position, all per-parcel checks on the City Webmap. A park with a conversion application running is a different risk from a stable one, and the plan layer is where that shows first. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: In a Chilliwack park, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. Cottonwood Village is that form; Baker Trail Village is the opposite form (a bare-land strata lot you own). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes (§2.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent; in a designated 55-plus park the park may refuse where the purchaser does not meet the age requirement. Build the 10-day window into subject dates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: What happens if a park is sold or redeveloped?** For a conversion of all or a significant part of the park, the landlord must have all permits and approvals first — in Chilliwack that means the City's approvals against the parcel's OCP designation and zone — give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can I trust the schedule labels on the live plans page?** No — not for Sardis, Eastern Hillsides or the permit areas. In the current OCP, Sardis is Schedule B5 (not "Schedule D, Bylaw No. 5024"), Eastern Hillsides is Schedule B4 (not "Schedule C" — Schedule C is the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area), and there is no "DPA2 Hillside & Upland": Schedule D lists D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development separately. Do not copy the page's labels into an offer (§5 defect set). https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: Can a manufactured home go on ALR land in Chilliwack?** It counts as a residence under the ALR frame: the parcel's permitted residences are the 500 m² principal residence (suite inside it only) plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size, and anything beyond that — or subdivision or non-farm use — needs Agricultural Land Commission approval, whatever the Agricultural Area Plan shows nearby. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which schools serve a Chilliwack park address?** Catchments are address-based in SD33 and no locator run for a park address is on record in this program, so a park's catchment is UNVERIFIED — run the exact address in the district's School Locator. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
