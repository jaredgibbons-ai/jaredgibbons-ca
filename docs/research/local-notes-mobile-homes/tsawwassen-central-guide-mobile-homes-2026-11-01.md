## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA) — the governing statute for any pad tenancy, stated in full because it is the frame a Central buyer takes to the nearest real parks (Tsawwassen Lands / Surrey / Langley):** the Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the owner also owns is dealt with through the land title system instead, and if a person rents both the home and the site, the Residential Tenancy Act — not the MHPTA — applies. **CORE TENURE LINE: a home on a RENTED PAD is not land ownership — the buyer owns the home (a chattel) and rents the pad.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Buying in a park is an assignment, not a new tenancy:** the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director): the request is made on RTB Form 10, the owner must respond so the home owner receives it by the 10th day, silence by the end of the 10th day is consent deemed given, and the owner must not charge a fee or receive a benefit for consenting. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Pad-rent increase caps — verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026), percentages ONLY:** a landlord may increase pad rent once every 12 months with at least 3 full months' notice, capped at the inflation rate (12-month average change in the BC all-items CPI) PLUS a proportional amount (change in local government levies plus change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of sites in the park). The formula outcomes are **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount**. **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is stated as fact anywhere in this file** — any dollar rent met in a listing or directory is listing/directory-stated for that home and date only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park rules:** park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term; since the April 30, 2024 regulation change, park rules can only be changed once a year and with three months' notice on an RTB-approved form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0072-000659
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation — only as official sources support:** a landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Added compensation where a home cannot be relocated, and bad-faith closure compensation, are as the Province's June 6, 2018 bulletin states them. Anything finer for a specific park: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2018MAH0066-001111
- **Age rules in parks are park designations, not strata bylaws:** the Province states the general rule for a designated 55-plus park — at least one tenant on the site must be 55 or older — and an individual park's own tenancy agreement and rules can state occupancy more strictly. Age rules are carried in this program ONLY from operator/Form B/registered-bylaw sources; listing-stated ages are labelled listing-stated, and conflicts stay UNRESOLVED. No park in Central carries any age rule, because no park in Central is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry:** a sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry, the province's central register of manufactured home ownership, administered through BC Registries; on a sale the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a manufactured home:** the owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; a manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home and https://www.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **PTT / transfer treatment — stated ONLY as official sources support:** the Province's Newly Built Home Exemption expressly includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) as a newly built home — that is the own-land case. Whether PTT applies to a resale manufactured home on a rented pad is NOT settled by any official source read in this ground's research: **UNCONFIRMED — flat site-copy claims either way are UNVERIFIED and none is asserted here.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Strata-form parks — the BC strata block (governs any bare-land strata manufactured-home community, e.g. the form cross-referenced at Beachcomber Cottages; none is in Central):** Form B diligence under Strata Property Act s. 59 (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; strata must provide it within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, including legacy-resident, caregiver and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and there is no conversion route. A bare-land strata buyer OWNS the strata lot — that is land ownership in strata form, the opposite tenure from a rented pad. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta OCP / zoning frame:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, NO lettered schedules (farm land "Agriculture (A)"); the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under OLD Bylaw 3950 — the "Schedule D.1 of 8400" conflation is an ERROR wherever met, including in batch 1/2/7 siblings. Zoning: the research cites RS/RD zones under a Delta zoning bylaw whose NUMBER is CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted; central subdivision minimums on the City zone sheets run RS7 335 m², RS6 390 m², RS5 550 m² (or 460 m²), RS4 610 m², RD4 504 m², RD1 836 m², confirmed parcel by parcel. No manufactured-home park zone or park parcel is documented for Central. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies:** Delta's SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) permit up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots — added-unit permission on owned lots. A pad tenancy is a tenancy under the MHPTA, not a lot the tenant can densify; SSMUH creates no rights on a rented pad. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf

The program's verified ledger holds DSSS 8288 adoption UNCONFIRMED; this file follows UNCONFIRMED and flags the asserting sibling rather than asserting 8288. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **GST — only at the cited-CRA level:** for vacant land, CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales by individuals are exempt (personal-use land), while a sale can be taxable where land was used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts. GST treatment of a specific manufactured-home purchase (new home, resale chattel, own-land placement) beyond the official positions cited in this section: UNCONFIRMED per transaction — state the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — per this file's research verification status:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M, phasing out to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. Whether a specific manufactured home (new vs resale, pad vs own land) qualifies under any of these is a per-transaction question: UNCONFIRMED until the home's status is established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a mobile home park in Tsawwassen Central?** No verified park of any tenure — pad-rental, bare-land strata, co-op, life lease, leasehold or First Nations lease — was found in this sub-area in the research file or any sibling. The centre's confirmed stock is freehold houses/duplexes and strata condos. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What about the manufactured-home community near Tsawwassen Mills?** That is Tsawwassen RV Resort, 4761 Nulelum Way, on Tsawwassen First Nation lands in the North ground — an all-ages RV resort, documented in the Tsawwassen North files. It is not 55+, not in the City of Delta, and no official/operator source read establishes year-round manufactured-home tenure for it; a directory's land-lease description is a tenure record only. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9
- **Q: If I buy a mobile home on a rented pad somewhere, do I own land?** No. **A home on a rented pad is not land ownership — you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** under a pad tenancy agreement governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. Own-land stock is different: a bare-land strata lot is owned in strata form (Form B, fees, bylaws), and a co-op home is held through co-op shares. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than inflation plus the park's proportional levy/utility amount: 2.3% + proportional for increases effective in 2026, and 2.2% + proportional for 2027 (RTB, verified Nov 1, 2026). No dollar pad rent is stated as fact in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park owner refuse my purchase?** The assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10 — but the owner must respond within 10 days, silence is consent deemed given, and no fee can be charged for consent. The seller must hand you the written tenancy agreement and park rules before consent is even requested. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Q: What happens if a park closes or is redeveloped?** The landlord must have all permits and approvals first, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; you may leave early on at least 10 days' written notice. A sale of the park alone does not end your tenancy — the assigned tenancy continues on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered; a lawyer or notary files the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. A missing label is a red flag for financing, moving and resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene a cheaper "mobile-style" option?** No — they are 55+ apartment-form strata condos (1150 54A St; 1140 55 St), bought as strata lots with a Form B package. They are Delta's verified Tsawwassen 55+ ownership stock, but they are not manufactured homes and not pads. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/tsawwassen-central-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Do I pay property tax on a home on a rented pad?** Yes — the home's owner is assessed and taxed like other residential owners, but the assessed value excludes the land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land; a principal-residence manufactured home can qualify for the Home Owner Grant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: Does SSMUH let a park tenant add units on the pad?** No. SSMUH (Delta Bylaws 8394/8395) grants added units on owned single-detached/duplex lots; it does not apply to pad tenancies. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
