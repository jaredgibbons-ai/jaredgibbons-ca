## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. A mobile home pad on a private Campbell Valley farm parcel is not, without more, an MHPTA park tenancy — the parcel's own title, zoning and ALR position govern, and both are UNVERIFIED per parcel in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser. Before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules that apply. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director — the decision-maker is the "director," never an "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; the park owner must respond so the home owner receives the response by the 10th day after the request is received, silence by the end of the 10th day is deemed consent, and no fee or benefit may be charged for consent. On assignment the purchaser becomes the tenant on the same terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Park rules are part of the tenancy agreement; a landlord may change park rules only once a year and with 3 full months' notice to tenants, and a rule is enforceable only if it applies to all tenants fairly, is clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, is properly noticed, and does not change a material term of the tenancy agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, with at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula: the inflation rate (the 12-month average change in the BC all-items Consumer Price Index) plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utilities, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park). Verified live from the RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as a park rate anywhere in this file, and no pad figure is documented in this slug's own research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42) — only as official sources support.** A landlord converting all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use, or to a residential use other than a manufactured home park, must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice to end tenancy, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; a tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, paying rent to the move-out date. Additional assessed-value and $5,000 / 12-months-pad-rent amounts are stated only as the M1 files state them, with the 15-day dispute window. Finer detail: UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited provincial pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; on a sale, the original signed Bill of Sale and the Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership are filed through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary), and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. This applies to a manufactured home sitting on a Campbell Valley farm parcel exactly as it does in a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Property tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, and the assessed value does not include the value of the land when the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. A manufactured home occupied as a principal residence by its registered owner can qualify for the Home Owner Grant, applied for each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Township of Langley property taxes:** taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened in this program settles the property transfer tax treatment of a home-only purchase on a rented pad, so no flat claim either way is stated as fact. The one PTT point the Province states expressly is that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a new manufactured home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. How PTT and GST are apportioned on a specific Campbell Valley farm/acreage purchase is likewise UNVERIFIED — no farm-transfer tax source was opened in this topic's research. A lawyer or notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption (research file §4.)
- **PTT general rates — for transfers where PTT does apply (own-land / strata-lot transfers, the Campbell Valley farm form).** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 fully residential own-land purchase with no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 first-time-buyer figure at that price point in the program anchors); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M fully residential→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (applies only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is verified in Campbell Valley).** Form B diligence (within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse / caregiver / legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. On ALR ground, depositing a strata plan in any case causes subdivision and needs ALC permission (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Township frame.** Zoning Bylaw 2500 governs; the coach-house definition is in s.102 (a fee-simple accessory unit in the standing frame) and the Township suite cap is 120 m². SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, NO 6-unit tier — Campbell Valley acreage parcels are measured in acres, well over the ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** Servicing: Township Development and Servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED in the verified ledger. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **ALR block — manufactured homes on farm ground, in full.** For parcels that ARE in the ALR: one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; at most one additional residence is permitted, up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. A manufactured home counts as a residence under that frame — it is permitted on ALR ground only where it fits the permitted-residence counts and sizes, or through the ALR Use Regulation s.34(3) / ALC Policy L-25 manufactured-home residence route and, failing that, a Non-Adhering Residential Use application to the ALC. **A general farm-worker manufactured-housing entitlement is UNCONFIRMED** — no source opened in this program states one. Subdivision (including depositing a strata plan, Bulletin 09 s.6) and non-farm use inside the ALR require ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment):** farm classification is voluntary and property-by-property — $2,500 gross farm income if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha — and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. The flat "$2,500+" shorthand is an error pattern owned by the horse-properties page only; it is NOT documented in this slug's research and is never attributed to this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **GST on a manufactured home — only at cited-CRA level.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — a used home is generally exempt and a new home is taxable only as sourced at that general level (CRA GI-003 general level for land sales in the acreages sibling); anything finer is UNCONFIRMED until the home's and seller's status are established. Quota and supply-management positions for any operation in this feed: UNVERIFIED in every particular — no marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in this topic's research. (Research file §4; ~/workspace/local-notes/acreages/campbell-valley-acreages-farms-for-sale-acreages-2026-11-01.md)
- **First-home programs.** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: If I buy in a Langley park elsewhere, do I own the land?** In a pad-rental park, no: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad** — the park owns the land, and your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, by no more than the provincial cap — 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027 (percentages only). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me as a buyer?** Yes, in a pad-rental park — assignment needs the park owner's prior written consent, requested on RTB Form 10, response due by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee for consent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf
- **Q: How is a manufactured-home purchase registered, and why does the CSA label matter?** Through the Manufactured Home Registry — the sale is only effective once registered, filed by a lawyer or notary with the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, and the CSA certification label must be attached to the home at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
