## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT MOBILE HOMES

- **MHPTA — the governing Act for a pad purchase.** The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act applies to a person who owns a manufactured home and rents the site it sits on; a home on land the buyer owns is dealt with through the land title system instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment, not a new tenancy.** When a home in a park is sold, the existing pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser; before the seller asks the park owner to consent, the seller must give the proposed purchaser a copy of any written part of the tenancy agreement and any written park rules. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Park-owner consent and the 10-day rule (MHPTA s.28 / RTB Form 10).** Prior written consent (or deemed consent, or an order of the director — never "arbitrator"), requested in writing on RTB Form 10; response must reach the home owner by the 10th day after receipt, silence by end of day 10 is deemed consent, NO fee or benefit may be charged; on assignment the purchaser becomes tenant on the same terms, the pad rent stays the same and the next increase date does not reset. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo62/loo62/161_2000
- **Park rules.** Part of the tenancy agreement; changeable only once a year and with 3 full months' notice; enforceable only if fair to all tenants, clear enough for a reasonable tenant to follow, properly noticed, and not changing a material term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/manufactured-home-tenancy
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages ONLY.** Once every 12 months, at least 3 full months' notice, capped by formula (CPI + proportional amount = levy + regulated-utility change, divided by sites); the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. Verified live from RTB (Nov 1, 2026): **2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure is stated as fact anywhere in this file — this directory page itself states no pad rent for any park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Park closure / conversion / compensation (MHPTA s.42).** All necessary permits and approvals BEFORE notice, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes; early move-out on at least 10 days' written notice paying rent to the move-out date; the 15-day dispute window and additional assessed-value / $5,000/12-months-pad-rent amounts only as the M1 files state them. Finer detail UNCONFIRMED beyond the cited pages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Home-as-chattel / Manufactured Home Registry.** A sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; original signed Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership filed through a qualified supplier (lawyer/notary); a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (research file §4.)
- **Property tax on a park home.** Assessed/taxed like other residential property; assessed value excludes land value when the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner; Home Owner Grant for a principal residence, applied for yearly. The monthly-cost stack a park buyer carries is pad rent + home insurance + tax on the home excluding land + utilities as the specific park's agreement allocates them — structure only, no dollar figure for any element. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home (research file §4.)
- **PTT / transfer treatment of a manufactured home — UNCONFIRMED as a flat rule.** No primary source opened settles PTT on a home-only purchase on a rented pad; NO flat claim either way as fact (both flat page claims UNVERIFIED). The Province states expressly that the newly built home exemption includes "a manufactured home that is placed and affixed on a parcel of vacant land" (not previously occupied) — relevant to placing a NEW home on the buyer's own land, not to a resale home on a rented pad. A lawyer/notary must confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure; per this slug's lots sibling, PTT treatment follows the actual transaction (home vs land). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **PTT general rates (own-land / strata-lot transfers).** 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked example, inputs stated: $700,000 fully residential, no exemption = **$12,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $900K→$16,000; $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→**$93,000 including the further 2% tier**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Strata-form parks — BC strata block (only if a manufactured-home community is a bare-land strata; none is cross-referenced as such for Langley here).** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + up to 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle, 5+ lots, since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a 45+ bylaw is VOID and does NOT convert to 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning / SSMUH — Langley frame.** Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500 / SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (NO 6-plex tier), suite 120 m², coach house fee-simple s.102, servicing Bylaw 5382 CONFIRMED; the park-zone section of Bylaw 2500 was not opened in this research — UNVERIFIED. City: Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number only (adoption UNVERIFIED), SSMUH 3284, suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%. **SSMUH does not apply to pad tenancies.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4.)
- **GST — only as CRA sources support.** GST applies to new construction, not resales — used home generally exempt, new home taxable only as sourced at that general level; anything finer UNCONFIRMED until home/seller status is established.
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST/HST Rebate is for new HOUSING (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026 per CRA**, never March 12): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. NHR 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000. FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. A resale park home is not new housing for this rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing.** Lender/insurer rules for manufactured homes (chattel vs land-home, age/foundation) UNVERIFIED at official-source level — confirm for the specific home/tenure before removing subjects.
- **Foreclosure — where met.** Settled court process (Rules 21-7/13-5, PD-66, Form 60); Form B + PTT/GST on court sales UNCONFIRMED. REGISTRY LEDGER: Langley is NOT in the ledger. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/168_2009_07

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many mobile home parks are there in Langley?** No official count exists — there is no official Langley park registry, and this page's six-park list is site copy sourced from MLS listings. The live listing pages show additional parks with homes for sale that this page does not name, so six is not a complete count. (Research file §§5, 7.)
- **Q: Are the age and pet rules on this directory page reliable?** They are the page's listing-sourced copy, and the page itself warns they come from recent MLS listings and can change — confirm the current rules and pad fee with the park manager before writing an offer, exactly as the page advises. Every rule in the page's list is UNVERIFIED against a park source. (Research file §5.)
- **Q: Why are Pioneer Park and Langley Grove Estates not on this page?** UNVERIFIED as an editorial matter — the research documents only that they do not appear, while both have homes in the live listing cards. Their treatments live in their home files (§2), not here. (Research file §5.)
- **Q: In a pad-rental park, do I own the land?** No: **you own the home (a chattel) and rent the pad**; your right to the site is the assigned pad tenancy under the MHPTA. Pioneer Park is the exception in form — a co-op, documented at its Aldergrove guide home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/starting-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much is pad rent in these parks?** This page states no pad rent for any park, and no official source publishes a Langley figure, average or range. The figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (Research file §§2, 5.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027; once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice; the proportional amount is park-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does a park have to approve me, and does my rent reset when I buy?** Approval: yes — prior written consent on RTB Form 10, response by the 10th day, silence deemed consent, no fee. Reset: no — on assignment the pad rent stays the same and the next increase date does not change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/forms/rtb10.pdf (research file §4.)
- **Q: What happens if a park closes?** Permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation by the effective date (MHPTA s.42). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What should I check before anything else on a park home?** The CSA label — without the original legible CSA decal the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — and the Manufactured Home Registry record, since the sale is only effective once registered. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Which schools serve a park address?** SD35, address-based; no catchment for any specific park address is on record — UNVERIFIED until run in the SD35 locator. Brookswood Secondary and Aldergrove Community Secondary in §1 are physical locations only. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
