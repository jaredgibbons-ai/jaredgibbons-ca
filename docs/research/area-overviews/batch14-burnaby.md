# Area overviews for the website --- batch 14 (Burnaby)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, and industrial or conservation designations) should still be confirmed with the City of Burnaby or the Burnaby School District (SD41) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. The City of Burnaby organizes growth around four official town centres: Metrotown (the regional city centre), Brentwood, Edmonds, and Lougheed. Many names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas inside or beside those centres and the city's major parks. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.
