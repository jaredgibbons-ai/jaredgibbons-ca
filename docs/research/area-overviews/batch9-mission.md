# Area overviews for the website --- batch 9 (Mission)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, flood mapping, reservoir or shoreline rules, and ALR status) should still be confirmed with the District of Mission, the Fraser Valley Regional District, BC Hydro, or the Mission School District (SD75) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short "Jared's take" already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. Where a formal take was not found on the fetched page, the overview still follows the existing page copy and official sources.

The District of Mission's urban area sits on the north side of the Fraser River, with Highway 11 (the Mission Bridge) to Abbotsford and Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway) east and west. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas. Dewdney, Deroche, and Durieu are in the Fraser Valley Regional District, not inside District of Mission limits. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.

## Dewdney Deroche

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/dewdney-deroche.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Dewdney Deroche, near Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Dewdney Deroche Homes & Acreages \| near Mission

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Dewdney and Deroche, near Mission, in FVRD Electoral Area G along Highway 7 toward Nicomen Island.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Dewdney Deroche; Dewdney Deroche real estate; living in Dewdney Deroche, near Mission; Deroche BC homes; Dewdney BC acreages

Dewdney Deroche is a real estate board name for two unincorporated communities east of the District of Mission, along Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway). They are in Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area G, which the FVRD also describes as including Nicomen Island, Deroche, Dewdney, and Hatzic Island. Listings may carry a Mission mailing address, but the District of Mission does not govern these parcels.

Homes for sale in Dewdney Deroche are mainly acreages, farms, and country houses rather than townhouse streets. Nicomen Island and the Fraser River sit to the south of the highway communities, and the valley wall rises to the north. Daily services are a drive west into Mission or east toward Agassiz and Harrison Mills. Rural lots are often in the Agricultural Land Reserve; building size, extra dwellings, and changes of use must be checked on the specific parcel.

Transit is limited on the rural highway; most trips are by car. Nearby public schools include Dewdney Elementary and Deroche Elementary, with middle and secondary students often attending schools in Mission. Catchments still vary, so check the Mission School District (SD75) school locator. Confirm servicing, well and septic, floodplain designation, and ALR status with the FVRD.

## Durieu

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/durieu.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Durieu, near Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Durieu Homes & Acreages for Sale \| near Mission

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Durieu, near Mission, a rural FVRD Electoral Area F community toward Hatzic Prairie and McConnell Creek.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Durieu; Durieu Mission real estate; living in Durieu, near Mission; Hatzic Prairie nearby; Durieu BC acreages

Durieu is a rural community near the District of Mission, in Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area F. The FVRD also names Hatzic Prairie and McConnell Creek in this electoral area. Durieu is not inside District of Mission limits, even when a listing uses a Mission address. The local roads sit north of the Hatzic area, away from the urban grid.

Homes for sale in Durieu are mainly acreages, hobby farms, and country houses on larger lots. Neighbours include Hatzic Prairie and the rural roads toward Dewdney. There is no urban shopping street here; most errands are a drive into Mission or along Highway 7. Much of the land is agricultural or forested, so ALR status and any forestry or watercourse rules should be confirmed on the parcel.

Transit is limited; most trips are by car. Nearby public schools include Dewdney Elementary, with middle and secondary students often attending schools in Mission. Check the SD75 school locator for the address you are considering. Confirm well and septic servicing, access, and zoning with the FVRD before you plan a new dwelling or a change of use.

## Hatzic

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/hatzic.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Hatzic, Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Hatzic Homes for Sale \| Mission BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Hatzic, Mission, around Hatzic Lake and Hatzic Park, including City bench streets and nearby FVRD prairie and island lots.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Hatzic; Hatzic Mission real estate; living in Hatzic, Mission; Hatzic Lake homes; Hatzic Park Mission

Hatzic, next to and partly inside the District of Mission, is a real estate board name that covers more than one jurisdiction. Streets on the Hatzic bench sit inside the District of Mission. Hatzic Prairie is in FVRD Electoral Area F, and Hatzic Island is in Electoral Area G. Confirm which government applies to the parcel before you compare taxes, zoning, or services.

Homes for sale in Hatzic include houses on the municipal bench streets, lakefront and rural lots around Hatzic Lake, and properties on the prairie and the island. Hatzic Park, at 8422 Draper Street, is a City of Mission park with playgrounds, paved trails, and benches. The lake and the surrounding farm roads are the landmarks people use to describe this side of Mission.

Municipal streets are a short drive from downtown Mission, Highway 7, and Highway 11. Rural prairie and island lots have less frequent transit; most trips are by car. Nearby public schools include Hatzic Elementary, with middle and secondary students often attending schools in Mission. Catchments still vary, so check the SD75 school locator. Confirm floodplain, lakeshore, and servicing rules with the District or the FVRD, as appropriate.

## Mission BC

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-bc.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Mission BC, Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Mission BC Homes for Sale \| District of Mission

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Mission BC, in the District of Mission core, near downtown, Mission City Station, the Leisure Centre and the Mission Bridge.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Mission BC; Mission BC real estate; living in Mission BC, Mission; Mission City Station; Mission Leisure Centre

Mission BC is a real estate board name for the urban core of the District of Mission, on the north bank of the Fraser River. Downtown Mission gathers around 1st Avenue and Mission City Station, with Highway 11 crossing the Mission Bridge to Abbotsford and Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway) running east and west through town.

Homes for sale in Mission BC include houses on the established hillside and valley streets, townhouses, and apartment buildings closer to downtown and the station. The Mission Leisure Centre is at 7650 Grand Street. Fraser River Heritage Park has trails, picnic sites, and a rose garden above the river. Centennial Park, at 11th Avenue and Taulbut Street, offers playgrounds, courts, and trails. Heritage Park Centre is another recreation destination on the east side of town.

Mission City Station is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express, with weekday peak-period trains into Metro Vancouver. BC Transit's Mission Exchange connects local routes, including service to Abbotsford and neighbourhood routes such as 32 West Heights, 33 Cedar Valley, and 34 East Side. Nearby public schools include École Mission Central, Edwin S. Richards, Windebank, and Cherry Hill elementary schools, plus Heritage Park Secondary and Mission Secondary. Check the SD75 school locator.

## Mission West

**Suggested H2:** Living in Mission West, Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Mission West Homes for Sale \| Mission BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Mission West, Mission, on the west side of the District toward West Heights and Silverdale, near the Leisure Centre and Highway 7.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Mission West; Mission West real estate; living in Mission West, Mission; West Heights Mission; Silverdale Mission homes

Mission West is a real estate board name for the west side of the District of Mission. It is inside municipal limits, not in the Fraser Valley Regional District. The City does not publish a board-style boundary for this name. Official clues include the west-side streets toward West Heights and Silverdale, which BC Transit serves with route 32 West Heights, and the short drive east to downtown Mission.

Homes for sale in Mission West are often houses on larger lots, with some townhouses closer to the built-up west side of town, rather than a downtown apartment core. The Mission Leisure Centre on Grand Street and the shops of downtown Mission are a short drive. Highway 7 continues west toward Maple Ridge and east through the town centre to Hatzic.

Transit is available on the West Heights route and other local buses that connect to the Mission Exchange and Mission City Station; many trips are still by car. Nearby public schools are on the west and central side of the District; catchments still vary, so check the SD75 school locator. Confirm zoning, lot size, and servicing with the District of Mission for a specific address.

## Stave Falls

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/stave-falls.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Stave Falls, Mission

**Suggested meta title:** Stave Falls Homes & Acreages \| Mission BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Stave Falls, Mission, around Stave Lake, Hayward Lake and the Stave River, near Stave Falls Elementary.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Stave Falls; Stave Falls Mission real estate; living in Stave Falls, Mission; Hayward Lake Mission; Stave Lake homes

Stave Falls, in the District of Mission, is a rural community at the north end of the municipality, around Stave Lake, Hayward Lake, and the Stave River. It is inside city limits, even though the streets feel country. The Stave Falls Generating Station, a BC Hydro site, is a local landmark. Shoreline around the lakes is part of a hydroelectric reservoir, so water levels, access, and tenure should be confirmed with BC Hydro and the District.

Homes for sale in Stave Falls are mainly acreages, forested lots, and country houses, including properties with a view of the lakes. Recreation gathers around Hayward Lake and the dam area, with trails and day-use sites rather than a town shopping street. Everyday errands are a drive south toward Steelhead and the urban core of Mission.

Transit is limited; most trips are by car. Nearby public schools include Stave Falls Elementary. Middle and secondary students often attend schools in urban Mission. Check the SD75 school locator. Confirm well and septic or municipal servicing, reservoir setbacks, and rural zoning with the District before you plan a new dwelling.

## Steelhead

Steelhead, in the District of Mission, is a hillside and acreage community west and north of the urban core, on the way toward Stave Falls and Hayward Lake. The District's rural planning work names Steelhead Hall as a local gathering place. Steelhead is inside municipal limits, not an FVRD electoral-area community.

Homes for sale in Steelhead are mainly houses on larger, often forested lots, rather than downtown apartments or compact townhouse streets. The setting is between the west side of Mission and the Stave Lakes recreation area, so many residents drive to Hayward Lake or into town for shops and the Mission Leisure Centre.

Transit is limited on the hillside roads; most trips are by car, with connections toward Highway 7 and downtown Mission. Nearby public schools include Stave Falls Elementary, with other Mission elementary and secondary schools a drive into town. Catchments still vary, so check the SD75 school locator. Confirm servicing, slope, and rural zoning with the District for a specific parcel.
