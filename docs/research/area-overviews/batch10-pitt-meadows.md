## Central Meadows

Central Meadows is a real estate board name for the urban core of the City of Pitt Meadows, around Harris Road. The City does not publish a boundary under this name. Official anchors include Pitt Meadows City Hall, Pitt Meadows Library at 12099 Harris Road, Pitt Meadows Elementary at 11941 Harris Road, and Pitt Meadows West Coast Express Station at 12185 Harris Road.

Homes for sale in Central Meadows include apartments and townhouses closer to the station and civic buildings, and houses on the surrounding residential streets toward Ford Road, Hammond Road, and Blakely Road. Harris Road is the main north--south street, running from the Fraser River dikes north across Lougheed Highway (Highway 7). Everyday shops and services cluster along this corridor.

The West Coast Express and TransLink buses on Lougheed Highway and Harris Road connect Pitt Meadows to Maple Ridge, Port Coquitlam, and Vancouver. The Golden Ears Bridge and the Pitt River Bridge are a short drive. Nearby public schools include Pitt Meadows Elementary, Davie Jones Elementary, and Pitt Meadows Secondary. Catchments still vary, so check the Maple Ridge--Pitt Meadows School District (SD42) school locator. Parts of the core sit in a municipal drainage area; confirm dike, flood, and servicing designations with the City.

Mid Meadows is a real estate board name for an established residential part of the City of Pitt Meadows, around the Bonson Road and Blakely Road streets between the Harris Road core and the south and west sides of town. The City does not publish a boundary for this name, and board listings can sit close to South Meadows along the same roads.

Homes for sale in Mid Meadows are mainly houses, with townhouses and some apartment buildings closer to the built-up streets. Davie Jones Elementary is on Blakely Road, and Edith McDermott Elementary is on Bonson Road. Pitt Meadows Athletic Park and the Pitt Meadows Arena, also on Bonson Road, are nearby recreation sites. The dike trails along the Pitt River and Fraser River are a short ride or walk from many of these streets.

Harris Road and Lougheed Highway are the main routes to Pitt Meadows Station, Meadowtown Centre, and the Golden Ears and Pitt River bridges. Nearby public schools include Davie Jones Elementary, Edith McDermott Elementary, and Pitt Meadows Secondary. Check the SD42 school locator. Confirm drainage, dike, and zoning designations with the City for a specific address.

North Meadows is a real estate board name for the north side of the City of Pitt Meadows, generally north of Lougheed Highway (Highway 7), toward Pitt Lake and the Alouette River. The City does not publish a boundary under this name. Official roads and places on this side of the city include Harris Road as it continues north, Neaves Road, Kennedy Road, and Hoffmann Park, reached from 122 Avenue and Advent Road.

Homes for sale in North Meadows include country houses, acreages, and farm properties, with some residential streets closer to the highway. Much of the land north and northeast of the urban core is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. The City's dike and greenway network continues toward the Pitt and Alouette rivers, and Pitt Lake sits farther north as a regional landmark.

Transit is limited north of the highway; most trips are by car, with Lougheed Highway and Harris Road the main links to Pitt Meadows Station and Meadowtown Centre. Nearby public schools include Highland Park Elementary on Advent Road and Pitt Meadows Elementary closer to the civic core. Check the SD42 school locator. South Meadows is a real estate board name for the south side of the City of Pitt Meadows, toward the Fraser River. The City does not publish a boundary for this name, and it can sit close to Mid Meadows along Bonson Road. Official landmarks on this side of town include Harris Landing, at Harris Road and Fraser Way, and the Pitt River Regional Greenway along the dike.

Homes for sale in South Meadows include houses, townhouses, and side-by-side homes on the residential streets between the civic core and the river. The dike paths are used for walking, cycling, and riding, and they connect west toward the Pitt River. Edith McDermott Elementary on Bonson Road is nearby, and the Harris Road corridor leads back to the library, shops, and Pitt Meadows Station.

TransLink buses and the West Coast Express serve the Harris Road core a short drive north. The Pitt River Bridge and Golden Ears Bridge are close by. Nearby public schools include Edith McDermott Elementary, Davie Jones Elementary, and Pitt Meadows Secondary. Check the SD42 school locator. Low-lying lots beside the Fraser should be checked for dike, flood, and drainage designations with the City.

Official places on this side of the city include Pitt Meadows Regional Airport, which sits north of the Fraser River between the Pitt River and Golden Ears bridges, Osprey Village on the Pitt River waterfront, and large agricultural parcels the City identifies in the southwest.

Homes for sale in West Meadows include houses and townhouses on the residential streets toward the airport and the river, as well as farm and acreage properties. Pitt Meadows Athletic Park and the arena on Bonson Road are close to the airport side of town. Highland Park Elementary on Advent Road is another nearby school.

Lougheed Highway, the Golden Ears Bridge, and the Pitt River Bridge are the main regional routes; Meadowtown Centre sits on Lougheed Highway near the Maple Ridge border. ## Notes on sources and items left out

Nothing in the body text is marked UNCONFIRMED. Facts were taken from official pages of the City of Pitt Meadows (including Osprey Village, parks and greenways, the library, the Athletic Park, and Official Community Plan material), the Maple Ridge--Pitt Meadows School District (SD42), and TransLink, and were checked against the existing jaredgibbons.ca page for each area so the new copy complements, and does not repeat or contradict, any short take already on the page. The following were left out because they were not clearly confirmed on official sources, or because they could mislead:

-   Population, land-area, dike-length, park-size, and housing-count figures (even where a city page publishes them).
-   Exact legal boundaries for the board names Central Meadows, Mid Meadows, North Meadows, South Meadows, and West Meadows. The copy uses official civic landmarks only.
-   Store tenants and private marina or airport-operator details, other than Osprey Village and Meadowtown Centre as recognized commercial nodes.
-   Drainage rates, flood-construction elevations, and airport noise contours, which are parcel-specific.
-   The planning sheet marked all five Pitt Meadows pages as already having a short "Jared's take." A formal verdict was found only on the West Meadows guide (open space, farmland, and aircraft noise). No formal take block was found on the fetched Central, Mid, North, or South Meadows listing pages, which open with a short "browse listings" line instead.
