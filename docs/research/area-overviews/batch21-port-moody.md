# Area overviews for the website --- batch 21 (Port Moody, Anmore, and Belcarra)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, heritage, foreshore, and slope rules) should still be confirmed with the City of Port Moody, the Village of Anmore, the Village of Belcarra, or School District 43 (Coquitlam) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. The City of Port Moody sits at the east end of Burrard Inlet, with Moody Centre, Inlet Centre, the west-side streets toward Coquitlam, and the Heritage Mountain hillside on the north shore. Anmore and Belcarra are listed in this Port Moody group on the planning sheet, but they are separate village municipalities, not Port Moody neighbourhoods. Several other names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.

## Anmore

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/anmore-homes-for-sale.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Anmore, near Port Moody

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Anmore, a separate village municipality near Port Moody, toward Buntzen Lake and the north-side forests.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Anmore; Anmore real estate; living in Anmore, BC; Village of Anmore; Buntzen Lake

Anmore is the Village of Anmore, a separate municipality in Metro Vancouver, north of the City of Port Moody, around Sunnyside Road and the forested slopes toward Buntzen Lake. It is not a neighbourhood of Port Moody. The village has a semi-rural character, with larger lots and a quieter setting than Port Moody Centre or Inlet Centre. Belcarra is the neighbouring village to the west, on the inlet.

Homes for sale in Anmore are mainly houses, often on larger lots, rather than apartments. Buntzen Lake and the surrounding forest trails are the everyday recreation. Everyday shops are limited inside the village and are a short drive down into Port Moody, including the Inlet Centre and Moody Centre shops.

Transit is limited in the village, with buses more frequent down in Port Moody at Moody Centre Station and Inlet Centre Station. Nearby public schools are in School District 43 (Coquitlam), which also serves Port Moody, Anmore, and Belcarra. Check the SD43 school locator. Confirm village zoning and lot rules with the Village of Anmore.

Heritage Mountain, in the City of Port Moody, is a planned hillside community on the north side of the inlet, around Heritage Mountain Boulevard, above Moody Centre and the Ioco shore. The City treats it as a distinct hillside residential area. Heritage Woods sits farther up the slope, and Mountain Meadows is a neighbouring mid-slope pocket.

Port Moody Centre, in the City of Port Moody, is the historic townsite and civic core, around Moody Centre Station, St. Johns Street, Murray Street, and the east end of the inlet. The City recognizes Moody Centre as a distinct area, separate from Inlet Centre (Newport Village and Suter Brook) farther west along the inlet. Rocky Point Park sits at the inlet's east end.

Homes for sale in Port Moody Centre include older houses on the townsite streets, plus apartments and townhouses closer to the station and the inlet. The park, the inlet trails, and the station precinct are the everyday setting. Shops and services are in the townsite and a short trip to Inlet Centre.

The Millennium Line and West Coast Express at Moody Centre Station, and buses on St. Johns Street, serve the area. Nearby public schools include those in the Moody Centre family. Check the SD43 school locator.
