# Area overviews for the website --- batch 16 (Coquitlam)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, escarpment, and ravine rules) should still be confirmed with the City of Coquitlam or School District 43 (Coquitlam) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. The City of Coquitlam is organized around City Centre (Town Centre Park and Lafarge Lake), historic Maillardville, Burquitlam / Austin Heights, Westwood Plateau, and the Northeast / Burke Mountain growth area. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.
