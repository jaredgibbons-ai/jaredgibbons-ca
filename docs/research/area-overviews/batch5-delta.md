# Area overviews for the website --- batch 5 (Delta)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, heritage rules, flood mapping, ALR status, and foreshore tenure) should still be confirmed with the City of Delta, the Agricultural Land Commission, or the Delta School District (SD37) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement the short "Jared's take" already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. Where a formal take was not found on the fetched page, the overview still follows the existing page copy and official sources.

The City of Delta is made up of three communities: North Delta, Ladner, and Tsawwassen. Many of the names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas, not official City neighbourhoods. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.

Ladner Rural is a real estate board name for the farm and river land west and southwest of Ladner Village, in the City of Delta. It is not an official City neighbourhood, and the City publishes no boundary for the name. The board area is separate from East Delta and includes the Westham Island side, which is reached only by the Westham Island Bridge off River Road west of Ladner.

Properties here include farmhouses, acreages, and some river-side dwellings. Much of the land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve and is designated for agriculture in the Official Community Plan, so building size, extra dwellings, and changes of use are tightly limited. Confirm ALR status, zoning, and servicing --- some parcels are on well and septic rather than City water and sewer --- before you write an offer. The George C. Reifel Migratory Bird Sanctuary is at the north end of Westham Island. Deas Island Regional Park and Ladner Village shops, the leisure centre, and the library are a short drive from the inland side.

Transit is limited on the rural roads and on Westham Island; Ladner Exchange is the usual bus connection, with route 601 to the Canada Line. Nearby public schools include Ladner-area elementary schools and Delta Secondary, but catchments vary widely on rural roads. Check the exact address with the Delta School District (SD37) locator. Also confirm flood construction and drainage or irrigation details with the City.

## East Delta

East Delta is a real estate board name for the farming area east of Ladner, in the City of Delta, along Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10) toward Burns Bog and the Surrey border. It is not a separate town or an official City neighbourhood. The City plans this land under its city-wide Official Community Plan; an older East Ladner area plan no longer applies as a separate schedule. Highways 99, 17, and 10 are the main regional routes.

Properties for sale include farms, acreages, and some houses on the urban edge of east Ladner. Much of the land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, so farm-house size, additional dwellings, and subdivision are controlled by provincial and City rules and must be checked parcel by parcel. Historic East Delta Hall, at 10379 Ladner Trunk Road, is a City-owned community hall in this farming area. Boundary Bay Airport is nearby. John Oliver Park, at 11590 Ladner Trunk Road, is a local park. Shopping, Delta Hospital, and civic facilities are a drive west into Ladner, or north toward North Delta. This is low farm land behind dikes, so confirm flood construction and drainage with the City.

Bus service is limited on the rural roads; Ladner Trunk Road and the highway corridors are the usual links to Ladner Exchange or North Delta. There is no single school that serves the whole board area. Check the exact address with the Delta School District (SD37) locator.
