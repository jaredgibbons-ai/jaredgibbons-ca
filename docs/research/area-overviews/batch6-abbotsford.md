# Area overviews for the website --- batch 6 (Abbotsford)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, flood mapping, steep-slope rules, airport zoning, and ALR status) should still be confirmed with the City of Abbotsford, the Fraser Valley Regional District, the Agricultural Land Commission, or the Abbotsford School District (SD34) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement the short "Jared's take" already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. Where a formal take was not found on the fetched page, the overview still follows the existing page copy and official sources.

The City of Abbotsford describes distinct neighbourhoods and agricultural areas, including Aberdeen, Bradner, Clayburn, City Centre, Historic Downtown, the UDistrict, McKee, Eagle Mountain, Matsqui Prairie, and Sumas Prairie. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas that group more than one of those places. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.

## Abbotsford East

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-east.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Abbotsford East, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Abbotsford East Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Abbotsford East, Abbotsford, near McKee Peak, Clayburn and the Whatcom Road interchange. Parks, schools and Highway 1 access.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Abbotsford East; Abbotsford East real estate; living in Abbotsford East, Abbotsford; McKee Peak homes; Clayburn Abbotsford

Abbotsford East is a real estate board name for the east side of the City of Abbotsford, around Whatcom Road and Highway 1 (Exit 95). The City plans this side of town as several neighbourhoods, including McKee, Eagle Mountain, Clayburn, and McMillan, rather than as a single official area called Abbotsford East. The streets climb from the valley floor toward McKee Peak, with Old Clayburn Road and McMillan Road among the main local routes.

Homes for sale in Abbotsford East include houses on the established McMillan and Clayburn streets, newer hillside houses toward McKee Peak and Eagle Mountain, and townhouses closer to Whatcom Road. The Abbotsford Recreation Centre is on McMillan Road. Clayburn Village, a historic commercial pocket the City names as its own neighbourhood, sits on this side of town. DeLair Park and the east reaches of the Discovery Trail are nearby. Daily shopping is along Whatcom Road and a short drive west toward the city centre and Mill Lake.

The Whatcom Road interchange is the usual Highway 1 connection, and BC Transit local routes serve McMillan, McKee, Sandy Hill, and Auguston. The Fraser Valley Express (route 66) runs on the highway toward Langley and Chilliwack. Nearby public schools include Sandy Hill Elementary, Mountain Elementary, Margaret Stenersen Elementary, Clayburn Middle, and Robert Bateman Secondary. Catchments vary across this large board area, so check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator for the exact address. On lower lots near the Sumas River or Whatcom Road, and on hillside lots, confirm flood mapping and steep-slope rules with the City.

## Abbotsford West

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-west.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Abbotsford West, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Abbotsford West Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Abbotsford West, Abbotsford, near Clearbrook, Highstreet and Exhibition Park. See west-side parks, schools and Highway 1 access.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Abbotsford West; Abbotsford West real estate; living in Abbotsford West, Abbotsford; Clearbrook Abbotsford homes; Highstreet Abbotsford

Abbotsford West is a real estate board name for the west side of the City of Abbotsford, around Clearbrook Road and Mount Lehman Road. The City uses neighbourhood names such as Clearbrook and Townline for parts of this area, rather than "Abbotsford West" as a single official neighbourhood. Highway 1 runs along the south edge, with interchanges at Mount Lehman Road (Exit 83) and Clearbrook Road (Exit 87). Maclure Road, Townline Road, and Downes Road are the usual local streets.

Homes for sale in Abbotsford West range from detached houses on the Townline and Blueridge streets to townhouses and apartments closer to Clearbrook and the Highstreet shopping and transit area. Matsqui Recreation Centre is on Clearbrook Road. Abbotsford Exhibition Park, on Haida Drive, is the large sports and events park on this side of town. Fishtrap Creek Park and Ellwood Park add local green space. Clearbrook Library is nearby. Highstreet is the main shopping node, with more daily services along Clearbrook Road.

A new transit exchange at Highstreet is the west-side bus hub, and the Fraser Valley Express (route 66) uses Highway 1. Nearby public schools include Blue Jay Elementary, Harry Sayers Elementary, Clearbrook Elementary, Eugene Reimer Middle, Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle, and Rick Hansen Secondary. Confirm the catchment with the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator, because elementary and secondary boundaries in this part of the city split by address.

## Aberdeen (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/aberdeen.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Aberdeen, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Aberdeen Homes & Acreages for Sale \| Abbotsford

**Suggested meta description:** Aberdeen, Abbotsford acreages and homes near Fraser Highway and Bradner Road, west of Clearbrook and close to Highway 13 and the airport.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Aberdeen Abbotsford; Aberdeen Abbotsford real estate; living in Aberdeen, Abbotsford; Aberdeen acreages Abbotsford; Aberdeen Elementary

Aberdeen, in the City of Abbotsford, is one of the rural neighbourhoods the City names in its official community profile. The board area covers country roads west of the urban west side, toward the Township of Langley border and Highway 13 (the Aldergrove--Bellingham Highway). Highway 1 is reached at 264 Street (Exit 73) or Mount Lehman Road (Exit 83).

Homes for sale in Aberdeen are mainly acreages, farms, and country houses, with a smaller cluster of residential lots around the Fraser Highway pocket. Much of the land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, so building size, extra dwellings, and changes of use must be checked on the specific parcel. Aberdeen Elementary, at 2975 Bradner Road, is in the community. Aldergrove Regional Park lies just west, across the Langley border on Lefeuvre Road. Matsqui Recreation Centre and the shops of Clearbrook and Highstreet are a short drive east. Abbotsford International Airport sits to the southeast, and some lots fall under airport zoning; confirm that on the City's maps before you plan a taller building or a new dwelling.

Transit is limited on the rural roads; most trips are by car, with Highway 1 and Fraser Highway the main routes. Nearby public schools include Aberdeen Elementary, with middle and secondary students often attending schools in west Abbotsford such as Eugene Reimer Middle and Rick Hansen Secondary. Catchments still vary, so check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator. Rural lots may be on well and septic; confirm servicing, ALR status, and floodplain designation with the City.

## Bradner (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/bradner.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Bradner, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Bradner Homes & Acreages for Sale \| Abbotsford

**Suggested meta description:** Bradner, Abbotsford farms and acreages on the city's west side, near Bradner Park, Glen Valley Regional Park and Highway 1.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Bradner Abbotsford; Bradner Abbotsford real estate; living in Bradner, Abbotsford; Bradner acreages; Bradner Elementary

Bradner, in the City of Abbotsford, is a rural community the City lists among its established neighbourhoods, on the rolling land north of Highway 1 and west of Mount Lehman Road. Bradner Road is the main local spine, with Downes Road, Ross Road, and the Glen Valley reach of River Road among the other country roads. The Fraser River and Metro Vancouver's Glen Valley Regional Park lie to the north.

Homes for sale in Bradner are farms, hobby acreages, and country houses, not condos or townhouses. Confirm Agricultural Land Reserve status and zoning on the parcel, because they control what can be built or added. Bradner Elementary, at 5285 Bradner Road, and Bradner Park, at 5292 Bradner Road, sit in the historic centre, along with Bradner Community Hall. Shopping and indoor recreation are a drive south or east toward Mount Lehman, Clearbrook, and Matsqui Recreation Centre.

Highway 1 is reached at 264 Street or Mount Lehman Road. Bus service on the rural roads is limited. Nearby public schools include Bradner Elementary, with middle and secondary catchments that often point toward west Abbotsford schools. Check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator for the address. Also confirm well and septic records, and any flood or watercourse constraints, especially on lower Glen Valley land near the river.

## Central Abbotsford

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/central-abbotsford.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Central Abbotsford Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford, around Mill Lake, Historic Downtown and UFV. Condos, townhomes and houses near city amenities.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Central Abbotsford; Central Abbotsford real estate; living in Central Abbotsford, Abbotsford; Mill Lake Abbotsford homes; Historic Downtown Abbotsford

Central Abbotsford is a real estate board name for the middle of the City of Abbotsford, around South Fraser Way, McCallum Road, and Mill Lake. The City plans this core as City Centre, Historic Downtown, and the UDistrict, rather than as one official neighbourhood called Central Abbotsford. Highway 1 is immediately south, with interchanges at Clearbrook Road and McCallum Road (Exit 90). The University of the Fraser Valley and Rogers Forum sit along King Road on the south side of this core.

Homes for sale in Central Abbotsford include apartments and townhouses around Mill Lake and Historic Downtown, student-oriented housing near UFV, and detached houses in the older residential pockets such as the streets around Babich and Ravine Park. Mill Lake Park is the City's main urban park, with paths, play and sport areas, and the Kariton House gallery nearby. Jubilee Park and Ravine Park connect toward Historic Downtown on Essendene and Montrose Avenues. The civic precinct, Fraser Valley Regional Library, and the shops along South Fraser Way and the city centre are close, so many daily trips can be done without a long drive.

BC Transit local routes concentrate on this core, and the Fraser Valley Express (route 66) uses Highway 1. Nearby public schools include Centennial Park Elementary, Terry Fox Elementary, Godson Elementary, Jackson Elementary, Chief Dan George Middle, Abbotsford Middle, W.A. Fraser Middle, Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and W.J. Mouat Secondary. Catchments in the centre of the city change street by street, so check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator for the home you have in mind.

## Matsqui (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/matsqui.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Matsqui, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Matsqui Homes & Acreages for Sale \| Abbotsford

**Suggested meta description:** Matsqui, Abbotsford farms and village homes by the Fraser River, with Matsqui Trail, Highway 11 and the Mission Bridge close by.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Matsqui Abbotsford; Matsqui Abbotsford real estate; living in Matsqui, Abbotsford; Matsqui Prairie farms; Matsqui Village

Matsqui, in the City of Abbotsford, is the rural north side of the city, on Matsqui Prairie and in historic Matsqui Village, which the City names as an agricultural area along the Fraser River. Highway 11 (the Abbotsford--Mission Highway) and the Mission Bridge are the main links north to Mission. Harris Road, Gladwin Road North, Page Road, and Riverside Road are the usual prairie and village roads.

Homes for sale in Matsqui include working farms, acreages, and houses in Matsqui Village near Foreman Road and Elizabeth Avenue. Much of the prairie is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. Matsqui Trail Regional Park follows the Fraser River dike, from the Glen Valley side toward the Mission Bridge and Page Road. Willband Creek Park is along Highway 11. Village services are modest; larger shopping and recreation are a drive south into the city centre or west toward Clearbrook. This is diked floodplain, so confirm flood mapping and dike-related building rules with the City for the specific lot.

Highway 11 is the regional route; bus service is limited compared with the city centre. Nearby public schools include Máthxwi Elementary (formerly Matsqui Elementary) in Matsqui Village, with middle and secondary students often attending Clayburn Middle or Robert Bateman Secondary. Confirm the catchment with the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator, and check ALR and servicing details before you offer.

## Poplar (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/poplar.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Poplar, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Poplar Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Poplar, Abbotsford, south of Highway 1 near Marshall Road, Exhibition Park and the Huntingdon border crossing.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Poplar Abbotsford; Poplar Abbotsford real estate; living in Poplar, Abbotsford; South Poplar Abbotsford; Marshall Road Abbotsford homes

Poplar, in the City of Abbotsford, is a board sub-area for the land south of Highway 1, toward the Canada--United States border at the Huntingdon / Sumas crossing. The City uses names such as South Poplar for parts of this area. Clearbrook Road (Exit 87), Marshall Road, Peardonville Road, King Road, and Huntingdon Road are the main streets. Abbotsford International Airport and industrial land sit close to the west and south, so airport zoning can apply on some parcels.

Homes for sale in Poplar include suburban houses and townhouses on the north, nearer Marshall Road and Clearbrook Road, and acreages and farms farther south toward King Road and Huntingdon Road. South Poplar Park and Abbotsford Exhibition Park are the nearby parks. Matsqui Recreation Centre is a short drive north of the highway, and Mill Lake, UFV, and Rogers Forum are to the east. Confirm ALR status on the rural lots, and check industrial or airport overlays on properties along the Marshall Road corridor.

Local BC Transit routes serve Clearbrook Road and Marshall Road, and Highway 1 is immediately north. Nearby public schools include South Poplar Traditional Elementary, at 32746 Huntingdon Road, Dormick Park Elementary, and Dave Kandal Elementary, with middle and secondary catchments that can include Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle, W.A. Fraser Middle, Rick Hansen Secondary, or W.J. Mouat Secondary. A traditional school is not automatically the catchment school for every nearby address. Check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator.

## Sumas Mountain (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-mountain.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Sumas Mountain Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford BC

**Suggested meta description:** Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford homes and acreages near Auguston, McKee and Sumas Mountain Regional Park, with Whatcom Road and Highway 1 close by.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale on Sumas Mountain; Sumas Mountain Abbotsford real estate; living in Sumas Mountain, Abbotsford; Auguston Abbotsford homes; Sumas Mountain Regional Park

Sumas Mountain, in the City of Abbotsford, is the mountain on the city's east side, reached from Whatcom Road and Highway 1 (Exit 95). Sumas Mountain Road, Straiton Road, and Ledgeview Drive are among the main mountain roads. Some upper or outer roads are outside City limits and fall under the Fraser Valley Regional District, so confirm which government and which zoning apply to the parcel before you offer.

Homes for sale on Sumas Mountain include serviced subdivision houses on the lower benches and acreages higher up, including around Straiton. Sumas Mountain Regional Park, managed with Metro Vancouver and the FVRD, is the big natural park on the mountain, with trails and Chadsey Lake. The McKee trail network and Ledgeview Golf & Country Club are on the lower slopes. Shopping and the Abbotsford Recreation Centre are a drive down toward Whatcom Road and McMillan Road. Steep-slope and geotechnical rules can apply; confirm them with the City or the FVRD for the lot. Rural parcels may be on well and septic.

Bus service is limited higher on the mountain; the Whatcom interchange is the usual highway connection, with some local routes toward Auguston and McKee. Nearby public schools include Auguston Traditional Elementary, Mountain Elementary, Sandy Hill Elementary, Clayburn Middle, and Robert Bateman Secondary. Auguston Traditional is a district program school, so it is not automatically the catchment school. Check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator. ASIA Sumas Mountain is also a district program, not a catchment assignment.

## Sumas Prairie (Abbotsford)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/sumas-prairie.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Sumas Prairie, Abbotsford

**Suggested meta title:** Sumas Prairie Farms & Homes for Sale \| Abbotsford

**Suggested meta description:** Sumas Prairie, Abbotsford farmland between the city and Chilliwack, along Highway 1. See ALR farms, flood checks, parks and local schools.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Sumas Prairie; Sumas Prairie Abbotsford real estate; living in Sumas Prairie, Abbotsford; Sumas Prairie farms; Kilgard Abbotsford

Sumas Prairie, in the City of Abbotsford, is the broad agricultural valley the City names in its official community profile, east of the urban city and west of Chilliwack. Highway 1 crosses the prairie, with interchanges at Whatcom Road, Cole Road, No. 3 Road, and Flood Hope Road toward Yarrow. Sumas Way (Highway 11), Vye Road, Marion Road, Keith Wilson Road, and Campbell Road are the usual farm roads.

Homes for sale on Sumas Prairie are mainly farms, acreages, and country houses, including rural residential pockets near Kilgard and the Vye Road corridor. Almost all of this land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. Hougen Park, on McDermott Road, is a local riverside park. Sumas Mountain Regional Park sits to the north. Larger shopping is a drive west into Abbotsford or east toward Vedder and Chilliwack. Because this is protected low land, confirm current flood mapping, dike and pump-station context, and insurance with the City for the specific parcel, in addition to ALR and zoning rules.

Highway 1 is the main regional route; local bus service on the prairie roads is limited. Nearby public schools include Semá:th Elementary (formerly Upper Sumas Elementary), at 36321 Vye Road, and Barrowtown Elementary. Middle and secondary catchments can point to schools in Abbotsford rather than to one prairie secondary. Check the Abbotsford School District (SD34) school locator for the exact address.
