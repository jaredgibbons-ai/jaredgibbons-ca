# Area overviews for the website --- batch 11 (Agassiz and Harrison)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, flood mapping, and ALR status) should still be confirmed with the District of Kent, the Village of Harrison Hot Springs, the Fraser Valley Regional District, or the Fraser-Cascade School District (SD78) --- and, for Lake Errock, the Mission School District (SD75) --- for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. Harrison Hot Springs and Harrison Lake share one guide page; the two overviews distinguish them as a village and a board name, without inventing legal boundaries.

## Agassiz

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/agassiz-bc-guide.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Agassiz, District of Kent

**Suggested meta title:** Agassiz Homes for Sale \| District of Kent BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Agassiz, in the District of Kent, near Pioneer Avenue, Kent Elementary, Agassiz Elementary-Secondary and Highways 7 and 9.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Agassiz; Agassiz BC real estate; living in Agassiz, District of Kent; Pioneer Avenue Agassiz; District of Kent homes

Agassiz, in the District of Kent, is the municipality's main townsite on the north side of the Fraser River. Municipal Hall is at 7170 Cheam Avenue, and Pioneer Avenue is the main street for shops and services. Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway) and Highway 9 meet here; Highway 9 continues north to Harrison Hot Springs and south toward Highway 1 at Bridal Falls.

Homes for sale in Agassiz include houses and townhouses in the compact townsite, plus farm and acreage properties on the surrounding Agricultural Land Reserve land. Pioneer Park and Centennial Park are District of Kent parks near the town streets. Everyday groceries and services are in the townsite, with Harrison Hot Springs a short drive up Highway 9 and Chilliwack across the river via the Highway 9 / Highway 1 connection.

Transit is limited; most trips are by car. Nearby public schools include Kent Elementary on McCullough Road and Agassiz Elementary-Secondary on Cheam Avenue, in the Fraser-Cascade School District (SD78). Confirm the school for a specific address with the district. Parts of the townsite are on the Fraser floodplain; confirm floodplain designation, dike status, and ALR rules with the District of Kent before you plan a new dwelling or a change of use.

## Harrison Hot Springs

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/harrison-hot-springs-bc-guide.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Harrison Hot Springs, Village of Harrison Hot Springs

**Suggested meta title:** Harrison Hot Springs Homes for Sale \| BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Harrison Hot Springs, at the south end of Harrison Lake, near Beachfront Park, the lagoon, Esplanade Avenue and Highway 9.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Harrison Hot Springs; Harrison Hot Springs real estate; living in Harrison Hot Springs; Harrison Lagoon; Esplanade Avenue Harrison

The Village of Harrison Hot Springs is its own municipality at the foot of Harrison Lake, reached from Agassiz on Hot Springs Road (Highway 9). It is not part of the District of Kent. Esplanade Avenue runs along the lakeshore, where many of the village's apartment buildings and hotels sit.

Homes for sale in Harrison Hot Springs include apartments and townhouses near the waterfront, and houses on the residential streets back from the lake and the Miami River. Beachfront Park and the Harrison Lagoon are the Village's main lakeshore recreation spaces, with a walking path along the water. Rendall Park has a sandy beach and a designated swimming area. The Village also maintains Spring Park, Woods Park, Qwólts Park, and Peace Park. The FVRD's East Sector Lands Recreation Area, on McCombs Drive, adds trails just outside the village core.

Most trips are by car, with Highway 9 the link to Agassiz services and to Highway 7 and Highway 1. Nearby public schools include Harrison Hot Springs Elementary, with older students often attending Agassiz Elementary-Secondary in the District of Kent. Confirm placements with the Fraser-Cascade School District (SD78). Lakeshore and river-side lots should be checked for floodplain and dike designations with the Village.

## Harrison Lake

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/harrison-hot-springs-bc-guide.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Harrison Lake, near Harrison Hot Springs

**Suggested meta title:** Harrison Lake Homes for Sale \| near Harrison

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and cabins for sale around Harrison Lake, near Harrison Hot Springs, including lakeshore lots toward Sasquatch Provincial Park.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale on Harrison Lake; Harrison Lake real estate; living on Harrison Lake, near Harrison Hot Springs; Sasquatch Provincial Park; Harrison Lake cabins

Harrison Lake is a real estate board name for homes and recreational properties around Harrison Lake, beyond the streets of the Village of Harrison Hot Springs. Some lots sit inside the Village, some may be in the District of Kent, and some are in the Fraser Valley Regional District. Confirm which government applies to the parcel before you compare zoning, taxes, or services.

Homes for sale under the Harrison Lake name are mainly lakefront houses, cabins, and acreages rather than village apartment buildings. Sasquatch Provincial Park, a BC Parks site on the east side of the lake north of the village, is a nearby recreation landmark. Boat access, forest service roads, and the lakeshore itself are what define this market, more than a town grid.

Everyday shops, the public beach, and Harrison Hot Springs Elementary are in the Village, with a drive along the lake or back down to Highway 9. Schooling is generally with the Fraser-Cascade School District (SD78); confirm the school with the district, as lake-side addresses can sit in different communities. Access, waterfront tenure, flood or erosion risk, and year-round road status should be confirmed with the appropriate local government before you write an offer.

## Lake Errock

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/lake-errock.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Lake Errock, near Mission and Agassiz

**Suggested meta title:** Lake Errock Homes for Sale \| FVRD Area C

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Lake Errock, an FVRD Electoral Area C lakeside community near Harrison Mills, between Mission and Agassiz on Highway 7.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Lake Errock; Lake Errock real estate; living in Lake Errock, near Mission; Harrison Mills nearby; Lake Errock waterfront

Lake Errock is an unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area C community on a lake of the same name, along Highway 7 between Mission and Agassiz, near Harrison Mills. It is unincorporated land in Fraser Valley Regional District Electoral Area C, not part of Mission, Kent, or Harrison Hot Springs. Highway 7 (Lougheed Highway) is the main road in.

Homes for sale in Lake Errock include waterfront houses, cabins, and rural lots around the lake, with some strata or freehold properties near the local golf course. There is no urban shopping street; everyday services are a drive west into Mission or east toward Agassiz and Harrison Mills. The lake itself is the recreation focus, with a quieter setting than the resort village at Harrison Hot Springs.

Transit is limited; most trips are by car on Highway 7. Lake Errock is in the Mission School District (SD75). Nearby public schools include Deroche Elementary, with middle and secondary students often attending schools in Mission. Check the SD75 school locator. Confirm well and septic servicing, lakeshore rules, floodplain designation, and zoning with the FVRD for a specific parcel.

## Notes on sources and items left out

Nothing in the body text is marked UNCONFIRMED. Facts were taken from official pages of the District of Kent, the Village of Harrison Hot Springs, the Fraser Valley Regional District, the Fraser-Cascade School District (SD78), the Mission School District (SD75), and BC Parks, and were checked against the existing jaredgibbons.ca pages so the new copy complements, and does not repeat, the guide intros already on those pages. The following were left out because they were not clearly confirmed on official sources, or because they could mislead:

-   Population, land-area, park-size, flood-crest elevations, and housing-count figures (even where a village bylaw or parks plan publishes them).
-   Exact legal boundaries for the board name Harrison Lake, and for which lake-shore lots sit in the Village, the District of Kent, or the FVRD.
-   Hotel, resort, and store tenant lists, and private golf-course or strata rules.
-   Parcel-specific flood-construction elevations and dike-crest measurements.
-   The planning sheet marked all four pages as not yet having a "Jared's take." No formal take or verdict block was found. The Agassiz, Harrison Hot Springs, and Lake Errock pages already have long guide intros (Agassiz as Kent's service centre, the Village on the lakeshore, and Lake Errock as an Area C community near Harrison Mills). The overviews complement those intros. Harrison Lake shares the Harrison Hot Springs guide page.
