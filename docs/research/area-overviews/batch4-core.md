## Pacific Douglas (South Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/pacific-douglas.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Pacific Douglas, South Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Pacific Douglas Homes for Sale \| South Surrey

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Pacific Douglas, South Surrey, the City's Douglas neighbourhood, near the Peace Arch border crossings and Highway 99.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Pacific Douglas; Pacific Douglas South Surrey real estate; living in Pacific Douglas, South Surrey; Douglas South Surrey homes; Peace Arch Surrey homes

Pacific Douglas is the board's name for the neighbourhood the City of Surrey calls Douglas, in South Surrey. The City describes it as Surrey's most southern urban neighbourhood, between the Peace Arch and Pacific Highway border crossings, next to the United States. It sits east of Highway 99, west of Highway 15 (176 Street), and north of the border. Agricultural Land Reserve land and The Glades Woodland Garden lie along the north edge.

Homes for sale in Pacific Douglas are mostly newer houses and townhouses in a planned residential area. The City notes two small commercial nodes, a neighbourhood park, and an elementary school in the community. Peace Arch Provincial Park is just to the west, across Highway 99. Daily shopping is a short drive north toward 8 Avenue and the shops of South Surrey, including the 24 Avenue corridor.

This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood. Highways 99 and 15 are the usual ways in and out, and both lead directly to a border crossing. Land use here is now guided by Surrey's Official Community Plan; the older Douglas Neighbourhood Concept Plan still informs amenities and infrastructure, but its land-use designations have been superseded. Nearby public schools include Douglas Elementary and Earl Marriott Secondary. Catchments are set by address, so check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator for the home you have in mind.

Pacific Douglas real estate suits buyers who want a newer South Surrey neighbourhood close to the border highways, with a quieter residential street pattern than the town-centre areas farther north.

## Serpentine (Cloverdale)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/serpentine.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Serpentine, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Serpentine Homes & Acreages for Sale \| Surrey

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Serpentine, Surrey, a Cloverdale board area near the Serpentine River, Highway 10 and the Agricultural Land Reserve.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Serpentine Surrey; Serpentine Surrey real estate; living in Serpentine, Surrey; Serpentine Cloverdale acreages; Surrey ALR acreages

Serpentine is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name within Cloverdale, not a City of Surrey official neighbourhood. The City has no community profile or mapped boundary under this name, so treat any advertised edge as a board line, not a municipal one. In general, the name is used for rural land on Surrey's southern lowlands along the Serpentine River, in the agricultural area between Cloverdale and South Surrey.

Homes for sale here are mainly acreages, farms, and houses on larger lots, rather than townhouses or condos. Much of the Serpentine and Nicomekl lowland is in the Agricultural Land Reserve and is floodplain, protected in part by dykes and pump stations. Some properties are in the ALR and some are not. Check zoning, ALR status, and flood mapping with the City --- and with the Agricultural Land Commission if the land is in the ALR --- before you write an offer.

King George Boulevard, Highway 99, and the shops and services of Cloverdale and South Surrey are a drive away, not a walk. This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood, and bus service is limited on rural roads. School catchments vary across this large area, so do not assume a home is in a particular school. Confirm the exact address with the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator.

Serpentine real estate suits buyers who want space and a rural setting inside Surrey's boundaries, and who are prepared to check servicing, zoning, and flood mapping on a specific property.

## County Line / Glen Valley (Langley)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/county-line-glen-valley.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in County Line and Glen Valley, Langley

**Suggested meta title:** Glen Valley & County Line Homes \| Langley

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in County Line and Glen Valley, Langley, on the Fraser River, near Glen Valley Regional Park and the Abbotsford border.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Glen Valley Langley; Glen Valley Langley real estate; living in Glen Valley, Langley; County Line Langley homes; Glen Valley Regional Park

County Line / Glen Valley is a board sub-area name for rural land in the northeast Township of Langley, toward the Abbotsford border, on the south side of the Fraser River. Glen Valley is also a geographic name for this river-side farming area. It is not one of the Township's urban community names, such as Fort Langley or Walnut Grove.

Homes for sale here are mainly farms, acreages, and houses on larger lots. Much of the land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. The flats along the river are low, and flood mapping and dike status matter on many parcels. Check zoning, ALR status, and flood mapping with the Township --- and with the Agricultural Land Commission if the land is in the ALR --- before you write an offer.

Glen Valley Regional Park, a Metro Vancouver park, sits on the Fraser and is reached from 272 Street at River Road. It is used for river views, fishing, and paddling, under park rules. Fort Langley is a short drive west for shops, the library, and services. This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood, and there is little or no all-day bus service on the river roads, so most trips are by car. Nearby public schools to check include Fort Langley Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, though both sit outside the valley itself. Confirm the catchment for the exact address with the SD35 school locator.

Living in County Line or Glen Valley suits buyers who want farm and acreage country on the Fraser, and who are comfortable checking flood, dike, and ALR rules on the specific property.

## Otter District (Langley)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/otter-district.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Otter District, Langley

**Suggested meta title:** Otter District Homes & Acreages \| Langley

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Otter District, Langley, a rural southeast Township area on the Aldergrove side, near Otter Co-op and Highway 13.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Otter District; Otter District Langley real estate; living in Otter District, Langley; Otter Co-op Langley; Langley rural acreages

Otter District is a board sub-area name for rural land in the southeast Township of Langley, on the Aldergrove side of the Township. It is not an official Township community name in the same way as Aldergrove or Brookswood-Fernridge. A useful landmark is the Otter Co-op at 3650 248 Street. Fraser Highway and 248 Street are the main local roads.

Homes for sale here are mainly farms, acreages, and rural houses. Many parcels are in the Agricultural Land Reserve. Check zoning, ALR status, and any stream or servicing limits with the Township --- and with the Agricultural Land Commission if the land is in the ALR --- before you write an offer. Shopping, the library, and the Aldergrove Community Centre are a drive north into Aldergrove.

This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood. Buses run along parts of Fraser Highway, but most daily trips are by car. Nearby public schools include North Otter Elementary. D.W. Poppy Secondary is the usual family secondary for much of this side of the Township, though some addresses may go to a different school. The old Otter Elementary closed in 2004, so do not rely on historic school names. Confirm the catchment for the exact address with the SD35 school locator.

Otter District real estate suits buyers who want a rural Township setting near Aldergrove, with room for a farm or acreage lifestyle, and who will check ALR and zoning rules carefully.

## Whalley (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/whalley.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Whalley, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Whalley Homes for Sale \| Surrey City Centre

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Whalley, Surrey, including Surrey City Centre condos and townhomes near Expo Line SkyTrain stations.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Whalley; Whalley Surrey real estate; living in Whalley, Surrey; Surrey City Centre homes; Whalley SkyTrain condos

Whalley is the historic heart of North Surrey and includes Surrey City Centre, the city's downtown core. The City of Surrey traces the name to Arthur Whalley, who opened a gas station and store at what became known as Whalley's Corner in 1925. Today the area takes in the towers and shops around the SkyTrain stations, and the older streets of houses and townhouses around them.

Homes for sale in Whalley include new and older condos, townhouses, and detached houses. City Centre is the walkable core, with Central City, SFU's Surrey campus, Holland Park, and the Chuck Bailey Recreation Centre close to the stations. Green Timbers Urban Forest sits on the east side of the community. Growth around the stations is guided by the City Centre Plan, so it is worth checking what the plan allows on a given street if you are buying or renovating.

Whalley is one of the few Fraser Valley communities with SkyTrain in the neighbourhood. Gateway, Surrey Central, and King George stations are all on the Expo Line here, and the Surrey Langley SkyTrain project will extend the line east from King George. Nearby public schools include Old Yale Road Elementary, Forsyth Road Elementary, K.B. Woodward Elementary, and Kwantlen Park Secondary. Catchments split by address, so check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator for the home you have in mind.

Whalley real estate is a fit if you want to be close to SkyTrain, shops, and City Centre services. A condo by the station and a house a few blocks out are two different kinds of purchase, and it pays to look at the specific street, not only the Whalley name.

## Cedar Hills (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/cedar-hills.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Cedar Hills, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Cedar Hills Homes for Sale \| Surrey BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Cedar Hills, Surrey, an established North Surrey neighbourhood between Scott Road and City Centre, near Robson Park.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Cedar Hills; Cedar Hills Surrey real estate; living in Cedar Hills, Surrey; Robson Park Surrey; Cedar Hills Elementary

Cedar Hills is an established residential pocket in North Surrey, between Scott Road and the Whalley / City Centre area. It is a neighbourhood of mostly houses on settled streets, rather than the towers around the SkyTrain stations to the east. Some newer townhouses and duplexes are mixed in.

Homes for sale in Cedar Hills are mainly family houses, and many have a secondary suite, or room for one. Always confirm that any suite was built with a permit. Robson Park, near 100 Avenue, is the main local park, with open space, sports fields, tennis and pickleball, and a playground. Moffat Memorial Park is a short way southwest. Local shops sit at the neighbourhood's main corner, and Scott Road and King George Boulevard are close for a larger trip.

This is not, for most streets, a walk-to-SkyTrain neighbourhood. Buses connect to Surrey Central and Scott Road stations. Nearby public schools include Cedar Hills Elementary, Prince Charles Elementary, and L.A. Matheson Secondary. Catchments are set by address, so check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator.

Cedar Hills real estate suits buyers who want a house in an established North Surrey neighbourhood, with a local park and a shorter hop to City Centre than you get in Fraser Heights, without living in the station-area towers.

## Bolivar Heights (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/bolivar-heights.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Bolivar Heights, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Bolivar Heights Homes for Sale \| Surrey BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Bolivar Heights, Surrey, a North Surrey hillside neighbourhood near Invergarry Park, City Centre and the Fraser River.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Bolivar Heights; Bolivar Heights Surrey real estate; living in Bolivar Heights, Surrey; Invergarry Park Surrey; Bolivar Heights views

Bolivar Heights is a hillside neighbourhood in North Surrey, just north of Surrey City Centre, rising toward Invergarry Park and the Fraser. It sits between the City Centre streets to the south and the freeway and river side of North Surrey. Highway 1 and the Port Mann are close.

Homes for sale here are mainly older family houses on regular lots, with some apartments closer to the City Centre edge. Invergarry Park is the large forested park on the slope down toward the Fraser, with walking trails. Bolivar Park, on Crestview Drive, is the closer everyday park for many streets. The City's City Centre Plan includes this area, so check what the current plan allows on the lot you are looking at, and on the lots beside it, before you buy or renovate.

SkyTrain is a short bus ride, and it is walkable from the south edge of the neighbourhood, but not from every street farther up the hill. Nearby public schools include James Ardiel Elementary and Surrey Traditional. Secondary catchments can split between Kwantlen Park Secondary and L.A. Matheson Secondary. Check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator for the exact address.

Bolivar Heights real estate is a fit if you want a house and a yard close to City Centre, and you are comfortable looking carefully at an older home and at what the City's plan may allow nearby.

## Bridgeview (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridgeview.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Bridgeview, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Bridgeview Homes for Sale \| Surrey BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Bridgeview, Surrey, a low-lying Fraser River neighbourhood near Scott Road Station, the Pattullo Bridge and Bridgeview Park.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Bridgeview; Bridgeview Surrey real estate; living in Bridgeview, Surrey; Scott Road Station; Bridgeview Park

Bridgeview is a small, low-lying neighbourhood in North Surrey, along the Fraser River, just east of the Pattullo Bridge and Scott Road. It is an older pocket of houses, mixed with industrial land, next to the river. The City includes it in planning for South Westminster.

Homes for sale in Bridgeview are mainly detached houses, with only a small number of townhouses. Local amenities include Bridgeview Park and the Bridgeview Community Centre on 126A Street. The North Surrey Sport & Ice Complex is just to the south. Brownsville Bar Park and the public wharf offer river access nearby. Because much of Bridgeview is low ground beside the Fraser, check flood mapping, building rules, and insurance with the City before you buy or plan a rebuild.

Scott Road SkyTrain Station sits at the west edge of the neighbourhood, so this is one of the few house-oriented parts of Surrey with SkyTrain close by. The Pattullo Bridge is the usual hop to New Westminster. Nearby public schools include Bridgeview Elementary and Kwantlen Park Secondary. Confirm the catchment for the exact address with the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator.

Bridgeview real estate suits buyers who want a house close to SkyTrain and the river, and who are prepared to look carefully at floodplain rules, zoning, and neighbouring industrial land.

## Port Kells (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/port-kells-surrey.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Port Kells, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Port Kells Homes for Sale \| Surrey BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes and acreages for sale in Port Kells, Surrey, a rural northeast corner near Highway 1, Port Kells Park and the Township of Langley border.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Port Kells; Port Kells Surrey real estate; living in Port Kells, Surrey; Port Kells Park; Port Kells acreages

Port Kells is a small, more rural corner of northeast Surrey, near Highway 1 and the Township of Langley border. The City of Surrey includes it in the broader Guildford community. It has a very different feel from City Centre: larger lots, some acreages, and industrial land close by. Check zoning on the specific property, because residential and industrial uses sit near each other here.

Homes for sale in Port Kells are mainly houses and acreages, not condos. Port Kells Park, at 19340 88 Avenue, has an outdoor pool, softball diamonds, and a playground. The Port Kells Library branch is in the community as well. Tynehead Regional Park is a short drive west. Guildford Town Centre is the usual place for a larger shopping trip.

This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood. Highway 1 is the main regional route, and TransLink's 388 bus runs along 88 Avenue toward Carvolth Exchange. Nearby public schools include Port Kells Elementary. Secondary catchments in this part of Surrey can include École Salish Secondary. Confirm the catchment for the exact address with the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator.

Port Kells real estate is a fit if you want more land and a quieter, semi-rural setting in northeast Surrey, with the highway close by, and you are comfortable living near industrial and rural uses.

## Queen Mary Park (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/queen-mary-park-surrey.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Queen Mary Park, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Queen Mary Park Homes for Sale \| Surrey BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Queen Mary Park, Surrey, a central neighbourhood around Queen Mary Park and Healy Creek, between City Centre and Fleetwood.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Queen Mary Park Surrey; Queen Mary Park Surrey real estate; living in Queen Mary Park, Surrey; Healy Creek Surrey; Queen Mary Park

Queen Mary Park is a residential neighbourhood in central Surrey, named for the park at 8972 Queen Mary Boulevard. The City places the park in its Whalley and City Centre community, north of 88 Avenue and west of 132 Street. The edges of the board area are approximate, so ask if a specific address is included.

The park itself is mostly natural area, with trails and a playground, set aside in large part to protect Healy Creek, a tributary of Bear Creek and the Serpentine River. Inouye Park, across Queen Mary Boulevard, is another nearby green space. Homes for sale around the park include houses, townhouses, and apartments. 88 Avenue and King George Boulevard are the usual roads for shops and services.

This is not, for most streets, a walk-to-SkyTrain neighbourhood. The Expo Line stations are to the north in City Centre; buses serve 88 Avenue and King George Boulevard. Catchments in this part of Surrey split by address, so check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator for the home you have in mind.

Queen Mary Park real estate suits buyers who want an established central Surrey neighbourhood with a large park and creek trails close by, and a short drive to both City Centre and Newton.

## Bear Creek / Green Timbers (Surrey)

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/bear-creek-green-timbers.html

**Suggested H2:** Living in Bear Creek and Green Timbers, Surrey

**Suggested meta title:** Bear Creek & Green Timbers Homes \| Surrey

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Bear Creek and Green Timbers, Surrey, beside Bear Creek Park and Green Timbers Urban Forest, along Fraser Highway and 88 Avenue.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Bear Creek Surrey; Green Timbers Surrey real estate; living in Green Timbers, Surrey; Bear Creek Park; Green Timbers Urban Forest

Bear Creek / Green Timbers is a board sub-area name that groups the residential streets around two of Surrey's largest parks: Bear Creek Park and Green Timbers Urban Forest. The City places both parks in its Whalley and City Centre / North Surrey area. The neighbourhood is in central Surrey, between City Centre and Fleetwood, along Fraser Highway, 88 Avenue, and 104 Avenue. The edges are approximate.

Bear Creek Park, at 13750 88 Avenue, is next to the Surrey Arts Centre and has an outdoor pool, a spray park, gardens, athletic fields, a walking track, a dog off-leash area, a skatepark, and nature trails. Green Timbers Urban Forest Park, at the 14600 block of 100 Avenue, is a protected second-growth forest with walking trails, a fishing lake, picnic areas, and the Surrey Nature Centre. Surrey residents voted to designate part of the park as an urban forest. Homes for sale nearby include houses, some on larger, treed lots, along with townhouses and a smaller number of apartments.

This is not a SkyTrain neighbourhood today. The Surrey Langley SkyTrain project will add Green Timbers Station at 140 Street and Fraser Highway, on the west edge of the urban forest. Nearby public schools include Bear Creek Elementary, Green Timbers Elementary, and Enver Creek Secondary. Catchments in this area split by address and do not all feed the same secondary school, so check the Surrey Schools (SD36) locator.

Bear Creek and Green Timbers real estate is a good fit if you want an established central Surrey neighbourhood with major parks next door, and you are looking ahead to SkyTrain on Fraser Highway.

-   Named catchment schools for Serpentine as a whole. The board area is large and rural, and catchments vary; buyers are directed to the SD36 locator only.
-   A SkyTrain opening year. Stations are described as part of the approved Surrey Langley SkyTrain project, without a service date.
-   A short "Jared's take" paragraph was not found on the fetched Port Kells or Queen Mary Park listing pages, though the planning sheet marked those pages as already having one. The overviews still follow the existing page copy and official sources.
-   Store tenants, specific bus route numbers except the 388 in Port Kells, and any claim that Serpentine or Otter District is an official City or Township neighbourhood name.
