# Area overviews for the website --- batch 19 (New Westminster)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, heritage, and floodplain rules) should still be confirmed with the City of New Westminster or School District 40 (New Westminster) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. The City of New Westminster is organized around recognized neighbourhoods including Downtown, Uptown, Queen's Park, Sapperton, Queensborough, Moody Park, the West End, Connaught Heights, and Glenbrooke North. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas or waterfront precincts. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries. The heading "GlenBrooke North" follows the planning-sheet spelling.

## Notes on sources and items left out

Nothing in the body text is marked UNCONFIRMED. Facts were taken from official pages of the City of New Westminster, TransLink, and School District 40. The following were left out because they were not clearly confirmed on official sources, or because they could mislead:

-   Population, land-area, and housing-count figures, and all sale statistics.
-   Exact legal boundaries for board names including Fraserview and The Heights. The Heights often overlaps the City's Brow of the Hill neighbourhood; Fraserview describes south-facing river-slope streets and should not be confused with Vancouver's Fraserview.
-   Store tenants, hospital-campus development timelines, and parcel-specific heritage or floodplain designations.
-   The planning sheet marked all 12 New Westminster pages as not yet having a "Jared's take." The overviews follow official sources and do not copy marketing intros. "West End" here means New Westminster's west-side neighbourhood, not Vancouver's West End.
