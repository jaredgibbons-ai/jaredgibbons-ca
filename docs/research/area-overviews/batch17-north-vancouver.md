# Area overviews for the website --- batch 17 (North Vancouver)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, ravine, and slope rules) should still be confirmed with the City of North Vancouver, the District of North Vancouver, or School District 44 (North Vancouver) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. North Vancouver is two municipalities: the City of North Vancouver, centred on Lonsdale and the waterfront, and the surrounding District of North Vancouver. The first sentence of each overview names the municipality. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries. Upper Lonsdale can straddle the City--District line, so the municipality should be confirmed for a specific address.

-   Store tenants, private trail-access rules, and parcel-specific ravine or slope designations.
