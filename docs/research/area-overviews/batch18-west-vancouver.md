# Area overviews for the website --- batch 18 (West Vancouver)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, slope, and title conditions) should still be confirmed with the District of West Vancouver or School District 45 (West Vancouver) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement any short intro already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. The District of West Vancouver runs along Burrard Inlet from the Lions Gate Bridge and Park Royal west toward Horseshoe Bay, and up the mountain to Cypress Provincial Park. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas or subdivision pockets. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries. The spelling "Caulfeild" follows the official local form.
