# Area overviews for the website --- batch 8 (Maple Ridge)

Easy-to-read neighbourhood copy for Jared's area pages. No sales statistics. Official details (catchments, zoning, flood mapping, steep-slope rules, and ALR status) should still be confirmed with the City of Maple Ridge or the Maple Ridge--Pitt Meadows School District (SD42) for a specific address.

Each section is written to complement the short "Jared's take" already on the page, not to replace it. The page URL is listed under the heading. Where a formal take was not found on the fetched page, the overview still follows the existing page copy and official sources.

The City of Maple Ridge describes distinct neighbourhoods and historic communities, including Albion/Kanaka, Port Hammond, Port Haney, Ruskin, Silver Valley, Thornhill, Town Centre, Webster's Corners, Whonnock, and Yennadon. Several names below are real estate board (MLS) sub-areas that group more than one of those places. Those are described in plain language, without invented boundaries.

## Albion

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Albion; Albion Maple Ridge real estate; living in Albion, Maple Ridge; Albion Sports Complex; Kanaka Creek Regional Park

Albion, in the City of Maple Ridge, is the neighbourhood the City lists as Albion/Kanaka. It sits on the east side of the urban area along Lougheed Highway (Highway 7), with 240 Street as a main local route. The City has an adopted Albion Area Plan for this part of town. South of the built-up streets, some land remains in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Fraser River forms the south edge of the city here.

Homes for sale in Albion include houses and townhouses on the planned streets around the commercial corner at 104 Avenue and 240 Street, as well as houses closer to Kanaka Creek. The Albion Fairgrounds are used through the year for community events. The Albion Sports Complex is home to the ice rink and the Golden Ears Winter Club curling facility. Kanaka Creek Regional Park, a Metro Vancouver park, follows the creek toward the Fraser.

Transit is by car and bus. TransLink's 746 Albion route runs to Haney Place Exchange in Town Centre, and Lougheed Highway is the main road west toward the Golden Ears Bridge and east toward Mission. Nearby public schools include Albion Elementary, c̓əsqənelə Elementary, and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary. Kanaka Creek Elementary sits toward the west edge. Catchments still vary, so check the Maple Ridge--Pitt Meadows School District (SD42) school locator. Confirm ALR status, floodplain designation, and servicing with the City for a specific parcel.

## Cottonwood

**Page:** https://jaredgibbons.ca/cottonwood-mr.html

**Suggested meta title:** Cottonwood Homes for Sale \| Maple Ridge BC

**Suggested meta description:** Homes for sale in Cottonwood, Maple Ridge, near Kanaka Creek Regional Park, Cliff Falls, Kanaka Creek Elementary and the bus to Haney Place.

**Keyword phrases:** homes for sale in Cottonwood; Cottonwood Maple Ridge real estate; living in Cottonwood, Maple Ridge; Kanaka Creek Regional Park; Cliff Falls Maple Ridge

Cottonwood is a real estate board name for a residential part of the City of Maple Ridge, just west of Albion. The City does not list Cottonwood as a named neighbourhood on its Our Neighbourhoods page, and there is no Cottonwood area plan. Official anchors include Kanaka Creek Elementary on 234A Street, Alexander Robinson Elementary on 238B Street, and TransLink's 745 Cottonwood route to Haney Place Exchange.

Homes for sale in Cottonwood are mainly houses and townhouses on the streets between Town Centre and the Albion Area Plan. Kanaka Creek Regional Park runs through this side of the city, with trails to Cliff Falls and the Bell-Irving Hatchery. Thomas Haney Secondary is close by on 116 Avenue.

Most daily trips are by car or the 745 bus. Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road are the east--west routes, and Haney Place Exchange connects to the R3 Lougheed RapidBus and to the West Coast Express at Port Haney. Nearby public schools include Kanaka Creek Elementary and Alexander Robinson Elementary. Check the SD42 school locator for the address you are considering. Riparian setbacks along Kanaka Creek, and any floodplain or slope designations, should be confirmed with the City and Metro Vancouver.

East Central is a real estate board name for streets on the east side of Maple Ridge Town Centre, in the City of Maple Ridge. It is not a neighbourhood name on the City's official list. North Maple Ridge is a real estate board name for the north side of the City of Maple Ridge. The City does not publish a boundary or a neighbourhood called North Maple Ridge. Silver Valley, in the City of Maple Ridge, is one of the neighbourhoods the City names, and it has its own area plan. The planned community lies on the north side of the Alouette River, with Dewdney Trunk Road among the approaches from Town Centre and Yennadon. Homes for sale in Silver Valley are mainly houses and townhouses on the hillside streets of the area plan, with some mixed-use planning around River Village. Trail connections are part of the official plan, and Maple Ridge Park in nearby Yennadon is a short drive for picnics and a playground. Golden Ears Provincial Park is reached from this side of the city.

TransLink's 733 and 741 routes serve the northern streets toward Haney Place Exchange; many trips are still by car. Nearby public schools include Yennadon Elementary and Alouette Elementary, with Garibaldi Secondary also on the east-central side of the city. Check the SD42 school locator. Hillside lots may have steep-slope or watercourse rules; confirm those with the City.

Thornhill, in the City of Maple Ridge, is one of the neighbourhoods the City names, on the east side toward 256 Street and Dewdney Trunk Road. The Official Community Plan treats Thornhill as a long-term urban reserve: urban development is not supported until an area plan and related regional approvals are in place. Until then, the land remains rural in character.

Homes for sale in Thornhill are mainly houses, acreages, farms, and nurseries rather than apartment buildings. The City describes a mountain-biking and hiking trail network on 256 Street. Thornhill Park serves the local streets. Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road are the main roads west toward Town Centre and east toward Websters Corners, Whonnock, and Mission.

TransLink's 748 Thornhill route runs toward Haney Place Exchange. Nearby public schools include Blue Mountain Elementary and Webster's Corners Elementary. Check the SD42 school locator. Many lots are on well and septic; confirm servicing, ALR status, and urban-reserve rules with the City before you plan a new dwelling or a change of use.

## Notes on sources and items left out

Nothing in the body text is marked UNCONFIRMED. Facts were taken from official pages of the City of Maple Ridge (Our Neighbourhoods, the Official Community Plan, and the Albion, Silver Valley, Town Centre, Hammond, and Lougheed Transit Corridor area plans), the Maple Ridge--Pitt Meadows School District (SD42), TransLink, Metro Vancouver, and BC Parks, and were checked against the existing jaredgibbons.ca page for each area so the new copy complements, and does not repeat or contradict, the short "Jared's take" already on the page. The following were left out because they were not clearly confirmed on official sources, or because they could mislead:

-   Population, land-area, park-size, and housing-count figures (even where a city or park page publishes them).
-   Exact legal boundaries for the board names Cottonwood, East Central, North Maple Ridge, Northeast, Northwest Maple Ridge, Southwest Maple Ridge, and West Central. The copy describes the general area and the official City neighbourhood names that sit inside them.
-   Store tenants and private attraction lists, other than City-recognized civic and recreation sites (the ACT, the Leisure Centre, Albion Sports Complex, Meadowtown Centre as a transit and shopping node, and Maple Ridge Golf Course as named in the Hammond Area Plan).
-   Detailed flood-construction elevations, escarpment setbacks, and ALR floor-area caps, which are parcel-specific.
-   A short "Jared's take" or verdict was found on Albion, the North Maple Ridge guide, Silver Valley, Southwest Maple Ridge, Websters Corners, and Whonnock, matching the planning sheet. No formal take was found on the fetched Northwest Maple Ridge page, though the sheet marked it as already having one. Cottonwood, East Central, Northeast, Thornhill, and West Central were marked as not yet having a take; Thornhill and West Central do have a short intro line on the listing page, but not a formal take block.
