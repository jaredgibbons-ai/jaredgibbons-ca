## Kept for later pages
- **Port Coquitlam:** OCP Bylaw 3838 plans Dominion Riverfront as townhouses and low-rise apartments; names Lincoln Park and Mary Hill (developed from the 1950s) and Riverwood and Citadel Heights (1980s and 90s) as established neighbourhoods. No per-neighbourhood housing type is stated; Glenwood, Oxford Heights and Birchland Manor are not named in the OCP.
- **Squamish:** SSMUH is District of Squamish Zoning Bylaw No. 2200, 2011, Amendment Bylaw (Small-Scale Multi-unit Housing) No. 3036, 2024, adopted June 2024 (day not verified); it created the R-1 zone and rezoned RS-1, RS-2, RS-3, RS-1A, RS-2A and RMH-2 parcels. The District's Missing Middle page misprints the year as 2025.
