## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Altamont is a residential hillside area of the District of West Vancouver, above Marine Drive between Dundarave to the east and Caulfeild to the west; "Altamont" is the name used on jaredgibbons.ca, and an official District neighbourhood or planning-area name "Altamont" is NOT CONFIRMED — the name does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, whose commercial structure is the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, the Park Royal regional shopping centre, and the village centres of Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. Street-by-street boundaries are UNVERIFIED; the area is generally the hillside above the Marine Drive / Queens Avenue corridor below Dundarave and the British Properties edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- Where condo stock would concentrate in West Vancouver — the Ambleside waterfront and town centre and the Dundarave village edge — is outside Altamont. Inside Altamont the housing is detached houses in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, and the research file confirms no named condo buildings or townhome complexes inside the area on an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- Main streets: Marine Drive is the east–west waterfront route below the area, Queens Avenue climbs the hillside, and Taylor Way connects Marine Drive to Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway); the Lions Gate Bridge is the fixed crossing to Vancouver. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- Transit anchors: TransLink route 251 (Queens / Park Royal), operated as West Vancouver Blue Bus, serves the Queens Avenue corridor below the hillside, and route 250 runs on Marine Drive between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. There is no rapid transit and no condo node inside Altamont. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- Market numbers: West Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Altamont against Ambleside or Dundarave strata stock will meet them there; inside Altamont itself there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for a West Vancouver building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Altamont sits under the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010; houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones, whose Section 200 minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with the zone for a specific lot shown on the District's Zoning Map. No Altamont-specific local area plan is confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Altamont is confirmed, so this is relevant only to presales a buyer compares elsewhere in West Vancouver. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **West Vancouver tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Altamont assessed values the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos for sale in Altamont itself?** No verified strata apartment condos exist in Altamont on any source checked — the area is detached houses in the RS single-family zones, and the research file records strata condos and townhomes inside Altamont as none confirmed. A "condo" search result carrying an Altamont label should be checked for whether the property is actually in Ambleside or Dundarave. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real condo stock?** In the District's centres named in the OCP — the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre and the Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay village centres — not on the Altamont hillside. The best-known waterfront building below the area, Villa Maris, is in Dundarave. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Is Altamont even an official neighbourhood name?** Not on the sources read: the name is used on jaredgibbons.ca, but it does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's OCP, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and street-by-street boundaries are UNVERIFIED — which is one reason attached product gets filed under neighbouring names. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Altamont?** No. Secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted uses in the RS zones, but they are accessory to the detached lot — they are not separate strata lots and cannot be bought on their own title. The ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop, still as one lot's ownership unless a future rezoning and stratification occur. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: If I buy a condo in Ambleside or Dundarave instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a building directory still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What replaces the Form B diligence for an Altamont house purchase?** There is no strata, so there is no Form B, depreciation report or CRF to review. The equivalent checks are the lot's RS zone and minimum site area on the District's Zoning Map, and the hazard permit areas (wildfire interface, watercourse setback, foreshore) that can apply to hillside and creek lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: How is transit from Altamont, really?** By bus on the corridors below the hillside: route 251 on Queens Avenue and route 250 on Marine Drive to Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. Most Altamont streets are a hill walk or a drive from those stops — there is no transit node inside the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Altamont address?** School District 45 (West Vancouver Schools) catchments are address-based and SD45 directs families to the school locator on its admissions page; no Altamont catchment is confirmed in the research file, so run the exact address. SD45 has no middle tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is 8–12. Irwin Park Elementary (2455 Haywood Avenue) is in Dundarave south-east of the area and Westcot Elementary sits at the foot of the British Properties. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Are there extra taxes at Altamont price points?** Yes, potentially: the Province's additional school tax applies at 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026 (rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027), on top of District taxes due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% staged penalties. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Could condo or multiplex product be coming to Altamont's lower streets?** The rules are in transition: on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025, and Altamont sits directly above Dundarave. The District's final adopted amendments as they apply to Altamont's lower streets are UNVERIFIED — verify per property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The name itself is unofficial.** "Altamont" does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's OCP, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 — a buyer searching strata registries, the zoning map or the OCP by that name will find nothing, because the District plans this hillside as part of the wider Dundarave–Ambleside side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **The density rules changed under the area on July 21, 2025.** The Province directed West Vancouver to increase density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Dundarave by December 31, 2025 — Altamont's lower streets are exactly that adjacency, so infill potential quoted from older material may already be stale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Two hazard permits ride on hillside lots.** Work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface needs a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit, and work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank needs an Environmental Development Permit; a Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront or coastal-flood-risk property on the lowest streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **The seawalk below the hill dates to Canada's centennial.** The Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 and runs roughly 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park (150 25th Street, with more than 50 metres of sandy beach) to Ambleside — the waterfront amenity for an area that has no park confirmed inside its own lines. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **The RS zone range is extreme.** Minimum site areas in the zones that can apply run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1) — two Altamont lots on adjoining streets can carry completely different subdivision and suite potential, confirmed only on the District's Zoning Map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **A West Van name from the earlier program is a rental, not a condo.** Westerleigh PARC is a rental residence — it surfaces in West Vancouver attached-housing searches but there is nothing to buy. (Standing correction supplied with this assignment.)
