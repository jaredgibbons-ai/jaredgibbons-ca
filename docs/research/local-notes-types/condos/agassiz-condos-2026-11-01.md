## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that settles the UNCONFIRMED plan, unit-count and restriction items in Section 2 for any Agassiz complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Town Centre Gable's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (expanded May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Agassiz complex is verified 55+ in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Municipal law here is District of Kent law, not "Agassiz" law:** zoning is the District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended); the plan is the District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025, which designates the townsite core "Commercial – Town Centre" and directs growth into the townsite. Kent Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone, under which serviced residential lots in Agassiz can take up to four dwelling units without rezoning, subject to conditions; the District also permits secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones. https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001 and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/agassiz-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Floodplain rules:** Agassiz sits on the Fraser River floodplain behind dikes. Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5; the District has a Floodplain Bylaw that applications must comply with, plus area-specific flood construction levels — there is no single townsite-wide FCL in the sources checked, so the FCL is a per-property check. The local system includes Maria Slough and the Hammersley, Kilby and Bateson pump stations. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)
- **District of Kent taxes — District figures only:** taxes are District of Kent municipal taxes (there is no separate "Agassiz" tax bill). The District's 2026 schedule (per $1,000 of taxable value) sets the Class 1 Residential total municipal rate at 2.80493, plus a separate dyking line of 0.03076, for an all-in residential total of 4.65328 including hospital, regional district, MFA, BC Assessment and school tax. Notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day), with penalties after. The FVRD rural tax regime does NOT apply inside the District of Kent municipality — do not apply rural-area or any other municipality's penalty figures to a townsite property; penalty amounts beyond "penalties after the due date" are UNCONFIRMED from a District source in this file. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)
- **ALR:** the townsite is ringed by ALR farmland, and some townsite-edge "Residential – Future" lands are still in the ALR and stay in farm use until an ALC exclusion is approved (a Council-approved Neighbourhood Plan is required first). On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame applies on top of Kent zoning, and strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to any remaining developer sale at a newer complex such as MountView). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $500,000 (a round illustration only — no Agassiz median exists in any published source in this program), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no Agassiz presale project is verified by name in this program; where a unit is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Renting a unit out (landlord frame):** RTB rent increases are capped at 2.3% for 2026 and 2.2% for 2027 for ordinary tenancies. (Standing provincial figures for this program.)

- **Q: Can I add a suite or more units to a townsite house?** The District permits secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones, and the RS1 (SSMUH) zone allows up to four units on serviced lots that qualify — all subject to the Floodplain Bylaw and building rules for the specific lot. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf

- **Both seniors care campuses changed hands in 2018.** Park Place Seniors Living acquired Agassiz Seniors Community (previously Cheam Village) and Glenwood Seniors Community on September 6, 2018 — pre-2018 directories listing "Cheam Village" or "Glenwood Care Centre" describe these same two buildings under old names. https://www.parkplaceseniorsliving.com/park-place-acquires-4-new-locations-in-agassiz-bc-and-grande-prairie-ab/ (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/agassiz-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
