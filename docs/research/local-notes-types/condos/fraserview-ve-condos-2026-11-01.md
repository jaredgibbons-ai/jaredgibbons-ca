## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fraserview is the residential district on Vancouver's south slope to the Fraser River, inside the Victoria-Fraserview local area, between the Knight Street Bridge corridor and Killarney. Fraserview is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas — it is the southern portion of Victoria-Fraserview, above the Fraser River and around the Fraserview Golf Course lands. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the district in the sources read; exact board boundary roads: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: in small low-rise buildings on and just off the **Victoria Drive frontage** (Fraserview Place and the 6991 Victoria Drive building at the district's east edge, and the new V on E49 at Victoria Drive and East 49th Avenue at the north edge) and in one small low-rise on **Newport Avenue** overlooking Bobolink Park. The interior south-slope streets around the golf course are detached houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- Main roads: Victoria Drive and Fraser Street are the north–south spines at the district's edges, crossed by East 41st Avenue, East 49th Avenue and East 54th Avenue, with SE Marine Drive along the river slope below and Kerr Street toward the east edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- Transit: route 8 Fraser runs along the west side, route 49 runs on East 49th Avenue, and route 100 runs on Marine Drive; no SkyTrain station sits inside Fraserview VE. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this district's small low-rises (12 units at Fraserview Place) the depreciation report and CRF are disproportionately important — a single roof or envelope project is spread over very few lots. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold on its published or apparent size, subject to confirming the lot count for 2288 Newport Avenue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals allowed with restrictions" line published for Fraserview Place therefore cannot amount to a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Fraserview VE building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. The house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Victoria Drive condo frontage sits under commercial districts instead — the 6991 Victoria Drive strata plan is recorded under zone C-2 Commercial. Parcel-level zone for any other specific building: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md) and https://opengovca.com/vancouver-property/017-250802770039
- **Area plan:** the Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions work applies, alongside the citywide Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; a Fraserview-specific adopted plan: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- **Floodplain:** the district is on the south slope to the Fraser River; the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, with Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it — most relevant to the lowest streets above the river. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** every residential property owner must submit an annual property status declaration; properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026. The City's tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/evidence-exemptions.aspx and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — directly relevant to V on E49 if developer units remain. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the governing frame for any developer sale at V on E49. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
