## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At The Arden, the Form B and strata plan are also where the 22-vs-16 unit-count conflict (Section 2) gets settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Portica West (1993) is the building in this file where the depreciation report carries the most weight — it predates the other three counted buildings by 15 years or more. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Portica West's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children under Strata Property Regulation 7.01. No Glenwood building in this file is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw 2008, No. 3630 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 2013, No. 3838. Glenwood's detached homes sit under the RS1–RS4 zones (minimum lot widths at least 15 m RS1, 12 m RS2, 30 m RS3, 9.5 m RS4), and townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw. The City permits 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/glenwood-pq.md)
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026. Utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/glenwood-pq.md)
- **Flood:** a significant portion of Port Coquitlam is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain and is protected by dykes; Glenwood sits on the city's north side, and lot-level floodplain status is UNVERIFIED and must be checked with the City. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/glenwood-pq.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to any remaining developer sale at Lexford). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $660,000 (the Port Coquitlam community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026 — community level, illustration only; no Glenwood median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,200 (2% × $460,000) = **$11,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (Lexford), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
