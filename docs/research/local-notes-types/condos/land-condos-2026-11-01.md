## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The land rules that govern a condo-relevant land purchase come first, in the order a buyer meets them: the ALR gate, parcel-by-parcel zoning, GST, PTT — then the strata rules, which apply only after a strata plan is deposited and create no rights in a vacant parcel today.

- **The ALR stratification bar (where farm land is in scope):** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239), and subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission (Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A buyer pricing an ALR parcel on a future condo or townhouse strata project is pricing a permission that does not exist until the Commission grants it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **ALR housing frame:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. A parcel's ALR status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address. Whether any specific parcel in this page's feed is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **Zoning and OCP verification, parcel by parcel:** this page spans many municipalities, so there is no single zoning bylaw or OCP to cite for it — each parcel takes its permitted uses, density and development timing from its own municipality's in-force zoning and OCP designation, verified for that PID with that municipality. Zoning, servicing (municipal water/sewer versus well/septic) and development timing or cost for any specific parcel in the feed: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — listing text (for example a Squamish card's rezoning history and a Gibsons card's RU2 use list) is listing-agent copy, not a municipal confirmation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **GST on a land purchase:** CRA's GST/HST Memorandum 19.5 states that for GST/HST purposes, all supplies of land situated in Canada are taxable, unless explicitly exempted; and a GST/HST registrant selling a taxable parcel of land will normally collect and remit the GST/HST to the CRA on its GST/HST return for the period in which the sale occurred. Whether GST applies to a specific land sale therefore depends on the seller and the parcel's history — exempt sales of vacant land typically include personal-use land sold by an individual, subject to exceptions — and no figure or rule for a specific feed parcel is stated in this file: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel; confirm with the buyer's legal professional before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/19-5/land-associated-real-property.html and https://www.bakertilly.ca/insights/indirecttaxalert-sale-of-vacant-land-by-an-individual-subject-to-gst-hst (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) on land — worked example with its inputs stated:** the general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; the tax is based on the fair market value of the property (land and improvements) on the day it is registered at the Land Title Office, unless an exemption applies. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $500,000 (a stated arithmetic illustration only, not a feed price or a land statistic), fully taxable with no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **The strata rules apply only after a strata plan is deposited:** a vacant parcel has no strata corporation, no Form B, no CRF and no bylaws. If a strata plan is later deposited on non-ALR land and units are sold, the BC standard block is what a buyer of those units gets: Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59) — the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote; a minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws void since November 24, 2022; and only 55+ age bylaws legal (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. None of that can be promised for a parcel today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates on what gets built:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — so a home or condo later built and first sold on a parcel bought from this feed is a GST purchase. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** if units on a parcel are later marketed before completion, REDMA s. 21 gives a purchaser a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale exists on this page today — the rule is the future frame for a development parcel, nothing more. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% — stated for the eventual home purchase on the land, not for the land purchase itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Who the seller's agent works for:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party — relevant when a buyer contacts a land listing's brokerage directly. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)

First, the parcel's own municipal zoning and OCP designation must permit that form — verified for that PID with that municipality, never from listing text. - **Q: How many homes can I build on ALR land?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less in total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), with a secondary suite permitted only inside that residence; one additional detached residence may also be permitted without an ALC application, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it — more than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address; subdivision of reserve land requires ALC permission apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. Run the check before pricing any rural lot — reserve status controls the residence caps and the subdivision gate. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)

Get the treatment confirmed by your legal professional before writing — do not assume either answer from the listing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/19-5/land-associated-real-property.html and https://www.bakertilly.ca/insights/indirecttaxalert-sale-of-vacant-land-by-an-individual-subject-to-gst-hst (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/land.md)
