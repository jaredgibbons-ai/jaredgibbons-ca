A multi-unit house on a Bayridge lot under those rules is a freehold house with suites, not a strata apartment condo, unless a strata plan is actually registered for the specific lot — UNCONFIRMED for any Bayridge lot in the sources checked. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The BC strata rules below are province-wide and would govern any strata lot a buyer did find on this slope; the city items are West Vancouver's, not Burnaby's — the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 cited in the companion Burnaby files has no application in the District of West Vancouver.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase of any strata lot, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. With no verified strata building in this area, this is a per-lot check if a stratified property is ever marketed here. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a West Vancouver strata reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District zoning and plan (not Burnaby's):** the governing bylaws are West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — Section 200 RS single-family zones state minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3 and RS10, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5 — and the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018; Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, bringing the OCP into alignment with the Interim Housing Needs Report. A local area plan naming Bayridge is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/bayridge.md)
- **Foreshore and hazard permits — the live check on this shore:** the District requires a Foreshore Development Permit for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding), an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/bayridge.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/bayridge.md)
- **Additional school tax at these price points:** the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/bayridge.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant only if a developer ever markets a stratified project on this slope; no such project was verified here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
