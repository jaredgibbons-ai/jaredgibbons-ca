## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md) http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** BCFSA states a licensed real estate professional in BC must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018, except in the rarest of circumstances — the host cannot simply "also represent" a buyer who walks in. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md) https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)
- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This is the document step after the open house — the open house shows the unit, the Form B shows the building. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. In this roster that lands hardest on the oldest buildings — Mountain View Estates (1988/1989) and Creekside Estates (1993). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory still shows "rentals not allowed" for Mountain View Estates — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and an open-house host repeating it is repeating an unenforceable line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Mountain View Estates is presented as a 55+ community — confirm on the Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan and zoning:** the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 5500) presents the long-term vision for the whole city; the Sardis neighbourhood plan is Schedule B5 of that bylaw. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026 the City published a 5% late payment penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. On a sale, the buyer's and seller's lawyer or notary allocates the bill and the Home Owner Grant claim on the Statement of Adjustments. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (developer sales, for example remaining new product at Mountainview Lane or The York), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame where an open house is a developer sales centre rather than a resale showing. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md) http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The disclosure rule has an open-house carve-out with a tripwire.** Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation only while the host is hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers to general questions — the moment the host solicits or receives your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, the exception ends. That is the legal line between small talk and sharing with the seller's side. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for Chilliwack — anyone quoting a "Chilliwack median" from that source is quoting nothing.** Chilliwack is CADREB territory and outside SnapStats coverage; this file publishes no median, sales count or days-on-market figure, and sold history for every building in Section 2 stays UNVERIFIED pending a Paragon export. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)
