## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Mayfair Terrace, ask for the current service agreement alongside the Form B — the mandatory service plan sits on top of strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Shaughnessy Square's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — the frame for Mayfair Terrace's registered 55+ position, the city's only one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw 2008, No. 3630 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 2013, No. 3838. The City's SSMUH amendment summary for Bylaw No. 3630 renames the RS zones "Residential Small-Scale," permits up to 4 dwelling units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, replaces "coach house" with "accessory dwelling unit" (ADU floor area limit 90 m², excluded from FAR), and caps a secondary suite at 40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling unit — but whether that amendment package is the in-force consolidated text today is UNVERIFIED in the research, so confirm a specific lot with the City. Secondary-suite owner-occupancy rule and short-term rental rules for Port Coquitlam: UNVERIFIED — no official City source stating either was read in this program. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157 and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/port-coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026; penalties are set by provincial legislation and the City cannot waive them. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/port-coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to any remaining developer sale at Luxora). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $660,000 (the Port Coquitlam community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,200 (2% × $460,000) = **$11,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (Luxora), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
