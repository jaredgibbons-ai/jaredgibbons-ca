## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR stratification bar — leads this section, because guide-feed copy raises strata ideas:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; see also Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **ALC residence caps (for parcels in the ALR):** one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, not more permissive. The guide page's ALR section states the same 500 m² / 90 m² frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **Township zoning and OCP frame:** Campbell Valley sits under the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 — the coach-house definition source is s.102 of that bylaw — and the Township's plan frame as carried in the home file. A specific parcel's zoning, permitted dwellings and barns are UNCONFIRMED at parcel level; Township confirmation is required for the specific parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **Township SSMUH — the correct rule:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-plex tier exists in the Township. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; eligibility for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **Suites and coach houses (Township):** the Township secondary-suite cap in this program's settled position is 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500, s.102 — never strata, never a condo. The "15% of lot to max 100 m²" coach/garden-suite rule is Abbotsford's, NOT the Township's, and is not applied here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **BC Assessment farm-class thresholds (for farmed parcels):** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (over 4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. The farm-class position of Campbell Valley's stock as a group is UNCONFIRMED — classification is property-by-property. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **Form B (BC standard block):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — a strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, with the most recent depreciation report attached. No strata stock is verified in this sub-area, so no Campbell Valley purchase in this file runs on a Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** depreciation report every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023. **Rentals / age:** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry — no such line arises in this sub-area's verified stock, because there is none); only 55+ age bylaws legal (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01, with exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township property taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 (including the unclaimed home owner grant) and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **Pad rent (parks):** where a Campbell Valley purchase involves a manufactured-home pad, pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act: the rent increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months. No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. PTT/GST apportionment on a specific farm/acreage purchase: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/campbell-valley-guide.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%.
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is on this guide page; elsewhere, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s. 18 requires deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
