## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no verified strata in Steelhead for this to attach to today; the document matters if a strata product ever surfaces here, and it is the first check that a "condo" listing in this sub-area is strata at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Section 2 building carries a directory rental line to void in-entry, because no Steelhead strata building was verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Steelhead building is published as age-restricted, because no Steelhead strata building was verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Mission plan and zoning:** the City of Mission's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026 — live guides stating a June 1, 2026 adoption date are WRONG (see the flag at the top of this file). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020: the rural area is divided into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s inside the Rural Residential designation; a City rezoning notice describes the RU16 zone as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size, and City application material for the neighbouring Stave Falls area confirms RU16-to-RU16s rezonings are the route used to permit a secondary detached unit on a rural lot outside the Urban Growth Boundary on land designated Rural Residential. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **Secondary dwellings:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. On rural lots, a detached second dwelling generally requires the "s" variant of the parcel's zone, obtained by rezoning. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** — City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3). This is the CITY regime, and Steelhead is inside the City of Mission; the staged 5% + 5% figures quoted for rural Fraser Valley land belong to the Taxation (Rural Area) Act regime for unincorporated areas and do NOT apply here. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Wells, septic and ALR:** the guide feed's own listings show the area's servicing pattern — drilled wells, septic systems and propane heat on acreage parcels. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is one structural reason no ALR parcel here can become condo stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Steelhead was verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured homes and pad rent:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act limits pad-rent increases in manufactured home parks (2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount). Steelhead's manufactured homes in the feed are on owned land, not park pads, so the MHPTA regime does not apply to them — a buyer who wants park-tenure product is looking at a different sub-area and a different tenure entirely. No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
