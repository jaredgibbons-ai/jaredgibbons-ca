# Sunshine Hills Woods — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sunshine Hills Woods is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name that combines two City of Delta neighbourhoods from the former North Delta Area Plan (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) — Sunshine Hills (neighbourhood 12 of 14) and Sunshine Woods (neighbourhood 13 of 14); the City publishes no street-by-street boundary for either, and the working grid used in the research is 72 Avenue north, 64 Avenue south, Watershed Park and Lyon Road west, Scott Road (120 Street) east. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the south-east corner against Scott Road, south of 72 Avenue — Scott & Nicholson at 6950/6960 Nicholson Road, and the Village at Sunshine Hills project at 11996 70 Avenue (70 Avenue and Scott Road). The detached interior around Watershed Park and Cougar Canyon has no apartment condo buildings confirmed. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- This corrects the expectation in the research file, which recorded no condo building in the area confirmed from a City or strata source: both buildings below sit south of the 72 Avenue line (outside the detached core the research described) and are classified Sunshine Hills Woods in MLS listings and presale material. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunshine-hills-woods.md)
- Market context: the sub-area's SnapStats row for Condos & Townhomes in September 2026 was Inventory 22, Solds 2, Sales Ratio 9% — a combined condo-and-townhome figure, not condos alone (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunshine-hills-woods.md).
- Green anchors: Watershed Park at 11600 Kittson Parkway is described by the City as Delta's largest park, with eleven kilometres of gravel trails; Cougar Canyon Environmental Reserve is at 11300 72 Avenue. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- Transit runs on the edges, not the interior: route 340 via Scott Road, 64 Avenue and Kittson Parkway; route 364 along the south edge; route 319 along 72 Avenue at the north edge; and the R6 RapidBus on Scott Road at the east side. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Scott & Nicholson is a 2020s building, so its first mandatory-cycle report is an early-life document; the 1989 Sunshine Woods townhome complex sits at the other end of the same cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for the Sunshine Woods townhome complex reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunshine-woods-6300-cottonwood-street
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No age bylaw for either Sunshine Hills Woods apartment building was published in the sources checked: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is parcel-by-parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, under the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 — a single consolidated plan with no area-plan schedules (the former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950); OCP Map 2 shows a Small Scale Residential designation for the detached interior of this area, while the condo buildings stand on the Scott Road / 72 Avenue corridor edge, where a City corridor report confirms townhouse projects have been built along 72 Avenue under the Mixed Residential designation. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6017/7676/9593/022d99cd68f15bb5dc1b474b79ce06c4_Proposed207220Avenue20Corridor20Pre-Zoning2028LU00987629.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) permit small-scale multi-unit housing on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide — relevant to the area's house lots, not to its strata buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Scott & Nicholson units are resales; a Village at Sunshine Hills purchase from the developer would be a new-build purchase. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Assignment consent and fees for Village at Sunshine Hills are UNCONFIRMED until its disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to a Village at Sunshine Hills purchase if it completes as new product. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Federal savings vehicles and mortgage insurance:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. CMHC published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: I thought Sunshine Hills Woods was all detached houses. Where are the condos?** At the south-east corner only: Scott & Nicholson (6950/6960 Nicholson Road, 188 units per a listing description) and the Village at Sunshine Hills project (11996 70 Avenue, 90 condos per its presale directory). Both stand south of 72 Avenue against Scott Road; the Watershed Park interior has no apartment buildings confirmed. https://www.real-estate.ca/listing/r3103586-501-6950-nicholson-road-delta-british-columbia-v4e0a8/brochure and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/
- **Q: Is Scott & Nicholson in Scottsdale or Sunshine Hills Woods?** Listings classify it as Sunshine Hills Woods, and its Nicholson Road address sits south of 72 Avenue — south of the Scottsdale working grid. The Scottsdale research file lists it under Scottsdale, which is the conflict; this series counts it once, here. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6960-nicholson-road/212
- **Q: When was Scott & Nicholson built — 2021, 2022 or 2023?** Listings give all three years across its two addresses (2021 most often, 2022 for several 6960 units, 2023 for one 6950 unit), which fits phased completion; the exact year for a specific building is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan or Form B settles it. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3101799-6960-nicholson-road-407-delta-bc-v4e-0a9/brochure and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3068408-6960-nicholson-road-406-delta-bc-v4e-0a9/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a Scott & Nicholson unit?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you; read the registered bylaws in the document package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Scott & Nicholson?** One current unit listing records cats OK — that is single-unit listing data. The building-wide pet bylaw (including any dog size/number limits) is UNCONFIRMED from public sources; confirm it in the strata documents before buying with a pet. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6960-nicholson-road/212
- **Q: Is the Village at Sunshine Hills finished?** UNCONFIRMED. Its presale directory states the developer expected completion in summer 2026, and this research (Nov 1, 2026) found no source confirming completion or registration. Treat any purchase there as a presale — with the REDMA 7-day rescission right — until completion is verified. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/ and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Q: What amenities come with Scott & Nicholson's strata fee?** Listings describe 12,000 sq ft of amenities — gym, clubhouse, fire pit lounge, BBQ area — plus a clubhouse with kitchen and party room, exercise centre and playground in the development description. Whether every amenity sits inside one strata or is shared across the development's phases is a Form B question: UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6960-nicholson-road/212
- **Q: Which schools serve the Nicholson Road condos?** Catchments are address-based; the research file's locator run for the area's sample detached address (11285 Bond Blvd) returned Sunshine Hills Elementary (K–7) and Seaquam Secondary (8–12), and a Scott & Nicholson listing names Cougar Canyon Elementary and Seaquam Secondary for its unit — the two elementary results differ by address, so run the exact unit address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121403-207-6950-nicholson-road-delta-british-columbia-v4e0a8-664900/brochure
- **Q: Does Cougar Creek affect a condo purchase here?** Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse running through Surrey and Delta, with City bank-stabilization work in Cougar Canyon; streamside setback detail is lot-specific and matters mainly to the detached and townhome lots near the canyon — for the Scott Road apartment buildings, no creek-related designation was confirmed: UNCONFIRMED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The condo corner is a different neighbourhood in practice:** Scott & Nicholson listings market walk-to access to Scottsdale Centre, Cougar Canyon and Seaquam Secondary, and the Village at Sunshine Hills directory places Scottsdale Centre mall — Save-On-Foods, Walmart Supercentre, Dollarama, BC Liquor — directly across 70 Avenue. The detached interior's amenity is Watershed Park; the condo corner's amenity is the mall and Scott Road transit. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/6960-nicholson-road/212 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/
- **Village at Sunshine Hills is mixed-use, not pure residential:** its directory entry lists 8,458 sq ft of ground-floor commercial space and three underground parking levels for 144 vehicles serving 90 homes — the commercial component means section cost-sharing and retail-neighbour questions belong in the disclosure-statement read. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/
- **Scott & Nicholson also contains townhome-form units:** at least one current listing at 6950 Nicholson Road is for a 2-level townhome unit inside the same development name — as with Muse in Annieville, the complex name alone does not tell a buyer the unit's form. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121403-207-6950-nicholson-road-delta-british-columbia-v4e0a8-664900/brochure
- **The area's detached market was North Delta's softest in September 2026:** the sub-area's detached sales ratio was 6% — the softest of North Delta's four sub-areas that month — while its Condos & Townhomes ratio was 9%, context for sellers pricing a condo against detached alternatives (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunshine-hills-woods.md).
- **A 72 Avenue corridor pre-zoning file is the next supply signal:** a City corridor report (LU009876) covers proposed 72 Avenue corridor pre-zoning and confirms townhouse projects have been built along the corridor under the Mixed Residential designation — the corridor, not the park interior, is where this area's attached stock keeps arriving. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6017/7676/9593/022d99cd68f15bb5dc1b474b79ce06c4_Proposed207220Avenue20Corridor20Pre-Zoning2028LU00987629.pdf
