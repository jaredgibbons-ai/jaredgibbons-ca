Nearby areas: English Bluff to the west, Tsawwassen Central to the east, Pebble Hill / Tsawwassen East to the south-east, Beach Grove to the south. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere in this pocket, on the sources checked — the stock confirmed on official sources is freehold single detached houses under the RS zones and duplex product under the RD zones where those zones apply, and no condo or townhome complex name with a street address in Cliff Drive is confirmed on an official, developer, strata or manager source. See Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The BC strata rules below are the frame a buyer uses here in practice — not because a Cliff Drive condo is verified, but because the nearest strata purchases a Cliff Drive search actually lands on sit in the neighbouring sub-areas, and because any future strata product in the pocket would be governed by exactly these rules.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed / allowed with restrictions" line for a building a Cliff Drive buyer ends up considering in a neighbouring sub-area is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No building in this pocket is published as age-restricted, because no building in this pocket is verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, adopted July 8, 2024, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)"; there is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan schedule in Bylaw 8400 (the former plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** City of Delta pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. On the City pages as cited, Tsawwassen lots sit under the RS and RD zones assigned parcel by parcel, with subdivision minimums including RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², while RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 sit at 4,000 m². (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **SSMUH:** Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — Delta's updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies; that, not a condo building, is this pocket's density story. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD2:** the OCP's hazard DPAs in Tsawwassen include SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment), immediately west of this pocket. Which specific Cliff Drive parcels fall inside SD2: UNVERIFIED — it is read parcel by parcel off the OCP DPA maps. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Flood frame:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the frame if any future project in or near the pocket is sold on developer contracts; no such project in the pocket was verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

- **Q: Is Cliff Drive an official neighbourhood I can rely on in an offer?** No — it is a Paragon sub-area name; the City plans this ground as part of Tsawwassen under OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) and publishes no boundary for the name. Describe a property by street, zone and address-based catchment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cliff Drive home?** SD37, address-based, K–7 elementary and 8–12 secondary with no middle tier. The recorded sample (1299 56 St) gives Cliff Drive Elementary, 5025 12 Ave, and South Delta Secondary, 750 53 St; run any other address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Can a detached Cliff Drive home add units?** Delta's SSMUH updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies — a parcel-level City check, and the actual density route in a pocket with no condo buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Q: What hazard check matters on the pocket's west edge?** Whether the parcel falls inside hazard Development Permit Area SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment), read parcel by parcel off the OCP DPA maps before subjects come off. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Q: If I buy a strata unit just outside the pocket, what document matters most?** The Form B (SPA s. 59): fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance and insurance summary, with the budget and the most recent depreciation report attached — refreshed every five years since July 1, 2024 for stratas of five or more lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit I buy near here?** Long-term, yes — rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a directory records; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are there published market stats for Cliff Drive condos?** No — SnapStats does not cover South Delta at all and there is no Cliff Drive row in its reports; any Cliff Drive condo figure quoted from that source does not exist. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **Q: What taxes close a purchase here?** PTT at 1% to $200,000 and 2% to $2,000,000 (3% above), with the first-time exemption reachable only at or below $835,000 fair market value (partial to $860,000) and the newly built exemption full to $1,100,000 — and Delta property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after that date and after September 2, 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
