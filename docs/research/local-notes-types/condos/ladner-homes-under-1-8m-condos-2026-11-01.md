## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals allowed with restrictions" line for a building in Section 2 (for example Park Regent Estates, Sunningdale) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Directly relevant to Delta Green — confirm its registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024, consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **PTT in this band:** 1% on the first $200,000 and 2% on the portion to $2,000,000 — a purchase under $1.8M is taxed entirely in those two tiers and never reaches the 3% tier or the further 2% on residential value over $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — so only this band's lowest tail can qualify, and a purchase between $860,000 and $1,800,000 is outside the program entirely. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000 — a threshold that sits inside this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the rule for Bridge & Elliott, which is excluded from the resale band count above. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood frame:** Delta operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. The live band page's foundation / high-water-table / flood-construction-level claims are site copy and are UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm flood requirements for the specific lot with the City. - **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Ladner homes are for sale under $1.8M right now — and how many are condos?** UNCONFIRMED as figures — SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures and no price-band cut, and no official source publishes a Ladner band count or a condo slice of it; the live page's feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen, and the page itself is framed as a detached feed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-under-1-8m.html
- **Q: Which Ladner condo buildings are in this band?** No public source states a building-level band membership — every building in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED for the band. What is sourced is type: central-Ladner 1980s–1990s low-rises (Ladner Pointe, Sunningdale, Park Regent Estates, Green Gables), a 2021 55+ building (Delta Green), a 2016 building (The Pointe), and the Hampton Cove waterfront apartment building (Pilothouse). A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption in this band?** Only at the very bottom: the full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (first $500,000 exempt), partial only below $860,000 — above that the program does not apply at all, subject to its other conditions. For most of this band, budget full PTT at the 1% / 2% tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Does the newly built exemption reach into this band?** Yes — its full threshold is $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, so a never-occupied new unit in the band's lower half can qualify in a way an over-$1.8M purchase cannot. The band's presale example, Bridge & Elliott, is new product governed by GST and REDMA, not resale band stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://headwaterprojects.com/development/bridge-and-elliott
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term, yes — rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still records restrictions; at Delta Green a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at Delta Green?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases. Confirm the registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What pets can I bring?** Building by building: directory/listings record pets allowed with restrictions at Park Regent Estates and Sunningdale (4758), pets allowed at Sunningdale (4768), "Pets welcome" at Green Gables, "Pets ok (2)" at Pilothouse, and pets allowed at Ladner Pointe. The registered bylaws decide. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: How do I judge levy risk in the 1980s–1990s buildings?** Through the depreciation report (mandatory refresh every five years since July 1, 2024) and the CRF balance on the Form B — envelope, roof and parkade items are the read for this era of wood-frame low-rise. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Ladner condo?** SD37, address-based — the Ministry-listed Ladner schools are in Section 1; catchments are not assigned by sub-area name or by price band, so run the building's exact address in the locator, and treat the live page's "all elementaries feed Delta Secondary" line as UNVERIFIED. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask?** Whether the building is within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (Streamside DPA), and how the depreciation report and the Form B insurance summary treat Ladner's setting behind the City's dike, seawall and pump-station system. The band page's water-table and foundation claims were not verified on an official source and are not repeated here as facts. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-homes-under-1-8m.md)
