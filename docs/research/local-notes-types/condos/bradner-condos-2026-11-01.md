# Bradner (Abbotsford) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Headline fact: Bradner has NO condo stock — and by rule, it cannot stratify its farm residences.** Bradner is a rural agricultural community partly in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules state that residences in the ALR cannot be stratified (cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately). This file documents the absence and the rules behind it rather than padding a buildings list that does not exist.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Bradner has NO condo stock. No named condo building or townhome complex in Bradner was confirmed on any official source — the area research file lists named condo/townhome complexes as UNVERIFIED (none confirmed), and the September 2026 board data shows zero attached listings.**

- The reason is structural, not a research gap: Bradner lands are zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural under the City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, and part of the area is in the Agricultural Land Reserve. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Bradner has no condos, so this section leads with the ALR/strata rules that keep it that way, then the provincial condo rules that govern the nearest real condo purchases in Abbotsford West and Central Abbotsford.

- **No stratification in the ALR:** residences in the Agricultural Land Reserve cannot be stratified (converted into strata lots and sold separately) under the ALC housing rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR housing caps:** one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; an additional detached residence for farm use or another permitted use capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **City zoning:** Bradner lands are A1/A2 Agricultural under the City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw (Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 city-wide); the Abbotsford Official Community Plan (adopted June 27, 2016; city-wide update adopted December 2025 — Section 5) applies to Bradner. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development/development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH does not create Bradner condos:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation (brief names this as Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — number/date UNCONFIRMED on the City pages opened). SSMUH targets lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use; Bradner's agricultural A1/A2 zoning and the ALR no-stratification rule are the controlling framework here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **For a condo purchase in Abbotsford West/Central — Form B and depreciation reports:** the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must hold a depreciation report on a five-year cycle (30-year projection, at least three CRF funding models, no deferral vote), and since November 1, 2023 stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rentals and age (provincial):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44); short-term rentals can still be restricted. The only legal age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Taxes and rebates on a condo elsewhere in Abbotsford:** BC PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above; first-time buyers pay no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). New condos carry 5% GST; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4; Royal Assent March 12, 2026 per sources checked — brief said March 13) returns 100% of GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on/after March 20, 2025; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate
- **Presales, savings vehicles, mortgage insurance (redirected buyers):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day presale rescission right and s. 18 requires deposits in trust; the Home Buyers' Plan allows $60,000 per buyer and the First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums run 0.60%–4.00% by loan-to-value (4.50% top band with a non-traditional down payment; +0.20% where the over-25-year surcharge applies). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo in Bradner?** No — there is no condo or townhome complex confirmed in Bradner, and the September 2026 board data shows zero attached listings. The closest condo stock is in Abbotsford West (Clearbrook, Highstreet, Westerleigh) and Central Abbotsford. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/bradner-guide.md)
- **Q: Could a Bradner farm home be turned into condos later?** No. Under the ALC housing rules, residences in the ALR cannot be stratified — they cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What housing CAN be added on a Bradner farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m², a secondary suite inside that residence only, and one additional small residence for farm use or another permitted use — 90 m² maximum on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or detached building?** No — a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the size-capped "additional residence" tied to farm use or another permitted use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is Bradner in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** Partly — it is a rural agricultural community with lands zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural, part of it in the ALR; whether a specific parcel is in the ALR must be confirmed for that parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Which schools serve Bradner?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. For the test address 5396 Bradner Road (Bradner Elementary's own address), the SD34 School Locator returns Bradner Elementary (K–5) → Eugene Reimer Middle (6–8) → Rick Hansen Secondary (9–12). Run any other address through the locator. (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01, per the research file)
- **Q: If I buy a condo in Clearbrook instead, what strata documents matter most?** The Form B (fees, CRF balance, parking/storage allocation), the depreciation report (mandatory on a five-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024), two years of minutes and the registered bylaws — especially any 55+ bylaw, which is the only age bylaw still enforceable. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Can I rent out a condo I buy in Abbotsford West?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022; a building can still restrict short-term rentals, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/prohibition-on-rental-and-age-restrictions-in-strata-buildings-556/
- **Q: What closing costs should a first-time condo buyer budget in Abbotsford?** PTT (with the first-time exemption worth up to $8,000 at values to $835,000, and the newly-built exemption full to $1,100,000), 5% GST on new construction only (with the FTHB GST rebate worth up to $50,000 on agreements on/after March 20, 2025), and a CMHC premium of 0.60%–4.50% of the loan if the down payment is under 20%. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate
- **Q: Is there any Bradner-specific tax break?** The City offers a Revitalization Tax Exemption for eligible properties in designated revitalization areas, but no Bradner-specific exemption was confirmed in the material read — treat any claimed Bradner exemption as UNVERIFIED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/tax-incentives-exemptions

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The no-strata rule is the single most important "condo" fact about Bradner:** because ALR residences cannot be stratified, there is no legal pathway for a Bradner farm property to become condo-style separately-sold homes — buyers pricing a parcel on a future strata exit are pricing something the ALC rules prohibit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025** (Abbotsforward 2050; public hearing November 25, 2025), emphasizing apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enabling 37,185 units over 20 years — growth directed at the urban nodes, while Bradner's framework remains the A1/A2 agricultural zoning and the OCP's rural village input gathered from Bradner and other rural villages. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development/development/official-community-plan
- **The Bradner Road corridor itself is an active City file:** the City maintains a Bradner Road engagement page covering the road toward the Highway 1 corridor — road work, not housing, is the change to watch in this community. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/bradnerroad
- **The catchment question for Bradner was genuinely unsettled until the locator run:** district sources conflicted (the boundary-maps page named W.A. Fraser Middle for 5396 Bradner Road while Eugene Reimer's family-of-schools page named Reimer); the SD34 School Locator run on 2026-11-01 settled it as Eugene Reimer Middle, matching the feeder pattern Bradner Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
- **A Bradner logging right-of-way is on the public record:** retention of a Bradner logging right-of-way by A&A Trading Ltd. was confirmed in a City of Abbotsford council-agenda context (May 1, 2023 agenda) — a reminder that working-land rights, not development rights, are what change hands out here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2023-05/2023-05-01%20-%20Agenda%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Meeting.pdf
- **Property-tax timing is city-wide and applies to Bradner acreages:** Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
