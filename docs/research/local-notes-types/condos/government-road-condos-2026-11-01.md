# Government Road — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**CLASSIFICATION NOTE — read first:** the condo stock counted here (the Carrigan Court / Cardston Court / Horne Street pocket) sits **south** of Lougheed Highway and is MLS-classified Government Road. It is not The City of Lougheed's tower stock, which stands north of the highway and is not imported into this file. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Government Road is a residential/light-industrial corridor running **south of Lougheed Highway**, between the Lougheed Town Centre/Cameron lands to the east and Burnaby Lake/Sperling to the west. Government Road itself is the spine; the residential pocket hangs off it on Carrigan Court, Cardston Court and Horne Street. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: that one pocket — seven complexes built 1974–2007, from 1970s low-rise and towers to a 2007 Polygon low-rise at the corridor's west end. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md) and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-government-road-apartments/SearchResults.form/
- Schools: École Seaforth Elementary (7881 Government Road), Stoney Creek Community Elementary (2740 Beaverbrook Crescent), Cameron Elementary (9540 Erickson Drive) and Burnaby Mountain Secondary (8800 Eastlake Drive). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- Transit: route 110 runs on Government Road itself (Lougheed Station ↔ Metrotown via Beaverbrook, Eastlake, Brighton, Phillips and Winston) — the corridor's own street carries the bus. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- Green anchor: Stoney Creek Park and its trail system on the east side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- Planning frame: the Lochdale Urban Village Plan was adopted July 25, 2022 — it governs the lands **north across Lougheed Highway**, not this corridor's south side, but it is the document reshaping the corridor's northern edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this corridor's 1974–1985 stock the Form B is also the document that resolves the flagged conflicts in Section 2 — the Harrington/Discovery Place II name and year, and which "Lougheed Estates" a listing actually means. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids two published lines in Section 2: the "Rentals" half of Concorde Place's "No Pets or Rentals" listing headline, and the "with restrictions" element of Lougheed Estates' published rental position. Woodstone Place's "rentals allowed" is simply current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Burnaby's current zoning bylaw is the **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026). The 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 is repealed — any source still citing No. 4742 is superseded. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP; the Lochdale Urban Village Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the lands north across Lougheed Highway. Under the R1 SSMUH rules in place since June 2024, a residential lot can hold 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after September; Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — everything in Section 2 is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to this resale stock. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are these buildings part of The City of Lougheed?** No. The City of Lougheed towers stand north of Lougheed Highway; every complex in Section 2 sits south of the highway in the Government Road corridor and is MLS-classified Government Road. The stock is also a different era — 1974–2007 low-rise and mid-rise, not new town-centre towers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- **Q: What is the newest stock in the corridor?** Polygon's 2007 pair at the west end: the Sandlewood condo low-rise (9233 + 9283 Government Street, wood-frame, Sandlewood Club amenities) and the Terramor townhomes next door at 9133 Government Street. Everything else counted here is 1974–1985. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9233-government-street/106
- **Q: A Concorde Place listing says "No Pets or Rentals" — is that enforceable?** Half of it. The rentals half is void — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and the building directory itself records rentals allowed. The pets half matches the directory (pets not allowed) and remains a live bylaw question for the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3138063-507-9521-cardston-court-burnaby-bc-v3n-4r8
- **Q: Is 3970 Carrigan Court called Discovery Place II or The Harrington?** Both names are in current use — the directory says Discovery Place II (strata plan NWS2364, built 1986); listings market The Harrington (built 1987 per Zolo, 1986 in one listing). It is one building, counted once in Section 2; order documents by the strata plan number, not the marketing name. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/discovery-place-ii-3970-carrigan-crt-court
- **Q: How old is the oldest stock, and what has been done to it?** Lougheed Estates (9202 Horne Street) and Lougheed Estates II (3901 Carrigan Court) both date to 1974. Published work includes elevators replaced in 2025 at 9202 Horne; the balance of the envelope/mechanical picture is a depreciation-report question — every strata here with 5+ lots has owed one every five years since July 1, 2024. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/lougheed-estates-9202-horne-street
- **Q: Are the "townhomes" inside Discovery Place I a separate complex?** No. The T-prefixed units are marketed as one-level townhome-style units within the 3980 Carrigan Court tower complex. The corridor's actual townhome complexes are Terramor (9133 Government Street) and Tudor Village (8983 Horne Street), documented separately in Section 2. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/3980-carrigan-court/2007

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two of the seven complexes share one name, loosely.** "Lougheed Estates" is the directory name at 9202 Horne Street and "Lougheed Estates II" at 3901 Carrigan Court — yet some current listings label the Horne Street building "Lougheed Estates II" as well. Document orders and depreciation reports run to the strata corporation; confirm the plan number before ordering anything. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/lougheed-estates-ii-3901-carrigan-court
- **The newest condos and the townhomes are one Polygon project area.** Sandlewood (2007 condos) and Terramor (2007 townhomes) sit side by side on Government Street and share the club-amenity set in listings — but they are different strata product, and a search that merges 9133 and 9233 Government Street will mix them. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9233-government-street/106
- **Zero detached sales is a recorded month here.** SnapStats' September 2026 Government Road detached row is 24 listings and 0 sales, while condos & townhomes recorded 3 sales from 23 listings — in this corridor the strata pocket is the more liquid half of the market. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/government-road.md)
- **The plan reshaping the area stops at the highway.** The Lochdale Urban Village Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the lands north across Lougheed — the corridor's own south-side zoning story is the general Burnaby framework, so "Lochdale village" marketing for a south-side address should be read as proximity, not plan coverage.
