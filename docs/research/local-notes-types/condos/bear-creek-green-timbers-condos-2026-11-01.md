## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 2020/2021 Kings Landing buildings this is where the young strata's CRF position and any early special levies are settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Bear Creek Green Timbers may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots below maximum FAR). The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Kings Landing buildings are completed stock trading as resales, so GST turns on the specific unit's status, not the building's age alone. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — no active presale building was verified in this sub-area in this pass; the frame is stated because Kings Landing product is sometimes still marketed in presale language. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

The sub-area's other named attached stock — Carlingwood and the Kings Landing townhomes — is townhouse form. Sources: Section 2 entries.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The whole verified apartment supply is one developer's one project.** Both apartment buildings in this sub-area are Dawson + Sawyer's Kings Landing phases on the same avenue — a buyer comparing "different buildings" here is mostly comparing phases, ages (2020 vs 2021) and strata plans inside a single master-planned community. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The board name joins two parks the City files under different community names.** Bear Creek Park sits in the City's Whalley framing (with the Surrey Arts Centre nearby) while Green Timbers Urban Forest anchors the north-centre — a search run under only "Bear Creek Green Timbers" misses City documents filed under either park name alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md)
- **The coming station is at the forest's edge, not in the middle of the sub-area.** Green Timbers Station will stand at 140 Street and Fraser Highway on the westernmost edge of Green Timbers Urban Forest — station proximity varies sharply across this large sub-area, and the project itself names Surrey Memorial Hospital and the RCMP E-Division headquarters among the facilities it will serve. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- **Attached sellers face a thin, slow pool.** September 2026 gave this sub-area just 37 attached listings and 3 attached sales (8%), against Whalley next door holding 635 attached listings — complex-level comparables inside Kings Landing and Carlingwood matter more here than any city figure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md)
- **The townhouse stock is materially older than the condo stock.** Carlingwood was built around 2000, two decades before the Kings Landing apartment buildings — depreciation-report and envelope questions that barely arise in the 2020/2021 buildings are central in the townhouse complex. Sources: Section 2 entries.
