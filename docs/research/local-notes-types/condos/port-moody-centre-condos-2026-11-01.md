## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Moody Centre is the city's downtown and station core at the head of the Port Moody Arm of Burrard Inlet, around St. Johns Street, Clarke Street and Murray Street, between Moody Centre Station and Rocky Point Park, taking in the Newport and Suter Brook village areas by Inlet Centre Station. Exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/city-hall/resources/Documents/OCP-neighbourhood-plan-areas_-_edited_for_EngagePM_website.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-moody-centre.md)
- Where condo stock concentrates: around the two stations — Suter Brook Village and Newport Village by Inlet Centre Station, Klahanie along Klahanie Drive, and the St. Johns Street / Moody Street shelf by Moody Centre Station (Section 2). The detached stock on the Moody Centre side streets is among the city's oldest. (Research file, Sections 4–5)
- Market figures, September 2026 (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, as stated in the research file): sub-area Port Moody Centre — detached 15 active listings, 2 sold, 13% sales ratio; condos & townhomes 120 active listings, 16 sold, 13% sales ratio. Community (Port Moody; medians are published at community level only) — detached median $1,685,000, $608/sq ft, 96% sale-to-list, 33 days on market; condos & townhomes median $715,000, $666/sq ft, 94% sale-to-list, 29 days on market. Twelve-month attached trend: the community median moved from $850,000 (September 2025) to $715,000 (September 2026) on 431 sales. (Research file, Section 2)
- Transit: Moody Centre Station combines Millennium Line SkyTrain (Evergreen Extension), West Coast Express commuter rail and a bus loop; Inlet Centre Station is the next SkyTrain station east. St. Johns Street and Clarke Street are the main east–west roads, with Barnet Highway running west toward Burnaby and Vancouver. https://maps.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/system-maps/fwc26_regional_transit_connections.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- Schools and catchments: SD43 catchments are address-based and do not follow neighbourhood lines — a locator run for 100 Newport Drive returned Mountain Meadows Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary, while Moody Elementary, Moody Middle and Port Moody Secondary (300 Albert Street) are the central schools. Check the exact address in the locator before relying on a school name. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43 (Research file, Section 4)
- Parks, recreation and civic: Rocky Point Park (pier, boat launch, outdoor pool, skate park, playground; start of the Shoreline Trail); Port Moody Recreation Complex, 300 Ioco Road; Kyle Centre, 125 Kyle Street; Port Moody Public Library, 100 Newport Drive. Eagle Ridge Hospital is in Port Moody at 475 Guildford Way, just east of the centre. https://engage.portmoody.ca/rocky-point-and-old-orchard-parks-master-plans/news_feed/background-rocky-point-today and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Port-Moody/eagle-ridge-hospital (Research file, Section 4)
- Heritage: Moody Centre is a heritage conservation area (with the Ioco townsite), so a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction or reconstruction on included properties — relevant to the older detached stock around Clarke and Murray Streets, not to the strata shelf. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/ (Research file, Section 4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Boardwalk (2006–2008 wood-frame) the depreciation report is the first-order document; at Platform it is also where the 2019-vs-2021 build-year conflict gets settled against the strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Boardwalk's directory line is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed; short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted building in this file is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw — and 50 Electronic's "50" is an address/brand, not an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is **Port Moody OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (adopted April 28, 2026; repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — never cite 2955 as current), Chapter 14, §14.6 (Moody Centre, including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area) and §14.5 (Inlet Centre and Coronation Park). Zoning is Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937: RS zones for single-detached, RT and RM zones for multi-residential, TC and CRM zones for mixed-use; the transit-oriented areas are exempt from small-scale multi-unit housing rules. In Moody Centre, RS1 lots in OCP Single Family–Low Density areas can be rezoned to RS1-S for small lots of at least 9 m width and 325 m² area — a standard available in Moody Centre (and Seaview and Pleasantside) but **not** in College Park. Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached zones except manufactured-home parks, and a suite may not be stratified. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf and https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx (Research file, Section 4)
- **Port Moody property taxes and utilities:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities (water, sewer, waste) are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026 — budget both bills, not just the tax notice. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities (Research file, Section 4)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all four counted buildings are resale stock; Inlet District and Westport Village presales carry GST. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $715,000 (the Port Moody community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Port Moody Centre median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,300 (2% × $515,000) = **$12,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (relevant to the presales above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** presales exist in this sub-area (Inlet District; Westport Village). REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Port Moody replaced its OCP this year, and the old number is everywhere.** OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 was adopted April 28, 2026 and repeals Bylaw No. 2955; listing packages and older guides still cite 2955. Any OCP argument for a Port Moody Centre purchase should run off 3522 — and "Port Moody Centre" itself is a board name, not an OCP neighbourhood (the plan says Moody Centre and Inlet Centre). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf
- **One Noons Creek Drive address holds three different seniors services.** 101 Noons Creek Drive is simultaneously the Housing Registry seniors residence (directory name "Inlet Centre"; residence name Alex Graham Manor), the 10-bed Crossroads hospice, and The Club, the 50+ drop-in café run out of the residence's former dining room. A buyer reading only one source will mislabel the building. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/port-moody-centre-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, Section 5)
- **The attached median fell while the detached median held.** Port Moody's community condos & townhomes median moved from $850,000 in September 2025 to $715,000 in September 2026 (431 sales over the 12 months), while the detached median moved from $1,615,000 to $1,685,000 — price a centre condo against the attached trend, not the city's detached headline. SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. (Research file, Section 2)
- **A verified building can still be unverifiable.** Sonrisa trades openly on St Johns Street with consistent listing detail across brokerages — yet no developer, strata, manager or City source for it surfaced in two passes, so this program carries it UNVERIFIED and out of the count. Listing consistency is not verification. (Section 2 sources.)
- **The small-lot map is drawn by neighbourhood, and this sub-area is on the right side of it.** The RS1-S small-lot standard (9 m width, 325 m²) is available in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside — College Park, across the city, is not on the list, so the same detached lot carries different subdivision potential depending on which Port Moody sub-area file it sits in. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx (Research file, Section 4)
- **Heritage rules touch the cheapest detached stock, not the condos.** The older houses around St. Johns, Clarke and Murray Streets sit in the Moody Centre heritage conservation area, where a heritage alteration permit can be required for construction or reconstruction — a cost and timeline item that never appears in listing text. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
