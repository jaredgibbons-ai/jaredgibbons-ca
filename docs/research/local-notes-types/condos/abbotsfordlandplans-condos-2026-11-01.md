- **SSMUH:** the live page's FAQ summary (eligible single-family and duplex lots inside the Urban Development Boundary connected to municipal water and sanitary sewer permitting typically 3 to 4 units depending on lot size, plus secondary suites and garden suites) matches the established frame: Abbotsford amended its Zoning Bylaw in June 2024 for provincial small-scale multi-unit housing, with accessory units counting toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots bigger than 280 m². ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between Abbotsford's OCP and its Zoning Bylaw?** The Zoning Bylaw (2014, No. 2400) regulates what is legally permitted to be built on a parcel today without rezoning; the Official Community Plan sets the long-term future land use designation and rezoning policy for the area. The City directs both checks — current zoning and future land uses, plus development permit areas — to its property information search or a property search in WebMap. Note the bylaw-number caution at the top of this file: the live page cites the OCP as "Bylaw No. 2500-2016" while this program's verified research carries Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated, and the current citation is UNVERIFIED in this program. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
- **Q: Which Abbotsford plans are actually adopted neighbourhood plans?** The adopted neighbourhood plans listed in the OCP as carried in this program are UDistrict, City Centre, Historic Downtown and McKee, with the McKee Neighbourhood Plan verified as Bylaw 3400-2023, Schedule E of the OCP. The live page's fifth topic, a Marshall Road Corridor Plan, is UNVERIFIED as an adopted City plan on any City source in this program. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
- **Q: Is the 2016 OCP still the plan to read?** No. Council adopted the Abbotsforward 2050 update to the Official Community Plan in December 2025; the new plan is current, and designations should be read under it. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/council-brief/december-2025 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
- **Q: How do BC's SSMUH rules apply in Abbotsford?** Under the June 2024 SSMUH amendments, accessory units count toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots bigger than 280 m² — with secondary suites (within the principal unit, registered, under 50% of its size) and garden suites / coach houses (up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m²) as the accessory forms. The amending bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages in this program. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
- **Q: Can ALR parcels be rezoned for urban subdivisions?** Not through the City alone: removing land from the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and inside the ALR the ALC residence frame and the City's A1/A2 subdivision minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) govern. The live page's related claim that about 72% of Abbotsford's land base is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source in this program. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
- **Q: How did the plan areas' part of Abbotsford sell in September 2026?** No plan-area figures exist in the permitted source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsfordlandplans.md)
