## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 1976 Wolfe Road townhouse complex this is the central diligence document; for The Robson it is also where the 2022-vs-2023 build-year conflict gets settled against the registered strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. A 1976 townhouse complex's report (roof, cladding and window renewal cycles — one current listing credits the 45185 Wolfe Road complex with updated cladding, windows and roof) is where its fee level is explained. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Robson's directory line reads "rentals allowed," which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Chilliwack Proper West building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (the "Chilliwack 2050" OCP) — its schedules include the Downtown Form & Character Development Permit Area as Schedule D-6, the DPA that covers The Robson's downtown ground. Cite the plan schedules correctly: in the OCP framework the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000; its Section 6 sets the agricultural zones, Section 7 the rural zones, and Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone. The live page's specific R1-B / R3 infill-zone claims around Bernard Avenue and Ashwell Road were not confirmed section-by-section in the bylaw — UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024 (the date is recorded in Section 14's amendment record in the consolidated bylaw), allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff report. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **Secondary suites and ADUs:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), and garden suites are capped at 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g). The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits the rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling in the City regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. This staged 5% + 5% is Chilliwack's own city scheme — do not confuse it with Mission's flat 10%, or with the rural Taxation (Rural Area) Act regime (which shares the 5% first stage but runs its second stage at October 31 and is paid to the Province). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Flood and ALR:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA reserve zone, Section 12.02 of Bylaw No. 5000) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Chilliwack Proper West lots are UNVERIFIED. Properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; ALR land inside the Chilliwack Proper West working scope is UNVERIFIED (the scope is the urban city-proper side), and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any case (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Robson (2022/2023) trades as resale stock now, so confirm a specific unit's status before assuming GST treatment. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Chilliwack Proper West was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This market runs on a different board's numbers — or none.** Chilliwack trades on CADREB, SnapStats does not cover the city, and CADREB publishes board-wide figures only. Any listing copy quoting a "Chilliwack Proper West median" is quoting a number no published source in this program produces. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **The Robson's build year disagrees between the directory and its own listings.** Directory: 2022. Listings: 2023, consistently. The difference matters for warranty stage and depreciation-report timing, and it settles only on the registered strata plan — a buyer's agent should not repeat either year as settled until then. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **One Wolfe Road complex has no citable name.** The 45185 Wolfe Road townhouse complex (built 1976) is well documented in listings, but the only name for it in the sources checked sits on zealty.ca, which this program never cites — so the complex is identified by address alone here, and a buyer searching by the name may not find this file's entry. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Chilliwack's suite cap is a "lesser of" test, not a flat number.** A secondary suite must be both under 90 m² AND under 40% of the building's habitable floor space, inside a building with only one principal dwelling — and the one-ADU-per-lot rule means a legal suite forecloses a coach house on the same lot. Sellers marketing "suite + coach house potential" are marketing two units the bylaw counts as one slot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **The tax penalty calendar is Chilliwack's own.** 5% after July 2, 2026 and a second 5% after August 4, 2026 — a different second-stage date than the rural regime's October 31 and a different structure than Mission's flat 10%. Cross-river comparisons in listing copy routinely get this wrong. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **The downtown condo sits inside a form-and-character permit area.** OCP Bylaw 5500's Schedule D-6 makes the whole downtown a Form & Character Development Permit Area under Local Government Act s. 488(1)(d), with the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan and Downtown Design Guidelines behind it — exterior alterations and redevelopment around The Robson run through that lens, not just the zoning bylaw. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154
- **The hospital is the area's true anchor, and it is a Fraser Health community hospital** — primary, core secondary and some specialty services at 45600 Menholm Road, on the Hodgins Avenue side of the Proper West ground, minutes from both the Robson Street and Wolfe Road strata stock. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
