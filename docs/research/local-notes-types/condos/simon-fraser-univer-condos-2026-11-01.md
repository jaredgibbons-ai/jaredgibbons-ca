## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The area is the top of Burnaby Mountain in the City of Burnaby, comprising the Simon Fraser University (SFU) Burnaby campus and the UniverCity residential community beside it; the board sub-area is listed as "Simon Fraser Univer." and SnapStats prints the full name as "Simon Fraser University SFU." A separate neighbouring sub-area, "Simon Fraser Hills," covers the Forest Grove side and is not this area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: in UniverCity's four neighbourhoods — West Highlands, East Highlands, High Street and the Slopes — around University High Street, University Crescent and Tower Road, south and east of campus; the OCP envisions a dense, mixed-use community on about 65 hectares allowing up to 4,536 residential units housing up to 10,000 residents at build-out. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- Transit: TransLink's R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at the SFU Exchange, running at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak to Burrard Station; the 145 bus connects SFU Exchange to Production Way–University Station on the Millennium Line. The proposed Burnaby Mountain Gondola is **not yet approved or funded** per TransLink. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- Landmarks: the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area surrounds the campus and community — 578 hectares, 34 multi-use trails over 28 kilometres, summit 366 metres — and shopping is centred on University High Street in the Cornerstone/High Street core. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- Burnaby IS covered by SnapStats: for the Simon Fraser University SFU sub-area in September 2026, Condos & Townhomes recorded 91 active listings, 6 solds and a 7% sales ratio; Burnaby-wide (community level only — SnapStats publishes no SFU sub-area median), Condos & Townhomes recorded a median sale price of $686,000, $763 per sq ft, 98% sale-to-list and 36 average days on market. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In UniverCity the Form B sits alongside the ground lease — read both: confirm the lease commencement date, the remaining term, that rent is prepaid for the full term, and any rent-review or end-of-term provisions, because the lease is what a buyer's lender and lawyer will scrutinize. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed or capped is void to that extent — the published "Pets & rentals allowed" lines at The Nest and Altitude are consistent with current law. Note the limit: this rule governs strata bylaws, not the SFU ground lease itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age status: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — the current bylaw is new:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) — the 1965 bylaw is repealed and must not be cited as current if a source cites it. UniverCity itself was approved through OCP and zoning amendments in 1996 for the community south and east of campus. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Burnaby OCP:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP Bylaw; UniverCity is governed by its own OCP framework dating to the 1996 approvals. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Burnaby property tax:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live for Arbour presales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000. PTT and financing treatment of a leasehold interest should be confirmed on the specific lease before an offer. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Arbour and any remaining developer sales. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50%. Separately, leasehold financing is the binding constraint here: some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term, so a leasehold-experienced broker should be involved before subject removal — lender treatment per building: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)

Never price or finance a unit here as freehold. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **SFU gave up land to create the park that surrounds you.** As part of the original arrangement, SFU transferred more than 320 hectares of university land to the City of Burnaby for the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area — the community's setting is a negotiated conservation boundary, not an accident. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **There are no fee-simple lots inside UniverCity at all.** Every market home is a strata unit on the 99-year prepaid ground lease, so "lot size" comparisons between buildings come down to unit size, floor and outlook rather than land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Two buildings share the "Cornerstone" name in different tenures.** The Hub strata building sits on Cornerstone Mews (counted above), while the Cornerstone building at 8950 University High Street is presented in current sources as furnished student rentals — a search on the name alone mixes strata and rental product. https://www.apartments.com/cornerstone-burnaby-bc/bfbp10b/ and https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3097415-9009-cornerstone-mews-615-burnaby-bc-v5a-0b9/brochure
- **The elementary school inside the community is not the catchment for every mountain address.** The SD41 locator returned Cameron Elementary for 8800 Eastlake Drive even though University Highlands Elementary sits inside UniverCity — location is not catchment on the mountain either. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/simon-fraser-univer.md)
- **Altitude's two towers carry two different built years in public records.** The directory records Tower 1 (9060 University Crescent) as built 2014; listings at 9080 University Crescent record 2015 — a small example of why the strata plan registration, not a listing, settles a building's age. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/altitude-tower-1-9060-university-high-street
- **Burnaby rewrote its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026 and replaced the 1965 bylaw (No. 4742) outright — any older source citing the 1965 bylaw for UniverCity is citing a repealed bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
