## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- London Landing is the waterfront pocket at the east edge of Steveston, at the south foot of No. 2 Road where London Road and Dyke Road meet the South Arm of the Fraser River. It sits inside the Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan); in board listing practice this ground falls inside Steveston South. No City source read publishes a boundary for a "London Landing" neighbourhood — the sourced anchors are London's Wharf Park at 6062 Dyke Road, London Road (The Pier at London Landing, 6160 London Road), Princess Street (London Landing townhouses, 13200 Princess Street) and the No. 2 Road addresses 12771–13131 now in rezoning. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: two clusters. On London Road at the wharf, The Pier at London Landing (6160/6168 London Road) stands over the dyke beside London's Wharf Park, with townhouse stock behind it on London Road and Princess Street/Princess Lane. On No. 2 Road, Harbour Walk (13040 No. 2 Road) carries the pocket's second apartment building. Just north of the pocket's No. 2 Road strip, across Steveston Highway, the older Nautica North and Nautica South buildings (12633/12639 No. 2 Road) are Steveston South stock at the pocket's edge — documented in Section 2 with the edge flagged, not counted as in-pocket buildings. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The waterfront anchor: London's Wharf Park, 6062 Dyke Road, is just under 4 acres, with the No. 2 Road Pier, expansive river views, trail connections to the South Arm Dyke and Steveston Greenways trail networks, a playground, benches, open lawn and a public restroom at the north end of the pier; the City ran a 2026 "Have Your Say" process on recent changes and future improvements at the park. The City's Steveston Greenways material describes London's Landing / No. 2 Road Fishing Pier as a historic area starting at the South Dyke Trail, with views of Steveston Island (Shady Island) and the historic Cannery Channel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- Village services: Steveston Community Park, 4011 Moncton Street, holds the Steveston Community Centre, pool and library complex for the district, and Richmond Public Library's Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub) is at 4320 Moncton Street; village shops, markets and services centre on Moncton Street in Steveston Village to the west. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In London Landing's ZMU mixed-use buildings, work the strata documents hard: these are mixed-use strata (residential over commercial/industrial-permitted uses under ZMU20), so read the depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md) and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every building in Section 2 exceeds five lots on any count published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a London Landing building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. (The Pier's published position is rentals allowed in any case.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No London Landing building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — the pocket's own zones:** London Landing sits inside the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000). Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: Section 20.20 is the site-specific "Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU20) – London Landing (Steveston)" zone, created by Bylaw 8818 (September 24, 2012), providing for commercial, residential and industrial uses with a maximum floor area ratio of 1.2 plus 0.1, maximum lot coverage of 76% and maximum height of 21.0 m; the ZMU24 zone at 13040 No. 2 Road was created by Bylaw 9094 in 2014. In July 2026 City staff reported an application to redesignate No. 2 Road lands here from "Industrial" to "Local Village – Tier 1" in the Steveston Waterfront Neighbourhood Land Use Map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **The live rezoning on No. 2 Road:** on July 8, 2026, City staff reported application RZ 25-009451 by Jim Pattison Developments to rezone 13131, 13111, 13031, 12931 and 12771 No. 2 Road from Light Industrial (IL) to a new "Residential / Limited Commercial (ZMU63) – London Landing (Steveston)" zone, with OCP Amendment Bylaw 10771 and Zoning Amendment Bylaw 10772, to permit a five- and six-storey residential and commercial mixed-use project — an application in process, not an approval. Views, traffic and construction on that strip are a live file for every No. 2 Road purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **Flood:** the pocket fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system; Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level, protected by 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks — for waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots, confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition. The City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **Heritage:** the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront west of this pocket, and properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan; whether any London Landing parcel sits inside the conservation area boundary is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **District energy:** the Alexandra District Energy Utility applies to the West Cambie / Alexandra area only — it does not apply in London Landing. The Pier's listings describe building geothermal heating and cooling; no City source checked states a district-energy or other area utility arrangement for this pocket: UNCONFIRMED beyond the building's own system. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the buildings in Section 2 are resale stock; GST becomes relevant again only if the RZ 25-009451 project proceeds to developer sales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the resale buildings in Section 2. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in the pocket was confirmed on a developer source in this pass — the No. 2 Road project is a rezoning application, not a presale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
