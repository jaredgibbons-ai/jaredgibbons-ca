## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In Uptown's older stock (Ashington Court, 1973; Amara Terrace, 1980s; Queens Terrace and Penny Lane, mid-1990s) the depreciation report is the first-order document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Queens Terrace's directory line, and The Woodward's "rentals not allowed" line, are **VOID** as restrictions (long-term rentals are allowed; short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted building in this file is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw — the 55+ program verified zero 55+ ownership in Uptown. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, under which Uptown is a Frequent Transit Development Area — an area where the City directs higher-density, transit-supportive growth. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001: apartment buildings concentrate along the Sixth Street / Sixth Avenue core under the multiple-unit residential and commercial high-rise districts, while the side streets sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) — RS-1 (s. 310) sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; secondary suites are allowed in the RS districts, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf (Research file, Sections 4–5)
- **RT CORRECTION — required:** the research file's Section 5 states townhouse form is regulated under "Townhouse Residential Districts RT-A and RT-B." **RT-A/RT-B DO NOT EXIST in New Westminster.** The actual townhouse residential districts in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 are **RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B, RT-2C and RT-3, RT-3A, RT-3B**. The research file's RT-A/RT-B line was an error and is corrected here; the same error was also found in the Brunette and Glenbrooke North research files and corrected in the Sapperton condos file — do not repeat RT-A/RT-B in any Uptown copy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/uptown-nw.md, Section 5; Sapperton condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/sapperton-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones only the low-lying edges — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end industrial, Zone 3 the Quay, Zone 4 Brunette River industrial. No floodplain zone for the Uptown plateau was confirmed; lot-level status is checked with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- **Heritage:** the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area sits just east of Uptown — Uptown itself is outside it, so heritage status here is property-by-property, checked against the City's heritage register rather than assumed from the neighbourhood. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php (Research file, Section 4)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining on Friday, July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all seven counted buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $560,000 (the New Westminster community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Uptown median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $7,200 (2% × $360,000) = **$9,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale was verified in this sub-area in the research pass. If a purchase here is a developer sale, REDMA applies conditionally: s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file, UNVERIFIED items)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One street, three buildings, one trap:** on Queens Avenue, the subsidized seniors rental (Hillside Place, 1025) faces a general-market strata tower (Amara Terrace, 1026) directly across the street, and Queens Terrace (1032) stands on the same block. A search built on the name "Hillside" or the street alone will mix rental seniors housing with ordinary condos for sale — only 1025 is seniors housing, and it is rental. https://www.readkong.com/page/affordable-housing-bc-housing-9785076 and https://vadapm.com/amara-terrace-1-bedroom-apartment-rental-806-1026-queens-new-westminster
- **There are two Hillside Places in the same small city, in different sub-areas.** Hillside Place at 1025 Queens Avenue (Uptown) is non-profit seniors rental; Hillside Place at 509 Carnarvon Street (Downtown NW) is a general-market strata marketed to families and investors. A listing, a strata document or a showing booked under the bare name can belong to either — the street address is the only safe identifier. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/hillside-place-509-carnarvon-street (55+ file, Section 2)
