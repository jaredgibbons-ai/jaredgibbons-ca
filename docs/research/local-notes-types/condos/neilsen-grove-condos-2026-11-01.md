# Neilsen Grove — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Spelling, kept exact throughout: the AREA is "Neilsen Grove"; the SCHOOL is "Neilson Grove Elementary"** — School District 37 spells the school "Neilson Grove Elementary" on its own site, at 5500 Admiral Boulevard. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/

Neilsen Grove is a Paragon sub-area name for north Ladner, in the City of Delta — it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name. The sub-area sits on and around the Marina Garden Estates lands named in City of Delta council reports, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The sub-area is the harbour-side pocket of north Ladner: Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard are its spine, with Neilson Grove Elementary at 5500 Admiral Boulevard and Ladner Harbour Park at 4905 McNeelys Way serving this shore of Ladner. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park
- Hawthorne is to the south, and Delta Manor and the Ladner village core to the southwest (working geography from the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/neilsen-grove-guide.md).
- "Hampton Cove" and "Marina Garden Estates" are the development names used on City and developer material for this pocket — most of the strata stock here was built as one master-planned waterfront community between a marina and a golf course. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/pilothouse-in-delta/
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: there is exactly one apartment-condo building pattern here — Pilothouse on Admiral Way in Hampton Cove, plus the older Shorewalk building on River Road at the sub-area's west edge. Everything else strata in Neilsen Grove is townhouses (Section 2). A City memorandum on the Marina Garden Estates application also describes a multi-family apartment building within that application, with no street address in the City source. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/84874/
- Transit: TransLink route 618, the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange, runs through the area via Ferry Road to Commodore Drive and returns via Admiral Way, Admiral Boulevard, Westminster Avenue, Crescent Drive and 57 Street; route 601 runs from South Delta to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line), stopping at Ladner Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Schools: the SD37 School Locator result for the sample address 5500 Admiral Boulevard is Neilson Grove Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12) at 4615 51 St.; catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Ladner Leisure Centre, 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent, Ladner Pioneer Library, 4683 51st Street, and Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd (beside Ladner Exchange), are the civic anchors. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. At Pilothouse, the Form B is also where Hampton Club amenity access and any marina-related charges show up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. No Neilsen Grove building is verified as 55+ — Pilothouse's downsizer marketing is not a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **New-home warranty is still running on the newest stock.** Strata records for Fairwinds note the structural warranty runs to 2027 under BC's 2-5-10 new-home warranty — the same check applies to Pilothouse and the 2023–24 Westhampton homes: ask which warranty years remain on the specific building. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **Delta zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600.** Zoning is parcel-by-parcel under Bylaw No. 7600; the Hampton Cove lands were built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning (a Westhampton listing records zoning CD430 for that complex), so each complex's zone and rules are read off its own CD zone and the City zoning maps. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5311-admiral-way/9 and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — no lettered schedules, no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — relevant to Neilsen Grove's detached lots, not to the Hampton Cove strata lands. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale condos and townhouses here carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Pilothouse a 55+ building?** Not verified as one. Project reporting says Polygon designed it with downsizers in mind, but no source checked states a registered 55+ bylaw — and only a 55+ bylaw would be lawful in any case (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2). If age restriction matters to you, read the bylaws in the Form B package, not the marketing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/pilothouse-at-hampton-cove-5535-admiral-way-delta-87-one-two-and-three-bedroom-homes-by-polygon-pilothouse-ltd and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What do Pilothouse fees have to cover that a normal condo's don't?** A waterfront building plus shared access to the Hampton Club — listings describe a 12,000 sq ft clubhouse with outdoor pool, hot tub, fitness centre, lounge and guest suites serving the Hampton Cove community. What your unit's fee actually includes is stated in the strata budget attached to the Form B; do not assume club access is free or mandatory without reading it. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3100590-5535-admiral-way-129-delta-bc-v4k-0e3/brochure
- **Q: Can I keep a boat here?** The community sits between a marina and a golf course, and listings name Captain's Cove Marina among the nearby amenities. Whether any moorage right attaches to a specific strata lot: UNCONFIRMED — no source checked ties a slip to a Pilothouse or Shorewalk unit. Treat marina access as a separate arrangement unless the Form B or title says otherwise. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117031-129-5535-admiral-way-delta-british-columbia-v4k0e3-389000/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Pilothouse?** A listing states "Pets ok (2)" and the Hampton Cove townhouse listings also describe pet-friendly complexes. Pet bylaws are strata-specific — confirm number and any size limits in the registered bylaws. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3100590-5535-admiral-way-129-delta-bc-v4k-0e3/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** For long-term rentals, BC law voids strata rental-restriction bylaws as of Nov 24, 2022 — Fairwinds' strata records already record rentals allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **Q: Shorewalk is from 1991 — what envelope work has been done?** A current listing says the building has been rainscreened and received a new roof in 2025. Verify both in the depreciation report, engineering documents and minutes, and check the CRF balance against the next items on the 30-year list. https://www.boldcalgaryrealestate.ca/listing-crea/r3073407-321-4955-river-road-delta-british-columbia-v4k4v9/brochure and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: What insurance exposure comes with living on the river?** The Form B must include a summary of the strata's insurance coverage; in a waterfront low-rise, the water-damage deductible in that summary is the number to read, because deductibles are routinely charged back to the unit a leak started in. Flood protection for the area is the City's dike and drainage system (Section 4, flood question below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is Neilsen Grove behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and properties within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which is a live issue on harbour-side lots. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Which school is in the area, and how is it spelled?** Neilson Grove Elementary — spelled "Neilson" — at 5500 Admiral Boulevard, while the area is "Neilsen Grove". The locator result for the school's address is Neilson Grove Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); check any other address in the locator. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 618 loops from Ladner Exchange through Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard; from the Exchange, route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hampton Cove was planned as roughly 670 homes, mostly townhouses — Pilothouse is the exception.** Project reporting on Beaufort Landing records Hampton Cove at some 670 residences, primarily townhomes, in five phases between a golf course and a marina, and describes Pilothouse as the community's only apartment building. Condo supply in Neilsen Grove is one building deep. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/beaufort-landing-5551-admiral-way-ladner-124-three-and-four-bedroom-townhomes-by-polygon-beaufort-landing-ltd and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/pilothouse-at-hampton-cove-5535-admiral-way-delta-87-one-two-and-three-bedroom-homes-by-polygon-pilothouse-ltd
- **The City files describe more apartment product inside Marina Garden Estates.** A City memorandum describes a multi-family apartment building within the Marina Garden Estates application, with no street address in the City source — a current presale project in or next to Neilsen Grove is otherwise UNCONFIRMED. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/84874/
- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion and major construction expected to begin in 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **The warranty clock is a live value item here.** With Fairwinds' structural warranty recorded as running to 2027 and Westhampton homes built in 2023–2024, two otherwise similar Hampton Cove purchases can carry very different remaining 2-5-10 warranty coverage — check the building's warranty dates before comparing prices. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **The spelling split trips up searches and contracts.** The area is "Neilsen Grove" and the school is "Neilson Grove Elementary" — both spellings are in official use, for different things, and a search run under only one spelling will miss listings filed under the other. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **There is no Neilsen Grove market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Ladner community section and no Neilsen Grove row — its only Delta section is North Delta — so any "Neilsen Grove median condo price" quoted from that source does not exist (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/neilsen-grove-guide.md).
