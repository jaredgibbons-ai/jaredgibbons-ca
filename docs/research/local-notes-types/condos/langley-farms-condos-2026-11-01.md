# Langley Farms — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This is a topic file, not a neighbourhood: "Langley farms" covers farm land, working farms and agricultural land for sale across Langley, principally the Township of Langley, where the farm belt sits. Langley City is urban and holds no farm belt. The board's rural Langley sub-areas in the SnapStats data are Salmon River, Campbell Valley, County Line Glen Valley and Otter District. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md)
- The scale of the farm belt is the boundary fact: the Township of Langley states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve. Farm parcels are spread across that reserve — there is no farm "condo district," no farm-belt street with strata buildings, and no single MLS sub-area for this topic. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Where Langley condo stock actually concentrates — stated here as labelled Langley-wide context only, not as farm-area stock: the urban communities, above all Willoughby (369 attached listings in September 2026, the deepest in Langley) and Langley City's downtown core. Those buildings are documented in their own area files, not this one. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/willoughby-heights.md).
- Langley-wide market context (all property, Township and City combined): detached inventory 449 with 64 sales, and condos & townhomes inventory 699 with 128 sales in September 2026. SnapStats publishes no farm category, so there are no farm-sale statistics in that report. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md).

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero apartment-condo stock on Langley farmland: no named apartment-condo building on a Langley farm parcel, or anywhere in the ALR farm belt, was verified in any source checked for this file. Condos are not a farm-area housing form in Langley.**

- The structural reason is stated by the Agricultural Land Commission itself: a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, "Strata plans cause subdivision"), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under s. 3 of the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1), as summarized in the bulletin). An approving officer under the Land Title Act, the Local Government Act or the Strata Property Act is likewise prohibited from approving a subdivision of ALR land unless so permitted (ALCA s. 18(5), per the bulletin). Apartment condos on farmland would need that ALC permission first — no source checked shows a Langley farm parcel that has it for a condo building. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The area research file reaches the same conclusion from the tenure side: "Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Langley pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source)." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md)
- What a Langley farm purchase actually contains, per the ALC housing rules: generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. The housing on a farm is a house (and, where it qualifies, one additional residence) — not strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Labelled Langley-wide condo context (official-source figures only, per this file's brief): Langley-wide, SnapStats recorded 699 condos & townhomes listings and 128 sales in September 2026, and 1,636 attached sales over the twelve months to September 2026 across all Langley sub-areas — urban stock, concentrated in Willoughby and Langley City, not farm land. No building in that urban stock is documented in this file; see the Willoughby Heights condos file for named, verified urban buildings. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md).

### Townhomes — not condos, listed here so they are not confused

- No townhome complex on Langley farmland was verified either, and for the same ALC reason: a townhome strata plan is still a strata plan, and strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR. Townhome complexes exist in Langley's urban communities; none is documented here because none sits in the farm topic's scope. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic, the "rules that affect condos" are mainly the rules that keep condos off farmland, followed by the standard strata rules that would govern any strata lot a buyer meets elsewhere in Langley.

- **ALR strata-subdivision rule (the governing fact for this file):** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR; subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application under ALCA s. 25 or permitted under the ALR General Regulation, and a Registrar of Titles must not accept a plan that would cause a subdivision of agricultural land unless so permitted (ALCA s. 19, per the bulletin). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR principal-residence cap:** a principal residence on ALR land built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **One additional residence on ALR land:** the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. This additional residence is a house on the same title, not a strata unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Form B (applies to any strata lot, none verified on farmland):** an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days, for a maximum of $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a "farm condo" is ever marketed in Langley, the Form B and the strata plan number are the first proof to demand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata-wide):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, and since November 1, 2023 stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals and age (strata-wide):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44) — any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" is void since that date — and the only valid age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township layer:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township, including its rural zones; the Township states lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning, and its SSMUH bylaw (Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) sets the small-lot tiers at 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², with lots over 4,050 m² ineligible for those tiers and no 6-unit tier. Farm parcels are typically well over 4,050 m², which is why the suite/accessory route, not SSMUH density, is the farm-land rule. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Langley City layer (urban context only):** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 defines a secondary suite by the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the home — a City rule for City lots, stated here only because farm-topic readers sometimes cross-shop City property; it does not apply to Township farm land. (Bylaw as named in this file's brief; the suite definition was not re-read from the bylaw text for this file — UNCONFIRMED beyond the brief.)
- **Buyer-layer taxes:** BC Property Transfer Tax runs 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above; the First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). GST at 5% applies to new housing, not resales; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, with Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. Whether any of these programs applies to a particular farm purchase (which can include non-residential land and farm business assets) is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no source checked rules on farm-parcel eligibility. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Langley farm?** No verified product exists. No apartment-condo building on a Langley farm parcel was found in any source checked, and the ALC's own bulletin explains why: a strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR, which needs Commission permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Could a developer stratify a farmhouse or barn conversion in the ALR?** Only through the same gate: the bulletin states subdivision of agricultural land — including by strata plan — is not permitted unless the Commission permits it after an application or the ALR General Regulation permits it. An outcome for a specific conversion proposal is UNCONFIRMED; it is an application, not a right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What is the closest thing to multi-unit living on a farm parcel?** The ALC's own housing rules: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (with a secondary suite permitted only inside it), plus one additional residence where it qualifies — 90 m² or less on parcels up to 40 ha, 186 m² or less above 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws. All on one title, none of it strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: A listing calls an acreage property a "strata." Should I believe it?** Check the title form, not the marketing word. The farm research file records that no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source and farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold. If a strata plan number exists, a Form B can be requested and must be provided within 7 days; if the seller cannot produce a strata plan, treat the "strata" wording as UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 4 units on a farm lot?** Not on a typical farm parcel: the Township states lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning — the 3- and 4-unit SSMUH tiers are for small urban lots (up to 280 m² and under 4,050 m² respectively). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: If I buy a farm and later want to sell suites separately, can I stratify then?** That is exactly the act Bulletin 09, s. 6 covers: depositing a strata plan subdivides the land, and on ALR land that requires ALC permission. Plan on the answer being an application with no published approval rate in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED for any individual parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What tax programs matter if I cross-shop a new urban condo instead?** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (100% of GST to $1 million, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031, Bill C-4) and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption from PTT (full to $1,100,000) are the two big ones; both are new-home programs, and neither was verified in this file for a farm purchase. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What actually drives a farm parcel's value, if not condo potential?** Farm classification and its income tests are one documented driver: BC Assessment requires minimum gross farm income of $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 under 0.8 ha. Losing classification changes the tax picture in a way no condo comparison captures. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Where do I check whether a parcel is even in the ALR?** On the ALC maps, by PID or civic address; the Township's 235 km² ALR figure is Township-wide and says nothing about an individual parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **Q: Are there rental or age rules on a farm house?** Strata rental and age bylaws do not apply to a freehold farm parcel at all — there is no strata corporation. The void-since-2022 rental rule and the 55+ rule in Section 3 matter only if the property is a strata lot, which no verified Langley farm property in this file is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **235 km² is the Township's own ALR figure** — most of Langley's land base is reserve land, so the default assumption for any Langley parcel outside the urban containment areas should be "check the ALC map first," for housing form as much as for farm use. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **The fill rule surprises farmhouse builders:** the ALC's 500 m² principal-residence permission carries a second cap most buyers miss — no more than 1,000 m² of fill may be placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **A suite on ALR land can only live inside the principal residence** under the no-application route; a detached second home is the separate "additional residence" permission with its own 90 m² / 186 m² caps. Marketing that describes a detached suite as part of the principal-residence permission is mixing two different ALC rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Water licensing is a farm diligence item with a paper trail:** under the Water Sustainability Act, domestic groundwater use does not need a licence but non-domestic use, including irrigation, does; well records are searched in GWELLS and well reports must be filed within 90 days of construction, alteration or decommissioning. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md)
- **The farm pages' own feed proves the point:** the live langley-farms page carries an ALR property search block and a feed of Agri-Business listings across rural sub-areas including Salmon River, County Line Glen Valley, Campbell Valley and Otter District — farm listings, not strata buildings. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-farms.html (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/langley-farms.md)
