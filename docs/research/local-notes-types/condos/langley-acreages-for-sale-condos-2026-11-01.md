## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On this page the Form B matters mainly as a negative check: an acreage house is ordinarily not strata at all, and if a listing claims strata tenure, the Form B and the strata plan are the documents that prove it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF — these duties attach to strata corporations, not to a freehold acreage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A non-strata acreage has no strata rental bylaw at all; rental use there is governed by zoning and, in the ALR, by the ALR residence rules below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building on this page is strata apartment stock, so no 55+ bylaw is recorded for this page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning and plans:** rural Langley acreages sit under the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (the feed's cards cite RU-2 and RU-3); the Township's current Official Community Plan framework is OCP Bylaw No. 6200, adopted December 1, 2025. Township taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Township SSMUH does not rescue a large rural lot:** under Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024), eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² — and there is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020. Most acreages fail on size and servicing alone. A Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under Zoning Bylaw s. 102, as amended. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **ALR housing frame (the frame that governs a large share of Langley acreage):** land in the ALR may generally have one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; and one additional residence may be permitted at up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Building more, or larger, requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Farm class (BC Assessment):** $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered by October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is sold on this page. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Are there any condos on the Langley acreages page?** No — zero. - **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage and sell the homes as condos?** No. ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring ALC permission (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — and the ALR housing frame itself allows one principal residence of 500 m² or less, a suite only inside it, and one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How many homes can I build on an ALR acreage in Langley?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), with a secondary suite only inside that residence, plus one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval and local bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on a Langley acreage?** No for most acreages: Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m², with no 6-unit tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: What farm income does a Langley acreage need for farm class?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 in farm-gate sales for 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that size, and $10,000 under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. Whether any specific parcel holds farm class is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any feed property in this file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)

- **SSMUH's size gate excludes almost every acreage by arithmetic.** The Township's eligible-lot ceiling is 4,050 m² — about one acre — so even the smallest property in this page's own 1–10 acre definition sits at or past the gate before the Urban Containment Boundary and municipal water-and-sewer tests are reached. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Farm class is an October 31 habit, not a purchase-day fact.** The BC Assessment thresholds turn on the operation's actual farm-gate sales and area, and applications must be delivered by October 31 — a buyer who assumes the seller's farm class carries over without re-qualifying can lose it in the first cycle. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **The Township's tax penalty is two-step, not one.** 5% lands at midnight on the July 2, 2026 due date and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026 — a completion straddling either date changes the adjustment math on a Township acreage. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Shops, not suites, are this feed's second-unit story.** The first page's dominant product was houses with shops — a 61' × 35' shop in Salmon River, a 3,600 sq ft heated shop with a 9-stall barn in Campbell Valley, twin shops in Otter District — so permit checks on outbuildings matter more here than any strata document. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-acreages-for-sale.md)
