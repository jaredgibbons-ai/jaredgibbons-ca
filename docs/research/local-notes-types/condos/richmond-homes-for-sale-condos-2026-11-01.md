## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory "rentals not allowed" line in Section 2 (including Quilchena Green's, carried in the Terra Nova note) is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Separate mechanism, not affected by the 2022 change: Omega's LEMR units are restricted by a housing covenant, not a strata bylaw — the covenant still binds. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500 and the 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000, frame land use city-wide; the Steveston Area Plan is Schedule 2.4 of the OCP. Single-detached districts sit in RS1/RS2 zones (Section 8.1 of Bylaw 8500), a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (suite rules in Section 5.4: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and the City's Bill 44 minimums are 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Flood construction levels and dike setbacks sit in Bylaw No. 8204. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/richmond-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Alexandra District Energy Utility (ADEU) — a carrying cost in the Alexandra pocket:** the West Cambie buildings in Section 2 (Alexandra Court, Omega and their neighbours) are served by the City's district energy utility for geothermal heating/cooling, and the utility charge is separate from the strata fee — pull it from the strata documents and price it into carrying costs rather than assuming standard utilities. It applies to the Alexandra district only; the River Drive buildings' geothermal systems run through their stratas, not the City utility. (Sub-area file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-cambie-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Richmond property taxes:** for 2026, City of Richmond property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/richmond-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $800,000, fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract purchase arises in Richmond, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no pad-rent park is part of the verified roster above; where such parks are discussed in this program, MHPTA pad-rent increases are limited to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027.
