## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59); the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This is the central document at both ends of North Delta's age range — Delta Cedars (1972/1973) and Chancellor Place (1989/1990) at one end, the 2017–2023 Scott Road stock at the other. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Delta Cedars, Kennedy Gate and the Sunshine Woods townhome complex are old bylaw text, not current law — each is flagged void in its entry above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Applied here: Chancellor Place's older-listing "19+" line is not a permitted threshold; Delta Cedars' and Kennedy Gate's directory "age restrictions" lines state no age at all — confirm on the Form B whether any registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw, because no other threshold is enforceable and an unstated one cannot be assumed to be 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans — two flags:** (1) OCP: Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 — NO lettered area-plan schedules and NO Tsawwassen Area Plan in it (that plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950); Development Permit Areas are in Appendix B (ND12, ND3, ND1 for North Delta). (2) ZONING BYLAW NUMBER CONTESTED: City pages cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal document title says "8400, 2025" — 8400 is the OCP's number, and this file flags the collision and asserts neither zoning citation; read the parcel's zone sheet from the City's current zoning pages. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies — relevant to North Delta's house lots, not to its strata buildings. The batch-12 research file itself left Delta's zoning/suite position UNVERIFIED in its pass and carried no other municipality's figures across; the position above is from the City sources cited in this series' sub-area files. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/north-delta-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Delta property taxes:** the City's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every roster building except a developer sale at Village at Sunshine Hills is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any Village at Sunshine Hills purchase while its completion remains UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
