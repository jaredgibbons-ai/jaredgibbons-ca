## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" (for example Park Regent Estates') is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Directly relevant to Delta Green, the one Section 2 building verified as 55+ in listing sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, adopted July 8, 2024, no area-plan schedules (the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), agriculture policies at s. 2.6. City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (RS1–RS3 subdivision minimum 4,000 m²; RS4 610 m²; RS5 550/460 m²; RS6 390 m²; RS7 335 m²; RD1 836 m²; RD4 504 m², parcel by parcel; no RD5 zone exists), while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) and the City's provincially required updates allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **PTT in this band:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion — plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Neither exemption reaches this band: the First-Time Home Buyers' Program full exemption caps at $835,000 and the Newly Built Home Exemption is gone at $1,150,000, so buyers here budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked examples (inputs stated):** (1) A $2,000,000 purchase, no exemption: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,800,000 = $36,000, total $38,000. (2) A $3,500,000 purchase, all residential, no exemption: $38,000 on the first $2,000,000 (as above), plus 3% × $1,500,000 = $45,000, plus the further 2% × $500,000 over $3,000,000 = $10,000, total $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Home owner grant at this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the frame for any developer sale in Ladner, which is in any case excluded from the resale band count. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood, streamside and ALR:** flood construction level and insurance questions are address-specific for any low-lying listing in this band; land within 30 m of a slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA; ALR status is parcel-specific on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

- **Q: How much property transfer tax applies over $1.8M?** The Province's general tiers — 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000 — plus a further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000. On the worked inputs in Section 3: $38,000 at $2,000,000 and $93,000 at $3,500,000 all-residential, before any exemption (none reaches this band). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the first-time or new-home exemption help at $1.8M?** No — the first-time program caps out at $835,000 and the newly built exemption is eliminated at $1,150,000, both far below this band's floor. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant?** It depends on assessed value, not list price: full regular grant at or below $2,075,000 assessed, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over that, and $0 over $2,189,000 — many homes in this band straddle that line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: Can I rent out a Ladner condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a directory records (Park Regent Estates carries a "rentals allowed with restrictions" directory line); at a 55+ building the tenant must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at Delta Green?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases. Confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What should I check on a riverfront or acreage home in this band?** Three parcel-level checks: flood and streamside position (behind the City's dike system; within 30 m of a slough or ditch puts the lot in the Streamside DPA), foreshore tenure for any dock or moorage (provincial Crown land, by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC), and the parcel's ALR status on the ALC's maps. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
