## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Moody on the Hill (2024) the Form B is also where the remaining 2-5-10 warranty position and the strata's first depreciation-report cycle are confirmed. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for Moody on the Hill reflects old bylaw text, not current law — no such line was published for it in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Moody on the Hill is not published as age-restricted in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Heritage Conservation Area — the defining overlay for this sub-area:** the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area is currently the only HCA in New Westminster; it places a layer of heritage protection over ALL properties in the area regardless of construction age, classed Protected or Non-protected. Building Permit applications for work such as new buildings, demolition, or changes affecting the front, sides or visible roofline of Protected properties, and subdivision applications, require a Heritage Alteration Permit (HAP) reviewed for design-guideline compliance, and Protected properties also need an HAP for exterior changes that do not require a Building Permit. Protected properties are also under the Heritage Property Maintenance Standards Bylaw; standard in-kind maintenance does not require an HAP; Protected-property incentives include additional density, special BC Building Code provisions, density transfer, relaxed laneway/carriage house guidelines and no heritage permit fees. How the HCA applies to a strata building's common-property exterior work is a per-property City question — confirm for the specific building before pricing exterior work. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area, including the Queen's Park Heritage Conservation Area (Schedule B, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 7926, 2017); zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — houses under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series; RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m., FSR 0.5) overlaid by the HCA guidelines and HAP rules. New Westminster's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B — no RT-A/RT-B districts exist, and they must never be cited for this city. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **Flood / hazards:** the governing overlay here is heritage, not floodplain — a floodplain zone for this area is UNVERIFIED in the sources read. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility plus City water/sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Moody on the Hill was built in 2024, so whether GST is payable turns on the specific unit's sale history (a first sale from the developer vs a resale) — UNCONFIRMED per unit; a current listing for the building notes "GST is already paid" for its penthouse unit, which is a unit-level statement, not a building-level one. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://mapliv.com/ca/address/601-6th-street-new-westminster-bc-v3l-3c1
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — potentially relevant to a first-sale unit at a 2024 building, not to resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Moody on the Hill traded by assignment of contract while it was completing (older listings are assignment listings) — an assignment is governed by the original developer contract's assignment terms, which are UNCONFIRMED until that contract is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: What does the Heritage Conservation Area mean for a condo purchase here?** Every property in the area is classed Protected or Non-protected regardless of age; specified exterior work, new buildings, demolition and subdivision need a Heritage Alteration Permit, and Protected properties carry minimum maintenance standards plus incentives (additional density, density transfer, relaxed laneway/carriage guidelines, no permit fees). ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The apostrophe is a filing system.** "Queens Park" (board, no apostrophe) vs "Queen's Park" (City, with apostrophe) is not pedantry — documents, permits and the HCA itself are filed under the City spelling, listings under the board spelling, and searching only one misses the other half of the record. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **Three "Queens" buildings belong to other sub-areas.** Queen's Park Estate is a Queensborough townhouse project (240 Jardine Street), and Queens Terrace and Amara Terrace are Uptown NW buildings on Queens Avenue — a buyer searching "Queens" names assembles a stock list for three different sub-areas and none of them is this one. Sources: Section 2 exclusion entries.
- **The rezoning for the area's one new building is on the public record.** Council adopted Zoning Amendment (228–232 Sixth Street) Bylaw No. 7996, 2018 for the old La Rustica restaurant site — the approval history behind Moody on the Hill, inside a heritage conservation area where a new mid-rise is the exception that proves the HCA rule. https://www.patrickjohnstone.ca/2018/08
- **Protected status can add value, not just process.** Protected-property incentives in the HCA include additional density, density transfer, relaxed laneway/carriage house guidelines and no heritage permit fees — the same classification that triggers Heritage Alteration Permits also carries development value a Non-protected property does not. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **A zero-sale month says nothing about values here.** September 2026 recorded no sales in either segment for this sub-area; the file's pricing anchor is the New Westminster community evidence, not a sub-area figure that does not exist. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
- **The park is inside the neighbourhood, not beside it.** Queen's Park sits in the eastern portion of the neighbourhood itself, with the Arena and Sportsplex in it — "steps to Queen's Park" listing copy for the residential streets around it describes the sub-area's own centre, not an amenity in the next area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/queens-park.md)
