## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On this page the Form B is a tenure test: a genuine strata unit produces one; an ALR farm parcel does not, because it is not strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF — strata-corporation duties that do not attach to a fee-simple farm. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Boarding horses for paying clients is not a strata-rental question at all — it is a zoning, ALR and business-licence question for the specific parcel, UNVERIFIED for any parcel in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building on this page is strata apartment stock, so no 55+ bylaw is recorded for this page. Langley City's four verified 55+ strata are in the langleycity file and are never re-counted in a Township file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (Langley City file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langleycity-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township zoning and plans:** equestrian and farm properties sit under the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and the Township's rural community plans (Campbell Valley, Salmon River, Otter District, Aldergrove); the Township's current Official Community Plan framework is OCP Bylaw No. 6200, adopted December 1, 2025. Township taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.md)
- **Township SSMUH:** under Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024), eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² — and there is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020; a Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under Zoning Bylaw s. 102, as amended. Working equestrian acreages fail the SSMUH gates on size and servicing. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.md)
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and one additional residence at up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.md)

- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is sold on this page. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
