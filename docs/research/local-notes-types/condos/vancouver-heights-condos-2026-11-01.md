## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this sub-area's older low-rises (The Sienna and Evergreen Place are 1989–1998 stock) the depreciation report and CRF balance on the Form B are the first-order documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" lines the building directory records for The Sienna, Heritage Green and Evergreen Place are **VOID** as long-term rental restrictions (short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted building in this file is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current zoning bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — never the former 1965 bylaw, No. 4742. The **Burnaby 2050 OCP** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older neighbourhood community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Hastings corridor through the Heights is planned as the **Heights Urban Village**. The R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date. This is Burnaby's calendar — and there is **no Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here**, because this is not the City of Vancouver. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the four counted buildings are resale stock; the Siena presale carries GST. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $686,000 (the Burnaby community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Vancouver Heights median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,720 (2% × $486,000) = **$11,720 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (relevant to the Siena presale). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** presale product exists in this sub-area (Siena, 4451 Hastings St). REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. The 4269 Hastings Street project is at second reading only and is not presale stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Vancouver Heights in Vancouver?** No — it is a City of Burnaby neighbourhood in North Burnaby, east of Boundary Road, on and below the Capitol Hill rise. The City of Vancouver (Hastings-Sunrise) is across Boundary Road to the west. Burnaby taxes, Burnaby bylaws, School District 41 and Burnaby market statistics apply — and there is no Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here. (Research file, Section 1.)
- **Q: Where are the condos, and which buildings are verified by name?** On and just off Hastings Street: four counted buildings — The Sienna (3939 Hastings St, 1998, 22 units), The Carleton (4223 Hastings St, 2020, 22 units), Heritage Green (3978 Albert St, 1994, 28 units) and Evergreen Place (3861 Albert St, 1989). Three more named buildings — The Mondeo (3811 Hastings St), Verdi (3971 Hastings St) and Carleton Terrace (4160 Albert St) — appear in listings only and are carried UNVERIFIED, not counted. The sub-area's attached market is thin: 22 active condos & townhomes listings in September 2026. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: How did Vancouver Heights sell in September 2026?** Attached: 22 active listings, 1 sold, 5% sales ratio; detached: 13 listings, 5 sold, 38%. Community medians (Burnaby, the only level published): attached $686,000, detached $1,825,000. SnapStats publishes no Vancouver Heights median — sub-area medians are UNVERIFIED. SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite at Seton Villa?** No. Seton Villa (3755 McGill St) is a 19-storey non-profit retirement centre operated by Action Line Housing Society, registered in BC's Assisted Living Registry — independent/supportive suites and assisted living that you rent into or are placed into. There is no strata ownership to purchase, and it is the sub-area's only verified seniors building; there is no verified 55+ ownership stock here. https://www.setonvilla.com/about-us (55+ file, Section 2.)
- **Q: Which secondary school serves the Heights — Burnaby North?** Not on the locator evidence. SD41 locator runs for two in-area Pandora Street addresses (4375 and 4715 Pandora St) both returned **Alpha Secondary**, with Rosser Elementary for the first and Confederation Park Elementary for the second. Burnaby North Secondary (751 Hammarskjold Dr) is located in North Burnaby, but location is not catchment. Run the exact address before writing an offer. (SD41 School Locator runs recorded in the research file, 2026-11-01.)
- **Q: What does the R1 zoning mean for a Heights house lot?** Multiplex potential: under Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district, a lot can hold 3 units (lots up to 280 m²), 4 units (larger lots) or 6 units (lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop), and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified and sold individually. Two otherwise similar houses can carry very different builder value depending on lot area and the 400 m transit test. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Is a new community centre coming to Confederation Park?** Not on any current schedule a buyer can rely on: the City's planned 3-level Confederation Park Community Centre — beside Eileen Dailly Pool and the McGill Branch library, retaining both and replacing the 1971 seniors centre — **has been deferred by the City**. The current pool, library and seniors centre are the offer, not a future one. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/confederation-park-community-centre
- **Q: What transit serves a Hastings or Albert Street condo?** RapidBus, not SkyTrain — there is no station in the sub-area. The R5 runs on Hastings at least every 10 minutes at peak (15 off-peak) between Burrard Station and SFU, with in-area stops at Hythe, Holdom and Kensington; since September 7, 2026 the R2 also runs along Hastings and down Willingdon Avenue to Metrotown, every 6–7 minutes at peak. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $686,000 fair market value (the September 2026 Burnaby community attached median, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $11,720. A first-time buyer at that value is inside the First-Time Home Buyers' Program range (full exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What strata diligence matters most in these older low-rises?** The Form B package: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024, no deferral), the CRF balance (minimum 10% operating-fund contribution since November 1, 2023), approved special levies still payable and the insurance summary. The counted stock runs 1989–2020, so the 1989–1998 buildings (Evergreen Place, Heritage Green, The Sienna) are the ones where envelope, roof and levy history decide the purchase — and ignore any "rentals not allowed" wording in a directory line: long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
