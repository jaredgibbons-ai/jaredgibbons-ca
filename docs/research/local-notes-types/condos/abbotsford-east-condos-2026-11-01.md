# Abbotsford East — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford East" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; it is not a single City neighbourhood name — the City plans the east side through named neighbourhoods and plan areas including McMillan, Old Clayburn, East Townline, Whatcom and McKee. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Rough extent from City plan and park locations: Highway 1 to the south, the Sumas Mountain slopes and McKee Road area to the east and northeast, Old Yale Road and Old Clayburn Road through the middle, and the McMillan/Clearbrook side to the west. The mountainside is planned separately through the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), Schedule E of the Official Community Plan. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: the east side is overwhelmingly detached housing — it carried 165 detached listings vs 69 attached listings in September 2026. The apartment condos cluster at the southwest corner of the sub-area, not in the hillside neighbourhoods: (1) the Whatcom Road / Old Yale Road node at Highway 1 (Waterleaf), and (2) the Gladys Avenue / Sumas Way / South Fraser Way edge (Sage). No condo building was verified in McKee, Auguston, Sandy Hill, Clayburn or Eagle Mountain. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and the SnapStats figures in the research file (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-east-guide.md).
- Highway 1 access is at Whatcom Road; BC Transit routes serving the east side include 2 (Highstreet/McMillan), 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee), 15 (Auguston), 16 (McMillan) and the 66 Fraser Valley Express on Highway 1. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=2

The five-building, wood-frame form is consistent; the total count and the project-wide completion date are not settled by the sources checked. https://lumon.com/ca/professionals/insights/pro-blog/sage-abbotsford/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/sage-by-diverse-presale-condos/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase (Waterleaf is the area's resale condo stock) the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote; the report projects 30 years of repair/replacement costs and includes at least three CRF funding models. Stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44, Building and Strata Statutes Amendment Act, 2022), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children of a qualifying resident. No 55+ condo building was verified in Abbotsford East — whether Waterleaf or Sage carries any age bylaw is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs (urban residential zones in the research file include RS3, minimum interior lot 540 m², and RS5, 420 m²); the city-wide plan is the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated; new OCP adopted December 2025 — Section 5), and the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) plans the mountainside as ground-oriented neighbourhoods, which is why no tower or mid-rise condo form is planned up the hill. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/ and https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **SSMUH:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing and Transit-Oriented Areas legislation (brief names this as Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — number/date UNCONFIRMED on the City pages opened; June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed). Zoning Bylaw s. 140.3.8 also permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, subject to criteria in City reports (minimum 400 m² lot, 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, not on a cul-de-sac bulb). https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on new condos (Sage):** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; sources checked give Royal Assent as March 12, 2026 (brief said March 13). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists underneath. https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate and https://raincityproperties.com/journal/gst-new-home-rebate-bc-first-time-buyer-2026
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption (the Sage case): full exemption to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Presales and assignments (REDMA):** Sage is sold under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act — s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. Whether a Sage contract can be assigned, and at what fee, is a contract/disclosure term: UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any condo stock in the hillside neighbourhoods (McKee, Auguston, Sandy Hill)?** None was verified. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan frames the mountainside as ground-oriented housing, and every verified apartment-condo building in the sub-area sits at the southwest corner — Waterleaf at Whatcom/Old Yale and Sage at Gladys/Sumas Way. Buyers who want a condo and a Bateman/Yale school address are effectively choosing between those two nodes. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Q: Sage is wood-frame. Does that matter for insurance and fees?** Construction type is one of the inputs strata insurers price on, and it is confirmed for Sage (6-storey wood-frame) and described for Waterleaf (wood-frame) — but no source publishes either building's strata insurance deductible or fee schedule, so both are UNCONFIRMED until the Form B (resale) or disclosure statement (Sage) is read. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/sage-abbotsford/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Sage or Waterleaf unit?** For a long-term tenancy, a strata cannot stop you — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw, and Sage's own bylaw package is UNCONFIRMED until registration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What will my strata fee cover at Sage?** Listings state secure underground parking and storage are included in the homes, and the project markets a central-courtyard amenity ("The Greenhouse") — but the fee amount and coverage list live in the disclosure statement's proposed budget and are UNCONFIRMED from public marketing. Do not budget off a presale estimate without reading it. https://www.royallepage.ca/en/investorsedge/property/british-columbia/abbotsford/312-34375-gladys-avenue/27056607/mlsr3077505/
- **Q: How do I check Waterleaf for coming special levies?** Through the depreciation report attached to the Form B: every strata with 5+ lots must now have one on a five-year cycle, with a 30-year projection and three CRF funding models; the CRF must also receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Waterleaf or Sage?** UNCONFIRMED — no public source checked states a pet bylaw for either building. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws (Waterleaf) or disclosure statement (Sage) are the only reliable answer.
- **Q: I'm a first-time buyer at Sage. What stacks?** Potentially three things: the FTHB GST rebate (up to $50,000 back on a new home to $1 million, agreements on/after March 20, 2025), the BC Newly Built Home PTT exemption (full to $1,100,000), and FHSA/HBP savings room ($40,000 / $60,000). Each has its own eligibility test — confirm all three before removing subjects. https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Q: What is the commute/transit reality from the east-side condos?** Both nodes sit by Highway 1 (Whatcom Road interchange for Waterleaf; Sumas Way/Highway 11 for Sage), and the 66 Fraser Valley Express runs the highway toward Langley/Lougheed Station and Chilliwack — but there is no rail transit in Abbotsford, and local east-side routes (2, 7, 9, 15, 16) are bus routes with suburban frequencies. Published official drive times: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=66
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend from Waterleaf or Sage?** Catchments are address-based. Locator runs in the research file for east-side addresses: 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive returns Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary, and 3836 Old Clayburn Road returns Sandy Hill Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary. Neither test address is a condo address — run the building's own address in the SD34 School Locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-east-guide.md)
- **Q: Is flood risk a condo issue on the east side?** The low land at the south and southeast edge, toward Sumas Prairie, is floodplain by the Sumas River, and City flood notices name properties south of the Sumas River and along Whatcom Road in evacuation order/alert areas. Both condo nodes sit near that edge — check the specific parcel in the City's WebMap rather than assuming a building is in or out. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025.** The Abbotsforward 2050 update (public hearing November 25, 2025) emphasizes more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enables 37,185 units over 20 years per the City's 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report — the east side's next condo supply, if any, will be designated under this plan, not the 2016 OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Sage is a phased project, and the phases do not finish together.** Listings in 2026 show completed/occupied buildings at 34395 Gladys Avenue while the phase now selling (Phase 3) carries an estimated completion of Spring 2027 — early residents live beside later-phase construction. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/34395-Gladys-Ave-205-Abbotsford-BC-V2S-2H5/464627451_zpid/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/sage-by-diverse-presale-condos/
- **The McKee mountainside is being planned for ground-oriented growth, not condos:** the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) designates Urban 2 – Ground Oriented forms for the slope neighbourhoods, and its open-space network protects streamside setbacks, critical habitat and slopes over 40% — hillside buyers should expect townhouses and detached homes, with trail and geotechnical conditions, rather than apartment product. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Supportive housing is being added on the Gladys Avenue corridor:** the City reported BC Housing lease agreements for 61 supportive units on Riverside Road under construction and another 48 units on Gladys Avenue advancing through approvals (October 2026) — context Sage buyers will see on their own street. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Clayburn Village, at the west foot of Sumas Mountain, is a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP** — it does not contain condos, but it shapes what can be built or altered on the east side's oldest streets, and it is the reason "new condo near Clayburn" listings should be read carefully: the village itself is protected, low-density and detached. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **The City's Housing Accelerator Fund work is complete:** up to $25.6 million in federal HAF funding, all initiatives reported complete in October 2026, with multi-family permitting above program expectations — supply pressure that shows up first in nodes like Gladys Avenue, not on the hillside. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
