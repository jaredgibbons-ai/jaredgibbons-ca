# Mission — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This file is city-wide: Mission's board geography has several sub-areas, but the urban "Mission" sub-area carries nearly all of the attached market — 104 of the city's 122 active attached listings in September 2026 (SnapStats FVR). City-wide that month: attached median $430,000 and 61 days on market. SnapStats publishes no building-level figures. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the downtown/central core — 1st and 2nd Avenues, Horne Street, 7th Avenue and 14th Avenue (Montgomery, Mission Manor, Stave Lake Landing, Preston), the Cedar Street corridor in Cedar Valley (Silver & Cedar), and the Briskham Street pocket west of downtown (Briskham Manor, Briskham Hill). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit anchor: Mission City Station at 33200 North Railway Avenue is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express (weekday rush-hour service), and BC Transit Route 31 runs Mission–Abbotsford. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- One Mission-specific quirk for family buyers: School District 75 runs a middle-tier system (K–6 / 7–9 / 10–12), unlike SD42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Mission's oldest stock (Glenkirk Court, 1975; Mission Manor, 1992; Briskham Manor, 1993) this is the central diligence document. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Building directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Cherry Lane and Stave Lake Landing — that is old bylaw text, not current law. Two Mission-specific notes: a current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals," which is the kind of restriction that CAN still be enforced; and Preston's rentals were secured by a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Briskham Manor and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date — the City's property tax page states a "10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026" (the Community Charter general scheme; Mission has no 5% + 5% instalment scheme, and the staged-penalty figures sometimes quoted for the wider region belong to the rural/FVRD regime, not the City). Mission taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the other levies (City tax rates page). https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar, Briskham Hill), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Montgomery and Silver & Cedar purchases. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Montgomery and Silver & Cedar. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Cherry Lane a condo or a townhouse complex?** Townhouse strata. The building directory's own product variants for Cherry Lane (32165 Seventh Avenue, built 1989, 22 units) include "Townhouse," and the same is true of Luther Place, whose MLS records state dwelling type Townhouse outright. Listing feeds that file either under "condos" are using the word loosely. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cherry-lane-32165-seventh-ave
- **Q: What does the 55+ rule at Briskham Manor and Luther Place restrict?** Only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is legal in BC (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Briskham Manor's pet rules are separately published: cats and dogs OK, two-pet limit, roughly 24–25 lb size limit per listings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85456/FullPublic.pdf?i=1506184905
- **Q: Can I rent out a Mission condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, which is why the "rentals not allowed" directory lines for Cherry Lane and Stave Lake Landing are outdated law. Short-term rentals are different: a bylaw can still restrict them, and one current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the tax penalty if I miss Mission's July 2 deadline?** A flat 10% penalty on any current balance outstanding on July 3, 2026 — not a staged 5% + 5%. Budget for the diking levy as an additional line on the Mission tax bill. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: How old is Mission's condo stock, really?** It spans 1975 (Glenkirk Court) to 2026 completions (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar). The middle band — Mission Manor (1992) and Briskham Manor (1993) — is wood-frame, and Briskham's strata fee includes gas and hot water per listings, a real offset when comparing fees across eras. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mission-manor-33165-second-avenue
- **Q: Do the new Redekop Faye buildings charge GST?** Yes on developer sales — Montgomery and Silver & Cedar are new construction, so 5% GST applies, with the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (up to $50,000 back on homes to $1 million) and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption (PTT fully exempt to $1.1 million) both in play for qualifying buyers. Montgomery listings state this explicitly ("price excludes GST. FTHB Rebate available"). Preston (built 2023) is now resale stock, so a purchase there is generally GST-exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How does the West Coast Express work from Mission?** Mission City Station (33200 North Railway Avenue) is the line's eastern terminus and service is weekday rush-hours only — a downtown-Vancouver commute is realistic on the train's schedule, not outside it. BC Transit Route 31 covers Mission–Abbotsford trips. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Is flood risk a factor for Mission condos?** The city's south edge along the Fraser is floodplain and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke; Mission owners also pay a diking levy. The named central buildings are not on the river edge, but flood status and strata insurance terms are parcel- and building-specific — check both rather than assuming from the map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a central Mission condo?** SD75 runs a three-tier system (elementary K–6, middle 7–9, secondary 10–12), so a Mission address feeds a middle school that a Maple Ridge/Pitt Meadows buyer (SD42) would not expect. Catchments are address-based — run the building's address through the SD75 locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Briskham Manor's build year?** Most listings and the MLS data sheet say 1993; one listing says 1992. This file uses 1993 as the majority/sheet year and flags the conflict — the strata plan (LMS1049) registration record settles it definitively. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85456/FullPublic.pdf?i=1506184905

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Mission just replaced its OCP.** Bylaw 6405-2026 was adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 — planning documents and listing copy citing the old OCP are already a bylaw out of date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
- **One developer is reshaping the city's condo map.** Redekop Faye built Preston on Fourteenth (2023), Montgomery (completing 2026) and Silver & Cedar (completing 2026) — three of the city's newest apartment buildings share one builder, which makes cross-building comparisons of construction and warranty unusually direct. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Preston's rentals rest on a housing agreement, not just the 2022 law.** Its presale record states condo rentals were permitted for 100 years — a project-level housing agreement term that survives independently of the province-wide voiding of rental bylaws. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/preston-on-fourteenth-west-heights-mission-presale-condos-by-redekop-faye/
- **Montgomery carries two addresses.** The developer markets 7375 Horne Street while resale listings address units at 33281 2 Avenue — the same building. Confirm which address a specific unit's title and strata records use before ordering documents. https://montgomerycondos.ca/?option=com_content&amp%3Btask=view&amp%3Bid=17&amp%3BItemid=33
- **Luther Place also carries two addresses** — 7525 Martin Place in listings, 32286 7th Avenue in the building directory — and its units surface in "condo" feeds even though the MLS dwelling type is Townhouse. Both facts will confuse a buyer's document search if not flagged up front. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/luther-place-32286-7-ave
- **The rural edge runs on different rules.** City notices cite the RU16 rural zone's 1.6 ha minimum lot size, and the staged 5% + 5% tax penalty quoted for parts of the Fraser Valley belongs to the rural (Taxation (Rural Area) Act) regime — inside City of Mission limits the penalty is the flat 10% (Section 3). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md) and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Central amenities a condo buyer actually uses:** Mission Leisure Centre at 7650 Grand Street and Mission Memorial Hospital at 7324 Hurd Street anchor the central core the condo buildings cluster around. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/mission-homes-for-sale.md)
