## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In brand-new buildings like the Town Centre's, early Form Bs rest on the developer's interim budget — read them with the disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. New stratas have their own first-report deadlines — for the Town Centre buildings, ask when the first report is due and what it assumes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (expanded May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Aldergrove Town Centre building is verified as 55+; Creekside Villa (townhouse form) is the area's verified 55+ strata. An older Creekside Villa listing described a unit as a "45+ rancher" while its own MLS data recorded "Restricted Age: 55+" — under current law only the 55+ form can be valid, and the registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs Aldergrove; zones named in Township documents for Aldergrove parcels include Rural Zone RU-2, Community Commercial Zone C-2 and Comprehensive Development Zone CD-192, and the bylaw's Section 110.1 ladder runs from R-1B/R-1C urban lots at a 464.5 m² minimum to SR-2 at 8,094 m². Plans: the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community under the Township's Official Community Plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)
- **SSMUH in the Township = Zoning Bylaw 6020** (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing), adopted November 18, 2024 — it amended Zoning Bylaw 2500 to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings; the Township estimated about 55% of its single-family and duplex lots (roughly 15,000 properties) would qualify for 3 to 4 units. **There is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020** — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is WRONG; no such claim was found in this area's research, so no defect is recorded here. Secondary suite cap: 120 m² under the Township frame in this program. A coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never a strata unit. (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/aldergrove-condos-2026-11-01.md, SSMUH sources cited there.)
- **Township property taxes:** property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most Town Centre units are new or nearly new, so whether GST applied, and whether a rebate was already claimed, is a unit-by-unit question. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $611,000 (the Langley community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $8,220 (2% × $411,000) = **$10,220 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** for any remaining Town Centre presale (including The Hart), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18; assignment rights are whatever the contract grants. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **ALR check for the area, not the building:** much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Township directs owners to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource — irrelevant inside the Town Centre buildings themselves, but decisive for any acreage alternative a condo buyer is cross-shopping in Aldergrove. Strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The condos sit on the old mall.** Aldergrove Town Centre replaces the decades-old Aldergrove Mall at 272 Street and Fraser Highway — the project's own marketing frames it as breathing new life into the historic downtown core, with over 400 homes and 45,000 sq ft of commercial space including a daycare across a six-building community. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/ (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/aldergrove-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The building names are a history lesson, on purpose.** Shortreed, Jackman and Vanetta are named for Aldergrove pioneering families, The Hart for Frederick John Hart, and Creekside for its position beside Bertrand Creek. Useful when a listing uses a building name with no address: all of them are on Aldergrove Town Centre Drive. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- **Two "Creeksides" and two "Jackmans" will mix up a name-only search.** Creekside (the Town Centre apartment building, 27249 Aldergrove Town Centre Dr) is not Creekside Villa (the 55+ townhouse complex, 27435 29A Ave) and neither is Creekside Estates (27125 31A Ave, townhouses, no age restriction found); the Jackman condo building is not Jackman Manor, the long-term care home at 27477 28 Ave. (Home file and 55+ notes cited in Section 2.)
- **Project totals moved between reports — do not quote the early ones.** A 2022 construction report described three six-storey buildings plus a 10-storey building totalling 260 homes; the 2025 marketing describes six buildings and over 400 homes with The Hart as the fifth building. The scheme grew; only the disclosure statement for your building states its own count reliably. (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/aldergrove-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The recreation centre arrived before the condos.** The Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre's arena opened August 18, 2018, and its canopy-covered outdoor pools are open year-round — the amenity the Town Centre markets against was already operating years before the first Town Centre building completed in 2025. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx

Expect infill plexes, not more condo buildings, as Aldergrove's next supply wave outside the core; any source claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong.
