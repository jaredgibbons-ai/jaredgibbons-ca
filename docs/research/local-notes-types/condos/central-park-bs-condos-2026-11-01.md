Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The Central Park sub-area is the residential area around Central Park in south-west Burnaby, on the Vancouver border at Kingsway, directly west of Metrotown. "Central Park" is the GVR board sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," matching Central Park itself, the City park the area surrounds; the site slug suffix "-bs" follows this site's Burnaby South naming scheme. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area; the residential area surrounds the park, between Boundary Road (the Vancouver border) and Metrotown, along Kingsway and Imperial Street — exact MLS boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. Heritage Burnaby describes the park itself as lying between 49th Avenue to the south, Kingsway to the north, Boundary Road to the west and Patterson Avenue to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md) and https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Textual%20Record&ps=50&q=&place_facet=barnetroad000000%7CBarnet%20Road&place_facet=imperialstreet000000%7CImperial%20Street&p=1
- Where the condo stock concentrates: east of the park, on the Wilson Avenue / Barker Avenue / Patterson Avenue grid between the park and Metrotown — a 1990s Bosa concrete cluster (Paramount, Paramount II, Place Meridian), a 1970s–1990s Patterson/Sardis cluster (Central Park Place at two addresses, Grand Central Park Place, Wimbledon Club), one 2017 tower (Aldynne on the Park), and the sub-area's newest tower, Central Park House (2025), at the Patterson Station end of Wilson Avenue. Detached houses hold the streets north and south of the park itself. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below. Note the feed trap: several of these buildings are filed under "Metrotown" in some listing feeds and "Central Park BS" in others — the addresses sit in the park-side grid the research file describes, and this file follows the research file's ground.

SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: Burnaby-wide Condos & Townhomes median was $686,000, $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market in September 2026. There is no Central Park sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
- Main streets: Kingsway runs along the park's north side and Imperial Street along the south; Boundary Road forms the west edge at the City of Vancouver border and Patterson Avenue the east side of the park. Wilson Avenue, Barker Avenue and Sardis Street carry the tower stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this sub-area that review spans a 1977 building (Central Park Place, 4160 Sardis), a 1990s Bosa cluster, and a 2025 tower still in its warranty period — three entirely different diligence files. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Place Meridian's published project sequence (repiping and boiler 2023, elevator 2024, pool 2024, common areas 2025) is the kind of completed-work record a depreciation report should corroborate — check it against the report rather than the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/5967-wilson-avenue/700
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Applied here: the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for **Central Park Place (4160 Sardis Street)** is void and unenforceable, however the registered bylaws read; Aldynne on the Park's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law. Any other Central Park directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" is likewise old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/central-park-place-4160-sardis-street
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw is published for any Section 2 building in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per building; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (Bylaw No. 4742), which is repealed — flag any source still citing the 1965 bylaw as current. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; Metrotown is Burnaby's regional city centre and downtown under that OCP, and the City lists higher-density housing, including towers, walk-ups and townhouses, in the town centres. For the park-side detached streets, the R1 SSMUH District allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — a test many lots here meet, with two Expo Line stations framing the area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Central Park House (2025), not to the 1977–2017 resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant only to a new-unit purchase, not to Central Park resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A market residential presale inside the Central Park sub-area verified on a developer source in the research pass: UNCONFIRMED (development at the Metrotown edge is covered on the Metrotown pages) — Central Park House is a completed 2025 building in current listings, so purchases there now run as resales unless a unit is still on a developer contract, which is UNCONFIRMED per unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md) and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One name, three buildings, two tenures of use.** "Central Park Place" is a 1977 residential building at 4160 Sardis Street, a residential building at 5652 Patterson Avenue whose build year sources dispute (1981 vs 1992), and an 18-storey Bosa office tower at 4555 Kingsway built in 2000. Any search, document order or depreciation-report request made on the name alone can land on the wrong corporation. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/central-park-place-4160-sardis-street and https://images.loopnet.fr:443/d2/3NThwOcNaAIaBCAkBEz_tW8tZcgcIazRu073JPMlRZw/Kingsway4555.pdf
- **The park's edges are heritage-recorded, and they fix the sub-area's shape.** Heritage Burnaby records Central Park's ceremonial entrance gate on Kingsway (associated dates 1913) and the Jubilee Grove Arch at Kingsway and Patterson Avenue (1939), and describes the park as lying between 49th Avenue, Kingsway, Boundary Road and Patterson Avenue — the same four edges the condo grid sits outside of, east of Patterson. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/list?objectType_facet=Landmark%20Description%20&objectType_facet=Textual%20Record&ps=50&q=&place_facet=barnetroad000000%7CBarnet%20Road&place_facet=imperialstreet000000%7CImperial%20Street&p=1
- **The newest tower was planned at a different size than it was listed at.** Central Park House began in reporting as a 39-storey proposal with 293 units at 5977 Wilson Avenue, was marketed as a 41-storey project of 356 condominiums and 6 townhomes, and trades in 2025/2026 listings at 5987 Wilson Avenue as a built 2025 tower — proposal-era unit counts and the civic address both shifted, so quote the strata plan, not the presale coverage. https://www.urbanyvr.com/wilson-bluesky-burnaby-condos/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/wilson-bluesky-metrotown-burnaby-presale-condos/
- **A neighbouring building's listing is marketing against this stock.** A Paramount (5833 Wilson) listing sells its own unit partly on the claim that its building has an "UPDATED ENVELOPE unlike 5899 Wilson" — inter-building envelope comparisons now appear in live listing copy in this cluster, which is a prompt to read both buildings' depreciation reports, not a verified fact about either. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3080796-5833-wilson-avenue-1604-burnaby-central-park-bs-homes-for-sale-778000/brochure
- **The BC Parkway runs through the park under the SkyTrain line.** Central Park carries the BC Parkway trail through it beneath the elevated Expo Line — the Patterson-to-Metrotown walk that tower listings call "steps to the park" is a lit, paved parkway route, and it is also why the park-side buildings' transit pitch is a walk, not a station address, for every building except the Patterson-corner stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
