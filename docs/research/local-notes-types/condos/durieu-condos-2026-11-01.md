## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the fee-simple acreages and farm parcels that make up this community's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule applies to the freehold farm product that makes up this community — and neither creates a second-dwelling right on a Durieu acreage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Planning and zoning (local):** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern Electoral Area F (the Hatzic Valley OCP covers the inhabited valley) — the specific bylaw numbers and zone names for Durieu lots are UNVERIFIED in this pass, and City of Mission plans (including Mission OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, which governs the city only) do NOT apply. Confirm a parcel's designation and zone with the FVRD for the PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/durieu.md)
- **Secondary suites in Area F have a hard shape limit:** the FVRD records significant limits on development options in Electoral Area F with the majority of lands in the ALR, and that secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure, such as a garden suite, or a multi-unit structure for farm workers. City of Mission suite rules (registration, the $63 annual fee) do NOT apply here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/durieu.md)
- **Rural property taxes:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, rural property taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. This is the regime that applies in Durieu — not Mission's flat 10%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/durieu.md)
- **ALR housing and stratification rules:** parcel-level ALR status is per address: UNVERIFIED — first-page feed text itself puts one Durieu Road parcel in the ALR and states roughly 18 acres of a Seux Road parcel are NOT in the ALR, both as listing claims to verify on the ALC maps by PID. - **Q: Can I add a suite or a second home on a Durieu acreage?** Two gates apply. Confirm both the FVRD zoning and the parcel's ALR status for the specific PID before relying on a second-dwelling plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/durieu.md) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

A Durieu farm listing is a house on land under the ALC residence frame, not condo stock, whatever a feed label suggests. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
