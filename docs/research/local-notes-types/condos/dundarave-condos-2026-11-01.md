## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Dundarave's 1960s–90s buildings the depreciation report attached to the Form B is the document that prices the next decade of concrete, elevator and envelope work — read it before the view. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Every counted building in Section 2 is at or above the five-lot threshold on its published count, and the small buildings (Les Terraces, 11 homes) carry the same report duty across far fewer owners. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — Seastrand's directory line already records rentals allowed, and no Dundarave strata can lawfully hold a long-term rental ban today. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Per the 55+ pass, no Dundarave strata holds a verified 55+ bylaw — Dundarave has 0 verified 55+ ownership buildings. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning (West Vancouver's own bylaws only):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 is the District's zoning bylaw — it specifies how land can be used and regulates intensity, height, density, setbacks and lot coverage. The Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 (Council adopted Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025 on December 1, 2025, aligning the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report). Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre is a named Form & Character Development Permit area, so new development, additions and renovations in the village core are reviewed against OCP design guidelines; a Foreshore Development Permit applies to waterfront properties and others at risk of coastal flooding, and an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. Density is in transition: on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025 — the District's final adopted zoning amendments for the Dundarave-adjacent areas were still outstanding in the research file, so verify current zoning per property. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010 and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/dundarave.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes:** for 2026, due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half the bill is collected for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. At Dundarave price points the Province's additional school tax also applies: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/dundarave.md)
- **Secondary suites (West Vancouver):** the District permits secondary suites under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 with its own registration process — one per lot, within the principal building (not detached), with the registered owner occupying either the principal dwelling or the suite. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/real-estate-FAQS/secondary-suites-in-west-vancouver/ (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/dundarave-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $2,500,000 (round illustration only — no area median exists to use), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $15,000 (3% × $500,000) = **$53,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no live Dundarave presale was verified in this pass (the Villa Maris/Shoreland redevelopment is a preliminary concept, not a disclosure-statement project) — where a unit is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.mattgul.com/blog/46521 and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
