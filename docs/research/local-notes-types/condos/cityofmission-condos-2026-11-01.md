## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest stock (Glenkirk Court, 1975; Mission Manor, 1992; Briskham Manor, 1993) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Mission may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Stave Lake Landing and Cherry Lane directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. Preston's rentals rest on a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Briskham Manor and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans — the OCP:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing and repealing Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement formally incorporated as required under the Local Government Act. Live guides stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption are wrong. The batch-15 research file itself had the adoption UNVERIFIED (its latest City document was the April 7, 2026 public-hearing notice, City Hall, 8645 Stave Lake Street); the adopted date and number above are this program's settled position. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. City zoning, secondary-suite and SSMUH specifics as applied to a specific parcel: check with the City — per-parcel OCP designation, zone, Development Permit Area overlays and ALR status are per-parcel checks, UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md) and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026 and (Mission BC condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/mission-bc-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The four area plans, as the live page describes them** (the page's own text, pointing at mission.ca documents; the plan documents themselves were not opened in the batch-15 pass, so each plan's current status and adoption date is UNVERIFIED here beyond the City policy record below): the Cedar Valley Local Area Plan and Cedar Valley Engineering Plan "provide detailed guidance for the future development of the Cedar Valley community" on sustainability principles; the Downtown Mission Plan "is designed as an action plan to stimulate both private and public investment and improve the economics of the downtown"; the Silverdale Central Neighbourhood Plan "was undertaken to realize the vision of an urban destination mixed-use neighbourhood as outlined within Mission's Official Community Plan within the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area (SCPA)"; and the Stave Heights Neighbourhood Plan "provides a clear guide for new development, public investment, and an opportunity for a diversity of housing within the environmentally sensitive hillside landscape." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md) and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cityofmission.html
- **Silverdale layering:** under the City's SCPA Neighbourhood Planning Terms of Reference (Policy LAN.64(C), adopted February 18, 2020), neighbourhood plans are required for SCPA areas anticipating growth in density or experiencing development pressures, and must be consistent with the OCP including its Development Permit Areas. Significant portions of the Silverdale Master Infrastructure Strategy (the 2018 technical strategy) have been superseded by the Central Neighbourhood Plan and its supporting technical documents and by the Utilities Master Plan — a reader following the page's "Silverdale Plan" tile meets the Central Neighbourhood Plan name, which the live page never mentions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md) and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/silverdale-master-infrastructure-strategy-mis
- **Development Permit Area guidelines are a live file at the City:** the City is updating the existing Development Permit Area (DPA) Guidelines in the OCP, with a further public hearing notice issued for August 17, 2026 pointing to the DPA Guidelines Review project page. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md) and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-august-17-2026
- **Mission property tax penalty is a FLAT 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2, 2026 due date — the City states a 10% penalty is added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026, and that it has no authority to waive or modify the penalty (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw). **Keep the two regimes explicitly separate:** the staged 5% + 5% pattern belongs to the separate rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act) for FVRD electoral areas outside the City — it is NOT the City of Mission penalty, and the two are never blended in this file. The City does not accept credit card payments. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md) and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **ALR:** Mission's rural sub-areas include farm land; on ALR parcels the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — which is why no ALR parcel is condo stock. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar, Briskham Hill), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 secondary date is superseded). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Montgomery and Silver & Cedar purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Montgomery and Silver & Cedar. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Mission replaced its OCP in 2026 — and the replacement changed almost nothing on purpose.** Bylaw 6405-2026 was adopted April 21, 2026 to replace and repeal Bylaw 6350-2025 for a procedural reason (formally incorporating a Regional Context Statement), with the vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction unchanged. Planning documents citing the old bylaw number are a bylaw out of date; live guides stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption date are simply wrong. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Plan boundaries and board sub-area boundaries are different lines.** Silverdale's growth ground falls on the Mission West / Mission sub-area side of the board map, not in any sub-area named for it — so a sub-area figure (for example the "Mission" sub-area's 104 attached listings) describes a board area containing several plan areas plus ground outside all of them, never a plan area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmission.md)
