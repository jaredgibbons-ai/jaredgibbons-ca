# Mid Meadows, Pitt Meadows — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mid Meadows is a Paragon sub-area of the City of Pitt Meadows covering the Bonson Road area south of the CPKC rail line — the City's own labels for this part of the city are North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village; the City does not publish an official neighbourhood boundary or name for "Mid Meadows." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- From official facility addresses, the area runs along Bonson Road south of the Canadian Pacific rail line and Hammond Road and north of the Fraser River dike, roughly between Harris Road in the west and the Maple Ridge boundary in the east. Bonson Road is the main road through the area, with Fraser Way and Barnston View Road in Osprey Village. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: there is no apartment-condo concentration — the attached stock is townhomes, concentrated on Bonson Road, Fraser Way and Barnston View Road in North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village. In September 2026 the sub-area's combined Condos & Townhomes category recorded 12 inventory, 4 solds and a 33% sales ratio — the strongest of the three Pitt Meadows sub-areas that month — but that SnapStats category combines condos and townhomes and does not separate them (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Transit: routes 722 and 719 run as a combined circular service through Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown, Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Meadows Station, and route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place; no West Coast Express station is in this sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Pitt Meadows IS covered by SnapStats at community level: Condos & Townhomes in September 2026 recorded a median sale price of $662,500, $566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list and 40 days on market (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). SnapStats publishes no Mid Meadows sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These strata rules are stated in full because they govern every townhome strata in Section 2 exactly as they govern apartment condos.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are prohibited in Pitt Meadows:** a City staff report on Zoning Bylaw amendments states that short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the Zoning Bylaw — a strata bylaw question never arises, because the City does not permit the use. (Standing fact for Pitt Meadows, confirmed against the City source.) https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Mid Meadows townhome complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones (R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, R-6 up to five, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit), and RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 is the townhouse zone (minimum lot area 0.4 ha). The 1,215 sq m figure in the R-zones is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **City plan:** the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, was adopted September 27, 2022 and covers the whole city including the Bonson Road area; no neighbourhood or area plan specific to Bonson Road was found in official sources. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) where the zone permits; one garden suite per lot (33 to 90 sq m) in the zones listed in the City guide, and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. Under provincial rules applied citywide, lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Floodplain and dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states **over 95%** of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River (an older City release from the superseded OCP era stated approximately 86% — the current figure is over 95%). The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes; this sub-area is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Property tax and drainage levy:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The bylaw charges a separate residential drainage levy — for 2026, $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for Class 1, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 townhome complexes (2005–2007 era) are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current residential presale project in or beside this sub-area was verified in the research pass, so REDMA is background only here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in Mid Meadows?** None verified. No condo building name was confirmed on an official, developer or strata source for the Bonson Road area — the attached stock is the Osprey Village / Bonson Road townhomes in Section 2. A buyer who wants an apartment condo in Pitt Meadows is looking at the Harris Road corridor in Central Meadows, a different sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: SnapStats shows a 33% sales ratio for "condos" here — is the condo market hot?** That figure is the combined Condos & Townhomes category (12 inventory, 4 solds, September 2026), and on the verified stock it is townhome activity. Do not quote it as an apartment-condo figure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a townhome in Osprey Village?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under Pitt Meadows' Zoning Bylaw, per the City's own staff report — quite apart from any strata bylaw or the provincial principal-residence rules. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Can I rent out a Mid Meadows townhome long-term?** Strata law no longer stops you — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. Confirm the specific complex has no surviving registered restriction issue on the Form B, and remember the short-term prohibition above is a City zoning rule, not a strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Coho or Coho II?** No pet rule for either complex was published in the sources checked: UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC — read the registered bylaws in the strata package before writing. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is flood risk a factor for a Bonson Road townhome?** Yes as a diligence item: over 95% of the city's land base is within the Fraser / Pitt River floodplain per the City's 2020 Business Plan, the area sits behind the dike system (60 km of dikes citywide) in drainage Area 3, and owners pay a separate drainage levy ($0.1082 per $1,000 for residential in 2026). Confirm the specific lot's floodplain and drainage details with the City and read the strata's insurance terms. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Osprey Village address?** SD42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). Davie Jones Elementary (12030 Blakely Road) and Edith McDermott Elementary (12178 Bonson Road) are both physically in the sub-area, and Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116B Avenue) is the city's only public secondary. The locator result for the research file's sample address (10932 Barnston View Road) was Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary — catchments are address-based, so run the exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: How do I get to the West Coast Express from Mid Meadows?** There is no WCE station in the sub-area: routes 719 / 722 run through Bonson Road and Fraser Way and stop at Pitt Meadows Station (north of the area), and route 701 on Hammond Road runs to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: What extra line is on a Pitt Meadows tax bill?** A separate drainage levy inside the annual tax rates bylaw — $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for residential (Class 1) in 2026, on top of the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after August 1. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Mid Meadows" is a board name, not a City name.** The City calls this area North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village, and its heritage register calls the Park Road / Bonson Road area "Somerset" (the McMyn/Mason House stood at the south-west corner of Park Road and Bonson Road). Searching City documents for "Mid Meadows" finds nothing — search "Bonson." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The floodplain figure changed, and the old one still circulates.** The City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the land base is in the floodplain; an older City release from the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 era said approximately 86%. Any source quoting ~85–86% is quoting the superseded figure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **A replacement secondary school is in the works.** SD42 lists a Pitt Meadows Secondary replacement — funded by the Ministry of Education and Child Care with a neighbourhood learning centre, in the design and development phase — and that school serves this sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **A rapid bus is planned north of the area.** TransLink's Langley–Haney Place Bus Rapid Transit will run via the Golden Ears Bridge and Lougheed Highway north of this sub-area and connect to the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus — a future-service fact, not a current one; today's service is the 719 / 722 / 701 buses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The dike is the neighbourhood's front yard.** Shoreline Park (51 Bonson Road) sits in Osprey Village on the Fraser River dike, and the Harris Landing / Pitt River Regional Greenway's first phase runs 10.2 km along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes — the same dike system behind the floodplain and drainage-levy facts in Section 3. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The airport is across Harris Road.** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is west of the area across Harris Road, and the City maintains a page for safety and noise concerns — west-edge addresses should check it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **There is no library branch in this sub-area.** The city's only branch is Pitt Meadows Public Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) at 200-12099 Harris Road, in Central Meadows — the recreation anchor inside Mid Meadows is instead the South Bonson Community Centre (10932 Barnston View Road) and the arena / athletic park cluster on Bonson Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
