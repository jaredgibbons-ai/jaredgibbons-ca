# Aldergrove, Township of Langley — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Read this first:** Aldergrove's apartment-condo stock is essentially one project. Before Aldergrove Town Centre, the core was large-lot single-family homes with some townhouses and apartments including fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street, per the Township's core plan — no older named condo building in the core was verified in this pass. Every verified apartment-condo building below is in Aldergrove Town Centre, on the old Aldergrove Mall site at Fraser Highway and 272 Street. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Aldergrove is a community in the Township of Langley; the Township's eastern boundary is 276 Street at Abbotsford and its southern boundary is the United States border. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- The Aldergrove Core Area is centred on Fraser Highway and 272 Street, bounded by Bertrand Creek to the north and east, 29 Avenue to the south and 270 Street to the west, per the Township plan; wider community boundaries are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: in one place only — Aldergrove Town Centre, at the Fraser Highway / 272 Street crossroads, on a new internal street, Aldergrove Town Centre Drive. Its buildings (Shortreed, Jackman, Vanetta, Creekside, and The Hart releasing next) all share that one site, beside Bertrand Creek. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit from that crossroads: TransLink route 503 (Aldergrove/Langley – Fraser Hwy Express) runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station via King George Station, and BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove Connector) links Aldergrove with Abbotsford from the same stop. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Aldergrove. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- The Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre is at 27032 Fraser Highway — pools, the Otter Co-op Outdoor Experience waterpark, an NHL-size arena with 500 seats (opened August 18, 2018), a fitness centre and an indoor walking track — a few blocks west of the Town Centre site. https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx
- SD35 schools physically in Aldergrove include Parkside Centennial Elementary (3300 270 St), Shortreed Community Elementary (27330 28 Ave), Betty Gilbert Middle School (26845 27 Ave) and Aldergrove Community Secondary (26850 29 Ave); a locator run for 26850 29 Ave returned Parkside Centennial, Betty Gilbert and Aldergrove Community Secondary, and catchments are address-based. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

All five buildings below are in one development: **Aldergrove Town Centre**, by Janda Group, replacing the decades-old Aldergrove Mall. Its marketing describes a six-building community introducing over 400 new homes and 45,000 sq ft of commercial space, with buildings named after Aldergrove pioneering families. An earlier construction report described a smaller scheme (260 homes) — the project grew between reports, so treat project totals as developer-reported and phase-dependent. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/ and http://storeys.com/aldergrove-town-centre-development-langley-begins-construction/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In brand-new buildings like the Town Centre's, early Form Bs rest on the developer's interim budget — read them with the disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with 30-year projections and at least three CRF funding models, and it cannot be deferred by a vote. New stratas have their own first-report deadlines — for the Town Centre buildings, ask when the first report is due and what it assumes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ only.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, the only legal age bylaw is 55+, in force as the only form since Nov 24, 2022; it restricts who may live in a unit, not who may own it. No Aldergrove Town Centre building is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs Aldergrove; zones named in Township documents for Aldergrove parcels include Rural Zone RU-2, Community Commercial Zone C-2 and Comprehensive Development Zone CD-192, and the bylaw's Section 110.1 ladder runs from R-1B/R-1C urban lots at a 464.5 m² minimum to SR-2 at 8,094 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **SSMUH in the Township: Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024**, amended the Zoning Bylaw to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings; the Township estimated about 55% of its single-family and duplex lots (roughly 15,000 properties) would qualify for 3 to 4 units. That is the rule reshaping the house streets around the Town Centre — it does not change an existing condo's bylaws. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/Report-to-Council----Small-Scale,-Multi-Unit-Housing,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Plans:** the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, and the Township's Official Community Plan update work includes an Aldergrove component. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for eligible first-time buyers with agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Most Town Centre units are new or nearly new, so whether GST applied, and whether a rebate was already claimed, is a unit-by-unit question. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, no rebate at $450,000 or more, for any new-home buyer who qualifies. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 of value (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Township taxes themselves were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** For any remaining Town Centre presale (including The Hart), the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and the buyer has a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it; deposits are held in trust or covered by deposit protection insurance, and assignment rights are whatever the contract grants. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, rising to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment; a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **ALR check for the area, not the building.** Much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Township directs owners to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource — irrelevant inside the Town Centre buildings themselves, but decisive for any acreage alternative a condo buyer is cross-shopping in Aldergrove. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an older, cheaper condo building in Aldergrove to compare against the Town Centre?** None was verified in this pass. The Township core plan describes the pre-Town Centre core as large-lot single-family homes with some townhouses and fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street — so the realistic comparison set is Town Centre buildings against each other, and against townhouses, not against an older condo stock. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- **Q: Which Town Centre building is which?** Shortreed (27215), Jackman (27218, 71 homes, 6 storeys, wood-frame) and Vanetta (27254) are the Phase 1 buildings; Creekside (27249, 66 homes, 4 storeys, wood-frame, beside Bertrand Creek) is Phase 2; The Hart is the fifth building, released in 2025, with its civic address UNCONFIRMED. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/509-27218-aldergrove-town-centre-dr-1925649 and https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- **Q: Are the buildings wood-frame or concrete/steel?** Jackman and Creekside are recorded as wood-frame; Vanetta is described in listings as steel-frame with concrete floors, the project's only steel-frame building — a listing claim to confirm in the building's own documents before paying a premium for it. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/107-27249-aldergrove-town-centre-dr-1991091 and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/27254-aldergrove-town-centre-drive/711
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in a brand-new building, and can they jump?** The Form B states the unit's fee and the CRF balance, but in a first-year strata those figures come from the developer's interim budget, not from actual operating history — the first annual general meeting budget is where buyers learn the real number. Ask for the interim budget, the warranty (2-5-10) terms, and when the first depreciation report is due. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I rent it out?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, so a Town Centre strata cannot ban long-term renting; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the registered bylaws for your building are the check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Listings differ by building and unit — Jackman is listed with "2 dogs or 2 cats" for one unit, a Creekside listing says "Cats OK" for another. That variance is exactly why the registered pet bylaw, not the listing, decides it. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3072387-404-27218-aldergrove-town-centre-drive-langley-british-columbia-v4w0e4-429900/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/27249-aldergrove-town-centre-drive/206
- **Q: How many parking stalls and lockers do I get?** Listings for Town Centre units repeatedly describe one underground stall and two storage lockers, but allocations are unit-specific — the Form B is where the stall and locker numbers for your unit are legally stated. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3089864-27218-aldergrove-town-centre-drive-201-langley-bc-v4w-0e2/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a resale unit in a 2025 building?** Possibly — GST treatment of a nearly-new unit depends on its history (for example, whether it has been occupied and whether a rebate was claimed), not just its age. If you are an eligible first-time buyer, the Bill C-4 rebate covers new homes up to $1 million at 100% (maximum $50,000); get the GST position confirmed in writing by your lawyer/notary before subjects are removed. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How do I get to Surrey or Vancouver without a car?** The 503 Fraser Hwy Express from 272 Street at Fraser Highway to Surrey Central Station via Langley Centre and King George Station; TransLink's timetable (effective June 8, 2026) shows roughly 70 minutes end-to-end on early-morning trips, stretching to about 90 minutes at peak. BC Transit route 21 covers the Abbotsford direction. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- **Q: Is the SkyTrain coming to Aldergrove?** No. The funded line is the 16 km Surrey–Langley (Dave Barrett) Extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to Langley City Centre, expected in service in late 2029 — it terminates west of Aldergrove, in Langley City, and Aldergrove's link to it is the 503 bus to Langley Centre. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515 and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The condos sit on the old mall.** Aldergrove Town Centre replaces the decades-old Aldergrove Mall at 272 Street and Fraser Highway — the project's own marketing frames it as breathing new life into the historic downtown core, with over 400 homes and 45,000 sq ft of commercial space including a daycare across a six-building community. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- **The building names are a history lesson, on purpose.** Shortreed, Jackman and Vanetta are named for Aldergrove pioneering families, The Hart for Frederick John Hart, and Creekside for its position beside Bertrand Creek — developed with naming input tied to the Aldergrove Historical Society, per the marketing firm. Useful when a listing uses a building name with no address: all of them are on Aldergrove Town Centre Drive. https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- **Project totals moved between reports — do not quote the early ones.** A 2022 construction report described three six-storey buildings plus a 10-storey building totalling 260 homes; the 2025 marketing describes six buildings and over 400 homes with The Hart as the fifth building. The scheme grew; only the disclosure statement for your building states its own count reliably. http://storeys.com/aldergrove-town-centre-development-langley-begins-construction/ and https://fifthave.ca/the-vibrant-new-hart-of-aldergrove-town-centre/
- **The recreation centre arrived before the condos.** The Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre's arena opened August 18, 2018, and its canopy-covered outdoor pools are open year-round — the amenity the Town Centre markets against was already operating years before the first Town Centre building completed in 2025. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/arena-opens-at-the-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/parks-recreation/aldergrove-community-centre.aspx
- **SSMUH is quietly changing the streets around the project.** Township Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) enabled 3–4 units on most former single-family/duplex lots — an estimated 55% of such lots Township-wide — and in March 2026 the Township was already advancing a form-and-character development permit area for SSMUH. Expect infill plexes, not more condo buildings, as Aldergrove's next supply wave outside the core. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
