# North Vancouver — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "North Vancouver" is two municipalities, and this file labels every building **City** or **District**: the City of North Vancouver (compact, on the Burrard Inlet waterfront along the Lonsdale corridor) and the District of North Vancouver (the large residential municipality wrapped around it, from Deep Cove and Lynn Valley to Capilano and the Lions Gate area). They have different councils, OCPs, zoning bylaws and tax bills. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) **City — Lower Lonsdale** (Esplanade, 1st–3rd Streets, Chesterfield Place, the Shipyards waterfront); (2) **City — Central Lonsdale** (Lonsdale Avenue, Chesterfield Avenue, Keith Road, 13th–17th Streets — the densest cluster in this file); (3) **District — Lynn Valley Town Centre** (Mountain Highway, Emery Court, Library Lane); (4) **District — Lions Gate Village / Pemberton / Lower Capilano** (Marine Drive, Lions Gate Lane, Fullerton Avenue — the Woodcroft towers and the Park West buildings); (5) **District — Edgemont Village** (Connaught Crescent, Highland Boulevard — small boutique stock only). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The City is the North Shore's condo and apartment market (the Lonsdale corridor and the waterfront), while the District is predominantly detached houses with townhome and condo product concentrated in its centres (Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lions Gate Village, Edgemont Village). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- Transit anchors for condo buyers: the SeaBus sails from Lonsdale Quay (City, foot of Lonsdale Avenue) to Waterfront Station in Vancouver, with the Lonsdale Quay bus exchange beside the terminal; the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs Park Royal to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange; route 230 runs Lonsdale Quay to Upper Lonsdale via Lonsdale Avenue and route 229 runs Lonsdale Quay to Lynn Valley via Esplanade, Lonsdale Avenue and 29th Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf
- North Vancouver market numbers are NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 reports cover only Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows, Maple Ridge) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in the 1970s concrete stock (Canterbury House 1973, the Woodcroft buildings 1973–1977, Harbourview Park 1985), where parkade, plumbing, roof and elevator cycles land together — Woodcroft listings already advertise exactly those completed projects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Building directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Ridge Park Gardens and Canterbury House — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Ridge Park Gardens and Sundale are both presented as 55+ in current sources — confirm on the Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Two municipalities, two rulebooks:** City condos sit under the City of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700 and City OCP Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400; District condos sit under the District's own zoning bylaw and District OCP Bylaw 7900. City rules do not apply in the District and District rules do not apply in the City — confirm the municipality for the specific building before relying on any zoning statement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **City permit areas:** the City's Shipyards and Harbourside Waterfront precincts (Lower Lonsdale) are Development Permit Areas where a permit is required before new development, and Streamside Protection rules apply near watercourses — land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or 10 metres of a ravine bank. Relevant to strata capital planning in the waterfront buildings. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a resale-era Park West unit was taxed as new at first sale; current resales are not), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at North Shore prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms — for any remaining presale product (Magnolia at Edgemont), they are UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this building in the City or the District — and why does it matter?** Because the two municipalities run different zoning bylaws, OCPs and tax bills: the City under Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 / OCP No. 8400, the District under its own zoning bylaw / OCP Bylaw 7900. Every building in Section 2 is labelled City or District; a "North Vancouver" address alone does not tell you which rules apply. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: The directory says my building bans rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC (the "rentals not allowed" lines at Ridge Park Gardens and Canterbury House are old bylaw text). What can still bind you: a short-term rental bylaw, and a 55+ age bylaw limiting who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are Ridge Park Gardens and Sundale really 55+?** Both are presented as 55+ in current sources — Ridge Park Gardens listings state one owner must be 55+, and a Sundale listing calls it a "quiet 55+ building." A 55+ bylaw is the one age bylaw BC law still allows (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/ridge-park-gardens-2059-chesterfield-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Is 1345 Chesterfield (Chesterfield Manor) a normal strata purchase?** No. Listings state it is a corporation share purchase in Chesterfield Manor Estates Ltd., which owns the land and building, rather than conventional strata ownership — financing, resale and governance work differently, and there is no strata Form B. Get legal advice specific to share-purchase housing before writing. https://www.jimpilkington.com/north-vancouver/central-lonsdale/
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1970s concrete tower?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must now have one every five years with a 30-year projection and three funding models, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. At Woodcroft, listings advertise completed roof, plumbing, elevator and exterior work — ask which projects remain in the report, not just which are done. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It varies by building and pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws). Published examples: Vista Place — pets allowed with restrictions; The Versatile Building — pets allowed with restrictions; Canterbury House — pets allowed with restrictions; Ridge Park Gardens — pets allowed with restrictions. TIME is described in listings as pet-friendly. For Sundale, Woodcroft and Park West, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws in the strata package. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-versatile-building-111-east-3-street
- **Q: What does the strata fee cover at Woodcroft's Hollyburn building?** One concrete published example: a current Hollyburn (2020 Fullerton Ave) listing states the maintenance fee covers heat, hot water and BC Hydro, with an indoor pool, hot tub, sauna and gym on site. Fee coverage differs by Woodcroft building — confirm on the Form B for the specific building. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/2020-fullerton-avenue/901
- **Q: How is transit from the Lonsdale condos, really?** The SeaBus sails from Lonsdale Quay to Waterfront Station, the R2 RapidBus runs Park Royal to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange, route 230 runs up Lonsdale Avenue and route 229 runs Lonsdale Quay to Lynn Valley — Lower and Central Lonsdale buildings sit on all of it; Lynn Valley and Lions Gate Village buildings feed into the same exchanges by bus. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Lynn Valley or Lonsdale condo?** School District 44 serves both municipalities (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12) and catchments are address-based. The one locator result verified in the research file: 230 Keith Road West (City) returns Queen Mary Community Elementary and Carson Graham Secondary. Run the building's exact address with SD44 — do not assume from the nearest school. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the new GST rebate on a North Van presale?** Only on a new build: the FTHB GST/HST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on/after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. Resales — most of this file's stock — carry no GST and use the regular First-Time Home Buyers' PTT program instead (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"North Vancouver" condo searches cross a municipal border without saying so.** Chesterfield Avenue alone carries City buildings at 1320, 1345, 1420, 1610 and 2059, while the District's stock starts at its own centres — a buyer comparing two "North Vancouver" condos can be comparing two different tax bills, two zoning bylaws and two councils. Every building in Section 2 carries its City/District label for this reason. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **One Chesterfield building is not a strata at all.** Chesterfield Manor (1345 Chesterfield Ave) is a share purchase in the company that owns the land and building — it shows up in condo searches and condo price comparisons, but the purchase, financing and document package are not strata. https://www.jimpilkington.com/north-vancouver/central-lonsdale/
- **Woodcroft is several buildings wearing one name.** Listings treat Whytecliff, Hollyburn and The Capilano as distinct buildings within Woodcroft Estates, with different completed-project lists (roof, plumbing, elevators, exterior concrete) and years built recorded from 1973 to 1977 — compare the specific building's depreciation report, not the complex's reputation. https://vancouvernashdom.com/north-vancouver-pemberton-nv-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2
- **Lynn Valley's condo stock is a town-centre creation.** The District OCP names Lynn Valley Town Centre as the District's Municipal Town Centre, and the verified apartment stock (Parkside at Lynn's two 12-storey concrete buildings, Taluswood on Library Lane) sits inside that centre beside Kirkstone Park — outside the centre, Lynn Valley is detached houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **The two municipalities' incomes explain the two markets.** The 2021 Census median household income was $123,000 in the District and $86,000 in the City (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — the City is the attainable condo corridor, the District the detached market, under one search name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **TIME's strata has been spending on the podium, not just the lobby.** Current listings note the TIME strata has undertaken roof-deck and podium waterproofing and approved lobby renovations — in a 2004 concrete building over parking, podium waterproofing is the capital item that decides future levies, and here it is already underway. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/175-west-1st-street/305
