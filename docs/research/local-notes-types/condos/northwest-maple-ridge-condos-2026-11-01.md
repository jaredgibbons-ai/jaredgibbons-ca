## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59); the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This is the document that settles Westbrooke's unit count, bylaws and build record. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Westbrooke is a mid-2000s building and The Meadows is 1988 stock — both sit squarely in the age band where roof, piping and envelope work appears in those reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any older directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" for a complex here is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Northwest complex is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — RS-1 subdivision minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%, and the rural/agricultural zones carry much larger minimums (RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha, A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research files (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — do not treat either number as settled. The Lougheed Transit Corridor Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; City news release February 4, 2026) and its corridor — the city's western boundary to 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road — covers this sub-area's south edge, which is the policy frame for future strata supply here (see Section 5). The Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025, on the city's east side. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/northwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **ALR stratification bar and housing frame:** ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping for any specific lot. On ALR parcels, residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/northwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Floodplain:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Which Northwest lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/northwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — on non-ALR lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/northwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales — Westbrooke and The Meadows are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any future corridor presale product, not to the existing resale stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
