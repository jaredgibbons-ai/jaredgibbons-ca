## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These are the provincial and city rules that will govern any Brunette strata purchase if and when stock trades here; there is no in-area building for them to attach to today.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for any future Brunette building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Floodplain — the corridor rule:** the City's floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level, with the Brunette River running through it; the City's flood management page places sections of the mainland within the provincial floodplain, and lot-level status on the corridor is property-specific — confirm the flood construction level and an insurance quote before subjects come off on any creek-adjacent lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brunette.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area, and zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001. Single-detached homes sit under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series) — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.), floor space ratio not over 0.5. CORRECTION to the research file: the research file cites townhouse districts "RT-A and RT-B" — no RT-A/RT-B districts exist in New Westminster; the townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B, and RT-A/RT-B must never be cited. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brunette.md)
- **Frequent Transit Development Area:** the Sapperton and Braid stations area is one of the City's Frequent Transit Development Areas in Our City 2041 — station-proximate lots may carry different expectations than interior RS-1 streets; confirm the designation for the specific parcel at the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brunette.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility plus City water/sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brunette.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Brunette was verified in this pass, so this is the frame only if a developer contract ever appears here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5%. A corridor property's floodplain position can separately affect a lender's and insurer's view — no source checked publishes a Brunette lot-by-lot position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
