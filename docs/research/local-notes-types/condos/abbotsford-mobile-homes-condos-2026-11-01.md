# Abbotsford Mobile Homes — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Page position — read first.** This is a manufactured/mobile-home topic page laid over the whole City of Abbotsford, not a condo page. The core distinction: a home in a manufactured-home park sits on a rented pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA). Park rules are NOT strata bylaws, there is no strata corporation for a pad-rental home, and there is no Form B for a pad-rental purchase. The page also includes homes on owned land (freehold or bare-land strata), which are a different tenure again and are not apartment condos. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Park rules vs strata bylaws — leads this section:** the Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s.59, within 7 days, maximum $35 plus 25¢/page, depreciation report attached) is a strata document. A pad-rental park has no strata corporation to issue one; the park buyer's diligence is the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, the park rules, and the Manufactured Home Registry record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Pad-rent increase limits (MHPTA):** 3 full months' written notice, once per 12 months only, up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. No rounding up and no carry-forward of an unused portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **NO pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file.** No official source publishes an Abbotsford pad-rent figure, average or range. The live page's "$650 to $950/month" range and one card's "$782/month" line are UNVERIFIED site/listing copy, flagged as such, and are not adopted. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Assignment (MHPTA s.28):** a tenant may assign the site tenancy only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the Regulation), an order of the director, or authorization in the tenancy agreement; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The request runs on form RTB-10. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** the landlord must have all necessary permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Manufactured Home Registry + CSA decal:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular); a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification through an electrical inspection and approval label. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata only, for contrast):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the CRF. A pad-rental park has neither. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws — two regimes:** strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. A park's rental rule is NOT a strata bylaw and is not voided by that change. Only 55+ age bylaws are legal for strata (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01 exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion); a park's "55+" label is a park rule, and the feed's claim that most Central Abbotsford/Clearbrook parks are 55+ is UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **City frame — CONFLICT FLAGGED:** the current Abbotsford OCP is **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025)**; older OCP bylaw numbers in program files are stale. Zoning is Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400 (2014) per the research file, but no manufactured-home-park zone section was extracted: park-zone figures and park count are UNVERIFIED. Abbotsford's SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED on City pages and is not asserted here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a further 5% September 16, 2026. How a specific park home is assessed depends on its tenure and registration — a per-property check. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Live-page PTT claim NOT carried:** the page states PTT "does not apply" to a home on a rented pad. No Province PTT passage stating that treatment was opened in this program, so the claim is UNVERIFIED — have a lawyer or notary confirm it per purchase. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC, general):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%.
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is on this page; elsewhere, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s.18 requires deposits in trust.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is an Abbotsford park home a condo?** No. On a rented pad you own the home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA — no strata corporation, no strata fees, no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Abbotsford?** UNCONFIRMED — no official source publishes a figure, average or range, and the live page's $650–$950 range is UNVERIFIED site copy. The governing figure is the specific site's written tenancy agreement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up?** Once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, up to 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Can the park refuse my purchase?** Assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, a director's order, or agreement authorization) under MHPTA s.28; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: What happens if the park closes?** Under MHPTA s.42: permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation paid on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular); a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification before the home can be listed for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT on a pad-rental purchase?** UNVERIFIED in this program — the live page says no PTT applies, but no Province source stating that treatment was opened; confirm with a lawyer or notary for the exact structure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Are most Abbotsford parks 55+?** UNVERIFIED as a city-wide claim. Paradise Park and Georgian Park cards describe 55+ communities (listing text), and this program's standing record lists Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates as verified 55+ parks — confirm the specific park's rule in its own documents. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Is there a median price for Abbotsford mobile homes?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category; the Abbotsford figures in Section 1 are all-residential community context only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: What replaces the Form B?** The written site tenancy agreement, the park rules, and the Manufactured Home Registry record — the sale is only effective if registered with the Registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-mobile-homes.md)
