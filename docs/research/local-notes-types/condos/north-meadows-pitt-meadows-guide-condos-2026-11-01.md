## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For The Fairways (built 2007) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — but see the Pitt Meadows prohibition below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a city layer, separate from the void strata bylaws: a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide, including for the Pitt Lake cabins in the guide feed. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No source checked publishes a 55+ bylaw for The Fairways — see the in-entry position in Section 2; the sub-area's actual 55+ stock (Meadowland Village, Meadowlands co-op) is Central Meadows stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020). The North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026 (the guide page gives the adoption date as September 29, 2026), covers approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, with development to proceed gradually and further planning, technical studies and Council approvals required. In the rural north: A-1 General Agricultural subdivision minimum 8 ha, A-2 Large Lot Agricultural 16 ha, RR-2 4 ha, RR-3 2 ha, RR-4 1 ha, RS Large Lot Residential 0.2 ha; a garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/north-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Floodplain and ALR:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — the guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is superseded and retired here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes; the northern drainage areas (Alouette Pump Station catchment, Fenton, and the Pitt Polder catchment) are drained by floodboxes and pump stations, and the Codd Island Wetlands are undiked floodplain wetlands. The same Business Plan states 78% of the city's land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve; on ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application where one residence already exists when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/north-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The specific dike/drainage levy set for a given rural lot: UNVERIFIED in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/north-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Fairways is resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no residential presale in the sub-area was verified in the sibling research — the North Lougheed plan area is a future-development frame, not a marketed project. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
