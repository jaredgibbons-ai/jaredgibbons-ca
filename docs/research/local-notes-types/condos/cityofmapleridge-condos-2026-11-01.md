## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the Fraserview Village stock (1987–1997) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Maple Ridge may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Rosewood Court and Fraser Glen directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 — only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is lawful, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. That rule cuts two ways in this city: the Fraserview Village buildings are published as 55+ (confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B), while Canuck Plaza's "25+" label is VOID because no age other than 55+ can be a lawful strata age bylaw, and Haney Pioneer Village is a 60+ co-op whose age rule is a co-op rule, not a strata bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** zoning is Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019. The OCP citation is CONTESTED in program sources (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) — flagged here, never asserted as settled in this file; read the current City document for a specific property. Settled plan dates: updates to the OCP and the Hammond, Silver Valley and Town Centre area plans were adopted November 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025, to meet the Province's December 31, 2025 deadline for aligning local plans with provincial housing targets; the Silver Valley Area Plan is read as Section 10.3 of the Official Community Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md) and https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan: ADOPTED January 27, 2026** per the City record (City news release February 4, 2026; OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021, third reading and adoption January 27, 2026) — cited as adopted in this file. See Section 5 for the note on the earlier research pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md)
- **North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan:** the Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw was adopted June 23, 2026 (City announcement June 24, 2026), formalizing an approximately 500-acre economic hub in northeast Maple Ridge; the plan establishes future land use designations only and approves no construction or development projects itself. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md) and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **SSMUH and housing need:** based on the City's interim Housing Needs Report, Maple Ridge will need 27,778 new homes by 2041, and the fall 2025 OCP and area-plan updates include new provisions for small-scale multi-unit housing (3 to 6 units on single-detached lots) and increased density around transit hubs. SSMUH product is generally not strata condo product. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md) and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026 (the first business day in July), with a 5% late penalty on outstanding balances from July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on any balance outstanding on September 2, 2026; an unclaimed home owner grant counts as an outstanding balance. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/cityofmapleridge.md) and https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Inspire's completing buildings, remaining developer sales at Podium, Brickwater and Era), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 secondary date is superseded). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — directly relevant to Inspire (estimated completion January 2027 for Building 3) and any remaining developer contracts in the newer Town Centre projects. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
