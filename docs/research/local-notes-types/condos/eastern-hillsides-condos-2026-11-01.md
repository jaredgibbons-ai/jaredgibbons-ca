## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Eastern Hillsides is a hillside neighbourhood in southeast Chilliwack and a Neighbourhood Plan area, Schedule B4 of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). The plan was adopted in 2012, amended in 2017 to clarify development-approval requirements protecting future development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow, and updated a third time in 2025 as part of the OCP update. https://www.chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962
- Boundaries: west edge the Ryder Lake uplands, south edge Lookout Ridge and Elk Mountain, east edge the Cascade foothills / Fraser Valley Regional District boundary, and north edge the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1) and valley-floor farmland. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Internal roads named in City park listings include Allan Road, Rowanna Crescent and Marble Hill Road. Highway 1 forms the north edge; an interchange specific to the plan area was NOT confirmed on a City source in the research: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/eastern-hillsides.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: **it does not — there is no verified apartment-condo or townhome stock in this plan area at all (Section 2).** The established stock is freehold detached housing on individual lots, organized into development cells with unit allocations under the Neighbourhood Plan. Chilliwack's verified apartment stock sits elsewhere in the city (Chilliwack Proper / Garrison / Sardis, per earlier files in this series) and is NOT imported into this file. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- Anchors outside the plan area: there is NO school located inside the Eastern Hillsides plan area — the Neighbourhood Plan states this directly; the nearest City recreation centre is Cheam Leisure Centre, 45501 Market Way, in Sardis; the nearest library is Chilliwack Library, 45860 First Avenue; Chilliwack General Hospital is at 45600 Menholm Road and the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 – 7955 Evans Road, both on the valley floor. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why this section still matters with zero stock:** if a strata purchase does occur here (a bare-land strata lot or a future project), BC strata housing is governed by the Strata Property Act, with disputes at the Civil Resolution Tribunal — and the standard block below is the rule set that would apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B (Strata Property Act, s.59).** On any strata resale, the Form B states the unit's monthly fee, any amount owed, any special levy already approved, the CRF balance, parking and storage entitlements, and — since April 1, 2023 — a summary of the strata's insurance coverage. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **Minimum CRF contribution:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating fund must be contributed to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Chilliwack OCP and the plan area:** the current city-wide plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500; Eastern Hillsides is its Schedule B4 Neighbourhood Plan area. The 2050 OCP update replaced the plan's land-use designations with city-wide OCP designations, while the plan's development cells and unit allocations continue to regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Chilliwack zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) minimum lot area is set by the bylaw's slope table and ranges from 360 m² to 990 m² (minimum lot width 12 m, depth 20 m); R3 (Small Lot Residential) minimum lot area 360 m²; HR (Hillside Residential) minimum lot area 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases; RH (Rural Hillside) minimum lot area 8 ha. These detached-lot minimums are why apartment form is not the form of this area. The zone applying to any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — confirm per property. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024)** brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into Chilliwack's zoning. The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not Eastern Hillsides, but it shows Chilliwack's SSMUH rollout is not uniform city-wide. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Hillside hazard rules — the defining rules for this area:** steep slopes, main watercourses and ravines cross the plan area and the plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 amendments exist specifically to protect development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow, and Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported. The City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands. Hillside lots are also subject to the 2050 OCP's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5), under which a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295
- **GST on new construction:** 5% GST on new homes only. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Presales (REDMA):** disclosure statement plus 7-day rescission (later of signing or receiving disclosure), deposits held in trust. No presale project in this plan area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Chilliwack tax dates:** a 5% late payment penalty is applied for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026, per the City's property tax FAQ. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board boundary is a trap in this area.** Chilliwack is CADREB territory, not FVREB — anyone quoting an "Eastern Hillsides median" from a SnapStats FVR report is quoting nothing, because that report has no Chilliwack section. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/eastern-hillsides.md)
- **There is no school, recreation centre, library branch or bus route inside the plan area** — the Neighbourhood Plan and the City/BC Transit listings say so directly. Every service trip goes to the valley floor or Sardis. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Marble Hill has a development moratorium history.** Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported, and the City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands — check whether a lot sits in the moratorium area before counting on subdivision or a build. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2756/2017%2011%2021%20%20%2D%20%20OCP31%20RZ1130%20%20Full%20Council%20Report%20Marble%20Hill%20Area%2Epdf
- **The plan's unit counts still govern, even though its land-use labels changed.** The 2050 OCP update replaced the Neighbourhood Plan's land-use designations with city-wide designations, but the plan's development cells and unit allocations continue to regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **The area's parks are the amenity.** City listings include Allan Creek Park (51314 Rowanna Crescent), Forest Gate Park (51442 Allan Road), Marble Hill Natural Area (7515 Marble Hill Road), Allan Creek Trail, Chilliwack Community Forest (51642 Allan Road) and the Elk Mountain Trail. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1754&dowhat=locationView&plID=203
- **The historic wagon road is a protected heritage asset in the plan.** The Neighbourhood Plan identifies the historic wagon road (Browlee Road) for protection, and states wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Chilliwack's tax penalty is two-stepped in 2026:** 5% on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — budget the full notice on either penalty date. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
