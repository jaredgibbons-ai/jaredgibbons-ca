## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. **The Lee Building exception matters city-wide:** an undivided-interest / tenants-in-common unit has no strata and no Form B — see the Main bullet in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — every directory "rentals not allowed" line in any sub-area file is VOID for long-term rentals, as flagged in-entry in those files; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. That rule is live in this roster at Terraces on 7th (Fairview VW, verified 55+ ownership), OPAL by Element (South Cambie, MLS-stated "one resident must be 55+," mixed tenure flagged) and Tapestry at Wesbrook Village (University VW, 55+ resting on MLS wording only, Form B check still owed). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Vancouver property answers to the City of Vancouver's Zoning and Development By-law and its district schedules, applied per sub-area in this program's Vancouver files; at city-wide level in the research pass, the bylaw number and district-schedule detail are UNVERIFIED — no City zoning document was opened for the city-wide file, and none is invented here. Heritage areas (Chinatown, Gastown, Shaughnessy, Yaletown) carry City heritage regulations and design guidelines. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Tenure — the Vancouver trap, stated once for the whole city:** a large part of Vancouver's attached stock is not freehold strata. False Creek South is largely City leasehold / co-op / non-market (excluded in aggregate, Section 2); Champlain Heights is City prepaid 99-year leasehold plus co-ops (0 freehold counted); UniverCity is leasehold prepaid on SFU Community Trust 99-year ground leases and Tapestry at Wesbrook Village is leasehold strata on UBC land — never describe any of that stock as freehold. Tenure for any specific building not stated in its sub-area file: UNCONFIRMED — read the title. (Files named in Section 2.)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** the City collects property tax twice a year — advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026 (the second business day of February and of July respectively), with the last day to pay the 2026 main tax, claim the homeowner grant and complete a deferment application to avoid a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance being July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Empty Homes Tax (Vancouver only):** every Vancouver residential property owner must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under the City's Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a property with no declaration is deemed vacant, and a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The rate stated by secondary sources for the current reference year is 3% of assessed value (secondary source — the research pass did not state the rate on the City pages it read, so the rate is carried as secondary-sourced, not City-verified). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf and https://birdsnestproperties.ca/empty-homes-tax/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $800,000 (an arithmetic illustration only — no Vancouver median exists in the permitted source), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract purchase arises in Vancouver (new phases at Oakridge Park and River District product are the roster's presale-adjacent stock), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
