## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

There is no condo stock in Salmon River for these rules to attach to today. They are included because they govern any strata lot that does exist in the district, and because they explain why condo form is structurally unlikely on the district's farm land. (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/salmon-river-condos-2026-11-01.md)

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days; the maximum charge is $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a Salmon River property is offered as a strata lot, the Form B is the document that proves what the strata is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the only exemption that could plausibly touch a small rural strata here); and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A freehold house is not governed by strata rental bylaws at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No age-restricted building is verified in Salmon River. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning and SSMUH — Bylaw 6020:** rural land is under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (the district's farm and acreage homes sit in the rural zones, including RU-2; the RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage were not confirmed from a readable bylaw section: UNVERIFIED; zone assignments for a parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource). The Township's SSMUH rules under Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allow 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m², with lots over 4,050 m² ineligible for the multi-unit tiers and NO 6-plex tier in the Township; on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or one accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit, depending on zoning. The Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area (Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102). In Bylaw 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit under the zoning bylaw, never strata. On Salmon River acreages, that suite/accessory route — not a strata condo building — is the added-unit route the rules describe. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/salmon-river-guide.md)
- **ALR housing rules:** a principal residence on ALR land built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence; and the ALC permits one additional residence without an application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/salmon-river-guide.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/otter-district-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Water and septic (rural servicing frame):** rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS, the provincial database of registered wells and mapped aquifers, for a parcel's record, and note well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well — and Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/salmon-river-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Application of either program to a specific Salmon River acreage is UNCONFIRMED until eligibility is checked — acreage values and land use can change the analysis. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
