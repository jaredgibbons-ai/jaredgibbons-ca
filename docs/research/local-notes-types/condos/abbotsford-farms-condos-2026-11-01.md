## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR stratification bar — leads this section:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); see also Strata Property Act s.239 (subdivision) and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **ALC residence caps:** one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only when located within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local bylaws may be more restrictive, not more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **BC Assessment farm-class thresholds:** $2,500 of farm income for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must file a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **Form B (strata only, stated for contrast):** Strata Property Act s.59 — the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus 25¢/page, with the most recent depreciation report attached. A farm purchase has no strata and no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata only):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither applies to a farm parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata only):** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are legal, under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion. Neither rule creates condo stock on ALR land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **City plans — CONFLICT FLAGGED:** the current Abbotsford OCP is **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025)**. The research file cites "OCP Bylaw 2600-2016" and live pages have cited "2500-2016" — both are stale/conflicted against the 2050 adoption and are flagged in-file for Jerry. Zoning is under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400, per the research file; A1/A2 zone-by-zone specifics were not opened and are UNVERIFIED. Abbotsford's SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED on City pages — it is not asserted here, even though the research file asserts a number. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 — the March 12 secondary date is superseded): 100% of GST to a $1M price, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements signed March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. How GST is apportioned between farmland, buildings, residence and quota on a specific farm purchase: UNVERIFIED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I stratify an Abbotsford farm residence and sell the units as condos?** No — not on ALR land without ALC permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR under ALC Bulletin 09, s.6. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can the house on an ALR farm be?** A principal residence up to 500 m² total floor area (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), with a suite only inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm classification?** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha (>4 ha), or $10,000 (under 0.8 ha), with applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/abbotsford-farms.md)
