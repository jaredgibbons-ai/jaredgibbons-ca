## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals not allowed" line in the 3075 Skeena Street directory entry in Section 2 is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 3838 and zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 (2008): detached homes under RS1–RS4 (minimum lot widths from at least 9.5 m in RS4 to at least 30 m in RS3), townhouse zones RTh1–RTh3, and 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units permitted on RS lots without rezoning depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Flood:** land toward the Pitt River is low-lying and a significant portion of the city is within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplain behind dykes; lot-level status for Riverwood is UNVERIFIED — confirm the specific property with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes unpaid after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees billed separately, due March 31, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Fremont Green is resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No Riverwood presale project was verified on a developer source in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's 3% Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Riverwood is Port Coquitlam. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there condos in Riverwood, or just townhouses?** Mostly townhouses. One apartment-condo building is verified in-area — Fremont Green (550 Seaborne Place, Mosaic, 2014, freehold strata) — plus named townhouse complexes (Mountain View Estates, Dominion, Riverwood at 3075 Skeena Street). The research file's official-source pass confirmed no other condo buildings inside Riverwood. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is 553 Seaborne Place the same as Fremont Green?** No. 550 Seaborne Place is Fremont Green, the strata building you can buy into; 553 Seaborne Place is Mosaic's purpose-built rental building next door — its own site says its homes are exclusively for renters. https://rentatmosaic.com/fremont/
- **Q: Where is the rest of Port Coquitlam's condo stock?** In Central Pt Coquitlam (Montrose Square, Symphony at Gates Park, Lumiere, The POCO and four more cross-referenced) and Glenwood PQ (The Arden, The Sapphire, Portica West, Lexford by Polygon) — counted in those files, not here. (Central file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-pt-coquitlam-condos-2026-11-01.md) (Glenwood PQ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/glenwood-pq-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What did the Riverwood attached market do in September 2026?** Condos & townhomes combined: 21 listed, 8 sold, a 38% sales ratio — the strongest attached pace in Port Coquitlam that month. The Port Coquitlam community median for condos & townhomes was $660,000; sub-area medians are not published. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Q: The directory says rentals are not allowed at 3075 Skeena — can I rent my unit out?** That line is VOID for long-term rentals: BC voided strata rental-restriction bylaws on November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the registered bylaws on the Form B are the final word. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve a Riverwood condo or townhouse?** SD43 catchments are address-based; the locator run for 1260 Riverwood Gate returned Blakeburn Elementary, Minnekhada Middle and Terry Fox Secondary — the secondary school is in the neighbourhood. Run the building's exact address before relying on it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Q: Is Riverwood in the floodplain?** The Pitt River side is low-lying and much of the city is floodplain behind dykes; lot-level status is UNVERIFIED in the research file. For a strata purchase, the building's insurance and depreciation report carry the exposure — read both in the Form B package, and confirm the lot with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Q: Can an older Riverwood house lot hold more homes?** RS-zoned lots can hold secondary suites and 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Q: How do I get downtown or to SkyTrain?** TransLink buses connect the area to Port Coquitlam Station (West Coast Express) and Coquitlam Central Station (Millennium Line); specific route numbers through Riverwood are UNVERIFIED. Coast Meridian Road runs south to Lougheed Highway and the Pitt River Bridge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The same developer sells and rents on the same street.** Mosaic built both Fremont Green (550 Seaborne Place, strata, 2014) and the rental building at 553 Seaborne Place, and built Dominion's 114 rowhomes/townhouses at 2423 Avon Place in 2012 — a buyer comparing "Fremont" addresses has to establish which tenure each address is before comparing anything. https://rentatmosaic.com/fremont/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Riverwood's attached market outran its detached market in September 2026.** 38% sales ratio for condos & townhomes against 20% for detached (SnapStats GVR September 2026) — the townhouse-heavy stock is what moves here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **A complex named "Riverwood" inside Riverwood is townhouses, not the neighbourhood's condo building.** The 3075 Skeena Street complex (built 1995 per the directory) shares the sub-area's name; the one apartment-condo building carries a different name entirely (Fremont Green). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/riverwood-3075-skeena-street?prompt=login
- **The secondary school is the neighbourhood's namesake anchor.** Terry Fox Secondary at 1260 Riverwood Gate is named for Port Coquitlam's Terry Fox, who grew up in the city, and the locator run for its own address returns it as the catchment secondary — the school path (Blakeburn → Minnekhada → Terry Fox) is one of the few fully locator-settled paths in the PoCo sub-areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **PoCo advertises the lowest average taxes in Metro Vancouver — and bills utilities separately.** The City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver; utilities are flat annual water and sewer fees due March 31, separate from the July 2 tax due date, so a strata budget and a house budget carry the charge differently. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
