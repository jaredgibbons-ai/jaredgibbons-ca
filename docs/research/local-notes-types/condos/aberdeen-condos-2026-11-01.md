# Aberdeen (Abbotsford) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Headline fact: Aberdeen has no verified condo stock.** This file documents that plainly rather than padding it: Aberdeen is a rural, agricultural community of farm and acreage properties. The only strata housing confirmed anywhere in the board sub-area is one townhome complex at its western edge (Section 2). A buyer who needs a condo should be looking at Abbotsford West or Central Abbotsford.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Aberdeen" is both the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report and an official City of Abbotsford name — profile area 6, "Aberdeen," in the City's 2020 Demographic Profiles, separate from Bradner–Mt. Lehman (area 1) and West Abbotsford (area 7). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- What it is: the rural community around Bradner Road, south of Fraser Highway and west of Clearbrook, north of the Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands. Main roads are Bradner Road (north–south), Fraser Highway (north edge), Mt. Lehman Road to the west, and Huntingdon Road and King Road to the south. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- There is no town centre, transit node or multi-family corridor in Aberdeen: BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove/Abbotsford) runs along Fraser Highway at the north edge toward Bourquin Exchange, and no local route was verified running through the interior rural roads. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Where condo stock concentrates: nowhere in Aberdeen. The nearest verified condo concentrations are the Clearbrook corridor and Highstreet node in Abbotsford West, and the Gladwin Road / Historic Downtown cluster in Central Abbotsford — see the companion files abbotsford-west-condos-2026-11-01.md and central-abbotsford-condos-2026-11-01.md in this folder.
- The board data agrees the attached market here is negligible: Aberdeen recorded 7 attached listings and 0 attached sales in September 2026, against 25 detached listings — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/aberdeen-guide.md).
- Governing plan: there is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan; the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated) governs. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Aberdeen has NO condo stock — no apartment condominium building inside the sub-area was verified on any official, developer, strata or building-directory source checked for this file or for the area research file.**

- The single strata complex confirmed in the board sub-area is a townhome project, not condos: **Lantern Park — 27640 Lantern Avenue**, at the western edge of the sub-area by the Aldergrove border, built by the Reimer Group (developer source cited in the research file). Townhomes are strata, but they are not the apartment-condo product this page type covers; its unit count, year and restrictions are UNCONFIRMED from the source cited. https://www.readkong.com/page/built-27640-lantern-avenue-aldergrove-abbotsford-8573656
- Everything else in Aberdeen is freehold farm and rural residential land: the housing stock is overwhelmingly single-detached houses on rural and agricultural lots, with subdivision minimums of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One (A1) zone and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two (A2) zone — zoning that does not produce condo buildings. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Because there is no condo stock, this section covers (a) why the zoning/ALR framework prevents condos here, and (b) the provincial condo rules that apply the moment a buyer redirects to the nearest real condo stock in Abbotsford West or Central Abbotsford.

- **Why no condos — agricultural zoning and the ALR:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, Section 200 agricultural zones apply across rural Aberdeen (A1 minimum subdivision 8.0 ha; A2, 16.0 ha). Rural parcels are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve; subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings need City zoning compliance and, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, Agricultural Land Commission approval. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The one strata complex (Lantern Park townhomes) is still strata law:** purchases require the Form B Information Certificate package, and the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, projecting 30 years of costs with at least three CRF funding models; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata in BC may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ strata was verified in Aberdeen. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and SSMUH:** the Abbotsforward 2050 OCP (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated; new OCP adopted December 2025 — Section 5) governs, and in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial SSMUH and Transit-Oriented Areas legislation (brief names this as Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — number/date UNCONFIRMED on the City pages opened). SSMUH applies to lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use, which is not Aberdeen's agricultural zoning — no SSMUH condo supply is expected here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Second dwellings on farm lots (the Aberdeen substitute for "more units"):** the A1 zone permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot under the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². These are farm dwellings, not strata condos, and cannot be sold as separate strata lots. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Taxes on a condo purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford (for redirected buyers):** BC Property Transfer Tax is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above; the First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000, partial to $860,000), and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). New condos also carry 5% GST; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4; Royal Assent March 12, 2026 per sources checked — brief said March 13) returns 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate
- **Presales (REDMA), HBP/FHSA, CMHC — for redirected buyers:** presale contracts elsewhere in Abbotsford carry the REDMA s. 21 seven-day rescission right and trust-held deposits (s. 18); the Home Buyers' Plan allows $60,000 per buyer and the First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums run 0.60%–4.00% by loan-to-value, 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year surcharge applies. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo in Aberdeen?** No verified apartment condo exists in the sub-area. The only strata complex confirmed is the Lantern Park townhomes at 27640 Lantern Avenue on the western edge. Buyers set on a condo are redirected to Abbotsford West (Clearbrook, Highstreet) or Central Abbotsford (Gladwin Road, Historic Downtown). https://www.readkong.com/page/built-27640-lantern-avenue-aldergrove-abbotsford-8573656
- **Q: Why is there no condo building here — could one be built?** The land base is agricultural: A1/A2 zoning with 8.0 ha / 16.0 ha subdivision minimums and ALR rules on top. A multi-family condo rezoning on ALR farm land would need both City and, where required, Agricultural Land Commission approvals — no such project was verified in any source checked. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: Is a Lantern Park townhome bought the same way as a condo?** Largely yes — it is freehold strata, so the purchase uses the same Form B / strata documents package, depreciation report and CRF review as an apartment condo. The complex's own fees, restrictions and unit count are UNCONFIRMED from public sources and must come from its strata documents. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Could I buy a farm property and stratify it into condo-style lots later?** Not on ALR land as a workaround: added dwellings in the ALR are size-capped Accessory Farm Dwelling Units or suites tied to the farm parcel (90 m² / 186 m² caps), governed by the ALR Use Regulation and City zoning — they are not a route to separate strata titles for sale. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: If I buy the nearest condo instead (Clearbrook), can I rent it out?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws and any 55+ residency bylaw still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What about schools if I buy at Lantern Park's edge of Aberdeen?** Catchments are address-based (SD34 Policy 18). The locator run for 2975 Bradner Road (Aberdeen Elementary's own address) returns Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary; run the specific address before relying on it. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q: Is there transit I could rely on without a car?** Only at the edge: route 21 runs on Fraser Highway at the north edge toward Bourquin Exchange; no route through the interior rural roads was verified. Aberdeen is a drive-everywhere area, which is part of why it has no condo market. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- **Q: Does the airport affect Aberdeen property?** Yes for building: land inside the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations area (SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500) is subject to height limits, and a developer on AZR land must obtain NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals before the City issues a building permit. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- **Q: Are Aberdeen homes on city water and sewer?** Rural lots outside the urban boundary may use wells and onsite sewage instead of community water and sewer — a servicing model that also explains the absence of multi-family buildings. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: What taxes/fees should a redirected condo buyer budget in Clearbrook or downtown?** PTT at the BC rates (with first-time and newly-built exemptions as in Section 3), 5% GST on new construction only, strata fees plus the building's CRF health as shown on the Form B, and CMHC premiums of 0.60%–4.50% of the loan if the down payment is under 20%. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025** (Abbotsforward 2050; public hearing November 25, 2025), emphasizing more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enabling 37,185 units over 20 years — the priority areas are the urban nodes, not agricultural Aberdeen, so the new OCP does not signal condo supply coming to Bradner Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **The City's agriculture work (AgRefresh) entrenches the farm pattern:** the A1 Uplands / A2 Lowlands structure with 8 ha and 16 ha subdivision minimums is the City's stated framework for areas like Aberdeen — land assembly for a condo project runs against both the zoning and the ALR. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **The attached-sales count underlines the point:** Aberdeen's attached segment recorded zero sales in September 2026 (7 listings, sales ratio not reported) while its detached segment turned over 3 sales — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/aberdeen-guide.md).
- **The nearest growth is at Highstreet, not in Aberdeen:** the City's transit and housing investment concentrates at the Highstreet Transit Exchange and the South Fraser Way corridor in Abbotsford West — which is where a condo buyer priced out of, or curious about, Aberdeen will actually find buildings. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- **Aberdeen Elementary is the area's one school and its catchment chain is settled:** the SD34 School Locator run for the school's own address (2975 Bradner Road) returns Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary, matching Eugene Reimer's published feeder pattern — useful for the acreage buyers who are this area's real market. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
