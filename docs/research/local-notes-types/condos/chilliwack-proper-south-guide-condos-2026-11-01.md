## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Elmwood Terrace (1986) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141, adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968 — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Vibe's directory line reads "rentals allowed," which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Elmwood Terrace is presented as a 55+ community in its listings, and the Cottage Grove unit in the feed is described as 55+ gated product — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for either before treating a unit as generally occupiable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Chilliwack plan:** the city-wide plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, whose Schedule B2 is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covering this ground; the plan was first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533. Cite the schedules correctly: the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." The guide states the plan's designations as Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). One date is NOT adopted here: the guide states Bylaw 5500 "the City adopted on December 16, 2025" — that adoption date was not confirmed on a City page in this program: UNVERIFIED. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/chilliwack-proper-south-guide.md)
- **SSMUH and suites:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Under Bylaw 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s. 2); only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), garden suites capped at 100 m² (s. 4.06(1)); a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Flood:** as the guide states it, under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 4519, habitable floor space in areas protected by a standard dike must be at or above the applicable Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m of freeboard, and the City has an ongoing $30 million program to raise the dyke system to the current provincial elevation — the City's floodplain bylaw text and flood pages were not opened in the research pass, so the Bylaw 4519 specifics and the $30 million figure as stated are UNVERIFIED in this file; the parcel-level Flood Construction Level for any specific lot is likewise UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/chilliwack-proper-south-guide.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **ALR:** the guide states city-wide that 67% of Chilliwack's land (17,322 hectares) is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, matching the City figure carried in the research; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). ALR status for any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — parcel-by-parcel ALC map check. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all three Section 2 buildings trade as resale stock now (The Elliot is recorded Sold Out / Complete on its marketing page). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Chilliwack Proper South is named in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
