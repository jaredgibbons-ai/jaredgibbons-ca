## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase of a STRATA lot the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The trap on this page: the typical non-strata half duplex has NO Form B at all, because there is no strata — diligence runs on title, the co-ownership position and City permit/registration records instead. Only a strata-form half duplex produces a Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. A two-lot strata duplex sits below the five-lot depreciation-report threshold on the rule as stated, and a non-strata duplex has no CRF at all — either way, there is no strata reserve document to lean on for this page's product; the building's own condition is inspected, not read off a report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Note the limit of the rule on this page: it governs strata bylaws; a non-strata half duplex's rental position turns on City suite rules and any co-ownership agreement or registered covenant, not on a strata bylaw at all. The suite itself is rentable only as a lawful secondary suite (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ duplex product is named on this page in the research file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **What Surrey counts as a secondary suite (the rule that governs this whole page):** a dwelling unit that is an accessory use, with habitable floor area not exceeding 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex residential building, and not a separate strata lot — the current cap is the 40% rule in Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; a "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is not stated as a rule anywhere in this file. Only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and each application is reviewed for legal restrictions such as Land Use Contracts and Building Schemes. To be legal, the owner must meet the conditions of zoning, hold the necessary building permits, meet the BC Building Code, and pay the secondary suite utility and service fees. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.md)
- **City zoning and plans — Surrey rules:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 — a City corporate report records that on June 15, 2026, Surrey 2050 OCP received final adoption from Council. The former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning. SSMUH frame: Surrey adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning changes on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the new zoning does NOT retroactively legalize suites built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — this page's feed is resale duplex stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031, on new homes only; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is sold on this page — the feed is resale duplexes. Where a developer-contract purchase arises elsewhere, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/crescent-bch-ocean-pk.md)

- **Q: Is a half duplex with a suite a condo?** No. On this page's own identity call, the homes are listed in Paragon as "DUPXH 1/2 Duplex" under Residential Attached, and the typical card is a non-strata purchase with "NO STRATA FEES" in its text — an undivided-interest / fee-simple style purchase, not a strata lot. - **Q: The listing says "legal suite" — can I rely on that?** No. The page itself warns that a listing noting a suite is not proof the suite has permits, and no permit or registration record for a specific card property was opened in the research pass, so the permitted status of any individual suite shown is UNVERIFIED. - **Q: How big can the suite legally be?** Its habitable floor area cannot exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, it must sit within the duplex building, and it cannot be a separate strata lot. A suite over the 40% line falls outside Surrey's definition whatever the listing text says. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Can a half duplex have two suites?** No — only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted inside a coach house or garden suite. A card advertising both a legal suite and a second guest suite needs the second unit's status explained in writing before subjects come off. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Q: Does Surrey's SSMUH zoning legalize an existing unpermitted suite?** No. The City states the legislation does not provide retroactive legalization for suites built without the necessary building permits; owners must still go through the permit process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is there a duplex-only sales figure or median for South Surrey?** No. SnapStats publishes Detached and Condos & Townhomes splits only — September 2026 community medians were $1,628,000 detached and $700,000 for Condos & Townhomes — and no duplex-only figure exists in program sources. - **Q: What documents replace the Form B on a non-strata half duplex?** There is no strata package: diligence runs on the title (including the undivided-interest structure and any co-ownership agreement, easements or Building Scheme), the City's suite registration and permit records, and a building inspection. If the specific property IS a strata lot, then the Form B, registered bylaws and strata records apply as well — Surrey's suite pages do not address strata permission, per the page's own site copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.md)
