## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The attached stock in this scope is townhouse strata, but the same Strata Property Act rules apply to a townhouse strata lot as to an apartment condo lot.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the central diligence document for every complex in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in this scope may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Wherever a directory or listing line for a complex here says "rentals not allowed," that line is void in-entry; no such line was found for the complexes above — Brighton's building record instead records rentals as allowed, which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful in a strata; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. Two city-wide traps: Haney Pioneer Village is a 60+ co-op under co-operative law, NEVER a 55+ strata, and the SPA exemptions do not govern it; and Canuck Plaza's "25+" label elsewhere in Maple Ridge is VOID — no age other than 55+ can be a lawful strata age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — urban-fringe houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605: minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%), and the rural/agricultural zones carry much larger minimums (A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — flag it, never assert one. The Albion Area Plan update was adopted October 28, 2025. The Silver Valley Area Plan (OCP Section 10.3, per the guide page) covers the north part of this scope, and its update was adopted November 25, 2025; no plan is written for "North Maple Ridge" as a whole. The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; City news release February 4, 2026) — its corridor (the city's west boundary to 221 Street) lies south/west of this scope. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/north-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. On ALR parcels the provincial ALR rules also govern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/north-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales — the verified complexes here are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract (Hillside Homes was a presale collection; whether any unit remains on a developer contract is UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% (0.60% at LTV up to 65% to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no manufactured-home park inside this scope was verified; where a Maple Ridge park home is bought instead of a strata unit, pad rent may rise at most once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, by no more than the yearly limit plus a proportional amount — 2026 = 2.3% plus proportional, 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. No pad-rent dollar figure is published here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
