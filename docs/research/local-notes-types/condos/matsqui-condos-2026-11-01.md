- BC Geographical Names lists "Matsqui" as an official urban community in the City of Abbotsford. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/25776.html

- Máthxwi Elementary is at 33661 Elizabeth Avenue. https://mathxwi.abbyschools.ca/
- BC Transit Route 31 (Abbotsford/Mission) serves the area — 31A trips stop at Matsqui Village, running between Bourquin Exchange and Mission City Station via Junction Mall. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31

There is no town centre, tower corridor or transit node with apartment condos here — the land pattern is A1 Agricultural and RR Rural Residential lots (see Section 2). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These are the provincial condo rules a buyer would use if a strata unit were ever offered here, plus the Abbotsford rules that explain the local housing form.

- **Form B — Information Certificate (Strata Property Act, s.59).** The seller's strata must provide a Form B stating the monthly strata fee, any special levy already approved, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage entitlements and any judgments or proceedings; since April 1, 2023 the Form B must also include a summary of the strata's insurance coverage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports.** Strata corporations with 5 or more lots must obtain a depreciation report estimating repair/replacement cost and expected life of major items, renewed at least once every five years; a pre-July 1, 2024 strata in the Fraser Valley Regional District with no report must obtain one by July 1, 2026. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **CRF minimum contribution.** Effective November 1, 2023, when approving budgets at the AGM, strata corporations must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** Effective November 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — existing ones are invalid; stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2).** The only age bylaw a strata may still have is 55+; exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 cover younger spouses/partners, caregivers, and children in defined cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Abbotsford zoning / OCP.** Zoning Bylaw 2400 (2014) governs land use. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 The city-wide plan is Abbotsforward 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2600-2016 (as updated), whose rural-centres policy recognizes Matsqui Village among Abbotsford's seven historical rural centres. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH.** Abbotsford's SSMUH amendments (Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024) implement the Province's small-scale multi-unit housing rules in the Zoning Bylaw; the City's SSMUH application guide summarizes the Zoning Bylaw updates. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf SSMUH is a ground-oriented form (suites, duplexes, multiplexes) — it does not create apartment-condo zoning on A1/RR prairie land, and any ALR parcel is also subject to Agricultural Land Commission housing limits: generally one residence per parcel, principal residence capped at 500 m², an additional detached residence capped at 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on new construction.** New homes carry 5% GST; resale homes do not. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of GST on a new home up to $1M (max $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, max $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 — stated in the task brief; CRA page: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** General rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M and 3% above, plus 2% on residential value above $3M. The first-time buyer exemption covers tax on the first $500,000 (max saving $8,000) for homes up to $835,000, with a partial exemption to $860,000 and none at/above $860,000. The separate Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales / REDMA.** A presale buyer gets a disclosure statement and a 7-day rescission period under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, running from the later of signing or receiving the disclosure statement; deposits are held in trust. Assignments are governed by the contract — developer consent and a fee are commonly required. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA.** The RRSP Home Buyers' Plan allows $60,000 per person; the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC states premium rates range from 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount, and insurance is required when the down payment is less than 20% on a home priced $1.5M or less. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Local tax wrinkle specific to the prairie:** properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate dyking district levy on top of general municipal rates, per the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I actually buy a condo in Matsqui Prairie?** No verified apartment-condo building exists in the sub-area — attached inventory and sales were both 0 in September 2026, and the zoning is agricultural/rural-residential single-detached. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: If a strata unit appeared here, what would strata fees cover?** That is set by that strata's budget and disclosed on the Form B (s.59), which must state the monthly fee, CRF balance and, since April 1, 2023, a summary of insurance coverage. There is no Matsqui building whose fee/coverage can be quoted: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Could I rent out a strata unit here?** Provincially, long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What about pets?** Pet bylaws were not removed by the 2022 changes — they remain building-by-building in the strata's bylaws, disclosed with the Form B package. With no verified building here, any specific pet rule is UNCONFIRMED.
- **Q: How do I judge a building's finances?** Read the Form B for the CRF balance and approved levies, and the depreciation report for major-item timing; stratas with 5+ lots in the FVRD without a report had to obtain one by July 1, 2026, and must now contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **Q: Is there transit I can rely on?** Only bus service: Route 31 / 31A to Matsqui Village between Bourquin Exchange and Mission City Station. There is no rapid transit in or planned for the prairie in any source checked. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31
- **Q: Which schools serve the prairie?** Máthxwi Elementary is in the village; a locator run for its own address (33661 Elizabeth Ave) returned Máthxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary. Catchments are address-based — run the specific address in the SD34 School Locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q: Is flood risk part of buying here?** Yes. The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water entering Matsqui Prairie, and prairie properties pay the separate Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy — design protection is not a parcel-level guarantee, so confirm flood status with the City. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: Would a new-build condo here get the GST rebate?** A new home (any form) can qualify for the FTHB GST rebate — 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025 — if the buyer and home meet CRA conditions; resale homes get no GST rebate because no GST applies. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What insurance deductible should I worry about in a strata?** The strata's master policy deductible is disclosed in the Form B insurance summary (required since April 1, 2023); owners typically carry their own unit policy for deductible assessments. No Matsqui building exists to quote a deductible from: UNCONFIRMED.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The prairie's plan status protects the current form.** Abbotsforward 2050 (OCP Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated) recognizes Matsqui Village under its rural-centres policy as one of seven historical rural centres — there is no neighbourhood plan designating the prairie for apartment development in any source checked. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **AgRefresh changed farm rules, not housing form.** The City's agricultural review led to OCP Amendment Bylaw 3247-2022 and Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 3246-2022 — agricultural policy, not a condo or multi-family rezoning of the prairie. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city_hall/plan_for_200K/plan_for_200K_-_agrefresh.htm
- **No presale or new-construction condo project in Matsqui Prairie was verified** on a City development page or developer site in the Nov 1, 2026 research. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/matsqui-guide.md, §4)
- **Abbotsford tax dates changed the penalty pattern buyers may remember:** taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **The nearest recreation and library are outside the sub-area** — Matsqui Recreation Centre, 3106 Clearbrook Road, and Clearbrook Library, 32320 George Ferguson Way — which is the practical reality behind the "no condo stock" finding: services cluster south of the prairie, not in it. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **Provincial strata changes that would apply to any future strata here:** the 10% minimum CRF contribution (Nov 1, 2023), Form B insurance summaries (Apr 1, 2023), void rental bylaws and 55+-only age bylaws (Nov 24, 2022), and five-year depreciation-report renewal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
