## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. On any leasehold strata purchase (see the Seasons exclusion in Section 2), the lease documents matter as much as the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. The counted buildings here are 1992–1995 wood-frame stock — the depreciation report is the central document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — Parkgate Place's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" is read subject to that: any long-term rental restriction in the registered bylaws is VOID, while short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the District's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900 and zoning is Zoning Bylaw 3210. Parkgate is one of the District's six Village Centres, and the OCP's 2030 housing-mix target for the District is 55% detached / 45% attached. Under Zoning Bylaw 3210 as amended, most single-family lots may hold a main house, a secondary suite and a coach house; December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties, and in April 2026 Council voted against adopting the six-home near-transit tier — multiplex rules are in flux and must be checked lot by lot. The Seymour Local Plan separately contemplates townhouse development on assembled sites on the north side of Mount Seymour Parkway (3200–3500 blocks) at 0.6–1.2 FSR. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas:** slope and creek hazard DPAs apply across the District; a property-level GeoWeb hazard report is standard diligence for Seymour lots near the river, a creek ravine or a steep bank, and can trigger geotechnical reports for renovations and rebuilds. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **District of North Vancouver property taxes:** Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026; the published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value across all taxing authorities; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. Utility fees (water, sewer, drainage, solid waste) are billed separately. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the counted buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No Seymour NV presale was verified on a developer source in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's 3% Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Seymour NV is the District of North Vancouver. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board and the District draw different maps here.** MLS data files the Parkgate condos under "Northlands" and Raven Woods under "Roche Point," while the District's own Seymour community and Local Plan take in Parkgate Village. A buyer searching only "Seymour NV" on a listing portal will miss the sub-area's counted condo stock entirely. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md) and Section 2 entries.
- **The biggest condo complex near Seymour is the one you cannot buy freehold.** Seasons at Raven Woods (266 units per its MLS data sheet) is larger than the counted Parkgate buildings, and it is First Nations leasehold — the tenure, not the building, is the story, and it is why this file's count is 2 and not 3. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85043/FullPublic.pdf?i=949371375
- **Parkgate's condos were built for downsizers before downsizing was a program.** The 1992–1995 Banff Court and Parkgate Avenue units run to house-sized floorplans (listings record 2-bedroom units of 1,100–1,600 sq ft) directly across from the community centre, library and Safeway — the amenity set is the village, not the building. Section 2 entries; shopping centre detail: (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **The District voted against the six-home tier.** In April 2026 Council declined the six-home near-transit SSMUH tier while December 2025 ground-oriented rules allow up to four homes on 4,000+ eligible lots — Seymour's detached lots carry real but capped multiplex value, and the cap is District policy, not just provincial law. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **Winter is a showing topic at the top of the Parkway.** Upper Seymour streets sit on the snow line below the Mt Seymour ski area (39 runs, 12 lit for night skiing, inside the provincial park), and the Province requires drivers to obey winter tire and chain signs on designated routes from October 1 to April 30. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
- **The golf course across from the condos is public and District-owned.** Northlands Golf Course (3400 Anne Macdonald Way, par 71, Les Furber design, open since 1996/97) is a municipal course — the view amenity for Parkgate Place carries no private-club membership structure behind it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/seymour-nv.md)
