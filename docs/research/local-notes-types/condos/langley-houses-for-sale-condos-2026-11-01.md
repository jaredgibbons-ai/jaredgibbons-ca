## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s.59 — Form B Information Certificate (strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page — the diligence document for any strata unit surfaced near this page, including a bare-land strata house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in Section 2 wherever a directory line says "rentals not allowed" (Parkway Estates, Parkway II): VOID for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; short-term rental rules in either Langley municipality are UNVERIFIED — no municipal STR rule was read on an official page in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children (SPA Regulation 7.01 exemptions) — live in the cross-referenced roster at the three Langley City buildings and Waterfront at Bedford Landing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Municipal frame for houses (this page's core rules):** Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — an eligible lot (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible; there is NO 6-unit tier in the Township. Township secondary suites are capped at 120 m² (Zoning Bylaw s.102 as amended, including by Bylaw #6147, July 21, 2025) — with a documented conflict carried in the program between the Township's January 2025 suites guide and the July 2025 s.102 text on suites in townhouses/rowhouses, both documents reported by date. City SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, or 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. City suites under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (cite the number; adoption date UNVERIFIED, conflicted in sources): the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of gross floor area, registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4); carriage/garden ground-floor unit capped at 100 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Willoughby plan frame (Township context):** the Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979) and is now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); the Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY — never describe them as adopted. (Standing correction for this program.)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Worked example (inputs stated): PTT on the detached median $1,345,000 with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,145,000 ($22,900) = $24,900. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is the subject of this detached page; where a presale arises in the cross-referenced roster (Matteo, Walnut Park), REDMA s.21 (7-day rescission) and s.18 (deposits in trust) are the frame. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. Worked example (inputs stated): a $1,345,000 purchase is above the $1.5M insured-mortgage ceiling's lower bands in part — no premium figure is worked here because the applicable CMHC band depends on the down-payment tier for the specific mortgage; the published range only is stated. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Property taxes:** the City of Langley's 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a penalty on payments received after the due date (rate not stated on the City page read); the City's penalty percentage and the Township's 2026 due date and penalty are UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What counts as a "house" on this page?** Detached houses in the City and the Township — one house on its own lot, at any price — with acreage and farms left off by the page's own definition, though large-lot cards appear in the visible feed, so treat the exclusion as the page's stated intent rather than a verified filter. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-for-sale.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: What did Langley detached houses sell for in September 2026?** The Langley community (City + Township combined) detached median was $1,345,000, on 449 listings and 64 sales, at $482/sq ft, 97% sale-to-list and 20 days; across the 13 months to September 2026 the detached median ranged from $1,315,000 (August 2026) to $1,455,000 (January 2026), with 902 detached sales in total. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How many units can an eligible Township lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 6020: 3 units for lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units for lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots over 1 acre are not eligible, eligibility also requires the Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation and no Transit-Oriented Area location, and there is no 6-unit tier. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How many units can a City of Langley house lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284, RS1/RS2 lots permit up to 4 units, or 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — a different answer from the Township's, which is why the municipality check comes first. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** Township: 120 m² maximum (s.102). City: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with the registered owner residing on the lot (Bylaw 3300, s.2.4). Verify against the right municipality's bylaw before marketing a suite. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Which school district serves Langley houses, and how are catchments set?** SD35 Langley for both municipalities; catchments are address-based. Recorded locator runs exist only for Aldergrove, Salmon River and Willoughby sample addresses — confirm any other address with the SD35 School Locator before relying on a school claim. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Why does the same Langley house face a different month in different sub-areas?** Tempo differs sharply: Walnut Grove detached sold at a 30% ratio (13 sales on 43 listings) against Brookswood's 7% (7 on 98) in September 2026 — price off the detached community figures and read the sub-area tempo, not just the median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-houses-for-sale.md)
- **Q: If I want a condo instead, where is Langley's verified stock?** In the four clusters cross-referenced in Section 2 — Langley City (3 buildings), Willoughby Heights (11 projects), Aldergrove Town Centre (5) and Fort Langley (3), 22 counted in their home files — with the combined Condos & Townhomes median at $611,000 in September 2026 (combined, not condo-only). Sources: Section 2.
