## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

There is no verified strata stock in Websters Corners today; this block is the standard BC frame a buyer needs if strata product ever appears here, and for the strata purchases buyers from this area most often make in neighbouring sub-areas.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Form B exists for a Websters Corners acreage house — if a listing here claims strata documents, that is a classification error to resolve before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Wherever a directory or listing line for a strata property says "rentals not allowed," that line is void. For Websters Corners itself the operative rental rule is different: a secondary suite in an acreage house is governed by the zoning and ALR rules below, not by strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful in a strata; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No strata exists in this sub-area to carry such a bylaw. Two city-wide traps: Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge is a 60+ co-op, NEVER a 55+ strata; and Canuck Plaza's "25+" label is VOID — no age other than 55+ can be a lawful strata age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs, with the rural minimums in Section 1 (RS-2 0.4 ha; RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha; A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — flag it, never assert one. The OCP update was adopted November 25, 2025 and the Albion Area Plan update on October 28, 2025; no area plan for Websters Corners was found in the sibling pass. The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan — Council approved the OCP amendments creating a plan area on both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres, per the City release) — touches this area's northwest side; it establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction. The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; City news release February 4, 2026) — its corridor (the city's west boundary to 221 Street) lies far west of Websters Corners. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/websters-corners-guide.md)
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation also governs — one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31), and an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met. This — not any strata rule — is the added-unit regime that actually applies in Websters Corners. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/websters-corners-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales; there is no new strata construction verified in this sub-area. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — no presale project in this sub-area was verified, so this applies only if a developer project is ever marketed here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% (0.60% at LTV up to 65% to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no manufactured-home park inside Websters Corners was verified; where a Maple Ridge park home is bought instead of a strata unit, pad rent may rise at most once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, by no more than the yearly limit plus a proportional amount — 2026 = 2.3% plus proportional, 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. No pad-rent dollar figure is published here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
