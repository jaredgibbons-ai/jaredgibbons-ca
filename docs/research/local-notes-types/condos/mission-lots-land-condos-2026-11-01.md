## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Read this section as the rules that decide whether land can ever become condo stock, plus the standard strata block a land buyer will meet the moment a strata is created on a neighbouring or future parcel.

- **The ALR stratification bar — the core condo rule for this topic.** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6); a strata plan deposit is itself a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and subdivision of ALR land needs Agricultural Land Commission permission (Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — the ALC's bulletin adds that a lease of part of an ALR parcel for more than 3 years is itself a subdivision requiring the Commission's permission. On ALR parcels the housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with a secondary suite only if entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit suites. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A vacant lot has no Form B; the document matters here when a buyer compares a lot purchase against a resale in an existing Mission strata, where the Form B is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. A developer creating a new strata on Mission land creates these obligations with the strata — they are part of the carrying-cost picture a builder-buyer is really pricing. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — a future strata on this land could not lawfully carry a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, and no existing Mission strata may enforce one; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion) — the only age bylaw a new strata on a Mission lot could adopt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Two planning systems on one page:** parcels inside the City of Mission follow City planning — Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 with the same vision, framework and mapping plus a Regional Context Statement; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 — while parcels in Lake Errock, Dewdney Deroche, Durieu and the Hatzic fringe follow FVRD electoral-area plans (Areas C, G and F). The specific subdivision requirements, servicing standards and approval steps for any individual parcel in either system: UNVERIFIED in the research pass — confirm with the approving authority for the PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Suites on a future build, City side:** all secondary suites in Mission must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, and a new suite goes through the building permit process. Feed claims (a UC372 secondary-suite claim, an RR7s coach/garden-home claim, a "PLA APPROVED FOR DUPLEX" claim) are listing text only: UNVERIFIED against the City bylaw. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Servicing:** feed cards variously claim fully serviced city lots, municipal water with septic prepped, drilled well and approved septic in place, and completed perc/geotechnical/RAPR work — every one is listing text for that parcel only. Parcel-level servicing status, connection costs and development cost charges (DCCs) for any lot on this page: UNVERIFIED — no municipal servicing or DCC schedule was opened in the research pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Property taxes, two systems on one page:** City of Mission lots — 2026 taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw). Rural (FVRD) lots — the owner receives the notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. Vacant land is taxed under the same due-date frames; any vacant-land-specific rate or speculation/vacancy tax application to a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **GST and purchase taxes on land and new builds:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales; how GST applies to a specific vacant-lot purchase depends on the seller's and the parcel's status — take conveyancing advice per parcel. For qualifying new homes built on the lot: the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 (CRA News No. 122: Royal Assent March 13, 2026; secondary sources saying March 12 are not followed — CRA governs); the GST New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum across the $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption a buyer of a newly built home on this land would claim. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A raw-lot purchase is not a REDMA presale; a developer's pre-construction sale of homes to be built on assembled Mission land is — stated so a buyer does not assume a 7-day rescission exists on a land contract. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% — the published range is 0.6%–4.5% — plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies; land and construction financing terms differ from a completed-home mortgage, so confirm the facility with the lender. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA), where a buyer compares a lot against a park home instead:** pad-rent increases are capped at 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. No park is lots-and-land stock — the line is here only because the two topics compete for the same entry-level buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a median price for Mission lots and land?** Not in the permitted source: SnapStats publishes no land-only split — its Mission figures cover dwellings only (September 2026 detached median $814,000; Condos & Townhomes median $430,000) — so no land count, share or median is stated here, and a lot must be priced on comparable lot sales for its own sub-area and servicing state, not on dwelling medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I buy an ALR lot and build strata condos or townhomes on it?** No. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6), a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (SPA s. 239), and ALR subdivision needs Commission permission (ALCA s. 21(1)). The ALR housing frame is one principal residence to 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size — a house-and-second-residence frame, not a multi-unit strata frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Near Mission's strata stock, what should a land buyer check before assuming condo-style potential?** Three things, in order: the governing authority (City vs FVRD — the approval path, servicing standards and tax regime all change with it), the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps by PID (the stratification bar above), and the actual zone text for the PID — because every density, suite and coach-home label on this feed (Silverdale designation language, RR7s, UC372, "PLA approved for duplex") is listing text, UNVERIFIED against the bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Are all the listings on this page inside the City of Mission?** No — the first page alone spans six board sub-areas, including Lake Errock (FVRD Area C), Dewdney Deroche (Area G) and Durieu (Area F), which are unincorporated FVRD ground with rural taxes paid to the Province, alongside City lots that pay City taxes. Establish which government approves the build before anything else. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)

City lots: pay the City — due July 2, 2026, flat 10% penalty on outstanding current balances on July 3, 2026, plus diking levies where they apply. FVRD lots: pay the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes Office — due July 2, 5% on amounts unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid October 31. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-lots-land.md)
- **Q: If I build a new home on the lot, what first-buyer help exists?** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (100% of GST to $1 million, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031), the BC Newly Built Home Exemption (PTT fully exempt to $1.1 million, partial to $1.15 million), the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000) and the First Home Savings Account ($40,000 lifetime) — each with its own eligibility rules; confirm the build contract's structure qualifies before counting any of them. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does buying land next to an existing condo complex give me any rights in that complex?** No. A neighbouring strata's amenities, parking and common property belong to that strata corporation's owners; adjacency confers no use rights, and the neighbouring building's Form B, fees and bylaws describe that building only. What adjacency does give you is a comparable: the neighbouring strata's fee level and depreciation position show what strata carrying costs look like on the same ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
