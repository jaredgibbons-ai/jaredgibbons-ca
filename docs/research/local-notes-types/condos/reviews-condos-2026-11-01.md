## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document a condo buyer who arrives from this reviews page will rely on — the reviews page itself contains no strata information about any building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. This is a strata rule only: a manufactured-home park's no-rentals rule is a park rule under the MHPTA and is NOT voided by that change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A park's "55+" label is a park rule, not an SPA bylaw — the regimes stay separate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Regulator — the frame a reviews reader is actually hiring under:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), which states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and work for only the buyer or the seller in a transaction (dual agency prohibited in B.C. in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/reviews.md)
- **Testimonial-display rules:** any rule, board standard or platform policy governing how a REALTOR® may display client testimonials on his own website — attribution, consent, or whether a selection may be shown without the full set — is UNVERIFIED in this file: no BCFSA, board or platform policy page on testimonial display was opened in this pass, and none is described here from industry habit. - **City zoning and plans:** no single municipality governs this page — its intro spans Surrey (Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; the replaced OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current) and Langley (Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, cited by number only) among others. Zoning for any specific purchase is that property's own municipality's bylaw — UNCONFIRMED from this page. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a condo purchase is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
