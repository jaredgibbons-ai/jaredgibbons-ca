## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These rules govern the Greentree Village townhouse strata directly (townhouse strata are strata corporations under the same Act), and any apartment purchase a buyer makes in a neighbouring Burnaby sub-area instead.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Greentree Village this is phase-specific: each "Greentree Village" phase is its own strata corporation, so the Form B, depreciation report and CRF of the phase containing the unit — not a neighbouring phase with the same name — govern the purchase. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. For 1970s townhouse phases, the depreciation report is where envelope, road and recreation-facility renewal costs surface — read the phase's own report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No usable source checked publishes a rental restriction for any Greentree Village phase — if a phase's older bylaw text or a directory line surfaces a "rentals not allowed" statement, it is void for long-term rentals and must be read that way in Section 2's terms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Greentree Village phase is published as age-restricted on a usable source — UNCONFIRMED either way until the phase's registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning (Burnaby):** the current Burnaby Zoning Bylaw is the Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026); the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is repealed — do not cite it as current. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict, and the City's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing district (from June 2024) lets surrounding house lots hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — that SSMUH permission applies to the freehold house lots around the village, not to the strata townhouse parcels themselves. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/greentree-village.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Greentree Village phases are 1970s resale stock, so GST is not in a typical purchase here. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to 1970s resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No market residential presale inside Greentree Village is verified — purchases here are resale/Form B transactions. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in the exemplar files.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the published bands (the stated range is 0.6%–4.5%), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Burnaby tax timing:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/greentree-village.md)
