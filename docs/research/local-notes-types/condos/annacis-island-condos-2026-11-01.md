# Annacis Island — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Annacis Island is an island in the Fraser River in the City of Delta, in the Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge corridor; street access is by Derwent Way, Cliveden Avenue, Eaton Way and Audley Boulevard. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- The current Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), treats Annacis Island as an industrial area; it is not one of the residential neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere on the island. There is no residential condo stock to map — see Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- Nearby areas are Nordel and the rest of North Delta to the south across the Alex Fraser Bridge, New Westminster to the north, Richmond to the west, and the Tilbury industrial lands to the south-west. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Transit is industrial-edge only: TransLink route 340 runs Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Highway 91, the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Cliveden Interchange, serving the island's industrial area edge; no bus exchange on the island itself was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero residential condo stock — stated plainly: no apartment condo building, townhome complex or other residential strata was confirmed on Annacis Island in any source checked, and the research file confirms essentially no housing of any kind on the island.** https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

- What exists instead is the Annacis Business Park: freehold industrial lots and strata industrial units, plus port-terminal land. Strata industrial units are NOT condos in the residential sense — they are warehouse/industrial strata lots, and they are not counted as condo stock in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- The island's named anchors are industrial, not residential: the Annacis Auto Terminal at 820 Dock Road, a Port of Vancouver terminal operated by Wallenius Wilhelmsen, and Metro Vancouver's Annacis Island Wastewater Treatment Plant. https://www.portvancouver.com/project/annacis-auto-terminal-optimization-project and https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Annacis Island sub-area row — its Delta coverage is the North Delta community and its four residential sub-areas only — which is consistent with there being no residential market to report. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- No public school, City park, recreation centre, library branch, shopping centre or grocery store on the island was confirmed in official sources. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why there is no condo stock — the city layer:** the Delta OCP 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400) names Annacis Island with River Road East, Tilbury Industrial Park and Roberts Bank as where Delta's industries are primarily located, and the island carries an Industrial designation in that plan; Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares in Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Zoning:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 applies to island parcels, but the industrial zone names and minimum lot standards for Annacis parcels were UNVERIFIED from a City zone sheet in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- **No lettered area-plan schedules:** the current OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024); the former area plans sat under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950, and there is no separate Annacis Island area plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Strata law still applies — to industrial strata:** where a strata plan is filed on the island, the Strata Property Act framework applies — owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation — and on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years — this applies to industrial stratas as well as residential ones. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental and age bylaws (for completeness):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), and the only age-restriction bylaw the Strata Property Act (ss. 123.1/123.2) permits is a 55+ bylaw — neither creates any residential use on industrial land, and neither changes the island's zero residential stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Buyer taxes (general BC layer, for buyers redirected to residential areas):** 5% GST applies to new homes only, not resales; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12; CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above; the First-Time Home Buyers' Program exempts the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000), and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 and partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos on Annacis Island at all?** No. No residential housing mix — no houses, condos or townhomes — was confirmed on the island; the OCP designation is industrial. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: I saw strata units for sale in the Annacis Business Park — are those condos I can live in?** No. Those are strata industrial units (warehouse/industrial bays). Strata title is a form of ownership, not a residential use; the island's land use is industrial and port-terminal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Could a live-work unit exist there?** No live-work or residential unit on the island was confirmed in any source checked: UNCONFIRMED — and the OCP treats the island as industrial, so do not assume any unit carries a residential use without a City zoning confirmation for that parcel. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Where is the nearest residential condo stock?** The nearest verified condo concentrations are covered in their own files in this series (Nordel and Scottsdale, across the Alex Fraser Bridge in North Delta); no source checked names a specific nearest building to the island, so any "closest building" claim is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- **Q: Is there a school catchment for the island?** No public school on the island was confirmed, and the island has no residential population to catchment; SD37 catchments are address-based through the SD37 School Locator for residential addresses elsewhere in Delta. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Can I get there by transit if I work on the island?** Route 340 serves the industrial area edge via the Cliveden Interchange between Scottsdale Exchange and 22nd Street Station; no bus exchange on the island itself was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- **Q: Does the strata depreciation-report rule affect an industrial bay purchase?** Yes — stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, and that duty is not limited to residential stratas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What about flood risk?** The island is riverfront industrial land surrounded by Fraser River channels; the City of Delta is the diking authority with over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure citywide, and parcel-level Flood Construction Level and covenant detail is UNVERIFIED and must be confirmed per property. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The auto terminal got bigger, not quieter:** the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority's Annacis Auto Terminal Optimization Project consolidated the Annacis and Richmond auto terminals into the Annacis facility, enabling it to handle up to 480,000 vehicles annually — a 36% increase in capacity — with on-terminal works complete in early 2025. https://www.portvancouver.com/project/annacis-auto-terminal-optimization-project
- **Two approval regimes share one island:** island parcels can be City-zoned industrial land or Port of Vancouver terminal land — the auto terminal at 820 Dock Road is designated "Port Terminal" under a port authority project permit (PER-21-019, issued September 26, 2022) — and the approval path differs by regime. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf
- **The region's wastewater plant is the other permanent neighbour:** Metro Vancouver lists Annacis Island among its secondary wastewater treatment plants, serving the Fraser Sewerage Area — a permanent, high-activity industrial neighbour for any unit on the island. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- **Delta holds a disproportionate share of the region's industrial land:** the OCP cites 1,527 hectares of industrially zoned and designated land in Delta, approximately 13% of Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory — policy pressure runs toward keeping islands like this industrial, not converting them to housing. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **BCIT has a campus on the island — and still points students off-island for housing:** BCIT's Annacis Island Campus page lists off-campus housing resources and hotels in surrounding areas (New Westminster, North Delta, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam) rather than any on-island accommodation. https://www.bcit.ca/housing/off-campus-housing/annacis-island/
