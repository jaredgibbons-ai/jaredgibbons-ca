## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Edgewater Estates has no Form B** — it is undivided interest, not strata (Section 2); a buyer there needs the co-ownership documents instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older Lynnmour strata bylaw purporting to restrict long-term rentals is void as a restriction (relevant in a neighbourhood with a large Capilano University tenancy pool); stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023, per Strata Property Regulation 7.01) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Lynnmour strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan is **OCP Bylaw 7900**; the zoning bylaw is **Zoning Bylaw 3210**. Lynnmour's south end is part of the Lynn Creek Town Centre, one of the four centres where the OCP directs 75–90% of residential growth. Zoning Bylaw 3210 (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots; December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties; Phibbs Exchange and the Capilano University area were designated Transit-Oriented Areas under Bylaw 8679 (2024). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- **District of North Vancouver property taxes:** council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026 (2.03% services + 1.5% infrastructure renewal); the published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value across all taxing authorities; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. District utility fees are billed separately. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- **Hazards:** the low ground between Lynn Creek and the Seymour River is where flood and creek-hazard mapping matters most; the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level hazard reports through its GeoWeb viewer — pull the report for any low-lying lot before subject removal. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to any remaining developer sale in the town-centre towers). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (Any page stating "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% — that compression is wrong as a rate statement.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given here because no verified Lynnmour median exists to use as an input. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (e.g., future Seylynn Centre sales), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The secondary catchment correction matters for resale copy.** An earlier research draft named Sutherland Secondary as Lynnmour's catchment secondary; the SD44 locator returns Windsor Secondary for the in-area sample address (1491 Hunter Street). Listing copy that promises "Sutherland catchment" for a Lynnmour home would be wrong on the only locator evidence in hand. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- **Lynnmour Elementary is an end-of-life building with an active replacement campaign.** SD44's November 2025 letter to the Minister states the current school dates to 1973, enrolment reached 256 in September 2025, and the district's capital plan seeks a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds — a material fact for families pricing an Inter River or town-centre home off the school. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **Apex's age disagrees with itself in public sources — the towers are newer than one directory says.** Current listings and building records put Apex at 2024 (276 homes, EPS9690); an older directory line giving a 2017 completion is stale. The same directory family gives Compass two different completion years (2016 vs 2018). Settle age-dependent questions (Newly Built Home Exemption, depreciation timing) from the strata plan and Form B, not from a directory. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **The recreation centre is brand new and was developer-delivered.** The Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre opened February 19, 2025, delivered as one component of Intergulf's Hunter at Lynn Creek development along the southern edge of Seylynn Park — the neighbourhood's main indoor hub is newer than most of its condo stock, and it arrived as a development amenity, not a standalone civic project. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation
- **"Lynnmour seniors housing" in search results is almost always Lynn Valley.** The Whiteley Court Kiwanis campus (Lynn Manor plus the 106-unit Lynn Woods beside it) is the North Shore's big subsidized seniors cluster — and it is in Lynn Valley, not Lynnmour. The two neighbourhoods share the "Lynn" name and little else for housing purposes. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/lynnmour-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **The reserve at the end of Lillooet Road is a water-supply landscape, not a normal park.** The Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve is 5,668 hectares managed by Metro Vancouver primarily for water supply, with more than 100 km of trails and the stocked Rice Lake fishing lake, drawing over 900,000 visitors a year — the trail network Lynnmour residents use daily starts just past Capilano University. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx
- **Two Lynnmours price differently.** Town-centre product near Mountain Highway prices off the Lynn Creek growth plan (OCP Bylaw 7900's growth-centre direction), while Inter River detached prices off quiet residential streets — do not comp one against the other. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
