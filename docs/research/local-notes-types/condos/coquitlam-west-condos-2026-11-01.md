# Coquitlam West — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Coquitlam West is the City of Coquitlam's south-west side; the City plans the same ground as Southwest Coquitlam, covering the neighbourhoods around the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre SkyTrain stations and along Austin Avenue. Exact MLS sub-area boundary lines are UNVERIFIED — the City describes the planning area by its station areas and the Austin Avenue corridor, not by boundary roads. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- Main roads serving the area: North Road, Clarke Road, Austin Avenue and Lougheed Highway; Burnaby (Lougheed / Cariboo) sits across North Road to the west and Central Coquitlam to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: almost entirely at the two station nodes — the Whiting Way / Clarke Road tower cluster between Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations (567 Clarke & Como, Lougheed Heights, Marquee, Precidia, Elements at Burquitlam, Brookside Manor) and the North Road tower at Burquitlam Station (The Burquitlam Capital). The Austin Avenue and interior streets remain freehold houses. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- Strata is the volume market here: in September 2026 the sub-area recorded 359 condos & townhomes listings (35 solds, 10% sales ratio) against 76 detached listings (2 solds). At the Coquitlam community level that month, condos & townhomes posted a median sale price of $625,000, $687/sqft, 96% sale-to-list and 23 days on market (SnapStats GVR September 2026 report — community-level figures only; SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- Transit anchors: Burquitlam Station (Millennium Line, Evergreen Extension) and Lougheed Town Centre Station (Millennium and Expo Lines). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any older directory or listing line saying rentals are banned in a Coquitlam West building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm any age bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** small-scale residential zones are R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996; the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, and lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules. The Transit-Oriented Areas Update and the adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors OCP and Zoning amendments create medium-density housing and townhousing opportunities around the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Watercourse permit area:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required for land within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, with Zoning Bylaw s. 519 flood-protection setbacks and s. 523 riparian rules behind it — relevant to strata capital planning in slope-side buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a first sale at Elements at Burquitlam), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Elements at Burquitlam and to the Burquitlam Park District presale in Section 5. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Burquitlam or Lougheed — where is each tower, actually?** Both station areas sit inside this one sub-area: The Burquitlam Capital (691 North Road) is at Burquitlam Station, and the Whiting Way / Clarke Road cluster (Lougheed Heights, Marquee, Precidia, Elements, Brookside Manor, 567 Clarke & Como) sits between Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations — a current Marquee listing describes that position expressly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md) and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/652-whiting-way/1704
- **Q: How new is the condo stock here?** Almost all of it is 2019 or later concrete: Lougheed Heights and The Burquitlam Capital (2019), Marquee (2020), 567 Clarke & Como (2021), Precidia (2024) and Elements at Burquitlam (2026). The one older verified building in this file is Brookside Manor (1993) — a different depreciation-report and levy conversation than the towers. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in these towers?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable province-wide since November 24, 2022. Brookside Manor's directory line reads "rentals allowed with restrictions," which predates or sits alongside that change; what can still bind you is a short-term rental bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC and vary by building. The one published example in this file: a current Precidia listing records "Cats OK." For the other towers, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws in the strata package. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/699-whiting-way/2607
- **Q: What did condos do in September 2026?** At the sub-area level, condos & townhomes recorded 359 listings and 35 solds (10% sales ratio); at the Coquitlam community level the median was $625,000 with 23 days on market (SnapStats GVR September 2026). SnapStats publishes no sub-area median, so there is no verified Coquitlam West median price. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Whiting Way or Clarke Road condo?** School District 43 catchments are address-based and SD43 is a three-tier district. The one locator run verified in the research file is for 575 Poirier Street (Parkland Elementary, École Montgomery Middle, Centennial Secondary) — that is a Poirier-side address, not a station-area one. Run the building's exact address in the SD43 locator before relying on any school claim. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Q: Is there a levy risk difference between the 1993 low-rise and the new towers?** Yes, in kind: stratas with 5+ lots must now hold a depreciation report refreshed every five years and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF annually. A 1993 wood-era low-rise (Brookside Manor) is in the roof/envelope/plumbing cycle the report prices; the 2019–2026 towers are in warranty and early-life cycles instead. Read the specific building's report either way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Do the new planning amendments change what my condo is worth?** The adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments create medium-density and townhousing opportunities around the two stations and along Austin Avenue — they bear directly on house lots and future supply near the towers, which is the context a tower buyer is pricing against. Confirm whether a specific lot is inside an amended area with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST rebate and the PTT exemption on a new tower unit?** On a new build, potentially both: the FTHB GST/HST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on/after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. Resales carry no GST and use the regular First-Time Home Buyers' PTT program instead. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two sources give The Burquitlam Capital two different sizes.** A building source records 193 homes in 23 storeys (strata plan EPS5721); the developer records 189 condominiums plus four townhomes — 193 homes in total, counted differently. The strata plan, not either marketing count, governs unit entitlement and fees. https://www.estateblock.com/coquitlam/601-691-north-rd-1916128 and https://magusta.com/our-portfolio/the-burquitlam-capital/
- **A third station-area district is already in presale.** Burquitlam Park District, by Intergulf at Clarke Road and Como Lake Avenue, is planned in two phases: 40- and 33-storey condo towers with 663 strata homes in phase one, a further 213 market condominiums in a 27-storey tower in phase two, plus a rental apartment tower and a 5-storey non-market rental building, with completion estimated for 2028 (presale-directory source, secondary). The rental components are not strata condos and are not counted in Section 2. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/burquitlam-park-district/
- **The City renamed the ground under the towers.** The same station areas the MLS calls Coquitlam West are planned by the City as Southwest Coquitlam, and the Transit-Oriented Areas Update plus Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments were adopted following a public hearing — planning documents and listing copy using older area names are describing the same stations. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Detached and strata moved in opposite directions in September 2026.** Detached recorded 2 solds on 76 listings (3% sales ratio) while condos & townhomes recorded 35 solds on 359 listings (10%) — in this sub-area the condo market is the liquid one and the house market is the stalled one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **The newest tower was completing during the research itself.** Elements at Burquitlam (628 Whiting Way) is dated 2026 by the building directory with no units listed for sale on that page at crawl time — early resales there are effectively new-build purchases for GST and warranty purposes until the facts of the specific unit say otherwise. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/elements-at-burquitlam-628-whiting-way
