## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Semiahmoo Estates and The Capistrano in Section 2 are void in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Directly relevant here: Semiahmoo Estates, Suncliff Place and The Capistrano are all presented as 55+ in their sources — the registered bylaw on the Form B is the proof for each. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — kept separate by municipality.** Surrey addresses: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026; Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only), Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; Surrey SSMUH allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. White Rock addresses: the City of White Rock's OWN bylaws — Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 and OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220 — with White Rock SSMUH caps recorded in this program's rules-gaps file (3 units ≤280 m² / 4 units >280 m² / 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, Bylaw 2024 No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024). White Rock tax dates for a specific listing: UNVERIFIED in this file. Never carry a Surrey rule across the line into a White Rock listing. - **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to Odyssey by Forge Properties as newly completed stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the upper tiers are live at this page's price shape for detached product. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked examples (inputs stated):** (1) PTT at this page's upper shape — a $3,500,000 purchase with no exemption: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,800,000 = $36,000, plus 3% × $1,500,000 = $45,000, plus the further 2% on the portion above $3,000,000 (2% × $500,000 = $10,000), total $93,000. (2) PTT on a $1,000,000 condo purchase with no exemption: $2,000 plus 2% × $800,000 = $16,000, total $18,000; minimum down payment on those inputs is 5% × $500,000 + 10% × $500,000 = $75,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer sale surfaced under this page's title, which is never counted as resale stock here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% of the total mortgage amount; homes at $1,500,000 or more require 20% down and insured mortgages are not available. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
