## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest stock (Glenkirk Court, 1975; Mission Manor, 1992; Briskham Manor, 1993) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Mission may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Stave Lake Landing and Cherry Lane directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. Preston's rentals rest on a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Briskham Manor and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. City of Mission zoning, secondary-suite and SSMUH rules as applied to a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED in the batch-11 pass — no Mission zoning/suite page was opened for that file, and Surrey's figures are not carried into a Mission listing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-bc.md)
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date — the City's property tax page states a "10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026" (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw). **Keep the two regimes explicitly separate:** the staged 5% after July 2 plus a further 5% after October 31 belongs to the separate rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act) for FVRD electoral areas outside the City — it is NOT the City of Mission penalty, and the two are never blended in this file. Mission taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the other levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **ALR:** Mission's rural sub-areas (Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Lake Errock, Stave Falls, Steelhead, and the Hatzic fringe) include farm land; on ALR parcels the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — which is why no ALR parcel is condo stock. Which parcels on this page's feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-bc.md)
- **Flood:** the city's south edge along the Fraser is floodplain and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke; flood status and strata insurance terms are parcel- and building-specific — check both rather than assuming from the map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-bc.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar, Briskham Hill), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Montgomery and Silver & Cedar purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Montgomery and Silver & Cedar. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA), where a park home is compared instead of a condo:** pad-rent increases are capped at 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. No Mission park was verified as condo stock in this file — the line is here only because the city feed includes manufactured-home product. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

Short-term rentals are different: a bylaw can still restrict them, and one current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

The 5% + 5% pattern is the separate FVRD rural regime for electoral areas outside the City. Budget for the diking levy as an additional line on the Mission tax bill. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: How old is Mission's condo stock, really?** It spans 1975 (Glenkirk Court) to 2026 completions (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar). The middle band — Mission Manor (1992) and Briskham Manor (1993) — is wood-frame, and Briskham's strata fee includes gas and hot water per listings, a real offset when comparing fees across eras. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mission-manor-33165-second-avenue
- **Q: Do the new Redekop Faye buildings charge GST?** Yes on developer sales — Montgomery and Silver & Cedar are new construction, so 5% GST applies, with the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (up to $50,000 back on homes to $1 million) and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption (PTT fully exempt to $1.1 million) both in play for qualifying buyers. Preston (built 2023) is now resale stock, so a purchase there is generally GST-exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How does the West Coast Express work from Mission?** Mission City Station (33200 North Railway Avenue) is the line's eastern terminus and service is weekday rush-hours only — five morning westbound and five afternoon eastbound trains, 75 minutes to Waterfront, no weekend or holiday service. BC Transit Route 31 covers Mission–Abbotsford trips. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf

The Ministry-listed names include Mission Senior Secondary, Hatzic Middle School and École Heritage Park Middle School. Catchments are address-based — run the building's address through the SD75 locator; no catchment is asserted here without a run. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-bc.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Briskham Manor's build year?** Most listings and the MLS data sheet say 1993; one listing says 1992. This file uses 1993 as the majority/sheet year and flags the conflict — the strata plan (LMS1049) registration record settles it definitively. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85456/FullPublic.pdf?i=1506184905

A rural Mission listing is a house on land under the ALC residence frame, not condo stock, whatever a feed label suggests. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
