## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Albion's attached stock is townhouse strata, but the same Strata Property Act rules apply to a townhouse strata lot as to an apartment condo lot.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For The Warwick, first establish whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Albion may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Wherever a directory or listing line for an Albion complex says "rentals not allowed," that line is void and is flagged as such in Section 2 wherever it appears; no such line was found for the complexes above — Albion Station is instead marketed as rental friendly, which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Albion complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — houses sit under its compact urban zones (RS-1, s. 605: minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%), with RM townhome sites around the Albion Commercial Corner and ALR parcels in South Albion. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — do not treat either number as settled. The Albion Area Plan is Chapter 10.2 of the OCP, and Council adopted updates to it on **October 28, 2025**: enhanced walking and cycling connections, support for local commercial nodes, and buffers where urban development meets farmland. The Lougheed Transit Corridor plan area lies west of Albion (the corridor runs from the city's west boundary to 221 Street); its adoption status is flagged contested in this program's record — verify the current position on the City's project page before relying on any adoption claim for a corridor property. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId= and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/albion.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/albion.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (any remaining developer sales at The Warwick), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. A current Warwick listing notes qualified first-time buyers may be eligible for a GST rebate — that is a per-buyer eligibility question, not a building fact. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.estateblock.com/maple-ridge/4-10392-240a-st-1942748
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to any developer sale at The Warwick, not to resales. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract at The Warwick. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no manufactured-home park inside Albion was verified; where a Maple Ridge park home is bought instead of a strata unit, pad rent may rise at most once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, by no more than the yearly limit plus a proportional amount — 2026 = 2.3% plus proportional, 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
