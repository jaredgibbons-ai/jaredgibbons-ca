## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Canterbury against Ambleside strata stock will meet them there; inside Canterbury itself there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Ambleside building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Canterbury sits under the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010; houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones, whose Section 200 minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with the larger RS1/RS2 minimums the ones associated with the British Properties benches, and the zone for a specific lot shown on the District's Zoning Map. No Canterbury-specific local area plan is confirmed. Whether a Canterbury lot carries British Properties building-scheme or covenant restrictions beyond District zoning is UNVERIFIED — confirm on the individual title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Canterbury is confirmed, so this is relevant only to presales a buyer compares elsewhere in West Vancouver. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **West Vancouver tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Canterbury / British Properties assessed values the Province's additional school tax will commonly apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Canterbury?** No. Secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted uses in the RS zones, but they are accessory to the detached lot — they are not separate strata lots and cannot be bought on their own title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: If I buy a condo in Ambleside instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a building directory still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What replaces the Form B diligence for a Canterbury house purchase?** There is no strata, so there is no Form B, depreciation report or CRF to review. The equivalent checks are the lot's RS zone and minimum site area on the District's Zoning Map, any British Properties building-scheme or covenant on the individual title (UNVERIFIED in the research file — pull the title), and the hazard permit areas that can apply to hillside and creek lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: How is transit from Canterbury, really?** By Blue Bus from Park Royal Exchange: route 254 serves the British Properties road network, and route 250 runs on Marine Drive below the hillside to downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. Canterbury is a drive-to-everything address — the confirmed services (Park Royal, Ambleside, the Community Centre at 2121 Marine Drive, the library at 1950 Marine Drive) are all downhill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Canterbury address?** School District 45 (West Vancouver Schools) catchments are address-based and SD45 directs families to the school locator on its admissions page; no Canterbury catchment is confirmed in the research file, so run the exact address. The schools in the British Properties orbit are Chartwell Elementary (1300 Chartwell Drive), Westcot Elementary (760 Westcot Road) and Sentinel Secondary (1250 Chartwell Drive). SD45 has no middle tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is 8–12. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: Are there extra taxes at Canterbury price points?** Commonly, yes: the Province's additional school tax applies at 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026 (rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027), on top of District taxes due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% staged penalties. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **Q: Could condo or multiplex product be coming to Canterbury's lower streets?** The rules are in transition: on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave by December 31, 2025, and Canterbury sits above Ambleside. Whether Canterbury's lowest streets fall inside the "adjacent" areas is UNVERIFIED — verify per property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
