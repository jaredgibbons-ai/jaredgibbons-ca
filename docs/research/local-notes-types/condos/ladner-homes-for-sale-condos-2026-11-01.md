## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Delta Green (55+) and for the Seafarer Marina Estates and Fentons Court conflicts in Section 2, the Form B and title are where the live facts are settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in the sub-area files wherever a directory line says "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions": those lines are VOID for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. This is the live restriction at Delta Green (4686 51 Street), published as 55+ in its home file — unlike a rental line, it can still bind a buyer, and a tenant in a 55+ building must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans:** Delta's current OCP is Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules — the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under that same previous bylaw; neither schedule is part of Bylaw 8400, and farm land in Bylaw 8400 is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/ladner-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Zoning bylaw number — CONTESTED, flagged, not asserted:** City of Delta pages cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title in program sources reads "8400, 2025" — the two collide (8400 is the OCP number). This file flags the collision and asserts neither as settled; the sub-area files in this program cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 from City zoning-map sources, and a specific parcel's zone is read off the City's zoning maps in any case. (Standing correction for this program.) (Files: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/delta-manor-condos-2026-11-01.md and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/hawthorne-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH:** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning on June 24, 2024 (Bylaws 8394 and 8395), allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — this affects the detached streets around the condo pockets, not the existing strata buildings themselves. The live page states Delta zoning permits registered secondary suites and detached garden suites / coach houses on qualifying lots, subject to floodplain, coverage and parking rules — site copy in this pass; the current bylaw wording for a specific lot was not re-opened here: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-homes-for-sale.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/ladner-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Delta property taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/ladner-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (relevant to the Bridge & Elliott presale), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given in this file because no Ladner median is published in the permitted stats source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** Bridge & Elliott is a presale — REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
