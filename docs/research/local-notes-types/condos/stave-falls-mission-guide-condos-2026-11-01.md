## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the fee-simple acreages and pad-rent manufactured homes that make up this community's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Park age rules in a manufactured-home park are a park-tenancy matter under the park's own documents, not a strata bylaw — do not conflate the two. No Section 2 building carries a directory rental line to void in-entry, because no Stave Falls strata building was verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning (local):** Stave Falls is inside the City of Mission, so the City's Official Community Plan and zoning govern — Mission's current OCP is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, with the Rural Mission Strategy providing direction for rural communities including Stave Falls (the strategy is now finalized per the City's project and news pages; its Stave Falls-specific recommendations are UNVERIFIED in this file). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020: the rural area is divided into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s); a City rezoning notice describes the RU16 zone as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size, and City application material for the Stave Falls area confirms RU16-to-RU16s rezonings are the route used to permit a secondary detached unit on a rural lot outside the Urban Growth Boundary on land designated Rural Residential.
