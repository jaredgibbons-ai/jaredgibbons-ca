## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Townhomes here are strata, so the standard strata block applies in full.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Hampton Cove, the Form B and budget are also where Hampton Club access and charges show up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" for a complex above is flagged as void for long-term rentals (Fairwinds' records already state rentals allowed); short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful, with exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023). No complex in Section 2 is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)"; zoning parcel-by-parcel under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (Hampton Cove lands were built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning). SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **PTT in this band:** 1% to $200,000 and 2% on the portion to $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in those two tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — only the band's lower slice can use it. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new Skye townhome. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new homes only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — directly relevant to Skye. Assignment terms for Skye: UNCONFIRMED — no published term was verified. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood frame:** Delta operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Ladner townhomes are for sale under $1.2M right now?** UNCONFIRMED as a figure — SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures and no price-band cut; the live page's feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-townhomes-under-1-2m.html
- **Q: Which complexes are in this band?** No source states complex-level band membership — every complex in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED for the band. What is sourced is type and era: Hampton Cove phases from 2016–2024, central complexes from 1976–1998, and a 2026 presale at Skye. A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: What is the difference between a townhome here and a condo?** Tenure is similar — freehold strata, monthly fees, bylaws, Form B — but the form is ground-oriented townhouse living, not a shared apartment building, and fee coverage differs accordingly; a presale purchase also carries REDMA disclosure and rescission rules a resale does not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption in this band?** Only at the lower end — full exemption to $835,000, partial to $860,000; most of this band sits above those ceilings. The Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000) is the one that can reach a new townhome in the band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: I signed a presale at Skye — can I change my mind?** Yes, inside the window: REDMA gives seven days from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Can I rent out my townhome?** Long-term, yes in law — rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and a presale's disclosure statement should be checked for its rental disclosure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do Hampton Cove fees cover — is the Hampton Club included?** Listings describe Hampton Club access for Westhampton and Pilothouse, and a 12,000 sq ft clubhouse in project reporting — but what a specific unit's fee includes is stated in the strata budget attached to the Form B, and the club's fee as stated in the live page's copy is UNVERIFIED in the research. Read the budget, do not assume. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3097966-5311-admiral-way-100-ladner-bc-v4k-0e2/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Listings describe Woodward Landing as pet-friendly and Hampton Cove complexes generally as pet-friendly — pet bylaws are strata-specific; the registered bylaws decide number, size and type. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Ladner townhome?** SD37, address-based — the verified sample (5500 Admiral Boulevard) feeds Neilson Grove Elementary and Delta Secondary; the live page's broader claim assigning Hampton Cove townhomes to that catchment as a class is NOT verified, because catchments follow the address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask?** Whether the complex is within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (Streamside DPA), and how the depreciation report and insurance treat Ladner's setting behind the City's flood-protection system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
