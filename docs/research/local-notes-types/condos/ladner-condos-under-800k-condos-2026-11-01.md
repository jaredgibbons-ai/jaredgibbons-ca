## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed / allowed with restrictions" line for a building in Section 2 (for example Park Regent Estates, Sunningdale) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Directly relevant to Delta Green — confirm its registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)"; zoning parcel-by-parcel under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **PTT in this band:** 1% to $200,000 and 2% on the portion to $2,000,000 — a purchase under $800,000 is taxed entirely in those two tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — in principle usable anywhere in this band, subject to all other conditions. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the rule for Bridge & Elliott. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood frame:** Delta operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Ladner condos are for sale under $800,000 right now?** UNCONFIRMED as a figure — SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures and no price-band cut, and no official source publishes a Ladner condo count; the live page's feed is the only place the current band supply can be seen. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-condos-under-800k.html
- **Q: Which Ladner buildings are in this band?** No public source states a building-level band membership — every building in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED for the band. What is sourced is type: central-Ladner 1980s–1990s low-rises (Ladner Pointe, Sunningdale, Park Regent Estates, Green Gables), a 2021 55+ building (Delta Green), a 2016 building (The Pointe), and the Hampton Cove waterfront apartment building (Pilothouse). A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $800K?** In principle, yes — full exemption to a fair market value of $835,000 (first $500,000 exempt), partial to $860,000, if all personal and property conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term, yes — rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still records restrictions; at Delta Green a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. Short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at Delta Green?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases. Confirm the registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What pets can I bring?** Building by building: directory/listings record pets allowed with restrictions at Park Regent Estates and Sunningdale (4758), pets allowed at Sunningdale (4768), "Pets welcome" at Green Gables, "Pets ok (2)" at Pilothouse. The registered bylaws decide. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: How do I judge levy risk in the 1980s–1990s buildings?** Through the depreciation report (mandatory refresh every five years since July 1, 2024) and the CRF balance on the Form B — envelope, roof and parkade items are the read for this era of wood-frame low-rise. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Ladner condo?** SD37, address-based — verified samples: 5500 Admiral Boulevard → Neilson Grove Elementary and Delta Secondary; 4600 53 Street → Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary. Run the exact address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask?** Whether the building is within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch (Streamside DPA), and how the depreciation report and the Form B insurance summary treat Ladner's setting behind the City's dike, seawall and pump-station system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Are the Hampton Cove buildings Shorewinds and Riverstone real?** UNCONFIRMED — those apartment names appear only in the live page's own copy; no developer or strata source confirming them was read in the research. The verified Hampton Cove apartment building is Pilothouse. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/ladner-condos-under-800k.md)
