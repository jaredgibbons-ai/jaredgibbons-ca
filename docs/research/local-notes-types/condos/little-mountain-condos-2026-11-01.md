# Little Mountain — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Little Mountain is NOT Chilliwack Mountain.** Little Mountain is the smaller hillside immediately east of downtown Chilliwack, anchored by Little Mountain Elementary at 9900 Carleton Street. Chilliwack Mountain is a separate hillside community on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, west of the downtown core. Listings, schools and hazard rules for the two areas are not interchangeable, and no Chilliwack Mountain building, listing or figure is used anywhere in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase in a strata (here, realistically the townhouse stock such as Little Mountain Meadows), the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. Pure freehold detached purchases have no Form B — diligence runs on title, permits and the hillside reports below instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both apply to any Little Mountain townhouse strata purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44) for long-term rentals in any BC strata; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Little Mountain Meadows' published line is about age ("NO AGE RESTRICTIONS" per a listing), not rentals — any rental line in an older directory or bylaw set for the complex is void for long-term rentals regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is lawful in a strata, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Little Mountain complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020 No. 5000. The hillside residential zones include HR (Hillside Residential) — minimum lot area 0.2 ha where served by a community water system and 0.4 ha for all other uses, minimum lot width 25 m — and SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential), whose minimum lot area is set by the zone's slope table, starting at 360 m² for the lowest slope band and rising to 990 m² for the highest. Which zone applies to a specific lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. The Sardis plan cited elsewhere in this program (Schedule B5 of Bylaw 5500) is a different Chilliwack neighbourhood's plan and does not govern Little Mountain. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md) and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Hillside development permit areas — the central local rule:** Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) protects developments from hazardous hillside conditions and environmentally sensitive areas, and a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional is required with each application; Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all lands in the City with slopes of 20% or more, and within a designated DPA a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction, addition or alteration of a building, or alteration of land, except where exemptions apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Floodplain setbacks at the base of the hill:** the hill itself is upland, but the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal — these matter for base-of-hill and Hope River-side lots rather than the hilltop. Parcel-level flood status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** rates are the sum of each taxing authority's levy, with separate rates for each property class and additional taxes levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. 2026 due date and penalties: tax notices are due the first working day after July 1 each year, with a 5% late payment penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw is Bylaw 5395 (June 18, 2024) — the provincial SSMUH frame as adopted in Chilliwack; lot-level eligibility on a slope-constrained hillside parcel also has to clear the DPA 2 / DPA 5 process above. (Standing correction for this batch; lot-level application: UNCONFIRMED.) (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project on the hill was verified in the research; the rule is stated because hillside subdivisions here are sometimes sold off plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: How big are hillside lots?** The zones set the floor, not the market: HR (Hillside Residential) requires at least 0.2 ha with community water (0.4 ha otherwise) and 25 m width, while SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) runs on a slope table from 360 m² to 990 m² — a "big" hillside lot may not subdivide the way its area suggests. - **Q: Is there bus service on Little Mountain?** The confirmed service is at the base: BC Transit route 57 Broadway runs between Downtown Chilliwack and Cottonwood Mall via Yale Road at Menzies Street and Young Road at Olds Drive; no stop on the hill's residential streets was confirmed, so hilltop homes are a walk or drive from the stop. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=57

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two "mountains" are different hills in different parts of the city.** Little Mountain sits immediately east of downtown; Chilliwack Mountain is across the Fraser River side, west of the core. Search results, listing feeds and even school assumptions cross between them constantly — every fact in this file was kept on the east-of-downtown hill only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md)
- **Slope, not size, decides what a lot can do.** In the SHR zone the minimum lot area rises with the slope band (360 m² up to 990 m²), and DPA 5 captures every City lot at 20% slope or more — two lots of identical area on this hill can carry very different subdivision and building rights. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **A geotechnical report is a standard cost here, not an exception.** DPA 2 requires one from a qualified professional with each application — budget for the report and the permit before assuming a build, addition or subdivision timeline on any hillside lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **The elementary school is on the hill; everything else is below it.** Little Mountain Elementary (9900 Carleton Street) is the only one of the four named schools physically on the hill — Strathcona Elementary, Chilliwack Middle and Chilliwack Secondary all sit at or below the west base, which shapes the daily trip for middle and secondary students. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md)
- **The board name and the local name do not fully agree.** The one verified townhouse complex on the hill, Little Mountain Meadows on Quarry Road, is filed in listings under the board sub-area "Chilliwack Proper East" — a buyer searching only the "Little Mountain" sub-area can miss stock that sits on the hill, and vice versa. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3052629-9855-quarry-road-120-chilliwack-bc-v2p-3m3/brochure
- **There is no official boundary to settle an argument.** The City publishes no street-by-street boundary for the Little Mountain name, and the "Shannon Heights" subdivision name seen in one live listing is UNVERIFIED on any official or developer source — treat both as local usage, not designations. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/little-mountain.md)
- **The tax penalty clock has two dates, not one.** Chilliwack adds 5% after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026, and charges drainage and dyking levies on top of municipal rates — a different structure from Mission's flat 10% across the river. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
