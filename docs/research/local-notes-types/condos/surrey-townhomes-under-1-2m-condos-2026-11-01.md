## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Strata paperwork is where a Surrey townhome is actually priced: the Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, the Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price — and this page's own feed contains a 55+ complex, which is why the documents come first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. For townhouse complexes the report's roof, envelope, road and parkade/garage cycles are the levy story — and the page FAQ's strata-fee range ($250–$475 per month) is the site's own copy, not confirmed against an official source, and is not repeated here as fact: take the fee from the specific unit's Form B. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the "no rentals" line in older Elgin Pointe material in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this roster, Glenwood Village (per its feed card) and Bishop's Green (per a current listing) are described as 55+ communities — descriptions, not bylaws; confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit in either as generally occupiable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. **SSMUH:** Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, with no retroactive legalization of an unpermitted suite; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite — the comparison frame when a townhome buyer cross-shops a house with a helper in the same budget. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, directly relevant to closing adjustments on any band purchase completing around those dates. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year plus any application fees is required before operating — on top of whatever the strata's own bylaws allow. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this topic that means any remaining developer townhome sales in the newest complexes (for example the 2022-built Panorama Ridge complexes' first sales are done; a developer-held unit anywhere is a per-unit check), not the resale roster. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase anywhere in this band meets only the first two tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so the lower part of this band can sit inside the full-exemption range on price alone, and the part above $860,000 cannot. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a qualifying newly built townhome under $1,100,000 is inside that threshold on price alone, and one between $1,150,000 and $1,200,000 is not. The $1.2M line also sits inside the insured-mortgage range: $500,000–$1,499,999 purchases take 5% down on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion above it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — no presale complex is counted in Section 2's resale roster; a not-yet-built townhome project marketed under this ceiling is a developer contract under REDMA, not band resale stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
