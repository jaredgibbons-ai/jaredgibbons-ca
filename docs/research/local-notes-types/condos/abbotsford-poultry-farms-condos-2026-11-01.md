## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR land cannot be stratified — the lead rule for this file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A poultry farm's residence stays on the farm title; there is no strata exit for any part of an operating farm parcel's housing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-poultry-farms.md)
- **ALR housing frame (ALC):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-poultry-farms.md)
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment):** farm classification is a voluntary program. The Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for consideration for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-poultry-farms.md)
- **Quota and supply management — the board frame, as far as this program verifies it:** the live page names the four sector regulators (BC Chicken Marketing Board, BC Egg Marketing Board, BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission, BC Turkey Marketing Board) and states quota volumes are specified in the contract (e.g., kilograms of live weight per 8-week cycle for broiler chickens). Beyond that naming, quota is UNVERIFIED in this file in every particular — whether quota is included or excluded on any given listing, the transfer, approval and premises-certification rules of any board or commission, and any quota price or per-unit value — because no marketing-board or Province supply-management source was opened in this program. Verify the quota position with the applicable board directly before subjects are removed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-poultry-farms.md)

- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** general rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. Worked example — inputs: hypothetical fair market value $1,000,000, treated as fully residential for arithmetic illustration only, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $16,000 (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000 PTT**. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-poultry-farms.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — stated to close the loop for this series: a Form B Information Certificate (strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page) applies to strata lots only. A poultry farm is not a strata purchase — there is no Form B, and the diligence substitutes are title, ALC records, BC Assessment farm-class records and the applicable board's quota records. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

Neither applies to a farm title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — a strata rule with no application to a farm parcel, stated here because this series carries the standard block in every file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

- **GST and rebates (stated only as far as the sources go):** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales, as a general frame. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. GST treatment of a going-concern farm purchase is UNVERIFIED in this file (see the PTT apportionment note above). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** not applicable to farm purchases — REDMA s. 21's 7-day rescission and s. 18's trust-deposit rules govern developer marketing of strata and subdivision product, not operating farms.
