## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At The Fairways, the Form B is also where the complex's true unit count and registered bylaws are settled — both are UNCONFIRMED in public sources (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for The Fairways is VOID as a restriction. **Short-term rentals are a separate, live layer in Pitt Meadows: STRs are PROHIBITED at the city level** — a city rule that sits on top of strata law and is not cured by the voiding of strata rental bylaws. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The Fairways' registered age position is UNCONFIRMED — do not describe it as 55+ or as unrestricted on bylaw; no public source checked states an age restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs. The rural zones set this sub-area's lot pattern: A-1 General Agricultural — minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha, one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m; A-2 Large Lot Agricultural — minimum 16 ha; RR-2 — minimum 4 ha; RR-3 — minimum 2 ha; RR-4 — minimum 1 ha (RR zones are for land outside the ALR); RS Large Lot Residential — minimum 0.2 ha. A garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m (a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both); on ALR land the ALC also allows one additional detached residence without an application where conditions are met, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. The Official Community Plan is "I See Pitt Meadows 2040"; the North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026, covers approximately 50 ha north of Lougheed Highway between Meadows Gardens Golf Course and Harris Road, with development to proceed gradually and further approvals required. Zoning requires residential and agricultural buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in the A-1 and A-2 zones. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-meadows-pi.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes:** City taxes apply under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026; the rural north also sits inside dike/drainage service areas, and the specific levy set for a given lot is UNVERIFIED from an official source in this pass. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-meadows-pi.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Fairways is resale stock (built 2007). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $662,500 (the Pitt Meadows community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,250 (2% × $462,500) = **$11,250 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (not applicable to this area's resale stock). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no residential presale in the sub-area was verified (the North Lougheed Area Plan contemplates gradual development, with no specific project verified); for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for North Meadows: no in-area presale verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-meadows-pi.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
