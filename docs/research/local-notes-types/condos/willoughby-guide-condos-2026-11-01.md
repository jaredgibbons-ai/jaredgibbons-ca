## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Only what the Form B allocates counts for parking and storage — listings' stall and locker claims are unit-specific. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Every cross-referenced Willoughby building is well over five lots, so the five-year cycle applies across the local stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No cross-referenced Willoughby building is published as age-restricted in the home file — UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township plans — current position:** the existing Willoughby Community Plan (Bylaw 1836, 1979) now sits as Schedule 6 to the Township's Official Community Plan as updated by OCP Bylaw 6200, adopted December 1, 2025. Update Bylaws 6201 (Willoughby Community Plan), 6202 (Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan) and 6203 (Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) stand at first and second reading only (May 25, 2026), with Council directing a Public Hearing for June 15, 2026 — they are NOT adopted law in this file's position, and the guide page's June 15, 2026 adoption/repeal presentation is UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/willoughby-guide.md)
- **Township zoning and SSMUH:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. The Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (s. 403) runs from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m², with minimum frontage 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded — there is NO 355 m² figure in s. 403. Secondary suites (s. 102) are capped at a maximum gross floor area of 120 m², one per principal dwelling unit, with a building permit required. SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller and 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example Matteo while it sells as a presale), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — the March 12 secondary date is superseded) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to a Matteo presale purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Assignment consent and fees for Matteo and Walnut Park are contract terms — UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
