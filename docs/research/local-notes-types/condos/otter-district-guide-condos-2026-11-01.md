## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Otter District this document has almost no work to do: there is no verified strata stock in the sub-area, and the park homes in Section 2 have no strata corporation to issue one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Stated for completeness — no verified strata in the district carries either duty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. The voiding does not reach Langley Grove Estates: a park's rental rule is a park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not a strata bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Otter District building is published as age-restricted; listings describe Langley Grove Estates as having no age restrictions — listing-stated, with the park's own published rules UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The ALR frame — the governing fact for much of this district:** ALR land may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning. The principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence (a separate or detached dwelling is not a secondary suite) and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED for any specific property — confirmed on the ALC's ALR maps, not from the sub-area name. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/otter-district-guide.md)
- **Township zoning and the coach house:** rural homes in this part of the Township sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, and the feed's own listings name RU-2 zoning for acreage parcels; zone assignments for a specific parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource. In Bylaw 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — it is a fee-simple second unit under the zoning bylaw, never strata — and no coach-house size cap appears in s. 100 as read in this program, so no cap is stated. The Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area (Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 definition). https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/ (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/otter-district-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020, and why it mostly does not reach this district:** the bylaw amendments adopted November 18, 2024 (Bylaw 6020) permit SSMUH in residential zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex use, but eligibility requires the property to be within the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, not larger than 4,050 m² and not in a zone with a minimum lot size larger than 4,050 m²; properties that do not meet those criteria are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages fall under. There is NO 6-plex / 6-unit tier in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/otter-district-guide.md)
- **Pad rent (the park):** pad rent at Langley Grove Estates is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act: the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure for the park is published in this file — no official or operator source states one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/otter-district-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/otter-district-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Water and septic (rural servicing frame):** rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS, the provincial database of registered wells and mapped aquifers, for a parcel's record — and Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/otter-district-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Otter District was verified in this program; where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: What is a coach house in Langley Township?** Under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit, never strata. A ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit with different rules, and a listing's "coach home" wording is not the bylaw definition. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/otter-district-guide.md)
