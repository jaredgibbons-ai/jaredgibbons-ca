## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the roster's oldest stock (Creekside Estates, 1993; Mountain View Estates, 1988/1989) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory "rentals not allowed" line for Mountain View Estates is void in-entry on that basis. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Mountain View Estates is published as a 55+ community — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500, including its Schedule B neighbourhood plans (B1 Agricultural, B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, B3 Downtown, B4 Eastern Hillsides, B5 Sardis, B6 South Vedder, B7 Yarrow) and Schedule D development permit areas D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Wording warning carried from the research:** the live band page describes "Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland)" for upland estates. The OCP Schedule D list names D-2 as "Hillside Environment" and carries a separate D-5 "Hillside Development" — the live page's DPA2 name and single-area framing are UNVERIFIED against the current OCP list and should not be copied. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, which amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description recorded in the program's rules-gaps file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md, citing ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6)
- **Suites and ADUs (city-wide):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04 (Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04, as confirmed in the program's rules-gaps verification). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026 the City published a 5% late payment penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **ALR:** agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of economic activity on the City's own statement. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — an ALR acreage in this band can never be converted to condo stock. The ALC caps a principal residence at a total floor area of 500 m² or less (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill), permits a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — note the rebate is fully phased out before this band's $1.8M floor, so no band purchase qualifies. CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists and is likewise far below this band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — at this band's floor and above, the 3% tier applies only above $2 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — no band purchase qualifies. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — also below this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer contract in the city, wherever it sits on price. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
