## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Coquitlam is the established central part of the City of Coquitlam, around Como Lake Avenue and Austin Avenue, with the City Centre / Pinetree Way edge at its east side. No City neighbourhood plan boundary for "Central Coquitlam" is stated on a City page in the sources read — the exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; the City plans this part of Coquitlam through its City Centre and Town Centre planning and the Transit-Oriented Areas framework. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Central Coquitlam's stock is 2007–2016 concrete towers, so the reports now land on parkade membranes, elevators and envelope items in the Grand Central and Altamonte buildings first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line showing a rental cap or ban in the Section 2 towers reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. The long-standing plan was the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001; the City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Council report list: Bylaw 5511, 2026 and Bylaw 5512, 2026), streamlined to align with the provincial housing changes, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. Small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² (R-1/R-3) and 465 m² (R-2), and the City's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Watercourse protection:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s.523 covers riparian areas — a strata capital-planning and lot check, not a tower-interior issue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Pine & Glen presale contracts), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Pine & Glen purchases. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to Pine & Glen contracts. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this "Central Coquitlam" or "North Coquitlam"?** Both labels point at the same towers. The research file treats the Pinetree Way station edge as part of Central Coquitlam, while listing feeds file Oasis, MThree and the Grand Central buildings under "North Coquitlam." Price and diligence should follow the building and its strata plan (Oasis BCS4512, MThree EPS2687, Grand Central BCS3495), not the feed label. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a tower unit long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are a separate question: a bylaw can still restrict them, and Pine & Glen's neighbouring 22-storey tower is purpose-built market rental, not strata stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the towers?** Unit listings record cats OK at MThree, Grand Central 3 and Altamonte, and the directory records pets allowed at Oasis. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC and building-level published sets are thin — the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/oasis-2955-atlantic-ave
- **Q: How do the three Grand Central buildings differ?** By age and address: Grand Central One (2978 Glen Drive) is the 2009 building, Grand Central 2 (2968 Glen Drive) is recorded as built 2013, and Grand Central 3 (2975 Atlantic Avenue) as built 2014, all by Intergulf, with the complex recorded under strata plan BCS3495 in the research file. Compare each building's own depreciation report and CRF, not the complex name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md) and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2968-glen-drive/2501
- **Q: What will Pine & Glen add, and when?** Onni's project at 1175 Pinetree Way: 705 condominiums in 50- and 45-storey towers plus 186 market rental apartments in a 22-storey tower, over a 6-storey podium with retail, office and a daycare, estimated completion 2028. Presale contracts carry the REDMA 7-day rescission right, and new-build GST applies with the FTHB GST rebate and BC Newly Built Home Exemption in play for qualifying buyers. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/pine-glen-by-onni/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Pinetree Way tower?** School District 43 catchments are address-based, and SD43 is a three-tier district (elementary / middle / secondary). The verified locator result in the research file is for 1169 Pinetree Way (the library's City Centre branch): Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary. Run the tower's exact address — central-area addresses can feed different schools, including Central Elementary, Hillcrest Middle and Dr. Charles Best Secondary. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Q: How is the commute from the tower cluster?** The cluster sits on the Evergreen Extension: Coquitlam Central, Lincoln and Lafarge Lake-Douglas stations serve the Pinetree Way edge, and Coquitlam Central adds West Coast Express service. Commute times in minutes to Vancouver, Burnaby or Port Moody are UNVERIFIED — no official source read states them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** At sub-area level, 36 condos & townhomes listings and 5 solds (14% sales ratio); at the Coquitlam community level, the condos & townhomes median was $625,000 with 23 days on market. SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians and no building-level figures. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026.** The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026, streamlining the old Citywide OCP (Bylaw No. 3479, 2001) — 55 land use designations reduced to 18 and over 60 maps simplified to 19 — with a fuller OCP update anticipated to begin in 2027. Planning documents and listing copy citing the old Citywide OCP designations are already a plan out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **The tower cluster's board label disagrees with its research-file area.** Every current listing checked for Oasis, MThree and the Grand Central buildings carries the board sub-area "North Coquitlam," while the SnapStats sub-area table and the research file carry this stock in the Central Coquitlam frame. A buyer searching only one label sees only part of the cluster. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Grand Central 3's build year is not settled in public sources.** Current listings and listing records mostly say 2014; one listing record says 2012. This file uses 2014 and flags the conflict — the strata plan (BCS3495) registration record settles it definitively. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/2975-atlantic-avenue/2506
- **The next tower at the cluster is mostly not condos you can assume.** Pine & Glen's 705 condos come with 186 market rental apartments in a separate 22-storey tower on the same podium — rental-only stock that will show up in "units at Pine & Glen" counts but never in strata resale. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/1175-pinetree-way-coquitlam-city-centre-onni-presale-condos/
- **MThree's amenity club is at the top, not the podium.** Cressey's project page puts The Summit Club — 9,000 sq. ft. of amenities — at the top of the 48-storey tower, and current listings place the cardio and weight rooms on the 47th and 48th floors with a garden space on the 5th. Amenity location affects which floors carry view-and-amenity premiums. https://cressey.com/residential/mthree/ and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1188-pinetree-way/4305
- **The interior's density story is suites, not strata.** On the Como Lake / Austin streets, the City's SSMUH framework (up to 3 units on lots ≤280 m², up to 4 units under 4,050 m², suites and carriage homes included) is the density mechanism — those units are not strata condos and do not appear in condo searches, which is why the sub-area's 36 attached listings sit almost entirely at the station edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-coquitlam.md)
