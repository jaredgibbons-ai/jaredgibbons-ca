# Calverhall — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG — read first:** Calverhall is in the **City of North Vancouver, not the District of North Vancouver**. The research file states this as an open correction: the City's own Integrated Stormwater Management Plan names "the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods" as City neighbourhoods, and City zoning (Zoning Bylaw No. 6700) and City tax rates govern here — not the District's Bylaw 3210 / Bylaw 7900. Some secondary listing sources still label Calverhall "(District)" and at least one current Calverhall-area listing text refers to "District review/approval" for a coach house — both are wrong for this sub-area. This file follows the City geography, per the research file and the City's own documents. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Because there is no condo stock in Calverhall, this section does two jobs: it records the BC strata rules that govern any attached purchase a Calverhall buyer makes in a neighbouring sub-area, and the City rules that govern the attached forms (suites, duplexes, multiplexes, coach houses) that actually exist here.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. There is no Calverhall building to order one for — this applies the moment a search moves into Central Lonsdale, Lower Lonsdale or Moodyville. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No Calverhall building entry exists to flag this against — the rule matters for any neighbouring-sub-area condo a Calverhall buyer purchases instead, where older directory lines saying "rentals not allowed" are void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Calverhall building is published as age-restricted, because there are no Calverhall strata buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning (City of North Vancouver — NOT the District's):** Calverhall is governed by the City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400 (amended December 8, 2025 by Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114 for provincial housing requirements) and Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700. Do NOT import the District of North Vancouver's OCP Bylaw 7900 or Zoning Bylaw 3210 into a Calverhall file — wrong municipality, wrong rates, wrong permit rules. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Bylaws/Consolidated/8400-C.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **The GO Zone — the attached form that is actually coming to Calverhall:** the City adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone on December 8, 2025, under Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9137): a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a larger lot allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. City-wide, the change raised permitted units to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and up to 6 per lot on approximately 600 properties. Future Calverhall "attached" purchases will most likely be strata or freehold units in these small multiplexes — none completed and named in the sub-area was verified yet. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Development-Application-Resources/Extract-from-CNV-Bylaw-9137-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Suites and coach houses:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and a Building Permit. A coach-house approval here is a CITY approval — listing text that says "subject to District review" for a Calverhall address is misstating the approving municipality. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Streamside permits:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and the City's stormwater plan names a creek channel from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods — check creek-side lots before pricing a multiplex or coach-house build. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to a future GO Zone multiplex unit sold new, and to any new-build condo in a neighbouring sub-area. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No named residential presale project inside Calverhall is verified — REDMA becomes relevant only if a GO Zone project is marketed off plans. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in the exemplar files.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the published bands (the stated range is 0.6%–4.5%), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **City tax timing:** City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. Using District rates for a Calverhall estimate misprices the file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos for sale in Calverhall?** No verified apartment condos or townhome complexes exist inside the sub-area — the stock is detached houses, several with legal suites. A "Calverhall condo" search result is either a house, a suite inside a house, or a building in a neighbouring sub-area (Central Lonsdale, Lower Lonsdale, Moodyville). https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/calverhall-real-estate/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Q: Is Calverhall in the City or the District?** The City of North Vancouver. The City's stormwater plan names the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods as City neighbourhoods; City OCP Bylaw No. 8400, City Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 and City tax rates apply. Sources that label it "(District)" are wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Q: Can I buy the suite in a Calverhall house separately?** No. A secondary suite is part of the house's single title; the City permits one suite per single-family property with the owner residing at the property. It is rental or family space, not a purchasable strata lot, unless a future GO Zone project stratifies a multiplex lot — no such completed complex in Calverhall is verified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Q: What attached housing could exist here in a few years?** GO Zone multiplexes. Since December 8, 2025, most Calverhall house lots allow up to 4 units (up to 6 in the higher designations, up to 3 on lots of 280 m² or less), and a suite can combine with a coach house on qualifying lots. That — not apartment buildings — is the density path the City chose for this neighbourhood. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Development-Application-Resources/Extract-from-CNV-Bylaw-9137-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Calverhall address?** Brooksbank Elementary (980 13th Street East) and Sutherland Secondary (1860 Sutherland Avenue) are beside the area, and the new 585-seat Cloverley Elementary opened in September 2026 next door — but SD44 catchments are address-based, the Cloverley catchment review potentially moved Brooksbank, Queensbury, Queen Mary and Ridgeway addresses, and the research file's catchment result for Calverhall is UNVERIFIED. Run the exact address with SD44; do not rely on an old listing's school claim. SD44 has no middle tier (K–7, 8–12). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Q: If I buy a condo nearby instead, can I rent it out long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, whatever an older directory line in a Lonsdale or Moodyville building says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What taxes and utilities should I budget on a Calverhall house?** City of North Vancouver 2026: taxes due July 2, 2026, total residential rate 3.34028 per $1,000 plus storm drainage 0.10697 per $1,000. City single-family utility fee totals for 2026 were UNVERIFIED in the research file — confirm on the City's property-tax page for the specific property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Q: Is there a benchmark price for Calverhall condos?** No — a secondary source states Greater Vancouver REALTORS® did not publish a Calverhall condo or townhome benchmark for September 2026, and North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage. Any "Calverhall condo average" a website shows is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.wallacegreen.ca/calverhall
- **Q: What should I check before buying a house here for its suite or multiplex value?** Three things: the lot's OCP designation (Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 vs 2 / Residential Level 4 decides 4 vs 6 units), the GO Zone lot-size band (280 m² threshold), and the streamside permit area (15 m from a watercourse bank, 10 m from a ravine bank) on creek-side lots. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Development-Application-Resources/Extract-from-CNV-Bylaw-9137-Zoning-Bylaw-Amendment.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The municipality error is live in the market, not just in old files.** Secondary sources label Calverhall "(District)," and a current listing for a Cloverley Street house describes coach-house potential as "subject to all District review/approval/permits" — for a City of North Vancouver address, the approving body is the City, under City Bylaw No. 6700. A buyer who files, budgets or appeals in the wrong municipality loses time. https://www.realestatevalley.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate.php?p=1 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **The density arrived by bylaw, not by building.** On December 8, 2025 the City adopted the GO Zone (Bylaw No. 9137), lifting permitted units to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties and up to 6 on about 600 — Calverhall's future attached stock will be small strata multiplexes on today's house lots, and lot value now turns on the OCP designation, not on apartment zoning that does not exist here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **A new school opened next door in September 2026 — the first in North Vancouver in 35 years.** Cloverley Elementary is a 585-seat school with 92 licensed child care spaces, in the adjoining Cloverley neighbourhood; its catchment review potentially redraws four existing elementary catchments around Calverhall. Note the spelling split in official sources: "Cloverley" (SD44 / Province) vs "Cloverly" (City stormwater plan) — search both. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **The City's newest recreation centre opened the same summer.** The Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre, 130 East 23rd Street, opened July 25, 2026, south-west of the area — the amenity anchor for a neighbourhood that has no recreation facility inside its own streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Grand Boulevard is a heritage character area at the pocket's west edge.** The research file records Grand Boulevard in the City's heritage record — relevant to any GO Zone or coach-house plan on the boulevard-side lots, where heritage character, not just unit count, shapes approval. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **There is a creek under the neighbourhood's stormwater file.** The City's Integrated Stormwater Management Plan names a creek channel from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods — the same document that proves the City geography — and the streamside permit area follows that channel. It is the lot-level check most Calverhall listings never mention. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
