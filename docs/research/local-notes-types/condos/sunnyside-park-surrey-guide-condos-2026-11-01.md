## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1990/1991 wood-frame building (Semiahmoo Estates) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — that voids, in-entry, the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Semiahmoo Estates in Section 2. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Semiahmoo Estates is recorded as age-restricted and described in listings as 55+, and Bishop's Green townhouses are described as a 55+ community — confirm on the Form B whether each registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey plan:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 — a City corporate report records that on June 15, 2026, Surrey 2050 OCP received final adoption from Council; the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current here. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH zones RA to R6 allow up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.md)
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** secondary suites in Surrey are capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, one per dwelling; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated as current here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.md)
- **Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan:** approved by Council on January 31, 2022 and later updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations; the plan area, 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue from Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School, borders this sub-area. The guide page states the plan covers 136 hectares and was updated on July 27, 2026 for King George Boulevard bus rapid transit (Corporate Report CR171) — site copy. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Odyssey), not resales (Semiahmoo Estates). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — the March 12 secondary date is superseded) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to Odyssey purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Odyssey is built (2025 per listings), so remaining developer sales are completed-home sales — assignment terms for any original presale contract are UNCONFIRMED until that contract is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
