## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The mechanics — designation, then zoning, then development permit area:** in the verified Delta model, work in that order: OCP designation first (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area in OCP Appendix B — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. The same dating and layering check has to be run in Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford before a plan claim goes into an offer or a listing. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta — the verified model:** Delta Council adopted a new Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), on July 8, 2024; the City describes an OCP as the document that describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains long-term objectives and policies guiding land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure decisions. The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — Land Use Plan at s. 1.5, designations at s. 1.5.1 applied on Map 2 (including Agriculture (A)) — with NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in it; those were schedules of the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED:** City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, with zoning assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps that form part of that bylaw; an internal title in this program says "8400, 2025." Flag the collision wherever Delta zoning is cited and never assert one number as settled. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Delta SSMUH:** Delta's provincially required zoning updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, under the framework the 2024 OCP provides for Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Abbotsford — lookup level only:** the City of Abbotsford's Development Planning pages state that information about current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas can be found using the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap, and list the Council-adopted bylaws and policies that commonly regulate development. The Abbotsford OCP bylaw number and current consolidated structure: UNVERIFIED in the research pass — the City's development planning page was opened; the OCP bylaw document itself was not. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Surrey and Langley — UNVERIFIED in this pass:** the City of Surrey's current Official Community Plan bylaw number, land use designation list and secondary-plan structure, and the City of Langley's and Township of Langley's current OCP and zoning bylaw numbers and plan structures, were not verified — no source from those municipalities was opened for the research file, and nothing is stated here from general knowledge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md)
- **The ALR layer crosses all four municipalities:** where land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, Agricultural Land Commission rules apply on top of the municipal plan and zoning — generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel, a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area, and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6: strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR — ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Regional layer:** Delta's OCP carries a Regional Context Statement (Appendix A of Bylaw No. 8400) tying the municipal plan to Metro Vancouver's regional planning — the one verified example in the research of how a municipal land use plan nests inside a regional one. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A plan check never substitutes for this building-level check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" in any of the four municipalities reflects old bylaw text, not current law; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a project in any of the four municipalities is sold on developer contracts, the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which municipalities does this topic cover?** Four, per the live page's title: Surrey, Langley, Abbotsford and Delta — each of which plans through its own Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw; there is no single shared land use plan document for the four. https://jaredgibbons.ca/land-use-plans.html
- **Q: What is an Official Community Plan?** In Delta's own description — the verified example here — an OCP describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains long-term objectives and policies that guide decisions about land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: What is Delta's current land use plan?** The Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024: a single consolidated plan with the Land Use Plan at s. 1.5, designations at s. 1.5.1 on Map 2, and no Schedule D.1 or community area-plan schedules (those belonged to the superseded Bylaw No. 3950). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What is Delta's zoning bylaw number?** CONTESTED — City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017; an internal title in this program says "8400, 2025." Never assert one as settled; confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: How do I check zoning and future land use for an Abbotsford property?** The City of Abbotsford's Development Planning pages direct property checks to the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap for current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas. The Abbotsford OCP bylaw number itself: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Q: What are Surrey's and Langley's current plan bylaws?** UNVERIFIED in this pass — no City of Surrey, City of Langley or Township of Langley source was opened for the research file, and this file states nothing about them from general knowledge. What is verified is the structure: "Langley" is two municipalities with separate OCPs and zoning bylaws, so confirm which municipality the parcel is in before opening either plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md)
- **Q: Does an OCP designation settle what I can build?** No. In the verified Delta model, the OCP designation is the first of three layers — designation (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone under the zoning bylaw, then any Development Permit Area — and on ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission's rules apply on top of all three. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can farmland in any of these cities be turned into condos by stratifying the houses?** No — ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as subdivision in the ALR, and ALR residences cannot be stratified; the ALC layer sits on top of the municipal designation in all four municipalities. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: A listing cites an area plan — how do I know it is current?** Date it. Delta's governing OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024); any Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 reference is the superseded Bylaw No. 3950. The same dating check has to be run in Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford before a plan claim goes into an offer or a listing. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does a favourable plan designation change anything inside an existing condo building?** No — the plan governs land use, not strata bylaws. Inside a building, the Form B, registered bylaws, depreciation report and CRF position govern, and long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022 whatever a plan or directory says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
