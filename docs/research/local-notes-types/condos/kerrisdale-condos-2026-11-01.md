## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older Kerrisdale strata bylaw or directory line purporting to restrict long-term rentals is void as a restriction; rentals are allowed. Stratas may still restrict short-term rentals (and the City of Vancouver's own short-term rental rules also apply). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023, per Strata Property Regulation 7.01) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Kerrisdale strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Kerrisdale has no separate adopted community plan; citywide land-use direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Zoning is the City of Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. For detached-house lots, By-law No. 13817 amended By-law No. 3575 to replace the old RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or up to 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots. A secondary suite is an approved dwelling unit within a larger dwelling — only one per single-family home, it cannot be strata titled, and a third suite is not allowed. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/creating-a-secondary-suite.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax and city taxes:** the City charges an Empty Homes Tax of **3% of the property's assessed taxable value** on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year; payment due April 16, 2026) — budget for it on any Kerrisdale condo that will sit vacant or be held as a secondary property, and file the annual declaration. City property taxes: advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (Any page stating "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% — that compression is wrong as a rate statement.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given here because no verified Kerrisdale median exists to use as an input — do not substitute another area's figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many verified Kerrisdale apartment-condo buildings are on this roster?** Three: Sterling by Cressey (6400 West Boulevard, 37 homes, completed 2018), McKinnon by Cressey (6300 West Boulevard, 40 homes, completed 2019) and Boulevard Private Residences (5325 West Boulevard, 38 units, built 2017). Kerrisdale's housing is otherwise predominantly detached homes; the counted condo stock is a thin, village-edge shelf. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can buy in Kerrisdale?** No verified one. No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Kerrisdale strata — Sterling and McKinnon were checked specifically and are general-market. The seniors options in Kerrisdale itself are rental/care (Crofton Manor; Granville Gardens at the south edge), not ownership. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/kerrisdale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Kerrisdale condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are a separate gate: a strata may still restrict them and the City's short-term rental rules apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What extra Vancouver tax applies if the condo sits empty?** The City of Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year, payment due April 16, 2026), with an annual declaration required. City taxes themselves were due February 3, 2026 (advance) and July 3, 2026 (main), with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a Kerrisdale condo address?** SD39 catchments are address-based and split: the two locator samples on record are 5555 Carnarvon Street → Kerrisdale Elementary / Point Grey Secondary and 7455 Maple Street → McKechnie Elementary / Magee Secondary. Magee Secondary (6360 Maple Street) is physically in Kerrisdale. Confirm the exact address with the VSB locator before relying on a catchment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **Q: Is there SkyTrain in Kerrisdale?** No — transit is bus-based (West 41st and Arbutus/West Boulevard routes, including the R4 RapidBus corridor to the east), and the Arbutus Greenway's temporary path crosses West 41st at the village's east end for walking and cycling. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx?mgid=123893
- **Q: Does a Kerrisdale house lot have multiplex value?** Generally, the zoning now allows it: By-law No. 13817 rezoned Vancouver's RS districts to R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units per lot subject to the district schedule — check heritage status and confirm the specific lot with the City. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: What should I check on an older Kerrisdale low-rise?** The Form B package first (fees, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, depreciation report): Kerrisdale's older low-rise stock predates the current depreciation-report regime's bite, and rainscreening history matters — the one older building described in current listings (Brownstone, 2105 W 42nd Ave, built 1994) markets itself as "fully rainscreened," which is exactly the question to ask of any 1990s wood-frame building here. Status of that building beyond the listing: UNCONFIRMED (Section 2). https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2105-west-42nd-avenue/214
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** The BC rates in Section 3 (1% / 2% / 3%, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M), less any First-Time Home Buyers' Program or Newly Built Home Exemption the purchase qualifies for. No Kerrisdale median is verified, so no dollar illustration is given. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is Crofton Manor a condo I can buy into?** No — it is a rental/care retirement residence (Venvi/Cogir) on a 5.65-acre site at 2803 West 41st Avenue, and its site is a live City planning file (renewal/replacement of the facility is a stated minimum requirement of any redevelopment). Do not confuse it with Crofton House School at 3200 West 41st Avenue. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/crofton-manor.aspx

- **Crofton Manor's site is a planning file, not a settled building.** The City states the 5.65-acre site's seniors facility "is in need of a renewal," that retention and replacement with minimum disruption is a minimum requirement of any redevelopment, and that the ARKS Community Vision calls for additional planning study and consultation before a rezoning for seniors housing on the site. - **The community centre predates most of the village stock around it.** The Kerrisdale Community Centre complex has operated jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943 — one of Vancouver's first community centres — and includes the Cyclone Taylor Arena (5670 East Boulevard), with the Kerrisdale Play Palace running inside the arena in summer. https://kerrisdalecc.com/contact-us/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **There is no Kerrisdale area plan to read.** Kerrisdale has no separate adopted community plan; the governing citywide document is now the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026) — a buyer looking for a "Kerrisdale plan" will not find one. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
