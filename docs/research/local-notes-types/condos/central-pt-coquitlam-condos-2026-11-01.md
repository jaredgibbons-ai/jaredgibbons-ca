## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Mayfair Terrace, ask for the current service agreement alongside the Form B — the mandatory service plan sits on top of strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Shaughnessy Square's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — the frame for Mayfair Terrace's registered 55+ position, the city's only one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw 2008, No. 3630 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 2013, No. 3838. Under the zoning bylaw as read in the research file, apartment zone RA1 allows FAR 1.0, increasable to 1.5 where required parking is provided underground or within a structure and further to 2.0 on payment of a $50/sq ft contribution to City reserve funds for community amenities, and RA2 allows FAR up to 2.5, concentrated downtown and along main roads. RS-zoned lots across the city can hold 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity; the City permits secondary suites only in the RS-1, RS-2, RS-3 and RS-4 zones, with a building permit required. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/central-pt-coquitlam.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/central-pt-coquitlam-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026. The City's 2026 increase was 1.95% for the average home, and the City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/central-pt-coquitlam.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to developer sales at The POCO and any remaining developer sale at Luxora). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $660,000 (the Port Coquitlam community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,200 (2% × $460,000) = **$11,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (The POCO, estimated completion 2028; any remaining Luxora developer sale), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
