## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **REDMA rescission and deposits — central to this page:** under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21, a purchaser may rescind a purchase agreement by serving written notice on the developer within seven days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement; under s. 15 the developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement; deposits are held in trust under s. 18. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A presale has no Form B at signing — it becomes available only once the strata exists and the unit resells or completes into strata ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF — the regime a completed presale building enters, and a reason a brand-new building's early fees are not its long-run fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals, including in a newly completed presale strata. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Langley presale project in this file is recorded as 55+ — any age claim for an unnamed project is UNCONFIRMED until its disclosure statement and draft bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Municipal zoning and plans — two Langleys, kept separate:** Langley presales sit in either the Township of Langley or the City of Langley, with different bylaws. Township: the Official Community Plan is Langley OCP Bylaw 1979 No. 1842 as amended, most recently by the 2025 OCP Update, Bylaw No. 6200, adopted December 1, 2025; zoning is under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 as amended, including SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m² on eligible lots, NO 6-plex tier; suite cap 120 m²). The Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979) in origin, now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200. Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 received first and second reading only on May 25, 2026 — they are not adopted law in any source opened. City of Langley plan and zoning facts for a specific project: per-project check, UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.md)
- **GST and rebates — the presale tax core:** 5% GST applies to new construction (a presale completes as a new home), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **Worked example, with its inputs stated:** on a $1,000,000 newly constructed home bought by an eligible first-time buyer under an agreement in the Bill C-4 window, GST at 5% is $50,000 and the FTHB rebate at 100% (price at the $1M full-rebate ceiling) returns $50,000, for net GST of $0 before any other taxes or adjustments — inputs: $1,000,000 price, 5% GST, eligible first-time buyer, agreement date in window; any price above $1M phases the rebate down to zero at $1.5M. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024, and the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer — the exemption most Langley presale buyers will actually use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Deposit range on the live page — UNVERIFIED:** the page states "Most Langley projects ask for 10% to 20% in total, paid in two to four instalments" and shows a worked-cost table with 15% deposit examples from $600,000 to $1,250,000; no developer source for any named project was opened, so the range and table are the page's presentation only — a specific project's deposit schedule is whatever its disclosure statement and contract state. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Langley projects are selling now, and when do they complete?** UNVERIFIED in this file: the live page's browse section named no projects in the text read, and no developer source for a named project was opened — project names, pricing and completion dates must be confirmed project-by-project from developer sources and disclosure statements, not from a roundup or the program's presale pack alone. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.md)
- **Q: How long can I take to cancel a presale after signing?** Seven days: under REDMA s. 21 you may rescind by written notice to the developer within seven days after the later of the purchase agreement date and the date the developer obtained your receipt for the Disclosure Statement — get the contract and disclosure statement to a presale lawyer inside that window. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **Q: Do I pay property transfer tax on a Langley presale?** Often not on a principal residence: BC's Newly Built Home Exemption fully exempts a qualifying purchase at a fair market value of $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — and you do not need to be a first-time buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST back on a new Langley home?** Yes: under Bill C-4 (Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) an eligible first-time buyer receives a rebate of 100% of the GST on a newly constructed home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with no rebate at $1.5 million or more, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How much deposit will I have to pay, and when?** UNVERIFIED as a Langley-wide range: the live page states 10%–20% in two to four instalments, but no developer source was opened — the binding figure is the schedule in the specific project's contract, with all deposits held in trust under REDMA s. 18. https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
