## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the core diligence document in the bare-land stratas too: in Creekside Mills, The Cottages and Aquadel Crossing the Form B is where lot-versus-common-property responsibilities, fees and bylaws are confirmed — what the strata owns versus what sits on the lot is the question to answer from it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Bare-land stratas are stratas for these purposes — Creekside Mills' 129 lots put it squarely inside the frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any "rentals not allowed" line in a directory or listing for a Lindell Beach bare-land strata is VOID as a long-term rental restriction. **Short-term rentals are a separate, live gate at the south end:** stratas may still ban or limit them by bylaw; the FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H (Lindell Beach) to proactively address short-term vacation rentals, FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it, and a provincial short-term rental registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — none of the three Lindell Beach communities holds one in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Governing zoning and OCP (FVRD — never Chilliwack's bylaws, and never the Park Board's):** the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115 (titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019), covers the south end; zoning is in the FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 — the zone names and minimum parcel area/frontage figures applying to Lindell Beach parcels are UNVERIFIED and must be checked per address. PlanCultus (Bylaw No. 1080, 2016) does NOT apply here — it applies only inside Cultus Lake Park at the north end. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249 and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/zoning (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-south.md)
- **Taxes — rural regime, not Chilliwack's dates:** Electoral Area H properties pay FVRD and provincial rural taxes plus service-area levies. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid after October 31. Chilliwack's penalty dates NEVER apply to Cultus Lake. Service detail: the South Cultus Lake sewer service area was established by Bylaw No. 1416, 2017, with a parcel tax under Bylaw No. 1497, 2018; Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025 set the per-parcel amount as the annual requisition divided equally by parcels on the roll; the FVRD's Area H residential rates list Water – Cultus Lake and Sewer – Cultus Lake South as "No Utility Billing / Property Taxes: Yes" (tax-funded) — confirm the specific lot is a participant. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **Manufactured-home / pad product:** where pad product is discussed (Lindell Beach Holiday Resort), tenancies sit under BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act frame and pad-rent increase limits apply: 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. The Holiday Resort's own current terms are UNVERIFIED (Section 2) — never publish a pad-rent dollar figure for it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (round illustration only — no area median exists to use), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no south-end presale was confirmed on an official source (Aquadel Crossing's developer marks it "SOLD OUT!") — where a unit is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.aquadelcrossing.com/ and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The south end's sewer tax is shared equally, not metered.** Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025 set the South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax as the annual requisition divided equally by the parcels on the roll — every participating parcel pays the same amount regardless of use, and the FVRD's Area H rates show the south sewer and the Cultus Lake water service as tax-funded with no separate utility bill (the north sewer, by contrast, carries a flat rate plus taxes). https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839 and https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **"Bare land strata" is the whole story at Lindell Beach.** All three named communities sell a detached house or cottage, but the title is a bare land strata lot: strata fees, bylaws, a depreciation report and a CRF stand behind the cottage, and the Form B — not the cottage's appearance — is where a buyer learns what the strata maintains and what the lot owner does. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206
- **The local hazards are creek fans, not the Fraser River.** The governing local hazards at Lindell Beach are lake-level and creek (Frosst Creek) debris/flood events on the Cultus Lake alluvial fans; the FVRD funds sediment-basin cleanouts at Frosst Creek and Elbow Creek in this area, and its Area H guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding. Property-specific hazard reports are UNVERIFIED. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
