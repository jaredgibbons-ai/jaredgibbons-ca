## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In a 40-plus-storey tower, the depreciation report's elevator, parkade, envelope and amenity cycles are the levy story — read it before pricing the view. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every tower in Section 2 is deep inside the mandatory cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any City Centre tower directory line stating "rentals not allowed" reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No tower in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **PTT at this band — the rule that bites hardest:** general rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion — plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band: the First-Time Home Buyers' Program caps at $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption is fully eliminated at $1,150,000, so a buyer here budgets full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Home owner grant phase-out at this band:** the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none, even as principal residences. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a developer or first sale in a 2025 tower), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — note the rebate itself phases out below this band's floor, so it does not offset GST on an over-$1.8M new purchase. CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year — recorded for completeness; neither changes the tax math at this band. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. CMHC insurance is a sub-20%-down product with a $1.5M price ceiling in the sources checked — a purchase in this band is conventionally financed, so the premium table is background, not a closing line. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Floodplain and ALR — for the detached side of the band:** the City states Surrey's coastal floodplain makes up about 20% of the city's land area and names Crescent Beach, Panorama/Gray Creek, Cloverdale, Colebrook, Mud Bay and Nico-Wynd/Crescent Road among its residential areas; ALR status is parcel-specific through the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping tools. Both are address-specific checks for any large or low-lying listing in this band. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)

- **Q: How many Surrey homes are for sale over $1.8M right now — and how many are condos?** UNCONFIRMED as a figure. The live page's feed is the only place the current band count can be seen, and it changes as listings come and go. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: Which Surrey condos are actually in the over-$1.8M class?** No public source checked supports that claim for any specific building — every tower in Section 2 carries an UNCONFIRMED price-band status. What the sources do support is building type: 3 Civic Plaza is its developer's "first luxury hotel and condominium development" in Surrey, The Grand on King George is marketed as "Surrey's first luxury high-rise," and One Central markets top-floor sub-penthouse product. A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time, not a building fact this file can state. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is there extra property transfer tax in this band?** Yes, in two steps: the 3% tier applies to everything over $2,000,000, and a further 2% applies to the residential value over $3,000,000. Model both before offering — and net in no exemption, because neither the first-time program ($835,000 cap) nor the newly built exemption (gone at $1,150,000) reaches this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Why is the condo side of a $1.8M band so thin in Surrey?** On the sourced figures: Surrey's condos & townhomes median was $512,944 in September 2026 — the band floor is roughly three and a half times the median attached price — while the detached median was $1,215,000. The band is the upper tail of the detached market first; the condo entries are the newest, tallest City Centre towers' top floors, not a broad condo segment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a City Centre tower unit long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. What can still bind you is a short-term rental bylaw in the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the home owner grant do at this level?** Less than buyers expect: the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 of assessed value, the regular grant reduces by $5 per $1,000 over it, and it is $0 over $2,189,000 — two otherwise similar band homes straddling the threshold carry a real annual-cost difference. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: Are the King George Hub rental tower's units part of the condo stock?** No. The Hub's fifth phase is a secured purpose-built rental tower (402 units, completed 2026 per the developer) — excluded from Section 2's condo entries. The Hub condos are Hub One, Hub Two, Plaza One and Plaza Two only. https://pci-group.com/projects/king-george-hub/
- **Q: What should I check on a large Surrey property near farmland or the coast?** Two parcel-level checks: ALR status, through the Agricultural Land Commission's maps and online mapping tools; and floodplain status, since the City puts about 20% of Surrey's land area in its coastal floodplain, names Crescent Beach and Panorama/Gray Creek among its residential areas, and notes most of the floodplain is dyke-protected. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: Do schools matter at this price point?** The same way as at any price: SD36 catchments follow the address through the School Locator, not the sub-area name or the price — confirm by address even on a band purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey's tallest building is a hotel with condos on top.** 3 Civic Plaza (52 storeys, 157.3 m, completed 2018) is the city's only building over 150 m, and its residences sit above a Marriott Autograph Collection hotel and KPU campus floors — the mixed-use structure behind its "luxury" label, and a strata whose common property includes hotel and university neighbours. https://centurygroup.ca/projects/3-civic-plaza/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_buildings_in_Surrey,_British_Columbia
- **One developer built four of the seven towers in this file.** King George Hub's condo phases (Hub One and Two, 738 suites, 2021/2022; Plaza One and Two, 886 suites, 2025) are all PCI Developments product beside one station — cross-tower comparisons of construction and amenity models in Whalley are unusually direct. https://pci-group.com/projects/king-george-hub/
- **The band sits above both community medians.** Surrey detached median $1,215,000 and South Surrey / White Rock detached median $1,628,000 (September 2026) both sit below the $1.8M floor — a band listing is priced into the upper tail by definition, and the showing and offer pace of a thin band is not the pace of the median market. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **"City Centre" and "Whalley" are the same ground with two names.** The City names Whalley among its communities while the towers market themselves as Surrey City Centre / Surrey Downtown — searches under only one name miss listings filed under the other, exactly as the tallest-buildings record notes most of Surrey's high-rises sit in the Whalley district. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_tallest_buildings_in_Surrey,_British_Columbia
- **Surrey has a new Official Community Plan.** The Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) received final adoption June 15, 2026 — older material citing the PlanSurrey 2013 OCP (Bylaw No. 18020) as current is out of date, which matters when a band listing's feature sheet quotes plan designations.
