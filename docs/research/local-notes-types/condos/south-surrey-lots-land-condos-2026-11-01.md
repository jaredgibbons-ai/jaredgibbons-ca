## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The land frame first — Surrey zoning, parcel by parcel in COSMOS:** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs Surrey lots, and any lot's zone is checked on the City's COSMOS map; the live page's guide copy states the old RF zone is now R3 Urban Residential, RH is now R1 Suburban Residential, RQ is now R2 Quarter Acre Residential and the old one-acre zone is now RA Acreage Residential (site copy — Bylaw 12000 is confirmed current in this program; the old-to-new mapping was not re-read from the bylaw in the research pass). White Rock lots sit under White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Official Community Plans:** Surrey's governing plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only. White Rock lots answer to White Rock's own OCP, Bylaw No. 2220 (2017) — the two municipalities' plans are separate and neither substitutes for the other. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **How many homes can go on a Surrey lot (SSMUH):** inside the urban containment boundary, on lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger lot, up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite. A secondary suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, and a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. White Rock's Bylaw 2506 carries the same provincial tiers: up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop; the page's stated RS-1 minimum new lot (742 m², 18 m wide, 30.5 m deep) and Surrey RA minimum lot (4,050 m², 50 m wide, 60 m deep, Bylaw 12000 Part 12) are site copy, not re-read from the bylaws — UNVERIFIED beyond site copy. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **ALR stratification bar (east-side parcels):** larger acreage lots sit on the community's east side near Hazelmere and the farm areas, and on ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence; depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)), so ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission (Strata Property Act s. 239 is the strata-side bar). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Subdivision process (Surrey, site copy):** the live page states a subdivision goes through City planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter listing conditions, 12 months to meet them (an extension can be requested for a fee), conditions that can include road dedication, covenants, tree permits and development cost charges, and a registered land surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office — no City subdivision page was opened in the research pass, so the PLA timeline is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy. Servicing: the page states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing before an application can proceed, and some acreage land is unserviced — parcel-level servicing is UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Form B, depreciation reports, CRF, rentals and age (the strata block — it applies only once a lot becomes a strata, which no lot on this page yet is):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating fees, amounts owing, approved levies, the CRF balance and insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. Only a 55+ age bylaw is legal (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. None of this creates or describes stock on a vacant lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey property taxes and short-term rentals:** Surrey taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. Surrey short-term rentals (under 90 consecutive days per patron) are permitted for owners only, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot host, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. White Rock's short-term rental rule: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **GST on vacant land — only as a source states it:** the live page's copy states most vacant-land sales by individuals who held the land for personal use are GST exempt, while land sold in a business or a parcel split into more than two lots can be taxable — no CRA material was opened in the research pass, so that treatment is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy and is a question for the buyer's accountant, not advice from this file. Separately, the verified general rule: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The page's owner-builder sketch (BC Housing Owner Builder Authorization, plan to live in the home at least a year, exam, no home warranty insurance, 10-year personal liability for defects) is likewise UNVERIFIED beyond site copy — confirm with BC Housing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **GST rebates (for the home eventually built):** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax on land — worked example with inputs stated:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; PTT applies to land purchases on the same tiers, with the residential/non-residential split modelled by the buyer's lawyer or notary. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $700,000 (the community's Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is NOT a land figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — it is a newly-built-home exemption, not a vacant-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project is sold off this land page; where a developer-contract purchase does arise in Surrey, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There is no such thing as a published South Surrey land median.** SnapStats does not classify land separately, so any "South Surrey lot median" in marketing copy comes from outside the permitted source — the community's detached median ($1,628,000) and attached median ($700,000) for September 2026 describe built homes across all nine sub-areas and say nothing about a lot's value, which turns on zone, servicing and buildable envelope. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **One page, two bylaw regimes.** The filter crosses the Surrey–White Rock municipal seam without marking it on the cards: the same search returns lots governed by Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 and lots governed by White Rock Zoning Bylaw No. 2506 (adopted June 27, 2024) — the municipality, not the page, decides the rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Surrey's zone names changed under the old listings.** The page's guide copy maps the old RF single-family zone to R3, RH half-acre to R1, RQ to R2 and the one-acre zone to RA — a buyer reading an older listing's zone code against the current Bylaw 12000 will mismatch the parcel unless they convert (site copy; the mapping was not re-read from the bylaw in this pass). https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html
- **The ALR line is the east side's real boundary.** The Hazelmere-side acreage on this feed sits near the farm areas; an ALR finding caps the housing program at one principal residence (≤500 m², suite inside it) plus one additional residence (90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size) and bars stratification without ALC permission — a materially different build than the same acreage outside the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Servicing claims drive land value more than the parcel's shape.** Feed cards use "serviced," "subdivision potential" and preliminary-engineering language — none verified against City records in this research, and on acreage ground the servicing answer (city water/sewer versus well/septic with Fraser Health testing) is the value question. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Surrey has a new Official Community Plan.** The Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) was adopted June 15, 2026 — any older page or document citing the PlanSurrey 2013 OCP (Bylaw No. 18020) as current for a South Surrey lot is out of date. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
