## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Elmwood Terrace (1986) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack Proper South may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Vibe's directory line reads "rentals allowed," which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Elmwood Terrace is presented as a 55+ community in its listings, and the Cottage Grove unit in the feed is described as 55+ gated product — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for either before treating a unit as generally occupiable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500; its Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covers this ground (per the batch research for the Proper pages). Cite the schedules correctly: the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000; its structure includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone in Section 12. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **SSMUH:** Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) is Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Bylaw 5000, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. **Secondary suites and ADUs:** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit; only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), garden suites capped at 100 m², ADU parking under s. 5.04. The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level under the Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **Flood and ALR:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper South lots are UNVERIFIED. ALR residences cannot be stratified in any case (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all three Section 2 buildings trade as resale stock now (The Elliot is recorded Sold Out / Complete on its marketing page). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Chilliwack Proper South was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The feed is condo-led even though the copy is house-led.** Of the seven cards read in full for the research, five were low-rise condo units — the attached product is what actually moves through this page, whatever the detached-first description implies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **The page cannot agree with itself about which board feeds it.** Its FAQ credits the Fraser Valley board's MLS® feed; CADREB's own territory description makes Chilliwack a CADREB market. Board identity matters because it decides whose statistics (here: none published at sub-area level) a buyer can ask for. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **The Vibe's four addresses do not share one build year in listings.** Phase addresses show 2007–2009 across listings against the directory's single 2008 for the complex — warranty and depreciation timing for a specific unit follows its own phase, not the marketing year. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The Elliot's marketing survived its sell-out.** The project page still reads "Sold Out / Complete" with no unit count published — a buyer looking for the building's size or original disclosure material will not find it there; the Form B and strata plan carry it. https://kleinprojectmarketing.ca/wp-content/uploads/projects/DPSGKZ/Elliot%20%20-%20Chilliwack.pdf
- **The school name in the page copy is not the Ministry name.** The page's "McCammon Traditional Elementary" is "McCammon Elementary" in the BC Ministry K-12 file — search and catchment work should use the Ministry name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **Chilliwack's suite cap is a "lesser of" test, not a flat number.** A secondary suite must be both under 90 m² AND under 40% of the building's habitable floor space, inside a building with only one principal dwelling — and the one-ADU-per-lot rule means a legal suite forecloses a coach house on the same lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-south.md)
- **The tax penalty calendar is Chilliwack's own.** 5% after July 2, 2026 and a second 5% after August 4, 2026 — a different second-stage date than the rural regime's October 31 and a different structure than Mission's flat 10%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
