## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Note the trap in Section 2: Kensington Gardens has no strata corporation, so no Form B exists for it at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the rule behind the VOID flags in Section 2 (Toscana's "No Rentals" headline is the worked example). **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** is the City's current OCP. Zoning is **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — the former Zoning Bylaw 1965, No. 4742, is repealed. On June 10, 2024 Council rezoned all R District lots to the R1 SSMUH District; R1 lots are eligible for up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, with the total inclusive of secondary suites; forms include single-family homes, laneway homes, suites, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and, on some properties, fiveplexes and sixplexes. A secondary suite classified under the BC Building Code must remain on the same title as the primary unit and cannot be stratified. A suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule for Burnaby are UNVERIFIED — the City pages read in the research pass state neither. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, after which a 5% late penalty applies, with an additional 5% penalty after the September due date on outstanding current taxes; the City has no authority to waive or modify the penalties. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Short-term rentals in Burnaby:** UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in the research pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $686,000 (the Burnaby community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026 — for this city-wide file it IS the page's community figure, but it is still an illustration, not a price for any building), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,720 (2% × $486,000) = **$11,720 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where presales exist in this roster (Centrale on Capitol Hill; Oasis and Brentwood Block in Brentwood Park), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
