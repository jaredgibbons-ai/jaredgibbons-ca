## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 1988/1989 and 1993 buildings in Section 2 this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — that voids, in-entry, the building directory's "rentals not allowed" lines for Mountain View Estates and Evergreen Estates in Section 2. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Two Section 2 buildings are presented as 55+ in listings (Mountain View Estates and the unnamed 46693 Yale Road building) — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for each. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Chilliwack plan:** the city-wide plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, and its Schedule B2 is the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covering this ground; the plan was first added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533 and updated in 2025 as part of the 2050 OCP. Cite the schedules correctly: the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." The guide states the plan's designations as Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000; the zone of any specific parcel is a per-parcel City check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel here. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.md)
- **SSMUH and suites:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Under Bylaw 5000, a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building (s. 2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s. 4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s. 5.04); no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Flood:** as the guide states it, citing the Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, Chilliwack Proper is subject to the flood risk of the Fraser River, the dyke system is built to a 1-in-200-year flood standard, and at higher river stages areas outside the City's dyking system flood. Which flood rules and flood construction level apply to any specific lot is a per-parcel City check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.md)
- **ALR:** the City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base; on ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). ALR status for any specific Proper East parcel: UNVERIFIED — parcel-by-parcel on the ALC map. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May, taxes are due the first working day after July 1, a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Chilliwack Proper East is named in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
