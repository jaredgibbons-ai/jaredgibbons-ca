## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that decides this file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). On ALR parcels the ALC's as-of-right package is generally one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional one, a principal residence of 500 m² or less, and a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; anything beyond needs ALC approval, and ALC Policy L-26 (amended May 2025) allows an additional residence beyond the regulation only if necessary for a farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no in-area application today (no strata stock), and it is the document that would govern any future strata purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for an Abbotsford strata is VOID as a restriction. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Mt Lehman's SSMUH position: **Abbotsford's SSMUH bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages — it is marked here and is never asserted as "3560-2024."** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Abbotsford's current Official Community Plan is **Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025** — the 2016 OCP (Bylaw 2600-2016) is NOT current and must never be cited as current. Abbotsforward 2050 directs growth to Abbotsford's urban centres and protects the agricultural land base. Zoning is under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014; the workhorse rural zone is Agricultural One Zone (A1), s. 210 — intent "to accommodate Agricultural and Agri-Tourism uses on lots that are 8.0 ha and larger," with principal uses including Agricultural Use, Agri-Tourism, Farm Retail Sales, a Single Detached Dwelling and a Winery, plus accessory family/employee residential uses and a Secondary Suite. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan and https://www.kpu.ca/sites/default/files/City of Abbottsford_ Agricultural Zoning Bylaw_A1_consolidated 2014.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes (2026):** set by Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026; all taxes were due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026, and penalties are legislated and cannot be waived. Area-specific charges (for example prairie dyking districts) can appear on parcels at the plateau's foot. Farm class (BC Assessment Class 9) requires actual production of qualifying agricultural products for sale; 50% of Class 9 land's assessed value is exempt from school taxes, and farm class is earned by production, not automatic on ALR land. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — with no new condo construction in this community, GST is not a live Mt Lehman condo item. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $497,500 (the Abbotsford community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Mt Lehman figure exists, and the illustration is for a condo purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $5,950 (2% × $297,500) = **$7,950 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Mt Lehman was verified in this program; for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for Mt Lehman: no in-area presale verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condo buildings does Mt Lehman have?** None verified — zero. The community's housing is freehold acreage homes, working farms and estate properties, and ALR law bars stratifying farm residences. Anyone marketing a "Mt Lehman condo" is marketing an urban Abbotsford building under a rural name. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Can a Mt Lehman farm's second home be sold to me separately as a condo?** No. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); a second residence on ALR land is an accessory farm/employee residence or a suite inside the principal residence, and it conveys only with the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual condo stock?** Urban Abbotsford — the central, west and east sub-areas hold the city's verified roster, documented in their own condo notes files. Mt Lehman buyers who need strata product are shopping a drive away, in a different sub-area. (Section 2 cross-references.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or second dwelling on a Mt Lehman acreage for family or a caregiver?** That is the realistic multi-household route here: A1 zoning permits a Secondary Suite as an accessory use, and on ALR land the ALC allows a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, with anything beyond the permitted number/size needing ALC approval — check the specific parcel's zone and ALR status first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is Mt Lehman in the floodplain?** The village itself is upland — that elevation is the origin of the "Mount" — but it is ringed by ALR farmland above Matsqui Prairie's diked floodplain, protected by the 11.3 km Matsqui Dike. Plateau-edge and prairie-floor parcels need their own flood check. https://www.env.gov.bc.ca/wsd/public_safety/flood/pdfs_word/dikes_listed_by_authority-2013.pdf
- **Q: Is there a Mt Lehman market-stats sheet I can price from?** No — board reporting has no Mt Lehman row. The closest published figures are Abbotsford-wide (September 2026 attached median $497,500) and the neighbouring rural sub-areas; pricing has to be built from Bradner/Matsqui/Aberdeen rural comparables plus acreage sales. SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mt-lehman.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve Mt Lehman?** Mt Lehman Elementary (K–5) at 6381 Mt Lehman Rd is in the village; SD34's feeder pattern sends its students to Eugene Reimer Middle from Grade 6, in the Rick Hansen Secondary family. Confirm a specific address in SD34's School Locator. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
- **Q: Does farm classification lower my taxes?** It can, materially — but Class 9 farm status requires actual production of qualifying agricultural products for sale, and 50% of Class 9 land value is exempt from school taxes. Verify the parcel's current BC Assessment classification rather than assuming ALR land is farm-classed. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
- **Q: What transit can I actually use day to day?** Very little. Route 21 runs along Mt Lehman Road's south end roughly hourly on weekdays with reduced weekend service, and no route reaches the village core — a car is a practical requirement for a Mt Lehman home. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=21&schedule=2026-04-05
- **Q: What PTT should I budget on an Abbotsford condo?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $497,500 fair market value (the September 2026 Abbotsford community attached median, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $7,950. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
