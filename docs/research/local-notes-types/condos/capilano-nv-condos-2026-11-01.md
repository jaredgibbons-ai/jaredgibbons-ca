# Capilano (North Vancouver) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Capilano in this file is the Capilano Road corridor in the **District** of North Vancouver (not the City): the residential streets on both sides of Capilano Road between the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1) and the Cleveland Dam, around the Capilano River canyon and the Capilano Suspension Bridge area. The District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, covers this area. Exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are Capilano Road itself, Capilano Suspension Bridge Park at 3735 Capilano Road, and the Cleveland Dam at the corridor's north end. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- Naming split a buyer will meet: District and school sources use "Upper Capilano" for the plateau north of Highway 1 (Canyon Heights, Capilano Highlands, Grousewoods) and "Lower Capilano / Lions Gate" for the Marine Drive end south of the highway. This file follows the research file and covers the Capilano Road corridor itself — the condo stock on Marine Drive south of Highway 1 (Lions Gate / Pemberton / Norgate) is a different area and is not counted here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- Condo stock on the corridor is scarce and scattered: the one verified apartment building in Section 2 sits on Capilano Road itself in the corridor's mid-section, near the Edgemont Village side. The rest of the corridor is detached houses under the District's R1 and R2 zones. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- Main streets and transit: Capilano Road is the spine — south to Marine Drive and the Lions Gate Bridge, north to the Cleveland Dam and Grouse Mountain (6400 Nancy Greene Way, reached only by Capilano Road). TransLink route 236 runs on Capilano Road the length of the corridor toward Grouse Mountain and Lonsdale Quay; route 246 crosses the corridor via Capilano Road and Montroyal Boulevard with downtown trips over the Lions Gate Bridge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- Nearby areas: Canyon Heights and Edgemont Village (east), Capilano Highlands and Grousewoods (north), Norgate and Pemberton Heights in the lowlands (south-east, below Highway 1), and — across the Capilano River — West Vancouver's Park Royal / Ambleside side. Edgemont is one of the District OCP's six Village Centres and is the corridor's walk-to/drive-to retail; no shopping centre or grocery store on the Capilano Road corridor itself is confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- Market numbers: North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Canyon Park's 14 units put it inside this requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets on a North Vancouver building is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the corridor sits under the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and Zoning Bylaw 3210. Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 zone minimum lot at 4,000 m² and the standard R2 minimum at 660 m², with modifiers down to R2(S1) at 335 m²; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a detached accessory dwelling unit. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — canyon-edge lots on this corridor are exactly the lots those areas were written for. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project on the Capilano Road corridor is confirmed, so this applies only to presales a buyer compares elsewhere in North Vancouver. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District of North Vancouver tax timing:** District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condos are there on the Capilano Road corridor, really?** Very few. One named apartment building is verified in this file — Canyon Park, 2832 Capilano Road, a 14-unit building built in 2012. The research file confirms no named condo buildings on the corridor on an official or developer source, and the corridor's housing is otherwise detached houses in the R1/R2 zones. Two Capilano Crescent strata addresses exist in listing traffic but their building names are UNCONFIRMED on a citable source (Section 2). https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/2832-capilano-road/206 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: Is the Marine Drive condo stock near Capilano Mall part of this area?** No — that is Lower Capilano / Lions Gate, south of Highway 1, in the Pemberton and Norgate sub-areas. The research file separates it from the Capilano Road corridor, and this file follows the research file. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/1330-marine-drive/201 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: Can I have a pet at Canyon Park?** The current listing source describes Canyon Park as a pet-friendly building. Confirm the exact bylaw wording on the Form B before relying on it — pet bylaws still bind, unlike rental bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/2832-capilano-road/206
- **Q: Can a strata here ban me from renting my unit out?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately on the corridor?** No. Since June 2024 a District single-family lot within the Urban Containment Boundary can hold a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — but they are one lot's ownership, not separate strata lots, unless a future rezoning and stratification occur. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Capilano Road address?** School District 44 catchments are address-based, and which plateau school a Capilano Road address feeds is UNVERIFIED from the sources read — check the exact address with SD44. The plateau elementaries beside the corridor are Canyon Heights (4501 Highland Boulevard), Highlands (3150 Colwood Drive) and Cleveland (1255 Eldon Road), with Handsworth Secondary (1033 Handsworth Road) the Handsworth-family secondary school. Do not assume Capilano Elementary: it sits at 1230 West 20th Street in the City lowlands south of this corridor. SD44 has elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) only, no middle tier. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks apply to a canyon-side purchase here?** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Areas; whether a lot is in one is a District GeoWeb check, and the District's flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. Homes below the Cleveland Dam also sit downstream of Capilano Lake — confirm the parcel's hazard and insurance picture from the GeoWeb reports. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: How does traffic affect corridor homes?** Capilano Road is the only road up to Grouse Mountain and carries the Suspension Bridge Park traffic (the park is at 3735 Capilano Road) past corridor homes. The transit offset is route 236 on Capilano Road and route 246 across the corridor toward downtown over the Lions Gate Bridge. Price road exposure street by street, not by the sub-area name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **Q: What should I check in Canyon Park's strata documents?** The Form B package: the latest depreciation report, financials, CRF balance and planned work. With 14 lots, Canyon Park is inside the post-July 1, 2024 rule requiring a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, and the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Is Capilano in the City or the District of North Vancouver?** This corridor is in the District — District OCP Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210 apply, and District tax timing (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 1, 2026) is the timing that matters. The City of North Vancouver, with its own bylaws, starts in the lowlands to the south-east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The dam above the corridor is a 1954 drinking-water dam.** The Cleveland Dam at the head of Capilano River Regional Park was completed in 1954 and stands 91 m high, holding back Capilano Lake, the drinking-water reservoir above the corridor; Fisheries and Oceans Canada's Capilano River Hatchery below the dam was completed in 1971 and normally draws about 250,000 visitors a year. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **The school with the area's name is not in the area.** Capilano Elementary, 1230 West 20th Street, carries the Capilano name but sits in the City of North Vancouver lowlands south of the corridor — marketing it as this area's school without an address-level catchment check would be wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **The library with the area's name is in Edgemont Village.** The Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, is described by the North Vancouver District Public Library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village," east of the corridor — the corridor's services cluster in the neighbouring village centre, not on Capilano Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **A detached lot here can already hold three homes' worth of occupants.** Since June 2024, a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house — three units total — are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the District's SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on non-exempt lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. That infill potential, not strata product, is the corridor's density story. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
- **The flood data is modelled to 2100.** The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m — a canyon-edge buyer's renovation budget should be priced against that horizon, not against today's water levels. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/capilano-nv.md)
