## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These strata rules are stated in full because they govern every townhome strata in Section 2 exactly as they govern apartment condos.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — but see the Pitt Meadows prohibition below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a city layer, separate from the void strata bylaws: a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Mid Meadows townhome complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022); no neighbourhood or area plan specific to Bonson Road was found in official sources. Houses sit under RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones, and RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 is the townhouse zone (minimum lot area 0.4 ha). The City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024: lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units. The 1,215 sq m figure in the R-zones is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Floodplain and dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — the guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is superseded and retired here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes; this sub-area is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Property tax and drainage levy:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The bylaw charges a separate residential drainage levy — for 2026, $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for Class 1, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 townhome complexes (2005–2007 era) are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no current residential presale project in or beside this sub-area was verified in this program. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
