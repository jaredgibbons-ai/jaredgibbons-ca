# Edmonds — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Edmonds is in southeast Burnaby, centred on Kingsway at Edmonds Street around Edmonds Station, and is one of Burnaby's four town centres. "Edmonds" is the GVR board sub-area name (listings also label it "Edmonds BE"). The City publishes no street-by-street MLS boundary; the Edmonds Community Plan (adopted March 2025) plans the centre in six districts — Central Edmonds, South Edmonds, the Station District, West Edmonds, Northwest Edmonds and East Edmonds. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- Main roads: Kingsway and Edmonds Street, with Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue serving the station area and Canada Way / 10th Avenue linking east toward New Westminster. Royal Oak and Metrotown are to the west, Highgate sits within the town centre's east side, and South Slope is to the south-west. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) the Kingsway / Highgate node — the Kings Crossing towers by Cressey at Kingsway and Edmonds Street, and the older low-rise stock on Edmonds Street itself (Toscana); (2) South Edmonds / Southgate City — Ledingham McAllister's master-planned node south of the station (Azure, Azure 2, and the Cedar Creek low-rise community on 14th Avenue / 15th Street / 16th Street). Interior streets away from Kingsway remain detached houses. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Attached is the market here: in September 2026 the sub-area recorded 89 condos & townhomes listings (7 solds, 8% sales ratio) against 9 detached listings (2 solds). At the Burnaby community level that month, condos & townhomes posted a median sale price of $686,000, $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list and 36 days on market (SnapStats GVR September 2026 — community-level figures only; no sub-area median is published). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- Transit anchor: Edmonds Station on the Expo Line, with a bus loop; TransLink route 116 also links Edmonds Station to Metrotown Station via Imperial, Sussex, Marine Drive, Marine Way, Byrne Road, Southridge Drive, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue, serving south Edmonds street by street. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. The age split in this file is the point: Toscana (2012) is entering its second report cycle while the 2020–2024 towers are in first-cycle and warranty years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Toscana's listing headline still says "No Rentals" — that half of the headline is old bylaw text, not current law; its "No Pets" half is a different matter (Section 4). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — and note the Edmonds Centre for 55+, inside Edmonds Community Centre at 7433 Edmonds Street, is a City recreation program, not a housing bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning and plans:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026 — the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is not current and must not be cited as current. Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District (June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, with multi-unit lots able to be stratified. The Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (a first sale at Azure or Azure 2), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales in Southgate City's later phases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Kingsway towers or Southgate — what's the real difference?** Vintage and form. The Kingsway node is Cressey's Kings Crossing towers (Tower 2 built 2020) plus the 2012 wood-frame Toscana low-rise; South Edmonds is Ledingham McAllister's Southgate City (Azure and Azure 2, completed 2024) and its Cedar Creek low-rises (2021–2023). All of it prices off the same Burnaby attached median ($686,000, September 2026), but warranty status, construction (concrete vs wood-frame) and depreciation-report cycles differ building by building. Sources: Section 2 entries and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Q: Toscana says "No Pets or Rentals" — can I rent it out? Can I bring a dog?** Renting, yes for a long-term tenancy: rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. The pet prohibition is different — pet bylaws remain enforceable, and a current Toscana listing records pets as "No." Treat the rental line as void and the pet line as live until the registered bylaws on the Form B say otherwise. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3178809-ph1-7738-edmonds-street-burnaby-bc-v3n-1b8 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the newer buildings?** Published examples: Kings Crossing 2 listings record "Cats OK"; Azure 2 listings record "Cats OK"; Cedar Creek's Signature Collection listings state rentals allowed and pets friendly. Full pet bylaws (numbers, size, dogs) are UNCONFIRMED from public sources for all three — read the registered bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7358-edmonds-street/1802 and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7769-park-crescent/2103 and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-east-edmonds-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=4
- **Q: How far is the SkyTrain, honestly?** Azure at Southgate City is described by its project source as two blocks from Edmonds Station; Cedar Creek listings put the walk at 8 minutes; a Kings Crossing 2 listing puts it at a 10-minute walk. Edmonds Station is on the Expo Line. https://www.skytraincondo.ca/presale/azure-at-southgate-city/ and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7133-14th-avenue/204 and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7358-edmonds-street/1802
- **Q: Is Hall Towers a condo building I can buy into?** No. Hall Towers (7264 Kingsway) is 331 units of BC Housing non-market rental housing owned by the Provincial Rental Housing Corporation — there is nothing to buy, and it is slated for replacement under the Kingsway + Edmonds master plan without displacing tenants. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=59431
- **Q: Which schools serve an Edmonds condo?** SD41 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). In-area schools include Edmonds Community Elementary (7651 18th Ave), Twelfth Avenue Elementary (7622 12th Ave) and Byrne Creek Community Secondary (7777 18th St). The verified locator run, for 7651 18th Ave, returns Edmonds Community Elementary and Byrne Creek Secondary — catchments are address-based, so run the building's exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Q: What will the Kingsway + Edmonds redevelopment do to the area around my tower?** The City and BC Housing plan for the 4.5-acre publicly owned southwest corner of Kingsway and Edmonds proposes replacing Hall Towers' 331 units without displacing tenants, plus over 1,400 purpose-built rental units and approximately 1,000 market strata units; the project received second reading June 5, 2023 and is under review to align with the Burnaby 2050 OCP. That is future supply and construction on the Kings Crossing towers' doorstep — a pricing context, not a completed fact. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Q: How competitive is it to sell a condo here?** Deep competition: 89 attached listings against 7 attached solds in September 2026 (8% sales ratio), while detached was 2 solds on 9 listings (22%). The research file's guidance is to price off the Burnaby attached median ($686,000), not off asking prices. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Q: What amenities does the neighbourhood itself provide?** Edmonds Community Centre (7433 Edmonds Street) has a 6-lane pool, leisure pool with waterslides and lazy river, steam room and sauna, two gyms and the Edmonds Centre for 55+; the Tommy Douglas Library branch serves this quadrant at the Kingsway node; the Edmonds UPCC is at #201-7315 Edmonds St; Byrne Creek Ravine Park and Taylor Park (7599 Mission Ave, with bicycle and dog parks) are the green space. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Azure carries three addresses.** The strata address in listings is 7683 Park Crescent, the developer's presentation centre was at 7662 18th Street, and unit listings also appear at 7788 18th Street — one building, three addresses in the wild. Confirm which address the specific unit's title and strata records use before ordering documents. Sources: Section 2 Azure entry.
- **Southgate City is a 15-to-20-year build-out, and Azure is only phase one.** The master plan for the former Safeway distribution site (framed by 11th and 14th Avenues and 15th and 18th Streets) contemplates up to 20 towers of 24 to 46 storeys and about 6,400 homes at completion (planning-report source, secondary, 2017) — an Azure or Cedar Creek buyer is buying into a construction horizon, with the plan's own phasing requiring separate rezonings. https://www.ssaltd.com/news-resources/20-condo-towers-planned-for-new-48-acre-southgate-neighbourhood-at-edmonds-station/
- **Cedar Creek's buildings were phased a year apart.** Listings date 7133 14th Avenue to 2021, 7579 16th Street to 2022 and 7599 15th Street to 2023 — same developer, same community name, three different warranty and depreciation-report positions. Compare the specific building's documents, not the community's marketing. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7133-14th-avenue/601 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3076712-510-7579-16th-street-burnaby-british-columbia-v3n0h7-719000/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120419-103-7599-15th-street-burnaby-british-columbia-v3n0h9-725000/brochure
- **The town centre got a brand-new plan in 2025 — and then the citywide OCP changed over it.** The Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025; the Burnaby 2050 OCP followed on December 9, 2025 and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Kingsway + Edmonds master plan is expressly under review to align with the new OCP. Listing copy citing pre-2025 Edmonds planning is out of date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Route 116 is the south Edmonds workhorse.** Beyond the SkyTrain, TransLink route 116 runs Metrotown Station to Edmonds Station via Griffiths Drive, Southridge Drive and 18th Avenue — the bus the Cedar Creek and Southgate streets actually live on, and the one listing copy rarely names. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Burnaby is also rebuilding the cycling network through the town centre.** The City is improving the Edmonds Town Centre cycling network as part of the Priority Cycling Network in its Transportation Plan (Connecting Burnaby), funded in part through TransLink regional cycling investments — a dated City program item that affects street frontages around the station node. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
