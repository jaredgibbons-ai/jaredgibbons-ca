# English Bluff, Tsawwassen (Delta) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Building-directory and listing sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- English Bluff is the west shore of Tsawwassen, in the City of Delta, running along English Bluff Road from 12 Avenue south to 1 Avenue and the Strait of Georgia shore, around English Bluff Elementary School at 402 English Bluff Road; exact City boundary lines for the name are UNVERIFIED, and "English Bluff" is not confirmed as a standalone official City of Delta neighbourhood name — Tsawwassen is the Official Community Plan community area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://eb.deltasd.bc.ca/
- The defining landmarks are Fred Gingell Park, 253 English Bluff Rd (picnic tables, trails, viewpoint, and stairs from the upper park down to the beach), and Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave (playgrounds, trails, viewpoint, washrooms, water park). https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3
- Transit on the bluff itself is community-shuttle scale: route 604 English Bluff / Bridgeport Station runs via 56 Street, 16 Avenue, Beach Grove Road, 12 Avenue, English Bluff Road and 1 Avenue with peak service to Bridgeport Station, and the 614 English Bluff shuttle loops via 1 Avenue, English Bluff Road and 12 Avenue from South Delta Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt603.pdf
- Where condo stock concentrates: it does not concentrate here at all. The Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400, focuses Tsawwassen growth on the Urban Centre, the 56 Street corridor, Southlands and Tsawwassen Springs — all outside English Bluff. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The nearest condo corridors are inland: the 56 Street / 12 Avenue town-centre cluster in Tsawwassen Central and the Springs Boulevard buildings in Tsawwassen North (covered in their own files in this series). Sources: the building entries in the Tsawwassen Central and Tsawwassen North condo notes, this series, 2026-11-01.
- Schools for the sample address 402 English Bluff Road, from the SD37 School Locator: English Bluff Elementary (K–7), 402 English Bluff Rd, and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; SD37 has no middle-school category, and catchments for other addresses are UNVERIFIED. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

There is almost no strata stock here, so this section is (a) the hazard rules that govern any future multi-unit or strata project on the bluff, and (b) the standard BC strata and tax rules a buyer needs when the search spills inland to Tsawwassen Central / North, where the condos actually are.

- **Development Permit Area SD2 — the rule that defines this shore.** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 names "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)," Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions): the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, the ocean-frontage slopes are subject to erosion, and existing vegetation and tree cover on the slopes must be protected. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SD2 building rules:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation, business or storage of goods damageable by floodwaters, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding is required; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The beach is not part of the lot.** Delta states foreshore and water use is interjurisdictional (federal Fisheries Act, provincial Water Sustainability Act and Riparian Areas Regulation, flood protection requirements and Delta bylaws), and the foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Delta zoning and plan:** housing sits under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (RS zones on this shore); the current OCP is the consolidated Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — it has no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the old Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950), and farm land in the plan is designated "Agriculture (A)." https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH (the only route to small strata-type product here):** Delta's Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing amendments — Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — amended Zoning Bylaws No. 7600 and No. 2750 to permit SSMUH on properties that permit single detached or duplex dwellings; Delta's notice to the Province states the bylaws allow four units on all residential properties in the City, and six units on properties within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (Scott Road is in North Delta, not Tsawwassen). On this shore the SD2 hazard conditions still sit on top of that permission. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Form B (applies the moment a buyer looks at a strata unit inland):** under the Strata Property Act, s. 59, the Form B: Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the rules, current budget, any owner-developer Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report must be attached. The strata must provide it within 7 days of request; the maximum charge is $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for copying. Since 2023, Form Bs must also provide summaries of strata insurance and electrical planning reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are no longer waivable for most stratas:** regulations in force July 1, 2024 require strata corporations with five or more strata lots to obtain a depreciation report every five years, and the report can no longer be deferred by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection of repair and replacement costs and three CRF cash-flow models. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, with no bylaw amendment needed. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — only 55+ survives:** under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to age 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **BC Property Transfer Tax exemptions a condo buyer checks first:** the first-time home buyers' program exempts PTT on the first $500,000 of purchase price where the property's fair market value is $835,000 or less (effective Apr 1, 2024), with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000, on land 0.5 hectares or smaller used only as the buyer's principal residence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Newly built home exemption (not first-time-only):** full PTT exemption threshold $1,100,000 fair market value, phasing out over the $50,000 range above it and fully eliminated at $1,150,000 (effective Apr 1, 2024); a unit in a newly built condo building not occupied since construction can qualify. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on new construction only:** the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026) together with the GST/HST new housing rebate returns 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more, maximum $50,000, for agreements in the Mar 20, 2025–2031 window; the older new housing rebate alone is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer has a statutory 7-day rescission period under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, running from the later of signing the purchase agreement or receiving/acknowledging the disclosure statement, with the deposit refunded on rescission; deposits are held in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Federal savings and insurance figures:** Home Buyers' Plan withdrawal limit $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (figures verified against CRA pages in this program's earlier passes). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC mortgage loan insurance premiums** on the published homeowner schedule run from 0.60% of the loan (loan-to-value up to 65%) to 4.50% (90.01%–95% LTV with a non-traditional down payment). https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** the 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in English Bluff at all?** None verified. No named strata condominium building inside English Bluff was confirmed on any City, developer, manager or directory source in this pass, and the area research file found the same — the stock is freehold detached housing under the RS zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/english-bluff-guide.md and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Q: Where do English Bluff downsizers actually buy a condo?** Inland, in the two verified clusters: the 56 Street / 12 Avenue town-centre buildings in Tsawwassen Central, and Tsawwassen Springs on Springs Boulevard in Tsawwassen North. The OCP itself focuses Tsawwassen growth on the Urban Centre, the 56 Street corridor, Southlands and Tsawwassen Springs — not on the bluff. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Could a bluff lot be split into a small strata or multiplex instead?** Delta's SSMUH bylaws (Nos. 8394 and 8395, adopted Jun 24, 2024) allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, but on this shore the SD2 hazard permit area sits on top of that permission, with a Professional Engineer's conditions, a Flood Construction Level and a registered flood covenant. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What does SD2 mean for insurance and financing on a bluff property?** The hazard designation is in the City's own plan — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence — so it will surface in any serious buyer's, lender's or insurer's search; the research file's advice is to confirm insurability and lender comfort early. Whether any specific insurer will write a specific bluff lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is the beach in front of a waterfront house private?** No. The foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC, and Delta notes foreshore use is interjurisdictional — upland ownership does not include the beach below the natural boundary. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q: Can I clear trees to open up the view?** Carefully, if at all: the SD2 justification requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes, and that vegetation is part of what holds the slope. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What transit serves the bluff if I give up a car?** Route 604 (peak service to Bridgeport Station) and the 614 community shuttle loop, both via English Bluff Road, 12 Avenue and 1 Avenue to South Delta Exchange — a shuttle-scale service, not a frequent-transit corridor. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt603.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a bluff address?** Address-based in SD37. For the sample address 402 English Bluff Road: English Bluff Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12); check any other address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: If I buy a resale condo inland instead, can the strata stop me renting it out later?** No — every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. A 55+ age bylaw, where one exists, still governs who may live in the unit, tenant included. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I read before subject removal on any Tsawwassen strata unit?** The Form B (fees, owing amounts, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary), with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must refresh its depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The OCP deliberately steers growth away from this shore.** Bylaw No. 8400 focuses Tsawwassen growth on the Urban Centre, the 56 Street corridor, Southlands and Tsawwassen Springs — a named major project inside English Bluff is UNVERIFIED, because the plan does not put one there. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **The flood covenant runs with the land.** SD2's guidelines require a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding — it will show on title and bind the next owner, which is why bluff listings have to be read title-first. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The stairs at Fred Gingell Park are the public beach access for the upper bluff** — a City report states stairs run from the upper park at 253 English Bluff Rd down to the beach, which matters to homes on the landward side with no stairs of their own. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search?page=3
- **Delta's flood system is municipal-scale, and the bluff is its edge case:** the City operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure — but SD2 lots carry site-specific engineer-set conditions on top of that system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **The RS3 lot standard explains why there is no condo product to find:** a 4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area (estate-scale by Delta standards, where the wider Bylaw 7600 ladder starts at RS7's 335 m²) keeps this shore in large detached lots. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Health care is inland:** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Boulevard in Ladner, is the nearest hospital, and Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826-4949 Canoe Pass Way in Tsawwassen. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
