## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a 1977–1998 low-rise, the depreciation report's envelope, roof and parkade items are the levy story. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" (for example Tsawwassen Greene's, Heron Cove's) or "rentals allowed with restrictions" (Royal Oaks, Windgrove, Windsor Woods) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Directly relevant to The Lexington, the one Section 2 building with a stated 55+ restriction in listing sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)"; the OCP focuses commercial uses along 56th Street and in the Tsawwassen Urban Centre and adds Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines. City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (RS1–RS3 subdivision minimum 4,000 m²; RS4 610 m²; RS5 550/460 m²; RS6 390 m²; RS7 335 m²; RD1 836 m²; RD4 504 m², parcel by parcel), while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **PTT in this band:** 1% on the first $200,000 and 2% on the portion to $2,000,000 — a purchase under $1.8M reaches only those two tiers, never the 3% tier or the further 2% on residential value over $3 million, unless an exemption reduces it further. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. Both thresholds sit inside this band's lower range, unlike the over-$1.8M band where neither reaches. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant at this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — above this band's ceiling, so a principal residence purchased inside the band is under the phase-out threshold on value alone (eligibility rules still apply). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
