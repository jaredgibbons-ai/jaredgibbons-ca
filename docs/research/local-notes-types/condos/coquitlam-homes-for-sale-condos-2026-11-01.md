**City-wide method — read first.** This is a whole-city file. Its Section 2 roster is assembled from this program's OWN verified sub-area condo files in ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/ — each building is named once, in the sub-area file that verified it, and this file cross-references those entries and adds NO new unverified stock. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. In this city's roster the reports land very differently by era: the 1981–1993 stock (Eagle Ridge Court, Bromley Manor, Brookside Manor, the Princess Crescent cluster) is in the roof/envelope cycle, while the 2019–2026 station towers are in warranty and early-life cycles. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2 wherever a directory line survives (Brookside Manor, Eagle Ridge Court, 455 Bromley Street). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Coquitlam's own short-term rental rules: UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. In this roster only Princess Gate is published as 55+ — confirm the filed bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** zoning is **Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996**. The governing plan is the **Interim Official Community Plan adopted April 27, 2026 (Bylaws 5511/5512)**, superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001 — Council's streamlined Interim OCP consolidated a former OCP of more than 1,000 pages (55 land use designations reduced to 18, over 60 maps simplified to 19) and incorporates provincial housing legislation requirements, with a comprehensive OCP review anticipated to begin in 2027. Small-scale residential zones R-1 through R-4: up to four dwelling units in low-density neighbourhoods, with R-1/R-2 caps of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger, in forms including secondary suites, laneway homes, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes. A Coquitlam secondary-suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED — the City guide read in this program confirms suites as a permitted form but states no cap and no owner-occupancy rule. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1932 and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Watercourse protection:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in most of Coquitlam (50 metres in Northeast Coquitlam), and Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s. 523 covers riparian areas — relevant to slope-side strata capital planning and, at Fraser Mills, to flood construction level, strata insurance and deductible position on a Fraser River floodplain site. (Sub-area files: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-coquitlam-condos-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cape-horn-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Pine & Glen and the Fraser Mills presale contracts; a first sale at Elements at Burquitlam), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $625,000 (the Coquitlam community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $8,500 (2% × $425,000) = **$10,500 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Pine & Glen and Fraser Mills purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the governing frame for Pine & Glen (705 condos, est. 2028) and the Fraser Mills buildings (Debut, Encore, Chapter). Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
