"VE" is the GVR board's Vancouver East suffix; the part west of Main is Mount Pleasant VW. Exact board sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" or a rental cap for a Mount Pleasant VE strata is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Vancouver's Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs, as amended by site-specific CD-1 bylaws along the corridors; the Broadway Plan (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 2024) is the area plan for this sub-area, planning buildings up to 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station — the plan is policy, not zoning, and most higher-density forms still require rezoning. Citywide land-use direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. For detached-house lots, By-law No. 13817 replaced the RS districts with the R1-1 district, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3%:** the City charges 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026 — a live holding cost for any vacant Mount Pleasant VE unit. City 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-pleasant-ve.md)
- **Short-term rentals:** BC's Short-Term Rental Accommodations Act principal-residence requirement has applied in Vancouver since May 1, 2024 (stays under 90 consecutive days limited to the host's principal residence plus one secondary suite or accessory unit on the same property, with provincial registration), and a strata may still limit or ban short-term rentals by bylaw — separate from the void long-term rental restrictions above. https://venturepacific.ca/journal/short-term-rental-rules-multiplex-units-bc
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to developer sales at Main & Sixth). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (illustration only — no Mount Pleasant VE or Vancouver median is published in any source this program cites), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (Main & Sixth, expected completion 2027), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many named apartment-condo buildings does Mount Pleasant VE have?** Four verified in this file: The Independent at Main (285 E 10th Ave), The District (250 E 6th Ave / 255 E 7th Ave, 233 units, built 2012), Cartier Place (3131 Main St, built 1991) and the Main & Sixth presale (73 condos, expected 2027). The sub-area also carries extensive rental walk-up stock that is not condo stock. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Is there any 55+ condo I can buy in Mount Pleasant VE?** No verified one — the 55+ program found no age-restricted strata in the sub-area; its seniors buildings (Cavell Gardens, The Aster) are rental. A 55+ buyer here is buying a regular condo with no age bylaw. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/mount-pleasant-ve-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Mount Pleasant VE condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are a different layer: the provincial principal-residence rule and any strata short-term bylaw still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the Empty Homes Tax mean for a vacant unit here?** Budget 3% of assessed taxable value per year, on top of ordinary property tax, with an annual declaration due each February — it applies to investor-held vacant stock in this sub-area. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain actually reach Mount Pleasant?** On the Province's schedule, fall 2027: Mount Pleasant Station is at Main Street and Broadway, and Broadway's four lanes at the station block reopened in July 2026. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026TT0046-000500.pdf
- **Q: The Independent's listings and directory disagree on its age — which is right?** Neither is settled in this file: the directory states 2017 and listings record 2019, so the year is UNCONFIRMED here. Settle it from the strata plan and Form B documents, because the year drives depreciation-report and warranty expectations. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Are the heritage apartment buildings on Broadway buyable condos?** Not on any source verified here: the Lee Building and Wenonah Apartments are carried as heritage apartment buildings in the City's context statement, with no strata ownership verified — do not treat a heritage apartment address as condo stock without a title check. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/mount-pleasant-historic-context-statement.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mount Pleasant VE condo address?** SD39 catchments are address-based; the one locator run on record (2600 East Broadway) returned Chief Maquinna Elementary / Vancouver Technical Secondary, and the research file names Mount Pleasant, Florence Nightingale and Queen Alexandra elementaries in and serving the area. Any other address is UNVERIFIED until run. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/039
- **Q: Is the north edge in a floodplain?** The Great Northern Way lowland is former False Creek shoreline inside the City's designated floodplain, with flood construction levels under the Building By-law; the grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the slope. Check the specific parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $800,000 fair market value (illustration only; no Vancouver median is published in the sources cited), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $14,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
