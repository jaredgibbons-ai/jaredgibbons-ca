## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that explains this whole file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. The Agricultural Land Commission's Information Bulletin 09 (issued October 18, 2022), s. 6, treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision; under the Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation, and the Strata Property Act route (s. 239) does not get around that bar. Practical consequence: farm ground in Ladner Rural produces farm houses and farm dwelling units, never a strata condo building — which is why Section 2 is zero. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Confirm ALR status parcel by parcel:** a specific parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **A1 dwelling rules (the housing this ground actually allows):** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate); one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha — on the same farm home plate, with ALC approval required for more than one; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. Accessory uses also include one secondary suite accessory to a farm house and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Migrant farm worker housing in A1:** permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha; new buildings for it must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no area-plan schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (the A1 zone sheet above is a City zoning document), while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/ladner-rural.md)
- **Form B (for completeness — it has nothing to attach to here):** Strata Property Act s. 59 requires a strata corporation to provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Ladner Rural there is no verified strata apartment building for a buyer to request one from; if a float-home interest is presented as strata, the Form B and the strata plan are exactly the documents that would prove it — request them before treating any such claim as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata rules, stated for the same reason):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; minimum CRF contribution at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. These rules bind strata corporations — none is verified in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata rules):** rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022, and only a 55+ age bylaw is lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — both are strata-bylaw rules and neither has a verified building to apply to in Ladner Rural. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Ladner Rural?** No verified apartment condos — zero. The sub-area's housing is farm houses and farm dwelling units on ALR/A1 ground (which cannot be stratified) plus float homes on West River Road, which are moorage product, not strata apartment condos. Sources: Section 2 entries; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Why can't a farm property here be turned into condos?** Because stratification is a subdivision, and ALR land cannot be subdivided without Agricultural Land Commission permission: ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision, and ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is a Canoe Pass float home a condo?** Not on any source checked. The listing texts present the West River Road homes with strata-style language and boat slips, but the community's tenure, moorage and legal structure are UNVERIFIED — no official, developer or strata source for it was opened. Diligence the tenure first: establish what is actually owned (the float, the slip, any lease or strata interest) before pricing it like a condo. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Ladner Rural?** Delta's A1 zone permits one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a Ladner Rural farm have a second dwelling?** Yes, within limits: one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house; more than one needs ALC approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Ladner Rural farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, apart from the home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: How do I check whether a parcel is actually in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — the ALC states the online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What should I check on a rural parcel besides the house?** Water and flood position: Delta's agricultural ditches run in drainage mode in winter and irrigation mode in summer under City control gates, the whole lowland sits behind the City's dike system, and the parcel's flood construction level is property-specific — get all three from the City and the parcel record, not from a listing's wording. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Which schools serve a Ladner Rural address?** SD37, address-based — the Ladner schools confirmed against the Ministry K-12 file are listed in Section 1, but no locator result was run for any rural address, so the catchment for a specific farm or float-home address is UNVERIFIED until it is run through SD37's route. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
