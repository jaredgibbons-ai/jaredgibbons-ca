# Sumas Mountain — Condos: Local Notes (Abbotsford / FVRD)

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sumas Mountain rises between Abbotsford and Chilliwack, above Sumas Prairie to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north. The built part is inside the City of Abbotsford; the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- City-side neighbourhoods on the west and south slopes include Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side; Clayburn Village sits at the northwest foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave
- Schools on the slopes: Auguston Traditional Elementary, 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive; Mountain Elementary, 2299 Mountain Drive; Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary, 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive. No secondary school is on the mountain. https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/
- Transit: BC Transit routes 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee) and 15 (Auguston) serve the built slopes and the foot. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7
- Where condo stock actually concentrates: nowhere on the mountain. There is no tower, no low-rise apartment corridor and no town centre on the slopes — the housing is detached houses in the City neighbourhoods and rural homes on large lots above. SnapStats recorded attached (condos & townhomes) inventory of 0 and solds of 0 for Sumas Mountain in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/).

Rental/pet/age restrictions: UNCONFIRMED from the sources above — read the strata's filed bylaws.

Building-directory source (BCCondosAndHomes, secondary) records it as built in 2007 and records its bylaws as "pets allowed, pets allowed w/rest."; a directory page for a variant address (36169 Lower Sumas Mtn Road) records built 2008 and "rentals allowed w/restrictions" — the two directory entries conflict on year and rental wording, so year built is best stated as 2007/2008: UNCONFIRMED to a single year, and any rental notation is in any event overridden for long-term rentals by the November 24, 2022 provincial change (Section 3). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B (Strata Property Act, s.59)** — the disclosure certificate for any strata lot (including a bare-land strata lot at Straiton or a townhouse at Junction Creek): monthly fee, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, proceedings, plus an insurance summary required since April 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports** — stratas with 5+ lots must obtain one and renew at least every five years; pre-July 1, 2024 FVRD stratas with none had to obtain one by July 1, 2026. **CRF minimum** — at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the CRF at each AGM budget since November 1, 2023. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental bylaws void** since November 24, 2022 (long-term); short-term rentals may still be restricted. Directory lines such as "rentals allowed w/restrictions" for Junction Creek do not restore a long-term rental ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2)** — the only permitted age bylaw is 55+, with the May 1, 2023 expanded exemptions. No mountain complex above is recorded as 55+ in any source checked: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Two governments, two rule books.** City side: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2400 (2014) — Country Residential minimum lot 2.0 ha, Suburban Residential 0.4 ha, Rural Residential 4.0 ha — and Abbotsforward 2050, OCP Bylaw 2600-2016, with the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) as OCP Schedule E for the west slope. Rural side: the FVRD Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated OCP Bylaw No. 584, with no public sewer or water at rural density (wells and septic). https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH.** Abbotsford Bylaw 3560-2024 (adopted June 25, 2024) implemented provincial small-scale multi-unit housing in the City's Zoning Bylaw. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf SSMUH rules for the rural FVRD part of the mountain were NOT confirmed in the research: UNVERIFIED. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/sumas-mountain-guide.md, UNVERIFIED list)
- **Steep-slope control.** The City OCP includes a Steep Slope Development Permit Area, and the McKee plan protects unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% as open space — the practical reason future multi-family form here is planned as ground-oriented product at the foot, not apartment buildings on the slopes. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **GST on new construction.** 5% GST on new homes; FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) — 100% to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html Older GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out (task brief figure).
- **PTT.** 1% / 2% / 3% general structure (+2% over $3M residential); first-time buyer exemption on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales / REDMA.** Disclosure statement and 7-day rescission; deposits in trust; assignment only as the contract permits. No presale project on the mountain itself was verified — the McKee-foot proposal in Section 5 is a rezoning-stage proposal, not a presale. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP $60,000 / FHSA $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime.** https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** of the mortgage amount; insurance required under 20% down on homes $1.5M or less. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Tax split on the mountain:** City-side taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026). Rural FVRD-side property is taxed by the Province (Surveyor of Taxes), due July 2 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, with 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any condo tower or low-rise I can buy on Sumas Mountain?** No — no apartment-condo building was verified on the mountain, and attached inventory was 0 in September 2026. The strata choices that exist are detached-house bare-land strata (Straiton) and a townhouse complex at the foot (Junction Creek). Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata, and how is Straiton different from a condo?** You own your detached house and lot as a strata lot and share common property (roads, gate, landscaping) with the strata corporation — the City report records 26 single-family strata lots in EPS1299 at 4595 Sumas Mountain Road. Fees, bylaws and a Form B apply exactly as in a condo, but there is no shared building envelope. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Junction Creek?** The building directory records "pets allowed, pets allowed w/rest." for the complex — confirm the exact number/size rules in the filed bylaws attached to the Form B, because directory summaries conflict between its own pages. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/junction-creek-36169-lower-sumas-mountain-road
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata home on the mountain?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022; a strata may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What will strata fees cover in a bare-land strata?** Only what that strata's budget covers — typically common roads, gates, landscaping and insurance on common property, not your house's roof or exterior. The specific inclusions for Straiton or Junction Creek are in their budgets/Form B: UNCONFIRMED from public sources.
- **Q: How do I check a complex's finances and coming levies?** Form B for the CRF balance and approved levies, plus the depreciation report (mandatory for 5+ lot stratas, five-year renewal; FVRD catch-up deadline was July 1, 2026). https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **Q: Which schools — and why do neighbours get different answers?** Because the mountain splits. Locator runs returned Auguston Traditional → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary for 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, but Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw → W.A. Fraser Middle → Yale Secondary for 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive. Run the exact address. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q: Is the mountain in the floodplain?** The mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie, named in City flood notices. A slope lot's issue is geotechnical, not flood: the OCP's Steep Slope Development Permit Area applies on slope land. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Q: What about quarry noise and trucks if I buy near the main road?** Five quarries operate on Sumas Mountain and their gravel trucks use an access road to Highway 1 through the Lightning Rock site, per Semá:th First Nation; a City report also covers gravel extraction at Jamieson Quarry. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/
- **Q: City water/sewer or well/septic?** It depends which side of the boundary the lot is on: the rural FVRD part has no public sewer or water at rural density and homes use wells and onsite (septic) services. Confirm servicing for the specific lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The next housing on the mountain is planned at its foot, in ground-oriented form.** The City's July 10, 2025 council-consideration notice for Bylaw No. 3761-2025 (file PRJ20-108) describes a McKee Road lands proposal of approximately 108 single detached lots, 192 townhouses and four rural residential lots, with about 5 ha of park/open-space land. It is a proposal at the foot of the mountain — not a presale, and not condos. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023)** is Schedule E of the City OCP and guides the west-slope mountainside neighbourhood; its Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation is where townhome/multiplex forms are planned. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2016b2600schedulee
- **A new McKee neighbourhood park** is in planning near Dawson Road and Sumas Mountain Road, and a McKee Trail Strategy is being built for the trail network — both signal where City investment is going on the slopes: parks and ground-oriented neighbourhoods, not condo sites. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/input-wanted-development-mckee-trail-strategy
- **Lightning Rock was returned to Semá:th (Sumas) First Nation.** Semá:th states ownership of the culturally significant Lightning Rock site on the mountain was transferred to the Nation under an agreement with the Province, and that an alternate quarry-truck route study was agreed by the Province, Semá:th and the City — context any buyer on the quarry routes should know. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/
- **Chilliwack got an SSMUH extension next door, Abbotsford did not need the same one for this area:** the Province's September 16, 2024 bulletin lists Chilliwack among communities granted a partial extension (Proper neighbourhood / Hazel Park, to December 31, 2030) — relevant if a buyer is comparing the Chilliwack side of the mountain. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Thin market warning:** with only 3 detached listings and 0 sales recorded for the sub-area in September 2026, there is no meaningful condo (or any attached) price signal on the mountain — anyone quoting one is using Abbotsford-wide attached figures, which combine condos and townhomes city-wide. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/).
