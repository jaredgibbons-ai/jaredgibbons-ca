## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

### Strata Property Act — resale and ownership

- **Form B — Information Certificate (s.59).** On a resale, the strata corporation provides a Form B stating the unit's monthly strata fee, any amount owed, any special levy already approved, the CRF balance, parking and storage entitlements, any judgments/proceedings, and — since April 1, 2023 — a summary of the strata's insurance coverage. The BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports.** Effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Strata fees and the CRF.** Since November 1, 2023, strata corporations must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving budgets. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** Since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata corporation in BC may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — existing ones are invalid. Any directory line for a building in this band stating "rentals not allowed" is void as a long-term rental restriction. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — 55+ only (ss.123.1 / 123.2).** The only permitted age-restriction bylaw is 55+, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and — expanded May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner and defined children. The bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

### Taxes and rebates — purchase

- **GST — resale vs new.** Resale condos are generally exempt from GST; new construction carries 5% GST in BC. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4).** The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) rebates 100% of the GST on a new home valued up to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M — note the phase-out ends exactly at this page's ceiling — for agreements entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST New Housing Rebate (the older rebate, still in place).** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out for homes between $350,000 and $450,000.
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) — general rates.** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Inside this band only the 1% and 2% tiers are ever met. PTT is based on fair market value on the day the transfer is registered at the Land Title Office, and is not the same as annual property taxes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT — first-time home buyers' program.** Full exemption conditions: principal residence only, fair market value $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), 0.5 hectares or smaller, only residential improvements — exempting PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000). A partial exemption may apply between $835,000 and $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **PTT — newly built home exemption.** Full exemption threshold $1,100,000 (effective April 1, 2024), phasing out over the $50,000 range above it, eliminated at $1,150,000. A buyer above $1,150,000 in this band gets neither BC exemption. The two exemptions are mutually exclusive — elect the larger. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Additional PTT.** A foreign national, foreign corporation or taxable trustee must also pay additional PTT on the residential portion in a specified area; the rate is published on a separate provincial page not re-read for the research file, so the rate is UNVERIFIED here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/homes-under-1500000.md)

### Presales and assignments — REDMA

- **Disclosure and 7-day rescission.** A developer must give the buyer a disclosure statement filed with BCFSA before the contract binds; the buyer may cancel within 7 days after the later of signing the purchase agreement and receiving/acknowledging the disclosure statement, with the deposit refunded from trust. New condos priced inside this band are where the new-build branch (GST + newly built exemption) usually applies. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

### Financing supports

- **CMHC mortgage insurance — the band's defining rule.** Required when the down payment is under 20% on a home priced $1.5M or less; at $1,500,000 or more insurance is not available at all. Minimum down payment is 5% on the first $500,000 and 10% on the portion above. Published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00% of the mortgage amount (0.6%–4.5% range including the 4.50% top band with a non-traditional down payment), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies, and the premium may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **FHSA.** CRA describes the FHSA as a registered plan letting a first-time buyer save for a qualifying first home tax-free up to set limits; participation room in the first year the account is opened is $8,000, lifetime $40,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **HBP.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows a $60,000 RRSP withdrawal per buyer for a qualifying home, and FHSA and HBP can be combined for the same home when all conditions are met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/campaigns/cra-guide-taxes-housing-benefits.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why does this page stop at $1.5 million — is that just an asking-price filter?** It is a price filter, but the line also matches a federal financing rule: CMHC states homes $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available, while homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 need only 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the rest. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: How many condos are for sale under $1.5M right now?** No sourced number exists. The live feed showed 238 listings in total at the crawl moment (November 1, 2026), but that is the feed's own changing count across all property types, not a condo count — SnapStats publishes no price-band cut, so no band count, share or median is stated in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: Are these Jared Gibbons' own listings?** No. The cards are MLS® Reciprocity listings from many brokerages (RE/MAX offices, Century 21 Coastal Realty, Oakwyn Realty, LPT Realty ULC and others on page one); the page footer states the data comes in part from the Reciprocity program of GVR, FVREB or CADREB, and the boards assume no responsibility for its accuracy. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: Why is page one all detached houses if I want a condo?** Page one showed 12 detached-led Residential cards priced $985,000–$1,499,900, in the upper part of the band; the sort order producing that is not stated on the page. The band's condo stock sits lower in the band — filter by type rather than browsing the feed in order. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: How much down payment do I need at $1.4 million?** On CMHC's published rules: 5% × $500,000 ($25,000) plus 10% × $900,000 ($90,000) = $115,000 minimum, with mortgage insurance required because the down payment is under 20%. At $1,500,000 the minimum jumps to 20% ($300,000) and insurance disappears. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: How much PTT would I pay on a $1.4 million home in this band?** On the general tiers alone: 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% × $1,200,000 ($24,000) = $26,000 — nothing in this band reaches the 3% tier or the further 2% over $3,000,000; a BC exemption could reduce the bill further if you qualify (a newly built purchase at $1.4M gets neither, because the newly built exemption is gone at $1,150,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does a new condo in this band still get the GST rebate?** The FTHB GST/HST Rebate rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000) and phases out to $1.5M — so a new condo priced between $1M and $1.5M gets a partial rebate, and at the page's ceiling the rebate reaches zero. Agreements must be on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: A listing under $1.5M says "rentals not allowed" — can I still rent it out?** Long-term: yes in law — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw; check the filed bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the feed NOT tell me about a condo in this band?** The building. Strata fees, approved levies, CRF health and deductible exposure live in the Form B package, not in a price-filtered feed — and the feed itself stamps its data "last updated November 1, 2026 at 03:40 PM (UTC)" with a notice that the boards assume no responsibility for accuracy. Confirm status, price and tenure on the specific home before acting. https://jaredgibbons.ca/homes-under-1500000.html
- **Q: I'm selling just above $1.5M — does the line affect my buyer pool?** Yes: a list price just under $1,500,000 keeps a home inside the insured-mortgage universe for buyers with less than 20% down; a price at or over it removes that buyer group entirely. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two federal lines end at exactly this page's ceiling.** The insured-mortgage line (20% down, no insurance at $1,500,000+) and the FTHB GST rebate phase-out (to $1.5M) both terminate at $1,500,000 — the band is defined by financing law as much as by budget. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **March 13, 2026 — the FTHB GST rebate became law.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 per CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122; some secondary coverage says March 12 — this file follows the CRA. The rebate reaches back to agreements on or after March 20, 2025. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **April 1, 2024 is the date inside both BC thresholds in this band.** The first-time program's $835,000 ceiling (partial to $860,000) and the newly built exemption's $1,100,000 threshold (out at $1,150,000) both took effect that day — material using older thresholds is pre-2024. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
