Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Downtown is New Westminster's historic Columbia Street core and Fraser River waterfront, between the river and the Royal Avenue slope, planned through the Downtown Community Plan (Schedule C of the OCP). Precise Downtown Community Plan boundary roads: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Boundary with the Quay — read this first.** The board lists Downtown and Quay as two separate sub-areas, even though the City's OCP neighbourhoods map names one area "Downtown (including Quayside)." This file covers the board's Downtown only: the Columbia Street / Carnarvon Street core above the waterfront. Buildings on Quayside Drive (for example Pier West, 660 Quayside Drive) belong to the separate quay file and are NOT counted here, and one Columbia Street tower — Trapp + Holbrook, 668 Columbia Street — is classified Quay in current listings, so it is not counted here either (see Section 2). - Where the condo stock concentrates: two clusters — the Plaza 88 / Carnarvon Street towers wrapped around New Westminster Station (Azure 1, Azure 2, Marinus, Ovation, 618 Carnarvon), and the older Columbia / Carnarvon / Agnes stock west and east of the stations (Westminster Towers, Carnarvon Place, Carnarvon Towers, C2C Lofts, News North, Argyle House, First & Royal). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Sub-area attached volume: Downtown carried 102 condos & townhomes listings with 15 solds in September 2026 (15% sales ratio) and zero detached listings — the second-largest attached inventory of any New Westminster sub-area, behind Uptown's 108. SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: the New Westminster condos & townhomes median was $560,000 in September 2026 ($587/sqft, 95% sale-to-list, 27 days on market). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- Transit anchors: New Westminster Station and Columbia Station, both on the Expo Line, are in Downtown; TransLink routes listed for New Westminster Station include 102, 103, 105, 106, 109, 112 and 123. The Province's Transit Oriented Development Areas enable density within 800 metres of the city's five SkyTrain stations, two of which are in Downtown. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- Main roads: Columbia Street, Carnarvon Street, 8th Street, Royal Avenue and McBride Boulevard. Landmarks: the Anvil Centre, 777 Columbia Street (civic and cultural centre, with a library kiosk and holds locker), Westminster Pier Park and the Waterfront Esplanade at the foot of Columbia Street, and École Qayqayt Elementary, 85 Merivale Street. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Downtown's 1976–1996 stock (Argyle House, Carnarvon Place, Westminster Towers, Carnarvon Towers) this file matters more than the finishes. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line showing a rental cap or ban in the Section 2 buildings reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, including Columbia Street historic and high-rise commercial districts. The governing plan is Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), with the Downtown Community Plan as Schedule C of the OCP; Downtown is designated a Regional City Centre, and the OCP is under a 2025–2026 update process (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 8574, 2026). Provincial Transit Oriented Development Areas enable density within 800 metres of the city's five SkyTrain stations — two of them in Downtown. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study places the Downtown waterfront in Floodplain Zone 3 — the Fraser River waterfront zone — where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level, but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the specific building's flood construction level compliance and the strata's insurance and deductible position before subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City also operates its own electrical utility alongside City water and sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (relevant to First & Royal developer sales and any 811 Carnarvon presale contracts), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the framework for any future 811 Carnarvon or Columbia Square contracts. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Downtown the same market as the Quay?** No. On the board taxonomy they are separate sub-areas — Downtown is the Columbia Street core above the waterfront; the Quay is the Quayside Drive waterfront pocket, researched in its own file. In September 2026 the Quay sold 16 of 59 listings (27%) against Downtown's 15 of 102 (15%) — do not quote one area's momentum for the other. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Q: Why is Trapp + Holbrook not in this file if it's on Columbia Street?** Because current listings classify 668 Columbia Street under the Quay sub-area, and this program follows the board classification. The building (2015, 196 homes, heritage Trapp and Holbrook blocks) is covered in the quay file. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3067333-668-columbia-street-1610-new-westminster-bc-v3m-1a9/brochure
- **Q: Can I buy a unit in the tallest tower at New Westminster Station?** No. Aqua at Plaza 88 (900 Carnarvon Street, 40 storeys, 398 units, completed 2019) is a purpose-built rental building held by a single owner — there are no strata units to buy. The buyable Plaza 88 towers are Azure 1, Azure 2 and Marinus. https://storeys.com/starlight-investments-aqua-plaza88-listing/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Downtown condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever an older listing or directory line says. Short-term rentals are separate and can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** Downtown: 102 condos & townhomes listings, 15 solds (15% sales ratio), and no detached stock at all. At the New Westminster community level the condos & townhomes median was $560,000, $587/sqft, 95% sale-to-list and 27 days on market. SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians and no building-level figures. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Downtown address?** School District 40 catchments are address-based (elementary K–5, middle 6–8, secondary 9–12). The locator result recorded in the research file for 85 Merivale Street is École Qayqayt Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary; check the exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Q: Is a Downtown condo in the floodplain?** The waterfront is — the City's floodplain study puts the Downtown waterfront in Floodplain Zone 3, where many newer buildings sit above the Flood Construction Level while egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the building's compliance, strata insurance and deductible before subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)

- **The newest Downtown building carries an 1890 house in its plaza.** First & Royal's developer restored the E.M.N. Woods House (built 1890, designed by Samuel Maclure), moved it 65 feet south on the same street, and made it the centre of the project plaza — heritage fabric inside a brand-new wood-frame strata. https://firstandroyal.com/
- **Two Downtown towers have unsettled build years in public sources.** 618 Carnarvon's building snapshot says 2022 while unit records say 2023 and some listings say 2024; First & Royal's directory says 2025 while its listings say 2026. This file flags both conflicts rather than picking silently — the strata plan registration records settle them. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3168756-2201-618-carnarvon-street-new-westminster-bc-v3m-0n9 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/first-royal-by-areva-82-1-street
- **Ovation's address changed between project and pavement.** The Domus Homes project was published at 813 Carnarvon Street; the completed tower's civic address in listings is 823 Carnarvon Street. Searches under only one number miss half the record. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/news/blog/ovation-813-carnarvon-street-new-westminster-204-homes-in-a-32-storey-tower-by-domus-homes
- **Downtown has two stations and a provincial density overlay.** New Westminster and Columbia stations are both in Downtown, and the Province's Transit Oriented Development Areas enable density within 800 metres of the stations — the frame behind both the 811 Carnarvon proposal and the Columbia Square redevelopment application, neither of which is strata stock today. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md) and https://storeys.com/new-westminster-88-tenth-street-columbia-square-shopping-plaza-shopping-centre/
