### Named in sources but NOT counted — with the reason stated

- **Any building whose only evidence is its zone:** an apartment, townhouse or manufactured-home zone is not a building, not an age restriction and not a rental rule — do not label product from a zone card. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Old Surrey zone codes in listing text:** "RF-12" and the other RF/RH/RQ codes no longer exist in that form — Surrey's R1–R7 zones replaced them on July 8, 2024 (RF-12/RF-12C → R4; RF-SD → R6). A listing quoting an RF code is describing a repealed bylaw label, not a current zone and not a building. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)

### Tenure traps — excluded, with the reason stated

- **Manufactured-home park zones are not condo stock and not seniors zones:** the Township's MH-1 Residential Mobile Home Park Zone (minimum new lot 1.7 ha per its directory card), Surrey's RM-M Manufactured Home Residential Zone, and Abbotsford's RSMH/RSH and RH1 (manufactured home park) zones are form zones for pad-rental parks — a home in one is bought on a rented pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not as a strata lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **ALR land cannot be treated as future condo land:** strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6, and most subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval, including parcels under 2 acres — an ALR parcel's zone card does not make it stratifiable. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Care and institutional uses on CD zoning are not condos:** care uses appear in these bylaws as institutional / community-care type uses in specific zones and comprehensive-development (CD) zones, which is why care campuses so often sit on site-specific CD zoning — the operating licence layer is provincial (BC's Assisted Living Registry), not municipal zoning, and a CD card is not a purchasable building. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This provincial document layer sits on top of every zone in every city in the directory — zoning never substitutes for it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, in every municipality in this directory; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw, and municipalities additionally regulate short-term rentals through their own bylaws and licensing — two separate layers. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (expanded May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. There is no "55+ zone" in any bylaw on this page — age restriction is a strata bylaw layer, not a zoning layer, and no municipal bylaw can restore a lower age threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The bylaws behind the directory (name AND number, contested items flagged):** Surrey — **Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000**; Township of Langley — **Zoning Bylaw No. 2500**. The Abbotsford, Delta and Mission bylaw numbers beyond those two: UNCONFIRMED in the research file — take them from the page's own bylaw links, not from memory. Two naming splits the page itself flags, recorded so the rebuild keeps the flags: the Township's RU-6 zone is named "Rural Processing Zone" in s.104.1 while its Section 200 heading reads "Agricultural Processing Zone," and RU-11 carries the same kind of split ("Rural Estate Winery Zone" / "Rural Winery Zone") — bylaw inconsistencies, flagged, never resolved by guessing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Surrey frame (settled position):** Zoning By-law No. 12000's residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones on July 8, 2024 under provincial SSMUH legislation (RF-12/RF-12C → R4, shown on the page's R4 card as "Formerly RF-13, RF-12, RF-12C"; RF-SD → R6), and a secondary suite in Surrey is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area — a "90 m² cap" is NOT current and appears nowhere as a suite rule in the Surrey cards read; the 90 m² figure on this page is the ALR additional-residence limit, a different rule. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Three suite regimes on one page, never to be mixed:** Surrey's cap is 40% of the principal unit's habitable area; on ALR land a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and Abbotsford's A1 card states its own suite figure of up to 100 m² under that city's bylaw as the page summarizes it. Quoting one city's number for a property in another is the classic error this directory exists to prevent. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **SSMUH, three different municipal implementations of one provincial law:** Abbotsford carries dedicated SSMUH 1–4 zones; Surrey carries the full R1–R7 replacement set with former codes shown; the Township's SSMUH delivery sits inside its SR/R zone text (e.g., SR-1 and SR-3 cards describing small-scale multi-unit housing developments). For a condo buyer cross-shopping a house lot, this is the rule set that decides whether the lot can add units without rezoning. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **ALR housing frame (applies on top of zoning in every city here):** one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within that principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local governments can add further restrictions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Township frame (as the page presents it):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500's rural family (RU-1 minimum new lot 1.7 ha; RU-2/RU-3/RU-4/RU-5/RU-8/RU-11 at 8 ha) does the agricultural work and the R-CL compact-lot family carries the Willoughby infill densities, including the R-1B/R-2 minimum lot of 464.5 m² verified elsewhere in this program; SR-3B was added by Bylaw No. 6144 (July 27, 2026) per its card. **Abbotsford frame (as the page presents it):** the A1 zone carries agriculture with one single detached dwelling, a secondary suite (to 100 m² per the card) and one accessory farm dwelling unit on lots of at least 0.4 ha, with an 8.0 ha minimum new lot; the A2 minimum rises to 16 ha. Card-level detail beyond the research file's consistency checks is UNVERIFIED — the bylaw PDFs were not re-opened card-by-card. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is tied to this page — it covers five municipalities and has no median of its own, so any example figure would be an invention. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where any unit in any city in this directory is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home pad rents (where parks are discussed):** pad tenancies sit under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not in any zoning bylaw; the provincial pad-rent increase limit is 2.3% + proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is published here. RTB rent increases for ordinary tenancies are 2.3% (2026) / 2.2% (2027). (Standing provincial figures for this program.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a "55+ zone" I should search in?** No. No municipality in this directory zones for age. Age restriction is a strata bylaw under the Strata Property Act (only 55+ is valid), layered on top of whatever ordinary residential zone the building sits in — a "55+ search" is a bylaw-document search, not a zoning search. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: My Surrey listing says RF-12 — what zone is the lot actually in now?** R4 Small Lot Residential Zone: Surrey's R1–R7 zones replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones on July 8, 2024, with RF-12 and RF-12C becoming R4 and RF-SD becoming R6; the directory's Surrey cards show the former code on each card so old listing text can be translated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** It depends which regime governs the property, and the three on this page must not be mixed: in Surrey the cap is 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area (a "90 m² cap" is not current); on ALR land a suite is permitted only within the principal residence under the ALC frame; and Abbotsford's A1 card states a suite of up to 100 m² under that city's bylaw as the page summarizes it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What homes does the ALR allow on a farm parcel?** One principal residence of up to 500 m² total floor area (no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), a secondary suite only inside that principal residence, and one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha, without an ALC application — with municipal zoning still able to limit further. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage into condo units?** No — strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6), and most subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval, including parcels under 2 acres. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a condo long-term, whatever city it is in?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are a separate layer: a strata can still restrict them by bylaw and the municipality may licence them separately. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: We're considering a manufactured home — what does the zoning tell us?** Which park form is lawful where: parks sit in dedicated manufactured-home zones (Abbotsford RSMH/RSH and RH1, Township MH-1, Surrey RM-M in this directory). The zoning tells you the park can exist there; your actual rights as a pad tenant come from the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and the park's own rules — and a park's zone says nothing about whether it is age-restricted. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: Can a care home or seniors residence be built on a residential street?** Only if the zone (or a site-specific CD zone created by rezoning) permits the institutional/community-care use — which is why so many care campuses sit on CD zoning. Whether the finished residence is a registered assisted-living residence is a separate provincial question answered by the Assisted Living Registry, not by the zoning map. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html and https://connect.health.gov.bc.ca/assisted-living-residence
- **Q: Can I just rely on a zone card instead of checking with the city?** No. The page itself says summaries may not reflect recent amendments, it was last checked against the bylaws on October 2, 2026, and one mid-page segment (the Delta tail / Mission opening) could not even be re-read for the research file after the fetch service returned a 429 — that segment's detail is UNVERIFIED. Confirm the zone, permitted uses and ALR status for the specific parcel with the municipality and the ALC before buying. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Q: What should I check before buying acreage, in order?** The page's own six checks: ALR status (ALC map, then confirm with the municipality); the zone and its minimum lot size (never assume subdivision — on ALR land it needs ALC approval); how many homes are allowed (zoning and ALC rules both bite); septic records and inspection; water source (well yield/quality/licence vs. municipal); and farm class with BC Assessment, plus Premises ID if livestock or poultry are planned. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey's 2024 zone replacement is the single biggest "old listing text is wrong" trap in the market area.** R1–R7 replaced RF/RH/RQ on July 8, 2024 under provincial SSMUH legislation — any pre-2024 document, old MLS sheet or remembered zone code for a Surrey lot needs translating through the directory's former-code cards before it is relied on. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **The Township is still adding zones.** The directory's SR-3B card records that zone as added by Bylaw No. 6144 on July 27, 2026 — a live example of why the page carries a check date (October 2, 2026) and why a card is a summary, not the bylaw. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **The directory itself flags two bylaw naming inconsistencies** worth keeping in the rebuild: the Township's RU-6 zone is named "Rural Processing Zone" in s.104.1 while its Section 200 heading reads "Agricultural Processing Zone," and RU-11 carries the same kind of split ("Rural Estate Winery Zone" / "Rural Winery Zone"). If a search or a legal description uses the other name, it is the same zone. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **Farm class is the zoning-adjacent question acreage buyers forget.** BC Assessment's farm-class thresholds are $2,500 gross annual farm income for an operation of 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31 — the directory's buyer checklist points farm buyers at exactly this check. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification and https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **The page's address tool and its directory don't cover the same ground.** The free address check covers Surrey, Langley (City and Township) and Abbotsford only — a Delta or Mission address can be read in the directory but not run through the tool, so those parcels go straight to the municipality. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
- **One segment of the live page is unverified in this program.** A repeat fetch of the page returned a 429 from the fetch service, so the Delta section's tail and the Mission section's heading and opening cards were not re-read; the sections' existence is established (Delta A1–A3 and RS-series cards, Mission RR7/RR7s cards at the directory's end), but that segment's detail is UNVERIFIED and should be re-read before the rebuild republishes it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/zonings.md)
