## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In Yaletown, the Form B package is also where a building's tenure-adjacent facts surface — read it alongside the title, never instead of it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Most of Section 2's shelf is 1990s–2000s concrete — the age band where envelope, parkade and elevator cycles dominate those reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older "rentals not allowed" line for a Yaletown building is VOID for long-term rentals; the directory positions recorded in Section 2 (rentals allowed at Yaletown Park 3, City Crest and Pacific Landmark II) are consistent with that rule, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as age-restricted in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans, zoning and heritage:** Yaletown sits inside the City of Vancouver's Zoning and Development By-law framework, and its historic core is the Yaletown Historic Area (HA-3): the City has created regulations and companion design guidelines to preserve the historic fabric of its protected areas (Chinatown, Gastown, Shaughnessy and Yaletown), and the HA-3 schedule encourages conversion and renovation of Yaletown's heavy-timber, brick and concrete buildings and compatible new buildings for a mix of commercial, industrial and residential uses. A renovation plan for a warehouse-conversion loft (The Murchies shelf) is governed by that heritage frame, not just the strata bylaws. The city-wide bylaw number/detail was UNVERIFIED in the research pass at city level and is not invented here. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/yaletown-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Land tenure — the first check in Yaletown:** tenure varies by building, and the research pass opened no official source stating which Yaletown buildings are leasehold and which are freehold. In this file's fresh pass, four counted buildings state Freehold Strata in their sources (Azura 2, The Bentley, City Crest, Pacific Landmark II); the rest are UNCONFIRMED, and one feed listing describes a Howe Street building as prepaid Crown lease to 2084 (listing copy only). Price a prepaid-lease building against its lease term, not against freehold comparables, and read the title and strata plan for the exact unit before anything else. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/yaletown-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline — the City collects twice a year, the second business day of February and of July. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/yaletown-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Empty Homes Tax (Vancouver):** every Vancouver residential owner must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a property with no declaration is deemed vacant, and a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The rate stated by secondary sources for the current reference year is 3% of assessed value (secondary source — the research pass did not state the rate on the City pages it read, so the rate is carried as secondary-sourced, not City-verified). An investor-buyer needs the declaration calendar explained before closing, not after. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf and https://birdsnestproperties.ca/empty-homes-tax/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/yaletown-condos-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Section 2 is resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $800,000 (an arithmetic illustration only — no Yaletown median exists in the permitted source), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to resale stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no active Yaletown presale is counted in Section 2; where a developer-contract purchase does arise downtown, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Waterworks is one building inside a much bigger strata.** The 1008 Cambie Street tower holds 209 units, but its strata complex totals 535 units across Cambie Street and Expo Boulevard addresses — a Waterworks buyer's levies and votes are set at complex scale, not tower scale. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterworks-1008-cambie-street
- **Crestmark II cannot decide what year it was built.** A building source gives 1997 (22 storeys, 221 units) for 1228 Marinaside Crescent, one current listing agrees at 1997, and another current listing for the same address gives 1998 — the year is carried as 1997–1998 UNCONFIRMED in Section 2, and the depreciation report is the document that will settle it. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/crestmark-ii and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/1228-marinaside-crescent/302
- **Yaletown's character is a planning rule, not a vibe.** The City's own description — heavy timber, brick and concrete buildings, narrow streets, covered loading docks serving as public space — is written into the HA-3 heritage frame that governs conversions and new buildings, which is why the Homer Street loft shelf (The Murchies, 1912, converted 1997) looks and trades differently from the Marinaside towers. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **A missed form is the most expensive Yaletown mistake that has nothing to do with the condo.** Every Vancouver owner must file the Empty Homes Tax declaration by the 2nd business day in February under By-law No. 11674 — a property with no declaration is deemed vacant whether anyone lives in it or not, and the unpaid vacancy tax then bears a 5% penalty as of the 10th business day of April. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
