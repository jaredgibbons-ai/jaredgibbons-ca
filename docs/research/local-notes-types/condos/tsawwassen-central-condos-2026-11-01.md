# Tsawwassen Central, Delta — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Building-directory and listing sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen Central" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; the working centre is the junction of 56 Street and 12 Avenue, around the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site, inside the Tsawwassen Urban Centre planning area under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The town-centre site itself: Thrifty Foods sits on the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site, whose City-listed addresses are 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 St, beside South Delta Exchange, and Tsawwassen Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 1321A 56 St at the town centre. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Transit hub: South Delta Exchange, at 56 Street and 18 Avenue beside the recreation centre — route 601 runs South Delta / Boundary Bay to Bridgeport Station via 56 Street and the exchange, and route 602 runs Tsawwassen Heights to Bridgeport Station via the exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates — two clusters: (1) the 56 Street corridor at and just south of 12 Avenue (Windsor Woods, Oliva, Tsawwassen Greene, Cambridge Place), and (2) the 54A Street corridor south of 12 Avenue around Winskill Park and the KinVillage campus (The Lexington, Heron Place, and the KinVillage rental buildings). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Schools for the sample address 1299 56 St, from the SD37 School Locator: Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave., and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B is the buyer's core document:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance, and must have the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. The strata must provide it within 7 days; maximum charge $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Since 2023 it must also summarize strata insurance and electrical planning reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports — mandatory refresh:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot defer it by vote; the report projects 30 years of component repair/replacement costs with three CRF funding models. This is the document that prices the roof, envelope and elevator future of the 1987–1998 buildings in Section 2. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — existing ones (including any a directory still records for buildings such as Tsawwassen Greene) are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws — directly relevant to The Lexington:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 are the only lawful age-restriction route: a strata may restrict residency to 55+, and any lower age bylaw (19+, 40+, 45+) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and provincial exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents, and defined cases of children in the resident's care (Regulation 7.01; spouse/child exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta zoning:** central Tsawwassen lots sit under the RS and RD zones of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps; subdivision minimums in the central zones include RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — it has no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." The OCP carries Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) covering siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping — the design layer for any new multi-family project in the centre. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots — Delta's notice to the Province states four units on all residential properties in the City, and six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta, not this area). https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Flood:** Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; the town centre is inland of the Boundary Bay and Strait of Georgia shorelines, and no area-specific flood construction level for the central buildings was confirmed — UNCONFIRMED per building. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT exemptions:** first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial $835,000–$860,000, 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence. Newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, and a never-occupied unit in a new condo building can qualify. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on new construction only:** FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026) with the new housing rebate — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the new housing rebate alone is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST rebate question. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day statutory rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement; deposits held in trust. The live presale comparison in this area is the Town Centre redevelopment file in Section 5, if and when it reaches marketing — its marketing status is UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Assignments:** whether a presale contract here can be assigned is a contract and disclosure-statement question for the specific project — no project-specific assignment term was verified for any Tsawwassen Central project: UNCONFIRMED.
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan limit $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (verified against CRA pages in this program's earlier passes); CMHC homeowner premiums 0.60% (LTV to 65%) to 4.50% (90.01%–95% LTV, non-traditional down payment). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which buildings here are actually 55+?** Of the verified roster, only The Lexington (1150 54A St) carries a stated age restriction in listing sources — 55+, at least one occupant must be 55+. No age restriction was stated in sources read for Windsor Woods, Oliva, Heron Place, Tsawwassen Greene or Cambridge Place — for those buildings an age bylaw is UNCONFIRMED, and the registered bylaws on the Form B package decide it. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098719-1150-54a-street-110-delta-bc-v4m-4b5/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at The Lexington?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the 55+ requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases. Confirm the building's own registered bylaw matches the provincial frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** Long-term, yes, in any building here — BC strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still records "rentals not allowed" (Tsawwassen Greene). Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and at a 55+ building the tenant must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What pets can I bring?** Building by building. Stated in listings: Windsor Woods — small pets welcome, maximum 30 lbs; Oliva — 1 dog to 22 lbs or 2 cats; the building directory records Tsawwassen Greene as pets allowed with restrictions and Windsor Woods as pets allowed. Heron Place, The Lexington and Cambridge Place pet rules: UNCONFIRMED — read the registered bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1363-56-street/218 and https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3104395-1315-56-street-203-delta-bc-v4l-2a6/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tsawwassen-greene-1140-55-street
- **Q: What do strata fees cover, and where do I see the real number?** The binding figure is on the Form B: monthly fees for the specific lot, amounts owing, approved levies still payable and the CRF balance, with the current budget attached. Listing-advertised fees (for example, an Oliva listing showing a monthly maintenance figure for its unit) are unit-specific and date-specific — never compare buildings on listing fees alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: How do I judge levy risk in the 1987–1998 buildings?** Through the depreciation report: since July 1, 2024 every strata of five or more lots must refresh it every five years, with a 30-year cost projection and three CRF funding models — compare the CRF balance on the Form B against the report's near-term projects. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Do units come with parking and storage?** Listings for Windsor Woods, Oliva, The Lexington and Heron Place all record underground parking, and several record a storage locker for the specific unit — but stall and locker allocation is a Form B item (assigned, limited common property, or rented), and that is where it must be confirmed. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/1363-56-street/218 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2944703-1153-54a-street-300-delta-bc-v4m-4e4/brochure
- **Q: What is the building's insurance deductible — and who pays it?** Since 2023 the Form B must include a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage; the strata's water-damage deductible and any owner responsibility for it are read from that summary and the bylaws, not from the listing. Building-specific deductible figures for the Section 2 buildings: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I live here without a car?** This is Tsawwassen's transit centre: South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue runs the 601 to Bridgeport Station and the 602, and the town-centre buildings sit within the 56 Street / 12 Avenue shopping and library cluster. Route frequency and travel times: check the current TransLink timetables — not stated here. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- **Q: What is planned on the mall site across from Windsor Woods and Oliva?** A redevelopment process is open: applicant Century Group Lands Corporation, City File LU009754, covering 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street and an unaddressed parcel. What will be built, and when: UNCONFIRMED in the sources read — ask for the current file status before pricing a view or a construction period. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mall site is the area's live planning file.** The Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (Thrifty Foods) is in a redevelopment process under City File LU009754 (Century Group Lands Corporation) — the single biggest future change for the 56 Street condo cluster, and it sits directly in the outlook of the town-centre buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **The 54A Street corridor is becoming a seniors-housing campus, in phases.** The Tides (152 rental homes, KinVillage, 1070 54A St) opened with residents moving in in August 2025; Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street was before Council in a June 15, 2026 report (File LU009921) as a rezoning (Bylaw No. 8576) for a six-storey, 143-unit affordable seniors rental building with a ground-floor community centre replacing the aging KinVillage Community Centre at 5430 10 Avenue, sharing access and parking with The Tides. Rental, not strata — but it changes traffic, parking and the buyer mix on the same street as The Lexington and Heron Place. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-150-affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-delta and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7117/8545/4074/Rezoning_Application_for_a_Seniors_Affordable_Rental_Building_at_1050_54A_Street_KinVillage_Association.pdf
- **The Urban Centre has its own design permit layer.** The OCP's Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) speak to siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping — renovations and redevelopment in the centre are read against them, not just the zoning. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Oliva's solar array is part of its fee story, per its listings:** the building was built under LEED principles with rooftop solar panels, which listings credit with keeping maintenance fees low — a claim to test against the strata budget, not to take from the listing. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3104395-1315-56-street-203-delta-bc-v4l-2a6/brochure
- **Health care anchor:** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital, and Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **The ferry is a local fact, not a commute promise:** BC Ferries' Tsawwassen terminal is at the southwest end of Highway 17, with published crossing times of 1 hour 35 minutes to Victoria (Swartz Bay) and 2 hours to Nanaimo (Duke Point). Road commute times to Vancouver or Surrey Central: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA and https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
