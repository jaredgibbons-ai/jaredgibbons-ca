## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. With only two strata complexes in this pocket, the Form B for Berkley Terraces or for a Baden Park phase is effectively the whole diligence file. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This is why the "rentals not allowed" / "no rentals except to immediate family" lines at Berkley Terraces in Section 2 do not bar a long-term tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The directory's "age restrictions" line at Berkley Terraces does not by itself prove a valid 55+ bylaw — read the filed bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Blueridge is governed by the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and Zoning Bylaw 3210; the Seymour Local Plan maintains the predominantly single-family character of Blueridge, which is why apartment form exists only at the two Section 2 sites. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; that permission applies to the surrounding house lots, not to the strata complexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Slope hazard — the Berkley-side check:** District landslide work names the Berkley escarpment as an area with landslide risk; after the January 19, 2005 landslide on the Berkley Escarpment the District began proactive District-wide hazard management, and its Landslide Risk Assessment was updated in 2026. Berkley Terraces sits on Berkley Road — pull the District GeoWeb hazard layers for the complex's parcel before subject removal. District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B also sets Streamside, Wildfire, Creek and Slope Hazard Development Permit Areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — directly relevant at Baden Park, where 2026 completions may still be developer sales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to developer sales at Baden Park, not to resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any remaining developer-contract purchase at Baden Park. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in the exemplar files.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the published bands (the stated range is 0.6%–4.5%), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Blueridge at all?** Yes, but only two complexes: Berkley Terraces (999 Berkley Road, District of North Vancouver), a 31-unit low-rise, and the new garden flats at Baden Park on Lytton Street. The research file's Seymour Local Plan protection of single-family character is why there is nothing else — a buyer who wants Blueridge is usually choosing between those two buildings or a house. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/berkley-terraces-999-road (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)

Listings for the same Lytton Street community are split between the Blueridge NV and Windsor Park NV sub-areas; this file follows the research file's Blueridge geography. - **Q: Berkley Terraces is listed as built in 1989 and in 1992 — which is right?** UNCONFIRMED. The building directory states 1989; current unit listings state 1992. Verify the year against the strata plan and the Form B for the specific unit before relying on either for warranty, insurance or depreciation-report timing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/berkley-terraces-999-road and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3097487-999-berkley-road-311-north-vancouver-bc-v7h-1y3/brochure
- **Q: A listing says Berkley Terraces allows no rentals except to family. Can I rent my unit out long-term?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, whatever the directory or listing line says. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does Berkley Terraces' "age restrictions" line stop a younger buyer?** Only a 55-or-older bylaw is valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, and the directory does not state the age or the bylaw's terms — UNCONFIRMED until the filed bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/berkley-terraces-999-road
- **Q: Are the Baden Park flats a presale, and does GST apply?** The community's completions are in 2026 and listings state built 2026, freehold strata. A unit bought on a developer contract is a new-home sale: 5% GST applies, REDMA's 7-day rescission applies, and the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate and the Newly Built Home PTT Exemption may apply; a resale of a completed unit follows the Form B route instead. Establish which one the specific unit is first. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/baden-park-north-vancouver/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What hazard check matters on Berkley Road?** Landslide. The District names the Berkley escarpment for landslide risk, managed proactively since the January 19, 2005 slide and updated in the 2026 Landslide Risk Assessment — pull the GeoWeb hazard layers for any Berkley Road purchase, condo or house. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Blueridge condo address?** Blueridge Elementary (2650 Bronte Drive) and Seymour Heights Elementary (2640 Carnation Street) are both in the pocket and Windsor Secondary (931 Broadview Drive) is the Seymour secondary beside it, but SD44 catchments are address-based and the research file's catchment result for its sample address was UNVERIFIED — run the exact address with SD44. SD44 has no middle tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary). Note the standing catchment caution for the wider Seymour/Lynnmour side: Lynnmour-area secondary catchment is Windsor, not Sutherland, where that question arises. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Q: How is the commute from a Blueridge condo?** Route 214 runs through the pocket (Berkley Road, Carnation Street) to Phibbs Exchange, with some trips through to Burrard Station; the R2 RapidBus at Phibbs has run one-seat to Metrotown since September 7, 2026. There is no SkyTrain on this side of the District — the bus-to-Phibbs leg is part of every trip. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Q: What should I check on the older building (Berkley Terraces)?** The Form B package: the latest depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, no deferral vote), financials, CRF balance (minimum 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023) and any planned levies — plus the slope-hazard position above. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
