## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the core diligence for the Nature's Walk, Coho II and Shoreline townhome purchases that make up this sub-area's attached market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void — and Pitt Meadows separately prohibits short-term rentals city-wide.** Since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw — that voids, in-entry, the Coho II directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" in Section 2 as a restriction. The voiding does NOT create a short-term-rental right here: short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw — a City staff report to Council (October 23, 2024) states this expressly — and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022) — https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp. Housing sits under RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 urban residential zones, RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse, minimum lot area 0.4 ha) and RM-2 (townhouse and low-density apartment, minimum lot area 3,035 sq m); residential buildings are sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches in the RS zone. Under the provincial SSMUH rules as applied citywide (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024), lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Flood / dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — that current City figure governs; the guide page's "over 85%" figure is superseded and retired here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; this sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, though the urban South Bonson pocket itself is developed residential / village land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Property taxes and drainage levy:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The same bylaw charges a separate residential (Class 1) drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS; a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no current residential presale project in or beside this sub-area was verified in this pass. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in South Meadows?** None verified. - **Q: Can I Airbnb a townhome in Osprey Village?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw — prohibited city-wide by the City layer, separate from strata bylaws — and the provincial principal-residence requirement also applies in Pitt Meadows. A strata's silence on short-term rentals does not make the use legal. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Can I rent out a Nature's Walk or Coho II home long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, so directory lines like Coho II's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" do not create an enforceable long-term cap. Long-term renting is the City's permitted residential use; short-term is the prohibited one — keep the two layers separate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** South Meadows: 13 condos & townhomes listings, 3 solds (23% sales ratio). At the Pitt Meadows community level the condos & townhomes median was $662,500, $566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list and 40 days on market. SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Bonson address?** No public school building sits inside the sub-area. SD42 catchments are address-based with no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12); the locator result recorded for 10932 Barnston View Road is Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116B Avenue, the city's only public secondary school), and Edith McDermott Elementary on Bonson Road also serves addresses to the north — check the exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: Is South Meadows in a floodplain?** Effectively yes, city-wide: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes; this sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3, and Shoreline Park itself sits on the dike. Confirm floodplain, dike and drainage details for the specific lot with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: What extra tax line do Pitt Meadows owners pay?** A separate drainage levy inside the annual tax bylaw: for 2026, $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for residential (Class 1), on top of the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after August 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the townhome complexes?** The building directory records "Pets allowed w/rest." for Coho Chapter II. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC; for Nature's Walk and Shoreline no published building-level set was found — the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern for the specific complex. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/coho-chapter-ii-19572-fraser-way
