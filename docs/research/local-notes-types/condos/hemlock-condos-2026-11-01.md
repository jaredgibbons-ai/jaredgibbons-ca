# Hemlock — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**GEOGRAPHY FLAG (read first): "Hemlock" is a Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name that SnapStats reports under the community Mission — but Hemlock Valley is NOT inside the City of Mission. It is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) Electoral Area C land at Sasquatch Mountain Resort, governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan. Listings carry "Mission, BC V0M 1A1" and "Agassiz, BC V0M 1A1" mailing addresses; neither mailing town is the governing authority. Per this program's rule, no SnapStats figures are quoted for Hemlock in this file: FVRD electoral areas have no SnapStats coverage of their own, and the Mission community figures in the research file describe a city market roughly 55 km away — they are board reporting context, not Hemlock Valley evidence, and are not used as Hemlock numbers here.** (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The place: Hemlock Valley is the residential village at Sasquatch Mountain Resort (formerly the Hemlock ski area). The live page is titled "Hemlock Valley Homes for Sale | Sasquatch Mountain Resort" and its listings sit on streets including Sakwi Creek Road, Edelweiss Drive, Mt Downing Road, Enzian Way and Snowflake Crescent. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- Plan basis: the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019) governs the plan area, and the FVRD administers it. The plan area is based on the Controlled Recreation Area (CRA) established by the Province of British Columbia in 2016 as part of approval of the Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan; the CRA carries the licence of occupation that lets the resort developer use Crown land for the purposes in the Resort Master Plan. Neighbourhood planning areas inside the OCP area are Hemlock West (the historic resort community), Hemlock East and Hemlock North, each to be detailed through neighbourhood plans as the resort expands. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Neighbours and distances, per the OCP: the largest nearby town is Agassiz in the District of Kent, approximately 38 km from Hemlock Valley; Mission is approximately 55 km west; the Village of Harrison Hot Springs sits at the southern end of Harrison Lake, south of the plan area. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- Community scale: the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide describes Hemlock Valley, a designated place in FVRD Area C, as having approximately 200 dwellings — mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals — with Sasquatch Mountain Resort at the village's upper end. The OCP records an existing accommodation base of 226 resort residential units at Hemlock equating to 1,072 bed units, about 60% of them ski-in/ski-out or within comfortable walking distance of the base area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: at the base area. The one verified apartment-condo building stands on Enzian Way at the slopes (Section 2); the rest of the strata stock verified is townhouse-style and half-duplex product on Edelweiss Drive, Sakwi Creek Road and Snowflake Crescent, mixed in with detached cabins and chalets. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Schools — no school in the valley: the OCP states the small permanent population will limit the viability of locating schools and medical services within the Controlled Recreation Area, so a safe and efficient road network is a high priority so permanent residents can access outside services. The nearest schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file are, in SD78 Fraser-Cascade: Kent Elementary, 7285 Mccullough Rd, Agassiz; Agassiz Elem-Secondary, Agassiz; Harrison Hot Springs Elementary, Harrison Hot Springs; and in SD75 Mission: Deroche Elementary, 10340 N Deroche Rd, Deroche, and Dewdney Elementary, 37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd, Dewdney. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- Transit: no public transit service to Hemlock Valley was confirmed on an official source — UNVERIFIED. Access is by road from the Agassiz / Harrison Mills side; the FVRD evacuation guide's evacuation route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation, Harrison Mills and sites along that route. ### Tenure traps excluded

- **Non-strata row house / fourplex product is excluded:** a current Edelweiss Drive listing (20668 Edelweiss Drive) describes a "NO STRATA" freehold fourplex unit with sub-type Row House (Non-Strata) — freehold multi-unit product, not a strata condo, and excluded on that basis even though it trades in the same village and price conversation. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3075649-20668-edelweiss-drive-3-mission-bc-v0m-1a1/brochure
- **Resort bed-unit product in the OCP is a plan target, not strata stock:** the OCP's build-out distribution proposes 40% public (nightly-rental) bed units, 45% private bed units and 15% resident/employee deed-restricted housing at full build-out — a forward plan for the resort (toward 19,969 bed units and a Balanced Resort Capacity of 16,641 visitors per day), not a description of today's tenure split, and deed-restricted employee housing is non-market product excluded from condo stock. Today's freehold/strata split for the valley: UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Crown-land resort tenure:** the Controlled Recreation Area carries the licence of occupation that lets the resort developer use Crown land for the purposes in the Resort Master Plan — that licence is the resort operator's tenure, not a homebuyer's title; a specific lot's tenure (freehold vs leasehold) is confirmed on its title and Form B, and any leasehold unit surfacing in a valley listing is excluded from freehold strata stock. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- No corporation-share, undivided-interest, co-op or life-lease complex inside Hemlock Valley was confirmed on a source checked. The Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association (established 1980) is a non-profit that works on behalf of valley homeowners — it is a community association, not a strata corporation or a co-op tenure. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the Enzian Way building (directory year 1980) this is the central diligence document — it is also where the building's strata plan number and registered bylaws are confirmed, both UNVERIFIED from public sources in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. A 60-unit 1980s strata at a ski hill carries exactly the roof, envelope and amenity exposure (outdoor pool, hot tub, recreation center) these two rules price into fees — read the depreciation report before comparing Hemlock fees to a city building's. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The Enzian Way directory line reads "rentals allowed w/restrictions": to the extent any registered restriction in that building is a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, it is void; restrictions of other kinds (and any FVRD or licensing rules for nightly rental) are separate questions, confirmed on the Form B and with the FVRD. Hemlock's pattern is short-term/vacation use — the FVRD describes the valley's dwellings as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals — but no specific property's rental permission is confirmed by that description. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Hemlock Valley building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Local plan — FVRD, not a city:** the governing plan is the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan (November 2019) for Electoral Area C, built on the provincially approved Hemlock Valley All Seasons Resort Master Plan and its 2016 Controlled Recreation Area. Servicing is a resort-community system administered in the FVRD plan framework, not City of Mission services; the OCP's Infrastructure & Services chapter covers water supply, sanitary sewer, utilities, stormwater, snow clearing and storage, solid waste and emergency/protective services. Parcel-level servicing (which system a given lot is on, and its fees): UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Hazards and Development Permit Areas:** the OCP carries a Hazard & Risk Management chapter including a Wildfire Interface Hazard section, and Development Permit Areas in Schedule I that include protection of development from hazardous conditions. Parcel-level hazard/DPA status: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the FVRD before valuing a slope lot. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **RURAL property tax regime — explicitly different from Mission's:** Hemlock Valley is unincorporated, so the owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or any city. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, rural property taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on October 31. Do NOT apply the City of Mission's flat 10% penalty (Community Charter scheme) here — the two regimes are separate, and this file's properties sit in the rural one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Enzian Way building and the other verified strata stock are 1980s–1990s resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Hemlock Valley was verified in this pass; the OCP/Master Plan build-out is a long-range vision, not a marketed presale confirmed here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Resort/vacation property is a lender-by-lender file regardless of premium band — confirm insurability for the specific unit before subject removal. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Hemlock Valley part of Mission?** No. SnapStats groups the "Hemlock" sub-area under the community Mission and many listings carry a Mission mailing address, but Hemlock Valley is unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C land governed by the FVRD's Hemlock Valley Official Community Plan; it is not inside the City of Mission, and Agassiz mailing addresses do not make it District of Kent land either. Title, zoning, permits and taxes run through the FVRD and the Province. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and what is the penalty?** The Province — rural-area properties receive their tax notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office. Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). Mission's flat 10% city penalty does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: What is the condo building at Sasquatch Mountain?** One verified apartment building: Hemlock Valley, 21000 Enzian Way — the directory records 60 units, built 1980 (a unit listing says 1978; exact year UNCONFIRMED), with an outdoor pool, recreation center, sauna/steam room, shared laundry, storage and hot tub, and bylaws recorded as pets allowed with restrictions and rentals allowed with restrictions. Listings record its units as Apartment/Condo, slope-side at the base area. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/hemlock-valley-21000-enzian-way
- **Q: Can I rent my Hemlock unit out?** The valley's pattern is vacation and short-term use (the FVRD describes the dwellings as mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals), and long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022. But a specific unit's permission stack — strata bylaws on short-term rental, FVRD rules, and any licensing — is parcel-level and UNCONFIRMED until the Form B and the FVRD position are read. One Snowflake Crescent listing states there is no short-term rental ban for Hemlock Valley; that is listing copy, not a bylaw record. - **Q: Where do Hemlock Valley children go to school?** There is no school in the valley — the FVRD plan states the permanent population is too small to make a school inside the Controlled Recreation Area viable, so students travel to outside communities. The nearest schools confirmed in the BC Ministry K-12 file are in Agassiz (SD78) and at Deroche/Dewdney (SD75), but the catchment assignment for a specific Hemlock Valley address is UNVERIFIED. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **Q: How big is the valley, really?** Approximately 200 dwellings, mainly vacation homes and short-term rentals (FVRD Evacuation Guide), with an existing accommodation base of 226 resort residential units equating to 1,072 bed units per the OCP. The OCP/Master Plan vision of 19,969 bed units is a multi-phase plan longer than the life of the plan itself — do not price a cabin as if the expanded resort already exists. - **Q: What runs the mountain?** Sasquatch Mountain Resort is the current name of the Hemlock Valley ski area: the FVRD plan records 4 lifts, 146.8 hectares of developed ski terrain on 35 named trails and a 100% natural snowpack, on the south and southwest slopes. - **Q: What services does a full-time resident give up?** On the OCP's own analysis, schools and medical services are not viable inside the CRA, so everyday services are a trip to Agassiz (approximately 38 km) or beyond; servicing inside the valley is a resort-community system (water, sewer, snow clearing, solid waste) administered in the FVRD framework, and parcel-level servicing and fees are UNVERIFIED. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing address lies twice.** Valley listings carry both "Mission, BC V0M 1A1" and "Agassiz, BC V0M 1A1" addresses for the same FVRD Electoral Area C ground — a buyer's tax notice comes from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes, permits come from the FVRD, and neither Mission nor Kent city hall has the file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- **The tax penalty is the rural one, and it is staged.** 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11 — a buyer cross-shopping a Mission city condo (flat 10% on July 3) and a Hemlock unit is comparing two different penalty regimes, two different payees, and two different plans. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **The whole village exists because of a Crown licence.** The Controlled Recreation Area was established by the Province in 2016 as part of approval of the Resort Master Plan, and it carries the licence of occupation that lets the resort developer use Crown land for the Master Plan's purposes — the resort's existence is a provincial tenure instrument, which is why the FVRD plan, not a municipal one, governs the village. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **The expansion numbers are a vision, not a build-out schedule.** The OCP records a phased vision toward 19,969 bed units and a Balanced Resort Capacity of 16,641 visitors per day, against a built base of 226 resort residential units (1,072 bed units) and a current Balanced Resort Capacity of 1,640 guests per day. The gap between those two pairs of numbers is the single most mis-sold fact in the valley. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
- **The homeowners have been organised since before the current resort name.** The Hemlock Valley Homeowners Association was established in 1980 — the same year the building directory gives for the Enzian Way building — and works on behalf of valley homeowners as a non-profit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- **There is an evacuation guide for this village.** The FVRD publishes a Hemlock Valley Evacuation Guide whose route runs past Sts'ailes First Nation and Harrison Mills — a single-road mountain community plans its exit in writing, and a full-time buyer should read that document before the strata documents. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/hemlock.md)
- **The ski hill runs on natural snow only.** The OCP records a 100% natural snowpack across 146.8 hectares on 35 named trails — no snowmaking figure appears in the plan, so season length is a weather fact, not an operator promise. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11691
