Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Structural position — read first. The ALR stratification bar leads this file.** ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the Agricultural Land Reserve: a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1); the research file also cites ALCA s. 19 for the registration side, and Strata Property Act s. 239 is the strata-plan deposit provision the bulletin works through). ALR residences therefore cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — a permission rule, stated exactly, not an absolute "never." There is no condo building list for acreage ground, and there cannot be one from ALR land. This file therefore documents zero apartment condos and explains the rules that produce that zero. ## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero apartment-condo stock — stated plainly at the top of this section. There is no condo building list for acreage ground.** No condo building is named in this file, because the topic is acreage land and the research confirms the structural position: acreage housing in the ALR is freehold in practice and single-residence-led — one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), and no stratification without ALC permission — so the multi-unit strata profile of urban pages does not exist on ALR parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)

- The condo connection, explained precisely: **bare-land strata is a strata form** — a strata plan can create bare lots rather than building units, and bare-land strata product exists on serviced urban ground elsewhere in this program. What bars it on acreage ground is not the strata form itself but the ALR rule: depositing a strata plan (including a bare-land strata plan) is a subdivision, and ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6). Off the ALR, bare-land strata follows the ordinary Strata Property Act route; on ALR ground, it needs ALC permission first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The stratification bar (this topic's defining rule, stated exactly):** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"), issued October 18, 2022, gives the ALC's interpretation of subdivision in the ALR under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and the ALR regulations — a strata plan (including a bare-land strata plan deposited under the Strata Property Act) may not be approved or registered unless permitted under that regime: ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation. Say it correctly: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission** — do not write "can never be stratified," and do not cite Bulletin 05 for stratification (Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **ALR housing rules:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a **principal residence must be ≤500 m²** in total floor area, a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence, and **one additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha**, with local governments able to regulate further but not to be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **ALR short-term accommodation limits, relevant to acreage income plans:** renting out the entire principal residence for short-term stays is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment Class 9) — worked thresholds with their inputs stated:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of **$2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha** — and new applications for farm classification must be submitted **by October 31** for the following taxation year. Worked example — inputs: operation area 5 ha, actual value of the 1 ha in excess of 4 ha taken as $400,000 for illustration: threshold = $2,500 + (5% × $400,000) = $2,500 + $20,000 = **$22,500** minimum gross farm income. The $400,000 excess-area value is an illustration input only — use the parcel's own BC Assessment value. Classification continues only while the thresholds keep being met; BC Assessment will remove it if a property no longer meets the requirements or the owner fails to provide supporting information when asked. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Form B (for the urban condo stock this topic is not):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to a freehold acreage parcel, which is the point: there is no strata corporation on ALR acreage land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata stock generally):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither rule creates strata stock on acreage ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; on an acreage parcel there is no strata bylaw at all — tenancy questions are lease, bylaw and (on ALR land) ALC questions, not strata questions. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — again, strata law, with no freehold-acreage application. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $1,975,000 (a feed-card figure at reading, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $35,500 (2% × $1,775,000) = **$37,500 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. Farm and mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential portions — have a lawyer or notary model the exact bill. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on Fraser Valley acreage ground was confirmed on a developer's own site in the research; where any developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Fraser Valley acreage?** No — not as strata on ALR ground. Depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6). The condo-form purchase in the valley is an urban apartment from the city rosters, documented in the city condo notes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Is bare-land strata a way around that?** No. Bare-land strata is a genuine strata form — lots without buildings — but on ALR ground the strata plan itself is the subdivision the ALC regime controls, so a bare-land strata plan needs ALC permission exactly as a building strata plan does. Off the ALR it follows the ordinary strata route. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is there a published Fraser Valley acreage market statistic?** No. SnapStats publishes no acreage segment: no acreage count, share or median exists for the Fraser Valley or any community in it. Acreages sit inside their own communities' ordinary Detached rows, split only as Detached vs Condos & Townhomes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How many homes can I have on an ALR acreage?** Generally one principal residence (maximum 500 m² total floor area), plus, where the property qualifies, one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; more than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may restrict further. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income does an acreage need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; new applications are due by the legislated October 31 deadline. The worked example in Section 3 shows the over-4-ha formula with its inputs stated. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Which communities does this page's feed cover?** At reading, the visible cards were labelled with Langley sub-areas (County Line Glen Valley, Willoughby Heights, Campbell Valley, Salmon River, Otter District) and Abbotsford sub-areas (Abbotsford West, Poplar) — a regional feed, which is why no single community's statistics are presented as this page's figures. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out an acreage house short-term for income?** On ALR land, renting the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence can be short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism on an actively farmed, farm-classified property. Off the ALR, the municipality where the parcel sits sets the short-term rental rules. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What servicing diligence matters most on an acreage?** Well and septic records, water licences, floodplain and access are parcel facts the regional page cannot verify — one feed card already shows why, describing an ALR parcel bordered by a park "partially outside the ALR" with buyer-to-verify language on other uses (listing text). https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The bar on acreage condos is a subdivision rule, not a housing rule.** ALC Bulletin 09 is titled "Subdivision and Plans That Cause Subdivision in the ALR" (issued October 18, 2022), and s. 6 places strata plans in that category — which is why the ALR residence caps (500 m² principal; 90 m² / 186 m² additional) describe whole freehold houses, never strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The stratification rule is a permission rule — say it exactly.** ALR land cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission (ALCA s. 21(1); Bulletin 09 s. 6). Copy that says "can never be stratified" overstates the law, and Bulletin 05 is not a stratification source at all. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **SnapStats has no acreage segment and no land category.** This feed's listings are counted inside ordinary community Detached rows; there is no acreage count, share, median or price-per-acre figure in the permitted source, and none is stated in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **The feed crosses a board line inside one page.** Its visible cards mix Langley sub-areas with Abbotsford sub-areas (two different board communities), so even community-level medians cannot be presented as this page's figures — the research file states this openly rather than picking one community's numbers. https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Farm classification has a hard calendar edge.** The October 31 application deadline and the size-band income thresholds ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% / $10,000) decide whether an acreage keeps its Class 9 position into the next tax year — a closing that slips past the deadline without an application can cost the buyer a full year of classification. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
