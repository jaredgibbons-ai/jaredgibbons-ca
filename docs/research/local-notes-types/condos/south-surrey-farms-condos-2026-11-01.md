## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The condo rules below apply to urban South Surrey stock in its home files. On farm parcels themselves, the ALR block governs first — it is placed first here because it is the reason this file has no buildings.

- **ALR stratification bar:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). There is no Form B, no strata council and no depreciation report for a farm home — diligence runs through title, the ALC record, City zoning and BC Assessment farm class instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **ALR principal residence:** one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Note the program's settled Surrey position: Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal habitable area — a "90 m²" cap is NOT the current Surrey suite rule, and the 90 m² figure below belongs only to the ALR additional-residence rule. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **ALR additional residence:** at most one additional residence is permitted, of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha, and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **Quota and supply management:** UNVERIFIED in every particular in this file — whether any quota is included on a specific South Surrey farm, and any marketing-board transfer, approval or barn-standard rule. No marketing-board or CFIA source was opened in the research pass. Get any quota position in the contract, in writing, and verify it with the applicable marketing board directly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **Wells, septic and water licensing:** UNVERIFIED as stated rules in this file. The live page's account (GWELLS well records; domestic groundwater use not needing a licence while irrigation and other non-domestic use does under the Water Sustainability Act; Fraser Health Authorized Person rules for onsite systems) is site copy only — no Province or Fraser Health source was opened in the research pass to confirm it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-farms.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Applies to urban South Surrey resales in their home files; a farm parcel has no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children, with Reg 7.01 exemptions. No farm-parcel application: ALR dwellings are not strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is REPLACED and is cited only as predecessor, never as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its A-1/A-2 specifics for a farm lot are UNVERIFIED in this file (see Section 1). Surrey's SSMUH framework (July 8, 2024) is an urban-lot framework and does NOT create extra dwellings on ALR parcels, where the ALC caps above govern. White Rock is a SEPARATE municipality with its own bylaws (Zoning 2024 No. 2506 / OCP 2017 No. 2220) — never apply Surrey's bylaws to White Rock ground or vice versa; no White Rock farm ground is in this page's scope as read. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Surrey property taxes (2026 brochure):** the City states this year's property tax increase is 2.6%, with the City portion representing about $75 per year for the average single family home. Farm-class tax treatment is a BC Assessment position (see the thresholds above), not a City rate. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/2026-tax-matters-brochure.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 date in some secondary sources is superseded). - **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farms feed is broader than farms.** Cards read for the research file include a nursery/landscape operation, estate/residential acreages and rezoning / land-banking holdings in Elgin Chantrell, Morgan Creek and Grandview Surrey alongside working-farm parcels in Hazelmere and Pacific Douglas — the page title's "Farms" scope and its feed do not match, a mismatch flagged in the research file for Jared/Jerry. - **The suite rule flips at the ALR line.** On an urban Surrey lot a suite is capped at 40% of the principal habitable area under City rules; on ALR land a suite exists only inside the principal residence, and the detached second dwelling is a separate, size-capped "additional residence" (90 m² / 186 m²). The same street can carry both regimes parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **The 40-hectare line more than doubles the second-home cap.** The additional-residence limit is 90 m² at 40 ha or less and 186 m² only above 40 ha — most South Surrey acreages a buyer tours will sit on the 90 m² side of that line. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **Farm class is an annual income test with a hard date.** The thresholds scale with operation size ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000) and the application deadline is October 31 — a buyer who plans to keep farming must file the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase, or the classification position for the next tax year is at risk. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **The ALC ceiling is not a permit.** The ALC rules cap what may exist; the City of Surrey may further regulate or restrict ALR residences through zoning, and anything beyond the permitted number or size needs ALC approval. Both layers must clear before a residence plan is priced into an offer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-farms.md)
- **Price a South Surrey farm from its parts, not from a farm statistic — there is none.** No published South Surrey farm price or per-acre figure exists in this file, and the S Surrey White Rock residential figures are board context only. A valuation stands on the specific land, buildings, residence and any quota included — and the quota position is UNVERIFIED here until the contract and the marketing board say otherwise.
