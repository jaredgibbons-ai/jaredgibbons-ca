## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Granville is a recognised smaller area of the city but not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas; the corridor runs through the Fairview local area, with the Shaughnessy local area at its south end. The South Granville Business Improvement Association district runs along Granville Street from the Granville Bridge to 16th Avenue; the residential side streets run east toward Oak Street and west toward Burrard Street. Exact board boundary roads: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- Where condo stock actually concentrates: on the residential side streets west and east of Granville — West 6th, West 8th and West 14th Avenues in the blocks between Burrard/Fir and Oak — not on the Granville Street retail frontage itself. The same side streets also hold the corridor's large stock of purpose-built rental apartment buildings (see the exclusions in Section 2), which is the defining trap of this area: many "apartment buildings" here are not condos at all. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- Landmarks and retail: Gallery Row (the art-gallery stretch of the South Granville shopping district), the Stanley Theatre at 2750 Granville Street (650 seats, opened 1931 as a cinema and vaudeville house), Granville Park at 3001 Fir Street, and Vancouver General Hospital at 899 West 12th Avenue a few blocks east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- Transit: the corridor is bus-served today, including the 99 B-Line on West Broadway; the Broadway Subway — the 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six underground stations including South Granville Station — is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this corridor the Form B is also the fastest way to prove a building is a strata at all — the rental towers in Section 2 have no Form B to give. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Landmark Sunset's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law — and any directory line anywhere in this corridor saying rentals are not allowed would be void for long-term tenancies regardless. Note the reverse trap: a rental *building* (The Leeward, Santa Monica) is not made buyable by this rule — there is nothing to buy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. As the research file states it: side-street house lots sit under the citywide R1-1 multiplex regime created when By-law No. 13817 replaced the old RS districts — up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule; the Granville Street frontage itself sits under commercial / mixed-use districts, exact schedule per parcel UNVERIFIED; and a corridor strata example, The Avedon, is recorded in its listing as zoned RM-3. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md) and https://faithwilson.com/listings/103-1468-w-14th-avenue/
- **Area plan — the Broadway Plan:** the Plan covers Clark Drive to Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, was approved June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for a South Granville Station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station (40-storey maximum in the Granville/Burrard Slopes – Area A policy). Every Section 2 building sits inside the Plan area — but the Plan is policy, not zoning: most higher-density forms still require rezoning. Citywide direction otherwise comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** the City requires every owner to submit an annual property status declaration; properties declared or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026 — directly relevant to this corridor's second-home and investor stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City tax timing:** Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live here for FORM by Anthem (2026). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. Pintura, The Avedon and Landmark Sunset resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any remaining developer sales at FORM and for future Broadway Plan presales in the corridor. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Granville" here downtown Granville Street?** No. This file covers South Granville — the west-side corridor from the Granville Bridge to West 16th Avenue, inside Fairview. The downtown entertainment corridor is a different market and none of its buildings appear here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **Q: What condo stock does the corridor actually have?** Four verified strata buildings in this pass: FORM by Anthem (2026, West 6th), Pintura (2005, concrete, West 8th), The Avedon (2007, concrete, West 14th) and Landmark Sunset (1976/1978, wood-frame, West 14th) — plus a large surrounding stock of rental apartment buildings that are not for sale by unit at all. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Why do so many South Granville "apartment buildings" never have units for sale?** Because they are purpose-built rentals held as whole assets: The Leeward is a 48-suite rental tower (1968) and Santa Monica a 13-suite rental building (1929), and the West 11th–16th / Fir–Birch district is documented as an intact pre-WWII rental apartment neighbourhood. A building name on the Heritage Register (Douglas Lodge, Crescent Court) is not evidence of strata tenure. https://goodmanreport.com/sold-properties/1184-the-leeward-1686-w-12th-avenue-vancouver/ and http://heritagevancouver.org/top10-watch-list/2007/3-south-granville-apartment-district/
- **Q: Can I rent out a corridor condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022; Pintura is described in listings as rental-friendly and Landmark Sunset's directory entry says rentals allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to a unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Pintura — "rental and pet friendly" per listing copy; Landmark Sunset — pets allowed, "no more than two" per a current listing; FORM — cats OK in the listing pet field. The Avedon's pet rules: UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws remain enforceable, so read the registered bylaws. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2996783-1530-w-8th-avenue-805-vancouver-bc-v6j-5m6/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/landmark-sunset-1412-west-14-ave
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain reach South Granville, and does the Broadway Plan change my building?** South Granville Station is one of six underground stations on the Broadway Subway, scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. The Broadway Plan (1st–16th, Vine to Clark) contemplates 30–40 storeys near the station, which changes land-value assumptions block by block — but the Plan is policy, not zoning, and most tower forms still need rezoning. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **Q: Landmark Sunset — 1976 or 1978?** Sources conflict: a listing says 1976, a building directory says 1978. Section 2 flags both; the strata plan registration record is the definitive check. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3094656-1412-w-14th-avenue-103-vancouver-bc-v6h-1r3/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/landmark-sunset-1412-west-14-ave
- **Q: What should I check in the corridor's 1970s wood-frame stock?** The depreciation report and CRF: stratas with 5+ lots must have a report every five years (no deferral vote since July 1, 2024) and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Landmark Sunset listings note heat and hot water are included in strata fees — confirm what envelope and boiler items remain in the report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

The school the research file locates in Fairview is L'École Bilingue Elementary, 1166 West 14th Avenue — a K–7 single-track French Immersion school. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's densest-looking blocks are its least buyable.** The South Granville Apartment District — roughly West 11th to West 16th Avenues, Fir to Birch — is one of the city's only intact pre-WWII apartment neighbourhoods, held as rental assets; the strata stock sits on the quieter avenues north and south of it. http://heritagevancouver.org/top10-watch-list/2007/3-south-granville-apartment-district/
- **Terraces on 7th (1570 West 7th Avenue) is deliberately absent from this file's count.** It was counted under Fairview in the earlier program; a buyer cross-shopping both areas will meet it there, not here, so totals across the two files do not double count it. (Task brief for this file.)
- **The station is dated, not rumoured.** The Broadway Subway's South Granville Station is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027 on the Province's published date — corridor pricing already trades on that date, so a buyer should underwrite to it rather than to "someday." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **Pintura is two products under one name.** The West 8th Avenue development carries both apartment condos (1530 West 8th Avenue) and concrete townhouses (1576 West 8th Avenue) — a "Pintura" listing must be matched by address and type before it is compared. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105734-1576-w-8-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v6j4r8-2498000/brochure
- **The heritage apartment buildings carry two addresses each.** Douglas Lodge is both 1507–1509 West 12th Avenue and 2799 Granville Street; Crescent Court is both 1504–1512 West 14th Avenue and 3003–3007 Granville Street — a search on one address alone gives a half picture of the same building. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
- **The hospital is the corridor's quiet anchor tenant.** Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue — BC's referral centre for many provincial programs and a teaching hospital — sits a few blocks east of Granville Street, which is a structural source of both owner and renter demand on the side streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/granville.md)
