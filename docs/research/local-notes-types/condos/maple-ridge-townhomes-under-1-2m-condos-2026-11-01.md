## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

A townhouse strata lot is governed by the same Strata Property Act framework as an apartment condo lot — this section applies to every complex in Section 2.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Garage layout (tandem vs side-by-side) and backyard tenure (common vs limited common property) are unit-and-plan questions the Form B package answers, not page facts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/maple-ridge-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. For the 1990s complexes in Section 2 (Hollyhock 1996, Fraser Glen 1994, Dogwood Lane 1990), the report's roof, siding, window and deck timelines are the levy story. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Fraser Glen directory line in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this file's roster, Fraser Glen and Dogwood Lane are published as 55+ townhome communities — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. Do not confuse either with Haney Pioneer Village, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Zoning is Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019. The Official Community Plan citation is **CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024)** — flag the contest, never assert one number as settled. **Lougheed Transit Corridor: ADOPTED January 27, 2026** per the City record (February 4, 2026 news release; OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) — cited here as adopted; the earlier contrary research reading is noted in Section 5. **Albion Area Plan: adopted October 28, 2025** (Chapter 10.2 updates: enhanced walking and cycling connections, support for local commercial nodes, and buffers where urban development meets farmland). Suites/SSMUH: one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. (Albion condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/albion-condos-2026-11-01.md) and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/maple-ridge-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/maple-ridge-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction (any remaining developer sales at The Warwick or Rydge), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. **Band interaction:** this band caps at $1.2M, so a qualifying newly built townhome anywhere in the band sits under the $1.1M full-exemption ceiling only if its value is at or under $1.1M (partial to $1.15M; none above $1.15M), while a qualifying first-time buyer gets full-exemption treatment only at or under $835,000 — subject to each program's other conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract at The Warwick, Rydge or Hillside Homes. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
