## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic the operative rules are the ALR rules that keep condos off farm land, then the farm-class and tax frames, then the quota frame only as far as sources verify it — which is not at all — then the standard BC strata rules stated only for contrast.

- **ALR stratification bar — leads this section, because it is the governing fact for this file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; see also Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). Subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.md)
- **ALR residence frame:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. The guide's statement that ALR rules generally allow barns, a principal residence and, with restrictions, additional dwellings is consistent with this frame at that general level. Those are houses on one title — neither is a condo unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.md)
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program. The income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line for these thresholds is an ERROR — the three tiers above are the rule. The farm-class position of BC chicken farms as a group: UNCONFIRMED — classification is property-by-property and no source classifies them as a group. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.md)
- **Quota, supply management and the BCCMB transfer process — UNCONFIRMED in every particular:** that BC chicken production is supply managed in the terms the page states; the BCCMB's licensing, minimum-pricing and production-control mandate; how quota is measured; that quota never transfers automatically and that a share sale is still a quota transaction; the joint seller/buyer application and Board review of buyer, site and transaction; the Board cancelling the seller's quota and allotting quota to the buyer; the Board-approved-building condition; any quota price or valuation basis; and the guide's three-routes-in framing, including buying quota separately onto an approved site. No marketing-board source was opened in this program; the guide's quota account is site copy only — verify each point with the BC Chicken Marketing Board directly before subjects are removed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html
- **New entrant program status — UNCONFIRMED:** the guide states the program historically ran as grower draws, is not currently accepting applications or conducting draws in any region, and has a New Entrant Grower consultation underway — no BCCMB source was opened, so do not plan an entry around that status on the guide's say-so. - **Rentals and age (strata-wide, contrast only):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, and the only valid age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule touches a freehold chicken farm — there is no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (residence side only):** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — secondary sources saying March 12 are superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. - **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000.
