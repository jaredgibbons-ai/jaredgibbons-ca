## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Edgemont Villa the Form B package is doubly important: the exact wording of the 55+ bylaw is confirmed from the bylaws attached to it, not from any listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. All three counted buildings exceed five lots — including 20-unit Edgemont Villa (built 2003) and 22-unit Crescentview (built 2023, whose first report cycle still matters for reserve planning). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19 — the frame for Edgemont Villa's listing-stated 55+ position, the village's only one. Stratas may still ban or limit SHORT-TERM rentals by bylaw — Crescentview's published no-Airbnb policy is exactly that kind of bylaw item. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning (District of North Vancouver — not the City):** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900, under which District growth is directed to its town and village centres, with Edgemont designated as one of the six village centres while surrounding established streets remain low-density. The zoning bylaw is Zoning Bylaw 3210 (cited in District material as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024, for the suite and coach-house rules); the District's zoning rewrite was in progress in 2026 per the research file, so verify the current consolidated text and zone for a specific lot with the District. Suites: since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. SSMUH: the District's GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; the Province's Bill 25 (November 2025) requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026, with the District's compliance amendments still in preparation in spring 2026. Hazard frame: Schedule B of the OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Mackay Creek and Mosquito Creek run through the neighbourhood's ravines. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/edgemont.md)
- **District of North Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and single-family utility fees include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (combined single-family utilities $2,571 in 2026). Strata units carry their own utility arrangements — confirm on the Form B. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/edgemont.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all three counted buildings are completed resales today (Connaught 2019, Crescentview 2023), so GST is not in a resale purchase. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $1,500,000 (round illustration only — no area median exists to use), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $26,000 (2% × $1,300,000) = **$28,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no live Edgemont presale was verified in this pass — Connaught completed in 2019 and Crescentview in 2023, so diligence is strata documents and building condition, as with any resale. Where a unit is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/connaught and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condo buildings does Edgemont Village actually have?** Three verified in this file, all on Connaught Crescent: Connaught (59 apartments + 23 townhomes, completed 2019), Edgemont Villa (20 condos, built 2003, 55+) and Crescentview at Edgemont (22 condos, completed 2023). Everything else in Edgemont is detached houses, plus the Amica care residence, which is rental. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Is Connaught a presale I need to worry about completing?** No. Grosvenor states construction commenced in 2016 and completed in 2019 — the homes are resales, so diligence is strata documents and building condition, not assignment or completion risk. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/connaught
- **Q: What 55+ homes can I buy in Edgemont?** On current evidence, one building: Edgemont Villa, 3151 Connaught Crescent — a 20-unit (listing-stated), freehold strata built in 2003, described across multiple listings as a boutique 55+ residence in the heart of the village. Confirm the exact age bylaw in the strata documents before an offer — the listing wording is not the bylaw. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3102670-3151-connaught-crescent-206-north-vancouver-bc-v7r-4x6/brochure
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in Edgemont Villa?** Under provincial strata rules, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt. Live-in caregivers are also exempt, as are children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children the resident cared for before 19 — Regulation 7.01, expanded effective May 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out an Edgemont condo?** Long-term: yes in law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC; in a 55+ building the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. Short-term: stratas may still ban or limit it, and Crescentview's published policy states short-term rentals like Airbnb are not permitted — confirm the current bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/crescentview-north-vancouver/
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Edgemont Villa or Crescentview?** UNCONFIRMED for both — no public source checked states either building's pet bylaw (a current Connaught listing records cats OK for that project). Read the bylaws in the Form B package for the specific building; do not rely on a listing summary. (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/edgemont-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Is Amica Edgemont Village something I buy into?** No. It is a rental senior-living residence at 3225 Highland Boulevard offering independent living, assisted living, memory care and licensed residential care, registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry (operator Edgemont Senior Living Inc.). https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village
- **Q: Which schools serve a Connaught Crescent address?** UNVERIFIED per address — SD44 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a Connaught Crescent address is on record in this program. The locator results that are on record split the neighbourhood: 4501 Highland Boulevard → Canyon Heights Elementary / Handsworth Secondary; 3150 Colwood Drive → Highlands Elementary / Handsworth Secondary. Confirm the exact address with SD44. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/edgemont.md)
- **Q: Can I add a suite for family or a caregiver to an Edgemont house instead?** In many cases yes as a housing form: since June 2024 the District permits a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, subject to the lot's zone and any creek/slope development permit areas. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf
