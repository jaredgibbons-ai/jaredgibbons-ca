## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in BC. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/surrey-open-houses.md)
- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This is the document step after the open house — the open house shows the unit, the Form B shows the building. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. In Surrey's verified roster this lands hardest on the oldest buildings — Carriage House (1987), Semiahmoo Estates (1990/1991) and Odyssey Towers (1995). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The building directory still shows "rentals not allowed" for Semiahmoo Estates — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and an open-house host repeating it is repeating an unenforceable line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Three of this file's seven verified apartment buildings are presented as 55+ communities (Fleetwood Gardens, Carriage House, Semiahmoo Estates) — confirm on each Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 — a City corporate report records that on June 15, 2026, Surrey 2050 OCP received final adoption from Council (the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current in this file). Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (developer sales at Odyssey), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Surrey open house?** No official source requires it. BCFSA's Real Estate Rules excuse disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: Is the host at an open house the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side of the transaction; BCFSA requires a licensee to work for either the buyer or the seller and to inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients — assume anything non-factual you tell the host can reach the seller. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How do I know the open house time is current?** Each listing on the surrey-open-houses page carries an OPEN HOUSE label with a date and start and end times, and the site's open-house FAQ states the page updates automatically throughout the day as new MLS open houses go live — check the page the day you plan to go. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/surrey-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Which Surrey areas can I cover in one trip?** Group by the City's own communities — City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley. The verified condo buildings in this file cluster so a run works: Fleetwood Gardens (Fleetwood) pairs with Carriage House (Cloverdale) on the east side; Semiahmoo Estates, Odyssey and South Point Walk pair on 152 Street in South Surrey; Odyssey Towers stands alone in Whalley. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q: So many Surrey open houses are in 55+ buildings — can I buy if I am under 55?** Three of the seven verified apartment buildings here are presented as 55+ (Fleetwood Gardens, Carriage House, Semiahmoo Estates). A 55+ bylaw is the one age bylaw BC law still allows (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — whether you can occupy, or only own and rent, depends on the registered bylaw on the Form B. Ask before the open house, not after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed in these buildings?** It varies and pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws). Published examples: Carriage House — listings state no pets permitted; Semiahmoo Estates — directory records pets not allowed; Fleetwood Gardens — current listings record cats OK for the units checked. For Odyssey, South Point Walk, Suncliff Place and Odyssey Towers, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws in the strata package. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/17695-58-avenue/102
- **Q: The directory says a building bans rentals. If I buy as an investor, can I rent it out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. What can still bind you: a short-term rental bylaw, and a 55+ age bylaw limiting who may live in the unit (which decides your tenant pool in Fleetwood Gardens, Carriage House and Semiahmoo Estates). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I ask for after I like a unit at an open house?** The strata package behind the Form B: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024), financial statements, CRF balance and planned work — the items BCREA advises reviewing to identify future special levies. In the 1987–1995 buildings (Carriage House, Semiahmoo Estates, Odyssey Towers) that package matters more than the staging. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Is "Odyssey" in Whalley the same as "Odyssey" in South Surrey?** No. Odyssey Towers (13880 101 Avenue, Whalley, built 1995, concrete frame) and Odyssey by Forge Properties (2620 152 Street / 2635 King George Boulevard, South Surrey, built 2025, wood-frame) are different buildings that share a name — check the address on the open-house card before driving. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3101359-13880-101-avenue-1504-surrey-bc-v3t-5t1/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The disclosure rule has an open-house carve-out with a tripwire.** Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation only while the host is hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers to general questions — the moment the host solicits or receives your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, the exception ends. That is the legal line between small talk and sharing with the seller's side. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Surrey has a new Official Community Plan.** The Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) received final adoption on June 15, 2026 per a City corporate report — older material citing the PlanSurrey 2013 OCP (Bylaw No. 18020) as current is out of date, which matters when an open-house feature sheet quotes plan designations for a condo's neighbourhood. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Half the verified Surrey condo roster is 55+.** Fleetwood Gardens (2005), Carriage House (1987) and Semiahmoo Estates (1990/1991) are all presented as 55+ communities — Surrey's established low-rise condo stock skews seniors-oriented, which shapes both the open-house traffic and the resale buyer pool for those buildings. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Two "Odyssey" buildings, one weekend.** A Surrey open-house run can list Odyssey Towers in Whalley (1995 concrete) and Odyssey in South Surrey (2025 wood-frame) on the same day — same name on the card, different city district, different construction, different tax treatment (resale vs developer sale). Check the address, not the name. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **"Lakewood" listings in a Surrey search are usually Abbotsford stock.** The verified Lakewood Gardens (2491 Gladwin Road) is an Abbotsford West building; no Surrey Lakewood apartment building was verified in this pass. A buyer who drives to a "Lakewood" open house from a Surrey search may be looking at the wrong city's building. Status of any Surrey Lakewood: UNCONFIRMED.
- **The feed is the schedule.** There is no separate official open-house calendar: the page's listings each carry the OPEN HOUSE label with the date and start/end times, and the publisher's FAQ states the page updates automatically throughout the day as new MLS open houses go live — how those dates and times are entered on the brokerage side (board systems, ShowingTime, TouchBase) is not documented on an official public source and is UNVERIFIED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html and (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/surrey-open-houses.md)
