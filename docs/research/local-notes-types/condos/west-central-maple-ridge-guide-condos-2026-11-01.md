## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In West Central this is the document that resolves the open questions in Section 2 (Era's unit counts, Emerson's construction, Inspire's per-building completion, Canuck Plaza's restrictions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in West Central may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No West Central building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B — and any lesser age label (the "25+" label flagged at Canuck Plaza) is VOID as an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; the standard house zone is RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%). **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted:** the program's research files cite the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan two ways (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024), with Council adopting updates to the OCP and the Town Centre Area Plan on November 25, 2025; quote planning policy for a specific building from a current City document, not from either contested number. The Town Centre Area Plan (first adopted November 2008) covers the downtown core at the sub-area's east edge, and Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor:** the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record (Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021), as the guide page states — cited here as adopted on that basis. An earlier contrary reading in this program (that City sources showed only initial bylaw readings at the end of 2025, and the plan was not adopted on that date) is recorded in Section 5 so the conflict stays visible; confirm a corridor parcel's current designation in the City's current document before valuing on it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Flood and escarpment rules on the south and east edges:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic, no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the Fraser River Escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and lots within 300 m of the escarpment crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED at sub-area level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (relevant to Inspire's developer sales and any never-occupied Era units — confirm occupancy history), not ordinary resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of the 3% + 2% structure, not a separate rate — no such row was spotted on the guide page as read.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for developer-contract purchases at Inspire (Building 3, estimated completion January 2027) and any future Era phases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC premium range was stated on the guide page as read in the research file; any page stating 2.80%–4.00% + 0.20% is WRONG against CMHC's published range. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
