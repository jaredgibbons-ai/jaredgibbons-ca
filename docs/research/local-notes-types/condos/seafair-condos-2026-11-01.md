## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At Edgewater Park (built 1975) the depreciation report is where envelope and rainscreen history and remaining obligations surface — reconcile it against any listing claim about completed siding/window work. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — both Seafair complexes are well above the threshold on any of their published unit counts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals allowed with restrictions" lines published for Edgewater Park and Seafair West therefore cannot amount to a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither Seafair complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** houses sit in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m²; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use (Section 5.4: 33.0 m² minimum, maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended, with City Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. The zoning district of a specific strata complex parcel: UNVERIFIED in this pass — confirm in Bylaw 8500 for the parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Official Community Plan:** the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, covers Seafair (planning area 6), with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans addressing neighbourhood issues; a Seafair-specific area plan name or approved major project: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Flood protection and Flood Bylaw 8204:** Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations including the west dike beside Seafair (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide); the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — directly relevant to the Williams Road / dyke-edge complexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **District energy (ADEU):** the Alexandra District Energy Utility serves the West Cambie / Alexandra neighbourhood (Bylaw No. 8641 is framed for buildings within the Alexandra neighbourhood, and the utility operator describes the ADEU system as serving the West Cambie neighbourhood). Neither the Seafair research file nor the ADEU sources place Seafair in the ADEU service area — no ADEU connection or ADEU rate is stated for any Seafair complex: UNCONFIRMED / not applicable on the sources checked. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=78100 and https://luluislandenergy.ca/adeu/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Seafair complexes are resale stock (1975 and 2003). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A current market presale in Seafair verified on a developer source: UNVERIFIED — this rule is stated for completeness only. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "apartments" on Francis Road are a 1969 rental building.** Wall Seafair at 3851 Francis Road (78 units, 3 storeys, built 1969 per the rental directory) is operated as renovated rental apartments by Wall Financial Corporation — buyers searching "Seafair apartments" will land on rental marketing, not strata stock. https://www.apartments.com/profile/l55webd/ and https://www.wallfinancialcorporation.com/properties/seafair
- **Edgewater Park has already done one big envelope cycle — on paper.** A current listing records a full siding replacement (rainscreen), new windows and patio doors for the complex at a substantial per-unit cost already paid. For a 1975 frame-wood complex that history is central to value — and it must be verified in the depreciation report and strata minutes, not taken from listing copy. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3108737-1-3031-williams-road-richmond-british-columbia-v7e1h9-1079000/brochure
- **Williams Road is both the amenity and the boundary.** Edgewater Park's dyke access (West Dyke Trail to Garry Point and Steveston Village) comes from sitting on Seafair's south boundary street — the same position that puts it closest to the dike system and Bylaw 8204's flood framework. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **Seafair's recreation anchor is a school-park campus.** Hugh Boyd Community Park at 9200 No. 1 Road combines the secondary school, West Richmond Community Centre, pitch and putt, artificial turf fields, tennis courts and a playground on one site — the amenity both townhouse complexes trade on is a City park/centre, not a strata facility. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seafair.md)
- **There is no Seafair library branch.** Richmond Public Library's nearest branches named in the research file are Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub), 4320 Moncton Street, and Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate — a listing that implies a walk-to library in Seafair itself is overstating the geography.
