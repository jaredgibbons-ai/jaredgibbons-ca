## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Arista is a strata townhome complex, so the same Form B package governs a purchase here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. The live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, bylaws, depreciation report and minutes, and to confirm what the monthly fees include. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://jaredgibbons.ca/arista-townhomes.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/arista-townhomes.md)
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Arista's published rental position is UNCONFIRMED in any case; read the registered bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Arista is not recorded as 55+ in the research file — any age claim for this complex is UNCONFIRMED until the Form B and registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans — Surrey rules:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, which received final adoption on June 15, 2026 per a City corporate report. The former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Secondary suites and townhouse suites — Surrey:** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite is permitted per dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite. Surrey Council has approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones for new developments in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — those permissions are for new developments in specified zones and areas, not a blanket right in a 2011-era complex like Arista. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/arista-townhomes.md)
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Arista is completed resale stock per the page (2011), so a resale carries no GST. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is sold on this page — Arista is completed resale stock. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise elsewhere in South Surrey, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
