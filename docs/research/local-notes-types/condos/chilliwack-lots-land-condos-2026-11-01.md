No building from that roster is re-counted here, and no building in that roster is a lot or land.
- **The strata angle that does apply: bare-land strata (a strata of lots).** The Strata Property Act defines a "bare land strata plan" as a strata plan on which the boundaries of the strata lots are defined on a horizontal plane by reference to survey markers and not by reference to the floors, walls or ceilings of a building. In a bare-land strata, the strata lot is essentially a plot of land: the plan is filed before any homes are constructed, the lot owner builds (or contracts to build) the home on the lot, and the common property can include roads, streetlights, a septic system, a clubhouse, playground and forested areas. You cannot tell by looking whether detached homes sit on a bare-land strata — the words "bare land strata" appear on the first page of the strata plan. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol27/consol27/98043_01 and https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/
- **What that means for a Chilliwack lot buyer:** if a lot is a bare-land strata lot, there IS a strata corporation — with bylaws, strata fees for the common property, and the Section 3 strata rules — even though there is no building. If the lot is freehold, none of that applies. The title and, where one exists, the strata plan decide which purchase you are in. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

### The land-side rules

- **ALR administration:** properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **ALR residences cannot be stratified:** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision") — creating a bare-land strata on ALR land is itself a subdivision and needs the same ALC permission; ALR residences cannot be stratified (Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A principal residence on ALR land is capped at 500 m² total floor area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm Home Plate:** the OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Development Permit Areas:** the OCP's Schedule D lists D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial. Which DPAs apply to a given lot is a per-parcel check. Wording warning: the live page describes "Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland)" — the OCP list names D-2 as "Hillside Environment" and carries a separate D-5 "Hillside Development," so the live page's DPA2 name is UNVERIFIED against the current OCP list. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md)
- **SSMUH — Bylaw 5395:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, which amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description recorded in the program's rules-gaps file. Eligibility is parcel-specific. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6)

### Strata Property Act — the bare-land strata block

- **Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59):** where a lot is a bare-land strata lot, a resale purchase still runs through the strata: the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fee, any amount owed, any special levy already approved, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a freehold lot there is no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote — this applies to a bare-land strata corporation as it does to a building strata, and its report covers the common property (roads, systems, amenities), not the houses on the lots. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata, including a bare-land strata, may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful, with exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Both rules apply to a bare-land strata exactly as to a condo building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Insurance difference a bare-land buyer must know:** the strata corporation insures common property, common assets, and buildings shown on the strata plan — in a bare-land strata the house sits entirely inside the strata lot and is not shown as a building on the plan, so the lot owner insures the house; the strata does not. https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/

### Taxes and rebates — purchase

- **GST on land and new homes: UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel.** GST treatment of a vacant-lot purchase depends on the seller and the lot's history — no single rate is stated here because no source in the research file states one for land; confirm with the buyer's legal professional. For a newly built home on the lot: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — a vacant lot purchase's eligibility is a PTT-return question: UNCONFIRMED here for bare land. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000, for a qualifying newly built home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the regime applies to developer marketing of strata lots, including bare-land strata lots sold by an owner developer; no Chilliwack bare-land presale project is named in this file: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year — both are for a qualifying home purchase, and whether a bare lot alone qualifies is a program-conditions question: do not assume it. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — for insured home purchases; vacant-land financing is a lender-specific product and the CMHC home-purchase schedule is not stated here as a land-loan rule. Financing terms for land on the live page: UNVERIFIED — no lender source was opened. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md)

- **Q: Are there any condo buildings in this file?** None — count 0 by design. This is a lots-and-land topic; the only strata form a lot can take is a bare-land strata lot (Section 2). - **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in plain terms?** A plot of land that is a strata lot: the Strata Property Act defines a bare land strata plan by lot boundaries drawn on a horizontal plane by survey markers, not by a building's floors, walls or ceilings. You own the lot, you build the house, and a strata corporation owns the common property (roads, systems, amenities) and charges strata fees. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol27/consol27/98043_01
- **Q: How do I tell if a Chilliwack lot is bare-land strata or freehold?** Not by looking — detached homes on a bare-land strata look like any detached homes. The words "bare land strata" appear on the first page of the strata plan at the Land Title Office; if the homes are drawn on the plan, it is a building strata instead. https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/
- **Q: Who insures the house on a bare-land strata lot?** You do. The strata corporation insures common property, common assets and buildings shown on the strata plan — a house entirely inside a bare-land strata lot is not shown on the plan, so it is the lot owner's policy, not the strata's. https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/
- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is agricultural land?** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its Official Community Plan carries an Agricultural Area Plan for that land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: How do I check if a Chilliwack lot is in the ALR, and can I stratify it?** The City directs owners to the City Webmap to check ALR status. Stratifying ALR land is a subdivision: ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision, and subdivision of ALR land requires Commission permission — an approving officer (including under the Strata Property Act) cannot approve it otherwise, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Can a serviced Chilliwack lot carry more than one home?** The City's SSMUH amendment, Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff-report description — eligibility is parcel-specific and is confirmed with the City. Separately, the Zoning Bylaw permits one ADU per lot (suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40%; garden suite capped at 100 m²). (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, items 6 and 7f)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR — even with no building.** ALC Information Bulletin 09 (October 18, 2022) devotes a section to "Strata Plans Cause Subdivision": filing a bare-land strata plan on reserve land triggers the same ALC permission requirement as a conventional subdivision. Most lot buyers never hear this until the application stage. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The live page's hillside permit-area name does not match the OCP.** The page says "Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland)"; the City's OCP page lists D-2 Hillside Environment and a separate D-5 Hillside Development. Confirm the applicable areas and their requirements with the City for a hillside lot rather than copying the page. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for Chilliwack land — anyone quoting one is quoting nothing.** Chilliwack is CADREB territory, SnapStats does not cover the city and publishes no vacant-land category in any case, and CADREB publishes board-wide only with no land category. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md)
- **Agriculture is the land base, not the backdrop.** At 67% of the city's total land base on the City's own statement, reserve status — not zoning alone — controls whether an ALC application is needed for a house, fill, a non-farm use or a subdivision on most large Chilliwack parcels. Check the parcel on the City Webmap first. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **A unit number on a land listing is a clue, not a building.** A card shown in the research pass is a unit-numbered land listing on Chilliwack Mountain — the bare-land strata form surfacing in the feed. The research publishes no named complex for it, so the strata plan on title, not the feed label, is what settles the purchase type. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-lots-land.md)
- **Chilliwack's tax penalty clock has two dates, not one.** The City adds 5% after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — a staged scheme a land buyer carrying a parcel over the summer should budget for from the tax notice, not from a neighbouring city's rule. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
