## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — every directory "rentals not allowed" line in Section 2 (Rosewood Court's included) is VOID. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw — any other age threshold (Canuck Plaza's directory "25+" label included) is not enforceable — with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs. The Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024 in current sources) — flagged, never resolved by guessing. The City records that OCP and area-plan updates for the Town Centre, Hammond and Silver Valley were adopted in November 2025 and the Albion Area Plan update was adopted October 28, 2025. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/maple-ridge-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED January 27, 2026** per the City record (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; City news release February 4, 2026) — cited here as adopted; an earlier program reading that treated the corridor plan's status as unsettled is superseded by the City record (see Section 5). (West Central condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-central-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026 (the first business day in July), with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding after the due date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding around September 1–2, 2026. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/property-taxes/understanding-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/maple-ridge-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Pad rents (MHPTA) — for the excluded parks in Section 2:** manufactured-home pad rent increases are capped at 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027, under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act regime. (Standing program rule; park-specific rents: UNCONFIRMED.)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $525,000 (the Maple Ridge community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,500 (2% × $325,000) = **$8,500 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (Podium, Brickwater Village and Inspire had developer sales), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
