## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The attached stock here is townhouse strata; the same Strata Property Act rules apply to a townhouse strata lot as to an apartment condo lot.

- **Form B:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59); the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" for a complex here is old bylaw text, not current law; Brighton's building record in any case records rentals as allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Silver Valley complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; its RS-1 subdivision minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research files (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — do not treat either number as settled. The Silver Valley Area Plan update was adopted November 25, 2025. The Albion Area Plan (adopted October 28, 2025) and the Lougheed Transit Corridor Plan (adopted January 27, 2026 — see Section 5) cover other parts of Maple Ridge, not Silver Valley. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/silver-valley.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/silver-valley.md)
- **ALR bar on stratification:** ALR status is per-parcel in this part of Maple Ridge. Where a parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, its residences cannot be stratified — ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6, Strata Property Act s. 239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) — which is one reason the sub-area's large-lot edge produces freehold houses, not strata product. Check the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping for any specific lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/silver-valley.md)
- **Floodplain:** the City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; which Silver Valley lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/silver-valley.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales — the verified townhome complexes are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract (Hillside Homes was a presale collection; whether any unit remains on one is UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
