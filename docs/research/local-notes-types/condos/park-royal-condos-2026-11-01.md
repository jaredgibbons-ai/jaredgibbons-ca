## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Park Royal is the MLS sub-area around the Park Royal shopping centre at Marine Drive and Taylor Way — West Vancouver's eastern gateway, at the foot of the Lions Gate Bridge and the mouth of the Capilano River. The centre opened in 1950, is presented by the centre as Canada's first covered shopping mall, operates as North and South malls divided by Marine Drive, and is classified in the District's OCP as West Vancouver's regional shopping centre. http://parkroyal.ca/ and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/park-royal.md)
- Nearby areas: Ambleside (west), Dundarave (further west along Marine Drive), and — across the Capilano River in the District of North Vancouver — Norgate, Pemberton Heights and Lower Capilano. (Research file, Section 1)
- Where condo stock concentrates: in and around the centre — the Evelyn residences on Arthur Erickson Place above the Village at Park Royal, the WestRoyal towers on Taylor Way, and the low-rise stock on the streets south of Marine Drive toward Ambleside, including Keith Road. Detached houses sit on the quiet streets south of Marine Drive. (Research file, Section 5; 55+ file, Section 1)
- Market figures: **none citable.** The SnapStats GVR September 2026 report has no West Vancouver community or sub-area rows; inventory, solds and medians for Park Royal are UNVERIFIED in this file, and sub-area medians are Paragon-only. (Research file, Section 2)
- Transit: Park Royal Exchange is the western terminus of the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station (roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak); the exchange is also the hub for West Vancouver's Blue Bus routes, including the 250/250A downtown–Horseshoe Bay/Dundarave service via the Lions Gate Bridge. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/210/tt250.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- Schools: the nearest SD45 elementary schools are Irwin Park Elementary (K–7, 2455 Haywood Avenue) toward Ambleside/Dundarave and Westcot Elementary (K–7, 760 Westcot Road) at the foot of the British Properties; the east-side catchment secondary school is West Vancouver Secondary (Grades 8–12, 1750 Mathers Avenue). Confirm the catchment by exact address with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045 (Research file, Section 4)
- Recreation: Ambleside Park and the east end of the Centennial Seawalk are immediately east; Capilano River Regional Park runs up the canyon across the river, with the Cleveland Dam (completed 1954) and the Capilano River Hatchery (completed 1971; reported closed to the public for construction starting October 1, 2026, until further notice). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park and https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html (Research file, Section 4)
- Health: the North Shore's hospital is Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver — across the Capilano River. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital (55+ file, Section 2)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At the WestRoyal (1993, concrete), the depreciation report and CRF balance are the first-order documents; at any Park Royal address, the Form B sits **behind** the title tenure check in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed with restrictions" element of the WestRoyal's directory line is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed; short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither counted building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** District of West Vancouver **Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 (2010)** and **OCP Bylaw No. 4985 (2018)** govern the municipal side; the OCP was amended again December 1, 2025 (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning it with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report) — cite the current consolidated OCP, not the 2018 original. The Park Royal Shopping Centre lands are a named Form & Character Development Permit area, and foreshore/environmental DP rules apply along the waterfront. On Capilano IR No. 5, the Squamish Nation controls zoning under the Park Royal Lease arrangements. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits (55+ file, Section 5; Research file, Section 4)
- **Density is in active transition here:** on July 21, 2025 the Province issued ministerial directives requiring OCP amendments increasing density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area by December 31, 2025; the District missed that deadline (and delivered 58 of its 220-unit year-one housing target), a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 implemented the neighbouring Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, and the Park Royal–Taylor Way directive remains outstanding with quarterly provincial progress reports required through 2026 and 2027. Treat any density-based value claim for a specific parcel as UNCONFIRMED until an adopted bylaw names it. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377 (Research file, Section 4)
- **Suites and coach houses:** both are permitted in the District and require building permits, Zoning Bylaw s. 130 and BC Building Code compliance, and a business licence to operate as rentals. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots (55+ file, Section 3)
- **District taxes and the additional school tax:** municipal-side 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. The Province's additional school tax applies at West Vancouver values: 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Homes on reserve land are under a different tax/tenure regime — UNCONFIRMED per title. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate (Research file, Section 4)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both counted buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $1,000,000 (round illustration only — **no West Vancouver median is published in SnapStats**, so no market figure is used), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $16,000 (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Note: PTT treatment of a reserve-land leasehold differs from a freehold purchase — confirm with the buyer's lawyer/notary where tenure is leasehold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (e.g., developer-page product in Section 2), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a home near Park Royal freehold or leasehold?** It depends on the parcel, and the two can be streets apart. Municipal-side homes are freehold under District bylaws; lands on Capilano IR No. 5 are leasehold under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, with the Squamish Nation controlling zoning. Confirm tenure on the individual title before pricing or financing — lenders treat reserve-land leaseholds individually. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: Which condo buildings are actually at Park Royal?** Two verified developments: The WestRoyal (328/338 Taylor Way, built 1993, two towers) and Evelyn by Onni on Arthur Erickson Place (768 recorded built 2019) above the Village at Park Royal. Everything else named in Section 2 is detached houses inside the Evelyn masterplan, a developer-page proposal, a rental proposal, or a care residence. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Can I rent out a WestRoyal unit?** Yes, long-term — the "rentals allowed with restrictions" element of its directory line is void province-wide since November 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-westroyal-338-taylor-way and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is the WestRoyal a 55+ building? A listing called it "perfect for downsizing."** No. Building sources record pets/rentals with restrictions and no age restriction; "downsizer" copy describes the likely buyer, not a bylaw. The legal test is a 55+ bylaw in the strata's filed bylaws — confirm on the Form B. Park Royal has zero verified 55+ ownership stock. https://www.zolo.ca/west-vancouver-real-estate/338-taylor-way/15b (55+ file, Section 4)
- **Q: What is Amica Lions Gate — can I buy there?** No. It is a rental retirement residence at 701–727 Keith Road (Independent Living, registered Assisted Living, Long-term Care, Memory Care). Nothing to buy. https://www.amica.ca/lions-gate
- **Q: How is transit if I give up driving?** This is West Vancouver's transit hub: Park Royal Exchange is the R2 RapidBus terminus (one seat to Lonsdale Quay, Phibbs Exchange and Metrotown since September 2026) and the main Blue Bus exchange, including downtown express buses over the Lions Gate Bridge. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **Q: What are homes worth at Park Royal?** UNVERIFIED from any citable source in this program — SnapStats publishes no West Vancouver figures at all, and sub-area medians are Paragon-only. Do not quote a "Park Royal median" from any other source without naming it. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **Q: Will the provincial density directive change my condo's value?** It is unresolved: the Park Royal–Taylor Way directive remains outstanding with quarterly progress reports through 2026–2027, while a draft Park Royal North vision contemplates up to 27 towers. Until an adopted bylaw names the parcel, density value is UNCONFIRMED. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Q: Which schools serve a Park Royal address?** Irwin Park and Westcot elementary schools (K–7) are the nearest SD45 schools and West Vancouver Secondary is the east-side secondary — confirm the catchment (Irwin Park vs Westcot) for the exact address with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $1,000,000 fair market value (round illustration only; no West Vancouver median is published), fully residential freehold, no exemption — the bill is $18,000. Leasehold tenure changes the analysis; confirm with a lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Canada's first covered shopping mall is also a First Nations leasehold story.** Park Royal opened in 1950; part of its lands are Capilano IR No. 5 under a lease dated April 1, 1974, and on the reserve portion the Squamish Nation — not the District — is the zoning authority, with the District providing building design review and inspection so construction meets municipal standards. http://parkroyal.ca/ and https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **The province has already overridden West Vancouver once, next door.** When the District missed its December 31, 2025 directive deadlines, a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 amended the District's bylaw to implement the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan itself. The Park Royal–Taylor Way directive is the same instrument, still outstanding — buyers here are pricing inside an unresolved provincial process. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377 (55+ file, Section 5)
- **"Evelyn" is a masterplan, not a building.** The counted condo entry is the 768 Arthur Erickson Place residences (built 2019); the same Arthur Erickson Place street also carries Evelyn detached houses built 2024/2025. Quoting "Evelyn" unit counts or ages without the civic address mixes two different products. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/evelyn-768-arthur-erickson-place and https://www.zolo.ca/west-vancouver-real-estate/592-arthur-erickson-place
- **The additional school tax is a real carrying cost at these values.** On top of District taxes: 0.2% on the assessed portion between $3M and $4M and 0.4% above $4M in 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — a line item downsizers moving from a long-held house often meet for the first time. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **The salmon hatchery across the river is closed for construction.** The Capilano River Hatchery (completed 1971, normally ~250,000 visitors a year) was reported closed to the public starting October 1, 2026, until further notice — relevant to anyone buying on the river trails' amenity value this season. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park (Research file, Section 4)
- **West Vancouver's OCP moved again in December 2025.** Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025 on December 1, 2025 to align the OCP with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report — always cite the current consolidated Bylaw 4985, not the 2018 original, and never the White Rock 2506/2220 pair. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update (55+ file, Section 5)
