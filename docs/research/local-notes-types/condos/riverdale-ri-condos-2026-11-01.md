# Riverdale — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography note:** "Riverdale" is not a City of Richmond OCP area or sub-area plan name — no City planning-area or neighbourhood material read uses the name for this pocket, and the City plans and serves this ground as part of **Thompson**. "Riverdale RI" is the board-style sub-area name used for this page. The ground itself is real and precisely located: the Riverdale Drive pocket in north-west Richmond, west of No. 2 Road and south of Westminster Highway, running south toward Granville Avenue — the City's park page for McCallan Neighbourhood Park locates that park "West of No. 2 Road and South of Westminster Hwy., off Riverdale Drive and Larkspur Avenue" in the Thompson recreation area. No official boundary for a Riverdale neighbourhood is published. One name trap, stated plainly: there is **no** SD38 school named Riverdale Elementary in Richmond — Riverdale Elementary is in Surrey (SD36); no Surrey school content belongs in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Riverdale this applies to the two No. 2 Road townhouse communities in Section 2 — a nine-unit strata and a small townhouse strata carry the same document discipline as a tower, with far fewer owners sharing each major cost. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. The 6675 No. 2 Road community (nine units per listings) is over the threshold; Oval Gardens' unit count is UNCONFIRMED, so its position is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Riverdale townhouse strata reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither Section 2 townhouse community is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000, is the city-wide plan covering the pocket; no plan or approved major project specific to a Riverdale pocket was confirmed. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m², RS1/K 315.0 m² and RS1/G 2,000 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Suites and added units:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. For most Riverdale buyers this — not condo stock — is the attached-housing conversation in this pocket. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks; the Middle Arm dyke trails east and west of the pocket run on that dike system. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Aircraft noise:** the pocket sits on the Sea Island / YVR side of Richmond; aircraft noise policy for Richmond is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area. A street-by-street noise contour finding for Riverdale: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. - **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Section 2 townhouse communities are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the resale stock in Section 2. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in the Riverdale pocket was confirmed on a developer source in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board name and the City name describe the same ground differently.** Listings sell "Riverdale, Richmond's most prestigious neighbourhood" (a Riverdale Drive listing's words), while every City document the research file read plans and serves the same streets as Thompson. A buyer searching City material for "Riverdale" finds nothing — the verifiable anchors are Riverdale Drive, McCallan Neighbourhood Park and Thompson Community Centre. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md) and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3078367-6400-riverdale-drive-richmond-riverdale-ri-homes-for-sale-3850000/brochure
- **The pocket's recreation spine is a trail junction, not a centre.** McCallan Neighbourhood Park (3.514 acres) sits beside the McCallan Road Trail, which connects the Railway Avenue cycling corridor to the Terra Nova / West Dyke Trails westward and the Middle Arm Dyke Trails eastward — the dyke-trail network is this neighbourhood's recreation spine, and Thompson Community Centre (5151 Granville Avenue) sits south-east of the pocket, not in it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Lot-size minimums here span a 5-to-1 range inside one bylaw section.** The RS1/RS2 subzone minimums the research file lists for this pocket run from RS1/B at 360.0 m² to RS1/G at 2,000 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones — two houses on nearby streets can sit under entirely different rebuild math, which is why Section 3 insists on the specific lot's subzone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **The only strata buying here is small-strata buying.** Nine units (6675 No. 2 Road) means every roof, fence and repaving decision is split nine ways and one difficult owner is a ninth of the corporation — the Form B, CRF balance and five-year depreciation report matter more, not less, because the community is tiny. Sources: Section 2 entries; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **The school with this area's name is in another city.** Riverdale Elementary belongs to Surrey Schools (SD36); nothing about it — catchment, programs, ratings — transfers to Richmond's Riverdale pocket, and listing copy that borrows it is wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
- **Richmond has no SnapStats coverage in this program.** There is no community median to quote for Riverdale — any Richmond median in marketing copy comes from a source outside this file's verification, and this file substitutes none. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/riverdale-ri.md)
