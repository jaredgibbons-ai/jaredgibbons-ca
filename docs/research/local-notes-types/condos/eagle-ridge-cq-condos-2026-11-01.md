## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At Eagle Ridge Court (built 1981, a strata conversion of former rental housing) the depreciation report and the conversion-era building systems are the central diligence items. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Eagle Ridge Court's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" must be read with that law: any long-term rental restriction in the registered bylaws is unenforceable, while any short-term restriction can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 complex is published as 55+ — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Council report list: Bylaw 5511, 2026 and Bylaw 5512, 2026), superseding the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. Small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² (R-1/R-3) and 465 m² (R-2), and the City's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
- **Watercourse protection:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s.523 covers riparian areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Lansdowne Drive stock in Section 2 is 1980s resale stock, so GST does not apply to it. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Eagle Ridge CQ is confirmed — this applies only to presales a buyer compares in neighbouring sub-areas. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Eagle Ridge the same as Upper Eagle Ridge?** No. SnapStats carries them as separate sub-areas with separate rows, and listing sources file the higher Lansdowne stock (1215 Lansdowne Drive) under Upper Eagle Ridge and Greyhawke Estates (1495 Lansdowne Drive) under Westwood Plateau. Mixing their listings or solds misstates both markets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md) and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1215-lansdowne-drive/411
- **Q: What strata stock does Eagle Ridge CQ actually have?** Two Lansdowne Drive complexes verified in this pass: Eagle Ridge Court (1170 Lansdowne Drive, built 1981, 36 units, form UNCONFIRMED) and the 16-unit townhouse complex at 1135 Lansdowne Drive (built 1989, name UNCONFIRMED). The sub-area's 13 attached listings in September 2026 sold none. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Eagle Ridge Court's directory says rentals are restricted — can I rent my unit out?** A long-term rental-restriction bylaw has been unenforceable in BC since November 24, 2022, whatever the directory or old bylaw text says. A short-term rental restriction can still bind — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Eagle Ridge Court?** The directory publishes "Pets allowed w/rest." — pets are allowed with restrictions. The specific limits (number, size, type) are in the registered bylaws; pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eagle-ridge-court-1170-lansdowne-drive
- **Q: Why does a 1981 complex need conversion-era diligence?** Because City archives record Eagle Ridge Court as a strata title conversion of existing rental housing (Subdivision Committee, July 3, 1980 — the developer undertook that existing tenants would not be ejected to ease unit sales). Conversion buildings carry the original rental-era systems into strata ownership; the depreciation report, CRF balance and any envelope/roof history in the Form B package are the purchase decision. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Lansdowne Drive address?** School District 43 catchments are address-based. The verified locator result in the research file is for 1195 Lansdowne Drive (Gleneagle Secondary): Eagle Ridge Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026.** The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026, streamlining the old Citywide OCP (Bylaw No. 3479, 2001), with a fuller OCP update anticipated to begin in 2027. Planning documents and listing copy citing the old Citywide OCP designations are already a plan out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **Eagle Ridge Court started as rentals.** The City's July 3, 1980 Subdivision Committee minutes record the strata title conversion of 1170 Lansdowne Street, including a recommendation to waive the strata title conversion guideline vacancy factor of 3.5% and an undertaking protecting existing tenants — the complex's strata history begins as a conversion, which is why its documents read differently from a purpose-built strata of the same age. http://searcharchives.coquitlam.ca/uploads/r/city-of-coquitlam-archives/9/2/8/928c605cc00a7d36fe6b1e528d6f6c09799dadfb8f8f89fdddbeee2c24bb9842/Subdivision_Committee_-_1980.pdf
- **One street, three sub-areas.** Lansdowne Drive addresses are filed by listing sources under Eagle Ridge CQ (1135 and 1170), Upper Eagle Ridge (1215) and Westwood Plateau (Greyhawke Estates, 1495) — a "Lansdowne Drive comp set" that ignores the sub-area lines mixes three different board markets at three elevations. https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1215-lansdowne-drive/411 and https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/1495-lansdowne-drive/140
- **Listings and sales point opposite ways here.** In September 2026 strata listings outnumbered detached listings 13 to 8, yet detached sold at 25% and strata sold nothing — the sub-area's standing problem is strata liquidity, not strata supply. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
- **The plaza at the corner is the area's commercial anchor, not housing.** EagleRidge Plaza (1192–1198 Lansdowne Drive, at Guildford Way) is described in its commercial listing as below Westwood Plateau and a short walk from Gleneagle Secondary, Scott Creek Middle and Eagle Ridge Elementary — the same school chain the research file's locator run returned for 1195 Lansdowne Drive. https://www.loopnet.co.uk/listing/1192-1198-lansdowne-dr-coquitlam-bc/13576968/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
- **The plateau amenities are not in this sub-area.** Westwood Plateau's golf-course and summit amenities sit in the separate Westwood Plateau / Upper Eagle Ridge sub-areas above Eagle Ridge — the research file's standing caution is that no plateau amenity should be marketed as inside Eagle Ridge without an address check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/eagle-ridge-cq.md)
