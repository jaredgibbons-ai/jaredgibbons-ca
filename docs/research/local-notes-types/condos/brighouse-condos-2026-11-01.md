## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Brighouse is Richmond's downtown core, around No. 3 Road, Minoru Boulevard and Westminster Highway; the board (GVR) uses "Brighouse" as the listing sub-area name, and the City plans the same core as Brighouse Village, one of the villages in the City Centre Area Plan, described as the civic and mixed-use focus of the City Centre. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- Where condo stock concentrates: the No. 3 Road / Cook Road station core and the Minoru Boulevard civic strip. The Brighouse Village Specific Land Use Map (2031), amended by Bylaw 10190, sets the village's transects — Urban Core T6 (45 m), Urban Centre T5 (25 m) and General Urban T4 (15 m) — with the village centre at No. 3 Road and Cook Road and the Canada Line station and bus exchange in the village; the map notes maximum building height in parts of the village may be subject to established Airport Zoning Regulations. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- Transit: Richmond–Brighouse Station is the Canada Line's terminus station, with a bus exchange, at the middle of the village on No. 3 Road. TransLink's published timetable (effective September 7, 2026) gives scheduled station-to-station times of 26 minutes from Richmond–Brighouse to Waterfront Station and 15 minutes to YVR–Airport Station, the latter requiring a transfer at Bridgeport Station. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=51899 and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt991.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. The 1970s stock in this file (Regency Park Towers, Ascott Wynde) is exactly where the depreciation report matters most. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Ascott Wynde's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No source states a 55+ bylaw for any counted Brighouse building — Brighouse has ZERO verified 55+ strata (ownership) complexes per the 55+ program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brighouse-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 9000, whose City Centre Area Plan designates Brighouse Village for high-density, high-rise, mixed-use development (transects to T6 / 45 m, Section 1). Single Detached zones RS1/RS2 in Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 apply outside the City Centre plan designations, with secondary suites under Section 5.4. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Flood rules:** new construction in Richmond is subject to flood construction levels under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, within a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event (dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m). For ground-oriented units, confirm the flood construction level position and what it means for insurance and future alterations. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Richmond property taxes (city-wide rule):** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (a round illustration figure only — Richmond has no published SnapStats median to use, and this is NOT a market figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (the CF Richmond Centre redevelopment lands are the live local example of future product), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Richmond's first high-rises started as rentals.** The City's Heritage Inventory records that Park Towers' first two towers were completed in 1972 opening as apartments, while the third tower finished in 1974 as a stratified condo — the complex's strata history is not uniform across the three towers, which is a Form B and strata-plan question for any unit there. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/inventory/Details.aspx?ID=226
- **Airport rules cap some Brighouse heights.** The City's own Brighouse Village map notes maximum building height in parts of the village may be subject to established Airport Zoning Regulations — part of why heights step down across the core despite the T6 (45 m) transect at the centre. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **The seniors cluster and the condo core were one combined development.** Kiwanis Towers exists because the Kiwanis Society replaced an aging seniors complex on its site with two concrete towers, financed in part by selling a portion of the site to Polygon, which also built three market condominium towers on its parcel — the subsidized seniors towers and the market towers beside them have entirely different tenure. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=42205 (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brighouse-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **The research file's "low-rise building" is a townhouse complex.** Ascott Wynde was carried in the area research as a low-rise Brighouse building from a rental-listing source; the building directory describes it directly as a 102-unit townhouse complex (1977, NWS837) — a buyer searching it as an apartment building will misprice the form. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/ascott-wynde-8751-citation-drive
- **The Paramount's own sources disagree with each other.** Presale sources give 546 total residential units in one place and 536 market units in another, and current listings record the towers as built in both 2022 and 2023 — settle unit count and completion from the disclosure statement and strata plan, not from any listing. https://www.urbanyvr.com/gbl-architects-richmond-no-3-road/ and https://staging.mikestewart.ca/presale/paramount-presale-richmond-brighouse-condos-office-retail/
- **Flood construction levels are a Richmond-wide fact, not a building defect.** New construction is subject to flood construction levels under Bylaw 8204 inside a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event — ground-floor and ground-oriented buyers should confirm the level position for insurance and alterations rather than treat it as a surprise. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **The 55+ programming hub is in the park, not in a condo.** The Seniors Centre at the Minoru Centre for Active Living, 7191 Granville Ave, in Minoru Park, is a 33,000 sq ft seniors centre for adults 55 and older inside a 110,000 sq ft facility — Brighouse's seniors infrastructure is civic, while its seniors housing is rental and care. https://www.seniorlivingmag.com/richmond-seniors-centre/ (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brighouse-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
