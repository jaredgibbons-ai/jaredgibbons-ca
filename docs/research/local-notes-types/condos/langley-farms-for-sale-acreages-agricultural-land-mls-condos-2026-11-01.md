## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR stratification bar — LEADS this section:** on ALR land a strata plan cannot simply be deposited — ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6 treats strata plans as causing subdivision in the ALR; Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) are the statutory frame. The research file carries Bulletin 09 s.6 as the settled reference; its detailed conditions were not re-read in that pass, so no condition beyond that reference is stated — detail UNVERIFIED beyond the reference. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
- **ALC residence caps:** a principal residence permitted without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only when located entirely within the principal residence (a detached dwelling is not a secondary suite) and local bylaws permit suites; one additional detached residence may be permitted at 90 m² or less on properties 40 ha or less, or 186 m² or less on properties over 40 ha, and Township bylaws must also permit it. Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies, and local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
- **BC Assessment farm thresholds:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if over 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — gross income at farm gate prices, with farm classification applications due by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
- **Zoning frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township; Township SSMUH was implemented by Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier); Bylaw No. 2500 s.102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage. A general RU-zone minimum parcel size from direct bylaw text, and any farm home plate rule, are UNVERIFIED in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
- **Form B (where any strata lot is involved):** SPA s.59 — Form B Information Certificate (fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Depreciation reports / CRF:** stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental / age bylaws (BC standard):** rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 for strata property; only 55+ age bylaws remain legal under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with SPA Regulation 7.01 exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion (younger spouses/partners, defined children, legacy residents, live-in caregivers). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. No GST-on-farmland rule beyond this standard block is stated — the research file opens no farmland-GST source, so any farm-specific GST treatment is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given — no farm median exists in the permitted source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (residential frame; farm financing is lender-specific and UNCONFIRMED here). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html

- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Langley?** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Most of the Township is reserve land:** 235 km² of the Township is in the ALR, so most Langley farm parcels carry ALC housing and use rules layered over Township zoning — the ALR check comes before pricing, not after. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
- **Farm classification is an income test, not a zoning label:** a parcel keeps farm classification only while the operation keeps meeting the size-scaled gross income thresholds in Section 3, with an October 31 application deadline — a buyer whose plan cannot meet them risks the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-farms-for-sale-acreages-agricultural-land-mls.md)
