## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In the core's mixed-use towers (3 Civic Plaza shares a complex with a hotel and a university campus; King George Hub towers share podiums with office and retail), read the depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a core building (Camberley Green's building page is one example in wider Whalley) is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted: in Surrey, only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host one, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required first, under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and the Business License Bylaw. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted core tower is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. The downtown's own layer is the City Centre Plan (2017, update underway adding a Central Business District and an Entertainment District). Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Density charges changed mid-2026: Community Amenity Contributions were superseded by the City's Amenity Cost Charge and bonus density provisions as of June 15, 2026, with the CAC page kept as a historic reference for eligible in-stream applications — exact per-file amounts are application-specific and UNVERIFIED as general figures. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/community-amenity-contributions (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/surrey-city-centre.md)
- **Secondary suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit — the "90 m² cap" sometimes quoted is NOT current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **District energy:** One Central's project record states the City of Surrey required use of its Neighbourhood Energy Utility, with a central plant in the building's basement serving multiple buildings in the area — a utility-cost line to read in the strata budget of any West Village tower, confirmed per building on the Form B package. https://ndy.com/experience/one-central-west-village-surrey-bc/
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — a never-occupied unit in a 2025-completed tower (Plaza One, Plaza Two, The Grand on King George, Park George) can still be a developer sale, so establish per unit whether the sale is a developer sale or a resale. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption to check on a never-occupied developer sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for PARKWAY and any other developer sale in the core. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Surrey City Centre is Surrey. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/surrey-city-centre.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Your search has to be filed under "Whalley."** Board reporting and MLS sub-area data for downtown Surrey sit under Whalley — a literal "City Centre" sub-area search returns nothing, because no such board sub-area exists, even though the City officially calls the downtown "City Centre (Whalley)." https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/whalley
- **One project site holds six residential towers with two different tenures.** King George Hub's four condo towers (Hub One, Hub Two, Plaza One, Plaza Two — 1,624 condos at full build-out per PCI) stand beside two purpose-built rental towers (The Line, Tether — 771 rental homes per PCI). A listing that says only "King George Hub" has not told you whether the unit can be bought at all. https://pci-group.com/projects/king-george-hub/
- **The inventory depth cuts both ways.** Whalley's 635 attached listings in September 2026 were the deepest of any Surrey sub-area — downtown sellers compete with new-build launches, and downtown buyers have more comparable sales to price against than almost anywhere else in the city. - **Two of the core's headline towers carry unit counts that do not agree with themselves.** 3 Civic Plaza is 349 units in the research file's developer source and 348 residential suites in a construction-industry source; One Central is 550 homes (including townhouses) in its engineering firm's record and 439 condos & 11 townhomes in its presale record. Price per unit and strata-vote math off either figure needs the strata plan, not the brochure. https://vrca.ca/news/3-civic-plaza-becomes-tallest-building-in-surrey/ and https://ndy.com/experience/one-central-west-village-surrey-bc/
- **The CAC era ended mid-2026.** Density charges on new City Centre projects switched from Community Amenity Contributions to the Amenity Cost Charge regime on June 15, 2026 — the same date the Surrey 2050 OCP (Bylaw No. 21900) was adopted — relevant to presale pricing comparisons between in-stream older projects and new launches, and a date older Surrey guides get wrong. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/community-amenity-contributions (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/surrey-city-centre.md)
- **The plan is changing under the towers.** The City Centre Plan update adds a Central Business District and an Entertainment District (Council endorsed the Entertainment District in October 2025), and large multi-phase applications such as Onni's Regency Gardens redevelopment (10520 132 St / 105–105A Ave area, Application No. 7924-0084-00) are in process — construction disruption and amenity upside both follow the plan, street by street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/planning-reports/planning-report-7924-0084-00
- **Stale restriction lines are everywhere in Whalley building data.** Because rental-restriction bylaws died province-wide on November 24, 2022 without any bylaw amendment being required, older building pages still showing "rentals not allowed" (Camberley Green's page among them) are describing a void bylaw — the Form B, not the aggregator page, is the document to trust. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
