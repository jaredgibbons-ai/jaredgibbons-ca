## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Westhill Place is an early-1970s wood-frame complex — the depreciation report and CRF balance on the Form B are the first-order documents for any purchase there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Westhill Place's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No College Park building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Moody's current Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (adopted April 28, 2026; it repeals OCP Bylaw No. 2955 — never cite 2955 as current). The Zoning Bylaw is Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937: RS zones for single-detached housing; RT and RM zones for multi-residential forms (the Seaview Drive townhouses and Westhill Place sit in the multi-residential framework). Under Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460 (June 2024), small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside transit-oriented areas: up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops. **Trap specific to this sub-area:** the RS1-S small-lot subdivision standard (minimum 9 m lot width, 325 m² lot area) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside — **College Park is not on that list**, so do not price a College Park lot off the small-lot subdivision offer. Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached zones except manufactured-home parks, and a secondary suite may not be stratified. Environmental Development Permit Areas DPA 4 (environmentally sensitive areas) and DPA 5 (hazardous lands / steep slope) apply in parts of the area, and lots near watercourses need a riparian assessment under Zoning Bylaw s. 5.4; no floodplain or ALR land is confirmed in College Park. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf and https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/college-park-pm.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/college-park-pm-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Port Moody property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty after September 15, 2026. Utility fees are billed separately; the 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/business-and-development/utilities.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/college-park-pm.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — College Park's verified stock is resale stock, so GST does not apply to a Westhill Place or Seaview Drive purchase. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $715,000 (the Port Moody community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no College Park median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,300 (2% × $515,000) = **$12,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (not applicable to this area's resale stock). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside College Park was verified in this program (the one live project its searches surfaced, Westport Village, sits in Moody Centre); for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for College Park: no in-area presale verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/college-park-pm-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
