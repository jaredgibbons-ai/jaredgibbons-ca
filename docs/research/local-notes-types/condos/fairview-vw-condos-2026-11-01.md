- Fairview is on the south shore of False Creek, stretching from Granville Island east to Science World, and south to Shaughnessy; it takes in all of False Creek South, South Granville north of 16th Avenue, the Granville and Burrard corridors and the Broadway spine between Clark Drive and Vine Street; exact boundary roads in City text: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Fairview the Form B is also the document that exposes tenure: a leasehold strata's Form B and title show the ground lease, and at Terraces on 7th the mandatory service agreement sits alongside the strata documents. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. For Hycroft Towers (1952) the report's envelope and systems findings are the central document of the purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed would be void to that extent. Note the limit of the rule in this area: it does nothing for a leasehold or co-op purchase, where the ground lease or co-op rules — not strata bylaws — govern occupancy and resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. This is directly live in Fairview: Terraces on 7th's published 55-plus restriction is the permitted form of age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the FM-1 District Schedule (Fairview Slopes) applies above the shore — its intent is to retain the existing residential character of houses and low-profile multiple dwellings, with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60 in the schedule overview; R1-1 covers the southern house streets and C-3A the arterial commercial sites. The Broadway Plan covers the northern half of Fairview (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) and the Broadway Subway lands in fall 2027 per the Province, with South Granville and Broadway-City Hall stations at the area's edges. Parcel-level zone and plan coverage: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **District energy charges:** no source checked states a district-energy utility charge for a counted Section 2 building — applicability and amount: UNCONFIRMED; False Creek South buildings connect to the False Creek energy system in parts of the shore, but no building-specific charge is stated in the sources checked, so none is quoted. Check the Form B.
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Fairview's counted stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. PTT treatment of a leasehold interest differs from freehold — a False Creek South leasehold purchase needs tax advice on its specific lease before an offer, not the standard table. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to Broadway Plan presales along the corridor. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Note separately: leasehold properties are harder to finance and insure than freehold — a lender's leasehold policy, not the premium table, is usually the binding constraint on False Creek South stock. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a False Creek South "condo" actually a condo?** Often not in the freehold sense: the City counts 12 leasehold strata buildings (669 units), 7 co-ops (517 units) and 6 rental buildings on its False Creek South land, with leases expiring in the next 15 to 25 years from 2021. Confirm tenure on title before pricing anything on the waterfront. https://council.vancouver.ca/20211021/documents/pspc2revisedstaffpresentation.pdf
- **Q: What does the 55+ rule at Terraces on 7th actually require?** The published terms are a 55-plus age restriction, 1 cat or 1 dog, and a mandatory service agreement with 10 meals per month, weekly housekeeping and 24-hour concierge. The age bylaw is lawful under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 (55+ is the only permitted form, with exemptions for legacy residents, caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners). The service agreement is a separate contract — get its current monthly cost in writing; it is UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3117996-1570-w-7th-avenue-1001-vancouver-bc-v6j-5m1/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Fairview condo?** For a long-term tenancy in a freehold strata, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. In a co-op or on a ground lease, the co-op rules or lease govern instead, and the 2022 change does not override them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How old is the stock, really?** Fairview spans the region's widest age range in one file: Hycroft Towers was completed in 1952 (158 units, Art Deco, Semmens & Simpson), Manhattan West in 1992, The Oaks in 2004, and Terraces on 7th in 2002/2003 (sources conflict — see Section 2). Building age drives the depreciation report conversation more here than almost anywhere else in the batch. https://blurealty.com/buildings/hycroft-towers/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fairview address?** SD39 catchments are address-based. The two VSB elementaries located in Fairview are L'Ecole Bilingue Elementary (1166 West 14th Avenue) and False Creek Elementary (900 School Green); no public secondary school inside the local area was confirmed in the research pass. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **Q: When does the Broadway Subway actually open?** The Province states the 5.7 km Millennium Line Broadway Extension, with six stations, will open in fall 2027; South Granville and Broadway-City Hall stations serve Fairview's west end and Cambie edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **Q: What is the Fairview Slopes zoning doing to resale value?** The FM-1 district's stated intent is to retain houses and low-profile multiple dwellings at a maximum 0.60 FSR — the Slopes' low-rise character is a zoning product, not an accident, which cuts both ways: limited assembly upside, protected scale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **Q: Is flood risk a Fairview issue?** Yes on the shore: the City's designated floodplain includes False Creek South, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED — check the parcel before removing subjects on a waterfront unit. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City owns about 80% of False Creek South.** That single fact explains the area's tenure mix — 12 leasehold strata buildings, 7 co-ops and 6 rental buildings on City land per the City's October 21, 2021 presentation — and why "waterfront Fairview" and "freehold Fairview" are different markets. https://council.vancouver.ca/20211021/documents/pspc2revisedstaffpresentation.pdf
- **Most False Creek leases expire in 2036 per the secondary guide** — inside a standard 25-year mortgage horizon, which is why lenders and buyers discount the stock and why the remaining lease term, not the suite, is the first number to check. https://www.rew.ca/guide/articles/false-creek-vancouver-1.2096229
- **Three heritage houses at 16th and Burrard are one townhome project.** The Maguire (1913), Bayne (1919) and Elson (1919) residences were restored as Heritage: Sixteenth & Burrard — 12 homes total — and the City's November 14, 2017 hearing minutes record all three as designated protected heritage property, which binds future exterior work. http://council.vancouver.ca/20171114/documents/phea20171114min.pdf
- **Hycroft Towers predates the strata era it now lives in.** Completed in 1952 as a rental-era Art Deco tower (Semmens & Simpson), it is today Strata Plan VAS90 with 158 units — an early-plan strata whose documents reward a slow read. https://blurealty.com/buildings/hycroft-towers/
- **South Granville's Gallery Row is in Fairview.** The South Granville shopping district and its Gallery Row art galleries sit inside the local area north of 16th Avenue — the retail amenity base for the Slopes and Oak Street stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
- **Firehall Library is the branch.** Vancouver Public Library's Firehall Branch is at 1455 West 10th Avenue, and Charleson Park (999 Charleson Street) sits on the False Creek South shore — the two civic anchors for the waterfront stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fairview-vw.md)
