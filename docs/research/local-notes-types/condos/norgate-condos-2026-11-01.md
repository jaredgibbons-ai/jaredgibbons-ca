## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Norgate strata is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Norgate building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw — all 9 verified North Vancouver 55+ strata sit outside this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the District of North Vancouver's Official Community Plan is **OCP Bylaw 7900**, which directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods; Norgate's nearest growth node is the Lions Gate Village Centre at Capilano Road and Marine Drive, planned under the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan. Zoning is **District Zoning Bylaw 3210** (the Marine Drive condo buildings sit in the Marine Drive commercial/mixed-use framework; the retail strata at 1150 Marine Drive is recorded under C9 – Marine Drive Commercial zoning). Suites and density: since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. SSMUH caution: the District's GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — but a March 2, 2026 District Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area, which includes Norgate, from SSMUH. Verify the specific lot on GEOweb before relying on density. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **District property taxes (2026):** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value (municipal rate 1.83419), and combined single-family utilities total $2,571 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). These are District rates — the City of North Vancouver's rates do not apply here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Norgate's verified stock is resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $900,000 (illustration only — no Norgate or North Vancouver median is published in any source this program cites), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $14,000 (2% × $700,000) = **$16,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (not applicable to this area's resale stock). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Norgate was verified in this program (the Lions Gate Village towers its searches surfaced, including Park West, are board-classed Pemberton NV); for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for Norgate: no in-area presale verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "City of North Vancouver" label in some listings for this bench is wrong.** The research file's official sources put Norgate in the District; a buyer who budgets on City tax rates or asks the City permit desk about a Norgate lot is working from the wrong municipality's numbers. District 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% penalties, and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Norgate's condos are all on one street edge.** Every verified condo building in this file is a Marine Drive address at the subdivision's south boundary — the post-war crescents behind them contain no strata product at all on any source checked, which is why a "Norgate condo" search that returns a Tatlow or Garden Avenue address should be read with suspicion. (Section 2 sources.)
- **One developer complex straddles a board line.** The Drive's two Marine Drive addresses fall in two different board sub-areas (1177 in Norgate, 1330 in Pemberton NV) — sub-area stats, school-search defaults and listing feeds will treat two halves of one complex as different neighbourhoods. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/1330-marine-drive/201
- **SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver figures at all.** The GVR September 2026 report's communities are Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge — so any "Norgate median price" quoted from SnapStats does not exist; sub-area medians are Paragon-only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **The land across Welch Street is not District land and never will be planned by the District.** It is Capilano IR No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn). In October 2024 Squamish Nation Council approved mixed-use market residential, Nation housing and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre for the reserve's Marine Drive site — the southern outlook of Welch Street homes is an active development front. https://www.squamish.net/squamish-nation-council-approves-land-uses-for-three-sites-extends-moratorium-on-third-party-development-proposals/
- **The recreation centre for this bench has no pool.** The Lions Gate Community Recreation Centre (1733 Lions Gate Lane, opened June 4, 2022) is District-owned and NVRC-operated with a gymnasium, kitchen, multipurpose and art spaces, the NVDPL Lions Gate Express Library and Capilano Community Services Society — and no pool. https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/docs/About-Us/june_23_2022_commission_meeting_agenda_package_final.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **East-edge lots need a creek check, not an assumption.** Mackay Creek runs along the subdivision's east side, and the District's OCP Schedule B establishes Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard Development Permit Areas; the District's GEOweb flood construction levels are modelled on a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-single-family-residential-1-5.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
