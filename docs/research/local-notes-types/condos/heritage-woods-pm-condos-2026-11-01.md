## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that governs the townhouse purchases that make up this sub-area's strata market — and the document where Cobalt Sky's conflicting published unit count (Section 2) gets settled against the strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Cobalt Sky's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children under Strata Property Regulation 7.01. No Heritage Woods strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is **Port Moody OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (adopted April 28, 2026; it repeals Bylaw No. 2955, which is not the current plan and is never to be cited as such). Chapter 14, Section 14.3 covers Heritage Woods with the Heritage Mountain grouping, with policies to retain the existing residential and forested character of the grouping. Zoning is the City of Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937: single-detached homes sit in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/heritage-woods-pm-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas; under the June 2024 Zoning Bylaw amendment (Amendment Bylaw No. 94, 2024, No. 3460), the City must allow up to four units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², and up to six units on such lots within 400 m of frequent bus stops. Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached residential zones (except manufactured home parks) under the City's 2004 amendment, and in the duplex zone since 2018; a suite may not be stratified. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ and https://api.ghdcdn.com/portmoody-edocs/v1/eDocs/Get?docnumber=632150 (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/heritage-woods-pm-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Creek and slope rules that bite on this hillside:** lots with a watercourse need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep slope development permit areas can apply. No floodplain or ALR land was confirmed in Heritage Woods. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md)
- **Port Moody property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/utilities (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $715,000 (the Port Moody community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026 — community level, illustration only; no Heritage Woods median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,300 (2% × $515,000) = **$12,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no Heritage Woods presale project was verified on a developer site or a City development page; where a unit anywhere is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md)
