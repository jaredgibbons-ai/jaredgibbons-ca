## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified — the rule that leads this section, because it is why the prairie has no condo stock.** On land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, a residence cannot be turned into strata lots (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Sumas Prairie is ALR ground: a prairie farmhouse is a house on land under the ALC residence frame, permanently — it cannot be strata-subdivided into condo units, and no ALR parcel is condo stock. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The ALC housing frame on ALR parcels:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with a maximum of 1,000 m² of fill permitted for that residence; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. ALR status for any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED — per-parcel check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **City plan — corrected:** Abbotsford's current Official Community Plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025. The guide page cites the OCP as "Bylaw 2600-2016" and other program sources have carried "2500-2016" — both are stale/conflicted, neither is current, and the conflict is flagged here and at the top of this file rather than resolved by picking one of the old numbers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **Zoning frame:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2014 No. 2400 applies city-wide — there is NO Bylaw 3100. The City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2), so most prairie parcels cannot be split further in any form, strata or fee-simple. The zone of any specific parcel: UNVERIFIED, per-parcel check. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **Suites and coach/garden suites (Abbotsford's own rules, not exported elsewhere):** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400, a secondary suite must be less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — subject on ALR land to the ALC frame above (suite only within the principal residence). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **SSMUH:** Abbotsford's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages in this program's checks and is never asserted here as "3560-2024" or any other number. How SSMUH applies on ALR prairie parcels is a per-parcel question and is not stated in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **Flood status for any specific parcel** (elevation, Flood Construction Level, dyke/pump position): UNVERIFIED — confirm with the City for the specific property before subjects come off. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/sumas-prairie-guide.md)
- **The strata rules, for contrast — they bite only if the same buyer buys strata in urban Abbotsford:** **Form B** (SPA s. 59) states strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote; **CRF:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022. **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 — only 55+ is lawful, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. None of this applies to a prairie farm purchase, because there is no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Sumas Prairie exists in any source checked — the line is here only because the standard block requires it. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
