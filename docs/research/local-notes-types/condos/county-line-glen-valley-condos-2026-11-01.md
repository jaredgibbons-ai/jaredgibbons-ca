## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified — the rule that leads this section.** On land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, a residence cannot be turned into strata lots (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). The ALR frame governs any parcel in the Reserve, which is the first check on this sub-area's acreage stock: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it. Whether any specific parcel in this feed is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping, not checked parcel by parcel in this pass. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/county-line-glen-valley.md)
- **Township SSMUH — stated so it is not misapplied to this sub-area:** the Township of Langley adopted Bylaw No. 6020 on November 18, 2024, allowing 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and NO 6-plex / 6-unit transit tier exists in Bylaw 6020. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/county-line-glen-valley.md)
- **Township secondary suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. LIVE CONFLICT, reported with dates and not resolved: the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses and describes suites as within detached single-family dwellings, while the current s. 102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source, and this file follows the bylaw where the two differ. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/county-line-glen-valley.md)
- **Township coach houses:** in Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; no size cap for a coach house appears in s. 100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass — a coach-house size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. **Township short-term rentals:** the Township does not issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations; the permitted-use status of STRs under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 was not readable on a Township page in that pass — STR status in the Township is UNVERIFIED beyond the licensing point. - **The strata rules, for contrast — they bite only if the same buyer buys strata elsewhere in Langley:** **Form B** (Strata Property Act s. 59) states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022. **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful, with exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). None of this applies to a Glen Valley acreage purchase, because there is no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **GST and taxes on an acreage purchase:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — this sub-area's stock is resale acreage product. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
