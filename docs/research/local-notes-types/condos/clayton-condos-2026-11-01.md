## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Clayton is a Surrey board sub-area in the Cloverdale community; the City of Surrey plans the same ground as Clayton Heights, a roughly 809-hectare (2,000-acre) plan area running from Fraser Highway south to the Agricultural Land Reserve to the north and west, with the Township of Langley at the east edge. The board's street-level boundary line itself is UNVERIFIED — it sits behind a members-only map layer. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md) https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/clayton-general-land-use-plan
- The City divides the area into East Clayton, West Clayton and North Clayton neighbourhood concept plan (NCP) areas; East Clayton's NCP was endorsed in 2003 over about 314 hectares, West Clayton's was approved in July 2015 over about 288 hectares with 12,000+ future residents planned, and the North Clayton NCP has not yet been initiated. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md) https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/east-clayton https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- Both NCP designation sets have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan update — Surrey's current OCP is Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026, not the predecessor OCP. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
- The apartment-condo stock clusters along 68 Avenue, 194 Street and 195A Street in the older East Clayton core; the newest building, Harlo, sits in East Clayton near 65B Avenue and 195A Street. (Building entries in §2, each cited there.)
- SnapStats' September 2026 report counts Clayton at 34 detached and 96 condos-and-townhomes active with 10 and 20 sold — a 29% detached and 21% attached sales ratio, the highest ratios in the Cloverdale set — and Clayton is attached-led, with 96 attached listings against 34 detached. SnapStats publishes no sub-area median prices; Cloverdale community medians for September 2026 were $1,212,500 detached and $740,000 attached. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
- Transit today is bus service on Fraser Highway and the collector grid; the Surrey Langley SkyTrain — a 16 km Expo Line extension from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, anticipated in service in 2029 — will add two stations inside Clayton: Hillcrest-184 Street at the northwest corner of 184 Street and Fraser Highway, and the future Clayton Station at 190 Street and Fraser Highway. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- The Form B Information Certificate is the buyer's core document in every Clayton strata: under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a fee of no more than $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for copies. It is where the real numbers live — strata fees, contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocation, and any bylaw that actually applies to the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- Depreciation reports: since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years — that captures every complex in §2. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Minimum CRF contribution: since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the annual operating fund to the CRF each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- Rentals: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Where a directory line above says rentals are "allowed with restrictions" (Calera, Compass), the restriction half of that line no longer has legal effect; a strata can still regulate short-term rentals separately. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- Age bylaws: the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+, under ss. 123.1 and 123.2 of the Strata Property Act, with exemptions added May 1, 2023 (including for caregivers and for spouses/partners who met the rule when they moved in). No Clayton complex in §2 is published as 55+; if a listing ever describes one that way, the bylaw itself is confirmed on the Form B, not from listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- Pets: pet limits remain lawful strata bylaws. Directory lines above state pets allowed with restrictions at Waterstone, Calera and Yale Bloc, a two-pet allowance at Compass, and dogs OK in a Harlo listing header — treat all of these as pointers only; the enforceable limit is the registered bylaw set attached to the Form B. (Building entries in §2, each cited there.)
- Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. On July 8, 2024 the City renamed several residential zones (RF-12/RF-12C became R4; RF-SD became R6), so older Clayton documents may cite zone names that no longer exist in the current bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
- Small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH): Surrey permits 3 units on lots under 280 m² and 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This matters around Clayton's house lots, not inside the strata complexes. One secondary suite per house is permitted under the BC Building Code framework; in Surrey a suite is capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area and is never permitted inside a coach or garden suite, and an unpermitted suite is not made legal by the SSMUH rules. Coach/garden suites run 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), need lane access on smaller lots, and require one added parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
- Short-term rentals in Surrey: owner-only, primary residence only, stays under 90 consecutive days, with a City business licence at $358 per year. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- ALR edge: Clayton's north and west edges meet the Agricultural Land Reserve. On ALR land a principal residence is capped at 500 m² with one additional residence of 90 m² (lots of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (lots over 40 ha) — relevant to acreage buyers at the fringe, not to the strata stock. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty applied after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- GST on new builds is 5%. It applies to developer sales — including Harlo's developer units and Steelix on 188th presales — and not to ordinary resales. The first-time home buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on a new home priced up to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5M, for agreements entered into from March 20, 2025 to 2031. The older GST New Housing Rebate covers homes the FTHB rebate does not: 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- BC Property Transfer Tax: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion up to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion of a price above $3M. The First-Time Home Buyers' exemption is full up to $835,000 fair market value (on the first $500,000) and partial up to $860,000; the Newly Built Home Exemption is full up to $1.1M and partial up to $1.15M — the exemption that matters for Harlo and Steelix on 188th purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- Federal first-time buyer tools: the Home Buyers' Plan allows a $60,000 RRSP withdrawal; the First Home Savings Account allows $40,000 lifetime at $8,000 per year. CMHC mortgage insurance premiums run 0.6%–4.5% depending on down payment and amortization. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- Presales: REDMA gives a 7-day rescission period under s. 21 and requires purchase deposits to be held in trust under s. 18 — the framework that applies to Steelix on 188th contracts and to any developer-unit sale at Harlo. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Is Clayton mostly townhouses, or are there real apartment condos?** Both, and townhouses dominate. SnapStats counted 96 attached listings in Clayton in September 2026 against 34 detached, but the verified apartment-condo stock is just five complexes — Waterstone, Calera, the Compass apartment phase, Yale Bloc and Harlo — against a much longer townhouse list. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md; §2 above)
- **Which buildings are the newest?** Harlo, recorded built 2025, is the newest completed complex; before it, Yale Bloc 3 (2019) and the Compass and Calera buildings (2008–2010) bracket the range. Steelix on 188th is presale only, with completion not yet announced by its presale source. (§2 above, each cited there.)
- **Can I rent my unit out?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, so directory lines saying "rentals allowed with restrictions" at Calera or Compass no longer restrict a long-term rental. Short-term rentals are different: Surrey allows them only in an owner's primary residence, under 90 consecutive days, with a $358/year business licence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Are pets allowed?** Published sources say pets are allowed with restrictions at Waterstone, Calera and Yale Bloc, two pets at Compass, and dogs OK in a Harlo listing header — but the enforceable rule is the registered bylaw set on the Form B, and pet bylaws remain fully lawful in BC. (§2 and §3 above.)
- **Why do sources disagree on Calera's size?** Because two are published: the building directory says 153 units and an MLS data sheet for a unit in the complex says 95 units in the development under strata plan BCS3888. Neither has been reconciled against the strata plan itself, so this file marks the count UNCONFIRMED rather than picking one. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/calera-18818-68-avenue https://www.cotala.com/tours/76013/FullPublic.pdf?i=1054685387
- **Does buying at Harlo or Steelix on 188th carry GST?** Yes — 5% GST applies to new-build sales, and a Harlo listing itself advertises a GST rebate as applicable. Eligible first-time buyers may recover it under the Bill C-4 FTHB GST rebate (100% to a $1M price, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1.5M); the Newly Built Home Exemption can also remove BC PTT up to $1.1M. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **When does the SkyTrain actually reach Clayton?** The Surrey Langley SkyTrain is anticipated in service in 2029, with two stations in Clayton — Hillcrest-184 Street and the future Clayton Station at 190 Street. "Anticipated" is the project's own wording; it is not a guaranteed opening date. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain
- **Which schools serve a Clayton condo?** The BC Ministry K-12 file lists Katzie, Hazelgrove, Regent Road, Sunrise Ridge, Don Christian and Martha Currie elementaries, plus Clayton Heights Secondary and École Salish Secondary, at addresses inside the area — but no School District 36 catchment-locator run was completed for Clayton in this research, so the catchment for any specific building is UNVERIFIED until checked by address. Note the Ministry file's own level field mislabels Clayton Heights Secondary; its level is read from its name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
- **What does the Form B tell me that a listing won't?** The strata's actual fee for the unit, the CRF balance, whether parking and storage are strata lots, common property or limited common property, and the bylaws in force — for $35 plus copying at most, delivered within 7 days under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Is there a median condo price for Clayton itself?** No published one. SnapStats reports sub-area activity only (Clayton attached: 96 active, 20 sold, 21% ratio in September 2026); its prices are community-level — Cloverdale attached median $740,000 for September 2026 — and no sub-area median exists to quote. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- The Clayton Community Centre at 7155 187A Street opened October 16, 2021 — 75,000 sq ft including a 14,000 sq ft library branch — and it is the amenity anchor the whole sub-area walks to; strata complexes here sell against it because most Clayton buildings have modest in-house amenities by comparison. https://www.surrey.ca/news-events/news/city-of-surrey-officially-opens-clayton-community-centre-4
- Waterstone is not one building of one age: the original 194 Street buildings date to 2010 and the Esplanade phase to 2015, so two units both marketed as "Waterstone" can be five years apart in envelope and warranty terms. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/waterstone-esplanade-6480-194-street https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6480-194-street/319
- West Clayton is planned around a rapid transit station at 184 Street and Fraser Highway that does not exist yet — the plan was approved in July 2015 with 12,000+ future residents, and the SkyTrain now under construction is the delivery mechanism, anticipated in service in 2029. Land and presale pricing near 184 Street already trades on that station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md) https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/
- Older Clayton documents cite zone names that no longer exist: Surrey renamed RF-12/RF-12C to R4 and RF-SD to R6 on July 8, 2024, so a disclosure statement or older strata document quoting the old names is not necessarily wrong about the property — it is quoting a repealed label. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/clayton.md)
