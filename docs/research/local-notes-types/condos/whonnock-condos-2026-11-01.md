## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

*This section gives the standard BC strata rules that would apply if strata stock existed here, plus the rural rules that actually govern Whonnock property today. The strata rules are stated so a buyer who finds a future strata listing in this sub-area has the frame — they do not imply any strata exists in Whonnock now.*

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Whonnock purchase in the current stock involves a Form B, because no strata stock was verified (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. There is no Whonnock directory entry in this file for this rule to void in-entry — it is stated as the governing law for any future strata here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No 55+ housing of any kind was found in Whonnock in this program (Section 2), so no Whonnock building is described as age-restricted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Zoning is Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019; its schedule lists minimum lot areas including A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text.
