# Cedardale — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cedardale is a small residential neighbourhood in the east part of the District of West Vancouver, below the British Properties and west of the Capilano River corridor, around Burley Drive, Kings Avenue and Mathers Avenue, between Sentinel Hill / Ambleside below and the Taylor Way corridor at its east side. The District uses the name "Cedardale" on its neighbourhood context map and its municipal map, but a District planning-area status and published street-by-street boundaries are NOT CONFIRMED — it is a mapped neighbourhood name, not a named OCP plan area. District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere — there is none (Section 2). The verified housing form is detached houses on RS-zone lots on the Burley Drive / Kings Avenue / Mathers Avenue grid — a small, purely residential pocket with its own elementary school and two District parks, at the District's east edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Nearby areas: Park Royal (east/south-east, at the foot of Taylor Way), Ambleside (south, along Marine Drive), Sentinel Hill and Queens (west), the British Properties (north, uphill), and — across the Capilano River — North Vancouver's Capilano / Norgate side. Park Royal, at 2002 Park Royal South, is the District's regional shopping centre named in OCP Policy 2.3.2 — Cedardale is the closest West Vancouver detached-house neighbourhood to it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Main streets and transit: Burley Drive, Kings Avenue and Mathers Avenue are the internal streets; Taylor Way reaches Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway) directly, and Marine Drive / Taylor Way reach the Lions Gate Bridge. TransLink route 251 (Park Royal / Queens) runs through the area via Marine Drive, 17th Street, Fulton Avenue, 11th Street, Kings Avenue, Burley Drive, Mathers Avenue and 15th Street — Cedardale's own streets carry a bus to Park Royal Exchange, where the R2 RapidBus has run one-seat to Metrotown since September 7, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Anchors inside the area: École Cedardale (K–7), 595 Burley Drive — a single-track French Immersion program and an authorized IB World School — plus Cedardale Park and Burley Drive Park, both listed in the District's Parks Master Plan. The Capilano Pacific Trail runs along the Capilano River corridor at the east edge of the District, beside Cedardale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- Market numbers: West Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The provincial strata block below governs any strata purchase a Cedardale buyer makes elsewhere in West Vancouver; inside Cedardale itself there is no strata stock for it to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Ambleside or Park Royal building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Cedardale sits under the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010; houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones, and the residential zone names evidenced in and beside Cedardale in District records are RS3 and RS5 (Cedardale Park is listed RS3 and Burley Drive Park RS5 in the Parks Master Plan). Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among the permitted uses in the single-family zones; the bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. A Cedardale-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Provincial density directive — per-property check required:** on July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025. Cedardale sits between Ambleside and Park Royal–Taylor Way, so whether its streets fall inside those "adjacent" areas must be verified per property. The District missed the December 31, 2025 deadline and a provincial order that took effect April 7, 2026 implemented the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, with District progress reports to the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining directives. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Cedardale is confirmed — no approved major project inside Cedardale is confirmed on a District source — so this is relevant only to presales a buyer compares elsewhere in West Vancouver. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C4.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **West Vancouver tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. The Province's additional school tax can apply at higher Cedardale assessments — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo or townhome in Cedardale itself?** No verified strata stock exists in the neighbourhood — the confirmed housing form is detached houses in the RS zones on the Burley Drive / Kings Avenue / Mathers Avenue grid, and the research file records strata condos and townhomes inside Cedardale as none confirmed. The nearest real condo stock is at Park Royal and in Ambleside, documented in their own files. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: A rental site lists "Cedardale condos" — where are they really?** Outside the neighbourhood. The aggregator results under the Cedardale name lead with Park Royal buildings (for example Executive on the Park, 423 6th Street) or single-family houses offered for rent. Check the street address against the Burley/Kings/Mathers grid before treating a result as Cedardale stock. https://www.zumper.com/apartments-for-rent/west-vancouver-bc/cedardale
- **Q: Is École Cedardale my catchment school if I buy on Burley Drive?** Not by address alone. École Cedardale (595 Burley Drive) is a single-track French Immersion school and authorized IB World School; Early French Immersion enrolment generally occurs at Kindergarten (or Grade 1 until the end of Term One, space permitting) and placement is by lottery held in early December. The English catchment school for a Cedardale address is UNVERIFIED in the research file — the neighbouring English-program schools are Westcot Elementary (760 Westcot Road) and West Vancouver Secondary (1750 Mathers Avenue), and the exact feeder must be run in the SD45 locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Cedardale?** No. Secondary suites and detached secondary suites are permitted uses in the RS zones, but they are accessory to the detached lot — they are not separate strata lots and cannot be bought on their own title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: What zoning covers a Cedardale lot, and how big are the minimum lots?** The RS zones in Section 200 of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — RS3 (975 m² minimum) and RS5 (488 m²) are the zones evidenced in District records in and beside the area, within the Section 200 range of 488 m² to 8,094 m². Confirm the specific lot's zone on the District's Zoning Map, because subdivision and suite potential differ lot by lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: Could density rules change Cedardale's streets?** Possibly — the Province's July 21, 2025 directive targeted increased density in single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave and at Park Royal–Taylor Way, and Cedardale sits between Ambleside and Park Royal–Taylor Way. Whether its streets fall inside those "adjacent" areas is UNVERIFIED and must be checked per property; the District's implementation was still being reported quarterly through 2026–2027 after the April 7, 2026 provincial order on the Ambleside plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: If I buy a condo at Park Royal or in Ambleside instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a building directory still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What replaces Form B diligence for a Cedardale house purchase?** There is no strata, so there is no Form B, depreciation report or CRF to review. The equivalent checks are the lot's RS zone and minimum site area on the District's Zoning Map, the watercourse permit check on east-edge lots near the Capilano River corridor (the District's Environmental Development Permit Area covers watercourses and riparian habitat), and whether the lot falls inside the Province's density-directive areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: How do I get around from Cedardale without driving everywhere?** Route 251 runs on Cedardale's own streets (Kings Avenue, Burley Drive, Mathers Avenue) to Park Royal Exchange, where the R2 RapidBus runs one-seat to Metrotown and route 250 runs on Marine Drive to downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge; route 254 select trips also route via Inglewood Avenue, Burley Drive and Kings Avenue. By car, Taylor Way reaches Highway 1 directly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Q: Are there extra taxes at Cedardale price points?** Potentially: the Province's additional school tax applies at 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026 (rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027), on top of District taxes due July 2, 2026 with 5% + 5% staged penalties, and metered quarterly water and sewer utilities. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The school inside the neighbourhood is a lottery school, not a catchment guarantee.** École Cedardale was authorized as an IB World School in 2016 and offers the IB Primary Years Programme in French Immersion format; Early French Immersion is offered at École Cedardale and École Pauline Johnson, applicants may choose either or both schools, and placement is by lottery in early December — even a French Immersion family on Burley Drive is not guaranteed the in-area school. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **A pocket this small has two District parks of its own.** Cedardale Park and Burley Drive Park are both listed in the District's Parks Master Plan (Cedardale Park as RS3, Dedicated) — the neighbourhood's amenity story is those parks, the school and the Capilano Pacific Trail on its east edge, not any commercial node: shopping or grocery inside Cedardale itself is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed on a District source). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Cedardale is a mapped name, not a planning area.** The District uses "Cedardale" on its neighbourhood context map (in its Five Year Financial Plan) and municipal map, and SD45 uses the school name, but no District source publishes street-by-street boundaries or a Cedardale local area plan — legal and permit searches run on the street address and RS zone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **The fastest bus on the North Shore terminates beside the neighbourhood.** Park Royal Exchange, beside Cedardale, is the western terminus of the R2 RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station — a detached-house pocket with a rapid-transit terminus at its edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **Half the tax bill is not the District's.** Approximately 50% of the West Vancouver tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities, and utilities are metered and billed quarterly — a buyer used to annual utility billing elsewhere should not assume the same calendar. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for this market.** West Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the reports used for this program, so any "Cedardale median" quoted from those reports does not exist; sub-area inventory, solds and medians are UNVERIFIED and no substitute source is used. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cedardale.md)
