## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 2008–2018 buildings cross-referenced in Section 2, the depreciation report attached to the Form B is the central value document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — that voids, in-entry, the rental portion of the directory lines for Kingston House and Tehama in Section 2 ("rentals allowed w/restrictions"). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. None of the four verified apartment buildings in Section 2 is published as 55+ — any age claim for them is UNCONFIRMED until the Form B and registered bylaws are read. (The 55+ description attached to The Carrington in listing text concerns a townhome queue item, not the verified condo stock.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, which received final adoption on June 15, 2026. The former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning; a Prescott Commons MLS data sheet records CD zoning for that complex. The ground's local plans are the Rosemary Heights Central NCP, Rosemary Heights West NCP and Rosemary Heights Business Park Plan. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/morgan-creek-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Detached-lot rules that also apply in this sub-area:** SSMUH allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024. A secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000 — a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/morgan-creek-guide.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/morgan-creek-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all four verified buildings are completed resale stock (2008–2018), so a resale carries no GST. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is sold on this page — the verified buildings are completed resale stock. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise elsewhere in South Surrey, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
