## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in Coal Harbour's hotel-mixed towers (Fairmont Pacific Rim, Paradox) the Form B is also where the residential strata's separation from the hotel component shows. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is far above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by a strata, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing — the two layers are distinct, and the City licence specifics for a given building: UNCONFIRMED in this pass. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is void as a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Coal Harbour parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 together with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754), which states Coal Harbour should be an integral part of downtown Vancouver. The DD schedule permits commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any Coal Harbour purchase, where investor-held units are common. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Additional school tax:** at Coal Harbour price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Floodplain:** Coal Harbour is on the Burrard Inlet waterfront, and the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain — parcels in it are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; whether a specific Coal Harbour parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Coal Harbour stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the top tier is routinely in play at Coal Harbour price points. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current Coal Harbour presale offering; a specific current presale in Coal Harbour: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a published range of 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Coal Harbour a separate neighbourhood or part of Downtown?** Both, in different systems: "Coal Harbour" is the Paragon sub-area name, but the City of Vancouver's official local area is Downtown — Coal Harbour is a neighbourhood inside it with its own Official Development Plan (By-law 6754). It is not part of the West End, even though the City's areas page links the Coal Harbour locality name to the West End page. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Can I rent out a Coal Harbour condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are separate: a strata can still restrict them and the City of Vancouver licences them. The Empty Homes Tax declaration applies if the unit sits empty instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is a unit in the Fairmont Pacific Rim or the Paradox a normal strata purchase?** The residences are strata condominiums (Fairmont Pacific Rim Residences: Freehold Strata, Strata Plan BCS3699, 173 residences above a 377-room hotel; Paradox: 217 apartments alongside 147 hotel rooms), but the hotel components mean the Form B, air-space parcels and cost-sharing arrangements need reading, not just the suite documents. https://thebrandrealestategroup.com/coal-harbour-condo-buildings/ and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradox_Hotel_Vancouver
- **Q: Which Coal Harbour buildings have townhouses as well as apartments?** On the developer's own figures: Cascina (117 suites + 30 townhouses), Denia (86 suites + 4 townhomes) and Escala (98 suites + 7 townhouses), plus townhouse homes at 1 and 2 Harbour Green and 17 adjacent townhomes at Carina. The townhouse units are strata too, but they are documented separately in Section 2 and are not apartment condos. https://aspac.ca/project-category/past-project/
- **Q: Are the harbour views protected?** Partly, by plan: the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan lists public street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets to the water, and requires the waterfront walkway system to be continuous along the Coal Harbour waterfront. A specific unit's view is still a unit-by-unit check, not a plan guarantee. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Q: Is there a school in Coal Harbour?** The VSB states it added new elementary school capacity in downtown Vancouver with the completion of a new elementary school in Coal Harbour, named on the VSB site as the Seaside school in Coal Harbour; its street address is UNVERIFIED in the research pass, no public secondary school inside Coal Harbour was confirmed, and catchments are address-based — run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. Elsie Roy and Crosstown elementaries are in Yaletown and Crosstown, not Coal Harbour, and must not be presented as Coal Harbour schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Q: What extra taxes hit a high-end Coal Harbour purchase?** Beyond standard PTT (with the further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million), the Province's additional school tax applies on assessed value above $3 million (0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026), and the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to every unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** The downtown core's SkyTrain hub (Waterfront and Burrard stations, where the Expo, Millennium and Canada Lines meet) sits in the containing Downtown local area south-east of Coal Harbour, and the R5 RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station; bus routes serving Coal Harbour itself by number: UNVERIFIED on a TransLink source in the research pass — measure the specific building's walk to the station rather than relying on listing language. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Carina's and Escala's unit counts?** Because the published sources genuinely differ: Carina is recorded as 93 units in total by one building source and as 86 apartments plus 17 townhomes by another, and Escala as 98 suites by its developer Aspac and 89 units by a secondary source. Both conflicts are flagged in Section 2 — the strata plan and the Form B are the settling documents. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/carina and https://aspac.ca/project-category/past-project/
- **Q: Is there a median Coal Harbour condo price I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used, per the research program's rule. Price from the specific building's recent sales instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Five of the waterfront towers are one developer's Italian-named set.** Aspac's Waterfront Place is a collection of five buildings on Nicola, Cardero, Broughton and Jervis Streets, completed in 2003 as a collection, with towers named for cities in Italy and Spain — Avila (build 1995–1998), Bauhinia, Cascina and Denia (the last two won a 2004 CHBA Gold Georgie for Best Multi-Family Development of the Year) and Escala. Cross-building comparisons inside Coal Harbour are unusually direct because so much stock shares one builder and one architect group. https://aspac.ca/project-category/past-project/
- **The Paradox tower changed names and hotels without changing its condos.** The 1151 West Georgia Street tower was completed in 2016 as the Trump International Hotel and Tower Vancouver; the hotel closed and filed for bankruptcy in 2020, the Trump branding was removed in December 2021, and the hotel reopened as the Paradox Vancouver on April 1, 2022 — the 217 residential apartments sat above all of it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paradox_Hotel_Vancouver
- **Coal Harbour has a new elementary school, but its address is not yet in the research file.** The VSB states a new elementary school in Coal Harbour was completed and added elementary capacity in downtown Vancouver; the research pass could not verify its street address or a catchment result for the sample address (480 Broughton Street, the community centre) — verify both in the VSB locator before promising either. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **The view corridors are a plan document, not a sales line.** The Official Development Plan (By-law 6754) names the street-end view corridors — Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero — and states land uses and circulation should discourage through commuter traffic, which is why the waterfront streets read quieter than the downtown core behind them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **The floodplain check is a waterfront check.** The City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain, and Coal Harbour parcels on Burrard Inlet are the edge to check against that dataset — parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements, and the building's insurance and strata documents should be read against them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
- **There is no detached fallback in this sub-area.** The research file records that single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form — a buyer set on this waterfront is choosing between towers and their strata documents, not between housing forms, which is why the building-level diligence in Sections 2 and 3 carries the whole purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
