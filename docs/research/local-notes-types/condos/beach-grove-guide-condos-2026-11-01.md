## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1977 building (Tsawwassen Mews) and a 2005 building (Heron Cove) alike, this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — that voids, in-entry, the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Heron Cove in Section 2. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Beach Grove building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (1985), and is never attributed to Bylaw 8400; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), and the designation list does NOT include "Single Family Residential". A City report (LU009394) describing 1728 Beach Grove Road under the Tsawwassen Area Plan is speaking of the former plan — historical framing only. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/beach-grove-guide.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" (the bylaw whose s. 6.2.3 sets the Beach Grove waterfront setback below), while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. What is not contested: zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps; the Beach Grove waterfront zone sheet RD2 states a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum for waterfront lots in Beach Grove. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/beach-grove-guide.md)
- **Waterfront setback and Hazard DPA SD3:** zoning s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting on Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level; Hazard DPA SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands), OCP Bylaw No. 8400 Appendix B, provides no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific lot falls inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/beach-grove-guide.md)
- **SSMUH:** Delta's zoning allows up to 4 units on single-detached and duplex lots in total, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites — the guide page's Planning wording that reads as a suite "plus" the 4-unit limit is a live-page defect recorded in Section 5. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/beach-grove-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Beach Grove itself is named in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises elsewhere, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 2, 2026. Property tax or levy notes specific to Beach Grove: UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/beach-grove-guide.md)
