# Cape Horn — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cape Horn is the established residential slope in south Coquitlam above the Cape Horn interchange, where Highway 1 (Trans-Canada Highway), Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) meet at the Port Mann Bridge approach; the City tracks the area as planning areas 15 and 16 in its development statistics. No City neighbourhood plan boundary for Cape Horn is stated on a City page in the sources read — the exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; the working description is the slope above the interchange, below Austin Avenue / Mariner Way. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: there is no completed apartment-condo stock on the slope itself — see the plain statement at the top of Section 2. The only strata product filed under Cape Horn by listing sources is the Fraser Mills presale site below the slope, on the Fraser River waterfront at United Boulevard & King Edward Street (Debut, Encore and Chapter by Beedie Living), plus one older townhome complex on Cape Horn Avenue itself (Crescentview, Section 2). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Sub-area attached volume: Cape Horn carried 13 condos & townhomes listings with 0 solds in September 2026 (sales ratio not applicable — 0 solds), against 18 detached listings with 2 solds (11%). SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: Coquitlam condos & townhomes median was $625,000 in September 2026 ($687/sqft, 96% sale-to-list, 23 days on market). The 13 attached listings are consistent with Fraser Mills presale inventory being filed under this sub-area, not with resale stock on the slope. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- The City's own permit record underlines the slope's form: the 2022 development statistics for Cape Horn (planning areas 15 and 16) record 18 net residential units created that year (36 created, 18 demolished), all in single-family / secondary-suite / carriage-house form, with no townhouse or apartment units. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- Main roads: Mariner Way and Cape Horn Avenue are the residential roads connecting the slope to the interchange; United Boulevard is the light-industrial and commercial road below the slope, and the Fraser Mills site sits at United Boulevard & King Edward Street. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- Transit anchors: the nearest SkyTrain stations are Braid, Lougheed Town Centre and Burquitlam — none is inside the sub-area. Bus routes serving Cape Horn's interior streets: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)

A later developer article records Debut as sold out. Completion timing conflicts between sources: the developer's approval article expected move-in by 2026, current listings record the year built as 2027, and the developer's Chapter article expects Debut, Encore and Chapter all complete by 2028 — completion is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan registers. Published rental/pet/age restrictions: UNCONFIRMED. - **Encore — 53 Kong Court (Beedie Living).** The second Fraser Mills tower: 32 storeys, 306 homes of condominiums and three-bedroom stacked townhomes, concrete construction, built to Energy Step Code 3, with almost 27,000 sq ft of amenities (developer sources). A presale source states the project is in the Cape Horn district of Coquitlam and expects completion in the summer of 2028; one current listing records the year built as 2029 — the conflict is noted, not resolved. Published restrictions: UNCONFIRMED. REDMA applies. https://www.beedie.ca/residential/encore-fraser-mills/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/encore-at-fraser-mills-presales/ and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3074359-53-kong-court-306-coquitlam-bc-v0v-0v0/brochure
- **Chapter — 58 Kong Street (Beedie Living).** The third Fraser Mills building and the developer's only wood-frame complex on the site: two six-storey low-rises over a shared parkade, 173 suites in total (82 units in the northern building, 91 in the southern), stratified housing, expected complete in 2028 (developer source). A presale-directory record for the same address records 173 units, 6 floors, low-rise form and preconstruction status. Published restrictions: UNCONFIRMED. REDMA applies. http://www.beedie.ca/2024/05/pre-sales-open-for-third-building-at-coquitlams-fraser-mills/ and https://www.stanleylamprec.com/buildings/view/27576/chapter/coquitlam/cape-horn/58-kong-st

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. There is no resale Form B at Fraser Mills yet — presale diligence there is the disclosure statement, not a Form B. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This will apply to Debut, Encore and Chapter once they register and age into the cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any future directory line showing a rental cap or ban at a Fraser Mills building will reflect old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Cape Horn building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — Chapter is marketed partly to downsizers and seniors, which is marketing, not an age bylaw; confirm any age bylaw on the disclosure statement / Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Council report list: Bylaw 5511, 2026 and Bylaw 5512, 2026), superseding the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. On the slope, small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² (R-1/R-3) and 465 m² (R-2), and the City's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **Floodplain — the Fraser Mills diligence item:** the Debut site is preloaded to stand 20 feet above median sea level because it is on the Fraser River floodplain — flood construction level, strata insurance and deductible position are first-order questions for every Fraser Mills purchase. On the slope, the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse, and Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks while s.523 covers riparian areas. https://storeys.com/beedie-fraser-mills-debut/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (all three Fraser Mills presales), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Fraser Mills purchases. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the governing frame for Debut, Encore and Chapter contracts. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026.** The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026, streamlining the old Citywide OCP (Bylaw No. 3479, 2001), with a fuller OCP update anticipated to begin in 2027. Planning documents and listing copy citing the old Citywide OCP designations are already a plan out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **The sub-area's attached listings are a construction site, not a resale market.** Cape Horn's 13 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026 sit against a City permit record (2022, planning areas 15/16) of zero townhouse or apartment units created — the listings are Fraser Mills presale contracts at three buildings (Debut 318 homes, Encore 306, Chapter 173) that between them will add 797 homes below the slope. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md) and https://www.beedie.ca/residential/encore-fraser-mills/
- **A 12-kilometre water main is being built through the area.** Metro Vancouver's Coquitlam Water Main Cape Horn Section is approximately four kilometres long and 2.2 metres in diameter, running to Hickey Drive and Mariner Way, including a trenchless tunnel crossing under Lougheed Highway — construction disruption on the Mariner Way corridor is a live diligence item for slope addresses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **Debut's podium hides the townhouses.** Of Debut's 318 strata units, 286 are high-rise apartments and 32 are townhouses inside the five-storey podium — "townhomes at Debut" are podium units in a tower strata, not a townhouse complex. https://storeys.com/beedie-fraser-mills-debut/
- **The interchange is the amenity and the discount at once.** Highway 1, Lougheed Highway (Highway 7), the Mary Hill Bypass (Highway 7B) and United Boulevard all meet below the slope with the Port Mann Bridge immediately east — the research file's standing advice is to walk a slope lot at rush hour and price view-and-noise lots street by street, because no official source states commute times for this interchange. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **Chapter is marketed to seniors but is not published as 55+.** The developer describes Chapter's buyers as families upgrading or seniors downsizing; no age-restriction bylaw is published in the sources checked. In BC only a 55+ bylaw under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 can restrict age — marketing language cannot. http://www.beedie.ca/2024/05/pre-sales-open-for-third-building-at-coquitlams-fraser-mills/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
