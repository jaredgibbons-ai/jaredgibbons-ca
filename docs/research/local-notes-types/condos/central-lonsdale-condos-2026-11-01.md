# Central Lonsdale — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Lonsdale is the City of North Vancouver's own name for the Lonsdale Avenue corridor core: the mixed-use blocks along Lonsdale Avenue around 13th to 23rd Streets, with Lions Gate Hospital, City Hall, the City Library and the new Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre inside or at the edge of the area. The City's Lonsdale Great Street project area extends from the Upper Levels Highway to Victoria Park and from St. Georges Avenue to Chesterfield Avenue; full neighbourhood street-by-street limits beyond that project area are UNVERIFIED. City-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2014, amended December 8, 2025). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- Condo stock actually concentrates on and immediately beside Lonsdale Avenue between 13th and 23rd Streets and on the numbered cross-streets one block east and west of it (13th Street East/West, 15th Street, 17th Street, Keith Road, 20th–22nd Streets, Chesterfield Avenue and St. Georges Avenue) — that is where every verified building in Section 2 sits. The research file calls the mix the North Shore's most urban: strata condos and apartments in low-, mid- and high-rise buildings on and beside the avenue, plus ground-oriented houses, duplexes and multiplexes on the side streets under the Ground Oriented Zone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- Nearby areas: Lower Lonsdale and Lonsdale Quay / the Shipyards (south, down Lonsdale Avenue to the waterfront), Upper Lonsdale (north, up Lonsdale Avenue past Highway 1 into the District), Grand Boulevard / Boulevard (east), and Mahon Park / Norgate (west). Buildings south of 13th Street are Lower Lonsdale stock and are not counted here (see the Crest note in Section 2). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- Main streets and transit: Lonsdale Avenue is the spine — south to Lonsdale Quay and the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront Station, every 15 minutes during the day), north to the Highway 1 interchange. TransLink route 230 runs the full length of Lonsdale Avenue through Central Lonsdale, from Lonsdale Quay to Upper Lonsdale; the R2 RapidBus runs east–west on 3rd Street / Marine Drive at the area's south edge and since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat from Park Royal through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- Anchors inside the area: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street; North Vancouver City Library, 120 West 14th Street; Queen Mary Community Elementary, 230 Keith Road West; Carson Graham Secondary, 2145 Jones Avenue, at the north end; and the Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre, main entrance 130 East 23rd Street, which opened Saturday, July 25, 2026 with an NHL-sized arena, a 25-metre pool, steam room, sauna, hot pools, fitness area and a full-sized gymnasium (at opening the aquatics centre was still in final checks and health-authority approvals, expected ready for fall programming). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- Market numbers: the City of North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In a corridor whose verified stock runs from a 1971 walkup (Georgian Manor) to 2026 towers (Elle, Lennox), the Form B package is where the age difference becomes a money difference. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every counted building in Section 2 is above the 5-lot threshold on the counts published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This is why the "rentals not allowed" directory lines at Victoria Park West and Ridge Park Gardens in Section 2 do not bar a long-term tenancy, and why The Grande's "rentals allowed with restrictions" line must be read in the filed bylaws rather than assumed to be a ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Ridge Park Gardens' published 55+ position ("one owner must be 55+") is consistent with the only valid form; the directory's bare "age restrictions" line at the excluded Chesterfield Manor, and its listing's "19+" term, are not — a further reason that building's documents need specialist review. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2014, amended December 8, 2025) and Zoning Bylaw No. 6700. Council approved the Lonsdale Great Street Plan on April 20, 2026 — a 20-year vision to strengthen and revitalize Central Lonsdale as a vibrant neighbourhood main street, with the first phase of public-space improvements underway in 2026. Side streets sit in the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, adopted December 8, 2025: a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; the adoption increased permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allows up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. The City sized its December 2025 updates to a 20-year housing need of 21,301 units on top of its existing 29,021 units. One secondary suite is permitted per single-family property (owner must reside at the property), and a suite may combine with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and Building Permit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- **Hazard permits:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank — relevant near the Wagg Creek and Mahon Park ravine lands at the area's west side, which is the Ridge Park Gardens side of the corridor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — a live cost on Elle, Lennox and the remaining Millennium homes, all marketed as new product. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption bands matter in this corridor because several new buildings price across them. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. This governs One20 purchases now and any remaining developer-held inventory sold off a disclosure statement. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C4.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **City tax timing:** City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where in Central Lonsdale do condos actually exist?** On and one block off Lonsdale Avenue between 13th and 23rd Streets. The nine verified buildings in Section 2 run from Georgian Manor (1971) and The Grande (1999) through Anderson Walk (2012) and Centreview (2017) to the 2025–2026 Polygon and Millennium product (Lennox, Elle, Millennium Central Lonsdale) — the corridor is the City's densest condo stock, and the side streets beyond the avenue blocks are GO Zone houses, duplexes and multiplexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- **Q: A directory says my building bans rentals. Can I still rent my unit out long-term?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, whatever the older directory lines at Victoria Park West and Ridge Park Gardens still show. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Ridge Park Gardens really 55+?** The published evidence says yes in the legally valid form: listings describe a 55+ community and state "one owner must be 55+," and the directory records age restrictions. Only a 55-or-older bylaw is valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children — confirm the filed bylaw wording on the Form B. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3025512-2059-chesterfield-avenue-407-north-vancouver-bc-v7m-2p4/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Chesterfield Manor looks like the cheapest condo on the corridor — what is the catch?** Tenure. A current listing states the home is a share ownership requiring 35% down, with a 19+ term — that is corporation-share ownership, not a freehold strata lot, which is why the building is excluded from this file's count. The directory and the listing also conflict on pets (directory: pets not allowed; listing: 1 cat or dog up to 35 lbs allowed). Specialist legal and financing review is required before treating it as interchangeable with a strata purchase. https://www.jimpilkington.com/north-vancouver/central-lonsdale/ and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/chesterfield-manor-1345-ave
- **Q: Is Lonsdale Square a condo building I can buy into?** No. It is a purpose-built rental building (built 2024, 113 units, 6 storeys) on the Harry Jerome Neighbourhood Lands — there is no strata lot to purchase. The same lands hold the new recreation centre (opened July 25, 2026) and further City lands planned for housing in high-rise and ground-oriented forms. https://www.apartments.com/lonsdale-square-north-vancouver-bc/rhkesr2/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- **Q: Which buildings allow pets?** From the published sources in Section 2: Georgian Manor allows pets (2 cats per the listing) and rentals; Victoria Park West, The Grande and Ridge Park Gardens allow pets with restrictions (Ridge Park Gardens: 2 cats or 1 dog and 1 cat per a listing); Anderson Walk allows pets with restrictions and rentals allowed. Centreview, Millennium Central Lonsdale, Lennox and Elle are UNCONFIRMED on pets — confirm on the Form B or disclosure statement. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/anderson-walk-119-west-22-street and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3077672-140-e-20th-street-10-north-vancouver-bc-v7l-3a4/brochure
- **Q: Elle and Lennox are both Polygon — what is the difference in form?** Elle is a 22-storey mixed-use tower with 136 condominiums (38 adaptable) over a retail/office podium at 128 West 15th Street; Lennox is a 6-storey wood-frame mixed-use building with 64 condominiums at 110 East 17th Street. Both were presold with estimated completions in 2025–2026 and current listings state year built 2026. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/elle-by-polygon-homes-north-vancouver-presale-condominiums/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/lennox-on-lonsdale-north-vancouver-presale-condos-by-polygon-homes/
- **Q: What should I check on the older corridor buildings (1971–1999 stock)?** The Form B package: the latest depreciation report, financials, CRF balance and planned work or levies. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with 5+ lots must have a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, and since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund. A current Victoria Park West listing notes the building "has undergone numerous infrastructure upgrades and comes with a depreciation report" — that document, not the listing line, is the check. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/160-west-keith-road/101

SD44 has elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) only, no middle tier. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The hospital, the library and the new recreation centre are all inside this one sub-area.** Lions Gate Hospital at 231 East 15th Street, the City Library at 120 West 14th Street and the Harry Jerome Community Recreation Centre at 130 East 23rd Street (opened July 25, 2026) all sit on the Central Lonsdale grid — but at the recreation centre's opening the aquatics centre was still in final checks, testing, staff training and health-authority approvals, expected ready for programming and lessons in the fall, so a possession-date promise of pool access in summer 2026 would have been wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/central-lonsdale.md)
- **The corridor's cheapest-looking building is not a strata at all.** Chesterfield Manor (1345 Chesterfield Avenue, built 1968) trades as share ownership requiring 35% down per a current listing — the price signal and the tenure are a package, and the building is excluded from this file's condo count for exactly that reason. https://www.jimpilkington.com/north-vancouver/central-lonsdale/
- **Two of the corridor's directory "bans" are legally dead, one age rule is alive.** The "rentals not allowed" lines at Victoria Park West and Ridge Park Gardens cannot bar a long-term tenancy since November 24, 2022 — but Ridge Park Gardens' 55+ rule is the one age bylaw BC law preserves, and it still governs who may occupy the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/ridge-park-gardens-2059-chesterfield-ave
- **The density rewrote itself on December 8, 2025.** City-wide, single-detached dwellings fell from 20% of total units in 2001 to 11% in 2021 while apartments in buildings of five or more storeys rose from 15% to 29% (Statistics Canada 2021, via the City's census backgrounder), and the GO Zone adopted December 8, 2025 then raised permitted units to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties — Central Lonsdale is where that shift is concentrated, and its side-street lots now price on OCP designation, not on the old single-family zoning.
