# Poplar — Condos: Local Notes (Abbotsford)

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

Note on geography: the research file's Poplar is the Paragon sub-area south of Highway 1 around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road (City profile name "South Poplar"). The condo stock that listings place in "Poplar" concentrates at the sub-area's north edge, on King Road beside the University of the Fraser Valley / Rogers Forum district — not in the rural Huntingdon Road south. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/poplar-guide.md, §1)

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Poplar sits south of Highway 1, around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road, west of the Marshall Road / McCallum Road area and east toward Clearbrook Road; exact Paragon boundary lines are not published in text form — boundaries are shown in the City's interactive WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Highway 1 runs along the north edge with interchanges at McCallum Road and Clearbrook Road; King Road and Huntingdon Road run east–west, Gladwin Road and Marshall Road run north–south, and Highway 11 (Sumas Way) to the east connects to Mission and the Huntingdon/Sumas border crossing. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Schools inside the sub-area: South Poplar Traditional Elementary, 32746 Huntingdon Road; Jackson Elementary, 33165 King Road; Abbotsford Traditional School (Grades 6–12), 2272 Windsor Street. https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ and https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- Transit: BC Transit routes 3 (Clearbrook / Huntingdon), 5 (Clearbrook / Bourquin via Hospital, on Marshall Road at the east edge), 6 (Gladwin Road) and 12 (UFV / Bourquin via Jackson Street and McCallum Park and Ride). https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: a single low-rise cluster on King Road at the north edge — the College Park buildings at 33708, 33718, 33728 and 33738 King Road, beside UFV and Rogers Forum. Listings for those addresses place them in Poplar and describe them as walking distance to UFV and the Abbotsford Centre / Rogers Forum. https://realestatecoalharbour.com/houses-for-sale-abbotsford/SearchResults.form/?_pg=11 and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/33718-king-road/412
- The rest of Poplar — the King Road / Windsor Street townhome pockets and the Huntingdon Road rural south — has no verified apartment-condo building; the research file records "few or none verified inside Poplar on an official source; low-rise condo buildings are concentrated north of Highway 1 along South Fraser Way rather than inside Poplar," with the King Road cluster above being the listing-verified exception at the edge. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/poplar-guide.md, §5)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B — Information Certificate (Strata Property Act, s.59).** States the unit's monthly strata fee, approved special levies, the CRF balance, parking/storage and proceedings; since April 1, 2023 it must include a summary of the strata's insurance coverage. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report, renewed at least once every five years; a pre-July 1, 2024 FVRD strata with none had to obtain one by July 1, 2026. For 1990s King Road buildings, the report is where roof, plumbing and boiler timing shows up. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **CRF minimum.** Since November 1, 2023, AGMs must contribute at least 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental bylaws void — directly relevant here.** Building-directory data for College Park (33728 King Road) still records "rentals not allowed," but as of November 24, 2022 every residential rental-restriction bylaw in BC is invalid, with no bylaw amendment needed; short-term rental restrictions remain possible. Do not rely on the directory line. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/college-park-33728-king-road?prompt=login and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2).** Only 55+ age bylaws remain valid; exemptions (May 1, 2023 expansion) cover younger spouses/partners, caregivers and defined children. No King Road building above is recorded as 55+ in any source checked: age restriction UNCONFIRMED for each — check the bylaws filed with the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Abbotsford zoning / OCP.** Zoning Bylaw 2400 (2014); urban lots in Poplar are in the RS family, and City reports state RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 420 m². The governing plan is Abbotsforward 2050, OCP Bylaw 2600-2016 (as updated), with no Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH.** Abbotsford Bylaw 3560-2024 (adopted June 25, 2024) brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into the Zoning Bylaw; the City's SSMUH guide summarizes the updates. SSMUH affects Poplar's house lots (suites/multiplexes), not the existing King Road strata buildings. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf Separately, Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (criteria include 400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage). https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on new construction.** 5% GST on new homes only. FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html Older GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out (task brief figure).
- **PTT.** 1% first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above (+2% on residential value over $3M). First-time buyer exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (max $8,000 saving) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales / REDMA.** Disclosure statement plus a 7-day rescission period (later of signing or receiving the disclosure); deposits in trust; assignment only as the contract allows, usually with developer consent and a fee. Relevant to new product like Kings Crossing townhomes rather than the 1990s King Road resales. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA.** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per person from an RRSP; FHSA $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **CMHC insurance.** Premiums range 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount; required under 20% down on homes $1.5M or less. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Airport note for the wider sub-area:** lots inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area face AZR height limits and NAV CANADA / Transport Canada approvals — a land/development issue, not a King Road condo issue. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Poplar's condos, really?** In one place: the four low-rise King Road buildings at 33708–33738 King Road beside UFV/Rogers Forum, all built 1995–1998. The Huntingdon Road half of Poplar is houses and rural lots. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/college-park-33728-king-road?prompt=login
- **Q: The directory says College Park doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent my unit?** Yes, for long-term rental — that directory line reflects a bylaw type voided province-wide on November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, so check the current bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the King Road buildings?** Listing-stated, building by building: 33708 King Road — 2 pets allowed, cat or dog, no size restrictions; 33718 King Road — 2 dogs of any size allowed; 33738 King Road — cats OK for the unit listed. Confirm against the filed bylaws in the Form B package before relying on any of these. https://realestatecoalharbour.com/houses-for-sale-abbotsford/SearchResults.form/?_pg=11 and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3102284-33718-king-road-304-abbotsford-bc-v2s-8j3/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/33738-king-road/312
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in these buildings?** The Form B states the fee and the strata budget shows coverage; listing amenity lists for the cluster include exercise centre, guest suite and storage, and a 33708 listing notes gas fireplace / hot water items in the building context. The exact inclusions for a specific building are in its budget: any figure not in that budget is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3099862-402-33738-king-road-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2s8j5-424990/brochure
- **Q: Parking and storage?** Listing-stated examples: 33708 units with 2 spaces (1 inside, 1 outside); 33718 top-floor unit with 2 secure underground stalls; 33738 with underground plus visitor parking and a storage locker in the amenity list. Your unit's stall/locker entitlement is what the Form B says — not the listing. Sources as in the pet/parking answers above.
- **Q: These are 1990s buildings — what capital work should I expect?** Read the depreciation report (mandatory, renewed every five years). A 33708 listing already names roofing, plumbing upgrades and central hot water boilers as building maintenance history — exactly the items a depreciation report schedules next. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **Q: What about levies and the CRF?** The Form B shows the CRF balance and any levy already approved; since November 1, 2023 every strata must put at least 10% of its operating fund into the CRF annually, which supports — but does not guarantee — levy readiness. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Insurance — what deductible am I exposed to?** The strata's insurance summary has been a required part of the Form B since April 1, 2023; check the water-damage deductible there and carry unit-owner insurance for deductible assessments. No building-specific deductible is published for these buildings: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Q: How is transit and the student-rental angle?** The buildings sit by UFV and Rogers Forum with BC Transit routes 3, 5, 6 and 12 serving the wider sub-area and Highway 1 interchanges at McCallum and Clearbrook — the reason this cluster exists here and not in south Poplar. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend?** Catchments are by exact address. Locator runs for 32746 Huntingdon Road and 33165 King Road both returned Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary (regular stream); the Traditional pathway is South Poplar Traditional → Abbotsford Traditional School (6–12). Run a King Road address in the locator before relying on it. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "Poplar" condo market and the "Poplar" house market are two different places.** SnapStats recorded Poplar attached inventory of 30 with 3 solds in September 2026 — but that attached figure combines condos and townhomes, and most of Poplar's attached stock is townhomes (Argyle, Kings Crossing) plus the one King Road condo cluster. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/).
- **New supply arriving in Poplar is townhome, not condo:** Kings Crossing, 1641 King Crescent — 20 townhomes by Rockwood Homes Inc., completion expected 2026 (developer source). No new apartment-condo project inside Poplar was verified on a City page. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- **Directory data lags the law.** College Park's "rentals not allowed" directory line survived years after the November 24, 2022 voiding — a live example of why the filed bylaws plus the current Act, not a directory, decide rental questions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Flood context is about the south, not the King Road cluster.** Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system, which the City describes as low-lying and subject to Nooksack River overflow flooding; a lot-level finding requires Zoning Bylaw Schedule E Floodplain Boundaries in the City WebMap. That check matters for Huntingdon Road houses — do not assume it answers anything for a King Road strata lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Abbotsford's 2026 tax penalty dates:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Provincial changes with dates that affect every resale in the cluster:** rental bylaws void Nov 24, 2022; Form B insurance summary Apr 1, 2023; 55+ exemptions expanded May 1, 2023; 10% CRF minimum Nov 1, 2023; depreciation reports on a five-year cycle with the FVRD catch-up deadline July 1, 2026; FTHB GST rebate in law March 13, 2026 (Bill C-4). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
