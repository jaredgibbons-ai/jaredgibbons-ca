## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no verified strata in Steelhead for this to attach to today; the document matters if a strata product ever surfaces here, and it is the first check that a "condo" listing in this sub-area is strata at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Steelhead building is published as age-restricted, because no Steelhead strata building was verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Mission's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026 (live guides stating a June 1, 2026 adoption date are wrong). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020: the rural area is divided into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s inside the Rural Residential designation; a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
- **Secondary dwellings:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. On rural lots, a detached second dwelling generally requires the "s" variant of the parcel's zone, which City material for neighbouring Stave Falls shows owners obtaining by rezoning (e.g., RU16 to RU16s). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3) — the City's property tax page states a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026. This is the CITY regime; the staged 5% + 5% figures quoted for rural Fraser Valley land belong to the Taxation (Rural Area) Act regime for unincorporated areas and do not apply inside Mission. Steelhead is inside the City of Mission. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Wells, septic and ALR:** the feed's own listings show the area's servicing pattern — drilled wells, septic systems and propane heat on acreage parcels. Wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is one structural reason no ALR parcel here can become condo stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Steelhead was verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured homes and pad rent:** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act limits pad-rent increases in manufactured home parks (2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount). Steelhead's manufactured homes in the feed are on owned land, not park pads, so the MHPTA regime does not apply to them — a buyer who wants park-tenure product is looking at a different sub-area and a different tenure entirely. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
