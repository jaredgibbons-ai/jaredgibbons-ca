# Main (Main Street Corridor) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. The Lee Building exclusion in Section 2 shows the limit of this protection: an undivided-interest building has no Form B at all, so the absence of one is itself the first diligence answer. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — all three verified corridor buildings are above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The Independent's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law — and any directory line anywhere in this file saying rentals are not allowed would be void for long-term tenancies regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. As the research file states it: the residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3–4 units, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6–8 units; a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Main Street commercial frontage itself is UNVERIFIED in the research file. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Area plan — the Broadway Plan:** the Plan covers the corridor's north end (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues), was approved June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for buildings up to 20–30 storeys near the future Mount Pleasant Station, with renter protections and tenant relocation requirements that matter on any older rental-building purchase. The Plan is policy, not zoning — most sites still need rezoning. South of 16th Avenue the corridor runs on citywide direction from the Vancouver Official Development Plan (in force March 31, 2026). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Flood:** the corridor's north end touches the former False Creek shoreline lowland, where the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain and parcels carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements; the residential grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** the City requires every owner to submit an annual property status declaration; properties declared or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026 — a live holding cost for investors in the corridor's older stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City tax timing:** Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all three verified corridor buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for future Broadway Plan presales around Mount Pleasant Station; no specific current corridor presale was verified on a developer source in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Note the interaction with Section 2's trap: CMHC insurance presupposes a financeable title — an undivided-interest unit like the Lee Building's cannot be financed at all on the listings' own wording. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Main a Vancouver West or Vancouver East area?** East, on the research file's geography: the corridor runs through Mount Pleasant, Riley Park and Sunset, and Main Street is the dividing line between the board's Mount Pleasant VE and VW sub-areas. Listings for the corridor's own buildings (The Independent, Cartier Place) are filed under Mount Pleasant VE / Vancouver East. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Q: What condo stock does the corridor actually have?** Three verified buildings in this pass: The Independent (258 condos, 21-storey concrete tower, East 10th at Main), Cartier Place (low-rise, 1991, 3131 Main Street) and 3333 Main Street (low-rise, 2011/2012, 90 properties per a building source). Everything else on the corridor is houses, walk-up rentals, or the undivided-interest Lee Building. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: The Lee Building looks cheap for its size and location — what is the catch?** Tenure. Listings state it is undivided interest under a tenants-in-common agreement, that it cannot be financed (cash purchase only), and that taxes are bundled into the monthly fee rather than paid on a strata lot. There is no strata corporation, no Form B and no CMHC-insured mortgage route. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3119836-506-175-e-broadway-vancouver-british-columbia-v5t1w2-399000/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a corridor condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and The Independent's directory entry states rentals allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to a unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: The Independent — pets allowed per directory, cats OK in listing pet fields; Cartier Place — cats only, no dogs, per current listing copy and pet field. 3333 Main Street's pet rules: UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws remain enforceable, so read the registered bylaws for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-independent-at-main-285-east-10-ave and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3131-main-street/108
- **Q: The Independent — built 2017 or 2019?** Sources conflict: a building directory says 2017; current listings say 2019. Section 2 flags both; the strata plan / occupancy record is the definitive check. The same caution applies at 3333 Main Street (2011 vs 2012 across listings). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-independent-at-main-285-east-10-ave and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/285-east-10th-avenue/305
- **Q: When does the SkyTrain reach Main Street, and what does the Broadway Plan allow near it?** Mount Pleasant Station at Main and Broadway is one of six underground stations on the Broadway Subway, scheduled to enter service in fall 2027; the Broadway Plan provides for 20–30 storeys near the station — as policy, with most sites still requiring rezoning, and with renter protections that affect older rental buildings in the plan area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Q: What should I check in Cartier Place, the corridor's 1991 low-rise?** The depreciation report: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years (no deferral vote since July 1, 2024) and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Listings note recent building upgrades — ask what remains in the report, not just what is done. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve a corridor condo?** SD39 catchments are address-based, with no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12), and the corridor crosses at least three catchments. Elementaries located in the local areas it runs through: Livingstone (315 East 23rd Avenue) and Wolfe (4251 Ontario Street) in Riley Park, and Queen Alexandra (1300 East Broadway) at the north-east edge; Vancouver Technical Secondary (2600 East Broadway) is the large comprehensive serving this part of the east side. Run the exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Q: Is there a median condo price for the Main corridor I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used. Price from the specific building's recent sales — and never from Lee Building sales, which price a different tenure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor's most famous "condo" building cannot be mortgaged.** The Lee Building (1912) sells as undivided interest under a tenants-in-common agreement dating to late 1993 — its listings say "cannot be financed" in plain words — so its prices are not comparables for strata product at The Independent two blocks away. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2949237-175-e-broadway-706-vancouver-bc-v5t-1w2/brochure
- **The Independent carries two addresses.** It is marketed as 285 East 10th Avenue and also addressed 2508 Watson Street — documents, parking and storage records may use either, so match by strata lot, not by address alone. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-independent-at-main/
- **"Main" is three products wearing one name.** The Broadway / Kingsway station blocks, the South Main retail blocks around Cartier Place and 3333 Main, and the quieter Riley Park side streets south of King Edward price off different drivers — Broadway Plan expectations at the north end, R1-1 multiplex math on the side streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **The corridor's civic anchor sits exactly on the corridor.** The Mount Pleasant Community Centre and library at 1 Kingsway (opened December 2009, roughly 30,000 sq ft, LEED Gold, co-located with rental housing) is where Kingsway meets Main — the amenity base for The Independent and the north-end low-rises. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
- **Cartier Place's pet rule is cats-only, in the listing's own words.** "Cats only, sorry no dogs!" — a bylaw-style restriction that survives the 2022 rental-bylaw change, because pet bylaws were never voided. A dog owner should treat Cartier Place as a no until the registered bylaws say otherwise. https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/108-3131-main-street-vancouver-british-columbia-v5t-3g8-30289230/
- **The north end's flood question is real and the south end's is not.** The designated floodplain reaches the corridor only at the former False Creek shoreline lowland by Terminal Avenue; the grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope. A ground-floor unit's diligence differs by which end of Main it is on — parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/main.md)
