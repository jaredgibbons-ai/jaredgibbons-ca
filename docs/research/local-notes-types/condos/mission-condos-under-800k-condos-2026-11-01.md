## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Mission building's registered bylaws were read for this file — pet, rental and fee claims are verified in the strata documents, not the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. In the roster this lands hardest on the oldest buildings — Glenkirk Court (1975), Mission Manor (1992), Briskham Manor (1993), Stave Lake Landing (1997). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" (Stave Lake Landing's and Cherry Lane's directory lines are the roster examples) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and Glenkirk Court's listing-stated "No short term rentals" line is the kind of restriction that can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Directly relevant to Briskham Manor, recorded in the source files as a 55+ complex — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Mission plan, zoning and taxes — corrected figures applied:** OCP **Bylaw 6405-2026** (adopted April 21, 2026); Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, with the City's SSMUH page stating that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted. **Property-tax penalty: FLAT 10%** on unpaid taxes after July 2 — Mission's 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw) — never 5%+5% for the City of Mission (5%+5% is the separate FVRD rural regime). The City's tax-rates page also states additional taxes are levied for diking on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — an address-level check for any low-lying unit, not a building fact for the roster. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/mission-condos-under-800k.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (developer sales at Montgomery; any remaining developer sales at Silver & Cedar), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase in this band pays in the 1% and 2% tiers only. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — **because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling**, subject to the program's personal and property conditions. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Montgomery's developer contracts. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
