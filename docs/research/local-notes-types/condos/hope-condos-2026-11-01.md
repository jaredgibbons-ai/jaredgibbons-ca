## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Hope is a district municipality in the Fraser Valley Regional District at the confluence of the Fraser and Coquihalla rivers, at the eastern end of the Fraser Valley where the Fraser Canyon begins. Its named areas are the townsite grid (Wallace Street downtown spine, Park Street, 7th Avenue), Silver Creek (west), Kawkawa Lake (east), and Flood/Laidlaw (west fringe, where most manufactured-home parks line Flood-Hope Road). https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw and https://hopebc.ca/neighborhoods-of-hope-bc/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- Where condo stock concentrates: it barely exists to concentrate. Hope's strata stock is three named complexes — one form-conflicted low-rise on Scott Drive and two townhouse complexes on Douglas Street and 7th Avenue (Section 2) — inside a housing market the District's own numbers describe as mostly single-family: permits from 2021 to October 2024 were mostly single-family dwellings (70 units) plus suites (22). The townsite core around Wallace Street holds what apartment and townhouse product exists; Silver Creek is rancher-style family homes and Kawkawa Lake is recreational properties. https://hope.ca/Home/DownloadDocument?docId=e153cf66-dc40-4da9-be75-4cc2789bafe3 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- Market figures: **Hope is NOT covered by SnapStats.** No Hope row exists in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (its communities stop at Mission), CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only, and Hope is its own municipality — not a sub-area of Mission or Chilliwack in any reporting found. All Hope market figures — inventory, solds, sales ratio, median price, days on market — are UNVERIFIED from that source, and no substitute community is used; Chilliwack-board figures do not apply. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- Population context (2021 Census): 6,686 people (+8.2% from 6,181 in 2016), 3,243 total private dwellings, 2,939 occupied by usual residents, median age 54.8 — an older-than-average town, which is why its rental and care ladder (Section 2 traps) is as large as it is relative to its size. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/page.cfm?topic=10&dguid=2021A00055909009&lang=E (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- Schools (SD78 Fraser-Cascade): Coquihalla Elementary (K–6, 455 6th Ave, ~350 students) takes the old Town of Hope, east to Sumallo Lodge including Kawkawa Lake/Othello, west to Ruby Creek, plus Spuzzum and Yale; Silver Creek Elementary (K–7) takes Silver Creek and west of it; Hope Secondary School (Gr. 7–12, 444 Stuart St) takes all Grade 7–12 students west to Ruby Creek and up the Fraser Canyon to China Bar — one secondary school for the whole area. Two Rivers Education Centre alternate (Gr. 8–12) is at 425 Park St. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/078 and https://sd78.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/PDC-Meeting-Agenda-Package-2021-04-20.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- Services and transit: Fraser Canyon Hospital, 1275 7th Ave, is a 10-bed Fraser Health community hospital with 24/7 emergency stabilization and care, transferring complex cases to Abbotsford Regional Hospital or Royal Columbian Hospital. The Hope & Area Recreation Centre (1005 6th Ave — arena, Dan Sharrers Aquatic Centre, fitness facilities) is managed by the FVRD. Transit is thin: BC Transit Route 72 connects Hope and Agassiz via Highway 7, with onward connections to Chilliwack via Route 71; the FVRD administers the routes and service is a few trips daily. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Hope/fraser-canyon-hospital and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/transit.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Timberlane, the Form B and strata plan are the documents that settle the form question the listings leave open (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children under Strata Property Regulation 7.01. No Hope strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — the town's 55+ rule lives in a rental building's admission policy (Stalo), not in any strata. Manufactured-home parks are a different legal world: a park's age rule is a park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework (Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, B.C. Reg. 481/2003, referencing Human Rights Code s. 10(2)(b)(i)), which is why Lismore's and Windmill's unstated ages stay UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **District plans and zoning (District sources only):** the current Official Community Plan is **OCP Bylaw 1617, 2025, adopted February 9, 2026**, updated together with the Zoning Bylaw to meet current provincial housing requirements. The governing zoning bylaw is **District of Hope Zoning Bylaw No. 1324, 2012** (consolidated to March 20, 2025); its amendment list includes Bylaw 1580, 2024 (Small Scale Multi-Unit Housing, RS-1 zone, adopted June 10, 2024). https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw and https://hope.ca/uploads/dm/86919/Zoning_Bylaw_No._1324_2012_Consolidated_March_20_2025 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/hope-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Suite / SSMUH rules:** the District's Interim Housing Needs Report (Nov 2024) records that the 2024 SSMUH amendments allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones, against a calculated 20-year housing need of 2,123 units. https://hope.ca/Home/DownloadDocument?docId=e153cf66-dc40-4da9-be75-4cc2789bafe3 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- **Short-term rentals:** Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 1603, 2025 (adopted September 8, 2025) made short-term rentals a permitted use in designated zones in Hope, and Council voted to opt in to the provincial registry and require a District business licence as of January 1, 2026 — stated here from a secondary summary of the District's bylaws and minutes; confirm the current District guide before relying on fee figures. https://www.bnbcalc.com/blog/short-term-rental-regulation/Hope-Canada-Guide (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/hope-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Flood rules are a building rule here, not background:** the Fraser River's design flood profile is anchored to the Hope gauging station (the 1894 flood, treated as roughly a 500-year event), and new provincial floodplain maps covering the Coquihalla and Fraser rivers from Yale to Mission were released in June 2026. Construction in hazard areas needs a Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit — Council (Jan. 13, 2025) approved one for an addition conditional on a satisfactory qualified-professional report — and the District administers the Coquihalla River dikes (River Parade Flood Protection, 0.93 km; Glenhalla Subdivision, 1.01 km). Parcel-level flood construction levels: UNVERIFIED. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)

- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $500,000 (**illustration only — no Hope median exists, because SnapStats does not cover the District**), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no Hope presale was confirmed in this program (the two named District applications in public-hearing records are rezonings to Light/Service Industrial, not housing); where a unit anywhere is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)

- **Q: How do the seniors manufactured-home parks work — do I own anything?** You own the home and rent the pad. - **Q: Is Hope in a floodplain?** Parts of it are, and Hope is literally the reference point: the Fraser River's design flood profile is anchored to the Hope gauging station (the 1894 flood, treated as roughly a 500-year event), and new provincial floodplain maps for this reach were released in June 2026. Check the parcel against the maps and expect a Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit, with a qualified-professional report, for construction in hazard areas — a dike nearby does not remove the permit step. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715
- **Q: Can I rent out a Hope strata unit, or run it short-term?** Long-term: no strata in BC can ban rentals anymore (rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022). Short-term in Hope specifically: Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 1603, 2025 permits STRs in designated zones, with provincial registration and a District business licence required from January 1, 2026 (per a secondary summary — confirm the District guide). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What schools are in Hope?** Coquihalla Elementary (K–6, ~350 students), Silver Creek Elementary (K–7), and Hope Secondary (Gr. 7–12), all SD78 (Fraser-Cascade), plus the Two Rivers alternate program (Gr. 8–12). Coquihalla covers the townsite, Kawkawa Lake and east to Sumallo Lodge (plus Yale/Spuzzum); Silver Creek covers Silver Creek and west of it; all Hope-area students feed Hope Secondary for senior grades. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/078
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $500,000 fair market value (illustration only; no Hope median is published), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $8,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: If I need help later, can I stay in Hope?** The in-town ladder is unusually complete for a town of 6,686: independent non-profit rental with services (Park Street Manor, 65+), assisted living (Riverside Manor's 12 publicly subsidized assisted living suites, plus its 18 independent suites), then long-term care (Fraser Hope Lodge, 49 complex care beds + 1 respite) and hospice on the same 7th Avenue campus — all rental or care, none of it purchasable. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-At-Location/2/F/assisted-living-at-riverside-manor and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/A/9/fraser-hope-lodge

- **Hope got a brand-new OCP in 2026.** Official Community Plan Bylaw 1617, 2025 was adopted February 9, 2026, with a companion zoning update — and the District's housing-needs math behind it calculates a 20-year need of 2,123 units, mostly from anticipated growth, in a town that built 70 single-family homes and 22 suites in 2021–Oct 2024. https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw and https://hope.ca/Home/DownloadDocument?docId=e153cf66-dc40-4da9-be75-4cc2789bafe3
