## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Note the standing exception in Section 2: an undivided-interest building such as The Lee Building has no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" in a downtown building is void for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and feed-card claims that Airbnb is permitted in a building are UNVERIFIED listing copy in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No downtown building cross-referenced in Section 2 is published as age-restricted in its home file — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses; CD-1 and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Empty Homes Tax (Vancouver):** the Empty Homes Tax applies in the City of Vancouver — every owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and properties declared, determined or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. The Province's Speculation and Vacancy Tax is separate and in addition. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/evidence-exemptions.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.md)
- **Additional school tax:** the Province's additional school tax can apply at downtown price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given in this file because no Vancouver median is published in the permitted stats source — an example built on a feed asking price would present listing copy as a market figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a downtown unit is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
