(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)

### Not a condo — the one named attached property in the sub-area

- **Val Maria — 21163 Lougheed Highway.** A 55+ age-restricted manufactured home park. It is NOT a condo and NOT a strata: listings for units in the park record the sub-type as Mobile Home / Manufactured Home and the ownership as "Leasehold not prepaid-NonStrata" — the buyer owns the home and rents the pad, and listing pages for the park are classified Southwest Maple Ridge. Do not count it as condo stock, and do not apply strata rules (Form B, depreciation reports, CRF) to it — none of them exist for a non-strata leasehold park. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3098427-21163-lougheed-highway-52-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-2r4/brochure

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

With no verified strata condo stock in the sub-area, this section records the rules that will govern any strata purchase here (including any future Hammond density) and the land rules that shape the area.

- **Form B and strata records:** on any resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This matters if any of the sub-area's unverified attached listings turn out to be small stratas whose old bylaws still show a rental ban — that line is outdated law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw in a strata; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Note the limit of this rule here: Val Maria's 55+ restriction is a manufactured-home park's tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw, and it is not governed by these sections. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research file (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. The Hammond Area Plan (OCP s. 10.5) is the local plan, and a Hammond Area Plan update enables mixed-use, higher-density buildings — the route by which real condo stock could appear here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Floodplain and escarpment rules:** the sub-area's river edge carries a flood construction level of 6.7 m geodetic and secondary suites are prohibited on floodplain properties; escarpment rules require a geotechnical covenant within 300 m of the crest and a detailed seismic report within 100 m, septic systems are to be avoided, and sewers are required for new residences. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Manufactured homes on rented pads (Val Maria) are a different financing category from strata condos — a lender will treat the leasehold, non-strata structure differently, and the CMHC condo premium table should not be assumed to apply the same way. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Hammond or Port Haney?** None verified. No named apartment-condo building in Southwest Maple Ridge could be confirmed from official, directory or listing sources; the sub-area's housing is detached homes, and its one named attached property (Val Maria) is a manufactured home park. SnapStats counted 8 attached listings in September 2026, but no public source tied them to named condo complexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Q: Is Val Maria a condo? Can I get a normal condo mortgage on it?** It is not a condo and not a strata — listings record ownership as "Leasehold not prepaid-NonStrata" and the form as a manufactured home in a 55+ park, meaning the home is owned and the pad is rented. There is no strata, no Form B, no depreciation report and no CRF; financing and resale work like a manufactured-home purchase, not a strata purchase. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3098427-21163-lougheed-highway-52-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-2r4/brochure
- **Q: Does the 55+ rule at Val Maria work like a strata age bylaw?** No. The Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 age-bylaw framework (including its exemptions) applies to strata corporations. Val Maria's 55+ restriction is a park tenancy rule under a leasehold, non-strata structure — ask the park for its own rules document rather than relying on strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Will condos ever be built in Hammond?** The planning door is open: a Hammond Area Plan update enables mixed-use, higher-density buildings, and current listings already show SSMUH-era infill being marketed in Hammond (a permit-ready six-plex listing at the Hammond entry off Lougheed Highway). No condo project in the sub-area was verified as built or selling in the sources checked — anything beyond the plan language is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md) and https://vancouvernashdom.com/maple-ridge-southwest-maple-ridge-houses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2
- **Q: How flood-exposed is this sub-area?** The river edge is floodplain: the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic, and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties. Exposure is parcel-specific along the low Hammond flats — check the specific lot against the City's floodplain mapping rather than the sub-area label. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Q: What is the commute/transit picture?** West Coast Express stations bracket the sub-area — Maple Meadows Station (20010 Dunn Avenue) west and Port Haney Station east — and the Golden Ears Bridge ties Hammond to Langley. There is no rapid transit in the sub-area itself. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **Q: If I do find a small strata here, can it ban rentals?** No — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC; only short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What will property taxes cost me in penalties if I miss the deadline?** Maple Ridge taxes are due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026. Strata buyers should also confirm whether the seller's tax account is current at completion. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hammond is one of Maple Ridge's oldest settlements.** It began as Port Hammond Junction, on CPR station land donated by the Hammond brothers — the heritage building stock around the Hammond core follows from that railway origin. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **The bridge corridor is slated to widen.** The Golden Ears Way expansion from 2 to 4 lanes is in design (City source) — the sub-area's main road link to Langley is a known future construction corridor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **A new aquatics/recreation centre is a live question, not a done deal.** Proposed Hammond Aquatics/Recreation Centre and Multi-Use Community Park assent-vote bylaws (Bylaws 8123-2026 and 8124-2026) were put to voters; the outcome is UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked — do not price a home here on the facility being built. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
- **The "condos" in Southwest search results are mostly not condos.** With zero verified apartment buildings and one manufactured-home park, attached listings marketed under this sub-area need form-by-form checking — manufactured homes, duplexes and houses with suites all surface under broad attached searches. Sources: Section 2 and the research file.
- **Septic and slope rules quietly shape lots here.** Escarpment-area rules call for a geotechnical covenant within 300 m of the crest and a detailed seismic report within 100 m, septic systems are to be avoided, and sewers are required for new residences — costs a buyer of an older Hammond property can inherit on a rebuild. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md)
