## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The Township plan stack, cited exactly as verified:** the Official Community Plan is given effect through Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842 as amended; in 2025 the Township completed an OCP update to align with housing-related amendments to the Local Government Act, and on December 1, 2025 Council adopted the 2025 Official Community Plan Update Bylaw No. 6200 (as amended). The December 2025 update set included the Aldergrove, Murrayville, Fort Langley, Walnut Grove and Northwest Langley community plans and the Yorkson, Smith and Williams Neighbourhood Plans. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/townshipoflangleyplans.md)
- **Willoughby Community Plan — origin and current schedule, per standing correction:** the Willoughby Community Plan originates as Bylaw 1836 (1979) and now sits as Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). The Township's 2026 update package proposes a new Willoughby Community Plan (Bylaw No. 6201), a Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan (Bylaw No. 6202, consolidating the Carvolth, Latimer, Jericho, Routley and Southwest Gordon Estate neighbourhood plans) and a Gordon Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw No. 6203, consolidating the Central Gordon Estate and Northeast Gordon Estate neighbourhood plans), plus a new transit-oriented-development Development Permit Area. Status, exactly as the Township records it: Council gave first and second reading to Bylaw Nos. 6201, 6202 and 6203 on May 25, 2026 and directed staff to hold a Public Hearing on June 15, 2026 — **first/second reading only; none of the three is recorded as adopted on any Township source opened, and none is cited as adopted anywhere in this file.** https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Murrayville Community Plan (verified at bylaw level):** Bylaw 1988 No. 2661, adopted April 17, 1989 as an amendment to the Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842, since amended including by the 2025 OCP Update (Bylaw No. 6200, December 1, 2025); the plan describes Murrayville on both sides of the Fraser Highway immediately east of the City of Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/townshipoflangleyplans.md)
- **Brookswood-Fernridge / Rinn (verified at bylaw level via search result in the research file):** the Rinn Neighbourhood Plan is Bylaw No. 6010 within the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan (Bylaw 2017 No. 5300), adopted June 10, 2024, with a Rinn Update 2026 (Bylaw No. 6219) recorded May 25, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Neighbourhood-Plan-Bylaws/Rinn-Neighbourhood-Plan-(No.-6010).pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/townshipoflangleyplans.md)
- **Zoning and SSMUH (Township):** zoning is under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 as amended. The SSMUH amendments are Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on eligible lots at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m², with NO 6-plex tier — and on April 27, 2026 Council adopted Schedule 4 of the OCP implementing a Development Permit Area with form and character guidelines for SSMUH. The Township suite cap is 120 m². SSMUH eligibility turns on Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit-Oriented Area, and ≤4,050 m². The Township's one Transit-Oriented Area is on 196 Street in Willowbrook for the proposed Surrey-Langley SkyTrain station, designated by provincial Order-in-Council on May 2, 2025, with minimum allowable densities of 8–20 storeys within 800 m depending on proximity. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/townshipoflangleyplans.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/townshipoflangleyplans.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A plan designation changes none of this: a Township condo is bought on its Form B, not on its plan tile. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Township building is recorded as 55+ in this plans file — any age claim is per-building, on its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract purchase arises on Township land, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A plan designation is not a disclosure statement and confers no presale rights. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
