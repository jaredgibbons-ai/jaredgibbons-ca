## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The residential rules below are the BC standard block. In this district they have no residential building to attach to today; they are recorded so the file is complete, and so a reader who finds a "Lake City condo" claim elsewhere can test it. The district-specific rules that actually bite — zoning, plan hierarchy, flood — follow the standard block.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. (Industrial strata purchases use the same certificate machinery, with commercial content.) https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term residential rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No Lake City directory line was found to flag — there is no residential directory stock here — but the rule is stated so it is not misapplied to an industrial unit's lease terms, which are a contract question, not this bylaw question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with the exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion. No residential building in this district exists to carry one: not applicable on the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — the rule that actually governs this district:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is repealed and must never be cited as current. Properties previously zoned under repealed districts were reassigned to the closest comparable new district, and the City states the reassignment approach for employment and industrial lands follows the Burnaby 2050 OCP and Metro Vancouver designations. The specific new district and permitted-use list for an individual Lake City parcel: UNVERIFIED — confirm in writing with the Burnaby Planning and Building Department. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/lake-city-industrial.md)
- **Plan hierarchy:** the Lake City Community Plan (adopted by Council in 2000) designates Lake City as a mixed-use area serving land uses related to the commercial/office and industrial/business centre frameworks, but the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — read the 2000 Lake City map under the 2050 OCP. The 2050 policy directions aim to protect existing industrial land, intensify employment lands and use them efficiently. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/lake-city-industrial.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Commercial/industrial class rates for a specific Lake City property: UNVERIFIED here — read them off the property's own tax notice. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/lake-city-industrial.md)
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales; commercial/industrial purchases carry their own GST treatment, which is outside this residential file's scope — take it from the purchaser's accountant, not from a condo guide. The residential rebates are recorded for completeness: the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. The residential exemptions do not convert an industrial purchase into a residential one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No residential presale project in this district was verified: UNCONFIRMED, and none is expected under the current designations. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year — residential first-home programs, recorded for completeness; they do not apply to an industrial purchase. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a residential insured-mortgage product; commercial financing is a different market. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo in Lake City Industrial?** No residential condo stock was verified in the district on any source checked — the count is zero. A listing marketed as a "Lake City condo" should be checked for what it actually is: most likely an industrial strata unit or a building in a neighbouring residential sub-area using the station name loosely. - **Q: Is an industrial strata unit the same as a condo?** No. Both are strata lots under the Strata Property Act, but an industrial unit's value turns on zoning, permitted use, ceiling height, loading and lease terms — not on the residential considerations (schools, residential rental rules, home buyer programs) in a condo file. This file expressly excludes strata industrial units from its condo count.
- **Q: Why are there no Lake City sales numbers?** Because SnapStats publishes no Lake City sub-area row for Burnaby in its September 2026 report — there is no sub-area inventory, solds or sales ratio to report.
