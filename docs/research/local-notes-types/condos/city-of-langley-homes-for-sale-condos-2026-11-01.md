## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the City's 1988–1990 stock (all three roster buildings) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in the City may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of both Parkway directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. Langley City short-term rental rules specifically: UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/city-of-langley-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. All three roster buildings and Huntsfield Green are published as 55+ communities — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan was adopted November 22, 2021; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 — cite the number; its adoption date is conflicted in sources, so the adoption date is UNVERIFIED and no date is stated in this file. Under Bylaw No. 3300 a secondary suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building, the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4), at least one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry; a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/city-of-langley-homes-for-sale.md) and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2
- **SSMUH:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. A first-page feed card markets a lot as having "fourplex potential" with "R1 zoning permits up to four units" — listing text only; check the specific lot against Bylaw No. 3284. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/city-of-langley-homes-for-sale.md) and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **City of Langley property taxes:** payments received after the July 2, 2026 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. The penalty percentage for 2026: UNVERIFIED — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/city-of-langley-homes-for-sale.md) and https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 secondary date is superseded). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase in the City. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
