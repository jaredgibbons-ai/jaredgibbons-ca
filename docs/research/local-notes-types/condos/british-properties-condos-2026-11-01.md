## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For a new building like Hawksley, the equivalent package is the developer's disclosure statement plus the strata's first budgets and warranty documents — read both, not the marketing summary. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets on a West Vancouver building is old bylaw text, not current law. No such line is published for Hawksley, Aerie II or Highgrove in the sources read — their restrictions are UNCONFIRMED, not banned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. Houses in the original subdivision sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones — Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses — while the Rodgers Creek lands above the subdivision sit in site-specific Comprehensive Development zones (CD3). The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **British Properties covenants sit on top of zoning:** British Properties subdivisions carry restrictive covenants registered at the Land Title Office — under them, an owner must file an application for approval of plans with British Pacific Properties Limited before building a new home, extending or altering an existing building, or erecting a fence or any structure; the covenants differ by subdivision, bind every subsequent owner, and District zoning and building bylaws apply in addition. Whether a specific strata lot in the Uplands or Whitby Estates carries a covenant step is a title-by-title check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **Hazard permits:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank — the creeks crossing this hillside — and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. Both are live checks for Uplands product at the forest edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — a live cost on Hawksley, which current listings market as new product (one current listing states "NO GST" for that unit; treat any such line as unit- and offer-specific and confirm in the contract, not as a building-wide rule). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.zolo.ca/west-vancouver-real-estate/3963-uplands-way/403
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. This is the governing frame for the Rodgers Creek Area 5 and Cypress Village homes when they reach the market. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C4.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **West Vancouver tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. At British Properties values the Province's additional school tax commonly applies — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
