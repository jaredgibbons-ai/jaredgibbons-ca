# West Newton, Surrey — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Newton is the Newton land west of King George Boulevard toward the Delta border (Scott Road / 120 Street side), including the Highway 99 industrial area at the south; the City's West Newton plan documents govern the exact lines. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- Two RapidBus corridors bracket the area: the R1 on King George Boulevard (Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange, via Surrey Central) on the east edge, and the R6 Scott Road RapidBus on the west edge between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: almost all of it is one tight cluster on 72 Avenue around 126–128 Street, directly across from Kwantlen Polytechnic University's Surrey campus and a short walk east to Newton Exchange — College Park, Newton Court and The Savoy are neighbouring addresses on the same block. The second pocket is the south end at 128 Street and 64 Avenue, beside Tamanawis Park, where the Harth presale is planned. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- There are no condo towers in West Newton; the verified apartment stock is 1990s low-rise, and the Highway 99 portion of the plan area is industrial, not housing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- The Newton Town Centre at King George Boulevard and 72 Avenue is the City's designated town centre serving the area, at the sub-area's east edge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- SD36 schools named in the research file: T.E. Scott Elementary (7079 148 St address as listed in the research file — note the plan-area text places it inside West Newton), Bear Creek Elementary (13780 80 Ave) at the north edge, and Frank Hurt Secondary (13940 77 Ave) east of King George Boulevard; a locator run for 7079 148 St returned T.E. Scott Elementary and Frank Hurt Secondary. Assignment is by address through the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, any amount the owner owes, any approved special levy still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with a 30-year repair/replacement projection and at least three CRF cash-flow funding models; it can no longer be deferred by a vote, and stratas of four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in West Newton, where the resale stock is 1993–1996 wood-frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. Building pages for College Park / Newton Court-era stock that still show rental restrictions are showing pre-2022 wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse/partner, and children in defined care cases. No West Newton building in Section 2 is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs, with R1–R6 residential zones in the neighbourhood portion of West Newton and industrial zones in the Highway 99 industrial area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **OCP: the current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, which received final adoption on June 15, 2026.** The old PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is not current — do not cite it as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- Within West Newton specifically, the City states the West Newton South plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect; the Newton Town Centre Plan still covers the King George Boulevard / 72 Avenue core. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **SSMUH:** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, plus one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code. This affects the house streets around the condos, not existing strata buildings. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026, creating the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate: 100% of the GST on a new home valued up to $1 million, phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million, maximum $50,000, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. This is the rebate that applies to a Harth presale for an eligible first-time buyer. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** Any buyer of a new home may claim 36% of the GST back, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, with no rebate at $450,000 or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program eliminates PTT on the first $500,000 of value (maximum saving $8,000) for homes at $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption to $860,000; the separate Newly Built Home Exemption gives full PTT relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://auxiliummortgage.com/blog/bc-first-time-homebuyers-2026-federal-provincial-programs-incentives/
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** A presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement and has a 7-day right of rescission from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of that statement; deposits are held in trust or covered by deposit protection insurance. Assignment rights, if any, are whatever the Harth purchase contract and disclosure statement say — there is no general right to assign. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** The RRSP Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per buyer, repayable over 15 years; the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and the two can be combined for the same home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC insurance premium range.** CMHC's published homeowner schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% at that band with a non-traditional down payment; it is a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why does everything seem to be on one block of 72 Avenue?** Because that is where the stock is: College Park (12633), Newton Court (12733) and The Savoy (12739) are neighbouring 1990s low-rise buildings across from Kwantlen Polytechnic University, within a walk of Newton Exchange. Outside that cluster and the Harth presale site at 64 Avenue, West Newton is houses and townhouses. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/31122/newton-court/surrey/west-newton/12733-72-ave
- **Q: Can I rent my unit to a KPU student?** Long-term rental bans are void in BC since Nov 24, 2022, so a strata cannot stop a long-term tenancy — but it can still restrict short-term rentals, and the tenancy itself must comply with the building's other bylaws (pets, noise, occupancy). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: The building page says "rentals allowed with restrictions" — what does that mean now?** For Newton Court that wording is published by a building directory, but the restriction it refers to has had no legal effect since Nov 24, 2022. Read the current registered bylaws and the Form B, not the directory page. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/newton-court-12733-72-avenue
- **Q: What should I worry about in a 1993–1996 wood-frame building?** The next big-ticket items in the depreciation report — roof, building envelope, balconies, plumbing — and whether the CRF and its funding models cover them without a levy. Since July 1, 2024 the report is mandatory every five years for stratas with five or more lots, so a current one must exist; if the seller cannot produce it, that is information in itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What do the strata fees cover, and are parking and storage included?** The Form B states the unit's monthly fee, its parking and storage allocations, and the CRF balance, and must be provided within 7 days of a request. Listings in the Savoy cluster describe underground parking and lockers, but the allocation for the specific unit is only settled on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** A building directory records Newton Court as pets allowed with restrictions, and a Savoy listing records pets allowed for that building. The size/number limits live in the registered bylaws — confirm them before subjects if the pet is non-negotiable. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/newton-court-12733-72-avenue and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3090093-403-12739-72-avenue-surrey-bc-v3w-2m7
- **Q: If I buy the Harth presale, when can I change my mind?** Within 7 days of the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging receipt of the developer's disclosure statement, under REDMA, with the deposit refunded. After that window, your rights are the contract's, not the statute's. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Does a presale save me GST and PTT?** Possibly both, if you qualify: an eligible first-time buyer gets 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million under the Bill C-4 rebate (maximum $50,000), and a new principal-residence home gets full PTT relief up to $1,100,000 under the Newly Built Home Exemption. Both are eligibility-tested — confirm with your lawyer/notary and accountant before relying on either. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://auxiliummortgage.com/blog/bc-first-time-homebuyers-2026-federal-provincial-programs-incentives/
- **Q: How is the commute without a SkyTrain station in the area?** Two RapidBus routes do the heavy lifting — R1 east to Surrey Central Station and R6 west/north to Scott Road Station, both Expo Line connections — plus routes 323 and 364 through the area streets. Door-to-door times: UNCONFIRMED, no official source states them. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- **Q: Is the south end, near Highway 99, a condo area?** No — the City plan identifies the southern part of the West Newton plan area as the Highway 99 industrial area. The residential condo stock is at the north/central 72 Avenue cluster and the 64 Avenue presale site. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West Newton South no longer exists as a plan.** The City states that plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) and is no longer in effect — land-use claims for south West Newton now run through the OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **The only new condo supply is one presale at the area's south edge.** Harth at Tamanawis Park (Almas Group, 128 St & 64 Ave) is marketed as a mid-rise collection beside Tamanawis Park and Tamanawis Secondary — a different location and product from the 1990s 72 Avenue cluster, and its unit count and completion date are UNCONFIRMED on the developer page checked. https://almasgroup.ca/harth-presale-condos-in-newton-bc/
- **The KPU effect is the cluster's whole story.** Newton Court is marketed on being across from Kwantlen Polytechnic University, and the Savoy listings make the same point — the 72 Avenue buildings trade on student, staff and transit demand at Newton Exchange, not on a town-centre retail strip of their own. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/31122/newton-court/surrey/west-newton/12733-72-ave
- **No SkyTrain is coming to Newton on the funded line.** The project under construction is the 16 km Surrey–Langley (Dave Barrett) Extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to Langley City Centre, expected in service in late 2029; West Newton's rapid transit is, and remains on any funded timeline, the R1 and R6 RapidBus routes. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- **The south end drops into floodplain country.** The southern part of West Newton falls toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands, the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton — a lot-by-lot check for any property at that edge, and a reason the condo stock sits well north of it. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
