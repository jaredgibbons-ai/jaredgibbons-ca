Apartment forms exist only in zoning and in a rezoning proposal (Sections 2 and 5), not as built buildings. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/Zoning/Waterworks%20Master%20Plan%20REV%204%20-%20October%202021.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Oliver's Landing and Ocean's Crest, read the Form B alongside the neighbourhood's statutory building scheme (below) — the scheme, not the strata, controls much of what an owner may alter. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Both Furry Creek stratas are above the five-lot threshold on their published unit counts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children under Strata Property Regulation 7.01. No Furry Creek strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — Oliver's Landing listings record no age restriction at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Local planning — SLRD Electoral Area D (there is no municipal bylaw set here):** the Official Community Plan is Electoral Area D Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1135-2013 (adopted October 28, 2013), with the Howe Sound East Sub-area Plan as Schedule C covering Furry Creek; zoning is SLRD Electoral Area D Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016, whose Furry Creek zones include CD3 (Furry Creek Comprehensive Development 3), FCR residential zones, FCC1 (Furry Creek – Commercial 1) and FCC2 (Furry Creek Golf Course Commercial 2). https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/ and https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **No SSMUH density windfall here:** the Province has determined the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit small-scale multi-unit housing densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for SSMUH purposes; SLRD Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities briefly added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. Suite rights are whatever the base zoning and the building scheme allow. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **Building schemes sit on top of zoning:** the SLRD records that the Furry Creek neighbourhood has existing statutory building schemes and covenants, which constrain what owners can build or alter — read the scheme before planning changes, additions or a suite. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **Hazard development permits:** expanded Development Permit Areas for Howe Sound East were adopted through OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 1717-2021 (adopted May 25, 2022), covering landslide hazard areas, excavated rock and foundation stability, seismic liquefaction and fan stability, coastal hazards and creek hazards; creek-fan guidelines address dike design to IOD standard dike criteria and a flood construction level achieved by landfill. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **Taxes — rural regime:** as an SLRD electoral-area community, properties pay provincial rural property tax plus SLRD service levies for Furry Creek-specific services (water, wastewater treatment, street lighting, solid waste disposal, open spaces maintenance, fire protection, dyking/drainage — e.g., Furry Creek Dyking Local Service Establishment Bylaw No. 481; Furry Creek Sewer Local Service Bylaw No. 1619). Under the rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11), rural property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid after October 31. Per-lot levy amounts: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant only if a future Furry Creek phase sells off developer contracts). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $500,000 (**illustration only — no Furry Creek median exists, because SnapStats does not cover the area**), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no current Furry Creek presale was confirmed on an official source; if a future phase (including the proposed Lot 2 apartments) sells off plans, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

The built strata stock is Oliver's Landing (56 townhouses) and Ocean's Crest (12 single-family strata homes); apartment buildings exist only in a rezoning proposal for the unbuilt Lot 2 waterfront lands. - **Q: A listing called Oliver's Landing a great retirement spot. Is it 55+?** No — that is a seller's lifestyle remark, not a bylaw. - **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units under the new provincial housing rules?** No. The Province has confirmed Furry Creek is exempt from the additional 3–6 unit SSMUH densities, Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the added units from the FCR3 and CD3 zones, and statutory building schemes and covenants also apply. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Q: Can I rent out a Furry Creek strata unit long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022, so a strata cannot ban long-term rentals; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw, and the building scheme is a separate document to check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What extra carrying costs are specific to Furry Creek?** A bundle of SLRD local services — water, sewer, dyking/drainage, fire protection, street lighting, solid waste and open spaces — levied on top of provincial rural property tax; get the current levy list from the SLRD before comparing carrying costs with Squamish. Per-lot amounts: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/inside-slrd/bylaws?community=All&type=All&page=4 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks matter here?** The expanded Howe Sound East Development Permit Areas (Bylaw No. 1717-2021) for landslide, slope/foundation stability, liquefaction, coastal and creek hazards — creek-fan lots carry dike and flood construction level requirements, which affect both building plans and insurance conversations. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
- **Q: Where do Furry Creek kids go to school?** Squamish. SD48's Policy 501 catchment table puts Furry Creek in the Squamish Elementary (K–6) catchment, with Don Ross Middle School (7–9) and Howe Sound Secondary School (10–12) as the Squamish-area middle/secondary schools; there is no school in Furry Creek itself. https://22.files.edl.io/0f17/07/30/26/170123-5484041d-9258-492f-a2ad-1e6938a06351.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Furry Creek was briefly given SSMUH densities, then had them taken back (2025).** Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra 3–6 unit densities that had briefly been added to the FCR3 and CD3 zones after the Province determined the community is not within an urban containment boundary — anyone quoting "you can build four units now" from a 2024 source is out of date. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd
- **Oliver's Landing is still building out, three decades in.** Development-permit documents for "Oliver's Landing at Furry Creek, Buildings 16, 17, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23" were issued for form-and-character review in Dec 2024–Mar 2025 under the BC Building Code 2024 — the final phase of the waterfront strata was still in the permit stream in 2025, on strata plan LMS4257 lands. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/olivers-landing-formcharacter-dp-schedule-2-site-plans (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/furry-creek-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **The whole community runs on SLRD local services, not a city.** Water, sewer, dyking, fire, street lighting and open-space maintenance at Furry Creek are each their own SLRD local service with its own bylaw (e.g., Dyking Bylaw No. 481, Sewer Bylaw No. 1619) — which is why two otherwise-similar Sea-to-Sky homes can carry different levy stacks. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/
- **The apartment buildings in the sales story do not exist yet.** The 2022 rezoning presentation for Furry Creek proposed replacing the planned Lot 2 waterfront townhouses with low- to mid-rise apartments — a design concept in a rezoning process, with the same presentation recording overall covenant room for up to 920 homes, 300 hotel rooms and an 80-berth marina. None of that is built stock, and no completion date is published. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/Furry%20Creek%20Public%20Information%20Meeting%20Presentation%20July%2028%2C%202022%20Rev%20%232%20July%2028.pdf
- **A development application for Furry Creek has been in the SLRD process since 2021** — it was one of two Howe Sound East applications (with Britannia South/Tiger Bay) that drove the expanded hazard Development Permit Areas adopted in 2022, and the CD3 zone contemplates further build-out; applicant, unit counts and current status on an official source are UNCONFIRMED. https://getinvolved.slrd.bc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/planning/staff-reports/Area%20D%20DPA%20Housekeeping%204th%20Reading%20Report-FULL.pdf
- **Squamish is the corridor's seniors-housing centre, not Furry Creek.** Westwinds (232 affordable low-income seniors rentals, 38275 Third Ave, Squamish) opened in April 2022 beside the registered Shannon Falls Retirement Residence on the same street — a Furry Creek senior who needs supported or subsidized housing is, in practice, moving to Third Avenue in Squamish. https://www.newswire.ca/news-releases/hundreds-of-new-affordable-homes-for-seniors-open-in-squamish-887232529.html (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/furry-creek-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
