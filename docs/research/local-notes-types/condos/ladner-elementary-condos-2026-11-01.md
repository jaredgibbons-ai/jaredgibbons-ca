# Ladner Elementary — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. "Ladner Elementary" is a Paragon sub-area name for central Ladner, in the City of Delta — it follows Ladner Elementary School, 5016 44 Avenue, and is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name. The area is planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), including the Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The working area is central Ladner around Ladner Elementary School at 5016 44 Avenue, Memorial Park at 5010 47 Ave, and Arthur Drive, based on the school and park addresses on SD37 and City of Delta pages. No official boundary for the sub-area is published by the City. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/list-of-parks/lists/all-parks/memorial-park
- The village core sits west of Chilukthan Slough; the OCP's Ladner Village Core material addresses the Village's historic buildings, which shapes renovation and redevelopment in the core. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: two pockets. First, the 47 Avenue / 47A Avenue blocks east of Memorial Park — Fentons Court, Park Regent Estates and their neighbours. Second, the village core itself — Delta Green on 51 Street, the Seafarer Marina buildings on 48 Avenue by the river, and the Bridge & Elliott presale at Bridge and Elliott Streets (Section 2).
- Transit: route 609 runs between South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange via Arthur Drive and Ladner Trunk Road, serving the Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary area; route 601 runs from South Delta to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt609.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Schools physically in Ladner include Ladner Elementary, 5016 44 Ave; Delta Secondary, 4615 51 St; Hawthorne Elementary, 5160 Central Ave; Holly Elementary, 4625 62 St; Neilson Grove Elementary, 5500 Admiral Blvd; and Port Guichon Elementary, 4381 46A St. Catchments are address-based — the locator result for 5016 44 Avenue is Ladner Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12). https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Cromie Park is at 4907 42b Ave, Ladner Leisure Centre at 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent, Ladner Pioneer Library at 4683 51st Street, and Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, has the Ladner Exchange bus loop next door. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/delta-council-approves-agreements-construction-new-indoor and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, whatever an older directory entry for Park Regent Estates says. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives — directly relevant to Delta Green.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner and defined children of a qualifying resident (Strata Property Regulation 7.01). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Presale rules (REDMA) — directly relevant to Bridge & Elliott.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement, and under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act the buyer has seven days to rescind, starting on the later of signing the purchase agreement or signing the written acknowledgement of the disclosure statement; deposits are generally held in trust. Assignment terms (consent, fees) are set by the contract and disclosure statement — no published Bridge & Elliott assignment term was verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/ and https://www.presalehomes.ca/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-presale-condo-deposit-structures-in-bc
- **Delta zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, and the village development permit areas.** Zoning is parcel-by-parcel under Bylaw No. 7600, and the OCP adds the Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas — the LVC guidelines address the Village's historic buildings and shape exterior work in the core, which matters for Delta Green, Seafarer and any village-core strata renovation. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — no lettered schedules, no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". The village's six-storey permission that Bridge & Elliott used came through the December 2021 Ladner Village OCP amendment. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://renx.ca/smaller-bc-communities-growing-up-as-big-devs-come-to-town
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction.** Bridge & Elliott homes carry GST; resale condos do not. The first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at Delta Green?** Delta Green is marketed as 55+ living. Provincial rules require an exemption for a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption); children can also be exempt in defined cases, and a live-in caregiver is exempt. Confirm the building's registered bylaw itself before relying on the marketing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3091870-4686-51-street-107-delta-bc-v4k-2v7/brochure
- **Q: Can I buy at Delta Green as an investment and rent it out?** Ownership is not age-restricted — the bylaw governs who may reside — and long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide. But a tenant must still satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, which narrows the rental pool by design. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: I signed at Bridge & Elliott — how long do I have to change my mind?** Seven days under REDMA s. 21, from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. After that, the contract binds — including its assignment terms. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Q: Does a presale here get taxed differently from a resale?** Yes. A new home carries 5% GST (partly recoverable — up to 100% for eligible first-time buyers to $1 million under Bill C-4), and PTT is charged on the total paid; the newly built home PTT exemption is full to $1,100,000. A resale condo has no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Q: What comes with a Seafarer Marina unit — is the boat slip real?** A current listing describes a unit with its own 30-foot boat slip and river views from a large deck. Whether a slip is assigned, leased or part of the strata lot is a title and Form B question for the specific unit — do not assume the slip transfers on marketing copy alone. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4803-48-avenue/110
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the older 47 Avenue buildings?** Park Regent Estates' directory entry records pets allowed with restrictions, and its listings call the building pet-friendly. Pet bylaws are strata-specific — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/park-regent-estates-4989-47-ave
- **Q: How do I judge a small self-managed building like Park Regent?** Self-management means no professional manager catching missed filings. Read two years of minutes, the budget, the CRF balance and the depreciation report attached to the Form B, and check who holds the strata's records — the document package is the diligence. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118792-208-4989-47-avenue-ladner-british-columbia-v4k1r1-499000/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Q: Parking and storage — what's typical here?** Fentons Court's profile records underground parking and storage; Park Regent's directory entry records storage as an amenity. The Form B states the specific unit's allocation — assigned stalls and lockers are not all legally equal. https://www.michaellepore.ca/development/fentons-court/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is the village core a heritage area that limits renovations?** The Ladner Village Core development permit area in the OCP addresses the Village's historic buildings and can shape exterior renovations and redevelopment in the core — relevant to strata exterior work, not to interior unit renovations. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Q: What about flooding and the slough?** Ladner is low land behind the City's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection and 31 drainage pump stations — and any property within 30 m of a stream, including Chilukthan Slough and drainage ditches, is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Seafarer-area buyers should also ask the strata about flood and water-damage deductibles on the Form B insurance summary. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Bridge & Elliott is the village's density turning point.** Trade coverage notes multifamily housing has rarely been available in Ladner Village, that Delta council approved the OCP amendment for Ladner Village in December 2021 allowing buildings up to six storeys on the Headwater site, and that the project is now in presale/under construction — the first six-storey condo building in the village core. https://renx.ca/smaller-bc-communities-growing-up-as-big-devs-come-to-town and https://storeys.com/ladner-headwater-six-storey-project/
- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion and major construction expected to begin in 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **Ladner Village revitalization is an active City program.** The City's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan, including the Chisholm Street Wharf project, is the named project set for the core where Delta Green and Bridge & Elliott sit. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **The newest resale stock is very new, and very small-scale.** Manor House on Arthur Drive completed in 2025 as a boutique townhouse-style strata, and Delta Green added a 55+ apartment building in 2021 — central Ladner's condo supply is growing a few units at a time, not in towers. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/manor-house-delta-4569 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3091870-4686-51-street-107-delta-bc-v4k-2v7/brochure
- **The sub-area name is a school name, not a catchment promise.** SD37 catchments are address-based; a home marketed as "Ladner Elementary area" can still fall to Port Guichon Elementary or another school — the locator result in Section 1 is for the school's own address only. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **There is no Ladner Elementary market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no South Delta community section — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no SnapStats condo figures exist for this sub-area (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/ladner-elementary-guide.md).
