## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic the operative rules are the ALR rules that keep condos off farm land, then the Township frame, the farm-class and tax frames, then the quota frame only as far as sources verify it — which is not at all — then the standard BC strata rules stated only for contrast.

- **ALR stratification bar — leads this section, because it is the governing fact for this file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR under the framework addressed in ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 (see also Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)), and subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.md)
- **ALR residence frame:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Those are houses on one title — neither is a condo unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.md)
- **Township frame (for Township parcels):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs zoning, with the coach-house definition in s.102 (a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — never strata); Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², makes lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and has NO 6-plex tier — most poultry acreage parcels exceed the ceiling by a wide margin in any case; the Township secondary-suite cap in this program's settled position is 120 m². City of Langley parcels sit under the City's own bylaws, not the Township's — which municipality a specific parcel is in is UNCONFIRMED at parcel level until checked. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.md)
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program. The income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. The farm-class position of Langley poultry farms as a group: UNCONFIRMED — classification is property-by-property. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.md)
