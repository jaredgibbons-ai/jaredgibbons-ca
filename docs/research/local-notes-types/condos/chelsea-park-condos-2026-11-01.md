## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. Stated here for completeness — no strata purchase inside Chelsea Park itself is verified, so the Form B rules bite on the Ambleside/Dundarave stock a Chelsea Park condo searcher ends up considering instead. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This matters when a directory shows an old "rentals not allowed" line on any West Vancouver building a buyer cross-shops from Chelsea Park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District zoning and plans:** Chelsea Park sits in the District of West Vancouver under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Houses sit in the RS single-family zones — including RS2 (Section 202), RS3 (Section 203), RS5 (Section 205) and RS7 (Section 207) — with Section 200 minimum site areas of 8,094 m² (RS1), 1,625 m² (RS2), 975 m² (RS3), 731 m² (RS4) and 488 m² (RS5); the bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. There is no Chelsea Park-specific local area plan — UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
- **Hazard permit areas:** District-wide, an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both are live checks on the pocket's upper and ravine-edge lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Chelsea Park price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — no new condo construction is verified in Chelsea Park. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at West Vancouver prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. At Chelsea Park prices the first-time programs are usually out of range — run the bands on the actual price. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Chelsea Park is confirmed on a District source — UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Insured mortgages are capped by price, so most West Vancouver purchases are conventional — confirm with the lender. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **The "park" in Chelsea Park is a dedicated RS7 park site.** Chairlift Park is listed in the District Parks Master Plan as RS7 and Dedicated — the parkland is dedicated, which is a stronger protection than an ordinary park zoning, and it anchors the Chelsea Close end of the pocket. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
