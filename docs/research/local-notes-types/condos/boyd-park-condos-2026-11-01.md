# Boyd Park — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography note — read first:** "Boyd Park" is a park-anchored page name, not a City of Richmond neighbourhood. The City's own area list for its Official Community Plan work uses Seafair and West Richmond for this part of the city, and the label comes from Hugh Boyd Community Park at 9200 No. 1 Road; no City boundary for a Boyd Park neighbourhood is published. The research file, working from official sources only, confirmed no condo building in this area. This pass found one — because board listings file the low-rise complex directly across No. 1 Road from the park under the sub-area name "Boyd Park." That complex is documented in Section 2; everything else around the park is detached-house stock on the official record. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At Apple Greene — a 1973–1975 wood-frame complex — this is the central diligence document: listings themselves note major completed building work and a special levy already paid, which is exactly the history the Form B and depreciation report set out in full. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Apple Greene exceeds five lots on any count published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The building directory's "rentals allowed with restrictions" line for Apple Greene reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Apple Greene is not published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000, covers the area; no Boyd Park plan exists in its structure — the surrounding ground is planned as Seafair / West Richmond. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500; houses around the park sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in those zones (Section 5.4: 33.0 m² minimum, maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **District energy:** the Alexandra District Energy Utility applies to the West Cambie / Alexandra area only. No source checked states any district-energy arrangement for Apple Greene or the Boyd Park area — listings instead record heat and hot water included in strata fees, a strata-cost item, not a City utility. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Apple Greene is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to a 1970s resale. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in the Boyd Park label area was confirmed on a developer source in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Boyd Park a real Richmond neighbourhood?** No — the City's own area list uses Seafair and West Richmond here; "Boyd Park" on this page means the neighbourhood around Hugh Boyd Community Park at No. 1 Road and Francis Road, and no City boundary for a Boyd Park neighbourhood is published. Board listings do use "Boyd Park" as a sub-area name — it is the name the Apple Greene listings carry. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Q: Are there any condos here at all?** One complex: Apple Greene (8720–8880 No. 1 Road, strata plan NWS518), a low-rise wood-frame complex at No. 1 Road and Francis Road across from Seafair Shopping Centre. Everything else in the label area is detached houses on the official record. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: How old is Apple Greene, and how big?** Phased 1973–1975: the directory records 1975 for the 8720 No. 1 Road building and 45 units in that building, while listings record 1973 for units at 8720, 8840 and 8860 No. 1 Road and 1975 at 8740 and 8760. A listing header describes a 411-unit development, which reads as complex-wide; the strata-wide total for NWS518 is UNCONFIRMED. https://bccondosandhomes.com/building/apple-greene-8720-no-1-road
- **Q: Can I have a pet at Apple Greene?** The building directory records the bylaws as pets not allowed. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws), so treat that line as live until the registered bylaws on the Form B say otherwise. https://bccondosandhomes.com/building/apple-greene-8720-no-1-road
- **Q: Can I rent out an Apple Greene condo long-term?** Yes — the directory's "rentals allowed with restrictions" line reflects a rental-restriction bylaw, and those have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws govern that question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do Apple Greene strata fees cover?** Listings for the complex list heat, hot water and city utilities among the inclusions, alongside the clubhouse, indoor and outdoor pools, hot tub, sauna, gym, guest suite and library. The exact inclusion set is building- and budget-specific — read it off the Form B for the unit. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3094573-8760-no-1-road-305-richmond-bc-v7c-4l5/brochure
- **Q: Has the building had major work done?** Current listings say yes — one describes major infrastructure upgrades (roof, exterior, balconies, windows, pipes, lobby) with a $25K special levy fully paid, and another records balcony, building-envelope, roof and window work upgraded in 2022. Those are listing statements about work already done; the depreciation report and Form B are where a buyer confirms what is done, what is paid, and what is still coming. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/8740-no-1-road/207
- **Q: Which schools serve an Apple Greene address?** School District 38 catchments are address-based, with no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). The SD38 locator result for 9200 No. 1 Road is UNVERIFIED — the locator was not run for the research file. Hugh Boyd Secondary School is physically at 9200 No. 1 Road and R.M. Grauer Elementary is at 4440 Blundell Road, and Apple Greene listings name Grauer Elementary and Boyd Secondary as the nearby schools — but run the building's exact address in the locator before relying on a catchment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Q: How do I get to the Canada Line from here?** Route 401 runs on No. 1 Road past the park and the complex, between Richmond–Brighouse Station (Canada Line) and Steveston Exchange. There is no SkyTrain station in the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Q: What are the market numbers for Boyd Park condos?** None are quoted in this file: Richmond is outside SnapStats coverage in the reports held for this program, so there is no verified sub-area or community median, inventory or sales ratio from this file's sources. With one complex in the area, price off Apple Greene's own sale history instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The research file found no condos here — the board name found one.** Working from official City sources only, the area research records detached housing around the park and no named condo building. It is the MLS sub-area label "Boyd Park" on listings at 8720–8880 No. 1 Road that ties Apple Greene to this page — a buyer searching only City planning material would never see the complex assigned to this name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md) and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3147887-315-8720-no-1-road-richmond-bc-v7c-4l5
- **One complex, six addresses, two build years.** Apple Greene's buildings run 8720 through 8880 No. 1 Road under one strata plan (NWS518), with directory and listing years split between 1973 and 1975 by address — document orders, depreciation reports and levy history run to the corporation and the specific building, not to a single "Apple Greene" date. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2510212-213-8720-no-1-road
- **The amenity set is a 1970s resort package that still drives the fees.** Indoor and outdoor pools, hot tub, sauna, gym, clubhouse, guest suite and library in a low-rise wood-frame complex means the depreciation report — now mandatory every five years — carries pool, envelope and parkade-free building systems that newer low-rises do not have. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The park across the street is a full community campus.** Hugh Boyd Community Park holds the pitch and putt, turf fields, courts and playground, West Richmond Community Centre is on the same site, and Hugh Boyd Secondary School shares the 9200 No. 1 Road address — the complex's "across from the park" position comes with sports lighting, tournament traffic and school-day activity as the other side of the same fact. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **The name on the park is Richmond's first warden.** Hugh Boyd was the first Warden of the Corporation of the Township of Richmond, and the first council meetings were held in the dining room of his house on Sea Island after the Township was incorporated; the secondary school and the park carry his name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
- **Richmond has no SnapStats coverage in this program.** There is no community median to quote for Boyd Park — any Richmond median in marketing copy comes from a source outside this file's verification, and this file substitutes none. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/boyd-park.md)
