# East Newton, Surrey — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Note on schools: "East Newton Elementary" does not exist — it appears in neither the BC Ministry of Education K-12 schools file (2025/2026) nor SD36's directory, and no school name in this file is taken from a district URL slug. https://www.surreyschools.ca/

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- East Newton is the Newton land east of King George Boulevard. The City's East Newton plan material is bounded by King George Highway to the west, 76 Avenue to the south, 152 Street to the east and Newton Athletic Park to the north. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- King George Boulevard is the western edge and the transit spine: the R1 RapidBus runs on it between Newton Exchange and King George Station via Surrey Central Station. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip
- Newton Exchange and the City-designated Newton Town Centre sit at 72 Avenue and King George Boulevard, at the area's northwest corner — west of the boulevard, but it is the node East Newton condos market against. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: there are no towers in East Newton. The apartment-condo stock is low-rise and clusters in two pockets — the 70 Avenue pocket west of 140 Street (Chelsea Gardens) and the 75A Avenue / 76 Avenue pocket just east of King George Boulevard around 137–138 Street (Silva, Mirra, Earl's Court), within a walk of Newton Exchange. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The rest of East Newton is single-family subdivision streets built out under the East Newton NCPs, plus the Chimney Hill pocket carried by Chimney Hill Elementary and Chimney Heights Park. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2016-R228.pdf and https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/
- The East Newton Business Park plan area (128 St to 144 St, 80 Ave to the south side of 88 Ave) frames the north end — relevant to north-edge condos for neighbouring uses, not housing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-business-park
- SD36 schools named in the research file for this area are Chimney Hill Elementary (14755 74 Ave), M.B. Sanford Elementary (7318 143 St), Tamanawis Secondary (12600 66 Ave) and Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave); assignment is by address through the SD36 School Locator, not by nearest building. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate is the document that discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, any amount the owner owes, any approved special levy still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with a 30-year repair/replacement projection and at least three CRF cash-flow funding models; it can no longer be deferred by a vote, and stratas of four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. Any East Newton building page still showing "rentals allowed with restrictions" is showing pre-2022 wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse/partner, and children in defined care cases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs multi-family in Surrey, including the R-series residential zones found in Newton. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **OCP: the current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, which received final adoption on June 15, 2026.** The old PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is not the current plan — do not cite it as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- That OCP update is why the old East Newton plans no longer govern: the City states the East Newton North NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and East Newton South's land-use designations were superseded by it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **SSMUH (small-scale multi-unit housing):** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house. This affects the single-family streets around the condos, not existing strata buildings. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026, creating the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate: 100% of the GST on a new (or substantially renovated) home valued up to $1 million, phasing out on a straight line between $1 million and $1.5 million, to a maximum rebate of $50,000, for purchase agreements entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate still exists underneath it.** For any buyer of a new home, the GST/HST New Housing Rebate returns 36% of the GST, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, with no rebate at $450,000 or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program eliminates PTT on the first $500,000 of value — a maximum saving of $8,000 — for homes valued at $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out to $860,000. A separate Newly Built Home Exemption gives full PTT relief on a new home used as a principal residence up to $1,100,000, with partial relief to $1,150,000. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://auxiliummortgage.com/blog/bc-first-time-homebuyers-2026-federal-provincial-programs-incentives/
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** A presale buyer in BC must receive the developer's disclosure statement, and has a 7-day right of rescission running from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement; deposits are held in trust or covered by deposit protection insurance. Assignment terms are set in the purchase contract and disclosure statement — there is no general right to assign. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** The RRSP Home Buyers' Plan allows a withdrawal of up to $60,000 per buyer, repayable over 15 years; the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, deductible going in and tax-free on a qualifying withdrawal, and the two can be used for the same home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC insurance premium range.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at a loan-to-value of 65% or less to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment; the premium is a one-time charge that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condo towers in East Newton?** No. Every verified apartment-condo building in the sub-area is low-rise — Silva is three 4-storey wood-frame buildings, and Chelsea Gardens, Mirra and Earl's Court are all low-rise form. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/silva-newton-town-centre-surrey-presale-condos-by-wanson-group/
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in these buildings, and how do I check?** Coverage is building-by-building and is stated on the Form B, which must disclose the unit's monthly fee, the CRF balance and an insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of a request. Do not rely on a listing's fee figure alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out an East Newton condo?** Long-term rental bans no longer exist — every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022. Two limits can still apply: a strata can restrict short-term rentals, and in a 55+ building like Chelsea Gardens the tenant still has to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Chelsea Gardens really 55+? Its older pages say 19+.** Current listings state a 55+ restriction, and a 19+ bylaw has been legally invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when BC confined age bylaws to 55+. Confirm the registered bylaw and your own exemption position (spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy resident) before subjects. https://vancouvernashdom.com/surrey-open-houses-condos/listing.r2974069-201-13870-70-avenue-surrey-v3w-0r9.104420669/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Building-by-building. A Chelsea Gardens listing states two pets, maximum 15 inches at the shoulder; a Mirra listing states cats only for that unit. Both are listing-stated — the registered pet bylaw for the building governs. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3060317-13870-70-avenue-202-surrey-bc-v3w-0r9/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/13740-75a-avenue/310
- **Q: What about parking and storage in the new buildings?** Silva's listings describe underground parking and storage lockers, and a Mirra listing describes two parking stalls and a full-size locker for that unit — but stall and locker numbers are unit-specific legal allocations, confirmed on the Form B, not the listing. https://www.apartments.com/profile/wzqwmsh/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: How do I know if a big levy is coming in a 1990s building?** Read the depreciation report: since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must have one on a five-year cycle, projecting 30 years of repair and replacement costs with three CRF funding models — the gap between the CRF balance and those models is where levies come from. Earl's Court listings note a 2018 roof, which is exactly the kind of item to verify against the report, not the listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What is the strata insurance deductible exposure?** The Form B must include a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage; the buyer's own unit-owner policy is what covers a strata deductible charged back to the unit, so match the deductible amounts in that summary to your policy before completion. The deductible amounts for any specific East Newton building: UNCONFIRMED from a public source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: If I buy a new Silva unit, do I still pay GST?** GST applies to new construction. If you are an eligible first-time buyer, the Bill C-4 FTHB rebate now returns 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; other new-home buyers fall back to the older New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What transit do I actually get?** The R1 RapidBus on King George Boulevard to King George Station via Surrey Central, plus routes 321 (152 St / King George) and 324 (132 St) through the grid. There is no SkyTrain station inside East Newton — the ride starts with a bus to the Expo Line. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The plan documents most sellers quote no longer govern.** The East Newton North NCP was rescinded and East Newton South's designations were superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) — a density claim sourced to an old NCP is sourced to a dead document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **The newest supply is already resale.** Silva, the sub-area's one large new condo community (261 homes in three buildings), completed in phases recorded as 2024 (Silva 2) and 2025 (Silva 3, the final phase) — so "new" East Newton condos now trade as nearly-new resales, with GST treatment depending on the seller and the unit's history, not the building's age. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/silva-2-13760-75a-avenue and https://www.zumper.com/address/13760-75a-ave-surrey-bc-v3w-can
- **A very large King George corridor project is proposed just north of the sub-area.** Queens on King George, by Dawson + Sawyer at 7850 King George Boulevard, is described by its presale source as a 36.3-acre master plan of 21 six-storey wood-frame buildings totalling 3,478 condominiums and townhomes, with completion phases from 2028. It is a proposal/presale, not a built building, and it sits on the corridor East Newton buyers use for Newton Town Centre services. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/queens-on-king-george-surrey/
- **No SkyTrain is funded to Newton.** The rapid-transit project under construction south of the Fraser is the 16 km Surrey–Langley (Dave Barrett) Extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to Langley City Centre, expected in service in late 2029 — it does not run down King George Boulevard to Newton. East Newton's rapid transit remains the R1 bus. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515 and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip
- **The 55+ stock here is a full community, not a single building.** Chelsea Gardens spreads across four civic addresses (13860–13888 70 Ave) on 23 acres with its own clubhouse, guest suites and RV parking — which is why its fees, amenities and social calendar do not compare directly with a standard low-rise like Earl's Court a few blocks away. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/30783/chelsea-gardens/surrey/east-newton/13870-70-ave
