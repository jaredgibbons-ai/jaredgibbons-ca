- Caulfeild is the District of West Vancouver's western neighbourhood: Lower Caulfeild at the foreshore, Upper Caulfeild north toward Highway 1, Lighthouse Park to the west and Marine Drive along the south. No street-by-street written boundary is published by the District — the exact line is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- Main streets: Marine Drive along the waterfront; Caulfeild Drive, Keith Road and Headland Drive in the interior; Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway) crosses the north side. Caulfeild Village Centre is the commercial centre named for the area in Official Community Plan policy (OCP Policy 2.3.4). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Caulfeild this applies to the one townhome strata (Caulfeild Cove, built 1974) — a 1970s wood-frame complex is exactly where the depreciation report matters most. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The building directory still shows "rentals not allowed" for Caulfeild Cove — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Caulfeild strata is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District zoning and plans:** West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 governs, under Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. The former Caulfeild Land Use Contract area is now CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) under Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024: Residential Area A allows a maximum of three dwelling units per lot and prohibits stratification of buildings or land; nearby RS single-family zones state minimum site areas from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Heritage:** Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area, planned by Francis Caulfeild from 1899; owners within the area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for any work — this applies to the Caulfeild Cove area lots as much as to detached houses, and any strata exterior work plan runs through it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Hazard permit areas:** the District applies an Environmental Development Permit Area for watercourses (work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank), a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface) and a Foreshore Development Permit Area for coastal hazards — all three can apply in Caulfeild, the foreshore area on Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Caulfeild price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — there is no new condo construction verified in Caulfeild. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at West Vancouver prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. At Caulfeild prices the first-time programs are usually out of range — run the bands on the actual price. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Caulfeild is verified — the rule is stated for completeness if a Village Centre project is ever marketed. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Insured mortgages are capped by price, so most West Vancouver purchases are conventional — confirm with the lender. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in Caulfeild at all?** None verified. The research file confirms no named condo buildings on an official or developer source, the District-wide condo file found no verified apartment stock in Caulfeild, and CD86 Residential Area A prohibits stratification outright. The only verified strata complex is the Caulfeild Cove townhomes on Marine Drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: Is Caulfeild Cove a condo or a townhouse complex?** Townhouse. Listings record Sub-Type Townhouse and the directory's product variants are townhouse forms; units are 3-level frame-wood homes built 1974, 30 units in the complex per the directory. It is a freehold strata, so the Form B / depreciation-report diligence in Section 3 applies in full. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3089835-4957-marine-drive-15-west-vancouver-olde-caulfeild-homes-for-sale-1899000/brochure
- **Q: The directory says Caulfeild Cove bans rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, so that directory line is old bylaw text. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you; read the registered bylaws in the strata package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Caulfeild Cove?** The directory records pets allowed. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws), and any size or number limits sit in the registered bylaws — UNCONFIRMED from public sources beyond the directory line. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive
- **Q: Why can't a Caulfeild house lot just be stratified into condos?** In the former Land Use Contract lands, CD86 Residential Area A caps density at three dwelling units per lot and prohibits stratification of buildings or land — added units there cannot be sold as separate strata lots. The CD86 zoning was set by Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024 under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: What extra permit does Lower Caulfeild work need?** A Heritage Alteration Permit — Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area, and any work inside it needs the permit on top of ordinary permits. Foreshore, wildfire-interface and watercourse development permits can also apply lot by lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Caulfeild address?** School District 45 catchments are address-based. The verified locator result in the research file is for 4685 Keith Road (the Caulfeild Elementary address): Caulfeild Elementary (K–7) and Rockridge Secondary (8–12), and both schools are physically in the area, Rockridge across from Caulfeild Village. Run any specific address in the SD45 locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: How do I get to Park Royal or downtown without a car?** Route 253 runs Caulfeild Village to Park Royal via Caulfeild Drive, Cherbourg Drive, Keith Road and Marine Drive, with select trips continuing to Vancouver over the Lions Gate Bridge; route 262 links the village to Horseshoe Bay and Lions Bay, and route 250 runs on Marine Drive. The area is bus-served but car-oriented for most daily trips. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: What is the tax hit if I miss West Vancouver's deadline?** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026: a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. Separately, the Province's additional school tax (0.2% between $3 million and $4 million of residential assessed value, 0.4% above $4 million in 2026) can apply at Caulfeild values. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual condo market if Caulfeild has none?** Ambleside and the Dundarave Bellevue strip, east along Marine Drive — the District-wide condo file verifies the stock there building by building (for example Grosvenor Ambleside, Parkview Tower, Westerlies, Sea Wind). ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The zoning itself explains the missing condos.** Caulfeild's former Land Use Contract was replaced by CD86 zoning in 2024 (Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024), and CD86 Residential Area A prohibits stratification — so the absence of condo stock is a bylaw outcome, not a market accident, and it will not change without a rezoning. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **The spelling is a search trap.** The District and SD45 use "Caulfeild" (with the second "u"), not "Caulfield" — searches under the common spelling miss District documents and some listings, and at least one listing portal files the neighbourhood under "Caulfield." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Caulfeild Cove predates the heritage-era framing around it.** The townhome complex was built in 1974 — a 1970s wood-frame strata sitting in a neighbourhood whose Lower Caulfeild subdivision was planned from 1899 and is now a designated Heritage Conservation Area; its depreciation report, not the area's estate-home market, is the pricing document. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Both of the area's schools are inside the neighbourhood.** Caulfeild Elementary (4685 Keith Road) and Rockridge Secondary (5350 Headland Drive) are both physically in Caulfeild — unusual for a West Vancouver pocket this size, and the reason the SD45 locator result for the sample address returns two in-area schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **The foreshore is a permit area, not just a view.** Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots sit in the Foreshore Development Permit Area for coastal hazards, and Caulfeild Park's washrooms are open 7 a.m. to 9 p.m., May through October, under a Caulfeild Park Management Plan — the park at the neighbourhood's south edge is managed as an environmental and heritage site, not a simple beach park. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Half the tax bill is not the District's.** Approximately 50% of a West Vancouver property tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities — a Caulfeild Cove owner's tax notice is mostly not District spending. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
