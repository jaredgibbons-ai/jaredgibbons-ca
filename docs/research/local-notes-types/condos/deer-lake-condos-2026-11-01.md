## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern any strata purchase a Deer Lake buyer ends up making in a neighbouring sub-area, and because they are the rules sellers of the area's future stratified multiplex lots will sell under. They do not create condo stock in Deer Lake itself.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption matters here, because a small stratified multiplex on a Deer Lake R1 lot can fall under the five-lot line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Burnaby building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — the rule that could create strata here:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026 — the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is not the current bylaw and must not be cited as current. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Burnaby plan framework:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Burnaby's community plans are policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Heritage overlay:** Sperling Avenue fronting the park lies within the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct and listed properties carry heritage review — a constraint on the multiplex/stratification math for precinct lots specifically. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant if a future Deer Lake multiplex or neighbouring-area presale is marketed to this buyer pool. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Deer Lake at all?** No verified apartment-condo stock, and no attached market activity: SnapStats recorded 0 condos & townhomes listings and 0 solds in the sub-area in September 2026, against 13 detached listings. A buyer set on strata living near the lake is shopping the neighbouring sub-areas, not this one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: Is Deer Lake Place or Upper Deer Lake the same thing?** No. In the SnapStats GVR report, Deer Lake, Deer Lake Place and Upper Deer Lake are three separate Burnaby sub-areas with separate inventory and solds — attached product filed under the other two names is not Deer Lake product. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: A listing near the lake calls itself a Deer Lake townhouse. Should I believe the label?** Check the MLS sub-area field, not the prose: townhouse listings that use "Deer Lake" lifestyle wording in this pass carried neighbouring sub-area labels (for example Burnaby Lake) in their own area fields. The Deer Lake sub-area's own attached count is zero. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: Could a Deer Lake house lot become strata homes?** Potentially, under the citywide R1 SSMUH district: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — but heritage-precinct and park-edge lots carry extra review, so eligibility is lot by lot under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: If a small Deer Lake multiplex is stratified, does it need a depreciation report?** Only if it has five or more lots: since July 1, 2024 stratas with 5+ lots must obtain one every five years, and stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. A 3- or 4-unit strata here could be exempt — which makes the Form B and the strata's own financials the whole diligence picture. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deer Lake address?** Buckingham Elementary (K–7), 6066 Buckingham Ave, sits inside the sub-area and Burnaby Central Secondary (8–12), 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, sits at its north side; SD41 has no middle-school tier (K–7 / 8–12). The locator run verified in the research file, for 6066 Buckingham Ave, returns exactly those two schools — catchments are address-based, so check every other address in the SD41 locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: How do I price anything here with no attached sales?** From the wider market, over a longer window: the research file's own guidance is to build pricing from wider central-Burnaby and Buckingham Heights comparables, because the sub-area may record one detached sale in a month (1 sold on 13 listings in September 2026) and no attached sales at all. The Burnaby community attached median ($686,000, September 2026) is a city figure, not a Deer Lake comparable. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: What is the lifestyle draw if there is no condo stock?** The park itself: Deer Lake Park connects the lake trails to the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, the Burnaby Art Gallery, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant, and the City's 2026 events program brought Symphony and Opera in the Park (July 18–19) and the Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival (August 8) to the park — that cultural precinct, not a building amenity package, is the area's offer. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: Does the heritage precinct affect a purchase on Sperling Avenue?** Yes — properties on the City's heritage register or inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct (for example the 1912 Woodward House at 5195 Sperling Avenue) carry heritage review, which bears on renovations, rebuilds and any multiplex plan. Check the register and the precinct status before pricing in changes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The boardwalk by the boat launch is closed for a full replacement.** The City closed that section of boardwalk from September 21, 2026, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive (about five extra minutes of walking); a small section of the nearby picnic area is also closed during construction — a park-edge seller should disclose it before a buyer finds the fencing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Burnaby's rulebook changed under this area twice in two years.** The R1 SSMUH district arrived in June 2024, the Burnaby 2050 OCP was adopted December 9, 2025 (replacing the 1998 OCP and overriding older community-plan maps where they conflict), and the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 took effect July 1, 2026 — any Deer Lake document still citing the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 or the 1998 OCP is two generations out of date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **City Hall is the sub-area's north-west neighbour.** Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just north-west of the sub-area — the civic precinct, Burnaby Hospital at 3935 Kincaid St to the west, and the Bob Prittie Metrotown library branch serving this quadrant are the working amenities; there is no enclosed mall or shopping centre inside the sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **The heritage houses are working artist residences.** Baldwin House and Eagles Estate, heritage homes around the lake, are part of the Deer Lake Artist Residency program, a 10-minute walk from the Shadbolt Centre and the Burnaby Art Gallery — the precinct is a living cultural district, not just a register listing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Zero attached stock is a standing condition, not a slow month.** The research file characterizes the sub-area as almost entirely detached single-family housing with effectively all-freehold tenure (13 detached listings, 0 attached, September 2026) — a condo buyer should treat "Deer Lake" attached listings from any portal as a labelling problem to verify, every time. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
