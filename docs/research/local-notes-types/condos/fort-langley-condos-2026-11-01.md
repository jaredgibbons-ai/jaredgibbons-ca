# Fort Langley, Township of Langley — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fort Langley is a village in the Township of Langley on the south bank of the Fraser River, described by the Township as the birthplace of BC, with its commercial core on Glover Road. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66 and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
- The wider village is framed by River Road, 88 Avenue, 96 Avenue and 240 Street, with the Salmon River and Bedford Channel at its edges; exact Paragon sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: in one riverside pocket — Bedford Landing, on Billy Brown Road along the Bedford Channel, west of the Glover Road village core. Two of the village's three verified apartment-condo buildings are there, a few hundred metres apart. The third, Lily Terrace, is on Francis Avenue at the village's east side, steps from the Glover Road shops. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- There are no condo towers in Fort Langley, and no high-rise is contemplated by the village framework: the downtown core is a designated Heritage Conservation Area, and new development must complement the heritage character (see Sections 3 and 5). https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- Glover Road is the village commercial street holding the shops; Lee's Market, the village grocery, is at 23320 Mavis Avenue, and there is no shopping centre in Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
- Transit is a single bus: TransLink route 562 runs through the village on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange, via Walnut Grove. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Fort Langley. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- SD35 schools physically in the village are Fort Langley Elementary (8877 Bartlett St) and Langley Fine Arts School (9096 Trattle St), a district K-12 fine arts choice school; a locator run for 8877 Bartlett St returned Fort Langley Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown, and catchments are address-based. https://fortlangley.sd35.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Fort Langley this is also where the Waterfront 55+ bylaw position and any Bedford Landing parking allocations are confirmed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with 30-year projections and at least three CRF funding models, non-deferrable by vote. The two Bedford Landing buildings (2009 and 2012) are at the age where envelope, deck and parkade items dominate those reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals may still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; bylaws set below 55 have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw restricts residence, not ownership, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse/partner, and children in defined care cases — directly relevant to Waterfront at Bedford Landing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Village plan: the Fort Langley Community Plan, Township of Langley Bylaw No. 2527** (adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan), is the governing plan for the village. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Heritage Conservation Area.** That plan designates a Heritage Conservation Area under the Local Government Act covering the downtown core (added by Bylaw 3710, October 27, 1997), to protect the heritage character of downtown Fort Langley; non-exempt work requires approval under the heritage rules, with exemptions including minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. Condo buyers should note this governs exteriors and common-property alterations near the core, on top of strata approval. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Design controls on top of zoning.** The village is also covered by a Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, May 3, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, April 28, 2014); the separate Building Façade Design Guidelines are non-binding policy. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** applies in the village; Residential Zone R-1E is named for the village in a Township public-hearing document, and minimum lot areas/frontages for the village zones are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024**, amended Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings. In Fort Langley it operates inside the heritage and design controls above — SSMUH permission is not an exemption from a Heritage Conservation Area or development permit requirement. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Flood — a condo issue here, not just a house issue.** Fort Langley is low-lying: the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for the Fort Langley community and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise. Parcel-level floodplain status for a specific building: UNVERIFIED — get it from the Township, and check the strata's insurance summary on the Form B for flood coverage and deductible terms. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for eligible first-time buyers, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Fort Langley's verified condo stock is resale (2009–2019), so this matters here only for future new product, not for the buildings in Section 2. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, for qualifying new-home buyers. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 3, 2026, and utilities are now billed separately. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** Any Fort Langley presale (see The 1912 in Section 5) requires a disclosure statement and carries a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it; deposits are held in trust or covered by deposit protection insurance, and assignment rights are contract-specific. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment; a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condo buildings are there in Fort Langley, really?** Three verified apartment-condo buildings: The Village at Bedford Landing (23285 Billy Brown Rd, 2012), Waterfront at Bedford Landing (23215 Billy Brown Rd, 2009) and Lily Terrace (23189 Francis Ave, 2018–2019). Everything else attached in the village is townhouses or houses. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Waterfront at Bedford Landing really 55+?** Current listings say yes — "Ages 55+" and a "55+ community." A 55+ bylaw is legal in BC (it is the only age bylaw form left), but it restricts who may live there, not who may buy, and exemptions exist for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers and legacy residents. Confirm the registered bylaw and your own position on the Form B. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/23215-Billy-Brown-Rd-416-Langley-BC-V1M-0B7/465100660_zpid/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Bedford Landing condo?** Long-term rental bans are void in BC since Nov 24, 2022, and a building-data source records The Village at Bedford Landing as rentals allowed. In the 55+ Waterfront building, a tenant would still have to satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/108-23285-billy-brown-rd-92001 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do the strata fees cover, and what is the deductible risk in a riverside building?** The Form B states the fee, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance. In a low-lying riverside location, read that insurance summary for flood-related coverage and the deductible amounts, and match them to your own unit-owner policy — the specific deductible figures for these buildings are UNCONFIRMED from a public source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Should I worry about levies in the 2009 and 2012 buildings?** Those are the questions the depreciation report answers: every strata of five or more lots must now have one renewed every five years, with 30-year cost projections and three CRF funding models. Compare the CRF balance on the Form B against the report's near-term items (envelope, decks, parkade membrane) before deciding a unit is cheap or expensive. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I change the exterior, add a heat pump, or enclose a balcony?** Two approvals, not one: the strata's, and — for properties in or affecting the downtown Heritage Conservation Area — the Township's heritage process, unless the work falls in an exemption such as a minor addition under 50 m² not fronting a road and conforming to the guidelines. The Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, 2014) also shape what can be built or changed in the village core. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** UNCONFIRMED for all three buildings from a public source in this pass — no listing or directory checked states a pet bylaw for The Village, Waterfront or Lily Terrace. Ask for the registered bylaws with the Form B if a pet is part of the purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: What is parking like?** The Village at Bedford Landing is recorded with underground and visitor parking; unit-specific stall and locker allocations are stated on the Form B, not in listings. Village street parking near Glover Road is a separate, seasonal pressure — check the building's visitor parking rules if guests matter. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/108-23285-billy-brown-rd-92001
- **Q: How do I get anywhere without a car?** Route 562 is the village's only confirmed bus, running on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange via Walnut Grove. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Fort Langley, and the funded Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension (King George Station to Langley City Centre, late 2029) does not serve the village — its Langley terminus is well south of the Fraser River village. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- **Q: Which school catchment is a Bedford Landing address in?** The locator result in the research file is for 8877 Bartlett St (Fort Langley Elementary / D.W. Poppy Secondary, no middle school shown) — a sample, not a promise for Billy Brown Road. Run the exact address in the SD35 School Locator before relying on a school. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The newest village housing project is a heritage restoration, not a condo building.** The 1912, by Lanstone Homes (the same builder as Lily Terrace), is a presale collection of three heritage-inspired townhomes plus the restored 1912 George Albion Towle House, with pre-sales stated as coming Winter 2027 and move-in Fall 2027 — developer-stated timing, and a townhouse product, so it does not add to the village's condo count. https://www.the1912.ca/
- **salishan Place by the River changed the village's civic core.** The Township's arts, culture and heritage facility at 23430 Mavis Avenue — developed in partnership with the Katzie, Kwantlen, Matsqui and Semiahmoo First Nations — now houses both the Fort Langley Community Library and the museum, a short walk from the Bedford Landing condos. https://www.tol.ca/en/centre-information.aspx
- **Flood protection is being upgraded, and it is a Township project, not a strata one.** The Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for Fort Langley, with flood box gates that close as Fraser River levels rise, and Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for pump station upgrades. Ask what phase that work is in when you buy — it affects the village, not your building's own systems. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
- **Bedford Landing is a mill site turned neighbourhood.** The community was built on the grounds of the McDonald Cedar Mill, which opened in 1921 on an 88-acre riverfront property, and its restored pump house and replicated boom-crane wharf remain on the waterfront; the Fort-to-Fort Trail runs about 8 km round trip through Bedford Landing along the river. That history is why the condo buildings sit in a planned riverside enclave rather than on the Glover Road grid. https://hexaflexagon-salmon-whfg.squarespace.com/bedford-landing
- **A March 2026 Township SSMUH step touches Fort Langley specifically.** The Township's March 9, 2026 council report on an SSMUH development permit area names the Fort Langley Community Plan among the plans Bylaw No. 6116 would amend — meaning the form-and-character rules for new small-scale multi-unit housing in the village were still being written in 2026, inside the existing heritage framework. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
