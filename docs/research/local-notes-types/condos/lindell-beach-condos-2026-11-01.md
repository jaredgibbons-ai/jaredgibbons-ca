## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase in a bare-land strata (Creekside Mills, The Cottages, Aquadel if strata on title) the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a bare-land strata the Form B and the strata plan — not a building inspection alone — are how a buyer learns what the corporation owns and maintains (roads, water/sewer infrastructure, amenities). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older bare-land strata bylaw purporting to restrict long-term rentals is void as a restriction. Short-term rentals are a different, live gate here (see the STR bullet below). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023, per Strata Property Regulation 7.01) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Lindell Beach strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw, and any "45+" label in a listing is not a valid strata age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Governing plan and zoning (FVRD, not Chilliwack):** the plan is FVRD OCP Bylaw No. 1115, 2011 for Electoral Areas E and H — titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," so Lindell Beach is covered by name — as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019; under its designations the only residential use permitted is single-family, and its resort-residential policies (§5.9) calculate permitted sites/strata lots/units on "useable land." Zoning outside Cultus Lake Park is FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 (approved October 27, 2022, consolidating nine earlier bylaws); Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 applies only inside the Park. One zone is confirmed from a primary/developer source: PRD-1, Private Resort Residential Development (Creekside Mills). Zone and minimum parcel size for the older beach-strip lots: UNVERIFIED — check per address in Bylaw 1638 / the FVRD webmap. **No Chilliwack bylaw number governs this ground, and none is borrowed here.** https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/consolidated-zoning-bylaw-approved.html?media=contrast (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **Short-term rentals:** the FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H (Lindell Beach) to proactively address short-term vacation rentals and RV use for accommodation. The mechanism is zoning: Bylaw 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use except where a zone expressly permits it. Provincially, hosts have needed a registration number since May 1, 2025; electoral areas are exempt from the provincial principal-residence rule unless the board opts in (no opt-in found for Area H — UNVERIFIED). A seller's Airbnb history is not proof a buyer can continue it. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22845 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **Flood / alluvial-fan layer:** FVRD Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022 applies region-wide except municipalities and Park lands — so it applies here — and requires alluvial-fan development to meet the flood construction level/setback set by a site-specific geohazard assessment by a Professional Engineer/Geoscientist. The FVRD's Frosst Creek works (two debris basins and two dikes, south and north sides of the creek, partly on private property) protect the community on the Frosst Creek alluvial fan at the lake's south end. Property-specific hazard reports: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/flood-protection.html?media=contrast (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **Rural taxes (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11):** Lindell Beach properties pay rural (provincial) property taxes plus FVRD service parcel taxes. Rural taxes are due and payable July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on October 31. The South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax (service area established by Bylaw No. 1416, 2017; parcel tax by Bylaw No. 1497, 2018; Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025) is the annual requisition divided equally among parcels on the roll, and south-shore water/sewer are tax-funded with no separate utility bill (Section 1). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (Any page stating "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% — that compression is wrong as a rate statement.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given here because no verified Lindell Beach median exists to use as an input. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home parks (the Holiday Resort / park discussion):** park tenancies run on park rules and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, not strata bylaws; annual pad-rent increases are capped at inflation plus a proportional amount for specified cost increases — 2.3% + proportional for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file. A park's age rule must be verified from the park itself in writing — a listing adjective is not verification (the Cultus Lake Village conflict in Section 2 is the standing example).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The north side of the lake got its sewage upgrade first (2025).** The FVRD's North Cultus wastewater plant was commissioned in April 2025 (construction July 2023, substantial completion December 2024) under the Liquid Waste Management Plan (approved 2019), producing Class A effluent with phosphorus removal via rapid infiltration basins — while the south shore (Lindell Beach) remains on the South Cultus Lake Sewage Treatment service, funded by its equal-share parcel tax (Bylaw 1497, as amended by 1779, 2025). https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/wwtp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **Lindell Beach is named in its own OCP.** OCP Bylaw No. 1115, 2011 is titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley" — the community is small, but it is a named planning community of Electoral Area H, not a Chilliwack neighbourhood that drifted south. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html
- **The Holiday Resort's tenure was settled in court, not in a brochure.** The leading public record for Lindell Beach's biggest complex is a 2009 BC Court of Appeal decision (2009 BCCA 5) describing leased pads and owner-placed trailers — a reminder that resort "ownership" at the lake can mean owning a trailer and renting the ground under it. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm
- **Area H is growing fast — on paper, at least (2021 Census).** Electoral Area H grew 33.1% between 2016 and 2021 (1,847 → 2,459 people), the context behind the new strata cottage communities at the lake's south end — none of which adopted an age restriction in any source checked. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/Page.cfm?lang=e&topic=9&dguid=2021S0503930
- **"Chilliwack" on the envelope, FVRD at the counter.** Building permits, zoning enforcement and services for a Lindell Beach address all run through the FVRD; Chilliwack City Hall has no jurisdiction, even though the mailing address, the school district (SD33) and the MLS board (CADREB) all say Chilliwack. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/About~the~FVRD/Documents/Strategic~Plans/2020%203rd%20Quarter%20Update.pdf
- **South-shore services carry no utility bill — the north shore's does.** The FVRD's Area H 2026 rates show Water – Cultus Lake and Sewer – Cultus Lake South as tax-funded ("No Utility Billing"), while Sewer – Cultus Lake North carries a $1,369.00 flat rate for 2026 plus taxes — two sides of the same lake on two different billing models. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
