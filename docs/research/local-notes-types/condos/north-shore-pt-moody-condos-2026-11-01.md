## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It is the governing document for any townhouse-strata purchase here (for example Sentinel Hill) as much as for an apartment purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a north shore strata is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No north shore building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Moody's current Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 2026, No. 3522** (adopted April 28, 2026; it repeals OCP Bylaw No. 2955 — never cite 2955 as current). Chapter 14 covers the north shore in Section 14.2 (Pleasantside and April Road), Section 14.3 (the Heritage Mountain grouping) and Section 14.4 (Ioco Lands, the former Imperial Oil lands). The Zoning Bylaw is Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937: RS zones for single-detached housing; RT and RM zones for multi-residential forms. Small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside transit-oriented areas: up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops (Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460). Small-lot subdivision from RS1 to RS1-S (minimum 9 m width and 325 m² lot area) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and **Pleasantside** where the OCP designates Single Family–Low Density — Pleasantside is the one north shore area on that list. Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached zones except manufactured-home parks, and a secondary suite may not be stratified. The Ioco townsite is a heritage conservation area (heritage alteration permit may be required). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/y10lcutj/port-moody-official-community-plan-bylaw-2026-no-3522.pdf and https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-shore-pt-moody.md)
- **Port Moody property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty after September 15, 2026. Utility fees are billed separately; the 2026 utility bill was due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/en/business-and-development/utilities.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/north-shore-pt-moody.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the north shore's verified stock is resale stock, and no in-area presale was verified. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $715,000 (the Port Moody community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no North Shore median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,300 (2% × $515,000) = **$12,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (not applicable to this area's resale stock). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on the north shore was verified in this program — the one live file, 38 Shoreline Circle, is at development-application stage (spring 2026 submission, hearing anticipated spring 2027), not a selling presale; for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for North Shore Pt Moody: no in-area presale verified. https://www.38shorelinecircle.ca/ and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
