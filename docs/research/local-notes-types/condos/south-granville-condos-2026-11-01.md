## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Granville is the Granville Street corridor roughly between West 5th and West 16th Avenues, spanning the Fairview and Shaughnessy local areas; the South Granville Business Improvement Association district runs along Granville Street from the Granville Bridge to 16th Avenue, branded "Gallery Row" for its concentration of art galleries. It is a recognised smaller area but not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: on the residential side streets west and east of Granville — West 6th, West 8th and West 14th Avenues between Burrard/Fir and Oak — not on the Granville Street retail frontage itself; the same side streets hold a large stock of purpose-built rental apartment buildings, which is this corridor's defining trap. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/granville-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- Landmarks: the Stanley Theatre at 2750 Granville Street (650 seats, opened 1931 as a cinema and vaudeville house; its restoration received a 1999 City of Vancouver Heritage Award), Granville Park at 3001 Fir Street, Granville Loop Park at 1435 West 5th Avenue, and Vancouver General Hospital at 899 West 12th Avenue a few blocks east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)
- Transit: the corridor is bus-served today, including the 99 B-Line along Broadway; the Broadway Subway — the 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six stations including South Granville Station — is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 GVR report contains no City of Vancouver community sections) — no median, inventory, sales-ratio or days-on-market figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this corridor the Form B is also the fastest way to prove a building is a strata at all — the rental towers restated in Section 2 have no Form B to give. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Landmark Sunset's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law — and any directory line anywhere in this corridor saying rentals are not allowed would be void for long-term tenancies. Note the reverse trap: a rental building is not made buyable by this rule — there is nothing to buy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No corridor building counted in the sibling file is published as 55+ — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. (Terraces on 7th, at the area's edge, is a published 55+ building — but it is Fairview's file, not this corridor's count.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575; side-street house lots sit under the citywide R1-1 multiplex regime created by By-law No. 13817 (up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on former single-family lots), and a corridor strata example, The Avedon, is recorded in its listing as zoned RM-3. The Broadway Plan covers Clark Drive to Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, was approved June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for a South Granville Station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station — but the Plan is policy, not zoning, and most higher-density forms still require rezoning. Citywide direction otherwise comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md) and (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/granville-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%):** the City requires every owner to submit an annual property status declaration; properties declared or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026. Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live in the corridor for FORM by Anthem (2026, counted in the sibling file). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any remaining developer sales at FORM and for future Broadway Plan presales. Named presale projects in this research file's pass: UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
