The Northwest Burke Vision area is bordered by those three neighbourhoods to the south and Pinecone Burke Provincial Park to the north and east; full sub-area boundary lines from City plan maps are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- Main streets: David Avenue and Coast Meridian Road serve Burke Mountain, with Harper Road off Coast Meridian Road giving primary access to Pinecone Burke Provincial Park. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: it does not concentrate as apartment condos at all — see Section 2. SnapStats publishes the two forms combined as "Condos & Townhomes," so the apartment share of that count is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This applies to every Burke Mountain townhouse strata in Section 2 exactly as it does to an apartment condo. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The Forester directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law; any Burke Mountain directory line stating the opposite would reflect old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Burke Mountain complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Coquitlam zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000 (1996) — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² (18.5 m width) for R-1 and R-3 and 465 m² (12.5 m width) for R-2. The City's current Official Community Plan framework is the Interim OCP — Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP Bylaw 3479; do not cite 3479 as current. The Burke Mountain neighbourhood plans (Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek, Partington Creek) and the Northwest Burke Vision sit under that framework. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **SSMUH as adopted in Coquitlam:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **Watercourse permit area:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam (30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam); Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks and s.523 covers riparian areas — a real constraint on creek-edge townhouse complexes' capital planning. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a developer sale at Rocklin), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Rocklin or other Burke Mountain presale townhouse projects. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)

- **The mountain has been a planned growth area for three decades.** Burke Mountain has been defined in City plans as an area for population growth since the early 1990s (Evergreen Line growth areas identified in 1993, further growth areas in 2000–2001), with the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek plans created to guide that growth — which is why the stock is uniformly new-plan townhouse and detached form. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **The nearest hospital is across the city line.** Eagle Ridge Hospital, 475 Guildford Way, Port Moody, on the Port Moody / Coquitlam border, is the nearest hospital for this area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/burke-mountain.md)
- **Coquitlam rewrote its OCP in 2026.** The Interim OCP (Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026) superseded OCP Bylaw 3479 — feature sheets and older guides citing 3479 for a Burke Mountain property are citing a repealed plan.
