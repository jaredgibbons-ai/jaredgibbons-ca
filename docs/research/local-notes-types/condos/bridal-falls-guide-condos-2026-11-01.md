## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a bare-land strata lot at 53480 Bridal Falls Road this package — not the resort's marketing — is what establishes the fees, what they cover, and the registered pet, age and use rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. A bare-land strata resort with a clubhouse, pool and serviced lots carries exactly the kind of common-property assets a depreciation report prices — ask for the current one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, so no long-term rental-restriction bylaw in the 53480 Bridal Falls Road strata can be enforced, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the registered bylaws on the Form B package are the document that settles what applies to a specific lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw for the 53480 Bridal Falls Road strata is established on a source opened in this program: UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read — and the neighbouring Mt Cheam park is all-ages in any case (Section 2), under park rules, not the SPA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **FVRD plan and zoning — bylaw numbers UNVERIFIED, stated as such:** the guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) Official Community Plan, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, was adopted by the FVRD Board in January 2021 after a public hearing on December 15, 2020 — the bylaw text was not opened on an FVRD source in this program, so the number and adoption details are UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record and are not asserted here. The FVRD zoning bylaw for Electoral Area D and the zone of any specific Bridal Falls parcel are likewise UNVERIFIED in this file; listing records for 53480 Bridal Falls Road state zoning CD1 for that address. Chilliwack's OCP and zoning bylaws do NOT apply in Bridal Falls — do not borrow them across the municipal line. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/bridal-falls-guide.md) https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3103511-53480-bridal-falls-road-86-chilliwack-bc-v0x-1x1/brochure
- **RURAL property taxes govern here — the staged provincial regime, not a city regime:** Bridal Falls is unincorporated, so property taxes are levied under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act: taxes in a notice mailed on or before May 31 are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to taxes unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to taxes still unpaid on October 31 (s. 11). For contrast only — explicitly NON-governing — the City of Chilliwack's second penalty stage falls after August 4, 2026, and the City of Mission's regime is a flat 10% after July 2; neither applies in Electoral Area D. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **ALR frame:** on ALR land the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Bridal Falls property: UNVERIFIED — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA), for the park product nearby:** pad-rent increases in a manufactured home park are capped at 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. That regime applies to pad tenancies like Mt Cheam's (Section 2) — it does NOT apply to the freehold strata lots at 53480 Bridal Falls Road, which have strata fees instead of pad rent. No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Whether GST applies to a specific cottage-and-lot purchase at 53480 Bridal Falls Road depends on whether the cottage is new construction — a per-deal check, UNCONFIRMED for any specific unit here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Bridal Falls is named in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
