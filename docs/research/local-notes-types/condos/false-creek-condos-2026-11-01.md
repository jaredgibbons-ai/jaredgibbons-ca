## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- False Creek is the inlet between the downtown peninsula and the south-shore neighbourhoods. False Creek South is located between the Cambie and Burrard Street bridges on the south shore, excluding Granville Island and Sen̓áḵw, as the City states. The City's Coastal Adaptation Plan states False Creek North includes portions of Northeast False Creek, Yaletown and Chinatown, and False Creek South passes through Fairview, Southeast False Creek, Olympic Village and the False Creek Flats along the south shore. ### Excluded in aggregate — leasehold, co-op and non-market stock (False Creek South)

- **False Creek South leasehold stock — excluded, not counted.** The City holds title to 80 acres of land in False Creek South; when the neighbourhood was built in the 1970s and 1980s, much of the City land was leased to tenants via 60-year ground leases, with most of the leases expiring in the next 15 to 25 years, and no new housing has been built in False Creek South since the 1980s. The stock on that land — leasehold strata, co-operatives and non-market housing — is excluded from this file's counts in aggregate on those City figures, because the lease terms and end date for any specific building are UNVERIFIED and must be read off that building's own lease documents. This is the classic City of Vancouver prepaid 99-year / long ground-lease pattern in its 60-year False Creek South form: the buyer does not own the land, and remaining lease term drives financing and price. Any False Creek South purchase starts with the ground lease, not the suite. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. Around False Creek the Form B is also where title must be checked first: freehold strata (the counted Southeast False Creek stock) versus leasehold (False Creek South) is the fork the whole purchase turns on. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every counted building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by a strata, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing — the two layers are distinct, and the City licence specifics for a given building: UNCONFIRMED in this pass. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is void as a long-term ban. Note the boundary of this rule: it governs strata bylaws on freehold strata lots — it does not cure a False Creek South ground lease, whose terms are a separate document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Land tenure — False Creek South:** the City holds title to 80 acres in False Creek South, leased under 60-year ground leases with most expiring in the next 15 to 25 years. In March 2024 Council directed staff to report back with an updated and enhanced False Creek South development framework addressing current housing pressures, and in summer 2025 the City conducted technical investigations in the neighbourhood to support a future planning process. Leasehold pricing, lender appetite and remaining term are building-specific and UNVERIFIED for any individual building. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **City zoning and plans:** there is no single district schedule for False Creek under Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — the Southeast False Creek / Olympic Village lands and the False Creek North lands sit under site-specific CD-1 Comprehensive Development districts and Official Development Plans, including the False Creek North Official Development Plan; FM-1 applies on the Fairview Slopes above the south shore, permitting single detached houses, duplexes and multiple dwellings with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60. The Northeast False Creek Plan proposes a mixed-use neighbourhood with replacement of the Georgia and Dunsmuir viaducts by an at-grade street network. The zone for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **Floodplain — the defining risk file for this sub-area:** the lands around False Creek are inside the City's designated floodplain system; the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan covers the floodplain area encompassing False Creek North, False Creek South, Granville Island and the Flats, and parcels in the floodplain are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels. The Northeast False Creek Plan requires new developments to be built to protect homes and critical infrastructure from flooding and rising sea levels, with a continuous line of flood protection across that site. The flood construction level for a specific building is a core due-diligence item on both shores; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. Note for leasehold buildings: City property tax mechanics on City-held ground-lease land follow the lease — how taxes are billed for a specific False Creek South building: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the counted False Creek stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current False Creek presale; a specific current presale around False Creek verified on a developer source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a published range of 0.6%–4.5%. Separately, leasehold tenure (False Creek South) and floodplain position can affect a lender's view of a specific property — no source checked publishes a building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **False Creek South has had no new housing since the 1980s — on purpose-built leasehold land.** The City transformed industrial land into the waterfront neighbourhood in the 1970s and 1980s on its own 80 acres, leased out on 60-year ground leases; the March 2024 Council direction for an updated and enhanced False Creek South development framework, and the City's summer 2025 technical investigations in the neighbourhood, are the live file that will shape what happens as those leases run down. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **The Olympic Village buildings were athletes' housing first.** The Southeast False Creek site was the Vancouver Olympic Village for the 2010 Winter Games — the buildings were turned over to VANOC on November 1, 2009 and returned to the City on March 31, 2010, after which they provided approximately 1,000 residential units including 250 affordable housing units, a community centre, daycare and retail space. That history is why market condos and non-market housing sit inside one development under one name. https://www.gamesbids.com/forums/topic/3992-southeast-false-creek-development-olympic-village/?do=findComment&comment=110322
- **A new St. Paul's Hospital is planned at the east end.** The new St. Paul's Hospital campus is planned at Station Street in the False Creek Flats — the current hospital, at 1081 Burrard Street, is across the inlet in Downtown, and Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, sits south of the south shore in Fairview. Neither existing hospital is on a False Creek shoreline. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **The viaducts are planned to come down.** The Northeast False Creek Plan proposes replacing the Georgia and Dunsmuir viaducts — which currently bisect the north-east shore area — with an at-grade street network, alongside new parks, plazas and a cultural centre; north-shore buildings near Expo and Pacific Boulevards price against that plan, not just the current road pattern. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **One shore's school is not the other shore's school.** Crosstown and Elsie Roy elementaries serve the north shore side and False Creek Elementary the south shore side, and catchments are address-based and differ by shore — a listing that borrows the wrong shore's school is wrong even though both are "False Creek." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
- **False Creek was an industrial centre first.** The City's Coastal Adaptation Plan states False Creek was historically one of Vancouver's major industrial centres and has transitioned into an area of higher-density housing, commercial areas and parks — the reason the shoreline stock is almost entirely strata form built in planned phases (1970s–80s leasehold south, 2010 Olympic Village south-east, CD-1 towers north), with no detached shoreline stock at all. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/false-creek.md)
