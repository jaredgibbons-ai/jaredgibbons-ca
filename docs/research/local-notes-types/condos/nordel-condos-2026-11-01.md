# Nordel — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Nordel is in North Delta, the part of the City of Delta north of Burns Bog and south of the Fraser River; the City publishes no street-by-street written boundary, and the ND12 Nordel Social Heart Development Permit Area in the 2024 OCP is centred on 84 Avenue and 112 Street. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) the 84 Avenue corridor at the Social Heart — Delta Gardens at 11501/11507 84 Avenue, beside the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue; (2) the Sunstone pocket at Nordel's south side — The Residences at Sunstone Village at 8360 Delsom Way, the only apartment condos inside the Sunstone master-planned community. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Nordel holds North Delta's civic core: North Delta Recreation Centre (11415 84 Avenue), George Mackie Library (8440 112th Street), North Delta Community Park (11311 84 Avenue) and the North Delta Public Health Unit (11245 84 Avenue) are all in Nordel. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/north-delta-recreation-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations
- Market context: Nordel's SnapStats sub-area row for Condos & Townhomes in September 2026 was Inventory 12, Solds 2, Sales Ratio 17% — a combined condo-and-townhome figure, not condos alone (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/nordel.md).
- Transit: route 312 runs Scottsdale Exchange to Scott Road Station via 112 Street, and route 391 runs Scottsdale Exchange to Scott Road Station via 116 Street, both with stops at Nordel Way and both connecting to the Expo Line at Scott Road Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Both verified Nordel apartment buildings are 2019–2020 builds, so their reports are about warranty-expiry and envelope planning rather than end-of-life systems — the townhome collections from 2009–2017 sit in the same mandatory cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any older directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Nordel complex reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Note the distinction that matters in Sunstone: the 70-unit Sunstone rental apartments are a 55+ rental community by operation, which is a tenancy matter — it does not make the neighbouring strata condos age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **City zoning and plans:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 applies parcel-by-parcel, under the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 — the current OCP has no lettered area-plan schedules (the North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950). The ND12 Nordel Social Heart Development Permit Area (Appendix B, Bylaw No. 8400) plans mixed residential development around 84 Avenue and 112 Street, which is the policy frame for the Delta Gardens node. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) permit small-scale multi-unit housing on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide — relevant to Nordel's house lots, not to its strata buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both verified Nordel buildings are resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Federal savings vehicles and mortgage insurance:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. CMHC published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Delta Gardens in Nordel, Scottsdale or Annieville?** Listings use all three names for the same complex. Geographically it stands at 84 Avenue and 115 Street, at the Nordel Social Heart beside the recreation centre, library and public health unit — this file and the research file treat it as Nordel, and buyers should search all three sub-area names or they will miss listings. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/north-delta-recreation-centre (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/nordel.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Nordel condo?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you, and the registered bylaws should be read on the Form B package regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Delta Gardens or Sunstone Village Residences?** UNCONFIRMED from public sources for both apartment buildings — neither building's pet bylaw was published in the sources checked, so the Form B and registered bylaws govern. (The directory does record the separate Sunstone townhome complex at 8355 Delsom Way as pets allowed.) http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunstone-8355-delsom-way
- **Q: Is Sunstone a seniors' community?** Only part of it: the Sunstone rental apartments are a 70-unit independent-living community for residents 55+ (at least one resident over 55), opened in 2020 per the operator listing — that is rental product beside the strata condos and townhomes, not an age bylaw on them. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: What does the Sunstone amenity package actually rest on?** A shared club structure: a Kaleido townhome listing describes exclusive access to the 12,000 sq ft Sunstone Club with outdoor pool, gym, basketball court and lounge — part of a Nordel strata fee in Sunstone buys into that shared amenity structure rather than an in-building gym alone. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/10735-84-avenue/31
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 2020 building?** Lower system-age risk than a 1970s building, but the check is the same: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots, 30-year projection) plus the CRF balance on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve an 84 Avenue condo?** Catchments are address-based: the research file's locator run for 11415 84 Avenue returned Richardson Elementary (K–7), 11339 83 Avenue, and North Delta Secondary (8–12), 11447 82 Avenue. A Sunstone (Delsom) address can differ — one Sunstone townhome listing names Gray Elementary and Sands Secondary as its catchment — so run the exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3116956-51-10489-delsom-crescent-delta-british-columbia-v4c0b9-859800/brochure
- **Q: Is more condo supply coming to the Social Heart?** Two applications were active in the research: file LU009689 proposes an apartment and townhouse project at 112 Street and 82 Avenue, and file LU009611 proposes a mixed-use residential and commercial building at 112 Street and 84 Avenue, both inside the ND12 permit area. These are applications, not built stock. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7117/7676/6583/9e2d0d28ed6635b4006024a144519a9d_LU009689_PIM_Boards_V3.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/1417/7677/3212/0bebff4ffb96dacf3a0a2d7b7545400a_LU009611_PIM_Boards_V3.pdf
- **Q: What is parking like at the 84 Avenue buildings?** It varies by unit, not by building name: one Delta Gardens apartment listing notes two side-by-side stalls for that unit, and a Delta Gardens townhome listing notes secured underground parking. The stall legally attached to a unit is stated on the Form B — confirm there. https://vancouvernashdom.com/north-delta-scottsdale-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Sunstone's condos were marketed as a one-off:** the 2016 launch report states the 54 Sunstone Village Residences condominiums "are the first and will be the only condominiums available in all of Sunstone" — everything else in the master plan is townhomes, single-family homes by Polygon/Morningstar, or the 70 rental apartments. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/sunstone-at-10500-delsom-crescent-delta-by-delsom-developments
- **The Sunstone land play is much bigger than the condo building:** a LoopNet overview describes Delsom Estates as a 100-acre master-planned community of eight multi-family developments with 650–700 multi-family units and 178 single-family homes, with Sunstone Village at its entrance at Delsom Way, Delsom Crescent and 84th Avenue. https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/10727-Delsom-Cres-Delta-BC/33040875/
- **Delta Gardens is two products under one name:** apartment units on 84 Avenue and ground-level townhomes on 115 Street share the Delta Gardens name and the 2020 build year in listings — a search filtered to "condos" alone will silently drop half the complex. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/8447-115-street/1
- **The Social Heart is a permit area, not just a nickname:** ND12's stated objective in the 2024 OCP is to revitalize Nordel as the community focus for North Delta, and the two active applications at 112 Street (82 Avenue and 84 Avenue) sit inside it — the blocks around Delta Gardens are the designated intensification core for all of North Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Nordel's west edge is a protected bog:** Delta Nature Reserve access is at 10388 Nordel Court — the publicly accessible part of Burns Bog, protected as the Burns Bog Ecological Conservancy Area — which caps development on that edge and is the green backdrop for the area's west-side streets. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve
