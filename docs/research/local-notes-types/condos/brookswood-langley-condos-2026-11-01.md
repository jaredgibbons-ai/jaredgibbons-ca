## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Brookswood this document route applies to townhouse strata such as Bromley once it completes — it does NOT apply to the pad-rental parks in Section 2, which have no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. That voiding does not touch the parks in Section 2: a park's "rentals not allowed" line is a park rule under a tenancy regime, not a strata bylaw, and it is not voided by the strata change. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — the Brookswood parks' 55+ labels are park rules under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy frame, not SPA bylaws, and the strata exemptions do not automatically carry into a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township plans and zoning:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers the area (OCP Bylaw 1979 No. 1842 as amended for this area by Amendment Bylaw 2017 No. 5300, adopted October 23, 2017), with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. Base zoning is Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500: Section 110.1 as read in Township council reports sets SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² (40 m frontage), SR-2 minimum 8,094 m² (45 m) and SR-3 minimum 1,765 m² (27.45 m); RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brookswood-langley.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brookswood-langley-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020:** the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments under Zoning Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allow 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the SSMUH tiers. **There is NO 6-plex / 6-unit tier in the Township of Langley.** The Brookswood DEVELOPMENT guide page's 6-plex claim is therefore WRONG and is flagged here as a defect of that development-guide page; the Brookswood area guide page makes no such claim, and no defect is recorded against the area guide. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Township):** the Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 definition); a building permit is required for all suites and an annual secondary suite infrastructure fee is charged on property taxes. A coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 — it is never strata product and never condo stock. (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/brookswood-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brookswood-langley.md)
- **Pad rent (parks):** where a Brookswood purchase is a park home, pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act: the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months; on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same. No pad-rent dollar figure for any Brookswood park is published in this file — no official or operator source states one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **ALR:** much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; whether a specific Brookswood parcel is in the ALR or a floodplain is UNVERIFIED per parcel — the Township directs applicants to check designations in GeoSource. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is one structural reason ALR parcels here never become condo stock. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brookswood-langley.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant in Brookswood to developer sales at Bromley, not to resale townhouses or park homes. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $611,000 (the combined Langley community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is NOT a Brookswood figure, because no Brookswood median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $8,220 (2% × $411,000) = **$10,220 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** Bromley is selling as a presale with Phase 1 completion estimated Summer 2027 on the developer's sheet; REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
