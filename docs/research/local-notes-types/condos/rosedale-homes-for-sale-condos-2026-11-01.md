## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B (for strata stock generally — Rosedale has none verified):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to a freehold house or acreage parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata stock generally):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. That voiding does not create a right to rent out a home in a pad-rent park: Mt Cheam's "no rentals" position is a park rule / tenancy matter, not a strata bylaw, and it is not voided by the strata amendment. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Plans and zoning — the honest position:** Rosedale parcels sit in FVRD Electoral Area D, and which FVRD Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw govern a specific parcel, and their bylaw numbers, are UNVERIFIED in this pass — confirm with the FVRD for the parcel. For contrast only, and NOT governing Rosedale: the City of Chilliwack's plan is OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" and its zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — those bylaws stop at the city boundary. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/rosedale-homes-for-sale.md)
- **ALR frame for acreage parcels:** any Rosedale parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC housing frame — one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — and ALR residences cannot be stratified: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Whether a specific Rosedale parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/rosedale-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Property taxes — rural, not City:** Rosedale is unincorporated, so City of Chilliwack tax dates do NOT apply. The rural / FVRD tax notice, due date and penalty schedule for a Rosedale parcel: UNVERIFIED in this pass — no Province rural-tax or FVRD tax source was opened. For contrast only (the City's schedule, which does not govern Rosedale): City of Chilliwack notices are due the first working day after July 1, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/rosedale-homes-for-sale.md)

- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is stated here because no verified Rosedale price figure exists in the permitted sources to use as an input — any example would invent its input. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on Rosedale ground was confirmed in the research; where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA) — directly relevant at Mt Cheam:** a manufactured-home park tenancy is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act; pad-rent increases are limited to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. A buyer at Mt Cheam owns the home and rents the pad — there is no strata lot, no Form B and no CRF. (Program record: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/chilliwack-mobile-homes-condos-2026-11-01.md)
