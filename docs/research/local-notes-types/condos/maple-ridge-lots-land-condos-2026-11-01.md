## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

On a land page, the strata rules that matter are the ones that decide whether strata product can be created on the ground at all, plus the standard purchase rules that will apply once it is.

- **Why strata product IS coming to some of this ground — the adopted corridor plans:** the Lougheed Transit Corridor Plan was ADOPTED on January 27, 2026 — Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted at that meeting, after a January 20, 2026 public hearing (City news release February 4, 2026). The corridor runs from the city's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, and it is the plan frame for transit-oriented strata development on the city's west-side and Town Centre land. An earlier research pass in this program read the January meeting differently; the City's Engage record governs. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Albion Area Plan:** adopted October 28, 2025 — the plan frame for the east-side growth ground in this feed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **City zoning — the scale gate:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. Subdivision minimums: RS-1 — 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%; RS-2 — 0.4 ha; RS-3 — 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text; A-1 — 2.0 ha; A-2 — 4.0 ha; A-3 — 8.0 ha. The same "land" search spans two completely different products: a serviced RS-1 infill lot and an 8-hectare agricultural parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **City plan citation — CONTESTED:** the research files cite the Official Community Plan both as Bylaw 7060-2014 and as Bylaw 7998-2024 (adopted June 25, 2024, program-verified in the batch-12 files), with an OCP update also recorded. Do not treat either number as settled; quote a parcel's designation from a current City document. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **SSMUH — small strata-scale product without a rezoning:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. SSMUH units are how serviced Maple Ridge lots add gentle density; whether a multi-unit SSMUH project is held as strata, rental or freehold lots is a project-level choice, not stated for any feed parcel. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Why strata product is NOT coming to ALR ground:** residences on Agricultural Land Reserve land cannot be stratified — ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6, Strata Property Act s. 239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1). Subdivision of ALR land also requires Agricultural Land Commission permission apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation. The ALC housing frame on ALR parcels is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (a secondary suite only inside it) plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Which feed parcels are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Thornhill Urban Reserve — a hold, not a timeline:** the OCP's Urban Reserve designation identifies lands held for long-term future urban services and housing, and OCP policy states development will not happen until specific triggers and milestones have been attained. The live page's specifics (hectarage, section number, population trigger) were NOT confirmed in the OCP chapter read in this program: UNVERIFIED. Do not price a Thornhill parcel on a subdivision or strata date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Floodplain:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Servicing:** whether a specific lot has municipal water, sanitary sewer and storm connections at the lot line — or needs a well and an onsite sewage system under the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person — is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED per parcel. Strata development economics turn on this answer. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Development cost charges for Maple Ridge: UNVERIFIED** in the research pass — no DCC bylaw or rate page was opened and no DCC figure is stated here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Form B (applies the moment the product is strata):** on a resale purchase — including a bare-land strata lot — the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59), reviewed for the depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (for any strata created or bought):** effective July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental and age bylaws (for any strata created or bought):** rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted), and the only legal age bylaw is 55+ under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes — carrying cost on a held lot. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction; the live page's GST position on vacant-land sales (and its lender down-payment ranges) is UNVERIFIED in the research file — no CRA or lender source was opened for it, and it is not restated as fact here. Confirm GST treatment on a land purchase with a CPA or lawyer. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame that will govern any strata product pre-sold off this ground. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the market numbers for this page land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the Section 1 figures are Maple Ridge residential context only, and land-specific statistics (listing counts, medians, price per acre) are UNVERIFIED — not published in the permitted source. Price a lot on its own zone, services and comparable land sales. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I stratify a property I build on a Maple Ridge lot?** On ordinary serviced land, strata subdivision is a City approval path governed by the parcel's zone and designation under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 — confirm with the City for the exact parcel. On ALR land, no: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and ALR subdivision itself needs Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create?** It depends on the zone: RS-1 requires 668.0 m² (18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth), RS-2 requires 0.4 ha, RS-3 requires 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text, and the agricultural zones run from A-1 at 2.0 ha to A-3 at 8.0 ha. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Where is new condo product most likely to appear?** On the ground the adopted plans designate for it: the Lougheed Transit Corridor (adopted January 27, 2026, western boundary to 221 Street) and the Town Centre / East Central–West Central redevelopment corridors named in the page's own copy — where the existing attached shelf already concentrates (East Central 120 attached listings and West Central 78 in September 2026, against 0 in Websters Corners and Whonnock). https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I subdivide land in the Thornhill Urban Reserve now?** The OCP holds Urban Reserve land for long-term future urban services and housing until specific triggers and milestones are attained; the live page's specific figures for it are UNVERIFIED in this file. Treat a Thornhill Reserve parcel as a hold, not a development timeline. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I build a second home on a rural lot?** On ALR land, at most one additional detached residence under the ALC frame where conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — with the principal residence capped at 500 m² and any suite inside it; City bylaws must also permit it. Off-reserve lots follow the City's suite and SSMUH rules instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How many units can a serviced house lot hold without a rezoning?** One secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain area), with SSMUH paths to three, four or six units on eligible single-detached and duplex lots — eligibility is a parcel-level check with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same purchase as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot sits inside a strata corporation: there is a Form B, strata fees, bylaws (only a 55+ age bylaw is legal; rental bylaws are void), a CRF and — for stratas with 5+ lots — a five-year depreciation report cycle. A freehold lot has none of that structure. Whether any current feed parcel is bare-land strata is UNVERIFIED — check title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is serviced?** Servicing is a parcel-level check with the City — whether municipal water, sanitary sewer and storm connections are at the lot line, or whether the lot needs a well and a Sewerage System Regulation onsite sewage system installed by an Authorized Person. No feed parcel's servicing is verified in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a vacant lot?** The research file does not verify the live page's GST summary and restates none of it — GST treatment turns on the seller's status and the buyer's intended use, so confirm it with a CPA or lawyer before writing, and do not rely on a listing page's summary. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/maple-ridge-lots-land.md)
