## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Two jurisdictions share this one board sub-area: (1) the UEL, an unincorporated community between the City of Vancouver and UBC on the Point Grey Peninsula, administered directly by the Province under the University Endowment Land Act with no elected municipal council, in Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A; and (2) UBC's own residential neighbourhoods on campus lands. Day-to-day UEL services are delivered by the UEL Administration, guided by the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- The UEL Administration's realtor information sheet states the UEL is not part of the City of Vancouver or UBC, has a population of nearly 4,000, and that water billing is not included on the UEL property tax bill. https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- UBC's residential neighbourhoods served by the University Neighbourhoods Association (UNA) are Chancellor Place, East Campus, Hampton Place, Hawthorn Place and Wesbrook Place (Wesbrook Village), home to roughly 15,000 residents in total. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- Tenure by side: the UEL's older residential streets are largely freehold lots, while UBC's neighbourhoods (Wesbrook Village, Hampton Place, Chancellor Place, Hawthorn Place) are built on 99-year prepaid leasehold — UBC Campus and Community Planning states residential properties on UBC's endowment lands are never sold outright but are leased as prepaid 99-year leases. The remaining lease term runs from each project's original lease date, not from the buyer's purchase date. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf (Research file, Sections 4–5)
- Where condo stock concentrates: Wesbrook Village, the village centre south of West 16th Avenue on Wesbrook Mall and around Birney Avenue / Webber Lane, holds the counted buildings in Section 2; Hampton Place (1990s, UBC's first campus neighbourhood) and the other campus neighbourhoods hold further leasehold strata stock in condo and townhouse form (Section 2). https://ubcproperties.com/history/ (Research file, Section 5)
- Market figures: none quoted. The SnapStats reports held contain no Vancouver/UEL coverage, so all market figures for this area are UNVERIFIED and no figures are stated here. (Research file, Section 2)
- Pacific Spirit Regional Park wraps around the campus and the UEL: roughly 860–874 hectares of forest with over 55 km of trails, operated by Metro Vancouver. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/pacific-spirit-regional-park
- Transit is bus-based: the UBC Exchange is a major TransLink bus hub, and the R4 41st Ave RapidBus runs from UBC Exchange to Joyce–Collingwood Station. The Broadway Subway's Arbutus terminus is interim — TransLink notes a future phase of rapid transit could extend to UBC's Point Grey campus, with bus service continuing between Arbutus and UBC in the meantime. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/broadway-subway-project
- Schools (SD39, Vancouver School Board): University Hill Elementary, 5395 Chancellor Boulevard, a kindergarten-to-grade-7 school of about 385 students; University Hill Secondary, 3228 Ross Drive, where it moved in 2013; and Norma Rose Point School, 5488 Ortona Road, kindergarten to grade 7, with 770-plus students. Locator results run for this program: 5395 Chancellor Blvd returns University Hill Elementary / University Hill Secondary, and 5488 Ortona Rd returns Norma Rose Point / University Hill Secondary; the elementary boundary line for other addresses is UNVERIFIED. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/university-hill-elementary/about-us and https://www.vsb.bc.ca/norma-rose-point-school/about-us (Research file, Section 4)
- Health, shopping and community: UBC Hospital is at 2211 Wesbrook Mall (Vancouver Coastal Health) and includes an Urgent Care Centre. Wesbrook Village is anchored by a Save-On-Foods at 5945 Berton Avenue. The UNA operates the Wesbrook Community Centre, 3335 Webber Lane, and the Old Barn Community Centre. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/ubc-hospital and https://www.myuna.ca/facilities/ (Research file, Section 4)
- Culture and scale: the Museum of Anthropology is at 6393 NW Marine Drive and the UBC Botanical Garden, established in 1916, is at 6804 SW Marine Drive. UBC's own facts page counts 60,309 students on the Vancouver campus. https://botanicalgarden.ubc.ca and https://www.ubc.ca:443/about/facts.html (Research file, Section 4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. At Tapestry, the Form B and the filed bylaws are also the documents still owed to settle the listing-stated 55+ restriction and service package (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC (long-term rentals are allowed; short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here — stated expressly.** The Empty Homes Tax is a City of Vancouver tax, administered through the City's own property-status declaration for Vancouver homeowners; this area is not in the City of Vancouver at all — the UEL is provincially administered and UBC's neighbourhoods are on university lands — so no EHT declaration or tax applies to a University VW property on that ground. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/declare-property-status.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- **No City of Vancouver bylaws apply here.** On the UEL side, the governing instruments are the UEL Administration, the UEL Official Community Plan and the Land Use, Building and Community Administration Bylaw, under the University Endowment Land Act; on the UBC side, land use is the university's and neighbourhood services are the UNA's (below). Establish which side of the line a specific property sits on before applying any rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- **Head-lease diligence — unique to this area.** On every UBC-neighbourhood purchase, order and read the head lease per property: start date, prepaid status, remaining term, UBC's rights under the lease, and any faculty/staff resale restrictions on the specific home. The remaining term runs from the project's original lease date, not the purchase date, and it is the term a lender lends against. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf (Research file, Section 6)
- **UNA services levy.** The University Neighbourhoods Association delivers municipal-like services to roughly 15,000 residents of UBC's residential neighbourhoods under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC — confirm the UNA levy and any neighbourhood-specific charges in the strata budget / Form B for the specific unit, on top of strata fees. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **Property tax — provincial rural regime, not city tax.** Property taxes here are payable to the Province's Surveyor of Taxes, due July 2, with a 5% penalty on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion still unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). Two UEL traps from the Administration's realtor sheet: the population is nearly 4,000, and water billing is **not** on the tax bill — water is billed separately. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $835,000 (the First-Time Home Buyers' Program ceiling figure, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is not a price for any property in this area), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,700 (2% × $635,000) = **$14,700 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA) — conditional only:** no presale was verified in this area in the research pass. If a presale is offered here, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this actually in Vancouver?** No. The postal address reads "Vancouver," but the property is either in the provincially administered UEL or in a UBC campus neighbourhood — neither is in the City of Vancouver, and no City of Vancouver bylaws apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- **Q: Is a Wesbrook condo freehold or leasehold?** Leasehold. All UBC-neighbourhood homes sit on prepaid 99-year leases from UBC — the land is never sold outright — while the UEL's older streets are largely freehold. Confirm tenure on title for the specific property. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf
- **Q: How much lease is left, and when does the clock start?** The remaining term runs from the project's original lease date, not from your purchase date — so two buildings of different ages carry different remaining terms. The exact dates for a specific building are read from its head lease on title; for Tapestry they are UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf (Research file, Section 6)
- **Q: Do I have to file Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration?** No. The Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver, and this area is not in the City — there is no EHT declaration or tax for a University VW property. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/declare-property-status.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and when?** The Province's Surveyor of Taxes, under the rural regime — due July 2, with a 5% penalty on portions unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on portions unpaid October 31. Water is billed separately; it is not on the tax bill. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- **Q: What is the UNA levy on top of strata fees?** The University Neighbourhoods Association delivers municipal-like services to UBC's residential neighbourhoods under its Neighbours Agreement with UBC, funded in part by a services levy on residents — confirm the levy amount for the specific unit in the strata budget and Form B; no per-unit amount is stated in the sources checked. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **Q: Is Tapestry really 55+?** Listing-stated, not document-settled. MLS R2919542 states the condominium community is "restricted to 55+ years" and describes a mandatory service package, but no Form B or registered-bylaw source checked states the restriction — that check is still owed before relying on it. The rental side at 3338 Wesbrook Mall is a separate retirement residence, not the condos at 3382. https://www.concertproperties.com/condos/completed?pa__&page=2 (55+ file, Section 2)
- **Q: Which condo buildings are verified by name here?** Two counted: Tapestry at Wesbrook Village condominium homes (3382 Wesbrook Mall, Concert, completed 2010, 46 condominium homes) and Eton by Polygon (Wesbrook Village; unit count and build year UNCONFIRMED from the developer source). The similar names around them — Seasons, Georgia Point, Vista Point, Granite Terrace, Mews, Village Gate Homes — are rental or campus housing, not condos for sale (Section 2). https://www.concertproperties.com/condos/completed?pa__&page=2 and https://polyhomes.com/community/eton/
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit as an investor?** Long-term, yes on the provincial rule — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, though short-term rentals can still be restricted. Check the specific strata's bylaws and any head-lease terms; at Tapestry a tenant would also have to satisfy the listing-stated 55+ restriction, which is itself still document-unconfirmed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which schools serve a Wesbrook address?** SD39: a locator run for 5488 Ortona Rd returned Norma Rose Point School (K–7) and University Hill Secondary, and 5395 Chancellor Blvd returned University Hill Elementary and University Hill Secondary. The elementary boundary line for other addresses is UNVERIFIED — run the exact address in the locator before relying on a school name. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/norma-rose-point-school/about-us (Research file, Section 4)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The postal city is the trap, and it changes the tax bill, the bylaws and the declaration list.** A home here can read "Vancouver" on every envelope while sitting in the provincially run UEL (largely freehold) or in a UBC-governed leasehold neighbourhood — with rural-regime taxes to the Surveyor of Taxes, no City of Vancouver bylaws, and no Vancouver Empty Homes Tax. Establish the jurisdiction from title and the UEL/UBC sources, not from the address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands and https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf
- **The lease clock started when the project was built, not when you buy.** Because every UBC-neighbourhood lease is a prepaid 99-year lease running from its original date, the oldest neighbourhood, Hampton Place (1990s, UBC's first), carries the shortest remaining terms in the area, and a newer Wesbrook building carries more — the head lease, not the listing, states the number a lender will use. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf and https://ubcproperties.com/history/
- **One Wesbrook Mall project is two different products at two different addresses.** Tapestry's linked midrises split by address: 3338 Wesbrook Mall is Building 1, the rental retirement residence (now Seasons Wesbrook Village), and 3382 Wesbrook Mall is Building 2, the 46 condominium homes a buyer can own. A search or a showing booked on the wrong address lands in the wrong tenure. https://www.modo.coop/building-directory and https://www.concertproperties.com/condos/completed?pa__&page=2
- **Even the rental residence's operator name is not settled online.** BC's Assisted Living Registry names Seasons Retirement Communities (Wesbrook Village) GP Inc. as the legal operator of the 3338 Wesbrook Mall residence, class SN — while Chartwell publishes a full page for the same address. - **The forest and the university are the amenity package.** Pacific Spirit Regional Park's roughly 860–874 hectares and 55-plus km of trails wrap the campus, and UBC itself counts 60,309 Vancouver-campus students — the demand base behind the parent-buyer and student-rental markets that support these leasehold condos. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/pacific-spirit-regional-park and https://www.ubc.ca:443/about/facts.html
