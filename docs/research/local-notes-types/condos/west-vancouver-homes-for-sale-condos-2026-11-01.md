# West Vancouver — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The frame is the single District of West Vancouver, between the Capilano River and Horseshoe Bay, under Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) **Ambleside** — the apartment strip along Bellevue Avenue and Marine Drive between roughly 13th and 19th Streets, zoned RM1 and RM2 under Bylaw No. 4662, plus the towers behind Marine Drive on Duchess Avenue and 13th Street; (2) **Dundarave** — the Bellevue Avenue waterfront strip west of Ambleside (the 2200–2500 blocks of Bellevue), smaller boutique buildings a short walk from Dundarave village at 24th/25th Street and Marine Drive. Outside those two strips, the District is single-family RS zones — there is no verified apartment-condo stock in the British Properties, Caulfeild or Horseshoe Bay in the sources checked. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below; zoning per the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- The Official Community Plan emphasizes the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre as the heart of West Vancouver, with retail, service, restaurants, arts and culture spaces, offices, civic services and waterfront recreation; the Ambleside Local Area Plan boundary runs south of Inglewood Avenue between 13th and 23rd Streets. Park Royal, at Marine Drive and Taylor Way on the District's east edge, is recognized in the OCP as the regional shopping centre. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- Transit: TransLink route 250 runs Downtown Vancouver via Georgia Street, the Stanley Park Causeway and the Lions Gate Bridge along Marine Drive to Dundarave, with some trips continuing to Horseshoe Bay Ferry Terminal; routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed the neighbourhoods into Park Royal exchange. There is no rapid transit in the District. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- West Vancouver market numbers are NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 reports cover only Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows, Maple Ridge) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in Ambleside's 1970s stock (Parkview Tower 1972, Sea Wind 1975, Westerlies 1977), where the first mandatory-cycle reports land on aging parkades, piping and elevators. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Building directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Parkview Tower and Sea Wind — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — do not assume an Ambleside building is, or is not, age-restricted; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District zoning and plans:** West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 governs, under OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Apartment areas in Ambleside are zoned RM1 and RM2, and Form and Character Development Permit guidelines apply in the Ambleside Apartment Area and the Ambleside Village Centre. A July 21, 2025 provincial ministerial directive requires increased density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Foreshore and flood rules:** a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding); new habitable space must be built to the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding, and work within 15 metres of the natural boundary (high tide mark) must follow the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines. This is strata capital-planning context for the Bellevue Avenue waterfront buildings (Sea Wind, Bella Vista, Regatta Pointe, Bellevue Sunset, Les Terraces). https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits
- **Tenure check on the east edge:** part of the Park Royal area sits on Squamish Nation land (Capilano IR No. 5) under the Park Royal Lease — waterfront and reserve-adjacent parcels on the District's east side need a tenure check per property; the verified condo buildings in Section 2 are not in that area, but any east-edge strata purchase needs its title read. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — nearly all of this file's stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at West Vancouver prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. At typical Ambleside prices the first-time programs are usually out of range — run the bands on the actual price. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Insured mortgages are capped by price, so most West Vancouver condo purchases are conventional — confirm with the lender. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are West Vancouver condos, actually?** Almost all of them are in two places: Ambleside (Bellevue Avenue and Marine Drive, zoned RM1/RM2, plus the Duchess Avenue and 13th Street towers) and the Dundarave Bellevue strip. The rest of the District is single-family RS zoning — a "West Vancouver condo" search that wanders into the British Properties or Caulfeild is searching where the verified stock is not. Section 2 lists the verified buildings by name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: The directory says my building bans rentals and pets. Can I rent my unit out? What about a dog?** Two different answers. Renting: yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, so the "rentals not allowed" lines at Parkview Tower and Sea Wind are old bylaw text. Pets: pet bylaws remain enforceable, and both buildings are published as pets not allowed — read the registered bylaws in the strata package before assuming a pet works. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1970s Ambleside concrete building?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must now have one every five years with a 30-year projection and three funding models, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. Parkview Tower (1972), Sea Wind (1975) and Westerlies (1977) are exactly the vintage where parkade membrane, piping and elevator items cluster. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Does the waterfront come with extra rules?** Yes. Work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area needs a Foreshore Development Permit before a building permit, new habitable space must meet the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding, and work within 15 metres of the high-tide mark follows the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines — a strata planning and insurance consideration for every Bellevue Avenue waterfront building, not just detached homes. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits
- **Q: What is different about Grosvenor Ambleside versus the older buildings?** Age and scale: built 2017, two terraced buildings of six and seven storeys with 98 and 42 residences (140 total) above street-level shops, cafés and restaurants, with a pedestrian galleria between them — the only verified post-2010 apartment-condo project in Ambleside in this file, against a 1970s–1980s established stock. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/grosvenor-ambleside
- **Q: Which schools serve an Ambleside condo?** School District 45 serves the District (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12) and catchments are address-based through the district locator. The verified sample in the research file: 1329 Duchess Avenue returns Hollyburn Elementary and West Vancouver Secondary. Run the building's exact address — do not assume from the nearest school. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 250 runs along Marine Drive over the Lions Gate Bridge to downtown Vancouver, continuing to Dundarave and on some trips to Horseshoe Bay; routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed into Park Royal exchange. Ambleside buildings sit directly on the 250 corridor. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- **Q: Is any of this stock leasehold?** The verified Section 2 buildings are presented as ordinary strata in their sources. The tenure caution in the District is the east edge: part of the Park Royal area sits on Squamish Nation land (Capilano IR No. 5) under the Park Royal Lease. For any property near that edge, read the title before writing — tenure for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the title is read. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Will the density pressure around Ambleside change my building?** The policy direction is documented: a July 21, 2025 provincial ministerial directive requires increased density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave. That affects land around the condo strip, not the strata's own bylaws — what it means for a specific building's redevelopment value is UNCONFIRMED and needs a planner's read of the RM zoning and the Ambleside Local Area Plan. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Q: What does the strata insurance deductible risk look like here?** A strata's water-damage deductible can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused the loss; the amount sits in the strata's insurance summary in the document package and is UNCONFIRMED for every building in this file until read. In waterfront buildings with older piping, buyers typically match their own unit policy to that deductible. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West Vancouver's condo map is two strips, not a district-wide market.** The District's own planning structure works through Ambleside, Dundarave and Horseshoe Bay as its village/centre areas, and the verified apartment stock sits only in the first two — the RS single-family zones (minimum site areas from 488 m² in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1 under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, Section 200) cover everything else. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **The province has ordered more density beside the condo strip.** The July 21, 2025 provincial ministerial directive pushing density at Park Royal–Taylor Way and in areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (news.gov.bc.ca release) is part of the land story for every Ambleside purchase — the established 1970s buildings sit in the District's designated growth focus. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Grosvenor Ambleside is the exception that proves the stock's age.** Built 2017 with 140 residences over retail, it is the only verified modern project among 1972–1990 buildings in Section 2 — Ambleside buyers are mostly choosing between one new project and a deep bench of 35- to 55-year-old concrete buildings with correspondingly different depreciation-report profiles. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/grosvenor-ambleside
- **The biggest Ambleside buildings are the older towers, not the waterfront boutiques.** The directory's Ambleside top-buildings list runs Westerlies 89 units, Executive on the Park 88 units, Oceanbrook 86 units, Parkview Tower 80 units and Westshore Place 64 units (building directory, secondary source) — while the Bellevue waterfront buildings run small (Sea Wind 18 residences, Bella Vista 6 suites per current listings). Executive on the Park, Oceanbrook and Westshore Place are named here from that directory list only; their addresses and details are UNCONFIRMED in this pass and they are not counted in Section 2. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/market-report/west-vancouver/ambleside/condos/may-2025
- **Ambleside Park is the strip's front yard.** The District describes Ambleside Park as a waterfront park with sandy beaches and a designated swim area, skatepark, playground, tennis and pickleball courts and the Ambleside Par 3 Golf Course, on the Spirit Trail — the amenity base that the Bellevue and Marine Drive buildings price in. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/ambleside-park
- **The library and community centre are inside the condo strip.** West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre at 2121 Marine Drive and West Vancouver Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive both sit within the Ambleside/Dundarave corridor — daily-life infrastructure within walking distance of every building in Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
