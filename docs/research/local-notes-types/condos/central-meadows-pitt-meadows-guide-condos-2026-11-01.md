## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Central Meadows" is an MLS® sub-area name for the Harris Road civic-centre core of the City of Pitt Meadows; the City does not publish a boundary for it, and the closest official planning name is the OCP Municipal Town Centre, embracing Harris Road and the North Lougheed Area, with Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north edge, the CPKC rail line along Hammond Road, and City Hall at 12007 Harris Road. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Central Meadows' 2008–2010 Harris Road stock (Origin, Solaris) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — but see the Pitt Meadows prohibition below, which removes the question locally. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a city layer, separate from the void strata bylaws: a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list for short-term rentals. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Meadowland Village listings record a 55+ age restriction — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. The Meadowlands co-operative's 55+ rule is a co-op membership rule, not a strata bylaw, and is not governed by ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs, under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022), which designates the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial/residential development. The City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024: lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Floodplain is the city's defining land rule:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain — the guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is superseded and retired here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; Central Meadows is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes and drainage levy:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The same bylaw carries a separate drainage levy — for 2026 the residential (Class 1) drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Section 2 apartment buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no current presale project in Central Meadows was verified in this pass. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are this guide's buildings counted?** In the home file: both apartment condos (Solaris at Meadows Gate, Origin) are counted in ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-condos-2026-11-01.md. This guide file counts zero so no building is ever counted twice.
- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Central Meadows condo?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under Pitt Meadows' Zoning Bylaw at all, and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. Long-term rental is a separate question — strata rental bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Is Solaris really the city's only concrete tower?** Current listings describe Solaris at Meadows Gate (12069 Harris Road, built 2010, 144 units per the building directory) as "Pitt Meadows' only concrete high rise." That is listing language; the verified facts from the directory are the year, the unit count and the restriction set (pets not allowed, rentals allowed). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Harris Road buildings?** It differs by building: the directory records Solaris as pets not allowed (and a current unit listing records "No Cats"), while Origin listings state "1 dog or 2 cats permitted" with cats OK. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws on the Form B govern — Origin's position is listing-level only and a verified bylaw summary is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road and https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/11950-harris-road/216
- **Q: Is Meadowland Village a condo complex?** No — it is a townhouse/rancher strata at 18960 Advent Road (built 1988, freehold strata), board-classified Central Meadows, with a published 55+ age restriction and pets allowed with restrictions in current listings. Its unit count is genuinely unsettled in public sources (26 homes in listings vs a 20-unit directory header) — UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan is read. https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7
- **Q: And the Meadowlands building on Advent Road — can I buy a unit there?** Not as a strata purchase. Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Road) is a 55+ housing co-operative listed by CHF BC — occupancy is by co-op membership, there is no strata lot to buy in the ordinary way and no Form B. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: How much of the city is floodplain, and what does that mean for a condo?** The City's 2020 Business Plan puts over 95% of Pitt Meadows in the floodplain, protected by a 60 km dike system; Central Meadows is in diking-district drainage Area 3. The guide page's "over 85%" figure is stale — retire it. Practically: ask about the building's flood construction level, the strata's insurance (including flood/dike-breach coverage and deductibles), and expect the separate drainage levy on the tax bill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: Why is there a drainage levy on my tax bill?** The 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 carries a separate drainage levy ($0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for residential in 2026) — the cost of running the diking/drainage system in a city that is over 95% floodplain. It is a City charge, not a strata fee. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: What is the commute from the Harris Road condos?** Pitt Meadows Station (West Coast Express) is on Harris Road in the urban core — the City states the train trip to downtown Vancouver is about 45 minutes, Monday to Friday in peak periods. Driving commute times are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Meadows condo?** SD42 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12) and catchments are address-based. The verified locator result in the research file is for 12007 Harris Road (City Hall): Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, both physically in the area (11941 Harris Road and 19438 116B Avenue; Pitt Meadows Secondary is the only public secondary school in the city). Run the building's exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
