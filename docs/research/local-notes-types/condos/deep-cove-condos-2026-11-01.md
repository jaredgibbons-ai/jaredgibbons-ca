## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Deep Cove is a waterfront village in the District of North Vancouver at the municipality's far eastern edge, on Indian Arm at the foot of Mount Seymour — the handful of streets around Gallant Avenue, Deep Cove Road and Panorama Drive. Deep Cove is confirmed in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, as one of six Village Centres (with Lions Gate, Queensdale, Maplewood, Parkgate and Edgemont). Exact official boundary streets for the village are UNVERIFIED — no OCP schedule or District page read states them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- Condo stock actually concentrates on the village's two core streets: Gallant Avenue, the village's main street (the retail row and two of the three apartment addresses in Section 2), and Panorama Drive, the waterfront loop around Panorama Park. The surrounding plateau streets are detached houses under the District's R1 and R2 zones. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- There is effectively one road in and out: Deep Cove Road / Gallant Avenue into the village, with Dollarton Highway west to Mount Seymour Parkway, Phibbs Exchange and Highway 1. TransLink buses reach the village from Phibbs Exchange via Dollarton Highway and Mount Seymour Parkway, terminating at Panorama Drive / Gallant Avenue in the village, and since September 7, 2026 the R2 RapidBus extension via Phibbs Exchange gives the village's bus corridor a one-seat ride to Metrotown. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- Anchors: Panorama Park on Panorama Drive (beach, swimming area, picnic shelter, launch for kayaks and small boats, Deep Cove Yacht Club and the Indian Arm view); Seycove Secondary, 1204 Caledonia Avenue; Cove Cliff Elementary, 1818 Banbury Road; Deep Cove Park, 1991 Rockcliff Road. The nearest SkyTrain station is none within the District — the SkyTrain connection is by bus to downtown Vancouver over the Second Narrows, or by bus to Phibbs and the R2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- Nearby areas: Parkgate (west, under Mount Seymour Parkway), Woodlands and Indian River (north along Indian Arm), and Dollarton / Windsor Park (south-west along Dollarton Highway). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- Market numbers: North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Deep Cove's two small stratas (11 and 21 units), one owner's levy vote moves the budget — read the minutes, not just the balance. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Both verified Deep Cove stratas (11 and 21 units) are inside this requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This is why the "rentals not allowed" directory line at Deep Cove Estates in Section 2 does not bar a long-term tenancy — and why the Panorama Place directory line "rentals allowed with restrictions" cannot restrict a long-term rental either. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A directory line that says only "age restrictions" (Panorama Place, Section 2) does not by itself prove a valid 55+ bylaw — read the filed bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Deep Cove sits under the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 — one of its six Village Centres — and Zoning Bylaw 3210. Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 zone minimum lot at 4,000 m² and the standard R2 minimum at 660 m², with modifiers down to R2(S1) at 335 m²; the Gallant Avenue commercial frontage where Deep Cove Estates sits is zoned VC-G (Village Commercial – Gallant Avenue) per its commercial listings. OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — the village sits between the Indian Arm shoreline and the forested Mount Seymour slope, so hazard-area and flood-construction-level checks (District GeoWeb levels are modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m) belong in every purchase here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md) and https://www.loopnet.ca/listing/4390-gallant-ave-north-vancouver-bc/37598427/
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Deep Cove is confirmed — the village stock in Section 2 is 1983–1990 resale stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District of North Vancouver tax timing:** District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much condo stock does Deep Cove actually have?** Three small apartment buildings, all 1980s–1990: Panorama Place (2181 Panorama Drive, 11 units), Deep Cove Estates (4390 Gallant Avenue, 21 units), and an apartment building at 4323 Gallant Avenue whose name is UNCONFIRMED on a citable source. The research file confirms the village is predominantly freehold detached housing, with named complexes not confirmed in that file — the buildings above were verified separately for this file. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/panorama-place-2181-drive and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/deep-cove-estates-4390-gallant-ave (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: Deep Cove Estates' directory says rentals are not allowed. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes, long-term — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Panorama Place is listed with "age restrictions." What does that mean for a younger buyer?** Only a 55-or-older bylaw is valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, and the directory does not state the age or the bylaw terms — that is UNCONFIRMED until the filed bylaws are read on the Form B. Exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/panorama-place-2181-drive and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the village buildings?** From the published sources in Section 2: Panorama Place allows pets with restrictions, and Deep Cove Estates allows pets. Confirm the exact bylaw wording on the Form B — pet bylaws still bind. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/panorama-place-2181-drive and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/deep-cove-estates-4390-gallant-ave
- **Q: Is Deep Cove Estates purely residential?** No — it is a mixed-use strata on Gallant Avenue: commercial listings for its ground-floor units describe strata retail and office units zoned VC-G (Village Commercial – Gallant Avenue). A residential buyer is sharing a strata with commercial owners on the village's main street. https://www.loopnet.ca/listing/4390-gallant-ave-north-vancouver-bc/37598427/
- **Q: What should I check in such small stratas (11 and 21 units)?** The Form B package: the latest depreciation report, financials, CRF balance and planned work. Both buildings are inside the post-July 1, 2024 rule requiring a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, and the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund — in an 11-unit strata, one roof or envelope project is a large per-unit levy, so the report matters more here, not less. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: How isolated is the village, practically?** There is one road corridor in and out (Deep Cove Road / Gallant Avenue, then Dollarton Highway to Mount Seymour Parkway and Highway 1), and parking in the village core is an active, contested file: the District endorsed a Deep Cove Parking and Access Implementation Plan in 2015 and presented a further Parking Review to Council on April 22, 2024 addressing pay parking, resident parking and time-limited parking. A condo's stall and storage are worth real money here — verify both in the strata plan, not the listing text. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deep Cove address?** The village schools are Cove Cliff Elementary (1818 Banbury Road) and Seycove Secondary (1204 Caledonia Avenue), with Dorothy Lynas and Sherwood Park elementaries in the same Seycove family of schools. School District 44 catchments are address-based and no specific Deep Cove condo address was run through the SD44 locator in the research file — that result is UNVERIFIED; run the exact address. SD44 has elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) only, no middle tier. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: What is "Livable Deep Cove," and does it affect the village buildings?** It is the District's project to redesign lower Gallant Avenue — the street Deep Cove Estates and 4323 Gallant Avenue front. In the District's September 2022 engagement, 818 surveys were completed and 82% supported change; lower-Gallant construction and streetscape changes are a live file for both village buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: Are there new condos or presales coming to Deep Cove?** None confirmed. The verified stock is 1983–1990 resale buildings, and the research file records no named new complex — any "coming soon" Deep Cove project claim is UNCONFIRMED until a developer or District source names it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The secondary school's name is an Indigenous place name, gifted in 2022.** Seycove Secondary's gifted name, sə́yəmətən, was shared by the səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh) Nation in June 2022 — "place of serenity near the water" — and the village sits on the Nation's inlet territory. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Village parking has been a formal District file for a decade.** The District endorsed the Deep Cove Parking and Access Implementation Plan in 2015, and its April 22, 2024 Parking Review presentation to Council covered pay parking, resident parking and time-limited parking — the scarcity a summer visitor sees at Panorama Park is structural, not seasonal bad luck. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **The Quarry Rock trailhead is inside the village.** The Baden Powell Trail and the Quarry Rock hike are accessed from Panorama Park, and Deep Cove Park at 1991 Rockcliff Road adds a tennis court, playground and gravel field — the trail traffic shares the same streets and parking as the two Gallant Avenue buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **One of the village's two named stratas is part commercial.** Deep Cove Estates' ground floor is strata retail and office space zoned VC-G on Gallant Avenue — the strata's insurance, parking and common-area decisions are made with commercial owners at the table, which a buyer reading only the residential listings would not see. https://www.loopnet.ca/listing/4390-gallant-ave-north-vancouver-bc/37598427/
- **The village's growth is capped by its own OCP designation.** Deep Cove is one of six Village Centres in District OCP Bylaw 7900 — a village-centre designation, not a town centre — which is why the verified stock is three small 1980s–1990 buildings and not towers: the plan contemplates village scale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
