## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Dollarton is the District of North Vancouver area along Dollarton Highway on the north shore of Burrard Inlet, between Maplewood at the west end and Deep Cove to the east. The port authority's shoreline report places Maplewood Flats at the west end, the Tsleil-Waututh Nation reserve lands (Burrard Inlet IR #3) along the shore backed by Dollarton Highway, and Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) farther east. A single District boundary line for the whole sub-area beyond that shoreline description is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: in one place only — Cates Landing, the Polygon complex on Cates Landing Way beside Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) at the east end of the shore (Section 2). The rest of Dollarton is detached houses on R1 and R2 lots along and above Dollarton Highway; a neighbourhood source describes Dollarton as almost entirely detached, with condo and townhome inventory minimal and rare when it appears. The attached stock at the west end (Maplewood Village Centre, Old Dollarton Road) is Maplewood, not Dollarton, and is excluded in Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md) and https://www.wallacegreen.ca/dollarton
- Transit anchors: TransLink route 211 (Phibbs Exchange / Burrard Station) runs via Dollarton Highway between Deep Cove and Phibbs Exchange, with some trips continuing to Burrard Station; route 212 runs via Dollarton Highway and Main Street to Phibbs Exchange; route 215 (Indian River) runs via Main Street, Dollarton Highway and Old Dollarton Road, then Riverside Drive and Mount Seymour Parkway. The R2 RapidBus connects at Phibbs Exchange and, since September 7, 2026, runs one-seat from Park Royal through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At Cates Landing, read the Form B together with the building's shoreline and insurance position below — the park shoreline beside it is documented as eroding. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No directory or listing line checked for Cates Landing states a rental ban (a current listing calls it "pet & rental friendly") — if an older Cates Landing document surfaces a "rentals not allowed" line, it is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Cates Landing source checked publishes an age restriction — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Dollarton is governed by the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (including the Maplewood Village Centre plans for the west end), and Zoning Bylaw 3210. Under Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525, waterfront principal buildings must sit at least 7.6 m back from the Ocean Natural Boundary Line under the R1/R2 setback table — a lot-level fact for the detached shore, and context for any Cates Landing alteration near the water. Since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Shoreline and foreshore — the Dollarton-specific layer:** the port authority states the Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change, and a shoreline adaptation and restoration project at the park, proposed by the District and the Tsleil-Waututh Nation, is under review by the port authority. Burrard Inlet waters and the intertidal area are federal lands and waters managed by the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority — dock, seawall or foreshore work is not a District-only approval. District OCP Schedule B also sets Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside, Wildfire, Creek and Slope Hazard Development Permit Areas, and the District publishes GeoWeb flood construction levels modelled to a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Cates Landing is a completed 2017 resale complex, so GST is not in a typical purchase here. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to a 2017 resale. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Dollarton is verified — Cates Landing completed in 2017, so purchases there are resale/Form B transactions, not REDMA contracts. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in the exemplar files.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the published bands (the stated range is 0.6%–4.5%), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, a waterfront building's flood and erosion position can affect a lender's and insurer's view of a specific unit — no source checked publishes a Cates Landing building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Cates Landing in Dollarton or Roche Point?** Both, depending on the source. It sits on the Dollarton Highway shore beside Cates Park — Dollarton in the research file's geography — but MLS classifies its units to Roche Point and listings describe it as near Deep Cove. Search Roche Point as well as Dollarton or the complex will not appear. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3081404-3825-cates-landing-way-408-north-vancouver-bc-v7g-0a6/brochure (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Q: How many homes are at Cates Landing, and how many are apartments?** Developer-era sources state 95 residences total: 80 apartment residences and 15 waterfront townhouses, in a 4-storey complex completed in 2017 by Polygon. The townhouses are a separate form inside the same strata — confirm the specific unit's form and strata lot on title. https://condotrend.com/cates-landing/ and https://www.livabl.com/articles/archives/cates-landing-north-vancouver-condo
- **Q: Can I rent out a Cates Landing condo long-term, and can I have a pet?** A current listing describes the complex as freehold strata and "pet & rental friendly," and the building directory states pets allowed with restrictions. Independently of any bylaw text, strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable for long-term rentals anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022. Pet terms are a Form B / filed-bylaw question. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3093442-104-3825-cates-landing-way-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7g0a6-1300000/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cates-landing-3873-way
- **Q: What is the shoreline risk beside the complex?** The port authority states the Cates Park shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change, threatening park infrastructure and cultural sites; a District / Tsleil-Waututh shoreline adaptation project is under port-authority review. For the unit itself: check the strata's insurance and deductible position and the District GeoWeb flood construction level (modelled to 2100 plus 0.6 m) before subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Q: Is any part of the waterfront approval federal?** Yes. Burrard Inlet's waters and intertidal area are port-authority-managed federal lands and waters, so foreshore, dock or seawall work needs that check in addition to District permits — relevant to the townhouse component's private yards opening onto the waterfront. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cates Landing address?** No public school inside Dollarton is confirmed. The schools beside the area are Cove Cliff Elementary (1818 Banbury Road) and Seycove Secondary (1204 Caledonia Avenue) in Deep Cove and Seymour Heights Elementary (2640 Carnation Street) to the north-west; SD44 catchments are address-based and the research file's catchment result for its Dollarton sample address is UNVERIFIED — run the exact Cates Landing Way address with SD44. Dorothy Lynas Elementary (4000 Inlet Crescent) is the Seymour-area French Immersion choice program, not a catchment school. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Q: Are there other condos on Dollarton Highway if Cates Landing has nothing for sale?** No other apartment-condo building on the Dollarton shore is verified. The nearest attached stock is at the Maplewood end (Old Dollarton Road / Seymour River Place), which is a different sub-area, or in Deep Cove — a buyer set on Dollarton is waiting on one complex. - **Q: What does the strata include at Cates Landing?** Current listings describe a fitness room, guest suite, bike room, kayak and paddleboard storage, dog wash, meeting rooms and an on-site building manager, with radiant in-floor heating in the units. What the monthly fee covers for a specific unit is a Form B question. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3093757-3825-cates-landing-way-207-north-vancouver-bc-v7g-0a6/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's only condo complex is filed under a different sub-area.** Cates Landing is the entire Dollarton condo story, and MLS puts it in Roche Point — a Dollarton-only Paragon search returns an apparently condo-less neighbourhood and misses the one complex that exists. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md) and https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3081404-3825-cates-landing-way-408-north-vancouver-bc-v7g-0a6/brochure
- **The park beside the complex is a cultural site, and its shoreline is an active federal review file.** Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) holds important cultural significance for the Tsleil-Waututh Nation, Burrard Inlet's waters are port-authority jurisdiction, and the proposed shoreline adaptation and restoration project there is a District / Tsleil-Waututh proposal under port-authority review — waterfront work near the complex sits inside that process, not just District permitting. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **The west end is planned to change, and it is Maplewood's plan, not Dollarton's.** The District OCP's Maplewood Village Centre plans Old Dollarton Road retail with apartments and live/work units above — future attached supply at the west end will be counted in Maplewood, which is why this file excludes Maplewood North and Elevation Residences even though both sit on Old Dollarton Road addresses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **The fastest bus on the North Shore now connects at Dollarton's exchange.** Since September 7, 2026 the R2 RapidBus runs one-seat from Park Royal through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station — Dollarton's routes 211/212/215 all feed Phibbs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **There is no school inside the sub-area.** Despite the family-house stock, no public school in Dollarton itself is confirmed on SD44's pages — the named schools are all in Deep Cove or up in the Seymour pocket, and catchment must be run address by address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **One developer-era number, two forms.** Listings and directories often quote Cates Landing as "95 homes" without the split — 80 of those are apartments and 15 are townhouses, and the two forms price, insure and resell differently inside the same strata. https://condotrend.com/cates-landing/
