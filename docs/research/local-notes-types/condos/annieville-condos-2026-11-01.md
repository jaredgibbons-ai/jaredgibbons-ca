# Annieville — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Annieville is in north-west North Delta in the City of Delta, along the Fraser River frontage; the former North Delta Area Plan map (Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) shows Annieville along River Road, with 116 Street at its east side and the Fraser River at its north side, and the City publishes no street-by-street written boundary. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf

Both sit east of the 116 Street line the former plan map shows, yet current MLS listings and the building directory classify both as Annieville — this file follows the MLS classification and flags the conflict here rather than hiding it. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- The rest of Annieville is detached and duplex stock: the research file describes a predominantly single detached area, with duplex product appearing through rezonings and projects like Annieville Heights (developer source). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annieville.md)
- The research file itself confirmed no named strata condo buildings in Annieville on an official source — the two buildings in Section 2 were verified independently for this file from MLS listing data and a building directory, not from City sources. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annieville.md)
- Market context: Annieville's SnapStats sub-area row for Condos & Townhomes in September 2026 was Inventory 13, Solds 2, Sales Ratio 15% — a combined condo-and-townhome figure, not condos alone (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annieville.md).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This is the central document for Delta Cedars, a 1970s building whose first mandatory-cycle report covers 50-year-old systems. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Delta Cedars reflects old bylaw text, not current law; the directory records Muse as rentals allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Delta Cedars is recorded as age-restricted — confirm on the Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw, because no other age threshold is enforceable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 applies parcel-by-parcel, under the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 — the current OCP has no lettered area-plan schedules (the North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950). Annieville-specific OCP items are the ND3 Annieville Development Permit Area for the River Road village and the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area on the river frontage, both in Appendix B of Bylaw No. 8400. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies — relevant to Annieville's detached lots, not to the two Scott Road strata buildings. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Annieville buildings are resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Federal savings vehicles and mortgage insurance:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. CMHC published mortgage default insurance premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there really condos in Annieville? I thought it was all houses.** Almost all houses — but two strata buildings stand on the Scott Road east edge and are classified Annieville in MLS data: Delta Cedars (8635 120 Street, 96 units, building directory) and Muse (9015 120 Street). The City-source research found none, which is why the area reads as detached-only in official material. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street
- **Q: The directory says Delta Cedars bans rentals and pets. Is that real?** The rental ban is not enforceable — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Pet bylaws remain enforceable, and the directory records pets not allowed at Delta Cedars — confirm the registered bylaws, not the directory summary, before buying with a pet. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Delta Cedars an age-restricted building?** The directory records "age restrictions" without stating the threshold. The only age bylaw BC law permits is 55+ (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2) — read the exact bylaw on the Form B; any other stated threshold is UNCONFIRMED and would not be enforceable as written. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How old is Muse, really — 2017 or 2019?** Sources conflict: the building directory says 2017 and multiple MLS listings say 2019. Treat the year as UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan / Form B settles it; the complex is late-2010s either way. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121148-309-9015-120-street-delta-british-columbia-v4c6r7-412000/brochure
- **Q: Is a Muse unit a condo or a townhouse?** Both exist at 9015 120 Street under one complex name — listings show apartment units and townhouse units at the same address. Confirm the specific unit's form on its listing and strata plan; do not rely on the complex name alone. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **Q: What is the special-levy risk in a 1970s low-rise like Delta Cedars?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years with a 30-year projection, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF yearly. A thin CRF in a 1973 wood-frame building is the warning pattern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve the Scott Road buildings?** Catchments are address-based in SD37 and must be run in the locator for the specific address; the research file's Annieville sample (9240 112 Street, a detached address) returned Annieville Elementary and Delview Secondary — do not carry that result across to 120 Street addresses without running them. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: How is transit from Delta Cedars or Muse?** Both buildings sit on Scott Road; routes 312 and 391 connect the area to Scott Road Station (Expo Line), the 312 via 112 Street and the 391 via 116 Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt312.pdf
- **Q: Does the Fraser River escarpment hazard affect these buildings?** The ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area covers Annieville's river frontage and the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence for that area; the two condo buildings are on the Scott Road east edge, away from the frontage — whether either parcel sits in a permit area is a lot-specific check, UNCONFIRMED here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Annieville's condo stock hides in plain sight:** neither building appears in City-source research at all — both surfaced only in MLS listing data and a building directory — because the City's own geography describes Annieville as a detached/duplex neighbourhood and the former plan map puts its east edge at 116 Street, west of both buildings. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Delta Cedars' listings disagree with its directory on the build year:** the directory says 1973; several 2026 unit listings say 1972. On a building this age the one-year difference changes nothing about due diligence — the depreciation report and Form B matter, not the marketing year. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3100131-8635-120-street-358b-delta-bc-v4c-6r5/brochure
- **Delta Cedars carries the full 1970s amenity set:** the directory lists an elevator, outdoor pool, recreation centre, sauna/steam room, shared laundry and storage — shared laundry (not in-suite) is a real daily-life difference from Muse, whose directory lists in-suite laundry. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/delta-cedars-8635-120-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **The River Road infill pattern is the area's other housing story:** a City bylaw notice for 10604 River Road proposed rezoning from RD3 to RS6 for a two-lot subdivision under Bylaw No. 7600 — large river-side lots splitting into small lots is how Annieville adds homes, not condo buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **Annieville Elementary sits inside the neighbourhood but may not be your catchment:** for the sample address 9240 112 Street the SD37 locator returns Annieville Elementary (K–7), at that same address, and Delview Secondary (8–12), 9111 116 Street — east-side and Scott Road addresses can fall elsewhere, and catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
