## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 1976 Wolfe Road townhouse complex this is the central diligence document; for The Robson it is also where the 2022-vs-2023 build-year conflict gets settled against the registered strata plan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Robson's directory line reads "rentals allowed," which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Chilliwack Proper West building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (the "Chilliwack 2050" OCP) — its schedules include the Downtown Form & Character Development Permit Area as Schedule D-6, the DPA that covers The Robson's downtown ground, and the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan as Schedule B2. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/chilliwack-proper-west-guide.md)
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 is Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing amendment to Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/chilliwack-proper-west-guide.md)
- **Secondary suites and ADUs:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, Section 2 defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit of not more than 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, located within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit — the cap is the lesser of 90 m² and 40%. Section 4.06(1) permits only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), and garden suites are capped at 100 m² by Section 4.06(1)(g); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s. 5.04). A suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new secondary suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/chilliwack-proper-west-guide.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 of every calendar year; a 5% late payment penalty applies for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% for taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. This staged 5% + 5% is Chilliwack's own city scheme — do not confuse it with Mission's flat 10%, or with the rural Taxation (Rural Area) Act regime (which shares the 5% first stage but runs its second stage at October 31 and is paid to the Province). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Flood and ALR:** the guide states that under the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 4519, habitable floor space in areas protected by a standard dike must be at or above the applicable Flood Construction Level plus 0.6 m of freeboard, and that the City has an ongoing $30 million program to raise the dyke system — the City's floodplain bylaw text and flood pages were not opened in the research pass, so the Bylaw 4519 specifics, the $30 million program figure and the Young Road dyke statement as stated are UNVERIFIED in this file; parcel-level Flood Construction Levels are likewise UNVERIFIED. City-wide, 67% of Chilliwack's land (17,322 hectares) is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any case (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALR status for any specific Chilliwack Proper West parcel is a parcel-by-parcel check on the ALC map: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/chilliwack-proper-west-guide.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Robson (2022/2023) trades as resale stock now, so confirm a specific unit's status before assuming GST treatment. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Chilliwack Proper West was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
