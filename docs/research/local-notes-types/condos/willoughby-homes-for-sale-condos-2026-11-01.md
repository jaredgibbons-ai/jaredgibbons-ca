## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Willoughby's stock is young (the verified towers completed in 2024), so the Form B's levy and CRF lines are short — read them anyway; youth is not a substitute for the document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — no Willoughby strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the Township does NOT issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations, including Airbnb and Vrbo, per its own online business-licence application conditions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/willoughby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Willoughby project in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township plans — status carried exactly:** the Willoughby Community Plan Update bylaws No. 6201, 6202 and 6203 (replacing the Willoughby Community Plan and creating the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) stand at FIRST/SECOND READING ONLY (May 25, 2026) in this program's settled position — they are not stated here as adopted, and what gets built beside a condo bought today must be checked against the bylaws' current reading status, not assumed from the plan-update documents. The Transit Corridor is the area's designated high-density spine in the update documents. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/willoughby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Township zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2500:** s. 403 (Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL) sets a minimum lot area of 220 m² for front-loaded and rear-loaded lots, minimum lot frontage 11 m (front-loaded) / 8.2 m (rear-loaded), and a maximum lot area of 464.5 m². There is NO 355 m² figure in s. 403 and none is stated anywhere in this file. Section 102 defines "Coach House" as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot; a coach-house size cap and an owner-occupancy rule for coach houses / secondary suites in the Township are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/willoughby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Secondary suites (Township):** Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 caps a secondary suite at a maximum gross floor area of 120 m², each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and a building permit is required for all suites. The feed's detached cards repeatedly describe "legal" suites — verify the specific suite's permit rather than repeating the listing. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/willoughby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **SSMUH (Township):** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) amended Bylaw No. 2500 to allow small-scale multi-unit housing in zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings, subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, lot not larger than 4,050 m²) — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots, and NO 6-unit (6-plex) tier currently applies in the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/willoughby-homes-for-sale.md)

- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. - **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any Willoughby unit still on a developer contract. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
