## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero condo stock — structural, stated plainly: no apartment-condo building, strata condo complex or townhome complex is part of a turkey farm purchase in any source checked for this file, and none can be created on ALR farm land without Agricultural Land Commission permission, because ALR residences cannot be stratified — a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239; ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6) and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). The "buildings" in this topic are turkey barns and a farm residence on freehold farm land — documented below so the zero is verified, not assumed.**

- What is actually being bought in this market, per the research file: a turkey farm sale can combine ALR land, turkey barns and other farm buildings, a principal residence (ALC frame: 500 m² or less) and possibly one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), plus — separately, and only if the contract states it — production quota. No strata lot appears anywhere in that package. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- The barns are farm structures in law, not strata buildings: under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, s. 5, the use of agricultural land to construct, maintain or operate a structure, other than a residential structure, that is necessary for a farm use is designated as a farm use, and under s. 4 it may not be prohibited by a local government enactment; s. 5 states for greater certainty that this includes a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation — the wording under which a turkey barn is treated as a farm building in the ALR. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019 (Poultry farms file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- The precise bare-land distinction: bare-land strata is a real strata form generally — a strata plan can create bare lots rather than building units, and it follows the ordinary Strata Property Act route off the ALR. On ALR ground it is barred by the subdivision rule above, exactly as a building strata plan is: the plan itself is the subdivision the ALC regime controls. (Fraser Valley acreages file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- Tenure traps excluded, with reasons: corporation-share, undivided-interest, leasehold, co-op, non-market rental, life-lease and pad-rent product is not freehold strata condo stock and would be excluded wherever it appeared; none was verified on any turkey-topic property in any source checked — the stock here is freehold farm land, and no named complex of any of those tenures is counted. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic the operative rules are the ALR rules that keep condos off farm land, then the quota frame as far as any source states it, then the standard BC strata rules stated only for contrast.

- **ALR stratification bar (LEADS this section — the governing fact for this file):** ALR residences cannot be stratified. ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 states that strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR; a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). Say it correctly: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission** — no source checked shows a turkey-farm parcel with such a permission for residential strata use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR residence frame:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction); a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; and at most one additional residence is permitted, of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Those are houses on one title — neither is a condo unit, and neither can be sold separately without the subdivision gate above. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program. The income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Quota and supply management — UNVERIFIED in this file in every particular:** the live page states that turkey quota is administered by the BC Turkey Marketing Board; that primary quota may only be transferred on application by both the transferor and transferee with the Board's written approval, with the Board able to impose conditions or refuse; that secondary quota is non-transferable; and that a transfer can be declined where the buyer lacks adequate room in their Turkey Production Unit. No marketing-board source was opened for this topic, so each of those statements — the primary vs. secondary quota distinction, the claimed non-transferability of secondary quota, the joint application and written-approval process, the Board's conditions/refusal powers, the production-unit capacity ground, Premises ID and Board registration requirements, and any quota price — is site copy only and UNVERIFIED here. Verify the quota class and transfer position with the BC Turkey Marketing Board directly before subjects are removed; whether any of an operation's quota can transfer at all is the first question on a turkey deal. https://jaredgibbons.ca/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Barn and environmental standards, and avian influenza:** UNVERIFIED in this file — barn construction, ventilation, manure-storage, mortality, groundwater-licensing and biosecurity requirements for a BC turkey operation, and any disease-control effect on a sale, were not confirmed on an official source opened for this topic; the live page's due-diligence list (barn capacity and condition, Premises ID, manure and runoff compliance) is site copy only. https://jaredgibbons.ca/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Form B (contrast only — no strata lot exists in this topic):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a "farm condo" is ever marketed in this topic, the Form B and the strata plan number are the first proof to demand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata-wide, contrast only):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals and age (strata-wide, contrast only):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, and the only valid age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule touches a freehold turkey operation — there is no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (residence side only):** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. How GST is apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific turkey-farm purchase: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. How PTT is apportioned across a farm purchase's land, buildings, residence and quota: UNVERIFIED in this file — a lawyer/accountant question on the actual asset allocation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC (residence side only):** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. Whether any of these programs applies to a particular farm purchase is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no source checked rules on farm-parcel eligibility. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
