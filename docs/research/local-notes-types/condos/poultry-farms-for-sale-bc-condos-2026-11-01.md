## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero verified in-area condo stock — stated plainly at the top of this section. Zero is structural, by design: no apartment-condo building, strata condo complex or townhome complex is part of a poultry farm purchase in any source checked. The "buildings" in this topic are poultry barns and a farm residence on freehold farm land — documented below so the zero is verified, not assumed.**

- What is actually being bought: land (usually ALR), the barn package, the residence, and quota where the seller includes it — with the Abbotsford West listing behind the current page expressly stating quota is not included. No strata lot appears anywhere in that package. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- The barns are farm structures in law, not strata buildings: under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, s. 5, a structure necessary for a farm use is a designated farm use that a local government enactment may regulate but not prohibit, and s. 5 states for greater certainty that this includes a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019
- Tenure confirms the zero: poultry operations sit on freehold farm land, almost always in the ALR in the Fraser Valley belt; hatcheries, growers, transporters and processors are licensed by the BC Chicken Marketing Board on an annual basis. A strata corporation, a Form B and a strata fee have no place in that structure. (Settled treatment: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- Why a condo building cannot simply appear on this land: a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6), and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). No source checked shows a poultry-farm parcel with such a permission for residential strata use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic the operative rules are the farm rules that replace strata law entirely, then the standard strata rules stated only for contrast. No single city's rules are presented as province-wide.

- **ALR stratification bar (the lead rule):** depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)), so ALR residences cannot be stratified without Agricultural Land Commission permission. The ALR housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Supply management and quota (BCCMB frame, as settled in this program's C2 file):** BC poultry production is supply-managed. For chicken, quota is the amount of chicken, expressed in kilograms of live weight, that the BC Chicken Marketing Board allots to a grower; the board's General Orders name mainstream and specialty quota classes and treat transfer of quota with registered premises and acquisition or disposition without premises as different cases, and the board approves quota transactions, checking good standing and cycle allocation. The batch-13 research file itself opened no marketing-board source and marks quota mechanics UNVERIFIED in its own pass — the frame above is carried from the program's settled C2 treatment, and any specific transfer must still be verified with the applicable board. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf (Settled treatment: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The other boards:** the BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission's mainstream quota is hen-based (one unit = one placeable broiler breeder hen) and specialty is hatched-chick-based; turkey quota under the BC Turkey Marketing Board is pounds of turkey meat per specified period; the BC Farm Industry Review Board supervises the system and hears appeals of a board's decision within 30 calendar days. https://bcbhec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-Annual-Report.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals
- **BC Assessment farm classification (province-wide):** a voluntary program with income thresholds of $2,500 (operation 0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (operation over 4 ha), and $10,000 (operation under 0.8 ha); applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer who plans to keep farming must file the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Municipal layer — labelled examples only, never province-wide:** Abbotsford parcels sit under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 and the Abbotsforward 2050 OCP (adopted December 2025) — that is Abbotsford's frame only. Chilliwack parcels sit under Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — Chilliwack's frame only. A parcel's own municipality governs; confirm there. (Settled treatment: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Biosecurity and avian influenza:** BC Gov guidance structures a poultry farm around a Controlled Access Zone and a more restricted Restricted Access Zone where birds are housed; highly pathogenic avian influenza is reportable in Canada, the CFIA establishes primary control zones where it is detected, and on a quarantined farm no birds, bird products or by-products enter or leave without CFIA permission — which can affect placements, showings and sale timing. https://www.inspection.gc.ca/en/animal-health/terrestrial-animals/diseases/reportable/avian-influenza/what-expect-your-animals-are-infected
- **Strata rules, for contrast only:** if a buyer cross-shops a strata condo instead of a farm — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus 25¢/page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws void since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws legal (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2) with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. None applies to a freehold poultry operation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Buyer-layer taxes:** how PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific poultry purchase is UNVERIFIED — a lawyer/accountant question on the actual asset allocation. The general residential rules are stated for the residence side only: PTT 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; GST 5% on new construction only; First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out; First-Time Home Buyers' Program full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Presales (REDMA) / pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no presale exists in this topic; where a developer-contract purchase arises elsewhere, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period with deposits in trust under s. 18. Manufactured-home parks are not part of this topic; where pad-rent parks are discussed in this program, MHPTA pad-rent increases are limited to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any condo or strata component in a poultry farm purchase?** No verified one. The package in the sources is land, barns, the residence and quota where included — on freehold farm land, usually ALR. There is no strata corporation, no Form B and no strata fee. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: Does this page list poultry farms across all of BC?** No — as read, its feed showed four cards, all in Burnaby or Abbotsford, and only one described an operating commercial poultry operation; the others described poultry potential or poultry barns among other uses. The feed count is a snapshot, not a BC inventory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: Is quota included when I buy a poultry farm?** Not automatically. The live page states quota is not included unless expressly stated in the contract, and the one operating farm in its feed states quota is not included; quota transfers require board approval under the BCCMB frame in Section 3. Read the specific contract and verify the transfer position with the applicable board before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: Can I stratify the farmhouse or build strata housing on the farm later?** Only through the ALC gate: a strata plan causes a subdivision in the ALR (Bulletin 09, s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)), and subdivision requires Commission permission. An approval for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED — do not price a farm on a strata-conversion assumption. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What residence can a BC poultry parcel in the ALR have?** One principal residence of 500 m² or less, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), with local rules possibly more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: What income must the operation show to keep farm classification?** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: Can a municipality stop me building another poultry barn in the ALR?** It may regulate but not prohibit: a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation is a designated farm use under B.C. Reg. 30/2019 that a local government enactment may not prohibit — but ALC guidance adds it must be shown to be required for the farm use on the property and in line with the level of that farm use. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019
- **Q: How is quota priced?** UNVERIFIED from official sources: quota trades are facilitated privately and no official source publishes quota transaction prices. Any per-kilogram figure a listing or seller quotes is a private claim, not a published market price. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Q: What happens to a sale during an avian influenza outbreak?** HPAI is reportable; in a CFIA primary control zone, permits are required to move birds, eggs and by-products, and on a quarantined farm nothing bird-related enters or leaves without CFIA permission — which can delay placements, showings and completion. https://www.inspection.gc.ca/en/animal-health/terrestrial-animals/diseases/reportable/avian-influenza/what-expect-your-animals-are-infected
- **Q: Are market statistics published for poultry farms in BC?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and no official source opened publishes BC poultry-farm counts, farms-for-sale counts, quota prices or per-acre poultry values — each is UNVERIFIED and a price opinion stands on the specific property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The BC-wide title sits over a four-card Fraser Valley / Burnaby feed.** At research reading, every card behind this page was in Burnaby or Abbotsford and only one was an operating poultry farm — a buyer reading the page as a list of operating BC poultry farms would over-read the feed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Quota is measured three different ways in one topic:** broiler quota in kilograms of live weight per cycle, turkey quota in pounds of turkey meat per specified period, and hatching-egg quota in hen units (mainstream) or hatched chicks (specialty). A "quota included" line means nothing until the sector and the unit are named. (Settled treatment: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The farm-class deadline survives the sale.** A buyer who plans to keep farming a farm-classified property must file the General Application for Farm Classification with BC Assessment by October 31 after purchase — late applications are not accepted. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **The page itself states there is no single price figure** — price is driven by quota holdings, barn capacity and condition, land and location, and many sales are off-market. That is the page's own framing, recorded as site copy, not a statistic. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.md)
- **Biosecurity zones are part of the property, in practice:** BC Gov guidance structures a poultry farm around a Controlled Access Zone for the production area and a Restricted Access Zone where birds are housed — showings of an operating poultry farm are a biosecurity event, not a normal open house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/animal-and-crops/animal-health/ai_enhanced_biosecurity_for_small_scale_poultry_producers.pdf
