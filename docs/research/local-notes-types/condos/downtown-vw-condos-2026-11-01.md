## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Downtown VW is the downtown peninsula's Vancouver West board side: the central business district and the Georgia / Granville / Burrard / Robson core, on Burrard Inlet's south shore. The City describes the containing Downtown local area as a peninsula bounded by Burrard Inlet on the north, False Creek and Yaletown to the south, the West End and Stanley Park to the west, and Gastown, Chinatown and Strathcona to the east; a board-published boundary for Downtown VW alone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: almost entirely strata apartment form in towers above and beside the business district — in this file's selective set, the Georgia/Howe luxury core (Private Residences at Hotel Georgia), the Howe Street corridor south toward False Creek (Tate Downtown, 1188 Howe) and the Georgia Street east edge (Spectrum 1). Detached houses are not a VW-core housing form. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md) and the building entries in Section 2 below
- Main streets: West Georgia Street, Granville Street, Burrard Street and Howe Street. The City's Downtown history names Georgia and Granville Streets as the city-centre streets the centre moved up to from Water and Cordova Streets after incorporation. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- Transit anchors: the core is the hub where the Expo Line, Millennium Line and Canada Line meet (Waterfront and Burrard stations); the R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station, route 22 also runs from Burrard Station, and route 50 starts at Waterfront Station and runs via Chinatown, Granville Island and the False Creek south shore to Olympic Village Station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- Civic anchors in the core: Vancouver Public Library Central Library, 350 West Georgia Street, and St. Paul's Hospital, 1081 Burrard Street — a Providence Health Care hospital, not a Vancouver Coastal Health site. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 FVR and GVR reports contain no Vancouver rows) — no median, inventory, sales-ratio or days-on-market figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in the core's hotel-mixed tower (Private Residences at Hotel Georgia) the Form B is also where the residential strata's separation from the hotel and office components shows. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is far above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by a strata, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing — the two layers are distinct, and the City licence specifics for a given building: UNCONFIRMED in this pass. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is void as a long-term ban; the Hotel Georgia directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with the law but is not what makes renting lawful. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — with CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also applying in parts of the area. A plan named only for the Downtown local area or the VW core: UNVERIFIED in the research pass; the citywide Vancouver Plan applies. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any VW-core purchase, where investor-held units are common. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **Additional school tax:** at VW-core price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in it is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; the VW core's Burrard Inlet shoreline is the edge to check against that dataset; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most VW-core stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the top tier is in play at Hotel Georgia price points. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current Downtown VW presale; a specific current presale inside the VW core verified on a City or developer source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a published range of 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The city centre moved uphill, and the VW core is where it landed.** The City states the first passenger train arrived at the foot of Howe Street in 1887, and that in the next seven years a building boom moved the city centre from Water and Cordova Streets up to Georgia and Granville Streets — the streets at the heart of today's VW core; streetcar service began in 1890. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **The Hotel Georgia residences start where the old hotel's crown ends.** The 156 Private Residences begin on floor 14, just above the crown of the 1927 Hotel Georgia, and run to a penthouse that is the entire 48th floor — a unit's floor determines whether it sits in the historic hotel's podium shadow or the glass spire above it. https://www.jasonhutchison.ca/building/residences-hotel-georgia/
- **Two of the core's best-known towers are deliberately filed elsewhere.** Shangri-La and Vancouver House stand on VW-core streets but were counted in the downtown-ve file in C5, and the Fairmont Pacific Rim and Paradox residences are counted under Coal Harbour — a buyer searching only "Downtown VW" listings will miss all four unless they search by building name and address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md) and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/downtown-ve-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **The VW core has a hospital but no confirmed core elementary.** St. Paul's Hospital at 1081 Burrard Street is a genuine in-area amenity on the Burrard corridor, while the two Downtown elementaries sit at the area's south and east edges and no secondary school inside the area was confirmed — family buyers should run the VSB locator before assuming anything. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
- **Tate's townhouses hide at the base of a 40-storey tower.** The Tate Downtown development includes ground-oriented townhouse units (documented at 513 – 1283 Howe Street) inside a 333-unit strata whose manager records completion in 2018 — a buyer who filters searches to "townhouse" only, or to "apartment" only, sees only part of one development. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/513-1283-howe-st-106866 and https://vadapm.com/building-data/tate-downtown-1283-howe-street
- **No park, community centre or pool in the VW core was confirmed by name on a City page in the research pass** — the Coal Harbour Community Centre sits in the separate Coal Harbour sub-area and the West End's facilities sit in a separate local area, so neither is claimed here; the Central Library at 350 West Georgia Street is the core's confirmed civic anchor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/downtown-vw.md)
