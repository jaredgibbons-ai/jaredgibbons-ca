## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Sunnyside Park" is the Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name; the City of Surrey uses "Sunnyside" for the park, hall and area facilities in South Surrey, on the Semiahmoo peninsula between 16 Avenue and 24 Avenue. Do not confuse it with "Sunnyside Heights," a separate Grandview Heights neighbourhood plan area near 168 Street, 24 Avenue and Highway 99. The rest of the sub-area's attached stock is townhouses (Section 2, separate list) and the balance is detached houses. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For strata purchases generally, the Province's buyer paperwork path is Form B and Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies, with filings confirmed at the Land Title Office. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. For Sunnyside this is the Semiahmoo Estates question — a 1990/1991 wood-frame building whose first mandatory-cycle report covers roof, envelope and parkade cycles. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The building directory still shows "rentals not allowed" for Semiahmoo Estates — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Semiahmoo Estates is recorded as age-restricted and described in listings as 55+, and Bishop's Green townhouses are described as a 55+ community — confirm on the Form B whether each registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 — a City corporate report records that on June 15, 2026, Surrey 2050 OCP received final adoption from Council (the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current in this file). Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH zones RA to R6 allow up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan (16 Avenue to 24 Avenue, Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School) borders this sub-area and was updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunnyside-park-surrey.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Odyssey), not resales (Semiahmoo Estates). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at South Surrey prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to Odyssey purchases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Odyssey is built (2025 per listings), so remaining developer sales are completed-home sales — assignment terms for any original presale contract are UNCONFIRMED until that contract is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many apartment-condo buildings are actually in Sunnyside Park?** Two verified in-sub-area buildings — Semiahmoo Estates (2239 152 Street) and Odyssey (2620 152 Street / 2635 King George Boulevard) — plus the King George Corridor edge building South Point Walk (2960 151 Street). Everything else attached in the sub-area is townhouses (Peninsula Village, Bishop's Green) or detached houses. A third 152 Street building (2229 152 Street) is UNCONFIRMED in this file. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Peninsula Village a condo building?** No. Peninsula Village (2500 152 Street) is a gated townhouse complex built in 1993 — townhouse strata lots with a clubhouse and a private gate to the Peninsula Village Shopping Centre. It is documented under townhomes in Section 2 and is not counted as an apartment condo. https://www.robbiejohal.com/listing/r3098715-2500-152-street-67-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Q: The directory says Semiahmoo Estates bans rentals and pets. What is actually enforceable?** Rentals: a long-term rental-restriction bylaw has been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, so the directory's "rentals not allowed" line is old bylaw text. Pets: pet bylaws remain enforceable, and the directory records pets not allowed — read the registered bylaws. Age: the directory records age restrictions and listings describe a 55+ community; a 55+ bylaw is the one age bylaw BC law still allows. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/semiahmoo-estates-2239-152-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How old is Semiahmoo Estates, exactly?** The sources conflict: the building directory states 1991, while MLS listings and the building's MLS data sheet state 1990. This file carries it as 1990–1991 UNCONFIRMED — the strata plan and the depreciation report will settle it, and the difference matters less than the building's wood-frame construction (frame wood, brick and stucco exterior per the MLS data sheet) and its 57-unit scale. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/semiahmoo-estates-2239-152-street and https://www.cotala.com/tours/84334/FullPublic.pdf?i=586673301
- **Q: What is Odyssey, and is it finished?** Odyssey by Forge Properties is two 4-storey wood-frame low-rises with 132 condominiums between 152 Street and King George Boulevard (presale-directory source, secondary); current listings record units as built 2025 at both 2620 152 Street and 2635 King George Boulevard, so the buildings are complete and trading as resales/developer sales, not as a future presale. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/odyssey-by-forge-properties-south-surrey-luxury-presale-condos/
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy at Semiahmoo Estates?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must now have one every five years with a 30-year projection and three funding models, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. A 57-unit wood-frame building from 1990/1991 is at the age where roof and envelope items dominate the model — read the report before comparing its fee to Odyssey's. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve a 152 Street condo?** Surrey Schools (SD36) catchments are address-based through the district's School Locator. The verified locator result in the research file: 1785 148 Street returns H. T. Thrift Elementary and Semiahmoo Secondary (1785 148 Street). Run the building's exact address — the sub-area boundary is not the catchment boundary. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunnyside-park-surrey.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer stack the GST rebate and the PTT exemption at Odyssey?** Yes, they are separate programs: the FTHB GST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on/after March 20, 2025), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. Eligibility tests differ per program, and an Odyssey resale from a non-developer seller carries no GST at all — confirm which sale you are in before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How is transit from the 152 Street buildings?** Route 321 runs Surrey Central Station to White Rock Centre / White Rock South via King George Boulevard and 152 Street, stopping at White Rock Centre near this area; the Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was updated to align with the planned King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit. There is no rail transit in South Surrey. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunnyside-park-surrey.md)
- **Q: Is Sunnyside Park the same as Sunnyside Heights?** No. Sunnyside Heights is a Grandview Heights plan area by 168 Street, 24 Avenue and Highway 99; Sunnyside Park facilities such as Bakerview Park (1845 154 Street, with Sunnyside Community Hall) are in South Surrey proper. The plan rules and the school catchments differ — keep the names straight in search and in marketing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunnyside-park-surrey.md)
