## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

There is no verified strata stock in Popkum today; this block is the standard BC frame a buyer needs if strata product ever appears here, plus the FVRD, rural-tax and ALR rules that actually govern a Popkum purchase now.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Form B exists for a Popkum house or land parcel — if a listing here claims strata documents, that is a classification error to resolve before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. That voiding does NOT create a right to rent out a home in a pad-rent park: Mt Cheam's "no rentals" position (Section 2) is a park rule/tenancy matter, not a strata bylaw, and it is not voided by the strata amendment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful in a strata; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No strata exists in Popkum to carry such a bylaw, and the neighbouring Mt Cheam park is all-ages in any case (Section 2), under park rules, not the SPA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **FVRD plan and zoning — numbers UNVERIFIED where no FVRD source was opened, stated as such:** the guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) Official Community Plan, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, was adopted by the FVRD Board in January 2021, after a public hearing on December 15, 2020 — the bylaw text itself was not opened on an FVRD source in the research pass, so the bylaw number and adoption details are UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record and are not asserted here. The specific FVRD zoning bylaw and the zone of any Popkum parcel are likewise UNVERIFIED in this file — confirm the parcel's zone with the FVRD before relying on any use or building claim. Chilliwack's OCP and zoning bylaw numbers do NOT apply in Popkum — never borrow them across the municipal line. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/popkum-guide.md)
- **RURAL property taxes govern here — the staged provincial regime, not a city regime:** Popkum is unincorporated, so property taxes are levied under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act: taxes in a notice mailed on or before May 31 are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to taxes unpaid after July 2, and a further 5% to taxes still unpaid after October 31 (s. 11). For contrast only — explicitly NON-governing — the City of Maple Ridge's staged 5% + 5% dates (July 3 and September 2) and the City of Mission's flat 10% after July 2 are municipal regimes and do not apply in Electoral Area D. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **ALR frame:** on ALR land, the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when it is entirely contained within the principal residence and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Popkum property: UNVERIFIED — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA), for the park product nearby:** pad rent may rise at most once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, by no more than the yearly limit plus a proportional amount — 2026 = 2.3% plus proportional, 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. That regime applies to pad tenancies like Mt Cheam's (Section 2). No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Popkum is named in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% (0.60% at LTV up to 65% to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
