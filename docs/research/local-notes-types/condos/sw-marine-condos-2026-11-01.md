## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- S.W. Marine is the Greater Vancouver board's sub-area name ("S.W. Marine") for the southwest corner of the City of Vancouver along Southwest Marine Drive and the north arm of the Fraser River, taking in the Southlands area and the streets between roughly Granville Street and the Musqueam lands/Camosun Street side. Nearby areas are Kerrisdale, Marpole, Dunbar-Southlands and Oakridge, with Musqueam Indian Reserve 2 adjoining to the west/southwest. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- No market figures are stated for this area: the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report held for this program contains no City of Vancouver community sections at all — its GVR communities are Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only — so there is no Vancouver West community row and no S.W. Marine sub-area row to cite, and none is substituted. Vancouver is not SnapStats-covered in this program. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- Zoning frame: Southlands is governed by the RA-1 (Limited Agriculture) District, created in 1955 specifically for Southlands, whose intent is "to maintain and encourage the semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural nature of this District," permitting one-family dwellings and, in specific circumstances, infill one-family dwellings; the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve designation was implemented over the area in 1973, and the City's Southlands Plan (1988) remains the area plan, now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Elsewhere in the sub-area, standard detached lots fall under the R1-1 district (By-law No. 13817) — RA-1 lots are governed by their own district schedule, not R1-1. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- Schools (Ministry-listed names, SD39): Southlands Elementary (5351 Camosun Street), an International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme school and the designated catchment school for students from the Musqueam Nation. For the sample address 5351 Camosun Street the SD39 locator gives Southlands Elementary and Point Grey Secondary; catchments for other addresses are address-specific and otherwise UNVERIFIED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://media.vsb.bc.ca/media/Default/medialib/2026-southlands-slp.817ed592572.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- Flood setting: the City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements apply flood construction levels and setbacks to lands in the designated floodplain, which includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain, and the Standards contain a dedicated section (Section 6.0) for the RA-1 District; the City publishes the designated floodplain as an open dataset. Designation for an individual lot must be confirmed against that dataset. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- Transit is bus-based: TransLink's 49 (Metrotown Station–UBC), a Frequent Transit Network route, runs along West 49th Avenue and, west of Dunbar Street, along Southwest Marine Drive itself, with stops on SW Marine Drive (e.g., at Blenheim and Balaclava Streets) before continuing to UBC; the nearest Canada Line station is Marine Drive Station in Marpole, east of the sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)

Shopping is outside the sub-area: there is no retail strip within S.W. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. For Gulf View Court, a 1980s low-rise, the depreciation report and CRF position are the central diligence items. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a corridor building is VOID for long-term rentals (Gulf View Court's listing data carries a rental-restriction field; it does not survive this rule); short-term rentals can still be restricted, and Vancouver's own short-term rental rules apply inside the city. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No counted building here is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the RA-1 District Schedule (Zoning and Development By-law, City of Vancouver) shapes most of this sub-area: intent to maintain the semi-rural, equestrian and limited agricultural character, permitting single detached houses and, in specific circumstances, infill single detached houses — there is no strata, townhouse or apartment form in the district schedule. The area plan is the Southlands Plan (1988), now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026). Standard detached lots outside RA-1 fall under R1-1 (By-law No. 13817, up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units) — those permissions must not be quoted for RA-1 lots without a zoning confirmation. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **ALR overlay:** the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve designation was implemented over Southlands in 1973; whether an individual lot sits inside the ALR boundary must be confirmed parcel by parcel, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence rules on top of RA-1. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **Flood:** the City's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements (adopted September 16, 2014, effective January 1, 2015, amended March 7, 2023) impose flood construction level and setback requirements under the Vancouver Building By-law on designated-floodplain lands, and Section 6.0 of the Standards deals specifically with the RA-1 District. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%.** The City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty; for the 2026 cycle the annual declaration was due February 3, 2026 and payment April 16, 2026. Vancouver's 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the counted building is resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the resale building in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in S.W. Marine was confirmed on a developer source in the research pass — the one corridor application on record (2090 SW Marine Dr) was withdrawn in September 2025. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/2090-sw-marine-dr?tool=qanda
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos to buy in S.W. Marine?** One verified building, at the eastern edge: Gulf View Court (8772 SW Marine Drive, 24 units, freehold strata, build year UNCONFIRMED between 1986 and 1988) — counted here under the research file's street geography, with its MLS Marpole classification flagged at the top of this file. - **Q: Why is there no condo or townhouse stock to downsize into in Southlands?** Zoning: the RA-1 district (in place since 1955) permits single detached houses and only limited infill single detached houses, so there is effectively no strata, rental-apartment or townhouse supply inside Southlands itself; the sub-area's attached stock is confined to the conventional streets at its Kerrisdale and Marpole edges. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf
- **Q: Are The Granary flats or District Flats an S.W. Marine option?** No — those are Southlands **Tsawwassen** projects (Century Group, City of Delta), counted in the Boundary Beach condos file. - **Q: Can I rent out a Gulf View Court unit?** Long-term, yes as a matter of provincial law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, whatever the listing data's rental-restriction field says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term rental rules apply. Read the registered bylaws on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Gulf View Court?** A current listing records pets "Cats OK" for the building — pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws on the Form B are the final word for any other pet position. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/8772-south-west-marine-drive/402
- **Q: Is there any seniors housing in the area?** Only rental and care, nothing to buy: the Hungarian Senior Citizens Home (1564 SW Marine Drive, 54 units, non-profit, 1988) at the eastern edge, and Casa Mia (1920 SW Marine Drive), a VCH-contracted long-term care home in a 1932 heritage estate. There is no independent-living or assisted-living retirement residence in the sub-area. https://canadabuys.canada.ca/en/tender-opportunities/tender-notice/bcitt1070-2425-080 and http://www.vch.ca/en/location/casa-mia
- **Q: Can I use Vancouver's multiplex rules to split a big Southlands lot?** Do not assume so: By-law No. 13817's R1-1 multiplex permissions apply to standard detached lots, but RA-1 Southlands lots are governed by their own district schedule (one dwelling per lot, limited infill in specific circumstances), not R1-1 — a zoning confirmation is required before quoting any multiplex or subdivision value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-ra-1.pdf
- **Q: Is flooding a concern on the riverfront streets?** Yes, and it is regulated: the City's Flood Plain Standards apply flood construction levels and setbacks to designated-floodplain lands including the Fraser River coastal floodplain, with a dedicated section for the RA-1 district. Check the specific lot against the City's designated-floodplain dataset and look for registered flood covenants on title before subject removal. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: What does the Empty Homes Tax mean for an estate purchase here?** Vancouver charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the declaration due February 3, 2026 for the 2026 cycle — on S.W. Marine assessed values, leaving a purchased home vacant while planning a move is an expensive default. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two districts named Southlands, two municipalities, one search box.** Vancouver's RA-1 Southlands (this file) and Delta's Century Group Southlands at Tsawwassen (Boundary Beach file) share a name and nothing else — different cities, different zoning, different housing forms. Every "Southlands condo" result has to be municipally placed before it is compared. (Boundary Beach condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/boundary-beach-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The semi-rural character is locked in by bylaw, not by neighbours.** Southlands' RA-1 district has held since 1955 and its area plan since 1988 — "protected character" is a documented policy fact here, which is also why no downsizer product will be built inside Southlands under the current framework. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf
- **The one care home in the area is inside a 1932 heritage mansion.** Casa Mia — built for brewer George Conrad Reifel, "A"-listed on the Vancouver Heritage Register — was rezoned CD-1 in the 2014 process specifically to become a Community Care Facility for seniors with heritage designation attached; the care home and the protected mansion are the same property. https://council.vancouver.ca/20140218/documents/p3.pdf (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/sw-marine-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Casa Mia's bed count depends on which source you read.** Vancouver Coastal Health's current listing says 64 subsidized beds; published total-bed figures run 87, 90 and 95 across a 2016 announcement, a 2017 advisory-committee letter and a Family Practice listing. Anyone quoting a single total is picking one source — the file flags the conflict rather than choosing. http://www.vch.ca/en/location/casa-mia (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/sw-marine-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Three tenures sit within a few blocks of each other.** City of Vancouver freehold (RA-1/R1-1) and Musqueam reserve leasehold at the area's edge finance, insure and resell differently — and Southlands Elementary, inside the city, is the catchment school for Musqueam students, so the two communities are interleaved even though the land jurisdiction is not. https://media.vsb.bc.ca/media/Default/medialib/2026-southlands-slp.817ed592572.pdf
- **"SW Marine Drive" in a headline does not mean S.W. Marine.** The Marine Gateway and W1 towers at 433–488 SW Marine Drive are Marpole product at Marine Drive Station, and Waterside at 2250 SE Marine Drive is a prepaid-leasehold building on the SE corridor in Vancouver East — Marine Drive addresses run across the whole south side of the city, and the sub-area in this file is only the Granville-to-Musqueam stretch. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/2003-455-sw-marine-dr-106232 and https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/202-2250-se-marine-dr-49256
- **A withdrawn rezoning shows which way proposals go here.** The 2090 SW Marine Dr application (heritage retention plus two infill buildings with 25 secured market rental units, R1-1 to CD-1) was withdrawn in September 2025 — rental infill at the sub-area's edge is proposed project-by-project, not permitted as of right. https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/2090-sw-marine-dr?tool=qanda
