## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In London Landing's mixed-use buildings, read the depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early — the strata shares a building with commercial components. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (London Landing file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/lower-landing-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a Steveston building is VOID for long-term rentals (The Pier's published position is rentals allowed in any case); short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No London Landing building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000) guides the whole district. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: houses in RS1/RS2 Single Detached zones (Section 8.1) with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under Bill 44; village commercial and mixed-use zones include Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village. London Landing's own zones are site-specific: ZMU20 – London Landing (Steveston) (Section 20.20, created by Bylaw 8818 on September 24, 2012; maximum FAR 1.2 plus 0.1, lot coverage 76%, height 21.0 m) and ZMU24 at 13040 No. 2 Road (Bylaw 9094, 2014). A live application (RZ 25-009451, reported July 8, 2026) proposes rezoning 12771–13131 No. 2 Road to a new ZMU63 – London Landing (Steveston) zone for a five- and six-storey mixed-use project — an application in process, not an approval. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/steveston-homes-for-sale.md) (London Landing file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/lower-landing-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Heritage:** the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront; properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan, and protected heritage buildings and other resource types such as docks and landscape features are identified in the Official Community Plan. Whether any London Landing parcel sits inside the conservation area boundary is UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks — for waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots, confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/steveston-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Richmond property taxes:** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/steveston-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $835,000 (the First-Time Home Buyers' Program ceiling, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,700 (2% × $635,000) = **$14,700 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the resale buildings in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in Steveston was confirmed on a developer source in the research pass — the No. 2 Road project is a rezoning application, not a presale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/steveston-homes-for-sale.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The page's name for its best condo pocket is wrong, and the zoning bylaw proves the right one.** "Lower Landing" appears in no City source read; "London Landing (Steveston)" is written into Zoning Bylaw No. 8500 itself (ZMU20 at Section 20.20, Bylaw 8818, 2012; ZMU24 at Bylaw 9094, 2014; a proposed ZMU63 in 2026). A buyer searching "Lower Landing" will miss the record. (London Landing file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/lower-landing-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Garry Street is a seniors-housing street because of a temple land deal.** Wisteria Place exists because the Steveston Buddhist Temple retained ownership of its land and spearheaded the project through a separate non-profit, the Wisteria Senior Health and Housing Society, with Optima Living as operator — which is why Steveston South's newest seniors building is non-profit rental beside the temple, not a strata project a buyer can purchase into. Lions Park and River's End Co-op sit on the same street. https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/
- **Two seniors buildings share one Chatham Street block, with two different admission doors.** The Maple Residences (serviced rental, apply to the society) and Bill Rigby Senior Apartments (subsidized, apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry) stand at 4071 and 4100 Chatham Street — a buyer touring one will see the other, and they are not the same product, price system or application route. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The assisted-living building on Moncton Street is priced as a share of income, not a rent.** Austin Harris Residence residents pay 70% of after-tax income for the suite plus hospitality services under the Independent Living BC Program — the reason it cannot be compared monthly-rate-to-monthly-rate with The Maple Residences two streets away. https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/
- **The industrial strip beside the London Landing condos is mid-conversion.** The five No. 2 Road lots in RZ 25-009451 (12771–13131) are Light Industrial today; the same July 2026 staff process also carries an OCP redesignation of No. 2 Road lands here from "Industrial" to "Local Village – Tier 1." The pocket's neighbours are changing by application, and the file numbers (RZ 25-009451, Bylaws 10771/10772) are the verifiable way to track it. (London Landing file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/lower-landing-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The school locator simply has no answer for Moncton Street.** The SD38 locator returned no result for 4011 Moncton Street because the address falls outside its listed ranges for the street — village catchment marketing in a listing is a claim to verify with the district directly, not a fact to repeat. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Richmond has no SnapStats coverage in this program.** There is no community median to quote for Steveston — any Richmond median in marketing copy comes from a source outside this file's verification, and this file substitutes none. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/steveston-homes-for-sale.md)
