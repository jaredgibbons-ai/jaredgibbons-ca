# Hawthorne — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Hawthorne is a Paragon sub-area name in Ladner, in the City of Delta — it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and it falls under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Hawthorne is the north-Ladner pocket built around Hawthorne Elementary, 5160 Central Avenue, and Hawthorne Park, 5151 56 Street. https://ha.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/hawthorne-park
- Neilsen Grove is to the north, Delta Manor to the south around Delta Secondary and Memorial Park, and Holly to the east (working geography from the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/hawthorne-guide.md).
- Central Avenue, 57 Street and Ladner Trunk Road (Highway 10) are the roads named on TransLink's route 618 loop through Hawthorne; route 618 runs from Ladner Exchange via Harvest Drive, Ladner Trunk Road, Central Avenue, Linden Drive, Westminster Avenue and Ferry Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: the 53 Street corridor west of Central Avenue — Ladner Pointe and Green Gables sit on the same few blocks of 53 Street — plus The Pointe on 55B Street and Keira Gardens on Linden Drive. This is Ladner's main low-rise condo corridor, a short drive or bus ride from Ladner Exchange, where route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- The SD37 School Locator result for the sample address 5160 Central Avenue is Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12) at 4615 51 St.; catchments are address-based, so check any specific address in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Ladner Leisure Centre, 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent, and Ladner Pioneer Library, 4683 51st Street, are the recreation and library anchors for the area; Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, sits beside Ladner Exchange. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection of repair and replacement costs and three CRF cash-flow models. For Hawthorne's 1987–1991 wood-frame buildings, the envelope, roof and parkade items in that report are the core due-diligence read. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. No Hawthorne building is verified as 55+ in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600.** Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps that form part of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; the 53 Street buildings sit in zones set when they were built, so a building's current zone is read off the City's zoning maps, not assumed. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it has no lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — this affects the detached streets around the condo corridor, not the existing strata buildings. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale condos carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) for homes valued at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person withdrawn from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined for the same home. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment — the 0.6%–4.5% range buyers see quoted. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the difference between Ladner Pointe and The Pointe?** They are different buildings on different streets despite the near-identical names: Ladner Pointe is the 1990–91-era complex at 4821/4845 53 Street, while The Pointe is the 2016 building at 4815 55B Street. Check the civic address on the Form B, not the marketing name. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3163648-b205-4821-53-street-delta-bc-v4k-2z3 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3070278-4815-55b-street-301-delta-bc-v4k-3v7/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out my unit?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Listings record pets allowed at Ladner Pointe and "Pets welcome" at Green Gables. Pet bylaws are strata-specific and can limit number, size and type — the registered bylaws attached to the Form B decide. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3163648-b205-4821-53-street-delta-bc-v4k-2z3 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3115694-314-4885-53-street-delta-british-columbia-v4k2z3-639000/brochure
- **Q: Green Gables is from 1987 — has the building envelope been dealt with?** A current listing describes Green Gables as rain screened. Beyond that one word, ask for the depreciation report's envelope items, any engineering reports, and the strata minutes for envelope history — that paper trail, not the listing, answers the question. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3115694-314-4885-53-street-delta-british-columbia-v4k2z3-639000/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Q: How do I know if a special levy is coming?** The Form B must disclose any approved-but-unpaid special levy and the CRF balance, and the attached depreciation report shows the 30-year repair list with three funding models — a thin CRF against a big envelope or roof item is the classic levy setup. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: What does the strata insurance deductible look like?** Since 2023 the Form B must include a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage. In wood-frame low-rises the water-damage deductible is the exposure that matters, because deductibles are routinely charged back to the unit a leak started in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is parking one stall or two?** It varies by building and unit: a top-floor listing at The Pointe records two parking stalls plus a bike room in the garage, while older-building listings typically record one stall. The Form B states the specific unit's parking and storage allocation — rely on it, not the listing. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3070278-4815-55b-street-301-delta-bc-v4k-3v7/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve the 53 Street buildings?** Catchments are address-based; the locator result for 5160 Central Avenue is Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12), but each building address must be run in the SD37 locator itself. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Hawthorne in a flood area?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and any property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Flood construction requirements are confirmed lot by lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: What new supply is coming near Hawthorne?** The named project in the sub-area is Wildwood Ladner, a presale of 40 four-bedroom townhomes by Shato Holdings Ltd. at 4900 Central Avenue — townhomes, not condos, per the developer source cited in the area research file (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/hawthorne-guide.md; developer site https://wildwoodladner.com/).

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion and major construction expected to begin in 2027. That crossing is the commute every Hawthorne condo buyer is pricing. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **The newest condo building here is already a decade old.** The Pointe (2016) is the most recent apartment building verified in Hawthorne; the rest of the apartment stock is 1987–1991 wood frame. New supply in and around the sub-area is arriving as townhouses (Wildwood Ladner) and as the village-core presale in the Ladner Elementary sub-area, not as new Hawthorne apartment buildings (Sections 2 and 4 above).
- **The 2024 OCP removed the old area-plan structure.** Older City documents refer to a Ladner Area Plan as Schedule B.3 of the former OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), has no lettered schedules — future change in Hawthorne is governed by the single Land Use Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH changed the detached streets in 2024.** Delta's June 24, 2024 SSMUH adoption (Bylaws 8394 and 8395) lets many detached lots around the condo corridor add suites, duplex units, coach houses and garden suites without a rezoning. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Ladner Village revitalization is an active City program.** The City's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan, including the Chisholm Street Wharf project, covers the village core west of Hawthorne. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **There is no Hawthorne market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Ladner community section and no Hawthorne row — its only Delta section is North Delta — so any "Hawthorne median condo price" quoted from that source does not exist (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/hawthorne-guide.md).
