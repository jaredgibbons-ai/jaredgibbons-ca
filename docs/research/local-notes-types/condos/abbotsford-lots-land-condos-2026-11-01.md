## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR land cannot be stratified as residences — the lead rule for this file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A farm parcel's house can never be sold as a strata condo lot, and no ALR parcel in this feed is future condo stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **ALR housing frame (ALC):** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; building more or larger requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may further restrict residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Agricultural subdivision scale:** the City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). Urban residential zone minimum lot sizes are UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this program — the Zoning Bylaw text sits on laws.abbotsford.ca, which was unreachable from the research fetch service on 2026-11-01; program-carried council-report figures exist but no minimum-lot figure is relied on in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Zoning and plan checks are parcel-by-parcel:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400 sets what can be built as-of-right; the Abbotsforward 2050 OCP (adopted December 2025, current) sets future land use. The City's Development Planning page directs current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas to the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap. Which development permit areas (the live page names Steep Slope, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Interface and Floodplain) apply to a given lot, its floodplain position under Zoning Bylaw Schedule E, and its servicing (municipal connections at the lot line vs well and septic) are all per-parcel checks: UNVERIFIED for any specific feed parcel in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **SSMUH and suites on non-ALR urban lots:** Abbotsford amended its Zoning Bylaw in June 2024 for provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas (3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots bigger than 280 m²), accessory units counting toward the total. The SSMUH amending bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages in this program — it is not asserted in this file. A secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the principal unit's size; coach houses and garden suites may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m², with 1 required parking stall for each accessory unit type under Zoning Bylaw Section 150. SSMUH product is generally not strata condo product. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Development cost charges:** UNVERIFIED in this program — no City of Abbotsford DCC bylaw or rate page was opened, and no DCC figure is stated in this file. Get the current rates for the specific proposal from the City before subjects come off. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — stated here because it is the standard block for this series and because it is the document that would govern any bare-land strata lot purchase: on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For an ordinary freehold vacant lot there is no strata and no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata parcels only):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. This matters only where a parcel is strata-titled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **GST — stated only as CRA sources support:** the live page's GST summary for land sales is UNVERIFIED in the research file — no CRA source was opened in that pass — and it is not restated as fact here. What this file does state: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales, as a general frame; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) rebates 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031, and it is a new-housing rebate, not a bare-land rebate — whether a specific land-plus-build arrangement qualifies is UNCONFIRMED per transaction and needs CRA-based advice; the GST New Housing Rebate is 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. GST treatment of a specific vacant-land purchase (which turns on the seller's status and the land's use) must be confirmed with a lawyer or accountant before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: hypothetical fair market value $800,000, fully residential, no exemption claimed (a stated hypothetical, not a parcel price): $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — it applies to newly built homes, not to bare land standing alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** vacant land is not a presale product; where a developer sells a strata lot or unit before completion, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (mortgage default insurance applies to homes, not to a bare-land purchase standing alone). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Pad-rent parks (only where parks are discussed):** pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, and the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. A pad-rent park parcel is not strata condo stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the market numbers in this file land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the Section 1 figures are Abbotsford residential context only (detached median $949,000; attached median $497,500, September 2026), and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Price a lot on its own zone, services and comparable land sales. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I build a house on vacant ALR land in Abbotsford?** Under the ALC frame, generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite only inside that principal residence; and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). City zoning must also permit the residence, and anything larger or more numerous needs ALC approval. The live page's FAQ answer omits the fill limit and the additional-residence tiers. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Can I stratify an ALR home or split it into condo units later?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). An ALR purchase is a single-title purchase; do not price in a future strata exit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How small a lot can I create in Abbotsford?** On the agricultural zones the City's parcel-size work records subdivision minimums of 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2). Urban residential zone minimums are UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this program — confirm the specific zone's minimum with the City for the parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How do I check zoning, plan designation and permit areas for a lot?** The City's Development Planning page directs current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas to the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap; which permit areas and floodplain provisions apply to a specific lot is that per-parcel check, and no feed parcel's status is verified in this file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-lots-land.md)
