# Canyon Heights — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Canyon Heights is an Upper Capilano neighbourhood in the District of North Vancouver, on the Highland Boulevard corridor, west of Edgemont Village and east of the Capilano River. Exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchor is the Highland Boulevard corridor, with Canyon Heights Elementary at 4501 Highland Boulevard inside the area. Nearby areas: Edgemont Village (east), Capilano Highlands (north), Forest Hills (north-east), Grouse Woods (north-west) and Queensdale (south-east). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- Where the District's condo stock concentrates — Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lions Gate Village and Edgemont Village — is outside Canyon Heights, with Edgemont the immediate neighbour to the east. Inside Canyon Heights the housing is detached houses under the District's R1 and R2 detached residential zones (Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525), and the research file confirms no named condo buildings or townhome complexes in the area on an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- Main streets: Highland Boulevard is the main road, carrying TransLink routes 246 and 232; Capilano Road on the west side carries routes 232 and 236 toward Grouse Mountain and south to Marine Drive and the Lions Gate Bridge; the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1) is reached via Capilano Road or Edgemont Boulevard. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- Transit anchors: route 246 (Downtown / Highland) runs via Marine Drive, Capilano Road, Ridgewood Drive, Edgemont Boulevard, Highland Boulevard and Montroyal Boulevard, with downtown-bound trips crossing the Lions Gate Bridge; route 232 (Grouse Mountain / Phibbs Exchange) runs via Highland Boulevard, Edgemont Boulevard and Capilano Road; route 236 runs on Capilano Road. There is no rapid transit and no condo node inside Canyon Heights. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- Market numbers: the District of North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Canyon Heights against Edgemont Village or the District's other centres will meet them there; inside Canyon Heights itself there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Edgemont or District-centre building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Canyon Heights sits under the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (including its Schedule B Development Permit Areas) and District Zoning Bylaw 3210 — Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 minimum lot at 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth and standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite and a coach house is a detached accessory dwelling unit. A Canyon Heights-specific plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). Canyon Heights is in the District, not the City of North Vancouver. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at North Shore prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Canyon Heights is confirmed, so this is relevant only to presales a buyer compares in Edgemont or the District's centres. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571 in 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos for sale in Canyon Heights itself?** No verified strata apartment condos exist in Canyon Heights on any source checked — the area is detached houses on R1/R2 lots between the Capilano River and the Mosquito/Mackay creek ravines, and the research file records named condo buildings and townhome complexes as none confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real condo stock?** Edgemont Village, immediately east on the same Highland Boulevard corridor: Crescentview at Edgemont (3121 Connaught Crescent, a 3-storey wood-frame low-rise completed 2023 with 22 condominiums) and Edgemont Villa (3151 Connaught Crescent, built 2003) are the verified boutique stock, with the District's larger centres at Lynn Valley and Lions Gate Village beyond. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Is the "EIGHT" project on Highland Boulevard a Canyon Heights condo?** No. The listing for 3206 Highland Boulevard describes eight duplex-style 3-bedroom homes "in the heart of Edgemont Village" — Edgemont product in duplex form, not a Canyon Heights apartment condo. https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/3206-highland-boulevard-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7r-2v9-30289712/
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Canyon Heights?** No. Since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, but they sit on one lot's title — there is no separate strata lot to buy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: How much density could a Canyon Heights lot take under SSMUH?** The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set the same 3 / 4 / 6 ladder by lot size and bus-stop distance. It is a per-lot classification, not a street-wide entitlement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Canyon Heights address?** The locator result verified in the research file, for 4501 Highland Boulevard, is Canyon Heights Elementary (K–7) and Handsworth Secondary (8–12); Canyon Heights Elementary is described by SD44 as a K–7 school "located at the foot of Grouse Mountain" in the Handsworth Family of Schools. Highlands Elementary (3150 Colwood Drive) and Cleveland Elementary (1255 Eldon Road) sit beside the area — catchments are address-based, so confirm each address with SD44. SD44 has no middle tier. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: How is transit from Canyon Heights without a SkyTrain?** By bus: route 246 runs from Highland Boulevard directly downtown over the Lions Gate Bridge, route 232 links the area to Grouse Mountain and Phibbs Exchange, route 236 runs on Capilano Road, and the SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks matter on river-side and steep lots?** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. A west-side or steep lot can fall in one of these areas along the Capilano River side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: If I buy in Edgemont Village instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a directory still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the library and recreation offer if there is no condo amenity building?** The Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, is in Edgemont Village east of Canyon Heights on the same boulevard, and the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, south-east of the area, is the nearest full-service NVRC centre with a main pool and leisure pool and indoor racquet facilities. Trail access is Capilano River Regional Park's Capilano Pacific Trail and Coho Loop Trail on the west side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The boulevard changes neighbourhoods mid-corridor.** Highland Boulevard carries both Canyon Heights and Edgemont addresses: the verified condo stock on Connaught Crescent and the "EIGHT" duplex project at 3206 Highland Boulevard are Edgemont, while the Canyon Heights stretch west of the village is detached houses only — a "Highland Boulevard condo" search result is almost never in Canyon Heights. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md) and https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/3206-highland-boulevard-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7r-2v9-30289712/
- **Three units per house lot is already the rule.** Since June 2024 every single-family lot within the Urban Containment Boundary can hold a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — in Canyon Heights the realistic "smaller attached purchase" is a house with a suite, bought as one title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **The flood data runs to 2100.** The District's GeoWeb flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m — a long-horizon number that can surprise a buyer pricing a river-side renovation off today's water levels. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Murdo Frazer's courts were restated in 2025.** The Edgemont-area District park east of Canyon Heights carries 6 tennis courts and 6 pickleball courts in the District's Outdoor Sports Courts Optimization Strategy, approved July 21, 2025 — the shared amenity base for a neighbourhood with no strata amenity rooms. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **French Immersion is not catchment-locked here.** Handsworth Secondary offers French Immersion, and SD44 states French Immersion is offered across the district regardless of geographical catchment, so a Canyon Heights address does not lock a child out of the program. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **No project is coming to change the count.** No approved major project or presale specific to Canyon Heights is confirmed on a District source — the zero-condo finding in Section 2 is the current state, not a gap a named pipeline is about to fill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
