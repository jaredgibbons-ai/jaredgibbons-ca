## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality and identity: Shaughnessy is the City of Vancouver's original prestige residential district, on the ridge between roughly West 16th Avenue (north) and West 37th/41st Avenues (south), Oak Street (east) and Arbutus/East Boulevard (west). First Shaughnessy, the Heritage Conservation Area north of King Edward Avenue, is bounded by West 16th, Marpole, Wolfe and Richelieu Avenues to the north, Arbutus Street to the west, West King Edward Avenue to the south, and the west lane of Oak Street to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Market figures: **none are published in this file** — the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report contains no City of Vancouver sections at all, so no sub-area or community median, inventory or sales ratio exists in the permitted source for Shaughnessy, and none is substituted. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Where the condo stock sits: there is essentially one address — Shaughnessy Place at 4900 Cartier Street beside VanDusen Botanical Garden at the district's south-east edge — plus townhouse-form strata at the Oak Street / West 37th Avenue edge (Section 2). The balance of the district is freehold estate houses; the First Shaughnessy District Schedule's stated intent is to protect the district's estate character, and there is no commercial strip inside Shaughnessy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Heritage frame: First Shaughnessy was designated a Heritage Conservation Area on September 29, 2015 (Heritage Conservation Area Official Development Plan, By-law No. 11349); protected (pre-1940) heritage properties can only be removed from the protected list through a Council process following a heritage character and value assessment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Schools (SD39, Ministry-listed names, address-based): Shaughnessy Elementary (4250 Marguerite Street) and Prince of Wales Secondary (2250 Eddington Drive) — the locator run for 4250 Marguerite Street returned exactly that pair. Independent schools in-area: York House School (4176 Alexandra Street, independent girls' JK–12, founded 1932) and Little Flower Academy (4195 Alexandra Street, independent Catholic girls' 8–12, founded 1927). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Anchors and transit: VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak Street at West 37th Avenue, 55 acres, Park Board / Vancouver Botanical Gardens Association) anchors the south-east edge. Shaughnessy has no rapid transit station; the frequent transit corridors around it are King Edward Avenue (#25), West 41st Avenue (#41), West 49th Avenue (#49) and Arbutus Street / West Boulevard (#14), and the Arbutus Greenway runs along the western edge. Nearest village shopping: Kerrisdale and South Granville; nearest Park Board community centre: Kerrisdale Community Centre, 5851 West Boulevard. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- Nearby areas: Kerrisdale, Arbutus Ridge, South Granville, Oakridge, Quilchena and Douglas Park. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. For Shaughnessy Place, the Form B is also where the mixed apartment/townhouse cost sharing is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. A 1970s gated complex with a pool and extensive grounds is exactly the profile where both documents drive the price discussion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a Shaughnessy building is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted (and the City of Vancouver separately licenses short-term rentals). **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Shaughnessy building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** citywide land use is governed by the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026) and the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. First Shaughnessy has its own District Schedule plus the HCA Official Development Plan (By-law No. 11349, 2015): maximum floor space ratio 0.45 where a protected heritage property is conserved, otherwise 0.25 plus 139 sq m to a maximum floor area of 911 sq m; multiple dwellings are permitted only on sites without protected heritage property. Subdivision By-law Schedule A sets First Shaughnessy minimums of 100 ft (30.48 m) parcel width and 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 sq m) parcel area. South of King Edward, the multiplex by-law (By-law No. 13817) replaced the RS districts with R1-1 (up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units) — its interaction with the First Shaughnessy District Schedule must be confirmed property by property. Shaughnessy Place's own zoning is the site-specific CD-1 (65), By-law No. 4532 (1971). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md) and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/cd-1(065).pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%.** Shaughnessy is inside the City of Vancouver, so the City's Empty Homes Tax (3% of assessed value) applies to a vacant Shaughnessy property, on top of the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax framework. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes and additional school tax:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after the due date. Because most Shaughnessy homes assess above $3 million, BC's additional school tax is material: 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% effective January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Shaughnessy Place is resale stock; a newly completed Whitford home purchased from the developer is the GST-exposed form here. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the top band is live at Shaughnessy price points. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Whitford was marketed as a presale and completes through late 2026 per its sources — early purchasers' rescission windows are spent, but assignment and completion terms still run off the disclosure statement. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premiums 0.60%–4.50% (insured-mortgage price ceilings put CMHC insurance out of reach for most Shaughnessy purchases in practice — the published premium range is stated for completeness). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Shaughnessy at all?** One verified apartment-condo address: Shaughnessy Place (4900 Cartier Street), a gated 1970s strata community beside VanDusen Garden that also contains townhouse units — counted once, as one mixed complex. Everything else in the district is estate houses plus townhouse-form strata at the edges. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Whitford a condo building?** No — Whitford (Oak Street at West 37th Avenue) is a 68-home townhouse and garden-suite community by Grosvenor and Citimark, framed by its own developers as townhomes. Its single-level garden suites live like condos but are documented here as townhouse-form product and are not counted as apartment condos. https://WWW.GROSVENOR.COM/news-insights/grosvenor-and-citimark-moving-forward-with-fastest-selling-west-side-townhome-project-in-vancouver-7817e06251d856afc7cda3356b7853e5
- **Q: Is this the same as The Shaughnessy condo tower?** No. The Shaughnessy is in Port Coquitlam. Shaughnessy the neighbourhood is Vancouver West; Shaughnessy Place is its one condo complex. (Central Pt Coquitlam file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-pt-coquitlam-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What does the Heritage Conservation Area do to a purchase?** North of King Edward Avenue, protected pre-1940 homes cannot simply be demolished — removal from the protected list needs a heritage character and value assessment and Council approval — but conservation unlocks incentives: coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill options, and the higher 0.45 FSR (versus 0.25 plus 139 sq m, capped at 911 sq m, otherwise). Establish protected status before pricing any First Shaughnessy property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **Q: What are Shaughnessy condos worth?** No figure is quoted in this file: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the reports held for this program, so there is no verified median. With one complex, price off Shaughnessy Place's own sale history. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Shaughnessy Place address?** SD39 catchments are address-based; the locator-settled pair for the in-area sample address (4250 Marguerite Street) is Shaughnessy Elementary and Prince of Wales Secondary. Run the Cartier Street address in the locator before relying on it — no locator run for 4900 Cartier Street is on record. York House School and Little Flower Academy are the in-area independent schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply?** Yes — Shaughnessy is in the City of Vancouver, so a vacant property is exposed to the City's 3% Empty Homes Tax, in addition to provincial vacancy taxes. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Is there any 55+ or seniors housing in Shaughnessy?** None of any kind — the 55+ program found zero seniors housing (ownership, rental or care) inside the sub-area. A buyer wanting an age-restricted or serviced option has to leave the neighbourhood. (Standing fact for this batch.)
- **Q: How big can a replacement house be on an estate lot?** Smaller than the lot suggests: the First Shaughnessy District Schedule caps floor space at 0.45 FSR with a conserved protected heritage property (0.25 plus 139 sq m, max 911 sq m, otherwise), and subdivision minimums are 100 ft width and 13,000 sq ft area — confirm the specific lot with the City. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Shaughnessy's only condo complex exists because of a garden.** The CD-1 (65) by-law (No. 4532, 1971) rezoned the VanDusen Gardens site and the Shaughnessy Place site in one instrument — botanical garden on one part, "town houses and garden apartments" on the other — which is why the district's sole strata complex sits against the garden fence at 4900 Cartier Street and nowhere else. https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/cd-1(065).pdf
- **Its phases disagree about their own birthday.** Apartment listings say 1976, a building page says 1978 for Shaughnessy Place I, and a townhouse record says 1980 for Phase II — this file carries all three as UNCONFIRMED individually rather than picking one, because the depreciation report, not the marketing, is where the build date matters. https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/shaughnessy-place-i
- **The newest strata in Shaughnessy replaced eight house lots — with townhouses, not condos.** Whitford's 68 homes went onto an Oak Street assembly of eight single-family lots (seven per one trade report — the lot count itself conflicts, UNCONFIRMED) under the Cambie Corridor framework, and the developers still framed the product as townhomes and garden suites. Even Shaughnessy's densification arrives in townhouse form. https://WWW.GROSVENOR.COM/news-insights/grosvenor-and-citimark-moving-forward-with-fastest-selling-west-side-townhome-project-in-vancouver-7817e06251d856afc7cda3356b7853e5
- **Heritage protection is also a density tool.** The HCA's incentives — coach house, multiple conversion and infill permissions, plus the 0.45 FSR available only when a protected heritage property is conserved — mean a protected house can carry more buildable floor space than an unprotected neighbour (0.25 plus 139 sq m). Retention is priced into the zoning, not just the architecture. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **The additional school tax is a five-figure line here.** At Shaughnessy assessments, BC's additional school tax (0.2% on $3M–$4M, 0.4% above $4M, rising to 0.3%/0.6% on January 1, 2027) is a material annual carrying cost on top of City taxes — model it before comparing a Shaughnessy Place unit's strata fees against a house's upkeep. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
- **Prince of Wales Secondary started life on the elementary school's site.** The secondary opened in 1920 at today's Shaughnessy Elementary address (4250 Marguerite Street) and moved to Eddington Drive in 1960 — the two catchment schools for the district share one origin site. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
