## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the Bench houses, resort interests and pad homes that make up this page's stock — there is no verified strata building in Hatzic for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in Hatzic, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Park age rules are a different instrument: Mission Mobile Home Park's 55+ is a park rule under the MHPTA, and the SPA exemptions do not govern it — read the park rules instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Property taxes, two systems on one page — confirm the PID first.** City of Mission properties (Bench): 2026 taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw), plus a diking levy on top of the other levies. Rural (FVRD Prairie/Island) properties: the owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City; under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. Never import Mission's flat 10% into the Prairie or Island, and never import the rural 5% + 5% onto the Bench. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/hatzic.md)
- **Secondary suites, two frames on one page:** inside the City (Bench only), all secondary suites must be registered with the City, a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, and suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes. In FVRD Electoral Area F (Prairie), secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure such as a garden suite. Which frame applies depends on which side of the City/FVRD line the parcel sits on. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/hatzic.md)
