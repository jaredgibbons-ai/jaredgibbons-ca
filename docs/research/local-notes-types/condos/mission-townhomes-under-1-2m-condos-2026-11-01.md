## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a townhouse strata, the Form B is also where the complex's form (townhouse vs detached strata lot) and its registered bylaws are settled — read it before relying on a feed label. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Cherry Lane (1989) and Luther Place (1988) are at the age where roof and envelope cycles dominate the report; Briskham Hill (2025/2026) is at the opposite end. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of Cherry Lane's directory line in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex and Cherry Lane's directory records age restrictions — confirm on each Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans, zoning and taxes:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 with the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, plus a Regional Context Statement; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. City of Mission zoning, secondary-suite and SSMUH rules as applied to a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED in the batch-11 pass — Surrey's figures are not carried into a Mission listing. One suite rule the City does publish: secondary suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes in Mission (see the open-houses file's City source). **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date (10% added July 3, 2026; Community Charter ss. 233–234). **Keep the regimes separate:** the staged 5% after July 2 plus a further 5% after October 31 is the separate rural (Taxation (Rural Area) Act) regime for FVRD electoral areas, never the City's. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **ALR edge:** Mission's rural sub-areas include farm land; on ALR parcels the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Which parcels on this page's feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction (developer sales at Briskham Hill), not resales (Cherry Lane, Luther Place). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase in this band pays in the 1% and 2% tiers only. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — because this band caps at $1.2M, a qualifying first-time buyer is under the full-exemption value ceiling only on a purchase at or under $835,000, not across the whole band. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to a new Briskham Hill purchase near the top of the band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract; presales are never counted as resale band stock in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
