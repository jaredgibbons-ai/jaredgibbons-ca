## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Oakridge VW is the area around West 41st Avenue and Cambie Street in south-central Vancouver, centred on the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre and the Oakridge–41st Avenue Canada Line station; "VW" is the GVR board's Vancouver West suffix. Nearby areas: South Cambie, Langara, Marpole, Riley Park, Shaughnessy, Kerrisdale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/oakridge-vw.md)
- Planning framework: Oakridge is a Municipal Town Centre within the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved May 9, 2011); Phase 3 (approved 2018) extended townhouse and low-rise policy into the neighbourhood streets around the station. Citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- Where condo stock concentrates: at the town centre itself (the Oakridge Park redevelopment at West 41st and Cambie) and along the Cambie corridor north and south of West 41st — the named buildings in Section 2. The side streets are post-war detached bungalows, many traded on land value under the corridor plan. (Research file, Section 5)
- Market figures: **none citable.** The SnapStats GVR September 2026 report provided for this research contains no Vancouver community or sub-area rows; all market figures for Oakridge VW are UNVERIFIED in this file, and sub-area medians are Paragon-only. (Research file, Section 2)
- Transit: Oakridge–41st Avenue Station on the Canada Line (being rebuilt with the Oakridge Park development — new escalators, elevator, weather-protected entrance and direct underground mall access; TransLink expected completion in summer 2026 after temporary 11 p.m. closures) and the R4 41st Ave RapidBus along West 41st Avenue between Joyce–Collingwood Station and UBC. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/bus-projects/rapidbus-projects and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/01/oakridge-41st-avenue-station-to-close-at-11-p-m-starting-jan-26-until-march/ (Research file, Section 4)
- Schools: the VSB lists two elementary schools inside Oakridge — Sir William Osler Elementary (5970 Selkirk Street) and Van Horne Elementary (5855 Ontario Street); locator runs for those two addresses returned Osler / Van Horne respectively, both feeding Eric Hamber Secondary as the regular secondary. Sir Wilfrid Laurier Elementary (7350 Laurel Street) serves the south side. Catchments for other addresses: UNVERIFIED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods (Research file, Section 4)
- Anchors: Queen Elizabeth Park (52 hectares, 125 m above sea level, the city's highest point) is immediately northeast; Langara Golf Course (6706 Alberta Street) and Langara College (West 49th Avenue) sit just south; the BC Children's / BC Women's hospital campus is a short distance north on Oak Street. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/queen-elizabeth-park.aspx and http://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/langara-golf-course.aspx (Research file, Section 4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the corridor's 2017–2021 buildings (West 41 Oakridge, Chelsea), the depreciation report and CRF balance on the Form B are the first-order documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line for a corridor building stating "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" is VOID as a restriction (long-term rentals are allowed; a strata may still restrict short-term rentals). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Oakridge-corridor building named in Section 2 is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** zoning is the City of Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575; the area framework is the Cambie Corridor Plan (2011; Phase 3, 2018) designating Oakridge a Municipal Town Centre, and citywide direction is now the Vancouver Official Development Plan (replaced the Vancouver Plan March 31, 2026). For detached lots outside the corridor designations, the R1-1 district (By-law No. 13817) allows up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units. Check which framework governs the specific lot before pricing land value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%:** homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value; the annual declaration was due February 3, 2026 and payment April 16, 2026. This is a City of Vancouver tax — it applies to every Oakridge condo held vacant, on top of the provincial speculation and vacancy tax where that applies. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file, Section 4)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file, Section 4)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the corridor's completed buildings are resale stock, but Oakridge Park presales are new construction and carry GST. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $1,000,000 (round illustration only — **no Oakridge or Vancouver median is published in SnapStats**, so no market figure is used), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $16,000 (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (relevant to Oakridge Park presales). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** presales exist in this sub-area (Oakridge Park). REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts, with deposits held in trust under s. 18; confirm completion timing, strata formation and remaining developer obligations phase by phase — developer materials describe first residential readiness in early 2027, but phases deliver over several years. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file, Section 6)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which condo buildings can I actually buy in Oakridge today?** The completed, buyable shelf is the corridor shelf — Chelsea by Cressey (4675/4685 Cambie St, 2021), West 41 Oakridge (655/677 W 41st Ave, 2017), The Parker (305 W 41st Ave, 2019) and the Claridge House condo tower (485 W 42nd Ave) — all verified by name in Section 2 and counted in the Cambie condos file, because MLS classifies them under Cambie. Oakridge Park's own towers are not completed stock yet. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Is Chelsea in Oakridge or West Vancouver?** Neither confusion is right: Chelsea by Cressey is on Cambie Street at West 31st Avenue in Vancouver, across from Queen Elizabeth Park, on the Oakridge corridor. The "Chelsea Park" research area is in the District of West Vancouver — a different municipality. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/news/blog/chelsea-79-home-in-a-6-storey-midrise-at-4663-cambie-street-by-cressey
- **Q: When can I move into Oakridge Park?** Developer reporting puts the first residential towers ready in early 2027, with phases delivering over several years; retail's first phase opened in 2026. A purchase today is a presale/assignment question, not a resale — with REDMA's 7-day rescission and GST as new construction. https://burnabyhouse.com/news/oakridge-park-developers-ask-city-ease/
- **Q: Can I rent out an Oakridge condo?** Yes, long-term — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw and by City licensing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo to buy in Oakridge?** No verified one. The 55+ program found zero age-restricted strata in the sub-area; the in-area seniors buildings (Legacy Senior Living; the Louis Brier / Weinberg campus) are rental/care, and Oakridge Park's towers carry no age restriction in any source checked. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/oakridge-vw-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What does the Empty Homes Tax mean for a condo I hold vacant?** A 3% City tax on assessed value if the home is declared or deemed vacant, with an annual declaration required — budget it alongside strata fees and ordinary taxes (advance February 3, main July 3, 2026). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: How many units are in West 41 Oakridge?** UNCONFIRMED — a directory header describes the 655 W 41st address as a 40-unit development, while a current listing's data records 97 units in development for the complex. The strata plan and Form B settle it; do not quote either figure in a listing. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Oakridge address?** For the two addresses actually run in the SD39 locator: 5970 Selkirk Street → Sir William Osler Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary; 5855 Ontario Street → Van Horne Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary. All other addresses: UNVERIFIED — run the exact address. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods (Research file, Section 4)
- **Q: Is the station-to-mall connection finished?** TransLink expected the Oakridge–41st upgrade (escalators, elevator, weather-protected entrance, direct underground mall access) complete in summer 2026 after early-closure work through winter/spring 2026 — confirm the final handover state before selling it as a completed feature. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/01/oakridge-41st-avenue-station-to-close-at-11-p-m-starting-jan-26-until-march/
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $1,000,000 fair market value (round illustration only; no Vancouver median is published), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $18,000. A first-time buyer at $835,000 or less pays no PTT on the first $500,000 under the FTHB program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board files Oakridge's condos under a different sub-area's name.** MLS data for Chelsea and West 41 Oakridge carries Area Minor "Cambie," with Oakridge VW listed only as a nearby neighbourhood — a buyer searching "Oakridge VW condos" on a portal can miss the corridor's entire completed shelf, and an agent quoting an "Oakridge condo count" is usually quoting Cambie data. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/4685-cambie-street/203 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/677-west-41st-avenue/211
- **SnapStats simply does not cover Vancouver.** The GVR September 2026 report's communities are Burnaby, New Westminster, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only — any "Oakridge median" attributed to SnapStats does not exist. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **The town centre's first towers mix strata with social housing from day one.** Developer reporting puts the first five Oakridge Park towers at roughly 700 strata condos plus 180 social housing units, with the civic centre and further social housing completing in the first half of 2027 — the early resale market sits beside a substantial non-market population by design. https://burnabyhouse.com/news/oakridge-park-developers-ask-city-ease/
- **The west side of West 41st has a second master plan running.** The former Oakridge Transit Centre depot (closed 2016) is Grosvenor's Mayfair West — approved in 2020, denser scheme advanced in 2026 — directly beside the Louis Brier / Weinberg seniors campus: years of construction next to the area's care hub, and land-value support for Oak Street sellers at the same time. https://www.grosvenor.com/property/property-america/oakridge (Research file, Section 4)
- **A new university campus opened inside the mall development.** Fairleigh Dickinson University's Vancouver campus officially opened inside Oakridge Park on October 15, 2026 — a rental-demand anchor for corridor condos that no pre-2026 guide mentions. https://www.fdu.edu/vancouver/news/fdu-vancouver-celebrates-successful-grand-opening-of-new-campus-with-alumni-industry-partners-and-government-and-community-leaders/ (Research file, Section 5)
- **The "seniors centre" at Oakridge Park is an amenity, not housing.** The development's own leasing material lists "a senior's centre and a daycare" among its civic components — in a 3,000+ home project where no source identifies a single age-restricted unit.
