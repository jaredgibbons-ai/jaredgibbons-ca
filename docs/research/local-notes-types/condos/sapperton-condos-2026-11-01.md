## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In the Brewery District towers, read the depreciation report and the mixed-use cost sharing early. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. The 1976–1982 Cedar Street buildings are the age band where the report matters most. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a Sapperton building is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925 (2017), under which the Sapperton and Braid station areas are a Frequent Transit Development Area and 97 Braid Street is designated the Sapperton Green Transit-Oriented Mixed-Use Community. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001): houses in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s. 310 — RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq m / 6,000 sq ft, FSR 0.5, with secondary suites and laneway/carriage houses regulated in the same bylaw), and townhouse districts **RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only** — the correction flagged at the top of this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **District energy:** Sapperton Green is planned to connect to the City's Sapperton District Energy System — a City utility cost future owners there should ask about; whether any existing Sapperton strata is connected is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level; the residential slope sits above it — check the exact lot against the City's flood maps. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on balances unpaid September 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Keary, if purchased as a presale/assignment completing as new housing, is the GST-exposed purchase in Section 2. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. This applies to The Keary presale/assignment purchases. Sapperton Green (roughly 4,700 homes proposed at 97 Braid Street under an endorsed master plan concept) is a plan, not a presale — construction timing is UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's 3% Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Sapperton is New Westminster. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The zoning error travels.** The research file's "RT-A and RT-B" townhouse districts do not exist in New Westminster — the bylaw's districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B. Any Sapperton copy repeating RT-A/RT-B is repeating the error; this file corrects it at the top so the correction travels with the file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **One master plan, four residential buildings, two tenures.** The Brewery District's residential towers split evenly: The Columbia (242) and The Sapperton (114) are strata condos, The Nelson and The Westminster (187 homes) are rental — a buyer touring "the Brewery District" is looking at two different products on adjoining lots. https://wesgroup.ca/commercial/office/brewery-district-building-7/
- **The Columbia has two addresses in the record.** The project was announced at 285 Nelson's Court; listing data runs the strata at 258 Nelson's Court. Search both before concluding a unit's history is thin. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/the-columbia-at-brewery-district-285-nelsons-court-new-westminster-242-homes-in-a-26-storey-tower-by-wesgroup-properties
- **Cedar Manor's unit-count conflict dissolves in the City's own table.** The 2009 Non-Market inventory's columns for 312 Hospital Street are 30 single + 20 disabled + 10 family = 60 total; later sources quoting "30" or "60" were reading different columns of the same row. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **The newest elementary school is named for the river.** Skwo:wech Elementary (opened June 6, 2022, $34.98 million, 400+ K–5 students with an attached child care centre) takes its name from the halq'eméylem word for sturgeon, honouring Coast Salish connections to the Fraser River — and it replaced the seismically unsafe Richard McBride Elementary on the same Richmond Street site used for this file's locator run. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
- **Sapperton Park predates the condos by a century.** Sapperton Park at Columbia and Sherbrooke was built in 1907 and named for the Sappers; the neighbourhood name itself comes from the Royal Engineers' 1858 camp. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sapperton.md)
