# Tsawwassen East, Delta — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Building-directory and listing sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen East" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the Boundary Bay side of Tsawwassen, east of 56 Street, taking in Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The east side's landmarks are Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach, 570 Boundary Bay Rd (Metro Vancouver) — Dyke Trail, Savannah and Raptor trails, Cammidge House — and Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave, on the south side. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Beach Grove, the waterfront pocket inside the east side, is bordered by 19th Avenue, 56th Street, 12th Avenue and the banks of Boundary Bay (neighbourhood description, secondary source). https://www.michaellepore.ca/neighbourhood/tsawwassen/page/2/
- Local roads are Boundary Bay Road, 12 Avenue and 56 Street; Highway 17 is reached via 56 Street or 52 Street, then Highway 17A and Highway 99 via the George Massey Tunnel. Route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: on and just off 56 Street at the Beach Grove edge — Royal Oaks at the north end by the town centre, then Heron Cove, Windgrove and Tsawwassen Mews strung along the 56 Street / 16 Avenue corridor. The Boundary Bay waterfront streets themselves are detached and duplex houses, including the RD2 zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- Schools for the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd, from the SD37 School Locator: South Park Elementary (K–7), 735 Gilchrist Dr., and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Shopping and services sit back in the centre: Thrifty Foods on the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street), Tsawwassen Library at 1321A 56 St and South Delta Recreation Centre at 1720 56 St. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/ and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days of request for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, and since 2023 it must also summarize insurance and electrical planning reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no vote to defer it; 30-year cost projections and three CRF cash-flow models. For a 1977 building (Tsawwassen Mews) and a 1992 building (Royal Oaks), this report is the levy forecast. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, all BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaws are invalid — so directory records of "rentals not allowed" (Heron Cove) or "rentals allowed with restrictions" (Royal Oaks, Windgrove) do not state the current legal position for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2); lower age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and the bylaw binds residents, not owners. No Section 2 building here has a verified 55+ bylaw — UNCONFIRMED per building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 — the east side's local overlay:** for the Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, OCP Bylaw No. 8400 provides that no building be located with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, that the minimum land surface elevation around construction be 2.3 metres GSC datum, and that no building be constructed within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a given corridor building lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dike and flood system:** the east side faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Waterfront setback in the zoning bylaw:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, s. 6.2.3, sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting on Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — relevant to any "private dock" claim on the shore, not to the corridor condos. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Delta zoning and plan generally:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, parcel by parcel; the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — no lettered schedules, no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the old plan was Schedule D.1 under Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta, six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **PTT exemptions:** first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial to $860,000, 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence; newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, including a never-occupied unit in a new condo building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on new construction only:** FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026) with the new housing rebate — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; new housing rebate alone 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day statutory rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the frame for any Southlands presale purchase (Section 5). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (CRA-verified in this program's earlier passes); CMHC homeowner premiums 0.60%–4.50% across the published LTV schedule. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are any of these buildings on the water?** No — the verified Beach Grove condo buildings (Royal Oaks, Heron Cove, Windgrove, Tsawwassen Mews) sit on the 56 Street / 16 Avenue corridor; the Boundary Bay waterfront stock is detached and duplex housing, including RD2 lots the bylaw describes as waterfront lots in Beach Grove. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Heron Cove unit if the directory says rentals are not allowed?** Long-term, yes — every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, whatever older records say. Short-term rentals are different: a strata can still ban or limit them by bylaw, so read the current registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What pets can I have?** Directory records: Royal Oaks — pets allowed with restrictions; Heron Cove — pets allowed with restrictions; Windgrove — pets allowed with restrictions in the directory, but a unit listing calls the building pet-friendly with no pet-size restriction. The conflict means the answer for a specific building is UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read; Tsawwassen Mews' pet rule is UNCONFIRMED outright. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/royal-oaks-1100-56-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/heron-cove-1704-56-street and https://vancouvernashdom.com/listings/SearchResults.form/?cat=SOLD&_pg=2
- **Q: Is flood risk priced into these buildings the way it is on the waterfront?** The hazard layer is real but graded: SD3's floor-level (2.9 m GSC datum), land-elevation (2.3 m) and 7.5 m tidal-setback rules are written for the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands, and the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike. Whether a specific corridor lot is inside SD3 is a DeltaMap check — do it lot by lot rather than assuming either way. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: How do I compare strata fees between a 1977 building and a 2005 building?** Off the Form B, not the listing: the certificate states the lot's monthly fees, amounts owing, approved levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the budget and the latest depreciation report attached — and since July 1, 2024 that report must be refreshed every five years for stratas of five or more lots, which is what makes an older building's fee comparable at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Parking and storage — what is standard here?** Royal Oaks listings record underground parking (one stall for the unit read) and the directory records a guest suite and club house; Heron Cove's directory records a bike room and guest suite. Stall and locker allocation for a specific lot is a Form B item — confirm it there. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105321-101n-1100-56-street-delta-british-columbia-v4l2n2-599000/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/heron-cove-1704-56-street
- **Q: What does the strata insurance cover, and what is the deductible?** Since 2023 the Form B must carry a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage; deductible amounts for these specific buildings are UNCONFIRMED in public sources — read them in that summary and check your own unit-owner policy against the strata's water-damage deductible. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Which schools serve a Beach Grove condo?** Address-based: the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd maps to South Park Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12) in the SD37 locator; run the building's exact address before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What bus serves the east side?** Route 601's Boundary Bay trips run via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, through South Delta and Ladner Exchanges to Bridgeport Station — the corridor buildings on 56 Street are on its path to the exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: What is being built at Southlands, and does it compete with these buildings?** Southlands Tsawwassen (Century Group) is recorded by the City at the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390, with the developer's office at 101 – 6388 Market Avenue. Product mix, phasing and completion dates for specific Southlands buildings: UNCONFIRMED in the sources read — treat it as a live development area to the south, not as a dated delivery. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Southlands is the east/south side's long build-out:** the City records the Southlands lands under Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390 — a master-planned development area at Market Avenue that will keep adding newer strata product beside the 1977–2005 corridor stock in Section 2. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- **The 2.9 m number is the one to remember on this shore:** SD3 sets the habitable floor level at 2.9 metres GSC datum with a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — any renovation or rebuild conversation on a foreshore lot starts there. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **A seawall has its own rule here:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, s. 6.2.3, lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback on Boundary Bay lots along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue — and the same passage states the bylaw sets no Beach Grove flood construction level, so the FCL question goes to the permit area and the City, not the zoning text. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The dike trail is the amenity, and it is a working dike:** Boundary Bay Regional Park's Dyke Trail runs on the Boundary Bay dike system the City maintains as flood protection — recreation on top of infrastructure, which is why foreshore work is permitted, not casual. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Health care:** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital; Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Ferry times are published; road times are not:** BC Ferries publishes 1 hour 35 minutes Tsawwassen–Swartz Bay and 2 hours Tsawwassen–Duke Point; road commute times from Beach Grove to Vancouver, Surrey Central or the border are UNVERIFIED — no official source read states them. https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
