## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Presales (REDMA) — central to this file:** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing the purchase agreement and acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement, and deposits are held in trust under s. 18. The disclosure statement — not the listing card, not the presentation centre — governs completion dates, deposits and what is being bought; have a lawyer read it inside the 7-day window. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **The regulator:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page states the developer pays the buyer's agent's fees and that registration order matters for representation — register with your own agent before a first presentation-centre visit. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A presale has no Form B until the strata exists — the REDMA disclosure statement is the presale equivalent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both apply to a presale project once it completes and registers as a strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children, with Reg 7.01 exemptions. None of the three presale projects in Section 2 (Harlowe, Calais, Rockford) is a 55+ project — a South Surrey presale 55+ claim is an error wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — two municipalities, never mixed:** Surrey buildings sit under the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is REPLACED and cited only as predecessor — and Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000. White Rock buildings (Foster Martin) sit under White Rock's OWN bylaws: Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP 2017 No. 2220 — never Surrey's. **Surrey SSMUH / suites (Surrey lots only):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, under the July 8, 2024 changes; a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction (every presale in Section 2), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 date in some secondary sources is superseded). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption most presale buyers in this file will test against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **New-home warranty:** the live page lists a 2-5-10 warranty (2 years materials and labour, 5 years building envelope, 10 years structure) as site copy attributed on the page to BCFSA presales information; the research file carries those terms as site copy only — UNVERIFIED on an official source in that pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
