## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Mission West this is the diligence document for an Archer Green townhouse purchase — there is no apartment building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both rules bind the Archer Green strata as they bind any BC strata of five or more lots. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Mission West may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Mission West complex checked carries a verified 55+ bylaw — Archer Green listings state no age restriction, and the Wilson Street parks' age rules, where they exist at all, are park rules, not SPA bylaws (see the park entries in Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. The Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area (3,440 acres, designated for urban growth) is the plan frame for the Archer Green / Nelson Street ground. A specific parcel's zone and designation: confirm with the City for the PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-west.md)
- **Secondary suites (City frame):** all secondary suites in Mission must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, a new suite goes through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus applicable utility user fees is applied to the property tax notice. The in-house size cap, any owner-occupancy rule and the exact parking count: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-west.md)
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date — the City's property tax page states a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw). **Keep the two regimes explicitly separate:** the staged 5% after July 2 plus a further 5% after October 31 belongs to the rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11) for FVRD electoral areas outside the City — it does NOT apply to any Mission West parcel. Mission taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the other levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **ALR:** any Mission West parcel that sits in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC housing frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; a strata plan deposit is a subdivision — Strata Property Act s. 239; ALR subdivision needs Commission permission — Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — which is why no ALR parcel is condo stock. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/mission-west.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (secondary sources that say March 12 are not followed — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Archer Green is complete and sold out on the directory record, so current purchases there are resales; REDMA is the frame for any future developer sale in the Silverdale growth area (for example, a developer contract at Nelson & Grove). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and https://www.livabl.com/mission-bc/archer-green
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% — the published range is 0.6%–4.5% — plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA), for the Wilson Street park homes excluded in Section 2:** pad-rent increases are capped at 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
