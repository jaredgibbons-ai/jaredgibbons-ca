## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that explains this whole file:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. The Agricultural Land Commission's Information Bulletin 09 (issued October 18, 2022), s. 6, treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision; under the Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation, and the Strata Property Act route (s. 239) does not get around that bar. Practical consequence: farm ground in Ladner Rural produces farm houses and farm dwelling units, never a strata condo building — which is why Section 2 is zero. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Confirm ALR status parcel by parcel:** a specific parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/ladner-rural-guide.md)
- **A1 dwelling rules (the housing this ground actually allows):** farming is a principal use in the A1 zone, and accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. One farm house per lot is permitted, maximum total floor area 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate); one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha — located on the same farm home plate, with ALC approval required for more than one; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/ladner-rural-guide.md)
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/ladner-rural-guide.md)
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024, with NO lettered schedules; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (the A1 zone sheet above is a City zoning document), while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. In Ladner Rural there is no verified strata apartment building for a buyer to request one from; if a float-home interest is presented as strata, the Form B and the strata plan are exactly the documents that would prove it — request them before treating any such claim as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
