## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this band the Form B, not the listing, settles fee, levy and deductible exposure — the live page's strata insurance-deductible ranges were not confirmed on an official source and are not repeated here as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/abbotsford-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Regency Park, College Park (33728 King Road), Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this roster Regency Park and Lakewood Gardens are published as 55+ communities — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit there as generally occupiable. No other Section 2 building is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the **Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 2025)** — the 2016 OCP (Bylaw 2600-2016) is NOT current and must never be cited as current. Zoning is Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2400 (2014). https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/abbotsford-condos-under-800k.md)

- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on September 16, 2026 — directly relevant to closing adjustments on any band purchase completing around those dates. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this file that means developer sales and presale contracts (for example Sage, and any developer-held unit at Montvue, The Mason or Auburn), not the resale buildings. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling, subject to the program's other conditions. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
