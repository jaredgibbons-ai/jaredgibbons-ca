## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Form B applies to the freehold strata townhomes in Section 2 (Higginson Villas, Wealick Place) — it does NOT exist for the Skynest or Yearling leasehold units, where the lease and community rules are the diligence documents instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property — no strata in Sardis South may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the City regulates short-term rental operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. Caution specific to this area: the 2022 voiding is a Strata Property Act rule — how any rental term inside a First Nations leasehold community's own documents is treated is a lease-document question, UNCONFIRMED here, and must not be assumed from the strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-south-guide.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful for a strata; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Sardis South's age labels are NOT strata 55+ bylaws on the sources checked: Higginson Villas is listed as a 55+ townhouse strata (confirm on its Form B), while Skynest's 45+ and Promontory Lake Estates' 45+ positions sit in leasehold/park documents, where a 45+ rule can exist outside the Strata Property Act frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — Sardis is its Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. SSMUH: Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Suites: only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; garden suites are capped at 100 m². Note: City zoning and bylaws do not govern the First Nations lease lands the Skynest and Yearling buildings sit on. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-south-guide.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-south-guide.md)
- **Manufactured home parks (MHPTA):** where pad-rent parks are discussed (the Unsworth Road park stock), the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is ever published in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. GST treatment of a resale leasehold interest at Skynest or The Yearling is a transaction-specific question — UNCONFIRMED here; obtain tax advice on the specific unit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — stated as the standard BC frame for freehold purchases; whether and how PTT applies to a First Nations leasehold interest is transaction-specific and UNCONFIRMED here. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for freehold presales; developer sales on First Nations lease land may sit under different documents, so a Skynest/Yearling developer contract's rescission position is UNCONFIRMED here and must be read in the contract itself. No freehold presale project in Sardis South was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC premium range was stated on the live guide page as read in the research file; any page stating 2.80%–4.00% + 0.20% is WRONG against CMHC's published range. Leasehold financing terms are lender-specific. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Sardis South's "condo market" is a leasehold market.** Both of its modern apartment buildings stand on Tzeachten First Nations land (Skynest at Watson/Vedder, The Yearling at Iron Horse) — the buildings are new (2022–2024), the amenities are real, and the tenure is leasehold in both cases, so every freehold assumption (Form B, depreciation report, PTT/GST treatment, lender terms) has to be re-derived from the lease documents. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The Yearling is marketed two ways at once.** The developer sells its 86 condos as ownership product while a rental platform markets units in the same building to tenants, noting the Residential Tenancy Act does not apply on Tzeachten land — an investor reading only the sales material will miss the tenancy frame entirely. https://www.zumper.com/address/6045-chilliwack-riv-rd-chilliwack-bc-v2r-6l5-can
