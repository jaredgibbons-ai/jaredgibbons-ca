## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest roster stock (Glenkirk Court, 1975; Mission Manor, 1992; Briskham Manor, 1993) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Mission may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Cherry Lane and Stave Lake Landing are void in-entry on that basis. Two roster-specific notes: a current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals," the kind of restriction that CAN still be enforced; and Preston's rentals were secured by a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Briskham Manor and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Mission's Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing and repealing Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added (live guides stating a June 1, 2026 adoption date are wrong). The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations, plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum lot size in City notices — directly relevant to the band's acreage product. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3) — the City states a 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. Mission taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies (City tax rates page) — a real line item on the band's low-lying acreage product. This is the CITY regime only; FVRD electoral-area land (e.g., Hemlock) runs on the rural 5% + 5% regime instead. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Flood:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — flood status, flood construction requirements and the diking levy are parcel-specific checks on any band property in the south end or Hatzic. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **ALR:** Agricultural Land Reserve land sits mainly on the city's rural edges, with status parcel-specific on Agricultural Land Commission mapping. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — an ALR acreage in this band can never be converted to condo stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — note the rebate is fully phased out before this band's $1.8M floor, so no band purchase qualifies. CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists and is likewise far below this band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — at this band's floor and above, the 3% tier is the working rate on most of the price. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — no band purchase qualifies. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — also below this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for developer contracts such as Montgomery's, wherever they sit on price. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Homes at or over $1.5 million are outside default-insurance eligibility in practice, so band purchases are conventional-financing files — stated here only as the premium frame; the eligibility point for a specific purchase is the lender's call. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
