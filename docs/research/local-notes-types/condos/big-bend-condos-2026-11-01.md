## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Big Bend is the City of Burnaby's main agricultural area in south Burnaby, on the Fraser River foreshore flats below the city's steep south slope — primarily an industrial and agricultural district, not a conventional residential neighbourhood. It combines ALR farmland, the Big Bend industrial lands along the river, Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park, and a small rural-residential component (the Riverside area). "Big Bend" is the GVR board sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," and it matches the name the City itself uses for its south Burnaby agricultural area. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The BC strata rules below are province-wide; they are stated here because the task's standard block requires them and because they would govern any strata lot a buyer did find in the district — but with zero verified strata buildings, the rules that actually decide a Big Bend purchase are the ALR and flood rules at the end of this section.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase of a strata lot, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. No Form B exists for the district's farm, industrial and freehold rural-residential stock. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This applies to any strata lot in the district, industrial strata included — it does not change ALR farm-use rules, which are a separate provincial regime. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No age-restricted building is published for this area — there is no verified building to attach one to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning — current bylaw:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (Bylaw No. 4742), which is repealed and should be flagged as such wherever a source still cites it. For Big Bend specifically: A1 is the City's agricultural zoning district (chickens may be kept in the A1 district under Zoning Bylaw rules), and the City is updating zoning regulations for agricultural districts through the 2026 rewrite — parcel-level minimum lot sizes and the current district for a specific farm parcel: UNVERIFIED, confirm in writing with the City and the ALC. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **ALR — the district's defining rule:** ALR status and permitted farm / non-farm uses for a specific property are checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder, and ALR land is governed by the provincial Agricultural Land Commission Act framework. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **Flood — the second defining rule:** the City states the nearly 800 hectares of low-lying land between the south slope and the Fraser River is a floodplain; the Fraser River poses a substantial flood hazard from snowmelt in late spring and early summer and from combined heavy rain and winter storm surge events. The City protects the lowland with river and shoreline dykes, pump stations and flood boxes, maintains a Flood Response Plan, and receives regular updates from the BC River Forecast Centre during flooding events and freshet. Confirm a parcel's flood construction requirements, dyke position and insurance before subjects come off. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Farm-class assessment and tax treatment for a specific Big Bend parcel is a BC Assessment / tax-notice check per property: UNVERIFIED here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Farm purchases can carry different assessment and tax treatment per parcel — UNVERIFIED at district level. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No market residential presale inside Big Bend was verified in the research file or this pass, so this frame currently has nothing to attach to here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Farm and industrial financing does not follow the residential insured-mortgage frame — treat any residential premium quote for a Big Bend farm or industrial parcel as UNCONFIRMED until a lender confirms the product. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **The 2050 OCP points this district away from housing, not toward it.** The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend — the opposite direction from the town-centre densification the same plan drives elsewhere in Burnaby. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **The flood protection is a system, not a single dyke.** The City protects the lowland with river and shoreline dykes, pump stations and flood boxes, maintains a Flood Response Plan, and takes regular BC River Forecast Centre updates during freshet — and its dyke-upgrade program runs along the Fraser River foreshore reaches within and beside Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park. Which reach a parcel sits behind is a per-parcel check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
- **The agricultural zoning was mid-rewrite when the new bylaw landed.** The Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) replaced the 1965 bylaw city-wide, and the City is updating zoning regulations for agricultural districts through that rewrite — a Big Bend zoning letter written off the old bylaw can already be stale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)

Copy claiming in-area amenities for a Big Bend property should be treated as unverified until a source states it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/big-bend.md)
