## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For either Whitby Estates townhome pocket, the Form B and the filed bylaws are where rental, pet and age lines marked UNCONFIRMED in Section 2 get settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — long-term rentals are allowed; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Framed honestly: this age-bylaw block is general BC law and attaches to nothing in this pocket today — there is no 55+ strata here; it governs any BC strata the buyer considers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** the District of West Vancouver's pair is Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. NEVER present White Rock's Zoning 2506 / OCP 2220 as West Vancouver's — that confusion has been caught elsewhere in this program and is not repeated here. The OCP directs growth to the town and village centres (Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild, Horseshoe Bay), not into the Properties. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/whitby-estates.md)
- **Building scheme:** British Properties subdivisions carry restrictive covenants registered at the Land Title Office — an owner must file an application for approval of plans with British Pacific Properties Limited (Suite 1001, 100 Park Royal, West Vancouver) before building a new home, extending or altering an existing building, or erecting a fence or any structure; covenants differ by subdivision, bind every subsequent owner, and District bylaws apply in addition. Whether the Whitby Estates subdivision's specific covenant set matches the general pattern is a per-property title check; whether British Pacific Properties was the original developer of the Whitby Estates subdivision specifically is UNCONFIRMED (confirmed for the British Properties generally). https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **Development permits:** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit applies to work within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface, and an Environmental Development Permit applies within 15 m of the top of a watercourse bank — both live checks on this slope. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **District taxes and utilities:** District of West Vancouver property taxes were due Thursday July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half the bill is collected for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes
- **Additional school tax:** 0.2% on the residential portion assessed between $3M and $4M and 0.4% on the portion above $4M for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — a routine line at this pocket's price points. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: $3,500,000 fair market value (illustration only — no sub-area median is published), fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 portion above $3M) = **$93,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' and Newly Built Home exemptions are stated for completeness — their thresholds sit far below this pocket's price points. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** this is the District of West Vancouver, not the City of Vancouver — the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/whitby-estates.md)
