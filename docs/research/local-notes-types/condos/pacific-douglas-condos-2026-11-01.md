## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It applies to this area's townhome/rancher stratas exactly as it does to apartment stratas. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in Section 2 would be VOID. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — the frame for The Greens at Douglas's published 55+ status, to be confirmed on its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only. Zoning is Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, parcel by parcel. The Douglas NCP (1999) designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, so a listing quoting a 1999 NCP designation is quoting history. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/pacific-douglas.md)
- **Surrey suites, coach houses and short-term rentals (non-ALR ground):** one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable area, not within a coach house or garden suite; coach houses and garden suites 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR. Short-term rentals: under 90 consecutive days per patron, only the owner may host from their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/pacific-douglas.md)
- **ALR frame for the north edge:** the plan area is bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve to the north. On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and subdivision — including depositing a strata plan — requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions. Which specific parcels are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/pacific-douglas.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $700,000 (the S Surrey White Rock community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026 — a COMMUNITY figure used as an arithmetic illustration only; it is NOT a Pacific Douglas figure, and no sub-area median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** Douglas Green Living is a developer-contract purchase while it remains a presale — REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Pad rents (MHPTA) — wherever a park is discussed for this ground:** manufactured-home pad rent increases are capped at 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. No park in-area is named in Section 2; the cap is stated so a park unit surfaced under this label is not mistaken for strata stock. (Standing program rule.)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
