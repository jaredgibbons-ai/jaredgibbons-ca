# West Chilliwack — Condos: Local Notes (City of Chilliwack)

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named building itself.

Geography note: the City of Chilliwack publishes no official boundary for the Paragon name "West Chilliwack." The research file treats it as the western rural and agricultural part of the city, planned through the Agricultural Area Plan (a schedule of the OCP), with the Evans Road corridor as its named spine. The one verified condo building below sits on Evans Road — and current MLS area labels for that same address read "Sardis West Vedder," which shows how loosely the "West Chilliwack" label sits on this ground. Follow the address, not the label. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/west-chilliwack-guide.md, §§1, 4–5)

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The City publishes no street-by-street boundary for "West Chilliwack": UNVERIFIED as a City boundary. The ground is the western rural/agricultural part of Chilliwack, where the Agricultural Area Plan states the farm sector is dominated by dairy and poultry operations, nurseries and greenhouses. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- Evans Road is the main local road through the area. BC Transit route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and Downtown Chilliwack, with timing points at Cottonwood Mall, Evans Rd at Yale Rd, Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd and Downtown Chilliwack. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
- Highway 1 serves the north side of Chilliwack; interchange detail specific to this sub-area is UNVERIFIED. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- The one school physically in the area is Evans Elementary (Kindergarten to Grade 5), 7600 Evans Road. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- The Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #104 – 7955 Evans Road, and Chilliwack General Hospital is at 45600 Menholm Road. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
- Where condo stock actually concentrates: it does not concentrate — there is exactly one verified apartment-condo building in the sub-area, on Evans Road (Section 2). Everything else verified here is freehold farm and rural land under the AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone. Chilliwack's main condo corridors (downtown, Sardis, Garrison) are east of this sub-area and are not counted here.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B (Strata Property Act, s.59).** For Creekside Estates, the Form B is where the monthly fee, any approved levy (ask specifically about roof/deck/window work — see Section 2's listing-stated update history), the CRF balance, parking/storage and the insurance summary (required on the Form B since April 1, 2023) are stated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report, renewed at least every five years; pre-July 1, 2024 stratas in the Fraser Valley Regional District with none had to obtain one by July 1, 2026. **CRF:** at least 10% of the operating fund must be contributed at each AGM budget since November 1, 2023. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental bylaws void** — long-term rental-restriction bylaws have had no effect since November 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws remain possible. **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2)** are the only age bylaws permitted, with the May 1, 2023 expanded exemptions for spouses/partners, caregivers and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Chilliwack zoning.** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs; the AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone that frames most of this sub-area sets a minimum lot area of 7.5 ha for land excluded/exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance, with a 25 m minimum lot width — which is why apartment development is not the form of this area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Chilliwack OCP.** The current city-wide plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500; the Agricultural Area Plan is adopted as a schedule of the OCP. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024)** brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into Chilliwack's zoning. Note the local wrinkle: the Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not West Chilliwack, but it shows Chilliwack's SSMUH rollout is not uniform city-wide. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Floodplain rules that matter even for a condo buyer here:** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal, and the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder systems; Chilliwack also levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **GST on new construction.** 5% GST on new homes only (Creekside Estates resales are not GST-taxable). FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html Older GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out (task brief figure).
- **PTT.** General 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus 2% on residential value above $3M; first-time buyer exemption on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. Note: Chilliwack is CADREB territory, not FVREB — the SnapStats FVR report has no Chilliwack section, so no local median is quoted in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales / REDMA.** Disclosure statement plus 7-day rescission (later of signing or receiving disclosure); deposits held in trust; assignment only as the contract allows. No presale project in this sub-area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP $60,000 per person / FHSA $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime.** https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** of the mortgage amount; required under 20% down on homes $1.5M or less. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Chilliwack tax dates:** property taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applied to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condo buildings are there in West Chilliwack?** One verified: Creekside Estates, 7694 Evans Road (strata plan LMS1320, built 1993). The large complex next door at 7610 Evans Road is a manufactured-home park, not condos. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110352-118-7694-evans-roadsardis-west-vedder-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r3w3-356000/brochure
- **Q: What do strata fees at Creekside Estates cover?** No public source publishes the building's budget: UNCONFIRMED. The answer for a specific unit is in the Form B and the strata's current budget — ask for both, plus the depreciation report, before subject removal.
- **Q: Has the building had big capital work?** A 2026 listing for a unit in the building states updates to decks and windows and a new roof scheduled for April with the levy paid (listing-stated, for that unit's sale). Treat that as a prompt to verify the full project history and what remains in the depreciation report, not as a building guarantee. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3101932-7694-evans-road-316-chilliwack-bc-v2r-3w3/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit at Creekside Estates?** Long-term: yes in law — rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be banned by bylaw; check the filed bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** No public source states this building's pet bylaw: UNCONFIRMED. Pet rules survive the 2022 changes, so the filed bylaws in the Form B package decide it.
- **Q: Parking and storage?** Listings for units in the building record carport parking and in-suite laundry; the specific stall (and any locker) assigned to a unit is stated on the Form B — confirm it there rather than from a listing. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3101932-316-7694-evans-roadsardis-west-vedder-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r3w3-329900/brochure
- **Q: What insurance deductible could I be assessed?** The strata's insurance summary must be part of the Form B since April 1, 2023 — the water-damage deductible shown there is the figure to insure against with a unit-owner policy. The building's actual deductible is not published: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Q: Which schools serve 7694 Evans Road?** SD33 catchments are address-based. The locator result in the research for the sample address 7600 Evans Road (Evans Elementary's own address, next door) was Evans Elementary → Vedder Middle → Sardis Secondary, with the locator note that Vedder Middle students attend Sardis Secondary. Run 7694 Evans Road itself in the SD33 locator before relying on it. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Is a unit here in a floodplain?** The area is protected by the City's Fraser/Vedder diking network and taxed with drainage and dyking levies, and the floodplain bylaw's river setbacks (60 m Fraser / 75 m Vedder / 30 m Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal) frame the area — but a building-level flood finding for LMS1320 is not published: check the City's floodplain mapping for the address. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Q: Will more condos be built in West Chilliwack?** No project in this sub-area was confirmed on a developer's site or a City page, and the AL zoning minimums (7.5 ha) plus ALR status work against apartment form here. Any claim of a coming West Chilliwack condo project is UNCONFIRMED until a City application or developer page names it.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board boundary is a trap in this sub-area.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report — the stats source for the Abbotsford files — contains no Chilliwack section at all, because Chilliwack is a different board (CADREB). Anyone quoting a "West Chilliwack median" from that report is quoting nothing. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/west-chilliwack-guide.md, §2)
- **MLS labels the Creekside Estates address as "Sardis West Vedder,"** while the Paragon research label for the same ground is "West Chilliwack" — search both names or you will miss the building entirely. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110352-118-7694-evans-roadsardis-west-vedder-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r3w3-356000/brochure
- **The Evans Road corridor got a health anchor:** the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre at #104 – 7955 Evans Road is co-located with the Chilliwack and Fraser Health Rural Primary Care Centre, on the same road as the sub-area's only condo building and its elementary school. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/Chilliwack-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Chilliwack's new OCP is recent:** the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500, is the current city-wide plan, with the Agricultural Area Plan carried as a schedule — the west side's farm-first planning is current policy, not a leftover. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **SSMUH arrived unevenly in Chilliwack:** Bylaw 5395 (June 18, 2024) implemented it, but the Province's September 16, 2024 extension bulletin gives part of the Proper neighbourhood and Hazel Park until December 31, 2030 to comply — check the specific zone rather than assuming a city-wide rule date. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Drainage and dyking are a separate line on the tax bill** in Chilliwack, levied on top of municipal rates — budget for the full notice, not just the municipal rate, whether the purchase is the condo or a farm parcel. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Provincial dates that changed this building's rule book:** rental bylaws void November 24, 2022; Form B insurance summary April 1, 2023; 10% CRF minimum November 1, 2023; FVRD depreciation-report catch-up deadline July 1, 2026; FTHB GST rebate in law March 13, 2026 (Bill C-4) for new homes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
