## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase (in Citadel, that means a townhouse strata purchase) the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Citadel's townhouse complexes date from 1988–1999, so the depreciation report is a first-order document here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element of Citadel Pointe's directory line is VOID as a restriction (rentals are allowed). Note that older MLS sheets for Citadel complexes still print rental-restriction fields; those bylaws no longer have effect. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Citadel complex is verified to have a 55+ bylaw — a seller marketing a Citadel townhome as "55+" would need the actual bylaw, not a site-influence tag. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw 2008, No. 3630 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 2013, No. 3838, which describes Riverwood and Citadel Heights as established residential neighbourhoods on the north side and supports an appropriate neighbourhood commercial development in the Citadel area. Citadel's detached homes sit under RS1, RS2, RS3 and RS4 (minimum lot widths of at least 15 m, 12 m, 30 m and 9.5 m respectively); townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 cover the attached stock (a unit checked at The Uplands is zoned RTH2). The City permits 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity, and permits secondary suites only in the RS-1 to RS-4 zones with a building permit. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/citadel-pq.md; 55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/citadel-pq-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026. The City's 2026 increase was 1.95% for the average home, and the City states it has the lowest average property taxes and utility fees in Metro Vancouver; utility bills are flat annual water and sewer fees, billed separately and due March 31, 2026. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/citadel-pq.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Citadel's stock is resale stock, so GST does not apply to a Citadel townhouse purchase. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $660,000 (the Port Coquitlam community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Citadel median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,200 (2% × $460,000) = **$11,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (not applicable to Citadel's resale stock). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no Citadel presale project was verified on a developer site or a City development page in this program, so no REDMA frame is engaged here; for completeness, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period on developer contracts with deposits held in trust under s. 18, wherever a presale does exist. Status for Citadel: no presale verified. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/citadel-pq.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

The MLS sheet checked for Citadel Pointe lists pet bylaws only, with no age restriction — and under BC law the only valid strata age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84964/FullPublic.pdf?i=320027865 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Citadel townhome?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022, whatever an older MLS sheet or directory line still prints (including Citadel Pointe's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" directory element). A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Citadel's townhome complexes?** At Citadel Pointe, the MLS sheet checked lists cats OK, dogs OK, with a two-pet number limit and restrictions, and the directory records pets allowed with restrictions. Other complexes: check the specific strata's bylaws on the Form B — pet rules are building-by-building. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84964/FullPublic.pdf?i=320027865
- **Q: How did Citadel sell in September 2026?** Detached homes posted a 45% sales ratio (20 listed, 9 sold) — the strongest detached pace of any Port Coquitlam sub-area that month — while condos & townhomes sat at 4% (23 listed, 1 sold). Strategy should differ sharply by home type. SnapStats GVR September 2026 report. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/citadel-pq.md)

Do not assume flood-free because it is a heights neighbourhood. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/media-centre/port-coquitlam-reminds-residents-prepare-possible-flooding (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/citadel-pq.md)
