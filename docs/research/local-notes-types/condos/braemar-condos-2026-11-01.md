## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Braemar against the District's centre condos (Lynn Valley, Lions Gate Village, Edgemont) will meet them there; inside Braemar itself there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for a District building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Braemar sits under the District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and the local North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan, Bylaw 6750; zoning is District Zoning Bylaw 3210 — Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 minimum lot at 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth and standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. Braemar is in the District, not the City of North Vancouver — City bylaws do not apply here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at North Shore prices. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Braemar is confirmed, so this is relevant only to District-centre presales a buyer compares. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos for sale in Braemar itself?** No verified strata apartment condos exist in Braemar on any source checked — the area is detached houses on R1/R2 lots, and the research file records named condo buildings and townhome complexes in Braemar as none confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real District condo stock?** In the District's centres: Lynn Valley Town Centre (for example Parkside at Lynn's two 12-storey concrete buildings at 2325 / 2375 Emery Court and Taluswood at 2651 Library Lane), Lions Gate Village (Woodcroft Estates on Fullerton Avenue; Park West at 1632 / 1675 Lions Gate Lane) and Edgemont Village (Crescentview at 3121 Connaught Crescent; Edgemont Villa at 3151 Connaught Crescent) — all verified in the North Vancouver model file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/north-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Is Braemar in the City or the District of North Vancouver?** The District. That decides the rulebook: District OCP Bylaw 7900, the North Lonsdale–Delbrook OCP Bylaw 6750 and District Zoning Bylaw 3210 apply — not the City's bylaws, even though Lonsdale Avenue addresses nearby sit in the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Braemar?** No. Since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, but they are accessory units on one lot's title — there is no separate strata lot to buy unless a future stratification occurs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: What is the building going up at the Delbrook Lands — can I buy there?** No. At 600 West Queens Road, south-west of Braemar, the District's project is a neighbourhood park plus a four-storey building of low-to-moderate-income rental housing and a seniors' care facility (Bylaws 8397, 8398 and 8399, 2019) — rental and care, not strata. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: If I buy a condo in a District centre instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which school is the Braemar draw, and does a Braemar address guarantee it?** École Braemar Elementary, 3600 Mahon Avenue, inside the area, offers English, Early French Immersion and Late French Immersion. The locator result verified in the research file is for the school's own address (3600 Mahon Avenue → École Braemar Elementary and Carson Graham Secondary); catchments are address-based, so confirm any other address with SD44. SD44 has no middle tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: How is transit from Braemar without a condo node?** Route 249 runs on Delbrook Avenue and West Queens Road to Lonsdale Quay and the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront Station), and route 241 runs via Lonsdale Avenue over the Lions Gate Bridge directly downtown. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks matter on Braemar's west-side lots?** The Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines west of the area are protected through the North Lonsdale–Delbrook plan's development permit areas for the natural environment, and District OCP Schedule B adds streamside, wildfire, creek and slope hazard areas — run the District GeoWeb property report before pricing a renovation. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: How much density can a Braemar lot actually take under SSMUH?** The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — a lot-specific check, not a street-wide promise. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Braemar's plan is shared, not standalone.** Braemar has no plan of its own: it is one of five neighbourhoods inside the North Lonsdale–Delbrook planning area under the North Lonsdale–Delbrook OCP, Bylaw 6750, beneath District OCP Bylaw 7900 — density decisions for its streets are made in that shared plan, which directs growth to the District's town and village centres instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **The nearest new attached building is rental and care, by bylaw.** The Delbrook Lands approvals (OCP Amendment Bylaw 8397, Rezoning Bylaw 8398, DCC Waiver Bylaw 8399, all 2019) fixed the 600 West Queens Road south portion as low-to-moderate-income rental housing plus a seniors' care facility — a buyer waiting for strata product on that site is waiting on the wrong tenure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Three units per house lot is already the rule.** Since June 2024 every single-family lot within the Urban Containment Boundary can hold a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house, and December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys — the "condo alternative" in Braemar is a suite inside a house purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **The school is the search anchor.** The research file notes "École Braemar" is itself a common search anchor for the area — buyers find this neighbourhood by a French Immersion elementary school (3600 Mahon Avenue), not by any building name, because there is no building stock to search by. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Princess Park is the east-side trailhead.** Princess Park, at the 3500-block of Princess Avenue, offers tennis courts, playgrounds, a picnic shelter and forested walking trails that form part of the Lynn Valley Link — the amenity that substitutes for a condo building's gym and grounds on these detached streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Utility bills are a District line item, not a strata line.** With no strata fees in the area, the carrying-cost comparison runs through District bills: 2026 single-family utilities total $2,571.20, including water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
