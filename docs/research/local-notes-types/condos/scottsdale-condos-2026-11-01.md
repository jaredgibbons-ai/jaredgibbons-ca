# Scottsdale — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scottsdale is the south-east corner of North Delta in the City of Delta, along Scott Road (120 Street) at the Surrey border; the City does not publish a street-by-street written boundary, and the working grid used in the research is 72 Avenue south, 84 Avenue north, 112 Street west, Scott Road east — a working grid, not an official boundary. Note: the Scottsdale Exchange itself sits on the Surrey side of Scott Road, per the City. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) the 80 Avenue / Scott Road tower node — Delta Rise (11967 80 Avenue) and the two Chancellor Place towers (11910 and 11920 80 Avenue); (2) the Scott Road low-rise strip south of there — Atria (7511 120 Street) and Trend (7445 120 Street). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Two buildings the research file lists under Scottsdale are NOT counted in this file, because MLS listings classify them elsewhere: Delta Cedars (8635 120 Street) is classified Annieville, and Scott & Nicholson (6950/6960 Nicholson Road) is classified Sunshine Hills Woods. Both are documented in those areas' files; counting them here as well would double-count North Delta's stock. Sources: Section 2 notes below.
- Market context: Scottsdale's SnapStats sub-area row for Condos & Townhomes in September 2026 was Inventory 24, Solds 2, Sales Ratio 8% — a combined condo-and-townhome figure, not condos alone (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/scottsdale.md).
- Transit is the area's defining feature: Scottsdale Exchange is the bus hub, with routes including the 310, 312, 316, 319, 322, 329, 340, 364 and 391, and the R6 Scott Road RapidBus between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/r6

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most at Chancellor Place (1989/1990 concrete towers), where the mandatory-cycle report prices elevators, parkade membranes and building envelope work. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "No Rentals" line in the older Chancellor Place listing is exactly the kind of text this change voided. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A "19+" restriction, as stated in the older Chancellor Place listing, is not a form of age bylaw the Act permits — confirm on the Form B what, if anything, is registered. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning, TOD and plans:** zoning is parcel-by-parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, under the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (no lettered area-plan schedules; the North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950). The 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area, and the City states Bill 47 requires minimum densities within a Transit Oriented Development Area extending 400 metres from Scottsdale Exchange — the policy frame behind the 80 Avenue towers. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites with reduced parking requirements — relevant to Scottsdale's house lots, not its towers. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all four Scottsdale complexes are resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12 — CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Federal savings vehicles and mortgage insurance:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. CMHC published premiums run 0.60% to 4.00%, and 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: A listing says Chancellor Place is "No Rentals, No Pets, 19+." Can all three still apply?** No. The rental ban has been void since November 24, 2022, and 19+ is not a permitted age threshold — only a 55+ bylaw is allowed under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2. A pet bylaw can still be enforceable. All three lines come from one older listing; the registered bylaws on the Form B are the only reliable statement. https://www.richmondcondoshomes.com/solds/listing.r2215734-1405-11910-80-avenue-delta-v4c-8e3.71280028/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How many units are in Delta Rise?** UNCONFIRMED from citable sources — listings confirm a 38-storey concrete tower built in 2017 with over 20,000 sq ft of amenities, but no unit count was published in a source this file can cite. The strata plan, available through the purchase document package, states the lot count. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117707-3401-11967-80-avenue-delta-british-columbia-v4c0e2-579000/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Delta Rise?** A current listing records pets as "Yes" for a unit at 11967 80 Avenue — that is listing data, and pet bylaws can carry size/number limits, so confirm the registered bylaw wording before buying with a large dog. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/11967-80-avenue/1509
- **Q: What is the levy risk in the 1989/1990 Chancellor Place towers versus the 2017 Delta Rise?** Age drives it: the depreciation report is mandatory every five years for both, but a 35-plus-year-old concrete tower's report prices elevator, parkade and envelope cycles that a 2017 tower has not reached. Compare the CRF balance and the report's funding models, not the headline strata fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is Atria really a condo building if it has shops/offices at grade?** Yes — it is a mixed-use strata (EPS930): a current commercial listing offers a ground-floor retail/office strata unit in the same 2012-built building as the residences above. Mixed-use stratas can have separate sections and cost-sharing formulas — check how costs split between the residential and commercial sections on the Form B. https://www.williamwright.ca/properties/102-7511-120-street/
- **Q: Which schools serve an 80 Avenue tower address?** Catchments are address-based: the research file's locator run for a Scottsdale sample address (7031 120 Street) returned Heath Elementary (K–7), 11364 72 Avenue, and Seaquam Secondary (8–12), 11584 Lyon Road. Run the tower's exact address — do not assume the sample result carries across Scott Road. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: How good is transit, honestly?** Scottsdale Exchange is the area's hub with ten routes named in the research file plus the R6 RapidBus to Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange — but the exchange itself sits on the Surrey side of Scott Road, so "steps to the exchange" claims should be measured from the building door. https://www.translink.ca/r6 and http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
- **Q: Will the TOD rules change my building or my fees?** The Bill 47 minimum densities apply within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange per the City — that affects redevelopment expectations for surrounding lots, not an existing strata's bylaws or fees. Two similar lots can sit under different density expectations depending on which side of the 400-metre line they fall. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is a Scottsdale duplex a strata purchase?** Not necessarily — a duplex can be strata or non-strata in BC; in a strata the owners own their lots and together own the common property, while a non-strata duplex has no strata corporation. Confirm the title form for the specific property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **North Delta's entire tower stock stands within about two blocks:** Delta Rise (2017) and both Chancellor Place towers (1989/1990) are all on 80 Avenue at Scott Road — outside that node, Scottsdale's condo stock is four-storey wood-frame (Atria, 2012; Trend, 2010). (Sources: Section 2 entries above.)
- **The "Scottsdale" mall and exchange are not quite in Scottsdale:** the City states Scottsdale Exchange sits on the Surrey side of Scott Road, and the Village at Sunshine Hills presale material likewise places Scottsdale Centre mall across 70 Avenue from that project, on the Scott Road corridor — cross-border geography is normal at this corner and affects exact walk-time claims. http://www.delta.ca/node/1838 and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/
- **Chancellor Place's two towers carry two postal codes and two build years in listings:** 11910 is V4C 8E3 and 11920 is V4C 8E8 in listing data, with 1989 and 1990 both appearing for the complex — a small thing that breaks automated searches if only one address/year is used. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3010989-11910-80-avenue-705-delta-bc-v4c-8e3/brochure and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2952847-11920-80-avenue-601-delta-bc-v4c-8e8/brochure
- **Scottsdale had North Delta's strongest detached signal in September 2026 while its attached market was quiet:** the sub-area's detached sales ratio was 19% (the strongest of the four North Delta sub-areas) against 8% for Condos & Townhomes the same month (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/scottsdale.md).
- **Delta Rise's amenity claim is unusually large for Delta:** listings put its amenity space at over 20,000 sq ft (gym, garden, recreation room, playground) — when comparing its strata fee to Trend's or Atria's, part of the fee is buying that amenity plant, not just insurance and maintenance. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3083630-506-11967-80-avenue-delta-british-columbia-v4c0e2-599500/brochure
