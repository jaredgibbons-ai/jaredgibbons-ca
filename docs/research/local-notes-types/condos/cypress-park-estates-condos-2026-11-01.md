# Cypress Park Estates — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

It is also a different place from Cypress Provincial Park and from Cypress Mountain Resort, which sit up the same road (Cypress Bowl Road) and are not residential stock. This file uses the MLS name because that is how the one condo building here is classified, and states the District names alongside it. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For a brand-new building like Hawksley, also establish whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale — the diligence route is different (disclosure statement vs Form B). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. A new strata's first depreciation-report cycle runs from its registration — confirm where Hawksley sits in that cycle on the Form B / disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No Hawksley source checked publishes a rental restriction — if developer-era or directory text for the building surfaces a "rentals not allowed" line, it is void for long-term rentals. Note this does not apply to the area's future purpose-built rental buildings, which are rental by tenure, not by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Hawksley source checked publishes an age restriction — UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning (West Vancouver — its own bylaws):** the area sits under the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. (The Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair sometimes seen in Fraser Valley files belongs to White Rock only — never West Vancouver.) Zoning here uses RS2, RS3 and RS5 single-family zones, CD3 for Rodgers Creek (density set by lot-size band, bylaw Section 601–603 area), and the CV (Cypress Village) Zone created by Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024 for Cypress Village. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **Wildfire and watercourse permits — the defining checks on this slope:** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and the District's Community Wildfire Resiliency Plan describes the Cypress Park area as consistently sloped terrain. An Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank — Cypress Creek and Rodgers Creek both run through the area — and steep-terrain subdivisions can trigger a difficult-terrain permit. These apply to strata alterations and landscaping at Hawksley and Chippendale Mews, not just to house lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — directly relevant at Hawksley (built 2026), where developer-held units are new-home sales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant only to developer sales, not resales. At this area's price points the additional school tax can also apply (next bullet). https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Additional school tax:** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — a live line item for the larger Hawksley units and the surrounding houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Hawksley or at a future Rodgers Creek / Cypress Village project such as Treehouse. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in the exemplar files.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the published bands (the stated range is 0.6%–4.5%), plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)

District sources use Cypress Park, Rodgers Creek and the Uplands instead, and Cypress Village / Cypress West are the OCP's future neighbourhoods. It is not Cypress Mountain or the provincial park. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; check the filed bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Three Cypress names share one road.** The residential pocket, Cypress Provincial Park and Cypress Mountain Resort are different places; a listing that trades on ski-hill proximity is describing a drive up Cypress Bowl Road, not a location inside the resort. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **Rodgers Creek just roughly doubled on paper.** A District-approved modification of the Rodgers Creek rezoning for Areas 5 and 6 raised the approved homes from 493 across 16 buildings to 974 across 17 buildings, lifted maximum heights from 12 storeys to 14 and up to 16 storeys on some sites, and added two 12-storey buildings with up to 280 purpose-built secured market rental homes combined, with a $7.14 million voluntary community amenity contribution from British Pacific Properties. Hawksley's buyers are buying into the first phase of a much larger build-out. https://www.mattgul.com/blog/47874/west-vancouver-approves-taller-towers-with-new-rentals-in-the-british-properties
- **Cypress Village is approved and its first works are permitted.** The Area Development Plan for Cypress Village and Eagleridge was approved by Council with Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024 creating the Cypress Village (CV) Zone; Development Permit 24-115 to British Pacific Properties Limited covers initial works (commenced January 2025), and the Eagleridge lands transferred to the District (262 acres) were dedicated as park in April 2025. The village's planned shopping, school and community centre are future amenities — do not price them as present. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md) and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/cypress-village-area
- **The school locator literally has no answer for the sample address.** 3900 Cypress Bowl Road falls outside the SD45 locator's listed ranges for Cypress Bowl Road (Even 3850–3850, Odd 3755–3757) — a listing that names a catchment school for an Upper Lands condo is stating something the district's own tool does not confirm. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **The area's "primary school" is three grades, at the bottom of the hill.** Cypress Park Primary is a K–3 IB PYP school at 4355 Marine Drive whose students have the opportunity to continue the IB programme at West Bay Elementary — it is not a full K–7 neighbourhood elementary for the Uplands, whatever a listing implies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **The new building's own numbers do not agree with each other yet.** Hawksley appears as 57 condos + 7 townhomes in its presale source and as a 72-unit development in other project and listing sources, at two different Uplands Way addresses — early-days documentation for a 2026 building is exactly why the strata plan and disclosure/Form B, not marketing pages, govern a purchase here. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/hawksley-british-properties-west-vancouver-presale-executive-condos/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3102313-701-3963-uplands-way-west-vancouver-bc-v7s-0b6
