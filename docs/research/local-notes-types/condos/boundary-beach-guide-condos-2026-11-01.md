## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a resale Southlands flat or a Monterra unit this is the central diligence document; for a Granary unit still on a developer contract, first establish whether the sale is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; a strata established on or after July 1, 2024 and before July 1, 2027 must obtain its first report within two years of its first AGM — the timeline that applies to a brand-new Southlands strata. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals (the same rule voids the directory "rentals not allowed" lines recorded elsewhere in Tsawwassen for Heron Cove, in the Beach Grove file). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Monterra's rancher format draws downsizer interest, but no source read states a 55+ bylaw for Monterra or for either Southlands project — age restriction UNCONFIRMED per complex until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — NO lettered schedules; there is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 in the current OCP (that was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950), and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" — the designation frame for Southlands' working-farm lands beside its housing pockets. Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/boundary-beach-guide.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017", while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. Zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps in either case. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/boundary-beach-guide.md)
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands)** — named for this exact ground in OCP Bylaw No. 8400, Appendix B: no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Boundary Beach parcel sits inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/boundary-beach-guide.md)
- **Dike and SSMUH:** the Boundary Bay shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. Delta's SSMUH zoning allows up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/boundary-beach-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (a developer sale at The Granary or another Southlands phase), not resales (Monterra, District Flats resales). First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption to check on a never-occupied Southlands developer sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for a Granary purchase while the project is on developer contracts (its presale record states completion TBA). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-granary-at-southlands-tsawwassen-by-century-group-presale-delta-condos-and-townhomes/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/boundary-beach-guide.md)
