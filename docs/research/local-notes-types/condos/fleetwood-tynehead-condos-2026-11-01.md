## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Fleetwood Gardens the Form B is where the 55+ bylaw and the pet limit are confirmed; at Manarola it settles the restrictions no public source publishes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Fleetwood Tynehead may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Fleetwood Gardens restriction line in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Fleetwood Gardens is published as a 55+ building in its listing record — a lawful form, but the registered bylaw on the Form B governs who may actually reside in a unit there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The Fleetwood Plan (Stage 1 endorsed March 7, 2022, updated Stage 1 endorsed October 16, 2023, with further Transit Oriented Areas alignment changes and Council consideration of the updated plan in 2026) is the local growth frame for this ground — a lot's future designation here is a moving target until the updated plan is considered. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/fleetwood-tynehead.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots below maximum FAR). No retroactive legalization of an unpermitted suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/fleetwood-tynehead.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Section 2 apartment buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — no active presale building was verified in this sub-area in this pass; the frame is stated because Fleetwood Plan growth makes presale marketing here likely. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two stations, one sub-area — and neither exists yet.** Fleetwood Tynehead is the rare Surrey sub-area with two named stations on the funded extension (160 Street and 166 Street on Fraser Highway) — but the Fleetwood Plan itself is mid-update, with Council consideration of the updated plan in 2026, so both the transit map and the density map for this ground are plans in motion, not settled facts. https://surreylangleyskytrain.gov.bc.ca/stations/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/fleetwood-land-use-plans/fleetwood-town-centre-plan
- **The attached market here is two different markets.** Town-centre-side low-rise apartments (Fleetwood Gardens 2005, Manarola 2014–2015) versus Tynehead-side townhouse communities of up to 302 homes (Canopy) — a single "Fleetwood attached" comparable set mixes forms, ages and buyer pools that do not compete with each other. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **This sub-area's attached pace beat most of Surrey in September 2026.** Its 11% attached sales ratio was third-highest of Surrey's 15 sub-areas on 136 listings and 15 sales — a genuinely active attached month, unlike the detached-led quiet in several neighbouring sub-areas. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/fleetwood-tynehead.md)
- **A 55+ building hides inside a family-market sub-area.** Fleetwood Gardens' 55+ bylaw makes its buyer pool, financing conversations and resale comparables different from every other named complex here — and its own listing record still carries a rental-restriction line that has been void law since 2022. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/311-16068-83-ave-1729077
- **Zone names in older Fleetwood material are stale.** Surrey's July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF/RF-SS zones to R3, RF-12/RF-12C/RF-13 to R4 and RF-SD to R6 — feed cards here quote R3, which is a current name; material quoting the old names is pre-2024. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/fleetwood-tynehead.md)
- **The east end is a different planning world.** The plan area runs from Green Timbers Urban Forest toward the ALR, and Tynehead-side acreages carry Anniedale-Tynehead plan and drainage context that standard Fleetwood lots do not — confirm ALR status and plan area for any east-end listing before valuing it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/fleetwood-tynehead.md)
