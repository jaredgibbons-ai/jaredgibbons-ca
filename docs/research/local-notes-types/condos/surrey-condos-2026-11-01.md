## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2 wherever a home-file directory line says rentals are restricted or not allowed (Semiahmoo Estates, St. Andrews at Northview, Fleetwood Gardens, Newton Court). Short-term rentals can still be restricted: in Surrey, only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host one, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required first, under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and the Business License Bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Live 55+ positions in this assembly include Fleetwood Gardens, Chelsea Gardens and Suncliff Place — confirm each on the Form B. The queue complexes' 55+ positions are NOT roster positions — see Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current; 18020 is the replaced predecessor only. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Read a Surrey parcel in layers: current OCP designation first (the Surrey 2050 update rescinded East Newton North and superseded East Newton South, the East Newton Business Park and West Cloverdale designations), then the GLUP/NCP/LAP, then the current zoning — the plan tile on the live page is not proof a designation is still operative. One tile verified at City-source level in the research pass: Darts Hill NCP, approved May 10, 2021, its designations incorporated into the OCP. South Westminster's tile is behind the City's March 10, 2025 update position (Corporate Report R048). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md)
- **Secondary suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. The often-quoted "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is never stated as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; Surrey adopted its SSMUH zoning change on July 8, 2024 (residential zones R1 to R7 replaced RF, RH and RQ; RF-12/RF-12C became R4; RF-SD became R6). The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md) (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% + 2% structure, not a separate rate — no such row was spotted on the live plans page as read.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Amson Bloc and any developer sale at the newer buildings in Section 2 (Viva, King + Crescent, Quinn, Galilea, Gabriel). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC premium range was stated on the live plans page as read; any page stating 2.80%–4.00% + 0.20% is WRONG against CMHC's published range. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
