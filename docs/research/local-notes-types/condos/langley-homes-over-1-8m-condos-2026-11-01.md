## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. In Willoughby's 2016–2025 stock this lands as a first-cycle question; in Langley City's 1988–1990 low-rises it is the levy story. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the "rentals not allowed" directory lines for both Parkway buildings in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this file's roster, all three Langley City apartment buildings and Huntsfield Green are published as 55+ communities — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit there as generally occupiable. No Willoughby building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Plans and zoning — two municipalities, kept separate:** Township of Langley property is governed by Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; its SSMUH amendments are Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): 3 units for lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units for lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, not larger than 4,050 m²), with no 6-unit transit tier currently applying in the Township. Most acreage parcels in this band exceed the 4,050 m² eligibility ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them. Township secondary suites: a suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. Langley City property is governed separately by Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300; under it, a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. A Langley City condo is not under Township rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **ALR frame for the band's acreage stock:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it. Whether any specific parcel in this feed is in the ALR is parcel-specific on ALC mapping: UNVERIFIED for any specific listing in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Property taxes:** no single Langley tax due date is stated in this file, because the community spans two municipalities with separate tax notices: UNVERIFIED in this file for either municipality. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example Matteo while it sells as a presale), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. At this band's price level (over $1.8M), the Bill C-4 rebate has fully phased out and the New Housing Rebate is long past its phase-out — neither reduces GST on a band purchase. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. **Band interaction, stated plainly: an over-$1.8M purchase is above every first-time-buyer and newly-built relief ceiling** — budget PTT in the 1% / 2% tiers, the 3% tier on any portion above $2M, and the further 2% on any residential portion above $3M. Worked example with stated inputs only: on a $2,000,000 purchase price, PTT = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = $38,000, before any exemption — an arithmetic illustration of the published tiers, not a price claim for any property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Matteo and any developer-contract unit in Willoughby's newest projects. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
