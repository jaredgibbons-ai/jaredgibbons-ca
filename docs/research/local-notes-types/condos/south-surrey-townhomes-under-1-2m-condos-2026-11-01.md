## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The live page states the same one-week frame. Strata paperwork is where a South Surrey townhome is actually priced: the Form B, the bylaws and rules, recent minutes, the budget and the depreciation report are the documents that matter. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. For townhouse complexes the report's roof, envelope, road and garage cycles are the levy story — and the complex's age split matters: Grandview Heights carries the newest complexes in this band and King George Corridor / Pacific Douglas the older ones (site copy), so an older complex's depreciation report and levy history matter more than its list price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Flagged in-entry in Section 2: the "no rentals" line in older Elgin Pointe material is VOID for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children, with Reg 7.01 exemptions. In this roster, Bishop's Green is described as a 55+ community in a current listing — a description, not a bylaw; confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit there as generally occupiable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — two municipalities, never mixed:** Surrey buildings sit under the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is REPLACED and cited only as predecessor — and Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000. White Rock buildings sit under White Rock's OWN bylaws: Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP 2017 No. 2220 — never Surrey's. The band crosses the municipal boundary, so check which municipality the unit is in before applying any rule. **Surrey SSMUH / suites (Surrey lots only):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, under the July 8, 2024 changes; a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit (the "90 m²" figure is NOT current). Surrey STRs are permitted, owner-only, at $358/yr under Surrey's rules — Surrey ground only. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this topic that means any remaining developer townhome sales in the newest complexes, not the resale roster. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (the March 12 date in some secondary sources is superseded). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase anywhere in this band meets only the first two tiers on price. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so the lower part of this band can sit inside the full-exemption range on price alone, and the part above $860,000 cannot. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a qualifying newly built townhome under $1,100,000 is inside that threshold on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — no presale complex is counted in Section 2's roster; a not-yet-built townhome project marketed under this ceiling (Harlowe's townhome component, documented in this batch's presales file) is a developer contract under REDMA, not band resale stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
