## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It applies to this area's townhome/rancher stratas exactly as it does to apartment stratas — and it is the document that settles The Greens at Douglas queue questions (bylaw, unit count, strata status). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; Surrey-wide, short-term rentals are permitted only where an owner hosts from their principal residence and holds a City business licence at $358 per year (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1). No Pacific Douglas complex in Section 2 carries a verified published rental line, so no in-entry void flag arises beyond this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/pacific-douglas-guide.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The Greens at Douglas's 55+ status is a program/queue status carried with its flag in Section 2 — UNCONFIRMED as a registered bylaw until the Form B and strata records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey plan and zoning:** the in-force check for any Douglas lot is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan designation — Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026); Bylaw No. 18020 is the predecessor only, never current — plus the lot's zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, NOT the 1999 Douglas NCP designations that listing text still quotes: the City states those designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update. A cluster of feed listings at 148–192 172 Street quotes the superseded NCP "Commercial/Residential" designation and an OCP "Urban" designation — listing copy only; the current designation for those parcels is UNVERIFIED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/pacific-douglas-guide.md)
- **Suites and coach houses (Surrey-wide):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, its habitable floor area at most 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Bylaw 12000 — the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule; a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m², rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. A feed card's "licenced revenue suite" wording is a claim to verify against City records, not a fact. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/pacific-douglas-guide.md)
- **ALR:** the plan area is bordered by the Agricultural Land Reserve to the north; any parcel in the ALR follows the ALC frame — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/pacific-douglas-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Douglas Green Living and Harlowe, as presales, are new construction; the Section 2 complexes are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** Douglas Green Living and Harlowe are the named presale projects touching this ground. REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **MHPTA note:** where a manufactured-home park on pad rent arises, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act limits pad-rent increases to 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027 — no pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file, and no park is confirmed in-area here. (Standing program rule.)
