## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Bromley Manor is a 1990/1991 wood-frame building, so its report is the document that prices envelope, window and parkade work. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "Rental: Not allowed" line on the old 455 Bromley Street sheet in Section 2 reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. The long-standing plan was the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001; the City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Bylaw 5511, 2026 and Bylaw 5512, 2026), with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. Small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² (R-1/R-3) and 465 m² (R-2), and the City's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Watercourse protection:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam (50 metres in Northeast Coquitlam), and Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s.523 covers riparian areas — relevant to the ravine-edge townhome complexes on the slopes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside this sub-area was verified in this pass — the rule is stated because buyers cross-shop Burke Mountain presales from here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many apartment condos are there in Coquitlam East?** One named building was verified in this pass: Bromley Manor at 450 Bromley Street, plus a second apartment address at 455 Bromley Street whose building name no source states. The sub-area's attached market is mostly townhomes and is thin — 9 condos & townhomes listings in total in September 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Q: Is the Pinetree Way tower cluster part of Coquitlam East?** No. Oasis, MThree and the Grand Central buildings sit at the City Centre edge and are covered in the Central Coquitlam file; listing feeds label them "North Coquitlam." Do not import their prices or strata profiles into a Coquitlam East search. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/central-coquitlam.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Bromley Manor unit long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, and the "Not allowed" rental line on the old 455 Bromley sheet is void. Short-term rentals are separate: a bylaw can still restrict them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Bromley Manor?** A current listing describes pets as welcome at 450 Bromley Street. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern for the specific unit. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2999199-450-bromley-street-406-coquitlam-bc-v3k-6s5/brochure
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** At sub-area level, 9 condos & townhomes listings and 1 sold (11% sales ratio) — a sample so small that one sale swings it. At the Coquitlam community level, the condos & townhomes median was $625,000 with 23 days on market. SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve the Lansdowne slope?** School District 43 catchments are address-based, in a three-tier system. The verified locator result in the research file is for 1195 Lansdowne Drive (Gleneagle Secondary): Eagle Ridge Elementary, Scott Creek Middle and Gleneagle Secondary. Run the exact address — other east-side addresses feed different schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Q: Is the "Eagle Ridge" project on Barnet Highway a condo building?** No — IntraUrban Eagle Ridge at 2660 Barnet Highway is strata industrial space (light-industrial and showroom units, IL-1 zoning, built 2025). It cannot be lived in and is not part of the residential condo stock. https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/2660-Barnet-Hwy-Coquitlam-BC/28767988/
- **Q: How old is the strata stock here, and what does that mean for levies?** The verified stock is 1980s–1991 wood-frame (Eagle Ridge Court 1981, Eagleridge Villas recorded 1983, Bromley Manor 1991). Every strata with five or more lots must now hold a depreciation report renewed every five years — read it for envelope, roof and parkade items before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What is the tax deadline risk in Coquitlam?** Taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants — a closing adjustment item on any mid-year purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026.** The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Bylaws 5511 and 5512, 2026), superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001, with a fuller update anticipated to begin in 2027. Listing copy and older planning documents citing Citywide OCP designations for east-side lots are already a plan out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **Bromley Manor's build year is not settled in public sources.** Most current listings say 1991; one says 1990. This file uses 1991 and flags the conflict — the strata plan registration record settles it definitively. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3098980-450-bromley-street-310-coquitlam-bc-v3k-6s5/brochure
- **The sub-area's name collision is industrial, not residential.** The only new "Eagle Ridge" strata product on this side of the city is an industrial park on Barnet Highway — a buyer searching "Eagle Ridge strata" will surface warehouse units alongside the 1980s villas on Eagleridge Drive. https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/2660-Barnet-Hwy-Coquitlam-BC/28767988/
- **Two Lansdowne complexes that look alike file under different sub-areas.** Eagle Ridge Court (1170 Lansdowne Drive) sits on the lower slope in this sub-area's frame, while Greyhawke Estates (1495 Lansdowne Drive) is placed by the building directory in Westwood Plateau — same street, different board geography, different price conversation. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/greyhawke-estates-1495-lansdowne-drive
- **One strata sale a month is the whole attached story.** With 9 attached listings and 1 sold in September 2026, there is no meaningful sub-area condo price trend to quote — any "Coquitlam East condo average" a portal generates rests on a handful of Bromley Street and townhome sales.
