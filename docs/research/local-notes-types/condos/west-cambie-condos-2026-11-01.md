## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Cambie is an official City of Richmond planning area in the north-central part of the city, abutting the east side of the City Centre, and includes the Alexandra neighbourhood. The Alexandra neighbourhood is bounded by Cambie Road, Garden City Road, Alderbridge Way and No. 4 Road; the DND lands are bounded by No. 4 Road, Alderbridge Way, Shell Road and Westminster Highway. The Area Plan also names two established residential neighbourhoods, The Oaks and Odlinwood. Nearby: Richmond City Centre to the west, East Cambie to the east, Bridgeport to the north, and the Garden City Lands beside the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: one pocket — the Alexandra / Alexandra Gardens cluster along Alexandra Road, Odlin Road, Tomicki Avenue and McKim Way, where Polygon and Concord Pacific built a run of low-rise strata buildings between 2011 and 2019 (Mayfair Place, Alexandra Court, Omega, Cambridge Park, Trafalgar Square). The research file records strata apartments and townhomes concentrated along Alexandra Road and Odlin Road in the Alexandra pocket, with freehold detached houses in The Oaks, Odlinwood and the older Alexandra lots. Outside that pocket, West Cambie is house stock. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below; (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In West Cambie, also pull the district-energy utility charges from the strata documents (below) — they are part of carrying costs and do not appear in the strata fee alone. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. All five Alexandra-pocket buildings exceed five lots on any count published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" for an Alexandra-pocket building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. Separate mechanism, not affected by the 2022 change: Omega's LEMR units are restricted by a housing covenant, not a strata bylaw — the covenant still binds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No West Cambie building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District energy — the Alexandra pocket's extra utility:** apartment buildings documented by the City in the Alexandra District Energy Utility list include buildings on Alexandra Road, Odlin Road and Tomicki Avenue — the Section 2 buildings sit on those streets, and their listings describe geothermal heating/cooling. Strata buyers in the Alexandra pocket should expect a district-energy utility connection to form part of carrying costs, charged separately from the strata fee. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** the West Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11A to the Official Community Plan (adopted July 24, 2006), under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000 (Schedule 1), with area plans held in Schedule 2 under Bylaws 7100 and 9000; the City began a further OCP update process in fall 2024 toward a 2050 horizon. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500; the research file records zones in the area including Single Detached (RS1/F) in the older Alexandra pattern, townhouse site-specific zones (ZT67, ZT71) on Alexandra Road, and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM) zoning on a Cambie Road site. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Aircraft noise and the Alexandra lot pattern:** the Area Plan states the area is affected by aircraft noise from Vancouver International Airport, that no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy, and that an Environmentally Sensitive Area sits in the south-east portion of Alexandra. The older Alexandra pattern also remained on septic sewage disposal — a servicing check for older lots, not for the strata buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes designed to handle a 1:500 flooding event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. Annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every building in Section 2 is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. The one development item in the research file is a rezoning application (RZ 23-015980) at 9720 Cambie Road for an apartment on an RSM/XL-zoned site — an application, not an approved project or a presale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md) and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What extra cost comes with the Alexandra buildings' geothermal heating?** The Alexandra District Energy Utility. The City's utility building list covers the Alexandra Road / Odlin Road / Tomicki Avenue buildings, and listings for Alexandra Court and Omega describe geothermal heating/cooling — the utility charge is separate from the strata fee, so pull it from the strata documents and price it into carrying costs rather than assuming standard utilities. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Q: Is an Omega unit a normal market condo?** Not necessarily. Omega (9333 Tomicki Avenue / 9388 Odlin Road, Concord Pacific, built 2014) contains Low End Market Rental units held under a housing covenant — a current listing offers five LEMR units together as affordable-housing product. A market unit and an LEMR unit in the same building are different legal products; confirm which one is being sold, from the title and covenant, before pricing. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3011284-9388-tomicki-ave-odlin-road-richmond-bc-v6x-0l5/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Alexandra pocket buildings?** Published positions: Mayfair Place — pets allowed with restrictions (building directory, for 9388 McKim Way). Alexandra Court — listings state pets allowed, one stating 2 pets with no size restriction; a verified bylaw summary is UNCONFIRMED. Cambridge Park, Trafalgar Square and Omega market units: UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws on the Form B govern. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mayfair-place-9388-mckim-way
- **Q: Can I rent out a West Cambie condo long-term?** For a market strata lot, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. The exception that matters here is not a bylaw at all: an Omega LEMR unit's rental is governed by its housing covenant (eligible tenants and capped rents), which the 2022 change does not void. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

All are low-rise; the directory records wood-frame construction for the Alexandra Court phases. Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a five-year cycle for all of them. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Mayfair Place's build year?** Current listings record 2011 and 2012 for Odlin Road units while the building directory records 2013 — the complex is multi-address (9399 Odlin Road, 9388 McKim Way, with listings also at 9288 and 9500 Odlin Road) and phased, so the year is address- and phase-specific. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Five "different" buildings are really one development pocket.** Mayfair Place, Alexandra Court, Omega, Cambridge Park and Trafalgar Square all sit within a few blocks on Alexandra Road, Odlin Road, Tomicki Avenue and McKim Way in the Alexandra Gardens pocket — and four of the five are Polygon buildings sharing the same clubhouse template (outdoor pool, guest suite, fitness space) at different scales (Mayfair Club 8,000+ sq. ft., Alexandra Court 12,000 sq. ft., Trafalgar Club 5,000 sq. ft. per listings). Compare them as phases of one idea, not as five unrelated buildings. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The heating system is a municipal utility, not a strata amenity.** The Alexandra buildings' geothermal heating/cooling runs through the City-related Alexandra District Energy Utility (delivered by the Lulu Island Energy Company, per the City council document the research file cites) — an ongoing utility relationship with its own charges that survives resale and never appears in a listing's strata-fee line. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Omega hides a rental-housing program inside a market building.** Its Low End Market Rental units are covenant-restricted affordable rentals sitting in the same strata as market condos — they surface in investor listings as a five-unit package, and a buyer who assumes every Omega unit is a market unit can price the wrong legal product. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3011284-9388-tomicki-ave-odlin-road-richmond-bc-v6x-0l5/brochure
- **The older Alexandra lots next door run on different rules entirely.** The Area Plan records the older Alexandra pattern as 0.4 ha single-family lots remaining on septic disposal, with no new single-family development permitted under the aircraft-noise policy — the condo pocket and the acreage pocket beside it price on completely different logic, and listings that borrow comparables across that line mislead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **Richmond has no SnapStats coverage in this program.** There is no community median to quote for West Cambie — any Richmond median in marketing copy comes from a source outside this file's verification, and this file substitutes none. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
- **A Cambie Road apartment application is in the system, not approved.** RZ 23-015980 at 9720 Cambie Road proposes an apartment on a site zoned Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM/XL) in the Alexandra neighbourhood — documented as a rezoning application only. It is not a presale, and no completion or pricing fact about it is verified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-cambie.md)
