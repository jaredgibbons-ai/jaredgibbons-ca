## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Catchments are the district's lines, not the market's (this topic's defining rule):** SD36 catchments are set by street address through the School Locator and boundary map; they can change on enrolment review; French Immersion has its own registration, separate from the catchment school; and SnapStats publishes NO school-catchment segment — there is no catchment-based count, share or median for any school, and any "in-catchment median" claim is UNVERIFIED by construction. Existence and assignment are different claims: the BC Ministry K-12 file is the existence authority (name + street address, 2025/2026); a district URL slug is not evidence of a school or a catchment. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A Form B confirms a unit's strata facts; it does not confirm a school catchment — that is the locator's job, per address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in South Surrey or White Rock may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. A tenant household still takes the address's catchment as the locator returns it — tenancy does not change the line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws — the catchment connection:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions (May 1, 2023 expansion) for a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents, children under 19 in the specified resident's care, and adult children the specified resident cared for before they turned 19. It is those child exemptions that make a 55+ home's catchment a live question for some households: an exempt child under 19 living in the home attends the catchment school for that address — which still has to be run per address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **One district, two cities, two bylaw books:** White Rock lots follow White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024 (SSMUH caps: 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots over 280 m², 6 units within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop) and OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220; Surrey lots follow Surrey's bylaws — the current OCP is **Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only, Zoning By-law No. 12000, and Surrey's SSMUH bands (3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on larger lots, up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop). The school district is the same SD36 on both sides; the building rules are not. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md)
- **Property taxes:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. White Rock addresses follow the City of White Rock's own tax calendar — UNVERIFIED in this pass; do not carry Surrey's dates across the municipal line. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. **Worked example with its inputs stated** — inputs: new home price $900,000, eligible first-time buyer, agreement signed after March 20, 2025: GST = 5% × $900,000 = $45,000; FTHB rebate = 100% of GST at this price = **$45,000 back** (under the $50,000 maximum). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $700,000 (the S Surrey White Rock combined attached community median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only, not a catchment figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A presale contract does not fix a catchment either — run the completed building's civic address in the locator. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles and CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
