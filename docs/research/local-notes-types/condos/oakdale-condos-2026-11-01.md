## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules govern any strata purchase an Oakdale buyer makes at Lougheed Town Centre or in Burquitlam, and any future stratified multiplex unit inside this sub-area.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption is the live question for any small stratified multiplex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and is void. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — current bylaw:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is repealed — any source still citing Bylaw 4742 for Burnaby is superseded. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/oakdale.md)
- **Burnaby 2050 OCP and the Lougheed town centre:** Lougheed is one of Burnaby's town centres, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — the Lougheed town-centre edge is where plan expectations change fastest, so review the 2050 land use map before assuming density beyond the R1 district. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/oakdale.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. A Coquitlam purchase across North Road carries Coquitlam's tax calendar instead — a different city, a different bill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/oakdale.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — directly relevant to the presale towers at Lougheed and in Burquitlam that Oakdale buyers cross-shop. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Lougheed Town Centre or Burquitlam. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year.
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
