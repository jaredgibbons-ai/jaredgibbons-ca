## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)
- **Representation at an open house:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)
- **How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication, and any identification or sign-in requirement for visitors:** UNVERIFIED — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either. A host may still ask for sign-in as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document step after the open house — the open house shows the unit, the Form B shows the building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Mission may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Whether any building on a given weekend's feed is age-restricted is a Form B question for that listing, not a feed fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans, zoning and taxes:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025; zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date (10% added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026; Community Charter ss. 233–234) — for the sellers behind these open houses, that is the City of Mission regime only. **Keep the regimes separate:** the staged 5% after July 2 plus a further 5% after October 31 is the separate rural (Taxation (Rural Area) Act) regime for FVRD electoral areas, never the City's. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)
- **Suites in homes on this feed:** all secondary suites in Mission must be registered with the City; a secondary suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes, and adding one goes through the building permit process; an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation and a possible decommission permit. One first-page card describes a suite "registered w. City" in its listing text — listing text only, not independently verified. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame if a developer unit, rather than a resale, is what an open house is showing. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **ALR, where a rural open-house property is farm land:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and the ALC frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence within the ALC size limits — a rural open house is a house on land, not a condo opportunity. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
