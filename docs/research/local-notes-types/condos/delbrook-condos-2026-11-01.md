# Delbrook — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Delbrook is a confirmed District of North Vancouver neighbourhood name: one of the five distinct residential neighbourhoods (Delbrook, Norwood/Queens, Upper Delbrook, Carisbrooke and Braemar) inside the District's North Lonsdale–Delbrook planning area, around Delbrook Avenue and West Queens Road. A current District boundary map for the whole neighbourhood is UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are the District's Delbrook Lands — on the west side of Stanley Avenue between West Windsor Road and West Queens Road, with Mission Creek to the west — and the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road. District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900. Note the split buyers meet in practice: the board-style name "Delbrook" also covers what District planning calls Upper Delbrook further north — this file covers the Delbrook neighbourhood around Delbrook Avenue / West Queens Road, not Upper Delbrook. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: two small pockets only. Queensbrook sits on West Queens Road at the area's south-west edge near Westview, and Delbrook Plaza sits on Delbrook Avenue itself at the Evergreen Place end. The rest of the neighbourhood is detached houses on R1 and R2 lots along Delbrook Avenue and its side streets — the research file describes the strata share as limited to the apartment stock near West Queens Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- Nearby areas: Edgemont Village (west, across Mosquito Creek), Upper Delbrook and Braemar (north and east), Upper Lonsdale / Queensdale (east, along Lonsdale Avenue — Queensdale, on Lonsdale Avenue at Queens Road, is one of the District OCP's six Village Centres), and Norgate / Pemberton Heights (south, below West Queens Road toward Marine Drive). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- Main streets and transit: West Queens Road is an east–west major arterial, and Delbrook Avenue is a two-lane minor arterial that transitions into Westview Drive south of West Queens Road and connects the surrounding residential areas to the Trans-Canada Highway (Highway 1). TransLink route 249 (Delbrook / Lonsdale Quay) runs via Westview Drive, Delbrook Avenue and Montroyal Boulevard to Lonsdale Quay, continuing as a 246 downtown trip; route 246 runs on Delbrook Avenue and route 232 runs on West Queens Road — direct buses toward downtown Vancouver, the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront Station) and Phibbs Exchange respectively. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- Anchors inside and beside the area: the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road (main pool, leisure pool and indoor racquet facilities), Delbrook Park on West Queens Road, and Westview Shopping Centre south of West Queens Road; the elementary schools beside the area are École Braemar Elementary, 3600 Mahon Avenue, and Carisbrooke Elementary, 510 Carisbrooke Road East, with Carson Graham Secondary, 2145 Jones Avenue, the secondary school south of the neighbourhood in the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- Market numbers: the District of North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. With only two small buildings in the area (22 units at Queensbrook; Delbrook Plaza's count UNCONFIRMED), each owner's share of any levy is correspondingly concentrated — the Form B math matters more, not less, in a small strata. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Queensbrook's 22 lots put it inside this requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Neither counted building in Section 2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line — Queensbrook's directory position is rentals allowed — so there is no void line to flag in this file; the rule is stated because buyers cross-shopping District buildings will meet such lines elsewhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Queensbrook is marketed as a 55+ community and its directory entry records age restrictions — that is consistent with the only valid form, but the filed bylaw's exact wording (including how the exemptions are handled) is read on the Form B, not from the marketing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and Zoning Bylaw 3210. The former North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan (Bylaw 6750, adopted 1995), which named Delbrook as one of its five neighbourhoods, is repealed — Bylaw 7900 now covers the area. Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 minimum lot at 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth) and the standard R2 minimum at 660 m² (18 m width, 34 m depth), with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 lot may hold one principal dwelling, one secondary suite and one coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. Delbrook Plaza's site carries site-specific CD38 zoning per the District council record. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md) and https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/550907.pdf
- **Hazard permits:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Mission Creek borders the Delbrook Lands on the west and Mosquito Creek lies farther west, so creek-side Delbrook lots are exactly the lots these areas were written for. The District's landslide hazard work rests on its Landslide Risk Assessment, commissioned after the January 19, 2005 Berkley landslide in the District's east end; slope lots on Delbrook's upper streets should be checked against the District's hazard reporting rather than assumed clear. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Delbrook is confirmed, so this applies only to presales a buyer compares elsewhere in the District. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C4.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 total $2,571.20, including water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (strata utility billing follows the building's own arrangements — confirm on the Form B). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condos are there in Delbrook, really?** Two verified buildings: Queensbrook (678 West Queens Road, 22 units, built 1997, 55+) and the residential component of Delbrook Plaza (3711/3721 Delbrook Avenue, built 1988). Everything else in the neighbourhood is detached houses on R1/R2 lots, plus the coming non-market rental and seniors-care building at 600 West Queens Road, which is not purchasable. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Q: Is Queensbrook really 55+ — and what does that mean for a younger spouse?** It is marketed as a 55+ community and the directory records age restrictions, pets allowed and rentals allowed. A 55-or-older bylaw is the only age bylaw valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, and the statutory exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children — so a younger spouse is not automatically barred, but the filed bylaw wording governs and must be read on the Form B before writing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/queensbrook-678-west-queens-road and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I have a pet at either building?** Queensbrook: the directory states pets allowed, and a current listing states "Cats OK" while the building description calls it pet friendly — confirm species/size wording on the Form B. Delbrook Plaza: a current listing describes the home as pet-friendly, and its published rental and age restrictions are UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws still bind, unlike rental bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120766-309-3721-delbrook-avenue-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7n3z4-755000/brochure
- **Q: Is Delbrook Plaza a shopping centre or a condo building?** Both — that is the point of the District's own description: "a mixed-use commercial/residential development, zoned CD38." The ground-floor commercial (café, medical, professional offices) serves the neighbourhood, and the residential units above/beside it (listed from 3711 and 3721 Delbrook Avenue, built 1988, Apartment/Condo sub-type) are the strata stock counted in this file. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/550907.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy into the new building going up at 600 West Queens Road?** No. The Delbrook Lands project is a District-led four-storey building with low-to-moderate-income rental housing and a seniors' care facility (reported as 86 affordable homes run by the non-profit Hollyburn Community Housing Society), plus a new neighbourhood park on the northern portion of the site. It changes the streetscape on Stanley Avenue and West Windsor Road, but there is no strata lot to purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md) and https://www.canadianrealestatemagazine.ca/news/north-vancouver-construction-of-affordable-homes/
- **Q: What should I check in Queensbrook's strata documents?** The Form B package: the filed age-bylaw wording, the latest depreciation report (a 22-lot strata is inside the every-five-years, no-deferral-vote requirement), financials, the CRF balance against the 10%-of-operating-fund minimum contribution, and planned work. In a 22-unit building each owner carries roughly 1/22 of any special levy. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Delbrook condo address?** School District 44 catchments are address-based and the locator result for these addresses is UNVERIFIED — run the exact address with SD44. The elementary schools beside the area are École Braemar Elementary (3600 Mahon Avenue) and Carisbrooke Elementary (510 Carisbrooke Road East), with Carson Graham Secondary (2145 Jones Avenue) south of the neighbourhood; do not name a catchment from the nearest school. SD44 has elementary (K–7) and secondary (8–12) only, no middle tier. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Q: Can a Delbrook house lot hold more than one home, if I skip the condos?** Yes in form, not in title: an R2 lot may hold a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary — but they are one lot's ownership, not separate strata lots. The District's SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on non-exempt lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks apply to a Delbrook purchase?** District OCP Schedule B creek, slope and wildfire permit areas, checked parcel by parcel on the District's GeoWeb: Mission Creek borders the Delbrook Lands to the west and Mosquito Creek lies farther west, and the District's landslide work (Landslide Risk Assessment, commissioned after the January 19, 2005 Berkley landslide) is the reference for upper-slope lots — relevant to renovation and rebuild budgets, not just to houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Q: Is Delbrook in the City or the District — and does it matter for a condo?** The District: District OCP Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210 apply, and District tax timing (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; total residential rate 3.28195 per $1,000) is the timing that matters. Carson Graham Secondary sits in the City, south of the neighbourhood, but the municipal line does not change the school district — both are SD44. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The neighbourhood's recreation centre moved, and its old site is the development story.** The current Delbrook Community Recreation Centre is at 851 West Queens Road; the former centre site at 600 West Queens Road (the Delbrook Lands, about 17,600 m²) is being redeveloped under Bylaws 8397–8399 into a neighbourhood park (northern portion) plus the four-storey rental and seniors-care building (southern portion) — a District-built project on District land, directed by Council on July 22, 2019. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Delbrook's "plaza" condos sit on a former service-station planning file.** The District's 2006 development-permit record for 3707 Delbrook Avenue describes the site as previously occupied by an Esso service station, with Delbrook Plaza (CD38) to its west and north and Evergreen Place to its south — the mixed-use node on Delbrook Avenue grew out of a gas-station redevelopment file, which is why the avenue has a commercial node at all. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/550907.pdf
- **The old Delbrook plan is repealed — do not rely on it.** The former North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan (Bylaw 6750, adopted 1995) named Delbrook as one of five neighbourhoods, but that plan is repealed and District OCP Bylaw 7900 now covers the area; a seller or agent quoting the 1995 plan's designations is quoting a dead bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Queensbrook's park is its front yard.** Listings describe the 22-unit building as backing onto Belhouse Park and its tennis courts, a short walk to the recreation centre and the Mosquito Creek Trail — the amenity package for the area's only 55+ building is the park-and-trail network, not in-building facilities beyond its club house, workshop, exercise centre and courtyard. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206
- **A detached lot here can already hold three homes' worth of occupants.** Since June 2024 a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on non-exempt lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. That infill potential, not new strata product, is Delbrook's density story — the only new multi-unit building in the area is non-market rental. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for this market.** The District of North Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the reports used for this program, so any "Delbrook median" quoted from those reports does not exist; sub-area inventory, solds and medians are UNVERIFIED and no substitute source is used. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
