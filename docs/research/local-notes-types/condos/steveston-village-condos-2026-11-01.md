## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. It applies to the mixed-use and townhouse strata in the core (including the 12040 2nd Avenue building) even though this sub-area has no apartment condos. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a village building is VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted, a live point in a waterfront visitor village. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No village-core building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000) guides the village. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: houses on village side streets in RS1/RS2 Single Detached zones (Section 8.1) with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²); village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3), Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village, and Steveston Office Commercial (ZC49) – Chatham Street (Steveston). https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-village.md)
- **Heritage — the defining rule of this sub-area:** Steveston Village is designated as a heritage conservation area in the Steveston Area Plan; protected heritage buildings and other resource types such as docks and landscape features are identified in the Official Community Plan, and properties inside the conservation area are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements. A buyer of a village house or mixed-use strata unit is buying into alteration controls other Richmond neighbourhoods do not carry. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Richmond property taxes:** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in Steveston Village was confirmed on a developer source in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Steveston Village is Richmond. (Parent file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/steveston-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos to buy in Steveston Village?** No verified apartment-condo building exists in the village core in any source checked. The buyable strata in the core is the townhouse-form building at 12040 2nd Avenue and the low-rise mixed-use strata over Moncton/Chatham retail; the nearest verified apartment condos (The Village, Imperial Landing) are counted in the Steveston South condos file. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is The Maple Residences a condo I can buy?** No. The Maple Residences at 4071 Chatham Street are 144 rental units owned and managed by the non-profit Steveston 284 Seniors' Housing Society, opened in 2012 at No. 1 Road and Chatham; all suites are rented as fully serviced suites with the services included in the monthly rental fee. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/RIR/120801/RIRN120801_A15.pdf
- **Q: How does Austin Harris Residence work — can I apply directly?** Not directly for a market suite: it is an affordable assisted-living residence (50 one-bedroom suites, S.U.C.C.E.S.S. Multi-Level Care Society) developed under the Independent Living BC Program, where residents pay 70% of after-tax income, and registration and eligibility assessment run through Vancouver Coastal Health. It is registered with BC's Assisted Living Registry, class SN. https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/ and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/austin-harris-residence
- **Q: Is the building called "Steveston Village" a condo building?** It is a strata building at 12040 2nd Avenue between Chatham and Moncton Streets — but its units are townhouses, with one retail unit, per its sales/property source. The name matches the neighbourhood, not a housing form; it is not counted as an apartment condo here. https://goodmanreport.com/off-market/1131-steveston-village-12040-2nd-ave-richmond/
- **Q: Why is The Village on Moncton Street filed under Steveston South?** Because its MLS data records the board sub-area as Steveston South, and this program files each complex in exactly one file to prevent double-counting. The street geography (village core) and the board filing (South) are both stated, in both files. (Steveston South condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/steveston-south-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What does the heritage conservation area actually stop me doing?** Exterior alterations to properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area require a Heritage Alteration Permit under the Steveston Area Plan, and protected heritage buildings and resource types such as docks and landscape features are identified in the Official Community Plan. It does not stop a purchase — it changes renovation plans, timelines and resale positioning. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: Can I rent out a village strata unit?** Long-term, yes as a matter of provincial law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals, which is the version of the rule a buyer in a waterfront visitor village will actually meet. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve a Steveston Village address?** UNVERIFIED for village addresses: the SD38 locator returned no result for the sample address 4011 Moncton Street because it falls outside the locator's listed ranges for Moncton Street, and SD38 has no middle school tier. Westwind Elementary (11371 Kingfisher Drive) and R.A. McMath Secondary (4251 Garry Street) are the public schools in the Steveston area — confirm schooling for the exact address with SD38 directly. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Is the village in a floodplain?** Yes — the village is behind the City dike system on the Fraser River floodplain, Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels, and the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses long-term flood protection for the Steveston waterfront and harbour. https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The village's biggest seniors building exists because a veterans' branch gave up its land.** The Maple Residences project was developed by the Steveston 284 Seniors' Housing Society — formed by the Army, Navy and Air Force Veterans of Canada (Unit 284) with Steveston (Unit 284) Holdings Ltd. — on the ANAF site at No. 1 Road and Chatham Street, as 144 seniors' units plus a commercial building containing the ANAF lounge and clubhouse. That is why the core's seniors housing is non-profit rental beside a veterans' clubhouse, not a strata building. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/RIR/120801/RIRN120801_A15.pdf (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/steveston-village-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Two seniors buildings share one Chatham Street block, with two different admission doors.** The Maple Residences (serviced rental, apply to the society) and Bill Rigby Senior Apartments (subsidized, apply through BC Housing's Housing Registry) stand at 4071 and 4100 Chatham Street — a buyer touring one will see the other, and they are not the same product, price system or application route. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **The assisted-living building on Moncton Street is priced as a share of income, not a rent.** Austin Harris Residence residents pay 70% of after-tax income for the suite plus hospitality services under the Independent Living BC Program — the reason it cannot be compared monthly-rate-to-monthly-rate with The Maple Residences two streets away. https://successbc.ca/assisted-living/services/austin-harris-residence/
- **The school locator simply has no answer for Moncton Street.** The SD38 locator returned no result for 4011 Moncton Street because the address falls outside its listed ranges for the street — village catchment marketing in a listing is a claim to verify with the district directly, not a fact to repeat. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Heritage protection here covers more than houses.** The Steveston Area Plan's conservation area identifies protected heritage buildings and other resource types such as docks and landscape features in the Official Community Plan — a waterfront village lot can carry protected features that are not a building at all. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
