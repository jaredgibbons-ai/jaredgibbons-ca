# East Central Maple Ridge — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "East Central" is a Paragon/board sub-area name, not a City of Maple Ridge planning name; the west end of the sub-area is covered by the City's Town Centre Area Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates, in two clusters: (1) the Fraserview Village 55+ cluster on and around 116 Avenue and 225–227 Streets (Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows, Castlemount, Riverside); (2) the downtown/Haney stock along Fraser Street and 227 Street, including the Brickwater buildings and the new Podium and Highpointe buildings. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- East Central carried Maple Ridge's largest attached inventory in September 2026: 120 active "Condos & Townhomes" listings and 11 sales (9% sales ratio), SnapStats GVR September 2026. Maple Ridge's community-level attached median that month was $525,000; SnapStats publishes no sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- Transit anchors: Haney Place Exchange with the R3 RapidBus, and Port Haney Station at 22300 River Road on the West Coast Express. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- The City's own name for the Brickwater development location is "Brickwater" (a sample address in City material is 11897 227 Street); City pages do not state the building type, which comes from developer and listing sources. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This is the key document for the 1987–1997 Fraserview Village stock. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Building directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Rosewood Court and Fraser Glen — that is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The whole Fraserview Village cluster is marketed as 55+ — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw and which exemption, if any, fits the buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research file (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. The Town Centre Area Plan covers the sub-area's west end, and a Town Centre Area Plan update added a Town Centre Core designation and a River Bend area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Transit-Oriented Areas:** Maple Ridge's TOA Bylaw No. 8001-2024 designates the Haney Place Exchange and Port Haney Station areas — the policy frame behind the new downtown density at Podium, Brickwater and Highpointe. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Flood and escarpment rules on the south edge:** along the River Road/Port Haney edge the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits secondary suites on floodplain properties; the escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and a geotechnical report is required within 300 m of the escarpment crest. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Podium, Brickwater, Highpointe resales of never-occupied units can differ — confirm occupancy history), not ordinary resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Brickwater phases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What does the 55+ rule at Fraserview Village actually restrict?** Only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is legal in BC (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2). Exemptions cover people who lived in the unit before the bylaw or age change, live-in caregivers, and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Rosewood Court's directory entry records age restrictions; the registered bylaw text on the Form B is what governs any specific buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The directory says Rosewood Court bans rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. What still binds: the 55+ bylaw limits who may live in the unit, and a short-term rental bylaw can still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do I get for my strata fee in Fraserview Village?** The village buildings share one amenity building — indoor pool, hot tub, exercise room/clubhouse, guest suite — and listings for The Willows state the fee includes amenity-building use plus gas; Rosewood Court and Emerald Manor listings state gas and hot water are included. Exact inclusions differ by building and must be read off the Form B. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3089543-11578-225-street-103-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-9w7/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the village buildings?** The directory records Rosewood Court as pets allowed with restrictions; a Willows listing describes two cats or two dogs (or one of each) as permitted. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws for the specific building govern — for Emerald Manor and Castlemount the published position is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1987–1997 building?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years with a 30-year projection. The village stock (The Willows 1987, Riverside 1988, Rosewood Court 1991, Emerald Manor 1997) is exactly the age band where roof, piping and parkade work shows up in those reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is the new downtown stock wood or concrete?** Mixed, per the sources: Podium is a 6-storey wood-frame building (presale source); Highpointe listings describe concrete and steel construction; Emerald Manor listings describe a mix ("Concrete, Frame Wood, Mixed (Exterior), Stucco" in one, concrete and metal frame in another). Construction affects insurance and sound transfer — verify per building, not by era. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/podium-maple-ridge-town-centre-presale-condos-and-townhomes-by-whitetail-homes/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3176529-412-11641-227-street-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-9j6
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Podium or Brickwater unit?** If the unit has never been occupied and is sold by the developer, yes — 5% GST on new construction, with the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate returning up to $50,000 on homes up to $1 million for qualifying agreements. A resale from an owner-occupier is exempt. Confirm the unit's occupancy and seller status before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How is transit from the downtown buildings?** Haney Place Exchange and the R3 RapidBus serve the town centre, and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) is the West Coast Express stop; TransLink's Langley–Haney Place BRT (22 km, 10-minute peak frequency) is in detailed planning with timelines not determined. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Q: Does the floodplain reach any East Central condos?** The south edge along River Road/Port Haney carries a 6.7 m geodetic flood construction level, and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties under Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h). The named condo buildings in Section 2 sit north of that edge, but flood status is parcel-specific — check the specific building's lot, not the sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Q: Why do listings disagree on Highpointe's name and Podium's unit count?** Highpointe appears as both "Highpointe" and "Highpoint" in listings — the directory's strata record (EPS9270) uses Highpointe. Podium's directory count of 125 units appears to combine the presale record's 120 condos plus 5 townhomes. Use the strata plan number, not the marketing name, when ordering documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/podium-11655-fraser-street

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Four separate strata corporations share one amenity building.** Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount are distinct buildings/stratas marketed together as Fraserview Village, and the amenity building (indoor pool, hot tub, clubhouse, guest suite) is a village facility — a unit's fee and voting rights sit with its own strata, not the village. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The village's published data is uneven.** Rosewood Court is the only Fraserview building with a full directory record (1991, 81 units). Emerald Manor's build year (1997) and The Willows' (1987) come from listings, and no public source checked publishes unit counts for Emerald Manor, The Willows or Castlemount — budget for a Form B and strata-documents order to get the real numbers. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Two of the "condo" counts downtown blend forms.** Podium's 125 directory units include 5 townhomes, and Brickwater's "200+ condominiums" is a four-building project total, not a single building — compare per-building counts only from strata plans. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/podium-maple-ridge-town-centre-presale-condos-and-townhomes-by-whitetail-homes/
- **The escarpment rules reach into East Central.** The escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street and a geotechnical report is required within 300 m of the crest — relevant to any future redevelopment or addition on the slope-side streets above the river, even though the existing condo buildings predate the policy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **A second rapid link is planned, not built.** TransLink's Langley–Haney Place BRT is in detailed planning (22 km, 10-minute peak headways) with timelines not determined — price downtown units on today's R3 and West Coast Express service, not the BRT. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge's OCP citation is unsettled in current sources** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs 7998-2024, update adopted November 25, 2025) — quote planning policy for a specific building from a current City document, not from memory. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/east-central-maple-ridge-guide.md)
