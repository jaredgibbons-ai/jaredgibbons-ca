## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The condo rules below apply to the urban Cloverdale stock in the companion file. On farm parcels themselves, the ALR block governs first — it is placed first here because it is the reason this file has no buildings.

- **ALR stratification bar:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). There is no Form B, no strata council and no depreciation report for a farm home — diligence runs through title, the ALC record, City zoning and BC Assessment farm class instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **ALR principal residence:** one principal residence per parcel is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **ALR additional residence:** one additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and it does not need to be intended for farm help, provided local bylaws are met. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences; building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. Surrey's own STR rules sit on top of this layer for any non-ALR unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9):** the farm operation must meet the minimum income threshold — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Parcel-level ALR status:** UNVERIFIED as a check method in this pass — a direct re-open of the ALC's ALR maps page returned a 429 rate-limit error and was not retried. Confirm a parcel's ALR status through the ALC record and title before pricing it. The live page's Surrey A-1 / A-2 zone naming for Cloverdale ALR parcels is likewise UNVERIFIED on a City source in this file — confirm the zone in City mapping / Zoning Bylaw 12000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Applies to urban Cloverdale resales in the companion file; a farm parcel has no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No farm-parcel application: ALR dwellings are not strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. **SSMUH:** Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, with no retroactive legalization of an unpermitted suite — an urban-lot framework that does NOT create extra dwellings on ALR parcels, where the ALC caps above govern. Surrey's urban suite rule (one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house) and coach-house rule (35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR) are stated for contrast; whether a coach house can serve as the ALR additional residence on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures. Farm-class tax treatment is a BC Assessment position (see the Class 9 thresholds above), not a City rate. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey, non-ALR layer):** only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. On ALR land the ALC approval layer above applies first. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. Whether a new farm residence purchase qualifies turns on its own facts — confirm with a tax professional; no farm-specific GST claim is made here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Farm / mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential value — confirm the split with a lawyer/notary before relying on any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for urban presales, not for farm-parcel sales. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Farm financing is commonly commercial / agricultural lending rather than insured residential lending — lender-specific: UNCONFIRMED as a general farm rule. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The farm feed and the residential table describe different markets.** Serpentine records 0 listings and 0 sales in SnapStats' residential splits while hosting the farm feed's visible agri-business listings — reading the zero rows as "nothing trades in Serpentine" inverts the evidence. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **The suite rule flips at the ALR line.** On an urban Surrey lot a suite can sit in defined accessory forms under City rules; on ALR land a suite exists only inside the principal residence, and the detached second dwelling is a separate, size-capped "additional residence." The same street can carry both regimes parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **The 40-hectare line doubles the second-home cap.** The additional-residence limit is 90 m² at 40 ha or less and 186 m² only above 40 ha (about 98.8 acres) — most Cloverdale acreages a buyer tours will sit on the 90 m² side of that line. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Farm class is an annual income test with a hard date.** The thresholds scale with operation size ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000) and the application deadline is October 31 — a buyer who closes after that date inherits the seller's classification position for the coming year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Cloverdale's name is an agricultural artifact.** The City states the community took its name from Clover Valley, after the clover growing in the valley, and grew from a largely rural and agricultural community settled in the mid to late 1800s — the farm belt is the original settlement form, not a leftover. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **The ALC ceiling is not a permit.** The ALC rules cap what may exist; the City of Surrey may further regulate or restrict ALR residences through zoning, and anything beyond the permitted number or size needs ALC approval. Both layers must clear before a residence plan is priced into an offer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
