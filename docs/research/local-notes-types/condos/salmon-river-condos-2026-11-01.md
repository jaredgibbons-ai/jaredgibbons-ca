# Salmon River — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Salmon River is a rural district in the Township of Langley, along the Salmon River in the township's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley. The district follows the Salmon River valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue, with Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side; no Township-published boundary for the board sub-area was confirmed. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- "Salmon River" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. That report records the housing mix directly: 22 detached listings against 4 attached listings and 0 attached sales in September 2026 — and "attached" in that report combines condos and townhomes, so even those 4 listings are not evidence of apartment condos. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md).
- There are no condo streets in this district: the roads are valley farm roads and upland acreage roads (56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent, 232 Street, 240 Street, River Road / Glover Road toward Fort Langley), and the Township's development applications route named no condo or townhome project inside the district when the area research checked it. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- Transit confirms the rural form: TransLink route 562 runs on Glover Road at the Fort Langley edge of the district; no route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads inside the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

There is no condo stock in Salmon River for these rules to attach to today. They are included because they govern any strata lot that does exist in the district, and because they explain why condo form is structurally unlikely on the district's farm land.

- **Form B and strata records:** an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days; the maximum charge is $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a Salmon River property is offered as a strata lot, the Form B is the document that proves what the strata is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption is the only one that could plausibly touch a small rural strata here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw. No age-restricted building is verified in Salmon River. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning and SSMUH:** rural land is under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (the district's farm and acreage homes sit in the rural zones, including RU-2; the RU-2 minimums were not confirmed from a readable bylaw section in the area research). The Township's SSMUH rules under Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allow 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², with lots over 4,050 m² ineligible for those tiers — and the Township states that on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or one accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit, depending on zoning. On Salmon River acreages, that suite/accessory route — not a strata condo building — is the added-unit route the rules describe. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **ALR and strata subdivision:** farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places — confirm a parcel on the ALC maps. A strata plan causes a subdivision: ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 is titled "Strata plans cause subdivision," and the bulletin states a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). That is the structural reason apartment condos do not appear on ALR valley land without an ALC process. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **ALR housing size rules:** a principal residence on ALR land built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, and the ALC permits one additional residence without an application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Buyer-layer taxes, stated for completeness:** a resale house purchase in this district carries BC Property Transfer Tax at 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% to $3 million and 5% above, with the First-Time Home Buyers' Program removing PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (partial to $860,000); GST at 5% applies to new construction, not resales, and the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Application of either program to a specific Salmon River acreage is UNCONFIRMED until eligibility is checked — acreage values and land use can change the analysis, and no source in this file rules on it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Salmon River at all?** No named apartment-condo building was verified in any source checked — Township development applications, the area research file, or listing/directory searches, which returned only detached houses and acreages. SnapStats recorded just 4 attached listings (condos and townhomes combined) and 0 attached sales in the sub-area in September 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx and Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md)
- **Q: Could a condo building be built on the valley farm land?** Not as-of-right on ALR land: a strata plan causes a subdivision under ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, and subdivision of agricultural land requires Commission permission after an application or a permission in the ALR General Regulation. Whether any specific parcel could obtain that permission is UNCONFIRMED — it is an application outcome, not a published rule. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: If I want strata living near Salmon River, where is it?** The Township's development pattern puts strata apartment buildings in its urban communities (Willoughby, Walnut Grove, Fort Langley area) — no source in this file names a Salmon River option, and the nearest options are outside this area file. UNCONFIRMED as to any specific nearest building.
- **Q: Is a Salmon River acreage with a suite a "condo alternative"?** It is a different legal thing: the Township states lots over 4,050 m² may add one secondary suite or one accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning, and on ALR land the additional-residence size caps are 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). A suite is part of one freehold title, not a strata lot — there is no Form B, no strata fee and no strata corporation. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: A listing says "strata" for a Salmon River property. What should I check?** Whether the title is actually a strata lot (including a bare-land strata) or freehold non-strata — listings checked for this file show both wordings in the wider valley area, including "Freehold NonStrata" on Salmon River houses. If it is a strata lot, request the Form B; the strata must provide it within 7 days. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a home I buy here?** A freehold house is not governed by strata rental bylaws at all. If the property were a strata lot, long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 in any case. Tenancy law itself is outside this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does the flood picture matter more here than condo rules?** Yes. The valley is Fraser River floodplain land; the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and has flood box gates that close as river levels rise — and upgrades are in detailed design and tendering. That file matters to insurance and lending on valley parcels in a way strata documents never will here. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River address?** For the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown; Coghlan Fundamental Elementary at that address is a district school of choice on the upland east side. Run the exact address — catchments are address-based. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: Is there any presale or new condo project planned in the district?** None was named in the Township development applications route checked for the area research, and none was found in the searches for this file. A future project is UNCONFIRMED — the honest answer is that no source states one. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The pump station is a live capital project (Township, current project page):** Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for the Salmon River Pump Station Upgrades — flood infrastructure, not strata infrastructure, is the district's active construction story. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **The "4 attached listings" figure misleads condo hunters:** SnapStats combines condos and townhomes in one attached category, and in September 2026 Salmon River had 4 attached listings and 0 attached sales against 22 detached listings and 5 detached sales — a detached market with a rounding-error attached tail. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md).
- **No school sits inside the valley itself:** no SD35 school physically inside the Salmon River valley was confirmed in the area research; the named schools are upland or district-choice schools reached by road. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/salmon-river.md)
- **Wells and septic are the district's "strata documents":** rural properties may use wells searchable in the provincial GWELLS database (well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well), and Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system and a letter of certification after construction. Those filings are the paper trail a buyer here actually reviews. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **The river itself limits building envelopes:** the Salmon River is a salmon-bearing watercourse covered by Metro Vancouver watershed material, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit where anything — strata or not — can be built near the river; parcel-level setbacks are a Township check, UNCONFIRMED for any specific lot in this file. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
