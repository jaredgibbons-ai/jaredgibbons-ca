# Brentwood Park — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Brentwood Park is the residential sub-area in north-west Burnaby around Brentwood Park Elementary, south and east of the Brentwood Town Centre. "Brentwood Park" is the GVR board sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," and it is also the name of Brentwood Park Elementary School (SD41); the City planning framework for the area is the Brentwood Town Centre, one of Burnaby's four town centres. A City page using "Brentwood Park" as an official neighbourhood name was not confirmed, and the City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area — exact MLS boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: in two clusters. The tower cluster rings the town centre and its two Millennium Line stations — the Amazing Brentwood towers at Willingdon and Lougheed, the SOLO District towers on Rosser Avenue and Skyline Drive/Court, Concord Brentwood along the south side of Lougheed Highway, Gilmore Place and Triomphe / Escala at Gilmore Avenue, and Affinity on Douglas Road — while an older mid- and high-rise cluster (Vantage Point, Anola Place) sits on Bellwood Avenue above the residential streets, and low-rise product (Memento on Beta Avenue) fills in toward the houses. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Sub-area attached numbers, September 2026: Condos & Townhomes inventory 360, solds 39, sales ratio 11% — the largest attached inventory of any Burnaby sub-area in this batch — against detached inventory 21, solds 3, sales ratio 14%. SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: Burnaby-wide Condos & Townhomes median was $686,000, $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market in September 2026. There is no Brentwood Park sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- Main streets: Lougheed Highway runs along the town centre's south side, Willingdon Avenue runs south to Highway 1, Metrotown and BCIT, and Delta Avenue / Parker Street serve the residential grid. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- Transit anchors: Brentwood Town Centre Station and Holdom Station, both Millennium Line, serve the area's edges; Brentwood Town Centre Station at Lougheed Highway and Willingdon Avenue is also an R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus connection point, and Gilmore Station (Millennium Line) serves the Gilmore Avenue towers at the sub-area's east side — Triomphe listings describe the building as one block / steps from Gilmore SkyTrain. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md) and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3101905-1888-gilmore-avenue-1801-burnaby-brentwood-park-homes-for-sale-649800/brochure

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In a sub-area with 360 attached listings, this is a building-by-building exercise — the Bellwood Avenue stock's files (Vantage Point, Anola Place) will read very differently from a 2024 Gilmore Place tower's. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any Brentwood directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a named building in Section 2 is old bylaw text, not current law — and it does not apply at all to the purpose-built rental towers (Amazing Brentwood Tower ONE rental, Brentwood Block rentals), which are landlord-owned, not strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw is published for any Section 2 building in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per building; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (Bylaw No. 4742), which is repealed — flag any source still citing the 1965 bylaw as current. The City states the new bylaw is a full repeal and replacement, with many former FAR-based districts replaced by height-based districts. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the area falls under the Brentwood Town Centre framework within that OCP. City-wide, Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024 (3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Oasis presales, Brentwood Block strata sales, any remaining developer sales at Slate / Gilmore Place), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to an Oasis or Brentwood Block strata purchase. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Oasis and Brentwood Block developer contracts. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Streamside check at the south edge:** Still Creek runs along the south side of the Brentwood area; parcel-level streamside setbacks are property-specific checks — UNVERIFIED for individual lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)

- **Q: How deep is the competition if I buy or sell a Brentwood condo?** Deeper than anywhere else in this Burnaby batch: 360 condos & townhomes listings produced 39 sales in September 2026 (11% sales ratio), so pricing is set inside the building and the two nearest towers first, not off the city story. Burnaby-wide, the Condos & Townhomes median that month was $686,000 at $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market — community figures only; SnapStats publishes no Brentwood Park median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- **Q: Which buildings are the newest?** Two Gilmore Place (2108 Gilmore Avenue) completed in 2024 at 64 storeys and 643 apartments — the tallest building in BC at 215.8 metres — and Concord Brentwood's Hillside East at 4890 Lougheed Highway is profiled as built in 2024, with Hillside West (4730 Lougheed) in 2022 and Amazing Brentwood Tower 3 (4650 Brentwood Boulevard) in 2021. Slate (2135 Springer Avenue) is marketed as Brentwood's newest landmark, but its completion year is UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Two_Gilmore_Place and https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/concord-brentwood-hillside-east-tower-d
- **Q: Is the Amazing Brentwood tower at Willingdon a condo building?** Careful — there are two near-identical names. The strata towers are at 4510 Halifax Way (Tower 1, 2019), 1955 Alpha Way (Tower 2) and 4650 Brentwood Boulevard (Tower 3, 2021). SHAPE's own rental site places "Tower ONE" purpose-built rental homes at 1820 Willingdon Avenue, professionally owned and managed by SHAPE — that building is rental, not strata. Confirm which address a listing belongs to before comparing. https://rental.theamazingbrentwood.com/faq and https://realestatecoalharbour.com/the-amazing-brentwood/listing.r2976362-burnaby.17189651153/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Brentwood condo long-term?** Yes — strata long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, whatever a directory or older bylaw says. Short-term rentals are different: one Amazing Brentwood Tower 1 listing markets a unit as "Airbnb Allowed," but that is listing copy for one unit, not a building rule — the registered bylaws on the Form B govern short-term use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-brentwood-park-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=18
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published positions are unit-listing fields, not building rules, in most cases here: an Amazing Brentwood Tower 3 listing and an Affinity II listing each record "Cats OK," and Altus / Stratus amenity lists include a dog park at Stratus. For any specific building, pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC and the registered bylaws are the answer — building-level pet rules for most Section 2 towers are UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4650-brentwood-boulevard/904 and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/2232-douglas-road/805

One concrete published example: a Vantage Point I (2020 Bellwood Avenue) listing states the strata fees include heat and hot water. The older Bellwood buildings also carry the elevator, parkade and envelope cycles their depreciation reports must now map every five years. https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-brentwood-park-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=6 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

- **Q: Does a first-time buyer get help on a new Brentwood tower?** On a new build, potentially both programs: the FTHB GST/HST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on or after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. - **Q: Why do sources disagree on Concord Brentwood's addresses and unit counts?** Because the project is phased and profile sites repeat figures across towers: one profile series gives both Hillside West towers 892 units each, and rankings list Phase One at 4890 Lougheed Hwy while a profile puts Hillside East Tower D at the same address built in 2024.
