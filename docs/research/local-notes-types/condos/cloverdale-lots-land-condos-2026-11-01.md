### The ALR edge — why part of this feed can never carry condos

- The feed's acreage parcels sit on the ALR edge of the community. ALR land cannot be stratified: subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission, and ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the ALR. A farm parcel's houses therefore cannot be sold as strata condos while the land is in the ALR. Which parcels on this feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The stratification bar at the ALR edge (this topic's condo rule):** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"), issued October 18, 2022 — a strata plan may not be approved or registered in the ALR unless permitted under the ALCA regime (ALCA s. 21(1)). Serviced urban lots inside the community carry no such bar; ALR parcels do. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR housing rules (they govern the feed's acreage parcels where a parcel is in the reserve):** a principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), where City bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)
- **Form B (for the strata stock this topic is not):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to a vacant freehold lot, which is the point: there is no strata corporation on a land parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata stock generally):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither rule creates strata stock on a vacant lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — this voids the rental half of the St. Andrews, Calera and Compass directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. On a land parcel there is no strata bylaw at all. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — again, strata law, with no vacant-lot application. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only, never cited as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000. Surrey's SSMUH changes of July 8, 2024 replaced the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones with the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD with R6 Semi-Detached — older land material using RF-12 / RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names. Feed cards describing lots as "R3 zoned" are listing text only — confirm the current zone of any parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (urban lots):** only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)

- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $740,000 (the Cloverdale combined attached community median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only, not a land price), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,800 (2% × $540,000) = **$12,800 PTT**. - **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on a Cloverdale land parcel was confirmed on a developer's own site in the research; where any developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
