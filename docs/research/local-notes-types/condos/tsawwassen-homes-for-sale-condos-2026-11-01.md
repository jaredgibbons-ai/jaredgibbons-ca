## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the Springs buildings — whose unit counts, strata plans and bylaws are UNCONFIRMED in public sources — the Form B and title are the only route to those facts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — no Tsawwassen strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The Lexington (1150 54A Street) is this assembly's published 55+ building — confirm the bylaw and any exemption on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan:** Delta's current Official Community Plan is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered area-plan schedules. The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 of the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (1985); any current copy citing "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" conflates the two bylaws. Farm land in Bylaw 8400 is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), and the Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area is in Bylaw 8400, Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** the program's Delta sources conflict on the zoning bylaw's citation (Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 in one line of sources; "8400, 2025" in another — 8400 is the OCP's number). This file flags the collision and asserts neither as the zoning bylaw's number; check the City's current consolidated zoning bylaw before quoting a zone section for a specific lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/tsawwassen-homes-for-sale.md)

- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000.
