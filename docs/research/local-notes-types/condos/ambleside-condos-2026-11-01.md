# Ambleside — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Ambleside is the District of West Vancouver's municipal town centre and waterfront: the Marine Drive commercial core around 14th–18th Streets, Ambleside Park and the Argyle Avenue waterfront, and the residential streets between the water and the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1). District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Street-by-street boundaries for the sub-area as a whole are UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- Condo stock actually concentrates in two bands: the waterfront avenues (Bellevue Avenue and the Marine Drive / Esquimalt Avenue blocks at the east end of the town centre) and the numbered-street blocks immediately north of Marine Drive between 13th and 18th Streets — that is where every verified building in Section 2 sits. Apartment and mixed-use form concentrates in the town-centre blocks under the OCP and the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan, while houses north of Marine Drive sit in the RS single-family zones. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- Park Royal, immediately east across Taylor Way, is a separate neighbouring sub-area, not part of Ambleside — buildings there are not counted in this file. Dundarave is west along Marine Drive and the Centennial Seawalk. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- Main streets: Marine Drive is the east–west waterfront route; Taylor Way (east) and 15th Street / Lions Gate Bridge access connect Marine Drive to Highway 1 and downtown Vancouver. TransLink route 250 runs on Marine Drive between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via the Lions Gate Bridge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- Anchors inside the area: West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive; West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive; Hollyburn Elementary, 1329 Duchess Avenue; West Vancouver Secondary, 1750 Mathers Avenue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- Market numbers: West Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in the September 2026 FVR + GVR reports (coverage is Surrey, S Surrey White Rock, Cloverdale, Langley, North Delta, Abbotsford, Mission, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, New Westminster, Pitt Meadows and Maple Ridge only) — no median or inventory figure is quoted in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This is why the "rentals not allowed" directory lines at Westerlies, Sea Wind and Park Marine in Section 2 do not bar a long-term tenancy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A directory line that says only "age restrictions" (Westerlies, Section 2) does not by itself prove a valid 55+ bylaw — read the filed bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** Ambleside sits under the District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. Apartment and mixed-use form concentrates in the town-centre blocks; the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan was implemented by a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 after the District missed the Province's December 31, 2025 adoption deadline. A Foreshore Development Permit is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area — waterfront property or other property at risk of coastal flooding — which applies to Ambleside's Bellevue Avenue and waterfront-edge buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, as used in batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **West Vancouver tax timing:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Ambleside assessed values the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where in Ambleside do condos actually exist?** In the town-centre and waterfront bands: Bellevue Avenue, Marine Drive and Esquimalt Avenue at the east end, and the 13th–18th Street blocks north of Marine Drive. The verified buildings in Section 2 — from a 1964 building (Park Marine) to Grosvenor Ambleside (phases completed 2018 and 2020) — all sit in those bands; the streets north toward the highway are RS-zone detached houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **Q: A directory says my building bans rentals. Can I still rent my unit out long-term?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022, whatever an older directory line at Westerlies, Sea Wind or Park Marine still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Westerlies is listed with "age restrictions." Does that stop a younger buyer?** Only a 55-or-older bylaw is valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, and the directory does not state the age or the bylaw's terms — that is UNCONFIRMED until the filed bylaws are read on the Form B. Exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/westerlies-1480-duchess-ave and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which buildings allow pets?** From the published sources in Section 2: Westshore Place is stated pet friendly (1 dog or 2 cats) and The Tides is stated to allow pets; Westerlies, Sea Wind and Park Marine are stated pets not allowed. Any building not listed there is UNCONFIRMED — confirm on the Form B. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3118747-204-650-16th-street-west-vancouver-british-columbia-v7v3r9-629000/brochure
- **Q: Is Westerleigh PARC a condo I can buy into?** No. It is a PARC Retirement Living rental community — there is no strata lot to purchase. https://parcliving.ca/home/
- **Q: Is Executive on the Park an Ambleside condo?** It is marketed at the gateway to West Vancouver beside Park Royal, and listing sources place it in the Park Royal neighbourhood — the research file treats Park Royal as a separate sub-area. The project also mixes tenures: 64 condominiums, 4 townhomes and 20 rental apartments per the presale source. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/executive-on-the-park-west-vancouver-presale-condos-townhomes-by-executive-group-development/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **Q: What should I check on an older Ambleside building (1960s–1970s stock)?** The Form B package: the latest depreciation report, financials, CRF balance and planned work or levies. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with 5+ lots must have a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, and since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Does a waterfront Bellevue Avenue building carry extra permit or flood issues?** The District requires a Foreshore Development Permit for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area — waterfront property and other property at risk of coastal flooding — and the research file names Ambleside's Bellevue/Argyle edge as exactly that stock. Check the building's permit and insurance picture, not just the unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **Q: Which schools are in Ambleside, and do they settle my catchment?** Hollyburn Elementary (1329 Duchess Avenue) and West Vancouver Secondary (1750 Mathers Avenue) are inside the area, but School District 45 catchments are address-based and the locator result for a specific condo address is UNVERIFIED — run the exact address in the SD45 locator. SD45 has no middle tier: elementary K–7, secondary 8–12. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **Q: Are taxes higher than the District bill suggests?** Potentially: District taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty that day, a further 5% September 2, 2026), and the Province's additional school tax adds 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026 — a live issue for the larger waterfront units. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The waterfront is now fully public — the last lot was bought in 2025.** On March 31, 2025 the District announced it had acquired 1444 Argyle Avenue, the last of 32 waterfront lots acquired since 1975, to form part of Brissenden Waterfront Park connecting Ambleside Park to John Lawson Park; public ownership of the waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is a stated community value in the District's 2013 Waterfront Guiding Principles. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **The town-centre plan arrived by provincial order, not by Council.** The Province directed adoption of the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan by December 31, 2025; the District missed the deadline, and a provincial order implemented the plan effective April 7, 2026, with District progress reports to the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining directives. Density expectations for town-centre and adjacent lots changed on that date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **The seawalk predates most of the condo stock.** The Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 for Canada's centennial and runs roughly 1.7 km from Ambleside Park west to Dundarave Park — the waterfront buildings in Section 2 (1964–1977) were built around an already-public shoreline. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **The fastest bus on the North Shore now starts at Ambleside's east edge.** Since September 7, 2026 the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs one-seat from Park Royal Exchange through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station, roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak — Ambleside is the closest West Vancouver residential area to that terminus. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
- **Two of the area's directory "bans" are legally dead, one is not.** The "rentals not allowed" lines at Westerlies, Sea Wind and Park Marine cannot bar a long-term tenancy since November 24, 2022 — but a "pets not allowed" bylaw at the same buildings has no equivalent override and still binds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Ambleside's civic core is inside the condo area, not beside it.** Municipal Hall, the Seniors' Activity Centre at 695 21st Street, the Community Centre at 2121 Marine Drive and the Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive all cluster in and around the town centre — the services downsizers cite are on the same streets as the buildings in Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/ambleside.md)
