### Tenure traps excluded

- **Agricultural Land Reserve parcels are not development land in the condo sense:** subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission (apart from limited cases in the ALR General Regulation), ALR residences cannot be stratified, and the A1 Agriculture zone sets an 8 ha subdivision minimum — an edge-of-Tsawwassen "lot" in the reserve is farm land with a housing envelope, not a future strata site. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The strata rules below are the standard BC block, included because this file is part of the condos series and because a bare-land strata lot (Section 2) is governed by them; the land-specific rules follow.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — for any bare-land strata lot, the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A conventional freehold lot has no Form B — the equivalent diligence is title, zoning and the permit-area checks below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata lots only):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; minimum CRF contribution at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 (long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted) — the rule that voids the directory "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Heron Cove and Tsawwassen Greene in the cross-referenced files. **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 only, with the May 1, 2023 exemption expansion (younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents, defined child cases). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated — NO lettered area-plan schedules; NO Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 in the current OCP (Schedule D.1 belonged to the OLD Bylaw 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1 purpose: maintaining agricultural lands; designations on Map 2; Agriculture policies at s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan). A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages and amendment bylaws cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" and the research file works from Bylaw No. 7600 zone sheets, while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. The zone figures below are as the City's zone sheets state them, confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary
- **Urban zoning minimums (zone sheets):** RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² minimum lot sizes for subdivision; the RS3 sheet (single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait — the English Bluff / Cliff Drive shore standard) sets a 4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area, 30 m lot width and 30 m average lot depth. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Farm-zone subdivision and housing:** A1 (s. 10.10.8) — subdivision only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, minimum lot created 8 ha, with a home-site severance exception under ALC policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also 8 ha; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. On reserve land without an ALC application: principal residence total floor area 500 m² or less, no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels) where Delta bylaws also permit. Delta's A1 zone mirrors this: one farm house per lot, 500 m² farm house maximum, 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum, one secondary suite inside the farm house, one accessory farm dwelling unit at the same 90 m² / 186 m² limits. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **SSMUH:** Delta Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. Added-unit permission is not subdivision permission: it does not create new lots and does not waive the zone minimums above. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Hazard permit areas:** SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)" (OCP Appendix B, hazardous-conditions category) covers the west shore — mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence; development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer, and no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) sets the 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and 7.5 m tidal setback. All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Water and septic on unserviced parcels:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **GST on land and new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction and to taxable land sales — the GST treatment of a specific Tsawwassen lot sale (the live page's summary: taxable for newly subdivided developer lots, generally exempt for an older lived-in house sold at lot value) was NOT verified against a CRA source in the research file and stays UNCONFIRMED here; confirm with a CPA per transaction. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4; Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031, and the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) applies to a new home built on the lot, not to the lot alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — at typical Tsawwassen lot prices the purchase is taxed in the 1% and 2% tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (a new home, not vacant land alone). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the 7-day rescission frame (REDMA s. 21, deposits in trust under s. 18) does not apply to a land purchase — it applies to developer marketing of strata product; in Tsawwassen that frame sits on the Southlands and Tsawwassen Lands projects cross-referenced in Section 2, and the regional presale example named in this program is Bridge & Elliott in Ladner. A bare-land strata lot sold by a developer can carry REDMA questions — take them per project, not from this topic file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year — both engage at the home purchase/build stage for a buyer building on the lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — construction financing on a lot is lender-specific and is not stated here. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a condo I can buy off this lots-and-land page?** No. This topic's feed is land — serviced urban infill lots, bluff and shore lots, and agricultural parcels. Tsawwassen's condo buildings exist, but they are documented and counted in the Central, East, North and Boundary Beach files; Section 2 above is cross-reference only and counts zero buildings in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Where do buildable lots in Tsawwassen come from?** Subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum — from 335 m² in RS7 up to 4,000 m² in RS3 — and added-unit development on existing lots, since Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots; on the agricultural edges, a new A1 lot needs 8 ha. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel near Tsawwassen?** Only within tight limits: A1 needs a minimum 8 ha new lot, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision; and ALR land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** A principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, no more than 1,000 m² of fill; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. An ALR residence cannot be stratified — there is no condo outcome on reserve land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does the four-unit SSMUH permission mean I can create four lots?** No. Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots, but that permission does not create new lots, does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area, and subdivision still runs against the zone minimums. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** West shore: SD2 (English Bluff / Tsawwassen Escarpment) — Professional Engineer's conditions for hazard-affected development and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea. Boundary Bay: SD3 — 2.9 m datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback. Any parcel within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch also falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is GST payable on a lot purchase?** UNCONFIRMED for any specific lot from the sources in this file — the live page's summary was not verified against a CRA source, and treatment depends on the seller and the lot's history. Budget the question, and confirm with a CPA before writing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Q: What PTT will a lot purchase carry?** The general tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% on the portion to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a typical Tsawwassen lot sits entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. The first-time and newly built exemptions attach to homes and buyers meeting their conditions, not to vacant land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: How do I know if a "lot" is actually bare-land strata?** From title, not the listing: the research file records that no official Tsawwassen-wide freehold versus bare-land strata split is published (UNVERIFIED), and a bare-land strata lot carries strata fees, bylaws and a Form B package that a freehold lot does not. Pull title and, if a strata plan is registered, the Form B before pricing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Tsawwassen land?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta / Tsawwassen and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hazard overlays sort the urban stock more than street names do.** West-shore lots carry SD2 geotechnical conditions and the 7.5 m sea setback; Boundary Bay lots carry SD3 floor-level and tidal-setback figures — both are settled before any design work starts, and neither appears in a listing's headline. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **The two-system split is the whole topic.** An urban lot's potential is set by its zone minimum (as low as 335 m² for a new RS7 lot) plus the four-unit added-housing permission; a farm parcel's potential is set by the 8 ha A1 subdivision minimum and the ALC's housing and subdivision rules. The same "land" search spans both rule books. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **The live page states specifics this program could not verify.** Neighbourhood-by-neighbourhood basement and Flood Construction Level rules, bluff geotechnical setback details beyond SD2, tree-protection permit specifics, typical historic lot widths, and a claim that urban lots south of 28 Avenue connect to full municipal water and sewer are stated as fact on the live page and are UNVERIFIED here — the research file lists them expressly, and none is repeated in this file as fact. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **A farm ditch is a streamside area.** Delta's Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA reaches every property within 30 m of a stream including sloughs and ditches, so the setback check applies on farmland ditches as well as urban lots — a surprise on agricultural parcels that look open. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
