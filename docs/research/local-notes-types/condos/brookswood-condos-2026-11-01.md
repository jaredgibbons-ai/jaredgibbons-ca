## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Brookswood this document route applies to townhouse strata such as Bromley once it completes — it does NOT apply to the pad-rental parks in Section 2, which have no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. That voiding does not touch the parks in Section 2: a park's "rentals not allowed" line is a park rule under a tenancy regime, not a strata bylaw, and it is not voided by the strata change — read the park's own rules and tenancy agreement instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. The 55+ labels on the Brookswood parks are park rules under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act frame, not SPA bylaws — the exemptions above do not automatically carry into a park; the park's rules and the tenancy agreement govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township plans and zoning:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers the area, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. Zoning is Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500: zones named in Township notices for the area include Suburban Residential SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3 and Rural Zone RU-2, and Section 110.1 as read in Township council reports sets SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² (40 m frontage), SR-2 minimum 8,094 m² (45 m) and SR-3 minimum 1,765 m² (27.45 m); RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/brookswood.md)
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020:** the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments under Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allow 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the SSMUH tiers. **There is NO 6-plex / 6-unit tier in the Township of Langley** — any page or listing claiming six units as-of-right on a Brookswood lot is wrong; the research file behind this file documents no such claim on the live Brookswood page, and none is repeated here. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Secondary suites (Township):** the Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 definition); the Township's Residential Construction Guide — Secondary Suites (last updated January 2025) states the same 120 m² (1,291 sq ft) finished-living-area limit, that a building permit is required for all suites, and that an annual secondary suite infrastructure fee is charged on property taxes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/brookswood.md)
- **ALR:** much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; whether a specific Brookswood parcel is in the ALR or a floodplain is UNVERIFIED per parcel — the Township directs applicants to check designations in GeoSource. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is one structural reason ALR parcels here never become condo stock. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/brookswood.md)
- **Pad rent (parks):** where a Brookswood purchase is a park home, pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act: the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months. No pad-rent dollar figure for any Brookswood park is published in this file — no official or operator source states one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant in Brookswood to developer sales at Bromley, not to resale townhouses or park homes. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $611,000 (the combined Langley community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is NOT a Brookswood figure, because no Brookswood median is published), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $8,220 (2% × $411,000) = **$10,220 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/brookswood.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** Bromley is selling as a presale with Phase 1 completion estimated Summer 2027 on the developer's sheet; REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
