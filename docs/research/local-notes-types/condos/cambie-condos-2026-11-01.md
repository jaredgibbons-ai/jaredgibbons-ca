## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The corridor street is Cambie Street, crossed by West King Edward Avenue, West 41st Avenue, West 49th Avenue and Marine Drive; Oak Street is the parallel arterial west of Cambie Street. The Cambie Corridor Plan runs from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: at the Canada Line station nodes — King Edward (Cambie Village), Oakridge–41st Avenue (the Oakridge Municipal Town Centre anchor), Langara–49th Avenue, and the Marine Drive / West 59th Avenue end (Cambie Gardens, Belpark) — plus the Queen Elizabeth Park rise around West 29th–35th Avenues (Chelsea, Livingstone, 35 Park West, Lina, The Charlotte). The side streets between stations are primarily houses, duplexes and multiplexes under R1-1. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- Transit: the Canada Line runs the length of the corridor along Cambie Street, with stations at King Edward, Oakridge–41st Avenue, Langara–49th Avenue and Marine Drive; TransLink route 41 runs via West 41st Avenue and route 49 via 49th Avenue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- Landmarks: Queen Elizabeth Park (52 hectares, 125 metres above sea level, the highest point in the City of Vancouver) sits on the corridor's east side at Little Mountain; Oakridge Park (the former Oakridge Centre site) anchors the south end at Cambie and West 41st. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 FVR and GVR reports contain no Vancouver rows) — no median, inventory, sales-ratio or days-on-market figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed or capped is void to that extent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. OPAL by Element is the corridor's published 55+ building ("One resident must be 55+" per a current listing) — that form of bylaw is legal; check the Form B for how it is registered. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.kentbraaten.com/listing-crea/r3024011-578-438-w-king-edward-avenue-vancouver-homes-for-sale-858000/brochure
- **City plan and zoning:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved by Council May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) guides growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River around the Canada Line stations; zoning is Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), arterial sites on Cambie Street sit under the C-2 District Schedule, and station-area parcels are typically site-specific CD-1 districts. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **District energy / geothermal charges:** The Camellia's listings record geothermal heating — in buildings with ambient/geothermal or district-energy systems the utility charge can sit outside the strata fee; whether a specific corridor building carries a separate energy-utility charge, and its amount: UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the Form B and the strata's utility invoices. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3092040-6733-cambie-street-401-vancouver-bc-v6p-3h1/brochure
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to Claridge House (2026) and Lina (2025) resales from the developer and to Oakridge Park presales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to Oakridge Park and any remaining developer sales at Claridge House. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Is "Cambie" a real neighbourhood?** Not as a City local area — the corridor runs through the official areas of South Cambie, Riley Park and Oakridge under the Cambie Corridor Plan, and the board carries both "Cambie" and "South Cambie" as overlapping searches. Name the official area and the nearest Canada Line station in any offer or listing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Q: Which station is my building actually near?** The corridor has four: King Edward, Oakridge–41st Avenue, Langara–49th Avenue and Marine Drive. Chelsea, Livingstone, 35 Park West, Lina and The Charlotte sit between King Edward and Oakridge–41st; Forty Nine West and The Camellia sit at Langara–49th; Belpark and Cambie Gardens sit at the Marine Drive end. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a corridor condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. - **Q: Is OPAL a normal condo purchase?** Only partly: OPAL is a 130-home retirement community of which 44 homes are strata condos (the rest are rental and complex-care suites), listings record Freehold Strata title and a 55+ requirement ("One resident must be 55+"), and it operates as a retirement residence — read the service and care agreements, not just the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/opal-438-west-king-edward-a-retirement-community-of-130-homes-in-three-buildings-of-a-6-storey-and-two-4-stories-by-opal-development

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor is a plan, not a neighbourhood.** The Cambie Corridor Plan was approved May 9, 2011, with Phase 3 in 2018 extending policy to the side streets within walking distance of the stations — two lots on the same block can carry different plan designations, and most corridor densities still require a rezoning application. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Cambie Gardens sits on the Pearson Dogwood site.** Onni's master-planned community at Cambie and West 59th Avenue is a 25-acre site whose later phases may be purpose-built rentals rather than condos — the tenure of each new tower must be checked building by building, not assumed from Phase 1. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/cambie-gardens-presale-luxury-vancouver-onni-condos/
- **OPAL spans a whole city block — and three tenures.** The 130-home OPAL community between Yukon and Cambie Streets mixes 44 strata condos, 56 rentals and 30 complex-care suites in three connected buildings; two units with the same address can have entirely different purchase documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/opal-438-west-king-edward-a-retirement-community-of-130-homes-in-three-buildings-of-a-6-storey-and-two-4-stories-by-opal-development
- **Queen Elizabeth Park is the city's high point.** At 125 metres above sea level atop Little Mountain, the park explains the view premiums on the West 29th–35th Avenue buildings — and why Chelsea was marketed across the street from it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **The Oakridge station itself is being rebuilt with private money.** Reports on Oakridge Park describe a $27 million Oakridge–41st Avenue Station overhaul, $25 million of it privately funded by the developers — corridor buyers near 41st are buying into a construction zone and a town centre at the same time. https://burnabyhouse.com/news/oakridge-park-developers-ask-city-ease/
- **The corridor's north end is a hospital district.** The City states the South Cambie portion is known for its medical facilities, including BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre at 4480 Oak Street — a distinct tenant and buyer pool for King Edward–area buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
