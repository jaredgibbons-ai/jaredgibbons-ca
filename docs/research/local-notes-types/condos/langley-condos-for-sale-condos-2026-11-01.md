## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s.59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in Section 2 wherever a directory line says "rentals not allowed" (Parkway Estates, Parkway II): those lines are VOID for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; Langley City's own short-term rental status was NOT VERIFIED on any City page in this program — flagged, not filled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-condos-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children (SPA Regulation 7.01 exemptions). Live in this roster at Parkway Estates, Parkway II, Glenmont Park and Waterfront at Bedford Landing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Two municipalities, two rule sets:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City buildings — cite the number; its adoption date is UNVERIFIED (conflicted in sources). City suite cap: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of gross floor area, registered owner must reside on the lot; City SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 (RS1/RS2: 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop). Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs the Township buildings; Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) has NO 6-plex tier — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m² — and the Township suite cap is 120 m². Township gentle-density context only; it does not change an existing strata building's bylaws. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-condos-for-sale.md) (File: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-condos-under-499k-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (live at Aldergrove Town Centre and Willoughby's newest buildings), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Worked example (inputs stated): PTT on the combined attached median $611,000 with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $411,000 ($8,220) = $10,220 — the $611,000 figure is the combined Condos & Townhomes median, NOT a condo-only price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** Matteo and Walnut Park engage REDMA where sold as presales — s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s.18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
