## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a townhouse, the Form B and the strata's depreciation report are also where exterior, roof and envelope obligations — the big townhouse cost items — are confirmed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Most of the named roster above is 1987–1993 stock — the reports matter more, not less, at that age. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals not allowed" line recorded for Willow Park Estates in Section 2 is void in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Seven of the twelve named complexes in Section 2 are presented as 55+ (Huntsfield Green, Willow Park Estates, Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green, Chelsea Green, Creekside Villa) — the registered bylaw on the Form B, not a listing summary, is the proof for each. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Townhouse suites — a live source conflict, flagged not resolved.** Under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may be located within a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome, is capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, and each principal dwelling unit may have up to one; the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states the opposite — suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses. The bylaw definition is the later source; where a listing in this band markets a lower-level suite or mortgage helper, confirm the specific unit's permits and strata bylaws before valuing the income. In Langley City, Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 caps a suite at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with the registered owner required to reside on the lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/langley-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Municipal framework:** Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m²); Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 403 (R-CL compact-lot zone) sets a 220 m² minimum and a 464.5 m² maximum floor area — a "220–355 m²" range that circulated in earlier program research is WRONG, no 355 figure exists in s. 403. Strata townhome lots are not the SSMUH shape, and no SSMUH claim is made for any complex in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/langley-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST on new townhomes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live in this band in Willoughby's newest projects. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — most of this band sits above the partial-exemption ceiling, so the exemption generally does not reach it. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the top of this band overlaps its partial range for qualifying new builds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked examples (inputs stated):** (1) Down payment — a $1,200,000 purchase at the band ceiling: 5% × $500,000 = $25,000, plus 10% × $700,000 = $70,000, total minimum down payment $95,000 (under 20%, so mortgage insurance applies). (2) PTT — a $1,200,000 purchase with no exemption: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,000,000 = $20,000, total $22,000. (3) GST rebate on a new $1,200,000 townhome — GST at 5% is $60,000; the Bill C-4 rebate at that price is $50,000 × ($1,500,000 − $1,200,000) ÷ $500,000 = $30,000, leaving $30,000 net GST for an eligible first-time buyer on those inputs. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer sale surfaced by this feed, which is never counted as resale band stock here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
