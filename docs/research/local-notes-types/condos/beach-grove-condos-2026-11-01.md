## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1977 building (Tsawwassen Mews) and a 2005 building (Heron Cove) alike, this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw. That voids, in-entry, the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for **Heron Cove** above (and the same directory line for Tsawwassen Greene in the Central file). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful; exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (expanded May 1, 2023). No Beach Grove building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — it contains NO lettered area-plan schedules; there is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 in the current OCP (that was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950), and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". A City report (LU009394) describing 1728 Beach Grove Road as having a "Single Family Residential designation in the Tsawwassen Area Plan" is speaking of the former plan — historical framing only. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages and amendment bylaws cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" (the bylaw whose s. 6.2.3 sets the Beach Grove waterfront setback, below), while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. What is not contested: zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps, and the Beach Grove waterfront zone sheet RD2 states a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum for waterfront lots in Beach Grove. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Waterfront setback:** Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting on Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** OCP Bylaw No. 8400, Appendix B — no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Beach Grove lot falls inside SD3 needs a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dike and flood system:** the Beach Grove shore faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **SSMUH:** Delta Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all Section 2 buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Beach Grove itself is named in the research file — the Tsawwassen presale frame in this batch sits at Southlands (Boundary Beach file) and on Tsawwassen Lands (townhomes file); the regional example named in this program is Bridge & Elliott in Ladner. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 2, 2026. Property tax or levy notes specific to Beach Grove: UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
