## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the fee-simple acreages and pad-rent manufactured homes that make up this corridor's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Park age rules in a manufactured-home park are a park-tenancy matter under the park's own documents, not a strata bylaw — do not conflate the two. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Planning and zoning (local):** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern this Electoral Area G ground — the specific bylaw numbers and zone names for Dewdney and Deroche lots are UNVERIFIED in this pass, and City of Mission plans (including Mission's OCP) do NOT apply. Confirm a parcel's designation and zone with the FVRD for the PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **Rural property taxes:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, rural property taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. This is the regime that applies in Dewdney-Deroche — not Mission's flat 10% and not Chilliwack's 5% + 5% by August. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA):** for the pad-rent parks excluded in Section 2, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rent-increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — the cap that governs a pad tenant's annual increase, confirmed from the park's own tenancy documents for any specific pad. (Program brief, MHPTA pad-rent limits.)
- **ALR housing rules:** parcel-level ALR status is per address: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping. Where land is in the ALR: generally one principal residence capped at 500 m² total floor area, plus at most one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels; and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no project in this area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies). Manufactured homes on rented pads commonly face different lender treatment than fee-simple homes — confirm financing for the specific tenure before writing. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing address is the trap.** Every card for this ground says "Mission," the page title says "Mission BC," and neither Dewdney nor Deroche is in the City of Mission — zoning, permits, servicing and taxes run through the FVRD (Electoral Area G) and the Province. Applying Mission bylaws or Mission's tax dates to a purchase here is the standard error. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **The school district and the government are different maps.** SD75 (Mission) serves these FVRD communities and the FVRD's election notice puts Deroche voters in the SD75 rural trustee election — a family can be in "Mission" for school and in no municipality at all for everything else. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **Two of this corridor's park names exist only in listing text.** Oasis Country Estates and Nicomen Village were not confirmed on any park-manager or developer source in the research pass — pad rents, park rules and approval requirements quoted from a listing card are the seller's text, not the park's documents. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **The tax calendar here is the rural one.** Due July 2, 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31, paid to the Province — a different second-stage date than Chilliwack's August 4 and a different structure than Mission's flat 10%, on properties whose mailing addresses name both of those cities' regions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/dewdney-deroche.md)
- **Freshet alerts have named these exact streets.** FVRD freshet evacuation alerts for Area G have covered Nicomen Island and the Taylor Road area in Deroche, and Dewdney Regional Park has appeared among flood-related park closures — flood diligence here is historical fact, not a formality.
