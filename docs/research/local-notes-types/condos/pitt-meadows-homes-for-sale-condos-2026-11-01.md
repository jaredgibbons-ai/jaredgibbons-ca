# Pitt Meadows — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- This file is city-wide: Pitt Meadows' board geography splits the city into five sub-areas (Central, Mid, North, South and West Meadows), and the condo stock sits mainly in two of them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the Harris Road corridor in Central and Mid Meadows — Solaris at Meadows Gate, Origin and Liv42 all stand on Harris Road — with the town-centre/Meadowtown area (Harris Road at Lougheed Highway) as the densest node. Apartment form is permitted in the R-4/R-6 and multi-family zones around Harris Road/Lougheed and Meadowtown under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below; zoning per the research file.
- Attached volume by sub-area in September 2026 (SnapStats GVR): Central Meadows 32 active attached listings and Mid Meadows 12; city-wide, 66 active attached listings, 14 attached sales and a community attached median of $662,500. SnapStats publishes no building-level or sub-area medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- Transit anchors: Pitt Meadows Station on the West Coast Express and the R3 RapidBus stop on Harris Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most for Pitt Meadows' 2008–2010 Harris Road stock (Origin, 12350 Harris Road, Solaris), now in its first mandatory reporting cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are prohibited in Pitt Meadows.** A City staff report states: "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — so an STR bylaw question never arises here; the use itself is banned city-wide. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list for short-term rentals. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Pitt Meadows condo building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs, under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Floodplain is the city's defining land rule:** the City's 2020 Business Plan puts over 95% of Pitt Meadows in the floodplain (and 78% in the Agricultural Land Reserve), behind a 60 km dike system; Floodplain Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 sets flood construction requirements. An earlier OCP-era figure of roughly 86% floodplain also circulates — the 2020 Business Plan's "over 95%" is the current City figure to use. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after August 1, 2026; the 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 carries a separate drainage levy on top of the general levy — a line item detached-house owners and condo owners alike pay in this diked city. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, floodplain location can affect a lender's and insurer's view of a specific property — no source checked publishes a Pitt Meadows building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Pitt Meadows condo?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the City's Zoning Bylaw at all, and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. Long-term rental of a strata unit is a separate question — strata rental bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Is Solaris really the city's only concrete tower?** Current listings describe Solaris at Meadows Gate (12069 Harris Road, built 2010, 144 units per the building directory) as "Pitt Meadows' only concrete high-rise" / "only concrete and steel building." That is listing language; the verified facts from the directory are the year, the unit count and the restriction set (pets not allowed, rentals allowed). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Harris Road buildings?** It differs by building and the public record is thin: the directory records Solaris as pets not allowed; Origin listings show cats OK with one stating "1 dog or 2 cats permitted"; an Orion listing calls that building pet-friendly. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws on the Form B govern — for Liv42 and Cedar Downs the position is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road and https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/11950-harris-road/201
- **Q: How much of the city is floodplain, and what does that mean for a condo?** The City's 2020 Business Plan puts over 95% of Pitt Meadows in the floodplain, protected by a 60 km dike system, with flood construction rules in Floodplain Bylaw No. 2384, 2008. Practically: ask about the building's flood construction level, the strata's insurance (including flood/dike-breach coverage and deductibles), and expect the separate drainage levy on the tax bill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Why is there a drainage levy on my tax bill?** The 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 carries a separate drainage levy — the cost of running the diking/drainage system in a city that is over 95% floodplain. It is a City charge, not a strata fee. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: What shape is the 2008–2010 stock in?** Origin (2008) shows a new roof and gutters in 2023 in one listing — exactly the kind of item to verify in the depreciation report, which every 5+ lot strata must now renew every five years. Solaris (2010) and 12350 Harris Road (2008) are in the same cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is Liv42 a 42-unit building?** UNCONFIRMED. No source checked publishes Liv42's unit count; the number in the name is branding, and repeating it as a unit count would be inventing a fact. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3108014-205-12409-harris-road-pitt-meadows-british-columbia-v3y0e7-554900/brochure
- **Q: What is the commute from the Harris Road condos?** Pitt Meadows Station (West Coast Express) and the R3 RapidBus stop on Harris Road serve the corridor, and the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project (including a Harris Road rail underpass) is the City's major rail-crossing project. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Does the airport affect the south-city condos?** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) sits in the south city; the Harris Road condo corridor is in Central/Mid Meadows, away from the airport lands. No source checked publishes building-specific noise or height-restriction findings for the Section 2 buildings — UNCONFIRMED, and worth a site visit for upper-floor Solaris units facing south. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the exemptions here?** Yes — the programs are provincial/federal, not city-specific: BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000, and the federal HBP ($60,000) and FHSA ($40,000 lifetime) stack on top. Pitt Meadows' resale condos are not new construction, so the GST rebate and Newly Built Home Exemption generally do not apply to them. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/ and https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number changed between City documents.** The City's 2020 Business Plan says over 95% of Pitt Meadows is floodplain; an earlier OCP-era figure of roughly 86% still circulates in secondary sources. Use "over 95%" and cite the Business Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **A 1,215 m² figure in City material is a subdivision minimum, not a development minimum.** The City's SSMUH update materials use 1,215 m² as a minimum lot size for subdivision in the residential zones — it is not a minimum site size for building multi-family housing, and quoting it as one misstates the rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **The North Lougheed corridor has a new area plan.** The North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026 — the corridor north of Lougheed Highway is the city's next designated growth area, which reframes how today's Central Meadows condos sit in the long-term density map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Solaris carries two civic addresses.** The directory address is 12069 Harris Road, but part of the complex is addressed 12079 Harris Road — when ordering strata documents or checking a tax record, confirm which address the specific unit carries. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **Meadowland Village's size is genuinely unsettled in public sources** — "just 26 homes" in a listing versus a "20 Unit Development" directory header. For an 8-home complex like Willow Creek (self-managed) or a ~20-unit complex, the strata may also be small enough that volunteer-council capacity, not just the CRF, is the real diligence item. https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7 and http://bchb.ca/portfolios/1-19236-119-avenue-pitt-meadows/
- **78% of the city is farmland.** The 2020 Business Plan puts 78% of Pitt Meadows in the Agricultural Land Reserve — which is why the condo stock is compressed onto the Harris Road corridor and why new supply is mostly infill and corridor density rather than new subdivisions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale.md)
