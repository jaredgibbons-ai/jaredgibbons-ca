## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Cottonwood's attached stock is townhouse strata, but the same Strata Property Act rules apply to a townhouse strata lot as to an apartment condo lot.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This applies to every Cottonwood townhome complex above. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any Cottonwood complex whose old bylaws or directory entry says rentals are banned is stating outdated law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Cottonwood complex is published as age-restricted in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research file (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in Cottonwood?** None verified. The research file and the directory/listing sources checked for these notes confirm zero named apartment-form condo buildings in Cottonwood MR; the attached stock that does exist is townhome complexes (Section 2). The SnapStats "Condos & Townhomes" count of 34 active listings (September 2026) blends both forms and is not evidence of apartment buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Q: Do strata rules apply to a townhome the same way as a condo?** Yes — a townhouse strata lot is governed by the same Strata Property Act framework: Form B on resale, mandatory depreciation reports for stratas of 5+ lots, the 10% minimum CRF contribution, and the same bylaw rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I rent out a Cottonwood townhome?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in these complexes?** Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws). The only Cottonwood complex with a published pet position in the sources checked is Carriage Lane — a directory listing header records pets allowed (building directory, secondary source). For every other complex the position is UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3172915-28-11358-cottonwood-drive-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-5v5
- **Q: How old is the stock, and what does that mean for levies?** The verified complexes span 1996 (Hollyhock) to 2020 (Willow & Oak), with The Meadows at Verigin's Ridge's year in conflict between sources (2008 vs 2012). For the 1990s–2000s complexes, the depreciation report is the document that shows upcoming roof, road and envelope work — read it before writing. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3090592-23560-119-avenue-28-maple-ridge-bc-v4r-2p5/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve Cottonwood addresses?** The two confirmed anchors are Kanaka Creek Elementary (11120 234A Street) and Alexander Robinson Elementary (11849 238B Street); catchments are address-based, so run the specific unit address through the SD42 locator before relying on either. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Q: What is the transit picture?** BC Transit route 745 runs Haney Place/Cottonwood — this is a bus-only area with no rapid transit or rail station; Haney Place Exchange in the town centre is the connection point. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Q: Does a Cottonwood purchase attract GST?** Only if the home is new construction. The verified complexes are resale stock (newest verified build year 2020), so a typical Cottonwood townhome purchase is a resale with no GST; PTT still applies, with the First-Time Home Buyers' Program removing PTT on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Q: Is "Cottonwood" an official neighbourhood?** No — it is a board sub-area name. The City of Maple Ridge publishes no Cottonwood neighbourhood boundary or area plan, so two listing feeds can draw the edge differently; Cottonwood Drive addresses west of the Kanaka Creek core are classified Cottonwood MR in listing feeds. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The headline attached numbers overstate the condo supply.** SnapStats counted 34 active "Condos & Townhomes" listings in Cottonwood MR in September 2026, yet no apartment building could be verified here at all — a buyer searching "Cottonwood condos" is really shopping townhome complexes, with strata fees, parking and yard obligations that follow townhouse form. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **The area's name comes from a street, not a plan.** Cottonwood Drive carries several of the verified complexes (Verigin's Ridge, Carriage Lane, Cottonwood Green), and the board applies the Cottonwood MR label along that corridor even west of Kanaka Creek — check the sub-area classification on the listing itself rather than assuming a City boundary. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **One complex, two published build years.** The Meadows at Verigin's Ridge is recorded as built 2012 in one listing record and 2008 in a Zolo property record; the MLS data sheet figure of 71 units in the development is the more solid published fact. The strata plan and Form B settle the year. https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/11282-cottonwood-drive/43
- **Maple Ridge's OCP citation is unsettled in current sources.** The research file found the Official Community Plan cited as both Bylaw 7060-2014 and Bylaw 7998-2024, with an OCP update adopted November 25, 2025 — quote the bylaw number only from a current City document for the specific property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **The Fraserview Village 55+ buildings are not here.** Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount — the east Maple Ridge seniors' condo cluster a buyer may be shown under a broad "east Maple Ridge" search — are in the East Central sub-area and are documented in the East Central condos file, not this one.
