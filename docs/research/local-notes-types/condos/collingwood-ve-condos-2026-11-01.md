# Renfrew-Collingwood (Collingwood VE) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Naming note, stated at the top:** the City of Vancouver's official local area is **Renfrew-Collingwood**, one of the City's 22 areas. "Collingwood VE" is the board-style short name for the Collingwood portion of that area around Kingsway, Joyce Street and Joyce-Collingwood Station at the Burnaby border; City plans and station work all use Renfrew-Collingwood. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The Collingwood portion runs along the city's east boundary with Burnaby (Boundary Road), with the Collingwood stretch of Kingsway and the Joyce-Collingwood Station area inside it; a Collingwood-only boundary set is not published by the City because Collingwood is not a separate local area — boundary roads for the portion on its own: UNVERIFIED. Nearby areas: Kensington-Cedar Cottage west, Killarney south, Victoria-Fraserview south-west, Burnaby across Boundary Road east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: in two clusters — the Joyce-Collingwood Station / Collingwood Village cluster on Vanness Avenue, Joyce Street and Oben Street (Joyce by Westbank, Circa, Brio, Urba, Aura), and the Kingsway corridor (Wall Centre Central Park at Boundary, Frame at Earles, Clive and Clarendon Heights off Kingsway). The interior streets are primarily single-family homes: the City's council summary of the Renfrew-Collingwood Vision states most of the community is single-family homes, with higher-density townhouses and mid-rise apartments clustered around the Joyce SkyTrain station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- Main roads: Kingsway, Joyce Street, Renfrew Street, Rupert Street, Nanaimo Street, Boundary Road, and Grandview Highway across the north side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- Transit: Joyce-Collingwood Station (Expo Line) and its bus exchange sit inside the area; the wider Renfrew-Collingwood area also reaches Nanaimo and 29th Avenue Stations (Expo Line) and Renfrew and Rupert Stations (Millennium Line). TransLink route 41 runs to Joyce Station via Joyce Street and Vanness Avenue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in the big station towers (Joyce, Wall Centre Central Park) that is where parkade, envelope and amenity obligations surface. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This applies directly to the published lines in Section 2 — Circa's "pet/rental friendly" description and Wall Centre Central Park's "rentals allowed" line are consistent with the law, and any older directory line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed would be void to that extent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** zoning is Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), and the C-2 District Schedule applies on arterial commercial streets such as Kingsway. The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan guides change in the station precinct, the Norquay Village Neighbourhood Centre Plan covers the Kingsway centre, and the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan (approved by Council in July 2025) covers the Millennium Line station areas at the area's north side — all under the Renfrew-Collingwood Community Vision. Parcel-level zone and plan coverage: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **District energy charges:** no source checked states a district-energy utility charge for a Section 2 building — applicability and amount: UNCONFIRMED; check the Form B for any separate utility billing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to first resales at Frame (2026) and Clive. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales at Frame. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is the building at Joyce Station a condo or a rental?** Both exist, on opposite sides of the street: Joyce by Westbank at 5058 Joyce Street is a 256-unit Freehold Strata tower (EPS7496, finished 2021); Joyce 2 at 5083 Joyce Street is a 360-unit purpose-built rental tower. Confirm the civic address before anything else. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/building/joyce-EPS7496 and https://www.mpamag.com/ca/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/westbanks-joyce-tower-placed-in-receivership/574795
- **Q: Can I rent out a Collingwood Village condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and the published lines for this area's buildings (Circa described as rental-friendly; Wall Centre Central Park "rentals allowed") are consistent with that. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration still applies if the unit sits empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Wall Centre Central Park — pets allowed per the building directory; Joyce by Westbank — pets allowed with restrictions per listings; Circa — described as pet-friendly. For the other Section 2 buildings, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/wall-centre-central-park-south-tower-1-5665-boundary-road-north
- **Q: What is Collingwood Village?** The transit-oriented development around Joyce-Collingwood Station on Vanness Avenue and Oben Street: a neighbourhood source states it was completed in 2006 with 16 condo and rental buildings. Because it mixes tenures, a "Collingwood Village" address is not by itself proof a building is strata — check the strata plan on title. https://faithwilson.com/neighbourhoods/collingwood/
- **Q: How big is Wall Centre Central Park, really?** The community is published at 1,060 homes (condos and townhouses) under Strata Plan EPS3434; the 5665 Boundary Road tower alone is recorded with 623 units in strata over 36 floors. Amenities, CRF and levy exposure are community-scale — read the depreciation report for the whole plan, not just one tower. https://vadapm.com/building-data/wall-centre-central-park-tower-1-5665-boundary-road
- **Q: Which schools serve a station-area address?** SD39 catchments are address-based. Schools located in the wider Renfrew-Collingwood area include Nootka Elementary (3375 Nootka Street), John Norquay Elementary (4710 Slocan Street), Renfrew Elementary (3315 East 22nd Avenue) and Windermere Secondary (3155 East 27th Avenue). Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Q: What plans are changing this area?** The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan around the station, the Norquay Village Neighbourhood Centre Plan on Kingsway, and the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan approved in July 2025 at the north side. Frame at Kingsway and Earles is the built example of the Norquay Village direction. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Q: Is flood risk an issue here?** Still Creek — one of the few remaining salmon-bearing streams in Vancouver — runs through Renfrew Ravine Park, and its floodplain is part of the City's designated floodplain dataset. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED; ravine-edge purchases need the parcel check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Clive's and Frame's completion years?** Clive: a current listing records year built 2025 while the presale source records completion in 2023. Frame: listings record 2026 while the presale source estimated 2025. Both conflicts are flagged in Section 2, not resolved — the strata plan registration and the Form B settle a building's dates. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/clive-at-collingwood-by-nexst-properties/ and https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/frame-vancouver/
- **Q: Is there a median condo price for Renfrew-Collingwood I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used, per the research program's rule. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two Joyce towers, two tenures, one station.** The strata Joyce (5058 Joyce Street, 2021) and the rental Joyce 2 (5083 Joyce Street) face each other across Joyce Street; the rental tower was placed in receivership by court order granted April 27, 2026, with leasing reported as continuing — the distinction matters for anyone comping "Joyce" units. https://www.mpamag.com/ca/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/westbanks-joyce-tower-placed-in-receivership/574795
- **The area's official name is not the board name.** City plans, the station precinct work and the community vision all say Renfrew-Collingwood; "Collingwood VE" is the board short form. A search run under only one name misses stock filed under the other. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Frame is a cinema-district neighbour with a paper trail in two names.** The same development is addressed as both 5189 Earles Street and 2727 Kingsway, and its developers are Coromandel Properties and Peterson Group (the research file's earlier pass named Peterson Group alone). https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/frame-vancouver/
- **The lakes are gone but the creek is not.** The City states the area once had significant wildlife and lakes that no longer exist, including Moody Lake where the SkyTrain line is today — while Still Creek still runs salmon-bearing through Renfrew Ravine Park (5.89 hectares at 3900 Renfrew Street). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
- **Libraries anchor both ends.** Collingwood Branch of Vancouver Public Library is at 2985 Kingsway and Renfrew Branch at 2969 East 22nd Avenue — the two civic anchors bracketing the condo clusters. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/collingwood-ve.md)
