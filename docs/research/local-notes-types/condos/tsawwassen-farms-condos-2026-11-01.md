## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Topic scope: farm land, working farms and agricultural land in and around Tsawwassen — principally the farm land on the Tsawwassen edges (the Southlands side and the farm belt between Tsawwassen and Ladner), which the Delta Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024) designates Agriculture (A), the designation whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands (OCP s. 1.5.1, Map 2). The urban Tsawwassen neighbourhoods themselves are residential; the farm topic is the agricultural fringe. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Tsawwassen is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), planned under OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — the current OCP consolidated the former area plans (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950) into a single Land Use Plan, and its Agriculture policies (s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan) apply to Delta's agricultural land. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Treaty-land boundary: Tsawwassen First Nation Lands sit on Tsawwassen's western edge; Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Final Agreement — so City of Delta zoning and Delta farm parcels do not extend onto Tsawwassen Lands. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- There is no single MLS sub-area for this topic; Tsawwassen farm parcels sit at the edges of the board's Tsawwassen sub-areas and in the Ladner-side farm belt (East Delta / Ladner Rural ground), and a parcel's sub-area label is a listing question, not a planning fact. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)
- Southlands: the City records the Southlands lands as the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390; developer marketing descriptions of its farmland, forest and trail areas are UNVERIFIED on an official source. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere in this topic — the nearest strata condo stock is in urban Tsawwassen (see the Tsawwassen Central file), outside the farm fringe this file covers.

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**There is no condo building list for this topic. Apartment-condo count = 0 by design.**

- The ALR/strata position, as the research states it: farm parcels here are overwhelmingly freehold, and strata farm product is not a Delta pattern — no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The strata rules are stated first for completeness — they govern Tsawwassen's urban condos, not farm parcels — followed by the ALR and Delta farm rules that actually govern this topic.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate (fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). It has no application to a freehold farm parcel — there is no strata to issue one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF since November 1, 2023 — strata-only rules, not farm rules. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws:** BC strata rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, and the only lawful age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023) — again strata-only; a farm tenancy or farm-worker housing question is an ALC and tenancy question, not a strata-bylaw question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1 purpose: maintain agricultural lands and promote a thriving agricultural sector; s. 2.6.1 recognizes farming as the primary use of agricultural land). A parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **ALR housing:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, and Delta bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta farm zoning:** in the A1 zone (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600), one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); subdivision in A1 (s. 10.10.8) requires OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with an 8 ha minimum lot size created (home site severance exception under ALC policy); A2 sets the same 8 ha minimum and A3 does not permit subdivision. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (adopted June 24, 2024) govern single-detached and duplex lots, not ALR farm parcels. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Farm classification:** BC Assessment requires a producing farm to meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **GST / PTT and buyer programs (for completeness, on a farm purchase):** GST treatment of farm land is transaction-specific (farm-use and going-concern questions) — no general farm GST rate is stated here: UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel. General PTT is 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; the First-Time Home Buyers' Program ($500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000) are residential programs whose fit to a farm purchase is parcel- and use-specific. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (a residential insured-mortgage table, background only here). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) and the GST New Housing Rebate (36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000) are new-housing rebates, not farm-land rebates. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** the 7-day rescission rule applies to developer-marketed strata and subdivision product; no presale project is documented for this farm topic: UNCONFIRMED / none. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Water and septic:** GWELLS is the registry for recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood and hazard DPA SD3:** Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure); on the Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands, hazard DPA SD3 provides a 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback; properties within 30 m of a stream, including farm ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA. **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there real farmland in Tsawwassen, or is it all residential?** Both — the OCP designates farm land on the Tsawwassen edges Agriculture (A) (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), ALR rules apply where land is in the ALR, and the urban neighbourhoods are residential. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a farm "condo" or stratify a farm property here?** No condo product exists for this topic (count 0 by design), and ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6, treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the ALR — stratification is not an ordinary route for ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Tsawwassen?** A principal residence built without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a farm have a second dwelling?** One additional residence without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Delta bylaws must also permit it; the A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A3 zoning does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How do I check whether a parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does Delta zoning apply on Tsawwassen Lands?** No — Tsawwassen First Nation has jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement); establish which side of the treaty-land boundary a parcel sits on before applying Delta or ALR farm rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: What should I check on a Southlands-side parcel?** Hazard DPA SD3 (2.9 m floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback), the Streamside DPA if any ditch or slough is within 30 m, the well record in GWELLS, and any water licence position if the operation irrigates. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Farm ditches count as streams for the setback check.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream — including sloughs and ditches — fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, so the rule reaches working farm land, not just urban lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **The farm ground here is a fringe, not a belt of its own.** It is the southern end of Delta's agricultural land, designated Agriculture (A) with the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) over it and ALC rules layered on where a parcel is in the ALR — pricing and diligence follow the Delta belt, not a separate Tsawwassen farm market. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The dike work is live and local.** The City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding — part of the flood-protection system (over 63 km of infrastructure, 31 pump stations) the Southlands-side parcels rely on. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **No soil class is published for a specific parcel in this file.** Agricultural capability is mapped per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder, and the ALC defines the classes from Class 1 (no or very slight limitations) down — any specific parcel's class here is UNVERIFIED until that map is read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/
- **There are no farm market figures to quote.** SnapStats publishes no farm category and does not cover South Delta at all (its Delta coverage is North Delta only) — no SnapStats figures are cited in this file, and farm sale counts, price per acre and farm days-on-market are UNVERIFIED.
