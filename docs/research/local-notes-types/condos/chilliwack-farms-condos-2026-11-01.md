# Chilliwack Farms — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Structural position — read first.** There is no condo building list for farmland, and there cannot be one from ALR land: ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. The research file states the same position from the farm side: farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold, strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern, and no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. This file therefore documents zero apartment condos and explains the rules that produce that zero. Chilliwack is CADREB territory and is NOT covered by SnapStats — no market numbers are stated anywhere in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero apartment-condo stock — stated plainly at the top of this section.** No condo building is named in this file, because the topic is farmland and no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source in the research. That zero is structural, not a gap in the search: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR), so a farm parcel's houses cannot be sold as strata condos while the land is in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)

- What the topic means for condo stock: a buyer searching "Chilliwack farms" for a condo-form purchase is searching the wrong tenure. The farm product here is freehold parcels with a principal residence (and, where the rules allow, one additional residence) — ownership of the whole parcel, not a strata lot in a building. The verified Chilliwack apartment-condo roster (The Vibe, Newmark, Mountain View Estates, Mountainview Lane, The York Residences, Garrison Central, The Boardwalk, Creekside Estates) sits on urban land in Chilliwack Proper, Garrison Crossing and the Evans Road corridor, and is documented in the city condo notes — none of it is farm product and none of it is counted here. - Townhouse complexes: none belong to this topic — no townhome complex on Chilliwack farmland was confirmed on an official, developer or strata source in the research, and a townhouse complex is urban product documented in the sub-area files, not farm product. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The stratification bar (the topic's defining rule):** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"), issued October 18, 2022, gives the ALC's interpretation of subdivision in the ALR under the Agricultural Land Commission Act and the ALR regulations — a strata plan may not be approved or registered unless permitted under that regime, and the program-wide verified position is that ALR residences cannot be stratified. A Chilliwack farm's residences therefore cannot be converted into strata condos while the land remains in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR housing rules:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. One additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Chilliwack bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR administration in Chilliwack:** properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications; the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. A specific parcel's reserve status is also checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Chilliwack plan and zoning:** Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500 — the Agricultural Area Plan is Schedule B1 of that bylaw (created as part of the OCP in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission; the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee monitors its implementation), and the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 (never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024"). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: Section 6 sets the AL, AU, AS, AR, AC and AFP agricultural zones (ss. 6.01–6.06) and Section 7 the rural zones including R (Rural) and RH (Rural Hillside); Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone (s. 12.02). Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024 — it governs single-detached and duplex lots, not ALR farm parcels. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Minimum lot areas inside Section 6 (newly created lots):** AL and AU — 7.5 ha where land is excluded or exempted from the ALR or a subdivision is approved within the ALR, 1 ha by boundary adjustment to allow the more efficient use of agricultural land, and 0.2 ha by homesite severance or an ALC-approved boundary adjustment; AS — 2 ha where a subdivision is approved in the ALR, 1 ha by boundary adjustment, 0.2 ha by ALC-approved boundary adjustment or homesite severance; AR — 0.2 ha where served by a community water system, 1 ha otherwise; AC — 2 ha, 1 ha, 0.2 ha on the same pattern; AFP — 0.4 ha for all uses. Minimum lot width is 25 m in all six zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Farm Home Plate:** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate is capped, as maximum lot coverage, at 4,000 m² for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use, with siting limits measured from the front/exterior side and rear lot lines; the requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha. The OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. The AU, AS and AFP sections state no Farm Home Plate figure. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Farm classification:** BC Assessment states a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if less than 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A buyer who cannot meet the threshold for the operation's size risks the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water and septic:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of water fees and rentals before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. Fraser Health states a home or building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026 the City published a 5% late payment penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. Additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Form B (for the urban condo stock this topic is not):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. It has no application to a freehold farm parcel, which is the point: there is no strata corporation on ALR farm land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata stock generally):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither rule creates strata stock on farmland. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; on a farm parcel there is no strata bylaw at all — tenancy and farmworker-housing questions are lease and bylaw questions, not strata questions. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — again, strata law, with no farm-parcel application. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on Chilliwack farmland was confirmed on a developer's own site in the research; where any developer-contract purchase does arise in Chilliwack, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Chilliwack farm?** No — not as strata. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6: strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR), the research confirms strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern and no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source, and farm parcels here are overwhelmingly freehold. The condo-form purchase in Chilliwack is an urban apartment from the city roster, documented in the city condo notes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)
- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is farmland?** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its Official Community Plan carries an Agricultural Area Plan for that land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: What zones cover Chilliwack farms?** Section 6 of Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets six agricultural zones: AL (Agricultural Lowland), AU (Agricultural Upland), AS (Agricultural Small Lot), AR (Agricultural Residential), AC (Agricultural Commercial) and AFP (Agricultural Food Processing) — a small-lot (AS) purchase and a lowland (AL) purchase are different products with different subdivision and use rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Chilliwack?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; Chilliwack also applies its Farm Home Plate rules (Development Permit Area 4) to residential building on agricultural land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can a Chilliwack farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; the City lists a new house, coach house or suite among the items that may need an ALC application. A secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel into smaller lots?** Only inside narrow rules: in the AL zone, for example, 7.5 ha is the minimum for ALR-excluded/exempted land or an ALC-approved subdivision, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance — and subdivision inside the ALR needs ALC approval. Minimum lot width is 25 m in all six agricultural zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: What keeps a farm's tax classification?** Meeting BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices for the operation's size — $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that, $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with the farm classification application submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Do I need a water licence for the farm's well?** For irrigation, yes — non-domestic groundwater use requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act, with water fees and rentals, before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers in this file?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market. The SnapStats reports held for this program cover the Fraser Valley and Greater Vancouver boards only, publish no farm category, and contain no Chilliwack figures — so any Chilliwack or farm-specific statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve farm families?** The rural Chilliwack schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 schools file held in this program include Greendale Elementary (6621 Sumas Prairie Rd, Chilliwack), Yarrow Community School (4605 Wilson Rd, Chilliwack), East Chilliwack Elementary (49190 Chilliwack Central Rd, Chilliwack) and Rosedale Traditional Community (50850 Yale Rd, Rosedale). Address-level catchments: UNVERIFIED — no catchment locator result was run for any farm address in the research. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The ban on farm condos is a subdivision rule, not a housing rule.** ALC Bulletin 09 is titled "Subdivision and Plans That Cause Subdivision in the ALR" (issued October 18, 2022), and s. 6 places strata plans in that category — which is why the ALR residence caps (500 m² principal; 90 m² / 186 m² additional) describe whole freehold houses, never strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The Farm Home Plate has an escape hatch under 0.8 ha.** The Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha in area — small farm-residential parcels sit outside the plate caps that govern larger AL, AR and AC lots. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **There is a flood-hazard zone inside the farm bylaw structure.** Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone (s. 12.02). Parcel-level Flood Construction Levels under the City's floodplain regulation: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Soil class claims on farm listings are site copy here.** No agricultural capability class for Chilliwack's farm districts was confirmed from a text source in the research — the live page's "Class 1 to Class 3" wording is site copy, not an official source, and is not relied on in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)
- **The Agricultural Area Plan has its own watchdog.** The AAP was created as part of the Official Community Plan in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission, and the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee (ARAC) monitors its implementation through reviewing agricultural development applications — a committee most acreage buyers never hear of until an application goes to it. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Do not import Fraser Valley numbers into a Chilliwack deal.** Chilliwack trades on CADREB and this program holds no CADREB statistics — pricing support has to come from the parcel's own comparables, not from a neighbouring board's medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md)
