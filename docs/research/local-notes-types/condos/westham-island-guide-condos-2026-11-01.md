## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase of a strata lot the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On Westham Island this document will normally never arise: there is no strata stock, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified (below) — island diligence runs on title, the ALC position and City records instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **No stratification in the ALR (standing rule):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). ALC residence caps on ALR land: a principal residence is limited to 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; and one additional residence may be permitted at up to 90 m² total floor area on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules also applying. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/westham-island-guide.md)
- **BC Assessment farm class (standing figures):** farm-class thresholds are $2,500 of farm income for land of 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha, and $10,000 for land under 0.8 ha, with applications by October 31 — the class test a small island acreage purchase has to plan around; verify the current-year figures with BC Assessment for a specific property before relying on them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/westham-island-guide.md)
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Stated for completeness of the BC condo block: no Westham Island property in this file is a strata, so neither document exists for an island purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any strata bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. On the island the operative rental limits are not strata bylaws at all: they are the ALC residence rules above (a suite only inside the principal residence) and Delta's bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No strata, and therefore no age-restriction bylaw, is recorded for any Westham Island property in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (1985), and is never attributed to Bylaw 8400; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), and the designation list does NOT include "Single Family Residential". Delta's SSMUH zoning allows up to 4 units on single-detached and duplex lots — on ALR land the ALC residence rules above govern first, and Delta's page does not override them. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/westham-island-guide.md)
