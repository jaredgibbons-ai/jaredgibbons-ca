## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The plan stack, as verified:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — "Chilliwack 2050" — presenting a long-term vision and growth strategy covering land use/transportation/utility, economic development, environmental protection/enhancement and social/cultural initiatives, with growth strategies for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses and protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve. The live page states the OCP was "adopted December 16, 2025"; the City's OCP page read states no adoption date, so that date is UNVERIFIED here and is recorded as the page's own statement only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md) and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Neighbourhood-plan citations, corrected:** the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan is Schedule B5 of Bylaw 5500 (not "Schedule D / Bylaw No. 5024" — no source opened confirms what Bylaw 5024 was); the Agricultural Area Plan is Schedule B1; the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan is Schedule B4. Live pages citing "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" for Sardis are stale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md) and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning frame:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets what can be built as-of-right today — the same OCP-vs-zoning distinction the live page is built on. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md)
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395 (Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000), adopted June 18, 2024. Within that frame a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling (s.2), only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot (s.4.06(1)), garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area, and ADU parking is set at s.5.04. SSMUH product is generally not strata condo product. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md)
- **Development Permit Areas:** the live page's "Development Permit Area 2 (DPA2 Hillside & Upland)" matches neither D-2 Hillside Environment nor D-5 Hillside Development as named in Schedule D, and its claim that DPA2 requires geotechnical, stormwater and wildfire-interface engineering before subdivision, land clearing or building is UNVERIFIED as stated — the DPA guideline documents themselves were not opened in this pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md) and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **ALR frame:** the live page states twice that approximately 67% of Chilliwack is inside the Agricultural Land Reserve — UNVERIFIED as stated, because no City or ALC source opened states that percentage. What is established: inside the ALR, one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (parcels over 40 ha); subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require Agricultural Land Commission approval; and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — which is why no ALR parcel is condo stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Floodplain:** the live page cites "Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519" and states new habitable floor space in designated floodplains must be built at or above the specified Flood Construction Level and set back from dikes and watercourses. No City floodplain bylaw or FCL table was opened in this pass: that bylaw number and those FCL mechanics are UNVERIFIED as stated — confirm the parcel's Flood Construction Level with the City before subjects come off on valley-floor land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — stated because this series carries the standard block in every file, and because it is the document that governs any condo purchase inside a plan area: on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A plan designation is not a strata document — it never substitutes for the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/chilliwacklanduseplans.md) and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 — the March 12 secondary date is superseded) — 100% of GST to a $1M price, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1.5M, agreements signed March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where new product inside a plan area is sold on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
