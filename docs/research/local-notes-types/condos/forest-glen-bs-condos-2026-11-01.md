## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Forest Glen is the Burnaby South sub-area between Metrotown and Edmonds, framed by **Kingsway, Willingdon Avenue, Bond Street and Nelson Avenue**, mixing the Kingsway condo corridor with detached residential streets. It is named for Forest Glen Park at 4855 Bond Street. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: two pockets. The **Hazel Street tower cluster** at the Metrotown edge (Section 2), and the older **Nelson Avenue / McMurray Avenue / Sanders Street** low-rise and tower stock toward Burnaby South Secondary. The streets between are detached houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- Schools: Clinton Elementary (5858 Clinton Street), École Inman (3963 Brandon Street), Nelson Elementary (4850 Irmin Street), École Marlborough (6060 Marlborough Avenue) and Burnaby South Secondary (5455 Rumble Street). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- Transit: route 110 runs on Bond Street and Nelson Avenue themselves (Metrotown–Lougheed via Willingdon, Deer Lake Parkway, Winston and Government) — the sub-area's own streets carry the bus, not just its edges. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- Anchors: Wesburn Community Centre at 4781 Parkwood Avenue; Burnaby Hospital sits immediately north-east of the sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- Planning frame: the Cascade Heights Community Plan was adopted in March 2025. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Forest Glen's 1984–2001 towers this is the central diligence document — and it is also the only reliable route past the conflicting published details flagged in Section 2 (the Dynasty's year, La Mirage's pet position). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No Forest Glen building in Section 2 publishes a "rentals not allowed" line; where listing copy advertises the opposite — Oakwood's "rental-friendly" and Deer Lake Breeze's "no rental restrictions" — that is current law everywhere in BC, not a building feature. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Burnaby's current zoning bylaw is the **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026). The 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 is repealed — any source still citing No. 4742 is superseded. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP, and the Cascade Heights Community Plan was adopted in March 2025. Under the R1 SSMUH rules in place since June 2024, a residential lot on the sub-area's detached streets can hold 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after September; Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/forest-glen-bs.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 stock is resale, including the 2022 Sussex. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant only to new construction, not this resale stock. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Note the Section 2 exclusion: share-sale tenure (Irving Apartments) sits outside standard condo financing entirely — its listing requires all cash or 35% down through a specific credit union. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

For records, strata documents and comparable searches, use the MLS classification — and check both names. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4711-hazel-street/3608
- **Q: What is the newest building, and the oldest?** Newest: Sussex (4711 Hazel Street), Townline, built 2022, 41 storeys and 321 suites. Oldest verified: Sanders Court (5025 Sanders Street), built 1984, fully rainscreened in 2007 with a 2018 roof. The 38-year spread is the whole diligence story in this sub-area — warranty-era questions at one end, envelope-cycle questions at the other. https://www.apartments.com/building/sussex-burnaby-bc/ldgwxpd/ and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/5025-sanders-street/306
- **Q: Can I have a dog in the Hazel towers?** Published positions vary and are bylaw questions, not law: current unit records show pets "Yes With" restrictions at Sussex and The Sovereign, "Cats OK" at The Newmark, and "No" at The Dynasty, Crystal Place and The Evergreen. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC — the registered bylaws on the Form B govern the specific building. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4888-hazel-street/1005
- **Q: La Mirage has two addresses — is it one strata?** It is treated here as one complex with two towers: 6070 McMurray Avenue (built 1987) and 6055 Nelson Avenue (built 1988, "La Mirage II").
