## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR stratification bar — leads this section, because feed cards raise it:** ALR residences cannot be stratified. Depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; see also Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **ALC residence caps (for parcels in the ALR):** one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, not more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **BC Assessment farm-class thresholds (for farmed parcels):** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **Township of Langley SSMUH — the correct rule:** Zoning Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **no 6-plex tier exists in it**. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the ceiling by a wide margin; eligibility for a specific parcel is UNVERIFIED. The Township's coach-house definition is in Township Zoning Bylaw 2500, s.102 (definition source only). The Township secondary-suite cap in this program's settled position is 120 m²; the "15% of lot to max 100 m²" coach/garden-suite rule is Abbotsford's, NOT the Township's, and is not applied here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **Form B (BC standard block):** SPA s.59 — a strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus 25¢/page, with the most recent depreciation report attached. No strata stock is verified in this sub-area, so no Campbell Valley purchase in this file runs on a Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** depreciation report every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023. **Rentals/age:** rental-restriction bylaws void since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws legal (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01 exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Township property taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 (including the unclaimed home owner grant) and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — March 12 secondary date superseded): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. PTT/GST apportionment on a specific farm/acreage purchase: UNVERIFIED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/campbell-valley.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%.
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is on this page; elsewhere, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s.18 requires deposits in trust.
