## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. In Willoughby's 2016–2024 stock this lands as a first-cycle question; in Langley City's older low-rises it is the levy story. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals, and a directory line like Yorkson Creek Parkside 2's "rentals allowed" is now simply the default law; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted in its sources — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. (The 55+ product on this feed is the Aldergrove manufactured-home park, which is park tenure, not a strata age bylaw.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Plans and zoning (two municipalities):** Willoughby Heights, Aldergrove and Walnut Grove are Township of Langley sub-areas — Township zoning is Bylaw No. 2500 (Willoughby's compact-lot R-CL zone, s. 403.7: lots from a minimum 220 m² to a maximum 464.5 m², minimum frontage 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded), and the Township's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; no 6-plex tier exists in it). The Willoughby Community Plan Update bylaws No. 6201, 6202 and 6203 replaced the Willoughby Community Plan and created the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan. Langley City is a separate municipality with its own plan and zoning — a Langley City condo is not under Township rules. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/langley-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **Property taxes:** no single Langley tax due date is stated in this file, because the community spans two municipalities with separate tax notices: UNVERIFIED in this file for either municipality. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/langley-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (any developer sales in Willoughby's newest projects), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — most purchases in this band are taxed in the 1% and 2% tiers only. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so the program bears only on the lowest-priced end of this band. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract unit in Willoughby's newest projects. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Manufactured homes in parks (MHPTA):** where a buyer in this band is looking at the Aldergrove park product instead of a condo, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act pad-rent framework applies — the permitted annual pad-rent increase is 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. Park rules and tenure are property-specific documents. (BC standard block for this program; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/langley-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
