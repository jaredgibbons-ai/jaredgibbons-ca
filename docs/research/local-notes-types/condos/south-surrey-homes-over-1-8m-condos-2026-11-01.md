## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. In this file's roster this lands hardest on the oldest buildings — The Capistrano (1980s listings), Semiahmoo Estates (1990/1991) and Suncliff Place (2002). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Semiahmoo Estates and The Capistrano in Section 2 are flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Three of the five verified buildings are presented as 55+ communities — confirm on each Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — kept separate by municipality:** Surrey side: the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026 — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only) and Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000. White Rock side: White Rock Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and White Rock OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220 — that pair belongs to White Rock ONLY, never to a Surrey parcel. The same question (suites, SSMUH, subdivision) can therefore have two different answers on one page. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Surrey suite and coach-house rules (Surrey side only):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Bylaw No. 12000 — the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule, never state it. Coach houses / garden suites: 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots. Short-term rentals in Surrey: owner-only hosting from the principal residence, City business licence $358/yr. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **ALR frame:** eastern South Surrey carries extensive Agricultural Land Reserve land; the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), a suite only inside the principal residence, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — confirmed with the ALC, not from a note on title. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the live rule for Odyssey as newly completed stock and for the presales named in Section 2. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $2,049,000 (the lowest card price visible on this page's feed at research reading, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is a detached listing's asking price, not a condo figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $1,470 (3% × $49,000) = **$39,470 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV ≤$835,000, partial to $860,000 — neither reaches this band. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — likewise below this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/south-surrey-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Presales (REDMA) — the frame for Harlowe, Calais and Rockford:** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A presale contract is not a resale: none of the three projects is counted as resale band stock in Section 2. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no pad-rent park is part of this band's verified stock; where such parks are discussed in this program, MHPTA pad-rent increases are limited to 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027.
