## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Tenure first (the local rule that overrides everything else here):** Champlain Heights mixes freehold detached houses, strata townhouses and condos, and leasehold complexes from its planned build-out; the research file's instruction is to lead every purchase with a tenure check and confirm tenure and lease terms (expiry, rent, prepaid vs monthly) on title before pricing, because lease terms change both financing and value. A strata Form B exists only for a strata purchase — it is not the document set for a co-op or a leasehold interest. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Form B and strata records (strata purchases only):** on a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Champlain Heights strata complex is void for long-term rentals since that date. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Champlain Heights complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B and registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. The house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), and Champlain Heights South is also the subject of a site-specific CD-1 district — CD-1 (139), Champlain Heights South, By-law No. 5381, being a by-law to amend By-law No. 3575, whose permitted uses include townhouse (including stacked townhouses) and apartments. Parcel-level zone for a specific complex: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md) and https://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/cd-1(139).PDF
- **Area plan:** Champlain Heights sits inside the Killarney local area; the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District) applies at Killarney's south edge, not inside Champlain Heights itself; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. A Champlain Heights-specific plan: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Floodplain:** the neighbourhood sits on the south slope above the Fraser River and the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the river's edge, south of the house streets; parcels in the floodplain carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** every residential property owner must submit an annual property status declaration; properties deemed or declared empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026. The City's tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/evidence-exemptions.aspx and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No Champlain Heights presale project was verified in this pass — this rule is stated for completeness if a leasehold or strata presale is offered. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Leasehold tenure can separately affect lender and insurer acceptance — lender position on a specific Champlain Heights lease: UNCONFIRMED; confirm before writing. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The leases were a deliberate 1970s plan, and renewals are live now.** CHF BC records that the City developed Champlain Heights from 1974 to 1986 leasing sites to non-market housing sponsors instead of selling them, and its 2026 lease-renewal update records new long-term lease approvals for co-ops including Cedar Mill (July 2025) — a buyer of any leasehold or co-op interest here is buying into an active lease-renewal landscape, not a settled one. https://www.chf.bc.ca/vancouver-lease-renewals-backgrounder-and-update/
- **Everett Crowley Park was a landfill.** The 40-hectare park at 8200 Kerr Street adjoining the neighbourhood (Park Board dataset figure 38.08 hectares) is a former city landfill and the area's green core — relevant context for homes on its edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **The "freehold" exception proves the rule.** Marine Woods marketing has to call a freehold townhouse "rare" in Champlain Heights (8401 Millstone Street listing) — in most Vancouver neighbourhoods freehold strata is the default and needs no adjective. If a Champlain Heights listing does not state tenure, assume nothing and check title. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2833579-8401-millstone-street-vancouver-bc-v5s-4s3
- **The neighbourhood has its own library branch but no community centre of its own name.** The Champlain Heights Branch library is at 7110 Kerr Street inside the neighbourhood, while pool, rink and fitness services are at Killarney Community Centre, 6260 Killarney Street. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Zoning already contemplates apartments here — tenure is the constraint, not the bylaw.** CD-1 (139), Champlain Heights South (By-law No. 5381, amending By-law No. 3575), lists townhouse including stacked townhouses and apartments among its permitted uses — the absence of apartment condos in Section 2 reflects the leasehold / co-op build-out that actually happened, not a zoning prohibition.
