## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The attached stock in this scope is townhouse strata, but the same Strata Property Act rules apply to a townhouse strata lot as to an apartment condo lot.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the central diligence document for every complex in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in this scope may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Wherever a directory or listing line for a complex here says "rentals not allowed," that line is void and is flagged as such in-entry; no such line was found for the complexes above — Brighton's building record instead records rentals as allowed, which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful in a strata; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. The one age-restricted complex in this scope, Haney Pioneer Village, is a 60+ co-op under co-operative law, not an SPA 55+ bylaw — the SPA exemptions do not govern it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — urban-fringe houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605: minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%), and the rural/agricultural zones on the acreage side carry much larger minimums (A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — do not treat either number as settled. No plan is written for "North Maple Ridge" as a whole; the Silver Valley Area Plan update, adopted November 25, 2025, covers the north part of this scope. The Lougheed Transit Corridor plan area lies south/west of this scope (the corridor runs from the city's west boundary to 221 Street); its adoption status is flagged contested in this program's record — verify the current position on the City's project page before relying on any adoption claim. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/north-maple-ridge.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. On ALR parcels the provincial ALR rules also govern. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/north-maple-ridge.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales — the verified complexes here are resale stock (newest verified build year 2023, Windfall). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract (Hillside Homes was a presale collection; whether any unit remains on a developer contract is UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Pad-rent parks (MHPTA):** no manufactured-home park inside this scope was verified; where a Maple Ridge park home is bought instead of a strata unit, pad rent may rise at most once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, by no more than the yearly limit plus a proportional amount — 2026 = 2.3% plus proportional, 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
