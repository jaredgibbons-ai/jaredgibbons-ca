## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Lakeside, the Form B and the strata plan are also where the freehold strata tenure is confirmed against the developer's "freehold waterfront land" description — the strata plan number is UNCONFIRMED in public sources opened. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Lakeside's 21 homes put it inside the five-or-more-lots depreciation-report frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Lakeside carries no age bylaw in any source checked — it is general-market. Short-term rentals are a separate gate: stratas may still ban or limit them by bylaw, and the building's own short-term rule is UNCONFIRMED — read the bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Governing zoning and plans (FVRD / Park Board — never Chilliwack's bylaws):** the FVRD's Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 zones the Park lease lands; zones at the north end include R-1 Suburban Residential (one single-detached dwelling per lot) and R-2 Urban Residential (one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite); the bylaw tables state no minimum lot area figure for R-1/R-2, so minimums are UNVERIFIED. PlanCultus, the Cultus Lake Park Plan, Bylaw No. 1080, 2016, is the Park's land use plan and designates the existing residential neighbourhoods as Residential, with single-family use and, subject to Park bylaws, secondary suites or coach houses; Area H land outside the Park falls under the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115. Lakeside sits on freehold land at the park boundary (developer source) — which plan and zone apply to its parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED in this pass, and no other FVRD bylaw number is borrowed here. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Area-H-Zoning-Bylaw-1375-Cultus-Lake-Park-Amended.pdf and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-north.md)
- **Taxes — lease charges and the rural regime, not Chilliwack's dates:** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill (current schedule and per-lot FVRD levies UNVERIFIED). For non-Park Electoral Area H land — the frame for a freehold parcel like Lakeside's — taxes are provincial rural taxes under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11: due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid after October 31, plus FVRD service-area levies where the lot participates. Chilliwack's penalty dates NEVER apply to Cultus Lake. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — any remaining developer sale at Lakeside is the new-construction case; resales are not. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (round illustration only — no area median exists to use), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (the building's own site states 5 of 21 homes remain), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. The Village Centre redevelopment is NOT a presale — it is a Park Board RFP process. https://www.lakesidecultuslake.com/ and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. Lenders and insurers treat Park Board leasehold homes differently from freehold — for the lease lots (not Lakeside), confirm financing terms against the lease before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: What condos can I buy at the north end of Cultus Lake?** One building: Lakeside at Cultus Lake, 3175 Columbia Valley Highway — 21 condominium homes on freehold waterfront land (developer source), built 2019 per listing data, each with a boat slip per the developer and the building's site. The developer describes freehold waterfront land, while homes inside Cultus Lake Park sit on leased lots administered by the Park Board under the Cultus Lake Park Act. - **Q: Can I rent out a Lakeside unit?** Long-term: yes in law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term/vacation rental use is a separate question governed by the strata's own bylaws (UNCONFIRMED for this building) — read them before counting on vacation-rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are any Lakeside homes still developer sales?** The building's own site states only 5 of its 21 homes remain. - **Q: Can I add a suite to a Park lease lot instead?** It depends on the zone: under FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016, R-1 allows one single-detached dwelling per lot, while R-2 allows one single-detached dwelling plus one secondary suite — and PlanCultus contemplates secondary suites or coach houses subject to Park bylaws. - **Q: Is bus service year-round?** No. Route 56 Cultus Lake runs between Chilliwack (Cottonwood Mall / Promontory area) and the Cultus Lake Marina stop in summer months only, and the Village Centre Plan states there are no year-round transit services to Cultus Lake Park. Year-round living at the north end means driving. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview?route=56

- **Q: What hazards should a north-end buyer note?** The FVRD Area H Evacuation Guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding among local hazards, and the FVRD provides a Flood and Debris Control service in Area H. Price insurance and emergency planning accordingly. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The north end's only condominium markets itself on scarcity, not age.** Lakeside's own site states only 5 of its 21 homes remain, with a private dock and a boat slip for each home — a recreational product aimed at weekend and second-home buyers, with no age restriction anywhere in its material. Do not let a "Cultus Lake condos" search imply a condo market here: it is one 21-home building. https://www.lakesidecultuslake.com/
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for anywhere at Cultus Lake.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Chilliwack section at all, and neither does the GVR report — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for this sub-area has no source in the named stats program. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-north.md
- **The Park is self-funded, which is why the lease charges matter.** Park Board community material records that Cultus Lake Park receives no local, provincial or federal funding and is entirely self-sustaining, with more than 1,500 residential leaseholders — lease charges are the Park's operating revenue, not a tax bill by another name. That frame does not apply to Lakeside's freehold parcel, which is why the tenure check comes first. https://www.admiraal.ca/cultus-lake?p=5
- **The lake's sewage servicing is being rebuilt in stages, north side first.** The FVRD's North Cultus wastewater treatment plant was commissioned in April 2025 under the FVRD Liquid Waste Management Plan (approved 2019), for Class A effluent plus phosphorus removal — 2025 was its first operational year, and it serves the lake's north side. Whether a specific north-end lot is connected is a per-lot check. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html and https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/wwtp
- **"Cultus Lake North" is an emergency-planning name as much as a real estate one.** The FVRD uses "Cultus Lake North" as a community location name in its Area H Evacuation Guide; it is not a municipal neighbourhood name, and no official MLS boundary for it is published by the Park Board or the FVRD. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **The Village Centre is a negotiation, not a presale.** Village Centre redevelopment is a Park Board request-for-proposals process with a preferred proponent in negotiations. A buyer told a new condo project is "coming" at the Village Centre is being told about an RFP — no developer, no disclosure statement, no units. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-north.md)
