## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a Chilliwack townhome this is also where tenure-adjacent facts (registered bylaws, rental disclosure) are confirmed before price is settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. The live page directs hillside-complex buyers to roof, siding, window and driveway timelines and any retaining-wall maintenance obligations in Promontory or Eastern Hillsides complexes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Cottonwood Village's 55+ presentation does not make it strata townhome stock (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans — Chilliwack rules:** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 5000 governs; its small-scale multi-unit housing amendment is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. The Official Community Plan is Bylaw 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050"), with the Sardis plan as Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan as Schedule B1. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/chilliwack-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — inside this band, a qualifying newly built townhome bought as a principal residence at $1,100,000 or less can be fully PTT-exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract townhome purchase arises (for example a new Garrison phase), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Presales are never counted as resale band stock in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
