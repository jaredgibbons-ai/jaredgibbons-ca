## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. With no building verified in Section 2, the Form B is the starting document for every Port Moody condo purchase in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" in a Port Moody building is VOID for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; Port Moody's own short-term rental rules: UNVERIFIED — no City of Port Moody STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/port-moody-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Port Moody building in this file is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans:** the Port Moody Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 2026 No. 3522** (adopted April 28, 2026, repealing Bylaw No. 2955) — cited in this file as OCP Bylaw No. 3522. Its Chapter 14 covers the city by neighbourhood: Section 14.2 (Pleasantside and April Road), 14.3 (the Heritage Mountain grouping: Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows, Heritage Woods), 14.4 (Ioco Lands), 14.5 (Inlet Centre and Coronation Park) and 14.6 (Moody Centre, including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area and the Heritage Commercial District). The board's "Port Moody Centre" sub-area is not itself an OCP neighbourhood name — the OCP covers that ground as Moody Centre and Inlet Centre. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/port-moody-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Zoning / SSMUH:** Port Moody Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones, with mixed-use development in TC and CRM zones at the centre; small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas, and the transit-oriented areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules. Watercourse lots need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on the hillsides. Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas, where a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction, reconstruction or changes on included properties. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/port-moody-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Port Moody property taxes:** City of Port Moody 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, and utilities are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/port-moody-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $715,000 (the Port Moody community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, board community including the Anmore/Belcarra grouping, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,300 (2% × $515,000) = **$12,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. - **Presales (REDMA):** where a Port Moody unit is on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
