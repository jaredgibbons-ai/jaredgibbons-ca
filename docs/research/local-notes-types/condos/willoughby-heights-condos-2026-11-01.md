# Willoughby Heights — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Willoughby Heights" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; the Township of Langley's official planning name is "Willoughby." The Willoughby Community Plan Area in Bylaw No. 6201 is bordered by the Surrey municipal boundary to the southwest, Highway 1 to the north, the Trans Mountain Pipeline right-of-way ridgeline, Lower Mainland Regional Park and 68 Avenue to the south, and 216 Street and the Willowbrook Connector to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/willoughby-heights.md) https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) the Latimer Heights / 200 Street corridor around 83 Avenue–86 Avenue — The Towers, Latimer Village, Yorkson Park and Yorkson Downs; (2) the Yorkson / 208 Street node around 82 Avenue–83 Avenue — Union Park, Yorkson Creek Parkside 2, Yorkson Creek and Walnut Park; (3) Willoughby Town Centre around 206 Street and 80 Avenue — The Hive and Yorkson Downs on Willoughby Town Centre Drive; (4) the southwest Willoughby edge — Elements on 66 Avenue and Matteo at 198B Street and 76 Avenue. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Willoughby Heights is the centre of the Langley attached market: 369 attached listings and 70 attached sales in September 2026, the deepest attached selection and largest attached sales count of any Langley sub-area in that report — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
- Transit and landmark anchors named in Township and TransLink material are the Langley Events Centre at 7888 200 Street, the Carvolth Exchange area at 200 Street and Highway 1, and routes 501 (Surrey Central–Langley Centre via 200 Street), 372 and 595. https://www.tol.ca/en/arts-culture-recreation/langley-events-centre.aspx and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt501.pdf
- The Willoughby Community Plan Update (Bylaws No. 6201, 6202 and 6203) replaced the old Willoughby Community Plan and created the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan; the Transit Corridor is the high-density spine where further condo form is planned. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days of the request; the maximum charge is $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for copying. The Form B discloses the monthly strata fee, amounts owed, approved special levies, bylaw amendments not yet filed, proceedings the strata is party to, parking and storage allocations, and summaries of strata insurance and electrical planning reports. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every verified Willoughby building in Section 2 is well over five lots, so the five-year cycle applies across the local stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line for a Willoughby building that says rentals are not allowed reflects old bylaw text, not current law — none of the Section 2 entries above carries such a line, but check older resale documents for one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, caregivers and defined family members. No verified Willoughby building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted; if a listing claims an age rule, confirm on the Form B whether a registered 55+ bylaw exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. Willoughby's compact-lot pattern is the Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (Section 403): under s. 403.7, R-CL lots run from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m², with minimum frontage of 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded — the detached and townhouse fabric the condo buildings sit among. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **SSMUH:** the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing rules under Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allow 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible for the SSMUH tiers and there is no 6-unit tier in the Township. SSMUH affects the small-lot streets around the condo nodes, not the strata apartment buildings themselves. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example Matteo while it sells as a presale), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) provides, together with the GST/HST New Housing Rebate, 100% of the GST on a new home valued up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA publication governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial from $835,000 to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to a Matteo presale purchase. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing the purchase agreement and acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust. Assignment consent and fees for Matteo and Walnut Park are contract terms — UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50% across the loan-to-value bands, plus a 0.20% surcharge in the over-25-year amortization case stated in the CMHC table. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Willoughby mostly towers or low-rise?** Low-rise. The only verified concrete high-rise project is The Towers at Latimer Heights (two towers, completed 2024); Union Park, The Hive, Matteo and Walnut Park are all published as 6-storey wood-frame, and the older Yorkson and Latimer Village stock is low-rise wood-frame. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-towers-latimer-heights-willoughby-langley-presale-condos-townhomes-by-vesta-properties/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/polygon-union-park-willoughby-langley-presale-condos/
- **Q: A listing for a Cascadia or Luxia home calls it a condo. Is it an apartment?** No. Cascadia at 6951 204 Street and Luxia at Yorkson at 7947 209 Street are townhome complexes; they are documented separately in Section 2 and are not counted as apartment condos in this file. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cascadia-by-streetside-development-6951-204-street (building directory, secondary source)
- **Q: Can I rent my Willoughby condo out?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable anywhere in BC since November 24, 2022. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you, and the registered bylaws govern pets. Yorkson Creek Parkside 2 is directory-recorded as rentals allowed in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yorkson-creek-parkside-2-8157-207-street (building directory, secondary source)
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It varies by building and pet bylaws remain enforceable. The one directory-verified line in this file is Yorkson Creek Parkside 2: pets allowed with restrictions. For The Towers, Union Park, The Hive, Elements and Matteo, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws attached to the Form B package. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yorkson-creek-parkside-2-8157-207-street (building directory, secondary source)
- **Q: How new is the stock, and why does that matter for levies?** Most verified buildings were built between 2016 and 2025, and The Towers in 2024 — young stock, but every strata with five or more lots must now carry a depreciation report refreshed every five years and fund the CRF at at least 10% of the operating budget, so a young building is not exempt from levy planning. Read the current report's funding models before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What is different about buying Matteo as a presale?** You are buying under REDMA: a 7-day rescission right, deposits in trust, GST at 5% on completion because it is new construction, and a completion date (mid-2028 in developer marketing) that is UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement and its amendments are read. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption can both be in play — see Section 3. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/matteo-langley-presale-condos/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which parking and locker actually come with a unit?** Only what the Form B allocates. Listings for Yorkson-area units variously claim one stall, two stalls, EV-ready stalls and roll-up storage garages — those are unit-specific listing claims, not building entitlements. The Form B states the stall and locker numbers allocated to the strata lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: How is transit from the Yorkson buildings?** The Carvolth Exchange at 200 Street and Highway 1 is the hub named in Township and TransLink material for the area, and Yorkson Park listings note the exchange across from that cluster; route 501 runs Surrey Central to Langley Centre via 200 Street. There is no rail transit in Langley today — any SkyTrain timing claim in a listing is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt501.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Willoughby condo?** Catchments are address-based in SD35. For the sample address 7888 200 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, Peter Ewart Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary; other Willoughby addresses can fall in different catchments, so run the building's exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/willoughby-heights.md) https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: Is a unit in Elements the same age as the complex?** No — listings for 20211 66 Avenue give built years from 2013 to 2018 depending on the building (A through F). Age, warranty status and depreciation-report timing follow the specific building, not the complex name. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/20211-66-avenue/d305

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The plan changed in 2026-era bylaws:** Bylaws No. 6201, 6202 and 6203 replaced the Willoughby Community Plan and created the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan — what gets built beside a condo bought today follows the new plans, and the Transit Corridor is the designated high-density spine. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **The Towers' headline count mixes product types:** the published "nearly 500 homes" for The Towers at Latimer Heights includes townhomes as well as condos, so resale depth for the towers alone is smaller than the headline suggests. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-towers-latimer-heights-willoughby-langley-presale-condos-townhomes-by-vesta-properties/
- **Yorkson Park is not one building:** the name covers several addresses on and around 86 Avenue and 203A Street (20328, 20290 and 20376 86 Avenue; 8561 203A Street), and the directory's 31-unit figure belongs to 20328 86 Avenue only, strata plan EPS7248. Comparing fees or reports across "Yorkson Park" units without checking which strata plan the unit sits in is a known trap. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3171831-201-20328-86-avenue-langley-bc-v2y-3l5 (building directory, secondary source)
- **Cascadia's research-file label was wrong in the source research:** the area research file names Cascadia at Willoughby Town Centre among condo projects, but listings and the directory show Cascadia at 6951 204 Street is townhomes (built 2024) in the Central Gordon Estate area — this file corrects it to the townhome section. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/cascadia-willoughby-langley-presale-townhomes-by-streetside-developments/
- **Selection is deepest here, on the record:** Willoughby Heights carried 369 attached listings in September 2026 against 100 detached listings — attached buyers here have more choice, and attached sellers more direct competition, than anywhere else in Langley that month. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/willoughby-heights.md).
- **Trinity Western University's Smith campus is in southeast Willoughby** at 8086 208 Street, and the Township's plan background names a campus expansion in southeast Willoughby — part of the rental-demand backdrop for the Yorkson buildings, stated as context only; no rental-yield figure is published in the sources checked. https://www.twu.ca/academics/smith-school-business
