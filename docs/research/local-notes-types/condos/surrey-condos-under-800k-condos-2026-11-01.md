## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this band the Form B, not the listing, is where fee, levy and deductible exposure is settled — the live page's strata-fee ranges were not confirmed on an official source and are not repeated here as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Most Section 2 buildings are 1990s–2010s wood-frame — the report's envelope, roof and parkade cycles are the levy story in this band. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Newton Court, St. Andrews and Calera directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this file's roster, Chelsea Gardens is the building whose current listings state a 55+ restriction (with the void 19+ wording still in an older directory) — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit there as generally occupiable. The Surrey 55+ roster held elsewhere in this program (15 strata plus bare-land and park entries) is cross-referenced here only for that one building; no other Section 2 building is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. **SSMUH:** Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, with no retroactive legalization of an unpermitted suite; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite — relevant to buyers comparing a band condo against a house with a helper. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, directly relevant to closing adjustments on any band purchase completing around those dates. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** renting a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, an apartment unit with one lock-off suite (if applicable) is a permitted housing type, and a business licence at $358 per year plus any application fees is required before operating. A strata STR bylaw is a separate, building-level check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this file that means developer sales (for example any remaining developer unit at Viva) and presale contracts, not the resale buildings. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling, subject to the program's other conditions (principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller). Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for the presales named in Section 2, which are not resale band stock. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

Any page that quotes a band median is quoting a number no source publishes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **The honest composition of the band is low-rise, not towers.** The verified named resale roster behind this band is Newton and Clayton low-rise wood-frame stock from 1993–2025 (College Park 1993 through Silva 2024/2025), plus one gated 55+-described community (Chelsea Gardens) — while the towers that dominate Surrey condo marketing sit in a different price conversation entirely. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **One name, two tenures of product: Compass.** In Clayton the Compass name covers both an apartment phase and townhouse units at neighbouring addresses — a feed result labelled "Compass" does not tell you which form you are looking at until the MLS sub-type is read. (Cloverdale condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cloverdale-bc-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **A directory can carry a legally dead age bylaw for years.** Chelsea Gardens' older directory still describes it as "adult-oriented (19+)" while current listings state 55+ — the 19+ form has been invalid since November 24, 2022. Directory age and rental lines are the two fields in this band most likely to be stale law. (East Newton condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/east-newton-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **The cap moved relative to the market in a single month.** Surrey's attached median fell from $560,000 in August 2026 to $512,944 in September 2026 (with days on market tightening from 32 to 25) — the distance between the $800K cap and the middle of the market is a moving figure, which is another reason this file states no band share. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Two of the band's districts have no SkyTrain on any funded timeline.** Newton — home to most of Section 2's buildings — is served by the R1 RapidBus; the funded rapid-transit project is the Fraser Highway extension to Langley (anticipated 2029), which does not run down King George Boulevard to Newton. Price a Newton unit on the bus corridor it has, not the SkyTrain map of a different corridor.
