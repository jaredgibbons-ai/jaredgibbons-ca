## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The condo rules a map-search user needs, in the order they bite: what a pin or card price actually is, then the strata document rules behind any result the user pursues, then the tax and savings frame for budgeting a search.

- **Map pins and card prices are feed data, not verified facts:** any price, pin position or filter result a map tool shows is live MLS® feed data at that moment — a snapshot that changes as listings come and go — not a verified statement that a property sits inside a price band, a sub-area or a buyer's budget. Feed freshness for this site's map is UNVERIFIED (no update interval or timestamp is stated on the page), and this program's own record shows why the caveat matters: a sibling price-band page's feed led with cards priced above its own stated ceiling, so a band or boundary drawn on a map must be re-checked against the listing itself, and band membership for any specific building is UNCONFIRMED until a live listing for that building is read. https://jaredgibbons.ca/map-search.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md) (Langley under-$499K file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-condos-under-499k-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — for any strata result a map user pursues, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate on a resale purchase, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Form B package, not the map card, is where age, pet and rental restrictions, fees and levies actually live. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — so a directory or listing line a map user meets saying "rentals not allowed" on a result reflects old bylaw text, not current law, for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — a map filter cannot apply an age bylaw for the user; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs who may live in a unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning bylaw + OCP, per municipality:** a site-wide map crosses many municipalities, each with its own zoning bylaw and OCP — there is no single bylaw to cite for this page, and the governing bylaw for any result is that parcel's own municipality's, verified parcel by parcel. The page's own GIS section points the same way: turn on the parcel, zoning, OCP, ALR, flood plain and environmentally sensitive area layers on the municipality's own GIS. What each municipality's GIS shows layer by layer, and the exact setback or easement figures for any lot: UNVERIFIED at municipal level in this program. https://jaredgibbons.ca/map-search.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md)
- **The ALR layer is a legal gate, not a map decoration:** a parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address, and housing on ALR land is capped at one principal residence of 500 m² or less plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (larger parcels), with a secondary suite only inside the principal residence — and depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring ALC permission (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is why the ALR layer the page tells readers to turn on matters before a build plan is priced. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — a map filter by price does not separate the two, so a new-build result and a resale result at the same card price carry different tax. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million, based on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies — the figure a map-search user is usually budgeting around alongside the price filters. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** a map result can be a developer contract rather than a resale; where it is, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — and a presale is never resale stock, however the pin is labelled. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Who a listing's agent works for, for a user who finds a home and contacts its brokerage:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/map-search.md)
