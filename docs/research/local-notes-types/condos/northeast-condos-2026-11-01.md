## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules are stated here because a buyer searching "Northeast Maple Ridge condos" will meet them the moment any strata unit appears — and because they govern the Maple Ridge condo stock this area's buyers usually end up comparing against, downtown. There is currently no verified strata building in this sub-area for them to attach to.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Maple Ridge building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Northeast building is published as age-restricted — there is no verified building here at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; its schedule minimum lot areas include A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha (refer to zone text). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in earlier program research (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024) — flagged as contested here, never asserted as one number. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Secondary suites (the attached form that actually exists here):** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs — one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31), an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Northeast was verified in this pass, so this is background law, not a live project term here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Which schools are physically inside the sub-area?** Blue Mountain Elementary (12153 248th Street), Webster's Corners Elementary (25554 Dewdney Trunk Road), Whonnock Elementary (27471 112th Avenue), Garibaldi Secondary (24789 Dewdney Trunk Road, which offers the IB Programme) and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary (10445 245th Street). SD42 has no middle tier — elementary is K–7, secondary is 8–12. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/ and https://www.sd42.ca/secondary-schools/
- **Q: What catchment does an address here feed?** Address-based, through the SD42 School Locator. The recorded sample — 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road — gives Webster's Corners Elementary (K–7) and Garibaldi Secondary (8–12); the French Immersion schools the locator shows for that address are choice programs, not catchments. Catchments for other Northeast addresses: UNVERIFIED until run in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3 if it sits inside the single detached building, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land an additional separate residence is allowed only under the ALR Use Regulation's s. 32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: What is the North 256 Street plan, and does it approve building?** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan — Council approved the OCP amendments creating a plan area on both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres) — establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction. Existing uses in the area include gravel extraction, institutional operations and civic services. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Q: How is transit, honestly?** Thin and road-based: route 701 on Dewdney Trunk Road (most east trips end at 248 Street) and route 748's Thornhill loop at wide intervals, weekdays and Saturdays only. The nearest West Coast Express stations are outside the sub-area. Price a home here as a drive-to-everything property. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt748.pdf
- **Q: What land checks matter before subjects come off on an acreage?** ALR status per parcel through the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder; the well record in the provincial GWELLS database; and septic compliance with the Sewerage System Regulation, which sets setbacks including 30 m from a well for a sewerage system unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Q: Does the floodplain reach this sub-area?** At its south edge: the City's floodplain framework addresses flood risks from the Fraser River, the Alouette River system, steep creek watercourses and groundwater-related flooding, and low land along the Fraser River at the sub-area's south edge is the area to check on City flood maps, property by property. Secondary suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area under the City's suite rules. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites

- **The sub-area name exists in exactly one statistical table.** "Northeast" appears as a sub-area only under Maple Ridge in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 extraction — and under no other community — which is part of how the research file pinned an ambiguous slug to this place. - **The Thornhill Urban Reserve is a designation, not a subdivision.** The OCP's Thornhill Urban Reserve designation identifies lands for long-term future urban services and housing — a long-horizon planning signal around 256 Street, not serviced lots today. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2
- **School bus service is a listed-school privilege here.** SD42's student transportation page lists regular school bus service for specific schools including Blue Mountain Elementary, Garibaldi Secondary, Webster's Corners Elementary and Whonnock Elementary — in a sub-area with no recreation centre or library branch of its own, bus eligibility is a real daily-life line item to confirm by address. https://www.sd42.ca/student-transportation/
- **The nearest civic anchors are all in Town Centre, to the west.** No City recreation centre or library branch inside this sub-area was verified; the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre (11925 Haney Place), the Maple Ridge Public Library (22470 Dewdney Trunk Road) and Ridge Meadows Hospital (11666 Laity Street, 24/7 emergency) all sit west of the sub-area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **Ruskin's working landscape is part of the setting.** The City states Ruskin has a long history of lumber and sawmills, with shake and sawmills still active along the Stave River, and Ruskin Community Hall remains an important meeting place — industrial neighbours are a standing feature of the east end, not an anomaly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
