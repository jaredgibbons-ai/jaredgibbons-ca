## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Arm is the City of Richmond's south-central residential district along Williams Road, between the Steveston side of the city and No. 4 Road, fronting the South Arm of the Fraser River along the city's south edge; the name refers to that river channel, and the City itself uses "South Arm" as the recreation area name for the district. The board's exact boundary for the sub-area is not published on an official source and is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- Where the housing stock concentrates: single-detached houses in the RS1/RS2 zones around the South Arm Community Park and school cluster — the district's documented base form is freehold detached housing, not condos. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- Main roads: Williams Road runs east–west through the district, with Garden City Road, No. 3 Road and No. 4 Road running north to the city centre and Steveston Highway connecting west Richmond to Highway 99; there is no SkyTrain station in the district — Canada Line access is by bus to Richmond–Brighouse Station. Transit route numbers serving Williams Road: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- Landmarks: South Arm Community Park, 8680 Williams Road — 57.3 acres with 10 sports fields, 9 baseball diamonds, 4 basketball courts, 8 tennis courts, 3 pickleball courts and a large playground — adjacent to South Arm Community Centre (8880 Williams Road) and to Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In South Arm the Form B is secondary to title: for Mayfair Court, the ground lease and its remaining term (a listing states almost 63 years remaining) are the documents that govern the purchase. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Mayfair Court's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with current law — and any "rentals not allowed" line for a Richmond strata would be void for long-term tenancies. Note the limit in this area: the 2022 change does nothing for a leasehold purchase, where the ground lease — not strata bylaws — is the governing document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No South Arm complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 — houses sit in the Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m²; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (Section 5.4: minimum 33.0 m², maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²). The district is covered by the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, with no separate South Arm area plan confirmed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Flood protection:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain behind the City perimeter dike system, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Richmond property tax:** taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live for Kingz Court townhome sales (2026). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000. PTT treatment of a leasehold interest (Mayfair Court) differs from freehold — take tax advice on the specific lease before an offer. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in the district was verified in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50%. Separately, leasehold properties are harder to finance and insure than freehold — for Mayfair Court a lender's leasehold policy, not the premium table, is usually the binding constraint. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any freehold apartment condos in South Arm?** None verified in this pass — the count is zero. The district's documented base form is detached houses in RS1/RS2; its one large named apartment complex, Mayfair Court, is leasehold, and its other named apartment building, Williams by Mosaic, is rental. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is a Mayfair Court unit a normal condo purchase?** No. A current listing describes Mayfair Court as a leasehold community with almost 63 years remaining in the lease; the directory records 246 units built 1973 (listings say 1974), pets not allowed and rentals allowed. Price, finance and insure it as leasehold: confirm the lease's remaining term, rent and end-of-term provisions on title before anything else. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3084639-8091-ryan-road-137-richmond-bc-v7a-2e4/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mayfair-court-8160-williams-road
- **Q: Can I have a pet at Mayfair Court?** The directory states the bylaws include "Pets not allowed" — pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws), so confirm on the Form B and registered bylaws before writing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mayfair-court-8160-williams-road
- **Q: What attached product can I actually buy here?** Townhomes: Mandalay on Hemlock Drive (named in the research file from a property-manager source) and Kingz Court at 8233 Williams Road (2026 townhomes in current listings). Both are documented in Section 2 as townhomes, not apartment condos. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md) and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/8233-williams-road/102
- **Q: Can I rent out a South Arm strata unit long-term?** For a strata lot, rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 — but in a leasehold complex the ground lease governs occupancy and resale alongside the bylaws, and the 2022 change does not override a lease. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Arm address?** Hugh McRoberts Secondary and James Whiteside Elementary sit beside South Arm Community Park, and Steveston-London Secondary (6600 Williams Road, Grades 8–12, 1,250 students) is at the west end of the Williams Road corridor; SD38 catchments are address-based with no middle school tier, so run the exact address in the SD38 locator. Kingz Court listings name William Bridge Elementary and Hugh McRoberts Secondary for that address — a listing claim, not a locator run. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Q: Is South Arm in the floodplain?** Yes — all of Richmond is in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by the perimeter dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event; Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels, which matter before planning additions or a rebuild near the south dike. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Q: Can a South Arm house have a suite?** The RS1/RS2 zones permit a secondary suite as a secondary use, and the City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit is allowed per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Q: Is there a median South Arm condo price I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Richmond is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used. Price from the specific complex's recent sales instead — and for Mayfair Court, from leasehold sales only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The district's biggest "condo" complex is a leasehold community.** Mayfair Court spans Williams Road and Ryan Road addresses (8051–8160) with 246 units per the directory, and a listing states almost 63 years remain on its lease — inside a standard mortgage horizon, which is why its units trade on a different basis from freehold Richmond condos. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/mayfair-court-8160-williams-road and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3084639-8091-ryan-road-137-richmond-bc-v7a-2e4/brochure
- **The park cluster is the district's documented anchor, not a slogan.** South Arm Community Park's 57.3 acres, 10 sports fields and the community centre, outdoor pool and two schools beside it are all City-published facts; the playground was officially opened in July 2023 with funding support from the South Arm Community Association. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Whiteside Elementary has already had its seismic upgrade.** The Province's project kept the building at 3,107 m² with occupancy from fall 2023 — a completed investment a buyer can verify, not a promise. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **The newest townhome complex is filed under a different neighbourhood name.** Kingz Court listings at 8233 Williams Road are filed under Saunders, with South Arm named only as a nearby neighbourhood — a search limited to "South Arm" misses the district's newest attached stock. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/8233-williams-road/102
- **Richmond dikes are being raised by more than a metre.** The perimeter system is moving from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for its 1:500 design event — the flood context behind Bylaw 8204's construction levels on every south-edge lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **There is no South Arm area plan.** The district runs under the city-wide OCP Bylaw 9000 framework rather than a Schedule 2 area plan of its own — density questions here are answered in Zoning Bylaw 8500, not in a neighbourhood plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
