## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in Hastings' small stock this is the central document: Trinity Manor (1974) turns on its completed envelope and systems work, and L'Atelier (a 1995 conversion of a 1930s building) turns on the conversion-era and envelope record. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by a strata, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing — the two layers are distinct, and the City licence specifics for a given building: UNCONFIRMED in this pass. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is void as a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Corridor commercial and mixed-use sites on Hastings Street — where the Section 2 condos stand — sit under other district schedules, including C-3A on parts of the street. The western edge of Hastings-Sunrise is included in the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, and the citywide Vancouver Plan also applies. The zone for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — confirm the seller's declaration status in due diligence on any Hastings purchase. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain, with Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels for parcels in it; the Burrard Inlet shoreline at the area's north side, including New Brighton Park, is the part to check against that dataset; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Hastings stock in Section 2 is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+ — the program's price band overlaps this corridor's low-rise price points, so run the buyer's eligibility early. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — resales only in Section 2, so not applicable there. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current corridor presale; a specific current presale in Hastings-Sunrise verified on a developer source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a published range of 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area was a resort before it was a suburb.** The City states Hastings-Sunrise sits on the northern half of a block of land the provincial government set aside in 1863 for a town expected to develop along the shore of Burrard Inlet; Gastown farther west became the port city instead, and a small resort, New Brighton, developed on the shoreline roughly where New Brighton Park is today. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **The shopping district has a second name.** The Hastings Street shopping district in Hastings-Sunrise is branded the "East Village" — L'Atelier's listing copy uses it ("a unique warehouse loft conversion in the heart of East Village") — so searches under only "Hastings-Sunrise" miss records filed under the district name. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md) and https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/2556-Hastings-St-E-202-Vancouver-BC-V5K-1Z3/2066401924_zpid/
- **The west edge carries a second plan.** The western edge of Hastings-Sunrise sits inside the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, which layers plan policy on top of base zoning — a redevelopment or multiplex assumption for a west-edge lot priced off R1-1 alone is missing a document. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **The newest apartment building on the corridor is rental, not condo.** Sunrise Heights at 2215 East Hastings Street (built 2020, 37 units, professionally managed) is purpose-built rental — a buyer touring "new Hastings apartments" there is looking at a rental building, and its existence does not add to the condo supply counted in Section 2. https://www.zumper.com/apartment-buildings/p917826/sunrise-heights-apartments-grandview-woodland-vancouver-bc
- **House-street lots carry multiplex rights corridor lots do not.** Under R1-1 a qualifying house-street lot can carry a multiplex of up to 6 units (up to 8 rental units) plus a secondary suite and laneway house in the forms the schedule states, subject to minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² — while Hastings Street commercial sites sit under different districts such as C-3A. The two land bases should never be comped against each other. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
- **The corridor's rapid bus is the area's SkyTrain substitute, and its stops name the micro-locations.** The R5 stops at Nanaimo, Renfrew and Kootenay are the corridor's transit anchors — a condo's R5 stop is the honest version of the "steps to transit" line in corridor listings, since no SkyTrain station exists in the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/hastings.md)
