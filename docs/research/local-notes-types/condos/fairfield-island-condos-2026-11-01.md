## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why this section still matters with zero stock:** if a strata purchase does occur here (a bare-land strata lot, as in Section 2), BC strata housing is governed by the Strata Property Act — and the standard block below is the rule set that would apply. Pure freehold detached purchases have no Form B; diligence runs on title, permits and the flood/ALR checks below instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase in a strata (here, realistically a bare-land strata lot), the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both apply to any Fairfield Island bare-land strata purchase — its report covers the common property, not the houses on the lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Fairfield Island may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. A listing line claiming "no other restrictions" for a bare-land strata (Section 2) is a listing statement, not the registered bylaws — read them on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Fairfield Island complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — there is no named complex at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the current plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500, including the Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan and the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial, D-10 Industrial). Which DPAs apply to a given Fairfield Island parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH and suites:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, which amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024. The suite and ADU figures in the Zoning Bylaw text are confirmed in the program's rules-gaps verification: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and the bylaw's parking rules sit at s. 5.04. Application to any specific parcel remains a per-parcel check. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, items 6 and 7f; Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/fairfield-island.md)

The live page invokes the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw, the Fraser River dike and flood construction levels, but the City flood sources were not opened in that pass (429), so no dike, flood construction level, setback or floodplain statement is relied on in this file. - **ALR:** agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of economic activity on the City's own statement. Properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations, ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment. The OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, and a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. - **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Fairfield Island was verified in the research; the rule is stated for completeness only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/fairfield-island.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
