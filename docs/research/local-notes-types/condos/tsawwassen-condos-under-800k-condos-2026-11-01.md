## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Building bylaws are strata documents, not published statistics — no Tsawwassen building's bylaws were read for the batch-10 research, so age, pet and rental claims are checked building by building in the registered bylaws and the Form B. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, which can resolve disputes including non-payment of strata fees and enforcement of bylaws (noise, pets, parking, rentals). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. In the roster this lands hardest on the oldest buildings — Tsawwassen Mews (1977), The Lexington (1987), Tsawwassen Greene (1990), Royal Oaks (1992) and Heron Place (1992). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Tsawwassen Greene and Heron Cove; that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. Short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Of the roster, only The Lexington carries a stated 55+ restriction in listing sources — confirm on each Form B whether a registered 55+ bylaw exists. The live page's wider 55+ claims for Tsawwassen buildings are UNVERIFIED copy (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. How property tax and related charges work for a leasehold unit on Tsawwassen First Nation lands: UNVERIFIED — no TFN source was opened. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase under $800,000 is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers, where the general tax applies. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band on a property the program covers, subject to all its other conditions. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. **How PTT applies to a prepaid leasehold purchase on Tsawwassen First Nation lands: UNVERIFIED** — the provincial page lists prepaid leases among transaction types but does not state the treatment for this band's TFN buildings, and no TFN source was opened. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame if any unit in this band is a developer sale rather than a resale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%. Mortgage availability on leasehold units, as the live page claims it: UNVERIFIED — establish the lender's position for the specific building before writing. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
