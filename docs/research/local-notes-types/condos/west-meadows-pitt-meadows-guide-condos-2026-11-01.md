## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

*No condo building is verified in this sub-area, so this section states the BC strata framework that would govern any strata unit a buyer finds marketed under the West Meadows name (most likely the Fraser Way townhome pocket or a mis-filed Harris Road listing), plus the Pitt Meadows rules that govern the sub-area itself.*

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — a small Fraser Way townhome strata could fall on either side of that line, so check the lot count. Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — but see the Pitt Meadows prohibition below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a city layer, separate from the void strata bylaws: a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide, so an STR question never turns on the strata's bylaws here. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No West Meadows strata is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022) — https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp — under which agriculture is the dominant land use in the city; zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. In this sub-area the main zones are A-1 General Agriculture (one farm house per lot, maximum gross floor area 500 sq m; minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha) and A-2 Large Lot Agricultural (subdivision minimum 16 ha), with RS Large Lot Residential (minimum lot area 0.2 ha) and R-3/R-4/R-6 Urban Residential in the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent pocket. Small-scale multi-unit housing under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings (2–6 units) where it applies — the only route by which small strata product could appear here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Floodplain is the city's defining land rule:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — this current City figure governs; the guide page's "over 85%" Financial Plan figure is superseded and retired here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; A-1 zoning requires residential buildings sited 15.0 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **ALR:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve; the large farm parcels in this sub-area are zoned A-1/A-2 under rules consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act. On ALR land the Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application where one residence already exists when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The bylaw carries a separate drainage levy — for 2026 the residential (Class 1) drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside a Farm class (Class 9) drainage rate of $1.8524 per $1,000 and a Farm general rate of $48.6824 per $1,000, so the property's BC Assessment class changes the bill materially. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no current residential presale project in or beside the sub-area was verified in the research file. Where a developer-contract purchase arises, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
