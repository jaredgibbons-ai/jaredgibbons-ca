## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- East Cambie is in the City of Richmond, on the east side of the city, abutting the Agricultural Land Reserve along No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway; it is an official City planning area (the East Cambie Area Plan, Schedule 2.11B to the Official Community Plan). The Area Plan's boundary maps are graphics whose roads are not stated in the plan text, so a full text boundary is UNVERIFIED; City data sheets confirm Cambie Road addresses are in the East Cambie Planning Area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- Main roads named in the plan: Cambie Road, No. 5 Road and Westminster Highway, with Highway 91 (the Richmond Freeway) and the Alex Fraser Bridge (named "Annacis Bridge" in the plan text) giving the area its cross-river access. West Cambie is to the west, Bridgeport to the north-west, and ALR farmland runs along the No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway edges. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere, in apartment form — see the first line of Section 2. The documented base form is single detached housing; the Area Plan anticipated residential projects along some major roads at townhouse or apartment densities, and the built strata product that has actually been verified in this area is townhouse form on and near the Cambie Road / No. 5 Road corridors. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These provincial rules govern the townhouse strata above and any apartment strata the Area Plan's corridor densities eventually produce in East Cambie; there is no apartment building in the area for them to attach to today.

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — for a 13-unit complex like Cambie Gardens, the Form B is effectively the whole financial picture. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any older directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Richmond strata reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Standing caution for Richmond searches generally: the 55+ buildings named in the wider program — Quilchena Green (Terra Nova) and Covenant Court, 9260 No. 2 Rd (Woodwards board area) — are NOT in East Cambie and must not be marketed as East Cambie stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Richmond zoning and plans:** the area's base zone is Single Detached (RS1/RS2) under Zoning Bylaw No. 8500 — the RS1/E subzone documented on Cambie Road sets a minimum lot area of 550.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m and minimum depth 24.0 m, with one principal dwelling per lot and a secondary suite as a permitted secondary use. The East Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11B to the Official Community Plan (adopted October 21, 2002; Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874) under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000; the City began a further OCP update process in fall 2024 toward a 2050 horizon. In East Cambie, multiple-family sites are Development Permit Areas under the Area Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Suites / SSMUH:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Flood rules — the Richmond-specific diligence item:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies; Richmond sits at an average of 1 metre above sea level behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way, and the Area Plan requires new development in East Cambie to provide adequate flood protection. Strata insurance and FCL compliance are building-specific checks on any East Cambie strata purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Richmond money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026; annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any future corridor presale sold under the Area Plan's anticipated densities. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in East Cambie?** None verified. The research file records "Notable condos: UNVERIFIED — no named condo building in East Cambie was confirmed," and this file's own searches found townhouse strata and houses only. The strata product that exists here is townhouse form (Cambie Gardens, Wembley, the Thorpe Road complex). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Q: So what strata can I actually buy in East Cambie?** Townhouses: Cambie Gardens (11331 Cambie Road, 13 duplex-style townhomes, built 2005) and Wembley (4991 No. 5 Road, by Citimark, built 2020) are the two named, verified complexes in this file, plus units at 11571 Thorpe Road whose complex name is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/11331-cambie-road/1 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3077365-4991-no-5-road-75-richmond-bc-v6x-0s9/brochure
- **Q: Is Cambie Gardens a condo building?** No — its own listing describes "an exclusive collection of just 13 duplex-style, two-level townhomes." Feeds that file it under "condos" are using the word loosely; the dwelling type is Townhouse. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/11331-cambie-road/1
- **Q: Will apartment condos ever be built here?** The Area Plan anticipates townhouse or apartment densities along some major roads, multiple-family sites are Development Permit Areas under the plan, and a City application already documents an RS1/E-to-RTL4 townhouse rezoning at 11671/11691 Cambie Road — the direction is corridor strata, townhouse first. No current apartment presale in the area was verified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Q: Does flood risk affect a strata purchase here?** Yes, structurally: all of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 with flood construction levels for habitable floors, and strata insurance pricing in Richmond reflects the dike-and-floodplain setting (49 km of dikes, 1:500 design event, dike raising to 4.7 m under way). Ask for the building's FCL compliance and insurance terms rather than assuming from the map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve an East Cambie townhouse?** Schools physically in the area: Mitchell Elementary (3800 No. 5 Road), Kathleen McNeely Elementary (12440 Woodhead Road) and H.J. Cambie Secondary (4151 Jacombs Road); SD38 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). The verified locator result, for the sample address 11671 Cambie Road, is Mitchell Elementary and Cambie Secondary — but McNeely also sits inside the area, so run every address in the SD38 locator. King George Secondary is a Vancouver (SD39) school and has no bearing here, despite a past filing error. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out an East Cambie townhouse?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind you, and the specific complex's registered bylaws are read in the strata package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the ALR edge mean for a Cambie Road purchase?** The planning area abuts the Agricultural Land Reserve along No. 6 Road and Westminster Highway, and the Richmond Nature Park is within the ALR; east-edge properties carry farm-neighbour interface realities (and the plan's goal of a distinct urban/agricultural boundary). ALR status itself is a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The plan is older than the townhouses.** The East Cambie Area Plan was adopted October 21, 2002 (Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874) and already anticipated corridor townhouse/apartment densities — Cambie Gardens (2005) and Wembley (2020) are that 2002 plan arriving, a generation apart. The City also began a further OCP update in fall 2024 toward a 2050 horizon, so the plan framework over any new strata here is mid-revision. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **The area's biggest park is a bog inside the ALR.** Richmond Nature Park, 11851 Westminster Highway, is 100+ acres of old bog forest with the Nature House interpretive centre, the Area Plan conserves it as a bog environment, and the plan also protects Bath Slough as a natural watercourse — the "green edge" beside the townhouse corridors is protected bog, not future development land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Mitchell Elementary carries a heritage ask.** The Area Plan asks the School Board to conserve the Mitchell School buildings as heritage structures, and Mitchell is the longest continuously operating school in the Richmond public system, named after Richmond pioneer Alex Mitchell — relevant context for any redevelopment expectation near the Cambie / No. 5 node. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **King George / Cambie Community Park is the plan's anchor park.** At 4100 No. 5 Road, with heritage trees, courts, playgrounds, tennis and a waterpark, the Area Plan calls it the principal community park, to be retained and expanded on its present site — the amenity the Thorpe Road and No. 5 Road townhouse stock actually lives beside. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **The bridge in the plan has two names.** The Area Plan text calls the Alex Fraser Bridge the "Annacis Bridge" — older planning documents, listing copy and local speech for this area use both names for the same Highway 91 crossing that gives East Cambie its Delta and Surrey access. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
