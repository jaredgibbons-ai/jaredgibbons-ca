## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a building in Section 2 is VOID. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs; the Official Community Plan is "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925, 2017. **The zoning districts for this city's duplex/multiplex ground are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — there are no RT-A or RT-B districts in New Westminster; any source naming one is in error.** In June 2026 Council adopted OCP and Zoning Bylaw amendments permitting up to six units of infill housing on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city under zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units); Queensborough's SSMUH compliance was extended by the Province to May 4, 2029. Secondary suites cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals, and properties with suites pay annual suite water and sewer charges. https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/new-westminster-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Floodplain — Quay diligence (Zone 3):** the City's floodplain study places the Quay in Floodplain Zone 3, the Fraser River waterfront zone, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. For any Quayside Drive, Laguna Court, Reliance Court or Renaissance Square purchase, the building-specific questions — flood construction level, strata insurance terms, and parkade/egress position — belong on the diligence list alongside the Form B. (Quay condos file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/quay-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** for 2026, due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes remaining (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants) on July 3, 2026, and a second 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City held no 2026 tax sale. https://www.newwestcity.ca/2026/09/24/2026-no-property-tax-sale.php (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/new-westminster-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $560,000 (the New Westminster community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $7,200 (2% × $360,000) = **$9,200 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract (Pier West's status is UNCONFIRMED — confirm before treating any unit as a presale), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The RT-A/RT-B correction, on the record.** A research file for this city's Fraserview ground named RT-A/RT-B zoning districts; New Westminster has none — its districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001.
