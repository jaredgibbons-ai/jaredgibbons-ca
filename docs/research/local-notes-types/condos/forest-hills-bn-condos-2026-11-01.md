## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Forest Hills is the residential pocket on the lower south-west slopes of Burnaby Mountain in the City of Burnaby, built around Forest Grove Drive and Beaverbrook Crescent, below Simon Fraser University (SFU). "Forest Hills" is the GVR board sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR report (listing copy shortens it to "Forest Hills BN" for Burnaby North); the City-facing name for the pocket is "Forest Grove," after Forest Grove Drive, Forest Grove Elementary and Forest Grove Park. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS® sub-area — exact boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- Edges and neighbours: Simon Fraser University and Simon Fraser Hills to the east and up the mountain, Oakdale and Sullivan Heights to the south-east, the Lochdale / Parkcrest side to the west, and Lougheed Town Centre to the south. Forest Grove Drive and Beaverbrook Crescent run through the pocket; Gaglardi Way is the road up Burnaby Mountain to SFU, and Lougheed Highway runs along the south side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the attached stock is townhome complexes on Forest Grove Drive and Ash Grove Crescent — not apartment towers. In September 2026 the sub-area recorded 6 condos & townhomes listings with 1 sale (17% sales ratio) against just 1 detached listing with no sale — an unusually attached-heavy small pocket. No SkyTrain station sits inside Forest Hills; Production Way–University Station (Millennium Line) is south-west and is the transfer point for SFU bus service. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- Burnaby community-level context (SnapStats publishes medians at community level only, not for Forest Hills): Condos & Townhomes median sale price $686,000, $763/sqft, sale-to-list 98%, 36 days on market in September 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)

One Beaverbrook Crescent complex (Avonlea, Section 2) is filed by listing sources under Simon Fraser Hills and is treated accordingly. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Forest Hills' 1981–1986 townhome stock this is the central diligence document — Rossmoor's published rainscreen renewal is exactly the kind of completed envelope work the Form B and depreciation report should confirm. For strata purchases here, review the documents under the provincial strata framework; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. All four complexes in Section 2 exceed five lots where a count is published (Wembley Estates: 28 units), so each is on the five-year cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and is void — and listing copy saying "rentals welcome" (Rossmoor) is consistent with the law, not a special permission. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Forest Hills complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Co-op housing is not strata:** the three Forest Grove / Ash Grove co-operatives in Section 2 are membership housing under BC Housing's directory, not Strata Property Act purchases — no Form B, no depreciation-report regime, and no market resale applies to them. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- **Burnaby zoning — current bylaw:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is repealed — any source still citing Bylaw 4742 for Burnaby is superseded. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Burnaby 2050 OCP:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Creek and slope:** Stoney Creek runs through the area toward Burnaby Lake and the homes sit on sloped lots below the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area — parcel-level setbacks, slope conditions and hazard status are property-specific checks: UNVERIFIED for individual lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the 1981–1986 resale stock in Section 2. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No market residential presale inside Forest Hills was verified on a developer source: UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year.
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there apartment condos in Forest Hills, or only townhomes?** Only townhome complexes were verified: Rossmoor (1981), Henley Estate (1984), Wembley Estates (1984) and Ashbrook Place (1986). No apartment-condo building inside the sub-area was verified, and SnapStats publishes the attached figures combined as "Condos & Townhomes," so the split is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md) and Section 2 sources
- **Q: Is the complex at 8400 Forest Grove Drive a condo I can buy?** No — 8400 Forest Grove Drive is Hillside Place Housing Co-operative in BC Housing's directory: membership housing, not a strata purchase. The same applies at Pine Ridge Co-operative (8763 Ash Grove Crescent) and Whattlekainum Co-operative (8740 Forest Grove Drive). https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Forest Hills townhome long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever older bylaw text says. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws on the Form B govern that question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** It varies by complex and the published record is listing copy, not bylaws: a current Rossmoor listing records "Cats OK" and states pets are welcome, and an Ashbrook Place listing calls the complex "petfriendly." Confirm the registered pet bylaw for the specific complex on the Form B before relying on either. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9151-forest-grove-drive/16 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3107651-40-9000-ash-grove-crescent-burnaby-british-columbia-v5a4m3-1099999/brochure
- **Q: What envelope work has been done on the 1980s stock?** One published example: Rossmoor is described in a current listing as a rainscreened complex with HardiePlank siding, windows, doors, fencing, roadway and lighting renewed. For the other complexes, completed envelope work: UNCONFIRMED — the depreciation report (now required every five years for stratas with five or more lots) is the document that answers it. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9151-forest-grove-drive/16
- **Q: How thin is this market?** Very: September 2026 recorded 1 attached sale on 6 listings (17%) and no detached sale on 1 listing. Any price opinion needs a longer window and comparables from Simon Fraser Hills, Sullivan Heights and the wider Lougheed side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Q: Which schools are in the sub-area?** Two SD41 elementaries sit inside it: Forest Grove Elementary (K–7), 8525 Forest Grove Dr, and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (K–7), 2740 Beaverbrook Cres, with Burnaby Mountain Secondary (8–12), 8800 Eastlake Dr, the nearest secondary. Catchments are address-based — the SD41 locator result for the sample address is UNVERIFIED, so run the exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Q: Will the Burnaby Mountain Gondola affect my townhome?** It is proposed, not built: TransLink's endorsed Route 1 runs straight from Production Way–University Station to SFU with cabins passing above the Forest Grove neighbourhood; Burnaby Council endorsed it in 2022, over 85% of 2020 engagement participants supported the straight route, and the project is not yet approved or funded — it must be included in a future investment plan and approved by the TransLink Board and the Mayors' Council. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Three of the pocket's biggest "complexes" are co-ops, not condos.** Hillside Place (8400 Forest Grove Dr), Whattlekainum (8740 Forest Grove Dr) and Pine Ridge (8763 Ash Grove Cres) are all housing co-operatives in BC Housing's Zone 5 directory — they surface in address searches as large multi-unit sites, but none of them can be bought as strata. https://bchousing.org/publications/Zone-5-Choices-for-Families.pdf
- **The gondola would fly over this neighbourhood.** TransLink states the proposed Burnaby Mountain Gondola would serve the 25,000 daily trips made by SFU students, staff, faculty, visitors and UniverCity residents, and its endorsed straight route passes above the Forest Grove neighbourhood — a disclosure item for Forest Grove Drive sellers and a non-asset for buyers until it is approved and funded. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Two elementary schools inside one small sub-area.** Forest Grove Elementary (8525 Forest Grove Dr) and Stoney Creek Community Elementary (2740 Beaverbrook Cres) both sit inside this pocket, and Forest Grove Park (8505 Forest Grove Dr) holds the outdoor tennis and basketball courts — the pocket's amenity core is school-and-park, not retail; there is no enclosed mall or shopping centre inside the sub-area, and Lougheed Town Centre (south) is the shopping destination. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **The attached side supplied the sub-area's only sale.** In September 2026 Forest Hills' single sale came from the attached stock (1 of 6 listed, 17%) while its single detached listing did not sell — the reverse of the pattern in Burnaby's detached pockets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **One complex's neighbour is a tank farm.** Wembley Estates sits roughly 50 metres from the entrance to the Burnaby Terminal on the strata council's own filing — a fact no listing copy mentions, and the reason that complex carries a federal hearing record at all. https://docs2.cer-rec.gc.ca/ll-eng/llisapi.dll/fetch/2000/90464/90552/548311/956726/2392873/2451003/2482312/B173-1_-_Trans_Mountain_Response_to_Wembley_Estates_IR_No._1_-_A3Y3W9.pdf?nodeid=2482313&
- **The library branch is a temporary location.** The Cameron Branch serving this quadrant of Burnaby Public Library is at 9855 Austin Ave, listed as a temporary location in the research pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
