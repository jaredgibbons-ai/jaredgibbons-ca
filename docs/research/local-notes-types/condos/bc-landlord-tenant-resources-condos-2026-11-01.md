## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The operative rules for this page are the tenancy rules a condo landlord or a buyer of a tenanted condo actually lives under, stated only as far as the research file verifies them, followed by the strata position and the standard BC buyer block.

- **Rent increases (RTB, as cited on the live page):** the 2026 rent increase limit for residential tenancies is 2.3%, and the RTB has also published the 2027 limit, which is 2.2%; rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7 showing the exact dollar amount and effective date; only the current year's limit can be used (no carry-forward, no rounding up); a larger increase requires an arbitrator application on form RTB-52 or the tenant's voluntary written agreement; a tenant does not have to pay an increase above the legal limit or given without proper notice. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md)
- **Tenancy agreements (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a landlord must prepare a written agreement for every tenancy on the RTB-1 template or an equivalent containing all standard terms required by the Residential Tenancy Act; both parties sign and date it; the landlord must give the tenant a copy within 21 days of signing; a tenancy can be month-to-month (periodic) or fixed term. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Condition inspections (RTB, as cited on the live page):** landlord and tenant inspect together at move-in and move-out and complete the Condition Inspection Report (RTB-27); the landlord books the move-in inspection and gives the tenant a copy of the move-in report within 7 days; to keep the right to claim against a deposit a landlord must offer at least 2 opportunities to inspect, using form RTB-22 when the tenant was unavailable at the times first offered; a tenant must take part and sign to keep their own right to the deposit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Deposits (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a security deposit can be no more than half of the first month's rent, and a pet damage deposit the same cap, no matter how many pets, with no pet damage deposit for certified guide or service dogs and no other pet fees; a landlord cannot keep a deposit without the tenant's written permission or an RTB order; once the landlord has the tenant's forwarding address in writing (form RTB-47), the deposits must be returned with any interest within 15 days if there are no issues, failing which the tenant can apply through the direct request process and the landlord may be ordered to pay double; deposit interest for 2026 is 0% per the page. Deposit interest owed for years before 2026 at a year-by-year level: UNCONFIRMED — the page states the 2026 rate and points to the RTB calculator, and no year-by-year table was opened. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Ending a tenancy — landlord's notices (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a landlord can end a tenancy only for reasons allowed under the Residential Tenancy Act, on the correct form, in writing, signed and dated, with the unit address, effective date and reason; eviction notices cannot be served by text message. RTB-30 (10 Day Notice for Unpaid Rent or Utilities — tenant has 5 days to pay or dispute); RTB-33 (One Month Notice for Cause — tenant has 10 days to dispute); RTB-32L / RTB-32P (Three Month Notice for landlord's use / purchaser's use — these must be generated through the RTB web portal and a non-generated notice is legally unenforceable; 3 months' notice, 21 days to dispute, one month's rent compensation, the person moving in must live in the unit at least 12 months, and personal-occupancy notices are not allowed in some buildings with 5 or more rental units); RTB-29 (Four Month Notice for Demolition or Conversion — permits/approvals required, 30 days to dispute, one month's rent compensation; major renovations or repairs are a separate process requiring an RTB order of possession). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Ending a tenancy — tenant's notice and mutual agreement (RTB, as cited on the live page):** no RTB form is required for a tenant's own notice, which must be in writing with name, date, unit address, move-out date and signature, and for a month-to-month tenancy must be received at least one full rental month before the effective date and before the day rent is due; a fixed-term tenancy cannot be ended early by tenant's notice alone except in specific situations. On form RTB-8 (Mutual Agreement to End a Tenancy) neither side can be required to sign, mutual agreement carries neither side's notice compensation, and a text message is not a legal way to end a tenancy by mutual agreement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Repairs (RTB, as cited on the live page):** landlords are responsible for most repairs including heating, plumbing, common areas and normal wear and tear; tenants are responsible for minor upkeep and damage caused by them, their pets or their guests; regular repair requests should be in writing; emergency repairs (health/safety, e.g., major leaks, blocked sewers, primary heating, locks, electrical) can be arranged by the tenant at reasonable cost after 2 phone attempts and a reasonable wait to reach the landlord's emergency contact. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Dispute resolution (RTB, as cited on the live page):** four processes — direct request (written submissions only, narrow situations), facilitation, participatory hearing (binding arbitrator decision), and expedited hearing (RTB tries to schedule within 12 days); monetary claims must generally be $35,000 or less, with a limited exception for certain 12-month compensation claims; applications are online with a Basic BCeID or on paper (RTB-12 series); tenants must keep paying rent during the process; orders are enforced through the courts — orders of possession through the Supreme Court of BC and monetary orders through Small Claims Court. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Buying or selling a tenanted property (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a landlord cannot end a tenancy just because they want to sell; on a sale the buyer becomes the new landlord and the tenancy continues on the same terms; showings and open houses need written notice to the tenant or the tenant's permission; the owner at the time the tenant moves out is responsible for returning the deposits, so buyer and seller may deal with deposits in their contract or at closing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Strata rental position — the condo-specific overlay:** a rented strata condo carries two rule sets at once. The tenancy itself runs under the Residential Tenancy Act frame above; the strata layer cannot add a long-term rental ban, because rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or older bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a tenanted condo, the Form B package is read alongside the tenancy documents, not instead of them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — an age bylaw, where one exists, binds who may reside in the unit and is separate from the tenancy rules above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning / OCP:** no single city zoning or OCP bylaw applies to a province-wide page, and none is stated — the zoning and OCP bylaw for a specific condo is the one named on that area's own file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md)
- **Pad rent (parks, regime boundary only):** pad rent for a manufactured home site tenancy is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the rates above: the rent increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months. Where the MHPTA speaks of the decision-maker, the settled wording in this program is "an order of the director." MHPTA rules beyond the pad-rent limits and that wording: UNCONFIRMED — no MHPTA source beyond the RTB manufactured-homes rent page was opened. No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — secondary sources saying March 12 are superseded; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a tenanted purchase is a presale assignment, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — a separate clock from every RTB notice period above. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50% — stated as the standard buyer frame; this tenancy page itself states none of these figures in the research file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much can rent be increased in BC in 2026?** Up to 2.3% for a residential tenancy (the published 2027 limit is 2.2%), only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7; anything above the limit needs an RTB-52 arbitrator application or the tenant's voluntary written agreement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/rent-rtb/rent-increases (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md)
- **Q: How much can a landlord charge for deposits?** A security deposit of no more than half the first month's rent, and — if pets are allowed — a pet damage deposit of no more than half the first month's rent regardless of the number of pets; once the landlord has the tenant's forwarding address in writing (form RTB-47), the deposits, with any interest, must be returned within 15 days if there are no issues, and a landlord cannot keep any of a deposit without the tenant's written permission or an RTB order. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: I am buying a tenanted condo — does the tenancy end on completion?** No: a landlord cannot end a tenancy just because they want to sell; on a sale the buyer becomes the new landlord and the tenancy continues on the same terms. Only once all conditions of sale are met can a purchaser who intends to live in the unit (or have a close family member live there) ask the seller in writing to give a Three Month Notice (RTB-32P), which must be generated through the RTB web portal — a non-generated notice is legally unenforceable. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can the strata stop me renting out a condo I buy?** Not by a long-term rental-restriction bylaw — those bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022, whatever an older bylaw text or a building directory says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the tenancy itself must still follow the Residential Tenancy Act frame in Section 3. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What form starts a tenancy, and when must the tenant get a copy?** Form RTB-1, the Residential Tenancy Agreement — or an equivalent agreement containing all the standard terms the Residential Tenancy Act requires. The landlord must give the tenant a copy within 21 days of signing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Who returns the deposits when a tenanted home is sold?** The owner at the time the tenant moves out is responsible for returning the deposits, so buyer and seller should deal with the security and pet damage deposits — each no more than half the first month's rent — in their contract or at closing rather than leaving it to closing week. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can I show or hold an open house in a tenanted property?** Only with written notice to the tenant or the tenant's permission — the page's rule, sourced to the RTB, is that a landlord or agent must give written notice or get the tenant's permission before showings or open houses of a tenanted property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can a tenancy be ended by text message, or a notice served by text?** No on both counts as the page states them: eviction notices cannot be served by text message, and a text message is not a legal way to end a tenancy by mutual agreement — a mutual agreement is form RTB-8, and neither side can be required to sign it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Does the 2.3% limit also cap manufactured home pad rent?** Not on its own: a manufactured home site tenancy sits under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, whose limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' notice once every 12 months. MHPTA rules beyond those limits are UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md)
- **Q: What if the landlord and tenant disagree — where does it go?** To RTB dispute resolution: direct request, facilitation, participatory hearing or expedited hearing (RTB tries to schedule within 12 days), on the RTB-12 series applications; monetary claims must generally be $35,000 or less, tenants must keep paying rent during the process, and orders are enforced through the courts. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.md) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
