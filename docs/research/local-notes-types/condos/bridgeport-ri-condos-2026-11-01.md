# Bridgeport — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography note — read first:** two different "Bridgeports" have to be kept apart in this file. The City plans the station ground as **Bridgeport Village**, the northern village of the City Centre Area Plan, and the plan **prohibits residential use** in the Urban Centre T5 and General Urban T4 (Areas A and B) designations that cover most of the village core — the core's stock is industrial, commercial, hotel and entertainment buildings, and its strata industrial and commercial units are **not condos** and are not counted anywhere in this file. The board sub-area "Bridgeport RI" is wider than the village: board listings file the River Drive waterfront strip, west along the North Arm, under Bridgeport RI, and that strip is where the area's apartment condos actually are — the Parc Riviera buildings. A buyer who searches only the station blocks will find no condos; a buyer who searches only the board name will miss that the station core is non-residential by plan. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Bridgeport is Richmond's north riverfront area on Lulu Island, around Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line, River Road, Bridgeport Road and Great Canadian Way, across the North Arm of the Fraser River from Vancouver. The City documents give the planning area as "City Centre (Bridgeport Village)"; "Bridgeport" on its own is not a City planning-area name, and no street-by-street boundary for the sub-area is published on a City source read. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: one strip — River Drive, running west from the No. 3 Road side along the North Arm dike, where Dava Developments built the Parc Riviera master-planned riverfront community (apartment buildings at 10033 and 10177 River Drive, townhouses at 10333 River Drive). The City Centre Area Plan describes the village core itself as an industrial area in transition with large and small parcels, an incomplete street grid and former rail alignments. Outside River Drive, the documented homes nearest the village are on the Bridgeport Road corridor to the south (single-detached houses and a documented townhouse rezoning at 10760 and 10780 Bridgeport Road and 3033, 3091 and 3111 Shell Road). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In the Parc Riviera buildings, read the Form B together with the strata's utility inclusions — listings note geothermal heating/cooling and, at 10033 River Drive, water and sewer utility inside the strata fee, which changes the carrying-cost comparison against other buildings. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Both River Drive apartment buildings exceed five lots on any count published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a River Drive building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No River Drive building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Bridgeport Village land use is set by the City Centre Area Plan transects under the Richmond Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000 (the Bridgeport Village Specific Land Use Map was amended by Bylaw 10190), with the older Bridgeport Area Plan at Schedule 2.12 of OCP Bylaw 7100 covering the Bridgeport Road corridor to the south; zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500. The City identifies Bridgeport Station as a Transit-Oriented Area under Bill 47, and states that Federal Airport Zoning Regulations and their maximum heights govern over the TOA heights here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Aircraft noise:** the City Centre Area Plan map states Richmond's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy applies throughout Bridgeport Village, and a City data sheet places sites here in Aircraft Noise Sensitive Use Areas 1B and 2. The River Drive buildings sit under the same airport-side regime — check the specific property's position in disclosure rather than assuming the waterfront setting means quiet. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204; a City data sheet places Bridgeport Village sites in Flood Construction Level Area A. The River Drive buildings front the North Arm dike itself. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Richmond property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **District energy:** the Alexandra District Energy Utility applies to the West Cambie / Alexandra area only. No City source checked states a district-energy utility for Bridgeport or the River Drive buildings; their listings describe building geothermal heating and cooling, a strata system — confirm from the strata documents whether any separate utility charge applies to a specific building: UNCONFIRMED beyond the listing descriptions in Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The River Drive apartment buildings are resale stock; the 2025 Parc Portofino townhouses may still include developer sales, where GST applies. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant only to any remaining developer sale, not to resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase in the Parc Riviera community. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there condos at Bridgeport Station itself?** No — the City Centre Area Plan prohibits residential use in the Urban Centre T5 and General Urban T4 (Areas A and B) designations covering most of Bridgeport Village, so the station core's stock is industrial, commercial, hotel and entertainment buildings. The board sub-area's apartment condos are on River Drive to the west: Parc Riviera – Monaco (10177 River Drive, 2018) and Parc Riviera (10033 River Drive, 2014). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Q: A listing near the station calls a unit a "condo" — is it one?** Check the designation before the label. Strata lots in the village core sit on designations where residential use is prohibited (office, hotel, retail, light industry) — a commercial or industrial strata unit is not a home you can live in, and it is excluded from this file's count for exactly that reason. Verify the zoning and the strata plan's permitted use, not the listing headline. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Q: What is Parc Riviera, and who built it?** A riverfront master-planned community by Dava Developments on River Drive, with about 5 acres of parkland and dyke trails per listings: an apartment building at 10033 River Drive (2014), the Monaco building at 10177 River Drive (2018, strata plan EPS4428), townhouse homes at 10199/10233 River Drive, and Parc Portofino townhouses at 10333 River Drive (2025). City council material from 2016 already documents Dava's development permit for the first phase at 10111, 10197 and 10199 River Drive, with the building addressed as 10177 River Drive. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3164595-210-10177-river-drive-richmond-bc-v6x-0s2
- **Q: How many units are in the Monaco building?** UNCONFIRMED — sources conflict: a listing header describes a 98-unit wood-frame development at 10177 River Drive, while a property page for a unit in the building states the home is one of 35 units built at that address. - **Q: Are pets allowed in the River Drive buildings?** The building directory records pets allowed at both 10177 River Drive and 10033 River Drive, and a Monaco listing describes a pet-friendly building. - **Q: Can I rent out a River Drive condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever older directory or bylaw text says. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws on the Form B govern that question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do strata fees cover on River Drive?** Listings for both buildings describe geothermal heating and cooling inside the building systems, and a 10033 River Drive listing states the strata fees include Richmond water and sewer utility, with an indoor pool, hot tub, exercise centre and clubhouse on site. Read the exact inclusion set off the Form B — it is why these fees do not compare line-for-line with buildings where utilities sit outside the fee. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/10033-river-drive/503
- **Q: How is the commute from River Drive?** The buildings are about 1 km from the Canada Line per a current listing for 10033 River Drive — Bridgeport Station is the junction for the Richmond–Brighouse, YVR–Airport and Waterfront legs, with a regional bus exchange (403, 430 Express, 601, 352, 604 documented). ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The station core and the condo strip answer to two different plans.** Bridgeport Village's transects prohibit homes where the casino, hotels and warehouses stand; the homes the board calls "Bridgeport RI" sit west on River Drive under the Parc Riviera approvals, whose first phase City council documented in a 2012 development permit with Dava Development Ltd. as applicant. A listing that borrows "steps to Bridgeport Station" language for a River Drive home is describing roughly a kilometre, per the listings themselves. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md) and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3166279-503-10033-river-drive-richmond-bc-v6x-0l1
- **Airport law outranks transit law at this station.** The City states Bridgeport Station is a Transit-Oriented Area under Bill 47, but Federal Airport Zoning Regulations and their maximum heights govern over the TOA heights here — the usual "density must rise beside the station" assumption runs into a federal ceiling in this village. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Two strata names in the area rest on one banned directory alone.** Bridgeport Court (3051 Airey Drive) and Bridge Point Place (3111 Beckman Place) appear in no developer, strata or listing source checked in this pass — they are carried in Section 2 as UNCONFIRMED and uncounted, so a buyer handed either name knows it still needs a strata-plan check.
