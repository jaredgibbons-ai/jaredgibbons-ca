Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sea Island is a separate island in the Fraser River in the City of Richmond, BC — Lulu Island (the main part of Richmond) to the south and east, and the City of Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. "Sea Island" is both the board sub-area name and the City's own planning area 12 on the Official Community Plan Key Map, so the City name and the board name match. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- Where the housing is: the island is dominated by Vancouver International Airport (YVR) on federal land; the only residential pocket is **Burkeville**, in the south-east part of the island near Miller Road and Russ Baker Way. There is no condo cluster anywhere on the island — see the bold statement at the top of Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- Market figures: Richmond is outside SnapStats coverage in the reports used for this program, so **no inventory, solds, sales-ratio, median or days-on-market figures are published in this file** — no substitute source has been used. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- Main roads and bridges: Grant McConachie Way serves the airport terminals, Russ Baker Way runs south to the Dinsmore Bridge and Richmond, and Miller Road runs through Burkeville; bridges off the island are the Arthur Laing Bridge (to Vancouver), the Moray Channel Bridge, the Dinsmore Bridge and the No. 2 Road Bridge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- Transit: the Canada Line airport branch serves Sea Island at Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations, with a change at Bridgeport Station for Richmond–Brighouse branch trains. Local bus route numbers for Burkeville streets: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- Nearby areas: Bridgeport (Richmond, across the Moray Channel / Dinsmore Bridge), Terra Nova (Richmond, across the Middle Arm), and Vancouver (across the North Arm via the Arthur Laing Bridge). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Sea Island has zero verified residential condo stock. No apartment-condo building and no townhome complex on Sea Island was confirmed on a developer or strata source in this pass — the documented housing form on the island is single-detached houses in Burkeville, and the rest of the island is federal airport land and airport-serving uses.** (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)

### What the island's housing actually is

- **Burkeville — detached houses, not strata.** Housing on Sea Island exists only in Burkeville: a pocket of single-detached houses on the south-east part of the island, built for aviation workers in three designs; the City of Richmond Archives states Burkeville is the only remaining population centre on Sea Island. A secondary market source describes the pocket as over 300 houses, first constructed in 1941 under the wartime housing program for Boeing Aircraft of Canada workers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md) and https://www.realestatevalley.ca/sea-island-real-estate/

### Excluded — not condos

- **Strata industrial and airport-serving units are not condos.** Strata product associated with the island is industrial / airport-commercial in character, sitting on or beside federal airport land governed by the YVR land use plan and federal Airport Zoning Regulations rather than the City bylaw. Industrial strata units cannot be lived in and are not counted as residential condo stock in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Airport facilities are not buildings in this file's sense.** Land use at the airport is guided by the YVR 2037 Master Plan, approved by the federal Minister of Transport; terminal, cargo and hotel structures at YVR are not strata residential stock. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Templeton Station-area retail is commercial, not residential.** The designer outlet at the Templeton Station area rests only on the outlet owner's own site, which is not an official source — its name and address are UNVERIFIED here, and in any case it is a retail centre, not a condo building. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why this section still exists on a zero-stock island:** the BC strata framework below governs any strata purchase in the province, and it is the framework a buyer would meet the moment any strata unit — residential or otherwise — were offered on Sea Island. The city and federal rules after it are the ones that actually shape Burkeville purchases.
- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase of any strata lot, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Richmond zoning is Zoning Bylaw 8500 (2009) and the plan is the Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 (2012) — "Richmond's 2041 OCP" — under which Sea Island is planning area 12 for the Burkeville pocket. Most of the island is federal airport land governed by the YVR 2037 Master Plan and federal airport zoning regulations rather than the City bylaw, and senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP. The exact City zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/landuse/zoning.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond's Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204 (2008) designates all lands within the City as floodplain and sets flood plain construction levels. The City dike system behind Sea Island includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed for a 1:500 flood event, and the current focus is raising dikes from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Aircraft noise and height:** federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada set zoning in areas surrounding the airport, including maximum building heights; aircraft noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, and the City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity — both are Burkeville purchase diligence, affecting additions, rebuilds and tree height as well as daily living. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Richmond property taxes:** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; this city-wide rule applies to Burkeville homes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current market presale on Sea Island was verified on a developer source in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Are there any condos on Sea Island?** None verified. No apartment-condo building or townhome complex on the island was confirmed on a developer or strata source in this pass; the island's housing is detached houses in Burkeville, and the rest of the island is airport and airport-serving land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: What about the strata units near the airport?** They are industrial / airport-commercial strata, not homes — they cannot be lived in and are excluded from the residential condo count in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: What housing actually exists on Sea Island?** Burkeville: a pocket of single-detached houses in the island's south-east, built for aviation workers in three designs, and described by the City Archives as the only remaining population centre on Sea Island. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Burkeville address?** There is no operating school on Sea Island — Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed. School District 38 catchments are address-based with no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12); for the sample address 7140 Miller Road (Sea Island Community Centre), the SD38 locator gives Brighouse Elementary and Richmond Secondary — both off-island. Check the exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: How does transit serve Sea Island?** The Canada Line airport branch stops at Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations, with a change at Bridgeport Station for Richmond–Brighouse branch trains. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: What should a buyer know about aircraft noise?** YVR noise is addressed in the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, including work to promote compatible land use planning near the airport, and the Airport Authority publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data a buyer can review before writing. The City's ANSD policy framework also applies to noise-sensitive development near the airport. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: Who plans land use on Sea Island?** Two authorities, split by the fence line: most of the island is federal airport land under the YVR 2037 Master Plan and federal Airport Zoning Regulations (which also cap building heights around the airport), while the Burkeville pocket sits inside the City of Richmond under OCP Bylaw 9000, planning area 12. Senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: Is Burkeville in a floodplain?** Richmond's Bylaw 8204 designates all lands in the City as floodplain, and the island sits behind the City dike system (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations, designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m under way). Confirm the lot's flood construction level requirements with the City before planning a rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: When are Richmond taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Thursday, July 2, 2026 (the first business day of July), with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Q: Is there a grocery store or library on the island?** Neither was confirmed on an official source in this pass: a grocery store on Sea Island is UNVERIFIED, and there is no Richmond Public Library branch on Sea Island in the branch picture the research file records — the nearest branches are on Lulu Island. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Burkeville is a wartime company town that outlived the war.** A secondary market source records the pocket as constructed in 1941 by the Canadian government's wartime housing program to house Boeing Aircraft of Canada workers, in detached bungalow forms of the period, with many homes since renovated or rebuilt — which is why the streetscape mixes original wartime houses with modern replacements, all detached, never strata. https://www.realestatevalley.ca/sea-island-real-estate/
- **The island's school is a museum stop, not a school.** Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed; the building survives as a Richmond Museum location, and Burkeville children are bused or driven off-island to their SD38 catchment schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **The airport does not answer to City Hall.** YVR states the airport occupies federal property within the City of Richmond, and the YVR 2037 Master Plan — approved by the federal Minister of Transport — governs airport land use. A buyer expecting City rezoning politics to shape the island's future is watching the wrong government for most of its land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Federal zoning reaches into Burkeville backyards.** The federal Airport Zoning Regulations cap maximum building heights in areas surrounding the airport so aircraft can operate safely — a live constraint on additions, rebuilds and even tree height on a Burkeville lot, checked through Transport Canada rather than the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **The dike is being raised a full metre-plus.** Richmond's flood system is designed for a 1:500 flood event, and the current program focus is raising dikes from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise — the number behind Burkeville's flood-insurance and rebuild conversations, under Bylaw 8204's flood construction levels. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
- **Three Canada Line stations, no condo at any of them.** Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport stations serve the island, but the station lands are airport/commercial — Sea Island is the rare SkyTrain-served sub-area where station proximity produces no strata stock at all. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/seaisland.md)
