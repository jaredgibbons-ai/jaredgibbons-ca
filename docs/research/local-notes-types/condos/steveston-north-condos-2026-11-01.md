"Steveston North" is the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR) board sub-area name; the City does not use it as a planning name — the district spans the northern part of the Steveston planning area (Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000) and edges into the City's Seafair planning area to the north. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. It is the diligence document for the townhouse stratas in Section 2 (Heron, Innova30) even though this sub-area has no apartment condos. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" line for a Steveston North complex (Heron and Innova30 both carry the latter) is VOID in its rental half for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Steveston North complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000) guides the Steveston planning area. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: houses in RS1/RS2 Single Detached zones (Section 8.1) with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under Bill 44. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-north.md)
- **Flood:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Richmond property taxes:** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in Steveston North was confirmed on a developer source in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Steveston North is Richmond. (Parent file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/steveston-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos to buy in Steveston North?** No verified apartment-condo building exists in the sub-area in any source checked — the documented form is detached houses, and the named new strata complexes (Heron, Innova30) are townhouses. A buyer set on an apartment condo in Steveston is shopping Steveston South's buildings (see the Steveston South condos file) or London Landing (parent assembly file). Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Heron or Innova30 a condo?** Both are townhouse strata — Heron (5651 Steveston Highway, 2022, 28 units per listing data) and Innova30 (7580 No. 1 Road, 2023, 30 homes). A Form B, depreciation report and CRF position still apply to a purchase; they are simply not apartment condos and are not counted as such here. https://www.estateblock.com/richmond/6-5651-steveston-hwy-1972993 and https://www.estateblock.com/richmond/15-7580-no-1-rd-122259
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit at Heron or Innova30?** Long-term, yes as a matter of provincial law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, whatever the recorded "rentals allowed with restrictions" line says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; read the registered bylaws on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Ukrainian Village a condo I can buy?** No — Ukrainian Village (8720 Railway Avenue) is non-profit subsidized seniors rental in BC Housing's Zone 3 directory, applied for through the Housing Registry; Maple Vine Court (7500 Francis Road) is likewise non-profit rental and also appears in BC Housing's family directory. Neither is strata ownership. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-3-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf
- **Q: Where is the line between Steveston North and Steveston South?** In listing practice, Steveston Highway — but the board's exact boundary is not published on an official source and is UNVERIFIED as an official boundary, which is why edge addresses (8720 Railway Avenue, 7500 Francis Road, 5651 Steveston Highway) are flagged rather than smoothed in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-north.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Steveston North address?** SD38 catchments are address-based, with no middle-school tier. The settled locator result is Diefenbaker Elementary and Boyd Secondary for 9200 No. 1 Road; Hugh Boyd Secondary is at 9200 No. 1 Road itself and R.M. Grauer Elementary is at 4440 Blundell Road. Run the exact address in the locator before relying on any school. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Can a Steveston North house have a suite or added units?** Yes — the RS1/RS2 zone lists a secondary suite as a secondary use (Section 5.4: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²), and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Is Steveston North in a floodplain?** Yes — it is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain, behind the City perimeter dike system (designed for a 1:500 event, dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m); Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
