## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Montecito is in northeast Burnaby, south-west of Burnaby Mountain, above Lougheed Highway and Burnaby Lake; for City administrative and planning purposes it is also known as "Sperling-Broadway." The neighbourhood sits between Kensington Avenue to the west and the Greystone / Arden side to the east, with Halifax Street to the north and Lougheed Highway to the south — exact MLS® boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: a small pocket of apartments and condos around Halifax Street at Phillips Avenue, at the area's north-west side — the rest of the sub-area is detached houses on the sloping streets below Burnaby Mountain, with scattered townhomes. Attached supply is thin: 12 attached listings in September 2026, with **zero** attached solds that month (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- Transit anchor: Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, an elevated Millennium Line station at Sperling Avenue and Lougheed Highway, is directly south of the sub-area, with bus routes 110, 134 and 144 connecting there — the 144 runs between Metrotown Station and SFU. The Halifax-side condo pocket is at the opposite, north end of the sub-area from the station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- Main roads: Lougheed Highway at the south edge; Kensington Avenue, Sperling Avenue, Duthie Avenue and Halifax Street run through the neighbourhood, and Burnaby Mountain Parkway climbs from the east side to SFU. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For a 1987 concrete high-rise like The Ambassador, the depreciation report and the rainscreen / envelope history are the central diligence items. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The Ambassador listing line "rentals allowed with restrictions" is therefore describing, at most, what survives of the old bylaw framework — long-term renting cannot be banned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No source checked publishes a 55+ bylaw for The Ambassador — age status UNCONFIRMED; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — the current bylaw is new:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) — the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must not be cited as such. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby OCP:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **R1 SSMUH district:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Burnaby property tax:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — The Ambassador (1987) is resale stock and carries no GST. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project inside Montecito was verified in this pass, so REDMA is background only here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condo buildings are actually in Montecito?** One verified strata apartment building — The Ambassador at 7321 Halifax Street (1987) — plus the rental-only Montecito Towers next door, which cannot be bought. Attached listings totalled 12 in September 2026 with no attached sales that month (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md) and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7321-halifax-street/601
- **Q: Can I have a pet at The Ambassador?** Current listings say no: one listing states "no pets permitted" and the current listing's pet field reads "No." Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws) — confirm on the Form B and registered bylaws before writing. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3032191-7321-halifax-street-904-burnaby-bc-v5a-4r5/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out an Ambassador unit?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, so the listing line "rentals allowed with restrictions" cannot mean a long-term ban. A short-term rental bylaw could still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is The Ambassador a concrete or wood building, and has it been rainscreened?** A current listing records construction as concrete, and current listing copy describes it as a rainscreened building with proactive strata management — verify the envelope scope and dates in the depreciation report and strata minutes, not from listing copy. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3032191-7321-halifax-street-904-burnaby-bc-v5a-4r5/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/7321-halifax-street/601
- **Q: Why do listings call 7321 Halifax "Simon Fraser Univer." if this is Montecito?** Because MLS® sub-area filing and neighbourhood geography are not the same thing: listings file the address under Simon Fraser Univer., while the research file's geography places the Halifax / Phillips apartment pocket in Montecito (Sperling-Broadway). Flagged at the top of this file; do not silently re-label the building. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Q: Which elementary school serves an Ambassador-area address — Montecito or Sperling?** Montecito Elementary, for the sample address checked. The SD41 locator run for 2176 Duthie Ave returned Montecito Elementary (K–7) and Burnaby North Secondary (8–12); Sperling appeared for that address only as Early French Immersion. The settled point is "Montecito, not Sperling" — and catchments are address-based, so run the building's exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Q: How do I get to SkyTrain from the Halifax pocket?** The station is at the sub-area's south edge — Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station (Millennium Line) at Sperling Avenue and Lougheed Highway, with buses 110, 134 and 144 connecting there — so a Halifax Street address is a bus ride or drive from the station, not a walk. Do not market the pocket as "walk to SkyTrain." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Q: What does Burnaby attached housing cost, as a benchmark?** Burnaby-wide (community level only), Condos & Townhomes recorded a median sale price of $686,000 in September 2026 at $763/sqft and 36 days on market; Montecito itself had no attached sales that month, so there is no sub-area median (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Q: Are the towers further west on Halifax Street part of Montecito?** No — listings file 4132, 4189 and 4425 Halifax Street under Brentwood Park. They are a different sub-area's stock and are excluded from this file. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4132-halifax-street/501

- **Half the "condo pocket" cannot be bought.** At Halifax and Phillips, the strata Ambassador (1987) stands beside Montecito Towers (7360 / 7376 Halifax, built 1972, 136 units), which is purpose-built rental only — a buyer touring the pocket has exactly one purchasable apartment building. https://www.apartments.com/montecito-towers-burnaby-bc/zczpkhx/
- **Montecito is not Sperling for school catchment.** The earlier confusion is settled: for the locator-checked Duthie Avenue address, the regular-program elementary catchment is Montecito Elementary, and Sperling Elementary appears only as the Early French Immersion option. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
- **Burnaby rewrote its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026 and replaced the 1965 bylaw outright — any older guide citing Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) for this area is citing a repealed bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The new OCP outranks the old plans.** The Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/montecito.md)
