## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is old bylaw text, not current law. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — directly relevant to Ivy Green, Spadina Regency and Elmwood Terrace in Section 2; confirm each registered bylaw on its Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the governing plan is the **Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500** (Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500), with the Sardis plan at Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan at Schedule B1. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000; Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024). Suite rules under Bylaw 5000 as verified in this program: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area, and ADU parking is set at s. 5.04. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/chilliwack-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/chilliwack-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **ALR frame, where a parcel in this band is in the Agricultural Land Reserve:** one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); ALR residences cannot be stratified — a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (SPA s. 239), ALR subdivision needs Agricultural Land Commission permission (ALCA s. 21(1)), and ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 states the strata-plan point. Parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A home under $1.8M is taxed in the 1% and 2% tiers only. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $800,000 (a stated arithmetic input only — no Chilliwack median exists in any source used here and none is implied), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted as resale band stock in this file; where a developer-contract purchase arises in Chilliwack, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Manufactured-home parks (MHPTA), stated because parks sit inside this city's band searches:** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, pad-rent increases are limited to 2.3% plus the proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027. A park home is not a strata condo and no park is counted in Section 2. (Program standing figures for MHPTA limits.)
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Short-term rentals in Chilliwack:** UNVERIFIED in this file — the STR bylaw position was not restated from a City source in the research pass, so no STR rule is asserted for a home in this band. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/chilliwack-homes-under-1-8m.md)
