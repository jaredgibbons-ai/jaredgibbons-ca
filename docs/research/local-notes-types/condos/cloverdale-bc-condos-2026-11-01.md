## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Cloverdale may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the "rentals allowed w/ restrictions" half of the St. Andrews, Calera and Compass directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No Cloverdale building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. On the Surrey 55+ roster held elsewhere in this program, no Cloverdale-community building is on the verified list in this pass — UNCONFIRMED, not a negative finding. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan is being updated (boundary expanded July 22, 2024); the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in the July 8, 2024 zoning changes, so pre-2024 zone names in older Clayton material are not current zones. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house; the City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. Surrey allows only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with lane access required for a coach house on smaller lots and one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. A strata short-term rental bylaw is a separate, building-level check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Cloverdale" means three different geographies.** The City community, the board community (Clayton + Cloverdale + Serpentine) and the Town Centre plan area are three different footprints. A listing's "Cloverdale" label does not tell you which one its market figures came from — this file uses the board set for figures and the City set for boundaries. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **Serpentine shows zero in the residential tables and still trades.** Its September 2026 residential rows are 0 / 0 in both splits, yet the Cloverdale farm feed's visible listings were both Serpentine agri-business listings on 176 Street — a commercial category the residential table does not capture. A zero row is not an empty market. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Two of the biggest "condo" amenities packages are clubhouses, not towers.** St. Andrews' 8,000+ sq ft Club and Waterstone's 15,000 sq ft clubhouse (indoor 60-foot pool) sit inside low-rise, gated, townhouse-scale communities — fee and amenity comparisons against a standard Clayton low-rise mislead without the clubhouse line. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The Ministry file mislabels two Cloverdale secondaries as elementary-level.** Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary and Clayton Heights Secondary carry a wrong level field in the BC K-12 file; their level is read from their names under this program's validated workaround. Any automated school list built from that field alone will misfile them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **Clayton was the community's fastest shelf in September 2026.** Clayton's sales ratios (29% detached, 21% attached) led the Cloverdale set that month, ahead of the Cloverdale sub-area (13% / 17%) — a Clayton condo and a town-centre condo faced different shelves, on medians published only at community level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
- **The Fairgrounds are outside the Town Centre Plan.** The Cloverdale Fairgrounds — home to the Cloverdale Rodeo, held there since 1945 — are designated a Special Study Area under a separate City study and are not within the Town Centre Plan's scope, so plan-area claims for fairgrounds-adjacent property need the separate study, not the plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-bc.md)
