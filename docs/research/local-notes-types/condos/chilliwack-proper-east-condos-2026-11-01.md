## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 1988/1989 and 1993 buildings in Section 2 this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack Proper East may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the Mountain View Estates and Evergreen Estates directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Two Section 2 buildings are presented as 55+ in listings (Mountain View Estates and the unnamed 46693 Yale Road building) — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for each. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500 — specifically its Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan for this ground, with the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 through D-10) applying per parcel. Cite the schedules correctly: the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. The live page's lot-size and zone framing (6,000–8,000 sq ft lots with "R1-A duplex or R1-B small-lot subdivision potential") conflicts with one of its own feed cards (a Lewis Avenue lot "rezoned from R 1-A to R 1-C") — zone claims for any specific parcel are UNVERIFIED; confirm the parcel's actual zone with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. **Secondary suites and ADUs:** under Bylaw 5000 a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite); garden suites are capped at 100 m²; ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite; and no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. The City also levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **ALR and flood:** where a parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), and the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Parcel-level ALR and flood-construction-level status: UNVERIFIED — check per PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all Section 2 buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Chilliwack Proper East was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Can I buy in Mountain View Estates if I am under 55?** The building is presented as a 55+ community. - **Q: The directories say two buildings here ban rentals — is that current?** No. Mountain View Estates and Evergreen Estates both carry "rentals not allowed" directory lines, and long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. - **Q: Which schools serve a Proper East condo?** SD33 schools named for this area and confirmed against the Ministry file are McCammon Elementary (9601 Hamilton St), Robertson Elementary (46106 Southlands Cres), Little Mountain Elementary (9900 Carleton St), Chilliwack Middle School (46354 Yale Rd) and Chilliwack Secondary (46363 Yale Rd) — but catchments are address-based, the live page and one listing card name different elementary schools, and no locator result is on record, so run the exact address in SD33's School Locator before relying on a school. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **Q: Can a Proper East house add a suite and a coach house?** No — only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits: a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²), under Bylaw 5000 ss. 2 and 4.06. Separately, SSMUH (Bylaw 5395) allows 3–4 units on eligible urban-growth-boundary parcels zoned single-detached/duplex. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **Q: How is transit changing here?** BC Transit's changes effective January 4, 2026 moved Route 57 Broadway's Carleton Street service one block east to McNaught Road with new stops on McNaught and Acorn, and added Sunday-evening service on Routes 51, 53 Fairfield and 57 Broadway. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **Q: When are property taxes due, and what else is on the bill?** Due the first working day after July 1 each year — for 2026, a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026 — and Chilliwack levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates.
