## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. This is the diligence package for any strata purchase in the neighbouring Metrotown / Central Park stock a Garden Village buyer ends up considering. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any "rentals not allowed" line in a directory entry for a Burnaby building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Burnaby zoning — the current bylaw is new:** the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (Bylaw No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) — the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must not be cited as such. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby OCP:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **The strata product that does exist here is multiplex, not condo towers:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. A stratified Garden Village multiplex unit is a strata lot, so the Form B / depreciation / CRF rules above apply to it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Burnaby property tax:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a brand-new stratified multiplex unit), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant if a stratified multiplex or a nearby presale is bought from a developer. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

Check the sub-area field on the listing, not the search portal's label. https://www.strawhomes.com/2201-6188-patterson-avenue-burnaby-bc-v5h-2n1/
- **Q: What does Burnaby attached housing cost, as a benchmark?** Burnaby-wide (SnapStats publishes community level only), Condos & Townhomes recorded a median sale price of $686,000 in September 2026, at $763/sqft with 36 days on market (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report). There is no Garden Village sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Q: If I buy a stratified multiplex unit here, is it a "condo" for strata-law purposes?** Yes — under the R1 SSMUH district, lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, and a strata lot purchase carries the same Form B, depreciation report and CRF framework as an apartment condo. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit I buy near Patterson Station?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which SkyTrain station serves the area?** Patterson Station (Expo Line) at Patterson Avenue and Central Park serves the north side; Metrotown Station, the main bus exchange, is the next station east. The south-side streets are materially farther from Patterson than the north-side ones — measure the walk for the specific address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Garden Village address?** SD41 (Burnaby) runs K–7 elementary and 8–12 secondary with no middle tier. Schools in or beside the sub-area include Windsor Elementary and Moscrop Secondary, with Burnaby South Secondary (5455 Rumble St) on the south side; the one locator run in the research file (for 5455 Rumble St) returned Nelson Elementary and Burnaby South Secondary. Catchments are address-based — run the exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Q: Does a new-build multiplex purchase get the new GST rebate?** If it is new construction and the buyer qualifies as a first-time home buyer: the Bill C-4 rebate returns 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000, agreements on/after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and BC's Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully to $1,100,000. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What is the Burnaby tax deadline a buyer should budget around?** 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date on outstanding current taxes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "condo" market here is a boundary illusion.** Garden Village recorded zero attached listings in September 2026 while the towers visible from its streets are filed under Metrotown and Central Park BS — a buyer comparing "Garden Village condos" is comparing buildings in other sub-areas, under the same city rules but a different market record. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md) and https://www.strawhomes.com/2201-6188-patterson-avenue-burnaby-bc-v5h-2n1/
- **Burnaby rewrote its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026 and replaced the 1965 bylaw outright — any listing copy, appraisal or older guide citing Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) district names for this area is citing a repealed bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The new OCP outranks the old neighbourhood plans.** The Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — check the 2050 map before assuming assembly or density value on a Kingsway-side lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **The subdivision is a single 1953 plan.** Heritage Burnaby records the Garden Village subdivision was undertaken in 1953 with a plan to build 500 modern homes on the 140-acre site — which is why the interior streets read as one planned post-war subdivision, and why there is no legacy apartment stock inside it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **District energy is planned for the neighbouring centres, not this subdivision.** Burnaby is creating a District Energy Utility to bring clean thermal energy from Metro Vancouver's Waste-to-Energy facility to homes and businesses, with service slated to be introduced in Metrotown and Edmonds, where almost half of Burnaby's residential growth over the next 20 years is expected. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Central Park is the amenity, and it is at the edge, not the centre.** The City lists 12 tennis courts with lights and a practice wall at Central Park, immediately north-east of the sub-area — north-side addresses use it daily; Rumble Street–side addresses are a materially longer walk. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
