# Central Meadows (Pitt Meadows) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Meadows is a City of Pitt Meadows board sub-area. The City does not publish an official neighbourhood boundary or name for it — the closest official planning name is the OCP Municipal Town Centre, covering the Harris Road corridor, the North Lougheed Area and the Civic Centre, with Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north edge, the Canadian Pacific (CPKC) rail line along Hammond Road, and Harris Road as the main street. City Hall is at 12007 Harris Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the Harris Road corridor in the Town Centre — Solaris at Meadows Gate and Origin both stand on Harris Road — plus the Advent Road pocket at the area's south-west side, which current listings board-classify as Central Meadows (Meadowland Village townhomes and the Meadowlands housing co-operative). The denser Harris Road stock further north (Liv42, 12350 Harris Road, Orion, Cedar Downs) is board-classified Mid Meadows and is covered in the city-wide file, not here. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below; sub-area labels per current listings.
- Sub-area attached volume: Central Meadows carried 32 condos & townhomes listings with 5 solds in September 2026 (16% sales ratio) — the largest attached count of Pitt Meadows' sub-areas that month. SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: Pitt Meadows condos & townhomes median was $662,500 in September 2026 ($566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list, 40 days on market). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Transit anchors: Pitt Meadows Station, on Harris Road in the urban core, gives West Coast Express service to Waterfront Station, Vancouver, Monday to Friday in peak periods — the City states the trip to downtown Vancouver is about 45 minutes. Buses include route 701 (Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place / Maple Ridge East, via Harris Road and Hammond Road), route 791 (Haney Place to Braid Station), the 719/722 Meadowtown–Fraser Way circular service, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus stop at Harris Road on Lougheed Highway. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Civic anchors on the corridor: Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre at 12027 Harris Road, Pitt Meadows Public Library at 200-12099 Harris Road, sxwhekwnes (Spirit Square) at 11985 Harris Road, and Meadowtown Shopping Centre at 19800 Lougheed Highway at the area's north-east edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Central Meadows' 2008–2010 Harris Road stock (Origin, Solaris) this is the central diligence document. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Origin (2008) and Solaris (2010) are in their first mandatory reporting cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw in most of BC — but see the Pitt Meadows STR prohibition below, which removes the question locally. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows.** A City staff report states: "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide, so an STR bylaw question never arises here. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list for short-term rentals. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Meadowland Village listings record a 55+ age restriction — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. The Meadowlands co-operative's 55+ rule is a co-op membership rule, not a strata bylaw, and is not governed by ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs, under the "I See Pitt Meadows 2040" Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022), which designates the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial/residential development. The City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024: lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Floodplain is the city's defining land rule:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain (an older City figure of approximately 86% is superseded — do not use it), and the city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes. Central Meadows is in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes and drainage levy:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The same bylaw carries a separate drainage levy — for 2026 the residential (Class 1) drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales — both Section 2 apartment buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current presale project in Central Meadows was verified in this pass — the rule is stated for any future Town Centre project. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, floodplain location can affect a lender's and insurer's view of a specific property — no source checked publishes a Pitt Meadows building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Central Meadows condo?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under Pitt Meadows' Zoning Bylaw at all, and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. Long-term rental of a strata unit is a separate question — strata rental bylaws are void since November 24, 2022. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Is Solaris really the city's only concrete tower?** Current listings describe Solaris at Meadows Gate (12069 Harris Road, built 2010, 144 units per the building directory) as "Pitt Meadows' only concrete high rise." That is listing language; the verified facts from the directory are the year, the unit count and the restriction set (pets not allowed, rentals allowed). https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Harris Road buildings?** It differs by building: the directory records Solaris as pets not allowed (and a current unit listing records "No Cats"), while Origin listings state "1 dog or 2 cats permitted" with cats OK. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws on the Form B govern — Origin's position is listing-level only and a verified bylaw summary is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road and https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/11950-harris-road/216
- **Q: Is Meadowland Village a condo complex?** No — it is a townhouse/rancher strata at 18960 Advent Road (built 1988, freehold strata), board-classified Central Meadows, with a published 55+ age restriction and pets allowed with restrictions in current listings. Its unit count is genuinely unsettled in public sources (26 homes in listings vs a 20-unit directory header) — UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan is read. https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7
- **Q: And the Meadowlands building on Advent Road — can I buy a unit there?** Not as a strata purchase. Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Road) is a 55+ housing co-operative listed by CHF BC — occupancy is by co-op membership, there is no strata lot to buy in the ordinary way and no Form B. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: How much of the city is floodplain, and what does that mean for a condo?** The City's 2020 Business Plan puts over 95% of Pitt Meadows in the floodplain, protected by a 60 km dike system; Central Meadows is in diking-district drainage Area 3. Practically: ask about the building's flood construction level, the strata's insurance (including flood/dike-breach coverage and deductibles), and expect the separate drainage levy on the tax bill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Why is there a drainage levy on my tax bill?** The 2026 Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 carries a separate drainage levy ($0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for residential in 2026) — the cost of running the diking/drainage system in a city that is over 95% floodplain. It is a City charge, not a strata fee. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: What shape is the 2008–2010 stock in?** Origin (2008) shows a new roof and gutters in 2023 in one listing — exactly the kind of item to verify in the depreciation report, which every 5+ lot strata must now renew every five years. Solaris (2010) is in the same cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What is the commute from the Harris Road condos?** Pitt Meadows Station (West Coast Express) is on Harris Road in the urban core — the City states the train trip to downtown Vancouver is about 45 minutes, Monday to Friday in peak periods — and the R3 RapidBus stops at Harris Road on Lougheed Highway. Driving commute times are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Meadows condo?** SD42 (Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows) has no middle tier — elementary K–7 and secondary 8–12 — and catchments are address-based. The verified locator result in the research file is for 12007 Harris Road (City Hall): Pitt Meadows Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary, both physically in the area (11941 Harris Road and 19438 116B Avenue; Pitt Meadows Secondary is the only public secondary school in the city). Run the building's exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the exemptions here?** Yes — the programs are provincial/federal, not city-specific: BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000, and the federal HBP ($60,000) and FHSA ($40,000 lifetime) stack on top. Central Meadows' resale condos are not new construction, so the GST rebate and Newly Built Home Exemption generally do not apply to them. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/ and https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number changed between City documents.** The City's 2020 Business Plan says over 95% of Pitt Meadows is floodplain; an earlier OCP-era figure of roughly 86% still circulates in secondary sources. Use "over 95%" and cite the Business Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Solaris carries two civic addresses.** The directory address is 12069 Harris Road, but part of the complex is addressed 12079 Harris Road — when ordering strata documents or checking a tax record, confirm which address the specific unit carries. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **The Advent Road pocket mixes three tenures on one street.** Meadowland Village (freehold strata townhomes, 55+), Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (55+ co-op membership housing) and detached houses all sit on or off Advent Road, and current listings board-classify the pocket as Central Meadows even though it reads as a quiet residential street rather than Town Centre. A search that treats every Advent Road "unit" as a strata condo will misprice the co-op and the townhomes alike. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://www.zolo.ca/pitt-meadows-real-estate/18960-advent-road/7
- **The Harris Road rail crossing is being rebuilt under the condos' street.** The Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project includes a new four-lane underpass below the CPKC main rail crossing at Harris Road, with work on the Kennedy Road overpass paused and priority given to the Harris Road underpass — directly relevant to Harris Road traffic past Solaris and Origin during construction. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **A 1,215 m² figure in City material is a subdivision minimum, not a development minimum.** The City's SSMUH update clarifies the 1,215 sq m figure in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 is a minimum lot area for subdivision in the R-3/R-4/R-6 zones and does not apply to development on existing lots — quoting it as a multi-family site minimum misstates the rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The North Lougheed corridor is the city's next growth area.** The research file records the Town Centre designation in the OCP and the North Lougheed Area within the Town Centre frame; the city-wide file records the North Lougheed Area Plan adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026 — the corridor north of Lougheed Highway reframes how today's Central Meadows condos sit in the long-term density map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/central-meadows-pitt-meadows.md) and (Research file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Meadow Highlands is not in this file — and is not a condo product.** The Meadow Highlands Cooperative on Hammond Road is a manufactured-home park governed historically by a land use contract, rezoned toward a new Residential Mobile Home (RMH) zone by Zoning Amendment Bylaw 2991, 2024 after the land use contract expired June 30, 2024; earlier program work also confirmed Meadow Highlands was NOT 55+. It is a leasehold/co-operative park form, not strata condos, and it is not counted here. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/240531/MRNN240531_A13.pdf
