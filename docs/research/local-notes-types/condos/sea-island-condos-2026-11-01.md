## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why this section still exists on a zero-stock island:** the BC strata framework below governs any strata purchase in the province, and it is the framework a buyer would meet the moment any strata unit — residential or otherwise — were offered on Sea Island. The city and federal rules after it are the ones that actually shape Burkeville purchases.
- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase of any strata lot, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Richmond zoning is Zoning Bylaw 8500 (2009) and the plan is the Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 (2012), under which Sea Island is planning area 12 for the Burkeville pocket. Most of the island is federal airport land governed by the YVR 2037 Master Plan and federal airport zoning regulations rather than the City bylaw, and senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP. The exact City zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond's Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204 (2008) designates all lands within the City as floodplain and sets flood plain construction levels. The City dike system behind Sea Island is designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **Aircraft noise and height:** federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada set zoning in areas surrounding the airport, including maximum building heights; aircraft noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, and the City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **Richmond property taxes:** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; this city-wide rule applies to Burkeville homes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current market presale on Sea Island was verified on a developer source in either pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's 3% Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Sea Island is Richmond. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **The dike is being raised a full metre-plus.** Richmond's flood system is designed for a 1:500 flood event, and the current program focus is raising dikes from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise — the number behind Burkeville's flood-insurance and rebuild conversations, under Bylaw 8204's flood construction levels. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
