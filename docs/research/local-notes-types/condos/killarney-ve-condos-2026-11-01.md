# Killarney (Vancouver East) — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Killarney is an official City of Vancouver local area (one of the City's 22) in the southeastern corner of the city, bordering Burnaby across Boundary Road to the east and the Fraser River to the south, with Champlain Heights inside it and the Fraserlands along the river. "Killarney VE" is the board sub-area name for the same area. Exact board boundary roads: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: almost entirely at the south edge, in the **River District (East Fraser Lands)** waterfront — Sawmill Crescent, River District Crossing, Riverwalk Avenue, Rivergrass Drive and East Kent Avenue, north of SE Marine Drive / Marine Way. The established streets and Champlain Heights are primarily single-family homes, townhouse complexes and co-op / leasehold housing — the City describes Killarney as primarily single-family residential, with some multi-family homes, and highrises in the recently developed Fraserlands. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- Main roads serving the area: Kerr Street, Rupert Street, East 41st Avenue, East 49th Avenue and SE Marine Drive / Marine Way. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- Transit: there is no SkyTrain station inside Killarney. Route 49 runs Metrotown Station to UBC via 49th Avenue; route 26 serves the area to Joyce Station; route 100 runs Marpole Loop to 22nd Street Station via Marine Drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 FVR and GVR reports contain no Vancouver rows) — no median, inventory, sales-ratio or days-on-market figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In River District's large towers the depreciation report is also where parkade, envelope and amenity (Club Central) obligations surface. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals allowed with restrictions" line on a Two Town Centre listing therefore cannot amount to a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No River District building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. The established house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), while the River District / East Fraser Lands parcels are under site-specific CD-1 districts — for example CD-1 (566), East Fraser Lands Town Square Precinct (By-law No. 10941), and CD-1 (567), East Fraser Lands Waterfront Precinct (By-law No. 10943), each being a by-law to amend By-law No. 3575. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md) and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/CD-1(566).pdf and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/CD-1(567).pdf
- **Area plan:** the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District) applies at the south edge — a mainly residential community with a variety of housing types and a commercial centre on the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site, with Wesgroup Properties owning the majority of the land; Area 1, Area 2 and Kerr Street have been rezoned with ODP / CD-1 amendments enacted. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — directly relevant to the River District buildings at the foreshore. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most River District stock is now resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any remaining developer sales in the River District build-out. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Killarney's condos, actually?** In the River District at the Fraser River edge — Sawmill Crescent, River District Crossing, Riverwalk Avenue, Rivergrass Drive and East Kent Avenue. The rest of Killarney (the house streets and Champlain Heights) has no verified freehold strata apartment buildings in the sources checked. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **Q: Is a Champlain Heights home a strata condo?** Usually not in the freehold sense: Champlain Heights consists mainly of townhouse complexes and co-op housing, and the majority of the area is leasehold — pre-paid 99-year City of Vancouver leases expiring in the 2080s. There is no strata Form B for a co-op or leasehold purchase; get leasehold-specific advice. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **Q: Can I rent out a River District condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and the published lines for this area's buildings (rentals allowed at 8533 River District Crossing; rentals allowed with restrictions at Two Town Centre) are consistent with that. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration still applies if the unit sits empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Two Town Centre — pets allowed with restrictions; Avalon 3 — pets allowed with restrictions per current listings. For the other Section 2 buildings, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws in the strata package. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/607-8580-river-district-crossing-102848 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3538-sawmill-crescent/1302
- **Q: Is flood risk a real issue for the River District towers?** The designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage, and parcels in it carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. For a specific building, check the parcel in the City dataset and read what the depreciation report says about parkade and flood-mitigation work. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **Q: How do I get downtown or to SkyTrain from River District?** By bus: there is no SkyTrain station inside Killarney. Route 100 runs via Marine Drive to 22nd Street Station, route 49 (on 49th Avenue, north of the district) runs to Metrotown Station and UBC, and route 26 runs to Joyce Station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a River District address?** SD39 catchments are address-based. Schools located in Killarney include Champlain Heights Elementary (6955 Frontenac St), Cook Elementary (3340 East 54th Ave), MacCorkindale Elementary (6100 Battison St), Weir Elementary (2900 East 44th Ave) and Killarney Secondary (6454 Killarney St); the locator result for the research file's sample address (6454 Killarney St) was Weir Elementary and Killarney Secondary. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)

MODE: the completion report says 257 strata units in total, while a presale directory itemizes 225 condominiums plus townhomes and live-work units — the difference is what is being counted. https://storeys.com/wesgroup-properties-mode-3438-sawmill-crescent-river-district-vancouver-complete/ and https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/mode-at-river-district/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One developer built most of what you can buy.** Wesgroup owns the majority of the East Fraser Lands and built the Town Centre buildings, the Avalon Park buildings, MODE and Paradigm; Polygon built Currents, New Water and Rhythm. Cross-building comparisons of construction and warranty in River District are unusually direct because the builder set is so small. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
- **The district sits on a sawmill site, and the newest neighbour is non-market.** The East Fraser Lands ODP covers the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site, and the 337-unit Sawmill Housing Development (rental + co-op) has now opened inside the district — the tenure mix is part of the plan, not an anomaly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md) and https://canadianarchitect.com/gbl-opens-337-unit-rental-and-co-op-building-in-vancouvers-river-district/
- **Champlain Heights' leases expire in the 2080s.** The majority of Champlain Heights is pre-paid 99-year City leasehold — a fact that does not appear in a quick listing read, and that changes financing, depreciation and resale analysis completely versus the freehold River District strata a few blocks south. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **One Town Centre answers to two addresses.** The developer markets 8550 River District Crossing, while MLS® sale records address One Town Centre units at 3557 Sawmill Crescent (Strata Plan EPS4966). Confirm which address a unit's title and strata records use before ordering documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3175717-717-3557-sawmill-crescent-vancouver-bc-v5s-0e2
- **Everett Crowley Park was a landfill.** The 40-hectare park at 8200 Kerr Street (the City describes 40 hectares with walking trails and an off-leash area; the Park Board dataset figure is 38.08 hectares) is Champlain Heights' green core and a former city landfill — relevant context for buyers on its edge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md) and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights
- **Killarney Community Centre is the amenity anchor away from the river.** At 6260 Killarney Street, it has an indoor pool, ice rink and fitness centre, with Killarney Park beside it — the established part of Killarney is served independently of the River District's Club Central. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/killarney-ve.md)
