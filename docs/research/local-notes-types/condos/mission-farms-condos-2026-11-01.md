## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero apartment-condo stock in the farm topic — stated plainly:** no named apartment-condo building in Mission's farm belt (Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Stave Falls, Steelhead, Mission West) was confirmed on any official, developer or strata source in the research, and no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold. Count of verified named apartment-condo buildings for this file: **0**. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md)

Why the zero is structural, not a gap in searching:

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 states the ALR residence rule set the research relies on, and the standing ALR frame in this program is that an ALR residence is not strata product: a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. A farm buyer therefore buys a freehold parcel (often with one house and, where permitted, one additional residence), not a strata lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The one additional residence is size-capped and still freehold.** The ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, with a total floor area of 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Mission bylaws must also permit it. It is a second house on the same freehold parcel, not a condo unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR status is parcel-specific.** A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. It has no application to a freehold farm parcel — which is the point: a farm purchase runs on title, ALC status and licences, not strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both rules apply to Mission's urban strata buildings, not to freehold farm land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44) for long-term rentals in any BC strata; short-term rentals can still be restricted. A farm parcel is not a strata, so this rule does not govern farm tenancies — farm worker housing and tenancy terms are a parcel and bylaw question instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is lawful in a strata, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Mission's 55+ farm-adjacent product is not strata product at all (life lease and co-op — see Section 2 exclusions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing Bylaw 6350-2025 (live guides stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption are wrong); zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, as amended (the City's 2025 housekeeping package amends it as Bylaw 6354-2025-5949(187)). Rural-zone minimum parcel sizes under the current bylaw from a City-hosted text: UNVERIFIED in the research — the RU80 / RU36 / RU16 figures (8.0 ha / 3.6 ha / 1.6 ha) were seen only in a non-municipal mirror of the predecessor Bylaw 5050-2009. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md) and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date — the City's property tax page states a "10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026" (Community Charter ss. 233–234 general scheme; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235 and no 5% + 5% city scheme). The staged 5% + 5% figures quoted for parts of the Fraser Valley belong to the separate rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, second date October 31) for FVRD electoral areas, not the City. Mission taxes also carry a diking levy. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Farm classification (the tax rule that actually moves farm value):** BC Assessment requires a producing farm to meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Water:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of water fees and rentals before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Farm and mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential portions — confirm the split with the buyer's lawyer rather than assuming the residential tiers cover the whole price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in the farm-belt districts was verified in the research; the rule is stated because Mission's verified presales (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar) are urban and are sometimes marketed to the same buyers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Farm financing is lender-specific and is not standard CMHC product — no source checked publishes a Mission farm lending position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Mission farm?** No — there is no verified apartment-condo stock in Mission's farm belt (Section 2 count: 0), farm tenure is overwhelmingly freehold, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 frame; principal residence ≤ 500 m²). A farm purchase is a freehold parcel purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where are Mission's farms?** In the city's rural districts — the board's rural Mission sub-areas are Dewdney Deroche, Durieu, Hatzic, Hemlock, Lake Errock, Mission West, Stave Falls and Steelhead, and the live mission-farms feed's listing at the time of reading sat in Dewdney Deroche. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Mission?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Mission farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City of Mission bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the following tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Are the Mission market numbers here farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Section 1 figures are Mission residential context only (detached median $814,000 and condos & townhomes median $430,000 in September 2026), and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md)
- **Q: Do I need a water licence to irrigate from a well?** Yes — non-domestic groundwater use such as irrigation requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, with fees and rentals payable, before the water can lawfully be used; domestic use is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Who can work on a farm property's septic system?** Only an Authorized Person — a Professional Engineer or a Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner — can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system under Fraser Health's statement of the Sewerage System Regulation; a home or building with indoor plumbing not connected to community sewer must use a complying onsite system. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: What is the tax penalty if I miss Mission's July 2 deadline?** A flat 10% penalty on any current balance outstanding on July 3, 2026 — not a staged 5% + 5%. The 5% + 5% staged figures belong to the rural FVRD regime, not the City. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools serve a Mission farm address?** The rural schools confirmed in the research are Deroche Elementary, Dewdney Elementary, Hatzic Elementary and Hatzic Middle School, with Mission Senior Secondary in town — but catchments are address-based and no locator result was run for any farm address, so the assigned school for a specific parcel is UNVERIFIED until the address is run through SD75. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-farms.md)

- **Old zoning texts still circulate.** Mission's zoning now runs under Bylaw 5949-2020 as amended, but mirrors of the predecessor Bylaw 5050-2009 (with its RU80 / RU36 / RU16 minimums) still appear in search results — get the zone and minimum parcel size from the City before pricing subdivision or a second dwelling. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **Farm classification has a hard calendar date.** A continuing farmer must apply by October 31 for the following taxation year, and the income thresholds scale with the operation's size — a buyer who closes after that date without a plan can lose the classification, and its tax treatment, for a year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The suite rule on ALR land is narrower than in town.** A secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence — a separate garden suite or coach house on ALR land must fit the one-additional-residence size caps (90 m² / 186 m²) instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
