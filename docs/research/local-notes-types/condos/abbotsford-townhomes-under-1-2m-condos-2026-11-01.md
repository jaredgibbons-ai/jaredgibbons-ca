## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Townhomes on this page are strata product under the Strata Property Act, so the standard condo block applies to them in full — the Form B route, depreciation reports and bylaw rules below govern a townhome purchase exactly as they govern an apartment purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. For townhome complexes the report is where roof, road/driveway and exterior-envelope timing shows up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — no Abbotsford townhome strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older bylaw text says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The live page's broader claim that most newer family complexes permit long-term rentals is consistent with the law but its "most complexes" pet/rental generalization is UNVERIFIED as a blanket claim in the research file — pet rules in particular are set complex by complex in each strata's bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age status is UNCONFIRMED per complex until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 2025 — it is the CURRENT OCP; the 2016 OCP is not. Zoning is Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **SSMUH and suites (context for the ground-oriented stock):** Abbotsford amended its Zoning Bylaw in June 2024 for provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas (3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots bigger than 280 m²). The SSMUH amending bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages in this program — it is not asserted in this file. A secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and less than 50% of the principal unit's size; coach houses and garden suites may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m². No suite claim is made for any strata complex on this page — strata bylaws and the specific unit's permits govern in practice. https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (e.g., Upterra, Kings Crossing if bought from the developer), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: hypothetical fair market value $700,000 (a stated hypothetical, not a complex price), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhome is still on a developer contract (the position for new complexes such as Upterra and Kings Crossing), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
