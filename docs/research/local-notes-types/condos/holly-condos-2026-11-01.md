## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These rules matter in Holly mainly for the townhouse and bare-land strata above, and for any future condo proposal on the 60B Street lands.

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. In a bare-land strata like Ashley Green, the Form B is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. No Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Presale rules (REDMA) — directly relevant to Skye.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement, and under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act the buyer has seven days to rescind, starting on the later of signing the purchase agreement or signing the written acknowledgement of the disclosure statement; deposits are generally held in trust. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/ and https://www.presalehomes.ca/blog/everything-you-need-to-know-about-presale-condo-deposit-structures-in-bc
- **Assignments.** Whether a Skye contract can be assigned, and on what fee and consent terms, is set by the purchase contract and disclosure statement — no published term was verified in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. Read the assignment clause before signing if resale-before-completion matters.
- **Delta zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600.** Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps that form part of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; the 60B Street townhouse lands went through City application processes (Section 1), which is the route any future multi-family project in Holly would also take. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it has no lettered schedules and no East Ladner area-plan schedule (the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)", which is the designation on Holly's east side. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction.** A new Skye townhome carries GST; a resale does not. The first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new 60B Street townhome. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Holly at all?** No apartment-condo building in Holly is verified on any source checked. The strata choice in Holly is townhouses (Skye at Holly Park and the 60B Street applications) and a bare-land strata of half-duplexes (Ashley Green). The nearest verified apartment condos are the 53 Street buildings in Hawthorne and Delta Manor. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What is the difference between the Skye townhouses and a condo?** Tenure is similar — freehold strata, monthly fees, bylaws — but the form is ground-oriented townhouse living next to Holly Park, not a shared apartment building, and a presale purchase carries REDMA disclosure and rescission rules that a resale does not. https://www.estateblock.com/delta/50-4732-60b-st-1966641 and https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Q: I signed a presale at Skye — can I change my mind?** Yes, inside the window: REDMA s. 21 gives seven days from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. After day seven the contract is binding. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in a bare-land strata like Ashley Green?** Far less than in an apartment building — the house itself is the owner's problem. One listing records a $65/month fee at Ashley Green; the Form B and budget show exactly what that buys. Never assume building insurance or exterior maintenance is included in a bare-land strata. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3102296-4756-62-street-25-delta-bc-v4k-4v8/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhouse here?** For long-term rentals, BC law voids strata rental-restriction bylaws as of Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. In a presale, also check the disclosure statement's rental disclosure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets restricted?** No Holly-specific pet bylaw was verified in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. Pet rules live in each strata's registered bylaws, attached to the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Does a new townhome here get the PTT newly built exemption?** Potentially, yes: the newly built home exemption is full for homes valued at $1,100,000 or less and phases out by $1,150,000, with citizenship and principal-residence conditions — and GST also applies on new construction, partly offset for first-time buyers by the Bill C-4 rebate. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve Holly?** For the sample address at the school itself, Holly Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Holly behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and properties within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: How is the commute if there is no bus inside Holly?** The transit spine is Ladner Trunk Road to Ladner Exchange, then route 601 to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Highway 17A and Highway 99. A bus route with a stop inside Holly itself is UNVERIFIED — plan on driving or cycling to the Trunk Road stops or the Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
