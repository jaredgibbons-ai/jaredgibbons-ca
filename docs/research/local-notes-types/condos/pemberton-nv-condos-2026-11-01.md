## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Pemberton Heights is a level, grid-pattern bench of single-detached streets — West Keith Road, West 22nd Street and Pemberton Avenue are its search streets — above Marine Drive, at the Capilano River. The MLS name "Pemberton NV" separates it from the Town of Pemberton in the Sea-to-Sky corridor. Nearby: Norgate (east, District), Lower Capilano / Woodcroft (west, District), the City of North Vancouver's Central Lonsdale (east), Capilano / Upper Capilano (northwest). Immediately south across Marine Drive is Capilano Indian Reserve No. 5 (X̱wemelch'stn), Squamish Nation land. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/pemberton-nv.md)
- Planning framework: the District OCP (Bylaw 7900) directs 75–90% of residential growth into four centres — Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lynn Creek Town Centre, Maplewood Village Centre and Lions Gate Village Centre — and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods outside the centres. Pemberton Heights is outside the centres; Lions Gate Village Centre at Capilano Road and Marine Drive is the growth node at its southwest corner, which is why both counted condo buildings sit at the bench's edges, not on its streets. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/regional-context-statement-north-vancouver-district-2026.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- Where condo stock concentrates: the Marine Drive frontage (The Drive, 1330 Marine Drive) and the Lions Gate Village towers (Park West, Capilano Road / Lions Gate Lane). There is no condo stock on the bench's interior residential streets in any source checked. (Section 2 sources.)
- Market figures: **none citable.** SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver rows; sub-area inventory, solds and medians are UNVERIFIED in this file, and per-sub-area medians are Paragon-only. (Research file, Section 2)
- Transit: the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus stops on Marine Drive at Pemberton Avenue and at Capilano Road, running Park Royal to Metrotown Station since September 7, 2026 (roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak); Route 240 (Downtown/Lynn Valley) also runs the Marine Drive corridor. The SeaBus from Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/ and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus (Research file, Section 4)
- Schools: Capilano Elementary (K–7, 1230 West 20th Street) sits in the neighbourhood and is an authorized IB World School offering the Primary Years Programme (authorized 2011); the catchment secondary school is Carson Graham Secondary (Grades 8–12, 2145 Jones Avenue), also an IB World School. SD44 catchments are address-based and were under review in 2025–26 tied to the new Cloverley elementary opening September 2026 — confirm by exact address. https://ibo.org/school/004909/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044 (Research file, Section 4)
- Recreation and hazards: Sowden Park is a District park in the neighbourhood; the Lions Gate Community Recreation Centre (1733 Lions Gate Lane, opened June 4, 2022) offers a gymnasium, kitchen, multipurpose and art spaces — no pool — plus the NVDPL Lions Gate Express Library. The District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas (slope and creek hazards); lots near the Capilano River on the west edge warrant a GeoWeb flood/hazard check — Metro Vancouver warns the river below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres. https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/docs/About-Us/june_23_2022_commission_meeting_agenda_package_final.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/river-safety/about-the-capilano-river (Research file, Section 4)
- Health: Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street (City of North Vancouver), is the North Shore's hospital. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital (55+ file, Section 2)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Park West, the Form B and strata plan are also where the conflicting published unit counts (258 vs 263) get settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line for a building in Section 2 stating a long-term rental restriction is VOID (short-term rentals can still be restricted). **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither counted building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning:** District of North Vancouver **OCP Bylaw 7900** and **Zoning Bylaw 3210**. The OCP directs 75–90% of growth to the four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods elsewhere — do not price a Pemberton Heights lot on rezoning upside unless a specific application says otherwise. Schedule B of the OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/regional-context-statement-north-vancouver-district-2026.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- **Suites, coach houses and SSMUH:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; a secondary suite may be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size. GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3–6 units depending on size and transit proximity — **but** on March 2, 2026 District Council asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH, and the District's Bill 25 compliance amendments were still in preparation in spring 2026. Price a bench lot as a single-family lot unless a specific approval says otherwise. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- **District taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and combined single-family utilities total $2,571 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file, Section 4)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both counted buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $1,000,000 (round illustration only — **no North Vancouver median is published in SnapStats**, so no market figure is used), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $16,000 (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Pemberton NV was verified in this pass; where a unit anywhere is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What condos can I actually buy in Pemberton Heights?** Two verified buildings, both at the edges: Park West at Lions Gate (1633 Capilano Rd / 1632 & 1675 Lions Gate Ln, two towers, completed 2022 per the directory) and The Drive by Onni (1330 Marine Dr, built 2012). The bench's interior streets have no verified condo stock — the rest of the named strata shelf is the Lions Gate Village townhouses (Belle Isle, Mason, Ebb & Flow). (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: How many homes are in Park West?** UNCONFIRMED between sources — project reporting says 263 homes over two towers (with a 258-home plan breakdown), a commercial listing says 258 condominium units plus 13 retail strata units, and listings split on a 2022 vs 2023 build year. The strata plan and Form B are the settlement documents; do not quote a single figure from a listing. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: Is there a 55+ condo I can buy here?** No. The nine buildings on the maintained North Vancouver 55+ strata list are all in other neighbourhoods (Lynn Valley, Lonsdale, Delbrook, Edgemont), and the in-area seniors product is subsidized rental (Capilano House, Housing Registry) — nothing age-restricted to buy. https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-buildings-north-vancouver/ (55+ file, Section 2)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Park West or Drive unit?** Yes, long-term — strata rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Pemberton Heights in the City or the District?** The District — with its own OCP (Bylaw 7900), zoning bylaw (3210) and tax rates. The City of North Vancouver is a separate municipality to the east. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a bench house?** District zoning has permitted a house plus secondary suite plus coach house (three units) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. Do not price in full SSMUH density on top — Council voted in March 2026 to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Pemberton Heights address?** Capilano Elementary (K–7, IB Primary Years Programme, in the neighbourhood) and Carson Graham Secondary (8–12) — but SD44 ran a catchment review tied to the new Cloverley elementary opening September 2026, so confirm the exact address before relying on a catchment printed in an older listing. https://ibo.org/school/004909/
- **Q: Are there flood or slope risks to check?** At the edges, yes: the District designates Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas and publishes property-level GeoWeb reports; the Capilano River below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres in a release event. Pull the GEOweb flood construction level for any river-adjacent lot. https://metrovancouver.org/river-safety/about-the-capilano-river
- **Q: What are homes worth in Pemberton NV?** UNVERIFIED from any citable source in this program — SnapStats publishes no North Vancouver figures, and sub-area medians are Paragon-only. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $1,000,000 fair market value (round illustration only; no North Vancouver median is published), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $18,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The District is trying to keep this bench OUT of provincial multiplex density.** A March 2, 2026 Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH on the basis that it is better suited to development supporting rapid transit — the opposite pitch from most Metro Vancouver benches, where SSMUH upside is the selling point. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (55+ file, Section 5)
- **All of the sub-area's strata growth sits in one village corner.** Lions Gate Village began as the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan (July 2015 Public Realm Strategy followed); its built result so far is Park West's two towers plus the Belle Isle and Mason townhouses — every one general-market, none age-restricted. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4013488 (Research file, Section 4)
- **The land across Marine Drive is an approved development front, not a static edge.** On October 29, 2024, Squamish Nation Council approved land uses for the Xwmélch'sten (Capilano) IR 5 Marine Drive site — mixed-use market residential, Nation housing, and a new Administrative and Community Service Centre. South-facing bench homes look across Marine Drive at that site. https://www.squamish.net/squamish-nation-council-approves-land-uses-for-three-sites-extends-moratorium-on-third-party-development-proposals/ (Research file, Section 4)
- **Park West's own sources cannot agree on its size or year.** 258 vs 263 homes, 2022 vs 2023 — a two-tower building barely four years old whose marketing record already needs the strata plan to settle it. (Section 2 sources.)
- **The neighbourhood school is an IB school whose catchment was just reviewed.** Capilano Elementary is an authorized IB World School (PYP, 2011) inside the neighbourhood; SD44's catchment review tied to the Cloverley opening (September 2026) means a catchment printed in an older listing may already be stale. https://ibo.org/school/004909/ and https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/schools/Catchment-Review-Presentation-Boards-May-20-Revised-Graphics.pdf
- **The hatchery in the river park is closed for construction.** The Capilano River Hatchery (~250,000 visitors a year) closes to the public for construction starting October 1, 2026 per Metro Vancouver — relevant to anyone buying on the trail system's amenity value this season. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park (Research file, Section 4)
