# Capitol Hill — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Capitol Hill is a City of Burnaby hilltop neighbourhood on the north side of Hastings Street in North Burnaby, centred on Capitol Hill Reservoir Park at 229 N Hythe Ave. "Capitol Hill" is the GVR board sub-area name used in the SnapStats GVR report for Burnaby. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area; the neighbourhood spans the summit north of Hastings Street, between the Vancouver Heights / Heights Urban Village streets to the west and Parkcrest to the east — exact MLS boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: not on the hilltop, which is detached houses on a sloping, view-oriented grid, but at the hill's base — the Hastings Street frontage and the Holdom Avenue side, where the research file places the sub-area's limited low-rise apartments and condos. Every verified apartment building in Section 2 is a Hastings Street address. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- Sub-area attached numbers, September 2026: Condos & Townhomes inventory 18, solds 3, sales ratio 17% — attached product moved faster than detached that month (40 detached listings, 3 solds, 8%). SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: Burnaby-wide Condos & Townhomes median was $686,000, $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market in September 2026. There is no Capitol Hill sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- Main streets: Hastings Street is the main east–west road below the hill; Willingdon Avenue runs south to Highway 1, Metrotown and BCIT; Holdom Avenue and Fell Avenue climb the hill's residential grid. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- Transit anchors: the R5 Hastings St RapidBus runs along Hastings Street below the hill, linking Burrard Station in downtown Vancouver with SFU's Burnaby Mountain campus, at least every 10 minutes at peak and 15 minutes off-peak; on September 7, 2026 TransLink extended the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue, serving the Heights at the hill's west side. There is no SkyTrain station inside the sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)

### Not a strata condo — do not count

- **Kensington Gardens — 5740 / 5760 / 5770 Hastings Street.** The sub-area's largest attached complex on paper — the building directory's market report credits it with 125 units, and listings describe 7 individual 3-storey buildings on 5.15 acres beside the Kensington pitch-and-putt, frame-wood construction with stucco, built in the mid-1960s (one listing says 1965, another 1967). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. There is no Form B at Kensington Gardens — it is undivided-interest ownership, not strata (Section 2). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in the sub-area's older strata stock (Saturna, recorded as built 1993 in a listing index) and in any wood-frame low-rise approaching envelope and roof cycles. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Alto's directory line "rentals allowed with restrictions" should be read with that law: a long-term rental restriction in the registered bylaws is unenforceable, while any short-term restriction can still bind. Kensington Gardens' "rental allowed" position rests on its co-ownership documents, a different mechanism — do not generalize it to strata buildings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw is published for any Section 2 building in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per building; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026 (the former 1965 bylaw is no longer current). Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Hastings corridor below the hill is planned as the Heights Urban Village. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Centrale presale sales), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to a Centrale purchase. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — applies to Centrale developer contracts. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Burnaby tax timing:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% after the September due date. Strata owners pay through their own tax notice; at Kensington Gardens the maintenance fee itself includes property tax, per listings — a co-ownership tell, not a strata feature. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md) and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3083307-5760-hastings-street-23-burnaby-bc-v5b-1r6/brochure

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Kensington Gardens a condo complex I can buy into like any strata?** No. Listings state the ownership is undivided interest ownership: the complex (5740 / 5760 / 5770 Hastings Street, 7 three-storey buildings on 5.15 acres, 125 units per the directory's market report) is a co-ownership, not a strata corporation — no Form B, no strata depreciation report, and the maintenance fee includes property tax, heat and hot water. It files under "condos" in search feeds, which is exactly the trap. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3083307-5760-hastings-street-23-burnaby-bc-v5b-1r6/brochure
- **Q: So what is Capitol Hill's actual strata apartment stock?** A short Hastings Street list: Alto on Capitol Hill (5535 Hastings, 2023/2024), Beta (4721 Hastings, 2015), Altezza (4710 Hastings, 2012), Cedarwood (5340 Hastings, details UNCONFIRMED) and Saturna (5988 Hastings, recorded 1993), plus the Centrale presale at 4488 Hastings at the west edge. The hilltop itself is detached houses — 40 detached listings against 18 attached in September 2026. Sources: Section 2 entries, and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Alto's size and year?** The presale record describes 20 condominiums plus 11 live-work units (31 homes) in a 4-storey woodframe low-rise, completed 2023; the building directory records 36 units and a 2024 build year. Both are secondary sources — the strata plan and the Form B settle unit count and registration, and a buyer should quote neither figure as final until then. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/alto-on-capitol-hill-burnaby-presale-luxury-condos/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/alto-on-capitol-hill-5535-hastings-street
- **Q: Can I rent out an Alto unit long-term?** The directory records "rentals allowed with restrictions," and independently of any bylaw wording, strata long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. A short-term rental restriction can still bind — read the registered bylaws in the strata package. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/alto-on-capitol-hill-5535-hastings-street and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Hastings buildings?** Published positions differ by building: Alto — pets allowed with restrictions (directory); Altezza (4710 Hastings) — a current listing records pets allowed; the 6965 Hastings townhouses — a listing records 2 dogs or 2 cats allowed. For Beta, Cedarwood and Saturna, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so read the registered bylaws before writing. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/alto-on-capitol-hill-5535-hastings-street and https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/4710-hastings-street/301
- **Q: How did Capitol Hill attached product perform recently?** In September 2026 the sub-area recorded 18 attached listings and 3 sales (17% sales ratio), moving faster than detached (40 listings, 3 sales, 8%). Burnaby-wide, the Condos & Townhomes median that month was $686,000 at $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market — community figures only; SnapStats publishes no Capitol Hill median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station for the Hastings condos?** No station sits inside the sub-area. The rapid transit is bus: the R5 on Hastings to Burrard Station (downtown) and SFU at least every 10 minutes at peak, and since September 7, 2026 the R2 down Willingdon Avenue to Metrotown Station at peak every six to seven minutes, replacing the peak-only 222 bus. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Hastings Street condo?** It depends on the address. Capitol Hill Elementary (K–7) on Holdom Ave is the in-area SD41 school and Burnaby North Secondary (751 Hammarskjold Drive) is the North Burnaby secondary school — a Kensington Gardens listing names École Capitol Hill Elementary and Burnaby North Secondary as its catchment — but the research file's verified locator run for a west-side address (4715 Pandora St) returned Confederation Park Elementary and Alpha Secondary, not Burnaby North. Catchments are address-based: run the building's address in the SD41 locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md) and https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3093604-5770-hastings-street-102-burnaby-bc-v5b-1r6/brochure
- **Q: Does a first-time buyer get help on the Centrale presale?** On a new build, yes, potentially both programs: the FTHB GST/HST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on or after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. Resales in Alto, Beta or Altezza carry no GST and use the regular First-Time Home Buyers' PTT program instead. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What is planned below the hill that could change the condo picture?** A six-storey mixed-use project at 4269 Hastings Street — 37 non-market rental and strata homes over about 2,900 sq ft of ground-floor retail — has passed second reading in the Heights below the hill, and the City's Confederation Park Community Centre replacement project (3 levels beside the Eileen Dailly pool and McGill library at Willingdon and Albert) is deferred per the City. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The biggest "condo" complex on the hill is not a condo.** Kensington Gardens holds 125 of the units a portal would count as Capitol Hill condos, and it is undivided-interest co-ownership from the mid-1960s — strip it out and the sub-area's true strata apartment stock is five small Hastings Street buildings plus one presale. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/market-report/burnaby/capitol-hill-bn/condos/february-2026 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3083307-5760-hastings-street-23-burnaby-bc-v5b-1r6/brochure
- **The R2 extension is dated September 7, 2026.** TransLink extended the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings Street and Willingdon Avenue on that date, serving the Heights at the hill's west side and replacing the peak-only 222 bus — Hastings condo transit copy written before that date understates the service. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **The west-edge presale wears two sub-area names.** Centrale (4488 Hastings Street) is marketed by its developer as Capitol Hill and filed by listing feeds under Willingdon Heights; Strawhomes records the same split for its units (sub-area Willingdon Heights, complex Centrale, build year 2028). Which sub-area a portal assigns changes the comparables a buyer sees. https://www.strawhomes.com/202-4488-hastings-street-burnaby-bc-v0v-0v0/
- **Alto's live-work units blur the condo count.** Eleven of the homes in the presale record for 5535 Hastings Street are live-work units, not pure residential condominiums — one reason the presale count (20 condos + 11 live-work) and the directory count (36 units) do not reconcile on their face. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/alto-on-capitol-hill-burnaby-presale-luxury-condos/
- **The hill's civic cluster is mid-decision.** The Confederation Park Community Centre replacement — a 3-level facility that would retain the McGill Branch library (4595 Albert Street) and the Eileen Dailly pool (240 Willingdon Avenue) and replace the 1971 seniors centre — has been deferred by the City, and in August 2026 the Province funded 20 new before- and after-school child care spaces by repurposing school space at Confederation Park Elementary at the hill's west side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **The community hall predates the strata era.** The Capitol Hill Community Hall, built 1947–1948 on a triangular lot in Dynes Park adjacent to Hastings Street at the terminus of the Hastings streetcar line, is on the City's heritage register — the Hastings frontage the condos now line was a streetcar terminus before it was a condo corridor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
