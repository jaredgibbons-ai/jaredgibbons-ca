## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this corridor it is also the only document that proves whether the 55+ and no-pets descriptions attached to Suncliff Place and The Cambridge are registered bylaws or just listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Every complex cross-referenced in Section 2 qualifies, including the 1980s Cambridge buildings, where the report prices the next roof and envelope cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older directory or disclosure line in this corridor restricting rentals no longer has legal effect. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Listings describe both Suncliff Place and The Cambridge as 55+; any 19+ or other age line in an old document would be void, and the 55+ claims themselves stand or fall on the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Surrey plan and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026. The former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file; the corridor's 1995 concept-plan designations were superseded through Corporate Report R246 (endorsed December 2, 2024), so an old disclosure citing the 1995 plan is citing history. Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 governs zoning, shown parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **SSMUH and secondary suites (Surrey):** Surrey's SSMUH framework allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer — boulevard-adjacent house lots may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area, so check the parcel in COSMOS. Surrey permits only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **Floodplain at the north end:** the corridor's north edge is the Nicomekl River, and Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands. Flood construction levels are set parcel by parcel, so the flood status of any specific building site is UNVERIFIED without a parcel-level check. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. South-end addresses near the White Rock line need the municipality checked — a White Rock civic address pays White Rock taxes on White Rock's schedule, not Surrey's. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to remaining developer inventory at 2025 completions like King + Crescent and Halvo. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract purchase arises in the corridor's new buildings, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Can a house lot in the corridor add units?** Under Surrey's SSMUH framework, most eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary can carry 3 units (under 280 m²) or 4 units (280 m² or more), and eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop can carry up to 6 — boulevard-adjacent lots may qualify, so check the parcel's zone and Frequent Bus Stop Area status in COSMOS before pricing development potential. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The name is the trap: one road, two markets.** "King George Corridor" on this page, in the City plan and in the board data means 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River only. A Newton or City Centre listing on the same boulevard is a different sub-area with different numbers, and the Whalley towers on this road are counted in a different file entirely. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **One live-page defect is on the record for this guide page (Jerry's list).** In the guide page's main FAQ section, the question "Which schools serve the King George Corridor?" appears with NO answer; the answer appears only in a second, separate FAQ block lower on the page. No other defect for this page is documented in the research file, and none is generalized here. https://jaredgibbons.ca/king-george-corridor-guide.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **The guide research file's UNVERIFIED labels for Odyssey and King + Crescent are a pass artifact, not a doubt about the buildings.** The guide pass did not re-open developer pages for either building and carried them as sibling-page notes; the home condo file verified King + Crescent independently on listing and directory sources (four six-storey buildings, built 2025) and counts Odyssey in the Sunnyside Park Surrey file. - **Two of the corridor's complexes are secretly pairs with different ages.** Stratford Gardens carries 1995 and 1996 build years across its two addresses, and Ocean Bay Villas carries 2003 and 2004 across its three — a unit's building age follows its own address, not the complex name. The Cambridge name is also a pair, not a building: The Cambridge (15270 17 Avenue, 1985) and Cambridge II (15300 17 Avenue, 1987 or 1988 — listings conflict) are separate stratas a block apart. - **The sub-area straddles a municipal border.** At least one listing in this sub-area's own feed carried a White Rock civic address, which changes the tax authority, the tax due dates and the school side of the line — the postal address, not the sub-area label, decides. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **The corridor's plan is older than most of its buildings and no longer the law.** The 1995 concept plan's designations were superseded when the Surrey 2050 OCP update was carried through Corporate Report R246 in December 2024, and the current OCP is Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — marketing that quotes the 1995 plan, or the replaced Bylaw No. 18020, is quoting a superseded framework. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **The royal oaks are a diligence item, not just scenery.** The guide page states the royal oaks and the Semiahmoo Trail are City heritage sites — the trail is protected by Heritage Designation Bylaw, 2004, No. 15280 — which can affect frontage work near them; check heritage trees before planning work at the road edge. https://jaredgibbons.ca/king-george-corridor-guide.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/king-george-corridor-guide.md)
- **The condo side of this road is the liquid side.** In September 2026 the corridor's attached shelf recorded 27 solds — more than any other S Surrey White Rock sub-area — while its detached side was quiet (8 solds, 9% ratio). Pricing evidence for a corridor condo is the corridor buildings' own sales, not White Rock's detached-heavy figures.
