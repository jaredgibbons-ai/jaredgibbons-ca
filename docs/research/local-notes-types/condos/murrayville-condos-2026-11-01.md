## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Murrayville's counted buildings span 1990s stock (Autumn Ridge, Eaglecrest) and 2016 stock (Murrayville House), so the depreciation report behind the Form B is where the older buildings are actually priced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2: the building directory's entry for 21937 48 Avenue records "rentals not allowed," and that line is void for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/murrayville-21937-48-avenue
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. In Murrayville the age question is live but unsettled: Cedar Crest Estates listings conflict between 55+ and 45+, and the 21937 48 Avenue directory entry records age restrictions — read the registered bylaws on the Form B before treating any Murrayville age claim as settled. Langley City's four verified 55+ strata are in the langleycity file and are never re-counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (Langley City file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langleycity-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township zoning and plans:** Murrayville sits under the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. The Murrayville Community Plan is Amendment (Murrayville Community Plan) Bylaw 1988 No. 2661 to the Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw 1979 No. 1842, adopted April 17, 1989; the consolidated plan document records the further Amendment (2025 OCP Update) Bylaw No. 6200, December 1, 2025 — OCP Bylaw No. 6200 was adopted December 1, 2025. The Township's Development Permits page confirms there are currently two designated heritage conservation areas in the Township — the Fort Langley Community Plan and the Murrayville Community Plan — each with heritage guidelines in its plan, and a Heritage Alteration Permit is required before demolition, subdivision or redevelopment in one. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-permits.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/murrayville.md)
- **Township SSMUH (settled program position):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township. A Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under Zoning Bylaw s. 102, as amended; R-CL s. 403 context verified elsewhere in this program is 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot figures. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/murrayville.md)
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/murrayville.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Murrayville's counted roster is resale stock (1990s and 2016 buildings), so GST does not apply to it. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Murrayville was verified in this file; where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
