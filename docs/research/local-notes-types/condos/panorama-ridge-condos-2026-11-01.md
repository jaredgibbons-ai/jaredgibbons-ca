# Panorama Ridge, Surrey — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Read this first:** Panorama Ridge has almost no apartment-condo stock. The one verified apartment-condo project is a new presale, Amson Bloc. Everything else attached in the sub-area is townhouse strata, listed separately in Section 2 so it is not padded into a condo count. The sub-area's detached houses are the base housing form in the City's South Newton plan material. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Panorama Ridge sits inside the City's South Newton plan area, which covers the lands north of Highway 10, east of King George Boulevard and west of 152 Street; no stand-alone City boundary line for "Panorama Ridge" itself is published (UNVERIFIED as an official line). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- The plan area's commercial nodes are Panorama Heights at 152 Street and Highway 10, at the sub-area's southeast corner, and Sullivan Heights at 64 Avenue and 144 Street. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- Main corridors: 152 Street on the east, 144 Street through the middle, 64 Avenue east–west, 128 Street on the west, and Highway 10 along the south edge. Transit routes on that grid include 321 (152 St / King George Blvd), 322 (132 St and 64 Ave) and 324 (132 St), with the R1 RapidBus on King George Boulevard west of the area. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: it does not concentrate — it is a single project. Amson Bloc is at 5937 144 St, near 60 Avenue, in the Sullivan Heights side of the ridge. The townhouse strata clusters are at 62 Avenue (Nora at 12830 62 Ave on the Colebrook/west side, and The Uplands at 15152 62A Ave at the 152 Street edge) and at Highway 10 and 132 Street (Steller Panorama). Sources: the entries in Section 2 below.
- SD36 schools named in the research file: Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave), Colebrook Elementary (5404 125a St), McLeod Road Traditional (6325 142 St) and W.E. Kinvig Elementary (13266 70B Ave) at the west edge; a locator run for 13220 64 Ave returned North Ridge Elementary and École Panorama Ridge Secondary, which is why assignment must be checked by address, not assumed from the school on the same street. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

### Verified apartment-condo buildings — one

- **Amson Bloc — 5937 144 St.** A presale condo project by Amson Group at 144 Street and 59A Avenue, marketed as Panorama Ridge / Sullivan Heights, offering 121 condos with 1- to 3-bedroom floor plans, per a presale source. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/amson-bloc-by-amson-group-floorplans-prices/
- The developer's own page describes spacious homes up to 930+ sq ft in 1-, 2- and 3-bedroom layouts on a quiet residential street in Panorama Ridge, with the project address given as 5937 – 144 Street, Surrey. https://amson.ca/
- Timing, per the developer: Phase 1 homes arriving Summer 2026, with Phase 1 stated as over 90% sold and a Phase 2 release selling; a presale source gives an estimated completion of early 2027 for the project overall. Treat phase timing as developer-stated and check the disclosure statement for the contractual dates for your specific unit. https://amson.ca/
- Amson Bloc construction type (wood-frame vs concrete), storeys, and published rental/pet/age restrictions: UNCONFIRMED — neither the developer page nor the presale source checked states them. Those terms will be in the disclosure statement and the eventual registered bylaws. https://amson.ca/

### Checked and not found

Older low-rise condo stock here: UNCONFIRMED — if a listing calls a Panorama Ridge property a "condo," it is, on the verified record, almost always a townhouse strata like the three above.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, any amount the owner owes, any approved special levy still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a presale like Amson Bloc, the equivalent disclosure is the developer's REDMA disclosure statement (below), not a Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, projecting 30 years of repair and replacement costs with at least three CRF cash-flow funding models; it cannot be deferred by a vote, and stratas of four or fewer lots are exempt. This applies to the townhouse stratas in Section 2 as much as to apartment condos. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and the bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it. No Panorama Ridge complex in Section 2 is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs South Newton, with R-series residential zones and RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook; the research file records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² at Part 26 Section F of the bylaw for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **OCP: the current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026.** The old OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is not current. The South Newton NCP was rescinded by that update and is no longer in effect — land use now follows the OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **SSMUH:** Surrey allows up to 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, plus one secondary suite per house. Relevant to the detached streets around the townhouse clusters, not to existing strata lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: an eligible first-time buyer gets 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. This is the main tax item on an Amson Bloc purchase. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** Any new-home buyer may claim 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out from $350,000 to $450,000, with nothing at $450,000 or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 of value (maximum $8,000 saved) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; the Newly Built Home Exemption gives full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://auxiliummortgage.com/blog/bc-first-time-homebuyers-2026-federal-provincial-programs-incentives/
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** The developer must give you the disclosure statement, and you have 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it to rescind, with the deposit refunded; deposits are held in trust or covered by deposit protection insurance. Assignment rights are contract-specific — assume none unless the Amson Bloc contract and disclosure statement grant them. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 from an RRSP per buyer, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, usable together with the HBP for the same home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** CMHC's homeowner schedule runs 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment; a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Creek rules that reach strata lots.** The South Newton plan area includes fish-bearing creeks — Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries — so riparian setback rules can apply to creek-side properties, including lots in or beside the Colebrook-side complexes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there an older, cheaper condo building in Panorama Ridge I am missing?** On the verified record, no. The only apartment-condo project verified in the sub-area is Amson Bloc, a new presale at 5937 144 St with 121 condos; the established attached stock is townhouses (Nora, Steller Panorama, The Uplands). https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/amson-bloc-by-amson-group-floorplans-prices/
- **Q: A listing calls The Uplands a condo — is it?** No. The Uplands at 15152 62A Ave is 124 townhouses completed in 2005 by Polygon, with a clubhouse — a townhouse strata, with strata fees, bylaws and a depreciation report, but not an apartment building. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/31090/the-uplands/surrey/sullivan-station/15152-62a-ave
- **Q: What do I get for strata fees in the townhouse complexes vs an apartment?** It is stated per complex on the Form B — fee, CRF balance, insurance summary, parking/storage. Townhouse stratas typically insure the building exteriors and common property while owners carry more of their own interior/landscaping risk than in an apartment strata, but the exact split is in that complex's bylaws and insurance summary, not in a general rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a Panorama Ridge townhouse or the Amson Bloc unit?** Long-term rental bans are void in BC since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. For Amson Bloc, any rental terms in the disclosure statement and the eventual registered bylaws are the documents to read — none are published on the developer page checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://amson.ca/
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Amson Bloc?** UNCONFIRMED — the developer page does not state a pet policy. Nora's townhouse listings state pets allowed for that complex. In both cases the registered bylaws govern. https://amson.ca/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3159535-37-12830-62-avenue-surrey-bc-v3x-0k8
- **Q: How long do I have to back out of the Amson Bloc presale?** Seven days from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement, under REDMA, with the deposit refunded. Use that week for the disclosure statement, financing and the GST/PTT math — not after it closes. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Will I pay GST on Amson Bloc, and can I get it back?** GST applies to new construction. An eligible first-time buyer gets 100% back up to $1 million (maximum $50,000) under the Bill C-4 rebate; a non-first-time buyer is limited to the older New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, gone by $450,000) — a material difference at Panorama Ridge price points, so confirm eligibility before you sign, not at completion. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which school will my kids actually attend?** The one the SD36 locator assigns to your address. The research file's locator run for 13220 64 Ave (Panorama Ridge Secondary's own address) returned North Ridge Elementary and École Panorama Ridge Secondary — proof that "closest school" and "assigned school" are not the same question here. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q: What about levies in the 2005 Uplands complex?** A 2005 wood-frame townhouse complex is at the age where roofs, decks and envelope items dominate the depreciation report, which every strata of five or more lots must now renew every five years with 30-year projections and three CRF funding models. Read the current report and the CRF balance on the Form B before pricing any unit there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: How is transit, really?** Bus-only. Routes 321, 322 and 324 work the 152 St / 132 St / 64 Ave grid to Newton Exchange and Surrey Central, and the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard west of the area. There is no SkyTrain station in or planned for Panorama Ridge on the funded Surrey–Langley line, which runs along Fraser Highway. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The governing plan changed in 2026.** The South Newton NCP — the document that planned this area, including a City-endorsed update with density uplift along Colebrook Road — was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026). Anyone quoting South Newton NCP densities for a Panorama Ridge property is quoting a repealed plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Amson Bloc is a two-phase arrival, not a single completion.** The developer states Phase 1 homes arrive Summer 2026 with Phase 1 over 90% sold, and Phase 2 is a limited release now selling; a presale source puts overall completion in early 2027. Early Phase 1 owners will live beside Phase 2 construction — check which phase and which contractual completion window your unit is in. https://amson.ca/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/amson-bloc-by-amson-group-floorplans-prices/
- **The attached market here was the sub-area's strong segment in the last verified snapshot.** In September 2026, SnapStats recorded Panorama Ridge attached inventory at 27 listings with 5 sales (a 19% sales ratio, its Surrey attached "Sellers Best Bet" that month) against a 7% Surrey-wide attached average — a thin, townhouse-driven market, not a condo market. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the area research file.)
- **Highway 10 is the south edge, with the lowlands beyond it.** South-edge complexes like Steller Panorama (132 St & Highway 10) face highway exposure, and the Serpentine lowlands lie south of the highway — check exposure and the upland/floodplain line lot by lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Creeks run through the plan area, and they carry rules.** Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries are all named in the City's South Newton material, so a creek-side townhouse lot can carry riparian setbacks that limit decks, additions and rebuilds — invisible from a listing photo. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
