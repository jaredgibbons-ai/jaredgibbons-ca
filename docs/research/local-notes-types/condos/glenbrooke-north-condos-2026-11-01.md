## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this sub-area's 2005–2013 low-rise stock that means wood-frame envelope, roof and parkade items — read the depreciation report against the Form B for each of the Eighth Avenue and Park Crescent buildings. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This directly affects two Section 2 lines: Westhaven's published "rentals allowed w/restrictions" and the excluded Palace Quay's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" both reflect old bylaw text, not current law — long-term rentals are allowed in both. Eight West and 8 West are published as rentals allowed, which is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as age-restricted in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Heritage — the line that matters here is south of this sub-area:** Glenbrooke North is a City-recognised neighbourhood in the Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, but it is NOT a Heritage Conservation Area; the Queen's Park HCA adjoins it to the south and its Heritage Alteration Permit rules do not extend into Glenbrooke North on any source read. Do not price HCA constraints (or HCA "protection") into a Glenbrooke North condo without a City confirmation for the specific address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/glenbrooke-north.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area; zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — houses under the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series; RS-1 minimum site 557.40 sq. m., FSR 0.5) and multiple-unit residential under RM-1 where designated (site coverage capped at 40%, density 35 units per net acre / FSR 1.2). CORRECTION to the research file: where the research file cites townhouse districts "RT-A and RT-B," no such districts exist in New Westminster — the townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B, and RT-A/RT-B must never be cited. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/glenbrooke-north.md)
- **Ravine setbacks:** Glenbrook Ravine is a watercourse corridor in the City Green Zone; streamside setback detail for a specific lot is UNVERIFIED — a capital-planning and lot check for ravine-edge complexes, confirmed parcel by parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/glenbrooke-north.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility plus City water/sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/glenbrooke-north.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every Section 2 building is a resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to these resale buildings. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Glenbrooke North was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** CMHC's published premium range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What condo stock actually exists in Glenbrooke North?** Four verified low-rise apartment buildings/complexes in one pocket: Eight West (85 Eighth Avenue), 8 West (55 Eighth Avenue), The Winchester (675 Park Crescent) and Westhaven (625 Park Crescent) — plus townhouse units at The Winchester and at the unnamed 600 Park Crescent complex. There is no tower in the sub-area. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Are Eight West and 8 West the same building?** No — they are two addresses, 85 Eighth Avenue and 55 Eighth Avenue, but listings and directories swap the names Eight West / Eightwest / 8 West between them. Work from the street address and the strata documents for that address, never from the name alone. https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/85-eighth-avenue/208 and http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/8-west-55-eighth-ave
- **Q: How old are the buildings, and why do the years disagree?** The cluster is 2005–2013 stock: Westhaven 2005, The Winchester 2006, and the two Eighth Avenue buildings at the end of the run. For both Eighth Avenue buildings the sources split 2012 vs 2013 (current listings mostly 2012; the building directory 2013; one listing says "built in 2012/2013") — the strata plan registration settles it; treat the year as 2012/2013 until then. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eight-west-85-eighth-ave and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/westhaven-625-park-crescent
- **Q: Can I rent out a Glenbrooke North condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. That voids the restriction half of Westhaven's published "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line; Eight West and 8 West are published as rentals allowed in any case. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published lines: Eight West — pets allowed, with unit listings recording cats OK; 8 West — pets allowed; Westhaven — pets allowed with restrictions; The Winchester — unit listings record cats OK, and a townhouse listing in the complex records 2 pets OK. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC — the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern for the specific building. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/eight-west-85-eighth-ave and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/westhaven-625-park-crescent and https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/675-park-crescent/108
