# Local Notes — CONDO program summary (all batches, November 1, 2026)

**Program:** one local-notes file per researched area and sub-area for the region's apartment-condo stock — Fraser Valley, Metro Vancouver, the North Shore and the Sea-to-Sky/eastern-valley edge — built from the area-research program's 503 research files (`~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/`, batches 1–18 + missing/).

**Files:** 481 condos files in `~/workspace/local-notes/condos/` (this summary is the 482nd file in the folder), delivered in 21 batch zips (`condos-batch-1.zip` … `condos-batch-21.zip`). Every file has the same five sections: (1) streets and boundaries, (2) buildings and complexes, (3) rules that affect condos, (4) what buyers ask, (5) local details a buyer would not know — with a source URL after every fact and UNCONFIRMED wherever no source settles a point.

**Coverage (disk-diffed Nov 1, 2026):** 503 research slugs; 478 have an exactly-named condos file; the other 25 are "-guide" variant slugs whose base area has the file (e.g. `abbotsford-east-guide` → the `abbotsford-east` file). Three condos files exist for areas whose research file is the guide variant (aldergrove, fort-langley, how-to-buy-a-mobile-home-in-bc). **No researched area remains without a condos file.**

**Headline count: 583 verified named apartment-condo buildings/complexes region-wide**, each counted once, in its home (sub-area) file. Method: assembly/city files contribute only the home-file components they itemize; variant, topic and price-band files contribute 0; where a later home file superseded an assembly figure, the home file governs and the adjustment is listed below the table. This is a sum of verified entries, not a census — several downtown sets are expressly selective, and the Coquitlam assembly covers only the sub-areas that have files (13 of 23 board sub-areas have none; their absence is not claimed as zero stock).

## Headline findings

- **Tenure traps are the region's recurring pattern, and every file excludes them with the reason stated:** undivided-interest co-ownerships (The Lee Building, Kensington Gardens in Burnaby, Edgewater Estates in Lynnmour — no strata corporation, no Form B); corporation share purchases (Chesterfield Manor in North Vancouver, Irving Apartments Ltd. in Burnaby); leasehold in five flavours — City of Vancouver prepaid 99-year (False Creek South, Champlain Heights), SFU Community Trust 99-year (all 7 UniverCity buildings), UBC leasehold (Tapestry at Wesbrook Village), TFN leasehold (Boardwalk / Tsawwassen Landing), and First Nations Lease (Sardis South's apartment-form stock — Skynest, The Yearling — plus Mayfair Court in South Arm on a ~63-year remaining leasehold); co-ops, life leases, non-market and subsidized rental, and pad-rental parks.
- **The building directory's "rentals not allowed" lines are void** under the November 24, 2022 rental-bylaw rule — flagged in-entry wherever they appear, in every batch. The same directory repeats an identical "22 units" for three different Morgan Creek buildings (Edgewater, Kingston House, Tehama); all three counts are UNCONFIRMED in the file and the figure is adopted nowhere — a standing data-quality warning.
- **Verified stock concentrates in a few shelves** (Vancouver, Burnaby's Brentwood/Forest Glen corridors, New Westminster, Surrey's King George corridor and City Centre towers), while most rural, FVRD and neighbourhood files honestly report **zero** verified apartment-condo stock at the top of Section 2 — stated plainly, never padded.
- **Port Moody is complete:** all four board sub-areas have home files, and Platform by Aragon was verified fresh off the developer's own page in C20 and counted with its queue history visible — the same treatment Ocean Park Place received in C16.

- **SW Marine filing conflict** — Gulf View Court (8772 SW Marine Dr) is filed by MLS under Marpole; the file follows the research file's street geography (west of Granville) and counts it in SW Marine, with the conflict stated at the top (see open items).
