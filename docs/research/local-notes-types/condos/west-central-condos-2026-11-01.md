Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "West Central" is a Paragon/board sub-area name only, not a City of Maple Ridge planning name; the City does not list "West Central" among its neighbourhoods and publishes no boundary for it — UNVERIFIED as an official boundary. Working scope from the live page only: inventory between 207 Street and 222 Street, with corridors Lougheed Highway, Dewdney Trunk Road, Abernethy Way, 207 Street, 216 Street and 222 Street. Treat that as the page's working scope, never as a boundary citation. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- Seam warning: several buildings that current listings classify as West Central carry street addresses at or just east of 222 Street (the Era, Emerson, Inspire and 223 North addresses in Section 2 are 222–223 Street addresses). The board's listing classification and the live page's 207–222 Street working scope do not line up cleanly at the east edge — the same seam the research file flags for Eric Langton Elementary at 12138 Edge Street, which the neighbouring east-central page also names. Verify the sub-area classification for a specific unit in Paragon, not from the street number alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: the Dewdney Trunk Road / 222–224 Street downtown-west cluster (Falcon Manor, Era, The 222, Canuck Plaza) and the 122 Avenue cluster north of it (Emerson, Magnolia Gate, Inspire), plus low-rise stock along Lougheed Highway (223 North). West of 222 Street proper, the confirmed named stock in this pass is thin — Falcon Manor at 22150 Dewdney Trunk Road is the clearest in-scope example. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- West Central carried the second-largest attached inventory of any Maple Ridge sub-area in September 2026: 78 active "Condos & Townhomes" listings and 10 sales (13% sales ratio), SnapStats GVR September 2026. Maple Ridge's community-level attached median that month was $525,000; SnapStats publishes no sub-area median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- Transit anchors: Maple Meadows Station (20010 Dunn Avenue) serves the west side and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) sits at the east edge; both run weekday West Coast Express trains between Waterfront and Mission City. Haney Place Exchange and the R3 RapidBus are in the Town Centre to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59); the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In West Central this is the document that resolves the open questions in Section 2 (Era's unit counts, Emerson's construction, Inspire's per-building completion). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any West Central directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Section 2 building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No West Central building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; the standard house zone is RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%). The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research files (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an OCP update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. The Town Centre Area Plan covers the downtown core at the sub-area's east edge, and Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — status flag:** the batch-8 research file describes the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan as adopted by Council, directing growth around future BRT stations along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road. That adoption wording is CONTESTED in this program: the plan was NOT adopted on January 27, 2026, so do not present it as an adopted plan or price a corridor lot on it as settled policy — confirm the plan's current status in a current City document before relying on it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **Flood and escarpment rules on the south and east edges:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits secondary suites on floodplain properties; the escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and a geotechnical report is required within 300 m of the escarpment crest. Which West Central lots are affected is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED at sub-area level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (relevant to Inspire's developer sales and any never-occupied Era units — confirm occupancy history), not ordinary resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — the CRA date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for developer-contract purchases at Inspire (Building 3 was marketed with an estimated January 2027 completion) and any future Era phases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's east edge is a classification seam, not a line on a City map.** Era, Emerson, Inspire and 223 North all sit at 222–223 Street addresses yet are classified West Central in current listings, while the live page describes the sub-area as ending at 222 Street. Document searches and comparable sets built on street numbers alone will split this cluster in two. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md); sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Era contains its own rental building.** Phase 1 of the Era project mixes strata condos with 49 rental apartments in the same development — a unit's building and tenure, not the project name, decides whether the buyer gets a Form B, a depreciation report and a strata vote, or a tenancy. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/era-maple-ridge-phase-1-presale-maple-ridge-town-centre-condos/
- **The biggest "townhome" complex at the west edge is not strata at all.** Brookside Gardens (20834 Dewdney Trunk Road, 44 units, built 1978) is a rental complex held as an investment property — it inflates the area's apparent attached stock and never trades unit by unit. https://goodmanreport.com/content/BRO%2020834%20Dewdney%20Trunkl_web.pdf
- **West Central's detached market was the city's slowest in September 2026.** 66 detached listings produced 2 sales (3% sales ratio, the lowest of any Maple Ridge sub-area with a sale that month) while attached product moved at 13% — the sub-area's near-term demand signal is in its condos, not its houses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **There is no City recreation centre or library branch inside West Central.** The Maple Ridge Leisure Centre (11925 Haney Place) and Maple Ridge Public Library (130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road) are both in the Town Centre to the east — "walk to the leisure centre" is true only for the seam buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **Maple Ridge's OCP citation is unsettled in current sources** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs 7998-2024, update adopted November 25, 2025) — quote planning policy for a specific building from a current City document, not from memory. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
