## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The condo rules below apply to Surrey's urban stock in the area files. On farm parcels themselves, the ALR block governs first — it is placed first here because it is the reason this file has no buildings.

- **ALR stratification bar:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). There is no Form B, no strata council and no depreciation report for a farm home — diligence runs through title, the ALC record, City zoning and BC Assessment farm class instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **ALR principal residence:** one principal residence per parcel is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. Replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **ALR additional residence:** one additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **ALR short-term accommodation:** whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9):** the farm operation must meet the minimum income threshold — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Parcel-level ALR status:** UNVERIFIED as a check method in this pass — a direct re-open of the ALC's ALR maps page returned a 429 rate-limit error and was not retried. Confirm a parcel's ALR status through the ALC record and title before pricing it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Applies to urban Surrey resales; a farm parcel has no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No farm-parcel application: ALR dwellings are not strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. **SSMUH:** Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City's SSMUH page states the framework applies to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer, and the changes do NOT retroactively legalize an unpermitted suite. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules govern. Surrey's urban suite rule (one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house) is stated for contrast — on ALR land the suite must be inside the principal residence. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures. Farm-class tax treatment is a BC Assessment position (see the Class 9 thresholds above), not a City rate. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey, non-ALR layer):** only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host, tenants cannot operate one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. On ALR land the ALC approval / agri-tourism layer above applies first. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. Whether a new farm residence purchase qualifies turns on its own facts — confirm with a tax professional; no farm-specific GST claim is made here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Farm / mixed-use parcels can split residential and non-residential value — confirm the split with a lawyer/notary before relying on any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for urban presales, not for farm-parcel sales. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Farm financing is commonly commercial / agricultural lending rather than insured residential lending — lender-specific: UNCONFIRMED as a general farm rule. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Surrey farm?** No — not as strata. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), so farm housing is freehold: one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one small additional residence per parcel. Condo product under the Surrey name is urban stock in the area files. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single Surrey farm belt in the sources used here: the live feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City of Surrey bylaws must also permit it; anything beyond that requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: I want to live in the old house while I build the new one — allowed?** Only with ALC approval. Replacing a principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built; demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not need that approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Surrey farm house?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR, renting out the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence can be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property. The City separately requires an owner-host living at the property with a $358/year licence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: Are the Surrey market numbers in this file farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Section 1 figures are Surrey residential context only (detached median $1,215,000; Condos & Townhomes median $512,944 in September 2026), and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: Can Surrey's SSMUH rules add units to my farm parcel?** No. The SSMUH framework is for eligible urban lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on ALR land the ALC rules govern how many residences a parcel may have. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: How thin is the Surrey farm market?** Thin and parcel-specific: the Surrey-wide farm feed showed 3 listings at reading, and the acreage-associated Port Kells sub-area recorded 4 detached listings and 0 sales in September 2026.
