- Grandview-Woodland runs from Clark Drive (west) to Kamloops Street (east), East 12th Avenue / Grandview Highway (south) to Burrard Inlet (north) — the boundaries of the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan study area. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — decisive in this area's 1970s–1990s low-rise stock (Woodland Place 1976, Life on the Drive 1991), where envelope and parkade items dominate. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the small low-rise buildings here sit close to that line, so confirm lot count on the Form B before assuming a report exists. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed is void to that extent — older Commercial Drive low-rises are exactly where stale "no rentals" lines survive in directories. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan is the area's plan (the Vancouver Plan sits over it citywide); zoning under By-law No. 3575 is predominantly the R1-1 District Schedule on the house streets (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), with RT-family districts on parts of the older stock — the research file names RT districts generally; the specific RT sub-district for a given street: UNVERIFIED. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **District energy charges:** no source checked states a district-energy utility charge for a Section 2 building — applicability and amount: UNCONFIRMED; check the Form B.
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant to first resales at ACE (2026). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+ — the program's value ceiling is the operative number for much of this area's low-rise stock. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current corridor presale (e.g. the East Broadway project noted in Section 2 if it proceeds to sale as strata). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I rent out a Commercial Drive low-rise condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, whatever an older directory line says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to any unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Bohème in Grandview-Woodland or Hastings?** Both labels appear: listings file 1588 East Hastings Street under the board's Hastings sub-area, while the developer source places the building in the Grandview Woodlands neighbourhood and East Hastings is this area's main street under the Community Plan boundaries used here. It is counted once, in this file. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/1588-hastings-street-boheme-vancouver/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Q: What is the Marquee on the Drive building?** A 58-unit, 2014 condominium on the former Van East Cinema site at 2250 Commercial Drive — more than 75 percent of the cinema structure was reused, including the Commercial Drive façade. https://building.ca/vancouver-developer-launches-new-condo-recycled-from-old-cinema/
- **Q: How old is the typical stock here?** The counted buildings span 1976 (Woodland Place) and 1991 (Life on the Drive) low-rise strata through 2014–2015 corridor product (Marquee, Bohème) to ACE in 2026 — the depreciation report matters more at the old end; the GST-on-new question matters at the new end. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&sortby=levels&page=127
- **Q: Which schools serve an address here?** SD39 catchments are address-based. Schools located in the area include Lord Nelson Elementary (1355 Garden Drive), χpey̓ Elementary (1950 East Hastings Street), Britannia Elementary (1110 Cotton Drive) and Britannia Secondary (1001 Cotton Drive) — one of the few Vancouver-east areas in this batch with a secondary school inside it. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Q: What is Britannia, beyond the school?** The Britannia Community Services Centre combines the library, ice rink, pool and fitness centre for the area, and the Britannia Renewal project is the City's current renewal program for the site — the civic centre of gravity for the north half of the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Q: How is transit, honestly?** Three layers: the R5 RapidBus on Hastings (stop at Hastings and Commercial), route 20 on Commercial Drive and Victoria Drive, and Commercial-Broadway Station at the south-west edge for SkyTrain. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Q: Are the new East Broadway buildings condos or rentals?** Both exist side by side: Hub Place at 1649 East Broadway is a 12-storey purpose-built rental (excluded here), while Popolo at 2235 East Broadway is an 81-unit condominium building. Tenure is building-specific on this corridor — never assume from the streetscape. https://liv.rent/blog/renters/apartments-near-commercial-broadway-station/
- **Q: Does Trout Lake factor into a Grandview-Woodland purchase?** Trout Lake sits at the area's southern edge by East 12th Avenue — the research file places it at the edge of the area; homes at the south end market against it, but a specific unit's walking access is address-specific and UNCONFIRMED here. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A cinema became a condo without leaving.** Marquee on the Drive reused more than 75 percent of the Van East Cinema's structure — the heritage-feeling façade on Commercial Drive is the old theatre, not a replica. https://building.ca/vancouver-developer-launches-new-condo-recycled-from-old-cinema/
- **Bohème is two tenures in one envelope.** The complex at 1588 East Hastings holds 82 strata condos plus a separate 20-unit rental air-space parcel — "Bohème" listings and "Bohème" rentals are different legal products at the same address. https://goodmanreport.com/off-market/1049-boheme-1588-east-hastings-street-vancouver/
- **The area plan is a named plan, not a vibe.** The Grandview-Woodland Community Plan (with the citywide Vancouver Plan over it) is the document that sets the low-scale, early-1900s character the area markets on — and the reason corridor density concentrates on Commercial, Hastings and Broadway instead of the interior streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **χpey̓ Elementary carries an Indigenous name in the district directory.** The school at 1950 East Hastings Street is listed by the VSB under its Musqueam name — a detail that trips up address searches run under older school names. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/grandview-woodland.md)
- **Popolo's completion year is genuinely unsettled in public sources.** The rental-guide source states outright that sources disagree on when the 81-unit building at 2235 East Broadway was completed — this file leaves the year UNCONFIRMED rather than picking one; the strata plan registration settles it. https://liv.rent/blog/renters/apartments-near-commercial-broadway-station/
