## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the freehold lake and rural parcels that make up this community's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule applies to the freehold and park product that makes up this community. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Planning and zoning (local):** the FVRD's Electoral Area C Official Community Plan, Bylaw 1747, 2024 (adopted September 18, 2025), covers Lake Errock and includes a Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan — it replaced the former OCP Bylaw No. 0020, 1998 — and FVRD zoning applies lot by lot. A specific lot's designation, permit areas and permitted dwellings must be confirmed with the FVRD for the PID; the new bylaw's Lake Errock schedules have not been read in this program: UNVERIFIED at lot level. City of Mission and District of Kent plans do NOT apply. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/lake-errock.md)
- **Rural property taxes:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, rural property taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion that remains unpaid on October 31. This is the regime that applies in Lake Errock — not Mission's flat 10%. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/lake-errock.md)
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA):** for any pad-rent manufactured home in the feed, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rent-increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — confirmed from the park's own tenancy documents for any specific pad. (Program brief, MHPTA pad-rent limits.)
- **ALR housing rules:** parcel-level ALR status is per address: UNVERIFIED — one feed card claims a lot is "Out of the ALR" in listing text, which is not verification; check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping. Where land is in the ALR: one principal residence capped at 500 m², plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no project in this area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies). https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
