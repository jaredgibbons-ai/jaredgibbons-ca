## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no strata on the east shore for a Form B to attach to — the frame matters here because east-shore buyers cross-shop the lake's one strata condo (North file) and its bare-land stratas (South file), where Form B diligence is the deal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule creates an age restriction at Cultus Lake Village — a manufactured-home park's age rule is a park rule under tenancy regulation, not a strata bylaw, and that park's own rule is UNCONFIRMED (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Governing zoning and plans (FVRD / Park Board — never Chilliwack's bylaws):** FVRD Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 zones the Park lease lands (R-3 Waterfront Residential and R-4 Hillside Residential on the Lakeshore Drive / Munroe Avenue side; in R-3 the permitted primary uses are a single-detached dwelling or a manufactured home, one per lot; Amendment Bylaw No. 1672 added R-5 Lower Lakeshore Drive and R-6 Upper Lakeshore Drive and amended R-4 height; minimum lot area/frontage for R-3/R-4: UNVERIFIED). PlanCultus, Cultus Lake Park Plan Bylaw No. 1080, 2016, covers the Park including the east-side lots; the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115, covers Area H land outside the Park. No FVRD bylaw number beyond those two sources was opened for the east shore in this program — any other FVRD number is UNVERIFIED here, and Chilliwack's bylaws do not govern this ground. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20H%20-%20Zoning%20Bylaw%201375%20-%20Cultus%20Lake%20Park.pdf and https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/CLP-Plan-Bylaw-1080-hig-res.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-east.md)
- **Taxes — rural regime, not Chilliwack's dates:** Park lease lots pay Park Board lease charges rather than a standard municipal tax bill (current schedule UNVERIFIED). For non-Park Electoral Area H land, taxes are provincial rural taxes under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11: due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid after October 31. Chilliwack's penalty dates NEVER apply to Cultus Lake. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/new-resident-information/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Manufactured-home park tenancy:** site tenancies are governed by BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — the tenant owns the home and rents the site under a written agreement. The Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows a landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement — which is why Cultus Lake Village's own written rule, not a listing label, decides who can buy in. Pad-rent increase limits: 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus proportional; 3 full months' written notice, once every 12 months. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (round illustration only — no area median exists to use), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Note: a Park Board leasehold purchase and a manufactured home on a rented pad do not transfer title the way a freehold/strata purchase does — confirm PTT treatment of the specific tenure with the conveyancer before budgeting. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on the east shore was confirmed on any source — if one is marketed, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
