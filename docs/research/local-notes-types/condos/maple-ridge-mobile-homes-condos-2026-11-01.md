## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero strata-condo stock in this topic, stated plainly at the top: a manufactured home in a Maple Ridge park is not a condo. There is no strata corporation, no Form B, no contingency reserve fund and no depreciation report — the buyer owns a manufactured home and rents the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA). The parks and co-ops below are documented as tenure traps — product a buyer can mistake for strata ownership — not as condo buildings, and none is counted as an apartment condo.**

### The core distinction — park rule vs strata bylaw

- In a strata, an age restriction is a registered bylaw: BC law allows only a 55-or-older residency bylaw (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), it is disclosed on the Form B, and the May 1, 2023 exemptions for younger spouses/partners and defined children apply to it. In a manufactured home park, the 55+ rule is a **park rule** set by the park owner under the MHPTA tenancy — it is not an SPA bylaw, no Form B discloses it, and the SPA exemptions do not govern it; the written park rules, which the seller must hand to the proposed buyer before assignment is even requested, are the document that governs. Treat any "55+ strata" language in a park-home listing as a category error. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Read this section as "rules a condo buyer expects, and what actually applies instead" — the Strata Property Act block is stated because buyers arrive with condo assumptions, and each item names the manufactured-home rule that replaces it.

- **Form B — there is none.** A Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59) exists only for strata lots. The park-home equivalents are: the written park rules and the relevant parts of the site tenancy agreement (which the seller must give the proposed buyer before asking the landlord to consent to assignment), the home's Manufactured Home Registry record, and its CSA label status. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports — not applicable.** The rule that BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years (since July 1, 2024, no deferral vote) binds strata corporations only. A park has no depreciation report; the park's capital plans are the landlord's, and a buyer's protection is the MHPTA rent-increase regime below, not a strata report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum — not applicable.** The rule that stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund (since November 1, 2023) binds strata corporations only. A park home owner pays pad rent; there is no CRF to inspect and no special levy — and also no owner vote over the park's spending. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void — a strata rule, not a park rule.** Since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw. That voiding is a Strata Property Act change; it does not rewrite a park's rules. Whether a park home may be rented out is governed by the park rules and the site tenancy agreement — read them, do not import the strata rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ — park rule vs SPA bylaw.** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw in a strata, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A park's 55+ restriction (Val Maria, Alouette) is a park rule under the MHPTA, set in the written park rules — the SPA exemption list does not govern it, and the park's own wording decides who may live in the home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Pad tenancy law — the regime that actually governs.** Manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. On a sale where the home stays in the park, assignment of the site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Pad rent increases — verified limits.** Landlords in manufactured home parks can increase rent only with 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, and not above the yearly rent increase limit, plus a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); further increases only by Residential Tenancy Branch order. The limits: **2026 = 2.3% plus the proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Registration is what makes the sale effective.** In BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains the central register of manufactured home ownership, controls the movement of manufactured homes in BC, and improves the security of lenders financing purchases. Buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary). A home may not be sold without the original legible CSA decal attached (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular homes); a lost label requires re-certification through a Technical Safety BC electrical inspection, whose approval label can stand in place of the CSA label for a sale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Taxes on a home in a park.** The Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. Maple Ridge property taxes generally were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% September 2, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md) and https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **City zoning and plans.** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 includes Zone RMH (Manufactured Home Park Residential) at s. 626 — the zone the parks sit in. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research files (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an OCP update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **GST and purchase taxes.** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales; how GST and Property Transfer Tax apply to a specific manufactured-home purchase depends on whether land transfers with the home (a park unit transfers no land) — no source in the research file settles the treatment for a specific unit: UNCONFIRMED per unit; take conveyancing advice before writing. For qualifying new homes generally: the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 (CRA News No. 122: Royal Assent March 13, 2026); the GST New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum across $350,000–$450,000. BC Property Transfer Tax generally runs 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million and 3% above, with a First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (full to $835K FMV, partial to $860K) and a Newly Built exemption (full $1.1M, gone $1.15M) where land is acquired. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Park-home resales are not REDMA presales — stated so a buyer does not assume a 7-day rescission exists on a park purchase; subject periods are contractual here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Park-home financing is lender-specific chattel-style lending in many cases and one Maple Ridge park listing states no financing is permitted in that community at all — confirm financing with the lender and the park before subjects, never from the listing alone. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a park home a condo?** No. There is no strata corporation, no Form B, no CRF and no depreciation report. You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Val Maria and Alouette say 55+ — is that the same 55+ bylaw as a condo?** No — that is the core distinction in this file. In a strata, 55+ is a registered bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 with statutory exemptions. In a park, 55+ is a park rule in the written park rules; the SPA exemptions do not govern it, and the park's wording decides. Get the park rules in writing before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Do I own the land under a Maple Ridge park home?** Generally no: the Province describes park tenancies as owning the manufactured home and renting the site it sits on, and the home's assessed value for tax purposes does not include the land value in a park. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment of the site tenancy the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change. Increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit (2026: 2.3%; 2027: 2.2%) plus the proportional levy and utility amount. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What does the park have to approve before I can buy?** The assignment itself: under MHPTA s. 28 the seller may assign the site tenancy only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, an arbitrator's order, or authorization in the tenancy agreement), and the seller must first give you written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement. Build the consent timeline into subjects. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: What is the difference between a mobile home and a modular home in BC?** Both are manufactured homes built off-site. The Province's registry distinguishes them by CSA standard: mobile homes are built under the Z-240 standards and modular homes under the A-277 standards, and the CSA label on the home states which applies. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached. If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: When is the purchase actually complete?** When it is registered: a manufactured home sale is only effective if the transaction is registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms. Closing the contract alone does not transfer the home. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Can I move the home to another park or lot later?** A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Are the market numbers on the mobile-homes page manufactured-home prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category; the Maple Ridge figures (detached median $1,131,000 and condos & townhomes median $525,000 in September 2026) are residential context only, and manufactured-home-specific statistics, including any pad-rent range, are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The assignment does not reset the rent clock.** On assignment the pad rent stays the same for the incoming tenant and the next scheduled increase date does not move — a transfer is not a turnover, and any park-office suggestion that a purchase triggers a new market pad rent contradicts the Province's assignment guidance. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **The pad-rent cap has a second, uncapped-looking component.** On top of the percentage limit (2.3% in 2026, 2.2% in 2027) the landlord may add the proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus regulated park utility fees divided by the number of sites in the park — so two parks with the same percentage limit can post different lawful increases in the same year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Maple Ridge's 55+ manufactured and co-op landscape is wider than the parks.** Alongside the Val Maria and Alouette pad-rent parks, the city's seniors stock includes St. George's Village co-op, the Haney Pioneer Village co-op at 60+ (not 55+), Legion Gardens on a life lease, and the four Fraserview Village strata buildings in East Central — five different tenure forms that listing searches routinely blur into one "55+" bucket. https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/MRN/180831/MRNN180831_Z38.pdf
- **The CSA label is a sale gate, not a detail.** Without the original legible decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) the home cannot be sold at all until Technical Safety BC re-certification — on an older park unit, ask for a photo of the label before booking a showing, not at subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **The City zones parks deliberately.** Zone RMH, Manufactured Home Park Residential (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, s. 626), is a dedicated zone for this housing form — a park's land is not ordinary residential land awaiting rezoning, and any claim about a park's redevelopment value needs the RMH text and the MHPTA's park-closure rules, neither of which a listing will quote. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
- **Maple Ridge's OCP citation is unsettled in current sources** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs 7998-2024, update adopted November 25, 2025) — quote planning policy for a specific park from a current City document, not from memory. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-mobile-homes.md)
