## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A recip card is not a Form B and carries none of this — the certificate comes from the strata on a specific purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. This is a strata rule only: a manufactured-home park's no-rentals rule is a park rule under the MHPTA and is NOT voided by that change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A park's "55+" label is a park rule, not an SPA bylaw — the regimes stay separate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The data route, as the page itself discloses it:** "The data relating to real estate on this website comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of either the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB). Real estate listings held by participating real estate firms are marked with the MLS® logo and detailed information about the listing includes the name of the listing agent. This representation is based in whole or part on data generated by either the GVR, the FVREB or the CADREB which assumes no responsibility for its accuracy. The materials contained on this page may not be reproduced without the express written consent of either the GVR, the FVREB or the CADREB." https://jaredgibbons.ca/recip.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/recip.md)
- **Reciprocity display rules:** the board rules that govern reciprocity display on a member's website — what a participating firm must do for its listings to appear in other members' feeds, what Jared's site must do to carry them, and any permission or agreement required — are UNVERIFIED in this file: neither FVREB, GVR nor CADREB publishes its MLS®/reciprocity rule documents on a public page that could be read for this research, and a platform vendor's marketing page is not an official source. At the national level, CREA owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks, and agents must be members of CREA and of a local real estate board — complying with both CREA's rules, including the REALTOR® Code, and their local board's rules — to access the MLS® system at all. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/recip.md)
- **Feed mechanics:** how listings are selected into this site's feed, how often it refreshes, why the count is 310 at reading, and whether sold or expired listings ever appear in it are UNVERIFIED — the page text states no filter, no refresh interval beyond "real time"/"continuously" marketing language, and no inclusion rules. The feed's own footer stamp reads "Data was last updated November 1, 2026 at 05:40 PM (UTC)." https://jaredgibbons.ca/recip.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/recip.md)
- **Regulator — who you are dealing with:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, must disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and can only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction — dual agency was prohibited in B.C. in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. That frame matters on this page precisely because the homes shown are other firms' listings: the agent whose contact block sits beside each card is not the listing agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/recip.md)
- **City zoning and plans:** no single municipality governs this page — its first page is Surrey ground, where the current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026; the replaced OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning. Zoning for any specific listing reached from the feed is that listing's own municipality's bylaw — UNCONFIRMED per listing from the card alone. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a listing reached from this feed is a developer-contract presale, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Whether any specific card is a presale or a resale is a per-listing fact — UNCONFIRMED from the card alone. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
