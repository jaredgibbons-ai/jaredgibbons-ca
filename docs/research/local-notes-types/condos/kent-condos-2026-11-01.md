## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any older Kent strata bylaw or directory line purporting to restrict long-term rentals (including the "rentals allowed" framing on Gallagher Landing's directory entry) is void as a restriction; rentals are allowed. Stratas may still restrict short-term rentals. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023, per Strata Property Regulation 7.01) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Kent strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **District plans and zoning (District sources only):** the plan is the District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025, which directs most growth into the Agassiz townsite with Mount Woodside as a satellite growth village; zoning is the District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended), with OCP designations Agricultural, Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Institutional, Parks/Recreation/Open Space and Resource Management. SSMUH: Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone allowing up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots — and the District's own guide is explicit that there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands. Secondary suites are permitted in single-family homes in all residential zones. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001 and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Kent property taxes — municipality rules, NOT the rural regime:** Kent is a municipality, so the Taxation (Rural Area) Act penalty regime for FVRD electoral areas (5% after July 2 + a further 5% after October 31) does **NOT** apply inside Kent. District of Kent tax notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day), rates set before May 15 each year. Diking is a separate assessment-based rate line: the 2026 Tax Rates Summary carries a dedicated "Dyking" column — 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value for Class 1 Residential (0.29356 for Class 9 Farm) — on top of a total municipal rate of 2.80493 (Residential) and an all-in Residential total of 4.65328 including Hospital, Regional District, MFA, BC Assessment and School Tax columns. Specific penalty percentages for late Kent taxes beyond "penalties apply after the due date": UNCONFIRMED from a District source in this program — confirm with the District. https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Flood, diking and ALR:** Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5; the District's flat lands are Fraser/Harrison floodplain behind dikes, the District maintains 135 km of drainage ditches, and Mount Woodside's slope/geotechnical hazards sit in OCP Sections 5.2–5.3. About 50% of the District's land area (excluding forest reserves, Crown land, roads and highways) is in the Agricultural Land Reserve per OCP §4.2 — a different denominator from the 2013 provincial inventory's 6,502 ha / 35% figure; the two are not interchangeable. Parcel-level flood construction levels: UNVERIFIED. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (Any page stating "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% — that compression is wrong as a rate statement.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. No worked example is given here because no verified Kent median exists to use as an input — do not substitute another community's figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Manufactured-home parks (where parks are discussed):** under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act framework, annual pad-rent increases are capped at the rate of inflation plus a proportional amount for specified cost increases — 2.3% + proportional for 2026 and 2.2% + proportional for 2027. No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file. This applies to a park tenancy (e.g., the unlocated Continental Trailer Park entry above), never to strata ownership.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Kent" a place I can search for condos?** As a municipality, yes; as a board area name, no. "Kent" appears on no board list — the CADREB name for this land is Agassiz, and the MLS taxonomy carries the District under the Agassiz area. Searches, schools and daily life say Agassiz; taxes, bylaws, permits and the OCP say District of Kent. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Q: Are there apartment condos in Kent at all?** None verified in this program. The District's strata stock is townhouse product — in the Agassiz townsite and at Mount Woodside — plus detached and semi-detached homes. A buyer who needs a stacked low-rise apartment is looking at Chilliwack product, not Kent. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is at Mount Woodside?** The District's designated newer growth area: Harrison Highlands, a master-planned community of single-family, semi-detached and townhouse housing by Odessa Group, including Harrison Ridge Townhomes (1928 Woodside Blvd, EPS7292) and Briarwood (1911 Woodside Blvd, BCS3613, 18 units, form UNCONFIRMED). Slope and geotechnical policy (OCP §§5.2–5.3) applies there in a way it does not on the flat townsite. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Q: Is Harrison Ridge Townhomes a 55+ building?** No verified age bylaw exists for it. It markets to seniors without an age bylaw (55+ program finding) — marketing language is not a restriction, and any buyer can purchase and reside there regardless of age unless the registered bylaws say otherwise. (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/kent-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Kent strata unit long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Kent flood-prone, and what does diking cost?** The flat lands are Fraser/Harrison floodplain behind dikes (OCP §5.5), the District maintains 135 km of drainage ditches, and diking is its own rate line on the tax notice (2026 Class 1 Residential: 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value) — an assessment-based rate, not a flat per-parcel charge. Verify dike status, drainage and flood construction requirements for the specific parcel. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Kent home?** Kent Elementary (K–6) and Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (7–12), both in Agassiz, SD78 — with the Policy 7000 catchments stated in Section 1. Confirm a specific address with SD78. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Q: How many homes can a Kent house lot hold now?** Up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots in the RS1 zone under Bylaw No. 1737 (adopted June 10, 2024) — but the District confirms there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands, so rural Kent lots do not get the four-unit option. Secondary suites are permitted in single-family homes in all residential zones; on ALR land, farm home plate and ALC rules apply on top. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf
- **Q: When are Kent taxes due, and what penalty applies?** Notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day); penalties apply after the due date. Kent is a municipality — neither the FVRD rural-area regime (5% + 5%) nor any other municipality's penalty applies to a Kent bill. The exact Kent penalty percentage: UNCONFIRMED from a District source in this program. https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/
- **Q: Is Harrison Hot Springs part of Kent?** No — it is a separate village municipality carved out of Kent at the south end of Harrison Lake, with its own taxes, OCP and market, even though Kent surrounds it. Do not carry Harrison buildings or rules onto a Kent page, or the reverse. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The board name and the legal name are different words for the same ground.** Every regulatory answer (taxes, permits, bylaws, the OCP) lives under "District of Kent" at kentbc.ca, while every market search, school name and transit stop says "Agassiz" — a buyer searching only one name misses half the file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **The District's recreation centrepiece opened in Agassiz in September 2026.** The Lets'emot Regional Recreation and Aquatic Centre — a 27,000 sq ft expansion attached to the Community Recreation and Cultural Centre at 6660 Pioneer Ave, with an 8-lane lap pool, leisure pool with lazy river, whirlpool, steam room and sauna — had its grand opening September 25, 2026 per the District's September 9, 2026 council highlights, replacing the roughly 50-year-old seasonal Ferny Coombe Pool. "Lets'emot Regional Park" as an FVRD regional park is UNVERIFIED — no FVRD source for a park by that name was found; "Lets'emot" is the aquatic centre. https://www.kentbc.ca/news/posts/september-9-2026-council-highlights/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **Two of the District's biggest land uses are federal prisons.** Kent Institution (opened 1979, the only maximum-security facility in CSC's Pacific Region, rated capacity 378) and Mountain Institution (opened 1962, medium security, rated capacity 440) sit on shared Federal Reserve land near Agassiz; the OCP also names AAFC's Agassiz Research and Development Centre and UBC's Dairy Education and Research Centre as major floodplain land uses. https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/facilities-security/institutional-profiles/pacific/kent-institution.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/correctional-service/corporate/facilities-security/institutional-profiles/pacific/mountain-institution.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **The District has quantified its housing gap.** Kent's Interim Housing Needs Report (2024), as presented in the OCP update, found 495 new homes needed immediately and 1,695 more by 2041 — against a 2021 Census base of about 6,300 residents and 2,518 total private dwellings (2,351 occupied). Growth is directed mainly to the Agassiz townsite and Mount Woodside. https://forms.kentbc.ca/_Print/Index/6a0b39a0-ebb5-4763-89dd-b3690173f2af?PDF=True (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **The province paid to upgrade the pump station that protects the west side.** A $4.17 million provincially funded expansion and upgrade of the Hammersley Pump Station, about 6 km west of Agassiz, conveys Mountain Slough watershed runoff and protects agricultural lands and the nearby federal correctional infrastructure — part of the flood system every flat-land Kent purchase depends on. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2017TRAN0139-000928 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **There are no board statistics for Kent.** SnapStats' Fraser Valley report has no Kent or Agassiz row and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only — any "Kent market median" is UNVERIFIED and must not be printed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
