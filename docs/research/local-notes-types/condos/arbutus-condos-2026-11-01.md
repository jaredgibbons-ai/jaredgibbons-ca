## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For a 1975–76 enclave like Arbutus Village, envelope, parkade and systems age are the items to trace. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line for Arbutus Village saying rentals are not allowed would be void for long-term tenancies regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. The ridge's house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3–4 units, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6–8 units; a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Arbutus Village lands are the exception: they sit under their own CD-1 (78) district (4255 Arbutus Street). Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md) and http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **Area plan:** a separate adopted Arbutus Ridge community plan is UNVERIFIED in the sources read; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. The Arbutus Centre Policy Statement (2008) is the City policy document named in the CD-1 (78) file for the village lands. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** the City requires every owner to submit an annual property status declaration; properties declared or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City tax timing:** Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the existing Arbutus Village strata is resale stock; GST matters here only if a redevelopment strata block sells as new. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the governing frame if Arbutus Village redevelopment strata phases are marketed off-plan. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really condo stock in Arbutus Ridge?** Almost none on the ridge's own streets. The one established strata enclave tied to the area is Arbutus Village at its southern edge (1975 directory date; a unit listing says 1976) — and listing sources file it under the Quilchena sub-area, so a search filtered to "Arbutus Ridge" alone can miss it. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out an Arbutus Village unit long-term?** Yes as a matter of provincial law — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to a unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in Arbutus Village?** A current enclave listing's pet field says "Pets: No." Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws), so treat that field as a real warning and read the registered bylaws before writing. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2101-mcmullen-avenue/312
- **Q: What should I check first in a 1970s enclave building?** The depreciation report and CRF: stratas with 5+ lots must have a report every five years (no deferral vote since July 1, 2024) and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. At 50 years old, the report's envelope, parkade and piping items drive the real cost of a low purchase price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Are the new Arbutus Village buildings condos I can buy?** Partly, on paper: the redevelopment was reported with 399 strata units across its blocks, but its first block was split between market rental (115 units) and City-owned non-market homes (100 units), which are not for sale at all. No completed strata block was verified in this pass — completion status: UNCONFIRMED. https://vancouver.skyrisecities.com/news/2017/11/work-started-arbutus-village-development.29725 and http://denebmanagement.com/2017/12/04/construction-set-to-begin-on-508-unit-arbutus-shopping-centre-condo/
- **Q: Which schools serve an Arbutus Village or ridge address?** SD39 catchments are address-based, with no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). The elementaries located inside Arbutus Ridge are Carnarvon (3400 Balaclava Street) and Trafalgar (4170 Trafalgar Street); the research file's locator run was not done, so run the exact address in the VSB locator — and confirm the secondary separately, because no secondary was confirmed for this area in the research pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station in Arbutus Ridge?** No. The area is bus-served (#14 on Arbutus / West Boulevard, with the #25, #41, #49 and #99 nearby). The transit change in the wider corridor is the Broadway Subway work at the area's north edge — marketing for The Arbutus at 2892 Arbutus Street pitches a future Arbutus station, but that building is recorded here as not counted (see Section 2). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **Q: Does the R1-1 multiplex zoning change what a house lot beside the condos is worth?** It can: R1-1 allows up to 6 strata units or up to 8 rental units on a qualifying lot (minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by form), so neighbouring houses carry a builder value as well as a family value. The village lands themselves are CD-1 (78), not R1-1. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **Q: What does the Empty Homes Tax mean for a part-time owner here?** An annual declaration is mandatory; a home declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year is taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Is there a median condo price for Arbutus Ridge I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used. Price from the specific building's recent sales. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area's only condo enclave is zoned as its own district.** Arbutus Village does not sit under R1-1 like the rest of the ridge — it has its own CD-1 (78) bylaw (4255 Arbutus Street, enacted August 1, 1972) capping the village lands at 461 multiple-dwelling units, which is why the enclave's form is unlike anything else in the neighbourhood. http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **The same buildings carry two sub-area names.** Directories and listings file Arbutus Village under "Quilchena," while the village sits at Arbutus Ridge's southern edge — a buyer searching only one label will miss listings filed under the other. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&sortby=levels&page=127
- **The redevelopment's first homes were not condos at all.** Block A of the Larco rebuild was reported as 115 market rental units plus 100 City-owned non-market units — the strata blocks come later in the phasing, so "new at Arbutus Village" does not automatically mean "for sale." http://denebmanagement.com/2017/12/04/construction-set-to-begin-on-508-unit-arbutus-shopping-centre-condo/
- **There is no community centre or library branch inside Arbutus Ridge.** The City's own area page routes residents to the Dunbar, Kitsilano and Kerrisdale community centres and library branches — a real lifestyle trade a condo buyer downsizing from a ridge house should price in. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **The Greenway is the area's east edge, not a through-road.** The Arbutus Greenway's temporary path runs 8.5 km along the east side of Arbutus Street and West Boulevard between Fir Street and Milton Street — the corridor that separates the ridge from Shaughnessy and carries its walking/cycling traffic. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/arbutus.md)
- **Two build years attach to the same enclave.** The directory dates Arbutus Village to 1975; a current unit listing says 1976. Section 2 flags both rather than picking one — the strata plan registration is the definitive check. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/buildings?filter_titletoland=FREEHOLD%2BSTRATA&sortby=levels&page=127 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2101-mcmullen-avenue/312
