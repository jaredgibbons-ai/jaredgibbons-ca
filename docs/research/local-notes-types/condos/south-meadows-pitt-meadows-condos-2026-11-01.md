# South Meadows, Pitt Meadows — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Meadows is the Paragon sub-area name for the south end of the City of Pitt Meadows; it is not an official City neighbourhood name — the City describes this part of the city as **South Bonson**, the Bonson Road corridor south of Hammond Road and the CPKC rail tracks to the Fraser River, including the South Bonson Community Centre at Osprey Village. No official boundary is published for the MLS name; rough working limits only: Hammond Road and the rail line to the north, the Fraser River to the south, the Bonson Road corridor in the middle, and the Golden Ears Business Park lands toward Harris Road to the west; exact east and west limits: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Where the attached stock concentrates: Osprey Village and the Fraser Way / Sutton Avenue pocket at the river end of Bonson Road — and it is **townhome stock, not apartment condos** (see the bold statement at the top of Section 2). The village core is Fraser Way and Barnston View Road, with the South Bonson Community Centre and Pitt Meadows Art Gallery at 10932 Barnston View Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Sub-area attached volume: South Meadows carried 13 condos & townhomes listings with 3 solds in September 2026 (23% sales ratio), against 11 detached listings with 5 solds (45%). SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: the Pitt Meadows condos & townhomes median was $662,500 in September 2026 ($566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list, 40 days on market). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Main roads: Bonson Road, Hammond Road, Harris Road, Fraser Way and Barnston View Road; Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) and Golden Ears Way / Golden Ears Bridge run along the city's north side. Shoreline Park (51 Bonson Road) and the dyke-top trails run on the Fraser River dike through the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Transit: routes 719 and 722 run as a combined circular service through Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown, Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Meadows Station; route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge; the West Coast Express runs from Pitt Meadows Station north of the area — no station is in this sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- Nearby areas: Mid Meadows (Bonson Road / Osprey Village) to the north, Central Meadows (Town Centre) to the north-west across Harris Road, West Meadows (airport and farm lands) to the west, and the City of Maple Ridge to the east. Meadowland Village belongs to Central Meadows on the board classification and is dealt with in the central-meadows file — it is NOT counted here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — the core diligence for the Nature's Walk, Coho II and Shoreline townhome purchases that make up this sub-area's attached market. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void — and Pitt Meadows separately prohibits short-term rentals city-wide.** Since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw. That voiding does NOT create a short-term-rental right here: short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw — a City staff report to Council (October 23, 2024) states this expressly and proposed clarifying amendments on that basis — and the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows among the communities where it applies. The Coho II directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" in Section 2 therefore misleads twice: long-term caps are void, and short-term use is prohibited by the City layer regardless of strata bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** housing sits under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 urban residential zones, RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse, minimum lot area 0.4 ha) and RM-2 (townhouse and low-density apartment, minimum lot area 3,035 sq m); residential buildings are sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches in the RS zone. The governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022. Under the provincial SSMUH rules as applied citywide, lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Flood / dikes:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states **over 95%** of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — that current City figure governs (an older City release stated approximately 86%, and an ~85% figure sometimes quoted is wrong). The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; this sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3 (Kennedy, including McKechnie) of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, though the urban South Bonson pocket itself is developed residential / village land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes and drainage levy:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The same bylaw charges a separate residential (Class 1) drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS; a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is described in the same CRA publication. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current residential presale project in or beside this sub-area was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any apartment condos in South Meadows?** None verified. The sub-area's 13 condos & townhomes listings in September 2026 are townhome product — Nature's Walk, Coho II, Shoreline and similar complexes around Osprey Village — and no apartment building name was confirmed on an official, developer or strata source. Buyers who need a low-rise apartment are looking at Central Meadows stock instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a townhome in Osprey Village?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use under the City of Pitt Meadows Zoning Bylaw — prohibited city-wide by the City layer, separate from strata bylaws — and the provincial principal-residence requirement also applies in Pitt Meadows. A strata's silence on short-term rentals does not make the use legal. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Can I rent out a Nature's Walk or Coho II home long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, so directory lines like Coho II's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" do not create an enforceable long-term cap. Long-term renting is the City's permitted residential use; short-term is the prohibited one — keep the two layers separate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** South Meadows: 13 condos & townhomes listings, 3 solds (23% sales ratio). At the Pitt Meadows community level the condos & townhomes median was $662,500, $566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list and 40 days on market. SnapStats publishes no sub-area medians. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Bonson address?** No public school building sits inside the sub-area. School District 42 catchments are address-based with no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12); the locator result recorded for 10932 Barnston View Road is Davie Jones Elementary and Pitt Meadows Secondary (19438 116B Avenue, the city's only public secondary school), and Edith McDermott Elementary on Bonson Road also serves addresses to the north — check the exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Is South Meadows in a floodplain?** Effectively yes, city-wide: the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes; this sub-area sits behind the Fraser River dyke in drainage Area 3, and Shoreline Park itself sits on the dike. Confirm floodplain, dike and drainage details for the specific lot with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: What extra tax line do Pitt Meadows owners pay?** A separate drainage levy inside the annual tax bylaw: for 2026, $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value for residential (Class 1), on top of the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. Taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after August 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: How do I get to Vancouver from Osprey Village?** No station is in the sub-area: routes 719/722 circle Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Meadows Station, route 701 runs on Hammond Road to Coquitlam Central Station and Haney Place, and the West Coast Express runs weekday trains from Pitt Meadows Station to Waterfront Station. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED — no official source read states them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the townhome complexes?** The building directory records "Pets allowed w/rest." for Coho Chapter II. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC; for Nature's Walk and Shoreline no published building-level set was found — the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern for the specific complex. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/coho-chapter-ii-19572-fraser-way
- **Q: Is Meadowland Village part of South Meadows?** No — on the board classification it belongs to Central Meadows and is covered in that file. Do not count it in a South Meadows search, and do not let a portal's Osprey Village marketing pull Central Meadows stock into this sub-area's numbers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The MLS name and the City name are different places on paper.** "South Meadows" exists only in the board taxonomy; the City's own name for this pocket is South Bonson. City documents, permits and the community centre all use Bonson — a buyer searching City sources for "South Meadows" will find nothing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The floodplain figure has a stale twin.** The City's current figure is **over 95%** of the land base in the floodplain (2020 Business Plan); an older City release under the superseded OCP stated approximately 86%, and figures around 85% still circulate. The 95% figure governs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Two rental rules point in opposite directions here.** Provincially, long-term rental caps in strata bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 — but Pitt Meadows' zoning prohibits short-term rental accommodation outright, city-wide. A buyer underwriting either an Airbnb or a "no rentals allowed" assumption from a directory line gets both wrong. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **The dike is the park.** Shoreline Park and the dyke-top trails run on the Fraser River dike itself at 51 Bonson Road — the flood-protection structure is the neighbourhood's main recreational asset, which is why dike, drainage and the 15.0 m RS siting setback from watercourses and ditches belong in the same diligence conversation as the view. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The west edge is a business park, not future condos.** The Golden Ears Business Park is being built out in phases by Onni Group in the south Bonson / south Harris Road area per City material, and the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project adds a four-lane underpass below the CPKC crossing at Harris Road — employment and construction context for the sub-area's west side, not residential supply. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The detached ratio flatters the pocket.** South Meadows detached posted a 45% sales ratio in September 2026 on 11 listings and 5 solds — the strongest detached ratio among the Pitt Meadows sub-areas with nine or more listings — but one or two sales swing it, so it should not be read as a price signal for either houses or the townhome complexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/south-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
