## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Barnston Island is a Fraser River island between Surrey (Port Kells) on the south bank and Pitt Meadows across the main channel; there is no bridge — the only access is the free Barnston Island Ferry across Parson's Channel from 104 Ave in Port Kells, a barge-and-tug provincial inland ferry, capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers, running on demand roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m. (to 12:55 a.m. weekends), with crossings that can be interrupted by river traffic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- The island's main road is its perimeter dike road — the dike rings the island, protects it as low Fraser River floodplain, and doubles as the transportation route; Dyke Road addresses run around it (the SD36 locator test range was 2–328 Dyke Rd). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Where condo stock concentrates: nowhere — there is no condo stock on the island in any form (Section 2). The housing stock is a small number of farmhouses and rural single-detached homes on large farm parcels; Metro Vancouver's study puts the island population at roughly 150 residents, and the island has been farmed since the mid-1800s. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Market figures: none exist for the island. No board sub-area named "Barnston Island" was found in Fraser Valley board reporting, the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report has no Barnston Island row, and all Barnston-specific market figures (inventory, solds, median, days on market) are UNVERIFIED from that source. The research file's Surrey community totals are the whole City of Surrey market, which the island is not part of — they are not used here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Schools: School District 36 (Surrey) serves the island; island children cross by ferry to their assigned Surrey schools. The SD36 School Locator run for the island test address 52 Dyke Road (locator range 2–328 Dyke Rd) returned **Bothwell Elementary** and **École Fraser Heights** — names as the locator returned them. That locator result supersedes the earlier document-based suggestion that Port Kells Elementary serves island homes: Port Kells Elementary serves Port Kells / landing-side addresses, not island addresses. (SD36 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01, recorded in the research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- Services: none on the island — no shops, recreation facilities or medical facilities; Metro Vancouver's Barnston Island Regional Park provides riverfront rest areas and protects riparian habitat. All services are in Surrey (Port Kells/Fleetwood) or Pitt Meadows, via the ferry. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No strata exists on Barnston Island in any source checked, so no Form B exists to request for an island property — a seller offering "strata documents" for an island parcel is a red flag to verify before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Stated for completeness of the provincial frame; neither rule has an island strata to attach to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (expanded May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Both rules are largely academic on Barnston Island, where no strata product was identified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that decides this whole file:** strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; under Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1), ALR land cannot be stratified in the ordinary course. Combined with the island's agricultural OCP direction, that is why there is no condo product here and none can be assumed as future supply. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Planning authority: Metro Vancouver, not a municipality.** The Electoral Area A Official Community Plan (2018) sets the vision of Barnston Island as a thriving agricultural community and expressly does not apply to First Nations Reserves; Metro Vancouver — not a municipality — is the planning authority for the non-reserve lands, and also holds the zoning for Electoral Area A, including a Barnston Island zoning map (Map 12). There are no new developments of scale; the OCP direction is to keep the island agricultural. https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/official-community-plan and https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/zoning (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/barnston-island-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **ALR housing frame:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Exact ALR percentage of the island: UNCONFIRMED — pull the ALC map for a specific parcel before writing an offer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification (BC Assessment) — stated in full three-tier form:** $2,500 gross annual farm income for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line for farm class is an ERROR — the threshold depends on the operation's size. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Rural property taxes (Province) — the regime that actually applies here:** as unincorporated Electoral Area A land, taxes are collected under the provincial rural system (Surveyor of Taxes), with Metro Vancouver and school levies on top — no city tax bill exists. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11 regime as the Province publishes it: rural property tax notice mailed May 31, deadline to pay July 2, a 5% penalty applied to the unpaid balance on July 3, and an additional 5% penalty on November 1. Parcel-specific tax figures: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Flood construction:** the whole island is low Fraser River floodplain behind a perimeter dike, and Metro Vancouver publishes a Barnston Island Flood Construction Level Study — flood construction levels for a specific parcel are UNCONFIRMED until checked against that study. https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/resources (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/barnston-island-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $500,000 (a round illustration only — no Barnston Island median exists in any published source), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project exists on the island in any source checked; where a developer contract is used in BC, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos on Barnston Island?** No — none in any form. No strata product of any kind was identified on the island; the market stock is freehold farm parcels, plus Katzie IR 3 reserve land that is not market housing, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Surrey?** No. It uses Surrey mailing addresses, but the island is unincorporated Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A, plus the Katzie First Nation's Barnston Island IR No. 3 — services, taxes, building permits and planning run through Metro Vancouver and the Province, not Surrey City Hall. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a small lot and build a house on Barnston Island?** Most of the island is agricultural land under an OCP vision of a farming community; building is tied to farm parcels and ALR rules (one principal residence to 500 m², a suite only inside it, one additional residence of 90 m² at ≤40 ha or 186 m² above 40 ha), so a parcel-by-parcel check with Metro Vancouver and the ALC is essential before assuming a building lot exists. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do residents get on and off the island?** Only by the free Barnston Island Ferry from 104 Ave in Port Kells — about 5 minutes across Parson's Channel, on demand within operating hours (roughly 6:15 a.m.–11:55 p.m., to 12:55 a.m. weekends), capacity 5 vehicles and 50 passengers. It does not run all night, and crossings can be interrupted by river traffic — overnight, outside ferry hours, there is no vehicle access. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Who collects my property tax?** The Province, under the rural system — rural owners receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes and pay the Province, with a July 2 deadline, a 5% penalty on July 3 and a further 5% on November 1 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11 regime); Metro Vancouver and school levies ride on top. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/important-dates
- **Q: Does an island farm get farm-class tax treatment automatically?** No — farm class is applied for with BC Assessment by October 31, and the income threshold is three-tiered: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is the island in a floodplain?** Yes — it is a low Fraser River island protected by a perimeter dike that also serves as its main road, and Metro Vancouver maintains a flood construction level study for the island; confirm dike condition, flood construction level and insurance availability for the specific parcel early. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **Q: Where do island kids go to school?** School District 36 (Surrey) serves the island; children ride the ferry to their assigned Surrey schools — for the island test address 52 Dyke Road (locator range 2–328 Dyke Rd), Bothwell Elementary and École Fraser Heights (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01). Port Kells Elementary serves Port Kells / landing-side addresses, not island addresses. School-bus boarding priority on the ferry: UNVERIFIED — not stated on the Province's ferry page. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Q: Are there any market statistics for the island?** No. No board sub-area named "Barnston Island" was found in Fraser Valley board reporting and SnapStats FVR has no Barnston row; Surrey community figures describe the whole City of Surrey market, which the island is not part of, and are not used here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest condo stock, then?** Off-island, across the ferry in Surrey (Port Kells / Fleetwood side) or in Pitt Meadows — those buildings belong to those areas' own files; what is verified here is that reaching any of them means a ferry crossing first, every time. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "(Surrey)" label is a postal convention only.** No part of the island is inside the City of Surrey, it does not appear as a City of Surrey neighbourhood, and no Fraser Valley board sub-area named "Barnston Island" was found in SnapStats FVR reporting — a page that calls it a Surrey neighbourhood would be wrong. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **The dike is the road.** The perimeter dike doubles as the island's main transportation route, so flood infrastructure and daily driving are literally the same asset — a dike closure is also a road closure. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-planning/Documents/barnston-island-agriculture-study.pdf
- **The island's school answer was corrected by an actual locator run (Nov 1, 2026).** An earlier document-based reading pointed to Port Kells Elementary; the SD36 locator run for 52 Dyke Road returned Bothwell Elementary and École Fraser Heights for island addresses — Port Kells Elementary serves the landing side, not the island. (SD36 School Locator, run 2026-11-01, recorded in the research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
- **Services here are stitched together across jurisdictions.** Fraser Valley Regional Library serves island residents under a Metro Vancouver arrangement — Metro Vancouver publishes a library-information sheet specifically for Barnston Island residents, a small sign of how services here are assembled rather than delivered by a city. https://metrovancouver.org/services/electoral-area-a/resources (55+ notes: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/barnston-island-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **There is no bus service on the island and no bridge.** The ferry is the single connection, which is why the complete absence of on-island seniors and condo services (Section 2) matters more here than in any mainland area: a resident who stops driving has no local fallback. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **The ferry's capacity is five vehicles.** At busy times and during river-traffic interruptions, the queue is part of the commute — every door-to-door time for the island starts with a crossing that cannot be scheduled like a bridge. Timed commutes: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)
