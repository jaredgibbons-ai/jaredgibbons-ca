## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Kells sits in the northeast corner of Surrey, on the south bank of the Fraser River, north and south of Highway 1, east of Highway 15 (176 Street), against the Langley border. It has two distinct halves: a large light-industrial district north of Highway 1, and a rural/low-density residential pocket ("South Port Kells") south of the highway. The City's economic arm profiles Port Kells as one of Surrey's major industrial areas (781 acres, roughly 610 businesses, mainly light industrial IL zoning). https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- Nearby areas: Tynehead and Fleetwood (west/southwest), Guildford (west), Walnut Grove in the Township of Langley (east), Anniedale-Tynehead (immediately south, Neighbourhood Concept Plan in preparation). (Research file, Section 1)
- Where condo stock concentrates: **nowhere — there is no condo shelf.** The residential stock is single-detached houses on suburban and larger rural lots plus cluster housing shown in the South Port Kells Land Use Plan, predominantly freehold; north of Highway 1 the land is industrial buildings, not housing. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf (Research file, Section 5)
- Market figures, September 2026 (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, as stated in the research file): sub-area Port Kells — detached 4 active listings, 0 sold, sales ratio n/a; **condos & townhomes 0 active listings, 0 sold**. Community (Surrey, all of Surrey) — detached median $1,215,000, $454/sq ft, 95% sale-to-list, 28 days on market; condos & townhomes median $512,944, $491/sq ft, 99% sale-to-list, 25 days on market. The sub-area sample is too small for its ratios to mean anything on their own. (Research file, Section 2)
- Schools: Port Kells Elementary (K–7) at 19076 88 Avenue, School District 36 (Surrey); the current school building opened in 1907 and is on the Canadian Register of Historic Places. A locator run for 19076 88 Avenue returned Port Kells Elementary and Ecole Salish Secondary. Catchments are address-based — confirm other addresses in the locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/surrey and https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415 (Research file, Section 4)
- Transit and access: no rapid transit; local bus route numbers/frequency are UNCONFIRMED from an official source in this program. Highway 1, Highway 15 (south to the U.S. commercial border crossing) and Highway 17 / South Fraser Way run through or beside the area. The Barnston Island Ferry (free, provincial inland ferry, roughly 5-minute on-demand crossing, capacity ~6 vehicles) departs from the foot of 104 Avenue across Parson's Channel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file, Section 4)
- Recreation and shopping: no civic recreation centre and no retail centre in Port Kells itself; Tynehead Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) borders the area to the west, and the nearest full shopping is in Guildford and Langley's Walnut Grove. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file, Section 4)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no verified strata in Port Kells to request one from today — the rule is stated because it governs any strata purchase a buyer makes instead in Guildford/Fleetwood, and any future strata product here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing line anywhere stating a long-term rental restriction is VOID (short-term rentals can still be restricted). A freehold Port Kells house carries no strata rental bylaw at all. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Port Kells strata exists to hold such a bylaw on any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan and zoning:** **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026) is Surrey's current OCP — Bylaw No. 18020 is superseded and must not be cited as current. Area-level guidance is the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan; North Port Kells is primarily light industrial (IL) zoning. Surrey publishes Port Kells-specific Development Cost Charges (2025 Port Kells Industrial DCCs and Commercial DCCs, separate from city-wide rates) — a real line item for any industrial or commercial purchase with development intent. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (55+ file, Section 3; Research file, Section 4)
- **ALR rules govern the south pocket's farm parcels:** the South Port Kells plan maps ALR boundary lands through the residential pocket; ALR status changes what a buyer can build, and ALC housing rules generally allow one residence per parcel (principal residence 500 m² or less, secondary suite only inside the principal residence). Check the ALR boundary on any south-side parcel before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (55+ file, Section 3)
- **Surrey suites rule:** a secondary suite in Surrey is capped at **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area** — the suite rule that applies if a South Port Kells house (or any Surrey house a buyer chooses instead of a condo) is bought with suite income in mind; ALR parcels follow the ALC rule above instead. City of Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 suite provisions, as applied program-wide in this notes series.
- **Flood:** parcel-level flood construction level / floodplain requirements for South Port Kells lots are UNVERIFIED from the sources used in this program — confirm against the City's floodplain mapping and the property's title before subject removal on a low-lying south-side lot. (Research file, UNVERIFIED list)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file, Section 4)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Port Kells' stock is resale freehold. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $512,944 (the Surrey community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — no Port Kells attached median exists, and there is no attached stock to apply it to), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,258.88 (2% × $312,944) = **$8,258.88 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Port Kells was verified in this program; where a unit anywhere is still on a developer contract, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a condo or townhome I can buy in Port Kells?** No verified one. The board recorded 0 condos & townhomes listings in the sub-area in September 2026, and no named strata complex of any form verifies in-area on any source checked. A buyer set on strata product is shopping Guildford, Fleetwood or Walnut Grove, not Port Kells. SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. (Section 2 sources.)
- **Q: What CAN I buy in Port Kells?** A freehold house in South Port Kells (suburban lots to multi-acre rural/ALR parcels) or, north of Highway 1, an industrial property. The two markets share a name and almost nothing else. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Q: Is Port Kells really a board sub-area, or just an industrial park name?** Both things are true: it is a real FVREB sub-area in the board's SnapStats structure (Jared's ruling) AND the City profiles it as a major industrial area; it is not one of Surrey's six town centres. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file, Section 1)
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Port Kells / Surrey?** No. Barnston Island is Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR 3 — it only codes to Port Kells for board purposes because the free ferry to it sails from the foot of 104 Avenue in Port Kells. Do not price or describe an island property as Surrey stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Can I build a suite or second home on a South Port Kells acreage?** It depends on zoning and, critically, ALR status: the land use plan shows ALR lands inside the residential pocket, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence limits (secondary suite only inside the principal residence). Off-ALR, Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area. Confirm both before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is there any seniors housing in Port Kells?** None verified — of any type. No 55+ strata, rental, retirement residence, assisted-living residence, subsidized building or manufactured-home park carries a Port Kells address in any registry or directory checked; the Surrey names that surface in searches (Sullivan Mews, Westwood Estates, the City Centre residences) are all in other sub-areas. (55+ file, Section 2.)
- **Q: How did Port Kells sell in September 2026?** It didn't, in the sample: 4 detached listings, 0 sold; 0 attached listings, 0 sold. Community (Surrey) medians that month: detached $1,215,000; condos & townhomes $512,944. SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **Q: What transit can I use day to day?** There is no rapid transit, and local bus routes/frequency are UNCONFIRMED from an official source in this program. The one named service is the free Barnston Island Ferry — an on-demand barge with set operating hours, a neighbourhood feature rather than a commuting route. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Are there extra development charges in Port Kells?** Yes — Surrey publishes separate 2025 DCC schedules for Port Kells industrial and commercial development, apart from the city-wide rates. Confirm the schedule early in any development pro forma. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **Q: What PTT should I budget?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $512,944 fair market value (the September 2026 Surrey community attached median, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $8,258.88. A detached Port Kells purchase at the Surrey detached median ($1,215,000) would be $22,300 on the same formula. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One name, two unrelated markets, split by a highway.** North of Highway 1: an IL-zoned industrial district with its own DCC schedule and roughly 610 businesses. South of it: a rural-residential pocket under a separate adopted land use plan, with ALR parcels woven through it. A "Port Kells" search has to be split by highway side before it means anything. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
- **The board stats will mislead if read at face value.** The September 2026 sub-area row (4 detached listings, 0 sales, 0 attached listings) is a sample too small to signal demand — it mainly confirms there is almost no strata stock to trade. (Research file, Section 2.)
- **The island across the ferry is not in Surrey at all.** Barnston Island (Electoral Area A + Katzie IR 3) codes to Port Kells only for board reporting; its governance, services and land rules are not the City of Surrey's, and this file counts nothing there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **The school is a heritage building.** Port Kells Elementary's current building opened in 1907 and is on the Canadian Register of Historic Places — the old village core around 88 Avenue is the residential pocket's historic centre, in a sub-area otherwise defined by warehouses. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=11415
- **Anniedale-Tynehead is the change to watch.** The Neighbourhood Concept Plan in preparation directly south contemplates industrial, commercial and residential growth next to Port Kells — servicing, traffic and land values on the south edge move with that plan, not with anything happening inside Port Kells itself. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file, Section 4)
- **Surrey replaced its OCP in 2026.** Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), is the current plan — packages and older guides citing Bylaw 18020 are citing a superseded bylaw. (55+ file, Section 3.)
