## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this sub-area's 1989–1994 stock (Fraserview Park, Palace Quay, Queen's Park Place) this is the central diligence document — read it together with the building's flood and insurance position below. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The directory lines for Palace Quay and Victoria Hill (15 Royal Ave) both read "rentals allowed w/restrictions" — any long-term rental restriction behind those lines is unenforceable; a short-term restriction can still bind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Queen's Park Place (55 Blackberry Drive) is published as a 55+ building — confirm the filed bylaw wording and exemptions on the Form B before assuming who may live in a unit there. No other Section 2 building is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2998065-55-blackberry-drive-301-new-westminster-bc-v3l-5s7/brochure
- **City zoning and plans:** zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001; the governing plan is Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), including its Development Permit Areas (Schedule B) for the mainland. Houses on the slope sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5, and the RS districts allow secondary suites, with laneway / carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw. The bylaw's townhouse districts are **RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B and RT-2C (ss.410–413)**, with **RT-3, RT-3A and RT-3B in Queensborough (ss.417–419)** — there are no "RT-A / RT-B" districts in this bylaw, and any document using those names is wrong. The RT districts regulate density, height and coverage rather than setting a minimum site area: RT-2 (s.410) sets a base density of 18 units per net acre or a floor space ratio of 0.6, height capped at 25 feet and site coverage at 40 percent. The 836.10 m² (9,000 sq. ft.) figure in the bylaw is a density threshold in the RM multiple-dwelling districts (e.g., s.462.14), **not** a townhouse minimum. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fraserview-nw.md)
- **Floodplain:** the City states that both Queensborough and sections of the mainland are included within the provincial floodplain, and that the city covers twelve square kilometres of which five are within the flood boundaries on the Ministry of Environment Floodplain Map; the City's floodplain study Zone 2 covers the historically commercial and industrial Fraser River land at the west end of the city. Lot-level flood status for a specific Fraserview property is UNVERIFIED — confirm the building's flood construction level and the strata's insurance and deductible position before subject removal, especially on the Jamieson Court / Miner Street waterfront-edge pocket. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fraserview-nw.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility and City water/sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fraserview-nw.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 stock is 1989–2016 resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale project inside Fraserview NW was verified in the research file — REDMA is included because The Lookout's surviving source is a presale-era profile and buyers cross-shop presales elsewhere in New Westminster. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, a waterfront-edge building's floodplain position can affect a lender's and insurer's view of a specific property — no source checked publishes a building-by-building position here, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Fraserview NW the same as Vancouver's Fraserview?** No. Fraserview NW is New Westminster's Fraserview sub-area, on the south slope above the North Arm of the Fraser River, in School District 40; Vancouver's Fraserview is a separate Vancouver East sub-area with its own file, its own buildings and its own prices. The research file opens with this warning because the two are routinely confused in comp sets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fraserview-nw.md)
- **Q: What condo stock does the sub-area actually have?** Nine verified apartment-condo entries in two pockets: Palace Quay, Queen's Park Place and Fraserview Park on the Jamieson Court / Miner Street waterfront edge, and six Victoria Hill buildings on the hill (Victoria Hill, The Lookout, The Carlyle, The Grove at Victoria Hill, The Whittaker, Parkside at Victoria Hill) — plus Victoria Hill's Francis Way townhomes. SnapStats counted 53 condos & townhomes listed and 10 sold in the sub-area in September 2026 (19%). Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Palace Quay one building or two?** One complex at two addresses — 69 and 71 Jamieson Court — with 220 units per both the building directory and the furnished-rental directory. Sources disagree on the year (1991 in the rental directory and several listings, 1992 in the directory, 1993 in one listing); the builder is recorded as Bosa Development and the form as an 18-storey concrete high-rise. Verify the strata plan number for the specific unit before relying on a year. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court and https://rentitfurnished.com/vancouver/building/palace-quay
- **Q: Can I live at Queen's Park Place if I am under 55?** The building is published as a 55+ building (55 Blackberry Drive, built 1994). A 55+ bylaw under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 is the one valid age bylaw in BC, and its exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children of a 55+ resident. Whether you qualify is a Form B / filed-bylaw question — read them before writing. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2998065-55-blackberry-drive-301-new-westminster-bc-v3l-5s7/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Palace Quay or Victoria Hill unit long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, even though both buildings' directory lines read "rentals allowed w/restrictions." Short-term rentals are different: a bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws on the Form B govern that question. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/palace-quay-71-jamieson-court and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Victoria Hill buildings?** Published unit-level positions: cats OK at The Carlyle (280 Ross Drive), at The Grove (265 Ross Drive) and at Fraserview Park (74 Miner Street); a Palace Quay listing records 2 pets OK with no size restrictions. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC and building-level published sets are thin — the registered bylaws govern for the specific building. https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/280-ross-drive/1007 and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3093921-71-jamieson-court-1404-new-westminster-fraserview-nw-homes-for-sale-649900/brochure
- **Q: What is the Boiler House that Victoria Hill listings keep mentioning?** The shared amenity centre for the Victoria Hill buildings: listings for The Whittaker and The Grove describe its gym, basketball court, theatre, library and party room, and the Francis Way townhome listing describes the same access — one amenity centre serves several separate stratas, which is why the amenity lists of neighbouring buildings read alike. https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/225-francis-way/217 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3020564-245-francis-way-44-new-westminster-bc-v3l-0a7/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraserview NW address?** School District 40 catchments are address-based. The verified locator result in the research file is for 1010 Hamilton Street: Lord Kelvin Elementary (K–5), Fraser River Middle (6–8) and New Westminster Secondary (9–12), with Early French Immersion at École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary and Montessori (K–5) at Connaught Heights Elementary for that address. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's stock is one developer's hill plus one developer's court.** Six of the nine verified apartment entries are Onni's Victoria Hill buildings (2005/2007–2016, sharing the Boiler House amenity centre), and the largest waterfront-edge building, Palace Quay (220 units), is a Bosa building — cross-building comparisons inside this sub-area are unusually direct because so much stock shares two builders. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The Lookout's commonly quoted address is its sales centre.** The 2013 project profile puts The Lookout's buildings at 22 East Royal Avenue and its sales centre at 295 Francis Way — listings and directories that file The Lookout under Francis Way are using the sales-centre address. https://onni.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/Full-Article-small-ad-removed.pdf
- **The townhouse zoning names are routinely gotten wrong for this city.** New Westminster's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C (mainland) and RT-3/3A/3B (Queensborough) under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — an earlier program draft's "RT-A/RT-B" districts do not exist, and the 836.10 m² figure sometimes quoted as a townhouse minimum is an RM-district density threshold (s.462.14). Any Fraserview document using RT-A/RT-B should be sent back. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/fraserview-nw.md)
- **One Royal Avenue complex wears three addresses and two years.** The Victoria Hill building is filed at 15 Royal Avenue (directory, built 2007), at 11 E Royal Avenue under "The Residences Of Victoria Hill," and at 14 E Royal Avenue with a 2005 year in one listing — a buyer searching one address sees a third of the complex's listings. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/victoria-hill-15-east-royal-ave and https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/11-e-royal-avenue/402
