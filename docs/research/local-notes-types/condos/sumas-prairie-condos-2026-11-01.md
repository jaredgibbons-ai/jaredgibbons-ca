## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified — the rule that leads this section, because it is why the prairie has no condo stock.** On land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, a residence cannot be turned into strata lots (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A prairie farmhouse is a house on land under the ALC residence frame, permanently — it cannot be strata-subdivided into condo units, and no ALR parcel is condo stock. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/sumas-prairie.md)
- **The ALC housing frame on ALR parcels:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less; a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only within the principal residence; and an additional detached residence may be up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. ALR status for any specific Sumas Prairie parcel: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Live-page land claims NOT carried as verified:** the live page states Sumas Prairie "lies on the former Sumas Lake bed (drained in 1924)" and is "almost entirely within the Agricultural Land Reserve (A2 zone) and the City of Abbotsford Floodplain Development Permit Area, protected by the Barrowtown Pump Station and Sumas Dike." The Barrowtown Pump Station's role is corroborated by the City source in Section 1, but the drained-in-1924 date, the "almost entirely" ALR share, the A2-zone framing for the whole prairie, and the Floodplain Development Permit Area claim were not confirmed on an official source opened in this pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/sumas-prairie.md)
- **Zoning frame:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies city-wide, including its agricultural zones. The City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2) — so most prairie parcels cannot be split further in any form, strata or fee-simple. The zone of any specific parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/sumas-prairie.md)
- **Suites and coach/garden suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, a secondary suite must be less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — subject on ALR land to the ALC frame above (suite only within the principal residence). - **The strata rules, for contrast — they bite only if the same buyer buys strata in urban Abbotsford:** **Form B** (Strata Property Act s. 59) states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022. **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful, with exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion).
