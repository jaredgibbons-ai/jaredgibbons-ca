# Moody Park — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Moody Park is a City of New Westminster neighbourhood centred on Moody Park itself, in the Uptown-west area between 6th Avenue and 8th Avenue, bounded in the City's heritage context material by 8th Avenue, 8th Street, 10th Street and 6th Avenue. Uptown is to the east, Brow of the Hill to the west, Glenbrooke North to the north, and Downtown and the Quay down the hill to the south. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: this is a mainly detached-house area — in September 2026 the sub-area recorded 9 detached listings against only 4 condos & townhomes listings, and no condo/townhome sales. The apartment stock sits on the edges: Eighth Street and Eighth Avenue around the park (The Diplomat, Moody Gardens, The Doncaster, Villa Franciscan) and Eighth Avenue east toward 12th Street (Fraser Gardens). There are no towers in the sub-area; the verified stock is low-rise. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below; market figures as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report via (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- New Westminster community-level context (SnapStats publishes medians at community level only, not for Moody Park): Condos & Townhomes median sale price $560,000, $587/sqft, sale-to-list 95%, 27 days on market in September 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- Landmarks and services on the edges: Moody Park itself (outdoor pool, spray park, sports fields), Moody Park Arena at 701 Eighth Avenue, the New Westminster Public Library Main Branch at 716 Sixth Avenue, and the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue, fully opened May 2024. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- Transit: no SkyTrain station is inside Moody Park; the nearest are New Westminster Station and Columbia Station (Expo Line) downtown. TransLink routes 105 (Uptown / New Westminster Station) and 106 (Edmonds Station / New Westminster Station) serve the adjoining Uptown corridors. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Moody Park's 1970s stock (The Diplomat 1977, The Doncaster 1977, Villa Franciscan 1976) this is the central diligence document. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. All five buildings in Section 2 exceed five lots on the counts published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The Doncaster's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" reflects older bylaw wording — any long-term rental restriction in it is unenforceable; The Diplomat's directory line already records rentals allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Moody Park building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area, and zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310 — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² and FSR 0.5, and the RS districts allow secondary suites with laneway/carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw); the edge apartment buildings sit in multiple-unit residential districts — RM-1 (s.420) sets maximum site coverage of 40% and density of 35 units per net acre / FSR 1.2. Uptown adjoining the area is identified in the OCP as a Frequent Transit Development Area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Heritage:** Moody Park is one of the neighbourhoods documented in the City's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, and the City protects heritage properties through its heritage program — heritage status must be checked property by property before planning work on an older building or house. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster also operates its own electrical utility and City water/sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones only the city's low-lying edges (Queensborough, the west-end Fraser River industrial land, the Quay waterfront, and the Brunette River industrial area); no floodplain zone for the Moody Park plateau was confirmed on a City source — lot-level status UNVERIFIED, check with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every building in Section 2 is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No specific new-development project inside Moody Park was verified on a City development-application page in the research file — if presale product appears, REDMA is the frame. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md) and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many condos are there in Moody Park, really?** Very few. The verified apartment stock is five low-rise buildings on the Eighth Street / Eighth Avenue edges (The Diplomat, Moody Gardens, The Doncaster, Villa Franciscan, Fraser Gardens), and September 2026 showed only 4 condos & townhomes listings in the whole sub-area against 9 detached listings. This is a house neighbourhood with a thin condo edge. Sources: Section 2; (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Q: Can I have a pet at The Diplomat?** The building directory records pets not allowed at The Diplomat (707 Eighth Street), and a listing feature sheet for the building says the same ("sorry no pets"). Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC — unlike rental bylaws — so confirm on the Form B, but plan on no pets. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/diplomat-707-eighth-street
- **Q: What about pets at The Doncaster or Moody Gardens?** The Doncaster's directory line is pets allowed with restrictions. At Moody Gardens, one current listing states "2 small pets allowed" — listing-level only; the registered bylaw position is UNCONFIRMED until the strata package is read. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/the-doncaster-620-eighth-ave and https://vancouvernashdom.com/new-westminster-uptown-apartments/SearchResults.form/?_pg=6
- **Q: Can I rent out a Moody Park condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. The Diplomat's directory line already records rentals allowed; The Doncaster's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line is older wording. Short-term rentals are a separate question and can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How old is this stock, and what does that mean for levies?** The dated buildings are 1976 (Villa Franciscan), 1977 (The Diplomat, The Doncaster), 1983 (Fraser Gardens) and 1987 (Moody Gardens) — 40-to-50-year-old low-rises. Stratas with 5+ lots must now hold a depreciation report renewed every five years and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF annually; read both documents before subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is 621 Eighth Street (Devonshire House) a condo I can buy?** No — it is a rental apartment building (84 units, built 1967, offered as rentals), not a strata. It shows up in area searches by address but no strata lots are sold there. https://www.apartments.com/devonshire-house-new-westminster-bc/dhbfx2j/
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** There is no SkyTrain station in Moody Park. Routes 105 and 106 connect the Uptown corridors to New Westminster Station and Columbia Station (Expo Line) downtown at the foot of the hill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Moody Park condo?** School District 40 catchments are address-based. The verified locator run for 1714 Eighth Avenue returned École Lord Tweedsmuir Elementary (K–6, which also offers Early French Immersion), Fraser River Middle (6–8) and New Westminster Secondary (9–12) — SD40's only secondary school, a 1,900-seat replacement building at 820 Sixth Street that opened January 11, 2021 and is already operating above capacity per the district's long-range plan. Run the building's exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Q: What were New Westminster taxes due, and what is the penalty?** July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City also runs its own electrical utility, so the utility bill structure differs from BC Hydro cities. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Q: Do the New Westminster community stats describe Moody Park pricing?** Only loosely. SnapStats publishes medians at community level only: New Westminster Condos & Townhomes median was $560,000 in September 2026, but Moody Park itself had 0 condo/townhome sales that month, so there is no sub-area median — price off the specific building's own sale history instead. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The condo stock hides on the park's edges, not in the middle.** The residential streets between 6th and 10th are RS-zoned houses (RS-1: 557.40 m² minimum site, FSR 0.5); every verified apartment building sits on Eighth Street, Eighth Avenue or the avenues' edges under the multiple-unit RM districts. A "Moody Park condo" search that returns interior-street product is returning something else. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Two of the five buildings are the same age as each other and share a manager pattern worth checking.** The Diplomat (1977, NW920) is managed by Quay Pacific Property Management and Moody Gardens (1987, NWS2534) by Red Door Management per the research file's strata records — in small 1970s–80s low-rises, the manager and the depreciation report tell you more than the listing photos. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **The city's newest recreation asset is at the area's east edge.** The təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue fully opened in May 2024 — at 10,644 sq. m. the City's largest capital project to date, with an eight-lane 50-metre pool, leisure pool, fitness centre and gymnasia — a short walk from the Eighth Avenue buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **The replacement secondary school is already over capacity.** New Westminster Secondary (820 Sixth Street, opened January 11, 2021, $106.5 million, 1,900 seats) is SD40's only secondary school and its long-range facilities plan notes it is already operating above capacity — for family buyers the elementary catchment (Lord Tweedsmuir, with Early French Immersion on site) is the real differentiator between Moody Park addresses. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **Moody Park is a documented heritage neighbourhood.** The City lists Moody Park among its Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements — heritage status is checked property by property, and work on a listed property can need heritage approvals, which matters for strata exterior-work planning in the older low-rises. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
- **No floodplain zone covers the plateau.** The City's floodplain study zones only the low-lying edges (Queensborough, the Quay, the Brunette River and west-end industrial lands) — Moody Park's plateau carries no confirmed floodplain zone, a genuine contrast with the Quay buildings down the hill, though lot-level status is still a City check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/moody-park.md)
