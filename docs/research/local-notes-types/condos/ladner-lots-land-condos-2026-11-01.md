## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Land rules lead — ALR strata bar:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); Strata Property Act s.239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1) are the statutory frame carried in this program. An urban bare-land strata lot and an ALR parcel in the same feed sit under different rules — do not assume a strata split works on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **ALR residence caps:** one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite only within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **BC Assessment farm thresholds:** $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha if over 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must file a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **Zoning / OCP:** Delta OCP Bylaw No. 8400, 2024 (above); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". **Zoning bylaw number — CONTESTED, flagged, not asserted:** City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title reads "8400, 2025" — the two collide (8400 is the OCP number); neither is asserted as settled, and a parcel's zone is read off the City's zoning maps. Parcel-level ALR status, zoning, Flood Construction Level and geotechnical/preload position: UNVERIFIED for any specific lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **Delta SSMUH, DCCs and tree protection:** UNVERIFIED as verified rules in the research file — the live page's account (suites/garden suites/duplexes, DCCs, Tree Protection Bylaw permits) is site copy only, with no official Delta bylaw source opened in that pass; verify with the City for the specific lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **GST on land — only as sources state it:** GST on a specific lot is UNVERIFIED in the research file; the live page states as site copy only that GST (5%) commonly applies to newly subdivided vacant residential lots sold by a developer or builder — verify the seller's GST position and put explicit GST language in the contract for the specific lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-lots-land.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **Form B (for any strata lot, e.g. a bare-land strata lot):** Strata Property Act s.59 — the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached) within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **55+ bylaws:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children (SPA Regulation 7.01 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/ladner-lots-land.md)
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST rebates (new builds on a lot):** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — the March 12 secondary date is superseded) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. No worked example is given — no Ladner land median exists in the permitted source. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
