## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

For this topic the operative rules are the ALR rules that keep condos off farm land where the ALR applies, then one labelled municipal example, then the standard BC strata rules stated only for contrast.

- **ALR stratification bar (LEADS this section — the governing fact for this file, where the parcel is in the ALR):** ALR residences cannot be stratified. ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 states that strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR; a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). Say it correctly: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission**. Off the ALR — and this feed contains a parcel its card states is not in the ALR — strata follows the ordinary Strata Property Act route; the bar is an ALR bar, not a farm bar. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR residence frame (ALR parcels only):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction); a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence; and at most one additional residence is permitted, of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. Those are houses on one title — neither is a condo unit, and neither can be sold separately without the subdivision gate above. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm classification:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program — it is not automatic with a farm purchase, and it does not follow the portal's "farms" label. The income thresholds are $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. One describable card claims hobby farm status in its own text and another refers to a small-fruit classification, but no source classifies the feed's properties as a group. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Municipal example — Abbotsford only (labelled municipal example, never portal-wide):** for the Abbotsford portion of this portal's ground, the current Official Community Plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025 — the 2016 OCP is NOT current. In June 2024 Council adopted zoning amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing and Transit-Oriented Areas legislation; a brief names those amendments as Bylaw 3560-2024, but that number is UNCONFIRMED on the City pages opened and is never asserted in this file. Pitt Meadows, Maple Ridge and Chilliwack parcels in the feed sit under their own municipalities' bylaws, which were not opened for a portal-wide statement — zoning for this portal is UNVERIFIED as any general statement. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford (Poultry farms file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/poultry-farms-condos-2026-11-01.md) (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/farms.md)
- **Water, septic, soil and floodplain:** UNVERIFIED in this file as stated general rules — no Province water-licensing, health-authority, soil or flood source was opened for this topic; each is a per-parcel diligence item on any farm purchase from this portal. - **Form B (contrast only — no strata lot exists in this topic):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a "farm condo" is ever marketed through this portal, the Form B and the strata plan number are the first proof to demand. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata-wide, contrast only):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals and age (strata-wide, contrast only):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC, and the only valid age bylaw is a 55+ bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither rule touches a freehold farm parcel — there is no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

How GST is apportioned between land, buildings and residence on a specific farm purchase: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. How PTT is apportioned across a farm purchase's land, buildings and residence: UNVERIFIED in this file — a lawyer/accountant question on the actual asset allocation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC (residence side only):** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. Whether any of these programs applies to a particular farm purchase is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no source checked rules on farm-parcel eligibility. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any condo or strata component in a farm purchase from this portal?** No verified one. The purchase package in the sources is freehold land, farm buildings and a residence — and this portal's actual feed is freehold land parcels, acreages and houses. There is no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fee and no depreciation report, and on ALR land residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission (Bulletin 09, s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/farms.md)

- **Q: Is every farm parcel in the ALR?** No — and this feed proves it: the Silver Valley hobby-farm card states its property is NOT in the ALR. ALR status is a per-parcel check on the ALC maps by PID or civic address, never a portal assumption, and the residence and stratification rules in Section 3 bite only where the parcel is actually in the reserve. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/farms.md)
- **Q: Does buying farm land automatically keep its farm classification?** No — classification is a voluntary, property-by-property BC Assessment program with income thresholds by operation size, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase or the classification can be removed. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What income must a farm show to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, with late applications not accepted. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What residence can an ALR farm parcel have?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules possibly more restrictive — and only where the parcel is actually in the ALR. All on one title — none of it strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify a farm parcel later, or use bare-land strata instead?** On ALR land, no route avoids the gate: depositing any strata plan — building or bare-land — subdivides the land (Strata Property Act s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6) and requires Commission permission after an application or a permission in the ALR General Regulation. Bare-land strata is a genuine strata form off the ALR, where it follows the ordinary Strata Property Act route. An approval for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Which municipality's zoning governs a portal property?** The municipality the parcel sits in — this feed alone spans Abbotsford, Pitt Meadows, Maple Ridge and Chilliwack, each with its own zoning bylaw, and no municipal bylaw was opened for a portal-wide statement, so zoning is UNVERIFIED as any general claim here.
