- The Crest is a residential sub-area in south-east Burnaby, on the Edmonds / East Burnaby side of the city near the New Westminster border; the residential area surrounds The Crest Centre, around 10th Avenue, 6th Street and Edmonds Street. Nearby: Edmonds town centre to the west, East Burnaby and Cariboo to the north, and New Westminster across the municipal border to the east and south-east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)

The research file describes low-rise apartment buildings near The Crest Centre and along 10th Avenue as a housing pattern, but no apartment building there was verified by name on a usable source (see Section 2). In September 2026 the sub-area recorded 6 condos & townhomes listings and 2 condos & townhomes solds (a 33% sales ratio), against 14 detached listings and 2 detached solds (14%) — both attached figures rest on very small counts and should not be read as a stable market signal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)

Edmonds Station (Expo Line) is west of the sub-area and is its SkyTrain link; no SkyTrain station sits inside The Crest. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- Civic anchors: the Tommy Douglas Branch of Burnaby Public Library, 7311 Kingsway, serves this quadrant from Edmonds, west of the sub-area; the Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #201-7315 Edmonds St, and the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, in central Burnaby. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in The Crest's wood-frame townhouse stock (Deerfield is expressly a wood-frame development) the envelope, roofing and drainage cycles in the depreciation report are the diligence centre. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the named townhouse complexes in Section 2 are above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by a strata (strata short-term rental rules are a separate layer from any municipal licensing). Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" or "no rentals" for a Crest complex reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and must be read as void — including any such line in a listing for the Section 2 complexes, none of which published one in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 is current:** Burnaby's new Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is repealed — do not cite it. Under the small-scale multi-unit housing framework the research file records (carried from the June 2024 consolidation of the former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District), R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. City-wide direction is the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, which replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Edmonds Community Plan covers the town centre plan area west of The Crest. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does not apply here — that is a City of Vancouver tax, and this is Burnaby. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Crest stock in Section 2 is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to any new stratified multiplex product built on Crest R1 lots, not to the Section 2 resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A market residential presale inside The Crest verified on a developer source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED — REDMA is the frame if one appears. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — a published range of 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Burnaby rewrote its zoning bylaw in 2026, and the old one is repealed.** Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026 — any Crest document, listing or appraisal still citing Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is citing a repealed bylaw. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) sits above it and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **The sub-area's name is a shopping plaza, not a City neighbourhood.** "The Crest" matches The Crest Centre plaza in the 10th Avenue / Canada Way area, and the City has not confirmed it as an official neighbourhood name — its owner, anchor tenant and store list were also not confirmed on an official source in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. Searches and documents should carry "Burnaby" and a street name, not the sub-area name alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **Two sales a month is the whole market signal.** September 2026's Crest figures (2 attached solds, 2 detached solds) are the smallest samples in the Burnaby files of this program — price off a longer window and East Burnaby / Edmonds comparables, not one month, and attach the small-sample warning whenever the 33% attached sales ratio is quoted. - **The Edmonds towers are not Crest stock, however the listing is written.** Kings Crossing and its neighbouring towers stand west of the sub-area at Edmonds Station and are filed under Edmonds BE in current listings — a Crest search that imports their prices, ages or amenity sets is comparing a station-area tower market to a house-and-townhouse pocket. https://www.strawhomes.com/2211-7358-edmonds-street-burnaby-bc-v3n-0h1/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
