## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — for a Delta-side strata townhome (South Grove, The Pillars), the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a Tsawwassen Lands leasehold townhome, the Form B package sits alongside the lease documents — both sets are the diligence, neither replaces the other. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. South Grove (2021) and Tsawwassen Landing (2022) are young stratas whose first-cycle reports and CRF build-up are the fee story; The Pillars (1996) is read the other way — three decades of component history. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever a directory or older bylaw set says (the rule that voids the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded elsewhere in Tsawwassen for Heron Cove and Tsawwassen Greene). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. For a leasehold townhome, check the lease documents as well — a lease term can restrict what a bylaw no longer can, and no lease term for either North complex was read in this pass: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful; exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED per complex until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan (Delta side only):** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered area-plan schedules; NO Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 in the current OCP (that was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. None of this describes Tsawwassen Lands, where TFN law applies instead. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017", while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged and neither number is asserted as settled; Delta-side zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps in either case. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary
- **SSMUH:** Delta Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots on the Delta side. This does not change a strata townhome's own lot, but it is the infill pressure around the established Delta-side complexes. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Flood and streamside frame (Delta side):** Delta operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure, and all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — read the depreciation report and Form B against the complex's setting in that system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this band that is the developer-sale question on any remaining new North product. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. GST treatment of a leasehold purchase on Tsawwassen Lands is transaction-specific — UNCONFIRMED here; confirm with a CPA/notary on the specific unit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so only the lower slice of this band can use the program at all, and a purchase at the band's upper end cannot. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. PTT application to a Tsawwassen Lands leasehold transfer is transaction-specific — confirm on the specific purchase rather than assuming the Delta-side treatment. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for a developer-phase purchase such as a Boardwalk phase sold on developer contracts (Boardwalk6's presale record is the named example in this band). https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Leasehold financing is lender-by-lender — a North purchase needs the lender's leasehold position confirmed before subject removal; no lender policy is stated here. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Property taxes:** Delta side — 2026 due date July 2, 2026, 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. Tsawwassen Lands side — tax administration runs under Tsawwassen First Nation, not the City; the live page's claim that TFN taxes are set to match City of Delta rates is UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Strata disputes** in BC can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
