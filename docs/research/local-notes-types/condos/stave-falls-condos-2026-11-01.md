## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the fee-simple acreages and pad-rent manufactured homes that make up this community's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Park age rules in a manufactured-home park are a park-tenancy matter under the park's own documents, not a strata bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning (local):** Stave Falls is inside the City of Mission, so the City's Official Community Plan and zoning govern — per this program's brief, Mission's current OCP is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, with the Rural Mission Strategy providing direction for rural communities including Stave Falls. A specific lot's OCP designation and zone: UNVERIFIED in the research pass (City site bot-check; strategy document not opened) — confirm with the City before quoting density or second-dwelling potential. The FVRD/Province frame used for Dewdney-Deroche and Lake Errock in this batch does NOT apply here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/stave-falls.md)
- **Mission property taxes:** Stave Falls properties are inside the City of Mission and pay municipal taxes to the City. City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty on balances unpaid after July 2 — the City's tax page states: "A 10% penalty will be added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026." The flat 10% is the penalty under the Community Charter general tax collection scheme (ss. 233–234) as set by the Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has no alternative-scheme bylaw, and the rural-area penalty rules (5% + 5% to October 31, paid to the Province) do not apply inside the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/stave-falls.md)
- **Manufactured-home pad rent (MHPTA):** for the pad-rent parks excluded in Section 2, the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rent-increase limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — confirmed from the park's own tenancy documents for any specific pad. (Program brief, MHPTA pad-rent limits.)
- **ALR housing rules:** parcel-level ALR status is per address: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping. Where land is in the ALR: one principal residence capped at 500 m², plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no project in this area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. Where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% (plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies). Manufactured homes on rented pads commonly face different lender treatment than fee-simple homes — confirm financing for the specific tenure before writing. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
