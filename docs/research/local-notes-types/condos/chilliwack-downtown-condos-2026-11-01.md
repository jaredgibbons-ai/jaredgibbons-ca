## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 1980s Spadina stock this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in Chilliwack Downtown may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Two of the six counted buildings are 55+ (Ivy Green and Spadina Regency) and the third building on their street (Chilliwack Gardens) expressly is not — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for each. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500 — specifically its Schedule B3 Downtown Neighbourhood Plan for this ground, with the Schedule D-6 Downtown development permit area applying in the core. Cite the schedules correctly: the Sardis plan is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. The live page's "Urban Quarter" designation wording was not confirmed on the City OCP page opened in this pass: UNVERIFIED as stated. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-downtown.md)
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024), allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. **Secondary suites and ADUs:** under Bylaw 5000 a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite); garden suites are capped at 100 m²; ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite; and no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-downtown.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. The City also levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-downtown.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all Section 2 buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project in Chilliwack Downtown was verified in this pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies — the published range is 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
