## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The frame that replaces the strata rules on this page — MHPTA first:** manufactured home site tenancies are governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act; the Form B, depreciation-report and CRF rules below are stated because this is a condos-program file and because buyers routinely confuse the two regimes — none of them applies to a pad-rental site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Pad-rent increases (MHPTA / Residential Tenancy Branch):** a park landlord may increase rent only once every 12 months, only with 3 full months' written notice, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Pad-rent increase limits — VERIFIED, re-confirmed in this pass on the Province's manufactured-home rent-increase page:** the 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus a proportional amount; the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. This file opened that Province page in this pass and read the same two limits directly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **NO pad-rent dollar figure is ever published in this file:** no official source publishes a pad-rent figure, average or range for these parks, this page states no pad rent for any park, and the only pad-rent figure that matters for a purchase is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s. 28):** assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; under s. 28(1)(b) a tenant may assign only with the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — the Act's word is "director," never "arbitrator"; under s. 28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting to an assignment, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Park closure / change of use (MHPTA s. 42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date of the notice; a tenant may end the tenancy early on at least 10 days' written notice, the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days, and additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the $20,000 may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **The monthly-cost structure a park buyer carries (structure, not figures):** pad rent under the regulated increase formula above, plus home insurance, plus property tax on the home itself, plus utilities as the specific park's site tenancy agreement allocates them — no dollar figure for any element is stated here because no official source publishes one for these parks. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Form B (does NOT apply to a pad-rental site — stated to prevent the confusion):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase of a strata lot the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A park buyer gets the site tenancy agreement and the assignment consent process instead; the one strata variant in the program's Surrey record, the bare-land strata Sea Ridge Bays, is the exception where strata documents do apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata only — do not apply to pad-rental parks):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. A pad-rental park has no CRF — the park's capital planning is the park owner's, which is exactly why the MHPTA closure and increase rules above carry the buyer-protection weight on this page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata rule, stated for contrast):** since November 24, 2022, rental-restriction bylaws are void for strata property anywhere in BC — that voiding is a Strata Property Act change and does not create any right inside a manufactured home park, where the site tenancy agreement and the MHPTA govern instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (strata rule, stated for contrast):** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. A park's age rule is not an SPA bylaw: this page states no age rule for any park, every per-park age position in this file is UNCONFIRMED (including the held Breakaway Bays conflict in Section 2), and a park's age position can only be taken from the park's own documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Whether either rebate applies to a specific manufactured home purchase is a CRA-eligibility question on that home's facts; neither rebate is stated in the research file as applying to any park or home on this page, and none is claimed here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many mobile home parks does this page list?** 73 entries in page order, covering Abbotsford, Agassiz/Kent, Chilliwack, Coquitlam, Cultus Lake, Soowahlie, Hope, Langley, Maple Ridge, Mission, Rosedale, Ruskin and Surrey — with Cedar Grove and Mountainview Estates in Chilliwack each listed twice, so the distinct count is lower, and the list is site copy only because no official park registry exists. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: Is a mobile home in a park a condo?** No — a pad-rental park home is a manufactured home on a rented site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA; it is not a strata lot, park rules are not SPA bylaws, and there is no Form B, depreciation report or CRF for the site. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Fraser Valley parks?** No figure is stated in this file: this page publishes no pad rent for any park, no official source publishes a pad-rent figure, average or range, and the ranges on sibling pages of this site are unverified site copy (flagged in Section 5, not repeated as numbers here) — the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: How much can a park raise pad rent each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' written notice, once every 12 months, with no rounding up and no carry-forward of an unused portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What transfers to me when I buy a home in a park?** The site tenancy, by assignment under MHPTA s. 28: you need the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director), the owner cannot charge a consent fee, and the pad rent and the scheduled date of the next increase carry over unchanged to you as the new tenant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s. 42 the landlord must first have all required government permits and approvals, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on 10 days' written notice, has 15 days to dispute the notice, and may be eligible for additional compensation of the assessed value minus the $20,000 through dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: Which parks on this page are verified?** None from the page itself — every name and address is site copy with no operator source opened; the program's verified positions, stated as its record, are the pad-rental parks named in the count line (Surrey: Westwood Court, Westwood Estates, Newton MHP; Langley: Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park, Pineridge parks; Abbotsford: Paradise Park, Georgian Park, Maplewood Estates), while the held names (including Cedar Lane Park, Deer Creek Estates, Forest Green Estates, Breakaway Bays and Cottonwood Village) stay uncounted and unmerged, Newton MHP's park-level details stay UNCONFIRMED, and Cottonwood's Chilliwack placement stays UNRESOLVED. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Fraser Valley mobile homes?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category; the only figures in this file are all-residential community context for September 2026 (for example Langley Detached median $1,345,000 and Condos & Townhomes median $611,000, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report), and neither describes park homes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
- **Q: Can I rely on the directory's addresses as printed?** Not without checking: the directory repeats two Chilliwack parks, gives one park two different postal codes, files a Mission-address park inside the Hope sequence and prints one Langley postal code that sits outside Langley's usual code ranges — confirm the park's legal name and address from the listing and the park itself before writing an offer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.md)
