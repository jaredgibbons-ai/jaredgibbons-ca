## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in the hub's three areas may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental half of the St. Andrews, Calera and Compass directory lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted in its source file — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The Cloverdale Town Centre Plan is being updated (boundary expanded July 22, 2024); the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in the July 8, 2024 zoning changes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-neighbourhood-guides.md)
- **SSMUH and suites (Surrey-wide, all three areas):** Surrey allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-neighbourhood-guides.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required before operating. A strata short-term rental bylaw is a separate, building-level check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/cloverdale-lots-land.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $740,000 (the Cloverdale combined attached community median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,800 (2% × $540,000) = **$12,800 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale is cross-referenced as stock in Section 2. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles and CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
