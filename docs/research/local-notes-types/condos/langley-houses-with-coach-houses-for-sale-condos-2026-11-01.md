## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A coach-house property produces no Form B — it is not strata — so the equivalent diligence here is the building permit and final inspection for the second unit itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF — strata-corporation duties that do not attach to a fee-simple house with a coach house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or bylaw line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Renting a coach house is not governed by a strata bylaw at all: it is governed by the unit's zoning permission and permit, which is why the permit check in Section 2 comes first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building on this page is strata apartment stock, so no 55+ bylaw is recorded for this page. Langley City's four verified 55+ strata are in the langleycity file and are never re-counted in a Township file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (Langley City file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langleycity-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township coach house definition (settled program position):** in Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100's own text, searched in full in the rules-gaps pass, contains no size cap for a coach house — but the broader claim that no size cap exists anywhere in Bylaw 2500 is UNVERIFIED, because the individual zone sections, where such a cap would sit, were not opened in that pass.
