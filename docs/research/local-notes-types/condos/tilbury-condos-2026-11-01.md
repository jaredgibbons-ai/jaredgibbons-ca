# Tilbury — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Tilbury is the City of Delta's industrial district on the south arm of the Fraser River, between Ladner and North Delta; its streets include River Road / River Way, Tilbury Road, Hopcott Road, Progress Way, Vantage Way and 72 Street / 80 Street, in the Highway 17 (South Fraser Perimeter Road) corridor, with Tilbury Island in the Fraser River. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md)
- The City treats Tilbury as an industrial area — the Tilbury Industrial Park / Tilbury Island lands — and it is not one of the residential neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: nowhere inside the Tilbury industrial lands. There is no residential condo stock to map — see Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md)
- Nearby areas are Ladner (East Delta) to the south-west, Annieville and Nordel to the east along River Road, and Annacis Island across the Highway 91 corridor to the north-east. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/47276/
- The one City recreation facility inside the industrial area is Tilbury Ice, the arena at 7187 Vantage Way at 72 Street — a City lease posting for its restaurant space describes the surrounding area as mainly industrial warehouse space, off River Road at 72 Street and Vantage Way. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf

## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero residential condo stock — stated plainly: no apartment condo building, townhome complex or other residential strata was confirmed inside the Tilbury industrial lands in any source checked, and no residential housing mix as a market was confirmed in City of Delta sources.** https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

- What trades instead is industrial: strata industrial units (small-bay and mid-bay warehouse/office strata complexes on streets like Progress Way and Vantage Way) and freehold industrial lots. Strata industrial units are NOT residential condos and are not counted as condo stock in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- A "small residential pocket" inside Tilbury is NOT CONFIRMED from City sources; dwellings in the wider Tilbury postal area sit on the surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Tilbury sub-area row — its Delta coverage is the North Delta community and its four residential sub-areas only — and SnapStats' residential categories do not cover this industrial area in any case. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md)
- No public school, City park, library branch, shopping centre or grocery store inside the Tilbury industrial lands was confirmed in official sources. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Why there is no condo stock — the city layer:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400) states Delta's industries are primarily located along River Road East, in Tilbury Industrial Park, Annacis Island and Roberts Bank; Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares in Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory, approximately 13% of the region's inventory. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Zoning:** City material for Tilbury-area properties references the I2 and I7 industrial zone codes under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; the full zone names and minimum lot standards for Tilbury were UNVERIFIED from a City zone sheet — confirm the zone for any parcel on the City zoning maps. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **No lettered area-plan schedules:** the current OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), which covers Tilbury as industrial land; the former area plans sat under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH does not create condos here:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) apply to single-detached and duplex lots; Tilbury's industrial lands are not single-detached residential lots, so SSMUH is not a route to housing in the industrial park. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Strata law still applies — to industrial strata:** strata industrial ownership follows the Strata Property Act framework — owners own their strata lots and together own common property as a strata corporation — and on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote — this reaches industrial stratas too. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental and age bylaws (for completeness):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the only permitted age bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 is a 55+ bylaw — none of this creates a residential use on industrial land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Buyer taxes (general BC layer, for buyers redirected to residential areas):** 5% GST applies to new homes only, not resales; the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 — CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Royal Assent was March 13, 2026 (secondary sources say March 12; CRA's date governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. BC Property Transfer Tax runs 1% / 2% / 3% / 5% by band, with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption on the first $500,000 to $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in Tilbury at all?** No — not inside the industrial lands. The OCP treats Tilbury as industrial land, and no residential housing mix was confirmed inside it in City sources. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: A listing says "Tilbury" but shows a house — how?** Postal-area naming is wider than the industrial park: dwellings carrying a Tilbury-area address sit on surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands. Check which side of the industrial boundary the parcel is on before treating it as Tilbury product. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Are the strata bays on Progress Way / Vantage Way a cheap way to "buy a condo"?** They are strata industrial units, not homes: permitted uses, outdoor storage and parking differ by zone under Bylaw No. 7600 (City material references I2 and I7 codes in Tilbury), and a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Where is the nearest residential condo stock?** The nearest verified condo concentrations are covered in their own files in this series (Annieville, Nordel and Scottsdale to the east in North Delta; Ladner's sub-areas to the south-west). No source checked names a specific nearest building to Tilbury, so any "closest building" claim is UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md)
- **Q: Is there transit into Tilbury for workers?** A TransLink route serving inside the Tilbury industrial lands was not confirmed from a timetable in the research file; the nearest exchanges named are Ladner Exchange and Scottsdale Exchange. https://www.translink.ca/
- **Q: Does the depreciation-report rule affect an industrial strata purchase?** Yes — stratas with five or more lots must have a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, and stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What about flood and dike status?** Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront lands inside Delta's dike system; the City is the diking authority with over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, and parcel-level Flood Construction Level and covenant detail is UNVERIFIED and must be confirmed per property. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Could the industrial land be rezoned to condos later?** No source checked states any residential conversion plan for Tilbury; the OCP's stated direction is the opposite — Delta has two of the largest industrial areas in Metro Vancouver (Tilbury and Annacis Island) and its goal is that industry remains a strong component of the economy, with no changes proposed to the land areas within the Industrial designation. https://www.delta.ca/media/2890

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City's own marketing for Tilbury sells highways, not housing:** the City's Tilbury Ice lease brochure markets the area's position between Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge and Highway 99 / Massey Tunnel, off River Road at 72 Street and Vantage Way, with Highway 17 / SFPR proximity — the value case here is the Highways 17/91/99 triangle. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf
- **The Highway 17 alignment runs through the industrial area:** City application material shows Tilbury Industrial area properties affected by the South Fraser Perimeter Road alignment — frontage and access on specific parcels can be shaped by that corridor. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/47276/
- **Farm leases touch the edges:** City material shows farm use leases on some Tilbury-area parcels, and ALR status is parcel-specific through the Agricultural Land Commission — a leased or ALR edge parcel does not price or transact like a clean freehold industrial lot. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **The best-known industrial neighbour is an LNG plant:** the FortisBC Tilbury LNG facility is the area's best-known industrial operation; expansion approval detail from an official project page was UNVERIFIED in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md)
- **There is exactly one civic gathering point inside the district:** Tilbury Ice at 7187 Vantage Way is the one City recreation facility inside the industrial area — there is no residential amenity base (no school, park or library branch) confirmed inside the lands. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search
