### Tenure traps excluded

- **ALR land is excluded from any strata reading with the reason stated:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — a farm parcel can never become condo stock while it is in the reserve. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- Corporation-share, undivided-interest, leasehold, co-op, non-market rental, life-lease and pad-rent product is not freehold strata condo stock and is excluded wherever it appears in a land feed; none is counted here. Urban and farm parcels in this topic are overwhelmingly freehold, per the research file's tenure note. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

Read this section as the rules a condo buyer expects, and what actually governs land instead — the strata block is stated because bare-land strata lots exist and because buyers arrive with condo assumptions, and each land item names the rule that replaces it.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — for a bare-land strata lot, the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A fee-simple lot has no Form B at all — its diligence is title, zoning, servicing and plan designation in COSMOS. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Both bind strata corporations only — a fee-simple lot has neither document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — in a bare-land strata, no long-term rental-restriction bylaw can be enforced, whatever older bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. A fee-simple lot has no strata bylaws at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful in a strata, with exemptions for younger spouses/partners, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No bare-land strata community in this topic is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until a specific community's bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning — the land regime:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, which divides the city into zones and regulates use, density, siting and subdivision; the consolidated bylaw text is the source for any zone's minimum subdivision lot area, confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. A complete Surrey zone-minimum table: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH — added units are not subdivision:** Surrey's framework (adopted July 8, 2024; 20 single-family and duplex zones consolidated into 9) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Added-unit permission does not create new lots and does not waive the zone minimums. One secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite; unpermitted suites are not retroactively legalized. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **ALR stratification bar and subdivision:** subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation; a parcel's reserve status is checked on the Commission's maps by PID or civic address. A principal residence built without a Commission application must be 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence at 90 m² or less (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels) where Surrey bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, directly relevant to closing adjustments on a land purchase. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction; GST treatment of a specific Surrey lot sale depends on the seller and the lot's history and was not verified against a CRA source in the research file — UNCONFIRMED per transaction, confirm with a CPA, and do not rely on the live page's blanket summary. For qualifying new homes generally: First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to a $1M price, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md) and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the newly built frame applies to the home built on the land, not to the bare lot itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for a developer-sold bare-land strata lot or a presale on assembled land, not for an ordinary fee-simple lot resale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Land financing terms themselves (the live page's down-payment ranges) are UNVERIFIED on an official source in this pass. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in a lots-and-land search?** No — by design. This topic holds land products only, and Section 2 counts zero apartment-condo buildings. Surrey's verified condo buildings are counted in their own area files (Sullivan Station, East Newton, West Newton, Panorama Ridge, Cloverdale and others), cross-referenced in Section 2. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Where do buildable lots in Surrey come from?** New urban lots are created by subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum in Zoning By-law No. 12000, and by added-unit development on existing lots under SSMUH (3 units under 280 m², 4 units above it, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop); on the agricultural edges, reserve land generally needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel in Surrey?** Only within tight limits: reserve land generally needs Commission permission to subdivide, apart from limited approving-officer cases, and any new lot must also meet its By-law No. 12000 zone minimum. ALR residences cannot be stratified at all. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** A principal residence built without a Commission application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH permission mean I can split my lot?** No — added units and subdivision are different permissions. Surrey allows 3 to 4 units on most SSMUH lots and up to 6 near a frequent bus stop, but that does not create new lots and does not waive floodplain, riparian or servicing requirements. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a low-lying lot near the Nicomekl or Serpentine?** Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along those rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands, so a lowland parcel can carry flood-construction levels and diking-related requirements set by the City — confirm the parcel's position and levels with the City before writing. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Surrey land?** No land figure is stated in this file: SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED; the Section 1 figures are Surrey all-residential context only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, and is it a condo?** It is a strata lot with no building on it at purchase, inside a strata plan — the strata regime (Form B, CRF, bylaws, depreciation report) applies to the land, which is why Section 3 states the strata block. It is not an apartment condo, and none is counted as one here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Q: How do I check a lot's zoning and designation?** In COSMOS, not from the listing: zoning, OCP and secondary plan designations, water/sanitary/storm mains, road dedications and riparian or environmental permit areas are all parcel-level City records. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Q: What taxes should I budget on a Surrey lot?** Property transfer tax in the 1% and 2% general tiers at typical lot prices (3% above $2M, further 2% on residential value above $3M), GST treatment confirmed per transaction with a CPA, and Surrey's July 2 tax calendar with 5% penalties after it and a further 5% after September 2 for the ongoing ownership years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The same search spans two different legal systems.** An urban lot's potential is set by its By-law No. 12000 zone and the SSMUH framework; a reserve parcel's potential is set by the Agricultural Land Commission's housing and subdivision rules — and the reserve system bars stratification outright, which is why no ALR parcel in this feed can ever be read as future condo stock without a Commission process. Sources: Section 3 entries.
- **There are no Surrey land statistics to quote.** SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category — any "Surrey land median" or "price per acre" quoted from that source does not exist. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **The feed's range is wider than its title suggests.** The cards read span serviced single-family lots, a permit-ready duplex lot, a fourplex-positioned vacant lot, a 2.68-acre West Newton parcel and a 96-acre income-producing parcel on 64 Avenue — five different diligence sets under one heading. - **Creek headwaters sit inside ordinary-looking plan areas.** The South Newton plan area includes Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries — riparian rules can reach parcels a buyer would not describe as creek-side. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Two City figures a land budget needs are still owed verification.** Current DCC rates and the Tree Protection Bylaw's thresholds could not be opened in the research pass (fetch rate-limit) — a careful buyer prices both from current City documents, not from the live page's summaries, which are UNVERIFIED here.
