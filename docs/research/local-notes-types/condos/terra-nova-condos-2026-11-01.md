## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Terra Nova is the north-west Richmond pocket on Lulu Island, west of No. 1 Road at the end of Westminster Highway, beside the Middle Arm / Sturgeon Bank foreshore. The City plans the same ground as a sub-area in the Thompson planning area — Official Community Plan Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) — and the City Heritage Inventory describes the area as from No. 1 Road westward, with Westminster Highway to the south, the Fraser River to the north and west, and No. 1 Road to the east, including the foreshore outside the dyke. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- Where multi-family stock actually concentrates: along **Westminster Highway at the pocket's south edge** (Mayflower at 3880 Westminster Highway, Sonoma at 3555 Westminster Highway) and on **Cornwall Drive inside the pocket** (Quilchena Green at 5531 Cornwall Drive). All three verified complexes are townhouse form. The rest of the pocket is detached houses, with farm land in the AG1 / ALR framework to the west and south. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- Main roads: Westminster Highway, No. 1 Road and River Road; Terra Nova Rural Park is at the west end of Westminster Highway west of No. 1 Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- Transit: bus service on No. 1 Road and Westminster Highway connects the pocket to Richmond–Brighouse Station on the Canada Line; no Canada Line station is inside the sub-area. Route-level detail beyond that connection: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At Quilchena Green the Form B and registered bylaws are also the only documents that settle the flagged age-threshold conflict in Section 2 — listing labels do not. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every Terra Nova complex in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Quilchena Green for long-term rentals, and it equally means the contrary "rentals allowed with restrictions" listing line cannot be read as a long-term ban. Note the interaction with an age bylaw: a valid 55+ bylaw (if that is what the registered bylaws contain) still limits who may occupy, including as a tenant — the two questions are separate and both turn on the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — SPA ss. 123.1 / 123.2:** only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is valid in BC; any younger threshold (for example a "45+" label) cannot be enforced as an age-restriction bylaw under the current Act. Exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. This is why Quilchena Green's 45+ / 55+ label conflict matters and why it is carried as UNCONFIRMED pending the registered bylaws: if a 45+ figure appears anywhere in older marketing, it is not a lawful age-bylaw threshold today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs. For the farm land beside the pocket, Agriculture (AG1) in Section 14.1 provides for farming and compatible uses consistent with the Agricultural Land Reserve, one principal dwelling unit per lot and a secondary suite as a secondary use; subdivision in the ALR is not permitted unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission (2.0 ha minimum lot area where that approval is not required), and single detached housing is not to be built on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone. A Quilchena Green listing records that complex's zoning as RTL 1. For non-ALR residential lots the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. The zoning district of the Mayflower and Sonoma parcels: UNVERIFIED in this pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md) and https://faithwilson.com/listings/75-5531-cornwall-drive/
- **Official Community Plan:** Richmond Official Community Plan Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) is the sub-area plan for the pocket; the city-wide plan is the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **Flood protection and Flood Bylaw 8204:** Richmond is protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide), designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise under way; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets setbacks from a dike right-of-way and bars building below the flood plain construction level in its Schedule B — directly relevant to the Cornwall Drive and Westminster Highway complexes near the dyke. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **ALR beside the pocket:** Agricultural Land Commission guidance allows one principal residence and, if the regulation conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve — the rule set for the farm parcels beside the residential pocket, not for the strata complexes themselves. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **District energy (ADEU):** the Alexandra District Energy Utility serves the West Cambie / Alexandra neighbourhood (Bylaw No. 8641 is framed for buildings within the Alexandra neighbourhood, and the utility operator describes the ADEU system as serving the West Cambie neighbourhood). Neither the Terra Nova research file nor the ADEU sources place Terra Nova in the ADEU service area — no ADEU connection or rate is stated for any Terra Nova complex: UNCONFIRMED / not applicable on the sources checked. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=78100 and https://luluislandenergy.ca/adeu/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all three Terra Nova complexes are resale stock (1992–1996/97). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A current market presale in Terra Nova verified on a developer source: UNVERIFIED — this rule is stated for completeness only. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3.)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Quilchena Green is Terra Nova stock, whatever the name suggests.** The complex sits at 5531 Cornwall Drive inside the Terra Nova pocket (current listings classify it in the Terra Nova sub-area), and the earlier program's Quilchena RI file deliberately did not count it for that reason — buyers searching "Quilchena" will be shown Vancouver West Side and Richmond Quilchena material that has nothing to do with this gated complex. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/5531-cornwall-drive/72
- **Two of the three complexes share one builder and one amenity pattern.** Mayflower and Sonoma are both Polygon townhouse communities on Westminster Highway with clubhouse / pool / hot tub / gym amenity sets built a year apart (Sonoma 1995; Mayflower 1996/97) — cross-complex comparisons of construction and strata practice are unusually direct here. https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/3555-westminster-highway/29 and https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/3880-westminster-highway/32
- **The pocket's plan name is not its postal name.** The City plans this ground as Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova) inside the Thompson planning area, while the board sub-area is simply "Terra Nova" — a Paragon/City name mismatch the research file flags, and a reason document searches under "Thompson" alone will surface Terra Nova material. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **The area's Indigenous name is on the school.** Terra Nova was named Spul'u'kwuks by Indigenous Peoples who used it for seasonal fishing camps, and the elementary school inside the pocket carries that name (Spul'u'kwuks Elementary, 5999 Blanshard Drive); Newfoundland fishermen later renamed the area Terra Nova, "new land," and the Terra Nova Cannery operated there into the middle of the 20th century. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
- **Shopping in the pocket is asserted everywhere and verified nowhere official.** Terra Nova Village at 3671–3679 Westminster Highway and any grocery anchor are UNVERIFIED on an official source in the research file, even though strata listings routinely name the Terra Nova Shopping Centre / Save-On-Foods as a walk-to amenity — verify current tenancies on the ground rather than from listing copy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/terra-nova.md)
