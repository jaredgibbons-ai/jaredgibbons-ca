## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For the townhome complexes in Section 2, the Form B is also where any pet, rental-era and amenity facts are confirmed. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line for a complex here stating "rentals not allowed" is void as a long-term rental restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ on a source checked: UNCONFIRMED — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900 — a City corporate report records that on June 15, 2026, Surrey 2050 OCP received final adoption from Council (the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current in this file). Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH zones RA to R6 allow up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The Grandview Heights General Land Use Plan (approved 2005) is the area's planning frame, and where an older neighbourhood plan and the Surrey 2050 OCP conflict, the OCP is the current rule — at least one plan (Morgan Heights) has been rescinded through the OCP update. Surrey also allows lock-off suites in townhouses only in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones in transit-oriented and frequent-bus areas, per the research file's summary of the live page — not every complex qualifies. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.md)
- **GST on new condos and townhomes:** 5% GST applies to new construction (for example, remaining developer sales at The Loop or Chelsea at The Boroughs), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to new townhome purchases here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing the purchase agreement and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust. This applies to any remaining presale contracts at The Loop / The Boroughs phases; assignment terms are UNCONFIRMED until that contract is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Can I rent out a townhome here long-term?** In law, yes — long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; check the filed bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My townhome advertises a lock-off suite — is that automatic?** No. Surrey allows lock-off suites only in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones in transit-oriented and frequent-bus areas, so the permission is zone- and location-specific — confirm the complex's zone before pricing the suite. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a home near 24 Avenue and 160 Street?** Surrey Schools (SD36) catchments are address-based through the district's School Locator. Edgewood, Sunnyside and Pacific Heights elementary schools and Grandview Heights Secondary are physically in the area, but no catchment result for a specific Grandview address is confirmed in the research file — run the exact address in the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer stack the GST rebate and the PTT exemption on a new townhome here?** Yes, they are separate programs: the FTHB GST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on/after March 20, 2025), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. A resale from a non-developer seller carries no GST at all. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What should I check before removing subjects on a strata home here?** The Form B, the bylaws and rules, the Form F, and the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots) — the report is the document that shows coming special levies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
