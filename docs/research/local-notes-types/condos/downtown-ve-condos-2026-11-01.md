Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography flags, stated at the top:** (1) This file follows the research file's geography — the City of Vancouver's official "Downtown" local area on the downtown peninsula, bounded by Burrard Inlet north, False Creek south, the West End and Stanley Park west, and Gastown / Chinatown / Strathcona east. (4) This is the densest condo stock in the region: the list below is a selective, verified set of major named towers, not an exhaustive census. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Downtown is the downtown peninsula: the primary business district between Burrard Inlet and False Creek, bounded by Burrard Inlet on the north, False Creek and Yaletown to the south, the West End and Stanley Park to the west, and Gastown, Chinatown and Strathcona to the east. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: almost everywhere in the local area — the housing form is strata apartment towers, from the Georgia / Granville CBD core (Shangri-La, One Burrard Place, Capitol Residences, TELUS Garden) to Yaletown at the south shore (Vancouver House at the Granville bridgehead) and Gastown at the east edge (Woodward's). Detached houses are not a Downtown housing form. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- Main streets: West Georgia, Granville, Burrard, Howe, Hastings, Pender and Cordova Streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- Transit: Downtown is the hub where the Expo Line, Millennium Line and Canada Line meet — Waterfront, Burrard, Granville and Stadium-Chinatown stations serve the local area; the R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats (the September 2026 FVR and GVR reports contain no Vancouver rows) — no median, inventory, sales-ratio or days-on-market figure is quoted in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — in Downtown's hotel-mixed towers (Shangri-La, One Wall Centre) the Form B is also where the residential strata's separation from the hotel and commercial components shows. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is far above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted — and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City, so a strata rule plus a City licence are both in play for any short-term use. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is void as a long-term ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the area — permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses — with CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also applying in parts of the area. Heritage-area regulations apply in Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown (Gastown is north of Hastings Street between Richards and Main Streets; Yaletown is bordered by Homer and Nelson Streets to the north and east and Cambie, Mainland and Drake Streets to the south and west). Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any Downtown purchase, where investor-held units are common. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Additional school tax:** at Downtown price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **District energy charges:** several Downtown towers sit on district-energy or ambient systems and the charge can sit outside the strata fee; no source checked states a specific building's energy-utility charge — applicability and amount per building: UNCONFIRMED; read the Form B and the strata's utility invoices.
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most Downtown stock is resale. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the top tier is routinely in play at Downtown luxury price points. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any current Downtown presale offering. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a unit in Shangri-La or One Wall Centre a normal strata purchase?** The residential floors are strata condominiums (Shangri-La: 307 residential units in two separate stratas above a hotel; One Wall Centre: 74 condos on floors 31–48 above a hotel), but the building's hotel and commercial components mean the Form B, the air-space parcels and the cost-sharing agreements need reading, not just the suite documents. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_Shangri-La and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Wall_Centre
- **Q: Can I rent out a Downtown condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals are a separate question: stratas can still restrict them and Vancouver licences them. The Empty Homes Tax declaration applies if the unit sits empty instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Why is this Downtown tower filed under a different name in my search?** Because the board carries separate sub-areas for parts of the peninsula (Yaletown and Coal Harbour appear as their own Paragon sub-areas), while the City's Downtown local area includes Yaletown and Gastown. Vancouver House listings say "Yaletown," Shangri-La listings say "West End VW," and Fairmont Pacific Rim listings say "Coal Harbour" — search by building name and address, not sub-area alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: Are there leasehold condos downtown?** No named leasehold strata building on the peninsula was verified in this pass; the documented City leasehold concentrations are False Creek South (south shore, in Fairview) and Champlain Heights. Tenure for a specific Downtown building: confirm Freehold Strata on title — the counted buildings above are each recorded as Freehold Strata in their sources. https://realestatevancity.ca/2020/05/11/what-are-leasehold-properties-in-vancouver/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Downtown address?** SD39 catchments are address-based. The two VSB elementaries located in Downtown are Elsie Roy (150 Drake Street) and Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət (55 Expo Boulevard); no public secondary school inside the local area was confirmed in the research pass. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: What extra taxes hit a high-end Downtown purchase?** Beyond standard PTT (with the further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million), the Province's additional school tax applies on assessed value above $3 million (0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026), and the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to every unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: Do heritage rules affect a Gastown or Yaletown purchase?** Yes — the City's heritage areas work gives regulations for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown that preserve and protect historic fabric, which affects renovation and alteration plans from day one; Woodward's itself is a redevelopment of the historic department-store site. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: How do I get around without a car?** Downtown is the meeting point of the Expo, Millennium and Canada Lines (Waterfront, Burrard, Granville and Stadium-Chinatown stations), plus the R5 RapidBus on Hastings — but parking is a strata asset, not a given: confirm whether a specific unit carries a stall and a locker on its Form B. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Vancouver House's size and date?** The presale source records 548 units while an earlier project source records 502, and the building directory records built 2019 while listings record 2020 (completion was postponed to summer 2020). Both conflicts are flagged in Section 2 — the strata plan and the depreciation report are the settling documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/vancouver-house-1480-south-howe-street and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver_House

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Shangri-La's condos are two stratas, not one.** The tower's 307 residential units split into 234 live-work homes (floors 16–43) and 63 private-access residences (floors 44–60) in two separate stratas — a unit's floor determines which strata corporation, budget and depreciation report govern it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Living_Shangri-La
- **One Wall Centre's middle floors are neither hotel nor condo.** Floors 28–30 are resort floors operated independently of the Sheraton below them, between the 27 hotel floors and the 74 condos on floors 31–48 — three tenures stacked in one tower. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Wall_Centre
- **The city centre moved uphill.** The City states growth moved the centre from Water and Cordova Streets up to Georgia and Granville Streets after incorporation — the reason Gastown stock (Woodward's, 2009/2010) and CBD stock (Capitol Residences, 2011) read as different eras of the same local area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Downtown has elementaries but no confirmed secondary.** Elsie Roy and Crosstown elementaries sit inside the local area, but the research pass confirmed no public secondary school inside Downtown — a family buyer's catchment answer comes only from running the address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **The False Creek floodplain touches the south shore of the peninsula.** The City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it — relevant to Yaletown and Beach District parkades, including Vancouver House's. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Vancouver Central Library is the civic anchor.** Vancouver Public Library Central Library at 350 West Georgia Street sits in the middle of the CBD condo cluster — the nearest thing Downtown has to a community centre in the research pass, which confirmed no park, community centre or pool inside the local area by name on a City page. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
