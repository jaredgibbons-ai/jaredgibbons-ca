## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a strata townhome in this band, the Province's buyer paperwork path — Form B and Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — runs before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. In this file's roster this lands hardest on the oldest building, Semiahmoo Estates (1990/1991). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Semiahmoo Estates is flagged here as void in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Relevant to Semiahmoo Estates and Bishop's Green, both presented as 55+ communities in their sources. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. SSMUH: lots smaller than 280 m² may hold a maximum of 3 units, lots larger than 280 m² a maximum of 4 units, and eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop with 15-minute service a maximum of 6 units; the framework applies to eligible residential properties under 4,050 m² inside the Metro Vancouver Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Zone renames: the former RF and RF-SS zones were replaced by R3 Urban Residential and the former RF-SD Semi-Detached zone by R6 Semi-Detached, and the RM-D duplex zone was removed — check the current zone, not the zone name on an old listing. The R3/R6 lot-size minima stated on the live page (R3 560 m²; R6 200 m² interior / 226 m² corner) are UNVERIFIED — not confirmed on a City source in the research pass. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (any developer sales at Odyssey), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase inside this band reaches only the 1% and 2% tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — both thresholds sit inside this band's lower range. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked example (inputs stated):** PTT on a $1,800,000 purchase at the band ceiling, no exemption: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000, total $34,000 — and nothing in this band reaches the 3% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer sale surfaced in this band, never counted as resale band stock here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
