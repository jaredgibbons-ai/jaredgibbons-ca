## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Port Guichon is the historic harbour pocket of west Ladner, in the City of Delta — Ladner is one of Delta's three communities (Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta), and Ladner and Tsawwassen together are called South Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The working centre is River Road West at 46A Street: Port Guichon Elementary is at 4381 46A Street, and Ladner Harbour Park is at 4905 McNeelys Way on this shore. Official City boundary lines for the sub-area name are UNVERIFIED. http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/ladner-harbour-park
- The name is historic, not a marketing label: the Canadian Register of Historic Places entry for the Victoria Terminal Railway and Ferry Company Station places the station in the historic Port Guichon neighbourhood, and the elementary school site records the school was built as Fairview Elementary and later renamed for the Guichon family. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=6344 and http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/
- Transit is a loop bus, not a corridor: TransLink's Ladner Exchange bay map (exchange at 4645 Harvest Drive) lists route 618, the Ladner South loop, calling at 46A Street at River Road — the Port Guichon stop; routes 601 (South Delta to Bridgeport Station), 609 and 620 also depart Ladner Exchange. http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
- Where the condo stock concentrates: in one place only — the River Road West riverfront, where the sub-area's single named strata building stands (Section 2). The rest of the confirmed stock is freehold detached housing under the RS zones and A1 Agriculture zoning toward the west edge. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- Schools for the sample address 4381 46A Street, from the SD37 School Locator: Port Guichon Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12), 4615 51 St; catchments are address-based and other addresses are UNVERIFIED. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first:** under the Strata Property Act, s. 59, the Form B: Information Certificate for a River West unit discloses its monthly strata fees, any amount the owner owes, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report must be attached. The strata must provide it within 7 days of request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, a strata corporation with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it; reports project 30 years of repair/replacement costs with three CRF cash-flow models. River West's enclosure rehabilitation dates to 1999 per a unit listing, which is exactly the kind of item to trace through the current depreciation report. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3058563-4753-w-river-road-304-ladner-port-guichon-homes-for-sale-760000/brochure
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no strata corporation may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, no amendment needed. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — only 55+ is lawful:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 permit a 55+ residency bylaw; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and the bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it. No source states River West has a 55+ bylaw — UNCONFIRMED for this building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Flood and dike rules are the local overlay:** Port Guichon is low land on the Fraser River side of Ladner behind the City's dike system; Delta operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, and directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Flood covenants on title:** in Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street, the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — the pattern a buyer on river-side lots here should search title for. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Streamside rules:** all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Delta zoning and plan:** zoning is parcel by parcel under Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (RS1, RS5, RS6 and RS7 confirmed in west Ladner; A1 Agriculture toward the west edge, with an 8-hectare minimum lot area for parcels created by subdivision). The current OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — no lettered schedules, no Ladner or Tsawwassen area-plan schedule; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots; Delta's notice to the Province states four units are allowed on all residential properties in the City, and six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). The RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **BC Property Transfer Tax exemptions:** first-time buyers — PTT exempt on the first $500,000 where fair market value is $835,000 or less (effective Apr 1, 2024), partial exemption $835,000–$860,000, land 0.5 hectares or smaller, principal residence only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Newly built home exemption:** full exemption threshold $1,100,000, phasing out to complete elimination at $1,150,000 (effective Apr 1, 2024); a never-occupied unit in a newly built condo building can qualify — relevant to new product, not to a 1990/91 resale at River West. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST — new construction only:** the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026) with the new housing rebate returns 100% of GST to $1 million value, phasing to $1.5 million, maximum $50,000, agreements in the Mar 20, 2025–2031 window; the new housing rebate alone is 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day statutory rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits held in trust — the rule set if a buyer here compares River West against a Ladner presale. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan limit $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (verified against CRA pages in this program's earlier passes); CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% (90.01%–95% LTV, non-traditional down payment). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Port Guichon in Tsawwassen?** No — it is in Ladner, in west Ladner around River Road West and 46A Street; Ladner and Tsawwassen are separate Delta communities that together make up South Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: How many condo buildings are there here?** One verified: River West, 4753 River Road West, strata plan NWS3341, 85 homes per the building directory (secondary source). No townhouse complex inside the sub-area was confirmed on an official source. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4753-w-river-rd-52485
- **Q: What work has River West already had done?** A listing for a unit states a building enclosure rehabilitation was completed in 1999, with a clubhouse, fitness room, hot tub, workshop and secure parking. Treat it as listing-stated and trace the enclosure and the next capital items through the strata's depreciation report. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3058563-4753-w-river-road-304-ladner-port-guichon-homes-for-sale-760000/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a River West unit?** Provincially, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, and no amendment is needed for that to apply; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals. The building's own current bylaws should still be read on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets or ages restricted at River West?** UNCONFIRMED — no public source read states a current pet or age bylaw for this strata. A listing's "adult-oriented setting" wording is not a bylaw; the registered bylaws attached to the strata documents are the deciding source. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3058563-304-4753-w-river-road-ladner-british-columbia-v4k1r9-760000/brochure
- **Q: Is the building behind a dike, and can a flood covenant sit on my title?** Yes to the dike system — this is low Fraser River-side land protected by Delta's flood protection system — and covenants are a real Ladner pattern: the City has required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and rights-of-way for future dike raising to 5.5 m in Ladner approvals, so search title before subjects come off. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Q: What do strata fees cover, and how do I check for levies coming?** Read it off the Form B: it must state the lot's monthly fees, amounts owing, any approved special levy still to be paid, the CRF balance, and an insurance summary, with the budget and the most recent depreciation report attached — and since July 1, 2024 that report must be refreshed every five years for a strata of five or more lots. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Where do I park, and is there storage?** The directory records underground / under-building garage parking at River West, and a unit listing records one garage space for that unit; the specific stall and any locker for a given lot are Form B items — confirm them there, not from the listing. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4753-w-river-rd-52485
- **Q: Which schools and buses serve the building?** SD37 catchments are address-based; the sample address 4381 46A St maps to Port Guichon Elementary and Delta Secondary. For buses, route 618 (Ladner South loop) calls at 46A Street at River Road, and Ladner Exchange is the hub for the 601 to Bridgeport Station. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
- **Q: What is the hospital and village access like?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is Fraser Health's Delta community hospital with 24/7 emergency care, located beside Ladner Exchange; Ladner Village shops sit west of Chilukthan Slough per the OCP's Ladner Village Core material, a short distance east of Port Guichon. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The village next door is mid-revitalization, and it is City-led:** Delta's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan names the Chisholm Street Wharf project in Ladner Village — the amenity story for a River West buyer is the village core, not a mall. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **The dike is planned to go higher, and the paperwork can already be on title:** Ladner approvals have required rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — a buyer who skips the title search misses the one local fact most likely to affect a river-side lot's future use. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **This was a ferry landing before it was a neighbourhood:** the Victoria Terminal Railway and Ferry Company Station stands in the historic Port Guichon neighbourhood on the Canadian Register of Historic Places, and the school on 46A Street began as Fairview Elementary before being renamed for the Guichon family. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=6344 and http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **A grocery store inside Port Guichon itself: UNVERIFIED** — no source names one; the shopping is Ladner Village and the Ladner Exchange area to the east. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/port-guichon-guide.md
- **SSMUH quietly changed the side streets:** since the June 24, 2024 adoption of Bylaws 8394 and 8395, single-detached and duplex lots here can add secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites without rezoning — the infill a River West buyer will see appearing around the older detached stock. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Recreation is the Ladner Leisure Centre,** 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent, the main Ladner recreation facility on the City's pages — not a South Delta (Tsawwassen) facility. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre
