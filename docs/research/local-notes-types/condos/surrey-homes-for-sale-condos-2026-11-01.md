## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2 wherever a home-file directory line says rentals are restricted or not allowed (Semiahmoo Estates, St. Andrews at Northview, Fleetwood Gardens, Newton Court). Short-term rentals can still be restricted: in Surrey, only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host one, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required first, under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, Part 4, s. B.7(d) and Business License Bylaw No. 13680, s. 60.1. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Live 55+ positions in this assembly include Fleetwood Gardens, Chelsea Gardens and Suncliff Place — confirm each on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Former neighbourhood plans rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (including the South Newton and West Newton NCPs) are no longer in effect; land-use claims now run through the OCP and Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Secondary suites:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. The often-quoted "90 m²" cap is NOT current and is never stated as current in this file. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), with one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; Surrey's July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Amson Bloc and any developer sale at the newer buildings in Section 2 (Viva, King + Crescent, Quinn, Galilea, Gabriel). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: How many communities does Surrey have — six or seven?** Seven, including City Centre: City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley, per the City's own Our Communities page. The live page's "six distinct communities" wording is an error against that list. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Surrey condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever an older directory line says (four are flagged in Section 2). Short-term rentals are different: in Surrey only an owner-occupier may host, tenants cannot, and a $358-per-year business licence is required first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Why can't I find Camberley Green or The Greens at Douglas in any roster?** They are on the Surrey 55+ re-consolidation queue (with Ocean Park Place, Ocean Park Grove and Carrington) — flagged as pending in this program and deliberately excluded from every roster count, this file's included. Verify them building-by-building from strata records, not from roster arithmetic.
- **Q: Can a Surrey house add a suite or a coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area and never inside a coach house or garden suite — there is no current 90 m² suite cap in Bylaw No. 12000. A coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), and SSMUH allows 3–4 units on most lots by size band, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: Which SkyTrain serves Surrey now, and what is coming?** City Centre is serviced by four SkyTrain stations today, per the City; TransLink states the Surrey-Langley extension will run 16 km along Fraser Highway from King George Station to 203 Street in Langley City, with an anticipated in-service date of 2029. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/surrey-langley-skytrain (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: When are property taxes due in Surrey?** July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/surrey-homes-for-sale.md)
