### Tenure traps excluded

- **Bare-land strata inside Southlands:** industry reporting records Southlands housing forms as including bare-land strata single-family homes alongside non-strata row homes, traditional townhomes and condos (flats). A bare-land strata house is not a condo, however it is marketed — excluded from the apartment-condo count above, and its Form B / strata documents are read as a house purchase with a strata lot, not as apartment stock. https://renxhomes.ca/century-group-maintains-course-on-southlands-tsawwassen
- **Freehold cottages and estate homes inside Southlands** (for example the cottage and estate-cottage phases named in secondary sources) are detached freehold product, not strata condos — excluded. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/
- No corporation-share, undivided-interest, leasehold, co-op, life-lease, non-market rental or pad-rent complex inside Boundary Beach itself was confirmed on a source checked in this pass. The standing tenure trap on this shore is the foreshore: the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore, and private moorage is held by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, under General Permission or Specific Permission — beach access is not foreshore ownership. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a resale Southlands flat or a Monterra unit this is the central diligence document; for a Granary unit still on a developer contract, first establish whether the sale is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote; a strata established on or after July 1, 2024 and before July 1, 2027 must obtain its first report within two years of its first AGM — the timeline that applies to a brand-new Southlands strata. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no BC strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says (the same rule voids the directory "rentals not allowed" lines recorded elsewhere in Tsawwassen for Heron Cove and Tsawwassen Greene). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful; exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (expanded May 1, 2023). Monterra's rancher format draws downsizer interest, but no source read states a 55+ bylaw for Monterra or for either Southlands project — age restriction UNCONFIRMED per complex until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — it contains NO lettered area-plan schedules; there is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan / Schedule D.1 in the current OCP (that was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950), and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" — the designation frame for Southlands' working-farm lands beside its housing pockets. Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/boundary-beach.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, not settled:** City pages and amendment bylaws cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017", while an internal consolidated title in this program's research says "8400, 2025" — the two collide, and this file asserts neither number as settled. Zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps in either case. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/january-18-2021-virtual-public-hearing-amendments-secondary
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands)** — named for this exact ground in OCP Bylaw No. 8400, Appendix B: no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Boundary Beach parcel sits inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dike and flood system:** the Boundary Bay shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **SSMUH:** Delta Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites; the research file notes the live feed itself shows a coach house included with a 66 Street listing (feed evidence only). https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/boundary-beach.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (a developer sale at The Granary or another Southlands phase), not resales (Monterra, District Flats resales). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption to check on a never-occupied Southlands developer sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for a Granary purchase while the project is on developer contracts (its presale record states completion TBA). https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-granary-at-southlands-tsawwassen-by-century-group-presale-delta-condos-and-townhomes/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.50% at the top band, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
