## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a page whose heading misdescribes its own feed, the Form B is also the document that settles what a given unit actually is — strata lot, fees and bylaws — regardless of which feed carried it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. Much of Section 2's roster is 1990s–2010s wood-frame — the report's envelope, roof and parkade cycles are the levy story in this band. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. This voids the rental halves of the Newton Court, Sterling Court, St. Andrews and Fleetwood Gardens directory/listing lines in Section 2 in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this roster, Chelsea Gardens, Fleetwood Gardens, Suncliff Place and The Cambridge are the buildings whose sources describe 55+ occupancy (Chelsea Gardens with the void 19+ wording still in an older directory) — confirm each registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. **SSMUH:** Surrey allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, with no retroactive legalization of an unpermitted suite; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite — relevant to buyers comparing a band condo against a band house with a helper. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/surrey-homes-under-1-8m.md)
