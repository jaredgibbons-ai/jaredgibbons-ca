# Abbotsford West — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford West" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. The City's own demographic geography calls the wider area "West Abbotsford" (profile area 7) and treats Clearbrook, Peardonville, South Clearbrook and Townline as separate profile areas inside it; there is no Abbotsford West neighbourhood plan in the Official Community Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-west-guide.md)
- The frame roads are Highway 1 across the north-centre (interchanges at Mt. Lehman Road, Clearbrook Road and McCallum Road), South Fraser Way as the east–west arterial, Clearbrook Road and Mt. Lehman Road running north–south, and the Agricultural Land Reserve and Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands to the southwest. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- Where the condo stock concentrates: (1) the Highstreet / Mt. Lehman node at Highway 1 — the Highstreet Village condo buildings on Cardinal Avenue; (2) the Clearbrook corridor — low-rise buildings on and around Clearbrook Road, Simon Avenue, George Ferguson Way and South Fraser Way (Brookhollow, Glenwood Manor, JEM, Auburn, Town Centre Tower); (3) the Gladwin Road south end by Mill Lake (Lakewood Gardens); (4) Westerleigh in the northwest, Polygon's master-planned community on Westridge Place (Elmstone). Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Abbotsford West is the city's biggest attached market by listings: 210 attached listings and 16 attached sales in September 2026 — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
- Transit anchors on the west side are the Highstreet Transit Exchange on Highstreet Access Road between Mt. Lehman Road and Cardinal Avenue (six bus bays), with BC Transit routes 1 (Highstreet/UFV) and 2 (Highstreet/McMillan) and the 66 Fraser Valley Express toward Lougheed Station all running from Highstreet. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; reports project 30 years of costs and include at least three CRF cash-flow models. Stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in the west side's 1989–1997 low-rise stock (Lakewood Gardens, Brookhollow Estates, Town Centre Tower), where the first mandatory-cycle reports land on aging roofs, piping and parkades. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Building directories still show "rentals not allowed" for Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates — that reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Both Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates are recorded as age-restricted — confirm on the Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs, under the Abbotsforward 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated; new OCP adopted December 2025 — Section 5). The OCP's transit structure puts Secondary Transit Corridors connecting Highstreet and the hospital to the Primary Transit Corridor on South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing and Transit-Oriented Areas legislation (brief names this as Bylaw 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — number/date UNCONFIRMED on the City pages opened; June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed). Zoning Bylaw s. 140 carries the SSMUH rules and s. 140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Highstreet Village, Auburn, JEM), not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; sources checked give Royal Assent as March 12, 2026 (brief said March 13). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists but rarely pays at local prices. https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate and https://raincityproperties.com/journal/gst-new-home-rebate-bc-first-time-buyer-2026
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to Highstreet Village and Auburn purchases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms — for Highstreet Village's 2028-completion building and Auburn, they are UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: The directory says my building bans rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. What can still bind you: a short-term rental bylaw, and a 55+ age bylaw limiting who may live in the unit (relevant at Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/prohibition-on-rental-and-age-restrictions-in-strata-buildings-556/
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in the older Clearbrook buildings?** It varies and must be read off the Form B. One concrete published example: listings at Lakewood Gardens state the strata fee includes gas, heat and hot water — a real offset against the headline fee in a 1989 building. For any other building, coverage is UNCONFIRMED until the Form B is in hand. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/lakewood-gardens-2491-gladwin-road
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** The directories record Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates as pets not allowed; a current Elmstone listing notes cats OK for that unit. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC (unlike rental bylaws), so the registered bylaws for the specific building govern — UNCONFIRMED for Highstreet Village, Auburn, JEM and Town Centre Tower from public sources. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/brookhollow-estates-2772-clearbrook-road
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1990s low-rise?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must now have one every five years with a 30-year projection and three funding models. A thin CRF plus a model built on levies is the warning pattern; stratas must also contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is parking underground at the new buildings?** Listings for Auburn and the Highstreet Village condos show underground parking; Elmstone's parking arrangement per unit is UNCONFIRMED from public sources. The stall and locker legally attached to a unit are stated on the Form B — confirm there, not from the listing's parking count. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114873-609-32528-simon-avenue-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2t4j8-459900/brochure
- **Q: What is the strata insurance deductible risk?** A strata's water-damage deductible can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused the loss; the amount sits in the strata's insurance summary in the document package and is UNCONFIRMED for every west-side building until read. Buyers typically match their own unit policy to that deductible. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer stack the GST rebate and the PTT exemption at Highstreet Village or Auburn?** Yes, they are separate programs: the FTHB GST rebate returns up to $50,000 of GST on a new home up to $1 million (agreements on/after March 20, 2025), and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully up to $1,100,000. Eligibility tests differ per program — confirm both before writing. https://rncanada.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-gst-rebate and https://www.kraftmortgages.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-guide-surrey-bc-2026
- **Q: How is transit from the west-side condos, really?** The Highstreet Transit Exchange (six bays) is in the Highstreet Village's own node, with routes 1 and 2 and the 66 Fraser Valley Express to Lougheed Station; Clearbrook buildings sit on the South Fraser Way Primary Transit Corridor the OCP describes between Clearbrook and McCallum. There is no rail transit in Abbotsford. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange
- **Q: Which schools serve a Westerleigh or Clearbrook condo?** Catchments are address-based (SD34 Policy 18). Locator runs in the research file: 3614 Clearbrook Road returns Clearbrook Elementary → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 3433 Firhill Drive returns Blue Jay Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary. Run the building's address — the west side splits between two secondary schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-west-guide.md)
- **Q: Will airport or Highway 1 noise affect a unit?** The southwest edge sits near YXX lands and airport height rules apply inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area; Highstreet-area buildings sit directly on the Highway 1 corridor. No source checked publishes building-by-building noise readings — treat any specific noise claim as UNCONFIRMED and visit the unit at rush hour and under the flight path. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025.** The Abbotsforward 2050 update (public hearing November 25, 2025) emphasizes more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enables the 37,185 units targeted by the City's 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report over 20 years — the policy backdrop for continued condo supply around Highstreet and Clearbrook. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Highstreet Village is still building out.** Current listings show a condo building at 30424 Cardinal Avenue with completion in 2028 — buyers in the earlier 2025 buildings are living beside an active construction program, and the developer is still marketing new releases (The Sunset Collection). https://www.zolo.ca/abbotsford-real-estate/30424-cardinal-avenue/213 and https://highstreetvillage.ca/
- **Auburn's completion is imminent but was still a presale in fall 2026:** listings throughout 2026 stated estimated completion November 2026 for the 32528 Simon Avenue building, with its two commercial units marketed for completion in Q4 2026 — early owners should expect the retail level to finish after they move in. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/c8077487-100-32528-simon-avenue-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2t4j2-945000/brochure
- **The City's Housing Accelerator Fund program is complete:** Abbotsford was approved for up to $25.6 million in federal HAF funding and reported in October 2026 that all HAF initiatives are complete, with multi-family permitting above expectations — one reason the west side's attached listings (210 in September 2026) are the deepest in the city. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Two supportive-housing projects frame the Gladys/South Fraser edge:** the City has BC Housing lease agreements for 61 supportive units on Riverside Road under construction and 48 units on Gladys Avenue advancing through approvals (October 2026 City update) — relevant context for buyers comparing the Clearbrook/Simon Avenue corridor buildings. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Westerleigh is a single-developer community:** Elmstone's amenity value rests on Club West, the shared clubhouse for Polygon's Westerleigh — when comparing Elmstone fees to a standalone building, part of the fee buys into that shared amenity structure rather than an in-building gym alone. https://polyhomes.com/community/elmstone/
