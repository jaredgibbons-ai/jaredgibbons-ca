## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**Zero stock, stated plainly: there is no strata-condo stock in this topic — no apartment-condo building, strata farm complex or bare-land strata farm community was confirmed on any official source, and farm parcels in Maple Ridge are overwhelmingly freehold.** (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)

Why the zero is structural, not a gap in the search:

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** Under the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6, as carried in this program's standing research), a residence on Agricultural Land Reserve land cannot be stratified — so the principal housing form on Maple Ridge's 3,787 hectares of ALR land is legally incapable of becoming strata-condo product. A specific parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The ALR housing form is a single principal residence, capped in size.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. One additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Maple Ridge bylaws must also permit it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The City's agricultural zoning is a farm zone set, not a multi-family set.** The City publishes an A1, A2, A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, which flags that structures proposed in these zones may require ALC approval and records whether a property is in the ALR and in a flood plain. The live page's statement of five agricultural zones (A-1 through A-5) with minimum lots from 1.8 ha to 8 ha is site copy, not an official source, and the consolidated bylaw text could not be opened in the research pass: those zone minimums are UNVERIFIED — do not use them until checked against the bylaw. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Tenure traps checked and excluded:** no corporation-share holding, undivided-interest farm parcel, leasehold farm, housing co-op farm or pad-rent farm community was confirmed on an official source for this topic — any Maple Ridge farm listing presented in one of those forms would need its title and tenure documents read before it is treated as a purchase of land at all, and none is counted here. Status: UNCONFIRMED that any such product exists in this topic. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The standard condo rules are stated here because buyers reach this topic asking condo questions; on a farm purchase most of them do not bite, and the rules that do bite are the ALR ones first.

- **Form B and strata records:** a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59) exists only for strata lots. A Maple Ridge farm purchase has no Form B, no depreciation report and no CRF — the equivalent diligence is title, the ALC status check, the City's zoning confirmation and the farm-classification record below. If a listing in this topic ever presents itself as a strata product, that presentation itself is the red flag: ALR residences cannot be stratified. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Not applicable to a freehold farm parcel — stated so a buyer comparing a farm with a condo purchase sees the difference. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. Not applicable to a freehold farm parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. On farm land the binding rental question is different: a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence on ALR land, and any additional residence must meet the ALC size conditions in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw in a strata. There is no strata in this topic, so no age bylaw applies to a farm purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs zoning across the city, including the A1, A2 and A3 agricultural zones; the City's agricultural compliance checklist flags that structures in these zones may require ALC approval. The City's Official Community Plan citation is CONTESTED in the research files (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an OCP update adopted November 25, 2025) — do not treat either number as settled. The Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025; Albion adjoins the farm ground east along Lougheed Highway. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Farm classification — the tax rule that actually governs this topic:** BC Assessment requires a producing farm to meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if larger; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha — and farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Water:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of water fees and rentals before the water can lawfully be used; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Septic:** Fraser Health states a home or building with indoor plumbing that is not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person (a Professional Engineer or a Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner) can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect a sewerage system. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes; farm classification (above) is what changes a farm parcel's tax treatment, not any condo rule. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **GST:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not ordinary resales; farm purchases can carry their own GST treatment on the land and buildings, which no source in the research file settles — UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel; take tax advice before writing. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, for qualifying new homes; CRA's Excise/GST/HST News No. 122 gives Royal Assent as March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Mixed-use farm parcels are apportioned — confirm the residential portion with the conveyancer. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No presale project belongs to this topic — stated for completeness only. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Farm financing is ordinarily a lender-specific agricultural credit question, not a CMHC condo question — treat any blanket farm-financing claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo or a strata unit on a Maple Ridge farm property?** No — there is no strata-condo stock in this topic, and on ALR land a residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6, as carried in this program's standing research). What trades here is freehold land with a principal residence, bought as land, not as a strata lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can the house on ALR land be?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite only inside that residence. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: Can a Maple Ridge farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City of Maple Ridge bylaws must also permit it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: How do I know if a parcel is actually in the ALR?** Check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address. Reserve status controls the residence cap, the additional-residence limits and most use permissions, and the City's own A1–A3 checklist flags ALR status and flood plain status for agricultural properties. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the following tax year. Lose the income, and the classification — and its tax treatment — is at risk. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: Are the market numbers on the farms page farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Maple Ridge figures (detached median $1,131,000, condos & townhomes median $525,000 in September 2026) are residential context only, and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: Can I irrigate from a well on the property?** Only with the right paper: non-domestic groundwater use such as irrigation requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, with fees and rentals payable, before the water can lawfully be used; domestic use is exempt. Confirm the licence position on any farm that irrigates. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: The farms page lists five agricultural zones with minimum lot sizes — can I rely on them?** Not yet: the A-1 to A-5 zone table and its 1.8 ha to 8 ha minimums are live-page copy, not an official source, and the consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 text could not be opened in the research pass — UNVERIFIED. Get the parcel's exact zone and minimum lot size from the City before pricing subdivision or a second dwelling. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve farm families?** The rural schools confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file in this program include Webster's Corners Elementary (25554 Dewdney Trunk Road), Whonnock Elementary (27471 112 Avenue), Blue Mountain Elementary (12453 248 Street) and Garibaldi Secondary (24789 Dewdney Trunk Road). Address-level catchments: UNVERIFIED — no locator result was run for any farm address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The ALR is a minority of the city, in defined pockets.** At 3,787 hectares it is 15% of Maple Ridge's 24,881-hectare land area (2011 inventory) — so farm parcels sit in defined rural pockets rather than a continuous belt, and non-ALR rural parcels also trade in this topic with materially different residence and use rules. Check the specific parcel, never the district's reputation. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The no-stratification rule is why this file has no Section 2 inventory.** ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6 bars stratifying an ALR residence — the absence of farm condos in Maple Ridge is a legal feature of the land base, not a market gap waiting to be filled. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The City's own farm checklist asks the flood question.** Maple Ridge's A1–A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist records flood plain status, minimum basement elevation and geotechnical items for agricultural properties — on lowland parcels along the Alouette and Pitt rivers, those are subject-removal questions, not afterthoughts. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Farm classification runs on an October 31 clock.** A buyer planning to keep farming must submit the farm classification application by October 31 for the following taxation year — miss the date in the completion fall and the tax treatment for the next year is set without it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The rural sub-areas trade thin.** Whonnock and Northeast recorded zero detached sales in September 2026 and Thornhill one — a single month's sub-area row in farm country describes a thin market and is not price evidence for any specific parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **Maple Ridge's OCP citation is unsettled in current sources** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs 7998-2024, update adopted November 25, 2025) — quote planning policy for a specific farm parcel from a current City document, not from memory. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
