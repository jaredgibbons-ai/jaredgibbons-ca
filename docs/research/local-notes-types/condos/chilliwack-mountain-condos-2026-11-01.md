# Chilliwack Mountain — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Chilliwack Mountain is a residential hillside neighbourhood in the City of Chilliwack, on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, just west of Chilliwack Creek and west of the downtown core. The City treats Chilliwack Mountain as a neighbourhood in its own reporting but does not publish a street-by-street boundary for the board sub-area name. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/5861.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- The community sits above the Industrial Way / Progress Way industrial area on the valley floor; the City's Schweyey Road forcemain page places the mountain community above that industrial area, with sewage service along Schweyey Road for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way. The residential streets are the mountain roads themselves — Chilliwack Mountain Road, Grandview Drive and Hillkeep Place at the top, and Sunrise Drive on the slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237 and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Where attached stock concentrates — townhouses only, see Section 2: the Chilliwack Mountain Road corridor, where the verified townhouse complexes stand at 43610–43680 and 43998 Chilliwack Mountain Road, and Sunrise Drive, where Maple Hills stands at 8590 Sunrise Drive. No apartment-condo street exists on the mountain in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 below.
- The mountain is a detached-hillside market by zoning: the City's hillside zones set HR (Hillside Residential) lots at a minimum of 0.2 ha on community water and 0.4 ha otherwise, RH (Rural Hillside) at 8 ha, SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential) by a slope table from 360 m² to 990 m², and SR (Suburban Residential) at 650 m² on full services — lot forms that produce houses, not apartment buildings. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- Chilliwack is not covered by either SnapStats September 2026 report (the FVR report covers Surrey, South Surrey/White Rock, North Delta, Cloverdale, Langley, Abbotsford and Mission only), so every market figure for Chilliwack Mountain — including any attached-sales count — is UNVERIFIED in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

With no apartment condos on the mountain, these rules attach in practice to the townhouse stratas in Section 2 — which are strata corporations under the same Act — and to any future strata on the mountain.

- **Form B and strata records:** an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days (maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page), disclosing fees, amounts owed, approved levies, unfiled bylaw amendments, proceedings, and parking/storage allocations. For a Maple Hills or Osprey Ridge purchase, the Form B — not the listing's pet or fee lines — is the verified record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Osprey Ridge (34 homes) and any complex of Maple Hills' apparent scale are inside the mandatory cycle. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. If any mountain townhouse complex's old bylaws or a directory line says rentals are not allowed, that line is void since November 24, 2022. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw. This matters on the mountain in the negative: Reflections @ Cedar Sky is listed as having no age restrictions at all, and no mountain complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ — if a seller claims an age rule for a mountain complex, the registered bylaw on the Form B package is the only proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 and the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 5500) govern. No Chilliwack Mountain neighbourhood plan appears in Schedule B of the OCP (Schedules B1–B7); the mountain is covered by the OCP's Schedule D Development Permit Areas D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development, and DPA D-2 requires a development permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw, Bylaw 5395, was adopted June 18, 2024. Its practical reach on the mountain is limited by the hillside zones and DPA D-2 above — SSMUH eligibility for a specific mountain lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file and is a City check, not an assumption. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/chilliwack-mountain.md)
- **GST and presales:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — relevant if a new Osprey Ridge phase or a future mountain project sells as new product. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. For a true presale, REDMA gives a 7-day rescission period; whether any current mountain offering is a REDMA presale or a completed-unit sale is UNCONFIRMED per project. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% to $3 million, 5% above; First-Time Home Buyers' Program — no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption — full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there any apartment condo on Chilliwack Mountain?** None verified. The area research confirmed no condominium building on the mountain on an official source, and searches for this file found only townhouse complexes and detached houses in the sub-area. Apartment buildings in Chilliwack Proper (for example on Chilliwack Central Road or Young Road) are off the mountain and are not part of this area's stock. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/chilliwack-mountain.md)
- **Q: So what strata product can I actually buy on the mountain?** Townhouses: Reflections @ Cedar Sky (43680 Chilliwack Mountain Road, built 2018), Osprey Ridge (43998 Chilliwack Mountain Road, 34 homes, phased from 2022 on listing data) and Maple Hills (8590 Sunrise Drive, built 1994), plus the unnamed DP000817 complex at 43660/43710 Chilliwack Mountain Road whose name is UNCONFIRMED. Sources: Section 2.
- **Q: Is there an age restriction at the mountain townhouses?** At Reflections @ Cedar Sky, the listing states the opposite — "No age restrictions and pet friendly complex." No mountain complex in this file is published as 55+, and in BC the only enforceable age bylaw in any case is a 55+ bylaw under ss. 123.1/123.2. Confirm the registered bylaws for the specific complex before relying on either position. https://kw.com/property/43680-Chilliwack-Mountain-Road-23-Chilliwack-BC-V2R-6A6/2042669732905526 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a mountain townhouse?** For a long-term tenancy, a rental-restriction bylaw cannot stop you — those bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Listing data says yes, with limits, at all three named complexes: Reflections @ Cedar Sky is described as pet friendly, Osprey Ridge listing data shows pets allowed with restrictions (cats OK, dogs OK), and a Maple Hills listing shows cats and dogs OK with a number limit of two. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC, so the registered bylaws govern — the listing lines are UNCONFIRMED as bylaw summaries until the Form B package is read. Sources: Section 2.
- **Q: What does the hillside permit regime mean for a townhouse owner?** DPA D-2 covers the mountain: a development permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. For a strata, that regime sits behind major exterior or common-property projects, not day-to-day ownership — but it is a real cost and timing factor in a complex's long-range planning, and it is one more thing the depreciation report should reflect. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q: How do lot sizes and zoning shape the mountain's stock?** By slope and zone: SHR minimum lot area rises from 360 m² in the lowest slope band to 990 m² in the highest, HR starts at 0.2 ha on community water, and SR starts at 650 m² on full services — which is why the mountain's strata form is townhouses on assembled parcels and its default form is a detached house. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Is there transit or a school on the mountain for a condo-style, car-light lifestyle?** No. No BC Transit route in the Chilliwack list runs onto the mountain's residential streets — the closest service is route 59 Industrial via the Lickman Park & Ride — and no SD33 public school is located on the mountain in the provincial school directory; catchments are address-based in SD33's School Locator and no mountain catchment was confirmed in the research. Households here are car-dependent by design. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/ and https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Is the mountain on City sewer and water?** Partly, on the record: the City states the Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way, but service for a specific parcel or complex is not confirmed by that statement — verify connection status for the exact property before writing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- **Q: What are prices and sales doing for mountain condos?** UNVERIFIED — there are no mountain apartment condos to price, and Chilliwack is not covered by the SnapStats FVR or GVR September 2026 reports, so this file publishes no median, sales count or days-on-market figure for the area at all. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mountain has no neighbourhood plan:** Schedule B of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP contains no Chilliwack Mountain neighbourhood plan (Schedules B1–B7 cover other areas); the mountain's future form is governed by the hillside Development Permit Areas D-2 and D-5, not by a densification plan — a structural reason not to expect apartment product to be planned onto the mountain under the current OCP. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Two townhouse clusters sit almost side by side on Chilliwack Mountain Road and are easy to conflate:** the DP000817 complex at 43660/43710 (applicant Cedarsky Developments Ltd., in 2017 council records) and Reflections @ Cedar Sky at 43680. The sources checked do not state they are the same complex — treat them as separate entries until a source links them. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/agendapackage/reg/2017.01.24%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Agenda%20Package.pdf and https://kw.com/property/43680-Chilliwack-Mountain-Road-23-Chilliwack-BC-V2R-6A6/2042669732905526
- **Osprey Ridge's "year built" changes by listing** — 2022 in the building summary, 2023 and 2026 on individual units — because the 34-home development was phased. Warranty and depreciation-report timing follow the phase your unit sits in, not the complex name. https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/25-43998-chilliwack-mountain-rd-125893
- **Hillkeep Regional Park is the mountain's amenity anchor:** an FVRD nature reserve at the top of the mountain on Hillkeep Place, reached by Grandview Drive, with hiking trails and a viewing platform — the documented recreation draw for every complex on the mountain, in place of any in-complex amenity building (none of the Section 2 complexes is published with one). https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- **Chilliwack's late-payment penalties are a dated, published fact a new owner should know:** City of Chilliwack property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026, and drainage and dyking taxes are levied on top of the municipal rate. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **The nearest full services are off the mountain:** Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre at 9145 Corbould Street, Chilliwack Library at 45860 First Avenue, and Chilliwack General Hospital at 45600 Menholm Road are all on the valley floor — the mountain itself has no verified grocery store, leisure centre or library branch. https://www.ymcabc.ca/locations/chilliwack-landing-leisure-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/chilliwack-library and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
