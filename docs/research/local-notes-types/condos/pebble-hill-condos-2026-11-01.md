## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

The BC strata rules below are the frame a buyer uses here in practice — not because a Pebble Hill condo is verified, but because the nearest strata purchases a Pebble Hill search actually lands on sit in Tsawwassen Central, East and North, and because any future strata product in the sub-area would be governed by exactly these rules.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed / allowed with restrictions" line for a building a Pebble Hill buyer ends up considering elsewhere in Tsawwassen is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). No building in this sub-area is published as age-restricted, because no condo building in this sub-area is verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024, consolidated, no lettered schedules; there is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan schedule in Bylaw 8400 (the former plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** City of Delta pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled. Zoning is parcel-by-parcel on the City zoning maps as cited in the neighbouring Tsawwassen files. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **SSMUH:** Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — Delta's updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, and the City's Housing Accelerator initiatives include help for owners creating coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites; the live feed shows the pattern in Pebble Hill itself, with several listings describing legal or in-law suites in detached houses (listing copy, feed evidence only). That framework, not a condo building, is this sub-area's density story. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **Flood frame:** Tsawwassen's Boundary Bay shore is dike-protected delta land, and the Dyke Trail itself runs on the dike; the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the frame if any future project in or near the sub-area is sold on developer contracts; no such project in Pebble Hill was verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
