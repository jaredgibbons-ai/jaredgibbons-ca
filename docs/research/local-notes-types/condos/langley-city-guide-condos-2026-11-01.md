## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. All three verified City buildings are 1988–1990 buildings, so the Form B and the depreciation report behind it are where a City purchase is actually priced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2: both Parkway buildings carry directory lines saying "rentals not allowed," and those lines are void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; Langley City's own short-term rental status is UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. This is the live restriction in this roster: all three verified buildings are published as 55+ communities, so the age bylaw — unlike the rental line — can still bind a buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the City's Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3200 was adopted November 22, 2021. Zoning is **City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300**, which repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. The exact adoption date of Bylaw No. 3300: UNVERIFIED — sources in this program conflict on it, so no adoption date is stated in this file; the bylaw is cited by number only. Full land-plan detail is in the langleycity home file and is not duplicated here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md) (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langleycity-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 3300):** a secondary suite must not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md)
- **SSMUH (City):** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. That 6-unit tier is a City of Langley rule; it does NOT exist in the Township (Township Bylaw 6020 has no 6-plex tier). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md)
- **Langley City property taxes:** payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. The penalty percentage for Langley City 2026: UNVERIFIED — the City page read states that a penalty applies but does not state the rate. https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/langley-city-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the verified City roster is 1988–1990 resale stock, so GST does not apply to it. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in the City was verified in this program's roster pass; where any developer-contract purchase does arise in Langley City, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
