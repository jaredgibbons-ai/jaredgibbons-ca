## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Winchelsea (1993/1994) and the 1983 Smith Avenue townhouses, this document and the depreciation report carry the whole capital-planning picture — both complexes predate the current report cycle by decades. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. The 7-unit Smith Avenue complex is above the exemption line; a strata of four or fewer lots would be exempt — lot count per complex is UNCONFIRMED except where Section 2 states it (Smith + Norfolk, 16 homes). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Parkside Greene's listing line "Pets and rental are allowed" is consistent with current law; the Smith + Norfolk developer-era note that short-term rentals are typically not permitted unless allowed is the part that can still bind — confirm in the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw is published for Winchelsea, Parkside Greene or Smith + Norfolk in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per complex; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (Bylaw No. 4742), which is repealed — flag any source still citing the 1965 bylaw as current. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. For the detached streets around the attached pockets, Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, with a 281 m² minimum lot area and 10 m minimum lot width, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every verified complex in Section 2 is a resale (1983–2021), so GST is not in play on current Central stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to Central's resale stock. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. A market residential presale inside Central verified on a developer source: UNCONFIRMED — the newest verified complex here, Smith + Norfolk, completed in 2021. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Streamside check at the east edge:** Deer Lake and Deer Lake Brook drain the area's east side; parcel-level streamside setbacks and hazard status are property-specific checks — UNVERIFIED for individual lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's name is shorter than its slug.** Every official-style source in this pass — SnapStats, the research file — calls this ground "Central." The "BN" exists only in the site slug and in listing-feed labels ("Central BN"), and the research file flags that the ground is mid-city, not North Burnaby. A buyer searching only "Central BN" and a buyer searching only "Central Burnaby" can land on different listing sets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)
- **The whole apartment stock is one Adera low-rise with two addresses and two build years.** Winchelsea appears as 3738 Norfolk Street (built 1993, listings) and 3733 Norfolk Street (built 1994, building directory) — document orders, depreciation reports and Form B requests should be made against the strata corporation for the specific unit, not assumed from the street number. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/3738-norfolk-street/207 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/winchelsea-3733-norfolk-street
- **Zero attached sales is the headline, not a footnote.** Central recorded 0 condos & townhomes solds on 8 listings in September 2026 while neighbouring Central Park recorded 6 solds on 22 listings (27%) — the two similarly named sub-areas had opposite attached-market months, which is exactly why the name confusion in Section 4 matters to pricing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md) and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
- **The civic precinct is the east-end neighbour.** Burnaby City Hall and the governmental and cultural precinct sit at the sub-area's east end beside Deer Lake Park (5435 Sperling Ave) — a Central purchase is next to the city's civic core, not a town centre, and the research file confirms no shopping centre and no City recreation centre inside the sub-area on an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)
- **The newest townhouses wear a different neighbourhood's name.** Smith + Norfolk (3868 Norfolk Street, 16 townhomes, completed 2021) is marketed as being in the Cascade Heights neighbourhood while its listings file under Central BN — neighbourhood name, sub-area name and slug are three different labels for the same pocket, and searches under only one miss the others. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/smith-norfolk-burnaby/
