## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. West Pointe (2000) and Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar (2015) are both old enough for envelope and systems items to matter; 3590 West 39th Avenue sells from a disclosure statement instead (see the new-build notes below). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. West Pointe's listing field "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" is therefore void as a long-term rental restriction regardless of what the registered bylaws still say. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. Dunbar's house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3–4 units, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6–8 units; a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Southlands lots sit under their own agricultural / limited-agriculture districts; a current Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar-area source records that corridor's zoning context as commercial (C-2) for a nearby address — parcel-level zone for any Section 2 building: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Area plan:** Dunbar has no separate adopted community plan confirmed in the sources read: UNVERIFIED; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Flood:** the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage at the area's south end, and parcels in it carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — relevant to south-edge purchases, not to the three Dunbar Street buildings; parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** the City requires every owner to submit an annual property status declaration; properties declared or deemed empty in the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of the property's 2025 assessed taxable value, the declaration deadline for 2025 was February 3, 2026, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City tax timing:** Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — and it is live in this file: current 3590 West 39th Avenue listings state prices include GST, the tell that those units sell as new. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. West Pointe and Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption to test first on a 3590 West 39th Avenue purchase. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. The 3590 West 39th Avenue receivership sales proceed by disclosure statement and contract of purchase and sale per the current listings — a REDMA-frame purchase, not an ordinary resale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. (BC standard block, batches C1–C3)
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much condo choice is there in Dunbar, really?** Three verified apartment buildings: West Pointe (2000) and Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar (2015) on Dunbar Street, and the new concrete building at 3590 West 39th Avenue (2024). Everything else in the area is houses, the 3349 Dunbar Street townhouses, or Southlands country lots. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Dunbar condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, so West Pointe's "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" listing field cannot stop a long-term tenancy. Short-term rentals can still be restricted, and the Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to a unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published fields: West Pointe — pets allowed with restrictions, cats OK in the current listing; Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar — cats OK in a current listing; 3590 West 39th Avenue — current listings conflict (one says pets allowed, one unit's field says no pets). Pet bylaws remain enforceable, so read the registered bylaws for the specific building. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/406-5629-dunbar-st-1965952 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3590-west-39th-avenue/309
- **Q: Why do listings at 3590 West 39th Avenue say "price includes GST" and "receivership sale"?** Because those units are selling as new construction through a receivership, with a disclosure statement and 2-5-10 warranty — the buyer is purchasing from the developer side, not from a homeowner. That changes the paperwork (REDMA disclosure instead of a Form B), the tax (5% GST, partly offset by the Newly Built Home Exemption and the Bill C-4 rebate where the buyer qualifies), and the diligence (confirm the receiver's authority and warranty enrolment). https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3590-west-39th-avenue/309
- **Q: How many units are in Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar?** UNCONFIRMED — directory sources conflict (33 units on one page, 17 units on another for the same address and strata plan EPS3033). The Form B and the strata plan itself settle it; do not rely on either directory figure. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3160098-207-5555-dunbar-street-vancouver-bc-v6n-1w5
- **Q: What should I check in West Pointe, the oldest of the three?** The depreciation report: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years (no deferral vote since July 1, 2024) and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. In a 2000 building, envelope, roof and elevator items in the report matter more than the unit's finish. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve a Dunbar condo?** SD39 catchments are address-based, with no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). Elementaries located in Dunbar-Southlands on the VSB list: Kitchener (3455 West King Edward Avenue), Queen Elizabeth (4102 West 16th Avenue) and Southlands (5351 Camosun Street); a West Pointe listing places that building in the Southlands Elementary and Point Grey Secondary catchments — but run the exact address in the VSB locator, because listings are not the locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md) and https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/406-5629-dunbar-st-1965952
- **Q: Is Dunbar on a SkyTrain line?** No — it is bus-served, with route 7 on Dunbar Street and the east–west routes to UBC and downtown. The condo premium here is the village and the west-side schools, not rapid transit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Q: Does buying in Dunbar mean floodplain or ALR issues?** Only at the south edge: the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage and Southlands lots may sit in the ALR under agricultural districts. The three Dunbar Street condo buildings are on the retail frontages, not the river edge — parcel-level status for any specific lot: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Q: Is there a median condo price for Dunbar I can anchor to?** No published figure is quoted here: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in Jared's reports and no substitute source is used. With only three buildings, price from the specific building's recent sales. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Dunbar's newest condo building is selling out of a receivership.** Current listings for 3590 West 39th Avenue state "this is a receivership sale" with a disclosure statement and GST included — a buyer expecting an ordinary strata resale will misread the entire transaction. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3590-west-39th-avenue/309
- **Two addresses, one corner, possibly one project.** Presale coverage names "Dunbar at 39th" at 3636 West 39th Avenue with 29 condominiums (Wesgroup), while the built building sells from 3590 West 39th Avenue with 15 residential units in its retail brochure. Whether these are the same project is UNCONFIRMED — verify against the disclosure statement before citing either number. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/dunbar-at-39th/ and https://images1.showcase.com/d2/FQge6IWgpFk5MQfhc8xJXIrhVedAKioi2qPNLWwizZk/document.pdf
- **"Dunbar" the condo label can mean a different West Pointe.** A separate building also called West Pointe exists at 3250 West Broadway in Kitsilano (built 1990 in directory data) — same name, different building and neighbourhood. Building searches and strata documents must be matched by address, not name. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/west-pointe-3250-broadway-other
- **The whole Dunbar condo market is two streets.** All three verified apartment buildings sit on Dunbar Street or at its West 39th Avenue corner — there is no verified strata stock on the interior residential streets at all. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar's unit count is genuinely unsettled in public sources.** The same directory publishes both 33 units and 17 units for 5555 Dunbar Street — a reminder that directory unit counts are leads, not facts, until the strata plan is read. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3160098-207-5555-dunbar-street-vancouver-bc-v6n-1w5
- **Southlands is inside the same City area name but is a different product entirely.** Dunbar-Southlands pairs the Dunbar house streets with in-city country lots beside the Musqueam First Nation land, in an area the City notes was inhabited by Indigenous peoples as early as 400 B.C. — a Southlands address is not a Dunbar Village address, and neither prices like the other. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
