## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Both counted Walnut Grove buildings are 2008–2009 stock, so the depreciation report behind the Form B is the central diligence document for either. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. The Grove's directory-stated 288 units put it unambiguously inside the 5+ lot duty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — flagged in-entry in Section 2: the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Twin Lakes is void for long-term rentals and must not be read as current law. The Grove's directory line already reads "rentals allowed." Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. In Walnut Grove the published 55+ product is the townhome shelf (Twin Lakes, Spencer Green — brokerage/directory-stated 55+ communities) and the held Forest Green Estates listing claim; neither counted apartment building is published as age-restricted in this pass — UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Township plans and zoning:** Walnut Grove sits under the Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and the Walnut Grove Community Plan, Bylaw 1978 No. 1836 (adopted November 26, 1979; now Schedule 6 to the Township OCP Bylaw 6200, adopted December 1, 2025, per the guide page). https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/ATT-F---Walnut-Grove-CP---Redlined.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/walnut-grove-guide.md)
- **Township SSMUH (settled program position):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township. A Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² under Zoning Bylaw s. 102; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102 — NEVER strata, never a condo. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/walnut-grove-guide.md)
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/walnut-grove-guide.md)
- **Pad rent (parks):** where a Walnut Grove purchase is a park home (the tenure the Forest Green Estates listings suggest), pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act: the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months. No pad-rent dollar figure for any Walnut Grove park is published in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both counted Walnut Grove buildings are 2008–2009 resale stock, so GST does not apply to them. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Walnut Grove was verified in this pass; where a developer-contract purchase does arise, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
