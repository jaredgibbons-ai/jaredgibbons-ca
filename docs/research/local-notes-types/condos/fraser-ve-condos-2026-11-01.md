# Fraser — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Fraser VE" is the board/site name for the Fraser sub-area in Vancouver East, City of Vancouver. It is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas: the Fraser Street corridor runs through four official local areas — Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview — and a block-by-block mapping of the board sub-area to those four areas is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- The spine is Fraser Street itself, running south from the East Broadway / Kingsway area to SE Marine Drive, crossed by East Broadway, Kingsway, East King Edward Avenue, East 33rd Avenue, East 41st Avenue, East 49th Avenue and SE Marine Drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: in small strata buildings on and just off the Fraser Street frontage itself — a 1990s low-rise at 3981 Fraser, a boutique cluster at East 16th–19th Avenues (Vintage Eastside, Mondella, Bold on Fraser, Windsor Views a block east), mid-corridor low-rises at East 41st–49th (Century, Fraserview Court), and one large tower at the south end (Fraser Commons at Marine, 8188 Fraser Street). The side streets are houses with suites and laneway houses under the R1-1 schedule. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit: TransLink route 8 Fraser runs Waterfront Station to SE Marine Drive via Fraser Street, Broadway, Kingsway, Main Street and Hastings Street, ending at Fraser Street and East 65th Avenue beside Marine Drive Station (Canada Line); the N8 is the night service on the same corridor. Cross-corridor routes include the 49 at East 49th Avenue, the R4 41st Ave RapidBus and the 41 at East 41st Avenue, and the 9 at East Broadway. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats in this program's reports — no median, inventory or sales-ratio figure is quoted anywhere in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In this corridor's small buildings (The Sheridan at 11 units, Windsor Views at 16), the Form B and two years of minutes are effectively the whole risk picture. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every building in Section 2 has five or more units, so every one of them is in the mandatory regime — including the 1993 and 1999 buildings (3981 Fraser, Vintage Eastside), whose envelope and parkade cycles the report prices. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted — and Vancouver strata buyers must also clear the City's own short-term rental rules, which are a municipal layer on top of the strata bylaws. Any older directory line saying "rentals not allowed" in a Fraser building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — confirm any age bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Vancouver zoning:** the side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Fraser Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. The Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview/Killarney community visions and the citywide Vancouver Plan apply along the corridor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Vancouver tax calendar and Empty Homes Tax:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date (home owner grant also due July 3, 2026). Separately, Vancouver owners must file an annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration — the 2025 tax's payment was due April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty — so a vacant or secondary Fraser condo carries an extra annual declaration step no other city in this batch imposes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Floodplain at the south end:** the City's designated floodplain dataset covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the south end of the city, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — whether a specific corridor parcel (including the Fraser Commons site) is in the floodplain is a parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any individual building in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most of this corridor's stock is resale, so GST rarely arises except on a remaining developer unit. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — relevant to any new corridor presale sold under the Villages Plan (Section 5). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Fraser" a real Vancouver neighbourhood?** Not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas — the corridor runs through Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview, and "Fraser VE" is the board name. The practical consequence: City plans, visions and permit rules must be looked up under the official local area for the specific block, not under "Fraser." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Q: Is Fraser Commons a condo or a rental?** Both names exist at the same development: the 8188 Fraser Street tower (21 storeys, 363 units, built 2021 by Serracan) is strata condo; the 747 SE Marine Drive building marketed as "Fraser Commons Apartments" is purpose-built rental. Check which address a listing actually carries before comparing it to anything. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md) and https://www.apartments.com/fraser-commons-vancouver-bc/922t9j4/
- **Q: And the building at Fraser and 19th — condo or rental?** Also both, on the same corner: Bold on Fraser (688 East 19th Avenue, strata plan EPS4637, 36 units, built 2017) is strata; Comma Fraser is a 104-home purpose-built rental completed 2023. The two are routinely confused in search results. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3111855-302-688-e-19-avenue-vancouver-british-columbia-v5v1k2-679000/brochure and https://tetrarealty.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Comma-Fraser-E-19th-Ave-Fraser-St-Vancouver-BC.pdf
- **Q: How small are these strata corporations, really?** Small: The Sheridan has 11 units and Windsor Views 16, against 363 at Fraser Commons. In an 11-unit strata every owner carries roughly a ninth of any special levy, and a 4-or-fewer-lot depreciation exemption does not apply (all Section 2 buildings have 5+ units) — read the CRF balance on the Form B with the unit count in mind. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Are pets allowed on the corridor?** Published examples: The Sheridan and Vintage Eastside listings record "Cats OK"; a Century listing calls the building "pet and transit friendly"; Windsor Views' listing record states pets allowed. Full bylaws (dogs, numbers, size) are UNCONFIRMED from public sources for these buildings — the registered bylaws govern. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/4372-fraser-street/301 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/688-east-16th-avenue and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3173605-204-979-e-19th-avenue-vancouver-bc-v5v-1k8
- **Q: Can I rent out a Fraser condo, or run it as a short-term rental?** Long-term, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022. Short-term is doubly restricted: a strata bylaw can still ban it, and the City of Vancouver runs its own short-term rental licensing layer. Do not underwrite a Fraser purchase on short-term rental income without checking both. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve a corridor condo?** SD39 (Vancouver) catchments are address-based, K–7 / 8–12 with no middle tier. John Oliver Secondary (530 East 41st Avenue) sits on the corridor itself, and the verified locator result for that address is Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary and John Oliver Secondary — but a school sitting on the corridor does not make it the catchment for any other address. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Q: What transit does a Fraser condo actually get?** One bus, end to end: route 8 runs the full corridor from Waterfront Station to SE Marine Drive beside Marine Drive Station (Canada Line), with the N8 at night; the R4 RapidBus crosses at East 41st Avenue and route 49 at East 49th. There is no SkyTrain station on Fraser Street itself — the Canada Line connection is at the south end, at Marine Drive Station. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a condo I leave empty between tenants?** Vancouver owners must file an annual property status declaration, and a property that meets the tax's vacancy tests is taxed — the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date. Confirm the declaration status and any exemption on a specific purchase; this is a Vancouver-only step on top of provincial taxes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Q: Why are there no prices or market stats in this file?** Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage in this program's reports and no substitute source is used — any Fraser VE median or inventory figure quoted elsewhere is UNVERIFIED for the purposes of these notes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two of the corridor's biggest "condo" names are rentals.** Fraser Commons (747 SE Marine Drive address) and Comma Fraser (Fraser + 19th, 104 rental homes, completed 2023) are purpose-built rental — they dominate search results for the corridor's newest stock while contributing zero strata units. The corridor's genuinely new strata supply in this file is one tower: Fraser Commons at Marine, 8188 Fraser Street (2021). Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The City is planning retail growth at Fraser and 33rd.** The City's Villages Planning Program names Fraser Street and East 33rd Avenue as a village area, and the Villages Plan will be implemented through private development and City-initiated rezonings with retail expansion planned — frontage buildings near 33rd carry planning expectations the quiet side streets do not. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **The library branch has a different name than the corridor.** The South Hill Branch of Vancouver Public Library is at 6076 Fraser Street — a buyer searching "Fraser Street library" by corridor name will miss it; it is the branch serving the Sunset stretch of the corridor. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **The corridor's hospital is not the famous one.** Mount Saint Joseph Hospital sits at the Prince Edward Street entrance beside the corridor in Kensington-Cedar Cottage; Vancouver General Hospital (899 West 12th Avenue) is west of the corridor. Listing copy that assigns VGH to a Fraser address is stretching the geography. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **Memorial South Park is the corridor's sports park, and it is not on Fraser.** At 5955 Ross Street in Sunset, beside the corridor, with sports fields and courts per the Park Board — the amenity the East 49th–59th stretch of buildings actually uses, under a Ross Street address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
- **No Fraser-area community centre was confirmed.** The research pass could not confirm a City community centre inside the Fraser VE sub-area on a City source — the centres named in sources serve the adjoining local areas. A listing that promises a community centre "in the neighbourhood" for a Fraser address should be checked against the specific centre's address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/fraser-ve.md)
