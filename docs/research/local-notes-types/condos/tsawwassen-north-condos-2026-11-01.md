# Tsawwassen North, Delta — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Building-directory and listing sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen North" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the north side of Tsawwassen along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The defining boundary on this side is a jurisdictional one: the north side sits beside Tsawwassen First Nation Lands. Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing, with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so City of Delta zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Main roads: Highway 17 (to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Highway 17A), 52 Street, 56 Street and Canoe Pass Way; Highway 17A leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Transit: route 609 runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange; route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: in one development — Tsawwassen Springs, on Springs Boulevard at 52 Street, around the golf course. The rest of the City-of-Delta side is single detached houses under the RS and RD zones on the streets south of Highway 17, with farm and ALR land between Tsawwassen and Ladner. Sources: the building entries in Section 2 below, and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Services are south or on the corridor: South Delta Recreation Centre, 1720 56 St at South Delta Exchange; Tsawwassen Library, 1321A 56 St; Thrifty Foods on the Town Centre Mall site (1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street); and Fraser Health's South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way, on the Canoe Pass Way corridor. A grocery store on the north side itself, named by an official source: UNVERIFIED. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/ and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- Schools — the north side's trap: for the sample address 5000 Canoe Pass Way, the SD37 School Locator result is **Ladner Elementary (K–7), 5016 44 Ave., and Delta Secondary (8–12), 4615 51 St** — Ladner catchments, not Tsawwassen ones. Every address must be checked in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Jurisdiction first:** City of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 and OCP Bylaw No. 8400 apply only to the City side. On Tsawwassen Lands, Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction and land law apply instead — tenure, servicing and resale handling differ, and a buyer must establish which side of the boundary a property sits on before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provide-within-7-days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; since 2023 it must also summarize insurance and electrical planning reports. In a geothermal/concrete building, the fee inclusions (a 5011 Springs listing records heat and hot water inside the fee for its unit) are read here, not from marketing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3087621-5011-springs-boulevard-410-delta-bc-v4m-0b6/brochure
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote, with 30-year projections and three CRF cash-flow models; a strata corporation established on or after July 1, 2024 and before July 1, 2027 must obtain its first report within two years of its first AGM (the timeline that applies to any brand-new north-side strata). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** Nov 24, 2022 — no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid, no amendment needed; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2); lower ages have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and the rule binds residents, not owners. No Springs building has a verified 55+ bylaw — UNCONFIRMED per building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta zoning (City side):** Bylaw No. 7600 residential ladder includes RS4 at a 610 m² subdivision minimum, RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated — no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former plan was Schedule D.1 under Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)," and Agricultural Land Commission rules apply to land in the ALR, including one-residence-per-parcel limits relevant to large lots at the farm edge. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta; six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Flood:** farm and ALR land lies between Tsawwassen and Ladner, and Delta is the diking authority operating over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. A flood construction level for any specific Springs building lot: UNCONFIRMED. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT exemptions:** first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, and a never-occupied unit in a newly built condo building can qualify (the exemption to check on any remaining developer-held Springs unit). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on new construction:** FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, royal assent Mar 13, 2026) with the new housing rebate — 100% of GST to $1M value, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; new housing rebate alone 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Resale Springs units carry no GST question; a developer sale does. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales and assignments (REDMA):** a presale buyer has a 7-day statutory rescission period from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, with deposits held in trust. Assignment terms are contract-specific — no assignment term for any Springs phase was verified in a public source: UNCONFIRMED, read the disclosure statement. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (CRA-verified in this program's earlier passes); CMHC homeowner premiums 0.60% (LTV to 65%) to 4.50% (90.01%–95% LTV, non-traditional down payment). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal — the route the area research file flags for newer strata product here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Tsawwassen Springs condo freehold strata or leasehold?** The listings read for City-side Springs buildings record apartment/condo product in the Tsawwassen Springs subdivision, and a 5011 Springs listing records concrete construction and underground parking — but the title type for a specific building is a title-search and Form B question, and no source read states "freehold strata" for a Springs building in so many words: UNCONFIRMED here, verify on title. Do not confuse City-side Springs product with housing on Tsawwassen Lands next door, which sits under TFN jurisdiction and land law. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3087621-5011-springs-boulevard-410-delta-bc-v4m-0b6/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: What do Springs strata fees include?** More than a typical wood-frame building's, on the listings read: a 5011 Springs unit listing records caretaker, garbage, heat, hot water and sewer in the fee inclusions, consistent with the buildings' geothermal heating/cooling. The binding list for any lot is the Form B and the strata budget attached to it. https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3087621-5011-springs-boulevard-410-delta-bc-v4m-0b6/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Are the Springs buildings concrete or wood frame?** Listings record the later buildings as concrete — 5055 Springs ("concrete construction, geothermal heating/cooling, EV parking") and 5011 Springs ("concrete" construction in listing data). Construction for 5099 Springs beyond its geothermal heating: UNCONFIRMED. Confirm against the depreciation report, which inventories the building's components. https://kw.com/property/5055-Springs-Boulevard-401-Delta-BC-V4M-0A5/2028516335580008 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3070929-5011-springs-boulevard-112-delta-bc-v4m-0b6/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a Springs unit?** Long-term, the provincial answer governs: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and no Springs-specific short-term bylaw was verified — UNCONFIRMED, read the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Can I bring a dog?** UNCONFIRMED as a bylaw. A 5099 Springs listing calls a unit "pet friendly"; that is marketing language. Pet number/size rules live in the registered bylaws attached to the strata document package. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3085688-210-5099-springs-boulevard-tsawwassen-british-columbia-v4m0a2-1175000/brochure
- **Q: Which schools will my kids actually attend?** Possibly Ladner's. The SD37 locator result for 5000 Canoe Pass Way is Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary — so a "Tsawwassen" address on the north side can carry a Ladner catchment, and the exact address must be run in the locator before a school is promised. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What levy risk comes with a geothermal, concrete building?** Different risk, not no risk: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024) projects 30 years of component costs — geo-exchange fields, elevators and parkade systems included — with three CRF funding models to compare against the CRF balance on the Form B. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Is there an age restriction or a golf-membership catch?** Age restriction: UNCONFIRMED — no source read states a 55+ bylaw for any Springs building. Golf/amenity cost structure: a neighbourhood source states the wider Springs community's strata fees are kept low by letting residents pay for amenities and services if and when they are used (secondary source) — the actual amenity-fee arrangement for a specific building is UNCONFIRMED and belongs in the strata documents. https://www.michaellepore.ca/neighbourhood/tsawwassen/page/2/
- **Q: How do I get downtown or to the ferry without a car?** Route 609 runs via 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way through Tsawwassen First Nation to Ladner Exchange, and route 601 serves South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station; Highway 17 runs past the area to the BC Ferries terminal at 1 Ferry Causeway. Published ferry crossings: 1 hour 35 minutes to Swartz Bay, 2 hours to Duke Point. Road commute times: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
- **Q: If I buy a never-occupied developer unit at Springs, what taxes change?** Two: GST applies to new construction (with the Bill C-4 FTHB rebate — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000 — and the older new housing rebate to check), and the BC newly built home PTT exemption can apply to a never-occupied condo unit (full threshold $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000) regardless of first-time status. A resale unit triggers neither. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Springs was built in phases over most of a decade, and the phases are different buildings:** listings date 5099 Springs Boulevard to 2011, 5055 to 2014 and 5011 (the developer's "Phase 3 Condo") to 2018 — age, warranty history and depreciation position differ by address, not by brand. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3116351-313-5099-springs-boulevard-tsawwassen-british-columbia-v4m0a2-749900/brochure and https://kw.com/property/5055-Springs-Boulevard-401-Delta-BC-V4M-0A5/2028516335580008 and https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/page/40/?post_type=page&p=0
- **The geothermal system is literally under the buildings:** the developer's Phase 3 construction update describes 88 boreholes drilled 240 feet deep for the geo-exchange system at 5011 Springs Boulevard — the heating/cooling plant a buyer's depreciation report has to account for. https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/page/40/?post_type=page&p=0
- **More product is already in the City's pipeline on the same street:** a townhouse project at 4800 Springs Boulevard (with a portion of 5133 Springs Boulevard) appears on the City of Delta housing projects map — the next phase of the Springs Boulevard corridor to check before paying a scarcity premium. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **The bus goes through a First Nation to get to Ladner:** route 609's path via Canoe Pass Way passes through Tsawwassen First Nation — a reminder that the "north side" is a shared landscape of City land, TFN Lands and farm land, each with its own rules. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **The urgent-care address buyers drive past is on Canoe Pass Way:** Fraser Health's South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way, on this side of Tsawwassen; the hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **SSMUH is the quiet change on the older streets south of Highway 17:** since Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (Jun 24, 2024), detached and duplex lots on the City side can carry up to four units — the infill pressure to watch around the established detached streets, while the farm belt to Ladner stays ALR. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
