## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no verified strata in Whonnock for this to attach to today; it is the document to demand if any strata product is ever offered here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC. **Age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions under Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (expanded May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Any age bylaw below 55 (19+, 40+, 45+) is invalid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; its schedule lists minimum lot areas including A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text — lot minimums measured in hectares are why this district has acreages instead of condo buildings. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, never resolved:** program sources conflict on the current Maple Ridge Official Community Plan bylaw number (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024); do not print an OCP number until the conflict is resolved against the City's own bylaw page. What is settled: Council adopted updates to the OCP on November 25, 2025, and the Albion Area Plan update on October 28, 2025; no area plan for Whonnock was found. Page defect documented in the research file, stated precisely: the guide page's Planning section groups the Albion update with the November 2025 adoptions — Albion was adopted October 28, 2025, not in November. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor:** where it is mentioned at all, the settled position is ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021), with an earlier contrary reading in this program noted and superseded. It does not create any Whonnock condo stock in any source checked. (Standing position carried from the program's Maple Ridge files.)
- **Suites and ALR:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area. On ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation also governs: one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31), and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. ALR status is per-parcel — check the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** for 2026, due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Inputs: fair market value $525,000 (the Maple Ridge community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, illustration only — it is NOT a Whonnock figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,500 (2% × $325,000) = **$8,500 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Whonnock is verified in this program; where a developer contract is ever used in BC, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Can I house family or a caregiver on a Whonnock acreage instead?** Generally yes, if the zoning lines up: one secondary suite per lot is permitted in the A-1, A-2 and A-3 zones, inside the single detached building, up to 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land the suite must sit inside the principal residence under the ALR Use Regulation (s.31). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Could a strata be created on ALR land here?** Strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision under ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6, and most subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval — an ALR parcel cannot simply be stratified into condo units. Parcel-level ALR status: check the ALC map before assuming anything. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **Q: What about wells and septic?** Whonnock acreages run on private services: wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. Pull both records during subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Which schools serve a Whonnock address?** Whonnock Elementary (27471 112 Ave) sits in the area and Garibaldi Secondary (24789 Dewdney Trunk Rd) is the east-side secondary school, with names and addresses confirmed against the BC Ministry K-12 file — but address-level catchments are UNVERIFIED in this program, so confirm any address in the SD42 School Locator. SD42 runs regular school bus service only for listed schools, which include Whonnock Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
- **Q: Is Whonnock in a floodplain?** Parts of the low south edge sit in the Fraser River floodplain framework, and the City prohibits secondary suites in the Floodplain Area — check City flood mapping for the specific parcel before removing subjects. Parcel-level flood status: UNCONFIRMED in these notes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: What PTT should I budget on a Maple Ridge attached purchase?** On the Section 3 worked example — inputs: $525,000 fair market value (the September 2026 Maple Ridge community attached median, illustration only), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $8,500. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which hospital serves Whonnock?** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge — the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place. Both are in central Maple Ridge, not Whonnock. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/whonnock-guide.md)
