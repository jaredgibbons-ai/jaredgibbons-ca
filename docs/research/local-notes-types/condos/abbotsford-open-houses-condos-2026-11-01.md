## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/abbotsford-open-houses.md)
- **Representation at an open house:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/abbotsford-open-houses.md)
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** not stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask on the seller's instructions. How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication on the brokerage/board side is likewise UNVERIFIED on any BC Gov or BCFSA source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/abbotsford-open-houses.md)
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — which is why the Regency Park directory line in Section 2 is flagged VOID in-entry. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Abbotsford's current Official Community Plan is **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025)** — the 2016 OCP is not current. Zoning is the City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014; its s. 140 allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s. 140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, a suite being less than 50% of the size of the principal unit. Abbotsford's SSMUH bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — it is not asserted in this file. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/abbotsford-open-houses.md)
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026; penalties are legislated and not waivable. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/abbotsford-open-houses.md)
- **ALR frame for the acreage end of this page's scope:** on an ALR parcel, one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), any suite inside the principal residence, and ALC permission required to subdivide — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), so ALR ground cannot be stratified without ALC permission. Parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED — check ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $497,500 (the Abbotsford community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $5,950 (2% × $297,500) = **$7,950 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer-contract purchase arises on this feed (Sage in Abbotsford East is the named example), REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
