## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020, the rule this page must be read against (SETTLED):** Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) amended Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 to allow small-scale multi-unit housing on eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, lot not larger than 4,050 m²) — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots, lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **NO 6-unit tier currently applies in the Township**. The development guide's framing differs in two places, both flagged for Jerry: it describes a 6-plex tier for transit-adjacent lots (no such tier exists in Bylaw No. 6020, and Transit Oriented Area lots are excluded from SSMUH eligibility), and it states "ALR properties are excluded entirely" without parcel-level caution — whether a specific Brookswood parcel is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED per parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md)
- **Community plan:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge, replacing an earlier community plan, with Booth, Fernridge and Rinn Neighbourhood Plans under it; the Township states the plan allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses. The development guide's further claim that the Township designated Brookswood-Fernridge as its "next major area of growth after Willoughby" is UNVERIFIED — no Township source opened in this program states that designation. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md)
- **Zoning and lot sizes:** Township Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 zones named in Township notices for the area include SR-1, SR-2, SR-3 and RU-2. Read from Bylaw 2500 Section 110.1 in Township council reports: SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² and minimum frontage 40 m; SR-2 minimum 8,094 m² and 45 m; SR-3 minimum 1,765 m² and 27.45 m. RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md)
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Township):** the Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² maximum gross floor area (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102). A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102 — NEVER strata, never a condo. (Home file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/brookswood-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Presales (REDMA) — the frame for every developer sale named here:** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Bromley (Section 2) is selling as a presale with Phase 1 completion estimated Summer 2027 on the developer's sheet, so developer contracts there sit inside this frame. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It applies to a completed strata project on resale — there is no resale strata building in Brookswood for it to attach to today (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. A newly completed Brookswood strata project inherits both duties from registration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR / second dwellings:** the Agricultural Land Commission's housing rules permit one principal residence and, subject to conditions in the ALR Use Regulation, one additional residence, limited to 90 m² or less on parcels 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha. Township zoning for a second dwelling or coach house on non-reserve Brookswood acreage is UNVERIFIED. ALR residences cannot be stratified, so ALR parcels never become condo stock. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md)
- **Township property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-17/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the tax that attaches to a Brookswood presale such as Bromley. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — the exemption a completed new Brookswood build is tested against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
