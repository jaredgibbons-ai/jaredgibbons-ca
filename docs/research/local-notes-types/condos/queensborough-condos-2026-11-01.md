# Queensborough — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography note:** Queensborough is New Westminster's island neighbourhood on the east tip of Lulu Island — District Lots 757 and 758 in the Zoning Bylaw wording — bordered by the Fraser River (main arm) and the North Arm, joined to mainland New Westminster only by Highway 91A, the Queensborough Connector, over the Queensborough Bridge. It is **across the river from, and separate from, the Quay**: the mainland waterfront towers counted in the Quay file (RiverSky, Anchor Pointe, Riviera and the rest of Quayside Drive) are not Queensborough buildings and are not imported here. Queensborough's own condo stock is the low-rise strata row at **Port Royal**, the newer development area at the island's north end, plus one older low-rise at Star Crescent and one mixed-anchored building on Hampton Street at Ewen Avenue. The research file confirmed no building name on an official source; every building below was verified by name in this pass on listing, strata or developer-side sources. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The whole Queensborough land mass east of the Richmond boundary, ringed by the Fraser River main arm to the south and east and the North Arm to the north — the east tip of Lulu Island, sharing the island with the City of Richmond. Salter Street is the Port Royal spine; Ewen Avenue is the Main Street commercial strip; Howes Street serves the south side. Every trip to Uptown, Downtown or Royal Columbian Hospital crosses the Queensborough Bridge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: Port Royal, at the north end — a run of Aragon low-rise strata buildings along Salter Street (Paddler's Landing, Regatta, Glasshouse Lofts, Portage, Light House, Timber House) capped by the community's only high-rise, The Peninsula at 210 Salter Street, with Residences by the River (83 Star Crescent, 1998) as the older waterfront stock at the island's north-east tip and Via (288 Hampton Street) anchoring the Ewen Avenue side. In September 2026 Queensborough recorded 72 condos & townhomes listings and 9 sales (a 13% sales ratio) against 41 detached listings and 1 sale (2%) — the largest attached stock of any New Westminster sub-area in this batch. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Queensborough, read the Form B together with the building's flood and insurance position below — in a Floodplain Zone 1 neighbourhood, the strata's insurance cover, deductibles and flood history are part of the same diligence. At Via, also confirm the section position (Section 2). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every building in Section 2 exceeds five lots on any count published. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Queensborough building reflects old bylaw text, not current law. (Portage's published position — rentals allowed, 100% — is consistent with current law in any case.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Queensborough building in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Floodplain — Queensborough's defining rule:** Queensborough is Floodplain Zone 1 in the City's floodplain study: a low-lying area with high groundwater, poor compressible soils and poor drainage, protected by a shared dyking and pumped drainage system with Richmond. The City states that in the 1960s a perimeter dyke was built on Queensborough protecting approximately 350 hectares, that Queensborough has 350 hectares of designated floodplain and is the highest priority for flood protection improvements in the city. The City's Flood Hazard Development Permit Area sets minimum construction levels of 3.53 metres above Geodetic Survey of Canada datum for Area A (generally downstream from Derwent Way on the south side and Wood Street on the north side) and 4.20 metres above GSC datum for Area B (upstream of Derwent Way and Wood Street). The City received $800,000 from the provincial Community Emergency Preparedness Fund for the Queensborough Dike Shoreline Protection Rehabilitation project, restabilising eroding dike slopes behind 345 Gifford Street and behind 805 Boyd Street. Pull the specific property's flood construction level, confirm the strata's insurance and deductible position, and get an insurance quote before subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- **City plans and zoning:** the Queensborough Community Plan (a schedule to Our City 2041, the City of New Westminster Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017) governs the area, together with the Queensborough Eastern Neighbourhood Node Master Plan; zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001. The (RLR) Residential – Low Rise designation in the OCP amendment materials (Bylaw No. 8574, 2026) provides compact-lot single detached form, low-rise buildings up to six storeys, and stacked and side-by-side townhouses. Zoning Bylaw s.190.36 provides that no Queensborough (District Lots 757 and 758) subdivision may produce a lot under one-half acre (0.20 ha) or with frontage under 66 feet (20.12 m) unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility and City water/sewer services; Queensborough drainage is pumped as part of the shared dyking and pumped drainage system with Richmond. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. It is live in Queensborough at Portside (presale townhomes) and at any remaining developer sale at Timber House (2023). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — relevant to developer sales at Portside and Timber House, not to resales. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Portside, and at Timber House if the unit is a developer sale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, every Queensborough building sits in Floodplain Zone 1, so a lender's and insurer's view of a specific property turns on the building's flood construction level and insurance position — no source checked publishes a building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Queensborough in the floodplain?** Yes — the whole neighbourhood is Floodplain Zone 1, a low-lying area with high groundwater and poor drainage behind a 1960s perimeter dyke protecting about 350 hectares, with a pumped drainage system shared with Richmond. New construction must meet flood construction levels of 3.53 m (Area A) or 4.20 m (Area B) above GSC datum. The practical questions are building-specific: the lot's flood construction level position, strata insurance cover and deductibles, and any flood claims history in the strata documents. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- **Q: Who built Port Royal, and how old is the stock?** Aragon built the Salter Street row in sequence: Paddler's Landing (listings 2006/2007), Regatta (2008/2009), Glasshouse Lofts (2013), Portage (2015), The Peninsula tower (2017), Light House (2020 — Aragon's thirteenth Port Royal building, per its profile) and Timber House (2023). The island's other verified low-rise is older: Residences by the River (1998) at 83 Star Crescent, and Via (2009) on Hampton Street. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is there really a high-rise in Queensborough?** One: The Peninsula (210 Salter Street), a 22-storey concrete tower of 163 homes at the island's tip, bordered by the Fraser River on three sides, with a private marina. Everything else verified in Port Royal is 4–5 storeys. One building-data source records its build year as 2016 against the listings' 2017 — flagged in Section 2. http://atlas-anchor.com/portfolio/peninsula-port-royal-new-westminster-bc/
- **Q: Timber House — how many homes, and is it a presale?** Built 2023 by Aragon (strata plan EPS9220), still inside its 2-5-10 warranty per the building-data source. The unit count is UNCONFIRMED: sources record 87 homes in 5 storeys and 77 market homes across three timber low-rises. - **Q: Can I rent out a Queensborough condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Portage's own MLS data sheet already records rentals allowed (100%). Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws on the Form B govern that question. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84329/FullPublic.pdf?i=294324733

The Peninsula's current listings state 2 pets allowed; Paddler's Landing listings describe the building as pet-friendly; for Regatta, Glasshouse Lofts, Light House, Timber House, Residences by the River and Via, a verified bylaw position is UNCONFIRMED — pet bylaws remain enforceable, so the Form B bylaws govern. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84329/FullPublic.pdf?i=294324733

- **One developer wrote almost the whole condo row, in order.** Aragon's Port Royal sequence — Paddler's Landing, Regatta, Glasshouse Lofts, Portage, The Peninsula, Light House ("their thirteenth building within the Port Royal neighbourhood"), Timber House — means cross-building comparisons of construction, warranty and bylaw drafting inside Queensborough are unusually direct: the same builder's details (reclaimed brick, fir floors and doors, 10-foot ceilings) repeat from 2006 to 2023. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **The dyke under the view is an active work site file.** The City's dike rehabilitation funding ($800,000, provincial Community Emergency Preparedness Fund) restabilises eroding dike slopes at two specific spots — behind 345 Gifford Street and behind 805 Boyd Street, on the island's north-east side, the same shore as Residences by the River and the Port Royal row. Queensborough is the City's highest priority for flood protection improvements. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/queensborough.md)
- **Two Salter Street addresses are rentals wearing condo clothes.** The Dockyards (202 Salter Street) and Shoreline (320 Salter Street) surface on rental platforms with monthly rents and separate parking/locker charges, and no strata-sale source backs either in this pass — a buyer browsing "Port Royal waterfront homes" will meet both and should know neither is verified purchasable strata stock. Sources: Section 2 exclusion entries.
- **Portage is two civic addresses and one strata.** Listings address the same EPS2586 building as both 260 and 262 Salter Street, and its MLS data sheet is the fullest published restriction set in the neighbourhood: cats and dogs OK to a limit of two, rentals allowed at 100%. Address and strata-plan checks on title are what keep a Portage file straight. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84329/FullPublic.pdf?i=294324733
- **Via is a sectioned strata — the Form B has two layers.** The strata's own AGM package is addressed to "Via – The Residential Section" (BCS3566) and lists locations on both Hampton Street and Ewen Avenue: sections can carry their own bylaws, budgets and contingency funds inside one corporation, so a Via buyer's documents are section documents as well as corporation documents. https://www.quaypacific.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/Via-Corporate-AGM-Package-1.pdf
- **Secondary school is a bridge trip; elementary and middle are not.** Queensborough is the only New Westminster neighbourhood with its own on-island elementary (Queen Elizabeth, K–4) and middle school (Queensborough Middle, 5–8, at 833 Salter Street) — but New Westminster Secondary (9–12) is on the mainland, so the bridge becomes part of the school run for four years of every island family's life. - **The island's sewer rule is written into the Zoning Bylaw.** Section 190.36 bars creating a Queensborough lot under one-half acre or under 66 feet of frontage unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system — most lots are sewer-connected, but the connection status of a specific lot governs subdivision and redevelopment promises, and it is a title-level check, not a listing fact.
