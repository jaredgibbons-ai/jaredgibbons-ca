## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Cariboo is the north-east/east Burnaby sub-area around **Cariboo Road and Cariboo Hill**, inside the City's Cariboo-Armstrong planning area. Its residential streets run south from the Lougheed Highway corridor toward 16th Avenue and the New Westminster border side of east Burnaby. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- The verified condo stock concentrates in one pocket: **Manchester Drive**, south of Lougheed Highway, where four wood-frame and concrete complexes sit together. That pocket is MLS-classified Cariboo. It is distinct from The City of Lougheed tower stock north of the highway, which is NOT imported into this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md) and https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-cariboo-apartments/SearchResults.form/
- Green anchors: Stoney Creek Park (2 tennis courts, 2 pickleball courts) and the Cariboo/Brunette River Conservation Lands on the north side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- Schools: Armstrong Elementary (8757 Armstrong Avenue), Cariboo Hill Secondary (8580 16th Avenue) and Lyndhurst Elementary (9847 Lyndhurst Street). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. In Cariboo's 1981–1994 stock this is the central diligence document — the published upgrade histories in Section 2 (rainscreens, re-piping, roofs, elevators) are exactly the cycles the Form B and depreciation report price out. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. No complex in Section 2 publishes a "rentals not allowed" line — Barclay Woods, Brookside Park and Strathmore Towers all publish rentals allowed, which is simply current law; any older bylaw text saying otherwise is unenforceable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Cariboo complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** Burnaby's current zoning bylaw is the **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026). The 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 is repealed — any source still citing No. 4742 is superseded. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP. Under the R1 SSMUH rules in place since June 2024, a residential lot can hold 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after September; Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — everything in Section 2 is resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to this resale stock. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Cariboo's condos, actually?** In one pocket: Manchester Drive, south of Lougheed Highway. Four complexes — Barclay Woods, Brookside Court, Brookside Park and Strathmore Towers — hold the sub-area's verified apartment stock; the rest of Cariboo is detached streets, parks and conservation land. https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-north-cariboo-apartments/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Are the Manchester Drive buildings part of The City of Lougheed?** No. They sit south of Lougheed Highway and are MLS-classified Cariboo; the City of Lougheed towers are a separate stock handled under the Lougheed-area files. The distinction matters because the Manchester buildings are 1981–1994 low-rise and tower stock, not new town-centre towers. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- **Q: Which buildings are wood-frame and which are concrete?** Barclay Woods and Brookside Court are wood-frame; Strathmore Towers (1994) is three concrete high-rises. Brookside Park's construction type is UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked. Wood-frame versus concrete changes the insurance, depreciation-report and levy conversation — confirm the specific building on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/strathmore-towers-9633-manchester-drive
- **Q: Barclay Woods is listed as built in 1980 and 1981 — which is right?** UNCONFIRMED. Unit listings state 1980; the directory page for 9867 Manchester Drive states 1981. Verify against the strata plan before relying on either year. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/barclay-woods-9867-manchester-drive
- **Q: Can I have a dog?** It depends on the complex. Brookside Court's published position is one cat allowed, no dogs. Barclay Woods publishes pets allowed with restrictions (one listing specifies 1 cat or 1 dog). Brookside Park and Strathmore Towers publish pets allowed. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC — the registered bylaws on the Form B govern. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/listing/r3178490-106-9880-manchester-drive-burnaby-bc-v3n-4r3
- **Q: Can I rent out a Cariboo condo long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and the registered bylaws govern that question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How active is the market?** Very quiet in September 2026: SnapStats' Cariboo row shows Detached at 0 active listings and 0 sales, and Condos & Townhomes at 23 active listings and 1 sale (4%). At the whole-community level, Burnaby Condos & Townhomes recorded 1,333 active listings, 147 sales, a $686,000 median, $763/sqft, a 98% sale-to-list ratio and 36 days on market; SnapStats publishes medians at community level only, never for this sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve the area?** Armstrong Elementary (8757 Armstrong Avenue), Lyndhurst Elementary (9847 Lyndhurst Street) and Cariboo Hill Secondary (8580 16th Avenue) are the named schools in and beside the sub-area. Burnaby School District 41 runs 41 elementary and 8 secondary schools with no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12), and catchments are address-based through the SD41 locator — the research file's locator sample for this area is UNVERIFIED, so run the specific building's address before relying on a catchment. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md) and https://burnabyschools.ca/about-sd41-overview/
- **Q: What is happening with the Cameron Community Centre?** It is being replaced in a $267M project; in the meantime the temporary Cameron operates inside The City of Lougheed shopping centre at 110-9855 Austin Avenue — programming continues, but not in a standalone building. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- **Q: What should I check first in these 1980s buildings?** The depreciation report and the published capital-work history. Brookside Court lists rainscreen and re-piping in 2025, windows in 2019 and an elevator in 2017; Barclay Woods lists windows 2013, plumbing 2014, common areas 2015 and roof 2018. Work already done is only half the question — the Form B shows what the next cycle costs and what the CRF holds against it. https://www.zolo.ca/burnaby-real-estate/9880-manchester-drive/312

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
- **September 2026 recorded zero detached listings and zero detached sales for Cariboo.** The sub-area's entire recorded market that month was 23 condos & townhomes listings and 1 sale — pricing evidence here is thin by structure, not by week. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cariboo.md)
