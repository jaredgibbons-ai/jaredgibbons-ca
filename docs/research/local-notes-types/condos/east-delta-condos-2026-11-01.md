Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves.

**Geography flag — read first.** "East Delta" is NOT an official City of Delta neighbourhood name. The City plans Delta as three communities — Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta — and publishes no boundary for "East Delta"; the name is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name for the rural farm land and the East Ladner urban edge east of the Ladner village core. This file follows the research file's definition: the farm belt and eastern Ladner fringe, not a separate town. Port Guichon, by contrast, is in Ladner, not in this file. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

These strata rules are stated for completeness — they would govern any strata purchase on the urban edge (for example the Skye townhouses) — but the farm rules below are what actually govern most of East Delta.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum charge $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot defer it by vote. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" is void. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 are the only lawful age-restriction route (55+ only); exemptions cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (Regulation 7.01; spouse/child exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No East Delta building is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — no lettered schedules (the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, consolidated away in 2024); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)", and the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) apply to Delta's agricultural land. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta farm zoning:** in the A1 zone (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; the bylaw provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. A2 sets the same 8 ha subdivision minimum; A3 does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots — relevant to urban-edge lots, not to ALR farm parcels. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **ALR housing:** the ALC generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence if local bylaws allow it; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond that needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on new homes:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, for agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; the First-Time Home Buyers' Program exempts the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 and gone at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan limit $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day statutory rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits held in trust — relevant to a new townhome purchase on the urban edge such as Skye. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any condos in East Delta?** No verified apartment-condo building exists in East Delta on any source checked — count 0. The strata product on the urban edge is townhouses (Skye at Holly Park, 60B Street); the nearest verified apartment condos are in Ladner proper (see the Ladner Elementary, Delta Manor and Hawthorne local-notes files). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md)
- **Q: Is East Delta a real neighbourhood?** No — it is a Paragon sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood; the City plans Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta only, under OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can a farm parcel here have a second dwelling?** Under ALC rules, one principal residence plus, in some cases, one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); Delta's A1 zone allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those same limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What is the smallest new farm lot that can be created?** 8 ha in the A1 zone, subject to OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, s. 10.10.8). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Why can't ALR residences just be stratified into condos?** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the ALR, so stratification is not an ordinary route for ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve East Delta?** Catchments are address-based in SD37 and no sample-address result is published for this sub-area — check the exact address in the SD37 School Locator: UNVERIFIED for any specific farm address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What about wells and septic?** Search the well in the provincial GWELLS registry (wells built before the Water Sustainability Act may not appear); Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Is the farm land flood-protected?** Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding — confirm the requirement for a specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata townhome on the urban edge?** Long-term, yes in law — BC strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The old East Ladner Area Plan no longer exists as a schedule.** It was Schedule B.1 under the former OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), folded the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan, and the farm part falls under the municipal-wide agricultural policies. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Check the home plate, not just the house.** The A1 farm home plate maximum is 3,600 m², which caps the residential footprint including the farm house and accessory buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **There are no East Delta market figures to quote — from any legitimate source.** SnapStats covers only 14 communities and its Delta coverage is North Delta only; South Delta (Ladner / Tsawwassen / East Delta) is NOT covered, and North Delta numbers must never be presented as East Delta numbers. No SnapStats figures are cited in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md)
- **The Boundary Bay Dyke Trail runs on the south side of the farm area**, from 17A Avenue to Mud Bay in Boundary Bay Regional Park (Metro Vancouver). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- **Streamside rules reach farm ditches.** The City requires checks for flood protection requirements, the Riparian Areas Regulation and streamside rules before development near regulated water bodies — and all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
