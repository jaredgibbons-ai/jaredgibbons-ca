## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

A townhouse strata lot is governed by the same Strata Property Act framework as an apartment condo lot. Because this band crosses municipalities (Surrey and Langley City within the confirmed scope), city rules are stated per municipality and never merged.

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, the Form F, strata bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age restrictions, pet rules and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). In this file's roster, Huntsfield Green is published as a 55+ community — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. The feed's own West Newton card likewise describes a gated 55+ complex in listing text — a reminder that two townhomes at the same price can be open to very different buyer pools. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md)
- **City plans and zoning — Surrey stock (Clayton, West Newton):** the governing plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — never cite the predecessor OCP Bylaw 18020 as current. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. **SSMUH (Surrey):** 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **City plans and zoning — Langley City stock:** Langley City is a separate municipality from both Surrey and the Township of Langley; its zoning is Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, under which a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. Township of Langley rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020) do NOT govern a Langley City townhome — and no Township complex is rostered in this file, because no Township sub-area is in the confirmed feed scope. No Langley City tax due date is stated in this file: UNVERIFIED here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md) and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST on new homes:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. A qualifying new townhome anywhere in this under-$900K band sits under the Bill C-4 rebate's $1M full-rebate ceiling on price alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase under $900,000 meets only the 1% and 2% tiers. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so part of this band (to $835,000) can sit inside the full-exemption value range on price alone, and part (above $860,000) cannot, though the program's other conditions still have to be met either way. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a qualifying newly built townhome anywhere on this page is inside that threshold on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** the $900,000 line sits inside the insured-mortgage range: homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% down on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000, mortgage loan insurance is required when a buyer puts less than 20% down, and published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md)
