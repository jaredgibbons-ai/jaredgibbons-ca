## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The core distinction — park rules are NOT strata bylaws, and there is no Form B for pad rental:** the Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59, provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached) is a strata document. A pad-rental park has no strata corporation to issue one. The park buyer's equivalent diligence is the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement, the park rules, and the Manufactured Home Registry record — a different paper trail under a different Act. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata only — stated so the contrast is explicit):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither duty applies to a manufactured-home park — there is no CRF behind a park home, which is precisely why the park's own capital and rules documents matter. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void — strata only:** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 for strata property. A park's "rentals not allowed" rule is not a strata bylaw and is NOT voided by that change; it stands or falls under the MHPTA and the park's rules. Flagging a park's no-rentals line as "void" would be an error. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ rules — two different regimes:** for strata, Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children under SPA Regulation 7.01. A park's "55+ community" label (most first-page cards on this page carry one) is a park rule taken from listing copy only beyond the program-verified parks — and the SPA exemptions do not automatically apply inside a park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Pad rent increases (MHPTA):** a park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. The landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **NO pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file.** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, and this page itself states no pad-rent range in the copy read. The site-wide defect stands as program context: two conflicting unverified ranges exist on sibling pages — $600–$900/mo on cheap-mobile-homes-langley-under-500k vs $650–$950/mo on cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley — neither adopted here. Individual cards' pad-fee lines are individual listings' copy for individual parks, not Langley figures. Budget from the specific park's written agreement, never from a range. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cheap-mobile-homes-langley-under-500k.html and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s. 28):** assignment of the site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Registration and certification:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Municipal zoning and plans:** the manufactured-home-park zone name and rules in Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 are UNVERIFIED in this program — neither bylaw's park-zone section was opened; the City bylaw is cited by number only because its adoption date is conflicted in sources. Township small-scale multi-unit housing is governed by Bylaw 6020, which has NO 6-plex tier, and the Township secondary-suite cap is 120 m². (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the stock on this page is resale park homes. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. How PTT applies to a specific park purchase (no land transfers) is per-unit conveyancing advice — UNCONFIRMED per unit in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale product is on this page; where a developer-contract purchase does arise elsewhere, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Park-home resales are not REDMA presales — subject periods are contractual here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC published premium range 0.60%–4.50%. Park-home financing is lender-specific chattel-style lending in many cases — confirm financing with the lender and the park before subjects, never from the listing alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

There is no strata corporation, no strata fees and no Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)

- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Up to the Residential Tenancy Branch limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-15/langley-mobile-homes.md)
- **Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?** No. On assignment under s. 28 of the MHPTA, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent increase does not change; assignment requires the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given, or an order of the director) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent.
