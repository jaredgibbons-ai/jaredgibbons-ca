## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a 1987–1998 low-rise, the depreciation report's envelope, roof and parkade items are the levy story. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory line stating "rentals not allowed" (for example Tsawwassen Greene's) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Directly relevant to The Lexington and Tsawwassen Greene. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land designated "Agriculture (A)"; the OCP adds Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines. Zoning parcel-by-parcel under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (RS1–RS3 subdivision minimum 4,000 m²; RS4 610 m²; RS5 550/460 m²; RS6 390 m²; RS7 335 m²; RD1 836 m²; RD4 504 m²). SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **PTT at this band — the rule that bites hardest:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion — plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Neither exemption reaches this band: the First-Time Home Buyers' Program caps at $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption is fully eliminated at $1,150,000, so a buyer here budgets full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Home owner grant phase-out at this band:** the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 — full regular grant at or below it, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, for agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031 — the rebate phases out below this band's floor, so it does not offset GST on an over-$1.8M new purchase. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year — recorded for completeness; neither changes the tax math at this band. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% are a sub-20%-down, price-capped insured product — a purchase in this band is conventionally financed, so the table is background. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits held in trust. No presale project specific to this band was verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood and ALR — for the detached side of the band:** Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure); ALR status is parcel-specific through the ALC's maps. Both are address-specific checks for any bluff, foreshore or farm-edge listing in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

- **Q: How many Tsawwassen homes are for sale over $1.8M right now — and how many are condos?** UNCONFIRMED as a figure. - **Q: Which Tsawwassen condos are actually in the over-$1.8M class?** No public source checked supports that claim for any specific building — every entry in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED for the band. What the sources support is type: Tsawwassen's condo stock is low-rise (a 270-unit town-centre complex, a 2007 LEED-principles building, two verified 55+ buildings, and small 1990s buildings), with no tower district. A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is there extra property transfer tax in this band?** Yes, in two steps: the 3% tier applies to everything over $2,000,000, and a further 2% applies to the residential value over $3,000,000. Net in no exemption — neither the first-time program ($835,000 cap) nor the newly built exemption (gone at $1,150,000) reaches this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: What does the home owner grant do at this level?** Less than buyers expect: the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 of assessed value, the regular grant reduces by $5 per $1,000 over it, and it is $0 over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: Can I rent out a Tsawwassen condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a directory records; at a 55+ building the tenant must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at The Lexington?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases. Confirm the registered bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What should I check on a bluff or waterfront home in this band?** Which hazard DPA applies — SD2 English Bluff (escarpment) or SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands (2.9 m floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback) — and, on farm-edge lots, the parcel's ALR status on the ALC's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Why is the condo side of a $1.8M band so thin in Tsawwassen?** On the sourced facts: Tsawwassen's verified condo stock is entirely low-rise, concentrated on the 56 Street and 54A Street corridors, and no source documents any Tsawwassen condo building as upper-end or tower product — the band's feed labels are detached sub-areas. That is the honest shape of this band, not a gap this file fills by assertion. Sources: Section 2 and the research file (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-homes-over-1-8m.md).
- **Q: Do schools matter at this price point?** The same way as at any price: SD37 catchments follow the address through the School Locator (sample: 1299 56 St → Cliff Drive Elementary and South Delta Secondary), not the sub-area name or the price. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
