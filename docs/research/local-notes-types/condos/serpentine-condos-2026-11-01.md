## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **ALR frame for the feed's acreage ground (the rule book that actually governs most of this ground):** the visible cards are dominated by farm and acreage parcels. On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence, and subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited regulation exceptions — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; ALCA s. 21(1)), so ALR ground cannot be stratified without ALC permission. Which feed parcels are actually in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping; listing text is not an ALR determination. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/serpentine.md)
- **Form B (for strata stock generally — this ground has none verified):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to a freehold acreage parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata stock generally):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; and since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. Neither rule creates strata stock on this ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; on a farm parcel there is no strata bylaw at all. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children — strata law, with no application to this file's acreage ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only. Zoning is Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, parcel by parcel in COSMOS. There is no City plan, neighbourhood page or boundary for "Serpentine" — the name is a board label only. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/serpentine.md)
- **Surrey SSMUH (urban lots only — not ALR parcels):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Secondary suites (Surrey, non-ALR): only one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, not within a coach house or garden suite, capped at 40% of the principal unit. Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey, non-ALR): 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access; one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/serpentine.md)
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/serpentine.md)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required. On ALR parcels the ALC short-term accommodation limits in the first bullet govern instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals

- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with its inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $1,212,500 (the Cloverdale community detached median for September 2026, used as an arithmetic illustration only — it is NOT a Serpentine figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $20,250 (2% × $1,012,500) = **$22,250 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project on this feed's ground was confirmed on a developer's own site in the research; where any developer-contract purchase does arise in Surrey, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
