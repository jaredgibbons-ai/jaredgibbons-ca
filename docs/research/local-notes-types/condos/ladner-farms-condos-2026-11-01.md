## 2. BUILDINGS AND COMPLEXES

**This topic has zero apartment-condo stock, and the reason is structural — stated plainly at the top:** the research file confirms no strata farm complex on an official source and states that strata farm product is not a Delta pattern; farm parcels in the belt are overwhelmingly freehold. The legal frame explains why: under the Agricultural Land Commission's Information Bulletin 09 (issued October 18, 2022), depositing a strata plan in a Land Title Office causes a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under s. 3 of the ALR General Regulation (Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); an approving officer under the Strata Property Act is prohibited from approving a subdivision of ALR land unless so permitted, and a Registrar of Titles must not accept a plan that would cause subdivision of agricultural land unless permitted. In short: an ALR residence cannot simply be stratified — stratification is a subdivision, and subdivision is the Commission's call. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

### Apartment condos

- None. Verified named apartment-condo buildings on Ladner farm / ALR land: **0** — and the research file names no verified Ladner building in this topic's scope at all. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)

### What farm "attached" housing actually is here — not condos

- The dwellings the A1 zone permits on a farm lot are a single farm house (maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², or 5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate), one secondary suite accessory to a farm house (permitted only inside the principal residence under the ALC housing rules), one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot (maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate, ALC approval required for more than one), an additional farm house only with a Professional Agrologist's necessity report and written ALC approval, and migrant farm worker housing under set criteria. None of these is a strata lot; each is part of one freehold farm parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)

### Townhouse complexes — documented separately, NOT counted as apartment condos

- None on farm land, for the same structural reason: a townhouse strata is a strata plan, and a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision requiring Commission permission. No townhouse complex on Ladner farm land was confirmed on an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

### Tenure traps excluded

- Farm parcels held or marketed with corporation shares, undivided interest, leasehold, co-op or pad-rent arrangements are not freehold strata condos and are excluded wherever they appear; farm leases are a classification and income arrangement (see Section 3), not a housing tenure a condo buyer purchases. No such product is counted above. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **The ALR stratification rule (the rule this whole file turns on):** a strata plan causes a subdivision, so strata conversion or strata development of ALR land requires the Agricultural Land Commission's permission (application under ALCA s. 25) or compliance with s. 3 of the ALR General Regulation; without it, neither an approving officer nor the Registrar of Titles may approve or accept the plan. ALC Information Bulletin 09, October 18, 2022. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALC housing rules on ALR land:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **A1 subdivision floor:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **If a strata purchase ever is involved (off-ALR or Commission-permitted), the standard BC block applies:** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote; minimum CRF contribution of 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws void since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted); and only 55+ age bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-child exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged as required:** the OCP is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules, farm land designated "Agriculture (A)". City of Delta pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled; the A1 zone text cited in the research is Part 10, Agriculture Zones, as amended by Bylaw 8285, 2023. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024 — small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots, which does not override the ALR rules above on reserve land. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Farm classification (the tax position that substitutes for condo math on a farm):** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income thresholds at farm gate prices are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year, and leased land can qualify where the owner applies, a lease is in place, the leased land makes a reasonable contribution to the operation and the lessee-farmer meets the income requirements. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a condo on a Ladner farm?** No product of that kind is verified — farm parcels in the belt are overwhelmingly freehold, no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source, and the dwellings on a farm lot (farm house, in-house suite, accessory farm dwelling unit) are parts of one parcel, not separately titled strata lots you can buy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: Could a farm residence be stratified later so I could sell the suite separately?** Not without the Agricultural Land Commission: depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision of ALR land, which requires Commission permission after an application (or a permitted case under the ALR General Regulation) — and Delta's A1 zone separately sets an 8 ha minimum for any lot created by subdivision. Do not price a farm purchase on a stratification plan that has no ALC permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: How many homes can one Ladner farm parcel have?** Generally one residence per parcel in the ALR unless an additional residence qualifies: Delta's A1 zone permits one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot (90 m² maximum on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on larger lots) on the same farm home plate as the farm house, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and an additional farm house only with an agrologist's necessity report and written ALC approval. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: How big can the farm house be?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area (the A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum), with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction and a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m². (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: What keeps a farm's tax classification?** Meeting BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income for the operation's size ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha), with the application in by October 31 for the following tax year — including where production runs through a farm lease that meets the stated conditions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: Where are Ladner's actual condos, then?** In the village and residential sub-areas, off the farm belt — the buildings documented in the Ladner sub-area condo files and the Ladner condos-under-$800K band file. None sits on ALR farm land, which is exactly the structural point of this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: Can farm workers be housed on the property?** Yes, within the A1 migrant farm worker housing criteria: the lot must be in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, with written ALC approval, the employing farm at least 8 ha and the lot at least 4 ha, and new buildings must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: What water and flood checks replace the condo document check on a farm?** The parcel's irrigation source and any water licence position (non-domestic groundwater use requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act), the City's ditch and drainage position for the parcel (UNVERIFIED in the research for any specific parcel), the onsite sewage position, and the parcel-specific Flood Construction Level — none of which is stated for any parcel in the research. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Q: Are there farm market statistics to price against?** No — SnapStats publishes no farm category and no South Delta coverage; farm sale counts, price per acre and farm days-on-market are UNVERIFIED from the sources used, and pricing evidence is parcel-by-parcel comparable work, not a published series. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Strata" and "subdivision" are the same act on ALR land.** The ALC's Bulletin 09 states it directly: depositing any strata plan — including bare land and phased stratas — causes a subdivision, so the Commission's permission is required as with any other subdivision. That one sentence is why there is no Ladner farm condo market. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The accessory dwelling has two size ceilings, not one.** An accessory farm dwelling unit is capped at 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha — and an additional farm house is a different, heavier test (agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval), not just a bigger version of the same permission. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Equestrian use has a stall ceiling in the zoning.** The A1 zone's accessory uses include equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **The "Ladner farms" feed is wider than Ladner.** At reading it carried East Delta parcels on Ladner Trunk Road as well as Ladner Rural listings — a buyer searching "Ladner" gets the whole Ladner-side Delta farm belt, and the file states that openly rather than presenting East Delta results as village-adjacent. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Farm classification runs on an October 31 deadline.** BC Assessment farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year — a closing-date item a farm buyer can miss in the first year of ownership. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Home site severance is the one subdivision exception with a name.** The A1 zone's 8 ha subdivision minimum carries a home site severance exception under Agricultural Land Commission policy — the narrow route, not a general subdivision right. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
