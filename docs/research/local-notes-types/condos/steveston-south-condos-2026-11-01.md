The board's exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; in listing practice the north/south split is generally drawn at Steveston Highway, with the village core, Garry Point and the residential streets south of the highway in Steveston South. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. In Imperial Landing's mixed-use waterfront buildings, read the depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt); since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC — any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a Steveston South building is VOID for long-term rentals (both counted buildings here publish rentals allowed in any case); short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Only 55+ age bylaws are legal** (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions under Strata Property Regulation 7.01 for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and, from May 1, 2023, younger spouses/partners and defined children. Neither counted building is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 9000) guides the whole district. Zoning is Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500: houses in RS1/RS2 Single Detached zones (Section 8.1) with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 (a suite must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m²) and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under Bill 44; village-core strata sits in Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village and Steveston Commercial (CS3) zones. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-south.md)
- **Heritage:** the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront; properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Flood:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels and dike setbacks — for waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots, confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Richmond property taxes:** property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Section 2 buildings are resale stock. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12, CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not applicable to the resale buildings in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No active presale in Steveston South was confirmed on a developer source in the research pass. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax here:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax applies only inside the City of Vancouver — Steveston South is Richmond. (Parent file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/steveston-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)

- **Two "Landings" and a "Village" share these streets, and none of the names mean what a search assumes.** Imperial Landing (Onni, Bayview/English) is not Steveston Landing (3800 Bayview Street, a 1989 commercial/retail property) and neither is London Landing at the east edge; "The Village" (4280 Moncton Street) is not the "Steveston Village" building (12040 2nd Avenue, filed in the Steveston Village condos file). Contracts and strata documents run under the specific building name and address. https://dorsetrealty.com/properties/steveston-landing/ and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/imperial-landing-12333-english-ave
- **Garry Street is a seniors-housing street because of a temple land deal.** Wisteria Place exists because the Steveston Buddhist Temple retained ownership of its land and spearheaded the project through a separate non-profit, the Wisteria Senior Health and Housing Society, with Optima Living as operator — which is why Steveston South's newest seniors building is non-profit rental beside the temple, not a strata project a buyer can purchase into. Lions Park and River's End Co-op sit on the same street. https://optimaliving.ca/2021/10/wisteria-place-opens-its-doors-to-new-residents-and-the-richmond-community/
- **Imperial Landing's own dates do not agree with each other.** The building directory gives 2004 and 45 units for the English Avenue building; the 2011 Richmond Review history records building permits approved that year for six waterfront buildings with 52 homes expected on the same Bayview lands. A buyer comparing "Imperial Landing" units across phases is comparing different buildings and vintages under one name. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/imperial-landing-12333-english-ave and https://pagesarchive.blackpress.ca/BC/Processed/RIR/110805/RIRN110805_A03.pdf
- **The flood project has a navigation gate in it.** The City's Steveston Island Dike project proposes a dike around Steveston Island from London Farm to Garry Point Park with a navigation gate for Steveston Harbour — flood protection here has to keep a working fishing harbour open. https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-south.md)
- **Minato West's two official descriptions describe two different buildings.** BC Housing's seniors directory lists it as seniors housing; its operator, Metro Vancouver, describes a general affordable complex of 110 suites built in 1977. Verify the current tenancy mix with the operator before treating it as seniors housing. https://metrovancouver.org/services/housing/minato-west
- **Richmond has no SnapStats coverage in this program.** There is no community median to quote for Steveston South — any Richmond median in marketing copy comes from a source outside this file's verification, and this file substitutes none.
