# Delta Manor — Condos: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named buildings themselves. Delta Manor is a Paragon sub-area name for central Ladner, in the City of Delta — it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and it sits under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The working centre of Delta Manor is the grid around Delta Secondary School, 4615 51 Street, and Memorial Park, 5010 47 Avenue. https://de.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/list-of-parks/lists/all-parks/memorial-park
- Ladner Elementary, 5016 44 Ave., is the elementary catchment returned by the SD37 School Locator for the sample address 4600 53 Street; SD37 catchments are address-based, so any other address must be checked in the locator itself. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Hawthorne is to the north, Holly to the east, and Port Guichon and the Ladner village core to the west (working geography from the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/delta-manor-guide.md).
- Ladner Village shops sit west of Chilukthan Slough, with commercial development on the east side of the slough, per the OCP's Ladner Village Core material. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- Where the condo stock actually concentrates: one tight pocket — the 53 Street / 54A Street blocks just east of the village core, where the Sunningdale buildings, Westham Lane, and the Adlington Court and Rosewood Court townhouse complexes all sit within a few blocks of each other (see Section 2). A second transit node matters for condo buyers: Ladner Exchange, beside Delta Hospital at 5800 Mountain View Blvd, where TransLink route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Route 618 is the Ladner North loop from Ladner Exchange via Ladner Trunk Road, Central Avenue, Westminster Avenue and Crescent Drive; route 609 runs Ladner Exchange to South Delta Exchange via Arthur Drive and Ladner Trunk Road. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf
- Ladner Leisure Centre, 4600 Clarence Taylor Crescent, is the main Ladner recreation facility, and Ladner Pioneer Library is at 4683 51st Street. http://delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/ladner-leisure-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports are now mandatory on a cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection of repair and replacement costs and three CRF cash-flow models. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. Directory notes saying a Ladner building has "rentals allowed with restrictions" predate or ignore this change — the registered bylaws plus this law govern, not the directory line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. No Delta Manor building is verified as 55+ in Section 2 — if a listing claims an age restriction, it is only lawful if it is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 7600.** Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps that form part of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600; the existing Ladner condo buildings sit in site-specific or multi-family zones set when they were built, so a building's current zone is read off the City's zoning maps, not assumed. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it has no lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — this affects the detached streets around the condo pocket, not the existing strata buildings themselves. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale condos carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) for homes valued at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person withdrawn from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined for the same home. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment — the 0.6%–4.5% range buyers see quoted. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What do strata fees cover in the Sunningdale buildings?** It is set building by building in the strata budget attached to the Form B. One 4758 listing records amenities of caretaker, garbage, gas, hot water, management and snow removal, which is the kind of coverage to confirm line by line on the current Form B — fees and coverage change, and no fee figure is published for the complex as a whole. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3101049-4758-53-street-307-delta-bc-v4k-2z1/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out my condo here?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older directory entry says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, so read the registered bylaws before counting on nightly-rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Directory data for Sunningdale records pets allowed with restrictions at 4758 53 Street and pets allowed at 4768 53 Street, and a 4728 listing says "pet friendly x 1 (dog/cat)". Pet bylaws are strata-specific and can limit number, size and type — the registered bylaws attached to the Form B are the deciding document. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunningdale-4758-53-street
- **Q: Is parking underground, and is storage included?** Listings at Sunningdale record garage parking under the building plus guest parking, and a storage locker with the 4728 unit described. Parking and storage can be assigned, rented, or part of the strata lot — the Form B states the specific unit's allocation, which is what to rely on. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3083434-305-4728-53-street-delta-british-columbia-v4k2z1-519900/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: How healthy is the building financially — and could a big levy be coming?** Read the CRF balance and the depreciation report attached to the Form B. Stratas with five or more lots must now refresh that report every five years, and its three CRF funding models show whether fees are keeping up with the 30-year repair list or whether owners face catch-up levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: What does the strata's insurance cover, and what is the deductible?** Since 2023 the Form B must include a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage. The water-damage deductible in that summary is the number that matters most for a wood-frame low-rise: owners are routinely responsible for deductibles charged back after a leak from their unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is this area behind dikes, and does that affect insurance or building work?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. Separately, any property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building work. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend?** Catchments are address-based. For the sample address 4600 53 Street the SD37 locator returns Ladner Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); run the exact unit address in the locator before relying on it. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I get to Vancouver without a car?** Route 601 runs from South Delta to Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line, stopping at Ladner Exchange, via Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99; local routes 609 and 618 connect the Delta Manor streets to the Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **Q: The buildings are 1990s wood frame — what should I check?** The 4728 Sunningdale building is described in listings as rainscreened, which is the envelope question that matters most in this era of Ladner low-rises. Ask for the depreciation report's envelope and roof items, any engineering reports in the strata documents, and the minutes for past or planned envelope work — the Province's strata buying guidance is to review bylaws, rules, fees and special levies before removing subjects. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3083434-305-4728-53-street-delta-british-columbia-v4k2z1-519900/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion, major construction expected to begin in 2027, and early construction work already continuing. That crossing is the commute every Delta Manor condo buyer is pricing. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **Ladner Village is in an active revitalization program.** The City's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan, including the Chisholm Street Wharf project, is the named project set for the village core a short walk west of the 53 Street condo pocket. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **The 2024 OCP quietly removed the old area-plan structure.** Buyers reading older City documents will see references to a Ladner Area Plan as a schedule of the former OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), has no such schedule — future change in central Ladner is governed by the single Land Use Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH changed the streets around the condos in 2024.** Delta's June 24, 2024 SSMUH adoption (Bylaws 8394 and 8395) lets many detached lots around Delta Manor add suites, duplex units, coach houses and garden suites without a rezoning — which is why infill keeps appearing on the blocks between the condo pocket and the village. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Delta's 2026 tax calendar penalizes late payment twice.** The City's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 — relevant to completion-date adjustments on any condo closing that straddles those dates. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **There is no Ladner market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Ladner community section and no Delta Manor row — its only Delta section is North Delta — so any "Delta Manor median condo price" a buyer is quoted from that source does not exist (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/delta-manor-guide.md).
