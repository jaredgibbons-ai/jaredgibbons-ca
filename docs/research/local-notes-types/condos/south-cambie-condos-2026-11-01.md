## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Cambie is the City of Vancouver's Cambie Street corridor between roughly West 16th Avenue and the Fraser River (Marine Drive), including the Cambie Village stretch and the area around the King Edward and Oakridge–41st stations' northern edges; the board sub-area name is "South Cambie," with nearby areas Oakridge, Riley Park, Shaughnessy, Douglas Park, Marpole and Langara. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- Where the condo stock concentrates: at the King Edward Canada Line node (OPAL, 528 West King Edward Avenue) and on the Queen Elizabeth Park rise at Cambie and West 31st–35th (Parc Elise, Chelsea) — the side streets between stations are primarily houses under the R1-1 multiplex district. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- Transit: the Canada Line stations at King Edward Avenue and Oakridge–41st Avenue serve the area, with Langara–49th Avenue at the corridor's south end, as set out in the Cambie Corridor Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- Landmarks: Queen Elizabeth Park (52 hectares, 125 metres above sea level, the highest point in the City of Vancouver) anchors the east side; Douglas Park Community Centre is at 801 West 22nd Avenue; BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre share the campus at 4500 Oak Street on the west side; VanDusen Botanical Garden (5251 Oak Street, 22 hectares) sits at the northwest edge. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. At OPAL the Form B is only part of the package — the retirement-residence service and care agreements carry costs on top of strata fees, and their current amounts are UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Parc Elise's and 528 West King Edward's directory lines "rentals allowed" are consistent with current law — and any directory line for a Section 2 building describing long-term rentals as not allowed would be void to that extent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. OPAL is the area's published 55+ building ("One resident must be 55+") — that form of bylaw is legal; check the Form B for how it is registered. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan and zoning:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved by Council May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) guides growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River around the Canada Line stations; zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule created by By-law No. 13817 (up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on former single-family lots), and station-area parcels are typically site-specific districts. Citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%):** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Flood rules at the south end:** the streets running down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment, and the Vancouver Building By-law's Flood Plain Standards set a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum) for new construction in designated flood plains; lot-by-lot designation must be confirmed with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — South Cambie's counted stock is resale (2017–2021). The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any future Heather Lands developer sales, which will be leasehold product. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% to 4.50%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is OPAL a normal condo purchase?** Only partly: OPAL is a 130-home retirement community of which 44 homes are strata condos (the rest are rental and complex-care suites), built 2019, with a 55+ requirement ("One resident must be 55+") and retirement-residence services — read the service and care agreements, not just the Form B, and note the current service charge is UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/opal-438-west-king-edward-a-retirement-community-of-130-homes-in-three-buildings-of-a-6-storey-and-two-4-stories-by-opal-development
- **Q: Can I rent out a South Cambie condo long-term?** Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and the directories for Parc Elise and 528 West King Edward state rentals allowed. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration (3%) still applies if the unit sits empty instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Parc Elise — pets allowed with restrictions per the directory; 528 West King Edward — pets allowed per the directory, cats OK per a current listing; Chelsea — cats OK per a current listing. OPAL's pet rules: UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. Pet bylaws remain enforceable — read the registered bylaws. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/parc-elise-north-building-4488-cambie-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/528-west-king-edward-avenue
- **Q: Why do Parc Elise addresses show two different built years?** Public records split by address: the directory records the North Building at 4488 Cambie Street as built 2017, while listings at 4408 Cambie Street record 2019. The development totals 102 units (83 apartments, 19 townhomes) across two 6-storey buildings — match a unit to its own building's record before comparing. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/parc-elise-a-new-condo-development-on-vancouvers-cambie-corridor/
- **Q: What is the Heather Lands and will its homes be freehold?** No — the 8.5-hectare Heather Street site (MST Nations with Canada Lands Company) was rezoned March 31, 2026 for roughly 4,200 homes, predominantly discounted and market **leasehold** strata. Price and finance any future purchase there as leasehold from the first conversation. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Q: Which station is my building actually near?** OPAL and 528 West King Edward sit at the King Edward station node; Parc Elise and Chelsea sit between King Edward and Oakridge–41st, at the Queen Elizabeth Park rise. Name the station in any offer — "Cambie corridor" alone spans four stations. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Cambie address?** SD39 catchments are address-based. Edith Cavell Elementary (500 West 20th Avenue) is the corridor's named public elementary school, and Sir Winston Churchill Secondary (7055 Heather Street) is located inside the corridor — but location is not catchment: the locator run in the research file for 500 West 20th Avenue returned Edith Cavell Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary. Run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **OPAL spans a whole city block — and three tenures.** The 130-home community between Yukon and Cambie Streets mixes 44 strata condos, 56 rentals and 30 complex-care suites in three connected buildings; two homes with the same street address can have entirely different purchase documents. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/opal-438-west-king-edward-a-retirement-community-of-130-homes-in-three-buildings-of-a-6-storey-and-two-4-stories-by-opal-development
- **This sub-area has four tenure structures in a few blocks.** Side-street houses are freehold, corridor apartments are freehold strata, the corridor's large new rental buildings are purpose-built rental, and the Heather Lands' future product is predominantly leasehold strata on MST/CLC land — the research file's own summary of the split a buyer must be walked through. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **The corridor's north end is a hospital district.** BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre at 4500 Oak Street make the campus one of the largest health-care employment and patient-trip generators on the west side — a distinct tenant and buyer pool for King Edward–area buildings. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **Queen Elizabeth Park is the city's high point.** At 125 metres above sea level atop Little Mountain, the park explains the view premiums on the West 31st–35th Avenue buildings — Parc Elise was marketed as sitting at its entrance. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/parc-elise-a-new-condo-development-on-vancouvers-cambie-corridor/
- **The secondary school in the corridor is not the catchment for the corridor's west side.** The VSB locator returned Eric Hamber Secondary for 500 West 20th Avenue even though Sir Winston Churchill Secondary stands inside the corridor at Heather and West 54th — catchment follows the address, not the map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
- **The plan, not the base zoning, drives corridor land value.** The Cambie Corridor Plan (2011, Phase 3 in 2018) designations decide what a Cambie-fronting lot can become, and most of those densities still require a rezoning — price land on policy support, not as-built density. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-cambie.md)
