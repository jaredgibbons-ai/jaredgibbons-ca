## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. For Kitsilano's 1996–2000 wood-frame low-rises, envelope and roof cycles are the items to trace in the report. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — every building in Section 2 is above the threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Kits West's "rental-friendly" listing description is consistent with current law — but a directory or listing line anywhere in this file saying rentals are banned would be void for long-term tenancies regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No Section 2 building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per building; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. Kitsilano's RT areas sit under the RT-7 and RT-8 District Schedules — RT-8 states a minimum site area of 306 m² for a duplex, and the RT-7 / RT-8 intent is to encourage retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the historical character of the area; house lots elsewhere sit under R1-1 (minimum site area 306 m² for 3–4-unit multiple dwellings, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 or more units). A current Jade West listing records that building's zoning as RM-4. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md) and https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/101-3270-w-4th-ave-1930633
- **Area plan:** the Broadway Plan covers Vine Street to Clark Drive and 1st Avenue to 16th Avenue, including parts of Kitsilano, and is the framework tied to the Broadway Subway; a Kitsilano-specific community plan: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Coastal flood:** the City's Coastal Flood Risk Assessment covers a Point Grey–Kitsilano study area — the shoreline from Point Grey to Kitsilano Point faces the Strait of Georgia, is exposed to waves from the northwest, flood protection is limited and riprap west of Kitsilano Beach is discontinuous; the Province advises planning for 0.5 m of sea level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100. Relevant to the beach-side low-rises; parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor stock. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all Section 2 buildings are resale stock. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current market presale in Kitsilano was verified on a developer source in this pass, so REDMA is background only here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of condo stock does Kitsilano actually have?** Mostly 4-storey wood-frame low-rises from 1996–2013 on West 4th Avenue (Kits West, Star of Kitsilano, Avanti, Delano), one older freehold strata (Jade West, 1988), and one concrete mid-rise (Solo, 1998, 176 units). There are no condo towers in the verified stock. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Kitsilano condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, and Kits West is described in listings as rental-friendly. Short-term rentals are different: a strata bylaw can still restrict them, and Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to a unit left empty. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Kits West is described as pet-friendly in a current listing; a current Delano listing's pet field shows a number limit; listings at 3250 W 4th and Azul show cats OK. For Solo, Star of Kitsilano, Avanti and Jade West, pet rules are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — pet bylaws remain enforceable, so read the registered bylaws. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2958271-2858-w-4th-avenue-202-vancouver-bc-v6k-1r2/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2983-west-4th-avenue/106
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Kits West's build year?** The property-manager source says 2013; a current MLS® listing says 2014. Section 2 flags the conflict rather than resolving it — the strata plan (EPS1696) registration record is the definitive check. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md) and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2958271-2858-w-4th-avenue-202-vancouver-bc-v6k-1r2/brochure
- **Q: What should I check in a 1990s Kits wood-frame building?** The depreciation report: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years (no deferral vote since July 1, 2024) and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. At Delano, listings note a new roof in 2018 — ask what envelope, deck and plumbing items remain in the report, not just what is done. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve a Kitsilano condo?** SD39 catchments are address-based, with no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). Schools located in Kitsilano: Bayview (3505 West 7th Ave), Gordon (2268 Bayswater St), Hudson (1530 Maple St) and Tennyson (2650 Maple St) elementaries, and Kitsilano Secondary, 2706 Trafalgar Street — a real secondary school (the Ministry file's level field mislabels some secondary schools as "Elementary"; read the level from the school's name). The locator result for the research file's sample address (3505 West 7th Ave) was Bayview Elementary and Kitsilano Secondary. Run the building's exact address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station in Kitsilano?** No — Kitsilano is bus-served (routes 2, 4, 7, 9, 14, 44, 84 and the 99 B-Line). The change coming is at the south edge: the Broadway Subway is extending the Millennium Line along Broadway, and the Broadway Plan (1st to 16th Avenue, Vine to Clark) is the land-use framework tied to it — Broadway-edge buildings sit inside that plan area, beach-block buildings do not. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Q: Does the character zoning affect a condo purchase?** Indirectly: the RT-7 / RT-8 schedules state an intent to retain buildings consistent with the area's historical character, which shapes what redevelopment pressure exists around the low-rise stock — confirm the exact district schedule for the building's parcel before assuming any assembly value. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West 4th Avenue is the whole condo map.** Five of the six verified apartment buildings in this file (Kits West, Star of Kitsilano, Avanti, Jade West, Delano) sit on one street — a "Kitsilano condo" search is, in practice, a West 4th Avenue search, plus Solo on Marstrand Avenue at the north-west edge. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **A 129-unit supportive-housing project was approved inside Kitsilano.** Council approved the rezoning at 2086–2098 West 7th Avenue and 2091 West 8th Avenue — 129 studio homes — on July 27, 2022. Buyers on the West 7th / 8th blocks should know the project is approved, not rumoured. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **The Broadway Plan stops at 1st Avenue.** The Plan covers 1st to 16th Avenue from Vine Street to Clark Drive — so a condo south of 1st Avenue carries Broadway Plan / Subway expectations that a beach-block condo north of it does not, even though both are "Kitsilano." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Delano's roof is dated in its own listings.** Listing copy records a new roof in 2018 for the 1996 Delano building — a checkable capital fact that belongs in any fee comparison against Star of Kitsilano (also 1996) across the corridor. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r2967808-2983-w-4th-avenue-211-vancouver-bc-v6k-1r5/brochure
- **Solo is the corridor's only concrete mid-rise in this file.** At 7 storeys and 176 units (LMS3170, 1998, Intergulf) it is an outlier among the 50–84-unit wood-frame low-rises — its depreciation report and CRF comparison set is concrete-building issues (parkade, elevators), not the wood-frame envelope cycle of its West 4th neighbours. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
- **Kitsilano Secondary's name in a listing is not a catchment promise.** Four elementaries and the secondary sit inside the area, but SD39 catchments are address-based; the one verified locator run (3505 West 7th Ave) returned Bayview Elementary and Kitsilano Secondary for that address only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/kitsilano.md)
