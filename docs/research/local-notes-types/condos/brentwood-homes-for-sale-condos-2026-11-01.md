## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a sub-area with 360 attached listings this is a building-by-building exercise — the Bellwood Avenue files will read very differently from a 2024 Gilmore Place tower's. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral by vote; since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a Section 2 building is old bylaw text, not current law — and the rule does not apply at all to the purpose-built rental towers, which are landlord-owned, not strata. **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ bylaw is published for any Section 2 building in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per building; confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning and plans:** Burnaby's zoning is **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — the 1965 bylaw, No. 4742, is repealed and must not be cited as current. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; Brentwood sits under the Brentwood Town Centre framework within it. City-wide, Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024 (3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/brentwood-park-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes after the July due date, with a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (Oasis presales, Brentwood Block strata sales, any remaining developer sales at Slate/Gilmore Place), not resales. First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs): 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC) — worked example with inputs stated:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example — inputs: fair market value $686,000 (the Burnaby community Condos & Townhomes median for September 2026, an illustration only — it is NOT a Brentwood Park figure), fully residential, no exemption claimed: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $9,720 (2% × $486,000) = **$11,720 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to an Oasis or Brentwood Block purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Oasis and Brentwood Block developer contracts. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
