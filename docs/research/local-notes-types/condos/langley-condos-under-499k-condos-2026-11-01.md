## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At this price point the Form B, Form F, bylaws, rules, fees and special levies are where a condo is actually priced — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — no strata in this band may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw, whatever older directory or bylaw text says; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Parkway Estates and Parkway II in Section 2 are void in-entry for long-term rentals. Langley City's own short-term rental status was NOT VERIFIED on any City page in this program — flagged, not filled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/langley-condos-under-499k.md)
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Directly relevant in this band: three of the four verified 55+ condo buildings in Langley (Parkway Estates, Parkway II, Glenmont Park, Waterfront at Bedford Landing) are named in Section 2, and the feed itself surfaced a 55+ Langley City unit whose listing text states heat, hot water, electricity and gas are included in the maintenance fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Two municipalities, two rule sets:** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 governs the City buildings (City secondary suite cap: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, registered owner must reside on the lot; City SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024). Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs the Township buildings; Township SSMUH Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) has NO 6-plex tier — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m² — and the Township suite cap is 120 m². (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/langley-condos-under-499k.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — live in this band at Aldergrove Town Centre and in Willoughby's newest buildings. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere on this page is inside the full-exemption value range on price alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked examples (inputs stated):** (1) Down payment — a $499,000 purchase at the band ceiling: the price is at or under the $500,000 CMHC tier boundary, so the minimum down payment is a flat 5% = $24,950. (2) PTT — a $499,000 purchase with no exemption: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $299,000 = $5,980, total $7,980; a qualifying first-time buyer exempt on the first $500,000 pays $0 on those inputs. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any developer sale surfaced by this feed (Matteo, Walnut Park), which is never counted as resale band stock here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60%–4.50% of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
