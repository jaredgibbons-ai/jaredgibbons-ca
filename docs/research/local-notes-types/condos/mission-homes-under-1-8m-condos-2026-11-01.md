## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

- **Form B:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest stock (Glenkirk Court, 1975; Mission Manor, 1992; Briskham Manor, 1993) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote. **Minimum CRF contribution:** at least 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void** since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a building in Section 2 (Cherry Lane, Stave Lake Landing) is flagged here as void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and one current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals," the kind of restriction that CAN still be enforced. Preston's rentals rest on a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 — only 55+ is lawful; exemptions for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Briskham Manor and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the Official Community Plan is Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026, replacing and repealing Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added (live guides stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption are wrong); zoning is Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020. The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations, plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. SSMUH: zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-homes-under-1-8m.md) and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026
- **Mission property tax penalty is a flat 10%** on current taxes left unpaid after the July 2 due date — 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a "10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026" (Community Charter ss. 233–234; Mission has no 5% + 5% city scheme — the staged figures belong to the separate rural/FVRD regime under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, second date October 31). Mission taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **PTT in this band:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase under $1.8M is taxed entirely in the first two tiers (the 3% tier starts above the band's ceiling). First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — directly relevant to Montgomery and Silver & Cedar purchases. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on new condos only:** First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; GST New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resale units in Section 2 carry no GST; developer sales at Montgomery and Silver & Cedar do. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement, deposits in trust — the rule for any remaining developer sales at Montgomery and Silver & Cedar. Presales are never counted as resale band stock in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Flood frame:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie sit behind the Dewdney Dyke system; flood status is property-specific — check the address against City and FVRD floodplain mapping. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Mission homes are actually under $1.8 million?** That count cannot be stated from the permitted source: SnapStats publishes Mission totals with no price-band cut (231 detached and 122 attached active listings in September 2026), so no band count, band share or band median is claimed in this file. What can be said is that both community medians — $814,000 detached and $430,000 attached — sit well under the ceiling. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **Q: Which Mission buildings are in this band?** No public source states a building-level band membership — every building in Section 2 is UNCONFIRMED for the band. What is sourced is type and era: 1975–1997 wood-frame stock (Glenkirk Court, Mission Manor, Briskham Manor, Stave Lake Landing), a 2023 building (Preston) and two 2026 completions (Montgomery, Silver & Cedar). A specific unit's price is a listing fact at the time. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Cherry Lane a condo or a townhouse complex?** Townhouse strata. The building directory's own product variants for Cherry Lane (32165 Seventh Avenue, built 1989, 22 units) include "Townhouse," and the same is true of Luther Place, whose MLS records state dwelling type Townhouse outright. Listing feeds that file either under "condos" are using the word loosely. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/cherry-lane-32165-seventh-ave
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $1.8M?** Only at the bottom of the band: full exemption to a fair market value of $835,000 (first $500,000 exempt), partial to $860,000, none above — if all personal and property conditions are met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Can I rent out a Mission condo?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022, which is why the "rentals not allowed" directory lines for Cherry Lane and Stave Lake Landing are outdated law. Short-term rentals are different: a bylaw can still restrict them, and one current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals." https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live at Briskham Manor or Luther Place?** On the provincial rules, yes: a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident is exempt from a 55+ bylaw, as are live-in caregivers and defined child cases (May 1, 2023 expansion). Briskham Manor's pet rules are separately published: cats and dogs OK, two-pet limit, roughly 24–25 lb size limit per listings. Confirm the registered bylaws on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85456/FullPublic.pdf?i=1506184905
- **Q: Do the new Redekop Faye buildings charge GST?** Yes on developer sales — Montgomery and Silver & Cedar are new construction, so 5% GST applies, with the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (up to $50,000 back on homes to $1 million) and the BC Newly Built Home Exemption (PTT fully exempt to $1.1 million) both in play for qualifying buyers. Montgomery listings state this explicitly ("price excludes GST. FTHB Rebate available"). Preston (built 2023) is now resale stock, so a purchase there is generally GST-exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: What is the tax penalty if I miss Mission's July 2 deadline?** A flat 10% penalty on any current balance outstanding on July 3, 2026 — not a staged 5% + 5%. Budget for the diking levy as an additional line on the Mission tax bill. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: How does the West Coast Express work from Mission?** Mission City Station (33200 North Railway Avenue) is the line's eastern terminus and service is weekday rush-hours only — a downtown-Vancouver commute is realistic on the train's schedule, not outside it. BC Transit Route 31 covers Mission–Abbotsford trips. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/mission-homes-under-1-8m.md)
- **Q: How do I judge levy risk in the 1970s–1990s buildings?** Through the depreciation report (mandatory refresh every five years since July 1, 2024) and the CRF balance on the Form B — envelope, roof and parkade items are the read for this era of wood-frame stock, and Glenkirk Court (1975) is the city's oldest named condo building in this file. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
