## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT CONDOS

*No condo building is verified in this sub-area, so this section states the BC strata framework that would govern any strata unit a buyer finds marketed under the West Meadows name (most likely the Fraser Way townhome pocket or a mis-filed Harris Road listing), plus the Pitt Meadows rules that govern the sub-area itself.*

- **Form B and strata records:** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing the Form B for the most recent depreciation report, the strata's financial statements, the CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with no deferral vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — a small Fraser Way townhome strata could fall on either side of that line, so check the lot count. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Short-term rentals are prohibited in Pitt Meadows.** A City staff report states: "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — the use itself is banned city-wide, so an STR question never turns on the strata's bylaws here. Pitt Meadows also appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list for short-term rentals. (City-wide Pitt Meadows condo notes, batch C2: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 allow only a 55-or-older residency bylaw; exemptions cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No West Meadows strata is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, under which agriculture is the dominant land use in the city; zoning is Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011. In this sub-area the main zones are A-1 General Agriculture (one farm house per lot, maximum gross floor area 500 sq m; minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha) and A-2 Large Lot Agricultural (subdivision minimum 16 ha), with RS Large Lot Residential (minimum lot area 0.2 ha) and R-3/R-4/R-6 Urban Residential (up to 3, 4 or 6 units per lot; R-6's sixth unit conditional on an adaptable unit) in the Fraser Way / Wildwood Crescent pocket. Small-scale multi-unit housing under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings (2–6 units) where it applies — the only route by which small strata product could appear here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Floodplain is the city's defining land rule:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — this current City figure governs; an older City figure of approximately 86% from the superseded OCP era is not the current number. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; A-1 zoning requires residential buildings sited 15.0 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **ALR:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve; the large farm parcels in this sub-area are zoned A-1/A-2 under rules consistent with the Agricultural Land Commission Act. On ALR land the Agricultural Land Commission allows one additional detached residence without an application where one residence already exists when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The bylaw carries a separate drainage levy — for 2026 the residential (Class 1) drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside a Farm class (Class 9) drainage rate of $1.8524 per $1,000 and a Farm general rate of $48.6824 per $1,000, so the property's BC Assessment class changes the bill materially. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **GST on new condos:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4) rebates 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; CRA's Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 states Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026 (some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs). The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000, none at $860,000+. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase and https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Presales (REDMA):** the Real Estate Development Marketing Act s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. No current residential presale project in or beside the sub-area was verified in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md) and https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings vehicles:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer from RRSPs; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime, $8,000 per year. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **Mortgage default insurance (CMHC):** published premiums run 0.60% (LTV up to 65%) to 4.00% (90.01%–95% LTV), 4.50% at the top band with a non-traditional down payment, plus 0.20% where the over-25-year amortization surcharge applies. Separately, floodplain and ALR status can affect a lender's view of a specific parcel — no source checked publishes a West Meadows property-by-property position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: How much of this area is floodplain, and what protects it?** Over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser River and Pitt River floodplains per the City's 2020 Business Plan — use that figure, not the older approximately 86% figure still circulating from the superseded OCP era. Protection is a 60 km dike system plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes, funded in part by the separate drainage levy on the tax bill. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Q: Does the airport affect homes here?** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is inside this sub-area at 1028-18799 Airport Way, with three paved runways and a water runway, owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society; the City maintains a page for safety and noise concerns. - **Q: What can actually be built or bought on the farm parcels?** Under A-1 zoning: one farm house per lot (maximum 500 sq m gross floor area), one additional farm house for farm operations over 8 ha and under 40 ha (two if 40 ha or more), minimum subdivision area 8 ha (16 ha under A-2), and homes sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and ditches. For 2026 the residential drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value; farm-class properties pay $1.8524 per $1,000 drainage plus a $48.6824 per $1,000 general rate, so confirming the property's assessment class before quoting carrying costs matters more here than in most suburbs. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number changed between City documents.** The City's 2020 Business Plan says over 95% of Pitt Meadows is floodplain; an older release from the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 era stated approximately 86%. The current figure is "over 95%" — quote it and cite the Business Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **West Meadows is an airport and farm sub-area wearing a residential name.** Its verified contents are YPK airport (three paved runways and a water runway), A-1/A-2 farm parcels, dike trails and one small house pocket — the "Meadows" name matches its Central/Mid/South siblings, but the housing form does not. A buyer searching West Meadows for condos is searching the wrong sub-area; the condo corridor starts across Harris Road. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **78% of the city is farmland, and this sub-area is where much of it sits.** The 2020 Business Plan puts 78% of Pitt Meadows in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and this sub-area's A-1 subdivision minimum is 8 ha (A-2: 16 ha) — which is why there is no condo supply here and none is planned in the OCP's agriculture-dominant framework. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Two road-and-rail projects frame the sub-area's edges.** The Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project includes a four-lane Harris Road underpass under the CPKC rail line at the area's east edge, and TransLink and the City of Maple Ridge have an agreement to design the widening of Golden Ears Way from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, on the corridor north of the area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **The dike trail starts at the condo corridor's doorstep.** Harris Landing, at Harris Road and Fraser Way, is the start of the Pitt River Greenway — about 10.2 km west along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes for walking, cycling and bird watching — so the trail amenity marketed to Harris Road condo buyers runs through this sub-area, not theirs. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
- **Farm-class taxes are an order of magnitude different.** Under Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 the Farm class general rate is $48.6824 per $1,000 against the residential general structure — a carrying-cost fact that surprises acreage buyers who budget from residential Pitt Meadows figures. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/west-meadows-pitt-meadows.md)
