## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 9 counted strata complexes, and both presales once strata-registered):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every counted Burke Mountain strata is well over five lots on the sources checked (Reef's count is UNCONFIRMED but its strata plan EPS1759 townhouse form is a multi-lot complex; confirm the lot count on its Form B). Stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in this file carries a published "rentals not allowed" line — the published lines (Forester, Reef, Secret Ridge: rentals allowed) are consistent with current law; any directory line a buyer meets stating the opposite is flagged VOID for long-term rentals under this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Burke Mountain complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (the Roxton Avenue cluster):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly. The Roxton listings make the same point in marketing language: "NO strata! YOU OWN YOUR LOT!" https://www.zolo.ca/coquitlam-real-estate/3416-roxton-avenue/108
- **City zoning and plan — standing corrections applied:** Zoning Bylaw **No. 3000, 1996** governs — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² and 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² and 12.5 m width (R-2). The **Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — was adopted April 27, 2026 and supersedes OCP No. 3479**; the Burke Mountain neighbourhood plans (Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek, Partington Creek) and the Northwest Burke Vision sit under that framework. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse check — doubled reach on the mountain:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within **50 metres** of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam (30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam); Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks and s. 523 covers riparian areas. Several counted complexes sit between named creeks (Kentwell between Burke Mountain Creek and Smiling Creek; Secret Ridge between two greenbelt creeks; Rocklin on the Creek on a protected creek) — a capital-planning and lot-level check, confirmed per property. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Coquitlam — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (the Rocklin and Versant presales, and any developer-sale unit at a phased complex), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for Rocklin and Versant. - **Two projects share the Rocklin name on the same street pair.** Rocklin on the Creek (Woodbridge, 2021, 1220 Rocklin Street / 1225 Mitchell Street) and Rocklin (StreetSide, completing 2026, 1295 Rocklin Street) are unrelated projects whose addresses interleave with the Forester and Ballantree Mitchell Street addresses — a Form B or disclosure statement ordered against the wrong Rocklin is this mountain's easiest paperwork error. Sources: Section 2 entries.
