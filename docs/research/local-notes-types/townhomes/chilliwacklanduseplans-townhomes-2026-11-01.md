## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (the BC standard frame for any Chilliwack strata townhome — counted in sub-area files, not here).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — long-term rentals are allowed anywhere in BC; any directory "rentals not allowed" line surfacing for a Chilliwack strata is flagged void in-entry wherever met. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a bylaw set below 55 does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City of Chilliwack rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.

**City of Chilliwack plans, zoning, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **OCP:** Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — "Chilliwack 2050," presenting a long-term vision and growth strategy for residential (urban, rural and hillside), commercial, industrial and institutional uses and protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve. The live page's stated adoption date (December 16, 2025) is UNVERIFIED — the City's OCP page read states no adoption date, and the date is recorded as the page's own statement only (research file §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html).
- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets what can be built as-of-right today — the OCP-vs-zoning distinction the live page is built on. Its §6 farm zones and the Schedule D-4 Farm Home Plate development permit area are the layers a farm parcel answers to where farm files meet them (research file §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp).
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, **adopted June 18, 2024** — established from the amendment record in the consolidated Bylaw 5000 (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **Suites and accessory dwellings (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling (s. 2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot (s. 4.06(1)); garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area; ADU parking is set at s. 5.04 (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **ALR frame:** one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). **BC Assessment farm thresholds (full three-tier form):** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha (>4 ha), $10,000 (<0.8 ha), applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR wherever met (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Property taxes (City ground):** notices are mailed at the end of May; taxes are due the first working day after July 1, with a **5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026** (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).
- **Floodplain:** the live page cites "Floodplain Regulation Bylaw No. 4519" and Flood Construction Level mechanics — **UNVERIFIED as stated**: no City floodplain bylaw or FCL table was opened in the research pass. Confirm the current bylaw and the parcel's FCL with the City (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html).
- **Per-parcel check route:** the City's OCP page itself points readers to the City of Chilliwack Webmap for a parcel's zoning, OCP designation, DPA overlays and floodplain layers — per-parcel results are UNVERIFIED for any individual property in this file (research file §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug carries March 13 and flags the March 12 secondary date as superseded): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.

- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the Chilliwack plans page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — Chilliwack is outside Vancouver, and the 3% tax is Vancouver only.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: What is Chilliwack's current Official Community Plan?**
A: The Chilliwack 2050 OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. The live page's stated adoption date (December 16, 2025) is UNVERIFIED on the City page read (§3; https://chilliwack.com/ocp).

**Q: Which neighbourhood plans does the 2050 OCP actually contain?**
A: Seven, all in Schedule B: B1 Agricultural Area, B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, B3 Downtown, B4 Eastern Hillsides, B5 Sardis, B6 South Vedder, B7 Yarrow. Schedule C is the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule — the live page's "Sardis (Schedule D)" and "Eastern Hillsides (Schedule C)" labels do not match the current OCP (§1, §3).

**Q: Is there a Rosedale neighbourhood plan in the City's OCP?**
A: No — there is no Rosedale plan in Schedule B as the City lists it, and Rosedale sits in FVRD Electoral Area D, not the City of Chilliwack (§1; research file §4).

**Q: How does SSMUH apply in Chilliwack?**
A: Through Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. Within that frame a secondary suite caps at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, and garden suites cap at 100 m² (§3).

**Q: What rules govern a townhouse strata purchase in Chilliwack?**
A: The BC standard block in §3 — Form B diligence, 5-year depreciation reports for stratas with 5+ lots (sub-5-lot stratas sit under the threshold), the 10% CRF minimum, rental-restriction bylaws void since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid. A fee-simple townhome has no strata at all — no Form B, no strata fees.

**Q: Can ALR parcels in Chilliwack be subdivided for housing?**
A: Not through the City alone: subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require ALC approval, and the residence frame (500 m² principal / suite inside only / 90 m² or 186 m² additional) governs. The page's related claim that about 67% of Chilliwack is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source opened (§3; research file §4).

**Q: How do I check the OCP designation and zoning on a specific Chilliwack address?**
A: On the City of Chilliwack Webmap — the City's own OCP page points readers there — checking zoning, OCP land use, Schedule D permit areas and floodplain layers. Per-parcel results are not stated in this file (§3; https://chilliwack.com/ocp).

**Q: When are City of Chilliwack property taxes penalized?**
A: A 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026 — City ground rates, stated as the City publishes them (§3; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).

**Q: Are there market statistics for the plan areas?**
A: None stated — Chilliwack has NO SnapStats coverage and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only, with no plan-area table. Any figure would require a Paragon pull (§1; research file §2).

**Q: The page names District 1881 downtown — is that a verified townhouse or condo count?**
A: No — District 1881 is the live page's own reference in its Downtown plan line, not a developer-source confirmation in this file's research, and this plans file counts no buildings at all (§2.3).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The page's schedule labels are one OCP out of date.** Its Sardis and Eastern Hillsides labels look like previous-OCP numbering carried into 2050 OCP copy — in the current OCP they are B5 and B4, and the letters the page uses belong to the heritage schedule (C) and the permit-area schedule (D). Do not copy the page's labels into an offer or a listing (§3; research file §§1, 4).
- **"DPA2" is two different permit areas wearing one name.** The City's Schedule D separates D-2 Hillside Environment from D-5 Hillside Development — a hillside parcel can sit under either or both, with different report requirements, and the page's single "DPA2 Hillside & Upland" label cannot tell a buyer which (§1, §3; https://chilliwack.com/ocp).
- **Farm ground has its own permit layer most buyers never see.** Schedule D-4 is the Farm Home Plate development permit area, sitting alongside Zoning Bylaw 5000's §6 farm zones — a farm-home build answers to both layers, plus the ALC frame where the parcel is in the ALR (§1, §3).
- **Rosedale does not belong on a City of Chilliwack page's plan list.** It appears on the live page as a village plan, but it sits in FVRD Electoral Area D and has no plan in the City's Schedule B — a jurisdictional error, not a naming variant (§1; research file §4).
- **This hub is figure-free by design, not by omission.** With no SnapStats coverage and CADREB publishing board-wide figures only, any "Chilliwack plan-area median" a page offered would be invented — the honest content of a plans page is the plan stack itself (§1; research file §2).
