## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no townhouse stock here, so this section is (a) the fee-simple position that describes the stock that actually exists, (b) the flood and dike rules that are this sub-area's real diligence layer for any housing form, and (c) the standard BC strata and tax rules a buyer needs for River West or for the townhouse complexes one sub-area east.

- **Fee-simple is the tenure that exists here — plainly.** A detached house in Port Guichon is fee-simple: NO strata corporation, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title (including the flood covenants below), zoning and municipal flood-construction requirements. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Flood and dike rules are the local overlay.** Port Guichon is low land on the Fraser River side of Ladner behind the City's dike system; Delta operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, and directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Flood covenants on title.** In Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street, the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — the pattern a buyer on river-side lots here should search title for, on a house purchase exactly as on a strata one. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Streamside rules.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on adding a suite, coach house or any ground-oriented unit on a Port Guichon lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (RS1, RS5, RS6 and RS7 confirmed in west Ladner; A1 Agriculture toward the west edge, with an 8-hectare minimum lot area for parcels created by subdivision); an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither number is asserted as settled here; the zone on any parcel is read off the City's zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — it contains NO lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (former area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them — the only route to ground-oriented added units here.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. SSMUH product is fee-simple in form unless separately strata-registered — do not market it as a townhouse complex. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Strata rules (for River West, and for townhouses east in Ladner).** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59): discloses monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days of request. Since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it; since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata).** Every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — a directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry. Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 only a 55+ age bylaw is valid, with exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. No Port Guichon building is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (new construction only).** Resale homes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax and buyer programs.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; the first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000, and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs, the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/ and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** No presale project inside Port Guichon is verified, so REDMA is background only: where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it, with deposits held in trust. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Port Guichon in Tsawwassen?** No — it is in Ladner, in west Ladner around River Road West and 46A Street; Ladner and Tsawwassen are separate Delta communities that together make up South Delta. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Are there any townhomes in Port Guichon at all?** None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes. The research file, the condos run and the 55+ run each checked and found the same: the stock is freehold detached housing plus one apartment building (River West). Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/port-guichon-guide.md
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual townhouse stock?** East, in the neighbouring Ladner sub-areas: Adlington Court and Rosewood Court on 54A Street in Delta Manor, and Manor House, Faireharbour, Apex Homes and the fee-simple 48 Landing row houses in the Ladner Elementary sub-area — see those files in this series. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: Is River West a townhouse complex? It looks low-rise and ground-oriented.** No — it is a low-rise apartment/condo building: freehold strata plan NWS3341, 85 homes per the building directory, wood frame, with underground/under-building garage parking. Low-rise is not the same as townhouse form. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/202-4753-w-river-rd-52485
- **Q: What about the float homes further west on River Road?** Canoe Pass Village is a floating-home strata in the Ladner Rural sub-area — a different form and a different sub-area, excluded from this file on both grounds. https://www.floatinghomesbc.ca/links-and-contacts
- **Q: Is the area behind a dike, and can a flood covenant sit on my title?** Yes to the dike system — this is low Fraser River-side land protected by Delta's flood protection system — and covenants are a real Ladner pattern: the City has required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and rights-of-way for future dike raising to 5.5 m in Ladner approvals, so search title before subjects come off, on any purchase here. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Port Guichon house instead of finding a townhouse?** Possibly: a secondary suite is an accessory use in the RS1 zone, and Delta's SSMUH amendments (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, June 24, 2024) add duplex, coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — but any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is also in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, and A1 Agriculture zoning at the west edge carries an 8-hectare subdivision minimum. Confirm the parcel's zone and permit areas with the City first. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Q: If I buy at River West instead, what do I read before subject removal?** The Form B: monthly fees, amounts owing, any approved special levy still to be paid, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the budget and the most recent depreciation report attached — a listing states a building enclosure rehabilitation was completed in 1999, which is exactly the kind of item to trace through that report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3058563-4753-w-river-road-304-ladner-port-guichon-homes-for-sale-760000/brochure
- **Q: Which schools and buses serve the area?** SD37 catchments are address-based; the sample address 4381 46A St maps to Port Guichon Elementary and Delta Secondary. For buses, route 618 (Ladner South loop) calls at 46A Street at River Road, and Ladner Exchange is the hub for the 601 to Bridgeport Station. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
- **Q: What is the hospital and village access like?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, is Fraser Health's Delta community hospital with 24/7 emergency care, located beside Ladner Exchange; Ladner Village shops sit west of Chilukthan Slough per the OCP's Ladner Village Core material, a short distance east of Port Guichon. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The village next door is mid-revitalization, and it is City-led:** Delta's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan names the Chisholm Street Wharf project in Ladner Village — the amenity story for a Port Guichon buyer is the village core to the east, not a mall. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **The dike is planned to go higher, and the paperwork can already be on title:** Ladner approvals have required rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — a buyer who skips the title search misses the one local fact most likely to affect a river-side lot's future use. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **This was a ferry landing before it was a neighbourhood:** the Victoria Terminal Railway and Ferry Company Station stands in the historic Port Guichon neighbourhood on the Canadian Register of Historic Places, and the school on 46A Street began as Fairview Elementary before being renamed for the Guichon family. https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=6344 and http://pg.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **A grocery store inside Port Guichon itself: UNVERIFIED** — no source names one; the shopping is Ladner Village and the Ladner Exchange area to the east. Source: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/port-guichon-guide.md
- **SSMUH quietly changed the side streets:** since the June 24, 2024 adoption of Bylaws 8394 and 8395, single-detached and duplex lots here can add secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites without rezoning — the infill a Port Guichon buyer will see appearing around the older detached stock, in fee-simple form. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
