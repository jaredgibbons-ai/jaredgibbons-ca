## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata in §2.2 — a band buyer who narrows to a townhouse runs this diligence in the complex's count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The condos sibling flags directory "rentals not allowed" lines as void in-entry for Abbotsford buildings in its roster (Regency Park, Lakewood Gardens, Brookhollow Estates and College Park at 33728 King Road) — the same voiding governs any townhouse directory line a band buyer meets (condos sibling, §3).
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Abbotsford's verified 55+ position is the §2.3 cross-reference — 26 strata + 3 parks + 2 life leases — stated there, never re-counted.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Most of this band's actual stock is fee-simple detached housing, where the equivalent diligence is title, permits and City records rather than strata documents.
