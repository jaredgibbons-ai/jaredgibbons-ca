# West Chilliwack — Townhomes: Local Notes (City of Chilliwack)

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**ZERO VERIFIED TOWNHOUSE STOCK: no townhouse complex — strata or fee-simple — is verified in West Chilliwack on any source checked, in the research file, or in either sibling run. Section 2 states this at the top, names what was checked, and deals with the one complex (Cottonwood Village) that listing form fields try to file as townhouse stock and that is counted NOWHERE.**

Geography note: the City of Chilliwack publishes no official boundary for the Paragon name "West Chilliwack." The research file treats it as the western rural and agricultural part of the city, planned through the Agricultural Area Plan (Schedule B1 of the OCP), with the Evans Road corridor as its named spine. The corridor's two large residential addresses sit on Evans Road — and current MLS area labels for those same addresses read "Sardis West Vedder," which shows how loosely the "West Chilliwack" label sits on this ground. Follow the address, not the label. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/west-chilliwack-guide.md, §§1, 4–5)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the rules that would govern a townhouse purchase here if stock existed or is built, and the municipal/ALR rules that explain why the stock does not exist in this sub-area.

- **If it is strata: Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance, and must be provided within 7 days of a request. That is the diligence track for Creekside Estates (the sub-area's one strata building) and for any townhouse strata ever built here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata).** Stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, with 30-year projections and three CRF cash-flow models, non-deferrable by vote; and at least 10% of the operating fund must be contributed to the CRF at each AGM budget since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata).** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, so any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Chilliwack strata is void for long-term rentals. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives (strata).** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. Cottonwood Village's 55+ rule is NOT a strata bylaw — it is a manufactured-home park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and park rules, which is one more reason it is not townhouse stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/161_2000_pit
- **If it is fee-simple: no strata at all.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (zoning, OCP, floodplain bylaw) plus any registered covenants govern instead. West Chilliwack's rural homes are fee-simple in exactly this sense, but they are detached farm and acreage homes, not townhouse complexes (Section 2).
- **Chilliwack zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** The AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone that frames most of this sub-area sets a minimum lot area of 7.5 ha for land excluded/exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance, with a 25 m minimum lot width — which is why townhouse development is not the form of this area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Chilliwack OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500.** The current city-wide plan is Bylaw No. 5500 ("Chilliwack 2050"); the Agricultural Area Plan is its Schedule B1 and covers this sub-area, and the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan is Schedule B5. Any live page stating "Schedule D" or citing OCP Bylaw 5024 for this ground is stale — flag it as a Jerry sweep item; the current citations are Bylaw 5500, Schedule B1 (Agricultural) and Schedule B5 (Sardis). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=6230
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024)** brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into Chilliwack's zoning. The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not West Chilliwack, but it shows Chilliwack's SSMUH rollout is not uniform city-wide, and ALR land is in any case governed by the ALR rules below, not by SSMUH density. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **ALR — stratification is barred and farm thresholds are three-tiered.** Parcel-level ALR status is per address in this sub-area. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — a strata townhouse complex cannot be created out of ALR farm residences. In the AL zone, subdivision inside the ALR needs Agricultural Land Commission approval, and the zone's minimums (7.5 ha, or 0.2 ha for an approved homesite severance) decide what is even possible. Farm-class qualification is three-tiered ($2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Floodplain rules.** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal, and the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder systems; Chilliwack also levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Chilliwack tax dates.** Property taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applied to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST on new construction.** 5% GST on new homes only (resales are not GST-taxable). FTHB GST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; the first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales — REDMA.** No presale project in this sub-area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. Where a presale does exist elsewhere in Chilliwack, REDMA requires a disclosure statement, s. 21 gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it to rescind, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://homehappy.ca/bc-first-time-buyer-programs/
- **CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** of the mortgage amount on the published homeowner schedule; required under 20% down on homes $1.5M or less. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Board and stats.** Chilliwack is CADREB territory, not FVREB — the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report has no Chilliwack section, so no median is quoted in this file, and a page claiming an FVREB feed for this sub-area would be wrong (no such claim is documented in this area's research file, so no defect is recorded — stated so the absence is on the record). Yale Road–corridor buildings follow their current MLS sub-area labels, which is the same lesson as the Evans Road labels in Section 2 (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/west-chilliwack-guide.md, §2).

- **Q: Can a farm parcel here be turned into townhouses?** Not on the verified rules: the AL zone's minimum lot areas (7.5 ha; 0.2 ha only for an ALC-approved homesite severance) and ALR approval requirements govern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified at all (Strata Property Act, s. 239). - **Q: What flood checks matter on a west-side lot?** Measure the bylaw setbacks — 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River, 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal — confirm the parcel's floodplain status with the City, and budget for the drainage and dyking levies on top of municipal tax rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Q: Which schools serve Evans Road?** SD33 catchments are address-based. The locator result for the sample address 7600 Evans Road (Evans Elementary's own address) was Evans Elementary → Vedder Middle → Sardis Secondary, with the locator note that Vedder Middle students attend Sardis Secondary. - **Q: Is there a bus on Evans Road?** Yes — route 52 Evans runs between Cottonwood Mall and Downtown Chilliwack via Evans Rd and Yale Rd, with timing points at Evans Rd at Yale Rd and Hodgins Ave at Ashwell Rd. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=52
