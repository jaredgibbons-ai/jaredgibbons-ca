## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Kingz Court — a 2026 complex with no published strata plan number in the sources read — the Form B and the disclosure history are the diligence; directory and listing lines are secondary-source data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Kingz Court's published 22 units put it over it). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line for a South Arm strata stating rentals are restricted or not allowed is VOID for long-term rentals on this rule — none is carried in §2 for the counted complex (its published restrictions are UNCONFIRMED), so no in-entry flag arises. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No source states a 55+ bylaw for Kingz Court or any South Arm strata — the 55-plus program verifies ZERO 55+ ownership complexes in this district; the Covenant Court 55+ statement is listing-stated only, with registered wording UNCONFIRMED, and that complex is counted nowhere (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/south-arm-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — City of Richmond rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. None was verified in South Arm (§2); do not describe Kingz Court that way — listings charge strata fees there.
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 9000 — no separate South Arm area plan is confirmed, so the district sits in the OCP 9000 framework rather than a Schedule 2 area plan of its own. Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use (Section 5.4: minimum 33.0 m², maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
- **Flood rules:** the district is on Lulu Island in the Fraser River floodplain along the South Arm channel, behind the City perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — confirm both for any Williams Road purchase before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-arm.md)
