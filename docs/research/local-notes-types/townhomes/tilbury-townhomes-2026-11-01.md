## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

With zero townhouse stock, this section records why the zero is structural (the plan and zoning layer), the strata rules that still govern the industrial strata a buyer will meet here, and the buyer frame for anyone redirected to a residential Delta sub-area.

- **Why there is no townhouse stock — the plan layer:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), covers Tilbury as industrial land — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules (the former area plans sat under the repealed Bylaw No. 3950, and Tilbury was not among its residential neighbourhoods in any case). There is no residential designation for the Tilbury industrial lands in the plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **Zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** City material for Tilbury-area properties references the I2 and I7 industrial zone codes; the full industrial zone names and minimum lot standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet — UNVERIFIED. Delta's zoning-bylaw number is CONTESTED in the program record (cited as No. 7600, 2017 in the research and sibling files, and elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled; confirm the zone and standards for any parcel on the City zoning maps. Permitted-use, outdoor-storage and parking rules differ by zone, so a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **SSMUH does not create townhouses here:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) apply to single-detached and duplex lots; Tilbury's industrial lands are not single-detached residential lots, so SSMUH is not a route to housing in the industrial park. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Strata law still applies — to industrial strata:** on an industrial strata resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and the most recent depreciation report attached). **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote — this reaches industrial stratas too. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. For an industrial unit, add a Phase I environmental site assessment for the unit's and the complex's prior uses to the usual residential-style strata read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (for completeness):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID since November 24, 2022, short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the only permitted age bylaw under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 (Reg 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023) is a 55+ bylaw — none of this creates a residential use on industrial land, and no residential or age-restricted use is stated for any Tilbury industrial strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Living in an industrial unit — UNCONFIRMED, do not assume:** no source checked states any residential, caretaker-suite or live-work permission in Tilbury's industrial zones (City material references I2 and I7 codes; the full zone standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet). Do not price or market a Tilbury unit as livable without the parcel's zone sheet and a City answer in hand. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Flood homework is a first subject on any riverfront parcel:** Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront lands inside Delta's dike system, so confirm the parcel's Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and any Section 219 flood covenants on title before subject removal — for an industrial purchase exactly as for a home. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR and lease status on edge parcels:** City material shows farm use leases on some Tilbury-area parcels, and ALR status is parcel-specific through the Agricultural Land Commission — a leased or ALR parcel does not price like a clean freehold industrial lot. An ALR residence also cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), which is one more reason no strata townhouse product can appear on ALR edge parcels. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Buyer frame (general BC layer, for buyers redirected to residential Delta sub-areas):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%; REDMA s. 21 7-day rescission / s. 18 trust deposits where a presale exists elsewhere. Fee-simple contrast for those redirected searches: a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. None of that frame has a Tilbury townhouse to attach to. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. No Tilbury-specific tax or levy note was confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses in Tilbury at all?** No — zero verified, strata or fee-simple. Tilbury is designated industrial land in the OCP, and no residential housing mix was confirmed inside the industrial lands in City sources. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: A listing says "Tilbury" but shows a house — how?** Postal-area naming is wider than the industrial park: dwellings carrying a Tilbury-area address sit on surrounding Ladner / East Delta farm and riverfront parcels, outside the industrial park lands. Check which side of the industrial boundary the parcel is on before treating it as Tilbury product — and run that parcel's own ALR, flood and zoning checks as a Ladner/East Delta purchase. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Are the strata bays on Progress Way / Vantage Way a cheap way to "buy a townhouse"?** No. They are strata industrial units, not homes: permitted uses, outdoor storage and parking differ by zone (City material references I2 and I7 codes in Tilbury), and a use that works in one bay can be prohibited two streets over. Living in one is UNCONFIRMED as permitted on any source checked — do not buy on that assumption. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Could I get a caretaker suite or live-work setup in an industrial unit?** No source checked states any residential, caretaker-suite or live-work permission in Tilbury's industrial zones, and the full I2/I7 zone standards were not confirmed from a City zone sheet — UNCONFIRMED. Get the parcel's zone sheet and a City answer before pricing any unit as livable. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/90324/
- **Q: Where is the nearest actual townhouse stock?** In the neighbouring residential sub-areas, covered in their own files in this series — Annieville, Nordel and Scottsdale to the east in North Delta, Sunshine Hills Woods at the Scott Road edge, and Ladner's sub-areas to the south-west. No source checked names a specific nearest townhouse complex to Tilbury, so any "closest complex" claim is UNCONFIRMED. Basis: the sibling files in this series (Section 2 above).
- **Q: Does the depreciation-report rule affect an industrial strata purchase here?** Yes — stratas with five or more lots must have a depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year, and the strata must provide a Form B on resale. Add an environmental read (Phase I) for prior industrial uses, which a residential townhouse purchase does not need. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What about flood and dike status for a Tilbury parcel?** Tilbury Island and the River Road lands are Fraser River riverfront lands inside Delta's dike system; the City is the diking authority with over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and parcel-level Flood Construction Level and covenant detail is UNVERIFIED and must be confirmed per property before subject removal. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Could the industrial land be rezoned to townhouses later?** No source checked states any residential conversion plan for Tilbury; the OCP's stated position is the opposite — Delta's industrially zoned and designated land (1,527 hectares, about 13% of the region's inventory per Metro Vancouver's 2020 inventory) is a named pillar of the regional industrial base, with Tilbury Industrial Park listed among Delta's primary industry locations. Do not pay a housing-hope premium on industrial land here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Is there transit, a school or a park for a family moving to Tilbury?** There is no family to move there on the sources checked: no TransLink route serving inside the industrial lands was confirmed from a timetable (nearest exchanges: Ladner and Scottsdale), and no public school, City park, library branch, shopping centre or grocery store inside the lands was confirmed in official sources. https://www.translink.ca/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search
- **Q: Who buys in Tilbury, then?** Owner-user businesses and industrial investors — logistics, warehousing, manufacturing and service-industrial users buying strata bays or freehold sites near Highways 17, 91 and 99; value turns on the highway triangle and on yard and ceiling specs, not on housing amenities. The City's own Tilbury Ice posting markets exactly that position between the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Massey Tunnel. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-09/Tilbury%20Ice%20-%20For%20Lease%20Brochure.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/tilbury.md, §5)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Tilbury" in a housing search is the industrial park's name spilling into postal addresses.** The Paragon sub-area name, the Tilbury Industrial Park name and the FortisBC Tilbury LNG facility all use the same word; none of them is a residential neighbourhood, and the former North Delta Area Plan did not list Tilbury among its residential neighbourhoods. A townhouse search filtered to "Tilbury" returns industrial strata and farm-edge houses wearing a postal label. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
