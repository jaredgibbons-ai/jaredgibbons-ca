## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Fee-simple reality for this topic (stated plainly):** a freehold farm parcel has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only, plus the ALR layer below. The strata rules that follow in this section govern Tsawwassen's urban townhouses (cross-referenced in Section 2), not farm parcels. Basis: Section 2 above. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)
- **Form B diligence (urban strata townhouses only):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata only):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata only):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for an urban Tsawwassen strata is flagged void in its home file. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). NONE of Delta's five verified 55+ complexes is townhouse-form, and none is imported here as townhome stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules; farm land designated Agriculture (A) (s. 1.5.1 purpose: maintain agricultural lands; s. 2.6.1 recognizes farming as the primary use of agricultural land). A parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research file cites the A1 Agriculture zone of Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600. The program's standing correction records the Delta zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025"), so the number is flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. In the A1 zone as cited: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **ALR housing caps:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha, and Delta bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision requires OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with an 8 ha minimum lot size created (home site severance exception under ALC policy); A2 also sets an 8 ha minimum and A3 does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites (urban Delta, for contrast):** Delta's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024 and govern single-detached and duplex lots, not ALR farm parcels; on ALR land a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence (above). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification — full three-tier thresholds:** BC Assessment requires a producing farm to meet minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and is never used here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Taxes and rebates (residential frame, for completeness):** general PTT 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 (a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears); PTT exemptions (first-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (a page stating 2.80%–4.00% is WRONG — no such page statement is documented in this topic's research file). GST treatment of farm land itself is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer markets a new home, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust — no presale project is documented for this farm topic. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Water, septic, flood:** GWELLS is the registry for recorded wells (well reports within 90 days of construction/alteration/decommissioning; older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, non-domestic use including irrigation requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed/installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure); hazard DPA SD3 (Boundary Bay foreshore and the Southlands) provides a 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback, and any Delta property within 30 m of a stream — including farm ditches — is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA. Delta property taxes: 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here:** the 3% Empty Homes Tax is Vancouver only and is never used for a Delta property. Basis: City of Vancouver tax, stated for contrast only.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a townhouse complex on Tsawwassen farm land I can buy into?** No verified one — the count for this topic is zero. Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold farm houses, strata farm product is not a Delta pattern, and ALR residences cannot simply be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). Tsawwassen's verified townhouse stock is in the urban sub-areas (Section 2 cross-references). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Is there real farmland in Tsawwassen, or is it all residential?** Both — the OCP designates farm land on the Tsawwassen edges Agriculture (A) (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024, s. 1.5.1), ALR rules apply where land is in the ALR, and the urban neighbourhoods are residential. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Tsawwassen?** A principal residence built without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a farm have a second dwelling or a suite?** A suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. One additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and Delta bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel into townhouse lots?** No route is stated in any source checked: a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, and A3 zoning does not permit subdivision at all. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** The full three-tier test: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How do I check whether a parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status is parcel-specific. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Does Delta zoning apply on Tsawwassen Lands?** No — Tsawwassen First Nation has jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands (over 724 hectares under the Final Agreement); TFN leasehold product is excluded from this file with that reason stated. Establish which side of the treaty-land boundary a parcel sits on before applying Delta or ALR farm rules. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Q: What should I check on a Southlands-side parcel?** Hazard DPA SD3 (2.9 m floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback), the Streamside DPA if any ditch or slough is within 30 m, the well record in GWELLS, and the water licence position if the operation irrigates. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Are there Tsawwassen farm market statistics to price from?** No — SnapStats publishes no farm category and does not cover South Delta, and farm sale counts, price per acre and farm days-on-market are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/tsawwassen-farms.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Farm ditches count as streams for the setback check.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream — including sloughs and ditches — fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, so the rule reaches working farm land, not just urban lots. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **The farm ground here is a fringe, not a belt of its own.** It is the southern end of Delta's agricultural land, designated Agriculture (A) with the OCP's Agriculture policies (s. 2.6) over it and ALC rules layered on where a parcel is in the ALR — diligence follows the Delta belt, not a separate Tsawwassen farm market. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The dike work is live and local.** The City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding — part of the flood-protection system (over 63 km of infrastructure, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure) the Southlands-side parcels rely on. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **No soil class is published for a specific parcel in this file.** Agricultural capability is mapped per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder, and the ALC defines the classes from Class 1 (no or very slight limitations) down — any specific parcel's class here is UNVERIFIED until that map is read. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/
