## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (strata).** SPA s.59: within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page — fees, amounts owing, levies, CRF, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation / CRF.** 5+ lots: depreciation report every 5 years since Jul 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); CRF ≥10% of operating budget since Nov 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — every directory "rentals not allowed" line in the home files (e.g. Rosewood Court, Fraser Glen) is flagged VOID in-entry there.
