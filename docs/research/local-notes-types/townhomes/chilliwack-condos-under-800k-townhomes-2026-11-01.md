## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (for any strata unit a band buyer pursues, condo or townhouse):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this band the Form B, not the listing, is where fee, levy and deductible exposure is settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. In the referenced roster this lands hardest on the oldest buildings — the condos sibling names Elmwood Terrace (1986), Mountain View Estates (1988/1989) and Creekside Estates and Evergreen Estates (both 1993). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The condos sibling flags the Mountain View Estates and Evergreen Estates directory "rentals not allowed" lines void in-entry on exactly this rule — any such line surfacing for a band unit is read the same way here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). In the referenced roster, Mountain View Estates and Elmwood Terrace are presented as 55+ communities in their listings — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B before treating any unit there as generally occupiable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse is counted in this band file (§2) — a feed unit described as fee-simple needs a title check against the strata record for its complex.
- **City plan, zoning and SSMUH:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500, with Schedule B2 (Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island), B3 (Downtown) and B5 (Sardis) covering the ground this feed draws from; the OCP's Schedule D permit areas are D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate, D-5 Hillside Development, D-6 Downtown, D-7 Mixed-Use Areas, D-8 Residential, D-9 Commercial and D-10 Industrial — which areas apply to a given building's parcel is a per-parcel check: UNVERIFIED for any specific building. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. The SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Secondary suites under Bylaw 5000 are capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, with only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot and garden suites capped at 100 m² (ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04). https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground only):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. FVRD electoral areas use the separate rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, 5% after July 2 + 5% after Oct 31); never mix the two. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Taxes and rebates — with worked examples, inputs stated:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — flagged; the tier math governs (no price in this band reaches those tiers). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling — the other program conditions (principal residence, 0.5 hectares or smaller, residential improvements only, buyer conditions) still apply per buyer. **Worked example 1 — $500,000 resale condo, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**; with the first-time buyers' exemption applying in full, the tax on the first $500,000 is exempt, so the same purchase pays **$0** in PTT (maximum saving $8,000 on the Province's own terms). **Worked example 2 — $700,000 resale condo, no exemption:** $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**; with the first-time buyers' exemption applying in full, $12,000 − $8,000 = **$4,000**. Run the exempt figure against the buyer's actual qualification before quoting it. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this band that means any unit still on a developer sale, not the resale buildings. No CMHC defect is asserted here — this file's own research documents no such defect for this slug. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
