## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 5 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. For the 1979–1999 stock (Old Kits Townhomes, Bayside Manor, Yorkville Mews, Kits Palisades), the depreciation report and CRF balance are the diligence centre of the purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Kits Quartet, at 4 or 6 units (UNCONFIRMED), may sit under or over that threshold — confirm from its disclosure statement. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" line for Balsam West (an apartment building, documented in Section 2) is flagged VOID in-entry for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No counted Kitsilano townhouse complex carries a published 55+ bylaw in the sources checked — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ strata ownership stock in Kitsilano. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Kitsilano; the area's fee-simple ground-oriented forms are the detached house and the duplex under the RT-7 / RT-8 and R1-1 schedules below, and a Kitsilano listing with no strata fee charged should be title-checked as one of those forms before it is read as a townhouse complex unit.
- **City zoning:** Kitsilano's RT areas sit under the RT-7 and RT-8 District Schedules of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — the RT-8 schedule states a minimum site area of 306 m² for a duplex, and the RT-7 / RT-8 intent is to encourage retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the historical character of the area; house lots elsewhere sit under R1-1 (minimum site area 306 m² for 3–4-unit multiple dwellings, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 or more units, with secondary suites and laneway houses in the forms the schedule states). Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Plan:** the Broadway Plan covers Vine Street to Clark Drive and 1st Avenue to 16th Avenue, including parts of Kitsilano, and is the framework tied to the Broadway Subway — south-edge townhouses (Kits Quartet's West 11th Avenue block among them) carry plan expectations the beach blocks do not. A Kitsilano-specific community plan: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Flood:** the City's Coastal Flood Risk Assessment covers a Point Grey–Kitsilano study area — the shoreline from Point Grey to Kitsilano Point faces the Strait of Georgia, is exposed to waves from the northwest, flood protection is limited and riprap west of Kitsilano Beach is discontinuous; the Province advises planning for 0.5 m of sea level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100. Beach-block townhouse buyers should get the parcel-level flood and insurance picture early. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared vacant; every Vancouver owner must file an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example Kits Quartet if bought from the developer), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Kitsilano townhouse presale is carried in this file; the frame governs any Broadway-corridor townhouse product sold off-plan at the area's south edge. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What townhouse stock does Kitsilano actually have?** Five verified strata complexes: Kits Palisades (2119 Yew Street, 1999), Bayside Manor (1659 Balsam Street, 1983), Old Kits Townhomes (1620 Balsam Street, 1979), Yorkville Mews (1848 West 1st Avenue, 1986) and Kits Quartet (3120 West 11th Avenue, 2026). Small clusters, mostly 1979–1999 wood-frame — nothing like a suburban townhouse subdivision. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2119-yew-street/104
- **Q: Is Kits Palisades a townhouse complex or a condo building?** Both forms appear at the one address: the property manager describes a 12-unit townhouse complex, one current listing records a townhouse unit with strata fees, and another unit at 2119 Yew Street is MLS-classified as an apartment. Confirm the specific unit's form and strata lot on the Form B before comparing it to either stock. https://vadapm.com/kits-palisades-2-bedroom-townhouse-rental-103-2119-yew-vancouver
- **Q: How many homes are in Kits Quartet — four or six?** UNCONFIRMED: the listing's building details say 4 units in the development; the listing copy says six townhomes. The disclosure statement and strata plan settle it — do not quote either figure as fact until they are read. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/1-3120-w-11th-ave-43326
- **Q: Can I rent out my Kitsilano townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still prints "rentals not allowed" (as it does for Balsam West, an apartment building nearby). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw and are separately regulated in Vancouver — read the registered bylaws on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I worry about in a 1979–1983 Kitsilano strata?** The building envelope and the reserve fund: Old Kits Townhomes (1979) and Bayside Manor (1983) predate modern rainscreen practice, and since July 1, 2024 their stratas must hold a depreciation report on a five-year cycle and contribute at least 10% of the operating budget to the CRF. The Form B package — report, CRF balance, insurance summary — is where the answer lives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Does the character zoning affect a Kitsilano townhouse purchase?** The RT-7 / RT-8 schedules state an intent to retain buildings consistent with the area's historical character — that mainly prices the house and duplex stock, but it also means exterior alterations to a strata townhouse can face both strata approval and the character-retention framework. Confirm the exact district schedule for the address before planning changes. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station in Kitsilano?** Not yet — Kitsilano is bus-served (routes 2, 4, 7, 9, 14, 44, 84 and the 99 B-Line), with the Broadway Subway extending the Millennium Line along Broadway at the area's south edge. A West 11th Avenue townhouse sits inside the Broadway Plan area that the subway anchors. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/transit-system-maps/regional-maps/2026/january/vancouver.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Balsam Street townhouse?** Address-based in SD39 — four elementaries (Bayview, Gordon, Hudson, Tennyson) and Kitsilano Secondary sit inside the area, and the locator result for 3505 West 7th Avenue is Bayview Elementary and Kitsilano Secondary. That confirms only that address; run the exact townhouse address in the locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: What flood check does a beach-block townhouse need?** The City's coastal assessment describes the Point Grey–Kitsilano shoreline as exposed to northwest waves with limited, discontinuous flood protection, and provincial guidance plans for 0.5 m of sea-level rise by 2050 — get the parcel-level flood picture and an insurance quote before subject removal on any unit north of 4th Avenue. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax catch a Kitsilano townhouse used as a second home?** It can — the tax is 3% on homes declared vacant, every owner files an annual declaration, and the research file flags it as directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor stock. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **There was a salmon cannery at the foot of Macdonald Street.** The City's history records that at the turn of the century the area from Burrard to Alma was dense forest despite earlier logging, and a canning factory at the foot of Macdonald Street once could not cope with the "hundreds of thousands of salmon" caught in 1900 — the beach blocks were industrial waterfront within living memory of the oldest houses. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kitsilano.aspx
- **Kits Beach's official name and address rarely appear in listings.** Kitsilano Beach Park is at 1499 Arbutus Street, and the City repeats the travel-magazine line that Kits Beach is among the "top 10 best city beaches in the world" — the park, not the sand alone, is the amenity the north-of-4th townhouse premium is buying. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kitsilano.aspx
- **A supportive-housing rezoning already reshaped one south-Kits block.** Council approved 129 studio homes at 2086–2098 West 7th Avenue and 2091 West 8th Avenue on July 27, 2022 — south-edge buyers near West 7th and Vine should know the block's form changed by rezoning, not by the Broadway Plan. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kitsilano.aspx
- **The library is on Macdonald, not on 4th.** The Kitsilano Branch of Vancouver Public Library sits at 2425 MacDonald Street — mid-neighbourhood, serving the residential streets where the townhouse clusters actually are. https://www.vpl.ca/location/kitsilano-branch
- **The research file could not confirm the Kitsilano Community Centre on a City source.** The confirmed recreation anchor in the sources read is Kitsilano Beach Park itself — a buyer mapping amenities should treat the beach park, not a community centre, as the verified in-area facility. UNVERIFIED as an in-area facility in the research file. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/kitsilano-beach.aspx
- **No hospital sits inside Kitsilano.** Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is the hospital named on the health-authority location page east of the area — "close to VGH" in listing copy means a drive across Burrard, not a walk. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
