### 2.2 Apartment condos and townhouse components — where they count instead

- Apartment condos, townhouse-style units inside apartment strata, podium/stacked units and tower townhouse components all count in the **condos program only**, in their area home files — built form governs per component, and each component counts in exactly one program. A map result that reads "Townhouse" on a tower address is a component of that tower's strata, not a townhouse complex, and is never re-counted in a townhomes file on the strength of a map label (program rule; condos sibling §2).
- Mixed complexes are split by address/form in their area files — the townhouse component in the townhomes file, the apartment component in the condos file. A map search collapses that split back into one pin; the files, not the map, carry the count.

### 2.3 Presales — counted separately, never mixed

### 2.4 Excluded stock — tenure traps a map cannot flag, with reasons

A map treats all of the following as ordinary pins. None is townhouse ownership stock, and each is excluded in its area file for the stated reason:

- **Leasehold — TFN ground leases, City ground leases, UniverCity (SFU) 99-year prepaid leasehold:** not freehold title; excluded wherever met, with the lease structure stated in the area file.
- **Corporation shares / undivided interest:** the buyer holds shares or an undivided interest, not a strata lot or fee-simple parcel — excluded.
- **Co-operatives:** membership/share purchase, not a strata or fee-simple purchase — excluded (a co-op townhouse complex is documented as a co-op in its area file, never counted as townhouse stock).
- **Life leases and membership / non-strata seniors housing:** a purchased or membership right to occupy, not title — excluded.
- **Non-market rental and purpose-built rental:** nothing to buy — excluded, however townhouse-like the built form.
- **Pad-rental manufactured-home parks: NEVER townhouse stock** — the pad is rented under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act.
- **Bare-land strata and single-family strata:** documented as their own form in area files, not counted unless genuinely townhouse-form strata — a map label cannot make that determination.

## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

A map search is a finding tool. The rules below are what the finding has to be verified against — none of them is visible on a map.

**The strata block (applies to any strata townhouse a search surfaces).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Form, tenure and restrictions are read off the Form B, strata plan and title — never off a listing card or map pin (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold — lot count comes from the Form B / strata plan, not from counting pins or units on a map. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such in the area file where the complex is counted.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). A map has no "verified 55+ bylaw" layer — an age claim is a registered-bylaw question in the area file.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. A map result cannot tell a buyer which of the two diligence tracks a townhouse is on; the title can.

**The map-behind-the-map: GIS layers, zoning and the ALR.**

- The official checks behind the page's own GIS advice, from primary sources: a parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps by PID or civic address (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps); housing on ALR land is capped at one principal residence of 500 m² or less (suite inside the principal residence only) plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (larger parcels) — and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, strata plans cause subdivision), which is why the ALR layer the page tells readers to turn on matters before a townhouse plan is priced (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- Zoning, OCP designation, SSMUH and secondary-suite rules are the rules of the municipality the parcel sits in, cited in that area's file with the bylaw name/number — there is no site-wide zoning frame to state for a region-wide tool, and none is stated here. What each municipality's own GIS shows layer by layer is UNVERIFIED in this pass at municipal level (research file §4).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of 3% **plus** the further 2% wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked tier math: §2.5.
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug notes some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs, and no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). A map price filter does not separate new construction (GST applies) from resale (it does not) — two pins at the same price can carry different tax.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 annual. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the map-search page, so it is NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax is **3% — Vancouver only**; it is never applied to a townhouse found in any other municipality, and a region-wide map does not change that.
- **Presales / REDMA.** 7-day rescission under s. 21 (from the later of signing or receiving the disclosure statement) and deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any presale townhome a search surfaces (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
