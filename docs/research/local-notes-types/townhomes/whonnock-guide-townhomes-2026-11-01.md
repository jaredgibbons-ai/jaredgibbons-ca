## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B (frame, if a strata townhouse ever trades here):** SPA s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Whonnock complex exists for this to attach to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation/CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Whonnock complex carries a directory rental line in this file (there is no complex), so no in-entry flag arises. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Whonnock complex is published as age-restricted; the 55-plus siblings verify no 55+ stock for this ground, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Haney Pioneer Village is a 60+ co-op in Haney — never 55+ strata, never Whonnock stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
