## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no strata townhouse complex to diligence here — the diligence is tower diligence.** A Downtown ground-oriented unit is a strata lot in an apartment tower, so the full apartment-strata package governs: Form B Information Certificate under Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every Downtown tower strata is far over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any Downtown listing or directory line reading "rentals not allowed" is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in Downtown; the sibling 55-plus file on disk carries the area's age-restricted position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the area — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses; CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-C-3A.pdf
- **Heritage controls:** the City's heritage areas work gives regulations for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown — Gastown is north of Hastings Street between Richards and Main Streets, and Yaletown is bordered by Homer and Nelson Streets to the north and east and Cambie, Mainland and Drake Streets to the south and west; heritage rules affect renovation and alteration plans for older ground-oriented units. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in it is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels — a check on any False Creek-edge ground-level unit and its building. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. This tax does not exist in Burnaby or Coquitlam and must never be carried into those files. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. The Province's additional school tax can also apply at Downtown price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA), where a Downtown presale is bought:** the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No verified Downtown presale is carried in Section 2, so this is the standing frame only. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any real townhouse complexes downtown?** None verified as standalone complexes — the research pass and this pass verified zero named townhouse complexes inside the Downtown local area. What exists is townhouse-form units at the bases of apartment towers, counted as condos in this program. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/downtown-ve.md)
- **Q: A listing calls a Yaletown unit a "townhouse." What am I actually buying?** A strata lot in an apartment tower — MLS sub-type "Townhouse" describes the unit's two-level, ground-oriented layout, but built form governs: your strata is the tower, with its depreciation report, CRF and levy history. Read the tower's Form B package, not a townhouse-complex package. https://vancouvernashdom.com/lux-yaletown/SearchResults.form/?_pg=3
- **Q: Can I rent out a Downtown unit long-term?** Under provincial law, strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a listing still prints one. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and the City's Empty Homes Tax declaration is a separate annual filing every owner must make. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the Empty Homes Tax hit if I leave a Downtown home empty?** 3% of assessed value for a property deemed vacant, on top of the annual declaration duty — Vancouver only; the City's 2025 annual report describes the 3% rate as consistent and stable. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate filing. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org
- **Q: Is the West End part of Downtown for my search?** No — the West End is its own official local area west of Downtown, and board searches behave differently again: Yaletown and Coal Harbour appear as their own Paragon sub-areas, and Coal Harbour stock is not counted in this file at all. Name the sub-neighbourhood and the station, not just "downtown." https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Which schools are actually in Downtown?** Two VSB elementaries — Elsie Roy, 150 Drake Street, and Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət, 55 Expo Boulevard. No public secondary school inside the local area was confirmed, and catchments are address-based — run the exact address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **Q: Do Gastown and Yaletown heritage rules affect a ground-floor unit?** Yes — the City's heritage areas carry regulations and design guidelines for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown that bind exterior and alteration work from day one. Check the heritage status of the building before pricing a renovation. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: What zoning governs a Downtown purchase?** Mostly the Downtown District (DD) schedule in By-law No. 3575, which permits the uses in the Downtown District Official Development Plan; CD-1 and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. There are no house-lot minimums to price against — C-3A shows no minimum site area for its listed uses. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new home, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Downtown began as Granville, now Gastown.** The City states Vancouver began as a collection of buildings called Granville on Burrard Inlet, competing to be the west coast terminus of the Canadian Pacific Railway; growth later moved the city centre from Water and Cordova Streets up to Georgia and Granville Streets — the layer cake a Downtown buyer is pricing, from heritage warehouse conversions to new towers. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx
- **The Coal Harbour link points at the West End.** On the City's areas page the Coal Harbour locality link resolves to the West End page, even though Coal Harbour sits at Downtown's north-west waterfront — one reason this program files Coal Harbour as its own board sub-area and counts none of it here. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **No secondary school was confirmed inside the local area.** The VSB neighbourhood list names only the two elementary schools under Downtown — families pricing a Downtown home on a secondary catchment are pricing an address outside the local area's own school list, and must run the locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **The False Creek floodplain touches the south shore.** The City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — ground-level Yaletown units sit closest to that line, and the building's insurance and strata documents should be read against it. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **The additional school tax starts at $3 million.** At Downtown penthouse and townhouse-form price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
