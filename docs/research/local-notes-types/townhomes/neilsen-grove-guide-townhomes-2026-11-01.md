# Neilsen Grove — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

**Spelling lock, kept exact throughout: the AREA is "Neilsen Grove"; the SCHOOL is "Neilson Grove Elementary"** — School District 37 spells the school "Neilson Grove Elementary" on its own site, at 5500 Admiral Boulevard. MLS feed text may carry either spelling; the notes' own wording follows the lock. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/

Neilsen Grove is a Paragon sub-area name for north Ladner, in the City of Delta — it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name. The sub-area sits on and around the Marina Garden Estates lands named in City of Delta council reports, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/202969/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. In Hampton Cove, the Form B and budget are also where Hampton Club amenity charges show up — do not assume club access is free or included without reading them. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. The spread matters here: Chesapeake Landing (1998–2001) and Woodward Landing (2006) are at envelope/roof age, while Westhampton (2023–24) is near-new. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, so any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Neilsen Grove complex is void for long-term rentals (Fairwinds' strata records already record rentals allowed). Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. None of the six townhouse complexes in Section 2 is verified as 55+ — the sub-area's verified 55+ complex is South Pointe, the bare-land strata that is not counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly because buyers ask.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Neilsen Grove (Section 2) — a listing calling a Hampton Cove townhouse "fee simple" contradicts the verified record and needs a title check. (Complex entries in Section 2 above.)
- **New-home warranty is still running on the newest stock.** Strata records for Fairwinds note the structural warranty runs to 2027 under BC's 2-5-10 new-home warranty — the same check applies to the 2023–24 Westhampton homes, whose warranty years run longest: ask which warranty years remain on the specific complex. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — no lettered schedules, no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, not asserted.** Sources conflict on the zoning bylaw citation for Delta (a "Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" citation appears in the sibling files, against a competing "8400, 2025" citation in standing corrections); this file asserts neither. The Hampton Cove lands were in any case built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning (a Westhampton listing records zoning CD430 for that complex), so each complex's rules are read off its own CD zone and the City zoning maps. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5311-admiral-way/9
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — relevant to Neilsen Grove's detached lots, not to the Hampton Cove strata lands. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale townhouses here carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment — the 0.6%–4.5% range buyers see quoted. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are Hampton Cove townhouses strata or fee-simple?** Strata. Fairwinds and Chesapeake Landing are expressly recorded as freehold strata, Charterhouse listings record freehold strata, and Westhampton listings carry a Strata sub-type — there is no verified fee-simple townhouse complex in Neilsen Grove. "Freehold strata" still means strata fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation report; it is not fee-simple ownership. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832 and https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3103064-5900-ferry-road-65-delta-bc-v4k-5c3/brochure
- **Q: What do the fees have to cover that a normal townhouse complex's don't?** A waterfront community plus shared access to the Hampton Club — listings describe a 12,000 sq ft clubhouse with outdoor pool, hot tub, fitness centre, lounge and guest suites serving Hampton Cove. What your unit's fee actually includes is stated in the strata budget attached to the Form B. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3100590-5535-admiral-way-129-delta-bc-v4k-0e3/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out my townhouse?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: BC voids strata rental-restriction bylaws as of Nov 24, 2022, and Fairwinds' strata records record rentals allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Woodward Landing listings describe the complex as pet-friendly; for the other five complexes no public source carried states a pet rule: UNCONFIRMED per complex. Pet bylaws are strata-specific — confirm number and any size limits in the registered bylaws. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3095102-39-5300-admiral-way-delta-british-columbia-v4k5g6-1339000/brochure
- **Q: Is any Hampton Cove townhouse complex 55+?** None is verified as 55+. The sub-area's verified 55+ complex is South Pointe — a bare land strata of detached ranchers, not townhouses — and Pilothouse's downsizer marketing in the condo building is not an age bylaw either. If age restriction matters, read the bylaws in the Form B package, not the marketing. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/neilsen-grove-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I keep a boat here?** The community sits between a marina and a golf course. Whether any moorage right attaches to a specific strata lot: UNCONFIRMED — no source checked ties a slip to a unit in any of the six complexes. Treat marina access as a separate arrangement unless the Form B or title says otherwise. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/pilothouse-in-delta/
- **Q: Chesapeake Landing is from 1998–2001 — what should I check?** The depreciation report's envelope, roof and deck items, any engineering reports, and the strata minutes for capital history, plus the CRF balance on the Form B against the next items on the 30-year list — at 25-plus years, that paper trail, not the listing, prices the risk. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: How many homes are in each complex?** Verified counts exist for two: Fairwinds at 148 homes and Beaufort Landing at 124 townhomes. Charterhouse, Westhampton, Woodward Landing and Chesapeake Landing counts are UNCONFIRMED on the sources carried — do not quote a count for them without the strata plan. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/beaufort-landing-5551-admiral-way-ladner-124-three-and-four-bedroom-townhomes-by-polygon-beaufort-landing-ltd
- **Q: Is Neilsen Grove behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and properties within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, a live issue on harbour-side lots. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Which school is in the area, and how is it spelled?** Neilson Grove Elementary — spelled "Neilson" — at 5500 Admiral Boulevard, while the area is "Neilsen Grove". The locator result for the school's address is Neilson Grove Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); check any other address in the locator. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/ and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 618 loops from Ladner Exchange through Admiral Way and Admiral Boulevard; from the Exchange, route 601 runs to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Ladner Trunk Road, Highway 17A and Highway 99. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt618.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hampton Cove was planned as roughly 670 homes, mostly townhouses — the condos are the exception.** Project reporting records Hampton Cove at some 670 residences, primarily townhomes, in five phases between a golf course and a marina, and describes Pilothouse as the community's only apartment building. Neilsen Grove is a townhouse sub-area with one condo building, not the reverse. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/beaufort-landing-5551-admiral-way-ladner-124-three-and-four-bedroom-townhomes-by-polygon-beaufort-landing-ltd
- **The build years span 27 years inside one community name.** Chesapeake Landing's phases date to 1998–2001 and Woodward Landing to 2006, while Westhampton homes were built in 2023–2024 — two "Hampton Cove / Marina Garden Estates" townhouses can be a generation apart in envelope, warranty and depreciation-report terms. Compare the complex, not the community name (Section 2 above).
- **The warranty clock is a live value item here.** With Fairwinds' structural warranty recorded as running to 2027 and Westhampton homes built in 2023–2024, two otherwise similar Neilsen Grove purchases can carry very different remaining 2-5-10 warranty coverage — check the complex's warranty dates before comparing prices. https://www.estateblock.com/ladner/54-5550-admiral-way-100832
- **The spelling split trips up searches and contracts.** The area is "Neilsen Grove" and the school is "Neilson Grove Elementary" — both spellings are in official use, for different things, and MLS feed text carries both, so a search run under only one spelling will miss listings filed under the other. https://ng.deltasd.bc.ca/
- **Delta's zoning bylaw number is contested in the sources — do not quote one.** The sibling files and standing corrections carry competing citations; what governs a Hampton Cove purchase in practice is the complex's own site-specific CD zone (Westhampton's is recorded as CD430) and its registered strata bylaws. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5311-admiral-way/9
- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion and major construction expected to begin in 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **There is no Neilsen Grove market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Ladner community section and no Neilsen Grove row — its only Delta section is North Delta, and South Delta is not covered by SnapStats at all — so any "Neilsen Grove median townhouse price" quoted from that source does not exist, and no townhome-only figure exists in any case because SnapStats reports attached product only as Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/neilsen-grove-guide.md).
