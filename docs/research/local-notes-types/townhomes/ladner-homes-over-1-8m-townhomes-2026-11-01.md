## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The strata block below governs any strata townhouse bought in this band; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast for a non-strata purchase (48 Landing is Ladner's verified fee-simple example, in its home file).

- **Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At this price level the depreciation report and any approved-but-unpaid levy are material closing figures, not paperwork. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met on a Ladner complex is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals in its home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Ladner townhouse complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead.
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — NO lettered schedules (the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the OLD Bylaw 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1, agriculture policies at s. 2.6). **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City zone sheets cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"); the research ladder: RS1–RS3 at 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, RS4 610 m², RS5 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m², RD2/RD3 4,000 m², RD4 504 m², no RD5; zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. No prevalence claim about suites in recent Ladner builds is made in this file — the live page editorial's suite claims are UNVERIFIED (§1). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood, streamside, ALR and foreshore — the band's diligence layer:** Ladner sits behind Delta's dike and drainage system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure); all properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area; ALR status is parcel-specific on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, and **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore, held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC — relevant to the riverfront stock in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Farm-class thresholds, in full three-tier form** (relevant to acreage listings in this band): $2,500 of gross farm income on farms of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha on larger farms; $10,000 on farms under 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met. (Program standing record; research file documents no farm-class figure of its own.)
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. **Home owner grant at this band:** the Province states the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Property transfer tax — the band's core tax rule, with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — flagged: the tier math governs, and no such compressed row is restated as a rate in this file. **Worked example 1 — $2,000,000 purchase, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. **Worked example 2 — $3,500,000 purchase, fully residential, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 over $3M ($10,000) = **$93,000**. On a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first-time buyers' full exemption caps at fair market value $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and eliminated at $1,150,000 — buyers in this band budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST / rebates / programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new-build townhouse in this band sits above the $1.5M phase-out, so the rebate is $0 at band prices even for a qualifying first-time buyer; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K (likewise below this band); HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (insured financing is not the typical frame at this band, and no CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none). 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhouse in this band is a developer sale, REDMA s. 18 (disclosure statement, deposits in trust) and s. 21 (7-day rescission from the later of signing or acknowledging it) govern; no presale is counted as resale band stock (§2). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many Ladner homes are for sale over $1.8M right now?** That figure cannot be stated from the sources used here: SnapStats publishes no price-band cut and does not cover South Delta at all. The live page's feed is the only place the current band count can be seen, and it changes as listings come and go. (§1 above)
- **Q: Are there townhomes over $1.8M in Ladner?** This file verifies none as band stock — band status is UNCONFIRMED per building for every Ladner townhouse complex. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is the PTT on a $2 million Ladner home?** $38,000 with no exemption: $2,000 (1% on the first $200,000) + $36,000 (2% on the next $1,800,000). Neither the first-time nor the newly built exemption reaches $2 million. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is there extra tax over $3 million?** Yes — a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on top of the 3% tier above $2 million. On a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase the total is $93,000 (worked tier-by-tier in §3). A summary row calling the over-$3M rate "5%" is a compression of 3% + 2%, not a separate tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the home owner grant still help at this level?** Partly, then not at all: the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 assessed value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and the regular grant is $0 over $2,189,000. Two otherwise similar homes straddling the threshold carry a real annual-cost difference. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the new GST rebate on a $1.9M new townhouse?** No — the Bill C-4 FTHB GST rebate phases out entirely by $1.5M (100% to $1M, maximum $50,000), so a band-price new build receives $0 from it; the older New Housing Rebate's $350K–$450K band is further below still. GST itself (5%) still applies to the new build. (§3 above)
- **Q: What should I check on a riverfront or acreage home in this band?** Three parcel-level checks: flood and streamside position (behind Delta's dike system; within 30 m of a slough or ditch is the Streamside DPA), foreshore tenure for any dock or moorage (provincial Crown land, by lease or licence), and ALR status on the Commission's maps — ALR residences cannot be stratified. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which Ladner areas appear on this page?** On the page text read for the research: Holly, Ladner Elementary, Ladner Rural, Hawthorne and Neilsen Grove — feed labels only, not a price claim about any sub-area. (§1 above)
- **Q: If I buy a strata townhouse at this level, what document matters most?** The Form B package — fees, amounts owing, approved-but-unpaid levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, current budget and the most recent depreciation report. At band prices, an approved levy is a five- or six-figure closing fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Does price change the school catchment?** No — SD37 catchments follow the address, not the price or the sub-area name. Check the exact address in the locator and the published Ladner Area catchment maps even at this price point. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The band has no statistics — by design, not by omission.** Two independent limits stack: SnapStats does not cover South Delta at all, and it publishes no price-band cut anywhere. Any "average days on market for Ladner homes over $1.8M" quoted from that source does not exist. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/ladner-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **The further 2% is the tax buyers miss.** At $3.5M the PTT is $93,000, of which $10,000 is the further-2% layer over $3M that listing summaries compress into a single "5%" row — the compression hides which dollars are taxed at which rate on a mixed-class property, where only the residential portion carries the extra layer. (§3 above)
- **The grant disappears inside this band.** A principal residence assessed at $2,075,000 keeps the full regular grant; at $2,189,000 it is $0. Sellers just over the line are competing against that annual difference whether they know it or not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **The page's own spelling is a trap for the rebuild.** The live editorial uses "Neilson Grove" — the school's spelling — for the area in places. The program rule is area "Neilsen Grove", school "Neilson Grove Elementary", and MLS listing cards carry the wrong spelling inside feed text as well. (§1 above, §3 page check)
- **Foreshore is tenure, not title.** On the riverfront stock in this band, upland title, a dock or moorage arrangement, and provincial foreshore tenure are three different things — the foreshore is Crown land held by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **The dike system is the community's foundation fact.** Ladner's high-value stock sits on low delta land behind over 63 km of City-maintained flood protection infrastructure and 31 drainage pump stations — flood construction and insurance questions are address-specific for any listing in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
