## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (any Port Moody strata, incl. a future Sentinel Hill purchase):** Strata Property Act s.59 — Form B states monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, minimum CRF contribution is 10% of the operating budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws VOID** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a North Shore strata is VOID for long-term rentals (flagged in-entry wherever met; none is carried for a counted complex here because none is counted); short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Port Moody's own STR position: UNVERIFIED in this research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No North Shore building is verified to carry one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules + registered covenants/HOA only. None verified here (§2).
- **City plan/zoning:** **Port Moody OCP Bylaw 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026, repeals Bylaw No. 2955** (2955 is NEVER current); Ch.14 §§14.2–14.4 cover this shore. Zoning Bylaw 2018, No. 2937: RS = single-detached, RT/RM = multi-residential; SSMUH in RS/RT outside transit-oriented areas (up to 4 units on 280–4,050 m² lots, up to 6 within 400 m of frequent bus stops, Bylaw Amendment No.94, 2024, No.3460, per siblings); RS1-S small lots (min 9 m / 325 m²) available in Pleasantside (one of only three areas) where OCP designates Single Family–Low Density. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
