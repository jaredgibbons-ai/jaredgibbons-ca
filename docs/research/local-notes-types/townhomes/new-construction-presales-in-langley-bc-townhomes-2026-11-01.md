## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**REDMA — the core of this file.**

- **s.21 rescission:** a purchaser may rescind by serving written notice on the developer within 7 days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement (research file §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs).
- **s.15 / disclosure:** a developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (research file §4; same BCFSA source).
- **s.18 deposits in trust:** deposits must be held in trust as REDMA s.18 requires (standing purchase block; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
- The page's deposit range ("10% to 20% in total, in two to four instalments") and its worked-cost table are the page's own presentation with no developer source opened — UNVERIFIED here beyond the PTT/GST rules verified below (research file §4).
- The page's 2-5-10 warranty statement (2 years materials/labour, 5 years envelope, 10 years structure) was not opened on a BC Housing or other official source — UNVERIFIED as stated (research file §4).

**Q: How long can I take to cancel a presale after signing?**
A: Seven days under REDMA s.21, from the later of signing and your Disclosure Statement receipt — get both documents to a presale lawyer inside that window (§3; https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs).

**Q: Where is my deposit held?**
A: In trust, as REDMA s.18 requires — verify the trust holder named in the disclosure statement before paying any instalment (§3).

**Q: Do I pay PTT on a Langley presale?**
A: Often not on a principal residence: the Newly Built Home Exemption fully exempts a qualifying purchase at $1,100,000 or less, phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024, with no first-time-buyer requirement (§3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption).

**Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST back?**
A: Yes: 100% of GST up to $1 million under Bill C-4 (Royal Assent March 13, 2026), phasing to $1.5 million, maximum $50,000 (§3; CRA News No. 122).
