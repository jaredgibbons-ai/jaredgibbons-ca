# Tsawwassen North, Delta — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen North" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the north side of Tsawwassen along Highway 17 and 52 Street toward Ladner, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The defining boundary on this side is a jurisdictional one: the north side sits beside Tsawwassen First Nation Lands. Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing, with jurisdiction over land management on Tsawwassen Lands — over 724 hectares transferred under the Tsawwassen First Nation Final Agreement — so City of Delta zoning does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. That boundary decides which townhouse product in this file is City-side strata and which is TFN leasehold, and the two are never mixed in the count. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- Main roads: Highway 17 (to the BC Ferries Tsawwassen terminal and to Highway 17A), 52 Street, 56 Street and Canoe Pass Way; Highway 17A leads to Highway 99 and the George Massey Tunnel. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Transit: route 609 runs from South Delta Exchange via 56 Street, 12 Avenue, 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way, through Tsawwassen First Nation, to Ladner Exchange; route 601 serves 56 Street and South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Where townhouse-form product actually sits: (1) on the City side, one City-listed townhouse project in the pipeline at 4800 Springs Boulevard beside the Tsawwassen Springs condo buildings — a QUEUE item, not built stock (Section 2); and (2) on Tsawwassen Lands, the Orca Way / Wolf Way / Hemlock Way townhouse communities (Seaside, Boardwalk, Tsawwassen Landing) — all leasehold, all excluded from City-side townhouse stock with the reason stated in Section 2. The rest of the City side is single detached houses under the RS and RD zones south of Highway 17, with farm and ALR land between Tsawwassen and Ladner. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- Schools — the north side's trap: for the sample address 5000 Canoe Pass Way, the SD37 School Locator result is **Ladner Elementary (K–7), 5016 44 Ave., and Delta Secondary (8–12), 4615 51 St** — Ladner catchments, not Tsawwassen ones. Every address must be checked in the locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Market stats: the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no South Delta (Ladner / Tsawwassen) community section — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no SnapStats figures exist for Tsawwassen North, and none are cited in this file. Source: the area research file for tsawwassen-north-guide (Section 2), read 2026-11-01.

## 2. COMPLEXES

**Verified City-side townhouse stock in this sub-area: ZERO built complexes — 0 strata, 0 fee-simple.** That zero is the finding, stated at the top as required. The townhouse-form product a buyer will actually encounter under a "Tsawwassen North" search is Tsawwassen First Nation leasehold product, excluded below with its reason, plus one City-side pipeline project carried as a QUEUE item and never folded into the count.

### QUEUE — City-side pipeline (not built stock, never counted)

- **Townhouse project at 4800 Springs Boulevard.** Listed on the City of Delta housing projects map, under a City application file covering 4800 Springs Boulevard and a portion of 5133 Springs Boulevard. The research and condos files record it as a townhouse project in the application/snapshot stage — no completed complex, no strata plan, no unit count and no tenure (strata vs fee-simple) is published in the City snapshot read. Status, unit count and tenure: UNCONFIRMED beyond the City listing itself. Do not quote it as available townhouse stock. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf

### Tsawwassen Springs — apartment condos, documented separately (NOT townhomes)

- Tsawwassen Springs, on Springs Boulevard at 52 Street (Shato Holdings / Talisman Homes), is the north side's City-side strata development, and its verified buildings are apartment condos: 5099 Springs Boulevard (built 2011 per listings), 5055 Springs Boulevard (built 2014, concrete construction per listings) and 5011 Springs Boulevard (the Phase 3 condo, built 2018 per listings, concrete, geothermal). Full detail is in the sibling file tsawwassen-north-condos-2026-11-01.md on disk. Detached houses also form part of Tsawwassen Springs; they are freehold product, not townhouses, and are not counted here. https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/about/team/ and https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/page/40/?post_type=page&p=0
- Across all of Tsawwassen, the condos run verified 16 apartment buildings in total (de-duplicated for the East / Beach Grove overlap). None of those 16 is counted in any townhomes file. Basis: the three Tsawwassen condos sibling files on disk.

### EXCLUDED — Tsawwassen First Nation leasehold townhouses (tenure trap, excluded with reason)

These are real townhouse-form homes a buyer will find, and every one is excluded from this file's City-side townhouse count for the same reason: **the land is Tsawwassen Lands under a 99-year prepaid leasehold structure, not City of Delta freehold or freehold-strata title** — different tenure, different governing law, different financing and resale handling. They are documented so a buyer is never told "there are no townhouses in Tsawwassen North" without the leasehold half of the picture.

- **Seaside (Seaside by Mosaic) — Orca Way (including 4638, 4656, 4716 and 4726 Orca Way addresses in MLS records).** MLS full reports record townhouse units in the Seaside complex in the Tsawwassen North board area, built 2019–2022, with **Title to Land: Leasehold prepaid-Strata**, strata plans EPS4960 and EPS5770 in the reports read. Leasehold prepaid-strata on Tsawwassen Lands — excluded from the freehold townhouse count. Sources: MLS full-report PDFs in search results, read 2026-11-01 (listing-sheet PDFs hosted at s.realtyninja.com).
- **Boardwalk, including Boardwalk6 — Tsawwassen First Nation, by Aquilini / TFSI Development.** Boardwalk6 is a collection of **22 parkside townhomes**; the presale source states the ownership form expressly: "Leasehold via a 99-year prepaid lease," with the First Nation landowners leasing to the developer, which subleases to homeowners for 99 years. The developer's own site repeats that homes at Boardwalk are purchased with a 99-year leasehold. Leasehold — excluded. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/ and https://ownboardwalk.com/
- **Tsawwassen Landing — 4738 Hemlock Way, by Onni Group.** A townhouse and single-family home community on Tsawwassen Lands (project sources place it on Tsawwassen Drive North / Hemlock Way, completed 2022 in the project listing read). It sits under Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction, not City of Delta zoning — excluded from City-side stock on the same leasehold/jurisdiction ground; a project-specific lease document for a given unit must be read before any tenure statement is made about that unit. https://condotrend.com/projects/tsawwassen-landing/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Tsawwassen Shores generally:** the wider TFN residential development (over 1,500 homes on leasehold land in a neighbourhood source's description, including townhouses) is leasehold product on treaty land — the general statement behind the three named exclusions above. https://www.michaellepore.ca/neighbourhood/tsawwassen/page/2/

### EXCLUDED — other forms that are never townhouse stock

Pad-rental park homes are never townhouse stock, and this park is also not 55+ and not in the City of Delta. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that decides everything in this sub-area:** City of Delta zoning and the BC Strata Property Act's freehold-strata regime govern the City side. On Tsawwassen Lands, Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction and land law apply instead, and the townhouse product there is held under 99-year prepaid leases (Boardwalk) or leasehold prepaid-strata plans (Seaside). A buyer must establish which side of the boundary a property sits on, and which tenure document they are actually buying, before pricing anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **City-side strata (when the 4800 Springs project, or any City-side strata townhouse, completes): the Form B regime.** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports (City-side strata):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata corporation established on or after July 1, 2024 and before July 1, 2027 must obtain its first report within two years of its first AGM — the timeline that will apply to any brand-new north-side strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (City-side strata):** Nov 24, 2022 — no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Whether and how that provincial rule interacts with a specific TFN leasehold project's own documents is a per-project legal question — do not assume the City-side answer carries over unchanged to a leasehold unit: UNCONFIRMED per project. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (City-side strata):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for any north-side complex, City-side or TFN-side. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified on the City side. A fee-simple townhouse/rowhouse would carry no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — diligence on title and municipal records only. The absence of that product on the City side, against leasehold product on the TFN side, is this sub-area's defining townhouse fact.
- **Delta plan (City side):** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated — no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former plan was Schedule D.1 under Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)," and Agricultural Land Commission rules apply to land in the ALR, including one-residence-per-parcel limits relevant to large lots at the farm edge. Any source attributing "Schedule D.1" to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted (City side):** the research and sibling files cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017) and its residential ladder (RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² subdivision minimums). The program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH (City side):** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta; six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). This applies to City-side detached/duplex lots, not to TFN Lands. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **ALR stratification bar (farm edges):** an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Farm-status thresholds run in three tiers — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. Sources: ALC Bulletin 09 and the ALCA, as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Flood (City side):** farm and ALR land lies between Tsawwassen and Ladner, and Delta is the diking authority operating over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. A flood construction level for any specific Springs-area lot: UNCONFIRMED. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Presales (REDMA) — live on the TFN side:** Boardwalk phases are sold as presales; a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — and for TFN product the disclosure package is also where the lease structure is spelled out. Read both together. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000), the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031), the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000), the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000), the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year) and CMHC premiums (0.6%–4.5%) are the standard BC/Canada frame for a City-side purchase. How each applies to a specific TFN leasehold purchase is a lawyer/notary question for that tenure — do not assume the freehold answer: UNCONFIRMED per tenure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Delta property taxes (City side):** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. Tax administration on a specific Tsawwassen Lands property is a separate question to confirm for that property. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the area research file for tsawwassen-north-guide, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any freehold townhouses in Tsawwassen North?** None verified as built stock on the City side: zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes. The City-side townhouse supply is one pipeline project at 4800 Springs Boulevard (QUEUE, tenure UNCONFIRMED). The built townhouse-form homes in the wider north-side search are TFN leasehold communities. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **Q: What am I actually buying at Boardwalk or Seaside, then?** A home under a 99-year prepaid lease structure on Tsawwassen Lands: Boardwalk's presale source states the ownership form as "Leasehold via a 99-year prepaid lease" (landowners lease to the developer, which subleases to homeowners), and Seaside's MLS reports record "Leasehold prepaid-Strata" title with strata plans EPS4960 / EPS5770. You are not buying City of Delta freehold title. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/boardwalk6-by-aquilini-townhomes/
- **Q: Does the 99-year lease run out in my lifetime, and can I get a mortgage?** The lease term stated in the sources is 99 years, prepaid. Mortgage availability, lease-remaining-term lender rules and resale handling for a specific TFN project are lender- and project-specific and were not verified in a primary source in this pass: UNCONFIRMED — take the project's lease and disclosure documents to your lender and lawyer before writing. https://ownboardwalk.com/
- **Q: Is Tsawwassen Springs a townhouse community?** No — its verified City-side buildings are apartment condos (5099, 5055 and 5011 Springs Boulevard) plus freehold detached houses. The townhouse project beside it, at 4800 Springs Boulevard, is a separate City application file and is not built stock. https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/about/team/ and http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a TFN leasehold townhouse?** Do not rely on the BC strata answer. City-side BC strata rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, but a TFN project's lease and project documents govern its own rental terms — project-specific and UNCONFIRMED in the sources read. Read the lease before assuming any rental right. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools will my kids attend from a north-side townhouse?** Possibly Ladner's. The SD37 locator result for 5000 Canoe Pass Way is Ladner Elementary and Delta Secondary — so a "Tsawwassen" address on the north side can carry a Ladner catchment, and the exact address (including TFN addresses) must be run in the locator before a school is promised. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is the manufactured-home community near the Mills a townhouse alternative?** No. Tsawwassen RV Resort (4761 Nulelum Way) is a pad-rental, land-lease manufactured-home community — you would own a home and rent its site, a different tenure again, and pad-rental parks are never counted as townhouse stock in this program. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83798-tsawwassen-rv-resort-4761-nulelum-way-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-4g9
- **Q: If I buy a Boardwalk presale, when can I change my mind?** Within REDMA's 7-day rescission window from the later of signing or acknowledging the developer's disclosure statement, with the deposit in trust. For TFN product, use that same window to have the lease structure reviewed — it is the cheapest diligence week you will ever get. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: How do I get downtown or to the ferry without a car?** Route 609 runs via 52 Street and Canoe Pass Way through Tsawwassen First Nation to Ladner Exchange, and route 601 serves South Delta Exchange toward Bridgeport Station; Highway 17 runs past the area to the BC Ferries terminal. Published ferry crossings: 1 hour 35 minutes to Swartz Bay, 2 hours to Duke Point. Road commute times: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf and https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
- **Q: Will the 4800 Springs Boulevard project be strata or fee-simple?** UNCONFIRMED — the City housing snapshot lists a townhouse project at that address without stating tenure, unit count or completion timing in the material read. Any agent quoting those details today is quoting something other than the City snapshot. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The treaty-land boundary is the single biggest townhouse fact on the north side.** Homes a few blocks apart sit under entirely different governments and tenures: City of Delta freehold/strata law on one side, Tsawwassen First Nation jurisdiction (724+ hectares under the Final Agreement) and 99-year leasehold product on the other. Establish which side a property is on before pricing tenure, servicing or resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **The Springs was built in phases over most of a decade, and the phases are different buildings:** listings date 5099 Springs Boulevard to 2011, 5055 to 2014 and 5011 (the developer's "Phase 3 Condo") to 2018 — all condos, which is why the Springs name alone never tells a buyer they are looking at a townhouse. https://www.tsawwassensprings.ca/page/40/?post_type=page&p=0
- **More City-side product is in the pipeline on the same street:** the townhouse project at 4800 Springs Boulevard (with a portion of 5133 Springs Boulevard) on the City housing projects map is the next phase of the Springs Boulevard corridor to check before paying a scarcity premium for TFN leasehold product on the ground that "nothing else is coming." http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/2026-01-15%20Delta%20Housing%20Snapshot.pdf
- **The bus goes through a First Nation to get to Ladner:** route 609's path via Canoe Pass Way passes through Tsawwassen First Nation — a daily reminder that the "north side" is a shared landscape of City land, TFN Lands and farm land, each with its own rules. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- **The urgent-care address buyers drive past is on Canoe Pass Way:** Fraser Health's South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way, on this side of Tsawwassen; the hospital is Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital
- **SSMUH is the quiet change on the older City-side streets south of Highway 17:** since Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (Jun 24, 2024), detached and duplex lots on the City side can carry up to four units — infill pressure to watch around the established detached streets, while the farm belt to Ladner stays ALR and TFN Lands follow TFN law, not the City bylaw. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
