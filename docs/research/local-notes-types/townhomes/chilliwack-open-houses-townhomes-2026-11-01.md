## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this topic file, this section carries the open-house frame first (verbatim, as the sibling topic files carry it), then the townhouse-purchase frame a buyer needs after the open house.

### The open-house frame

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018, except in the rarest of circumstances. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in British Columbia. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)

### The townhouse-purchase frame (after the open house)

- **Strata diligence — the Form B regime:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The open house shows the unit; the Form B shows the complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. An open-house host or feature sheet repeating a directory's "rentals not allowed" line for a Chilliwack townhouse complex is repeating unenforceable text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Confirm on each Form B whether a registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw before assuming who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** where a Chilliwack townhouse is genuinely fee-simple, it carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple complex is counted in this topic file (it counts none at all); a "no-strata townhouse" on the feed needs its tenure proved from title, not from the card. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan and zoning:** the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 5500, presenting the long-term vision and growth strategy for the whole city, including protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve; its Schedule B neighbourhood plans are B1 Agricultural Area Plan, B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, B3 Downtown, B4 Eastern Hillsides, B5 Sardis, B6 South Vedder and B7 Yarrow, and its Schedule D includes D-1 Drinking Water Supply, D-2 Hillside Environment, D-3 Riparian Area, D-4 Farm Home Plate and D-5 Hillside Development. Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 sets the urban residential zones, including R1-A (minimum lot area 500 m2 on community water and sewer, minimum lot width 15 m on the same services) and R1-C (300 m2 interior, 360 m2 corner). https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026 the City published a 5% late payment penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026; on a sale the buyer's and seller's lawyer or notary allocates the bill and the Home Owner Grant claim on the Statement of Adjustments. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhouse on the feed is a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST applies to new construction only, not to resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Chilliwack townhouse open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file Q1)
- **Q: How do I know the open house time on the page is current?** Each listing card shows an "OPEN HOUSE:" label with a day, a date and start and end times, but the research found one displayed card with no open-house label and could not confirm every card in the full feed carries one — check the page the day you plan to go and confirm with the listing brokerage before driving out, especially for outlying areas like Yarrow or Promontory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-open-houses.html (Research file Q2)
- **Q: Is there a 1-hour time error on the Chilliwack page?** No offset is documented in this topic's research file — it documents no card time block paired with a separate "Open House on…" line for Chilliwack, so no offset is claimed either way in this file. Confirm the time with the listing brokerage for any townhouse you are driving to. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/chilliwack-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file Q3)
- **Q: Which Chilliwack areas can I cover in one townhouse run?** Group by the City's own OCP neighbourhood plan areas — Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, Downtown, Eastern Hillsides, Sardis, South Vedder and Yarrow; Sardis-side areas cluster south of Highway 1 and Chilliwack Proper clusters north of it. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file Q4)
- **Q: If I buy after an open house in Chilliwack, when are property taxes due?** The City mails tax notices at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026 the City published a 5% penalty for taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026, and your lawyer or notary allocates the bill between buyer and seller on the Statement of Adjustments. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file Q5)
- **Q: The feature sheet says the townhouse complex bans rentals. Can I rent it out if I buy?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. What can still bind you: a short-term rental bylaw, and a 55+ age bylaw limiting who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I ask for after I like a townhouse at an open house?** The strata package behind the Form B: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024), financial statements, CRF balance and planned work — the items BCREA advises reviewing to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: How many Chilliwack townhouses hold open houses this weekend?** UNVERIFIED — no official source publishes open-house counts, SnapStats publishes no open-house category and Chilliwack has no SnapStats coverage at all. The feed itself is the count on the day. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-open-houses.html (Research file §2)
- **Q: Are the schools settled for the address I visited?** No — SD33 catchments are address-based through its School Locator; the district will review catchments for Watson Elementary, Unsworth Elementary, Tyson Elementary and Stitó:s Lá:lém Totí:lt beginning in fall 2026, and Cheam Elementary catchment changes are already approved effective September 2027.
