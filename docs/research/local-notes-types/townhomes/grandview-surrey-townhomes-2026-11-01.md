## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every counted Grandview strata complex in §2.1 and to Harlowe's strata on completion).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For Berkeley Village the Form B is also where the published rental/pet position (UNCONFIRMED in §2.1) is settled (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Every counted complex in §2.1 is over the threshold on its documented scale. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met for a Grandview complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged in-entry wherever a sibling records one (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No verified 55+ complex exists anywhere in Grandview on this program's sources, and Harlowe is explicitly NOT 55+ (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified for Grandview (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Surrey rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. The corridor research records the Surrey 2050 OCP update superseding earlier corridor designations following Council's December 2, 2024 endorsement of Corporate Report R246 (king-george-corridor research file §4). **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** one per unit, not a separate strata lot; habitable floor area not exceeding **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only — the owner must live at the property as their primary residence and tenants cannot run one; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals).
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).
