## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Citadel Pointe and every PoCo strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Citadel Pointe's registered bylaws are the diligence, not the directory line (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: both Citadel Pointe's directory line ("rentals allowed w/restrictions") and Shaughnessy Square's carry a restriction element that is **void for long-term rentals**; rentals are allowed, and the lines are carried only with that flag. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Port Coquitlam's own STR bylaw/licence position is UNVERIFIED — no City STR source was read in this file's research (research file §4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Citadel Pointe carries no age restriction in any source read; the city's only verified 55+ strata is Mayfair Terrace (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Port Coquitlam zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes — per the research file and the PoCo sibling files on disk.**

- **OCP: Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3838**, adopted in 2005 and substantially updated in 2013 to align with Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy; the City states it will undertake an OCP update to better align with Metro 2050 and provincial housing legislation, with 2024–2025 work largely in response to that legislation, including SSMUH accommodations and defined Transit Oriented Areas (research file §4; https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan).
- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630**, as amended for SSMUH: the amendment summary renames the RS zones "Residential Small-Scale," permits up to 4 dwelling units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, replaces "coach house" with "accessory dwelling unit" (ADU floor area limit 90 m², excluded from FAR), and sets the secondary suite cap at **40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling unit**, with suites permitted in a house, duplex or triplex where units are vertically separated. **Whether that amendment package is the in-force text of Bylaw No. 3630 today is UNVERIFIED** — the summary read is a bylaw-amendment attachment, and the consolidated bylaw read carries the suite definition without the full SSMUH schedule; confirm the specific lot with the City (research file §4; https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157). A secondary-suite owner-occupancy rule for Port Coquitlam is UNVERIFIED — no owner-occupancy clause was read (research file §4). Listing claims of SSMUH/coach-house futures (as in the Salisbury Avenue listing text) are listing copy, not adopted facts (research file §1).
- **Property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026; penalties are set by provincial legislation and the City cannot waive or modify them (research file §4; https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug notes some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs, and no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the Port Coquitlam page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Port Coquitlam is not Vancouver.
- **Presales / REDMA.** 7-day rescission under s. 21 and deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract (Luxora, in the condos roster) (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
