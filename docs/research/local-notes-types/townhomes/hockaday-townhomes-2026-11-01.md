## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The provincial small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules, so property-level septic or well status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
