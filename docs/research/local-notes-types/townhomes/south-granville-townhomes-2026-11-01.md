## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this corridor the Form B is also the fastest way to prove a building is a strata at all — the rental towers in §2 have no Form B to give. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. The Pintura townhouse component's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so confirm its position from the Form B package (the development as a whole, apartments plus townhouses, is the strata whose report matters). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The unnamed 933 West 8th Avenue listing's "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" line is flagged on exactly this basis in §2 — it cannot amount to a long-term ban. The reverse trap also applies: a rental *building* (The Leeward, Santa Monica) is not made buyable by this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-children exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No building counted for this ground is published as 55+ in a townhome file — the corridor's published 55+ building, Terraces on 7th, is Fairview's apartment stock (cross-referenced in §2), and its mandatory service agreement is a contract cost on top of strata fees wherever it is bought. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse stock is verified in this corridor: a fee-simple purchase on the side streets is a detached or character house — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants or heritage designations only. The Heritage project's three houses are designated protected heritage property — heritage rules ride on top of whichever tenure title shows.
- **City zoning and plan:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. Side-street house lots sit under the citywide **R1-1** multiplex regime created when **By-law No. 13817** replaced the old RS districts — up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule; the Granville Street frontage sits under commercial / mixed-use districts, exact schedule per parcel UNVERIFIED; a corridor strata example, The Avedon, is recorded in its listing as zoned RM-3. The **Broadway Plan** (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 2024) contemplates 30–40 storeys near South Granville Station — but the Plan is policy, not zoning: most higher-density forms still require rezoning, and purpose-built rental buildings in the corridor carry tenant-relocation implications under the Plan's renter protections. Citywide direction otherwise comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan (replaced the Vancouver Plan March 31, 2026). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY:** 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to this corridor's second-home and investor stock. It does not apply in Burnaby or anywhere outside the City. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City property taxes (2026):** advance taxes due February 3, 2026, main taxes July 3, 2026, 5% penalty on unpaid balances; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (the Pintura townhouses are 2005 resales — no GST).
