## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no Form B at Kensington Gardens — it is undivided-interest ownership, not strata (Section 2). Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are at or over the threshold (Cassia 60 homes, Hilltop Greene 32, 388 Ellesmere exactly 5). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line was published for any counted complex in the sources checked, so there is no in-entry line to flag in Section 2 — if an older bylaw or directory line surfaces for any of them, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. (The neighbouring Alto apartment building's directory line "rentals allowed with restrictions" is read the same way in the sibling condos file.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined spouses/partners and children). No 55+ bylaw is published for any counted complex in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED per complex; confirm on the Form B. The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ ownership complex in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none are verified on Capitol Hill, but the rule is stated plainly for any non-strata row product a buyer meets on the hill's house streets: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) governs — never the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Hastings corridor below the hill is planned as the Heights Urban Village. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Centrale presale), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for the Centrale apartment presale at the sub-area's west edge. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Burnaby tax timing:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% after the September due date. Strata owners pay through their own tax notice; at Kensington Gardens the maintenance fee itself includes property tax, per listings — a co-ownership tell, not a strata feature. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md)
