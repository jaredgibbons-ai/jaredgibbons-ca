## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Coal Harbour is the waterfront neighbourhood on the north side of the downtown peninsula, on Burrard Inlet between the downtown core and Stanley Park. Stand-alone boundary roads for Coal Harbour: UNVERIFIED — the City does not publish a stand-alone Coal Harbour boundary in a text source read for the research file. The containing Downtown area is a peninsula bounded by Burrard Inlet on the north, False Creek and Yaletown to the south, the West End and Stanley Park to the west, and Gastown, Chinatown and Strathcona to the east. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx
- Governing plan: the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754), which states Coal Harbour should be an integral part of downtown Vancouver; parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 together with that plan. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Main streets: West Georgia Street, West Hastings Street, West Cordova Street, Burrard Street and Thurlow Street; the Official Development Plan lists public street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets to the water. Search names in the research file include West Cordova Street, West Hastings Street, Broughton Street, Nicola Street, Cardero Street and Jervis Street. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Where the housing stock concentrates: almost entirely strata apartment form in residential towers along the waterfront and on West Cordova Street, West Hastings Street and the downtown street grid extended into Coal Harbour under the Official Development Plan, with ground-oriented townhouse homes at the base of residential buildings as part of the plan form; single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- Anchors: Coal Harbour Community Centre, 480 Broughton Street; Harbour Green Park on the waterfront; Devonian Harbour Park, 1929 West Georgia Street, described by the Park Board as a gateway between Stanley Park and Coal Harbour; the Official Development Plan requires the waterfront walkway system to be continuous along the Coal Harbour waterfront. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/community-and-cultural-centres.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no standalone townhouse complex to diligence here — the diligence is tower diligence.** A Coal Harbour townhouse-form unit is a strata lot in an apartment tower, so the full apartment-strata package governs: Form B Information Certificate under Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In the hotel-mixed towers (Fairmont Pacific Rim, Paradox) the Form B is also where the residential strata's separation from the hotel component shows. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every Coal Harbour tower strata is far over the threshold; a strata of four or fewer lots sits under that threshold, and none is verified here. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Coal Harbour building is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in Coal Harbour; age bylaws for the tower stock: UNCONFIRMED per building — check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified in Coal Harbour. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and registered covenants/HOA documents only. No Coal Harbour product verified in this pass takes that form.
- **City zoning and plans:** Coal Harbour parcels sit under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 together with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754). The DD schedule permits commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan, with development permits approved by the Development Permit Board or the Director of Planning; a minimum lot size figure for DD parcels: UNVERIFIED in the schedule. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Vancouver's house-zone multiplex framework does not create Coal Harbour townhouse stock — there are no house lots in this sub-area; the plan form is tower strata under the DD schedule and the Official Development Plan above. A suite or multiplex claim for a Coal Harbour address: UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's CD-1/DD documents are read.
- **Floodplain:** Coal Harbour is on the Burrard Inlet waterfront, and the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain — parcels in it are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; whether a specific Coal Harbour parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. Ground-level townhouse-form units are the lots closest to that check. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property tax calendar and additional school tax:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. At Coal Harbour price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Coal Harbour stock is resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any real townhouse complexes in Coal Harbour?** None verified as standalone complexes — the research pass and this pass verified zero named standalone townhouse complexes. What exists is townhouse-form units inside tower developments: Cascina (30 townhouses), Denia (4), Escala (7), Carina (17 adjacent), and townhouse homes at One and Two Harbour Green — all counted as apartment stock in the condos program. https://aspac.ca/project-category/past-project/
- **Q: A listing calls a Coal Harbour unit a "townhouse." What am I actually buying?** A strata lot in an apartment tower — MLS sub-type "Townhouse" describes the unit's two-level, ground-oriented layout, but built form governs: your strata is the tower, with its depreciation report, CRF and levy history. Read the tower's Form B package, not a townhouse-complex package. https://vancouvernashdom.com/vancouver-west-coal-harbour-townhouses/SearchResults.form/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Coal Harbour townhouse-form unit long-term?** Under provincial law, strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a listing still prints one. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw and are separately licensed by the City — and the Empty Homes Tax declaration is a separate annual filing every owner must make. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is the Empty Homes Tax hit if I leave a Coal Harbour home empty?** 3% of assessed value for a property deemed vacant, on top of the annual declaration duty — Vancouver only; the City's 2025 annual report describes the 3% rate as consistent and stable. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate filing. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org
- **Q: Are the harbour views protected?** Partly, by plan: the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan lists public street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets to the water, and requires the waterfront walkway system to be continuous. A specific unit's view is still a unit-by-unit check, not a plan guarantee. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Q: Is Coal Harbour part of the West End?** No — Coal Harbour sits inside the City's Downtown local area with its own Official Development Plan and its own Paragon sub-area; the West End is a separate official local area. The City's areas page links the Coal Harbour locality name to the West End page, which is a linking quirk, not geography. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Is there a school in Coal Harbour?** The VSB states a new elementary school in Coal Harbour was completed and added elementary capacity in downtown Vancouver (named the Seaside school in Coal Harbour on the VSB site); its street address is UNVERIFIED in the research pass, no public secondary school inside Coal Harbour was confirmed, and catchments are address-based — run the building's exact address in the VSB locator. Elsie Roy and Crosstown elementaries are in Yaletown and Crosstown, not Coal Harbour. https://www.vsb.bc.ca/capital-planning
- **Q: What extra taxes hit a high-end Coal Harbour purchase?** Beyond standard PTT (with the further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million), the Province's additional school tax applies on assessed value above $3 million (0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026), and the annual Empty Homes Tax declaration applies to every unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Why do sources disagree on Carina's and Escala's unit counts?** Because the published sources genuinely differ: Carina is recorded as 93 units in total by one building source and as 86 apartments plus 17 townhomes by another, and Escala as 98 suites by its developer Aspac and 89 units by a secondary source. Both conflicts are flagged in the sibling condos file — the strata plan and the Form B are the settling documents. https://aspac.ca/project-category/past-project/
- **Q: What market stats can I rely on for Coal Harbour townhouses?** None from SnapStats — Vancouver is outside its coverage in Jared's FVR + GVR September 2026 reports, SnapStats is never cited for Vancouver in this program, and no substitute source is used in this file. Sub-area inventory, solds and medians are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/coal-harbour.md)
