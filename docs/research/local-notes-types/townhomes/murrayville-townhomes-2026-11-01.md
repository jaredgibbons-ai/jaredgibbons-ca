## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every complex in §2.1 — all are strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — the "rentals not allowed" line recorded for 21937 48 Avenue (§2.1) is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). This is the exact point Cedar Crest Estates turns on (§2.1) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Not applicable to the counted roster (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Township of Langley zoning, plans, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs Murrayville (research file §4; condos sibling §3). **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water AND sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at 280 m² or smaller or 4 units between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is **NO 6-plex tier** in the Township (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **Suites and coach houses:** a Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102); a coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 unit, never strata (condos sibling §3). R-CL s. 403 context verified elsewhere in this program: 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot figures (condos sibling §3).
- **Township taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026 (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

**Q: Is Murrayville in Langley City or the Township?**
A: The Township. It sits on both sides of the Fraser Highway immediately east of the City of Langley, so Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, Township SSMUH Bylaw 6020 and Township tax dates apply — not the City's bylaws (§§1, 3).

**Q: What should I read before buying a Murrayville townhome?**
A: The Form B, the registered bylaws and rules, two years of minutes and the depreciation report. Murrayville's counted stock spans 1988–2019 builds, so the depreciation report is where the older complexes are actually priced (§3).

**Q: Can I rent out a Murrayville townhome?**

**Q: How many units can an eligible Murrayville lot hold under SSMUH?**
A: 3 units at 280 m² or smaller, 4 units between 280 m² and 4,050 m², if the lot is inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated and not in a Transit-Oriented Area. There is no 6-unit tier in the Township (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve a Murrayville townhome?**

A: No. Murrayville House, Autumn Ridge and Eaglecrest are apartment-condo buildings, counted in the condos sibling (3 buildings) and documented in §2.3 so they are not confused with the townhouse shelf.

**Q: Is there a fee-simple townhome option in Murrayville?**
A: None verified. Every counted Murrayville townhouse complex records Freehold Strata title; a fee-simple purchase here would be a detached house or a coach-house unit, not a townhouse complex (§2.2).
