## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata frame — stated for completeness, because no strata townhouse exists here to apply it to:** if a Stave Falls property were a strata townhouse, the diligence would be the Form B under Strata Property Act s. 59 (monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years since July 1, 2024 for strata corporations with five or more lots (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); a CRF minimum of 10% of the annual operating budget since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; and only 55+ age bylaws lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). **Fee-simple reality for this sub-area:** the acreage stock carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants only. Do not import strata expectations onto a Stave Falls acreage purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **City plan — OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026:** the current Official Community Plan is the municipal frame for Stave Falls, together with the City's Rural Mission Strategy, which provides direction for the City's rural communities including Stave Falls and is now finalized (its Stave Falls-specific recommendations are UNVERIFIED in this file). **The guide page's FAQ states the City adopted Bylaw 6405-2026 "on June 1, 2026" — that date is WRONG and is documented in THIS file's own research (§5): April 21, 2026 is correct.** Mission's Lougheed Highway Corridor Plan is IN PREPARATION, NOT adopted — Council endorsed Focused Land Use Option #2 on July 20, 2026 — so no corridor-plan adoption is claimed for the Highway 7 approach to this area. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/stave-falls-mission-guide.md)
- **Zoning and secondary dwellings:** Mission's Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 divides the rural area into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s); a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size, and City application material for the Stave Falls area confirms RU16-to-RU16s rezonings are the route used to permit a secondary detached unit on a rural lot outside the Urban Growth Boundary on land designated Rural Residential. Any Stave Falls parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/stave-falls-mission-guide.md)
- **Secondary suites:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); **suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes**; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/stave-falls-mission-guide.md)
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; Stave Falls' rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Stave Falls lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/stave-falls-mission-guide.md)
- **City property taxes — FLAT 10% penalty:** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026**. That is the whole penalty: a flat 10% on unpaid taxes after July 2 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235). Stave Falls is inside the city, so this city regime applies — the staged 5% + 5% RURAL regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11) applies in FVRD electoral areas, never here, and the two regimes are never mixed in this file. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/stave-falls-mission-guide.md)
- **Servicing, wells and septic:** the guide feed's own listing text repeatedly references drilled wells and septic systems on acreage lots — listing copy only; parcel-level servicing (City water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED. On ALR parcels the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)).
