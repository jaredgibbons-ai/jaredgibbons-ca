## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** both verified Annieville townhouse complexes are strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified here; Annieville's duplex and detached product (e.g. Annieville Heights) is the freehold side of the area, and "freehold strata" in a listing is still strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** on a resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59) — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any "rentals not allowed" line attached to an Annieville complex in a directory or older bylaw set — the Delta Cedars directory line next door is the local example — is void in-entry. Muse's directory records rentals allowed in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for either townhouse complex in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400, applies — no lettered schedules (the North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous Bylaw No. 3950). Annieville-specific items are the ND3 Annieville Development Permit Area for the River Road village and the ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area on the river frontage, both in Appendix B of Bylaw No. 8400; the OCP states the ND1 area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research file applies Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 parcel-by-parcel (zone sheets read include RD3 at a 4,000 m² minimum lot, RS6 at 390 m² and RS1 at 4,000 m²), but the program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS6.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — Delta's small-scale multi-unit rules allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the bylaw applies; relevant to Annieville's detached lots, not to the two strata complexes. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST/HST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; PTT (first-time-buyer exemption on the first $500,000 at values $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. Both Section 2 complexes are resales, so GST does not apply to the purchase price itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services. Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Muse unit a condo or a townhouse?** Both exist at 9015 120 Street under one complex name and one strata — listings show apartment units and townhouse units at the same address, and the townhouse listing for unit 104 states Title to Land: Freehold Strata. Confirm the specific unit's form on its listing and the strata plan; do not rely on the complex name alone. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street and https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3099623-9015-120-street-104-delta-bc-v4c-6r7/brochure
- **Q: How old is Muse, really — 2017 or 2019?** Sources conflict: the building directory says 2017 and multiple MLS listings say 2019. Treat the year as UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan or Form B settles it; the complex is late-2010s either way. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **Q: Are the Delta Gardens townhouses really in Annieville?** Their own listing says so — unit 1 at 8447 115 Street is classified Annieville with Subdivision Name Delta Gardens — but the wider complex is classified Nordel and Scottsdale in other listings, and the former plan map puts Annieville's east edge at 116 Street. The registered strata plan, not the feed label, is the stable identifier. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/8447-115-street/1
- **Q: Can I rent out an Annieville townhouse?** A strata cannot enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw — those are void since November 24, 2022 — though short-term rentals can still be banned, and Muse's directory records rentals allowed in any case. Read the registered bylaws and the Form B for the specific complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Muse: the directory records pets allowed with restrictions. Delta Gardens townhouses: UNCONFIRMED from the sources checked. Pet bylaws remain enforceable in BC strata, so confirm the registered bylaw set before buying with a pet. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **Q: Is there any 55+ townhouse in Annieville?** None verified. The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ ownership stock in the sub-area, and no source states an age restriction for either townhouse complex — step-free ground-level layout at Delta Gardens is a design feature, not an age bylaw. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/8447-115-street/1 (sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/annieville-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What do strata fees cover, and where do I check them?** The current fee for a specific unit is stated on the Form B, which also discloses amounts owing, approved levies and the CRF balance; listings publish a fee for individual units (the Muse townhouse listing and the Delta Gardens listing each publish one) but the Form B is the verified figure at purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Where do I park and store things at Muse?** The townhouse listing for unit 104 records underground parking, and the directory lists storage among the complex amenities, with in-suite laundry — stall and locker allocations for a specific unit are confirmed on the Form B, not the listing. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3099623-9015-120-street-104-delta-bc-v4c-6r7/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/muse-north-delta-9015-120-street
- **Q: Which schools serve the Scott Road townhouses?** Catchments are address-based in SD37 and must be run in the locator for the specific address; the research file's Annieville sample (9240 112 Street, a detached address) returned Annieville Elementary and Delview Secondary — do not carry that result across to 120 Street addresses without running them. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Does the Fraser River escarpment hazard affect these complexes?** The ND1 Fraser River Escarpment Development Permit Area covers Annieville's river frontage and the OCP lists mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence for that area; the two townhouse complexes are on the Scott Road / 84 Avenue side, away from the frontage — whether either parcel sits in a permit area is a lot-specific check, UNCONFIRMED here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Annieville's townhouse stock hides at its edges:** neither complex appears in City-source research at all — both surfaced only in MLS listing data and a building directory — because the City's own geography describes Annieville as a detached/duplex neighbourhood and the former plan map puts its east edge at 116 Street, west of both complexes. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf
- **One complex, three sub-areas:** Delta Gardens is classified Nordel, Scottsdale and Annieville across different listings for the same development — the apartment building at 11501 84 Avenue in one feed, the townhouses at 8447 115 Street in another. Search all three names or you will miss half the resales. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/8447-115-street/1
- **The River Road infill pattern is the area's other housing story:** a City bylaw notice for 10604 River Road proposed rezoning from RD3 to RS6 for a two-lot subdivision under Bylaw No. 7600 — large river-side lots splitting into small lots is how Annieville adds homes, not townhouse buildings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/224579/
- **Annieville Elementary sits inside the neighbourhood but may not be your catchment:** for the sample address 9240 112 Street the SD37 locator returns Annieville Elementary (K–7), at that same address, and Delview Secondary (8–12), 9111 116 Street — east-side and Scott Road addresses can fall elsewhere, and catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **The neighbourhood park is mid-plan:** Annieville Lions Park, 9150 112 Street, sits beside Annieville Elementary, and the City has an Annieville Lions Park Master Plan project — the park beside the school is a live City project file, not a finished fact. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/annieville-lions-park
- **The nearest library is technically in the next sub-area:** George Mackie Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library), 8440 112th Street, is just east in Nordel, and the North Delta Recreation Centre at 11415 84 Avenue serves North Delta from Nordel — the Delta Gardens townhouse listing itself markets a 1-minute walk to that recreation centre. https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/8447-115-street/1
