## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block — stated for contrast, because it does NOT apply to a poultry farm purchase.**

- **No strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees on a poultry farm.** A farm parcel is fee-simple: diligence is title, ALR status, zoning, floodplain, farm classification, barn/environmental compliance and the quota position — not a Form B package. For completeness, the strata rules that govern urban townhouses elsewhere in this program are: Form B under SPA s. 59 (within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page, with fees, levies, CRF balance, insurance, budget and depreciation report); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold) and a 10% CRF minimum since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; and only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). None of those instruments exists for a fee-simple farm parcel.

**The farm frame — the rules that actually decide this topic.**

- **ALR housing frame (ALC, carried from the sibling passes):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences, and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **ALR subdivision:** strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision under the framework addressed in ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 (direct-read confirmed Nov 1, 2026), and subdivision of ALR land generally requires ALC approval — the live page's FAQ statement to that effect is consistent with this frame, while its "rarely granted" characterization is the page's own wording (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-poultry-farms-for-sale.html).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — full three-tier thresholds (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met):** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; **$2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha** if the operation is greater than 4 ha; **$10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**. Classification is voluntary and property-by-property; applications must be submitted to BC Assessment by **October 31** for consideration for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. The farm-class position of Langley poultry farms as a group is UNVERIFIED — no source opened in this program classifies them as a group (research file §§4, 5; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Township of Langley frame:** SSMUH under Bylaw 6020 — the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw carries **NO 6-plex tier**; the secondary suite cap is 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500, never strata — with a live Township conflict flagged, not resolved: the s. 102 text as of July 2025 against the January 2025 guide. R-CL (s. 403) carries a 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum. None of those instruments turns an ALR farm residence into townhouse stock — the ALR stratification bar above governs first (program standing Township frame; research file §4 for the ALR position).
