## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted stratas):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Timberlane, the Form B and strata plan are the only documents that settle its form conflict (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any Hope complex in §2; Timberlane's listing copy records "rentals allowed," which states the post-2022 position in any event. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Hope strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — the town's 55+ product is rental and care (§2), and its verified 55+ ownership count is zero. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Hope's detached stock is fee-simple on that basis; its named townhouse complexes are strata, so a buyer importing fee-simple expectations into a Riverwynd or Dogwood purchase is misreading the tenure.
- **District plan and zoning:** the current plan is **Official Community Plan Bylaw 1617, 2025, adopted February 9, 2026**, with a companion updated Zoning Bylaw; the OCP and Zoning Bylaw were updated together to meet current provincial housing requirements, and the 2024 SSMUH amendments allow 3 units on parcels under 280 m² and 4 on larger parcels in former 1–2-unit zones. https://hope.ca/official-community-plan-and-zoning-bylaw (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- **Flood — the District's first diligence item:** the Province's design flood profile for the Fraser River is anchored to the Hope gauging station (the 1894 flood is the design event, treated in updated frequency analysis as roughly a 500-year event), and new provincial floodplain maps covering the Coquihalla and Fraser rivers from Yale to Mission were released in June 2026. The District administers Coquihalla River dikes — River Parade Flood Protection (0.93 km) and Glenhalla Subdivision (1.01 km), both regulated — and construction in hazard areas needs a Flood and Erosion Hazards Development Permit with a satisfactory qualified-professional report (the route Council applied Jan. 13, 2025 at 62180 Delair Road). Parcel-level flood construction levels and any diking/drainage parcel charge: UNVERIFIED. Pull the parcel's flood status before pricing or writing — a dike nearby does not remove the permit step. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026WLRS0029-000715 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/air-land-water/water/integrated-flood-hazard-mgmt/dikes_listed_by_riverwatercoursepdf.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
- **Tax and levy notes:** District of Hope municipal taxes plus FVRD levies (recreation is an FVRD service here); the Annual Tax Rate Bylaw for 2025 is No. 1601, 2025, and Council's finance report (April 28, 2025) finalized the 2025 increase at 2.81% for items the District taxes directly. The July 2 due date for property taxes and residential utilities rests on District notices carried in the local paper and could not be confirmed on a District page in the research pass: UNVERIFIED. Utility fees (water/sewer/solid waste) ride on the tax notice, with 5% + 5% late penalties under Utility Bylaw No. 1153 — a utility-bylaw penalty, not a municipal tax-penalty scheme; do not quote it as one. http://hope.ca/p/tax-rates (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/hope.md)
