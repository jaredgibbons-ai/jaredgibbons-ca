## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata cross-reference in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this band the Form B, not the listing and not the live page's copy, settles fee, levy and deductible exposure — the live page's strata insurance-deductible ranges were not confirmed on an official source and are not repeated here as fact (see the page-defects item below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every cross-referenced complex in §2 sits over that threshold on its verified scale; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER it. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Directory "rentals not allowed" lines attached to Abbotsford buildings in this program's rosters (for example Regency Park, College Park at 33728 King Road, Lakewood Gardens and Brookhollow Estates in the condos sibling) are void in exactly this sense — flagged in-entry in that sibling and carried here as the governing rule for any townhouse directory line of the same kind. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). - **The band has no official statistics at all.** No count, share, median, days-on-market or sale-to-list figure exists for Abbotsford homes under $800,000 — or for Abbotsford townhouses alone — in any source used here: SnapStats publishes neither a price-band cut nor a form-only cut. - **Almost half the city's attached pool is in one sub-area.** Central Abbotsford carried 256 of Abbotsford's 572 attached listings and 35 of its 58 attached sales in September 2026 — the largest pool and the largest sales count of any Abbotsford sub-area — followed by Abbotsford West (210 listings, 16 sold); the 13-month attached median trend ran $495,200 (September 2025) to $497,500 (September 2026), ranging between $425,000 and $575,000 across the period. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/abbotsford-condos-under-800k.md)
- **One project holds both forms this page type keeps separate.** Highstreet Village is 131 townhomes **and** 9 condominium buildings on one 12-acre site (developer source) — a feed unit there can be either form, and the program counts the townhomes in the West townhomes file and the buildings in the condos files, never merged. https://highstreetvillage.ca/ (§2 above)
