## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 8 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 13-unit complex like Cambie Gardens, the Form B is effectively the whole financial picture. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in Section 2 carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line; the Sausalito and Capistrano directory lines read "rentals allowed with restrictions," and any restriction behind that wording is likewise void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No counted complex is verified 55+; the sibling 55-plus file checked Cambie Gardens and Cambie Court specifically and found no age restriction stated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in East Cambie. For reference, a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Do not describe any Section 2 complex that way; all eight are strata.
- **Richmond zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs — the area's base zone is Single Detached (RS1/RS2) under Section 8.1, and the RS1/E subzone documented on Cambie Road sets a minimum lot area of 550.0 m², minimum width 18.0 m and minimum depth 24.0 m, with one principal dwelling per lot and a secondary suite as a permitted secondary use. Townhouse sites proceed under low-density townhouse zones (the RTL4 rezoning record in Section 2 is the worked example) and multiple-family sites are Development Permit Areas under the Area Plan. The East Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11B to the Official Community Plan (adopted October 21, 2002; Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874) under the city-wide 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **SSMUH / suites:** the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood — the Richmond-specific diligence item:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies; Richmond sits at an average of 1 metre above sea level behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way, and the Area Plan requires new development in East Cambie to provide adequate flood protection. Strata insurance and FCL compliance are building-specific checks on any East Cambie strata purchase. https://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Richmond money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026; annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date, and the typical metered single-family utility total for 2026 is $2,251 per year. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this area; the frame for any future corridor presale sold under the Area Plan's anticipated densities is disclosure under REDMA s. 18 and a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, with deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What strata can I actually buy in East Cambie?** Townhouses: eight verified strata complexes — Cambie Gardens (11331 Cambie Road, 13 duplex-style townhomes, built 2005), Wembley (4991 No. 5 Road, by Citimark, built 2020, 109 townhomes per the presale record), Normandie (11571 Thorpe Road, 77 units), Cambie Court (12311 Cambie Road), and the Polygon-era McNeely / Jack Bell pocket: Sausalito, Francisco Village, Capistrano and Malibu. No apartment condos and no fee-simple townhouse complex are verified in the area. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Cambie Gardens a condo building?** No — its own listing describes "an exclusive collection of just 13 duplex-style, two-level townhomes." Feeds that file it under "condos" are using the word loosely; the dwelling type is Townhouse, tenure freehold strata (plan BCS1148 per the sibling program). https://www.zolo.ca/richmond-real-estate/11331-cambie-road/1
- **Q: Sausalito — is it 1993 or 1994, and does it matter?** Listings say 1993, a building directory says 1994; this file follows the listings. It matters only for warranty-era and depreciation-report expectations — the strata's own documents, not either published year, govern. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3075752-12311-mcneely-drive-48-richmond-bc-v6v-2s2/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out an East Cambie townhouse?** For a long-term tenancy, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Directory lines reading "rentals allowed with restrictions" (Sausalito, Capistrano) cannot restrict a long-term rental today; a short-term rental bylaw can still bind you — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does flood risk affect a townhouse purchase here?** Yes, structurally: all of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204 with flood construction levels for habitable floors, behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 event with raising to 4.7 m under way. Ask for the complex's FCL compliance and insurance terms rather than assuming from the map. https://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve an East Cambie townhouse?** Schools physically in the area: Mitchell Elementary (3800 No. 5 Road), Kathleen McNeely Elementary (12440 Woodhead Road) and H.J. Cambie Secondary (4151 Jacombs Road); SD38 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). Listings place Cambie Gardens in the Mitchell / Cambie catchments and Sausalito in the McNeely / Cambie catchments — run every address in the SD38 locator, because both elementaries sit inside the area. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The plan is older than the townhouses.** The East Cambie Area Plan was adopted October 21, 2002 (Land Use Map updated by Bylaw 9874) and already anticipated corridor townhouse/apartment densities — Cambie Gardens (2005) and Wembley (2020) are that 2002 plan arriving, a generation apart. The City also began a further OCP update in fall 2024 toward a 2050 horizon, so the plan framework over any new strata here is mid-revision. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Four of the eight complexes share one builder era.** Sausalito, Francisco Village, Capistrano and Malibu are all early-1990s Polygon-era gated townhouse complexes in the McNeely / Jack Bell pocket — same vintage means the same depreciation-report questions (roofs, decks, exterior systems) at every one of them; Capistrano listings already note a new roof in 2025 and extensive exterior/deck repairs. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3114874-42-12871-jack-bell-drive-richmond-british-columbia-v6v2t8-1038000/brochure
- **The area's biggest park is a bog inside the ALR.** Richmond Nature Park, 11851 Westminster Highway, is 100+ acres of old bog forest with the Nature House interpretive centre, the Area Plan conserves it as a bog environment, and the plan also protects Bath Slough as a natural watercourse — the "green edge" beside the townhouse corridors is protected bog, not future development land. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **Mitchell Elementary carries a heritage ask.** The Area Plan asks the School Board to conserve the Mitchell School buildings as heritage structures, and Mitchell is the longest continuously operating school in the Richmond public system, named after Richmond pioneer Alex Mitchell — relevant context for any redevelopment expectation near the Cambie / No. 5 node. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=558
- **King George / Cambie Community Park is the plan's anchor park.** At 4100 No. 5 Road, with heritage trees, courts, playgrounds, tennis and a waterpark, the Area Plan calls it the principal community park, to be retained and expanded on its present site — the amenity the Thorpe Road and No. 5 Road townhouse stock actually lives beside. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
- **The bridge in the plan has two names.** The Area Plan text calls the Alex Fraser Bridge the "Annacis Bridge" — older planning documents, listing copy and local speech for this area use both names for the same Highway 91 crossing that gives East Cambie its Delta and Surrey access. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/east-cambie.md)
