## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. For the two 1970s Wolfe Road complexes, the Form B is also where the strata plan behind each address is confirmed (see the Parkside Gardens overlap flag in §2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Parkside Gardens (42 units on its data sheet) and the 45185 Wolfe Road complex (unit numbers to 120) are plainly over the threshold on their published scales; Townsend Terrace's and Centre Field's lot counts are UNCONFIRMED — the 5+ test for those two is confirmed on the Form B/strata plan. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the 4 is evidenced as such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. A 1970s complex's report (roof, cladding and window renewal cycles — one current listing credits the 45185 Wolfe Road complex with updated cladding, windows and roof) is where its fee level is explained. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Parkside Gardens data-sheet line "Rentals Allowed w/Rest." is flagged in-entry on exactly this rule: any long-term rental-restriction component is void; only a short-term-rental restriction can survive. In Chilliwack, short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted complex here is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

On a new townhouse elsewhere, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Does the hospital precinct change what a townhouse is worth?** Unproven as a claim. Chilliwack General Hospital (45600 Menholm Road) anchors this side of the city, but no official source quantifies a price or rental effect around it — treat proximity claims as marketing until deal evidence says otherwise. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **This market runs on a different board's numbers — or none (2026-11-01).** Chilliwack trades on CADREB, SnapStats does not cover the city, and CADREB publishes board-wide figures only. Any listing copy quoting a "Chilliwack Proper West median" is quoting a number no published source in this program produces. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **All of the sub-area's verified townhouses share one street and one park.** Townsend Terrace, Centre Field, Parkside Gardens and the 45185 Wolfe Road complex all stand on Wolfe Road across from Townsend Park — a buyer comparing "Chilliwack Proper West townhouses" is comparing four stratas on one corridor, with fees, depreciation reports and pet bylaws as the real differences. (§2 above)
- **One Wolfe Road complex has no citable name.** The 45185 Wolfe Road townhouse complex (built 1976) is well documented in listings, but the only name for it in the sources checked sits on a source this program never cites — so the complex is identified by address alone here, its listing-copy name ("Townsend Greens") is held UNCONFIRMED with that disclosure, and a buyer searching by that name may not find this file's entry. (§2 above)
- **The oldest complex on the corridor carries its plan number in the open.** Parkside Gardens' MLS data sheet prints Strata Plan NWS54, 42 units in strata, self-managed, Flood Plain: Yes — a 1971 strata whose diligence is its depreciation report and its flood position, not its listing photos. https://www.cotala.com/tours/77950/FullPublic.pdf?i=505822823
- **Chilliwack's suite cap is a "lesser of" test, not a flat number.** A secondary suite must be both under 90 m² AND under 40% of the building's habitable floor space, inside a building with only one principal dwelling — and the one-ADU-per-lot rule means a legal suite forecloses a coach house on the same lot. Sellers marketing "suite + coach house potential" are marketing two units the bylaw counts as one slot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/chilliwack-proper-west.md)
- **The tax penalty calendar is Chilliwack's own.** 5% after July 2, 2026 and a second 5% after August 4, 2026 — a different second-stage date than the rural regime's October 31 and a different structure than Mission's flat 10%. Cross-river comparisons in listing copy routinely get this wrong. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **The downtown condo sits inside a form-and-character permit area.** OCP Bylaw 5500's Schedule D-6 makes the whole downtown a Form & Character Development Permit Area — exterior alterations and redevelopment around The Robson run through that lens, not just the zoning bylaw; and the plan citations that matter for this city are Bylaw 5500 with Schedule B1 (Agricultural Plan) and Schedule B5 (Sardis), never a stale "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" attribution. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8154
- **The hospital is the area's true anchor, and it is a Fraser Health community hospital** — primary, core secondary and some specialty services at 45600 Menholm Road, on the Hodgins Avenue side of the Proper West ground, minutes from the Wolfe Road strata stock. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital
