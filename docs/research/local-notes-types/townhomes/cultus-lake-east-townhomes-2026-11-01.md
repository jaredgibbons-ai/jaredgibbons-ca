## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero and the east shore's tenure is leasehold and pad tenancy, this section works in three parts: the strata rules that would govern if strata stock existed or is built, the FVRD / Park Board frame that actually governs this ground, and the purchase-tax frame.

- **Form B diligence (strata frame, if a strata ever surfaces here):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no verified strata on the east shore for this to attach to today; it is also the first check that a "townhouse" listing here is strata at all — on this shore, it will not be. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any east-shore complex in §2 — there is no strata complex and no line to flag in-entry. For Park lease lots, the lease and Park bylaws govern rental use, not strata law; for a park home, the tenancy agreement and park rules govern. Short-term rental rules for a specific east-shore lot: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). **This frame does NOT settle Cultus Lake Village's age rule:** a manufactured-home park's age rule is a park rule under tenancy regulation, not a strata bylaw — the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation (B.C. Reg. 481/2003) expressly recognises a park reserved for tenants who have reached 55 and allows a landlord to refuse an assignment where the proposed purchaser does not meet the park's age requirement. That is why the park's own written rule, not a listing's 45+ or 55+ label, decides who can buy in (§2). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal (here, FVRD) rules plus registered covenants only. No fee-simple townhouse stock exists on the east shore (§2); the shore's actual tenures are Park leasehold and pad tenancy, neither of which is fee-simple.
- **Governing zoning and plans (FVRD / Park Board — never Chilliwack's bylaws):** FVRD Cultus Lake Park Zoning Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 sets the zones for the Park lease lands, including R-3 Waterfront Residential and R-4 Hillside Residential on the Lakeshore Drive / Munroe Avenue side; in R-3 the permitted primary uses are a single-detached dwelling or a manufactured home, one dwelling per lot, for lots fronting on Cultus Lake.
