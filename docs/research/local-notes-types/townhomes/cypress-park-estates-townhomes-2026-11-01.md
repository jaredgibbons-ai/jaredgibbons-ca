## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the Hawksley strata, and Chippendale Mews if its strata is confirmed on title):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a new building like Hawksley, establish first whether the unit is a developer sale (disclosure statement route) or a resale (Form B route) — the diligence package differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt. A new strata's first cycle runs from its registration, so confirm where Hawksley sits in that cycle on the disclosure statement / Form B. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line is published for Hawksley or Chippendale Mews in the sources read — their restrictions are UNCONFIRMED, not banned; if such a line surfaces in older bylaw text, it is void for long-term rentals. This rule does not apply to the area's future purpose-built rental buildings, which are rental by tenure, not by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex documented in Section 2 is published as 55+ — the sibling 55-plus file checks Hawksley expressly and records it as general-market, NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple and bare-land forms:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules and registered covenants only. The pocket's RS-street houses are fee-simple houses, and its bare-land strata houses (Section 2) DO sit in a strata — bare-land strata owners hold a Form B diligence route for the strata's common affairs even though the lot carries a house; the title, not the built form, decides.
- **District plans and zoning — West Vancouver's own pair only:** District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — never White Rock's Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair, which belongs to White Rock only. Zoning here uses RS2, RS3 and RS5 single-family zones (Section 200 minimum site areas: 8,094 m² RS1, 1,625 m² RS2, 975 m² RS3, 731 m² RS4, 488 m² RS5), CD3 for Rodgers Creek (density set by lot-size band), and the CV (Cypress Village) Zone created by Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 5206, 2024. The OCP names Cypress Village and Cypress West as future neighbourhoods in the Upper Lands. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/cypress-park-estates.md)
- **SSMUH as the District states it:** the bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules, carried in the British Properties research, state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — a house-lot rule that shapes the RS streets, not the strata complexes themselves.
