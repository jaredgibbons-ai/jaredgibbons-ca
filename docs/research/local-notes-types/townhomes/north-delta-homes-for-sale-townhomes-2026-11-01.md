## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all 19 assembled complexes — every one is strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: provided within seven days of request, fee capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; states the monthly fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). For mixed complexes (Muse, Delta Gardens, Nicholson Green) the Form B and strata plan are also what settle which component a specific unit belongs to.
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). The assembled roster spans 1979–1980 (Sunridge Estates) and 1988–1989 stock at one end to 2022 (Oakcrest) at the other — the report's contents, not the headline fee, are the diligence.
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Minimum 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). This file's own records carry the void lines in-entry: Sunshine Woods' directory "rentals not allowed," Delta Cedars' directory "rentals not allowed," and Kennedy Gate's directory "rentals not allowed" (queued stock) are each void for long-term rentals wherever they appear. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw.
- **Age.** Only a 55+ bylaw is lawful since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01), with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ (or 19+) bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08). Applied here: Chancellor Place's older-listing "19+" line is not a permitted threshold; Delta Cedars' and Kennedy Gate's "age restrictions" lines state no age at all and verify nothing (§2.5).

**Fee-simple contrast.** None of the 19 assembled complexes is fee-simple. A true fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. North Delta buyers meet that frame on detached and half-duplex product, not on any verified townhouse complex here.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs over any March 12 sibling line): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, agreements from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). The assembled roster is resale stock, which does not attract GST. **New Housing Rebate:** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" summary compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example on the community attached median: a $622,500 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $10,450 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $422,500). Exemptions: first-time buyers full on the first $500,000 at values to $835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built homes full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC.** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted here: this slug's research file documents no CMHC premium error on a north-delta-homes-for-sale page.
- **Presales (REDMA).** A presale buyer has a 7-day rescission right under REDMA s. 21, with deposits held in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01) — the frame for any Village at Sunshine Hills purchase while its completion remains UNCONFIRMED (§2.4).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How many townhouse complexes are verified in North Delta, in total?**
A: Nineteen assembled here: 18 counted in their sub-area files (Annieville 1 — the Muse townhouse component; Nordel 11; Scottsdale 2; Sunshine Hills Woods 4) plus Sunridge Estates, counted in this assembly file under the variant rule with its count-home annotation. All 19 are strata; no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in North Delta (§2.1, §2.2).

**Q: Is North Delta the same market as Ladner?**
A: No. North Delta and Ladner are different board communities — North Delta is its own SnapStats community (Annieville, Nordel, Scottsdale, Sunshine Hills Woods), while Ladner is in South Delta. The October 21, 2026 audit's finding that this page's copy was 61.6% duplicate of the Ladner page is a copy defect to fix, not a market fact (research file: `~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/north-delta-homes-for-sale.md`).

**Q: Which North Delta sub-area moved fastest in September 2026?**
A: Scottsdale detached, at a 19% sales ratio on 53 listings and 10 solds; in Condos & Townhomes, Nordel led at 17% — on only 12 listings and 2 solds, so that ratio carries a small-count caution. Sub-area medians are not published; the community attached median was $622,500 (SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report, via the research file).

**Q: Is a unit at Muse or Delta Gardens a condo or a townhouse?**
A: Both forms exist under each name — Muse (9015 120 Street) has apartment and townhouse units under one strata, and Delta Gardens pairs 84 Avenue apartments with 115 Street townhomes. Confirm the specific unit's form on its listing and strata plan; do not rely on the complex name alone (§2.1, §2.3).

**Q: Is Delta Gardens in Nordel, Scottsdale or Annieville?**
A: Listings use all three names for the one complex. The City's own application file (LU007852) places the site in the ND12 Nordel Social Heart permit area, and that primary City placement governs in this program — the townhouses count once, in the Nordel file. Search all three sub-area names or you will miss listings (https://delta.civicweb.net/document/164210/, via the Nordel file).

**Q: Is Delta Cedars a 55+ building? Is Kennedy Gate?**
A: Neither is verified as 55+. Delta Cedars' directory record says "age restrictions" and never states the age; Kennedy Gate's sources conflict outright — a current listing says "NO AGE restriction," a directory says "age restrictions" with no age, and they disagree on the build year (1987 vs 1989). The only age bylaw BC law permits is 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2), so read the exact registered bylaw on the Form B; Kennedy Gate is queued, counted nowhere, until that is done (§2.5).

**Q: Why is Sunridge Estates counted here and not in the Sunshine Hills Woods file?**
A: Because that sub-area file was built before this fresh verification and does not count it, while this slug's own research names the complex and fresh listings establish it at title level (Freehold Strata, Plan NWS1316, 115 units in strata) — the variant rule's two-part test. It is counted ONCE, here, with the follow-up flagged for the sub-area file's next revision (§2.1).

**Q: Can I rent out a North Delta townhouse long-term, even where a directory says rentals are banned?**
A: Yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC; the "rentals not allowed" lines in this file's records (Sunshine Woods, Delta Cedars) are void in-entry. What still binds: a valid 55+ bylaw limits who may live in the unit, and a short-term rental bylaw can still apply (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).

**Q: Which plan and zoning govern a North Delta townhouse purchase?**
A: The OCP is consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — cite its Appendix B permit areas (ND12 Nordel Social Heart, ND3 Annieville, ND1 Fraser River Escarpment), never a lettered area-plan schedule; those belonged to the repealed Bylaw No. 3950 framework. The zoning bylaw number is CONTESTED in current sources (No. 7600, 2017 on City pages vs an internal "8400, 2025" title) — take the parcel's zone from the City's current zoning pages for the specific address (§3).

**Q: What does the Form B tell me before I buy?**
A: The monthly strata fee, anything the seller owes the strata, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance details, the strata's rules, its budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the depreciation report attached — within seven days of request for a capped fee of $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (Strata Property Act s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). On this roster it is also the document that settles the open items: Sunridge Estates' exact build year, and any age bylaw behind a directory's unstated "age restrictions" line.

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **One complex, three sub-area names.** Delta Gardens is labelled Scottsdale, Annieville and Nordel across listings for the same development — apartments on 84 Avenue in one feed, townhouses on 115 Street in another — and the City permit-area placement, not any feed label, is what this program counts it under. Search all three names or you will miss half the resales (§2.1).
- **The community's attached ratios are built on pairs of sales.** Every North Delta Condos & Townhomes sub-area ratio in September 2026 rests on exactly 2 solds — Nordel's community-leading 17% is two sales, not a trend — while the community attached median swung between $412,000 (May 2026) and $899,900 (June 2026) across the 13-month trend on 3–14 sales a month. Quote North Delta attached figures with their counts attached (research file: `~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/north-delta-homes-for-sale.md`).
- **The OCP citation trap.** Agents still cite lettered area-plan schedules for North Delta properties, but Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) contains no area-plan schedules — the former schedules (including the Tsawwassen Area Plan, Schedule D.1) belonged to the repealed Bylaw No. 3950 framework, and farm land under the current plan is designated "Agriculture (A)". Cite Appendix B permit areas instead (https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).
- **The zoning-bylaw number trap sits on top of it.** Delta's OCP is Bylaw 8400 and its zoning bylaw is cited as No. 7600 on City pages — an internal title reading "8400, 2025" for zoning collides with the OCP number. A document that cites "Zoning Bylaw 8400" for a Delta parcel has mixed the two bylaws up (§3).
- **Two Sunstone names, opposite tenures, one crescent apart.** The Sunstone pocket pairs the 70-unit Sunstone rental apartments (55+, tenancy rule) at 10775 Delsom Crescent with the strata Residences at 8360 Delsom Way and seven strata townhouse collections on the same crescents, none of which carries a published 55+ bylaw. Confirm the exact building, not the village name (§2.3, §2.5).
- **Sunridge Estates hid in plain sight.** A 115-unit strata townhouse complex (Plan NWS1316) on Baker Road appears on this page's own feed — unit 66 is the research file's Sunshine Hills Woods townhome card — yet neither its home sub-area townhomes file nor the condos community name-list counted it before this assembly's fresh verification. Large, older strata complexes on residential side streets are exactly the stock a name-list built from directories can miss (§2.1).
- **Delta Cedars hides the same way.** Neither Scott Road building in Annieville appears in City-source research at all — both surfaced only in MLS listing data and a building directory — because the former plan map puts Annieville's east edge at 116 Street, west of both buildings, while MLS classifies both as Annieville (basis: `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/annieville-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`).
- **Port Guichon is not a North Delta fact — and not a Tsawwassen one either.** It is in Ladner, in South Delta; it is noted here only because Delta-wide copy routinely misfiles it, and this file imports no Ladner-side framing in either direction (§1).
