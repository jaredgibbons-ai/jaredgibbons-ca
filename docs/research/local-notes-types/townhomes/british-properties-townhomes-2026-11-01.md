## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a new building like Hawksley, establish first whether the unit is a developer sale (disclosure statement route) or a resale (Form B route) — the diligence package differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three complexes above are over the threshold; a new strata's first cycle runs from its registration, so confirm where Hawksley sits in that cycle on the disclosure statement / Form B. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line is published for any complex in Section 2 in the sources read — their restrictions are UNCONFIRMED, not banned; if such a line surfaces in older bylaw text, it is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in Section 2 is published as 55+ — the sibling 55-plus file checks Hawksley expressly and records it as general-market, NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules and registered covenants only. The neighbouring detached houses in Whitby Estates and the original subdivision are fee-simple houses, not fee-simple townhouse complexes, and are not counted.
- **District plans and zoning:** District of West Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — never White Rock's Bylaw 2506/2220 pair, which belongs to White Rock only. Houses in the original subdivision sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones (Section 200 minimum site areas: 8,094 m² RS1, 1,625 m² RS2, 975 m² RS3, 731 m² RS4, 488 m² RS5, RS6 by site of record; secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses), while the Rodgers Creek lands sit in site-specific Comprehensive Development zones (CD3). The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **British Properties covenants sit on top of zoning:** subdivisions carry restrictive covenants registered at the Land Title Office — under them, an owner must file an application for approval of plans with British Pacific Properties Limited before building a new home, extending or altering an existing building, or erecting a fence or any structure; the covenants differ by subdivision, bind every subsequent owner, and District bylaws apply in addition. Whether a specific strata lot in Whitby Estates or the Uplands carries a covenant step is a title-by-title check: UNCONFIRMED per lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **Hazard permits:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — live checks for Uplands product at the forest edge and for strata alterations at all three complexes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **West Vancouver taxes:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. At British Properties values the Province's additional school tax commonly applies — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/british-properties.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (any developer-held Hawksley unit), not resales. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply in West Vancouver — it is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Hawksley and for the Rodgers Creek Area 5 and Cypress Village homes when they are marketed. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
