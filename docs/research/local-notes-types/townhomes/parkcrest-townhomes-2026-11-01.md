## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no strata stock in Parkcrest to apply strata diligence to today — the rules below govern any strata townhouse a buyer is told is "in Parkcrest" (it will in fact sit in a neighbouring sub-area's row) and any future stratified SSMUH product.** Form B diligence: Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no Form B at Kensington Gardens — it is undivided-interest ownership, not strata (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Parkcrest directory "rentals not allowed" line exists to flag — there is no directory stock here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ ownership complexes in Parkcrest. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the actual Parkcrest stock):** detached houses and half duplexes here are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **City zoning:** Burnaby's Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, No. 4742, is repealed and superseded — any Parkcrest document citing the 1965 bylaw is out of date. The R1 SSMUH District rules are stated in Section 2. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Parkcrest is in Burnaby, and the 3% EHT is Vancouver only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Burnaby tax timing:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Parkcrest is confirmed; where a presale is bought elsewhere, the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and the 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21) govern, with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy, no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance — explicitly, none is found in the research file for this area, so there is nothing to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file.
