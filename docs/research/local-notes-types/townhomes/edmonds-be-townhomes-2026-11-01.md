Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Edmonds is in southeast Burnaby, centred on Kingsway at Edmonds Street around Edmonds Station, and is one of Burnaby's four town centres. "Edmonds" is the GVR board sub-area name in the SnapStats GVR report for Burnaby (listings also label it "Edmonds BE"). The City publishes no street-by-street MLS boundary; the Edmonds Community Plan, adopted by the City in March 2025, plans the town centre in six districts — Central Edmonds, South Edmonds, the Station District, West Edmonds, Northwest Edmonds and East Edmonds. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- Main roads: Kingsway and Edmonds Street, with Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue serving the station area and Canada Way / 10th Avenue linking east toward New Westminster. Royal Oak and Metrotown are to the west, Highgate sits within the town centre's east side, and South Slope is to the south-west. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: (1) the Stride Avenue corridor south of the station — Riverstone, Kensington, Eden at Edmonds, Maplewood Terrace, Sylvan Court on Edmonds Street itself; (2) the 17th Avenue / Leeside node — Leeside Creek, Element 1 and Element 2; (3) the South Edmonds / 14th–16th Avenue edge of Southgate City — Kingsgate Gardens and the Southgate townhomes at 7333 16th Avenue. Interior streets away from Kingsway remain detached houses. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- Attached is the market here: in September 2026 the sub-area recorded 89 condos & townhomes listings (7 solds, 8% sales ratio) against 9 detached listings (2 solds, 22%). At the Burnaby community level that month, Condos & Townhomes posted a median sale price of $686,000; $763/sqft; sale-to-list 98%; 36 days on market — cited exactly as the report states them, as "Condos & Townhomes" COMBINED, never as a townhome-only figure; SnapStats publishes no Edmonds sub-area median. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- Transit anchor: Edmonds Station on the Expo Line, with a bus loop; TransLink route 116 also links Edmonds Station to Metrotown Station via Imperial, Sussex, Marine Drive, Marine Way, Byrne Road, Southridge Drive, Griffiths Drive and 18th Avenue, serving south Edmonds street by street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt116.pdf
- Schools: SD41 (Burnaby) runs K–7 elementary and 8–12 secondary with no middle-school tier. In-area schools on the Province's list include Edmonds Community Elementary (K–7), 7651 18th Ave, Twelfth Avenue Elementary (K–7), 7622 12th Ave, and Byrne Creek Community Secondary (8–12), 7777 18th St; listings for the Stride/17th Avenue complexes name Stride Avenue Community School, Taylor Park Elementary and Byrne Creek Secondary as catchments — catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the SD41 locator. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 11 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. The age split is the diligence point here: Kensington (1992) and Sylvan Court (1990) are deep into depreciation-report cycles while Element 2 (2025) is in first-cycle and warranty years. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Every counted complex above is over the five-lot threshold on its published or listing-evidenced size. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted townhouse complex in Section 2 carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line; the one such line in the wider Edmonds stock is the apartment condo Toscana's "No Rentals" headline, flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 above and in the sibling condos file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No building in Section 2 is published as 55+ in the sources checked — and note the Edmonds Centre for 55+, inside Edmonds Community Centre at 7433 Edmonds Street, is a City recreation program, not a housing bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified among named complexes in Edmonds. For reference, a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. The 7353 12th Avenue four-plex in Section 2 is the local fee-simple attached form (duplexes, "no strata fees" on the listing's own wording), and it is not a townhouse complex.
- **Burnaby zoning:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District (June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, with multi-unit lots able to be stratified — the 7270 17th Avenue sixplex is that rule arriving in Edmonds. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Kingsway + Edmonds Conceptual Master Plan is under review to align with the new OCP. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any remaining developer sales in Southgate City's later phases. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Stride Avenue or 17th Avenue — where is the townhouse choice?** Both corridors: Stride Avenue carries Riverstone (2003), Kensington (1992), Eden at Edmonds (2012) and Maplewood Terrace (2006), with Sylvan Court (1990) on Edmonds Street; 17th Avenue carries Leeside Creek (2005), Element 1 (2020) and Element 2 (2025). The Stride stock is older and larger-complex; the 17th Avenue stock is the station-side newer product. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out my Edmonds townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Kensington's listing data already records rentals allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Published examples: Kensington records pets allowed with restrictions and rentals allowed; Kingsgate Gardens' listing records one medium-sized dog (under 50 lbs) or one cat unless approved by council; Riverstone records pets allowed with restrictions. Full pet bylaws (numbers, size, dogs) for the other complexes are UNCONFIRMED from public sources — read the registered bylaws. https://www.estateblock.com/burnaby/15-7184-stride-ave-1627488 and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3082528-7428-14th-avenue-11-burnaby-bc-v3n-0c2/brochure
- **Q: How far is the SkyTrain, honestly?** Element 2's marketing puts Edmonds Station 600 m away; Leeside Creek listings put the walk at 5 minutes; Kensington is described as a short stroll to Edmonds SkyTrain Station. Edmonds Station is on the Expo Line. Measure the walk for the specific complex — the Stride Avenue addresses sit farther south than the 17th Avenue ones. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/element-2-burnaby-presale-townhomes/
- **Q: Is Hall Towers a townhouse or condo complex I can buy into?** No. Hall Towers (7264 Kingsway) is 331 units of BC Housing non-market rental housing owned by the Provincial Rental Housing Corporation — there is nothing to buy, and it is slated for replacement under the Kingsway + Edmonds master plan without displacing tenants. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/edmonds-be.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Edmonds townhouse?** SD41 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). Listings for the Stride/17th Avenue complexes name Stride Avenue Community School, Taylor Park Elementary and Byrne Creek Secondary as catchments, while the research file's verified locator run (for 7651 18th Ave) returns Edmonds Community Elementary and Byrne Creek Secondary — catchments are address-based, so run the building's exact address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041

It is counted here once as a townhouse complex because townhouse-form homes are its named product; a specific unit's form must be checked unit by unit. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/element-2-burnaby-presale-townhomes/
