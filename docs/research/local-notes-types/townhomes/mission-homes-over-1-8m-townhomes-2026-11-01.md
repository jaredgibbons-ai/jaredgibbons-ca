## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the cross-referenced strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document; for Archer Green, whose published details are UNCONFIRMED in the assembled sources, the Form B package is the diligence, not an optional extra. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Cherry Lane (22 units), Luther Place (36 units) and Briskham Hill (19 homes) are over the threshold on their published counts; Archer Green's lot count is UNCONFIRMED — its 5+ test is confirmed on its Form B/strata plan. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the four is evidenced as such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for **Cherry Lane** is flagged VOID in-entry on exactly this rule (and the same voiding applies to Stave Lake Landing's directory line in §2). Two roster-specific notes from the condos program: a current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals," the kind of restriction that CAN still be enforced; and Preston's rental permission rests on a 100-year housing agreement, a different legal mechanism than a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex and Cherry Lane's directory records "age restrictions" — both published positions only; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: What PTT do I actually pay at this level?** Run the tiers, not a flat rate: at the $1,800,000 floor with no exemption the tax is $34,000 (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $1,600,000); at $3,500,000 it is $93,000 on the inputs stated in §3, with the slice above $3M taxed at 3% + a further 2%. No PTT exemption reaches this band. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate help at this price?** No. The Bill C-4 rebate (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) phases out completely by $1.5M — below this band's floor. The same is true of the BC First-Time Home Buyers' PTT exemption (ends at $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (ends at $1,150,000). Band buyers should budget full GST on a new build and full PTT. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Luther Place shows up under "Condos" in my feed — is it a condo?** No — it is a townhouse strata (7525 Martin Place / 32286 7th Avenue, built 1988, 36 units, Plan NWS2729). The MLS dwelling-type field says Townhouse and governs over the feed filing; the same discipline separates Briskham Hill (townhouses, 7540 Briskham St) from Briskham Manor (apartments, 7554 Briskham St) on the same street. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Mission townhouse long-term?** Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, which is why the "rentals not allowed" directory line for Cherry Lane is outdated law, flagged VOID in §2/§3. Short-term rentals can still be restricted (a current Glenkirk Court listing states "No short term rentals"), and Preston's rental permission rests on a 100-year housing agreement rather than the bylaw change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Does Mission flood at the high end too?** The city's south edge is Fraser River floodplain and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system, and Mission taxes carry a diking levy — some of the band's acreage product sits in those rural sub-areas, so flood status is property-specific and must be checked against City and FVRD floodplain mapping before buying. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a top-end Mission acreage?** SD75 Mission Public Schools, with catchments address-based through the district's School Locator Tool. SD75 runs a middle tier (K–6 / 7–9 / 10–12) unlike SD42 next door; the downtown sample address in the research (7466 Welton St) resolves to École Mission Central Elementary, Heritage Park Middle School and Mission Senior Secondary. Run any acreage address individually — rural catchments do not follow the downtown pattern by assumption. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Q: What is the tax penalty if I miss Mission's July 2 deadline on a band property?** A flat 10% on any current balance outstanding on July 3, 2026 — not a staged 5% + 5%. On a $1.8M+ assessment base that is a materially larger dollar figure than on a median home, and the diking levy rides on top for low-lying parcels. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

Listing copy citing the old OCP number is a bylaw out of date; copy claiming the new OCP changed the development map is claiming more than the City's own hearing notice says. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/mission-homes-over-1-8m.md)
