## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because there is no townhouse stock, this section covers (a) why the zoning/ALR framework prevents townhouses here, (b) the fee-simple reality that replaces strata rules on the prairie, and (c) the provincial strata rules that apply the moment a buyer redirects to real townhouse stock elsewhere in Abbotsford.

- **Fee-simple reality — the core position here:** Matsqui homes are fee-simple freehold farm and rural-residential properties. For a fee-simple home there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014), ALR rules where the parcel is in the ALR, and any registered covenants only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **If a strata townhouse is bought elsewhere in Abbotsford — the BC standard block:** **Form B diligence** (Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); **depreciation reports** every five years for stratas with **5+ lots since July 1, 2024**, not deferrable by vote (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); **CRF minimum 10%** of the annual operating budget **since November 1, 2023**; **rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022** (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — flag any directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry wherever it is met in the Abbotsford files); **only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022** (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded **May 1, 2023** for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR housing frame (the prairie's actual density rules):** under ALC housing rules, ALR land may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; the **principal residence is capped at 500 m²** total floor area; a **secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence**; and an **additional residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**. In the A1 zone specifically: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only under the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by lot size, plus one secondary suite per lot to **100 m²**. In the RR zone: a coach house only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, with a coach house or suite limited to one per lot and the **lesser of 90 m² or 40%** of the principal dwelling's net floor area. None of these creates a separate title for sale. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/matsqui.md)
- **Farm-class thresholds (full three-tier form):** $2,500 of farm income for parcels of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha for larger parcels; $10,000 for parcels under 0.8 ha; applications by **October 31**.
