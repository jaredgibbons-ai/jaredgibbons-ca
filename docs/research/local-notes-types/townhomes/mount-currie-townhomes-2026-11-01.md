## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mount Currie is the main community of the **Líl̓wat Nation** (Ucwalmícwts-speaking; the band was formerly styled the Mount Currie Indian Band). The Nation states the community is home to approximately 1,495 of its more than 2,275 citizens, and is named for Mount Currie, the 2,591 m (8,501 ft) mountain at the centre of Líl̓wat Traditional Territory. https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-currie.md)
- Land status: the community sits on Líl̓wat reserve lands — the Nation describes 10 reserves totalling about 2,930 ha, a small fraction of a Traditional Territory of approximately 780,000 hectares extending north to Gates Lake, east to the Upper Stein Valley and west to the coastal inlets. Reserve lands are federal lands set aside for the Nation; they are not a municipality, and the surrounding unincorporated Pemberton Valley is Squamish-Lillooet Regional District (SLRD) Electoral Area C. Statistics Canada treats "Mount Currie" as an Indian reserve census subdivision (code 5931843) within the Squamish-Lillooet Regional District. https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/ and https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/ipp-ppa/details/page.cfm?Lang=E&SearchText=&DGUID=2021A00055931843&GENDER=1&AGE=1&RESIDENCE=1&HP=0&HH=0

### Housing that exists here — documented with its tenure stated, excluded from all counts with the reason

- **Nation-administered housing on reserve land — the whole housing system, excluded because it is not market stock of any form.** The village neighbourhoods (Mount Currie Village IR 1/IR 10, Xet̓ólacw IR 6, Lillooet Lake Road IR 3/IR 8, Kwetsa7 IR 2) are single-detached family housing, newer subdivision homes and CSA Z-240 modular/manufactured homes built on allocated lots — family community housing allocated through the Nation, not strata, not fee-simple, and not for sale to the general public. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This section works in two parts: the on-reserve reality that governs this ground (where the standard strata/municipal frame does **not** apply), and the standard BC frame stated for comparison, so a reader never mistakes which regime they are in.

- **On-reserve housing process (the regime that actually applies):** home ownership for Líl̓wat citizens runs through the Nation's five steps — Housing Manager first, then the Lands Department land encumbrance check, Band Council Resolution (the Council acts as guarantor), Indigenous Services Canada subsidy and Ministerial Loan Guarantee checks, then a lender. Because the Band Council acts as guarantor and a Ministerial Loan Guarantee backs the loan, standard mortgage and foreclosure mechanics do not apply the way they do off reserve. https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf
- **No strata frame on this ground:** there is no strata corporation, **no Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59 does not attach to reserve-land housing)**, no strata fees, no CRF and no depreciation report for any home in this community. For the record, the off-reserve BC strata frame a Pemberton buyer would meet instead is: Form B provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); a minimum CRF contribution of 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (any directory "rentals not allowed" line for an off-reserve strata is void in-entry); and only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, including the May 1, 2023 exemption expansion). None of that creates any right or product in Mount Currie itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** There is no fee-simple purchase in Mount Currie: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — and also no freehold title, no municipal zoning and no MLS® open market. A non-member cannot buy a home here; inquiries are directed to the Líl̓wat Nation (info@lilwat.ca, 604-894-6115). https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/
- **Land use:** there is no municipal zoning bylaw for the reserve lands — land allocation runs through the Nation's Lands Department and Band Council processes. Whether the Nation has enacted a formal land code under the First Nations Land Management Act is UNVERIFIED in this pass. The surrounding off-reserve valley is SLRD Electoral Area C and the Village of Pemberton's rules govern Pemberton land only; no SLRD or Pemberton bylaw number is carried for the reserve lands, and none applies to them. https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf
