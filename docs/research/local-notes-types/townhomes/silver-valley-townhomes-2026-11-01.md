## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the three counted complexes, via their count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5 or more lots must obtain one every 5 years; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold. Brighton (145 units) and Windfall (54 units) are over the threshold; Archstone Rockridge's count is UNCONFIRMED but it presents as a multi-building strata (count home, §2).
- **CRF minimum.** At least 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government, strata finances: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — any directory or bylaw line for a complex here reading "rentals not allowed" is void and flagged as such (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals).
- **Age.** Only 55+ bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). **Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses** — none of the three counted complexes is verified 55+ (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).

**Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here (§2.3). A true fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. "Freehold strata" (Brighton, Windfall) is strata title, not fee-simple townhousing.
