## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the queued strata complexes, and Straiton):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a bare-land strata like Straiton, the Form B covers common-property obligations (roads, gate, landscaping), not a shared building. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. All three queued townhouse complexes and Straiton (26 lots) sit over that threshold on their verified scales; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER it — none here is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: King's Gate's "rentals not allowed" complex-data line and Junction Creek's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" directory variant are both void in any long-term component. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No mountain townhouse complex is recorded as 55+ in any source checked: King's Gate expressly records "Age Restrictions: No," and no age restriction appears for Sage at Auguston or Junction Creek — age status for Junction Creek beyond that absence: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. - **Q: Are there actually townhouses on Sumas Mountain, or is it all houses?** There are three verified townhouse strata complexes in this program — Junction Creek (Lower Sumas Mountain Road), Sage at Auguston (4401 Blauson Blvd, 120 units, BCS2034) and King's Gate (36260 McKee Road, 67 units) — counted once in the guide sibling; this variant file counts 0 new because its own research names none as verified. Everything else named on the mountain is detached houses, rural lots, or the Straiton bare-land strata. Sources: §2 above.
- **Q: SnapStats showed zero attached listings — does that mean I can't buy one?** No. It means none was listed in that one month (September 2026) in a sub-area that also recorded only 3 detached listings and 0 sales of any kind. Thin supply, not no supply — expect to wait for a listing in one of the three complexes rather than to comparison-shop across many. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §2)
- **Q: Is Sage at Auguston in Sumas Mountain or Abbotsford East?** Both labels attach to it: MLS listings file 4401 Blauson Boulevard under "Abbotsford East," while Auguston is a City-named Sumas Mountain slope neighbourhood and the building record verifies the complex by name, plan BCS2034 and freehold strata title. This program counts it once, in the Sumas Mountain guide file, on built form and the building record — if an Abbotsford East page also names it, it is the same complex, not a second one. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata, and how is Straiton different from a townhouse?** You own your detached house and lot as a strata lot and share common property (roads, gate, landscaping) with the strata corporation — the City report records 26 single-family strata lots in EPS1299 at 4595 Sumas Mountain Road. Fees, bylaws and a Form B apply exactly as in a townhouse strata, but there is no shared building envelope and no townhouse unit. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Are any of the mountain townhouse complexes 55+?** None is recorded as 55+ in any source checked. King's Gate's complex data expressly records "Age Restrictions: No" and a fresh listing describes its strata as "welcoming all ages"; no age restriction appears for Sage at Auguston (a family community beside Auguston Traditional Elementary) or Junction Creek. Gated is not 55+. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Directory/listing-stated only: Junction Creek's directory records "pets allowed, pets allowed w/rest."; King's Gate's fresh listing states two cats or two dogs with a 10 kg maximum size, and its complex data records pets allowed with restrictions; Sage at Auguston listings state combination cat/dog rules that vary by listing. Confirm the exact number/size rules in the filed bylaws attached to the Form B. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhouse on the mountain?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022 — including the "rentals not allowed" line in King's Gate's complex data. A strata may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools — and why do neighbours get different answers?** Because the mountain splits. Locator runs returned Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary for 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, but Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary → William A. Fraser Middle → Yale Secondary for 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive. Run the exact address — and use Clayburn Middle's Ministry address, 35139 Laburnum Ave, not the live page's unverified 4300 Wright Street. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **Q: Is the mountain in the floodplain?** The mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie, named in City flood notices and evacuation alerts. A slope lot's issue is geotechnical, not flood: the OCP's Steep Slope Development Permit Area applies on slope land, and the McKee plan protects slopes over 40% as open space. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **Q: City water/sewer or well/septic for a townhouse?** The three townhouse complexes are in the built City neighbourhoods; the well-and-septic world is the rural FVRD part, which has no public water or sewer at rural density and whose homes use wells and onsite (septic) services — check a rural lot's well record in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm the septic system was installed by an Authorized Person under the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §6)
- **Q: Are more townhouses coming to the mountain?** One proposal, at the foot: the McKee Road lands proposal (Bylaw No. 3761-2025) describes approximately 192 townhouses among 108 detached lots and four rural residential lots. It is a rezoning-stage proposal — not built, not a presale, and not counted as stock. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf

- **This slug's research and the guide slug's research are the same mountain read two ways.** The batch-9 file adapts the batch-1 guide research for the `sumas-mountain` slug and marks named townhome complexes UNVERIFIED on an official source — which is why this file queues three complexes that are, on listing and building-record evidence, real and verified in the guide file. - **The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023)** is Schedule E of the City OCP and guides the west-slope mountainside neighbourhood; its Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation is where townhome/multiplex forms are planned, and its open space network is made up of streamside setbacks, critical habitat, geotechnical areas and slopes over 40%, with trail corridors identified in the McKee Trail Strategy. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **Abbotsford's current plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025).** Any Sumas Mountain page still citing OCP Bylaw "2600-2016" (or "2500-2016") as current is carrying a stale plan reference — a defect to flag for Jerry, sourced to this area's own research file, which carries the 2016 number in its plan wording. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **Two tax systems share one mountain.** City-side lots pay City of Abbotsford taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3 and a second 5% September 16, 2026), while rural FVRD lots pay the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes (due July 2; 5% on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% after October 31) — the penalty calendar for a rural cross-shop differs from the City one a townhouse buyer will actually face. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **Quarry traffic is part of life on the main road:** Semá:th (Sumas) First Nation states that gravel-hauling vehicles travel to and from five quarries on Sumas Mountain using an access road through the Lightning Rock site, with an alternate-route study agreed by the Province, Semá:th First Nation and the City; a City council report separately covers a soil removal permit to extract gravel to the 170 m elevation at Jamieson Quarry for Mainland Sand & Gravel Ltd. Semá:th also states the culturally significant Lightning Rock site was transferred to the Nation under an agreement with the Province. A buyer at Junction Creek or Straiton, both on the Sumas Mountain Road corridor, should drive the route at truck hours before writing. https://sumasfirstnation.com/press-releases/sacred-land-returned-to-semath/ and https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/40562/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
- **A new McKee neighbourhood park is in planning** near the intersection of Dawson Road and Sumas Mountain Road — City investment on the slopes is going to parks and ground-oriented neighbourhoods at the foot, not to product on the upper mountain. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/sumas-mountain.md, §4)
