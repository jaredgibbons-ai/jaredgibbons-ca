## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 5 cross-referenced strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. West Newton's verified stock is 1986–2004 wood-frame — this report is where roof, envelope, balcony and plumbing horizons are priced; every cross-referenced complex is over the five-lot threshold on its verified scale, and a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold (none of the 5 is such a strata). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The "Rentals Not Allowed" line in Glenwood Village's MLS building data (§2) is flagged in-entry on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Directly relevant to Glenwood Village and Lakeview Estates — and the reason Glenwood Village Heights' "NO AGE RESTRICTIONS" is a real product difference, not marketing. An older source describing Glenwood Village as designed for adults "45+" predates the current law; its MLS restricted-age field now records 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in West Newton — do not import that profile into any §2 complex; all five are strata.
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NEVER cited as current in this file. The West Newton South plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update; the Newton Town Centre Plan still covers the King George Blvd / 72 Ave core. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the house streets around the complexes, not existing strata lots, and does NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; coach houses and garden suites are allowed in all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with lane access required on smaller R3–R6 lots and one extra parking space. Lock-off suites in townhouses are permitted only in new construction in transit-oriented / frequent-bus-stop areas in RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones (City's 2025 change, carried in the count home) — no existing West Newton complex gains a legal lock-off suite from that change. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites and (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-newton-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home; tenants cannot operate them; stays are under 90 consecutive days per patron. - **Q: West Newton detached sold at 20% — does that pace apply to townhouses?** No. That 20% was the detached ratio, the highest in Surrey in September 2026; attached (condos & townhomes combined) sold at 6% on 130 listings in the same month. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two complexes on the same street carry almost the same name and opposite age rules.** Glenwood Village (7156 121 St, NWS2768) is 55+; Glenwood Village Heights (6875 121 St, NWS3423) advertises no age restrictions — re-confirmed on fresh listings Nov 2, 2026. A search filtered on the name alone will mix the two; the strata plan number is the reliable separator. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **Older descriptions of Glenwood Village say "45+".** That wording predates Nov 24, 2022, when BC invalidated every strata age bylaw below 55; the complex's MLS restricted-age field now records 55+, and the registered bylaws and Form B are what govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Boundary Park's 55+ complexes are split between two sub-areas.** Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Drive) is filed by MLS under West Newton and counted in the West Newton guide file, while the Lakewood complexes on the same drive are filed under Panorama Ridge — the same park-edge cluster appears under two different area count homes depending on the complex. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-newton-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **West Newton South no longer exists as a plan.** The City states that plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) — density claims for lots near the southern complexes, including development-assembly claims made in listing text (the research records a multi-lot assembly card on 122a Street citing RA zoning and a transit-oriented-area designation, in listing text only), should be checked against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the old plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps and https://jaredgibbons.ca/west-newton.html
- **A school address error was corrected in this program and still circulates.** T.E. Scott Elementary is at 7079 148 St; the 7075 124 St address that appeared in earlier material is wrong. Any school material for this area should use the Ministry address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/west-newton.md)
- **The phantom school name matters here.** "East Newton Elementary" does not exist — the BC Ministry K-12 file (2025/2026) and SD36's directory agree there is no such school.
