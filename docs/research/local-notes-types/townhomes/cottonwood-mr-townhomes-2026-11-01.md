# Cottonwood MR, Maple Ridge — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

**Name distinction, stated near the top so the two are never conflated:** "Cottonwood MR" is a MAPLE RIDGE board sub-area (the MR suffix is the board's distinguisher). It is NOT Cottonwood Village in Chilliwack, which is a pad-rental manufactured-home park. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction, applied honestly here:** three counted complexes are confirmed strata (The Meadows expressly, Cottonwood Terrace and Carriage Lane on their directories' strata-bylaw records); six are townhouse complexes whose tenure no public source checked states — for those, tenure is the first diligence step, because the two forms are bought completely differently. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — the central check on the 1990s Cottonwood Drive stock (Cottonwood Terrace 1993, Hollyhock 1996, Carriage Lane 1998/1999). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Carriage Lane is therefore VOID and is flagged void in-entry in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/carriage-lane-11358-cottonwood-drive
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for any counted complex — Maple Ridge has zero verified 55+ strata townhouses — and the sub-area's one 55+ townhouse-form community, St. George's Village, is a co-op, not a strata (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Co-op contrast (St. George's Village):** a co-op purchase is a share purchase with board approval and the co-op's own transfer fee — no Form B, no strata fees in the Strata Property Act sense, and financing is lender-specific. Do not run a strata checklist on a co-op unit. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 contains the land-use and development regulations for every property in the city; its residential zones include RS-1 through RS-3, RST, RT-1, RT-2 and RM-1 to RM-4, and the bylaw schedule gives a minimum lot area of 668 m² for RS-1. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, never resolved by guessing:** Maple Ridge OCP citations in the program's sources conflict (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024). This file flags the conflict and asserts neither. What is settled: no Cottonwood-specific area plan exists, so the OCP's general policies govern this sub-area. Two dated plan facts nearby: the Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025 (Albion is east of 240 Street, outside this sub-area), and the Lougheed Transit Corridor plan south of the area was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record (Council approved the OCP amendments January 27, 2026; OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 given third reading and adoption January 27, 2026; the corridor ends at 221 Street) — an earlier contrary reading of that corridor's status is noted in Section 5. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area, building permit required; eligible single-detached and duplex lots can have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH rules, subject to zoning, bylaw and building code requirements — relevant to Cottonwood's detached streets, not to its townhouse complexes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Creek and flood:** Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse through the area and the regional park protects the creek corridor; whether a specific Cottonwood MR lot is in a mapped floodplain is per-lot on the City flood maps at MapleRidge.ca/Flood. No ALR land inside Cottonwood MR was identified on the sources read; ALR status is per-parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's mapping, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified in any case (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/be-flood-ready-guide-fraser-river-city-maple-ridge
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST/HST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; PTT (first-time-buyer exemption on the first $500,000 at values $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No presale was verified in this sub-area, so no live REDMA item applies; on any future presale, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission and s.18 requires deposits in trust. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a Cottonwood townhouse strata or fee-simple?** It depends on the complex, and for six of the nine counted complexes no public source checked states the tenure — The Meadows is expressly freehold strata (BCS2464) and Cottonwood Terrace and Carriage Lane are strata on their directories' bylaw records. For Willow & Oak, Cottonwood Green, Cottonwood Grove, Maplewood Creek, Hollyhock and The Falls, confirm tenure on title before writing: strata means fees and a Form B; fee-simple means NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Carriage Lane townhouse? The directory says rentals are not allowed.** That line reflects a rental-restriction bylaw, and rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 — a strata cannot ban long-term rentals by bylaw, though short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/carriage-lane-11358-cottonwood-drive and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** The Meadows: the MLS data sheet records cats OK with a total of 2 pets in its data. Cottonwood Terrace: the directory records pets allowed. Carriage Lane: pets allowed with restrictions (directory). The Kanaka Way / Cottonwood Drive family complexes are marketed as pet friendly with pet bylaws only (cats/dogs OK, number limits). Pet bylaws remain enforceable — confirm the registered set for the specific complex. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse I can buy in Cottonwood?** No strata one — Maple Ridge has zero verified 55+ strata townhouses. The 55+ townhouse-form community at the sub-area's north edge, St. George's Village, is a co-operative: you buy shares with board approval, not a strata lot, and there is no Form B. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
- **Q: How do I judge a 1990s complex's finances?** Through the depreciation report and the Form B: stratas with five or more lots must renew the report every five years since July 1, 2024 and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. On 1993–1999 wood-frame stock, the report's roofing, envelope and drainage timing is the purchase. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: What parking and storage do these complexes have?** The Meadows' MLS data sheet records 2 covered parking spaces for the unit read, with a double garage in-unit; Carriage Lane listings describe a double garage and driveway. Stall and locker allocations are unit-specific and are confirmed on the Form B, not the listing. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85722/FullPublic.pdf?i=1796450768 and https://www.livingfraservalley.com/listing/r3080602-11358-cottonwood-drive-35-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-5v5/brochure
- **Q: Which schools does a Cottonwood townhouse feed into?** Two elementaries sit inside the area — Kanaka Creek (11120 234A St) and Alexander Robinson (11849 238B St) — but catchments are address-based: the locator run for the Kanaka Creek Elementary address returns Kanaka Creek Elementary and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary, and SD42 runs three east-zone secondaries, so run the complex's exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Is flood or creek setback a townhouse issue here?** For creek-adjacent lots, yes: Kanaka Creek is salmon-bearing, Metro Vancouver flags steep slippery cliffs along the creek at Cliff Falls, and suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area — check setbacks, slope and the City flood maps for any lot backing onto the creek corridor. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** The 745 Haney Place / Cottonwood circular bus to Haney Place exchange; there is no West Coast Express station in the sub-area — downtown commuters drive or bus to the Port Haney or Maple Meadows stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- **Q: Is Cottonwood MR the same as Cottonwood Village?** No — that is the trap in the name. Cottonwood MR is this Maple Ridge sub-area of houses and townhouse complexes around Kanaka Creek. Cottonwood Village is a pad-rental manufactured-home park in Chilliwack — a different city, a different tenure, and never townhouse stock. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City does not use this neighbourhood's name at all:** Maple Ridge's neighbourhoods page does not list Cottonwood, and no Cottonwood area plan exists — the board name survives in listings and SnapStats while the City and the school district anchor the area by Kanaka Creek and its two elementary schools. With no area plan, rezoning expectations should be set by the OCP's general policies, not neighbourhood-plan promises. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Two housing things here are both called Cottonwood Park / Cottonwood-something and are not housing you can buy:** Cottonwood Park (11600 Cottonwood Dr) is income-based subsidized rental run by Red Door Housing Society, and Cottonwood North Park (12111 238B St) is a City picnic park. Neither is a townhouse complex. https://learningondemand.bchousing.org/sites/default/files/media/documents/Zone-4-Seniors-and-Adults-with-Disabilities.pdf and https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Cottonwood-North-Park-44
- **The co-op at the north edge is easy to mistake for a strata:** St. George's Village's homes are townhouses and its listings read like strata listings, but its own membership form gives it away — a 55-year age qualification for MEMBERSHIP, clear title to the land held by the co-op, and a $3,500 share-transfer fee. It also sits beside St George Maple Ridge Anglican church (23500 Dewdney Trunk Rd), which is the likely source of a stray "St. Andrew's" seniors name that matches nothing in Maple Ridge. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf and https://stgeorgemapleridge.ca/
- **The creek is working salmon habitat, not just scenery:** Kanaka Creek Regional Park's Bell-Irving Hatchery supports chum and coho salmon and cutthroat trout, the Canyon Trail runs 1.2 km one way from the hatchery to Cliff Falls, and the Riverfront Trails run 3 km one way at the creek oxbow and the Fraser River — the amenity the Cottonwood Drive complexes back onto, and the reason creek setbacks bind. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- **The transit corridor to the south changed status on January 27, 2026:** the research file, read earlier, described the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan as adopted by Council directing growth around future Bus Rapid Transit stations; the City record settles it as ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (OCP amendments approved and OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 adopted that day, corridor ending at 221 Street). An earlier contrary reading in the program's files is superseded by that record — the corridor is south of Cottonwood, so its effect here is on Town Centre land, not on Cottonwood lots directly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Detached sellers here face the area's depth, townhouse sellers its scarcity of names:** Cottonwood carried 55 active detached listings against 5 sales in September 2026 (9% sales ratio — the fourth-highest detached inventory among Maple Ridge sub-areas) while its attached stock traded under complex names the City-source research could not confirm at all — the named-complex record exists only in MLS and directory data, which is why Section 2 states its basis entry by entry (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report).
