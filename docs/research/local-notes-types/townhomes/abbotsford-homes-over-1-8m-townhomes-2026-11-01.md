- **The page's own ALR summary is missing a tier.** The FAQ's "500 square metres cap, with a secondary 90 sq m residence permitted" omits the ALC's 186 m² additional-residence tier for properties over 40 ha — on a large Bradner or Matsqui parcel, that omission understates what the ALC actually permits by more than double. The ALC page governs. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **This band's floor is roughly double the city's detached median — and the band still has no statistics.** September 2026 put the Abbotsford detached community median at $949,000 against a band floor of $1,800,000, yet no permitted source publishes anything about the band itself: no count, no median, no days-on-market. Every "luxury market" number quoted for Abbotsford at this level is either a community figure in disguise or unsourced. - **The hillside half of this band sits inside a development permit area most buyers have never heard of.** McKee Peak and Eagle Mountain subdivisions largely sit inside the OCP's Steep Slope Development Permit Area, under the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) — additions, pools and retaining work on those lots are permit-area conversations, and any geotechnical covenant is on the individual title, not in the listing. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Clayburn Village sits inside this band's geography and outside its form entirely.** The village at the west foot of Sumas Mountain is protected through a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP and contains no townhouse complexes — exterior-alteration rules, not strata rules, are the diligence there. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
