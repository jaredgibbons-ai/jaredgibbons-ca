## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all three counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Fairview Village (1982) and Marvista (1980), the depreciation report and rainscreen/envelope history are the core documents; listing-level pet/rental lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Marvista (12 units) and Tisdall Row (5 units) are at or over the threshold, and Fairview Village's unit count is UNCONFIRMED but its courtyard-complex scale is over five on any reading of the listings. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund — in a 5-lot strata like Tisdall Row that minimum builds a small absolute reserve. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Marvista's published line already reads "Rentals Allowed"; any directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets elsewhere in Fairview is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The area's published 55+ building is Terraces on 7th — apartment-form Fairview stock (Section 2), whose 55+ position and mandatory service agreement are carried in the condos and 55-plus files; no counted townhouse complex in this file is published as 55+ (age position for the counted three: UNCONFIRMED beyond Marvista's no-published-age-restriction record — check the Form B). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified in Fairview — all three counted complexes are strata, and the Heritage project's tenure is UNCONFIRMED. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and registered covenants/HOA documents only. Freehold houses and duplexes on the Slopes and southern streets are that form, but they are houses, not townhouse complexes, and are not counted here.
- **City zoning:** the FM-1 District Schedule covers the Fairview Slopes and states its intent is to enhance the small-scale residential character of the Fairview Slopes neighbourhood by encouraging retention of existing houses and permitting new low-profile residential development; FM-1 permits single detached houses, duplexes, multiple dwellings and multiple conversion dwellings, with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60 and no minimum site area stated in the schedule overview. Southern residential lots also fall under R1-1 (minimum site area 306 m² to 557 m² by unit count; a multiplex of up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units); C-3A covers the commercial mixed-use streets. Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 is the governing bylaw. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-FM-1.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans and heritage:** the Broadway Plan covers Fairview's northern half (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues) around the Broadway Subway (opening fall 2027 per the Province). The Heritage project's three houses are designated protected heritage property (City hearing minutes, November 14, 2017) — designation binds future exterior work for any buyer of those homes, whatever the tenure proves to be. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/broadway-plan.aspx/ and http://council.vancouver.ca/20171114/documents/phea20171114min.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0050-000621
- **Floodplain:** the False Creek shoreline along the north edge is inside the City's designated floodplain dataset, and the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan states the floodplain area includes False Creek South passing through Fairview along the south shore; land in it carries Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — a check on seawall-edge purchases, and a second reason (with leasehold) that False Creek South product never comps cleanly against Slopes strata. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. This tax does not exist in Burnaby or Coquitlam. City property tax calendar: 2026 advance taxes due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a Fairview presale is bought (the queue items in Section 2, if they proceed as presales), the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
