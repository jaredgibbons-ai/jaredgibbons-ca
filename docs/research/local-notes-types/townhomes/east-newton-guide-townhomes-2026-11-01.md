# East Newton, Surrey — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Note on schools: "East Newton Elementary" does not exist — it appears in neither the BC Ministry of Education K-12 schools file (2025/2026) nor SD36's directory, and no school sits at 7235 144 St; no school name in this file is taken from a district URL slug. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- East Newton is the Newton land east of King George Boulevard. The City's East Newton plan material is bounded by King George Highway to the west, 76 Avenue to the south, 152 Street to the east and Newton Athletic Park to the north. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- King George Boulevard is the western edge and the transit spine: the R1 RapidBus runs on it between Newton Exchange and King George Station via Surrey Central Station; routes 321 (152 St / King George) and 324 (132 St) run through the grid. No SkyTrain station is inside East Newton. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip
- Newton Exchange and the City-designated Newton Town Centre sit at 72 Avenue and King George Boulevard, at the area's northwest corner — west of the boulevard, but it is the node East Newton townhouses market against. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: in the Chimney Hill pocket in the southeast (Chimney Ridge, The Summit), along 72 Avenue mid-area (Newton Park I), on 138 Street south of 66 Avenue (Charleston Green), and inside the mixed Chelsea Gardens community on 70 Avenue (its townhouse section at 13888 70 Ave). The rest of East Newton is single-family subdivision streets built out under the East Newton NCPs. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- The East Newton Business Park plan area (128 St to 144 St, 80 Ave to the south side of 88 Ave) frames the north end — relevant to north-edge homes for neighbouring uses, not housing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-business-park
- SD36 schools named in the research file for this area are Chimney Hill Elementary (14755 74 Ave), M.B. Sanford Elementary (7318 143 St), Tamanawis Secondary (12600 66 Ave) and Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave), all confirmed against the BC Ministry of Education K-12 schools file (2025/2026); assignment is by address through the SD36 School Locator, not by nearest building. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/
- Market frame (SnapStats, September 2026, as the research file states it): East Newton attached (condos & townhomes combined) — Inventory 132, Solds 11, Sales Ratio 8%; Surrey-wide attached median $512,944, $491 per sq ft, sale-to-list 99%, 25 days on market. SnapStats publishes no townhome-only figure and no sub-area medians — never quote these as townhouse-only numbers. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the area research file for east-newton-guide (Section 2), read 2026-11-01.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **All five verified complexes are strata, so the Form B is the core document:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports — mandatory 5-year cycle:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot defer it by vote; the report projects 30 years of component costs with three CRF funding models. For Newton Park I (1984), Charleston Green (1988) and the 2000-built Chimney Hill pair, this report is the levy forecast. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum 10%:** since Nov 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute at least 10% of its annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund — compare the CRF balance on the Form B against that floor and the depreciation report's models. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, no amendment needed. Any directory or MLS field for an East Newton complex still recording a rental restriction is showing pre-2022 wording (flagged in-entry wherever it appears in this file's sources). Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — 55+ is the only legal form:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 — a strata may restrict residents to 55+; any lower age bylaw (including the older "19+" wording still attached to Chelsea Gardens in an old directory) has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it, and Regulation 7.01 exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse/partner, and children in defined care cases. Directly relevant to Charleston Green and Chelsea Gardens. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in East Newton. A fee-simple townhouse elsewhere in Surrey carries no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — do not import that assumption into any complex in Section 2; all five are strata.
- **Surrey zoning:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs, including the R-series residential zones in Newton (bylaw minimum lot areas include R3 at 464 m² for a single-family home and 557 m² for a duplex, R4 at 250 m², R5 at 200 m² / 225 m² duplex). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Surrey OCP — current plan only:** the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, received final adoption on June 15, 2026. The old PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current in this file. The City states the East Newton North NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and East Newton South's designations were superseded by it. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps
- **SSMUH — in force in Surrey since July 8, 2024:** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This affects the single-family streets around the complexes, not the strata lots inside them; the SSMUH changes do not retroactively legalize a suite built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — the 40% cap:** Surrey's suite framework caps a secondary suite at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area (the City states the same 40% / minimum 30 m² frame for the new townhouse lock-off suites it approved for transit-oriented and frequent-bus-stop areas in RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones, for new construction only). An existing East Newton townhouse does not gain a legal lock-off suite from that announcement — it is a new-build, location-limited permission. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** STRs are permitted only as the City states them — owner-operated, in the owner's principal residence (plus at most one secondary suite or accessory unit on the same lot), for stays under 90 days, under a City business licence ($358/yr as the research program states it; the City's 2024 corporate report that introduced the regime proposed a $350/yr licence plus application fees — confirm the current fee on the City's licence page before quoting it), with provincial registration also required. A non-owner-occupied townhouse bought purely as a nightly rental has no legal path in Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **GST on new construction:** the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) returns 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate alone is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. All five Section 2 complexes are resales — no GST question arises on them. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT and exemptions:** first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, principal residence; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** no townhouse presale was verified inside East Newton in this pass. Where a presale exists nearby, REDMA s. 18 (disclosure statement) and s. 21 (7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure acknowledgement, deposits in trust) are the frame. The large corridor proposal north of the sub-area (Queens on King George) is documented in the condos sibling file, not counted here. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Manufactured-home parks (for the excluded parks):** Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy and the provincial Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act govern pad tenancies; Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by that Act. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Surrey property taxes (city-wide, not area-specific):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the area research file for east-newton-guide, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are all East Newton townhouses strata?** Every verified one is: Chimney Ridge, The Summit, Newton Park I, Charleston Green and the Chelsea Gardens townhouse section are all strata complexes (Charleston Green expressly freehold strata, plan NWS2625; Chelsea Gardens plan LMS1416). No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the sub-area. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3093324-6537-138-street-46-surrey-bc-v3w-0c5/brochure
- **Q: What is the difference between Chelsea Gardens' two addresses?** Form. 13870 70 Ave is apartment-style condos and 13888 70 Ave is ground-level townhouse-style homes, inside one gated LMS1416 community on 23 acres — two listings under one name can be different building forms and fee schedules. Confirm the unit's section on the Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/13888-70-avenue/328
- **Q: Is Chelsea Gardens really 55+? An old page says 19+.** Current listings state 55+, and a 19+ bylaw has been legally invalid since Nov 24, 2022, when BC confined strata age bylaws to 55+. Confirm the registered bylaw and your exemption position (spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy resident) before subjects. https://vancouvernashdom.com/surrey-open-houses-condos/listing.r2974069-201-13870-70-avenue-surrey-v3w-0r9.104420669/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out an East Newton townhouse?** Long-term, yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Two limits can still apply: a strata can restrict short-term rentals by bylaw, Surrey's STR regime requires owner-occupancy and a licence in any case, and in a 55+ complex (Charleston Green, Chelsea Gardens) the tenant must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **Q: Why can't I get a mortgage at Sullivan Mews?** Because, per its listing, it is not a strata — the purchase is a membership, so there is no strata title for a lender to register against, and the listing states a purchase must be cash. It is excluded from this file's townhouse count for exactly that reason. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3058951-15020-66a-avenue-49-surrey-bc-v3s-2a5/brochure
- **Q: How do I judge levy risk in a 1984–2000 complex?** Through the depreciation report: mandatory every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024, projecting 30 years of costs with three CRF funding models — compare the CRF balance on the Form B against the near-term projects in that report (roofs, envelope, plumbing in this age band). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex by complex, from listings only: Chelsea Gardens — two pets, maximum 15 inches at the shoulder (listing-stated); Charleston Green — pets allowed per listings in the sibling 55-plus file. Chimney Ridge, The Summit and Newton Park I pet rules: UNCONFIRMED from a public source. The registered bylaws govern in every case. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3060317-13870-70-avenue-202-surrey-bc-v3w-0r9/brochure
- **Q: Can I add a lock-off suite to my townhouse for rental income?** Only within narrow limits: Surrey's 2025 townhouse lock-off permission is for new construction in transit-oriented / frequent-bus-stop areas in specified zones, with the suite under 40% of the principal unit's habitable area and at least 30 m². It does not retrofit a legal suite into an existing complex like Chimney Ridge, and strata bylaws apply on top of the City permission. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Q: Which schools serve a Chimney Hill townhouse?** The in-area SD36 schools are Chimney Hill Elementary and M.B. Sanford Elementary, with Tamanawis and Panorama Ridge secondaries — but assignment is by exact address through the SD36 School Locator, and there is no "East Newton Elementary" (it does not exist, and no school sits at 7235 144 St, whatever an old draft or a district URL slug suggested). Run the address. https://www.surreyschools.ca/schools/chimneyhill/ and https://www.surreyschools.ca/
- **Q: What transit do I actually get?** The R1 RapidBus on King George Boulevard to King George Station via Surrey Central, plus routes 321 and 324 through the grid. There is no SkyTrain station inside East Newton — the ride starts with a bus to the Expo Line. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R1/direction/0/round-trip

- **The phantom school is a research-program catch, and it matters for listings:** "East Newton Elementary" was traced to a district URL that renders a generic locator shell for any slug; the BC Ministry K-12 file (2025/2026) and SD36's directory agree no such school exists and none sits at 7235 144 St. Any listing copy naming it is naming a school that does not exist. Source: the area research file for east-newton-guide (UNVERIFIED items, item 2), read 2026-11-01. https://www.surreyschools.ca/
- **No SkyTrain is funded to Newton.** The rapid-transit project under construction south of the Fraser is the 16 km Surrey–Langley (Dave Barrett) Extension along Fraser Highway from King George Station to Langley City Centre, expected in service in late 2029 — it does not run down King George Boulevard.
