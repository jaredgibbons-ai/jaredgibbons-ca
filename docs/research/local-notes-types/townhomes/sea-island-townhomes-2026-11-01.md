## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why the strata block is stated on a zero-stock island:** there is no counted strata complex for it to attach to today. It is stated because a buyer who finds an apparent "strata" unit near the airport needs the test for whether it is residential townhouse stock at all, and because the Burkeville house purchase — this island's actual product — is governed by the fee-simple, City/federal and tax rules that follow.
- **Form B diligence (any future strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Directory and listing rental/pet lines are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum (any future strata purchase):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID / age bylaws:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw), and only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; exemptions for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers and legacy residents, expanded May 1, 2023; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No directory "rentals not allowed" line exists to flag in this file because no complex is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (Burkeville — the island's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. Most of the island is federal airport land governed by the YVR 2037 Master Plan and federal Airport Zoning Regulations rather than the City bylaw; the exact Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **City zoning / OCP:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 and Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 frame the Burkeville pocket (Sea Island is planning area 12 in the OCP framework), while senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP — airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process. Flood construction levels and dike setbacks for new construction sit under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Aircraft noise and height — the island-specific diligence:** federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada cap maximum building heights around the airport so aircraft can operate safely; aircraft noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data, and the City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05, "Aircraft and Land Use Noise") applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. Check all three before pricing an addition or rebuild on a Burkeville lot. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/ and https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of low-lying islands in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations; the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and the City is raising dikes from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. Confirm the lot's flood construction level position under Bylaw 8204 with the City before planning a rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **Richmond property taxes (city-wide rule, applies to Burkeville homes):** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)

New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2%, flagged wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers — full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. **No CMHC defect is asserted for this slug** — this slug's own research does not document the live mortgage calculator page's 2.80%–4.00% range as an error, so it is not documented for this slug and not asserted here. No Alexandra District Energy Utility (ADEU) charge applies on this ground — ADEU charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Burkeville is a wartime aviation housing pocket that outlived its era.** Sea Island was once mostly farm land with small communities including Eburne and Burkeville; the Vancouver Airport opened on the island, and Burkeville — houses built for aviation workers in three designs — is the only remaining population centre on Sea Island per the City of Richmond Archives. http://richmondarchives.ca/tag/planning/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sea-island.md)
- **The island's school is a heritage stop, not a school.** Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed; Burkeville children are catchment-assigned off-island (the documented locator result for 7140 Miller Road is Brighouse Elementary / Richmond Secondary). https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/
- **The airport does not answer to City Hall.** YVR occupies federal property within the City of Richmond and its land use follows the YVR 2037 Master Plan — so the island's biggest neighbour's changes never appear on a City development tracker, and federal Airport Zoning Regulations can reach into a Burkeville backyard (heights for additions, rebuilds and even trees). https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement and https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Three Canada Line stations, no condo or townhouse at any of them.** Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport give the island rapid transit most Richmond pockets do not have — and there is still no verified attached housing at any station, because the land around them is airport land. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- **The everyday anchors are small and specific.** Burkeville Park and the Sea Island Community Centre are at 7140 Miller Road; Larry Berg Flight Path Park is on Russ Baker Way; McDonald Beach Park is at the end of McDonald Road; Iona Beach Regional Park is at 900 Ferguson Road at the island's north-west end; and the Sea Island Conservation Area is a designated protected habitat area named by YVR. There is no public library branch on the island, and a grocery store on Sea Island is UNVERIFIED on any official source. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf
- **The nearest hospital is off-island.** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, Richmond (Vancouver Coastal Health) — a Burkeville buyer's hospital trip crosses the Dinsmore or Moray Channel bridge. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
