## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (strata stock — The Fairways today):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5+ lots must obtain one every 5 years, not deferrable by vote; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the annual operating budget since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

**Q: Can an acreage lot have a second dwelling?**
A: One garden suite (33–90 sq m) per lot in the A-1, A-2, RR and RS zones, OR a secondary suite, never both; on ALR land the ALC also allows one additional detached residence to 90 sq m (≤40 ha) where conditions are met. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (research file)

**Q: Is North Meadows in a floodplain?**
