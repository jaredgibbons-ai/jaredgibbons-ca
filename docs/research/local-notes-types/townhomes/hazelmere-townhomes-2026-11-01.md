## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Hazelmere Village, the area's one counted complex).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, to be provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, any special levies approved but not yet due, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government, strata Forms information: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). For Hazelmere Village the Form B is also the document that settles the title-level tenure wording held UNCONFIRMED in §2.1.
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots are UNDER the threshold and exempt (BC Government, depreciation reports: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports).
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government, strata finances: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a strata can no longer restrict rentals, and any directory or listing line restricting rentals at Hazelmere Village is legally void on the rental part (BC Government, strata rental rules: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate, municipal matter (below).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions added May 1, 2023 for caregivers and for spouses/partners and specified family members (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). The listing statement "no age restrictions" at Hazelmere Village is consistent with that framework but remains listing-stated — read the bylaws.

**Fee-simple contrast.** Hazelmere Village is strata, so its buyers get a Form B, monthly strata fees and strata bylaws. The surrounding acreage homes are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — but they carry the ALR rulebook below instead, which is the heavier diligence load in this area (Strata Property Act scope: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_01; Agricultural Land Commission: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, ALR, suites and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH).**

- Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan).
- Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — Hazelmere is predominantly General Agriculture (A-1) zoning over ALR land. CAUTION: the A-1 minimum-lot figures that circulate in live-page copy (4 ha inside the ALR / 2 ha outside) are UNVERIFIED in this pass against the Part 10 bylaw text — no A-1 numeric figure is asserted in this file; read the zone text directly (City of Surrey, Zoning Bylaw 12000: https://www.surrey.ca/bylaws-services/bylaws).
- ALR residence rules (the frame that governs most Hazelmere acreage): a principal residence on ALR land is capped at 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and ONE additional residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha (Agricultural Land Commission, residences in the ALR: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alc/content.residences). These rules — not the City suite rules — decide what a coach house or second dwelling on a Hazelmere acreage may be.
- Secondary suites in Surrey (non-ALR context) are capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area (City of Surrey, secondary suites: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites).
- SSMUH CAUTION: Surrey's SSMUH allowances (implemented July 8, 2024 — 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop) apply to eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary on community water/sewer (City of Surrey, SSMUH: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing). Most Hazelmere acreage sits outside that shape — larger rural lots, ALR designation, private servicing — so SSMUH generally does NOT unlock units here; a parcel-by-parcel check (containment boundary, ALR status, servicing) is required before assuming any allowance applies, and ALR parcels answer to the ALC rules first.
- Short-term rentals: Surrey permits short-term rentals only in the operator's own principal residence, with a City business licence at $358/year as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries the licence page's $358 figure and directs the reader to the licence page for the current amount (City of Surrey, short-term rentals: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals). Strata bylaws on short-term accommodation also apply inside Hazelmere Village.
- Property tax timing: Surrey property taxes are due July 2; a 5% penalty applies to amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey, property taxes: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, to a maximum rebate of $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). It applies to NEW housing only — Hazelmere Village is 2001 resale stock, which does not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate (GST/HST).** Separate from the first-time buyer rebate: a 36% rebate of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 of value (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax (PTT).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion of a property valued above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000).
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time home buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 of value where the fair market value is $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption up to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP withdrawal limit is $60,000 from RRSPs (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); the FHSA allows $40,000 of contributions at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC mortgage default insurance premiums for high-ratio purchases run from 0.6% to 4.5% of the mortgage amount depending on the loan-to-value ratio (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research file documents no CMHC premium error on a Hazelmere page.
- **Presales.** No presale townhouse project is verified in Hazelmere in this pass, so the REDMA rules are stated for completeness only where a presale is later found: a buyer of a presale unit has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
