## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this plan topic file, this section carries the plans frame — because that frame is what decides where townhouse product can exist in each municipality — followed by the standard BC strata and tax block that governs any actual townhouse purchase under those plans.

### The plans frame

- **The mechanics — designation, then zoning, then development permit area:** in the verified Delta model, work in that order: OCP designation first (s. 1.5.1 / Map 2), then the parcel's zone on the zoning maps, then any Development Permit Area — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. The same dating and layering check has to be run in Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford before a plan claim goes into an offer or a listing. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta — the research-verified model:** Delta Council adopted a new Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), on July 8, 2024; the City describes an OCP as the document that describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains long-term objectives and policies guiding land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure decisions. The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — Land Use Plan at s. 1.5, designations at s. 1.5.1 applied on Map 2 (including Agriculture (A)) — with NO lettered schedules and NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in it; those were schedules of the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and the "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" conflation is an error wherever met. Delta SSMUH: up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024). Delta zoning number: CONTESTED (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Township of Langley — program-carried frame (standing corrections; NOT research-verified for this slug):** the Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). The update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 (Willoughby Community Plan replacement, Transit Corridor Plan, Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — adoption/repeal claims for them are UNVERIFIED, and reading-stage bylaws are never upgraded in this file. A Township public-hearing notice for June 15, 2026 for Bylaws 6201, 6202 and 6203 was located in a fresh check for this series — a hearing notice is evidence of the hearing, not of adoption. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-06-08-Public-Notice.pdf and https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Township townhouse-relevant rules (program-carried):** Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs; the compact-lot pattern is the Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (s. 403): lots run from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102 — never strata, never a townhouse complex. Secondary suite cap: 120 m². SSMUH is Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible) and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — a page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is WRONG; the research file for THIS slug documents no such claim on the land-use-plans page, so no defect is recorded for this page on that point. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Abbotsford — lookup level only (research-verified at that level):** the City of Abbotsford's Development Planning pages state that information about current zoning, future land uses and development permit areas can be found using the City's property information search or a property search in WebMap, and list the Council-adopted bylaws and policies that commonly regulate development. The Abbotsford OCP bylaw number and current consolidated structure: UNVERIFIED in the research pass — the City's development planning page was opened; the OCP bylaw document itself was not. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/development-planning
- **Surrey and the City of Langley — UNVERIFIED in this pass:** the City of Surrey's current Official Community Plan bylaw number, land use designation list and secondary-plan structure, and the City of Langley's current OCP and zoning bylaw numbers and plan structures, were not verified in the research file — no source from those municipalities was opened for it, and nothing is stated here from general knowledge. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/land-use-plans.md)
- **The ALR layer crosses all four municipalities:** where land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, Agricultural Land Commission rules apply on top of the municipal plan and zoning — generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel, a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area with a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6: strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — ALR residences cannot be stratified, so no plan designation in any of the four cities creates strata townhouse stock on ALR land. Farm-classification thresholds, where a farm parcel is in issue, run in full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Regional layer:** Delta's OCP carries a Regional Context Statement (Appendix A of Bylaw No. 8400) tying the municipal plan to Metro Vancouver's regional planning — the one verified example in the research of how a municipal land use plan nests inside a regional one. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf

### The purchase block (any actual townhouse under these plans)

- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A plan check never substitutes for this building-level check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (the plan and zoning layers above) plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only.
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating budget must go to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID / age bylaws 55+ only:** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 in all four municipalities — any directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **PTT and exemptions:** general PTT 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever met); first-time buyers' exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Rebates and programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; new construction only); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST does not apply to resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — none of this topic's four municipalities is Vancouver. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a project in any of the four municipalities is sold on developer contracts, REDMA s. 18 requires the disclosure statement with deposits in trust, and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it. A favourable plan designation does not change those rights. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

- **Q: Does a favourable plan designation change anything inside an existing townhouse complex?** No — the plan governs land use, not strata bylaws.
