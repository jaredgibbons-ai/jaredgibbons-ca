## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Blu Living in full — it is a strata complex).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, bylaws, minutes and depreciation report, and to confirm what the monthly fees include (research file §6; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Blu Living's page-stated 64 homes would put it well over the threshold — but the count itself is UNCONFIRMED (§2.1), so the threshold position is read off the strata plan, not the page. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any "rentals not allowed" line for Blu Living is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). Blu Living carries no verified age bylaw and is not 55+ in any source checked (§2.1) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Not applicable to Blu Living itself (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Blu Living is the opposite case: strata fees, a Form B and registered bylaws govern.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite per dwelling unit, and never within a coach house or garden suite (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite).
- **Suites in townhouses:** Surrey Council approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones **for new developments** in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — those permissions are not a blanket right in an existing complex like Blu Living (https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting.
