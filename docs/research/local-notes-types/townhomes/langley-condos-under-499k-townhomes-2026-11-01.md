## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata complex cross-referenced in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** Form B Information Certificate under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, within 7 days of request, fee capped at $35 plus 25¢/page (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5 or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). Every cross-referenced complex above whose scale is verified (Willow Park Estates 64, Churchill Park 147, Spencer Green 97, Creekside Villa 55, Longlea 58 queued) is well over the threshold.
- **CRF.** Minimum 10% of the annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry for Parkway Estates and Parkway II ("rentals not allowed" directory lines, void) and Willow Park Estates (same line, void) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws). Highgate Terrace (Willoughby, apartment-form, 19645 64 Ave) is age-restricted with the exact age UNCONFIRMED — never state it as 55+.

**Fee-simple contrast.** Westbrooke and the Bedford Landing row homes (§2.2) carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.

**Municipal frame — two Langleys, two bylaws.**

- **Township:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. SSMUH is Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier exists in it — any claim of a 6-plex tier, or that the Willoughby Plan was "replaced" by Bylaws 6201/6202/6203, is WRONG; the standing correction governs, adoption UNVERIFIED). Secondary suite cap 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata — and s. 102 permits a suite in a townhouse at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses: a live Township conflict, flagged, not resolved. R-CL s. 403 is 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum (a "up to 355 m²" cap is nowhere in the bylaw — error wherever met). Willoughby Community Plan: Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 stand at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY (Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026).
- **City of Langley:** Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 (cite the number; its adoption date is conflicted in program sources — flagged UNVERIFIED). A secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot (s. 2.4), and a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m² (research file §4). City SSMUH: Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 (research file §4). City short-term rental status: NOT VERIFIED on any City page in the program — no City STR rule is stated, which matters for investor math on this band (research file §4).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing — with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown).**

- **Worked example 1 — at the ceiling, $499,000, no exemption (inputs: price $499,000, resale, no exemption).** Down payment: the price is at or under the $500,000 tier boundary, so the minimum down payment is a flat 5% = **$24,950**. PTT: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $299,000 = $5,980, total **$7,980**; a qualifying first-time buyer exempt on the first $500,000 pays $0 on those inputs (condos sibling, §3: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax).
- **Worked example 2 — program anchor, $600,000, no exemption** (above this band; shown so band buyers trading up see the next tier): PTT = 1% × $200,000 + 2% × $400,000 = **$10,000** (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **PTT general rule.** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers as above, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears. First-time buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling carrying March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, March 20, 2025–2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). A resale unit under this ceiling does not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 at $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html; https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained). **Page-defect discipline:** no CMHC premium-range error is documented in this slug's research file, so none is asserted for this slug — the 2.80%–4.00% range documented as a live-page error elsewhere in the program is explicitly NOT asserted here.
- **Presales.** REDMA s. 21: 7-day rescission; s. 18: deposits in trust (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
