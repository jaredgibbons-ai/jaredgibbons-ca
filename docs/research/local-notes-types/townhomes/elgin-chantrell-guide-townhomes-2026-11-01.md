## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted strata complexes in the count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For The Crescent, the Form B and the registered strata plan (LMS4241) are also where the title conflict in §2.1 gets resolved — listing copy does not govern (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both counted complexes are over the threshold on their documented scale (The Crescent 57 homes; Elgin Pointe on its complex scale — confirm per complex on the Form B). **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §2.1: Elgin Pointe's older "no rentals" listing line is void for long-term rentals and must not be repeated as a current rule. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). The Crescent is documented as a 55+ community — that form of bylaw remains valid; confirm its exact wording on the Form B (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The surrounding acreage homes are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Surrey rules and registered covenants only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here (§2.2).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and ALR — standing corrections applied.**

- **Secondary suites:** one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, habitable floor area not exceeding **40% of the principal dwelling unit** under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; a suite is not a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite of at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with one extra parking space required (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites).
