## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata component):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For one of only three townhomes in a 35-home project, the Form B and depreciation report are where a buyer learns how tower-scale costs (elevator, envelope, amenity building) are shared with the townhome lots — read them before the view. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — the Bellevue townhome component is 3 lots, but it sits inside its project's larger (35-home) strata, so the project-level report and CRF govern, not the component count. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in §2 carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry — Dundarave Landing's directory lines record rentals *allowed* at both its addresses (§2), and the counted component's rental position is UNCONFIRMED, so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Per the 55-plus pass, no Dundarave strata holds a verified 55+ bylaw — Dundarave has 0 verified 55+ ownership buildings, and the counted townhome component carries no age restriction in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Dundarave (§2). A fee-simple townhome, where one exists elsewhere, carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Dundarave's fee-simple ground-oriented stock is detached houses and half-duplexes under District rules; do not import strata expectations into those deals, or townhouse-complex expectations into a duplex.

No public source checked states a 55+ age-restriction bylaw for any Dundarave strata — including the counted component — and Dundarave has 0 verified 55+ ownership buildings in the 55-plus pass. The nearest retirement residence, Westerleigh PARC at 725 22nd Street, is rental and sits, by its operator's own wording, "on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood." (§2 above)
- **Q: What extra permits touch a Bellevue Avenue or village-core property?** A Foreshore Development Permit for waterfront/coastal-flood-risk properties, Form & Character review inside the Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre permit area, and an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of a watercourse bank. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Dundarave townhome address?** Irwin Park Elementary (K–7, 2455 Haywood Avenue) is in Dundarave itself; the catchment secondary school is West Vancouver Secondary (Grades 8–12, 1750 Mathers Avenue). SD45 catchments are address-based — confirm the exact address with West Vancouver Schools. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/045
- **Q: What will taxes look like?** District property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty July 2, further 5% September 2); water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly; and above $3 million assessed value the Province's additional school tax applies (0.2% on the $3–4 million portion, 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising January 1, 2027). ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The village's most famous "condo" cannot be bought — and its replacement is not a presale.** Villa Maris has been a single-owner rental building for 50 years; the redevelopment concept reported for it and the Shoreland has not even been submitted to the District, proposes rental first at the Shoreland site, and leaves the Pink Palace's strata replacement at an undecided size. Anyone marketing future Pink Palace condos today is ahead of the record. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf
- **A zoning section settles a marketing argument.** "Village Point townhomes" survives in old ads and portal copy, but Bylaw 4662's CD2 zone was written for that one site (2388 Marine Drive) and permits apartments only, capped at 15 units — the same 15 the ads counted. When marketing and the bylaw disagree about built form in West Vancouver, the site-specific zone is the document to pull first. (§2 above)
- **Dundarave's only presale spent 2026 in receivership.** Pierwell's approved development permit dates to July 2022, its receiver was appointed in March per the 2026 reporting, and the court-approved sale price reported for the whole project was about $37.5 million against secured claims exceeding $54 million — the gap between marketing and record is why this file counts it 0 and documents it instead. https://www.connectcre.ca/stories/seattle-developer-to-buy-stalled-pierwell-project-for-37-5m/
- **The newest tower answers to two addresses in public sources.** The Bellevue by Cressey appears as 2299 Bellevue Avenue in the area research and 55-plus sibling and as 2289 Bellevue Avenue in current listings and its presale source — settle the civic address from the strata plan before a title search, a locator run or showing directions rely on either figure. (§2 above)
- **The seawalk is the flat route between the two villages.** The Centennial Seawalk runs about 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park to Ambleside — the level, paved waterfront route that connects Dundarave on foot to the Ambleside amenities, library and seniors centre corridor, and the reason the Bellevue shelf buildings trade on walkability rather than on the village strip alone. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/centennial-seawalk
- **Village-core changes are design-reviewed, not just permit-checked.** The Dundarave Village Neighbourhood Centre is a named Form & Character Development Permit area in the OCP, so exterior work in the core is reviewed against design guidelines — including for the low-rise strata buildings a buyer might plan to renovate. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **The "Dundarave" retirement supply is actually at 22nd Street.** Westerleigh PARC's own marketing places it "on the edge of the Dundarave neighborhood" — a few blocks east of the 24th–25th Street village, next to the West Vancouver Community Centre and Seniors' Activity Centre corridor. Buyers searching "Dundarave retirement" are usually being shown this rental building. https://parcliving.ca/blog/the-best-senior-living-location-in-west-vancouver-bc/
- **The Blue Bus is municipal.** West Vancouver's bus system is owned and operated by the District of West Vancouver under contract to TransLink — it is not a TransLink-operated service, which is why Dundarave's Marine Drive service has its own local character. https://www.westvancouver.ca/transportation-roads/blue-bus
