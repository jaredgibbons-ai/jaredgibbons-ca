## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted Elgin Chantrell complexes — both are strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, to be provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, any special levies approved but not yet due, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government, strata Forms information: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). For The Crescent, the Form B is also where the title conflict flagged in §2.1 gets resolved — the certificate and the registered strata plan (LMS4241) govern, not listing copy.
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots are UNDER the threshold and exempt (BC Government, depreciation reports: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). Both Elgin Chantrell complexes are well above five lots.
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government, strata finances: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a strata can no longer restrict rentals. This matters directly at Elgin Pointe, where older listing copy says "no rentals": that restriction is legally void and must not be repeated as a current rule (BC Government, strata rental rules: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate, municipal matter (below).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions added May 1, 2023 for caregivers and for spouses/partners and specified family members (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). The Crescent is marketed and documented as a 55+ community — that form of bylaw remains valid; confirm its exact wording (including any "55+ with exceptions" clauses) on the Form B.

**Fee-simple contrast.** Neither verified Elgin Chantrell complex is fee-simple — both are strata, so buyers get a Form B, monthly strata fees and strata bylaws. A true fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse would have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association — and the diligence package is correspondingly thinner (Strata Property Act scope: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_01).

SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex, and ALR parcels are governed by the ALR rules first.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, to a maximum rebate of $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). It applies to NEW housing only — both Elgin Chantrell complexes are resale stock, which does not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate (GST/HST).** Separate from the first-time buyer rebate: a 36% rebate of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 of value (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax (PTT).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion of a property valued above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000).
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time home buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 of value where the fair market value is $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption up to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP withdrawal limit is $60,000 from RRSPs (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); the FHSA allows $40,000 of contributions at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC mortgage default insurance premiums for high-ratio purchases run from 0.6% to 4.5% of the mortgage amount depending on the loan-to-value ratio (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research file documents no CMHC premium error on an Elgin Chantrell page.
- **Presales.** No presale townhouse project is verified in Elgin Chantrell in this pass, so the REDMA rules are stated for completeness only where a presale is later found: a buyer of a presale unit has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
