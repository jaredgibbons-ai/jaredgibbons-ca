## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Skye and Hollyview are both strata (one conventional, one bare-land), so the strata block governs Holly's verified stock; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast. City rules are cited once for Delta in this file.

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request (maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page), and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. In a bare-land strata like Hollyview Estates or Ashley Green, the Form B is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain — the house itself is the owner's problem. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. Skye (56 lots) and Hollyview (13 lots) are both inside this rule; a strata under five lots would sit UNDER that threshold. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving the budget at the AGM. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and any directory line saying a Holly complex has "rentals not allowed" is VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. No Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Presale rules (REDMA) — directly relevant to Skye.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18 framework, deposits held in trust), and under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act the buyer has seven days to rescind, starting on the later of signing the purchase agreement or signing the written acknowledgement of the disclosure statement. Read the Skye disclosure statement, not the marketing, for unit count, phasing and completion dates. Whether a Skye contract can be assigned, and on what fee and consent terms, is set by the purchase contract and disclosure statement: UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse or row house has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus covenants or HOA terms on title only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Holly, so a "no strata" claim on a Holly listing must be proved on title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots and combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction.** A new Skye townhome carries GST; a resale does not. The first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged as such wherever met. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new 60B Street townhome. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Down-payment programs and mortgage insurance.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across its loan-to-value bands. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
