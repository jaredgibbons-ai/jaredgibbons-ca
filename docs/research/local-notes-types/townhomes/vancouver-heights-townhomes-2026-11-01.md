## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata frame):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a Heritage Green unit marketed as a "townhouse," the Form B and strata plan are what settle the unit's form — the directory's townhouse-variant filter does not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (relevant to any future small R1 multiplex strata on a Heights house lot — check the lot count before assuming a report exists). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. The counted apartment buildings (22–28 published units where counts exist) sit above the 5-lot threshold. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The building-directory lines for The Sienna, Heritage Green, Evergreen Place and The Heights all carry a "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element — **each such line is VOID in-entry** to the extent it purports to restrict long-term rentals, and it is flagged as such in §2 and in the condos file wherever it is met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-children exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. The 55-plus sibling verifies **no** 55+ ownership in this sub-area: no counted building carries a verified age bylaw, and Seton Villa's minimum resident age is UNCONFIRMED (it is rental in any case). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** This sub-area's dominant product is the fee-simple detached house: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026; Heights Urban Village direction for Hastings-fronting lots) plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Older homes here predate modern drainage and oil-tank rules — the research file's tip to budget for an oil-tank scan and perimeter-drain inspection on pre-1970s houses is carried as UNVERIFIED general guidance, not a sourced area fact, exactly as the research file flags it. Parcel-level flood, slope or geotechnical hazard status: UNVERIFIED per lot.
- **SSMUH on house lots:** under the R1 SSMUH District, most Heights lots allow 3–6 homes (6 only within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop) and multi-unit product may be stratified — so two otherwise similar houses can be worth very different amounts to builders depending on lot area and the 400 m transit test (the R5/R2 Hastings corridor makes that test live on many lots). Review the Burnaby 2050 OCP land use map and the Heights Urban Village direction before assuming redevelopment or assembly value on Hastings-fronting and near-Hastings properties, because the new OCP takes precedence over older community plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Burnaby — the name trap, stated expressly:** the EHT is a City of Vancouver tax (3% of assessed value) and it does **not** apply in Vancouver Heights, because Vancouver Heights is in Burnaby. A buyer importing a Vancouver tax checklist on the strength of the name will budget a tax that does not exist here. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is its own regime and is not the EHT.
- **City property taxes (2026):** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (including a Siena presale purchase), not resales.
