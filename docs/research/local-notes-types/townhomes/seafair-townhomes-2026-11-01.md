## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Edgewater Park (built 1975) the depreciation report is where envelope and rainscreen history and remaining obligations surface — reconcile it against any listing claim about completed siding / window work. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — both Seafair complexes are well above the threshold on any of their published unit counts. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The "rentals allowed with restrictions" lines published for Edgewater Park and Seafair West therefore cannot amount to a long-term ban, and any directory "rentals not allowed" line found for a Seafair complex on a Form B package is flagged VOID in-entry for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither counted complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership complex inside Seafair — no home here may be marketed as a 55+ purchase. Age bylaws per complex: UNCONFIRMED beyond the absence above; check the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's detached stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning:** houses sit in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. The zoning district of a specific strata complex parcel: UNVERIFIED in this pass — confirm in Bylaw 8500 for the parcel. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the city states them:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Official Community Plan:** the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, covers Seafair (planning area 6), with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans addressing neighbourhood issues; a Seafair-specific area plan name or approved major project: UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Flood protection and Flood Bylaw 8204:** Richmond is a low-lying island city in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations including the west dike beside Seafair (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide); the system is designed for a 1:500 flood event and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise; the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction level requirements — directly relevant to the Williams Road / dyke-edge complexes. Richmond's 2026 typical single-family metered utility total is $2,251 per year, including a $342 flood protection line. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **District energy (ADEU) — not a Seafair charge:** the Alexandra District Energy Utility serves the West Cambie / Alexandra neighbourhood only (Bylaw No. 8641 is framed for buildings within the Alexandra neighbourhood). ADEU charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost; no source places Seafair in the ADEU service area, and no ADEU rate is stated for any Seafair complex. https://luluislandenergy.ca/adeu/
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies in Richmond. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both Seafair complexes are resale stock (1975 and 2003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Seafair presale is counted in this file; the frame is stated for completeness only. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
