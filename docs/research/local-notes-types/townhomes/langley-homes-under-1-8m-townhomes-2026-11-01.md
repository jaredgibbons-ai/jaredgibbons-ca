## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This applies to a strata townhouse in either Langley municipality; it does not apply to a fee-simple unit (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met for a Langley complex (for example Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace in the Willoughby file) is flagged VOID in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Directly relevant in this band: Willow Park Estates and Huntsfield Green are published 55+ townhouse communities, counted in their home files. Highgate Terrace (Willoughby) is age-restricted with the exact age UNCONFIRMED — never state it as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. Willoughby's one verified fee-simple townhouse complex (the Westbrooke row homes) is counted in the Willoughby file. (Willoughby townhomes sibling on disk.)
- **TOWNSHIP OF LANGLEY frame (Township sub-areas only):** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. The compact-lot pattern is R-CL (s. 403): lots from a minimum of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m², minimum frontage 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded. **SSMUH = Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — NO 6-plex tier exists in it** (a live page claiming one is WRONG; no such claim is documented in this file's research, so no defect is asserted for this page on that point). Secondary suite cap: 120 m². Coach house = fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata. Willoughby Community Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 = first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — this file's research describes them as having replaced the plan, which conflicts with that reading-stage record; the reading-stage record governs here and adoption stays UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
