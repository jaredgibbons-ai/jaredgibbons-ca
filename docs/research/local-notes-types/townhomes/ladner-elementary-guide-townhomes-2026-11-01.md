# Ladner Elementary — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. "Ladner Elementary" is a Paragon sub-area name for central Ladner, in the City of Delta — it follows Ladner Elementary School, 5016 44 Avenue, and is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name. The area is planned under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), including the Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas. https://le.deltasd.bc.ca/ and http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This sub-area holds both tenures side by side, so the strata block and the fee-simple block both apply — which one governs a purchase depends on the complex in Section 2.

- **Form B first (strata complexes only).** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. Manor House, Faireharbour and Apex Homes purchases run on this document; a 48 Landing purchase has no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple, plainly.** A 48 Landing row house has NO strata corporation, NO Form B and NO strata fees — the buyer deals with municipal rules (zoning, the village development permit areas below) and any covenants or easements on title only. Insurance, exterior maintenance and drainage are entirely the owner's. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle (strata).** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. For 1976-built Faireharbour, that report is the central diligence document. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10% (strata).** Since November 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving the budget at the AGM. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata).** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, whatever an older directory entry says. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. Any "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this sub-area is void in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives (strata).** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01) cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and — since May 1, 2023 — a younger spouse or partner and defined children of a qualifying resident. The bylaw restricts who may live in the unit, not who may own it. No townhouse complex in this sub-area is verified as 55+; the sub-area's verified 55+ building (Delta Green) is apartment form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Presale / new-construction rules (REDMA) — relevant to Bridge & Elliott and to any developer-sold townhouse.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement, and under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act the buyer has seven days to rescind, starting on the later of signing the purchase agreement or signing the written acknowledgement of the disclosure statement; deposits are generally held in trust. The disclosure-statement duty sits in REDMA s. 18's framework. Assignment terms are set by the contract and disclosure statement — no published assignment term for a complex here was verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled, plus the village permit areas.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates — neither number is asserted as settled here. On top of zoning, the OCP's Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) development permit areas apply in the core — the LVC guidelines address the Village's historic buildings and shape exterior work, which matters for strata exterior renovations and for a fee-simple row house alike. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — it contains NO lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. Inside a strata complex the bylaws sit on top; on a fee-simple lot the municipal permission is the whole test (plus title covenants). http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **GST on new construction.** 48 Landing homes and any developer-sold new townhouse carry GST; resale townhomes do not. The first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs and mortgage insurance.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across its loan-to-value bands. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the actual townhouse choice in the village core?** Four verified complexes in two tenures: strata townhouses at Manor House (2025), Faireharbour (1976) and Apex Homes (2025), and fee-simple non-strata row houses at 48 Landing (2026). Everything else named for this sub-area is either an apartment building or UNCONFIRMED. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What is the practical difference between 48 Landing and Manor House?** Tenure. Manor House is strata: monthly fees, bylaws, a Form B, a CRF and a depreciation report. 48 Landing is fee-simple: no fees and no bylaws, but also no strata carrying the roof, exterior or shared systems — and as new construction it carries GST, partly recoverable for eligible first-time buyers under Bill C-4. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4778-48b-street and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Faireharbour was built in 1976 — what should I check first?** The depreciation report and the minutes. A strata of five or more lots must refresh its depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024; its envelope, roof and drainage items, plus the CRF balance and any approved-but-unpaid levy on the Form B, are the purchase. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata townhouse here?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A fee-simple row house has no strata rental rule at all — municipal and title rules are the only test. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets or ages restricted at the townhouse complexes?** UNCONFIRMED for Manor House, Faireharbour and Apex Homes — no public source checked states a current pet or age bylaw for any of them. Read the registered bylaws in the Form B package; do not rely on listing adjectives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse here?** No verified one. The sub-area's verified 55+ building is Delta Green, and it is apartment form (24 units, EPS8064, built 2021); Ladner's other verified 55+ stock (South Pointe, Laburnum Park Place) is bare-land strata. Townhouse-form 55+ stock in Ladner: none verified. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/179807/
- **Q: I bought a presale in the village — how long do I have to change my mind?** Seven days under REDMA s. 21, from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. After that, the contract binds — including its assignment terms. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Q: Is the village core a heritage area that limits renovations?** The Ladner Village Core development permit area in the OCP addresses the Village's historic buildings and can shape exterior renovations and redevelopment in the core — relevant to strata exterior work and to a fee-simple owner's renovation alike, not to interior unit renovations. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8117/7676/8582/5ae6d3e43e2c83050f29daf504b779ec_Ladner20Village20Core20LVC.pdf
- **Q: What about flooding and the slough?** Ladner is low land behind the City's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection and 31 drainage pump stations — and any property within 30 m of a stream, including Chilukthan Slough and drainage ditches, is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Strata buyers should also read the water-damage deductible on the Form B insurance summary. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Will my kids automatically attend Ladner Elementary if I buy in this sub-area?** Not automatically — SD37 catchments are address-based. The locator result for the school's own address, 5016 44 Avenue, is Ladner Elementary with Delta Secondary, but every home address must be checked in the SD37 School Locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

- **The newest townhouse supply arrived in two tenures at once.** Manor House (strata, 2025) on Arthur Drive, Apex Homes (strata, 2025) on 48 Avenue and 48 Landing (fee-simple, 2026) on 48B Street all landed within about a year — central Ladner's townhouse choice went from one 1976 complex (Faireharbour) to four complexes, and the tenure split is the first thing to check on any new listing. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3103291-4569-arthur-drive-2-delta-ladner-elementary-homes-for-sale-1299000/brochure and https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/4778-48b-street

It belongs to the apartment roster, never the townhouse count. https://renx.ca/smaller-bc-communities-growing-up-as-big-devs-come-to-town and https://headwaterprojects.com/development/bridge-and-elliott

- **Ladner Village revitalization is an active City program.** The City's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan, including the Chisholm Street Wharf project, is the named project set for the core where all four townhouse complexes sit. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
