## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the strata rules that govern the sub-area's one strata building and any strata product that verifies later, and the City/RA-1 rules that govern the neighbourhood's actual stock.

- **Form B diligence (any strata, including Gulf View Court):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — relevant to any small strata that ever forms on the sub-area's conventional edge streets. Gulf View Court (24 units) sits above the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term-rental licensing rules apply on top in this city. This rule is live in-entry for Gulf View Court (§2): its listing data carries a rental-restriction field, and any such bylaw is void for long-term rentals regardless of what the field says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No S.W. Marine building is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — the 55-plus pass verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in the sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. That is the basis on which almost all of S.W. Marine's detached stock trades; do not import strata expectations into a fee-simple RA-1 purchase, or fee-simple expectations into Gulf View Court, which is strata.
- **RA-1 and the Southlands Plan (the rules that produce the zero):** the RA-1 (Limited Agriculture) District, created in 1955 for Southlands, permits one-family dwellings and, in specific circumstances, infill one-family dwellings — which is why there is effectively no townhouse supply inside Southlands itself. The ALR designation has applied over the area since 1973 (confirm the specific parcel's ALR status with the ALC), and the Southlands Plan (1988) remains the area plan, now read within the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026). Elsewhere in the sub-area, standard detached lots fall under the R1-1 multiplex rules (By-law No. 13817 amending the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575: up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units) — but RA-1 lots are governed by their own district schedule, not R1-1, and must be checked individually. Do not quote R1-1 permissions for a Southlands lot without a zoning confirmation. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-southlands.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/sw-marine.md)
- **Flood rules:** the Flood Plain Standards and Requirements apply flood construction levels (FCLs) and setbacks to lands in the designated floodplain, which includes the Fraser River coastal floodplain, with a dedicated Section 6.0 for the RA-1 District, under the Vancouver Building By-law. For riverfront and low-lying lots, pull the City's designated-floodplain dataset, confirm the FCL, and look for registered flood covenants on title before subject removal.
