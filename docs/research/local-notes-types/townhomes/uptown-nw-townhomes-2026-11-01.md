## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (any Uptown strata, incl. Penny Lane):** SPA s.59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page. In Uptown's older low-rise stock the depreciation report is the first-order document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation / CRF:** 5+ lot stratas: depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws VOID** since Nov 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: The Woodward's "rentals not allowed" line and Queens Terrace's restriction element (both condos sibling) are VOID for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Uptown building verified to carry one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 4. What buyers ask

- **Q: Which townhouse complexes are verified in Uptown?** None counted as townhomes — 0 new (0 strata, 0 fee-simple, 0 presales). Uptown's own research leaves named townhome complexes UNVERIFIED, and its verified attached stock is 7 apartment-condo entries in the condos file. (§2)
- **Q: Is Penny Lane a townhouse complex?** Its listings split: one unit is filed Apartment, another Row/Townhouse and marketed as a townhome. It is counted once, in condos, on the apartment-listing evidence with the split stated — documented here, never double-counted. Buy/title it as the strata low-rise it is and settle form from the strata plan/Form B. (§2)
- **Q: Can I rent out an Uptown strata long-term?** Yes — rental bylaws void since Nov 24, 2022, including The Woodward's printed "rentals not allowed" line (VOID, §2/§3). Short-term rentals can still be restricted. (§3)
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse in Uptown?** No verified 55+ ownership of any form here; the seniors buildings on Sixth/Queens are non-profit rental or assisted living, excluded in §2. (§2)
- **Q: RT-A/RT-B — is that Uptown townhouse zoning?** No. Those districts do not exist; the real districts are RT-2–RT-2C + RT-3/3A/3B in Bylaw No. 6680. Any copy repeating RT-A/RT-B repeats the research error corrected in this file. (§3)
- **Q: How did Uptown sell in Sept 2026?** Attached 108 listed / 13 sold (12%); detached 14 / 0. Community attached median $560,000 (combined, never townhome-only). (§1)
- **Q: Is Uptown in the floodplain?** No floodplain zone for the plateau was confirmed — Zones 3 (Quay/Downtown) and 4 (Brunette) are the low edges below/away from it. Confirm a specific lot with the City. (§1)
- **Q: Which schools serve an Uptown home?** Address-based: east-side sample → Lord Kelvin; west-side sample → École Lord Tweedsmuir; both → Fraser River Middle + New Westminster Secondary (the district's only secondary, already above capacity). (§1)
- **Q: What does the Frequent Transit Development Area label mean for my lot?** A planning direction for the core in Our City 2041 (Bylaw 7925, 2017), not a rezoning — confirm the exact designation/zoning with the City before pricing in redevelopment value. (§3)
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station in Uptown?** No — New Westminster + Columbia stations are downtown at the foot of the hill; routes 105/106 connect Uptown to them. Test the hill walk from the specific building. (§1)
