## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no verified strata stock here; this is the standard BC frame for strata purchases a buyer from this area makes in Thornhill, and the fee-simple frame that actually applies in Websters Corners.

- **Form B (strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Form B exists for a Websters Corners acreage house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory rental line is met for a complex in this file (there is none), so no in-entry VOID flag arises here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No strata here carries one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; municipal rules (Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019) plus registered covenants/HOA only. Websters Corners houses are fee-simple in exactly this sense; diligence is title, zoning, ALR, well and septic.
- **City zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 — area outside the Urban Area Boundary, RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha, A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha. **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, resolved nowhere** (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024). Settled adoption dates in this program: OCP updates adopted November 25, 2025; **Albion Area Plan adopted October 28, 2025** — the guide page's Planning section grouping Albion with "November 2025" adoptions is a defect documented in this slug's own research. Lougheed Transit Corridor **ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026** per City record (Bylaw 7781-2021; corridor ends at 221 Street, far west of here). North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres); it sets designations, does not approve construction. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/websters-corners-guide.md)
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per lot in listed zones including A-1, A-2, A-3, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation also governs (s. 31 suite in principal residence; s. 32 additional residence only if conditions met). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and additional 5% September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT 1% on first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on residential portion above $3M; a row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of 3%+2% — no such row is documented in this slug's research. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on first $500,000 at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phase to $1.5M, max $50,000, Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST on new construction, not resales. **CMHC 2.80%–4.00% error: NOT documented in this slug's research and therefore NOT asserted.** https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
