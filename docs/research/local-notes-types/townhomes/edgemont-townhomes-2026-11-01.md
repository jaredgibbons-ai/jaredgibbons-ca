## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Edgemont Ridge Estates the Form B package is doubly important in this file: it is where the STRATA tenure carried in §2 (inferred from strata fees on current listings) is confirmed lot by lot, and where its UNCONFIRMED unit count can be settled from the strata plan. At Edgemont Villa — documented, not counted, in §2 — the exact wording of the 55+ bylaw is likewise confirmed from the bylaws attached to the Form B, not from any listing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Connaught's project strata and Edgemont Ridge Estates (1991 — the older building, where the report carries the most weight) are both above the threshold on their published/attributed scale; Edgemont Ridge's exact lot count is UNCONFIRMED in this file (§2), so its report duty is stated as the project-level position a buyer confirms on the Form B. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (Crescentview's published no-Airbnb policy, in the apartment section of §2, is exactly that kind of bylaw item). No counted complex in §2 carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry — both counted complexes' rental positions are UNCONFIRMED — so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

The locator results that are on record split the neighbourhood: 4501 Highland Boulevard → Canyon Heights Elementary / Handsworth Secondary; 3150 Colwood Drive → Highlands Elementary / Handsworth Secondary. Confirm the exact address with SD44. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house to an Edgemont house instead?** In many cases yes as a housing form: since June 2024 the District permits a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, subject to the lot's zone and any creek/slope development permit areas. (§3 above)
- **Q: What are the District taxes for budgeting?** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. Strata units carry their own utility arrangements — confirm on the Form B. (§3 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Edgemont's attached supply stands on two streets, not one.** The condos program found the entire apartment supply on Connaught Crescent (Edgemont Villa 2003, Connaught 2019, Crescentview 2023) — but the townhouse supply splits: Connaught's 23 townhomes are on that same crescent, while Edgemont Ridge Estates (1991) sits on Edgemont Boulevard itself, a generation older than everything on the crescent. A buyer comparing "Edgemont townhouses" is comparing a 1991 gated strata against a 2019 concrete project component — different depreciation, different CRF questions, same village. (§2 above)
- **Connaught answers to two civic numbers and two project totals.** Current unit listings use 3220 Connaught Crescent while the developer/presale sources use 3230 Connaught Crescent for the same project, and the totals run 82 homes (Grosvenor: 59 apartments + 23 townhomes) against 83 apartments and townhomes in an NVRC document. Settle the address and the unit's strata plan from the title and Form B, not from either marketing figure. (§2 above)
- **The village grocery site was rebuilt around the attached stock.** The Grosvenor Edgemont project redeveloped the village's main grocery site at the Edgemont/Ridgewood gateway with an expanded grocery store (Thrifty Foods), ground-floor shops and its 82 condos and townhomes — the retail that Edgemont Villa, Crescentview, Connaught and Amica residents walk to is this renewed core, not the strip it replaced. https://www.nvrc.ca/sites/default/files/2022-09/grosvenor_edgemont_public_call_for_art_submissions_v3b.pdf
- **The care ladder is in the same few blocks.** A buyer can move from an owned townhome or suite in the village core to rental independent/assisted/memory and licensed residential care at Amica Edgemont Village (3225 Highland Boulevard) without leaving the village — Amica is registered with the provincial Assisted Living Registry, operator Edgemont Senior Living Inc. https://www.amica.ca/edgemont-village (§2 above)
- **The elementary catchment split runs through the neighbourhood.** The Highland Boulevard side samples to Canyon Heights Elementary and the Colwood Drive side to Highlands Elementary in the SD44 locator, both feeding Handsworth Secondary — a street-by-street fact that affects resale to families even for a townhouse buyer thinking about exit value. No locator run for either counted complex's address is on record — do not assume which side of the split they fall on. (§1 above)
- **Creek ravines are a permit fact, not just scenery.** Mosquito Creek and Mackay Creek run through Edgemont's ravines; District planning material describes Mosquito Creek as a "creekside natural area with well used trails" and MacKay Creek as a "creekside natural area with fish habitat," and the OCP's hazard Development Permit Areas (Streamside, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard) apply to creek and slope lots — relevant to the sloped plateau lots around both counted complexes. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4096768 (§3 above)
- **A "55+" search and a "townhouse" search return the same wrong building here.** Edgemont Villa is the answer to the first search (apartment form, 55-plus program) and the most common false hit on the second — the two programs keep it in exactly one place each, and this file's §2 entry exists so the townhouse file explains the miss instead of absorbing it. (§2 above)
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for North Vancouver.** The SnapStats GVR September 2026 report contains no North Vancouver section at all — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for Edgemont has no source in the named stats program and should be treated as UNVERIFIED; per-sub-area medians exist only in Paragon.
