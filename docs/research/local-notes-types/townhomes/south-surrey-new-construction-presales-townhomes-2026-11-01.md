## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Presales / REDMA — core to this file.**

- **REDMA s.21 — 7-day rescission:** a presale buyer may rescind by written notice within 7 days of the later of signing the purchase agreement and acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement. **REDMA s.18 — deposits in trust:** purchase deposits must be held in trust as the Act requires. The disclosure statement — not the listing card, not the presentation centre — governs completion dates, deposits and what is being bought; have a lawyer read it inside the 7-day window (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
- **The regulator:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page states the developer pays the buyer's agent's fees and that registration order matters for representation — register with your own agent before a first presentation-centre visit (https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers; research file §§4, 6).
- **The live page's presale summary is site copy:** its REDMA disclosure/7-day account, its deposits "usually 10%–20% total, staged over construction and held in trust" line, its 2-5-10 warranty terms (2 years materials and labour, 5 years building envelope, 10 years structure), its Newly Built Home Exemption thresholds and its GST rebate thresholds were carried in the research file as site copy attributed there to BCFSA presales information, UNVERIFIED on an official source in that pass. The REDMA s.21/s.18 frame above is stated from the Act itself; every other figure in this section is stated from its own official source, not from the page (research file §4).

**The strata block (applies to a presale project once it completes and registers, and to completed strata townhomes).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A presale has no Form B until the strata exists — the REDMA disclosure statement is the presale equivalent (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both Harlowe's townhome and condominium components sit over the threshold once complete, on the developer's stated 86-home total. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term tenancies; any directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry wherever it surfaces. Short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions) — and none of Harlowe, Calais or Rockford is a 55+ project; a South Surrey presale 55+ claim is an error wherever it appears (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. One feed card on this page is a move-in-ready half duplex with no strata fees (research file §1) — fee-simple form is settled on title for the specific lot, not from the card.

**City rules — two municipalities, never mixed.**

- **Surrey:** Surrey 2050 OCP **Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026) — cited as 21900, NEVER 18020 as current; Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. **SSMUH (July 8, 2024):** 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; secondary suite cap **40% of the principal habitable area**. Surrey STRs are permitted, owner-only — licence figure CONFLICT: **$358/yr carried, $350/yr + fees disclosed** (City 2024 corporate report proposal); check the City licence page before quoting (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **White Rock (Foster Martin ground):** White Rock's OWN bylaws — Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP 2017 No. 2220 — never Surrey's (https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only — every presale in §2).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST applies to new construction, not resales — a nearly-new resale like the King & Crescent card carries no GST. **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).

Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; **newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000** — the exemption most presale buyers in this file will test against (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).

**Q: What protection does a BC presale buyer have?**
A: Under REDMA the developer must give you a disclosure statement before you sign, you may rescind by written notice within 7 days of the later of signing and acknowledging it (s.21), and deposits are held in trust (s.18). Have a lawyer read the disclosure statement inside that window (§3).

**Q: Can I plan a move on a card's completion wording?**
A: No. "Estimated completion is early 2028" is the developer's estimate in listing text; the disclosure statement's date range and amendment history are the only project documents that govern (§§2.1, 3; research file §6).

**Q: Do I pay property transfer tax and GST on a new South Surrey townhome?**
A: GST: yes, 5% on a developer sale, subject to the FTHB GST rebate (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025) and the older New Housing Rebate. PTT: the general tiers apply, subject to the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) for a qualifying new principal residence. A resale — even a nearly-new one — carries no GST (§3).

A: Both bases are on the record and neither is settled: the feed files its card under Pacific Douglas, while the project's own listing text describes it as being in Grandview Heights. The project is documented once, here, as a presale, with the conflict flagged (§2.1).

**Q: Are any of these presales 55+?**

Adaptable-homes language (Calais) is design, not an age bylaw. A 55+ bylaw is in any case a strata-bylaw matter that exists only once a strata is registered, confirmed on the Form B after completion (§3; 55-plus sibling §2).
