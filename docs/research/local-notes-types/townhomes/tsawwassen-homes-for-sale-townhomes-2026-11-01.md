## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every Tsawwassen strata townhome in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Monterra's and Sundial's registered bylaws are the diligence, not directory lines (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: Monterra's recorded "Rentals Not Allowed" line and The Lexington's "rentals not permitted" line are both **void for long-term rentals**; rentals are allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Tsawwassen's only verified 55+ bylaws sit in the two Central apartment-form buildings in §2.4; Monterra's "Adult Oriented" site-influence line states no age bylaw and is NOT a 55+ verification (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** High Street Rowhomes (§2.2) is the worked example: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. TFN leasehold (§2.6) is neither fee-simple nor strata — it is a third tenure, priced off its own lease documents.
