## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **THE defining diligence item for this sub-area is the Heritage Conservation Area — not floodplain** (a floodplain zone for Queens Park is UNVERIFIED; Zone 3 belongs to the Quay and Downtown NW files and is never imported here). The Queen's Park HCA is currently the only HCA in New Westminster: **all** properties in it are classed Protected or Non-protected regardless of age. A Heritage Alteration Permit (HAP) is required for new buildings, demolition, subdivision, and changes to the front, sides or visible roofline of a Protected property — and for some Protected-property exterior changes that need no Building Permit at all. The Heritage Property Maintenance Standards Bylaw applies to Protected properties. In-kind maintenance needs no HAP. Protected-property incentives: additional density, density transfer, special Building Code provisions, relaxed laneway/carriage guidelines, and no heritage permit fees. For the counted strata (Clinton Place, built 1974, inside the HCA geography): exterior and common-property work can engage the HAP regime — confirm the parcel's Protected/Non-protected status and the strata's obligations with the City before pricing any exterior project; parcel-level status for any specific property: UNVERIFIED here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/planning-building-and-development/projects-on-the-go/articles/5870.php and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Queens_Park_HCA_Statement_of_Significance_and_Guidelines_2017_11_14%20(1).pdf
- **Form B diligence (Clinton Place):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Clinton Place's 14 lots put it over the threshold; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER it. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. A 1974-vintage complex makes the current depreciation report the single most important document in the package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Clinton Place's published restrictions are UNCONFIRMED in this file — any directory line a buyer meets stating rentals are not allowed is flagged VOID for long-term rentals under this rule; the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern what remains. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Clinton Place is not published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (none verified here):** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. In Queens Park the fee-simple stock is the HCA's heritage houses, which carry the HAP regime above instead of strata rules — a different diligence file entirely. Basis: the tenure definitions applied across this program + the HCA sources above.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw **No. 6680, 2001**; OCP **Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**, with the HCA at Schedule B via Amendment Bylaw 7926, 2017. Queens Park's house stock sits in RS districts (RS-1: minimum site 557.40 m² / 6,000 sq ft, FSR 0.5) overlaid by the HCA — subdivision inside the HCA also requires an HAP. The city's townhouse districts are RT-2–RT-2C (ss. 410–413) and RT-3/3A/3B in Queensborough (ss. 417–419) — no research file for this area cited the non-existent "RT-A/RT-B," and none is corrected here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Zoning_Bylaw_6680_2001%20(consolidated%20June%208,%202026).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php
- **Suites as the City states them:** one secondary suite per house; the City allows a property to have both a secondary suite and a laneway/carriage house (Zoning Bylaw s. 190.29 — confirm the current section text in the consolidated bylaw before relying on it); inside the HCA, Protected properties get relaxed laneway/carriage guidelines as an incentive. Adaptable housing: at least 40% of single-storey units in multiple-unit buildings must be adaptable (Adaptable Housing Bylaw 7464, 2011). https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/development/planning-and-development/planning/secondary-suites-and-laneway-carriage-houses
- **Property tax:** City of New Westminster taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 3, 2026. The City also runs its own electrical utility. There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in New Westminster — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/taxes-and-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (Clinton Place is a resale). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **The apostrophe is a filing system:** board data says "Queens Park," the City says "Queen's Park" — searches, titles and page copy that mix the two will miss records in one spelling or the other. - **Protected status ignores age:** in the Queen's Park HCA, a 1974 strata and a 1910 house are both classed — Protected or Non-protected — by designation, not by vintage; "it's not a heritage house" is not an exemption argument. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Queens_Park_HCA_Statement_of_Significance_and_Guidelines_2017_11_14%20(1).pdf
