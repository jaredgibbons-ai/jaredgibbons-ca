## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Park Place, and any strata unit in the carried River District developments):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Park Place's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so confirm its position from the Form B package. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" line for Park Place's 3461 East 49th Avenue address is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No counted Killarney VE townhouse complex carries a published 55+ bylaw — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ strata ownership stock in this area at all; the area's seniors product is care and rental housing, not ownership stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Killarney VE in this pass; the area's fee-simple ground-oriented stock is the detached house under R1-1 (below), and a Killarney listing that reads as a townhouse but shows no strata should be title-checked for leasehold (Champlain Heights) before it is read as fee-simple.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex, 464 m² for a multiple dwelling with 5 units, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. East Fraser Lands parcels are under CD-1 districts. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan:** the East Fraser Lands Official Development Plan (River District) applies at the south edge — a mainly residential community with a variety of housing types and a commercial centre on the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site, with Wesgroup Properties owning the majority of the land; Area 1, Area 2 and Kerr Street have been rezoned with Official Development Plan / CD-1 amendments enacted. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Flood:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — this applies to the Fraserlands / River District foreshore; whether a specific Killarney parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED, check the City dataset parcel by parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared vacant; every Vancouver owner must file an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example WeKillarney if bought from the developer), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for WeKillarney and any future River District townhouse release. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a standalone townhouse complex in Killarney itself?** One verified: Park Place, 3417–3461 East 49th Avenue — Freehold Strata townhomes beside Killarney Park and the community centre. Everything else townhouse-form is a component of a River District development, an unnamed small strata, or Champlain Heights product filed under its own sub-area. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/park-place-3425-east-49-ave
- **Q: Is Champlain Heights part of Killarney or not?** Both, depending on the map: the City counts Champlain Heights inside its Killarney local area, while MLS treats Champlain Heights as its own sub-area. The practical difference is tenure — much of Champlain Heights is City leasehold or co-op, which is why this program files it separately. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **Q: A Champlain Heights townhouse looks cheap for Vancouver — why?** Usually the tenure: homes on City of Vancouver prepaid 99-year leases expiring in the 2080s, or co-op homes, do not carry freehold value. Marine Woods is the caution in the other direction — one listing calls a unit there a rare freehold townhouse — so read the title, not the neighbourhood reputation. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2833579-8401-millstone-street-vancouver-bc-v5s-4s3
- **Q: Can I rent out a Park Place townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory page for one Park Place address still prints "rentals not allowed." Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — confirm on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Where are the River District townhouses?** Inside mixed developments, not in standalone complexes: Avalon 1 has eight 2-storey townhouse buildings beside its towers, MODE includes 6 townhomes among its 257 strata units, and Paradigm includes a 3-storey townhouse component among its 268 homes. All three developments are filed in this program's condos notes. https://wesgroup.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Avalon-1-Homeowner-Manual-3-1.pdf
- **Q: Is there a SkyTrain station for Killarney townhouses?** No station sits inside the area — Killarney is bus-served, with route 49 on 49th Avenue to Metrotown Station and UBC, route 26 to Joyce Station, and route 100 on Marine Drive to 22nd Street Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt049.pdf
- **Q: Does the Fraser River floodplain touch Killarney townhouses?** At the river edge, yes — the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage and the River District foreshore, with by-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it. Park Place and the house streets sit well north of the foreshore; check the specific parcel in the City dataset before pricing any river-side unit. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Which schools serve a Park Place townhouse?** Address-based in SD39 — the locator result for the research file's sample address (6454 Killarney Street) is Dr. George M. Weir Elementary and Killarney Secondary, and four elementaries (Champlain Heights, Cook, MacCorkindale, Weir) sit inside the area, so the answer changes by block. Run the exact address in the locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: What is WeKillarney?** A new 2026 townhouse collection at 2333 East 54th Avenue, marketed under that subdivision name in current listings. Its tenure is UNCONFIRMED in the sources checked — get the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and confirm strata versus fee-simple before pricing it against Park Place resales. https://www.zillow.com/homedetails/2333-E-54th-Ave-2-SL-Vancouver-BC-V5S-1X1/462707988_zpid/
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply in Killarney?** Yes — it is a Vancouver tax: 3% on homes declared vacant, an annual declaration for every owner, and a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after the payment date (April 16, 2026 for the 2025 tax year). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Killarney was forest and farms until after WWII.** The City's history records that the area remained second-growth forest and farms until after the war, and that the first non-native settler, William Rowling (1868), came to own 3.5 kilometres of Fraser River shoreline including all of Killarney's riverfront — which is why the house stock is post-war and the waterfront is all new. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **The community centre is the area's real amenity anchor.** Killarney Community Centre, 6260 Killarney Street, has an indoor pool, ice rink and fitness centre with Killarney Park beside it — and Park Place's townhomes sit on the same East 49th Avenue corridor, a fact listing copy rarely connects. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/killarney-community-centre.aspx
- **Everett Crowley Park is a former landfill turned forest park.** At 8200 Kerr Street, the City describes 40 hectares of walking trails and an off-leash dog area (the Park Board dataset figure is 38.08 hectares) — the big green space on Killarney's east side is a rehabilitated site, not old-growth. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/killarney.aspx
- **The River District sits on a sawmill site.** The East Fraser Lands plan area is the former Canadian White Pine Sawmill site, and Wesgroup Properties owns the majority of the land — one developer's phasing decisions drive nearly all of the area's new attached supply, townhouses included. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **The library serving Killarney carries the other neighbourhood's name.** The Vancouver Public Library branch for the area is the Champlain Heights Branch at 7110 Kerr Street — a small sign of how completely the two names interleave on the ground, even though the board files them as separate sub-areas. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/killarney-community-centre.aspx
- **Health care runs through Knight Street, not a Killarney hospital.** The South Community Health Centre at 6405 Knight Street provides public and community health services for South Vancouver; the research file could not confirm a nearest-hospital name and address on a health-authority page for this sub-area — UNVERIFIED. https://www.vch.ca/en/location-service/home-health-south-community-health-centre
