## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Queensborough is the City of New Westminster's island neighbourhood on the eastern tip of Lulu Island, comprising District Lots 757 and 758 in the Zoning Bylaw wording, bordered by the Fraser River (main arm) to the south and east and the North Arm to the north, and sharing Lulu Island with the City of Richmond. It is joined to mainland New Westminster only by Highway 91A, the Queensborough Connector, over the Queensborough Bridge across the North Arm to Marine Way / Stewardson Way, with Highway 91 at the south end in Richmond — every trip to Uptown, Downtown or Royal Columbian Hospital crosses that bridge. Queensborough is New Westminster (across the Fraser from the mainland city), not part of Richmond. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: (1) the Ewen Avenue corridor — Coopers Landing (935 Ewen), English Mews (1111/1135 Ewen) and the queue complexes at 843, 1211 and 728 Ewen; (2) Port Royal at the north end — Portside on Furness Street (488 Furness) beside the Salter Street apartment row documented in the condos file. Ewen Avenue is the Main Street commercial strip; Salter Street, Howes Street and Furness Street serve the island. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- Market depth: Queensborough carried the largest attached stock of any New Westminster sub-area in this batch in September 2026 — 72 condos & townhomes listings and 9 solds (13% sales ratio) against 41 detached listings and 1 sold (2%). New Westminster community-level context (SnapStats publishes medians at community level only, not for Queensborough): condos & townhomes median $560,000, $587/sqft, 95% sale-to-list, 27 days on market in September 2026 — cited exactly as the report states it, as "Condos & Townhomes" COMBINED, never as a townhome-only figure. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.
- Flood context — the area's defining diligence frame: Queensborough is Floodplain Zone 1 in the City's floodplain study: a low-lying area with high groundwater, poor compressible soils and poor drainage, protected by a shared dyking and pumped drainage system with Richmond; a perimeter dyke built in the 1960s protects approximately 350 hectares, and Queensborough is the highest priority for flood protection improvements in the city. Flood construction levels are 3.53 metres above Geodetic Survey of Canada datum for Area A and 4.20 metres for Area B. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Flood_Hazard_DPA.pdf
- On-island anchors: Ryall Park (sports fields, courts, spray park, community garden, Boro All Wheel Park) beside the Queensborough Community Centre; Port Royal Park with a riverfront trail; New Westminster Public Library's Queensborough Branch at 920 Ewen Ave. The city's main aquatic centre, the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre, 65 East Sixth Avenue, is on the mainland in Uptown. Transit: no SkyTrain station is in Queensborough; buses connect to 22nd Street Station and New Westminster Station, including TransLink route 104 (Annacis Island / 22nd Street Station, with Port Royal service on weekdays). The nearest hospital is Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street, on the mainland. https://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/book-facilities-fields-and-parks/articles/5540.php and https://www.nwpl.ca/ and https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. In Queensborough, read the Form B package together with the flood items below: insurance terms and deductibles are a live cost on this island. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are well over five lots on the sources checked (Portside 170, Coopers Landing 154; English Mews' count is UNCONFIRMED but its multi-building form is over the threshold on any reading of the listings). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Coopers Landing's listing line "rentals allowed with restrictions" is carried in Section 2 as listing data; any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Queensborough complex is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in Queensborough — every named complex checked is general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none are counted in Queensborough. If a fee-simple row home is offered here, the rule stated plainly is: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 6680) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Do not assume the Coracle "no strata fees" marketing line makes a unit fee-simple — its listing title field reads Freehold Strata (Section 2). https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3085978-260-ewen-avenue-202-new-westminster-bc-v3m-0j1/brochure
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (adopted September 17, 2001; consolidated as of June 8, 2026) governs — its Townhouse Districts (RT-2) at s. 410 state the intent "to allow townhouse development," with row houses or townhouses a permitted use; the district family is RT-2–RT-2C and RT-3/3A/3B ONLY (RT-A/RT-B do not exist in this bylaw). The Official Community Plan is Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, with the Queensborough Community Plan as a schedule; the (RLR) Residential – Low Rise designation in the OCP amendment materials (Bylaw No. 8574, 2026) provides compact-lot single detached form, low-rise buildings up to six storeys, and stacked and side-by-side townhouses. Zoning Bylaw s. 190.36 bars a Queensborough subdivision lot under one-half acre (0.20 ha) or under 66 feet (20.12 m) of frontage unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system. https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php and https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/100_Introduction(2).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Draft_Queensborough_Land_Use_Designations_and_Map___March_9_2026.pdf
- **Flood rules — the defining Queensborough diligence:** the whole neighbourhood is Floodplain Zone 1 behind a 1960s perimeter dyke protecting about 350 hectares, with a pumped drainage system shared with Richmond. The City's Flood Hazard Development Permit Area sets minimum construction levels of 3.53 metres above GSC datum for Area A and 4.20 metres for Area B; the City received $800,000 from the provincial Community Emergency Preparedness Fund for the Queensborough Dike Shoreline Protection Rehabilitation project at two locations on the northeast side of Lulu Island (behind 345 Gifford Street and behind 805 Boyd Street). Pull the lot's flood construction level and get an insurance quote before subjects come off. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Flood_Hazard_DPA.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/2023/02/14/city-of-new-westminster-receives-provincial-funding-for-queensborough-dike-rehabilitation.php
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Queens presale), not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Queensborough is in New Westminster, and the 3% EHT is Vancouver only. New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for Queens at 1005 Ewen Avenue. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Queensborough's secondary school run crosses a bridge in both directions of the decision.** Queen Elizabeth Elementary and Queensborough Middle are on the island, but New Westminster Secondary is on the mainland — a family buying a Portside or Coopers Landing townhouse for "schools on the island" is buying that arrangement only to Grade 8. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/
- **The island's newest townhouse project and its oldest counted one face each other across the same park.** Coopers Landing (2005) sits across from the Queensborough Community Centre and Ryall Park on Ewen Avenue, while Portside (2021–2023) anchors the Port Royal riverfront — the two ends of the island's townhouse story are a five-minute drive apart on one island with one bridge off it. https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/935-ewen-avenue/79 and https://anthemproperties.com/these-riverfront-homes-in-new-westminster-start-at-just-629900/
- **A Queensborough lot can be unsubdividable for a sewer reason, not a size reason.** Under Zoning Bylaw s. 190.36, no Queensborough subdivision may produce a lot under one-half acre (0.20 ha) or under 66 feet (20.12 m) of frontage unless the land can connect to the City sanitary sewer system — most lots are sewer-connected, but the connection status of a specific lot governs what can be done with it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/100_Introduction(2).pdf
- **The City runs its own electrical utility.** New Westminster operates its own electrical utility alongside City water and sewer services — a Queensborough townhouse's utility accounts are not all with the provincial providers a mainland buyer expects. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **The dyke is a named, funded work program, not background scenery.** The City's $800,000 provincially funded Queensborough Dike Shoreline Protection Rehabilitation project restabilised eroding dike slopes behind 345 Gifford Street and behind 805 Boyd Street on the northeast side of Lulu Island — a buyer on that shore can ask which reach their lot sits behind. https://www.newwestcity.ca/2023/02/14/city-of-new-westminster-receives-provincial-funding-for-queensborough-dike-rehabilitation.php
- **Care is entirely off-island.** BC's Assisted Living Registry lists New Westminster's registered seniors assisted-living residences (Thornebridge Gardens, 649 8th Avenue; Victoria Heights, 230 Ross Drive) and its long-term care home (AgeCare Royal City, 77 Jamieson Court) all on the mainland — the sibling 55-plus file's zero for Queensborough reflects the island itself, not a research gap.
