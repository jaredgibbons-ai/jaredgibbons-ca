## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Central Abbotsford" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; it is not confirmed as an official City neighbourhood name — the City plans the core through the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019). https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/community-planning/neighbourhood-plans
- The City's own commercial study describes the City Centre as roughly bordered by Peardonville Road and Mill Lake to the south, Ware Street, Mill Lake Road and Horn Street to the east, Parkview Street and Tims Street to the west, and a line roughly parallel to George Ferguson Way and Maclure Road to the north, centred on South Fraser Way. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/HDNP%2BCommercial%2BMarket%2BStudy.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: (1) the George Ferguson Way / Maclure Road corridor (Oakhurst Park, Canterbury Gate, Willband Creek Estates); (2) the Mill Lake / Ware Street edge (Summerset Place); (3) Historic Downtown's Rail District on Montrose Avenue (The Abby Townhome Series); (4) the King Road / College Park edge by UFV (The Argyle — the King Road cluster cross-referenced in Section 2 and counted once, in the Poplar file). Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- This is Abbotsford's attached-housing centre of gravity: 256 attached listings and 35 attached sales in September 2026, the most of any Abbotsford sub-area, with the fastest attached sales ratio (14%) — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/central-abbotsford-guide.md).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** every verified complex in Section 2 is strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this area; "freehold strata" in a listing is still strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate on a resale; BCREA advises reviewing it for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote (30-year projection, at least three CRF funding models); stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This is the central check on the 1980s George Ferguson/Maclure stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any older bylaw or listing line restricting long-term rentals in a Section 2 complex is void in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Oakhurst Park, Summerset Place and Willband Creek Estates are the verified 55+ townhouse complexes here — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning/plans:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; the central area is planned under the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) within Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025 as the current OCP — the research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE and flagged here as a Jerry sweep item. Historic Downtown's Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation enables rowhouses, townhouses, multiplexes and duplexes. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH/suites:** the bylaw's s.140 small-scale provision allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings. June 2024 SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED and "3560-2024" must not be asserted. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule only) — standing corrections in the commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on new townhomes (Rail District phases):** 5% GST on new construction, not resales. FTHB GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — this date governs over March 12 in some sibling files): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT:** 1% first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M; FTHB exemption on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** Rail District townhome phases sold as presales carry REDMA s.21 7-day rescission (later of signing / acknowledging the disclosure statement) and s.18 deposits in trust; assignment consent/fees are contract terms, UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings / insurance:** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a central townhouse strata or fee-simple?** Strata in every verified case — Oakhurst Park (NWS1894), Summerset Place, Willband Creek Estates, Canterbury Gate and The Abby Townhome Series all carry strata title or published strata fees. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Central Abbotsford. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3085200-61-32959-george-ferguson-way-abbotsford-bc-v2s-7w9
- **Q: Three of these complexes are 55+. What does that actually restrict?** Residency, not ownership: an under-55 buyer can own, but may not live in the unit unless exempt (younger spouse/partner, legacy resident, live-in caregiver, defined children — Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023). The bylaw is the only legal age bylaw in BC strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ townhouse complex?** For a long-term tenancy, the strata cannot rely on a rental-restriction bylaw — those are void since Nov 24, 2022 — but the tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Willband Creek Estates: pet friendly per the sibling file. Canterbury Gate: one listing states no pets for its unit. Summerset Place: listing sources conflict — UNCONFIRMED. Oakhurst Park and The Abby: UNCONFIRMED as bylaws. Read the registered bylaws for the specific complex. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is there RV parking anywhere?** Oakhurst Park lists RV parking among its amenities, with a clubhouse, per the sibling file and listings (workshop, bike storage, guest parking also listed). Stall availability and any extra charge are UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3085200-61-32959-george-ferguson-way-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2s7w9-349500/brochure
- **Q: How do I judge a 1980s complex's finances?** Read the Form B for the fee, CRF balance and approved levies, and the depreciation report for major-item timing: 5+ lot stratas must renew it every five years and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. One Oakhurst listing notes "all the pipes in complex have been replaced" for its marketing — a listing claim, not a substitute for the report. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What insurance deductible am I exposed to?** The strata's water-damage deductible, shown in the Form B insurance summary, can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused a loss. The amount for every central complex is UNCONFIRMED until its documents are read. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: I'm buying a Rail District townhouse presale. What protects me?** REDMA: a filed disclosure statement, deposits held in trust (s.18), and a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement (s.21). Completion dates and assignment terms are in the disclosure statement, not the brochure. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Q: Which schools does a central townhouse feed into?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. Locator runs in the research file: 33130 Bevan Ave returns Godson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary. Run the complex's own address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/central-abbotsford-guide.md)
- **Q: Is flood risk a central townhouse issue?** The central upland is not Sumas Prairie, but Willband Creek runs through the central area — Willband Creek Estates takes its name from it — and parcel-level floodplain status requires a City floodplain map check for the specific complex. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford replaced its Official Community Plan in December 2025.** Abbotsforward 2050 (public hearing November 25, 2025) emphasizes more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enables the 37,185 units the City's 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report targets over 20 years. The research file's "2600-2016" OCP citation is stale under the standing correction — flag for Jerry. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **The Rail District is a 10-to-12-year build-out, not a finished neighbourhood.** The 19-acre former Clayburn Brick Plant site between Pine Street and Morey Avenue is planned as five neighbourhoods in nine phases; The Abby Townhome Series (91 homes in Phase 1) and The Mason condos are early phases, and the community amenity building, The Station, serves all five planned neighbourhoods. Townhouse buyers are buying into a construction zone with the amenity payoff at the end. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/rail-district-abbotsford/ and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-abby-townhome-series-at-the-rail-district-by-infinity-properties-presale-abbotsford-townhomes/
- **The King Road cluster's sub-area label is unreliable in feeds:** the same Argyle address (34248 King Road) appears as Poplar, Abbotsford East and Central Abbotsford in different listings. The registered strata plan, not the feed label, is the stable identifier — and under this program the complex is counted once, in the Poplar townhomes file, and cross-referenced here. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3085815-34248-king-road-37-abbotsford-bc-v2s-0b1/brochure
- **Downtown's main intersection is being rebuilt:** the City is realigning Montvue Avenue — removing the single-lane connection to Essendene Avenue, connecting Montvue directly to West Railway Street, and turning the closed section into West Railway Plaza — as part of the Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan, on the Rail District townhouses' own street network. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/montvue-avenue-and-west-railway-plaza
- **The OCP's growth arrow points at the hospital–Mill Lake gap:** Abbotsforward 2050 states a new mixed-use centre between the hospital and Mill Lake Park and a Primary Transit Corridor along South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road — the corridor the George Ferguson townhouse cluster sits beside. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- **Federal Housing Accelerator money is spent, not pending:** the City was approved for up to $25.6 million in HAF money and reported in October 2026 that all HAF initiatives are complete, with multi-family permitting above expectations — part of why so much new mid-rise and townhouse product landed in the core at once. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
