## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows:** a City staff report to Council (October 23, 2024, for the November 5, 2024 meeting) states it plainly — "Short-Term Rentals: Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw" — and the proposed amendment was to clarify that prohibition and distinguish the use from other permitted uses. Separately, the Province's principal-residence requirement lists Pitt Meadows among the communities where it applies. A West Meadows (or any Pitt Meadows) townhouse business plan built on short-term rental income fails at the zoning bylaw, before any strata bylaw is read. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/short-term-rentals/principal-residence-requirement
- **Floodplain — the city's defining land rule, with a LIVE CONFLICT flagged:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the floodplain for the Fraser River and, to a smaller degree, the Pitt River — **this file carries "over 95%"**; live pages quoting "over 85%" (attributed to the City's Financial Plan 2026–2030, as the sibling guide file records for the West Meadows guide page) are a LIVE CONFLICT against the Business Plan figure and are not adopted here. The city is almost entirely protected by 60 km of dikes, plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; A-1 zoning requires residential buildings sited 15.0 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches, and the Kennedy drainage area (Area 3) in this part of the city discharges to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Form B diligence (applies to the strata townhouses a West Meadows buyer will actually meet — Shoreline and Coho II, filed under South Meadows):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no in-area strata stock to apply it to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Both rules govern the Fraser Way stratas documented in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and in Pitt Meadows they are prohibited city-wide by zoning (above), which is the stronger rule. Coho Chapter II's directory line reads rentals allowed with restrictions (listing/directory data — confirm on the Form B); any "rentals not allowed" line for a Pitt Meadows strata is VOID for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). **The Fairways' age position is UNCONFIRMED and it must never be called 55+** (The Fairways is a North Meadows condo complex in the city assembly, not West Meadows stock); the sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ housing of any tenure in West Meadows, and the Ford Road Co-operative is not a seniors building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. For farm parcels, add the ALR check: 78% of Pitt Meadows is in the Agricultural Land Reserve per the City's 2020 Business Plan. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs — A-1 General Agriculture (one farm house per lot, maximum gross floor area 500 sq m; minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha), A-2 Large Lot Agricultural (subdivision minimum 16 ha), and RS / R-3 / R-4 / R-6 in the east-edge pocket (up to 3, 4 or 6 units per lot; R-6's sixth unit is conditional on an adaptable unit; residential subdivision minimum 1,215 sq m in R-3 / R-4 / R-6). The plan is "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** secondary suites and garden suites are listed accessory uses in the RS zone; the City garden suite guide sets one suite per lot at 33 to 90 sq m, and a lot cannot have both a garden suite and a secondary suite; small-scale multi-unit housing under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings (2–6 units) where it applies. On ALR land, the ALC allows one additional detached residence without an application where one residence exists when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. Pitt Meadows 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026; the same bylaw carries a separate drainage levy — residential (Class 1) $0.1082 per $1,000, Farm class (Class 9) $1.8524 per $1,000 drainage alongside a $48.6824 per $1,000 farm general rate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are the West Meadows townhouses, actually?** There is no verified townhouse complex inside the board's West Meadows. The Fraser Way townhouses buyers find — Shoreline (19452 Fraser Way) and Coho II (19572 Fraser Way) in Osprey Village — are board-classified South Meadows and are counted in the city assembly, not here. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3097386-19452-fraser-way-54-pitt-meadows-bc-v3y-0a3/brochure
- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb in a Pitt Meadows townhouse?** No. Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw — the City states the prohibition expressly — and the provincial principal-residence requirement also applies in Pitt Meadows. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Is the Ford Road co-op a way to buy a townhouse here cheaply?** It is not a purchase of title at all: the Ford Road Housing Co-operative is membership housing (apartments plus 3–4 bedroom townhouses) — no strata lot, no fee-simple lot, nothing to resell as real estate. It is also not a 55+ building. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: How much of this area is floodplain, and what protects it?** Over 95% of Pitt Meadows lies within the Fraser and Pitt River floodplains per the City's 2020 Business Plan — not the "over 85%" figure quoted on some live pages, which is a live conflict flagged in Section 3. Protection is 60 km of dikes plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Q: Can I rent out a Fraser Way townhouse long-term?** Yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, and Coho II's directory line already reads rentals allowed with restrictions. Short-term rentals are a different answer — prohibited by City zoning (above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is anything here 55+?** No verified 55+ housing of any tenure exists in West Meadows, and the city's best-known age question — The Fairways — has an UNCONFIRMED age position and must never be called 55+. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/west-meadows-pitt-meadows-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What will actually trade here — farm, house, or townhouse?** On the September 2026 evidence: 1 detached listing, 0 attached listings, 0 sales — a thin, lumpy market where the Pitt Meadows community medians (detached $1,160,000; "Condos & Townhomes" combined $662,500) anchor pricing, not sub-area comps. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Fraser Way address?** For the sample address 19452 Fraser Way: Davie Jones Elementary (K–7) and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12) — SD42 has no middle tier, no school building sits inside this sub-area, and catchments are address-based, so run the exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Why is there a drainage levy on the tax bill?** Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 charges it separately — the cost of the diking and drainage system in a city that is over 95% floodplain. For 2026: $0.1082 per $1,000 residential, $1.8524 per $1,000 for the Farm class (plus a $48.6824 per $1,000 farm general rate) — confirm the property's BC Assessment class before quoting carrying costs. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new home, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The airport is inside the sub-area, not beside it:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK), at 1028-18799 Airport Way, has three paved runways and a water runway and is owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society — disclose aircraft activity early and use the City's airport page for the noise-concern process rather than discovering it at subject removal. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **Harris Landing, at Harris Road and Fraser Way, is the start of the Pitt River Greenway** — about 10.2 km west along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes for walking, cycling and bird watching, with the Metro Vancouver Pitt River Regional Greenway following the dike tops between Kennedy Road and Ford Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- **78% of the city is in the Agricultural Land Reserve** (City 2020 Business Plan), and A-1 subdivision minimum is 8 ha — the single most common West Meadows pricing error is valuing a farm parcel as if it could be split like a suburban lot. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Golden Ears Way is being widened from two lanes to four** between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street under a TransLink / City of Maple Ridge design agreement — the corridor change that will most affect this area's commutes, on the record before it is built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge
- **The nearest hospital is in the next city:** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge (Fraser Health), with 24/7 emergency care serving Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121-11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
- **No recreation centre or library branch sits inside the sub-area either:** the Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre is at 12027 Harris Road and the city's only library branch is Pitt Meadows Public Library, 200-12099 Harris Road — both just east of Harris Road, in Central Meadows. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/drop-schedules-hours-rates and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
