## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all counted stock is strata).** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Hawthorne complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases). No Hawthorne townhouse complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern only. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Hawthorne (Section 2), so that frame applies to no counted complex here.
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future."** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan with no lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning:** Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 assigns zoning parcel by parcel on its zoning maps; the batch-7 research file records the Ladner zone ladder (RS5 550 m² / 460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m², RD4 504 m²) — read the specific complex's zone (for example Keira Gardens' CD376) off the City maps, not off marketing. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — this affects the detached streets around the townhouse corridor, not the existing strata complexes. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Taxes:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST and rebates:** resale townhouses carry no GST; on new construction (Wildwood), the FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate is 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, with the compression flagged:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2%, not a single 5% rate. First-time buyers: no tax on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per person; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA) — Wildwood:** the developer must deliver a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. Assignment rights are contract-specific: UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this file's count different from the Hawthorne guide file's?** No — same sub-area, same stock. The guide sibling counts 3 completed strata complexes; this variant counts 0 new and cross-references them, per count-once discipline. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Are Hawthorne townhouses strata or fee-simple?** Strata — all three completed complexes counted in the guide sibling are strata tenure, and no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified. That means strata fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation report on every purchase here. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Can I rent out my townhouse?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Keira Gardens, listing records state cats and dogs OK with restrictions. For Central Park and Central Park West, no public source checked states a pet rule: UNCONFIRMED — the registered bylaws decide. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **Q: What is Oak Glenn Terrace — townhouse or condo?** UNCONFIRMED. It sits in QUEUE in both this file and the guide sibling and is counted nowhere until the strata plan settles its form. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110710-19-5661-ladner-trunk-road-delta-british-columbia-v4k1x3-710000/brochure
- **Q: How do I know if a special levy is coming?** The Form B must disclose any approved-but-unpaid special levy and the CRF balance, and the attached depreciation report shows the 30-year repair list with three funding models. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: When is Wildwood done, and what protects a presale buyer?** A presale profile states expected completion between February and April 2027 — developer-stated, not contractual. Protections are the REDMA disclosure statement, the 7-day rescission right (s. 21) and trust-held deposits (s. 18). https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/wildwood-ladner-townhomes-ladner/ and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Which schools serve the Central Avenue townhouses?** Catchments are address-based; the locator result for 5160 Central Avenue is Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12), but each complex address must be run in the SD37 locator itself. School names here use Ministry-listed names only. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Hawthorne in a flood area?** Ladner is low land protected by the City's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and any property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Confirm flood construction requirements lot by lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Is there a Hawthorne townhouse median price I can rely on?** No — SnapStats does not cover South Delta at all and publishes no townhome-only figure in any case; any "Hawthorne median townhouse price" quoted from that source does not exist. (Research file, §2.)
