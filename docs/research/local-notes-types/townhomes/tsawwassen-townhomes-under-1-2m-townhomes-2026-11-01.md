## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The Delta-side strata block governs a Central / Beach Grove / Boundary Beach purchase in this band; the leasehold paragraph governs a North purchase; the fee-simple contrast is stated because the page's own framing calls Delta-side complexes "freehold" without distinguishing freehold strata from fee-simple.

- **Form B diligence (Delta-side strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (none of the Delta-side complexes in §2 is sub-5-lot on its published count — Sundial 84, South Grove 37; The Pillars' component count is UNCONFIRMED, read off the strata plan). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met on a Delta-side complex in this band (documented examples in their home files: Sundial, Tsawwassen Greene, Monterra, Heron Cove) is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. South Grove's listing "Rentals Allowed" line is consistent with the rule but remains listing data. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Delta-side townhouse complex in §2 is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Leasehold diligence (Tsawwassen North):** there is no Form B route that substitutes for the lease documents — the diligence set is the head lease and sublease terms, the remaining term of the 99-year lease, how the leasehold prepaid-strata is structured (strata plan EPS7865 at Tsawwassen Landing on the listing record), and how Tsawwassen First Nation tax administration works for that home. Read the lease before the floor plan; lender treatment of the remaining term is a financing question to settle before subjects, not after. (Developer source: https://ownboardwalk.com/) (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified on the Delta side of this band — Sundial and The Pillars are strata, South Grove and Monterra are freehold *strata* (still strata: fees, bylaws, Form B). A page or listing calling any of them simply "freehold" without the word "strata" is compressing the tenure.
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO area-plan schedules (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950, superseded); Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"); zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. City bylaws do not govern Tsawwassen Lands parcels at all (§1). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Flood and streamside frame (Delta side):** Delta operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, and all properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — read the depreciation report and Form B against the complex's setting in that system. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Delta property taxes (Delta side):** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. Tax administration for homes on Tsawwassen Lands runs under Tsawwassen First Nation, not the City — the live page's tax-matching claim is UNVERIFIED (§3 page check). https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax — with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — **every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers**; the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise under $1.2M. A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — flagged: the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). **Worked example 1 — $900,000 townhouse, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. **Worked example 2 — $1,150,000 townhouse, no exemption:** $2,000 + 2% × $950,000 ($19,000) = **$21,000**. **First-time buyers' program against the band:** the full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so only the lower slice of this band can use the program at all, and a purchase at the band's upper end cannot; all the program's other buyer and property conditions also apply. Check the exemption against the exact price, not the band: two townhomes on the same page can sit on opposite sides of the program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **GST / rebates / programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most of this band is resale stock. No CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
