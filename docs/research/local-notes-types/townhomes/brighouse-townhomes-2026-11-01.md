## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1977 complex like Ascott Wynde, the Form B and depreciation report are the diligence — envelope, plumbing and parkade history matter more than finishes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Ascott Wynde's published 102 units put it well over it). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where met: Ascott Wynde's directory "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element is VOID (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No source states a 55+ bylaw for Ascott Wynde or any counted Brighouse building — Brighouse has ZERO verified 55+ strata (ownership) complexes per the 55-plus program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brighouse-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — City of Richmond rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. None was verified in Brighouse (§2); do not describe Ascott Wynde that way — it is strata.
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 9000, whose City Centre Area Plan designates Brighouse Village for high-density, high-rise, mixed-use development (transects to T6 / 45 m, §1). Single Detached zones RS1/RS2 in Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 apply outside the City Centre plan designations, with secondary suites under Section 5.4. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Flood rules:** new construction in Richmond is subject to flood construction levels under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204, within a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event (dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m). For a ground-oriented complex like Ascott Wynde, confirm the flood construction level position and what it means for insurance and future alterations. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Richmond property taxes (city-wide rule):** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; the City collects for other authorities including the Province (school tax), TransLink, BC Assessment, Metro Vancouver and the Municipal Finance Authority. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)

- **Richmond's first high-rises started as rentals.** The City's Heritage Inventory records that Park Towers' first two towers were completed in 1972 opening as apartments, while the third tower finished in 1974 as a stratified condo — the complex's strata history is not uniform across the three towers, which is a Form B and strata-plan question for any unit there. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/inventory/Details.aspx?ID=226 (Condos sibling, §5)
- **Airport rules cap some Brighouse heights.** The City's own Brighouse Village map notes maximum building height in parts of the village may be subject to established Airport Zoning Regulations — part of why heights step down across the core despite the T6 (45 m) transect at the centre, and part of why a 1977 ground-oriented complex survives at the village edge. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **The seniors cluster and the condo core were one combined development.** Kiwanis Towers exists because the Kiwanis Society replaced an aging seniors complex on its site with two concrete towers, financed in part by selling a portion of the site to Polygon, which also built three market condominium towers on its parcel — the subsidized seniors towers and the market towers beside them have entirely different tenure. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=42205 (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/brighouse-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Citation Drive is a cluster, not a complex.** Ascott Wynde (8751), Belmont Park (8540) and the apartment product at 8870 Citation Drive are separate strata complexes on one street at the village's south edge — a search that treats "Citation Drive" as one building will merge three different stratas, ages and fee structures. (§2 above)
- **Flood construction levels are a Richmond-wide fact, not a building defect.** New construction is subject to flood construction levels under Bylaw 8204 inside a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event — ground-floor and ground-oriented buyers should confirm the level position for insurance and alterations rather than treat it as a surprise. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
