## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The MHPTA frame leads this file — it, not the Strata Property Act, governs the product this page prices.**

- **No pad-rent figure exists — the no-figure rule.** No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average, range or per-park tariff: SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category and no rent figure of any kind, and neither the Province, the Township of Langley nor the City of Langley publishes park-level or average pad rents. The live page's "$650 – $950 per month" range is therefore UNVERIFIED site copy, flagged for the rebuild either to source park-by-park from park operators or to remove; the page's related claims — that 55+ parks "often cost less" and its lists of what pad rent "usually" includes or excludes — are likewise site copy, stated here only as the page's claims (research file §§1–2: https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html).
- **The regulated increase formula — the only pad-rent "number" an official source gives.** For 2026, the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is **2.3% plus a proportional amount**; for 2027 it is **2.2% plus a proportional amount**. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. A landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **Additional increases** above the limit require a Residential Tenancy Branch application (RTB Form 52) for financial loss from increased operating or financing costs, or for substantial and essential repairs or renovations; tenants do not have to pay an increase above the legal amount and may deduct an overpayment from future rent or apply for dispute resolution (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s. 28).** Assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it. Before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf).
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42).** Where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).
- **Registration and the CSA label.** In BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry, through a lawyer or notary filing the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms; a home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular), and a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **Tax on a park home.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home).

**The strata block — stated for contrast, because it does NOT apply to a pad tenancy.** A buyer of a strata townhouse (the stock counted in this program's area files) gets: a Form B Information Certificate under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, within 7 days, fee capped at $35 plus 25¢/page (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04); a 5-year depreciation report cycle for stratas with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024 — sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports); a minimum CRF contribution of 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances); rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; and only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion — a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). **None of that machinery exists in a pad-rental park** — there is no Form B, no depreciation report and no CRF; the diligence documents are the site tenancy agreement, the park rules and the Manufactured Home Registry record.

**Fee-simple contrast.** A true fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. A park home is neither strata nor fee-simple land ownership: the home is owned, the land is tenanted, and the MHPTA frame above replaces both diligence tracks.

**Municipal frame.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 govern park zoning in their respective municipalities; the manufactured-home-park zone name and rules in either bylaw were not opened in the research pass: UNVERIFIED (research file §4). City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300's adoption date is conflicted in this program's sources — flagged UNVERIFIED, resolved nowhere. Township SSMUH is Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier exists in it); a secondary suite in the Township is capped at 120 m², and a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102, never strata — none of which creates pad-rental product.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide) — as they bear on a manufactured-home or townhouse purchase.**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling carrying March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, to a maximum of $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a summary reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Program worked-example anchor: $600,000 → $10,000 PTT with no exemption (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $400,000). Whether PTT applies to a specific park-home transfer turns on the interest transferred — confirm with the lawyer or notary closing the Manufactured Home Registry filing; no park-specific PTT position is stated in this slug's research: UNVERIFIED per transaction.
- **HBP / FHSA.** Home Buyers' Plan limit $60,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 lifetime at $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). **Page-defect discipline:** the live mortgage calculator page's 2.80%–4.00% premium range is documented as an error in other slugs' research — it is NOT documented in this slug's research file, so it is NOT asserted for this slug; only the published 0.6%–4.5% range is stated. Park-home financing is lender-specific — confirm with the lender and the park before subjects (condos sibling, §3).
- **Presales.** No presale is counted in this file (§2.4); the standing rule is REDMA s. 21's 7-day rescission right and s. 18's requirement that deposits be held in trust (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How much is pad rent in Langley?**
A: No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range, so no figure is stated here — and the live page's "$650 – $950 per month" range is UNVERIFIED site copy, not an official statistic. A pad rent is set park by park in the written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement; the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent, obtained from the park and the tenancy agreement before an offer (research file §7: https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html).

**Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?**
A: Up to the yearly limit set by the Residential Tenancy Branch: for manufactured home site tenancies the 2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus a proportional amount. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park. Increases require 3 full months' notice and can occur only once every 12 months; anything above the limit requires an RTB order (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Does the park raise my pad rent when I buy the home?**
A: No. On assignment of the site tenancy under s. 28 of the MHPTA, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not change; the landlord's prior written consent is required (or deemed given under the regulations) and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf).

**Q: Is a home in a pad-rental park a townhouse?**
A: No — and this file counts zero townhouses for exactly that reason. A park home is a manufactured home owned on a rented site under the MHPTA: no strata lot, no Form B, no strata fees, and no land ownership. Pad-rental parks are never townhouse stock in this program (§2.5).

**Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted to another use?**
A: Under s. 42 of the MHPTA, as the Province states it: the landlord must first have all necessary permits and approvals, then give 12 months' notice and pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances claim additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Do I pay property tax on a manufactured home in a Langley park?**
A: Yes, on the home: a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land where the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home).

**Q: What should I check before buying a park home?**
A: The CSA label first — without the original legible decal the home cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification — then the written site tenancy agreement (current rent, last increase date, what the rent includes), the park rules the seller must provide before assignment, and the Manufactured Home Registry record; the purchase is only effective once registered through a lawyer or notary (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

**Q: Are the Langley 55+ parks townhouses I can count on a townhouse search?**
A: No. Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge are 55+ pad-rental parks and Pioneer Park is a 55+ co-operative — all documented in the 55-plus program (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`), and none is strata townhouse stock (§2.5).

**Q: How does the Langley attached market context read?**

For September 2026: Langley Detached 449 active / 64 sold / median $1,345,000; Condos & Townhomes 699 active / 128 sold / median $611,000 (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via research file §2). Those are all-residential context figures, not park or pad-rent figures.

**Q: What does pad rent include?**
A: UNVERIFIED as a general Langley statement — inclusions are set park by park in the written site tenancy agreement. The live page's "usually includes / does not include" lists are site copy, not an official source (research file §4: https://jaredgibbons.ca/cost-of-mobile-home-pad-rental-fees-in-langley.html).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The page's headline number has no source.** The "$650 – $950" average range on the live page is the one figure a Langley pad-rent searcher most wants — and no official source publishes any Langley pad-rent figure at all. The honest product on this page is the formula (2.3% + proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% + proportional amount for 2027), not a range (research file §§1–2).
- **A sale does not reset the rent.** Assignment under MHPTA s. 28 carries the existing pad rent and the existing increase date to the buyer — the opposite of a market re-pricing — which is why the current agreement, not a listing's monthly-cost line, is the diligence document (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf).
- **The proportional amount is park-specific arithmetic.** It is the park's levy change plus its regulated-utility change divided by its own site count — so two Langley parks can lawfully post different percentage increases in the same year, and neither is an overcharge on that fact alone (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **Certification is the value gate.** Park product divides into renovated, readily saleable homes and original-condition fixer product — and a home without its original legible CSA label (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) cannot be sold at all until Technical Safety BC re-certification (research file §5: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **Tax follows the home, not the land.** In a park, the assessed value excludes the land — the accurate version of the live page's "you pay taxes on the structure only" line (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home).
- **Two municipalities, one provincial tenancy law.** Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 differ, but the MHPTA increase formula, assignment rule and closure compensation are identical on both sides of the municipal line (research file §1).
