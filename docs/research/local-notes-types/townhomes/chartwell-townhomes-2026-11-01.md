## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern any strata purchase a Chartwell buyer ends up making elsewhere in West Vancouver, and because they are the rules any future strata product here would sell under. They do not create townhouse stock in Chartwell itself.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Chartwell complex exists to flag, because no complex is verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No 55+ complex is verified in Chartwell (see the sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** Chartwell's houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title (including any British Properties building-scheme or covenant on the individual title: UNVERIFIED in the research pass, confirmed title by title), municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/chartwell.md)
- **West Vancouver zoning:** houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010; Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses; the zone for a specific lot is shown on the District's Zoning Map. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/chartwell.md)
- **West Vancouver OCP and SSMUH:** District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018; a Chartwell-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). The Province's SSMUH legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 dwelling units on single-family and duplex parcels greater than 280 m², and a minimum of 6 units on such parcels greater than 280 m² near frequent bus service; West Vancouver's local implementing-bylaw status: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. On July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025 — whether Chartwell's lowest streets fall inside the "adjacent" areas must be verified per property. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/chartwell.md)
- **Hazards:** a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank — both can apply on Chartwell's hillside and creek lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/chartwell.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes and additional school tax:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. At Chartwell / British Properties price points the Province's additional school tax will commonly apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/chartwell.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply here — Chartwell is in the District of West Vancouver, not the City of Vancouver. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
