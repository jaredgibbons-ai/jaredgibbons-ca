## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Meadows is the Paragon sub-area name for Pitt Meadows' civic core; the City publishes no official neighbourhood boundary for it. The closest official planning name is the OCP Municipal Town Centre — the Harris Road corridor, the North Lougheed Area and the Civic Centre — with Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north edge, the CPKC rail line along Hammond Road, and Harris Road as the main street. City Hall is at 12007 Harris Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp (research file, read 2026-11-01)
- Nearby areas: Mid Meadows (Bonson Road / Osprey Village) to the south-east, West Meadows to the west, North Meadows across Lougheed Highway, and Maple Ridge to the east. (Research file §1)
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: the Advent Road pocket at the area's south-west side (Meadowland Village, beside the Meadowlands co-op) is the one verified named townhouse location in this sub-area; townhouse-form stock also appears at 12188 Harris Road with no confirmed complex name (see §2). The Harris Road corridor itself is apartment stock (Solaris, Origin). Sources: Section 2 below and the sibling condos file on disk.
- Market: Central Meadows carried 32 condos & townhomes listings with 5 solds in September 2026 (16% sales ratio), against 15 detached listings with 4 solds (27%). Pitt Meadows community "Condos & Townhomes" COMBINED median was $662,500 in September 2026 ($566/sqft, 97% sale-to-list, 40 days on market) — cited exactly as the report states it, never as a townhome-only figure, and SnapStats publishes no sub-area median. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Meadowland Village, the counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level pet/age lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern, including whether the registered age bylaw is in the lawful 55+ form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Meadowland Village (20 or 26 lots on either published figure) is over the threshold on both. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer** — the provincial voiding does not create a short-term rental right here. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this file would be flagged VOID in-entry; none is published for Meadowland Village in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 (via the sibling condos file)
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, including the May 1, 2023 expansion for younger spouses/partners and defined children). Meadowland Village's published 55+ position is listing-level — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. The Meadowlands co-op's 55+ rule is a co-op membership rule, not a strata bylaw, and is not governed by ss. 123.1/123.2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Central Meadows. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so a strata complex is never sold with the wrong document set.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs — RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) sets a minimum lot area of 0.4 ha for townhouse sites; houses sit under RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones, and the 1,215 sq m figure in those zones is a subdivision minimum only, not a development minimum on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. The plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, which designates the Town Centre for high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use development. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf (research file)
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units (SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024); one secondary suite per principal dwelling (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) or one garden suite per lot (33 to 90 sq m) where the zone permits — never both on one lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf (research file)
- **Floodplain — LIVE CONFLICT flagged:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser/Pitt floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes; this area is in drainage Area 3 of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. Live pages quoting "over 85%" with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution conflict with the 95% figure — this file carries 95% and flags the conflict; an older OCP-era release stated approximately 86%. Confirm flood construction level, covenants and insurance for the specific lot. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf (research file)
- **Taxes and rebates:** Pitt Meadows 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after August 1, 2026, and a separate residential drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value on top of the general rate of $2.8438 per $1,000 (research file). PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is verified or counted in this file; the frame is stated for completeness — disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18), 7-day rescission (REDMA s. 21), deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What townhouses can I actually buy in Central Meadows?** One verified named complex: Meadowland Village, 18960 Advent Road — a freehold-strata rancher-townhome complex (plan NWS2727, built 1988) with a published 55+ age restriction. Townhouse-form units also trade at 12188 Harris Road, but that complex's name is UNCONFIRMED in public sources. Basis: §2 above.
- **Q: Is Meadowland Village 20 homes or 26?** UNCONFIRMED. A current listing says "just 26 homes"; a building-directory header says "20 Unit Development." The strata plan (NWS2727) and the Form B settle it — do not quote either figure as fact. https://www.estateblock.com/pitt-meadows/7-18960-advent-rd-39761
- **Q: Is the Advent Road building beside it the same thing?** No. Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (19032 Advent Road) is a 55+ co-op — occupancy is by membership, there is no strata lot to buy and no Form B. Meadowland Village is the strata purchase; the co-op is excluded from the count for exactly that reason. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Meadowland Village unit, or run an Airbnb?** Long-term rental cannot be barred by a strata bylaw (void since Nov 24, 2022), but the complex's 55+ age bylaw still governs who may live there — confirm its exact form on the Form B. An Airbnb is a different answer: short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we both live at Meadowland Village?** Provincial exemptions to a lawful 55+ bylaw cover a younger spouse/partner (expanded May 1, 2023), live-in caregivers and legacy residents — but the exemption has to match the registered bylaw, so read it on the Form B before writing an offer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How much of Pitt Meadows is floodplain?** Over 95% of the land base, per the City's 2020 Business Plan, behind 60 km of dikes — and this file flags the live conflict with pages quoting "over 85%." For a townhouse purchase that means: flood construction level, strata insurance and deductibles, and the separate drainage levy are standard subjects, not extras. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** Central Meadows: 32 condos & townhomes listings, 5 solds, 16% sales ratio. The Pitt Meadows combined median was $662,500 — SnapStats combines condos and townhomes at community level only, so there is no townhome-only figure. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Meadows townhouse?** SD42, address-based, no middle tier: the verified locator run for 12007 Harris Road returned Pitt Meadows Elementary (K–7) and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12). Run the exact address in the locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Are Solaris and Origin townhouse options?** No — Solaris (12069 Harris Road, built 2010) and Origin (11950 Harris Road, built 2008) are the Harris Road apartment pair, documented in the condos series. A buyer set on a townhouse in this sub-area is really choosing between Meadowland Village and unnamed Harris Road townhouse-form stock. Basis: sibling condos file §2.
- **Q: What is the commute?** Pitt Meadows Station is in the neighbourhood: West Coast Express to Waterfront Station in about 45 minutes by the City's own statement, plus the R3 RapidBus at Harris Road / Lougheed Highway. Driving times: UNVERIFIED (no official source states them). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain (research file)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One street, two 55+ tenures (Advent Road):** Meadowland Village (freehold strata, buyable) and Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (membership, not buyable as a strata lot) sit on the same street, and listings board-classify the pocket as Central Meadows even though it reads as a quiet residential street rather than Town Centre. A search that treats every Advent Road "unit" as the same product will misprice both. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and §2 above.
- **The floodplain number changed between City documents (dated):** the 2020 Business Plan states over 95%; a superseded OCP-era release stated approximately 86%; live pages now quote "over 85%" with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution. This file carries 95% and flags the conflict rather than smoothing it over. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Solaris carries two civic addresses:** the directory address is 12069 Harris Road, but part of the complex is addressed 12079 Harris Road — confirm which address a specific unit carries when ordering strata documents or checking a tax record. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road
- **The Harris Road rail crossing is being rebuilt under the townhouse corridor:** the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project includes a new four-lane underpass below the CPKC main rail crossing at Harris Road, with the Kennedy Road overpass work paused and priority given to the Harris Road underpass — directly relevant to traffic past the Advent Road pocket's main route into the core. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects/pitt-meadows-road-and-rail-improvements-project/past-project (research file)
- **The drainage levy is its own tax line (2026):** Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the residential drainage rate at $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, separate from the $2.8438 general municipal rate — the cost of the dike/drainage system, billed to strata and house owners alike. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf (research file)
- **78% of the city is farmland (2020 Business Plan):** the Agricultural Land Reserve covers 78% of Pitt Meadows' land base, which is why the buyable townhouse stock is compressed into pockets like Advent Road instead of spreading across new subdivisions. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf (research file)
