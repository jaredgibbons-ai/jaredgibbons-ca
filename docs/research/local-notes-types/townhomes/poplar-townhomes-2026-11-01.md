## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B — Information Certificate (Strata Property Act, s.59).** For the three strata complexes cross-referenced in Section 2, the Form B states the unit's monthly strata fee, approved special levies, the CRF balance, parking/storage and proceedings, and must include a summary of the strata's insurance coverage; the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attach. Kings Crossing buyers get the developer's disclosure statement instead while it is a presale — its tenure is UNCONFIRMED, so which document governs is itself settled from that disclosure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. For the 1989–1990 complexes (Crown Point, Kingsview Estates), the report is where roof, envelope and plumbing timing shows up. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid — Kingsview Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Crown Point and Kingsview Estates are the complexes a source states are 55+; The Argyle is listing-stated as having no age restrictions, and Kings Crossing's developer source states none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhouses — none verified in Poplar, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus covenants/HOA obligations on title only. No Poplar complex in Section 2 may be described that way on current evidence; Kings Crossing is the open question, tenure UNCONFIRMED.
- **Abbotsford zoning / plan — standing correction applied:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; urban lots in Poplar are in the RS family (City reports state RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 420 m²), and southern rural lots fall under A1 (minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha). The CURRENT plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025) — the batch-7 research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" wording (and any live-page "2500-2016") is STALE/conflicted; apply Abbotsforward 2050 and flag the stale numbers. No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH / suites in Abbotsford — bylaw number UNCONFIRMED, never assert "3560-2024":** Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (criteria include a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m minimum frontage in City reports), the City's 2025 updates set the suite maximum (in most zones) at less than 50% of the principal unit's size, and coach/garden suites are capped at 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule ONLY). SSMUH affects Poplar's house lots, not its existing strata complexes. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **Tax timing:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **GST — FTHB rebate (new product like Kings Crossing):** Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, compression flagged:** 1% on the first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row compresses that 3% + 2% and is not a single rate. First-time buyer exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000 saving) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales — REDMA (Kings Crossing):** a developer must deliver a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing or acknowledging it, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Which Poplar townhouse complexes are 55+?** Crown Point (NWS2984, built 1989) and Kingsview Estates (built 1989/1990), per the sources in Section 2. - **Q: Are pets allowed?** Source-stated only: Crown Point's MLS sheet records cats/dogs allowed, number limit one, with restrictions; Kingsview Estates' directory records pets not allowed; an Argyle listing states pets allowed for its unit. The filed pet bylaw in the Form B package decides. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is Kings Crossing strata or fee-simple?** UNCONFIRMED — no source checked states its tenure. It is a 20-townhome project by Rockwood Homes Inc. with completion expected in 2026; the disclosure statement will state the tenure, and REDMA gives you 7 days to rescind after receiving it. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- **Q: What should I budget beyond the mortgage on a 1989–1990 strata townhouse?** The strata fee on the Form B, plus levy risk: read the depreciation report (mandatory, renewed every five years for 5+ lots) for the next roof/envelope/plumbing cycle, and the CRF balance against the 10% annual minimum in force since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Poplar house instead of buying a townhouse?** A secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under s.140.3.8 (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage in City reports), sized at less than 50% of the principal unit under the 2025 updates; a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m². That is a fee-simple house strategy — no strata, no Form B — not a townhouse purchase. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend from King Road?** Catchments are by exact address. The batch-7 research file records published feeder patterns (not locator runs): Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and the Traditional pathway South Poplar Traditional Elementary → Abbotsford Traditional School (6–12). Run a King Road address in the SD34 locator before relying on it. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms and https://abbymiddle.abbyschools.ca/node/10331
