## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Tenure first, Form B second.** On a freehold strata purchase (the Southeast False Creek form), the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate under Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a False Creek South purchase the Form B is not the first document — the ground lease is: freehold strata versus City-ground-lease leasehold versus co-op shares is the fork the whole purchase turns on, and no Form B cures a short remaining lease term. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every apartment development documented in Section 2 is far over the threshold; a strata of four or fewer lots sits under that threshold, and none is verified here. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID — with a tenure boundary:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals. The boundary of this rule: it governs strata bylaws on strata lots — it does not cure a False Creek South ground lease or a co-op's occupancy rules, whose terms are separate documents; the Lamey's Mill listing's "pets & rentals permitted w/ restrictions" line must be read against that building's lease, not against the strata rule alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified around False Creek; the sibling 55-plus file on disk carries the area's age-restricted position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified on the False Creek shoreline. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and registered covenants/HOA documents only. The FM-1 houses on the Fairview Slopes above the south shore are that ownership form, but they are houses in the Fairview local area, not False Creek townhouse complexes, and are not counted here.
- **Land tenure — False Creek South framework:** the City holds title to 80 acres in False Creek South, leased under 60-year ground leases with most expiring in the next 15 to 25 years. In March 2024 Council directed staff to report back with an updated and enhanced False Creek South development framework addressing current housing pressures, and in summer 2025 the City conducted technical investigations in the neighbourhood to support a future planning process. Leasehold pricing, lender appetite and remaining term are building-specific and UNVERIFIED for any individual building. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **City zoning and plans:** there is no single district schedule for False Creek under Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — the Southeast False Creek / Olympic Village lands and the False Creek North lands sit under site-specific CD-1 Comprehensive Development districts and Official Development Plans, including the False Creek North Official Development Plan; FM-1 applies on the Fairview Slopes above the south shore, permitting single detached houses, duplexes and multiple dwellings with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60. The Northeast False Creek Plan proposes a mixed-use neighbourhood with replacement of the Georgia and Dunsmuir viaducts by an at-grade street network. The zone for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/northeast-false-creek-play-and-work.aspx
- **SSMUH / suites:** Vancouver's house-zone multiplex framework does not create False Creek shoreline townhouse stock — the shoreline parcels sit under CD-1 districts and Official Development Plans, and the south-shore ground is City leasehold land. A suite or multiplex claim for a shoreline address: UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's CD-1/ODP documents and, on the south shore, its lease are read.
- **Floodplain — the defining risk file for this sub-area:** the lands around False Creek are inside the City's designated floodplain system; the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan covers the floodplain area encompassing False Creek North, False Creek South, Granville Island and the Flats, and parcels in the floodplain are subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels. The Northeast False Creek Plan requires new developments to be built to protect homes and critical infrastructure from flooding and rising sea levels, with a continuous line of flood protection across that site. The flood construction level for a specific building is a core due-diligence item on both shores — ground-level townhouse-form units sit closest to it; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. Note for leasehold buildings: City property tax mechanics on City-held ground-lease land follow the lease — how taxes are billed for a specific False Creek South building: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the documented False Creek stock is resale. Separately, leasehold tenure and floodplain position can affect a lender's view of a specific property — no source checked publishes a building-by-building position, so treat any blanket claim as UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA), where a False Creek presale is bought:** the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **False Creek South has had no new housing since the 1980s — on purpose-built leasehold land.** The City transformed industrial land into the waterfront neighbourhood in the 1970s and 1980s on its own 80 acres, leased out on 60-year ground leases; the March 2024 Council direction for an updated and enhanced False Creek South development framework, and the City's summer 2025 technical investigations in the neighbourhood, are the live file that will shape what happens as those leases run down — and the October 7, 2025 Creekview Co-op overholding report shows that process already touching individual buildings. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx and https://council.vancouver.ca/20251007/documents/r3.pdf
- **One south-shore townhouse listing carried a court-approval condition.** The 1201 Lamey's Mill Road townhouse listing states "ALL OFFERS SUBJECT TO APPROVAL OF THE SUPREME COURT OF B.C." alongside its lease-to-2040 terms — a reminder that in False Creek South the unusual condition is in the listing text, not buried in the strata documents, and it must be priced and timed like any other condition. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3042688-1201-lameys-mill-road-21-vancouver-false-creek-homes-for-sale-559000/brochure
- **The Olympic Village buildings were athletes' housing first.** The Southeast False Creek site was the Vancouver Olympic Village for the 2010 Winter Games, after which it provided approximately 1,000 residential units including 250 affordable housing units — which is why market strata and non-market housing sit inside one development under one name, and why the non-market share is excluded from this program's counts rather than netted out of a building total.
