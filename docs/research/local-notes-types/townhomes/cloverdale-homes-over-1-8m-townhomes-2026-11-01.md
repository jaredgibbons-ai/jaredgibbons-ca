## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The strata block below governs any strata townhouse bought in this band; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast for a non-strata purchase.

- **Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At this price level the depreciation report and any approved-but-unpaid levy are material closing figures, not paperwork. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met on a Cloverdale complex is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals in its home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Cloverdale townhouse complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in the Cloverdale board community (home file, §2).
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The City is updating the Cloverdale Town Centre Plan (plan area at Highway 10 and Highway 15; boundary expanded north to 64 Avenue on July 22, 2024; update driven in part by the planned new hospital and BC Cancer Centre). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. On ALR land the ALC frame governs instead — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence; ALR status is parcel-specific on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). The feed's own cards at this band describe both "legal" and "unauthorized" suites — verify legality in documents, not listing text. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it to a client. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw even though long-term rental bylaws are void. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. **Home owner grant at this band:** the Province states the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the value is over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Property transfer tax — the band's core tax rule, with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — flagged: the tier math governs, and no such compressed row is restated as a rate in this file. Every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion only if it passes $2,000,000 — a purchase between $1,800,000 and $2,000,000 is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. **Worked example 1 — $2,000,000 purchase, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. **Worked example 2 — $3,500,000 purchase, fully residential, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 over $3M ($10,000) = **$93,000**. On a mixed-class property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion. **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first-time buyers' full exemption caps at fair market value $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and eliminated at $1,150,000 — buyers in this band budget full PTT. The research confirms the live page's own PTT summary matches the Province's published tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/cloverdale-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **GST / rebates / programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new-build townhouse in this band sits above the $1.5M phase-out, so the rebate is $0 at band prices even for a qualifying first-time buyer; a March 12, 2026 date appears in other federal material for the same Royal Assent — CRA News No. 122 states March 13 and March 13 governs in this program. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K (likewise below this band); HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (insured financing is not the typical frame at this band, and no CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none). 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What is the PTT on a $2 million Cloverdale home?** $38,000 with no exemption: $2,000 (1% on the first $200,000) + $36,000 (2% on the next $1,800,000). Neither the first-time nor the newly built exemption reaches $2 million. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is there extra tax over $3 million?** Yes — a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on top of the 3% tier above $2 million. On a fully residential $3,500,000 purchase the total is $93,000 (worked tier-by-tier in §3). A summary row calling the over-$3M rate "5%" is a compression of 3% + 2%, not a separate tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the home owner grant still help at this level?** Partly, then not at all: the 2026 threshold is $2,075,000 assessed value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and the regular grant is $0 over $2,189,000. Two otherwise similar homes straddling the threshold carry a real annual-cost difference. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the new GST rebate on a $1.9M new townhouse?** No — the Bill C-4 FTHB GST rebate phases out entirely by $1.5M (100% to $1M, maximum $50,000), so a band-price new build receives $0 from it; the older New Housing Rebate's $350K–$450K band is further below still. GST itself (5%) still applies to the new build. (§3 above)
- **Q: What are the house size limits on ALR land in Cloverdale?** Under the ALC housing rules, an ALR parcel may have one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), with any secondary suite inside the principal residence; ALR status is parcel-specific, so check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps before relying on those limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Which Cloverdale areas appear on this page?** On the page text read for the research: Cloverdale BC (custom detached houses) and Clayton (two parcels framed as development opportunities) — feed labels only, not a price claim about any sub-area. (§1 above)
- **Q: If I buy a strata townhouse at this level, what document matters most?** The Form B package — fees, amounts owing, approved-but-unpaid levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, current budget and the most recent depreciation report. At band prices, an approved levy is a five- or six-figure closing fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
