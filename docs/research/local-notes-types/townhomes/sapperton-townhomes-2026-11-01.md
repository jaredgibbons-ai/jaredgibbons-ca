## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata):** SPA s.59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page. Listing-level restriction lines are not bylaws — the Form B/registered bylaws govern, and at a 2025-built 9-lot complex the depreciation report cycle and CRF balance are still the documents to read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation / CRF:** stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024 (Sapperton Living, at 9 lots per listings, is over the threshold); a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws VOID** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Sapperton strata is VOID for long-term rentals, flagged in-entry wherever met (no counted complex here carries such a published line in the sources checked); short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Sapperton complex is verified to carry one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 + registered covenants/HOA only. None counted here (§2).
