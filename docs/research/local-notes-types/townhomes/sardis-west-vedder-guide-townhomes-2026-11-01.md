## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that confirms tenure, age bylaws and pet limits for Cascara Village, Keypoint Villas and the five apartment buildings in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. The Sapphire is over the threshold on its published count (49 units); Cascara Village is over it on its advertised count (22 homes); the lot counts of the other §2 complexes are UNCONFIRMED — the 5+ test is confirmed on each Form B. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid. Any directory or listing line of the form "rentals not allowed / allowed with restrictions" for a §2 complex is void in its restriction element for long-term rentals and is flagged void wherever met. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the City regulates STR operations under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. In a 55+ building a tenant must still meet the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy residents, defined child cases). Four of the five apartment buildings in §2 are published as 55+ (Knight Lodge, Cotton Ridge, The Sapphire, 7651 Amber Drive); Morningside carries no published age label — UNCONFIRMED either way. No townhome-form complex in §2 carries a verified age restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified here (§2). A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only.
- **City plans and zoning:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — Sardis is its Schedule B5. Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000. SSMUH: Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. Suites: only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; garden suites are capped at 100 m². https://chilliwack.com/ocp (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-west-vedder-guide.md)

The City also levies drainage and dyking taxes on top of municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/sardis-west-vedder-guide.md)
