## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated for completeness — Rosedale has no verified strata stock for it to attach to).**

- **Form B diligence.** If a Rosedale property ever proves to be strata (the §2.1 address is the only candidate, UNVERIFIED), SPA s. 59 applies: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports / CRF.** Stratas with 5+ lots: depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold); CRF minimum 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Mt Cheam's "no rentals" position is a park rule under the MHPTA, not a strata bylaw, and is NOT voided by the strata amendment (condos sibling §3).
- **Fee-simple reality for Rosedale.** The verified stock is fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal (here, FVRD/provincial) rules and registered covenants only. That is the default diligence frame for a Rosedale purchase.

**FVRD / rural frame — NOT Chilliwack's rules.**

- **Planning frame: FVRD Electoral Area D.** Which FVRD Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw govern a specific Rosedale parcel, and their bylaw numbers: **UNVERIFIED** in this research — no FVRD planning bylaw for Area D was opened, and FVRD bylaw numbers are never borrowed from Chilliwack. **Chilliwack OCP Bylaw 5500 does NOT apply to Rosedale.** The live page's "Rosedale Neighbourhood Plan and Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan" framing is UNVERIFIED site copy (research file §1/§4).
- **Rural property taxes (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11).** Rosedale is unincorporated, so taxes run under the provincial rural regime: a **5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31** on unpaid rural taxes (standing program record for FVRD electoral areas). City of Chilliwack tax dates do NOT apply to Rosedale (research file §4). The parcel's own rural tax notice is the document to read; the FVRD tax-notice mechanics for a specific parcel were not opened in this research: UNVERIFIED at parcel level.
- **ALR frame.** Any Rosedale parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC housing frame: one principal residence at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — an ALR acreage can never become townhouse strata stock (research file §4: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). ## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The mailing shorthand lies.** Rosedale addresses read "Rosedale / Chilliwack" in everyday use, but the governing body is the FVRD — a buyer who reads Chilliwack's bylaws or tax calendar for a Rosedale parcel is reading the wrong government's rules (§1/§3).
- **"Mostly senior occupied" is not an age restriction.** Mt Cheam's own listing pairs that phrase with "Families are ALLOWED" — and the park is pad-rent tenure in any case, so it can never be 55+ strata stock (§2.5).
- **Cheam Lake Wetlands is the jurisdictional tell.** The FVRD regional park at 53480 Elgey Road (107 hectares, ~2 km of trails, floating boardwalk, open 7 a.m. to sunset, pets not permitted under FVRD Bylaw No. 1500, 2018) is how the research confirms Rosedale sits in Electoral Area D — the park page states it outright (research file §4: https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/cheam-lake-wetlands.html?media=standard).
- **The whole townhouse question here is one address.** A single feed card — unit 8 at 52324 Yale Road — is the only multi-unit signal in the research, and it rests on listing text alone. Everything else in the feed is a house or an acreage (§2.1).
- **Rosedale's penalty calendar has a second date Chilliwack's does not.** The rural regime's further 5% lands after October 31, not on a municipal September/August date — a closing that straddles tax season prices differently here than inside the city (§3).
- **Area D is bigger than Rosedale.** Bridal Falls and Popkum sit in the same electoral area — a buyer cross-shopping those communities is under the same FVRD frame, not three different ones (§1).
