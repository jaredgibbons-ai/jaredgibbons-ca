## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial and District rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Chelsea Park against the District's real townhouse pockets (Whitby Estates, Caulfeild) or its Ambleside/Dundarave condos will meet them there; inside Chelsea Park itself there is no strata stock for the strata rules to attach to.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases elsewhere in the District):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. This matters when a directory shows an old "rentals not allowed" line on any West Vancouver building a buyer cross-shops from Chelsea Park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined spouses/partners and children). The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ stock in Chelsea Park. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the stock that does exist here):** a Chelsea Park detached-house purchase carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, OCP Bylaw No. 4985) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Chelsea Park is in the District of West Vancouver — White Rock's Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair belongs to White Rock only and is never attributed here, and the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply.
- **District zoning and plan:** District of West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, under Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 — the District-wide plan covering Chelsea Park; a Chelsea Park-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). Houses sit in the RS single-family zones — including RS2 (Section 202), RS3 (Section 203), RS5 (Section 205) and RS7 (Section 207; the District Parks Master Plan lists Chairlift Park in this area as RS7) — with Section 200 minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5. The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — the SSMUH/suite frame as the District states it; the zone and minimum for a specific lot are confirmed on the District's Zoning Map. District building-permit guidance addresses secondary suite and detached secondary suite (coach house) applications. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
- **Hazard permit areas:** District-wide, an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both are live checks on the pocket's upper and ravine-edge lots; a creek, foreshore or slope fact specific to Chelsea Park is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — at Chelsea Park prices the first-time programs are usually out of range; run the bands on the actual price); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (insured mortgages are capped by price, so most West Vancouver purchases are conventional — confirm with the lender). 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **District tax timing and additional school tax:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. At Chelsea Park price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/chelsea-park.md)
