## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the Bromley presale strata on completion and to any Brookswood strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For a presale like Bromley, the disclosure statement and the eventual Form B are the diligence, not developer marketing (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Bromley's 26 homes put it over the threshold once complete. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A "rentals not allowed" line in Brookswood park listing copy is a park-rule statement under tenancy law, not a strata bylaw, and is not carried as a strata fact here (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). No Brookswood strata carries a verified age bylaw — the area's 55+ rules live in its parks, under park rules, not the SPA (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome (the queued Oxford Mews form) would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Brookswood (§2.2).

**Township of Langley zoning, plans, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **Community plan:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge (Booth, Fernridge and Rinn neighbourhood plans under it); the Township states it allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx).
- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500. Zones named in Township notices for the area include Suburban Residential SR-1, SR-2, SR-3 and Rural RU-2; read from Bylaw 2500 s. 110.1 in Township council reports: SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² / frontage 40 m; SR-2 minimum 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 minimum 1,765 m² / 27.45 m. RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf). - **SSMUH:** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible; **NO 6-plex tier exists in the Township.** Asserted precisely: a live Brookswood DEVELOPMENT page claiming a 6-plex tier under Bylaw 6020 would be WRONG — but **this file's own research does not document that page, and the Brookswood area guide makes no such claim (no defect recorded there), so no defect is asserted in this file** (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).

Township zoning for a second dwelling or coach house on non-reserve Brookswood acreage is UNVERIFIED in the research file (research file §4). On ALR land, the ALC frame permits one principal residence and, subject to conditions, one additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); parcel-level ALR status and floodplain position are UNVERIFIED per parcel — the Township directs applicants to GeoSource (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx).
- **Wells and septic (acreage lots):** the Province's GWELLS application is the registry for recorded wells (older wells may not appear); Fraser Health requires a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons).
