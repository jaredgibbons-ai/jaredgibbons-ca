## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "North Vancouver" is not one municipality: it is the District of North Vancouver (the large residential municipality wrapped around the City, from Deep Cove and Lynn Valley to Capilano) and the City of North Vancouver (the compact waterfront municipality along the Lonsdale corridor), with different councils, OCPs, zoning bylaws and tax bills. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- City planning: City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400, and Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700; the Lonsdale corridor — Lower, Central and Upper Lonsdale — is its spine. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- District planning: District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210; District neighbourhoods include Lynn Valley, Edgemont Village, Pemberton / Lions Gate, Capilano, Seymour and Dollarton. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Municipality traps carried from the sibling files: Calverhall is CITY, not District; Norgate is DISTRICT; Lynnmour is District — its secondary catchment is Windsor Secondary, NOT Sutherland. Confirm the municipality per property, because the same street style sits in either municipality along the shared edges. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Where townhouse product concentrates, per the research file: the District's centres (Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lions Gate Village, Edgemont Village) and ground-oriented streets at the north edge of Lower Lonsdale in the City; the District is otherwise predominantly detached houses. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- Schools: one district serves both municipalities — School District 44, elementary K–7 and secondary 8–12, no middle tier; catchments are address-based and must be confirmed with SD44 for the exact address. A verified sample: 230 Keith Road West → Queen Mary Community Elementary and Carson Graham Secondary. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
- Transit: the SeaBus sails from Lonsdale Quay (City) to Waterfront Station; the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs Park Royal to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange; route 230 runs to Upper Lonsdale and route 229 to Lynn Valley. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf
- Market stats: North Vancouver is NOT covered by SnapStats — no SnapStats figure is cited in this file, and no substitute source is used. Real numbers require a Paragon/GVR pull. (Research file §2)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Two municipalities, two bylaw sets — never mix them.** City of North Vancouver: OCP Bylaw 8400 (2014) and Zoning Bylaw 6700 (1995). District of North Vancouver: OCP Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210. A City rule does not apply in the District and a District rule does not apply in the City; confirm the municipality on title before applying any rule in this section. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- **City SSMUH / Ground Oriented Zone:** in the City's GO Zone, a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 and up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4, with maximum floor area 0.85× lot area (LN1) and 1.0× (LN2/RL4). https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Strata townhomes — Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s.59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This applies to Belle Isle, Mason and every strata component above — and does NOT apply to Chesterfield Manor (share purchase, excluded) or to the Mount Seymour Park co-op. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Every directory or listing line for a complex in this file that says "rentals not allowed" is void on that point and is flagged as such in-entry in the sibling files. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for any townhouse complex assembled in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified for this slug. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Do not quote a strata document set for a fee-simple purchase, or municipal-only diligence for a strata purchase.
- **Hazard and permit areas (City):** the Shipyards and Harbourside Waterfront precincts are Development Permit Areas, and Streamside Protection rules apply within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or 10 metres of a ravine bank — relevant to ground-oriented product near creeks in both municipalities; District buyers run the District's GeoWeb hazard report for slope and creek DPAs. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **Presales (Towns at Lynn, Magnolia):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s.18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s.21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Buyer frame (every purchase in this file):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the first-time buyer exemption (first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

Approved claims may appear only as page statements in their exact form (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years in the FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1% of Fraser Valley Realtors; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points for 2025 and 2026 YTD; more than $500M in sales). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is North Vancouver one city for zoning purposes?** No — City OCP Bylaw 8400 / Zoning Bylaw 6700 and District OCP Bylaw 7900 / Zoning Bylaw 3210 are separate sets, and a "North Vancouver" address alone does not tell you which applies. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: Where are the actual townhouse complexes, not just townhouse-shaped units in towers?** The standalone complexes documented in the sibling files are Belle Isle (88) and Mason (40) in Lions Gate Village / Pemberton NV, and Edgemont Walk in Edgemont (tenure UNCONFIRMED in the assembled source); Cates Landing, Beacon, Millennium Central Lonsdale and Vista 29 carry townhouse components inside apartment strata. Basis: the sibling files cited in Section 2.
- **Q: A directory says my townhouse complex does not allow rentals. Can I rent it out?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022 — the directory line does not bind you, though short-term rental bylaws can still apply. Read the registered bylaws on the Form B, not the directory. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Chesterfield Manor a cheap strata townhouse alternative?** It is neither strata nor a townhouse complex in this program's count: it is a corporation share purchase in Central Lonsdale, excluded program-wide, with different financing (a listing states 35% down) and no Form B. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-lonsdale-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Can I rely on a SnapStats median for a North Vancouver townhouse?** No — SnapStats does not cover North Vancouver at all, and even where it does cover a community it combines condos & townhomes in one category, never a townhome-only figure. (Research file §2)
- **Q: Which secondary school serves a Lynnmour townhouse?** Windsor Secondary is the Lynnmour catchment carried in this program — NOT Sutherland. SD44 catchments are address-based, so run the exact address with the district before relying on any catchment. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
- **Q: Are the Towns at Lynn and Magnolia homes I can move into now?** Both were presales in their sibling sources (Towns at Lynn estimated completion 2025; Magnolia built 2027 in its listing) — completion status must be re-verified, and a presale purchase carries REDMA s.21's 7-day rescission and s.18 trust-deposit rules. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: What is the townhouse co-op at Parkgate, and can I buy a unit on MLS?** Mount Seymour Park Housing Co-operative (3822 Brockton Crescent, 57 townhouse units) is a housing co-operative — homes are held through membership shares, not strata lots, so it is excluded from townhouse stock and has no Form B. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/seymour-nv-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Does a fee-simple townhouse exist anywhere in North Vancouver?** None is verified in the sibling files assembled for this slug — every verified entry is strata or tenure-UNCONFIRMED. If a listing claims fee-simple, verify title before treating it as the first. Basis: Section 2 above.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two-municipality split is also an income split:** the 2021 Census median household income was $123,000 in the District and $86,000 in the City (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — part of why City corridor product and District centre product price differently under one search name. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/details/page.cfm?Lang=E&DGUIDlist=2021A00055915046,2021A00055915051
- **All of Pemberton NV's townhouse growth sits in one village corner:** Belle Isle and Mason are both on Curling Road in Lions Gate Village, the centre the District's Lower Capilano–Marine planning built out — the rest of the sub-area is detached Pemberton Heights streets. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pemberton-nv-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Lions Gate Hospital is in the City but serves both municipalities:** 231 East 15th Street, with the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 221 West Esplanade in Lower Lonsdale. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **The City's small-lot multiplex numbers are written in the GO Zone, not in a listing:** up to 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and up to 4–6 units on larger lots depending on designation — a ground-oriented lot's upside is a designation check, not a marketing line. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **A combined unit count can hide the townhouse share:** Baden Park's project source quotes 102 garden flats and townhomes together, and Cates Landing is quoted as "95 homes" without its 80-apartment / 15-townhouse split — in both cases the townhouse-only number is the one no headline gives you. Basis: the sibling files cited in Section 2.
