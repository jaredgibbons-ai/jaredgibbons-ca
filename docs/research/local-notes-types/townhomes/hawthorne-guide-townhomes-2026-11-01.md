Hawthorne is a Paragon sub-area name in Ladner, in the City of Delta — it is not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and it falls under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B first (all counted stock here is strata).** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection of repair and replacement costs and three CRF cash-flow models. For Central Park (2007), Central Park West (2008) and Keira Gardens (2011), roof, envelope and deck items in that report are the core read. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. Read the CRF balance on the Form B against that floor and against the depreciation report's near-term items. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, so any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Hawthorne complex is void for long-term rentals. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. No Hawthorne townhouse complex is verified as 55+ in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly because buyers ask.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Hawthorne (Section 2), so that frame does not apply to any counted complex here — if a listing calls a Hawthorne townhouse "fee simple," get the title, because it contradicts the verified record. (Complex entries in Section 2 above.)
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it has no lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, not asserted.** Sources conflict on the zoning bylaw citation for Delta (a "Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" citation appears in the sibling files, against a competing "8400, 2025" citation in standing corrections); this file asserts neither. Zoning is in any case assigned parcel by parcel — e.g., Keira Gardens' listing records zoning CD376, a site-specific comprehensive development zone — so read the specific complex's zone off the City's zoning maps, not off a bylaw number quoted in marketing. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — this affects the detached streets around the townhouse corridor, not the existing strata complexes. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale townhouses carry no GST. On a new home (Wildwood), the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales — REDMA.** For Wildwood, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act; s. 21 gives a buyer 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement to rescind, and s. 18 requires purchase deposits to be held in trust. Assignment rights are contract-specific — assume none unless the contract and disclosure statement grant them. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) for homes valued at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person withdrawn from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined for the same home. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs from 0.60% of the loan at loan-to-value up to 65%, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95% LTV, and 4.50% at that top band with a non-traditional down payment — the 0.6%–4.5% range buyers see quoted. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are Hawthorne townhouses strata or fee-simple?** Strata — all three completed complexes verified in this file (Central Park, Central Park West, Keira Gardens) are strata tenure, and no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the sub-area. That means strata fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation report on every purchase here. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: How many townhomes are in each complex?** Verified counts exist for two: Central Park West is recorded at 10 townhomes and Keira Gardens at 24 units. Central Park's unit count is UNCONFIRMED — no source checked publishes it. https://www.michaellepore.ca/development/central-park-west/ and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **Q: Can I rent out my townhouse?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022; Keira Gardens listings already record rentals allowed with restrictions. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105066-206-4808-linden-drive-delta-british-columbia-v4k0b5-674000/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** At Keira Gardens, listing records state cats and dogs OK with restrictions. For Central Park and Central Park West, no public source checked states a pet rule: UNCONFIRMED — pet bylaws are strata-specific and the registered bylaws attached to the Form B decide. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **Q: Is Keira Gardens an apartment building or townhouses?** Townhouse form in strata tenure: listings describe private walk-up entries and a townhouse-style layout in a 3-storey, 24-unit strata (plan BCS4147) — it was documented as townhouse-style strata, not an apartment condo, in the condos sibling, and it is counted here, not there (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/hawthorne-condos-2026-11-01.md). https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105066-206-4808-linden-drive-delta-british-columbia-v4k0b5-674000/brochure
- **Q: What is Oak Glenn Terrace — a townhouse or a condo?** UNCONFIRMED. The research file names it a townhouse complex; current listings for 5661 Ladner Trunk Road classify the units as apartments/condos. It is in the QUEUE in Section 2 and counted nowhere until the strata plan settles it — do not rely on either label in an offer. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3110710-19-5661-ladner-trunk-road-delta-british-columbia-v4k1x3-710000/brochure
- **Q: How do I know if a special levy is coming?** The Form B must disclose any approved-but-unpaid special levy and the CRF balance, and the attached depreciation report shows the 30-year repair list with three funding models — a thin CRF against a big roof or envelope item is the classic levy setup in 2007–2011 wood-frame complexes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: What does the strata insurance deductible look like?** The Form B must include a summary of the strata corporation's insurance coverage. In wood-frame townhouse complexes the water-damage deductible is the exposure that matters, because deductibles are routinely charged back to the unit a leak started in. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is Wildwood a safe presale bet, and when is it done?** Wildwood Ladner is a 40-home strata townhome presale by Shato Holdings at 4900 Central Avenue; a presale profile states expected completion between February and April 2027, which is developer-stated, not contractual. Your protections are the REDMA disclosure statement, the 7-day rescission right (s. 21) and trust-held deposits (s. 18) — read the disclosure statement for the outside completion date for your unit. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/wildwood-ladner-townhomes-ladner/ and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Which schools serve the Central Avenue townhouses?** Catchments are address-based; the locator result for 5160 Central Avenue is Hawthorne Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12), but each complex address must be run in the SD37 locator itself. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Hawthorne in a flood area?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and any property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch falls in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Flood construction requirements are confirmed lot by lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hawthorne's townhouse supply is one street deep.** All three completed complexes and the one presale sit on or just off Central Avenue (4910, 4887 and 4900 Central Avenue, plus Keira Gardens on Linden Drive) — a buyer searching the whole sub-area is really searching one corridor (Section 2 above).
- **The newest townhouse product is also the only presale.** Wildwood Ladner's 40 homes are larger than the three completed complexes combined on verified counts (10 + 24, with Central Park uncounted) — when it completes, it redraws this sub-area's townhouse supply in one step. https://wildwoodladner.com/
- **The 2024 OCP removed the old area-plan structure.** Older City documents refer to a Ladner Area Plan as Schedule B.3 of the former OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), has no lettered schedules — future change in Hawthorne is governed by the single Land Use Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta's zoning bylaw number is contested in the sources — do not quote one.** The sibling files and standing corrections carry competing citations; what is not contested is the parcel-level map and site-specific CD zones like Keira Gardens' CD376. A page or listing that states a single Delta zoning bylaw number as settled fact is ahead of its sources (Section 3 above). https://www.cotala.com/tours/85471/FullPublic.pdf?i=1607361366
- **SSMUH changed the detached streets in 2024, not the strata corridor.** Delta's June 24, 2024 SSMUH adoption (Bylaws 8394 and 8395) lets many detached lots around the townhouse complexes add suites, duplexes, coach houses and garden suites without a rezoning — the competition for a townhouse buyer's dollar here increasingly includes a house with a suite. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion and major construction expected to begin in 2027. That crossing is the commute every Hawthorne townhouse buyer is pricing. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **There is no Hawthorne market data in the standard stats pack.** The SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Ladner community section and no Hawthorne row — its only Delta section is North Delta, and South Delta is not covered by SnapStats at all — so any "Hawthorne median townhouse price" quoted from that source does not exist, and no townhome-only figure exists in any case because SnapStats reports attached product only as Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined (area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/hawthorne-guide.md).
