## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (any strata townhouse a buyer finds through this band):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws:** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Tsawwassen strata is flagged void in its home file. Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast (stated plainly):** a fee-simple townhouse or row home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only. The one fee-simple collection cross-referenced in Section 2 sits under that frame, not the strata frame. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Delta plan and zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, no lettered schedules (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); the OCP focuses commercial uses along 56th Street and in the Tsawwassen Urban Centre and adds Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines. The program's standing correction records the Delta zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted; the research file's zone ladder as cited is RS1–RS3 at a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, RS4 610 m², RS5 550/460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m², RD2/RD3 4,000 m², RD4 504 m², with no RD5 zone, confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **PTT at this band — the rule that bites hardest:** general PTT is 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches the 3% tier on its top portion — plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 (a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears; no such row is documented in this topic's research file). **Worked example, inputs stated:** a purely residential purchase at $3,500,000 with no exemption — PTT = $2,000 (1% of $200,000) + $36,000 (2% of $1,800,000) + $30,000 (3% of $1,000,000) + $10,000 (further 2% of the $500,000 over $3,000,000) = $78,000. Arithmetic on the published tiers only; a specific purchase's tax is confirmed by the buyer's lawyer/notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first-time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 (partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both far below this page's floor, so buyers in this band budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant phase-out at this band:** the Province states the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 — a full regular grant at or below that assessed (or partitioned) value, reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over $2,075,000, and $0 for the regular grant if the value is over $2,189,000 — so many homes in this band receive a reduced grant or none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) phases out below this band's floor, so it does not offset GST on an over-$1.8M new purchase; the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) likewise sits below the band. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums are 0.6%–4.5% — a page stating 2.80%–4.00% is WRONG (no such page statement is documented in this topic's research file); a purchase in this band is conventionally financed in any event. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer markets a new home in this band, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. No presale project specific to this band was verified. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Flood and ALR:** Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure); ALR status is parcel-specific through the ALC's maps. Both are address-specific checks for any bluff, foreshore or farm-edge listing in this band. Delta property taxes: 2026 due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here:** the 3% Empty Homes Tax is Vancouver only and is never used for a Delta property. Basis: City of Vancouver tax, stated for contrast only.
