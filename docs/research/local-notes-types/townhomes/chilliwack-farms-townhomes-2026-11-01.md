## 2. COMPLEXES

Farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold land, and the research file confirms no strata farm complex on an official source: "strata farm product is not a Chilliwack pattern (no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source)." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/chilliwack-farms.md, §5)
- The reason is structural: on ALR land a residence cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and agricultural land must not be subdivided unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6). The legal structure a townhouse strata needs does not arise on reserve land as of right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- Farm-sector feeds are generic: a listing card appearing on a farms page is NOT a confirmed operation in any named sector (dairy, poultry, berry, forage or any other), and no card is presented that way in this file. Quota and marketing-board items stay UNVERIFIED without a marketing-board source — none is cited here. (Research file, §5 — its typical-buyer wording is a characterization, not a sourced sector statistic, and is not restated as fact.)
- Apartment condos: none documented under this topic — Chilliwack's apartment stock is filed in the city's sub-area condos files, not here, and apartment buildings are never townhouse stock in any case.
- Bare-land strata: none verified on Chilliwack farm land in this pass, and on ALR parcels the no-stratification rule above bars the form without ALC permission. A bare-land strata elsewhere in the city would be its own form, filed by sub-area, not under this topic.
- Pad-rental parks are NEVER townhouse stock: no pad-rental park is counted here, whether or not one sits in a farm district.
- Tenure traps excluded with reason: leasehold, corporation-share, undivided-interest, co-operative, life-lease and non-market rental product — none verified on Chilliwack farm land in this pass, none is townhouse stock, and none is counted. (Research file, §5)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This topic has no townhomes, so this section carries the full ALR frame that governs Chilliwack farm land — the rules a townhouse buyer redirected to a farm parcel actually faces — followed by the standard provincial frame for any strata townhouse purchase elsewhere in the city.

- **No stratification in the ALR without ALC permission:** depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR housing caps:** one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² total floor area built without an ALC application, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence where conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City of Chilliwack bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR administration in Chilliwack:** properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and the City lists a new house, coach house or suite, placing fill or removing soil, a non-farm use, and subdivision or a boundary adjustment among the items that may need an ALC application, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. ALR status for a parcel is checked on the City Webmap or the ALC maps. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm-class thresholds — the full three tiers (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of (1) $2,500 if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; (2) $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; (3) $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. Farm classification applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **City plan: Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 5500.** The Agricultural Area Plan is Schedule B1 of that OCP and the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan is Schedule B5. Any page citing a "Schedule D" Agricultural Plan or OCP Bylaw 5024 for this ground is stale — the current citations are Bylaw 5500, Schedule B1 (Agricultural) and Schedule B5 (Sardis); the research file itself does not document that stale citation on this topic page, so it is flagged here as the standing correction for Chilliwack ground generally, not as a defect of this slug's own research. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf and https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Farm Home Plate:** the OCP includes Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate, to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Farm Home Plate regulations in the Zoning Bylaw are not met. In Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, the Farm Home Plate in the AL, AR and AC zones is capped at 4,000 m² lot coverage for the principal residential use and 1,000 m² for an additional residential use, with siting limits of 70 m / 80 m from the front or exterior side lot line (or 50 m / 60 m from the rear lot line), and the requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Agricultural zones and minimum lot areas:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, Section 6 — AL and AU subdivide at 7.5 ha (excluded/exempted or ALR-approved), 1 ha by boundary adjustment, 0.2 ha by ALC-approved homesite severance or boundary adjustment; AS at 2 ha / 1 ha / 0.2 ha on the same pattern; AR at 0.2 ha with community water, 1 ha otherwise; AC at 2 ha / 1 ha / 0.2 ha; AFP at 0.4 ha; minimum lot width is 25 m in all six zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH in Chilliwack:** SSMUH amendments are in Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024 — the farm zones are not where SSMUH lands, and on ALR land the ALC housing rules above govern residences in any case. Basis: sibling topic file on disk, `~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/chilliwack-farms-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`, §3. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Fee-simple reality on a farm parcel, stated plainly:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — City zoning, ALR rules, and any registered covenants on title govern. That is the diligence frame for every purchase under this topic.
- **For a strata townhouse elsewhere in Chilliwack — the provincial frame:** Form B under Strata Property Act s. 59 (fees, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, depreciation report attached); depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); at least 10% of the operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; long-term rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid (ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Water and septic:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of fees and rentals before lawful use; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed, installed, repaired, maintained or inspected only by an Authorized Person. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Tax timing:** Chilliwack property taxes carry a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026; drainage and dyking levies sit on top of municipal rates. Basis: sibling townhomes file on disk, `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-chilliwack-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`, §3. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **GST and rebates (new construction only):** 5% GST on new homes, not resales; the FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate is 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. **PTT, compression flagged:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row compresses that 3% + 2%. **HBP** $60,000; **FHSA** $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; **CMHC** premiums 0.6%–4.5%. **Presales (REDMA):** 7-day rescission (s. 21) and deposits in trust (s. 18) — no farm-land townhouse presale was verified for these to attach to. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there townhouses on Chilliwack farm land?** No — this topic file carries no townhouse roster, farm parcels are freehold land, and ALR residences cannot be stratified without ALC permission. Chilliwack townhouses are an urban sub-area product, filed by sub-area in this program. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How much of Chilliwack is farmland?** The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, and its Official Community Plan carries an Agricultural Area Plan (Schedule B1 to Bylaw 5500) for that land. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: What zones cover Chilliwack farms?** Section 6 of Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets six agricultural zones: AL, AU, AS, AR, AC and AFP — a small-lot (AS) purchase and a lowland (AL) purchase are different products with different subdivision and use rules. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Chilliwack?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; Chilliwack also applies its Farm Home Plate rules (Development Permit Area 4) to residential building on agricultural land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **Q: Can a Chilliwack farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area in excess of 4 ha above 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — and a new owner continuing to farm must apply by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" answer understates the test. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Why are there no market numbers in this file?** Chilliwack is a CADREB market. The SnapStats reports held for this program cover the Fraser Valley and Greater Vancouver boards only, publish no farm category, and contain no Chilliwack figures — so any Chilliwack or farm-specific statistic is UNVERIFIED here rather than borrowed from a neighbouring board. (Research file, §2)
- **Q: Does a farm listing on this page mean the operation is a dairy / poultry / berry farm?** No. Farm-sector feeds are generic — a card on the page is not a confirmed operation in any named sector, and quota or marketing-board status is UNVERIFIED without a marketing-board source. Verify the operation, its licences and any quota from the seller's records, not from the feed. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **Q: Is irrigation water included with a farm parcel?** Not by assumption: non-domestic groundwater use requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, while domestic use is exempt — confirm the licence position for the parcel before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: What is the Farm Home Plate, in one sentence?** Chilliwack's cap and siting control on the residential footprint of a farm parcel — 4,000 m² maximum coverage for the principal residence in the AL, AR and AC zones, sited within the bylaw's distance limits, with a Development Permit required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Agriculture is the city's dominant land use, not a fringe of it:** 67% of Chilliwack's total land base and 29% of its economic activity, on the City's own statement — which is why a "farms" topic page for this city is a land-use file, not a housing-type file. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **The Agricultural Area Plan has a monitor, not just a text:** the AAP was created as part of the Official Community Plan in 2012 in partnership with the Chilliwack Agricultural Commission, and the Agricultural and Rural Advisory Committee (ARAC) monitors its implementation by reviewing agricultural development applications. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **The Farm Home Plate rules switch off under 0.8 ha:** in the AL, AR and AC zones the Farm Home Plate requirements do not apply where a lot is under 0.8 ha — the same 0.8 ha line that triggers BC Assessment's highest farm-income tier ($10,000) for small operations. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Flood hazard is a named zone in the bylaw, not just a map overlay:** Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone. Parcel-level Flood Construction Levels: UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **The rural schools are the topic's family anchor, and their catchments were never run:** Greendale Elementary, Yarrow Community School, East Chilliwack Elementary and Rosedale Traditional Community are confirmed against the Ministry K-12 file with street addresses, but no locator result exists for any farm address — a buyer who needs a specific school must run the exact parcel address. (Research file, §4)
- **Soil class on the live page is copy, not evidence:** the page's "Class 1 to Class 3" wording could not be confirmed from a text source for any specific district or parcel, so no capability class is stated in this file — check the parcel's class on official mapping before pricing soil quality. (Research file, §4)
