## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Aberdeen" is both the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report and an official City of Abbotsford name — profile area 6, "Aberdeen," in the City's 2020 Demographic Profiles, separate from Bradner–Mt. Lehman (area 1) and West Abbotsford (area 7). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- What it is: the rural community around Bradner Road, south of Fraser Highway and west of Clearbrook, north of the Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands. Main roads are Bradner Road (north–south), Fraser Highway (north edge), Mt. Lehman Road to the west, and Huntingdon Road and King Road to the south. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- There is no town centre, transit node or multi-family corridor in Aberdeen: BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove/Abbotsford) runs along Fraser Highway at the north edge toward Bourquin Exchange, and no local route was verified running through the interior rural roads. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Where townhouse stock concentrates: in exactly one place — the western edge of the sub-area by the Aldergrove border (Lantern Park, Section 2). Everywhere else is freehold farm and rural residential land. Sources: Section 2 below, and the research file (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/aberdeen-guide.md).
- The board data agrees the attached market here is negligible: Aberdeen recorded 7 attached listings and 0 attached sales in September 2026, against 25 detached listings — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
- SnapStats does not publish a townhome-only figure for Aberdeen: it splits only Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined, and its price figures are Abbotsford-wide (attached median $497,500, $433/sq ft, 97% sale-to-list, 32 DOM — September 2026). - Governing plan: there is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan; Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, is the current Official Community Plan (see the stale-citation flag in Section 3). https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** Lantern Park is strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer would own the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Aberdeen; "freehold strata" in the Lantern Park record is still strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** on a Lantern Park resale the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; BCREA advises reviewing it for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote (30-year projection, at least three CRF funding models); stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. Lantern Park, at 23 homes, is inside the requirement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Lantern Park is expressly NOT age-restricted per the sibling file's MLS record, and no 55+ townhouse complex was verified in Aberdeen. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Why there is no more townhouse stock — agricultural zoning and the ALR:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, Section 200 agricultural zones apply across rural Aberdeen (A1 minimum subdivision 8.0 ha; A2, 16.0 ha). Rural parcels are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve; subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings need City zoning compliance and, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — standing correction in the commissioning brief. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm-class thresholds (full three-tier form):** $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 — standing correction in the commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error; no such flat line is documented in this area's research file.
- **City plan / SSMUH:** Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, is the current OCP — the research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE and flagged here as a Jerry sweep item. June 2024 SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED and "3560-2024" must not be asserted. SSMUH applies to lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use, which is not Aberdeen's agricultural zoning. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule only) — standing corrections in the commissioning brief. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Second dwellings on farm lots (the Aberdeen substitute for "more units"):** the A1 zone permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot under the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². These are farm dwellings tied to the parcel, not strata townhouses, and cannot be sold as separate strata lots. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Taxes on a townhouse purchase:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M; the FTHB program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000), and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). Resale townhouses carry no GST; a new townhouse would carry 5% GST, with the FTHB GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) returning 100% to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, for agreements on/after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, and the older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out) underneath. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA), HBP/FHSA, CMHC:** no presale project was verified in Aberdeen; if one were offered, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission and s.18 requires deposits in trust. HBP allows $60,000 per buyer; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse in Aberdeen?** In one complex only: Lantern Park, 27640 Lantern Avenue, 23 freehold strata townhomes (EPS7130, built 2021) at the Aldergrove-border edge. No other townhouse complex was verified in the sub-area. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/6-27640-lantern-ave-112242
- **Q: Is Lantern Park strata or fee-simple?** Strata — freehold strata, EPS7130, managed per the building record by Associa. There are strata fees and a Form B package on resale; it is not a fee-simple purchase. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/6-27640-lantern-ave-112242
- **Q: Is Lantern Park 55+?** No — MLS data in the sibling file records "Age Restrictions: No." It is a family townhome complex. In BC the only legal strata age bylaw is 55+ in any event (SPA ss.123.1/123.2). https://www.oneflatfee.ca/mls/listing.r3151809-23-27640-lantern-avenue-abbotsford-v4x-0c3.109470932/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Lantern Park unit, and are pets allowed?** The sibling file records rentals allowed and pets allowed with restrictions for this complex; separately, long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, while pet bylaws remain enforceable — confirm both in the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Why is there no other townhouse complex here — could one be built?** The land base is agricultural: A1/A2 zoning with 8.0 ha / 16.0 ha subdivision minimums and ALR rules on top, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. A townhouse rezoning on ALR farm land would need City and, where required, ALC approvals — no such project was verified. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: What parking does a Lantern Park home have?** The building record lists visitor parking, single garages and double garages across the complex; the stall legally attached to a specific unit is stated on its Form B. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/6-27640-lantern-ave-112242
- **Q: How do I check Lantern Park for coming levies, as a 2021 build?** Through the depreciation report in the Form B package: at 23 lots the strata must have one every five years, with a 30-year projection and three CRF funding models, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF annually. The building record also states the 2-5-10 warranty runs to 2031. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What insurance deductible should I budget for?** The strata's water-damage deductible can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused a loss; the amount is in the strata insurance summary and is UNCONFIRMED for Lantern Park until its documents are read. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Which schools serve Lantern Park's edge of Aberdeen?** Catchments are address-based (SD34 Policy 18). The locator run for 2975 Bradner Road (Aberdeen Elementary's own address) returns Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary; run the Lantern Avenue address itself before relying on it. https://aberdeen.abbyschools.ca/ and https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q: Could I buy a farm property instead and add a second home for family?** Within tight caps only: an A1 Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit (90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha; 186 m² over 40 ha) or a suite to 100 m², tied to the farm parcel — and the homes cannot be stratified and sold separately. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025** (Abbotsforward 2050; public hearing November 25, 2025), emphasizing more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enabling 37,185 units over 20 years — the priority areas are the urban nodes, not agricultural Aberdeen, so the new OCP does not signal townhouse supply coming to Bradner Road. The research file's "2600-2016" OCP citation is stale under the standing correction — flag for Jerry. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Lantern Park sits on the municipal seam:** its marketing describes it as "Aldergrove / Abbotsford Border" product by the Reimer Group, and its postal addresses read as the Aldergrove edge — a buyer searching only "Aberdeen" or only "Aldergrove" can miss the sub-area's one townhouse complex. https://www.readkong.com/page/built-27640-lantern-avenue-aldergrove-abbotsford-8573656
- **The attached-sales count underlines the point:** Aberdeen's attached segment recorded zero sales in September 2026 (7 listings, sales ratio not reported) while its detached segment turned over 3 sales — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/aberdeen-guide.md).
- **The City's agriculture work (AgRefresh) entrenches the farm pattern:** the A1 Uplands / A2 Lowlands structure with 8 ha and 16 ha subdivision minimums is the City's stated framework for areas like Aberdeen — land assembly for a townhouse project runs against both the zoning and the ALR. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **The airport is a building rule, not just noise:** land inside the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations area (SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500) is subject to height limits, and a developer on AZR land must obtain NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals before the City issues a building permit. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- **Aberdeen Elementary is the area's one school and its catchment chain is settled:** the SD34 School Locator run for the school's own address (2975 Bradner Road) returns Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary, matching Eugene Reimer's published feeder pattern. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
