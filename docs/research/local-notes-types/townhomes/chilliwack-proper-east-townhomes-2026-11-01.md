## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Thornberry Lane (8 units, listing-stated) is over that threshold; Heritage Lane's lot count is UNCONFIRMED but its unit numbers reach at least 13, so it is treated as over the threshold on that evidence — confirm on the Form B. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER the threshold — neither counted complex is such a strata on the evidence checked. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. Heritage Lane's directory line reads "rentals allowed," consistent with current law; the void rule is flagged in-entry in §2 for the two apartment buildings whose directories still print "rentals not allowed" (Mountain View Estates, Evergreen Estates). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted townhouse complex here is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Chilliwack Proper East (§2) — a listing calling a counted complex "fee simple" contradicts the verified record and needs a title check. (Heritage Lane's "Freehold Strata" ownership line is strata title, not fee-simple non-strata title.)
- **City plan:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — specifically its **Schedule B2** Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan for this ground, with the Schedule D development permit areas (D-1 Drinking Water Supply through D-10 Industrial) applying per parcel. **Standing schedule correction:** the Sardis plan is **Schedule B5** and the Agricultural Plan is **Schedule B1** of the same bylaw — a live page citing "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" for Sardis or agricultural ground is stale. This slug's own research does NOT document that stale citation on the chilliwack-proper-east page, so it is recorded here as the standing correction only, not asserted as a defect of this page. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted **June 18, 2024**, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. **Secondary suites and ADUs (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite); garden suites are capped at 100 m²; ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite; and no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, with new suites not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a **5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026** — the City of Chilliwack's own two-stage calendar, not the rural regime and not Mission's flat 10%. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **ALR and flood:** where a parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), and the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Parcel-level ALR and flood-construction-level status: UNVERIFIED — check per PID. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/chilliwack-proper-east.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $600,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $400,000 ($8,000) = **$10,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both counted complexes are resale stock. No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug: this file's own research documents none, so the known 2.80%–4.00% page error is NOT asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21).
