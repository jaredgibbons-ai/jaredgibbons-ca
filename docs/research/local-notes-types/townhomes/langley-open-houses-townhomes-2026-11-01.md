## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction, and must disclose at the outset whether they will be able to represent the consumer as a client. Dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018, except in the rarest of circumstances — the host cannot simply "also represent" a buyer who walks in. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **The one-hour offset (this feed only, on this file's own research):** on every labelled card read, the card's top time block is exactly one hour later than the "Open House on…" line directly under it, and the listing text matches the block on some cards and neither on another (a card whose text says "OPEN HOUSE SUN. Nov 1st 2-4pm" sits under a 01:00 PM–03:00 PM block and a 12:00PM–2:00PM line). Which time is authoritative is UNVERIFIED. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before setting out — this warning applies to a townhouse open exactly as to any other listing on the feed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes, after the open):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line a host repeats at a townhouse open is void for long-term rentals, and is flagged VOID in-entry wherever it appears in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only, stated plainly. If the townhouse on the feed is fee-simple, there are no strata documents to ask for at the open; diligence is title, zoning and covenants. A coach house in the Township of Langley is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 — never strata, and never townhouse stock in this program. (Standing corrections, 2026-11-01.)
- **Two municipalities, two rule sets:** Willoughby Heights, Aldergrove and Walnut Grove are Township of Langley sub-areas — Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs, and the Township's SSMUH bylaw is **Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024; NO 6-plex tier exists in it** (a page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong). The Township's secondary suite cap is 120 m², and its R-CL zone (s. 403) is 220 m² minimum lot / 464.5 m² maximum. The Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); the Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 only, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — never upgrade reading-stage bylaws. Langley City is a separate municipality: its Zoning is **Bylaw 2025 No. 3300** (cite the number; its adoption date is conflicted in sources — UNVERIFIED where a date is needed). A Langley City open house is not under Township rules. (Standing corrections, 2026-11-01; research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2% structure, not a separate rate — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted). First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the frame if an event on this feed is a developer/presentation-centre event rather than a resale open. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Down-payment programs / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per person; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Langley townhouse open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation while the host is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but once you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Which time do I show up — the card says 2–4, the line under it says 1–3?** Neither is verified as authoritative: this file's research documents exactly that one-hour split on every labelled card on this feed, with listing text that sometimes matches the block and sometimes matches neither. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before you drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Does every listing on the Langley open-houses page hold an open house?** Not on the evidence of the first page: five of twelve cards carried an open-house time block and seven carried no open-house label or time at all — treat an unlabelled card as a listing to confirm privately, not as an open house. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Is the host the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side; a BC licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and dual agency is prohibited except in the rarest circumstances. Use the open for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: The townhouse looks strata — what do I ask for after the open?** The Form B package: strata fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas of 5+ lots since July 1, 2024). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: What if the townhouse is fee-simple — is there still a Form B?** No. A fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules (Township Bylaw 2500 or Langley City Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, depending on which municipality the unit is in) and any registered covenants or HOA documents. Ask the host which tenure the unit is before asking for strata documents that may not exist. (Standing corrections, 2026-11-01.)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Langley townhouse I buy at an open?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a directory or host says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which Langley areas appear on the feed, and how should I group a run?** On the first page read: Aldergrove, Willoughby Heights and Walnut Grove. The labelled cards sit in Willoughby Heights and Walnut Grove, neighbouring Township sub-areas, while the Aldergrove cards sit at the community's east end — plan Aldergrove as its own trip rather than an add-on. Whether the full feed reaches every Langley sub-area is UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/langley-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Is there a Langley townhouse median I can price an open against?** No townhome-only one: SnapStats reports Langley attached product only as Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined (699 active, 128 sold, median $611,000 in September 2026), and Willoughby Heights — where most of the attached depth is — at 369 attached active / 70 sold with no sub-area median published.
