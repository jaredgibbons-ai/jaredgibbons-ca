## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The ALR frame governs this topic — full frame stated.** On ALR land: one principal residence per parcel is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²**, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside the principal residence** (a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites); one additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum **90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha** — and further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR, and replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — the rule that makes §2's zero structural rather than a gap in the search. There is therefore no Form B, no strata fee and no depreciation report for a farm home: the strata diligence block below applies only to the urban townhouse stock a buyer might compare a farm purchase against, and it is stated so the contrast is explicit.
- **Form B diligence (urban strata comparison stock only):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No farm parcel in this topic has one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata comparison stock only):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Neither rule touches a freehold farm parcel. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental bylaws (strata comparison stock only):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid — every directory "rentals not allowed" line for a strata is VOID on that rule; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). On farm land the rental question is different — see the ALR short-term item below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple position — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. A Surrey farm dwelling goes further: it is a freehold single-family residence on an ALR parcel, governed by Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, the Surrey 2050 OCP (**Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED, never cite 18020 as current) and the ALC frame above, which is the stricter layer.
- **Urban rules that do NOT carry onto ALR parcels:** Surrey's SSMUH framework (in force July 8, 2024 — 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop) applies to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer — it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules govern. The urban secondary-suite cap of **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** likewise does not create a detached suite on ALR land: the suite must be inside the principal residence. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals — two layers, both restrictive here:** in the ALR, whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. The City layer separately permits STRs owner-only (owner living at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them) with a business licence the City's STR page states at **$358 per year** — carried here with a **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. Neither layer gives a farm buyer a whole-house STR by right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9) — full three-tier thresholds:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area of the farm operation is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31**, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement of these thresholds is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2, further 5% after September 2 — city-wide figures; farm-class assessment changes the parcel's tax treatment, not the due dates. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 farm purchase with no exemption and the whole value taxed at the general rates = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000** — an illustration of the tier math only; a real farm bill turns on the residential/non-residential split. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (farm financing is commonly commercial/agricultural lending rather than insured residential lending — lender-specific: UNCONFIRMED as a general farm rule). 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales; whether a new farm residence purchase qualifies for a rebate turns on its own facts. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse on a Surrey farm?** No — not as strata. ALR residences cannot be stratified, so farm housing is freehold single-family product: one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one small additional residence per parcel. Surrey's townhouse complexes are urban stock, counted in the sub-area townhomes files. (§2 above) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single Surrey farm belt in the sources used here: the live feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets. The feed is generic — those cards are not confirmed farm-sector operations. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-farms.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; anything beyond that requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I live in the old house while I build the new one?** Not by right: replacing a principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built — demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not need that approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Surrey farm house?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR it requires ALC approval, an additional residence can be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism on an actively farmed, farm-classified property, and the City separately requires an owner-host living at the property with a $358/yr licence (see §3 for the disclosed $350 conflict). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Are the Surrey market numbers in this file farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the §1 figures are Surrey residential context only (detached median $1,215,000 and "Condos & Townhomes" median $512,944 in September 2026), and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-farms.md)
- **Q: What income does a Surrey farm need to keep farm classification?** BC Assessment's three tiers: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of actual value over 4 ha, or $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — application deadline October 31 for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is there a Form B or depreciation report on a farm home?** No. There is no strata, so there is no Form B, no strata fee, no CRF and no depreciation report. The diligence substitutes are title, the ALC/ALR record, City zoning and flood checks, servicing (water/septic — UNVERIFIED as a general picture in this pass, check per parcel), and the farm-class position. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Surrey farm trading is thin and parcel-specific.** The Surrey-wide farm feed showed 3 listings at reading, and the acreage-associated Port Kells sub-area recorded 4 detached listings and 0 sales in September 2026 — there is no deep Surrey farm market to price against; production history, water position, buildings and ALC approvals in place are what a farm buyer's lender and lawyer price. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-farms.html
- **Urban density rules stop at the farm gate.** Surrey's SSMUH unit counts (3 / 4 / 6 by lot size and bus-stop proximity) apply to eligible urban lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on ALR land the count of residences is set by the ALC frame — one principal plus at most one small additional — no matter how large the parcel is. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **The replacement-house trap is an approval, not a demolition detail.** Living in the existing house while a new principal residence is built needs ALC approval; a buyer who assumes they can phase the build around occupancy learns otherwise at the permit stage. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Agri-tourism is the only STR door for the second house — and it has two locks.** An additional residence can be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation, and only on a property that is actively farmed AND holds farm classification — lose the classification and the door closes. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Two of the feed's three listings sit in creek country.** The Colebrook / Sullivan side lies in the South Newton plan area, whose fish-bearing creeks (Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek, Hyland Creek tributaries) carry riparian rules that can limit building envelopes on creek-side farm parcels — a constraint no acreage figure shows. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **The site's ALR tool is not a verification source.** The live page's ALR property search tool covers Surrey, Langley and Abbotsford addresses, but its data sources were not verified in this pass — a tool result is a starting point for the ALC and City checks, not a substitute for them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-farms.html
