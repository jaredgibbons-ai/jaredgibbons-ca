## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every counted complex in §2.1 — all strata or tenure-UNCONFIRMED; for a tenure-UNCONFIRMED Cottonwood complex, this block is the first thing the title search settles).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For the six tenure-UNCONFIRMED Cottonwood complexes, whether a Form B even exists is part of the diligence: if the title is fee-simple, there is no Form B and §3's fee-simple track applies instead (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Maple Ridge complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such in its home file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Applied here: **zero** Maple Ridge strata townhouse complexes are verified 55+; the city's verified 55+ strata are apartment-form (Fraserview Village), its 55+ co-op is St. George's Village, and Haney Pioneer Village is **60+** co-op — a higher bar, never restated as 55+ (§2.6).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in the city (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only.
