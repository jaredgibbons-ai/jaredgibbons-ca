## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this topic file, this section carries the open-house frame first (verbatim, as the sibling topic files carry it), then the townhouse-purchase frame a buyer needs after the open house — because the open house is where a Surrey townhouse purchase starts, and the strata documents are where it is actually diligenced.

### The open-house frame

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in British Columbia. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **What that means in practice:** chatting factually with the host at an open house does not by itself make you their client — but once you start sharing your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage. Use the townhouse open house for facts (fees, bylaws, parking, the depreciation report's date); save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. ### The townhouse-purchase frame (after the open house)

- **Strata diligence — the Form B regime (Surrey's verified townhouse stock is strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The open house shows the unit; the Form B shows the complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. On Surrey's established townhouse stock (1987–2000 complexes dominate the verified roster) that report is the levy-risk document — ask its date at the open house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. An open-house host or feature sheet repeating a directory's "rentals not allowed" line for a Surrey townhouse complex is repeating unenforceable text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. A large share of Surrey's verified townhouse stock is presented as 55+ (Peninsula Village, Bishop's Green, The Crescent among the cross-references in Section 2) — confirm on each Form B whether the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw before assuming who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** where a Surrey townhouse is genuinely fee-simple, it carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple complex is counted in this topic file (it counts none at all), and the cross-referenced complexes in Section 2 are all strata — a "no-strata townhouse" on the feed needs its tenure proved from title, not from the card. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs, including its SSMUH zones RA to R6 (up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 on larger lots, up to 6 on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop). Secondary suites in Surrey are capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, one per dwelling. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only, with a City business licence the City's own page prices at $358 per year (plus any applicable application fees) — carried here at $358, with the disclosed conflict that the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City's licence page before quoting. A strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhouse on the feed is a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). An open house at a presale presentation centre does not shorten that window. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST applies to new construction only, not to resales of the established complexes in Section 2. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Surrey townhouse open house?** No official source requires it. BCFSA's Real Estate Rules excuse disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file Q1)
- **Q: Is the host at the open house the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side of the transaction; BCFSA requires a licensee to work for either the buyer or the seller and to inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients — assume anything non-factual you tell the host can reach the seller. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file Q5)
- **Q: How do I know the open house time is current — and is there a time error on the Surrey page?** Each listing on the page carries an OPEN HOUSE label with a date and start and end times, and the site's open-house FAQ states the page updates automatically throughout the day as new MLS open houses go live — check the page the day you plan to go. On accuracy: for THIS page a 1-hour card offset is UNVERIFIED (the research fetch returned no cards), so no offset is claimed either way in this file — confirm the time with the listing agent for any townhouse you are driving to. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html (Research file Q2)
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID at an open house?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers (Research file Q3)
- **Q: Which Surrey areas can I cover in one townhouse run?** Group by the City's own communities — City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley — which is also how the site splits its open-house pages (South Surrey and Cloverdale have their own). The cross-referenced townhouse stock in Section 2 clusters in South Surrey on the 152 Street corridor, so a South Surrey run covers the deepest townhouse set in one trip. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities (Research file Q4)
- **Q: So many Surrey townhouse opens are in 55+ complexes — can I buy if I am under 55?** Ownership itself is not age-restricted — a 55+ bylaw (the only age bylaw BC law still allows, SPA ss. 123.1/123.2) governs who may RESIDE, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Whether you can occupy, or only own, depends on the registered bylaw on the Form B — ask before the open house, not after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: The feature sheet says the townhouse complex bans rentals. Can I rent it out if I buy?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. What can still bind you: a short-term rental bylaw (plus Surrey's owner-only STR licence, $358/yr on the City's page), and a 55+ age bylaw limiting who may live in the unit, which decides your tenant pool in complexes like Peninsula Village and Bishop's Green. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What should I ask for after I like a townhouse at an open house?** The strata package behind the Form B: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024), financial statements, CRF balance and planned work — the items BCREA advises reviewing to identify future special levies. In Surrey's 1987–2000 townhouse stock that package matters more than the staging. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: How many Surrey townhouses hold open houses this weekend?** UNVERIFIED — no official source publishes open-house counts, and SnapStats publishes no open-house category. The feed itself is the count: the page's listings each carry the OPEN HOUSE label with date and start/end times, and the publisher's FAQ states the page updates throughout the day as new open houses go live. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-open-houses.html (Research file §2)
- **Q: Where do I start my homework before offer day?** At the regulator: the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for purchasing information — read its consumer pages before offer day, not after. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home (Research file §6)
