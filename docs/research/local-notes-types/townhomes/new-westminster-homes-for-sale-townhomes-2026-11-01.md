## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every counted complex in §2.1 — all strata, including the townhouse components of the two mixed Glenbrooke North stratas).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Clinton Place (14 units, §2.1) is over the threshold on its verified count. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and note the city-specific overlay in the suite rules below: a New Westminster secondary suite cannot be used for short-term rentals at all, as the City's guide states it. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a city complex is void for long-term rentals and flagged as such in its home file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No New Westminster townhouse complex in §2.1 is verified 55+ in this program; the Blackberry Drive 55+ line is listing text, UNCONFIRMED (§2.6).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified in the city (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**New Westminster zoning, OCP, floodplain, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: Zoning Bylaw No. 6680 (2001). OCP: "Our City 2041," Bylaw No. 7925 (2017)** — the City's long-term land-use frame; the City is working through provincially driven change under an Interim Development Review Framework (first endorsed 2024, updated June 2026) (research file §4: https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects).
- **Townhouse districts — the correction, stated in full: RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B and RT-2C plus RT-3, RT-3A and RT-3B ONLY. RT-A and RT-B do NOT exist in Bylaw No. 6680.** The Brunette and Glenbrooke North research files carry "RT-A/RT-B" in their zoning sections and their 55-plus siblings repeat the error; the sub-area townhomes files correct it in-file, and this assembly corrects it again wherever those sub-areas are touched (§2.1). Related figure discipline: **836.10 m² is a density threshold in an RM apartment district** — it is not a townhouse lot figure and is never cited as one.
- **Floodplain — a defining diligence item for this city.** The Quay and Downtown waterfront sit in **Floodplain Zone 3 (Quay / Downtown NW)** and the Brunette corridor in **Floodplain Zone 4 (Brunette)**, as carried in the sub-area townhomes files (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/quay-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/brunette-townhomes-2026-11-01.md). For any townhouse in those zones, floodplain status belongs in the first round of diligence — insurance, flood construction level and strata insurance deductibles — alongside the Form B, not after it.
- **Infill / SSMUH:** in June 2026 Council adopted OCP and Zoning Bylaw amendments to permit up to six units of infill housing (secondary suites, laneway and carriage houses, triplex, quadplex and other multiplex forms) on single detached and duplex properties across most of the city, under zones RGO-A (up to four units) and RGO-B (up to six units), without a site-specific rezoning. Earlier record: Bylaw No. 8453, 2024 rezoned the roughly 160 "restricted zone" duplex properties to a new RD-1 district permitting rental secondary suites; **Queensborough's SSMUH compliance was extended by the Province to May 4, 2029** — Queensborough runs on its own timeline (research file §4: https://www.newwestcity.ca/devprojects; https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Attachment_C2___Small_Scale_Multi_Unit_Housing_Legislation_and_Implementation_Summary.pdf).
- **Secondary suites:** a suite is a separate dwelling unit within a house, accessory to a single detached dwelling or duplex and contained within the same structure; most single detached dwellings and some duplexes are zoned to permit them; they must meet the Zoning Bylaw, Building Code and Tree Protection and Regulation Bylaw; **they cannot be strata-titled, sold or used for short-term rentals**, and all properties with suites pay annual secondary suite water and sewer charges. **Suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED** — neither is stated in the City guide read in this pass (research file §4: https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Secondary_Suite_Guide_August_2025.pdf).
- **City property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes remaining (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants) on July 3, 2026, and a second 5% penalty applies to any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City held no 2026 tax sale — all delinquent property taxes had been collected (research file §4).
