## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no local strata townhouse stock to apply Form B diligence to — but Delbrook's two apartment stratas carry the full regime, and any future townhouse strata here will too:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a 22-lot strata like Queensbrook, each owner carries roughly 1/22 of any special levy — the Form B math matters more, not less, in a small strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Queensbrook's 22 lots put it squarely inside this requirement. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Neither counted apartment building here carries a published "rentals not allowed" line — Queensbrook's directory position is rentals allowed — so there is no void line to flag in-entry in this file; the rule is stated because buyers cross-shopping District and City buildings will meet such lines elsewhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 — including younger spouses/partners and defined children, so a younger spouse is not automatically barred, but the filed bylaw wording governs). Queensbrook is Delbrook's one verified 55+ building and it is apartment-form; there is no 55+ townhouse here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (all of Delbrook's verified house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (District Zoning Bylaw 3210) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **District zoning and plan — the DISTRICT pair only:** Official Community Plan Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210. Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) states the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth, and the R2 zone minimum lot is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 lot may hold one principal dwelling, one secondary suite and one coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. The former North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan (Bylaw 6750, adopted 1995) is repealed — a seller quoting its designations is quoting a dead bylaw. The City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Mission Creek borders the Delbrook Lands on the west and Mosquito Creek lies farther west, so creek-side and upper-slope Delbrook lots are exactly the lots these areas were written for; check the District's GeoWeb hazard layers before pricing a renovation or rebuild. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/delbrook-transportation-study.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and District single-family utility fees in 2026 total $2,571.20, including water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies here — that is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The recreation centre inside the area is the real one; the famous lands are the old one.** The Delbrook Community Recreation Centre at 851 West Queens Road — main pool, leisure pool and indoor racquet facilities — is operating inside the sub-area, while the former centre's lands at 600 West Queens Road are the redevelopment site. Buyers routinely merge the two addresses; they are different properties on the same road. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts
- **Queensbrook's 55+ status is in the District's own record, not just its marketing.** A 2004 District council report states the 22 units at 678 W. Queens Road, built in 1997, were the only one of three sites designated in 1995 that actually redeveloped for 55+ housing, and a 1996 District report required a Housing Agreement "to ensure the project is occupied by seniors" — the designation has a paper trail most marketed "55+" buildings cannot produce. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418188.pdf
- **Queensbrook's park is its front yard.** Listings describe the 22-unit building as backing onto Belhouse Park and its tennis courts, a short walk to the recreation centre and the Mosquito Creek Trail — the amenity package for the area's only 55+ building is the park-and-trail network, not in-building facilities beyond its club house, workshop, exercise centre and courtyard. https://www.zolo.ca/north-vancouver-real-estate/678-west-queens-road/206
- **The old Delbrook plan is repealed — do not rely on it.** The former North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan (Bylaw 6750, adopted 1995) named Delbrook as one of its five neighbourhoods, but that plan is repealed and District OCP Bylaw 7900 now covers the area; a seller or agent quoting the 1995 plan's designations is quoting a dead bylaw.
