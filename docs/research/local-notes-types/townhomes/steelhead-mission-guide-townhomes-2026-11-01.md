## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Mission's counted strata complexes in the assembly; it currently applies to nothing in Steelhead).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. All four assembled complexes are 5+ lots on their published counts except Archer Green, whose lot count is UNCONFIRMED — the 5+ test for Archer Green is confirmed on its Form B/strata plan. Cherry Lane's lot count carries the §2.1 conflict and is settled nowhere. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (Mission assembly §3; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Cherry Lane is void in-entry — flagged in §2.1 wherever it appears (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex — that is the published position; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer, and no other assembled complex has a published 55+ position in the sources read. No Steelhead strata carries any age bylaw — there is no Steelhead strata (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. A: An in-house secondary suite is permitted inside a single family home if it is registered with the City (new suites need a building permit; a $63 annual suite fee plus utility fees applies), but a detached coach home or garden cottage is a secondary detached dwelling — on rural lots that generally requires the secondary-dwelling ("s") variant of the parcel's zone, which City material shows owners obtaining by rezoning (e.g., RU16 to RU16s). Suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes in Mission (§3).
