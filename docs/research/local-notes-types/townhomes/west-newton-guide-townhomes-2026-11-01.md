# West Newton, Surrey — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- West Newton is the Newton land west of King George Boulevard toward the Delta border (Scott Road / 120 Street side), including the Highway 99 industrial area at the south; the City's West Newton plan documents govern the exact lines. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- Two RapidBus corridors bracket the area: the R1 on King George Boulevard (Newton Exchange to Guildford Exchange, via Surrey Central) on the east edge, and the R6 Scott Road RapidBus on the west edge between Scott Road Station and Newton Exchange; routes 323 (via 124 St) and 364 (via 64 Ave) run through the area. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: in three pockets — the Scottsdale / 121 Street corridor at the west edge (Glenwood Village, Glenwood Village Heights, Lakeview Estates at the Boundary Park edge), the 64 Avenue / 128 Street pocket beside Tamanawis Park (Palette on the Park), and the Strawberry Hill side (Kirkbridge Place on 122 Street). The 72 Avenue cluster at the east edge is apartment condos, not townhouses (Section 2). Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- There are no townhouse towers and no verified fee-simple townhouse complex in West Newton; the verified townhouse stock is 1986–2004 strata, and the Highway 99 portion of the plan area is industrial, not housing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- The Newton Town Centre at King George Boulevard and 72 Avenue is the City's designated town centre serving the area, at the sub-area's east edge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/newton-town-centre-plan
- SD36 schools named in the research file: T.E. Scott Elementary (7079 148 St, inside the plan area on the King George side), Bear Creek Elementary (13780 80 Ave) at the north edge, and Frank Hurt Secondary (13940 77 Ave) east of King George Boulevard; a locator run for 7079 148 St returned T.E. Scott Elementary and Frank Hurt Secondary. Assignment is by address through the SD36 School Locator. The correct name form for the McLeod Road school in the wider Newton area is McLeod Road Traditional. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- Market frame (SnapStats, September 2026, as the research file states it): West Newton attached (condos & townhomes combined) — Inventory 130, Solds 8, Sales Ratio 6%; Surrey-wide attached median $512,944, $491 per sq ft, sale-to-list 99%, 25 days on market. (The same report put West Newton detached at a 20% sales ratio, its "Sellers Best Bet" — a detached figure, never a townhouse figure.) SnapStats publishes no townhome-only figure and no sub-area medians. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the area research file for west-newton-guide (Section 2), read 2026-11-01.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **All five verified complexes are strata, so the Form B is the core document:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports — mandatory 5-year cycle:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote, with 30-year projections and three CRF funding models. West Newton's stock is 1986–2004 wood-frame — this report is where roof, envelope, balcony and plumbing horizons are priced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum 10%:** since Nov 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute at least 10% of its annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. That includes the "Rentals Not Allowed" line MLS building data still records for Glenwood Village (flagged in-entry in Section 2): it reflects an old bylaw record, not current law. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw, and Surrey's own STR regime applies on top (below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **Age bylaws — 55+ only:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 — only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful; lower age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022. The bylaw binds residents, not owners, and Regulation 7.01 exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse/partner and defined child cases. Directly relevant to Glenwood Village and Lakeview Estates — and the reason Glenwood Village Heights' "NO AGE RESTRICTIONS" is a real product difference, not marketing. An older source describing Glenwood Village as designed for adults "45+" predates the current law; its MLS restricted-age field now records 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in West Newton. A fee-simple townhouse carries no strata, no Form B, no strata fees and no bylaws — diligence on title and municipal records. Do not import that profile into any Section 2 complex; all five are strata.
- **Surrey zoning:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 — R1–R6 residential zones in the neighbourhood portion of West Newton and industrial zones in the Highway 99 industrial area at the south of the plan area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton
- **Surrey OCP — current plan only:** the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026, is the current plan. The old PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as current in this file. Within West Newton, the City states the West Newton South plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect; the Newton Town Centre Plan still covers the King George Boulevard / 72 Avenue core. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **SSMUH — in force in Surrey since July 8, 2024:** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, plus one secondary suite per house under the BC Building Code. This affects the house streets around the complexes, not existing strata lots; it does not retroactively legalize an unpermitted suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — the 40% cap, and townhouse lock-offs:** Surrey caps a secondary suite at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area; the City's 2025 change permits lock-off suites in townhouses only in new construction in transit-oriented / frequent-bus-stop areas in RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones, at a minimum 30 m² and under 40% of the principal unit's habitable area. No existing West Newton complex gains a legal lock-off suite from that change. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Short-term rentals in Surrey:** STRs are permitted only owner-operated, in the owner's principal residence (plus at most one secondary suite or accessory unit on the same lot), for stays under 90 days, under a City business licence ($358/yr as the research program states it; the City's 2024 corporate report introducing the regime proposed a $350/yr licence plus application fees — confirm the current fee on the City's licence page before quoting it), with provincial registration also required. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **Flood check for south-end streets:** the south end of West Newton falls toward the Serpentine & Nicomekl lowlands, the dyke-protected floodplain south of Newton, while the residential upland portion (where all five complexes sit) is outside that lowland floodplain — confirm a specific lot against City flood mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **GST / PTT / programs (resale frame):** all five complexes are resales, so no GST arises; the new-build frame — FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031), New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000), first-time-buyer PTT exemption (first $500,000, full to $835,000, partial to $860,000), newly built home PTT exemption (full to $1,100,000, gone at $1,150,000), Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000), FHSA ($40,000 / $8,000 per year) and CMHC premiums (0.6%–4.5%) — applies to the Harth presale and any future new townhouse product, not to Section 2 resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** for the Harth presale at the area's south edge, REDMA s. 18 (disclosure statement) and s. 21 (7-day rescission from the later of signing or disclosure acknowledgement, deposits in trust) govern; assignment terms are whatever the contract and disclosure statement say — none verified: UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/ and https://almasgroup.ca/harth-presale-condos-in-newton-bc/
- **Pad tenancy (Newton Mobile Home Park):** runs under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata law — on a sale the pad tenancy agreement is assigned to the purchaser, subject to the park owner's consent process in that Act. Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy applies on redevelopment, and Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by that Act. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf
- **Surrey property taxes (city-wide):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the area research file for west-newton-guide, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Glenwood Village Heights the same as Glenwood Village?** No — and the difference is the whole purchase. Glenwood Village, 7156 121 St (NWS2768, 1988, 86 homes), is the verified 55+ gated strata complex. Glenwood Village Heights, 6875 121 St (NWS3423, 1991–1992), states "NO AGE RESTRICTIONS" in current listings. Check the strata plan number on the listing and the Form B, not just the name. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **Q: Are all West Newton townhouses strata?** Every verified one is: Palette on the Park, Kirkbridge Place, Glenwood Village, Lakeview Estates and Glenwood Village Heights are all strata complexes (Glenwood Village expressly freehold strata). No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the sub-area. https://www.surreytownhouse.com/buildings/view/30677/palette-on-the-park/surrey/west-newton/12711-64-ave
- **Q: The data says rentals are not allowed at Glenwood Village. Is that still true?** Not for long-term rentals. Every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on Nov 24, 2022, even where MLS building data still records the old restriction. The tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw or an exemption, and the strata can still restrict short-term rentals — which Surrey's owner-occupancy STR regime limits in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we live at Glenwood Village or Lakeview Estates?** Yes, where the 55+ bylaw applies: provincial law exempts a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident, plus live-in caregivers, children under 19 in the resident's care, adult children cared for before 19, and legacy residents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How does buying at Newton Mobile Home Park differ from buying a strata townhouse?** You buy the manufactured home and rent the pad it sits on — no strata fees, purchase subject to park approval, and your land cost is pad rent under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act rather than a strata fee under the Strata Property Act. It is also why the park is never counted as townhouse stock here. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83905-newton-mobile-home-park-6571-king-george-blvd-surrey-bc-v3w-4z4
- **Q: What should I worry about in a 1986–2004 wood-frame townhouse complex?** The next big-ticket items in the depreciation report — roof, envelope, balconies, plumbing — and whether the CRF (minimum 10% of the operating budget since Nov 1, 2023) and its funding models cover them without a levy. Since July 1, 2024 the report is mandatory every five years for stratas of five or more lots, so a current one must exist. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Complex by complex: Glenwood Village building data records pets allowed (a listing adds a 20 kg maximum — listing-stated). Palette on the Park, Kirkbridge Place and Lakeview Estates pet rules: UNCONFIRMED from a primary public source. The registered bylaws govern — confirm before subjects if the pet is non-negotiable. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603
- **Q: Can I run an Airbnb from a West Newton townhouse?** Only inside Surrey's owner-occupancy frame: the unit (or another unit on the same lot) must be your principal residence, you must be the owner-operator, stays must be under 90 days, and you need the City licence and provincial registration — and a strata short-term-rental bylaw can still prohibit it outright. A non-resident investor purchase has no legal STR path. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **Q: How is the commute without a SkyTrain station in the area?** Two RapidBus routes do the heavy lifting — R1 east to Surrey Central Station and R6 west/north to Scott Road Station, both Expo Line connections — plus routes 323 and 364 through the area streets. Door-to-door times: UNCONFIRMED, no official source states them. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/route/R6/direction/0/round-trip
- **Q: Is the south end, near Highway 99, a townhouse area?** No — the City plan identifies the southern part of the West Newton plan area as the Highway 99 industrial area. The verified townhouse stock sits at the 121 Street corridor, 64 Avenue and 122 Street pockets, well north of it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newton

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two complexes on the same street carry almost the same name and opposite age rules.** Glenwood Village (7156 121 St, NWS2768) is 55+; Glenwood Village Heights (6875 121 St, NWS3423) advertises no age restrictions. A search filtered on the name alone will mix the two — the strata plan number is the reliable separator. https://www.estateblock.com/surrey/204-7156-121-st-42603 and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6875-121-street/132
- **Older descriptions of Glenwood Village say "45+".** That wording predates Nov 24, 2022, when BC invalidated every strata age bylaw below 55; the complex's MLS restricted-age field now records 55+, and the registered bylaws and Form B are what govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Boundary Park's 55+ complexes are split between two sub-areas.** Lakeview Estates (6090 W Boundary Drive) is filed by MLS under West Newton, while the Lakewood complexes on the same drive are filed under Panorama Ridge — the same park-edge cluster appears on two different area pages depending on the complex, and Lakeview Estates is counted in this file only. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **West Newton South no longer exists as a plan.** The City states that plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026) — density claims for lots near the southern complexes should be checked against the current OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000, not the old plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/west-newtown-ncps
- **West Newton's subsidized seniors housing and its assisted living share one address and one operator.** At 12075 75A Ave, PICS runs both the 77-unit subsidized independent-living building (Guru Nanak Niwas, over-55, low income) and a 49-unit publicly subsidized assisted-living residence — the local ladder from independent to assisted living without leaving the complex, and neither side is for sale. https://pics.bc.ca/free-programs-services/senior-care-facility/ and https://www.seniorsadvocatebc.ca/quickfacts/location/1866/
- **The King George Boulevard park is small, and that is the supply.** Newton Mobile Home Park has 34 home sites — for a buyer set on the 55+ park form in West Newton, turnover, not a large resale pool, is the supply, and it is pad rental, not townhouse ownership. https://www.mhbo.com/mobile-home-park/83905-newton-mobile-home-park-6571-king-george-blvd-surrey-bc-v3w-4z4
- **Health care:** Surrey Memorial Hospital, 13750 96 Ave, and the Surrey-Newton Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 6830 King George Blvd, serve the area. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Surrey/surrey-newton-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
