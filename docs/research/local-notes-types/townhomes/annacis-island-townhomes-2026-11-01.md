# Annacis Island, Delta — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Annacis Island is an island in the Fraser River in the City of Delta, in the Highway 91 / Alex Fraser Bridge corridor; street access is by Derwent Way, Cliveden Avenue, Eaton Way and Audley Boulevard. https://www.delta.ca/
- "Annacis Island" is the Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name as given; it is not one of the residential neighbourhoods named in the former North Delta Area Plan, and the current Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), treats it as an industrial area — there is no separate Annacis Island area plan and no lettered schedule for it in the consolidated OCP. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The island is bounded by the Fraser River channels around it (the surrounding channels are called Annacis Channel and Annieville Channel); nearby areas are Nordel and the rest of North Delta to the south across the Alex Fraser Bridge, New Westminster to the north, Richmond to the west, and the Tilbury industrial lands to the south-west. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Where townhouse stock concentrates: nowhere. There is no residential land on the island to map — see Section 2. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- Transit is industrial-edge only: route 340 runs Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via Scott Road, Highway 91, the Alex Fraser Bridge and the Cliveden Interchange, serving the island's industrial area edge; no bus exchange on the island itself was confirmed. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- Market stats: the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no Annacis Island sub-area row — its Delta coverage is the North Delta community and its residential sub-areas only — so no SnapStats figures exist for Annacis Island and none are cited in this file. North Delta is a SnapStats-covered community; Annacis Island simply has no residential row in it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)

## 2. COMPLEXES

**Verified townhouse stock: ZERO complexes — 0 strata, 0 fee-simple.** That zero is the finding, stated plainly at the top as required. Annacis Island is Delta's industrial island, and the research file confirms no residential housing mix on it — no houses, no condos, no townhomes. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

- The Delta Official Community Plan 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400) names Annacis Island with River Road East, Tilbury Industrial Park and Roberts Bank as where Delta's industries are primarily located, and the island carries an Industrial designation in that plan; Delta's industrially zoned and designated land totaled 1,527 hectares in Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory, approximately 13% of the region's inventory. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- What trades instead is the Annacis Business Park: freehold industrial lots and strata industrial units, plus port-terminal land. Strata industrial units are NOT townhouses — they are warehouse/industrial strata lots — and they are not counted as townhouse stock in this file. The sibling condos file (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/annacis-island-condos-2026-11-01.md, on disk) reaches the same zero for residential condos. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- The island's named anchors are industrial, not residential: the Annacis Auto Terminal at 820 Dock Road, a Port of Vancouver terminal whose permit lists the land use designation as "Port Terminal" and the tenant as WWL Vehicle Services Canada, Ltd. (Wallenius Wilhelmsen), and Metro Vancouver's Annacis Island Wastewater Treatment Plant, one of Metro Vancouver's secondary treatment plants, serving the Fraser Sewerage Area. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf and https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- No public school, City park, recreation centre, library branch, shopping centre or grocery store on the island was confirmed in official sources — consistent with there being no residential population to serve. https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- No 55+ townhouse stock exists here either: the sibling 55-plus file (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/annacis-island-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, on disk) verifies no 55+ housing of any kind on the island. None of Delta's five verified 55+ complexes (Delta Green, The Lexington, Tsawwassen Greene, South Pointe, Laburnum Park Place) is on Annacis Island, and none is townhouse-form in any case — they are apartment-form or bare-land strata in other Delta sub-areas and are never imported as townhome stock. (Sibling file above.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why there are no townhouse rules to apply on the island itself:** the governing layer is industrial. The Delta OCP 2024 (Bylaw No. 8400, consolidated, no lettered schedules) designates Annacis Island industrial, and port-terminal parcels are also subject to Vancouver Fraser Port Authority permitting — the approval path for a parcel depends on which regime it sits under, and neither regime is residential townhouse development. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** City pages cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017), while an internal title elsewhere reads "8400, 2025" — the zoning-bylaw number is CONTESTED in the program's sources and is flagged here, never asserted as settled. The industrial zone names and minimum lot standards for Annacis parcels were not confirmed from a City zone sheet: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/annacis-island.md)
- **SSMUH does not create island housing:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow four units on residential properties (six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road, in North Delta) — they apply to residential land, and Annacis Island has none. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **If a buyer pivots to a North Delta strata townhouse instead (the nearest real stock), the BC standard block applies:** Form B diligence under Strata Property Act s. 59 (fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata, general):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata, general):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a BC strata is void in-entry; only 55+ age bylaws remain valid (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 and Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast (general):** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse exists on Annacis Island because no townhouse of any tenure exists here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Buyer frame (for the North Delta purchase this file points to):** FTHB GST/HST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; PTT with first-time-buyer and newly-built exemptions; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. No island-specific levy was confirmed on a City source. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse on Annacis Island?** No — there is none to buy. The island is industrial land under the Delta OCP 2024, and the research confirms no residential housing mix on it at all. The nearest townhouse stock is in North Delta, on the mainland south of the Alex Fraser Bridge. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: But I saw strata units listed in the Annacis Business Park — aren't those townhouses?** No. Those are strata industrial units — warehouse/industrial strata lots whose owners together own the common property as a strata corporation. Same Act, completely different product: no home, no residential use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Could the island be rezoned residential one day?** No source read signals that: the current OCP (Bylaw No. 8400, 2024) designates the island industrial and names it among the places Delta's industries are primarily located, and the port auto terminal was expanded, not wound down (see Section 5). Any future change is UNCONFIRMED speculation — do not price it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Where do people who work on the island actually live?** The residential areas across the bridge: Nordel and the rest of North Delta to the south, New Westminster to the north, Richmond to the west. Route 340 connects Scottsdale Exchange to 22nd Street Station via the island's edge, which is the transit link island workers use. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
- **Q: If I buy a strata townhouse in North Delta instead, what document matters most?** The Form B Information Certificate — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary and the most recent depreciation report — plus two years of strata records. It is the diligence package a strata purchase turns on. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Strata or fee-simple — which will I find in North Delta?** Both forms exist in Delta's wider market, and the distinction is the core diligence item: strata means fees, bylaws and a Form B; fee-simple means NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with municipal rules and registered covenants only. Confirm the tenure on title for the specific complex before comparing prices. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Can a North Delta strata still ban rentals or set an age limit?** Rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022, so a "rentals not allowed" line in an old bylaw set or directory is not enforceable; the only valid age bylaw is 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What rebates apply if I buy a new townhouse on the mainland side?** The standard frame: the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M), the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000), PTT exemptions, the $60,000 Home Buyers' Plan, the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year) and CMHC premiums of 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Is flood risk a factor for island property?** For industrial parcels, yes — the island is surrounded by Fraser River channels, and Delta is the diking authority operating over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure citywide; Flood Construction Level, dike setbacks and flood covenants belong in the subject list for any island parcel. Parcel-specific detail: UNVERIFIED. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Q: Which hospital serves the island?** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd, in Ladner; the North Delta Public Health Unit is at 11245 84 Avenue. There is no health facility on the island itself. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The auto terminal got bigger, not smaller:** the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority's Annacis Auto Terminal Optimization Project, in partnership with Wallenius Wilhelmsen, consolidated the Annacis and Richmond auto terminals into the Annacis facility and enabled it to handle up to 480,000 vehicles annually — a 36% increase in capacity — with on-terminal works complete in early 2025 per the port authority's timeline. That is the island's growth story, and it is industrial. https://www.portvancouver.com/project/annacis-auto-terminal-optimization-project
- **The region's sewage is treated here:** Metro Vancouver's Annacis Island Wastewater Treatment Plant is a secondary treatment plant serving the Fraser Sewerage Area — a permanent, high-activity industrial neighbour that shapes truck traffic and odour expectations for nearby industrial units, and one more reason the island is not housing land. https://metrovancouver.org/services/liquid-waste/wastewater-treatment-plants-and-processes
- **Two governments touch an island parcel:** island land can be City-zoned industrial land or Port of Vancouver terminal land (the auto terminal at 820 Dock Road is designated "Port Terminal" under a port authority permit, PER-21-019) — establish which regime a parcel sits under before drafting anything. https://www.portvancouver.com/sites/default/files/wp-permits/wpid-48523/2022-09-26-WEB-COPY_PER-21-019-Issued-Project-Permit-and-Report-VFPA-obo-WWL-Annacis-Auto-Terminal-Optimization.pdf
- **Delta's industrial base is regionally significant:** 1,527 hectares of industrially zoned and designated land in Metro Vancouver's 2020 Regional Industrial Lands Inventory, approximately 13% of the region's inventory — Annacis Island is one of its four named homes (with River Road East, Tilbury Industrial Park and Roberts Bank). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **There is no school catchment to check because there are no homes:** no public school on the island was confirmed in School District 37 material, and SD37 catchments are address-based through the School Locator — an island address has no residential population to catchment. Families shopping "Annacis" are really shopping North Delta catchments. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **The bridge is the whole commute case:** access is Highway 91 via the Cliveden Interchange and the Alex Fraser Bridge, into the business park via Chester Road / Audley Boulevard and Cliveden Avenue — the same corridor route 340 uses. Published drive times: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt340.pdf
