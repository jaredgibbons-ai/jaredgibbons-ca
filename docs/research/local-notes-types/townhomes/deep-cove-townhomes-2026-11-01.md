- Deep Cove is the waterfront village at the District of North Vancouver's far eastern edge, on the shore of Indian Arm at the foot of Mount Seymour, built around Gallant Avenue, Deep Cove Road and Panorama Drive. Deep Cove is one of the six Village Centres named in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (with Lions Gate, Queensdale, Maplewood, Parkgate and Edgemont) — a confirmed District planning name. Exact boundary streets: UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are the village core on lower Gallant Avenue, Panorama Park on Panorama Drive, and the Cove Cliff / Banbury Road streets above the village. - Neighbours: Cove Cliff and Dollarton to the west along Dollarton Highway, Parkgate and Seymour to the south-west at Mount Seymour Parkway, Woodlands and Indian Arm to the north up the inlet, and Belcarra across Indian Arm. Deep Cove Road / Gallant Avenue and Dollarton Highway are the only road in and out — west to Mount Seymour Parkway, Phibbs Exchange and Highway 1; there is no second road connection north up Indian Arm for cars. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern the Cove Gardens diligence question directly (if its tenure confirms as strata) and any strata purchase a Deep Cove buyer makes elsewhere in the District. They do not create verified townhouse stock in Deep Cove itself.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally; the document that would settle Cove Gardens):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Cove Gardens, the first diligence step is confirming strata title itself — title and strata plan, not a directory line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Deep Cove Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Panorama Place's directory "age restrictions" line states no age and is UNCONFIRMED as a 55+ bylaw (see the sibling 55-plus file, which verifies no 55+ ownership complex in Deep Cove). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** Deep Cove's houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. If Cove Gardens were to confirm as fee-simple rather than strata, the same plain rule would apply to it; until title confirms, its tenure stays UNCONFIRMED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **District zoning:** District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 — the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth, and the R2 zone minimum lot is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **SSMUH:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set the same 3 / 4 / 6 structure by lot size and bus-stop distance. The District's OCP is Bylaw 7900 (District of NV — not the City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700, which do not apply in Deep Cove). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Hazards and permits:** OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Deep Cove's forested slope, creek and shoreline lots can fall in several at once, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. Parking and access are a standing District file in the village: a Parking and Access Implementation Plan endorsed in 2015 and a further Parking Review implementation plan to Council on April 22, 2024, covering on-street pay parking, resident parking and time-limited parking. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **District property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply here — Deep Cove is in the District of North Vancouver, not the City of Vancouver. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale exists in this sub-area to apply it to today; the frame, if a future village project is marketed off-plan, is disclosure under REDMA s. 18 and a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, with deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in Deep Cove Estates?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even though the building directory still prints "rentals not allowed" for Deep Cove Estates. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Panorama Place a 55+ building?** UNCONFIRMED: the building directory states "age restrictions" without stating an age, and only a 55-or-older bylaw is lawful today (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2). The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ ownership complex in Deep Cove. Read the registered bylaws before marketing or buying it as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deep Cove townhouse or house?** Cove Cliff Elementary (1818 Banbury Road) in the village area and Seycove Secondary (1204 Caledonia Avenue) beside it are the in-area schools, but Seycove's family also includes Dorothy Lynas and Sherwood Park elementaries on the Parkgate side — catchments are address-based and the address-level result is UNVERIFIED in the research pass, so confirm the exact address with SD44. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: Can a detached lot hold a suite or a coach house?** Yes. Under Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525, an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite and a coach house is a separate accessory dwelling unit; since June 2024, three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, and provincial SSMUH rules also apply by lot size and bus-stop distance. A coach house is a second unit on a house lot — not a townhouse you buy separately. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: What should a Deep Cove buyer check about parking and access?** That the village runs on a single road in and out and a managed parking regime: the District endorsed a Parking and Access Implementation Plan in 2015 and brought a further Parking Review implementation plan to Council on April 22, 2024 covering on-street pay, resident and time-limited parking — summer visitor pressure is a priced feature of village-core streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks matter on a Cove lot?** The GeoWeb layers: Schedule B creek, slope, wildfire and streamside permit areas overlap heavily in the Cove, and the District's flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m. Check the property-level GeoWeb report before pricing a renovation or rebuild. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: How does transit work from Deep Cove?** Local buses connect the village terminus to Phibbs Exchange via Dollarton Highway and Mount Seymour Parkway; since September 7, 2026 the R2 RapidBus runs one-seat from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown, every 6–7 minutes at peak. There is no SkyTrain station or SeaBus terminal in or near the Cove. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deep-cove.md)
- **Q: Is there a seniors residence in the Cove for a downsizer?** None verified inside Deep Cove — no seniors residence of any kind carries a Deep Cove address in the registry, health-authority or operator sources checked by the sibling 55-plus file. The nearest market independent-living rental its operator names is Cedar Springs PARC at 3633 Mt Seymour Parkway, a 10-minute drive west in Parkgate/Seymour — a nearest option, not a Deep Cove option.
