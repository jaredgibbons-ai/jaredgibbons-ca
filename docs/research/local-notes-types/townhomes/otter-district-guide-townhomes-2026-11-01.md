## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated for completeness — no strata townhome is counted on this ground).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — stated plainly. None is counted in Otter District (§2.2).

**Township of Langley rules only — Langley City rules expressly excluded.**

- **ALR frame — the governing fact for much of this district:** ALR land may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence maximum is a total floor area of 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only when entirely contained within the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED for any specific property — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- **Zoning:** rural homes in this part of the Township sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, and the feed's own listings name RU-2 zoning for acreage parcels; a specific parcel's zone comes from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/).
- **SSMUH (Township):** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in the Township**. Eligibility requires Urban Containment Boundary location, municipal water and sewer, and no minimum-lot-size zone over 4,050 m²; a rural Otter lot that fails those tests falls to one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages fall under. **No defect is asserted on this point for this slug:** this file's research documents no 6-plex claim on the otter-district-guide page, so none is asserted here (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **Suites and coach houses:** a Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102); a coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 second unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. Live conflict flagged, not resolved: s. 102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says not permitted (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **Water, septic and flood:** rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS for a parcel's record — and Fraser Health requires a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair and a letter of certification after. Flood position is UNVERIFIED for this district — the Township flood material verified in this program covers the Salmon River valley north of the Otter uplands, and no flood claim is made for the upland acreages (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers; https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons).
- **Township taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are billed separately (https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there any townhouses in Otter District?**
A: None verified — the standalone Otter District townhomes file carries a verified zero (0 strata, 0 fee-simple), the board counted 0 attached listings here in September 2026, and this guide file adds 0 new (§§1, 2).

**Q: Do Langley City rules ever apply here?**
A: No — Otter District is Township of Langley ground throughout this file. The City's bylaws, including its 6-unit SSMUH provision, are expressly excluded; the Township's Bylaw 6020 (3–4 units, no 6-plex tier) is the SSMUH frame (§3).

**Q: How did Otter District sell in September 2026?**

**Q: What is actually for sale under the Otter District label?**
A: Two different products in the feed snapshot: freehold acreages (including multi-dwelling and equestrian properties) and manufactured homes in Langley Grove Estates on pad rent. They are different tenure, lending and resale markets, and neither is a townhouse (§§1, 2).

**Q: Can I build a second house on an Otter District acreage in the ALR?**
A: Within the ALC frame, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² at 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² above 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws; the principal residence is capped at 500 m² and a suite must be entirely inside it. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first — it is UNVERIFIED in this file (§3).

**Q: Can I put four homes on an Otter District lot under SSMUH?**
A: Only if the lot is eligible under Bylaw 6020 — inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water and sewer, and no larger than 4,050 m². Most rural Otter acreages fail those tests and fall to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning. There is no 6-plex tier in the Township (§3).

**Q: What is a coach house in the Township?**
A: Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit, never strata and never a townhouse complex (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve an Otter District home?**
A: The guide names North Otter Elementary (5370 248 St), **Shortreed Community School** (27330 28 Ave — the guide's "Shortreed Community Elementary" is not the Ministry name) and Aldergrove Community Secondary (26850 29 Ave). Catchments are address-based and UNVERIFIED for Otter District addresses — run the exact address in the SD35 School Locator (§1).

**Q: What should I check on an Otter District acreage before closing?**
A: The parcel's ALR status, its zone in GeoSource, the well record in GWELLS, and the Fraser Health septic record and letter of certification — plus the approvals history for any second dwelling, shop or coach home already on the land (§3).
