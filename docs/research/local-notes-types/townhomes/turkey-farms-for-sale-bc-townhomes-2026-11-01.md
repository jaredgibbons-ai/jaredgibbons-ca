## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (contrast only — no strata lot exists in this topic; stated so a buyer can test any "farm condo" or "farm townhome" marketing against it).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. If a "farm condo" is ever marketed in this topic, the Form B and the strata plan number are the first proof to demand (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 in strata anywhere in BC. That rule does not touch a freehold turkey operation — there is no strata corporation (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The farm purchase itself is the fee-simple contrast: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees — title, ALR rules, local bylaws and the contract's quota position only.

**ALR frame — the governing rules for this topic.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified:** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 states strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR; a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). Stated correctly: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission**, and no source checked shows a turkey-farm parcel with such a permission for residential strata use (condos sibling, §3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **Residence caps:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²** (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted **only within the principal residence**, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to **90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**; local government or First Nation government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — in FULL three-tier form:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program; the thresholds are **$2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**; applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification). **Flat-line flag, scoped precisely:** a flat "$2,500+" line for farm classification would be an ERROR against this three-tier form — but no such flat line is documented in this file's own research for the turkey-farms page, so that error is NOT asserted for this slug.
- **Quota and supply management: UNVERIFIED in every particular.** The live page states turkey quota is administered by the BC Turkey Marketing Board; that primary quota may only be transferred on application by both the transferor and transferee with the Board's written approval, with the Board able to impose conditions or refuse; that secondary quota is non-transferable; and that a transfer can be declined where the buyer lacks adequate room in their Turkey Production Unit. **No marketing-board source was opened in the research pass, so each statement is UNVERIFIED site copy only** — verify the quota class and transfer position with the Board directly before subjects are removed (research file §§1, 4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/turkey-farms-for-sale-bc.html).
- **Barn and environmental standards, and avian influenza: UNVERIFIED** — barn construction, ventilation, manure-storage, mortality, groundwater-licensing and biosecurity requirements, and any disease-control effect on a sale, were not confirmed on an official source opened in this pass; the live page's due-diligence list is site copy only (research file §4).
- **Parcel-level position: UNVERIFIED** — whether a specific turkey parcel is in the ALR, its zoning, servicing, floodplain position and its municipal or rural tax schedule are per-parcel checks with the applicable local government, BC Assessment and title (research file §4).

**Q: What residence can a BC turkey-farm parcel in the ALR have?**
A: One principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules possibly more restrictive (§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: What income must a turkey operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: The full three-tier form: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase (§3; research file §7).

A: The live page itself warns a transfer can be declined where the buyer lacks adequate room in their Turkey Production Unit — exactly the term to verify with the Board, because that capacity ground is UNVERIFIED in this file and rests on site copy only (§3; research file §6).

A: Only through the ALC gate: depositing a strata plan — building or bare-land — subdivides the land (SPA s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6), and on ALR land that requires Commission permission after an application or a permission in the ALR General Regulation.
