## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata complex, and Shaughnessy Place townhouse units):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Whitford is a 2026 build — for a developer-held unit the disclosure-statement route applies instead; for a resale, the Form B package is the diligence. A Shaughnessy Place II townhouse buyer diligences the whole mixed complex's documents, not a townhouse-only package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Whitford (68 lots) and Shaughnessy Place both sit above it. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term-rental licensing rules apply on top in this city. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any complex in §2 — all restrictions there are UNCONFIRMED — so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Shaughnessy building is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — the 55-plus pass verifies zero 55+ housing of any tenure in the district. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Shaughnessy (§2); the district's estate houses are fee-simple, but First Shaughnessy houses carry the HCA framework below on top of ordinary city rules — fee-simple here does not mean unregulated.
- **Heritage and zoning (the rules that define this district):** First Shaughnessy (north of King Edward Avenue, within the City-defined HCA boundaries) is governed by the Heritage Conservation Area Official Development Plan (By-law No. 11349, adopted September 29, 2015) and its own District Schedule in the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575: protected pre-1940 properties can only be removed from the protected list through a Council process after a heritage character and value assessment, and the HCA framework gives owners of protected homes additional options — coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill buildings — as incentives to conserve the heritage house. Build size is capped (0.45 FSR with a conserved protected heritage property; otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m² to a 911 m² maximum) and subdivision minimums are 100 ft width / 13,000 sq ft area. South of King Edward (Second and Third Shaughnessy), the standard citywide framework applies: the Vancouver Official Development Plan (which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026) and, for detached lots outside the HCA, the R1-1 multiplex rules under By-law No. 13817 (up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units) — the interaction between R1-1 and the First Shaughnessy District Schedule must be confirmed property by property. The §2 complexes sit at the district's southeast edge under their own site rules (Whitford under the Cambie Corridor Phase 3 framework that rezoned its Oak Street assembly; Shaughnessy Place under CD-1 (65), By-law No. 4532). https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf and http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)
