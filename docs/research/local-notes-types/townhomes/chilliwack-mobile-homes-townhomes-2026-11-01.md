## 3. Rules that affect townhomes — and the manufactured-home frame that actually governs this topic

**Why the strata block does not attach to pad-rental stock.** A home on a rented pad is not a strata lot: there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees for the pad tenancy itself — the governing statute is the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA), below. The strata block applies in this file ONLY to bare-land strata product like Baker Trail Village (§2.3), where the buyer does get strata documents.

**The strata block (bare-land strata product only).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a bare-land strata lot is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, provided within seven days of request, capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating the monthly strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years; sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). Baker Trail Village, at 157 units per the condos sibling's sources, is above the threshold.
- **CRF.** Minimum 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals and age.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022, and since that date the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2 and Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. Baker Trail Village's specialist-list record states "No age restrictions" (§2.3) — a strata age position, separate from any park age rule below.

**The MHPTA frame (pad-rental stock — the bulk of this topic).**

- **Pad-rent increases.** A park landlord may increase site rent only once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, and by no more than the yearly limit: **2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount** for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; **2027 = 2.2% plus the same proportional amount**. Standard increases are notified on RTB Form 11a; an above-limit increase requires an RTB application (Form 52) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **NO pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file, ever.** The live page states a monthly pad-fee range for Chilliwack and Sardis parks — that range is UNVERIFIED site copy: no official source publishes Chilliwack pad rents and no park rent roll was opened in this pass, so the range is noted here as an unverified claim with its amounts withheld (research file §4). Separately, listing-level pad figures met in individual listings for the same park can conflict with each other; any such conflict is noted as a conflict with amounts withheld. The only pad figure that matters is the specific site's tenancy agreement figure, confirmed with the park.
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s. 28).** A tenant may assign a site tenancy or sublet the site only with the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent under the Regulation), a director's order, or authorization in the tenancy agreement; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting (s. 28(3)). The consent request runs on RTB-10 (research file §4).
- **Park closure/conversion (MHPTA s. 42).** Conversion of all or a significant part of a park requires all necessary permits in place first, 12 months' notice, and $20,000 compensation per tenant on or before the notice's effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where a home cannot be moved (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).
- **Manufactured Home Registry and CSA labels.** In BC a manufactured-home sale is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry; a home may not be sold unless its original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 series for mobile homes, A-277 for modular), and a lost label is replaced through Technical Safety BC's electrical-inspection approval-label route; a Registry transport permit expires 30 days after issue (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **PTT on a home-only pad purchase: UNVERIFIED in this file.** The live page states PTT "generally does not apply" to buying only a manufactured home on a rented pad — no passage on the Province PTT page opened in this program states that treatment, so the claim is carried as UNVERIFIED; a lawyer or notary confirms it per purchase (research file §4). Where land IS bought (bare-land strata or freehold), the general PTT tiers below apply.

**City frame.** Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, under OCP Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 (https://chilliwack.com/ocp); the SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, and the suite caps (lesser of 90 m² and 40%; 1 ADU per lot; garden suites to 100 m²) are confirmed city-wide — no manufactured-home-park zone section of Bylaw 5000 was extracted in this pass, so no park-zone figure is stated: UNVERIFIED (research file §4). City property taxes: 5% on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — how a specific park home is assessed and taxed depends on its tenure and registration, a per-property check (https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide — land purchases and new housing).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling stating March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **PTT.** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" summary compresses 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example on a land purchase: $800,000 with no exemption = $14,000. First-time buyer exemption: full on the first $500,000 to $835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 lifetime, $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **CMHC.** Premiums 0.6%–4.5% by loan-to-value (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). Stated explicitly: this slug's research documents NO CMHC premium page error, so the known 2.80%–4.00% error is NOT asserted here. Chattel financing terms for pad homes are lender-specific: UNCONFIRMED in this file (research file §4).
- **Presales (REDMA).** 7-day rescission under s. 21; deposits in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01). None counted on this topic (§2.6).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there any townhomes among Chilliwack's mobile homes?**
A: No — zero verified. No conventional townhouse complex is manufactured-home product, and a park home whose listing form field says "townhouse" is still a manufactured home on a rented pad in law (§2.5). This file counts 0 townhouse complexes and carries no roster of its own.

**Q: A listing calls a Cottonwood Village home a "duplex-style townhouse" — is it one?**
A: No. Cottonwood Village (7610 Evans Rd) is a pad-rental manufactured-home park on leased land; the form field describes the home's shape, not a townhouse strata. Price and diligence follow the tenure — MHPTA, park consent, pad rent — not the form field (§2.5).

**Q: What pad rent should I expect in a Chilliwack park?**
A: No figure is stated in this file, deliberately. No official source publishes Chilliwack pad rents and the live page's range is UNVERIFIED site copy, withheld here. The governing figure is the specific site's tenancy agreement — confirm the current rent, what it includes, and its increase history with the park for the exact site (§3).

**Q: How much can pad rent go up?**
A: Once every 12 months on 3 full months' notice, capped at the yearly limit — 2026: 2.3% plus the proportional levy/utility amount; 2027: 2.2% plus the proportional amount — with anything above the limit requiring an RTB order (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Is Baker Trail Village a townhouse complex or a park?**
A: Neither, in the usual senses — it is a bare-land strata of manufactured homes at 46511 Chilliwack Lake Rd: you own the strata lot (no pad rent) and the home on it, under the Strata Property Act, at 157 units per the siblings' sources (§2.3). It is documented as its own form and counted as a townhouse complex nowhere.

**Q: Do I need the park's permission to buy a home on a rented pad?**
A: The tenancy assignment does: under MHPTA s. 28 the assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (requested on RTB-10), a director's order, or authorization in the tenancy agreement — and the landlord cannot charge a fee for considering or consenting (research file §4).

**Q: Why does the CSA label matter?**
A: A manufactured home may not be sold without its original legible CSA decal (Z-240 mobile / A-277 modular); a lost label must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical-inspection route before the home can be listed for sale (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

**Q: What happens if the park closes or converts?**
A: Under MHPTA s. 42 the landlord needs all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation per tenant, with additional-compensation routes where a home cannot be moved (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Is Rainbow Estates 40+ or 45+?**
A: UNCONFIRMED — its own two feed cards state both (40+ in one, 45+ in the other) for the same 9055 Ashwell Rd community. The rule that governs is the park's current document for the specific site, not either listing (§2.5).

**Q: Does property transfer tax apply when I buy a home on a rented pad?**
A: UNVERIFIED in this file — the live page says PTT generally does not apply to a home-only pad purchase, but no Province PTT passage opened in this program states that treatment. Have a lawyer or notary confirm the tax and Registry handling for the exact structure before subjects come off (§3).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The form field lies politely.** Chilliwack park listings can carry "townhouse" / "duplex-style townhouse" form fields for homes that are, in law, manufactured homes on rented pads — Cottonwood Village is the standing example. Tenure, not shape, decides the statute, the consent process and the financing conversation (§2.5).
- **One community, two age rules, both in print.** Rainbow Estates' own feed cards call the same Ashwell Road community 40+ and 45+ — a buyer who relies on either card without the park's document is relying on listing text that contradicts itself (§2.5).
- **There is exactly one own-your-land manufactured-home strata verified city-wide, and it is not near the parks buyers picture.** Baker Trail Village sits on Chilliwack Lake Road in the river valley, at 157 bare-land strata lots — most "mobile home" searches that assume pad rent miss the one Chilliwack product with no pad rent at all (§2.3).
- **No pad-rent number in this file is an omission — it is the finding.** No official source publishes Chilliwack pad rents; the live page's range could not be verified against any park rent roll, so this program publishes the increase caps (2.3% / 2.2% plus proportional) and withholds every dollar figure (§3).
- **A park can be real and still be counted nowhere.** Cottonwood Village's board placement is UNRESOLVED in this program, so it is documented wherever it is met and counted in no program — a buyer will find its listings under Sardis West Vedder labels while its file home is deliberately undecided (§2.5).
- **The sale is not effective until the Registry says so.** However a Chilliwack manufactured home is tenured, the home's transfer must be registered in the Manufactured Home Registry to be effective in BC — a closing step with no equivalent in a conventional townhouse purchase (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **No market numbers exist for this topic.** Chilliwack is outside SnapStats coverage and CADREB publishes no housing-form statistics — any "Chilliwack mobile home median" a buyer is quoted comes from neither source (research file §2).
