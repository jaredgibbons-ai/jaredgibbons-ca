## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines and the Oval Gardens HOA-fee field in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. Small-strata warning specific to this pocket: a nine-unit corporation (6675 No. 2 Road) splits every roof, fence and repaving decision nine ways — the Form B, CRF balance and depreciation report matter more in a tiny strata, not less. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are at or over the threshold on the sources checked (the nine-unit community is over five; Oval Gardens' and Tiffany Estate II's counts are UNCONFIRMED but their multi-unit form is over five on any reading of the listings). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in this file carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the sources checked — if a directory prints one for any of them, it is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason and is flagged here in advance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ stock of any tenure in the pocket — every complex checked, including all three counted here, is general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's main stock, incl. the Tiffany Estates houses):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 8500) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. A co-op home (Granville Gardens) is neither strata nor fee-simple: there is no Form B and no individual title — diligence is the co-op's own membership, housing-charge and occupancy documents. https://www.granvillegardenshousingcoop.ca/
- **City zoning and plan:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs — houses in the pocket sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m², RS1/K 315.0 m² and RS1/G 2,000 m², and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use under Section 5.4 (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended, with City Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. The city-wide plan is the City of Richmond 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000. **ADEU charges are a West Cambie / Alexandra carrying cost only** — no ADEU charge applies to this pocket, and none should be priced into a Riverdale purchase. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Flood rules:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. The pocket also sits on the Sea Island / YVR side of Richmond — aircraft noise policy is set out in City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 and the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy Area; a street-by-street noise contour finding for Riverdale: UNVERIFIED. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Riverdale is in Richmond, and the 3% EHT is Vancouver only; Richmond also has no SnapStats coverage, so no combined "Condos & Townhomes" figure exists for this file. City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale inside the pocket is verified; where a presale is bought elsewhere in Richmond, the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and the 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21) govern, with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
