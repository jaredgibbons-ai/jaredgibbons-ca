## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all 3 cross-referenced strata complexes).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are over the threshold on their verified scale (Brighton 145 units, Windfall 54; Archstone Rockridge's count is UNCONFIRMED but the complex presents as multi-building strata). A sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line was found for the counted complexes — Brighton's building record instead records rentals as allowed, consistent with current law; any such line met elsewhere for a complex here is flagged VOID in-entry (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). No counted complex here is verified as 55+, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village in this scope, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata (§2.5).

**Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in this scope (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead.
