## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Hazelmere Village, the area's one counted complex).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For Hazelmere Village the Form B is also the document that settles the title-level tenure wording held UNCONFIRMED in §2.1 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Hazelmere Village is over the threshold on its complex scale — confirm on the Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and any listing or directory line restricting long-term rentals at Hazelmere Village is void on the rental part (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). The listing statement "no age restrictions" at Hazelmere Village is consistent with that framework but remains listing-stated — read the bylaws (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Hazelmere Village is strata, so its buyers get a Form B, monthly strata fees and strata bylaws. The surrounding acreage homes are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — but they carry the ALR rulebook below instead, which is the heavier diligence load in this area.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and ALR — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH caution for this ground:** Surrey's allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing (3 units under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; adopted July 8, 2024) must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** one per unit, not a separate strata lot; habitable floor area not exceeding **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap (research file §4, sibling passes; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **ALR housing frame (the ALC's published rules, governing any Hazelmere parcel in the ALR):** generally no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence of up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); a secondary suite only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it; local governments may further restrict through zoning (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
