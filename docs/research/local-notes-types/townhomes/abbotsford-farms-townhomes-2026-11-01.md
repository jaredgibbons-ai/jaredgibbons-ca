## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated for contrast — no strata purchase exists on this topic).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A farm purchase has NO strata and NO Form B; the diligence substitutes are title, ALC records, BC Assessment farm-class records and the applicable board's quota records (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Neither applies to a farm parcel.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — a strata rule with no application to a farm parcel, stated because this series carries the standard block in every file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No farm parcel carries a 55+ rule in any source read (§2.4).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. A farm parcel is fee-simple land, but its dwelling is a farm residence under the ALC frame below — not a townhouse.

**ALR, farm-class, municipal rules and taxes — per this slug's research, with standing conflicts flagged.**

- **ALR stratification bar (lead rule):** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"); see also SPA s.239 and ALCA s.21(1) (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (properties 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification (full three-tier form):** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line would be an ERROR — none is stated here (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Can I stratify an Abbotsford farm residence and sell the units as townhouses or condos?**
A: No — not on ALR land without ALC permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR under ALC Bulletin 09, s.6. That is why this file counts 0 townhouses (§§2, 3).

**Q: Are market statistics published for Abbotsford farms?**
A: No farm figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category. The September 2026 Abbotsford residential figures in §1 (Detached median $949,000; Condos & Townhomes median $497,500) are board context only, and farm counts, prices, quota prices and per-acre values are UNVERIFIED (research file §7).

**Q: What residence can an Abbotsford ALR farm have?**
A: One principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with local rules possibly more restrictive (research file §7; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: What income must a farm operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase (research file §7; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

**Q: Is quota included when I buy an Abbotsford farm?**

**Q: When are property taxes due on an Abbotsford farm?**
A: For a City of Abbotsford parcel: due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026 (research file §7; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice).
