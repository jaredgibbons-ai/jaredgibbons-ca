## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified standalone townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the strata rules that govern the townhouse units inside Kerrisdale's counted condo developments (and any strata product that verifies later), and the City of Vancouver rules that govern the neighbourhood's actual stock.

- **Form B diligence (any strata, including Sterling/McKinnon townhouse units):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A Kerrisdale townhouse unit inside a counted condo development is diligenced exactly like its apartment neighbours — same strata, same Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — directly relevant to Kerrisdale's new small multiplex stratas (e.g., a sub-5-lot half-duplex strata). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term-rental licensing rules apply on top in this city. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any Kerrisdale complex in §2 — no counted complex carries one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Kerrisdale building is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — Sterling and McKinnon were checked specifically in the sibling programs and are general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. That is the basis on which almost all of Kerrisdale's detached and half-duplex stock trades (fresh half-duplex listings state "no strata fees" expressly); do not import strata expectations into a fee-simple Kerrisdale purchase, or fee-simple expectations into a Sterling/McKinnon townhouse unit, which is strata.
- **City plan:** Kerrisdale has no separate adopted community plan; it falls under citywide policy. Citywide land-use direction now comes from the **Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026**. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **Multiplex zoning (the rule remaking Kerrisdale's lots):** By-law No. 13817 amended the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 to replace the old RS districts with the **R1-1 district**, allowing up to 6 strata units (or up to 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule and site-specific City confirmation; heritage-listed status should be checked first. This is the mechanism behind the new small strata product surfacing in §2 (half-duplex and multiplex stratas) — none of it is a townhouse complex, and each new small strata still sits under the Form B / depreciation-report frame above (sub-5-lot stratas under the depreciation-report threshold). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **City of Vancouver taxes:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a **5% penalty** applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City of Vancouver charges an Empty Homes Tax of **3% of the property's assessed taxable value** on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year; payment due April 16, 2026). This is a City of Vancouver tax on Vancouver properties — it does not apply in any other municipality in this program and is never carried into another city's file. A Kerrisdale home that will sit vacant or be held as a secondary property must budget for it. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kerrisdale.md)
