## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Apple Greene — the pocket's one strata complex, apartment-form):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The directory pet/rental lines in Section 2 are directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. The same diligence frame is what any future townhouse-strata purchase here would use; there is no townhouse strata for it to attach to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Apple Greene (NWS518, a multi-building 1973–1975 complex) is well over the threshold on either published unit figure. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. In a complex of this age, the depreciation report and the capital-project record are the central diligence items (see Section 5). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Apple Greene's directory "rentals allowed with restrictions" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason; its "pets not allowed" line is a pet bylaw and remains enforceable until the registered bylaws say otherwise. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in this pocket carries a verified age restriction — Apple Greene's resort-style amenity set (pools, clubhouse, guest suite, library) is a 1970s amenity package, not an age bylaw, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ housing of any tenure inside the pocket. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's detached stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 8500) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. There is no verified fee-simple townhouse complex in the pocket; the fee-simple product here is detached houses with suite potential (below). https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **City zoning / OCP (Richmond — standing program figures):** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 (the City of Richmond 2041 OCP, covering the area with Schedule 2 area and sub-area plans; no Boyd Park plan exists in that structure — the surrounding ground is planned as Seafair / West Richmond), Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. Houses around the park sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL also apply in the bylaw as amended. The zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones — Section 5.4 states a suite minimum of 33.0 m² and a maximum of the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m² — and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Verify the lot's subzone minimums in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1 before promising redevelopment value. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise, under the Flood Protection Management Strategy and the Dike Master Plans; the west dike beside this area carries the West Dyke Trail. New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under Flood Bylaw 8204. A street-by-street aircraft-noise contour finding for the Boyd Park area: UNVERIFIED (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05 sets the policy framework). https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The research file found no condos here — the board name found one.** The official record around the park is detached housing only, yet board and listing sources file the large Apple Greene cluster (six No. 1 Road addresses, NWS518) under "Boyd Park." A buyer searching only the park name will meet an apartment complex the City sources never mention — and a buyer searching only City sources will miss the pocket's entire strata stock. Basis: sibling condos file, §2/§5.
- **One complex, six addresses, two build years.** Apple Greene trades under 8720, 8740, 8760, 8840, 8860 and 8880 No. 1 Road with 1973 and 1975 build years by building — a single-complex search under one address understates the stock, and a single complex-wide year misstates every building but one phase. - **Apple Greene's levy history is the real due-diligence item, not an age rule.** A current listing for 8740 No. 1 Road in the sibling record states the building has completed major improvements and that a $25K special levy is fully paid, and an earlier listing described a full exterior redo — roof, rain screen, siding, balconies. In a 1973–1975 complex of this size, the capital-project record matters more than any restriction. Listing-stated — confirm against the strata documents. Basis: sibling 55-plus file, §5.
- **The park across the street is a full community campus.** Hugh Boyd Community Park holds a pitch and putt course, artificial turf fields, turf sports fields, basketball courts, tennis courts, a playground and paved walking paths, with West Richmond Community Centre (fitness centre, gymnasium, squash and badminton courts) and Hugh Boyd Secondary School on the same site — homes facing it get the fields and courts, and the sports lighting, weekend tournaments and community-centre traffic that come with them. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?ID=8 and http://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/wrichmond.htm
- **The name on the park is Richmond's first warden.** Hugh Boyd was the first Warden of the Corporation of the Township of Richmond, and the first council meetings were held in the dining room of his house on Sea Island after the Township was incorporated; the secondary school and the community park carry his name. http://richmondarchives.ca/tag/city-government/
