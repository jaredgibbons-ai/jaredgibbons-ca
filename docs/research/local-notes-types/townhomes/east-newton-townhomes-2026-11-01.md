## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all six counted East Newton complexes — all are strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, to be provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, any special levies approved but not yet due, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government, strata Forms information: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots are UNDER the threshold and exempt (BC Government, depreciation reports: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). All six East Newton complexes are well above five lots, so each should hold a current report — read it for the big-ticket renewals (roofs, siding, parkades where present).
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government, strata finances: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a strata can no longer restrict rentals, and any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" in an East Newton complex is legally void on the rental part and should be ignored in favour of the current bylaws (BC Government, strata rental rules: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate, municipal matter (below).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions added May 1, 2023 for caregivers and for spouses/partners and specified family members (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Charleston Green and the Chelsea Gardens townhouse section are 55+ complexes — a valid 55+ bylaw can still bind there; any older "19+" style restriction quoted from a directory is void.

**Fee-simple contrast.** None of East Newton's six counted complexes is fee-simple — every verified complex here is strata, so every buyer in this stock gets a Form B, monthly strata fees and strata bylaws. A true fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse would have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association — and a buyer comparing East Newton stock against a fee-simple complex elsewhere in Surrey should not assume the same diligence package exists (Strata Property Act scope: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_01).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, suites and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH).**

- Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan).
- Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; townhouse complexes sit in the City's multiple-residential zones and their strata bylaws, not the zoning bylaw, govern day-to-day use inside a complex (City of Surrey, Zoning Bylaw 12000: https://www.surrey.ca/bylaws-services/bylaws).
- Secondary suites in Surrey are capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area (City of Surrey, secondary suites: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites). A suite inside a strata townhouse additionally needs the strata's written permission where its bylaws require it — the Form B and bylaws govern.
- SSMUH: Surrey implemented the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules on July 8, 2024 — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (City of Surrey, SSMUH: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing). SSMUH applies to single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex — a strata lot owner cannot add units to a strata lot.
- Short-term rentals: Surrey permits short-term rentals only in the operator's own principal residence, with a City business licence at $358/year as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries the licence page's $358 figure and directs the reader to the licence page for the current amount (City of Surrey, short-term rentals: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals). Inside a strata complex, the strata's own bylaws on short-term accommodation also apply.
- Property tax timing: Surrey property taxes are due July 2; a 5% penalty applies to amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey, property taxes: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, to a maximum rebate of $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). It applies to NEW housing only — a resale East Newton townhouse does not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate (GST/HST).** Separate from the first-time buyer rebate: a 36% rebate of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 of value (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax (PTT).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion of a property valued above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000).
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time home buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 of value where the fair market value is $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption up to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP withdrawal limit is $60,000 from RRSPs (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); the FHSA allows $40,000 of contributions at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC mortgage default insurance premiums for high-ratio purchases run from 0.6% to 4.5% of the mortgage amount depending on the loan-to-value ratio (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research file documents no CMHC premium error on an East Newton page.
- **Presales.** No presale townhouse project is verified in East Newton in this pass, so the REDMA rules are stated for completeness only where a presale is later found: a buyer of a presale unit has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Is there really no "East Newton Elementary"?**

**Q: Which East Newton townhouse complexes are strata, and are any fee-simple?**
A: All six verified complexes are strata — Chimney Ridge, The Summit, Newton Park I, Charleston Green and the Chelsea Gardens townhouse section (counted in the guide townhomes file) plus Wedgewood Estates at 13880 74 Ave, whose listing records "Title To Land: Freehold Strata" (https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3103853-13880-74-avenue-120-surrey-bc-v3w-7e6/brochure). No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in East Newton.

**Q: What does the Form B tell me before I buy?**
A: The monthly strata fee, anything the seller owes the strata, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance details, the strata's rules, its budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the depreciation report attached — provided within seven days of request for a capped fee of $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).

**Q: Can a strata here stop me renting out my townhouse?**
A: No — rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since November 24, 2022 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are different: Surrey allows them only in your own principal residence with a City licence (https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals), and strata bylaws on short-term accommodation still apply.

**Q: Charleston Green and Chelsea Gardens are 55+ — is that bylaw still valid?**
A: Yes. Since November 24, 2022 a 55+ bylaw is the ONLY age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with May 1, 2023 exemptions for caregivers and specified family members. Any older "19+" restriction quoted from a directory is void.

**Q: What is Sullivan Mews, and why isn't it in the count?**
A: Sullivan Mews (15020 66A Ave) is a membership community, not a strata: there is no strata plan and no individual title, purchases are cash-only, and a monthly membership fee covers taxes, heat, electricity, management, insurance and water (documented in `~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-newton-55-plus-2026-11-01.md`). Without title or a strata plan it is not townhouse stock in the strata or fee-simple sense, so it is documented but counted nowhere.

**Q: How much property transfer tax would I pay on an $800,000 resale townhouse?**
A: $14,000 with no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 plus 2% on the remaining $600,000 (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). A qualifying first-time buyer gets a full exemption on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000, phasing out at $860,000.

**Q: Does the new first-time buyer GST rebate help me here?**
A: Only on NEW housing — it rebates 100% of GST up to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, under Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). East Newton's verified stock is resale, which does not attract GST.

**Q: How is the East Newton market doing?**
A: SnapStats reports Surrey only as Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined — there is no townhome-only figure, and sub-area medians are NOT published (community medians only). For September 2026 the research file records East Newton at 120 active / 7 sold / 6% sales ratio detached, and 132 active / 11 sold / 8% attached, against Surrey community medians of $1,215,000 detached and $512,944 attached ($491/sq ft, 99% sale-to-list, 25 days on market) (research file §6, SnapStats September 2026 Fraser Valley report).

**Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to my townhouse lot?**
A: Not inside a strata complex — Surrey's SSMUH allowances (3–4 units, up to 6 near frequent transit; July 8, 2024) apply to single-family lots, not strata lots, and a secondary suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area and still needs strata permission where the bylaws require it (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites).
