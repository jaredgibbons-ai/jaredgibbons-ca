## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Huntsfield Green and any City strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Longlea's 58 lots and Huntsfield Green's strata both sit over the depreciation threshold below (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — long-term rentals are allowed anywhere in BC; the Parkway buildings' directory "rentals not allowed" lines are flagged void in-entry in §2.4. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the City's own STR rule is UNVERIFIED in this program, so no Airbnb claim is made from City rules (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation; research file §4).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a bylaw set below 55 does NOT convert to 55+). Huntsfield Green and Glenmont Park carry the City's verified 55+ positions (§2) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. None is verified inside the City (§2.3).

**City of Langley zoning, SSMUH, suites and taxes.**

- **Zoning / OCP:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 — cited by number, as required; **its adoption date is conflicted in the sources and is UNVERIFIED here.** The City's Official Community Plan was adopted November 22, 2021 (research file §4).
- **SSMUH:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. A first-page card's "fourplex potential / R1 zoning permits up to four units" wording is listing text only, to be checked against Bylaw 3284 for the specific lot (research file §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284).
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a secondary suite must not exceed the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot (s. 2.4); at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. A carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m² (research file §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-2025-no-3300pdf-2).
- **Property taxes:** City of Langley payments received after the July 2, 2026 due date incur a penalty; the City's payment page lists City Hall at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee on credit-card payments. **The penalty percentage for 2026 is UNVERIFIED** — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate (research file §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes).
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program (research file §4).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug carries March 13 and flags the March 12 secondary date as superseded): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
