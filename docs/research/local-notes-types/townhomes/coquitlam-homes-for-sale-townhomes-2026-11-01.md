## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the 14 counted strata complexes).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Era matters in this city: the 1980s–90s stock (Coquitlam East's Atlin Place pair, the Lansdowne pair) is in the roof/envelope cycle, while the Burke Mountain stock is in warranty/early-life cycles — the depreciation report reads very differently across the two (condos city sibling §3) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold — the 1135 Lansdowne complex's 16 units as described in one listing would sit OVER it; confirm lot counts on each Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a §2.1 complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals). Coquitlam STR rules are UNVERIFIED in this research — no City STR bylaw/licence/principal-residence rule was read on an official City page (research file §4).
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws). In the condos roster only Princess Gate (Canyon Springs) is published as 55+ — an apartment condo, not a §2.1 townhouse; confirm any age bylaw on the Form B. The 55-plus sibling for this slug (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/coquitlam-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`) consolidates the city's 55+ roster from nine sub-area files.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The Roxton Avenue cluster (Burke Mountain) is the set's one fee-simple entry: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only (§2.1).

**Coquitlam zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000 (1996)** (standing correction). **Plan: Interim OCP adopted April 27, 2026 (Bylaws 5511/5512), superseding OCP No. 3479** — a streamlined consolidation (1,000+ pages to 55 designations-to-18 / 60+ maps-to-19 in the research file's account) incorporating transit-oriented areas, SSMUH and housing-needs requirements, with a comprehensive review anticipated to begin in 2027 (research file §4: https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1932). Cite the Interim OCP, not a superseded area plan.
- **SSMUH / small-scale residential.** Up to four dwelling units in low-density neighbourhoods city-wide; zones R-1 through R-4 (suites, laneway homes, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes), with R-1/R-2 caps of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger (research file §4: https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId). **Suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED** — the City guide read states neither; verify a specific suite's permits with the City before writing "legal suite" (research file §§4, 6).
- **Watercourse protection.** Northeast Coquitlam's Watercourse Protection DPA reaches 50 m from top of bank (30 m elsewhere in Coquitlam) — lot-level status UNVERIFIED per lot (Burke Mountain count home §3: https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits).
- **Coquitlam property taxes.** Due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants (research file §4: https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes).

GST applies to new construction (Versant, Rocklin presales), not resales.
