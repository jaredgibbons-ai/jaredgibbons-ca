# Salmon River, Langley — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Salmon River is a Township of Langley rural district along the Salmon River in the township's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley; "Salmon River" is also the Fraser Valley board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR report. No Township-published boundary for the sub-area was confirmed. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The district follows the Salmon River valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue, with Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side; main roads are 56 Avenue and Rawlison Crescent on the upland, 232 Street and 240 Street as the north–south edges, and River Road / Glover Road toward Fort Langley, with Highway 1 access south via the 200 Street or 232 Street interchanges. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Transit: TransLink route 562 (Walnut Grove / Langley Centre) runs on Glover Road at the Fort Langley edge of the district; no TransLink route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads, and there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Schools: SD35 catchments are address-based. For the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 School Locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown; Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, 4452 256 Street, is a district school of choice on the upland east side, its position inside this board sub-area not confirmed. No SD35 school physically inside the Salmon River valley itself was confirmed. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/coghlan/welcome-to-coghlan-fundamental-elementary.18027
- Flood: the valley is Fraser River floodplain land — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, has flood box gates that close as river levels rise, and is in detailed design and tendering for upgrades Council authorized. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Market stats (SnapStats covers Langley): Salmon River sub-area, as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report — Detached: inventory 22, solds 5, sales ratio 23%; **Condos & Townhomes COMBINED**: inventory 4, solds 0, sales ratio not published. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The governing fact for this sub-area: there is no townhouse strata stock to apply strata diligence to.** Most Salmon River properties are non-strata freehold acreages; where a strata parcel exists, BC strata law is the Strata Property Act and strata disputes go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. A buyer told a valley property is a "townhouse" should verify title form first — on the verified record here, it will be a freehold acreage house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **ALR stratification bar:** an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places — confirm a specific parcel on the ALC maps — and that bar is the structural reason townhouse strata do not appear on ALR valley land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **ALC residence caps:** principal residence at most 500 m²; suite within the principal residence only; one additional residence at most 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and at most 186 m² over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. **Farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Township of Langley zoning and SSMUH:** rural land is under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (farm and acreage homes sit in the rural zones, including RU-2; the RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage were not confirmed from a readable bylaw section — UNCONFIRMED). SSMUH is Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township (a page claiming one is WRONG; no such claim is documented for this sub-area in its research files, so no defect is recorded); the Township states that on lots larger than 4,050 m² its rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning; the suite cap is 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, never strata; R-CL s.403 sets 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum. The Township Official Community Plan and the Rural Plan govern the district. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **Strata standard block (applies only if a genuine strata parcel is ever verified here):** Form B diligence (Strata Property Act s.59 — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for 5+ lot stratas since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% of the operating budget since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (flag any directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in this file currently triggers this block. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Fee-simple position (the actual stock):** a Salmon River acreage carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal records, and the rural systems below: wells (search GWELLS, the provincial well/aquifer database; well reports are due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well) and septic (Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair, and a letter of certification after). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Salmon River is a salmon-bearing watercourse, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit building envelopes near the river — parcel-level setback distances for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED; check with the Township before planning a build. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- **Buyer frame (every purchase in this file):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the first-time buyer exemption (first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) — confirm the farm/ALR position for a specific parcel; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. REDMA s.21 7-day rescission and s.18 trust deposits apply where a presale exists — no presale is verified in this sub-area. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses in Salmon River?** None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes in the research pass or either sibling condos file. The stock is freehold acreage houses; the nearest townhouse stock is in Fort Langley, Walnut Grove and Willoughby. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Q: SnapStats shows 4 attached listings — aren't those townhouses?** The 4 are a combined "Condos & Townhomes" inventory figure for the sub-area (September 2026, 0 sold), not four named townhouse complexes — no townhome project was named on the Township's development applications route. (Research file §2)
- **Q: Can I stratify a big farmhouse into townhouse units?** Not on ALR land: an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and subdivision of ALR land needs Commission permission. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can an acreage add a second dwelling?** On ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an application at up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), subject to Township bylaws; the Township's SSMUH rules also allow a suite or accessory unit on lots over 4,050 m² depending on zoning, and a coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never a strata townhouse. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Is the valley in a floodplain?** Yes — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise and was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet; upgrades are in detailed design and tendering. Flood status drives insurance and lending on valley-floor parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River acreage?** For the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary — Ministry-listed names as carried in the research file — and other addresses may fall in different catchments. Run the exact address; no home is promised a specific school. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: What replaces strata diligence on an acreage purchase?** The well and septic paper trails: the GWELLS record for the parcel (well reports due within 90 days of work on a well) and Fraser Health's authorized-person filings and letter of certification for the onsite sewerage system — plus title, ALR status and flood checks. There is no Form B because there is no strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- **Q: Is there a bus into the valley?** No route inside the valley roads was confirmed — route 562 runs on Glover Road at the Fort Langley edge between Langley Centre and Walnut Grove / Carvolth Exchange. A Salmon River acreage is a car-dependent purchase on the transit evidence here. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- **Q: The guide page showed a condo — is there condo stock after all?** That card is the documented feed defect in Section 5: a Langley City unit at Cornerstone North by Marcon labelled "Salmon River" in the guide feed. Its own listing text places it in Langley City — it is not Salmon River stock. https://jaredgibbons.ca/salmon-river-guide.html
- **Q: Where is the hospital for this area?** Langley Memorial Hospital, 22051 Fraser Highway, Langley (24/7 emergency), southwest of the district; the Langley Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 202 – 20434 64th Avenue, Langley. No hospital or clinic inside the district was confirmed. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Langley/langley-memorial-hospital
