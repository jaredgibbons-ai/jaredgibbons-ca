## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has **no application** to the fee-simple acreages and pad-rent manufactured homes that make up this community's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to; it is the central document for the cross-referenced urban Mission strata in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, no deferral by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Stave Falls directory line is carried in §2, so no in-entry void flag arises here; the rule is stated because it governs the cross-referenced Mission strata (the assembly file flags Cherry Lane's directory "rentals not allowed" line VOID in-entry). Park age/rent rules in a manufactured-home park are a park-tenancy matter under the park's own documents, not a strata bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in Stave Falls is verified as 55+ — the 55-plus sibling verified none here, and Ruskin Place is expressly NOT 55+ on its own listing text. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple home carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020) plus any registered covenants govern instead. That is the governing frame for Stave Falls' actual stock: fee-simple acreage houses and lots, plus park tenancies for the Wilson Street parks — two different purchases that a feed label can blur.
