## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no townhouse complex to attach to in this sub-area (§2), but it is the central document for the strata product that does exist here — the three apartment buildings and Cottage Grove's ranchers. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — no such strata is verified here. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Vibe's directory line reads "rentals allowed," consistent with current law; no counted entry in this file carries a "rentals not allowed" line, and any older directory or bylaw text stating one for a Proper South strata is void on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Elmwood Terrace is presented as 55+ in its listings and Cottage Grove is verified 55+ in the 55-plus program — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for either before treating a unit as generally occupiable; Hazelwood Grove's age position is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Chilliwack Proper South (§2) — most of this sub-area's detached stock is fee-simple housing, which is a different statement from a fee-simple townhouse complex.
- **City plan:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500; its **Schedule B2** Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan covers this ground. The research file records that the live page attributes policies to a "Chilliwack 2050" plan whose name and application were not confirmed on a City page in the research pass — the position is settled here from the City's OCP page and the sibling Proper files: Bylaw 5500 is current and Schedule B2 covers the Proper ground. **Standing schedule correction:** the Sardis plan is **Schedule B5** and the Agricultural Plan is **Schedule B1** of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024." This slug's own research does NOT document that stale citation on the chilliwack-proper-south page, so it is recorded as the standing correction only, not asserted as a defect of this page. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning, SSMUH and suites:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs; its structure includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone in Section 12. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted **June 18, 2024**, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. A secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space, inside a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit; only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite), garden suites capped at 100 m², ADU parking under s. 5.04. The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone; a suite must be built with a building permit, meet the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a **5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026** — the City's own two-stage calendar. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $550,000 resale strata unit with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $350,000 ($7,000) = **$9,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug: this file's own research documents none, so the known 2.80%–4.00% page error is NOT asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21).
