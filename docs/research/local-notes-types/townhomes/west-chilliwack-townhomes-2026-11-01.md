## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the rules that would govern a townhouse purchase here if stock existed or is built, and the municipal/ALR rules that explain why the stock does not exist in this sub-area. City rules are cited once per city in this program and carried per file.

- **If it is strata: Form B first.** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. That is the diligence track for Creekside Estates (the sub-area's one strata building) and for any townhouse strata ever built here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with 30-year projections and three CRF cash-flow models, non-deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid, so any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Chilliwack strata is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Cottonwood Village's 55+ rule is NOT a strata bylaw — it is a manufactured-home park rule under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act — one more reason it is not townhouse stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **If it is fee-simple: no strata at all.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (zoning, OCP, floodplain bylaw) plus any registered covenants govern instead. West Chilliwack's rural homes are fee-simple in exactly this sense, but they are detached farm and acreage homes, not townhouse complexes (Section 2).
- **Chilliwack zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** The AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone that frames most of this sub-area sets a minimum lot area of 7.5 ha for land excluded/exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for an ALC-approved homesite severance, with a 25 m minimum lot width — which is why townhouse development is not the form of this area. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Chilliwack OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500.** The Agricultural Area Plan is its Schedule B1 and covers this sub-area; the Sardis Neighbourhood Plan is Schedule B5. Any live page stating "Schedule D" or citing OCP Bylaw 5024 for this ground is stale — flag it as a Jerry sweep item; the current citations are Bylaw 5500, Schedule B1 (Agricultural) and Schedule B5 (Sardis). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024)** brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into Chilliwack's zoning. The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not West Chilliwack, and ALR land is in any case governed by the ALR rules below, not by SSMUH density. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **ALR:** parcel-level ALR status is per address. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — a strata townhouse complex cannot be created out of ALR farm residences. Farm-class qualification is three-tiered ($2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Floodplain rules:** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder systems and levies additional taxes for drainage and dyking on top of municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Chilliwack tax dates:** property taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applied to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST on new homes only (resales are not GST-taxable). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted). Worked tier example, on a $700,000 purchase elsewhere in Chilliwack: $200,000 × 1% = $2,000, plus $500,000 × 2% = $10,000, total $12,000 before any exemption. First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in this sub-area was confirmed on a developer's own site: UNVERIFIED. Where a presale does exist elsewhere in Chilliwack, REDMA requires a disclosure statement (s. 18, deposits in trust) and gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging it to rescind (s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per person; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC defect is asserted for this area — this file's research documents none. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Board and stats:** Chilliwack is CADREB territory, not FVREB, and NO SnapStats figures exist for Chilliwack — none are cited in this file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-chilliwack.md)
