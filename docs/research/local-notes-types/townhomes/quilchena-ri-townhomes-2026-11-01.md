## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

*No completed townhouse complex is verified in this pocket, so this section states the BC strata framework that will govern Quilchena West on completion (and governs Innova30 today), plus the Richmond rules that govern the pocket itself.*

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally, and Quilchena West resales later):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A presale purchase itself runs on the developer's disclosure statement instead (REDMA, below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — a 110-home Quilchena West strata and a 30-home Innova30 strata are both well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a pocket complex exists to flag, because no completed complex is verified here; any such line in Quilchena West's eventual directory entries will be void for long-term rentals for the same reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). This is the provision that decides the Quilchena Green label conflict flagged in Section 2: whatever its historical label (45+ in one source, 55+ in another — flagged, not resolved), no age bylaw below 55 can be enforced today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's actual stock):** the pocket's houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 8500) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in the RS1/RS2 zones.
- **Richmond zoning and plans:** Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs — Single Detached (RS1/RS2), Section 8.1, is the common single-family zoning in this pocket, with minimum lot areas by subzone including 360 m² (RS1/B, RS2/B), 550 m² (RS1/E), 828 m² (RS1/F) and 2,000 m² (RS1/G), and a secondary suite as a permitted secondary use. The city-wide plan is Richmond's 2041 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000, with area and sub-area plans held in Schedule 2 under Bylaws 7100 and 9000; the Laurelwood Sub-Area Plan is the City sub-area plan for the Granville Avenue / Railway Avenue bend. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=24187
- **SSMUH / suites:** the City states at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit such as a coach house is allowed per lot on covered lots, with Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (RSM) zoning applied under Bill 44 and provincial minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on larger lots — the realistic "multi-unit" route in this pocket today is a house lot, not a townhouse complex. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes designed to handle a 1:500 flooding event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies — a live diligence item for a Quilchena West presale contract and for any house purchase in the pocket. https://www.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Richmond money dates:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. Annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date; the City's discounted cost for a typical single-family metered residence for 2026 totals $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental programs $443, flood protection $342). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/quilchena-ri.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction — directly relevant to a Quilchena West presale — not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA) — Quilchena West:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until the Quilchena West disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
