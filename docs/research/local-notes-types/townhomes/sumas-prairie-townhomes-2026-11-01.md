## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**No verified townhouse stock exists in Sumas Prairie for the strata block to attach to. The block is stated in full so the standard is on record; the Abbotsford/ALR frame below it is what actually governs this ground.**

**The strata block (BC standard — no Sumas Prairie complex is currently verified under it).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: Form B within seven days of request, capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page — strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). Prairie farm and acreage purchases are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants on title only, stated plainly.
- **Depreciation reports / CRF.** Five-or-more-lot stratas: depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024 (sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since that date (SPA ss. 123.1–123.2, Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **PTT.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" summary compresses 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears; it is not a flat 5% (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: $800,000 with no exemption = $14,000. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on the first $500,000 to $835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.

- **CMHC.** Premiums 0.6%–4.5% by loan-to-value (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums).
