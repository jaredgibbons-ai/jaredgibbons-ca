## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all 4 cross-referenced strata complexes).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in the count home are listing/directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — Coho II's directory line is flagged VOID for long-term rentals in §2.1 for exactly this reason. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer** — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw; the provincial voiding does not create a short-term rental right here (research file §4; https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699, read via search result 2026-11-01).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). No cross-referenced complex carries a verified age restriction, and the 55-plus sibling verified no 55+ housing of any tenure in Mid Meadows (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).

**Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Mid Meadows (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so the four strata complexes are never sold with the wrong document set.

**City plan, zoning, suites, flood and taxes (City of Pitt Meadows).**

- Plan and zoning: the governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, covering the whole city including the Bonson Road area; no neighbourhood or area plan specific to the Bonson Road area was found in official sources; it repealed the former OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007. Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs — RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha; houses sit under RS (minimum lot area 0.2 ha), R-3 (up to 3 units per lot), R-4 (up to 4 units) and R-6 (up to five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements on lots close to frequent transit), each with a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 sq m — a subdivision minimum only, per the City's SSMUH implementation update.
