## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to The Pillars' townhouse component and every strata complex in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For a mixed complex like The Pillars, the Form B is also where the strata's structure across its apartment and townhouse components is confirmed (count-home file §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. No complex in §2 is verified as sub-5-lot — Windgrove's published count is 12, and The Pillars' component count is UNCONFIRMED, so its threshold position is read off the strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §2.2: Heron Cove's directory "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). No Beach Grove complex in §2 is verified as 55+ (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Beach Grove (§2); the Tsawwassen assembly's one fee-simple entry, High Street Rowhomes, is Southlands ground counted in its home file (§2.1).
