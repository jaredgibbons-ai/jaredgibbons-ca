## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata townhomes — Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s.59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Both verified complexes in this file are strata, so this document set is the diligence core for a Scottsdale townhouse purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The sibling condos file flags the trap in-entry for this sub-area: an older Chancellor Place listing stating "No Rentals, No Pets & 19+ Age Restriction" is dated listing text, not current law — the rental line is void and a 19+ threshold is not a permitted age bylaw. The same reading applies to any directory "rentals not allowed" line for Briarwood or Oakcrest. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for either townhouse complex in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Scottsdale. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. On duplexes specifically, the Province confirms a duplex can be strata or non-strata — establish the title form for a specific property through BC Land Title / BC Assessment before applying either document set. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Delta plan:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, applies — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules (the former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; any source attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong). OCP Map 2 shows Small Scale Residential and Mixed Residential designations in and around this area, and the 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research file cites Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 and its ladder (RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² subdivision minimums; there is no RD5 zone). The program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD1.pdf
- **SSMUH and transit-oriented density:** Delta's SSMUH bylaws are Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta, and Delta's SSMUH regulations allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites with reduced parking. Separately, the City states Bill 47 requires minimum densities within a Transit Oriented Development Area extending 400 metres from Scottsdale Exchange — two similar lots can sit under different density expectations on either side of that line. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Flood / ALR:** no floodplain statement specific to Scottsdale was confirmed in the sources read, and OCP Map 2 shows no Agricultural designation for this part of North Delta; parcel-level ALR status is a check through the Agricultural Land Commission. Where ALR land is involved anywhere in Delta, an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Delta property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services, and Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- **Buyer frame (every purchase in this file):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the first-time buyer exemption (first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) — directly relevant to a 2022-built Oakcrest resale position only as a lawyer/notary confirms it for that transaction; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. - **Q: Is Oakcrest a fee-simple complex because it is new?** No — citable listing records state Ownership Type: Freehold Strata for Oakcrest units, built 2022. New does not mean fee-simple; the Form B document set applies. https://www.estateblock.com/delta/30-11556-72a-ave-116781
- **Q: Can I rent out a Scottsdale townhouse?** A strata residential rental-restriction bylaw has been void since Nov 24, 2022, so a directory or old listing "no rentals" line does not bind you — but short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the registered bylaws on the Form B are the document to read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Nicholson Green a townhouse complex in Scottsdale?** Not verified as either: listings classify 6866 Nicholson Road as Sunshine Hills Woods, and units there are recorded in both Row / Townhouse and Apartment/Condo sub-types. It is carried in this file as a documented conflict, not as Scottsdale townhouse stock. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3119168-416-6866-nicholson-road-delta-british-columbia-v4e3m6-879900/brochure
- **Q: What does the $622,500 North Delta median tell me about townhouse prices?** Only the combined picture: SnapStats publishes "Condos & Townhomes" together at community level (North Delta, September 2026: median $622,500), and Scottsdale's own combined figures are inventory 24 / solds 2 / 8% ratio with no sub-area median published. There is no townhome-only figure. (Research file §2)
- **Q: Which schools serve a townhouse near Scott Road and 72nd?** Address-dependent: the locator result for the sample address 7031 120 St is Heath Elementary and Seaquam Secondary (SD37, no middle tier). Run the complex's exact address in the locator before promising a catchment. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Does the TOD area change what my lot can become?** Potentially: the City states Bill 47 minimum densities apply within 400 metres of Scottsdale Exchange, and the 2024 OCP designates a Scottsdale Exchange TOD Area — confirm which side of the TOD line a property is on before pricing development potential. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is a Scottsdale duplex always a strata?** No — the Province lists duplexes as a form strata housing can take, which means a duplex may be strata-titled or not; check the title and strata plan for the specific property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Q: Where is the exchange, exactly — Delta or Surrey?** On the Surrey side of Scott Road, per the City — which matters for any "steps to the exchange" claim made for a Delta-side townhouse. http://www.delta.ca/node/1838
