## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no verified strata townhouse stock in Websters Corners today; this block is the standard BC frame a buyer needs if strata product ever appears here, and for the strata townhouse purchases buyers from this area most often make in neighbouring Thornhill (Section 2, cross-reference).

- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Form B exists for a Websters Corners acreage house — if a listing here claims strata documents, that is a classification error to resolve before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold (no strata of any size is verified in this sub-area). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a strata property is flagged VOID in-entry in this program. For Websters Corners itself the operative rental rule is different: a secondary suite in an acreage house is governed by the zoning and ALR rules below, not by strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No strata exists in this sub-area to carry such a bylaw, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Websters Corners' houses are fee-simple in exactly this sense, which is why the diligence that applies here is title, zoning, ALR, well and septic — not strata documents.
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge **Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** governs, with the rural minimums in Section 1 in full three-tier farm form — A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, with RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha (a flat "$2,500+" farm figure would be an error — the tiers govern). **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, resolved nowhere** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024).
