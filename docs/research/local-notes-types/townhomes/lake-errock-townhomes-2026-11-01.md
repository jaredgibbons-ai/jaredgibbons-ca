## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section still matters with zero townhouse stock:** a Lake Errock buyer comparing a Morris Valley strata lot (Tapadera, Eagle Point, River Reach) against a city townhouse elsewhere needs both halves — the strata rules that DO apply to those bare-land and single-family stratas, and the fee-simple reality that governs most of the community. Both are stated below, kept separate.
- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase here — Tapadera LMS648, Eagle Point, River Reach):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a bare-land strata the Form B is also where lot-use rules (RV / park model / manufactured home permissions) are proved — listing text is not proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Tapadera and the Morris Valley estate stratas are over the five-lot threshold on their community scale — confirm per corporation on the Form B; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the named stratas here is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Tapadera's listing line "rentals allowed" needs no void flag — it permits rather than restricts — but any older bylaw document for any strata here that restricts long-term rentals is void on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No named strata here is verified as 55+ — Tapadera expressly states no age restrictions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** Most Lake Errock homes are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; FVRD plan rules plus any registered covenants govern instead. A buyer must not import townhouse expectations (strata insurance, depreciation reports, Form B package) into a fee-simple lake purchase — and must not assume a Morris Valley "estate" address is fee-simple either: Eagle Point and River Reach carry strata fees on their listing records. Title decides, every time.
- **Local plan — FVRD, not a city:** the governing plan is the FVRD Electoral Area C Official Community Plan, **Bylaw 1747, 2024 (adopted September 18, 2025)**, including the Lake Errock Neighbourhood Plan; it replaces OCP Bylaw No. 0020, 1998. **No FVRD zoning bylaw number is stated in this file: UNVERIFIED — no FVRD zoning bylaw text was opened in this pass, and no neighbouring municipality's bylaw number (Mission's, Chilliwack's or Kent's) is borrowed.** Tapadera's own development guide refers to a Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 for Holiday Park requirements — a guide's reference, not the bylaw text, carried here as UNVERIFIED for zoning purposes. A specific lot's designation, permit areas and permitted dwellings: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the FVRD for the PID. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp
- **SSMU / suites in FVRD ground:** no FVRD SSMUH or secondary-suite rule for Lake Errock was confirmed on an FVRD source opened in this pass: UNVERIFIED. Feed cards claiming "R-2 zoning allowing a coach house" (Malcolm Road building lots) are listing text only and are not adopted. Do not apply Surrey's, Mission's or any city's SSMUH tiers here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/lake-errock.md)
- **ALR frame (applies parcel by parcel):** any Lake Errock-area parcel in the Agricultural Land Reserve follows the ALC housing frame — one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). **ALR stratification bar:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — a farm property cannot be converted into townhouse strata lots. Whether a specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — one feed card claims a lot is "Out of the ALR" in listing text, which is not verification. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/lake-errock.md)
- **BC Assessment farm thresholds (full three-tier form, where farm class is claimed):** $2,500 of farm income for parcels of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha for larger parcels; $10,000 for parcels under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. (Standing program correction, Nov 1, 2026.)
- **RURAL property tax regime — explicitly different from Mission's:** Lake Errock is unincorporated, so the owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or any city. Under the **Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11**, rural property taxes are due **July 2**, a **5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on October 31**. Do NOT apply the City of Mission's flat 10% penalty or any city's staged dates here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $600,000 resale lake home with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $400,000 ($8,000) = **$10,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted for any property here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). No presale project in Lake Errock was verified in this pass; any future presale is counted separately from completed stock, never mixed. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
