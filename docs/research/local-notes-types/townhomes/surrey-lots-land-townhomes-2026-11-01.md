### The strata form that does apply to land — bare-land strata (documented as its own form, NOT counted)

- **Bare-land strata:** the Strata Property Act defines a "bare land strata plan" as a strata plan on which the boundaries of the strata lots are defined on a horizontal plane by reference to survey markers and not by reference to the floors, walls or ceilings of a building. In a bare-land strata, the strata lot is essentially a plot of land: the plan is filed before any homes are constructed, the lot owner builds (or contracts to build) the home on the lot, and the common property can include roads, streetlights, a septic system, a clubhouse, playground and forested areas. You cannot tell by looking whether detached homes sit on a bare-land strata — the words "bare land strata" appear on the first page of the strata plan. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol27/consol27/98043_01 and https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/
- **What that means for a Surrey lot buyer:** if a lot is a bare-land strata lot, there IS a strata corporation — with bylaws, strata fees for the common property, and the Section 3 strata rules — even though there is no building. If the lot is freehold, none of that applies. The research file records that bare-land strata lots exist in newer Surrey communities, but no Surrey-wide freehold versus bare-land strata split for land is published on an official source: UNVERIFIED, and no named bare-land strata complex is listed or counted for this topic. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The townhome-relevant frame on land is buildability, stated plainly:** a buyer who wants townhouse-form product on a Surrey lot is buying zoning, servicing and approvals — Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, which divides the city into zones and regulates use, density, siting and subdivision — not an existing complex. No strata, no Form B and no strata fees attach to a freehold lot itself. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Bare-land strata exception — Form B diligence:** where a lot IS a bare-land strata lot, a resale purchase still runs through the strata: Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a freehold lot there is no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports (bare-land stratas included):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold. A bare-land strata's report covers the common property (roads, systems, amenities), not the houses on the lots. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (bare-land stratas):** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — any "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Both rules apply to a bare-land strata exactly as to a building strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple lots — stated plainly:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Land-use designations come from the OCP and its secondary plans (Neighbourhood Concept Plans and town centre plans), confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **SSMUH — added units are not subdivision:** Surrey's framework (in force **July 8, 2024**; 20 single-family and duplex zones consolidated into 9: RA, R1, R2, R2-O, R3, R4, R5, R5-S, R6) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Added-unit permission does not create new lots and does not waive the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000; a complete Surrey zone-minimum table is UNVERIFIED in this file (individual zone minimums exist in the program's master research — for example R3 at a 660 m² minimum subdivision lot area in the South Newton plan area). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Secondary suites on urban lots:** Surrey permits only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR — residences cannot be stratified, and subdivision needs Commission permission:** ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 (with Strata Property Act s. 239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) states the frame: subdivision of Agricultural Land Reserve land requires permission from the Agricultural Land Commission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission; a parcel's reserve status is checked on the Commission's maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **ALR housing rules and the farm thresholds, in full three-tier form:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or smaller or 186 m² on larger parcels, where Surrey bylaws also permit it. Farm-class gross annual value thresholds (Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95): **$2,500** if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; **$2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation in excess of 4 ha** if it is greater than 4 ha; **$10,000** if it is less than 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" line, where a page states one, is an ERROR against this tiered structure and is not used in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://assessmentappeal.bc.ca/other-guides-resources/help-your-with-appeal/farm-class-guide/
- **Environment and servicing on lots:** Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands; South Newton plan material names Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries, so riparian rules can reach parcels a buyer would not describe as creek-side. The provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; a home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, directly relevant to closing adjustments on a land purchase. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT general rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2% structure, flagged as a compression, not a single rate. First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 (a vacant lot's eligibility is a PTT-return question: UNCONFIRMED here for bare land). Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, for a qualifying newly built home. GST on a vacant-lot purchase depends on the seller and the lot's history — the live page's blanket GST summary is UNVERIFIED in the research file; confirm per transaction with a CPA. For a newly built home on the lot: 5% GST applies; FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year (whether a bare lot alone qualifies is a program-conditions question — do not assume it); CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% for insured home purchases (vacant-land financing is lender-specific and that schedule is not a land-loan rule). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer markets strata lots (including bare-land strata lots), the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouse complexes in this file?** None — count 0, stated plainly. This is a lots-and-land topic; a townhouse is a built home, not a lot. Surrey townhouse complexes live in the Surrey area townhomes files in this folder. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-lots-land.md)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in plain terms?** A plot of land that is a strata lot: the Strata Property Act defines a bare land strata plan by lot boundaries drawn on a horizontal plane by survey markers, not by a building's floors, walls or ceilings. You own the lot, you build the house, and a strata corporation owns the common property (roads, systems, amenities) and charges strata fees. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol27/consol27/98043_01
- **Q: How do I tell if a Surrey lot is bare-land strata or freehold?** Not by looking — detached homes on a bare-land strata look like any detached homes. The words "bare land strata" appear on the first page of the strata plan at the Land Title Office; if the homes are drawn on the plan, it is a building strata instead. https://visoa.bc.ca/resources/about-bare-land-strata-corporations/
- **Q: Can I turn ALR land into a bare-land strata, or stratify an ALR residence?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and subdivision of reserve land generally requires Agricultural Land Commission permission after an application, apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: Where do buildable lots in Surrey come from?** New urban lots are created by subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum in Zoning By-law No. 12000, and by added-unit development on existing lots under SSMUH (3 units under 280 m², 4 units above it, up to 6 near a frequent bus stop); on the agricultural edges, reserve land generally needs Commission permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Does SSMUH permission mean I can split my lot?** No — added units and subdivision are different permissions. Surrey allows 3 to 4 units on most SSMUH lots and up to 6 near a frequent bus stop, but that does not create new lots and does not waive floodplain, riparian or servicing requirements on the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** A principal residence built without a Commission application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted at 90 m² or less (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (larger parcels) where Surrey bylaws also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income does a Surrey farm parcel need for farm class?** The tiered test, not a flat figure: $2,500 of gross annual value on a farm operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha above that; and $10,000 under 0.8 ha. https://assessmentappeal.bc.ca/other-guides-resources/help-your-with-appeal/farm-class-guide/
