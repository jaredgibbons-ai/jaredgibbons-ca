## 2. COMPLEXES

**This farm topic file carries NO complex roster of its own — verified townhouse complexes counted here: 0 (strata 0, fee-simple 0), stated plainly at the top as instructed for zero verified townhouse stock.**

- **Why zero is the correct count:** the research file confirms no strata farm complex on an official source and states farm parcels in the belt are overwhelmingly freehold and strata farm product is not a Delta pattern. A farm residence is a farm house on a freehold parcel, governed by the ALC housing rules and Delta's A1 zoning in Section 3. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **ALR stratification bar:** ALC Information Bulletin 09 (issued October 18, 2022), s. 6 treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision in the ALR; depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under s. 3 of the ALR General Regulation (Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); an approving officer is prohibited from approving, and a Registrar of Titles must not accept, a plan that would cause subdivision of ALR land unless so permitted. In short: an ALR residence cannot simply be stratified into townhouse units. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

### What farm "attached" housing actually is here — not townhouses

- The dwellings the A1 zone permits on a farm lot are a single farm house (maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², or 5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate), one secondary suite accessory to a farm house (permitted only inside the principal residence under the ALC housing rules), one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot (maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house, ALC approval required for more than one), an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) only with a Professional Agrologist's necessity report and written ALC approval, and migrant farm worker housing under set criteria. None of these is a strata lot or a townhouse; each is part of one freehold farm parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this farm topic file, this section carries the farm/ALR frame — because that frame is what decides whether any attached or second-unit product can exist on a Ladner farm parcel at all — followed by the standard townhouse-side contrast. City rules are cited once for Delta in this file.

- **Fee-simple reality for this topic, stated plainly:** a freehold farm parcel has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Delta's A1 zoning) and registered covenants/HOA documents only, plus the ALR layer below. The strata rules that follow govern Ladner's urban townhouses (in their home files), not farm parcels. Basis: Section 2 above. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **The ALR stratification rule (the rule this whole file turns on):** a strata plan causes a subdivision, so strata conversion or strata development of ALR land requires the Agricultural Land Commission's permission (application under ALCA s. 25) or compliance with s. 3 of the ALR General Regulation; without it, neither an approving officer nor the Registrar of Titles may approve or accept the plan. ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6, October 18, 2022; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALC housing rules on ALR land, in full:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha; local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Delta plan:** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, NO lettered schedules — the Tsawwassen/Ladner area plans were schedules of the superseded Bylaw No. 3950, and any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" attribution is an error wherever met. Farm land is designated Agriculture (A) (s. 1.5.1; Agriculture policies s. 2.6). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not asserted:** the research cites the A1 Agriculture zone text as Part 10, Agriculture Zones, as amended by Bylaw 8285, 2023; City pages cite the Zoning Bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged here and neither number is asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 residential limits:** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate); farming is a principal use, and accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 second and additional dwellings:** one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot, maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house, ALC approval required for more than one; an additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Subdivision of farm land:** in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Migrant farm worker housing in A1:** permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha; new buildings for it must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites (urban Delta, for contrast):** Delta's SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024 and allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots — they do not override the ALR rules above on reserve land; on ALR land a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Farm classification — full three-tier thresholds:** BC Assessment requires a producing farm to meet minimum gross farm income at farm gate prices — **$2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**, with farm applications submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears. Leased land can qualify where the owner applies, a lease is in place, the leased land makes a reasonable contribution to the operation and the lessee-farmer meets the income requirements. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Strata block (urban townhouses only, for completeness):** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); minimum CRF contribution of 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry; short-term rentals can still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner, caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-child exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/

GST/PTT treatment of a specific farm purchase (farm-use and mixed-use apportionment) is transaction-specific — UNCONFIRMED at topic level; confirm with the buyer's lawyer/accountant. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Is there a townhouse complex on Ladner farm land I can buy into?** No verified one — the count for this topic is zero. - **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Ladner?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a Ladner-area farm have a second dwelling?** Yes, within limits: Delta's A1 zone permits one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house; more than one needs Agricultural Land Commission approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. A secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Could a farm residence be stratified later so I could sell the suite separately?** Not without the Agricultural Land Commission: depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision of ALR land, which requires Commission permission after an application (or a permitted case under the ALR General Regulation) — and Delta's A1 zone separately sets an 8 ha minimum for any lot created by subdivision. Do not price a farm purchase on a stratification plan that has no ALC permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Delta farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: in the A1 zone a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, Official Community Plan compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** The full three-tier test: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha — gross income at farm-gate prices, applications by October 31 for the following tax year, including where production runs through a qualifying farm lease. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How do I check whether a parcel is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status is parcel-specific and controls residences, the accessory dwelling limits and most use permissions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can farm workers be housed on the property?** Yes, within the A1 migrant farm worker housing criteria: the lot must be in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, with written ALC approval, the employing farm at least 8 ha and the lot at least 4 ha, and new buildings must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Strata" and "subdivision" are the same act on ALR land.** The ALC's Bulletin 09 states it directly: depositing any strata plan — including bare land and phased stratas — causes a subdivision, so the Commission's permission is required as with any other subdivision. That one sentence is why there is no Ladner farm townhouse market. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **The accessory dwelling has two size ceilings, not one.** An accessory farm dwelling unit is capped at 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha — and an additional farm house is a different, heavier test (agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval, 180 m² / 233 m² caps by lot size), not just a bigger version of the same permission. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Equestrian use has a stall ceiling in the zoning.** The A1 zone's accessory uses include equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **The "Ladner farms" feed is wider than Ladner.** At reading it carried East Delta parcels on Ladner Trunk Road as well as Ladner Rural listings — a buyer searching "Ladner" gets the whole Ladner-side Delta farm belt, and this file states that openly rather than presenting East Delta results as village-adjacent or as North Delta. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/ladner-farms.md)
- **Farm classification runs on an October 31 deadline.** BC Assessment farm applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year — a closing-date item a farm buyer can miss in the first year of ownership. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Home site severance is the one subdivision exception with a name.** The A1 zone's 8 ha subdivision minimum carries a home site severance exception under Agricultural Land Commission policy — the narrow route, not a general subdivision right. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
