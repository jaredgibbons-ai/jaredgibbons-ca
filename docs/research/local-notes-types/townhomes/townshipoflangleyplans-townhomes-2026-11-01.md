## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **A hearing date is not an adoption date, and this page conflates them.** June 15, 2026 is, on Township records opened, the directed Public Hearing date for Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 — the tiles present that same date as the day repeals happened. That single date swap is the defect, and it belongs to this slug (§1).
- **The oldest plan in the stack is still load-bearing.** Murrayville's 1989 Bylaw 2661, as amended by the December 2025 update, still governs parcels east of the City of Langley — age alone does not retire a Township plan (§3).
- **The plan stack is read in layers, in order.** OCP (Bylaw 6200 update), then community plan, then neighbourhood plan, then zoning under Bylaw 2500 — the plan shows where the Township wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today (research file §6).
- **SSMUH has its own permit layer inside the OCP.** The April 27, 2026 Schedule 4 Development Permit Area means a 3–4 unit project can need a Development Permit on form and character even where zoning permits the units (§3).
