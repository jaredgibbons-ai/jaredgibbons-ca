## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all six guide-counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a presale like Amson Bloc, the equivalent disclosure is the developer's REDMA disclosure statement, not a Form B. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, projecting 30 years of repair and replacement costs with at least three CRF funding models, not deferrable by vote. This is the central document for The Uplands (2005) and the Lakewood complexes (c. 1990–91). A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the six is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: Steller Panorama's "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns," The Uplands' "rentals allowed w/restrictions" and the Lakewood directory rental lines each carry a long-term component that is void; only a short-term-rental restriction can survive. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children); the bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it. In this sub-area the rule is live only at Lakewood Heights and Lakewood Place — Panorama Mews' "ideal place for seniors" listing phrase is not a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Panorama Ridge (§2) — that frame applies to the sub-area's detached houses, not to any counted townhouse complex here.
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor (18020 REPLACED, never current). The South Newton plan that formerly covered this ground was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update, with designations updated by Corporate Report R246 (endorsed December 2, 2024). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/panorama-ridge.md, §4)
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs, with R-series residential zones and RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook; the research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² at Part 26 Section F of the bylaw for this plan area. Surrey's SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024** in the program record) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/panorama-ridge.md, §4)
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Surrey):** only one secondary suite is permitted per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite, and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40%** of the principal dwelling unit. All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite: at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/panorama-ridge.md, §4)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host, and tenants cannot operate one. - **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
