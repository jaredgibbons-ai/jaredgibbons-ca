## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (the area's one strata, The Fairways — condos program):** SPA s.59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page. At The Fairways the Form B/strata plan (BCS2427) is also where the conflicting unit count and the UNCONFIRMED age position are settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation / CRF:** 5+ lot stratas must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Minimum CRF contribution 10% since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws VOID** since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Pitt Meadows strata is VOID for long-term rentals. **Separate city layer: short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a city rule the strata-law voiding does not cure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+).
