## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 4 cross-referenced strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — none of the four cross-referenced complexes is such a strata on its published scale (Archer Green's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its 5+ test is confirmed on its Form B/strata plan). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Cherry Lane and Luther Place are flagged VOID in-entry in §2 on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Cherry Lane and Luther Place are both published as 55+ complexes — confirm on the Form B that the registered bylaw is a 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Mission (§2) — a listing calling a cross-referenced complex "fee simple" in the non-strata sense contradicts the strata-plan record and needs a title check (see the Cherry Lane feed conflict in §2).
- **City plan:** Mission's current Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026**, replacing and repealing OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added; the bylaw was put to a public hearing on April 7, 2026.
