## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Short-term rentals PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows:** a City staff report (Oct 23, 2024, for Nov 5, 2024 Council) states "Short-Term Rentals: Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw"; the Province's principal-residence requirement also lists Pitt Meadows. A townhouse business plan built on STR income fails at zoning, before any strata bylaw. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Floodplain — LIVE CONFLICT flagged, retired to 95%:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states **over 95%** of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser River (and, to a smaller degree, Pitt River) floodplain — **this file carries "over 95%."** The live guide page quotes "over 85%" attributed to the City's Financial Plan 2026–2030 — a LIVE CONFLICT between two City sources, documented in this slug's own research as a page error/superseded figure, not adopted here. Protection: 60 km of dikes plus ditches, pump stations and flood boxes; Kennedy drainage area (Area 3) discharges to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers; A-1 requires residential buildings sited 15.0 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Form B (applies to the strata townhouses a West Meadows buyer actually meets — Shoreline/Coho II, board-filed South Meadows):** SPA s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No in-area strata stock exists to apply it to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation/CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF. Both govern the Fraser Way stratas documented above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; STRs can still be restricted by bylaw — and in Pitt Meadows are prohibited city-wide by zoning (stronger rule, above). Coho II's directory line reads rentals allowed with restrictions (directory data — confirm on Form B); any "rentals not allowed" line for a Pitt Meadows strata is VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ housing of any tenure in West Meadows; The Fairways (a North Meadows condo in the city assembly, not West Meadows stock) has an UNCONFIRMED age position and must never be called 55+; the Ford Road Co-op is not a seniors building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple (the area's actual stock) — plainly:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011) and registered covenants only. For farm parcels add ALR: 78% of Pitt Meadows is in the ALR per the City's 2020 Business Plan. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Zoning/plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — A-1 General Agriculture (one farm house per lot, max GFA 500 sq m; subdivision minimum 8 ha), A-2 Large Lot Agricultural (subdivision minimum 16 ha), RS/R-3/R-4/R-6 in the east-edge pocket (up to 3, 4 or 6 units per lot; R-6's sixth unit conditional on an adaptable unit; residential subdivision minimum 1,215 sq m in R-3/R-4/R-6). Plan: "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022 (repealed OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007). SSMUH/suites: secondary suites and garden suites are accessory uses in RS; City garden suite guide — one suite per lot, 33–90 sq m, a lot cannot have both a garden suite and a secondary suite; SSMUH under OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartments (2–6 units) where it applies. On ALR land the ALC allows one additional detached residence without application where one residence exists when construction begins, max 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT 1% first $200,000, 2% $200,000–$2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on residential portion above $3M ("Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of 3%+2%, flagged; the count home's shorthand "(1%…5% above)" is read the same way, not as a separate tier). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phase to $1.5M, max $50,000, Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000; CMHC 0.6%–4.5%. **CMHC 2.80%–4.00% error: NOT documented in this slug's research, NOT asserted.** Pitt Meadows 2026 taxes due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, 5% penalty after July 2 and further 5% after August 1, 2026; separate drainage levy — residential Class 1 $0.1082/$1,000, Farm Class 9 $1.8524/$1,000 drainage alongside $48.6824/$1,000 farm general rate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
