## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Representation first (the rule a reader of a profile page acts on), then the strata block.**

- **Representation:** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by BCFSA, and BCFSA states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party (research file §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers).
- **Read this page for what it is: a profile, not evidence.** Its rankings and volume figures are the page's own statements with no source or measurement period attached — a consumer doing due diligence should ask for the underlying board or brokerage results, and can check a licensee's standing through BCFSA (research file §6).

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhome cross-referenced in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a cross-referenced complex is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in the cross-referenced South Surrey stock in §2.
