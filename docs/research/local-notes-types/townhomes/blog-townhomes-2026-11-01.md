## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This section carries the verified buyer frame the blog's topics touch — market updates, tax and rules explainers, and buying guides — worked for a townhome purchase, because a blog reader who lands on a rules post and then buys a townhome is diligenced under this frame, not under the post alone.

- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. A blog post or directory written before those dates may state a superseded rule book on either point. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **PTT:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 (never a single "5% over $3M" rate); First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Financing supports:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (a figure stating 2.80%–4.00% for CMHC premiums is WRONG against that published range). https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a blog topic touches a presale townhome, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
