- **Hydro shaped this valley before the houses did.** Construction of the Stave Falls Dam and Powerhouse began in 1909 and was completed by January 1, 1912 (BC Hydro); Hayward Lake is an active hydroelectric reservoir first and a recreation area second, and the City restricts parking on the roads leading to it at busy times — summer access pressure is part of the ownership picture. https://www.bchydro.com/community/recreation_areas/hayward_lake.html
- **The two nearest elementary schools sit in two different communities.** Stave Falls Elementary is northeast in Stave Falls and Silverdale Elementary is south in Silverdale, and SD75 catchments are set by district policy — a Steelhead address cannot be school-matched by community name alone. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/steelhead.md)
- **Mission's current OCP is newer than most listing copy.** Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026 was adopted April 21, 2026; any guide stating a June 1, 2026 adoption is wrong wherever that date is documented, and material citing the predecessor Bylaw 6350-2025 is a bylaw out of date. (§3 above)

A buyer who stops at the search result will think Steelhead has a clubhouse townhouse complex; it does not. (§2 above)
- **The hospital is in town, not out here.** Mission Memorial Hospital, 7324 Hurd Street, provides 24/7 emergency stabilization and care, general medicine and ambulatory care (Fraser Health) — every service run from a Steelhead acreage is a drive to the urban core southeast.
