## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the strata rules that govern the bare-land strata stock that actually exists here (and would govern a townhouse purchase if stock existed), and the FVRD rural rules that explain the ground.

- **Form B diligence (any strata — including the bare-land stratas):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Creekside Mills, The Cottages and Aquadel Crossing the Form B is where lot-versus-common-property responsibilities, fees and bylaws are confirmed — what the strata owns versus what sits on the lot is the question to answer from it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Bare-land stratas are stratas for these purposes — Creekside Mills' 129 lots put it squarely inside the frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; any "rentals not allowed" line in a directory or listing for a Lindell Beach bare-land strata is VOID as a long-term rental restriction — flagged here as the standing in-entry rule, since no counted complex in §2 carries such a line. **Short-term rentals are a separate, live gate at the south end:** stratas may still ban or limit them by bylaw; the FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H (Lindell Beach) to proactively address short-term vacation rentals, FVRD Zoning Bylaw No. 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use unless the zone expressly permits it, and a provincial short-term rental registration number has been required since May 1, 2025. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). None of the three Lindell Beach communities holds one in any source checked — and a "45+" label, of the kind in the Cultus Lake Village park's conflicting listings, could not be a valid strata bylaw in any case; that park is a pad-rental tenancy, not a strata, and its rule stays UNCONFIRMED (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** The south end's freehold lots carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; FVRD rules plus any registered covenants govern instead. A buyer importing strata expectations — or a seller marketing a bare-land strata cottage as a "townhouse" — is misdescribing the tenure or the form either way.
- **Governing plan and zoning (FVRD — never Chilliwack's bylaws, never the Park Board's):** the FVRD Official Community Plan for Electoral Areas E and H, Bylaw No. 1115 (titled "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019), covers the south end; zoning is in the FVRD Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021 — the zone names and minimum parcel area/frontage figures applying to Lindell Beach parcels are UNVERIFIED and must be checked per address. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Bare land strata" is the whole story at Lindell Beach.** All three named communities sell a detached house or cottage, but the title is a bare land strata lot: strata fees, bylaws, a depreciation report and a CRF stand behind the cottage, and the Form B — not the cottage's appearance, and not a "townhouse" label in a search portal — is where a buyer learns what the strata maintains and what the lot owner does. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=15206
- **The south end's sewer tax is shared equally, not metered.** Amendment Bylaw No. 1779, 2025 set the South Cultus Lake sewer parcel tax as the annual requisition divided equally by the parcels on the roll — every participating parcel pays the same amount regardless of use, and the FVRD's Area H rates show the south sewer and the Cultus Lake water service as tax-funded with no separate utility bill (the north sewer, by contrast, carries a flat rate plus taxes). https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com:443/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=30839
- **The local hazards are creek fans, not the Fraser River.** The governing local hazards at Lindell Beach are lake-level and creek (Frosst Creek) debris/flood events on the Cultus Lake alluvial fans; the FVRD funds sediment-basin cleanouts at Frosst Creek and Elbow Creek in this area, and its Area H guide lists flooding, landslide and slope stability, interface fire, debris slides and flash flooding. Property-specific hazard reports are UNVERIFIED. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/FVRD-Area-H-Update-with-Emergency-and-Evacuation-Plans-May-14-2025.pdf
- **There are no stats for anywhere at Cultus Lake in the named programs.** The SnapStats FVR and GVR September 2026 reports contain no Chilliwack section at all, and CADREB publishes no area stats — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for this sub-area has no source in those programs and should be treated as UNVERIFIED. Lindell Beach is a CADREB board sub-area under the Cultus Lake MLS area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/cultus-lake-south.md)
- **One park, two age labels, no operator source.** The Cultus Lake Village park's age rule appears as "45+" in one 2026 listing and "55+" in a later one for the same home — a conflict the program carries openly in the East, North and South files rather than settling from listing copy, because no operator source states the rule. (§2 above)
- **Aquadel Crossing answers to two civic numbers in public sources.** The developer address is 1885 Columbia Valley Rd while a current listing records the community at 1880 Columbia Valley Road — a small conflict that matters for title searches, locator runs and showing directions, and one to settle from the strata plan rather than from either marketing source. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cultus-lake-south-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Soowahlie First Nation (IR 14) is at the south end of the lake.** The PlanCultus plan map shows Soowahlie First Nation (IR 14) at the south end — a neighbouring jurisdiction a south-end buyer will see on maps and in addressing, distinct from both the Park and the FVRD lots. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/about-the-fvrd/electoral-areas/electoral-area-h.html
- **The Holiday Resort's tenure is documented by a court, not a brochure.** The only hard source on the Lindell Beach Holiday Resort's model is the BC Court of Appeal's 2009 record (owned pre-fabricated trailer on a leased pad; 130 sites, 100 occupied at that time) — the resort itself publishes no current lease terms, so any pad-rent or term figure quoted today is UNVERIFIED until the resort states it. https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/CA/09/00/2009BCCA0005.htm
