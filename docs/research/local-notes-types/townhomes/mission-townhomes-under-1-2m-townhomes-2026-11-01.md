## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all cross-referenced strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — none of the cross-referenced complexes is such a strata on its published scale (Archer Green's lot count UNCONFIRMED; confirm its 5+ test on the Form B). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Cherry Lane and Luther Place are flagged VOID in-entry in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Cherry Lane and Luther Place are both published as 55+ — the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020) plus registered covenants/HOA only. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Mission — a "fee-simple" claim for a cross-referenced complex needs a title check against its strata plan.

- **City property taxes — FLAT 10%:** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **10% penalty on any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026** (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; NO s. 235 alternative-scheme bylaw). The FVRD rural regime (5% after July 2 + 5% after October 31, Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11) applies outside the City only — never blended here. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **PTT — worked examples, inputs stated, tier math shown.** Rates: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of 3% + 2% — the tier math governs, and no purchase in this band reaches the $2M tier. *Example A:* a $600,000 resale townhouse, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $400,000 ($8,000) = **$10,000**. *Example B:* the same $600,000 purchase with the first-time buyers' exemption applying in full (fair market value $835,000 or less, all conditions met) — the exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 of value: tax is payable only on the $100,000 above $500,000, all of it in the 2% tier = 2% × $100,000 = **$2,000** (a saving of $8,000, the Province's stated maximum saving on the first $500,000). Run the buyer's actual qualification through the Province's calculator before quoting either figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **New-build GST — worked example, inputs stated.** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. *Example:* a $900,000 new Briskham Hill townhouse bought from the developer = 5% × $900,000 = $45,000 GST before rebates. The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122) returns 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031 — on those inputs a qualifying first-time buyer recovers the full $45,000; a non-qualifying buyer falls back to the New Housing Rebate frame (36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out), which at a $900,000 price is fully phased out.
