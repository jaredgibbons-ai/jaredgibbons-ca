## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Fraser VE" is the board / site name for the Fraser sub-area in Vancouver East. Fraser is not one of the City of Vancouver's 22 distinct local areas — the City's areas page lists a smaller area named Fraser, and the Fraser Street corridor runs through four official local areas: Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview. Fraser Street itself is the spine, running south from the East Broadway / Kingsway area to SE Marine Drive. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Fraser E49's four homes sit under that threshold; the three older clusters' lot counts are UNCONFIRMED, so confirm each complex's position from its Form B package. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Fraser VE complex was found in this pass, so there is no in-entry VOID flag to make in Section 2 — but any such line a buyer finds in a directory for these complexes is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No counted Fraser VE townhouse complex carries a published 55+ bylaw in the sources checked; the area's 55+ product is the North Arm Housing Co-op, which is co-op tenure, not a strata age bylaw (see Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes and duplexes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly. That is the correct frame for the corridor's duplex and laneway product; a "townhouse" listing with no strata fees charged is usually this form, not a strata lot.
- **City zoning:** the residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); the district schedule for the Fraser Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the Kensington-Cedar Cottage Vision and the Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney Visions apply to the local areas the corridor runs through, alongside the citywide Vancouver Plan; the City's Villages Planning Program names Fraser Street and East 33rd Avenue among its village areas, with retail expansion planned through the Villages Plan. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax:** 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared vacant; every Vancouver owner must file an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A Fraser VE townhouse bought as a secondary or held-vacant property carries this extra annual step. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example Fraser E49), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Fraser VE townhouse presale is carried in this file, but the frame governs any new multiplex product sold off-plan on the corridor — Fraser E49's own builder documents reference a disclosure statement. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there actually townhouses on the Fraser corridor, or is it all condos and houses?** Both exist, but the verified named townhouse stock is four small strata clusters — The Oliver (501 East 44th Avenue, 2017), The Carolina (3918 Carolina Street, 2020), The Saint George (5743 St. George Street, 2014) and Fraser E49 (595 East 49th Avenue, 2026). The corridor's deeper attached supply is apartment condos (Section 2) and its ground-oriented fee-simple supply is duplexes. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/501-east-44th-avenue
- **Q: The listing says "townhouse" but shows no strata fee — what am I buying?** Usually a fee-simple duplex or half-duplex on an R1-1 lot: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees, and diligence runs on title and municipal rules instead. Side-street listings in this area expressly market duplexes with "NO STRATA FEES." Check the Title to Land field before assuming either way. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Is "The Saint George" the big building near Main Street?** No. The Fraser VE complex is the small 2014 strata cluster at 5743 St. George Street; the Mount Pleasant building of the same name at 2471 St. George Street is a different strata in a different sub-area. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/5743-st-george-street
- **Q: Can I rent out my Fraser strata townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What is Fraser E49, exactly?** A brand-new 4-home strata fourplex by Alabaster Homes at 595 East 49th Avenue — the developer calls the form a fourplex and its own documents refer to strata lots and a disclosure statement, while MLS splits the individual homes between Townhouse and Half Duplex sub-types. Price it as new multiplex strata product, not as a townhouse subdivision unit. https://www.alabastermultiplex.ca/
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a Fraser townhouse?** Only if it is declared vacant or is a secondary property caught by the rules — the tax is 3%, every owner must file the annual declaration regardless, and the 2025 payment deadline was April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a townhouse near Fraser and 41st?** Address-based in SD39. The locator result for 530 East 41st Avenue is Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary and John Oliver Secondary — but that confirms only that address; run the exact townhouse address in the locator and never promise a school from the street name. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: What can the R1-1 lot under a side-street house actually hold?** Up to 6 units in a multiplex, or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas of 306 m² (house/duplex or 3–4 units), 464 m² (5 units) and 557 m² (6–8 units) — frontage and site area, not just the house, drive value on these streets. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new home such as Fraser E49, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: When are Vancouver taxes due?** Advance taxes February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, each with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance — a two-date calendar most non-Vancouver buyers have never seen. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Fraser" is a corridor, not a neighbourhood.** The board name Fraser VE spans four official City local areas — Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Riley Park, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview — so two homes both sold as "Fraser" can sit in different City planning areas, different community visions and different school catchments. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **The library gives the south corridor its other name.** The South Hill Branch of Vancouver Public Library is at 6076 Fraser Street — "South Hill" is the local name buyers will hear for the library's part of the corridor. https://www.vpl.ca/location/south-hill-branch
- **The City is planning retail growth at Fraser and 33rd.** The Villages Planning Program names Fraser Street and East 33rd Avenue as a village area with retail expansion to come through the Villages Plan — frontage lots near 33rd carry plan expectations the quiet interior blocks do not. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/share-your-thoughts-on-vancouver-villages-nov-2025.aspx
- **Memorial South Park is the corridor's big green space, and it is one block off Fraser.** At 5955 Ross Street in Sunset, the Park Board lists sports fields and courts — the park buyers picture as "on Fraser" actually fronts Ross Street. https://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=186
- **The hospital beside the corridor is not the one buyers assume.** Mount Saint Joseph Hospital sits at the Prince Edward Street entrance beside the corridor in Kensington-Cedar Cottage; Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, is west of the corridor. https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/visitor-guides/mount-saint-joseph-hospital-patient-visitor-guide/when-you-arrive
- **The research file could not confirm any community centre inside Fraser VE.** The nearest City community centres named in the sources read serve the adjoining local areas — a buyer who wants a pool or rink programs map should look at the neighbouring areas' centres, not assume one on the corridor.
