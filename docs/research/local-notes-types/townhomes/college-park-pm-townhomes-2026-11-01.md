## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every College Park strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Westhill Place is an early-1970s wood-frame complex (condos program) — there, and at the three counted townhouse strata, the depreciation report and CRF balance on the Form B are the first-order documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: Westhill Place's directory "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element is VOID as a restriction — rentals are allowed. Port Moody's own STR bylaw/licence position is UNVERIFIED — no City STR source was read in this research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No College Park building is verified to carry one (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2937) plus registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in College Park (§2) — all three counted complexes are strata, whatever a "freehold townhomes" shorthand says.
- **City plan — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction: Port Moody Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026, which repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — 2955 is NEVER cited as current.** Chapter 14 sets policy for the city's neighbourhoods; the Suncor refinery lands beside College Park and Seaview are designated a Special Study Area in the OCP — the main planning item hanging over this end of the city, and a disclosure item on any College Park purchase. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/college-park-pm.md) https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **City zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 2018, No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones. Under Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460, small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas (the TOAs are exempt from the SSMUH rules): up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops. **Trap specific to this sub-area: the RS1-S small-lot subdivision standard (minimum 9 m width, 325 m²) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and Pleasantside OCP Single Family–Low Density areas — College Park is not on that list**, so do not price a College Park lot off the small-lot offer; confirm any split idea with the City.
