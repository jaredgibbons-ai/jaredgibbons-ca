### 2.1 Why there can be no strata townhouse on ALR land

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 states the stratification bar; the Strata Property Act s. 239 treats depositing a strata plan as a subdivision (SPA s. 239 deposit = subdivision), and the Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) restricts ALR subdivision and use to what the Act and regulations permit. Any listing presenting attached strata product on a Langley farm parcel must be checked parcel by parcel, because the parcel may not be in the ALR at all — built form and feed labels never override the ALR check (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).

### 2.4 The attached forms that DO exist on farm land — none of them townhouses

- **Secondary suite — inside the principal residence only.** On ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only when it is located entirely within the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling is not used as a secondary suite, and local government bylaws must also permit secondary suites. A suite is part of one fee-simple house, not a separate townhouse unit (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Additional residence — capped, detached, fee-simple.** One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — a maximum total floor area of 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Coach house — a fee-simple second unit, never strata.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 s. 102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot. It is an accessory unit on a fee-simple lot; it is not stratified, has no Form B and is not a townhouse (research file §4).
- **Principal residence — capped, fee-simple.** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies, and local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

### The farm / ALR frame — LEADS this section

- **ALR stratification bar.** On ALR land a strata plan cannot simply be deposited — ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 treats strata plans as causing subdivision in the ALR; Strata Property Act s. 239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) are the statutory frame (§2.1). The research file carries Bulletin 09 s. 6 as the settled reference; its detailed conditions were not re-read in that pass, so no condition beyond that reference is stated — detail UNVERIFIED beyond the reference (research file §4).
- **ALC residence caps.** Principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area, ≤1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite only entirely within the principal residence; one additional residence at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha), and Township bylaws must also permit it (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm thresholds — in full three-tier form:** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income requirements at farm gate prices — **$2,500** if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha and 4 ha; **$2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha** if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and **$10,000** if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — with farm classification applications due by **October 31**. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Zoning frame.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township. **SSMUH (standing correction):** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — **NO 6-plex tier exists in it**; secondary suite cap 120 m²; coach house = fee-simple second unit under Bylaw 2500 s. 102, never strata (§2.4). A general minimum parcel size for the rural (RU) zones from the direct bylaw zone text, and a farm home plate rule, are UNVERIFIED in this file's research (research file §4).
- **Water and septic.** Search GWELLS for the well record; non-domestic groundwater use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, while domestic use does not. Off-sewer homes need an onsite system under the Sewerage System Regulation, designed, installed, repaired, maintained or inspected only by an Authorized Person (§1; research file §4).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How much of Langley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve?**
A: The Township of Langley states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; check a specific parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address (§1; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx).

**Q: Can I buy a strata townhouse on a Langley farm?**
A: Not on ALR land — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Langley's strata townhouses sit on urban ground and are counted in their area files; the fee-simple row homes cross-referenced in §2.3 are the closest non-strata attached form, and they are urban product too (§2.1).

**Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Langley?**
A: A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence (§§2.4, 3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?**
A: On ALR land, one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. A coach house under Bylaw 2500 s. 102 is the Township's fee-simple second-unit form off that route — never strata (§§2.4, 3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?**
A: BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. The full three-tier form is in §3 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error (§3; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **Most of the Township is reserve land.** 235 km² of the Township is in the ALR, so most Langley farm parcels carry ALC housing and use rules layered over Township zoning — the ALR check comes before pricing, not after (§1; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx).
- **Farm classification is an income test, not a zoning label.** A parcel keeps farm classification only while the operation keeps meeting the size-scaled gross income thresholds in §3, with an October 31 application deadline — a buyer whose plan cannot meet them risks the classification and its tax treatment (§3).
- **The page's own marketing claims are not carried as fact.** "Fertile soil," "mild climate," a "supportive agricultural community" and "viticulture" are live-page copy with no official source opened for soil class, climate or sector claims in the research pass; soil capability is UNVERIFIED per parcel (§3; research file §1).

- **A suite on ALR land cannot be a detached cottage.** The ALC permits a secondary suite only entirely within the principal residence — the additional-residence route (90 m² / 186 m²) is the only detached second-dwelling path, and Township bylaws must also permit it (§§2.4, 3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
