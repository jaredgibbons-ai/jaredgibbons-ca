# Fort Langley, Township of Langley — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Fort Langley is a village in the Township of Langley on the south bank of the Fraser River, described by the Township as the birthplace of BC, with its commercial core on Glover Road. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66 and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
- The wider village is framed by River Road, 88 Avenue, 96 Avenue and 240 Street, with the Salmon River and Bedford Channel at its edges; exact Paragon sub-area boundary roads are UNVERIFIED. https://www.pc.gc.ca/apps/dfhd/page_nhs_eng.aspx?id=66
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: two pockets — Bedford Landing on Billy Brown Road along the Bedford Channel, west of the Glover Road core (fee-simple row homes plus the Flatiron live/work townhouses), and King Street in the village heart (Leah). The 1912 presale will add three more townhomes at village scale. Sources: the entries in Section 2 below.
- There are no townhouse towers and no large townhouse subdivisions in Fort Langley, and the village framework works against them: the downtown core is a designated Heritage Conservation Area, and new development must complement the heritage character (see Sections 3 and 5). https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- Glover Road is the village commercial street holding the shops; Lee's Market, the village grocery, is at 23320 Mavis Avenue, and there is no shopping centre in Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
- Transit is a single bus: TransLink route 562 runs through the village on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange, via Walnut Grove. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Fort Langley. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- SD35 schools physically in the village are Fort Langley Elementary (8877 Bartlett St) and Langley Fine Arts School (9096 Trattle St), a district K-12 fine arts choice school; a locator run for 8877 Bartlett St returned Fort Langley Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary, with no middle school shown, and catchments are address-based. https://fortlangley.sd35.bc.ca/ and https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Two tenures, two diligence tracks — this sub-area has both.** For the strata complexes (Leah, Flatiron): Form B, bylaws, depreciation report and CRF, per the bullets below. For the fee-simple Bedford Landing row homes: NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, the Community Plan, heritage/design controls) plus any registered covenants or building scheme on title govern instead. Confirm which track a Billy Brown Road address is on before drafting subjects; the community name does not tell you. (Complex entries in Section 2 above.)
- **Form B first (strata stock).** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle (strata stock).** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with 30-year projections and at least three CRF funding models, non-deferrable by vote. Both Leah (2024) and Flatiron (2012) are over that lot threshold on their verified counts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum 10% (strata stock).** Since November 1, 2023, a strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata stock).** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for Leah or Flatiron is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals may still be restricted by bylaw. Fee-simple row homes carry no strata rental bylaw at all — rental limits there, if any, come from title covenants and Township bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form (strata stock).** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; bylaws set below 55 have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. No Fort Langley townhouse complex in Section 2 is verified as 55+ — the village's 55+ building is the Waterfront apartment condo, cross-referenced above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Village plan: the Fort Langley Community Plan, Township of Langley Bylaw No. 2527** (adopted October 26, 1987, as an amendment to the Township Official Community Plan), is the governing plan for the village. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Heritage Conservation Area.** That plan designates a Heritage Conservation Area under the Local Government Act covering the downtown core (added by Bylaw 3710, October 27, 1997), to protect the heritage character of downtown Fort Langley; non-exempt work requires approval under the heritage rules, with exemptions including minor additions under 50 m² that do not front a road (other than a lane) and conform to the guidelines. This reaches townhouse buyers twice: strata approval for a unit's exterior, and Township heritage approval where the complex sits in or affects the core. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Design controls on top of zoning.** The village is also covered by a Development Permit Area (Bylaw 3204, May 3, 1993) and the Fort Langley Design Guidelines (Bylaw 5063, April 28, 2014); the separate Building Façade Design Guidelines are non-binding policy. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** applies in the village; Residential Zone R-1E is named for the village in a Township public-hearing document, and minimum lot areas/frontages for the village zones are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section. The Bedford Landing buildings sit in site-specific CD zones (the Waterfront's is CD-55 — a zone number, not an age designation). https://tol.maps.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/0c6ddccb89034b6787c285311714b8ce/data and https://www.cotala.com/tours/85164/FullPublic.pdf?i=603115479
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024**, amended Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in most zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings. There is NO 6-plex tier in the Township's SSMUH framework — the research file documents no page claim of one for Fort Langley, so there is no defect to flag here; any Fort Langley page that does claim a 6-plex tier would be wrong. In the village, SSMUH operates inside the heritage and design controls above — it is not an exemption from them. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Secondary suites: capped at 120 m².** Township reporting on its suite rules records the maximum allowable gross floor area for a secondary suite at 120 m², and Council's 2025 direction extended suites to ground-oriented housing types including townhouses and fee-simple rowhouses — inside a strata townhouse, the registered bylaws must also allow it, and parking requirements apply. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Coach houses: a fee-simple second unit, never strata.** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is a second dwelling unit on a fee-simple lot — it is part of the same fee-simple title as the principal house, not a separate strata lot, and it cannot be sold separately from that lot. Do not price or market a Fort Langley coach house as strata product. (Standing correction for this run; zoning framework: https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx)
- **ALR edges — stratification is barred and farm thresholds are three-tiered.** Farm land on the village edges is in the ALR in places. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)) — an acreage-edge "townhouse strata" on ALR land is not a lawful form. The ALC also permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met — up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — subject to Township bylaws. Farm-class qualification itself is three-tiered ($2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Flood — a townhouse issue here, not just a house issue.** Fort Langley is low-lying: the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for the Fort Langley community and its flood box gates close when Fraser River levels rise. Parcel-level floodplain status for a specific complex: UNVERIFIED — get it from the Township, and for strata units check the insurance summary on the Form B for flood coverage and deductible terms. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for eligible first-time buyers, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Fort Langley's completed townhouse stock is resale or near-new resale, so this matters here mainly for The 1912 presale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, for qualifying new-home buyers. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 3, 2026, and utilities are now billed separately. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** The 1912 presale requires a disclosure statement; REDMA s. 21 gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it to rescind, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust. Assignment rights are contract-specific — assume none unless the contract and disclosure statement grant them. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment; a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Market context, as the research file states it.** Langley IS covered by SnapStats: in September 2026 the report recorded Fort Langley attached inventory at 9 listings with 1 sale (11% sales ratio), and Langley-wide attached medians at community level only. That attached figure combines condos and townhomes — SnapStats splits Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined and publishes no townhome-only figure, so none is quoted here (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/fort-langley-guide.md).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many townhouse complexes are there in Fort Langley, really?** Two verified strata complexes (Leah, 41 homes, 2024; Flatiron, 11 live/work units, 2012), plus fee-simple non-strata row homes at Bedford Landing, plus a 3-home presale (The 1912). The village's three apartment buildings are condos, not townhouses. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: What is the difference between a Bedford Landing row home and a Leah townhouse?** Tenure. A Bedford Landing row home recorded as Row House (Non-Strata) is fee-simple: your lot, your title, no strata fee, no Form B, no depreciation report — and no strata to maintain the roof or envelope either. Leah is freehold strata: monthly fees, bylaws, a Form B and shared common property. Two homes on the same street can sit on opposite tracks. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2516238-23086-billy-brown-road and https://www.estateblock.com/langley/30-9122-king-st-122804
- **Q: Can I run a business from a Flatiron unit?** The complex is recorded as live/work, built by Parklane — but "live/work" in a listing is not a business licence. Check the strata bylaws, the CD zoning's permitted uses and the Township's business-licence requirements for your specific use before subjects. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/8-23230-billy-brown-rd-1844419
- **Q: Can I rent out a Leah or Flatiron townhouse?** Long-term rental bans are void in BC since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A fee-simple row home has no strata bylaw at all — check title covenants and Township rules instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Leah's building records state pets allowed / pets allowed with restrictions, and Flatiron's recorded strata restrictions state pets allowed with restrictions. Confirm number/size limits in the registered bylaws for the specific complex. https://www.estateblock.com/langley/30-9122-king-st-122804 and https://www.estateblock.com/langley/8-23230-billy-brown-rd-1844419
- **Q: Is any Fort Langley townhouse complex 55+?** No townhouse complex here is verified as 55+. The village's verified 55+ building is Waterfront at Bedford Landing — an apartment condo (BCS3620), not a townhouse. Do not let the shared "Bedford Landing" name move that age rule onto the row homes or Flatiron. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/fort-langley-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a townhouse or a coach house to a row-home lot?** The Township caps a secondary suite at 120 m² of gross floor area and has moved to allow suites in ground-oriented housing including townhouses and fee-simple rowhouses — but a strata unit also needs its bylaws to allow it, and a coach house under Zoning Bylaw 2500, s. 102, is a fee-simple second unit on the same title, never a strata lot. Heritage and development-permit rules in the village core sit on top of both. https://udi.org/advocacy/updates/township-of-langley-secondary-suites-and-lock-off-units
- **Q: Can I change the exterior, add a heat pump, or enclose a deck?** For strata units, two approvals, not one: the strata's, and — for properties in or affecting the downtown Heritage Conservation Area — the Township's heritage process, unless the work falls in an exemption such as a minor addition under 50 m² not fronting a road and conforming to the guidelines. Fee-simple row homes skip the strata step, not the heritage/design step. https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx
- **Q: What flood questions should I ask on Billy Brown Road?** Whether the parcel is in the Township's floodplain mapping, what the strata's insurance summary (strata units) says about flood coverage and deductibles, and what phase the Salmon River Pump Station upgrades are in — the Township states that station provides the community's flood protection, with flood box gates that close as Fraser River levels rise. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: How do I get anywhere without a car?** Route 562 is the village's only confirmed bus, running on Glover Road between Langley Centre and Carvolth Exchange via Walnut Grove. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express in Fort Langley, and the funded Surrey–Langley SkyTrain extension (King George Station to Langley City Centre, late 2029) does not serve the village. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0022-000515
- **Q: Which school catchment is a Bedford Landing address in?** The locator result in the research file is for 8877 Bartlett St (Fort Langley Elementary / D.W. Poppy Secondary, no middle school shown) — a sample, not a promise for Billy Brown Road. Run the exact address in the SD35 School Locator before relying on a school. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Bedford Landing" is a tenure trap, not a building.** The same community name covers a 55+ apartment condo (Waterfront, BCS3620), an all-ages apartment condo (The Village, EPS998), an 11-unit live/work strata (Flatiron) and fee-simple non-strata row homes. A listing that says only "Bedford Landing" has told you nothing about tenure, age rules or who maintains the roof — the civic address and the title tell you. (Section 2 above.)
- **The newest village housing project is a heritage restoration with three townhomes.** The 1912, by Lanstone Homes (the same builder as Lily Terrace), is a presale collection of three heritage-inspired townhomes plus the restored 1912 George Albion Towle House, with pre-sales stated as coming Winter 2027 and move-in Fall 2027 — developer-stated timing, at village scale, and its tenure is not stated by the developer source. https://www.the1912.ca/
- **There is no 6-plex tier in the Township — and no Fort Langley page in the research file claims one.** The Township's SSMUH framework (Bylaw 6020, November 18, 2024) works in the single-family/duplex zones it amended; a marketing claim of a 6-plex right in the village would contradict it, and the heritage framework would still apply. No defect is documented for this area's page on that point — stated here so the absence is on the record. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **A March 2026 Township SSMUH step touches Fort Langley specifically.** The Township's March 9, 2026 council report on an SSMUH development permit area names the Fort Langley Community Plan among the plans Bylaw No. 6116 would amend — the form-and-character rules for new small-scale multi-unit housing in the village were still being written in 2026, inside the existing heritage framework. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **salishan Place by the River changed the village's civic core.** The Township's arts, culture and heritage facility at 23430 Mavis Avenue — developed in partnership with the Katzie, Kwantlen, Matsqui and Semiahmoo First Nations — now houses both the Fort Langley Community Library and the museum, a short walk from the Bedford Landing townhouses. https://www.tol.ca/en/centre-information.aspx
- **Bedford Landing is a mill site turned neighbourhood.** The community was built on the grounds of the McDonald Cedar Mill, which opened in 1921 on an 88-acre riverfront property, and its restored pump house and replicated boom-crane wharf remain on the waterfront; the Fort-to-Fort Trail runs about 8 km round trip through Bedford Landing along the river. That history is why strata buildings and fee-simple rows sit side by side in one planned enclave rather than on the Glover Road grid. https://hexaflexagon-salmon-whfg.squarespace.com/bedford-landing
- **Flood protection is being upgraded, and it is a Township project, not a strata one.** The Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection for Fort Langley, with flood box gates that close as Fraser River levels rise, and Council has authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for pump station upgrades. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/fort-langley-community-projects.aspx
