## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. Both counted complexes are 1980s wood-frame strata, so the depreciation report, CRF balance and any envelope/roof history in the Form B package are the core diligence items. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this file's orbit (for example the 455 Bromley Street feature-sheet line, flagged in Section 2) is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in Coquitlam East (see the sibling 55-plus file); a "50+" line surviving in any older east-side listing material (for example Glen Eagles in Eagle Ridge CQ) cannot be valid in that form today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the sub-area's detached stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 3000) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in Section 2, so this is the rule for the detached houses a Coquitlam East townhouse buyer cross-shops. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Coquitlam zoning and SSMUH:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — minimum lot size for subdivision is 650 m² with 18.5 m width for R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width for R-2. Coquitlam's adopted SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. The Interim OCP Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, supersede OCP No. 3479 as the plan frame. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Watercourse and hazards:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam and within 30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s. 523 covers riparian areas — a live check on the ravine- and forest-edge lots around the Atlin Place pocket. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities:** property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants); utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste, with a Voluntary Residential Water Metering Program under which metered properties are billed every four months instead of the annual flat rate. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/coquitlam-east.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — both counted complexes are 1980s resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this sub-area today; the frame, if a future project is marketed off-plan, is disclosure under REDMA s. 18 and a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, with deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
