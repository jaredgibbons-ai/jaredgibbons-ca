## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Canyon Park and any strata purchase compared nearby):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Canyon Park's 14 lots put it squarely inside the rule. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No corridor complex carries a confirmed directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry — Canyon Park's rental position is UNCONFIRMED (Section 2) — but the rule governs any attached purchase the buyer compares nearby. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No corridor complex is published as 55+; the area's verified 55+ stock is apartment-form in Edgemont Village (sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the corridor's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **District zoning and plan:** the District of North Vancouver's **Official Community Plan is Bylaw 7900** and its **Zoning Bylaw is 3210** (the City of North Vancouver's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 pair does not apply on this corridor). Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799): the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth, and the R2 zone minimum lot is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH as the District states it:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Hazard permit areas:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — canyon-edge and ravine lots on this corridor are exactly the lots these areas were written for. The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does **not** apply in the District of North Vancouver and is omitted. District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value, and District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60 (combined single-family utilities $2,571.20). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The dam above the corridor is a 1954 drinking-water dam.** The Cleveland Dam at the head of Capilano River Regional Park was completed in 1954 and stands 91 m high, holding back Capilano Lake, the drinking-water reservoir above the corridor; Fisheries and Oceans Canada's Capilano River Hatchery in the park below the dam was completed in 1971 and normally draws about 250,000 visitors a year for the coho and chinook runs. The park offers the Capilano Pacific Trail and the Coho Loop Trail. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/capilano-river-regional-park and https://www.pac.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/sep-pmvs/hatcheries-ecloseries/capilano-eng.html
- **The school and the library with the area's name are both somewhere else.** Capilano Elementary (1230 West 20th Street) sits in the City lowlands south of the corridor, and the Capilano Library (3045 Highland Boulevard) is described by the North Vancouver District Public Library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village," east of the corridor — the corridor's services cluster in the neighbouring village centre, not on Capilano Road. The nearest full-service recreation centre is the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road. https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library
- **A detached lot here can already hold three homes' worth of occupants.** Since June 2024, a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite plus a coach house — three units total — are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the District's SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on non-exempt lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. That infill potential, not strata product, is the corridor's density story. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **The flood data is modelled to 2100.** The District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m — a canyon-edge buyer's renovation budget should be priced against that horizon, not against today's water levels. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **The District is the big municipality, not the small one.** The District of North Vancouver's 2021 Census population was 88,168, in 32,700 of 34,179 total private dwellings on 160.66 km² (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — the corridor's rural-canyon feel sits inside a large urban district, which is why its permit areas, not a village plan, govern the lots. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/index.cfm?Lang=E
