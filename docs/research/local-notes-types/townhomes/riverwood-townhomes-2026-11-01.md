## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold — none of the counted complexes is such a strata on its verified scale. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: the Riverwood (3075 Skeena Street) directory line "rentals not allowed" and the restriction element in Mountain View Estates' "rentals allowed with restrictions" line are both void for long-term rentals on exactly this rule. Port Coquitlam's own short-term-rental bylaw/licence position is UNVERIFIED — no City STR source was read in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No counted complex here is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3630) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Riverwood (§2).
- **City plan:** Port Coquitlam's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 3838**, which names Riverwood as an established residential neighbourhood; no Riverwood-specific major project was confirmed in the research pass. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2026-02/3838%20-%20Official%20Community%20Plan.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Port Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 (2008) governs: single-detached homes sit under RS1–RS4 zones (minimum lot widths of at least 15 m in RS1, 12 m in RS2, 30 m in RS3 and 9.5 m in RS4), and townhouse zones RTh1, RTh2 and RTh3 exist in the bylaw. The City permits 3 to 6 small-scale multi-unit housing units on RS-zoned lots without rezoning, depending on lot size, location and transit proximity. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/sites/default/files/2025-08/3630 - Zoning Bylaw 2008.pdf and https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/business-development/property-development-building/small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/riverwood.md)
