## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Aldergrove is a community in the Township of Langley; the Township's eastern boundary is 276 Street at Abbotsford and its southern boundary is the United States border. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- The Aldergrove Core Area is centred on Fraser Highway and 272 Street, bounded by Bertrand Creek to the north and east, 29 Avenue to the south and 270 Street to the west, per the Township plan; wider community boundaries are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: along 32 Avenue and its side streets (31A Avenue, 29A Avenue) in the core — the Township core plan describes the pre-Town Centre core as large-lot single-family homes with some townhouses and apartments including fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Transit from the core crossroads: TransLink route 503 (Aldergrove/Langley – Fraser Hwy Express) runs from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station, and BC Transit route 21 (Aldergrove Connector) links Aldergrove with Abbotsford; there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Aldergrove. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- SD35 schools physically in Aldergrove include Parkside Centennial Elementary (3300 270 St), Shortreed Community Elementary (27330 28 Ave), Betty Gilbert Middle School (26845 27 Ave) and Aldergrove Community Secondary (26850 29 Ave); a locator run for 26850 29 Ave returned Parkside Centennial, Betty Gilbert and Aldergrove Community Secondary, and catchments are address-based. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

All six verified complexes above are strata, so the strata block applies in full; the fee-simple paragraph that follows it applies to any non-strata townhouse a buyer cross-shops, none of which was verified here.

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In 1970s–1990s complexes like Aldergrove's, the Form B plus two years of minutes is where roof, envelope and plumbing history shows up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, with 30-year projections and at least three CRF funding models, and it cannot be deferred by a vote. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum.** Since November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving budgets. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID.** As of November 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw; stratas may still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw. Any directory or listing line for an Aldergrove complex saying "rentals not allowed" is void in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ only.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, the only legal age bylaw is 55+, in force as the only form since November 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (younger spouses/partners, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children); it restricts who may live in a unit, not who may own it. Creekside Villa is the one complex here a source states is 55+; any older "45+" wording for it cannot be a valid strata bylaw today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhouses — none verified here, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners-association obligations on title. Do not describe any Aldergrove complex above that way; all six verified complexes are strata.
- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs Aldergrove; zones named in Township documents for Aldergrove parcels include Rural Zone RU-2, Community Commercial Zone C-2 and Comprehensive Development Zone CD-192, and the bylaw's Section 110.1 ladder runs from R-1B/R-1C urban lots at a 464.5 m² minimum to SR-2 at 8,094 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **SSMUH in the Township: Zoning Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024)** amended Zoning Bylaw 2500 to enable 3–4 units on most lots previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings (about 55% of such lots, roughly 15,000 properties, per the Township). NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020 — a page claiming a Langley Township 6-plex tier is WRONG and would be flagged as a defect; no such claim is documented in this area's research file, so no defect is recorded here. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses in the Township:** the secondary-suite cap is 120 m², and a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never a strata lot. A house with a coach house is not townhouse stock and is not counted in Section 2. (Task brief, Township of Langley rules; bylaw: https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf)
- **Plans:** the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, and the Township's Official Community Plan update work includes an Aldergrove component. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **GST — FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for eligible first-time buyers with agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Aldergrove's townhouse stock is resale, so GST generally does not apply — the rebate matters only for a new-build cross-shop. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — New Housing Rebate.** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000, no rebate at $450,000 or more, for any new-home buyer who qualifies. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 of value (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. Township taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 3, 2026, and utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Presales — REDMA.** No townhouse presale in Aldergrove was verified in this pass. Where a presale exists anywhere in BC, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of the disclosure statement, and s.18 requires deposits to be held in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with the HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, rising to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are Aldergrove townhouses strata or fee-simple?** Every verified complex in this file is strata — Alderwood Manor, Creekside Estates, Creekside Villa, Twin Firs and Cedar Park Estates by strata plan, Bertrand Estates by listing wording. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified, so budget for strata fees and a Form B review on any named complex here. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Which complex is 55+?** Creekside Villa (27435 29A Ave, LMS361, 55 units, built 1992) is the one a public source states is 55+. No source checked states an age restriction for the other five complexes: age restriction UNCONFIRMED for each — check the filed bylaws. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/aldergrove-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: I keep seeing three "Creeksides" — which is which?** Creekside Estates (27125 31A Ave) is a townhouse strata (NWS2271); Creekside Villa (27435 29A Ave) is the separate 55+ rancher townhouse strata (LMS361); Creekside at Aldergrove Town Centre (27249 Aldergrove Town Centre Dr) is an apartment-condo building. A name-only search will mix all three. (Research file §5; condos file §2.)
- **Q: Can I rent out an Aldergrove townhouse?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022, so a strata here cannot ban long-term renting; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in a 55+ complex a tenant must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: These are 1970s–1990s complexes — what capital work should I expect?** Read the depreciation report (mandatory on a five-year cycle for 5+ lot stratas) and the minutes. Listings already surface the pattern: a Bertrand Estates listing notes roofs redone in recent years, and a Cedar Park Estates listing notes rain screening, windows, roof and siding work done about 10 years before that listing — exactly the items the next depreciation report schedules again. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3107203-102-27044-32-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v4w3t1-614999/brochure and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3117093-170-27456-32-avenue-langley-british-columbia-v4w3p3-569900/brochure
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Only where a public source states it: Cedar Park Estates' MLS sheet records cats OK, dogs OK, number limit two, with restrictions; Creekside Villa listings state 2 pets maximum with a shoulder-height limit. For the other complexes, published pet rules are UNCONFIRMED — the filed pet bylaw in the Form B package decides. https://www.cotala.com/tours/84636/FullPublic.pdf?i=1269626953
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in these older complexes?** Only what that strata's budget states — listing amenity lines for Alderwood Manor, for example, list trash, maintenance grounds, management, sewer and snow removal for the unit described. Your unit's fee, CRF balance and any approved levy are stated on its Form B, not in listing copy. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3086553-27090-32-avenue-24-langley-bc-v4w-3t7/brochure
- **Q: Is a coach house or a house with a suite a cheaper townhouse alternative?** It is a different tenure: in the Township a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 and a secondary suite is capped at 120 m² — neither is a strata townhouse, neither carries a Form B, and neither is counted in Section 2. (Task brief, Township of Langley rules.)
- **Q: How do I get to Surrey Central without a car?** The 503 Fraser Hwy Express from 272 Street at Fraser Highway to Surrey Central Station via Langley Centre and King George Station; TransLink's timetable (effective June 8, 2026) shows roughly 70 minutes end-to-end on early-morning trips, stretching to about 90 minutes at peak. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a 32 Avenue townhouse?** SD35 catchments are address-based; the locator run in the research file (for 26850 29 Ave) returned Parkside Centennial Elementary, Betty Gilbert Middle and Aldergrove Community Secondary. Run the exact townhouse address in the locator before relying on it. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Aldergrove's townhouse era predates its condo era by about 50 years.** The oldest verified townhouse complex here, Alderwood Manor, is recorded as built in 1971; the first Town Centre apartment building completed in 2025. A townhouse buyer is buying into mature, self- or professionally-managed small stratas — Alderwood Manor is self-managed under NWS46 — not into a new-build warranty world. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3086553-27090-32-avenue-24-langley-bc-v4w-3t7/brochure
- **The variant file is the same ground, not a second market.** Twin Firs and Cedar Park Estates reach this file from the `aldergrove-langley` variant research file; counting them in both an "Aldergrove" file and an "Aldergrove Langley" file would double-count one corridor's stock. They are counted once, here. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md, §5)
- **SSMUH is changing the streets around the complexes, not the complexes.** Township Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) enabled 3–4 units on most former single-family/duplex lots — an estimated 55% of such lots Township-wide — and in March 2026 the Township was advancing a form-and-character development permit area for SSMUH. Expect infill plexes beside the 32 Avenue stratas, not new townhouse complexes, as the next supply wave. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **The recreation centre is older than every new home in the core.** The Aldergrove Credit Union Community Centre's arena opened August 18, 2018, and its canopy-covered outdoor pools are open year-round — the amenity beside the townhouse corridor was operating years before the Town Centre completed. https://www.tol.ca/en/news/arena-opens-at-the-aldergrove-credit-union-community-centre.aspx
