### Named in sources but NOT counted — with the reason stated

- **Old Surrey zone codes in listing text:** "RF-12" and other RF/RH/RQ codes no longer exist in that form — R1–R7 replaced them July 8, 2024 (RF-12/RF-12C → R4, card shown as "Formerly RF-13, RF-12, RF-12C"; RF-SD → R6). ### Tenure traps — excluded

- **Manufactured-home park zones are NEVER townhouse stock:** Township MH-1 Residential Mobile Home Park Zone (minimum new lot 1.7 ha per its card), Surrey RM-M Manufactured Home Residential Zone, Abbotsford RSMH/RSH and RH1 (manufactured home park) zones are form zones for pad-rental parks — a home in one is bought on a rented pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not as a strata or fee-simple lot. Pad-rental parks are never counted in this program. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file as above.)
- **ALR land is not future townhouse land by zone alone:** strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6, and most subdivision of ALR land needs ALC approval, including parcels under 2 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Care/institutional uses on CD zoning are not townhomes:** care uses appear as institutional/community-care uses in specific zones and site-specific comprehensive-development (CD) zones; the operating-licence layer is provincial, not municipal zoning, and a CD card is not a purchasable complex. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html

### Presales — counted separately

- None counted here — topic files carry no presale count. Where a presale townhouse exists, it is counted separately (never mixed with completed stock) in its area file, under REDMA (§3).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B (strata townhomes, region-wide):** SPA s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This provincial layer sits on top of every zone in every city in the directory — zoning never substitutes for it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation/CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid in any municipality in this directory; any directory/listing "rentals not allowed" line for a strata townhouse is VOID in-entry. Short-term rentals are a separate layer: a strata can still restrict them by bylaw and municipalities regulate/licence them separately (Pitt Meadows prohibits STR accommodation in its Zoning Bylaw — see the Pitt Meadows files). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). There is no "55+ zone" in any bylaw on this page — age restriction is a strata-bylaw layer, not a zoning layer, and no municipal bylaw can restore a lower age threshold. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; municipal zoning (below, per city) plus any registered covenants/HOA govern instead. Which frame applies is decided by title/strata plan for the specific complex in its area file, never by its zone.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this page list townhouse complexes I can buy?** No — it is a zoning directory with an address-check tool, an ALR section and a buyer checklist. It carries no roster of its own (§2); complexes are verified and counted in the area townhomes files. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: My Surrey listing says RF-12 — what zone is that now?** R4: R1–R7 replaced RF/RH/RQ on July 8, 2024 (RF-12/RF-12C → R4; RF-SD → R6), and the directory cards show the former code so old text can be translated. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** It depends on the city and the land, and the regimes must not be mixed: Surrey 40% of habitable area; Langley City lesser of 100 m² and 40%; Township of Langley 120 m² cap; Abbotsford under 50% (A1 card states 100 m² as the page summarizes it); ALR — suite only inside the principal residence. (§3, per-city lines.)
- **Q: Is there a "55+ zone" to search in?** No. No municipality here zones for age; age restriction is a strata bylaw (only 55+ valid, SPA ss. 123.1/123.2) layered on an ordinary residential zone — a 55+ search is a bylaw-document search, not a zoning search. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What homes does the ALR allow on a farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m² (fill ≤1,000 m²), a suite only inside it, and one additional detached residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) without an ALC application — municipal zoning can limit further, and ALR land cannot be stratified into townhouses (Bulletin 09 s. 6). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata townhouse long-term, whatever city it is in?** Yes — strata rental bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022 province-wide. STRs are separate: strata bylaw plus municipal regulation (and, in Pitt Meadows, an outright zoning prohibition). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: We're considering a manufactured home — what does zoning tell us?** Only which park form is lawful where (Abbotsford RSMH/RSH/RH1, Township MH-1, Surrey RM-M). Your rights come from the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and park rules; a park zone says nothing about age restriction, and a pad-rental park is never townhouse stock. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: Can I rely on a zone card instead of checking with the municipality?** No — the page was checked October 2, 2026 per its header, bylaws amend constantly (Township SR-3B was added July 27, 2026), card-level detail beyond the research's consistency checks is UNVERIFIED, and one mid-page segment could not even be re-read for the research (429, stopped). Confirm zone, uses and ALR status per parcel with the municipality and the ALC. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file as above.)
- **Q: What do I check before buying acreage, in order?** The page's own six: ALR status (ALC map, then municipality); zone and minimum lot size (never assume subdivision — ALR subdivision needs ALC approval); how many homes are allowed (zoning and ALC both bite); septic records/inspection; water source (well yield/quality/licence vs municipal); farm class with BC Assessment, plus Premises ID if livestock/poultry are planned. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html
- **Q: My Delta research cites "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" for a townhouse area plan — is that right?** No — that attribution is a documented conflation: Delta's consolidated OCP Bylaw 8400 (2024) contains no lettered schedules; the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous Bylaw 3950. And Delta's zoning bylaw number itself is CONTESTED (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025") — flag it, never assert either as settled from this file. (Standing corrections.)
