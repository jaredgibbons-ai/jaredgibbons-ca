## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The governing fact for this sub-area: there is no townhouse strata stock to apply strata diligence to.**

- **Title first.** Most Salmon River properties are non-strata freehold acreages; where a strata parcel exists, BC strata law is the Strata Property Act, with Province guidance on gov.bc.ca and strata disputes handled by the Civil Resolution Tribunal. A buyer told a valley property is a "townhouse" should verify title form first — on the verified record here, it will be a freehold acreage house (count home §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **ALR stratification bar:** an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places — confirm a specific parcel on the ALC maps — and that bar is the structural reason townhouse strata do not appear on ALR valley land (count home §3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- **ALC residence caps:** principal residence at most 500 m²; suite within the principal residence only; one additional residence at most 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and at most 186 m² over 40 ha, subject to Township bylaws. **Farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. **No flat "$2,500+" line is documented in this slug's research, so no such defect is asserted for this page** (count home §3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
