## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The Delta frame below governs any townhouse purchase a buyer finds through this band, in whichever Tsawwassen sub-area the unit actually sits.

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — NO lettered schedules (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded Bylaw No. 3950; there is NO Schedule D.1 in Bylaw No. 8400, and the "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" conflation is an error wherever met). The OCP focuses commercial uses along 56th Street and in the Tsawwassen Urban Centre and carries the Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines; farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1), and Agricultural Land Reserve rules apply where land is in the ALR — ALR status is parcel-specific, checked on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas in Tsawwassen (OCP Bylaw No. 8400):** SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, where SD3 provides a 2.9 m Geodetic Survey of Canada datum floor level, a 2.3 m datum minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback — relevant to the Beach Grove / Boundary Beach stock that appears in this band's feed. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Flood protection:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — flood construction and insurance questions are address-specific for any low-lying listing in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** The research file cites Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 zone minimums (RS1/RS2/RS3 at 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD2/RD3 at 4,000 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; no RD5 zone), while a competing "8400, 2025" citation circulates in standing corrections — the number is flagged as contested and neither is asserted as settled; zoning is parcel-by-parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies — directly relevant to the suite/infill potential buyers price into detached homes in this band. SSMUH product is fee-simple in form unless separately strata-registered. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Strata rules (any strata townhouse bought through this band).** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59): discloses monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance, parking/storage allocations and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days of request. Since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata).** Every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — a directory "rentals not allowed" line for any complex a band buyer lands on is void in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 only a 55+ age bylaw is valid, with exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Tsawwassen's one verified fee-simple townhouse collection sits in Tsawwassen East (Southlands) in its sub-area file — its band status is UNCONFIRMED (§2), because a collection's existence says nothing about its current prices.
- **GST and rebates (new construction only).** Resale homes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax at this band — tier math shown, worked examples with inputs stated.** General PTT as published by the Province: 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A purchase inside this band (under $1.8M) reaches only the 1% and 2% tiers — the 3% tier and the further 2% do not apply at this band's prices. A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% on the residential portion — the tier math governs, not the compressed row. **Worked example 1 (top of band):** inputs — fair market value $1,800,000, no exemption. PTT = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. **Worked example 2 (mid-band townhouse):** inputs — fair market value $1,200,000, no exemption. PTT = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. **Worked example 3 (exemption reach):** inputs — fair market value $835,000, qualifying first-time buyer. The first-time buyers' program full exemption caps at $835,000 and removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000 on the Province's terms); the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — so only the lower part of this band can touch either exemption, and most of the band budgets full PTT. Run any exempt figure against the buyer's actual qualification before quoting it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant note for this band:** the Province states the grant threshold for 2026 is $2,075,000 — above this band's ceiling, so a principal residence purchased inside this band is under the phase-out threshold on value alone (eligibility rules still apply). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Buyer programs and mortgage insurance.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs, the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan. No CMHC defect is asserted for any property in this band — no file's own research documents one. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** Where a presale exists in Tsawwassen, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Presale prices in a feed are never resale band stock (§2). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **No SnapStats for this band — two separate reasons, both stated.** First, South Delta is not covered by SnapStats (its Delta coverage is North Delta only, a different community, never used here). Second, SnapStats publishes no price-band cut at any level — there is no SnapStats count, share or median for Tsawwassen homes under $1.8M, and none is stated or estimated anywhere in this file. SnapStats in any case reports attached product only as Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined — never a townhome-only figure.
