## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B diligence (strata stock — the queued townhouse candidates if/when counted):** SPA s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report; within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page. Form B does NOT exist for the Skynest/Yearling leasehold units — the lease and community rules are the diligence documents there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; sub-5-lot UNDER threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals:** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 for strata property. Caution specific to this area: the voiding is an SPA rule — how a rental term inside a First Nations leasehold community's documents is treated is a lease-document question, UNCONFIRMED here, never assumed from strata law. STR operations are regulated by the City under Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (City bylaws do not govern the First Nations lease lands). (Research file; condos sibling §3)
- **Age:** only 55+ lawful in a strata (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2; May 1, 2023 exemptions). - **City plans and zoning:** OCP Bylaw 2025, **No. 5500** — Sardis = **Schedule B5**. Zoning Bylaw 2020, **No. 5000**. **SSMUH Bylaw 5395 (Jun 18, 2024):** 3–4 units on eligible urban-growth-boundary parcels. **Suites:** only 1 ADU per lot; secondary suite lesser of 90 m² / 40%; garden suite 100 m² cap; no new suite below the flood construction level. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (research file)
