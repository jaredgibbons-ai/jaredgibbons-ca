## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases — Berkley Terraces and any Baden Park strata unit):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 2026 Baden Park unit, establish first whether it is a developer sale (disclosure statement route) or a resale (Form B route). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt — no counted complex exists here to apply it to, but both documented strata communities are well over five lots. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory/listing "rentals not allowed" / "no rentals except to immediate family" lines at Berkley Terraces are flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Blueridge complex is verified as 55+ — the 55-plus sibling records zero, and Berkley Terraces' "age restrictions" directory line does not by itself prove a valid 55+ bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (District Zoning Bylaw No. 3210) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here, so this rule is stated for the house form a Blueridge buyer will actually meet, not misapplied to a counted complex.
- **District plans and zoning — the DISTRICT pair only:** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7900 and Zoning Bylaw No. 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799): R1 minimum lot 4,000 m² (30 m width, 34 m depth); R2 minimum lot 660 m² (18 m width, 34 m depth), with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 lot allows a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house. The Seymour Local Plan maintains Blueridge's predominantly single-family character. The City's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **SSMUH / suites as the District states them:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. A suite or coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a house lot — never a townhouse complex. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Hazard permits — the Berkley-side check:** District landslide work names the Berkley escarpment as an area with landslide risk; after the January 19, 2005 landslide on the Berkley Escarpment the District began proactive District-wide hazard management, and its Landslide Risk Assessment was updated in 2026. OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard, and the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. Berkley Road addresses carry this check. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **District taxes:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value; combined single-family utilities are $2,571.20 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/blueridge-nv.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (a developer-held Baden Park unit), not resales. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here — it is a City of Vancouver tax only, at 3%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any remaining developer-contract purchase at Baden Park. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
