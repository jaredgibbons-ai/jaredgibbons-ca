## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the strata product that does exist here — the three apartment buildings and Cottage Grove's ranchers).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. It has no townhouse complex to attach to in this sub-area (§2), but it is the central document for the strata product that does exist here. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — no such strata is verified here. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income. The Vibe's directory line reads "rentals allowed," consistent with current law; no counted entry in this file carries a "rentals not allowed" line, and any older directory or bylaw text stating one for a Proper South strata is void on exactly this rule (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). Elmwood Terrace is presented as 55+ in its listings and Cottage Grove is verified 55+ in the 55-plus program — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B for either before treating a unit as generally occupiable; Hazelwood Grove's age position is UNCONFIRMED (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).

**Q: Can a house here have a suite AND a coach house?**
A: No. Only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Bylaw 5000. Separately, SSMUH (Bylaw 5395) allows 3–4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary (§3).

**Q: Is flood a subject-removal item here?**
A: Yes. New suites are not permitted below the flood construction level, flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw (RS-FHA reserve zone, Section 12 of Bylaw 5000), and the guide frames habitable floor space against the Flood Construction Level plus freeboard under the City's floodplain bylaw — confirm the parcel's own flood construction level and floodplain position with the City before removing subjects, for strata and detached purchases alike (§§1, 3).

**Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?**
A: Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026 (§3).
