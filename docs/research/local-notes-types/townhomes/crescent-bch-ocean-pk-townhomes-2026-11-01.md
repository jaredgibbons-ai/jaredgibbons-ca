## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies in full to the queued strata candidates in §2.1 if/when they are counted, and to Ocean Park Place as strata apartment stock).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §§2.1 and 2.3: every "rentals not allowed" line recorded for Ocean Park Village, Ocean Park Place and one Courtyards directory page is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). Ocean Park Place's and Ocean Park Grove's 55+ positions are listing text only and UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read (§§1, 2.1, 2.3) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is documented on this ground (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only.
