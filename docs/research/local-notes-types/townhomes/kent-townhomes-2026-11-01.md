## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Harrison Ridge, the Form B is also the document that settles the "FREEHOLD" listing shorthand against the strata record (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any older Kent strata bylaw or directory line purporting to restrict long-term rentals is void as a restriction — rentals are allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Kent strata is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw — the District's verified 55+ ownership count is zero, in Agassiz and at Mount Woodside alike. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Freehold dominates the District on that basis (detached and farm properties); its townhouse product is strata (§2), and Briarwood shows the middle case a buyer must not misread: detached-form homes that still carry strata fees and strata documents.
- **District plan and zoning:** the plan is the **District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025**, which directs most growth into the Agassiz townsite with Mount Woodside as a satellite growth village, under OCP designations Agricultural, Residential, Commercial, Industrial, Institutional, Parks/Recreation/Open Space and Resource Management; zoning is the District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended). SSMUH: Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone allowing up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots — and the District's own guide is explicit that there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands, so rural Kent lots do not get the four-unit option. Secondary suites are permitted in single-family homes in all residential zones. The District's Interim Housing Needs Report (2024) found 495 new homes needed immediately and 1,695 more by 2041. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/kent-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Flood, dikes and drainage — the District's standing frame:** the District's flat lands are Fraser/Harrison floodplain protected by dikes (Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5); the District maintains 135 km of drainage ditches and supports Fraser River gravel removal to reduce flood risk; the Province's Lower Mainland dike inventory covers Kent on map sheet 28 (dike infrastructure, pump stations and flood boxes regulated under the Dike Maintenance Act), and named works include the Hammersley Pump Station about 6 km west of Agassiz. Riparian and steep-slope/geotechnical hazards sit in OCP Sections 5.2–5.3 — the slope policies are the Mount Woodside diligence, the dike policies are the flat-townsite diligence. Parcel-level flood construction levels: UNVERIFIED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/drought-flooding-dikes-dams/integrated-flood-hazard-management/governance/dike-management/dike-inventory (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **District taxes — diking is its own line:** tax notices are mailed in early June and are **due July 15** (or the next business day if the 15th falls on a weekend), after which late-payment penalties apply; rates must be set before May 15 each year. The 2026 Tax Rates Summary carries a dedicated "Dyking" column charged as a rate per $1,000 of taxable value — 0.03076 for Class 1 Residential and 0.29356 for Class 9 Farm — on top of a total municipal rate of 2.80493 (Residential) and an all-in Residential total of 4.65328 including Hospital, Regional District, MFA, BC Assessment and School Tax columns. Diking is an assessment-based rate, not a flat per-parcel charge. Properties also carry FVRD levies. https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/kent.md)
- **ALR frame:** agriculture is a headline OCP priority (§4.2) and most of the District's flat land outside the townsite is farmed (§1 figures, two measurement bases). ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — a structural reason ALR farm parcels cannot become townhouse stock. ALC caps where a farm residence is in issue: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm home-plate and ALC rules apply on top of District zoning on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); the condos sibling for this area carries the same March 13 date (the March 12 figure in some secondary sources does not govern); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — directly relevant to Harrison Ridge's newer phases. **CMHC page defect:** this slug's own research does NOT document a live-page CMHC premium range, so no page error is asserted for this slug — the published CMHC range is stated here as 0.6%–4.5% only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

(§2 above)
- **Q: Is Harrison Ridge a 55+ building?** No verified age bylaw exists for it. It is documented in §2 as its own form, not counted as a townhouse, which also resolves the form question the condos sibling left UNCONFIRMED. (§2 above)
- **Q: What will a Harrison Ridge Phase 4 presale involve?** When the developer opens it (the site says "coming soon"; nothing is counted yet): a REDMA disclosure statement before signing, deposits held in trust under s. 18, a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, and 5% GST on the new construction — with the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) potentially applying to an eligible first-time buyer. (§2 and §3 above)
- **Q: What does the diking line on a Kent tax notice mean?** Diking is a standing, assessment-based cost of owning floodplain land here, not a one-off levy: the 2026 Residential diking rate is 0.03076 per $1,000 of taxable value, inside an all-in Residential total of 4.65328. Notices are mailed in early June and due July 15. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a Kent farm parcel be stratified into townhouses?** No. An ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — and roughly half the District's non-forest, non-Crown land base is ALR. (§3 above)
- **Q: How many homes can a Kent house lot hold now?** Up to four dwelling units on single-dwelling lots in the RS1 zone under Bylaw No. 1737 (adopted June 10, 2024) — but the District confirms there are no RS1 properties outside Agassiz and Harrison Highlands, so rural Kent lots do not get the four-unit option. Secondary suites are permitted in single-family homes in all residential zones. (§3 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Kent = the legal name; Agassiz = the market name.** Searches, schools and daily life say Agassiz; taxes, bylaws, permits and the OCP say District of Kent. A buyer who searches only one name misses half the record — including which file in this program counts the townhouses. (§1 and §2 above)
- **Two ALR percentages for the same District are both "right."** The OCP's approximately 50% excludes forest reserves, Crown land, roads and highways from the denominator; the Ministry of Agriculture inventory's 35% uses its own 18,776 ha study basis. Quoting either without its basis misstates the District's farm share. (§1 above)
- **Mount Woodside plays by slope rules; the townsite plays by dike rules.** OCP Sections 5.2–5.3 (riparian, steep-slope, geotechnical) govern the hillside pocket where Harrison Highlands sits, while Section 5.5 flood/diking policy governs the flat townsite — a Kent buyer's hazard diligence flips depending on which pocket the home is in. (§3 above)
- **The "FREEHOLD townhome" at Harrison Ridge is a strata purchase.** Listing shorthand and the strata record conflict on the counted complex; this file follows the EPS plan record and the directory's strata listing, and the Form B — not the listing adjective — is what a buyer should price and insure against. (§2 above)
- **Harrison Highlands is in Kent, not Harrison Hot Springs.** The master plan's name points at the neighbouring village, but the land, taxes and OCP are the District of Kent's — and its townhouse count lives in this Kent file, not in the Harrison Hot Springs file. (§2 above)
- **The District's newest recreation asset opened in fall 2026.** The Lets'emot Regional Recreation and Aquatic Centre (27,000 sq ft attached to the CRCC at 6660 Pioneer Avenue) had its grand opening set for September 25, 2026 — and despite the name, there is no FVRD "Lets'emot Regional Park"; the name belongs to the aquatic centre. (§1 above)
- **Diking is priced into Kent ownership every year, visibly.** Unlike most municipalities, Kent prints diking as its own tax-rate column (2026 Residential 0.03076 per $1,000) — a buyer can see the floodplain cost on the notice instead of discovering it in a levy letter. (§3 above)
