## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section exists on a zero-stock corridor:** there is no counted strata complex for the strata rules to attach to today. They are stated because (a) a buyer who widens the search to a strata townhouse in Central Lonsdale or Lynn Valley needs the diligence frame, and (b) the corridor house purchase that is this area's actual product is governed by the fee-simple and two-municipality rules that follow — which side of Highway 1 the lot sits on changes its minimums, taxes and permit areas entirely.
- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase off-corridor):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It does NOT apply to Chesterfield Manor (share purchase, excluded) or to a corridor house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum (any strata purchase off-corridor):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID / age bylaws:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw), and only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No directory "rentals not allowed" line exists to flag in this file because no corridor complex is verified — the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ product of any tenure on this corridor. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple houses (the corridor's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, the municipal rules of the side the lot is actually in (below) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Grand Boulevard is additionally a City heritage character area on the City side — frontage and character rules, not just zoning, shape what can be built facing the boulevard. http://www.cnv.org/~/media/7BBC365465654E02B81F82788C2E01DC.pdf
- **City side — plans and zoning (south of Highway 1):** City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2014, amended December 8, 2025) and Zoning Bylaw No. 6700. The Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, adopted December 8, 2025, allows a lot of 280 m² or less up to 3 units, and a lot over 280 m² up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations — raising permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 properties. GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m corner) and 15.0 m in other designations. Never quote these numbers for a District-side lot. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **District side — plans and zoning (north of Highway 1):** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799): R1 minimums 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth, R2 minimums 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 lot may hold a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite and a coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary. Never quote these numbers for a City-side lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **SSMUH as each municipality states it:** City side — the GO Zone unit counts above (3 / 4 / 6 by lot size and designation) ARE the City's small-scale multi-unit framework for corridor lots; District side — principal dwelling plus secondary suite and coach house on an R2 lot, three units total on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. There is no single "Boulevard SSMUH rule" — the municipality decides. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Hazards — City side:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and a development permit is required before new development there. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Hazards — District side:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas for Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard, any of which can apply on the District portion toward Lynn Valley. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **Tax timing — both municipalities, different rates:** City side — 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. District side — 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000, and single-family utility fees total $2,571.20 in 2026. Confirm the municipality on title before comparing either figure. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no verified presale exists on the corridor, so there is nothing for the REDMA frame to attach to in this file; for completeness, a presale buyer elsewhere must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

- **Q: Is Chesterfield Manor a cheap Boulevard townhouse alternative?** It is neither Boulevard nor a townhouse nor a strata: Chesterfield Manor (1345 Chesterfield Ave) is a Central Lonsdale corporation share purchase, excluded program-wide, with different financing (a listing states 35% down) and no Form B. - **Q: A search site shows "Grand Boulevard condos" — are they on the boulevard?** On the sibling record, no: rental aggregators file Central Lonsdale towers under "Grand Boulevard," and those buildings are a different area, excluded from the corridor count. - **Q: What replaces Form B diligence for a corridor house purchase?** Title, the correct municipality's zoning and permit-area checks, and any registered covenants — a corridor house is fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. The strata document set only enters the picture if the search widens off-corridor. Basis: Section 3 above.
- **Q: What can a corridor house lot hold instead of a townhouse?** By side: City GO Zone — up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, up to 4 or 6 units on a larger lot depending on the OCP designation; District R2 — a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite and a coach house. Never quote one side's numbers for the other. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a corridor address?** Queensbury Elementary (2020 Moody Avenue) and Ridgeway Elementary (420 East 8th Street) sit beside the corridor and Sutherland Secondary (1860 Sutherland Avenue) is the secondary school west of it — but catchments are address-based, the locator was not run for this file, so any specific assignment is UNVERIFIED, and the 2026/27 Cloverley catchment review may move Ridgeway and Queensbury addresses. (For Lynnmour / Lynn Valley addresses further north-east, the program's standing correction is Windsor Secondary, never Sutherland.) https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
- **Q: Does the heritage status change a rebuild on the boulevard?** On the City side, yes: Grand Boulevard is a City heritage character area in the Green Necklace system, so a boulevard-facing rebuild carries character expectations a side-street lot does not — price the heritage frontage, not just the lot. http://www.cnv.org/~/media/7BBC365465654E02B81F82788C2E01DC.pdf
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 240 runs on the corridor's own streets (Boulevard Crescent, Grand Boulevard East) straight to downtown Vancouver via 15th Street, Keith Road, Marine Drive and the Lions Gate Bridge — a detached-house street with a one-seat downtown bus. Route 230 on Lonsdale links to the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront). https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt240.pdf
- **Q: Are there creek or slope permit issues on the District-side streets?** Possibly: District OCP Schedule B sets Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Areas that can apply toward Lynn Valley; on the City side the test is the Streamside DPA (15 m watercourse / 10 m ravine). Run the check that matches the municipality. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **One street name, two city halls.** The same "Boulevard" search returns two tax bills (City total residential 3.34028 vs District 3.28195 per $1,000 for 2026), two zoning bylaws (City 6700 vs District 3210), two OCPs (City 8400 vs District 7900) and two hazard-permit regimes — split by Highway 1, with the exact split point UNVERIFIED in the research file. No other sub-area in this program changes rulebooks mid-street. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf
- **The boulevard was a priced-to-exclude garden suburb.** The Canadian Register of Historic Places records Grand Boulevard as North Vancouver's most prominent garden subdivision, planned from 1906 by the North Vancouver Land and Improvement Company, with early building restrictions including a provision that residences must cost more than $4,000 to construct to keep the area prestigious — the wide lots facing the median are the surviving form of that scheme. https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=2425
- **The park median is 105 metres wide and 1.6 kilometres long.** The Register describes the central garden boulevard between the two sides of the roadway at that scale, facing mansions and bungalows with views of Burrard Inlet, the mountains and downtown Vancouver — the frontage a corridor buyer is actually paying for. https://www.historicplaces.ca/fr/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=2425
- **The City rewrote the corridor's density on December 8, 2025.** The GO Zone adoption that day raised permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties and took the larger-lot ceiling to 6 units in the higher designations — a City-side fact from the research file that no District-side lot shares. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **The two municipalities are different sizes entirely.** The City's 2021 Census population was 58,120 on 11.83 km²; the District's was 88,168 (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census, via the City census backgrounder and the Focus on Geography Series) — the compact city core and the sprawling district meet on this one corridor. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/About-the-City/2021-census-Population-and-Dwelling.pdf and https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/as-sa/fogs-spg/page.cfm?topic=1&dguid=2021A00055915055&lang=e
- **Care and library anchors sit west of the corridor, not on it.** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street (Vancouver Coastal Health), and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre, 221 West Esplanade, serve the area from the west side; the North Vancouver City Library is at 120 West 14th Street. A library branch inside the sub-area: UNVERIFIED; a grocery store name and address inside the sub-area on an official source: UNVERIFIED (Park & Tilford Shopping Centre is named by the City east of the corridor, across from the Moodyville side). https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
