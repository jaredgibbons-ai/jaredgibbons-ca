## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the Princess Crescent stratas, and any strata purchase compared nearby):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. All four Princess Crescent complexes date from 1993–1995, so the depreciation report is the first document to read in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. None of the four Princess Crescent complexes' current rental bylaws was confirmed on a citable source in any sibling pass (UNCONFIRMED, Section 2) — any directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets on one of them is flagged VOID for long-term rentals under this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Princess Gate's published "55+" description operates under this rule — confirm the filed bylaw and its exemption wording on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the detached streets):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in this sub-area.
- **Coquitlam zoning and plan — standing corrections applied:** Zoning Bylaw **No. 3000, 1996** governs — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² and 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² and 12.5 m (R-2). The City adopted an **Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Bylaws 5511, 2026 and 5512, 2026), superseding the Citywide OCP No. 3479, 2001** — any Canyon Springs land-use claim sourced to the old OCP is out of date. SSMUH as the City states it: up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Watercourse rules reach the cluster's edge lots: a Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area within 30 m of the top of a watercourse or ravine bank, Zoning Bylaw s. 519 flood protection setbacks and s. 523 riparian setbacks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the Princess Crescent stock is 1993–1995 resale stock. Coquitlam property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered (a buyer may compare North Coquitlam or Burke Mountain product), the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
