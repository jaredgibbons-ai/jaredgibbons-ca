### Worked examples — inputs stated, illustrative arithmetic only (not market facts, not band counts)

- **EXAMPLE 1 — PTT at the band floor (inputs stated).** Inputs: purchase price $1,800,000; resale detached home; fully residential; no exemption (none reaches this band — see Section 3). Gross PTT: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,600,000 = $32,000, total **$34,000**. No portion reaches the 3% tier, and the further 2% does not arise. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **EXAMPLE 2 — PTT above $3M (inputs stated).** Inputs: purchase price $3,500,000; resale; fully residential (the further 2% applies to the whole price on this input). Gross PTT: $2,000 + (2% × $1,800,000 = $36,000) + (3% × $1,500,000 = $45,000) = $83,000, plus the further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000: 2% × $500,000 = $10,000. Total **$93,000**. The example shows why a single "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression and is WRONG as a rate: the 3% tier and the further 2% tax different portions. On a mixed-class or farm-classed property the further 2% applies only to the residential portion — and up to 0.5 ha of farm-classed dwelling land is treated as residential — so the same price can produce a different total; model the actual class mix before offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **EXAMPLE 3 — home owner grant straddle (inputs stated).** Inputs: two otherwise similar principal residences, one assessed at $2,075,000, one at $2,189,000. At or below the 2026 threshold of $2,075,000 the full regular grant applies; above it the grant is reduced by $5 for each $1,000 of assessed value over the threshold, and over $2,189,000 the regular grant is $0. Many homes in this band will receive a reduced grant or none, even as principal residences. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The townhouse rules below are carried because a townhouse buyer can land in this band's search by mistake — and because the band's own stock (detached houses and acreages, with the occasional strata form) is diligenced under the same provincial frame. Where the band's stock is detached/acreage, the parcel rules govern; where any unit is strata, the Form B frame governs.

- **Form B diligence (any strata unit in or near this band):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 — and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses to apply one to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple home in this band (the band's normal form) carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019) and any registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly, plus the parcel checks below. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **City zoning — the standard urban house zone:** RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) under Zoning Bylaw No. **7600-2019** s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%; accessory uses include a secondary suite inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area. **SSMUH:** eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws — a development-value question on any large urban lot in this band, settled lot by lot, not by the listing price. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **City plan:** the OCP citation is **CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024)** — flagged, never resolved by guessing. Growth is planned area by area: the City's OCP updates and area plans for the Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley and Albion were adopted in fall 2025 to align with provincial housing legislation — the Silver Valley Area Plan modernizes the 2002 plan and introduces a River Village Mixed-Use designation, and the **Albion Area Plan (adopted October 28, 2025)** creates buffers where urban development meets farmland. The **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — cited as adopted; no contrary reading is documented in this file's own research file. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan and https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Program standing corrections, 2026-11-01)
- **Floodplain — a must-check for river-edge estates:** Fraser River frontage sits in a floodplain where the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Escarpment — a must-check on the south-western slopes:** in the Fraser River Escarpment policy area (bounded by 207 Street west, 124 Avenue north, 224 Street east and the escarpment crest along the south), properties within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit, and properties within 100 m of the crest require a detailed geotechnical report addressing potential seismic slope failure. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Watercourse and ALR:** Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse running through the Cottonwood / Albion side of the city, protected by Kanaka Creek Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) — creek-adjacent acreages carry parcel-specific watercourse and development-permit questions. ALR status is parcel-specific: the Agricultural Land Commission's own site directs owners and buyers to its maps and online mapping tools to answer "Is my property in the ALR?" — no area-wide ALR statement is made for any acreage in this band without a parcel check. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Property taxes (Maple Ridge):** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **PTT at this band:** general tiers 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000 — every purchase in this band reaches into the 2% tier almost entirely, and purchases over $2,000,000 reach the 3% tier on the top portion — plus, if a property has residential property worth over $3,000,000, a further 2% on the residential property value greater than $3,000,000 (only the residential portion on a mixed-class property; up to 0.5 ha of farm-classed dwelling land treated as residential). A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% tier and the further 2% into one rate and is flagged wherever it appears. PTT is based on fair market value on the day of registration at the Land Title Office and is not annual property tax. Additional PTT for a foreign national, foreign corporation or taxable trustee applies on the residential portion in a specified area; the rate is published on a separate provincial page not re-read for this research file, so the rate is UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band:** the first-time home buyers' program full exemption caps at a fair market value of $835,000 — far below this page's floor — so buyers in this band budget full PTT (EXAMPLES 1–2, Section 2). The newly built home exemption's thresholds were not re-read for this research file: UNVERIFIED here (the series frame — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — is carried in the first-time-buyer calculator files on disk, and even at that frame it cannot reach a $1.8M floor). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Federal frame at this band:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) phases out entirely by $1.5M and so pays $0 on a new build at this band's floor; the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) is likewise out of range. HBP $60,000 and FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year are savings-program facts, not price relief at this level. CMHC premiums (0.6%–4.5%) do not arise on an insured purchase here: mortgage insurance is unavailable at $1.5M or more, so a band purchase is uninsured with 20% or more down (see the mortgage-calculator townhomes file on disk). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a home in this band is a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
