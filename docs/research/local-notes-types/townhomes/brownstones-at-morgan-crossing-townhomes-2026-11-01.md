## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated in full; it governs a Brownstones purchase if the queued listing evidence — Freehold Strata — is confirmed on title and the Form B).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, bylaws, minutes and depreciation report, and to confirm the current fee and what it covers (research file §6; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. The page-stated 98 units would put this complex well over the threshold — but the count is UNCONFIRMED (§2.1), so the position is read off the strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §2.1: to the extent the directory's "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line describes a long-term rental restriction, it is void. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). No age bylaw is verified for Brownstones (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
