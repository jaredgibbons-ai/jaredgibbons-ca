# Altamont, West Vancouver — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Altamont is a residential hillside area of the District of West Vancouver, above Marine Drive between Dundarave to the east and Caulfeild to the west; District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- "Altamont" is the name used on jaredgibbons.ca; an official District neighbourhood or planning-area name "Altamont" is NOT CONFIRMED — the name does not appear as a named neighbourhood in the District's OCP, whose commercial structure is the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, the Park Royal regional shopping centre, and the village centres of Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay. Paragon (GVR) sub-area match and code: UNVERIFIED. Street-by-street boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes them; the area is generally the hillside above the Marine Drive / Queens Avenue corridor below Dundarave and the British Properties edge. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Nearby areas: Dundarave (east), Caulfeild (west), the British Properties (north, uphill), Ambleside (south-east) and Chelsea Park (north-west, uphill). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Main roads: Marine Drive is the east–west waterfront route below the area; Queens Avenue climbs the hillside; Taylor Way connects Marine Drive to Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway), and the Lions Gate Bridge is the fixed crossing to Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Transit: route 251 (Queens / Park Royal), operated as West Vancouver Blue Bus, serves the Queens Avenue corridor below the hillside, running from Marine Drive at 25th Street to Park Royal via 27th Street, Queens Avenue, 15th Street, Mathers Avenue, Burley Drive, Kings Avenue, 11th Street, Fulton Avenue and 17th Street; route 250 runs on Marine Drive between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver via Georgia Street, the Stanley Park Causeway and the Lions Gate Bridge. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Schools: School District 45 catchments are address-based and SD45 directs families to the school locator; no sample address inside Altamont could be taken from an official source, so the catchment for Altamont addresses is UNVERIFIED — check each address in the locator. SD45 has no middle tier — elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12. Nearby: Irwin Park Elementary (K–7), 2455 Haywood Avenue, in Dundarave; Westcot Elementary (K–7) at the foot of the British Properties; West Vancouver Secondary, 1750 Mathers Avenue. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf

### What the stock actually is

- Detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, with secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among the permitted uses in the single-family zones. A suite or coach house inside or beside a detached house is a fee-simple second unit, not a townhouse complex, and is never counted as townhouse stock. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- Apartment condos inside Altamont: also zero verified — see the sibling condos file on disk. The District's real apartment stock is in Ambleside and Dundarave, not on this hillside.
- **Villa Maris ("Pink Palace") — Dundarave waterfront, below and east of the hillside — documented here only to prevent a mis-file.** The District's 2024 Statement of Heritage Value describes it as an iconic 101-unit apartment building, built 1965, and lists continuous use as rental residential high-rise among its character-defining elements; it is purpose-built single-owner RENTAL in Dundarave, not Altamont and not strata stock — a tenure trap, never counted in any townhomes file. Full entry is in the Dundarave condos sibling file on disk. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf
- No co-operative, life-lease, non-market rental or pad-rental townhouse complex was verified inside Altamont — UNCONFIRMED as categories here, and none is counted. No presale townhouse project inside Altamont was verified; District major projects are listed by the District at its major-projects page, and no approved major project inside Altamont is confirmed on a District source. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The governing pair is West Vancouver's own:** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, District of West Vancouver. White Rock's 2506/2220 pair is never attributed to West Vancouver. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **If a strata townhouse is ever found on this hillside, the BC standard block applies in full:** Form B diligence (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws:** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a West Vancouver strata is void for long-term rentals (short-term rentals can still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws are valid since Nov 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Altamont complex exists to apply either rule to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality in Altamont:** the verified stock is fee-simple detached — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — governed by municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662) and any registered covenants on title only. Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are permitted uses in the single-family zones, subject to the bylaw's conditions. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Zoning numbers:** Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record; the ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. The zone for a specific lot is on the District's Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Provincial density directive:** on July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025 — Altamont sits directly above Dundarave, so infill rules on its lower streets are in transition and must be verified per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Hazards:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both can apply on Altamont's hillside and creek lots; a Foreshore Development Permit can apply to the lowest streets below the hillside. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Buyer frame:** PTT + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No presale townhouse exists in Altamont to apply REDMA s. 21 (7-day rescission) or s. 18 (deposits in trust) to today. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply in West Vancouver and is omitted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **District taxes:** West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026. At Altamont price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy for Altamont, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the research file for altamont, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses in Altamont?** None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes on any source checked. The area is detached houses in the RS single-family zones. A "townhouse" search result carrying an Altamont label should be checked for whether the property is actually in Ambleside, Dundarave or Caulfeild. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real townhouse stock?** In the District's centres and pockets documented in their own files — Caulfeild Cove in Caulfeild, the Whitby Estates pockets inside Chartwell, and the townhome components inside The Bellevue by Cressey (Dundarave) and Hawksley (British Properties) — each counted once in its home sub-area file and assembled in the West Vancouver city townhomes file. The Altamont hillside itself contributes none. (Sibling files on disk, Section 2 cross-references.)
- **Q: Is a coach house the same as a townhouse here?** No. A detached secondary suite (coach house) is a permitted second unit on a fee-simple detached lot under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662 — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. It is not a townhouse complex and is never counted as one. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I build multiple homes on an Altamont lot?** The bylaw's ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — and the Province's July 21, 2025 directive targets the Dundarave-adjacent single-family/duplex areas for increased density. What applies to a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until the current zoning and the District's response are checked for that lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Q: Which schools will my kids attend?** UNVERIFIED for any Altamont address in the sources read — SD45 catchments are address-based, there is no middle tier (K–7 elementary, 8–12 secondary), and the nearest named schools are Irwin Park Elementary in Dundarave and Westcot Elementary at the foot of the British Properties. Run the exact address in the SD45 locator. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: What permits can surprise me on a hillside rebuild?** A Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface and an Environmental Development Permit within 15 metres of a watercourse bank — both live possibilities on Altamont lots, and both add geotechnical and permit cost. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is the big pink waterfront building a condo I can buy?** No. Villa Maris is in Dundarave, below the hillside, and the District's 2024 Statement of Heritage Value records continuous use as a rental residential high-rise — purpose-built rental, nothing in the existing building to buy. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 251 on Queens Avenue below the hillside and route 250 on Marine Drive (Horseshoe Bay–Dundarave–Park Royal–downtown via the Lions Gate Bridge). Most Altamont streets are a hill walk or a drive from either route. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt251.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- **Q: What extra tax applies at Altamont prices?** The Province's additional school tax where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million — 0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising January 1, 2027. Budget it in closing math. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Altamont is not an official District name.** The OCP's structure is Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild Village and Horseshoe Bay — "Altamont" is a site/listing name for the hillside between Dundarave and Caulfeild, with no published street boundaries. Two agents can draw its lines differently and both be working from the same absence. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **The seawalk below the hillside dates to Canada's centennial.** The Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 and runs roughly 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park to Ambleside, below the Altamont hillside; Dundarave Park, 150 25th Street, has more than 50 metres of sandy beach, a playground, washrooms and a seasonal concession. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- **The District's main indoor facilities are in Ambleside, not on the hill:** the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive, and the West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive — the library opened as a War Memorial, dedicated to those who served in the Second World War. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre and https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library
- **The hospital for the area is across the municipal line:** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver (Vancouver Coastal Health), with the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/north-vancouver-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **West Vancouver's 2021 Census population was 44,122** (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — District context for a hillside pocket with no centre of its own; shopping inside Altamont itself is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed on a District source), and Park Royal at Marine Drive and Taylor Way is the OCP-recognised regional shopping centre. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/search-recherche/results-resultats.cfm?Lang=E&SearchText=Vancouver and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **Utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District** — a carrying-cost difference from flat-fee municipalities that hillside buyers rarely budget for. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
