There is no bus service on the island itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/barnston-island.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the strata rules that would govern a townhouse purchase if stock existed or could be built, and the Electoral Area A / ALR / rural rules that explain the stock that actually exists here.

- **Form B diligence (any strata, if one ever surfaces here):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No strata exists on Barnston Island in any source checked, so no Form B exists to request for an island property — a seller offering "strata documents" for an island parcel is a red flag to verify before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Stated for completeness of the provincial frame; neither rule has an island strata to attach to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any Barnston complex in §2 — there is no complex and no line to flag in-entry; the rule is stated so a future island strata listing's stale line is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

No island building is published as age-restricted, because no island strata building was verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** The island's actual stock is fee-simple farm parcels: NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees on its farmhouses; Metro Vancouver's Electoral Area A OCP/zoning plus ALR rules and any registered covenants govern instead. A buyer importing strata expectations — or a seller marketing a farm parcel as a "townhouse alternative" — is misdescribing the tenure either way.
- **Planning authority: Metro Vancouver, not a municipality.** The Electoral Area A Official Community Plan (2018) sets the vision of Barnston Island as a thriving agricultural community and expressly does not apply to First Nations Reserves; Metro Vancouver — not a municipality — is the planning authority for the non-reserve lands, and also holds the zoning for Electoral Area A, including a Barnston Island zoning map (Map 12). - **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that decides this whole file:** strata plans in the ALR cause subdivision per ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6; under Strata Property Act s. 239 and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1), ALR land cannot be stratified in the ordinary course. Combined with the island's agricultural OCP direction, that is why there is no townhouse product here and none can be assumed as future supply. ALR housing frame: one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², a secondary suite is permitted only when located within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses on Barnston Island?** None verified — 0 strata, 0 fee-simple, and the zero is structural: no strata product of any kind was identified on the island, the market stock is freehold farm parcels plus Katzie IR 3 reserve land that is not market housing, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)).
