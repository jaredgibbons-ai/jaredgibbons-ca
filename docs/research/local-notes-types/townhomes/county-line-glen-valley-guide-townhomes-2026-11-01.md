## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Why there is no townhouse stock here is a rules story first — the ALR frame governs this sub-area's acreage.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** On land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, a residence cannot be turned into strata lots (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). The ALR frame: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it. Whether any specific parcel in the feed is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — the full three-tier thresholds.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm-gate prices: $2,500 where the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha where the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is less than 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears. **No flat "$2,500+" line is documented in this slug's research, so no such defect is asserted for this page** — the full form is stated so it is never compressed here (count home §3; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

**Township of Langley frame — Township rules throughout; Abbotsford rules are never imported.**

- **Zoning / plans.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. The Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025) — it does not govern this rural sub-area, but the citation discipline matters program-wide: Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 stand at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026; any page or research file presenting June 15, 2026 repeals by Bylaws 6202/6203 as done is WRONG — Township records support only the directed hearing that day. **No such claim is documented in this slug's research, so it is carried as the standing correction only, not asserted as a defect of this page** (count home §3; https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx).
- **SSMUH — stated so it is not misapplied here.** The Township adopted Bylaw No. 6020 on November 18, 2024, allowing 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — a page claiming a 6-plex tier would be WRONG, but **this file's own research does not document any such claim for a County Line Glen Valley page, so no defect is asserted in this file**. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **Secondary suites (Township).** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with Agricultural Land Commission regulations. **Live conflict, flagged, not resolved:** the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses and describes suites as within detached single-family dwellings, while the current s. 102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source (research file §4).
- **Coach houses (Township).** In Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata. No size cap for a coach house appears in s. 100 as read: a coach-house size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file. Abbotsford's coach/garden rule does NOT apply in this Township sub-area and is never imported (research file §4).
