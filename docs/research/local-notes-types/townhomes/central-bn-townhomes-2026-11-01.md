## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. Because each of the four complexes is a separate strata corporation, each requires its own Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. On published counts this captures Smith + Norfolk (16 lots) and the 3058 Smith Avenue complex (7 lots); Parkside Greene's and Norfolk Terrace's lot counts are UNCONFIRMED, so their threshold status is UNCONFIRMED — a 4-lot-and-under strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Norfolk Terrace's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with the law as it now stands; any older "rentals not allowed" text surfacing in any Central strata's bylaws is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Central townhouse complex is published as age-restricted, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ strata ownership in the sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Central. If a fee-simple row home surfaces here, the rule is stated plainly: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, City of Burnaby rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and the apartment districts R3–R8. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **SSMUH (R1):** Burnaby's R1 district (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m²; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; permitted forms include single-family, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. SSMUH speaks to the detached streets around the townhouse pocket, not to the strata complexes themselves. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, and a further 5% after the September due date; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify these penalties. Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, and Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home) for 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-bn.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% per CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all four Central complexes are resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — Central is in Burnaby, and that tax is Vancouver-only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Is "Central BN" in North Burnaby?** No. The board name is "Central"; the area is mid-city along Canada Way around City Hall. MLS listings file it under the Burnaby North area-major heading, which is a filing artefact, not the geography. Source: research file (Section 1).
- **How much townhouse choice is there in Central?** Very little at any given moment: the whole sub-area had 8 Condos & Townhomes listings and 0 solds in September 2026, across four townhouse complexes and one apartment building. Buyers here wait for listings rather than choosing among them. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Parkside Greene — is it 2011 or 2012?** Listings conflict (2011 in two, 2012 in two). The Form B and strata plan will settle the strata's registration era; do not rely on either listing year. Source: Section 2 entry.
- **What is Smith + Norfolk?** The sub-area's newest townhouse complex: 16 three-storey, 2-bedroom townhomes completed in 2021 by Dinani Group at 3868 Norfolk Street. Its presale-era fee estimate (about $0.26/sqft) is not a current fee — read the current figure off the Form B. Source: Section 2 entry.
- **A 7-unit strata from 1983 — what is different about buying there?** The 3058 Smith Avenue complex is self-managed and small: with 7 lots it is over the five-lot threshold, so a current depreciation report must exist; with self-management, the quality of the Form B package and minutes is the diligence. Source: Section 2 entry; Section 3.
- **Can I rent out a Central townhouse?** A long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void in every BC strata since Nov 24, 2022; Norfolk Terrace's directory records rentals allowed in any case. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws via the Form B. Source: Section 3.
- **Are pets allowed at Norfolk Terrace?** The building directory records pets allowed in its bylaws summary — secondary data; the Form B and registered bylaws govern. Source: Section 2 entry.
- **Is there a SkyTrain station in Central?** No — the nearest are Metrotown (south-west) and Sperling–Burnaby Lake (north-east). The townhouse pocket is a bus-and-car pocket on Canada Way. Source: research file (Section 1).
- **Which secondary school serves the Norfolk/Smith pocket?** Listings in the pocket name both Burnaby Central Secondary and Moscrop Secondary in different listing copy — catchments are address-based and no locator run verifies either claim for a specific unit. Run the SD41 locator on the exact address before relying on a school. Source: research file (Section 1).
