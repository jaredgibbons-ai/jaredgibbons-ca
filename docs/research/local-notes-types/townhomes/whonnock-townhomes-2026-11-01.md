## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the frame, if a strata townhouse ever trades here):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Whonnock complex exists in Section 2 for this to attach to today; the frame is stated so a future listing is diligenced correctly rather than assumed fee-simple. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Whonnock complex carries a directory rental line in this file (there is no complex), so there is no in-entry flag to make — the rule is stated for any future stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Whonnock complex is published as age-restricted, the whonnock 55-plus siblings on disk verify no 55+ stock for this ground, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge is a 60+ co-op in Haney — never a 55+ strata and never Whonnock stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly, and the operative frame in Whonnock:** the stock that does exist here is fee-simple acreage housing, which carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019), ALR rules where they apply, and any registered covenants govern instead. A buyer importing strata expectations onto a Whonnock acreage — or strata fees onto a hypothetical fee-simple townhouse — is applying the wrong frame in both directions.
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — the schedule read lists minimum lot areas including A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text. **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). The Lougheed Transit Corridor (ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 per the City record, Bylaw 7781-2021) ends at 221 Street, far west of Whonnock — it does not designate Whonnock parcels. The live page describes Whonnock as outside the Urban Area Boundary with no municipal sanitary sewer and limited city water, properties relying on private wells and onsite septic under RS-2, RS-3 and ALR A-1/A-2 zones — recorded as consistent in zone names with the bylaw schedule, but the servicing statement itself was not reconciled line by line with a City source in this research pass: UNVERIFIED as a City-confirmed servicing statement. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://jaredgibbons.ca/whonnock.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/whonnock.md)
- **Suites, SSMUH and ALR:** one secondary suite per lot in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. On ALR parcels the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31) and an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met.
