## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhouse counted in the sub-area files).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The Lee Building exclusion in §2.4 is the standing warning: undivided-interest product has no Form B at all (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold — small Vancouver townhouse stratas are exactly the stock where the lot count decides this, so read it off the Form B and strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line surfacing for a Vancouver townhouse complex is void for long-term rentals and flagged as such in its home file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). The city's verified 55+ ownership position is the three-part statement in §2.4 — nothing beyond it is verified.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.
- **Leasehold diligence.** On City ground-lease stock (False Creek South, Champlain Heights) and university ground (Wesbrook/UniverCity form), the lease term, expiry and any prepaid position are title facts to be read off the lease and title, priced against the remaining term — never assumed from a programme-wide framing; §2.4 carries the corrected False Creek South position.

**Vancouver zoning, heritage, taxes and the Empty Homes Tax.**

- **Zoning:** the City of Vancouver's own Zoning and Development By-law governs; district detail is per-sub-area in this program's files and was not opened at city-wide level in this pass — UNVERIFIED here beyond that frame (research file §4).
- **Heritage areas:** Chinatown, Gastown, Shaughnessy and Yaletown carry City heritage regulations and companion design guidelines — confirm what applies to a specific townhouse before promising exterior changes (research file §4: https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx).
- **Vancouver property taxes.** Collected twice a year: advance taxes were due the second business day of February (February 3, 2026) and main taxes the second business day of July (July 3, 2026); the last day to pay the 2026 main tax, claim the homeowner grant and complete a deferment application to avoid a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance was July 3, 2026 (research file §4: https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx).
- **Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY.** Every Vancouver residential owner must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The research file read the declaration and penalty frame but left the rate UNVERIFIED in its pass — the rate carried in this program is 3%, stated here on the brief's standing figure for Vancouver files, and the research file's gap is flagged rather than silently overwritten (research file §4: https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf). This tax applies to the vancouver and yaletown files only — never to Richmond or Abbotsford files.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling notes some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs, and no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out.
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% tier plus the further 2% wherever met. Worked anchors (no exemption, fully residential): $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the Vancouver page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and not asserted.
