## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

*The topic's rules frame goes here, as the brief directs: because this file's stock is farm land, the governing frame is the ALR frame first, then the standard townhome/strata and buyer frames a buyer arriving from a townhouse search needs.*

- **ALR frame — residences cannot be stratified:** ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239 (a strata plan deposit is a subdivision); Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1). A buyer cannot convert an ALR farmhouse into strata townhouses, and no "strata farm" marketing changes that. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR housing caps, in full:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR status check:** a specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm classification — the full three-tier form:** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha. Applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement of these thresholds is an ERROR and is never used. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs zoning across the city, replacing Bylaw No. 3510-1985. The City publishes an A1, A2, A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist, which flags that structures in these zones may require ALC approval and records whether a property is in the ALR and in a flood plain. The live page's statement of five agricultural zones (A-1 through A-5) with minimum lots from 1.8 ha to 8 ha is site copy, not an official source, and the consolidated bylaw text could not be opened in the research pass: those zone minimums are UNVERIFIED — do not use them until checked against the bylaw. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
- **OCP citation: CONTESTED** — flagged at the top of this file (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024 in program sources); resolved in no file. Related dated planning facts carried in standing corrections, stated as such: the Albion Area Plan was adopted Oct 28, 2025, and the Lougheed Transit Corridor was ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 per the City record (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; City news release Feb 4, 2026; the corridor ends at 221 St) — neither is a farm-district instrument, and both are urban-side facts a farm buyer hears quoted; cite the corridor as adopted.
- **Water:** under the Water Sustainability Act, groundwater used for non-domestic purposes such as irrigation requires a water licence and payment of water fees and rentals before lawful use; domestic groundwater use does not require authorization. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Septic:** a building with indoor plumbing not connected to community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person (a Professional Engineer or a Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner) can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect it (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Strata frame — stated for contrast, because buyers arrive asking it:** IF a purchase were a strata townhouse elsewhere in Maple Ridge, the diligence is the Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 plus 25¢/page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); at least 10% of the operating budget to the CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (any directory "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals); and only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). None of that applies to a freehold farm parcel — there is no strata, no Form B and no strata fees on fee-simple land; municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Maple Ridge 55+ cross-reference (never re-counted here):** Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses program-wide, and the Haney Pioneer Village co-operative is 60+, never 55+ — both stated in the sibling 55-plus files; neither creates townhouse stock in this topic.
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses that 3% + 2% structure. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031 (new homes only — a farm parcel's residential portion needs tax advice, UNCONFIRMED per parcel). New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a strata townhouse on Maple Ridge farm land?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and no strata townhouse complex is verified in this topic. Farm parcels here are freehold. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can the house be?** A principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, fill for its construction capped at 1,000 m², and any secondary suite only inside that principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I add a second dwelling for family or a farm worker?** One additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — 90 m² or less at 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less above 40 ha — and City bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income keeps farm classification?** The three-tier test: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with the application in by October 31 for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is this parcel even in the ALR?** Check it on the ALC maps by PID or civic address — 3,787 ha of Maple Ridge is ALR land but that is only 15% of the city, so farm-belt parcels sit in defined pockets and non-ALR rural parcels also trade in this topic. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I rely on the page's farm-zone minimum lots?** No — the A-1 to A-5 / 1.8–8 ha table is UNVERIFIED site copy; the governing text is Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, checked with the City parcel by parcel. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf
- **Q: Do I need a water licence to irrigate?** Yes for non-domestic groundwater use, under the Water Sustainability Act, with fees and rentals payable before lawful use; domestic use is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Are the market numbers on this page farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Maple Ridge figures in Section 1 are residential context (detached median $1,131,000; Condos & Townhomes COMBINED median $525,000, September 2026), and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Which OCP bylaw number should my lawyer cite?** UNCONFIRMED in this program — the citation is CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) and is flagged, never resolved, in these files; have the lawyer pull the City's current consolidated OCP rather than quoting either number from a notes file.
- **Q: Is a feed listing proof of a working farm?** No — the topic feed is generic; never treat a card as a confirmed operation. Documented farm income history, water licences and ALC approvals are the diligence items a lender and lawyer ask for first. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Most of Maple Ridge is not ALR (2011 inventory, published 2013):** 3,787 ha of ALR land against a 24,881 ha city means "farm country" here is pockets — the northwest lowlands and river flats, and bench parcels in Websters Corners, Whonnock and Thornhill — so two "Maple Ridge farm" listings can sit under entirely different residence rules depending on a line on the ALC map. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/mapleridge2011_aluireport_complete.pdf
- **The City's own farm checklist asks the flood question for you:** the A1–A3 Agricultural Zoning Compliance Summary Checklist records flood plain status and whether ALC approval may be required — ask for those two answers on any lowland parcel before subject removal. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
- **The rural market is thin enough that one sale moves a district row:** Whonnock recorded 2 detached listings and 0 sales, Northeast 2 and 0, Thornhill 13 and 1 in September 2026 — district-level "trends" in this topic are single-parcel events, not a market. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **"Northeast" on a Maple Ridge farm listing means Northeast Maple Ridge** — a board sub-area of this city — not Northeast Coquitlam; the two are different municipalities with different bylaws. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **The October 31 farm-classification deadline is the date that outlives the purchase:** a buyer who intends to keep farming must apply by Oct 31 for the following tax year, or the classification — and its tax treatment — lapses regardless of what the seller had. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **No current ALR total exists in this program's sources:** the 3,787 ha figure is the most recent official area figure confirmed (2011 survey) — any newer hectare figure quoted for Maple Ridge is UNVERIFIED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/mapleridge2011_aluireport_complete.pdf
