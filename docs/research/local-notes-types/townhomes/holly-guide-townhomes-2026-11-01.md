## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Skye and Hollyview are both strata (one conventional, one bare-land), so the strata block governs Holly's verified stock; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast.

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. In a bare-land strata like Hollyview Estates or Ashley Green, the Form B is also how a buyer learns what the strata does and does not maintain — the house itself is the owner's problem. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. Skye (56 lots) and Hollyview (13 lots) are both inside this rule. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving the budget at the AGM. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, and any directory line saying a Holly complex has "rentals not allowed" is void in-entry for long-term rentals. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. No Holly complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Presale rules (REDMA) — directly relevant to Skye.** A presale buyer must receive a disclosure statement, and under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act the buyer has seven days to rescind, starting on the later of signing the purchase agreement or signing the written acknowledgement of the disclosure statement; deposits are generally held in trust. The disclosure-statement duty itself sits in REDMA s. 18's framework — read the Skye disclosure statement, not the marketing, for unit count, phasing and completion dates. Whether a Skye contract can be assigned, and on what fee and consent terms, is set by the purchase contract and disclosure statement: UNCONFIRMED from public sources. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse or row house has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus covenants or HOA terms on title only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Holly, so a "no strata" claim on a Holly listing must be proved on title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets and the Bassi notice cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (the Bassi application itself seeks rezoning to a new Townhouse Residential Zone under "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017"); an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither number is asserted as settled here; zoning is read parcel by parcel off the City's maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/4617/7676/7348/3ff51f67a63aa5b3830e9b533891cc59_LU009770_Resident20Notice20V4.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — it contains NO lettered schedules; the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land east of Holly is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **GST on new construction.** A new Skye townhome carries GST; a resale does not. The first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000 — the exemption that matters for a new 60B Street townhome. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs and mortgage insurance.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across its loan-to-value bands. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What townhouse choice actually exists in Holly?** Two verified complexes: Skye at Holly Park (new, 56 freehold-strata townhomes on 60B Street, Phase 1 move-in 2026 per the developer) and Hollyview Estates (13 bare-land strata townhomes on 64 Street, built 1992). Ashley Green is a bare-land strata of half-duplexes — a neighbouring form, not a townhouse complex. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: I signed a presale at Skye — can I change my mind?** Yes, inside the window: REDMA s. 21 gives seven days from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. After day seven the contract is binding, including its assignment terms. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/
- **Q: What is the difference between Hollyview's bare-land strata and Skye's strata?** At Hollyview the listing states it plainly: you share the common property only and take care of your own home and yard within your fence — roof, exterior and house insurance are yours. At Skye, a conventional townhouse strata, the strata carries more of the building envelope and common systems, which is what the larger fee buys. The Form B and budget for each complex state the exact split. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3072787-4767-64-street-13-delta-bc-v4k-3m2/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhouse here?** For long-term rentals, BC law voids strata rental-restriction bylaws as of Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. In a presale, also check the disclosure statement's rental disclosure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets restricted?** Skye's listing data states pets allowed; Hollyview's pet position is UNCONFIRMED. Pet rules live in each strata's registered bylaws, attached to the Form B package — confirm number, size and type limits there. https://www.estateblock.com/delta/50-4732-60b-st-1966641
- **Q: Does a new townhome here get the PTT newly built exemption?** Potentially, yes: the newly built home exemption is full for homes valued at $1,100,000 or less and phases out by $1,150,000, with citizenship and principal-residence conditions — and GST also applies on new construction, partly offset for first-time buyers by the Bill C-4 rebate. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How healthy are these stratas — could a levy be coming?** For Skye, read the disclosure statement and, once registered, the Form B: the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund yearly since November 1, 2023, and a 56-lot strata must refresh its depreciation report every five years. For 13-lot Hollyview the same 5-year depreciation cycle applies; its 1992 build year makes the report's roof and envelope items the first read (one listing notes a roof replaced in 2012 — confirm in the report, not the listing). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Which schools serve Holly?** For the sample address at the school itself, Holly Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); catchments are address-based, so run the exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is Holly behind dikes?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure — and properties within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Confirm flood construction requirements for the specific lot with the City. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: How is the commute if there is no bus inside Holly?** The transit spine is Ladner Trunk Road to Ladner Exchange, then route 601 to Bridgeport Station (Canada Line) via Highway 17A and Highway 99. A bus route with a stop inside Holly itself is UNVERIFIED — plan on driving or cycling to the Trunk Road stops or the Exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
