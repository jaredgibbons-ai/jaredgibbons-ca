## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**There is no verified townhouse stock in Durieu for the strata block to attach to — the block is stated so a buyer can see exactly what would apply if strata product ever appeared, and the fee-simple/ALR frame below is the one that actually governs this ground.**

**The strata block (BC standard — no Durieu complex is currently verified under it).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: an Information Certificate (Form B) within seven days of request, capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page, stating the strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). It has NO application to Durieu's fee-simple acreages — there is no strata to issue one.
- **Depreciation reports / CRF.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years (sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); since November 1, 2023, a minimum 10% CRF contribution applies (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022; since that date the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+ (SPA ss. 123.1–123.2, Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).

**The frame that actually governs Durieu: fee-simple rural land, FVRD rules, ALR.**

- **Fee-simple reality, stated plainly:** Durieu's houses sit on fee-simple (non-strata) rural lots — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. What governs is FVRD zoning/OCP, registered covenants on title, and the ALR where it applies.
- **Planning and zoning:** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern (the Hatzic Valley OCP covers the inhabited valley), but the specific OCP/zoning bylaw numbers and zone names applying to Durieu lots are **UNVERIFIED** — no FVRD Area F bylaw document was opened in this pass, the "RU36" label in one feed card is listing text only, and NO bylaw number is borrowed from Chilliwack or Mission in this file (research file §4). Confirm the zone for the PID with the FVRD.
- **Secondary suites in Area F:** the FVRD records that secondary suites are freely permitted with no farm-use restriction, but must be located WITHIN the principal dwelling — a separate detached structure such as a garden suite is not the Area F suite route (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6), and subdivision of ALR land requires ALC approval under the Agricultural Land Commission Act (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Whether a specific Durieu parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED at parcel level — feed ALR statements are listing claims.
- **BC Assessment farm status (where a farm purchase is in view):** the farm-class income thresholds run in three tiers — $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha, and $10,000 on land under 0.8 ha, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line for farm status is an ERROR — the tier and the parcel's area decide the figure (BC Assessment farm classification, as carried in this program's standing corrections).
- **Property taxes — RURAL regime (this ground).** A rural-area owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City of Mission (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area). Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11, taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01). **Do NOT apply the City of Mission's flat 10%-after-July-2 penalty here** — that is city ground only (§3 of the Hatzic file covers the split).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling stating March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **PTT.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" summary compresses 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears; it is not a flat 5% (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: $800,000 with no exemption = $14,000. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on the first $500,000 to $835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 lifetime, $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **CMHC.** Premiums 0.6%–4.5% by loan-to-value (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). Stated explicitly: this slug's research documents NO CMHC premium page error, so the known 2.80%–4.00% error is NOT asserted for Durieu.
- **Presales (REDMA).** 7-day rescission under s. 21; deposits in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01). None counted or queued here (§2.4).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there any townhomes in Durieu?**
A: None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes on any source checked, and the board's Durieu Condos & Townhomes row for September 2026 recorded 0 listings and 0 sales (§§1–2). Durieu's stock is fee-simple acreage houses, vacant land and farm packages.

**Q: Is Durieu part of Mission? I see "Mission" on every listing.**
A: No. The "Mission" on the cards is a mailing address and a board reporting grouping. Durieu is an unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area F community in the Hatzic Valley — the FVRD governs it, not the City of Mission (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).

**Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and when is it due?**
A: The Province — the Surveyor of Taxes Office issues the rural notice. Taxes are due July 2, with 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31, under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01). Mission's flat 10% city penalty does not apply here.

**Q: Can I get a Form B or strata documents for a Durieu house?**
A: No — there is no strata. Durieu houses are fee-simple: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. Diligence runs on title, registered covenants, FVRD zoning and, on ALR land, the ALC frame instead (§3).

**Q: Can I add a suite or a second home on a Durieu acreage?**
A: Two gates. In Area F a secondary suite must sit within the principal dwelling — a detached garden suite is not the suite route — and on ALR land an additional detached residence must fit the ALC caps (90 m² at ≤40 ha, 186 m² above 40 ha, principal residence to 500 m²). A feed card's "zoned for a secondary dwelling" line clears neither gate by itself (§3).

**Q: Can a Durieu farm be stratified into townhomes later?**
A: Not while it is in the ALR: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6), and ALR subdivision needs ALC approval. Confirm the parcel's ALR status by PID before valuing any strata or subdivision potential (§3).
