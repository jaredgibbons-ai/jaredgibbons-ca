## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial and District rules are stated here because a buyer cross-shopping Canyon Heights against Edgemont Village or the District's centres will meet them there; inside Canyon Heights itself there is no strata townhouse stock for the strata rules to attach to.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases in the neighbouring centres):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Edgemont or District-centre building a buyer is comparing against is old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined spouses/partners and children). The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ ownership stock in Canyon Heights. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the stock that does exist here):** a Canyon Heights detached house purchase carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 3210, OCP Bylaw 7900) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Canyon Heights is in the District of North Vancouver, not the City of North Vancouver — the City's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 do not apply here.
- **District zoning and plan:** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (including its Schedule B Development Permit Areas), and Zoning Bylaw 3210 — Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) sets the R1 minimum lot at 4,000 m² / 30 m width / 34 m depth and standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m, with modifiers R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m; an R2 principal building may include a secondary suite, and a coach house is a dwelling unit accessory to and detached from the principal dwelling unit. A Canyon Heights-specific plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **SSMUH/suites as the District states them:** since June 2024, a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — a per-lot classification, not a street-wide entitlement. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Canyon Heights is in the District of North Vancouver, not the City of Vancouver. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project inside Canyon Heights is confirmed, so this is relevant only to presales a buyer compares in Edgemont or the District's centres — a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **District tax timing:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571 in 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses for sale in Canyon Heights itself?** No verified townhouse complex exists in Canyon Heights on any source checked — the area is detached houses on R1/R2 lots between the Capilano River and the Mosquito/Mackay creek ravines, and the research file records named condo buildings and townhome complexes as none confirmed. Fresh searches in this pass returned detached-house listings only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real townhouse or condo stock?** Edgemont Village, immediately east on the same Highland Boulevard corridor: the verified boutique condo stock on Connaught Crescent (3121 and 3151 Connaught Crescent) is documented in the sibling programs, with the District's larger centres at Lynn Valley and Lions Gate Village beyond. (Sibling condos file on disk.)
- **Q: Is the "EIGHT" project on Highland Boulevard a Canyon Heights townhouse complex?** No. The listing for 3206 Highland Boulevard describes eight duplex-style 3-bedroom homes "in the heart of Edgemont Village" — Edgemont product in duplex form, not a Canyon Heights townhouse complex. https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/3206-highland-boulevard-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7r-2v9-30289712/
- **Q: Can I buy a suite or coach house separately in Canyon Heights?** No. Since June 2024 a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house (three units total) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, but they sit on one lot's title — there is no separate strata lot to buy. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: How much density could a Canyon Heights lot take under SSMUH?** The District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set the same 3 / 4 / 6 ladder by lot size and bus-stop distance. It is a per-lot classification, not a street-wide entitlement. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Canyon Heights address?** The locator result verified in the research file, for 4501 Highland Boulevard, is Canyon Heights Elementary (K–7) and Handsworth Secondary (8–12). Highlands Elementary (3150 Colwood Drive) and Cleveland Elementary (1255 Eldon Road) sit beside the area — catchments are address-based, so confirm each address with SD44. SD44 has no middle tier. Note the standing District rule: the North Vancouver secondary catchment relevant to Lynnmour/Lynn Valley addresses is Windsor Secondary — that is a different part of the District and does not displace the Handsworth result verified for this area's sample address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: How is transit from Canyon Heights without a SkyTrain?** By bus: route 246 runs from Highland Boulevard directly downtown over the Lions Gate Bridge, route 232 links the area to Grouse Mountain and Phibbs Exchange, route 236 runs on Capilano Road, and the SeaBus at Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: What hazard checks matter on river-side and steep lots?** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets seven Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard; the District publishes property-level GeoWeb reports, including modelled flood construction levels for a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. A west-side or steep lot can fall in one of these areas along the Capilano River side. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Q: If I buy a strata townhouse in Edgemont Village instead, can the strata ban rentals?** Not for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, whatever a directory still shows. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and a 55+ age bylaw can still limit who may live in the unit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse community in Canyon Heights?** None verified — the sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ strata, townhome complex or manufactured-home park in the sub-area. What a 55+ buyer can buy here is a freehold detached house with no age rule on who lives in it. (Sibling 55-plus file on disk.)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The boulevard changes neighbourhoods mid-corridor.** Highland Boulevard carries both Canyon Heights and Edgemont addresses: the verified attached stock on Connaught Crescent and the "EIGHT" duplex project at 3206 Highland Boulevard are Edgemont, while the Canyon Heights stretch west of the village is detached houses only — a "Highland Boulevard townhouse" search result is almost never in Canyon Heights. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md) and https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/3206-highland-boulevard-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7r-2v9-30289712/
- **Three units per house lot is already the rule.** Since June 2024 every single-family lot within the Urban Containment Boundary can hold a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — in Canyon Heights the realistic "smaller attached purchase" is a house with a suite, bought as one title. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **The flood data runs to 2100.** The District's GeoWeb flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m — a long-horizon number that can surprise a buyer pricing a river-side renovation off today's water levels. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **Murdo Frazer's courts were restated in 2025.** The Edgemont-area District park east of Canyon Heights carries 6 tennis courts and 6 pickleball courts in the District's Outdoor Sports Courts Optimization Strategy, approved July 21, 2025 — the shared amenity base for a neighbourhood with no strata amenity rooms. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **French Immersion is not catchment-locked here.** Handsworth Secondary offers French Immersion, and SD44 states French Immersion is offered across the district regardless of geographical catchment, so a Canyon Heights address does not lock a child out of the program. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
- **The library is on the same boulevard, in the next neighbourhood.** The Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, is described by the North Vancouver District Public Library as "located in the heart of Edgemont Village" — east of Canyon Heights on the same street its townhouse searches run on, and the Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, south-east of the area, is the nearest full-service NVRC centre. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/canyon-heights-nv.md)
