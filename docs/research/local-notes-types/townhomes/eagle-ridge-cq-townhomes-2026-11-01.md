## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes, and Eagleridge Villas if its strata tenure is confirmed):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. For Eagleridge Villas the Form B is also the tenure test: if no strata exists, there is no Form B, and diligence runs on title and municipal records instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — both counted complexes (16 and 36 units) are over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. For 1980s stock like this corridor's, the depreciation report and CRF balance are the pricing documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Eagle Ridge Court's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" is therefore read as old bylaw text — VOID for long-term rentals, flagged here in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in Section 2 is published as 55+, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in Eagle Ridge. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified in this sub-area. The standing rule, stated plainly for any fee-simple product a buyer meets here: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 governs — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² with 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² with 12.5 m width (R-2); Coquitlam's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes, and lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. The Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — was adopted April 27, 2026 and supersedes OCP No. 3479; the City states the Interim OCP did not update existing policies unless required by new provincial legislation. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **Watercourse check:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam — a parcel-level check for slope and creek-edge lots near the complexes. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Coquitlam — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no verified presale is carried in Section 2; the standing frame is that a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
