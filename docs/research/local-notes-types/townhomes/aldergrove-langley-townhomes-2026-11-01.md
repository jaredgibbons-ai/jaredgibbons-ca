## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

All six verified complexes for this ground are strata, so the strata block applies in full; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast.

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Aldergrove's 1970s–1990s complexes, the Form B plus two years of minutes is where roof, envelope and plumbing history shows up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line for an Aldergrove complex saying "rentals not allowed" is VOID in-entry for long-term rentals — no such line is carried for a counted complex in §2, and Twin Firs' listing-stated "No Restrictions" is consistent with the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Creekside Villa is the one complex for this ground a source states is 55+; an older listing described a Creekside Villa unit as a "45+ rancher" while its own MLS data recorded "Restricted Age: 55+" — a sub-55 age bylaw cannot be valid today, and the registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — Township rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. None was verified for Aldergrove ground (§2); do not describe any counted complex that way.
- **Township zoning and plans:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs Aldergrove; zones named in Township documents for Aldergrove parcels include Rural Zone RU-2, Community Commercial Zone C-2 and Comprehensive Development Zone CD-192, and the bylaw's Section 110.1 ladder runs from R-1B/R-1C urban lots at a 464.5 m² minimum to SR-2 at 8,094 m² (SR-1 3,716 m², SR-3 1,765 m²). Plans: the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community under the Township's Official Community Plan (Bylaw 1979 No. 1842). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020:** the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw (adopted November 18, 2024) amended Zoning Bylaw 2500 to enable 3–4 units on most lots previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings (about 55% of such lots, roughly 15,000 properties, per the Township). **There is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020** — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is WRONG; no such claim is documented in this area's research, so no defect is recorded here. Secondary suite cap: 120 m² under the Township frame in this program. A coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 — never a strata unit, and a house with a coach house is not townhouse stock. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/aldergrove-langley-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township property taxes:** property taxes and utility fees were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026, with a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)
- **ALR frame:** much of the Township's land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and the Township directs owners to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource — decisive for any acreage alternative a townhouse buyer cross-shops in Aldergrove, irrelevant inside the 32 Avenue stratas themselves. On ALR parcels: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); principal residence capped at 500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/aldergrove-langley.md)

New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2%. Exemptions: first-time buyers — full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. **No CMHC defect is asserted for this slug** — this slug's own research does not document the live page's 2.80%–4.00% range as an error, so it is not documented for this slug and not asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
