## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The live page's own advice — Form B, two years of strata council and AGM minutes, the latest depreciation report (roof, siding, window and deck timelines), pet and parking bylaws, and the strata insurance policy — is carried as the page's guidance and matches this diligence frame. Garage layout (tandem vs side-by-side) and backyard tenure (common vs limited common property) are unit-and-plan questions, not page facts. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/maple-ridge-townhomes-under-1-2m.md)
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every cross-referenced complex above is over the five-lot threshold on its verified scale (Ridgemont 97; Brighton 145; Windfall 54; Spencer Brook 40; The Warwick 31); a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the cross-referenced complexes is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where the record carries the line: Fraser Glen's directory "rentals not allowed" and Rosewood Court's identical apartment-building line — any long-term component is void. Spencer Brook's directory line is "rentals allowed" and needs no flag. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Maple Ridge's position is stated in §2: zero verified 55+ strata townhouses — directory/listing descriptions (Fraser Glen, Dogwood Lane) are not bylaw proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; City of Maple Ridge zoning plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex is cross-referenced in §2 — every counted complex in the count homes is strata — so a Maple Ridge listing calling a counted complex "fee simple" contradicts the verified record and needs a title check.
