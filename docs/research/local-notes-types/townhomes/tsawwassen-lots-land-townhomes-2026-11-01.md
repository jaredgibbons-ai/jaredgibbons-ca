## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

A land purchase that will become townhomes runs through both blocks below: the land rules decide what can be built and whether it can be stratified at all; the strata/tax block governs the finished townhouse purchase.

- **Stratification gate (the rule that decides whether a lot can ever become strata townhomes):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). On urban land, stratification runs through the zoning and subdivision rules below; on Tsawwassen Lands, City stratification rules do not apply at all — TFN land law governs (§2).
- **Urban zoning minimums:** inside the residential neighbourhoods, lots sit under the RS and RD zones; minimum lot sizes for subdivision include RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m², with zoning confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. The RS3 zone (single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait) sets a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum, lot width 30 m and average lot depth 30 m — the estate-scale standard that fits the English Bluff and Cliff Drive shore. **Delta zoning bylaw number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (research and City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Farm-zone subdivision and housing:** in the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets 8 ha; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on larger parcels, where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm-class thresholds, in full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on farms of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha on larger farms; $10,000 on farms under 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met. (Program standing record.)
- **Added units are not subdivision:** Delta's SSMUH amendments (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots — added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide, does not create new lots, and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area on the parcel. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Hazard permit areas:** SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)" (OCP Appendix B, hazardous conditions) covers the west shore — the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, development affected is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer, and no building may sit within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) provides a 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, a 2.3 m GSC datum minimum land surface elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback. All properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Water and septic on unserviced parcels:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules; farm land on the Tsawwassen edges is designated Agriculture (A) (s. 1.5.1, Map 2; agriculture policies at s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan). A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Form B / strata block (for the finished townhouse, and for any bare-land strata lot):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A bare-land strata lot purchase runs on this document too — read as a house purchase with a strata lot, not as apartment stock. **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met on a Tsawwassen strata is flagged VOID in-entry in its home file (documented examples: Heron Cove, Tsawwassen Greene). **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple lot or townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead — the position for a freehold urban lot in this topic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 serviced urban lot with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000** (PTT applies to the land purchase itself; GST treatment of a specific lot sale is transaction-specific — the research expressly leaves the live page's GST summary UNVERIFIED, so confirm with a CPA per transaction, §3 page check). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — it attaches to a qualifying new *home* purchase, not to raw land as such; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where land is sold as part of a developer project with homes to be built, REDMA s. 18 (disclosure statement, deposits in trust) and s. 21 (7-day rescission from the later of signing or acknowledging it) govern the developer sale; no presale project is counted in this topic file in any event. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a Tsawwassen lot and build strata townhomes on it?** Only where the land system allows it: an urban RS/RD lot runs on zoning minimums and subdivision rules; an ALR parcel cannot be stratified at all (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) and generally needs ALC permission to subdivide; a Tsawwassen Lands parcel is under TFN law, not City bylaws. Establish which system the parcel sits in before pricing anything. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does the four-unit SSMUH permission mean I can create four lots?** No — Delta allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots, but that permission does not create new lots and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area. Subdivision still runs against the zone minimums (335 m² in RS7 up to 4,000 m² in RS3; 8 ha in A1). (§3 above)
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** A principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less total floor area, no more than 1,000 m² of fill; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. An additional detached residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or 186 m² (larger parcels). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** West shore: SD2 — Professional Engineer conditions for hazard-affected development and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea. Boundary Bay: SD3 — 2.9 m datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback. Confirm both with the City before writing, not from a listing's wording. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, strata fees for common property, registered bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The split for Tsawwassen land parcels is UNVERIFIED on any official source — read the tenure off title per parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $700,000 lot?** PTT of $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $10,000 at 2% — worked in §3). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history: the research leaves the live page's GST summary UNVERIFIED, so confirm with a CPA per transaction rather than relying on any page summary. (§3 above)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Tsawwassen land?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. (§1 above)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the 500 m² principal-residence cap, the additional-residence limits, and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. Run it before subjects come off. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What servicing surprises come up on unserviced parcels?** Wells (check the GWELLS registry; older wells may not appear), water licensing for non-domestic use, and onsite sewage — Fraser Health requires a system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person, for any home not connected to a community sewer. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which townhome files should I read alongside a lot purchase?** The home files for the sub-area the lot sits in — Central (Sundial counted; South Grove queued), East/Southlands (3 collections), Beach Grove (The Pillars component), Boundary Beach (Monterra) — each counted once, there, and cross-referenced in §2. A lot is never priced off a complex's listing without reading that complex's own file.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The two-system split is the whole topic.** An urban lot's potential is set by its zone minimum (as low as 335 m² for a new RS7 lot) and the four-unit added-housing permission, while a farm parcel's potential is set by the 8 ha A1 subdivision minimum and the ALC's housing and subdivision rules — the same "land" search spans both, and the wrong rule set prices the parcel wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Hazard overlays sort the urban stock more than street names do.** West-shore lots carry SD2 geotechnical conditions and the 7.5 m sea setback; Boundary Bay lots carry SD3 floor-level and tidal-setback rules — both apply before any design work starts. (§3 above)
- **Southlands is where the land topic becomes housing.** The Granary's flats and District Flats' homes sit on former farm-designated ground at the community's south edge (City File LU006390 for the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd.) — the one place a "land" buyer is really watching a farm parcel turn into strata product, counted in the Boundary Beach condos file and the East townhomes file, by form. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **A flat "$2,500 farm income" line is wrong.** Farm class runs in three tiers by farm size ($2,500 / 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) — acreage listings in this feed that quote only the first figure understate the test for small and large farms alike. (§3 above)
- **The live feed's own total is unverified — deliberately.** A 429 rate-limit response stopped the continuation fetch on 2026-11-01 and it was not retried; this file describes only the cards actually read, rather than restating a feed total no one completed counting. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/tsawwassen-lots-land.md)
- **Delta's zoning bylaw number is contested in the sources — do not quote one.** What governs in practice is the parcel's own zone sheet (RS3 for the confirmed bluff-shore standard, A1 for farm land) and, for a strata lot, its registered bylaws. (§3 above)
