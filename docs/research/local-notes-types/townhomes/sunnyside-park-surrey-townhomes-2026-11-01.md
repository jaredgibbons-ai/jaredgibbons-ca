## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata diligence — the Form B regime (all 4 counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane and Highgrove the Form B is also where any 55+ bylaw is actually proved — listing descriptions are not proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Peninsula Village's own depreciation report (Strata Plan LMS 133, JRS Engineering, October 15, 2014) is the corridor example of the document set a buyer receives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry: the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for the neighbouring apartment building Semiahmoo Estates is void on the same rule, and any equivalent directory line for a townhouse complex here is void in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners, defined children). Bishop's Green and St. Martin's Lane are described in listings as 55+ communities and Peninsula Village listings state "One person 55+" — each registered bylaw must still be confirmed on the Form B, and Highgrove's age position is in direct conflict between listings (Section 2) and is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Every verified townhouse complex here is strata, so a Sunnyside buyer marketed a "no-strata townhouse" should ask which of the forms in Section 2 it actually is before pricing it. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the former PlanSurrey 2013 OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan (16 Avenue to 24 Avenue, Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School) borders this sub-area and was updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH zones RA to R6 (in force for this area since July 8, 2024 in the program record) allow up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — the detached-street frame around the townhouse complexes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated as current here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunnyside-park-surrey.md, via the sibling guide files)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home, tenants cannot operate them. The City's own Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is $358 per year (plus any applicable application fees) — that $358 City-page figure is the one carried here. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees, and secondary guides repeat $350 — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it to a client. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw even though long-term rental bylaws are void. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Presales (REDMA) — live here via Elisa:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21).
