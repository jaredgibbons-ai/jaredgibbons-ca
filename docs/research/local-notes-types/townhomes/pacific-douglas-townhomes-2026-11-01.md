## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted complexes and the queued strata stock).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Douglas Point's and Berkeley Village's registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Both counted complexes' lot counts are UNCONFIRMED in citable sources, so read the count off the Form B and strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022. Douglas Point's published line includes "Rentals Allowed," which is consistent with the law; any older "rentals not allowed" line surfacing for any complex in §2 would be void for long-term rentals and is flagged as void wherever met. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and Surrey's STR frame is below (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Neither counted complex is age-restricted on any source read — Douglas Point states the opposite in terms, and Harlowe/Calais/Rockford are explicitly NOT 55+. The Greens at Douglas is the sub-area's only published 55+ complex, and it is queued, not counted (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.3). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and STRs.**

- **OCP — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction:** Surrey's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 21900, "Surrey 2050"** (Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900), finally adopted June 15, 2026, replacing Bylaw No. 18020 — **18020 is NEVER cited as current** (City of Surrey, Bylaw No. 21900 text and Corporate Report R133, June 29, 2026: https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments). The Douglas NCP (1999) designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; zoning is under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 — a listing quoting a 1999 NCP designation (as the 148–192 172 Street assembly cards quote "Commercial/Residential") is quoting history, and the current designation for those PIDs is UNVERIFIED in this file's research (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp).
- **Secondary suites (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, and its habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey frame):** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites).
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments took effect **July 8, 2024** (standing program correction; City of Surrey SSMUH frame under Zoning By-law No. 12000: https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000). SSMUH speaks to the detached lots around the complexes, not to the strata complexes themselves.
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (program figure; Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1, per research file §4) versus the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and direct any buyer to the City licence page before quoting either figure.
- **ALR edge:** the plan area is bordered by the ALR to the north; an ALR parcel follows the ALC frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m², a suite only within the principal residence, plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug notes some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs, and no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction (Harlowe, Douglas Green Living), not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
