## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Monterra is strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Monterra, the Form B and strata plan LMS469 are also where the Townhouse-vs-Duplex listing conflict in §2 is settled for a specific lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Monterra's unit count is UNCONFIRMED, so its position is read off the strata plan — a gated community of its described scale is not treated here as sub-5-lot without the plan). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: Monterra's data-sheet "Rentals Not Allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No 55+ bylaw is verified for Monterra — "Adult Oriented" in a site-influences field is not a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in this file's own count (§2) — a listing calling a Monterra home "fee simple, no strata" contradicts the LMS469 record and needs a title check.
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024 — NO lettered schedules; the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps; Monterra's data sheet records zoning CD201 for its lot. https://www.cotala.com/tours/72783/FullPublic.pdf?i=1871186173
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** OCP Bylaw No. 8400, Appendix B — no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum, and no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific Boundary Beach parcel sits inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot in this file. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dike and flood system:** the Boundary Bay shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority and operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **SSMUH:** Delta Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. This affects the pocket's detached lots, not the strata lots inside Monterra. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/boundary-beach.md)
- **ALR rule (Southlands edge):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Community Act s.21(1) as cited in the program record) — Southlands product sits on former farm-designated ground under City approvals, and no ALR parcel is townhouse stock.
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 resale Monterra townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a Southlands unit is still a developer sale, the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
