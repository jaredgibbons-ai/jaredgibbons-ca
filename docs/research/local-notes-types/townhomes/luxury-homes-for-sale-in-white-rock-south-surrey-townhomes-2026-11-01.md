## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Form B is identical in law on both sides of the White Rock / Surrey line — the Strata Property Act is provincial; what differs by municipality is the planning and tax frame below. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every cross-referenced complex above is over the five-lot threshold on its verified scale (Peninsula Village 95 homes; The Crescent 57 homes); a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the cross-referenced complexes is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where the cross-referenced record carries the line: Semiahmoo Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" and The Capistrano's listing "no rentals" wording (condos sibling) — any long-term component is void. No counted entry in §2 carries a "rentals not allowed" line of its own. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Three cross-referenced townhouse complexes carry a published 55+ position (Peninsula Village "One person 55+"; Bishop's Green; The Crescent) — in each case the registered bylaw on the Form B governs, not the listing description. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal zoning plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex is cross-referenced in §2 — every verified townhouse complex in this page's scope is strata, and the detached luxury houses in the feed are fee-simple single-family homes, a different purchase entirely.

- **Surrey plan — Surrey side ONLY:** current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900** (the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current); Zoning Bylaw **No. 12000** governs. **SSMU (in force for this area since July 8, 2024 in the program record):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City states the changes do NOT retroactively legalize a suite or coach house built without permits. **Secondary suites:** one per dwelling unit, capped at **40%** of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area under Bylaw No. 12000. **Short-term rentals:** permitted, owner-only (host must be the owner living in the home); the City's page states the licence fee as **$358 per year** — **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 — Surrey side only, never carried into White Rock. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
