## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Villa Montecito):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1973 wood-frame complex, the depreciation report, CRF balance and any envelope history in that package are the purchase. Directory restriction lines in Section 2 are directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Villa Montecito's 75 lots are well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Montecito complex was found in this pass — the directory records Villa Montecito's rentals as allowed with restrictions — so there is no in-entry VOID flag to make in Section 2; any such line a buyer finds elsewhere for this complex is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No counted Montecito townhouse complex carries a published 55+ bylaw — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ strata ownership stock in Montecito; the area's seniors facility, Swedish Assisted Living at 7230 Halifax Street, is care housing, not ownership stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Montecito; the area's fee-simple stock is the detached house on R1 lots (below), and under Burnaby's SSMUH rules a fee-simple lot with two or more primary units may be stratified — so a "townhouse" listing here must be title-checked to learn which side of that line it sits on.
- **City zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026** — not the former 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plan:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — check the 2050 map before assuming density or subdivision potential on any large Montecito lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **City taxes and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a garbage toter fee; the detached single-family water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the tax notice — strata townhouse utility treatment differs by complex: UNCONFIRMED for Villa Montecito, read it off the Form B. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Montecito's counted stock is a 1973 resale complex, so GST is not in its purchase price. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Montecito townhouse presale is carried in this file; the frame governs any future SSMUH strata product sold off-plan on the area's house streets. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City does not call this area Montecito.** For Burnaby administrative and planning purposes the same ground is "Sperling-Broadway" — a buyer reading City documents, community-plan maps or facility pages under "Montecito" will miss material filed under the planning name. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex
- **The sports complex fields were closed most of 2026 and are back.** Burnaby Lake Sports Complex Fields 1 and 2 were closed for turf upgrades scheduled May to September 2026 and were listed OPEN again in the City's October 29, 2026 field-status update; the complex has 5 lit artificial turf fields, and the new Burnaby Lake Recreation Complex is under construction beside Bill Copeland Sports Centre (3676 Kensington Avenue, NHL-sized rink, 1,984 seats) while programs continue. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/bill-copeland-sports-centre
- **You can walk into a regional park from the SkyTrain station.** Burnaby Lake Regional Park — a wildlife sanctuary with a rowing course, pavilion and public canoe launch, a Nature House, and viewing towers and docks — is directly south across Lougheed Highway, and Metro Vancouver notes the walk-in access from Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **The library branch for this quadrant is temporary and inside a mall.** Burnaby Public Library's branch serving this quadrant is the Temporary Cameron Branch at The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre, 9855 Austin Avenue; the permanent Cameron replacement status is UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
- **There is no mall or town centre inside Montecito.** Burnaby's plan framework designates Brentwood (west) and Lougheed (east) as the town centres serving this area, with Kensington Square at Hastings and Kensington as the local plaza — "walk to shops" claims for a Montecito townhouse should be tested against that map. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **The seniors facility on Halifax Street is care housing, not a 55+ purchase option.** Swedish Assisted Living at 7230 Halifax Street is the area's seniors address in the sibling 55-plus file; Montecito has no verified 55+ strata ownership stock at all.
