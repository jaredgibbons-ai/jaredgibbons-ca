## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Archer Green, the one strata complex on this ground).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). For an Archer Green purchase this is the central diligence document — including settling the unit count no public source states (§2.1).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — flag any "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry wherever it appears for this stock. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). No Mission West complex checked carries a verified 55+ bylaw — Archer Green listings state no age restriction, and the Wilson Street parks' age positions, where they exist at all, are park rules under the MHPTA, not SPA bylaws (§2.5).

**Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Mission West (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only.
