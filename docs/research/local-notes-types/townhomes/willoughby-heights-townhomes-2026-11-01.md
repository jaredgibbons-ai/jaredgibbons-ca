# Willoughby Heights, Langley — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Willoughby Heights" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; the Township of Langley's official planning name is "Willoughby." The Willoughby Community Plan Area description in Bylaw No. 6201 is bordered by the Surrey municipal boundary to the southwest, Highway 1 to the north, the Trans Mountain Pipeline right-of-way ridgeline, Lower Mainland Regional Park and 68 Avenue to the south, and 216 Street and the Willowbrook Connector to the east. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- The name Willoughby was suggested for the post office at the corner of 208 Street and 83 Avenue after the settler Willoughby, per the Township schools history document held by SD35. Buyers also search "Yorkson," "Latimer Heights," and by the hubs — Langley Events Centre, Carvolth Exchange and Willoughby Town Centre. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/discover/department-operations/human-resources/schools---info and https://www.tol.ca/en/arts-culture-recreation/langley-events-centre.aspx
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: (1) the Yorkson / 208 Street node around 82–84 Avenue — Yorkson Creek, Gabriola Park, Luxia at Yorkson; (2) the Latimer / 200–204 Street corridor — Cascadia, Kensington Lofts; (3) the southwest edge on 197 Street and 64–67 Avenue — Willow Park Estates, Bakerview Estates — the area's older stock. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- Willoughby Heights is the centre of the Langley attached market: 369 attached listings and 70 attached sales in September 2026, the deepest attached selection and largest attached sales count of any Langley sub-area in that report — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. The report combines condos and townhomes in one "attached" category and publishes no Willoughby Heights–specific median; Langley-wide (Township and City combined) attached figures for September 2026 are: median sale price $611,000; $513 per sq ft; sale-to-list 97%; 25 days on market — cited exactly as the report states them, as "Condos & Townhomes" COMBINED, never as a townhome-only figure. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- Transit and roads: Highway 1 runs along the north boundary with the 200 Street, 208 Street and 216 Street interchanges; route 501 runs Surrey Central Station to Langley Centre via Fraser Highway and 200 Street, route 372 runs Clayton Heights to Langley Centre via 72 Avenue, and route 595 runs Langley Centre to Maple Ridge via the Golden Ears Bridge; service hubs are the Langley Events Centre / Carvolth Exchange area at 200 Street and Highway 1. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt501.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 7 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every counted strata complex above except Bakerview Estates (10 units — still over five) is well over the threshold; the five-year cycle applies across the local strata stock. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace are flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Willow Park Estates is the one verified 55+ complex; Highgate Terrace's age restriction has no published age and is UNCONFIRMED — if its registered bylaw sets any age other than 55, it cannot be valid in that form today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (Westbrooke, Brighton West, non-strata row houses):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly. The Westbrooke builder source makes the same point: full ownership of building and land, no strata corporation unless owners later create one for a joint project. http://www.qualico.com/news/foxridge-homes-bc-completes-first-phase-of-freehold-row-homes-in-westbrooke-community
- **Township zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs. Willoughby's compact-lot pattern is the Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (Section 403): under s. 403.7, R-CL lots run from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum of 464.5 m², with minimum frontage of 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. Secondary suite cap: 120 m². SSMUH affects the small-lot streets around the townhouse nodes, not the strata complexes themselves. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Plans — reading stage flagged, never upgraded:** the Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025). The Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 (Willoughby Community Plan replacement, Transit Corridor Plan, Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — any page presenting June 15, 2026 repeals or adoptions as done is UNVERIFIED. The research file's statement that the bylaws "replaced" the plan is therefore carried here at reading stage, not as adopted law. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Brighton West presale), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for Brighton West and Compass Commons. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. (The known Brookswood-page 6-plex error is a different area's defect and is not recorded here; the correct Township rule is stated above without a defect entry.) The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the research file for willoughby-heights, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Strata or non-strata — which townhouse am I actually looking at in Willoughby?** Almost always strata: 7 of the 8 completed named complexes verified here are freehold strata (Cascadia, Luxia, Yorkson Creek, Gabriola Park, Kensington Lofts, Willow Park Estates, Bakerview Estates). The fee-simple exception with a name is the Westbrooke row homes by Foxridge — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. Check the Title to Land field before assuming. http://www.qualico.com/news/foxridge-homes-bc-completes-first-phase-of-freehold-row-homes-in-westbrooke-community
- **Q: Can I rent out my Willoughby townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still prints "rentals not allowed" (Willow Park Estates, Highgate Terrace). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse in Willoughby?** One verified: Willow Park Estates, 6467 197 Street — 64 townhouse units, built 1987, listed as a 55+ community. Highgate Terrace nearby is age-restricted on its building record but no source states the age — treat it as UNCONFIRMED 55+ (and it is apartment-form in any case) until the registered bylaws are read. https://www.zolo.ca/langley-real-estate/6467-197-street/46
- **Q: How deep is the choice right now?** Willoughby Heights carried 369 attached listings and 70 attached sales in September 2026 — Langley's deepest attached selection — but SnapStats combines condos and townhomes, so that depth is attached-wide, not townhome-only. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Yorkson Creek — is it 152 units or 171?** UNCONFIRMED: the sources checked give both figures (a building summary at 152, a full-report listing at 171 total strata units). The strata plan and Form B settle it; do not quote either number as fact. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3099880-20738-84-avenue-18-langley-bc-v2y-0j6/brochure
- **Q: What does the plan update mean beside my townhouse?** Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 would replace the Willoughby Community Plan and create the Transit Corridor and Gordon Neighbourhood Plans — but at the last verified stage they were at first/second reading (May 25, 2026) with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026. Price future form off the adopted documents, not the reading-stage drafts. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Yorkson townhouse?** Address-based in SD35 — listings for Yorkson Creek units name Willoughby Elementary, Yorkson Creek Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary as nearby catchments, while the Township sample address (7888 200 Street) returns Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, Peter Ewart Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary. Run the exact address in the locator; never promise a school from the complex name. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new townhouse, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Are the rooftop patios and flex rooms real, or marketing?** Real in the stock: Kensington Lofts listings describe 365 sq ft rooftop patios, Yorkson Creek and Gabriola Park listings describe lower-level flex rooms with separate entrances, and Luxia listings describe rooftop patios on homes without a second ground-level outdoor space. Verify the specific unit's floorplan — features vary by plan, not just by complex. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3103368-8371-202b-street-55-langley-bc-v2y-4k6/brochure
