## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

The hub itself sells no housing, so this section states the rules a buyer meets when they follow the hub into one of the cross-referenced complexes, plus the Surrey-wide rules that apply in all three areas.

**The strata block (applies to all 7 cross-referenced Cloverdale complexes — all are strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, any special levies approved but not yet due, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports).
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a directory or bylaw line reading "rentals not allowed" for any cross-referenced complex is void and is flagged as such wherever it appears (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate, municipal matter (below).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08; Regulation 7.01), with exemptions for spouses/partners, caregivers and legacy residents, expanded May 1, 2023. A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No cross-referenced Cloverdale townhouse complex is verified 55+ — band status is UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read.

**Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse would have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association. None is verified in the Cloverdale board community, so every verified townhouse a buyer reaches from this hub is a strata purchase with a Form B.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, suites and SSMUH (all three guide areas).**

- Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050"), adopted June 15, 2026**; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan). Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.
- **SSMUH:** Surrey implemented the provincial rules on **July 8, 2024** — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (City of Surrey, SSMUH: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing). SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex.
- **Secondary suites:** one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** (City of Surrey, secondary suites: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites).
- **ALR (the Serpentine guide's ground):** on ALR parcels, a principal residence capped at 500 m² with any suite inside it, plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); subdivision requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions; **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) (Agricultural Land Commission: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Short-term rentals:** Surrey permits them only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at **$358/year** as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries $358 and directs the reader to the licence page before quoting (City of Surrey, short-term rentals: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals).
- **Property tax timing:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2; a 5% penalty applies to amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey, property taxes: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). 100% of GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum rebate $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST on new or substantially renovated housing, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000). Exemptions: first-time buyers — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; newly built homes — full exemption to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP limit $60,000 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount by loan-to-value (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research documents no CMHC premium error on the hub page — the known 2.80%–4.00% page error pattern is NOT documented for this slug and is therefore not asserted here.
- **Presales / developer sales.** A presale buyer has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01) — the frame for Charlton's developer sales in the home file.
