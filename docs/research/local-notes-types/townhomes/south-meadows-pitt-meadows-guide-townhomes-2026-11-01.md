## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the pointer complexes are all strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing/directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Coho is over the threshold on its published count (70 units); the other pointer complexes present as multi-unit strata, with the 5+ test confirmed on each Form B. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the §2 complexes is presented as such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid — Coho II's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" is flagged VOID for long-term rentals in-entry in §2 for exactly this reason. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer** — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw, per the City's zoning bylaw amendments staff report; the provincial voiding does not create a short-term rental right here. Do not price any South Meadows home on short-term-rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in §2 carries a verified age restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** none counted here (§2). A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so the strata pointer complexes are never sold with the wrong document set.
- **City plan and zoning:** the governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, covering the whole city including the South Bonson area; it repealed OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007, and no separate neighbourhood plan for South Bonson was found. Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs: houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones — RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha, with residential buildings sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches; R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4 and R-6 up to five (six where adaptable-housing requirements are met near frequent transit), each with a 1,215 sq m minimum lot area for subdivision (a subdivision minimum only); RM-1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha and RM-2 minimum lot area 3,035 sq m. Any specific parcel's actual zone is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. SSMUH unit counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 sq m lot-size threshold and the 400 m frequent-transit test; the City's OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers 2–6 units through secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-guide.md)
