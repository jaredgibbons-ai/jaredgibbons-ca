## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section still matters with zero stock:** if a strata purchase does occur here (a bare-land strata lot or a future project), BC strata housing is governed by the Strata Property Act, with disputes at the Civil Resolution Tribunal — and the block below is the rule set that would apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears in this program. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple stock — stated plainly:** the established Eastern Hillsides stock is fee-simple detached housing: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 5000 and the OCP below) and any registered covenants (including geotechnical covenants) or HOA documents only. Basis: Section 2 above. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **City plan:** the governing plan is the Eastern Hillsides Neighbourhood Plan, Schedule B4 of the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan (Bylaw 2025, No. 5500). The 2050 OCP update replaced the plan's land-use designations with city-wide OCP designations, while the plan's development cells and unit allocations continue to regulate how many units may be developed in the plan area. For reference across the city: Sardis is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same OCP. Under the previous OCP (Bylaw 2014, No. 4025) the Eastern Hillsides Comprehensive Development Plan formed part of the bylaw and Development Permit Area 2 covered the hillside/upland area — where older and newer documents differ, the 2050 OCP and the 2025-updated Neighbourhood Plan are the current sources. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/pdfbook/planning/2040OCP/files/assets/common/downloads/publication.pdf
- **City zoning:** City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs — SHR minimum lot area 360 m² to 990 m² by the slope table (width 12 m, depth 20 m); R3 minimum lot area 360 m² (width 12 m interior, 13.5 m corner); HR minimum lot area 0.2 ha with a community water system and 0.4 ha in all other cases (width 25 m); RH minimum lot area 8 ha (width 25 m). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH — Chilliwack Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024)** brought the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules into Chilliwack's zoning. The Province granted Chilliwack an extension for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park, with a new compliance date of December 31, 2030 — that extension area is not Eastern Hillsides, but it shows Chilliwack's SSMUH rollout is not uniform city-wide. Suite rules for a specific hillside lot are as the City states them in Bylaw 5000 as amended: confirm per property. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **Hillside hazard rules — the defining rules for this area:** steep slopes, main watercourses and ravines cross the plan area and the plan identifies geotechnical hazard areas; the 2017 amendments exist specifically to protect development from rock fall, landslide or earthflow with unstable conditions, and Council directed an expanded Marble Hill development moratorium area in 2017 after continuing ground movement was reported. The City's Greenspace Plan identifies the lower and upper Marble Hill slide areas as undevelopable lands. Hillside lots are also subject to the 2050 OCP's Hillside Development Permit Area (Schedule D-5), under which a development permit is required for works including subdivision of land with 20% or greater slopes. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=1962 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=8295 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2650/2021-05-28%20GSP%20MASTER%202020%20-%20Urban%20Format.pdf
- **Flood / ALR:** the plan area is hillside land above the valley floor, with valley-floor farmland along its north edge; parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED and must be checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder — and where ALR land is met, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). A floodplain finding for a specific hillside lot: UNVERIFIED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Heritage:** the Neighbourhood Plan identifies the historic wagon road (Browlee Road) as a heritage asset to protect, and states wagon roads in the area include Nixon, Ruddock, Allan and Hinkley — a title-and-permit check on lots touching those roads. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=3557
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT general rates are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2% structure, not a single rate. First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. **Chilliwack property-tax penalties (2026):** 5% on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and a further 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026, per the City's property tax FAQ. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a presale is in scope, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). No presale project in this plan area was confirmed: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
