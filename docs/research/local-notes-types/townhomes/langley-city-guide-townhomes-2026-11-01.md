## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Langley City strata townhomes, including the queued Longlea Estates form).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property — any directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Separately, Langley City's own short-term rental rules are UNVERIFIED in this program — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page (research file §4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — stated plainly. None is counted in Langley City (§2.2).

**Langley City zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and taxes — City rules only; Township rules do NOT apply here.**

- **Zoning: City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300** — cite the number; **its adoption date is conflicted and is flagged UNVERIFIED, not resolved** (standing correction). It repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md).
- **OCP:** City of Langley Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3200, adopted November 22, 2021 (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md).
- **SSMUH (City — different from the Township):** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and **6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop**. This 6-unit provision is a CITY rule; it does not exist in the Township's Bylaw 6020, and neither rule may be carried across the municipal boundary (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md).
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed **the lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-14/langley-city.md).
- **City property taxes:** payments received after the July 2 due date incur a penalty; City Hall is at 20399 Douglas Crescent and a 2.2% processing fee applies to credit-card payments. **The penalty percentage for 2026 is UNVERIFIED** — the City page read states a penalty applies but does not state the rate (research file §4; https://www.langleycity.ca/PayPropertyTaxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA, same source).

Worked anchors: $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).

**Q: How did Langley City sell in September 2026?**
A: Detached 64 listings / 8 sold / 13%; Condos & Townhomes 195 / 34 / 17%. No City-only median is published — the combined-Langley medians ($1,345,000 / $611,000) blend in the Township and are not City figures (§1).

**Q: Can a Langley City lot hold six homes?**
A: In the CITY, yes in one case: under Bylaw No. 3284, RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Do not carry that rule into the Township, whose Bylaw 6020 has no 6-unit tier — and do not carry the Township rule into the City (§3).

**Q: What are the City's suite rules?**
A: Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, a suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot, one extra off-street parking space is required, and the suite must be permitted, inspected and recorded in the City's registry (§3).

**Q: Is Langley City the same as the Township of Langley?**
A: No — the City is its own municipality with its own OCP (Bylaw No. 3200) and its own Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300 (whose adoption date is conflicted and UNVERIFIED). A Township fact applied to a City property is an error (§§1, 3).

**Q: Which schools are in Langley City?**
A: Seven Ministry-listed schools physically in the City are named in §1, including Langley Secondary (21405 56 Ave). Alice Brown Elementary's placement in the City is UNVERIFIED, and catchments are UNVERIFIED for every City address (§1).

**Q: Is SkyTrain coming to Langley City?**
A: The guide and the City's vision page both put the Surrey Langley SkyTrain extension's in-service date at late 2029, with Langley City Centre Station east of 203 Street and Industrial Avenue. There is no City station today (§1).

**Q: Can I run a short-term rental in Langley City?**
A: That cannot be answered from this program: the City's STR status is UNVERIFIED — confirm with the City before any Airbnb/Vrbo claim is written (§3).
