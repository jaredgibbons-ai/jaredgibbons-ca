## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Jurisdiction first — the rule that frames everything here:** a University VW property is in the UEL (provincial administration under the University Endowment Land Act), on UBC campus lands, or — for streets east of Blanca — actually in the City of Vancouver, and permitting, bylaws and tax billing differ in each. Establish the jurisdiction before applying any rule in this section. **Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply in the UEL or on UBC lands** — it is a City of Vancouver tax on City of Vancouver properties only, and this sub-area is not in the city; it is stated here expressly because this slug is grouped with Vancouver files in the batch. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/local-governments/improvement-districts-governance-bodies/university-endowment-lands (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **Head-lease diligence (the leasehold townhouse stock):** for any Hampton Place / Wesbrook purchase, order and read the head lease before subject removal — confirm the lease start date, prepaid status, remaining term, and any UBC rights (including faculty/staff resale restrictions on some program homes). The land is never sold outright; the remaining term runs from the project's original lease date and matters to lenders and pricing, most acutely in Hampton Place, the earliest neighbourhood, which carries the shortest remaining terms. https://elementslab.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/Wesbrook-Study_reducedsize_secure2.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **Form B diligence (leasehold strata is still strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Form B is owed for Tapestry's condominium homes (§2) exactly as for any strata — it is also the document that settles Tapestry's MLS-only 55+ claim, which is why that check is flagged as owed in §2. Confirm the UNA services levy and any neighbourhood-specific charges in the strata budget alongside it — the UNA, not a city, delivers municipal-like services to the UBC neighbourhoods. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. For UBC-area homes, verify rental rules building by building in the strata bylaws **and** any UBC/UEL restrictions — student demand is strong but lease-up is tied to the academic calendar (the research file flags the UBC-specific half of that check as general practice guidance, UNVERIFIED against a UBC source). No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any complex in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). The area's one claimed 55+ restriction — Tapestry's — rests on MLS wording only (§2); until the Form B / registered bylaws confirm it, treat "55+" at Tapestry as UNCONFIRMED, however the listings word it. The 55-plus sibling counts Tapestry's 46 condominium homes as the area's one verified 55+ ownership complex on that same flagged basis. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in University VW (§2); the UEL's freehold houses carry UEL Administration rules instead of city rules (the UEL realtor information sheet stresses the UEL is not part of the City of Vancouver or UBC, and that water billing is not included on the UEL property tax bill — check that sheet before closing). https://www.universityendowmentlands.gov.bc.ca/library/Reports/Realtor%20Information%202026-03-12.pdf

Rural tax notices are mailed by May 31, and payment must be received by the due date to count as on time. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there townhouses near UBC I can buy?** Townhouse-form homes exist to buy — Sandringham in Hampton Place and Wordsworth in Wesbrook Village are documented in §2 — but every one is a 99-year prepaid leasehold strata interest on UBC land, which is why this program file counts 0 townhouse complexes: the exclusion is tenure, not absence. A buyer who accepts leasehold gets real townhouse choice here; a buyer who needs freehold or non-leasehold strata should look to the UEL's detached streets or off-campus. (§2 above)
- **Q: What am I actually buying with a UBC leasehold townhouse?** The remaining years of a prepaid 99-year lease — no ground rent for the rest of the term, sellable and mortgageable like other strata — plus a strata lot. The remaining term runs from the project's original lease date, not your purchase date, so a 1994 Hampton Place home and a brand-new Wesbrook home carry very different remaining terms at the same price point. Read the head lease first. (§1 and §3 above)
- **Q: Is Tapestry a townhouse complex?** No. Tapestry at Wesbrook Village is two six-storey apartment-form mid-rises (134 rental apartments + 46 condominium homes, completed 2010); its condominium homes are leasehold strata apartments counted in the condos file. Its rental side (3338 Wesbrook Mall) now operates as a rental retirement residence. Nothing at Tapestry is a townhome in any program file. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Tapestry really 55+?** UNCONFIRMED beyond listing wording. MLS R2919542 states the community is "restricted to 55+ years" and describes a mandatory service package — but no Form B or registered bylaw in any pass confirms either, and that check is still owed. Only a registered 55+ bylaw is enforceable in BC in any case; do not buy, or pass, on the listing line alone. (§2 and §3 above)
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply to a Wesbrook purchase?** No — University VW is not in the City of Vancouver. The UEL is provincially administered and UBC lands are the university's; the 3% Empty Homes Tax is a City of Vancouver tax and does not reach either. (A home east of Blanca that is actually inside the city is a different property — jurisdiction first.) (§3 above)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and when?** On the UEL side, the Province's Surveyor of Taxes under the rural regime: due July 2, 5% penalty on portions unpaid July 2 and a further 5% on portions still unpaid October 31 — and water is billed separately from the tax notice. UBC-neighbourhood owners also carry the UNA services levy through the strata budget. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which elementary school serves a Wesbrook townhouse?** Norma Rose Point School is the Wesbrook Place school — the documented locator run for 5488 Ortona Road returns Norma Rose Point School / University Hill Secondary — while Chancellor-side addresses return University Hill Elementary / University Hill Secondary (5395 Chancellor Boulevard run). The boundary between the two elementary catchments for other addresses is UNVERIFIED; run the specific address. (§1 above)
- **Q: I saw "Wesbrook townhomes" listed for rent — can I buy one instead?** Those are Granite Terrace (3233 Wesbrook Mall), Wesbrook Properties' rental suites and townhomes — rental only, nothing to buy. The buyable townhouse-form stock is the leasehold strata product in §2 (Sandringham, Wordsworth). (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a UBC townhouse to students?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void in BC strata since November 24, 2022 — but verify the specific building's bylaws and any UBC/UEL restrictions before underwriting student rent, and price the academic-calendar lease-up cycle honestly. Some program homes also carry faculty/staff resale restrictions that narrow the buyer pool. (§3 above)
- **Q: What are University-area homes worth?** UNVERIFIED from any published stats source in this program: the SnapStats GVR report contains no City of Vancouver or UBC/UEL data at all. A Paragon/MLS pull is required — and any comparison against a freehold Dunbar home needs the leasehold term priced in, which no stats sheet does for you. (§1 above)

- **The postal address is the trap.** Every home in this sub-area mails as "Vancouver, BC" — yet none of the UEL/UBC ground is in the City of Vancouver, so city taxes (including the Empty Homes Tax), city bylaws and city permitting simply do not apply. Jurisdiction is the first diligence item, ahead of price. (§1 above)
- **Two buildings, two tenures, one project name.** Tapestry's two mid-rises split by address: 3338 Wesbrook Mall is rental (now a rental retirement residence whose operator the Assisted Living Registry names as Seasons, while Chartwell also publishes a page for the same address — a conflict carried unresolved in the siblings) and 3382 Wesbrook Mall is the condominium building. Search by address, not by name. (§2 above)
- **Even the sale sources disagree about Tapestry.** The condos/55-plus passes record that Concert's own press release is dated January 15, 2024 yet describes the Wesbrook sale as happening "on December 2, 2024," and that sale reports' unit total does not match the developer's 134 rental + 46 condominium project description — exact closing date and total unit count are UNCONFIRMED, and the strata documents and head lease are the authority for a specific unit. (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/university-vw-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`)
- **The oldest neighbourhood carries the shortest clock.** Hampton Place was UBC's first campus neighbourhood (1990s) — its townhouses (e.g., Sandringham, built 1994) have the least remaining lease term on campus, which is a pricing and financing fact, not a defect — but it must be in the comparison from the first showing. https://ubcproperties.com/history/
- **A school street name in the research file was wrong — and was caught.** The locator has no "Ortona Avenue"; Norma Rose Point School is at 5488 Ortona **Road**, and the earlier "Avenue" recording could not have produced the catchment result claimed for it. The corrected chain (Ortona Road → Norma Rose Point / University Hill Secondary) is the one cited in §1. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/university-vw.md)
- **The "city" services here come from a neighbourhoods association.** The UNA — not a municipality — runs the Wesbrook and Old Barn community centres and delivers municipal-like services to about 15,000 residents under an agreement with UBC; its levy rides inside strata budgets, which is why a Wesbrook Form B shows a line no Vancouver strata shows. https://www.myuna.ca/about-us/
- **There are no SnapStats numbers for this area.** The SnapStats GVR September 2026 report contains no Vancouver or UBC/UEL section at all — any inventory, sales-ratio or median figure quoted for University VW has no source in the named stats program and should be treated as UNVERIFIED; per-sub-area medians exist only in Paragon.
