## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Heron, the Form B is also where the strata plan number and the settled unit count are confirmed — both are UNCONFIRMED / in conflict on the secondary sources in §2. Directory and listing rental/pet lines are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Heron's 28 units per the listing/developer record put it well over it, on either unit-count basis in §2). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where met: Heron's listing line "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" and Innova30's "rentals allowed with restrictions" line each carry a rental-restriction element that is VOID for long-term rentals (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No source states a 55+ bylaw for Heron or any Steveston North strata — the 55-plus program verifies ZERO 55+ ownership complexes in this sub-area. One-level ranchers on these streets are sometimes marketed to downsizers, but a detached freehold house carries no strata age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/steveston-north-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — City of Richmond rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. None was verified in Steveston North (§2). Note the wording trap: Heron's tenure is "Freehold Strata" — that is strata title (a strata lot on freehold land), not a fee-simple townhouse; the full strata diligence above applies to it.
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 9000, with the Steveston Area Plan as Schedule 2.4 covering this district's southern streets. Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m² and a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use, and the City states it allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots (Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL). https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-north.md)
