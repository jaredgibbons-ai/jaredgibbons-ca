## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Huntsfield Green, to the queued Longlea Estates if purchased as strata, and to every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Huntsfield Green's published restrictions beyond its 55+ position are UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Longlea Estates (58 units on its fresh-verified feature sheet) is well over the threshold on its verified scale; Huntsfield Green's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its position is read off the Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: the directory "rentals not allowed" lines carried for Parkway Estates and Parkway II (§2.5) are **void for long-term rentals**; rentals are allowed, and the lines are carried only with that flag. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Huntsfield Green is verified 55+ in the sibling programs (§2.1) — the City's one verified 55+ townhouse-form complex (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified in the City (§2.4). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**City of Langley zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and taxes.**

- **Zoning: City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300** (cite the number), which repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. **Adoption date: conflicted in the program's sources and UNVERIFIED — flagged, not resolved:** the rules-gaps file's date does not match the City's November 17, 2025 public-hearing notice for Bylaw No. 3300, so no adoption date is stated in this file (research file §4, via batch-13/langleycity.md; sibling assembly, §3).

- **SSMUH:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by **Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024** — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a secondary suite must not exceed **the lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the gross floor area of the building; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
