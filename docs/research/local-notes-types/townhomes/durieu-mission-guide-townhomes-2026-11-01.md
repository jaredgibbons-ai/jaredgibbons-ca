## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (the BC standard frame — stated because buyers ask, though no strata townhome is counted in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. **No Durieu purchase in §2 carries a Form B** — there is no strata here; Durieu purchases run on title, FVRD rules and registered covenants (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw in a strata. With no strata in Durieu, a Durieu rental question is a tenancy and local-government question, not a strata-bylaw question — do not import a directory "rentals not allowed" line into a Durieu purchase (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in a strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). No Durieu strata carries any age bylaw — there is no Durieu strata, and no verified 55+ ownership stock is carried for Durieu in the 55-plus sibling (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, FVRD rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Durieu (§2.2); Durieu's acreage purchases are fee-simple LAND purchases on the same no-strata diligence track.
