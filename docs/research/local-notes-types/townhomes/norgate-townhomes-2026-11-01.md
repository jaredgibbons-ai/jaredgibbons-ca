## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section is mostly a contrast section:** Norgate has no strata townhouse to apply strata diligence to (§2). The rules below are stated in full because the format requires them and because a Norgate buyer cross-shopping the counted Pemberton NV townhouses (§2) needs them — and because the fee-simple reality of the subdivision itself is the rule most often mis-stated for this area.
- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses — applies to the Pemberton NV stock cross-referenced in §2, not to anything inside the subdivision):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no Form B for a Norgate house purchase — no strata exists to issue one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Neither rule touches a fee-simple Norgate house. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Norgate strata exists to carry such a line; any "rentals not allowed" line met on the Marine Drive apartment stock or the Pemberton NV townhouses is VOID in-entry and must be flagged as such wherever it is met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No Norgate complex carries any age bylaw — the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — the governing frame inside the subdivision.** A Norgate house purchase carries **NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees** — District municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title govern instead. Do not import strata expectations into a Norgate rancher purchase, or house expectations into a strata townhouse purchase in Lions Gate Village.
- **District plan:** the District of North Vancouver's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 7900**; it directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods — Norgate's nearest growth node is the Lions Gate Village Centre at Capilano Road and Marine Drive, planned under the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan. **The City of North Vancouver's OCP/Zoning numbers (8400/6700) do not apply west of the municipal boundary and are never cited for this ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **District zoning and added homes:** **Zoning Bylaw 3210** (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; a secondary suite may be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size, and coach houses may rise to two storeys. SSMUH caution for this bench: the District's GEOweb classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 above 280 m², and up to 6 above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — but a March 2, 2026 District Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area, which includes Norgate, from SSMUH. Verify the specific lot on GEOweb before relying on density. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **District property taxes (2026):** property taxes are billed by the District of North Vancouver; 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value (municipal rate 1.83419), and combined single-family utilities total $2,571 in 2026 (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/norgate.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 purchase (round illustration only — no area median exists to use, no SnapStats for North Vancouver) with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The CMHC premium-range error documented elsewhere in this program (a live page stating 2.80%–4.00%) is **not documented for this slug** in its research or siblings and is therefore **not asserted** here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

(§1 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Norgate address?** Norgate Xwemélch'stn Community Elementary (K–7, an IB Primary Years Programme school in the subdivision) and Carson Graham Secondary (8–12), as the research file states them. SD44 catchments are address-based — confirm the exact address with the district. (§1 above)
