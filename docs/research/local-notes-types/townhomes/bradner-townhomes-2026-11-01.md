## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Bradner has no townhomes, so this section leads with the ALR and farm rules that keep it that way, then the Abbotsford and provincial rules that govern the nearest real townhouse purchases and any house purchase here.

- **No stratification in the ALR without ALC permission:** depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and agricultural land must not be subdivided unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). This is the single rule that decides the townhouse question in Bradner. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR housing caps:** one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m² gross floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction on the ALC's stated terms; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; an additional detached residence for farm use or another permitted use capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm-class thresholds — the full three tiers (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and must never be published for Bradner):** (1) $2,500 in farm income on parcels of 0.8–4 ha; (2) $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; (3) $10,000 in farm income on parcels under 0.8 ha; farm classification applications must be submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **City zoning and plan — standing correction applied:** Bradner lands are A1/A2 Agricultural under the City of Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014. The CURRENT city plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025). The batch-8 research file's plan wording (an OCP "Adopted June 27, 2016") and any "2600-2016" / "2500-2016" reference are STALE — apply Abbotsforward 2050 and flag the stale references wherever they appear. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development/development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH in Abbotsford — bylaw number UNCONFIRMED, never assert "3560-2024":** what is confirmed for Abbotsford generally is that the City's 2025 zoning updates changed the secondary-suite maximum (in most zones) to less than 50% of the size of the principal unit, and coach/garden suites are capped at 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule ONLY). SSMUH does not create Bradner townhouses — the A1/A2 zoning and the ALR no-stratification rule are the controlling framework here, and on ALR land the suite must sit inside the principal residence in any case. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Fee-simple reality in Bradner, stated plainly:** a Bradner house purchase has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal zoning, ALR rules, and any registered covenants on title govern. The strata block below applies only if the buyer redirects to a townhouse elsewhere in Abbotsford.
- **For a strata townhouse elsewhere in Abbotsford — Form B, depreciation reports, CRF:** the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59); since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must hold a depreciation report on a five-year cycle (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold), and since November 1, 2023 stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF annually. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals and age (provincial):** long-term rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line as void in-entry; short-term rentals can still be restricted. The only legal age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with the May 1, 2023 expanded exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Tax timing:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **GST and rebates:** the FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate is 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. **PTT, compression flagged:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row compresses that 3% + 2%; first-time buyer exemption on the first $500,000 up to $835,000 (partial to $860,000), Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). **HBP** $60,000; **FHSA** $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; **CMHC** premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse in Bradner?** No — there is no townhouse complex, strata or fee-simple, confirmed in Bradner in either research pass, and the September 2026 board data shows zero attached listings. The closest townhouse stock is in Poplar and Central/West Abbotsford (see those files). (Research file, §2 and §5)
- **Q: Could a Bradner farm home be turned into townhouses or condos later?** Not as of right. Depositing a strata plan is a subdivision of ALR land and cannot be done without the ALC's permission under ALCA s. 21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What housing CAN be added on a Bradner farm parcel?** One principal residence up to 500 m², a secondary suite inside that residence only, and one additional small residence for farm use or another permitted use — 90 m² maximum on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on larger parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the size-capped "additional residence" tied to farm use or another permitted use. Abbotsford's general suite cap (less than 50% of the principal unit) and coach/garden cap (15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m²) do not override the ALR rule on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with the application due by October 31 for the following taxation year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Is Bradner in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** Partly — it is a rural agricultural community with lands zoned A1 and A2 Agricultural, part of it in the ALR; whether a specific parcel is in the ALR must be confirmed for that parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Which schools serve Bradner?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. For the test address 5396 Bradner Road, the SD34 School Locator returns Bradner Elementary (K–5) → Eugene Reimer Middle (6–8) → Rick Hansen Secondary (9–12). Run any other address through the locator. (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01, per the research file)
- **Q: If I buy a townhouse in Poplar or Clearbrook instead, what strata documents matter most?** The Form B (fees, CRF balance, parking/storage), the depreciation report (mandatory on a five-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024), two years of minutes and the registered bylaws — especially any 55+ bylaw, the only age bylaw still enforceable. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhouse I buy elsewhere in Abbotsford?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022; a strata can still restrict short-term rentals, and a tenant in a 55+ complex must still satisfy the age bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there any Bradner-specific tax break?** The City offers a Revitalization Tax Exemption for eligible properties in designated revitalization areas, but no Bradner-specific exemption was confirmed in the material read — treat any claimed Bradner exemption as UNVERIFIED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/tax-incentives-exemptions
