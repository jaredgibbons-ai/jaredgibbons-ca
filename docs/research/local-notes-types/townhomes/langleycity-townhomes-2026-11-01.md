## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Huntsfield Green, to the queued Longlea Estates if purchased as strata, and to every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Huntsfield Green's published restrictions beyond its 55+ position are UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Longlea Estates (58 units on its fresh-verified feature sheet) is well over the threshold on its verified scale; Huntsfield Green's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its position is read off the Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines carried for Parkway Estates and Parkway II (§2.3) are **flagged void in-entry** on exactly this rule — they are apartment buildings, but the same voiding governs any identical line in a townhouse strata's older bylaws.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Huntsfield Green is verified 55+ in the sibling programs (§2.1) — the City's one verified 55+ townhouse-form complex.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified in the City (§2.4). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**City of Langley zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and taxes.**

- **Zoning: City of Langley Zoning Bylaw, 2025, No. 3300**, which repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 (cited at s. 1.1 of the new bylaw). **Adoption date: UNVERIFIED — flagged, not resolved:** the program's rules-gaps file gives a date that does not match the City's November 17, 2025 public-hearing notice for Bylaw No. 3300, so no adoption date is stated in this file (research file §4: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/public-hearing-noticepdf).
- **OCP:** adopted November 22, 2021, built on the "Nexus of Community" vision (research file §4).
- **SSMUH:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by **Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024** — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw 2025 No. 3300):** a suite must not exceed **the lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the gross floor area of the building (s. 1.3 / s. 2.4(c)); the registered owner must reside on the lot (owner-occupancy, s. 2.4(c)(i)); at least one off-street parking space in addition to the two normally required for a single detached use; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry (research file §4).
- **Garden suites / carriage homes (Bylaw No. 3300, s. 2.4(d)):** permitted in the R1 zone; maximum gross ground floor area 100 m²; maximum total floor area of accessory buildings on an R1 lot 60 m²; registered owner must reside on the lot (research file §4). Note the contrast with the Township, where a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under a different bylaw (§2.2) — City and Township suite rules are never interchangeable.
- **Short-term rentals in Langley City: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule could be read on an official City page in this program (research file §4). Do not market a City townhouse as an STR on an uncited rule.

**Q: How many units can a Langley City single-family lot hold?**
A: Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1/RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (§3).

**Q: What are the City's suite rules?**
A: Under Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, owner-occupancy, one extra off-street parking space, and permit/inspection/registry recording. Garden suites and carriage homes in R1 cap at 100 m² ground floor area with a 60 m² accessory-building total (§3).

**Q: Can I rent out a City strata townhouse long-term?**
A: A rental-restriction bylaw is void since November 24, 2022 — including the directory "rentals not allowed" lines carried for the Parkway buildings, flagged void in §2.3. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the City's own STR rule is UNVERIFIED, so no Airbnb claim is made from City rules (§3).

**Q: When is the SkyTrain coming, and does it change City townhouse value today?**
A: The City's own vision page states "SkyTrain is coming to Langley City by the end of 2029"; there is no station in the City today, and current bus routes/frequencies are UNVERIFIED in this pass. Any anticipation premium is a market claim this file does not make (§1).
