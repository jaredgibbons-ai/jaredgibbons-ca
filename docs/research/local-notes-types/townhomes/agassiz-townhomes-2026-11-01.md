## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

All six counted complexes are strata, so the strata block applies in full; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast for any non-strata product a buyer cross-shops.

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that settles every UNCONFIRMED plan, unit-count and restriction item in §2 — including the Gallagher Landing and Sawyers Landing upgrades. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where met: Town Centre Gable's directory "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element is VOID (§2). Huckleberry Lane's listing-stated "Rentals Allowed" is consistent with the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted Agassiz complex is verified 55+ in any source checked — Huckleberry Lane's listing states the opposite ("no age restrictions") — and Maplewood Village's 55+ label is a bare-land strata matter, not a townhouse fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — District of Kent rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the townsite (§2); the townsite's detached houses are fee-simple, but they are not townhouses.
- **Municipal law here is District of Kent law, not "Agassiz" law:** zoning is the District of Kent Zoning Bylaw No. 1219, 2001 (as amended); the plan is the District of Kent Official Community Plan 2025, which designates the townsite core "Commercial – Town Centre" and directs growth into the townsite to protect farmland. Kent Bylaw No. 1737, adopted June 10, 2024, created the RS1 zone, under which serviced residential lots in Agassiz can take up to four dwelling units without rezoning, subject to conditions (the District's SSMUH route); the District also permits secondary suites in single-family homes in all residential zones. https://www.kpu.ca/isfs/foodpolicydatabase/district-kent-zoning-bylaw-no-1219-2001 and https://www.kentbc.ca/media/tdabxdtl/district-of-kent-ssmuh-information-guide-final.pdf (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/agassiz-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Floodplain rules:** Agassiz sits on the Fraser River floodplain behind dikes. Flood Protection & Erosion is OCP Section 5.5; the District has a Floodplain Bylaw that applications must comply with, plus area-specific flood construction levels — no single townsite-wide FCL exists in the sources checked, so the FCL is a per-property check on every townhouse purchase here. https://media-003-ca.cdn.govstack.com/kentbc-ca/media/2m3dsbwb/official-community-plan_2025-update.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)
- **District of Kent taxes:** taxes are District of Kent municipal taxes (there is no separate "Agassiz" tax bill). The District's 2026 schedule (per $1,000 of taxable value) sets the Class 1 Residential total municipal rate at 2.80493, plus a separate dyking line of 0.03076, for an all-in residential total of 4.65328 including hospital, regional district, MFA, BC Assessment and school tax. Notices are mailed in early June and due July 15 (or the next business day), with penalties after — penalty amounts beyond "penalties after the due date" are UNCONFIRMED from a District source in this file; do not apply any other municipality's or the rural-area penalty regime to a townsite property. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/mxrbvfky/tax-rates-summary-2026.pdf and https://www.kentbc.ca/living-here/home-and-property/property-taxes/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)
- **ALR frame:** the townsite is ringed by ALR farmland, and some townsite-edge "Residential – Future" lands are still in the ALR and stay in farm use until an ALC exclusion is approved (a Council-approved Neighbourhood Plan is required first). On ALR parcels: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); the principal residence is capped at 500 m², a suite is permitted only within the principal residence, and an additional residence is capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.kentbc.ca/media/vpwaiuh5/2026-01-21-dok-2025-ocp-update.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/agassiz.md)

- **Taxes and rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (relevant to any remaining developer sale at a newer complex such as MountView). **FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2%. Worked example, inputs stated: fair market value $500,000 (a round illustration only — no Agassiz median exists in any published source in this program), fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**. Exemptions: first-time buyers — full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
