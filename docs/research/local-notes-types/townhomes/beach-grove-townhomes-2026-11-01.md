## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (The Pillars townhouse component is strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a mixed complex like The Pillars, the Form B is also where the strata's structure across its apartment and townhouse components is confirmed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold (no complex in Section 2 is verified as sub-5-lot — Windgrove's published count is 12, and The Pillars' component count is UNCONFIRMED, so its threshold position is read off the strata plan). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in Section 2: Heron Cove's directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The Pillars' building source states expressly that it has no age restrictions (55-plus sibling); no Beach Grove complex in Section 2 is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Beach Grove (Section 2) — a listing calling a Pillars townhouse "fee simple" contradicts the strata-fee record and needs a title check.

- **The "Beach Grove townhouse" is a corridor product, not a beach product.** The verified townhouse component stands on 56 Street at the pocket's west edge; the live page's own feed is mostly detached houses on Enderby Avenue, View Crescent and Beach Grove Road, plus the one townhome at 1700 56 Street. A buyer searching the page name expecting waterfront townhomes will not find one. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **One complex, two programs, no double count.** The Pillars is the Tsawwassen overlap case in miniature: its penthouses are condos, its ground-oriented homes are townhouses, and the two sibling programs split it by address and form. The East guide townhomes file's older blanket line (the whole complex as apartment form, never in a townhomes file) is flagged at the top of this file as the divergent basis this file does not follow — a researcher reading only that file would miss Beach Grove's only townhouse stock. (Condos sibling and East guide townhomes file, both on disk.)
- **The 2.9 m number governs this shore.** SD3 sets the habitable floor at 2.9 metres GSC datum with a 2.3 m minimum land elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback — and the zoning bylaw, unusually, states no Beach Grove flood construction level of its own, so the FCL question goes to the permit area and the City, not the zoning text. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **A seawall has its own rule here.** Zoning s. 6.2.3 lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback on Boundary Bay lots along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **The dike trail is the amenity, and it is a working dike.** Boundary Bay Regional Park's Dyke Trail runs on the Boundary Bay dike system the City maintains as flood protection (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure city-wide) — recreation on top of infrastructure, which is why foreshore work is permitted, not casual. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **The old area-plan name still surfaces in City paperwork.** A City report on 1728 Beach Grove Road described the property under the Tsawwassen Area Plan — that plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the current OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan with no lettered schedules. Marketing or reports quoting a current "Tsawwassen Area Plan" — or a "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" — are quoting a superseded document or a conflation. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/beach-grove.md)
- **Delta's zoning bylaw number is contested in the sources — do not quote one.** City material and the research files carry competing citations (7600, 2017 vs "8400, 2025"); what governs a Beach Grove purchase in practice is the parcel's own zone sheet (RD2 for the confirmed waterfront sample) and, for The Pillars, its registered strata bylaws.
