## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in BC. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home

- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes, after the open):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line a host repeats at a townhouse open is void for long-term rentals, and is flagged VOID in-entry wherever it appears in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) and registered covenants/HOA documents only, stated plainly. If the townhouse on the feed is fee-simple, there are no strata documents to ask for at the open; diligence is title, zoning and covenants. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in the Cloverdale board community (home file, §2).
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. One secondary suite per dwelling unit is permitted, capped at **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; an existing unpermitted suite is not retroactively legalized. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it to a client. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw even though long-term rental bylaws are void. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not area-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; a March 12, 2026 date appears in other federal material for the same Royal Assent — CRA News No. 122 states March 13 and March 13 governs in this program. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2% structure, not a separate rate — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted). First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the frame if an event on this feed is a developer/showhome event rather than a resale open (the Charlton showhome card on the first page is exactly that case). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
