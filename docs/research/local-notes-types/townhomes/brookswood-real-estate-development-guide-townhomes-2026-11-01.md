## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the Bromley presale strata on completion and to any Brookswood strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For a presale like Bromley, the disclosure statement and the eventual Form B are the diligence, not developer marketing (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Bromley's 26 homes put it over the threshold once complete. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Brookswood strata carries a verified age bylaw (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome (the queued Oxford Mews form) would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — stated plainly. None is counted in Brookswood (§2.2).

**Township of Langley zoning, plans, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **Community plan:** the Brookswood-Fernridge Community Plan covers Brookswood-Fernridge (Booth, Fernridge and Rinn neighbourhood plans under it); the Township states it allows previously undeveloped areas to grow while protecting trees and watercourses (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/news/brookswoodfernridge-community-plan-adopted.aspx).
- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500. Read from Bylaw 2500 s. 110.1 in Township council reports: SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² / frontage 40 m; SR-2 minimum 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 minimum 1,765 m² / 27.45 m. RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf). R-CL s. 403 context verified elsewhere in this program: 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot figures.
- **SSMUH — and the defect asserted for THIS slug only:** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on eligible lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger eligible lots up to 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible; Transit Oriented Area lots are not eligible; **NO 6-plex tier exists in the Township.** **Defect asserted:** THIS development page repeatedly presents a 6-plex tier for transit-adjacent lots ("Lots near frequent transit corridors may qualify for up to six units") — that claim is documented in THIS file's own research (research file §§1, 4) and is WRONG under Bylaw 6020. This assertion is slug-specific: the Brookswood AREA guide makes no such claim and no defect is recorded there (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx; https://jaredgibbons.ca/brookswood-real-estate-development-guide.html).
- **Suites and coach houses:** a Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102); a coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 second unit, never strata. Live conflict flagged, not resolved: s. 102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the Township's January 2025 guide says not permitted. On ALR land, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and the ALC frame permits one principal residence and, subject to conditions, one additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED per parcel (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Township taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026; utility fees are billed separately (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).
