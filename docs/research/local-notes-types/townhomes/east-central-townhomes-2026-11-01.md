## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that settles Fraser Glen's and Dogwood Lane's actual age/pet bylaws — directory lines are not the bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. The 1990–1996 stock in the carried roster (Dogwood Lane, Fraser Glen, Alouette Estates if strata) is exactly the age band where roof, piping and envelope work shows up in those reports. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" lines for Fraser Glen (carried roster) and Sommerville Gardens (queue) are flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners, defined children). Program position for this city, stated plainly: Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses — a listing calling Fraser Glen or Dogwood Lane "55+" is marketing until the registered bylaw is read, and Haney Pioneer Village is 60+ co-op, never 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area in any sibling. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. If Alouette Estates (or any queue item) proves fee-simple on title, its diligence is title-and-municipal only; until then its tenure is UNCONFIRMED and neither frame may be quoted for it.
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs; houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%), and Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same bylaw. One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible lots may carry up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged in every file that meets it, resolved in none:** the research and sibling files cite the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan as Bylaw 7060-2014 in some sources and Bylaw 7998-2024 in others, with an update adopted November 25, 2025. Neither number is asserted as settled in this file; quote planning policy from a current City document for the specific parcel. The Town Centre Area Plan (OCP s. 10.4) covers the west end; its update expanded boundaries to incorporate River Bend, consolidated designations and added a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher densities around the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station; Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers those two TOAs. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Lougheed Corridor — ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 (City record):** Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was adopted Jan 27, 2026, the corridor running from the City's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road — cited here as adopted, with the earlier contrary reading in program material noted in Section 5 of the guide sibling. **Albion Area Plan:** adopted Oct 28, 2025 — east of this sub-area, part of the same fall 2025 update series. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Flood and escarpment:** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the Fraser River Escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and lots within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. The 116 Avenue townhouse corridor sits north of the river flats, but status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Presales (REDMA):** where any townhome unit is bought as a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2% and worked examples must show the tier math; exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%.
