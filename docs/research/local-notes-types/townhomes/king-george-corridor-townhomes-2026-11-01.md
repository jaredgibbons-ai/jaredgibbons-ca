## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Peninsula Village the Form B is also where the published 55+ position is actually proved — listing statements are not proof. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Both counted complexes are over the five-lot threshold on their verified scale (Peninsula Village 95 homes; Keystone on its complex scale — confirm per complex on the Form B); a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — neither counted complex is such a strata. Peninsula Village's own 2014 depreciation report (Strata Plan LMS 133) is the corridor example of the document set a buyer receives — and its age is itself a diligence point: ask for the most recent report. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/ and https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted entry in §2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" directory line; where any older directory or bylaw text for these complexes states one, it is void on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Peninsula Village is published as 55+ in listings ("One person 55+") — the registered bylaw on the Form B governs. Keystone listings state no age restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in the corridor (§2) — a listing calling a counted complex "fee simple" contradicts the verified record and needs a title check.
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. The corridor's 1995 concept-plan designations were superseded through Corporate Report R246 (endorsed December 2, 2024), so an old disclosure citing the 1995 plan is citing history, not current zoning. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs, shown parcel by parcel in the City's COSMOS map; its SSMUH framework (in force for this area since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — boulevard-adjacent house lots may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area, so check the parcel in COSMOS. The SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite runs 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it to a client. A strata can still restrict short-term rentals by bylaw even though long-term rental bylaws are void. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals

- **The name is the trap: one road, two markets.** "King George Corridor" on this page, in the City plan and in the board data means 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River only. A Newton or City Centre listing on the same boulevard is a different sub-area with different numbers, and the Whalley towers on this road are counted in a different file entirely. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/king-george-corridor.md)
- **South Surrey's best-known 55+ townhouse complex and its nearest 55+ neighbour are in different MLS sub-areas.** Peninsula Village (2500 152 St, King George Corridor filing) and Bishop's Green (2533 152 St, Sunnyside Park filing) stand a few blocks apart on the same street, both gated 55+-described townhouse complexes — a buyer searching only one sub-area's page misses the other. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl2464/listing/Listing_780_extra/128267.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/2533-152-street/98
- **One listing also calls Peninsula Village "The Peninsula."** Same address (2500 152 St), same strata plan (LMS 133), two marketing names — an alert set on one name does not cover the other. https://www.jagsidhu.com/listing/r3097116-2500-152-street-48-surrey-bc-v4p-1m8/brochure
- **Keystone's own listings argue about which sub-area it is in.** One current listing records Subdivision: King George Corridor; others record Elgin Chantrell for the same 2925 King George Boulevard address. - **The corridor's plan is older than most of its buildings and no longer the law.** The 1995 concept plan's designations were superseded when the Surrey 2050 OCP update was carried through Corporate Report R246 in December 2024, and the current OCP is Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — marketing that quotes the 1995 plan, or the replaced Bylaw No. 18020, is quoting a superseded framework. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south
