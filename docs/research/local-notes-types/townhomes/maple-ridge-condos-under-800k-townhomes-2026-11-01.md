### Worked examples — inputs stated, illustrative arithmetic only (not market facts, not band counts)

- **EXAMPLE 1 — resale townhouse at the community attached median (inputs stated).** Inputs: purchase price $525,000 (the September 2026 Maple Ridge condos & townhomes combined median, used as an arithmetic input only — it is not a townhouse price); resale (no GST); buyer does NOT qualify for any PTT exemption. Gross PTT: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $325,000 = $6,500, total **$8,500** — taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers, as every price under $800,000 is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **EXAMPLE 2 — same price, qualifying first-time buyer (inputs stated).** Inputs: price $525,000; resale; buyer meets all first-time program conditions (fair market value $835,000 or less, 0.5 ha or smaller, only residential improvements, principal residence). Exemption on the first $500,000: $2,000 + (2% × $300,000 = $6,000) = $8,000 saved. Net PTT: $8,500 − $8,000 = **$500**. The example shows why, anywhere in this band, the buyer-side conditions — not the price — decide the exemption file by file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **EXAMPLE 3 — top of the band (inputs stated).** Inputs: price $799,000; resale; no exemption. Gross PTT: $2,000 + (2% × $599,000 = $11,980) = **$13,980**. The 3% tier and the further 2% over $3,000,000 do not arise anywhere in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the confirmed Maple Ridge townhouse stock is strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. For tenure-UNCONFIRMED complexes (Alouette Estates; six Cottonwood names), whether a Form B regime applies is unconfirmed until title is pulled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. In this band the depreciation report is the levy-risk document — the research file deliberately does NOT repeat the live page's strata-fee and deductible ranges, which are site copy verified on no official source. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry (Carriage Lane's directory line is so flagged in its home file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses; the 55+ labels on Fraser Glen and Dogwood Lane are listing-stated only, and Haney Pioneer Village is 60+ co-op tenure, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** a genuinely fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Maple Ridge in any area townhomes file on disk (East Central 0, Cottonwood 0, Southwest 0), so an under-$800K "no-strata townhouse" listing needs its tenure proved from title. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan and zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw **7600-2019** governs (its s. 402.8(h) floodplain suite prohibition is in the floodplain entry below). The OCP citation is **CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024)** — flagged, never resolved by guessing. The Town Centre Area Plan update and Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 (Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs) frame the core stock this band draws from; the **Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025** and the **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — both cited as adopted. SSMUH: eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws, settled lot by lot. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town and https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP (Program standing corrections, 2026-11-01)
- **Floodplain at the south edge of the core:** the Port Haney / River Road edge sits along the Fraser River — flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties — for a strata building, flood status is a building-level check on title and in the strata documents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Property taxes (Maple Ridge):** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **PTT in this band:** general tiers 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 (never a single "5% over $3M" rate — and neither top tier arises under $800,000). **First-time home buyers' program:** full exemption requires fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), 0.5 ha or smaller, only residential improvements, principal-residence use only, exempting PTT on the first $500,000; partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all the program's other conditions. **Newly built home exemption:** full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (series frame). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Federal buyer frame:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) — new builds only; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST applies to new construction only, not to resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a unit under the cap is a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). A presale listing under $800,000 is not resale band stock and is never counted as such in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Schools:** SD42 catchments are address-based; SD42 has no middle tier — elementary Kindergarten to Grade 7, secondary Grades 8 to 12. For the downtown sample address in the underlying research (11897 227 Street), the district locator gives Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary, with French Immersion options at Maple Ridge Elementary (early), Eric Langton Elementary (late) and Maple Ridge Secondary — choice programs, not catchment schools; catchments for other addresses are not confirmed. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/maple-ridge-condos-under-800k.md)
