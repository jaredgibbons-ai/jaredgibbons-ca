## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every complex counted in the count home — all are strata — and to any Murrayville strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — the "rentals not allowed" line recorded for 21937 48 Avenue (§2.1) is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). This is the exact point Cedar Crest Estates turns on (§2.1) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Not applicable to the counted roster (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.

**Township of Langley zoning, plans, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **Community plan:** the Murrayville Community Plan is Bylaw 1988 No. 2661 (adopted April 17, 1989), as amended including by the 2025 OCP Update, Bylaw No. 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025) (§1). The Township's Development Permits page confirms there are currently two designated heritage conservation areas in the Township — the Fort Langley Community Plan and the Murrayville Community Plan — each with heritage guidelines in its plan, and a Heritage Alteration Permit is required before demolition, subdivision or redevelopment in one (condos guide sibling §3; https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-permits.aspx).
- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs Murrayville. **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment (SSMUH) Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on municipal water AND sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at 280 m² or smaller or 4 units between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and there is **NO 6-plex tier** in the Township (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx). **Willoughby Plan frame, for contrast only:** the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025), and its Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 stand at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY (hearing directed June 15, 2026) — reading-stage bylaws are never presented as adopted law; neither instrument governs a Murrayville lot in any case.
- **Suites and coach houses:** a Township secondary suite is capped at 120 m² (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102); a coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500, never strata. A live s. 102-vs-January-2025-guide conflict on the coach-house form is flagged in this program, not resolved (condos guide sibling §3).
- **Township taxes:** due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026 (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST applies to new construction, not resales — Murrayville's counted roster is resale stock (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA, same source).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. **Worked anchors (general tiers):** $700,000 → **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
