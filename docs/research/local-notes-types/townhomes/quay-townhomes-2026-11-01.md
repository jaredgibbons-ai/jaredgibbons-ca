## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Floodplain Zone 3 is the defining diligence item for Quayside stock — townhouse-form units included:** the City's floodplain study places the Quay in Floodplain Zone 3, the Fraser River waterfront zone, where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. For a ground-oriented townhouse unit at a tower's base, confirm the unit's own floor level against the building's Flood Construction Level compliance, plus the strata insurance and deductible position, before subject removal. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf and http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Form B diligence (every strata complex in Section 2, including RiverSky 2's townhouse units):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A townhouse unit inside a tower strata is a strata lot like any other — there is no separate townhouse Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every Quay strata named here is well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. RiverSky 1's listing line already reads rentals allowed; any "rentals not allowed" line found in an older Quay building's directory entry or Form B package is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason and should be flagged in-entry wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file checked every named Quayside strata and found NO verified 55+ housing of any tenure in the Quay — no Quay building, townhouse-form units included, may be marketed as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** there is no verified fee-simple townhouse stock in the Quay; stated plainly for the form — a fee-simple townhome has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, and diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Nothing in Section 2 is bought that way.
- **City zoning and plan:** waterfront zoning is in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — multiple-unit residential and comprehensive development districts for the Quayside area; minimum lot sizes are zone-specific and a Quay-specific minimum is UNVERIFIED. The Downtown Community Plan (Schedule C of the OCP) governs the downtown waterfront, under Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017). **The Townhouse Districts in Bylaw 6680 are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist; this research file uses no RT-A/RT-B wording, so no correction is needed in-file.** https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** no SSMUH or suite rule specific to the Quay's strata waterfront was confirmed on a City source in the research file — the stock is strata apartment form, where suites are not an accessory-use question; a Quay-specific SSMUH figure is UNCONFIRMED and none is published here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 3, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a New Westminster presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a townhouse complex on the Quay?** No standalone one — the verified count is 0 strata and 0 fee-simple. The waterfront's stock is apartment stratas; the closest townhouse-form product is the TH-series homes at the base of RiverSky 2, which are strata lots inside that tower's plan (EPS5412). https://www.zolo.ca/new-westminster-real-estate/988-quayside-drive/th2
- **Q: If I buy a RiverSky 2 townhouse unit, what am I actually buying?** A freehold strata lot in a 33-storey tower strata — full Form B diligence, tower depreciation report, tower CRF and tower insurance, including the flood position — not a small townhouse strata's simpler file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/quay.md)
- **Q: What does Floodplain Zone 3 mean for my purchase?** The Quay is the City's Fraser River waterfront flood zone: many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level, but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. Confirm the specific building's compliance and insurance position lot by lot, especially for ground-level units. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Quay strata home long-term?** Yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is any Quay building 55+?** No — the sibling 55-plus file checked every named Quayside strata and found no age restriction anywhere; do not buy here expecting a 55+ bylaw. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/quay-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: How active is this market, honestly?** The Quay sold 16 of 59 "Condos & Townhomes" listings in September 2026 (27% sales ratio) — the strongest sub-area ratio among the New Westminster files in its research batch — against a community combined median that eased to $560,000. Building-by-building pricing beats city-wide averages here. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: What are Westminster Landing and Riverbend — can I buy in?** No. They are co-operative / non-market complexes in the City's non-market inventory (84 and 72 units, mixed tenures) — membership housing, not strata title for sale. https://newwestcity.ca/database/rte/Housing%20web%20update%20-%20Non-Market%20details.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Quayside Drive address?** For the sample station address (800 Carnarvon Street): École Qayqayt Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary — but SD40 catchments are address-based, so run the exact Quayside Drive address in the locator. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q: Two stations — is that real for every building?** New Westminster Station and Columbia Station (both Expo Line) sit at the top of the waterfront and route 103 runs Quayside — but door-to-station walks lengthen for the east-end buildings; measure the specific building's walk rather than pricing a generic "two-station" claim. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new home, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
