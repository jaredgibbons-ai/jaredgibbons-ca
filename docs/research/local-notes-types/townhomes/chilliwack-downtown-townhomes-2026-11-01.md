## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Cedar Court — the counted complex is strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, approved special levies not yet due, the CRF balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). Cedar Court's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so which side of the threshold it sits on is UNCONFIRMED — the Form B package answers it.
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a strata can no longer restrict rentals, whatever an older directory or bylaw summary still prints (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2 and Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions for spouses/partners, caregivers and legacy residents, including the May 1, 2023 expansion. A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No age bylaw for Cedar Court is published on a source checked: UNCONFIRMED. Two downtown apartment buildings (Ivy Green, Spadina Regency) ARE verified 55+ — that status belongs to those buildings, not to the townhouse stock (§2.2).

**Fee-simple contrast.** No counted complex here is fee-simple. A true fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse would have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association, stated plainly so a buyer does not import strata diligence into a non-strata purchase (Strata Property Act scope: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_01).

**Chilliwack zoning, OCP, suites and SSMUH.**

- OCP: Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — Schedule B3 Downtown Neighbourhood Plan for this ground, Schedule D-6 Downtown development permit area in the core (https://chilliwack.com/ocp). Zoning: Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 (https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 is the City tax page; the bylaw record is via the City's OCP/bylaw pages cited in the research file).
- SSMUH: Chilliwack's amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Bylaw 5000, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3–4 units on urban-growth-boundary parcels zoned for single-detached and duplex housing (research file §4). SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex.
- Suites/ADUs under Bylaw 5000: a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space; only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot (secondary suite, coach house or garden suite); garden suites capped at 100 m²; ADU parking 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite, 2 spaces for a 2+-bedroom coach or garden suite (research file §4). Inside a strata townhouse, the strata's bylaws govern first.
- Property taxes (City ground): notices mailed end of May, due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 (https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, for qualifying agreements from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). Resale townhouses do not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate (GST/HST).** Separate: 36% of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A summary reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — it is not a flat 5% (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000). Exemptions: first-time buyers — full exemption on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000, phasing out to $860,000; newly built homes — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP $60,000 from RRSPs (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 lifetime at $8,000 per year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount by loan-to-value (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). **Page-defect note, stated explicitly:** this slug's research file documents NO CMHC premium error on a Chilliwack Downtown page, so the known 2.80%–4.00% page error is NOT asserted for this slug.
- **Presales.** None counted (§2.4); the rule: a presale buyer has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and deposits must be held in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

**Q: Can I rent out a Cedar Court townhouse?**
A: For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since November 24, 2022, and the listing checked in any case states rentals allowed (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate municipal/strata question — read the current bylaws on the Form B.

**Q: What does the Form B tell me before I buy at Cedar Court?**
A: The monthly strata fee, anything the seller owes the strata, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, the rules, the budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the depreciation report attached — within seven days of request, capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). It is also where Cedar Court's unit count and depreciation-report status — both UNCONFIRMED in public sources — get settled.

**Q: Which plan governs a downtown infill or townhouse purchase?**
A: The Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500 — Schedule B3 (Downtown Neighbourhood Plan) with the Schedule D-6 Downtown development permit area in the core (https://chilliwack.com/ocp). Confirm a parcel's designation and DPA position with the City before valuing redevelopment potential; a listing's own designation text is a claim, not a City record.

**Q: How much property transfer tax on an $800,000 resale townhouse?**
A: $14,000 with no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 plus 2% on the remaining $600,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). A qualifying first-time buyer gets a full exemption on the first $500,000 at values up to $835,000, phasing out at $860,000.

**Q: Does the first-time buyer GST rebate help here?**
A: Only on NEW housing — 100% of GST to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1,500,000, Bill C-4 Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). Cedar Court is resale stock, which does not attract GST.

**Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due?**
A: The first working day after July 1; for 2026, 5% on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026 — a summer completion straddles both dates (https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).
