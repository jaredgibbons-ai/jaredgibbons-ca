## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence — the position here is inverted, stated plainly:** there is no completed strata townhouse complex in Cape Horn, so there is no resale Form B to pull for townhouse stock on the slope. When a strata complex does apply (Strata Property Act s. 59), the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Fraser Mills, presale diligence is the developer's disclosure statement, not a Form B — do not let a sales centre present the two as equivalents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — this will apply to Debut, Encore and Chapter once they register and age into the cycle. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund — a first-budget check on every Fraser Mills disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any future directory line showing a rental cap or ban at a Fraser Mills building will reflect old bylaw text, not current law — flag it in-entry if it appears. This rule does not convert Crescentview into stock: a purpose-built rental building is rental by tenure, not by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Cape Horn building is published as 55+ in the sources checked — Chapter is marketed partly to downsizers and seniors, which is marketing, not an age bylaw; confirm any age bylaw on the disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here either — the completed count is zero on both tenures, stated plainly. For reference, a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. The slope's detached houses, secondary suites and carriage/laneway homes are fee-simple house forms under the SSMUH rules below, not fee-simple townhouse complexes, and are not counted.
- **City plans and zoning:** City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996, and the Interim Official Community Plan adopted April 27, 2026 (Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026), which supersedes the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001; a future OCP update project is anticipated to begin in 2027. On the slope, small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 carry subdivision minimums of 650 m² with 18.5 m width (R-1/R-3) and 465 m² with 12.5 m width (R-2), and the City's SSMUH rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules, so property-level septic or well status is UNVERIFIED per lot. A Cape Horn-specific neighbourhood plan: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md) and https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **Flood and watercourse — the two diligence items by location:** at Fraser Mills, the Debut site is preloaded to stand 20 feet above median sea level because it is on the Fraser River floodplain — flood construction level, strata insurance and deductible position are first-order questions for every Fraser Mills purchase. On the slope, the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse, and Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses while s. 523 covers riparian areas; slope/geotechnical status for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://storeys.com/beedie-fraser-mills-debut/ and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cape-horn.md)
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities:** property tax payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). Utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste; the City's 2026 budget materials record an average-household total property-charges increase of $137 combined, and metered properties under the Voluntary Residential Water Metering Program are billed every four months instead of the annual flat rate. - **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the governing frame for Debut, Encore and Chapter contracts.
