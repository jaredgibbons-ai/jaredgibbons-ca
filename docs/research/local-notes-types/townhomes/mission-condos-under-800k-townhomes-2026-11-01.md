### Worked examples — inputs stated, illustrative arithmetic only (not market facts, not band counts)

- **EXAMPLE 1 — resale unit at the community attached median (inputs stated).** Inputs: purchase price $430,000 (the September 2026 Mission Condos & Townhomes COMBINED median, used as an arithmetic input only — it is not a condo-only or townhome-only price, and it rests on 10 sales); resale (no GST); buyer does NOT qualify for any PTT exemption. Gross PTT: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $230,000 = $4,600, total **$6,600** — taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers, as every price under $800,000 is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **EXAMPLE 2 — same price, qualifying first-time buyer (inputs stated).** Inputs: price $430,000; resale; buyer meets all first-time program conditions (fair market value $835,000 or less — anywhere in this band sits under that ceiling). The whole $430,000 sits inside the exempt first $500,000, so the exemption covers the full $6,600: net PTT **$0**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **EXAMPLE 3 — top of the band (inputs stated).** Inputs: price $799,000; resale; no exemption. Gross PTT: $2,000 + (2% × $599,000 = $11,980) = **$13,980**. With a qualifying first-time buyer instead: $13,980 − $8,000 (tax on the exempt first $500,000: $2,000 + 2% × $300,000) = **$5,980** net. The 3% tier and the further 2% over $3,000,000 do not arise anywhere in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **EXAMPLE 4 — new-build GST rebate tiers (inputs stated).** Inputs: NEW unit, price $600,000; GST 5% = $30,000; buyer qualifies for the FTHB GST rebate. Rebate: 100% of GST to $1M, so **$30,000** (under the $50,000 maximum) — the frame that matters for a new Briskham Hill purchase or a developer sale at Montgomery, subject to the specific unit's status. The older New Housing Rebate frame (36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is stated in Section 3 for comparison; a buyer claims under the program they qualify for, on advice, not both by assumption. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes — all four cross-referenced townhouses and all Mission apartment buildings are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document; for Archer Green, whose year, count and restrictions are UNCONFIRMED, it is the diligence itself. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. All four cross-referenced townhouses are 5+ lots on their published counts except Archer Green, whose lot count is UNCONFIRMED — its 5+ test is confirmed on its Form B/strata plan. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" lines for Cherry Lane and for Stave Lake Landing are void in-entry — flag them wherever they appear. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex and Briskham Manor is apartment-form 55+ stock — those are the published positions; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified for Mission in the assembled files. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only — stated so no buyer imports strata expectations onto a fee-simple purchase elsewhere in the city.
- **City plan — OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026:** the current Official Community Plan replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added. **A "June 1, 2026" adoption date for this OCP would be WRONG — but it is NOT documented in this file's research, so no Jerry item is recorded from this file on that point;** the correct date is stated here because the OCP is cited. The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **City zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted; secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **City property taxes — FLAT 10% penalty (city ground only):** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026** — a flat 10% on unpaid taxes after July 2 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235). Any "5% + 5%" staging for CITY taxes is the wrong regime — 5% after Jul 2 + 5% after Oct 31 is the separate RURAL regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11) for FVRD electoral areas, never inside the city. Taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Flood and dykes:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — confirm floodplain status and flood construction requirements for the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction (a new Briskham Hill purchase, a developer sale at Montgomery), not resales. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; no sibling file for this slug carrying March 12 was found on disk in this pass) — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Worked tiers are in Section 2, Example 4. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted; in any event neither top tier arises under $800,000). First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in this band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. Worked tiers are in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, with deposits in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Montgomery Condos developer contracts and any Briskham Hill unit still on a developer contract. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
