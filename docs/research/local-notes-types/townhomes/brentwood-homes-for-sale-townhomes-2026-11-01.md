## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the one counted complex and every tower strata documented in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The Springer Avenue complex's restrictions are UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024. The Springer Avenue strata's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so whether it sits over the threshold is UNCONFIRMED — a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER it; read the count off the Form B and strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No complex in §2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the sources read — any such line surfacing in older bylaw text is void for long-term rentals and flagged as such.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Brentwood Park townhouse complex is verified 55+; the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ strata ownership in the sub-area.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.4). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Burnaby zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — **NEVER the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742**. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and apartment districts R3–R8 (City of Burnaby, consolidated bylaw: https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf).
- **Plans.** The **Burnaby 2050 OCP** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps; Brentwood is one of the town centres it plans for continued high-density growth. The 1996 Brentwood Town Centre Development Plan and 2013 Brentwood Site Master Plan remain the local layer, with amendments in consultation since January 29, 2024 — outcome UNVERIFIED (research file §4).
- **SSMUH (R1).** 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m². SSMUH speaks to the detached streets around the towers, not to the strata complexes themselves. This slug's research file opened no Burnaby suite/SSMUH page — the R1 figures above are carried from the count home's City sources, and any further Burnaby suite rule is UNVERIFIED in this file (research file §4).
- **Burnaby property taxes.** A 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date, and the City states it has no authority to waive or modify them; Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026 (City of Burnaby: https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties).
