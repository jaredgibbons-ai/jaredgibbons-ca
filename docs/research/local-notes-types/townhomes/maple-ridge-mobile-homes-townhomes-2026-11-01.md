## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

*The topic's rules frame goes here: the MHPTA frame first — it is the law a buyer of this product actually lives under — then the strata contrast and the standard buyer frame.*

- **MHPTA governs the pad, not the Strata Property Act:** manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. There is NO Form B, NO strata fees, NO CRF and NO depreciation report in a park purchase — the diligence documents are the tenancy agreement, the written park rules and the Manufactured Home Registry record. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Pad-rent increases — the verified provincial limits:** landlords can increase rent only with 3 full months' notice, only every 12 months, and never above the yearly limit PLUS a proportional amount (the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park); a landlord may also apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. **The limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus the proportional amount for increases in 2026, and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for increases in 2027** (verified live from the Province in this pass). No pad-rent DOLLAR figure is stated anywhere in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale in a park (MHPTA s. 28):** assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer when the home is sold and remains in the park; a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it. Before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement. On assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase does not change. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Registration is what makes the sale effective:** the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains the central register of ownership, controls the movement of manufactured homes in BC, and supports lender security. Buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership through a lawyer or notary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **CSA label is a sale gate:** manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes — modular built under CSA A-277, mobile under CSA Z-240. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if the label is lost, the home must be re-certified (Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home). The Province directs buyers to check the label at the point of sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Moving a home:** a permit to move is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a park home:** the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value does not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Park rule vs strata bylaw — the distinction buyers get wrong:** in a strata, an age restriction is a registered bylaw (only 55+ is lawful — SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023) disclosed on the Form B. In a park, a "55+" rule is a park rule under the MHPTA tenancy — no Form B discloses it and the SPA exemptions do not govern it; the written park rules govern. Any "55+ strata" language in a park-home listing is a category error. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Strata frame — for the townhouse a buyer might choose instead:** Form B diligence (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, max $35 plus 25¢/page, with rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached); depreciation reports every five years for 5+ lot stratas since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); 10% CRF minimum since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals; fee-simple townhouses (none counted in this file) carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **City zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, s. 626, Zone RMH (Manufactured Home Park Residential) is the dedicated park zone; the RMH zone's detailed rules are UNVERIFIED from the consolidated text (research pass). OCP citation: CONTESTED — see the flag at the top. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses that 3% + 2% structure (no product in this topic approaches it, but the frame is stated whole). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. How each applies to a manufactured home on a rented pad (as opposed to land) is transaction-specific: UNCONFIRMED per transaction — confirm with the lawyer/notary handling the Registry filing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a park home a townhouse?** No. You own a manufactured home and rent the pad under the MHPTA — no strata, no Form B, no title to the land. It is detached-form living at the lowest price point in the city, under tenancy law, not strata law. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the next scheduled increase date does not reset. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: How much can pad rent rise each year?** At most the yearly limit plus the proportional levy/utility amount, on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months: 2.3% + proportional in 2026 and 2.2% + proportional in 2027, with anything more requiring a Residential Tenancy Branch order. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park have to approve my purchase?** Yes in a park: assignment of the site tenancy needs the landlord's prior written consent under MHPTA s. 28 (or deemed consent, an arbitrator's order, or tenancy-agreement authorization) — build the consent timeline into subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** It cannot be sold until re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection and the approval label prepared can stand in for the CSA label to list the home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Mobile or modular — does it matter?** Both are manufactured homes in the Registry; the CSA standard differs (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) and the label on the home states which applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is the "55+" at Val Maria or Alouette a strata bylaw?** No — it is a park rule under the MHPTA. The written park rules, which the seller must hand you before assignment is requested, are the governing document — not a Form B. Cross-reference: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/maple-ridge-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Am I taxed on the land?** In a park, no: the Province states the assessed value does not include the land value where the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land owned by someone else. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Q: When is my purchase actually complete?** When it is registered — the Registry filing (Bill of Sale + Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership, through a lawyer or notary) is what makes the transfer effective, not the contract alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are the Maple Ridge numbers on this page manufactured-home prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category; the Section 1 figures are all-residential context, and manufactured-home-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
