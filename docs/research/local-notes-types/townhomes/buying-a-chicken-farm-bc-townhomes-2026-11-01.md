## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (contrast only — no strata lot exists in this topic; stated so a buyer can test any "farm condo" or "farm townhome" marketing against it).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. If a "farm condo" is ever marketed in this topic, the Form B and the strata plan number are the first proof to demand (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 in strata anywhere in BC. That rule touches neither a freehold chicken farm nor a freehold hobby acreage — there is no strata corporation (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The farm purchase itself is the fee-simple contrast: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees — title, ALR rules, local bylaws and the contract's quota position only, stated plainly.

**ALR frame — the governing rules for this topic.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified:** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 states strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR; a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (Strata Property Act s. 239), and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation (ALCA s. 21(1)). Stated correctly: ALR land cannot be stratified **without the ALC's permission**, and no source checked shows a chicken-farm parcel with such a permission for residential strata use (condos sibling §3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **Residence caps:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²** (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted **only within the principal residence**, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to **90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**; local government or First Nation government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. The live guide's statement that ALR rules generally allow barns, a principal residence and, with restrictions, additional dwellings is consistent with this frame at that general level (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://jaredgibbons.ca/buying-a-chicken-farm-bc.html).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — in FULL three-tier form:** farm classification is a voluntary, property-by-property program; the thresholds are **$2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**; applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification). **Flat-line flag, scoped precisely:** a flat "$2,500+" line for farm classification would be an ERROR against this three-tier form — but no such flat line is documented in this file's own research for the buying-a-chicken-farm-bc page, so that error is NOT asserted for this slug.

**Q: What residence can a BC chicken-farm parcel in the ALR have?**

**Q: What income must a farm operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: The full three-tier form: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase (§3; research file §7).
