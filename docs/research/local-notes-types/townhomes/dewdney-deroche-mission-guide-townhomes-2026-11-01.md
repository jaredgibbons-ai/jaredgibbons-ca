## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (the BC standard frame — stated because buyers ask, though no strata townhome is counted in §2; it governs no park in this file).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. **No Dewdney-Deroche purchase in §2 carries a Form B** — there is no strata here; a manufactured-home park purchase runs on park documents and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act instead (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Neither rule applies to a pad-rental park (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; only a short-term-rental restriction can survive in a strata. **Neither rule governs a manufactured-home park:** a park's rental position is a park-tenancy matter under the park's own documents and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata bylaws — do not import a directory "rentals not allowed" line into a park purchase (count home §3; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in a strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). A park's age rule is likewise a park-tenancy matter, not a strata bylaw — Oasis's "adult side" cannot be read through strata law, and no age number for that side is verified (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, local-government rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Dewdney-Deroche (§2.2); the corridor's fee-simple houses and acreages sit on the same no-strata diligence track.
