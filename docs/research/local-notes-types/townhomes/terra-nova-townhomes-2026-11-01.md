## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern, including Quilchena Green's age-bylaw wording. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all four counted complexes are well over the threshold on their published forms (Quilchena Green 91 units, London Mews 66 units; Mayflower and Sonoma unit counts UNCONFIRMED but plainly multi-building strata communities). Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The building-directory "rentals not allowed" line for Quilchena Green is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason; a current listing for the same complex records rentals allowed with restrictions, which is equally listing/directory data until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). This provision frames — but does not resolve — the Quilchena Green label conflict flagged in Section 2: whatever its historical label (45+ in a 2016 sheet, 55+ in current listings), no age bylaw below 55 can be enforced today, and the registered bylaw's exact test (one occupant vs one owner on title) is UNCONFIRMED until read. No other counted complex carries a published age restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's detached stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 8500) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. There is no verified fee-simple townhouse complex in Terra Nova; the fee-simple product here is detached houses and ALR farm parcels, which follow different rules again (below). https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **City zoning / OCP (Richmond — standing program figures):** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 (the City of Richmond 2041 OCP), Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. The sub-area plan is OCP Schedule 2.2B Thompson (Terra Nova). Quilchena Green carries RTL 1 zoning on its MLS record. For farm land beside the pocket, Agriculture (AG1) in Zoning Bylaw 8500 Section 14.1 provides for farming and compatible uses consistent with the Agricultural Land Reserve, one principal dwelling unit per lot, and a secondary suite as a secondary use; subdivision in the ALR is not permitted unless approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, and where that approval is not required the minimum lot area is 2.0 ha; single detached housing is not to be built on a lot under 828.0 m² in that zone. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=53061
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** for non-ALR residential lots, the City states Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. AG1 farm lots follow the ALR framework instead: Agricultural Land Commission guidance allows one principal residence and, if the regulation conditions are met, one additional residence per parcel. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Flood:** Richmond is protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations (49 km of dikes, 39 pump stations city-wide), designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise under way; Flood Bylaw 8204 sets setbacks from a dike right-of-way and bars building below the flood plain construction level in its Schedule B. The pocket's west and north edges are the dike itself — get the parcel-level position early on any west- or north-edge purchase. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — all four counted complexes are 1990s resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no verified presale exists in Terra Nova, so there is nothing for the REDMA frame to attach to in this file (the No. 1 Road corner item in Section 2 is a QUEUE proposal note, not a presale); for completeness, a presale buyer elsewhere must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Quilchena Green is Terra Nova stock, whatever the name suggests.** The gated 91-unit complex on Cornwall Drive carries the name of a different Richmond pocket (Quilchena, around Quilchena Elementary / Moresby Drive) — portals and buyers routinely file it in the wrong sub-area, and this program deliberately did not count it in the Quilchena RI file for exactly that reason. Its home count is here. Basis: sibling condos and Quilchena RI townhomes files on disk.
- **Its age label disagrees with itself across time.** A 2016 MLS sheet records 45+; current listings record 55+. Both labels survive in the market record, the conflict is flagged in every sibling file that touches the complex, and only the registered bylaw settles it — a diligence point no portal headline will show a buyer. Basis: sibling 55-plus file, §2/§3.
- **Two of the four complexes share one builder and one amenity pattern.** Mayflower and Sonoma are both Polygon townhouse communities on Westminster Highway (1995–1997 era) with the same resort-style set — clubhouse, outdoor pool, hot tub, gym — so a buyer cross-shopping them is really comparing floorplans, yards and strata health, not amenity lists. Basis: Section 2 above.
- **The pocket's plan name is not its postal name.** The City plans this ground as Thompson (Terra Nova) — OCP Schedule 2.2B — and the Heritage Inventory lists its planning area as Thompson, while the board and the market call it Terra Nova. City documents searched under "Terra Nova" alone will miss the plan file. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/sale.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **The area's Indigenous name is on the school.** Terra Nova was named Spul'u'kwuks — Indigenous Peoples used the area for seasonal fishing camps during salmon and sturgeon runs, Newfoundland fishermen later renamed it Terra Nova ("new land"), and the Terra Nova Cannery operated there until the middle of the 20th century; Spul'u'kwuks Elementary carries the original name inside the pocket. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Terra-Nova_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure.pdf
- **The rural park is a no-dogs park.** Terra Nova Rural Park, 2631 Westminster Highway, is a 63-acre site with trails, the Adventure Play Environment and the Sharing Farm on the Pacific Flyway beside the Sturgeon Bank Wildlife Management Area — and it is a NO DOGS ALLOWED area under Animal Control Bylaw 8612, which matters to dog-owning buyers who assume dyke-side living means dyke-side dog walks. (The West Dyke Trail itself runs from the park south to Garry Point Park.) https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks/parksearch/park.aspx?id=80
- **Shopping in the pocket is asserted everywhere and verified nowhere official.** Terra Nova Village at 3671–3679 Westminster Highway and any grocery anchor: UNVERIFIED on any official source read — listings market "steps to shopping" (a Mayflower listing names a grocery anchor in its copy), but no official source in the research file confirms the centre or its tenants. - **Care anchors are in the wider Thompson / City Centre area, not the pocket.** Thompson Community Centre is at 5151 Granville Avenue; the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway (Vancouver Coastal Health), and Richmond City Centre Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #110, 4671 No. 3 Road; the nearest full library branch named on an official source is Richmond Public Library Brighouse (Main) Branch, 100-7700 Minoru Gate — there is no library branch in the pocket. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/centres/thompson.htm and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/richmond-city-centre-urgent-and-primary-care-centre/plan-your-visit and https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations
