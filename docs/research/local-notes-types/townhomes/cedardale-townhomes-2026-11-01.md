## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no local strata townhouse stock to apply Form B diligence to — but the regime governs any strata purchase a Cedardale buyer makes nearby, and any future strata here:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Cedardale has no counted strata stock and therefore no directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry; the rule is stated because Cedardale buyers cross-shopping Ambleside and Park Royal directories will meet such lines there, and every one of them is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). West Vancouver's verified 55+ strata buildings are all in Ambleside (apartment-form, in the 55-plus program) — Cedardale has no 55+ complex of any form, per the sibling file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (all of Cedardale's verified stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Whether any Cedardale lot carries a British Pacific Properties covenant is a title-by-title question, UNVERIFIED in the research file.
- **District zoning and plan — West Vancouver's own pair only:** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. (White Rock's Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair belongs to White Rock ONLY and is never attributed to West Vancouver; the District's chartwell / chelsea-park / bayridge / cedardale names are all District of West Vancouver sub-areas, and this file's municipality is the District throughout.) Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; RS3 and RS5 are the zones evidenced in and beside Cedardale in District records (Cedardale Park is listed RS3, Burley Drive Park RS5). The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop; the District's own implementing-bylaw status for provincial SSMUH beyond that statement is UNVERIFIED per property. A Cedardale-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The school inside the neighbourhood is not the neighbourhood's school.** École Cedardale at 595 Burley Drive is a French Immersion IB World School authorized in 2016, admitted by lottery — the amenity is real, but it is a program of choice, not a catchment, and marketing it as the catchment school is the area's standing trap. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/ecole-cedardale-french-immersionib-candidate/
- **This small pocket has its own two District parks.** Cedardale Park and Burley Drive Park are both listed in the District's Parks Master Plan (Cedardale Park as RS3, Dedicated) — for a neighbourhood this size, its own parks plus the Capilano Pacific Trail along the river corridor at its east edge are the amenity story. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/PARKS_MASTER_PLAN_FINAL.pdf
- **Cedardale's own streets carry a bus to Park Royal.** Route 251 runs via Kings Avenue, Burley Drive and Mathers Avenue to Park Royal Exchange, where the R2 RapidBus now runs one-seat to Metrotown — a transit story stronger than most of West Vancouver, on streets that otherwise read as purely car-oriented. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt251.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
