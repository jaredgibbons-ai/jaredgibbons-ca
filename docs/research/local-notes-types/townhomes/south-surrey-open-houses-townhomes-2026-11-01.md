## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Open-house law first (this file's topic), then the strata block.**

- **BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), as carried in this file's own research** (reused there from batch-13/open-houses.md, whose source is the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report): a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs (research file §4; condos sibling §3; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download).
- **Representation:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page's own "Before you walk in" copy is consistent with that frame — it warns the listing agent works for the seller and advises asking for the Form B, depreciation report and minutes at a strata open house (research file §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers).
- **Identification / sign-in for open-house visitors, and how open-house times are set and entered board-side: UNVERIFIED** — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either (research file §4).
- **Confirm the time before you drive:** this page's cards could not be checked at all in the research pass (no cards returned), and the 1-hour offset is UNVERIFIED for this page (§1 FILE STATUS) — read both time places on the rendered card and confirm with the listing brokerage (research file §6).

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhome cross-referenced in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a cross-referenced complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged on that basis in its home file. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in the cross-referenced South Surrey stock in §2.

**Q: Who does the agent hosting an open house represent?**
A: The seller, unless you have your own client relationship with a licensee. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation only while the host is hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers — once you discuss motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee, you are sharing with the other side's representative (§3).

A: The page's own answer (site copy): Grandview Heights and White Rock first, then the King George Corridor and Morgan Creek, with Grandview "mostly newer townhomes and houses." No official source publishes open-house scheduling statistics — treat that as the page's general statement and confirm each home's own time with the listing brokerage (research file §7).

**Q: What should I ask for at a strata townhome open house?**

A: No — rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Surrey STRs are permitted owner-only under the licence frame in §3 (§3).
