## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. In Hampton Cove, the Form B and budget are also where Hampton Club amenity charges show up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection and three CRF cash-flow models. All six carried complexes are over the five-lot threshold on their verified scale; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the six is such a strata. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, so any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Neilsen Grove complex is void in-entry for long-term rentals (Fairwinds' strata records already record rentals allowed). Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. None of the six townhouse complexes is verified as 55+ — the sub-area's verified 55+ complex is South Pointe, the bare-land strata not counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Neilsen Grove (§2).
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan — no lettered schedules, no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered schedule (e.g. "Schedule D.1") to Bylaw 8400 is wrong — that conflation is an error wherever met. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, not asserted.** Sources conflict (a "Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" citation against a competing "8400, 2025" citation); this file asserts neither. The Hampton Cove lands were built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning (a Westhampton listing records zoning CD430), so each complex's rules are read off its own CD zone and the City zoning maps. https://www.zolo.ca/ladner-real-estate/5311-admiral-way/9
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — relevant to Neilsen Grove's detached lots, not to the Hampton Cove strata lands. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale townhouses here carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% on the residential portion — the tier math governs, not the compressed row. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $900,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Down-payment programs.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **Mortgage default insurance.** CMHC's published homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across the loan-to-value bands. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
