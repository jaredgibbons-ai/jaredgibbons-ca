## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Meadowland Village, the counted strata complex in the count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Listing-level pet/age lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern, including whether Meadowland Village's registered age bylaw is in the lawful 55+ form (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote — Meadowland Village (20 or 26 lots on either published figure) is over the threshold on both. A sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer** — the provincial voiding does not create a short-term rental right here: short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in the City's Zoning Bylaw (research file §4; https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699). Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this file would be flagged VOID in-entry; none is published for Meadowland Village in the sources checked (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Meadowland Village's published 55+ position is listing-level — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. The Meadowlands co-op's 55+ rule is a co-op membership rule, not a strata bylaw, and is not governed by ss. 123.1/123.2 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. None is verified in Central Meadows (§2.2).

- **One street, two 55+ tenures (Advent Road):** Meadowland Village (freehold strata, buyable) and Meadowlands Housing Co-operative (membership, not buyable as a strata lot) sit on the same street, and listings board-classify the pocket as Central Meadows even though it reads as a quiet residential street rather than Town Centre. A search that treats every Advent Road "unit" as the same product will misprice both (§2.6; https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/).
- **The floodplain number changed between City documents.** The 2020 Business Plan states over 95%; a superseded OCP-era release stated approximately 86%; this guide page quotes "over 85%" with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution. This file carries 95% and flags the conflict rather than smoothing it over (§§1, 3).
- **The guide page contradicts itself twice in plain sight.** Its footer carries the wrong email while its contact sections carry the correct one, and its Planning section's station sentence ("in my Central Meadows guide area, not in Central Meadows") contradicts itself in a single line — both are Jerry items documented in this slug's own research (§1).
- **Open-house times on this feed run an hour apart inside the same card.** Two cards show a header time and an embedded time exactly one hour apart — confirm any open-house time from a second source before driving to it (§1).
- **Solaris carries two civic addresses:** the directory address is 12069 Harris Road, but part of the complex is addressed 12079 Harris Road — confirm which address a specific unit carries when ordering strata documents or checking a tax record (§2.4; https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/solaris-at-meadows-gate-12069-harris-road).
- **The drainage levy is its own tax line (2026):** Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 sets the residential drainage rate at $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, separate from the $2.8438 general municipal rate — the cost of the dike/drainage system, billed to strata and house owners alike (§3).
- **78% of the city is farmland (2020 Business Plan):** the Agricultural Land Reserve covers 78% of Pitt Meadows' land base, which is why the buyable townhouse stock is compressed into pockets like Advent Road instead of spreading across new subdivisions (§3; research file §4).
