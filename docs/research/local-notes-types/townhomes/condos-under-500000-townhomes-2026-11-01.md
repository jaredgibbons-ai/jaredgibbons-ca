## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata stock, including Huntsfield Green):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At this price point the Form B, Form F, bylaws, rules, fees and special levies are where a home is actually priced — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a building named in §2 is flagged VOID in-entry on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Directly relevant in this band: Huntsfield Green is a published 55+ community and the visible feed itself contains a 55+ apartment building — a buyer under 55 must read the registered bylaw, not the listing headline. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (for Surrey stock, Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — the former Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED, never cite 18020 as current) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in this file; a "no strata fees" claim in a band listing is a tenure claim to verify on title, not a band fact.
- **Surrey rules for the South Surrey stock referenced in §2:** SSMUH in force July 8, 2024 (3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop); one secondary suite per house, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area**; STRs permitted owner-only with a City licence the STR page states at **$358 per year** — carried with a **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. Surrey property taxes are due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2, further 5% after September 2. Langley City and Township rules differ by municipality — confirm the parcel's municipality before applying any Surrey figure to a Langley unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Why $500,000 is a financing line, not just a budget:** CMHC states homes $500,000 or less require a minimum 5% down payment, while homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on any amount over $500,000; homes at $1,500,000 or more require a minimum 20% down payment and mortgage loan insurance is not available. Mortgage loan insurance is required with less than 20% down and protects the lender, not the buyer; CMHC premiums run **0.6%–4.5%** of the total mortgage amount, payable in full at purchase or added to the mortgage. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase under $500,000 meets only the first two tiers, and never the 3% tier or the further 2%; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, so a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere on this page is inside the full-exemption value range on price alone; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. **Worked examples, inputs stated:** (1) Down payment — a $500,000 purchase at the band ceiling: minimum down payment is a flat 5% = **$25,000**, because the price is at or under the $500,000 tier boundary. (2) PTT — a $500,000 townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**; a qualifying first-time buyer exempt on the first $500,000 pays $0 on those inputs. (3) PTT — a $450,000 townhouse with no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $250,000 ($5,000) = **$7,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the frame for any developer sale surfaced by this feed, which is never counted as resale band stock here. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
