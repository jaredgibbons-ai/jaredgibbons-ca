## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mount Pleasant VE is the eastern half of the City of Vancouver's Mount Pleasant neighbourhood, **east of Main Street**: roughly from Main Street to the Clark Drive/Nanaimo side, between East 2nd Avenue/Great Northern Way and East 16th Avenue/Kingsway. Its MLS® sub-area name (GVR board) is "Mount Pleasant VE" (VE = Vancouver East); the western portion is the "Mount Pleasant VW" sub-area. - Where the townhouse stock concentrates: thinly, and mostly in disguised forms. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For 9 On The Park, the Form B is the confirming document behind the listing record's published pet/rental summary. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (relevant in this neighbourhood, where small strata conversions of character houses can run under five lots — check the lot count before assuming a report exists). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. 9 On The Park, at 9 published units, sits above the 5-lot depreciation threshold. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. 9 On The Park's listing record states rentals allowed, consistent with current law; any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Mount Pleasant VE strata is VOID in-entry and must be flagged as such wherever it is met — one such stale line exists in a building-directory record for a Mount Pleasant apartment building (1099 East Broadway, "rentals not allowed" per that directory) and it is flagged here as the pattern to correct on sight, in the condos program's stock, not this file's. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No source states an age restriction for 9 On The Park — general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse stock is verified in Mount Pleasant VE (§2): a fee-simple purchase on these side streets is a detached character house — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; City rules (zoning, heritage register status) plus any registered covenants govern instead. Do not import strata expectations into a character-house purchase, or house expectations into the one strata townhouse complex.
- **City plan and zoning:** citywide land-use direction comes from the **Vancouver Official Development Plan**, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; the **Broadway Plan** (approved June 22, 2022; updated December 12, 2024) is the 30-year framework for this corridor, supporting 20–30 storeys near Mount Pleasant Station, with most corridor sites still needing rezoning. For detached-house lots, Vancouver's multiplex rules apply citywide: **By-law No. 13817** replaced the RS districts with the **R1-1 district**, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots — the value story on a character-house lot, and the mechanism by which future small strata townhouse product may appear in this sub-area (any such project is verified fresh, by name and tenure, before it is counted). The Mount Pleasant Industrial Area (Great Northern Way/Flats) is planned for intensified employment use rather than wholesale conversion to housing. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://syc.vancouver.ca/projects/broadway-plan/broadway-plan-summary-highlight-document.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax charges **3% of assessed value** on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026 — a live holding cost for investors holding any of the area's older rental or vacant stock, and a tax that exists **only inside the City of Vancouver**: it does not apply in Burnaby, Richmond, the North Vancouvers or anywhere else in this program. Run the 3% math into any vacant-unit holding-cost analysis here before quoting net returns. Separately, the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is its own regime and is not the same tax. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **City property taxes (2026):** 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances (City of Vancouver deadlines page). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (including a Main & Sixth presale purchase), not resales. No worked PTT example is given for this slug: there is no verified Mount Pleasant VE price figure to use as an input (no SnapStats for Vancouver), and no figure is invented. For an investor holding a vacant unit in this sub-area it is a first-order holding cost, separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax. (§3 above)
