## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section exists on a zero-stock island:** there is no counted strata complex for the strata rules below to attach to today. They are stated because (a) a buyer who finds an apparent "strata" unit near the airport needs the test for whether it is residential townhouse stock at all, and (b) the Burkeville house purchase that is this island's actual product is governed by the fee-simple, City/federal and tax rules that follow. If a strata property is ever involved, review the strata documents under the provincial framework; BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Form B diligence (any future strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum (any future strata purchase):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID / age bylaws:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw), and only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No directory "rentals not allowed" line exists to flag in this file because no complex is verified — the rule is stated so a future listing line is never taken at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (Burkeville — the island's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Most of the island is federal airport land governed by the YVR land use plan and federal airport zoning regulations rather than the City bylaw; the exact Richmond zoning bylaw zone and minimum lot size applying to Burkeville lots: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **City zoning / OCP (Richmond — standing program figures):** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000, Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204. Sea Island is planning area 12 in the OCP framework for the Burkeville pocket, while senior governments are not regulated by the City OCP — airport-side changes follow the federal / YVR process under the YVR 2037 Master Plan, approved by the federal Minister of Transport. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the City states Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots — but on Sea Island the federal airport overlay (below) and the UNVERIFIED Burkeville zone position mean suite potential is a per-lot City confirmation, never an assumption. https://richmond.ca/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Aircraft noise and height — the island-specific diligence:** federal Airport Zoning Regulations administered through Transport Canada set zoning around the airport so aircraft can operate safely, including maximum building heights; aircraft noise is managed through the YVR 2025–2029 Noise Management Plan, YVR publishes annual noise reports with monitoring data, and the City's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy Area framework (City Bulletin DEVAPPS-05) applies to noise-sensitive development in the airport's vicinity. Check both before pricing an addition or rebuild on a Burkeville lot. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/ and https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/noise-management/reports and https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/devapps0534378.pdf
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of low-lying islands in the Fraser River floodplain, protected by a perimeter dike system with drainage pump stations — 49 km of dikes and 39 pump stations, designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise under way; Flood Bylaw 8204 sets flood construction levels for new construction. Confirm the lot's position with the City before planning a rebuild. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Richmond property tax timing:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026; this city-wide rule applies to Burkeville homes. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — academic on this island today, where no new residential construction product is verified. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no verified residential presale exists on Sea Island, so there is nothing for the REDMA frame to attach to in this file; for completeness, a presale buyer elsewhere must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Burkeville is a wartime housing pocket that outlived the war.** Sea Island was once mostly farm land with small communities including Eburne and Burkeville; the Vancouver Airport opened on the island, and Burkeville — houses built for aviation workers in three designs — is the only remaining population centre. (Research file, City of Richmond Archives.) http://richmondarchives.ca/tag/planning/
- **The island's school is a heritage stop, not a school.** Sea Island Elementary School on Wellington Crescent has closed; Burkeville children are catchment-assigned off-island (the sample-address locator result is Brighouse Elementary / Richmond Secondary). https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/locations/sea-island-school/
- **The airport does not answer to City Hall.** YVR occupies federal property within the City of Richmond, its land use follows the YVR 2037 Master Plan approved by the federal Minister of Transport, and the City OCP does not regulate senior governments — so the biggest neighbour's changes never appear on a City development tracker. https://www.yvr.ca/en/about-yvr/community/engagement and https://news.yvr.ca/yvrs-new-master-plan-has-arrived/
- **Federal zoning reaches into Burkeville backyards.** Transport Canada's Airport Zoning Regulations cap building heights around the airport to protect aircraft operations — an addition, rebuild or tall tree on a Burkeville lot can be a federal question, not just a City permit question. https://news.yvr.ca/statement-regarding-airport-zoning-regulations/
- **Three Canada Line stations, no condo at any of them.** Templeton, Sea Island Centre and YVR-Airport give the island rapid transit most Richmond pockets do not have — and there is still no verified attached housing at any station, because the land around them is airport land. https://www.translink.ca/rider-guide/visiting-vancouver
- **The everyday anchors are small and specific.** Burkeville Park and the Sea Island Community Centre are at 7140 Miller Road; Larry Berg Flight Path Park is on Russ Baker Way; McDonald Beach Park is at the end of McDonald Road; Iona Beach Regional Park is at 900 Ferguson Road at the island's north-west end; and the Sea Island Conservation Area is a designated protected habitat named by YVR. There is no public library branch on the island, and a grocery store on Sea Island is UNVERIFIED on any official source. https://www.richmondmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Sea-Island_Self-Guided-Historical-Tour-Brochure-1.pdf
- **The nearest hospital is off-island.** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway, Richmond (Vancouver Coastal Health). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
