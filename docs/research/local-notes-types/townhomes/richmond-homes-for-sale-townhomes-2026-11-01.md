## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every complex in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Published rental/pet/age restrictions for the §2.1 complexes are as their home files state them; anything not published there is UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Most §2.1 complexes are well over it on their published forms; read the lot count off the Form B where a home file leaves it UNCONFIRMED. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line surfacing for a §2.1 complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such in-entry in its home file. Registered covenants (e.g., Omega's LEMR units, §2.5) are not bylaws and are not voided.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ — directly relevant to Quilchena Green's conflicting 45+/55+ labels (§2.1): the registered wording on the Form B governs, and the conflict stays flagged, never resolved from listings.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified in Richmond (§2.3). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Richmond zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and taxes.**

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Detached districts sit in RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1; minimum lot areas by subzone incl. RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m², RS1/K 315.0 m²); the Alexandra townhouses sit in site-specific Town Housing zones (research file §4: https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf; west-cambie townhomes file).
- **OCP: Bylaw 9000 (2041 OCP)** — district plans are schedules inside it (Steveston Area Plan = Schedule 2.4) (research file §4).
- **Suites / SSMUH.** A secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2 (Bylaw 8500 s. 5.4: at least 33.0 m², at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²); the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots (research file §4: https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm).
- **Flood: Bylaw 8204** sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels city-wide (§1).
- **Richmond property taxes.** 2026 taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026 (research file §4: https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). **Q: Does the Alexandra energy utility add to West Cambie carrying costs?**

A: In any strata here, a long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted, and registered covenant rental units (Omega LEMR) are a separate, surviving restriction (§3).

**Q: Will flood rules affect a Richmond townhouse purchase?**
A: The whole city sits in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system; Bylaw 8204 flood construction levels bind new construction and renovation permits, and the strata's insurance position is a Form B item (§1/§3).
