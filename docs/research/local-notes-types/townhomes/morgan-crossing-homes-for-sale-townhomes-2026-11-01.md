## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to any Morgan Crossing strata townhome if Brownstones' status is ever verified, and to the village's strata condos).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For 2008–2011 buildings, the depreciation report is the central value document — the live page itself advises reading it and the last two years of minutes closely for envelope, roof and elevator work (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate; research file §4).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — rentals are allowed for long-term tenancies anywhere in BC; any directory "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals wherever it surfaces for a complex in this file. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Card text on this page includes "Pets & rentals allowed" for one unit — listing text for that unit only, not a village-wide rule (research file §6; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). No Morgan Crossing complex carries a verified age bylaw — the 55-plus sibling verifies ZERO 55+ stock at Morgan Crossing (55-plus sibling §2) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.

Pet rules remain enforceable bylaws and vary by building — one card's "Pets & rentals allowed" line is that unit's listing text only. Read the registered bylaws on the Form B (§3; research file §6).
