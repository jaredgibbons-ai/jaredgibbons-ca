## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Savile Row's published restrictions are UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No building in Section 2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the sources read; if one surfaces in older bylaw text for Savile Row, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Savile Row is not published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. The detached houses around the lake are fee-simple houses, not fee-simple townhouse complexes, and are not counted.
- **City plans and zoning:** the City of Burnaby's current zoning bylaw is the **Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 is repealed and is never the frame for this file. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. On the residential streets, the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing rules apply: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified — the realistic route by which small strata product could appear on these detached streets. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/burnaby-lake.md) and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Lake-edge checks:** the lake shore is low, marshy protected wildlife habitat — Metro Vancouver's park materials describe the shoreline habitat the trails and boardwalks are built around — so lake-edge and creek-edge lots need parcel-level flood, habitat and watercourse-setback checks; a floodplain designation for the sub-area's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/burnaby-lake.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home). The 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use and a garbage toter fee; the detached single-family water charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/burnaby-lake.md)

Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply in Burnaby — it is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
