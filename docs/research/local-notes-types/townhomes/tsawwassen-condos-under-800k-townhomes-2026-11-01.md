## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This band's stock is apartment condos, so the strata block below is the condo buyer's block; it is stated here because a townhouse buyer comparing across the Tsawwassen files meets the same provincial rules, and the fee-simple contrast is stated plainly where it matters.

- **Worked PTT example 1 — general tax, resale condo at $600,000 (inputs stated).** Input: fair market value $600,000, general PTT applies (no exemption). Tier math: 1% on the first $200,000 = $2,000; 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $600,000 = 2% × $400,000 = $8,000; the 3% tier (above $2,000,000) and the further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 do not arise. **Total general PTT = $2,000 + $8,000 = $10,000.** A purchase under $800,000 is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers where the general tax applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked PTT example 2 — at the band ceiling, $800,000 (inputs stated).** Input: fair market value $800,000, general PTT applies (no exemption). Tier math: 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($800,000 − $200,000 = $600,000) = $12,000. **Total general PTT = $14,000.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Worked PTT example 3 — qualifying first-time buyer at $800,000 (inputs stated).** Inputs: fair market value $800,000 (at or under the $835,000 full-exemption ceiling, effective April 1, 2024), property 0.5 hectares or smaller with only residential improvements, used only as the buyer's principal residence, and all buyer-side conditions met. The exemption applies to the first $500,000 of the price: exempt portion tax = 1% × $200,000 + 2% × $300,000 = $2,000 + $6,000 = $8,000 (the maximum saving). Taxable remainder: $800,000 − $500,000 = $300,000, taxed as 2% × $300,000 = $6,000 in the tier structure as the program applies it — **total PTT payable = $14,000 − $8,000 = $6,000**. A partial exemption is available above $835,000 and below $860,000. The exemption is claimed through the buyer's legal professional on the PTT return, and file-by-file eligibility governs over any worked example. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **"Over $3M — 5%" compression flag:** the general structure is 3% above $2,000,000 plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses those two into one figure. No such row is documented in this file's research, and the $3M tiers never arise inside this band in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT on a TFN prepaid leasehold purchase: UNVERIFIED.** The provincial page opened for the research lists prepaid leases among transaction types but does not state the treatment for this band's TFN buildings, and no TFN source was opened. How property tax and related charges work for a leasehold unit on Tsawwassen First Nation lands is likewise UNVERIFIED. Confirm both with a legal professional before relying on Examples 1–3 for a Tsawwassen North unit. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Form B diligence (strata condos — and strata townhomes alike).** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Age restrictions, pet rules and parking assignments live in the registered bylaws and the Form B, not in listing text — no Tsawwassen building's bylaws were read for this research, so the live page's 55+ and pet/rental claims are UNVERIFIED building by building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a building in this band is void in-entry for long-term rentals. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal, which can resolve disputes including non-payment of strata fees and enforcement of bylaws (noise, pets, parking, rentals). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
