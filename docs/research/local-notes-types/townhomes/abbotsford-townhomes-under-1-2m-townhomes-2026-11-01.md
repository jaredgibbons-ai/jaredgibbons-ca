## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata in §2.3 — a band buyer who narrows to a townhouse runs this diligence in the complex's count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry wherever a directory "rentals not allowed" line is met for a cross-referenced complex; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The live page's generalization that most newer family complexes permit long-term rentals is consistent with the law, but its "most complexes allow two pets" generalization is UNVERIFIED as a blanket claim — pet rules are set complex by complex in each strata's bylaws (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-townhomes-under-1-2m.html).
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Abbotsford's verified 55+ position is the §2.5 cross-reference, stated there, never re-counted.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Abbotsford zoning, OCP, SSMUH/suites and taxes — with the two standing conflicts flagged.**

- **OCP — standing correction governs:** the current plan is **Abbotsforward 2050, adopted Dec 2025**. "2600-2016" and "2500-2016" citations are stale/conflicted, are flagged, and are never presented as current in this file.
- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw, 2014, No. 2400** governs (research file §4).
- **SSMUH — NUMBER CONFLICT FLAGGED:** the research file asserts the SSMUH amendment is "Bylaw No. 3560-2024," adopted June 25, 2024 (research file §4; https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/57884/). The bylaw **number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages** in this program and is **never asserted** in this file; the substance the City pages establish is: up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas (3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger lots), accessory units counting toward the total.
- **Suites (Abbotsford's rules ONLY):** a secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and **less than 50% of the principal unit's size**; a coach house or garden suite may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²**. No suite claim is made for any strata complex on this page — strata bylaws and the specific unit's permits govern (research file §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/buildingpermits/secondary-suites).
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026 (research file §4; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Abbotsford is not Vancouver.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs — see FILE STATUS): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction (e.g., Upterra, Kings Crossing if bought from the developer), not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out.
- **PTT:** tiers and worked band anchors are in §2.9 ($700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000), with the "Over $3M — 5%" compression flagged there. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on the first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the Abbotsford townhomes-under-$1.2M page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here.
