# Mission Farms — Townhomes: Local Notes (Topic File)

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices. This is a FARM TOPIC file in the townhomes series.

**REGIME SPLIT — read first:** "Mission farms" spans TWO tax and planning regimes that must never be mixed. Ground inside the City of Mission sits under the City regime (OCP Bylaw 6405-2026; FLAT 10% tax penalty after July 2). Ground in the surrounding FVRD electoral areas sits under the separate RURAL regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11: 5% after Jul 2 + 5% after Oct 31). The splits that decide which regime a parcel is in: Hatzic Bench = City of Mission; Hatzic Prairie = FVRD Area F; Hatzic Island = Area G; Durieu = Area F; Dewdney–Deroche = Area G; Stave Falls IS inside the City of Mission. Section 3 keeps the two regimes separate throughout.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

*The topic's rules frame goes here: the ALR frame first, the two tax regimes kept separate, then the standard strata/buyer frames for the townhouse a buyer might choose instead.*

- **ALR frame — residences cannot be stratified:** ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR housing caps, in full:** a principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; one additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and local bylaws (City of Mission inside the city; FVRD in the electoral areas) must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **ALR status check:** a specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference and official confirmation comes from the ALC Mapping and GIS team. Mission's total ALR area from an official text source: UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm classification — the full three-tier form:** BC Assessment's minimum gross farm income (farm gate) thresholds are $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha above 4 ha; and $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and is never used. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **CITY OF MISSION regime (city ground only — Hatzic Bench, Stave Falls, the urban core and rural city districts):** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026 (it replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added). **Any live guide stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption date for this OCP is WRONG** — this topic's research file does not itself document that wrong date on the mission-farms page, so it is flagged here as the standing Jerry item it is program-wide, not asserted as a defect of this page specifically. Zoning: Bylaw 5949-2020 as amended. Taxes: City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on any current balance outstanding July 3, 2026 — Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235. Taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **FVRD RURAL regime (electoral-area ground only — Hatzic Prairie / Area F, Durieu / Area F, Hatzic Island / Area G, Dewdney–Deroche / Area G):** the separate rural tax regime under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11 — 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31. That staged 5% + 5% structure applies ONLY outside the City; it is never the City of Mission rule, and the City's flat 10% is never the electoral-area rule. Planning on this ground runs under FVRD bylaws (for Hatzic Valley, the Area F OCP Bylaw 999), not the City's OCP or zoning bylaw. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- **City zoning detail — held UNVERIFIED where the source failed:** the rural-zone minimum parcel sizes under the current Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 could not be confirmed from a City-hosted current text in the research pass (the RU80 / RU36 / RU16 figures — 8.0 ha / 3.6 ha / 1.6 ha — were seen only in a non-municipal mirror of the predecessor Bylaw 5050-2009): UNVERIFIED — get the zone and minimum from the City before pricing subdivision or a second dwelling. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, with suites and detached accessory units handled by zone — city ground only. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Water:** non-domestic groundwater use (e.g. irrigation) requires a water licence and payment of fees and rentals under the Water Sustainability Act before lawful use; domestic use is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Septic:** a building with indoor plumbing not connected to community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, and only an Authorized Person (Professional Engineer or Registered Onsite Wastewater Practitioner) can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect it (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood and dykes:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — the detailed flood/dyke position for the Dewdney–Deroche and Hatzic lowlands from an official text source is UNVERIFIED in the research pass; confirm floodplain status and flood construction requirements parcel by parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- **Strata frame — stated for contrast:** IF a purchase were one of Mission's urban strata townhouses (counted in the assembly file, not here), diligence is the Form B (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, max $35 plus 25¢/page; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached); five-year depreciation reports for 5+ lot stratas since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); 10% CRF minimum since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (flag any directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). A freehold farm parcel carries NONE of that: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal/FVRD rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Foreclosure note:** Mission has NO Supreme Court registry — the program's foreclosure mapping for Mission matters is UNVERIFIED, and no registry claim is made in this file.
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses that 3% + 2% structure and applies only to the residential portion. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** none verified in this topic; the standing frame is disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and 7-day rescission (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a strata townhouse on a Mission farm?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and no townhouse complex is counted in this topic. Mission's townhouses are urban product, counted in the city assembly file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is my Hatzic farm in the City or the FVRD?** It depends which Hatzic: the Bench is City of Mission; the Prairie is FVRD Area F; the Island is Area G — and the answer changes your tax penalty regime (flat 10% city vs 5% + 5% rural) and your planning bylaw. Confirm by PID before writing. (Standing corrections, 2026-11-01; https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf)
- **Q: How big can the farmhouse be?** 500 m² or less total floor area without an ALC application, fill capped at 1,000 m², and any secondary suite only inside that principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I add a second dwelling?** One additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — 90 m² or less at 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less above 40 ha — and the local bylaw (City or FVRD, per the regime split) must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income keeps farm classification?** $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — application by October 31 for the next tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What is the rural-zone minimum lot size?** UNVERIFIED on a current City-hosted text — the RU80 / RU36 / RU16 figures circulating in mirrors are from the predecessor bylaw. Get the current zone from the City (or the FVRD, on electoral-area ground) before pricing a subdivision. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **Q: Do I need a water licence to irrigate?** Yes — non-domestic groundwater use requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act, with fees and rentals payable; domestic use is exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Are the Mission numbers on this page farm prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the Section 1 figures are Mission residential context (detached median $814,000; Condos & Townhomes COMBINED median $430,000, September 2026), and farm-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: The feed showed one listing — is that the whole Mission farm market?** It is a snapshot of the feed at reading (a single Agri-Business listing in Dewdney Deroche), not a market statistic — the rural districts traded 0–3 detached sales each in September 2026, so the market is thin and parcel-specific either way. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-farms.html
- **Q: Which schools serve the Deroche side?** Deroche Elementary and Dewdney Elementary are the rural schools confirmed on the Ministry file, with Hatzic Middle School and Mission Senior Secondary above them — catchments are address-based and UNVERIFIED for any farm address until run in the SD75 locator. (BC Ministry K-12 schools file, 2025/2026, via the research file.)
