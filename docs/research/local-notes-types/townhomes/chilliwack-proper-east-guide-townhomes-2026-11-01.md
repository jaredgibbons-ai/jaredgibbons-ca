## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted complexes in the count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Thornberry Lane (8 units, listing-stated) is over the threshold; Heritage Lane's lot count is UNCONFIRMED but its unit numbers reach at least 13, so it is treated as over the threshold on that evidence — confirm on the Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Chilliwack short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141. Heritage Lane's directory line reads "rentals allowed," consistent with current law; the void rule is flagged in-entry in §2.3 for the two apartment buildings whose directories still print "rentals not allowed" (Mountain View Estates, Evergreen Estates) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). Neither counted townhouse complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED per complex until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Chilliwack Proper East (§2.2).

**City of Chilliwack plans, zoning, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **City plan:** the governing plan is the Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — specifically its **Schedule B2** Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan for this ground; the guide states the plan was added to the 2040 OCP as Schedule D through Bylaw 2018, No. 4533 (public hearing June 5, 2018), updated in 2025 as part of the 2050 OCP, to guide future decision making on residential infill development. Its designations as the guide states them are Residential 1 (one and two family housing) and Residential 2 (attached housing and narrow lot infill). **Standing schedule correction:** the Sardis plan is **Schedule B5** and the Agricultural Plan is **Schedule B1** of the same bylaw — a live page citing "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" for Sardis or "Schedule C" for agricultural ground is stale. This slug's own research does NOT document that stale citation on the chilliwack-proper-east-guide page, so it is recorded here as the standing correction only, not asserted as a defect of this page (research file §4; https://chilliwack.com/ocp; https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-east-guide.html).
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 governs. Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted **June 18, 2024**, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf).
- **Suites and accessory units (Bylaw 5000):** a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space (s. 2); only 1 accessory dwelling unit may be located on a lot, with permitted types including secondary suite, coach house and garden suite, garden suites capped at 100 m² (s. 4.06(1)); ADU parking is 1 space for a 1-bedroom ADU or any secondary suite and 2 spaces for a 2-or-more-bedroom coach or garden suite (s. 5.04); no secondary suite is legal unless built with a building permit meeting the BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf).
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; for 2026, a **5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026** — the City of Chilliwack's own two-stage calendar, not the rural regime and not Mission's flat 10% (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).
- **ALR and flood:** where a parcel is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)), and the ALC housing frame is one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). The City states agriculture accounts for 67% of the City's total land base. Parcel-level ALR and flood-construction-level status: UNVERIFIED — check per parcel (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Is there an official median price for Chilliwack Proper East townhouses?**
A: No. Chilliwack is not covered by SnapStats and CADREB publishes no sub-area statistics — no median, sales ratio or inventory figure for Proper East exists in the sources used, and none is stated here. Price from the property's own CADREB comparables (§1).

**Q: Thornberry Lane has only 8 units — does the depreciation-report rule still apply?**
A: Yes. The five-year depreciation-report duty since July 1, 2024 applies to stratas with five or more lots; at 8 units Thornberry Lane is over the threshold, and it cannot defer by vote. In an 8-lot strata, one roof or paving project is spread across very few owners (§3).

**Q: Which plan governs Proper East, and which schedule is it?**
A: The Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500 — specifically Schedule B2, the Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan. Sardis is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of the same bylaw (§3).

**Q: Can a Proper East house add a suite and a coach house?**
A: No — only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot where the zone permits: a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²), under Bylaw 5000. Separately, SSMUH (Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024) allows 3–4 units on eligible urban-growth-boundary parcels zoned single-detached/duplex. A suite must be permitted and meet flood-protection requirements; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve a Proper East townhouse?**
A: The guide page names Bernard Elementary (45465 Bernard Ave), Robertson Elementary (46106 Southlands Cres), Strathcona Elementary (46375 Strathcona Rd), Chilliwack Middle School (46354 Yale Rd) and Chilliwack Secondary (46363 Yale Rd) — names and addresses confirmed against the Ministry file — but catchments are address-based and no locator result for this area is on record, so run the exact address in SD33's School Locator before relying on a school (§1).

**Q: When are property taxes due, and what is the penalty?**
A: Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026 (§3).
