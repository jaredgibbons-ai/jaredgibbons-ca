## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (for any strata townhouse a band buyer does pursue, in its home file):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Chilliwack complex is flagged void in-entry on exactly this rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Most stock in this band, on the live page's own framing, is fee-simple houses and acreages, not strata product at all. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **City plan and zoning:** the current Official Community Plan is Chilliwack 2050, Bylaw No. 5500 (see §1 for the Schedule B1/B5 standing correction). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — its §6 sets the farm zones, and Development Permit Area 4 – Farm Home Plate applies where the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. Zoning figures for the agricultural and rural zone minimums in §6: UNVERIFIED in the research pass — the consolidated bylaw PDF could not be opened after the 429 errors, so no zone minimum is stated here. https://chilliwack.com/ocp and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009
- **SSMUH and suites (Chilliwack):** the SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024, allowing 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing. A secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04 (Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04). (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, items 6 and 7f)
- **ALR and farm rules:** properties in the ALR are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, fill or soil removal, a non-farm use, or a subdivision or boundary adjustment — check ALR status on the City Webmap. The ALC caps a principal residence at 500 m² total floor area, permits a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and limits an additional residence to up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha. Farm-class thresholds, where farm class is in issue, run in the full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Chilliwack property taxes (city ground only):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. This is the CITY scheme — FVRD electoral areas use the separate rural regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11, 5% after July 2 + 5% after Oct 31); never mix the two. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Taxes and rebates — with worked PTT examples, inputs stated:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — flagged; the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. At this band both exemptions are value-exhausted for most purchases, which is why the worked examples below carry no exemption. **Worked example 1 — $1,900,000 resale, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,700,000 ($34,000) = **$36,000**. **Worked example 2 — $2,500,000 resale, no exemption:** $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $500,000 ($15,000) = **$53,000**. **Worked example 3 — $3,200,000 resale, no exemption, residential portion taken as the full value:** $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,200,000 ($36,000) + further 2% × $200,000 above $3M ($4,000) = **$78,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted here — this file's own research documents the live mortgage calculator page's 2.80%–4.00% range nowhere for this slug, so no such assertion is made for this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
