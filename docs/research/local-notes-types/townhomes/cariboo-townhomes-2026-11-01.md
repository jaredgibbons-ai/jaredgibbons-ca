## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Strathmore Lane's published position is rentals allowed, so there is no void directory line to flag in this file's counted stock; the rule is stated because buyers cross-shopping Burnaby directories will meet "rentals not allowed" lines elsewhere. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Cariboo complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B; the 55-plus sibling verified no 55+ ownership complex in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning — Burnaby's current bylaw only:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, Bylaw No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026, replacing the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) — the 1965 bylaw is never the governing frame in this file. The new bylaw carries an R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District and an R2 Townhouse District in its Part 3 district list. Under the R1 SSMUH rules carried in the research file, lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan was adopted December 9, 2025 and replaces the 1998 OCP. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies here — that is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there really only one townhouse complex in Cariboo?** On named, tenure-verified evidence, yes: Strathmore Lane's townhouse component on Morrey Court (strata, built 2005). The Manchester Drive stock beside it is apartment condos (4 complexes in the condos sibling), and the interior streets are detached houses. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/strathmore-lane-3379-morrey-court
- **Q: Is Strathmore Lane in Cariboo or Sullivan Heights?** Both labels appear: the condos sibling and the Cariboo apartment index place the pocket in Cariboo; fresh listings for 3379 Morrey Court label the MLS sub-area Sullivan Heights. This file follows the Cariboo assignment and counts the complex here only — confirm the sub-area on the listing's full report before comping. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3013711-3379-morrey-court-22-burnaby-bc-v3j-7y4/brochure
- **Q: Strathmore Lane at 3388 Morrey Court looks like apartments — is it the same complex?** Same directory name, different built form: the 3388 listings are apartment-style units with an elevator, while 3379/3368 are townhouse units. Built form governs — only the townhouse component is counted here. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/strathmore-lane-3388-morrey-court
- **Q: Can I rent out a Strathmore Lane townhouse?** The directory states rentals allowed, and in any event long-term strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws on the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How quiet was this market in September 2026?** Very: SnapStats' Cariboo row shows 23 Condos & Townhomes listings and 1 sale (4%), and 0 detached listings and 0 detached sales — pricing evidence here is thin by structure, and the figures are combined condos-and-townhomes, never townhome-only. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.)
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse in Cariboo?** None verified: the 55-plus sibling found no verified 55+ strata ownership complex in this sub-area at all, in any built form. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/cariboo-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Morrey Court townhouse?** Address-based in SD41 — Armstrong Elementary, Lyndhurst Elementary and Cariboo Hill Secondary all sit in or beside this area, and the research file's locator result is UNVERIFIED, so run the exact address in the SD41 locator; never promise a school from the complex name. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: What does the Cameron rebuild mean for this pocket?** The City is replacing the Cameron Community Centre and Library ($267 million; aquatic facility, two-court gymnasium, weight room, indoor running track, fitness studios) while a temporary centre and library operate inside The City of Lougheed — a real amenity story with a construction-period gap buyers should know about. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-07-23/council-approves-award-contract-advance-construction-replacement-cameron
- **Q: Can a Cariboo house lot add units under the new bylaw?** Under the R1 SSMUH rules, 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, under Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026) — confirm the district for the specific lot in the 2026 bylaw, not the repealed 1965 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new townhouse, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **September 2026 recorded zero detached listings and zero detached sales for Cariboo.** The sub-area's entire recorded market that month was 23 condos & townhomes listings and 1 sale — a buyer comping a detached house here must leave the sub-area row and use the Burnaby detached median ($1,825,000, September 2026) and neighbouring rows instead. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.)
- **One directory name, two built forms, two MLS sub-areas.** Strathmore Lane is carried at 3379, 3368 and 3388 Morrey Court; the first two are the 2005 townhouses, the third is apartment units, and listings split the pocket between "Cariboo" and "Sullivan Heights" — the single easiest mis-file in this sub-area, which is why Section 2 states every basis. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/strathmore-lane-3388-morrey-court
- **The green space is conservation land, not a manicured park.** The City's park dedication bylaw dedicates the Cariboo and Brunette River Conservation Lands and the Stoney Creek Park and Trail System on the area's north side; Stoney Creek Park carries 2 tennis courts and 2 pickleball courts, and creek-side lots carry watercourse setbacks that travel with the creek, not the lot line. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- **Cariboo Hill Secondary dates the neighbourhood's growth era:** the City's heritage record dates the school to 1961, built because Armstrong, Seaforth and Lyndhurst elementaries had grown, opening with 410 students and peaking at over 1,100 in 1974, with additions in 1969, 1971 and 1974. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark837
- **The hospital is across the city, the urgent care is south-west:** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), is west of Cariboo, and the Edmonds Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at #201-7315 Edmonds St in the Edmonds town centre. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/service-directory/locations/burnaby/edmonds-urgent-and-primary-care-centre
- **There are no sub-area medians for Cariboo.** SnapStats publishes medians at community level only, so any "Cariboo median" quoted from that report does not exist; the Burnaby Condos & Townhomes median ($686,000, September 2026) is the closest published figure and is a combined category.
