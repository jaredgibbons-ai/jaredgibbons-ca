## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

**The commercial frame, exactly as this file's research documents it.** This page's stock is commercial, so this section carries the commercial rules the research actually documents first, states the commercial silences as UNCONFIRMED, and then gives the residential townhome block only as the cross-reference a townhouse buyer landing on this page needs — labelled as residential, not applied to commercial transactions.

- **The listing-data notice the page carries:** the page's footer states the data relating to real estate on the website comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of either the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB); listings held by participating firms are marked with the MLS® logo and include the listing agent's name; the representation is based on board-generated data the boards do not warrant for accuracy; and the materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. Treat the feed as a finding aid, and get the authoritative record (the Paragon/MLS® listing and the municipality's zoning confirmation for the intended use) before relying on it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/commercial.html
- **The regulator's framework applies to commercial trading services as it does to residential:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed real estate professional in BC must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings — whether the professional will be able to represent the consumer as a client, what the duties to clients and non-clients are, and how to file a complaint with BCFSA — and dual agency (representing two or more clients in the same transaction whose interests conflict) was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The national frame:** the Canadian Real Estate Association (CREA), a trade association, owns the trademarks Multiple Listing Service® ("MLS®") and REALTOR®, and agents and brokers must become members of a local real estate board and CREA in order to access the MLS® system and must adhere to CREA's rules and their boards' rules. https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage

- **Residential townhome block — cross-reference only (does NOT govern commercial transactions).** For a reader who lands here looking for a townhouse: strata townhome diligence runs on the Form B under Strata Property Act s. 59 (fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); CRF at least 10% of the annual operating budget since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (any directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023); fee-simple townhomes have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Residential tax/rebate figures — cross-reference only (NOT commercial treatment).** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, for eligible first-time buyers of new RESIDENTIAL homes; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; residential PTT 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would compress that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this research); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5% (residential insured mortgages only). None of these figures is asserted for any listing on the commercial page. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA) — residential/development frame, stated for completeness:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21).
