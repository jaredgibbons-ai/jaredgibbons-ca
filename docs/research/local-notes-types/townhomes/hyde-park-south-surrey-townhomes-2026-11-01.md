## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted complexes in full — each is a strata townhome complex, with its own Form B package).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner are separate stratas, so order the package for the strata the unit actually sits in. The live page itself names the Form B, bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report as the pre-subject package (research file §6; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Hyde Park's developer-carried 156 homes put it well over the threshold; Hyde Park Corner's page-stated 16 homes would also clear it — but the Corner count itself is UNCONFIRMED (§2.1), so its threshold position is read off the strata plan, not the page. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any "rentals not allowed" line for either complex is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). Neither complex carries a verified age bylaw; neither is 55+ in any source checked (§2.1) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Not applicable to either complex (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Both Hyde Park complexes are the opposite case: strata fees, a Form B and registered bylaws govern.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite per dwelling unit, and never within a coach house or garden suite (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite).
- **Suites in townhouses:** Surrey Council approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones **for new developments** in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — those permissions are not a blanket right in existing complexes like these (https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting.
- **Property taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Is Hyde Park a neighbourhood in South Surrey?**
A: No — Hyde Park is a named townhome complex in Grandview Surrey, near 28 Avenue and 156 Street. A Zenterra brochure lists it at 156 townhomes in the developer's project history, and there is no Hyde Park board sub-area or SnapStats row (§1).

**Q: Are Hyde Park and Hyde Park Corner the same complex?**
A: No. Hyde Park Corner is a separate phase at 2828 156 Street, completed 2021, with its own strata plan — the live page states the separation and the developer brochure lists the two separately. Establish which strata a unit sits in before comparing fees or documents (§2.1).

**Q: Who built Hyde Park, how many homes, and when?**
A: Zenterra Developments; 156 townhomes for the main complex — the count is carried on the developer's own brochure, the strongest sourcing in this file — completed 2016 per MLS listing data. Hyde Park Corner (2021) states 16 homes on the page only, held UNCONFIRMED (§2.1).

**Q: What market numbers apply to Hyde Park?**
A: None of its own: SnapStats publishes no Hyde Park row. The board context for its ground is Grandview (Detached 66 / 9 / 14%; Condos & Townhomes 146 / 20 / 14% in September 2026) and the S Surrey White Rock community figures (attached median $700,000) — context only, not Hyde Park prices (§1).

**Q: What should I check before buying at Hyde Park?**
A: First, which strata the unit belongs to — the two complexes share a street and a name root but have separate plans, fees, bylaws and depreciation reports. Then the package the live page itself names: the Form B, the bylaws and rules, the last two years of minutes and the depreciation report — plus the floor plan, since tandem-garage, double-garage and main-floor primary bedroom layouts all exist in the main complex (§2.1; research file §6).

**Q: Can I rent out a Hyde Park townhome?**
A: Long-term rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022, so the strata cannot enforce one; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Surrey's STR frame is owner-only with the licence conflict disclosed in §3. Read the registered bylaws attached to the Form B for the correct strata (§3).

**Q: Are either of these complexes 55+?**
A: No source checked says so for either complex, and no verified 55+ complex exists anywhere in the Grandview sub-area in this program's checks — and in law only a 55+ bylaw could restrict age at all (§2.1; §3).

**Q: Which schools serve Hyde Park?**
A: The live page names Sunnyside Elementary and Grandview Heights Secondary as the catchments for both complexes and itself advises confirming with the Surrey Schools locator; both schools are confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file (2828 159 St and 16987 25 Ave) — but no SD36 locator run for a Hyde Park address is on record, so the catchments are UNVERIFIED: run the unit's exact address before relying on a school (§1).

**Q: The presales page says Hyde Park was built in 2021 — is that right?**
A: That line is site copy on the presales page's completed-communities list, and 2021 is Hyde Park Corner's year. MLS listing data for the main complex's own addresses states 2016. This file follows the listing data and flags the list line as the Corner's year misapplied (§2.1).

**Q: Is there anything for sale at Hyde Park right now?**
A: UNVERIFIED from the page: the page text read displays no live listing cards and no feed count, so check the South Surrey townhome search, or ask for the current feed for 2888 156 Street / 2828 156 Street (§1).
