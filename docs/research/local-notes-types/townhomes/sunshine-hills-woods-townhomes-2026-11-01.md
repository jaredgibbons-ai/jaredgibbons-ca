## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata diligence — the Form B regime (all 4 counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. BCREA advises reviewing the Form B for the depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies — for Nicholson Green the Form B is also the document that settles the specific unit's form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. That cycle reaches both ends of this sub-area's stock — Sunshine Woods (1989) at the mature end and Chelsea Gate / Cyrus Hill (2018–2021) at the new end. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry: the building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Sunshine Woods (6300 Cottonwood Street) is void on this rule — it reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunshine-woods-6300-cottonwood-street
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners, defined children). No age bylaw for any counted complex here was published in the sources checked: UNCONFIRMED — and no 55+ townhouse complex is verified anywhere in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. The sub-area's confirmed pattern is the opposite: predominantly freehold detached houses, with strata appearing only in the named townhouse complexes at the area's edges. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/sunshine-hills-woods.md, §5)
- **City plan:** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), applies — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950. OCP Map 2 shows a Small Scale Residential designation for this part of North Delta, while a City corridor report confirms townhouse projects have been built along the 72 Avenue corridor under the Mixed Residential designation. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/6017/7676/9593/022d99cd68f15bb5dc1b474b79ce06c4_Proposed207220Avenue20Corridor20Pre-Zoning2028LU00987629.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research and sibling files cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017) and its residential ladder (RS1 at 4,000 m², RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m²; duplex minimums RD1 at 836 m², RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m², RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone). The program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. No City source names a predominant zone for Sunshine Hills Woods. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **SSMUH:** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws (Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) permit small-scale multi-unit housing on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide, and Delta's rules allow secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination with detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies — relevant to the area's house lots, not to its strata townhouse lots. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/ and http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Streamside rules:** Cougar Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse, and any property within 30 m of a stream falls in Delta's Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit additions and rebuilds — directly relevant to Chelsea Gate, whose presale record places it alongside Cougar Creek, and to canyon-side lots generally. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. All 4 counted complexes are resale stock, so no GST applies to a resale purchase and the REDMA s. 21 7-day rescission / s. 18 trust-deposit frame has no live townhouse presale to attach to in this sub-area. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **EMAIL + CLAIMS CHECK:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and NO instance of the wrong email jared@jaredgibbons.ca is documented in-file — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. - **The Nicholson Road pocket is a different daily life from the park interior.** The townhouse and condo pocket at Nicholson Road markets walk-to access to the Scott Road shopping node (Scottsdale Centre across 70 Avenue in the Village at Sunshine Hills directory: Save-On-Foods, Walmart Supercentre, Dollarama, BC Liquor) and Scott Road transit, while the interior's amenity is Watershed Park's eleven kilometres of gravel trails. Same sub-area label, two different products. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/village-at-sunshine-hills/ and https://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/park-and-amenity-search/watershed-park
- **A co-op hides in the townhouse pocket.** Cougar Canyon Housing Co-operative (6800–6846 Nicholson Road, 18 units) sits in the same Nicholson corridor as the strata complexes, states "No age requirement" in the City guide, and is family co-op housing — a search that treats every attached complex on Nicholson Road as a strata townhouse will mis-describe it. http://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/AffordableHousingGuide_April2026.pdf
- **The sub-area name itself is two neighbourhoods.** "Sunshine Hills Woods" is a Paragon combination of Sunshine Hills and Sunshine Woods, and the City publishes no boundary for the combined name — the complex named Sunshine Woods (6300 Cottonwood St, 1989) predates the combined label, and two listings under the same sub-area name can sit in different elementary catchments (Sunshine Hills vs Cougar Canyon). Verify by address, not by the label. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/2317/7677/3211/efc5943aed27af7974bb64e787f9311b_Schedule_C_-_OCP.pdf and https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Delta's flat-rate utility fee is a line item house buyers forget.** The City's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home — a fixed annual cost that sits outside both strata fees and property tax for the detached streets around these complexes. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
