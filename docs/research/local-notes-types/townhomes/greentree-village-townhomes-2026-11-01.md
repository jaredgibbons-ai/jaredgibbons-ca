## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (each counted phase — separately):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Because each Greentree phase is its own strata corporation, a Form B from one phase says nothing about any other phase — order the package for the phase the unit actually sits in, identified by civic address and strata plan, not by the village name. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a 4-lot-and-under strata sits UNDER that threshold. Phase lot counts are UNCONFIRMED here, so each phase's threshold status is UNCONFIRMED — for 1974–1975 wood-frame phases, the depreciation report is the single most important document in the package either way. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Greentree phase carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the usable sources read (the phases' restrictions are UNCONFIRMED); if one surfaces in a phase's older bylaw text, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file checked the Greentree townhomes and verified no age restriction — the village is described in listings as family-oriented. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Greentree Village — the village's townhouse stock is strata. Detached houses on village streets are ordinary fee-simple houses: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, City of Burnaby rules and any registered covenants only.
- **City zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and the apartment districts R3–R8. The village's own 1972 community plan remains the historical plan document for the site (link in Section 1). https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **SSMUH (R1):** Burnaby's R1 district (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m²; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; permitted forms include single-family, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. SSMUH speaks to detached lots, not to the existing strata phases. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps — read the 1972 Greentree plan as the village's origin document under the 2050 OCP, not as current policy standing alone. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, and a further 5% after the September due date; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify these penalties. Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, and Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home) for 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/greentree-village.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% per CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — every Greentree home is a resale. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — Greentree Village is in Burnaby, and that tax is Vancouver-only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Where is Greentree Village, actually?** South of Canada Way behind BCIT, west of Deer Lake — the V5G pocket bounded by Wayburne Drive, Woodsworth Street, Forest Lawn Cemetery and Moscrop Street. Any source placing it at Marlborough/Sussex near Metrotown is wrong, including this area's own research file; the City's 1972 community plan fixes the site. Source: the flag at the top; City plan (Section 1).
- **Is Greentree Village one strata?** No. It is one planned community built as multiple strata phases, each its own corporation. Your Form B, fees, CRF and depreciation report come from your phase only. Sources: Section 2 counting basis; Section 3.
- **How many townhouse complexes are there?** Four phase corporations are verified (Garden Grove Drive, Fernglen, Cedarglen Place, Laurelwood Place). Three more phase streets (Elmgrove, Driftwood, Village Drive) are documented but UNCONFIRMED — their only detailed roster sits on a directory this program does not cite. Source: Section 2.
- **How old is the stock?** 1974–1975 for the verified phases (the Fernglen listing states 1974; Cedarglen and Laurelwood listings state 1975; the Garden Grove year conflicts between 1975 and 1997 in sources and is UNCONFIRMED). These are 50-year-old wood-frame strata — the depreciation report and levy history are the diligence. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **What amenities does the village have?** Listings describe an indoor pool, gym and party room as community amenities, a recreation facility across the street from the Garden Grove phase, and Greentree Village Park/playground inside the village — listing data; the recreation facility's official name and operator are UNCONFIRMED. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Can I rent out a Greentree townhouse?** A long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void in every BC strata since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Each phase's registered bylaws are UNCONFIRMED until read on that phase's Form B. Source: Section 3.
- **Is Greentree Village 55+?** No — the sibling 55-plus file checked the townhomes and verified no age restriction; listings describe the village as family-oriented. Source: sibling 55-plus file on disk.
- **Which schools serve the village?** Listings state Gilpin Elementary and Moscrop Secondary — listing statements, not locator runs. The SD41 locator result for a specific village address is UNVERIFIED; run it before relying on a school. Do not use any source naming École Marlborough Elementary for this pocket — that school belongs to the research file's wrong geography. Source: Section 1.
- **Does Greentree count as Deer Lake for listings?** No. The village is its own board sub-area; the Deer Lake files record zero Greentree stock, and this file counts the village once, here. Deer Lake Park's trails are at the village's east side, which is a setting, not a filing. Sources: the count-once note at the top; Section 2 entries.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- Greentree Village is older than the SkyTrain, older than BCIT's current campus form, and older than most of Burnaby's townhouse stock: it was planned in a single City report dated November 6, 1972 (Manager's Report No. 72, Rezoning #1/72) at about 450 units on roughly 50 acres — a complete townhouse village approved before "townhouse" was a common Burnaby form. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/nkpx5fd25q/greentree-village-community-plan-1972
- The village's east boundary is a cemetery: Forest Lawn Cemetery closes the site on the east, which is why the village's streets are all internal crescents and places with no through traffic — and why Deer Lake trails, not roads, continue east. https://burnaby.widen.net/s/nkpx5fd25q/greentree-village-community-plan-1972
- A "100% sales ratio" for this sub-area in September 2026 means two listings sold out of two listings. With stock this thin, sub-area statistics here describe individual sales, not a market. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
