## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no verified townhouse in this sub-area for these rules to attach to today; they are stated because a buyer searching "Northeast Maple Ridge townhomes" will meet them on the nearest real townhouse purchase elsewhere in Maple Ridge, and because they frame any future attached listing here.

- **Form B diligence (any future strata townhouse):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget and the most recent depreciation report attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Maple Ridge building is void for long-term rentals and must be flagged in-entry wherever one is carried. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Northeast complex is verified as 55+ — there is no verified complex at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality here, stated plainly:** a Northeast acreage purchase has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019), ALR rules where they apply, and any registered covenants on title govern. That is the frame for the stock that actually exists in this sub-area.
- **Maple Ridge zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; its schedule minimum lot areas include A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, A-4 1.8 ha, A-5 8.0 ha, RS-2 0.4 ha, RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha (refer to zone text), RS-1d 2,000 m² and RS-1c 1,200 m². http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, never resolved:** program sources cite the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan as Bylaw 7060-2014 in some places and Bylaw 7998-2024 in others; this file asserts neither as settled — quote planning policy from a current City document for the specific parcel. What is settled for this sub-area: the North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan (OCP amendments covering both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue, about 500 acres) establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction, and the OCP's Thornhill Urban Reserve designation identifies lands for long-term future urban services and housing. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2
- **Suites / SSMUH as Maple Ridge states them:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation also governs — one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31), an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met. A suite is a fee-simple second unit, never a townhouse. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Tax timing:** a City of Maple Ridge 2026 property-tax due date confirmed on a City source in this pass: UNVERIFIED (research file, §4) — no date is asserted here.
- **GST and rebates (for a new-build purchase elsewhere in the city):** 5% GST on new construction only; the FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — rebates 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate is 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, compression flagged:** 1% on the first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row compresses that 3% + 2%. First-time buyer exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. **HBP** $60,000; **FHSA** $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; **CMHC** premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

- **Q: Which schools are physically inside the sub-area?** Blue Mountain Elementary, Webster's Corners Elementary, Whonnock Elementary, Garibaldi Secondary and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary. SD42 has no middle tier — elementary is K–7, secondary is 8–12. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools/ and https://www.sd42.ca/secondary-schools/
- **Q: What catchment does an address here feed?** Address-based, through the SD42 School Locator. The recorded sample — 25554 Dewdney Trunk Road — gives Webster's Corners Elementary and Garibaldi Secondary; the French Immersion schools shown for that address are choice programs, not catchments. Other addresses: UNVERIFIED until run. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: Can an acreage home here add a suite or a second dwelling?** One secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3 if it sits inside the single detached building, is at most 40% of gross floor area and is not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR land an additional separate residence is allowed only under the ALR Use Regulation's s. 32 conditions. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Q: What is the North 256 Street plan, and does it approve building?** Council approved OCP amendments creating a plan area on both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres); it sets future designations and does not itself approve construction. Existing uses include gravel extraction, institutional operations and civic services. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic
- **Q: How is transit, honestly?** Thin and road-based: route 701 on Dewdney Trunk Road (most east trips end at 248 Street) and route 748's Thornhill loop at wide intervals, weekdays and Saturdays only. Price a home here as a drive-to-everything property. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/194/tt701.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt748.pdf
- **Q: What land checks matter before subjects come off on an acreage?** ALR status per parcel through the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder; the well record in the provincial GWELLS database; and septic compliance with the Sewerage System Regulation, which sets setbacks including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Q: Does the floodplain reach this sub-area?** At its south edge: the City's floodplain framework addresses Fraser River, Alouette River system, steep-creek and groundwater flooding, and low land along the Fraser River at the south edge is the area to check on City flood maps, property by property. Secondary suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
