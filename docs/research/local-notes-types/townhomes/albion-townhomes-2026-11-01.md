## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all four counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For The Warwick, first establish whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all four counted complexes are well over the threshold on their published counts where counts exist (Ridgemont 97, Spencer Brook 40, Warwick 31; Albion Station's count is UNCONFIRMED but the complex presents as multi-building strata). A strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line was found for the counted complexes — Albion Station is marketed as rental friendly and Spencer Brook's record states rentals allowed, both consistent with current law; any such line met elsewhere for an Albion complex is flagged VOID in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Albion complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — the 55-plus sibling verified NO 55+ stock of any kind in Albion — and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Albion (Section 2).
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs — RS-1 (s. 605: minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%) is the standard house zone in the schedule read, with RM townhome sites around the Albion Commercial Corner (The Warwick's listing records zoning RM-1 for that complex) and ALR parcels in South Albion. **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). The Albion Area Plan is Chapter 10.2 of the OCP, and Council adopted updates to it on **October 28, 2025**: enhanced walking and cycling connections, support for local commercial nodes, and buffers where urban development meets farmland; its residential designations include Low Density Residential, Ground-Oriented Residential, Cluster Residential and Townhouse, plus an Albion Village Commercial node. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/albion?bidId= (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/albion.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor note:** the corridor plan area lies west of Albion (the corridor runs from the city's west boundary to 221 Street, adopted Jan 27, 2026 per the City record, Bylaw 7781-2021 — the position carried in the Maple Ridge guide files); it does not designate Albion parcels. Verify any corridor claim for a specific property on the City's current document. (Guide condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-central-maple-ridge-guide-condos-2026-11-01.md)

- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted). Worked tier example, on an $800,000 townhouse: $200,000 × 1% = $2,000, plus $600,000 × 2% = $12,000, total $14,000 before any exemption. First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000 — relevant to any developer sale at The Warwick, not to resales. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
