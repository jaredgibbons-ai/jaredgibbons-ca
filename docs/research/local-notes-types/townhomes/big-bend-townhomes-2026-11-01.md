## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Big Bend is the City of Burnaby's main agricultural area in south Burnaby, on the Fraser River foreshore flats below the city's steep south slope. **Stated openly, following the research file: Big Bend is primarily an industrial and agricultural district, not a conventional residential neighbourhood** — it combines ALR farmland, the Big Bend industrial lands along the river, Burnaby Fraser Foreshore Park, and a small rural-residential component (the Riverside area). "Big Bend" is the GVR board sub-area name as it appears in the SnapStats GVR September 2026 report under community "Burnaby," and it matches the name the City itself uses for its south Burnaby agricultural area. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention and (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- Boundaries: the City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area — exact MLS boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. The City describes the lowland as nearly 800 hectares between the steep south slope and the river, along nearly 7 km of the North Arm of the Fraser River. Nearby areas: South Slope and Garden Village above the bluff to the north, Edmonds to the north-east, the Glenlyon / Byrne Creek industrial lands within the wider south Burnaby employment area, and Richmond across the North Arm to the south. Marine Way / Marine Drive runs along the top of the foreshore flats and Byrne Road and Sussex Avenue descend to the river lands. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres

- Flood — the district's second defining fact: the nearly 800 hectares of low-lying area between the steep south slope and the Fraser River is a floodplain; the Fraser River poses a substantial flood hazard from snowmelt in late spring and early summer and from combined heavy rain and winter storm surge events. The City protects the lowland with river and shoreline dykes, pump stations and flood boxes, maintains a Flood Response Plan, and receives regular updates from the BC River Forecast Centre during flooding events and freshet. http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- Agriculture — the defining fact: land in Big Bend is in the provincial Agricultural Land Reserve (ALR), administered by the Agricultural Land Commission, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend. The City runs a Burnaby Farm Tour Map for the Big Bend farming district, promoting farm-gate and nursery sales, and states Big Bend has a culturally diverse farming heritage, with historic Chinese Canadian small-scale farms that still exist today. A1 is the City's agricultural zoning district in Big Bend (chickens may be kept in the A1 district under Zoning Bylaw rules). https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/agriculture-big-bend and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

The nearest library branch is the Tommy Douglas Branch, 7311 Kingsway, up the slope in Edmonds; the city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St. There is no SkyTrain station in Big Bend — the district is car- and truck-served, and specific TransLink bus routes serving the foreshore streets were not confirmed: UNVERIFIED. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The rules that actually decide a Big Bend purchase come first, because there is no townhouse stock here: ALR and flood.** ALR status and permitted farm / non-farm uses for a specific property are checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder, and ALR land is governed by the provincial Agricultural Land Commission Act framework — run the ALR check before pricing development or non-farm value into any parcel. Flood: the district is a roughly 800-hectare Fraser River floodplain protected by dykes, pump stations and flood boxes — confirm the parcel's flood construction requirements, dyke position and insurance before subjects come off, not after. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and http://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/water-and-sewers/flood-prevention
- **Strata rules — stated because they would govern any strata lot a buyer did find in the district, and because an unnamed attached listing count exists (Section 2).** Form B diligence: Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. If a property marketed as "Big Bend" carries no strata plan, there is no Form B — verify the tenure before applying strata diligence at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023) — the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ stock of any type in Big Bend. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple property (the district's actual residential form — Riverside rural-residential homes and farm parcels):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules, ALR status and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **City zoning:** Burnaby's Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, No. 4742, is repealed and superseded — any Big Bend document citing the 1965 bylaw is out of date. A1 is the City's agricultural zoning district in Big Bend, and the City is updating zoning regulations for agricultural districts through the 2026 rewrite — parcel-level minimum lot sizes and the current district for a specific farm parcel: UNVERIFIED, confirm in writing with the City and the ALC. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025) supports protecting and expanding agriculture in Big Bend. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Big Bend is in Burnaby, and the 3% EHT is Vancouver only. City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date — farm-class assessment and tax treatment for a specific Big Bend parcel is a BC Assessment / tax-notice check per property: UNVERIFIED here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Presales (REDMA):** no residential presale in Big Bend is confirmed; where a presale is bought elsewhere, the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and the 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21) govern, with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
