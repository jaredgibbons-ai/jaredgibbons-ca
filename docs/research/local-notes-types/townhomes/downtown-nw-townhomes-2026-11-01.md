## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata complex, and the Brickstone Walk building):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a Brickstone Walk (828 Royal) purchase, the Form B is also the document that settles which strata — and which bylaws — a townhouse-filed unit actually sits in. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing/directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Downtown townhouse complex exists to flag — the counted complex's directory line is "rentals allowed" in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ complex is verified in Downtown (see the sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple position:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in Section 2, so the plain rule is stated for completeness: a fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only. Downtown's verified townhouse stock is strata, so a Downtown townhouse buyer should expect the full Form B track. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **New Westminster zoning — with a research-file ERROR corrected in-file:** Downtown zoning is in Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, including Columbia Street historic and high-rise commercial districts; minimum lot sizes are zone-specific in the bylaw text, and a Downtown-specific minimum is UNVERIFIED. **Correction:** the research file states that townhouse form elsewhere in the city is regulated under "Townhouse Residential Districts RT-A and RT-B" — **RT-A/RT-B do not exist in New Westminster; that is an ERROR in the research file.** The city's Townhouse Residential Districts are **RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only**, and the corrected district set is what this file carries. The governing plan is Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), with the Downtown Community Plan as Schedule C; Downtown is designated a Regional City Centre, and the OCP is under a 2025–2026 update process (OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 8574, 2026). The City's OCP Development Permit Area 1.3 (Townhouses) guidelines govern townhouse design where the DPA applies and state that townhouse tenure may be ownership (strata titled), non-ownership (rental) or co-operative — the DPA is a design frame, not a tenure grant. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md, zoning line corrected as stated) and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/BL_8547__DPA_Map_and_Guidelines___1_3_Townhouses___December_3__2025.pdf
- **Flood — this sub-area's defining diligence item:** the City's floodplain study places the Downtown waterfront in **Floodplain Zone 3** — the Fraser River waterfront zone — where many newer buildings have floor space above the Flood Construction Level, but egress and the waterfront path sit below it. The Royal Avenue slope complexes sit above the waterfront, but a Downtown buyer's building-level check is the same: confirm the specific building's flood construction level compliance, strata insurance and deductible position before subject removal. The City publishes flood management information for its low-lying zones. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. New Westminster operates its own electrical utility alongside City water / sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Downtown strata unit confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/downtown-nw.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a future 811 Carnarvon presale contract), not resales.
