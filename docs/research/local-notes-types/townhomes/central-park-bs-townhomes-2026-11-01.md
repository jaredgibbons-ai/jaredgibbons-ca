## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence:** no standalone strata townhouse complex exists here to attach a Form B to — but the two townhouse components in Section 2 sit inside apartment strata (Central Park House; Aldynne on the Park), so a buyer of either component buys a strata lot in a tower strata and the full Form B diligence applies: Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a 4-lot-and-under strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both tower strata above are far over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. This is flagged in-entry in Section 2 for Central Park Place, 4160 Sardis Street, whose directory line reads "rentals not allowed" — that line is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Central Park building is published as 55+, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ strata ownership in the sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Central Park BS. If a fee-simple row home surfaces here, the rule is stated plainly: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, City of Burnaby rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and the apartment districts R3–R8; the Wilson/Patterson towers sit in the apartment districts, which is the zoning expression of why this sub-area's attached stock is towers, not townhouse complexes. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **SSMUH (R1):** Burnaby's R1 district (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m²; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; permitted forms include single-family, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. SSMUH is the only route by which new townhouse-form homes could appear on this sub-area's detached streets — none is verified built. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, and a further 5% after the September due date; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify these penalties. Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, and Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home) for 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/central-park-bs.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% per CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — Central Park BS is in Burnaby, and that tax is Vancouver-only, even though Boundary Road and Vancouver are at the sub-area's west edge. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Are there any townhouse complexes around Central Park?** No standalone complex is verified. The townhouse-form homes that exist are 6 townhomes inside the Central Park House tower project and a townhouse-form home inside Aldynne on the Park — both are strata lots in tower projects. Source: Section 2.
- **A listing calls 5822 Patterson Avenue a townhouse — is it in a townhouse complex?** No. It is a townhouse-form home within Aldynne on the Park, Polygon's 2017 apartment project at 5883 Barker Avenue. You would be buying into that tower's strata, fees and bylaws. Source: Section 2 entry.
- **What does buying a Central Park House townhome actually involve?** The same diligence as any lot in that 41-storey strata: the Form B package for the whole tower strata, its depreciation report, CRF balance and levy history — the townhome's two-storey form does not separate it from the tower's finances. Source: Section 3.
- **Can I rent out a unit at Central Park Place, 4160 Sardis Street? The directory says rentals are not allowed.** That directory line is void for long-term rentals — no BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been valid since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted; read the registered bylaws via the Form B. Source: Section 3.
- **Is École Inman Elementary our catchment school?** No. Inman is a French Immersion choice program, not a catchment school; catchments here are address-based in SD41 and the locator result for a specific address is UNVERIFIED until run. Sources: research file (Section 1).
- **Is this the same area as "Central" in Burnaby listings?** No. "Central" is the Canada Way / City Hall sub-area; Central Park BS is the Burnaby South pocket around Central Park and Metrotown's west edge. The names collide; the areas do not touch. Source: the note at the top of this file.
- **Does the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax apply on the Boundary Road side?** No. The tax is Vancouver-only; every address in this sub-area is in Burnaby, including the ones on Boundary Road's Burnaby side. Source: Section 3.
- **Could new townhouses appear here under SSMUH?** Burnaby's R1 district permits rowhouses among its small-scale forms on qualifying lots, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — but no SSMUH townhouse project in this sub-area is verified built. Source: Section 3.
- **What is Kingsway Court on Inman Avenue?** Subsidized non-profit rental housing (106 bachelor + 10 one-bedroom units) run by the Central Park Citizen Society — not for sale in any form. Source: Section 2 (sibling 55-plus file).
