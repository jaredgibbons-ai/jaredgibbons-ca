## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no verified townhouse stock in Harbour Chines for these rules to attach to today; they are the rules a buyer applies the moment any attached product is marketed under this sub-area name — most realistically a resale unit in a neighbouring complex mislabelled, or the Port Coquitlam exclusion below mis-filed.

- **Form B diligence (strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry: Harbour Place Estates' directory line "rentals allowed with restrictions" carries a rental-restriction element that is VOID for long-term rentals under this rule (the complex itself is excluded from this file on geography). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Harbour Chines complex is published as age-restricted — there is no verified complex to publish one against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (none verified here):** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Harbour Chines' verified stock is detached houses, which sit in the same no-strata world; do not let a "townhouse-style" marketing line on a detached listing import strata diligence that does not exist — or skip the Form B on a true strata unit mislabelled as a house. Basis: the tenure definitions applied across this program (see `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/central-coquitlam-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`, §3).
- **City zoning and plan — standing corrections applied (Coquitlam):** Zoning Bylaw **No. 3000, 1996** — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, subdivision minimums 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m (R-2). The **Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — was adopted April 27, 2026 and supersedes OCP No. 3479**; cite the interim OCP, never 3479. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. On Harbour Chines' detached lots, SSMUH — not a townhouse complex — is the route to added density. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse check:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Coquitlam outside Northeast Coquitlam (50 m there); Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks and s. 523 covers riparian areas. Lot-level status for any Harbour Chines property: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Property tax:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, covering January 1 through December 31, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Coquitlam — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
