## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata component):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Vista 29 the Form B package must be read **with the title**: the development's 55+ occupancy rule sits in a registered restrictive covenant as well as the bylaws (next bullets), so the covenant — not just the Form B — is a diligence document for the townhome component counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Vista 29's strata (50 lots) and Somerset Green's strata (143 units per the District report) are both well above the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry in this file: Kings Court's directory line "rentals not allowed" (§2) is **VOID in-entry** and is flagged there, not repeated as a live restriction. At Vista 29, a long-term tenant must still satisfy the 55+ age bylaw **and** the occupancy covenant — voiding the rental bylaw does not void either age instrument. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws — and the Vista 29 covenant layer:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. The bylaw restricts **residents, not owners** — people under 55 can own a lot in a 55+ corporation but may not be able to live in it. **Vista 29 has a second, separate instrument:** the District's 2004 approval included a restrictive covenant limiting occupancy to households where one member is 55 years of age or older, alongside the CD52 zoning. The covenant survives independently of any later bylaw change — have both reviewed on a purchase of the townhome component counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Upper Lonsdale (§2). A fee-simple purchase here is a detached house on the slope — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; municipal rules (which municipality depends on the side of 29th Street — next two bullets) plus any registered covenants/HOA govern instead.

At Vista 29 the occupancy covenant applies on top of the bylaw — listings phrase the rule as "One owner to be 55+," but the registered bylaw and covenant govern over listing wording. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Vista 29's 55+ rule only a strata bylaw?** No — it has a second layer: the District's approval for the site included a restrictive covenant limiting occupancy to households where one member is 55 or older, written into the CD52 rezoning in addition to the strata's own bylaws. Have both instruments reviewed on a purchase; the covenant survives independently of any later bylaw change. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a Vista 29 townhouse long-term?** The rental question and the age question are separate: every strata rental-restriction bylaw in BC became invalid on November 24, 2022, so a long-term rental is permitted in law — but the tenant still has to satisfy the 55+ age bylaw (or an exemption) **and** the occupancy covenant. The same void rule is why Kings Court's directory line "rentals not allowed" is flagged VOID in its §2 entry. (§3 above)
- **Q: My spouse is under 55. Can we both live in a Vista 29 townhouse?** Under provincial strata rules, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt. Live-in caregivers are also exempt, as are children under 19 in the resident's care and adult children the resident cared for before 19 — Regulation 7.01, expanded effective May 1, 2023. At Vista 29, confirm the covenant's wording as well as the bylaw's before relying on an exemption. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Chartwell Churchill House something I can buy into, since it sits between the two 55+ buildings?** No. It is a private-pay rental retirement residence at 150 West 29th Street, registered with the Assisted Living Registry (operator Chartwell Master Care LP, class SN), offering independent living and assisted living in rental suites — not strata ownership. (§2 above)
- **Q: A unit on one side of 29th Street and an identical one on the other — same taxes, same rules?** No. Both municipalities set 2026 taxes due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after July 2, further 5% after September 1), but the City total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 plus 0.10697 storm drainage, against the District's 3.28195 per $1,000 — and the OCP, zoning bylaw, suite rules and permit desk all follow the municipality, not the sub-area name. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Queens Road / 29th Street address?** UNVERIFIED per address — SD44 catchments are address-based and no locator run for either complex's address is on record in this program. The serving schools as the research file states them are Westview and Larson elementary schools (Larson is dual-track English/French Immersion), École André-Piolat (francophone, K–12) on West Kings Road, and Carson Graham Secondary for most addresses — with easternmost addresses to be checked against the Argyle boundary. (§1 above)
- **Q: How do I commute downtown without driving from the 29th Street block?** Route 230 buses run the full length of Lonsdale Avenue past the 29th Street and Kings Road blocks, down to Lonsdale Quay, where the SeaBus crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes, every 15 minutes during the day and every 10 minutes at peak. (§1 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The whole 55+ cluster exists because one block was designated for it.** The District's North Lonsdale–Delbrook Community Plan designated all of the 100 block of West Queens Road / West 29th Street for seniors' or empty-nester multi-family development at a maximum density of 36.3 units per acre — which is why Somerset Green (just across the City line), Vista 29 and the Churchill House rental block all sit within a few hundred feet of each other at Queensdale, and why no other block in Upper Lonsdale has any. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/council_reports/512271.pdf
- **Somerset Green has two addresses.** The complex is listed as 121 West 29th Street, but units are also addressed as 2800 Chesterfield Avenue — searching only one address misses listings under the other. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3120637-201-2800-chesterfield-avenue-north-vancouver-british-columbia-v7n4m1-735000/brochure (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/upper-lonsdale-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Somerset Green's build year is not consistent across sources.** Most current listings state 1987; at least one current listing (MLS R3117942) states 1989. This file quotes neither as settled — the strata plan and Form B settle it.
