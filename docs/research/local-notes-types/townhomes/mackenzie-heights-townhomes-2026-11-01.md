## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- MacKenzie Heights is a west-side residential pocket on the slope above the Fraser River, between Shaughnessy and Kerrisdale, centred on the West 37th to West 49th Avenue blocks west of Granville Street. The City lists "MacKenzie Heights" as a recognised smaller area, but it is not one of the City's 22 distinct local areas — it sits inside the Kerrisdale local area. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary; exact board boundary roads: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: almost nowhere. The pocket's verified stock is detached single-family houses on tree-lined slope streets; the research file states strata condos and townhouses concentrate toward the West 41st Avenue village edges rather than inside the pocket. The one named townhouse complex verified inside the research geography in this pass sits on the pocket's northern edge street, West 37th Avenue (The Heights, Section 2). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/mackenzie-heights.md)
- Shopping and spine: Kerrisdale Village on West 41st Avenue is the pocket's shopping district — more than 250 businesses and services between Maple and Larch Streets and along West and East Boulevards, per the Kerrisdale Business Association as cited in the research file. West 41st Avenue is the east–west spine; Granville Street is the east edge; West Boulevard / Arbutus Street carries traffic north–south; the Arbutus Greenway runs along the pocket's eastern side for 8.5 km and crosses West 41st Avenue at the village. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- Schools: Vancouver School Board (SD39) catchments are address-based, with no middle-school tier — elementary is K–7 and secondary is Grades 8–12. Schools located in the surrounding Kerrisdale local area: Maple Grove Elementary, 1924 West 45th Avenue; McKechnie Elementary, 7455 Maple Street; and Magee Secondary, 6360 Maple Street, which part of the pocket feeds, by address — the rest feeds Point Grey Secondary. The catchment result for the research file's sample address (1924 West 45th Avenue) is UNVERIFIED because the VSB locator was not run, and no home is promised a specific school. Crofton House School, 3200 West 41st Avenue, is an independent JK–12 girls' day school founded in 1898 on a 10-acre campus at Kerrisdale's western edge. School names in this file use Ministry-listed names only. Name caution: Sir Alexander Mackenzie Elementary is in Kensington-Cedar Cottage on the east side — a different place, routinely confused in search. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods and https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries and https://www.croftonhouse.ca/apply/how-to-apply

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (The Heights, the one counted strata complex):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — The Heights, at 5 lots on the listing record, sits exactly on that threshold, so the duty applies. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (and in Vancouver are separately governed by City licensing and the provincial principal-residence requirement). No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a MacKenzie Heights complex appears in Section 2; any such line found on a Form B package here is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No counted complex here carries a published 55+ bylaw in the sources checked, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership complex in MacKenzie Heights — no home in this area may be marketed as a 55+ purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the pocket's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units; parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. This multiplex regime — not any existing townhouse subdivision — is how new strata product arrives in the pocket. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan:** MacKenzie Heights has no separate adopted community plan; it falls under Kerrisdale's citywide-policy position, and citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3%:** Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a Vancouver presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The neighbourhood name itself is unstable across sources.** The City lists "MacKenzie Heights" as a smaller area inside Kerrisdale; the research file places the pocket at West 37th–49th Avenues; listing portals attach the same name to West 25th–35th Avenue blocks. Any "MacKenzie Heights townhouse" claim should be checked by civic address and Paragon sub-area, never by the label alone. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2837-west-31st-avenue
- **The pocket's only verified townhouse complex sits on its boundary street.** The Heights stands on West 37th Avenue — the one street both geographies touch — which is why it survives the geography check that excludes the King Edward Avenue product further north. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2942292-2507-w-37th-avenue-vancouver-bc-v6n-2t3
- **The two edge buildings carry two addresses each in public sources.** Sterling appears as 6400 West Boulevard (research file / developer) and 2102 West 48th Avenue (listings / directory); McKinnon as 6300 West Boulevard (research file) and 6333 West Boulevard (companion-building source). Same corner blocks — match documents by strata plan, not by the address a portal happens to use. https://cressey.com/residential/sterling/
- **Both edge buildings mix condos and townhomes.** Sterling's 37 residences and McKinnon's 40 homes each include townhome and penthouse components per developer sources, so neither headline count is a pure apartment-condo count — and neither townhome component is counted as townhouse stock in this file. https://cressey.com/residential/mckinnon/
- **The pocket has no community plan of its own.** It rides on Kerrisdale's citywide-policy position and the Vancouver Official Development Plan (in force March 31, 2026) — there is no local plan document promising future townhouse density here. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- **The secondary-school split runs through the pocket.** Maple Grove and McKechnie elementaries feed toward Magee Secondary for part of the pocket and Point Grey for the rest — the single most common school claim to verify address-by-address before it is paid for. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **Kerrisdale Community Centre is the pocket's civic anchor, and it is old infrastructure.** At 5851 West Boulevard, with an indoor pool, ice rink and fitness centre, it has been operated jointly with the Vancouver Park Board since 1943 — the amenity base for the whole slope, including The Heights at its north end. https://vancouver.ca/parks-recreation-culture/kerrisdale-community-centre.aspx
