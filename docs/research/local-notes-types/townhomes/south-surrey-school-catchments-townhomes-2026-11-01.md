## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Why the strata block appears in a file with no roster:** buyers use this page precisely while shopping strata townhouses in South Surrey and White Rock, so the diligence rules for that stock are stated here; the stock itself is counted in the sub-area files.

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: Form B within 7 days of request, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page; it states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the depreciation report attached. A Form B confirms a unit's strata facts — it does **not** confirm a school catchment; that is the locator's job, per address (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5 or more lots must obtain one every 5 years since July 1, 2024; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold.
- **CRF minimum.** At least 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line for a South Surrey/White Rock strata as void (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals). A tenant household still takes the address's catchment as the locator returns it — tenancy does not change the line.
- **Age.** Only 55+ bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse here carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Its catchment is decided by exactly the same address-based locator as a strata unit's.

**Surrey / White Rock zoning, OCP, suites, SSMUH and short-term rentals.**

- Surrey's current OCP is **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — never Bylaw No. 18020 as current (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan). Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 for Surrey lots; White Rock lots follow White Rock's own bylaws — the district is the same on both sides of the municipal line, the building rules are not.
- Secondary suites in Surrey are capped at **40%** of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites).
- SSMUH: Surrey — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. White Rock (Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506, adopted June 27, 2024): 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 over 280 m², 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop (research file §4). SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex.
- Short-term rentals in Surrey: permitted only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at **$358/year** as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year — this file carries the licence page's $358 figure with the conflict disclosed (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals).
- Surrey property taxes: due July 2; 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How do I find the catchment school for a South Surrey townhouse?**
A: Enter the exact address in the SD36 School Locator (https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36). Catchments are set by street address, not by neighbourhood or MLS sub-area name — the listing's area name cannot answer the question (research file §7).

**Q: Is there a recorded catchment result for a South Surrey address?**
A: One: 1785 148 St (Sunnyside Park) → H.T. Thrift Elementary / Semiahmoo Secondary (locator run 2026-11-01). It is evidence for that address only (§1).

**Q: Does all of Sunnyside Park feed Semiahmoo?**
A: UNVERIFIED as an area claim — the live page's own Sunnyside summary splits the area at 152 Street, which is exactly why area-level school claims fail. Run the exact address (research file §6).

**Q: Does all of White Rock go to one secondary school?**
A: The live page states it does not (White Rock Elementary → Semiahmoo; Peace Arch Elementary → Earl Marriott) — but those feeder statements are site copy, not confirmed against a district document, so treat them as to-be-verified and run the address (research file §7).

**Q: Is there an "East Newton Elementary"?**
A: No. It appears in neither the BC Ministry K-12 file (2025/2026) nor SD36's directory, no school stands at 7235 144 St, and there is NO East Newton locator result — the recorded address ranges jump 7069–7195 → 7263–7263, so no range contains 7235. A surreyschools.ca slug URL renders a generic locator shell for any name and proves nothing (research file §§1, 7). The live catchments page does not name it — no defect on this page.

**Q: Is there a published "in-catchment" price premium or median?**
A: No. SnapStats has no catchment segment — there is no in-catchment median for Semiahmoo, Elgin Park, Earl Marriott or Grandview Heights to quote; any premium claim must come from the listing's own comparables (research file §6).

**Q: My townhouse's strata says rentals are not allowed — can I rent it to a family who needs the catchment?**
A: Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 (§3). The tenant household takes the address's catchment exactly as the locator returns it.

**Q: Does buying fee-simple instead of strata change my catchment?**
A: No. Catchment is address-based and tenure-blind; what changes is the diligence — a fee-simple townhouse has NO Form B, NO strata and NO strata fees (§3).

**Q: When should I run the locator?**
A: Twice, per the page's own practice: before booking a showing and again before subjects are removed, because lines can change on enrolment review (research file §6).

**Q: French Immersion — is that my catchment school?**
A: No — French Immersion has its own registration, separate from the catchment school (research file §4).
