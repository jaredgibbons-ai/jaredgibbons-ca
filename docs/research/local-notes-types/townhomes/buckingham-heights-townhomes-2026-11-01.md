## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated because they govern any strata purchase a Buckingham Heights buyer makes in a neighbouring sub-area, and any future stratified multiplex unit inside this one; inside Buckingham Heights today there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption is the live question for any small stratified multiplex. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Buckingham Heights complex carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag — there is no complex — but the rule governs any attached purchase the buyer compares nearby. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ complex of any form is verified in this sub-area (sibling 55-plus file on disk). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **Burnaby zoning — standing correction applied:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)**, never the former 1965 No. 4742. The R1 SSMUH District carried into it allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Royal Oak Community Plan covers the plan area west of Buckingham Heights. Heritage-precinct and park-edge lots (the Sperling Avenue edge, inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct) carry extra review — eligibility is confirmed lot by lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The boardwalk at the area's doorstep is closed into 2027.** The section of boardwalk by the Deer Lake boat launch closed September 21, 2026 for a full replacement, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive (about five extra minutes of walking); a small section of the nearby picnic area is also closed during construction. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Deer Lake Park is the city's cultural centre, not just a park.** The City describes it as a peaceful natural environment serving as Burnaby's cultural centre, where trails connect the lake, Burnaby Art Gallery, Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant; the park is open dawn to dusk year-round at 6450 Deer Lake Ave, and the City's 2026 events program brings Burnaby Blooms (May 3), Symphony and Opera in the Park (July 18–19), the Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival (August 8) and the Haunted Village (October 23–25 and 30–31) to the park and museum. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-04-17/burnaby-unveils-2026-civic-special-events-program
- **Burnaby replaced its OCP and its zoning bylaw within seven months.** The Burnaby 2050 OCP was adopted December 9, 2025 (replacing the 1998 OCP and taking precedence over older community-plan maps), and Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026 — any density or land-use claim for a Buckingham lot sourced to the old OCP or the old bylaw is out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **The detached utility bill has a fixed shape here.** Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a garbage toter fee; the detached single-family water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **City Hall is at the lake, not downtown.** Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, north-west of the sub-area at Deer Lake — civic business for this slope is a short drive, and the Bob Prittie Metrotown Branch is the quadrant's public library branch. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/city-departments/burnaby-public-library
