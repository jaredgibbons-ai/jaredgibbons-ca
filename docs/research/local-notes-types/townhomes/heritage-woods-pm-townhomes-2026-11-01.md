## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Cobalt Sky, the Form B and strata plan BCS2068 are also where the conflicting published unit count (§2) gets settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry above: the "rentals allowed w/restrictions" element in Cobalt Sky's and Mountain's Edge's directory lines is VOID as a restriction — rentals are allowed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Heritage Woods strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — zero verified 55+ ownership in this sub-area and city-wide in Port Moody. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Heritage Woods' detached homes are fee-simple freehold on that basis; its townhouses are strata titled (§2), so a buyer importing fee-simple expectations into a Heritage Woods townhouse purchase is misreading the tenure.
- **City plan:** Port Moody's current Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026, repealing Bylaw No. 2955** — 2955 is never the current plan. Chapter 14, Section 14.3 covers Heritage Woods with the Heritage Mountain grouping (Heritage Mountain, Twin Creeks, Noons Creek, Mountain Meadows), with policies to retain the existing residential and forested character of the grouping. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md; condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/heritage-woods-pm-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **City zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 puts single-detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones; small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas. Hillside diligence: lots with a watercourse need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply. No floodplain or ALR land was confirmed in Heritage Woods. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/heritage-woods-pm.md)
