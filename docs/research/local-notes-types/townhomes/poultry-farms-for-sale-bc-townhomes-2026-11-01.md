## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**How the townhome rules land on a farm topic — stated directly:** there is no townhouse stock here (§2), so the strata block below applies only if a buyer cross-shops a strata townhome in a nearby area file; the rules that actually govern this topic's product are the ALR, farm-classification and tax frames that follow.

**The strata block (for any strata townhome a farm buyer cross-shops in an area file).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). No Form B exists for a farm purchase — a farmhouse on ALR land cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a strata property is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as void wherever met; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. A poultry farm parcel is likewise non-strata freehold farm land in the usual case, but its governing frame is the ALR regime below, not townhome rules (research file §4).

**The farm frames (the rules that actually govern this topic).**

- **ALR housing frame (province-wide, ALC):** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²** (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite is permitted **only when located within the principal residence**, and at most one additional residence is permitted of up to **90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**; local government bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences but may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **No stratification in the ALR:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — the legal reason a poultry parcel's homes never become strata townhouses.
- **BC Assessment farm classification (province-wide, three-tier form stated in full):** farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are **$2,500 if the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**. Applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, late applications are not accepted, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it is met — the threshold is tiered by operation size (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Quota and supply management: UNVERIFIED in every particular** — the boards' and commission's mandates as the live page describes them, whether quota runs with the land, the transfer application/approval process in any sector, any Quota Exchange or going-concern mechanism, any primary vs. secondary quota distinction, any premises or barn-certification requirement, and any quota price. No marketing-board, commission or Province supply-management source was opened in this file's research; the page's account is site copy only (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html).
- **Barn, environmental and disease-control rules: UNVERIFIED** — barn construction, ventilation, manure-storage, mortality, groundwater-licensing and biosecurity requirements, and any avian-influenza / disease-control effect on a sale, were not confirmed on an official source opened in the research (research file §4).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **PTT (province-wide general tiers, as the research states them):** 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax). **How PTT and GST are apportioned between land, buildings, residence and quota on a specific poultry purchase is UNVERIFIED** — no Province farm-transfer source was opened; model it with an agricultural lawyer and accountant before an offer (research file §4).
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only — residence side only on a farm purchase).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the condos sibling for this slug carries March 13 — no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **PTT exemptions (residence frame):** first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%, Vancouver only) does not apply to farm parcels in Burnaby or Abbotsford.

**Q: What residence can a BC poultry parcel in the ALR have?**
A: Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, with local government rules possibly more restrictive (§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Can the farmhouse on a poultry parcel be stratified or sold as a townhouse later?**
A: No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). The residence stays part of the farm parcel; that is why this topic file counts zero townhouse stock (§2, §3).

**Q: What income must a poultry operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: BC Assessment's tiered thresholds: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" figure is an error (§3; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

**Q: Are there any townhouses on this page at all?**
A: None — counted townhouse stock in this topic file is 0 (0 strata, 0 fee-simple). The four feed cards are farm land, barns and farmhouses; verified townhouse rosters live in the Abbotsford, Langley and Chilliwack area files (§2).

**Q: What drives the price of a poultry farm, if there is no published figure?**
A: The page itself states there is no single price figure — price is driven by quota holdings, barn capacity and condition, land and location, and many sales are off-market. That is the page's own framing, recorded as site copy, not a statistic; a valuation stands on the specific land, barns, residence and any quota included, checked part by part (research file §§2, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html).

- **A BC-wide title sits over a four-card feed.** At reading, "Poultry Farms for Sale BC" surfaced exactly four listings — in Big Bend (Burnaby), Bradner, Sumas Prairie and Abbotsford West — and only one was an operating poultry farm. - **The one operating farm excludes the thing that makes it operate.** Its card states quota is not included — land, barns and a licence to farm are not the same asset as the right to produce, and the quota position lives in the contract and with the board, not in the listing (research file §5).
- **The farm-class deadline survives the sale.** A buyer who plans to keep farming must file the General Application for Farm Classification with BC Assessment by October 31 after purchase; late applications are not accepted, and the operation must keep meeting its tiered income threshold (§3; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **The ALR suite rule is stricter than the city suite rule.** In the ALR a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence — there is no ALR equivalent of a detached coach house as of right under the frame stated in §3 (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Two of the four cards are potential, not poultry.** One is a Burnaby lot listing livestock/poultry among possible uses; the other is a Bradner acreage offering potential for a dairy/poultry operation. Neither is a poultry farm today, whatever the page title says (research file §1).
- **No board source stands behind the page's quota summary.** The three-board account and the "quota does not run with the land" line are the live page's own copy; this file's research opened no marketing-board source, so both stay UNVERIFIED site copy until checked with the applicable board (research file §1).
