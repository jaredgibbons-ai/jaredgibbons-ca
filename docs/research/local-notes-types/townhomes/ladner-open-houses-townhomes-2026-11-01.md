## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim as set out in the source:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download — Note: this file's own research file could not re-open that source document after a fetch-service 429 rate-limit on 2026-11-01 and therefore marked the exception UNVERIFIED in its own pages; the verbatim text above is carried from the sibling tsawwassen-open-houses townhomes file, which did open it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/ladner-open-houses.md)
- **Representation at an open house:** in British Columbia, licensed real estate professionals must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction; disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes, after the open):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line a host repeats at a townhouse open is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases). NONE of Delta's five verified 55+ complexes is townhouse-form, so no 55+ townhouse open in Ladner is verified stock in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only, stated plainly. Ladner's fee-simple exemplar is 48 Landing (Row House Non-Strata, counted in the ladner-elementary-guide townhomes file); if the townhouse on the feed is fee-simple, there are no strata documents to ask for at the open.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Ladner townhouse open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation while the host is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but once you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Is the host the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side; a BC licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and cannot simply also represent a walk-in buyer — dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Which time do I show up — the card block says 2–4, the line under it says 1–3?** Neither is verified as authoritative: this file's research documents exactly that one-hour split on every labelled Ladner card read, with the listing text matching the top block on the two cards whose text states a time. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before you drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/ladner-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Does every listing on this page hold an open house?** No, on the evidence read: five of six fully read cards carried an open-house time block and one Ladner Rural farm card carried no label or time at all — treat an unlabelled card as a listing to confirm privately, not as an open house. - **Q: The townhouse looks strata — what do I ask for after the open?** The Form B package: strata fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas of 5+ lots since July 1, 2024). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: What if the townhouse is fee-simple — is there still a Form B?** No. A fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents. Ask the host which tenure the unit is before asking for strata documents that may not exist.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Ladner townhouse I buy at an open?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a directory or host says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
