## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At band prices the Form B is also where a "luxury" claim is tested — fees, levies and depreciation timing, not finishes, decide carrying cost. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every Willoughby complex cross-referenced in §2 is over the five-lot threshold on its verified scale; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the cross-referenced complexes is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where the cross-referenced record carries the line: Willow Park Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" and the three Langley City apartment buildings' identical lines (condos sibling) — any long-term component is void. Cascadia's listing line "rentals allowed with restrictions" is read the same way. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A townhouse buyer who trusts the copy and never reaches the urban shelf misreads the page entirely. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **The rural end of this band trades on almost no monthly evidence.** Campbell Valley recorded 16 detached listings and no sale in September 2026; Otter District carried 2 listings; County Line Glen Valley carried 0 listings with 1 sale. Pricing at the acreage end needs a longer look-back and wider geography than any community median. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/langley-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **Langley City's entire verified apartment roster is three buildings — and all three are 55+.** Parkway Estates, Parkway II and Glenmont Park. The fourth name sometimes carried in that count, Huntsfield Green, is townhouse-form — a classification error that changes what a buyer is actually purchasing. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-homes-over-1-8m-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Willoughby's one fee-simple townhouse cluster has no strata at all — by the builder's own words.** Foxridge's Westbrooke completion report states the freehold row homes have "the same ownership structure as a single-family home. There are no strata fees or strata corporations overseeing the property." A buyer comparing Westbrooke against Cascadia or Luxia on monthly fees alone is comparing two different tenures. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/willoughby-heights-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **The Willoughby plan paperwork is mid-update and easy to overstate.** The Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (Dec 1, 2025); the update bylaws 6201/6202/6203 stand at first/second reading (May 25, 2026) with a hearing directed June 15, 2026. Any document presenting the update as adopted — or the plan as "replaced" — overstates the record. (Standing program position, Nov 1, 2026.)
- **Every rebate a first-time buyer knows phases out below this band's floor.** PTT first-time exemption (gone by $860,000), newly built exemption (gone by $1,150,000), FTHB GST rebate (gone by $1,500,000) — a $1.8M+ purchase is planned with none of them, which is why the §2 worked examples run with no exemption.
