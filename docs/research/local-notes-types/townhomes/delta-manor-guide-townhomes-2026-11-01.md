## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Both verified Delta Manor townhouse complexes are strata, so the strata block governs them; the fee-simple paragraph states the contrast a buyer comparing a non-strata Ladner house needs.

- **Form B first.** Under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, the Form B Information Certificate discloses the unit's monthly strata fees, amounts the owner owes, approved-but-unpaid special levies, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking and storage allocations, and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, and the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report must be attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and can no longer defer it by a vote; reports carry a 30-year projection of repair and replacement costs and three CRF cash-flow models. Adlington Court (63 lots) is squarely inside this rule. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund when approving the budget at the AGM. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. Any directory line saying a Delta Manor complex has "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" is void in-entry for long-term rentals and must not be republished as a current rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: only 55+ survives.** Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any age bylaw below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and exemptions (Strata Property Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) cover legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. This is the rule that makes Laburnum Park Place's 55+ bylaw (bare-land strata, Section 2) lawful; neither Adlington Court nor Rosewood Court is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse or row house has NO strata corporation, NO Form B, and NO strata fees — the buyer deals with municipal rules and any covenants or homeowners-association terms on title only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Delta Manor, so any Ladner listing marketed as "freehold, no strata" needs its title checked to prove the non-strata claim. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, with zoning assigned parcel by parcel on the bylaw's zoning maps; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates for the zoning bylaw. The number is contested between those sources and neither is asserted as settled here — a buyer reads the parcel's zone (for example the RS5 sheet) off the City's maps, not the bylaw number off a listing. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it contains NO lettered schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land in Delta is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered Ladner schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. Inside a strata townhouse complex, the strata bylaws sit on top of that permission. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **GST on new construction only.** Resale townhomes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) for homes valued at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and phases out by $1,150,000. https://danmarusin.com/blog/bc-property-transfer-tax-2026/
- **Down-payment programs and mortgage insurance.** The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs for a first home, and the First Home Savings Account allows $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit; the two can be combined. CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule runs 0.6%–4.5% of the loan across its loan-to-value bands. https://www.robv.ca/blog/first-time-home-buyer-bc and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** No presale townhouse project inside Delta Manor is verified in this pass, so REDMA is background only here: where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and s. 21 gives the buyer a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging that statement, with deposits held in trust. https://meridianlawgroup.ca/pre-sale-properties-and-a-developers-requirements-to-disclose-building-permits-financing-commitments-and-buyers-rights-to-terminate-agreements/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many townhouse complexes are there in Delta Manor, really?** Two verified, both strata townhouses on 54A Street: Adlington Court (63 units, LMS2857) and Rosewood Court (built 1996). Westham Lane on 52A Street is an apartment building (LMS2591, 58 units), and Laburnum Park Place is a bare-land strata of detached ranchers — neither is a townhouse complex. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What do strata fees cover in a townhouse strata here, versus the Sunningdale apartments?** It is set complex by complex in the budget attached to the Form B. Rosewood Court's listing data records maintenance grounds, management and snow removal; a townhouse owner typically carries more of the home's own exterior and insurance than an apartment owner does, and the Form B and bylaws state the exact split for the specific complex. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3068600-4748-54a-street-11-delta-bc-v4k-3p1/brochure and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rent out my townhouse?** For long-term rentals, yes in law: every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, whatever an older directory entry says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, so read the registered bylaws before counting on nightly-rental income. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed at Adlington Court or Rosewood Court?** UNCONFIRMED for both complexes — no public source checked states a current pet bylaw for either. Pet bylaws are strata-specific and can limit number, size and type; the registered bylaws attached to the Form B are the deciding document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is either townhouse complex 55+?** Neither is verified as 55+. Delta's verified Ladner 55+ ownership is Delta Green (apartment form, Ladner Elementary sub-area), South Pointe (bare-land strata, Neilsen Grove) and Laburnum Park Place (bare-land strata, carried in Section 2) — townhouse-form 55+ stock in Ladner: none verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: How healthy is the strata financially — could a levy be coming?** Read the CRF balance and the depreciation report attached to the Form B. Since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund each year, and a strata with five or more lots must refresh its depreciation report every five years; its three CRF funding models show whether fees are keeping up with the 30-year repair list. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Is this area behind dikes, and does that affect a townhouse purchase?** Ladner is low land protected by the City of Delta's flood protection system — over 63 kilometres of dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection, 31 drainage pump stations and roughly 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. Separately, any property within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area, which can limit building work. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend?** Catchments are address-based. For the sample address 4600 53 Street the SD37 locator returns Ladner Elementary (K–7) and Delta Secondary (8–12); run the exact unit address in the locator before relying on it. Ministry-listed school names only are used here. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: The complexes are 1990s wood frame — what should I check?** Ask for the depreciation report's envelope and roof items, any engineering reports in the strata documents, and the minutes for past or planned envelope work — the Province's strata buying guidance is to review bylaws, rules, fees and special levies before removing subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Q: Is there a fee-simple townhouse option in central Ladner with no strata at all?** Not verified inside Delta Manor. The verified fee-simple row-house product in central Ladner sits in the neighbouring Ladner Elementary sub-area (48 Landing, 48B Street — see that file in this series); inside Delta Manor, a "no strata" claim on any listing must be proved on title. (Section 2 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Highway 99 crossing is being rebuilt — on a new budget and timeline.** On July 6, 2026, the Province announced an updated total budget of $8.5 billion for the Fraser River Tunnel Project, the eight-lane immersed-tube tunnel replacing the George Massey Tunnel, with the federal government contributing up to $3 billion, major construction expected to begin in 2027, and early construction work already continuing. That crossing is the commute every Delta Manor townhouse buyer is pricing. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026TT0066-000796
- **Ladner Village is in an active revitalization program.** The City's Ladner Village Revitalization Action Plan, including the Chisholm Street Wharf project, is the named project set for the village core a short walk west of the 54A Street townhouse pocket. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/news-release-ladner-village-revitalization-projects
- **The 2024 OCP quietly removed the old area-plan structure.** Buyers reading older City documents will see references to a Ladner Area Plan as a schedule of the former OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), has no such schedule — future change in central Ladner is governed by the single Land Use Plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH changed the streets around the townhouses in 2024.** Delta's June 24, 2024 SSMUH adoption (Bylaws 8394 and 8395) lets many detached lots around Delta Manor add suites, duplex units, coach houses and garden suites without a rezoning — which is why infill keeps appearing on the blocks between the townhouse pocket and the village. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Delta's 2026 tax calendar penalizes late payment twice.** The City's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 — relevant to completion-date adjustments on any townhouse closing that straddles those dates. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
