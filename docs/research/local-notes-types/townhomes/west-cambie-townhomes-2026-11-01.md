Within the area, the Alexandra neighbourhood is bounded by Cambie Road, Garden City Road, Alderbridge Way and No. 4 Road, and the DND lands are bounded by No. 4 Road, Alderbridge Way, Shell Road and Westminster Highway. Nearby areas: Richmond City Centre to the west, East Cambie to the east, Bridgeport to the north, and the Garden City Lands beside the area. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Alexandra, the Form B package is also where the district-energy charges (below) surface — read the utility lines, not just the strata fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are well over the threshold (Jayden Mews alone is approximately 64 homes). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **The Alexandra exception a buyer must not miss:** Omega's Low End Market Rental units are restricted by a registered covenant, not a bylaw — covenants were not voided, and those units remain rental-restricted today. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a counted West Cambie townhouse complex was found in this pass, so there is no in-entry VOID flag to make in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No counted West Cambie townhouse complex carries a published 55+ bylaw — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ strata ownership stock in West Cambie; Covenant Court's 55+ rule belongs to a Woodwards apartment building counted in no total (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in West Cambie; the fee-simple stock is the detached house in The Oaks, Odlinwood and older Alexandra, where diligence runs on title, servicing (septic history, below) and flood construction level instead of strata documents.
- **City zoning and plan:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Area Plan and City applications show Single Detached (RS1/F) in the older Alexandra pattern and site-specific Town Housing zones such as ZT67 and ZT71 on Alexandra Road — Jayden Mews' own zone is ZT71. The West Cambie Area Plan is Schedule 2.11A to the Official Community Plan, under the city-wide 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm
- **Suites / SSMUH:** the City states Richmond allows at least one secondary suite and/or an accessory dwelling unit per lot on covered lots, with provincial small-scale multi-unit housing minimums of three units on lots 280 m² or less and four units on lots greater than 280 m². https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Alexandra District Energy Utility (ADEU) — a West Cambie carrying cost:** apartment and townhouse buildings in the Alexandra pocket connect to the City-owned district energy utility, and its charges form part of a strata home's carrying costs on top of the strata fee. The City's ADEU building list covers buildings on Alexandra Road, Odlin Road and Tomicki Avenue — pull the utility charges from the strata documents for any Alexandra Road purchase rather than assuming standard utilities. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Flood:** all lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204, which sets flood construction levels (FCLs) that habitable floors must meet unless an exemption applies. Richmond sits at an average of 1 metre above sea level behind 49 km of dikes designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dike raising from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way. Confirm the FCL position before planning any rebuild or major renovation on an older Alexandra lot. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Servicing and noise — older Alexandra:** the Area Plan records the older Alexandra lot pattern remaining on septic sewage disposal — confirm sewer connection status and cost before subject removal on any older Alexandra property — and states the area is affected by aircraft noise from Vancouver International Airport, with no new single-family development permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **City taxes and utilities:** City of Richmond 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid 2026 taxes after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% penalty on any outstanding balance after Tuesday, September 1, 2026. Annual flat-rate utility payments were due March 31, 2026, with a 10% discount only for payments received on or before the due date; the City's discounted cost for a typical single-family metered residence for 2026 totals $2,251 per year (water $631, sewer $835, garbage/recycling/environmental programs $443, flood protection $342) — strata townhouse utility treatment runs through the strata and the ADEU instead: read the Form B. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/finance/utility-fees/utilityrates.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the counted Alexandra stock is 2015–2018 resale product. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No West Cambie townhouse presale is carried in this file; the frame governs Camden Square–type product in the wider area and any future Alexandra townhouse release. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are West Cambie's townhouses?** All of the verified stock is on one street: Jayden Mews (9700 / 9740 Alexandra Road, approximately 64 homes, built 2015), Suncrest (9560 Alexandra Road, 2018) and Museo (9680 Alexandra Road) in the Alexandra pocket. The Oaks and Odlinwood are detached-house neighbourhoods; there is no townhouse stock there. https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=39261
- **Q: What extra monthly cost comes with an Alexandra Road townhouse?** District energy: buildings in the pocket connect to the Alexandra District Energy Utility, and its charges sit on top of the strata fee as part of carrying costs. The City's ADEU building list covers the Alexandra Road buildings — pull the actual charges from the Form B package instead of budgeting from the strata fee alone. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_-_2021_Annual_General_Meeting_Resolution_of_the_Lulu_Island_Energy_Company_Sp58691.pdf
- **Q: Is Museo 96 units, and was it built in 2016 or 2017?** Both figures are UNCONFIRMED: the 96-unit figure appears only in secondary directories and was not confirmed on a citable source, and listings at 9680 Alexandra Road record both 2016 and 2017 as the build year. The strata plan and Form B settle both — do not quote either number as fact. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3094851-9680-alexandra-road-59-richmond-bc-v6x-0p2/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out my Alexandra townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law if it is a market strata lot: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. The trap is next door at Omega, where Low End Market Rental units are restricted by a registered covenant that was not voided — if a listing's price looks low for the pocket, check whether the unit is an LEMR covenant unit before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Covenant Court a West Cambie option?** No — 9260 No. 2 Road is in Woodwards, and it is an apartment building with a 55+ rule, not a townhouse complex. It is counted in no total in this program; it surfaces in searches only because both are Richmond strata. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2898499-1089260-no-2-road-richmond-bc-v7e-2c8
- **Q: What flood rule applies to a West Cambie townhouse or lot?** All of Richmond is designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204, and habitable floors must meet the flood construction level unless exempt. For a strata townhouse that is mostly an insurance and disclosure question; for an older Alexandra lot it prices any rebuild. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve an Alexandra Road townhouse?** Address-based in SD38 — the locator result for 9671 Odlin Road is Tomsett Elementary and MacNeill Secondary, and Tomsett sits physically inside the area. That confirms only that address; run the exact townhouse address in the locator. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: What should I check on an older Alexandra house lot that I would not check elsewhere in Richmond?** Three things from the Area Plan itself: septic history (the older pattern remained on septic disposal), the aircraft-noise policy (no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy), and the 0.4 ha lot pattern that makes these lots redevelopment-shaped but policy-constrained. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Q: When are Richmond's money dates?** Property taxes July 2, 2026 (5% penalty after, plus a further 5% after September 1, 2026), and flat-rate utilities March 31, 2026 with the 10% early-payment discount lost if paid late — two separate calendars, both easy to miss in a completion week. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Q: How does the area connect to the Canada Line?** By bus: route 410 runs on Cambie Road between Richmond-Brighouse Station and 22nd Street Station — there is no station inside West Cambie, and the Area Plan's walkability claim is about the City Centre edge, not the Alexandra pocket. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt410.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Alexandra's big lots come with a septic past.** The Area Plan describes the older Alexandra pattern as single-family homes on 0.4 ha lots remaining on septic sewage disposal, with wells recorded in the provincial GWELLS registry where they exist — a servicing file no other Richmond townhouse pocket asks a buyer to read. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **Aircraft noise is a development rule here, not just a nuisance.** The Area Plan states West Cambie is affected by Vancouver International Airport noise and that no new single-family development is permitted in Alexandra under the OCP Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development Policy — which is part of why the Alexandra rebuild product is townhouse and apartment form under site-specific zones like ZT71. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
- **The greenway runs through the townhouse pocket.** Alexandra Park is at 9600 Odlin Road, linked by sections of the Alexandra Greenway between the Garden City Lands and Cambie Road — the park-and-greenway spine sits between the Alexandra Road complexes, a fact listing copy for Jayden Mews, Suncrest and Museo rarely maps. https://Richmond.Ca/city-hall/news/city2022/alexandra08jun2022.htm
- **The dikes are being raised around this area right now.** Richmond's flood system is designed for a 1:500 event, with dike raising from the current 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres under way to stay ahead of sea level rise — the FCL conversation in West Cambie sits on top of a live capital program, not a static bylaw. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **The area's library and hospital are outside it.** Richmond Public Library's Brighouse (Main) Branch is at 100-7700 Minoru Gate in the City Centre (the Cambie Branch, 150-11590 Cambie Road, is east of the area), and the nearest hospital is Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway — West Cambie is a residential pocket served from both sides, with no community centre of its own confirmed in the research file. https://rpl.yourlibrary.ca/about/hours-locations and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- **A DND lands block sits inside the planning area.** The Area Plan bounds a DND lands parcel by No. 4 Road, Alderbridge Way, Shell Road and Westminster Highway — federal land inside the West Cambie boundary that does not turn over like the surrounding residential lots. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/westcambie570.pdf
