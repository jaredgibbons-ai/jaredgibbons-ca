- What the topic means for townhome stock: the plans decide where townhouse stock can exist and what can replace it. The 2024 OCP's three major changes, per the City, are (1) identifying strategic growth in specific areas, including increasing density in urban corridors, (2) simplifying land use descriptions to streamline the development process, and (3) providing a framework to enable Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH). https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- The housing range the plans govern: single-detached and duplex housing under the RS/RD zones, ground-oriented SSMUH (suites, duplexes, coach/garden-suite and 3–4 unit infill forms the City describes) on single-detached and duplex lots, multi-unit housing — including townhouse-form housing — in the urban centres and corridors the OCP targets for strategic growth, and farm housing on Agriculture (A)-designated land. The City frames SSMUH as ground-oriented housing between single-detached homes and condominiums, intended to be attainable for middle-income families. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Strata vs freehold under the plans: both tenures exist across Delta; no Delta-wide tenure split is stated in any source read: UNVERIFIED. The plan does not decide tenure — a townhouse project can be strata or fee-simple under the same designation, and the STRATA versus FEE-SIMPLE question for a specific complex is answered on title and the Form B, not from the OCP. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/deltalandplans.md)
- Tenure traps are a title question, not a plan question: leasehold (including TFN, City ground leases and UniverCity-type tenure), corporation-share, undivided-interest, co-op, life-lease, non-market rental and pad-rental product is not townhouse stock wherever it appears in Delta, and no plan designation in this file converts it into townhouse stock. Pad-rental parks are NEVER townhouse stock in this program.
- Presales: no presale townhouse project is counted in this file; presales anywhere in this program are counted separately from completed stock, never mixed, and a plan designation that would allow a future townhouse project is not a presale.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Delta OCP — the governing document:** Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan, NO lettered schedules. There is NO Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen or Ladner Area Plan in Bylaw No. 8400; those area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, which Bylaw No. 8400 superseded — citing "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an error wherever it is met. The OCP's structure is the municipal-wide plan (s. 1.5 Land Use Plan, s. 2.0 Policies, Appendix B Development Permit Areas and Guidelines, Appendix D Maps); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1). https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED (see the flag at the top of this file):** the OCP's designations are implemented through the Delta Zoning Bylaw cited on City pages in the research file as No. 7600, which provides the parcel-level rules; zoning is assigned parcel by parcel on the zoning maps that form part of that bylaw, hosted on the City's mapping (maps.delta.ca). Hampton Cove townhouses, for example, were built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning — each complex's rules are read off its own CD zone and the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Residential zone ladder (zoning bylaw, minimum lot area for subdivision):** RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Agriculture zoning:** in the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH in Delta:** Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — the City has introduced the provincially required zoning updates that allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies. Servicing, flood and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel — SSMUH is not automatic, and it does not change the bylaws inside an existing townhouse strata. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Development Permit Areas:** the OCP's Appendix B carries the Development Permit Areas and Guidelines, including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA guidelines and the hazard DPAs SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **ALR layer on top of the plan:** where land is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, Agricultural Land Commission rules apply on top of the OCP designation and zoning — generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel, a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area, and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — farm land cannot produce strata townhouses while it remains in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Housing context the plan answers to:** Delta was one of 10 cities issued a provincial Housing Target Order mandating 3,607 net new housing units within five years, and the City's Housing Needs Report (2024) identified a 20-year housing need of 29,457 units. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Form B (strata townhomes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA govern instead, stated plainly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 — any Delta directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a townhouse complex reflects old bylaw text, not current law, and must be flagged in-entry; short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ is lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). NONE of Delta's five verified 55+ complexes is townhouse-form, so no 55+ townhouse rule in this section creates 55+ townhouse stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST rebates:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, with the tier flag:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2%, and worked examples must show the tier math. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a Delta townhouse project is sold on developer contracts, the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Delta's current Official Community Plan?** The Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted by Delta Council on July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan whose Land Use Plan (s. 1.5, Map 2) sets the designations for the whole city. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan or a Ladner Area Plan in Delta's OCP?** No. Those were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1). Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) superseded them with one consolidated plan — there is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, and any document citing one is citing a superseded plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: What is Delta's zoning bylaw number?** CONTESTED — do not rely on a single citation. City pages in the research file cite No. 7600; standing corrections carry a competing "8400, 2025" citation. This file follows the research file's City citation and flags the contest; confirm the current consolidated bylaw with the City for the specific parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: How do I find what can be built on a Delta property — including whether townhouses are allowed?** In order: the property's OCP designation in the Land Use Plan (s. 1.5.1 designations, applied on Map 2), then its zone on the zoning maps, then whether a Development Permit Area in Appendix B applies — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What did SSMUH change on Delta's single-family lots?** Delta introduced the provincially required zoning updates (Bylaws 8394/8395, June 24, 2024) allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, under the framework the 2024 OCP provides — but servicing, flood and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel, so it is not automatic. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Does an OCP designation that allows townhouse density mean my townhouse strata's rules change?** No — the plan governs land use, not strata bylaws or tenure. Inside an existing complex, the Form B, the registered bylaws, the depreciation report and the CRF position govern; and long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022 whatever the plan or a directory says. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What does the Agriculture (A) designation mean for a townhouse buyer?** In the OCP's s. 1.5.1 designations, Agriculture (A) is the designation applied on Map 2 to maintain agricultural lands. Land under it is farm land, not townhouse land — and where it is also in the ALR, ALC rules apply on top, including the bar on stratifying ALR residences (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: How small can a new Delta residential lot be?** It depends entirely on the zone: subdivision minimums run from RS7 at 335 m² up to the 4,000 m² low-density zones (RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2, RD3), and a new farm lot in A1 needs a minimum of 8 ha. The zone lookup comes before any lot-value opinion. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Why are there no Delta townhouse market numbers in this file?** Because none exist at Delta-wide level in the sources held: SnapStats covers North Delta only, South Delta is not covered, presenting a North Delta figure as a Delta figure would be wrong, and SnapStats never separates townhomes from condos in any case. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/deltalandplans.md)
- **Q: The seller's documents cite the old area plan — what do I do?** Treat it as a dating problem, not a fact: if a seller, a listing or an old document cites the Tsawwassen Area Plan, Schedule D.1, or any Bylaw No. 3950 schedule, it is citing a superseded plan — the governing document is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Delta was first in the region to finish its OCP update (July 8, 2024).** Delta Council adopted the new Official Community Plan on that date, making Delta the first municipality in the region to complete a review and update of its OCP — which is why Delta's plan documents are newer than most neighbours' a townhouse buyer might compare them with. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **The old area plans did not move — they ended.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan (Schedule D.1) and Ladner area plan material belonged to Bylaw No. 3950; Bylaw No. 8400 is a single consolidated plan with no lettered schedules at all, so a "Schedule D.1" citation in any current document is a tell that the document predates the 2024 plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **There is no RD5 zone.** The residential ladder in the zoning bylaw runs RS1–RS7 and RD1–RD4 with no RD5 — a listing or appraisal citing "RD5" in Delta is citing a zone that does not exist in the bylaw as read. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Hampton Cove's townhouses do not sit under a standard townhouse zone.** The Neilsen Grove townhouse complexes were built under site-specific comprehensive development zoning, so two Delta townhouse complexes can sit under entirely different zoning rules — read the complex's own CD zone, not a generic zone summary.
