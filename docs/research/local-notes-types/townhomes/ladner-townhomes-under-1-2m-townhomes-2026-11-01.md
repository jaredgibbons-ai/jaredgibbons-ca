## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata complex cross-referenced in Section 2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level fee, pet and rental lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating budget must go to the CRF. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID / age bylaws 55+ only:** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry; only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** 48 Landing — the band area's one verified fee-simple townhouse-form product, counted in the Ladner Elementary home file — has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. Every other verified Ladner townhouse complex cross-referenced here is strata. (Section 2 above)
- **City frame:** Delta OCP = consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950 — never cite it under 8400). Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel; Delta's ZONING bylaw number is CONTESTED (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted. Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 + 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — context for the detached stock a townhome buyer compares against, not a townhome rule. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood frame for the whole band:** the City operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a strata-document and title check on every complex in this band. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT in this band:** general PTT 1% to $200,000 and 2% to $2,000,000 — every price in this band is taxed entirely in those two tiers; the 3% tier and the further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 do not arise, and a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2%, flagged as such. First-time buyers: full exemption at $835,000 or less (first $500,000), partial to $860,000 — only the band's lower slice can use it at all. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Rebates and programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; new construction only); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST does not apply to resales. Delta taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% after, further 5% after September 2, 2026). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a developer sale carries the disclosure-statement duty (s. 18, deposits in trust) and a 7-day rescission right (s. 21). A presale marketed under $1.2M is not resale band stock and is never counted here as such. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
