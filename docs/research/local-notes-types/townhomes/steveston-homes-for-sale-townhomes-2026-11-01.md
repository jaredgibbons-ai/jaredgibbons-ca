## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the three counted complexes and every strata documented in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Where a complex's restrictions are UNCONFIRMED in §2, the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Currents' 18 homes put it over the threshold on the count home's record; London Station's and Princess Lane's lot counts are UNCONFIRMED, so their threshold position is UNCONFIRMED — read the count off the Form B and strata plan. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry: the Princess Lane directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" carries a rental-restriction element that is void for long-term rentals (§2.1). Richmond has no Omega-style LEMR covenant issue on this ground — the LEMR covenant point in this program belongs to another Richmond complex and is not imported here.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Steveston townhouse complex is verified 55+; the district's seniors stock is the rental, care and co-op product excluded in §2.6.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.4). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.
- **Heritage overlay.** A townhouse inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area — the §2.2 Village building sits between Chatham and Moncton Streets in the historic core — needs a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes under the Steveston Area Plan; confirm the permit position before pricing in exterior work (research file §4: https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML).

**Richmond zoning, OCP, suites/SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500.** Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1); village commercial and mixed-use zones named by the City include Steveston Commercial (CS3) and Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU32) – Steveston Village; Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL were added under provincial Bill 44 legislation (research file §4: https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf). **OCP: Richmond 2041 OCP framework, Bylaw 9000**, with the Steveston Area Plan as Schedule 2.4 (research file §4: https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf).
- **Suites.** A secondary suite in a single-detached house must be at least 33.0 m² and no more than the lesser of 40% of the dwelling's floor area or 110 m² under Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500, and the City also allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot under its Bill 44 housing rules (research file §§4, 6: https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm).
- **Flood.** Confirm dike setbacks and the flood construction level under Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 before pricing in a rebuild or addition on a dyke-adjacent lot (research file §6: https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf).
- **Richmond property taxes.** Due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026 (research file §4: https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Steveston is in Richmond. No Alexandra District Energy Utility (ADEU) charge applies on this ground: ADEU charges are a West Cambie/Alexandra carrying cost only in this program's Richmond work.

**Q: Does the floodplain change what I can do with a Steveston townhouse lot?**
