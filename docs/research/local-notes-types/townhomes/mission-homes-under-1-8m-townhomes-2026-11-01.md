## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes cross-referenced above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (all cross-referenced complexes are 5+ lots on their published counts except Archer Green, whose count is UNCONFIRMED). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Cherry Lane's directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex — the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in Mission's assembled roster. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.
- **City plan — OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026:** the current Official Community Plan replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added; it carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. **Any source stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption date is WRONG** — this band file's own research does not document that wrong date on this page, so it is flagged as the program-wide standing Jerry item, not asserted as a defect of this page. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **City zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted; secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **City property taxes — FLAT 10% penalty (City of Mission ground only):** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **10% penalty added to any current balance outstanding July 3, 2026** — the whole penalty, flat (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235). Any "5% + 5%" staging for CITY taxes is the wrong regime: 5% after Jul 2 + 5% after Oct 31 is the separate RURAL regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11) for FVRD electoral areas (e.g. Hatzic Prairie / Area F, Hatzic Island and Dewdney–Deroche / Area G), never inside the city. The two regimes are never mixed in this file. Taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Flood and ALR:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge and Hatzic Prairie is protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — confirm floodplain status and flood construction requirements per parcel. ALR land sits mainly on the city's rural edges, status parcel-specific on ALC mapping; an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), ALC caps bind (principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence ≤90 m² at ≤40 ha / ≤186 m² above 40 ha), and farm-classification thresholds run in the full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR). https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **PTT — the tiers, and worked examples with inputs stated and tier math shown.** General PTT: 1% on the first $200,000; 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000; 3% on the portion above $2,000,000; PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. **Flag:** a page table row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — it is not a flat 5% tier, and the extra 2% applies only to the residential portion above $3M.
  - *Worked example A — input: purchase price $430,000 (the September 2026 Condos & Townhomes COMBINED median, used as an arithmetic input only, not as a townhouse price):* PTT = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $230,000 ($4,600) = **$6,600**. First-time buyer at this price (≤ $835,000): the exemption covers the first $500,000, so PTT payable = **$0**.
  - *Worked example B — input: purchase price $814,000 (the September 2026 detached median, same arithmetic-input basis):* PTT = $2,000 + 2% × $614,000 ($12,280) = **$14,280**. First-time buyer: tax applies only above the exempt first $500,000 → 2% × $314,000 = **$6,280** payable (the exemption's maximum saving is $8,000).
  - *Worked example C — input: purchase price $1,800,000 (this page's own ceiling):* PTT = $2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. No first-time buyer exemption (eliminated above $860,000) and no newly built home exemption (eliminated at $1,150,000) at this price.
  - Exemptions as published: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST rebates:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031 (the frame that matters for a new Briskham Hill purchase; 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales). New Housing Rebate: 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC defect is asserted for any Mission complex — this file's research documents none. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** Montgomery Condos is sold as a presale — the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). A presale unit is never resale band stock. https://montgomerycondos.ca/ and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** this band file's research documents no defect in the page's own copy, no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance — none found in-file; the correct email is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. The known program pattern of a compressed "Over $3M — 5%" PTT row is NOT documented in this file's research and is therefore NOT asserted as a defect of this page — the correct tier structure is simply stated above. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver only and does not apply in Mission. Source: the research file, read in full 2026-11-01.

Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The $1.8M ceiling is not the constraint in Mission (September 2026):** with a detached median of $814,000 and a combined attached median of $430,000, "under $1.8M" describes most of the mainstream market — the homes the ceiling actually excludes are the top tail (large rural acreages and the highest-end detached homes), whose count cannot be stated from SnapStats. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **The attached market is a one-sub-area market:** the urban "Mission" sub-area held 104 of the city's 122 active Condos & Townhomes listings and all 10 attached sales in September 2026; Mission West held 8 active and no sales. Townhouse searching under this band is, in practice, searching the core plus one west pocket. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Mission's tax penalty is the simplest in the region — and the most often misstated:** flat 10% after July 2 inside the city (Community Charter ss. 233–234, Reg. 426/2003 s. 3, no s. 235 bylaw) versus the staged 5% + 5% rural regime in the FVRD electoral areas outside it (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). A Mission page carrying the rural staging is carrying the wrong municipality's rule. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Two official sources give the same middle school two street names:** Heritage Park Middle School appears at 33700 Bell Ave in SD75's catchment policy and at 33700 Prentis Ave (as École Heritage Park Middle School) in the Ministry K-12 file — recorded both ways in Section 1 rather than silently resolved. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075
- **The OCP anticipates townhouses the official record does not name:** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 carries an Urban Residential Townhouse designation, yet the research pass confirmed no townhome complex name on an official source — the designation is policy, not a roster; the roster in Section 2 rests on the sibling programs' verification, with its basis stated per complex. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Mission was the lowest-priced community in its research batch (September 2026):** medians of $814,000 detached and $430,000 Condos & Townhomes COMBINED — context for why first-time buyers priced out of Langley look here, stated as the combined figure it is, never as a townhouse-only price. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
