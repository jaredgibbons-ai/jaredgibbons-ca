## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no strata townhouse stock in Bayridge to apply strata diligence to today — the strata rules below govern any strata product a buyer is told is "in Bayridge" (it will in fact sit in another sub-area) and any future strata registration on a Bayridge lot.** Form B diligence: Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The sibling 55-plus file records Ambleside buildings whose building data still says "rentals not allowed" — any such line is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason; no Bayridge directory line exists to flag, because there is no Bayridge strata stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ ownership complexes in Bayridge, and records that the North Shore guide's three West Vancouver 55+ buildings (Westview Terrace, 1390 Duchess Ave; Tudor Gardens, 843 22nd St; Lincoln Gardens, 2110 Marine Dr) are all in Ambleside, east of Dundarave, outside Bayridge. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.realestatenorthshore.com/news/on-the-market/55-plus-buildings-west-vancouver/
- **Fee-simple houses (the actual Bayridge stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Secondary suites must be registered with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots/secondary-suites
- **District zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 governs — Section 200's RS single-family zones state minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3 and RS10, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, and list secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses; the ground-oriented dwelling rules cap a lot at 4 dwellings (6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop). The Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 4985, 2018; Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, bringing the OCP into alignment with the Interim Housing Needs Report. (The Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair sometimes seen in area files belongs to White Rock ONLY — it has no application in West Vancouver.) https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **Hazard permits — the live Bayridge checks:** the District requires a Foreshore Development Permit for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding), an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface) — the foreshore area is the live check on Marine Drive waterfront lots here, and new habitable space must meet the Flood Construction Level where that risk applies. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Bayridge is in the District of West Vancouver, and the 3% EHT is Vancouver only. District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. At Bayridge price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Bayridge is confirmed; where a presale is bought elsewhere, the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and the 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21) govern, with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
