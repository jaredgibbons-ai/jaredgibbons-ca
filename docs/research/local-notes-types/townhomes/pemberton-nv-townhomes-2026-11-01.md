## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all three counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Ebb & Flow the Form B package is also where the STRATA tenure carried in §2 (verified on listing evidence, including the EPS7673 plan reference) is confirmed lot by lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. All three counted complexes are far above the threshold on their published scale (Belle Isle 88, Mason 40; Ebb & Flow's count is UNCONFIRMED in this file but the complex is plainly multi-building scale on its listings). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. These are young buildings (2021–2023) — the depreciation report still governs the reserve trajectory a buyer is purchasing into. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in §2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry — the counted complexes' rental positions are UNCONFIRMED — so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No counted Pemberton NV complex carries any age restriction in any source checked — all three are general-market. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Pemberton NV (§2). A fee-simple townhouse, where one exists elsewhere, carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Pemberton Heights' fee-simple stock is detached houses under District rules, including the suite/coach-house permissions below; do not import strata expectations into those deals, or house expectations into a Curling Road strata purchase.
- **District plans and zoning (District of North Vancouver — not the City):** the Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 7900** and the zoning bylaw is **Zoning Bylaw 3210**. The village product in §2 exists because the OCP directs growth to its centres (Lions Gate Village Centre at Capilano Road and Marine Drive, implemented from the April 2013 Lower Capilano–Marine Village Implementation Plan and the July 2015 Lions Gate Public Realm Strategy) while preserving established low-density neighbourhoods elsewhere. Schedule B of the OCP establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard. **City of North Vancouver bylaws (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700) do not apply anywhere in this file's ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4013488 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/pemberton-nv.md)
- **Suites, coach houses and SSMUH (bench lots):** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary, with a secondary suite up to 49% of the house and no minimum size. Price a bench lot as a single-family lot unless a specific approval says otherwise. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/pemberton-nv.md)

- **Q: Can I rent out a Lions Gate Village townhouse long-term?** Yes in law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Curling Road or Glenaire Drive address?** UNVERIFIED per address — SD44 catchments are address-based, no locator run for any counted complex's address is on record in this program, and SD44 ran a catchment review tied to the Cloverley elementary opening in September 2026. - **Q: Are there flood or slope risks to check on a village townhouse?** At the west edge, yes: the Capilano River below Cleveland Dam can rise more than 5 metres in a release event, and the District publishes GEOweb flood construction levels modelled on a 2100 flood horizon plus 0.6 m. - **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a bench house instead?** District zoning has permitted a house plus secondary suite plus coach house (three units) on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. Do not price in full SSMUH density on top — Council voted in March 2026 to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area from SSMUH. (§3 above)
- **Q: What PTT should I budget on a village townhouse?** On the §3 worked example — inputs: $1,000,000 fair market value (round illustration only; no North Vancouver median is published in the sources cited), fully residential, no exemption — the bill is $18,000. A first-time buyer at or under the $835,000 ceiling pays no PTT on the first $500,000 under the First-Time Home Buyers' Program. (§3 above)
