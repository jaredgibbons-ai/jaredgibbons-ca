## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata complex):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Oakhaus is a 2025 build — for a developer-held unit the disclosure-statement route applies instead; for a resale, the Form B package is the diligence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — directly relevant to the small multiplex stratas now forming on Oakridge side streets under R1-1. Oakhaus (20 lots) sits above the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term-rental licensing rules apply on top in this city. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for the counted complex in §2 — its restrictions are UNCONFIRMED — so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Oakridge VW building is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — the 55-plus pass verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in the sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Oakridge VW (§2); do not import strata expectations into a fee-simple side-street purchase, or fee-simple expectations into Oakhaus, which is strata.
- **City plans and zoning:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) is the governing framework at the town centre — for side-street house lots, price against the corridor designation first and the house second, because Phase 3 policy supports townhouse and low-rise forms on many streets within walking distance of Oakridge–41st Station, implemented through rezoning. Citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. For detached lots outside the corridor designations, the R1-1 multiplex rules apply: up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units under By-law No. 13817, which amended the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/oakridge-vw.md)
- **City of Vancouver taxes:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a **5% penalty** applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/oakridge-vw.md)
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City of Vancouver charges an Empty Homes Tax of **3% of the property's assessed taxable value** on homes declared or deemed vacant (annual declaration due February 3, 2026; payment due April 16, 2026). This is a City of Vancouver tax on Vancouver properties — it applies to an Oakridge VW townhome that will sit vacant or be held as a secondary property, and it does not apply in any other municipality in this program. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/oakridge-vw.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs, and no such row is documented in this slug's own research, so the compression is flagged here as the standing reading, not as a defect asserted against this slug's page. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (including any developer-held Oakhaus unit), not resales. The CMHC premium-range error documented elsewhere in the program (a live page stating 2.80%–4.00%) is **not documented for this slug** in its research or siblings and is therefore **not asserted** here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
