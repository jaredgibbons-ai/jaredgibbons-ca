## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**No verified townhouse stock exists in Hatzic for the strata block to attach to. The block is stated in full so the standard is on record; the jurisdiction split below it is the frame that actually decides a Hatzic purchase.**

**The strata block (BC standard — no Hatzic complex is currently verified under it).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: Form B within seven days of request, capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page — strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). No Form B exists for a fee-simple Bench house, a pad home or a resort use-area — diligence there runs on title, park/resort documents and municipal/FVRD records.
- **Depreciation reports / CRF.** Five-or-more-lot stratas: depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024 (sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since that date (SPA ss. 123.1–123.2, Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Park age rules under the MHPTA are a different instrument from strata age bylaws — do not conflate them (§3, MHPTA note).

**The split that actually governs Hatzic — read the jurisdiction before the rule.**

- **Bench (City of Mission ground):** OCP = **Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** (program standing correction). This slug's research file documents the bylaw only to its April 7, 2026 public hearing (to replace OCP Bylaw 6350-2025) with adoption UNVERIFIED in that pass — the file follows the standing correction on currency and flags the research snapshot as superseded on that point; a "June 1, 2026" date for this OCP appearing anywhere is WRONG and is a Jerry item when a file's own research documents it (this slug's research does not carry that date, so it is flagged here as a standing correction, not as a documented defect of the Hatzic page) (research file §4: https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026). City suites: all secondary suites must be registered with the City, a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity, and **suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes** in the City (https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program). **City taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on unpaid taxes after July 2 (Community Charter ss. 233–234; City page: taxes due Thursday, July 2, 2026, 10% penalty on current balances outstanding July 3, 2026) — city ground ONLY (https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes). Mission has NO Supreme Court registry (standing note — relevant to foreclosure/court-ordered-sale searches, not to townhouse tenure).
- **Prairie (Area F) and Island (Area G):** FVRD/Province ground. Suites in Area F must be located within the principal dwelling — a detached garden suite is not the Area F suite route (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf). FVRD bylaw numbers for this ground: UNVERIFIED — none are borrowed from the City. **Rural taxes:** notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office, paid to the Province; due July 2, 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area).
- **ALR (all three parts, per parcel):** one principal residence to 500 m², a suite only within the principal residence, one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); ALR residences cannot be stratified (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED.
- **MHPTA note (pad stock):** where a purchase is a manufactured home on a rented pad (Green Acres; Mission MHP), the park frame governs — pad-rent increases capped at 2.3% plus proportional for 2026 and 2.2% plus proportional for 2027, assignment consent under MHPTA s. 28 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes). **No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file.**

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling stating March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **PTT.** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" summary compresses 3% + 2% and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: $800,000 with no exemption = $14,000. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on the first $500,000 to $835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 lifetime, $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **CMHC.** Premiums 0.6%–4.5% by loan-to-value (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). Stated explicitly: this slug's research documents NO CMHC premium page error, so the known 2.80%–4.00% error is NOT asserted for Hatzic.
- **Presales (REDMA).** 7-day rescission under s. 21; deposits in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01). None counted here (§2.4).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there any townhomes in Hatzic?**
A: None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes on the Bench, the Prairie or the Island, and the board counted 0 Condos & Townhomes listings in the Hatzic sub-area in September 2026 (§§1–2). **Q: Is Hatzic part of Mission?**
A: Partly. The Bench is inside the City of Mission; Hatzic Prairie is FVRD Electoral Area F and Hatzic Island is Electoral Area G — and all of it carries Mission mailing addresses and sits in the board's Hatzic sub-area (https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf).

**Q: Who do I pay property tax to on a Hatzic property?**
A: It depends on which Hatzic. Bench (City): the City — due July 2, 2026, flat 10% penalty on unpaid taxes after July 2. Prairie/Island (rural): the Province, via the Surveyor of Taxes Office — due July 2, 5% + 5% (October 31) under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act (§3). Check the PID's jurisdiction before quoting either.

**Q: Is an Everglades Resort lot a cheap way to own at Hatzic Lake?**
A: It is a different asset, not a cheap homesite: listings record an undivided-interest recreational holding with "No year round living" — seasonal use, resort documents, and no strata or fee-simple title of the kind a townhouse buyer expects (§2.5). Confirm what interest actually transfers before writing.

**Q: Can I add a suite in a Hatzic home?**
A: By jurisdiction: in the City (Bench), a suite must be registered and sits inside a single family home — suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; in Area F (Prairie), a suite must be within the principal dwelling; on ALR land the ALC caps sit on top of both (§3).

**Q: How did Hatzic sell in September 2026?**
A: Detached: 20 listings, 3 sold, 15% sales ratio (small-count caution — 3 sales). Attached: 0 listings, 0 sold, no ratio. No sub-area median is published; the Mission community medians are board context, not Hatzic figures (§1).

**Q: Is Hatzic Lake property flood-exposed?**
A: The valley has a documented history — the 1948 dyke break put Hatzic Prairie largely under water — but a specific property's flood status and dyke protection are parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED in this research. Check the PID against current floodplain mapping before relying on any position (§1).

**Q: Are the Shook Road "waterfront" claims reliable?**
A: Not from listing text: no official source opened in this pass confirms dock rights, moorage, beach tenure or direct lake frontage for any listed property — all UNVERIFIED per property. Confirm frontage and any shared-tenure terms on title and with the resort or park (§2.5).

**Q: Which schools serve Hatzic?**
A: Hatzic Elementary (8465 Draper St) and Hatzic Middle School (34875 Moffat Avenue), SD75, confirmed in the Ministry file, with Mission Senior Secondary (32939 7th Ave) for senior grades — but the catchment for a specific lake- or prairie-side address is UNVERIFIED; check the exact address with the district (§1).

**Q: How much property transfer tax on an $800,000 Bench house?**
A: $14,000 with no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 plus 2% on the remaining $600,000 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). PTT follows the property, not the jurisdiction split — the tax-penalty regimes in §3 are the part that changes by side of the line.
