## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It applies to the three counted London Landing complexes (in the count home) and to every strata documented in §2. Directory and listing rental/pet lines are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Currents' 18 homes put it over the threshold on the count home's record; London Station's and Princess Lane's lot counts are UNCONFIRMED, so their threshold position is read off the Form B). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry where met: the Princess Lane directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" carries a rental-restriction element that is VOID for long-term rentals (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No source states a 55+ bylaw for any strata on this ground — the 55-plus program verifies ZERO 55+ ownership complexes in this sub-area; the district's seniors stock is the rental, subsidized and co-op product excluded in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/steveston-south-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — City of Richmond rules plus any registered covenants/HOA on title only. None was verified on this ground (§2); the 13200 Princess Street development is City-documented as a townhouse development but its tenure is UNCONFIRMED, so it is not presented as fee-simple (or strata) stock.
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 governs; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw 9000, with the Steveston Area Plan as Schedule 2.4. Houses sit in Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot areas by subzone including RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m²; secondary suites sit under Section 5.4 (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²), and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL were added under provincial Bill 44 legislation. Village-core strata sits in ZMU32 / CS3 zones (§1). Properties inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes under the Steveston Area Plan. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/steveston-south.md)
