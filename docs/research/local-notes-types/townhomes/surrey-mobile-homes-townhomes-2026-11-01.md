## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Read this section as the MHPTA frame that replaces the townhome/strata frame for a park purchase, plus the strata block that still governs the own-land strata form above.

- **Pad tenancy law — the regime that actually governs a park home.** Manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. On a sale where the home stays in the park, assignment of the site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer; under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — and before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement. On assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad rent increases — the MHPTA limits, stated in full:** landlords in manufactured home parks can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; a landlord may also add a proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park — and may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. The limits: **2026 = 2.3% plus the proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount.** No pad-rent dollar figure for any Surrey park is published in this file — any dollar range on the live page or in a listing is UNVERIFIED site/listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Registration is what makes the sale effective.** In BC, the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; the Manufactured Home Registry maintains the central register of manufactured home ownership, controls the movement of manufactured homes in BC, and improves the security of lenders financing purchases. Buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **The CSA label is a sale gate.** A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached — Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular homes; the Province directs buyers to make sure the label is attached at the point of sale. If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Registry, a tax certificate for transport is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Park age rules are park rules, not strata bylaws.** A park's 55+ restriction (Westwood Court, Westwood Estates, Newton MHP) runs through the written park rules and the MHPTA assignment consent — the Strata Property Act's 55+ machinery (ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions) does NOT govern it, and the park's own wording decides who may live in the home. The one exception in this topic is Sea Ridge Bays, a bare-land strata, where the SPA bylaw and its exemptions DO apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Form B — there is none for a park home.** A Form B Information Certificate (Strata Property Act s. 59; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page) exists only for strata lots — so it applies to an own-land bare-land strata manufactured home in this feed (Sea Ridge Bays), and not to a pad-rental park home. The park-home equivalents are the written park rules and tenancy agreement, the Manufactured Home Registry record, and the CSA label status. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF — strata forms only.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the CRF. Both bind strata corporations only — they apply to Sea Ridge Bays as a bare-land strata and do NOT apply to a park, which has no depreciation report and no CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental rules — strata voiding does not rewrite park rules.** Strata rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — a Strata Property Act change; it does not rewrite a park's rules. Whether a park home may be rented out is governed by the park rules and the site tenancy agreement. Only 55+ age bylaws are lawful in a strata (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemption expansion) — the frame for Sea Ridge Bays only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A manufactured home on its own fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees and NO pad tenancy — municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) and title govern; the Manufactured Home Registry registration and CSA label rules above still apply to the home itself. (§2 above)
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current. Every Surrey property carries a zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS; the bylaw's manufactured-home-park zone rules as they apply to Surrey parks are UNVERIFIED in the research pass (City pages stopped by a fetch rate-limit, not retried). Surrey's Manufactured Home Park Policy adds a City layer on redevelopment: the property owner must give 12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after receiving all permits, with an affordable housing program required for residents; Surrey's Tenant Protection Bylaw, 2025, No. 21788 does NOT apply to property governed by the MHPTA. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **SSMUH and suites (for a buyer comparing a park home against a house lot):** Surrey's framework (in force July 8, 2024) allows 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house, capped at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite; unpermitted suites are not retroactively legalized. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. On a park home specifically, the Province states a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-mobile-homes.md)
- **Taxes and rebates on the purchase:** how GST and Property Transfer Tax apply to a specific manufactured-home purchase depends on whether land transfers with the home (a park unit transfers no land) — UNCONFIRMED per unit; take conveyancing advice before writing. BC PTT generally: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% + 2% tiers, flagged as such); first-time buyers' exemption full to $835K FMV on the first $500,000, partial to $860K; newly built home exemption full to $1.1M, partial to $1.15M, where land is acquired. For qualifying new homes generally: FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (park-home financing is lender-specific — confirm with the lender and the park before subjects). No CMHC range defect is asserted for this page — this file's own research does not document one, so the known live-page 2.80%–4.00% pattern is NOT asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA) — stated so it is not assumed:** REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s. 18 requires deposits in trust for developer marketing — park-home resales are NOT REDMA presales, so no 7-day rescission exists on a park purchase; subject periods there are contractual. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

- **Q: What do I actually own in a park like Westwood Court or Newton MHP?** The manufactured home — not the land. - **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change. Increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit (2026: 2.3%; 2027: 2.2%) plus the proportional levy and utility amount. No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What is the pad rent at a Surrey park?** Not published here, by standing rule. Listing-stated amounts differ by home and date and are UNVERIFIED site/listing copy — confirm the current rent for the specific pad in the tenancy agreement, through the assignment process. (§2 and §3 above)
- **Q: Westwood Court and Westwood Estates say 55+ — is that the same 55+ bylaw as a strata townhouse?** No — that is the core distinction in this file. In a strata, 55+ is a registered bylaw under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 with statutory exemptions. In a park, 55+ is a park rule in the written park rules; the SPA exemptions do not govern it, and the park's wording decides. Sea Ridge Bays is the exception that proves it: it is a bare-land strata, so the SPA bylaw applies there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What is the difference between a mobile home and a modular home in BC?** Both are manufactured homes built off-site. The Province's registry distinguishes them by CSA standard: mobile homes are built under the Z-240 standards and modular homes under the A-277 standards, and the CSA label on the home states which applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached. If the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: How does Sea Ridge Bays differ from the three parks?** You own the lot. Sea Ridge Bays is a bare-land strata — title is recorded as Freehold Strata, you pay a strata fee instead of pad rent, and strata law (including the 55+ bylaw and its exemptions, the Form B and the depreciation report) governs instead of the MHPTA. It is documented in §2 as its own form and is not counted as a townhouse. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/surrey-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The assignment does not reset the rent clock.** On assignment the pad rent stays the same for the incoming tenant and the next scheduled increase date does not move — a transfer is not a turnover, and any park-office suggestion that a purchase triggers a new market pad rent contradicts the Province's assignment guidance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The pad-rent cap has a second, proportional component.** On top of the percentage limit (2.3% in 2026, 2.2% in 2027) the landlord may add the proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus regulated park utility fees divided by the number of sites in the park — so two Surrey parks with the same percentage limit can post different lawful increases in the same year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Two Surrey parks share almost the same name, in two different sub-areas.** Westwood Court is the Fleetwood park at 8670 156 Street; Westwood Estates is the Queen Mary Park park on 134 Street, whose own listings disagree on 8224 vs 8254 as the civic address. An alert set on one name does not cover the other park. (§2 above)
- **The research program deliberately named no park from its own pass.** The topic's research file names no Surrey park as a sourced fact, because no park was confirmed from a developer, strata or park-operator source in its pass; the three park entries in §2 rest on the 55-plus program's listing and directory verification, labelled listing-stated accordingly — solid enough to document as parks, not a substitute for the park's own rules document, which the MHPTA assignment process requires to change hands before subjects come off. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/surrey-mobile-homes.md)
- **Surrey's tenant-protection bylaw does not cover park residents.** Bylaw No. 21788 (2025) expressly does not apply to property governed by the MHPTA — park residents' redevelopment protection is the provincial Act plus the City's Manufactured Home Park Policy (12 months' notice of a final moving date, only after all permits are received). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_reg_21788.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ManufacturedHomeParkPolicy.pdf
- **The CSA label is a sale gate, not a detail.** Without the original legible decal (Z-240 mobile, A-277 modular) the home cannot be sold at all until Technical Safety BC re-certification — on an older park unit, ask for a photo of the label before booking a showing, not at subject removal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
