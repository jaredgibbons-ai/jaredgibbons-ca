## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section is mostly a contrast section:** Park Royal has no strata townhouse to apply strata diligence to (§2). The rules below are stated in full because the format requires them, because the sub-area's two counted apartment stratas are the stock a townhouse search actually lands on, and because the tenure check that replaces strata diligence here — freehold versus reserve leasehold — is the first-order rule for this ground.
- **Tenure check first (the Park Royal diligence order):** confirm whether the property is freehold municipal land or a leasehold interest on Capilano IR No. 5, and if leasehold, get the head-lease terms, remaining term and any prepaid/rent-reset structure before pricing or financing discussions. Lenders treat reserve-land leaseholds individually — flag the tenure to the buyer's mortgage broker at the offer stage, not after subjects. Only after tenure is settled does the strata frame below apply, and it applies only to a freehold strata purchase. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/park-royal.md)
- **Form B diligence (strata purchases — the WestRoyal and Evelyn apartment stock in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At the WestRoyal (1993, concrete), the depreciation report and CRF balance are the first-order documents. A leasehold purchase has no equivalent freehold Form B frame — the lease documents govern alongside any strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry in this file: the "rentals allowed with restrictions" element of The WestRoyal's directory line (§2) is VOID as a restriction and is flagged there, not repeated as a live restriction. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No Park Royal building is verified to carry a 55+ bylaw — a listing calling a WestRoyal unit "perfect for downsizing" is marketing language, not a bylaw — and the 55+ program found zero 55+ ownership stock in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Canada's first covered shopping mall is also a First Nations leasehold story.** Park Royal opened in 1950; part of its lands are Capilano IR No. 5 under a lease dated April 1, 1974, and on the reserve portion the Squamish Nation — not the District — is the zoning authority, with the District providing building design review and inspection so construction meets municipal standards. The tenure split is the sub-area's defining fact, and it reaches residential parcels, not just the mall. http://parkroyal.ca/ and https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Park Royal's townhouse count is zero — and the nearest townhouses belong to another area's file.** The sub-area's general descriptions gesture at "townhouses near Taylor Way," but the Taylor Way product verifies as apartment towers, and the real townhouse stock up the hill (Hawksley, Aerie II, Highgrove) is British Properties ground counted in that area's townhomes file. A portal radius search blends all of it into one misleading list. (§2 above)
- **The province has already overridden West Vancouver once, next door.** When the District missed its December 31, 2025 directive deadlines, a provincial order effective April 7, 2026 amended the District's bylaw to implement the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan itself. The Park Royal–Taylor Way directive is the same instrument, still outstanding — buyers here are pricing inside an unresolved provincial process with quarterly progress reports through 2026 and 2027. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **"Evelyn" is a masterplan, not a building.** The counted condo entry is the 768 Arthur Erickson Place residences (built 2019); the same street also carries Evelyn detached houses built 2024/2025.
