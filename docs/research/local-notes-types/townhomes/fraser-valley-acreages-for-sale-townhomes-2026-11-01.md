## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The ALR frame — the rule set that governs this topic.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission.** Depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and a person must not subdivide agricultural land unless permitted by the Commission or under the ALR General Regulation (Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1); ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; the strata-plan deposit provision is Strata Property Act s. 239). This is a permission rule, not an absolute ban — do not write "can never be stratified," and do not cite Bulletin 05 for stratification, which contains no stratification statement (ALC Bulletin 09: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **ALR residence caps.** Land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; the principal residence must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, and an additional residence is capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a suite sits within the principal residence only; local governments may regulate further but not be more permissive than the ALC (ALC, housing in the ALR: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **ALR short-term accommodation.** Renting out the entire principal residence for short-term stays is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification (ALC: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9) — full three-tier thresholds.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of **$2,500 if the operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**. A flat "$2,500+" figure is an ERROR. New applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, and BC Assessment will remove farm classification if the requirements stop being met (BC Assessment: https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- Which parcels on this page's feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel; one visible card states in its own text that an 18-acre Poplar (Abbotsford) property "is in the ALR & currently farmed" — listing text only (research file §4: https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling carrying March 12 is superseded on this point). 100% of GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). Resale acreages do not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST on new or substantially renovated housing, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + further 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears; no such row is documented in this slug's research file (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Exemptions: first-time buyers — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; newly built homes — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
- **Home Buyers' Plan / FHSA.** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime at $8,000 per year (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). **No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug:** this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% premium-range error for the acreages page, so it is NOT asserted here.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there townhouse complexes on Fraser Valley acreages?**
A: None counted, and none expected on ALR ground: ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf). This topic file carries no roster of its own.

**Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR acreage into strata lots?**
A: Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s. 21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6).

**Q: How many homes can I have on an ALR acreage?**
A: Generally one principal residence (maximum 500 m² total floor area), plus, where the property qualifies, one additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local bylaws may restrict further (ALC: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: What farm income does an acreage need to keep farm classification?**
A: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31 (BC Assessment: https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification). A flat "$2,500+" answer would be wrong.

**Q: Is there a published Fraser Valley acreage or townhome market statistic for this page?**
A: No. SnapStats publishes no acreage segment and splits property types only as Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined — never a townhome-only figure — and no single community's figures describe this regional page (research file §2).

**Q: Which communities does this page's feed cover?**
A: At reading, Langley sub-areas (County Line Glen Valley, Willoughby Heights, Campbell Valley, Salmon River, Otter District) and Abbotsford sub-areas (Abbotsford West, Poplar), with copy spanning "Langley to Chilliwack and beyond" (research file §1: https://jaredgibbons.ca/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.html).

**Q: Do I get a Form B when I buy an acreage?**
A: No — acreage stock here is freehold, not strata: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title, the ALR status of the parcel, the municipality's zoning/OCP for where the parcel actually sits, and parcel-level servicing (well, septic, water licence), which is UNVERIFIED for any specific feed parcel in this file (research file §4).

**Q: Can I rent my ALR acreage home short-term?**
A: Not outright: renting the entire principal residence short-term requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property (ALC: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Does the first-time buyer GST rebate help on an acreage?**
A: Only on NEW housing — a newly built home on an acreage could qualify (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026); a resale acreage does not attract GST at all (CRA News No. 122: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
