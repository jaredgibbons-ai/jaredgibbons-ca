## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Land rules lead — ALR strata bar:** depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **ALR residence frame:** one principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² fill), suite only within it, + at most one additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may be stricter, never more permissive (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Farm classification:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 + 5% of actual value over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); applications by Oct 31; buyer continuing to farm files by Oct 31 after purchase (https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Delta plans/zoning/SSMUH:** OCP Bylaw 8400 (2024) (§1). **Zoning bylaw number CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025" (8400 is the OCP number). Parcel ALR/zoning/Flood Construction Level/geotechnical position UNVERIFIED per lot.
