## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Oakwood's strata, and each small strata project in Section 2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a four-lot strata the Form B package is short — read all of it, including who the other owners are and what the CRF actually holds. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports — the small-strata edge case, stated precisely:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with **five or more** lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Oakwood is well over the threshold; a four-unit Buxton or Elgin strata is **under** it — no statutory depreciation report is required, so the buyer must substitute their own building-condition diligence. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund — in a four-lot strata that minimum builds a very small reserve in absolute dollars. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets in this sub-area is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such; Oakwood's "rental-friendly" marketing line is simply the current law restated. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex or project in Section 2 is published as 55+, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ bylaw in the sub-area's attached stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified as a named complex here. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Note the trap in reverse on the new streets: several Buxton/Elgin/Dufferin homes that look like fee-simple houses are in fact Freehold Strata lots (Section 2) — the Title to Land field decides.
- **City zoning:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, Bylaw No. 14815 — adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026 — is the governing bylaw; it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742 is never the citation for current Burnaby zoning). Under its R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District, R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — the rule that produced the Section 2 multiplex form. The bylaw's district list also carries a Townhouse District (R2); Kingsway corridor parcels are not R1 — confirm the district parcel by parcel. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **Property tax and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Burnaby's 2026 detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Burnaby — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase — directly relevant to the 2026 Buxton/Elgin/Dufferin product):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (the 2026 multiplex units), not resales — and the new-home warranty position on a four-lot strata project is a diligence item in its own right. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **The four-lot strata is the new diligence trap.** A 2026 Buxton or Elgin buyer gets strata tenure without the five-lot depreciation-report duty, a CRF built on a 10%-of-a-small-budget minimum, and three neighbours whose votes decide every major repair — none of which shows in the listing's "townhouse" label. - **The zoning bylaw most guides still cite no longer exists.** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) repealed and replaced the 1965 bylaw effective July 1, 2026, and repackaged the districts into a height-based framework — any Forest Glen page quoting 1965-bylaw district numbers is quoting repealed law. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The park is small; the name is big.** The sub-area takes its name from Forest Glen Park at 4855 Bond St, a neighbourhood park on the City's parks list — the larger green spaces buyers picture (Central Park west, Deer Lake Park north) sit at or beyond the sub-area's edges. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
