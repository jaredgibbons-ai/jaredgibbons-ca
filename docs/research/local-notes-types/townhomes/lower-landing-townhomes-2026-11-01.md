## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **City zoning frame:** Zoning Bylaw 8500. Section 20.20 is the site-specific "Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU20) – London Landing (Steveston)" zone, created by Bylaw 8818 (September 24, 2012), to provide for commercial, residential and industrial uses in the Steveston area, with a maximum floor area ratio of 1.2 plus 0.1, maximum lot coverage of 76% and maximum height of 21.0 m. The ZMU24 zone for 13040 No. 2 Road was created by Bylaw 9094 (2014). Detached houses at the edges sit in RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under provincial Bill 44 legislation. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf and https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Plan:** London Landing sits inside the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000). https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Application_by_Jim_Pattison_Developments_for_Rezoning_at_13131__13111__1303179429.pdf
- **Live rezoning — application, not approval:** on July 8, 2026, City staff reported application RZ 25-009451 by Jim Pattison Developments to rezone 13131, 13111, 13031, 12931 and 12771 No. 2 Road from Light Industrial (IL) to a new "Residential / Limited Commercial (ZMU63) – London Landing (Steveston)" zone, with OCP Amendment Bylaw 10771 and Zoning Amendment Bylaw 10772, to permit a five- and six-storey residential and commercial mixed-use project, and to redesignate the lands from "Industrial" to "Local Village – Tier 1" in the Steveston Waterfront Neighbourhood Land Use Map. No approval is claimed. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Application_by_Jim_Pattison_Developments_for_Rezoning_at_13131__13111__1303179429.pdf
- **Flood:** the pocket fronts the South Arm of the Fraser River behind the City dike system; Richmond is made up of islands within the Fraser River floodplain at an average of 1 metre above sea level, protected by 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. New construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 — confirm the flood construction level and dike setback for any waterfront or dyke-adjacent purchase before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm and https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Form B diligence (the strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In the ZMU mixed-use buildings, read the depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early — these are mixed-use strata, not pure residential ones. Directory and listing rental/pet lines in Section 2 are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Currents (18 homes) is over the threshold; for London Station and Princess Lane, whose unit counts are UNCONFIRMED here, confirm the report's existence on the Form B. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Princess Lane directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for its long-term restriction element, for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ ownership in this pocket — Steveston's seniors stock (Maple Residences, Wisteria Place, Austin Harris) is village rental/care product outside the pocket, not ownership stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in the pocket. If the 13200 Princess Street development or any row home here is marketed as fee-simple, the rule is plain: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 8500, Bylaw 8204 flood rules) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Its tenure is UNCONFIRMED in this file, so that check is the buyer's first step, not an assumption. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendas/archives/dpp/2002/103002_minutes.htm
- **Taxes and rebates:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Richmond is NOT subject to Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax — that tax is Vancouver-only and does not appear as a cost here. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale inside the pocket was verified in this pass, so no live REDMA item arises; the RZ 25-009451 project is a rezoning application only. If product from it is ever marketed, the frame is a developer disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and a 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21), deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The name is a family, not a direction.** In 1881 brothers Charles and William London bought 200 acres at the south end of No. 2 Road at what became known as London's Landing — every official use since (the ZMU zones, the wharf park, the buildings) descends from that family name, which is why "Lower Landing" finds nothing in any City source. https://stevestonheritage.ca/about/
- **The fishing pier is a historic working site, not just a viewpoint:** the City's Steveston Greenways material describes London's Landing / No. 2 Road Fishing Pier as a historic area starting at the South Dyke Trail, with views of Steveston Island (Shady Island) and the historic Cannery Channel, and a fishing float, picnic tables, washrooms, interpretation signage and parking. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm
- **The pocket's zoning is site-specific, street by street:** ZMU20 was written for London Landing in 2012 (Bylaw 8818) and ZMU24 for one No. 2 Road site in 2014 (Bylaw 9094) — there is no general "London Landing zone" to quote; each building's permissions live in its own section of Bylaw 8500. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf and https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendafiles/Public_Hearing_5-20-2014.pdf
- **The district's civic anchor is west in the village:** Steveston Community Park, 4011 Moncton Street, holds the Steveston Community Centre, pool and library complex, and Richmond Public Library's Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub) is at 4320 Moncton Street — London Landing residents use village facilities, not in-pocket ones. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm
- **The nearest hospital is across Richmond:** Richmond Hospital, 7000 Westminster Highway (Vancouver Coastal Health) — a London Landing buyer's hospital is a cross-city trip, not a Steveston one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/health/about-bc-s-health-care-system/heath-care-partners/hospitallist.pdf
- **Imperial Landing is the comparison, not the neighbour in this file:** the 6.5-acre Imperial Landing Waterfront Park on the historic BC Packers cannery site and the Britannia Shipyards National Heritage Site sit west along the Greenways — buyers cross-shop that waterfront product against London Landing's, but it is Steveston Village ground, counted in the Steveston files, not here. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm
