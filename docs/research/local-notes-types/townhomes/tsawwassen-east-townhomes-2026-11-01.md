## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The rules below govern the stock counted in the guide file; they are restated here so this standalone file stands on its own, and nothing in this section changes any guide-file count.

- **Strata townhomes (The Granary, in the guide file): the Form B is the core document.** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a presale, the developer's disclosure statement comes first (REDMA, below). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple rowhomes (High Street, in the guide file): plainly no strata.** A non-strata freehold rowhouse has NO strata corporation, NO Form B, NO strata fees, no CRF and no strata bylaws. Diligence is title, zoning, the City permit record and any registered covenants or building scheme on title.
- **Depreciation reports (strata collections):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote, with 30-year projections and three CRF funding models; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata collections):** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — any "rentals not allowed" wording in an older document or directory entry is invalid for long-term rentals and is flagged void in-entry wherever this program meets it. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined child cases). No 55+ bylaw is verified for any Southlands collection — UNCONFIRMED per collection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B. Any source attributing "Schedule D.1" to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong. The former-plan framing in this research is followed exactly: a City report (LU009394) described 1728 Beach Grove Road as having a Single Family Residential designation in the Tsawwassen Area Plan — that was the FORMER plan's designation, not a current one, and the current OCP does not use a "Single Family Residential" designation. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3:** for the Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, the OCP provides that no building be located with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, that the minimum land surface elevation around construction be 2.3 metres GSC datum, and that no building be constructed within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-east.md, adaptation source as cited there)
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** City material cited in this research names Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (its s. 6.2.3 sets the waterfront setback below); the program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (a competing citation reads "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-east.md)
- **RD2 and the waterfront setback:** 1728 Beach Grove Road is zoned RD2 (Duplex/Single Detached Residential 2); RD2 carries a 4,000 m² minimum lot size for subdivision, a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove. Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. The RD2 facts rest on the batch-1 adaptation source's verified reading — a direct re-open of the RD2 PDF in the batch-10 pass returned an empty response. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-east.md)
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta, six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). This affects detached lots, not strata lots inside a townhouse complex. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **ALR edges:** an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Farm-status thresholds run in three tiers — $2,500 in farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error; no such statement is documented in this research. A listing's ALR claim (including the 6 Avenue feed card's exemption claim) is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address, never taken from listing text. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — relevant to any "private dock" claim on the shore, not to the Southlands townhomes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/tsawwassen-east.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day statutory rescission period from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging receipt of that statement (REDMA s. 21). Presales are counted separately from completed stock in the guide file and never mixed. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **GST on new construction:** new townhomes carry GST. FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate alone is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT and exemptions:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this research). First-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial to $860,000, principal residence; newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No CMHC premium-range error on a live page is documented in THIS file's research, so none is asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. Area-specific tax or levy notes are UNVERIFIED in this research. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Flood infrastructure:** the east side faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; the City operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
