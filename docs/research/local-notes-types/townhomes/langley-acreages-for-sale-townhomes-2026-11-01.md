## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **SSMUH:** Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots must be 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer; most acreages fail on size and servicing alone. NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020 — no page claim of a 6-plex tier is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted here (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).

**ALR frame in full.**

- One principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area, no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may be more restrictive (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Farm class (BC Assessment):** $2,500 in farm-gate sales at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. ## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Is an acreage a townhome for counting purposes?**
A: No. This is a topic file with no roster: **counted 0 — 0 strata, 0 fee-simple** (§2). Acreage houses are fee-simple detached product.

**Q: What counts as an acreage on this page?**
A: The page's own definition is a home on 1 to 10 acres, with land over 10 acres routed to the farms page — but the live feed at reading included a 39.5-acre estate and a bare-land listing, so treat the definition as stated intent, not a verified filter (research file §1; https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html).

**Q: How many homes can I build on an ALR acreage?**
A: Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less (≤1,000 m² fill), a suite only inside it, plus one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); more or larger requires ALC approval (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage into townhomes?**
A: No — ALR residences cannot be stratified; depositing a strata plan is a subdivision requiring ALC permission (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). That Bulletin citation is carried UNVERIFIED in the research because the Bulletin itself was not opened (§2.4).

**Q: What farm income does an acreage need for farm class?**
A: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha above that, $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31 — not a flat "$2,500+" (research file §4; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit).

**Q: Can I price an acreage off the Langley detached median?**
A: No. The $1,345,000 September 2026 detached median is a whole-community figure dominated by urban lots, expressly not an acreage-segment figure; no acreage-segment figure exists in SnapStats (§1).

**Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on an acreage?**
A: No for most acreages: Bylaw 6020 requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and on municipal water and sewer (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).

**Q: What should I check before removing subjects on 1–5 acres?**
A: The page's own advice, adopted in the research: test well flow and quality and the septic system, confirm permits for outbuildings, suites and shops, and ask the Township what the zone allows. Per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED in this file (research file §§4, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html).

**Q: Which schools serve a Langley acreage?**
A: UNVERIFIED by catchment. Only Ministry-listed names are carried — Shortreed Community School, 27330 28 Ave, SD35, and Dorothy Peacock Elementary, 20292 91A Ave, SD35 — and no locator run for an acreage address is documented, so run the exact address before relying on a school (§1).
