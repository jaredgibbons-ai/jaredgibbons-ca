## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the counted Ladner strata townhomes cross-referenced in §2, in their own files).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Ladner strata is void in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — stated plainly. None is counted on this file's ground (§2.2).

**Delta plan, zoning, SSMUH, ALR and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **OCP:** consolidated **Bylaw 8400 (2024)**, NO lettered schedules; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." The "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" formulation is an error wherever met — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950 (research file §1; https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).
- **Zoning number CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 while an internal title reads "8400, 2025." The A1 Agriculture zone is intended for farming, breeding pets, kennels and related uses; farming is a principal use, with accessory uses including a farm house, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls (research file §4; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- **A1 residential limits:** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot: maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate; ALC approval is required for more than one. An additional farm house (180 m² maximum under 8 ha; 233 m² at 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report and written ALC approval. Subdivision in A1 (s. 10.10.8) is not permitted except with OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable; minimum new lot 8 ha, with a home site severance exception (research file §4; https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf).
- **ALR:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). A specific parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).

**Q: Can I treat a Canoe Pass float home like a strata townhouse?**

**Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Ladner Rural?**
A: Delta's A1 zone permits one farm house per lot at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², on a farm home plate of at most 3,600 m² (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate) (§3).

**Q: Can a Ladner Rural farm have a second dwelling?**
A: Within limits: one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less at 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less above 40 ha — on the same farm home plate; more than one needs ALC approval, and an additional farm house needs an agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval (§3).

**Q: Can ALR land be stratified into townhouses?**
A: No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Subdivision of A1 farm land also needs a minimum 8 ha new lot, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable (§3).
