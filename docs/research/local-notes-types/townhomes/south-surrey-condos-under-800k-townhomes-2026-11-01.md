## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata townhome in §2.3).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A feed card's "no pets" line (Seapark East, §2.6) is listing text, not a bylaw — the Form B and registered bylaws govern (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: Semiahmoo Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" element is **void for long-term rentals**; rentals are allowed. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the municipal STR frames differ by city (below) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Suncliff Place's listing-stated 55+ position and Peninsula Village's 55+ position are confirmed only on their Form Bs (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None counted here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey and White Rock zoning, OCP, suites and STRs — the band crosses a municipal boundary.**

- **Surrey OCP — standing correction:** Surrey's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 21900, "Surrey 2050," finally adopted June 15, 2026 — Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current.** Zoning is under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000. **White Rock:** multi-unit zones (RT and RM) are set by White Rock Zoning Bylaw 2506, which sets a 742 m² minimum lot for them, per the live page's copy carried in this file's research; White Rock's own property tax due date and penalties and its short-term rental rules are UNVERIFIED in this file — no City of White Rock page was read in this pass, and Surrey's rules do not apply in White Rock (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-condos-under-800k.html).
- **Secondary suites (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule (research file frame; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments took effect **July 8, 2024** (standing correction; Zoning By-law No. 12000).
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame only):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (program figure, per this file's research: https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals) versus the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and direct any buyer to the City licence page before quoting either figure.
- **Property taxes (Surrey):** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; no sibling for this slug carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction (Harlowe presale; any developer sale at a newly completed building such as Odyssey — establish per unit whether a sale is a developer sale or a resale), not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression wherever met. A purchase in this band pays only the 1% and 2% tiers. **Worked examples, inputs stated, no exemption:** $700,000 → $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000**; at the band ceiling, $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000**. The program anchors at higher prices, for comparison where a buyer steps above the band: $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000** (same tier math). Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000 — because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
