## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all 36 counted strata complexes/phases).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Each Greentree Village phase is its own corporation: a Form B from one phase says nothing about any other phase (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Phase/complex lot counts that a count home marks UNCONFIRMED (e.g., Greentree phases, Capitol Hill plan numbers) carry UNCONFIRMED threshold status — read the count off the Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a §2.1 complex is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such in-entry wherever a count home carries one (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals).
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws). The 55-plus sibling for this slug (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/burnaby-homes-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`) consolidates the city's 55+ roster from 20 sub-area files — read it for any age-restricted claim; no §2.1 complex is counted here as 55+ on this file's own evidence.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified in Burnaby (§2.3). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Burnaby zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — **NEVER the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742** (standing correction; research file §4: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite).
- **Plan: Burnaby 2050 OCP, adopted December 9, 2025**, replacing the 1998 OCP (standing correction; 55-plus sibling: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan). The research file describes it as the City's long-term document integrating land use, transportation, housing, environment, community facilities and economic planning, reviewed every five years (research file §4: https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan).
- **SSMUH (R1).** On June 10, 2024 Council rezoned all R District lots to the R1 SSMUH District; R1 lots are eligible for 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent-transit stop, with secondary suites counted inside the total; forms include single-family homes, laneway homes, suites, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and, on some properties, fiveplexes and sixplexes (research file §4). Secondary suites under the BC Building Code must remain on the same title as the primary unit and cannot be stratified. **Suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED** — the City pages read state neither (research file §4). **Short-term rental rules: UNVERIFIED** in this research (research file §4).
- **Burnaby property taxes.** Due July 3, 2026; 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date on outstanding current taxes; the City has no authority to waive or modify them. Council approved a 2.9% increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan (research file §4: https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How many townhouse complexes are verified in Burnaby?**
A: 36 completed strata complexes/phases, 0 fee-simple, across the sub-area townhomes files on disk — Edmonds BE alone holds 11. This city file counts 0 new; every complex is counted once in its sub-area file (§2.1). Sub-areas without a townhomes file on disk are a coverage limit, not a zero (§2.1).

**Q: Is Greentree Village one strata?**
A: No. It is one 1972 planned community delivered as multiple strata phases south of Canada Way (V5G) — its townhomes file counts 4 verified phase corporations, each with its own Form B, CRF and depreciation report. A Form B from the wrong phase tells you nothing (§2.1).

**Q: A Burnaby listing says "Tower 1" — is it the rental building?**
A: Check the address. Strata Tower 1 is 4510 Halifax Way (ownership); SHAPE rental Tower ONE is 1820 Willingdon Ave (rental, excluded). One word apart, opposite tenures (§2.5).

**Q: Is an SFU townhouse/condo freehold like a Brentwood one?**
A: No. UniverCity stock sits on 99-year prepaid leasehold under SFU Community Trust ground leases — confirm tenure on title and price it as leasehold, not freehold (§2.5).

**Q: What is Kensington Gardens, and why is it excluded?**
A: A Hastings-corridor complex held in undivided-interest form, not strata title — excluded program-wide. A cheap-looking listing there is a share/interest sale, not a strata deal (§2.5).

**Q: Can I rent out a Burnaby strata townhouse long-term?**
A: A long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Read the registered bylaws in the Form B package — a directory "rentals not allowed" line is void for long-term rentals (§3).

**Q: My complex was built in the 1970s–80s (Greentree, Forest Hills) — what document matters most?**
A: The depreciation report and CRF balance on the Form B: 5+ lots means a report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, and the CRF minimum has been 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Where a phase's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, its threshold status is UNCONFIRMED until the Form B is read (§3).

**Q: Which Burnaby bylaw governs a townhouse lot now?**
A: Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (in effect July 1, 2026) under the Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025). Any guide still citing the 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 or the 1998 OCP is out of date (§3).

**Q: How many units can a Burnaby house lot hold under SSMUH?**
A: In R1, 3 to 6 units by lot area and frequent-transit proximity, suites counted inside the total — that speaks to detached lots, not to the strata complexes in §2.1, whose density is fixed by their strata plans (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve a Burnaby townhouse?**
A: The Ministry-listed SD41 schools are in §1 — but catchment assignment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED without an SD41 locator run on record; a listing's catchment claim is copy only (§1).
