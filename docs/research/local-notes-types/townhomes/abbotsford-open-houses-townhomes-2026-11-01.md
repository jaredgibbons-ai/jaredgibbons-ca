## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The open-house rule — verbatim.**

- **BCFSA Rule 5-10(3)** (Real Estate Services Rules; now carried as s. 54(3) in the renumbered Rules consolidation), quoted verbatim: *"(3) Unless a licensee solicits or receives information from a party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate, a disclosure to the party is not required under subsection (1) when the licensee is only (a) hosting an advertised open house, or (b) providing factual responses to general questions from the party."* (BC Laws, Real Estate Services Rules, B.C. Reg. 209/2021: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo108/loo108/209_2021). The hosting agent at an Abbotsford open house represents the seller: BCFSA states a licensee must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party (BCFSA: https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers).
- **The 1-hour offset, expressly NOT asserted for this page.** The offset requires both elements on the same cards — a card time block AND an "Open House on…" line. This page has **no card time block at all**; times live only in description text (§1). The offset therefore **cannot occur here and must not be asserted**; the offset feeds documented elsewhere (Langley city, Mission city, Tsawwassen, Cloverdale, Ladner) are not importable into this file. Authoritative time is UNVERIFIED as a general proposition wherever the offset IS documented — a question that does not arise on this page.

**The strata block (for the strata townhomes a visitor will tour on this feed; the complexes themselves are counted in their sub-area files).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports:** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals:** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flag any "rentals not allowed" line met on a tour as void. **Age:** only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse on this feed carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Abbotsford zoning, OCP, suites and taxes.**

- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, is CURRENT.** Any citation of "2600-2016" or "2500-2016" as the current Abbotsford OCP is **stale — flagged** wherever met.
- Zoning is under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014: s. 140 allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s. 140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, a suite being **less than 50%** of the principal unit's size — Abbotsford's under-50% cap is **Abbotsford's rule only, never exported** to another municipality. **SSMUH bylaw number UNCONFIRMED — never assert "3560-2024"** (research file §4: https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140). A: That is the page's own description of its feed. A browser visit confirms the feed renders (81 listings, "OPEN" badges), but the badge carries no date or time, so the claim remains UNVERIFIED beyond page 1 (research file §7).

**Q: Who does the hosting agent represent?**
A: The seller. Rule 5-10(3) excuses the representation disclosure only while the licensee is *only* hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers — once the host solicits or receives information about your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, the exception ends (§3, verbatim).

**Q: What should I avoid telling the host?**
A: Your motivation, budget and needs — that is exactly the information Rule 5-10(3) names. Use the open house for facts about the home; save your story for your own agent (research file §6).
