## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata complex in §2.1, in its home file's accounting).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Published rental/pet/age restrictions are as each home file states them; anything unpublished is UNCONFIRMED (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a §2.1 complex is void for long-term rentals and flagged in-entry in its home file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). **Purchase taxes, rebates and financing — with the band's worked tier math.**

- **PTT structure:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion to $2,000,000, 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, **plus** a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of that 3% + further 2% wherever met. Exemptions (first-time buyers; newly built to $1,100,000 full / $1,150,000 eliminated) will rarely attach at this band's prices (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **Worked example 1 — inputs: $2,500,000, purely residential, no exemption.** $200,000 × 1% = $2,000; $1,800,000 × 2% = $36,000; $500,000 × 3% = $15,000; no portion above $3,000,000. **PTT = $53,000.**
- **Worked example 2 — inputs: $4,498,000 (the feed's top card price at reading), purely residential, no exemption.** $2,000 + $36,000 + $1,000,000 × 3% ($30,000) + further 2% on the $1,498,000 residential portion above $3,000,000 ($29,960). **PTT = $97,960.** Program anchors for cross-check: $1.8M → $34,000; $3.5M purely residential → $78,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $700K → $12,000.
