## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated for completeness — no strata townhome exists on the island to apply it to).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for any strata product; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The island's actual stock is the fee-simple case: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — Delta bylaws, ALR rules and registered covenants govern a farmhouse purchase instead.

**Delta frame — OCP, zoning, SSMUH, ALR and taxes.**

- **OCP:** Delta Official Community Plan, **consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024)**, adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan with **NO lettered schedules**; the former Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (1985). Its designation list (s. 1.5.1) does NOT include "Single Family Residential," and farm land is designated **"Agriculture (A)"** (research file §§1, 4; https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf).
- **Zoning bylaw number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted.** Sources in this program carry both "Bylaw No. 7600, 2017" and "8400, 2025" for Delta's zoning bylaw; 8400 is the OCP number in the research file, so the zoning citation is held CONTESTED here and no zoning-bylaw number is asserted for Westham Island. One official sample only: a City report records 2194 Westham Island Road as zoned Agriculture (A 1) and entirely within the ALR, a 65.2 ha site (research file §4; https://delta.civicweb.net/document/220325/).
- **SSMUH:** Delta bylaws 8394 + 8395 (June 24, 2024); Delta's SSMUH frame allows up to 4 units on single-detached and duplex lots with support for accessory units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites — **on ALR land the ALC residence rules govern first** and Delta's page does not override them (research file §4; http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future).
- **ALR frame (governs most island parcels):** ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 / SPA s. 239 / ALCA s. 21(1)). A principal residence is capped at **≤500 m²** total floor area (fill ≤1,000 m²); a secondary suite is permitted only within the principal residence; one additional residence is capped at **90 m² (≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (>40 ha)**, with local government rules also applying. ALR status is parcel by parcel on the ALC map — no island-wide ALR percentage is stated (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Farm-class thresholds, stated in full because a flat "$2,500+" line is an **ERROR**: $2,500 of farm income for parcels of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the parcel's area value over 4 ha; $10,000 for parcels under 0.8 ha — assessed by October 31 (ALC / BC Assessment farm-class frame, standing program position).
- **Flood and dikes:** the island sits behind dikes on the Fraser River estuary; the City of Delta is the diking authority, operating over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure (research file §4; http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage).
- **Delta taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 2, 2026. ## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Are there any townhomes on Westham Island?**
A: None verified — count 0, strata and fee-simple. No townhouse complex is named for the island in any source read, and ALR residences cannot be stratified, so the island's housing is farmhouses and acreages (§2).

**Q: How do you get to Westham Island?**
A: By the Westham Island Bridge, the island's only road access — a TransLink-owned partial single-lane swing bridge over Canoe Pass, officially opened March 29, 1910, 50-ton load limit (§1).

**Q: What happened to the bridge in 2026?**
A: A marine vessel struck it on January 20, 2026 and it closed to vehicles for 67 days with emergency barge service running. It reopened to vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists on March 28, 2026 — corroborated fresh on City of Delta and TransLink sources; the research pass itself could not confirm the date and marked status UNVERIFIED, and both bases are stated in §1.

**Q: Is Westham Island in the ALR?**
A: Parcel by parcel — check the exact address on the ALC map. Where land is in the ALR, a principal residence is capped at 500 m², a suite is allowed only inside that residence, one additional residence is capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), and the residence cannot be stratified (§3).

**Q: Why is there no market data for Westham Island?**
A: SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures at all — its Delta coverage is North Delta only. No median, ratio or DOM figure for the island, Ladner or Tsawwassen can be stated from this program's sources; pricing evidence is the island's and Ladner's own recent farm and acreage sales (§1).

**Q: Which schools serve Westham Island?**
A: There is no school on the island in the Ministry file. Ladner's schools serve the island side of Delta and the guide names Delta Secondary (4615 51 St); the catchment for a specific island address is UNVERIFIED — check it in the SD37 locator (§1).

**Q: What are the float homes in the listings?**
A: Float homes at 3350 Westham Island Road surface in the guide's feed, but they are moorage-tenure product at a marina — not strata or fee-simple townhouses — and are excluded from this file's stock. Their moorage and financing facts are UNVERIFIED (§2.5).

**Q: What should I check before buying an island farm property?**
A: ALR status and zoning for the exact parcel, the ALC residence caps, dike and flood position (Delta is the diking authority), and the bridge's current notices before booking inspections or a move date (§§3, 6 of the research file).

**Q: Can I build a coach house or second home on an island farm?**
A: Delta's SSMUH frame supports accessory units off the ALR, but on ALR land the ALC rules govern first: one additional residence only, capped at 90 m² or 186 m² by parcel size, and never as a strata unit (§3).
