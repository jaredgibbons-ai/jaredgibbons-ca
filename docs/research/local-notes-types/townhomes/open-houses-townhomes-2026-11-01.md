## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The open-house rule — carried verbatim, cited directly.** This file's research DID open the Superintendent's source, so Rule 5-10(3) is cited directly here, not carried from a sibling: Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs (research file §4; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download).

- **The 1-hour offset — expressly NOT importable into this file.** The documented offset feeds in this program (Langley city, Mission city, Tsawwassen, Cloverdale, Ladner, one Foster Martin card on the South Surrey presales page, and the South Surrey duplexes page) each show a card whose top time block sits exactly one hour ahead of the "Open House on…" line beneath it. **This regional page has NO card time block at all, so the offset CANNOT occur here** — there is no second stated time on a card for a description time to disagree with, and those documented offset feeds are NOT imported into this regional file (research file §§1, 4, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html). The weakness on this page is different: the only time is inside description text the card visually truncates. Where the offset IS documented (the city feeds), which figure is authoritative is UNVERIFIED everywhere — confirm any open-house time with the listing brokerage before driving (research file §6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-open-houses.html).
- **Who the host works for.** In BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party — an unrepresented townhome buyer at an open house is exactly that consumer (research file §4; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers).
- **How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication, and any identification or sign-in requirement for visitors: UNVERIFIED** — no BC Gov or BCFSA source states either (research file §4).

**The strata block (applies when the townhome you view is strata — the diligence a buyer runs after the open house).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Restrictions are read off the Form B and registered bylaws, never off listing or open-house copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government strata housing pages: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory or bylaw line reading "rentals not allowed" for a strata townhome is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as void wherever it surfaces; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Which tenure a given open-house townhome is must be established per property; this topic file verifies none.
- **City zoning/OCP and SSMUH/suite rules** are municipal and are stated in the area files for the city the home sits in; no single city frame applies to a region-wide page, and none is stated here (research file §1).

The two-times problem belongs to sibling city feeds, where the top block was exactly one hour later than the line beneath it and neither figure is authoritative — confirm with the listing brokerage (research file §7; https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-open-houses.html).

**Q: Where is the open-house time on this page, then?**
A: Inside the card's description text, which the card visually truncates — the page-1 Langley example ends "OPEN HOUSE: SUNDAY, NOV 1, 2-4 PM." Open the full description, then still confirm before you drive (research file §§1, 6; https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html).

**Q: Who does the agent hosting a townhome open house represent?**
A: The seller, unless you have your own client relationship with a licensee: BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party (research file §7; https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers).

**Q: Does the hosting agent have to give me a disclosure of representation at an open house?**
A: Not for the hosting itself: Rule 5-10(3) provides that disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from you about your motivation, financial qualifications or needs (research file §7; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download).

**Q: So what should I avoid telling the host?**
A: Your motivation, finances or needs — once the host solicits or receives that information, the Rule 5-10(3) exception ends and you are sharing with the seller's side. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent (research file §6; http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download).

**Q: Is a townhome I see at an open house strata or fee-simple — and why does it matter?**
A: It must be checked per property — this feed does not settle tenure. A strata townhome carries Form B diligence, strata fees, a CRF and bylaws; a fee-simple townhome carries none of those — title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only (§3).

**Q: Can I rent out a strata townhome I buy?**
A: A long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void since November 24, 2022 in any BC strata; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Read the registered bylaws in the Form B package for the specific complex (§3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).

**Q: How many open houses are on this page, and how often does it update?**
A: The page's own display statements: 2,747 found, 1,500 displayed, 12 per page, 125 pages. How often the feed updates is UNVERIFIED — no update interval is stated, and SnapStats publishes no open-house category (research file §7; https://jaredgibbons.ca/open-houses.html).

**Q: Can I judge the townhome market from this page?**
A: No — it carries no sourced market figures and is not a market report. Judge a townhome against its own city file's community figures in this program (SnapStats Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined only), not against a site-wide feed (research file §6).
