## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata complex cross-referenced in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** Form B under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, within 7 days, fee capped at $35 plus 25¢/page (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5 or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). Bakerview Estates, the smallest verified Willoughby complex at 10 units, is still over the threshold.
- **CRF.** Minimum 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry for Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace (directory "rentals not allowed" lines, void) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Seven cross-referenced complexes are verified 55+ townhouse stock (Willow Park Estates, Churchill Park, Spencer Green, Chartwell Green, Chelsea Green, Creekside Villa, Huntsfield Green — the last two listing/directory qualifications stated in §2.1); Highgate Terrace's exact age stays UNCONFIRMED (§2.3).

**Fee-simple contrast.** Westbrooke and the Bedford Landing row homes (§2.2) carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. At the top of this band the contrast is material: a fee-simple purchase has no depreciation report and no CRF — the buyer prices roof, envelope and services directly.

The conflict is flagged, not resolved; the bylaw definition is the later source (research file §4). A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing — with worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown).**

- **Worked example 1 — $800,000 resale townhouse, no exemption (inputs: price $800,000).** PTT = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. Minimum down payment = 5% × $500,000 ($25,000) + 10% × $300,000 ($30,000) = **$55,000** (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **Worked example 2 — $1,000,000, no exemption.** PTT = $2,000 + 2% × $800,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. Down payment = $25,000 + 10% × $500,000 ($50,000) = **$75,000**.
- **Worked example 3 — at the ceiling, $1,200,000, no exemption.** PTT = $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Down payment = $25,000 + 10% × $700,000 ($70,000) = **$95,000**. At $1.15M the PTT anchor is **$21,000** ($2,000 + 2% × $950,000). All anchors are the program's standard worked examples on the stated inputs.
- **PTT general rule and exemptions.** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears. First-time buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — the $1.1M–$1.15M window sits inside this band's top end, so a newly built townhouse at $1.12M is a partial-exemption calculation, not a full one (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling carrying March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, March 20, 2025–2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 at $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html; https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained). **Page-defect discipline:** no CMHC premium-range error is documented in this slug's research file, so none is asserted for this slug — the 2.80%–4.00% range documented as a live-page error elsewhere in the program is explicitly NOT asserted here.
