## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this 1981–1986 stock the Form B and depreciation report are the central diligence documents — Rossmoor's published rainscreen renewal is exactly the kind of completed envelope work they should confirm. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Wembley Estates is published at 28 units; the other three complexes' counts are UNCONFIRMED but each is a multi-building complex well past the five-lot threshold on its face — confirm each position from its Form B package. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Forest Hills complex was found in this pass, so there is no in-entry VOID flag to make in Section 2 — Rossmoor's listing copy ("rentals welcome") is consistent with current law, not a special permission, and any "rentals not allowed" line a buyer finds in older bylaw text for these complexes is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No counted Forest Hills complex carries a published 55+ bylaw — all four were checked in the sibling 55-plus file and found family-marketed with no age restriction stated. Age position per complex beyond that: UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple houses (the pocket's other product):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. The co-operatives in Section 2 are a third thing again: membership housing with a share purchase, under neither frame.
- **City zoning — current bylaw:** the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026**, governs; the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is superseded and is never cited for Burnaby in this program. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plan:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Burnaby's community plans are adopted by Council as policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Creek and slope:** Stoney Creek runs through the area toward Burnaby Lake and the homes sit on sloped lots below the Burnaby Mountain Conservation Area — parcel-level setbacks, slope conditions and hazard status are property-specific checks: UNVERIFIED for individual lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Burnaby taxes and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. Burnaby's 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. **Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here** — Forest Hills is in Burnaby, not Vancouver; the 3% tax in the Vancouver files of this batch has no application to a Forest Hills townhouse. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/forest-hills-bn.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, not applicable to the 1981–1986 resale stock here); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Forest Hills townhouse presale is carried in this file, so the frame is stated for completeness only — it governs any future pocket presale and the UniverCity presales up the mountain, which are Simon Fraser Univer. files, not this one. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
