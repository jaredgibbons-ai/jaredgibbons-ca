## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (E15, the one counted strata complex):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Lee Building exclusion in Section 2 shows the limit of this protection: an undivided-interest building has no Form B at all, so the absence of one is itself the first diligence answer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — E15, at 49 or 51 homes on any source, is well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (and in Vancouver are separately governed by City licensing and the provincial principal-residence requirement). No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a corridor townhouse complex appears in Section 2; any such line found on a Form B package here is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No counted complex here carries a published 55+ bylaw in the sources checked, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership complex on the corridor — the corridor's 55+ building (155 East 37th Avenue) is social housing rental. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the side-street house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning:** the residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The district schedule for the Main Street commercial frontage itself: UNVERIFIED in the research file. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — the Broadway Plan:** the Plan covers the corridor's north end (Clark Drive to Vine Street, 1st to 16th Avenues), was approved June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for buildings up to 20–30 storeys near the future Mount Pleasant Station, with renter protections and tenant relocation requirements that matter on any older rental-building purchase. The Plan is policy, not zoning — most sites still need rezoning. South of 16th Avenue the corridor runs on citywide direction from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Flood:** the corridor's north end touches the former False Creek shoreline lowland, where the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain and parcels carry Building By-law flood standards; the residential grid south of 2nd Avenue sits on the Mount Pleasant slope. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3%:** Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a live holding cost for investors in the corridor's older stock. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example an M6 presale), not resales. City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for the M6 presale in Section 2 and for future Broadway Plan presales around Mount Pleasant Station. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the owner-approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in the research file or found in the sibling files checked — explicitly, none is found, so there is no Jerry sweep item from this file.
