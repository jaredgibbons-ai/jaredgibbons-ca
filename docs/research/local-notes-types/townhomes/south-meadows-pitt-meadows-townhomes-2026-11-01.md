## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the cluster complexes, wherever filed):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF minimum:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every cluster complex is over the threshold on its published form. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID — and Pitt Meadows separately PROHIBITS short-term rentals:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid, which is why Coho II's directory "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line is flagged VOID for long-term rentals in-entry in Section 2. The provincial voiding does NOT create a short-term rental right here: short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 (via the sibling condos file)
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex documented in Section 2 carries a verified age restriction. The Fairways (North Meadows) age position is UNCONFIRMED program-wide and is never called 55+ in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the Bonson Road detached streets):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. There is no verified fee-simple townhouse complex in this sub-area; the fee-simple stock here is detached houses. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **City zoning / OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones (RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha, residential buildings sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches; R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, R-6 up to five, or six where adaptable-housing requirements are met near frequent transit); RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha; RM-2 (townhouse and low-density apartment) minimum lot area 3,035 sq m. The 1,215 sq m figure in R-3/R-4/R-6 is a subdivision minimum only and does not apply to development on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. The governing plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505%2C%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf

One secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The MLS name and the City name are different places on paper.** "South Meadows" exists only as a Paragon sub-area label (and as the MLS filing for the Osprey Village cluster); the City calls the same ground "South Bonson." A buyer searching City documents, permits or the OCP for "South Meadows" will find nothing — search "South Bonson" and "Osprey Village" instead. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use
- **The floodplain figure has a stale twin.** Over 95% (City 2020 Business Plan) governs; an older City flood-mitigation release from the superseded OCP era stated approximately 86%, figures around 85% still circulate on live pages, and the research file treats the current City figure as governing. These notes carry 95% and flag the conflict wherever the area is written up. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **The dike is the park.** Shoreline Park, 51 Bonson Road, and the dyke-top trails run on the Fraser River dike itself, and Harris Landing / Pitt River Regional Greenway at Harris Road and Fraser Way is a Metro Vancouver regional greenway with a first phase of 10.2 km along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes — the recreation is genuine, and the structure underneath it is flood infrastructure first. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- **The recreation cluster is inside the townhouse pocket, the schools are not.** Bonson Park (11546 Bonson Road, disc golf), Pitt Meadows Athletic Park (11431 Bonson Road), the arena complex and the South Bonson Community Centre / Pitt Meadows Art Gallery (10932 Barnston View Road) all sit in or beside the pocket — while the nearest elementary schools (Edith McDermott, Pitt Meadows Elementary) are north of it. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-and-facilities-list
- **The west edge is a business park, not future condos.** The Golden Ears Business Park is being built out in phases by Onni Group in the south Bonson / south Harris Road area per City material, and the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project includes a new four-lane underpass below the CPKC crossing at Harris Road — employment and construction context for the sub-area's west side, not residential supply. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/news/media-releases/council-supports-business-park-land-use
- **Most of the city is farm reserve; this pocket is the exception.** The City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve — the urban South Bonson pocket itself is developed residential / village land, and ALR status at the sub-area's edges is a per-address check on Agricultural Land Commission maps. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **The nearest hospital and library are both outside the sub-area.** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge (Fraser Health, 24/7 emergency) serves Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows; the city's only library branch is Pitt Meadows Public Library (FVRL), 200-12099 Harris Road, in Central Meadows. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library
