## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The ALR block governs first on farm parcels — it is the reason this file has no complexes.**

- **ALR stratification bar.** ALR residences cannot be stratified: depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR and needs ALC permission — ALC Information Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1). There is no Form B, no strata council and no depreciation report for a farm home — diligence runs through title, the ALC record, City zoning and BC Assessment farm class instead (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf; research file §4).
- **ALR residence caps.** One principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²** (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted **only within the principal residence**; at most one additional residence of up to **90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**. Local government bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; research file §4).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — full three-tier form.** Farm classification is a voluntary program; the income thresholds are **$2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**. A flat "$2,500+" figure is an ERROR wherever it appears. Applications must be submitted by **October 31** for the next assessment and tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase (https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification; research file §4).
- **Quota and supply management:** UNVERIFIED in every particular in this file — whether any quota is included on a specific South Surrey farm, and any marketing-board transfer, approval or barn-standard rule. No marketing-board or CFIA source was opened. Get any quota position in the contract, in writing, and verify it with the applicable marketing board directly (research file §4).
- **Wells, septic and water licensing:** UNVERIFIED as stated rules in this file. The live page's account (GWELLS well records; domestic groundwater use not needing a licence while irrigation and other non-domestic use does under the Water Sustainability Act; Fraser Health Authorized Person rules for onsite systems) is site copy only — no Province or Fraser Health source was opened to confirm it (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-farms.html).
- **MHPTA frame (where parks are touched in §2.6).** Manufactured-home pad tenancies sit under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata law: pad-rent increases are capped at the provincial limit — **2026 = 2.3% + a proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + a proportional amount** — with at least three full months' written notice, once every 12 months. **No pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file, ever** (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).

**The strata block (applies to the urban townhouse stock cross-referenced in §2.4, in its count homes).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A farm parcel has no Form B (§3 ALR block) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term tenancies; any directory "rentals not allowed" line for an urban complex is flagged VOID in that complex's own file. **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). No farm-parcel application: ALR dwellings are not strata (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. Farm residences are freehold dwellings of this kind, but they are not townhouse complexes (§2.2).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Can I buy a townhome on a South Surrey farm?**
A: No — not as strata, and no fee-simple townhouse complex on farm land verifies either. This file counts 0 townhouse complexes on farm/ALR land: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision in the ALR needing ALC permission, so farm housing is freehold — one principal residence (500 m² or less) plus at most one small additional residence per parcel. Townhome product under the South Surrey name is urban stock counted in its count homes (§§2.1–2.4).

**Q: Where is South Surrey farm land, according to the live page?**
A: The live page places it mostly on the east side around Hazelmere near 16 Avenue and 184 Street and along the Little Campbell River, and in the Serpentine and Nicomekl lowlands toward Mud Bay — recorded as the page's site copy (§1; research file §7).

**Q: How big can a house be on ALR farmland in South Surrey?**
A: One principal residence per parcel without an ALC application, at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; anything larger requires ALC approval, and the City may regulate further (§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Is a second residence permitted on a South Surrey ALR acreage?**
A: At most one additional residence — up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha — and local government bylaws must also permit it (§3; research file §7).

**Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building on a South Surrey farm?**
A: No — a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. A detached second dwelling is the "additional residence", size-capped at 90 m² / 186 m². On a non-ALR urban Surrey lot the suite cap is a different rule again: 40% of the principal habitable area (§3).

**Q: What income must a farm operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: BC Assessment's three-tier thresholds: $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation; and $10,000 for an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next tax year, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" figure is an error (§3; research file §7).

**Q: Are the South Surrey market numbers in this file farm prices?**
A: No. SnapStats publishes no farm category; the §1 figures are S Surrey White Rock residential context only (Detached median $1,628,000; Condos & Townhomes median $700,000 in September 2026, combined splits — never townhome-only), and South Surrey farm counts, prices and per-acre values are UNVERIFIED (§1).

**Q: Is quota included when I buy a South Surrey farm?**
A: Only if the contract states it — quota inclusion and every marketing-board transfer or approval rule are UNVERIFIED in this file. Get the position in writing and verify it with the applicable marketing board directly; do not rely on a listing's wording alone, and never present a feed card as a confirmed operation in a named sector (§3).

**Q: Can Surrey's SSMUH rules add units to my farm parcel?**
A: No. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; on ALR land the ALC residence caps govern how many dwellings a parcel may have. Do not value an edge lot on urban SSMUH density (§3).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The farms feed is broader than farms.** Cards read for the research file include a nursery/landscape operation, estate/residential acreages and rezoning/land-banking holdings in Elgin Chantrell, Morgan Creek and Grandview Surrey alongside working-farm parcels in Hazelmere and Pacific Douglas — the page title's "Farms" scope and its feed do not match, a mismatch flagged in the research file for Jared/Jerry. A buyer who treats every card as a working farm misreads the feed (§1).
- **The suite rule flips at the ALR line.** On an urban Surrey lot a suite is capped at 40% of the principal habitable area under City rules; on ALR land a suite exists only inside the principal residence, and the detached second dwelling is a separate, size-capped "additional residence" (90 m² / 186 m²). The same street can carry both regimes parcel by parcel (§3).
- **The 40-hectare line more than doubles the second-home cap.** The additional-residence limit is 90 m² at 40 ha or less and 186 m² only above 40 ha — most South Surrey acreages a buyer tours will sit on the 90 m² side of that line (§3).
- **Farm class is an annual income test with a hard date.** The thresholds scale with operation size ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000) and the application deadline is October 31 — a buyer who plans to keep farming must file the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase, or the classification position for the next tax year is at risk (§3).
- **The ALC ceiling is not a permit.** The ALC rules cap what may exist; the City of Surrey may further regulate or restrict ALR residences through zoning, and anything beyond the permitted number or size needs ALC approval. Both layers must clear before a residence plan is priced into an offer (§3).
- **Price a South Surrey farm from its parts, not from a farm statistic — there is none.** No published South Surrey farm price or per-acre figure exists in this file, and the S Surrey White Rock residential figures are board context only. A valuation stands on the specific land, buildings, residence and any quota included — and the quota position is UNVERIFIED here until the contract and the marketing board say otherwise (§§1, 3).
