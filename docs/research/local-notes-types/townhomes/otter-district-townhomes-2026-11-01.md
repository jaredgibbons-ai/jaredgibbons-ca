## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block — stated for completeness; it governs no counted complex here, because there is none.**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. No Otter District purchase in §2 involves a Form B — the acreages are freehold and the park is pad-rental (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Neither applies to any stock counted or documented in §2 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — no directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried in this file for any Otter District complex, so there is no in-entry line to flag; the rule is stated so a line surfacing in a future listing is read as void for long-term rentals on sight. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Otter District property carries a verified age bylaw — the district's one park states the opposite ("no age restrictions," §2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast — the district's acreages ARE the fee-simple case.** A fee-simple property carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants only. A park home is different again: ownership of the home on a rented pad under the MHPTA (pad-rent increase limits: 2026 = 2.3% + proportional amount; 2027 = 2.2% + proportional amount — stated as the regulated formula only; no pad dollar figure is published in this file).

**Township of Langley zoning, ALR, SSMUH and servicing — Township rules apply; Langley City rules do NOT.**

- **ALR frame — the governing fact for much of this district:** on ALR land, one principal residence is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill); a secondary suite is permitted only when entirely contained within the principal residence (a separate or detached dwelling is not a secondary suite) and local bylaws permit it; and one additional detached residence is permitted at up to 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Two first-page listings state ALR status in their own text (the South Otter Schoolhouse property "within the ALR"; a 5.45-acre parcel "located in the ALR and zoned RU-2") — listing copy for those parcels only, not a district-wide designation. **Parcel-level ALR status for any specific Otter District property is UNVERIFIED** — confirm on the ALC's ALR maps (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).
- **Zoning:** rural homes in this part of the Township sit under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, and the feed's own listings name RU-2 zoning for acreage parcels; zone assignments for a specific parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/).
- **SSMUH (Township standing correction):** Zoning Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — **NO 6-plex tier exists in it**. Township eligibility for SSMUH requires the property to be within the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, not larger than 4,050 m² and not in a zone with a minimum lot size larger than 4,050 m²; properties that do not meet those criteria are instead eligible for one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — **the frame most rural Otter acreages will fall under**. Do not import the urban SSMUH numbers onto an acreage. Secondary suite cap: 120 m² (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **Coach house (Township definition, settled in this program):** in Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is a fee-simple second unit — an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; never strata, never a townhouse complex. A ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit with different rules. **Flagged, resolved nowhere (live Township conflict):** s. 102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. No coach-house size cap appears in s. 100 as read in the program's rules-gaps pass, so no cap is stated (research file §4).

**Q: Can I build a second house on an Otter District acreage in the ALR?**
A: Within the ALC frame, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² on a property of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on a property over 40 ha, provided there is only one existing residence when construction begins and Township bylaws allow it; the principal residence itself is capped at 500 m², and a secondary suite must be entirely inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first — parcel status is UNVERIFIED in this file (§3; research file §7).

**Q: Can I strata-title the second home later?**
A: Not on ALR land within the ALC frame: ALR residences cannot be stratified — a strata plan deposit is a subdivision (SPA s. 239) and subdivision of agricultural land is prohibited unless the Commission permits it (ALCA s. 21(1); ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6) (§3).

**Q: Is Langley Grove Estates a 55+ park, or strata townhouses?**
A: Neither. It is a pad-rental manufactured-home park (leasehold not prepaid-non-strata title in listings) whose listings state "no age restrictions." You would own the home and rent the pad under the MHPTA — no strata corporation, no Form B — and no pad dollar figure is published in this file (§2.4, §2.6).

**Q: Can I put 3–4 SSMUH units on an Otter acreage?**
A: Almost never on this ground: Township SSMUH eligibility requires Urban Containment Boundary location, municipal water and sewer, and a lot no larger than 4,050 m². A rural Otter lot that fails those tests falls to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning — and Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier in any case (§3).

**Q: What is a coach house in Langley Township?**
A: Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a coach house is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit, never strata. Whether any size cap applies was not confirmed in the bylaw sections read, and the townhouse-suite conflict on s. 102 is flagged in §3, resolved nowhere (research file §7).
