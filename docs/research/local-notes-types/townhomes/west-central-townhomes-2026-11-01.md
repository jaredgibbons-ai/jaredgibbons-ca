## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold (neither counted complex's unit count is published, so which side of the threshold each sits on is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan is read — both present as multi-unit complexes with management and contingency records). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund — the "strong contingency" line in the Sherwood Park listing is read against that floor on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a West Central complex is void for long-term rentals (no such line was found for the two counted complexes in the sources read). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither counted complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read — and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses anywhere in the city in this program. Any lesser age label is VOID as an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in West Central (Section 2) — a listing calling Sherwood Park or Maple Villas "fee simple" contradicts the strata-fee record and needs a title check.
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; the standard house zone is RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%). Maple Villas' listing records zoning RM-1 for that complex. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)
- **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted:** the program's research files cite the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan two ways (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). This file asserts neither; quote planning policy for a specific complex from a current City document. (Condos siblings on disk.)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED:** the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026 per the City record (Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021; the corridor ends at 221 St), directing growth around future BRT stations along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road — Dewdney Trunk Road is the counted complexes' own street. The earlier contrary reading in this program is noted in Section 5; confirm a specific parcel's designation in the City's current document before valuing on it. (Guide condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/west-central-maple-ridge-guide-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. SSMUH affects the detached lots around the complexes, not the strata lots inside them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/west-central.md)

- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales (both counted complexes are resale stock). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted). Worked tier example, on a $700,000 resale townhouse: $200,000 × 1% = $2,000, plus $500,000 × 2% = $10,000, total $12,000 before any exemption. First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
