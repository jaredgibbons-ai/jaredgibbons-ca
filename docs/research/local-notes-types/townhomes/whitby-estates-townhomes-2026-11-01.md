## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted pockets are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Highgrove's scale (18 concrete terrace homes with private elevators) and Aerie II's (8 homes), the Form B and depreciation report are where envelope, elevator and landscaping costs show — read them before the view. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — both Whitby pockets (8 and 18 lots) sit above it, so each strata's current report is a live diligence item. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No counted complex in §2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line — both pockets' restrictions are UNCONFIRMED — so the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a listing is read correctly on sight. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Whitby Estates strata holds a verified 55+ bylaw — the 55-plus pass verifies zero 55+ ownership in the pocket. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Whitby Estates (§2). A fee-simple townhome, where one exists elsewhere, carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Whitby's detached houses are freehold, but they carry the British Properties building-scheme covenants (§1) on top of District rules — freehold here does not mean unregulated.
- **District plans and zoning (West Vancouver's own bylaws only):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 specifies how land can be used and regulates intensity, height, density, setbacks and lot coverage; the Official Community Plan is OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, which treats the British Properties as an established existing neighbourhood and directs growth to the centres. Wildfire Hazard DP (within 100 m of undisturbed forest interface) and Environmental DP (within 15 m of a watercourse bank) apply on this slope (§1). https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/whitby-estates.md)
- **West Vancouver property taxes:** for 2026, due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; roughly half the bill is collected for other taxing authorities, and water/sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly.
