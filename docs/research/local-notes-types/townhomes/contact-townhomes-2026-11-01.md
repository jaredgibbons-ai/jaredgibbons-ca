## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The regulator's framework applies from the first message:** in BC, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party. Disclosure must happen at the outset of dealings, and dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. Practical consequence: until a client relationship is formed, an enquirer is a non-client — a first call or text starts the disclosure conversation, it is not yet the relationship itself. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **The tenure fork — the core townhome diligence item:** a STRATA townhome is bought with a Form B package, strata fees, registered bylaws and a depreciation report; a FEE-SIMPLE townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead, stated plainly. Tenure traps are excluded from townhome stock wherever they appear, with the reason stated: leasehold (including TFN, City ground leases and UniverCity SFU), corporation shares, undivided interest, co-ops, life leases and non-market rental are not strata or fee-simple townhouse ownership, and pad-rental parks are NEVER townhouse stock even when a form field says so. Establish which form a specific unit is in the first conversation.
- **Form B (strata townhomes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid, so any directory "rentals not allowed" line a townhome buyer is shown is void for long-term rentals and must be flagged in-entry; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City rules are city-by-city:** there is no single plan for the footprint — each municipality in the Areas Served list plans through its own Official Community Plan and zoning bylaw, and SSMUH / secondary-suite rules are as each city states them (for example, Delta: OCP Bylaw 8400 (2024) with no lettered schedules, zoning number CONTESTED, SSMUH Bylaws 8394/8395; Township of Langley: SSMUH Bylaw 6020 with NO 6-plex tier and a 120 m² suite cap). A townhome buyer's plan question is answered for the specific city and parcel, not from this page.
- **GST rebates:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, with the tier flag:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000 and 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2%, and worked examples must show the tier math. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhome enquiry is about a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

For a fee-simple townhome there is no Form B; the package is title, municipal rules and any covenants/HOA documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
