## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The BC strata rules below are the frame a buyer uses here in practice — not because a Cliff Drive townhouse is verified, but because the nearest strata purchases a Cliff Drive search actually lands on sit in the neighbouring sub-areas, and because any future strata townhouse in the pocket would be governed by exactly these rules.

- **Form B diligence (strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a building a Cliff Drive buyer ends up considering in a neighbouring sub-area is void for long-term rentals and must be flagged in-entry wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No complex in this pocket is published as age-restricted, because no townhouse complex in this pocket is verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. That is the tenure of the pocket's confirmed detached and duplex stock, not of any named townhouse complex here.
- **Delta OCP:** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future," adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan, NO lettered schedules; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." There is NO Tsawwassen Area Plan schedule in Bylaw 8400 — the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the superseded OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and citing "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an error wherever it is met. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning number — CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** sources conflict (City pages cited in the research file use Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, against a competing "8400, 2025" citation in standing corrections). This file asserts neither as settled; Tsawwassen lots sit under the RS and RD zones assigned parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/cliff-drive.md)
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (June 24, 2024) — Delta's updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots where the zoning applies; that, not a townhouse complex, is this pocket's density story. Secondary-suite rules are as the City states them on its housing pages. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD2:** the OCP's hazard DPAs in Tsawwassen include SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment), immediately west of this pocket. Which specific Cliff Drive parcels fall inside SD2: UNVERIFIED — read parcel by parcel off the OCP DPA maps. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Flood frame:** the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT + exemptions, with the tier flag:** general PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000 and 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of that 3% + 2%, not a separate rate, and worked examples must show the tier math. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST rebates:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, for agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a presale exists, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). No presale townhouse project in the pocket was verified. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here:** that tax is 3% and Vancouver ONLY — Delta has no equivalent stated in any source checked for this file.
