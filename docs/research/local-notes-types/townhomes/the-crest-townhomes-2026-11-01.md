## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this wood-frame townhouse stock (Deerfield is expressly a wood-frame development) the envelope, roofing and drainage cycles in the depreciation report are the diligence centre. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing/directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt — all three named complexes are above the threshold on their published/listing descriptions (Deerfield expressly at 105 units; the other two are multi-building complexes whose exact counts are UNCONFIRMED but plainly over five lots on their listing form). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No complex in Section 2 published a "rentals not allowed" line in the sources checked (Deerfield's directory line is rentals allowed with restrictions; Chancery Lane's is rentals allowed) — if such a line surfaces in older bylaw text for any Crest complex, it is void for long-term rentals and must be flagged in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Section 2 complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws are UNCONFIRMED per complex, and the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in The Crest (Derby Manor is 55+ rental, a different tenure entirely). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026) and registered covenants/HOA documents only. The sub-area's detached houses are fee-simple houses, not fee-simple townhouse complexes.
- **City zoning and plan — Burnaby's current pair:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026; the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is repealed — do not cite it. City-wide direction is the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, which replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Edmonds Community Plan covers the town centre plan area west of The Crest. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **SSMUH / suite rules as the City states them:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. That last point is the future townhouse-form supply line for Crest house lots — a permitted form today, not a counted complex. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **Burnaby taxes and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water (detached single-family water charge $652.33), annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date; for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/the-crest.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, relevant to any new stratified multiplex product built on Crest R1 lots, not to the Section 2 resales); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. The Crest stock in Section 2 is resale, so 5% GST is not in a typical purchase here. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply here — it is a City of Vancouver tax only, and this is Burnaby. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust.
