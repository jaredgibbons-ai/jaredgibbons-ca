## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern any strata purchase a Deer Lake Place buyer ends up making in a neighbouring sub-area, and because they are the rules sellers of the area's future stratified multiplex lots will sell under. They do not create townhouse stock in Deer Lake Place itself.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption matters here, because a small stratified multiplex on a Deer Lake Place R1 lot can fall under the five-lot line. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Deer Lake Place complex exists to flag, because no complex is verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No 55+ complex is verified in Deer Lake Place (see the sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** the pocket's houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **Burnaby zoning:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. The low-density lots around the pocket sit in Burnaby's R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District — Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into R1 in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m². https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans and hazards:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; community plans are adopted by Council as policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. The pocket sits at the west end of Deer Lake beside its shoreline wetlands, so parcel-level streamside setbacks, floodplain and hazard status are property-specific checks: UNVERIFIED for individual lots; ALR status for a specific property is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deer-lake-place.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. The 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee; the detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/deer-lake-place.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
