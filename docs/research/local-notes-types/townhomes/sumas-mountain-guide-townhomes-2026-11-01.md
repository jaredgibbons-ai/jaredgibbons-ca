## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Sumas Mountain rises between Abbotsford and Chilliwack, above Sumas Prairie to the south and the Fraser River lowland to the north. The built part is inside the City of Abbotsford; the upper and eastern rural part is unincorporated Fraser Valley Regional District (FVRD) land. http://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/sumas-mountain.html
- City-side neighbourhoods on the west and south slopes include Auguston, McKee and the Eagle Mountain side; Clayburn Village sits at the northwest foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- Main roads are Sumas Mountain Road (becoming Upper Sumas Mountain Road toward the regional park), McKee Road, Dawson Road and Whatcom Road, connecting down to Highway 1 at the Whatcom Road interchange at the south foot. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/new-park-dianne-brook-ave
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: at the foot and on the lower slopes, in the built Auguston / McKee neighbourhoods and on Lower Sumas Mountain Road — not on the upper or FVRD part of the mountain, where the housing is rural homes on large lots. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- Schools on the slopes: Auguston Traditional Elementary, 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive; Mountain Elementary, 2299 Mountain Drive; Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary, 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive. No secondary school is on the mountain, and catchments split by slope (Section 4). https://mountain.abbyschools.ca/schoolplan/
- Transit: BC Transit routes 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee) and 15 (Auguston) serve the built slopes and the foot. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=7
- Market context, as the research file states it: SnapStats recorded attached (condos & townhomes combined) inventory of 0 and solds of 0 for Sumas Mountain in September 2026, and only 3 detached listings with 0 sales — SnapStats publishes no townhome-only figure, and none is invented here. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

All three verified complexes are strata, so the strata block applies; the mountain's two-government split is the local complication.

- **Form B (Strata Property Act, s.59)** — the disclosure certificate for any strata lot here (townhouse or bare-land): monthly fee, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, proceedings, plus an insurance summary required since April 1, 2023. In a bare-land strata like Straiton, the Form B covers common-property obligations (roads, gate, landscaping), not a shared building. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports** — stratas with 5+ lots must obtain one and renew at least every five years. **CRF minimum** — at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the CRF at each AGM budget since November 1, 2023. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental bylaws VOID** since November 24, 2022 (long-term); short-term rentals may still be restricted. Flagged in-entry: King's Gate's complex data records rentals as not allowed, and a Junction Creek directory variant records "rentals allowed w/restrictions" — neither line restores a long-term rental ban. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2)** — the only permitted age bylaw is 55+, with the May 1, 2023 expanded exemptions (younger spouses/partners, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). No mountain townhouse complex above is recorded as 55+ in any source checked: King's Gate is expressly NOT age-restricted, and no age restriction appears for Sage at Auguston or Junction Creek — age status for Junction Creek: UNCONFIRMED beyond that absence. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhouses — none verified here, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus covenants/HOA obligations on title only. Most mountain houses are fee-simple freehold, but they are detached houses, not fee-simple townhouse complexes, and none is counted as one.
- **Two governments, two rule books — plan correction applied.** City side: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 (Country Residential minimum lot 2.0 ha, Suburban Residential 0.4 ha, Rural Residential 4.0 ha) and the CURRENT city plan, Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025) — the research file's "OCP Bylaw No. 2600-2016" wording is STALE; apply Abbotsforward 2050 and flag the stale number. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) is OCP Schedule E for the west slope. Rural side: the FVRD Sumas Mountain Official Community Plan, Consolidated OCP Bylaw No. 584, with no public sewer or water at rural density (wells and septic). https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **SSMUH — Abbotsford bylaw number UNCONFIRMED, never assert "3560-2024":** sibling files carry that number from secondary sources; this file does not assert it. Confirmed Abbotsford figures that affect a house lot a townhouse buyer might cross-shop: one secondary suite in single detached dwellings under s.140.3.8, the 2025 suite maximum of less than 50% of the principal unit, and coach/garden suites capped at 15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m² (Abbotsford ONLY). SSMUH rules for the rural FVRD part of the mountain: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/sumas-mountain-guide.md, UNVERIFIED list)
- **Steep-slope control.** The City OCP includes a Steep Slope Development Permit Area, and the McKee plan protects unstable geotechnical areas and slopes over 40% as open space — the practical reason new multi-family form here is planned as ground-oriented product at the foot, not buildings on the slopes. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Tax split on the mountain:** City-side taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty July 3, second 5% September 16, 2026). Rural FVRD-side property is taxed by the Province (Surveyor of Taxes), due July 2 under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, with 5% on any portion unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on any portion unpaid on October 31. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **GST — FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Follow March 13 over any sibling file stating March 12. The mountain's townhouse stock is resale, so GST generally does not apply here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — New Housing Rebate:** 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **PTT:** first-time buyer exemption on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales — REDMA.** No presale project on the mountain itself was verified — the McKee-foot proposal in Section 2 is a rezoning-stage proposal, not a presale. Where a presale exists, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission right and s.18 requires deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP $60,000 / FHSA $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime / CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%.** https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there actually townhouses on Sumas Mountain, or is it all houses?** There are three verified townhouse strata complexes: Junction Creek (Lower Sumas Mountain Road), Sage at Auguston (4401 Blauson Blvd, 120 units) and King's Gate (36260 McKee Road, 67 units). Everything else named on the mountain is detached houses, rural lots, or the Straiton bare-land strata. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: SnapStats showed zero attached listings — does that mean I can't buy one?** No. It means none was listed in that one month (September 2026) in a sub-area that also recorded only 3 detached listings and 0 sales of any kind. Thin supply, not no supply — expect to wait for a listing in one of the three complexes rather than to comparison-shop across many. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/sumas-mountain-guide.md, §2)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata, and how is Straiton different from a townhouse?** You own your detached house and lot as a strata lot and share common property (roads, gate, landscaping) with the strata corporation — the City report records 26 single-family strata lots in EPS1299 at 4595 Sumas Mountain Road. Fees, bylaws and a Form B apply exactly as in a townhouse strata, but there is no shared building envelope and no townhouse unit. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/45965/
- **Q: Are any of the mountain townhouse complexes 55+?** None is recorded as 55+ in any source checked. King's Gate's complex data expressly records "Age Restrictions: No," and no age restriction appears for Sage at Auguston (a family community beside Auguston Traditional Elementary) or Junction Creek. Gated is not 55+. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/sumas-mountain-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Directory/listing-stated only: Junction Creek's directory records "pets allowed, pets allowed w/rest."; King's Gate's complex data records pets allowed with restrictions and a listing states pets welcome; Sage at Auguston listings state combination cat/dog rules that vary by listing. Confirm the exact number/size rules in the filed bylaws attached to the Form B. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Can I rent out a townhouse on the mountain?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since November 24, 2022 — including the "rentals not allowed" line in King's Gate's complex data. A strata may still restrict short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What will strata fees cover in a bare-land strata vs a townhouse strata?** Only what that strata's budget covers — in a bare-land strata typically common roads, gates, landscaping and insurance on common property, not your house's roof or exterior; in a townhouse strata, building-envelope items are normally the strata's. The specific inclusions for Straiton, Junction Creek, Sage or King's Gate are in their budgets/Form B: UNCONFIRMED from public sources.
- **Q: Which schools — and why do neighbours get different answers?** Because the mountain splits. Locator runs returned Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary for 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive, but Irene Kelleher Totí:ltawtxw Elementary → W.A. Fraser Middle → Yale Secondary for 2540 Eagle Mountain Drive. Run the exact address. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q: Is the mountain in the floodplain?** The mountain itself is above the floodplain; the floodplain land is at its foot in Sumas Prairie, named in City flood notices. A slope lot's issue is geotechnical, not flood: the OCP's Steep Slope Development Permit Area applies on slope land. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie
- **Q: City water/sewer or well/septic for a townhouse?** The three townhouse complexes are in the built City neighbourhoods; the well-and-septic world is the rural FVRD part, which has no public sewer or water at rural density. Confirm servicing for any specific non-complex lot before assuming a townhouse-style purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The next townhouses on the mountain are planned at its foot, in one proposal.** The City's July 10, 2025 council-consideration notice for Bylaw No. 3761-2025 (file PRJ20-108) describes a McKee Road lands proposal of approximately 108 single detached lots, 192 townhouses and four rural residential lots, with about 5 ha of park/open-space land. It is a proposal — not built, not a presale, and not counted in Section 2. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2025-07/2025-07-10%20City%20Page.pdf
- **The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023)** is Schedule E of the City OCP and guides the west-slope mountainside neighbourhood; its Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation is where townhome/multiplex forms are planned. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Two tax systems share one mountain.** City-side lots pay City of Abbotsford taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3 and a second 5% September 16, 2026), while rural FVRD lots pay the Province through the Surveyor of Taxes (due July 2; 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after October 31) — the penalty calendar for a rural cross-shop differs from the City one a townhouse buyer will actually face. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Abbotsford's current plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025).** Any Sumas Mountain page still citing OCP Bylaw "2600-2016" as current is carrying a stale plan reference — a defect to flag for Jerry, sourced to this area's own research file, which carries the 2016 number in its plan wording. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/sumas-mountain-guide.md, §4)
