## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: District of West Vancouver — a single district municipality on the North Shore, between the Capilano River and Horseshoe Bay, under its Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018, and Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The District's heart, per the OCP: the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, with retail, service, restaurants, arts and culture spaces, offices, civic services and waterfront recreation; the Ambleside Local Area Plan boundary runs south of Inglewood Avenue between 13th and 23rd Streets. Park Royal, at Marine Drive and Taylor Way on the District's east edge, is recognised in the OCP as the regional shopping centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/23apr19.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Sub-area names in the master research set for the District: Altamont, Ambleside, Bayridge, British Properties, Canterbury, Caulfeild, Cedardale, Chartwell, Chelsea Park, Cypress, Cypress Park Estates, Dundarave, Sentinel Hill and West Bay. Several are site/listing names not confirmed as official District neighbourhood names — the District's own plan language works through Ambleside, Dundarave and Horseshoe Bay as its village/centre areas. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Main roads and transit: Marine Drive is the District's east–west main road and the Lions Gate Bridge at its east end connects West Vancouver to downtown Vancouver; Highway 1 (Upper Levels) crosses the District's upper bench to Horseshoe Bay. Route 250 runs Downtown Vancouver via Georgia Street, the Stanley Park Causeway and the Lions Gate Bridge along Marine Drive to Dundarave, with some trips continuing to Horseshoe Bay Ferry Terminal; routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed the neighbourhoods into Park Royal exchange. There is no rapid transit in the District. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- Tenure note for the east edge: part of the Park Royal area sits on Squamish Nation land (Capilano IR No. 5) under the Park Royal Lease (indenture dated April 1, 1974) — waterfront and reserve-adjacent parcels on the east side need a tenure check per property. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- Schools: School District 45 serves the District (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12, no middle tier) and catchments are address-based through the district locator. The verified sample in the research file: 1329 Duchess Avenue returns Hollyburn Elementary (1329 Duchess Avenue) and West Vancouver Secondary (1750 Mathers Avenue); Irwin Park Elementary is at 2455 Haywood Avenue. Run any complex's exact address — do not assume from the nearest school. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The governing pair is West Vancouver's own:** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 (the OCP was amended again December 1, 2025 by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, aligning it with the 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report — cite the current consolidated OCP). White Rock's 2506/2220 pair is never attributed to West Vancouver. Apartment areas in Ambleside are zoned RM1 and RM2, and Form and Character Development Permit guidelines apply in the Ambleside Apartment Area and the Ambleside Village Centre. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **Form B diligence (all 5 assembled strata entries):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Caulfeild Cove (built 1974), the depreciation report is the first-order document; at any Park Royal address, the Form B sits behind the title tenure check. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — the 3-lot Bellevue townhome component sits inside its project's larger strata, so the project-level report governs; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Caulfeild Cove's directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). West Vancouver's three verified 55+ strata buildings (Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens) are apartment buildings in Ambleside/Dundarave — none is a townhouse, and none is counted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex verifies District-wide, so the fee-simple rule is stated as the contrast a buyer will meet in the detached stock instead: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662: RS minimum site areas from 488 m² in RS5 to 8,094 m² in RS1; secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses) plus registered covenants only. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Ground-oriented density:** the bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop — the mechanism that enables small ground-oriented strata product, though few named complexes verify. A July 21, 2025 provincial ministerial directive requires increased density in the Park Royal–Taylor Way area and in single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Foreshore and hazards:** a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area, new habitable space must be built to the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding, work within 15 metres of the natural boundary must follow the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines, and an Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank — live issues for the Bellevue Avenue waterfront entries. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits
- **Buyer frame:** PTT + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. Presales (Rodgers Creek, when marketed): REDMA s. 21 7-day rescission + s. 18 deposits in trust. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply in West Vancouver and is omitted. District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% penalty July 2, further 5% September 2), and the additional school tax (0.2% on $3M–$4M, 0.4% above $4M for 2026) can apply at District price points. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are West Vancouver townhouses, actually?** In five verified pockets, not spread across the District: Whitby Estates (Aerie II, Highgrove), Caulfeild (Caulfeild Cove), and as small components inside two waterfront/bench projects (3 townhomes at The Bellevue by Cressey in Dundarave, 7 podium townhouses at Hawksley in the British Properties/Uplands). Ambleside, Altamont, Chartwell outside Whitby, Chelsea Park and Bayridge verify zero. (Section 2, home files on disk.)
- **Q: Are there any fee-simple townhouses in West Vancouver?** None verified in any covered sub-area — District attached ownership stock is strata where it exists. A "non-strata townhouse" claim for a District address is UNCONFIRMED until the title is read. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is the big pink waterfront building a condo I can buy?** No. Villa Maris is purpose-built rental per the District's 2024 Statement of Heritage Value — single-owner rental since 1965, nothing in the existing building for sale, and its redevelopment concept is preliminary and unsubmitted. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf
- **Q: Is Executive on the Park's townhouse component buyable strata stock?** Not verified as such: the project mixes 64 condominiums, 4 townhomes and 20 rental apartments per the presale source, and the home file records completion/occupancy as strata stock UNCONFIRMED. Read the disclosure statement and title before treating any component as completed strata. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/executive-on-the-park-west-vancouver-presale-condos-townhomes-by-executive-group-development/
- **Q: What is different about the Whitby Estates townhouses?** Scale and setting: 8 residences at Aerie II (built 2010) and 18 terrace homes at Highgrove (completed December 2013, roughly 3,282–3,569 sq ft, concrete, private garages, exclusive elevators) — strata title inside a detached estate subdivision, so strata fees and bylaws apply where a neighbouring detached house is freehold. https://storage.ubertor.com/wendytianying.ourubertor.com/content/featuresheet_35_document/94170.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Caulfeild Cove townhouse despite the directory saying no?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: the directory's "rentals not allowed" line reflects a bylaw that has been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted — read the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is any District townhouse stock on First Nations land?** No counted entry is. The tenure caution is the Park Royal east edge: Capilano IR No. 5 lands under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, where the Squamish Nation is the zoning authority — any property near that edge needs its title read before writing, and leasehold there is not freehold strata. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: Will the density directives change the townhouse supply?** The direction is documented — the July 21, 2025 provincial directive pushes density at Park Royal–Taylor Way and beside Ambleside and Dundarave, and Rodgers Creek Area 5 (354 units) is approved above the British Properties — but approved and directed density is not existing stock. What it means for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED without a planner's read. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698 and https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/development-applications/3900-cypress-bowl-road-area-5-rodgers-creek
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 250 along Marine Drive over the Lions Gate Bridge to downtown Vancouver, continuing to Dundarave and on some trips to Horseshoe Bay; routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed into Park Royal exchange. The waterfront entries sit on the 250 corridor; the bench pockets (Whitby, Caulfeild Cove) are a drive or hill walk from it. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **West Vancouver's condo map is two strips, not a district-wide market.** The verified apartment stock sits only in Ambleside and the Dundarave Bellevue strip (Ambleside = 8 buildings; Dundarave = 6), while the RS single-family zones cover everything else — which is why the District's whole verified townhouse roll-up fits in five entries. (Home condos files on disk.)
- **The District's best-known waterfront building cannot be bought.** Villa Maris has been rental for its whole life since 1965 per the District's heritage statement — buyers who assume every Bellevue Avenue high-rise is strata will waste their first enquiry there. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf
- **One sub-area answers to two names.** Some Whitby Road homes are marketed under Chartwell and others under Whitby Estates, so a search or comp set built on one name only will miss sales; the precise MLS boundary between the two sub-areas is UNVERIFIED. (Home file: whitby-estates condos, on disk.)
- **Caulfeild Cove predates the heritage framing around it.** The 1974 wood-frame townhome strata sits in a neighbourhood whose Lower Caulfeild subdivision was planned from 1899 and is now a designated Heritage Conservation Area — its depreciation report, not the estate-home market, is the pricing document. In CD86 Residential Area A nearby, stratification is prohibited outright. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive
- **The District's recreation and library anchors are on Marine Drive:** the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre, 2121 Marine Drive, and the West Vancouver Memorial Library, 1950 Marine Drive — opened as a War Memorial, dedicated to those who served in the Second World War. Ambleside Park below them offers sandy beaches, a designated swim area, skatepark, playground, tennis and pickleball courts and the Ambleside Par 3 Golf Course, on the Spirit Trail. The hospital serving the District is Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver. https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library and https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/ambleside-park and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
