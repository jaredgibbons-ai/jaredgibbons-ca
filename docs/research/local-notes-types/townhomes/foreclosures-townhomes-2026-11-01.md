### 2.1 Strata vs fee-simple townhouses in a court sale — the distinction that survives

- A **strata townhouse** in a court sale still sits in a strata corporation: fees, levies, CRF and bylaws bind the buyer after vesting, and the Form B question in §3 applies. A **fee-simple townhouse** has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and covenants/HOA only. Tenure of a specific court-sold unit: read its title and the sale conditions — UNCONFIRMED as any general statement.
- Apartment condos are documented in the condos sibling (0 roster, same design); townhouse-style units inside an apartment strata follow the condos treatment, not this file. Mixed complexes split by built form per component, each counted in exactly one program — no component is counted here at all.
- Bare-land strata / single-family strata, pad-rental parks (NEVER townhouse stock — Cottonwood Village precedent; Westwood Estates, Surrey, is pad-rental, excluded wherever met), leasehold (incl. TFN / City ground leases / UniverCity SFU / First Nations Lease pad parks), corporation shares, undivided interest, co-ops, life leases, membership/non-strata purchases and non-market rental are all excluded from townhouse stock for their tenure reason, in a court sale as in any sale.

## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Settled C2 Langley treatment, carried (the program's settled foreclosure frame).**

- **Start — Rule 21-7(1)–(5):** foreclosure/redemption must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — (2); joinder of mortgage claims/persons liable — (3); after a certificate of pending litigation, later registrants need not be served, are bound, may file a response — (4); Form 125 information notice served with the petition — (4.1); court may make a final order — (5)(a), order payment within a court-fixed redemption period with foreclosure in default — (5)(b), or determine/certify the amount due — (5)(c). No fixed redemption length in the Rule (research file §4; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03).
- **Beyond (5)(c) — as settled in the C2/condos treatment:** in default, a final order may be granted — 21-7(6); a party may apply at any time for an order to sell — 21-7(7); conduct of sale "in the manner the person considers appropriate or as the court directs" — Rule 13-5(3); court directions may appoint conduct, fix manner, fix reserve/minimum price, define bid/offer rights, settle conditions, obtain value evidence — 13-5(4), incl. sale "by contract conditional on the approval of the court" — 13-5(4)(b); confirmation/vesting application even before redemption expires — 21-7(9); sale result certified in **Form 60** — 13-5(6) (condos sibling §3; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02).
- **PD-66:** the Supreme Court's practice direction PD-66, "Foreclosure Proceedings," governs practice (it replaced PD-62 sealed-bid practice per the Court of Appeal, condos sibling §5); **competing/sealed-bid timing and manner under PD-66: UNVERIFIED beyond the rule text** — the live page's courthouse competing-offer account is its own statement, consistent with 13-5(4)(d) bid rights but not adopted as verified mechanics.
- **Registry:** proceedings commence at the registry in the municipality where the land is located if there is one, otherwise in its judicial district (Law and Equity Act s.21(2), condos sibling §3) — **specific mapping UNVERIFIED**; Delta/Mission/Surrey have NO registry (§1). Court Services Online eSearch: file number, registry, parties, documents; class "H - Foreclosure" (https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do). Order nisi wording: Land Title Act s.271(1); interest to redemption date: Law and Equity Act s.19 (condos sibling §3).
- **Form B availability in a court sale — UNCONFIRMED, not filled in.** In an ordinary resale SPA s.59 requires a Form B within 7 days (max $35 + 25¢/page, depreciation report attached); no official source read states a court sale will (or will not) produce a full Form B package — the seller is the person with conduct under court-settled conditions (13-5(3)–(4)). Read the specific sale's conditions (condos sibling §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). Form F case commentary (Madson / Gies, secondary) turns on the sale order's terms — not a rule for a specific file.
- **Strata block for a strata townhouse buyer:** depreciation reports 5+ lots every 5 years since Jul 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot UNDER threshold — chase the report first where documents are thin); CRF ≥10% since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a registered 55+ bylaw CAN still bind a court-sale buyer (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **"As-is" / no subjects:** the live page states buyers buy as-is with diligence before the final offer — page editorial only; **UNCONFIRMED as a stated process rule**; the specific file's court-settled conditions govern (research file §4).
- **PTT / GST treatment of court sales — UNCONFIRMED, neither filled in.** General rules only: PTT 1%/2%/3% + further 2% above $3M residential ("Over $3M — 5%" compression flagged); FTHB $500K/≤$835K→$860K; newly-built $1.1M/$1.15M. ## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?**
A: By petition in the Supreme Court under Rule 21-7(1), with Form 125 served (§3).

**Q: Can my accepted townhouse offer still lose?**
A: Until a confirming order is made the sale is not final (21-7(9)), and court directions may define bid/offer rights (13-5(4)(d)) — get the file's bid instructions from the seller's counsel (§3).

**Q: How long is the redemption period?**
A: Whatever the file's order fixes under 21-7(5)(b) — the Rules state no set length (§3).

**Q: Will I get a Form B and strata documents for a strata townhouse?**
A: UNCONFIRMED as a general promise — no source guarantees a full package in a court sale; read the court-settled conditions line by line with a lawyer (§3).

**Q: Is the title clean if I win?**
A: That is the confirming/vesting order's job (21-7(9)); the sale result is certified in Form 60 (13-5(6)) (§3).

**Q: Do I pay PTT/GST differently because it's a court sale?**
A: UNCONFIRMED — no court-sale-specific rule is stated in any source read; budget on general rules and confirm on the specific file (§3).

**Q: Are foreclosure townhouses cheaper?**
A: No published source supports a standard discount and SnapStats has no foreclosure category — UNCONFIRMED. The page's own caution: "foreclosures aren't always cheap" (research file §7).

**Q: Where is the file heard?**
A: Mapping UNVERIFIED — Delta, Mission and Surrey have no registry; commence-where-land-is / judicial-district rule is in §3. Check the file on Court Services Online (class H - Foreclosure).

**Q: Is a fee-simple townhouse court sale simpler?**
A: It carries no strata layer (no Form B question), but the court layer — petition, redemption, conduct, approval, vesting — is identical (§2.1, §3).

**Q: Can I inspect / is it vacant?**
A: Occupancy and access are facts of the individual file and its conditions — UNCONFIRMED as any general statement (§2.2).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **PD-66 replaced the old sealed-bid direction PD-62** — advice written around the old process is out of date (condos sibling §5; https://www.bccourts.ca/jdb-txt/ca/25/01/2025BCCA0108.htm).
- **The first order can already be final** — 21-7(5)(a) allows a final order at the first stage; a sale can be applied for at any time (21-7(7)) and confirmed before redemption expires (21-7(9)) (§3).
- **A later-registered interest does not slow the proceeding** once a CPL is registered (21-7(4)) — a mid-file title search can change without notice to a buyer (§3).
- **There is no official Fraser Valley foreclosure list** — CSO shows individual files only, SnapStats has no category; file-by-file eSearch is the only official window (§1, §3).
