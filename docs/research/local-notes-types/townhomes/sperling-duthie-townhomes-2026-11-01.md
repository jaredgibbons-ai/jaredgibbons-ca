## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata frame — stated for any strata purchase at the sub-area's edges):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Tapestry Lane or Sperling Heights, the Form B and strata plan — not the directory line — are the confirming documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (the new 2-unit duplex stratas in the board-filing cluster above sit under it — check the lot count before assuming a report exists). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Sperling Heights, at 7 published units, sits above the 5-lot threshold. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Sperling Heights' and Tapestry Lane's directory lines both state rentals allowed, consistent with current law; any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a strata in this area would be VOID in-entry and flagged as such — none is carried into §2 of this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-children exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. The 55-plus sibling verifies **zero** 55+ ownership complexes in this sub-area and no age restriction for any in-area building. Tapestry Lane's published line is a pet restriction ("Pets not allowed," directory data), not an age bylaw — do not conflate the two. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** This sub-area's actual product is the fee-simple detached house: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, heritage register / Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct status for Sperling Avenue properties) plus any registered covenants/HOA only. Do not import strata expectations into a Buckingham Heights or post-war-bungalow purchase.
- **SSMUH on house lots:** under the R1 SSMUH District (June 2024, carried into the Zoning Bylaw 2026 frame), standard lots here can hold 3–4 homes and larger lots within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop up to 6, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — the mechanism behind the new duplex supply and the pricing question on the area's biggest Buckingham Heights and Canada Way-edge lots. Review the Burnaby 2050 OCP land use map (adopted December 9, 2025) before assuming density or subdivision potential on a large lot, because the new OCP governs over older community plan maps. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
