## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (the BC standard frame — stated because buyers ask, though no strata townhome is counted in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line surfacing for a Langley strata is flagged void in-entry wherever met (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Campbell Valley strata carries a verified age bylaw — the area's verified 55+ position is zero (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is verified in Campbell Valley (§2.2); Campbell Valley acreage purchases are fee-simple LAND purchases on the same no-strata diligence track.

**Township of Langley zoning, SSMUH, ALR and taxes — standing frame applied.**

- **Zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500. Feed cards use RU1 and RU-3 labels in their own listing text — card labels are listing copy only, and a specific parcel's zoning is UNVERIFIED at parcel level (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/campbell-valley-guide.html).
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — NO 6-plex tier exists in it (3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 units on lots 280–4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible). Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **Suites / coach houses:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102, never strata — **a live Township conflict is flagged, resolved nowhere:** s. 102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted (count home §3; research file §4, settled positions).
- **ALR frame (for parcels that are in the ALR):** one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive. **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **BC Assessment farm classification (full three-tier form):** $2,500 of farm income if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met; **no such line is documented in this slug's research, so no defect is asserted for this page.** Applications by October 31 for the next assessment/tax year; a buyer continuing to farm must file the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Property taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current-year taxes unpaid at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026 (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).

**Q: A card talks about stratification potential — can an acreage become strata townhomes?**
A: On an ALR parcel, no — not without ALC permission: depositing a strata plan causes subdivision in the ALR (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6), and ALR residences cannot be stratified (SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Off-ALR, the question is a parcel-level Township question; card statements are listing copy, UNVERIFIED at parcel level (§§2.5, 3; research file §4).

**Q: What homes can an ALR parcel in Campbell Valley have?**
A: Under the ALC frame: one principal residence of 500 m² or less, a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), with Township rules possibly more restrictive (§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Is a coach house a townhouse I can buy separately?**
A: No — in the Township a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102, on the same title as the principal home; it is never strata and is never counted as a townhouse complex in this program (§§2.2, 3).

**Q: Do the Township's SSMUH unit counts apply to a Campbell Valley acreage?**
A: Generally no — Bylaw 6020 makes lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and acreage parcels are measured in acres. There is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020 (§3).

**Q: What farm income does a parcel need to keep farm classification?**
A: The full three-tier BC Assessment test: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha), or $10,000 (<0.8 ha) — a flat "$2,500+" is an error. A buyer continuing to farm files by October 31 after purchase (§3).

**Q: When are Township property taxes due?**
A: For 2026, Thursday, July 2, 2026 — 5% penalty at midnight July 2 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026 (§3; https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx).
