## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated because a buyer cross-shopping Canterbury against Ambleside strata stock will meet them there; inside Canterbury itself there is no strata stock for them to attach to.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Canterbury complex carries a directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag — there is no complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ complex of any form is verified in Canterbury (sibling 55-plus file on disk). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Whether a Canterbury lot carries British Properties building-scheme or covenant restrictions beyond District zoning: UNVERIFIED in the research pass — confirm on the individual title; no covenant term is stated here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/canterbury-wv.md)
- **District zoning and plan — standing corrections applied:** houses sit in the RS single-family dwelling zones of **Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010** (never White Rock's bylaws — the 2506/2220 pair belongs to White Rock only); Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses. The District-wide plan is the **Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018**; a Canterbury-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **SSMUH, stated as the Province states it:** provincial SSMUH legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 dwelling units on single-family and duplex parcels greater than 280 m², and a minimum of 6 units on such parcels greater than 280 m² near frequent bus service. West Vancouver's local implementing-bylaw status: UNVERIFIED — no District source confirming the adoption state was read in the research pass. Separately, on July 21, 2025 the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP to provide increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025 — whether Canterbury's lowest streets fall inside the "adjacent" areas must be verified per property. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Hazard permits:** an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both can apply on Canterbury's hillside and creek lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does **not** apply in West Vancouver and is omitted. District 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; the Province's additional school tax (0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027) will commonly apply at Canterbury / British Properties price points. District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Canterbury's name is a board and marketing name, not a District one.** The District's OCP plans this hillside as part of the wider British Properties residential area between the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre and the upper benches — a buyer searching District documents for "Canterbury" will find nothing, which is a naming gap, not a missing neighbourhood. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **The seawalk below the hillside dates to Canada's centennial.** The Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 and runs roughly 1.7 km east from Dundarave Park (150 25th Street) to Ambleside, below the Canterbury hillside — the waterfront amenity Canterbury marketing leans on is downhill, not in the enclave. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- **The Province has already directed more density beside this hillside.** On July 21, 2025, the Province directed West Vancouver to amend its OCP for increased density in the single-family and duplex areas adjacent to Ambleside and Dundarave (and at Park Royal–Taylor Way) by December 31, 2025. Whether Canterbury's lowest streets are "adjacent" is a per-property check, not a sub-area answer. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Half the tax bill leaves the District.** Approximately 50% of a West Vancouver tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities, and District water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly — a different billing rhythm from the flat annual notices buyers meet elsewhere. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **The District counted 44,122 people in 2021.** West Vancouver's 2021 Census population was 44,122 (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — context for a hillside enclave whose services all sit in the Ambleside town centre below it. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/search-recherche/results-resultats.cfm?Lang=E&SearchText=Vancouver
