## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 3349 Dunbar Street strata, and any Dunbar strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. The 3349 Dunbar Street complex's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so confirm its position from its Form B package. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Dunbar townhouse complex was found in this pass, so there is no in-entry VOID flag to make in Section 2 — but any such line a buyer finds for the 3349 Dunbar Street strata is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Dunbar townhouse carries a published 55+ bylaw in the sources checked — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ strata ownership stock in Dunbar at all — and Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar's name is its street number, not an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple product (Southlands lots, multiplexes, houses with suites/laneway):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. A Dunbar "townhouse-style" listing with no strata fees charged is usually this form or a duplex, not a strata lot.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Southlands country lots sit under their own agricultural / limited-agriculture districts; parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suite rules as the city states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit housing is the R1-1 multiplex regime above — up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on a qualifying lot, with a secondary suite and a laneway house permitted with a single detached house. That regime prices the house lots around any townhouse purchase; it does not change the strata complexes themselves. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan:** Dunbar has no separate adopted community plan confirmed in the sources read: UNVERIFIED; citywide land-use direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/dunbar.md)
- **Flood / ALR:** the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the area's south end, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels; Southlands lots near the river include land in the Agricultural Land Reserve. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%** of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. A Dunbar townhouse bought as a secondary or held-vacant property carries this extra annual step. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Dunbar townhouse presale is carried in this file; the frame is live in the area for the 3590 West 39th Avenue apartment sales, which proceed by disclosure statement through a receivership per the sibling condos file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually a townhouse complex I can buy in Dunbar?** No named complex was verified in this pass. The one documented townhouse address is 3349 Dunbar Street — an unnamed strata (strata fees charged in the listing record), built 1995, with a current listing describing a 2-level, 3-bedroom home with a 624 sq ft rooftop deck and pets "Yes With Restrictions." Everything else verified in Dunbar is detached houses, three apartment-condo buildings, or Southlands country lots. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3349-dunbar-street/102
- **Q: Strata or fee-simple — how do I tell which Dunbar product I'm looking at?** By the strata-fee record and title, not the marketing word "townhome." The 3349 Dunbar Street listing charges strata fees, so it is read as strata here; a Dunbar multiplex, duplex or house-with-laneway showing no strata fees is fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, diligence is title and City rules only. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3349-dunbar-street/102 and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Dunbar townhouse long-term?** Yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever older bylaw text says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package — and a unit left empty instead triggers the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax declaration at 3%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
