## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The lead rule for this file — ALR land cannot become strata townhouse product.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)).** A farm parcel's house can never be sold as a strata lot — so the entire rural side of this feed (Matsqui Prairie, Sumas Prairie, Bradner and Poplar ALR parcels) can produce fee-simple farm residences within the ALC limits below, but never a townhouse strata, however the listing markets "development potential" (condos sibling, §3).
- **ALR housing frame (ALC):** generally no more than one residence per parcel unless the parcel qualifies for an additional residence, and local governments may further regulate or restrict residences through zoning; a principal residence must have a total floor area of **500 m² or less**, with no more than **1,000 m² of fill** placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only when located **within** the principal residence; one additional residence, detached, is permitted up to **90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less** and up to **186 m² on properties over 40 ha**; anything more numerous or larger requires ALC approval (research file §4: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Live-page ALR answer flagged as incomplete, not copied:** the live page's FAQ answers "Can I build a house on vacant ALR land?" with the 500 m² figure alone; the ALC frame adds the 1,000 m² fill limit, the suite-within-principal-residence rule and the 90 m² / 186 m² tiers, none of which the FAQ states — the ALC figures above govern (research file §4).
- **Farm-class thresholds, in full three-tier form (stated wherever farm status is met in Abbotsford files):** **$2,500** of farm income for land of **0.8–4 ha**; **$2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha**; **$10,000** for land **under 0.8 ha**; applications by **Oct 31**. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met and is never carried in this file.
- **Agricultural zone scale:** minimum subdivision lot area **8.0 ha in A1** and **16.0 ha in A2** in the City's AgRefresh parcel-size work; the A1 zone permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to the same 90 m² / 186 m² maxima (research file §4: https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf).

**The strata block — stated because a serviced-lot buyer asks "could this become strata townhouses?", and because it governs any completed townhouse the buyer compares against.**

- **Form B diligence** (for completed strata product, not for a bare parcel): SPA s. 59 — Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page, with fees, levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). A bare-land strata lot, where one exists, is strata product under the same Act — confirm the parcel's tenure on title (§2.1).
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** stratas with 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold); CRF minimum 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals / age:** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse — and a fee-simple house on a serviced lot — carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees: title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Most product ever built on this feed's urban lots will be fee-simple; strata form is a developer's choice at subdivision, never a default.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Can I buy ALR land and build strata townhouses on it?**
A: No. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — a farm parcel can carry the ALC-permitted fee-simple residences within the §3 limits, but never strata lots (§3).

**Q: What CAN I build on vacant ALR land in Abbotsford?**
A: Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less total floor area with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite only inside that principal residence; and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). City zoning must also permit it, and anything larger or more numerous needs ALC approval (§3).

**Q: A listing says a lot has "Townhouse zoning" — is that verified townhouse potential?**
A: No. The TH2 and Townhouse 1 claims on this feed are listing text for two individual cards, UNVERIFIED as parcel facts in the research pass. Zoning, OCP designation, DPA overlays and floodplain position for a specific lot are confirmed in the City's WebMap / property information search before subjects come off (§§2.1, 3).

**Q: How small a lot can I create in Abbotsford?**
A: On the agricultural zones, the City's parcel-size work records subdivision minimums of 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2). Urban residential zone minimums are UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in this pass — confirm the specific zone's minimum with the City for the parcel (§3).

**Q: How much down payment and GST should I budget for a lot?**
A: Neither figure is stated in this file: the live page gives two different down-payment passages (35–50% for raw land in its body; 25–35% serviced / 35–50% unserviced in its FAQ) and a GST summary, and all of them are UNVERIFIED here — no CRA or lender source was opened in the research pass. Have a lender and a lawyer confirm both for the specific purchase (§3).

**Q: What PTT do I pay on a $1,200,000 lot?**
A: $22,000 on the Province's general tiers with no exemption: $2,000 plus 2% of the $1,000,000 above $200,000. The full anchor set is in §2.4.

**Q: If I build a house with a suite and a coach house on a serviced lot, what are the size rules?**
A: The suite must be within the principal unit, registered, and under 50% of the principal unit's size; a coach or garden suite may occupy up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's figures only — with accessory units counting toward the lot's SSMUH total (§3).

**Q: What does farm class actually require — is "$2,500+" the whole test?**
A: No — a flat "$2,500+" line is an error. The test is three-tiered: $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 (§3).

**Q: Are there land statistics — how fast do Abbotsford lots sell?**
A: No sourced answer exists: SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category, so there is no land count, median, price-per-acre or days-on-market figure in this file. The §1 residential rows are housing-market context only (§1).

**Q: Which school would a house built on this lot feed into?**
A: UNVERIFIED for every feed parcel — no locator run for a land parcel is on record. Two recorded Abbotsford chains exist for other addresses (§1); run the exact parcel address in the SD34 locator once a home is contemplated.
