## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Oliver's Landing, read the Form B alongside the neighbourhood's statutory building scheme (below) — the scheme, not the strata, controls much of what an owner may alter — and confirm which phase the lot sits in and whether any phase-completion obligations remain, given the 2024–25 permit activity in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both Furry Creek stratas are above the threshold on their published unit counts (56 and 12). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The counted complex's published listing position records no rental ban, so there is no directory "rentals not allowed" line to flag in-entry in §2 — the rule is stated here so any stale line met on a future listing is read correctly on sight, and the building scheme is a separate document to check before assuming rental use. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Furry Creek strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw — Oliver's Landing's current listings record no age restriction at all, and seller "retirement" language is not a bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Furry Creek (§2). A fee-simple townhome, where one exists elsewhere, carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — regional-district rules plus registered covenants/building schemes only. That distinction matters doubly here, because even Furry Creek's *strata* owners sit under a statutory building scheme on top of strata law, and its freehold hillside owners sit under the scheme without any strata at all — neither group should import the other group's expectations.
- **Local planning — SLRD Electoral Area D (there is no municipal bylaw set here):** the Official Community Plan is Electoral Area D Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 1135-2013 (adopted October 28, 2013), with the Howe Sound East Sub-area Plan as Schedule C covering Furry Creek; zoning is SLRD Electoral Area D Zoning Bylaw No. 1350-2016, whose Furry Creek zones include CD3 (Furry Creek Comprehensive Development 3), FCR residential zones, FCC1 (Furry Creek – Commercial 1) and FCC2 (Furry Creek Golf Course Commercial 2). Minimum lot figures in the FCR/CD3 zones: UNVERIFIED from the bylaw text in the research pass. https://slrd.civicweb.net/document/149848/ and https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw-electoral-area-d2pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **No SSMUH density windfall here:** the Province has determined the SLRD's master-planned communities of Britannia Beach, Furry Creek and Porteau Cove are NOT subject to the additional 3–6 unit small-scale multi-unit housing densities, because they are not within an urban containment boundary for SSMUH purposes; SLRD Amendment Bylaw No. 1887-2025 removed the extra densities briefly added to the Furry Creek FCR3 and CD3 zones. Suite/ADU rights are whatever the base zoning and the building scheme allow. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)
- **Building schemes sit on top of zoning:** the SLRD records that the Furry Creek neighbourhood has existing statutory building schemes and covenants, which constrain what owners can build or alter — exterior changes, additions and even some landscaping may need scheme approval. Read the scheme before planning changes. https://www.slrd.bc.ca/media/file/250129-ssmuh-area-d-amendment-bb-extension-update-rfd (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/furry-creek.md)

- **Q: What hazard checks matter here?** The expanded Howe Sound East Development Permit Areas (Bylaw No. 1717-2021) for landslide, slope/foundation stability, liquefaction, coastal and creek hazards — creek-fan lots carry dike and flood construction level requirements, which affect both building plans and insurance conversations. (§3 above)
- **Q: Where do Furry Creek kids go to school?** Squamish. SD48's Policy 501 catchment table puts Furry Creek in the Squamish Elementary (K–6) catchment, with Don Ross Middle School (7–9) and Howe Sound Secondary School (10–12) as the Squamish-area middle/secondary schools; there is no school in Furry Creek itself. - **The apartment buildings in the sales story do not exist yet.** The 2022 rezoning presentation for Furry Creek proposed replacing the planned Lot 2 waterfront townhouses with low- to mid-rise apartments — a design concept in a rezoning process, with the same presentation recording overall covenant room for up to 920 homes, 300 hotel rooms and an 80-berth marina. None of that is built stock, and no completion date is published. (§2 above)
- **The whole community runs on SLRD local services, not a city.** Water, sewer, dyking, fire, street lighting and open-space maintenance at Furry Creek are each their own SLRD local service with its own bylaw (e.g., Dyking Bylaw No. 481, Sewer Bylaw No. 1619) — which is why two otherwise-similar Sea-to-Sky homes can carry different levy stacks, and why there is no city tax notice to compare against. (§3 above)
- **A development application for Furry Creek has been in the SLRD process since 2021** — it was one of two Howe Sound East applications (with Britannia South/Tiger Bay) that drove the expanded hazard Development Permit Areas adopted in 2022, and the CD3 zone contemplates further build-out; applicant, unit counts and current status on an official source are UNCONFIRMED. (§2 above)
- **Squamish is the corridor's seniors-housing centre, not Furry Creek.** Westwinds (232 affordable low-income seniors rentals, 38275 Third Ave, Squamish) opened in April 2022 beside the registered Shannon Falls Retirement Residence on the same street — a Furry Creek senior who needs supported or subsidized housing is, in practice, moving to Third Avenue in Squamish. There is no 55+ product of any tenure in Furry Creek itself. (~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/furry-creek-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Mailing addresses mislead jurisdiction searches here.** Furry Creek listings surface under West Vancouver and Squamish postal framings in portals, but the ground is unincorporated SLRD Electoral Area D — the bylaws, tax notice and school catchment all run through the SLRD, the Province and SD48, not through either neighbouring municipality. (§1 above)
