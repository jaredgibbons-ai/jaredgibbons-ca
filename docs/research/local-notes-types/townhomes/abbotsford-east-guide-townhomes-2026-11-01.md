# Abbotsford East — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford East" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; it is not a single City neighbourhood name — the City plans the east side through McMillan, Old Clayburn, East Townline, Whatcom and McKee. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Rough extent: Highway 1 to the south, the Sumas Mountain slopes and McKee Road area to the east and northeast, Old Yale Road and Old Clayburn Road through the middle, and the McMillan/Clearbrook side to the west. The mountainside is planned separately through the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023), Schedule E of the Official Community Plan. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- Where the townhouse stock actually sits: not on the hillside. The verified complexes cluster on the McMillan/Old Yale flats at the west edge (Yale Court, Immel Ridge, East Ridge), at the Whatcom Road / Old Yale Road node by Highway 1 (Vantage at Whatcom), and on the Eagle Mountain / Old Yale east side (The Falls, Mountain View Village). No townhouse complex was verified in Auguston, Sandy Hill or Clayburn Village itself. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- The east side is still detached-first: 165 detached listings vs 69 attached listings in September 2026 — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-east-guide.md).
- SnapStats does not publish a townhome-only figure for Abbotsford East: it splits only Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined, and its price figures are Abbotsford-wide (attached median $497,500, $433/sq ft, 97% sale-to-list, 32 DOM — September 2026). No townhome-only median is stated in this file. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
- Highway 1 access is at Whatcom Road; BC Transit routes include 2 (Highstreet/McMillan), 7 (Sumas Mountain), 9 (McKee), 15 (Auguston), 16 (McMillan) and the 66 Fraser Valley Express. There is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in Abbotsford. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=2

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** every verified complex in Section 2 is strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this area, so do not market any Section 2 complex as fee-simple; "freehold strata" in a listing is still strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** on a resale purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate; the BC Real Estate Association advises reviewing it for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote, projecting 30 years of costs with at least three CRF funding models; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. That voids the Yale Court directory's "rentals not allowed" line for long-term tenancies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. No 55+ townhouse complex was verified in Abbotsford East. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning/OCP:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs. The current city-wide plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025 — the research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE and are flagged here as a Jerry sweep item; do not present either number as the current OCP citation. The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) plans the mountainside as ground-oriented forms. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **SSMUH/suites:** in June 2024 Council adopted Zoning Bylaw amendments implementing provincial small-scale multi-unit housing — June 2024 adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED and "3560-2024" must not be asserted. Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule only) — standing corrections in the commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **GST on new townhomes (East Ridge):** 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. The First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122 — this date governs over the March 12 date in some sibling files) rebates 100% of GST to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements signed on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. The older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) still exists underneath. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property Transfer Tax (BC):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a townhouse phase is sold as a presale, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement, and s.18 requires deposits to be held in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings / insurance:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a townhouse here strata or fee-simple?** Strata, in every verified case — Vantage (EPS3624), East Ridge (EPS11610), Yale Court (LMS2733), The Falls (LMS3797), Immel Ridge and Mountain View Village all carry strata title/fees. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Abbotsford East. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/54-3025-mcmillan-rd-1911730
- **Q: What do strata fees cover, and how do I check for levies?** Coverage is complex-by-complex and is read off the Form B, together with the depreciation report (mandatory every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots) and the CRF balance; the CRF must receive at least 10% of the operating fund annually. No fee amount for any east-side complex is published in a source checked — UNCONFIRMED until the Form B is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Yale Court's directory says rentals are not allowed. Can I rent my unit out?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/yale-court-2525
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Yale Court: pets allowed with restrictions (directory). Immel Ridge: pets allowed with restrictions, pet-friendly per listings. Mountain View Village: pets allowed with restrictions. Vantage, East Ridge and The Falls: UNCONFIRMED — read the registered bylaws. https://www.rodfriesen.com/abbotsford-townhomes/2525-yale-court-yale-court
- **Q: Is there an age restriction?** None was verified for any east-side townhouse complex. The only legal strata age bylaw in BC is 55+ (SPA ss.123.1/123.2); whether any complex here has one is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: What parking and storage do I get?** It is stated per unit on the Form B, not in marketing. Listing-level examples only: Vantage units show tandem garage parking; Yale Court units show double garage plus outside parking. Confirm the stall/locker legally attached to the specific unit. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3094121-12-36099-waterleaf-place-abbotsford-british-columbia-v3g0g5-705000/brochure
- **Q: What is the strata insurance deductible risk?** The strata's water-damage deductible can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused a loss; the amount is in the strata insurance summary in the document package and is UNCONFIRMED for every east-side complex until read. Buyers typically match a unit policy to that deductible. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: I'm a first-time buyer at East Ridge (new). What stacks?** Potentially the FTHB GST rebate (100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, agreements on/after March 20, 2025, Royal Assent March 13, 2026), the BC Newly Built Home PTT exemption (full to $1,100,000), and FHSA/HBP room ($40,000 / $60,000). Each has its own eligibility test. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. Locator runs in the research file: 36367 Stephen Leacock Drive returns Auguston Traditional Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary, and 3836 Old Clayburn Road returns Sandy Hill Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary. Neither is a townhouse address — run the complex's own address in the SD34 School Locator. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-east-guide.md)
- **Q: Is flood or slope risk a townhouse issue here?** Both edges matter: low land toward Sumas Prairie and along Whatcom Road is floodplain by the Sumas River, and the OCP applies a Steep Slope Development Permit Area on slope land. Check the specific parcel in the City's WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/evacuation-orders-modified-additional-properties-sumas-prairie

- **East Ridge's unit count is published two ways in the same data ecosystem:** the building record states 191 homes (EPS11610), while a directory listing page describes "one of 39 units built at 3025 McMillan Road after the original lot was redeveloped" — a phase/redevelopment figure, not the complex total. - **Yale Court's build year disagrees with itself:** complex information reports 1997, property records identify 1999, and individual listings use both. Do not quote a single year for Yale Court without the strata plan. https://www.rodfriesen.com/abbotsford-townhomes/2525-yale-court-yale-court
- **The McKee mountainside is planned ground-oriented, not apartment product:** the McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023) designates Urban 2 – Ground Oriented forms, with an open-space network protecting streamside setbacks, critical habitat and slopes over 40% — future east-side townhouse supply, if any, comes under that plan. https://letstalkabbotsford.ca/mckee-trail-strategy
- **Clayburn Village is a Heritage Conservation Area in the OCP** — it contains no townhouse complexes, and it shapes what can be built or altered on the east side's oldest streets. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **The City's Housing Accelerator Fund work is complete:** up to $25.6 million in federal HAF funding, all initiatives reported complete in October 2026, with multi-family permitting above program expectations. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
