### What the stock actually is

- Detached houses on District detached residential zones on Mahon Avenue, Delbrook Avenue and the side streets, under the R1/R2 zones of Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525. The applicable minimums run from R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m width, with standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m; the zone on a single lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- A principal house plus a secondary suite plus a coach house (three units total) has been permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary since June 2024. A suite or coach house is a fee-simple second unit, not a townhouse complex, and is never counted as townhouse stock. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- Apartment condos inside Braemar: also zero verified — see the sibling condos file on disk. The nearest verified District condo stock is in the District's centres (Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lions Gate Village, Edgemont Village), documented in the North Vancouver model file, not in Braemar.
- No co-operative, life-lease, non-market rental or pad-rental townhouse complex was verified inside Braemar — UNCONFIRMED as categories here, and none is counted. No presale townhouse project specific to Braemar was verified. The District's Delbrook Lands project at 600 West Queens Road, south-west of the area — a new neighbourhood park on the northern portion and a four-storey building with low-to-moderate-income rental housing and a seniors' care facility on the southern portion, under OCP Amendment Bylaw 8397, Rezoning Bylaw 8398 and DCC Waiver Bylaw 8399 (2019) — is rental/care product south-west of Braemar, not townhouse stock, and is never counted here. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The governing pair is the District's own:** Official Community Plan Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210, District of North Vancouver, with the North Lonsdale–Delbrook local plan, Bylaw 6750. The City of North Vancouver's 8400/6700 pair is never imported into a District file. https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/418109.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **If a strata townhouse is ever found in Braemar, the BC standard block applies in full:** Form B diligence (Strata Property Act s. 59 — fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws:** rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a District strata is void for long-term rentals (short-term rentals can still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws are valid since Nov 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Braemar complex exists to apply either rule to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality in Braemar:** the verified stock is fee-simple detached — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — governed by municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 3210) and any registered covenants on title only. The R2 zone allows a principal dwelling plus a secondary suite in the principal building and a coach house; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Hazards:** District OCP Schedule B establishes Development Permit Areas for Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard, and the District publishes property-level reports through GeoWeb — lots near the Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines west of the area warrant a check. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Buyer frame:** PTT + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No presale townhouse exists in Braemar to apply REDMA s. 21 (7-day rescission) or s. 18 (deposits in trust) to today. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply in the District of North Vancouver and is omitted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **District taxes and utilities:** District of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal rate is 1.83419 and the total residential rate is 3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value. District single-family utility fees in 2026 include water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60, with combined single-family utilities of $2,571.20 in 2026. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouses in Braemar?** None verified — zero strata and zero fee-simple townhouse complexes on any source checked. The area is detached houses on R1/R2 lots. A "townhouse" search result carrying a Braemar label should be checked for whether the property is actually a suite or coach house inside a detached-house lot, or stock in a neighbouring centre. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/braemar.md)
- **Q: Where is the nearest real District condo and townhouse stock?** In the District's centres: Lynn Valley Town Centre, Lions Gate Village and Edgemont Village — all verified in the North Vancouver model file and documented in their own sub-area files. Braemar itself contributes none. (Sibling condos file on disk.)
- **Q: Is a coach house the same as a townhouse here?** No. A coach house is a permitted second unit on a fee-simple detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 3210 — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees — alongside a secondary suite, three units total since June 2024 within the Urban Containment Boundary. It is not a townhouse complex. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: Does a Braemar address guarantee École Braemar?** No — catchments are address-based. The one locator-verified result is for the school's own address (3600 Mahon Avenue → École Braemar Elementary and Carson Graham Secondary). Confirm any other address with SD44 before promising a catchment seat, including for the French Immersion programs. https://braemar.sd44.ca/
- **Q: What is happening at the Delbrook Lands?** At 600 West Queens Road, south-west of Braemar, the District divided the former lands into a new neighbourhood park on the northern portion and a four-storey building with low-to-moderate-income rental housing and a seniors' care facility on the southern portion, under Bylaws 8397, 8398 and 8399 (2019). It is rental/care product, not for-sale townhouse stock. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529
- **Q: What permits can surprise me on a west-side lot?** The Mosquito Creek and MacKay Creek ravines are protected through the plan's natural-environment permit areas, and OCP Schedule B adds creek, slope and wildfire hazard areas — run the GeoWeb property report before pricing a renovation. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Route 249 runs on Delbrook Avenue to Lonsdale Quay and the SeaBus (12 minutes to Waterfront Station, every 15 minutes during the day), and route 241 runs via Lonsdale Avenue and the Lions Gate Bridge directly downtown. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt249.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/translink/schedules-and-maps/seabus
- **Q: How many units can a Braemar house lot carry?** Three by right since June 2024 (principal, secondary suite, coach house) within the Urban Containment Boundary, with the GEOweb SSMUH classification showing up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop on non-exempt lots — confirm the specific lot's classification before pricing the upside. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Q: Where do I recreate and borrow books?** The Delbrook Community Recreation Centre, 851 West Queens Road, south of the area, is the nearest NVRC full-service centre, with a main pool and leisure pool and indoor racquet facilities; Princess Park on the area's east side offers tennis courts, playgrounds, a picnic shelter and forested walking trails forming part of the Lynn Valley Link. A library branch physically inside Braemar is UNVERIFIED; NVDPL's Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, serves the District's west side from Edgemont Village. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts and https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/princess-park and https://www.nvdpl.ca/capilano-library

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The school is the neighbourhood's search anchor.** "École Braemar" is itself a common search term for the area, and the school at 3600 Mahon Avenue runs English, Early French Immersion and Late French Immersion in one building — a draw that detached-house listings trade on, while the catchment for any given house still has to be locator-checked. https://braemar.sd44.ca/ and https://www.sd44.ca/registration/french-immersion/
- **The recreation centre and the hospital are both outside the pocket.** The Delbrook Community Recreation Centre (851 West Queens Road) sits south of the area, and Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver (Vancouver Coastal Health), serves the whole North Shore, with the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 2nd floor, 221 West Esplanade. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/courts and https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
- **There is no shopping centre or grocery store in Braemar confirmed on a District source** — the OCP directs growth to its town and village centres, with Edgemont Village the nearest village centre to the west. Daily shopping is a drive or bus ride by design of the plan, not an oversight. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **The District's 2021 Census population was 88,168, living in 32,700 of 34,179 total private dwellings, on a land area of 160.66 km²** (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile, Catalogue no. 98-316-X2021001) — District context for a purely residential pocket inside it. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/index.cfm?Lang=E
- **Suite rules changed twice in six months and listings have not all caught up:** December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary. Price suite potential from the current bylaw text, not the listing template. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
