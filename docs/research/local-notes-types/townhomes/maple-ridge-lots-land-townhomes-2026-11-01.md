## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**On a land page, the rules that matter are the ones that decide whether townhouse product can be created on the ground at all, plus the standard purchase rules that will apply once it is.**

- **Why townhouse/strata product IS coming to some of this ground — the adopted corridor plans.** The **Lougheed Transit Corridor Plan was ADOPTED on January 27, 2026** per the City record — Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted January 27, 2026, after a January 20, 2026 public hearing (City news release February 4, 2026); the corridor runs from the city's western boundary to the Town Centre at **221 Street** along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road. An earlier contrary reading of the January meeting in this program's research is superseded by the City record — the earlier reading is noted here and in §5, and the adopted date governs (condos sibling, §3: https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP). The **Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025** — the plan frame for the east-side growth ground in this feed (condos sibling, §3).
- **City zoning — the scale gate.** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 (http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading). Subdivision minimums: RS-1 — 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width, 27.0 m depth, maximum coverage 40%; RS-2 — 0.4 ha; RS-3 — 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text; A-1 — 2.0 ha; A-2 — 4.0 ha; A-3 — 8.0 ha. The live page's own minimum-lot FAQ (RS-2 0.4 ha; RS-3 0.8 ha with community water or 2.0 ha without; A-1 2.0 ha) is consistent with those bylaw figures (research file §4).
- **City plan citation — CONTESTED, flagged wherever met, resolved nowhere.** The research files cite the Official Community Plan both as **Bylaw 7060-2014** and as **Bylaw 7998-2024** (the batch-12 research file states OCP Bylaw 7998-2024, adopted June 25, 2024, program-verified). Neither number is treated as settled in this file; quote a parcel's designation from a current City document (research file §1; condos sibling, §3).
- **SSMUH and suites on non-ALR lots.** One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Whether a multi-unit SSMUH project is held as strata, rental or freehold lots is a project-level choice, not stated for any feed parcel (https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites; research file §4).
- **Why strata product is NOT coming to ALR ground.** Residences on Agricultural Land Reserve land **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Subdivision of ALR land also requires Agricultural Land Commission permission apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/). The ALC housing frame: one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² (a secondary suite only inside it) plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha) (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Which feed parcels are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping.
- **BC Assessment farm classification — the full three-tier thresholds** (relevant to rural feed parcels held as farms): $2,500 where the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha where the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is less than 0.8 ha — gross income at farm-gate prices, applications due by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears (https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **Thornhill Urban Reserve — a hold, not a timeline.** The OCP's Urban Reserve designation identifies lands held for long-term future urban services and housing, and OCP policy states development will not happen until specific triggers and milestones have been attained (https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2). The live page's specifics — about 343 hectares, OCP Section 10.2.2, subdivision below 2.0 ha held until the city population exceeds 100,000 and an area plan and servicing strategy are adopted — were NOT confirmed in the OCP chapter read: UNVERIFIED as to hectarage, section number and trigger wording. Do not price a Thornhill parcel on a subdivision or townhouse date (research file §4).
- **Floodplain.** The City publishes North and South Alouette River and Fraser River floodplain maps; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties (https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0). Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED.
- **Servicing and permits.** Whether a specific lot has municipal water, sanitary sewer and storm connections at the lot line — or needs a well and an onsite sewage system under the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person — is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED per parcel (https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems). Development Permit Areas: the live page describes City DPAs for watercourse protection, steep-slope hazards and wildfire interface with 15-to-30-metre riparian setbacks — the specific setback range was not confirmed on a City or provincial page in the research pass: UNVERIFIED as stated (research file §4). Development cost charges: UNVERIFIED — no City DCC bylaw or rate page was opened and no DCC figure is stated (research file §4).
- **Maple Ridge property taxes — carrying cost on a held lot.** Due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026 (https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments).

**The strata block — applies the moment product on this ground is strata, including a bare-land strata lot purchase.**

- **Form B.** On a resale strata purchase the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, within 7 days, fee capped at $35 plus 25¢/page (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports / CRF.** Since July 1, 2024, stratas with 5 or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold. Since November 1, 2023, a minimum of 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the CRF (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Maple Ridge's zero verified 55+ strata townhouses (§2.3) means no in-city townhouse example tests that rule today.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple lot or row home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019, the OCP/area-plan frame and any registered covenants govern instead, stated plainly.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **GST — handled with care on land.** The live page's GST position on vacant land (5% GST on most vacant land sales with personal-use exceptions) and its lender down-payment ranges (35–50% on raw land; 25–35% on serviced lots) are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — no CRA or lender source was opened for them, and neither position is restated as fact here. Confirm GST treatment on a land purchase with a CPA or lawyer (research file §4: https://jaredgibbons.ca/maple-ridge-lots-land.html).
- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling carrying March 12 is superseded). 100% of GST to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, March 20, 2025–2031 — it attaches to a new home built/bought under its rules, not to a bare lot price quoted in this feed (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html).
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax — worked examples (inputs stated, tier math shown).** General rule: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Program anchors on stated inputs, no exemption: $700,000 → **$12,000** (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $500,000); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1.0M → **$18,000**. Whether a specific vacant-land transfer attracts PTT on the residential portion, and any exemption, is a closing question for the lawyer/notary — no parcel-specific PTT position is stated in this slug's research. First-time buyer and newly built exemptions (full exemption on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000) are dwelling exemptions, stated here so they are not misapplied to bare land (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 at $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html; https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% for insured dwelling purchases (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). **Page-defect discipline:** no CMHC premium-range error is documented in this slug's research file, so none is asserted for this slug — the 2.80%–4.00% range documented as a live-page error elsewhere in the program is explicitly NOT asserted here. Raw-land financing is generally uninsured lender product — confirm with the lender; the live page's down-payment ranges are UNVERIFIED (§3, GST bullet).

## 5. Local details a buyer would not know

- **The same "land" search spans two completely different products.** A serviced RS-1 infill lot needs only 668.0 m²; an A-3 agricultural parcel needs 8.0 ha to subdivide at all. Zone, not the word "lot," decides what a parcel can become (§3).
- **The corridor date moved — and the City record governs.** An earlier research pass in this program read the January 2026 Lougheed Transit Corridor meeting differently; the City's record establishes Bylaw 7781-2021 third reading + adoption on **January 27, 2026** (news release February 4, 2026), with the corridor ending at 221 Street. Any summary still carrying the earlier contrary reading is stale (§3).
- **Thornhill's most-quoted numbers are its least-verified.** The Urban Reserve hold itself is confirmed OCP policy; the hectarage, the section number and the population trigger printed on the live page are UNVERIFIED. Price Thornhill land as a hold, never on a subdivision date (§3).
- **A floodplain lot quietly loses its suite.** Beyond the 6.7 m flood construction level, Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties — a value fact that never appears in a land listing's headline (§3).
- **Albion's townhouses and Albion's land are the same story at different dates.** The Albion Area Plan (adopted October 28, 2025) frames the east-side growth ground in this feed, while Albion's 4 built strata townhouse complexes already count in the Albion townhomes file — a buyer comparing a finished Albion townhouse against an Albion building lot is comparing two stages of one plan area (§§2.3, 3).
