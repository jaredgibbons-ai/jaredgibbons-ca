## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. The same document is the diligence for a bare-land strata house, if the Murrayville-type card in §2.3 ever resolves to one (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — rentals are allowed for long-term tenancies anywhere in BC; the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Willow Park Estates, Parkway Estates and Parkway II in §2 are flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; short-term rental rules in either Langley municipality are UNVERIFIED — no municipal STR rule was read on an official page in this program (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation; research file §4).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The Westbrooke and Bedford Landing row homes (§2.2) and every detached house on this page's own feed carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. A detached house is not a townhouse because it has a suite: suite and SSMUH rules below are lot rules, not a change of form.

**Q: What did Langley detached houses sell for in September 2026?**

**Q: How many units can an eligible Township lot hold?**
A: Under Bylaw No. 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): 3 units for lots of 280 m² or less, and 4 units for lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m² (1 acre); lots larger than 1 acre are not eligible, and eligibility also requires the Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation and no Transit-Oriented Area location. There is no 6-unit tier in the Township (§3; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).

**Q: How many units can a City of Langley house lot hold?**
A: Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit a Plex-Home of up to 4 units, or 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — a different answer from the Township's, which is why the municipality check comes first (§3; https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284).

**Q: How big can a secondary suite be — including in a townhouse?**
A: Township: 120 m² maximum (Bylaw 2500 s. 102). City: the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with the registered owner residing on the lot (Bylaw 3300, s. 2.4). For a Township townhouse specifically, a live conflict is flagged in §3 — s. 102 permitting townhouse suites at 120 m² versus the January 2025 guide saying not permitted — resolved nowhere; confirm the specific property with the Township before relying on either document (§3).
