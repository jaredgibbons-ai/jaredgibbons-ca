- **Fee-simple townhouse / houseplex product:** SSMUH houseplex product built on fee-simple titles carries NO strata and NO Form B (§3) and is documented as its own form in the relevant sub-area file if verified there — never counted from a land listing.
- **Apartment condos:** a lot built as an apartment strata counts in the condos program only, including any townhouse-style units inside that strata. The verified South Surrey condo buildings are carried as context in the condos sibling for this slug — context, not stock for this page.

ALR parcels can never take this route for residences: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) (§3).

## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Why the strata block matters to a land file:** it is the rulebook that attaches the moment a lot's build is strata-titled. A fee-simple build never enters it.

- **Form B diligence (strata builds only).** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page, with the depreciation report and Rental Disclosure Statement attached. A vacant lot has no Form B; a completed strata townhouse on that lot will (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata builds only).** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER the threshold); CRF minimum 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. A small 3–4 lot strata created under SSMUH can sit under the depreciation threshold — a real design consideration for a strata houseplex build.
- **Rentals / age (strata builds only).** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). A 55+ rule on built product can only arise from a strata's registered bylaws — a vacant lot carries no age restriction (55-plus sibling for this slug, scope note).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple lot and fee-simple build carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. This is the default frame for South Surrey land.

**Surrey / White Rock zoning, OCP, SSMUH and subdivision — the two municipalities kept separate.**

- **Surrey OCP: Bylaw No. 21900 "Surrey 2050"** (adopted June 15, 2026) — NEVER Bylaw 18020 as current. **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** is confirmed as Surrey's current zoning bylaw (research file §4, program rules record). The page's old-to-new zone mapping (old RF → R3 Urban Residential; RH → R1 Suburban Residential; RQ → R2 Quarter Acre Residential; one-acre → RA Acreage Residential) and its RA minimum-lot figures (4,050 m², 50 m wide, 60 m deep, Part 12) were not re-read from the bylaw in this research and are carried as site copy only: UNVERIFIED beyond site copy. Any lot's zone is checkable on the City's COSMOS map (research file §4).
- **White Rock: Zoning Bylaw No. 2506** (adopted June 27, 2024 to incorporate SSMUH: up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 over 280 m², up to 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop); **OCP Bylaw No. 2220** — the 2506/2220 pair belongs to White Rock ONLY. The page's White Rock RS-1 minimum new lot dimensions (742 m², 18 m wide, 30.5 m deep) were not re-read from the bylaw and are site copy only (research file §4).
- **SSMUH (Surrey).** In force July 8, 2024: inside the urban containment boundary, lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 on a larger one, up to 6 for some lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (page copy, research file §4); a triplex/fourplex (houseplex) is allowed only in the R3 zone, on a lot with a lane or two road frontages, with no secondary suite (site copy). Lots without community water and sewer stay limited to two units even under the multi-unit rules (research file §4, over-$1.8M sibling research §4).
- **Suites inside the unit counts (Surrey).** A secondary suite is capped at **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area** (the old 90 m² cap is NOT current — never state it); a coach house / garden suite runs 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA/R2/R2-O/R3 lots (research file §4, program rules record).
- **Subdivision process (Surrey, site copy).** Planner review, a Preliminary Layout Approval (PLA) letter listing conditions, 12 months to meet them (extension for a fee), conditions that can include road dedication, covenants, tree permits and development cost charges, and a registered surveyor's final plan to the Land Title Office — no City subdivision page was opened in this research, so the PLA timeline is UNVERIFIED beyond site copy (research file §4).
- **Servicing.** The page states a new lot on a well or septic field needs Fraser Health testing and cannot proceed if standards are not met, and that some acreage land is unserviced (site copy). Parcel-level servicing: UNVERIFIED (research file §4).
- **ALR — the frame where farm parcels are touched.** Larger acreage lots sit on the community's east side near Hazelmere and the farm areas. ALR housing frame in full: one principal residence ≤500 m²; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — no townhouse strata can ever be built as-of-right on an ALR residence parcel. Farm-classification thresholds run in the full three-tier form (never a flat "$2,500+"): $2,500 of farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land's value above that band; 10% of value at the largest band — as the program's farm files state them (standing correction; a flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR wherever met). Parcel ALR status: UNVERIFIED per parcel (research file §4: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only from the principal residence, City business licence **$358/yr** — CONFLICT disclosed: the City's 2024 report proposed $350/yr + fees; $358 is carried, $350 disclosed, reader directed to the City licence page. **Q: If I build townhouses on a lot, will they be strata or fee-simple?**
A: That is a title choice made at development, not a fact about the lot. A strata build enters the §3 strata block (Form B on resale, depreciation reports at 5+ lots, CRF); a fee-simple build carries no strata at all. Either way the completed product counts in its sub-area home file, never in this topic file (§2.1).

**Q: How do I find a lot's zone?**
A: Surrey side: the City's COSMOS map, read against Zoning By-law No. 12000. White Rock side: Zoning Bylaw No. 2506. The page's old-to-new zone mapping is site copy, UNVERIFIED beyond it (§3).

**Q: How many homes can go on a South Surrey lot?**
A: In Surrey, inside the urban containment boundary: 3 units (≤280 m²), 4 (larger), up to 6 for some lots within 400 m of frequent transit; a houseplex is R3-only with a lane or two frontages and no secondary suite; unserviced lots stay at two units. White Rock's Bylaw 2506 tiers are 3 / 4 / 6 on the same lot-size logic (§3).

**Q: Can I stratify an ALR acreage into townhouses?**
A: No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and the ALC caps the program at one principal residence (≤500 m², suite inside it only) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) (§3).

**Q: A card says "serviced building lot" / "subdivision potential" — can I rely on it?**
A: No — both are listing copy, UNVERIFIED at parcel level. Prove them against City servicing records, Fraser Health testing where a well/septic is involved, and the PLA condition list (§1/§3).

**Q: Is GST payable on a vacant lot?**
A: UNVERIFIED beyond the page's site copy (personal-use individual sales generally exempt; business sales and splits into more than two lots can be taxable). No CRA source was opened in this research — confirm with your accountant before writing (§3).

**Q: What does the subdivision clock look like?**
A: Per the page's copy: a PLA letter with conditions and 12 months to meet them, extension for a fee — UNVERIFIED beyond site copy, because no City subdivision page was opened in this research (§3).

**Q: Does buying land get me the first-time buyer GST rebate?**
A: The rebate attaches to new housing, not bare land as such: Bill C-4 (Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying new-home agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window — the home eventually built is where the question is tested, with your lawyer/accountant (§3).
