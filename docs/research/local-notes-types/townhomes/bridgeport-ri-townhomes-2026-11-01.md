## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Directory rental/pet lines in Section 2 are directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. The River Drive listings describe geothermal heating and cooling inside the building systems and, at the Parc Riviera apartment phases, water and sewer utility inside the strata fee — read the exact inclusion set off each townhouse strata's Form B before comparing fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all four complexes above are over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line is published for any complex in Section 2 in the sources read; if one surfaces in older bylaw text, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in Section 2 is published as age-restricted — the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ stock in Bridgeport RI — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Do not describe any Section 2 complex that way; all four are strata.
- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 9000 (the City Centre Area Plan's Bridgeport Village Specific Land Use Map was amended by Bylaw 10190; the older Bridgeport Area Plan sits at Schedule 2.12 of OCP Bylaw 7100 for the corridor south), Richmond Zoning Bylaw No. 8500, and the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw No. 8204. The corridor townhouses sit under the Low Density Townhouses (RTM3) rezoning documented in Section 2. The City identifies Bridgeport Station as a Transit-Oriented Area under Bill 47, and states Federal Airport Zoning Regulations and their maximum heights govern over the TOA heights here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Aircraft noise:** the City Centre Area Plan map states Richmond's Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy applies throughout Bridgeport Village, and a City data sheet places sites here in Aircraft Noise Sensitive Use Areas 1B and 2 — the River Drive buildings sit under the same airport-side regime; check the specific property's position in disclosure rather than assuming the waterfront setting means quiet. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Flood:** Richmond is made up of islands within the floodplain of the Fraser River at an average of 1 metre above sea level; the City's flood protection system includes 49 km of dikes and 39 drainage pump stations, is designed to handle a 1:500 flood event, and dikes are being raised from 3.5 metres to 4.7 metres for sea level rise. All lands within Richmond are designated flood plain under Bylaw No. 8204; a City data sheet places Bridgeport Village sites in Flood Construction Level Area A. The River Drive complexes front the North Arm dike itself. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Richmond property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/bridgeport-ri.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (any remaining developer sale at Parc Portofino or North Gate), not resales. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply in Richmond — it is a City of Vancouver tax only and is never charged here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any developer-held unit at Parc Portofino or North Gate Residences. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
