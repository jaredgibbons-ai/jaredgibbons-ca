## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata townhome in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed / allowed w/restrictions" line for a complex in §2 is void in its restriction element for long-term rentals and is flagged void wherever met in its home file. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Surrey's municipal STR frame is below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None counted here (§2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey plans, zoning, SSMUH, suites and STRs — this page's own subject matter.**

- **OCP — current bylaw: Surrey 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026. Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as the current OCP.** Zoning is under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000. The City records that some older NCPs' land use designations have been superseded or rescinded through the Surrey 2050 OCP update — East Newton North was rescinded and East Newton South's, the East Newton Business Park's and West Cloverdale's designations superseded, with the OCP's designations now guiding land use while the NCPs continue to guide amenities and infrastructure implementation. A tile on the live page therefore does not by itself prove a plan's designations are still the operative ones — the current OCP designation must be checked for the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/east-newton-ncps (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md)
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's amendments took effect July 8, 2024: the residential zones R1 to R7 replaced the RF, RH and RQ zones (RF-12/RF-12C became R4 Small Lot Residential; RF-SD became R6 Semi-Detached) — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The zoning now does the small-lot permitting that older NCP designations predate, so a plan tile and the current zone can point in different directions on the same lot. https://jaredgibbons.ca/zonings.html (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md)
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and **not a separate strata lot**. The often-quoted 90 m² cap is NOT current and is never stated as current. Coach houses and garden suites run 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/surrey.md)
- **Short-term rentals:** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence, tenants cannot run one, and a City business licence is required first. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d) and Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1) versus the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and direct any buyer to the City licence page before quoting either figure. ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/surrey-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
