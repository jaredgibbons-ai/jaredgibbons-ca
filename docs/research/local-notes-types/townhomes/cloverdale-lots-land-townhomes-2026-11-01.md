## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

This is a land topic, so the rules below run in two layers: the land rules that decide what can ever be built on a feed parcel (zoning, SSMUH, ALR), and the standard strata/purchase block that applies the moment a buyer instead buys one of the counted Cloverdale townhouse complexes cross-referenced in §2.3.

**Land layer — City of Surrey and the ALC.**

- Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050"), adopted June 15, 2026**; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan). Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; check any parcel's current zone, OCP designation and servicing in COSMOS before pricing it (research file §4).
- **SSMUH:** Surrey implemented the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules on **July 8, 2024** — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The same changes replaced the former RF-12/RF-12C zones with the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD with R6 Semi-Detached — older land material using RF-12/RF-SD names is using pre-2024 zone names (City of Surrey, SSMUH: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** (City of Surrey, secondary suites: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites). Coach houses and garden suites run 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² only on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, with lane access required on smaller R3–R6 lots and one extra parking space (City of Surrey, coach houses: https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites).
- **ALR rules (they govern the feed's acreage parcels):** a principal residence capped at **500 m²**, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of **90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha)** or **186 m² (parcels >40 ha)**; subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited regulation exceptions (Agricultural Land Commission: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — a farm parcel can never become a strata townhouse project while it sits in the reserve. BC Assessment farm-class thresholds, stated in full three-tier form (a flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR): **$2,500** of farm income for parcels of 0.8–4 ha; **$2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha**; **$10,000** for parcels under 0.8 ha; applications by **October 31** (BC Assessment, farm classification: https://www.bcassessment.ca/). Which parcels on this feed are in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — check parcel by parcel on ALC mapping; listing text ("A1-zoned," "R3 zoned") is not an ALR or zoning determination.
- **Charges the file cannot quote:** development cost charge rates, Metro Vancouver regional charges and the GST treatment of a specific lot sale are UNVERIFIED in this file's research — the live page's FAQ names the charge categories (municipal DCCs, regional charges, latecomer agreements, frontage works, BC Hydro connections) and states GST and lender down-payment positions that no CRA or lender source opened in this pass confirms; neither position is restated as fact here. Get the DCC figure from the City and the GST answer from a CPA or lawyer before removing subjects (research file §§4–6).
- **Short-term rentals** (house-on-lot holding strategies): Surrey permits them only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at **$358/year** as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries $358 and directs the reader to the licence page for the current amount (City of Surrey, short-term rentals: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals).
- **Property tax timing:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2; a 5% penalty applies to amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey, property taxes: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

**Strata block — applies to the 7 counted Cloverdale complexes in §2.3 (all strata) when a buyer buys a townhouse instead of land.**

- **Form B diligence.** A strata buyer is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, provided within seven days of request; fee capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It states the monthly strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots sit UNDER that threshold (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports).
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; any directory or bylaw line for a counted complex reading "rentals not allowed" is void and is flagged as such wherever it appears (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2 and Regulation 7.01: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions for spouses/partners, caregivers and legacy residents, expanded May 1, 2023. A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in the Cloverdale board community; raw land on this feed is overwhelmingly freehold fee-simple land, which is a land purchase, not a townhouse purchase.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). 100% of GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum rebate $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). A newly built home on a Cloverdale lot can attract GST; a resale townhouse does not.
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST on new or substantially renovated housing, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses that 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Exemptions: first-time buyers — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000; newly built homes — full exemption to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP limit $60,000 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 of contributions at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount by loan-to-value (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research documents no CMHC premium error on a Cloverdale lots-and-land page — the known 2.80%–4.00% page error pattern is NOT documented for this slug and is therefore not asserted here.
- **Presales / developer sales.** A presale buyer has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01) — the frame if a townhouse project is ever built and sold off a parcel from this feed.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Does this page's feed include any townhouse complexes I can buy?**
A: No — the feed is vacant land only: serviced infill lots, lot-value properties, Clayton NCP assembly parcels and ALR acreage (research file §5). Cloverdale's buyable townhouse complexes — 7 strata complexes, 0 fee-simple — are verified and counted in the cloverdale-bc townhomes file (§2.3).

**Q: Are the market numbers on this topic land prices?**
A: No. SnapStats publishes no vacant-land category; the Cloverdale figures (detached median $1,212,500; "Condos & Townhomes" median $740,000, September 2026) are residential context only, and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED (research file §2).

**Q: How do I check a Cloverdale lot's zoning and subdivision potential?**
A: In the City of Surrey's COSMOS mapping — current zoning (post-July 2024 names: R4 and R6 replaced RF-12/RF-SD), OCP designation under Bylaw No. 21900, secondary plan status, services, road dedications and riparian setbacks (research file §§4, 6).

**Q: Can I subdivide or add a second home on an ALR parcel from this feed?**
A: Subdivision needs Agricultural Land Commission permission outside limited exceptions; housing is capped at a 500 m² principal residence with any suite inside it, plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — and ALR residences cannot be stratified at all (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Could I build townhouses on a Clayton assembly parcel?**
A: Only through the plan and zoning process for that parcel — the Clayton General Land Use Plan governs, East and West Clayton have NCPs, and North Clayton's plan has not yet been initiated, so a North Clayton parcel sits in reserve until its NCP is created. Nothing on a land listing confers townhouse density (research file §4).

**Q: What extra costs sit on top of a land purchase?**
A: Development cost charges, Metro Vancouver regional charges, latecomer agreements, frontage works and BC Hydro connections on any subdivision or build, plus GST where it applies — but the rates and the GST answer for a specific parcel are UNVERIFIED in this file; confirm both with the City and a CPA or lawyer before removing subjects (research file §§4, 6).

**Q: What does the Form B tell me if I buy a Cloverdale townhouse instead?**
A: The monthly strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the depreciation report attached — within seven days of request, fee capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).

**Q: Can a strata still ban rentals in a Cloverdale townhouse complex?**
A: No — rental-restriction bylaws have been void across BC since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals are separate: Surrey allows them only in the owner's own principal residence with a City licence ($358/year on the City's licence page; a 2024 corporate report proposed $350 plus fees — check the licence page before quoting) (https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals).

**Q: How much property transfer tax applies on land?**
A: The standard tiers: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — not a flat 5% over $3M (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). First-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are in §3.

**Q: Does the new first-time buyer GST rebate help on a lot purchase?**
A: The rebate is for NEW housing — 100% of GST up to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, under Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). Whether GST applies to a specific lot sale at all is UNVERIFIED in this file — get the answer for the parcel before writing.
