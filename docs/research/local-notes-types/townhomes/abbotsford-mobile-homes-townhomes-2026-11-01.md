## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies only to a bare-land strata manufactured home on owned land — the unnamed tenure in §2.6. A pad-rental park home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata corporation; its diligence is the written site tenancy agreement, the park rules and the Manufactured Home Registry record).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. A pad-rental park has neither a depreciation report nor a CRF (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Strata rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — that voiding does NOT touch a park rule: Georgian Park's listing-stated "no rentals allowed" is a park/tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw, and stands or falls on the park's own documents. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by strata bylaw where a strata exists (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). A park's "55+" label is not a strata bylaw and is not governed by that machinery (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A freehold manufactured home on owned land carries no strata and no Form B — title, City rules and the Registry record only; it is still not a townhouse (§2.2).

**MHPTA frame — the rules that actually govern a pad-rental purchase.**

- **Pad-rent increases:** a park landlord can increase rent only once every 12 months, with 3 full months' notice, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly limit — for manufactured home site tenancies the **2026 limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount** for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the **2027 limit is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount** (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **NO pad-rent DOLLAR FIGURE is ever published in this file.** No official or operator source states an Abbotsford pad rent. The live page's "$650 to $950/month" range and one Georgian Park card's "$782/month" line are **ALL UNVERIFIED site/listing copy** — flagged as such, never restated as fact; the governing figure is the specific site's tenancy agreement (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html). The same flag applies program-wide to the Langley page's $650–$950 range and the Chilliwack page's $550–$950 range — all UNVERIFIED site copy, noted here only as the standing MHPTA frame and not as facts about those pages' own research.
- **Assignment (MHPTA s. 28):** a tenant may assign a site tenancy, or sublet the site, only with (a) the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed obtained under the Regulation), (b) an order of the director, or (c) authorization in the tenancy agreement itself; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s. 28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The request runs on RTB-10 (research file §4; Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s. 28).
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42):** the landlord must have all necessary permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, may in defined circumstances apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).
- **Manufactured Home Registry + CSA label:** in BC the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered; mobile homes are built under CSA Z-240 and modular homes under CSA A-277, the home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached, and a lost label requires re-certification — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring and an electrical approval label is prepared, which can be used in lieu of a CSA label to list the home for sale (the official route behind the page's "silver label" shorthand) (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Is an Abbotsford park home a townhome?**
A: No. On a rented pad you own the manufactured home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA — no strata corporation, no strata lot, no Form B. Pad-rental parks are never townhouse stock in this program, and this file counts 0 townhouse complexes (§2; research file §4).

**Q: How much is pad rent in Abbotsford?**
A: No figure is stated in this file: no official source publishes Abbotsford pad rents, and the live page's $650–$950 range and one card's $782/month line are UNVERIFIED site/listing copy. The figure that governs is the specific site's tenancy agreement — confirm the current rent, what it includes and its increase history with the park for the exact site before offering (§3; research file §4).

**Q: How much can pad rent go up?**
A: Once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice, up to the yearly limit: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 (research file §7; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Which Abbotsford parks are verified as 55+?**
A: Three, as the 55-plus sibling carries them: Paradise Park (31313 Livingstone Ave), Georgian Park (3300 Horn St) and Maplewood Estates (2035 Martens St) — all pad-rental parks, documented as parks in §2.4, never as townhomes. The live page's claim that most Central Abbotsford/Clearbrook parks are 55+ is UNVERIFIED (§2.4; `~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`).

**Q: Does the park have to approve my purchase, and can it charge for that?**
A: Assignment needs the landlord's prior written consent (or deemed consent, a director's order, or authorization in the agreement) under MHPTA s. 28, consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s. 28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The request runs on RTB-10 (§3; research file §6).

**Q: What happens if the park closes or converts?**
A: Under MHPTA s. 42 the landlord must have all permits and approvals in place first, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes (§3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Why does the CSA label matter?**
A: A manufactured home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 for mobile homes, A-277 for modular homes); if it is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route before it can be listed for sale. The sale itself is only effective if registered in the Manufactured Home Registry (§3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

**Q: Do I pay PTT on a pad-rental purchase?**
A: UNVERIFIED in this file — the live page says flatly that PTT does not apply on a pad, but no Province PTT passage stating that treatment was opened in this program, so the claim is flagged, not adopted. A lawyer or notary should confirm PTT and registration handling for the exact structure before subjects come off (§3; research file §4).

**Q: Is there a median price for Abbotsford manufactured homes?**
A: No — SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category. The Abbotsford figures in §1 (Detached median $949,000; Condos & Townhomes median $497,500, combined and never townhome-only) are all-residential community context only, September 2026 (research file §7; SnapStats September 2026 report).

A: The Ministry file confirms Abbotsford's SD34 schools by name and address (§1) — but catchment assignment for a specific park address is UNVERIFIED, because this program holds feeder patterns, not a completed locator run for a park address (§1).
