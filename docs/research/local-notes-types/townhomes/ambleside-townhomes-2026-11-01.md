## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Ambleside is in the **District of West Vancouver** — the District's municipal town centre and waterfront: the Marine Drive commercial core around 14th–18th Streets, Ambleside Park and the Argyle Avenue waterfront, and the residential streets between the water and the Upper Levels Highway. District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no District source read publishes street-by-street boundaries for the Ambleside sub-area as a whole; the confirmed planning anchors are the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre on Marine Drive, the publicly owned waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets under the District's Waterfront Guiding Principles, and Ambleside Park itself. Ambleside is distinct from the Park Royal shopping-centre area immediately east — Park Royal is its neighbour, not part of this file. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- Nearby areas: Park Royal (east, across Taylor Way), Dundarave (west along Marine Drive and the Centennial Seawalk), the British Properties including Chartwell and Canterbury (north, uphill above Highway 1), and Sentinel Hill / Queens (north-west, uphill). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Where the attached stock actually sits: low-rise and mid-rise strata apartment buildings in the town-centre blocks and along Marine Drive and Bellevue Avenue, with detached houses in the RS zones on the streets north of Marine Drive. There is no townhouse node — see the verified zero in Section 2. Sources: the entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit and roads: TransLink route 250 runs on Marine Drive through Ambleside between Horseshoe Bay, Dundarave, Park Royal and downtown Vancouver; Park Royal Exchange, immediately east of Ambleside, is the western terminus of the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station. Marine Drive is the east–west waterfront route; Taylor Way and 15th Street / Lions Gate Bridge access connect it to Highway 1 and downtown Vancouver. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf and https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no local strata townhouse stock to apply Form B diligence to — but Ambleside's apartment stratas carry the full regime, and any future townhouse strata here will too:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — the diligence core for Ambleside's older Marine Drive and Bellevue Avenue buildings. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Westerlies, Sea Wind and Park Marine are flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). Ambleside's verified 55+ strata buildings (Westview Terrace, Tudor Gardens, Lincoln Gardens, per the 55-plus sibling) are apartment buildings — none is a townhouse, and Westerlies' directory "age restrictions" wording does not state an age and is UNCONFIRMED as a 55+ bylaw until the registered bylaws are read on a Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **District zoning and plan — West Vancouver's own pair only:** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. (White Rock's Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair belongs to White Rock ONLY and is never attributed to West Vancouver.) Section 200 of Bylaw 4662 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record, and lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; the bylaw's structure also carries Section 260 Ground Oriented Dwelling Zones, Section 300 Multiple Dwelling Zones (RM1 to RM5) and Section 700 Ambleside Centre Zones (AC1 & AC2). Form and Character Development Permit guidelines apply in the Ambleside Apartment Area and the Ambleside Village Centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010 and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **SSMUH as the Province and District state them:** provincial SSMUH legislation requires zoning to allow a minimum of 4 dwelling units on single-family and duplex parcels greater than 280 m², and a minimum of 6 units on such parcels greater than 280 m² near frequent bus service (stops with at least one route stopping at least every 15 minutes on average). **West Vancouver's local implementing-bylaw status is UNVERIFIED** — no District source confirming the adoption state of its SSMUH zoning amendments was read in the research pass, and none is stated here. Separately, the Ambleside Centre Local Area Plan was implemented by a provincial order that took effect April 7, 2026, after the District missed the Province's December 31, 2025 adoption deadline (the District delivered 58 of its 220-unit year-one housing target); the District must file progress reports with the Province every three months through 2026 and 2027 on the remaining directives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/local-governments-and-housing/housing-initiatives/smale-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026HMA0038-000377
- **Foreshore and hazard permits:** a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area — waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding — which applies to Ambleside's Bellevue Avenue and waterfront-edge properties; an Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on September 2, 2026; at Ambleside price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies here — that is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/property-taxes and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a BC presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for the Park Royal presale documented above, which is not Ambleside stock. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The waterfront took 50 years to assemble:** the District acquired waterfront lots from 1975 onward to complete a continuous public waterfront, and on March 31, 2025 it announced the final home, 1444 Argyle Avenue — the last of 32 lots — had been acquired, forming part of Brissenden Waterfront Park and connecting Ambleside Park to John Lawson Park under the 2016 Ambleside Waterfront Concept Plan. The publicly owned waterfront from 13th to 18th Streets is a stated community value in the District's 2013 Waterfront Guiding Principles. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/ambleside-waterfront-park-implementation-project
- **The Centennial Seawalk predates most of the stock:** built in 1967 for Canada's centennial, it runs roughly 1.7 km from Dundarave Park east to Ambleside — the walking link that makes the apartment strip function as one neighbourhood. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
- **Ambleside is the District's civic address, not just a shopping strip:** Municipal Hall, the West Vancouver Community Centre and Aquatic Centre at 2121 Marine Drive, the West Vancouver Memorial Library at 1950 Marine Drive and the Seniors' Activity Centre at 695 21st Street all cluster in and around the town centre. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/facilities/community-centre and https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/west-vancouver-memorial-library
- **Hollyburn Elementary's original structure was built in 1913** and SD45 describes the school as "situated in the picturesque seaside community of Ambleside" — the area's dated anchor for its early-1900s settlement, on a street grid the apartment buildings arrived in decades later. https://westvancouverschools.ca/schools/hollyburn/
- **The R2 RapidBus change is dated and specific:** since September 7, 2026 the R2 runs one-seat from Park Royal Exchange through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station, roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak — Ambleside is the closest West Vancouver residential area to that terminus. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/
- **The hospital is across the municipal line:** Lions Gate Hospital, 231 East 15th Street, North Vancouver (Vancouver Coastal Health), serves the North Shore including West Vancouver, and the North Vancouver Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at Suite 200, 221 West Esplanade, North Vancouver. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/lions-gate-hospital
