## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 11 complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Sunstone complexes, the Form B is also where the Sunstone Club cost-sharing shows up. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Sunbury Terrace (1989) is the age outlier in this roster; the 2009–2017 Sunstone stock's reports are about envelope and amenity planning. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Nordel complex is void in-entry — flag it. The directory's "rentals allowed" line for Sunstone (8355 Delsom Way) is consistent with current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is published for any Nordel townhouse complex — the Sunstone rental building's 55+ rule is an operator's tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw, and does not attach to the strata complexes beside it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Nordel. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Every verified complex here is strata, so that frame does not apply to any named Nordel stock.
- **Delta plan:** Official Community Plan, consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules; the former North Delta Area Plan was Schedule C.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and any source attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong. The ND12 Nordel Social Heart Development Permit Area (Appendix B, Bylaw No. 8400) is the policy frame for the 84 Avenue corridor stock. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** the research and sibling files cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017), with a residential ladder from RS1 at a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum through RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m² and RS7 at 335 m², with duplex zones RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m² and no RD5 zone. The program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as contested (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here and confirmed parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted on all single-detached and duplex lots citywide (four units; six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road), and Delta permits secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites in combination with single detached and duplex dwellings where the zoning applies. Relevant to Nordel's house lots, not to its strata complexes. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/235301/
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale townhouse complex is counted in this file; the two ND12 applications in the QUEUE are pre-application/application stage. Where any Nordel townhome is bought as a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000); the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000); the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year); CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Delta property taxes and utilities:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on balances outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026; the approved 2026 Financial Plan included a 2.9% property tax increase for City operational services. Delta's 2026 flat-rate utility fee is $1,658 for a single-family home, covering garbage/recycling and, where applicable, water and sewer. No Nordel-specific levy was confirmed — UNVERIFIED. http://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/utilities
- **Flood / Burns Bog edge:** the west edge borders the Delta Nature Reserve / Burns Bog Ecological Conservancy Area (access 10388 Nordel Court); a floodplain designation for Nordel's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read — Delta directs owners to confirm flood and development-permit status lot by lot, and ALR status is a property-specific ALC check. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/burns-bog-ecological-conservancy-area

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Delta Ridge a condo or a townhouse?** A townhouse — current listings classify its units as Row / Townhouse, 3 storeys, built 2017. It appears in the research file's condo list because directories group the 84 Avenue strata together; the dwelling classification governs. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3103372-5-11490-84-avenue-delta-british-columbia-v4c2m1-1039000/brochure
- **Q: What does a Sunstone strata fee actually buy?** In part, a shared club: listings for Kaleido and the other Sunstone collections describe exclusive access to the 12,000 sq ft Sunstone Club with outdoor pool, gym, basketball court and lounge — part of a Sunstone fee buys into that shared amenity structure rather than an in-building gym alone. The exact cost split is on the Form B. https://www.zolo.ca/delta-real-estate/10735-84-avenue/31
- **Q: Is Sunstone a seniors' community?** Only one building in it: the 70-unit Sunstone 55+ Apartments at 10775 Delsom Crescent are rental product for residents 55+. The strata townhomes and condos around it carry no published 55+ bylaw. https://www.comfortlife.ca/retirement-homes/sunstone-apartments/1370
- **Q: Can I rent out a Nordel townhouse?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and the registered bylaws should be read on the Form B package regardless. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed in the Sunstone collections?** The directory records the Sunstone complex at 8355 Delsom Way as pets allowed. For the other collections, no pet bylaw was published in the sources read — UNCONFIRMED complex by complex; the Form B and registered bylaws govern. http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/sunstone-8355-delsom-way
- **Q: Is Sunbury Terrace a normal townhouse strata?** Not quite: it is a bare-land strata plan (NWS2806 per the directory) of 12 half-duplex-style townhomes built 1989 — you own a bare-land strata lot with the home on it, which changes how lot-level responsibilities and insurance sit versus a conventional townhouse strata. Read the strata plan and bylaws, not just the Form B summary. https://www.tonycikes.com/listing/r3083112-8376-centre-street-delta-bc-v4c-3x4/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve a Delsom Crescent townhouse?** It depends on the exact address: the locator run for 11415 84 Avenue returned Richardson Elementary and North Delta Secondary, while a Sunstone townhome listing names Gray Elementary and Sands Secondary for its address. Run the exact address in the SD37 locator before marketing school appeal. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is more townhouse supply coming to the Social Heart?** Two applications were active in the research — LU009689 (apartment and townhouse, 112 Street and 82 Avenue) and LU009611 (mixed-use, 112 Street and 84 Avenue), both inside the ND12 permit area. Applications, not built stock, and neither is counted in Section 2. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/7117/7676/6583/9e2d0d28ed6635b4006024a144519a9d_LU009689_PIM_Boards_V3.pdf
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in Sunbury Terrace versus a 2015 Kaleido unit?** Different risk bands, same check: the depreciation report (mandatory every five years for 5+ lot stratas) plus the CRF balance on the Form B. A 1989 bare-land strata's report is about roofs and lot services; a 2015 strata's is about envelope and the shared club. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is Delta Gardens in Nordel, Scottsdale or Annieville?** Listings use all three names for the one complex — apartments on 84 Avenue, townhomes on 115 Street. The City's own application file places the site in the ND12 Nordel Social Heart permit area; search all three sub-area names or you will miss listings. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/164210/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Sunstone's condos were marketed as a one-off, which is why this pocket is townhouse-heavy:** a 2016 launch report states the 54 Sunstone Village Residences condominiums "are the first and will be the only condominiums available in all of Sunstone" — everything else in the master plan is townhomes, single-family homes by Polygon/Morningstar, or the 70 rental apartments. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/news/blog/sunstone-at-10500-delsom-crescent-delta-by-delsom-developments
- **The Sunstone land play is much bigger than any one complex:** a LoopNet overview describes Delsom Estates as a 100-acre master-planned community of eight multi-family developments with 650–700 multi-family units and 178 single-family homes, with Sunstone Village at its entrance at Delsom Way, Delsom Crescent and 84th Avenue. https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/10727-Delsom-Cres-Delta-BC/33040875/
- **Delta Gardens is two products under one name and one application:** the City file (LU007852) describes two 6-storey apartment buildings with 110 units and two 3-storey townhouse buildings with 12 units under one Comprehensive Development Zone in the ND12 area — the 2020 build year in listings covers both forms. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/164210/
- **The Social Heart is a permit area, not just a nickname:** ND12's stated objective in the 2024 OCP is to revitalize Nordel as the community focus for North Delta, and the two active applications at 112 Street sit inside it — lots and complexes inside the permit area carry different development expectations than the interior streets. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Nordel's west edge is a protected bog, not future lots:** Delta Nature Reserve (access 10388 Nordel Court) is the publicly accessible part of Burns Bog, protected as the Burns Bog Ecological Conservancy Area — west-edge lots border a conservation area, with the development-permit and setback checks that come with it. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/delta-nature-reserve
- **A named shopping centre and grocery store in Nordel could not be confirmed on an official City source in the research pass** — the OCP plans commercial facilities inside the ND12 permit area, but any page naming a specific Nordel centre or grocer is resting on the centre's own material: UNVERIFIED on a City source. Source: the research file, read 2026-11-01.
