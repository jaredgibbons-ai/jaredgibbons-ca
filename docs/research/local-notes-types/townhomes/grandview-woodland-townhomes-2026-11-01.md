## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **City zoning frame:** house lots sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a multiple dwelling with 3 or 4 units, a duplex or a single detached house, 464 m² for 5 units, and 557 m² for 6, 7 or 8 units; single detached houses may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and duplexes may include secondary suites and lock-off units; a multiplex of up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, is possible on qualifying lots. RT districts also apply on parts of the area's older house stock; the zone and district for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan is the area-specific plan, with the citywide Vancouver Plan over it — read the Community Plan before pricing land, because its study-area edges define where its policies reach. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-community-profile-2014.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-open-house-boards-180129.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%:** Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. The 3% rate applies in Vancouver only — it is stated here because this is a Vancouver file, and it never applies in Burnaby, Richmond or New Westminster. Due diligence on any purchase here should confirm the seller's declaration status. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Form B diligence (strata product, including the Woodland Block presale's strata form and the apartment strata in Section 2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver's own short-term rental rules apply on top. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a strata building in this area is flagged VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ ownership stock in Grandview-Woodland — the area's seniors housing is non-profit rental (Section 2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes / house forms:** the area's interior stock is fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees on a house, duplex or multiplex lot; diligence is title, municipal rules (By-law No. 3575 district schedules, the Community Plan) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. A multiplex lot's unit potential is a zoning-schedule question (R1-1 figures above), not a strata question. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for the Woodland Block presale. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until the disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Taxes and rebates:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Woodland Block presale), not resales. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file — none found, so nothing to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. - **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax hit a townhouse purchase here?** The tax itself is 3% on homes declared vacant under Vancouver's bylaw, and every owner must file the annual declaration — on a purchase, confirm the seller's declaration status and any unpaid EHT (the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty after). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the City's own licensing rules apply — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The hyphen is the tell.** The City's official name is "Grandview-Woodland"; the board's common form drops the hyphen. Copy that cannot settle the name usually cannot settle the boundary either — and the boundary is what decides whether edge projects like The Cut are in this market at all. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Trout Lake is at the area's southern edge, per the City** — close enough to anchor southern-block marketing, far enough that "steps to Trout Lake" claims for northern blocks deserve a map check. http://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/grandview.aspx
- **The area's ground was logged, then served by the Vancouver–New Westminster interurban railway,** and the first houses and city water arrived along Commercial Drive — the character-house stock on the interior streets descends from that settlement pattern, which is why the RT districts still sit over parts of the older house stock. http://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/grandview.aspx
- **There is no hospital inside Grandview-Woodland:** the health-authority pages name Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue, and Mount Saint Joseph Hospital, 3080 Prince Edward Street, for Vancouver — a hospital located inside the area itself: UNVERIFIED. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **The City profile names Commercial Drive and Hastings Street as the area's main commercial shopping streets** — but a specific grocery store name and civic address inside the area confirmed on an official source: UNVERIFIED. Treat any "grocery-anchored" claim for a specific block as unproven until the store is named and checked. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/grandview-woodland-community-profile-2014.pdf
- **Flood diligence here is a dataset check, not a neighbourhood trait:** the City's designated floodplain mapping is a citywide dataset covering coastal and Still Creek floodplains, and a parcel-level floodplain result for this area: UNVERIFIED — run the specific parcel rather than assuming the inlet-side blocks are or are not designated. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
