## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Short-term rentals PROHIBITED (city layer):** Pitt Meadows prohibits short-term rentals at the city level — carry this in every strata answer for this city: the provincial voiding of rental-restriction bylaws does not create a short-term rental right here. (Standing city rule for this slug, 2026-11-01; confirm the current bylaw wording for a specific property.)
- **Strata townhomes — Form B diligence:** Strata Property Act s.59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Applies to Coho, Meadowland Village and Willow Creek on their strata tenure; for Coho II, Shoreline, Nature's Walk and Bonson, tenure is UNCONFIRMED in the assembled sources, so the document set is confirmed only once title is checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the city STR prohibition above applies regardless). Any directory or listing "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this file is void on that point and flagged in-entry in the sibling files. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss.123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Meadowland Village carries a published 55+ restriction in its listing record — verify it in the registered bylaws on the Form B. The Fairways' age position is UNCONFIRMED and it must never be called 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in this assembly. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so a tenure-UNCONFIRMED complex is never sold with the wrong document set.
- **City plan and zoning:** Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020 (adopted September 27, 2022), and Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 — townhouses are a permitted form in the R-3, R-4, R-6 and RM-1 zones, with RM-1 set for townhouse developments at a minimum lot area of 0.4 ha; the 1,215 m² R-zone figure is a subdivision minimum only, not a development minimum on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH update. The North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Suites:** secondary suites and garden suites are accessory uses in the RS, RR, R-4 and R-6 zones; the City garden suite guide sets one suite per lot at 33 m² to 90 m². https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Floodplain — the standard Pitt Meadows subject:** over 95% of the land base is in the Fraser/Pitt floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the settled figure; the "over 85%" figure on live pages, now also appearing with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution, is flagged at the top of this file as a live conflict), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008; taxes carry a separate drainage levy. Check the flood construction level, any covenant on title and insurance early. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **ALR:** 78% of the land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; A-1 subdivision minimum is 8 ha and A-2 is 16 ha, and ALC rules govern farm parcels at the city's rural edges. An ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Property taxes:** 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 — Class 1 Residential $2.8438 general municipal, $0.1082 drainage levy and $0.0583 Metro Vancouver levy per $1,000; taxes due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Buyer frame (every purchase in this file):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the first-time buyer exemption (first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No verified presale exists in this assembly, so REDMA s.21 rescission / s.18 trust deposits are noted for completeness only if a future presale is verified. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number moved, and the old number acquired a new source:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the land base is floodplain, while the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 put it at about 86% — and live pages now quote "over 85%" with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution. These notes use 95% and flag the conflict rather than smoothing it over. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Mid Meadows has townhomes and no apartments:** the sibling pass verified zero apartment-condo buildings in Mid Meadows — its entire strata story is the Osprey Village townhouse pocket, which is also why its SnapStats attached sales ratio (33% in September 2026) is townhome-driven and must not be quoted as a condo ratio. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/mid-meadows-pitt-meadows-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **A funded replacement for Pitt Meadows Secondary is in design:** SD42's Strategic Facilities Plan page carries a funded replacement school with a neighbourhood learning centre in the design and development phase — a dated, sourced planning fact for families pricing the Harris Road corridor. https://www.sd42.ca/board-of-education/strategic-facilities-plan/
- **The Harris Road rail crossing is being grade-separated:** the Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project is a four-lane underpass below the CPKC rail crossing at Harris Road — the city's main north–south townhouse corridor runs through that work. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects/pitt-meadows-road-and-rail-improvements-project/past-project
- **The airport is in the south of the city:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK), owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society, sits in the south end near the South Bonson townhouse pocket, with a Fly YPK Neighbourly Program and a noise concern process — check flight paths against a specific South Bonson address. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport
- **The hospital is next door, and that is normal here:** Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge, serves both Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows, with the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, Maple Ridge. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital
