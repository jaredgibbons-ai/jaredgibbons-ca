## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 counted strata complex, and every apartment strata documented above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Chesterfield Manor has no Form B — share-ownership diligence is the corporation's own documents, a different process entirely. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — which is exactly the distinction in The Beeches' own history, where the strata's CRT case concerned a short-term hostel use, not a long-term tenancy. The directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Ridge Park Gardens and Victoria Park West (160 West Keith Road, in the condos sibling) are flagged VOID for long-term rentals in-entry in Section 2 and the sibling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://outlawhotels.com/country-and-city-reports/vancouver/north-van-airbnb-host/
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Ridge Park Gardens and Sundale are the corridor's two verified 55+ buildings — both apartment-form. Any "19+" term (Chesterfield Manor's listing) is not a lawful strata age bylaw and is a tenure warning, not an age restriction to price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the side-street house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (City Zoning Bylaw No. 6700) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Note the middle form documented in Section 2: a home can look detached and still sit in a bare-land-style small strata (the 3-home Bewicke Avenue strata) — the title, not the built form, decides which diligence applies.
- **City zoning and plan — the CITY pair only:** Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 8400 (2014, amended December 8, 2025) and Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 (1995). Side streets sit in the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone adopted December 8, 2025: a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; the GO Zone increased permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allows up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. One secondary suite is permitted per single-family property (owner must reside at the property), and a suite may combine with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and Building Permit. The District of North Vancouver's OCP 7900 / Zoning 3210 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Great Street plan:** Council approved the Lonsdale Great Street Plan on April 20, 2026 — a 20-year vision for the Central Lonsdale main street, with the first phase of public-space improvements underway in 2026, funded by the Province's Growing Communities Fund. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/News-Room/News-Releases/2026/7/7/The-City-of-North-Vancouver-launches-next-phase-of-Lonsdale-Great-Street-improvements
- **Hazard permits:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank — relevant near the Wagg Creek and Mahon Park ravine lands at the area's west side, which is The Beeches' side of the corridor. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies here — that is a City of Vancouver tax only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for One20. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
