## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all three counted complexes are over the threshold on their verified scale (Brighton 145 units, Windfall 54; Archstone Rockridge's count is UNCONFIRMED but the complex presents as multi-building strata). A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the 3 is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line was found for the counted complexes — Brighton's building record instead records rentals as allowed, which is consistent with current law; any such line met elsewhere for a complex here is flagged VOID in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted complex here is verified as 55+, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village in this scope, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in this scope (Section 2) — a listing calling a counted complex "fee simple" in the non-strata sense contradicts the verified record and needs a title check ("freehold strata" is strata title, not fee-simple townhousing).
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge **Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** governs — urban-fringe houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605: minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%), and the rural/agricultural zones on the acreage side carry the full three-tier farm minimums in the same bylaw's schedule — A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, with RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text (a flat "$2,500+" farm figure would be an error — the tiers govern). **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, resolved nowhere** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). No plan is written for "North Maple Ridge" as a whole; the Silver Valley Area Plan update, adopted November 25, 2025, covers the north part of this scope. https://www.mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/official-community-plan (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/north-maple-ridge.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor:** the corridor plan area lies south/west of this scope (the corridor runs from the city's west boundary to 221 Street) and was **ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026** per the City record (City news release Feb 4, 2026; OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 given third reading and adoption Jan 27, 2026) — cited as adopted here; the earlier contrary/contested reading in the sibling condos file is noted in Section 5. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. On ALR parcels the provincial ALR rules also govern, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (Section 2). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/north-maple-ridge.md)
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty September 2, 2026 on unpaid current taxes. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs, and no such row is documented in this file's research. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
