## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The farm rules govern most of this ground — read these first.** In the A1 Agriculture zone (Delta Zoning Bylaw, s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. The A1 zone provides one farm house per lot, a farm house maximum of 500 m², a farm home plate maximum of 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha. A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets an 8 ha subdivision minimum; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **ZONING BYLAW NUMBER — CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title reads "8400, 2025." The research file works from No. 7600; this file cites the zone sections as the research file states them and flags the number itself as CONTESTED rather than settling it. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **ALR stratification bar:** an ALR residence cannot be stratified — ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1). That is the legal reason the farm belt produces freehold farm product, not strata townhouses. The ALC generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application is capped at 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence if local bylaws allow it; and one additional detached residence is permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — anything beyond that needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm status thresholds — full three-tier form:** $2,500 in farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and is never used here. (Program standing correction, 2026-11-01)
- **Community plan:** Official Community Plan 2024, "Housing Our Future," Bylaw No. 8400 — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules — applies to the whole area; its Agriculture policies (OCP s. 2.6, informed by the City's 2023 Agricultural Plan) apply to Delta's agricultural land, and the urban part is covered by the same consolidated Land Use Plan. Any page citing a Tsawwassen-style "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" for Delta land is conflating the old Bylaw 3950 schedules with the current plan — flagged wherever met. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites (Delta, as the City states it):** Delta's small-scale multi-unit housing amendments were adopted June 24, 2024 (Bylaws 8394 + 8395), allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — urban-edge context, not a farm-belt or townhome-complex rule. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/233832/
- **Strata rules — stated for the urban-edge strata product only (Skye, in its home file):** Form B diligence under Strata Property Act s. 59 (monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with depreciation report and bylaws attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); CRF minimum 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry; only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** a fee-simple home in East Delta has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. Most East Delta land is in exactly this position. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md)
- **Wells and septic:** search the well in the provincial GWELLS registry (wells built before the Water Sustainability Act may not be in it); Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood and streamside:** the City requires checks for flood protection requirements, the Riparian Areas Regulation and streamside rules before development near regulated water bodies; the City is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **Taxes and rebates (any townhome purchase on the urban edge):** PTT 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged as such, never a single rate. Exemptions: first-time buyers (exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000); newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. Delta property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with 5% after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY and does not apply here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a developer sale exists on the urban edge, the buyer must receive the disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is East Delta a real neighbourhood I can search by?** No — it is a Paragon sub-area name for the Ladner farm belt and East Ladner edge; the City publishes no boundary for it. Price and compare any property as what it legally is: an ALR farm parcel or an urban East Ladner lot under the consolidated OCP, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/219618/
- **Q: Are there townhouses in East Delta?** No verified townhouse complex is counted in this file (0 strata, 0 fee-simple). The one named project on this ground, Skye at Holly Park on 60B Street, is counted once in the Holly townhomes file in this series; further East Delta townhome names are UNVERIFIED in the research file. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md)
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide a farm parcel here?** Subdivision in A1 needs a minimum 8 ha new lot, OCP compliance and ALC Act approval where applicable; and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can a farm parcel have a second dwelling?** In some cases: one principal residence (capped at 500 m² without an ALC application) plus one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels; Delta's A1 zone also allows one accessory farm dwelling unit within those size limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What does the 3,600 m² home plate mean for me?** It caps the residential footprint — farm house plus accessory buildings — in the A1 zone, separate from the 500 m² farm house maximum itself. Check the plate, not just the house, before pricing an expansion. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Q: Is there a Form B on an East Delta purchase?** Only if the property is strata — and no strata townhouse is counted in this file. A fee-simple farm or urban-edge home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal records and registered covenants. (Section 3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve an East Delta address?** Address-based in SD37 (K–7 / 8–12, no middle tier) — and no sample catchment was run for this sub-area, so the answer for any specific address is UNVERIFIED until it is run in the SD37 School Locator. Never market a farm property as "in" a school's area without that run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What about water and septic on a farm property?** Search the well in GWELLS (older wells may be unrecorded) and ask for the Authorized Person's sewerage filings — Fraser Health requires onsite systems to comply with the Sewerage System Regulation. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Can I use North Delta stats to value an East Delta home?** No. SnapStats' Delta coverage is North Delta, East Delta has no SnapStats row, and the research file states North Delta numbers must never be presented as East Delta numbers. Pricing evidence here is the property's own comparables, not a community table. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/east-delta.md)
- **Q: Does the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax apply?** No — the 3% Empty Homes Tax is Vancouver only. Delta's own calendar is what bites: 2026 taxes due July 2, 2026, 5% after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
