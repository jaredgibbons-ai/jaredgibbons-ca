## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The stratification bar (why there is no townhouse stock to regulate here):** ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6 ("Strata Plans Cause Subdivision") — a strata plan may not be approved or registered in the ALR unless permitted under the Agricultural Land Commission Act regime, and the program-wide verified position is that ALR residences cannot be stratified (with Strata Property Act s. 239 and ALCA s. 21(1)). Combined with the ALR housing caps below, this is the structural reason Section 2 is a zero. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALR housing rules, in full three-tier farm form where farm classification is in issue:** parcel-level ALR status is per address and was not confirmed for any parcel in the research: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping. Where land is in the ALR: generally no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area; and one additional detached residence up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. BC Assessment farm-classification minimum gross farm income is in full three-tier form — $10,000 (under 0.8 ha), $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), and $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (over 4 ha); a flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever it is met. Farm-sector feeds are generic — never present a feed card as a confirmed sector operation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Chilliwack plan and zoning:** Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 5500, with the Agricultural Area Plan as Schedule B1 (Sardis is Schedule B5 of the same bylaw — never "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024"). Zoning is Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000: in the AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone the minimum lot area is 7.5 ha for land excluded or exempted from the ALR or approved for subdivision within the ALR, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, and 0.2 ha for a homesite severance or boundary adjustment approved by the Agricultural Land Commission; minimum lot width is 25 m. Smaller residential lots exist near the school and along Chilliwack Central Road, but no residential zone minimum for them was confirmed from the bylaw text in the research: UNVERIFIED. Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024 — the City's SSMUH/suite rules are as that bylaw and the City's housing pages state them. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **Farm character the plan protects:** the Agricultural Area Plan states the farm sector in the plan area is dominated by dairy and poultry operations, nurseries and greenhouses. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf
- **Flood rules:** the City's Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal; the City maintains a diking network for the Fraser and Vedder River systems. Which East Chilliwack watercourses carry parcel-level setbacks is a per-property check: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Wells and septic:** servicing for a single parcel is UNVERIFIED in the research — verify well and septic on any rural parcel before removing subjects. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/east-chilliwack.md)
- **Chilliwack property taxes (CITY regime — see the statement at the top of this file):** rates are the sum of each taxing authority's levy, with separate rates for each property class and additional taxes levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. Notices are due the first working day after July 1 each year, with a 5% late payment penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. The FVRD rural regime does not apply inside the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Form B (strata law, stated for completeness):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — on a resale strata purchase the Form B states strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no application to the freehold farm and rural parcels that make up this sub-area, whose townhouse-form analogue does not exist here: a fee-simple purchase in this sub-area is a detached house on land, with NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — title and municipal/ALC rules only, stated plainly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame, for completeness):** since July 1, 2024, stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. Since Nov 1, 2023, stratas must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF. **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID** since Nov 24, 2022 for strata property anywhere in BC; **age bylaws:** Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 allow only a 55-or-older strata residency bylaw, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. None of these rules creates or affects stock in this sub-area, where no strata townhouse is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **GST rebates:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT + exemptions, with the tier flag:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2%, and worked examples must show the tier math. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
