## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no townhouse stock here, so this section is (a) the hazard and zoning rules that govern any future townhouse or small multi-unit project on the bluff, (b) the fee-simple position that describes the stock that actually exists, and (c) the standard BC strata and tax rules a buyer needs when the search spills inland, where the townhomes actually are.

- **Fee-simple is the tenure that exists here — plainly.** A detached house on the bluff is fee-simple: there is NO strata corporation, NO Form B, and NO strata fees. The buyer's diligence is municipal — zoning, the SD2 development permit area below, covenants on title — plus any homeowners-association terms if a specific lot carries them (none verified area-wide: UNCONFIRMED). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Development Permit Area SD2 — the rule that defines this shore.** OCP Bylaw No. 8400 names "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)," Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions): the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, the ocean-frontage slopes are subject to erosion, and existing vegetation and tree cover on the slopes must be protected. Any future townhouse project on this shore would be designed inside SD2 first and the zoning second. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SD2 building rules:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation, business or storage of goods damageable by floodwaters, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding is required; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **The beach is not part of the lot.** Delta states foreshore and water use is interjurisdictional (federal Fisheries Act, provincial Water Sustainability Act and Riparian Areas Regulation, flood protection requirements and Delta bylaws), and the foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017 (RS zones on this shore, assigned parcel by parcel; the batch-8 research itself states homes sit under "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 RS zones"); an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates for the zoning bylaw. Neither number is asserted as settled here. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024).** The current OCP is a single consolidated plan — it contains NO lettered schedules. The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the PREVIOUS OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; any file or page attributing a Schedule D.1 Tsawwassen Area Plan to Bylaw 8400 is WRONG — the "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" conflation is an error wherever met. Farm land in the plan is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH (the only route to small multi-unit product here).** Delta adopted its small-scale multi-unit housing zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide, subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot. On this shore the SD2 hazard conditions sit on top of that permission; SSMUH product is typically fee-simple or bare-land in form, and strata conversion would be a separate step — do not market a future SSMUH build as a "townhouse complex" until its tenure is registered. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Strata rules that apply the moment a buyer looks at a townhouse inland.** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59): discloses monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days of request, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it (a strata under five lots sits UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (inland strata).** Every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — a directory "rentals not allowed" line for an inland Tsawwassen building is VOID in-entry. Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 only a 55+ age bylaw is valid (with the May 1, 2023 exemptions for a younger spouse or partner, defined children, legacy residents and live-in caregivers); that is the framework behind The Lexington's and Tsawwassen Greene's 55+ rules in Tsawwassen Central. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (new construction only).** Resale homes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax and buyer programs.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of that 3% + 2% and is flagged as such wherever met. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000, and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs, the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** No presale project inside English Bluff is verified, so REDMA is background only: where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here:** the 3% Empty Homes Tax is VANCOUVER ONLY and is never used for a Delta property. Basis: City of Vancouver tax, stated for contrast only.
