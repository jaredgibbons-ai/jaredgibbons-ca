## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial and Burnaby rules govern any strata purchase an Oakdale buyer makes at Lougheed Town Centre or in Burquitlam, and any future stratified multiplex or rowhouse unit inside this sub-area.

- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no in-sub-area strata stock to apply this to today. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption is the live question for any small stratified multiplex built on an Oakdale lot. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Oakdale complex appears in Section 2 — there is no complex for one to attach to; any such line on a Lougheed or Burquitlam Form B package is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership and no seniors housing of any type in Oakdale — no home here may be marketed as a 55+ purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the sub-area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **Burnaby zoning — current bylaw:** the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, was adopted June 9, 2026 and took effect July 1, 2026**; the former Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 (1965) is repealed and must never be cited for Burnaby — any source still citing Bylaw 4742 is superseded. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites; and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **SSMUH / suites as the city states them:** the R1 district above IS Burnaby's SSMUH framework for these streets — the frequent-transit test matters most for lots toward the Lougheed Town Centre station side, where the jump from 4 units to 6 turns on a lot of at least 281 m² sitting within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. Distance to the corridor stops is therefore a priced fact, not a slogan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Burnaby 2050 OCP and the Lougheed town centre:** Lougheed is one of Burnaby's town centres, and the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — the Lougheed town-centre edge is where plan expectations change fastest, so review the 2050 land use map before assuming density beyond the R1 district. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. The detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, rates carry a 5% discount if paid by the due date, and for single-family homes sewer is billed as a sewer parcel tax on the property tax notice. A Coquitlam purchase across North Road carries Coquitlam's tax calendar instead — a different city, a different bill. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Lougheed or Burquitlam presale), not resales. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies in Burnaby. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any developer-contract purchase at Lougheed Town Centre or Burquitlam. No presale is counted in this file. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
