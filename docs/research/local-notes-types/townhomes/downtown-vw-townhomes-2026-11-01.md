## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Downtown VW is the downtown peninsula's Vancouver West board side: the central business district and the Georgia / Granville / Burrard / Robson core, on Burrard Inlet's south shore. The City describes the containing Downtown local area as a peninsula bounded by Burrard Inlet on the north, False Creek and Yaletown to the south, the West End and Stanley Park to the west, and Gastown, Chinatown and Strathcona to the east; a board-published boundary for Downtown VW alone: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx
- Main streets: West Georgia Street, Granville Street, Burrard Street and Howe Street. The City's Downtown history names Georgia and Granville Streets as the city-centre streets the centre moved up to from Water and Cordova Streets after incorporation. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/downtown.aspx
- Where the housing stock concentrates: almost entirely strata apartment form in towers above and beside the business district; detached houses are not a VW-core housing form, and townhouse-form units exist mainly as ground-oriented units at tower bases (Section 2). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- Transit anchors: the core is the hub where the Expo Line, Millennium Line and Canada Line meet (Waterfront and Burrard stations); the R5 Hastings St RapidBus terminates at Burrard Station, route 22 also runs from Burrard Station, and route 50 starts at Waterfront Station and runs via Chinatown, Granville Island and the False Creek south shore to Olympic Village Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt050.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no strata townhouse complex to diligence here — the diligence is tower diligence.** A Downtown VW ground-oriented unit is a strata lot in an apartment tower, so the full apartment-strata package governs: Form B Information Certificate under Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In the core's hotel-mixed tower (Private Residences at Hotel Georgia) the Form B is also where the residential strata's separation from the hotel and office components shows. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — every Downtown VW tower strata is far over the threshold; a strata of four or fewer lots sits under that threshold, and none is verified here. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing. Any Downtown VW listing or directory line reading "rentals not allowed" is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears; the Hotel Georgia directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with the law but is not what makes renting lawful. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in the VW core; the sibling 55-plus file on disk carries the area's age-restricted position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified in the VW core. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and registered covenants/HOA documents only. No VW-core product verified in this pass takes that form.
- **City zoning and plans:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — with CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also applying in parts of the area. C-3A shows no minimum site area for its listed uses; a minimum lot size figure for DD or CD-1 parcels: UNVERIFIED. A plan named only for the Downtown local area or the VW core: UNVERIFIED; the citywide Vancouver Plan applies. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-C-3A.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Vancouver's house-zone multiplex framework does not create VW-core townhouse stock — there are no house lots in this core; the governing form is tower strata under the DD schedule and CD-1 districts above. A suite or multiplex claim for a VW-core address: UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's district documents are read.
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in it is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; the VW core's Burrard Inlet shoreline is the edge to check against that dataset; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property tax calendar and additional school tax:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. At VW-core price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — most VW-core stock is resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
