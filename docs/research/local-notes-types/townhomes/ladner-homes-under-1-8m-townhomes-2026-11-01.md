## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata complex cross-referenced in Section 2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level fee, pet and rental lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating budget must go to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID / age bylaws 55+ only:** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory "rentals not allowed" line VOID in-entry for any cross-referenced complex; only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** 48 Landing — the band area's one verified fee-simple townhouse-form product, counted in the Ladner Elementary home file — has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only.
