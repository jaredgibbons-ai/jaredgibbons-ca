## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In a MIXED complex (both counted complexes here), the Form B is also where the buyer confirms which units are townhouse-form and how common expenses are allocated between the forms. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Both counted complexes are multi-unit Park Crescent stratas; confirm the lot count and the current report on each Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry: Westhaven's directory line "rentals allowed with restrictions" carries a rental-restriction element that is VOID for long-term rentals under this rule (Westhaven is apartment stock, documented above, not counted here). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither counted complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B. The sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ ownership stock in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (none verified here):** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Glenbrooke North's counted stock is strata only; do not let a "townhouse" marketing line on a Park Crescent apartment unit (see 600 Park Crescent, Section 2) blur the form — built form governs. Basis: the tenure definitions applied across this program.
- **City zoning and plan — corrected districts (standing position):** Zoning Bylaw **No. 6680, 2001**; OCP **Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**. Townhouse districts are **RT-2–RT-2C (ss. 410–413)** and **RT-3/3A/3B in Queensborough (ss. 417–419)** only — "RT-A/RT-B" in the research file is an error, corrected at the top of this file. RT-2's published form figures: base density 18 units per net acre or FSR 0.6, height cap 25 feet, site coverage cap 40%. RS-1: minimum site 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft), FSR 0.5. RM-1 (apartment districts near the counted stock): coverage 40%, 35 units per net acre / FSR 1.2. https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php/articles/5682.php and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Zoning_Bylaw_6680_2001%20(consolidated%20June%208,%202026).pdf
- **Suites as the City states them:** one secondary suite per house; the City allows a property to have both a secondary suite and a laneway/carriage house (Zoning Bylaw s. 190.29 — a provision title-search and advocate sources quote; confirm the current section text in the consolidated bylaw before relying on it). Adaptable housing: at least 40% of single-storey units in multiple-unit buildings must be adaptable (Adaptable Housing Bylaw 7464, 2011). https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/development/planning-and-development/planning/secondary-suites-and-laneway-carriage-houses

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The sub-area's townhouses hide inside mixed complexes:** both verified complexes pair townhouse units with apartment units under one strata — a search filtered to "townhouse" misses the apartment-side context, and a search filtered to the complex name misses the townhouse units entirely. The Form B and the strata plan are the only documents that show the whole picture. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **One street, three sub-area treatments:** Jamieson Court carries a Fraserview NW apartment tower (Palace Quay), a Fraserview NW townhouse complex (Jamieson Place, 72 Jamieson Court) and a Glenbrooke North care facility (AgeCare Royal City, 77 Jamieson Court) — address-level sub-area filing here is a listings convention, not geography, and this file follows the board filing with the conflict flagged at the top. Sources: Section 2 entries + sibling Fraserview NW files.
- **The heritage boundary is a hard line with soft marketing:** Queen's Park's HCA rules are routinely assumed to spill north; they do not. Glenbrooke North trades on Queen's Park adjacency without the HCA permit regime — verify the boundary side for any specific lot near Sixth Avenue. Basis: sibling condos file, §1.
- **Two buildings share one name in the directories:** Eight West / 8 West covers both 85 and 55 Eighth Avenue — a strata-fee or restriction figure pulled under the name alone can belong to the wrong building. Basis: sibling condos file, §2.
- **The middle school is the sub-area's anchor institution:** École Glenbrook Middle School, 701 Park Crescent, sits inside the sub-area — but its Late French Immersion is a Program of Choice, so "in the Glenbrook catchment" and "in the immersion program" are two different claims. https://www.sd40.bc.ca/schools/registration/
- **SnapStats spells it "GlenBrooke":** the report's capital-B spelling differs from the City's "Glenbrooke North" — searches and page copy should carry the City spelling, with the report spelling noted so the row is findable. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
