## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted strata complexes in the count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For Peninsula Village the Form B is also where the published 55+ position is actually proved — listing statements are not proof (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both counted complexes are over the threshold on their verified scale (Peninsula Village 95 homes; Keystone on its complex scale — confirm per complex on the Form B). Peninsula Village's own 2014 depreciation report (LMS 133) is the corridor example of the document set — and its age is itself a diligence point: ask for the most recent report. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing; count home §3).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met for a corridor complex is void for long-term rentals (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Peninsula Village is published as 55+ in listings ("One person 55+") — the registered bylaw on the Form B governs; Keystone listings state no age restriction (count home §3; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in the corridor (§2.2): a fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Surrey rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. Work from the current plan, not the 1995 concept plan: the corridor's designations now come from the Surrey 2050 OCP following Council's December 2, 2024 endorsement of Corporate Report R246 — check the parcel's current designation and zone in COSMOS before pricing development potential (research file §§1, 4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south).
- **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Homes fronting the boulevard's frequent bus service may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, habitable floor area not exceeding **40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area**; a suite is not a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only — the owner must live at the property as their primary residence and tenants cannot run one; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals).
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).
- **Heritage frontage:** the guide states the royal oaks and the Semiahmoo Trail are City heritage sites, which can affect work near them — check before planning frontage work (research file §6, site copy; https://jaredgibbons.ca/king-george-corridor-guide.html).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: How many townhome complexes are in the King George Corridor?**
A: Two verified, both strata, counted once in the king-george-corridor townhomes file — Peninsula Village (2500 152 St) and Keystone (2925 King George Blvd) — and this guide file adds 0 new. The corridor's other verified stock is 10 apartment-condo buildings in the condos program (§2).

**Q: Is the King George Corridor the whole of King George Boulevard?**
A: No — not on this page, in the City plan or in the board data. The plan area and sub-area run from 8 Avenue to the Nicomekl River in South Surrey; a Newton or City Centre listing on the same road is a different sub-area with different numbers, and Whalley towers are never corridor stock (§1).

**Q: Keystone fronts the boulevard like an apartment block — condos or townhomes?**
A: Townhouses. Listings classify its units Row / Townhouse, 3 storeys, built 2011, and built form governs over the streetscape impression. The corridor's apartment buildings are the 10 counted in the condos sibling; Keystone is not one of them. Its listings split its subdivision filing between King George Corridor and Elgin Chantrell — the corridor filing is followed (§2.1).

**Q: Is Peninsula Village really 55+ — and do both of us have to be 55?**
A: Listings state the rule as "One person 55+," and 55+ is the one age bylaw BC law permits — but a listing statement is not proof; the registered bylaw on the Form B is. Provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a qualifying resident (or a legacy-exempt resident) is exempt, as are live-in caregivers and legacy residents (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01 as expanded May 1, 2023) (§§2.1, 3).

**Q: How did the corridor sell in September 2026?**
A: Detached 85 / 8 / 9%; Condos & Townhomes 131 / 27 / 21% — the 27 attached solds were the most of any S Surrey White Rock sub-area that month. Sub-area medians are not published; the community medians ($1,628,000 / $700,000) are not the corridor's (§1).

**Q: Can a house lot in the corridor add units?**
A: Under SSMUH, most eligible lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary carry 3 units (under 280 m²) or 4 units (280 m² or more), and eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop can carry up to 6 — boulevard-adjacent lots may qualify, so check the parcel's zone and Frequent Bus Stop Area status in COSMOS. Price the BRT as a plan, not a service: station locations and timing for a specific address are UNVERIFIED (§§1, 3).

**Q: Which schools serve a corridor address?**
A: The one settled locator sample on this ground is 1785 148 St → H.T. Thrift Elementary / Semiahmoo Secondary (Ministry spelling "H T Thrift" — punctuation difference disclosed). Every other corridor catchment is UNVERIFIED: the corridor crosses several catchments, the nearest school is not proof, and the guide's own Semiahmoo Trail → Elgin Park statement is site copy. Note the guide's main FAQ leaves its schools question unanswered (§1).

**Q: What should I check at the corridor's north end?**
A: The floodplain. Parcels near the Nicomekl River sit in Surrey's coastal floodplain system and flood-construction requirements are parcel-specific; parcel-level flood levels are UNVERIFIED in this file (§1).

**Q: Strata or non-strata — which townhouse am I looking at in the corridor?**
A: Strata, on both verified named complexes: Peninsula Village is expressly Freehold Strata (LMS 133) and Keystone is strata townhouse product per its listings and the count home's stated basis. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in the corridor (§2).
