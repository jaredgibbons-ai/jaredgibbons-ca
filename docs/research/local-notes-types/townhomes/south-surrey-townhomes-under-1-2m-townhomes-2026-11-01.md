## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhome cross-referenced in §2).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days (one week, as the live page itself states), maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (research file §4; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in §2 is void for long-term rentals and is flagged on that basis in its home file. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted in the cross-referenced stock in §2.

**Surrey / White Rock zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suite cap: 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** — the "90 m²" figure is NOT current (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting.
- **White Rock ground** is governed by White Rock's own bylaws — Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP Bylaw 2017 No. 2220 — never Surrey's; the band crosses the municipal boundary, so check which municipality a unit is in before applying any rule (research file §6; standing correction, 2026-11-01).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing — with the band's worked PTT examples.**

- **PTT tiers:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000; 2% of the portion greater than $200,000 and up to and including $2,000,000; 3% of the portion greater than $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax).
- **Worked example — $900,000 townhome (inputs: price $900,000, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($900,000 − $200,000 = $700,000) = $14,000; **total PTT = $16,000.**
- **Worked example — $1,150,000 townhome (inputs: price $1,150,000, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($1,150,000 − $200,000 = $950,000) = $19,000; **total PTT = $21,000.**
- **Worked example — $1,200,000 townhome, the band cap (inputs: price $1,200,000, no exemption):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000; 2% × ($1,200,000 − $200,000 = $1,000,000) = $20,000; **total PTT = $22,000.**
- **Exemptions:** first-time buyers — full exemption on the first $500,000 at fair market value ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). The live page states the same thresholds as site copy (a resale townhome up to $835,000 can qualify for the first-time buyer exemption, saving up to $8,000; a brand-new townhome up to $1.1M for the newly built exemption, partial to $1.15M) — carried as site copy in the research file, with the general PTT rates above as the verified frame (research file §§4–5).
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST applies to new construction, not resales (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA, same source).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). **No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug** — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the south-surrey-townhomes-under-1-2m page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply in Surrey or White Rock — Vancouver only.
- **Presales / REDMA.** 7-day rescission under s. 21 and deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for Harlowe-type presale contracts; a presale is never resale band stock (§2.4) (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

**Q: What PTT would I pay at $900,000, $1.15M and the $1.2M cap?**
A: With no exemption: $900,000 → $16,000 (1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $700,000 = $14,000); $1,150,000 → $21,000 ($2,000 + 2% × $950,000 = $19,000); $1,200,000 → $22,000 ($2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = $20,000). The tier math is shown in full in §3.

**Q: Can an exemption cut that PTT?**
A: Possibly. The first-time buyer exemption covers the first $500,000 at prices up to $835,000 (phasing to $860,000), and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000 and eliminated at $1,150,000 — so a brand-new townhome at $1.15M sits exactly at the elimination point. Confirm eligibility on the Province's pages before relying on either (§3).

**Q: What should I check in a townhome's strata documents?**
A: The Form B Information Certificate, the bylaws, recent minutes, the budget and the depreciation report — the live page's own checklist, which also states the strata must provide the Form B within one week under the Strata Property Act. Mind the age split the page itself draws: Grandview Heights carries the newest complexes in this band and King George Corridor / Pacific Douglas the older, lower-priced ones (site copy) — an older complex's depreciation report and levy history matter more than its list price (§3; research file §6).
