## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The honest headline:** the BC strata-townhouse rulebook has **no residential building in this district to attach to**. The standard program frame is carried below so this file stays consistent with the rest of the townhomes program, followed by the rules that actually govern land here.
- **Form B diligence (program frame — no Lake City strata townhouse exists to apply it to):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states a strata lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (program frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote (a 4-lot-and-under strata sits UNDER that threshold); since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. These rules bind residential strata corporations — and, in their own commercial form, industrial strata corporations, whose buyers should ask for the same documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental and age bylaws (program frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid, and only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No residential building here carries either kind of bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none exist here. The rule, stated plainly for any fee-simple product anywhere in Burnaby: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. In this district the municipal rules permit no housing to begin with (below).
- **What actually governs here — plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** (adopted December 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP) directs employment lands to be **protected and intensified** for industrial uses — distribution, warehousing, repair, construction yards, infrastructure, outdoor storage, wholesale, manufacturing, trade and e-commerce. The Lake City Community Plan (2000) remains the district framework under it. Residential conversion of these lands is the opposite of the plan direction. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://burnaby.widen.net/s/7grv6g7v62/lum---lake-city
- **What actually governs here — zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. The specific 2026 district and permitted uses for any individual Lake City parcel are UNVERIFIED in this pass — confirm in writing with Burnaby Planning before relying on any use. The bylaw does carry a dedicated Townhouse District (R2) elsewhere in the city; nothing in this district's plan framework applies it here. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **SSMUH (program frame):** Burnaby's R1 small-scale multi-unit district (3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 near frequent transit; rowhouses among permitted forms) applies to residential lots — this district has none. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Flood and streamside checks:** the district sits in the Still Creek / Burnaby Lake lowland; floodplain and streamside conditions are parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel — an environmental/flood review is part of diligence on any land purchase here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/lake-city-industrial.md)
- **Burnaby property taxes (applies to the industrial parcels that do exist):** a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes after the July due date, and a further 5% after the September due date; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify these penalties. Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Taxes and rebates (program frame, carried in every townhomes file):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% per CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule. These are residential-buyer instruments; none attaches to an industrial parcel purchase, which carries its own GST treatment — take tax advice on any commercial deal. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — this is Burnaby, and that tax is Vancouver-only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Are there any townhomes in Lake City?** No — none, of either tenure. Lake City Industrial is an industrial district under the Lake City Community Plan (2000); its districts are commercial, business centre, institutional and park. Source: Section 1; Section 2.
- **A search showed me a "townhouse" near Lake City Way — what is it?** Almost certainly a mis-filed listing from a neighbouring residential sub-area, or industrial space described by form. No residential complex exists inside the district to match it. Verify the civic address against the district's streets (Lake City Way, Enterprise Street, Commerce Court, Production Crescent, Thunderbird Crescent). Source: Section 1.
- **Can I live in an industrial strata unit here?** The plan framework and the 2050 OCP's employment-lands direction (protect and intensify industrial land) provide no residential use — and the specific permitted uses for any parcel are UNVERIFIED until confirmed in writing with Burnaby Planning. Do not buy industrial space on a live-in assumption. Source: Section 3.
- **Why is there no SnapStats row for Lake City?** SnapStats reports residential board sub-areas; Lake City has no residential market, so no row exists and no sub-area median exists to quote. Anyone quoting a "Lake City median home price" is quoting somewhere else. Source: Section 1.
- **What about the film studios — is that changing the area into housing?** The studio campus (21 sound stages, 18.6 acres, opening summer 2025 per the project account) is an employment use that fits the district's direction — intensification of industrial land, not conversion to housing. Source: Section 2.
- **Is industrial strata like residential strata?** Partly: an industrial strata lot is still a strata lot, with a strata corporation, fees and bylaws, and the Form B / depreciation-report diligence habits transfer. The buyer, the financing and the tax treatment do not — it is commercial property. Source: Section 3.
- **Could the district ever get townhouses under SSMUH or the new zoning?** SSMUH applies to residential lots; this district has none, the 2000 community plan has no residential district, and the 2050 OCP protects employment lands. No rezoning to residential is verified or documented in any source read. Source: Section 3.
- **Where is the nearest actual townhouse stock?** In the neighbouring residential sub-areas around Burnaby Lake — Government Road and Cariboo to the east and south, Sperling-Duthie to the north-west — each documented in its own townhomes file on disk (e.g. `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/government-road-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`, 2 strata complexes; `~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/cariboo-townhomes-2026-11-01.md`). Source: sibling townhomes files on disk.
- **Are there any 55+ or seniors options in Lake City?** None — the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero, because there is no housing at all. Source: sibling 55-plus file on disk.
