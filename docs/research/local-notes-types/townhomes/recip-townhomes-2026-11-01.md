## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **The data route, as the page itself discloses it:** "The data relating to real estate on this website comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of either the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® (GVR), the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board (FVREB) or the Chilliwack and District Real Estate Board (CADREB). Real estate listings held by participating real estate firms are marked with the MLS® logo and detailed information about the listing includes the name of the listing agent. This representation is based in whole or part on data generated by either the GVR, the FVREB or the CADREB which assumes no responsibility for its accuracy. The materials contained on this page may not be reproduced without the express written consent of either the GVR, the FVREB or the CADREB." (research file §1; https://jaredgibbons.ca/recip.html).
- **Reciprocity display rules:** UNVERIFIED in this file — neither FVREB, GVR nor CADREB publishes its MLS®/reciprocity rule documents on a public page that could be read; a platform vendor's marketing page is not an official source. At the national level, CREA owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks, and agents must be members of CREA and of a local board, complying with CREA's rules including the REALTOR® Code and their local board's rules, to access the MLS® system at all (research file §4).
- **Regulator — who you are dealing with:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and can only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction — dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. That frame matters on this page precisely because the homes shown are other firms' listings (https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers; research file §4).
- **Form B diligence (for any strata townhome bought through this feed).** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A recip card is not a Form B and carries none of this (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term tenancies; any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex reached from this feed is flagged VOID in that complex's own file. Short-term rentals can still be restricted. **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. Which tenure a feed card actually is cannot be settled from the card; it is settled on title in the complex's own file.
- **City rules (first page is Surrey ground).** Surrey 2050 OCP **Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026) is current — NEVER 18020 as current; Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs zoning. SSMUH (July 8, 2024): 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; secondary suite cap **40% of the principal habitable area**. Surrey STRs are permitted, owner-only — licence figure CONFLICT: **$358/yr carried, $350/yr + fees disclosed** (City 2024 corporate report proposal); check the City licence page before quoting. Zoning for any specific listing reached from the feed is that listing's own municipality's bylaw — UNCONFIRMED per listing from the card alone (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure and is flagged wherever met; no such row is documented in this file's research. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%.** No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does **NOT** document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the recip page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply to Surrey ground — it is Vancouver only.
- **Presales / REDMA.** Where a listing reached from this feed is a developer-contract presale, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission and s. 18 requires deposits held in trust (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).
