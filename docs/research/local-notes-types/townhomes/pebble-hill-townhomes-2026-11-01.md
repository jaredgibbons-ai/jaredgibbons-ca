## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no townhouse stock here, so this section is (a) the fee-simple position that describes the stock that actually exists, (b) the Delta plan/zoning/flood frame, and (c) the standard BC strata and tax rules a buyer needs for the Tsawwassen strata purchases a Pebble Hill search actually lands on.

- **Fee-simple is the tenure that exists here — plainly.** A detached house or freehold half duplex in Pebble Hill is fee-simple: NO strata corporation, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title, zoning and municipal flood-construction requirements. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024.** A single consolidated plan — NO lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" conflation is an error wherever met); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates; neither number is asserted as settled here, and the zone on any parcel is read off the City's zoning maps. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/pebble-hill.md)
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City Housing Accelerator help for coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites; the live feed shows the pattern in Pebble Hill itself, with several listings describing legal or in-law suites in detached houses (listing copy, feed evidence only). SSMUH product is fee-simple in form unless separately strata-registered — do not market it as a townhouse complex. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood frame.** Tsawwassen's Boundary Bay shore is dike-protected delta land and the Dyke Trail itself runs on the dike; the City of Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. The batch-8 research could not re-verify the City flood/streamside pages in its pass (fetch-service 429 on 2026-11-01) — the figures here are carried from the program's Delta base research and are labelled as such, not as batch-8-verified. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Strata rules (for Tsawwassen strata purchases elsewhere).** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59): discloses monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days of request. Since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata).** Every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — a directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry. Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 only a 55+ age bylaw is valid, with exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (new construction only).** Resale homes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax and buyer programs.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000, and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,200,000 fee-simple Pebble Hill house with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs, the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan. No CMHC defect is asserted for any property here — no file's own research documents one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** No presale project inside Pebble Hill is verified, so REDMA is background only: where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 (carried from the program's Delta base research; the batch-8 Pebble Hill pass could not re-verify the City tax page, which returned a rate limit). https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does not apply here** — it is 3% and Vancouver-only; Delta has no equivalent tax stated in any source checked. (Stated to close the question buyers ask when comparing across the region.)
