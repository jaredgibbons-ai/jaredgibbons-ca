## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Sundial, and South Grove if re-consolidated, are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — for a 1976/1978 complex like Sundial, this report is the roof/envelope/plumbing forecast; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (neither Sundial at 84 directory units nor South Grove at 37 listing units is sub-5-lot on its published count). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: Sundial's directory "rentals not allowed" line, and Tsawwassen Greene's, are VOID for long-term rentals. South Grove's listing "Rentals Allowed" line is consistent with the rule but is listing data — the registered bylaws govern short-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No 55+ bylaw is verified for Sundial or South Grove — UNCONFIRMED for both; the sub-area's verified 55+ buildings are apartment-form (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area (Sundial is strata; South Grove is freehold *strata* — freehold strata is still strata, with fees, bylaws and a Form B).
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; no ALR land inside central Tsawwassen was confirmed on a City source in the research. The Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) cover siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping for the centre. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (research cites No. 7600, 2017 against a competing "8400, 2025" citation). What is not contested: zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps; the central ladder in the research includes RS4 610 m², RS5 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m² and RD4 504 m² subdivision minimums. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. This affects the detached streets around the counted complexes, not the strata lots inside them. Delta also permits 2 to 4 hens for personal use on a lot in any RS or RD zone (coop and run max 10 m², no roosters), with an annual licence under the Animal Control Bylaw — a small but real disclosure point for sellers with coops (research file, §6). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: an $850,000 resale Sundial townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $650,000 ($13,000) = **$15,000**.
