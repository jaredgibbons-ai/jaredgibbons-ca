Published drive times were not confirmed. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/3/D/Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre---Mission
- Transit: Mission City Station, 33200 North Railway Ave, is the eastern terminus of the West Coast Express (weekday rush-hour trains to Downtown Vancouver); BC Transit's Central Fraser Valley system serves Mission, including Route 31 Mission to Abbotsford. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/
- Schools: SD75 Mission Public Schools, with a middle tier — elementary K–6, middle schools Grades 7–9, Mission Senior Secondary Grades 10–12. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Mission's oldest townhouse stock (Luther Place 1988, Cherry Lane 1989) this is the central diligence document. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. All four assembled complexes are 5+ lots on their published counts except Archer Green, whose lot count is UNCONFIRMED — the 5+ test for Archer Green is confirmed on its Form B/strata plan. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Cherry Lane is void in-entry — flag it wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. Luther Place is described in listings as a 55+ complex — that is the published position; the registered bylaw on the Form B governs any specific buyer, and no other assembled complex has a published 55+ position in the sources read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this assembly. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so no buyer imports strata expectations onto a fee-simple purchase elsewhere in the city.
- **City plan — OCP Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026:** the current Official Community Plan replaces and repeals OCP Bylaw 6350-2025 and keeps the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction, with a Regional Context Statement added. **Any source stating a "June 1, 2026" adoption date for this OCP is WRONG — flag it as a Jerry sweep item wherever it appears.** The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan (adoption date April 21, 2026: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01.)
- **City zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted; secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **City property taxes — FLAT 10% penalty:** City of Mission 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a **10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026**. That is the whole penalty: a flat 10% on unpaid taxes after July 2 (Community Charter ss. 233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s. 3; Mission has NO alternative-scheme bylaw under Charter s. 235). Any "5% + 5%" staging for CITY of Mission taxes is the wrong regime — 5% after Jul 2 + 5% after Oct 31 is the separate RURAL regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11) that applies in FVRD electoral areas, never inside the city. The two regimes are never mixed in this file. Taxes also carry a diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Flood and dykes:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system — confirm floodplain status, flood construction requirements and the diking levy for the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf
- **ALR (rural edges):** Agricultural Land Reserve land sits mainly on the city's rural edges, status parcel-specific on ALC mapping. An ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC caps bind (principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence ≤90 m² at ≤40 ha / ≤186 m² above 40 ha); farm-classification thresholds run in the full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Presales (REDMA):** Montgomery Condos is sold as a presale and Briskham Hill was marketed as new product — a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://montgomerycondos.ca/ and https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — the frame that matters for a new Briskham Hill purchase; the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000); the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000); the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year); CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Page-claims and contact check:** this page's research file documents no page-copy claim wording and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance — none is documented, so no claims variant and no Jerry email-sweep item is recorded from this file. The correct email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca; approved claims (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years in the FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1% of Fraser Valley Realtors; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points for 2025 and 2026 YTD; more than $500M in sales) may be used only as page statements in that exact form. The one dated Jerry item this file does carry is the OCP adoption date above: "June 1, 2026" for Bylaw 6405-2026 is wrong wherever it appears; April 21, 2026 is correct. Source: the research file, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Mission townhouse complexes can I actually search by name?** The four assembled here: Cherry Lane and Luther Place on 7th Avenue, Briskham Hill on Briskham Street, and Archer Green in Mission West. Every other named Mission attached building in the sibling files is an apartment condo. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Luther Place a condo or a townhouse?** A townhouse — the MLS dwelling-type field says Townhouse, Strata Plan NWS2729, 36 units, built 1988. Some feeds file its units under "Condos"; this program follows the dwelling-type field and states the conflict. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/luther-place-32286-7-ave
- **Q: Can I rent out a Cherry Lane townhouse?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — the directory's "rentals not allowed" line is void since Nov 24, 2022. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and any valid age bylaw still governs who may live there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is Briskham Hill the same as Briskham Manor?** No — Briskham Hill (7540 Briskham St) is 19 new townhomes by Verado Homes, built 2025/2026; Briskham Manor (7554 Briskham St) is a 1993 apartment strata of 33 units (LMS1049). Same street, different form, different strata, different diligence. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3081797-14-7540-briskham-street-mission-british-columbia-v3v3l9-709900/brochure
- **Q: What do I need to know about Archer Green before offering?** Its townhouse-strata form is carried from the condos run's Mission West file, but its year, unit count and restrictions are UNCONFIRMED in the assembled sources — the Form B package is not optional diligence here, it is the diligence. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/mission-west-mission-guide-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: How late can I pay Mission property taxes?** Due July 2, 2026 — and the penalty is a flat 10% on any current balance outstanding July 3, 2026. There is no 5%-then-5% staging inside the City of Mission; that staged regime is the FVRD rural one. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Q: Does Mission have a middle school tier?** Yes — unlike SD42 next door, SD75 runs elementary K–6, middle schools Grades 7–9 and Mission Senior Secondary Grades 10–12, with address-based catchments through the SD75 School Locator Tool. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/075 and https://www.mpsd.ca
- **Q: Is the West Coast Express an all-day service?** No — Mission City Station is the line's eastern terminus with weekday rush-hour trains to Downtown Vancouver. Market downtown access on the train schedule, not on all-day transit. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Q: Are there townhouses in rural Mission — Stave Falls, Steelhead, Hatzic?** None verified by name in the assembled files, and five of the seven rural sub-areas recorded zero active attached listings in September 2026. Stave Falls is inside the City of Mission, so city taxes and zoning apply there — but rural stock is detached freehold on lots under zones such as RU16 (1.6 ha minimum in City notices). Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file; https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **Q: How is the attached market moving?** Carefully: Mission's Condos & Townhomes COMBINED median days on market hit 61 in September 2026 (from 20 in August) on only 10 sales, with the combined median swinging between $417,500 and $690,000 over 13 months — price a townhouse off its specific complex and recent comparables, not the volatile monthly combined median. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.

- **Mission's tax penalty is the simplest in the region — and the most often misstated:** flat 10% after July 2 inside the city (Community Charter ss. 233–234, Reg. 426/2003 s. 3, no s. 235 bylaw), versus the staged 5% + 5% rural regime in the FVRD electoral areas outside it (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11). A Mission page carrying the rural staging is carrying the wrong municipality's rule. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **The attached market is a one-sub-area market:** the urban "Mission" sub-area held 104 of the city's 122 active Condos & Townhomes listings and all 10 attached sales in September 2026 — Mission West held 8 active attached listings and no sales. Townhouse searching in Mission is, in practice, searching the core plus one west pocket. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.

Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
