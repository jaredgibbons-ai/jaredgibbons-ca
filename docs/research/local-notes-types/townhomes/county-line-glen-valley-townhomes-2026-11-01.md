## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Why there is no townhouse stock here is a rules story first — the ALR frame governs this sub-area's acreage.**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** On land in the Agricultural Land Reserve, a residence cannot be turned into strata lots (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/). The ALR frame: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel; a principal residence may be up to 500 m² total floor area without ALC application; one additional detached residence may be permitted of up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or up to 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence and only where local bylaws allow it (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Whether any specific parcel in the feed is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping.
- **BC Assessment farm classification — the full three-tier thresholds.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm-gate prices: $2,500 where the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha where the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is less than 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears (https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

**Township of Langley frame — Township rules throughout; Abbotsford rules are never imported.**

- **Zoning / OCP.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 governs (https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx). The Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025) — it does not govern this rural sub-area, but the citation discipline matters program-wide: Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 stand at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026; any page or research file presenting June 15, 2026 repeals by Bylaws 6202/6203 as done is WRONG — Township records support only the directed hearing that day.
- **SSMUH — stated so it is not misapplied here.** The Township adopted Bylaw No. 6020 on November 18, 2024, allowing 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — a page or research file claiming a 6-plex tier, or claiming the Willoughby Plan was "replaced" by Bylaws 6201/6202/6203, is WRONG; the standing correction governs and any such adoption is UNVERIFIED. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them (research file §4).
- **Secondary suites (Township).** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102 (as amended July 2025), a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, each principal dwelling unit may have up to one secondary suite, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations. **Live conflict, flagged, not resolved:** the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s. 102 definition (July 2025) says a suite may be located in a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome — the bylaw definition is the later source (research file §4).
- **Coach houses (Township).** In Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 102, a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata. No size cap for a coach house appears in s. 100 as read: a coach-house size cap is UNVERIFIED in this file (research file §4). Abbotsford's coach/garden 15%/100 m² rule does NOT apply in this Township sub-area and is never imported.
- **R-CL discipline (program-wide, stated so it is not misapplied).** Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 403 (Willoughby R-CL) is 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area — a "up to 355 m²" cap is nowhere in the bylaw and is an error wherever met. R-CL does not govern County Line Glen Valley's acreage parcels; the line is carried here only as the standing correction.
- **Short-term rentals (Township).** The Township does not issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations; the permitted-use status of STRs under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 was not readable on a Township page in the research pass — STR status in the Township is UNVERIFIED beyond the licensing point (research file §4).

**The strata block — stated because it would apply the moment any strata townhouse existed here; it currently applies to nothing in this sub-area.**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, within 7 days, fee capped at $35 plus 25¢/page for paper copies (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5 or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years; sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports).
- **CRF.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). No directory "rentals not allowed" line for any in-area complex exists to flag in this file, because no in-area complex exists.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. County Line Glen Valley's acreage purchases are fee-simple LAND purchases: the same no-strata diligence track (title, zoning, ALR, servicing), with no townhouse complex attached.

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** (CRA News No. 122 — March 13 governs). 100% of GST up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, maximum $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). Resale acreage does not attract GST on the same footing as new housing — confirm treatment per parcel with a CPA/lawyer; no parcel-specific GST position is stated in this slug's research.
- **New Housing Rebate.** 36% of GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Program worked-example anchors: $900,000 → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000 (1% × $200,000 + 2% × the balance, no exemption).
- **HBP / FHSA.** HBP $60,000 (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); FHSA $40,000 at $8,000/year (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5% (https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). **Page-defect discipline:** no CMHC premium-range error is documented in this slug's research file, so none is asserted for this slug — the 2.80%–4.00% page error documented elsewhere in the program is explicitly NOT asserted here.

**Q: Why can't an ALR farm just be stratified into townhouses?**
A: Because the law bars it: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). That bar, plus parcel sizes far above the Township's 4,050 m² SSMUH ceiling, is why this sub-area has no townhouse stock to count (§3).

**Q: Does SSMUH let me put 4–6 units on a Glen Valley acreage?**
A: No. Township Bylaw 6020 eligibility ends at 4,050 m² and requires Urban Containment Boundary land on municipal water and sewer — and there is NO 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020 at all. The parcels on this page are outside the SSMUH shape by size alone (§3).
