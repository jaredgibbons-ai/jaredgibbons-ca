## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Westbrooke today, and to The Meadows if and when it is verified and counted — both are strata-character stock on the sources checked).**

- **Form B diligence.** Strata Property Act s. 59: provided within seven days of request, fee capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; states the monthly fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the depreciation report attached (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). This is the document that settles Westbrooke's unit count, bylaws and build record — and the document a future verification of The Meadows should start from.
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; sub-five-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements). Westbrooke is a mid-2000s building and The Meadows is 1988 stock — both sit squarely in the age band where roof, piping and envelope work appears in those reports.
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Minimum 10% of the annual operating budget since November 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals). Applied in-entry: the directory summary for The Meadows ("rentals allowed w/restrictions") carries a rental component that is legally void and must not be repeated as a current rule (§2.5). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw.
- **Age.** Only a 55+ bylaw is lawful since November 24, 2022 (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws). No Northwest complex is published as age-restricted on any source checked, and Maple Ridge has zero verified 55+ strata townhouses city-wide — 55+ marketing language for any Maple Ridge townhome (the Fraser Glen / Dogwood Lane pattern elsewhere in the city) is NOT a verified bylaw.

**Fee-simple contrast.** No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here (§2.2).
