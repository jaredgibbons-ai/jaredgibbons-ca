# Chilliwack Mountain, Chilliwack — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Chilliwack Mountain is a residential hillside neighbourhood in the City of Chilliwack, on the named mountain on the south side of the Fraser River, just west of Chilliwack Creek and west of the downtown core; the City treats it as a neighbourhood in its own reporting but does not publish a street-by-street boundary for the Paragon sub-area name "Chilliwack Mountain." https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/5861.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- The community sits above the Industrial Way / Progress Way industrial area on the valley floor; the City's Schweyey Road forcemain page places the mountain community above that industrial area, with sewage service along Schweyey Road for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- The residential streets are the mountain roads themselves — Chilliwack Mountain Road, Grandview Drive and Hillkeep Place at the top, and Sunrise Drive on the slopes. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: the Chilliwack Mountain Road corridor — Reflections @ Cedar Sky and Eagles Perch at the 43610–43680 stretch, Westpointe at 43777, Osprey Ridge at 43998 — and Sunrise Drive, where Maple Hills stands at 8590 Sunrise Drive. No apartment-condo street exists on the mountain in any source checked. Sources: Section 2 below.
- Nearby areas: Chilliwack Downtown and Chilliwack Proper West to the east, Little Mountain to the southeast, Fairfield Island across the river to the north, and the Lickman Road / Industrial Way corridor below the mountain to the west. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- Market stats: Chilliwack is NOT covered by either SnapStats September 2026 report, so no SnapStats figures exist for Chilliwack Mountain and none are cited in this file. Chilliwack is CADREB ground — a page claiming an FVREB feed for this area is wrong. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/chilliwack-mountain.md)
- Schools: no SD33 public school is located on Chilliwack Mountain itself — the schools physically in Chilliwack in the provincial directory are all on the valley floor or in Sardis — and catchments are address-based through the SD33 School Locator; no mountain catchment was confirmed in this pass. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in/chilliwack and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- Transit: no BC Transit route runs onto the mountain's residential streets; the closest listed service is route 59 Industrial – Downtown Chilliwack via the Lickman Park & Ride, with peak-period trips, and Highway 1 access is via the Lickman Road interchange south of the mountain. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** four of the five counted complexes are confirmed strata; Westpointe's tenure is UNCONFIRMED and must be established on title before purchase. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** an owner, a purchaser, or a person authorized by either may request a Form B Information Certificate, and the strata corporation must provide it within 7 days (maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page), disclosing fees, amounts owed, approved levies and the insurance summary, with the most recent depreciation report attached. For a Maple Hills or Osprey Ridge purchase, the Form B — not the listing's pet or fee lines — is the verified record. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Osprey Ridge (34 homes) and Maple Hills (142 homes) are squarely inside the mandatory cycle. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The building directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Maple Hills is therefore VOID and is flagged void in-entry in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/maple-hills-8590-sunrise-drive
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023. No 55+ bylaw is verified for any mountain complex — Westpointe's "55+ gated community" is a listing description, and Reflections' listing states no age restrictions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City plan:** Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 5500 — no Chilliwack Mountain neighbourhood plan appears in its Schedule B (Schedules B1 to B7; Sardis is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1); the mountain is covered by the OCP's Schedule D Development Permit Areas D-2 Hillside Environment and D-5 Hillside Development. Any live page citing a "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" plan for this area is stale — a Jerry item. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Hillside rules that hit townhouse owners directly:** DPA D-2 covers the whole mountain, and a development permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks — the rule behind every addition, deck or rebuild conversation in a mountain strata. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 5000's hillside zones set HR lots at a minimum 0.2 ha on community water (0.4 ha otherwise), RH at 8 ha, SHR by a slope table from 360 m² to 990 m², and SR at 650 m² on full services. Chilliwack's SSMUH bylaw is Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Services:** the City states the Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way; service for a specific parcel or complex is not confirmed — verify water and sewer connection status for the specific lot. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST/HST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; PTT (first-time-buyer exemption on the first $500,000 at values $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No presale project on the mountain was confirmed in this pass, so no REDMA rescission item is live here; if a phase is ever sold as a presale, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission and s.18 requires deposits in trust. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026; additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rate. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy for this area, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the mountain's townhouses strata or fee-simple?** Strata in four of five counted cases: Maple Hills is freehold strata LMS1735 and Osprey Ridge is freehold strata EPS8601 expressly, and Reflections and Eagles Perch publish strata fees in their listings. Westpointe's tenure is UNCONFIRMED — check title before assuming either way, because strata means a Form B and monthly fees, and fee-simple means neither. https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/11-8590-sunrise-dr-1568486 and https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/25-43998-chilliwack-mountain-rd-125893
- **Q: Is Westpointe really 55+?** A current listing calls it "a 55+ gated community" and a neighbourhood source calls it an adult gated community — but no registered bylaw was read in this pass, so the age rule is UNCONFIRMED as a bylaw. If it is a bylaw, only a 55+ threshold is lawful in BC (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2). Read the bylaws before marketing or buying on that basis. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3121296-16-43777-chilliwack-mountain-roadchilliwack-mountain-chilliwack-british-columbia-v2r5v1-999900/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a Maple Hills townhouse? The directory says rentals are not allowed.** That directory line reflects a rental-restriction bylaw, and rental-restriction bylaws are void since November 24, 2022 — long-term rentals cannot be banned by bylaw, though short-term rentals can still be restricted. Confirm the current registered bylaws on the Form B. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/maple-hills-8590-sunrise-drive and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Reflections: a listing states "pet friendly." Osprey Ridge: listing data says pets allowed with restrictions (cats OK, dogs OK). Maple Hills: the directory says pets allowed with restrictions, and one listing records cats OK and dogs OK with a number limit of two. Eagles Perch: UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws remain enforceable — confirm the registered set for the specific complex. Sources: Section 2 above.
- **Q: How old is Maple Hills, really?** Sources disagree by phase: building summary 2004, directory 2005, individual listings 1994 and 2002. It is a phased complex; the Form B and strata plan for the specific unit settle its phase year. https://www.estateblock.com/chilliwack/11-8590-sunrise-dr-1568486 and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/8590-sunrise-drive/15
- **Q: What does the hillside permit area mean for me as an owner?** DPA D-2 covers the whole mountain: a development permit with a geotechnical hazard and slope assessment is required before subdivision, construction, land clearing or earthworks. Budget the report and permit timing into any build or major renovation plan, and expect the strata to apply the same lens to common-property work. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2881/DP2%5FGuide%5F%2D%5FMar%5F2026%2Epdf
- **Q: Is there a bus or a school on the mountain?** Neither. No BC Transit route runs onto the mountain's residential streets (the closest is route 59 at the Lickman Park & Ride), and no SD33 public school sits on the mountain — catchments are address-based in the SD33 School Locator and no mountain catchment was confirmed in this pass, so run the exact address. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/ and https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Are these homes on city sewer?** The City states the Schweyey Road forcemain provides sewage services for Chilliwack Mountain and portions of Industrial Way and Progress Way — but service for a specific parcel is not confirmed, so verify connection status for the specific complex or lot before writing. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3237
- **Q: Why is there no market data for the mountain in this file?** Because there is no verified source for it: Chilliwack is not covered by either SnapStats September 2026 report, and CADREB figures were not substituted. Any median price or days-on-market figure quoted for the mountain from those reports does not exist. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/chilliwack-mountain.md)
- **Q: What insurance exposure comes with a hillside strata?** The strata's water-damage deductible, shown in the Form B insurance summary, can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused a loss — and hillside complexes add slope, retaining-wall and drainage items to the depreciation report. The deductible amount for every mountain complex is UNCONFIRMED until its documents are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mountain's townhouse stock now has names — the older research did not.** City council records from 2017 tie a townhouse complex at 43660 and 43710 Chilliwack Mountain Road to Development Permit DP000817 (Cedarsky Developments Ltd.) without naming it; current listings name the built product on that stretch of road as Reflections @ Cedar Sky (43680), Eagles Perch (43610) and Osprey Ridge (43998), with Seasons at Cedar Sky (43685, marketed from 2015) still unseparated from the Cedar Sky project area in the sources read — which is why Seasons sits in the QUEUE, not the count. https://www.chilliwack.com/apps/council/meetings/agendapackage/reg/2017.01.24%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Agenda%20Package.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/chilliwack-real-estate/43610-chilliwack-mountain-road/3
- **Lot size on the mountain is slope-driven, not just area-driven:** in the SHR zone the minimum lot area rises from 360 m² in the lowest slope band to 990 m² in the highest — a lot's subdivision or rebuild potential depends on its measured slope. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **There is no neighbourhood plan coming to change this:** no Chilliwack Mountain plan appears in Schedule B (B1 to B7) of the Chilliwack 2050 OCP — the mountain is governed by the city-wide OCP and its hillside Development Permit Areas D-2 and D-5. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **The park at the top is regional, not municipal:** Hillkeep Regional Park (FVRD) is a nature reserve at the top of Chilliwack Mountain on Hillkeep Place, reached by Grandview Drive, with hiking trails and a viewing platform — the documented draw the mountainside complexes market against. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/parks-recreation/parks-trails/hillkeep.html
- **The nearest services are all off the mountain:** Chilliwack Landing Leisure Centre (9145 Corbould St), Chilliwack Library (45860 First Avenue), Chilliwack General Hospital (45600 Menholm Rd) and the Chilliwack Urgent and Primary Care Centre (#104 – 7955 Evans Road) — mountain households are car-dependent by design, with no bus route on the residential streets. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Chilliwack/chilliwack-general-hospital and https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/
- **Drainage and dyking taxes ride on top of the municipal rate** in Chilliwack — a small line on the tax notice that valley-floor systems explain, and one a mountain buyer still pays. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
