## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Parc Elise, Chelsea and Park Langara units the Form B is also what proves the unit sits in an apartment strata, not a townhouse complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — directly relevant to a 5-home project like Willa if its tenure verifies as strata: read the lot count before assuming a report exists. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" (or an equivalent restriction) for a South Cambie strata is VOID in-entry and is flagged as such wherever met; no such line is carried into §2 of this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-children exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. OPAL's "one resident must be 55+" position (siblings' record) is the lawful form; no other complex in §2 is published as age-restricted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse stock is counted in this file: a fee-simple purchase on these side streets is a detached house — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; City rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, heritage status) plus any registered covenants/HOA govern instead.
- **City plan and zoning:** the **Cambie Corridor Plan** (approved May 9, 2011; Phase 3 in 2018) is the corridor's growth framework — but plan designation is not zoning: most corridor densities still require a rezoning application, so price land on policy support, not as-built density. Citywide direction comes from the **Vancouver Official Development Plan** (replaced the Vancouver Plan March 31, 2026). For detached-house lots, **By-law No. 13817** replaced the RS districts with the **R1-1 district**, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots — the mechanism by which future small strata townhouse product (the Willa pattern) appears in this sub-area. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY:** 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. It exists only inside the City of Vancouver — it does not apply in Burnaby or anywhere else in this program (the Burnaby files in this batch carry no EHT). Separately, the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is its own regime. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **City property taxes (2026):** advance taxes due February 3, 2026, main taxes July 3, 2026, 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Flood edge:** the streets running down to Marine Drive sit at the edge of the Fraser River floodplain environment; the Vancouver Building By-law's Flood Plain Standards and Requirements set a minimum flood construction level of 4.6 m (GVRD datum) for new construction in designated flood plains — confirm designation lot by lot with the City. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/guidelines-flood-plain-standards-requirements.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No worked PTT example is given: there is no verified South Cambie price figure to use as an input (no SnapStats for Vancouver), and no figure is invented. No CMHC defect is asserted for this slug — its own research does not document one, stated expressly; the known live-page pattern (a stated 2.80%–4.00% + 0.20% range) is NOT documented for this slug. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
