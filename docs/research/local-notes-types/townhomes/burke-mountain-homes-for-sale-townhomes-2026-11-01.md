## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the 9 counted strata complexes and, once registered, both presales).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Directory rental/pet lines in §2.1 are not bylaws; the Form B and registered bylaws govern (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. The count home records every counted Burke Mountain strata as well over five lots on the sources checked, with Reef's lot count UNCONFIRMED — confirm on its Form B. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The published "rentals allowed" lines (Forester, Reef, Secret Ridge) are consistent with current law; any directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets for these complexes is void for long-term rentals and is flagged as such (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals).
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Burke Mountain townhouse complex is verified 55+ (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The Roxton cluster (§2.1) carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Do not order a Form B for a Roxton home; read the title and covenants instead (count home §2/§3).

**Coquitlam zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **Zoning: City of Coquitlam Zoning Bylaw No. 3000 (1996)** (standing correction). **Plans: Interim OCP Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026** (superseding OCP No. 3479), consolidating 55 land-use designations to 18 and incorporating transit-oriented areas, SSMUH and housing-needs requirements; a comprehensive OCP review is anticipated to begin in 2027 (Coquitlam research file §4: https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1932). The Burke Mountain layer is the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhood plans plus the Northwest Burke Vision (research file §4).
- **SSMUH / suites.** Up to four dwelling units in low-density neighbourhoods city-wide; the City's small-scale guide describes zones R-1 through R-4 (suites, laneway homes, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes), with R-1/R-2 caps of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger (Coquitlam research file §4: https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId). **This slug's own research opened no Coquitlam suite/SSMUH page, so suite rules are UNVERIFIED in this slug's research and are carried here from the city file** — and the feed's "legal suite" marketing (e.g., 3558 Monson Crescent) is listing copy only until the permit is checked (research file §4/§5).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs in every §3): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction (Versant, Rocklin presales), not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out.
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, **plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000** — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
