## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all seven counted Fleetwood Tynehead complexes — all are strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** A buyer of a strata townhouse is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, to be provided within seven days of request; the fee is capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page for paper copies. The Form B states the monthly strata fee, money the owner owes the strata, any special levies approved but not yet due, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, the strata's insurance, its rules, its current budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government, strata Forms information: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04).
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots are UNDER the threshold and exempt (BC Government, depreciation reports: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports). All seven Fleetwood Tynehead complexes are above five lots — Fleetwood Gate, the smallest counted complex at 32 homes, included.
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government, strata finances: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 — a strata can no longer restrict rentals. This bites twice in this file's own records: Veranda's published bylaw summary ("rentals allowed w/restrictions") and Fleetwood Gate's ("rentals not allowed") both carry rental components that are legally void and must not be repeated as current rules (BC Government, strata rental rules: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Short-term rentals are a separate, municipal matter (below).
- **Age.** Since November 24, 2022, the only age-restriction bylaw a BC strata may enforce is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08), with exemptions added May 1, 2023 for caregivers and for spouses/partners and specified family members (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). Fleetwood Gate's directory line says only "Age restrictions" without an age — that is not verification of a 55+ bylaw, and no counted Fleetwood Tynehead townhouse complex is verified 55+ in this file.

**Fee-simple contrast.** None of the seven counted complexes is fee-simple — every verified complex here is strata, so every buyer in this stock gets a Form B, monthly strata fees and strata bylaws. A true fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse would have NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — only municipal rules plus any registered covenants or a homeowners' association (Strata Property Act scope: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_01).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, suites and small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH).**

- Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file (City of Surrey, OCP: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan). The Fleetwood Plan (§1) is the local plan layer for the town-centre end of the sub-area.
- Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; townhouse complexes sit in the City's multiple-residential zones, and land at the Tynehead end can be ALR — the Agricultural Land Commission's rules govern ALR parcels first (City of Surrey, Zoning Bylaw 12000: https://www.surrey.ca/bylaws-services/bylaws; Agricultural Land Commission: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/).
- Secondary suites in Surrey are capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area (City of Surrey, secondary suites: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites). Inside a strata townhouse, the strata's bylaws govern whether a suite is possible at all.
- SSMUH: Surrey implemented the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules on July 8, 2024 — up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (City of Surrey, SSMUH: https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/housing/small-scale-multi-unit-housing). SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata townhouse complex.
- Short-term rentals: Surrey permits short-term rentals only in the operator's own principal residence, with a City business licence at $358/year as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries the licence page's $358 figure and directs the reader to the licence page for the current amount (City of Surrey, short-term rentals: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals). Strata bylaws on short-term accommodation also apply inside every complex here.
- Property tax timing: Surrey property taxes are due July 2; a 5% penalty applies to amounts unpaid after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (City of Surrey, property taxes: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing (BC/Canada-wide).**

- **First-time home buyers' GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on **March 13, 2026** (CRA News Release No. 122 — March 13 governs; any sibling file stating March 12 is superseded on this point). The rebate is 100% of the GST on a new home up to $1,000,000, phasing out to $1,500,000, to a maximum rebate of $50,000, for qualifying purchases from March 20, 2025 to 2031 (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). It applies to NEW housing only — a resale Fleetwood Tynehead townhouse does not attract GST.
- **New Housing Rebate (GST/HST).** Separate from the first-time buyer rebate: a 36% rebate of the GST on new or substantially renovated housing, to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000 of value (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/about-your-tax-return/tax-return/completing-a-tax-return/deductions-credits-expenses/new-housing-rebate.html).
- **Property Transfer Tax (PTT).** General rates: 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, PLUS a further 2% on the residential portion of a property valued above $3,000,000. A summary row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses the 3% + 2% structure and is flagged wherever it appears — the tax is not a flat 5% (BC Government, PTT: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). Worked example: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption pays $14,000 PTT (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $600,000).
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time home buyers: full exemption on the first $500,000 of value where the fair market value is $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000. Newly built homes: full exemption up to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government, PTT exemptions: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions).
- **Home Buyers' Plan / First Home Savings Account.** HBP withdrawal limit is $60,000 from RRSPs (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsp-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html); the FHSA allows $40,000 of contributions at $8,000 per year (Canada Revenue Agency: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html).
- **Mortgage insurance.** CMHC mortgage default insurance premiums for high-ratio purchases run from 0.6% to 4.5% of the mortgage amount depending on the loan-to-value ratio (CMHC: https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/industry-innovation-and-leadership/industry-expertise/resources/mortgage-loan-insurance-and-securitization/mortgage-insurance-premiums). No page defect is asserted in this file: this slug's research file documents no CMHC premium error on a Fleetwood Tynehead page.
- **Presales.** No presale is counted in this file (§2.4), but the corridor's growth makes the rule worth stating: a buyer of a presale unit has a 7-day right of rescission under s. 21 of the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, and developers must hold deposits in trust under s. 18 (BC Laws, REDMA: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

**Q: Does the new first-time buyer GST rebate help me here?**
A: Only on NEW housing — 100% of GST up to $1,000,000 (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1,500,000, under Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate/calculate/gst-hst-new-housing-rebate.html). - **Directory numbers held back on purpose.** Two figures a buyer will meet online for this area — Avalon Woods' unit count/strata plan and Manarola's unit count — were found only on a directory this program does not cite, so both are recorded here as UNCONFIRMED rather than repeated; the strata plan behind a Form B is where those numbers are properly verified (§2.1/§2.2).
- **Attached activity is comparatively strong.** Fleetwood Tynehead's September 2026 attached sales ratio (136 active / 15 sold / 11%) ranked third-highest in Surrey in the SnapStats report — but it is the combined condos-and-townhomes category, and the only median published is the Surrey-wide attached median of $512,944 (research file §6, SnapStats September 2026).
- **Tax deadline.** Surrey taxes are due July 2 with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 — relevant on any summer completion here (City of Surrey: https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes).
