### 2.1 Why the zero is structural, not a gap in the search

- A poultry farm sale can combine ALR land, poultry barns and other farm buildings, a principal residence (ALC frame: ≤500 m²) and possibly one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), plus — separately, and only if the contract states it — production quota (research file §5: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). ## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block — stated for contrast, because it does NOT apply to a poultry farm purchase.**

- **No strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees on a poultry farm.** A farm parcel is fee-simple: diligence is title, ALR status, zoning, floodplain, farm classification, barn/environmental compliance and the quota position — not a Form B package. For completeness, the strata rules that govern the urban townhouses in §2.2 are: Form B under SPA s. 59 (within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page, with fees, levies, CRF balance, insurance, budget and depreciation report) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold) and a 10% CRF minimum since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; and only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). None of those instruments exists for a fee-simple farm parcel.
- **Fee-simple farm frame.** Municipal rules (City of Chilliwack, or FVRD for Area D) plus the ALR regime and registered covenants govern. On ALR land: one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; local bylaws may be more restrictive (research file §4: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). Properties in the ALR are also subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations under the City's OCP frame (Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B1; DPA D-4 Farm Home Plate) (research file §4: https://chilliwack.com/ocp).

**Farm classification, quota and taxes — the rules that actually decide this topic.**

- **BC Assessment farm classification — full three-tier thresholds (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met):** $2,500 of farm income if the operation is 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) to 4 ha (10 acres); **$2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha** if the operation is greater than 4 ha; **$10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha**. Classification is voluntary and property-by-property; applications must be submitted by **October 31** for the next assessment/tax year, and a buyer who plans to continue farming must file a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase (research file §4: https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
