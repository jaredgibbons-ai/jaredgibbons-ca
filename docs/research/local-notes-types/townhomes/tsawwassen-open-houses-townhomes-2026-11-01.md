## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim as set out in the source:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for purchasing information. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **The one-hour offset (this feed only, on this file's own research):** the card-level OPEN HOUSE times and the open-house times inside the same listings' text differ by one hour on the cards read for the research. Which time is authoritative is UNVERIFIED. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before setting out — this warning applies to a townhouse open exactly as to any other listing on the feed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **How the dates and times get onto the page:** UNVERIFIED — how open house dates and times are set and entered for publication on the brokerage/board side, and how ShowingTime and TouchBase schedule, publish or announce open houses in this market, are not documented on any BC Gov, BCFSA or board public source found for the research. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **Identification or sign-in for visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **Form B diligence (strata townhomes, after the open):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line a host repeats at a townhouse open is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only, stated plainly. If the townhouse on the feed is fee-simple, there are no strata documents to ask for at the open; diligence is title, zoning and covenants.
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules; the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw No. 3950, and any "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" citation is an error. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (cited as Bylaw No. 7600, 2017 in City material, against a competing "8400, 2025" citation). **SSMUH:** Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024. (Standing corrections, 2026-11-01.)
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **GST / rebates:** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the frame if an event on this feed is a developer sales event rather than a resale open. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Tsawwassen townhouse open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation while the host is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but once you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Is the host the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side; a BC licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller and must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients. Use the open for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Which time do I show up — the card says 12–2, the listing text says 11–1?** Neither is verified as authoritative: this file's research documents exactly that one-hour split on this feed. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before you drive. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement for open house visitors — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **Q: The townhouse looks strata — what do I ask for after the open?** The Form B package: strata fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report (mandatory every five years for stratas of 5+ lots since July 1, 2024). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: What if the townhouse is fee-simple — is there still a Form B?** No. A fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents. Ask the host which tenure the unit is before asking for strata documents that may not exist.
- **Q: Can I rent out a Tsawwassen townhouse I buy at an open?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever a directory or host says. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which Tsawwassen areas appear on the feed?** On the cards read for the research: Tsawwassen Central, Tsawwassen North, English Bluff and Cliff Drive, across condos, townhomes and detached houses — the feed is Tsawwassen-wide and changes as listings come and go. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/tsawwassen-open-houses.md)
- **Q: How should I group a townhouse open-house run?** On Tsawwassen's actual geography: Tsawwassen North (the Highway 17 side), Tsawwassen Central and Cliff Drive (the 56 Street / 12 Avenue side) and English Bluff (the west bluff) — group by those clusters, use 56 Street and 12 Avenue as the spine, and use dead time at South Delta Recreation Centre (1720 56 Street) or Centennial Beach.
