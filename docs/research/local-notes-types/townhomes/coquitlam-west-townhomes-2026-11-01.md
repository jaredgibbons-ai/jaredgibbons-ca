## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (The Gable, the counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The same document set governs the Burquitlam Capital and Austin strata purchases documented in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Austin's directory line "rentals allowed" is consistent with that position; any "rentals not allowed" line printed for a complex in this file is flagged VOID in-entry in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in §2 carries a published age restriction — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ ownership stock in Coquitlam West, and The Gable is not an age-restricted complex in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Coquitlam West. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996) plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 governs; small-scale zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 set subdivision minimums of 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m width (R-2). The Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — was adopted April 27, 2026 and supersedes OCP No. 3479. Area-specific: the adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors OCP and Zoning amendments create medium-density housing and townhousing opportunities around the Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue — confirm whether a specific lot is inside an amended area before pricing in that potential. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968 (research file)
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= (research file)
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse; Zoning Bylaw s. 519 / s. 523 set flood and riparian setbacks. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits (research file)
- **Taxes and rebates:** Coquitlam property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026 (research file). PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a Komo presale), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA) — Komo:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until the Komo disclosure statement is read. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are the actual townhouses in Coquitlam West?** One verified standalone complex: The Gable by Mosaic, 688 Edgar Avenue — freehold-strata townhouses, built 2008 on the majority listing figure. Everything else townhouse-shaped in the sub-area is a component: 4 townhomes inside The Burquitlam Capital strata, townhome units inside The Austin tower, and 4 townhomes in the Komo presale. Basis: §2 above.
- **Q: Is a Burquitlam Capital townhome a different purchase than a tower unit?** No — same strata corporation (EPS5721, built 2019), same Form B document set, same bylaws. You are buying a ground-oriented unit inside an apartment strata, which is why this program counts the complex once, in the condos file. https://www.estateblock.com/coquitlam/601-691-north-rd-1916128
- **Q: Can I rent out a Gable townhouse long-term?** A strata bylaw cannot bar it — rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and The Gable's published restriction set is UNCONFIRMED until the Form B package is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How old is The Gable, really — 2008 or 2009?** Listings checked show both years by unit (2008 in most, 2009 in one). This file follows 2008 and carries the conflict openly; the strata plan and Form B settle the complex's completion. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3097366-688-edgar-avenue-81-coquitlam-bc-v3k-0a5/brochure and https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3051737-688-edgar-avenue-110-coquitlam-bc-v3k-0a5/brochure
- **Q: What does the new planning do to a house lot here?** The adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments create medium-density and townhousing opportunities around Burquitlam and Lougheed Town Centre stations and along Austin Avenue, on top of citywide SSMUH (up to 3–4 units on house lots). Whether a specific lot is inside an amended area is a City confirmation, not a listing claim. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968 (research file)
- **Q: Detached or strata — which market is moving?** In September 2026, strata: 359 condos & townhomes listings with 35 solds (10%) against 76 detached listings with 2 solds (3%). The combined median is a Coquitlam community figure ($625,000) — there is no townhome-only or sub-area median. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Which schools serve an Edgar Avenue townhouse?** SD43, address-based — the verified sample run in the research file (575 Poirier Street) returned Parkland Elementary, École Montgomery Middle and Centennial Secondary, and Burquitlam-side addresses can differ. Run the exact address in the locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43 (research file)
- **Q: Is Komo a townhouse project?** Mostly not: 246 condominiums plus four 2-bedroom townhomes facing Tyndall Street. A Komo townhouse buyer is buying one of four units in a condo project — read the REDMA disclosure statement for the whole development, not a townhouse-only summary. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/komo-by-strand-development-burquitlam-presale-tri-cities-condos/
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new townhouse (Komo), 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. Resales (The Gable) carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026 (dated fact):** Council adopted the Interim Official Community Plan (Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026) on April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479, as a streamlining foundation for a fuller update anticipated to begin in 2027. Any page still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **Edgar Avenue holds both ends of the townhouse story on one street:** The Gable (688 Edgar Avenue, 2008 strata townhouses) and Allison by Mosaic (708 Edgar Avenue, 2024 apartment building whose garden unit is marketed as "townhome-like") — same builder family, same street, opposite tenure documents. The listing adjective is not the form; the title is. §2 above.
- **The station towers are younger than the townhouse stock:** the Whiting Way / Clarke Road cluster runs 2019–2026 (Lougheed Heights 2019 through Elements at Burquitlam 2026, per the sibling condos file), while the sub-area's one standalone townhouse complex dates to 2008 and its one older low-rise (Brookside Manor) to 1993 — depreciation-report and warranty conversations differ by a generation within a few blocks. Basis: sibling condos file §2.
- **Detached and strata moved in opposite directions in the same month (September 2026):** a 3% sales ratio for houses against 10% for condos & townhomes — a house seller here is pricing into the slower of the city's two markets, whatever the tower cranes suggest. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **The townhouse supply story here is a planning document, not a building:** the Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments were adopted to create townhousing opportunities around the two stations and Austin Avenue — the next named townhouse complexes in this sub-area will come from that amendment map, and a lot's position inside or outside it is the first diligence question. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968 (research file)
- **A "55One" name does not make a seniors building:** the sibling 55-plus file records that 55One at 551 Emerson Street (Burquitlam) is not a seniors building despite its name and directory placement, and that no verified 55+ ownership stock exists in Coquitlam West at all — a downsizer searching this sub-area by name alone will be misled. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §2/§5.
