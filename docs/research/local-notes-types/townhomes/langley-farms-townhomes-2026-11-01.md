## 2. COMPLEXES

**This topic file carries NO complex roster of its own — verified townhouse complexes counted here: 0 (strata 0, fee-simple 0).** No strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source for Langley, and the research file states the pattern directly: farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold and strata farm product is not a Langley pattern. What follows is the tenure map a townhome searcher landing on a farm page needs.

### Why there can be no strata townhouse on ALR land

- An ALR residence cannot be stratified: ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6 states the stratification bar, the Strata Property Act s. 239 treats deposit of a strata plan as a subdivision, and the Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) restricts ALR subdivision/use to what the Act and regulations permit. A "strata townhouse on a Langley farm" is therefore not a product that exists to be counted — any listing presenting attached strata product on ALR land must be checked parcel by parcel, because the parcel may not be in the ALR at all. Sources: ALC Bulletin 09 and the ALCA, as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

### The attached forms that DO exist on farm land — none of them townhouses

- **Secondary suite — inside the principal residence only.** On ALR land a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; the principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. A suite is part of one fee-simple house, not a separate townhouse unit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Additional residence — capped, detached, fee-simple.** One additional residence is permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met: 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Coach house — a fee-simple second unit, never strata.** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 s. 102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot. It is an accessory unit on a fee-simple lot; it is not stratified, has no Form B and is not a townhouse. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **Suite size cap (Township):** the Township's secondary-suite cap carried in this program is 120 m². Sources: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 material, as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this topic file, this section carries the farm/ALR frame — because that frame is what decides whether any attached or second-unit product can exist on a Langley farm parcel at all — followed by the townhouse-side contrast.

### The farm / ALR frame

- **No stratification of ALR residences** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — see Section 2. Consequence for a townhome buyer: there is no Form B, no depreciation report, no CRF and no strata bylaw set on a farm parcel, because there is no strata. Diligence is title, ALC status, zoning and farm-classification records instead. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Residence caps in full:** generally no more than one residence per ALR parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (and ≤1,000 m² of fill) without an ALC application; suite within the principal residence only; one additional residence at ≤90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or ≤186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences, so the Township bylaw must permit the unit as well. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification — the full three-tier thresholds.** A producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm-gate prices: **$2,500 where the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha where the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is less than 0.8 ha**, with farm-classification applications due by Oct 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever it appears. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Township zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township; Township material names Rural Zones RU-1 and RU-2 and Suburban Residential Zones SR-1, SR-2 and SR-3. A general minimum parcel size for the RU zones from the direct bylaw zone text, and a farm home plate rule, are NOT confirmed — UNCONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/public-notices/public-notices-2026/2026-07-20-Public-Notice.pdf
- **SSMUH in the Township — Bylaw 6020, and its limits:** the Township's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw is Bylaw 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024, and **no 6-plex tier exists in it** — a page claiming a Langley 6-plex tier under Bylaw 6020 is WRONG and is a page defect to flag; the research file documents no such claim on the farms page, so no defect is recorded for this page. The Township states lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning — and ALR parcels sit under the ALC caps above regardless. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (Bylaw 6020 detail: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01.)
- **R-CL cluster housing (for contrast, where it appears on Langley pages):** Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 403 (R-CL) carries a 220 m² minimum and a 464.5 m² maximum — the figures a Langley attached-housing page must use, not any smaller cap quoted elsewhere. Source: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Willoughby Community Plan status (Township plan frame):** the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); the update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a public hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — any claim those update bylaws are adopted is UNVERIFIED. The research file documents no such claim on the farms page. Source: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Water:** domestic groundwater use needs no licence; non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. Well records are searched in GWELLS; well reports must be filed within 90 days of construction, alteration or decommissioning, and older wells may not appear. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Septic:** a home not connected to community sewer must use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation; only an Authorized Person can design, install, repair, maintain or inspect it, and must file a record of sewerage system before work and a letter of certification after. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Flood/dykes:** Township-wide floodplain mapping was not confirmed; the documented dyke system is on the Fraser River at the Salmon River mouth, and parcel floodplain status is checked in the Township's GeoSource — UNCONFIRMED at parcel level until checked. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx

### The townhouse-side contrast and the shared buyer frame

- **Fee-simple reality:** a farm parcel is fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. The rules that bind a second unit are municipal (Bylaw 2500, including the s. 102 coach-house definition and the 120 m² suite cap) plus the ALC caps and any registered covenants/HOA — stated plainly so no buyer imports strata expectations onto a farm purchase. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **Buyer frame (applies to a qualifying new home on the parcel, not to ALR land as such):** the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000); PTT and its exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000); the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year); CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. How GST and PTT apply to a specific farm purchase (farm-use and mixed-use apportionment) is transaction-specific — UNCONFIRMED at topic level; confirm with the buyer's lawyer/accountant. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no farm presale product is documented for this topic; where any Langley townhome is bought as a presale in its own area file, REDMA s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period and s. 18 requires deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a strata townhouse on a Langley farm?** No — an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Attached product on a farm parcel is a suite inside the principal house or a capped additional residence, both fee-simple. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How much of Langley is actually farm-reserve land?** The Township states 235 square kilometres is within the ALR; check a specific parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: How big can the house be, and can it have a suite?** Principal residence ≤500 m² without an ALC application (≤1,000 m² of fill), with a secondary suite permitted only inside that principal residence; the Township suite cap is 120 m². https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — ≤90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha, ≤186 m² above 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. A coach house under Bylaw 2500 s. 102 sits only in the second storey of a detached garage. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** The three tiers in full: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha) — gross income at farm-gate prices, applications by Oct 31. Anyone quoting a flat "$2,500+" is quoting an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Do I need a licence for the well?** Domestic groundwater use is exempt; irrigation and other non-domestic use requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, and the well's record is searched in GWELLS (older wells may not appear). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights/water-licences-approvals/new-requirements-for-groundwater-users
- **Q: A listing in the farms feed looks like a townhouse subdivision. Is it farm stock?** No. The feed is a generic Agri-Business filter across Langley sub-areas; a feed card is not a confirmed farm operation, and townhouse product in the feed belongs to its own sub-area's townhomes file, not to this topic. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-farms.html
- **Q: What septic paperwork should a farm seller have?** The Authorized Person's record of sewerage system filing (before construction/alteration/repair) and letter of certification (after) — Fraser Health's rules make those the compliance documents for any onsite system. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Is the Salmon River side flood-protected?** The Township's documented dyke system is on the Fraser River at the Salmon River mouth; parcel-level floodplain status is checked in GeoSource and is UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until checked — do not rely on a listing's wording. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: What soil class is Langley's farm land?** No agricultural capability class for the Township's main farm land was confirmed from a text source — capability is mapped per parcel in the BC Soil Information Finder, from Class 1 (no or very slight limitations) downward. UNCONFIRMED at topic level. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The 235 km² figure reframes every Langley "farm" search:** most parcels a buyer calls a farm are ALR reserve land first and real estate second — the ALC caps (500 m² principal, suite-inside-principal, 90/186 m² additional) bind before the Township's zoning is even reached. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **Farm classification is an income test that follows the buyer, not the seller:** if the buyer's plan cannot meet the BC Assessment threshold for the operation's size tier, the classification — and its tax treatment — is at risk from the first assessment cycle. The seller's farm income history and any water licences or ALC approvals in place are the documents a farm buyer's lender and lawyer ask for first. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The Township's SSMUH wording is narrower than buyers expect:** lots larger than 4,050 m² are stated eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning — one added unit, not a multi-unit conversion, and Bylaw 6020 contains no 6-plex tier at all. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **A coach house in Langley is defined by where it sits, not what it looks like:** Bylaw 2500 s. 102 puts it only within the second storey of a detached garage — a ground-floor garden suite marketed as a "coach house" is a different unit type with different rules. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **The minimum lot size for temporary farm labourer accommodation (Bylaw 2500 s. 201.3) could not be confirmed from a Township-hosted bylaw text in the research pass** — a real gap for working-farm buyers, carried as UNCONFIRMED rather than filled from a secondary source. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **General RU-zone minimum parcel size and any farm home plate rule are likewise UNCONFIRMED** from direct bylaw text — price a farm parcel's subdivision or second-home potential only after the zone text and the ALC position are both in hand.
