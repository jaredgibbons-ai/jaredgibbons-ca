## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to any strata townhome bought inside a plan area — a plan designation never substitutes for strata diligence).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A plan designation is not a strata document (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw.
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). A plan, zone or OCP designation never creates an age restriction — only a strata bylaw does.
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: What is the difference between Abbotsford's OCP and its Zoning Bylaw?**
A: The Zoning Bylaw (2014, No. 2400) regulates what is legally permitted on a parcel today without rezoning; the OCP sets the long-term future land use designation and rezoning policy. The City directs both checks — plus development permit areas — to its property information search or WebMap. Note the citation caution: the live page cites the OCP as "Bylaw No. 2500-2016," the verified lineage carries 2600-2016, the standing correction holds Abbotsforward 2050 (Dec 2025) current, and the current citation is UNVERIFIED in this pass (§§1, 3).

**Q: Which Abbotsford plans are actually adopted neighbourhood plans?**
A: UDistrict, City Centre, Historic Downtown and McKee, as carried in this program — with McKee verified as Bylaw 3400-2023, Schedule E of the OCP. The live page's fifth topic, a Marshall Road Corridor Plan, is UNVERIFIED as an adopted City plan on any City source opened in this pass (§3).

**Q: Is the 2016 OCP still the plan to read?**
A: No. Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, is current; designations should be read under it (§3).

**Q: How do BC's SSMUH rules apply in Abbotsford?**
A: Accessory units count toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas — 3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger lots — with secondary suites (inside the principal unit, registered, under 50% of its size) and garden/coach suites (up to 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m²) as the accessory forms. The amending bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED on City pages and is never asserted here (§3).

**Q: Can ALR parcels be rezoned for urban subdivisions?**
A: Not through the City alone: ALR exclusion requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and inside the ALR the residence frame and the A1/A2 subdivision minimums (8.0 ha / 16.0 ha) govern. The live page's related claim that about 72% of Abbotsford is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source opened in this pass (§3).

**Q: How did the plan areas' part of Abbotsford sell in September 2026?**
A: No plan-area figures exist in the permitted source. At board sub-area level, Central Abbotsford (containing three plan areas) recorded 70 detached listings with 12 sold (17%) and 256 Condos & Townhomes listings with 35 sold (14%), and Abbotsford East (containing McKee) recorded 165 detached with 15 sold (9%) and 69 attached with 4 sold (6%); whole-city medians were $949,000 detached and $497,500 attached — sub-area and city context only, never plan-area figures (§1).

**Q: What does the McKee plan mean for a mountainside townhouse or house purchase?**
A: The McKee Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 3400-2023, OCP Schedule E) sits with a Steep Slope Development Permit Area, and Clayburn Village carries Heritage Conservation Area protection — confirm the parcel's permit-area position and any registered geotechnical covenant on its own title before pricing an addition or subdivision (§3).

**Q: Can I rely on the live page's density figures for City Centre or UDistrict?**
A: Not as verified fact: the "6-to-20+ storey" City Centre figure and the mid-rise/high-rise node figures are the page's own numbers, UNVERIFIED as stated because the plan documents themselves were not opened in this pass. Check the parcel's actual designation in WebMap and read the adopted plan document from the City (§3).

**Q: Does a plan designation make a building 55+ or townhouse stock for this file?**
A: No to both. Only a strata corporation's 55+ bylaw creates an age restriction, and this plans file counts no buildings at all — Abbotsford's townhouse and 55+ rosters live in their sub-area and program files (§§2, 3).
