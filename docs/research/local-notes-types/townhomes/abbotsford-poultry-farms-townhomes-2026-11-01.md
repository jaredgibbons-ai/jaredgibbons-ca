## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated for contrast — no strata purchase exists on this topic).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A poultry farm is not a strata purchase — there is NO Form B; the diligence substitutes are title, ALC records, BC Assessment farm-class records and the applicable board's quota records (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023. Neither applies to a farm title (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — a strata rule with no application to a farm parcel, stated because this series carries the standard block in every file.
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No farm parcel carries a 55+ rule in any source read (§2.4).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. A farm parcel is fee-simple land, but its dwelling is a farm residence under the ALC frame below — not a townhouse.

**ALR, farm-class, municipal rules and taxes — per this slug's research.**

- **ALR stratification bar (lead rule):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) (research file §4, via the farms research discipline; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf).
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence without an ALC application at a maximum of 500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (properties 40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive, never more permissive (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification (full three-tier form):** $2,500 for an operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. A flat "$2,500+" line would be an ERROR — none is stated here (research file §4; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).
- **OCP — standing correction:** the current plan is **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025)**. "2600-2016" and "2500-2016" citations are stale/conflicted, are flagged, and are never cited as current in this file.
- **SSMUH bylaw number UNCONFIRMED** on City pages — "3560-2024" is NEVER asserted in this file; where a research file asserts it, it is stated as a conflict and the standing correction governs. Substance for house lots (not farm parcels): secondary suite under 50% of the principal unit; coach/garden suite 15% of lot to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule ONLY).
- **Quota and barn/environmental standards: UNVERIFIED in every particular** — quota inclusion, transfer/approval and premises-certification rules of the four named boards/commission, barn construction, Floodplain DPA / Flood Construction Level position, the BC Code of Practice for Agricultural Environmental Management, ventilation, manure, mortality, groundwater and biosecurity requirements, and avian-influenza effects — no marketing-board, City or CFIA source was opened in the research pass; the live page's account is site copy only (research file §4).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Can any part of a poultry farm be bought as a townhouse or strata unit?**
A: No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). The residence, additional residence and barns all stay on the single farm title, so this file counts 0 townhouses (§§2, 3).

**Q: Are market statistics published for Abbotsford poultry farms?**
A: No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats has no farm category, and no official source in the research pass publishes Abbotsford poultry-farm counts, quota prices or per-acre poultry values — each is UNVERIFIED, and a price opinion stands on the specific property, part by part (research file §7).

**Q: Is quota included when I buy an Abbotsford poultry farm?**
A: UNVERIFIED as a general rule — no marketing-board source was opened in the research pass. The live page states quota does not run with the land title and must be expressly specified in the contract, and the one operating farm in its feed states quota is not included; treat the page's account as site copy, read the specific contract, and verify with the applicable board before subjects are removed (research file §7; https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html).

**Q: What residence can an Abbotsford poultry parcel in the ALR have?**
A: One principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), a secondary suite only within the principal residence, and at most one additional residence of up to 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha), with local rules possibly more restrictive (research file §7; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: What income must a poultry operation show to keep farm classification?**
A: $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for a larger operation, and $10,000 for under 0.8 ha; applications are due by October 31, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31 after purchase (research file §7; https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification).

**Q: Is Abbotsford really "Canada's Poultry Capital" with BC's highest concentration of poultry farms?**
A: UNVERIFIED in this file — those are the live page's own claims, recorded as site copy; no City agricultural-plan, ALC or board source in the research pass confirms them, and this file does not repeat them as fact (§1).

**Q: What floodplain and environmental rules apply to a Sumas or Matsqui Prairie poultry parcel?**
A: UNVERIFIED in this file — the live page places those prairies inside a Floodplain Development Permit Area and under the BC Code of Practice for Agricultural Environmental Management, but both placements are site copy here. Confirm Flood Construction Level and permit requirements for the specific parcel with the City before planning a barn or house (§3).

**Q: When are property taxes due on an Abbotsford poultry farm?**
A: For a City of Abbotsford parcel: due July 2, 2026 by midnight, with a first 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026 (research file §7; https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice).

**Q: How is a poultry farm taxed on transfer?**
