## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the count home's 4 counted strata complexes).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. For the two 1970s Wolfe Road complexes, the Form B is also where the strata plan behind each address is confirmed (see the Parkside Gardens overlap flag in §2.1) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote. Parkside Gardens (42 units) and the 45185 Wolfe Road complex (unit numbers to 120) are plainly over the threshold on their published scales; Townsend Terrace's and Centre Field's lot counts are UNCONFIRMED — the 5+ test for those two is confirmed on the Form B/strata plan. A sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (count home §3; https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Parkside Gardens data-sheet line "Rentals Allowed w/Rest." is flagged on exactly this rule: any long-term rental-restriction component is void; only a short-term-rental restriction can survive. Short-term rental OPERATIONS are separately regulated by the City under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 (adopted November 24, 2021, which repealed the former Bed and Breakfast Bylaw 2003, No. 2968) — confirm the property can be licensed under it before valuing any STR income (research file §4; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf; BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion). No counted complex here is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).

The schools the guide names (Ministry-confirmed) are Bernard Elementary (45465 Bernard Ave), Chilliwack Central Elementary Community (9435 Young Rd), McCammon Elementary (9601 Hamilton St), Leary Integrated Arts & Technology (9320 Walden St) and Kw'íyeqel Secondary (8855 Elm Dr); SD33's middle tier is Grades 6–8, with elementary K–5 and secondary 9–12. Note the guide page itself prints four of these names in wrong forms — a Jerry item (§1; https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school).

**Q: Can a house here have a suite AND a coach house?**
A: No. Only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite (capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the building's habitable floor space), a coach house, or a garden suite (capped at 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06. Separately, the SSMUH amendment (Bylaw No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024) allows 3 to 4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary (§3).

**Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?**
A: Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026 (§3; https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522).
