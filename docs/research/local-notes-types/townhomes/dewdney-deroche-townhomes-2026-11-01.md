## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence — stated with its limit:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has **no application** to the fee-simple acreages and pad-rent manufactured homes that make up this corridor's stock — there is no verified strata building here for it to attach to. If a listing in this corridor ever presents as strata, the Form B is the first document to demand, precisely because no strata stock is verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote (a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER the threshold); since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Neither rule creates or affects stock in this area, where no strata building is verified. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata law, for contrast):** strata rental-restriction bylaws have been VOID since Nov 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, and only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful in a strata (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). **Neither rule governs a manufactured-home park:** a park's age rule and rental position are park-tenancy matters under the park's own documents and the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not strata bylaws — do not read Oasis's "adult side" through strata law, and do not import a directory "rentals not allowed" line into a park purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple position — the corridor's actual default.** A fee-simple purchase here carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; FVRD zoning, registered covenants and provincial rural rules govern instead. The corridor's houses and acreages are that product. A pad-tenancy purchase is the opposite profile: the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act governs the tenancy — pad rent may be raised only once in 12 months on at least three full months' written notice, capped at the provincial limit (2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, per the condos sibling's program record) — and park rules, resale/assignment approval and any age policy are park documents, to be read before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/dewdney-deroche-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Planning and zoning (local):** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern this Electoral Area G ground. **The specific OCP/zoning bylaw numbers and zone names applying to Dewdney and Deroche lots are UNVERIFIED** — the research pass could not open the FVRD bylaw documents (fetch service returned 429; that stretch was stopped rather than retried), and this file does not borrow them: **FVRD bylaw numbers are never borrowed from Chilliwack's (Bylaw 5000 / 5500) or Mission's numbers.** Confirm a parcel's designation and zone with the FVRD for the PID. **Mission's OCP 6405-2026 does NOT apply here** — that is City of Mission ground, west of this corridor. - **Rural property taxes — the regime that actually applies:** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the property tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays property taxes to the Province, not to the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the **Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s. 11**, rural property taxes are due **July 2**, a **5% penalty** is added to any portion that remains unpaid on July 2, and a **further 5% penalty** is added to any portion that remains unpaid on **October 31**. **Mission's flat 10% penalty is expressly DISAPPLIED here** — it is a City of Mission rule for city ground, and Dewdney-Deroche is not city ground; Chilliwack's August second-stage date does not apply here either. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **ALR housing rules:** parcel-level ALR status is per PID: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission mapping. Where land is in the ALR: generally one principal residence capped at 500 m² total floor area, plus at most one additional residence up to 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha); and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $500,000 fee-simple rural house with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. A manufactured home on a rented pad is a different purchase from the land it sits on — confirm with the conveyancer what, if anything, is transfer-taxed on the specific structure before quoting a figure; no pad-purchase PTT figure is stated in this file. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug: this file's own research documents none, so the known 2.80%–4.00% page error is NOT asserted here. Manufactured homes on rented pads commonly face different lender treatment than fee-simple homes — confirm financing for the specific tenure before writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
