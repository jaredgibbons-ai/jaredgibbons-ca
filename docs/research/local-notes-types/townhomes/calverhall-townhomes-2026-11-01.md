## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because there is no townhouse stock in Calverhall, this section does two jobs: it records the BC strata rules that govern any townhouse purchase a Calverhall buyer makes in a neighbouring sub-area, and the City rules that govern the attached forms (suites, duplexes, multiplexes, coach houses) that can actually be built here.

- **Form B diligence (any strata townhouse purchase, in a neighbouring sub-area):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no Calverhall complex to order one for — this applies the moment a search moves into Central Lonsdale, Lower Lonsdale or Moodyville. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt — a live point for future small GO Zone stratas here, where a 3- or 4-lot multiplex strata would sit under the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No Calverhall entry exists to flag this against — the rule matters for any neighbouring-sub-area townhouse a Calverhall buyer purchases instead, where older directory "rentals not allowed" lines are void for long-term rentals and must be flagged in-entry in that file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Calverhall building is published as age-restricted, because there are no Calverhall strata buildings; the 55-plus sibling verifies no 55+ townhouse here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the stock that actually exists here):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (City Zoning Bylaw No. 6700) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. A house with a legal suite is still one fee-simple title.
- **City plans and zoning — the CITY pair only:** City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400 (amended December 8, 2025 by Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114 for provincial housing requirements) and Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700. The District of North Vancouver's OCP 7900 / Zoning 3210 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://www.cnv.org/Community-Environment/Housing/Provincial-Housing-Legislation-Updates (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **The GO Zone / SSMUH as the City states them:** the City adopted the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone on December 8, 2025 (Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9137): a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units, a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m for a corner lot) and 15.0 m in other designations. City-wide, the change raised permitted units to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and up to 6 per lot on approximately 600 properties. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Suites and coach houses:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and a Building Permit. A coach-house approval here is a CITY approval — listing text that says "subject to District review" for a Calverhall address misstates the approving municipality. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **Streamside permits:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and the City's Integrated Stormwater Management Plan names a creek channel from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods — check creek-side lots before pricing a multiplex or coach-house build. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)
- **City taxes:** City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000. Using District rates for a Calverhall estimate misprices the file. City single-family utility fee totals for 2026: UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/calverhall.md)

Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply here — it is a City of Vancouver tax only, at 3%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: If a future multiplex here has only 3 or 4 strata lots, what diligence changes?** The depreciation-report rule changes: stratas with four or fewer lots sit under the five-lot threshold and are exempt from the five-year depreciation-report requirement that binds larger stratas since July 1, 2024.
