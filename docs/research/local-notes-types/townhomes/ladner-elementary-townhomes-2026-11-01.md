## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

This sub-area holds both tenures side by side (in the guide file's count), so the strata block and the fee-simple block both apply — which one governs a purchase depends on the complex in §2.

- **Form B first (strata complexes only):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Manor House, Faireharbour and Apex Homes purchases run on this document; a 48 Landing purchase has no Form B at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple, plainly:** a 48 Landing row house has NO strata corporation, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (zoning, the village permit areas below) and any covenants or easements on title only. Insurance, exterior maintenance and drainage are entirely the owner's.
- **Depreciation reports (strata):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote, with a 30-year projection and three CRF funding models; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (a small strata such as Apex Homes, at at-least-six homes on the listing record, is read off its strata plan before assuming either side of the line). For 1976-built Faireharbour, that report is the central diligence document. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line met for a complex in this sub-area is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. A fee-simple row house has no strata rental rule at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No townhouse complex in this sub-area is verified as 55+; the verified 55+ building (Delta Green) is apartment form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan and village permit areas:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (the former Ladner Area Plan was a schedule of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"; whether any ALR land sits inside this sub-area: UNVERIFIED. On top of zoning, the Ladner Village (LV1) and Ladner Village Core (LVC) Development Permit Areas (Appendix B) apply in the core — the LVC guidelines address the Village's historic buildings and shape exterior work, for strata renovations and fee-simple owners alike. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City zone sheets cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"). The urban Ladner ladder: RS5 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 duplex 836 m², RD4 duplex 504 m², with RS1–RS3 and RD2–RD3 at 4,000 m² in lower-density areas; no RD5 zone; zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites:** Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. Inside a strata complex the bylaws sit on top; on a fee-simple lot the municipal permission is the whole test (plus title covenants). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Streamside and flood:** all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream — including Chilukthan Slough and drainage ditches — fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area; Ladner is low land behind Delta's flood system (over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure). https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,000,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $800,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (48 Landing, any developer-sold townhouse), not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted — this file's own research documents none. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the frame for Bridge & Elliott and any developer-sold townhouse here. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the actual townhouse choice in the village core?** Four verified complexes in two tenures, counted in the guide file: strata townhouses at Manor House (2025), Faireharbour (1976) and Apex Homes (2025), and fee-simple non-strata row houses at 48 Landing (2026). Everything else named for this sub-area is either an apartment building, a presale, or QUEUED/UNCONFIRMED. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is the practical difference between 48 Landing and Manor House?** Tenure. Manor House is strata: monthly fees, bylaws, a Form B, a CRF and a depreciation report. 48 Landing is fee-simple: no fees and no bylaws, but also no strata carrying the roof, exterior or shared systems — and as new construction it carries GST, partly recoverable for eligible first-time buyers under Bill C-4. (§2–§3 above)
- **Q: Is Riverwest a townhouse complex on W River Road?** No — fresh listings record it Apartment/Condo, freehold strata, built 1990/1991 (year UNCONFIRMED between records). It is the Port Guichon guide file's apartment building, whatever sub-area label a given listing carries. (§2 above)
- **Q: Faireharbour was built in 1976 — what should I check first?** The depreciation report and the minutes. A strata of five or more lots must refresh its depreciation report every five years since July 1, 2024; its envelope, roof and drainage items, plus the CRF balance and any approved-but-unpaid levy on the Form B, are the purchase. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata townhouse here?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A fee-simple row house has no strata rental rule at all — municipal and title rules are the only test. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets or ages restricted at the townhouse complexes?** UNCONFIRMED for Manor House, Faireharbour, Apex Homes and 48 Landing — no public source checked states a current pet or age bylaw for any of them. Read the registered bylaws in the Form B package; do not rely on listing adjectives. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse here?** No verified one. The sub-area's verified 55+ building is Delta Green, and it is apartment form; Ladner's other verified 55+ stock (South Pointe, Laburnum Park Place) is bare-land strata. Townhouse-form 55+ stock in Ladner: none verified.
- **Q: I bought a presale in the village — how long do I have to change my mind?** Seven days under REDMA s. 21, from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging the disclosure statement, by written notice to the developer. After that, the contract binds — including its assignment terms. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is the village core a heritage area that limits renovations?** The Ladner Village Core Development Permit Area in the OCP addresses the Village's historic buildings and can shape exterior renovations and redevelopment in the core — relevant to strata exterior work and to a fee-simple owner's renovation alike, not to interior unit renovations. (§3 above)
- **Q: Will my kids automatically attend Ladner Elementary if I buy in this sub-area?** Not automatically — SD37 catchments are address-based. The locator result for the school's own address, 5016 44 Avenue, is Ladner Elementary with Delta Secondary, and the district publishes the Ladner Elementary catchment boundary in its Ladner Area catchment maps (updated October 2017) — a document-based source, not a promise for any other address. Check the exact address. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/student-registration/catchment-area-maps/
