## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **CORRECTION carried in this file:** the townhouse districts in New Westminster's Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 are RT-2, RT-2A, RT-2B and RT-2C (ss.410–413), with RT-3, RT-3A and RT-3B in Queensborough (ss.417–419). Districts named "RT-A" or "RT-B" do NOT exist in this bylaw — any page copy using them is wrong. The RT districts regulate density, height and coverage rather than setting a minimum site area (RT-2, s.410: base density of 18 units per net acre or a floor space ratio of 0.6, height capped at 25 feet, site coverage capped at 40 percent), and the 836.10 m² (9,000 sq. ft.) figure in the bylaw is a density threshold in the RM multiple-dwelling districts (e.g., s.462.14) — an apartment-district figure, NOT a townhouse minimum. The research file on disk states this correct position and this file follows it. Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 was adopted September 17, 2001 and is consolidated as of June 8, 2026. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php
- **Plan:** Our City 2041, the City's Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area, including its Development Permit Areas (Schedule B) for the mainland. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Form B diligence (both strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" or restriction line for a Fraserview NW complex (including the Palace Quay line noted in Section 2) is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies ZERO 55+ ownership stock in Fraserview NW; one listing describes the Queen's Park Place apartment building as a "quiet 55+ building," but no registered-bylaw source confirms a 55+ bylaw there — treat any 55+ claim in this sub-area as UNCONFIRMED until the bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Fraserview NW. If a fee-simple row home is marketed here, the rule is plain: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 6680) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **Flood:** the City states that both Queensborough and sections of the mainland are included within the provincial floodplain, and that five of the city's twelve square kilometres fall within the flood boundaries on the Ministry of Environment Floodplain Map — lot-level flood status for a Fraserview property: UNVERIFIED, check with the City before subjects come off. (The Floodplain Zone 3 diligence item belongs to the Quay and Downtown NW files, not this one.) http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Taxes and rebates:** City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. New Westminster is NOT subject to Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax — that tax is Vancouver-only and does not appear as a cost here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Fraserview NW the same as Vancouver's Fraserview?** No. This is New Westminster's Fraserview sub-area, on the south slope above the North Arm of the Fraser River (School District 40); Vancouver's Fraserview is a separate Vancouver East sub-area covered in its own file, with different prices, schools and rules. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/2_4 Parks and Open Space.pdf
- **Q: How many real townhouse complexes are there here?** Two verified in this pass: the 245 Francis Way townhouses at Victoria Hill (built 2007) and Jamieson Place at 72 Jamieson Court (built 1991, strata plan LMS128) — both strata. Most of the sub-area's 53 attached listings in September 2026 were apartment condos, not townhomes (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file).
- **Q: Fraser Mews is listed as a townhouse — is it one?** No. Some listings carry Sub-Type Townhouse, others Apartment; the built form is a 4-storey elevator building with underground parking, so it is an apartment strata with townhouse-style ground units. Diligence is apartment diligence (elevator, parkade, building envelope), whatever the sub-type field says. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3108113-365-ginger-drive-new-westminster-bc-v3l-3c2
- **Q: Can I rent out my Fraserview NW townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still prints a restriction line. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** Fraserview NW recorded 53 Condos & Townhomes listings and 10 solds (19% sales ratio); the New Westminster community combined median was $560,000 at $587/sqft, 95% sale-to-list, 27 days on market — combined condos and townhomes, never a townhome-only figure (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file).
- **Q: Which schools serve a Jamieson Court or Victoria Hill address?** Address-based in SD40. The verified locator run for the sample address 1010 Hamilton Street returned Lord Kelvin Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary — run the exact address; waterfront-edge addresses can differ. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
- **Q: Is flood risk a real diligence item on the waterfront edge?** Yes. The City states sections of the mainland sit inside the provincial floodplain and five of the city's twelve square kilometres fall within the Ministry flood boundaries — confirm the specific building's flood construction level and insurance position with the City before subjects come off. Lot-level status: UNVERIFIED in this file. http://www.newwestcity.ca/services/public-safety/flood-management
- **Q: How do residents reach SkyTrain?** There is no station in the sub-area; route 103 (Quayside / New Westminster Station) serves the waterfront, and the nearest Expo Line stations are New Westminster Station and 22nd Street Station. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2024-schedules/september/burnaby-new-west-p155.pdf
- **Q: Are there fee-simple (non-strata) townhouses in Fraserview NW?** None verified in this pass — both named townhouse complexes are strata. The non-strata stock here is detached houses under the RS districts. If a listing claims non-strata townhouse tenure, verify it on title before relying on it. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: What is the "Boiler House" that Victoria Hill listings mention?** The shared amenity centre for the Victoria Hill community — listings for 245 Francis Way describe a huge gym, theatre, half basketball court and lounge, with access shared across the community's buildings; amenity access and cost-sharing are strata documents questions, answered on the Form B. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3020564-245-francis-way-44-new-westminster-bc-v3l-0a7/brochure

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The townhouse zoning names most likely to be quoted at a buyer are wrong.** New Westminster has no RT-A or RT-B districts; the bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C on the mainland and RT-3/3A/3B in Queensborough, and the 836.10 m² figure sometimes quoted as a townhouse minimum is an apartment-district (RM) density threshold. A listing or page that cites RT-A/RT-B is working from an invented district. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **North Arm is a New Westminster board sub-area, not a Richmond one** — the esplanade below this slope is New Westminster ground, and comp sets pulled from Richmond's North Arm naming will misprice a Fraserview NW home. Source: the research file for fraserview-nw, read in full 2026-11-01.
- **The city's recreation anchor is up the hill, not on the waterfront:** the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue in Uptown is the city's main aquatic centre, and the Main Branch of New Westminster Public Library is at 716 6th Avenue — waterfront living here still means a hill climb for civic services. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and https://www.nwpl.ca/
- **New Westminster runs its own electrical utility** — a Fraserview NW home's electricity bill comes from the City, not BC Hydro; a 2026 utility dollar figure confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **The hospital is across the city:** Royal Columbian Hospital, 330 East Columbia Street in Sapperton (Fraser Health), is the city's hospital; a New Westminster Urgent and Primary Care Centre location: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/New-Westminster/royal-columbian-hospital
- **Detached evidence here is two listings deep:** September 2026 showed 2 detached listings and no detached sales in the sub-area, so any Fraserview NW house pricing case has to be built from the wider New Westminster community (detached median $1,365,000), not from sub-area solds that do not exist (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file).
