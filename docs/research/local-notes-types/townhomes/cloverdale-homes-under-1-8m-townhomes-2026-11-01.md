## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all strata complexes cross-referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For any strata unit bought while shopping this detached-led band, the Form B — not the band page — is where fee, levy and deductible exposure is settled. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Every cross-referenced complex is over the five-lot threshold on its published scale (Charlton 34, Laredo 22, Manor Ridge 80, Harvest 222 on their published counts; the rest on their complex scale — confirm per complex on the Form B); a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER the threshold — none cross-referenced here is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2 on exactly this rule: St. Andrews' "rentals allowed w/restrictions" directory line (the restriction component is void) and Manor Ridge's directory "rentals not allowed" line (void in full). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). One complex documented in §2 is verified 55+ — Manor Ridge (townhouse form) — and it is not in the counted townhouse roster, for the reasons stated in its entry. No roster complex is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. That contrast is the normal case for this band's lead product — detached houses are fee-simple — but no verified fee-simple TOWNHOUSE complex exists in the Cloverdale board community (§2), and a detached house's fee-simple title does not make it a townhouse. ("Freehold Strata" is strata title, not fee-simple non-strata title.)
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Zoning, SSMUH and the zone-name correction:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the July 8, 2024 changes renamed **RF-12 / RF-12C / RF-13 to R4**, RF-9 / RF-9C / RF-10 to R5, RF / RF-SS to R3, and RF-SD to R6.
