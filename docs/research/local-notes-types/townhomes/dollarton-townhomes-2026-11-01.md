## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the Cates Landing strata — apartments and its townhouse component alike):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Cates Landing, read the Form B together with the building's shoreline and insurance position below — the park shoreline beside it is documented as eroding. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold and are exempt. Cates Landing (95 residences) is well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory or listing line checked for Cates Landing states a rental ban (a current listing calls it "pet & rental friendly") — if an older Cates Landing document surfaces a "rentals not allowed" line, it is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Cates Landing source checked publishes an age restriction — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B, and the 55-plus sibling records the complex as general-market, NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the shore's house stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (District Zoning Bylaw No. 3210) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here, so this rule is stated for the house form, not misapplied to a counted complex.
- **District plans and zoning — the DISTRICT pair only:** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 7900 (including the Maplewood Village Centre plans for the west end) and Zoning Bylaw No. 3210, Section 525 (Bylaw 8799): R1 minimum lot 4,000 m²; R2 minimum lot 660 m², with modifiers R2(S1) 335 m², R2(S2) 1,100 m² and R2(S3) 4,000 m²; an R2 lot allows one principal dwelling, one secondary suite and one coach house, and the R1/R2 setback table sets a minimum setback to the Ocean Natural Boundary Line of 7.6 m for a principal building — directly relevant on Dollarton's waterfront lots, and context for any Cates Landing alteration near the water. The City's OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **SSMUH / suites as the District states them:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. A suite or coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a house lot — never a townhouse complex. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
- **Shoreline and foreshore — the Dollarton-specific layer:** the port authority states the Whey-ah-Wichen (Cates Park) shoreline is eroding and likely to worsen with sea level rise and climate change, threatening archaeological resources, shoreline habitat, park infrastructure and cultural sites; a shoreline adaptation and restoration project at the park, proposed by the District and the Tsleil-Waututh Nation, is under review by the port authority. Burrard Inlet waters and the intertidal area are federal lands and waters managed by the Vancouver Fraser Port Authority — dock, seawall or foreshore work is not a District-only approval. OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B also sets Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside, Wildfire, Creek and Slope Hazard Development Permit Areas, and the District publishes GeoWeb flood construction levels modelled to a 2100 (2080s) horizon plus 0.6 m. https://www.portvancouver.com/permit/shoreline-adaptation-and-restoration-whey-ah-wichen-cates-park and https://www.portvancouver.com/about (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/dollarton.md)
