## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

For this topic file, this section carries the farm/ALR rules frame — because that frame is what decides whether any residence on a poultry operation could ever be a townhouse (it cannot, on ALR land) — followed by the standard buyer frame.

### The farm / ALR frame (research-file sourced)

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified:** an ALR residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — a strata plan causes a subdivision, and subdivision of ALR land requires Commission permission. That is the structural reason a poultry farm's farmhouse can never be sold as townhouse strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **ALC residence caps:** principal residence at most 500 m²; a suite is permitted within the principal residence only; one additional residence at most 90 m² on properties of 40 ha or less and at most 186 m² on properties over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm-class thresholds — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of land over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever it appears. (Program standing rule, carried from the ALC/BC Assessment farm-class framework, 2026-11-01)
- **Township of Langley frame (where Langley poultry ground is concerned):** SSMUH is Zoning Bylaw amendment Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024, under Zoning Bylaw 2500 — there is NO 6-plex tier in the Township (a page claiming one is WRONG; no such claim is documented in this topic's research file, so no defect is recorded here); the suite cap is 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, never strata; and R-CL s.403 sets 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum. None of that creates townhouse strata on ALR land — the ALC stratification bar above governs farm parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Barns are farm-use structures, not housing stock:** under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, ss.4–5, a structure necessary for a farm use — expressly including a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation — is a designated farm use a local government enactment may not prohibit, though it may be regulated, and the structure must be required for the farm use and in line with its level. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/app/uploads/sites/763/2025/10/Necessary-Farm-Use-Structures-in-the-ALR-Guidelines.pdf
- **Quota is the productive asset, and it does not transfer automatically:** BC Chicken Marketing Board quota is the amount of chicken, in kilograms of live weight, allotted to a grower, in mainstream and specialty classes under the board's General Orders; quota transfers require board approval (the board checks good standing and cycle allocation), transfers are facilitated privately, and the board's quota exchange was stated to be in development — whether it is operating is NOT CONFIRMED. A listing may state quota is not included, as the Abbotsford West listing behind the live pages does. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf and https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Policy-Directions-Update-Sep-2026.pdf
- **Sector boards:** the BC Chicken Marketing Board (chicken), the BC Broiler Hatching Egg Commission (hatching eggs — mainstream quota is hen-based, specialty is hatched-chick-based), and the BC Turkey Marketing Board (turkey quota in pounds of turkey meat per specified period), supervised by the BC Farm Industry Review Board, which hears appeals within 30 calendar days of a board's final decision. Egg (layer) sector quota transfer rules from the BC Egg Marketing Board's own material: UNVERIFIED in this research file. https://bcbhec.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/2022-Annual-Report.pdf and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals
- **Biosecurity and avian influenza affect timing, not tenure:** highly pathogenic avian influenza is reportable to the CFIA; in a primary control zone, permits are required to move birds, products and by-products, and on a quarantined farm nothing bird-related enters or leaves without CFIA permission — which can delay placements, showings and completion on an operation sale. https://www.inspection.gc.ca/en/animal-health/terrestrial-animals/diseases/reportable/avian-influenza/what-expect-your-animals-are-infected
- **Municipal zoning is parcel-by-parcel:** this topic has no single municipality; for example, Chilliwack's Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 defines POULTRY as any domesticated fowl including chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese and swans, and lists General Agriculture as a permitted principal use in its AL, AU and AS zones. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf

### The townhouse-side contrast and buyer frame (one block)

- If a buyer instead buys a genuine townhouse in a farm-belt municipality, the strata document set applies in full: Form B diligence (Strata Property Act s.59); depreciation reports every five years for 5+ lot stratas since July 1, 2024; CRF funded at a minimum 10% of the operating budget since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. None of that machinery exists on an ALR farm purchase, which is the point of the contrast. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT and its exemptions as they apply to the specific (usually farm-class) transaction — confirm the farm/ALR PTT position for the parcel rather than assuming a residential exemption; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. REDMA s.21 7-day rescission and s.18 trust deposits apply to presales — no presale is part of this topic. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a poultry farm's farmhouse a townhouse I can buy separately?** No. On ALR land a residence cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), so the farmhouse sells only as part of the farm parcel — land, barns, residence and quota where included. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: The feed card says "poultry farm" — is it a working operation?** Not on the feed's word alone. The research file records the live pages' cards as residential/land listings whose text mentions a possible poultry use, a barn, or an operation with quota not included — a card is never a confirmed operation in this file, and no marketing-board source was opened in the research program to confirm any card. (Research file §1)
- **Q: Does quota come with the farm?** No, not automatically. Quota transfers need BC Chicken Marketing Board approval, the General Orders treat transfer with registered premises separately from acquisition/disposition without premises, and listings may state quota is not included. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BCCMB-GENERAL-ORDERS-Aug-26-2011.pdf
- **Q: What is quota actually measured in?** By sector: broiler quota in kilograms of live weight per cycle; turkey quota in pounds of turkey meat per specified period; hatching egg quota in hen units (mainstream) or hatched chicks (specialty). https://bcchicken.ca/BCChickenAnnualReport2017.pdf
- **Q: Can I get a quota price from a listing or a board page?** No official source publishes quota transaction prices, and trades are facilitated privately — quota prices are UNVERIFIED in this program, and any figure quoted from a listing is a listing figure, not a board figure. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/QUAC-Discussion-Paper-FINAL.Oct_.2024.pdf
- **Q: Can a municipality stop me building a poultry barn in the ALR?** Under ALR Use Regulation B.C. Reg. 30/2019, an intensive-livestock structure is a designated farm use a local enactment may not prohibit — though it may be regulated, and the structure must be required for the farm use and in line with its level. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019
- **Q: How big can the farmhouse and a second home be?** Principal residence at most 500 m² (suite within the principal residence only); one additional residence at most 90 m² at 40 ha or less, or 186 m² over 40 ha, subject to local bylaws. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What does farm class actually require?** The three tiers in full: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" claim is an error. (Section 3 above)
- **Q: What happens to a sale during an avian influenza outbreak?** HPAI is reportable; CFIA control-zone permits govern movement of birds, eggs and by-products, and a quarantined farm cannot move anything bird-related without CFIA permission — placements, showings and completion dates all move with that status. https://www.inspection.gc.ca/en/animal-health/terrestrial-animals/diseases/reportable/avian-influenza/what-expect-your-animals-are-infected
- **Q: Who do I appeal to if a board refuses a transfer?** The BC Farm Industry Review Board, the independent tribunal supervising the regulated marketing system — appeals are filed within 30 calendar days of the board's final decision. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/governments/organizational-structure/ministries-organizations/boards-commissions-tribunals/bc-farm-industry-review-board/npma-appeals

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The licence is annual, the quota is the asset:** hatcheries, growers, transporters and processors are licensed by the BC Chicken Marketing Board on an annual basis, and a "grower" in the General Orders is a person the board has allotted quota or issued a permit — a buyer steps into a licensed, quota-bound role, not just a land purchase. https://bcchicken.ca/BCChickenAnnualReport2017.pdf
- **A quota exchange was in development, not confirmed operating:** the board's Policy Directions Update states staff were directed to develop a BC Chicken Quota Exchange platform — whether it is operating is NOT CONFIRMED, so do not tell a buyer they can simply buy quota on an exchange. https://bcchicken.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Policy-Directions-Update-Sep-2026.pdf
- **Biosecurity has zones with legal weight in practice:** provincial poultry guidance works with a Controlled Access Zone for the production area and a more restricted Restricted Access Zone where birds are housed, with only essential visitors and no contact between poultry and wild birds — showings on a working operation follow the farm's biosecurity protocol, not a normal open-house pattern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/animal-and-crops/animal-health/ai_enhanced_biosecurity_for_small_scale_poultry_producers.pdf
- **"Quota not included" is a common, deal-changing structure:** the Abbotsford West listing behind the live pages states it expressly — the land and barns price one way, and the buyer's separate quota acquisition (with board approval) is a second transaction entirely. https://jaredgibbons.ca/poultry-farms-for-sale-bc.html
