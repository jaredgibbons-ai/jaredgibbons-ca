## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally — it is the document that settles Eton Crest):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the 2048 Triumph Street self-managed strata, the Form B and the strata's own records are the whole diligence file — a self-managed strata has no manager's building page to cross-check. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **Threshold note:** a strata of four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold — Eton Crest's lot count is UNCONFIRMED in this file (see Section 2), so whether the five-year depreciation-report duty applies to it cannot be stated until the strata plan is read; do not assume either way. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and in Vancouver short-term rentals are separately regulated by the City's own licensing. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Hastings strata is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in Hastings-Sunrise — the sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ strata stock of any form in the area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes and row product:** none is verified as a named complex here. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 schedule) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. The area's new half-duplex/multiplex product trades on exactly that basis ("no strata fee" in listing text), but it is house form, not a townhouse complex.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. Eton Crest's own listing records zoning RM-3A for its parcel — a corridor/multi-family district line that differs from the R1-1 house streets and is one more reason its strata documents, not the street's zoning, govern a purchase there. Corridor commercial and mixed-use sites on Hastings Street sit under other district schedules, including C-3A on parts of the street; the zone for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2118-eton-street
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them, in Vancouver form:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale framework for these streets — multiplexes to 6 units (8 rental), secondary suite and laneway house in the forms the schedule states. Eton Crest's lock-off secondary suite is a unit-level example of the form; whether a given suite is legal for a given lot is a parcel check, not a neighbourhood claim.
- **Plans:** the western edge of Hastings-Sunrise is included in the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, and the citywide Vancouver Plan also applies; a separate community plan named only for Hastings-Sunrise: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain, with Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels for parcels in it; the Burrard Inlet shoreline at the area's north side, including New Brighton Park, is the part to check against that dataset; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3% — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable tax rate of 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — confirm the seller's declaration status in due diligence on any Hastings purchase. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf?ref=theurbanist.org and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property tax calendar:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — the program's price band overlaps this corridor's low-rise price points, so run eligibility early; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example the ETON queue project, if it proceeds as a presale), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA), where a Hastings presale is bought:** the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for the ETON queue item in Section 2 if it proceeds to sale. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area was a resort before it was a suburb.** The City states Hastings-Sunrise sits on the northern half of a block of land the provincial government set aside in 1863 for a town expected to develop along the shore of Burrard Inlet; Gastown farther west became the port city instead, and a small resort, New Brighton, developed on the shoreline roughly where New Brighton Park is today. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **Two different "Eton" products share one street name.** Eton Crest (built 2014, listing address 2118 Eton Street) is the existing townhouse complex held out in Section 2 on tenure evidence, and ETON is a separate 21-townhome project marketed for Eton Street in Hastings-Sunrise by Silver Star Properties — a buyer, a search engine, or a listing agent can merge them; the addresses and documents must not be. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/2118-eton-street and https://propelmarketing.ca/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ETON_HastingsSunrise_Digital_AKit_Final-1-2.pdf
- **The shopping district has a second name.** The Hastings Street shopping district in Hastings-Sunrise is branded the "East Village" — searches under only "Hastings-Sunrise" miss records filed under the district name, and the sibling condos file's Sugar and L'Atelier entries sit in that pocket. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **The west edge carries a second plan.** The western edge of Hastings-Sunrise sits inside the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area, which layers plan policy on top of base zoning — a redevelopment or multiplex assumption for a west-edge lot priced off R1-1 alone is missing a document. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **The newest apartment building on the corridor is rental, not condo.** Sunrise Heights at 2215 East Hastings Street (built 2020, 37 units, professionally managed) is purpose-built rental — a buyer touring "new Hastings apartments" there is looking at a rental building, and its existence adds nothing to the ownership supply counted in this program. https://www.zumper.com/apartment-buildings/p917826/sunrise-heights-apartments-grandview-woodland-vancouver-bc
- **House-street lots carry multiplex rights corridor lots do not.** Under R1-1 a qualifying house-street lot can carry a multiplex of up to 6 units (up to 8 rental units) plus a secondary suite and laneway house in the forms the schedule states, subject to minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² — while Hastings Street commercial sites sit under different districts such as C-3A, and Eton Crest's parcel is recorded as RM-3A. The land bases should never be comped against each other. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
