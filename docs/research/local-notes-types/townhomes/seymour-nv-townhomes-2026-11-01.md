## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata complex):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At McCartney Lane the Form B package carries extra weight in this file: it is where the STRATA tenure carried in §2 (on listing evidence) is confirmed lot by lot, where the complex's UNCONFIRMED unit count and strata plan number can be settled from the strata plan itself, and where the pet position (listing copy only in §2) is settled against the registered bylaws. **The Mount Seymour Park co-operative has no Form B** — it is a co-operative, not strata (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. McCartney Lane is a late-1980s wood-frame complex — the oldest stock in this batch — so its depreciation report is the central diligence document; its exact lot count is UNCONFIRMED in this file (§2), so its report duty is stated as the project-level position a buyer confirms on the Form B. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry in this file's ground: Parkgate Place's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" (§2, apartment section) is read subject to that rule — any long-term rental restriction in its registered bylaws is VOID. McCartney Lane's own rental position is UNCONFIRMED, so no in-entry line arises there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. No Seymour NV complex in §2 is published as age-restricted on any source checked — McCartney Lane is general-market, and the co-operative sometimes miscategorised as seniors housing is all-ages on its own profile. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Seymour NV (§2). A fee-simple townhouse, where one exists elsewhere, carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. Seymour's fee-simple stock is detached houses under District rules, including the suite/coach-house and ground-oriented permissions below; do not import strata expectations into those deals.

- **Q: What hazard checks does a Seymour townhouse buyer run?** The District's GeoWeb property hazard report (slope and creek Development Permit Areas), read against the District's 2026-updated Landslide Risk Assessment — standard for any lot near the Seymour River, a creek ravine or a steep bank, strata or detached — plus the strata's depreciation report for the building itself. McCartney Creek Park sits beside the counted complex; creek proximity is part of its setting, not a footnote. (§1 and §3 above)
