## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because there is no townhouse stock, this section covers (a) why the zoning/ALR framework prevents townhouses here, (b) the fee-simple reality that replaces strata rules on the prairie, and (c) the provincial strata rules that apply the moment a buyer redirects to real townhouse stock elsewhere in Abbotsford.

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction, as it applies here:** Matsqui homes are fee-simple freehold farm and rural-residential properties. For a fee-simple home there is NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014), ALR rules where the parcel is in the ALR, and any registered covenants only. A strata townhouse purchase elsewhere in Abbotsford is the opposite: strata lot, strata fees, Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **ALR stratification bar:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — standing correction in the commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026. That bar, plus the A1 (8.0 ha) and RR (4.0 ha) subdivision minimums, is why a farm property cannot be converted into townhouse strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm-class thresholds (full three-tier form):** $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 — standing correction in the commissioning brief. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error; no such flat line is documented in this area's research file.
- **City plan / SSMUH:** Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025, is the current Official Community Plan, whose rural-centres policy recognizes Matsqui Village among Abbotsford's seven historical rural centres — the research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE and flagged here as a Jerry sweep item. June 2024 SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED and "3560-2024" must not be asserted, and SSMUH is a ground-oriented form that does not create townhouse zoning on A1/RR prairie land. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule only) — standing corrections in the commissioning brief. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Added dwellings on prairie land (the local substitute for townhouse density):** the A1 zone permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot only under the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, capped at 90 m² (lots ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (lots >40 ha), plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m²; in the RR zone a coach house is permitted only on a lot greater than 0.4 ha, with a coach house or suite limited to one per lot and the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the principal dwelling's net floor area. None of these creates a separate title for sale. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **If a strata townhouse is bought elsewhere in Abbotsford — the BC standard block:** Form B diligence (fee, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage, insurance summary); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, with no deferral vote; minimum CRF contribution of 10% of the operating fund since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, exemptions including the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner expansion). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Taxes on a purchase:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M; the FTHB program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000), and the Newly Built Home Exemption is full to $1,100,000 (partial to $1,150,000). A new home carries 5% GST, with the FTHB GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) returning 100% to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, for agreements on/after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, and the older GST New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out) underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale or new-construction townhouse project in Matsqui Prairie was verified; elsewhere, REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission and s.18 requires deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Local tax wrinkle specific to the prairie:** properties in the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District pay a separate dyking district levy on top of general municipal rates, per the City's 2026 Tax Rates Summary under Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026. Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I actually buy a townhouse in Matsqui Prairie?** No verified townhouse complex — strata or fee-simple — exists in the sub-area: attached inventory and sales were both 0 in September 2026, and the zoning is agricultural/rural-residential single-detached. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Why are there no townhouses — could a developer build some?** The minimum lot sizes alone block the form (A1 subdivision 8.0 ha; RR 4.0 ha), most parcels are in or beside the ALR, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. No townhouse rezoning or project was verified in any source checked. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What do I get instead of a townhouse here?** A fee-simple detached farm or rural-residential home: no strata fees, no Form B, no strata bylaws — instead zoning, ALR rules, well and septic diligence, and the Matsqui Prairie Dyking District levy on the tax bill. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026%20Tax%20Rates%20Summary%20May%2022.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a small second home for family on a prairie lot?** Within caps: in A1, one suite to 100 m² and one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit (90 m² on lots ≤40 ha; 186 m² over 40 ha) via the ALR Use Regulation or ALC approval, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha; in RR, one coach house or suite on a lot over 0.4 ha, at the lesser of 90 m² or 40% of the main house. Abbotsford's general suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling and its coach/garden cap is 15% of lot area to 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400310
- **Q: Is flood risk part of buying here?** Yes. The City states the Matsqui Dykes are designed to prevent Fraser River water entering Matsqui Prairie, and prairie properties pay the separate dyking district levy — design protection is not a parcel-level guarantee, so confirm flood status with the City for the specific lot. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/2022-spring-freshet-update-2
- **Q: If I redirect to a strata townhouse in Clearbrook or the Rail District, can I rent it out?** For a long-term tenancy, yes — rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term rental bylaws and any 55+ residency bylaw still apply. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: How would I judge a redirected townhouse complex's finances?** Read the Form B for the fee, CRF balance and approved levies, and the depreciation report for major-item timing; stratas with 5+ lots must renew the report every five years and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. There is no Matsqui complex whose figures can be quoted: UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Which schools serve the prairie?** Máthxwi Elementary is in the village; a locator run for its own address (33661 Elizabeth Ave) returned Máthxwí Elementary → Clayburn Middle → Robert Bateman Secondary. Catchments are address-based — run the specific address in the SD34 School Locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/schools/catchments
- **Q: Is there transit I can rely on?** Only bus service: Route 31 / 31A to Matsqui Village between Bourquin Exchange and Mission City Station. There is no rapid transit in or planned for the prairie in any source checked. https://www.bctransit.com/central-fraser-valley/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=31
- **Q: Would a new-build home here get the GST rebate?** A new home (any form) can qualify for the FTHB GST rebate — 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements on/after March 20, 2025, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 — if the buyer and home meet CRA conditions; resale homes attract no GST and no rebate. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **The prairie's plan status protects the current form.** Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025) recognizes Matsqui Village under its rural-centres policy as one of seven historical rural centres — there is no neighbourhood plan designating the prairie for townhouse development in any source checked. - **No presale or new-construction townhouse project in Matsqui Prairie was verified** on a City development page or developer site in the Nov 1, 2026 research. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/matsqui-guide.md, §4)
- **The recreation centre named for this area is not in this area.** Matsqui Recreation Centre and the MRC Sports Complex are at 3106 Clearbrook Road in Clearbrook, and the nearest library is Clearbrook Library, 32320 George Ferguson Way — services cluster south of the prairie, which is the practical reality behind the zero-townhouse finding. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/
- **The dyke is also the recreation.** Matsqui Trail Regional Park runs walking and cycling trails along the Fraser River dyke — a Fraser Valley Regional District regional park maintained and operated by the City of Abbotsford — so the flood infrastructure doubles as the sub-area's main park amenity. http://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/matsqui-trail-regional-park-grade-railway-crossing-closure
- **"Matsqui" names three different things.** The board sub-area (this file) is the City of Abbotsford's Matsqui Prairie; Matsqui Village is the historic rural centre inside it; and Matsqui First Nation is a separate nation whose land at Aldergrove hosts the 55+ Pioneer Park co-op. Searches blend all three, and only the first two are in Abbotsford. https://apps.gov.bc.ca/pub/bcgnws/names/25776.html and https://pioneerparkco-op.org
