## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The ALR frame governs this topic — full frame stated.** On ALR land: one principal residence per parcel is permitted without an ALC application at a maximum total floor area of **500 m²**, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside the principal residence** (a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites); one additional, detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum **90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha** — and it does not need to be intended for farm help, provided local bylaws are met. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR (number, size, siting), and building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR residences cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — the rule that makes §2's zero structural rather than a gap in the search. There is therefore no Form B, no strata fee and no depreciation report for a farm home: the strata diligence block below applies only to the urban townhouse stock a buyer might compare a farm purchase against, and it is stated so the contrast is explicit.
- **Form B diligence (urban strata comparison stock only):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No farm parcel in this topic has one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (strata comparison stock only):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Neither rule touches a freehold farm parcel. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental bylaws (strata comparison stock only):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid — every directory "rentals not allowed" line for a strata is VOID on that rule; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). On farm land the rental question is different — see the ALR short-term item below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple position — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. A Cloverdale farm dwelling goes further: it is a freehold single-family residence on an ALR parcel, governed by Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, the Surrey 2050 OCP (**Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED, never cite 18020 as current) and the ALC frame above, which is the stricter layer. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Urban rules that do NOT carry onto ALR parcels:** Surrey's SSMUH framework (in force July 8, 2024 — 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots ≥281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop) is an urban-lot framework; the urban secondary-suite cap of **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** and the coach-house/garden-suite route (35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR) do not create extra dwellings on an ALR parcel, where the suite must be inside the principal residence and any additional residence is size-capped by the ALC. Whether a coach house can serve as the ALR "additional residence" on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/cloverdale-farms.md)
- **Short-term rentals — two layers, both restrictive here:** in the ALR, whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. The City layer separately permits STRs owner-only (owner living in the home; tenants cannot operate them) with a business licence the City's STR page states at **$358 per year** — carried here with a **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. Neither layer gives a farm buyer a whole-house STR by right. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC Assessment farm classification (Class 9) — full three-tier thresholds:** $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha; $10,000 if the total area of the farm operation is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of **October 31**, for consideration for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement of these thresholds is an ERROR — see the defect item below. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a townhouse on a Cloverdale farm?** No — not as strata. ALR residences cannot be stratified, so farm housing is freehold single-family product: one principal residence (≤500 m²) plus at most one small additional residence per parcel. The townhouse complexes in the Cloverdale name are urban Clayton / town-centre stock, counted in the cloverdale-bc townhomes file. (§2 above) and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR farmland in Cloverdale?** One principal residence per parcel without an ALC application, at a maximum total floor area of 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; anything larger requires ALC approval, and the City may regulate further. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a second residence permitted on a Cloverdale ALR acreage?** One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and City of Surrey bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building on a Cloverdale farm?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," size-capped at 90 m² / 186 m². The urban 40%-of-principal suite cap and coach-house route do not override that. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **The suite rule flips on ALR land.** In urban Surrey a suite can sit in a house under the 40% cap and a coach house is a separate route; on an ALR parcel the suite must be inside the principal residence, full stop — a barn suite is not a suite, it is an unauthorized second dwelling unless it qualifies as the size-capped additional residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The additional residence does not need to house farm help.** The ALC states the permitted additional residence does not need to be intended for farm help, provided local bylaws are met — the constraint that binds is size (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size), not occupancy purpose. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm classification is an annual position, not a purchase feature.** The thresholds are tested against the operation's income and the application deadline is October 31 — a buyer who prices a parcel on its current farm-class taxes without testing their own operation's income against the three tiers is pricing an assumption. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Cloverdale took its name from clover.** The City states Cloverdale was settled in the mid to late 1800s, grew from a largely rural and agricultural community, and took its name from Clover Valley, after the clover growing in the valley — the farm belt is the community's original form, not its edge case. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities/cloverdale
