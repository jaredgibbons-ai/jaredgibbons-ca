## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (standard BC frame — no strata townhome exists here today; it governs the Area D bare-land strata next door and any future strata product).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report. No Form B exists for a Popkum house or land parcel — a listing here claiming strata documents is a classification error to resolve before subjects. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; sub-5-lot UNDER threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% since Nov 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rentals:** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The voiding does NOT create a rental right in a pad-rent park — Mt Cheam's no-rentals position is a park/tenancy matter, not a strata bylaw (§2.5).
- **Age:** only 55+ bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions). No age bylaw is verified for any Popkum product — there is no strata here — and Mt Cheam is expressly NOT 55+ (§2.5).
- **Fee-simple contrast:** the governing case in Popkum today — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; FVRD rules and registered covenants only.

## 4. What buyers ask

Servicing is parcel-specific; the guide's own checklist directs buyers to confirm water/sewer first (§3).

**Q: Will townhomes be built in Popkum under the current proposal?**

**Q: How does transit work here?**
