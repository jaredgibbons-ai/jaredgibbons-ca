## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the bare-land strata at 53480 Bridal Falls Road — the area's only strata product — even though no townhome strata exists here).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For a bare-land strata lot, this package — not the resort's marketing — establishes the fees, what they cover, and the registered pet, age and use rules (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. A bare-land strata resort with a clubhouse, pool and serviced lots carries exactly the common-property assets a depreciation report prices. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the registered bylaws on the Form B package settle what applies to a specific lot (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). No age bylaw is verified for the 53480 Bridal Falls Road strata — and Mt Cheam MHP's "mostly senior-occupied" character is expressly NOT a 55+ rule (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — none is verified here (§2.2). Bridal Falls' rural homes are the fee-simple case in practice: FVRD rules and registered covenants only.

**FVRD frame — plan, zoning, ALR, flood and taxes. Chilliwack's bylaws do NOT apply.**

- **Local government and plan:** the FVRD is the local government for Electoral Area D (planning and development approvals, building inspection, bylaw enforcement); member municipalities govern themselves, so City of Chilliwack zoning does not apply in Bridal Falls (research file §4; https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html). The one zone actually evidenced is the resort lots' CD1, from MLS listing records (§2.5).
- **ALR frame:** on ALR land, a principal residence is capped at 500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite is permitted only entirely within the principal residence where local bylaws permit; one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), subject to local bylaws. Parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED until checked on the ALC maps (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps).

Chilliwack's municipal penalty dates do NOT govern here (standing program position; Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11).
