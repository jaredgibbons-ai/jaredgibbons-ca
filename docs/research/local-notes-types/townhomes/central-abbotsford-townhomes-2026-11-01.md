## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

The full strata rule block for this sub-area's five counted complexes is in the T1 guide file's Section 3; the points below are the ones the non-guide research file adds or corrects. Nothing here changes a count.

- **OCP — CURRENT citation, with the stale ones flagged:** the city-wide Official Community Plan is **Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025**. The research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE/conflicted and are flagged as Jerry sweep items; apply 2050. Within it, the City Centre Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2913-2019) and Historic Downtown Neighbourhood Plan (Bylaw 2920-2019) are the adopted neighbourhood plans for the core, and Historic Downtown's Urban 2 – Ground Oriented designation enables rowhouses, townhouses, multiplexes and duplexes. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/Historic%20Downtown%20Neighbourhood%20Plan%20Final%20Event%20Boards.pdf
- **Zoning:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014 governs — RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m², RS5 420 m², RS7 (Infill Residential) new interior lots down to 300 m² (10.0 m width, 22.5 m depth). https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- **SSMUH / suites:** the bylaw's s.140 small-scale multi-unit provision allows 3 units on lots 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots greater than 280 m² in the stated conditions, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings. The SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" must not be asserted. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rule only. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Strata block (applies to all five counted complexes — summary; full text in the T1 guide file):** Form B diligence on every resale; depreciation reports every five years for strata corporations with five or more lots since July 1, 2024, not deferrable by vote; at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (any "rentals not allowed" line for a counted complex is void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023) — Oakhurst Park, Summerset Place and Willband Creek Estates are the verified 55+ townhouse complexes. Fee-simple contrast: a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area in either file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Buyer frame:** FTHB GST/HST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; PTT (1% first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M; first-time-buyer exemption on the first $500,000 at values $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** Rail District townhome phases sold as presales (see The Abby Townhome Series in the T1 guide file) carry REDMA s.21 7-day rescission from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, and s.18 deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Abbotsford property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; first 5% penalty July 3, 2026; second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Why are there two Central Abbotsford townhomes files?** Because the site has two page slugs for the sub-area (a guide page and a non-guide page) but only one stock of homes. The T1 guide file counts the 5 verified strata complexes; this file, built from the non-guide research, counts 0 new ones — the homes are the same homes, counted once. (Files: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/central-abbotsford-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md and this file.)
- **Q: Is a central townhouse strata or fee-simple?** Strata in every verified case — Oakhurst Park (NWS1894), Summerset Place, Willband Creek Estates, Canterbury Gate and The Abby Townhome Series all carry strata title or published strata fees. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Central Abbotsford in either file. (T1 guide file, Section 4.)
- **Q: Three of the complexes are 55+. What does that actually restrict?** Residency, not ownership: an under-55 buyer can own, but may not live in the unit unless exempt (younger spouse/partner, legacy resident, live-in caregiver, defined children — Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023). Confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a unit in a 55+ townhouse complex?** For a long-term tenancy, the strata cannot rely on a rental-restriction bylaw — those are void since November 24, 2022 — but the tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Willband Creek Estates: pet friendly per the sibling 55-plus file. Canterbury Gate: one listing states no pets for its unit. Summerset Place: listing sources conflict — UNCONFIRMED. Oakhurst Park and The Abby: UNCONFIRMED as bylaws. Read the registered bylaws for the specific complex. (T1 guide file, Section 4.)
- **Q: How do I judge a 1980s complex's finances?** Read the Form B for the fee, CRF balance and approved levies, and the depreciation report for major-item timing: 5+ lot stratas must renew it every five years and contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: I'm buying a Rail District townhouse presale. What protects me?** REDMA: a filed disclosure statement, deposits held in trust (s. 18), and a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing and acknowledging the disclosure statement (s. 21). Completion dates and assignment terms are in the disclosure statement, not the brochure. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Q: Which schools does a central townhouse feed into?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. Locator runs in the research file: 33130 Bevan Ave returns Godson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary, and 32161 Dormick Avenue returns Dormick Park Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary. Run the complex's own address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/central-abbotsford.md)
- **Q: Is The Argyle a Central Abbotsford complex?** Feeds disagree — the same 34248 King Road address appears as Poplar, Abbotsford East and Central Abbotsford in different listings. Under this program it is counted once, in the Poplar townhomes file, and cross-referenced here. The registered strata plan, not the feed label, is the stable identifier. (T1 guide file, Section 5.)
- **Q: Is flood risk a central townhouse issue?** The central upland is not Sumas Prairie, but Willband Creek runs through the central area — Willband Creek Estates takes its name from it — and parcel-level floodplain status requires a City floodplain map check for the specific complex. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford replaced its Official Community Plan in December 2025.** Abbotsforward 2050 (public hearing November 25, 2025) emphasizes more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enables the 37,185 units the City's 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report targets over 20 years. Any page still citing "2600-2016" or "2500-2016" as the OCP is stale — flag for Jerry. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **The non-guide research file is unusually honest about its own gap:** it states outright that condo building names and townhome complex names "could not be confirmed on an official source" — which is why this variant file adds no complexes. The counted stock comes from the guide research and sibling files, and a buyer reading either page should land on the same five names. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/central-abbotsford.md)
- **The OCP's growth arrow points at the hospital–Mill Lake gap:** Abbotsforward 2050 states a new mixed-use centre between the hospital and Mill Lake Park and a Primary Transit Corridor along South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road — the corridor the George Ferguson Way townhouse cluster sits beside. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/city-begins-implementing-updated-official-community-plan
- **The Rail District is a 10-to-12-year build-out, not a finished neighbourhood:** the 19-acre former Clayburn Brick Plant site between Pine Street and Morey Avenue is planned as five neighbourhoods in nine phases; The Abby Townhome Series (91 homes in Phase 1) and The Mason condos are early phases. Townhouse buyers there are buying into a construction zone with the amenity payoff at the end. (T1 guide file, Section 5.)
- **Downtown's main intersection is being rebuilt:** the City is realigning Montvue Avenue — removing the single-lane connection to Essendene Avenue, connecting Montvue directly to West Railway Street, and turning the closed section into West Railway Plaza — on the Rail District townhouses' own street network. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/montvue-avenue-and-west-railway-plaza
- **Federal Housing Accelerator money is spent, not pending:** the City was approved for up to $25.6 million in HAF money and reported in October 2026 that all HAF initiatives are complete, with multi-family permitting above expectations — part of why so much new mid-rise and townhouse product landed in the core at once. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
