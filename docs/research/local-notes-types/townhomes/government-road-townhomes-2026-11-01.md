## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **City zoning frame:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026 and in effect July 1, 2026 — never the repealed 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The industrial and office parcels along the corridor are not R1 — confirm the zone parcel by parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. The Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the urban village NORTH of this corridor across Lougheed Highway — it is not the Government Road corridor's plan, and Lochdale permissions must not be quoted for Government Road parcels. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Form B diligence (both strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Directory and listing rental/pet lines in Section 2 are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Terramor (177 units) is well over the threshold; Tudor Village's unit count is UNCONFIRMED but its strata-fee structure indicates a multi-lot corporation, so confirm the report's existence on the Form B rather than assuming the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The "No Rentals" element of the Concorde Place headline noted in Section 2 is flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals for exactly this reason; Terramor's directory line reads "rentals allowed," which is consistent with the current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies ZERO 55+ stock of any tenure in Government Road — neither counted townhouse complex carries a published age restriction in the sources read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Government Road. If a fee-simple row home is marketed here, the rule is plain: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Note the SSMUH wrinkle: R1 lots with two or more primary units MAY be stratified, so a newer multi-unit house-form property here can be strata even when it looks like a house — check the Title to Land field. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Hazards:** Stoney Creek runs through the area to Burnaby Lake and the lake's east shore is low and wet; a floodplain designation for Government Road's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read, and parcel-level hazard, watercourse-setback and ALR status are property-specific checks — ALR status through the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Taxes and rebates:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Burnaby's 2026 residential utility notice covers annual water, annual sewer use, a cross-connection device charge where applicable, and a residential garbage toter fee; the detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. Burnaby is NOT subject to Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax — that tax is Vancouver-only and does not appear as a cost here. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Can I rent out my Government Road townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Terramor's directory record reads "rentals allowed" in any case. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** Government Road recorded 23 Condos & Townhomes listings and 3 solds (13% sales ratio) against 24 detached listings and no detached sales; the Burnaby community combined median was $686,000 at $763/sqft, 98% sale-to-list, 36 days on market — combined condos and townhomes, never a townhome-only figure (as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file).
- **Q: Is Tudor Village really strata? It looks like garden apartments.** The current listing data records a strata fee ($724/mo on MLS R3163623) and an earlier listing records water and heat included in monthly fees — a strata-fee structure. - **Q: Which schools serve a Government Road townhouse?** SD41 schools in or beside the area are École Seaforth Elementary (7881 Government Rd), Stoney Creek Community Elementary (2740 Beaverbrook Cres), Cameron Elementary (9540 Erickson Dr) and Burnaby Mountain Secondary (8800 Eastlake Dr) — but catchments are address-based and the locator result for this file's sample address is UNVERIFIED, so run the exact address, including before making any Seaforth French immersion claim. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: How is transit without a car?** Route 110 runs on Government Road itself, linking Lougheed Station and Production Way–University Station to the east with Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station and Metrotown to the west — the corridor's townhouses sit on a published bus spine to three Millennium Line stations. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt110.pdf
- **Q: Can a house lot near my townhouse be redeveloped into more units?** If the lot is R1, the SSMUH district allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — and multi-unit R1 lots may be stratified. Corridor parcels in industrial or office zones follow their own zone, so confirm the parcel's zone first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Are the "townhomes" at Discovery Place I a separate complex I can buy into?** No — the T-prefixed one-level townhomes are units inside the Discovery Place I tower complex (3980 Carrigan Court), an apartment strata counted in the condos file. Buying one is apartment-strata diligence, not townhouse-complex diligence. Source: the sibling condos file on disk.
- **Q: What replaces the Cameron centre during construction?** A Temporary Cameron Community Centre and Library operates inside The City of Lougheed Shopping Centre, 110-9855 Austin Avenue, while the $267 million replacement is built; the old complex closed August 18, 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2024-07-23/council-approves-award-contract-advance-construction-replacement-cameron

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The governing zoning bylaw changed mid-2026.** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026 — any advice, listing copy or older page citing the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 for a Government Road property is citing a repealed bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The plan across the highway is not this corridor's plan.** The Lochdale Urban Village Community Plan (adopted July 25, 2022) covers the lands north across Lougheed Highway; Government Road parcels are governed by the Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) and the parcel's own zone. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **The original Seaforth School is a 1922 heritage building — and it is not on Government Road anymore.** The City's heritage record dates the original Seaforth School to 1922 and notes it has since been relocated to Burnaby Village Museum; the operating school at 7881 Government Rd is the modern École Seaforth Elementary. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark541
- **Burnaby Lake is a working regional park, not just scenery:** Metro Vancouver's Burnaby Lake Regional Park is a wildlife sanctuary with a rowing course, a rowing pavilion and public canoe launch at the Burnaby Lake Sports Complex, a Nature House, and viewing towers and docks — and the City and Metro Vancouver signed a 25-year lease in 2025 for the 360-acre park Metro Vancouver has managed since 1978. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park and http://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-02-05/city-burnaby-and-metro-vancouver-sign-burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Stoney Creek's park lands are dedicated by bylaw:** the Stoney Creek Park and Trail System and the Cariboo and Brunette River Conservation Lands run through the area's east side, and Stoney Creek Park carries 2 tennis courts and 2 pickleball courts on the City's outdoor courts list — creek-edge lots carry watercourse setbacks that travel with the creek. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html and https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/outdoor-courts
- **The area's hospital is across the city:** Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St (Fraser Health), serves Burnaby from the west side — a Government Road buyer's hospital trip is a cross-city trip, not a neighbourhood one. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Burnaby/burnaby-hospital
