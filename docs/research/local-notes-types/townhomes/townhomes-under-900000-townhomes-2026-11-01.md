### Worked band math — inputs stated, tiers shown

- **PTT on an $829,000 resale townhouse, no exemption (inputs: price $829,000 — the top of this feed's visible range — resale, no exemption claimed):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $629,000 = $12,580, total **$14,580**. A purchase anywhere under $900,000 meets only those two tiers. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time buyer effect at $829,000 (inputs: qualifying first-time buyer, fair market value $829,000 — inside the $835,000 full-exemption value limit by $6,000 of headroom):** the program exempts the tax on the first $500,000 — that slice would otherwise cost 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = $8,000, the Province's stated maximum saving — leaving PTT of $14,580 − $8,000 = **$6,580**. At $860,001 and above in this band, the exemption is gone on price alone; between $835,000 and $860,000 it is partial. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **At the ceiling, $900,000, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $700,000 = $14,000, total **$16,000** — and no first-time exemption at all on price alone, because $900,000 is above the $860,000 partial-exemption limit. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Minimum insured down payment at $569,000 (inputs: price $569,000 — the bottom of this feed's visible range — down payment under 20%, insured mortgage):** 5% × $500,000 = $25,000, plus 10% × $69,000 = $6,900, total **$31,900**, plus the CMHC premium (0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount) where insurance applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Newly built position anywhere in this band (inputs: qualifying newly built townhome, price at or under $900,000):** the newly built home exemption's full-exemption threshold is $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — so every price in this band is inside the full threshold on price alone; the program's other conditions still have to be met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata complex named in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. The Province's strata buying guidance directs buyers to the Form B, the Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Longlea Estates (58 units on its fresh-verified feature sheet) and Clayton Rise are both well over the threshold on their verified scales. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. That is why the "Rentals Not Allowed" line in Glenwood Village's MLS building data (§2) is flagged in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Glenwood Village's 55+ status is this band's live example: two townhomes at the same price can be open to very different buyer pools. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Every complex verified in §2 is strata ("freehold strata" is still strata); no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified for this band.
- **Surrey-side city rules (Surrey complexes only — Clayton and West Newton; they do NOT govern the Langley City stock):** current Official Community Plan = Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** (the former Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current); Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; SSMUH in force since **July 8, 2024** — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, and no retroactive legalization of unpermitted suites; one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**; STRs permitted owner-only with the host living in the home, business licence **$358 per year** on the City's page — **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees, so confirm the current figure on the City licence page; Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Langley-side city rules:** this file states no Langley City zoning, suite, STR-licence or tax figures, because this slug's research file documents none and importing Surrey's figures across the municipal line would be an error. Langley City figures for a specific townhouse: UNCONFIRMED in this file — confirm on the City of Langley's own pages before quoting them. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/townhomes-under-900000.md)
- **Taxes and rebates (province-wide, both municipalities):** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math in §2 governs, and nothing in this band reaches those tiers. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000.
