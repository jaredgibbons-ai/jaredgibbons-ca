## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (Knight 18; The Charles if its tenure proves strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 2026-built complex the Form B also settles which phase's strata the unit sits in — the document that reconciles Knight 18's name, phase count and unit count. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Knight 18 (7 units on the current-listing figure) and The Charles (16 townhomes, if strata) both sit over the threshold on their published counts. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. In brand-new complexes the first depreciation report and the warranty record (2-5-10) matter more than in mature stock — confirm both in the package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Knight 18's listing line "No Restrictions, Rentals Allowed" is consistent with current law, not a special permission — and the same voiding would govern The Charles if its tenure proves strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Knight corridor townhouse complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked — age bylaws: UNCONFIRMED per complex; check the Form B (or, for The Charles, the tenure documents first). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple side-street product:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. That is the correct default frame for the corridor's duplex and multiplex homes; if The Charles' tenure proves fee-simple rather than strata, its diligence moves to this frame entirely — which is why the tenure question in Section 2 is not a footnote.
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. The residential side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The King Edward Village site is a site-specific CD-1 district, and the district schedule for the Knight Street arterial frontage itself (commercial / mixed-use and industrial stretches): UNVERIFIED in the research pass. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suite rules as the city states them:** the R1-1 regime above is Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit housing frame — suite and laneway permissions with a house, and the 6-unit / 8-rental-unit multiplex ceilings — and it is what prices the side-street lots around both townhouse collections. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plans:** the Kensington-Cedar Cottage, Sunset and Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions apply to the local areas the corridor runs through, alongside the citywide Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; a Knight-corridor-specific adopted plan: UNVERIFIED. Date-check any policy citation for the King Edward node: the June 2025 Conwest referral records that Council repealed the Kensington-Cedar Cottage Community Vision in November 2023 — do not cite the old vision as current policy for that corner. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/knight.md) and https://council.vancouver.ca/20250603/documents/rr10.pdf
- **Flood:** the corridor's south end descends to the Fraser River's north arm at the Knight Street Bridge; the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, with Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it. The counted townhouse stock sits at the corridor's north end, away from the river slope — a specific parcel's status: UNVERIFIED, check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%** of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — the exemption to test first on a developer sale at Knight 18 or The Charles); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (a developer sale at either collection), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. Both corridor collections were sold under this frame; Knight 18's completion estimates slipped from Spring 2024 to 2026 in the sources — the disclosure statement's stated completion and any amendments are the diligence record for any remaining developer unit. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file or in the sibling condos/55-plus files checked — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file.
