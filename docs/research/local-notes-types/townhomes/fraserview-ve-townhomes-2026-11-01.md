## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence:** there is no counted strata townhouse complex in this file for the Form B frame to attach to — but it governs every Fraserview VE strata purchase a buyer does make (the Victoria Drive apartment stratas in the sibling condos file, and Avalon Mews resales across the Killarney filing line): Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. In this district's small low-rises (12 units at Fraserview Place) the report and CRF are disproportionately important — a single roof or envelope project is spread over very few lots. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The sibling condos file flags the directory line for Fraserview Place ("rentals allowed with restrictions") on exactly this basis — it cannot amount to a long-term ban. No townhouse-complex directory line arises in this file because no townhouse complex is counted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Fraserview VE building in the sibling programs is published as 55+ — the sibling 55-plus file verified no age-restricted strata building in the district at all; its verified seniors addresses are independent-living rental, assisted living, long-term care and social housing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes, duplexes and multiplexes (the district's actual ground-oriented form):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly, except where an individual multiplex unit proves on title to be a strata lot (the 7742 Victoria Drive tenure is UNCONFIRMED — check it). A "townhouse" listing here with no strata fees charged is usually this form.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units. The Victoria Drive frontage sits under commercial districts instead (the 6991 Victoria Drive strata plan is recorded under zone C-2); the Avalon Mews site is CD-1 (619) under By-law No. 11342. Parcel-level zone for any other specific property: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/bylaws/CD-1/CD-1(619).pdf
- **SSMUH / suite rules as the city states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit housing is the R1-1 multiplex regime above — up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on a qualifying lot, with suite and laneway permissions — and it is the live development frame for the district's house streets, including the fourplex product now listing on Victoria Drive. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan:** the Victoria-Fraserview / Killarney community visions work applies to the local area, alongside the citywide Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; a Fraserview-specific adopted plan: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/fraserview-ve.md)
- **Flood:** the district is on the south slope to the Fraser River; the City's designated floodplain includes the coastal floodplain along the Fraser River in South Vancouver, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — most relevant to the lowest streets above the river. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%** of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (a new fourplex unit, for example), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. No Fraserview VE townhouse presale is carried in this file; the frame governs any developer sale at V on E49 and any future district presale. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
