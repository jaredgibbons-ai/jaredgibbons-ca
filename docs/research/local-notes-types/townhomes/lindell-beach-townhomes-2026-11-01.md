## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero, this section works in two parts: the strata rules that govern the bare-land strata communities that do exist here (and any townhouse purchase if stock is ever built), and the FVRD rural rules that explain the stock that actually exists.

- **Form B diligence (any strata — including the bare-land stratas in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a bare-land strata lot the Form B is still the first diligence document — it is also the check that a "townhouse" listing at the lake is strata at all, and which form of strata it is. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is carried for any Lindell Beach complex in §2 — no source checked states one for the three bare-land communities; the rule is stated so a future listing's stale line is read correctly on sight. Note the separate FVRD short-term-rental zoning gate below — a strata's inability to ban long-term rentals does not make a short-term rental lawful on a lot whose zone does not permit it. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Lindell Beach strata is published as age-restricted on any source checked. The Cultus Lake Village park age conflict in §2 is a manufactured-home park matter, not a strata bylaw, and stays UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** The beach strip's cottages and custom lake homes are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; FVRD zoning plus any registered covenants govern instead. The three §2 communities are the exception — they ARE strata (bare-land strata), so strata fees and the Form B regime apply to them even though the homes are detached.
- **Regional plan:** the plan is **OCP Bylaw No. 1115, 2011 for Electoral Areas E and H** — the FVRD titles it "Columbia Valley, Lindell Beach and Chilliwack River Valley," so Lindell Beach is covered by name — as amended by Bylaw No. 1533, 2019. Under its designations the only residential use permitted is single-family; its resort-residential policies (§5.9) calculate permitted sites/strata lots/units on "useable land." These are FVRD bylaw numbers documented in this area's own research file; **no Chilliwack bylaw applies here, and any other FVRD bylaw number is UNVERIFIED — never borrow a Chilliwack number for this ground.** https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/government/bylaws/planning-land-use.html and https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11249
- **Regional zoning:** outside the Park, **Consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 1638, 2021** (approved Oct. 27, 2022, consolidating nine earlier bylaws); Bylaw No. 1375, 2016 applies only inside Cultus Lake Park and does NOT govern Lindell Beach. One zone is confirmed from a primary/developer source: PRD-1 — Private Resort Residential Development, full-time occupancy, no usage restrictions (the §2 resort communities' zone). Zone and minimum parcel size for the older beach-strip lots: UNVERIFIED — check per address in Bylaw 1638 / the FVRD webmap. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/consolidated-zoning-bylaw-approved.html?media=contrast and https://creeksidemills.com/faq
- **Short-term rentals:** control is via zoning — Bylaw 1638 excludes "Temporary Tourist Accommodation" from Residential use except where a zone expressly permits it, and the FVRD Board specifically enabled Electoral Area H to proactively address short-term vacation rentals and RV use for accommodation. Provincially, hosts needed a registration number by May 1, 2025; electoral areas are exempt from the principal-residence rule unless the board opts in (none found for Area H; UNVERIFIED). A seller's Airbnb history is not proof a buyer can continue it. https://pub-fvrd.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=22845 and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0032-000389
- **Flood / alluvial fans:** Floodplain Management Bylaw No. 1669, 2022 applies region-wide except municipalities and Park lands — so it applies here — and requires alluvial-fan development to meet the Flood Construction Level/setback set by a site-specific geohazard assessment by a Professional Engineer/Geoscientist. The FVRD's Frosst Creek works protect the community on the Frosst Creek alluvial fan at the lake's south end: two debris basins and two dikes. Property-specific hazard reports: UNVERIFIED. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Floodplain%20Management%20Bylaw%20No.%201669.pdf and https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/main/services/flood-protection.html?media=contrast
- **Rural property tax penalty — the regime that applies here:** under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, rural taxes are due and payable on **July 2** of the year levied, with a **5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on October 31** (s. 11). Lindell Beach properties pay provincial rural taxes plus FVRD service parcel taxes for water and sewer where connected. **This staged 5% + 5% rural regime — not any city's penalty schedule — is the one that applies to this unincorporated ground.** https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Manufactured homes and pad rent (MHPTA frame):** the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act limits pad-rent increases in manufactured home parks (2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount — no pad-rent dollar figure is published in this file, ever). The regime is relevant here only to the Holiday Resort's leased pads and to park product elsewhere at the lake; it does not apply to a home on land the owner also owns.
- **ALR frame:** much of the surrounding Columbia Valley is agricultural while the beach strip is cottage/residential; ALR status is per-parcel — check the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder. Where a parcel IS in the ALR: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239 — a deposit under s. 239 is a subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC caps are a principal residence of 500 m² or less, a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha), with anything beyond needing ALC approval. Farm-classification thresholds, where a farm parcel is in issue, run in the full three-tier form (0.8–4 ha: $2,500 of farm income; over 4 ha: $2,500 plus 5% of the value over 4 ha; under 0.8 ha: $10,000; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever a page states it; no such line is documented in this slug's own research and none is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. The FVRD has specifically enabled Area H enforcement on STRs and RV accommodation. (§3 above)
- **Q: What is the tax penalty if I miss the July 2 deadline?** The rural regime: 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31, under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s. 11 — plus FVRD service parcel taxes for water/sewer participants on the same bill. No city penalty schedule applies here. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve Lindell Beach?** Cultus Lake Community School (71 Sunnyside Blvd, SD33) is the lake's elementary school; SD33 groups the lake area as Cultus Lake Elementary → Mount Slesse Middle → G.W. Graham Secondary. Catchments are address-based — confirm the specific address in SD33's locator. (§1 above)
- **Q: Is the lot in a hazard area?** The south end sits on alluvial fans (Frosst Creek, Elbow Creek). FVRD Floodplain Bylaw 1669 requires a site-specific geohazard assessment by a Professional Engineer/Geoscientist setting the Flood Construction Level and setbacks for fan development — pull that file on any fan lot before subject removal. (§3 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mailing address lies about the government.** "Chilliwack, BC" is a postal address only — permits, zoning enforcement, water and sewer all run through the FVRD in Electoral Area H, and a buyer who calls Chilliwack City Hall about a Lindell Beach lot will be redirected. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lindell-beach.md)
- **The two shores of the same lake run on opposite tenures.** North-shore Cultus Lake Park homes sit on leased Park Board land; Lindell Beach, at the south end, is predominantly freehold — except its named resort, where you own a trailer on a leased pad. Three tenure models within one lake, and each property's title has to be read on its own. (§2 above)
- **South-shore water and sewer send no utility bill.** FVRD Area H rates fund Cultus Lake water and South Cultus Lake sewer through property taxes ("No Utility Billing / Property Taxes: Yes"), and the South Cultus sewer parcel tax is split equally across every parcel on the roll — while the lake's north sewer carries a $1,369.00 flat rate for 2026. Same lake, different billing model. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Services/Documents/Utility~Billing/Electoral Area H - Residential Rates 2026.pdf
- **The lake's sewage system is mid-upgrade, north side first.** The FVRD's North Cultus wastewater plant was commissioned in April 2025 (construction from July 2023) under the FVRD Liquid Waste Management Plan, for Class A effluent plus phosphorus removal; 2025 was its first operational year, north side only. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2025/north-cultus-wastewater-treatment-plant-comes-online.html
- **One road in, one road out.** Columbia Valley Road is the community's only access — summer lake traffic, emergencies and winter conditions are part of the ownership picture, and there is no transit alternative: no bus serves the south shore, and Route 56 reaches only the north-end Marina, in summer. (§1 above)
- **Area H residents vote for Chilliwack school trustees.** Although the FVRD governs the land, Area H elects SD33 (Chilliwack) school trustees — the school connection is the one civic tie to Chilliwack that is real. https://www.cultuslake.bc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Area-H-Update-October-1-2026-edit-two.pdf
- **A fresh townhouse search for this ground returns detached houses.** The November 2, 2026 verification search for Lindell Beach townhouse/strata product surfaced Aquadel Crossing and Creekside Mills listings — both filed by their own sources as Single Family Residence / detached houses on strata lots. A buyer who stops at the search result will think the lake has townhouse complexes; the form on the ground is bare-land strata cottages. (§2 above)
