## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted complexes in the count home).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). Neither counted complex is age-restricted on any source read; The Greens at Douglas is the sub-area's only published 55+ complex, and it is queued, not counted (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Pacific Douglas (§2.2).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and STRs — standing frame applied.**

- **OCP — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction:** Surrey's Official Community Plan is **Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026; REPLACES Bylaw No. 18020 — 18020 is NEVER cited as current). The Douglas NCP (1999) designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; zoning is under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 — a listing quoting a 1999 NCP designation (as the 148–192 172 Street assembly cards quote "Commercial/Residential") is quoting history, and the current designation for those parcels is UNVERIFIED in this file's research (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp).
- **Secondary suites (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, and its habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000; a suite is not a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey frame):** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites).
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1, per research file §4) versus the City's 2024 corporate report's proposed $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and check the City licence page before quoting either figure (research file §4).
- **ALR edge:** the plan area is bordered by the ALR to the north; an ALR parcel follows the ALC frame of one principal residence at a maximum of 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). Parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. CRA's wording for the phase-out is "gradually reduced" — a straight-line mechanism is UNVERIFIED. GST applies to new construction (Harlowe), not resales (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA, same source).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. Worked anchors: $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000 (plus the 2026–2028 repayment-relief deferral); FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). **No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug** — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the pacific-douglas-guide page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Pacific Douglas is in Surrey.
