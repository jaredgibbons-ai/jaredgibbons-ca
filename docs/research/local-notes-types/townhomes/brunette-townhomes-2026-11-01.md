## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no verified townhouse stock in Brunette for these rules to attach to today; they are the rules that govern any townhouse-form purchase a buyer is shown under this name — most realistically the Coquitlam-side Brunette Villas (Coquitlam rules then apply — see the harbour-chines file's Section 3 for the Coquitlam block) — and any future infill on the New Westminster side.

- **Form B diligence (strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots sit UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry: Brunette Villas' published "rentals not allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals under this rule (the complex is excluded from this file on geography; the voiding applies wherever it is counted). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Brunette complex is published as age-restricted — there is no verified in-area complex to publish one against. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes (none verified here):** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. Brunette's verified housing is RS detached, which sits in the same no-strata world. Basis: the tenure definitions applied across this program.
- **City zoning and plan — corrected districts (standing position):** Zoning Bylaw **No. 6680, 2001**; OCP **Our City 2041, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017**. Townhouse districts are **RT-2–RT-2C (ss. 410–413)** and **RT-3/3A/3B in Queensborough (ss. 417–419)** only — "RT-A/RT-B" in the research file is an error, corrected at the top of this file. RT-2's published form figures: base density 18 units per net acre or FSR 0.6, height cap 25 feet, site coverage cap 40%. RS-1 (the sub-area's house zone): minimum site 557.40 m² (6,000 sq ft), FSR 0.5. https://www.newwestcity.ca/zoning-bylaw/sb_expander_articles/1618.php/articles/5682.php and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Zoning_Bylaw_6680_2001%20(consolidated%20June%208,%202026).pdf
- **Suites as the City states them:** one secondary suite per house; the City allows a property to have both a secondary suite and a laneway/carriage house (Zoning Bylaw s. 190.29 — a provision title-search and advocate sources quote; confirm the current section text in the consolidated bylaw before relying on it). Adaptable housing: at least 40% of single-storey units in multiple-unit buildings must be adaptable (Adaptable Housing Bylaw 7464, 2011). https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/development/planning-and-development/planning/secondary-suites-and-laneway-carriage-houses
- **Floodplain — the corridor's defining diligence item:** the City's floodplain study places the industrial land along the Brunette River in **Floodplain Zone 4, below the Flood Construction Level** — the diligence question for any assembly, infill or insurance conversation on the corridor. (Zone 3 belongs to the Quay and Downtown NW files, not this one.) https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/Flood%20Plain_Study_Report%20_web%20accessible.pdf
- **Property tax:** City of New Westminster taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 3, 2026. The City also runs its own electrical utility — a New Westminster–specific line item on any closing here. There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in New Westminster — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/taxes-and-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
