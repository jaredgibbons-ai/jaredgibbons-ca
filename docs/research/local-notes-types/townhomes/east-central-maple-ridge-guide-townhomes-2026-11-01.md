# East Central, Maple Ridge — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

**Classification note, stated at the top:** the research file for this sub-area confirms NO named townhome complex on an official source — the townhome roster below comes from the sibling condos and 55-plus files on disk and from current listings, verified by name, form and tenure in this pass. Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses in this program: Fraser Glen and Dogwood Lane are marketed inside 55+ communities, but no Form B or registered-bylaw source verified in this pass establishes a 55+ strata-townhouse bylaw for either, so neither is counted or sold here as verified 55+ stock. ## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "East Central" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only; it is not a City of Maple Ridge plan or neighbourhood name, and no City plan named "East Central" exists — the downtown part is covered by the City's Town Centre Area Plan. The City publishes no boundary for the MLS sub-area; the wider sub-area takes in the residential streets east and north of downtown toward 232 Street and 124 Avenue. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- Main roads: Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) along the south side; Dewdney Trunk Road east–west through downtown; 224 Street and 226 Street north–south through the core; the Haney Bypass links Lougheed Highway to Port Haney at the southwest corner. https://www.mapleridge.ca/art-parks-rec/recreation-facilities/memorial-peace-park
- Where the townhouse stock actually sits: along the 116 Avenue corridor south of downtown — Fraser Glen (22740), Dogwood Lane (22555) and Duet (22977) — plus Gold Creek Estates on 228 Street and Alouette Estates at the east edge on 232 Street. The apartment stock concentrates in the Town Centre along 224, 226 and 227 Streets and 121 Avenue. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- Transit: Haney Place Exchange is in this sub-area (routes include the R3, 701, 733, 741, 743, 744, 745, 746, 748, 749 and 791); the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs from Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place; Port Haney Station, 22300 River Road, runs weekday West Coast Express trains between Waterfront and Mission City. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- Schools physically inside the sub-area: Golden Ears Elementary (K–7), 23124 118 Avenue; Harry Hooge Elementary (K–7), 12280 230 Street; Thomas Haney Secondary (7–12), 23000 116 Avenue; ci:tməxw Environmental Community (K–8), 23125 116 Avenue; District 42 Alternative Secondary (9–12), 23125 116 Avenue. Catchments are address-based in SD42 — for the downtown sample address 11897 227 Street, the locator gives Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042 and https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Market stats (SnapStats covered — Maple Ridge): East Central carried 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings, 11 sold, 9% sales ratio in September 2026 — the largest attached inventory of any Maple Ridge sub-area. That figure COMBINES condos and townhomes; SnapStats publishes no townhome-only figure. Maple Ridge community-level Condos & Townhomes median was $525,000 in September 2026; no sub-area median is published. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. This is the document that settles Fraser Glen's and Dogwood Lane's actual age/pet bylaws — the directory lines are not the bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. The 1990–1996 stock here (Dogwood Lane, Fraser Glen, Alouette Estates if strata) is exactly the age band where roof, piping and envelope work shows up in those reports. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022 no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Fraser Glen (and for Rosewood Court in the apartment stock) is void in-entry — flag it wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners, defined children). Program position for this city, stated plainly: Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses — a listing calling Fraser Glen or Dogwood Lane "55+" is marketing until the registered bylaw is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified here. A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. If Alouette Estates proves fee-simple on title, its diligence is title-and-municipal only; until then its tenure is UNCONFIRMED and neither frame may be quoted for it.
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs; houses sit under RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum coverage 40%), and Town Centre parcels carry Town Centre commercial, multi-family and comprehensive development zones under the same bylaw. One secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not within the floodplain area; eligible lots may carry up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, never resolved:** the research and sibling files cite the Maple Ridge Official Community Plan as Bylaw 7060-2014 in some sources and Bylaw 7998-2024 in others, with an update adopted November 25, 2025. Neither number is asserted as settled in this file; quote planning policy from a current City document for the specific parcel. The Town Centre Area Plan (OCP s. 10.4) covers the west end; its update expanded boundaries to incorporate River Bend, consolidated designations and added a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher densities around the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station; Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers those two TOAs. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026:** Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted at the Jan 27, 2026 council meeting (public hearing Jan 20, 2026; City news release Feb 4, 2026); the corridor runs from the City's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road. Cite it as adopted. An earlier contrary reading of this plan's status exists in program material — see Section 5. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Albion Area Plan:** adopted Oct 28, 2025 as part of the City's fall 2025 OCP/area-plan update series (Town Centre, Hammond, Silver Valley and Albion) — Albion is east of this sub-area, but the same update series is the planning context for East Central's Town Centre changes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-advances-housing-strategy-completion-area-plan-updates
- **Flood and escarpment:** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), no habitable floor space may sit below it on affected lots, a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties; the Fraser River Escarpment policy area's east boundary is 224 Street, and lots within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. The 116 Avenue townhouse corridor sits north of the river flats, but status is per-lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Presales (REDMA):** where any townhome unit is bought as a presale, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); the FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); the New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000); the Home Buyers' Plan ($60,000); the FHSA ($40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year); CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **Page-claims and contact check:** this area's research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. Source: the research file, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are the Fraserview Village buildings townhouses?** No. Rosewood Court, Emerald Manor, The Willows and Castlemount are apartment-form strata buildings sharing one amenity building; the townhouses near them are separate complexes — Fraser Glen and Dogwood Lane on 116 Avenue. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **Q: Is Fraser Glen really 55+?** That is UNCONFIRMED as a bylaw. The directory records "age restrictions" and listings market a 55+ community, but Maple Ridge has zero verified 55+ strata townhouses in this program — read the registered bylaws and the Form B before relying on any age rule, in either direction. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/fraser-glen-22740-116-ave
- **Q: The directory says Fraser Glen bans rentals. Can I rent my unit out?** Yes for a long-term tenancy — every BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw has been void since Nov 24, 2022. A short-term rental bylaw can still bind, and any valid age bylaw still governs who may live there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What do I actually buy at Haney Pioneer Village?** Co-op shares and membership, not a strata lot: it is an 84-unit townhouse co-operative for seniors aged 60+ (not 55+), and the co-op's own documents — not the Strata Property Act exemptions — set its household rules. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Are pets allowed at the 116 Avenue townhouses?** Building by building: the directory records Fraser Glen as pets allowed with restrictions and Gold Creek Estates as pets allowed with restrictions; a listing for Alouette Estates states no pet height/weight restrictions. Pet bylaws remain enforceable — the registered bylaws for the specific complex govern. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/gold-creek-estates-12191-228-street
- **Q: How exposed am I to a special levy in a 1990s townhouse complex?** The depreciation report is the check: stratas with 5+ lots must have one every five years with a 30-year projection, and the CRF balance is on the Form B. Dogwood Lane (1990), Fraser Glen (1994) and, if strata, Alouette Estates (1996) sit in the age band where roof, piping and envelope items appear. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is Alouette Estates the same as the Alouette retirement park?** No — and the names cause real confusion. Alouette Estates (11870 232 Street) is a general-market townhouse complex built 1996, tenure UNCONFIRMED; the Alouette Retirement Mobile Home Park (12868 229 Street) is a 55+ pad-rental park where you own the home and rent the pad. Different tenure, different statute, different file. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3105915-24-11870-232-street-maple-ridge-british-columbia-v2x6s9-711888/brochure
- **Q: Does Podium include townhouses?** Yes — 5 townhome units inside the 125-unit Podium project (120 condos + 5 townhomes per the presale record), built 2024. They are part of that project's strata, not a separate townhouse complex. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/podium-maple-ridge-town-centre-presale-condos-and-townhomes-by-whitetail-homes/
- **Q: Which schools serve a 116 Avenue townhouse?** Catchments are address-based in SD42, which has no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12; Thomas Haney is listed 7–12). Schools physically in the sub-area include Golden Ears Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary, but the exact address must be run in the SD42 locator — the downtown sample address maps to Eric Langton Elementary and Thomas Haney Secondary. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I get downtown without a car?** Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) runs West Coast Express trains to Waterfront on weekdays, and the R3 RapidBus runs from Haney Place Exchange to Coquitlam Central Station. TransLink's Langley–Haney Place BRT (22 km, 10-minute peak service) is in detailed planning with timelines not determined — price on today's service. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The research file's townhome blank is itself a fact (2026-11-01):** the City-source research pass confirmed no named townhome complex in East Central at all — every name in Section 2 rests on directory/listing sources and the sibling files. That is why tenures and unit counts here carry UNCONFIRMED labels other sub-areas do not need. Source: the research file's UNVERIFIED list, read 2026-11-01.
- **The Lougheed Transit Corridor's status flipped inside this program's own material:** earlier program reading treated the corridor plan as not-yet-adopted; the City record is now explicit — Bylaw No. 7781-2021 received third reading and adoption on Jan 27, 2026, the corridor ends at 221 Street, and the City announced adoption on Feb 4, 2026. Any page or note still calling it proposed is stale. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Four separate strata corporations share one amenity building at Fraserview Village** — indoor pool, hot tub, clubhouse, guest suite — so two units a block apart can carry different pet bylaws and different CRF positions while sharing the same pool. Compare building by building, never by the village name. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/rosewood-court-22611-116-ave
- **The newest seniors stock downtown is not for sale, and the 55+ strata supply is 1990s stock:** the Town Centre's recent growth (Podium, Brickwater, Highpointe, the TOA framework under Bylaw No. 8001-2024) is general-market product. Source: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/east-central-maple-ridge-guide-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md, §5.
- **East Central's attached inventory cuts both ways (September 2026):** 120 active Condos & Townhomes listings at a 9% sales ratio means a townhouse seller here competes with every newer downtown condo as well as the other townhouse complexes — pricing discipline matters more than scarcity. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **The escarpment rules reach into East Central:** the policy area's east boundary is 224 Street and a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant is required within 300 m of the crest — relevant to additions and rebuilds on the slope-side streets, even though the existing complexes predate the policy. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
