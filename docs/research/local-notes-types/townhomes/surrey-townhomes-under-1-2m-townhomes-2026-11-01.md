### Worked band math — inputs stated, tiers shown

- **PTT on an $800,000 resale townhouse, no exemption (inputs: price $800,000, resale, no exemption claimed):** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $600,000 = $12,000, total **$14,000**. A purchase anywhere under $1,200,000 meets only those two tiers — the 3% tier starts above $2,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT at the ceiling, $1,200,000, no exemption:** 1% × $200,000 = $2,000, plus 2% × $1,000,000 = $20,000, total **$22,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time buyer effect at $800,000 (inputs: qualifying first-time buyer, fair market value $800,000 — inside the $835,000 full-exemption value limit):** the program exempts the tax on the first $500,000 — that slice would otherwise cost 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = $8,000, the Province's stated maximum saving — leaving PTT of $14,000 − $8,000 = **$6,000**. Above $860,000 the exemption is gone on price alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Minimum insured down payment at $700,000 (inputs: price $700,000, down payment under 20%, insured mortgage):** 5% × $500,000 = $25,000, plus 10% × $200,000 = $20,000, total **$45,000**, plus the CMHC premium (0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount) where insurance applies. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (every strata complex cross-referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. That is why the "Rentals Not Allowed" line in Glenwood Village's MLS building data (§2) is flagged in-entry: any long-term rental-restriction component is void. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Glenwood Village's 55+ status is the band-relevant example in this file's feed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. The complexes cross-referenced in §2 are strata on their verified records; a listing calling one of them "fee simple" (as opposed to "freehold strata," which is still strata) contradicts the verified record and needs a title check. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/west-newton-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's framework (in force since **July 8, 2024** in the program record) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home; tenants cannot operate them. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it to a client. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 — city-wide figures, not band-specific. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math in §2 governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (so a qualifying new townhome between $1,150,000 and this page's $1,200,000 ceiling is outside it on price alone). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No CMHC premium-range defect is asserted for this page — this slug's own research does not document one (it carries CMHC's 0.6%–4.5% range itself). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
