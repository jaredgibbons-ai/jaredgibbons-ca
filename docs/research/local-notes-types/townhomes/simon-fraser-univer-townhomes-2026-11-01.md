## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The ground lease is the governing document (this area's first rule):** SFU Community Trust leases parcels to developers on 99-year leases and the land remains SFU's. Before anything else, confirm the lease commencement date, remaining term, whether rent is prepaid for the full term, and any rent-review or end-of-term provisions — the lease, not the building, is what a buyer's lender and lawyer will scrutinize. Lender treatment of leasehold (some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term) is general practice guidance in the research file — UNVERIFIED against any specific lender. https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/
- **Form B diligence (leasehold strata):** a UniverCity strata is still a strata corporation under the Strata Property Act, so s. 59 applies — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Form B does not answer the ground-lease questions above — read both packages. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (no UniverCity strata checked is near that size — the point is stated for completeness of the frame). **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a UniverCity strata is VOID in-entry and is flagged as such wherever met; per-building rental rules beyond that remain a bylaws read, and occupancy demand on the mountain is heavily student-driven (research file, flagged as general guidance). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 — younger spouse/partner, live-in caregiver, legacy-resident and defined-children exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+. The 55-plus sibling verifies **no** UniverCity building carries a sourced 55+ bylaw — general-market throughout. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** There is no fee-simple townhome purchase inside UniverCity: NO fee-simple title exists there at all. A fee-simple purchase (NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only) is only possible off the mountain, on the sub-area's edge pockets or in neighbouring sub-areas. Do not quote fee-simple expectations for a leasehold strata unit.
- **City plan and zoning:** citywide direction is the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** (adopted December 9, 2025); UniverCity is governed by its own OCP framework from the 1996 approvals (up to 4,536 units on about 65 hectares). Zoning is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026)** — the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) is superseded and is never cited as current in this file. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Burnaby:** the EHT is a City of Vancouver tax (3%) and does not apply here or in any Burnaby file — stated expressly because this sub-area's name invites the confusion in the other direction as well. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is its own regime and is not addressed by any figure in this file.
- **City property taxes (2026):** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (including an Arbour presale purchase), not resales. A worked value anchor exists at the community level only: the Burnaby Condos & Townhomes combined median was $686,000 in September 2026 (SnapStats) — it is a combined condos-and-townhomes community figure, **never a townhome-only or SFU-sub-area figure**, and no sub-area median is published. No CMHC defect is asserted for this slug — its own research does not document one, stated expressly; the known live-page pattern (a stated 2.80%–4.00% + 0.20% range) is NOT documented for this slug. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21) — the live regime for an Arbour purchase, stacked townhome or condo alike; the rescission clock does not change because the tenure is leasehold, but the disclosure statement's ground-lease terms are the document to read inside the 7 days. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are UniverCity townhomes freehold?** No — nothing in UniverCity is freehold. SFU Community Trust leases serviced parcels to developers through 99-year prepaid ground leases; you buy a leasehold interest in a strata unit (a current Origin listing states "Leasehold expires in 2109"). That is why this file counts zero townhome complexes: the form exists, the freehold tenure does not. (§2 above; https://univercity.ca/about-us/history/)
- **Q: So where are the townhouses on the mountain?** Inside the developments: Crescent Court (14 townhomes beside 185 apartments), Terraces at the Peak (townhouse units at 8980 University Crescent), Slopes Mews buildings (townhouse-form units at 9168/9228 Slopes Mews, 9888 University Crescent), and the Arbour presale's stacked townhomes (9688 University Crescent). All are leasehold strata units in developments the condos file counts — there is no standalone freehold townhouse complex to buy into. (§2 above)
- **Q: What should I read first on a UniverCity purchase — the Form B or the ground lease?** Both, in that order of risk: the Form B for the strata's finances and bylaws, and the ground lease for commencement date, remaining term, prepaid status and end-of-term provisions. The lease is what a lender and lawyer will scrutinize; some lenders cap mortgage terms against the remaining lease term (general guidance — UNVERIFIED per lender). (§3 above)
- **Q: Is The Ambassador an SFU townhouse option?** No on both counts: it is an apartment building (7321 Halifax Street), and it counts in the Montecito condos file only — MLS files it under Simon Fraser Univer., but its research geography is Montecito. (§2 above)
- **Q: How deep is the market I'm buying into?** Deep and competitive for sellers: 91 condos & townhomes listed in September 2026 with 6 sales (7% sales ratio, SnapStats) — many near-identical units in the same few buildings, so pricing and finish decide. Note that figure is the combined condos-and-townhomes category; SnapStats publishes no townhome-only figure for Burnaby. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I rent out a UniverCity unit to students?** Long-term rental-restriction bylaws are void province-wide since Nov 24, 2022, and the mountain's rental demand is heavily student-driven — but verify the specific strata's bylaws and the ground-lease documents building by building before pricing a unit as an investment (per-building rules UNVERIFIED in the sources checked). (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a UniverCity townhouse?** University Highlands Elementary sits inside the community and Burnaby Mountain Secondary is the on-mountain secondary — but the only SD41 locator run documented here (8800 Eastlake Drive) returned Cameron Elementary / Burnaby Mountain Secondary, so catchment follows the address range, not proximity. Other addresses: UNVERIFIED — run the address in the SD41 locator. (§1 above)
- **Q: Does the remaining lease term already show up in pricing between old and new buildings?** UNVERIFIED — the research file records this as a question for first-hand knowledge and no source checked publishes a term-versus-price comparison. Check each building's lease commencement on title instead of assuming (one current listing states a 2109 expiry for Origin). (§2 and §3 above)
- **Q: Should I pay for the gondola?** No. TransLink states the Burnaby Mountain Gondola is not yet approved or funded — it needs a future investment plan plus TransLink Board and Mayors' Council approval. Treat it as upside, not current transit; today's transit is the R5 RapidBus and the 145 to Production Way–University Station. (§1 above)
- **Q: Is there any 55+ or fee-simple product on the mountain?** Neither: the 55-plus sibling verifies zero 55+ stock of any tenure on Burnaby Mountain, and there are no fee-simple residential lots inside UniverCity. A buyer who needs either has to look off the mountain. (§2 above; ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/simon-fraser-univer-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
