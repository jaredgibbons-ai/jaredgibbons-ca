## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (stated in full for the contrast this topic turns on — the feed's typical product is NOT strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). A non-strata half duplex has NO Form B — that absence is the diligence difference, not a shortcut.
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, in strata — a non-strata half duplex has no strata rental bylaw at all; municipal rules and registered covenants govern instead. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by strata bylaw where a strata exists (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast — the operative case on this feed:** a fee-simple / undivided-interest half duplex carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA and, for an undivided interest, the co-ownership arrangement on title only. Title, not card text, settles which form a specific card is (§2.5).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the new zoning does NOT retroactively legalize suites built without permits (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites — the rule this page turns on (fresh check Nov 2, 2026 corroborates the City page):** a secondary suite is an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and is **not a separate strata lot**; only one suite per dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; each application is reviewed for legal restrictions such as Land Use Contracts and Building Schemes (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite). A "90 m²" cap is NOT current in Surrey and is not stated as a rule anywhere in this file (research file §4).
- **How a suite becomes legal:** meet the zoning conditions, hold the necessary building permits, meet the BC Building Code, and pay the secondary suite utility and service fees (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite). Site copy only, flagged as the page's own editorial: all suites must be registered with a Secondary Suite Declaration Form; the Secondary Suite Service Fee is stated as $893 per suite, with a $1,000 fee for each unregistered suite the City becomes aware of (research file §5; https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-duplexes-with-suites-for-sale.html).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, the City's 2024 corporate report figure of **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting.
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (research file §4, Surrey frame; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST applies to new construction, not resales (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA, same source).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met; no such row is documented in this file's own research. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Is a South Surrey half duplex with a suite a townhome?**
A: No — not in this program and not on this page. The page's stock is "DUPXH 1/2 Duplex" under Residential Attached; a half duplex counts as townhouse stock only if strata townhouse-form is verified for a named complex, and no card here passes that test. This file counts 0 townhomes (§2).

**Q: What does "NO STRATA FEES" on a card actually mean for diligence?**
A: That the typical purchase on this feed is a non-strata, undivided-interest / fee-simple style interest — so there is no Form B, no strata bylaws, no depreciation report and no CRF. The diligence moves to title, the co-ownership arrangement, City suite registration and permit history (§§2.5, 3).

**Q: How big can a secondary suite be in a Surrey duplex?**
A: Its habitable floor area cannot exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, it must sit within the duplex building, and it cannot be a separate strata lot (§3; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite).

**Q: Can a Surrey half duplex have two secondary suites?**
A: No — only one secondary suite is permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, and a suite is not permitted inside a coach house or garden suite. A card advertising both a legal suite and a second guest suite needs the second unit's status explained in writing before subjects come off (§3).

**Q: The listing says the suite is "legal" — can I rely on that?**
A: No. The page itself warns a listing note is not proof of permits, Surrey's SSMUH zoning does not legalize an unpermitted suite retroactively, and no permit record for any specific card was opened in this research — the permitted status of any individual suite here is UNVERIFIED. Pull the City registration status and permit history (§§2.5, 3).
