# Panorama Ridge, Surrey — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B first.** The Form B Information Certificate discloses a unit's monthly strata fees, any amount the owner owes, any approved special levy still to be paid, the contingency reserve fund (CRF) balance, parking/storage allocations and a summary of the strata's insurance; the strata must provide it within 7 days of a request, for a maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle.** Since July 1, 2024, every BC strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, projecting 30 years of repair and replacement costs with at least three CRF cash-flow funding models; it cannot be deferred by a vote, and stratas of four or fewer lots are exempt. This is the central document for The Uplands (2005) and the Lakewood complexes (c. 1990–91). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **CRF minimum 10%.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata's annual budget must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the contingency reserve fund — compare the Form B's CRF balance against both that floor and the depreciation report's near-term items. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void.** As of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — all existing ones are invalid, so any directory line saying "rentals not allowed" for a Panorama Ridge complex (older directory entries for the Lakewood complexes record rentals with restrictions) is void for long-term rentals. Stratas may still ban or limit short-term rentals by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws: 55+ is the only legal form.** Under the Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1 and 123.2, a strata may restrict residents to 55 or older; any bylaw set below 55 has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and the bylaw governs who may live in the unit, not who may own it, with exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and children in defined care cases. This is the live rule at Lakewood Heights and Lakewood Place — and only there among Section 2's complexes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly because buyers ask.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or building scheme govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Panorama Ridge (Section 2), so that frame applies to the sub-area's detached houses, not to any counted townhouse complex here. (Complex entries in Section 2 above.)
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000** governs South Newton, with R-series residential zones and RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook; the research file records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² at Part 26 Section F of the bylaw for this area. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **OCP: the current plan is the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026.** Bylaw No. 18020 is the old OCP and is never current. The South Newton NCP was rescinded by that update and is no longer in effect — land use now follows the OCP and Zoning Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **SSMUH: adopted July 8, 2024.** Surrey allows up to 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Relevant to the detached streets around the townhouse clusters, not to existing strata lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites: one per house, capped at 40%.** Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family/duplex dwelling, and the suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area; the City has also moved to permit lock-off suites in new townhouses in transit-oriented and frequent-bus-stop areas, which must be at least 30 m² and under 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area — a new-build feature to ask about, not a right in existing complexes. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **GST on new construction — the FTHB rebate.** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: an eligible first-time buyer gets 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out between $1 million and $1.5 million, for agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — the older New Housing Rebate.** Any new-home buyer may claim 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out from $350,000 to $450,000, with nothing at $450,000 or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax (PTT).** BC's First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 of value (maximum $8,000 saved) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; the Newly Built Home Exemption gives full relief on a new principal-residence home up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. General PTT above the exemptions is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. https://markdrelich.com/buy-smart-in-bc-2026-guide/
- **Presales and assignments — REDMA.** No townhouse presale is verified in Panorama Ridge; the sub-area's presale is the Amson Bloc condo project. Where a presale does apply, the developer must give the disclosure statement, REDMA s. 21 gives 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it to rescind with the deposit refunded, and s. 18 requires deposits to be held in trust; assignment rights are contract-specific. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs.** Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 from an RRSP per buyer, repayable over 15 years. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime, usable together with the HBP for the same home. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans/what-home-buyers-plan.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- **CMHC premium range.** CMHC's homeowner schedule runs 0.60% of the loan at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment; a one-time premium that may be added to the loan. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Creek rules that reach strata lots.** The South Newton plan area includes fish-bearing creeks — Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries — so riparian setback rules can apply to creek-side properties, including lots in or beside the Colebrook-side complexes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Market context, as the research file states it.** Surrey IS covered by SnapStats: in September 2026 the report recorded Panorama Ridge attached inventory at 27 listings with 5 sales (19% sales ratio), and Surrey-wide attached medians at community level only. That attached figure combines condos and townhomes — SnapStats splits Detached versus "Condos & Townhomes" combined and publishes no townhome-only figure, so none is quoted here (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/panorama-ridge-guide.md).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many townhouse complexes are there in Panorama Ridge, really?** Six verified, all strata: Nora, Steller Panorama, The Uplands and Panorama Mews (general market), plus Lakewood Heights and Lakewood Place (55+) on W Boundary Drive. The only other attached product verified in the sub-area is one condo presale, Amson Bloc. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: A listing calls The Uplands a condo — is it?** No. The Uplands at 15152 62A Ave is 124 townhouses completed in 2005 by Polygon, with a clubhouse — a townhouse strata, with strata fees, bylaws and a depreciation report, but not an apartment building. https://www.condosurrey.com/buildings/view/31090/the-uplands/surrey/sullivan-station/15152-62a-ave
- **Q: Do both of us have to be 55 to live in a Lakewood complex?** At Lakewood Heights, listings state only one occupant needs to meet the age requirement; provincially, a younger spouse or partner of a resident who meets the age requirement (or holds a legacy exemption) is exempt in any case. Confirm the complex's own bylaw wording in the Form B package. https://sothebysrealty.ca/en/property/british-columbia/region-fraser-valley/surrey-real-estate/3076580/6211-w-boundary-drive-no13/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I rent out a Panorama Ridge townhouse?** Long-term rental bans are void in BC since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. In the two Lakewood complexes, a tenant must also satisfy the 55+ bylaw or an exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Nora's townhouse listings state pets allowed for that complex, and Lakewood Heights' recorded restrictions include pets allowed with restrictions. For Steller Panorama, The Uplands, Panorama Mews and Lakewood Place, no public source carried states a pet rule: UNCONFIRMED per complex — the registered bylaws govern. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r3159535-37-12830-62-avenue-surrey-bc-v3x-0k8 and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/lakewood-heights-6211-west-boundary-drive
- **Q: What should I worry about in the 2005 Uplands or the c. 1990 Lakewood complexes?** Levies and the depreciation report. Every strata of five or more lots must now renew its report every five years, with 30-year projections and three CRF funding models; at 20–35 years old, roofs, decks and envelope items dominate those reports. Read the current report and the CRF balance on the Form B before pricing any unit there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Is there a fee-simple townhouse option so I can avoid strata fees?** None verified in this sub-area. All six verified townhouse complexes are strata; the fee-simple product here is detached houses, which carry municipal rules and any building scheme instead of a strata — a different purchase, not a townhouse complex. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: Can my townhouse have a suite?** Not by right in an existing strata lot — the suite rules govern houses. Surrey caps a house's secondary suite at one, at no more than 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, and its newer lock-off suite permission applies to new townhouse construction in transit-oriented / frequent-bus-stop areas, at least 30 m² and under 40% of the principal unit. Inside a strata, the registered bylaws would also have to allow it. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Q: Which school will my kids actually attend?** The one the SD36 locator assigns to your address. The research file's locator run for 13220 64 Ave (Panorama Ridge Secondary's own address) returned North Ridge Elementary and École Panorama Ridge Secondary — proof that "closest school" and "assigned school" are not the same question here. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
- **Q: How is transit, really?** Bus-only. Routes 321, 322 and 324 work the 152 St / 132 St / 64 Ave grid to Newton Exchange and Surrey Central, and the R1 RapidBus runs on King George Boulevard west of the area. There is no SkyTrain station in or planned for Panorama Ridge on the funded Surrey–Langley line, which runs along Fraser Highway. https://www.translink.ca/-/media/translink/documents/schedules-and-maps/schedules-by-region/2026-schedules/april/surrey-p170.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The governing plan changed in 2026.** The South Newton NCP — the document that planned this area, including a City-endorsed update with density uplift along Colebrook Road — was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026). Anyone quoting South Newton NCP densities, or citing OCP Bylaw 18020 as current, for a Panorama Ridge property is quoting a repealed plan. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **All of the sub-area's 55+ townhouse stock is on one short drive — and the drive is split between two sub-areas.** Lakewood Place (6061) and Lakewood Heights (6211) on W Boundary Drive are Panorama Ridge's verified 55+ list, while Lakeview Estates at 6090 on the same drive is filed under West Newton. A buyer searching only one sub-area will miss part of the Boundary Park cluster. https://www.zolo.ca/surrey-real-estate/6090-w-boundary-drive/104
- **"Ideal place for seniors" in a listing is not an age bylaw.** Panorama Mews (5867 129 St) uses that phrase for a main-floor-layout townhouse, but no source states an age restriction for the complex — only a filed 55+ bylaw makes a complex 55+, and in this sub-area only the two Lakewood complexes carry one on the verified record. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2989114-18-5867-129-street-surrey-bc-v3x-0j4
- **The attached market here was the sub-area's strong segment in the last verified snapshot — and it is townhouse-driven.** In September 2026, SnapStats recorded Panorama Ridge attached inventory at 27 listings with 5 sales (a 19% sales ratio, its Surrey attached "Sellers Best Bet" that month) against a 7% Surrey-wide attached average. The figure combines condos and townhomes; no townhome-only figure exists in that source. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the area research file, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/panorama-ridge-guide.md.)
- **Highway 10 is the south edge, with the lowlands beyond it.** South-edge complexes like Steller Panorama (132 St & Highway 10) face highway exposure, and the Serpentine lowlands lie south of the highway — check exposure and the upland/floodplain line lot by lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Creeks run through the plan area, and they carry rules.** Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries are all named in the City's South Newton material, so a creek-side townhouse lot can carry riparian setbacks that limit decks, additions and rebuilds — invisible from a listing photo. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Surrey has opened a door most townhouse buyers have not heard of: lock-off suites in new townhouses.** The City's 2025 bylaw direction permits secondary (lock-off) suites in new townhouse construction in transit-oriented and frequent-bus-stop areas, minimum 30 m² and under 40% of the principal unit — relevant when comparing a future new complex against the resale complexes in Section 2, none of which carries that permission by right. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
