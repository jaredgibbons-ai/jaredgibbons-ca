## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex above, and every tower strata documented in Section 2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. The Springer Avenue complex's restrictions are UNCONFIRMED — the Form B and registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. The Springer Avenue strata's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so whether it sits over the five-lot threshold is UNCONFIRMED — a 4-lot-and-under strata sits UNDER the threshold; read the count off the Form B and strata plan. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No complex in Section 2 carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the sources read; if one surfaces in the Springer Avenue strata's older bylaw text, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No Brentwood Park townhouse complex is published as age-restricted, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ strata ownership in the sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Brentwood Park. If a fee-simple row home surfaces here, the rule is stated plainly: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, City of Burnaby rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning:** the governing bylaw is the **Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815** (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and the apartment districts R3–R8. https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **SSMUH (R1):** Burnaby's R1 district (consolidated June 2024) allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of 281 m² or more within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m, minimum lot area 281 m²; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; permitted forms include single-family, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. SSMUH speaks to the detached streets around the towers, not to the strata complexes themselves. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the **Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan** was adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and takes precedence over older community-plan maps — Brentwood is one of the town centres the 2050 OCP plans for continued high-density growth. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Presale protection (Oasis, Brentwood Block, Étoile Gold):** under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act (REDMA), a presale buyer may rescind within **7 days** after the later of signing the purchase agreement or receiving the disclosure statement (s. 21), and all deposits must promptly be placed in trust with a brokerage, lawyer, notary or prescribed trustee in a BC savings institution (s. 18). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Burnaby property taxes:** a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, and a further 5% after the September due date; the City states it has no authority to waive or modify these penalties. Burnaby's 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, and Council approved a 2.9% increase (about $61 on the average home) for 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-6/brentwood-park.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% per CMHC's published homeowner premium schedule. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example an Étoile Gold presale townhome), not resales. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — Brentwood Park is in Burnaby, and that tax is Vancouver-only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Is there actually a townhouse complex in Brentwood Park, or is it all towers?** One standalone complex is verified: the 1976 Springer Avenue strata townhouses (Section 2). The sub-area's 360 attached listings in September 2026 were overwhelmingly tower condos. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file; listing R3101237 (Section 2).
- **A listing says "Townhouse" at 2351 Beta Avenue — is that a townhouse complex?** No. That is Starling at Lumina, a 28-storey tower; the TH-numbered units are townhouse-form homes inside the tower's strata. Built form governs — you are buying into a high-rise strata with high-rise amenities and fees. Source: Section 2 entry.
- **What is the difference between Tower 1 and Tower ONE at the Amazing Brentwood?** Tower 1 (4510 Halifax Way) is strata ownership; Tower ONE (1820 Willingdon Ave) is SHAPE purpose-built rental. The names differ by one word; the tenure is opposite. Source: sibling condos file on disk.
- **The Springer Avenue complex is from 1976 — what should I check?** The Form B package: strata fees, CRF balance, the most recent depreciation report if the strata has five or more lots, and any approved special levies. A 1976 wood-frame strata's report and levy history matter more than its listing photos. Sources: Section 3 (Form B; depreciation reports).
- **Can I rent out a Brentwood townhouse?** In any strata here, a long-term rental-restriction bylaw is void since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Springer Avenue strata's registered bylaws are UNCONFIRMED until read on the Form B. Source: Section 3.
- **Are any Brentwood townhouses 55+?** None verified — the sibling 55-plus file verifies zero 55+ strata ownership in the sub-area, and only 55+ bylaws are lawful in BC in any case. Sources: sibling 55-plus file on disk; Section 3.
- **Does the new-home GST rebate apply to a presale townhome at Étoile Gold?** Potentially: the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of GST on a new home to $1M, phasing out to $1.5M, for agreements from March 20, 2025 to 2031 — a presale is new construction, so GST applies and the rebate is the offset to test with your tax advisor. Source: CRA News No. 122 (Section 3).
- **How long do I have to back out of a presale contract?** Seven days under REDMA s. 21, from the later of signing or receiving the disclosure statement, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. Source: Section 3.
- **Which SkyTrain stations serve the townhouse streets?** Brentwood Town Centre and Holdom, both Millennium Line, with the R2 rapid bus at Brentwood — the Springer Avenue complex sits in the established streets south of the town centre, not inside it. Source: research file (Section 1).
