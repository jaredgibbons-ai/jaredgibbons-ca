## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata townhome in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any directory "rentals not allowed / allowed w/restrictions" line for a complex in §2 is void in its restriction element for long-term rentals and is flagged void wherever met in its home file (Newton Court's line in the condos assembly is one carried example). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Surrey's municipal STR frame is below (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None counted here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and STRs — standing corrections applied.**

- **OCP — current bylaw:** Surrey's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 21900, "Surrey 2050," adopted June 15, 2026 — Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current.** Zoning is under **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **SSMUH:** Surrey's amendments took effect **July 8, 2024**: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the July 8, 2024 changes renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** one secondary suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. The often-quoted 90 m² cap is NOT current and is never stated as current (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites).
- **Short-term rentals:** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence, tenants cannot run one, and a City business licence is required first. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr (program figure; Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d) and Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1, per research file §4) versus the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and direct any buyer to the City licence page before quoting either figure.
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).
