## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Form B (strata townhouses).** SPA s.59: within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page — fees, owing, levies, CRF, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement, depreciation report (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation / CRF.** 5+ lots every 5 years since Jul 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot UNDER threshold); CRF ≥10% since Nov 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 — directory "rentals not allowed" lines in the condos roster (Parkway Estates, Parkway II) are flagged VOID in-entry there and apply the same way to any townhouse directory line. Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions).
- **Fee-simple contrast (Westbrooke model).** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules + registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.
- **Two municipalities, two rule sets.** **City of Langley Zoning = 2025 No. 3300** (cite number; adoption date conflicted in sources — UNCONFIRMED). City SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (adopted Jun 17, 2024) and City suite cap (lesser of 100 m² / 40% GFA) per condos sibling §3. **Township: Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH = Bylaw 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024 — NO 6-plex tier** (a page/research claim of a Township 6-plex tier is WRONG, flagged); Township suite cap **120 m²**; coach house = fee-simple s.102 unit, never strata (standing corrections; research file §4).
- **Taxes:** Township due Jul 2, 2026 — 5% Jul 2 midnight, further 5% Sep 3 (Brookswood townhomes file §3, Township frame); City tax specifics per City notices — confirm per municipality, as the feed mixes both.
- **FTHB GST rebate.** Bill C-4 assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; condos sibling mentions March 12 only as superseded — flagged, not carried): 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, Mar 20 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K (https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST on new construction, not resales.
- **PTT:** 1%/2%/3% + further 2% above $3M residential ("Over $3M — 5%" compression flagged); FTHB $500K/≤$835K→$860K; newly-built $1.1M/$1.15M. **Worked example (inputs stated, from condos sibling):** PTT on the combined attached median $611,000, no exemption = 1%×$200,000 ($2,000) + 2%×$411,000 ($8,220) = **$10,220** — the $611,000 input is the COMBINED Condos & Townhomes median, NOT a townhome-only price (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
