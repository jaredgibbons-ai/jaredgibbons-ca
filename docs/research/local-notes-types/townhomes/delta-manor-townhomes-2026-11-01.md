## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted complexes are strata, in the guide file):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote (Adlington Court, at 63 lots on the guide file's record, is squarely inside this rule); a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — neither counted complex is such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line is documented for a Delta Manor townhouse complex in this file's research — the rule is stated so that any such line met on a listing is flagged VOID in-entry wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither counted townhouse complex is verified as 55+; Laburnum Park Place's 55+ bylaw is lawful on the same rule, in bare-land strata form (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Delta Manor — a listing marketed "freehold, no strata" here needs its title checked to prove the non-strata claim (freehold strata is still strata).
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024 — NO lettered schedules; the Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.3 under the OLD Bylaw 3950. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1); whether any ALR land sits inside Delta Manor itself: UNVERIFIED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City zone sheets cite No. 7600, 2017 against an internal title "8400, 2025"). The urban Ladner ladder in the research: RS5 (minimum lot area for subdivision 550 m², or 460 m² outside the SFR designation), RS6 (390 m²), RS7 (335 m²), RD1 duplex (836 m²), RD4 duplex (504 m²), RS1 at 4,000 m² in lower-density areas; there is no RD5 zone; zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/delta-manor.md)
- **SSMUH / suites:** Delta Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplexes, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone lists a secondary suite as an accessory use to a single detached dwelling. Inside a strata townhouse complex, the strata bylaws sit on top of that permission. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Streamside and flood:** all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area (Appendix B of Bylaw 8400); Ladner is low land behind Delta's flood system — over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations, approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs (no such row is documented in this file's research). Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**.
