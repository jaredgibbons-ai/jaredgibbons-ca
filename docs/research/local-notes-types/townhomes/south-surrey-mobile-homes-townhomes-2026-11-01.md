## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

This topic's governing frame is the **Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act (MHPTA)** for pad-rental product, the **Manufactured Home Registry** for every manufactured-home sale, and — for the one bare-land strata community — the standard strata block. **No pad-rent dollar figure is published anywhere in this section or this file.**

**The MHPTA frame (pad-rental product — Breakaway Bays and any park home).**

- **Tenure.** In a manufactured home park, many tenants own their manufactured home and rent the site it sits on under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement; where both home and site are rented, a Residential Tenancy Agreement applies instead. Manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the MHPTA, and landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes).
- **Assignment on a sale.** Assignment of a site tenancy permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer when the home is sold and remains in the park. Under s. 28 of the MHPTA a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent; and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase (BC Government information sheet: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf).
- **Pad-rent increases — percentages only, never a dollar figure.** A park landlord may increase rent only with 3 full months' notice, only once every 12 months, and only up to the yearly limit — **2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027**. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park; further increases require a Residential Tenancy Branch application (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s. 42).** Where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).
- **Taxes on a home in a park.** A manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home).
- **Park rules vs strata rules — do not import.** The strata rental voiding (below) is a Strata Property Act change; it does not rewrite a park's rules. Whether a park home may be rented out, and what age or pet rules apply, is governed by the park rules and the site tenancy agreement — read them. A park's 55+ restriction is likewise a park rule under the MHPTA, not an SPA bylaw, and the SPA exemption list does not govern it (documentation in the condos sibling).

**Registration and certification (every manufactured-home sale).**

- **Registration is what makes a sale effective:** the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if registered; the buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary). Manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes; the CSA label gives the standard — modular homes are built under the A-277 standards, mobile homes under the Z-240 standards (BC Government, Manufactured Home Registry: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **Lost CSA label:** a home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring, and an electrical approval label is prepared that can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).
- **Moving a home:** a permit to move is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

**The strata block (applies ONLY to Sea Ridge Bays, the bare-land strata — and to any conventional strata townhouse a buyer reaches from this ground, counted in §2.3's home file).**

- **Form B diligence.** A strata buyer is entitled to an Information Certificate (Form B) under s. 59 of the Strata Property Act, provided within seven days of request; fee capped at $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It states the monthly strata fee, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget and financial statement, any Rental Disclosure Statement, and the current depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/strata-information-buyers-sellers; Strata Property Act, s. 59: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_04). A pad-rental park home has NO Form B, no CRF and no depreciation report — the contrast is the topic's core diligence point.
- **Depreciation reports.** Since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more strata lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; stratas with fewer than five lots sit UNDER that threshold (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/depreciation-reports).
- **Contingency reserve fund.** Since November 1, 2023, a strata must contribute a minimum of 10% of its annual operating budget to the CRF (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/finances).
- **Rentals (strata only).** Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID province-wide since November 24, 2022 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-regulations/changes-to-legislation). This voiding does NOT apply to park rules (see the MHPTA frame above).
- **Age (strata only).** Since November 24, 2022, the only enforceable strata age bylaw is 55+ (Strata Property Act, ss. 123.1–123.2: https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/98043_08; Regulation 7.01), with exemptions for spouses/partners, caregivers and legacy residents, expanded May 1, 2023 — the framework that applies at Sea Ridge Bays, subject to its registered bylaws. A 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+.

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Do I own the land when I buy a manufactured home in South Surrey?**
A: It depends on the community, and this page's feed holds both forms: in a manufactured home park you generally own the home and rent the site under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA, while in own-land communities you buy the land with the home. The 160 Street cards are park-pad product; the Cranley Drive and 20 Avenue cards state the buyer owns the land — at Sea Ridge Bays (20 Avenue) that ownership is bare-land strata title, and the Cranley Drive community's title form is UNVERIFIED (research file §7; §2.6).

**Q: Does the park have to approve me, and can it charge for that?**
A: Yes to approval, no to a fee: assignment of the site tenancy requires the landlord's prior written consent under s. 28 of the MHPTA (or consent deemed given under the regulations), the seller must first give you the written park rules and the relevant tenancy agreement terms, and the landlord cannot charge a fee for giving consent (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf).

**Q: How much can pad rent go up?**
A: Up to the yearly limit — 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice. The proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park; on assignment to a new owner the rent and the next increase date carry over unchanged. This file publishes the percentage limits only — never a pad-rent dollar figure (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).

**Q: What happens if a South Surrey park closes or is converted?**
A: Under s. 42 of the MHPTA, as the Province states it: the landlord must have all permits and approvals first, then give 12 months' notice and pay $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date; tenants may leave early on 10 days' written notice, may dispute within 15 days, and may in defined circumstances claim additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000; if the landlord does not proceed within a reasonable period, a further payment of the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent applies (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes).

**Q: Which South Surrey manufactured-home communities are actually 55+?**
A: One verifies, in strata form: Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave), a bare-land strata where you own the lot — listings state "Sea Ridge Bays is 55+" and record title as Freehold Strata. No pad-rent park in South Surrey verifies as 55+: Breakaway Bays' age status is in direct conflict between a park directory (55+) and its own MLS listings ("NO AGE RESTRICTION") — UNCONFIRMED, not counted — and the Cranley Drive community's 55+ claim is listing text only, UNVERIFIED. Surrey's three verified 55+ parks are in Fleetwood and Newton (§2.3, §2.6).

The research pass could not confirm any of them on a City, developer, strata or park-operator source — the park names and operator attribution for 1840 160 Street, the Cranley Drive covenant claim, the fee figures inside listing cards and the two zoning claims are all UNVERIFIED in this file. Pull the park rules, the tenancy agreement and the title documents for the specific home before writing (research file §§4, 6).

**Q: Is there a Form B when I buy a park home?**
A: No — a pad-rental park home has no strata corporation, so there is no Form B, no contingency reserve fund and no depreciation report; your diligence documents are the park rules, the site tenancy agreement and the Manufactured Home Registry record. The exception on this very page is Sea Ridge Bays: as a bare-land strata it closes on strata documents including a Form B (§3).

**Q: What is the CSA label, and why does it gate a sale?**
A: The label certifies the standard the home was built to — Z-240 for a mobile home, A-277 for a modular. A home without its original legible CSA decal cannot be sold until it is re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection route, so label status separates readily saleable product from fixer product (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry).

It is also the topic's only verified 55+ community (§2.6).
- **The live page's guide is ahead of its sourcing.** The page already names parks, an operator, an age covenant and two zoning claims that this research could not confirm on any official or operator source — every one of them is carried UNVERIFIED here, and the rebuild should source each from the bylaw text or the operator before keeping it (research file §§1, 4).
- **Assignment does not reset the rent clock.** On a park sale, the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same and the scheduled date of the next increase does not move — a buyer inherits the park's increase calendar, not a fresh one (§3).
- **A park home is taxed without the land.** The Province assesses a manufactured home in a park like other residential property, but the assessed value excludes the land — one reason a park home's tax bill looks nothing like the own-land home's down the same corridor (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home).
- **Certification is a value gate.** A missing or illegible CSA label does not just delay a sale — the home legally cannot be sold until Technical Safety BC re-certification, which is why two otherwise similar homes on this feed can sit in different market positions (§3).
- **Surrey's 55+ parks are not in South Surrey.** All three of Surrey's verified 55+ pad-rental parks (Westwood Court, Westwood Estates, Newton MHP) sit in Fleetwood/Newton — a buyer searching this page for a verified 55+ park in South Surrey will not find one; the verified South Surrey 55+ manufactured-home option is a bare-land strata instead (§2.3).
