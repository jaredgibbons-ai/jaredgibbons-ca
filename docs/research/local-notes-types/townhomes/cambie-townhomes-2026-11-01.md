## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level lines in Section 2 (for example Aperture's pets line) are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — both counted complexes are over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line for a Section 2 complex describing long-term rentals as not allowed or capped is void to that extent — flag it in-entry wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither counted complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read. (The corridor's published 55+ building is OPAL, South Cambie stock, "One resident must be 55+" per a current listing — counted and detailed in the South Cambie files, not here.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here, stated plainly so the rule is not misapplied — a fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees, with diligence limited to title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and registered covenants/HOA only. The corridor's side-street houses, duplexes and R1-1 multiplexes are fee-simple houses, not fee-simple townhouse complexes, and are not counted.
- **City plan and zoning:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved by Council May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) guides growth along Cambie Street from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River around the Canada Line stations, including townhouse and rowhouse areas rezoned from the plan; zoning is Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — side streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units; 464 m² for 5 units; 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units), arterial sites on Cambie Street sit under the C-2 District Schedule, and station-area parcels are typically site-specific CD-1 districts. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** the City of Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed taxable value, and owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. This tax applies in this file because the corridor is in Vancouver — it never applies in Burnaby, Richmond, West Vancouver or Coquitlam files. Vancouver property taxes: 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Floodplain at the south foot:** the City's designated floodplain is a citywide dataset covering coastal and river floodplains, and the corridor's south foot runs down to the Fraser River at Marine Drive; whether a specific parcel near Marine Drive (including the Winona Park area) is in the designated floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (Oakridge Park presales), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — relevant to Oakridge Park and any remaining developer sales in the corridor. Assignment consent and fees are contract terms: UNCONFIRMED until each disclosure statement is read. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The corridor is a plan, not a neighbourhood (researched Nov 1, 2026).** The Cambie Corridor Plan was approved May 9, 2011, with Phase 3 in 2018 extending policy to the side streets within walking distance of the stations — two lots on the same block can carry different plan designations, and most corridor densities still require a rezoning application. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **Two different townhouse complexes share one street at the corridor's south end (checked Nov 2, 2026).** Winona Park Chateau (Formwerks, 344 West 62nd Avenue, 19 homes) and the Intracorp West 62nd Townhomes (238–276 West 62nd Avenue) both date from the same building wave, both face the Winona Park area, and listings file both under Marpole — a buyer comparing "West 62nd townhouses" can easily price one complex off the other's sales. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Aperture's townhomes share no walls (checked Nov 2, 2026).** The current listing describes a detached concrete townhome with no shared walls, built to LEED Gold standards, steps from Oakridge Park — strata title with a detached-house physical form, which is why its strata fee and depreciation diligence still apply in full despite the house-like product. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/759-w-41st-ave-101586
- **Cambie Gardens sits on the Pearson Dogwood site (checked Nov 1, 2026).** Onni's master-planned community at Cambie and West 59th Avenue is a 25-acre site whose later phases may be purpose-built rentals rather than condos — the tenure of each new tower must be checked building by building, not assumed from Phase 1. (Sibling condos file on disk.)
- **Queen Elizabeth Park is the city's high point (researched Nov 1, 2026).** At 125 metres above sea level atop Little Mountain, the park explains the view premiums on the West 29th–35th Avenue buildings — and why the townhouse-form units inside Chelsea, Lina and The Charlotte trade on a park address rather than a station address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
- **The corridor's north end is a hospital district (researched Nov 1, 2026).** The City states the South Cambie portion is known for its medical facilities, including BC Children's Hospital and BC Women's Hospital & Health Centre at 4480 Oak Street — a distinct tenant and buyer pool for King Edward–area homes, and the context for OPAL's retirement-community form at that node. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/cambie.md)
