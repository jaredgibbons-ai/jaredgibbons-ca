## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern any strata purchase a Garden Village buyer ends up making in the neighbouring Metrotown / Central Park stock, and because they are the rules a future stratified multiplex lot inside the subdivision will sell under. They do not create townhouse stock in Garden Village itself.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — directly relevant to a 3- or 4-unit stratified multiplex here. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Garden Village complex exists to flag, because no complex is verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No 55+ complex is verified in Garden Village (see the sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** Garden Village's post-war houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **Burnaby zoning:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — Patterson Station puts the sub-area's north-side lots closest to that 400 m test. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — check the 2050 map before assuming assembly or density value on a Kingsway-side lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (for example a brand-new stratified multiplex unit), not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale exists in this sub-area to apply it to today; the frame, if a stratified multiplex or a nearby presale is bought from a developer, is disclosure under REDMA s. 18 and a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, with deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhomes actually in Garden Village?** None verified. The research pass confirmed no named condo or townhome complex inside the sub-area on a developer or strata source, fresh checks in this pass found none either, and September 2026 recorded 0 attached listings and 0 attached solds here. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: The tower beside Central Park shows up in my search — is it Garden Village?** MLS sources file the Patterson Avenue towers under other sub-areas — a current 6188 Patterson Avenue listing records Sub Area Metrotown — so a "Garden Village townhouse" search result is usually a Metrotown or Central Park BS building at the area's edge, and apartment-form at that. Check the sub-area field on the listing, not the search portal's label. https://www.strawhomes.com/2201-6188-patterson-avenue-burnaby-bc-v5h-2n1/
- **Q: What does Burnaby attached housing cost, as a benchmark?** Burnaby-wide (SnapStats publishes community level only), Condos & Townhomes recorded a median sale price of $686,000 in September 2026, at $763/sqft with 36 days on market — a combined condos-and-townhomes figure, not townhome-only, and not a Garden Village figure. There is no Garden Village sub-area median. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: If I buy a stratified multiplex unit here, is it a townhouse for strata-law purposes?** It is a strata lot: under the R1 SSMUH district, lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, and a strata lot purchase carries the same Form B, depreciation report and CRF framework as a townhouse strata — but it is not a named townhouse complex, and this file counts none. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit I buy near Patterson Station?** For a long-term tenancy, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still prints "rentals not allowed." Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which SkyTrain station serves the area?** Patterson Station (Expo Line) at Patterson Avenue and Central Park serves the north side; Metrotown Station, the main bus exchange, is the next station east. The south-side streets are materially farther from Patterson than the north-side ones — measure the walk for the specific address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Garden Village address?** SD41 runs K–7 elementary and 8–12 secondary with no middle tier. Schools in or beside the sub-area include Windsor Elementary and Moscrop Secondary, with Burnaby South Secondary (5455 Rumble St) on the south side; the one locator run in the research file (for 5455 Rumble St) returned Nelson Elementary and Burnaby South Secondary. Catchments are address-based — run the exact address. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: Does a new-build multiplex purchase get the new GST rebate?** If it is new construction and the buyer qualifies as a first-time home buyer: the Bill C-4 rebate returns 100% of GST up to a $1 million price (maximum $50,000, agreements on/after March 20, 2025, before 2031), and BC's Newly Built Home Exemption removes PTT fully to $1,100,000. Resales carry no GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Can a Garden Village house lot add units?** Under Burnaby's R1 SSMUH District, a house lot may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and multi-unit lots may be stratified — which is why two similar 1950s houses here can be worth very different amounts to builders. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What is the Burnaby tax deadline a buyer should budget around?** 2026 taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date on outstanding current taxes. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "townhouse" market here is a boundary illusion.** Garden Village recorded zero attached listings in September 2026 while the towers visible from its streets are filed under Metrotown and Central Park BS — a buyer comparing "Garden Village townhomes" is comparing buildings in other sub-areas, under the same city rules but a different market record. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file, and https://www.strawhomes.com/2201-6188-patterson-avenue-burnaby-bc-v5h-2n1/
- **Burnaby rewrote its entire zoning bylaw in 2026.** The Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815) took effect July 1, 2026 and replaced the 1965 bylaw outright — any listing copy, appraisal or older guide citing Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) district names for this area is citing a repealed bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **The new OCP outranks the old neighbourhood plans.** The Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — check the 2050 map before assuming assembly or mid-rise value on a Kingsway-side lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **The subdivision is a single 1953 plan.** Heritage Burnaby records the Garden Village subdivision was undertaken in 1953 with a plan to build 500 modern homes on a 140-acre site — which is why the interior streets read as one planned post-war subdivision, and why there is no legacy townhouse stock inside it. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **District energy is planned for the neighbouring centres, not this subdivision.** Burnaby is creating a District Energy Utility to bring clean thermal energy from Metro Vancouver's Waste-to-Energy facility to homes and businesses, with service slated to be introduced in Metrotown and Edmonds, where almost half of Burnaby's residential growth over the next 20 years is expected. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
- **Central Park is the amenity, and it is at the edge, not the centre.** The City lists 12 tennis courts with lights and a practice wall at Central Park, immediately north-east of the sub-area — north-side addresses use it daily; Rumble Street–side addresses are a materially longer walk. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/garden-village.md)
