## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (both counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. Review also the Form F and the depreciation report before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Both counted complexes are well over the threshold; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER it. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (The Loop's developer material states exactly that pattern: rentals allowed, no short-term rentals). No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a complex in this file was documented in the research file; any such line found elsewhere for these complexes is void for long-term rentals and is flagged on that basis. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No source checked states a 55+ bylaw for The Loop or The Boroughs, and the sibling 55-plus file verifies ZERO 55+ complexes in Grandview Heights — neither counted complex is 55+ on any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast (stated plainly):** a fee-simple row home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. No fee-simple complex is counted in this file (Section 2). Basis: Section 2 above.
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — never cite Bylaw No. 18020 as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. The Grandview Heights GLUP (2005) is the area's planning frame, and where an older neighbourhood plan and the OCP conflict, the OCP is the current rule (Morgan Heights NCP rescinded through the OCP update). **SSMUH:** Surrey's framework took effect July 8, 2024 — the research file records the live page's summary of the R3 Urban Residential Zone (lots under 280 m² usually up to 3 units; lots from 280 m² to under 4,050 m² up to 4 units; eligible lots in Frequent Bus Stop Areas up to 6), but the City's zoning page itself was not re-read in that pass, so zone-level figures are carried as the page's summary, UNVERIFIED against the City page in that pass. **Suites:** Surrey's secondary-suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area; lock-off suites are allowed only in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones in transit-oriented and frequent-bus areas — not every complex qualifies, so a lock-off claim is confirmed against the complex's zone. **Short-term rentals:** permitted in Surrey for owners only — licence figure CONFLICT: the program figure is $358/yr while the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees; carry $358 with the $350 figure disclosed alongside and confirm on the City's licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments and (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.md)

- **The east side of the plan area is ALR land.** The Grandview Heights plan area includes Agricultural Land Reserve land in its east portion — parcel-level ALR status is checked in the Agricultural Land Commission's ALR Property and Map Finder before assuming a detached lot near the attached stock can be subdivided or rebuilt as expected. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **The planned Grandview Heights Community Park is UNVERIFIED as a status.** The live page summarises City plans for a community park beside the aquatic centre and Grandview Heights Secondary, but the City project page itself was not re-read in the research pass — treat the park's status as UNVERIFIED, and the Grandview Corners tenant list likewise (named on the live page, not re-read on a City or owner source). https://jaredgibbons.ca/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.html
