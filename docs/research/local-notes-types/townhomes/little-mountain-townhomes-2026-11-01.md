## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. Little Mountain Meadows' lot count is UNCONFIRMED in the records checked, so whether it sits over the 5-lot threshold is confirmed on its strata plan — a strata under five lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Little Mountain Meadows is recorded in listing text as having NO age restrictions — the opposite position; Braeside Estates' 55+ position is a bare-land strata matter, not a townhouse one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules plus any registered covenants or HOA documents govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified on this hill (Section 2) — most of the hill's housing is fee-simple detached houses, which are houses, not townhouse complexes.
- **City plan — Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500:** the current city-wide Official Community Plan is "Chilliwack 2050," Bylaw 2025, No. 5500. **Standing correction:** Sardis is Schedule B5 and the Agricultural Plan is Schedule B1 of that plan; a live citation of "Schedule D / Bylaw 5024" for Sardis is stale. This area's own research file does not document that stale citation on the Little Mountain page, so it is recorded here as a standing correction only, NOT asserted as a defect of this page. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 2020 No. 5000. The hillside residential zones are HR (Hillside Residential — minimum lot area 0.2 ha with community water, 0.4 ha otherwise, minimum width 25 m) and SHR (Suburban Hillside Residential — minimum lot area on a slope table from 360 m² to 990 m²). Which zone applies to a specific lot is per-parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Hillside DPAs — the central rule on this hill:** Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas) requires a Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional with each application, and Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all City lands with slopes of 20% or more; within a designated DPA a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction, addition or alteration, except where exemptions apply. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendments were made by Bylaw 5395 (June 18, 2024) — cited as the standing program correction; the research file does not state SSMUH unit counts for this hill, and none are asserted here. Note the provincial record grants Chilliwack an extension to Dec. 31, 2030 for part of the Proper neighbourhood and surrounding Hazel Park only — not for this hill. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2024hous0178-001534
- **City property taxes:** due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026; additional taxes are levied for drainage and dyking on top of the municipal rates. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Flood:** Floodplain Regulation Bylaw 2018, No. 4519 sets minimum building setbacks of 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal — relevant to base-of-hill and Hope River-side lots, not the hilltop. Parcel-level flood status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999
- **Taxes and rebates (buyer frame):** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" compresses that 3% + 2% structure and applies only to the residential portion; exemptions: first-time buyers (exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption (full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000). FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate: 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually a townhouse complex on Little Mountain?** One verified: Little Mountain Meadows, 9855 Quarry Road — a freehold strata townhouse complex (Strata Plan LMS1998). Everything else the research confirms on the hill is detached houses. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85223/FullPublic.pdf?i=2040367860
- **Q: Why does my search file it under Chilliwack Proper East?** Because the MLS board sub-area on its listing records is Chilliwack Proper East, while the hill's local name is Little Mountain — the classification conflict flagged at the top of this file. Search both labels or the complex name. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3052629-9855-quarry-road-120-chilliwack-bc-v2p-3m3/brochure
- **Q: Strata or fee-simple?** Strata. Title to Land is recorded as Freehold – Strata, so there are strata fees, bylaws, a Form B and a depreciation-report regime — "freehold strata" is not fee-simple ownership. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85223/FullPublic.pdf?i=2040367860
- **Q: Can I have pets?** Listing data records cats OK, dogs OK, number limit two, with restrictions — listing data only; the registered bylaws in the Form B package govern. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85223/FullPublic.pdf?i=2040367860
- **Q: Is it 55+?** No — listing text states the complex has NO age restrictions. The 55+ product on Quarry Road is Braeside Estates, a bare-land strata of detached ranchers, which is a different form and a different purchase. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3052629-9855-quarry-road-120-chilliwack-bc-v2p-3m3/brochure
- **Q: Was it built in 1995 or 1997?** UNCONFIRMED — the two listing records checked state different years (1995 and 1997). The strata plan and BC Assessment record settle it; do not quote either year as fact. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85223/FullPublic.pdf?i=2040367860
- **Q: Can I rent it out?** Long-term, yes in law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What hillside rules affect a renovation or addition?** DPA 2 requires a Geotechnical Report with each application and DPA 5 applies to any land with slopes of 20% or more — budget for the report and the permit before assuming a timeline. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Q: Which school will my kids attend?** Address-based in SD33 — Little Mountain Elementary is on the hill, but no catchment for a hill address was run in the research pass, so run the exact address in the SD33 locator rather than assuming. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
- **Q: Is there a bus on the hill?** The confirmed service is route 57 Broadway at the base, via Yale Rd at Menzies St — hilltop homes are a walk or drive from the stop. https://www.bctransit.com/chilliwack/schedules-and-maps/route-overview/?route=57
