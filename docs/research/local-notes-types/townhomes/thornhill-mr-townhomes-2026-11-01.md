## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 counted complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Rydge, first establish whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Rydge is over the threshold on its published count (61); The Terraces' and Terraces II's counts are UNCONFIRMED but both present as multi-unit strata. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the 3 is presented as such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No "rentals not allowed" line was found for the counted complexes; any such line met elsewhere for a Thornhill complex is flagged VOID in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted complex here is verified as 55+, and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses. Do not confuse this with Haney Pioneer Village elsewhere in Maple Ridge, which is a 60+ co-op, never a 55+ strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Thornhill (Section 2) — a listing calling a counted complex "fee simple" in the non-strata sense contradicts the verified record and needs a title check ("freehold strata" is strata title, not fee-simple townhousing).
- **City zoning and plans:** Maple Ridge **Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019** governs — its schedule lists the rural minimums that cover most of this hillside, in full three-tier farm form: A-1 2.0 ha, A-2 4.0 ha, A-3 8.0 ha, with RS-2 0.4 ha and RS-3 0.8 ha or 2.0 ha depending on the zone text (a flat "$2,500+" farm figure would be an error — the tiers govern). **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged, resolved nowhere** (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). The OCP's Urban Reserve designation identifies lands held for long-term future urban services and housing, and OCP policy states its development will not happen until specific triggers and milestones have been attained; the live page's specific Thornhill Urban Reserve figures (about 343 hectares / 848 acres, OCP s. 10.2.2, a population-over-100,000 trigger) were not confirmed in the OCP chapter read for the research file and are UNVERIFIED. The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres, per the City release) at this area's north side; it establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/official-community-plan-chapter-2 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/thornhill-mr.md)
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor:** the corridor plan area lies west of Thornhill (the corridor runs from the city's west boundary to 221 Street) and was **ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026** per the City record (City news release Feb 4, 2026; OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 given third reading and adoption Jan 27, 2026) — cited as adopted here; the earlier contrary/contested reading in the sibling condos file is noted in Section 5. It does not designate Thornhill parcels. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Suites and added units:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including A-1, A-2 and A-3, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of 40% of gross floor area, and not in the Floodplain Area; on ALR parcels the ALR Use Regulation also governs — one secondary suite in the principal residence (s. 31), and an additional residence only if the s. 32 conditions are met, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified (Section 2). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/thornhill-mr.md)

- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs, and no such row is documented in this file's research. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (relevant to any developer sale at Rydge, not to resales). Worked example, inputs stated: an $800,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction (any remaining developer sales at Rydge), not resales. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
