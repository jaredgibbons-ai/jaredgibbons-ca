## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial rules are stated here because they govern any strata purchase a Deer Lake buyer ends up making in a neighbouring sub-area, and because they are the rules sellers of the area's future stratified multiplex lots will sell under. They do not create townhouse stock in Deer Lake itself.

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt — the exemption matters here, because a small stratified multiplex on a Deer Lake R1 lot can fall under the five-lot line. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a Deer Lake complex exists to flag, because no complex is verified here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners and defined children). No 55+ complex is verified in Deer Lake (see the sibling 55-plus file). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** Deer Lake's houses are fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **Burnaby zoning:** the governing bylaw is the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) — never the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Plans and heritage:** the Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. Sperling Avenue fronting the park lies within the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct and listed properties carry heritage review — a constraint on the multiplex/stratification math for precinct lots specifically. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale exists in this sub-area to apply it to today; the frame, if a future multiplex is marketed off-plan, is disclosure under REDMA s. 18 and a 7-day rescission period under s. 21, with deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhomes in Deer Lake at all?** None verified, and no attached market activity: SnapStats recorded 0 condos & townhomes listings and 0 solds in the sub-area in September 2026, against 13 detached listings. A buyer set on townhouse living near the lake is shopping the neighbouring sub-areas, not this one. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Is Deer Lake Place or Upper Deer Lake the same thing?** No. In the SnapStats GVR report, Deer Lake, Deer Lake Place and Upper Deer Lake are three separate Burnaby sub-areas with separate inventory and solds — attached product filed under the other two names is not Deer Lake product. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: A listing near the lake calls itself a Deer Lake townhouse. Should I believe the label?** Check the MLS sub-area field, not the prose: townhouse listings that use "Deer Lake" lifestyle wording in this pass carried neighbouring sub-area labels (for example Burnaby Lake, Greentree Village) in their own area fields. The Deer Lake sub-area's own attached count is zero. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: Could a Deer Lake house lot become strata townhomes?** Potentially, under the citywide R1 SSMUH district: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified — but heritage-precinct and park-edge lots carry extra review, so eligibility is lot by lot under the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: If a small Deer Lake multiplex is stratified, does it need a depreciation report?** Only if it has five or more lots: since July 1, 2024 stratas with 5+ lots must obtain one every five years, and stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. A 3- or 4-unit strata here could be exempt — which makes the Form B and the strata's own financials the whole diligence picture. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata townhouse I buy near the lake?** For a long-term tenancy, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, even where a directory still prints "rentals not allowed." Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deer Lake address?** Buckingham Elementary (K–7), 6066 Buckingham Ave, sits inside the sub-area and Burnaby Central Secondary (8–12), 6011 Deer Lake Parkway, sits at its north side; SD41 has no middle-school tier. The locator run verified in the research file, for 6066 Buckingham Ave, returns exactly those two schools — catchments are address-based, so check every other address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: How do I price anything here with no attached sales?** From the wider market, over a longer window: the research file's own guidance is to build pricing from wider central-Burnaby and Buckingham Heights comparables, because the sub-area may record one detached sale in a month (1 sold on 13 listings in September 2026) and no attached sales at all. The Burnaby community attached median ($686,000, September 2026) is a city figure, not a Deer Lake comparable. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Does the heritage precinct affect a purchase on Sperling Avenue?** Yes — properties on the City's heritage register or inside the Deer Lake Park Heritage Precinct (for example the 1912 Woodward House at 5195 Sperling Avenue) carry heritage review, which bears on renovations, rebuilds and any multiplex plan. Check the register and the precinct status before pricing in changes. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **Q: What is the lifestyle draw if there is no townhouse stock?** The park itself: Deer Lake Park connects the lake trails to the Shadbolt Centre for the Arts, the Burnaby Art Gallery, Burnaby Village Museum and Hart House restaurant, and the City's 2026 events program brought Symphony and Opera in the Park (July 18–19) and the Burnaby Blues + Roots Festival (August 8) to the park — that cultural precinct, not a building amenity package, is the area's offer. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The boardwalk by the boat launch is closed for a full replacement.** The City closed that section of boardwalk from September 21, 2026, with completion scheduled by winter 2027 and a signed detour via Sperling Avenue and Deer Lake Drive (about five extra minutes of walking); a small section of the nearby picnic area is also closed during construction — a park-edge seller should disclose it before a buyer finds the fencing. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Burnaby's rulebook changed under this area twice in two years.** The R1 SSMUH district arrived in June 2024, the Burnaby 2050 OCP was adopted December 9, 2025 (replacing the 1998 OCP and overriding older community-plan maps where they conflict), and the Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 took effect July 1, 2026 — any Deer Lake document still citing the 1965 Zoning Bylaw No. 4742 or the 1998 OCP is two generations out of date. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **City Hall is the sub-area's north-west neighbour.** Burnaby City Hall is at 4949 Canada Way, just north-west of the sub-area — the civic precinct, Burnaby Hospital at 3935 Kincaid St to the west, and the Bob Prittie Metrotown library branch serving this quadrant are the working amenities; there is no enclosed mall or shopping centre inside the sub-area. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/deer-lake.md)
- **The heritage houses are working artist residences.** Baldwin House and Eagles Estate, heritage homes around the lake, are part of the Deer Lake Artist Residency program, a 10-minute walk from the Shadbolt Centre and the Burnaby Art Gallery — the precinct is a living cultural district, not just a register listing. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
- **Zero attached stock is a standing condition, not a slow month.** The research file characterizes the sub-area as almost entirely detached single-family housing with effectively all-freehold tenure (13 detached listings, 0 attached, September 2026) — a townhouse buyer should treat "Deer Lake" attached listings from any portal as a labelling problem to verify, every time. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **The Burnaby Art Gallery's niche is stated by the City itself:** the gallery in the park at the lake's north side is described by the City as the only public art museum in Canada dedicated to works of art on paper — part of why the precinct, not a strata amenity room, is what Deer Lake buyers actually pay for. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park
