## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to both counted complexes in the count home in full — each is a strata townhome complex — and to any Morgan Creek strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For the 1999–2003 counted complexes, the depreciation report is the central value document (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Both counted complexes' unit counts are UNCONFIRMED (§2.1), so their threshold positions are read off their strata plans, not off any published count. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — any "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line (including the directory lines carried in §2.3) is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). Neither counted complex carries a verified age bylaw; Sundance is expressly NOT 55+ on listing evidence (§2.1). The Carrington community's 55+ position is a queue question, not a count (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The sub-area's detached and half-duplex homes carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted on this ground (§2.2).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH and suites — standing corrections applied.**

- **Surrey OCP (standing correction):** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — cited as current; Bylaw No. 18020 is NEVER cited as current. **SSMUH:** adopted July 8, 2024 — maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area** under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000; a suite is capped at 40%, one per unit, and is not a separate strata lot. A "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000).
- **Surrey short-term rentals (standing correction):** permitted, owner-only; licence figure CONFLICT — **$358/yr** carried, with the City's 2024 corporate report's proposed **$350/yr + fees** disclosed; check the City licence page before quoting either figure.
- **Property taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2 (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. GST applies to new construction, not resales — Morgan Creek's counted stock is resale stock and carries no GST (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).

- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. **Worked anchors (general tiers):** $700,000 → **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**.
