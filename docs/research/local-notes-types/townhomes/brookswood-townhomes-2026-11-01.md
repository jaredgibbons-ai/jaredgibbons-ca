## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the Bromley presale strata on completion and to any Brookswood strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For a presale like Bromley, the disclosure statement and the eventual Form B are the diligence, not developer marketing (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Bromley's 26 homes put it over the threshold once complete. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — rentals are allowed for long-term tenancies anywhere in BC; any "rentals not allowed" line surfacing in park listing copy for the Brookswood parks is a park-rule statement under tenancy law, not a strata bylaw, and is not carried as a strata fact here. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Brookswood strata carries a verified age bylaw — the area's 55+ rules live in its parks, under park rules, not the SPA (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants/HOA only.
