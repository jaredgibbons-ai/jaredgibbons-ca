## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the count home's 7 strata complexes, in that file).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property — any directory "rentals not allowed" line (including Highgate Terrace's and Willow Park Estates', in the count home) is void in-entry for long-term rentals; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Willow Park Estates is Willoughby's verified 55+ townhouse complex (count home); Highgate Terrace's exact restricted age is UNCONFIRMED and is never stated as 55+ (§2.5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The Westbrooke row homes and the Brighton West presale are fee-simple: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — stated plainly (§§2.2, 2.4).

**Township of Langley zoning, plans, SSMUH and taxes — standing frame applied.**

The Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201 / 6202 / 6203 had **first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY**, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026. **The guide/research presentation of June 15, 2026 adoptions/repeals as done is therefore UNVERIFIED in this file — the standing correction governs over the research file's §4/§7 wording, and the file says so expressly.** What the update would do (replace the Willoughby Community Plan; create the Willoughby Transit Corridor Plan and the Gordon Neighbourhood Plan) is carried as the update's stated content, not as enacted law (research file §4; https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf).
