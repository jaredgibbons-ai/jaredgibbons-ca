## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Poplar is the Paragon sub-area south of Highway 1, around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road, west of the Marshall Road / McCallum Road area and east toward Clearbrook Road; the City's official demographic profile name for the same ground is "South Poplar." Exact Paragon boundary lines are not published in text form — boundaries are shown in the City's interactive WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Highway 1 runs along the north edge with interchanges at McCallum Road and Clearbrook Road; King Road and Huntingdon Road run east–west, Gladwin Road and Marshall Road run north–south, and Highway 11 (Sumas Way) to the east connects to Mission and the Huntingdon/Sumas border crossing. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock actually concentrates: two pockets only — the King Road corridor at the north edge beside the University of the Fraser Valley / Rogers Forum district (The Argyle, Kingsview Estates, Kings Crossing, and the College Park apartment cluster beside them), and the McCallum Road edge (Crown Point, and the stacked-townhome address in Section 2's queue). The research file states the same pattern: townhome complexes occur along King Road and around Windsor Street / Marshall Road. - Transit: BC Transit routes 3 (Clearbrook / Huntingdon), 5 (Clearbrook / Bourquin via Hospital, on Marshall Road at the east edge), 6 (Gladwin Road) and 12 (UFV / Bourquin via Jackson Street and McCallum Park and Ride). https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B — Information Certificate (Strata Property Act, s.59).** For the three strata complexes in Section 2, the Form B states the unit's monthly strata fee, approved special levies, the CRF balance, parking/storage and proceedings, and since April 1, 2023 must include a summary of the strata's insurance coverage. Kings Crossing buyers get the developer's disclosure statement instead while it is a presale (REDMA, below) — its tenure is UNCONFIRMED, so which document governs is itself a question to settle from that disclosure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report, renewed at least once every five years. For 1989–1990 complexes (Crown Point, Kingsview Estates), the report is where roof, envelope and plumbing timing shows up. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **CRF minimum.** Since November 1, 2023, AGMs must contribute at least 10% of the annual operating fund to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Rental bylaws VOID — directly relevant here, flagged in-entry.** Kingsview Estates' directory data still records "rentals not allowed," and Crown Point's sibling-file sources record rental wording of the same era — as of November 24, 2022 every residential rental-restriction bylaw in BC is invalid for long-term rentals, with no bylaw amendment needed; short-term rental restrictions remain possible. Do not rely on any directory rental line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ bylaws (ss.123.1/123.2).** Only 55+ age bylaws remain valid; exemptions (May 1, 2023 expansion) cover younger spouses/partners, caregivers and defined children, and the bylaw governs who may reside, not who may own. Crown Point and Kingsview Estates are the complexes here a source states are 55+; The Argyle is listing-stated as having no age restrictions, and Kings Crossing's developer source states none. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhouses — none verified here, stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus covenants/HOA obligations on title only. No Poplar complex in Section 2 may be described that way on current evidence; Kings Crossing is the open question, tenure UNCONFIRMED.
- **Abbotsford zoning / plan — correction applied.** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs; urban lots in Poplar are in the RS family (City reports state RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 420 m²), and southern rural lots fall under A1 (minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha). The CURRENT plan is Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025) — the research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" wording is STALE; apply Abbotsforward 2050 and flag the stale number. No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/poplar-guide.md, §1)
- **SSMUH / suites in Abbotsford — bylaw number UNCONFIRMED, never assert "3560-2024":** sibling files carry that number from secondary sources; this file does not assert it. Confirmed Abbotsford figures: Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings (criteria include a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m minimum frontage in City reports), the City's 2025 updates set the suite maximum (in most zones) at less than 50% of the principal unit's size, and coach/garden suites are capped at 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford ONLY). SSMUH affects Poplar's house lots, not its existing strata complexes. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **Tax timing:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **GST — FTHB rebate (new product like Kings Crossing).** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent on March 13, 2026: 100% of the GST back on a new home up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for eligible first-time buyers with agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. Follow March 13 over any sibling file stating March 12. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST — New Housing Rebate:** 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above (+2% on residential value over $3M); first-time buyer exemption — no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000 saving) up to $835,000, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, phasing out at $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales — REDMA (Kings Crossing).** A developer must deliver a disclosure statement; REDMA s.21 gives a 7-day rescission right from the later of signing or acknowledging receipt of it, and s.18 requires deposits to be held in trust. Assignment rights are whatever the contract grants. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC.** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; FHSA $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% of the mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Poplar's townhouses, really?** In the north-edge pockets: The Argyle, Kingsview Estates and Kings Crossing on/near King Road, and Crown Point on McCallum Road. The Huntingdon Road half of Poplar is houses and rural lots with no verified townhouse complex. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is this complex in Poplar or Central Abbotsford?** Both labels attach to the King Road / McCallum Road cluster in listing and directory data. This program counts each complex once (here, in Poplar) and cross-references it — if a Central Abbotsford page also names your complex, it is the same complex, not a second one. (Task brief for this run; sibling files as cited in Section 2.)
- **Q: The directory says Kingsview Estates doesn't allow rentals. Can I rent my unit?** Yes, for a long-term tenancy — that directory line reflects a bylaw type voided province-wide on November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and the complex's 55+ bylaw still governs who may live there. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Which Poplar townhouse complexes are 55+?** Crown Point (NWS2984, built 1989) and Kingsview Estates (built 1989/1990), per the sources in Section 2. The Argyle is listing-stated as having no age restrictions; Kings Crossing's developer source states no age restriction. https://www.cotala.com/tours/85537/FullPublic.pdf?i=1703196650
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Source-stated only: Crown Point's MLS sheet records cats/dogs allowed, number limit one, with restrictions; Kingsview Estates' directory records pets not allowed; an Argyle listing states pets allowed for its unit. The filed pet bylaw in the Form B package decides for your unit — directory and listing lines conflict often enough that none of them is proof. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Is Kings Crossing strata or fee-simple?** UNCONFIRMED — no source checked states its tenure. It is a 20-townhome presale by Rockwood Homes Inc. with completion expected in 2026; the disclosure statement is the document that will state the tenure, the estimated fees (if strata) and the deposit structure, and REDMA gives you 7 days to rescind after receiving it. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- **Q: What should I budget beyond the mortgage on a 1989–1990 strata townhouse?** The strata fee on the Form B, plus levy risk: read the depreciation report (mandatory, renewed every five years) for the next roof/envelope/plumbing cycle, and the CRF balance — since November 1, 2023 every strata must put at least 10% of its operating fund into the CRF annually, which supports but does not guarantee levy readiness. https://pushormitchell.com/2024/05/16/updates-to-strata-requirements-depreciation-reports/
- **Q: What is a stacked townhome, and is 1419 McCallum one I can count on?** A stacked townhome piles units over/under each other with apartment-style shared parking — the form a listing records at 1419 McCallum Road (built 2022). Its complex name and tenure are UNCONFIRMED, so it sits in this file's queue, not its count. https://www.vancouverhomesearch.com/listing/r3105078-1419-mccallum-road-4-abbotsford-poplar-homes-for-sale-469000/brochure
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Poplar house instead of buying a townhouse?** A secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under s.140.3.8 (400 m² minimum lot, 12.0 m frontage in City reports), sized at less than 50% of the principal unit under the 2025 updates; a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m². That is a fee-simple house strategy — no strata, no Form B — not a townhouse purchase. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140 and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Zoning%20Bylaw%20Updates%20Overview%202025.pdf
- **Q: Which schools would my kids attend from King Road?** Catchments are by exact address. Locator runs for 32746 Huntingdon Road and 33165 King Road both returned Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary (regular stream); the Traditional pathway is South Poplar Traditional Elementary → Abbotsford Traditional School (6–12). Run a King Road address in the locator before relying on it. https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
