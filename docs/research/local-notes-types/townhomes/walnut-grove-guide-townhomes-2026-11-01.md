## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the strata townhouse shelf documented in §2.5 and to any Walnut Grove strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For the 1991–1997 townhouse shelf in §2.5, the depreciation report is the central value document (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — the directory's "rentals not allowed" line for Twin Lakes (§2.5) is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert). Walnut Grove's published 55+ product is its townhouse shelf (§2.5); neither counted apartment building in §2.3 is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED per building until the registered bylaws are read (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. None is counted on this ground (§2.2).
- **Pad rent (parks):** where a Walnut Grove purchase is a park home (the tenure the Forest Green Estates listings suggest), pad rent is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the Strata Property Act: the rent increase limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, with 3 full months' notice and one increase per 12 months. No pad-rent dollar figure for any Walnut Grove park is published in this file (condos guide sibling §3; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes).
