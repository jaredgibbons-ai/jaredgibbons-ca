## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 strata complexes above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. For Carlingwood (c. 2000) and the Trails townhouses (1994–1995) the depreciation report is the central document; the Kings Landing townhouses (2016–2020) are young stock by comparison. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold — none of the counted complexes is such a strata on its verified scale. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory line for the Trails addresses in §2 ("rentals allowed with restrictions") is flagged in-entry on exactly this rule: any long-term rental-restriction component is void; only a short-term-rental restriction can survive. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted complex here is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No verified fee-simple townhouse complex exists in Bear Creek Green Timbers (§2).
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. A finer City planning breakdown of this exact sub-area ground: UNVERIFIED in the research pass (fetch 429, stopped rather than retried) — no planning name is asserted for the sub-area beyond the City community framing in §1. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force since **July 8, 2024**) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits — directly relevant here, because the feed's detached listings lean on basement suites. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites and coach houses (Surrey):** one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite; a coach house or garden suite must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house, and one extra parking space is required. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/bear-creek-green-timbers.md, citing ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)

The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here (this file's own research could not open the City STR page — fetch 429 — so the program-verified City figure is carried with its source). **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it.
