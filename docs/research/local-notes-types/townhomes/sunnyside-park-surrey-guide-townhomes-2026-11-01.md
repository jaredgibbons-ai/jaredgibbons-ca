## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to all 4 counted complexes in the count home — all strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. For Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane and Highgrove the Form B is also where any 55+ bylaw is actually proved — listing descriptions are not proof (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for strata property; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; 45+ void, does not convert). Bishop's Green and St. Martin's Lane are described in listings as 55+ communities and Peninsula Village listings state "One person 55+" — each registered bylaw must still be confirmed on the Form B, and Highgrove's age position is in direct conflict between listings in the count home and is UNCONFIRMED (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Sunnyside Park (§2.2).

**Surrey zoning, OCP, SSMUH, suites and STRs — standing frame applied.**

- **OCP — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction:** Surrey's Official Community Plan is **Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900** (adopted June 15, 2026; REPLACES Bylaw No. 18020 — 18020 is NEVER cited as current). The Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan was approved January 31, 2022 and later updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations; the plan area (16 Avenue to 24 Avenue, Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School) borders this sub-area. The guide page states the plan covers 136 hectares and was updated July 27, 2026 for King George Boulevard bus rapid transit (site copy) (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan; https://jaredgibbons.ca/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.html).
- **Zoning / SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000 provides SSMUH zones RA to R6, allowing up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (research file §4; https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **Secondary suites (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area** under Zoning By-law No. 12000; a suite is not a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule and is never stated (research file §4).
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence. **Licence figure CONFLICT, carried openly:** $358/yr versus the City's 2024 corporate report's proposed $350/yr + fees — carry **$358 with $350 disclosed**, and check the City licence page before quoting either figure.
