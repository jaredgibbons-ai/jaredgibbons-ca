## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Flagged in-entry in §2: Shaughnessy Square's directory restriction element is VOID, as is Citadel Pointe's in the assembly file. Port Coquitlam's own STR bylaw/licence position is UNVERIFIED — no City STR source was read in this sub-area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). The city's one verified 55+ strata is Mayfair Terrace (§2); no Central Pt Coquitlam townhouse complex carries a verified age bylaw, because no townhouse complex here is verified at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is verified in this sub-area or anywhere in PoCo in this program (§2), so this is the contrast frame, not a description of local stock.
- **City plan:** Port Coquitlam Official Community Plan **Bylaw No. 3838** governs the city and identifies Downtown as a mixed-use neighbourhood developing further in that role; established neighbourhoods named in the OCP include Lincoln Park, Mary Hill, Riverwood and Citadel Heights on the north side.
