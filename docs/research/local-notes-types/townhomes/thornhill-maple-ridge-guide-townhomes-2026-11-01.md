## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 3 cross-referenced complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Rydge, first establish whether the unit is a developer sale or a resale, because the document route differs. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Rydge is over the threshold on its published count (61); The Terraces' and Terraces II's counts are UNCONFIRMED but both present as multi-unit strata. A sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — none of the 3 is presented as such a strata. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and any directory or listing rental line for a §2 complex is void in its restriction element for long-term rentals wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in §2 carries a verified age restriction — all three are family townhomes with no age restriction in any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** none counted here (§2). A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so Thornhill's fee-simple acreages are never confused with fee-simple townhouse complexes, and its strata townhouses are never sold with the wrong document set.

- **The 256 Street plan area changes designations, not buildings.** The North 256 Street Industrial Lands Area Plan covers both sides of 256 Street north of 128 Avenue (about 500 acres) at this area's north side — it establishes future designations and does not itself approve construction, so disclose it on nearby listings as a designation change, not a development approval. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-approves-new-500-acre-256-street-industrial-area-plan-support-jobs-and-economic (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/thornhill-maple-ridge-guide.md)
- **Acreage diligence is the real Thornhill checklist.** Wells sit in the provincial GWELLS database and sewerage systems must meet the Sewerage System Regulation's setbacks (including 30 m from a well unless professional advice is obtained) — both are subject-removal items on the acreage stock that makes up most of this sub-area, and parcel-level well/septic, floodplain and wildfire-interface status are all UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/thornhill-maple-ridge-guide.md)
