# Tsawwassen Central, Delta — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory and listing sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen Central" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; the working centre is the junction of 56 Street and 12 Avenue, around the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site, inside the Tsawwassen Urban Centre planning area under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The town-centre site itself: Thrifty Foods sits on the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site, whose City-listed addresses are 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue and 1236 55 Street. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- South Delta Recreation Centre is at 1720 56 St, beside South Delta Exchange, and Tsawwassen Library (Fraser Valley Regional Library) is at 1321A 56 St at the town centre. http://www.delta.ca/parks-recreation/sport-recreation/indoor-facilities/south-delta-recreation-centre
- Transit hub: South Delta Exchange, at 56 Street and 18 Avenue beside the recreation centre — route 601 runs South Delta / Boundary Bay to Bridgeport Station via 56 Street and the exchange, and route 602 runs Tsawwassen Heights to Bridgeport Station via the exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock actually sits: unlike the apartment-condo stock, which clusters on 56 Street and on 54A/55 Street, the one verified townhouse complex in this sub-area — Sundial — sits on 10 Avenue, west of 56 Street, a short walk from the town centre, Winskill Park and the recreation centre. Source: the complex entry in Section 2 below.
- Schools for the sample address 1299 56 St, from the SD37 School Locator: Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7), 5025 12 Ave., and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Market stats: the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no South Delta (Ladner / Tsawwassen) community section — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no SnapStats figures, townhouse or otherwise, exist for Tsawwassen Central, and none are cited in this file. Source: the area research file for tsawwassen-central-guide (Section 2), read 2026-11-01.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Sundial is strata, so the strata document package is the diligence core:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance, and must have the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. The strata must provide it within 7 days; maximum charge $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports — mandatory refresh:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot defer it by vote; the report projects 30 years of component repair/replacement costs with three CRF funding models. For a 1976/1978 complex like Sundial, this report is the roof/envelope/plumbing forecast. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute at least 10% of its annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund — read the Sundial CRF balance on the Form B against that floor and against the depreciation report's funding models. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void:** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — existing ones (including the "rentals not allowed" line a directory still records for Sundial) are invalid, and no bylaw amendment is needed. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2); any lower age bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, and provincial exemptions (Regulation 7.01, expanded May 1, 2023) cover a younger spouse/partner, a live-in caregiver, legacy residents and defined child cases. No 55+ bylaw is verified for Sundial — UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast (for the wider Tsawwassen search):** no fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this sub-area. A fee-simple (non-strata) townhouse or rowhouse has no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees and no strata bylaws — diligence runs on title, zoning and municipal records instead. That contrast is stated here because buyers routinely assume every townhouse is strata; in this sub-area the one verified complex is strata.
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — it has no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." Any source attributing "Schedule D.1" to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong — that attribution belongs to the old bylaw. The OCP carries Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) for the town-centre area. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research and sibling files cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017) and its RS/RD zone ladder (subdivision minimums include RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² or 460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m²). The program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so the number is flagged here, never asserted as settled; zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Zoning Amendment Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024, permit small-scale multi-unit housing on single-detached and duplex lots — Delta's notice to the Province states four units on all residential properties in the City, and six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta, not this area). This affects the detached streets around Sundial, not the strata lots inside it. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Secondary suites (City wording):** Delta's secondary-suite framework sits inside the zoning bylaw; the BC Building Code definition the City's suite material uses caps a secondary suite at 90 m² and less than 40% of the habitable floor space of the building, inside a single real estate entity — relevant to a buyer assessing a detached lot near the centre, not to a strata townhouse unit. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/48825/
- **Flood:** Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; the town centre is inland of the Boundary Bay shoreline, and no complex-specific flood construction level for Sundial was confirmed — UNCONFIRMED. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT and exemptions:** BC property transfer tax applies on a strata townhouse purchase the same as on a condo; first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial $835,000–$860,000, 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence; newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on new construction only:** FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA Excise and GST/HST News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate alone is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. A resale Sundial unit carries no GST question. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan limit $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5% across the published loan-to-value schedule. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a new townhouse is sold before completion, REDMA gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or disclosure-statement acknowledgement (s. 21), and the disclosure-statement regime (s. 18) governs what the developer must disclose; deposits are held in trust. No live townhouse presale was verified in this sub-area — the Town Centre Mall redevelopment file (Section 5) is a process, not a marketed project: UNCONFIRMED as product. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Strata disputes** can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Page-claims and contact check for this area's research file:** the research file documents no page copy for jaredgibbons.ca, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually a townhouse complex in Tsawwassen Central, or is it all condos and houses?** One verified townhouse complex: Sundial, 5421 10 Avenue (84 units per the building directory; built 1976 per MLS listings, 1978 per the directory). Everything else verified in the centre is apartment condos or detached/duplex houses. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/sundial-5421-10-ave
- **Q: Is Sundial strata or fee-simple?** Strata — listings call it a strata complex and the directory records strata bylaws for it, so expect monthly strata fees, a Form B package and registered bylaws. The exact title form (freehold strata) is not stated in so many words in the sources read: UNCONFIRMED, verify on title. https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/159-5421-10th-avenue-delta-british-columbia-v4m-3t9-30319214/
- **Q: The directory says rentals are not allowed at Sundial. Can I rent my unit out?** Long-term, yes — BC strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, whatever older records say. Short-term rentals are different: a strata can still ban or limit them by bylaw, so read the current registered bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What pets can I bring to Sundial?** The directory records pets allowed with restrictions; a current listing describes the complex as pet friendly (2 cats/dogs). The binding number/size limits are in the registered bylaws — UNCONFIRMED until read. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/sundial-5421-10-ave and https://residentexperts.ca/portfolio/listing/159-5421-10th-avenue-delta-british-columbia-v4m-3t9-30319214/
- **Q: How old is Sundial, and what does that mean for levies?** Listings say 1976, the directory says 1978 — either way it is the centre's oldest attached stock, which is why the depreciation report matters: since July 1, 2024 every strata of five or more lots must refresh it every five years, with a 30-year cost projection and three CRF funding models to compare against the CRF balance on the Form B. A listing's "new roof" note is exactly the kind of item to verify against that report. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Is The Lexington or Tsawwassen Greene a townhouse option for downsizers?** They are 55+ options, but apartment condos, not townhouses — both are low-rise apartment buildings (Tsawwassen Greene has an elevator). A buyer who needs a ground-oriented home with a private yard and entry is looking at Sundial in this sub-area, not those two buildings. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/public/building/the-lexington-1150-54a-street and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/tsawwassen-greene-1140-55-street
- **Q: What do strata fees cover at Sundial, and where is the real number?** The binding figure is on the Form B: monthly fees for the specific lot, amounts owing, approved levies still payable and the CRF balance, with the current budget attached. Listing-advertised fees are unit-specific and date-specific — never compare complexes on listing fees alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I live here without a car?** This is Tsawwassen's transit and shopping centre: Sundial sits steps from the Town Centre Mall site, the library and Winskill Park per its listings, and South Delta Exchange at 56 Street and 18 Avenue runs the 601 to Bridgeport Station and the 602. Route frequency and travel times: check current TransLink timetables — not stated here. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3076737-5421-10-avenue-126-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-3t9/brochure and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt602.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Sundial address?** Address-based: the research file's sample address for the centre (1299 56 St) maps to Cliff Drive Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12) in the SD37 locator. Run the complex's exact address in the locator before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What is planned across from the town-centre stock?** A redevelopment process is open for the Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site: applicant Century Group Lands Corporation, City File LU009754, covering 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street and an unaddressed parcel. What will be built, and when: UNCONFIRMED in the sources read. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The mall site is the area's live planning file.** The Tsawwassen Town Centre Mall site (Thrifty Foods) is in a redevelopment process under City File LU009754 (Century Group Lands Corporation) — the single biggest future change for the central attached stock, Sundial included. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Sundial's two build years are both in print.** MLS listings record 1976 and the building directory records 1978 for the same complex — a small fact that tells a buyer to trust the strata documents and title, not any single web source, for this complex's particulars. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing-rebgv_sold/r3076737-5421-10-avenue-126-tsawwassen-bc-v4m-3t9/brochure and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/index.php/building/sundial-5421-10-ave
- **The 54A Street corridor is becoming a seniors-housing campus, in phases.** The Tides (152 rental homes, KinVillage, 1070 54A St) opened with residents moving in in August 2025, and Phase 2 at 1050 54A Street was before Council in 2026 (File LU009921) as a six-storey, 143-unit affordable seniors rental building replacing the aging KinVillage Community Centre function. Rental, not townhouse stock — but it is reshaping the streets around the centre's attached housing. https://www.bchousing.org/news/releases/more-than-150-affordable-homes-open-for-seniors-in-delta and https://letstalk.delta.ca/lu009921
- **The Urban Centre has its own design permit layer.** The OCP's Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) speak to siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping — any future townhouse infill in the centre is read against them, not just the zoning. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Health care anchor:** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital, and Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre

Road commute times to Vancouver or Surrey Central: UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcferries.com/travel-boarding/terminal-directions-parking-food/vancouver-tsawwassen/TSA and https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
