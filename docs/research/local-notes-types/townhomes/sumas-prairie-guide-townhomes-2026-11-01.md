## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (BC standard — stated in full; no Sumas Prairie complex is verified under it, because there is no townhouse stock for it to attach to).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate). Prairie farm and acreage purchases are fee-simple: **NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees** — municipal rules and registered covenants on title only, stated plainly.
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, in strata; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid in strata since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast — the operative case here:** fee-simple farm and acreage stock carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — Abbotsford municipal rules, the ALR frame below, and registered covenants on title govern.

- **Suites and coach/garden suites (Abbotsford's rules ONLY):** under Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, a secondary suite must be **less than 50%** of the size of the principal unit, and garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to **15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** — the 15% / 100 m² figure is Abbotsford's rule only, never exported to another city (research file §4). On ALR land the ALC frame below sits alongside.
- **ALR housing frame:** one principal residence per parcel unless the owner qualifies for an additional residence; principal residence total floor area **500 m² or less** (maximum 1,000 m² of fill for that residence); a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only **within** the principal residence; an additional detached residence may be up to **90 m²** on properties of 40 ha or less and up to **186 m²** on properties over 40 ha; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/). **Q: Why can't a prairie farm just be split into townhomes?**
A: Two locks: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and the City's agricultural subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2) — most prairie parcels cannot be split further in any form (§§2.5, 3).

**Q: What is being done about Sumas Prairie flooding?**
A: The Sumas River Watershed Flood Mitigation Planning Initiative (the Province, the Semá:th, Máthxwi and Leq'á:mel First Nations, and the Cities of Abbotsford and Chilliwack), signed April 2023 after the November 2021 floods, with about $220 million of provincial funding since 2021 including $76.6 million for Barrowtown Pump Station upgrades and $3.3 million for design and assessment work — the work is still in design and assessment, not complete (§1).

**Q: Can I build a second dwelling on a prairie farm parcel?**
A: Where the parcel is in the ALR: one principal residence to 500 m², a suite only within it, and at most one additional detached residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). Abbotsford's suite cap (less than 50% of the principal unit) and coach/garden cap (15% of the lot to 100 m²) sit alongside — confirm the parcel's ALR status and zone first (§3).

**Q: Is this Abbotsford or FVRD ground for rules and taxes?**
