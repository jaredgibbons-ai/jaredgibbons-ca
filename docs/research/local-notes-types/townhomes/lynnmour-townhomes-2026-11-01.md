## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Lynnmour is a neighbourhood of the **District of North Vancouver**, identified by the District as **Lynnmour North and Lynnmour South** — also known in the community as the **Inter River** and **Seylynn** neighbourhoods — within the wider Lynn Creek area. Its MLS® sub-area name as the Greater Vancouver REALTORS® board uses it is "Lynnmour." The setting is the flat-to-gently-sloped bench between Lynn Creek (west) and the Seymour River (east), from the mountain slopes down to Highway 1 and the Second Narrows crossing at its south end. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- Nearby areas: Maplewood (east, across the Seymour River), Lynn Valley (north), Lower Lynn / Lynn Creek Town Centre (southwest), and the City of North Vancouver (west, across Lynn Creek) — a different municipality with different bylaws. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)

- Where the townhouse stock concentrates: two pockets. The established strata townhouse complexes sit on the bench east of Mountain Highway — the Orwell Street / Premier Street pocket (Wedgewood, Connect at Nature's Edge) and the Purcell Way pocket by Capilano University (Lynnmour South) — while the new town-centre product at the south end is towers (counted in the condos file) with townhouse components inside them (§2). The research file characterises Lynnmour as the most attached-heavy of the District's east-side neighbourhoods: 1970s–90s low-rise condos and townhouse complexes along the Mountain Highway/Oxford Street side, newer purpose-built rentals, and the first town-centre towers at the south end; detached houses survive mainly on the Inter River (north) side, and strata dominates the south and central parts while freehold dominates the north. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- Growth centre: Lynnmour's south end forms part of the Lynn Creek Town Centre, one of the four centres where the District's OCP directs 75–90% of residential growth. On October 20, 2025, District council approved the Seylynn Centre project — two towers adding nearly 700 homes at the town centre (an approval, not a completed building — §2). https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf and https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- Schools: **Lynnmour Elementary** is the only school serving the Lynn Creek area (Lynnmour North and South / Inter River and Seylynn); the current building dates to 1973, enrolment reached 256 students in September 2025, and SD44's capital plan seeks a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds. **Catchment (settled locator chain):** for the sample address 1491 Hunter Street (locator range 1401–1491 Hunter St), the SD44 School Locator returns **Lynnmour Elementary (K–7) and Windsor Secondary (8–12)** (run 2026-11-01). Post-secondary: Capilano University's main campus is in the neighbourhood at 2055 Purcell Way, a public teaching university with more than 100 programs — a major local institution and a driver of rental demand near campus, directly beside the Lynnmour South townhouse pocket. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf and https://www.capilanou.ca/
- Transit: Phibbs Exchange, immediately south across Highway 1, is the North Shore's eastern transit hub — the R2 RapidBus runs from Phibbs to Park Royal and, since September 7, 2026, through to Metrotown Station, every 6–7 minutes at peak. Local routes serving the neighbourhood include the 245 Phibbs Exchange–Capilano University and the 227 Lynn Valley–Phibbs Exchange along Mountain Highway. https://buzzer.translink.ca/2026/09/major-r2-rapidbus-extension-provides-all-day-service-from-park-royal-to-metrotown/

The Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve begins at the end of Lillooet Road just past Capilano University — 5,668 hectares managed by Metro Vancouver, more than 100 km of trails, and the stocked Rice Lake fishing lake. https://www.nvrc.ca/facilities-fields/locations-hours/community-recreation-centres/lynn-creek-community-recreation and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata townhouses):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Wedgewood the Form B is the confirming document for the directory's pet/rental summary; for Connect at Nature's Edge and Lynnmour South it is the document that settles the restrictions this file leaves UNCONFIRMED. **Edgewater Estates has no Form B** — it is undivided interest, not strata (§2); a buyer there needs the co-ownership documents instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. All three counted complexes here are well above the 5-lot threshold on their published or listing-evidenced scale (Wedgewood's 55 units are published; the other two are multi-building-scale complexes whose exact counts are UNCONFIRMED in this file). https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Wedgewood's directory line records rentals allowed, which is consistent with the current law; any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a Lynnmour strata is VOID in-entry and must be flagged as such wherever it is met. The Capilano University tenancy cycle (September intake) is a market fact for investors here, not a bylaw question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No counted Lynnmour complex is published as age-restricted on any source checked — all three are general-market on the sources cited; the 55-plus sibling verifies no 55+ strata in this area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly.** No fee-simple townhouse stock is verified in Lynnmour (§2): a fee-simple purchase here is a detached house on the Inter River side — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; District rules plus any registered covenants/HOA govern instead. Do not import strata expectations into an Inter River house purchase, or house expectations into a strata townhouse purchase.
- **District plan:** the District of North Vancouver's Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 7900**; Lynnmour's south end is inside the Lynn Creek Town Centre, one of the four centres where the OCP directs 75–90% of residential growth. **These are District bylaws — the City of North Vancouver's OCP/Zoning numbers (8400/6700) do not apply east of Lynn Creek and are never cited for this ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/lynnmour.md)
- **District zoning and added homes:** **Zoning Bylaw 3210** (as amended by Bylaw 8698, June 2024) allows a main house, secondary suite and coach house on most single-family lots, and December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties; separately, Phibbs Exchange and the Capilano University area were designated Transit-Oriented Areas under Bylaw 8679 (2024), giving Lynnmour's south end a higher-density pathway. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf
- **District property taxes (2026):** property taxes are billed by the District of North Vancouver. Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026 (2.03% for services plus 1.5% for infrastructure renewal); the District's published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value across all taxing authorities; **taxes were due July 2, 2026, and a 5% penalty applied after July 2 with a further 5% after September 1, 2026**. District utility fees are billed separately and are rising fastest for sewer, driven by Metro Vancouver's North Shore Wastewater Treatment Plant costs. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Empty Homes / speculation taxes:** the City of Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a **Vancouver-only** tax and does **not** apply in the District of North Vancouver — do not carry it into a Lynnmour holding-cost calculation. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate provincial regime; whether a specific Lynnmour property is in a taxable area and exempt is per-property: UNVERIFIED in this file, confirm on the Province's SVT pages before quoting it.

Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Two Purcell Way addresses, two opposite tenures.** Lynnmour South (1825 Purcell Way) is a strata townhouse complex whose units sell individually on MLS; Lynmour Village (1923 Purcell Way), a few doors north, is a purpose-built rental apartment building you cannot buy into at all. Search results blend them — the address number is the whole difference. (§2 above)
- **The catchment secondary is the one nobody guesses.** Listings and third-party neighbourhood pages for Lynnmour routinely name Sutherland Secondary; the SD44 locator run for an in-area address returns **Windsor Secondary**. The error survives because Sutherland is the closer-feeling North Vancouver name — the locator, not proximity, governs. (§1 above)
- **The elementary school is a construction story.** Lynnmour Elementary's current building dates to 1973, enrolment hit 256 in September 2025, and SD44 is actively seeking provincial funding for a full replacement on seismic and condition grounds — a material fact for family buyers, in a good way long-term. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/board-of-education/Letter-to-Minister-Bowinn-Ma---Lynnmour-Replacement---Nov-2025.pdf
- **The recreation centre arrived as a developer component.** The Lynn Creek Community Recreation Centre (opened February 19, 2025, at 1491 Hunter Street) was delivered as one component of Intergulf's Hunter at Lynn Creek development — the condo towers at the town centre literally carry the neighbourhood's public hub at their base, library kiosk included. (§1 above)
- **The tallest tower claim belongs to this neighbourhood.** Denna Homes describes Seylynn Village's Apex (1500 Fern Street, 32 storeys, 276 homes) as the North Shore's tallest tower — in the same town centre where two more towers with nearly 700 homes were approved in October 2025. The bench townhouse pockets in §2 are the low-rise counterweight to that vertical growth. https://denna.ca/the-sentinel-development-brightens-tough-year-for-denna-homes/
- **A wilderness reserve starts at the end of a residential road.** The Lower Seymour Conservation Reserve — 5,668 hectares, 100+ km of trails, Rice Lake — begins at the end of Lillooet Road just past Capilano University, and draws over 900,000 visitors a year. Lynnmour South townhouse owners live minutes from its trailhead. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/Pages/Lower-Seymour-Conservation-Reserve.aspx
- **The directory-only name that listing evidence settled.** Lynnmour South sat UNCONFIRMED in the condos program because only a property-manager index named it; the townhouse program's fresh pass found MLS townhouse sales at its Purcell Way addresses and upgraded it — with the upgrade disclosed in §2 and its unit count and year still UNCONFIRMED, because the only source for those figures is a directory this program never cites.
