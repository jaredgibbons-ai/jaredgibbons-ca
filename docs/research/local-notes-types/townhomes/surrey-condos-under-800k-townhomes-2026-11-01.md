## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata stock referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The Newton Court directory line carried in §2 ("rentals allowed w/restrictions") is flagged VOID in-entry on exactly this rule, and the same flag applies to any such line for a townhouse complex referenced here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). The Chelsea Gardens record in §2 is the live illustration: a directory's "adult-oriented (19+)" description cannot be a lawful bylaw today; the MLS "55+" description is the operative one to confirm on the Form B. Program-wide, Surrey's 55+ roster is 15 strata + Sullivan Mews (membership) + 3 parks + Sea Ridge Bays (bare-land strata), with a re-consolidation QUEUE (Camberley Green, The Greens at Douglas, Ocean Park Grove, Carrington) — queue items are never silently folded into any count in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. No fee-simple townhouse is counted in this file — a card under this ceiling calling itself fee-simple is describing a different tenure from the page's strata-condo product.
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **SSMUH and suites (house comparison stock):** Surrey's framework (in force July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. One secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them; an apartment unit with one lock-off suite (if applicable) is a permitted housing type. The City's STR page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — carried here with a **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. A strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2, further 5% after September 2 — city-wide figures. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. A purchase in this band pays only the 1% and 2% tiers. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, subject to the program's other conditions (principal residence; property 0.5 hectares or smaller) — because this band caps at $800,000, a qualifying first-time buyer anywhere in the band sits under the full-exemption value ceiling; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. **Worked examples, inputs stated:** (1) an $800,000 townhouse at the band ceiling, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**. (2) a $600,000 townhouse, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $400,000 ($8,000) = **$10,000**; a qualifying first-time buyer on the same $600,000 purchase, exempt on the first $500,000, pays 2% × $100,000 = **$2,000** on those inputs. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — in this band that means developer sales (for example any remaining developer unit at Viva) and presale contracts, not the resale stock. No CMHC defect is asserted for any complex here — no file's own research documents one. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-9/surrey-condos-under-800k.md)
