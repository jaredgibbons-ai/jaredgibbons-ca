Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Abbotsford West" is the Fraser Valley Real Estate Board sub-area name used in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. The City's own demographic geography calls the wider area "West Abbotsford" (profile area 7) and treats Clearbrook, Peardonville, South Clearbrook and Townline as separate profile areas inside it; there is no Abbotsford West neighbourhood plan in the Official Community Plan. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-west-guide.md)
- Frame roads: Highway 1 across the north-centre (interchanges at Mt. Lehman Road, Clearbrook Road and McCallum Road), South Fraser Way as the east–west arterial, Clearbrook Road and Mt. Lehman Road north–south, and the Agricultural Land Reserve and Abbotsford International Airport (YXX) lands to the southwest. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange

- Abbotsford West is the city's biggest attached market by listings: 210 attached listings and 16 attached sales in September 2026 — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).
- SnapStats does not publish a townhome-only figure for Abbotsford West: it splits only Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined, and its price figures are Abbotsford-wide (attached median $497,500, $433/sq ft, 97% sale-to-list, 32 DOM — September 2026). No townhome-only median is stated in this file. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

Lehman Road and Cardinal Avenue (six bus bays), with routes 1 (Highstreet/UFV), 2 (Highstreet/McMillan) and the 66 Fraser Valley Express toward Lougheed Station. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/projects-go/highstreet-transit-exchange

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction:** every verified complex in Section 2 is strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer owns the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in this area; "freehold strata" in a listing is still strata. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Form B diligence (strata):** the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate on a resale; BCREA advises reviewing it for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** effective July 1, 2024, all BC stratas with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years with no deferral vote (30-year projection, at least three CRF funding models); stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. This matters most in the 1980s Garibaldi/Clearbrook stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Minimum CRF contribution:** effective November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating fund must go to the contingency reserve fund in the annual budget. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/more-help-and-support/subscribing-for-updates
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022 (Bill 44), no strata may enforce a long-term rental-restriction bylaw; short-term rentals can still be restricted. Any directory/listing "rentals not allowed" or "rentals allowed with restrictions" line for a complex in Section 2 is void for long-term tenancies in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **55+ is the only legal age bylaw:** SPA ss.123.1/123.2 permit only a 55-or-older residency bylaw, with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, and (from May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Carriage Lane, Fircrest Estates and The Wedgewood are the verified 55+ townhouse complexes here — confirm the registered bylaw on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **City zoning/OCP:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs, under Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025 as the current Official Community Plan — the research file's "2600-2016" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE and flagged here as a Jerry sweep item. The OCP puts Secondary Transit Corridors connecting Highstreet and the hospital to the Primary Transit Corridor on South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH/suites:** June 2024 Council adoption of the SSMUH Zoning Bylaw amendments is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED and "3560-2024" must not be asserted. Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings; Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rule only) — standing corrections in the commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **ALR / farm edge:** the ALR covers rural parcels on the west and southwest edges near Mt. Lehman Road and YXX. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — standing correction in the commissioning brief. Farm-class thresholds are three-tier: $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line would be an error; no such flat line is documented in this area's research file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **GST on new townhomes (Highstreet Village, Westerleigh phases):** 5% GST on new construction, not resales. FTHB GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — this date governs over March 12 in some sibling files): 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. GST New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT:** 1% first $200,000 / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M; FTHB exemption on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **Presales (REDMA):** Highstreet Village phases still completing carry REDMA s.21 7-day rescission (later of signing / acknowledging the disclosure statement) and s.18 deposits in trust. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Federal savings / insurance:** HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://homecalc.ca/first-time-home-buyer-programs-canada
- **Property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is a west-side townhouse strata or fee-simple?** Strata in every verified case — Clearbrook Village (NW 1689), Carriage Lane (NWS2539), Highstreet Village and the three Westerleigh complexes all carry strata title or strata fees. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Abbotsford West. http://www.clearbrookvillage.info/
- **Q: What will my strata fee cover?** Complex-by-complex, read off the Form B. Listing-level examples only: Carriage Lane listings state fees include heat, hot water and electricity for some units; Fircrest listings state heat and electricity. Those are single-unit listing statements, not verified complex-wide coverage. https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3098323-131-32691-garibaldi-drive-abbotsford-british-columbia-v2t5t7-389000/brochure
- **Q: Can I rent out a Carriage Lane or Fircrest unit?** For a long-term tenancy, a strata cannot stop you — rental-restriction bylaws are void since Nov 24, 2022, despite directory lines saying "rentals allowed with restrictions." In a 55+ complex the tenant must still meet the 55+ bylaw or an exemption, and short-term rentals can still be banned. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: My spouse is under 55 — can we live in Carriage Lane?** Yes under the statutory exemption: Strata Property Regulation 7.01 exempts a younger spouse/partner of a qualifying resident (expanded May 1, 2023), plus legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined children. The Wedgewood's own listing wording says only one resident needs to be over 55. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Are pets allowed?** Carriage Lane: pets allowed with restrictions (directory). Fircrest Estates: no pets per listings in the sibling file. The Wedgewood: no pets per listings. Clearbrook Village: one dog or one cat welcome per a single-unit listing — UNCONFIRMED as a bylaw. Highstreet Village and Westerleigh complexes: UNCONFIRMED. Pet bylaws remain enforceable — read the registered bylaws. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/carriage-lane-32691-garibaldi-drive
- **Q: How do I check an 1980s complex for coming levies?** Through the depreciation report attached to the Form B: 5+ lot stratas must have one every five years with a 30-year projection and three CRF funding models, and must contribute at least 10% of the operating fund to the CRF yearly. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Q: What insurance deductible am I exposed to?** The strata's water-damage deductible, shown in the Form B insurance summary, can be charged back to the owner whose unit caused a loss. The amount for every west-side complex is UNCONFIRMED until its documents are read. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Q: Parking and storage at the new complexes?** Listing-level only: Highstreet Village townhomes show double/tandem garages; Harvest shows a tandem 2-car garage; Bristol Heights shows a double tandem garage. The stall/locker legally attached to a unit is on the Form B. https://www.northshorerealty.ca/listing/r3099035-30930-westridge-place-48-abbotsford-bc-v2t-0h6/brochure
- **Q: Which schools serve a Westerleigh or Clearbrook townhouse?** Catchments are address-based (SD34 Policy 18). Locator runs in the research file: 3614 Clearbrook Road returns Clearbrook Elementary → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 3433 Firhill Drive returns Blue Jay Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary. Run the complex's own address. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/abbotsford-west-guide.md)
- **Q: I'm a first-time buyer at Highstreet Village. What stacks?** Potentially the FTHB GST rebate (to $50,000, agreements on/after Mar 20, 2025), the Newly Built Home PTT exemption (full to $1,100,000), and FHSA/HBP room ($40,000/$60,000) — separate eligibility tests for each. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Abbotsford adopted a new Official Community Plan in December 2025.** Abbotsforward 2050 (public hearing November 25, 2025) emphasizes more apartments, townhouses and SSMUH in priority areas and enables the 37,185 units targeted by the City's 2024 Interim Housing Needs Report over 20 years. The research file's "2600-2016" OCP citation is stale under the standing correction — flag for Jerry. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Highstreet Village's published totals do not agree with each other:** 131 townhomes (developer summary in the research file) vs 157 townhomes + 577 condominiums (presale directories), and 9 condominium buildings (developer, via the sibling condos file) vs 7 mid-rise buildings (directories). Current listings show a condo building completing in 2028 — townhouse buyers in the finished phases live beside an active build-out either way. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/highstreet-village-west-townline-abbotsford-presale-condos-and-townhomes-by-ab-wall-group/
- **Westerleigh's townhouses share one amenity bet:** Harvest, Bristol Heights and Hartwell are separate Polygon complexes whose value leans on Club West, the shared clubhouse (outdoor pool, hot tub, fitness centre, theatre) — part of each strata fee buys into that shared structure, not an in-complex gym alone. https://polyhomes.com/community/elmstone/
- **Clearbrook Village runs its own public paper trail:** the NW 1689 strata corporation posts bylaws, notices and council minutes on its own website — a buyer can read the complex's governance before ordering documents, which is unusual for a 1980s townhouse strata. http://www.clearbrookvillage.info/
- **The Garibaldi corridor is the city's 55+ townhouse pocket:** Carriage Lane (NWS2539) and Fircrest Estates sit on the same drive, and The Wedgewood is one street over on Countess — three of the west side's eight verified townhouse complexes are age-restricted, a fact general townhouse searches do not surface. Sources: Section 2 above, and ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/abbotsford-west-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **The City's Housing Accelerator Fund program is complete:** up to $25.6 million in federal HAF funding, all initiatives reported complete in October 2026, with multi-family permitting above expectations — part of why the west side's attached listings (210 in September 2026) are the deepest in the city. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
