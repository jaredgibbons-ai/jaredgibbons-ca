## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **The diligence frame, stated plainly for a zero-count row:** any townhouse a buyer actually purchases on the east side will almost always be an Edmonds BE strata purchase (see the cross-references in Section 2), so the strata package governs there: Form B Information Certificate under Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — the Edmonds-node complexes in Section 2 (22, 27 and 56 units where published) are all over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Kensington's published line already reads "Rentals Allowed"; any other directory "rentals not allowed" line a buyer meets on the east side is VOID for long-term rentals and is flagged as such. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No verified 55+ strata ownership complex exists in this sub-area — the quadrant's verified seniors buildings are rental, care or subsidized non-profit housing that cannot be purchased (sibling 55-plus file on disk). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none is verified in this row. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. The east side's new small multiplex product (for example the no-strata-fee four-home product marketed at 7353 12th Avenue under Edmonds BE filing) is fee-simple/duplex form, documented as a form in the Edmonds file — not a named townhouse complex in this row. https://vancouvernashdom.com/burnaby-east-edmonds-townhouses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=3
- **City zoning:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, Bylaw No. 14815 — adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026 — is the governing bylaw; it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742 is never the citation for current Burnaby zoning). Under its R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District (carried from the June 2024 consolidation), R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; the bylaw's district list also carries a Townhouse District (R2). The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP, and the Edmonds Community Plan was officially adopted in March 2025 for the town-centre part of the east side. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan
- **Hazard check:** creeks and streams run through Robert Burnaby Park and the ravine lands on the east side; a floodplain designation for East Burnaby's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read, and parcel-level hazard, watercourse-setback and ALR status are property-specific checks — ALR status through the Agricultural Land Commission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property tax and utilities:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date. Burnaby's 2026 detached single-family dwelling water charge is $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. There is NO Vancouver Empty Homes Tax in Burnaby — that 3% tax is Vancouver only and never applies here. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties and https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
