## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**Township of Langley frame — the s.102 coach-house frame in full.**

- **Coach house definition (settled):** in Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100's own text, searched in full, contains no size cap for a coach house — but whether a cap sits in the individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED, because those sections were not opened (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **Suite cap:** s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **LIVE TOWNSHIP CONFLICT flagged, not resolved:** the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) states a suite may be located in a townhouse or rowhouse. Both documents are reported with their dates; confirm with the Township in writing before relying on either (research file §4; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).
- **SSMUH:** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) — eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible; NO 6-plex tier exists in it — no 6-plex claim is documented in this file's research, so none is asserted here. Non-qualifying properties are instead eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning (research file §4; https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx).
- **R-CL:** s.403 = 220 m² min / 464.5 m² max. The page's R-CL(CH) Section 406 detail (either one SSMUH development or one house and one coach house, not both; 50% coverage; 390 m² / 465 m² corner minimums) was not opened in the bylaw itself and is UNVERIFIED as stated, carried as site copy only (research file §§4–5).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: What is a coach house in the Township of Langley?**
A: By s.102, a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100 states no size cap; whether a cap sits in the zone sections is UNVERIFIED (§3; ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md).

**Q: Is a coach house a townhome or a condo I can buy separately?**
A: Neither. It is a fee-simple second unit on the same lot as the principal house — no separate title, no strata, and this file counts 0 complexes (§2).

**Q: Is a carriage home the same thing?**
A: Similar building, different bylaw: Township Bylaw 2500 says "coach house," City Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 says "carriage home." The page's detailed City limits are UNVERIFIED because the City bylaw was not opened (§3).

**Q: How big can a Township secondary suite be?**
A: 120 m², no percentage cap, under s.102 as amended — subject to the flagged guide-vs-bylaw conflict on townhouse suites (§3).

**Q: Can a townhouse in the Township have a suite?**
A: CONFLICT, not resolved: the January 2025 guide says not permitted; the July 2025 s.102 text says a suite may be in a townhouse or rowhouse. Get the Township's current answer in writing (§3).

**Q: The listing calls it a coach house — is it legal as one?**
A: UNVERIFIED per property. The page itself warns a building marketed as a coach house may have been approved as a garage, studio or workshop; ask for the building permit and final inspection for the unit itself (research file §§4, 6).

**Q: Can an ALR property's additional residence sit over a garage?**
A: Yes, on the ALC's stated conditions — one-storey garage for the principal residence's vehicles, no larger than the residence's maximum, no internal connection, clustered close (§3; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).

**Q: Does SSMUH replace the coach-house route?**
A: They are alternatives governed by eligibility and zoning — an eligible lot's 3–4 unit SSMUH route under Bylaw 6020 and its suite/ADU route are not stacked; confirm which route the lot is on with Township staff (§3).

A: Board context only, not coach-house-segment figures: detached median $1,345,000 on 449 / 64; Condos & Townhomes median $611,000 on 699 / 128 (§1; SnapStats FVR September 2026 report).

**Q: Which schools serve a house with a coach house?**
