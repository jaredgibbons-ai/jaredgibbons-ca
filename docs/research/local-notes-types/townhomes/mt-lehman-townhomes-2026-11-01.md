## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count in the community is zero, this section works in two parts: the ALR/rural Abbotsford rules that explain why (§2's structural zero), and the strata rules that govern the nearest strata purchases (urban Abbotsford, and Eden Court if it is assigned to Abbotsford West).

- **The ALR stratification bar — the rule that decides this file:** ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239 — a deposit under s. 239 is a subdivision; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). On ALR parcels the ALC's as-of-right package is: generally one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional one; a **principal residence of 500 m² or less** (max 1,000 m² of fill); a **secondary suite only inside the principal residence**; and an **additional residence of 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha)** where permitted; anything beyond the permitted number/size needs ALC approval, and ALC Policy L-26 (amended May 2025) allows an additional residence beyond the regulation only if necessary for a farm use (the farm-worker housing route). The City may further restrict residences. ALR status is per-parcel — check the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder; the exact ALR share of the Mt Lehman community is UNVERIFIED. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/policies/alc_policy_l-26_-_non-adhering_residential_use_applications.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — thresholds in full three-tier form:** Class 9, Farm classification requires production of qualifying primary agricultural products for sale, with income thresholds of **$2,500 (parcels 0.8–4 ha), $2,500 plus 5% of the land's value over 4 ha (parcels over 4 ha), and $10,000 (parcels under 0.8 ha)**, with applications due by **October 31**. Farm buildings are Class 1, and 50% of Class 9 land's assessed value is exempt from school taxes. Farm class is earned by production, not automatic on ALR land. **A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever a page states it** — no such line is documented in this slug's own research and none is asserted here; the three-tier form above governs. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/understanding-property-classes-and-exemptions?TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=582130f6-038f-4842-ada7-0166ce03a246
- **Form B diligence (strata frame — for the nearest strata purchases):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It has no in-community application today (no in-community strata stock); it is the document that would govern an Eden Court purchase (confirming the listing-described 55+ rule and pet position) and any urban Abbotsford townhouse purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/

Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for an Abbotsford strata is VOID in-entry and must be flagged as such wherever it is met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals

Set pricing expectations from Bradner/Matsqui/Aberdeen rural comparables plus acreage sales, not a sub-area stats sheet. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can a Mt Lehman farm's second home be sold to me separately as a townhouse or condo?** No. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); a second residence on ALR land is an accessory farm/employee residence or a suite inside the principal residence, and it conveys only with the parcel. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house on a Mt Lehman acreage?** That is the realistic multi-household route here, under two regimes: Abbotsford caps a secondary suite at under 50% of the principal dwelling and a coach/garden suite at 15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rules only), A1 zoning lists a Secondary Suite as an accessory use — and on ALR land the ALC allows a suite only inside the principal residence, with anything beyond the permitted number/size needing ALC approval. Check the specific parcel's zone and ALR status first. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Mt Lehman in the floodplain?** The community sits on raised ground above Matsqui Prairie — the origin of the "Mount." The prairie at its foot is diked floodplain behind the 11.3 km Matsqui Dike, and the November 2021 disaster was a Sumas Prairie (Nooksack overflow) event, not a Mt Lehman event — parcel-level checks still matter for plateau-edge and prairie-floor properties. (§1 above)
- **Q: What are the tax penalties if I miss Abbotsford's deadline?** Taxes were due July 2, 2026 by midnight; 5% was added July 3, 2026 and a further 5% on September 16, 2026 — legislated, and cannot be waived. Farm-class parcels can carry materially different bills (Class 9 requires actual production; 50% of Class 9 land value is exempt from school taxes) — verify classification per parcel with BC Assessment. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is there a school in Mt Lehman?** Yes — Mt Lehman Elementary (K–5) at 6381 Mt Lehman Rd, operating on a site with schooling continuous since 1884, and a real selling point for young families in rural Abbotsford. SD34's feeder pattern sends its students to Eugene Reimer Middle from grade 6, in the Rick Hansen Secondary family — confirm a specific address in SD34's School Locator. (§1 above)
- **Q: Are the hall and church "heritage-registered"?** No — and do not let listing copy say so. Mount Lehman Hall (built 1904) and the church are historic village buildings, but **no Mount Lehman building appears on the City's published Community Heritage Register list**. Historic is true; "heritage-registered" is not. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I split a Mt Lehman acreage and build townhouses?** Start with a planner, not a pro forma: Abbotsforward 2050 protects the agricultural land base and directs growth to urban centres, most working land is A1-zoned (8.0 ha and larger intent), and ALR parcels carry the ALC residence/stratification bars in §3. Rural lot-splitting assumptions need a City/ALC answer first. (§3 above)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Mt Lehman" will never appear on a stats sheet.** It is a community name the board does not use — Abbotsford's sub-areas are the nine named in §1, and Lehman listings are quietly absorbed into Bradner/Matsqui-side reporting. A buyer asking an agent for "the Mt Lehman townhouse report" is asking for a document that does not exist. (§1 above)
- **The road's name outruns the community.** Mt Lehman Road runs from the village core (school at 6381, hall at 6418) south past the airport/Highstreet ground to Highway 1 — which is how a 55+ strata townhouse complex at number 3348, filed by the board as Abbotsford West, ends up answering "Mt Lehman townhouse" searches. Address road ≠ community. (§2 above)
- **Elevation is the pitch, and it is literal.** The community's name comes from raised ground above Matsqui Prairie's diked floodplain; the November 2021 flood disaster that buyers picture was Sumas Prairie, a different prairie in the same city. Plateau-edge parcels still need their own flood check — "upland community" is not a per-lot guarantee. (§1 above)
- **The wineries are the retail.** There is no retail centre in Mt Lehman — daily shopping is Abbotsford (Clearbrook/Highstreet) or Langley — but three estate wineries (Mt. Lehman, Singletree at 5782 Mt. Lehman Road, Seaside Pearl at 5290 Olund Road) are the community's farm-gate storefronts, verified on their own sites. (§1 above)
- **A transit exchange opened for a community with no village bus route.** The five-bay Highstreet exchange (opened September 6, 2026) is expressly meant to serve "growing neighbourhoods in the Mt. Lehman area," and Route 21 runs the road's south end — but no route reaches the village core, so every transit trip from the plateau starts with a drive. (§1 above)
- **Farm class is earned, and the threshold is not flat.** A Mt Lehman acreage does not get Class 9 farm taxes by being in the ALR: it must produce qualifying products for sale and meet the three-tier income thresholds ($2,500 / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31). A listing that says "farm status — taxes are cheap" without the production file is selling the wrong thing. (§3 above)
- **The fire hall carries the community's name into the urban fringe.** Abbotsford Fire Hall 5 (Mt. Lehman), 30373 Merryfield Avenue, serves this ground — and its address is a reminder that Mt Lehman, as a service name, stretches well south of the village core, like the road itself. (§1 above)
