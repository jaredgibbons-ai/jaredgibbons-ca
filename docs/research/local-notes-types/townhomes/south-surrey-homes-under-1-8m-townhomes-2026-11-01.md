## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata stock referenced in §2):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a strata townhome in this band, follow that paperwork path — Form B and Form F, bylaws and rules, fees and special levies — before subjects come off. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/first-time-owners
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The directory line for Semiahmoo Estates (apartment stock, §2) recording "rentals not allowed" is flagged VOID in-entry on exactly this rule, and the same flag applies to any such line for a townhouse complex referenced here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). South Surrey carries much of Surrey's 55+ townhouse stock (Bishop's Green, St. Martin's Lane, Peninsula Village and The Crescent are all published 55+ in their home file; Highgrove's age status is in direct conflict there and carried UNCONFIRMED). Program-wide, Surrey's 55+ roster is 15 strata + Sullivan Mews (membership) + 3 parks + Sea Ridge Bays (bare-land strata), with a re-consolidation QUEUE — none of that roster is re-derived or re-counted in this band file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly.** A fee-simple townhouse (and a fee-simple half duplex of the kind the live page names in this band) carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Check the Title to Land field: in this band the same street can hold strata townhouses and fee-simple rowhomes side by side.
- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Zoning is Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Surrey replaced RF with R3 Urban Residential and RF-SD with R6 Semi-Detached under its SSMUH framework, and removed the RM-D duplex zone — check the current zone, not the zone name on an old listing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH and suites:** Surrey's framework (in force July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; it applies to eligible residential properties under 4,050 m² inside the Metro Vancouver Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. One secondary suite per house, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area**, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. The live page's R3/R6 lot-size minima (an R3 minimum of 560 m² and R6 minima of 200 m² interior / 226 m² corner) are UNVERIFIED — not confirmed on a City source in the research pass; the live page is the only source for them. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home as their primary residence; tenants cannot operate them. The City's STR page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — carried here with a **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 per year plus fees; confirm on the City licence page before quoting. A strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2, further 5% after September 2 — city-wide figures. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — a purchase inside this band reaches only the 1% and 2% tiers; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both thresholds sit inside this band's lower range. **Worked example, inputs stated:** PTT on a $1,800,000 purchase at the band ceiling, no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales.
