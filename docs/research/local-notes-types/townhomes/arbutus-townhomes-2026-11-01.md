## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no local strata townhouse stock to apply Form B diligence to — but the area's one strata enclave carries the full regime, and any new multiplex strata here will too:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — a 1975-era strata like Arbutus Village is exactly the stock this diligence exists for. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (and in Vancouver are separately governed by the City's licensing and the provincial principal-residence requirement). No directory "rentals not allowed" line for an Arbutus Ridge complex appears in Section 2; any such line found on a Form B package here is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership in Arbutus Ridge — every verified seniors building here is a rental retirement residence or a care home — so no home in this area may be marketed as a 55+ purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m), 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units; parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. Citywide land-use direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026; a separate adopted Arbutus Ridge community plan is UNVERIFIED in the sources read. The Arbutus Village enclave is the exception on the ground: CD-1 (78) for 4255 Arbutus Street (Bylaw No. 4634, enacted August 1, 1972). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf and http://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/ByCD-1Number/cd1s/iCD-1(078)_SummaryAndBackupNotes.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay; heritage status can change the answer and must be checked, since the City describes the stock as including restored heritage houses. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax = 3%:** Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the tax is 3% of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year — the 2025 declaration deadline was February 3, 2026 and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date; failing to declare deems the property vacant and adds a $250 by-law ticket. The City's tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which can also apply. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/declare-property-status.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a Vancouver presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame that will govern any Arbutus Village redevelopment strata block if and when it is marketed. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The area was once called "Asthma Flats":** a neighbourhood profile records that in the 1920s residents of Point Grey and Kerrisdale referred to the future Arbutus Ridge area that way — it was a huge swamp crossable only by a three-plank sidewalk, and the City's own history ties the area's different development pattern to the CPR and that swampy ground. Sand brought in from False Creek in the 1940s and 1950s allowed the low-lying areas to be developed with houses, schools and shops — a drainage and soil history worth a geotechnical question on low lots, and parcel-level floodplain status is UNVERIFIED in the research file. https://www.michaeltudorie.com/blog/52777/neighbourhood-profile-arbutus and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Valley Drive is older than the grid:** the same profile records that Valley Drive dates from the 1870s, cut through the forest as the right-of-way for BC's first logging railway — one of the few streets in the community not oriented to the regular street grid, which is why lots on it price and sit differently. https://www.michaeltudorie.com/blog/52777/neighbourhood-profile-arbutus
- **The Greenway is a former rail line the City bought from CP Rail:** the Arbutus Corridor runs about nine kilometres from the Fraser River to False Creek along the area's east border, and its temporary path runs 8.5 km between Fir Street and Milton Street — the corridor is the area's spine for walking and cycling, not a park that was always there. https://vancouver.ca/streets-transportation/explore-the-arbutus-greenway.aspx
- **There is no community centre or library branch inside Arbutus Ridge:** the City routes residents to the Dunbar, Kitsilano and Kerrisdale community centres (plus the Kitsilano Ice Rink, Kerrisdale Cyclone Taylor Arena and Kerrisdale Pool) and to those neighbourhoods' library branches — a real, if small, lifestyle trade against neighbouring areas that a townhouse-node suburb would not impose. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **Quilchena Park's off-leash dog area is one of the City-listed local facilities,** in a neighbourhood the City otherwise describes through quiet parks and restored heritage houses — the amenity set here is residential, not commercial: the City names the Kerrisdale Business Improvement Association among the community resources it supports for the area, and no in-area shopping street was confirmed on an official source. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/arbutus-ridge.aspx
- **The land-use plan changed under the area on March 31, 2026:** the Vancouver Official Development Plan replaced the Vancouver Plan on that date — a buyer reading older "Vancouver Plan" material for these streets is reading a superseded document. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
