## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Mid Meadows is the Paragon sub-area name for the Bonson Road area south of the CPKC rail line, including the North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village areas named in City park and facility pages; the City publishes no official neighbourhood boundary for "Mid Meadows" — its own labels are North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village. From official facility addresses the area runs along Bonson Road south of the rail line and Hammond Road and north of the Fraser River dike, roughly between Harris Road in the west and the Maple Ridge boundary in the east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp (research file, read 2026-11-01)
- Nearby areas: Central Meadows (Town Centre) to the north-west, West Meadows to the west, South Meadows lands toward the Fraser River, and Maple Ridge to the east. (Research file §1)
- Roads and transit: Bonson Road is the main road; Hammond Road and the CPKC rail line are at the north edge, Harris Road at the west edge, and Fraser Way and Barnston View Road serve Osprey Village. Routes 719 and 722 run as a combined circular service through Bonson Road and Fraser Way to Meadowtown, Pitt Meadows Station and Maple Meadows Station; route 701 runs along Hammond Road at the north edge. No West Coast Express station is in this sub-area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt722.pdf (research file)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing/directory data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all four counted complexes are over the threshold on any published figure. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid — Coho II's directory line "rentals allowed w/restrictions" is flagged VOID for long-term rentals in-entry in §2 for exactly this reason. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows at the city layer** — the provincial voiding does not create a short-term rental right here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699 (via the sibling condos file)
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). No complex in §2 carries a verified age restriction — Nature's Walk's family/ground-oriented marketing is not an age bylaw, and the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ housing of any tenure in Mid Meadows. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified in Mid Meadows. A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly so the four strata complexes above are never sold with the wrong document set.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 governs — RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) sets a minimum lot area of 0.4 ha for townhouse sites; houses sit under RS, R-3, R-4 and R-6 zones, and the 1,215 sq m figure there is a subdivision minimum only, not a development minimum on existing lots, per the City's SSMUH implementation update. The plan is the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022, covering the whole city including the Bonson Road area; no neighbourhood plan specific to Bonson Road was found in official sources. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf (research file)
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units (Bylaw No. 2986, adopted June 25, 2024); one secondary suite (33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building) or one garden suite per lot (33 to 90 sq m) where the zone permits — never both. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf (research file)
- **Floodplain — LIVE CONFLICT flagged:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states over 95% of the Pitt Meadows land base lies within the Fraser/Pitt floodplain, protected by 60 km of dikes; this sub-area is in drainage Area 3 of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District, and Shoreline Park and the greenway sit on the Fraser River dike; RS zoning siting requires buildings 15 m from watercourses and key/constructed ditches. Live pages quoting "over 85%" with a City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution conflict with the 95% figure — this file carries 95% and flags the conflict. Confirm flood construction level, covenants and insurance for the specific complex. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf (research file)
- **Taxes and rebates:** Pitt Meadows 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, with 5% penalties after July 2 and after August 1, 2026, and a separate residential drainage levy of $0.1082 per $1,000 on top of the $2.8438 general rate (research file). PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — first $500,000 exempt at FMV $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Mid Meadows" is a board name, not a City name (and the MLS uses a third label):** the City calls this ground North Bonson, South Bonson and Osprey Village; the Paragon sub-area is Mid Meadows; current MLS listings label the Osprey Village complexes South Meadows. Three names, one pocket — a buyer searching only one label will miss listings filed under the others. (Research file §1; §2 entries)
- **The dike is the neighbourhood's front yard:** Shoreline Park at 51 Bonson Road and the Harris Landing / Pitt River Greenway (a first phase of 10.2 km along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes) run on the Fraser River dike through the townhouse pocket — the recreation is genuine, and the structure underneath it is flood infrastructure first. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway (research file)
- **A replacement secondary school is in the works (dated):** SD42's Strategic Facilities Plan lists a Pitt Meadows Secondary replacement — funded by the Ministry of Education and Child Care, with a neighbourhood learning centre, in the design and development phase — and that one school serves every townhouse in this sub-area for grades 8–12. https://www.sd42.ca/board-of-education/strategic-facilities-plan/ (research file)
- **The airport is across Harris Road:** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) sits west of the area, with a City page for safety and noise concerns — check flight paths against a specific South Bonson / Osprey Village address rather than assuming the river side is the only environmental factor. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage (research file, airport reference)
- **There is no library branch in this sub-area:** the city's only FVRL branch is Pitt Meadows Public Library at 200-12099 Harris Road in Central Meadows — an everyday-life detail that Osprey Village marketing does not mention. https://fvrl.bc.ca/locations/pitt-meadows-public-library (research file)
- **Two elementary schools inside one small sub-area, split address by address:** Davie Jones (12030 Blakely Road) and Edith McDermott (12178 Bonson Road) both stand in Mid Meadows, so "Bonson Road = one catchment" is wrong in both directions — the locator run for the South Bonson Community Centre address returned Davie Jones, not the closer-looking school name. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ (research file)
