# Tsawwassen East, Delta — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen East" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the Boundary Bay side of Tsawwassen, east of 56 Street, taking in Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- The east side's landmarks are Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach, 570 Boundary Bay Rd (Metro Vancouver) — Dyke Trail, Savannah and Raptor trails, Cammidge House — and Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave, on the south side. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park
- Local roads are Boundary Bay Road, 12 Avenue and 56 Street; Highway 17 is reached via 56 Street or 52 Street, then Highway 17A and Highway 99 via the George Massey Tunnel. Route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Where the townhouse stock actually sits: in two places, neither of them on the waterfront streets. The older apartment-condo corridor runs along 56 Street / 16 Avenue at the Beach Grove edge (Section 2, not townhomes), and the townhouse stock is the new Southlands master-planned community at the south end — Market Avenue and Boundary Bay Road, around the Market District — where Century Group is building townhomes, rowhomes and bare-land strata cottages in named collections. Sources: the complex entries in Section 2 below.
- The Boundary Bay waterfront streets themselves are detached and duplex houses, including the RD2 zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove (4,000 m² subdivision minimum). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- Schools for the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd, from the SD37 School Locator: South Park Elementary (K–7), 735 Gilchrist Dr., and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; catchments are address-based. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- Market stats: the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report contains no South Delta (Ladner / Tsawwassen) community section — its Delta coverage is North Delta only — so no SnapStats figures exist for Tsawwassen East, and none are cited in this file. Source: the area research file for tsawwassen-east-guide (Section 2), read 2026-11-01.

The count mixes townhouses and duplexes in the source's own wording, and no public source read states the tenure split — **tenure: UNCONFIRMED** (strata vs fee-simple not stated); treat the 33 as a collection size, not a pure townhouse unit count, until a project source separates them. https://storeys.com/century-group-southlands-tsawwassen/
- **High Street Rowhomes — Southlands, at Southlands Drive and Boundary Bay Road.** **10 homes**, 3 storeys, by Century Group. **Tenure: FEE-SIMPLE (non-strata freehold)** — the project source states expressly that the rowhomes are "non-strata, freehold" homes with no strata fees. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata townhomes (The Granary): the Form B is the core document.** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B: Information Certificate states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance net of approved expenditures, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. On a presale, the developer's disclosure statement comes first (REDMA, below) and the Form B regime applies on resale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple rowhomes (High Street): plainly no strata.** A non-strata freehold rowhouse has no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees, no CRF and no strata bylaws. Diligence is title, zoning, the City permit record and any registered covenants or building scheme on title — a buyer who asks for "the strata documents" on a High Street home is asking for documents that do not exist. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/high-street-tsawwassen/
- **Depreciation reports (strata collections):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote, with 30-year projections and three CRF funding models; a strata established on or after July 1, 2024 must obtain its first report within the new-strata timeline — the position a brand-new Southlands strata starts in. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **CRF minimum (strata collections):** since Nov 1, 2023, a strata corporation must contribute at least 10% of its annual operating budget to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are void (strata collections):** as of Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata may have a residential rental-restriction bylaw — any "rentals not allowed" wording in an older Southlands or corridor document is invalid for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be banned or limited by bylaw. Fee-simple homes have no strata bylaw layer at all, though municipal short-term-rental rules still apply city-wide. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata collections):** only a 55+ residency bylaw is lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2); lower age bylaws have been invalid since Nov 24, 2022, with Regulation 7.01 exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined child cases. No 55+ bylaw is verified for any Southlands collection — UNCONFIRMED per collection. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Delta plan:** the OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — no lettered schedules and no Tsawwassen area-plan schedule (the former Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)." Any source attributing "Schedule D.1" to Bylaw No. 8400 is wrong. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 — the east side's overlay:** for the Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands, the OCP provides that no building be located with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres Geodetic Survey of Canada datum, that the minimum land surface elevation around construction be 2.3 metres GSC datum, and that no building be constructed within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. SD3 names the Southlands expressly — a Southlands townhouse buyer should confirm how it was applied to their collection. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number flagged, not asserted:** the research and sibling files cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (2017); the program's standing correction records the zoning-bylaw number as CONTESTED (cited elsewhere as "8400, 2025"), so it is flagged here, never asserted as settled. The Beach Grove waterfront pocket is zoned RD2, a zone the bylaw states is for waterfront lots in Beach Grove, with a 4,000 m² subdivision minimum; s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback, and the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — four units on all residential properties in Delta, six within 400 m of frequent transit along Scott Road (North Delta). This affects detached lots, not strata lots inside a townhouse complex. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **ALR edges (Southlands farms):** Southlands sits beside working farm land. An ALR residence cannot be stratified (Agricultural Land Commission Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — the legal reason farm-adjacent housing product is structured as it is. Farm-status thresholds run in three tiers — $2,500 in farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — a flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. Sources: ALC Bulletin 09 and the ALCA, as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — relevant to any "private dock" claim on the shore, not to the Southlands townhomes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Presales (REDMA) — directly live here:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day statutory rescission period from the later of signing the purchase agreement or acknowledging receipt of that statement (REDMA s. 21); deposits are held in trust. Assignment terms are whatever the purchase contract and disclosure statement say — no assignment term for any Southlands collection was verified: UNCONFIRMED per collection. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **GST on new construction — the Southlands question:** new townhomes carry GST. FTHB GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) — 100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; the New Housing Rebate alone is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **PTT and exemptions:** first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less (Apr 1, 2024), partial to $860,000, principal residence; newly built home exemption — full threshold $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000, which can apply to a never-occupied new townhouse. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **HBP / FHSA / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Page-claims and contact check:** the research file documents no jaredgibbons.ca page copy, so no credential-claim variants and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instances are documented in-file — none to flag as Jerry sweep items from this file. The correct contact email program-wide is jaredgibbons@royallepage.ca. Source: the area research file for tsawwassen-east-guide, read in full 2026-11-01.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are any east-side townhouses actually on the water?** No. The verified townhouse collections are all inside Southlands, inland of the dike around the Market District; the Boundary Bay waterfront stock is detached and duplex housing, including RD2 lots the bylaw describes as waterfront lots in Beach Grove. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Q: What is the difference between a Granary townhome and a High Street rowhome if both are "townhomes" in Southlands?** Tenure. The Granary is strata product (the presale source publishes an estimated strata fee), so it carries strata fees, bylaws, a Form B on resale and a depreciation report. The High Street Rowhomes are expressly non-strata freehold — no strata fees and no strata documents at all. Same community name, opposite diligence packages. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/the-granary-at-southlands-tsawwassen-by-century-group-presale-delta-condos-and-townhomes/ and https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/high-street-tsawwassen/
- **Q: Is a Beachcomber Cottage a townhouse?** No — it is a detached cottage on a bare-land strata lot: its own form, documented separately in Section 2 and not counted as a townhouse. A buyer comparing it to a Granary townhome is comparing two different strata forms with different common-property obligations. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/
- **Q: Can I rent out a Southlands strata townhome?** Long-term, the provincial answer governs: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and no collection-specific short-term bylaw was verified — UNCONFIRMED, read the registered bylaws / disclosure statement. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Southlands townhouse?** On a new developer sale, yes — with the Bill C-4 FTHB rebate (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000) for eligible first-time buyers and the older New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) underneath it. On a resale, no GST question arises. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How do I get out of a Southlands presale if I change my mind?** Within REDMA's 7-day rescission window, running from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, with the deposit refunded. After that window, your rights are the contract's. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: What flood rules touch a Southlands home?** SD3 is written for the Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands: a 2.9 m GSC datum minimum habitable floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — and the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike the City maintains as diking authority. Confirm the lot's SD3 application with the City. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve a Southlands address?** Address-based: the research file's sample address for the east side (570 Boundary Bay Rd) maps to South Park Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12) in the SD37 locator. Run the collection's exact address before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: What pets can I have in a Southlands townhome?** Collection by collection, and for strata collections the registered bylaws govern. No collection-specific pet bylaw was verified in a public source for The Granary, Heirloom or Centennial Green: UNCONFIRMED — read the disclosure statement / bylaws before writing an offer around a pet. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Is Centennial Green a safe name to search for listings?** It is a real named Southlands townhouse collection per the presale source, but its unit count, address and tenure are UNCONFIRMED in the sources read — verify any listing's tenure and strata plan against the disclosure statement or title rather than the collection name alone. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Southlands took a decade to approve and is still early in its build-out:** development approval was gained in 2013, sales began in 2020, and by the mid-October reporting read about 120 homes had closed with about 100 under construction out of a planned ~950 — a buyer is buying into a community that will be under construction around them for years. https://renxhomes.ca/century-group-maintains-course-on-southlands-tsawwassen
- **The City records the lands, not the marketing names:** the City of Delta records the Southlands lands as the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390, with the developer's office at 101 – 6388 Market Avenue — the file number to quote in any City inquiry, whatever collection name is on the brochure. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- **The 2.9 m number is the one to remember on this shore:** SD3 sets the habitable floor level at 2.9 metres GSC datum with a 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction and a 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — and SD3 names the Southlands in its own title. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **A seawall has its own rule here:** Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback on Boundary Bay lots along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue — and the same passage states the bylaw sets no Beach Grove flood construction level, so the FCL question goes to the permit area and the City, not the zoning text. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The farm is not scenery, it is the plan:** Southlands is built around working agriculture — the community reporting describes 275 acres of publicly owned Southlands Farm plus a 50-acre community farm inside the 537-acre community — which is why ALR stratification limits and farm-edge rules show up in a townhouse file at all. https://renx.ca/century-group-long-awaited-southlands-project-begin
- **Health care:** Delta Hospital, 5800 Mountain View Blvd in Ladner, is the nearest hospital; Fraser Health lists a South Delta After-Hours Urgent and Primary Care Centre at 1826 – 4949 Canoe Pass Way. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Delta/delta-hospital and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Service-at-Location/7/8/Service---South-Delta-After-Hours-Urgent-and-Primary-Care-Centre
- **Ferry times are published; road times are not:** BC Ferries publishes 1 hour 35 minutes Tsawwassen–Swartz Bay and 2 hours Tsawwassen–Duke Point; road commute times from the east side to Vancouver, Surrey Central or the border are UNVERIFIED. https://www.bcferries.com/current-conditions/departures?redir=301
