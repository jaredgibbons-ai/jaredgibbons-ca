## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the Pintura townhouses, and any corridor strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In this corridor the Form B is also the fastest way to prove a building is a strata at all — the rental towers in Section 2 have no Form B to give. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. The Pintura townhouse component's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so confirm its position from the Form B package (the development as a whole, apartments plus townhouses, is the strata whose report matters). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The unnamed 933 West 8th Avenue listing's "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" line is flagged on exactly this basis in Section 2 — it cannot amount to a long-term ban. Note the reverse trap: a rental *building* (The Leeward, Santa Monica) is not made buyable by this rule — there is nothing to buy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No corridor building counted in this file is published as 55+ — the Pintura townhouses' age position: UNCONFIRMED, check the Form B. The area's published 55+ building, Terraces on 7th, is Fairview's file (cross-referenced in Section 2), and its mandatory service agreement is a contract cost on top of strata fees wherever it is bought. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple side-street houses:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants or heritage designations only, stated plainly. The Heritage project's three houses are designated protected heritage property — heritage rules ride on top of whichever tenure title shows.
- **City zoning:** Vancouver's zoning is the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575. Side-street house lots sit under the citywide R1-1 multiplex regime created when By-law No. 13817 replaced the old RS districts — up to 6 strata units or up to 8 secured rental units on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule (minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 to 8 units; a house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house); the Granville Street frontage itself sits under commercial / mixed-use districts, exact schedule per parcel UNVERIFIED; a corridor strata example, The Avedon, is recorded in its listing as zoned RM-3. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / suite rules as the city states them:** the R1-1 regime above is Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit housing frame — the suite and laneway permissions and the 6-unit / 8-rental-unit multiplex ceilings are what price the side-street lots around the corridor's strata stock. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — the Broadway Plan:** the Plan covers Clark Drive to Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, was approved by Council on June 22, 2022 and updated in December 2024, and provides for a South Granville Station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station (40-storey maximum in the Granville/Burrard Slopes – Area A station-area policy). Every Section 2 address sits inside the Plan area — but the Plan is policy, not zoning: most higher-density forms still require rezoning, and purpose-built rental buildings in the corridor carry tenant-relocation implications under the Plan's renter protections. Citywide direction otherwise comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-plan-broadway.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Flood:** the corridor's north end sits above the False Creek shoreline; whether a specific parcel is in the City's designated floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset parcel by parcel. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax: 3%** of the property's assessed taxable value for homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to this corridor's second-home and investor stock. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **City taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M) + exemptions (first-time buyers — no PTT on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales (the Pintura townhouses are 2005 resales — no GST). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame for any remaining developer sales at FORM by Anthem and for future Broadway Plan presales in the corridor. - **The board's "South Granville" label reaches far past South Granville.** MLS files addresses as far south as 6160 Granville Street and 7128 Adera Street (Kerrisdale) under "South Granville" — a portal search on the sub-area name alone imports a different neighbourhood's stock into this corridor's results. The sibling south-granville condos file documents the trap with both addresses. (Sibling file: `~/workspace/local-notes/condos/south-granville-condos-2026-11-01.md`.)
- **The station is dated, not rumoured.** The Broadway Subway's South Granville Station is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027 on the Province's published date — corridor pricing already trades on that date, so a buyer should underwrite to it rather than to "someday." https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **The hospital is the corridor's quiet anchor tenant.** Vancouver General Hospital, 899 West 12th Avenue — BC's referral centre for many provincial programs and a teaching hospital — sits a few blocks east of Granville Street, a structural source of both owner and renter demand on the side streets, including the West 14th Avenue strata stock a short walk away. https://www.vch.ca/en/location/vancouver-general-hospital
- **Fairview was named in 1886 by CPR Land Commissioner L.A. Hamilton**, whose survey established the numbered east–west avenues and named the cross streets after trees — which is why the corridor's townhouse addresses read as tree names (Fir, Birch, Hemlock) between Granville and Oak. Less than 100 years ago the False Creek and Fairview Slopes area was a forest of huge fir trees. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/fairview.aspx
