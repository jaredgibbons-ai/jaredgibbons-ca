## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Strata vs fee-simple — the core distinction, stated plainly:** Lantern Park is strata. A strata townhouse buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, and gets a Form B package. A fee-simple townhouse buyer would own the lot outright: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex was verified in Aberdeen; "freehold strata" in the Lantern Park record is still strata.
- **Form B diligence (Lantern Park):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. BCREA advises reviewing it for the most recent depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work. Listing-level rental/pet lines in §2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, projecting 30 years of costs with at least three CRF funding models, not deferrable by vote. Lantern Park, at 23 homes, is inside the requirement; a sub-5-lot strata would sit UNDER that threshold — no such strata is verified in Aberdeen. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. The sibling record for Lantern Park states rentals allowed in any event — no directory "rentals not allowed" line exists for this complex to flag; the rule governs any older bylaw text a buyer is shown. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Lantern Park is expressly NOT age-restricted per the sibling file's MLS record, and no 55+ townhouse complex was verified in Aberdeen. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Why there is no more townhouse stock — agricultural zoning and the ALR:** Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014, Section 200 agricultural zones apply across rural Aberdeen (A1 minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha; A2, 16.0 ha — the lot's zone for a single parcel is shown in the City's WebMap). Rural parcels are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve; subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings need City zoning compliance and, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Floodplain status is likewise lot-specific: Zoning Bylaw Schedule E sets floodplain boundaries, and any single lot's status must be checked for that lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §4)
- **Farm-class thresholds (full three-tier form):** $2,500 for 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error wherever it appears; no such flat line is documented in this area's research file.
- **City plan / SSMUH — corrections applied:** **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted December 2025) is the current OCP** — this slug's research file words the governing plan as "Abbotsforward 2050, the updated Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2600-2016," and that 2016 number is **stale/conflicted: apply Abbotsforward 2050 and flag it** (any live-page "2500-2016" citation is likewise stale). The research file also asserts the SSMUH amendment as Bylaw No. 3560-2024, adopted June 25, 2024 — the bylaw NUMBER is **UNCONFIRMED on City pages, marked here and never asserted**; how the SSMUH rules apply to a specific Aberdeen parcel is UNVERIFIED per parcel in any event, and SSMUH addresses lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use, which is not Aberdeen's agricultural zoning. Abbotsford substance: a secondary suite is capped at **under 50%** of the principal unit, and a coach/garden suite is capped at **15% of the lot to a maximum 100 m²** (Abbotsford's rule ONLY — never import it into Township/Surrey files). https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §4)
- **Second dwellings on farm lots (the Aberdeen substitute for "more units"):** the City-hosted A1 zone section permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the Agricultural Land Commission, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². These are farm dwellings tied to the parcel, not strata townhouses, and cannot be sold as separate strata lots. Do well and septic due diligence on any acreage cross-shop: look up the well record in the provincial GWELLS application and confirm the onsite sewage system was filed under the Sewerage System Regulation by an Authorized Person through Fraser Health. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §§4, 6)

Worked example, inputs stated: a $700,000 resale townhouse with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. - **Lantern Park sits on the municipal seam:** its developer marketing describes it as "Aldergrove / Abbotsford Border" product by the Reimer Group, and a current listing calls the pocket the "Aldergrove Aberdeen area" — a buyer searching only "Aberdeen" or only "Aldergrove" can miss the sub-area's one townhouse complex. https://www.readkong.com/page/built-27640-lantern-avenue-aldergrove-abbotsford-8573656 and https://www.canadashomesearch.com/listing/r3069252-10-27640-lantern-avenue-abbotsford-british-columbia-v4x1m3-739800/brochure
- **The attached-sales count underlines the point:** Aberdeen's attached segment recorded zero sales in September 2026 (7 listings, sales ratio not reported) while its detached segment turned over 3 sales on 25 listings (12%) — too small a base to price an Aberdeen townhome from sub-area figures alone; the complex's own sales are the evidence. - **The City's agriculture work (AgRefresh) entrenches the farm pattern:** the A1 Uplands / A2 Lowlands structure with 8 ha and 16 ha subdivision minimums is the City's stated framework for areas like Aberdeen — land assembly for a townhouse project runs against both the zoning and the ALR, whose residences cannot be stratified in any event. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The airport is a building rule, not just noise:** land inside the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations area (SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500) has height limits tied to the approach, outer and transitional surfaces, and NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals are required before a City building permit — check this before planning a tall shop or barn on an acreage cross-shop. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §4)
- **Aberdeen Elementary is the area's one school and its catchment chain is settled:** the SD34 School Locator run for the school's own address (2975 Bradner Road) returns Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary, matching Eugene Reimer's published feeder pattern — but the middle school itself (3433 Firhill Dr) sits in Abbotsford West, so the chain crosses sub-areas even though the elementary does not. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §4)
- **Servicing is part of the product here:** rural lots outside the urban boundary use wells and onsite sewage instead of community water and sewer — Lantern Park's strata servicing at the urban edge is the exception in this sub-area, not the pattern, and any acreage alternative must be diligenced on its GWELLS well record and its Sewerage System Regulation filing. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/aberdeen.md, §4)
