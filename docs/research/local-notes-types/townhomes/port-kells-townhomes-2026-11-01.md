## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase — stated as the governing frame; there is no verified Port Kells strata to request one from today):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It governs any strata purchase a buyer makes instead in Guildford/Fleetwood, and any future strata product here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. A freehold Port Kells house carries no strata rental bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Port Kells strata exists to hold such a bylaw on any source checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly, because it is the local stock.** A fee-simple home carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; municipal rules (Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000) plus any registered covenants or homeowners' association govern instead. Port Kells' verified housing is exactly this form — detached freehold — with no townhouse complex of either tenure (§2).
- **City plan:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Area-level guidance is the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan; North Port Kells is primarily light industrial (IL) zoning. Surrey publishes Port Kells-specific Development Cost Charges (2025 Port Kells Industrial DCCs and Commercial DCCs, separate from city-wide rates) — a real line item for any industrial or commercial purchase with development intent. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- **ALR frame — carried in full because the research documents ALR lands woven through the South Port Kells residential pocket:** ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)) — a stratified townhouse product cannot be created on an ALR parcel, which is one structural reason this file's count is zero. ALC housing caps: a principal residence of **500 m² or less**; a secondary suite **only inside the principal residence**; an additional residence of **90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha**, under the ALC's rules or with approval. Check the ALR boundary on any south-side parcel before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- **Secondary suites (Surrey, off-ALR parcels):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one per unit, not a separate strata lot, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. ALR parcels follow the ALC rule above instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Zoning and SSMUH:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; its SSMUH framework (in force since July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — permissions that do not override ALR status on a farm parcel. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals

FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Port Kells' stock is resale freehold.
