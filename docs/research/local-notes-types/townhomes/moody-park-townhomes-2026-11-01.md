## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **There is no local strata townhouse stock to apply Form B diligence to — but the edge apartment stratas carry the full regime, and any future townhouse strata in New Westminster will too:** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line appears for a Moody Park complex in Section 2 — the Diplomat's directory line is the opposite (rentals allowed) — but any such line found on a future Form B package is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership in Moody Park — no complex here may be marketed as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's actual stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 6680) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. That is the rule set for the detached houses and any fee-simple row product a buyer meets on the park streets.
- **City zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s. 310) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and FSR 0.5, and the RS districts allow secondary suites with laneway / carriage houses regulated under the same bylaw. Townhouse Districts are the RT series: s. 410 (RT-2) states its intent "to allow townhouse development" and permits "row houses, or townhouses" at s. 410.8, with building height not to exceed 25 feet (7.62 m) at s. 410.12. **The districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist in Bylaw 6680; this research file uses no RT-A/RT-B wording, so no correction is needed in-file (the error was found and corrected in other New Westminster research files in this program).** https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf and https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf
- **Plan:** Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), governs the area; Uptown adjoining it is identified in the OCP as a Frequent Transit Development Area. The OCP's Development Permit Area 1.3 (Townhouses) guidelines state townhouse tenure may be ownership (strata titled), non-ownership (rental) or co-operative — form guidance, not a count of existing stock. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/BL_8547__DPA_Map_and_Guidelines___1_3_Townhouses___December_3__2025.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** the RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses under Bylaw 6680 (RS-1: 557.40 m² minimum site area, FSR 0.5) — pull City permits before advertising suite or laneway income. A Moody Park-specific SSMUH unit-count figure on a City source: UNCONFIRMED in the research file. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones only the city's low-lying edges — Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront and Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area; no floodplain zone for the Moody Park plateau was confirmed on a City source, and lot-level status is UNVERIFIED for any specific lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of New Westminster 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 3, 2026. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is counted in this file; where a New Westminster presale is offered, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The "townhouse" zoning exists even though the townhouse stock does not:** Bylaw 6680's Townhouse District RT-2 (s. 410) expressly permits row houses and townhouses, and the OCP carries a whole Development Permit Area (1.3) for townhouse form — a buyer waiting for new townhouse product here is waiting on rezoning and assembly, not on a missing rule. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/BL_8547__DPA_Map_and_Guidelines___1_3_Townhouses___December_3__2025.pdf
- **The park is the amenity building:** Moody Park itself holds the outdoor pool, spray park and sports fields, Moody Park Arena is at 701 8th Avenue, and the təməsew̓txʷ Aquatic and Community Centre at 65 East Sixth Avenue fully opened in May 2024 — at 10,644 sq. m. the City's largest capital project to date. https://www.newwestcity.ca/facilities and http://www.newwestcity.ca/parks-and-recreation/parks/community-parks
- **New Westminster runs its own electrical utility** — City power, water and sewer, not BC Hydro for electricity — a carrying-cost detail that surprises buyers arriving from other suburbs; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Moody Park house: UNVERIFIED. https://www.newwestcity.ca/services/whats-happening/index/main/175/site_post/page10.php
- **Heritage is a live check, not a footnote:** Moody Park is one of the neighbourhoods documented in the City's Historic Neighbourhood Context Statements, and heritage status must be checked property by property before planning renovations to an older home. https://www.newwestcity.ca/heritage/heritage-research-and-resources/sb_expander_articles/683.php
- **The replacement New Westminster Secondary School, 820 Sixth Street, opened to students on January 11, 2021** — a $106.5 million, 1,900-seat building — and SD40 operates only this one secondary school, which is why the elementary catchment (Lord Tweedsmuir, with Early French Immersion on site) is the real school differentiator on these streets. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2020-2024/2021EDUC0003-000034.htm
- **The Main Branch of New Westminster Public Library is at 716 6th Avenue**, at the area's east edge in Uptown — walking-distance civic infrastructure the townhouse nodes in newer suburbs do not have. https://www.nwpl.ca/
