## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because the verified townhouse count is zero and the north end's tenure is Park leasehold plus one freehold apartment strata, this section works in three parts: the strata rules as they apply to Lakeside and to any strata that is ever built here, the FVRD / Park Board frame that governs the lease lots, and the purchase-tax frame.

- **Form B diligence (strata frame — Lakeside, and any future strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Lakeside, the Form B and the strata plan are also where the freehold strata tenure is confirmed against the developer's "freehold waterfront land" description — the strata plan number is UNCONFIRMED in public sources opened. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, no deferral by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. Lakeside's 21 homes put it inside the five-or-more-lots depreciation-report frame (condos sibling). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No source opened states a rental restriction at Lakeside; in any case a strata rental-restriction bylaw there would be VOID — its own short-term/vacation-rental bylaw position is UNCONFIRMED, so read the bylaws before counting on vacation-rental income. For Park lease lots, the lease and Park bylaws govern rental use, not strata law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). Neither the Park lease lots nor the Lakeside condominium carries any age bylaw in the sources checked. **This frame does NOT settle Cultus Lake Village MHP's age rule** (an East-file park): a manufactured-home park's age rule is a park rule under tenancy regulation (Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Regulation, B.C. Reg. 481/2003), not a strata bylaw, and that park's rule is UNCONFIRMED (§2). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/crbc/crbc/481_2003
