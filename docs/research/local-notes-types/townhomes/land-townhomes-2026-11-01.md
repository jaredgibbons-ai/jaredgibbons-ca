## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

The townhome question on a land purchase is never "what are the strata fees" — there is no strata. It is "can a townhouse project lawfully exist on this parcel, and in what tenure." The rules run in that order.

**The ALR gate (governs much of the rural land on a feed like this).**

- **ALR residences cannot be stratified.** The Agricultural Land Commission's Information Bulletin 09 (issued October 18, 2022) treats strata plans as plans that cause subdivision (s. 6, "Strata Plans Cause Subdivision"), and depositing a strata plan is a subdivision step — so a residence in the ALR cannot be converted into strata lots / strata townhomes (ALC: https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf). The statutory frame behind it: the Agricultural Land Commission Act prohibits subdivision of agricultural land except as permitted (ALCA s. 21(1)) (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/02036_01), and a strata plan is deposited under the Strata Property Act's subdivision provisions (SPA s. 239 line of authority, as carried in this program's standing corrections).
- **ALR residence caps:** land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless it qualifies for an additional residence; a principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** in total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside the principal residence**; and one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, or 186 m² or less on larger parcels**, where local bylaws also permit it. Local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC's requirements (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
- **Checking a parcel:** ALR status is checked on the ALC's maps by PID or civic address (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps). Subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the Agricultural Land Reserve General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/).
- **BC Assessment farm classification — the full three-tier test (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met):** a producing farm must meet a minimum gross annual value of **$2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is over 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is under 0.8 ha**, under the Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s. 5 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo102/loo102/411_95). Applications must be submitted to BC Assessment **by October 31** for the following taxation year; late applications are not accepted (BC Assessment: https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification?bcgovtm=Junewebinarannouncement&TermStoreId=2e29bc59-558d-448c-9c19-d11b487dbf89&TermSetId=0a8def97-2239-4f44-b0fc-f2f10a0ea2d4&TermId=edf88474-1907-4185-b77b-5cd510f603a0). Farm class is an assessment classification, not ALR status — a parcel can be in one, both or neither, and each is checked separately.

**Zoning, SSMUH and suites — parcel by parcel, never feed-wide.**

- Off-reserve lots take their form from their municipal zone, which is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED in this file for every feed parcel (research file §5). There is no feed-wide zoning bylaw to cite: the feed crosses many municipalities, and citing one city's bylaw for the whole page would be an error. SSMUH and secondary-suite rules are likewise the rules of the municipality the parcel actually sits in, as that city states them — confirm the parcel's municipality, zone and OCP designation before any townhouse-density claim is written (research file §4).
- **Fee-simple contrast (the end state a non-strata townhouse project would have):** a fee-simple townhouse carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants / HOA only. A strata townhouse project, where zoning and (off-ALR) subdivision permit it, carries the full strata block below once the strata plan is deposited.

**The strata block (applies only after a strata plan is deposited — i.e., to the finished townhomes, never to the vacant parcel).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** 5+ lots, every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — note precisely: an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of the 3% tier **plus** that further 2%, flagged as a compression wherever met. The tax is based on fair market value (land and improvements) on the day of registration at the Land Title Office, unless an exemption applies. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a vacant-land purchase is not itself a newly built home (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand). The worked tier math is in §2.5.
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs — the condos sibling for this slug notes some secondary sources say March 12; CRA governs, and no sibling here carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). It is a new-housing rebate, not a bare-land rebate — whether a specific land-plus-build arrangement qualifies is UNCONFIRMED per transaction.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 annual. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the land page, so it is NOT asserted here. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) does NOT apply — it is Vancouver only, and this feed is province-wide, not a Vancouver page.
- **Presales / REDMA.** Where a developer sells townhomes before completion on land like this, the buyer gets a 7-day rescission under s. 21 (from the later of signing or receiving the disclosure statement) and deposits are held in trust under s. 18 (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01).

## 4. What buyers ask

**Q: Can I buy a townhouse from the land page?**
A: No — this file carries no roster and counts 0 complexes. The page sells vacant land; a townhouse exists only after a project is built, and strata townhouses only after a strata plan is deposited (§2).

**Q: Can I build strata townhouses on ALR land?**
A: No. ALR residences cannot be stratified — the ALC's Bulletin 09 treats a strata plan as a plan that causes subdivision (s. 6), and ALR subdivision needs ALC permission under ALCA s. 21(1). The ALR housing frame is one principal residence (≤500 m², suite inside it only) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m²) (§3).

**Q: I saw a flat "$2,500 farm income" line — is that the farm-class test?**
A: It is an error wherever met. The test is three-tier: $2,500 for a 0.8–4 ha operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — and the application deadline is October 31 (B.C. Reg. 411/95, s. 5; §3).

**Q: What transfer tax do I pay on an $800,000 parcel?**
A: On the worked example in §2.5 (no exemption, $800,000 fair market value): $2,000 (1% tier) + $12,000 (2% tier) = $14,000. GST, if it applies to that parcel at all, is separate and UNVERIFIED per parcel (§2.5/§3).

**Q: Is every card on this page in the Fraser Valley?**
A: No. The first cards read include Quesnel, Fort St. John, Gibsons and Squamish alongside Langley, Surrey and Mission parcels — confirm the parcel's actual municipality before treating it as a Fraser Valley option (§1).

**Q: Does the first-time buyer GST rebate apply to the land itself?**
A: The Bill C-4 rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) is a new-housing rebate, not a bare-land rebate; whether a land-plus-build arrangement qualifies is UNCONFIRMED per transaction and needs CRA-based advice (§3).

**Q: If I build fee-simple townhouses instead of strata, what changes?**
A: Tenure and diligence: fee-simple rows carry no strata, no Form B and no strata fees — title, municipal rules and any registered covenants/HOA only. Strata townhouses carry the full strata block in §3, including depreciation reports for 5+ lots and the 10% CRF minimum (§3).

**Q: A card says a lot is "ready to build" or had rezoning work — can I rely on it?**
A: No. That is listing-agent copy until it is checked against the in-force municipal zoning and, for ALR parcels, the ALC rules for that PID (research file §6: https://jaredgibbons.ca/land.html).

**Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?**
A: On the ALC's maps, by PID or civic address (https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps). Do it before pricing any build plan — reserve status controls the residence caps and whether subdivision needs Commission permission (§3).

**Q: Are pad-rental manufactured-home parks a cheap way into townhouse-style living from a land search?**
A: They are a different tenure entirely and are never townhouse stock in this program: you rent the pad under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act and hold no strata lot or fee-simple land interest (§2.4).
