## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Why this section exists in a condo-band file:** the rules below are the townhome-series frame a buyer comparing a sub-$800K Ladner condo against a Ladner townhouse actually needs — stated once here, applied per complex in the home files cross-referenced in Section 2.
- **Form B diligence (strata purchases, condo or townhouse):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with rules, current budget and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. No Ladner building's bylaws were read for the research file, so every building-level age, pet and rental claim on the live page is UNVERIFIED until the registered bylaws are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years (sub-5-lot stratas sit UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the operating budget must go to the CRF. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID / age bylaws 55+ only:** rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 — flag every directory or page "rentals not allowed" line void in-entry; only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The live page's 55+ and rental claims for Ladner buildings are UNVERIFIED per building (see Section 2) — the provincial frame is settled, the building application is not. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Fee-simple contrast, stated plainly:** Ladner's one verified fee-simple townhouse-form product, 48 Landing (Ladner Elementary file), has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple product is verified inside this condo band. (Section 2 above)
- **City frame:** Delta OCP = consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), NO lettered schedules. Delta's ZONING bylaw number is CONTESTED (City pages cite No. 7600, 2017 vs an internal title "8400, 2025") — flagged, never asserted. Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 + 8395 were adopted June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — detached-stock context, not a strata rule. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Flood frame for the whole band:** the City operates over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure (dikes, seawalls, foreshore protection), 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure; all Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — for a strata purchase this surfaces in the depreciation report and insurance certificate, not the listing text. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **PTT in this band:** general PTT 1% to $200,000 and 2% to $2,000,000 — every price in this band is taxed entirely in those two tiers; the 3% tier and the further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 do not arise, and a page's "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of 3% + 2%, flagged as such. First-time buyers: full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less (exemption on the first $500,000), partial to $860,000 — in principle usable anywhere in this band, subject to all other program conditions. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Rebates and programs:** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031; new construction only); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. GST does not apply to resales. Delta taxes were due July 2, 2026 (5% after, further 5% after September 2, 2026). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: How many Ladner condos are under $800,000 right now?** No source used in this program can say: SnapStats publishes no South Delta figures, no price-band cut and no condo-only cut, and no official source publishes a Ladner condo count. - **Q: Is a townhouse ever part of this band?** Not as this page is built — it is an apartment-condo feed (cards labelled Hawthorne, Ladner Elementary, Delta Manor). A sub-$800K Ladner townhouse belongs to the townhome band file in this series; this file counts zero townhouses. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $800K?** In principle, yes — the full-exemption ceiling is a fair market value of $835,000, exempting the first $500,000 of price, with a partial exemption to $860,000 — provided every buyer-side and property condition is met, file by file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: What is the PTT on a $700,000 Ladner condo?** On that stated input: $12,000 before exemptions (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $500,000). It is arithmetic on an input, not a market figure. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are the Hampton Cove buildings (Shorewinds, Riverstone) verified options in this band?** UNCONFIRMED — those names and the Hampton Club appear only in the live page's own copy; no developer or strata source was read for them. Do not shortlist a building on page copy alone; verify it in its strata documents. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-7/ladner-condos-under-800k.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve a condo in this band?** SD37, address-based: 5500 Admiral Boulevard → Neilson Grove Elementary + Delta Secondary; 4600 53 Street → Ladner Elementary + Delta Secondary. Every other address must be run in the locator itself. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

The registered bylaws in the Form B package govern the specifics. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
