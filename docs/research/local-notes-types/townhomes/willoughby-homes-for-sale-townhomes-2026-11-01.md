## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the 7 strata complexes in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. Westbrooke's fee-simple row homes have no Form B at all — the contrast is the point (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: the home file flags the directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace as VOID for long-term rentals on exactly this rule (§2.4). Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw; municipally, the Township does NOT issue business licences for short-term rental accommodations, per its own online business-licence application conditions (research file §4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Willow Park Estates is the area's one verified 55+ complex; Highgate Terrace's unstated-age restriction is UNCONFIRMED and, if its registered bylaw sets any age other than 55, it cannot be valid in that form today (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** Westbrooke (and Brighton West as a presale, §2.3) carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Township zoning, SSMUH, suites, coach houses and plans — standing corrections applied.**

The compact-lot pattern is **R-CL, s. 403: minimum lot area 220 m², maximum 464.5 m²**, with minimum frontages of 11 m (front-loaded) and 8.2 m (rear-loaded). **Q: Is Cascadia a condo or a townhouse?**

**Q: Can I rent out a Willoughby townhouse long-term?**
A: Yes — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC, including the directory lines flagged void in §2.4/§3. Short-term rental is different: the Township does not issue business licences for STR accommodations, and a strata can still restrict STRs by bylaw (§3).

**Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse in Willoughby?**
A: One verified: Willow Park Estates (6467 197 St), a strata complex counted in the home file. Highgate Terrace (19645 64 Ave) is apartment-form with an age restriction whose exact age is UNCONFIRMED — it is not a verified 55+ complex and not a townhouse (§2.4).

**Q: What are the compact-lot rules in Willoughby?**
A: Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 403 (R-CL): minimum lot area 220 m², maximum 464.5 m², minimum frontages 11 m front-loaded and 8.2 m rear-loaded. There is no 355 m² figure in s. 403. The pattern changes garage, parking and lane expectations — verify which pattern a given townhouse sits on (§3; research file §7).

**Q: Can a Willoughby townhouse have a suite, or a coach house added?**
A: A coach house is a fee-simple s. 102 unit (second storey of a detached garage) — never strata, never a townhouse complex. For suites: s. 102 caps a secondary suite at 120 m², one per principal unit, permit required — but whether a townhouse suite is permitted at all is a live conflict between the s. 102 reading and a January 2025 Township guide, flagged in §3 and resolved nowhere: confirm with the Township for the specific lot (§3).

**Q: Has the Willoughby plan update changed what can be built beside me?**
A: Not yet on the record in this file: Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 only, with a hearing directed for June 15, 2026. A source presenting June 15, 2026 adoptions as done is UNVERIFIED — the governing plan today is Bylaw 1836 as Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025) (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve a Willoughby townhouse?**
A: It depends on the address. The recorded SD35 locator run for 7888 200 Street returns Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, Peter Ewart Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary; schools physically in Willoughby include Willoughby, Lynn Fripps and Richard Bulpitt elementaries and Yorkson Creek Middle School.
