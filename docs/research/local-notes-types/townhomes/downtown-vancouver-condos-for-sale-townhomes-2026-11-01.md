## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to any downtown strata purchase, including a podium townhouse unit inside a tower strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The registered bylaws are the diligence, not feed-card copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — rentals are allowed for long-term rentals anywhere in BC. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw, and Vancouver has its own short-term rental rules; feed-card claims that Airbnb/short-term rentals are permitted in a specific building are UNVERIFIED listing copy in this file (research file §5) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No downtown building cross-referenced in §2 is published as age-restricted in its home file — UNCONFIRMED either way until the registered bylaws are read on the Form B (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Vancouver zoning, taxes and the Empty Homes Tax.**

- **Zoning:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the core — permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan, including commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses; CD-1 and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area (research file §4; https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf).
- **Property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026 (research file §4; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx).
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax rate is **3%** of assessed taxable value. Every Vancouver owner must also submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date (research file §4; https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx). Carried openly: this file's research file itself left the current rate UNVERIFIED in its pass — the 3% rate is carried here as the program's standing Vancouver position, and the research-file gap is listed in §5. The Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply outside the City of Vancouver.
- **Additional school tax:** the Province's additional school tax can apply at downtown price points — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027 (research file §4; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; no sibling for this slug carries March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion above $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. **Worked examples (general PTT, before any exemption):** $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
- **HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%** (CMHC homeowner premium schedule). No CMHC page error is asserted for this slug — this file's research does not document the 2.80%–4.00% error for the downtown-vancouver-condos-for-sale page, so it is NOT documented for this slug and NOT asserted here.
- **Presales / REDMA.** 7-day rescission under s. 21 and deposits held in trust under s. 18 — the frame for any unit still on a developer contract (https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01). No presale is counted in this file.

The page carries NO editorial guide, byline/credential block or FAQ, so no credential-claim variant against the owner-approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales) is documented for this slug, and no wrong-email instance is documented (see §1 FILE STATUS).
