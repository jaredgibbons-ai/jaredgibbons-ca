## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to Monterra in its count home — strata townhome stock).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. Monterra's unit count is UNCONFIRMED in its count home, so its threshold position is read off strata plan LMS469, not a page (count home §2). **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §2.1: Monterra's data-sheet "Rentals Not Allowed" line is void for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex is counted on this ground (§2.2).

**Q: Can I rent out a Monterra home?**
A: Long-term, yes in law: its data-sheet "Rentals Not Allowed" line is a strata rental-restriction bylaw, void province-wide since November 24, 2022. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw (§3).

**Q: Is there a market median for Boundary Beach townhomes?**
A: No published one this program can state: SnapStats covers North Delta only, not South Delta, so no Boundary Beach median, inventory or sales figure exists in the sources used here — and SnapStats never publishes a townhome-only figure in any case (§1).

**Q: What flood rules touch a Boundary Beach home?**
A: Hazard DPA SD3 sets a 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor level, a 2.3 m minimum land elevation and a 7.5 m tidal setback, behind the Boundary Bay dike Delta maintains. Whether a specific parcel is inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot (§3).

**Q: Which schools serve Boundary Beach?**
A: Address-based in SD37. The recorded sample for 570 Boundary Bay Rd is South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary; Beach Grove Elementary is the other elementary on this side of Tsawwassen. Run the exact address in the locator (§1; https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/).

**Q: Who owns the beach in front of a waterfront home?**
A: The Province — nearly all saltwater foreshore is Crown land, and docks or moorage sit on tenure by lease or licence via FrontCounter BC (§2.5; https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage).

**Q: Is the guide page's Centennial Beach address right?**
A: No — that is a documented defect in this file's own research: the guide states "300 Boundary Bay Road"; the official address is 570 Boundary Bay Rd (§1; https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park).
