## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

Because this file counts zero townhouse complexes, this section carries the rules in the order a Ladner Rural buyer actually meets them: the ALR/farm frame first (as this file's research documents it), then the standard strata/fee-simple and tax blocks that would govern any townhouse-form purchase if one were ever verified here.

- **ALR stratification bar — the threshold rule for this sub-area.** An ALR residence cannot be stratified (Agricultural Land Commission Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). That is the legal reason farm land in Ladner Rural does not produce strata townhouse product: the farm house is a single freehold dwelling, not a strata lot. Sources: ALC Bulletin 09 and the ALCA, as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Check ALR status parcel by parcel.** A parcel's Agricultural Land Reserve status is checked on the Agricultural Land Commission maps by PID or civic address; the ALC states its online tools are for general reference only, with official confirmation from its Mapping and GIS team. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Farm-class thresholds — in full three-tier form.** $2,500 in farm sales on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the land value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; no such line is documented in this file's research. Sources: BC Assessment farm-classification material as carried in the program's standing corrections, 2026-11-01. Note: this file's own research could not re-open the BC Assessment source after a fetch-service 429 rate-limit on 2026-11-01 and marked farm-class treatment UNVERIFIED in its own pages — the three-tier form above is the program's standing verified form, carried here as such. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/ladner-rural.md)
- **A1 farm house limits.** The A1 zone permits one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; the farm home plate maximum is 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). Farming is a principal use; accessory uses include a farm house accessory to farming, one secondary suite accessory to a farm house, an accessory farm dwelling unit, migrant farm worker housing, and equestrian facilities to a maximum of 40 permanent stalls accessory to farming. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 second dwellings.** One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot: maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house; ALC approval is required for more than one. An additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha, 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 subdivision.** In the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is not permitted except where it complies with the Official Community Plan Bylaw and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable; the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Migrant farm worker housing in A1** is permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha; new buildings for it must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Delta plan and zoning.** The OCP is the consolidated Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — NO lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule; Development Permit Areas live in the OCP's Appendix B; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)," whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands. **Zoning bylaw number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City zone sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal document title reads "8400, 2025"); the A1 zone sheet itself is cited above without asserting the bylaw number. **SSMUH:** Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — SSMUH permissions do not override ALR/ALC controls on farm land. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Water, drainage and flood.** The City of Delta operates and maintains 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; in the agricultural area the ditches and pump stations function in drainage mode in winter and irrigation mode in summer, with control gate structures regulating water levels. The City also operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure — dikes, seawalls and foreshore protection. Parcel-level flood construction levels for any Ladner Rural parcel are UNVERIFIED — property-specific and not confirmed for any parcel in this pass. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **If a strata townhouse were ever verified here — the standard block.** Form B diligence under Strata Property Act s. 59 (fees, amounts owing, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports every five years for stratas of five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); CRF contribution of at least 10% of the annual operating budget since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (any directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry); only 55+ age bylaws valid since Nov 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Fee-simple townhomes, stated plainly:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA documents only. That is the tenure frame that actually fits Ladner Rural's inland parcels: freehold land governed by the A1 zone, the OCP and ALC controls, not by any strata.
- **Float-home tenure warning.** For a West River Road float home, diligence the tenure before the lifestyle: establish what is actually owned (the float, the slip, any lease or strata interest), what the moorage costs and rules are, and how financing and insurance treat a float home — none of that is established by listing text, and no source for the Canoe Pass community's structure is carried in the research. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-rural.html
- **GST / rebates.** 5% GST applies to new construction only, not resales or farmland sales as such — a buyer's GST position on a farm purchase is a legal-professional question, UNCONFIRMED in this file's research. FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — 100% of GST to $1M (maximum $50,000), phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031; New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million (a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" would be a compression of that 3% + 2% structure — no such row is documented in this file's research). First-time buyers' exemption: full on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly built home exemption: full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. Farm and mixed-use PTT treatment is property-specific and UNCONFIRMED in this file's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is documented in Ladner Rural in this research. Where a presale exists anywhere, the buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18, deposits held in trust) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21). https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Down-payment programs / CMHC:** Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per person; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC homeowner premiums 0.6%–4.5% — residential programs; their application to a float home or a farm purchase is UNCONFIRMED in this file's research. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
