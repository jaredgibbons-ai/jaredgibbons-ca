## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

There is no townhouse stock here, so this section is (a) the fee-simple position that describes the stock that actually exists, (b) the flood and dike rules that are this sub-area's real diligence layer for any housing form, and (c) the standard BC strata and tax rules a buyer needs for River West or for the townhouse complexes one sub-area east. **Batch-8 verification note, stated plainly:** several City of Delta flood/streamside/facility pages cited in the guide file could not be re-opened in the batch-8 pass (fetch-service 429 on 2026-11-01) and are UNVERIFIED *in the batch-8 research file*; the entries below carry them on the guide file's basis, labelled as such, because the guide pass did read them — the two positions are both stated rather than one silently overwriting the other.

- **Fee-simple is the tenure that exists here — plainly.** A detached house or half duplex in Port Guichon is fee-simple: NO strata corporation, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title (including the flood covenants below), zoning and municipal flood-construction requirements. (Guide-file basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/port-guichon-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Flood and dike rules are the local overlay (guide-file basis).** Port Guichon is low land on the Fraser River side of Ladner behind the City's dike system; Delta operates and maintains over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure, and directs owners to DeltaMap for parcel-level flood information. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection
- **Flood covenants on title (guide-file basis).** In Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street, the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — the pattern a buyer on river-side lots here should search title for, on a house purchase exactly as on a strata one. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Streamside rules (guide-file basis).** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on adding a suite, coach house or any ground-oriented unit on a Port Guichon lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024.** A single consolidated plan — NO lettered schedules and no Ladner area-plan schedule (former area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950); farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither number is asserted as settled here; the zone on any parcel is read off the City's zoning maps. The batch-8 pass could not re-open the Bylaw 7600 zone PDFs (429), so the guide file's zone table is not restated in this variant. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/port-guichon.md)
- **SSMUH / suites as Delta states them — the only route to ground-oriented added units here.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with City support for coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites; the live feed already shows coach-house half duplexes on Savoy Street (listing copy, feed evidence only). SSMUH product is fee-simple in form unless separately strata-registered — do not market it as a townhouse complex. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Strata rules (for River West, and for townhouses east in Ladner).** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59): discloses monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days of request. Since July 1, 2024 a strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years and cannot vote to defer it (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold); since November 1, 2023 the CRF must receive a minimum of 10% of the annual operating fund each year. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental and age bylaws (strata).** Every BC strata rental-restriction bylaw has been invalid since Nov 24, 2022 — a directory "rentals not allowed" line is void in-entry. Under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1 and 123.2 only a 55+ age bylaw is valid, with exemptions (expanded May 1, 2023) for legacy residents, live-in caregivers, a younger spouse or partner, and defined children of a qualifying resident. No Port Guichon building is verified as 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **GST and rebates (new construction only).** Resale homes carry no GST. On a new home, the first-time home buyers' GST/HST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, for agreements in the March 20, 2025–2031 window; the older GST New Housing Rebate is 36% of the GST, maximum $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property transfer tax and buyer programs.** General PTT is 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. The first-time buyers' exemption removes the tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing out to $860,000, and the newly built home exemption is full to $1,100,000, phasing out by $1,150,000. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,000,000 fee-simple Port Guichon house with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $800,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. The Home Buyers' Plan allows up to $60,000 per person from RRSPs, the First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime limit, and CMHC homeowner premiums run 0.6%–4.5% of the loan. No CMHC defect is asserted for any property here — no file's own research documents one. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Presales (REDMA).** No presale project inside Port Guichon is verified, so REDMA is background only: where a presale exists, the developer must deliver a disclosure statement and s. 21 gives a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it, with deposits held in trust under s. 18. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Delta property taxes.** The 2026 due date was July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026 — this item WAS re-verified in the batch-8 pass. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
