## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These are the provincial and city rules that will govern any Chineside strata purchase if and when stock trades here; there is no in-area townhouse complex for them to attach to today. The live density rule in this sub-area today is the SSMUH rule for house lots, included because it is what actually changes hands here. **No Vancouver rule appears in this section — the Empty Homes Tax does not exist in Coquitlam, and no Chilliwack rule appears either; this is a Coquitlam file.**

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata of four or fewer lots sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals — and a directory "rentals allowed w/restrictions" line, as in the excluded Harbour Place Estates entry in Section 2, is flagged the same way: void as a long-term restriction, in-entry, wherever it appears. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 — spouse/partner, caregiver and legacy exemptions). No 55+ townhouse complex is verified in Chineside; the sibling 55-plus file on disk carries the area's age-restricted position. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here as a named complex. The standing rule, stated plainly: a fee-simple townhouse or row home has NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only. Chineside's detached houses and their suites/carriage homes are bought on exactly that basis.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 governs; small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 set subdivision minimums of 650 m² with 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² with 12.5 m width (R-2). The City adopted an Interim Official Community Plan on April 27, 2026 (Bylaw 5511, 2026 and Bylaw 5512, 2026), superseding the Citywide Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3479, 2001, with a future OCP update project anticipated to begin in 2027. A Chineside neighbourhood plan: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** Coquitlam's small-scale multi-unit housing rules allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer fall outside the SSMUH rules, so property-level septic or well status for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 30 metres of the top of bank or top of ravine bank of any watercourse in this part of Coquitlam; Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks from watercourses and s.523 covers riparian areas. ALR status for this sub-area: UNVERIFIED — and the standing ALR rule is noted for completeness only: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)), so any future "strata townhouse" claim on ALR land in this area would fail on that rule alone. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** payment was due July 2, 2026 for the January 1 – December 31, 2026 year, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance of current taxes after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants). These are Coquitlam's dates — not Chilliwack's and not Vancouver's. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Taxes and rebates (any new-build purchase):** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K phase-out; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
