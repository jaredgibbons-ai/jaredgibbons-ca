## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

**The strata block (applies to the Bromley presale strata on completion and to any Brookswood strata townhome).**

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. In Brookswood this document route applies to a townhouse strata such as Bromley once it completes — it does NOT apply to the pad-rental parks in §2, which have no strata corporation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (Bromley's 26 homes put it over the threshold once complete). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. That voiding does not touch the parks in §2: a park's "rentals not allowed" line is a park rule under a tenancy regime, not a strata bylaw, and it is not voided by the strata change — flagged here so the two lines are never read as one rule. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful in strata (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Brookswood strata carries a verified age bylaw — the area's 55+ rules live in its parks, under park rules, not the SPA. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhome (the queued Oxford Mews form) would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, Township rules and registered covenants/HOA only. None is counted in Brookswood (§2).
