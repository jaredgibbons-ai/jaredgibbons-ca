## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally — there is no counted strata townhouse here to apply it to):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. It applies the moment a buyer crosses into a strata neighbourhood (Queensborough, the Quay, Uptown) — it does not apply to a Connaught Heights house purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports / CRF (provincial strata frame, stated for cross-shopping buyers):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory line arises in this file because no complex is counted. For a Connaught Heights house, suite rental is a municipal question (see zoning below), not a strata question. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful in a strata (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions, expanded May 1, 2023). There is no strata in this sub-area to carry one — the sibling 55-plus file verified no 55+ housing of any tenure in Connaught Heights. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple position (the form that actually exists here):** a Connaught Heights house is fee-simple — NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title, Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning — CORRECTED:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s. 310) — RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) and a floor space ratio of 0.5; the RS districts allow secondary suites, and laneway / carriage houses are regulated under the same bylaw. **Townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only; RT-A/RT-B do not exist** — the research file's RT-A/RT-B wording is corrected here (see the top-of-file flag). The bylaw's s. 410 "(RT-2) Townhouse Districts" schedule states the district's intent is to allow townhouse development and permits row houses or townhouses. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf (research file, as corrected)
- **Plan:** "Our City 2041," the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, governs the area, and the 22nd Street Station area is identified in it as a Frequent Transit Development Area — one of the areas where the City directs higher-density, transit-supportive growth. Confirm the exact OCP designation for a station-adjacent lot with the City before pricing in redevelopment value. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports (research file)
- **SSMUH / suites as the city states them:** in the RS districts as the research file states them — secondary suites allowed, laneway / carriage houses regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, within the RS-1 minimum site area (557.40 m²) and 0.5 FSR frame. Parcel-level eligibility: UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it for the specific lot. (Research file §4)
- **Flood:** the City's floodplain study zones only the low-lying edges (Zone 1 Queensborough, Zone 2 west-end Fraser River industrial land, Zone 3 the Quay waterfront, Zone 4 the Brunette River industrial area); no floodplain zone for Connaught Heights' residential streets was confirmed — lot-level status: UNVERIFIED, check with the City. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf (research file)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there really no townhouses in Connaught Heights?** No named townhouse complex was verified by name and tenure in any source checked — research file, both sibling files and a fresh search agree, and SnapStats recorded 0 condos & townhomes listings for the sub-area in September 2026. Individual townhouse-form houses exist; a complex does not, on the verified record. Basis: §2 above.
- **Q: Where do Connaught Heights downsizers buy townhouses instead?** Across the Queensborough Bridge in Queensborough (for example Coopers Landing, 935 Ewen Avenue; Mercer Village, 388 Duncan Street) — a different board sub-area, documented in the sibling condos file as out-of-area references, not as Connaught Heights stock. Basis: sibling condos file §2.
- **Q: Is a Connaught Heights house a strata purchase?** No — the stock is fee-simple houses: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees. Diligence is title, Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 and registered covenants. (Research file §5)
- **Q: What are RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts?** They do not exist in New Westminster — that wording in the research file is corrected in this file. The bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B; s. 410 (RT-2) permits row houses or townhouses. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf
- **Q: Can a house here add a suite or a laneway house?** The RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (RS-1: 557.40 m² minimum site area, 0.5 FSR). Pull the City permits and confirm the specific lot before advertising the potential. (Research file §4)
- **Q: What does the station-area designation mean for a house near 22nd Street Station?** The OCP makes the station area a Frequent Transit Development Area, directing higher-density, transit-supportive growth there — lots near the station carry a different planning future than the interior streets. Confirm the exact designation with the City before pricing it in. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports (research file)
- **Q: What sold here in September 2026?** Nothing, in either segment — 15 detached listings with 0 solds and no attached listings at all. Pricing evidence has to come from the wider New Westminster community (detached median $1,365,000; combined condos & townhomes median $560,000) and adjoining areas. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Connaught Heights address?** SD40, address-based: the verified locator run for 2201 London Street returned Connaught Heights Elementary, Fraser River Middle and New Westminster Secondary, with Montessori offered at Connaught Heights Elementary itself. Run the exact address. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/ (research file)
- **Q: Is the area in a floodplain?** No floodplain zone for the residential streets was confirmed in the City's floodplain study (its zones cover Queensborough, the west-end industrial land, the Quay and the Brunette River industrial area) — lot-level status is UNVERIFIED and is a City check, not an assumption. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf (research file)
- **Q: Is there any 55+ housing here if I want to stay in the west end?** None verified of any tenure — no 55+ strata, rental, retirement residence or subsidized seniors building with a Connaught Heights address appears in the sibling 55-plus file. The in-area option is a detached house (with a suite or laneway potential), not an age-restricted complex. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §2.
