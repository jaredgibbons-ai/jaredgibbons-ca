## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every Port Moody strata townhome, counted or queued).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A queued complex's registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry: Westhill Place's and Boardwalk at Klahanie's directory lines both carry a restriction element that is **void for long-term rentals**; rentals are allowed, and the lines are carried only with that flag. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Port Moody's own STR bylaw/licence position is UNVERIFIED — no City STR source was read in this file's research (research file §4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Port Moody complex is verified to carry one (§2.4) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhome would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Port Moody zoning, OCP, SSMUH and taxes.**

- **OCP — current bylaw, stated as a standing correction: Port Moody Official Community Plan Bylaw 2026, No. 3522, adopted April 28, 2026, which repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — 2955 is NEVER cited as current.** Chapter 14 covers the city by neighbourhood (14.2 Pleasantside and April Road; 14.3 the Heritage Mountain grouping; 14.4 Ioco Lands; 14.5 Inlet Centre and Coronation Park; 14.6 Moody Centre, including the Moody Centre Station Transit-Oriented Area). The board's "Port Moody Centre" sub-area is not itself an OCP neighbourhood name (research file §4; https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733).
- **Zoning / SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2937 puts single detached homes in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones, with mixed-use in TC and CRM zones at the centre; small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas, and the transit-oriented areas are exempt from the SSMUH rules. The College Park condos sibling states the amendment figures as up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m² and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops (Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460). Watercourse lots need a riparian assessment and Zoning Bylaw Section 5.4 setbacks, and environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on the hillsides. Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas, where a heritage alteration permit can be required (research file §4; https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/) (`~/workspace/local-notes/condos/college-park-pm-condos-2026-11-01.md`).
- **Property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities are billed separately, with the 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026 (research file §4; https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/).

**Purchase taxes, rebates and financing.**

- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122 (March 13 governs; the College Park condos sibling notes some secondary sources say March 12 — CRA governs, and that sibling does not carry March 12 as its own date): 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html). GST applies to new construction, not resales.
- **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is flagged as a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure wherever met. **Worked examples, inputs stated, no exemption:** $700,000 → $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → $2,000 + $20,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
