# Southwest Maple Ridge, Maple Ridge — Townhomes: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No sold prices, no listing addresses beyond the named complexes themselves. Building-directory, listing and presale sites are used only as secondary sources and are attributed as such.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Southwest Maple Ridge" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only; the City of Maple Ridge does not publish a boundary or a plan for a neighbourhood under that name. The official planning frame is the Hammond Area Plan (OCP s. 10.5): "The Hammond neighbourhood is located in the southwest corner of Maple Ridge. It is bounded on the west by the City of Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation, and on the south by the Fraser River; to the north are Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas, and to the east is mainly single-family development." The City's neighbourhoods page also lists Port Hammond and Port Haney in this part of the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan and https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Silver Valley, which has its own City area plan, is a separate MLS sub-area in the northeast of Maple Ridge — it is not part of Southwest Maple Ridge, and its townhouse complexes (for example Windfall) are not this sub-area's stock. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods and https://www.zolo.ca/maple-ridge-real-estate/23183-136-avenue/52
- Main roads and transit: Lougheed Highway runs along the north side; two West Coast Express stations bracket the sub-area — Maple Meadows Station (20010 Dunn Ave) on the west side and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) at the east edge, with weekday trains between Waterfront (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. The 743 and 744 buses loop through Hammond streets (207 Street, Lorne Avenue, Hammond) between Meadowtown and Haney Place via Maple Meadows Station; the 701 runs along Dewdney Trunk Road at the north edge and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs on the Lougheed corridor north of the area. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- The Golden Ears Bridge at the area's west side connects Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows to Langley and Surrey; the City states Golden Ears Way will be expanded from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, with detailed design underway. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/maintenance-and-upgrade-program/bridge-projects and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge
- Schools physically inside the sub-area: Hammond Elementary (K–7), 11520 203 Street; Maple Ridge Elementary (K–7), 20820 River Road; and Westview Secondary (8–12), 20905 Wicklund Avenue. SD42 has no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12). https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042 and https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools
- **School catchments — locator status carried exactly as documented:** SD42 catchments are address-based, and the district locator was run in the underlying research for the Hammond sample address 20577 Lorne Avenue (this sub-area) and returned NO RESULT, because the address falls in a gap between the locator's listed ranges for Lorne Avenue. Catchment schools for this sub-area are therefore NOT confirmed — no catchment is asserted in this file for any address, and any specific address must be run in the SD42 School Locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Parks and facilities: Hammond Park, 20577 Lorne Avenue, has benches, a playground, a sport court, a tennis court and a pickleball court; Hammond Community Park is the site of the Larry Walker and Hammond Stadium ballfields, Hammond Hall and the Hammond Outdoor Pool; the Maple Ridge Golf Course is at 20818 Golf Lane. No City recreation centre or library branch sits in the southwest — the Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place in the Town Centre to the east, and Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Hammond-Park-58 and https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre
- Market context (SnapStats, combined segment only — never a townhome-only figure): the sub-area (board name "Southwest," Maple Ridge) recorded Detached 41 active / 9 sold / 22% sales ratio and Condos & Townhomes 8 active / 3 sold / 38% sales ratio in September 2026; Maple Ridge community-level Condos & Townhomes median was $525,000 that month, and SnapStats publishes no Southwest-only median — as of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/southwest-maple-ridge.md).
- Health care: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, is the community hospital for Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows with 24/7 emergency care; the Ridge Meadows Urgent and Primary Care Centre is at 121 – 11900 Haney Place, open 7 days a week, 9:00 a.m.–8:00 p.m. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/Service-Directory/Locations/Maple-Ridge---Pitt-Meadows/ridge-meadows-hospital and https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/clinic/ridge-meadows-urgent-and-primary-care-centre

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

With zero verified townhouse complexes, this section records the rules that will govern any strata townhouse purchase here if stock appears (including under the Hammond and Lougheed corridor plans below), the fee-simple contrast, and the land rules that shape the area.

- **Strata diligence — the Form B regime (any future strata townhouse):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. BCREA advises reviewing the Form B for the depreciation report, financial statements, CRF balance and planned work to identify future special levies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; stratas with four or fewer lots are exempt. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Any directory or listing line stating "rentals not allowed" for a strata here is void in-entry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1, 123.2), with Regulation 7.01 exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 (legacy residents, live-in caregivers, younger spouses/partners, defined children). Val Maria's 55+ restriction is a manufactured-home park tenancy rule, not a strata bylaw, and is not governed by these sections. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast:** no fee-simple townhouse complex is verified here. A fee-simple townhouse/rowhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence runs on title, municipal records and any registered covenants or homeowners' association documents only. The sub-area's confirmed form today is freehold single-detached housing, with suites and garden suites as accessory uses in RS-1. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **City zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs. The standard house zone is RS-1 (s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², width 18.0 m, depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%); accessory uses include Secondary Suite Residential and Detached Garden Suite Residential. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED, flagged, never resolved:** the Official Community Plan citation is contested in the program record (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024). This file cites neither number as settled; confirm the current OCP bylaw on the City's site before quoting it. The Hammond Area Plan (OCP s. 10.5) has been given final reading and adopted by Council, and its update enables mixed-use, higher-density buildings in the plan area — the route by which real townhouse stock could appear here. A Hammond Development Permit Area (heritage) applies to properties within the plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor (the sub-area's north edge):** the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026 per the City record — Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted at the January 27, 2026 Council meeting, following the January 20, 2026 public hearing; the corridor runs from the City's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, with growth directed around future Bus Rapid Transit stations at 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street. The City states the plan does not immediately change any property's zoning. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Suites and SSMUH:** one secondary suite per lot inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, and not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Floodplain:** the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Escarpment:** the Fraser River Escarpment policy area (City Policies 6.23 and 6.24) is bounded by 207 Street west, 124 Avenue north, 224 Street east and the escarpment crest along the south; drainage from new construction must connect to the City storm system, properties within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit, properties within 100 m require a detailed report on seismic slope failure, septic fields are to be avoided and new residences require sanitary sewers. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Buyer frame (applies to any townhouse purchase here):** FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% of GST to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; PTT with the First-Time Home Buyers' exemption (no PTT on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000); Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. No presales are verified in this sub-area, so the REDMA s. 21 7-day rescission / s. 18 trust-deposit frame has no live project to attach to here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

- **Q: Is Val Maria a townhouse complex I can buy into like a strata?** No. Val Maria (21163 Lougheed Hwy) is a 55+ manufactured home park — listings record "Leasehold not prepaid-NonStrata" ownership, meaning you own the home and rent the pad. There is no strata, no Form B, no depreciation report and no CRF; it is never counted as townhouse stock. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3098427-21163-lougheed-highway-52-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-2r4/brochure

- **Q: Which school catchment is a Hammond address in?** Not confirmed for this sub-area. - **Q: Will townhouses ever be built in Hammond?** The planning door is open in two places: the adopted Hammond Area Plan update enables mixed-use, higher-density buildings in the plan area, and the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan (adopted Jan 27, 2026) directs growth around future BRT stations at 203, 207, Laity and 216 Streets — but the City states the corridor plan does not immediately change zoning, and no townhouse project in the sub-area was verified as built or selling in the sources checked. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Hammond house instead of buying a townhouse?** On a standard lot, one secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area) is permitted — but NOT on a floodplain property: Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation on floodplain lots. Eligible lots may also add up to three, four or six SSMUH units. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: How flood- and slope-exposed is this sub-area?** Parcel-specific. The river edge carries a 6.7 m flood construction level, and the escarpment policy area requires a geotechnical covenant within 300 m of the crest and a detailed seismic report within 100 m, with septic fields to be avoided and sewers required for new residences. Check the specific lot against the City's floodplain and escarpment material during subject removal. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 and http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Q: What is the commute picture if I buy here?** West Coast Express stations bracket the sub-area (Maple Meadows at 20010 Dunn Ave west, Port Haney at 22300 River Road east) with weekday trains to Waterfront, the 743/744 buses loop Hammond's streets, and the Golden Ears Bridge ties the area to Langley and Surrey. - **Q: If a small strata townhouse does list here, can it ban rentals or set an age limit?** Rentals: no — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been unenforceable since November 24, 2022 anywhere in BC; only short-term rentals can still be restricted. Age: only a 55+ bylaw is lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, Reg 7.01 exemptions as expanded May 1, 2023) — and Maple Ridge has zero verified 55+ strata townhouses, so any "55+ townhouse" claim here needs the registered bylaw produced. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The Lougheed corridor reading changed in January 2026 — cite it as adopted.** An earlier program reading treated the Lougheed Transit Corridor plan as still in process; the City record now shows OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 given third reading and ADOPTED on January 27, 2026, after the January 20, 2026 public hearing, with the corridor ending at 221 Street. Any older note calling the corridor plan merely proposed is superseded. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Hammond began as a railway land deal.** The City records that the Hammond brothers, William and John, donated land to the CPR on the condition the first railway station in Maple Ridge be built in Hammond (Port Hammond Junction); the junction served as a supply depot as the community grew around the station, yards and a cedar mill — the origin of the heritage building stock around the Hammond core, and of the Hammond Development Permit Area (heritage) that applies within the Hammond Area Plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/hammond-1st-reading-report and https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **The school locator has a hole on Lorne Avenue.** The SD42 locator returned no result at all for 20577 Lorne Avenue — the address falls between the locator's listed ranges — so a Hammond buyer can do everything right and still get no catchment answer from the tool; that gap is documented in this file rather than papered over with a nearest-school guess. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Two big Hammond recreation projects went to an assent vote, with the outcome unconfirmed in sources.** The City put the Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre (11509 207 Street / 20601 Westfield Avenue, Loan Authorization Bylaw No. 8123-2026) and the Hammond Multi-Use Community Park (20818 Golf Lane, the golf course site, Bylaw No. 8124-2026) to residents; whether the borrowing passed is UNCONFIRMED — do not price a home here on either facility being built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/what-residents-need-know-oct-17-assent-vote-three-recreation-ready-projects
- **Golden Ears Way is slated to double in width beside the sub-area.** The City states the road will expand from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, with detailed design underway — relevant to commute times and to noise for homes near the corridor. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge
- **Attached supply here is thin enough that form-checking matters more than choice.** With zero verified townhouse or condo complexes and one manufactured-home park, attached listings marketed under this sub-area are, on the sources checked, manufactured homes, duplexes, co-op homes or houses with suites surfacing under broad attached searches — the 8-listing September 2026 attached count is a feed count, not a complex roster.
