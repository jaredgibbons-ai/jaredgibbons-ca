## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every strata townhouse cross-referenced in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached. At this price point the Form B, bylaws, fees and special levies are where a townhouse is actually priced — age, pet and rental restrictions live in those documents, not in the listing price (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024, no deferral by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — rentals are allowed for long-term tenancies anywhere in BC; the "rentals not allowed" lines recorded for Willow Park Estates, Parkway Estates and Parkway II in §2 are flagged VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Langley City's own short-term rental status was NOT VERIFIED on any City page in this program — flagged, not filled (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation; research file §4).
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). Live in this band's cross-references at Willow Park Estates, Huntsfield Green, Creekside Villa, the Walnut Grove 55+ four and the three Langley City apartment buildings; the feed itself surfaced a 55+ Langley City unit whose listing text states electricity, gas and hot water are included in the maintenance fee — listing-stated, confirm in the strata documents (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws; research file §5).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** The Westbrooke and Bedford Landing row homes (§2.2) carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly. That contrast is the core diligence split on this band: the same ceiling buys a strata lot with a Form B in one complex and a fee-simple lot with none in another.

**Q: Strata or fee-simple at this price — what changes?**
A: Everything in the paper trail. Confirm which form a listing is before comparing its price to anything else in the band (§2, §3).

**Q: The feed shows a 55+ complex — can I buy if I am under 55?**
A: A 55+ bylaw is the one age bylaw BC law still allows (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2), with exemptions for legacy residents, live-in caregivers and (since May 1, 2023) younger spouses/partners and defined children. Whether you can occupy, or only own and rent, depends on the registered bylaw on the Form B (§3).

**Q: What should I check before offering on a sub-$399K townhouse?**
A: The strata documents, not just the list price: Form B, bylaws, rules, fees, special levies and any age, pet or rental restrictions decide what the unit actually costs and who may live in it — and set the search ceiling explicitly rather than trusting this page's filter, which carried two cards above its own ceiling at reading (§1, §3).
