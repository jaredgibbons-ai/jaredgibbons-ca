## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to every cross-referenced strata in §2.1).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The registered bylaws are the diligence, not listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 — flagged in-entry in §2.5 for the roster's directory lines; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw.
- **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions including the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+ — Trethewey Tower's stale 45+ directory line is the local example, §2.5).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.2). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only.

**Abbotsford zoning, OCP, SSMUH/suites and taxes — with the standing conflicts flagged.**

Substance: accessory units count toward up to 4 units on one lot in SSMUH areas (3 units on lots 280 m² or less, 4 on larger lots).
- **Suites (Abbotsford's rules ONLY):** a secondary suite must be within the principal unit, registered with the City, and **less than 50% of the principal unit's size**; a coach house (second storey of an accessory building) or garden suite (single-storey at-grade) may occupy **up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m²** (coach house max height 7.5 m / 2 storeys; garden suite 4.5 m / 1 storey), with 1 required parking stall per accessory unit type under Zoning Bylaw Section 150. Lehman Road and YXX; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); principal residence capped at 500 m² with a 1,000 m² fill limit, suite only inside it, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Floodplain boundaries are a Zoning Bylaw schedule (Schedule E); Fishtrap Creek runs through the Ellwood/Fishtrap Creek Park area; property-level floodplain status is UNVERIFIED for any individual property — check WebMap (research file §4; https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/).
