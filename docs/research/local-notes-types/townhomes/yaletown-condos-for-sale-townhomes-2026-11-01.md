## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

**The strata block (applies to the Yaletown Park 3 townhouse component and every strata documented in §2 — the diligence is the same strata diligence, because the component sits inside an apartment strata).**

- **Form B diligence.** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. A buyer of one of the 4 Yaletown Park 3 townhouses is buying a lot in that tower's strata: the Form B, the depreciation report and the registered bylaws for 977 Mainland Street are the diligence, not the townhouse's listing copy (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER the threshold. Every §2 complex is far over the threshold on its published unit counts where counts exist. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023.
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No §2 entry carries a published "rentals not allowed" line in the sources read — the Pacific Landmark II directory line reads "rentals allowed" — and any such line surfacing in older bylaw text for a Yaletown building is void for long-term rentals and flagged as such.
- **Age.** Only 55+ valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions incl. the May 1, 2023 expansion; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No Yaletown complex is verified 55+ in the condos or 55-plus siblings — the Yaletown 55-plus sibling verifies no 55+ strata ownership in the neighbourhood, and the city's verified 55+ ownership trio (Terraces on 7th in Fairview, OPAL by Element in South Cambie, Tapestry at Wesbrook Village) sits outside Yaletown and is carried in the vancouver assembly file, never imported here (55-plus sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/yaletown-condos-for-sale-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** None verified here (§2.4). A fee-simple townhouse would carry NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — title, City rules and registered covenants/HOA only. That contrast is exactly what a Yaletown Park 3 townhouse is **not**: it is strata product inside a tower.
- **Heritage overlay (HA-3).** The Historic Area District Schedule for Yaletown (HA-3) encourages conversion and renovation of the neighbourhood's heavy-timber, brick and concrete buildings, but the heritage-area regulations govern what can change — The Murchies (§2.2) is the standing example; confirm any renovation plan against the HA-3 schedule before promising it (research file §4: https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx).

**Q: What extra Vancouver charges apply to a Yaletown purchase?**
A: Two calendars: property taxes in two instalments (advance February, main July, 5% penalty after the main deadline) and the Empty Homes Tax — 3% on a property deemed vacant, with a mandatory annual declaration by the 2nd business day in February for every owner (§3).
