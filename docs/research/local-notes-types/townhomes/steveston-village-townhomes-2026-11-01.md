## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (SPA s. 59):** the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For the queued Village building — whose strata plan number, unit count and year are all UNCONFIRMED on the source in hand — the Form B and the strata plan are the documents that would settle its record; a sales-source description is not diligence. Directory and listing rental/pet lines are secondary-source data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold (the Village building's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its threshold position is read off the Form B). Since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since November 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory or listing rental line for a village strata is carried in §2 (the queued building's published restrictions are UNCONFIRMED), so no in-entry flag arises — the rule is stated so a future listing line is never taken at face value. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children; a 45+ bylaw is void and does NOT convert to 55+). No source states a 55+ bylaw for the queued Village building or any village strata — the 55-plus program verifies ZERO 55+ ownership complexes in the village core; its seniors buildings are the rental, assisted and subsidized traps in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws (55+ file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/steveston-village-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
