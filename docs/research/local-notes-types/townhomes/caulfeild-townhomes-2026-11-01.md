## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. At Caulfeild Cove (built 1973/1974), the depreciation report is the first-order document — a 1970s wood-frame complex is exactly where envelope, roof and systems cycles price the purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — Caulfeild Cove's 30 lots are well over the threshold. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Caulfeild Cove's directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in-entry in Section 2 for exactly this reason — it reflects old bylaw text, not current law. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined spouses/partners and children). No age restriction is published for Caulfeild Cove in any source checked — it is a general-market strata, not a 55+ complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the surrounding stock):** a Caulfeild detached-house purchase carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, District municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, OCP Bylaw No. 4985) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly. Caulfeild is in the District of West Vancouver — White Rock's Bylaw 2506 / 2220 pair belongs to White Rock only and is never attributed here.
- **District zoning and plan:** District of West Vancouver Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, under Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018 (including Policy 2.3.4 for the Caulfeild Village Centre). The former Caulfeild Land Use Contract area is now zoned CD86 (Caulfeild Zone) under Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024, with Residential Area A, Residential Area B, a Commercial Area and a Parks and Greenspaces Area; in Residential Area A the minimum permitted site area for a residential parcel is the site area lawfully registered on the parcel, density is a maximum of three dwelling units per lot, and stratification of buildings or land is prohibited. Nearby RS single-family zones under Section 200 state minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Appendix%20B%20-%205321%20Zoning%20Bylaw%20No%204662%202010%20Amendment%20Bylaw%20No%205321%202024.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Heritage and hazard permit areas:** Lower Caulfeild is a designated Heritage Conservation Area — Francis Caulfeild planned and designed the subdivision from 1899 to control the character of later development, and owners within the area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for any work, which any strata exterior-work plan at Caulfeild Cove runs through as well. The District also applies an Environmental Development Permit Area for watercourses (work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank), a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface) and a Foreshore Development Permit Area for coastal hazards — all three can apply in Caulfeild, the foreshore area on Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — at Caulfeild Cove prices the first-time programs are usually out of range; run the bands on the actual price); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% (insured mortgages are capped by price, so most West Vancouver purchases are conventional — confirm with the lender). 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — Caulfeild Cove is 1970s resale stock. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax does NOT apply — Caulfeild is in the District of West Vancouver, not the City of Vancouver. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **District tax timing and additional school tax:** District of West Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to unpaid current taxes on July 2, 2026 and a further 5% on outstanding current taxes on September 2, 2026; approximately 50% of the tax bill is collected by the District and remitted to other taxing authorities. At Caulfeild price points the Province's additional school tax can also apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale project in Caulfeild is verified; stated for completeness — a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Page defects, claims and contact check:** the research file documents no defect in this area's own page copy and no credential-claim variant against the approved set (REALTOR since 2015; 10 straight years FVREB Medallion Club; Top 1%; #1 in South Surrey/White Rock by MLS points 2025 + 2026 YTD; $500M+ sales), and no jared@jaredgibbons.ca instance is documented in-file — none to flag as a Jerry sweep item from this file. ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The zoning itself explains the missing townhouses.** Caulfeild's former Land Use Contract was replaced by CD86 zoning in 2024 (Amendment Bylaw No. 5321, 2024), and CD86 Residential Area A prohibits stratification — so Caulfeild Cove standing alone is a bylaw outcome, not a market accident, and it will not change without a rezoning. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **The spelling is a search trap.** The District and SD45 use "Caulfeild" (with the second "u"), not "Caulfield" — searches under the common spelling miss District documents and some listings, and at least one listing portal files the neighbourhood under "Caulfield." Caulfeild Cove's own board sub-area in listings is "Olde Caulfeild." (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md) and https://www.tomhassan.com/i-a3c211e2/managed/mls-info-sheet_046.pdf
- **Caulfeild Cove predates the heritage-era framing around it.** The townhome complex was built in 1974 (directory figure; 1973 in some unit listings) — a 1970s wood-frame strata sitting in a neighbourhood whose Lower Caulfeild subdivision was planned from 1899 and is now a designated Heritage Conservation Area; its depreciation report, not the area's estate-home market, is the pricing document. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/caulfeild-cove-4957-marine-drive and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **The District's heritage walk starts at the complex's cove.** The District's Caulfeild heritage walk notes Caulfeild Cove and the municipal dock where pilot boats were moored, and St. Francis-in-the-Wood church — the "Cove" in the complex's name is the historic pilot-boat cove two blocks from the strata. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **Both of the area's schools are inside the neighbourhood.** Caulfeild Elementary (4685 Keith Road) and Rockridge Secondary (5350 Headland Drive) are both physically in Caulfeild — unusual for a West Vancouver pocket this size, and the reason the SD45 locator result for the sample address returns two in-area schools. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
- **The foreshore is a permit area, not just a view.** Lower Caulfeild waterfront lots sit in the Foreshore Development Permit Area for coastal hazards, and Caulfeild Park's washrooms are open 7 a.m. to 9 p.m., May through October, under a Caulfeild Park Management Plan — the park at the neighbourhood's south edge is managed as an environmental and heritage site, not a simple beach park. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/caulfeild.md)
