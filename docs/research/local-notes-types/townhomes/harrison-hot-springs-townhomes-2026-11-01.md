## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the counted strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For Branches By The Lake the Form B package is also where the strata plan (recorded as EPS3064 in the research file from a source this program does not cite — UNCONFIRMED on a citable source) gets settled. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports and CRF (strata frame):** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** since November 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www.bcrea.bc.ca/legally-speaking/strata-depreciation-report-mandates-a-guide-for-real-estate-professionals-599/
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID (strata frame):** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Applied in-entry above: Harrison Lake Estates' directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws (strata frame):** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 for a younger spouse/partner, live-in caregivers, legacy residents and defined children). No Harrison strata is verified to hold a 55+ bylaw; the village's verified 55+ ownership count is zero. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple reality — stated plainly:** a fee-simple townhouse has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — municipal rules plus any registered covenants/HOA only. No fee-simple townhouse complex verifies in Harrison; most village houses are fee-simple detached homes and cottages on that same basis.
- **Village plan — conflict resolved at the top of this file:** the current consolidated Official Community Plan is **Bylaw No. 1184, 2022**, which repealed OCP Bylaw No. 864 (2007). The research file's 864-era descriptions (Village Centre, Lakeshore, Pine Avenue and Echo Avenue/Eagle Street neighbourhood plans, the 2017 "Resort Residential" provisions, and its plan for townhouses near Lillooet Avenue east) are labelled 864-era wherever used. Day-to-day use sits in Zoning Bylaw No. 1115, 2017 ("Tourist Accommodation" is a commercial-zone use). The Village's 2025 Interim Housing Needs Report commits the Village to reviewing/updating the OCP to align with 20-year housing needs. https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/Official-Community-Plan-Bylaw-No.-864-Consolidated.pdf (864 consolidated text, research file) and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/harrison-hot-springs-condos-2026-11-01.md (1184 currency)
- **Short-term rentals:** nightly rental runs on Village zoning/licensing, not the provincial principal-residence rule — the Province lists the Village among the resort regions **exempt** from that requirement. Since Dec 15, 2025, Business Licence Bylaw No. 1128 (amended by Bylaw No. 1231, 2025) requires at s. 2.1(h) a **separate Business Licence for each Dwelling Unit** so operated. Accommodation also carries the **3% MRDT** (designated recipient Tourism Harrison; current cycle Year 5 of 5, 2022–2026, with a **repeal/renewal date of January 1, 2027** — confirm renewal before underwriting 2027+ accommodation income). https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2023hous0060-001598 and https://www.harrisonhotsprings.ca/sites/default/files/2026-01/Bylaw-1128---Business-Licence-and-Regulation-%28Consolidated-to-December-15%2C-2025%29.pdf and https://tourismharrison.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-Tourism-Harrison-One-Year-Tactical-Plan-FINAL.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/harrison-hot-springs.md)
