## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (all 4 counted strata complexes):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level rental/pet lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws — the Form B and registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote — all four counted complexes are at or above the threshold as published (8, 10, 26, and Wellington Court UNCONFIRMED but a multi-unit strata). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Wellington Court's published position is rentals allowed, so there is no void directory line in this file's counted stock; the rule is stated because station-area directories nearby still print older lines, and every such line is void for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). No counted complex here is published as age-restricted, and the 55-plus sibling verified no 55+ strata ownership complex anywhere in Renfrew-Collingwood — age position per complex: UNCONFIRMED until the Form B is read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the area's house and duplex stock):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575) and any registered covenants only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning and plans — Vancouver's own instruments:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — minimum site area 306 m² for a single detached house or duplex and for a multiple dwelling of 3 to 4 units (minimum frontage 10.0 m), 464 m² for 5 units (13.4 m) and 557 m² for 6 to 8 units (15.1 m); a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house. The C-2 District Schedule applies on arterial commercial streets such as Kingsway. The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan is the framework to guide change in the station precinct, the Norquay Village Neighbourhood Centre Plan covers the Kingsway centre, and the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan, approved by Council in July 2025, covers the Millennium Line station areas at the area's north side, on top of the Renfrew-Collingwood Community Vision. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/rupert-and-renfrew-station-area-plan-approved-july-2025.aspx
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY:** properties declared, determined or deemed vacant are subject to a 3% tax of the property's assessed value under the Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674 framework; most homes that are principal residences or rented for at least six months of the year are not subject to it. Vancouver owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. This tax exists only inside the City of Vancouver — it does not apply in Burnaby, West Vancouver or North Vancouver, and no file for those cities carries it. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Floodplain:** Still Creek is one of the few remaining salmon-bearing streams in Vancouver, and its floodplain is part of the City's designated floodplain dataset — parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain is UNVERIFIED per parcel and must be confirmed in the City dataset. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/still-creek.aspx and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Presales (REDMA):** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust — the frame that governed Rae Garden's and the Chambers collections' original sales, and that governs any future station-precinct presale here. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How many real townhouse complexes are there in Collingwood?** Four verified standalone strata complexes: Wellington Court (3683 Wellington Avenue, 1992), Rae Garden (3619 Rae Avenue, 26 townhomes, 2022), Chambers (5003/5005 Chambers Street, 10 townhomes, 2020) and 8 on Chambers (5043 Chambers Street, 8 townhomes, 2019). Two more named collections (Norquay Premiere, Duchess & Horley) are in the QUEUE in Section 2 — named, tenure UNCONFIRMED, never counted. Sources: Section 2 entries.
- **Q: Chambers and 8 on Chambers — isn't that one project counted twice?** No: the listings themselves separate them — a 10-townhome collection at 5003/5005 Chambers Street completed in 2020, and an 8-townhome collection at 5043 Chambers Street built in 2019. Same street, two stratas; read the civic address and the Form B, not the street name. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/5003-chambers-street and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/5043-chambers-street
- **Q: Are the Wall Centre Central Park and Clive townhomes a cheaper way into a townhouse here?** They are townhouse-form units, but they are lots inside apartment stratas counted in the condos file — you are buying into a 1,060-home community's strata (Wall Centre) or a 68-unit mixed strata (Clive), with those stratas' fees, bylaws and depreciation reports, not a small townhouse-only strata like the four counted complexes. https://www.livabl.com/vancouver-bc/wall-centre-central-park and https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/clive-at-collingwood-east-vancouver-skytrain-presale-condos/
- **Q: Is the tower at Joyce Station a condo or a rental?** Both exist, on opposite sides of the street: Joyce by Westbank at 5058 Joyce Street is a 256-unit Freehold Strata tower (EPS7496, finished 2021); Joyce 2 at 5083 Joyce Street is a 360-unit purpose-built rental tower, placed in receivership by court order granted April 27, 2026 with leasing reported as continuing. Confirm the civic address before anything else. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/building/joyce-EPS7496 and https://www.mpamag.com/ca/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/westbanks-joyce-tower-placed-in-receivership/574795
- **Q: Can I rent out my Collingwood townhouse?** Long-term, yes under provincial law: strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022, and Wellington Court's published position is rentals allowed in any event. Short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and a unit left empty instead of rented runs into the Empty Homes Tax below. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: What happens if I leave my townhouse empty?** In Vancouver, the Empty Homes Tax: a home declared, determined or deemed vacant is taxed at 3% of its assessed value, every owner must file an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty. Homes used as a principal residence or rented at least six months of the year are generally not subject to it. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse in Collingwood?** None verified: the 55-plus sibling found no verified 55+ strata ownership complex anywhere in Renfrew-Collingwood, in any built form — the area's seniors housing is subsidized rental and care, nothing to buy. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/collingwood-ve-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Rae Garden or Raintree Gardens — are they the same?** No: Rae Garden is the 2022, 26-townhome strata at 3619 Rae Avenue counted here; Raintree Gardens is a 1993 apartment/condo strata at 3628 Rae Avenue, carried in the apartment record. Same street name, different buildings, different centuries of strata obligations. https://www.mikestewart.ca/presale/rae-garden-by-rosanni-properties-joyce-collingwood-east-vancouver-presale-townhomes/
- **Q: What do the station plans mean for a house-street lot?** The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan and the July 2025 Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan layer station-area expectations on top of the R1-1 base (multiplex up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, on qualifying lots) — check which plan, if any, covers the lot before pricing its land value, and do not comp station-area strata against interior house streets. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/rupert-and-renfrew-station-area-plan-approved-july-2025.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new townhouse, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **One street holds two separate boutique stratas, built a year apart.** Chambers Street carries Chambers (10 townhomes, completed 2020, at 5003/5005) and 8 on Chambers (8 townhomes, built 2019, at 5043) — a buyer searching the street name alone will merge two stratas' fees, bylaws and depreciation reports into one imaginary complex. https://archive.presalesbc.com/presales-listing/chambers-2/?es-pdf=9091 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/5043-chambers-street
- **The village beside the station is mostly not townhouses, and a third of it is rental.** Collingwood Village's 2,700 suites in 16 buildings (completed by November 2006) split 1,917 condominium to 783 rental, across four-storey townhouses, garden apartments, six-storey buildings and towers to 26 storeys — tenure in this pocket is confirmed building by building, never from the village name. https://docslib.org/download/5416602/collingwood-village-vancouver-bc
- **Still Creek is a salmon stream with a floodplain, in the middle of the city.** Renfrew Ravine Park, 3900 Renfrew Street, is 5.89 hectares with a portion of Still Creek running through it; the creek is one of the few remaining salmon-bearing streams in Vancouver and its floodplain sits in the City's designated floodplain dataset — ravine-edge purchases here carry flood construction level questions most Vancouver townhouse buyers never meet. https://covapp.vancouver.ca/parkfinder/ParkDetail.aspx?inparkid=160 and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/still-creek.aspx
- **The area once had lakes where the SkyTrain and the highway now run.** The City states the area once had significant wildlife and lakes that no longer exist, including Moody Lake where the SkyTrain line is today and three smaller beaver-built lakes where Grandview Highway now runs — the dated anchor for why the north side of the area drains the way it does. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/renfrew.aspx
- **Two libraries serve one local area:** the Collingwood Branch of Vancouver Public Library at 2985 Kingsway and the Renfrew Branch at 2969 East 22nd Avenue, with Renfrew Park Community Centre and Renfrew Pool at 2929 East 22nd Avenue — the Collingwood/Renfrew name split runs through the civic anchors exactly as it runs through the area name itself. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/renfrew.aspx
