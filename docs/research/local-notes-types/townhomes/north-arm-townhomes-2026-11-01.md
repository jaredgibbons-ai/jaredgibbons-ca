## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

These provincial and New Westminster rules govern any strata purchase a "North Arm" buyer actually makes in the neighbouring sub-areas, and any future strata product on a parcel marketed under this name. **Zoning here is New Westminster Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 and the plan is Our City 2041, OCP Bylaw No. 7925, 2017 — the New Westminster districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B only; any source stating RT-A / RT-B districts for New Westminster is in error (that error appeared in an earlier research pass and is corrected wherever this program meets it, as the Connaught Heights and Uptown NW condos files also record).**

- **Form B diligence (any strata purchase):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. There is no in-sub-area strata stock to apply this to today — it is the document that governs the Queensborough, Downtown, Quay and Connaught Heights purchases this search usually resolves to. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line for a North Arm complex appears in Section 2 — there is no complex for one to attach to; any such line on a neighbouring sub-area's Form B package is VOID for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, with spouse/caregiver/legacy exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). The sibling 55-plus file verifies NO 55+ strata ownership complex and no seniors housing of any tenure in North Arm — no home here may be marketed as a 55+ purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple homes (the slope form):** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only, stated plainly.
- **City zoning and plans:** houses on the slope above the foreshore sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) of Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 — RS-1: site not less than 557.40 sq. m. (6,000 sq. ft.), floor space ratio not over 0.5. The governing plan is Our City 2041, the Official Community Plan (Bylaw No. 7925, 2017), whose Green Zone includes the Esplanade (North Arm) and the Annacis Channel shoreline; no North Arm area plan was confirmed on a City source. Foreshore and water-lot rules are provincial and are not confirmed for a specific lot. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the city states them:** the research file states the RS-district position for the slope and does not publish a North Arm-specific SSMUH or suite figure — a specific lot's added-unit position is a parcel-level check against Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 as amended, and is UNVERIFIED at sub-area level in this file rather than restated from another city's rules. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Flood / foreshore:** the City floodplain study covers Fraser River land at the west end of the city in Floodplain Zone 2, and the North Arm is Fraser River foreshore — but the floodplain status of a specific North Arm lot is UNVERIFIED at lot level. Confirm the parcel's floodplain zone, any foreshore / water-lot tenure, and the insurance position before subject removal. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/north-arm.md)
- **New Westminster property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on current taxes remaining unpaid on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on any unpaid balance on September 3, 2026. The City also operates its own electrical utility alongside City water and sewer services. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/north-arm.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. No Vancouver Empty Homes Tax applies in New Westminster. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
