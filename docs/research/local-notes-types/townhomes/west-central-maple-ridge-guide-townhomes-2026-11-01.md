## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B (both cross-referenced complexes are strata):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — monthly fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. Listing-level pet/rental lines in Section 2 are listing data, not bylaws. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain one every five years, not deferrable by vote; a strata with fewer than five lots sits UNDER that threshold (neither cross-referenced complex's unit count is published, so its threshold position is UNCONFIRMED until the strata plan is read). **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF — read Sherwood Park's "strong contingency" line against that floor on the Form B. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental bylaws VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line was found for the two cross-referenced complexes in the sources read — if one appears, it is VOID in-entry for long-term rentals. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023). Neither cross-referenced complex is published as age-restricted — UNCONFIRMED either way until registered bylaws are read — and Maple Ridge has ZERO verified 55+ strata townhouses city-wide. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple contrast — plainly:** NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees; municipal rules plus registered covenants/HOA only. No verified fee-simple complex exists in West Central — a listing calling Sherwood Park or Maple Villas "fee simple" contradicts the strata-fee record and needs a title check. (Count home on disk.)
