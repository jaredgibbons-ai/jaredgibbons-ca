## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (strata purchases generally — there is no counted strata townhouse here to apply it to):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No directory "rentals not allowed" line arises in this file because no townhouse complex is counted — the rule is stated because buyers cross-shop the tower cluster, where the same voiding applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023 — spouse/partner, caregiver and legacy exemptions). The sub-area's verified 55+ stock is apartment-form (see §2), not townhouses. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none verified here. If a fee-simple row home is offered on the interior streets, the rule is plain: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996) and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 governs; small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3 set subdivision minimums of 650 m² with 18.5 m width (R-1 and R-3) and 465 m² with 12.5 m width (R-2). The Interim Official Community Plan — Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026 — was adopted April 27, 2026 and supersedes OCP No. 3479; the City states the Interim OCP streamlined the former plan and did not introduce new land-use policy except as provincial legislation required. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId= (research file)
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit (research file)
- **Watercourse setbacks:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse; Zoning Bylaw s. 519 sets flood protection setbacks and s. 523 covers riparian areas. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits (research file)
- **Taxes and rebates:** Coquitlam property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026 (research file). PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Presales (REDMA):** no townhouse presale is counted in this file; where a presale exists (Pine & Glen, apartment form), a buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Are there any townhouse complexes in Central Coquitlam?** None verified by name and tenure in this pass — the research file, the sibling condos file and a fresh search all return the same result. The attached stock that does exist by name is the Pinetree Way / Glen Drive tower cluster (apartment condos) and the Town Centre apartment-form 55+ buildings. A listing calling a tower unit a "townhome" does not make the building a townhouse complex. (Research file §5; sibling condos file §2)
- **Q: Why do listings say "North Coquitlam" for buildings this file calls Central?** Board and feed labels for the tower cluster run North Coquitlam / Canyon Springs / Town Centre, while the research geography files the Pinetree Way edge inside Central Coquitlam. This file follows the research file and street addresses, and states the conflict at the top rather than smoothing it over. Basis: sibling condos file §1.
- **Q: Can I rent out a strata unit here long-term?** Yes under provincial law — strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void since Nov 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Read the registered bylaws in the Form B package. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Q: Is there a 55+ townhouse here?** No verified 55+ townhouse. The verified 55+ ownership stock is apartment-form (Marlborough House, Princess Gate, Burlington Estates) plus pad-rental manufactured homes at Hideaway Park — neither is a townhouse complex. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §2.
- **Q: What did the attached market do in September 2026?** 36 condos & townhomes listings, 5 solds, 14% sales ratio for the sub-area; the Coquitlam community median for the combined category was $625,000. SnapStats combines condos and townhomes — there is no townhome-only or sub-area median. As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report, via the research file.
- **Q: Can a house lot here add units?** Yes under the City's SSMUH rules — up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², suites and carriage/laneway homes included, where zoning and municipal services allow; lots off municipal water or sewer fall outside the rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit (research file)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Coquitlam address?** SD43, address-based: the verified locator run for 1169 Pinetree Way returned Glen Elementary, École Maple Creek Middle and Pinetree Secondary. Run the exact address; never promise a school from the neighbourhood name. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43 (research file)
- **Q: What should I check on a creek- or ravine-edge lot?** Whether a Watercourse Protection Development Permit is required (within 30 m of the top of bank) and the Zoning Bylaw s. 519 / s. 523 flood and riparian setbacks — before pricing a renovation or addition. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits (research file)
- **Q: New-build GST — do I get it back?** On a new home, 5% GST applies; the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) returns 100% of GST to $1M (max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M) for qualifying first-time buyers with agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031, and the older New Housing Rebate returns 36% to a $6,300 maximum in the $350K–$450K band. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Coquitlam replaced its OCP in 2026 (dated fact):** Council adopted the Interim Official Community Plan (Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026) on April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479; the City states the update cut the policy count by about 70%, introduced no new growth areas except as provincial law required, and is the foundation for a fuller OCP update anticipated to begin in 2027. Any page still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is out of date. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **The "Central Coquitlam" attached market is one corridor:** every named attached building verified in the sibling programs stands on or just off Pinetree Way / Glen Drive / Atlantic Avenue at the City Centre edge — the Como Lake and Austin Avenue streets behind it are a detached-house market with an SSMUH story, not a strata story. Basis: sibling condos file §1–§2.
- **Hideaway Park carries a redevelopment fact beside its 55+ marketing:** the same 5.76-acre, 68-pad site at 4200 Dewdney Trunk Road has been described in a development-marketing document as a transit-oriented multifamily opportunity in a Shoulder Station Area near the Evergreen Line — a park buyer should read that document as carefully as the park's own marketing. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §5. http://www.database.land/Land/download-attachment/166
- **Two near-identical Burlington names, opposite answers:** Burlington Estates (2963 / 2973 Burlington Drive, built 1987) is the verified 55+ building; The Burlington across the street (2968 / 2978 Burlington Drive, LMS923, built 1993) has no age restriction in any source checked. Verify the civic address, not the street name. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §2.
- **The Dufferin Street pair is a care continuum in two addresses:** a rental retirement residence (Parkwood Manor, 1142 Dufferin St) and a complex-care centre (Dufferin Care Centre, 1131 Dufferin St) sit on the same short street — relevant context for a buyer purchasing for a later care move, and neither is buyable stock. Basis: sibling 55-plus file §2.
- **Property-tax penalties here are two-step (2026):** due July 2, 2026, then 5% on any unpaid balance after July 2 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants — a closing-date adjustment item for summer completions. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes (research file)
