## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Regulator — the frame a reviews reader is actually hiring under:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by BCFSA, which states a licensed professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and work for only the buyer or the seller in a transaction (dual agency prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances) (https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers; research file §4).
- **Testimonial-display rules:** UNVERIFIED in this file — any rule, board standard or platform policy governing how a REALTOR® may display client testimonials on his own website (attribution, consent, or whether a selection may be shown without the full set) was not opened on a BCFSA, board or platform policy page in this pass, and none is described here from industry habit. Whether the full review record behind this page (the Google profile the site's other pages cite, and the Rate-My-Agent profile the footer links to) supports any particular rating or count is likewise UNVERIFIED — neither profile was opened; the footer's rate-my-agent.com link is recorded as a footer link only (research file §4).
- **Form B diligence (for the strata townhome purchase a reader of this page may go on to make).** SPA s. 59: Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢ per page — fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance, rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached. The reviews page itself contains no strata information about any complex (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate).
- **Depreciation reports.** Stratas with 5+ lots must obtain a depreciation report every 5 years since July 1, 2024; a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold. **CRF minimum:** 10% of the operating budget since November 1, 2023 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals.** Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 for long-term tenancies; any directory "rentals not allowed" line is flagged VOID in the complex's own file. **Age.** Only 55+ age bylaws are valid since November 24, 2022 (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01; May 1, 2023 exemptions) (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation).
- **Fee-simple contrast.** A fee-simple townhome carries NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — municipal rules and registered covenants/HOA only, stated plainly.
- **City rules.** No single municipality governs this page — its intro spans Surrey (Surrey 2050 OCP **Bylaw No. 21900**, adopted June 15, 2026 — NEVER 18020 as current; Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; SSMUH July 8, 2024; suite cap 40% of principal habitable area; STRs permitted owner-only with the licence CONFLICT $358/yr carried / $350/yr + fees disclosed) and Langley among others. Zoning for any specific purchase is that property's own municipality's bylaw — UNCONFIRMED from this page (https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing).
- **FTHB GST rebate (new housing only).** Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122: 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1,500,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. **New Housing Rebate:** 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out (CRA: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html).
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — an "Over $3M — 5%" row is a compression of the 3% + further 2% structure and is flagged wherever met; no such row is documented in this file's research. Exemptions: first-time buyers full on first $500,000 at ≤$835,000, phasing to $860,000; newly built full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (BC Government: https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/understand).
