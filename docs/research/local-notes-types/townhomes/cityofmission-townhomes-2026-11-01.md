## 3. Rules that affect townhomes

- **Depreciation / CRF.** 5+ lots every 5 years since Jul 1, 2024 (sub-5-lot UNDER threshold); CRF ≥10% since Nov 1, 2023 (https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing).
- **Rentals / age.** Rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (Cherry Lane directory line flagged VOID in-entry, §2.1). Only 55+ bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — Luther Place's published 55+ position must be confirmed as a registered bylaw on the Form B.
