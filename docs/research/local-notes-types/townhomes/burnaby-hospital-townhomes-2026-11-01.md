## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT TOWNHOMES

- **Form B diligence (the 1 strata complex above):** Strata Property Act s. 59 — the Form B states the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance and an insurance summary, with the rules, current budget, any Rental Disclosure Statement and the most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page. For a 1980s wood-frame townhouse strata, the depreciation report and Form B are the central diligence documents — envelope, roof and plumbing cycles sit in them, not in listing copy. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Depreciation reports:** since July 1, 2024, strata corporations with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years, not deferrable by vote. **CRF minimum:** since Nov 1, 2023, at least 10% of the annual operating budget must go to the contingency reserve fund. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Rental-restriction bylaws are VOID:** since Nov 24, 2022, no BC strata residential rental-restriction bylaw is valid; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. No published rental line for 3980 Canada Way was found in the sources checked, so there is no directory line to flag in-entry in Section 2 — if an older bylaw or directory line surfaces for the complex, it is void for long-term rentals for exactly this reason. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation?keyword=strata&keyword=rentals
- **Age bylaws:** only 55+ residency bylaws are lawful (Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, exemptions expanded May 1, 2023, covering legacy residents, live-in caregivers and defined spouses/partners and children). No 55+ bylaw is published for 3980 Canada Way in the sources checked — UNCONFIRMED; confirm on the Form B. The sibling 55-plus file verifies no 55+ ownership complex in this sub-area. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Fee-simple townhomes:** none are verified in this sub-area, but the rule is stated plainly for any non-strata row product a buyer meets on the house streets: NO strata, NO Form B, NO strata fees — diligence is title, municipal rules and any registered covenants or HOA documents only.
- **City zoning and plan:** Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026) governs — never the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742. Burnaby consolidated its single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025, replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; the Cascade Heights Community Plan, for the neighbourhood at this sub-area's south side, was adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/burnaby-hospital.md)
- **Taxes and rebates:** PTT (1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% to $3M, 5% above) + exemptions (first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000); FTHB GST rebate — Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 (100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year; CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales — the counted complex is 1980s resale stock. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** no presale is verified in this sub-area; stated for completeness — a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (REDMA s. 21), with deposits in trust. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Burnaby tax timing:** City of Burnaby 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% after the September due date. The 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33; strata utility billing for the counted complex is UNCONFIRMED here. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/burnaby-hospital.md)
