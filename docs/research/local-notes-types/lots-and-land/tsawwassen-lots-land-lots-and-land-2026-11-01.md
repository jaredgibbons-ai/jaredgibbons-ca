## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Position, stated at the top: this topic file carries no roster — 0 named developments counted as lot inventory, 0 presale lot offerings verified.** Unlike the area files, a Tsawwassen-wide land feed is a real search — but what it surfaces is individual parcels (urban infill lots, shore lots, farm parcels), not named bare-land subdivisions. No named subdivision selling serviced vacant lots to individual builders was verified Tsawwassen-wide on an official or developer source in this research. No lot count, lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price is stated. The land, by system:

### System 1 — urban infill lots (fee-simple by default)

- Inside the residential neighbourhoods, lots sit under the RS and RD zones; minimum lot sizes for subdivision include RS4 610 m², RS5 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m², RD1 836 m² and RD4 504 m², with zoning confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. Fee-simple is the default tenure: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Bluff-shore lots:** the RS3 Single Detached Residential 3 zone sheet describes a zone for single detached housing in the vicinity of Georgia Strait, with a minimum subdivision lot area of 4,000 m², lot width 30 m and average lot depth 30 m — the estate-scale standard that fits the English Bluff and Cliff Drive shore. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Boundary Bay lots:** low-lying shore lots under the SD3 hazard levels (Section 3) — the hazard overlay sorts this stock before street names do.
- **Serviced vs unserviced, stated as the research states it:** urban infill is the serviced system in the research's framing, but no parcel-level servicing statement is documented — UNCONFIRMED per parcel. The live page's claim that urban lots south of 28 Avenue connect to full municipal water and sewer is page copy, UNVERIFIED on an official source (Section 5). For unserviced parcels: GWELLS holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, non-domestic use (including irrigation) requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; Fraser Health requires a home not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

### System 2 — agricultural parcels on the edges (ALR where applicable)

- Farm parcels sit under the A zones and, where a parcel is in the reserve, ALR rules. In the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is **8 ha**, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; A2 (s. 10.20.6) also sets 8 ha; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** no Tsawwassen-wide split is published on an official source — none is stated. A specific parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address; that single check controls the housing caps and subdivision permission in Section 3. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- An edge-of-Tsawwassen "lot" in the reserve is farm land with a housing envelope, not a future strata site: ALR residences cannot be stratified (Section 3).

### Bare-land strata — its own form

- A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure: the buyer owns the strata lot (essentially the building lot itself) and the house on it; Form B, strata fees for common property and registered bylaws apply to the lot. It is NOT fee-simple, and bare-land strata parcels can surface in a land feed — tenure is a title question per parcel. **Tsawwassen-wide freehold versus bare-land strata split: UNVERIFIED** — no official source publishes it. The verified Tsawwassen example in this program is **Beachcomber Cottages (Southlands), bare-land strata, documented ONCE in the Southlands land home file** — cross-reference only: `~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/tsawwassen-east-guide-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md`. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 — "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1, Map 2; policies s. 2.6). Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps — the zone minimums in Section 2 are what govern in practice. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide, does not create new lots, and does not waive a hazard or streamside permit area. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision / servicing — process documented, number not asserted:** a Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015) is cited in this program from a City council report dated January 24, 2024 (File 12100-05/8288) recommending first, second and third readings; **final adoption and parcel-level standards are UNCONFIRMED**, so the PROCESS is documented instead — subdivision/servicing confirmed with the City for the specific parcel under LGA s. 506. Sibling lots files asserting "8288, 2024" in governing terms are noted as conflicts, not followed. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Hazard permit areas — the overlays that sort Tsawwassen land:** **SD2 "English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)"** (OCP Appendix B, hazardous conditions) covers the west shore — the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence; development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer; no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea. **SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands):** 2.9 m GSC datum floor level, 2.3 m GSC datum minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback. **Streamside:** all properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, are in the Streamside Protection and Enhancement DPA — it applies on farmland ditches as well as urban lots. Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **ALR block, in full:** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where Delta bylaws also permit it. Delta's A1 zone mirrors this: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, one accessory farm dwelling unit of max 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, Beachcomber-type product included):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple urban lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** the Province's general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — new HOME, not bare land. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated:** $700,000 lot, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**; $2,000,000 lot = $2,000 + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 purely residential parcel = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. The live page's own GST summary for lot sales was **not verified against a CRA source in this slug's research** and is not carried here; anything finer than GI-003 for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME purchase, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here** (mortgage-calculator file only). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where do buildable lots in Tsawwassen come from?** Subdivision against the parcel's zone minimum (335 m² in RS7 up to 4,000 m² in RS3; 8 ha in A1), and added-unit development on existing lots (up to four units under SSMUH). On the agricultural edges, a new A1 lot needs 8 ha plus OCP compliance and ALC Act approval where applicable. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel near Tsawwassen?** Only within tight limits: A1 minimum 8 ha, A3 does not permit subdivision, and ALR land generally needs ALC permission to subdivide. (§3 above)
- **Q: How big a house can go on a reserve parcel?** Principal residence without an ALC application: 500 m² or less, ≤1,000 m² of fill; suite only inside the principal; one additional residence at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) where Delta bylaws also permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What extra rules apply to a bluff or beach lot?** West shore: SD2 — Professional Engineer conditions for hazard-affected development, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of the sea. Boundary Bay: SD3 — 2.9 m datum floor level, 2.3 m minimum land surface elevation, 7.5 m tidal setback. Confirm both with the City before writing, not from a listing's wording. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The Tsawwassen-wide split is UNVERIFIED — read tenure off title per parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Boardwalk / Tsawwassen Landing land a freehold lot option?** No — that product sits on Tsawwassen First Nation Lands under TFN law, as 99-year leasehold (leasehold prepaid-strata in listing records). Excluded tenure for any City-side land comparison. (§2 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a $700,000 lot?** PTT $12,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $10,000 at 2%). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — the live page's GST summary is UNVERIFIED in the research, so confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Are Centennial Green or the 4800 Springs Boulevard project lots I can buy?** Neither is inventory. Centennial Green is a QUEUED Southlands name (details UNCONFIRMED); 4800 Springs Boulevard is a City-listed townhouse project at the application stage (details UNCONFIRMED beyond the City listing). Both are documented + flagged, never absorbed. (§2 above)
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the ALR?** On the ALC maps, by PID or civic address — reserve status controls the 500 m² cap, the additional-residence limits, and whether subdivision needs Commission permission. Run it before subjects come off. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Tsawwassen land?** No figure is stated in this file: SnapStats does not cover South Delta and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. (§1 above)
