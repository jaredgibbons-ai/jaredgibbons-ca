## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050," adopted June 15, 2026):** the current plan. Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current. The City states the Douglas NCP's land use designations "have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 – Official Community Plan Update corporate report" — the in-force check for any Douglas lot is the Surrey 2050 designation plus the lot's zone, not the 1999 NCP designations listing text still quotes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- **Zoning — Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000:** parcel by parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision + servicing — Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 (verified on the City's own pages):** the bylaw under which subdivision and servicing requirements for a Douglas parcel run; parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. (Standing Surrey frame for this program.)
- **SSMUH as Surrey states it (in effect July 8, 2024):** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing framework under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary. Confirm the parcel's eligibility before pricing in added units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey frame):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, its habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and it can never be a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — never state it. A feed card's "licenced revenue suite" wording (259 172a Street) is a claim to verify against City records, not a fact. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Coach houses and garden suites (Surrey frame):** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Streamside setbacks:** in Part 7A of Zoning By-law No. 12000, varying by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey ground. (Standing Surrey frame for this program.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey frame):** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence at $358/yr (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1), with the City's 2024 corporate report's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a conflict — carry $358 with $350 disclosed, and check the City licence page before quoting either figure. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/pacific-douglas.md)
- **ALR block (ALR ground borders the plan area to the north; parcel status UNVERIFIED per lot):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Pacific Douglas bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $1.2 million → $22,000; $1.5 million → $28,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — court-ordered Surrey land sales run through the registries that serve the region, not a Surrey registry. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Pacific Douglas-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.
