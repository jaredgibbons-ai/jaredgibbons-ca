## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) is CURRENT — never Bylaw 18020 as current.** The South Newton plan that once covered this ground was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect; on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed Corporate Report R246 updating land use designations for this area. - **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs.** South Newton lands carry R-series residential zones, with RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook; a parcel's zone and its subdivision minimums are read off the bylaw for that zone. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH — Surrey's bands, as the City states them:** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the BC Building Code permits one secondary suite per house; properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions. Suite cap for this program's Surrey frame: a secondary suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. The City also states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits — an existing unpermitted suite still needs the permit and approval process, so verify suite permits on any listing here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH timing frame:** Surrey's SSMUH zoning framework dates from July 8, 2024 in this program's standing Surrey frame; the band figures above are as the City's SSMUH page states them. (Research file §4.)
- **Riparian / creek rules:** Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through South Newton, and riparian/setback requirements can limit building envelopes on creek-side lots — check City mapping before valuing an addition, a rebuild or an SSMUH scheme on a creek-side parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **ALR block — stated for completeness, for any parcel where ALR status is confirmed on the ALC maps:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; caps: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm-class thresholds in the full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. No ALR designation inside Sullivan Station itself is confirmed in this file (§2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block — stated for completeness, governing no Sullivan Station stock verified here:** where a bare-land strata lot does exist in BC, it is strata tenure — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million.
