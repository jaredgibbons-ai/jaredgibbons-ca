## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Governing law — MHPTA (provincial, the frame for every ordinary park in §2):** manufactured homes are built off-site and transported to a final location for installation, and BC's Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act covers most manufactured home tenancies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Pad-rent increases — Act percentages ONLY:** a park landlord may increase rent only once every 12 months, only with 3 full months' written notice, and only up to the yearly limit; the landlord may not round up and may not carry forward an unused portion of a prior year's limit. The 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is **2.3% plus a proportional amount**, and the 2027 limit is **2.2% plus a proportional amount**; the proportional amount is the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park. No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file — none is published by an official source for these parks. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment permanently transfers the site tenancy to the buyer; under s.28(1)(b) a tenant may assign only with the park owner's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an order of the director, or where the tenancy agreement authorizes it — the Act's word is "director," never "arbitrator"; under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting to an assignment, and on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / change of use (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to another use, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date of the notice; a tenant may end the tenancy early on at least 10 days' written notice, the dispute window for a 12-month notice is 15 days, and additional compensation of the assessed value of the home minus the $20,000 may be ordered where the tenant could not obtain the permits needed to move the home or could not move it to another site within a reasonable distance, with a further $5,000 or 12 months' pad rent, whichever is greater, payable if the landlord does not proceed with the conversion purpose within a reasonable period. (The $20,000 and $5,000 figures are the Act's compensation figures as the Province states them — statutory amounts, not pad rents.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Strata block (applies ONLY to the owned bare-land-strata form — Baker Trail Village / Sea Ridge Bays cross-referenced in §2 — never to a rented pad):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid in a strata (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw — and in a pad-rental park an age rule is a park designation in the park's own rules in any case, not a strata bylaw at all. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR framework, per parcel only:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR (asserted only in the horse-properties file in this group, where its own research documents it; never here). Whether any specific directory park sits on ALR ground is UNVERIFIED per park — no park's ALR status is asserted in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Municipal frames — stated per labelled example, never blended, examples only:** Township of Langley parks — Zoning Bylaw 2500 / SSMUH Bylaw 6020 / Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED for the Township frame generally; Surrey parks — Zoning Bylaw 12000 / OCP Bylaw 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) / servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986); Abbotsford parks — OCP Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025), with the Abbotsford Development Bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED (process documented, number not asserted); Chilliwack parks — OCP 5500 / SSMUH Bylaw 5395, with the Chilliwack servicing number HELD UNCONFIRMED (the candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is NOT adopted in this file — Approving Officer / PLA process instead); Maple Ridge parks — Zoning 7600-2019, OCP CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024), flagged and resolved nowhere, servicing Bylaw 4800-1993 stated only from a City source. No one city's bylaw is stated as this regional page's bylaw. (Standing corrections and verified servicing ledger for this program.)
- **PTT — worked example, labelled as a LAND purchase for contrast (a rented-pad home purchase is not a land transfer):** on a $500,000 land purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$500,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. The PTT treatment of a specific manufactured-home-on-pad transfer is a conveyancing question for that transaction and is not asserted beyond this labelled example. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. (Carried for any true land purchase under this topic; it does not convert a pad tenancy into a land sale.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own sources support it — the research encodes no prohibition position for park homes or park land, so none is asserted: UNCONFIRMED here. (Research file above.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a mobile home in a park, do I own the land?** No — in an ordinary park you own the manufactured home and rent the site it sits on under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. The only owned-lot park form in this program is the bare-land strata park (Baker Trail Village; Sea Ridge Bays in Surrey), cross-referenced in §2 and documented in its home file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: How many mobile home parks does this page list?** 73 entries in page order, covering Abbotsford, Agassiz/Kent, Chilliwack, Coquitlam, Cultus Lake, Soowahlie, Hope, Langley, Maple Ridge, Mission, Rosedale, Ruskin and Surrey — with two Chilliwack parks each listed twice, so the distinct count is lower, and the list is site copy only because no official park registry exists. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html
- **Q: How much is pad rent in Fraser Valley parks?** No figure is stated in this file: this page publishes no pad rent for any park, no official source publishes a pad-rent figure, average or range, and the dollar ranges on sibling pages of this site are unverified site copy (§5) — the figure that matters is the specific park's current rent in its written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html
- **Q: How much can a park raise pad rent each year?** Up to the MHPTA limit for manufactured home site tenancies: 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026, and 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' written notice, once every 12 months, with no rounding up and no carry-forward of an unused portion. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What transfers when I buy a park home — do I need the park's consent?** Yes: under MHPTA s.28 the site tenancy is assigned to you with the park owner's prior written consent (or an order of the director), the owner cannot charge a consent fee, and the pad rent and the next increase date carry over unchanged. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What happens if the park is sold or converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42 the landlord must first have all required government permits and approvals, give 12 months' notice, and pay $20,000 on or before the effective date; the tenant may leave early on 10 days' notice, has 15 days to dispute, and may be eligible for additional compensation of the assessed value minus $20,000 through dispute resolution. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Fraser Valley mobile homes?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category and no land category. The only figures in this file are all-residential community context as of September 2026 (for example Langley Detached median $1,345,000 and Condos & Townhomes median $611,000), and neither describes park homes. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/)
- **Q: Is a 55+ park the same as a 55+ strata?** No. In the verified 55+ parks in §2 the age rule is a park designation in the park's own rules and tenancy agreement; the Strata Property Act 55+ bylaw regime (ss.123.1/123.2) applies only to strata corporations — which is why the bare-land strata parks are the exception, not the model. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: How much PTT on a $500,000 purchase?** On a $500,000 LAND purchase, $8,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $300,000 ($6,000) (§3, labelled example). A rented-pad home purchase is not a land transfer; take the transfer-tax position for the specific transaction from the conveyancer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Can I rely on this directory's addresses to find a park?** Only as a starting list: it repeats two Chilliwack parks, gives one park two different postal codes, and no official registry exists to check it against — confirm the park's legal name and address from the listing and the park itself before writing an offer. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mobile-home-parks-fraser-valley.html
