## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs. Secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, resolved nowhere:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. R-CL s.403: minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m² — no "355 m²" figure exists in the bylaw. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **OCP / plans — Township:** the 2025 OCP Update, Bylaw No. 6200, was adopted December 1, 2025; the December 2025 update set included the Aldergrove, Murrayville, Fort Langley, Walnut Grove and Northwest Langley community plans and the Yorkson, Smith and Williams Neighbourhood Plans. The Willoughby Community Plan's origin bylaw 1836 now sits as Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200. Bylaws 6201/6202/6203: first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026 — never upgraded. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m² on eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit-Oriented Area); **NO 6-plex tier exists anywhere in Bylaw 6020.** An OCP Schedule 4 Development Permit Area for SSMUH form and character was adopted April 27, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** subdivisions require Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED per the verified ledger. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists in the Township — parcel-specific):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 parcel — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this page a listing page?** No — it is an index of Township community and neighbourhood plan tiles linking to tol.ca, with no MLS® listings or result count at reading. https://jaredgibbons.ca/townshipoflangleyplans.html
- **Q: How do I read a Township parcel's potential?** In layers: OCP designation (Bylaw No. 6200 update, Dec 1, 2025), then the community plan, then any neighbourhood plan, then current zoning under Bylaw No. 2500 — the plan shows where the Township wants growth; the zoning is what can be built as-of-right today. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **Q: Have the Carvolth, Jericho, Latimer, Routley and Gordon Estate plans been repealed?** UNVERIFIED as stated on the live page: its tiles state repeal on June 15, 2026 by Bylaw Nos. 6202/6203, but Township records support only first and second reading on May 25, 2026 with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — confirm adoption with the Township before relying on the repeal. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Q: What is the Willoughby Plan's current bylaw home?** Origin Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025). https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/willoughby-community-plan-update.aspx
- **Q: Does a plan designation mean I can subdivide?** No — designation is policy. Subdivision needs Approving Officer approval, and servicing is governed by the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382; ALR parcels also need ALC approval to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Q: How many units does SSMUH allow on a Township lot?** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m² on eligible lots under Bylaw 6020 — and there is no 6-plex tier. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: What were Langley market figures in September 2026?** Community context only — no plan-area figures exist: detached median $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales; Condos & Townhomes median $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M parcel?** $28,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 ($26,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Where is a specific parcel's plan and zoning confirmed?** With the Township, per parcel — OCP designation, community/neighbourhood plan, zoning and Development Permit Areas are per-parcel checks and are UNVERIFIED for any individual parcel in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
