"Capitol Hill" is the GVR board sub-area name used in the SnapStats GVR report for Burnaby. The City publishes no street-by-street written boundary for the MLS sub-area; the neighbourhood spans the summit north of Hastings Street, between the Vancouver Heights / Heights Urban Village streets to the west and Parkcrest to the east — exact MLS boundary streets are UNVERIFIED. Paragon sub-area code: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- Nearby areas: Vancouver Heights and the Heights Urban Village to the west and south-west, Willingdon Heights to the south, Parkcrest to the east, Lochdale and Montecito further east along Hastings. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park
- Main roads and transit: Hastings Street runs east–west below the hill; Willingdon Avenue runs south to Highway 1, Metrotown and BCIT; Holdom Avenue and Fell Avenue climb the residential grid. TransLink's R5 Hastings St RapidBus links Burrard Station (downtown Vancouver) with SFU's Burnaby Mountain campus at least every 10 minutes at peak; on September 7, 2026 TransLink extended the R2 RapidBus from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station via Hastings and Willingdon. There is no SkyTrain station inside the sub-area. The city's hospital is Burnaby Hospital, 3935 Kincaid St, south of the hill. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/confederation-park

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists on Capitol Hill. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings. What trades as "land" here is the fee-simple hillside house lot — view lots on the upper streets, family-grid lots toward Willingdon — priced in part for what the R1 SSMUH district lets it become, plus one rezoning-stage project on Hastings Street below the hill, documented as a proposal only.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and only verified land form

- **Established hillside house lots:** predominantly detached single-family houses on a sloping, view-oriented hillside, with a limited number of low-rise apartments and condos on and just off Hastings Street and Holdom Avenue at the hill's base — 40 detached listings against 18 attached listings in September 2026 (3 detached solds, 8% ratio; 3 attached solds, 17% ratio). The upper streets around Capitol Hill Reservoir Park are the view pocket; the flatter grid streets toward Willingdon Heights are the family pocket. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/capitol-hill-bn.md, §§2, 5; SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026.** Never cite the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 for a current Burnaby lot. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Hastings corridor below the hill is planned as the Heights Urban Village, and Burnaby's community plans are policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. Read Hastings-fronting lots against the 2050 OCP first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. On the hill, the 400 m frequent-transit test turns on distance to the Hastings R5/R2 corridor — measure it for the parcel, do not assume it from the street name. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. The base bylaw's own number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source read for this file, so no number is asserted for it — the process above is what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Slope and heritage diligence:** no area-wide flood construction level for the Capitol Hill slope was identified in the City sources read; parcel-level slope, geotechnical and hazard status for individual hill lots is UNVERIFIED — budget a geotechnical check into any hillside lot purchase. The Capitol Hill Community Hall (built 1947–1948, on a triangular lot in Dynes Park at the Hastings streetcar terminus) is on the City's heritage register — a reminder to check heritage status before assuming a Hastings-edge lot is a clean teardown. https://search.heritageburnaby.ca/link/landmark563
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no position for this sub-area is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Capitol Hill, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. The 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33 (Section 2). Area-specific levy notes for Capitol Hill: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot on Capitol Hill?** No verified one — there is no standing bare-land market here. Land trades as fee-simple hillside house lots; no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Kensington Gardens a cheap way to buy land on the hill?** No — Kensington Gardens is undivided-interest co-ownership (5740 / 5760 / 5770 Hastings St, 125 units per a directory report), EXCLUDED program-wide: no strata lot, no Form B, and no lot. Do not price it as strata or as land. (Sibling condos file, §2.)
- **Q: Where does Cassia belong — and is it lots?** Cassia (6965 Hastings St) is a built 60-home strata of garden flats and townhomes, counted once in the townhomes sibling; the feeds file it under Sperling-Duthie and the siblings place it at Capitol Hill's east edge. It is built product, not a lot offering. (Sibling townhomes file, §2.)
- **Q: What can a hill lot become under the new zoning?** Under the R1 SSMUH district in Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. The 400 m test is measured from the parcel to the Hastings corridor. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: What extra diligence does a hillside lot need?** Slope and geotechnical diligence: no area-wide flood construction level for the slope was identified in City sources, and parcel-level slope/geotechnical status is UNVERIFIED in the research — get the parcel checked before writing, not after. (Research file, §4.)
- **Q: Is the 4269 Hastings project a chance to buy land below the hill?** No — it is a six-storey mixed-use proposal (37 non-market rental and strata homes over retail) that has passed second reading. A proposal for homes, never for-sale lot inventory. https://londonpacific.ca/app/uploads/2023/04/4269-Hastings-Burnaby.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does a lot purchase qualify for the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a hill lot?** By exact address only — Capitol Hill, Confederation Park and Rosser elementaries all sit in or beside the sub-area, and the one settled locator run (4715 Pandora St → Confederation Park Elementary → Alpha Secondary) shows the secondary answer is not always Burnaby North. Run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: Why did attached homes outsell detached here in September 2026?** On the recorded numbers: 3 attached solds on 18 listings (17% ratio) against 3 detached solds on 40 listings (8%) — the hill's detached (view) market sat while Hastings-side attached product moved. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
