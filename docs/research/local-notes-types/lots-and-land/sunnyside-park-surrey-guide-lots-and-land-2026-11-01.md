## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey (standing correction governs):** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026; the former Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw, 1993, No. 12000 governs. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH — Surrey (adopted July 8, 2024):** SSMUH zones RA to R6 — up to 3 units on lots under 280 sq m, up to 4 units on lots over 280 sq m, and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, as the City states it. The zone and lot size for a specific parcel are confirmed on City mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, one per dwelling; the old 90 m² cap is NOT a current rule. https://jaredgibbons.ca/sunnyside-park-surrey-guide.html
- **Semiahmoo Town Centre Plan:** approved by Council Jan 31, 2022 and later updated to align with the King George Boulevard Bus Rapid Transit and the Surrey 2050 OCP designations; the plan area, 16 Avenue to 24 Avenue from Martin Drive to Earl Marriott Secondary School, borders this sub-area. The guide page states the plan covers 136 hectares and was updated July 27, 2026 for King George Boulevard bus rapid transit (Corporate Report CR171) (site copy). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/semiahmoo-town-centre-plan
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 8830 (1986) — verified on City pages per the program ledger. Confirm parcel-level servicing with the City; no servicing statement for a specific vacant lot is documented in this slug's research: UNCONFIRMED at parcel level. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Streamside setbacks — Surrey:** streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** licence $358/yr, with the $350 conflict disclosed (the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr plus fees) — direct readers to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (framework only — no ALR ground documented for a specific Sunnyside lot; parcel status UNVERIFIED):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no such line is asserted for this slug (its research does not document one). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Registry ledger:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (mapping UNVERIFIED) — a court-ordered sale touching Surrey ground is not mapped to a Surrey registry in this file. (Program ledger.)
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K→$14,000 ($6,000 FTHB); $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
