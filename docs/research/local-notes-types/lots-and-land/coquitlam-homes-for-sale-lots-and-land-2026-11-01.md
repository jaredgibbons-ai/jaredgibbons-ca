## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1 through R-4, with the subdivision minimums in Section 2; s.519 flood protection setbacks and s.523 riparian areas also sit in this bylaw. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP — Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479:** the current plan frame — Council's streamlined Interim OCP consolidated a former OCP of more than 1,000 pages (55 land use designations reduced to 18, over 60 maps simplified to 19) and incorporates provincial housing legislation requirements (transit-oriented areas, SSMUH, housing needs), with a comprehensive OCP review anticipated to begin in 2027. Any Coquitlam document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. https://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1932
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** up to four dwelling units in low-density neighbourhoods; R-1/R-2 caps of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger, in forms including secondary suites, laneway homes, duplexes, triplexes and fourplexes. Coquitlam secondary-suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED — the City guide read confirms suites as a permitted form but states no cap and no owner-occupancy rule. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Subdivision + servicing — Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 (VERIFIED):** subdivision minimums run off the zone (Section 2), and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 — the consolidated bylaw requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse Development Permit:** within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required — double the 30-metre reach in the rest of Coquitlam. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Short-term rentals in Coquitlam:** UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/coquitlam-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Coquitlam bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Coquitlam ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $1.0 million → $18,000; $1.5 million → $28,000; $2.0 million → $38,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Coquitlam-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this city, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Coquitlam land actually documented?** Sub-area by sub-area: 10 sub-area land files exist on disk (Section 2), and 13 sub-areas — including North Coquitlam and Westwood Plateau — have no land file yet. This city file holds no roster and takes no counts.
- **Q: How many homes can a Coquitlam single-family lot hold?** Up to four dwelling units in low-density neighbourhoods under zones R-1 to R-4 — 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m² and 4 units on lots 280 m² or larger in R-1/R-2 — subject to property-specific eligibility confirmed with the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: How small can a lot be subdivided?** By zone: 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, or 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003. Confirm the parcel's zone first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: Is Fraser Mills a lot development I can buy into?** No — Fraser Mills is a master-planned presale community (Debut, Encore, Chapter towers) on a Fraser River floodplain site, documented in the condos sibling as presales, not lots. Flood construction level and strata insurance position are part of that diligence, not a land purchase. ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/coquitlam-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What creek rule affects a Coquitlam lot?** The Watercourse Protection DPA reaches 50 metres from the top of bank in Northeast Coquitlam — double the rest of Coquitlam — with Zoning ss.519/523 flood and riparian setbacks on top. Check any creek-edge lot before promising additions or suites. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q: Why can't I find Central Coquitlam product under "Central Coquitlam"?** Because listing feeds file the Pinetree Way / Glen Drive towers under North Coquitlam, Canyon Springs and Town Centre labels. Search by building name and strata plan, not by the sub-area label alone. (Condos sibling, FILE STATUS.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a Coquitlam detached purchase?** On the standard tiers: $1.5 million → $28,000 and $1.0 million → $18,000 before any exemption; a qualifying first-time buyer exempts the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve my lot's address?** SD43, address-based, with the Ministry-listed schools in Section 1 — but no Coquitlam address-level locator result is verified for land purposes in this slug's research, so run the exact address in the SD43 locator before any school claim. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q: How did Coquitlam trade in September 2026?** Detached sold 44 of 425 at a $1,455,000 community median while "Condos & Townhomes" combined sold 89 of 806 at $625,000 — both medians their 13-month lows; community context only, and land has no SnapStats category. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)
