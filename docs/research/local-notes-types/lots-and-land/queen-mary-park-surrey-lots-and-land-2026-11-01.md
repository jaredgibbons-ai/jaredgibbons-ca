## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; every Surrey property carries a zone under Bylaw No. 12000, shown in the City's COSMOS map. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. With RapidBus corridors on both Scott Road and King George Boulevard, edge lots may carry added-unit value a listing does not mention — check the parcel's zone and Frequent Bus Stop Area status in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** Only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit; a suite is never permitted within a coach house or garden suite — and a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites.** All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, with lane access required for a coach house on smaller R3–R6 lots and one extra parking space required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. **The Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no number is stated in this slug's research or a cited City source opened for it, no other city's number is borrowed, and lot-specific servicing costs and timelines are UNCONFIRMED. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only: renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host and must live at the property as their primary residence; tenants cannot run one. Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (wherever a strata lot — including a bare-land strata lot — is the purchase).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block — conditional, no ALR ground documented for this sub-area.** If a parcel were in the ALR: residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class in FULL three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Per-parcel ALR status for any Queen Mary Park lot: UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — HOME exemptions, not bare-lot exemptions. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Finer points per lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, window March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; owners file the annual declaration. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas

- **Q: Are basement suites allowed in these houses?** Surrey permits one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite. A specific home's suite legality must be confirmed with the City — listings in this sub-area include homes described with unauthorized suites. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
