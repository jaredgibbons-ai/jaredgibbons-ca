## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050, Bylaw No. 21900 — adopted June 15, 2026 — current.** The former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is NOT current and is never cited as such in this file. Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Confirm a parcel's designation and zone in the City's own bylaw/mapping before valuing development or suite potential. https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH (urban lots only — not ALR parcels):** Surrey implemented the provincial rules on July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite is not a separate strata lot; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. Coach houses and garden suites: all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR, and one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in the City's mapping. The City's subdivision/servicing bylaw is the **Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified on the City's own Land Development pages in an earlier batch of this program (that sourcing is stated here because this slug's own research pass opened no servicing page); servicing runs through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process, and on acreage ground servicing is the threshold question — a lot on well/septic is not a serviced lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — NEVER a single riparian figure. A lowland parcel's setback is read against its stream class in the City's mapping. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at **$358/year** as stated on the City's licence page. DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year plus fees; this file carries $358 and directs the reader to the licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval outside limited regulation exceptions; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata frame (no bare-land strata is verified on this ground — §2; this block governs any strata purchase a buyer reaches from this label on adjacent ground, counted in its own home file):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $1,500,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Farm and business land sales sit on the business side of that line at this level of generality; anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Foreclosure geography:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure mapping for any court-ordered parcel on this feed is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted. (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/clayton-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
