## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: City of Mission Bylaw 6405-2026, ADOPTED APRIL 21, 2026 (standing correction only).** The research records the April 7, 2026 public hearing to replace and repeal 6350-2025 with substantively the same plan plus a Regional Context Statement; adoption was UNVERIFIED in that pass. No OCP-date defect is documented for this slug and none is asserted. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Zoning: City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020.** Parcel zone and minimum lot area are read per parcel from the consolidated bylaw; no lot-size table is documented in this research. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **SSMUH / subdivision:** Mission incorporated SSMUH into Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 on July 17, 2024 (Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163), merging R930s into R930). Mission-specific unit counts per lot are UNCONFIRMED in this research — Surrey's figures are NOT carried into Mission — and added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. Subdivision/servicing runs under **Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 (DSCB 5650-2017)** — stated only from a City of Mission source found in an earlier batch (the bylaw title also appears on the City's own site-assessment document), **not as a research-file fact for this slug**; the process is documented here, specific standards per parcel UNCONFIRMED. DCCs under Bylaw 6348-2025 (adopted May 5, 2025) apply on subdivision approval. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/site-assessment-development and https://www.mission.ca/planning/development-amenity-cost-charges
- **Secondary suites (Mission):** all suites must be registered with the City; suites sit in single family homes only and are **not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes** — a suite described on a band townhouse card is a claim to verify with the City, not a feature to price. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it (none does here). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (the Heritage Park Place question, §2):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land — worked through the band, inputs + tier math stated (anchors):** general tiers 1% to $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Fully residential, no exemption: $500,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 = **$14,000** ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer, per the program anchor); $1,000,000 = **$18,000**; $1,100,000 = **$20,000**; $1,200,000 (the band cap) = $2,000 + 2% × $1,000,000 = **$22,000**. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. personal-use land); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Mission property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, **FLAT 10% penalty** on balances outstanding July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; no s.235 alternative scheme) — not Surrey's 5% + 5% and not the FVRD rural regime. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

- **Q: What is the median Mission townhome price under $1.2M?** No such figure exists: SnapStats publishes no townhome-only split and no band cut. Its "Condos & Townhomes" community median was $430,000 in Sep 2026 on 10 solds (and $642,500 the month before) — context, never a band valuation. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: A card shows a detached rancher — is that a freehold lot?** Not necessarily: Heritage Park Place cards describe detached ranchers inside a strata subdivision. Check the strata plan — a detached strata lot carries Form B, fees and bylaws that a fee-simple lot does not. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the top of this band?** At $1,200,000, fully residential with no exemption: $22,000 (tier math in §3). At $800,000 it is $14,000 — or $6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer, per the program anchor. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can a townhome in this band have a suite?** No — Mission does not permit secondary suites in townhouses or rowhomes, and every Mission suite must be City-registered in a single family home. A suite claim on a townhouse card is a verification item, not income. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Mission lot instead of buying a townhome?** Subdivision is a City process under DSCB 5650-2017 and the zone minimums in Bylaw 5949-2020, with DCCs at approval — and ALC approval if the parcel is ALR. No parcel's potential is stated without its zone and ALR status. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does GST apply to a newly subdivided lot in Mission?** At the CRA GI-003 level, generally yes — a parcel created by subdividing into more than two parts, or land sold in the course of a business, is a taxable case, while most individual sales of personal-use vacant land are exempt. CPA per transaction; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. (§3 above)
- **Q: How is the commute from a Mission lot or townhome?** West Coast Express: five weekday morning trains, 75 minutes Mission City to Waterfront, five afternoon returns, no weekend service — a weekday asset only. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q: Which school catchment applies?** UNVERIFIED for any address here — SD75 is a three-tier, address-based system and no Mission locator run is on record. Confirm the exact address with the district; never promise a school from the Mission label. (§1 above)
- **Q: Why is the $1.2M ceiling rarely the binding number in Mission?** Because the published attached community median ($430,000, Sep 2026) sits $770,000 below the ceiling — arithmetic against a community median, labelled as such; it does not state how many listings fall inside the band, and no band count exists. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
