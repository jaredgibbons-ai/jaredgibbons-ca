## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Deep Cove is the waterfront village at the District of North Vancouver's far eastern edge, on the shore of Indian Arm at the foot of Mount Seymour, built around Gallant Avenue, Deep Cove Road and Panorama Drive. Deep Cove is one of the six Village Centres named in the District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900 (with Lions Gate, Queensdale, Maplewood, Parkgate and Edgemont) — a confirmed District planning name. Exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are the village core on lower Gallant Avenue, Panorama Park on Panorama Drive, and the Cove Cliff / Banbury Road streets above the village. Paragon (GVR) sub-area match and code: UNVERIFIED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Nearby areas: Cove Cliff and Dollarton (west, along Dollarton Highway), Parkgate and Seymour (south-west, at Mount Seymour Parkway), Woodlands and Indian Arm (north, up the inlet), and Belcarra across Indian Arm. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Roads and access: Deep Cove Road / Gallant Avenue and Dollarton Highway are the only road in and out — west to Mount Seymour Parkway, Phibbs Exchange and Highway 1; there is no second road connection north up Indian Arm for cars. Transit: local buses run from Phibbs Exchange via Dollarton Highway and Mount Seymour Parkway to the village terminus on Panorama Drive / Gallant Avenue; since September 7, 2026 the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs one-seat from Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown, roughly 5 a.m.–2 a.m., every 6–7 minutes at peak. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Deep Cove — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, no assembly or development site, and no presale lot offering was verified on any official or developer source. The confirmed current District work in the village is the Livable Deep Cove lower-Gallant project — a public-realm project, not a housing project and not lot inventory. Land trades as built fee-simple house lots, including waterfront freehold on the inlet streets; a lot's potential is set by its R1/R2 zone minimum, overlapping Schedule B hazard permit areas, and foreshore tenure on waterfront parcels.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210 ONLY:** Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) detached zones as stated in Section 2. The CITY of North Vancouver's Zoning Bylaw 6700 never applies to this District village. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **OCP — District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900:** covers this area and names Deep Cove a Village Centre; the Livable Deep Cove project is the current village-core implementation work under it. The City's OCP Bylaw 8400 never applies here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/livable-deep-cove-survey-2022.pdf
- **Added units / SSMUH, as the District and Province state them:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GeoWeb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. Eligibility is parcel-specific. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** no District of North Vancouver servicing-bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted — and the CITY's Subdivision Bylaw 8014 must never be imported into a District file. The process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's Section 525 zone minimums (Section 2), servicing runs on the District's 2026 single-family utility structure (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; combined $2,571.20), and Schedule B hazard permit areas (below) gate slope, creek and shoreline work. Confirm requirements with the District for the parcel. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas (OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B):** seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Deep Cove's forested slope, creek and shoreline lots can fall in several of these at once. Whether a lot is in one is a District GeoWeb map check. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Foreshore / dock diligence (waterfront lots):** confirm foreshore, dock and lease status parcel by parcel on title and District records; no foreshore tenure fact is confirmed in this file, so treat any moorage claim as UNVERIFIED until those records say otherwise. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Bare-land strata rules — stated for the queue contingency (no verified bare-land strata plan exists here):** if a Deep Cove lot's title confirms bare-land strata tenure (the Cove Gardens question is exactly this, held UNCONFIRMED in townhomes), the lot IS strata tenure in full: Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Deep Cove ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Deep Cove lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Deep Cove-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this village's land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Deep Cove, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new subdivision I can buy into in Deep Cove?** No verified one — the village is established District housing; land trades as built fee-simple house lots, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. The Livable Deep Cove project is public-realm work, not lots. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Deep Cove lot?** Only if its Section 525 zone minimum allows it: from R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width up to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m width, with standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m — confirm the lot's zone in the District's GeoWeb. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can the lot hold a suite and a coach house too?** Yes, as the District states it: since June 2024 three units total (principal, secondary suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, with the GeoWeb SSMUH classification potentially allowing up to 6 units on a non-exempt lot over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — parcel-specific. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: What comes with a waterfront lot — is the dock mine?** UNCONFIRMED until checked: no Deep Cove foreshore tenure fact is confirmed in this file, so confirm foreshore, dock and lease status on title and District records for the specific parcel before pricing any moorage claim. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: What hazard checks apply to a slope or shoreline lot?** Schedule B streamside, creek, slope and wildfire permit areas overlap heavily in the Cove, and the District's flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m — run the GeoWeb layers before pricing a renovation or rebuild. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: Is Cove Gardens a way to buy land in the Cove?** No — Cove Gardens is a built townhouse-form complex held in QUEUE in the townhomes sibling (tenure UNCONFIRMED, counted nowhere); it is not a lot offering, and this file takes no lot count from it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How does parking and access affect a village-core lot?** The village runs on a single road in and out and a managed parking regime: a Parking and Access Implementation Plan endorsed in 2015 and a further Parking Review to Council on April 22, 2024 covering on-street pay, resident and time-limited parking — summer visitor pressure is a priced feature of village-core streets. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=6278173
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Deep Cove lot?** By exact address only — no village catchment is settled in this file; Cove Cliff Elementary is in the village and Seycove Secondary beside it, but Seycove's family also includes Dorothy Lynas and Sherwood Park elementaries, so a street west of the village may feed a Parkgate-side school. Check the address with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
