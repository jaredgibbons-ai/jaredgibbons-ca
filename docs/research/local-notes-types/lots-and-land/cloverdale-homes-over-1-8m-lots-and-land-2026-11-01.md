## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / zoning:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; former RF-12 / RF-12C → R4 and RF-SD → R6. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, not within a coach house or garden suite, habitable floor area at most 40% of the principal dwelling unit — not a separate strata lot. Coach / garden suites: 35–75 m², up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR, lane access on smaller R3–R6 lots, one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. **The Surrey servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file** — no number is stated in this slug's research or a cited City source opened for it, and no other city's number is borrowed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under 90 consecutive days per patron, owner must live at the property as primary residence, tenants cannot run one. Licence figure CONFLICT: $358/yr program figure carried, with the City's 2024 corporate report proposing $350/yr + fees disclosed — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (any bare-land strata lot).** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block.** Residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class in FULL three-tier form: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no file's own research documents a flat form for this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT at this band — the exemptions do not reach it.** Neither BC purchase exemption reaches this band: the first-time home buyers' full exemption caps at $835,000 and the newly built home exemption's full threshold is $1,100,000 with elimination at $1,150,000 — both below the $1.8M floor, so buyers here budget full PTT (worked examples in §2). **The further 2% tier:** the Province applies a further 2% to the residential property value greater than $3,000,000, on top of the general tiers; on a mixed-class property it applies only to the residential portion. Where this file's research notes the live page's "Over $3M — 5%" row, that row is a compression of 3% + 2% — stated here in the full two-part form, never as a single 5% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Home owner grant phase-out (directly relevant at this band).** The Province states the 2026 grant threshold is $2,075,000 — full regular grant at or below that assessed value, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, and $0 for the regular grant over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (personal-use land is the example); a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs.** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements March 20, 2025–2031) is for new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the $1.8M floor?** $34,000 on the anchor math in §2 (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $1,600,000), before any exemption — and no BC purchase exemption reaches this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is there extra PTT over $3 million?** Yes — a further 2% on the residential value over $3,000,000, on top of the 3% tier: at $3,500,000 the total is $93,000 including that further tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the first-time or new-home exemption help at $1.8M?** No. The first-time program caps at $835,000 and the newly built exemption is gone at $1,150,000 — budget full PTT. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Will I still get the home owner grant?** Check the assessed value against the 2026 threshold: full regular grant at or below $2,075,000, reduced by $5 per $1,000 over it, $0 over $2,189,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/home-owner-grant
- **Q: A Clayton parcel is marketed as a development opportunity — what do I verify?** Its TOA tier and its current OCP designation with the City, parcel-by-parcel. Listing text is not a designation, the old NCP labels are superseded by Surrey 2050, and both points are UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/west-clayton-ncp
- **Q: What are the house limits on an ALR parcel in this band?** One principal residence at most 500 m², any suite inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); subdivision needs ALC approval. Check ALR status on the parcel before relying on those limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the suite in a custom home at this band legal?** Verify in documents, not listing text — the feed describes both legal and unauthorized suites at this band. Surrey's rule is one suite per dwelling unit at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Q: Is GST charged on a vacant parcel over $1.8M?** At CRA GI-003 level only: most sales by individuals are exempt (personal-use land); a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable. Anything finer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
