## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020,** adopted September 27, 2022, covering the whole city including the South Bonson area; it repealed the former OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones for house lots (minimums in Section 2), RM-1 / RM-2 for townhouse and low-density apartment sites; residential buildings sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches. Confirm the parcel's zone in the consolidated bylaw. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states it:** under provincial rules applied citywide, lots 280 sq m or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 sq m up to 4 units, and lots over 280 sq m within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6 units; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024, and OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers 2–6 units through secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings. Suites: one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites are one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. **Short-term rentals are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows.** https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2 (with the 1,215 sq m clarification above). A servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED (City minutes name a Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw with an Amendment Bylaw No. 2833, 2019, but no base-bylaw number on a primary source, so no number is quoted); the process documented instead is zone minimums + the 15 m ditch/watercourse siting rule + the drainage/dike position below, confirmed with the City for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Floodplain / drainage diligence:** over 95% of the city is in the floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — governing figure; see Section 5 for the live conflict), behind 60 km of dikes in drainage Area 3 for this sub-area. Pitt Meadows charges a separate drainage levy inside Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 — for 2026 the residential (Class 1) drainage rate is $0.1082 per $1,000 of taxable value, alongside the general municipal rate of $2.8438 per $1,000. Budget the levy as its own line and confirm floodplain, dike and drainage details for the specific lot with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **ALR rules (citywide frame — 78% of Pitt Meadows is ALR; per-parcel check at this sub-area's edges):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Strata block — applies to the built townhouse ground (Coho cluster, in its home file), NOT to bare land here:** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw — and in Pitt Meadows STRs are PROHIBITED citywide in any event); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **The Fairways' age position is UNCONFIRMED and is never called 55+ in this file; the Advent Road 55+ pocket is board-classified Central Meadows, not this sub-area.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a South Meadows lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no South Meadows-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for South Meadows, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Pitt Meadows property taxes:** 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026, under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), with a 5% penalty added after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026.
