## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Verified position, stated plainly at the top: Panorama Ridge has NO meaningful bare-land market documented in its research file. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or lot-only development is verified on Panorama Ridge ground. The area is built-out fee-simple detached ground on a ridge, with strata townhouse pockets and one condo presale — a "land" purchase here is an existing house lot bought for rebuild / added units, subject to the creek, slope-edge and servicing checks in Section 3. No lot counts, lot prices or lot-only inventory are asserted.**

- **What the ground is.** Detached houses are the South Newton plan area's base residential form, and the research records most housing here as fee-simple detached. The ridge lots are the product; there is no new bare-land subdivision feeding the market in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Fee-simple is the default lot form; no bare-land strata verified.** Strata product in the sub-area is townhouse/condo strata under the Strata Property Act — not bare-land strata. No bare-land strata is verified on Panorama Ridge ground in the research or any sibling checked. Tenure is read lot by lot on title. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lot character — zone minimum AS THE RESEARCH STATES IT.** South Newton lands are zoned under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (R-series residential, with RA at the rural edges toward Colebrook); the Panorama Ridge block of the research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² at Part 26 Section F of the bylaw. That figure is carried exactly as the research states it for this plan area — it differs from the general R3 figure carried in the East Newton research (464 m² for a single-family home), and the parcel's own zone section governs: confirm the specific lot's zone and section with the City before relying on either number. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Creek ground is part of the lot fabric.** The plan area includes fish-bearing creeks — Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries — so riparian setback rules can apply to creek-side lots and can limit the building envelope. This is a land fact, not a house fact: it survives a teardown. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **ALR vs non-ALR.** ALR land inside this sub-area: UNVERIFIED in the research — no ALR split is asserted. The Serpentine lowlands lie south of Highway 10. Check any Colebrook-edge parcel on the ALC maps. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Serviced vs unserviced — the Colebrook edge is the open question.** Whether individual Colebrook Road–edge properties are on septic or individual services is UNVERIFIED in the research (listed in its UNVERIFIED items). Do not assume community water/sewer at the rural edges; properties not connected to community water and sewer are also an SSMUH exemption category. Confirm servicing for the specific lot with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Not land stock — Amson Bloc (presale condos, counted separately).** Amson Bloc, by Amson Group, at 5937 144 St — a presale condo project (Phase 1 homes arriving summer 2026, Phase 2 selling, per the developer) — is documented in the condos sibling. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Zoning Bylaw 12000. The South Newton NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 update and is no longer in effect; Bylaw No. 18020 is never current. A seller quoting NCP densities is quoting a dead document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (R-series, RA at the Colebrook rural edges; R3 minimum subdivision lot area 660 m² as recorded for this plan area — see Section 2 for the two-figure caution). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house; unpermitted existing suites are not legalized by the new rules. Servicing is a condition in practice: properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions — directly relevant at the Colebrook edge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** One per house; Surrey's cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable area, one per unit, not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a Panorama Ridge parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no City figure for this area is stated in the research. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Riparian / creek diligence (the Panorama Ridge land item).** Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through the plan area; riparian/setback requirements and any development permit areas can limit building envelopes on creek-side lots — check City mapping and the lot's permit areas before valuing an addition, rebuild or subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Slope / south-edge diligence.** The research documents the ridge form, Highway 10 at the south edge and the lowlands beyond it: check highway noise exposure and confirm the lot is on the upland side of the floodplain picture before subjects. A parcel-level geotechnical report requirement for a specific Panorama Ridge lot: UNCONFIRMED in the research — obtain the City's requirements for the parcel rather than assuming a slope study is or is not required. (Research file §6.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under licence. Figure CONFLICT: $358/yr (program figure) vs $350/yr plus fees proposed in the City's 2024 corporate report — carry $358 with $350 disclosed; verify on the City licence page before quoting.
- **Bare-land strata — the form (no Panorama Ridge instance verified).** A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B, fees and bylaws apply to the lot. Surrey precedent: Sea Ridge Bays (15875 20 Ave, South Surrey), documented in its home area's files in the 55-plus program — cross-reference only. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **BC strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR rules (only where a parcel is confirmed ALR — none confirmed in-area).** Residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** PTT applies unless an exemption applies. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home, not the lot alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots beyond that line are generally taxable. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED per lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; residential owners file the annual declaration and a vacant parcel's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** Secondary sources disagree on the vacant-land position after March 27, 2023; no primary text opened in this pass: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide). No Panorama Ridge-specific levy found: UNVERIFIED as an area item. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure note.** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure mapping is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy on the ridge?** No bare-land subdivision is verified — the purchase is an existing fee-simple house lot for rebuild/added units. Strata complexes and the Amson Bloc presale are built/presale product, not lots. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: How much of my creek-side lot can I actually build on?** Riparian setbacks for Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries can limit the envelope — get the City's watercourse mapping and permit-area check for the parcel before valuing a rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Is a Colebrook-edge lot on sewer and water?** UNVERIFIED per lot in the research — septic/individual services are possible at that edge, and unconnected properties are an SSMUH exemption category. Confirm with the City before counting added units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How many units can the lot hold?** 3 under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible 281 m²+ lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — subject to zone, servicing and any creek/permit-area limits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What is the minimum lot I can subdivide to?** The research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² for this plan area (Bylaw 12000, Part 26 Section F) — confirm the parcel's own zone section with the City, because the general R3 figure elsewhere in Surrey research differs. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Q: Do I need a geotechnical study on the ridge/south edge?** The research does not document a parcel-level requirement — UNCONFIRMED. What is documented: Highway 10 noise/floodplain-edge checks for south-edge lots. Ask the City what studies the parcel's permit areas require before subjects. (Research file §6.)
- **Q: Will GST or PTT relief help on a lot purchase?** GST turns on the seller/subdivision history (CRA rules, Section 3); PTT relief turns on building/occupying a qualifying home; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. None can be assumed on a lot alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold land here?** Surrey taxes (due July 2; 5% + 5% penalties) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — Surrey is a taxable area. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Q: Can I trust the South Newton plan densities a seller quotes?** No — the South Newton NCP was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; the OCP (Bylaw 21900) and Zoning Bylaw 12000 govern. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Q: Which schools serve a ridge lot?** Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave), Colebrook Elementary (5404 125a St), McLeod Road Traditional (6325 142 St — correct name) and W.E. Kinvig Elementary at the west edge are the in-area schools. A settled SD36 locator chain IS documented for 13220 64 Ave: North Ridge Elementary → École Panorama Ridge (locator run 2026-11-01, research file §4). Any other address: run the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
