## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs.** Its s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties (see floodplain entry below). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024):** flagged here and wherever met in this file, resolved nowhere — quote planning policy for a specific parcel from a current City document, not from a bylaw number carried by memory. (Program standing correction, 2026-11-01.)
- **Plans:** the Town Centre is the city's planned condo core — the Town Centre Area Plan update expanded the plan boundaries to incorporate River Bend, consolidated the land use designations, and added a Town Centre Core designation supporting higher densities in a transit-oriented downtown anchored by the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station; Transit-Oriented Area Designation Bylaw No. 8001-2024 covers the Haney Transit Exchange and Port Haney Station TOAs. The **Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025** and the **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — both cited as adopted. SSMUH: eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws, settled lot by lot. https://mapleridge.ca/your-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan-area-plans/area-neighbourhood-plans/town and https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Transit for the central stock:** Haney Place Exchange routes include the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus (Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place), and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) runs West Coast Express trains on weekdays between Waterfront (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf and https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express
- **Floodplain at the south edge of the core:** the Port Haney / River Road edge sits along the Fraser River — the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, and no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots; for a strata building, flood status is a building-level check on title and in the strata documents. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only) — the 3% tier and the further 2% do not arise in this band. PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. Worked math in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at fair market value ≤$835,000 (maximum saving $8,000), partial to $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all the program's other conditions (citizenship or permanent residency, BC residence history, never having owned a principal residence, property 0.5 hectares or smaller with only residential improvements, principal-residence use). **Newly Built Home Exemption:** full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (strata parcels, including any bare-land strata parcel):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **ALR:** no ALR parcel is documented under this band in the research; where a Maple Ridge parcel is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval, ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), caps are principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), and farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. ALR status is parcel-specific via ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own research supports it — the research cites no prohibition source for this band, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY.

- **Q: Can a first-time buyer use the PTT exemption under $800K?** In principle, yes — the program's full exemption runs to a fair market value of $835,000 (exempting the first $500,000 of price), with a partial exemption to $860,000, provided the buyer meets all the program's personal and property conditions; the exemption is claimed through the buyer's legal professional on the PTT return. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: The page copy quotes strata-fee and deductible ranges — can I budget off them?** No. Those editorial figures (unit-type price ranges, an East Central housing-mix percentage, strata-fee ranges and insurance-deductible ranges) are site copy verified on no official source in this research, and none is repeated here as fact. - **Q: What should I check before removing subjects on a Maple Ridge condo?** The strata package (Form B, depreciation report, minutes, insurance certificate) under the provincial strata framework — plus, for a building at the Port Haney river edge, the floodplain check: flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic and a covenant on title for floodplain construction. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Is GST due on a newly subdivided lot in this city?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003 (developer/newly subdivided = generally taxable); an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt — and the FTHB GST rebate reaches new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
