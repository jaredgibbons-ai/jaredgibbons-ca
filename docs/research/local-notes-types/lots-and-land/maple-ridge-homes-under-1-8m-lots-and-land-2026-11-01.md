## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning and OCP:** Zoning Bylaw **7600-2019** applies city-wide (RS-1 at s.605, §2). - **Lougheed Transit Corridor — adopted position, with the correction stated:** cite the corridor as **ADOPTED January 27, 2026** per the City record — OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 received third reading and adoption on January 27, 2026, the City issued a news release on February 4, 2026, and the corridor ends at **221 St**. - **SSMUH / suites as the City states them:** one secondary suite per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum **40% of gross floor area**, and **not within the floodplain area**; eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to **three, four or six units** under the City's SSMUH bylaws. Per-parcel eligibility is confirmed with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision and servicing in Maple Ridge run under the **Maple Ridge Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993**, as amended — that number is stated here only because a City of Maple Ridge source supports it (the City's consolidated bylaw document, found in this pass); this slug's research file itself carries no servicing number, and no other city's number is borrowed. Servicing requirements for a specific parcel are confirmed with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/subdivision-development-bylaw-no-4800-1993
- **Floodplain and escarpment (south and west edges):** along the Fraser River / Port Haney edge the flood construction level is **6.7 m geodetic** (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies floodplain construction, no habitable floor space may sit below that level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s.402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. Lots within 300 m of the Fraser River Escarpment crest (east boundary 224 Street) require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0 and http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **ALR block — full form (the band's rural edges):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act, s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — applies to any strata lot in the city, including a bare-land strata lot:** Form B (SPA s.59), within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** tiers and worked examples are in §2. First-time buyers' exemption (full at ≤$835,000, exempting the first $500,000, partial to $860,000) and the newly built home exemption are HOME exemptions — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA) rebates 100% of GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, **NOT bare land**. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
