## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **District plans (District of North Vancouver — not the City):** the Official Community Plan is **Bylaw 7900** (Parkgate is one of its six Village Centres; its 2030 housing-mix target for the District is 55% detached / 45% attached) and the zoning bylaw is **Zoning Bylaw 3210**. The Seymour Local Plan separately governs this community: slow, limited growth, single-family character protected in the established neighbourhoods, townhouse development contemplated on assembled sites on the north side of Mount Seymour Parkway (3200–3500 blocks) at 0.6–1.2 FSR. **City of North Vancouver bylaws (OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700) do not apply anywhere in this file's ground.** https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf and https://councilarchive.dnv.org/upload/documents/Council_Reports/SLP_2003_6.0.pdf
- **SSMUH / ground-oriented potential, as the District states it:** under Zoning Bylaw 3210 as amended by Bylaw 8698 (June 2024), most single-family lots in the District may hold a main house, a secondary suite and a coach house; December 2025 Ground-Oriented Housing rules permit up to four homes on more than 4,000 eligible District properties, and in April 2026 Council voted against adopting the six-home near-transit tier — multiplex rules are still in flux and must be checked lot by lot. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/new-provincial-housing-legislation-fact-sheet.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/3-1-multiunit-housing.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED (the process is documented instead):** the District of North Vancouver's servicing / subdivision bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this program — no number is asserted for the District. The City of North Vancouver's Bylaw 8014 is a **City-files-only** instrument and is named here solely as a never-imported exclusion: it is not the District's bylaw and must never be carried into a District file as one. A Seymour subdivision or servicing question is taken to the District for the parcel, with the number settled from a District source at that point.
- **Natural Hazard Development Permit Areas:** the District designates Natural Hazard DPs for slope and creek hazards and publishes property-level hazard reports through GeoWeb; its Landslide Risk Assessment was updated in 2026. Sloped and creek-adjacent Seymour lots should be checked before purchase — a DP can trigger geotechnical reports for renovations and rebuilds. No other city's floodplain zone is imported here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/bgclandslideriskassessment2026updatereportfinal.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Seymour ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is not asserted for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata frame (for the Parkgate/Parkway strata pockets cross-referenced in §2 — no bare-land strata is verified here):** Form B under SPA s. 59 (fees, amounts owing, special levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; rules, budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and depreciation report attached; within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024; at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **District property taxes (2026):** Council approved a 3.53% municipal tax increase for 2026 (2.03% for services plus 1.5% for infrastructure renewal); the District's published 2026 Class 1 (residential) total rate is $3.28195 per $1,000 of assessed value across all taxing authorities; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 1, 2026. Utility fees are billed separately. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/tax-newsletter-2026.pdf and https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $1,500,000 purchase (round illustration only — no area median exists to use) with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**.
