## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Harrison Hot Springs part of Kent?** No — it is a separate village municipality carved out of Kent at the south end of Harrison Lake, with its own taxes, OCP (Bylaw No. 1184, 2022) and market, even though Kent surrounds it. https://www.kentbc.ca/media/0enenmdy/doc_636900753607636141.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/harrison-hot-springs.md)

- **Q: What did Harrison homes sell for in September 2026?** UNVERIFIED — there is no Harrison row in SnapStats and CADREB publishes no area stats, so no median, sales count or ratio is stated in this file and no other community's figure is substituted. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/harrison-hot-springs.md)
- **Q: A unit at the Harrison Beach Hotel is priced like a cheap condo — what is it?** Read the interest, not the price: the documented offering is a 1/3 fractional ownership with two weeks of use per year in a strata-hotel operation — a tenure record, not a lot and not a residential strata lot you can live in or rent freely. Confirm the exact interest on title. (§2)
- **Q: Is the village in a flood zone?** On the lakeshore and Miami River side, yes. The flood construction level is 14.55 m (Bylaw No. 1179, 2022); the existing dike crest (13.9 m) sits below the 1894 flood of record (14.1 m) and the required 15.1 m crest, and a combined fixed + deployable dike is in detailed design for Zones 3–6. Check a lot's floor elevation against the FCL before writing. (§3)
- **Q: Can I run a Harrison lot purchase as a nightly rental?** Possibly, but on Village rules: the Village is exempt from the provincial principal-residence rule as a resort region, Tourist Accommodation zoning under Bylaw No. 1115, 2017 applies, each dwelling unit so operated needs its own business licence since Dec 15, 2025, the 3% MRDT applies (renewal due Jan 1, 2027), and a strata's own bylaws apply on top where strata applies. (§3)
- **Q: Is the east-side fringe lot in the ALR?** Possibly — a small ALR fringe exists on the village's east side and the exact share is UNVERIFIED. Check the parcel on the ALC maps; subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval and ALR residences cannot be stratified. (§3)
- **Q: How much PTT on a $700K Harrison lot?** $12,000 at the general tiers with no exemption (§3) — a round illustration, because no Harrison median exists in any published source in this program. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Are the trailer parks on Hot Springs Road a way to buy land?** No — Glencoe and Harrison Holiday Park are pad-rental tenancies: the home is owned, the pad is rented, MHPTA pad-rent limits apply (2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount). Neither is lot stock or land ownership. (§2)
- **Q: Where do Harrison kids go to school?** Harrison Hot Springs Elementary (K–6) at 501 Hot Springs Road in the village, then Agassiz Elementary-Secondary School (7–12) in Agassiz — both SD78.
