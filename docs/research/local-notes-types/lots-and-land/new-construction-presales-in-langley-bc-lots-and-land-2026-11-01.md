## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **REDMA — the presale frame (applies to every Langley presale, either municipality):** under the Real Estate Development Marketing Act, a purchaser may rescind a purchase agreement by serving written notice on the developer within seven days after the later of the date the purchase agreement was made and the date the developer obtained the purchaser's receipt for the Disclosure Statement (s.21); a developer must give each purchaser a copy of the filed Disclosure Statement, and any filed amendment, before entering into the agreement (s.15); deposits must be held in trust as s.18 requires. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01
- **Township frame (projects on Township ground ONLY):** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs. SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024): 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², eligibility requires municipal water and sewer, and NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020. Secondary suite cap 120 m²; a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — never strata (**LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102, July 2025, permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted). R-CL s.403: 220 m² minimum lot area to 464.5 m² maximum LOT area — no "355 m²" figure exists. Subdivision and servicing: Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED; subdivisions are approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act. Willoughby Plan 1836 became Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 reached first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026 — never upgrade reading-stage bylaws. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Langley City frame (projects on City ground ONLY — never imported into Township ground):** Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, cited by number (adoption date UNVERIFIED); SSMUH Bylaw 3284; secondary suite = the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. City servicing-bylaw number: UNCONFIRMED in this file — no number is asserted for the City. (Program standing corrections.)
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists — parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (a completed bare-land strata lot, where one exists):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of this exists yet for an unbuilt presale unit at signing — see the REDMA block above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**, before any exemption. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Newly Built Home Exemption (the presale PTT break, verified and matching the live page's thresholds):** full exemption at fair market value ≤$1,100,000, partial phasing out over the $50,000 range above it, complete elimination at $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — and the buyer does not need to be a first-time buyer. FTHB PTT exemption: first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. A presale HOME, by contrast, is new housing — see the rebate below. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Which Langley projects are selling now, and when do they complete?** UNVERIFIED in this file: the live page's browse section named no projects in the text read, and no developer source for a named Langley project was opened in the research pass — project names, pricing and completion dates must be confirmed project-by-project from developer sources and disclosure statements. The project list is QUEUED pending that verification (§2). https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
- **Q: How long can I take to cancel a presale after signing?** Seven days: under REDMA s.21 you may rescind by written notice to the developer within seven days after the later of the purchase agreement date and the date the developer obtained your receipt for the Disclosure Statement — get the contract and disclosure statement to a presale lawyer inside that window. https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **Q: Where does my presale deposit sit?** In trust, as REDMA s.18 requires — verify the trust holder named in the disclosure statement before paying any instalment. The live page's 10%–20% range is its own presentation, UNVERIFIED for any named project; the binding schedule is the one in the specific project's contract. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/04066_01 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/new-construction-presales-in-langley-bc.html
- **Q: Do I pay property transfer tax on a Langley presale?** Often not on a principal residence: BC's Newly Built Home Exemption fully exempts a qualifying purchase at a fair market value of $1,100,000 or less, with a partial exemption phasing out to $1,150,000, effective April 1, 2024 — and you do not need to be a first-time buyer. On a $700,000 purchase with no exemption the PTT would be $12,000 (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **Q: Can a first-time buyer get the GST back on a new Langley home?** Yes: under Bill C-4 (Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA guidance) an eligible first-time buyer receives a rebate of 100% of the GST on a newly constructed home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, with no rebate at $1.5 million or more. The rebate is for new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Is a presale lot the same as buying resale land?** No. A presale is a REDMA developer contract with no Form B, no depreciation report and no registered bylaws at signing, and it is never counted as land or as resale stock in this program. **Presale LOT offerings verified for Langley: none — count 0 (§2).** https://www.bcfsa.ca/industry-resources/real-estate-developer-resources/real-estate-development-marketing-act-faqs
- **Q: Which bylaws govern a project — Township or City?** The project's own ground decides: Township ground takes Zoning Bylaw 2500, SSMUH Bylaw 6020 and Servicing Bylaw 5382; Langley City ground takes Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 and SSMUH Bylaw 3284, with a suite cap of the lesser of 100 m² and 40% against the Township's 120 m². Never blend the two frames. (§3.)
- **Q: What did Langley condos and townhomes sell for in September 2026?** As resale context only — not presale figures: the Langley community Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales, the low of its 13-month range. (Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
