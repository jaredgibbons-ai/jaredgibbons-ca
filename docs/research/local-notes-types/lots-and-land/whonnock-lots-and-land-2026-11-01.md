- **Suites and added units, as the City and the ALR Regulation state them:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted in listed zones including **A-1, A-2 and A-3**, contained within the single detached building, to a maximum of **40% of gross floor area**, and **not in the Floodplain Area**. On ALR parcels the provincial ALR Use Regulation also governs: it permits one secondary suite in the principal residence (s.31) and an additional residence only if the s.32 conditions are met. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo105/loo105/30_2019
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision and servicing in Maple Ridge run under the **Maple Ridge Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 4800-1993**, as amended — that number is stated here only because a City of Maple Ridge source supports it (the City's consolidated bylaw document, found in this pass); this area's research file itself carries no servicing number, and no other city's number is borrowed. On rural Whonnock parcels, servicing is the well/septic diligence in §2, confirmed parcel by parcel. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/subdivision-development-bylaw-no-4800-1993
- **ALR block — full form (Whonnock's agricultural ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act, s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is documented on this area's page in its research, so no page defect is asserted on that point. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — the frame if any strata lot is ever met here (none is verified, §2):** Form B (SPA s.59), within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax — worked anchors for acreage pricing:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Before any exemption: $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $800,000 → $14,000; **$1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000**; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier (the "Over $3M — 5%" shorthand sometimes seen is a compression of 3% + 2% — that page row is **not** documented in this slug's research, so no defect is asserted here on that point). First-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are HOME exemptions — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific Whonnock parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
