## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Bridal Falls part of Chilliwack?** No — it is an unincorporated community in FVRD Electoral Area D, east of the City of Chilliwack; the FVRD sets its plan and zoning and Chilliwack bylaws do not apply. (§1, §3.)
- **Q: What land can I actually buy here?** The verified land form is a bare-land strata lot at The Cottages at Bridal Falls (53480 Bridal Falls Road) — freehold strata, serviced lots with concrete pads for a cottage home or newer RV, no pad rent. No fee-simple lot subdivision verifies in this file's research. (§2.)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a manufactured-home pad?** No — it is freehold strata ownership under the Strata Property Act (Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the lot), not pad rental under the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act. (§2, §3.)
- **Q: What plan covers Bridal Falls?** The guide page states the Popkum-Bridal Falls (Area D) OCP, FVRD Bylaw No. 1501, adopted January 2021 — carried as guide-stated only, UNVERIFIED against the FVRD record in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bridal-falls-guide.html
- **Q: Are there published statistics for Bridal Falls?** No — SnapStats publishes no Chilliwack/FVRD figures and CADREB publishes no area stats held in this program; no other community's figures are substituted. (§1.)
- **Q: Which school is near Bridal Falls?** Rosedale Traditional Community, 50850 Yale Rd Rr 1, Rosedale (Ministry-verified, described by the guide as K–8); no school is in Bridal Falls itself, and address-level catchments including the grades 9–12 school are UNVERIFIED. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
