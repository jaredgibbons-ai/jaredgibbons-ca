Parcel-level subdivision requirements, approving-officer conditions and servicing standards are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED beyond the bylaw frame stated here.
- **SSMUH / added-unit potential as the City/province states it:** parcel-specific — the number of units a Vancouver lot can hold depends on its district schedule, servicing and any heritage or lease constraint; no city-wide unit count is stated in this slug's research, so no figure is asserted here: UNVERIFIED at city level. Confirm zone and services for the parcel before pricing in added units.
- **Heritage districts:** Chinatown, Gastown, Shaughnessy and Yaletown carry City heritage regulations and companion design guidelines — exterior change, demolition and redevelopment on land in those areas is governed by that frame, not by the base district alone. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%:** Vancouver owners of residential property must submit an annual property status declaration on or before the 2nd business day in February under the City's Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674, and a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The Empty Homes Tax rate carried in this program's Vancouver frame is 3% — a City of Vancouver tax that applies in Vancouver ONLY and is never exported to any other municipality's file. This slug's research pass read the declaration and penalty frame but did not state the rate on the City pages read, so the rate rests on the program's verified Vancouver frame, not on this slug's research alone. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Property taxes:** the City of Vancouver collects property tax twice a year — advance taxes due the second business day of February (February 3, 2026) and main taxes due the second business day of July (July 3, 2026) — and the last day to pay the 2026 main tax, claim the homeowner grant and complete a deferment application to avoid a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance was July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form, wherever a Vancouver bare-land strata plan is met):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% per the condos sibling. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant house lot anywhere in Vancouver from this page?** This city file carries no lot roster — land is documented in the sub-area lots-and-land files cross-referenced in Section 2. Start from the sub-area file for the ground you want, then confirm the specific parcel's tenure, zone and servicing. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Does SnapStats publish a Vancouver land or house median I can price against?** No — SnapStats publishes no Vancouver figures at all, and land has no SnapStats category in any community. Any Vancouver price claim needs a Paragon/board source. (Section 1.)
- **Q: What bylaw governs subdividing a Vancouver lot?** Subdivision By-law No. 5208, with fees under By-law No. 14538. The approving officer's parcel-level conditions are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Is all Vancouver land freehold?** No — tenure varies by ground. False Creek South sits on 60-year City ground leases (home file: false-creek lots file) and Champlain Heights carries a 99-year prepaid leasehold position; both are documented in their own files and neither is a city-wide rule. Read tenure off title for the parcel you are buying. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, if I meet one?** It is strata tenure on bare land: you own a strata lot, the strata's Form B, fees and bylaws apply, and the standard strata diligence in Section 3 applies to the lot itself.
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant Vancouver lot I hold?** Vancouver's Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674 requires every residential property owner to file an annual property status declaration by the 2nd business day in February; the EHT rate in the program's Vancouver frame is 3%, Vancouver ONLY. Whether a specific vacant parcel is taxable is a declaration question for that parcel — confirm with the City. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: How much property transfer tax on a $1.5M Vancouver lot?** $28,000 on the general scale (1% / 2% / 3% tiers); $3.5M attracts $93,000 including the further 2% tier on the residential portion above $3M. Exemptions are fact-specific — the FTHB exemption maxes at an $8,000 saving and the Newly Built Home Exemption is for a new home, not bare land. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve my Vancouver address?** SD39, address-based. Ministry-confirmed schools sampled in the research include Elsie Roy Elementary (150 Drake St), Crosstown Elementary (55 Expo Blvd), Lord Roberts Elementary (1100 Bidwell St) and King George Secondary (1755 Barclay St) — but catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED in this city file; run the exact address with SD39 before any school claim. (Section 1.)
- **Q: When are Vancouver property taxes due?** Twice a year: advance taxes February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
