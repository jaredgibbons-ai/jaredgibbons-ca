## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs, lot by lot. Secondary suites under s.102 (as amended July 2025): capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit, and on ALR lands a suite must comply with ALC regulations. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata — in s.102 a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage; no coach-house size cap appears in s.100 as read — a cap is UNVERIFIED. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits a suite in a townhouse/rowhouse, while the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide says suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area); lots over 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Most parcels in this feed exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403 (carried so it is never misapplied):** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file; R-CL is a Willoughby cluster zone and does not govern this sub-area's acreage parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED per the verified ledger. For this rural sub-area, servicing for any specific parcel (water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED in the research pass — confirm per PID with the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve — Glen Valley is farm ground and this is the first check on any parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Whether any specific parcel is in the ALR: UNVERIFIED — parcel-specific on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified here):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw.
