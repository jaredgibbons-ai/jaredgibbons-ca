## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans and zoning:** Richmond **Zoning Bylaw 8500** governs; the **Official Community Plan is Bylaw 9000**, whose City Centre Area Plan (plan adopted Sept 14, 2009; original adoption June 19, 1995) designates Brighouse Village for high-density, high-rise, mixed-use development (transects to T6 / 45 m, §1), with the Brighouse Village Specific Land Use Map (2031) amended by Bylaw 10190 and a height note for established Airport Zoning Regulations. Single Detached zones RS1/RS2 in Section 8.1 apply outside the City Centre designations, with secondary suites under Section 5.4. https://www.richmond.ca/city-hall/strategies/ocp/about.htm and https://cms.richmond.ca/AssetFactory.aspx?did=23858 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Subdivision and servicing — Richmond Bylaws 8751 + 8752 VERIFIED** (verified ledger, carried exactly): subdivision and servicing for a Richmond parcel run under those bylaws — confirm Approving Officer approval, servicing (roads, water, sewer, drainage) and floodplain compliance with the City of Richmond for the specific application. No other city's servicing number governs here. (Verified ledger, as instructed.)
- **Flood rules:** new construction in Richmond is subject to flood construction levels under the **Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204**, within a city-wide dike system designed for a 1:500 flood event (dike raising from 3.5 m to 4.7 m). For ground-oriented parcels, confirm the flood construction level position and what it means for insurance and future alterations. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **Richmond property taxes (city-wide rule):** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after Sept 1, 2026; the City collects for other authorities including the Province (school tax), TransLink, BC Assessment, Metro Vancouver and the Municipal Finance Authority. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/brighouse.md)
- **ALR block (standing frame — no ALR ground documented for the core in this slug's research, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; none is documented in this slug's research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
