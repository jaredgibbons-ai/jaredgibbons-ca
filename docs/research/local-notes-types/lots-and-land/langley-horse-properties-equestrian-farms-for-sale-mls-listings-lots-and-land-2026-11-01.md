## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs Township farm and equestrian parcels; confirm the specific parcel's zone with the Township. City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply to Township parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, eligibility requires the Urban Containment Boundary and municipal water and sewer, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Farm acreages are outside this frame by size and servicing. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. On ALR ground the ALC residence frame below governs first. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (the governing frame for most of this topic's ground — parcel status UNVERIFIED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); more or larger requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — THE ERROR, asserted HERE only, precisely as this slug's own research documents it, plus the correct form:** this page's buyer's guide states, verbatim in substance, "BC's Farm Class property tax applies to properties generating $2,500+ in qualifying annual farm income" as a flat rule. **That flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR.** The regulation is three-tiered: $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered to BC Assessment by October 31. Price and plan on the three-tier form, never the page's flat line. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **Strata block (where a bare-land strata parcel is met — ALC permission first on ALR ground):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple farm parcel carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property taxes — Township:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $2,000,000 farm purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 — on a $3,500,000 residential purchase that structure gives $93,000 including the further 2% tier ($2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 at 3% on $1,500,000 + $10,000 further 2% on the $500,000 over $3,000,000). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points — including the GST position of a working farm sale — are UNCONFIRMED beyond GI-003 in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

Quota / marketing-board transfer rules are likewise UNVERIFIED — no marketing-board source was opened. (Research file above.)
