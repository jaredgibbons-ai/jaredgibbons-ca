## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Tsawwassen Central has no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata community, or presale lot offering was verified in this sub-area on an official or developer source. What trades here is individual fee-simple urban infill lots under the RS and RD zones — a house lot on an established street, not development land at scale. Any lot count, typical lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price would be invented, so none is stated.**

### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple lots — the default and effectively only form.** The housing confirmed on official sources in the centre is freehold houses and duplexes under the RS and RD zones, plus named strata condo buildings (cross-reference below). A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf and (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-1/tsawwassen-central-guide.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** The current OCP consolidated the former area plans, including the Tsawwassen Area Plan (Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950), into a single Land Use Plan; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". **The "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" conflation is an ERROR wherever met** — Schedule D.1 belonged to Bylaw 3950. The OCP also carries Tsawwassen Urban Centre Development Permit Area guidelines (Appendix B) covering siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping — the design layer over any centre infill lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted.** Research and City sheets cite Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled; the ruling is Jared's. Zoning is confirmed parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Zoning / subdivision minimums (zone sheets):** RS4 at 610 m², RS5 at 550 m² (or 460 m² outside the Single Family Residential designation), RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m², RD1 at 836 m² and RD4 at 504 m². https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots in Delta per the City's notice to the Province (six within 400 m of frequent transit is the Scott Road / North Delta tier, not this area). Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission: it does not create new lots and does not waive the Urban Centre DPA or any other permit area. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision in Delta runs under the City's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing Bylaw No. 7162, 2015), under Local Government Act s. 506 for highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services. Parcel-specific requirements beyond that: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the City. Delta also permits 2 to 4 hens for personal use on a lot in any RS or RD zone (coop/run max 10 m², no roosters, annual licence under the Animal Control Bylaw) — a small but real seller disclosure point. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/ and https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **ALR rules (no ALR parcel verified in Central — stated for edge completeness):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full on a new HOME to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land), while sales can be taxable where land was used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE, an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt. The Tsawwassen lots-and-land page's own GST summary was left UNVERIFIED against a CRA source in the topic research and is not restated here. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — precisely:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — it becomes relevant when eligible new housing is built/bought, not on the lot purchase alone. New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP: $60,000. FHSA: $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums: 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + foreign-buyer prohibition — Delta only:** the Province's Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable-areas list includes the City of Delta; reserve, treaty and self-governing Indigenous Nation lands are NOT part of the taxable areas. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY — not applicable here. SVT payable on a specific vacant parcel, and any federal non-Canadian prohibition position for a specific buyer: UNCONFIRMED in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Dyke / flood diligence:** Delta is the diking authority and operates over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; the town centre is inland of the Boundary Bay shoreline, and no lot-specific flood construction level for a Central parcel was confirmed — UNCONFIRMED per parcel, check DeltaMap and title. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Bare-land strata block (completeness — none verified in Central):** where a bare-land strata lot exists, Form B diligence (SPA s. 59, within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page), 5-year depreciation reports for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) all apply to the lot. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. **Foreclosure note:** Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — registry mapping UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a new lot subdivision coming in central Tsawwassen?** None verified. The only large land file in the centre is the Town Centre Mall redevelopment process (LU009754, Century Group) — an assembly in process, not lots for sale, with what will be built UNCONFIRMED. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What does a Central lot actually look like?** An established urban lot under RS/RD zoning — the subdivision minimums run 335 m² (RS7) to 836 m² (RD1), so this is infill ground, not acreage. Confirm the parcel's own zone; the street does not tell you. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS4.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a big Central lot?** Only against its zone minimum plus City subdivision/servicing requirements (Bylaw No. 8288, 2024) and, in the Urban Centre, the OCP's DPA guidelines on siting, streetscape, massing and landscaping. Price a split only after the City confirms the parcel's zone and permit areas. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Q: Does SSMUH mean I can build four units instead of subdividing?** Yes as added units — Delta allows up to four units on single-detached/duplex lots (Bylaws 8394/8395) — but no new lots are created, and the Urban Centre DPA still applies. (Section 3 above)
- **Q: Is the lot on city water and sewer?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel — the topic research leaves the live page's servicing claim unverified, so do not rely on it. Confirm connections with the City; for an unserviced parcel, GWELLS plus Fraser Health's onsite-sewerage rules (Authorized Person, Sewerage System Regulation). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Will I pay GST and PTT on a lot?** PTT yes, on the general tiers (1%/2%/3% + further 2% over $3M residential) unless an exemption's own conditions are met — the exemptions attach to buyers/homes, not to vacant land alone. GST depends on the seller: CRA states individual personal-use sales are generally exempt, business/subdivided-more-than-two-parts sales can be taxable. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the mall redevelopment help or hurt a lot purchase nearby?** Ask about File LU009754 on any purchase near 56 Street and 12 Avenue — the application covers 1219 56 Street, 5499 12 Avenue, 1236 55 Street and an unaddressed parcel. Its outcome and timing are UNCONFIRMED, so it is a question to ask, not a premium to pay on spec. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/237772/
- **Q: Any TFN leasehold land in Central?** No TFN land is documented in this sub-area — Tsawwassen Lands sit on Tsawwassen's western/northern edge under TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws (documented in the North file). Central parcels are City-side fee-simple/strata; confirm tenure on title regardless. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/tsawwassen-north-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What are holding costs on a Central lot?** Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalties after July 2 and September 2, 2026); no strata fees on a fee-simple lot. If a first home will be built, the HBP ($60,000) and FHSA ($40,000/$8,000/yr) engage at the home stage, and the FTHB GST rebate attaches to new housing, not the land. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which school catchment is the lot in?** Only the sample is documented: 1299 56 St maps to Cliff Drive Elementary and South Delta Secondary. Run the lot's exact address in the SD37 locator. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
