## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026** — never cite the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 as current. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. The Kingsway corridor towers are not R1 — confirm the zone parcel by parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replacing the 1998 OCP and taking precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. The Cascade Heights Community Plan, for the neighbourhood north-east toward Burnaby Hospital, was officially adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/cascade-heights-community-plan
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Burnaby's servicing-bylaw BASE number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program (renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024) — no base number is asserted here. The documented process: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates. Confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- **New small-strata diligence:** on a 2026 four-lot strata project, the new-home warranty position is a diligence item in its own right, and 5% GST applies to new construction (the 2026 multiplex units), not resales — both positions as documented in the townhomes sibling. Read the specific project's disclosure/Form B and warranty enrolment, not marketing copy. (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/forest-glen-bs-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Bare-land strata rules (applies to any bare-land strata lot and to the small strata multiplex lots above):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a four-lot project sits under the 5+ threshold on the rule as stated — confirm the specific plan's position); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Forest Glen ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Forest Glen lot purchase alone earns no rebate; the rebate's housing angle is directly relevant to the 2026 new-build multiplex units documented in the siblings.
