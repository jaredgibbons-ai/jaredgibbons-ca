## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Chelsea Park has no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no named condo or townhome complex, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside the pocket on any source checked — the research file and all three siblings record the same position. ### Fee-simple lots — the only land form

- **RS street fabric:** single-family homes on Chelsea Court, Chelsea Place, Chelsea Close, Chairlift Road and Skilift Place in the RS single-family zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — RS2 (Section 202), RS3 (Section 203), RS5 (Section 205) and RS7 (Section 207) all appear in District material for this area (the Parks Master Plan lists Chairlift Park in this area as RS7). Current listings confirm the tenure on the ground: a listing for 2662 Chelsea Place records Ownership Type Freehold NonStrata, and a listing for 2790 Chelsea Close records Building Type Single Family Residence and Ownership Type Freehold NonStrata. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 (Section 200 RS minimums in Sections 1–2; lot zone on the District Zoning Map) and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. A Chelsea Park-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). White Rock's bylaws (Zoning 2024 No. 2506 / OCP 2017 No. 2220) belong to WHITE ROCK ONLY and are never imported here; Vancouver's bylaws and taxes likewise do not apply to this West Vancouver pocket. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/official-community-plan-review
- **Ground-oriented density / SSMUH, as the bylaw states it:** 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, ss.620–630). Verify the current infill zoning per property before marketing multiplex potential. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision runs against the Section 200 minimum site area for the lot's RS zone, confirmed on the District Zoning Map. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw NUMBER could not be confirmed on a primary source — held UNCONFIRMED, consistent with the District-wide assembly file; servicing for District lots is District water/sewer, metered and billed quarterly, with District water service on Chelsea Court itself evidenced by the District pump house at 2600 Chelsea Court. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard development permits (District-wide, as documented in this file's research):** an **Environmental Development Permit** is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a **Wildfire Hazard Development Permit** is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both can apply on upper and ravine-edge lots in the pocket. A **Foreshore Development Permit** is a District-wide waterfront/coastal-flooding instrument; a foreshore fact specific to this uphill pocket is UNVERIFIED, and no foreshore claim is made for a Chelsea Park lot on this file's evidence. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (no strata stock is documented in Chelsea Park; stated for any strata lot form):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (no Chelsea Park ALR parcel is confirmed here; standing rules for completeness only):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land + additional school tax (documented in this file's research):** PTT general tiers 1% / 2% / 3% + further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are home-purchase exemptions as BC states them — bare-land eligibility for the newly built home exemption: UNCONFIRMED here. Additional school tax: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Chelsea Park lot purchase alone earns no rebate. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Chelsea Park a Vancouver neighbourhood?** No — in this program it is a small District of West Vancouver pocket at the south edge of the British Properties (Chelsea Court / Place / Close, Chairlift Road, Skilift Place). The Vancouver buildings with Chelsea names (Chelsea Court in Mount Pleasant; Chelsea by Cressey on Cambie Street) are different stock in a different municipality, with different bylaws and taxes. (FILE STATUS block; Section 2.)
- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in the pocket?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented — no vacant-lot subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here is an existing fee-simple RS house lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are the lots, and can one be split?** It depends entirely on the RS zone: Section 200 minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), and RS2, RS3, RS5 and RS7 all appear in District material for this area. Confirm the specific lot's zone on the District Zoning Map before pricing subdivision potential. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I put multiple dwellings on one lot?** The ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, ss.620–630) — verify per property before marketing multiplex potential. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house?** District building-permit guidance addresses secondary suite and detached secondary suite (coach house) applications in these RS areas, subject to the bylaw's conditions — the added unit stays on the house lot's title. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010
- **Q: Which school serves a Chelsea Park lot?** UNVERIFIED until the exact address is run in the SD45 locator — the locator returned NO RESULT for the sample address 2600 Chelsea Court because it falls outside the locator's listed ranges for the street (even 2620–2748, odd 2611–2757). Westcot Elementary at the foot of the British Properties is nearby, but proximity is not catchment. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** District water service on Chelsea Court is evidenced by the District pump house at 2600 Chelsea Court, and District water/sewer are metered and billed quarterly; septic/well status is UNVERIFIED — confirm the specific lot's services with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Q: What permits does an upper or ravine-edge lot trigger?** Possibly a Wildfire Hazard DP (within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface) and an Environmental DP (within 15 m of a watercourse bank top), both District-wide permit areas documented in this file's research. Check before subjects. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: What extra tax applies at these prices?** The Province's additional school tax where the residential assessment exceeds $3M: 0.2% on $3M–$4M and 0.4% above $4M for 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — on top of District taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% + 5% penalties). https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
