## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020.** The rural area divides into Rural zones RU80, RU36 and RU16, each with a Secondary Dwelling ("s") variant (RU80s, RU36s, RU16s), and Rural Residential zones RR7 / RR7s inside the Rural Residential designation; a City rezoning notice describes RU16 as carrying a 1.6 ha minimum lot size, and City application material for neighbouring Stave Falls confirms RU16-to-RU16s rezonings are the route used to permit a secondary detached unit on a rural lot outside the Urban Growth Boundary on land designated Rural Residential. Any Steelhead parcel's actual zone: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/notice-rezoning-bylaws-june-1-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-rsd25-4
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** (settled program position; the companion guide research documents Bylaw 6405-2026's framework for this ground). See the defect note in §5 for the guide page's adoption-date error, which IS documented in the guide slug's own research. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **Secondary dwellings and suites:** a Mission secondary suite is a self-contained living space inside a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; a suite is not a coach home or garden cottage (those are detached secondary dwellings); suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; all suites must be registered with the City, new suites go through the building permit process, and an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus utility fees applies. On rural lots, a detached second dwelling generally requires the "s" variant of the parcel's zone, obtained by rezoning where the parcel does not already carry it. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **SSMUH:** Mission has adopted the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation — zones previously restricted to single-family or duplex dwellings as of December 7, 2023 are subject to it, subject to eligibility and servicing; Steelhead's rural lots sit outside the urban serviced area, so whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given Steelhead lot is per-parcel: UNCONFIRMED. Do not price a Steelhead acreage on urban SSMUH unit counts. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision + servicing process:** subdivision potential follows the parcel's rural zone minimums (RU16 at 1.6 ha per the City notice; RU80 / RU36 minimums are as the bylaw states them for the parcel's zone — read the zone section, no figure is supplied here beyond RU16's documented minimum) and, where ALR applies, ALC approval (§3 ALR block). Servicing is well and septic: wells are recorded in the provincial GWELLS database, and onsite sewerage must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation, including the 30 m setback from a well unless professional advice is obtained. Any Mission servicing (development) bylaw number: UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research — the process documented here is zone + GWELLS + sewerage-regulation confirmation for the parcel, and no number is supplied in its place. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo104/loo104/22_326_2004
- **Property taxes (City of Mission — Steelhead is city land):** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a FLAT 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026. The rural staged regime (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11, 5% + 5%) does NOT apply to a Steelhead parcel. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption netted): $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions:** the First-Time Home Buyers' Program and the Newly Built Home Exemption are home-buyer exemptions as the Province describes them; bare-land eligibility for either: UNCONFIRMED in this file — do not budget an exemption on a vacant-acreage purchase without a parcel-specific ruling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use / in-the-course-of-business / more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable — directly relevant if any future Cardinal Street–type rezoning ever produced developer-sold lots. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an acreage purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal purchase prohibition:** no primary prohibition text was opened for this slug in the research — the position for a Steelhead lot buyer is UNCONFIRMED here and nothing is conformed from another file.
- **BC strata block (applies in full to any bare-land strata lot if one is ever verified here — none is, §2):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (applies where a Steelhead parcel is ALR — status per-parcel, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
