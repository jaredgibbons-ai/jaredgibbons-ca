## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Scope note:** this page has no land and no municipality of its own. The blocks below are the program-level land frame a buyer who arrives via this page will meet on an actual parcel, carried for completeness and labelled as general BC/Surrey-frame rules — NOT asserted as rules documented in this slug's research, except the BCFSA consumer frame, which the research does document.
- **Consumer / regulator frame (documented in this slug's research):** BC real estate trading services are regulated by BCFSA; a licensed professional must give information about commissions and fees, inform consumers of duties owed to clients and non-clients, inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party, disclose at the outset whether they can represent the consumer and how to complain to BCFSA, and work for only buyer or seller (dual agency prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest circumstances). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Testimonial-display rules:** any BCFSA, board or platform rule governing how a REALTOR® may display testimonials (attribution, consent, selection without the full set): UNVERIFIED in this file — no such policy page was opened in the research pass, and none is described from industry habit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/reviews.html
- **Surrey frame (for Surrey parcels generally):** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026; 18020 REPLACED, never cite as current); Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; SSMUH Jul 8, 2024 (3 units ≤280 m² / 4 larger / up to 6 near frequent bus stops, on lots under 4,050 m² inside the urban containment boundary); suites one per unit at ≤40% of principal habitable area, never a separate strata lot; Subdivision and Development Bylaw 1986 No. 8830 verified on City pages; streamside setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning 12000 varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure; STRs permitted owner-only, licence $358/yr carried with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a CONFLICT. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **ALR block (general):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (bare-land strata lots included):** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; min 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT:** 1% / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked examples: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier. If a sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M, flag as OPEN reconciliation, follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Programs attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000/yr. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
