## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; minimum lot sizes (s.110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m frontage; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 / RU-3 — 8.0 ha; urban R zones from 464.5 m². RU-2 minimums as applied to a specific parcel remain as stated in the land home (UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section). City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, eligibility requires municipal water and sewer, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — this guide makes no 6-plex claim (status block). The Township's June 10, 2024 council report estimates only ~15% of Brookswood's single-family/duplex lots eligible for 3–4 units, varying by servicing. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED per the verified ledger. A permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under Tree Protection Bylaw 2019 No. 5478. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (parcel status in Brookswood is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty at midnight July 2, 2026 and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
