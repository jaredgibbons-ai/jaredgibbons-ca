## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

"Montecito" is the GVR board sub-area name as used in the SnapStats GVR report for Burnaby. The neighbourhood sits between Kensington Avenue to the west and the Greystone / Arden side to the east, with Halifax Street to the north and Lougheed Highway to the south — exact MLS® boundary streets: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex
- Nearby areas: Lochdale and Parkcrest to the west, Sperling-Duthie and Burnaby Lake to the south across Lougheed Highway, Westridge and the Burnaby Mountain / SFU side to the north-east, Government Road to the south-east. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- Main roads: Lougheed Highway at the south edge; Kensington Avenue, Sperling Avenue, Duthie Avenue and Halifax Street run through the neighbourhood, and Burnaby Mountain Parkway climbs from the east side to SFU. Transit anchor: Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station, an elevated Millennium Line station at Sperling Avenue and Lougheed Highway directly south of the sub-area, with bus routes 110, 134 and 144 connecting there — the 144 runs between Metrotown Station and SFU. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Montecito has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple house lot on the established streets, almost always with a detached house on it, bought for the lot, the setting and its R1 small-scale multi-unit potential. The one comprehensively interesting land fact is the zoning itself (Section 3).**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026:** adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright — the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must never be cited for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** it replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict — check the 2050 map before assuming density or subdivision potential on any large Montecito lot. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Lot area and the 400 m transit test are therefore the two numbers that price a Montecito teardown. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — confirmed instrument (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026):** Burnaby's servicing bylaw is the **Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw** — the former Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 as renamed and amended by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667 (2024), in force September 30, 2024. Its s. 4A requires parcels created by subdivision to conform to the Zoning Bylaw for the zone, and s. 5A lists the works and services a subdivision can be required to provide (highways and lanes, water, sewage and drainage systems connected to the City's systems). The City's Servicing Improvements Guide applies the updated bylaw to developments of four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot, including cash-in-lieu contributions to curbs, sidewalks, lanes, boulevards, street trees, streetlights and road widening. Confirm the parcel-level requirement with the City's Engineering Department before pricing a split. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Bare-land strata rules (the BC strata block — stated for the form; no Montecito plan is verified):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, with no deferral vote; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The same block governs Villa Montecito diligence in the townhomes sibling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Montecito ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and that three-tier position is asserted in this program only where the horse-properties research documents it. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Montecito lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Burnaby. No Montecito-specific speculation-tax or federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position is documented in this file's research — none imported, and the prohibition position for vacant land here is UNCONFIRMED rather than conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. Area-specific levy notes for Montecito: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant-lot subdivision I can buy into in Montecito?** No verified one. Montecito is an established neighbourhood — land trades as built house lots, one strata apartment building, one strata townhouse complex and rental product. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What can a Montecito house lot actually become?** Under the R1 SSMUH district, 3 units on a lot up to 280 m², 4 units on a larger lot, and 6 units only on a lot of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — and a lot with two or more primary units may be stratified. Measure the lot and run the 400 m test before pricing any multiplex value. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large lot here, and what servicing will the City require?** Subdivision runs under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (the 1971 bylaw as renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, in force September 30, 2024): new parcels must conform to the Zoning Bylaw, and the bylaw's works-and-services list covers highways, water, sewer and drainage connections; developments of four or more primary units also face the City's servicing-improvements regime, including cash-in-lieu items. Get the Engineering pre-application answer for the specific lot first. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052
- **Q: Is there any bare-land strata in Montecito?** None verified. The area's strata townhouse complex, Villa Montecito, is a conventional strata (plan NWS90) — you buy a strata townhouse with a Form B, not a bare-land lot. If a bare-land strata lot were offered here, it would still be strata tenure in full: fees, bylaws and Form B diligence. (Section 2; sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/montecito-townhomes-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Q: Is a Montecito lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established homes on annual water and sewer billing (detached water charge $652.33 for 2026, sewer as a parcel tax for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel's connections and any servicing upgrade the Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw will require for your project. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Does the Burnaby Lake / Sperling Avenue low ground create a flood problem for a lot?** The lake frontage is low and wet, but no floodplain designation for Montecito's residential streets was confirmed in the research — treat flood status as a parcel check, not a neighbourhood answer, and check it before subject removal on any south-edge lot. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). The FTHB GST rebate does not rescue a bare-lot purchase: it applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Montecito lot?** By exact address only. The one settled locator chain in this file (2176 Duthie Ave → Montecito Elementary → Burnaby North Secondary) does not transfer to other streets, and Sperling Elementary is the French Immersion trap on this map — run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: Is The Ambassador a Montecito building or an SFU building — and can I buy land under it?** Neither question has a land answer: it is a 1987 strata apartment building, counted in the montecito condos file only, and MLS also files it under Simon Fraser Univer. You buy a strata unit there, never land. (Sibling file: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/montecito-condos-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Q: How liquid is land value here, honestly?** Thin: September 2026 recorded 2 detached sales on 20 listings (10% ratio) and zero attached sales on 12 listings — price a lot or teardown off the specific street and the R1 math, not off a monthly median, because SnapStats publishes no Montecito sub-area median at all. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).

- **The City does not call this area Montecito (2026 documents):** for Burnaby administrative and planning purposes the same ground is "Sperling-Broadway" — a buyer reading City plans, the 2050 OCP maps or facility pages under "Montecito" alone will miss material filed under the planning name. https://www.burnaby.ca/recreation-and-arts/recreation-facilities/burnaby-lake-sports-complex
