## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Forest Hills — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings. What trades as "land" here is (1) the fee-simple detached house lot on the mountain's lower slopes, priced in part for its R1 SSMUH potential, and (2) the ground under four 1980s freehold-strata townhome complexes and three housing co-operatives — built strata/co-op fabric, documented below as land form only, never as lot offerings. The one live land-shaping item is a PROPOSAL — the Burnaby Mountain Gondola — documented as such, never as inventory.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026:** adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright — the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must never be cited for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** it replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², and permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. On these slopes the creek, slope and conservation-area-edge checks can govern before the unit count does. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Servicing Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. **The base bylaw's own number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — never filled in**; the rename, the amendment number and the process above are what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Creek and slope diligence:** Stoney Creek runs through the area toward Burnaby Lake — confirm streamside setbacks and any slope conditions for the specific parcel before pricing redevelopment value on Beaverbrook- and ravine-edge lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/strategies-and-plans/official-community-plan/community-plans
- **Bare-land strata rules (the BC strata block — stated for the form; no Forest Hills bare-land plan is verified; the same block governs diligence on the pocket's conventional strata complexes):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Forest Hills complex carries a verified age restriction — the siblings found every named complex family-marketed, NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Forest Hills ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Forest Hills lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Burnaby. No Forest Hills-specific speculation-tax or federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position is documented in this file's research — none imported, and the prohibition position for vacant land here is UNCONFIRMED rather than conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. Area-specific levy notes for Forest Hills: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Forest Hills?** No verified one — the pocket is built out: detached houses on the slopes, four 1980s strata townhome complexes and three housing co-operatives. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is this area called Forest Hills or Forest Grove?** Both, for different purposes: "Forest Hills" is the GVR board sub-area name (shortened to "Forest Hills BN" in listing copy); the City-facing pocket name is "Forest Grove," after Forest Grove Drive, the elementary school and the park. Search both names or you will miss listings. (Research file, §1.)
- **Q: What can a house lot here become under SSMUH?** 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, subject to a 10 m minimum lot width and 281 m² minimum lot area — but creek setbacks, slope and the conservation-area edge can govern first on these lots. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Will the gondola run over my lot?** Possibly over the neighbourhood, not yet over any specific lot on a final design: TransLink's endorsed Route 1 runs straight from Production Way–University Station to SFU with cabins passing above the Forest Grove neighbourhood, Council endorsed it in 2022, and it is not yet approved or funded. Do not pay today for a station that does not exist; do disclose the proposal when selling. https://www.translink.ca/news/2023/november/translink%20seeking%20feedback%20on%20burnaby%20mountain%20gondola
- **Q: Are the Forest Grove townhome complexes bare-land strata?** No — Rossmoor, Henley Estate (NW2129), Wembley Estates and Ashbrook Place are conventional freehold-strata townhouse plans, documented in the townhomes sibling. - **Q: Is Avonlea (2600 Beaverbrook Cres) Forest Hills land?** It sits on a Forest Hills street, but its listing source files it under Simon Fraser Hills, and the program follows the listing filing — counted 0 in every Forest Hills file, this one included.
