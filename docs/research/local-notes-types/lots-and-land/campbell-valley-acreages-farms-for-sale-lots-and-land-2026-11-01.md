## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township zoning:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs Township ground, including every Campbell Valley parcel. Feed cards use RU-3 labels in their own listing text — card labels are listing copy only, and a specific parcel's zone is confirmed with the Township per parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx (research file §4)
- **SSMUH as the Township states it:** Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **no 6-unit tier**. Campbell Valley acreage parcels in this feed are measured in acres, well over the 4,050 m² ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY — never state that they "replaced" Bylaw 6020; the Willoughby Plan is Bylaw 1836, now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200. (Research file §4; townhomes sibling §3.)
- **Suites / coach houses (Township):** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102, never strata — **a live guide-vs-bylaw conflict is flagged, not resolved:** s.102 as read in this program permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted. R-CL s.403 (220 m² minimum lot / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area — no 355 m² figure exists) is a Willoughby compact-lot rule, carried here only as part of the Township frame, not as a Campbell Valley rule. (Townhomes sibling §3.)
- **Subdivision / servicing:** Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 5382 (2019, confirmed) — the servicing frame for any Township subdivision or bare-land servicing question on this ground. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the Township. (Servicing ledger, as carried in this program.)
- **ALR block (for parcels that are in the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local bylaws may be more restrictive and may not be more permissive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form (asserted here because this file's own research documents it):** farm classification is a voluntary BC Assessment program — $2,500 of farm income if the operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met. Applications by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year; a buyer who plans to continue farming a farm-classified property must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies in full to any bare-land strata lot, if one is met):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Township property taxes:** Township property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a penalty of 5% is applied to current-year taxes, including the unclaimed home owner grant, at midnight July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**.
