## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026 — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. The ground's plan layer also includes the North Grandview Heights NCP for the neighbourhoods north of 28 Avenue from King George Boulevard to Highway 15. https://www.Surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs this ground. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH — Surrey, as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots under 280 m², up to 4 units on lots over 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, under the SSMUH zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Eligibility is parcel-specific; no SSMUH claim is made for the Hyde Park strata lots themselves, whose form is fixed by their strata plans. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing for subdivision is determined under Surrey's servicing bylaw No. 8830 (1986), verified on City pages, applied with the zone for the parcel under Bylaw No. 12000; subdivision itself requires City approval and Land Title registration — confirm the parcel's requirements with the City. (Verified servicing ledger for this program; City of Surrey pages.)
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home. Licence figure CONFLICT, carried disclosed: $358 per year on the City's Short-Term Rentals page, with the City's $350-per-year proposal also on record — confirm the current figure on the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (framework only — no ALR ground is documented for this slug; parcel status UNVERIFIED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (the tenure that governs both counted complexes, and any bare-land strata lot):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The live page's own rentals frame matches the November 24, 2022 position: a strata can no longer restrict long-term rentals, while bylaws limiting short-term rentals can still apply — confirm the current bylaws with the strata manager for the specific complex. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/hyde-park-south-surrey.html
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
