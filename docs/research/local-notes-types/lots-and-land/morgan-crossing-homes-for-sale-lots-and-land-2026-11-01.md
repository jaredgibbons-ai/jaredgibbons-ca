## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans:** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current; Bylaw 18020 is REPLACED, never cite as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH (Surrey, in force Jul 8, 2024):** inside the urban containment boundary, on lots under 4,050 m² — 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, 4 units on a larger lot, up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey):** only one suite per dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is verified on City pages as the subdivision/servicing bylaw. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement No per-lot servicing figure is stated: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey):** streamside setbacks are in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host and must live at the property as primary residence; business licence $358/yr carried, with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a CONFLICT — direct to the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (for any parcel where reserve status is confirmed):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents farm ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (applies to the village's strata buildings and in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The live page's own advice — read the depreciation report and two years of minutes closely in 2008–2011 buildings — is consistent with this frame. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/morgan-crossing-homes-for-sale.html
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above $2M, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked examples (fully residential, no exemption unless stated): $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% on $500K over $3M) = **$93,000**. If a sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M, it omits the further 2% tier — flag as OPEN reconciliation, follow $93,000. FTHB Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000 — the page notes many Morgan Crossing condos are priced under $835,000 (site copy). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000/yr. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **REDMA (presales):** s.21 — 7-day rescission by written notice; s.18 — deposits held in trust. Applies to presale contracts (e.g. Harlowe), not to resale village units. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
