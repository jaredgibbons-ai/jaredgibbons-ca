No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale LOT offering inside the sub-area is verified on any official or developer source, and the OCP itself steers Tsawwassen growth elsewhere — to the Urban Centre, the 56 Street corridor, Southlands and Tsawwassen Springs, all outside English Bluff. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024)** — a single consolidated plan with no Schedule D.1 and no Tsawwassen Area Plan (that schedule belonged to the superseded Bylaw No. 3950); any citation of "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR. Farm land in the plan is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017, while an internal title says "8400, 2025"; this file follows the City pages' citation for the RS zone content and asserts neither number as settled. The RS3 standard for this shore is in §2. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Hazard DPA SD2 — the defining rule set on this shore, in full:** Development Permit Area SD2, "SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment)", Category "b" (protection of development from hazardous conditions), covers this shore. The OCP states the waterfront area is subject to mud flows, debris flows, erosion, land slip and subsidence, and the ocean-frontage slopes are subject to erosion and require protection of existing vegetation and tree cover. Building rules: no building may sit with the underside of a wooden floor system, or the top of a concrete slab for habitation, business or storage of goods damageable by floodwaters, lower than the Flood Construction Level (unless a lesser elevation is set by the provincial ministry); no building within 7.5 m of the natural boundary of the sea or within 7.5 m of the inboard toe of any dike; the guidelines require a restrictive covenant acknowledging the land may be subject to flooding; and development affected by hazardous conditions is subject to conditions set by a suitably qualified Professional Engineer. A site-specific Flood Construction Level for an individual lot: UNVERIFIED. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta's rules allow up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots city-wide (SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024), subject to the bylaw and to any hazard or streamside permit area on the lot — on this shore the SD2 conditions sit on top of that permission and are the deciding layer. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in English Bluff?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering is verified here. What trades is improved fee-simple estate lots under the RS zones; the full treatment is in the land home file. (§2 above)
- **Q: Does the bluff affect building or renovating?** Yes — SD2 makes development conditional on a suitably qualified Professional Engineer's conditions, the Flood Construction Level, 7.5 m setbacks from the natural boundary of the sea and from any dike toe, and a registered restrictive covenant acknowledging flood risk. Budget for the geotechnical file and have it ready before subjects come off. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Who owns the beach below a waterfront lot?** Not the lot owner — the foreshore below the natural boundary is Crown land administered through FrontCounter BC, and foreshore use is interjurisdictional. Do not market or price private beach ownership, stairs or docks without checking tenure. https://www.delta.ca:443/building-development/development-planning-approvals/development-considerations
- **Q: Can I clear trees to open up the view?** The SD2 justification requires protection of existing vegetation and tree cover on the ocean-frontage slopes — tree removal for a view can need approvals and can affect the slope itself. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: How large are English Bluff lots?** Only the zoning minimums are documented: the RS3 standard fitting this shore is a 4,000 m² minimum subdivision lot area (30 m width, 30 m average depth). Existing lot sizes vary parcel by parcel and no area average is published: UNCONFIRMED. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS3.pdf
- **Q: Can a lot add a suite or coach house?** Delta's SSMUH zoning allows up to four units on single-detached and duplex lots, subject to the bylaw and to any slope or streamside permit area — on this shore, the SD2 hazard conditions are the deciding layer, not the unit count. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Which schools serve an English Bluff lot?** SD37 catchments are address-based. The one locator result recorded for this sub-area (402 English Bluff Road) is English Bluff Elementary (K–7) and South Delta Secondary (8–12) — a single-address result; any other address needs its own locator run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Will insurers and lenders accept a bluff property?** Confirm insurability and lender comfort early — the OCP-documented mud flow, debris flow, erosion and land-slip hazards are in the City's own plan, so they will surface in any serious buyer's search. No insurer or lender position for a specific lot is stated here: UNCONFIRMED at parcel level. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Does GST apply to a vacant-lot purchase here?** At CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable; the position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will PTT cost on a land purchase?** At the standard tiers (1% / 2% / 3%, plus the further 2% on the residential portion above $3M): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
