## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **BCFSA Rule 5-10(3) — carried VERBATIM, sourcing stated per file:** the sibling files for this slug (condos and townhomes siblings, §3 in each) source this text to Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report, at http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download — copied verbatim, not paraphrased: *"a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about their motivation, financial qualifications or needs."* Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** in British Columbia, real estate trading services are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** not stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, on the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. How open-house dates and times are set and entered for publication on the brokerage/board side is likewise UNVERIFIED on any BC Gov or BCFSA source. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/open-houses.md)
- **Zoning / OCP / SSMUH — no single frame governs this page:** a region-wide feed crosses many municipalities, each with its own OCP, zoning bylaw, SSMUH position and servicing rules. No one city's frame is stated for this page; the governing frame for any lot met at an open house is that parcel's own municipality's, stated in that area's files in this program. Importing one city's frame region-wide would be an error.
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no bare-land strata plan is verified on this page):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (where ALR ground is in scope — no ALR parcel is confirmed on this page):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). A parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $800,000 fair market value, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this page, so the position is **UNCONFIRMED** here and is not conformed from other files.
