## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Connaught Heights — stated plainly at the top because it is the honest position. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. The sub-area's entire listed stock in September 2026 was detached houses (15 listings, 0 condos & townhomes listings). Land here trades as fee-simple house lots, plus two City-owned small-sites lots that are an affordable-housing process — not inventory — and station-area assembly interest that is planning potential only.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — CORRECTED:** Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 governs. Houses sit in the Single Detached Residential Districts (RS series, s.310) — RS-1: minimum site area 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.), FSR 0.5; the RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses. **Townhouse districts are RT-2 through RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B ONLY — RT-A/RT-B do NOT exist** (see the FILE STATUS correction; s.410 (RT-2) permits row houses or townhouses). The 836.10 m² figure in the research file is an RM apartment-district density threshold and is not an RT subdivision minimum. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Plan:** "Our City 2041," the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 7925, 2017, governs the area, and the 22nd Street Station area is identified in it as a Frequent Transit Development Area — one of the areas where the City directs higher-density, transit-supportive growth. Confirm the exact OCP designation for a station-adjacent lot with the City before pricing in redevelopment value. https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **SSMUH / added units as the research states them:** in the RS districts — secondary suites allowed, laneway / carriage houses regulated under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001, within the RS-1 minimum site area (557.40 m²) and 0.5 FSR frame. A Connaught Heights-specific SSMUH unit-count figure on a City source beyond that frame is UNCONFIRMED; parcel-level eligibility is a City confirmation for the specific lot. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/connaught-heights.md)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — confirmed bylaw, process stated:** subdivision is regulated by the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw No. 7142, 2007, which requires all subdivisions be approved by the City's Approving Officer (the Director of Engineering Services) and ensures new lots are adequately serviced and meet the Zoning Bylaw's minimum lot size and frontage requirements. The City's process runs pre-application, application, and — where required — a Works & Services Agreement with detailed servicing drawings by an engineering consultant, before final approval and Land Title registration. Parcel-level standards are confirmed through that process; none is quoted lot-by-lot here. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/7142_Subdivision.pdf and https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Connaught Heights?** No verified one — the neighbourhood is RS-zoned detached houses beside an Expo Line station. Land trades as house lots; there is no bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale lot offering on the verified record. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot the zoning contemplates here?** RS-1 sets a minimum site area of 557.40 m² (6,000 sq. ft.) with an FSR cap of 0.5 — the frame a subdivision application is measured against, subject to the Approving Officer process under Bylaw No. 7142, 2007. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/Zoning_Bylaw_No_6680_2001_as_of_June_30_2022_(Complete).pdf
- **Q: I read about RT-A and RT-B townhouse districts — can I build townhouses under them?** No — RT-A/RT-B do not exist in New Westminster; that wording in the research file is corrected at the top of this file. The bylaw's townhouse districts are RT-2–RT-2C plus RT-3/3A/3B, and a townhouse form still needs the parcel to actually sit in one of those districts. https://newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/400___Multifamily_Residential(3).pdf
- **Q: Can a house here add a suite or a laneway house?** The RS districts allow secondary suites and regulate laneway / carriage houses under Zoning Bylaw No. 6680, 2001 (RS-1: 557.40 m² minimum, FSR 0.5). Pull the City permits and confirm the specific lot before advertising the potential. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/connaught-heights.md)
- **Q: What do the two City lots on London Street / Ninth Avenue mean for neighbouring land?** They are a City affordable-housing small-sites process, not a market project — and 2038 Ninth Avenue carries a Crown Land Grant for parks and recreation purposes, while the City's parks plan recommends acquiring more parkland in Connaught Heights. Do not price neighbouring lots off an assumed housing build on both sites. https://www.newwestcity.ca/housing/affordable-housing/connaught-heights-small-sites/sb_expander_articles/1840.php
- **Q: Does the station designation change what my lot is worth?** The OCP makes the 22nd Street Station area a Frequent Transit Development Area and Council endorsed the reGENERATE Vision in December 2024 — lots near the station carry a different planning future than the interior streets. What a specific lot may build is a City confirmation, not a listing claim (Marine Way assembly pitches citing 20 storeys / 5.0 FSR are UNCONFIRMED against adopted bylaws). https://www.newwestcity.ca/ocp/past-event-materials-and-council-reports
- **Q: Is a Connaught Heights lot serviced?** The City's subdivision process requires new lots to be adequately serviced with water, sewers, underground electrical, communications, street lighting, roads, sidewalks and street trees, and the City runs its own electrical utility and water / sewer services — confirm the specific parcel's servicing through the subdivision process before writing. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/rte/files/General%20Guide%20to%20Subdivision%20Process%202012-Final.pdf
- **Q: Is the area in a floodplain?** No floodplain zone for the residential streets was confirmed in the City's study (its zones cover Queensborough, the west-end industrial land, the Quay and the Brunette River industrial area) — lot-level status is UNVERIFIED and is a City check, not an assumption. https://www.newwestcity.ca/database/files/library/NWRFP_20_23_Addendum_1.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Connaught Heights lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (2201 London Street → Connaught Heights Elementary → Fraser River Middle → New Westminster Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD40 locator. https://newwestschools.ca/our-schools/registration/
