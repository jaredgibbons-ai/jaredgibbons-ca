## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Delbrook — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale lot offering was verified on any District or developer source. Land here trades as built fee-simple detached house lots on the R1/R2 bench above West Queens Road. The one land story that matters is municipal: the District's own Delbrook Lands at 600 West Queens Road, a redevelopment of the former recreation centre site documented below as a District project — never as for-sale inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and only verified form

- **Established bench lots:** detached houses on R1 and R2 lots along Delbrook Avenue and its side streets — the R2 zone's stated intent is ground-oriented detached housing in the form of a house which may include a suite and be accompanied by a coach house. Predominantly freehold detached; the strata share is limited to the apartment stock near West Queens Road (Queensbrook and Delbrook Plaza, documented in the condos sibling), and no named townhome complex in Delbrook is confirmed on an official or developer source. The bench street pattern is historic subdivision fabric — built lots, not a lot offering. The strata/freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Lot character, as the District states it (Zoning Bylaw 3210, Section 525, Bylaw 8799):** the R1 zone minimum lot is 4,000 m² with 30 m width and 34 m depth; the standard R2 zone minimum lot is 660 m² with 18 m width and 34 m depth; modifiers run R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width, R2(S2) at 1,100 m² / 24 m and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m. The zone on a single lot is confirmed through the District's GeoWeb. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Delbrook's established streets sit inside the District's serviced urban system — District single-family utility fees in 2026 total $2,571.20, including water at $932.80 and sewer/drainage at $1,255.60. Parcel-level servicing detail, and any septic or well position for a specific lot: UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm the specific parcel with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** no ALR parcel in Delbrook is documented in the research; parcel-level ALR status is listed UNVERIFIED there. No ALR percentage split is stated — check any specific parcel on Agricultural Land Commission mapping. (Research file above.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / Zoning — DISTRICT rulebook only:** District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, and Zoning Bylaw 3210 (Section 525 as amended by Bylaw 8799). The former North Lonsdale–Delbrook Official Community Plan (Bylaw 6750, adopted 1995), which named Delbrook as one of its five neighbourhoods, is repealed — Bylaw 7900 now covers the area. The City's Bylaws 8400 / 6700 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Added units / SSMUH as the District states it:** an R2 lot may hold one principal dwelling, one secondary suite and one coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GeoWeb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop — parcel-specific; run the lot in GeoWeb before pricing potential. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf and https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District servicing-bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted, and the CITY's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw 8014 is never imported into this District file. The subdivision position is documented instead from District sources: a split must first clear the Section 525 zone minimums for the lot's own zone and modifiers (Section 2), the lot's SSMUH classification is shown in the District's GeoWeb, and the Schedule B hazard permit areas (below) gate creek-side and slope lots. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the parcel. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/delbrook.md)
- **Hazards — Schedule B permit areas:** District OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B sets Development Permit Areas including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — Mission Creek borders the Delbrook Lands on the west and Mosquito Creek lies farther west, so creek-side Delbrook lots are exactly the lots these areas were written for. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/delbrook-transportation-study.pdf
- **Hazards — landslide diligence:** the District's landslide hazard work rests on its Landslide Risk Assessment, commissioned after the January 19, 2005 Berkley landslide in the District's east end; slope lots anywhere in the District, including Delbrook's upper streets, should be checked against the District's hazard reporting rather than assumed clear. Parcel-level flood, slope/landslide status: UNVERIFIED in the research. http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-1-district-landslide.pdf
- **Bare-land strata rules (no plan verified here — the BC strata block that WOULD apply in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Delbrook ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new subdivision I can buy into in Delbrook?** No verified one — Delbrook is an established detached bench; land trades as built fee-simple house lots, and no bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is happening at 600 West Queens Road — can I buy into it?** No. The District is redeveloping its own former recreation centre lands (about 17,600 m²) under Bylaws 8397–8399: a neighbourhood park on the northern portion and a four-storey low-to-moderate-income rental and seniors' care building on the southern portion. It is a District project on District land, not a lot or unit offering. https://app.dnv.org/OpenDocument/Default.aspx?docNum=4100529
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It depends on the parcel's zone and modifier under Section 525: minimums run from R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m width, with standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m. Confirm the zone in the District's GeoWeb before pricing any split. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can a Delbrook house lot hold a suite or a coach house?** Yes — an R2 lot may hold one principal dwelling, one secondary suite and one coach house, and since June 2024 three units total are permitted on single-family lots within the Urban Containment Boundary; the GeoWeb SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on a non-exempt lot over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Q: Which servicing bylaw governs a Delbrook subdivision?** That number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — no District servicing-bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source here, and the City of North Vancouver's Bylaw 8014 does NOT apply in the District. What is documented is the District process position: Section 525 minimums, the GeoWeb SSMUH classification, and the Schedule B hazard permit areas. Confirm requirements with the District for the parcel. (Section 3.)
- **Q: What hazard permits apply to a creek-side or upper-slope lot?** District OCP Schedule B sets permit areas for streamside protection, creek hazard, slope hazard and wildfire hazard — Mission Creek and Mosquito Creek flank the area, and the District's landslide work (its Landslide Risk Assessment, commissioned after the January 19, 2005 Berkley landslide) is the check for slope lots. Run the GeoWeb hazard layers before pricing a rebuild. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/delbrook-transportation-study.pdf
- **Q: Is Queensbrook a land purchase?** No — Queensbrook, 678 West Queens Road, is a built 55+ strata apartment building (22 units, built 1997, in the District's verified 55+ roster in the sibling record); a buyer there buys a strata unit with Form B diligence, not a lot. (Siblings above.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Delbrook lot?** By exact address only — École Braemar Elementary and Carisbrooke Elementary both sit beside the area, so the nearest school is not the catchment, and no Delbrook catchment is settled in this file. The one settled chain in a named sibling (3600 Mahon Avenue → École Braemar → Carson Graham) is that single address only and does not transfer. Run the address with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044
