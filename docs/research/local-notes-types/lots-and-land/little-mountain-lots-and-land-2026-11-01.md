## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Hillside development rules — the central rules for this area:** the City's **Development Permit Area 2 (Hillside and Upland Areas)** protects developments from hazardous hillside conditions and environmentally sensitive areas, and a **Geotechnical Report from a qualified professional is required with each application**; **Development Permit Area 5 (Hillside Development) applies to all lands in the City with slopes of 20% or more**, and within a designated DPA a Development Permit is required before subdivision, construction, addition or alteration of a building, or alteration of land, except where exemptions apply. Budget for the report and the permit before assuming a build, addition or subdivision timeline on any hillside lot — fee-simple or bare-land strata. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2881
- **Plan and zoning:** Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500 (DPA 2 and DPA 5 administered under it); zoning under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — HR and SHR minimums in §2. Slope decides the lot math: in SHR the minimum lot area rises with the slope band (360 m² up to 990 m²), so a "big" hillside lot may not subdivide the way its area suggests. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw is **Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024** — its application to a specific hillside lot is still subject to the DPA/geotechnical rules above. (Sibling files for this slug: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/little-mountain-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **BC strata block — governs the Braeside Estates LOT directly:** Form B (SPA s. 59) — on a resale purchase of a bare-land strata lot, the strata must provide a Form B Information Certificate stating the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owing, approved special levies still payable, the CRF balance, and a summary of the strata's insurance, with the rules, current budget and bylaws forming the document package — for a bare-land lot the Form B and bylaws are where road, gate, landscaping-standard and building-scheme obligations on the lot are read. Depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (Braeside's lot count is UNCONFIRMED, so its threshold position is confirmed on its strata plan/Form B); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — Braeside's listing-stated 55+ position is valid **only if** it is a filed 55+ bylaw: UNCONFIRMED; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block — stated for completeness, governing no verified hill stock:** if a parcel were in the ALR, subdivision would require Agricultural Land Commission approval and residences could not be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)); residence caps would be one principal residence per parcel (maximum 500 m²), a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence capped at 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha); farm class would run in the full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31). No ALR land on the hill was identified in the sources checked, so none of this is priced into a hill lot without a parcel-level ALC check. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000. The same tiers apply to a bare-land strata lot purchase — PTT follows the registered interest, not the built form. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. A resale bare-land strata lot sold by an individual sits on the exempt side of that line in the general case; a developer's first sale of a newly created lot can be taxable — the position for a specific lot is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
