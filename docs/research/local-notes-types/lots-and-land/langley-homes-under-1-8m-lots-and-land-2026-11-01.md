## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **TOWNSHIP OF LANGLEY — zoning and plan:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies Township-wide. The Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979) and is now **Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025)**. The Willoughby Community Plan Update Bylaws **6201/6202/6203 are at first/second reading (May 25, 2026) only**, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — they are never cited here as adopted. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **TOWNSHIP — SSMUH, suites and coach houses:** SSMUH amendments are in **Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024)** — **no 6-plex tier exists in it**. The Township secondary-suite cap is **120 m²**. A coach house is a **fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata**. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted — a buyer pricing a townhouse suite in the Township confirms the current s.102 text with the Township for the parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **TOWNSHIP — subdivision and servicing: Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED.** All Township subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, with servicing to Bylaw 5382 standards. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Other-Bylaws/Subdivision-and-Development-Servicing-Bylaw-2019-(No.-5382).pdf and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **CITY OF LANGLEY — zoning, kept separate:** **Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300** (the number is cited; its adoption date is conflicted in program sources and is flagged **UNVERIFIED** — no adoption date is asserted here). City secondary-suite size is the **lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of the principal unit. Nothing in the Township frame above (Bylaw 2500, Bylaw 6020, Bylaw 5382, the 120 m² cap) applies inside the City, and nothing in the City frame applies in the Township.
- **ALR block — full form (Langley's rural sub-areas):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6; Strata Property Act, s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s.21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — applies to any strata lot in either Langley, including a bare-land strata lot:** Form B (SPA s.59), within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** tiers and worked examples are in §2. First-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are HOME exemptions — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA) rebates 100% of GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, **NOT bare land**. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

The "355 m²" figure is not in the bylaw. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: Is a coach house a strata unit I can sell separately?** No — in the Township a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 on the same lot, never a strata lot. The Township suite cap is 120 m²; in the City of Langley a suite is the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. (§3.)
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a townhouse in the Township?** That is the flagged live conflict: s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m²; the January 2025 guide says not permitted. Treat it as unresolved in this file and confirm the current bylaw text with the Township before pricing it. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the new Willoughby plan already in force?** No — the Update bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading on May 25, 2026 only, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026. The plan in force is the Willoughby Community Plan as Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). (§3.)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the band ceiling?** At $1,800,000, $34,000 before any exemption ($2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000); at $1,500,000, $28,000; at $1,000,000, $18,000. The 3% tier starts above $2,000,000, outside this band. (§2.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Is GST charged on a vacant Langley lot?** At CRA GI-003 level only: generally exempt for an individual's personal-use land; potentially taxable for business/developer sales or parcels subdivided into more than two parts. The first-home GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Langley land purchase?** SD35, address-based. Only two locator chains are documented (7888 200 Street and 8877 Bartlett Street), each single-address and non-transferable; every other address is UNVERIFIED until run in the SD35 locator. (§1.) https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
