## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future"** — a single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules covering Ladner. Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here. Zoning is assigned parcel by parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies — context for the detached stock a band buyer compares against, not a condo rule. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Subdivision and servicing — process, adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED.** A City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015 (Local Government Act s. 506). Final adoption status: HELD UNCONFIRMED — confirm the governing standard with the City for any specific subdivision file. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside / flood diligence.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area; Ladner sits behind the City's system of over 63 km of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 km of drainage infrastructure. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **ALR (Ladner context; no ALR parcel is in this band's feed as documented).** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal 500 m² or less, suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² or less (40 ha or less) / 186 m² or less (over 40 ha). Farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — every purchase in this band is taxed entirely in the 1% and 2% tiers. Worked math in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time buyers: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000), full exemption at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — so a qualifying first-time buyer can in principle use the program anywhere in this band, subject to all the program's other conditions. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility beyond the documented vacant-land refund route is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. (Resale condos in this band are not vacant land; a presale purchase is new housing and carries GST — see the rebate line.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1,500,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (the band's actual tenure):** Form B within 7 days of request, maximum $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Building bylaws are checked per building in the registered documents — the live page's age/rental claims are UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Presales (REDMA) — the Bridge & Elliott frame:** the developer must deliver a disclosure statement; s. 21 gives the buyer 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging that statement to rescind, and deposits are held in trust. A presale is never resale band stock. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** Delta IS a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax (annual declaration required). The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no position beyond this file's own cited sources is stated — the research cites no prohibition source for this band, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed.
- **Delta property taxes.** 2026 due date July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
