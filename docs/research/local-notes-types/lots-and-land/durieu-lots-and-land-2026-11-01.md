- **Planning and zoning (FVRD):** FVRD official community plan and zoning bylaws govern this ground (the Hatzic Valley OCP covers the inhabited valley), but **no FVRD bylaw number is asserted in this file** — the specific OCP/zoning bylaw numbers and zone names applying to Durieu lots are UNVERIFIED in the research pass, no FVRD Area F bylaw document was opened, and no city's number (Mission's, Chilliwack's or any other) is borrowed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/durieu.md)
- **Secondary suites in Area F:** the FVRD records significant limits on development options in Electoral Area F with the majority of lands in the ALR, and that secondary suites are freely permitted with no restrictions related to farm use, but must be located within the principal dwelling and could not be a separate detached structure, such as a garden suite, or a multi-unit structure for farm workers. Feed cards describing a property as "zoned for a SECONDARY DWELLING or a SUITE" or offering a guest cottage are listing text only — confirm the FVRD zoning and, on ALR land, the ALC frame for the PID. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf and https://jaredgibbons.ca/durieu.html
- **SSMUH:** no FVRD SSMUH position for Electoral Area F is documented in this file's research — UNCONFIRMED for this ground, and no municipal SSMUH rule (Mission's or any other) is imported. (§3 suite line above governs second units here.)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (stated for completeness — no such lot is verified here):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $700,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Rural property taxes:** a property not in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion unpaid on October 31. Mission's flat 10% penalty does NOT apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Durieu part of Mission?** No — for mailing and board reporting it carries a Mission address, but it is an unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area F community in the Hatzic Valley; the FVRD's housing-needs report places Durieu among the Hatzic Valley settlement areas of Electoral Area F, and the City of Mission does not govern it. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot here?** Vacant and raw land is the feed's core product (a 5-acre vacant parcel, multi-title raw-land packages, farm parcels — §2), but no named subdivision selling serviced lots was verified; each parcel is checked for servicing, flood and ALR status by PID. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to?** The Province: rural-area properties receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office; taxes are due July 2, with 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. Mission's flat 10% penalty does not apply. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a second home?** Two gates: in Area F a secondary suite must sit within the principal dwelling (a detached garden suite is not the Area F suite route), and on ALR land an additional detached residence is capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha) on top of one principal residence of at most 500 m². A feed card's "zoned for a secondary dwelling" line clears neither gate by itself. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address — most inhabited valley land is in the reserve per the FVRD, but parcel status was not verified in the research pass, and feed ALR statements run both ways (one parcel in, part of another out) as listing claims only. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is this valley flood-exposed?** The valley floor has a documented 1948 dyke-break flood history (Hatzic Lake grew five times in size to 25 km²; Hatzic Prairie largely under water) — but a specific property's current flood status and dyke protection are parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED here. (§2 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a land purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($700,000, illustration only) the bill is $12,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Where do Durieu children go to school?** There is no school in Durieu itself on the confirmed record; the confirmed SD75 schools on the valley/Mission side are Hatzic Elementary, Hatzic Middle School and Mission Senior Secondary — but the catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED, with no locator run on record. (§1 above)
- **Q: A Stave Lake Road listing quotes Mission rules — is that right?** Not necessarily: Stave Lake Road cards sit toward the valley's western edge near the City boundary, and which side of the FVRD/City line a specific parcel sits on is UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the PID before quoting City or rural rules. (§1 above)
