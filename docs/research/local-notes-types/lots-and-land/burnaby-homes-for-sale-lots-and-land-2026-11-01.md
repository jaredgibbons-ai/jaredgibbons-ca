## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815 (adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026):** the current bylaw. The former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965, No. 4742, is repealed and is NEVER cited as current. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan (adopted December 9, 2025):** the City's current long-term plan integrating land use, transportation, housing, environment, community facilities and economic planning, reviewed every five years under provincial requirements, replacing the 1998 OCP. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan and https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 SSMUH lots are eligible to build up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, the total inclusive of any secondary suites; forms include single-family homes, laneway homes, secondary suites, duplexes, triplexes, fourplexes and, on some properties, fiveplexes and sixplexes; the R1 zone still allows single-family homes. A secondary suite classified under the BC Building Code must remain on the same title as the primary unit and cannot be stratified. Burnaby suite size cap and owner-occupancy rule: UNVERIFIED — the City pages read state neither. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing:** the servicing-bylaw base number is UNCONFIRMED (the bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024) — so this file documents the process instead: subdivision and servicing requirements for a specific Burnaby parcel are confirmed with the City under the bylaw in force at application; no base number is stated rather than guessed. (Standing Burnaby frame for this program.)
- **Short-term rentals in Burnaby:** UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this pass. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Burnaby bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Burnaby ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $800,000 → $14,000; $1.8 million → $34,000; $2.0 million → $38,000; $3.5 million → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000; a fully qualifying first-time buyer at $800,000 pays $6,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, after which a 5% late penalty applies, with an additional 5% penalty after the September due date on outstanding current taxes; the City has no authority to waive or modify the penalties. Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Burnaby-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in this slug's research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this city, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Burnaby land actually documented?** Sub-area by sub-area: 22 sub-area land files exist on disk (Section 2), and 13 sub-areas — including Metrotown and SFU — have no land file yet. This city file holds no roster and takes no counts.
- **Q: How many homes can a Burnaby house lot hold?** In the R1 SSMUH District, up to 3 to 6 dwelling units depending on lot area and proximity to a frequent transit network bus stop, secondary suites counted inside the total — checked under Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), in effect July 1, 2026, not under the repealed 1965 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Is an SFU-area lot or home freehold like a Brentwood one?** Not on the evidence read: UniverCity product sits on leasehold prepaid ground under SFU Community Trust 99-year ground leases, and one feed listing states a leasehold expiring in 2109 — confirm tenure on title before comparing prices. https://jaredgibbons.ca/burnaby-homes-for-sale.html
- **Q: A Hastings Street complex looks like cheap strata land value — is it?** Kensington Gardens is the trap: undivided-interest co-ownership with no strata corporation and no Form B, excluded program-wide; Hall Towers is BC Housing non-market rental. Neither is purchasable strata stock. (Condos sibling, §2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT do I budget on a Burnaby detached purchase?** On the standard tiers: $1.8 million → $34,000 and $2.0 million → $38,000 before any exemption; a qualifying first-time buyer exempts the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve my lot's address?** SD41, address-based, with the Ministry-listed schools in Section 1 — but no Burnaby address-level locator result is on record, so run the exact address with the district before any school claim; listing catchment text is copy only. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-13/burnaby-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Q: How did Burnaby trade in September 2026?** Detached sold 50 of 458 at a $1,825,000 community median while "Condos & Townhomes" combined sold 147 of 1,333 at $686,000 — community context only; land has no SnapStats category and sub-area medians are not published. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats FVR/GVR September 2026 report.)

- **Q: When are Burnaby property taxes due?** For 2026, Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the due date and a further 5% after the September due date; the City cannot waive or modify them. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties
