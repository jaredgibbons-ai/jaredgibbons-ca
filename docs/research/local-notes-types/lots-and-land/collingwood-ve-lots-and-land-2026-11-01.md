## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Renfrew-Collingwood — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale lot offering was verified on any City or developer source. What trades as "land" here is the redevelopment value of built fee-simple house lots on the R1-1 interior streets and of station-area and Kingsway parcels layered by the Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan and the July 2025 Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan. The named buildings in the siblings (Joyce by Westbank, Wall Centre Central Park, Frame) are built strata developments — never lot inventory — and the one tower a land buyer will hear about, Joyce 2, is an excluded rental in receivership, documented below with its reason.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning framework as the research states it:** the house streets sit under the R1-1 District Schedule of the City of Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 (minimums in Section 2), with C-2 on arterial commercial streets such as Kingsway, layered by the Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan, the Norquay Village Neighbourhood Centre Plan and the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan (approved July 2025), on top of the Renfrew-Collingwood Community Vision adopted by Council. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/joyce-collingwood-station-precinct-review.aspx
- **SSMUH / multiplex potential as the City states it:** under R1-1 a multiple dwelling may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units on a qualifying lot (minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by unit count, Section 2), and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — parcel-specific. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0, and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements — expressly including bare-land strata proposals) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Floodplain / creek diligence:** Still Creek is one of the few remaining salmon-bearing streams in Vancouver, helps prevent flooding, and its floodplain is part of the City's designated floodplain dataset; the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan includes nature-based solutions to manage flood risk around Still Creek. Parcels in the floodplain carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — whether a specific parcel is in the designated floodplain: UNVERIFIED; confirm the parcel in the City dataset, especially on ravine-edge purchases near Renfrew Ravine Park. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/still-creek.aspx and https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Leasehold positions — none on this ground, stated to prevent imports:** no leasehold, co-operative or prepaid-leasehold land position is documented for Renfrew-Collingwood in its research. The False Creek South 60-year City ground leases and Champlain Heights' 99-year prepaid leasehold are other areas' records (False Creek South ground only; Champlain Heights in Killarney) and are never merged into a Collingwood land position. (FILE STATUS block.)
- **Bare-land strata rules (no plan verified here — the BC strata block that WOULD apply in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Collingwood ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Collingwood lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here:** properties declared, determined or deemed vacant are subject to a 3% tax of the property's assessed value under the Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674 framework (per the townhomes sibling's cited Council source); most homes that are principal residences or rented for at least six months of the year are not subject to it. Owners must submit an annual property status declaration; the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. No provincial speculation/vacancy position beyond this is documented in this file's research — none imported. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for Collingwood land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Collingwood VE, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or bare-land subdivision I can buy into in Collingwood?** No verified one — the area is built east-side Vancouver; land value sits in existing house lots and station-area parcels, and no bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the difference between Joyce and Joyce 2?** Everything that matters: Joyce by Westbank at 5058 Joyce Street is a 256-unit Freehold Strata tower (EPS7496, finished 2021); Joyce 2 at 5083 Joyce Street is a 360-unit purpose-built rental tower placed in receivership by court order granted April 27, 2026, with leasing reported as continuing. Confirm the civic address before anything else. https://www.estateblock.com/vancouver/building/joyce-EPS7496 and https://www.mpamag.com/ca/mortgage-industry/industry-trends/westbanks-joyce-tower-placed-in-receivership/574795
- **Q: What can an interior house lot become under R1-1?** A multiplex of up to 6 units, or up to 8 rental units, on a qualifying lot (306 m² minimum for 3–4 units, 464 m² for 5, 557 m² for 6–8), or a single detached house with a secondary suite and a laneway house. Station-area lots may carry additional expectations under the precinct and station-area plans — check which plan covers the lot. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Which plans layer on top of the base zoning here?** The Joyce-Collingwood Station Precinct Plan around Joyce Station, the Norquay Village Neighbourhood Centre Plan at the Kingsway centre, and the Rupert and Renfrew Station Area Plan (approved July 2025) at the north side — price the plan that covers the lot, not just R1-1. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/rupert-and-renfrew-station-area-plan-approved-july-2025.aspx
- **Q: What does it cost to subdivide in Vancouver?** Under By-law No. 14538's 2026 Schedule F to Subdivision By-law No. 5208: $15,020 for a Class III (Minor) application, $87,200 Class II, $174,300 Class I (Major), and $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot for Class VI freehold rowhouses — application fees only, before servicing and legal costs. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Q: What creek or flood checks apply near Renfrew Ravine?** Still Creek runs through Renfrew Ravine Park and the area; its floodplain is in the City's designated floodplain dataset, and parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — confirm the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a Collingwood house left empty?** Yes — this is Vancouver: a home declared, determined or deemed vacant is taxed at 3% of its assessed value, every owner must file an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026 with a 5% late penalty. Homes used as a principal residence or rented at least six months of the year are generally not subject to it. https://council.vancouver.ca/20251029/documents/pspc6.pdf
- **Q: Is any Collingwood land leasehold, like False Creek South or Champlain Heights?** No leasehold position is documented for this area in its research — those are other areas' records (False Creek South's 60-year City ground leases; Champlain Heights' 99-year prepaid leasehold in Killarney) and are never carried into a Collingwood position. Confirm tenure on title for the specific lot. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Collingwood lot?** By exact address only — three elementaries sit inside the wider area (Nootka, John Norquay, Renfrew) with Windermere Secondary, and no Collingwood catchment is settled in this file. Run every address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
