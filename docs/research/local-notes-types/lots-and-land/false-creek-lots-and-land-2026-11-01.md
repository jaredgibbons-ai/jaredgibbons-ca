## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in False Creek — stated plainly at the top, for a tenure reason unique in this group: the south-shore land is City-held ground-lease land that is not sold fee-simple at all, the north-shore and Southeast False Creek parcels sit under site-specific CD-1 districts and Official Development Plans rather than lot offerings, and detached houses are not a False Creek shoreline housing form. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified on any official or developer source. The land story here is tenure: who holds title, and how much lease remains.**

### City leasehold — False Creek South (the defining land form here)

- **False Creek South ground leases — corrected position, from the City's own page:** the City holds title to 80 acres of land in False Creek South; when the neighbourhood was built in the 1970s and 1980s, much of the City land was leased to tenants via 60-year ground leases, with most of the leases expiring in the next 15 to 25 years, and no new housing has been built in False Creek South since the 1980s. Current listings show 2040/2050 terms, consistent with that window. The lease terms and end date for a specific building: UNVERIFIED — they must be read off that building's own lease documents. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **Co-operative ground on the same City tenure:** Creekview Housing Co-operative's City ground lease expired December 31, 2023 and is in overholding, per the October 7, 2025 Council report — documented as tenure context for the False Creek South leasehold/co-op ground, never as for-sale stock and never as fee-simple. (Standing corrected position for this program; City False Creek South page: https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx)
- **Non-merger, stated both ways:** the position above is False Creek South's alone. It is NOT Champlain Heights' position — Champlain Heights carries a separate 99-year prepaid leasehold position in its own files — and Champlain Heights' framing is never imported here, just as False Creek South's 60-year/expiring position is never exported there. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx

- **Fairview Slopes houses:** freehold houses sit on the slopes above the south shore under the FM-1 district, which permits single detached houses, duplexes and multiple dwellings with a maximum floor space ratio of 0.60. FM-1 ground is the fee-simple exception in this file; shoreline parcels are not FM-1 lots. The zone and lot size for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/northeast-false-creek-play-and-work.aspx
- **Shoreline parcels:** the Southeast False Creek / Olympic Village lands and the False Creek North lands sit under site-specific CD-1 Comprehensive Development districts and Official Development Plans, including the False Creek North Official Development Plan — there is no single district schedule for False Creek and no lot-minimum figure for a shoreline parcel: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/northeast-false-creek-play-and-work.aspx

- **No bare-land strata plan around False Creek was verified** on any source in the research or the siblings. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — no single district schedule:** Southeast False Creek / Olympic Village and False Creek North sit under site-specific CD-1 districts and Official Development Plans (including the False Creek North ODP); FM-1 applies on the Fairview Slopes above the south shore (single detached, duplex, multiple dwellings; maximum FSR 0.60). A specific parcel's zone: UNVERIFIED — read it by part (North, South or Flats), never for "False Creek" as a whole. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/northeast-false-creek-play-and-work.aspx
- **Plans as the OCP-equivalent frame here:** the Northeast False Creek Plan, the Southeast False Creek development, the False Creek Flats Plan and the pending False Creek South framework (Council direction March 2024) are the plan documents governing this ground — each covers its own part only. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). No split of City leasehold ground is a fee-simple subdivision at all — False Creek South ground follows the lease, not the Subdivision By-law. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Flood diligence — the defining risk file:** the lands around False Creek are inside the City's designated floodplain system; the City's False Creek Coastal Adaptation Plan covers the floodplain area encompassing False Creek North, False Creek South, Granville Island and the Flats, and the Northeast False Creek Plan requires new developments to be built to protect homes and critical infrastructure from flooding and rising sea levels, with a continuous line of flood protection across that site. Land in the designated floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels — the flood construction level for a specific building is a core due-diligence item on both shores; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata block (for the conventional strata stock; no bare-land strata is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. On leasehold strata, the ground lease is read alongside the Form B — remaining term drives financing and pricing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — ALR land inside the sub-area: UNVERIFIED in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. Homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. The City tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy the land under a False Creek South home?** No — the City holds title to 80 acres in False Creek South and the homes sit on 60-year ground leases; what transfers is the leasehold interest, never the fee-simple land. Read the building's own lease before subjects: remaining term drives financing and pricing. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **Q: Is False Creek South a 99-year prepaid leasehold expiring in the 2080s?** No — that framing is not the False Creek South position and belongs to a different area's tenure (Champlain Heights, in its own files). False Creek South is 60-year City ground leases, most expiring in the next 15–25 years, with current listings showing 2040/2050 terms. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/false-creek-south.aspx
- **Q: What happens when a False Creek South lease expires?** One documented case is already past expiry: Creekview Housing Co-operative's City ground lease expired December 31, 2023 and is in overholding per the October 7, 2025 Council report. For any other building, the end-of-lease position is in its own lease documents: UNVERIFIED per building. The City's updated False Creek South framework (Council direction March 2024) is pending and is flagged in §2, not absorbed.
- **Q: Is False Creek a City of Vancouver local area?** No — the City's 22 local areas treat it in parts: False Creek North inside Downtown, False Creek South inside Fairview, and the False Creek Flats at the east end in Strathcona; "False Creek" is the Paragon sub-area name spanning all three. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Is flooding a real factor for False Creek homes?** Yes — the area is inside the City's designated floodplain system, the Coastal Adaptation Plan covers the False Creek floodplain (North, South, Granville Island and the Flats), and parcels in the floodplain carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements; a specific parcel's status must be checked in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Can a False Creek lot be subdivided?** Fee-simple ground only, through the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (Schedule A minimums by district/category, Approving Officer; fees under No. 14538) — and only where a district schedule applies at all; shoreline parcels sit under CD-1/ODP frameworks, and City leasehold ground follows the lease, not the Subdivision By-law. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant False Creek property?** It can — Vancouver's EHT is 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant, every owner files an annual declaration, and it is a Vancouver-only tax. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Which schools are located around False Creek?** Crosstown šxʷwəq̓ʷəθət Elementary (55 Expo Boulevard) and Elsie Roy Elementary (150 Drake Street) on the north shore, and False Creek Elementary (900 School Green) on the south shore; no shoreline secondary school was confirmed, and catchments are address-based and differ by shore — the sample address's catchment is UNVERIFIED (locator not run). https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- **Q: What transit serves the south shore?** TransLink route 50 runs from Waterfront Station via Chinatown, Granville Island and the south shore to Olympic Village Station on the Canada Line, continuing as route 15. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt050.pdf
