# Aldergrove, Township of Langley — Lots and Land: Local Notes

Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No listing addresses or asking prices beyond named developments themselves. No sold prices.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Aldergrove is a community in the Township of Langley; the Township states its eastern boundary is 276 Street bordering the City of Abbotsford and its southern boundary is the United States border. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Core plan boundary (core only): the Aldergrove Core Area is centred on Fraser Highway and 272 Street, bounded by Bertrand Creek to the north and east, 29 Avenue to the south and 270 Street to the west. Wider community boundaries are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Nearby areas: Abbotsford across 276 Street to the east, rural south Langley to the west, the US border directly south; Highway 1 runs through the Township north of Aldergrove. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- Where land concentrates: two worlds — small urban lots in the core around Fraser Highway/272 Street, and large rural (RU-2) and suburban lots south and east of the core toward the border and Abbotsford. Sources: Sections 2–3 below.
- Transit: TransLink route 503 (Aldergrove/Langley – Fraser Hwy Express) from 272 Street at Fraser Highway via Langley Centre to Surrey Central Station (roughly 70 minutes end-to-end early morning, about 90 at peak, per the June 8, 2026 timetable); BC Transit route 21 links Aldergrove with Abbotsford from the same stop. No SkyTrain or West Coast Express station is in Aldergrove. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt503.pdf
- Schools physically in Aldergrove include Parkside Centennial Elementary (3300 270 Street), Shortreed Community School (27330 28 Avenue — Ministry-listed name; the research file's "Shortreed Community Elementary" wording is not used), Coghlan Fundamental Elementary, North Otter Elementary, Betty Gilbert Middle School and Aldergrove Community Secondary. The SD35 locator result documented in the research is for 26850 29 Avenue only: Parkside Centennial Elementary → Betty Gilbert Middle → Aldergrove Community Secondary. Any other address needs its own locator run. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or presale LOT offering was verified in Aldergrove on an official or developer source. Land here trades as individual fee-simple parcels — urban lots in the core, suburban and rural acreages outside it. The one large named development, Alder Grove Town Centre, is built/presale apartment-condo product, cross-referenced below and never counted as lots. Any lot count, subdivision lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price would be invented, so none is stated.**

### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple parcels — the default form.** The established stock is freehold single-family housing on its own lots; the core plan describes residential lands in the plan area as large-lot single-family homes, with some townhouses and apartments including fourplexes on 30 Avenue west of 272 Street. Bare land, when it trades, trades in that same fee-simple form — a parcel, not a strata lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/ocp-update/Att-B---Aldergrove-CP---Redlined.pdf
- **Lot character, as the research states it (Zoning Bylaw 2500, s.110.1 ladder):** SR-1 minimum lot area 3,716 m² (frontage 40 m); SR-2 8,094 m² / 45 m; SR-3 1,765 m² / 27.45 m; R-1E 930 m² / 22 m; R-1D 650 m² / 18.25 m; R-1B and R-1C 464.5 m² / 15.25 m. Zones named for Aldergrove parcels include Rural Zone RU-2, Community Commercial C-2 and Comprehensive Development CD-192 — their minimum lot areas/frontages are UNVERIFIED from a readable bylaw section, so no RU-2 subdivision figure is stated. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Bare-land STRATA: none verified in Aldergrove.** No bare-land strata subdivision is documented in the research file or the siblings for this area. Where bare-land strata exists elsewhere in BC it is strata tenure (Form B/fees/bylaws apply to the lot — Section 3 block), but no Aldergrove lot is documented in that form here.
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** much of the Township's land is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; no Aldergrove-specific ALR percentage is published in the research, so no split figure is stated. The Township directs applicants to check a property's ALR designation and watercourses in GeoSource before applying. Bertrand Creek bounds the core to the north and east. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Servicing:** municipal water/sewer vs well/septic for any single Aldergrove parcel: UNCONFIRMED in the research — the core is urban-serviced in character and the rural south is not documented parcel-by-parcel; confirm servicing for the specific lot with the Township before pricing a build. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500** governs, with the s.110.1 minimums in Section 2. Get the parcel's zone from the Township/GeoSource first — RU-2, C-2 and CD-192 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable section and are not filled in here. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Plans:** the Aldergrove Community Plan and the Aldergrove Core Area Plan govern the community, within the Township OCP update work. (Willoughby context carried for accuracy only: the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025), and Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY — none of that is an Aldergrove plan and none is stated as adopted beyond what is written here.) https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **SSMUH in the Township: Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024)** amended Bylaw 2500 to enable small-scale multi-unit housing in zones previously restricted to single-family/duplex. NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020 — any page claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses:** secondary-suite cap is 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — part of the same fee-simple title, never a separate strata lot, and it cannot be sold separately. LIVE s.102 CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved: the July 2025 bylaw text permits townhouse suites at 120 m² while the January 2025 guide says not permitted. A suite/coach-house plan for a specific lot needs the current Township position in writing. (Standing correction; framework: https://www.tol.ca/en/plans-reports-and-strategies/plans-and-strategies.aspx)
- **R-CL context (Willoughby, carried only to prevent misapplication):** R-CL s.403 is 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum — a Willoughby zone figure, NOT an Aldergrove standard; do not apply it to an Aldergrove parcel.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements beyond zone minimums:** UNCONFIRMED in the research for Aldergrove parcels — never filled in from another municipality. Confirm subdivision servicing (roads, water, sewer/septic, drainage) with the Township for the specific application.
- **ALR rules (where ALR ground exists):** check the parcel in GeoSource/ALC mapping first. Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood:** parcel-level floodplain status for Aldergrove properties is UNVERIFIED in the research — check the specific parcel (Bertrand Creek bounds the core) before building plans are priced.
- **Property transfer tax:** FTHB Program — no PTT on the first $500,000 (max $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (a new-HOME exemption — bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED, do not promise it on vacant land). General PTT is 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus 2% on the residential portion above $3M. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** CRA GI-003 states most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt, while business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out. HBP: $60,000 per buyer. FHSA: $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime, combinable with HBP. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Aldergrove-specific position is documented in this area's research, so none is imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY — not applicable here.
- **Bare-land strata block (no Aldergrove bare-land strata verified — applies only if a lot is in that form):** Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property taxes:** Township taxes due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 3, 2026; utilities now billed separately. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a serviced building lot in a named Aldergrove subdivision?** No named bare-land subdivision was verified here — the market is individual fee-simple parcels. Treat any "new subdivision lots" claim as UNCONFIRMED until a developer/municipal source names it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can an Aldergrove lot be?** It depends entirely on the zone: the s.110.1 ladder runs from 464.5 m² (R-1B/R-1C) to 8,094 m² (SR-2); RU-2 minimums are UNVERIFIED from a readable section. Get the zone before valuing a split. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: Is the rural lot in the ALR?** Much Township land is; check the parcel in GeoSource. In the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and residences cannot be stratified. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-applications.aspx
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a lot I buy?** Suite cap 120 m²; a coach house under s.102 stays on the same fee-simple title and cannot be sold separately. Note the live s.102 townhouse-suite conflict in Section 3 — get the Township's current position for your housing form. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 6 homes on a single-family lot?** No — Bylaw 6020 has NO 6-plex tier. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/2026-03-09-F.2-26-26-cpp-SSMUH-DP-Guidelines.pdf
- **Q: Is the lot on city water/sewer or well/septic?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel in the research — confirm with the Township for the exact lot; do not assume core servicing extends to rural parcels south/east of the core. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** If the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided, generally yes (taxable); an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt per CRA GI-003. Confirm in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold vacant land here?** Township taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% + 5% penalties after July 2 / September 3, 2026) plus separately billed utilities where connected, and farm-class compliance costs on farm parcels. No strata fees on a fee-simple lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **Q: Can I get farm class on a small Aldergrove acreage?** Only on the three-tier test — under 0.8 ha needs $10,000, the highest tier; acreage alone does not qualify. Applications by October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Which school will a lot near 29 Avenue feed?** Only the 26850 29 Avenue locator chain is documented (Parkside Centennial → Betty Gilbert → Aldergrove Community Secondary). Run the exact address in the SD35 locator. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
