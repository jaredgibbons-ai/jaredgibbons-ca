## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **MHPTA pad-rent limits — Act percentages ONLY:** landlords in manufactured home parks can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent once every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; the 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount; standard increases are notified on RTB Form 11a, and a landlord may apply to the RTB (Form 52) for an additional increase above the limit for qualifying expense or capital reasons. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** a tenant may assign a manufactured home site tenancy agreement, or sublet the site, only with (a) the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed obtained under the Regulation), (b) an order of the director authorizing it, or (c) authorization in the tenancy agreement itself; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The consent request runs on RTB-10. (Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s.28, BC Laws, verified in the research pass) and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/calculators-and-resources/tenancy-forms/forms-listed-number
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute the notice within 15 days, may in defined circumstances apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. The conversion notice is RTB-31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Manufactured Home Registry / CSA labels:** manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes, typically built in a factory off-site; mobile homes are built under CSA Z-240 and modular homes under CSA A-277; in B.C., the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered, and the Registry maintains the central register of ownership, controls movement of manufactured homes, and improves lender security. A home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached; if a label is lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the wiring and an electrical approval label is prepared, usable in lieu of a CSA label (the official route behind the live page's "Silver Label" shorthand). A permit to move a home is issued by the Registry with a tax certificate; the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **OCP / zoning / subdivision (own-land parcels only):** OCP Bylaw 5500 "Chilliwack 2050" governs the whole city; Zoning Bylaw 5000 governs lot by lot, and its manufactured-home-park zone section was NOT extracted in the research pass — park-zone figures UNVERIFIED. Subdivision of an own-land parcel runs through the City's Approving Officer and Preliminary Layout Approval process. **The servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is asserted, and no other city's number is borrowed.** City-wide suite/SSMUH frame (Bylaw 5395, Jun 18, 2024; suite lesser of 90 m² and 40%; 1 ADU per lot; garden suites to 100 m²) is confirmed for Chilliwack housing generally in the research. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/chilliwack-mobile-homes.md) and (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, items 6 and 7f)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where an own-land parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel on this topic. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for a Baker Trail Village-type lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw, and a park rule is not a strata bylaw in any case. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land (own-land purchases only):** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** an $800,000 own-land parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**; with a fully qualifying first-time-buyer exemption on the first $500,000, the same purchase is **$6,000**. The live page's claim that PTT "generally does not apply" to a home-only purchase on a rented pad is UNVERIFIED in the research — no Province PTT passage stating that treatment was opened; have a lawyer or notary confirm the position for the specific purchase. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **City property taxes (2026):** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% penalty applies after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% after August 4, 2026. How a specific park home is assessed and taxed depends on its tenure and registration — a per-property check, not stated generally here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in a Chilliwack park, do I own land?** No — on a rented pad you own the manufactured home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA, paying monthly site rent to the park. Land ownership exists only in the own-land forms: fee-simple lots, or bare-land strata lots such as Baker Trail Village. (§2 above)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in a Chilliwack park?** No figure is stated in this file: no official source publishes Chilliwack pad rents, and the live page's $550–$950/month range is UNVERIFIED site copy. The figure that governs is the specific site's tenancy agreement — confirm the current rent, what it includes, and its increase history with the park for the exact site. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, and no more than the yearly limit: 2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. Above-limit increases require an RTB order (Form 52). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park need to approve my purchase on a rented pad?** Yes — assignment of the site tenancy requires the landlord's prior written consent (RTB-10), an order of the director, or authorization in the tenancy agreement itself (MHPTA s.28), and the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting (s.28(3)). (§3 above)
- **Q: What is Baker Trail Village, and why is it different?** A 157-unit bare-land strata of manufactured homes at 46511 Chilliwack Lake Road — you own the home AND a strata lot, pay strata fees (not pad rent), and take a Form B. It is strata tenure on the lot, not a pad tenancy and not fee-simple. (§2 above)
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 for mobile, A-277 for modular); if it is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route before it can be listed for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes and a further payment (greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent) if the landlord does not proceed within a reasonable period. (§3 above)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT when I buy a park home?** For an own-land purchase, PTT applies on the general tiers (§3 worked example: $800,000 = $14,000, or $6,000 fully-qualifying FTHB). For a home-only purchase on a rented pad, the live page's "generally does not apply" claim is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm with a lawyer or notary for the exact structure before subjects come off. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is Rainbow Estates a 40+ or a 45+ community?** UNVERIFIED — the feed's two cards for the same Ashwell Road community state 40+ in one card's text and 45+ in the other's. Age rules must come from the park's current documents for the specific site, and a "45+" line is in any case not a valid strata age bylaw — in a park it would be a park rule, not a bylaw. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: Can I move the home to another site?** A Registry transport permit is required, supported by a tax certificate; the permit expires 30 days after issue and the move must take place within that window. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
