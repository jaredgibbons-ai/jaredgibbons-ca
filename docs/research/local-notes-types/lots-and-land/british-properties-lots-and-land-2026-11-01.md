## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The British Properties is the wider master-planned hillside area developed by British Pacific Properties in the District of West Vancouver: the large-lot residential benches above Ambleside and Park Royal, from the Taylor Way side west across the Chartwell/Canterbury benches and up to the Rodgers Creek / Cypress Bowl Road lands. District-wide land use is guided by the Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. An official District planning-area name "British Properties" and street-by-street boundaries are NOT CONFIRMED in the OCP, whose structure names the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, Park Royal and the village centres instead; the confirmed anchors are Taylor Way / Park Royal at the east foot, Chartwell Drive at the centre, and Cypress Bowl Road / Rodgers Creek at the upper west. Paragon (GVR) sub-area match and code: UNVERIFIED. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- History anchor: the Properties were developed from the early 1930s by a group of British investors led by the Guinness family, which purchased most of the municipality's Upper Lands and built the Lions Gate Bridge (1937–38) — the venture behind the master-planned, large-lot layout. https://westvancouver.ca/arts-culture/heritage/west-vancouver-yesterday-and-today
- Nearby areas: Ambleside and Park Royal (south, below Highway 1), Cedardale and Sentinel Hill (east, toward the Capilano River), Dundarave (west, below the benches), and Cypress Park / the Upper Lands (west and north-west). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Roads and transit: Taylor Way connects Marine Drive and Park Royal up into the Properties; Highway 1 (Upper Levels Highway) crosses above the original subdivision with access at Taylor Way and Cypress Bowl Road; the Lions Gate Bridge via Taylor Way and Marine Drive is the fixed crossing to Vancouver. TransLink route 254 (British Properties / Park Royal) climbs from Park Royal via Taylor Way, Southborough Drive, Eyremount Drive, Cross Creek Road, Chartwell Drive, Crestwell Road, Fairmile Road, King Georges Way, Robin Hood Road, Kenwood Road, Elveden Road, St. Andrews Road and Bonnymuir Drive to the Glenmore Drive terminus, with selected trips to downtown Vancouver. Park Royal Exchange is the western terminus of the R2 RapidBus, which since September 7, 2026 runs one-seat through Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange to Metrotown Station. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt254.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**The British Properties' land trades as fee-simple estate lots on the established benches — large RS-zone lots under registered British Pacific Properties covenants — and there is no standing vacant-lot subdivision program in the original subdivision. The genuine new-land story sits ABOVE it: British Pacific Properties' Rodgers Creek / Uplands program (Mulgrave Park estate lots, Hawksley, Cypress Village, and the CD3 Areas 5–6 caps), documented below from District and developer sources as development land, never as a lot price list. No bare-land strata plan in the wider area was verified.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established-bench estate lots:** detached houses on large hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 — the original subdivision is a single-family estate area, predominantly freehold detached; strata is concentrated in the Rodgers Creek / Uplands product above it and the small Whitby Estates pockets. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as stated:** Section 200 minimum site areas are 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record; the larger RS1/RS2 minimums are the ones associated with the Properties benches. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone for a specific lot is confirmed on the District's Zoning Map. Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are among the permitted uses. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Covenant fabric — part of the land itself here:** British Properties subdivisions carry restrictive covenants registered at the Land Title Office — an owner must file an application for approval of plans with British Pacific Properties Limited before building a new home, extending or altering an existing building, or erecting a fence or any structure; the covenants differ by subdivision, bind every subsequent owner, and District zoning and building bylaws apply in addition. (Developer source.) https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **Serviced position, as the District states it:** West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District — the serviced position for District lots as the District states it. Septic or well servicing by street: UNVERIFIED in the research; confirm the specific parcel with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED in the research. No ALR percentage split is stated; check any parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Hazard frame:** creeks cross this hillside and the forest interface borders it — an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area (within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010:** original-subdivision houses sit in the RS zones (Section 200 minimums in Section 2); the Rodgers Creek lands above sit in site-specific Comprehensive Development zones (CD3) instead. The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018:** the District treats the British Properties as an established existing neighbourhood; growth is directed to the town and village centres (Ambleside, Park Royal, Dundarave, Caulfeild, Horseshoe Bay), not into the Properties. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the District states it:** Section 200 lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the RS zones, and the ground-oriented rules above cap added dwellings per lot — eligibility and form are parcel-specific under Bylaw 4662; confirm with the District before pricing in suite potential. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of West Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's RS zone minimum site area (Section 2), servicing is District metered water and sewer billed quarterly, the BPP covenant plan-approval step (Section 2) runs before or alongside the District permit process, and hazard development permits (below) gate hillside work. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard Development Permits (District frame):** Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. Check both on every hillside lot early. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (for the Uplands/Whitby strata a land buyer may pivot to):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Properties ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an estate-lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no British Properties-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant estate lot in the original Properties?** No standing program was verified — the original subdivision trades as built estate lots. The one developer-named lot program is Mulgrave Park Estate Lots in the Rodgers Creek area above, on British Pacific Properties' own site; no count, size or price is carried here. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What extra approval does a rebuild need here?** British Pacific Properties plan approval under the registered covenants — before building a new home, extending or altering a building, or erecting a fence or any structure — in addition to District permits. The covenants differ by subdivision and bind every subsequent owner. (Developer source.) https://illustrator.uservoice.com/assets/243142901/Plan-Approval-Instructions-11.22.18.pdf
- **Q: How small can a Properties lot be subdivided?** By zone: RS minimums run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with RS2 at 1,625 m² and RS3 at 975 m² — and the benches are associated with the larger RS1/RS2 minimums. Confirm the parcel's zone on the District Zoning Map. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: What is being built above the Properties, and can I buy a lot in it?** Rodgers Creek under CD3: Areas 5 and 6 capped at 354 and 620 homes (974 combined), apartments to 16 storeys, with a 354-unit permit for Area 5 approved July 8, 2025, plus Cypress Village initial works under DP 24-115 and the Hawksley condo/townhome project. - **Q: Which hazard permits can hit a hillside lot?** A Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface, and an Environmental Development Permit within 15 metres of a watercourse bank top. Check both before subjects come off. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is Hawksley British Properties or Cypress Park Estates?** Both namings exist: the developer markets it as a British Properties community, while listings classify its subdivision as Cypress Park Estates. - **Q: Does the additional school tax apply at these prices?** Commonly, yes: 0.2% on the residential assessed portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Budget it in closing math. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
