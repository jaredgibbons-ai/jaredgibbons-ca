## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Catchments are the district's lines, not the market's (this topic's defining rule):** SD36 catchments are set by street address through the School Locator and boundary map; they can change on enrolment review; French Immersion has its own registration, separate from the catchment school; and SnapStats publishes NO school-catchment segment — there is no catchment-based count, share or median for any school, and any "in-catchment median" claim is UNVERIFIED by construction. Existence and assignment are different claims: the BC Ministry K-12 file is the existence authority (name + street address, 2025/2026); a district URL slug is not evidence of a school or a catchment. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36 and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/south-surrey-school-catchments.md)
- **OCP (Surrey side): Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 — adopted June 15, 2026 — current.** Bylaw 18020 is the replaced predecessor only and is NEVER cited as current. Zoning is under Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 for Surrey lots. White Rock lots follow White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506 (adopted June 27, 2024) and OCP Bylaw 2017, No. 2220 — the school district is the same SD36 on both sides; the building rules are not. https://www.surrey.ca/planning-development/plans-strategies/surrey-official-community-plan and https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- **Subdivision / servicing (Surrey):** the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw (verified on the City's own pages in an earlier batch of this program), and the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **SSMUH / suites / short-term rentals (Surrey lots):** Surrey's SSMUH bands are 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. A secondary suite is capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area. Short-term rentals are permitted only in the operator's own principal residence, owner-only, with a City business licence at $358/year as stated on the City's licence page — DISCLOSED CONFLICT: a City of Surrey 2024 corporate report proposed $350/year; the licence page's $358 figure is carried with the conflict disclosed. SSMUH applies to eligible single-family lots, not to lots inside an existing strata complex. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/secondary-suites and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/permits-licences/short-term-rentals
- **Bare-land strata block (for a bare-land strata lot on this ground, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A Form B confirms a lot's strata facts; it does not confirm a school catchment — that is the locator's job, per address. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel in this topic. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $900,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes:** Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. White Rock addresses follow the City of White Rock's own tax calendar — UNVERIFIED in this pass; do not carry Surrey's dates across the municipal line. **Foreclosure note:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — a Surrey foreclosure runs through a registry outside the city. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes and (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/king-george-corridor-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How do I find the catchment school for a South Surrey lot or home?** Enter the exact address in the Surrey Schools School Locator. Catchments are set by street address, not by neighbourhood or MLS sub-area name, so the listing's area name cannot answer the question — and the page's own practice is to check before booking a showing and again before subjects are removed, because lines can change on enrolment review. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36 and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-school-catchments.html
- **Q: Is there a recorded catchment result for a South Surrey address in this research?** Yes — one: 1785 148 St → H.T. Thrift Elementary / Semiahmoo Secondary (SD36 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Both schools are confirmed in the Ministry K-12 file at 1739 148 St and 1785 148 St respectively. It is evidence for that address only — the sample address is Semiahmoo Secondary's own address — not for the Sunnyside Park sub-area as a whole. (§1 above)
- **Q: Does all of Sunnyside Park feed Semiahmoo?** UNVERIFIED as an area claim — the live page's own Sunnyside summary splits the area at 152 Street, which is exactly why area-level school claims fail. Run the exact address. (§2 above)
- **Q: Does all of White Rock go to one secondary school?** The live page states it does not: it presents White Rock Elementary feeding Semiahmoo Secondary and Peace Arch Elementary feeding Earl Marriott Secondary. Those feeder statements are the page's own copy and were not confirmed against a district document opened in this pass, so treat them as to-be-verified and run the exact address in the locator. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-school-catchments.html
- **Q: My lot is on the same street as a school I want. Is it in that school's catchment?** Not on that evidence. Proximity and street name are not a locator result — the program's one recorded sample is a school's own address, and even it proves the answer for that address only. Run the lot's exact civic address before making any school claim. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=BC36
- **Q: Is there an "East Newton Elementary," and what is East Newton's catchment?** No such school exists — it appears in neither the BC Ministry K-12 file (2025/2026) nor SD36's directory, and no school stands at 7235 144 St. There is also no East Newton locator result on record: the recorded address ranges jump 7069–7195 → 7263–7263, so no range contains 7235. Never take a school name from a district URL slug. (§1 above)
- **Q: Is there a published catchment premium for Semiahmoo or Earl Marriott lots?** No published figure exists to quote: SnapStats has no catchment segment, so there is no "in-catchment median" for Semiahmoo, Elgin Park, Earl Marriott or Grandview Heights — any catchment premium claim for a lot must come from that lot's own comparables, not from a segment statistic. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does the municipality change the school answer for a lot?** No — SD36 covers both Surrey and White Rock addresses. What the municipality changes is the building rule book: White Rock lots follow White Rock Zoning Bylaw 2024, No. 2506; Surrey lots follow Surrey's bylaws, including the SSMUH bands and the 40% suite cap in §3. Check the municipality for building questions and the locator for school questions; neither answers the other. https://www.whiterockcity.ca/514/Resources-Documents
- **Q: Does buying fee-simple instead of strata change my catchment?** No. Catchment is address-based and tenure-blind; what changes is the diligence — a fee-simple lot has NO Form B, NO strata and NO strata fees, while a bare-land strata lot takes the strata block in §3. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($900,000, illustration only) the bill is $16,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
