## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **MHPTA pad-rent limits — Act percentages ONLY:** landlords in manufactured home parks can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent once every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; the **2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus a proportional amount** for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees, and the **2027 limit is 2.2% plus the same proportional amount**. **No pad-rent dollar figure is stated in this file.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** a tenant may assign a manufactured home site tenancy agreement, or sublet the site, only with (a) the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed obtained under the Regulation), (b) an order of the director authorizing it, or (c) authorization in the tenancy agreement itself; consent may be withheld only in prescribed circumstances, and under s.28(3) the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting. The consent request runs on RTB-10, Request for Consent to Assign a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement. (Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, s.28, verified against the current Act on BC Laws in this program's batch-12 verification pass.)
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** where a landlord intends to convert all or a significant part of a park to a non-residential use or a residential use other than a manufactured home park, the landlord must have all necessary government permits and approvals in place before issuing notice, must give 12 months' notice covering a full year, and must pay the tenant $20,000 compensation on or before the notice's effective date; the tenant may move out early on at least 10 days' written notice, may dispute the notice within 15 days, may in defined circumstances (home cannot be moved, no site within a reasonable distance, no tax owing on the home) apply for additional compensation of the home's assessed value minus the $20,000, and if the landlord does not take steps toward the stated conversion within a reasonable period the landlord must pay the greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Manufactured Home Registry / CSA labels:** manufactured homes include modular and mobile homes; mobile homes are built under CSA Z-240 and modular homes under CSA A-277; in B.C., the sale, transfer or purchase of a manufactured home is only effective if the transaction is registered, and the Registry maintains the central register of manufactured home ownership details. The manufacturer attaches a CSA label (with a unique identifier, usually near the front door or on the electrical panel); the home may not be sold unless it has the original and legible CSA decal attached; if a label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified — Technical Safety BC has a licensed electrical contractor inspect the home's wiring and an electrical approval label is prepared, usable in lieu of a CSA label to list the home for sale (the official route behind the live page's "silver label" shorthand). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: If I buy a home in an Abbotsford park, do I own land?** No — on a rented pad you own the manufactured home and hold a manufactured home site tenancy under the MHPTA, paying monthly site rent to the park. Land ownership exists only in the own-land forms: fee-simple lots, or a bare-land strata lot where that form exists. (§2.)
- **Q: How much is pad rent in an Abbotsford park?** No figure is stated in this file: no official source publishes Abbotsford pad rents, and the live page's $650–$950/month range and one card's "$782/month" are UNVERIFIED site copy. The figure that governs is the specific site's tenancy agreement — confirm the current rent, what it includes, and its increase history with the park for the exact site. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** Once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' notice, and no more than the yearly limit: 2026 is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 is 2.2% plus the proportional amount. (§3.)
- **Q: Does the park need to approve my purchase on a rented pad?** Yes — assignment of the site tenancy requires the landlord's prior written consent (RTB-10), an order of the director, or authorization in the tenancy agreement itself (MHPTA s.28), and the landlord must not charge anything for considering, investigating or consenting (s.28(3)). (§3.)
- **Q: Are Paradise Park, Georgian Park and Maplewood Estates land I can buy?** No — they are this program's verified 55+ manufactured-home parks (tenure records in the 55-plus sibling), and a park home there sits on a rented pad. Verified as parks is not verified as lot stock: you buy the home and take the site tenancy, not the land. (§2.)
- **Q: Why does the CSA label matter?** A manufactured home may not be sold unless the original legible CSA decal is attached (Z-240 for mobile, A-277 for modular); if it is lost, the home must be re-certified through Technical Safety BC's electrical inspection and approval-label route before it can be listed for sale. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted to another use?** Under MHPTA s.42: all permits in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes and a further payment (greater of $5,000 or 12 months' rent) if the landlord does not proceed within a reasonable period. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT when I buy a park home?** For an own-land purchase, PTT applies on the general tiers (§3 worked example: $800,000 = $14,000, or $6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer). For a home-only purchase on a rented pad, the live page's flat "PTT does not apply" claim is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm with a lawyer or notary for the exact structure before subjects come off. (§3.)
- **Q: Is there an official median price for Abbotsford manufactured homes?** No — SnapStats publishes no manufactured-home category, so no manufactured-home median, inventory or sales figure exists in the sources used. The Abbotsford figures in §1 (detached $949,000; Condos & Townhomes $497,500, September 2026) are all-residential community context only. (Research file above.)
- **Q: The page says most Central Abbotsford and Clearbrook parks are 55+ — is that right?** UNVERIFIED as stated — the research confirms three verified 55+ parks by name (Paradise Park, Georgian Park, Maplewood Estates) but no Abbotsford-wide age-restriction split was confirmed on a park or official source, and the page's "majority" claim is not carried as fact.
