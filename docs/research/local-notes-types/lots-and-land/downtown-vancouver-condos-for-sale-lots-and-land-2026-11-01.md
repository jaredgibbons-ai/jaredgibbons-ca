## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning framework:** DD schedule of By-law No. 3575 (uses per the Downtown District Official Development Plan), plus site-specific CD-1 districts and C-3A districts in parts of the area — there is no house-lot minimum table to quote; the parcel's own district governs. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **SSMUH position:** the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing framework is a house-lot framework — Downtown has no house-lot stock for it to attach to (§2), and no Downtown SSMUH position is documented in the research. Density is set by the DD/CD-1 position of the specific parcel. (Research file above.)
- **Subdivision + servicing — verified City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under Subdivision By-law No. 5208, verified on bylaws.vancouver.ca, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), substituting a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0 (the class a downtown tower parcel would meet), and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Heritage controls:** the City's heritage areas carry regulations for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown that preserve and protect historic fabric and affect renovation, alteration and any redevelopment of a heritage-district parcel from day one. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in it is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata diligence — the operative regime downtown (the BC strata block):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — read it with the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw, and Vancouver licences short-term rentals); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. This same block would apply in full to any bare-land strata lot, if one existed here — none is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no downtown ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. A downtown strata resale is not a vacant-land sale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here, rate 3%:** the City's Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed value for vacant properties; every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026 in the research's reading), and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. (The research file itself left the current rate UNVERIFIED in its pass; the 3% rate is the standing Vancouver position carried in this program's downtown-ve precedent file from the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 — stated here on that basis.) The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate filing; no rate beyond that is stated here. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Additional school tax (documented in this ground's own research):** 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot or a house lot downtown?** No — detached houses are not a Downtown housing form and no bare-land offering of any kind was verified for this page's ground. (§2; condos sibling.)
- **Q: Is this "Downtown West Vancouver"?** No. This is Downtown VANCOUVER, on the downtown peninsula in the City of Vancouver; West Vancouver is a separate municipality across Burrard Inlet with its own bylaws, taxes and market. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: What zoning governs a downtown parcel?** Most of the core sits under the Downtown District (DD) schedule of By-law No. 3575, which permits the uses in the Downtown District Official Development Plan — commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — with CD-1 site-specific districts and C-3A districts in parts of the area. There is no house-lot minimum table to quote. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Q: What subdivision form actually happens downtown?** Airspace and strata-plan work, not lot splits: the 2026 fee schedule's Class V (Air Space) fee is $125,100 where FSR exceeds 3.0, under Subdivision By-law No. 5208 with fees by By-law No. 14538, and major proposals go through the City's major-subdivision preliminary process with a title search and a lawyer's charge summary to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Q: Are there leasehold condos downtown, like False Creek South?** No named leasehold position is imported here — False Creek South's 60-year ground leases are south-shore ground, not this page's peninsula ground. Tenure varies by building and is UNVERIFIED building by building in this feed; confirm Freehold Strata on title for the specific building. (§2.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a downtown purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $1,500,000 → $28,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. (§3.)
- **Q: What extra taxes should a downtown buyer budget for?** The annual Empty Homes Tax declaration on every purchase (3% of assessed value if the home is vacant — Vancouver only), and — where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million — the Province's additional school tax: 0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

- **Q: Which schools serve a downtown address?** By exact address only — Elsie Roy and Crosstown elementaries are located in the area, no public secondary school inside the Downtown local area was confirmed, and no catchment is settled in this file.
