## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: District of West Vancouver — a single district municipality on the North Shore, between the Capilano River and Horseshoe Bay, under its Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- The District's heart, per the OCP: the Ambleside Municipal Town Centre, with the Ambleside Local Area Plan boundary south of Inglewood Avenue between 13th and 23rd Streets; Park Royal, at Marine Drive and Taylor Way on the District's east edge, is recognised in the OCP as the regional shopping centre. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/23apr19.pdf and https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Board/neighbourhood names used for West Vancouver in the master research set: Altamont, Ambleside, Bayridge, British Properties, Canterbury, Caulfeild, Cedardale, Chartwell, Chelsea Park, Cypress, Cypress Park Estates, Dundarave, Sentinel Hill and West Bay. Several are site/listing names not confirmed as official District neighbourhood names — the District's own plan language works through Ambleside, Dundarave and Horseshoe Bay as its village/centre areas; exact Paragon matches are not confirmed. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- Main roads: Marine Drive is the District's east–west main road; the Lions Gate Bridge at its east end connects to downtown Vancouver; Highway 1 (Upper Levels) crosses the upper bench to Horseshoe Bay, the BC Ferries terminal community for Nanaimo, Bowen Island and the Sunshine Coast. Transit: route 250 runs downtown via the Lions Gate Bridge along Marine Drive to Dundarave (some trips to Horseshoe Bay); routes 251, 253, 254 and 255 feed the neighbourhoods into Park Royal exchange. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
- **Tenure boundary on the east edge:** part of the Park Royal area sits on Squamish Nation land (Capilano IR No. 5) under the Park Royal Lease (indenture dated April 1, 1974) — District ground and reserve ground split at that edge, stated precisely in Section 2. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- Schools: School District 45 serves the District; SD45 has no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12) and catchments are address-based through its locator. For the sample address 1329 Duchess Avenue the locator returns Hollyburn Elementary (K–7), 1329 Duchess Avenue, and West Vancouver Secondary (8–12), 1750 Mathers Avenue; Irwin Park Elementary is at 2455 Haywood Avenue. Other addresses are not confirmed. No SD44-style settled secondary chain applies here — SD45 is a different district, and no locator chain beyond the sample above is documented in this file's research. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/ and https://westvancouverschools.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Location-Address.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: West Vancouver has no meaningful bare-land market documented in this file's research. No named bare-land subdivision selling vacant serviced lots, no bare-land strata community, and no presale LOT offering was verified district-wide. What trades as "land" here is the fee-simple single-family lot itself — an existing RS-zone lot, usually with a house on it, bought for the lot, the view or a rebuild — plus development/rezoning proposals documented as proposals only. No lot count, and no lot price, is stated. Sub-area product (condos, townhomes, strata) is documented in its home sub-area files, cross-referenced in the FILE STATUS block — never re-counted here.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and effectively the only land form

- **RS-zone lot fabric:** single-family RS zones under Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 dominate the District. Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record — from RS5 lots in the lower neighbourhoods to RS1 estates on the upper bench and in the British Properties pattern. These are zone minimum site areas, not a claim about any individual parcel. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Suites as lot value:** Section 200 also lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single family dwelling zones — suite potential is a standard feature of the District's detached lot stock, subject to the bylaw's conditions. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Bare-land strata: none verified district-wide.** No bare-land strata community name with an address was confirmed in this file's research or the District-wide siblings: none found. If a bare-land strata lot did surface, the position would be the BC rule stated plainly — a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure (Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply to the lot, Section 3) — but no such stock is documented here.
- **ALR:** ALR status is listed in the research as "none expected, not confirmed" — UNCONFIRMED. No ALR split is stated for the District, and no ALR figure is invented. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Serviced vs unserviced:** septic and well use in the District is UNVERIFIED in the research — no District-wide well/septic statement is made. Servicing for any single lot: confirm with the District — UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel in this file. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/west-vancouver-homes-for-sale.md)
- **Waterfront / foreshore lots:** the foreshore strip from Ambleside through Dundarave to West Bay is the District's premium lot stock, and it carries the Foreshore Development Permit and Flood Construction Level regime on any rebuild (Section 3) — price the permit regime, not just the view. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 (Section 200 RS minimums in Section 2; the zone for a specific lot is on the District's Zoning Map, Bylaw Section 852) and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. White Rock's bylaws (Zoning 2024 No. 2506 / OCP 2017 No. 2220) belong to WHITE ROCK ONLY and are never imported into District of West Vancouver ground. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/bylaws-licensing/find-bylaw/zoning-bylaw-no-4662-2010
- **SSMUH / ground-oriented density, as the District's bylaw states it:** the ground-oriented dwelling rules in Bylaw 4662 state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. District-wide SSMUH unit counts as the District states them: UNVERIFIED in the research — no count is asserted. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** the District describes subdivision as the process of adjusting or realigning an existing property line, or dividing land into two or more parcels, processed as a planning application with engineering review of servicing. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw NUMBER could not be confirmed on a primary source in this pass — held UNCONFIRMED (a fresh District-site search on Nov 2, 2026 returned the Fees and Charges Bylaw No. 5334, 2024 and the Zoning Bylaw, but no subdivision-bylaw number); parcel-level servicing standards are therefore documented from the application process, not a bylaw number. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/other-developments
- **Foreshore / flood / environmental permits:** a Foreshore Development Permit is required for work in the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding) before a building permit; new habitable space must be built to the Flood Construction Level where the property is at risk of coastal flooding; work within 15 metres of the natural boundary (high tide mark) must follow the Foreshore Protection Area guidelines; an Environmental Development Permit is required within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. Form and Character Development Permit guidelines also apply in the Ambleside Apartment Area and Ambleside Village Centre. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits and https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (applies to any strata lot, and would apply in full to a bare-land strata lot if one surfaced):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **ALR rules:** no ALR ground is documented in the District in this research (status UNCONFIRMED, Section 2), so no ALR rule is applied to a District parcel here. The standing ALR rules, stated for completeness only where ALR ground were ever confirmed: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land:** general tiers 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are home-purchase exemptions as BC states them — bare-land eligibility for the newly built home exemption: UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a West Vancouver lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply in West Vancouver, and no West Vancouver vacancy-tax position is documented in this file's research. Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: no position is documented in this file's own cited sources — UNCONFIRMED, and none is conformed from other files (positions diverge across the program).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in West Vancouver?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented: no named vacant-lot subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here means an existing fee-simple RS lot, usually with a house on it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are West Vancouver lots?** By zone minimum: RS1 8,094 m², RS2 1,625 m², RS3 975 m², RS4 731 m², RS5 488 m², RS6 by site of record — minimums under Zoning Bylaw 4662 s.200, not a statement about any one parcel. Check the parcel's zone on the District Zoning Map (Section 852). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I add units to a single-family lot?** The bylaw's ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, and 6 if the site is wholly or partly within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop; secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are also permitted uses in the single-family zones, subject to conditions. Verify the specific lot against both rules. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q: What does a waterfront lot trigger that an inland lot doesn't?** A Foreshore Development Permit before a building permit, Flood Construction Level for new habitable space at coastal-flood risk, and Foreshore Protection Area guidelines within 15 m of the natural boundary. https://www.westvancouver.ca/climate-environment/natural-environment-and-hazard-development-permit-areas/foreshore-development-permits
- **Q: My lot is beside a creek — anything special?** Yes: an Environmental Development Permit is required for work within 15 metres of the top of a watercourse bank. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is the Park Royal-area parcel I'm looking at freehold?** Not necessarily. Part of Park Royal sits on Capilano IR No. 5 under the 1974 Park Royal Lease, where the Squamish Nation is the zoning authority. Read the title before writing — parcel-level tenure is UNCONFIRMED until then. https://partii-partiii.fng.ca/fng-gpn-ii-iii/pii/en/474475/1/document.do
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large bench lot?** Possibly, against the zone minimum site area — but subdivision is a District planning application with engineering servicing review, and a District subdivision-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED in this file. No subdivision promise can be made from this file; confirm the zone and the application requirements with the District. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant West Van lot?** No — the 3% Empty Homes Tax is City of Vancouver only. It does not apply in the District of West Vancouver. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which school serves a lot I'm buying?** Run the exact address in the SD45 locator — catchments are address-based; the only locator result documented here is 1329 Duchess Avenue → Hollyburn Elementary → West Vancouver Secondary. https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
