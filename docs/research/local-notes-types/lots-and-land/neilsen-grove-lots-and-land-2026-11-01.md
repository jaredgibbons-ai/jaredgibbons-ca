- **Bare-land strata block (applies to South Pointe lots — full treatment in the land home file):** Form B first — under Strata Property Act s. 59 the Form B Information Certificate discloses the lot's monthly strata fees, amounts owed, approved special levies still to be paid, the CRF balance, parking/storage and a summary of the strata's insurance, with rules, current budget and most recent depreciation report attached; the strata must provide it within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25¢/page. Since July 1, 2024 every strata with five or more lots must obtain a depreciation report every five years. - **ALR rules (edge parcels only — no ALR parcel is verified inside this sub-area):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
