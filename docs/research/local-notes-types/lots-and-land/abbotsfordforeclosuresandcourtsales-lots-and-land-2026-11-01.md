## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Scope is the whole City of Abbotsford: properties sold under the BC Supreme Court foreclosure process — by court order with conduct of sale given to a party (usually the lender's side), or heading toward a final order of foreclosure. In BC a "court-ordered sale" is a sale inside a foreclosure proceeding, not a separate process; "bank foreclosure" is a misnomer — sales run through the court, not the bank. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Governing rules are provincial: Supreme Court Civil Rules (Rule 21-7 foreclosure; Rule 13-5 court-ordered sales), the Law and Equity Act, and the Land Title Act's order nisi reference. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01
- **Registry:** Abbotsford Registry, 32375 Veterans Way, Abbotsford, BC V2T 0K1, on the BC Courts locations list; Supreme Court scheduling for Abbotsford is handled from that address. Whether a given file is filed there is a per-file check on Court Services Online, which shows registry location per file. https://www.bccourts.ca/supreme_court/locations.htm?location=kamloops and https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Court records: Court Services Online eSearch is open to anyone with Internet access; file details include file number, registry, parties and filed documents; file classes include "H - Foreclosure." It publishes no area statistics. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- Market context, labelled community context ONLY (SnapStats FVR Sep 2026; land/foreclosures have NO SnapStats category): Abbotsford Detached — inventory 440, solds 45, median $949,000, $383/sq ft, 95% sale-to-list, 39 DOM; Abbotsford "Condos & Townhomes" combined — inventory 572, solds 58, median $497,500, $433/sq ft, 97%, 32 DOM. Sub-area texture (all properties, not foreclosures): attached concentrates in Central Abbotsford (256 listed, 35 sold) and Abbotsford West (210, 16); detached splits Abbotsford East (165, 15), Abbotsford West (138, 12), Central Abbotsford (70, 12). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Step 1 — petition:** a foreclosure/redemption proceeding must be started by petition in the Supreme Court — Rule 21-7(1); all persons whose interest is sought to be extinguished must be respondents — Rule 21-7(2); the petitioner must serve the court's foreclosure information notice, Form 125 — Rule 21-7(4.1). Rule 21-7 as read states no demand step before the petition; a prescribed-mortgage-terms demand is UNVERIFIED in this slug's research. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Order / redemption:** the court may order payment of what is due within a redemption period the court fixes — Rule 21-7(5)(b) (no fixed length in the Rules); it may certify the amount due — Rule 21-7(5)(c),(e). In default, a final order of foreclosure may be granted — Rule 21-7(6). A party of record may apply at any time for an order that the property be sold — Rule 21-7(7). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Confirming a sale:** even before redemption expires, the person with conduct of sale may apply for an order confirming the sale, directing proceeds and vesting title — Rule 21-7(9). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Conduct of sale / Form 60 / PD-66 (settled treatment; this slug's research held these UNVERIFIED in its own pass — see FILE STATUS):** conduct of sale and court directions (reserve/minimum price, bid and offer rights, particulars) sit in Rule 13-5 (sale may be by contract conditional on court approval — Rule 13-5(4)(b)); the result of a sale by court order is certified in Form 60 — Rule 13-5(6); Practice Direction PD-66 governs foreclosure proceedings. Bid timing/manner under PD-66 for a specific file: get the conduct-of-sale party's written instructions for that file. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo106/loo106/168_2009_02

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How does a BC foreclosure start?** By petition under Rule 21-7(1), with all interest-holders as respondents and Form 125 served with the petition. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: How long is the redemption period?** No fixed length — the period the court fixes under Rule 21-7(5)(b). https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Is my accepted offer final?** No — conduct of sale applies under Rule 21-7(9) for confirmation, proceeds direction and vesting, even before redemption expires; until that order, the sale is not final. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
- **Q: Can someone outbid me at approval?** Treat it as a real possibility: bid/offer rights sit in the court's Rule 13-5 directions and PD-66 practice — get the specific file's bid instructions in writing before spending on inspections. (§3.)
- **Q: Where do I check an Abbotsford court file?** Court Services Online eSearch (class "H - Foreclosure") shows file number, registry, parties and documents; Abbotsford Registry is at 32375 Veterans Way for in-person inquiries. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Is every Abbotsford file filed at the Abbotsford registry?** UNVERIFIED per file — the registry exists in the city (verified), but the filing rule was not re-verified in the research; check the registry shown on the individual file. https://justice.gov.bc.ca/cso/help/faq.do
- **Q: Will I get a Form B on a bare-land strata lot in foreclosure?** UNCONFIRMED — no source in the research states a general rule; what is produced is a per-file fact. (§3.)
- **Q: Do I pay PTT/GST the normal way on court-ordered land?** UNCONFIRMED — court-sale tax treatment is not stated in any source in the research; the general PTT and GI-003 frames in §3 are starting frames only, confirmed by your lawyer per transaction.
- **Q: Are foreclosures discounted in Abbotsford?** No foreclosure discount statistic exists in any official source — UNVERIFIED. General-market context only: detached 39 DOM at 95% sale-to-list (Sep 2026) says nothing about any individual court sale. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: As a seller in difficulty, what is my window?** The court-fixed redemption period is the window to pay what is due or sell before a final order can be granted under Rule 21-7(6) — earlier advice is worth more. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/lc/statreg/168_2009_03
