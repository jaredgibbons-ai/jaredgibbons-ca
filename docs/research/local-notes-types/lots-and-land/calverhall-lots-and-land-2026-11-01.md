## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Calverhall — stated plainly at the top. The area trades as established fee-simple City lots whose value now turns on the December 2025 Ground Oriented (GO) Zone density and the lot's OCP designation, with no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering verified on any official or developer source.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only) form

- **Established City lots:** detached houses on City residential lots under Zoning Bylaw No. 6700, with ground-oriented forms (houses with suites, duplexes, multiplexes and coach houses) permitted under the GO Zone on the side streets — predominantly freehold detached in the sources read; the strata/freehold split by street is UNVERIFIED. No named condo buildings or townhome complexes in Calverhall are confirmed on an official or developer source. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone, as stated:** GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m for a corner lot) and 15.0 m in other designations; older bylaw minimum lot areas include 464.5 m² in RS-2 and 334.4 m² in RS-3. These are bylaw minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and designation on a single lot are confirmed through the City. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Density as the City states it (the land-value fact here):** a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. The City adopted the GO Zone on December 8, 2025, increasing permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties City-wide and allowing up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses, as the City states them:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Serviced position:** City utility fee amounts for a Calverhall home: UNVERIFIED in the research (no single-family utility total was read on a City source); parcel-level septic or well status: UNVERIFIED. Confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City. https://www.cnv.org/home-property/property-taxes
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** parcel-level ALR status: UNVERIFIED in the research. No ALR percentage split is stated; check any parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Calverhall in the City or the District?** The City of North Vancouver — the City's stormwater plan names "the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods" as City neighbourhoods, and City zoning (Bylaw No. 6700), City OCP (No. 8400) and City tax rates apply, not the District's. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Living-City/Integrated-Stormwater-Management-Plan.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Calverhall?** No verified one — the area is established City houses, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. What trades is the lot under an existing house, priced on its GO Zone density. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How many units can a Calverhall lot hold?** Under the GO Zone (adopted December 8, 2025): up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, up to 4 units on a larger lot in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1, and up to 6 units in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Q: How narrow can a subdivided lot be?** GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 (10.0 m for a corner lot) and 15.0 m in other designations; older minimum lot areas include 464.5 m² (RS-2) and 334.4 m² (RS-3). https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Q: Can a lot carry both a suite and a coach house?** Yes, where it qualifies — one suite per single-family property (owner must reside at the property; suites legal City-wide since 1993), combinable with an accessory coach house where the front lot line is at least 10 m, with a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Q: Which elementary school will my child attend?** UNVERIFIED at address level — the new 585-seat Cloverley Elementary opened in September 2026 and its catchment review potentially moves Brooksbank, Queensbury, Queen Mary and Ridgeway addresses; Brooksbank Elementary (980 13th Street East) and Sutherland Secondary (1860 Sutherland Avenue) are beside the area but neither is confirmed as catchment. Check the exact address with SD44 and do not promise a school from an old listing. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2026INF0050-001056
- **Q: What permit does a creek-side lot need?** A streamside development permit — the permit area covers land within 15 m of a watercourse bank top or 10 m of a ravine bank top, and a creek channel runs from the Calverhall – Cloverly neighbourhoods on the City stormwater plan. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Q: What were 2026 City taxes?** Due July 2, 2026; total residential rate 3.34028 per $1,000 plus storm drainage 0.10697 per $1,000 — the City's own illustration is $4,790 total on an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What transit serves the area?** Route 232 via Brooksbank and Keith and route 255 via Grand Boulevard East and Keith are the local buses; the SeaBus from Lonsdale Quay crosses to Waterfront Station in 12 minutes. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt232.pdf
