## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule:** minimum site areas and unit forms in §2 (306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² ladder; multiplex to 6 units, 8 rental; secondary suite + laneway house with a single detached house). - **Plans:** the citywide Vancouver Plan applies, and the western edge of Hastings-Sunrise is included in the Grandview-Woodland Community Plan area — plan policy layers on top of base zoning there. A separate community plan named only for Hastings-Sunrise: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/hastings-sunrise.aspx
- **Subdivision — Vancouver Subdivision By-law No. 5208** (standing program frame; subdivision fees under Bylaw 14538). Subdivision potential runs off the parcel's district schedule minimums (§2) and the approving officer's requirements — confirm for the specific parcel with the City.
- **SSMUH in Vancouver form:** the R1-1 schedule in §2 IS Vancouver's small-scale framework for these streets (multiplexes, suite, laneway in the forms stated) — parcel-specific, not a neighbourhood claim.
- **Floodplain standards:** land in the designated floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — the live check for shoreline-adjacent lots. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Hastings bare-land strata stock is verified):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-yr cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is "Hastings" the same as Hastings-Sunrise — and is it in Burnaby?** Neither exactly: Hastings-Sunrise is Vancouver's official local area at the corridor's east end and the Paragon name "Hastings" is anchored on it here; Burnaby's Hastings is a separate municipality east across Boundary Road. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Hastings-Sunrise?** No verified one — the area is established fee-simple houses; no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (§2.)
- **Q: What can a house-street lot carry?** Under R1-1: a single detached house with secondary suite and laneway house, a duplex, or a multiplex to 6 units (8 rental), subject to the 306–557 m² minimum site ladder. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Is Eton Crest a verified strata land purchase?** No — it is held UNCONFIRMED: townhome form is verified, but no citable source states its title/tenure, so this file does not count it as verified stock. Read a Form B or title record to settle it. (FILE STATUS; §2.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant Hastings lot I hold?** Vancouver's EHT is 3% and owners must file the annual declaration (due Feb 3, 2026) — confirm how the declaration classes apply to the specific parcel with the City. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: What flood check matters on a north-slope lot?** Whether the parcel is in the City's designated floodplain — Building By-law setbacks and flood construction levels apply to parcels in it; the Burrard Inlet shoreline is the part to check. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** Three elementaries and Templeton Secondary are located in the area, but catchments are address-based and the sample-address locator result is UNVERIFIED — run every address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: How do I reach downtown or SFU without a SkyTrain station in the area?** R5 RapidBus on Hastings — west to Burrard Station, east to SFU Exchange, with Nanaimo/Renfrew/Kootenay stops in the area. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/ttR5.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided Vancouver lot from a developer?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: When are Vancouver taxes due?** Advance Feb 3, 2026 and main July 3, 2026, each with a 5% late penalty on the unpaid balance. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
