## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Kitsilano has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no named bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source in the research or the siblings. Land here trades as individual fee-simple lots on the interior streets — almost always with a character house, duplex or conversion dwelling on them — where the priced contest is retention under the RT schedules versus multiplex value under R1-1, plus a plan-expectation premium on the Broadway edge. Everything named below is built product or a plan/rezoning fact — never a lot for sale.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Interior-street lots:** single detached houses, duplexes and multiple dwellings under R1-1, RT-7 and RT-8, with strata apartment stock along the West 4th Avenue, Cornwall and Broadway edges. The RT schedules' stated intent is to encourage retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the historical character of the area — the defining land fact in Kitsilano. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The strata-versus-freehold split by street is UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-rt-7.pdf
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** the RT-8 schedule states a minimum site area of 306 m² for a duplex; R1-1 minimums run 306 m² (3–4-unit multiple dwellings) through 464 m² (5 units) to 557 m² (6 or more units), with secondary suites and laneway houses in the forms the schedules state. These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are confirmed parcel by parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Kitsilano homes are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Kitsilano house confirmed on a City source is UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR land in Kitsilano is UNVERIFIED in the research — no ALR parcel is confirmed and no split is stated. (Research file above.)
- **Shoreline position:** the City's Coastal Flood Risk Assessment covers a Point Grey–Kitsilano study area — the shoreline from Point Grey to Kitsilano Point faces the Strait of Georgia, is exposed to waves from the northwest, flood protection is limited and riprap west of Kitsilano Beach is discontinuous; the Province advises planning for 0.5 m of sea level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100. Beach-block lot value carries this file with it; parcel-level flood status is UNVERIFIED. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, RT-7 / RT-8 and R1-1 District Schedules:** the RT areas sit under RT-7 and RT-8 — RT-8 states a 306 m² minimum site area for a duplex, and the schedules' intent is retention and renovation of buildings consistent with the area's historical character; house lots elsewhere sit under R1-1 (306 m² for 3–4-unit multiple dwellings, 464 m² for 5 units, 557 m² for 6 or more units, with secondary suites and laneway houses as the schedules state). Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED — confirm the exact district schedule and any character-house rules before pricing a lot on multiplex value. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Plan — the Broadway Plan:** covers 1st to 16th Avenue from Vine Street to Clark Drive, including parts of Kitsilano, tied to the Broadway Subway — the land-use frame for the area's south edge only; the beach blocks north of 1st Avenue are outside it. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fresh check, Nov 2, 2026):** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). The small bare-land strata splits seen in this area (Section 2) run through the strata-subdivision provisions of that framework plus the district schedule — they are discretionary and slow, not a by-right lot split. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework here is the district schedules themselves — R1-1 multiplex / suite / laneway forms by lot size, and RT-7 / RT-8 two-family and multiple-dwelling forms shaped by the character-retention intent — so the "SSMUH question" on a Kitsilano lot is a schedule-and-character check, not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Coastal flood diligence:** the Point Grey–Kitsilano shoreline is exposed to northwest waves with limited, discontinuous flood protection, and provincial guidance plans for 0.5 m of sea-level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100 — get the parcel-level flood and insurance picture early on any beach-side lot. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **Bare-land strata / strata rules (the BC strata block — applies in full to a bare-land strata lot like the West 14th Avenue form in Section 2, and to every strata lot in the area):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a two-lot bare-land strata sits under that threshold — its diligence is the registered bylaws, the strata plan and title, not a depreciation report); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Kitsilano ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted in this program only where the horse-properties research documents it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Kitsilano lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, the 2025 tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor-held lots. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Speculation tax + purchase prohibition:** no Kitsilano-specific speculation-tax position is documented in this file's research beyond the City tax above — none imported. The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for Kitsilano land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for Kitsilano, and the position is not conformed from other files. No False Creek South lease position is stated for any Kitsilano lot (see the FILE STATUS block).
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a new lot subdivision in Kitsilano?** No verified one — the area is fully built out. Land trades as house lots with buildings on them, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Should I price a Kitsilano lot as a teardown or as a character house?** Check the district schedule first: the RT-7 / RT-8 schedules state an intent to retain buildings consistent with the area's historical character, so a lot's multiplex value under R1-1 (306 / 464 / 557 m² minimums by unit count) is only real where the schedule and character rules actually allow it. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/rt-8.pdf
- **Q: What is a "bare land strata lot" in Kitsilano, in practice?** At its documented scale here, a heritage-house split: one listing describes a West 14th Avenue property as a bare-land strata lot, one of two, holding a heritage-style house with suites. You would own a strata lot of land with the house on it — strata fees, bylaws and a Form B — in a two-lot corporation with no depreciation-report obligation (under the 5-lot threshold). It is not a fee-simple lot and not a subdivision template. https://vancouvernashdom.com/vancouver-west-kitsilano-houses/SearchResults.form/?_pg=2
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan change what my lot is worth?** Only if the lot is inside the Plan — 1st to 16th Avenue, Vine Street to Clark Drive, at the area's south edge, tied to the Broadway Subway. A beach-block lot north of 1st Avenue carries none of those expectations, even though both are "Kitsilano." https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: What shoreline check does a beach-block lot need?** The City's coastal assessment describes the Point Grey–Kitsilano shoreline as exposed to northwest waves with limited flood protection and discontinuous riprap west of Kitsilano Beach, with provincial planning figures of 0.5 m sea-level rise by 2050 and 1 m by 2100 — get the parcel-level flood picture and an insurance quote before subject removal on any unit north of 4th Avenue. http://vancouver.ca/files/cov/CFRA-Phase-1-Final_Report.pdf
- **Q: Is any Kitsilano land leasehold — like False Creek?** None is documented in this file's research. Kitsilano's documented tenures are fee-simple lots and strata; the False Creek South ground-lease position belongs to a different shore and is not imported into this file. If a specific listing claims leasehold, verify it on title — it would be an exception needing its own evidence. (FILE STATUS block; research file above.)
- **Q: Can I split a Kitsilano lot into two?** A fee-simple split must clear the district schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block through the Approving Officer; the bare-land strata route seen on West 14th Avenue is a strata subdivision, discretionary and slow — neither is a by-right entitlement priced off a neighbour's result. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Will the Empty Homes Tax catch a lot I hold as a second-home property?** It can — the tax is 3% on homes declared vacant, every owner files an annual declaration, and the research flags it as directly relevant to Kitsilano's second-home and investor stock. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Kitsilano lot?** By exact address only — four elementaries and Kitsilano Secondary sit inside the area, and the one settled locator chain in this file (3505 West 7th Avenue → Bayview Elementary → Kitsilano Secondary) confirms only that address. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
