## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- South Granville is the City of Vancouver's Granville Street corridor roughly between West 5th and West 16th Avenues, spanning the Fairview and Shaughnessy local areas; the board sub-area name is "South Granville," with nearby areas Fairview, Shaughnessy, Kitsilano (across the Granville Bridge side via Broadway), Arbutus Ridge, Douglas Park, and downtown Vancouver across False Creek. Alternate names in the research: "South Granville Rise," "Gallery Row" (the art gallery stretch), and "Fairview Slopes" (the residential slopes east of Granville). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/south-granville.md)
- The South Granville Business Improvement Association represents the commercial district along Granville Street from the Granville Bridge to 16th Avenue; the strip is branded "Gallery Row" for its concentration of art galleries, alongside fashion and home-décor shops and the Meinhardt Fine Foods grocery. http://www.southgranville.org/
- Culture and health anchors: the Stanley Theatre (2750 Granville Street at 12th Avenue) is a 650-seat performing-arts venue in an art deco building that opened in 1931; Vancouver General Hospital (899 West 12th Avenue) sits a few blocks east of the corridor and is BC's referral centre for many provincial programs. https://artsclub.com/about/venue-rentals/stanley-industrial-alliance-stage and https://www.vch.ca/en/location-service/echocardiography-vancouver-general-hospital
- Parks: Granville Park (3001 Fir Street) is the corridor's main neighbourhood park, and Granville Loop Park (1435 West 5th Avenue) sits at the corridor's north end by the Granville Bridge. https://covapp.vancouver.ca/ParkFinder/parkdetail.aspx?inparkid=44
- Schools: public schools are run by the Vancouver School Board (SD39); L'École Bilingue Elementary (1166 West 14th Avenue) is a K–7 single-track French Immersion school placed by the VSB's schools-by-neighbourhood list in Fairview. Catchments are address-based: the one locator run documented in the research — for the single sample address 1166 West 14th Avenue only — returns Emily Carr Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary as that address's regular-program catchment (not L'École Bilingue, the school located at that address); all other corridor catchments are UNVERIFIED. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
- Transit position: the Broadway Subway — the 5.7 km Millennium Line extension with six new underground stations including South Granville Station — is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027; until it opens, the corridor is bus-served, including the 99 B-Line along Broadway. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048 and https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/rapid-transit-projects/broadway-subway-project
- Market figures: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage — the September 2026 GVR report contains no City of Vancouver community sections at all. All market figures: UNVERIFIED. (Research file above.)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plan framework — Broadway Plan:** Council approved the Broadway Plan on June 22, 2022 — a 30-year framework for the area between Clark Drive and Vine Street and 1st to 16th Avenues, integrating housing, jobs and amenities with the Broadway Subway — with updates approved December 12, 2024 alongside the Broadway Public Realm and Streetscape Plan and the Civic District Plan. The plan provides for a South Granville station area with buildings up to 30–40 storeys near the station. It is a policy framework: most higher-density forms still require rezoning — do not price a lot as if the tower density were already zoned. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx and https://www.shapeyourcity.ca/broadway-plan?page=2
- **Citywide direction — Vancouver Official Development Plan:** the Vancouver Plan (approved July 22, 2022) was rescinded and replaced by the Vancouver Official Development Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Zoning / SSMUH — R1-1 multiplex district (By-law No. 13817):** the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 was amended to replace the old RS districts with R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units (or 8 secured rental units) on former single-family lots, subject to the district schedule's site standards; heritage-designated and First Shaughnessy properties carry additional rules, and a heritage or character-home retention incentive may apply — confirm before marketing "development potential." https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision in Vancouver is governed by the City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208, with subdivision fees under By-law No. 14538 (verified ledger position for Vancouver). Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City; no corridor-specific servicing figure is stated in the research. (Standing verified ledger; research file above.)
- **Heritage fabric on the corridor:** the corridor's South Granville apartment district holds heritage apartment buildings listed on the City of Vancouver Heritage Register at the Granville Street corners — Douglas Lodge (1507–1509 West 12th Avenue, also listed as 2799 Granville Street) and Crescent Court (1504–1512 West 14th Avenue, also listed as 3003–3007 Granville Street) — with Canterbury (1390 West 14th Avenue) also register-listed a block east of Granville. Register status belongs in any frontage-parcel diligence. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-vancouver-heritage-register.pdf
- **Renter-protection frame for rental-land purchases:** purpose-built rental buildings on the corridor carry tenant-relocation implications under the Broadway Plan's renter protections — verify current tenant status and the plan's protections before an investor purchase of a rental landholding. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Flood position:** parcel-level floodplain / flood-construction-level status: UNVERIFIED in this file's research — confirm per property with the City of Vancouver. No figure is imported from another Vancouver file. (Research file above.)
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no South Granville ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat line is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — Vancouver only:** 3% of the property's assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant (2025 reference year; payment due April 16, 2026). It applies in South Granville — a held-empty house-lot is the land-buyer exposure. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a South Granville lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for this area, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in South Granville?** No verified bare-land offering exists — and the corridor's land record is held once, in the granville lots file, which reaches the same honest-zero position. Land here trades as built house lots, frontage parcels with Broadway Plan policy, or whole rental buildings. (Section 2; ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/granville-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Does the Broadway Plan mean my lot is zoned for a tower?** No — the plan is a policy framework (approved June 22, 2022, updated December 2024) contemplating up to 30–40 storeys near the future South Granville Station; most higher-density forms still require rezoning. Price on policy support, not as-built density. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: What can I build on a detached side-street lot?** Under the R1-1 district (By-law No. 13817), up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units, subject to the district schedule — with additional rules for heritage-designated and First Shaughnessy properties. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Q: When does the subway arrive?** The Broadway Subway, including South Granville Station, is scheduled to enter service in fall 2027; until then the corridor is bus-served, including the 99 B-Line on Broadway. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025TT0110-001048
- **Q: Which bylaw governs subdividing a Vancouver lot?** The City's Subdivision By-law No. 5208 (fees under By-law No. 14538); conditions are confirmed with the City for the parcel. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Is a Granville-frontage parcel affected by heritage rules?** It can be — heritage apartment buildings on the corridor are listed on the City of Vancouver Heritage Register (Douglas Lodge; Crescent Court; Canterbury a block east), and register status belongs in frontage diligence. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/policy-vancouver-heritage-register.pdf
- **Q: If I buy a rental apartment building as a land play, what else applies?** The Broadway Plan's renter protections and tenant-relocation implications — verify current tenant status before an investor purchase. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/broadway-plan-approved-by-council.aspx
- **Q: Will the Empty Homes Tax hit a house-lot I hold empty?** The City charges 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant — Vancouver-only, and it applies on this corridor. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a South Granville address?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in the research (1166 West 14th Avenue → Emily Carr Elementary / Eric Hamber Secondary, regular program) does not transfer, and the French Immersion school located at that address (L'École Bilingue) is not that address's regular catchment. https://vsb.bc.ca/list-of-vsb-schools-by-city-neighborhoods
