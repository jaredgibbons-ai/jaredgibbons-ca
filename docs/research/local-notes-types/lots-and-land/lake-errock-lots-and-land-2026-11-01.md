- **Bare-land strata block (for a Tapadera-type lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Tapadera's own site states no age rule, per the townhomes sibling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $500,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $300,000 ($6,000) = **$8,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Rural property taxes:** a property not in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion unpaid on October 31. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Lake Errock part of Mission?** No — for mailing and board reporting it carries a Mission address, but it is an unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area C community; the FVRD's Area C OCP page names Lake Errock among the communities in its plan area, and the City of Mission does not govern it. https://haveyoursay.fvrd.ca/areacocp?sort_by=recent&tool=forum_topic
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant building lot here?** Building lots do appear in the feed (the Malcolm Road lots in §2), but no named fee-simple subdivision selling serviced lots was verified — each lot is checked parcel by parcel: its perc/geotech/riparian reports, its OCP designation and permit areas with the FVRD, and its well/septic position. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot at Tapadera Estates?** Strata tenure on the lot itself (Strata Plan LMS648) — you own the lot as a strata lot, pay strata fees for the common property (clubhouse, pool, river access on the strata's own site), and take a Form B; an RV, park model or manufactured home on the lot does not change the tenure. It is not fee-simple. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Eagle Point Estates fee-simple?** No — the verified listing record states Title to Land: Freehold Strata, single-family form. The houses are detached; the tenure is strata. Confirm fees, bylaws and restrictions on the Form B. (§2 above)
- **Q: Who do I pay property tax to?** The Province: rural-area properties receive their notice from the Surveyor of Taxes Office; taxes are due July 2, with 5% on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a coach house or second dwelling?** UNCONFIRMED as a general rule for this ground — no FVRD position is documented in the research, a listing's "R-2 allows a coach house" label is not verification, and ALR parcels (if the lot is in the reserve) are capped by the ALC frame in §3. Confirm with the FVRD for the PID before pricing a second dwelling.
- **Q: Is my lot in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address — a listing card's "Out of the ALR" line is not verification. If the lot IS in the reserve, subdivision needs ALC approval and residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($500,000, illustration only) the bill is $8,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Where do Lake Errock children go to school?** There is no school in Lake Errock on the confirmed record; the FVRD notice ties the Lake Errock portion of Area C to SD75 trustee elections, whose nearest confirmed schools are Dewdney and Deroche elementaries — but the catchment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I rely on "waterfront" and dock language in a listing?** No — the lake's boating restrictions and public access are UNVERIFIED on an official page, and frontage/dock tenure is a title and FVRD/Province confirmation, not listing text. (§2 above)
