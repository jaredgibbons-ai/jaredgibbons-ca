## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: East Delta land is ALR farm-belt land — fee-simple farm parcels under the A1 Agriculture zone, bought and sold as farms and rural holdings, not as a bare-land subdivision market. No verified bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale LOT offering is named in the research, and none is asserted here. The one named project on the urban edge, Skye at Holly Park, is a townhome project (homes, not lots) counted once in its Holly townhomes home file and cross-referenced below as an urban-edge development only.**

### Fee-simple farm parcels — the default (and effectively only) form

- Most East Delta land is farm or rural freehold rather than strata, mainly under A1 Agriculture zoning (one farm house per lot). Existing farm parcels vary in size and are confirmed parcel by parcel; no "typical" East Delta parcel size is published by any official source: UNCONFIRMED. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Subdivision math that governs any new lot: in the A1 zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy. A2 (s. 10.20.6) likewise sets an 8 ha minimum lot area created by subdivision; A3 (s. 10.30.6) does not permit subdivision. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A2.pdf
- Residential footprint on a farm parcel: one farm house per lot, farm house maximum 500 m², farm home plate maximum 3,600 m², one secondary suite inside the farm house, and one accessory farm dwelling unit of maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on lots over 40 ha (A1 zone details as the research states them). https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- Urban edge: East Ladner parcels take the RS and RD zones in the Ladner ladder, assigned parcel by parcel on the City's zoning maps — the zone on a specific edge parcel is a City-map read, not a sub-area assumption. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- Servicing: farm properties not connected to a community sewer use onsite sewage systems — Fraser Health requires a building with indoor plumbing that is not connected to a community sewer to use an onsite system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person; a property's well is searched in the provincial GWELLS registry, noting wells built before the Water Sustainability Act came into force may not be in the registry. Per-parcel water/sewer status: UNCONFIRMED until checked. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future".** A single consolidated plan with NO lettered schedules; the East Ladner Area Plan was Schedule B.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, and farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" under the municipal-wide agricultural policies (OCP s. 2.6). Any page attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged not settled.** City zone sheets (including the A1 sheet) cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, parcel by parcel; an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled here — the ruling is Jared's. The parcel's own zone sheet is always the read. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH.** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024, allowing combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies. SSMUH is added-unit potential on an existing lot, not a subdivision right — and it does not displace the ALR / A-zone subdivision rules in §2 on farm ground. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Subdivision and servicing — process, adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED.** A City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015, made under s. 506 of the Local Government Act to regulate works and services on subdivision — highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services. Final adoption status of Bylaw 8288: HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program — document the process (City subdivision application, servicing to the standard the City confirms for the file) and confirm the governing bylaw with the City before pricing a subdivision. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Streamside / flood diligence.** All Delta properties within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area; the City requires checks for flood protection requirements and the Riparian Areas Regulation before development near regulated water bodies, and is raising sections of the Boundary Bay dike between 72 Street and 88 Street with provincial grant funding. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf and https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **ALR block (this file's core rule set).** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified — strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less total floor area; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure.** Gross annual farm income required: $2,500 on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha. Applications are due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **PTT:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. PTT is on fair market value at registration. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions.** First-time buyers: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000), full exemption at fair market value $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — it is a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility beyond the documented vacant-land refund route is UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1,000,000, maximum $50,000, phasing out to $1,500,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (background only — no strata land verified here, §2):** Form B within 7 days of request, maximum $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Speculation and vacancy taxes — Delta's actual position.** The City of Delta IS a designated taxable area for the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax, so owners of residential-class property in Delta must complete the annual declaration. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is a City of Vancouver tax ONLY and does not apply in Delta. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no position beyond this file's own cited sources is stated — the research cites no prohibition source for East Delta, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed.
- **Delta property taxes and holding cost.** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is East Delta in North Delta or Ladner?** Ladner — South Delta. The research file places East Delta on the Ladner farm-belt side (the rural farm land and East Ladner urban edge east of the Ladner village core); it is a Paragon sub-area name, not an official City neighbourhood, and no North Delta figure applies to it. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel here?** Only within the A-zone / ALR frame: a new A1 lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable (home site severance excepted under ALC policy); A3 does not permit subdivision at all. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a second house on an ALR parcel?** Within the caps only: principal residence 500 m² or less, a suite only inside the principal residence, and one additional residence of 90 m² or less (40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (over 40 ha) — anything beyond needs ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can an ALR parcel be turned into bare-land strata lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified; strata plans cause subdivision in the ALR (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). (§3.)
- **Q: Is a farm parcel on city water and sewer?** Often not — unconnected buildings use onsite sewage systems under the Sewerage System Regulation (Authorized Person design/install), and wells are searched in GWELLS, with pre-Water Sustainability Act wells possibly unrecorded. Confirm per parcel. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: What farm income keeps farm class?** The three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" figure is wrong. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Is GST due when I buy vacant farm land?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt. The specific sale is a facts question — finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a farm address here?** SD37 School Locator by exact address (K–7 / 8–12, no middle tier) — no sample catchment was run for East Delta in the research, so none is stated: UNVERIFIED until the address is run. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
- **Q: Is flood risk a diligence item on the farm belt?** Yes — the City requires flood-protection and streamside checks before development near regulated water bodies, any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is in the Streamside Protection DPA, and Boundary Bay dike sections between 72 Street and 88 Street are being raised. https://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-receives-provincial-grant-boundary-bay-dike
- **Q: What is the Skye at Holly Park site in land terms?** A townhome project on the urban edge (4760 60B Street, developer source only) — its homes are counted in the Holly townhomes file; it is not a lot offering and no lot in it is counted here. https://skyehomes.ca/
