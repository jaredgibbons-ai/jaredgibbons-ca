## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900:** it directs 75–90% of growth to four centres and preserves established low-density neighbourhoods; Norgate's nearest growth node is the Lions Gate Village Centre (above). The City of North Vancouver's OCP Bylaw 8400 does not apply here and is never cited for this ground. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/ocp-implementation-summary.pdf
- **Zoning — District Zoning Bylaw 3210:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary; December 2023 changes let a secondary suite be up to 49% of the house with no minimum size and raised the maximum coach house height to two storeys. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential, as the District states it:** the District's GEOweb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop. Bill 25 (November 2025) requires full SSMUH density by June 30, 2026, but a March 2, 2026 District Council motion asked staff to pursue exempting the Lions Gate area — which includes Norgate — from SSMUH. Verify the specific lot's classification on GEOweb before pricing any density; do not price a Norgate lot on the 6-unit line. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf and http://docs.dnv.org/documents/8-4-detached-residential.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of North Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented. The documented process instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's zoning and SSMUH classification (above), servicing runs on the District's 2026 single-family utility structure (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; combined $2,571), and Schedule B hazard permit areas (below) gate creek-side and slope work.
