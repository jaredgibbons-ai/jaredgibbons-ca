## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815):** adopted Jun 9, 2026 and in effect Jul 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742), which is never cited as current for Burnaby. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024. The village's own strata parcels are governed by their strata plans, not by the R1 house-lot rules. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan:** adopted Dec 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP; it takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. The Greentree Village Community Plan (1972) is the village's own historic plan document (Section 2) and is read under the 2050 OCP today. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://burnaby.widen.net/s/nkpx5fd25q/greentree-village-community-plan-1972
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (surrounding fee-simple house lots only, NOT the strata townhouse parcels):** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and a minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — Burnaby servicing-bylaw base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** the former servicing base number was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no confirmed current base number is quoted in this file; the process is documented instead: the village itself was subdivided under its 1972 plan (Section 2), any new subdivision potential runs off the parcel's current zoning, and servicing is City water and sewer under the utility frame in Section 2. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City of Burnaby for the specific parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Strata block (applies to the village's building-strata phases; no bare-land strata is verified here):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — each Greentree phase is its own strata corporation with its own Form B, CRF and depreciation report; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw.
