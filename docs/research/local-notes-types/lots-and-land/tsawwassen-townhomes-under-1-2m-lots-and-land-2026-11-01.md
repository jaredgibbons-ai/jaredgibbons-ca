## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Delta plan (Delta side only):** the Delta Official Community Plan 2024, Bylaw No. 8400, is the City's adopted consolidated OCP covering Tsawwassen — it contains NO area-plan schedules (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950, superseded), and its Development Permit Areas live in Appendix B. "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an error wherever met. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Tsawwassen Lands side:** the Delta OCP and Delta zoning do not govern Tsawwassen Lands — Tsawwassen First Nation is self-governing with jurisdiction over land management there, and tax administration for homes on Tsawwassen Lands runs under Tsawwassen First Nation, not the City. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/natural-resource-stewardship/consulting-with-first-nations/agreements/tfn_tripartite-implementation_report_2010-11_eng.pdf
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** City pages cite "Delta Zoning Bylaw No. 7600, 2017", while an internal consolidated title in this program says "8400, 2025" — the collision is flagged, neither number is asserted as settled, and Delta-side zoning is confirmed parcel-by-parcel with the City. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/tsawwassen-townhomes-under-1-2m-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH:** Delta Bylaws No. 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots on the Delta side. This does not change a strata townhome's own lot, and SSMUH does not apply on Tsawwassen Lands. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — process, adoption HELD UNCONFIRMED:** a City council report (January 24, 2024) presents the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 to repeal and replace Bylaw No. 7162, 2015 (Local Government Act s. 506). Final adoption status: UNCONFIRMED in this file (sibling lots files assert "8288, 2024" — conflict noted, not asserted here). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **Flood / streamside frame:** the City of Delta operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure; all properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Delta property taxes (Delta side):** Delta's 2026 due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on residential value over $3,000,000 (never reached in this band). Worked math across the band in §2. **First-time buyers' program:** full exemption requires fair market value $835,000 or less, partial exemption above $835,000 and below $860,000 — only the lower slice of this band can use it at all, and all the program's other conditions also apply. Newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED — and no GST position specific to a Tsawwassen Lands leasehold purchase is stated in this slug's research, so none is asserted here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block (Delta-side strata and any bare-land strata parcel):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. BC strata disputes can go to the Civil Resolution Tribunal. On a Tsawwassen Lands leasehold purchase there is no Form B route that substitutes for the lease documents — the diligence set is the head lease and sublease terms, the remaining term, and how Tsawwassen First Nation tax administration works for that home. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/resolving-disputes/the-civil-resolution-tribunal
- **ALR block (farm-edge parcels):** ALR subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps 500 m² / suite-in-principal / 90 m² ≤40 ha / 186 m² >40 ha; farm class three tiers: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha), $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha, $10,000 (<0.8 ha), by Oct 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
