## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Tsawwassen East" is a Paragon (FVREB MLS®) sub-area name, not an official City of Delta neighbourhood name, and the City publishes no boundary for it; it is the Boundary Bay side of Tsawwassen, east of 56 Street, taking in Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore, under the Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024). https://delta.civicweb.net/document/215646/ and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- Landmarks are Boundary Bay Regional Park and Centennial Beach, 570 Boundary Bay Rd (Metro Vancouver) — Dyke Trail, Savannah and Raptor trails, Cammidge House — and Diefenbaker Park, 5579 1 Ave, on the south side. Local roads are Boundary Bay Road, 12 Avenue and 56 Street; Highway 17 is reached via 56 Street or 52 Street, then Highway 17A and Highway 99 via the George Massey Tunnel. Route 601 runs Boundary Bay trips via 12 Avenue, Boundary Bay Road, 3 Avenue and 67 Street to 1A Avenue, continuing via South Delta Exchange and Ladner Exchange to Bridgeport Station. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/boundary-bay-regional-park and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt601.pdf
- Where land actually sits, in two systems: (a) the large fee-simple waterfront/detached lots of Beach Grove and the Boundary Bay shore, and (b) the Southlands master-planned lands at the south end — Market Avenue and Boundary Bay Road — where a former farm-designated parcel is being built out in named phases (Section 2). The 56 Street / 16 Avenue corridor between them is built apartment-condo stock, not land. (Section 2 below)
- Schools for the sample address 570 Boundary Bay Rd, from the SD37 School Locator: South Park Elementary (K–7), 735 Gilchrist Dr., and South Delta Secondary (8–12), 750 53 St; catchments are address-based, other addresses UNVERIFIED. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Position, stated at the top: Tsawwassen East is the one Delta guide area in this set with a real, named land story — Southlands — but it is a planned-community build-out, not a vacant-lot market. Outside Southlands, what trades is individual large fee-simple lots (notably RD2 waterfront lots in Beach Grove). No bare-land subdivision selling serviced vacant lots to individual builders was verified here, and no lot count, lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price is stated.**

### Southlands — the planned community (land phases, by form)

- **Southlands Tsawwassen (Century Group) — Market Avenue / Boundary Bay Road.** The City of Delta records the lands as the Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd., File LU006390, with the developer's office at 101 – 6388 Market Avenue. Southlands as a whole is a 537-acre master-planned community planned for about 950 homes, with development approval in 2013 and sales beginning in 2020; by mid-October reporting about 120 homes had closed and about 100 were under construction — community totals across ALL housing forms, never quote them as lot counts. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/ and https://renxhomes.ca/century-group-maintains-course-on-southlands-tsawwassen and https://www.livabl.com/delta-bc/southlands-tsawwassen--mpc
- **The land-tenure mix IS the Southlands fact:** housing ranges across "single-family homes, bare-land strata single-family homes, non-strata row homes, traditional townhomes and condos," with each pocket of 10–25 homes potentially under a different ownership structure — no tenure is ever assumed from the Southlands name alone; every collection below carries its own tenure line. https://renxhomes.ca/century-group-maintains-course-on-southlands-tsawwassen
- **High Street Rowhomes — Southlands Drive and Boundary Bay Road. FEE-SIMPLE (non-strata freehold).** 10 homes, 3 storeys, by Century Group; the project source states expressly the rowhomes are "non-strata, freehold" with no strata fees. As land form: fee-simple lots with a house on each — no strata corporation, no Form B. Counted as townhouse-form housing in the East townhomes home file, documented here as the community's fee-simple phase only. https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/high-street-tsawwassen/
- **Beachcomber Cottages — Southlands. BARE-LAND STRATA — its own form.** Detached cottages (1,320–1,410 sq ft) around a shared courtyard, described by the presale source as bare-land strata homes. Plainly: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns the strata lot (essentially the building lot itself) and the cottage on it, and Form B, strata fees for common property, and registered bylaws apply to the lot. It is NOT a fee-simple lot and NOT a townhouse. Phase/unit count: UNCONFIRMED in the sources read. Counted/documented as its own form in the East townhomes file, not re-counted here. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/
- **The Granary (townhome portion) — Market District, Red Barn Boulevard and Market Avenue. STRATA.** 19 walk-up townhomes (plus 16 garden flats, apartment-form, counted in condos) — built housing on Southlands land, cross-referenced as a land phase only; its strata fee figure in the presale source is developer-estimated and is not a land fact. **Heirloom Townhomes — Southlands. Tenure UNCONFIRMED.** A group of 33 townhouses and duplexes recorded as sold out in a community profile — the source mixes forms and states no tenure split, so no land tenure is asserted for it here. **Centennial Green — Southlands. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; the current OCP consolidated the former area plans into a single Land Use Plan and does not use the old "Single Family Residential" designation (s. 1.5.1). **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)". https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Waterfront setback:** Zoning Bylaw s. 6.2.3 sets a waterfront setback for lots fronting Boundary Bay along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue, under which a seawall may encroach into the minimum front setback; the bylaw states no Beach Grove flood construction level — the FCL question goes to the permit area and the City, not the zoning text. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands) — the east side's overlay, and it names Southlands expressly:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than **2.9 metres GSC datum**; minimum land surface elevation around construction **2.3 metres GSC datum**; no building within **7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area**. Whether a given lot falls inside SD3 needs a DeltaMap check for that lot. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dyke / flood frame:** the east side faces Boundary Bay behind the Boundary Bay dike; Delta is the diking authority (over 63 km of flood protection, 31 pump stations, ~538 km of drainage). **Foreshore tenure:** the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission — a "private dock" is not land the seller owns. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024** — four units on all residential properties in Delta (the six-unit tier is Scott Road / North Delta). Added units are not subdivision and do not waive SD3 or any permit area. **Subdivision/servicing:** Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing No. 7162, 2015), under LGA s. 506; parcel-specific requirements beyond that UNCONFIRMED — confirm with the City. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2024-06/Ravi%20Kahlon_Small-Scale%20Multi-Unit%20Housing_June%2026%2C%202024.pdf and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR rules (farm-edge parcels):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — the legal reason farm-adjacent product is structured as it is; subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. ALC caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Bare-land strata block (applies to Beachcomber Cottages lots and any strata Southlands phase):** Form B (SPA s. 59) discloses monthly fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance and insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable; a strata established on/after July 1, 2024 follows the new-strata first-report timeline. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2; May 1, 2023 exemptions). **Fee-simple contrast:** High Street homes carry none of this — no strata, no Form B, no fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Presales (REDMA) — live in Southlands:** a presale buyer must receive the developer's disclosure statement (REDMA s. 18) and has a 7-day rescission period from the later of signing or acknowledging it (s. 21); deposits are held in trust. Assignment terms are contract-specific — none verified per collection: UNCONFIRMED. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **PTT on land:** 1% / 2% / 3% tiers plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-time buyers: exempt on first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — can apply to a never-occupied new HOME in Southlands, not to bare land alone. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST — land and new homes, CRA level only:** CRA GI-003: most individual personal-use vacant-land sales are exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable — developer/newly-subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. On a new Southlands HOME, GST applies with the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — the rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — and the New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350,000–$450,000) underneath it. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year; CMHC 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + prohibition — Delta only:** SVT taxable areas include the City of Delta; reserve/treaty/self-governing lands are excluded. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. SVT payable on a specific vacant parcel and any federal prohibition position per buyer: UNCONFIRMED in this research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Delta property taxes:** due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, further 5% after September 2, 2026. **Foreclosure:** Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — mapping UNVERIFIED. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Southlands and build my own house?** No phase is documented as selling vacant serviced lots — Southlands phases sell built homes in named collections, each with its own tenure. What you choose is a collection and a tenure (fee-simple, strata, or bare-land strata), not a lot. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: What is the difference between a Beachcomber Cottage lot and a High Street lot?** Tenure. Beachcomber is bare-land STRATA: a strata lot, Form B, fees and bylaws, with the cottage as the owner's house. High Street is expressly non-strata freehold: no strata documents exist to ask for. Same community name, opposite diligence packages. https://www.vancouverpresaleprojects.com/presale/southlands-tsawwassen/ and https://www.vancouvernewcondos.com/properties/high-street-tsawwassen/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Beach Grove waterfront lot?** RD2's subdivision minimum is 4,000 m², and the waterfront setback (s. 6.2.3), SD3 hazard rules and a DeltaMap lot check all sit on top of the zone math. Most waterfront parcels are one homesite at that minimum, not a subdivision play. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RD2.pdf
- **Q: What flood rules touch a Southlands or shore lot?** SD3: 2.9 m GSC datum habitable floor level, 2.3 m minimum land elevation around construction, 7.5 m setback from the natural boundary of a tidal area — and the shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike Delta maintains. Confirm the lot's SD3 application with the City before design work. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: Is the foreshore / a dock part of the land?** No — the Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; moorage is tenure by lease/licence via FrontCounter BC under General or Specific Permission. Price the house lot, not the water. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **Q: Is the farm land beside Southlands future development land?** The plan's farm acres (275-acre Southlands Farm + 50-acre community farm) are planned agriculture inside the 537-acre community, and ALR ground generally cannot be stratified and needs ALC approval to subdivide. Do not price farm ground as the next phase without a City/ALC source. https://renx.ca/century-group-long-awaited-southlands-project-begin
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Southlands purchase?** On a new developer home, yes — with the FTHB rebate for eligible first-time buyers (100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000) and the New Housing Rebate underneath. On a resale, no GST question arises. On bare land (if a parcel ever trades separately), CRA's GI-003 frame governs by seller/history. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: How do I get out of a Southlands presale?** Within REDMA's 7-day rescission window from the later of signing or acknowledging the disclosure statement, deposit in trust. After that, the contract governs. https://northam-law.com/pre-sale-condo-purchases-in-british-columbia-key-legal-protections-for-buyers/
- **Q: Is Fairway Views a real option on the east side?** It is a name on a live-feed listing card only — no developer, strata or manager source was opened for it in the research, so it is QUEUED, counted nowhere, and any listing under that name needs tenure/strata-plan verification against the disclosure statement, Form B or title. https://jaredgibbons.ca/tsawwassen-east.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Southlands / Beach Grove address?** Address-based: the sample (570 Boundary Bay Rd) maps to South Park Elementary and South Delta Secondary. Run the exact address in the SD37 locator before writing a school into an offer. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Southlands took a decade to approve and is still early:** approval in 2013, sales from 2020, about 120 homes closed and about 100 under construction out of ~950 planned at the reporting read — a buyer is buying into a community under construction around them for years, on land the City files as LU006390 (Southlands Properties of Century Industries Ltd.), whatever the brochure name. https://renxhomes.ca/century-group-maintains-course-on-southlands-tsawwassen and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/109911/
- **The 2.9 m number is the one to remember on this shore:** SD3 sets the habitable floor at 2.9 m GSC datum with a 2.3 m land-elevation minimum and a 7.5 m tidal setback — and SD3 names the Southlands in its own title. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **A seawall has its own rule here:** s. 6.2.3 lets a seawall encroach into the minimum front setback on Boundary Bay lots along Beach Grove Road south of 16 Avenue — and the same passage states the bylaw sets no Beach Grove flood construction level. https://delta.civicweb.net/filepro/document/177229/7600%20-%20Delta%20Zoning%20Bylaw.pdf
- **The Pillars is two products at two addresses.** 1700 56 Street is the townhouse component (Beach Grove townhomes); 1702 is the apartment component (condos only). A land or housing search that treats "The Pillars" as one tenure will misprice both. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/tsawwassen-east-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
