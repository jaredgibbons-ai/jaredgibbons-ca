## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: A1 and A2 Agricultural under Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014.** Confirm the parcel's A1 or A2 zone under the bylaw before assuming any farm or home-based use is permitted — that is the research file's own buying guidance. The city-wide A1/A2 subdivision minimums carried in the Abbotsford AgRefresh material are 8.0 ha (A1) and 16.0 ha (A2); the current zoning section for a specific parcel governs and should be read before those figures are relied on. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/bylaws/zoning-bylaw and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT.** Abbotsford's current Official Community Plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025. The research file's older plan framing (the OCP "Adopted June 27, 2016" reference, and any "2600-2016" / "2500-2016" citation) is STALE / conflicted — apply 2050 and flag the stale numbers as a Jerry sweep item. Rural-village input from Bradner and other rural villages was gathered when the OCP was made; no Bradner-specific neighbourhood plan is documented in the research file. https://www.abbotsford.ca/planning-development/development/official-community-plan and https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **ALR rules — the governing frame for ALR parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified — cannot be converted into strata lots and sold separately (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)). Residence caps as the ALC states them: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite permitted ONLY inside the principal residence; an additional residence (a second, detached, small-scale residence for farm use or other permitted use) capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Agri-tourism and temporary farm worker housing are permitted under conditions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form, never the flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, with applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR: it understates the test for small and large parcels alike, and no such line is asserted in this file or documented in Bradner's research file. Farm class is the holding-cost difference between a working Bradner parcel and an idle one — confirm the parcel's current class and the income record before pricing the tax position. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **SSMUH — present in Abbotsford, not a Bradner land lever:** June 2024 Council adoption of Abbotsford's SSMUH Zoning Bylaw amendments is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" must not be asserted). Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling and its coach/garden suite cap is 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford's rules ONLY) — but on ALR land the ALC suite rule (inside the principal residence only) and the A1/A2 zoning are the controlling framework, and SSMUH creates no Bradner lots and no Bradner strata. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/bradner-guide-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3)
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements:** for a non-ALR Bradner parcel, subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums and the City's works-and-services (Development Bylaw) requirements; for an ALR parcel, ALC approval is required in addition, stated as the ALC states it. The Development Bylaw's current number/year and its detailed servicing schedule were NOT confirmed on a City page read for this file: UNCONFIRMED — never quote a servicing standard for a Bradner parcel from a listing or a summary. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Working-land title items:** the research file documents a retained logging right-of-way (A&A Trading Ltd.) in a City context for Bradner — the diligence lesson is general: read the full title for rights-of-way, covenants and any ALC or farm-use encumbrances before valuing the parcel's usable area. Parcel-specific encumbrances: UNCONFIRMED until that parcel's title is pulled. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2023-05/2023-05-01%20-%20Agenda%20-%20Regular%20Council%20Meeting.pdf
- **If a strata purchase is made elsewhere in Abbotsford (redirect case):** the BC strata block applies — Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). None of it applies to a Bradner fee-simple parcel purchase itself: no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The home-based exemptions have land routes — a first-time buyer registering a vacant lot and building can keep the FTHB exemption if lot value plus build cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and occupying by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund lot PTT once a qualifying new home is built ($1,100,000 / $1,150,000 thresholds) — but at Bradner parcel values and under ALR housing caps those routes will rarely be the pricing driver, and land held as farmland gets neither exemption. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://wowa.ca/calculators/bc-property-transfer-tax
- **GST on farm and vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA sources support:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (personal-use land is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a subdivided parcel of more than two parts. Farmland is the caution: the CRA addresses sales of farmland by individuals in a SEPARATE info sheet and treats farmland sales as usually taxable subject to limited exemptions (including certain transfers to a related person for personal use). A Bradner farm purchase must therefore never be assumed GST-exempt on the "vacant land" rule alone — the GST treatment of any specific Bradner parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status, the parcel's farm use and the applicable CRA sheet are applied, and the contract must state the GST position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford — owners of residential-class property in Abbotsford must complete the annual declaration. Farm-classed and agricultural parcels sit in a different assessment world, and the application of the tax to any specific Bradner parcel (its class, its exemptions) is UNCONFIRMED until the parcel's assessment class and the current exemption list are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** the March 27, 2023 regulatory amendments repealed the provision that had made vacant land zoned residential or mixed-use inside a census metropolitan area or census agglomeration "residential property" — as amended, vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian, including for residential development, per the law-firm summaries; dwellings remain governed by the prohibition and specific application is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level in this file. https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **First-home programs — the land boundary, stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031; the GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise housing-only. On a Bradner parcel either rebate matters only if a qualifying new home is built and bought — and on ALR land that home must first fit the ALC caps above. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** City of Abbotsford taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026, each including any unclaimed Home Owner Grant. The City offers a Revitalization Tax Exemption for eligible properties in designated revitalization areas, but no Bradner-specific exemption was confirmed in the material read — treat any claimed Bradner exemption as UNVERIFIED. Farm class (above) is the real holding-cost lever, and it is earned by farm income, not by zoning alone. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice and https://www.abbotsford.ca/business-development/tax-incentives-exemptions
- **Market context, labelled as community-level only:** Abbotsford IS a SnapStats-covered community; Bradner's own September 2026 row (4 detached listings, 0 sales; 0 attached) and the Abbotsford-wide detached median ($949,000) are housing figures. SnapStats has NO land category and no farm-parcel figure — a Bradner parcel is priced from its soil, its ALR status, its farm-class position and its housing caps, none of which SnapStats measures. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What am I actually buying when I buy "land" in Bradner?** A whole fee-simple farm or rural-residential parcel under A1 or A2 Agricultural zoning, part of the community in the ALR. There is no lot subdivision, no bare-land strata and no presale lot product to buy instead — and no lot-size statistic is published for the area (UNVERIFIED), so each parcel is judged on its own title, zoning and ALR status. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR — and what changes if it is?** Confirm the parcel itself on the ALC map; the community is only partly in the reserve. If it is in: subdivision needs ALC approval, the residence cannot be stratified, and the housing caps are one principal residence to 500 m², a suite inside that residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (40 ha or less) or 186 m² (over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I split the parcel, or stratify it later for family?** Stratify: no, if it is ALR — residences in the ALR cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Split: only within the A1 (8.0 ha) / A2 (16.0 ha) subdivision minimums and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval. Do not price a Bradner parcel as if a full second house or a strata exit can be added. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. A detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," size-capped and tied to farm use or another permitted use. Abbotsford's general suite and coach/garden caps do not override the ALR rule on ALR land. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income do I need to keep farm class?** All three tiers, not a flat figure: $2,500 on a 0.8–4 ha farm operation; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value in excess of 4 ha over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A page or listing stating only "$2,500+" understates the test for small and large parcels. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced?** Assume well and onsite sewage until proven otherwise: Bradner is outside the urban servicing pattern, and lots outside the urban boundary in rural residential zones may not connect to the Community Water System or Community Sanitary Sewer System. Pull the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing for the parcel before offering. Parcel-level status: UNCONFIRMED in every source checked until those records are read. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a Bradner farm purchase?** Do not assume either way. The CRA's vacant-land sheet makes most individual personal-use sales exempt, but it treats farmland in a separate sheet as usually taxable with limited exemptions — a working farm parcel is the fact pattern where buyers get surprised. The answer is UNCONFIRMED for any specific parcel until vendor status and farm use are applied; the contract must state who bears GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the land?** Property taxes on the Abbotsford cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3; second 5% September 16), with farm class — if the parcel qualifies and the income is actually produced and reported — as the main reducer. Add well/septic maintenance and any right-of-way or covenant obligations on title. There is no strata fee: the parcel is fee-simple. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a Bradner parcel?** Catchments are address-based in SD34. The settled chain for the test address 5396 Bradner Road (Bradner Elementary's own address) is Bradner Elementary (K–5) → Eugene Reimer Middle (6–8) → Rick Hansen Secondary (9–12) (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Note the correction the locator settled: a district boundary-maps page named W.A. Fraser Middle for this address — the locator governs, and the middle school is Eugene Reimer Middle, NOT W.A. Fraser. Any other Bradner address needs its own locator run. https://eugenereimer.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/ermsfamilyofschools/eugenereimer
- **Q: How long would an ALR subdivision take?** No official timeline is published in any source checked: UNCONFIRMED. The sequence alone — ALC approval plus City Approving Officer review against agricultural-scale minimums most parcels cannot meet — is the answer a buyer should price on: for most Bradner parcels the subdivision question ends at the minimums, not at a timeline. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
