## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050," adopted June 15, 2026):** the current plan. Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is NEVER cited as current. The Douglas Neighbourhood Concept Plan process concluded in 1999 and its land use designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update — the in-force check for any Douglas lot is the Surrey 2050 designation plus the lot's zone under Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000, not the 1999 NCP designations listing text still quotes. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/douglas-ncp
- **Zoning — Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000:** parcel by parcel; the zone of a specific Pacific Douglas lot is UNVERIFIED until checked for that parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 (verified on the City's own pages):** the bylaw under which subdivision and servicing requirements for a Douglas parcel run; parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. Servicing caution with a date on it: a 2007 City corporate report states a City sanitary sewer system services about half the lands within the Douglas neighbourhood, primarily the more developed southwest area, flowing to the Douglas sanitary pump station — that statement is from 2007, and current parcel-level servicing (City water/sewer vs well/septic) is UNVERIFIED in this pass. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/RPT_2007-L001.pdf
- **SSMUH — Surrey (in effect July 8, 2024):** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing framework under Zoning By-law No. 12000 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary. Confirm the parcel's eligibility before pricing in added units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, its habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable area under Zoning By-law No. 12000, and it can never be a separate strata lot; the old 90 m² Surrey suite cap is NOT a current rule — never state it. A feed card's "licenced revenue suite" wording is a claim to verify against City records, not a fact. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Coach houses and garden suites — Surrey:** permitted in residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes; 35 m² minimum and 75 m² maximum, rising to a maximum of 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the principal dwelling has not achieved its maximum floor area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Streamside setbacks — Surrey:** in Part 7A of Zoning By-law No. 12000, varying by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey ground. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** permitted, owner-only — an owner must host from their principal residence and hold a City business licence at $358/yr (Zoning By-law No. 12000, Part 4 s. B.7(d); Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1), with the City's 2024 corporate report's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a conflict — carry $358 with $350 disclosed. - **ALR block (the plan area is bordered by the ALR to the north; parcel status UNVERIFIED per lot):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Registry / foreclosure geography:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — stated plainly; the registry mapping for Surrey court-ordered land sales is UNVERIFIED and is never filled in with a neighbouring city's registry. Foreclosure process, where met: the settled court process (Rules 21-7/13-5, PD-66, Form 60); Form B and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/consol2/consol2/96253_01
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
