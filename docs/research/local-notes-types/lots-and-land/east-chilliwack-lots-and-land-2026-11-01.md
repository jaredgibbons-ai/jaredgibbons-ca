## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: East Chilliwack has no meaningful bare-land market in the subdivision sense — no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source. What trades is whole fee-simple farm and rural-residential parcels, much of it in the ALR under the AL (Agricultural Lowland) zone — and on ALR parcels the law bars the strata form outright.**

- **Fee-simple farm and rural parcels are the only land form.** The research records the typical types as rural detached houses on half-acre to multi-acre lots, hobby farms, equestrian properties, and commercial dairy, poultry, nursery and forage farms in the ALR; the Agricultural Area Plan states the farm sector in the plan area is dominated by dairy and poultry operations, nurseries and greenhouses. A parcel here is bought whole — title, farm buildings, residence rights as the ALR rules allow — not as a serviced lot in a subdivision product. https://jaredgibbons.ca/east-chilliwack.html and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plan and zoning:** Chilliwack 2050 OCP, Bylaw No. 5500, with the Agricultural Area Plan as Schedule B1; zoning under Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 — the AL zone minimums in §2 govern the farm ground (Zoning Bylaw 5000 §6 farm-zone framework as the research states it). Confirm the parcel's zone section before assuming any farm or home-based use is permitted. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/2387/SB1%5FAgricultural%5FArea%5FPlan%5F1%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **SSMUH:** Chilliwack's small-scale multi-unit housing bylaw is **Bylaw 5395, adopted June 18, 2024**. On ALR farm parcels the practical added-unit rules are the ALR rules below, not the urban SSMUH frame. (Sibling files for this slug: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/east-chilliwack-condos-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/east-chilliwack-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block — full form:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence — never in a barn or a detached building; one additional residence for farm use or another permitted use, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — with applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; this slug's research does not document such a flat line on this slug's live page, so no page defect is asserted on that point (see §5). https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata block — stated for completeness, governing no East Chilliwack stock:** if a strata lot were ever lawfully produced on non-ALR ground here, the BC block would apply to the LOT — Form B (SPA s. 59); depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. The GST treatment of any specific East Chilliwack farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in East Chilliwack?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering exists here. What trades is whole fee-simple farm and rural-residential parcels under the AL zone; the Rosedale lots visible in the page feed are a scope question for Jared, not East Chilliwack inventory. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I build a second house on a farm property here?** Only within the ALC limits: one principal residence per parcel (maximum 500 m²), a secondary suite only inside that residence, and an additional small residence for farm use or another permitted use — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² only on parcels over 40 ha. Do not price a parcel as if a full second house can be added. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building?** No — under the ALC housing rules a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," which is size-capped and tied to farm use or another permitted use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a parcel later?** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval — the AL zone's minimums (7.5 ha, or 0.2 ha for an approved homesite severance, 1 ha for a boundary adjustment, 25 m minimum width) decide what is even possible — and a strata plan cannot be deposited without the ALC's permission (ALCA s. 21(1); SPA s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). How long an ALR subdivision would take: UNCONFIRMED — no official timeline is published. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: Is this parcel in the ALR?** Status is per address and no parcel was confirmed in this research — check the ALC map and the title before pricing subdivision, a second dwelling or farm-class value into the parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What does farm class require, exactly?** Three tiers, never a flat figure: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — and the application is due by October 31 for the next taxation year. A parcel that stops meeting its tier loses the classification and its tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a farm parcel?** It turns on the seller and the parcel's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable. The position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established; have the contract state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What flood or diking costs come with the land?** Buildings must sit at least 60 m from the Fraser River, 75 m from the Vedder River and 30 m from the Chilliwack River and Vedder Canal, and Chilliwack levies additional drainage and dyking taxes on top of the municipal rates — read the full tax notice, not just the municipal line, and measure the setbacks on any lot near a river, slough or canal. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=999 and https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2527
- **Q: Which schools serve a parcel here?** Catchments are address-based in SD33 and **UNVERIFIED for every East Chilliwack address in this program** — no locator run was recorded. The schools physically in or named for the area are East Chilliwack Elementary (K–5) and Cheam Elementary (K–5), and a new 500-space K–5 east-side elementary at 10125 McGrath Road is under construction with estimated occupancy April 2027. Run the exact address in the SD33 locator before relying on any catchment claim — including the live page's Chilliwack Middle / Chilliwack Secondary statement, which is not a locator result. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
