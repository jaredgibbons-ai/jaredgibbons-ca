## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: the City of Pitt Meadows, a municipality in Metro Vancouver, on the traditional territory of q̓ic̓əy̓ (Katzie) First Nation. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0
- Municipal setting: the Pitt River to the west, the Fraser River to the south, the City of Maple Ridge to the east, and Pitt Lake, the Pitt River and agricultural and conservation land to the north. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- Board sub-areas (Paragon/GVR) that make up the city page: Central Meadows, Mid Meadows, North Meadows, South Meadows and West Meadows. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf

- Main roads and commute: Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) crosses the city east–west over the Pitt River Bridge; Golden Ears Way leads to the Golden Ears Bridge over the Fraser River to Langley and Surrey; Harris Road is the main north–south street, and Harris Road at Lougheed Highway is the main town centre junction. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt791.pdf
- Transit: Pitt Meadows Station is in the city on the West Coast Express Mission City to Waterfront line; the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs Coquitlam Central Station to Haney Place with a Harris Road stop; routes 701, 791, 595 and the 719/722 loop serve the city. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/221/tt997.pdf

- Shopping anchors: Meadowtown Shopping Centre (19800 Lougheed Highway), Meadowvale Shopping Centre in the Harris Road North commercial area, and Osprey Village on Fraser Way at Barnston View Road. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Pitt Meadows has no meaningful bare-land subdivision market as documented in the research — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source in the research file. What actually trades as land here is (a) fee-simple urban lots inside the containment boundary (usually carrying a house, priced with R-3/R-4/R-6 multi-unit potential), and (b) large fee-simple farm and rural parcels — 78% of the city's land base is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, and that ALR ground, not subdivision lots, is the city's real land market.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only verified) form

- **Urban lots (inside the urban containment boundary):** houses sit on R-3, R-4 and R-6 lots (three, four and up to six units per lot under Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011). Lot character as the bylaw states it: R-3/R-4/R-6 minimum lot area for subdivision 1,215 m² and minimum lot width for subdivision 12 m; RS Large Lot Residential minimum lot area 0.2 ha. These are zone minimums, not a claim that every urban lot is that size. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Rural lots (outside the ALR):** RR rural residential zones — RR-2 minimum 4 ha, RR-3 minimum 2 ha, RR-4 minimum 1 ha — make up the rural fee-simple stock alongside the farm parcels below. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Farm parcels (ALR ground):** A-1 General Agricultural minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha and A-2 Large Lot Agricultural 16 ha under the same bylaw — and subdivision within the ALR additionally requires ALC approval (Section 3), so the bylaw minimum alone never settles whether a farm parcel can be split. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR split, as documented:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states that 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the Agricultural Land Reserve and that agriculture remains the predominant land use in the community. The non-ALR balance is the urban containment area plus the RR rural pockets. No finer parcel-level split is stated. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Servicing:** the research documents the city's diked, drained urban servicing context (a separate drainage levy funds the diking/drainage system; Section 3) and 15 m building setbacks from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in agricultural and rural zones. A parcel-level municipal water/sewer vs well/septic statement: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City; do not assume urban servicing on rural/ALR ground. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, under the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022; the North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0 https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R-3, R-4 and R-6 allow up to three, four and six dwelling units (in R-6 the maximum is five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code). The 1,215 m² figure is a subdivision minimum ONLY: the City's SSMUH implementation update states the housekeeping amendment clarified that the minimum lot area requirement applies to subdivision and not to development on existing lots — an existing smaller urban lot can still be developed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Suites:** secondary suites and garden suites are listed accessory uses in the RS, RR, R-4 and R-6 zones, and the City's garden suite guide sets one suite per lot at 33 m² to 90 m². https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2 (urban 1,215 m² / 12 m width; RS 0.2 ha; RR 4/2/1 ha; A-1 8 ha; A-2 16 ha; RM-1 minimum lot area 0.4 ha for townhouse developments). A servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED; servicing detail beyond the minimums and the drainage/dike position below is UNCONFIRMED and is not filled in. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Floodplain — the defining lot rule:** **over 95%** of the land base lies within the Fraser River and (to a smaller degree) Pitt River floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008; zoning requires buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in agricultural and rural zones. LIVE CONFLICT: live pages stating ~85–86% (Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution; the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 put it at about 86%) conflict with the Business Plan figure — flagged, never silently resolved. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **ALR rules (78% of the land base — the core land rule here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Short-term rentals:** STRs are PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows — a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw," and Pitt Meadows appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. The prohibition is a city use rule; it applies to a lot purchase priced on STR income just as it does to a strata unit. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt (CRA GI-003). This is the live distinction on a Pitt Meadows farm parcel: an individual's personal-use farm-land sale vs a developer's newly subdivided lot sale sit on opposite sides of it. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply to Pitt Meadows. No Pitt Meadows speculation/vacancy position beyond that is documented in the research file; the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies only in designated taxable regions as the Province states them — confirm the parcel's position on the provincial source before quoting any rate. No rate is asserted here.
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — vacant land:** the research file for this slug cites no source on the prohibition's application to vacant land — position UNCONFIRMED in this file. Do not import another file's position (positions diverge across the program). Get advice on the current official text for the specific lot — material here because so much Pitt Meadows land is ALR/farm ground.
- **Property taxes:** under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026, Class 1 Residential rates per $1,000 are $2.8438 general municipal, $0.1082 drainage levy and $0.0583 Metro Vancouver Regional District levy; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% after August 1, 2026. The drainage levy is a holding cost on every lot in this diked city. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf
- **Airport diligence (south-city lots):** Pitt Meadows Regional Airport (YPK) is in the south of the city, owned and operated by the Pitt Meadows Airport Society, with a Fly YPK Neighbourly Program and a noise concern process — check flight paths against the specific south-end address. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/ypk-airport

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a subdivision where I can just buy a lot?** On this file's evidence, no — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as individual fee-simple urban lots (usually with a house on them) and large rural/farm parcels. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How much of Pitt Meadows is farmland, and what does that mean for a lot?** 78% of the land base is in the ALR (City 2020 Business Plan). On ALR ground, subdivision needs ALC approval on top of the A-1 (8 ha) / A-2 (16 ha) bylaw minimums, and an ALR residence cannot be stratified. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a big rural lot?** It depends on the zone first — RR-2 4 ha, RR-3 2 ha, RR-4 1 ha minimums outside the ALR; A-1 8 ha / A-2 16 ha inside it, plus ALC approval for any ALR split. No subdivision promise can be made from this file — get the zone and ALR status confirmed. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Q: My urban lot is smaller than 1,215 m². Can I still build the R-6 units?** The 1,215 m² figure applies to subdivision, not to development on existing lots, per the City's own SSMUH update — an existing smaller lot can still be developed under R-3/R-4/R-6. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q: Is my lot in the floodplain?** Almost certainly — **over 95%** of the land base is floodplain behind the 60 km dike system (City 2020 Business Plan). Check the flood construction level under Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, any covenant on title, and insurance early; budget the separate drainage levy. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a garden suite to a lot?** In the zones that list it (RS, RR, R-4, R-6 accessory uses), the City guide allows one garden suite per lot at 33 m² to 90 m², on top of the secondary suite provisions. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q: Is the Osprey Village / Bonson ground a land play?** No — Bonson ground trades as built townhouse strata (Coho, Nature's Walk and neighbours, documented in their home sub-area files). Classification note: the Coho cluster sits under Mid Meadows in the sibling programs (MLS: South Meadows), and Bonson is counted in NO sub-area file because its sub-area is unsettled. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy farmland or a new lot?** At CRA GI-003 level: developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction — farm transactions have finer points that are UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I buy a lot and run short-term rentals in a garden suite?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows at all. Price the lot on long-term residential/farm use only. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Which schools serve a North Meadows acreage?** SD42 catchments are address-based across four K–7 elementaries and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12) — run the exact address in the SD42 locator; the only settled sample in the research is 11941 Harris Road → Pitt Meadows Elementary / Pitt Meadows Secondary. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The floodplain number is a live conflict, not a rounding difference.** The City's 2020 Business Plan states **over 95%** floodplain; the superseded OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007 put it at about 86%, and live pages now state ~85–86% sourced to the City Financial Plan 2026–2030. These notes retire to 95% and flag the conflict as a Jerry item — a buyer reading a live page will meet the other number first. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **78% farmland is why there are no subdivisions.** With 78% of the land base in the ALR, new housing supply is corridor infill and SSMUH on existing urban lots, not new bare-land subdivisions — the land scarcity is structural, not cyclical. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **A funded replacement for Pitt Meadows Secondary is in design.** SD42's Strategic Facilities Plan page carries a funded replacement school with a neighbourhood learning centre in the design and development phase. https://www.sd42.ca/board-of-education/strategic-facilities-plan/
- **The Harris Road rail crossing is being grade-separated.** The Pitt Meadows Road and Rail Improvements Project is a four-lane underpass below the CPKC rail crossing at Harris Road — the city's main north–south street, and the corridor most urban lots front. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/our-community/city-planning-projects/pitt-meadows-road-and-rail-improvements-project/past-project
- **The Pitt River Greenway runs 10.2 km in its first phase** west from Harris Landing (Harris Road at Fraser Way) to Ferry Slip Road along the Fraser and Pitt River dikes, planned to reach Grant Narrows Regional Park — dike-top trail access is part of what south/west lot buyers are actually buying. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/harris-landing-pitt-river-greenway
- **Heritage anchors on Harris Road:** the Pitt Meadows General Store and Post Office building at 12294 Harris Road (now the Pitt Meadows Museum and Archives) and the Hoffmann & Son Shop and House at 12265 Harris Road are both on the City heritage register — heritage status is a title-level check on older Harris Road lots. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/arts-culture-heritage/heritage
