## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**This is the Langley land TOPIC file, and it carries no invented roster: no named subdivision or bare-land development is documented here unless an official or developer source verifies it, and none is verified in this file's research — so none is named as inventory. What §2 documents is the land itself across the two Langleys, exactly as the research states it: the forms land takes in the feed, the ALR overlay that defines most of it, and the zone-driven scale that makes "Langley land" two completely different products.**

- Land types in the feed (from the research and the official material): urban infill building lots in the City and the Township's urban communities, suburban residential parcels under the SR zones, and large rural/ALR parcels in Salmon River, Campbell Valley, County Line Glen Valley and Otter District. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-lots-land.html
- The ALR overlay is the defining feature: 235 km² of the Township is in the Agricultural Land Reserve, so most rural Langley land carries ALC housing, use and subdivision rules layered over Township zoning (full ALR block in §3). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- Subdivision scale is zone-driven: a new RU-2 or RU-3 lot needs 8.0 ha, an RU-1 lot 1.7 ha, an SR-2 lot 8,094 m², while urban R-zone lots start at 464.5 m² — the same "land" search spans two completely different products. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf

- Bare-land strata, stated as its own form wherever it exists: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws apply to the lot even though there is no building (strata block in §3). ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

### Township of Langley frame (never blended with the City frame)

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs the Township; the Township states "Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies to the entirety of the Township." Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 2500, s. 110.1, as reproduced in a Township council report): SR-1 — 3,716 m² / 40 m / 55 m; SR-2 — 8,094 m² / 45 m / 61 m; SR-3 — 1,765 m² / 27.45 m / 30.5 m; RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 — 8.0 ha; RU-3 — 8.0 ha; urban R-1B/R-1C — 464.5 m²; R-1D — 650.0 m²; R-1E — 930.0 m²; R-2 — 464.5 m²; RM-1 — 3,716 m²; RM-2/RM-3/RM-3A/RM-4 — 1,672 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** allows 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020** — any source claiming a Township 6-plex tier is wrong. Added-unit housing eligibility requires a property to be connected to both municipal water and sewer; the Township's June 10, 2024 council report estimates approximately 55% of the Township's single-family and duplex lots (~15,000 properties) will qualify for 3 to 4 units, varying by servicing (Brookswood ~15% and Fort Langley ~22% eligible; Walnut Grove ~99% and Murrayville ~98%). https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; this file carries both positions and adopts neither — confirm with the Township for the parcel. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** the Residential Compact Lot Zone R-CL (s. 403) runs from a minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum LOT area of 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer, as required under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration in the Land Title Office. Servicing upgrades are determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — VERIFIED; the Township's development servicing forms include Septic Sewer Certification and Well Testing (Forms F2–F5), and all required works and services must be built to bylaw standards before subdivision approval or building permit issuance. Development cost charges: Township DCC Bylaw No. 5897. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Willoughby plan status — Township:** the Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). The Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — never present them as adopted. Land in Willoughby prices off the adopted documents, not the reading-stage drafts. https://ic10.esolg.ca/11207577_TownshipofLangley/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/2026/willoughby-community-plan-update/6201-Schedule-1.pdf
- **Environment — Township:** development permit types include Streamside Protection and Enhancement (Schedule 3 of the Township OCP, for watercourses not in the ALR); a permit is required to cut or remove a protected tree under Tree Protection Bylaw 2019, No. 5478. Township water and sewer service-area boundaries as a written description: UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/development-activity.aspx

### City of Langley frame (kept strictly separate)

- **Zoning — City:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City; the bylaw states it "applies to the entire area within the boundaries of the City of Langley" and repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100 (the City's media library still serves a consolidated Bylaw 2100 file — Bylaw 3300 is the current source). **The adoption date of Bylaw 3300 is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED in this file; the bylaw number (2025 No. 3300) is cited, not a date.** Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 3300, as printed): R1 Suburban Residential — 557 m² / 16 m frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential — 1,000 m², except lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m); R3 Low Rise Residential — 1,000 m² / 26.0 m. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Suites — City:** a City secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling — a different cap from the Township's 120 m², and the two are never interchanged in this file. (Program standing correction, 2026-11-01.)
- **Subdivision — City:** Bylaw 3300 states "No person shall subdivide land except in accordance with this bylaw", that subdivisions are subject to the City of Langley Subdivision Control Bylaw and the Development Cost Charge Bylaw, that a lot created by subdivision must meet the minimum lot area for its zone, and that the bylaw does not bind the Approving Officer to approve a subdivision that meets the minimum standards. The bylaw number of the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw: UNVERIFIED. City DCC Bylaw 2024 No. 3256. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw

### ALR, strata, tax and federal rules (both Langleys)

- **ALR block:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — creating a bare-land strata on ALR land is itself a subdivision needing the same permission. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), where municipal bylaws also permit it. Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels in either Langley):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this — no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Water and septic on rural lots:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only). PTT is on fair market value at registration, not annual taxes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at fair market value ≤$835,000 (maximum saving $8,000), partial to $860,000, on the program's personal and property conditions. **Newly Built Home Exemption:** full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own research supports it — the research encodes no prohibition position for Langley land, so none is asserted and the position is UNCONFIRMED here. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How much of Langley is in the ALR?** The Township of Langley states 235 square kilometres of the Township is within the Agricultural Land Reserve; check a specific parcel on the Agricultural Land Commission's maps. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What is the smallest rural lot I can create in the Township?** It depends on the zone under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s. 110.1: RU-1 requires 1.7 ha for a new lot, RU-2 and RU-3 require 8.0 ha, SR-2 requires 8,094 m² and SR-1 requires 3,716 m² — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission permission to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/TOL-Council-Report---Provincial-Housing-Legislation,-June-10,-2024.pdf
- **Q: Who approves a subdivision in Langley?** The Approving Officer, as required under the Land Title Act — in the Township the process runs application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration in the Land Title Office; in the City, Bylaw 3300 adds that meeting the minimum lot standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Can I build a second home on a rural Langley lot?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and municipal bylaws must also permit it; the principal residence is capped at 500 m² and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes — or six — on a Township lot?** Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) permits 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², with eligibility turning on municipal water and sewer connection. **There is no 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020.** https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a townhouse in the Township — and in the City?** Township position is IN CONFLICT and is flagged, not resolved: s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m²; the Township's January 2025 guide says not permitted — confirm with the Township. City position is separate: a suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. (Section 3.)
- **Q: What is a coach house — is it a strata unit?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 — never strata. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: Are the Langley market numbers in this file land prices?** No. SnapStats publishes no land category; the §1 figures are Langley residential context only (detached median $1,345,000 and "Condos & Townhomes" combined median $611,000 in September 2026), and land-specific statistics are UNVERIFIED. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a buildable lot?** SD35 School Locator by exact parcel address — catchments follow the address, not the district name; no catchment is asserted in this file because no locator run is documented in its research. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
