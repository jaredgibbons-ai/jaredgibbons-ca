Researched Nov 1, 2026. Every fact carries its source. Anything no source states is marked UNCONFIRMED. No listing addresses or asking prices beyond named developments themselves. No sold prices.

## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Cottonwood MR" is the Paragon sub-area name as listed — the MR suffix distinguishes it from other Cottonwoods in the board's data. Municipality: City of Maple Ridge. The City does not list Cottonwood as a named neighbourhood on its neighbourhoods page (which lists Albion/Kanaka, Port Hammond, Port Haney, Ruskin, Silver Valley, Thornhill, Town Centre, Webster's Corners, Whonnock and Yennadon), and no Cottonwood area plan appears on the City's area plans list. No City-published street-by-street boundary for Cottonwood MR was found; the Paragon code was not confirmed. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods and https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools
- The area's official anchors are Kanaka Creek Elementary at 11120 234A Street and Kanaka Creek, which runs through the area to the Fraser River; the residential area sits around Kanaka Creek Elementary (234A Street) and Alexander Robinson Elementary (238B Street), west of 240 Street and east of the Town Centre, with Kanaka Creek Regional Park along the creek. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools and https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/kanaka-creek-regional-park
- Nearby areas: East Central and the Town Centre to the west, Albion to the east beyond 240 Street, Thornhill to the north, and East Central's residential streets to the northwest. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Main roads: Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road are the east–west corridors south and north of the area, and Haney Place in the Town Centre is the bus exchange the area's routes connect to; local streets named in TransLink routing include 232 Street, 238B Street, Kanaka Way, 240 Street and 104 Avenue. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- Transit: the bus route named for this area is the 745 Haney Place / Cottonwood; the 746 Haney Place / Albion interlines with the 745 at Jackson Road at 103 Avenue, forming one circular service; no West Coast Express station is in Cottonwood MR — the nearest stations are in the Town Centre. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Route_Diagrams/194/r748.pdf
- Schools physically inside the area: Kanaka Creek Elementary (K–7), 11120 234A Street, and Alexander Robinson Elementary (K–7), 11849 238B Street, at the north edge; no secondary school is located inside Cottonwood MR. SD42 has no middle tier (elementary K–7, secondary 8–12). For the sample address 11120 234A Street, the district locator gives Kanaka Creek Elementary (K–7) and Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary (8–12) — a sample-address locator result documented in the research, with French Immersion options as stated there; catchments for other addresses are not confirmed. Run the exact lot address. https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools and https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market is documented for Cottonwood MR — stated plainly at the top. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified in the research or the siblings. What trades as land here is the fee-simple house lot itself: single-detached subdivision lots around the two elementary schools, re-traded as house lots and priced in part on their suite/SSMUH potential.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and documented form

- **The stock:** no Cottonwood area plan exists, so no City plan document sets out housing types for this area and city-wide housing rules apply; the residential streets around the two elementary schools are single-detached subdivisions under the RS zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. The documented base is freehold houses, with secondary suites and SSMUH units as the added-density paths. Fee-simple is the default form: own title, no strata, no Form B, no strata fees. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Lot character, as the bylaw states it:** the bylaw schedule gives a minimum lot area of 668 m² for RS-1; the other RS variants (RS-1a through RS-1d, RS-2, RS-3) carry their own schedules in the same bylaw, and the bylaw's residential zones also include RST, RT-1, RT-2 and RM-1 to RM-4. The 668 m² figure is the RS-1 zone minimum only — not a claim that every Cottonwood lot is that size; other zones' minimums are UNCONFIRMED in this file beyond their schedule existence. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures
- **Built strata ground is not land stock:** the siblings document built townhouse complexes on Cottonwood Drive / Kanaka Way / surrounding streets (The Meadows at Verigin's Ridge, Cottonwood Terrace, Carriage Lane and others, in the townhomes sibling) and no apartment-condo building at all (condos sibling). Those are built homes on strata lots, documented in their home files — none is a bare lot, and none is counted as land stock here. (Siblings: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/cottonwood-mr-townhomes-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cottonwood-mr-condos-2026-11-01.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 contains the land-use and development regulations for every property in the city; its residential zones include RS-1, RS-1a, RS-1b, RS-1c, RS-1d, RS-2, RS-3, RST, RT-1, RT-2 and RM-1 to RM-4, and the bylaw schedule gives a minimum lot area of 668 m² for RS-1 (Section 2). https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/land-development/planning-application-forms-procedures
- **OCP — citation CONTESTED, flagged wherever met, resolved nowhere:** Maple Ridge OCP citations in the program's sources conflict — 7060-2014 vs 7998-2024, with an OCP update adopted November 25, 2025 noted in the condos sibling. This file flags the conflict and asserts neither number. What is settled: no Cottonwood-specific area plan was found; the area is covered by the OCP's general policies. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods (sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/cottonwood-mr-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Neighbouring plans, precisely placed:** the Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025 — Albion is east beyond 240 Street, outside this sub-area. The Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan south of the area is ADOPTED January 27, 2026 (Bylaw 7781-2021; OCP amendments approved and the amending bylaw adopted that day, corridor ending at 221 Street) — cited here as adopted, noting the research file's earlier, lighter wording ("Council adopted the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan, directing growth around future Bus Rapid Transit stations") and the townhomes sibling's note that an earlier contrary reading of the corridor's status in program files is superseded by the January 27, 2026 City record. Its effect is on corridor/Town Centre land, not on Cottonwood lots directly. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway (sibling townhomes file above)
- **SSMUH / suites as the City states them — Maple Ridge's own figures, not Abbotsford's:** one secondary suite per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area, with a building permit required; eligible single-detached and duplex lots can have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH rules, subject to zoning, bylaw and building code requirements. Do NOT import Abbotsford's figures (suite under 50%; coach/garden 15% to 100 m²) into this Maple Ridge file — Maple Ridge's suite cap as stated here is 40%, inside the principal building. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision of a Cottonwood MR lot proceeds under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019's zone schedules (RS-1 minimum 668 m², Section 2) plus City servicing — no separate Maple Ridge servicing-bylaw number is asserted in this file's research, and parcel-level servicing standards are UNCONFIRMED here. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the specific parcel; no standard is invented. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-2/cottonwood-mr.md)
- **Flood / watercourse:** Kanaka Creek is a salmon-bearing watercourse through the area and the regional park protects the creek corridor; the City's flood material covers the Fraser River floodplain, and whether a specific Cottonwood MR lot is in a mapped floodplain is not confirmed — check the City flood maps at MapleRidge.ca/Flood, and note suites are prohibited in the Floodplain Area (above). https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/be-flood-ready-guide-fraser-river-city-maple-ridge
- **ALR rules (where a parcel is confirmed ALR — none identified in Cottonwood MR in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata is verified in Cottonwood MR (Section 2). Where a BC bare-land strata lot is bought, the full block applies to the lot: Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, Mar 20 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land — a Cottonwood lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no position on vacant land is documented in this file's own research — stated as UNCONFIRMED here, with no position imported from another file. The program's sources diverge on vacant land generally.
- **Speculation / vacancy taxes:** no Cottonwood MR–specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY, not applicable here.
- **Maple Ridge property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What land actually trades in Cottonwood MR?** Fee-simple house lots in the single-detached subdivisions around Kanaka Creek Elementary and Alexander Robinson Elementary. No bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big is a typical lot — what does the bylaw require?** The one minimum the research states is RS-1 at 668 m² minimum lot area; the other RS variants carry their own schedules in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019. Confirm the parcel's zone and schedule — do not quote 668 m² for a non-RS-1 lot. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: Can I add a suite to a Cottonwood house lot?** One secondary suite per lot, inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the Floodplain Area, building permit required. Confirm the lot is outside the mapped floodplain before underwriting rental income. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: How many units can SSMUH put on my lot?** Eligible single-detached and duplex lots can have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH rules, subject to zoning, bylaw and building code requirements — the lot's eligibility is the City check, not an assumption. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Q: Is there an area plan that will rezone my street?** No Cottonwood area plan exists — expectations run off the OCP's general policies (OCP bylaw number CONTESTED, Section 3) and the Lougheed Transit Corridor plan to the south (adopted January 27, 2026), not neighbourhood-plan promises. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- **Q: Is any Cottonwood land in the ALR or floodplain?** No ALR land in the sub-area was identified on the sources read (per-parcel ALC check still applies), and floodplain status is per-lot on the City flood maps — Kanaka Creek setbacks apply next to the creek corridor. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/be-flood-ready-guide-fraser-river-city-maple-ridge
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot here?** Generally yes where the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided — generally taxable under CRA GI-003; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cottonwood lot?** The in-area elementaries are Kanaka Creek (11120 234A Street) and Alexander Robinson (11849 238B Street); the documented sample locator result (11120 234A Street) gives Kanaka Creek Elementary → Samuel Robertson Technical Secondary. SD42 runs three east-zone secondaries — run the exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: What are holding costs on a vacant Cottonwood lot?** Maple Ridge taxes on the City cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3, 2026; additional 5% September 2, 2026). No strata fee — Cottonwood lots here are fee-simple. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments
- **Q: Is St. George's Village land ever for sale as lots?** No — it is a housing co-operative holding clear title to its land; members buy shares, not lots. It is not land inventory. https://www.stgeorgesvillage.ca/_files/ugd/070889_39221c746a9b483980e20a3596cdb580.pdf
