## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Subdivision and development process:** subdivision runs through the City against the zone minimums in §2 (and, on ALR ground, the ALC — below); **the Development Bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** — the process is documented here, no number is asserted, and requirements are confirmed with the City for the parcel. (Abbotsford frame, carried in the FILE STATUS block.)
- **ALR block — full form (Aberdeen's governing land regime):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum 500 m²; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha (the A1 zone's Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit figures in §2 track the same caps). Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf

Per-lot status: UNVERIFIED. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
