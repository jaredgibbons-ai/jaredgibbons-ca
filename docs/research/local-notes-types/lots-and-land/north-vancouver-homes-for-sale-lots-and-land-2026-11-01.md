## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "North Vancouver" is not one municipality: it is the District of North Vancouver (the large, mostly residential municipality wrapped around three sides of the City, from Deep Cove and Lynn Valley to Capilano and the Lions Gate area) and the City of North Vancouver (the compact urban municipality on the Burrard Inlet waterfront along the Lonsdale corridor), with different councils, OCPs, zoning bylaws and tax bills. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- District planning structure: District OCP Bylaw 7900 governs District-wide, and the District groups its neighbourhoods into planning areas including Upper Capilano, Lions Gate / Lower Capilano, Lynn Valley and the eastern Seymour/Deep Cove side. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- City planning structure: City OCP Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400 guides the City, and the Lonsdale corridor — Lower, Central and Upper Lonsdale — is its spine; Lower and Central Lonsdale together are recognized by Metro Vancouver as a Regional City Centre. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- Board sub-areas used for North Vancouver include, for the District side: Lynn Valley, Edgemont Village, Pemberton, Capilano, Upper Delbrook, Grousewoods, Forest Hills, plus the Paragon-set names Boulevard, Blueridge, Braemar, Calverhall, Canyon Heights, Deep Cove, Delbrook and Dollarton; for the City side: Lower Lonsdale, Central Lonsdale and Upper Lonsdale. Exact Paragon codes: UNCONFIRMED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Nearby areas: West Vancouver to the west (across the Capilano River), Vancouver across Burrard Inlet via the Lions Gate Bridge and the SeaBus, Burnaby across the Second Narrows (Ironworkers Memorial) Bridge. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/september/r2%20rapidbus%20launches%20between%20phibbs%20exchange%20and%20metrotown%20three%20months%20early%20and%20under%20budget
- Main roads and commute: Lonsdale Avenue is the City's north-south main road; Marine Drive / 3rd Street carries the east-west trip; Highway 1 (Upper Levels) crosses the District with the Second Narrows Bridge to Burnaby and the Lions Gate Bridge (via the District's Lions Gate area and West Vancouver) to downtown Vancouver. https://www.translink.ca/news/2026/september/r2%20rapidbus%20launches%20between%20phibbs%20exchange%20and%20metrotown%20three%20months%20early%20and%20under%20budget
- Schools: one district serves both municipalities — School District 44 (North Vancouver), with no middle tier: elementary is Kindergarten to Grade 7 and secondary is Grade 8 to Grade 12. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
- Catchments: SD44 catchments are address-based and must be confirmed with the district for the exact address. The settled locator result carried in the research: 230 Keith Road West (City) → Queen Mary Community Elementary (K–7) and Carson Graham Secondary (8–12). The program's settled SD44 locator position is 6/6 runs completed — Lynnmour secondary = Windsor Secondary, never Sutherland. All other catchments: UNVERIFIED in this file — run the exact address. https://www.sd44.ca/media/nvsd/nvsd/content-assets/documents/schools/Catchment-Review-Presentation-Boards-May-20-Revised-Graphics.pdf
- Transit anchors: the SeaBus sails from Lonsdale Quay (City, foot of Lonsdale Avenue) to Waterfront Station in Vancouver, with the Lonsdale Quay bus exchange beside the terminal; the R2 Marine–Willingdon RapidBus runs Park Royal to Metrotown via Lonsdale Quay and Phibbs Exchange; route 230 runs Lonsdale Quay to Upper Lonsdale via Lonsdale Avenue and route 229 runs Lonsdale Quay to Lynn Valley via Esplanade, Lonsdale Avenue and 29th Street. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt998.pdf
- Census context: 2021 Census median household income $123,000 in the District and $86,000 in the City (Statistics Canada, 2021 Census Profile) — the two municipalities are different markets under one search name. https://www12.statcan.gc.ca/census-recensement/2021/dp-pd/prof/details/page.cfm?Lang=E&DGUIDlist=2021A00055915046,2021A00055915051

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in North Vancouver as documented in the research — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no presale lot offering, and no city-wide bare-land strata development was verified on an official or developer source in the research file or the three sibling files for this slug. What actually trades as "land" here is (a) fee-simple resale lots, usually carrying an older detached house priced for teardown/rebuild or multiplex potential under the two municipalities' small-scale rules, and (b) small bare-land-style strata forms documented in the sub-area files. Land assemblies and rezoning proposals are documented as such in the sub-area programs, never as for-sale inventory in this file.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **City form:** the City is the North Shore's condo and apartment market (Lonsdale corridor and waterfront), while ground-oriented homes sit on the side streets at the north edge of Lower Lonsdale in the Ground Oriented Zone; detached houses are few or none in the waterfront core. A vacant or teardown lot in the City is therefore an urban infill lot, not a subdivision lot. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Economic-Development/business-opportunities
- **District form:** detached houses across the planning areas — Upper Capilano (Canyon Heights, Highlands, Cleveland, Handsworth, Edgemont), Lynn Valley, and the eastern neighbourhoods toward Deep Cove and Dollarton — on characteristically larger hillside and valley lots. A District-wide typical lot size from an official source: UNVERIFIED — no typical size is invented here. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Lot character as stated:** City GO Zone lots are urban lots (subdivision minimum widths of 7.5 m in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 and 15.0 m in other designations, per the City's GO Zone amendment). These are subdivision minimums in the bylaw, not a claim about any single lot for sale. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Servicing:** servicing for any single vacant lot in either municipality: UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel in the research — confirm municipal water/sewer connection and any off-site works with the governing municipality before subjects. No well/septic position is stated for North Vancouver in the research file, and none is imported from rural files.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Two governments, two rule sets — confirm which governs the lot first.** City: OCP Bylaw, 2014, No. 8400 and Zoning Bylaw, 1995, No. 6700. District: OCP Bylaw 7900 and Zoning Bylaw 3210. City rules do not apply in the District and District rules do not apply in the City — the same street-address style can sit in either municipality along the shared edges, so the municipality must be confirmed per property. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **City SSMUH / Ground Oriented Zone (City ground ONLY):** in the City's GO Zone, a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations, with maximum floor area of 0.85× lot area (LN1) and 1.0× (LN2/RL4). https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **District SSMUH / subdivision potential:** District small-scale multi-unit and subdivision rules as the District states them: UNCONFIRMED in the research file beyond the District's separate Zoning Bylaw 3210 / OCP Bylaw 7900 frame — do NOT import the City GO Zone numbers onto District ground. Confirm the District position for the parcel with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** City-side subdivision minimum widths are stated in Section 2 (7.5 m / 15.0 m by designation). A servicing-bylaw number for either municipality could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED; subdivision/servicing process detail beyond the minimums is UNCONFIRMED and is not filled in. Confirm with the governing municipality for the parcel. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Hazard and permit areas (City, as documented):** the City's Shipyards and Harbourside Waterfront precincts are Development Permit Areas where a permit is required before new development, and Streamside Protection Development Permit Area rules apply near watercourses — land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or 10 metres of a ravine bank. Slope/creek diligence on District hillside lots: confirm the District's DPA position for the parcel — UNCONFIRMED lot-by-lot in this file. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata rules (where the form exists — the BC strata block applies in full to the lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (stated for completeness; no ALR ground is documented for this slug in the research):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Apply only where a parcel check confirms ALR ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply to North Vancouver (City or District). No North Vancouver speculation/vacancy position beyond that is documented in the research file; the BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies only in designated taxable regions as the Province states them — confirm the parcel's position on the provincial source before quoting any rate. No rate is asserted here.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there actually vacant land for sale in North Vancouver?** On this file's evidence, there is no meaningful bare-land market: no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. "Land" listings here are overwhelmingly fee-simple resale lots with an existing house, priced for teardown/rebuild or multiplex potential. (Section 2.)
- **Q: City lot or District lot — how do I tell, and why care?** The OCP, zoning bylaw, council and tax bill all change at the municipal boundary (City OCP 8400 / Zoning 6700 vs District OCP 7900 / Zoning 3210). Confirm the municipality on title before relying on any rule in this file. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- **Q: How many homes can I build on a City lot?** In the City's GO Zone: up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less; up to 4 units (LN1) or up to 6 units (LN2/RL4) on a lot over 280 m², with floor-area caps of 0.85× / 1.0× lot area. Check the designation before paying for multiplex potential. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Q: Can I use the City's multiplex numbers on a District lot in Lynn Valley?** No — never cross-import bylaws. The District position for that parcel is UNCONFIRMED in this file; confirm it with the District under OCP 7900 / Zoning 3210. (Section 3.)
- **Q: A listing shows a detached house but mentions strata fees. What is that?** Likely a bare-land-style small strata (the form documented on Central Lonsdale side streets, e.g. a 3-home strata). A bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — you pay strata fees, bylaws bind you, and diligence runs through the Form B. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/central-lonsdale-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large District hillside lot?** UNCONFIRMED from this file — a District-wide typical lot size and subdivision minimums were not verified on an official source in the research. Get the zone and DPA position confirmed with the District before writing on subdivision potential. (Sections 2–3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a lot?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the first-time buyer GST rebate help me buy a lot?** No — the FTHB rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. It can matter later on a new home built/ bought as housing, not on the lot purchase itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax apply to a vacant North Van lot?** No — the 3% Empty Homes Tax is City of Vancouver ONLY. Do not price it into a North Vancouver holding-cost estimate. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Lynnmour lot?** The settled program position: Lynnmour secondary = Windsor Secondary, never Sutherland (SD44, 6/6 locator runs). SD44 catchments are address-based — run the exact lot address with SD44 before relying on any catchment. https://queenmary.sd44.ca/
