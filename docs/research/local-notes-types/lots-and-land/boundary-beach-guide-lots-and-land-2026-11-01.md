## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Bylaw No. 8400 (2024) — consolidated, NO lettered schedules.** The Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the previous Bylaw No. 3950; "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw No. 8400" is an ERROR wherever met. Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1). https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted** (City sheets cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title reads "8400, 2025"). Zoning is parcel by parcel on the City zoning maps.
- **SSMUH — Delta Bylaws 8394 and 8395 (June 24, 2024):** up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots; the City's Housing Accelerator initiatives include help for owners creating accessory dwelling units such as coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites. Added units are not subdivision and do not create new lots. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing — HELD UNCONFIRMED, process documented instead:** the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards number (8288, 2024 adoption) is NOT asserted in this file — sibling governing-terms assertions of 8288 are conflicts, never followed. Subdivision runs through the City's subdivision/servicing standards under the Local Government Act, confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/boundary-beach-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md
- **Hazard Development Permit Area SD3 (Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands), OCP Bylaw 8400 Appendix B — the core land diligence on this shore:** no building with the underside of a wooden floor system or the top of a concrete slab for habitable areas lower than 2.9 metres GSC datum; minimum land surface elevation around construction 2.3 metres GSC datum; no building within 7.5 metres of the natural boundary of a tidal area. Whether a specific parcel sits inside SD3 is a parcel-level check: UNVERIFIED for any individual lot here. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Dike / foreshore:** the Boundary Bay shore sits behind the Boundary Bay dike; the City of Delta is the diking authority and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure. The Province owns nearly all saltwater foreshore; tenure is by lease or licence only via FrontCounter BC, and private moorage is held under General Permission or Specific Permission. http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/crown-land-water/crown-land/crown-land-uses/residential-uses/private-moorage
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists — Delta farm-edge parcels):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (strata lots in the feed, Monterra included):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% on outstanding balances after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,200,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,200,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. At $3,500,000 the full tier math including the further 2% is $93,000; a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3,500,000 — that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged here and never silently carried. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a $1.2M lot?** $22,000 with no exemption — $2,000 at 1% on the first $200,000 plus $20,000 at 2% on the next $1,000,000. The FTHB GST rebate does not apply to bare land — it attaches to new housing. (§3.)
