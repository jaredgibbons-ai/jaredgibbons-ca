Secondary suites: all suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process; an annual secondary suite fee of $63 plus applicable utility user fees applies. The in-house suite size cap, any owner-occupancy rule, and the Zoning Bylaw Section 109 parking count for Mission: UNVERIFIED. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh

(~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/mission-bc-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any Mission West parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (for Archer Green-type built strata, and for a bare-land strata lot wherever one trades — none is verified on this ground):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $900,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $700,000 ($14,000) = **$16,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Mission West part of the City of Mission?** Yes — the Mission West board sub-area (Silverdale and the western rural fringe toward Stave Falls) sits inside the City, so City planning, zoning and taxes apply; Silverdale is defined in the City's OCP as the 3,440-acre Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area designated for urban growth. Rural character is not FVRD government. (§§1, 3 above)
- **Q: How did Mission West sell in September 2026?** On very small counts: 5 detached listings with 1 sold (a 20% sales ratio — the highest published detached ratio in Mission that month, but resting on a single sale) and 8 Condos & Townhomes listings with 0 sold. No sub-area median is published for Mission West, and Mission's community medians are not Mission West figures — and none of these figures is a land figure. (§1 above)
- **Q: What is the Silverdale Comprehensive Planning Area?** The City's 3,440-acre planning area in southwest Mission (sometimes known as Southwest Mission), designated in the OCP for urban growth and being planned through a Central Neighbourhood Plan and a Master Infrastructure Strategy — a plan in process, not lots for sale. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Can I rely on a Silverdale listing's density or timing claim?** No — claims like "2 to 10 units per acre" or population-doubling language are listing copy for that parcel only, UNVERIFIED as facts. Check the claim against the in-force OCP designation and zoning for the PID. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is the Silvermere Lake "lakefront" claim a right I can rely on?** No — "lakefront," boat-launch and shoreline language is listing text. Any specific property's dock, moorage, shoreline-tenure or water-access rights are UNVERIFIED on an official source; confirm on title before marketing water access. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are the Wilson Street park homes land purchases?** No — they are pad-tenure manufactured homes: you own the home, rent the site under the MHPTA, and confirm current pad rent and park rules from the park's own documents before writing. They are not counted as land in this file. (§2 above)
- **Q: When are property taxes due on a Mission West lot, and what is the penalty?** To the City of Mission: for 2026, due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a flat 10% penalty on current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026. The rural 5% + 5% calendar does not apply anywhere in Mission West. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite or extra units to a Mission West acreage home?** Suites must be registered with the City (building permit for a new suite; $63 annual suite fee plus utility fees; no suites in townhouses/rowhomes), and an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation and a possible decommission permit. SSMUH unit counts per lot are UNCONFIRMED in this file — confirm the lot's zone under Bylaw 5949-2020 with the City. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a lot purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($900,000, illustration only) the bill is $16,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which school serves a Mission West lot I build on?** Silverdale Elementary (29715 Donatelli Ave) is the Ministry-listed elementary school physically in the Silverdale side of this ground — but catchment assignment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED, with no locator run on record for this area. Check the exact address with SD75 before relying on it. (§1 above)
