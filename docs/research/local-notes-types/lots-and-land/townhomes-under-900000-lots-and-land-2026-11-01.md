## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

**Frames are kept separate in this section. A rule headed "Surrey" governs Surrey ground only; a rule headed "Langley City" governs Langley City ground only. Nothing here is a "regional" rule.**

- **Surrey frame — OCP / zoning:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan **Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — current; Bylaw No. 18020 is never cited as current. Zoning: Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000, read per parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Surrey frame — SSMUH / suites / coach houses:** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per dwelling unit, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area**, never inside a coach house or garden suite; coach/garden suites 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Surrey frame — subdivision / servicing:** zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, per parcel in COSMOS; the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830**, verified on the City's own Land Development / Servicing Agreement pages, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Surrey frame — streamside:** setbacks sit in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class**, with the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process alongside — no single riparian figure is stated for any Surrey parcel in this file. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Surrey frame — STR / taxes:** STRs permitted, owner-only, primary residence, under 90 consecutive days per patron, business licence **$358/yr** (conflict disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees). Surrey property taxes due July 2, 5% penalty after July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Langley City frame — zoning / suites:** Langley City is a separate municipality from both Surrey and the Township of Langley; its zoning is **Langley City Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300**, under which a secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot. Township of Langley rules (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020) do NOT govern a Langley City parcel, and Surrey's Bylaw No. 12000 does not either. No Langley City OCP number, subdivision/servicing bylaw number or tax due date is asserted in this file: UNCONFIRMED here, read from the City for the specific parcel. (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/townhomes-under-900000-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR block (provincial frame, applied only where a parcel IS in the ALR, in any municipality):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this band. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (built townhome lots in this band, and any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The research records a gated 55+ description for one feed complex in listing text only; the restriction itself is confirmed only from that complex's strata documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land (provincial, all frames):** 1% up to and including $200,000, 2% over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land, and on price alone every parcel in this band sits inside that threshold. Worked examples are in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Insured-mortgage context documented in the research (not a land rule):** CMHC states homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999 require 5% down on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion over $500,000, insurance is required under 20% down, and premium rates range from **0.6% to 4.5%** of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a list of vacant lots under $900,000 in this file?** No — this is a region-band file with no land roster of its own and no single city frame. Land status and the governing rules are UNCONFIRMED per parcel until its municipality, title and zone are read. (§2 above)
- **Q: Which city's rules govern a lot I find through this page?** The parcel's own municipality — Surrey rules for Surrey ground, Langley City rules for Langley City ground. Township of Langley rules govern neither, and no rule in §3 is a blended regional rule. (§3 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the cap?** At $900,000, fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 + 2% × $700,000 = **$16,000**, by the tier math shown in §2. At $500,000 it is $8,000. (§2 above)
- **Q: What does a fully qualifying first-time buyer pay at $800,000?** **$6,000** — exempt on the first $500,000, leaving the $300,000 portion above it taxed at 2% (the no-exemption bill at $800,000 is $14,000). Full exemption on price requires $835,000 or less; partial relief runs to $860,000. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Is a townhome lot the same as a bare-land strata lot?** No. A built townhome lot is a strata lot in a built complex; a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure on the lot itself — both carry Form B, fees and bylaws, and neither is fee-simple. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: Can I add a suite on a lot in this band?** It depends on the municipality, and the caps differ: Surrey caps a suite at 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area; Langley City caps it at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, with the registered owner residing on the lot. Read the parcel's own bylaw — do not carry one city's cap into the other. (§3 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a vacant lot I find?** At the CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt; business-course sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts are taxable examples. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)

- **Q: How did attached housing sell in September 2026 in the confirmed communities?** "Condos & Townhomes" combined medians: Surrey $512,944; Langley $611,000; Cloverdale $740,000 — whole-community attached figures, labelled context only; SnapStats publishes no band cut, no townhome-only split and no land category. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
