## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Bridgeport is Richmond's north riverfront area on Lulu Island, around Bridgeport Station on the Canada Line, River Road, Bridgeport Road and Great Canadian Way, across the North Arm of the Fraser River from Vancouver. The City plans this ground as "Bridgeport Village," the northern village of the City Centre Area Plan — City documents give the planning area as "City Centre (Bridgeport Village)"; "Bridgeport" on its own is not a City planning-area name. No street-by-street boundary for a "Bridgeport" sub-area is published on a City source read. Paragon (GVR) sub-area match and code for "Bridgeport RI": UNVERIFIED. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/bridgeport/map
- Character, stated openly: this is a heavily industrial and commercial area, not a conventional residential neighbourhood. The City Centre Area Plan describes Bridgeport as an industrial area in transition, with large and small parcels, an incomplete street grid and former rail alignments, and the plan prohibits residential use in the Urban Centre T5 and General Urban T4 (Areas A and B) designations that cover most of the village core. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf
- Nearby areas: Sea Island and YVR across the Middle Arm to the north-west, Brighouse / City Centre to the south along No. 3 Road, West Cambie and East Cambie to the south-east, and Vancouver across the North Arm to the north. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/station/bridgeport/map

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful residential bare-land market in Bridgeport. The village core is industrial and commercial land where residential use is prohibited by plan in the designations covering most of it; the only residential land fabric is the Bridgeport Road / Shell Road corridor to the south — established RS1/F house lots and one documented townhouse rezoning — plus the River Drive waterfront strip documented in the siblings as built strata product, not lots. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw 8500:** Richmond land use is governed by Zoning Bylaw 8500; Bridgeport Village land use is set by the City Centre Area Plan transects — Urban Centre T5 (residential prohibited; office, hotel, institutional, studio, retail and entertainment uses) and General Urban T4 with Industrial Reserve overlays (residential prohibited; light industry base use). Corridor homes sit in RS1/F with the RTM3 rezoning above. Confirm the parcel's zone and transect before pricing any use. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000:** the City Centre Area Plan is part of the Richmond OCP (Bylaw 9000), with the Bridgeport Village Specific Land Use Map amended by Bylaw 10190; the older Bridgeport Area Plan sits at Schedule 2.12 of the previous OCP, Bylaw 7100. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_4_-_Cary_Tsai_-_9100_9120_Bridgeport_Rd77457.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** Richmond's Bill 44 framework adds Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL to Bylaw 8500 — but SSMUH does not create residential rights inside the village core's residential-prohibited transects. Corridor-lot eligibility is parcel-specific; confirm with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED bylaws:** the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (verified, banked in the L3 Richmond batch). Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City for the specific lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood Control — Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204:** new construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under Bylaw 8204; Bridgeport Village sites are documented in Flood Construction Level Area A. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Airport overlays — diligence items the research documents:** the Aircraft Noise Sensitive Development (ANSD) Policy applies throughout Bridgeport Village; City data sheets place sites in Aircraft Noise Sensitive Use Areas 1B and 2; Bridgeport Station is a Bill 47 Transit-Oriented Area, and Federal Airport Zoning Regulation maximum heights govern over the TOA heights here. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Bridgeport ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Bridgeport lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Bridgeport-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for Bridgeport, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.
- **Richmond property taxes:** City of Richmond property taxes are due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant residential lot in Bridgeport?** No verified one — the village core prohibits residential use in its T5 and T4 (Areas A and B) designations, and the corridor trades as built house lots and strata product. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Why is the station area not full of condos?** Because the plan says so: Bridgeport Village is an industrial area in transition and its core transects prohibit residential use; the documented condos sit on the River Drive strip documented in the condos sibling. https://Richmond.Ca/__shared/assets/specific_map_transect_bridgeport23854.pdf
- **Q: Where are the actual homes?** On the Bridgeport Road corridor south of the village — RS1/F detached houses and the RTM3 townhouse rezoning at 10760/10780 Bridgeport Road and 3033/3091/3111 Shell Road — and on River Drive. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_2_Application_1082009BCLtd_BridgeportRd_PLN_07041850976.pdf
- **Q: Is North Gate in Bridgeport or Cambie?** Listings use East Cambie and West Cambie; the City rezoning file places the site in the Bridgeport Road corridor. This program follows the City file — search all three names or you will miss listings. (FILE STATUS; Section 2.)
- **Q: What do the airport rules do to a purchase here?** The ANSD Policy applies throughout the village, sites sit in Noise Areas 1B and 2, and federal airport height limits govern over the station TOA heights — work all three into disclosure and pricing before writing. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/_1_-_8320-8440_Bridgeport_Rd___8351_Sea_Island_Way_PH_07171747799.pdf
- **Q: What flood rules apply to a ground-oriented purchase?** Flood Construction Level Area A under Bylaw 8204, behind a 1:500-design dike system in a floodplain averaging 1 metre above sea level. Confirm the lot's flood construction level and dike setback with the City. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm
- **Q: Can a core industrial parcel be assembled for housing later?** Not on this file's evidence — residential use is prohibited in the covering designations, and the live applications documented (RZ 24-043066, RZ 13-628557) are commercial/application-stage files, never housing inventory. (Sections 2–3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a corridor home?** By exact address only — the SD38 locator result for this file's sample address (10760 Bridgeport Road) is UNVERIFIED because the locator was not run. Run the address in the SD38 locator; do not name a catchment from Tait or Talmey's district presence alone. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Is there an ADEU or district-energy charge on Bridgeport lots?** No position is carried here — ADEU applies ONLY in West Cambie/Alexandra and is never imported into this file. (FILE STATUS.)
