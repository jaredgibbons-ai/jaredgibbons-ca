### Worked examples — inputs stated, tier math shown (band discipline)

Band membership is worked per parcel price, never from a sub-area name. PTT only, on the Province's general tiers (1% on the first $200,000; 2% to $2,000,000; 3% above $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000 — no price in this band reaches the 3% tier): https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Input: $500,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $700,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $10,000 (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $800,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $12,000 (2% × $600,000) = **$14,000 PTT**. A fully qualifying first-time buyer at this price pays **$6,000** (the first $500,000 exempt, maximum saving $8,000).
- **Input: $900,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $14,000 (2% × $700,000) = **$16,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $1,000,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $16,000 (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $1,200,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $20,000 (2% × $1,000,000) = **$22,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $1,500,000 parcel, no exemption:** $2,000 + $26,000 (2% × $1,300,000) = **$28,000 PTT**.
- **Input: $1,800,000 parcel — the band ceiling itself, no exemption:** $2,000 + $32,000 (2% × $1,600,000) = **$34,000 PTT**.
