### Bare-land strata — its own form, and why it cannot arise on ALR farm ground

- **No bare-land strata plan on BC chicken-farm land was verified, and on ALR ground the zero is structural:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision and ALR subdivision requires ALC approval. Stated plainly for the form itself: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure (Form B / fees / bylaws apply to the lot); no such lot is asserted on a chicken-farm parcel in this file, and the feed's hobby acreages are fee-simple residential/hobby properties in their own card text, not strata lots. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The barns are farm structures in law, not strata buildings: under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, s. 5, a structure necessary for a farm use — stated for greater certainty to include a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation — is a designated farm use a local government enactment may regulate but not prohibit. A backyard coop on a hobby acreage is not that structure. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019

### Worked examples — inputs stated, tier math shown

A commercial chicken purchase mixes land, buildings, residence and possibly quota, and how PTT and GST are apportioned across that mix is UNVERIFIED in this file — so the examples below are arithmetic on a stated total only, not farm tax advice. General PTT tiers: 1% on the first $200,000; 2% to $2,000,000; 3% above $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Input: $1,500,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $26,000 (2% × $1,300,000) = **$28,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $1,800,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 + $32,000 (2% × $1,600,000) = **$34,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $2,000,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) = **$38,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $3,500,000 total, fully residential portion assumed for the further tier, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000 above $2,000,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 above $3,000,000) = **$93,000** on the stated total. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **ALR block (carried in full — ALR ground is central to this topic, province-wide):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local government or First Nation bylaws may further regulate or restrict residences and may not be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. **Farm class, three-tiered in full (province-wide):** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31 (late applications are not accepted), and a buyer who plans to continue farming must submit a General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase — a flat "$2,500+" figure is an ERROR, and no page stating a flat figure for this slug is documented in this file's research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
