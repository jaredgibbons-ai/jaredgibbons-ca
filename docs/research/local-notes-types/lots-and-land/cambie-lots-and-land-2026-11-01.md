## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market on the Cambie Corridor — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale lot offering was verified on any City or developer source. What trades as "land" here is the redevelopment value of built fee-simple house lots on the R1-1 side streets and of arterial and station-area parcels whose density is set by the Cambie Corridor Plan — most of which still requires a rezoning application, as the research states. The named projects below (Oakridge Park, Cambie Gardens, Claridge House, Chelsea) are built or building strata developments documented in the siblings — development sites in the corridor's history, never lot inventory.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plan / Zoning framework as the research states it:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) guides growth from 16th Avenue to the Fraser River, and the zoning instrument is the City of Vancouver Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 — corridor side streets sit under its R1-1 District Schedule (minimums in Section 2), arterial sites under C-2, and station-area parcels under corridor plan designations and CD-1 districts. There is no separate "Cambie" OCP — the corridor plan is the framework. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/cambie-corridor-plan.aspx and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **SSMUH / multiplex potential as the City states it:** under R1-1 a multiple dwelling may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units on a qualifying lot (minimum site areas 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by unit count, Section 2), and a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house — that multiplex potential is the side-street land value story, and it is parcel-specific. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0, and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements — expressly including bare-land strata proposals) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence:** the City's designated floodplain is a citywide dataset covering coastal and river floodplains, and the corridor's south foot runs down to the Fraser River at Marine Drive; whether a specific corridor parcel is in the designated floodplain: UNVERIFIED — check the parcel in the City dataset, with the sewer-separation program (Section 2) as the servicing companion check. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **False Creek South lease position — NOT this file's ground, never imported:** the 60-year City ground leases in False Creek South (expiring in the next 15–25 years; the Creekview co-op lease expired December 31, 2023 and is in overholding) belong to False Creek South ground on the south shore only. The corridor's north end touches False Creek at its top, but no False Creek South lease position is documented for a corridor parcel in this file's research, and none is carried here. (Program position, stated to prevent the import.)
- **Bare-land strata rules (no plan verified here — the BC strata block that WOULD apply in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003).
