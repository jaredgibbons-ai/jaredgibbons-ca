## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815):** adopted Jun 9, 2026 and in effect Jul 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742), which is never cited as current for Burnaby. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan:** adopted Dec 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP; it takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. Community plans are adopted by Council as policy guidelines for rezoning and development applications. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and a minimum lot area of 281 m²; permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. With two Expo Line stations framing this sub-area, the 400 m frequent-transit test will matter on more lots here than in most Burnaby sub-areas — but it is still a parcel-specific measurement, not an area-wide entitlement. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — Burnaby servicing-bylaw base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** the former servicing base number was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no confirmed current base number is quoted in this file; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's zoning (the R1 thresholds above) and servicing is City water and sewer under the utility frame in Section 2. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City of Burnaby for the specific parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Central Park bare-land strata is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Central Park ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no file in this group asserts it for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Central Park speculation/vacancy position is documented in this file's research — none is imported, even though Vancouver is directly across Boundary Road. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable in Burnaby. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes:** City of Burnaby property taxes for 2026 were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (and to an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Central Park the same as "Central"?** No — Central Park is the park-side sub-area on the Vancouver border west of Metrotown; "Central" is the separate Canada Way / Deer Lake civic sub-area to the north-east, with different stock and different numbers. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Central Park?** No verified one — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. The market is built houses on the park-side streets and strata product toward the stations. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How many homes can an R1 lot hold here?** 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, and 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; minimum lot width 10 m and minimum lot area 281 m² for SSMUH lots, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. With Patterson and Metrotown stations framing the area, measure the 400 m test on the actual parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Does Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax reach across Boundary Road?** No — the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. It does not apply to a Burnaby lot west of Metrotown, however close to the border the lot sits. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Are the Central Park House townhomes a bare-land strata I can buy land in?** No — they are a townhouse component inside a tower strata project (5987 Wilson Ave), counted in condos accounting in the siblings, not a bare-land strata and not a lot offering. The same applies to the Aldynne on the Park townhouse-form home on Patterson Ave. (Sibling files: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/central-park-bs-townhomes-2026-11-01.md and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-park-bs-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Which schools serve my lot?** UNVERIFIED for any specific address — no completed SD41 locator run for this sub-area is documented. The Province-listed schools in or near the area are Maywood Community Elementary, Chaffey-Burke Elementary and École Inman Elementary (French Immersion choice program, not a catchment), with Moscrop Secondary and Burnaby South Secondary the nearest secondaries on the list; run the exact address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: What plan governs a Kingsway-fronting lot's future?** The Burnaby 2050 OCP / Metrotown framework — Metrotown is Burnaby's downtown under the 2050 OCP, and that plan takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. Check it before assuming tower or assembly value. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Q: Which stations serve the area?** Patterson Station (Expo Line) at the park's north-east corner and Metrotown Station (Expo Line) east of the park. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/about-burnaby/town-centres
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does the Burnaby detached median price Central Park land?** No — the $1,825,000 figure is a community-level built-house median for all of Burnaby (September 2026, SnapStats); land has no SnapStats category and no Central Park sub-area median exists. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report)
