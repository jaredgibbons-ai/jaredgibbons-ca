## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Zoning — A-1 / A-2 naming UNVERIFIED on a City source.** The live page states Surrey A-1 / A-2 zoning applies to Cloverdale ALR parcels. No City of Surrey zoning page opened in this pass confirms the A-1 / A-2 zone names for these parcels: UNVERIFIED here — confirm a parcel's zone in the City's mapping / Zoning Bylaw 12000 before relying on it. https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-farms.html
- **ALR block — full frame.** (a) No stratification of ALR residences: ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1). (b) Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. (c) Principal residence: one per parcel without an ALC application, maximum total floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. (d) Suite: permitted in the ALR only inside the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. (e) Additional residence: one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and it does not need to be intended for farm help, provided local bylaws are met. (f) Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR (number, size, siting); building more than the permitted number, or larger than the size limits, requires ALC approval. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR short-term accommodation.** Whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; bed-and-breakfast use inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. The City's own short-term rental layer also applies off the reserve frame: owner-only, primary residence, licence $358/yr carried WITH the City's $350/yr plus fees proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form (Class 9).** To qualify, the farm operation must meet the minimum income threshold — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR pattern and is not used here; the live page's "$2,500 to $10,000+" summary is site copy and is not adopted. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-farms.html
- **Urban rules — for contrast only, they do not govern ALR parcels.** Surrey secondary suites on non-ALR urban lots: one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite. Surrey coach houses / garden suites on urban lots: 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), one extra parking space. SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024): 3 units under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 near a frequent bus stop — an urban-lot framework; whether a coach house can serve as the ALR "additional residence" on a given parcel is a parcel-level ALC + City check: UNVERIFIED as a general rule. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Off the ALR, Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities. On ALR land, ALC approval is the first gate (§3 ALR block). Lot-specific servicing costs and timelines for a farm parcel: UNCONFIRMED — where a number is unconfirmed, the process is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **BC strata block (form only — if a non-ALR bare-land strata lot is ever the purchase).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, unless an exemption applies. Anchors (inputs + tier math): $700,000 → **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,100,000 → **$20,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' and Newly Built Home exemptions turn on a qualifying home, not farmland alone — per-transaction advice required. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. A working-farm sale's GST position turns on the business facts of that sale — finer points: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; a farm parcel's position turns on its class and exemptions — confirm the declaration position per parcel. - **Q: The listing says "blueberry farm" — is that a confirmed operation?** No. Feed descriptions are listing descriptions, not verified farm-operation facts; this file presents no card as a confirmed farming operation in any named sector. - **Q: How big can a house be on ALR farmland in Cloverdale?** One principal residence per parcel without an ALC application, maximum total floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; anything larger requires ALC approval, and the City may regulate further. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a second residence permitted on a Cloverdale ALR acreage?** One additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and City bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a suite go in a barn or a detached building on a farm?** No — a secondary suite is permitted in the ALR only inside the principal residence; a detached second dwelling is the "additional residence," size-capped at 90 m² / 186 m². https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide a farm parcel?** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval — it is not a City-only process, and SSMUH density does not apply to ALR parcels. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income does the parcel need to keep farm class?** The full three tiers: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — applications by October 31. Check the parcel's own farm income against its tier before pricing it on farm-class tax treatment. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I rent the farmhouse short-term?** Not by right: whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval, on top of the City's owner-only licence rules. Bed-and-breakfast inside a lived-in principal residence is permitted within the ALC's four-bedroom / 30-day limits. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is the parcel on city water and sewer?** UNVERIFIED as a general picture in this research — the live page's wells / licences / septic description is site copy. Check servicing, water licence and septic for the specific parcel before subjects. (§2 above.)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Serpentine's empty residential table is a category effect, not an empty market.** Serpentine recorded 0 listings and 0 sales in both SnapStats residential splits in September 2026, while the live farm feed's visible listings were both Serpentine agri-business listings — a commercial category the residential table does not capture. Reading the 0 / 0 rows as "nothing trades there" is wrong. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report; https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-farms.html)
- **The 500 m² cap comes with a fill cap most buyers miss.** The ALC frame limits not just the principal residence's floor area but the fill placed for its construction — no more than 1,000 m² of fill. On lowland parcels where fill is how a building site is made, that second number can bind first. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The additional residence does not need to be for farm help — but the City still gets a say.** The ALC states the additional residence does not need to be intended for farm help where its conditions are met, provided local bylaws are met; local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences by number, size and siting. ALC permission is a ceiling, not a building permit. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **A hectare conversion that changes which cap applies.** The 90 m² / 186 m² additional-residence line turns on 40 hectares — 98.8 acres at the official conversion of 1 ha = 2.471 acres. A parcel described in acres near that line needs the conversion done before anyone quotes which cap applies. https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
