## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Edmonds has no verified bare-land market in the for-sale sense: no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision offered as lots, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. What actually trades as land here is (1) the fee-simple house lot on the interior streets, priced under a brand-new town-centre plan; (2) built strata product on assembled development land — Southgate City, documented in the siblings as homes, never re-counted here as lots; and (3) one large publicly owned development site at Kingsway and Edmonds, documented below as a proposal only — never inventory.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026.** Never cite the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 for a current Burnaby lot. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP and community plan — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; **the Edmonds Community Plan was adopted in March 2025** and plans the centre in six districts (Central Edmonds, South Edmonds, the Station District, West Edmonds, Northwest Edmonds, East Edmonds). A town-centre lot's value case is read off these two documents, in that order. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and http://www.city.burnaby.bc.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-04/Edmonds-Community-Plan.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Edmonds Station puts the station-side streets closest to the 400 m test — measure it for the parcel. Town-centre parcels outside the R1 district follow their plan designations instead; confirm the parcel's zone before applying the R1 ladder to it. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. The base bylaw's own number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source read for this file, so no number is asserted for it — the process above is what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Watercourse diligence:** Byrne Creek runs in a ravine through the area's south side (Byrne Creek Ravine Park; Ron McLean Park, 7820 Hedley Ave, at the south side). A floodplain designation for Edmonds' residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read; parcel-level hazard, watercourse-setback and ALR status are property-specific checks — for lots backing onto the ravine, check watercourse setbacks and development-permit conditions early, because setback surprises surface late in deals. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. This is the rule that decides GST on an assembly sold on by a developer in South Edmonds — at GI-003 level only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no position for this sub-area is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Edmonds, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. The 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33 (Section 2). Area-specific levy notes for Edmonds: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Edmonds?** No verified one — no bare-land subdivision offered as lots, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified. Land value here is carried by house lots on the interior streets and by assembled development sites, which are not retail lot inventory. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Hall Towers land I can buy into?** No — Hall Towers (7264 Kingsway) is 331 units of BC Housing non-market rental housing owned by the Provincial Rental Housing Corporation, EXCLUDED from every count. Its site is part of the Kingsway + Edmonds proposal, which would replace the 331 units without displacing tenants. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan
- **Q: What is actually proposed at Kingsway and Edmonds?** On the publicly owned 4.5-acre southwest corner: replacement of Hall Towers' 331 units without displacing tenants, plus over 1,400 purpose-built rental units and approximately 1,000 market strata units — second reading June 5, 2023, now under review to align with the Burnaby 2050 OCP. A proposal, not inventory. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/kingsway-edmonds-conceptual-master-plan
- **Q: Can I buy a lot in Southgate City?** No — Southgate City is a built/assembling master plan (52 acres; Azure completed 2024 as phase one) whose product is strata homes, documented in the condos/townhomes siblings. Later phases are plan-stage strata supply, not lot sales. (Sibling condos file, §2.)
- **Q: What can an interior house lot become?** Under the R1 SSMUH district in Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 on larger lots, 6 on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; multi-unit lots may be stratified. Town-centre parcels follow their Edmonds Community Plan district instead — confirm the zone first. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Which plan document prices my lot — the 2025 Edmonds plan or the 2050 OCP?** Both, in order: the Edmonds Community Plan (March 2025) sets the district, and the Burnaby 2050 OCP (December 9, 2025) takes precedence where the maps conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Q: What extra diligence does a Byrne Creek lot need?** Watercourse-setback and development-permit checks, early: the creek runs in a ravine through the south side, and no floodplain designation for the residential streets was confirmed in the research — parcel-level status is a specific check, UNVERIFIED until run. https://www.burnaby.ca/Things-To-Do/Explore-Outdoors/Parks.html
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does a lot purchase qualify for the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve an Edmonds lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run (7651 18th Ave → Edmonds Community Elementary → Byrne Creek Secondary) does not transfer to other streets; Twelfth Avenue and Taylor Park elementaries and Cariboo Hill Secondary also sit in or beside the sub-area. Run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
