## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No house-lot form and no meaningful bare-land market exist in Coal Harbour — stated plainly at the top, on the downtown-peninsula precedent. The research states expressly that single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form. What exists as "land" here is downtown development parcels under the Downtown District (DD) Schedule and the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan, already built out as strata towers — a parcel question is a development-permit and air-space question, not a lot purchase. No bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Downtown District (DD) Schedule, Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan, By-law 6754:** DD permits commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses in conformity with an Official Development Plan; the ODP states Coal Harbour should be an integral part of downtown Vancouver, and also covers the citywide Vancouver Plan context. There is no house-lot SSMUH ladder for DD parcels in the research — no SSMUH unit count is asserted for Coal Harbour parcels. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees under By-law No. 14538 (VERIFIED):** subdivision in Vancouver runs under Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0 (the class a downtown tower parcel would meet), and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Floodplain standards:** parcels in the designated floodplain (which includes the coastal floodplain) carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements — read any waterfront parcel and its building's insurance and strata documents against them. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Coal Harbour bare-land strata plan is verified; it governs the tower strata a parcel buyer actually meets):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw, and Vancouver separately licences short-term rentals); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Coal Harbour ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million — the top tier is routinely in play at Coal Harbour price points; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, VANCOUVER ONLY (applies here):** owners must submit an annual property status declaration (due February 3, 2026); the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any Coal Harbour purchase, where investor-held units are common. The EHT rate as the program states it for Vancouver is 3%. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes + additional school tax:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. At Coal Harbour price points the additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a house lot in Coal Harbour?** No — single detached houses are not a Coal Harbour housing form on the research, and no bare-land lot offering was verified. The purchase here is a strata unit (or, at parcel scale, a DD development site under the ODP), not a lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is Coal Harbour an official Vancouver neighbourhood?** Not as a City local area: Downtown is the official local area, Coal Harbour is a neighbourhood inside it with its own Official Development Plan (By-law 6754), and "Coal Harbour" is the Paragon sub-area name. It is not part of the West End, despite the City areas-page link quirk. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/areas-of-the-city.aspx
- **Q: What governs a Coal Harbour parcel?** The DD Schedule (By-law 3575) together with the Coal Harbour Official Development Plan (By-law 6754) — walkway continuity, open-space connections and the seven street-end view corridors are plan requirements, not sales language. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Q: What does a Vancouver subdivision cost in fees?** Under By-law 14538's Schedule F (in force January 1, 2026): Class I $174,300, Class II $87,200, Class III $15,020, Class V (Air Space, FSR over 3.0) $125,100, Class VI $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot — under Subdivision By-law No. 5208. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a Coal Harbour parcel or unit?** Yes — Vancouver's EHT is 3%, Vancouver only, with an annual property status declaration due February 3, 2026 and the 2025 tax payment due April 16, 2026 (5% penalty after). It applies here and in no other file in this batch. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Q: Is there a median Coal Harbour land or condo price to anchor to?** No figure is quoted: Vancouver is outside SnapStats coverage and no substitute source is used. Price from the specific building's or parcel's recent evidence instead. (Section 1.)
- **Q: Are the harbour views protected?** Partly, by plan: the ODP lists the street-end view corridors at Burrard, Thurlow, Bute, Jervis, Broughton, Nicola and Cardero Streets and requires the continuous waterfront walkway. A specific unit's or parcel's view is still a parcel-by-parcel check. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/ODP/odp-coal-harbour.pdf
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** SD39, address-based — the VSB confirms a new Coal Harbour elementary school was completed, but its street address and the 480 Broughton Street catchment result are UNVERIFIED, and Yaletown's and Crosstown's elementaries are not Coal Harbour schools. Run the exact address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
- **Q: Does the False Creek South lease expiry affect Coal Harbour land?** No — the 60-year City ground leases and the Creekview co-op overholding position are False Creek South ground only, across False Creek, and are never imported into Coal Harbour. No Coal Harbour leasehold position is documented in this file's research. (FILE STATUS.)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided parcel bought from a developer?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale; the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
