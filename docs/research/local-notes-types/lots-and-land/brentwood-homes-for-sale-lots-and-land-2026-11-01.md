## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815):** adopted Jun 9, 2026 and in effect Jul 1, 2026, replacing the former Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742), which is NEVER cited as current for Burnaby. The 2026 bylaw carries a dedicated Townhouse District (R2, with the R2r rental variant) alongside the R1 small-scale multi-unit district and apartment districts R3–R8. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2026-07/Zoning-Bylaw-Consolidated-Without-Schedules.pdf
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan:** adopted Dec 9, 2025, replacing the 1998 OCP; it takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict, and the Brentwood Town Centre framework (1996 Development Plan; 2013 Brentwood Site Conceptual Master Plan; amendments in consultation since Jan 29, 2024 — outcome UNVERIFIED) sits within it. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan and https://yourvoice.burnaby.ca/brentwood-town-centre-plan-amendments
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and a minimum lot area of 281 m²; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified, and secondary suites count toward the unit total and cannot be stratified. Near the town centre, the town-centre plan framework also applies — parcel-specific, confirm with the City. This slug's research file opened no Burnaby suite/SSMUH page; the R1 figures above are carried from the siblings' City sources, and any further Burnaby suite rule is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing — base number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** the former servicing base number was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024, and no confirmed current base number is quoted in this file; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's zoning (the R1 thresholds above), servicing is City water and sewer under the utility frame in §2, and parcel-level hazard/streamside checks gate edge lots. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City of Burnaby for the specific parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees and (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/brentwood-park-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Brentwood Park bare-land strata is verified):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. - **ALR block (for completeness — no Brentwood Park ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $1,500,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Burnaby property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to outstanding current taxes (or an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date, with a further 5% penalty after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan.
