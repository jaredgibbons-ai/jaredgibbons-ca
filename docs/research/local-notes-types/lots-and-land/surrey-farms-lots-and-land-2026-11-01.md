## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **ALR block — full frame.** (a) No stratification of ALR residences: ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1). (b) Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. (c) Principal residence: one per parcel without an ALC application, maximum total floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction. (d) Suite: permitted in the ALR only inside the principal residence; a separate or detached dwelling cannot be used as a secondary suite, and local bylaws must also permit suites. (e) Additional residence: one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application where its conditions are met — maximum 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less, 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha — and further residences beyond what is permitted can only be approved by the ALC where the residence is necessary for farm use. (f) Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR, and replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including ALC approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built. One hectare = 2.471 acres. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.nrcan.gc.ca/maps-tools-publications/tools/geodetic-reference-systems/converting-units-measurement/10798
- **ALR short-term accommodation.** Whole-house short-term rental of a principal residence is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification under the Assessment Act. The City layer also applies: owner-only, primary residence, licence $358/yr carried WITH the City's $350/yr plus fees proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** To qualify, the farm operation must meet the minimum income threshold — $2,500 if the total area of the farm operation is between 0.8 ha (1.98 acres) and 4 ha (10 acres); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 if the total area is less than 0.8 ha — and applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31, for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR pattern and is not used here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Urban rules — for contrast only, they do not govern ALR parcels.** Surrey SSMUH: a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — and the City's SSMUH page states the framework applies to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. SSMUH is an urban-lot framework; it is not a route to extra dwellings on ALR farm parcels, where the ALC rules above govern. Surrey secondary suites on urban lots: one per unit, at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Off the ALR, Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining off-site works, services and financial securities. On ALR land, ALC approval is the first gate (§3 ALR block). Lot-specific servicing costs and timelines for a farm parcel: UNCONFIRMED — where a number is unconfirmed, the process is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **BC strata block (form only — if a non-ALR bare-land strata lot is ever the purchase).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, unless an exemption applies. Anchors (inputs + tier math): $700,000 → **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,100,000 → **$20,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' and Newly Built Home exemptions turn on a qualifying home, not farmland alone — per-transaction advice required. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. A working-farm sale's GST position turns on the business facts of that sale — finer points: UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; a farm parcel's position turns on its class and exemptions — confirm the declaration position per parcel. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** No primary text was opened in this slug's research lineage for the vacant-land / farm-land position: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide) — farm-class treatment (§3) is the parcel-level item that changes the bill, not the calendar. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where are Surrey's farms?** There is no single Surrey farm belt in the sources used here: the live feed's three listings at reading sat in Sullivan Station (two, on the Colebrook side) and Fleetwood Tynehead (one), and the page's own related links point to Cloverdale Farms, South Surrey Farms and Port Kells Acreages as separate pockets. https://jaredgibbons.ca/surrey-farms.html

Production history, water, buildings and any board / quota position are proven from the seller's records for that parcel — and quota / board items are UNVERIFIED in this research lineage because no marketing-board source was opened. (§2 above.)
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land in Surrey?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Surrey farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it; anything beyond that requires an ALC application showing the residence is necessary for farm use. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: I want to live in the old house while I build the new one — allowed?** Only with ALC approval: replacing an existing principal residence has its own ALC conditions, including approval to live in the old residence while a new one is built; demolishing and removing the old residence first is the route that does not need it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I use SSMUH to add units to a farm parcel?** No — Surrey's SSMUH framework is for eligible urban lots under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary; on ALR land the ALC rules govern how many residences a parcel may have. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What income does the parcel need to keep farm class?** The full three tiers: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha) — applications by October 31, for the next assessment and tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Can I Airbnb a Surrey farm house?** Not as a whole-house rental by right: in the ALR, renting out the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence can be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property; and the City separately requires the host to be an owner living at the property as their primary residence, with a licence (figure conflict in §3). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
