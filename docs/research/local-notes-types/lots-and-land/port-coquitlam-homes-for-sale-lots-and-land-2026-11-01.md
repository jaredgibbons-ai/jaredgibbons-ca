## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 3838:** adopted in 2005 and substantially updated in 2013 to align with Metro Vancouver's Regional Growth Strategy; the City states it will undertake an OCP update to better align with Metro 2050 and provincial housing legislation, with 2024–2025 work largely in response to that legislation, including SSMUH accommodations and defined Transit Oriented Areas. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/reports-plans-publications/official-community-plan
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw, 2008, No. 3630:** the City's zoning bylaw. Its SSMUH amendment summary renames the RS zones "Residential Small-Scale," permits up to 4 dwelling units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, replaces "coach house" with "accessory dwelling unit" (ADU floor area limit 90 m², excluded from FAR), and sets the secondary suite cap at 40% of the floor area of the principal dwelling unit, with suites permitted in a house, duplex or triplex where units are vertically separated. **Whether that amendment package is the in-force text of Bylaw No. 3630 today is UNVERIFIED** — the summary read is a bylaw-amendment attachment, and the consolidated bylaw read carries the current secondary-suite definition (a second dwelling unit within a building containing a principal dwelling, forming a single real estate entity, not separately strata-subdividable) without the full SSMUH schedule; confirm the specific lot with the City. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157 and http://www.portcoquitlam.ca/media/file/3630-zoning-bylaw-2008pdf
- **Secondary-suite owner-occupancy rule for Port Coquitlam:** UNVERIFIED — no owner-occupancy clause was read in the City documents opened. **Short-term rentals in Port Coquitlam:** UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this pass. Flood status has to be checked lot by lot; it is not uniform across the low ground. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/our-government/capital-projects/cedar-drainage-pump-station-0
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no PoCo bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (ALR ground borders the city's east/north edges; parcel status UNVERIFIED per lot):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700,000 → $12,000; $900,000 → $16,000; $1.2 million → $22,000; $2.0 million → $38,000. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000; a fully qualifying first-time buyer at $800,000 pays $6,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Port Coquitlam property taxes:** 2026 taxes were due July 2, 2026 under Annual Tax Rates Bylaw, 2026, No. 4441; a 5% penalty applies to current taxes not paid before end of day July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty applies to current taxes outstanding after September 15, 2026; penalties are set by provincial legislation and the City cannot waive or modify them. https://www.portcoquitlam.ca/services/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does Port Coquitlam have SkyTrain?** No — its rail link is the West Coast Express: five weekday trains each way from Port Coquitlam Station (westbound 6:04–8:04 a.m.), no weekend or holiday service. A SkyTrain extension is an exploratory business case only (Mayors' Council Feb 19, 2026 work plan), with no funding, route or dates to price. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt997.pdf
- **Q: How many units can a Port Coquitlam house lot hold?** The City's SSMUH amendment summary for Zoning Bylaw No. 3630 frames up to 4 units on lots 281 m² to 4,050 m² and up to 6 units on lots 281 m² or larger within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop, an ADU capped at 90 m² and a secondary suite capped at 40% of the principal unit's floor area — but the in-force consolidated schedule is UNVERIFIED in this research, so confirm the specific lot with the City. https://pub-portcoquitlam.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=11157
- **Q: The feed shows the same Salisbury Avenue property twice — is that two lots?** No. The same property appears as both a residential and a land listing on the first page — de-duplicate before quoting any feed count, and diligence the single parcel's current bylaw position before repeating its redevelopment marketing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/port-coquitlam-homes-for-sale.html

- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale.
