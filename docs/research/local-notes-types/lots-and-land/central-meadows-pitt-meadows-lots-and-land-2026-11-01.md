## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Central Meadows is the Town Centre part of the City of Pitt Meadows, BC, in Metro Vancouver, on the traditional territory of q̓ic̓əy̓ (Katzie) First Nation; City Hall is at 12007 Harris Road. The City does not publish an official neighbourhood boundary or name for "Central Meadows" (a Paragon sub-area name; code UNVERIFIED) — the closest official planning name is the OCP Municipal Town Centre, covering the Harris Road corridor, the North Lougheed Area and the Civic Centre. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- Working geography from the Town Centre description: Harris Road as the main street (north–south), Lougheed Highway (Highway 7) at the north edge, and the Canadian Pacific (CPKC) rail line along Hammond Road. Nearby: Mid Meadows to the south-east, West Meadows to the west, North Meadows across Lougheed Highway to the north, and Maple Ridge to the east. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/city-hall/hearings/2020-11-05_-2869_2020_north_lougheed_ocp_amendment_bylaw.pdf
- Transit and commute: Pitt Meadows Station, on Harris Road in the urban core, gives West Coast Express service to Waterfront Station, Vancouver, Monday to Friday in peak periods — the City states the trip to downtown Vancouver is about 45 minutes; buses include route 701, route 791, the 719/722 circular service, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus stop at Harris Road. Golden Ears Way / Golden Ears Bridge to the east serves travel toward Langley and Surrey. Driving commute times: UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/city-services/transportation-infrastructure/transportation/bus-train-skytrain
- Civic core: Pitt Meadows Family Recreation Centre, 12027 Harris Road; Pitt Meadows Public Library, 200-12099 Harris Road (the city's only FVRL branch); sxwhekwnes (Spirit Square), 11985 Harris Road; Harris Road Park by Lougheed Highway. Shopping: Meadowvale Shopping Centre (Save-On-Foods, per the City's Commercial & Industrial Lands Analysis) in the Harris Road North commercial area, and Meadowtown Shopping Centre, 19800 Lougheed Highway (Real Canadian Superstore), at the north-east edge. Nearest hospital: Ridge Meadows Hospital, 11666 Laity Street, Maple Ridge. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/parks-recreation/parks-fields-facilities/parks-list/sxwhekwns-spirit-square and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/dmlive-155673-v1-gpra_report_to_pitt_meadows_20190405_v2_pdf.pdf
- Schools: SD42 catchments are address-based and SD42 has no middle-school tier — elementary K–7, secondary 8–12. The research file documents an SD42 School Locator result for the sample address 12007 Harris Road only: Pitt Meadows Elementary (K–7) and Pitt Meadows Secondary (8–12). Both schools are physically in the area — Pitt Meadows Elementary, 11941 Harris Road, and Pitt Meadows Secondary, 19438 116B Avenue, the only public secondary school in Pitt Meadows. That sample result does not transfer to other addresses. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/ and https://elementary.sd42.ca/pittmeadows/

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Central Meadows has no meaningful bare-land subdivision market — stated plainly at the top, on the city-assembly position. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified on an official or developer source in the research file or the siblings. What trades as land here is the fee-simple urban lot inside the containment boundary — usually carrying a house, priced with its R-3 / R-4 / R-6 multi-unit potential and its Town Centre designation — plus, at the north edge, the North Lougheed growth corridor documented below as a plan designation, never as inventory. Citywide, 78% of Pitt Meadows is in the Agricultural Land Reserve; the Town Centre core is the non-ALR urban side of that split.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only verified) form

- **Town Centre house lots:** detached houses on the residential streets under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones in Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, with multi-family buildings concentrated on Harris Road, where the OCP designates high and medium density multifamily and mixed-use commercial/residential development. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Lot character from the zones, as the bylaw states it:** RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha; R-3 / R-4 / R-6 minimum lot area **for subdivision** 1,215 m² (and minimum lot width for subdivision 12 m per the city-assembly file); RM-1 Multi-Family Residential 1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **The 1,215 m² clarification that changes lot math:** the 1,215 m² figure is a subdivision minimum ONLY — the City's SSMUH implementation update clarifies it does not apply to development on existing lots, so an existing smaller Town Centre lot can still be developed under R-3 / R-4 / R-6. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR, as documented:** the City's 2020 Business Plan states 78% of Pitt Meadows land falls within the ALR and agriculture remains the predominant land use citywide. ALR parcels inside the Town Centre core: per-address check only (research UNVERIFIED item) — no Town Centre ALR split is stated. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Servicing:** the research documents the diked, drained urban context (drainage Area 3 — Kennedy, including McKechnie — the urban development area serviced by ditches, culverts and storm sewers, discharging to the Alouette, Pitt and Fraser Rivers) and a separate drainage levy (Section 3). A parcel-level municipal water/sewer vs well/septic statement: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **Flood position:** over 95% of the city land base is in the Fraser / Pitt floodplain behind 60 km of dikes (City 2020 Business Plan — the governing figure in these notes; the live-page "over 85%" conflict is flagged at the top and in Section 5). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Market context, labelled correctly:** Pitt Meadows IS a SnapStats-covered community. September 2026 (SnapStats GVR): Central Meadows sub-area Detached 15 active / 4 sold (27% ratio) and "Condos & Townhomes" combined 32 / 5 (16%); Pitt Meadows community Detached median $1,160,000 and "Condos & Townhomes" combined median $662,500. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011, under the Official Community Plan "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," OCP Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022 (it repealed the former OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007); the North Lougheed Area Plan was adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/media/8989 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4, R-6 up to five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit; under provincial rules applied citywide, lots 280 m² or less allow up to 3 units, lots over 280 m² up to 4, and lots over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent transit bus stop up to 6; the City's SSMUH Zoning Amendment Bylaw No. 2986 was adopted June 25, 2024. The 1,215 m² figure applies to subdivision, not to development on existing lots (Section 2). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Suites:** one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 m², inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites one per lot at 33 to 90 m² in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — and a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the zone minimums in Section 2. A servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED (a fresh search for this batch found the City's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw named in City minutes, with an Amendment Bylaw No. 2833, 2019, but no base-bylaw number on a primary source, so no number is quoted); the process documented instead is zone minimums + SSMUH update + drainage/dike position below, confirmed with the City for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **Floodplain — the defining lot rule:** **over 95%** of the land base lies within the Fraser River and (to a smaller degree) Pitt River floodplain (City 2020 Business Plan — the figure these notes retire to), protected by a 60 km dike system, under Floodplain Designation and Construction Control Bylaw No. 2384, 2008 (per the city-assembly file); this area is in drainage Area 3 of the Pitt Meadows / Maple Ridge Diking District. LIVE CONFLICT: all five Pitt Meadows guide pages state "over 85%" (City Financial Plan 2026–2030 attribution) — flagged at the top, never silently resolved. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/node/163
- **ALR rules (78% of the city land base; Town Centre parcels per-address):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form, as carried in the city-assembly file ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Short-term rentals: PROHIBITED in Pitt Meadows** — a City staff report states "Short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Zoning Bylaw," and Pitt Meadows appears in the Province's principal-residence-requirement community list. A lot priced on STR income is priced on a use the City does not permit. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program (first $500,000 exempt at ≤$835,000, max $8,000, partial to $860,000) and Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Central Meadows lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY — it does NOT apply to Pitt Meadows. No Pitt Meadows speculation/vacancy position beyond that is documented in the research file; no rate is asserted here. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.

- **Property taxes and drainage levy:** under the 2026 Annual Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3047, 2026 (adopted May 5, 2026), Class 1 Residential rates per $1,000 are $2.8438 general municipal and $0.1082 drainage levy; taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 1, 2026. The drainage levy is a holding cost on every lot in this diked city. A 2026 flat utility fee amount: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2026-05/2026-Annual-Tax-Rates-Bylaw-No-3047%2C-2026.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a subdivision where I can just buy a lot in Central Meadows?** On this file's evidence, no — no named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades as individual fee-simple urban lots, usually with a house on them, priced on R-3/R-4/R-6 potential. (Section 2.)
- **Q: My Town Centre lot is smaller than 1,215 m². Can I still build the R-6 units?** The 1,215 m² figure applies to subdivision, not to development on existing lots, per the City's own SSMUH update — an existing smaller lot can still be developed under R-3/R-4/R-6. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q: How many units can a house lot actually carry?** Zone first (R-3: 3, R-4: 4, R-6: five, or six with an adaptable unit near frequent transit), then the provincial layer (3 units at ≤280 m², 4 over 280 m², 6 over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent transit stop). Confirm both layers for the parcel. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf
- **Q: Can I add both a secondary suite and a garden suite?** No — one secondary suite (33–90 m², inside the same building) or one garden suite per lot (33–90 m²) where the zone allows; a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Q: Is the Advent Road pocket part of Central Meadows or North Meadows?** Central Meadows, by board classification — current listings board-classify Meadowland Village (18960 Advent Road) and the Meadowlands co-op pocket as Central Meadows, not North Meadows, even though the street reads as a quiet residential pocket rather than Town Centre. Its townhome count home is the central-meadows townhomes file. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/central-meadows-pitt-meadows-condos-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Is my lot in the floodplain?** Almost certainly — **over 95%** of the city land base is floodplain behind 60 km of dikes (City 2020 Business Plan). Check the flood construction level under Bylaw No. 2384, 2008, any covenant on title, and insurance early; budget the separate drainage levy. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2022-03/pitt-meadows_business-plan_2020-web-full02.pdf
- **Q: Is the North Lougheed corridor a place to buy a lot now?** It is a plan designation (North Lougheed Area Plan, OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026), not an offering — no lot sale within it is verified in this file. Price any corridor parcel on its current zone and title, not on the plan alone. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/pitt-meadows-homes-for-sale-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §2.
- **Q: Can I buy a lot and run short-term rentals in a garden suite?** No — short-term rental accommodation is not a permitted use in Pitt Meadows at all. Price the lot on long-term residential use only. https://pub-pittmeadows.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=17699
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Meadows lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator sample in the research (12007 Harris Road → Pitt Meadows Elementary → Pitt Meadows Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD42 locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
