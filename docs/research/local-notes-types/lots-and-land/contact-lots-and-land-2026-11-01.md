## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

The rules below are the frame a land enquiry made through this page enters — the regulator's disclosure framework first, then the standard land-cost blocks a buyer asking through this page will meet in the relevant area file.

- **BC regulator framework (BCFSA):** in BC, a licensed real estate professional must give consumers information about commissions and fees, must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who represents another party. Disclosure happens at the outset of dealings; dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances. Until a client relationship is formed, an enquirer through this page is a non-client in that framework. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Listing-data notice the page carries:** the footer states the site's real estate data comes in part from the MLS® Reciprocity program of GVR, FVREB or CADREB; the boards assume no responsibility for accuracy; and the materials may not be reproduced without the boards' express written consent. CREA owns the MLS® and REALTOR® trademarks, and agents must be members of a local board and CREA to access the MLS® system. https://jaredgibbons.ca/contact.html and https://competition-bureau.canada.ca/en/how-we-foster-competition/promotion-and-advocacy/regulatory-adviceinterventions-competition-bureau/submission-ftc-and-doj-residential-real-estate-brokerage
- **Property transfer tax on land (the standard block, for a buyer who asks through this page):** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; anything finer for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Strata block — where a land enquiry turns out to be a bare-land strata lot:** the BC block applies to the LOT — Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. Which block governs a given enquiry is decided by the property's own area file, not this page. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
