## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans:** OCP Bylaw 2026 No. 3522 (adopted April 28, 2026, repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — never cite 2955 as current); Chapter 14 neighbourhood sections as in §1. Zoning Bylaw No. 2937: RS detached, RT/RM multi-residential, TC/CRM mixed-use at the centre. https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733 and https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **SSMUH position:** small-scale multi-unit housing, including coach houses and accessory dwelling units, is permitted in RS and RT zones **outside** the transit-oriented areas; the transit-oriented areas are **exempt** from the SSMUH rules — so a station-area lot's density comes from its OCP/zoning position, not from the provincial multiplex frame. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/housing/provincial-housing-legislation/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh/
- **Heritage controls:** Moody Centre and the Ioco townsite are heritage conservation areas — a heritage alteration permit can be required for new construction, reconstruction or changes on included properties, which affects any redevelopment of a heritage-area lot from day one. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **Riparian / slope diligence:** watercourse lots need a riparian assessment and Section 5.4 setbacks; environmentally sensitive and steep-slope development permit areas can apply on hillside lots. Whether a specific lot is in a permit area: UNVERIFIED per parcel. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/
- **Subdivision process:** subdivision runs through the City's development application process; no Port Moody subdivision or servicing bylaw number is asserted in this slug's research, so none is stated here — the process is documented, the number is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **Short-term rental rules in Port Moody: UNVERIFIED** — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in the research pass. (Research file above.)
- **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies to the city's strata stock and in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — read it with the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Port Moody ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
