## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Aberdeen has no meaningful named bare-land subdivision market. No named bare-land development, bare-land strata, or presale lot offering was verified in this area on an official or developer source. What trades here is individual fee-simple farm and rural-residential parcels — land first, house second. Any lot count, lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price would be invented, so none is stated.**

### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple farm and rural parcels — the default and effectively only form.** The housing stock is overwhelmingly single-detached houses on rural and agricultural lots; the area is rural in character with working farm parcels, and homes are effectively all freehold farm and rural residential lots. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014 and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** agricultural in scale. Subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha in the A1 zone and 16.0 ha in the A2 zone, and an additional farm dwelling is only contemplated on lots of at least 0.4 ha. These are zoning minimums, not a claim about any individual parcel's size — check the parcel's zone in the City WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** rural Aberdeen parcels are in or beside the Agricultural Land Reserve; no area-wide ALR percentage is published in the research, so no split figure is stated — ALR status is parcel-by-parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Servicing:** rural residential lots outside the urban boundary may use wells and onsite sewage rather than community water and sewer. Servicing for any single lot: confirm well record in GWELLS and the Fraser Health sewerage filing — never assume municipal services. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210 and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Market thinness, labelled correctly:** SnapStats recorded Aberdeen detached inventory at 25 with 3 sales (12% ratio) and attached inventory at 7 with 0 sales in September 2026. SnapStats has NO land category — it splits Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined — so no detached figure is presented here as a land figure; the numbers are community context only. Abbotsford-wide (community level) detached median was $949,000 in September 2026. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014**, including Section 200 agricultural zones. In Agricultural One Zone (A1) the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha; in Agricultural Two Zone (A2) it is 16.0 ha. A single parcel's zone is shown in the City WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT** (adopted December 2025). There is no Aberdeen neighbourhood plan. STALE-CITATION FLAG: the research file's "Bylaw No. 2600-2016" citation and any "2500-2016" citation are stale forms — flagged as a Jerry sweep item wherever a live page still carries them. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH:** June 2024 SSMUH adoption is City-confirmed, but the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" is asserted in some material and must NOT be stated as settled; the conflict is recorded here, not resolved. SSMUH applies to lots previously limited to single-detached/duplex use, which is not Aberdeen's agricultural zoning, so it does not create subdivision rights on A1/A2 ground. Abbotsford suite rules (Abbotsford ONLY): suite cap under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **Second dwellings on farm lots:** the City-hosted A1 section permits one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, to a maximum floor area of 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, plus one secondary suite per lot to a maximum of 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements beyond the zone minimums:** UNCONFIRMED in detail — no Aberdeen-specific subdivision/servicing standard beyond the A1/A2 minimums and the well/septic position in Section 2 was documented in the research; do not fill the gap from another city's rules. Confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **ALR rules:** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it; non-farm use and added dwellings need City zoning compliance plus, where the ALR Use Regulation requires it, ALC approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — there is no lawful bare-land-strata conversion of an ALR farm residence. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2M, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at values ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000, partial to $860,000); the Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000) is a new-HOME exemption — whether it reaches a bare-land purchase is UNCONFIRMED here; do not promise it on vacant land. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA source supports:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing into more than two parts; sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally taxable and an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt. Finer points for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — get a tax ruling/advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — land angle stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself — buying an Aberdeen parcel does not itself earn the rebate; it becomes relevant only if eligible new housing is built/bought under the program's own rules. The older New Housing Rebate (36%, max $6,300, $350K–$450K phase-out) is likewise a housing rebate. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer from an RRSP. FHSA: $8,000/year, $40,000 lifetime. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + purchase prohibition:** no Aberdeen-specific speculation-tax, vacancy-tax, or foreign-buyer position is documented in this area's research file, so none is imported from another city. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply here. Finer applicability for a specific buyer/parcel: UNCONFIRMED.
- **Airport diligence:** land covered by the Abbotsford Airport Zoning Regulations (AZR), SOR/83-253, shown on City drawing M-500, is subject to height limits (approach, outer, transitional surfaces), and a developer on AZR land must obtain NAV CANADA and Transport Canada approvals before the City issues a building permit — check before planning a tall shop or barn. https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/sor-83-253/FullText.html?wbdisable=true
- **Floodplain diligence:** Zoning Bylaw Schedule E sets floodplain boundaries; the floodplain status of any single Aberdeen lot must be checked for that lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **Property taxes:** City of Abbotsford taxes due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Bare-land strata block (for completeness — none verified in Aberdeen):** where a bare-land strata lot does exist in BC, it is strata tenure — Form B diligence applies, stratas with 5+ lots are on a 5-year depreciation-report cycle since July 1, 2024, a minimum 10% CRF contribution has applied since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws have been void since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions). No Aberdeen bare-land strata is verified, so this block governs no Aberdeen lot in Section 2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I subdivide my Aberdeen farm into smaller lots?** Almost never at urban scale: A1 subdivides at a minimum of 8.0 ha and A2 at 16.0 ha, and subdivision inside the ALR additionally requires ALC approval. Price "future subdivision" on the zone, not on frontage. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: Is the parcel in the ALR, and why does it matter?** Rural Aberdeen parcels are in or beside the ALR; ALR status controls subdivision, non-farm use and added dwellings, and ALR residences cannot be stratified. Check the specific parcel — status does not follow from the street address alone. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I add a second home for family or farm workers?** In A1, one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot (lot ≥0.4 ha; max 90 m² at ≤40 ha, 186 m² above 40 ha) under the ALR Use Regulation or ALC approval, plus one secondary suite per lot to 100 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Q: Is there city water and sewer?** Outside the urban boundary, lots may be on wells and onsite sewage instead. Pull the GWELLS well record and confirm the sewerage filing was made by an Authorized Person through Fraser Health before removing subjects. https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Does the airport limit what I can build?** If the lot is in the AZR area on drawing M-500, height is capped by the approach/outer/transitional surfaces and NAV CANADA + Transport Canada approvals precede a City permit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Q: Will I pay GST on vacant land?** It depends on the seller and the land's history: CRA states most individual personal-use sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivisions can be taxable. Confirm the vendor's position in the contract and get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes?** Only if the three-tier income test is met ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) with an application by October 31 — hobby acreage with no qualifying production does not qualify on acreage alone. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: Is the lot in a floodplain?** Check Zoning Bylaw Schedule E in the City WebMap for that lot; floodplain status is lot-specific and is UNVERIFIED for any lot until checked. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Q: What are the holding costs while I wait to build?** City taxes (due July 2, 2026, with 5% penalties July 3 and September 16, 2026), plus well/septic maintenance and farm-class compliance costs where applicable; no strata fees exist on a fee-simple parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a Bradner Road acreage?** Only the settled locator chain for 2975 Bradner Road is documented (Aberdeen Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary). Run your exact address in the SD34 locator. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The subdivision math is the whole story here.** An 8 ha A1 minimum and a 16 ha A2 minimum mean most Aberdeen "land value" is farm value plus a homesite — not development value. A listing that prices per-acre subdivision potential without naming the zone is skipping the deciding fact. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **There is no park, grocery store, or shopping centre verified inside Aberdeen** on an official source; the nearest verified facilities are Matsqui Recreation Centre (3106 Clearbrook Road), Clearbrook Park (3680 Clearbrook Road), Highstreet at Mt. Lehman Road, and the Clearbrook/South Fraser Way commercial area. Land buyers here are buying rural servicing and rural distance, knowingly. https://www.abbotsford.ca/parks-playgrounds
- **The airport file follows the land, not the house.** AZR height surfaces on drawing M-500 can cap a barn or shop even where zoning would allow the footprint — the approval sequence (NAV CANADA / Transport Canada before City permit) is the timeline risk. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **AgRefresh is the City's own framing of this ground:** the City's agriculture work addresses the A1 Uplands and A2 Lowlands patterns and the 8 ha / 16 ha minimums city-wide — useful primary context for why Aberdeen parcels stay large. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
