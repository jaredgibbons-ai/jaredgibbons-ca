## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 governs.** Parcel-level minimum lot sizes are set out in the bylaw itself, and rural zones such as RU16 carry a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices. Confirm the parcel's zone section before pricing any subdivision or second-dwelling potential. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026, adopted April 21, 2026** (settled program position), replacing and repealing Official Community Plan Bylaw 6350-2025, keeping the same vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction with a Regional Context Statement added, per the City's public hearing notice. The OCP carries Urban Residential and Suburban Residential designations for its housing areas, plus an Urban Residential Townhouse designation. The "June 1, 2026" date in live guides is the standing correction noted at the top of this file — not a defect asserted for this slug's page, whose own research does not document it. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/council-government/plans-strategies/official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / suites:** zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, per the City's SSMUH page; the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. Whether SSMUH unit counts apply to a given rural or acreage parcel in this band is per-parcel: UNCONFIRMED here. https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **Subdivision + servicing:** subdivision potential follows the parcel's zone minimums (RU16 at 1.6 ha in City notices for rural ground) plus City servicing; the band's acreage product sits outside the urban service core in the rural sub-areas — verify water, sewer/septic and road access parcel by parcel rather than assuming city services. Any servicing (development) bylaw number for Mission: UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research — the process documented here is zone + servicing confirmation with the City for the parcel, and no number is supplied in its place. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- **Flood and dyke:** the Fraser River floodplain runs along the city's south edge, and low areas such as Hatzic Prairie are protected by the Dewdney Dyke system; City tax rates also carry an additional diking levy on top of the municipal, school, regional district and BC Assessment levies. Confirm floodplain status, flood construction requirements and the levy for the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Planning~and~Land~Use/Area%20F%20-%20OCP%20Bylaw%20999%20-%20Hatzic%20Valley.pdf and https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes/tax-rates
- **Property taxes (City of Mission):** 2026 property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026, with a FLAT 10% penalty added to any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 framework as the City publishes it). This is a municipal regime — the rural 5% + 5% staged penalties (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11) apply only in FVRD electoral areas, not to City of Mission parcels in this band, however rural the parcel feels. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. Worked math at $1.8M / $2.0M / $3.5M is in §2. Anchors for reference: $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Exemptions do not reach this band:** the First-Time Home Buyers' Program (full exemption caps well below the floor) and the Newly Built Home Exemption (a new-HOME exemption, eliminated below this band's floor) — budget full PTT at this band; bare-land eligibility for any exemption is in any case UNCONFIRMED and none is netted in §2. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — and it phases out below this band's floor in any case. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal purchase prohibition:** no primary prohibition text was opened for this slug in the research — the position is UNCONFIRMED here and nothing is conformed from another file.
- **BC strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where one exists in the band — none is verified in this band, §2):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (applies where a band parcel is ALR — ALR land sits mainly on the city's rural edges, status parcel-specific on ALC mapping):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

The live page's feed displays its own count, which is a feed display, not a verified band statistic. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: What kind of Mission property sells over $1.8 million?** From the live feed's own listings: large custom detached homes and acreage estates — lakefront, hilltop and rural acreage product in sub-areas such as Mission West, Hatzic and Stave Falls — rather than attached product, whose community median is $430,000. https://jaredgibbons.ca/mission-homes-over-1-8m.html
- **Q: Is $1.8 million "luxury" in Mission, or just the top of a normal market?** The top tail: the September 2026 community medians are $814,000 detached and $430,000 attached, so the floor sits at more than double the typical detached sale price. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at $1.8M / $2M / $3.5M?** $34,000 / $38,000 / $93,000 (fully residential, worked in §2; the $3.5M figure includes the further 2% tier over $3M). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does any exemption or rebate help at this level?** No PTT exemption reaches the band, and the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land, and phases out by $1.5M in any case — budget full PTT and, on a developer lot sale, GST. (§3.)
- **Q: Is the acreage in this band on city services?** Not by assumption — the band's acreage product sits outside the urban service core in the rural sub-areas; verify water, sewer/septic and road access parcel by parcel. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/current-city-projects/rural-mission-strategy
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large rural parcel in the band?** Only as the parcel's zone allows — RU16 carries a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices — and ALR parcels need ALC approval. No subdivision outcome is promised from this file. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca
- **Q: Does Mission flood at the high end too?** The south edge is Fraser River floodplain and Hatzic Prairie sits behind the Dewdney Dyke system, and taxes carry a diking levy — flood status is property-specific and must be checked before buying. (§3.)
- **Q: Is GST due on a newly subdivided lot in this band?** Generally yes under CRA GI-003 (developer / newly subdivided = generally taxable). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves an over-$1.8M address?** SD75's School Locator by exact address — catchments follow the address, not the price; the only locator result documented in this research is the downtown sample in §1 (Mission Central Elementary → Heritage Park Middle School → Mission Senior Secondary). https://www.mpsd.ca
