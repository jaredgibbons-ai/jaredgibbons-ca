## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw, 2026, No. 21900 ("Surrey 2050") — CURRENT, adopted June 15, 2026.** The former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020 (PlanSurrey 2013), is superseded and must NEVER be cited as current; City corporate reports of June 2026 proceed on Bylaw No. 21900 as the adopted Surrey 2050 OCP and deal with closing out 18020 amendments. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning / SSMUH as the City states it:** Surrey's small-scale multi-unit housing framework applies to many house lots in the area. The live page summarises the City's R3 Urban Residential Zone (lots under 280 m² usually up to 3 units; lots from 280 m² to under 4,050 m² up to 4 units; eligible lots in Frequent Bus Stop Areas up to 6) — the City's zoning page itself was not re-read in this slug's research pass, so those zone-level figures are carried as the page's summary: UNVERIFIED at the City-source level here, confirm per parcel. Surrey also allows lock-off suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones in transit-oriented and frequent-bus areas — not every complex qualifies. https://jaredgibbons.ca/grandview-corners-condos-townhomes-for-sale.html
- **Secondary suite cap — Surrey: 40%.** The City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, and permits only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey, with a disclosed fee conflict:** only the property owner may host a short-term rental, the owner must reside at the property as their primary residence, and tenants cannot operate one; an owner must hold a City business licence before operating. The City's short-term rentals page states the licence fee is **$358 per year** (plus any applicable application fees) — that is the figure carried here. CONFLICT DISCLOSED: the City's 2024 corporate report on the bylaw amendments proposed a licence fee of **$350 per year** plus fees. The live City page's $358 governs as the current stated fee; the $350 report figure is recorded so the conflict is on the record, not hidden. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing:** the Surrey subdivision/servicing bylaw number was **not verified in this program — none is quoted here**. Subdivision and servicing for any Grandview parcel are per-parcel City checks (zone, GLUP/OCP designation, servicing and frontage requirements as the City applies them to that file), never a standard quoted from a listing or a neighbouring city's bylaw. (Program standing instruction for Surrey files, Nov 1, 2026)
- **ALR rules, where ALR ground is met (east portion of the GLUP area):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; a secondary suite only within the principal residence; one additional residence of 90 m² or less (parcels 40 ha or less) or 186 m² or less (parcels over 40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — three tiers, never a flat figure:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on an operation under 0.8 ha, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Strata block — the governing block for this page's stock:** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page, disclosing fees, levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with the May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Presales (REDMA):** where a presale exists (remaining developer sales in the counted complexes), the developer must deliver a disclosure statement, deposits are held in trust, and s. 21 gives the buyer 7 days from the later of signing or acknowledging the statement to rescind. That frame governs home presales here — it does not create any lot offering. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-time buyers' exemption: no tax on the first $500,000 (maximum saving $8,000) at $835,000 or less, phasing to $860,000; newly built home exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — both are home exemptions, and their application to a bare-land purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Finer points for any specific Grandview parcel: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, per CRA News No. 122) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
