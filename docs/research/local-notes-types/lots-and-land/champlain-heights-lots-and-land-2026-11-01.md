## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Champlain Heights — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. What trades as "land" here is tenure: most of the planned community sits on prepaid 99-year City leasehold, a small verified set of complexes holds freehold strata title, co-operative sites sit on City-leased land, and detached houses sit on fee-simple lots. A buyer who prices any of these as ordinary fee-simple land will be wrong.**

### Leasehold land — the majority form (not fee-simple, not bare-land strata)

- **The planned-community leasehold fabric:** the majority of Champlain Heights is leasehold property — pre-paid 99-year City of Vancouver leases expiring in the 2080s. A leasehold purchase is not a freehold purchase: there is no freehold title, and financing and resale turn on the remaining lease term and the lease terms. The research file records the lease terms for any given complex as UNVERIFIED in its pass — confirm tenure and lease terms (expiry, rent, prepaid vs monthly) on title for the specific property before pricing. A concrete listing example: a Marquette Crescent townhouse describes itself as "a City of Vancouver land lease property with an expiry of 2080." This is built lot fabric from the original planned build-out, not a lot offering. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champlain_Heights and https://storage.ubertor.com/cl6633/content/featuresheet_370_document/47910.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Co-operative sites on City-leased land — membership, not lots:** Cedar Mill Housing Co-operative is listed by the Co-operative Housing Federation of BC as located in the heart of Champlain Heights. Co-operative housing is membership housing — there is no strata Form B and no individual strata title or lot to buy. CHF BC records that the City developed Champlain Heights (with the south shore of False Creek) from 1974 to 1986 leasing rather than selling sites to non-market housing sponsors, and that Cedar Mill received Council approval for a new long-term lease in July 2025 — lease-renewal exposure that belongs to co-op membership, not to a land purchase. Alexander Laidlaw and Killarney Gardens co-operatives are listed under Killarney in the same directory; whether either sits inside the Champlain Heights pocket itself: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/ and https://www.chf.bc.ca/vancouver-lease-renewals-backgrounder-and-update/

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Tenure first (the local rule that overrides everything else here):** lead every purchase with a tenure check — freehold, strata, City leasehold and co-op are four different purchases on adjoining streets. A strata Form B exists only for a strata purchase; it is not the document set for a co-op or a leasehold interest. Lease terms (expiry, rent, prepaid vs monthly) change both financing and value. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-4/champlain-heights.md)
- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule + CD-1 (139):** R1-1 minimum site areas 306 m² (single detached house or duplex) / 464 m² (5-unit multiple dwelling) / 557 m² (6–8 units), with secondary suite and laneway house with a house and multiplexes to 6 units or 8 rental units as the schedule states; CD-1 (139) Champlain Heights South (By-law No. 5381) permits townhouse including stacked townhouses and apartments on its ground. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf and https://cd1-bylaws.vancouver.ca/cd-1(139).PDF
- **Plan — Killarney local area + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** Champlain Heights sits inside the Killarney local area; the East Fraser Lands ODP applies at Killarney's south edge, not inside Champlain Heights; a Champlain Heights-specific plan: UNVERIFIED; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/east-fraser-lands-river-district-documents-and-policies.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split must clear both the district schedule and the Subdivision By-law category for its block — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history, and no split of City leasehold ground is a fee-simple subdivision at all. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework here is the R1-1 schedule itself — house plus secondary suite and laneway house, or a multiplex to 6 strata units / 8 secured rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" on a Champlain Heights lot is a per-lot schedule check, not a separate overlay. CD-1 ground is governed by its own by-law, not by R1-1. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Strata diligence (the verified strata complexes — and any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw — Highgate's directory "rentals not allowed" line is VOID for long-term rentals on this basis, per the townhomes sibling); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Champlain Heights complex is published as 55+ in the sources checked. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Floodplain:** parcels in the City's designated floodplain carry Vancouver Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements; the floodplain covers the Fraser River coastal floodplain at the river's edge, south of the house streets. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED — check the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a new lot subdivision in Champlain Heights?** No verified one — the neighbourhood is a completed planned community. Land trades as built house lots, strata townhouse lots and leasehold homes, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What does "leasehold" mean for land here?** The buyer acquires the home on leased City land rather than freehold land — pre-paid 99-year leases expiring in the 2080s for the majority of the area (one Marquette Crescent listing states expiry 2080 expressly). Remaining term, rent structure and renewal position drive financing and value, and the lease terms for any given complex are UNVERIFIED until read on title. https://storage.ubertor.com/cl6633/content/featuresheet_370_document/47910.pdf
- **Q: Is Marine Woods really freehold?** Listing evidence says yes for the units described: an 8401 Millstone Street listing calls its home a "Rare Freehold Townhouse," a Cobblestone Avenue listing states "Freehold Strata," and a current 8416 Millstone Street listing confirms a strata with fees and a contingency fund (built 1994 in one listing, 1995 at another address — year UNCONFIRMED). "Rare" is the operative word — verify the specific unit's title rather than generalising. https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/listing/r2833579-8401-millstone-street-vancouver-bc-v5s-4s3 and https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/8416-millstone-street
- **Q: Is a co-op home a lot I can buy?** No. Cedar Mill Housing Co-operative, listed by CHF BC as located in the heart of Champlain Heights, is membership housing: occupants are co-op members, not strata lot owners, and there is no Form B or individual title. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: Can I split a fee-simple house lot into two?** Only if both resulting parcels clear the R1-1 schedule and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — and only fee-simple ground can be split this way at all; City leasehold ground follows the lease, not the subdivision by-law. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: What can a qualifying R1-1 lot hold?** A single detached house with a secondary suite and a laneway house, or a multiplex of up to 6 units (up to 8 rental units), with minimum site areas of 306 m² / 464 m² / 557 m² by unit count. CD-1 (139) ground follows its own by-law instead. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Is any of this bare-land strata?** None verified — the strata complexes here are conventional building-form strata, and the leasehold majority is not strata at all. If a bare-land strata lot were offered, it would still be strata tenure: Form B, strata fees and bylaws would apply to the lot itself. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Will the Empty Homes Tax catch a home I hold empty here?** It can — the tax is 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant, and every owner files an annual declaration. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Which schools serve a Champlain Heights lot?** By exact address only — Champlain Heights Elementary sits inside the neighbourhood and four elementaries sit inside Killarney, but no completed locator run is documented for this ground, so every catchment is UNVERIFIED. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
