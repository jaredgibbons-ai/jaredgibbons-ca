## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans:** the Cambie Corridor Plan (approved May 9, 2011; Phase 3 approved 2018) is the governing framework at the town centre — Phase 3 policy supports townhouse and low-rise forms on many streets within walking distance of Oakridge–41st Station, implemented through rezoning. Citywide direction now comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. Vancouver zoning runs under the Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 framework (per the siblings). https://www.cip-icu.ca/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Cambie-Corridor-Plan-FINAL-Aug-2011_WEB.pdf and https://council.vancouver.ca/20180417/documents/rr2_presentation.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees under By-law No. 14538 (VERIFIED frame):** subdivision of Vancouver land runs under Subdivision By-law No. 5208; its fee schedule sits in By-law No. 14538. Parcel-level subdivision requirements, approving-officer conditions and servicing standards are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED beyond the bylaw frame stated here. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Multiplex / subdivision potential off-corridor — R1-1:** for detached lots outside the corridor designations, the R1-1 multiplex rules apply: up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units under By-law No. 13817. Check which framework — corridor designation or R1-1 — governs the specific lot before pricing land value; the two routes price very differently. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground; the block also governs any small R1-1 strata formed on a house lot):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (a sub-5-lot strata sits UNDER that threshold — directly relevant to small multiplex stratas); minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%:** the City's Empty Homes Tax charges 3% of assessed value on homes declared or deemed empty, with the annual declaration due February 3, 2026 and payment due April 16, 2026. It applies to a vacant Oakridge lot-holding or second property held empty in this city — and it does NOT apply in any other municipality in this program. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance property taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property transfer tax — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $2,000,000 land-value house-lot purchase, fully residential, no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$2,000,000 ($36,000) = **$38,000**. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $800K cap→$14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $1.0M→$18,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000). A page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs; no such row is documented in this slug's own research, so the compression is stated here as the standing reading, not as a defect asserted against this slug. No $78,000 figure for $3.5M was met in this slug's research or siblings checked; where it appears elsewhere in the program it is an OPEN reconciliation item — follow $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
