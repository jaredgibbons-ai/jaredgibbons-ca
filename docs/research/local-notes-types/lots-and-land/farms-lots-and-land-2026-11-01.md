### Bare-land strata — its own form, and why it cannot arise on ALR farm ground

- **No bare-land strata plan on portal farm land was verified, and on ALR ground the zero is structural:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)) — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision and ALR subdivision requires ALC approval. Stated plainly for the form itself: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure (Form B / fees / bylaws apply to the lot); no such lot is asserted on a farm parcel in this file, and the feed's parcels and houses are fee-simple in their own card text, not strata lots. On non-ALR ground the ALC bar does not apply — but no bare-land strata farm offering was verified there either. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- The barns are farm structures in law, not strata buildings: under the Agricultural Land Reserve Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, s. 5, a structure necessary for a farm use — stated for greater certainty to include a structure for use in an intensive livestock operation — is a designated farm use a local government enactment may regulate but not prohibit. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/30_2019

### Worked examples — inputs stated, tier math shown

A farm purchase mixes land, buildings and residence, and how PTT and GST are apportioned across that mix is UNVERIFIED in this file — so the examples below are arithmetic on a stated total only, not farm tax advice. General PTT tiers: 1% on the first $200,000; 2% to $2,000,000; 3% above $2,000,000; plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

- **Input: $1,500,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $26,000 (2% × $1,300,000) = **$28,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $1,800,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 + $32,000 (2% × $1,600,000) = **$34,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $2,000,000 total, no exemption:** $2,000 + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) = **$38,000 PTT** on the stated total.
- **Input: $3,500,000 total, fully residential portion assumed for the further tier, no exemption:** $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $36,000 (2% × $1,800,000) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000 above $2,000,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 above $3,000,000) = **$93,000** on the stated total.
