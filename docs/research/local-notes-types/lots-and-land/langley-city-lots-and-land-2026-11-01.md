## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 governs the City; it repeals Zoning Bylaw 1996 No. 2100. **The bylaw is cited by NUMBER only — its adoption date is conflicted in sources and is UNVERIFIED in this file.** Minimum lot sizes (Bylaw 3300, as printed): R1 Suburban Residential — 557 m² / 16 m frontage; R2 Townhouse Residential — 1,000 m², except lots created by subdivision with a registered party wall agreement (maximum 280 m², minimum frontage 4.5 m); R3 Low Rise Residential — 1,000 m² / 26.0 m. Township Bylaw 2500 does NOT apply here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **SSMUH — City:** the City amended its Zoning Bylaw to permit small-scale multi-unit housing on RS1 and RS2 lots by Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024 — RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. This is the CITY's own SSMUH frame; the Township's Bylaw 6020 frame is different and is never imported here. (Research file above, §4, citing ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md.)
- **Suites — City:** a City secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling's gross floor area; the registered owner must reside on the lot; at least one extra off-street parking space is required; and the suite must be inspected, approved through a building permit and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Subdivision — City:** Bylaw 3300 states "No person shall subdivide land except in accordance with this bylaw"; subdivisions are subject to the City's Subdivision Control Bylaw and Development Cost Charge Bylaw; a lot created by subdivision must meet its zone's minimum lot area; and meeting the minimum standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve. The Subdivision Control Bylaw's number: UNVERIFIED. City DCC Bylaw 2024 No. 3256. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Short-term rentals — City:** UNVERIFIED — no City STR bylaw, licence requirement or principal-residence rule was read on an official City page in this program; confirm with the City before any STR claim. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, if met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 City lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own sources support it — the research encodes no prohibition position for City land, so none is asserted: UNCONFIRMED here. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. (Research file above.)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Langley City the same market as the Township for land?** No — the City is its own municipality with its own OCP (adopted November 22, 2021) and its own Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300; a Township fact applied to a City property is an error. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-city.html
- **Q: Is there a bare-land market in the City?** Not a meaningful one on this file's evidence: the City shelf is attached-led (195 attached vs 64 detached listings, Sep 2026), the research names no bare-land subdivision or bare-land strata development, and this file counts 0 new land developments. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report. (Research file above, §5.)
- **Q: How many units can a City single-family lot hold?** Under Bylaw No. 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024), RS1 and RS2 lots permit 4 units, and 6 units if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Q: What are the City's suite rules for an infill lot?** The lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, owner must reside on the lot, one extra off-street parking space, and the suite must be permitted, inspected and recorded in the City's secondary suites registry — under Bylaw 3300. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Q: Who approves a City subdivision, and do minimums guarantee it?** The Approving Officer — and Bylaw 3300 states expressly that meeting the minimum lot standards does not bind the Approving Officer to approve a subdivision. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/zoning-bylaw
- **Q: Are Parkway Estates, Parkway II and Glenmont Park land I can buy and build on?** No — they are the City's exactly-3 verified apartment-condo buildings, counted in the condos sibling; they are built condo stock, never land. ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-city-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Is Morningside Estates the same as Glenmont Park?** No — and they must never be merged. Glenmont Park is 5375 205 St, verified, in the exactly-3 roster. Morningside Estates is the neighbouring 5365 205 St address, held UNCONFIRMED in every sibling. ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/langley-city-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: How much PTT on a $700,000 City lot?** $12,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $500,000 ($10,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant City lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves a City lot?** SD35 School Locator by exact parcel address — catchments follow the address; no catchment is asserted here because no Langley City locator run is on record in this program. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
