## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026** — never cite the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 as current. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 SSMUH District in June 2024: R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop, and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replacing the 1998 OCP and taking precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict; Burnaby is organized into 20 Community Plan Areas under the 2050 OCP. The Edmonds Community Plan was officially adopted in March 2025 for the town-centre part of the east side. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Burnaby's servicing-bylaw BASE number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program (renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024) — no base number is asserted here. The documented process: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates. Confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- **Habitat diligence:** over half of Robert Burnaby Park is critical wildlife habitat and restoration work runs through it — for park- and creek-edge lots, check watercourse setbacks and the restoration project's trail closures early. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/ecosystem-restoration-robert-burnaby
- **Bare-land strata rules (applies to any bare-land strata lot, including a future stratified R1 multi-unit lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no East Burnaby ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an East Burnaby lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no East Burnaby-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for East Burnaby, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan (about $61 for the average home). 2026 detached single-family water charge $652.33, with a 5% discount if paid by the due date. Confirm the parcel's district and transit distance with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite

- **Q: What checks does a Robert Burnaby Park-edge lot need?** Watercourse-setback and habitat checks, early — many creeks run through the park, over half of it is critical wildlife habitat, and restoration closures run through it; no floodplain designation for the residential streets was confirmed, so the position is parcel-specific. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/ecosystem-restoration-robert-burnaby
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established single-family homes on municipal water (2026 charge $652.33) with sewer as a parcel tax — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City before writing. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

- **Q: How thin is the attached data here?** Very: this row recorded 5 attached listings and 0 attached sales in September 2026, so price any attached purchase off the Burnaby attached median of $686,000 and the neighbouring Edmonds and Highgate rows, not this row alone. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
