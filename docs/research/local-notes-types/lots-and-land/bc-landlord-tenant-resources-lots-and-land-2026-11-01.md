## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Rent increases (RTB, as cited on the live page):** the 2026 rent increase limit for residential tenancies is 2.3%, and the published 2027 limit is 2.2%; rent can be increased only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7 showing the exact dollar amount and effective date; only the current year's limit can be used (no carry-forward, no rounding up); a larger increase requires an arbitrator application on form RTB-52 or the tenant's voluntary written agreement; a tenant does not have to pay an increase above the legal limit or given without proper notice. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Tenancy agreements and inspections (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a landlord must prepare a written agreement for every tenancy on the RTB-1 template or an equivalent containing all standard terms required by the Residential Tenancy Act, give the tenant a copy within 21 days of signing; landlord and tenant inspect together at move-in and move-out and complete the Condition Inspection Report (RTB-27), with at least 2 opportunities to inspect offered and form RTB-22 used when the tenant was unavailable. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Deposits (RTB, as cited on the live page):** a security deposit can be no more than half of the first month's rent, and a pet damage deposit the same cap no matter how many pets, with no pet damage deposit for certified guide or service dogs; once the landlord has the tenant's forwarding address in writing (form RTB-47), the deposits must be returned with any interest within 15 days if there are no issues; deposit interest for 2026 is 0% per the page, and year-by-year interest for earlier years is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Ending a tenancy — landlord's notices (RTB, as cited on the live page):** RTB-30 (10 Day Notice for Unpaid Rent or Utilities — tenant has 5 days to pay or dispute); RTB-33 (One Month Notice for Cause — 10 days to dispute); RTB-32L / RTB-32P (Three Month Notice for landlord's use / purchaser's use — these must be generated through the RTB web portal and a non-generated notice is legally unenforceable; 3 months' notice, 21 days to dispute, one month's rent compensation, the person moving in must live in the unit at least 12 months, and personal-occupancy notices are not allowed in some buildings with 5 or more rental units); RTB-29 (Four Month Notice for Demolition or Conversion — permits/approvals required, 30 days to dispute, one month's rent compensation). Eviction notices cannot be served by text message. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Buying or selling a tenanted property (RTB, as cited on the live page) — the rule that touches land title:** a landlord cannot end a tenancy just because they want to sell; on a sale the buyer becomes the new landlord and the tenancy continues on the same terms; showings and open houses need written notice to the tenant or the tenant's permission; the owner at the time the tenant moves out is responsible for returning the deposits, so buyer and seller may deal with deposits in their contract or at closing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Dispute resolution (RTB, as cited on the live page):** four processes — direct request, facilitation, participatory hearing, and expedited hearing (RTB tries to schedule within 12 days); monetary claims must generally be $35,000 or less; orders are enforced through the courts — orders of possession through the Supreme Court of BC and monetary orders through Small Claims Court. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **MHPTA pad-rent block — regime boundary only:** pad rent for a manufactured home site tenancy is governed by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act, not the rates above; the limits are Act percentages ONLY — 2026 = 2.3% plus a proportional amount for changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees; 2027 = 2.2% plus the proportional amount — on 3 full months' notice, once every 12 months. **No pad-rent dollar figure is published as fact in this file.** Where the MHPTA speaks of the decision-maker, the settled program wording is "an order of the director." MHPTA rules beyond the pad-rent limits and that wording: UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Strata block (where a tenanted unit is a strata lot, incl. a bare-land strata lot):** rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math, general tiers only):** on a $800,000 tenanted-property purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** ($6,000 under the FTHB position at that value per the program anchors). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. Anchors: $500K→$8,000; $1.2M→$22,000; $1.5M→$28,000; $1.8M→$34,000; $2.0M→$38,000; $3.5M→$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier (a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M — OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only (kept distinct from tenanted-property sales):** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. The FTHB GST rebate is new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031); New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does this page list land or lots for sale?** No — it is a tenancy-law resource page with no listing feed and no land roster; §2 states that plainly. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: How much can rent be increased in BC in 2026?** Up to 2.3% for a residential tenancy (published 2027 limit 2.2%), only once every 12 months, on at least 3 full months' written notice on form RTB-7; anything above the limit needs an RTB-52 arbitrator application or the tenant's voluntary written agreement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: How much can a landlord charge for deposits?** A security deposit of no more than half the first month's rent, and a pet damage deposit of no more than half the first month's rent regardless of the number of pets; once the landlord has the tenant's forwarding address in writing, the deposits (with any interest) must be returned within 15 days if there are no issues. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can a landlord end a tenancy because the property is being sold?** No — the tenancy continues and the buyer becomes the new landlord on the same terms. Only once all conditions of sale are met can a purchaser who intends to live in the unit (or have a close family member live there) ask the seller in writing to give a Three Month Notice (RTB-32P), which must be generated through the RTB web portal. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Can I download an RTB-32P and serve it?** No — RTB-32L and RTB-32P must be generated through the RTB web portal, and a non-generated notice is legally unenforceable; budget the full 3 months and the one month's rent compensation. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: What form starts a tenancy, and when must the tenant get a copy?** Form RTB-1 (or an equivalent with all required standard terms); the landlord must give the tenant a copy within 21 days of signing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Does the 2.3% limit also cap manufactured home pad rent?** Not on its own — a manufactured home site tenancy sits under the MHPTA, whose limit is 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026 and 2.2% plus the proportional amount for 2027, on 3 full months' notice once every 12 months, with no dollar figure stated here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Can a showing or open house of a tenanted property just go ahead?** No — showings and open houses need written notice to the tenant or the tenant's permission, per the page's RTB sourcing. (BCFSA Rule 5-10(3) is not asserted for this slug — its research does not document an open-house card for this page, and there are no open-house listings on the page to conflict.) https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Who returns the deposits when a tenanted property sells?** The owner at the time the tenant moves out — so buyer and seller should deal with the deposits (each no more than half the first month's rent) in their contract or at closing. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Q: Are the RTB form versions on the page current at rebuild time?** UNVERIFIED in this file — the RTB updates its forms, and currency is a per-form check against the official RTB Tenancy forms page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A non-generated purchaser's-use notice is unenforceable, not just irregular (RTB, as cited on the live page).** RTB-32L/32P generated outside the RTB web portal have no legal effect — the portal step is the diligence item buyers of tenanted land most often miss. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
- **Personal-occupancy notices are barred in some larger rental buildings (RTB, as cited on the live page).** Personal-occupancy notices are not allowed in some buildings with 5 or more rental units — a building-size check comes before any RTB-32P plan. https://jaredgibbons.ca/bc-landlord-tenant-resources.html
