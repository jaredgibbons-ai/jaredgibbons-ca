## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs** across the city; the City's A1/A2/A3 checklist is a summary handout, not the bylaw text. The agricultural zones' minimum lot sizes and permitted-use details under the bylaw are UNVERIFIED in this file (consolidated text not opened; fetch 429) — verify the parcel's exact zone and minimum with the City before pricing subdivision or a second dwelling. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf and https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
- **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged wherever met, resolved nowhere:** the Official Community Plan is cited in program material as both Bylaw 7060-2014 and Bylaw 7998-2024. This slug's research cites no OCP number, and neither number is asserted here; quote planning policy for a specific parcel from a current City document. (Program frame; research file §1.)
- **ALR block — full form:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence; one additional residence without an ALC application where its conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller and 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and City bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income (farm gate) if the total area of the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha — with applications submitted by October 31 for the following taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such flat line is asserted in this file, and no flat line is documented on this slug's live page in its research. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Flood plain check the City itself uses:** the City's A1–A3 checklist records flood plain status, minimum basement elevation and geotechnical items for agricultural properties — parcel-level floodplain status and flood construction levels for any specific farm parcel are UNVERIFIED in this file; ask those questions on any lowland parcel before subject removal. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/a1-a2-a3-agr-zoning-checklist202103081624103844
- **Water licensing:** non-domestic groundwater (e.g. irrigation) requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act before lawful use; domestic use is exempt. Confirm the licence position for any irrigating operation before writing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision of an ALR parcel additionally requires ALC approval (above); no Maple Ridge subdivision/servicing bylaw number is verified on a primary source in this file — any such number is held UNCONFIRMED and none is asserted; confirm requirements with the City for the parcel. Septic runs through the Sewerage System Regulation / Authorized Person route (§1). (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/maple-ridge-farms.md)
- **BC strata block — stated for completeness, governing no farm stock here:** if a strata lot were ever lawfully produced on non-ALR Maple Ridge ground, the block would apply to the LOT — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (before any exemption): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are HOME exemptions; their application to a bare farm-parcel purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What actually trades as a "Maple Ridge farm"?** Whole fee-simple parcels — lowland farms in the northwest and river flats, bench and upland acreages in Websters Corners, Whonnock, Thornhill and the northeast — under the A1/A2/A3 agricultural zones and ALC rules where the parcel is in the ALR. There is no lot product and no strata farm product. (§2 above)
- **Q: How much of Maple Ridge is in the ALR?** The Ministry's Agricultural Land Use Inventory records 3,787 hectares — 15% of the city's 24,881-hectare land area (2011 survey). The current total is UNVERIFIED; check a specific parcel on the ALC maps. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/farming-natural-resources-and-industry/agriculture-and-seafood/agricultural-land-and-environment/strengthening-farming/land-use-inventories/mapleridge2011_aluireport_complete.pdf
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land here?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), with a secondary suite only inside that residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence without an ALC application where the conditions are met — 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger parcels — and City bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a farm parcel later?** Subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and a strata plan cannot be deposited without the ALC's permission — a farm home cannot later be converted into strata lots and sold separately as of right (ALCA s. 21(1); SPA s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** Three tiers, never a flat figure: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — application due by October 31 for the following tax year. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

Farm-land sales have separate CRA treatment — the position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
