## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What actually trades as land on Sumas Prairie?** Fee-simple farm parcels and acreages — commercial farm land with barns, country home sites and rural-residential acreages on the prairie floor and its upland fringe. One of the five feed cards read in full was a bare farm parcel offered as Land. There is no strata land form here at all. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a prairie parcel?** Generally not further: subdivision minimums are 8.0 ha in A1 and 16.0 ha in A2 in the City's parcel-size work, and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval in any case. Confirm the parcel's zone and ALR status before pricing any split. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is my parcel in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address — the live page's "almost entirely in the ALR" line is UNVERIFIED as stated, and no parcel's status is carried in this file. If the parcel IS in the reserve, the §3 ALC frame governs: one principal residence ≤500 m², suite within it only, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can a farmhouse here be stratified into units?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). A prairie farmhouse stays a house on land under the ALC residence frame. (§3 above)
- **Q: What is being done about Sumas Prairie flooding?** The Sumas River Watershed Flood Mitigation Planning Initiative — the Province, three First Nations (Semá:th, Máthxwi, Leq'á:mel) and the Cities of Abbotsford and Chilliwack — was signed in April 2023 after the November 2021 floods; the Province has provided approximately $220 million for Abbotsford and Sumas Prairie since 2021, including $76.6 million for Barrowtown Pump Station upgrades, plus $3.3 million for the initiative's design and assessment work — with its studies still at various stages of completion. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can I add a coach house or second dwelling?** Two frames sit together: the City's suite cap (under 50% of the principal unit) and coach/garden cap (15% of the lot to a maximum of 100 m²) — Abbotsford's rules only — and, on ALR land, the ALC frame (suite only within the principal residence; additional detached residence capped at 90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size). Confirm the parcel's ALR status and zone first. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools are on the prairie itself?** Two SD34 schools, confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file: Semá:th Elementary School at 36321 Vye Rd and Barrowtown Elementary at 5137 Tolmie Rd. Catchments are address-based and no locator run for a Sumas Prairie address is on record in this program, so confirm the exact address with the district. (§1 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on a land purchase here?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($1,200,000, illustration only) the bill is $22,000 with no exemption. GST depends on the seller and the parcel's history at the CRA GI-003 level — farm-business sales generally sit on the taxable side; confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: When are Abbotsford property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** For 2026, due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. (§3 above)
