## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

**Township vs City frames are kept strictly separate in every bullet below — a rule stated for one municipality is never applied to the other.**

- **TOWNSHIP — plans + zoning:** Zoning Bylaw **2500**. The Willoughby Community Plan was Bylaw **1836** and is now **Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200** (adopted December 1, 2025); the Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws **6201/6202/6203 had first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY** — never describe them as adopted or as having replaced the plan. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/resources/social-sustainability/housing/Report-to-Council----Small-Scale,-Multi-Unit-Housing,-June-10,-2024.pdf (research file §4) and (condos sibling §5, standing correction)
- **TOWNSHIP — SSMUH:** implemented through Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 Amendment Bylaw No. **6020** (adopted November 18, 2024, per the siblings) — **NO 6-plex tier**: 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m². A page or research claim of a Township 6-plex tier is WRONG. Township **suite cap 120 m²**. Township **coach house = fee-simple s.102 unit** under Bylaw 2500, never strata. (Research file above; condos sibling §3; townhomes sibling §3.)
- **TOWNSHIP — R-CL small lots:** Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.403 (R-CL) = **220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area (no 355 m² figure)**. (Program standing position.)
- **TOWNSHIP — subdivision / servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw **5382 (2019), confirmed** (program servicing ledger). No per-lot servicing figure is stated in this slug's research and none is asserted. (Servicing ledger, program standing record.)
- **CITY — zoning + SSMUH:** City of Langley Zoning Bylaw **3300 by number — cite the number; its adoption date is UNVERIFIED (conflicted in sources)**. City SSMUH Bylaw **3284** (adopted June 17, 2024, per the siblings): RS1/RS2 permit 4 units, or 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. City **suite cap = the lesser of 100 m² and 40%** of gross floor area, with the registered owner required to reside on the lot. (Condos sibling §3; townhomes sibling §3; 55-plus sibling §3.)
- **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies to the 22 buildings' strata lots and in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — read it with the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The live page's own condo checklist — strata bylaws, the depreciation report, recent strata minutes, the Form B information certificate and the contingency reserve fund — is the diligence core for any Langley condo. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-condos-for-sale.html
- **ALR rules (carried for any Township parcel where reserve status is confirmed — no parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class figures are **not asserted in this file** — no farm-class tier is documented in this slug's own research; confirm farm status and thresholds with BC Assessment for a specific parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax (both municipalities — a provincial tax):** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → $2,000 + $14,000 = **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → $2,000 + $20,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
