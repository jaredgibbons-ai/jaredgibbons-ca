## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Altamont has no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no named strata or townhome complex, and no presale LOT offering was verified inside Altamont on any source checked — the research file and all three siblings record the same position. What trades as land here is the individual fee-simple hillside lot in the RS single-family zones, almost always with a detached house on it, bought for the lot, the view corridor and rebuild/suite potential. No lot count or lot price is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the only land form

- **RS hillside lot fabric:** detached houses on hillside lots in the RS single-family dwelling zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010. Section 200 states minimum site areas of 8,094 m² in RS1, 1,625 m² in RS2, 975 m² in RS3, 731 m² in RS4 and 488 m² in RS5, with RS6 using the area of the site of record. The RS range that can apply here runs the full 488–8,094 m² ladder, so subdivision and suite potential differ lot by lot — the zone and minimum for a specific lot are confirmed on the District's Zoning Map. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Suites as lot value:** Section 200 lists secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses in the single-family zones; District building-permit guidance addresses detached secondary suites (coach houses) as a permitted form on these lots. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata community name with an address inside Altamont was confirmed in the research or siblings: none found. Stated plainly for any strata lot form, if one ever surfaced: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B / strata fees / bylaws would apply to the lot (Section 3) — but no such stock is documented here.
- **Serviced vs unserviced:** District water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District — the serviced position for District lots as the District states it. Septic or well servicing by street is UNVERIFIED in the research (UNVERIFIED item 11) — no street-level well/septic claim is made, and a specific lot's services are confirmed with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **ALR:** parcel-level ALR status is UNVERIFIED in the research; no ALR split is stated for Altamont and none is invented. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/altamont.md)
- **Hazard-shaped land:** this hillside's buildable value is permit-shaped — Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area ground (within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface), watercourse setbacks (15 m from the top of a bank), and, on the lowest streets below the hillside, the Foreshore Development Permit Area. Gross lot area is not buildable area here (Section 3). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + OCP (District):** Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 (s.200 RS minimums in Sections 1–2; lot zone on the District Zoning Map) and Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. An Altamont-specific local area plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). White Rock's bylaws (Zoning 2024 No. 2506 / OCP 2017 No. 2220) belong to WHITE ROCK ONLY and are never imported here. https://westvancouver.ca/government-administration/major-projects/official-community-plan-review
- **SSMUH / ground-oriented density, as the bylaw states it:** 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop (Bylaw 4662, ss.620–630). Verify current infill zoning per property before marketing multiplex potential on the lower streets — the July 2025 provincial directive targeted the Dundarave-adjacent single-family/duplex areas, and the District's final adopted amendments for those streets are UNVERIFIED. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf and https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision runs against the s.200 zone minimum site area for the lot's RS zone, confirmed on the District Zoning Map. A District subdivision/servicing bylaw NUMBER could not be confirmed on a primary source — held UNCONFIRMED, consistent with the District-wide assembly file; servicing for District lots is District water/sewer, metered and billed quarterly. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard development permits — the hillside rules:** an **Environmental Development Permit** is required for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; a **Wildfire Hazard Development Permit** is required for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface — both can apply on Altamont's hillside and creek lots and can add geotechnical and permit cost to a rebuild. A **Foreshore Development Permit** is required for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or property at risk of coastal flooding), which can apply to the lowest streets below the hillside. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (no strata stock is documented in Altamont; stated for any strata lot form):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules:** no ALR ground is documented in Altamont (parcel status UNVERIFIED, Section 2), so no ALR rule is applied to an Altamont parcel here. Standing rules, for completeness only: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land + the additional school tax that actually bites here:** PTT general tiers 1% / 2% / 3% + further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are home-purchase exemptions as BC states them — bare-land eligibility for the newly built home exemption: UNCONFIRMED here. **Additional school tax (documented in this file's research):** at Altamont price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Budget for it in closing math where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an Altamont lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in Altamont?** No meaningful bare-land market is documented — no vacant-lot subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. "Land" here is an existing fee-simple RS hillside lot, usually with a house on it. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big are Altamont lots?** It depends entirely on the RS zone: minimum site areas run 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), RS6 by site of record. Confirm the specific lot's zone on the District Zoning Map before pricing subdivision or suite potential. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house?** Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are permitted uses in the single-family zones under s.200, subject to the bylaw's conditions — a standard feature of these lots' value. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: Can I put multiple dwellings on one lot?** The ground-oriented rules allow 4 dwellings per lot, or 6 within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop — and the Dundarave-adjacent density rules are in transition under the July 2025 provincial directive, with the District's final amendments for Altamont's lower streets UNVERIFIED. Verify per property. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **Q: What permits does a hillside rebuild trigger?** Possibly three: Wildfire Hazard DP (within 100 m of an undisturbed forest interface), Environmental DP (within 15 m of a watercourse bank top), and — on the lowest streets — a Foreshore DP. Check all three before subjects. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Is the lot serviced, or on a well/septic?** District water and sewer are metered and billed quarterly by the District; septic/well use by street is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm the specific lot's services with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Q: What extra tax applies at Altamont prices?** The Province's additional school tax where the residential assessment exceeds $3M: 0.2% on $3M–$4M and 0.4% above $4M for 2026, rising to 0.3% / 0.6% on January 1, 2027 — on top of District taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% + 5% penalties). https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Will GST apply to my lot purchase?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. Confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves an Altamont lot?** UNVERIFIED until the exact address is run in the SD45 locator — no official sample address inside Altamont exists in the research. SD45 has no middle tier (K–7 / 8–12). https://westvancouverschools.ca/admissions/student-admissions-guidelines/
- **Q: Is the big pink building below the hill a condo I can buy into?** No — Villa Maris is in Dundarave, not Altamont, and it is purpose-built single-owner rental per the District's 2024 Statement of Heritage Value. Nothing in the existing building can be bought. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/Villa%20Maris%20%28Pink%20Palace%29%20-%202222%20Bellevue%20Avenue.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"Altamont" is a page name, not a District name.** The District's OCP never names it — its structure is Ambleside Town Centre, Park Royal, and the Dundarave / Caulfeild Village / Horseshoe Bay village centres. A buyer searching District documents for "Altamont" zoning or boundaries will find nothing; the operative documents are the District-wide OCP 4985 and Zoning Bylaw 4662, read lot by lot. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/OCP%20Bylaw%204985%2C%202018%20-%20Community%20Wide%20Directions_January%2031%202022.pdf
- **The hillside's permit map matters more than its street map:** the wildfire-interface line (100 m), the watercourse line (15 m from a bank top) and the foreshore area on the lowest streets each re-price what a lot can actually be built into — two lots on the same street can sit in different permit regimes. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Altamont's lower streets are the ones the Province named:** the July 21, 2025 directive singled out the single-family/duplex areas adjacent to Dundarave — the ground directly below this hillside — for increased density by December 31, 2025, while the District's final adopted amendments for those streets remain UNVERIFIED in the research. https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2025HMA0062-000698
- **The seawalk below the hill is a centennial artifact:** the Centennial Seawalk was built in 1967 for Canada's centennial and runs ~1.7 km from Dundarave Park to Ambleside — the waterfront amenity that anchors the hillside's value is public parkland, not lot frontage. https://westvancouver.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails/dundarave-park
