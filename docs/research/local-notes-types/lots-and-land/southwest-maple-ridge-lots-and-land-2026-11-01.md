## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- "Southwest Maple Ridge" is a Paragon (MLS) sub-area name only; the City of Maple Ridge does not publish a boundary or a plan for a "Southwest Maple Ridge" neighbourhood under that name. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- The official planning frame is the Hammond Area Plan (OCP s. 10.5), covering Hammond in the southwest corner; the City's neighbourhoods page also lists Port Hammond and Port Haney in this part of the city. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- The Hammond Area Plan describes its area: "The Hammond neighbourhood is located in the southwest corner of Maple Ridge. It is bounded on the west by the City of Pitt Meadows and the Katzie First Nation, and on the south by the Fraser River; to the north are Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road commercial areas, and to the east is mainly single-family development." https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Silver Valley, which has its own City area plan, is a separate MLS sub-area in the northeast of Maple Ridge — it is not part of Southwest Maple Ridge. https://www.mapleridge.ca/explore-maple-ridge/our-neighbourhoods
- Nearby areas: Pitt Meadows to the west across the municipal boundary, West Central and the Town Centre to the east, Port Haney at the sub-area's east edge, and the Fraser River along the south. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- Main roads and transit: Lougheed Highway along the north side; Maple Meadows Station (20010 Dunn Ave) on the west side and Port Haney Station (22300 River Road) at the east edge, West Coast Express weekdays between Waterfront (downtown Vancouver) and Mission City; buses 743 and 744 loop through Hammond streets (207 Street, Lorne Avenue, Hammond), 701 runs along Dewdney Trunk Road, 791 along the north side, and the R3 Lougheed Hwy RapidBus runs on the Lougheed Highway corridor north of the area. https://www.translink.ca/schedules-and-maps/west-coast-express and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt744.pdf
- The Golden Ears Bridge at the area's west side connects Maple Ridge and Pitt Meadows to Langley and Surrey, and the City states Golden Ears Way will be expanded from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, with detailed design underway. https://www.translink.ca/plans-and-projects/projects/maintenance-and-upgrade-program/bridge-projects and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge
- Schools physically inside the sub-area: Hammond Elementary (K–7), 11520 203 Street; Maple Ridge Elementary (K–7), 20820 River Road; and Westview Secondary (8–12), 20905 Wicklund Avenue. SD42 has no middle tier: elementary K–7 and secondary 8–12. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/042 and https://www.sd42.ca/elementary-schools
- Catchments: address-based in SD42; the district locator was run for a Hammond sample address (20577 Lorne Avenue) and returned NO RESULT because the address falls in a gap between the locator's listed ranges for Lorne Avenue, so catchment schools for this sub-area are NOT confirmed — check any address in the SD42 School Locator. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- Parks: Hammond Park, 20577 Lorne Avenue, has benches, a playground, a sport court, a tennis court and a pickleball court; Hammond Community Park is the site of the Larry Walker and Hammond Stadium ballfields, Hammond Hall and the Hammond Outdoor Pool; the Maple Ridge Golf Course is at 20818 Golf Lane in Hammond. https://www.mapleridge.ca/Facilities/Facility/Details/Hammond-Park-58 and https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market documented for Southwest Maple Ridge. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified on an official, developer or strata source in the research. Land here trades, where it trades at all, as fee-simple house lots inside a built single-family area — not as a bare-land program. That honest-zero position is stated at the top, as it should be.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default and only documented form

- **Confirmed form:** freehold single-detached housing, with suites and garden suites as accessory uses in RS-1, and higher-density mixed-use forms enabled by the adopted Hammond Area Plan update near the plan area's commercial nodes. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **Lot character — RS-1 zone minimums, as stated:** the standard house zone is RS-1 (Single Detached Residential) under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605 — minimum lot area 668.0 m², minimum lot width 18.0 m, minimum lot depth 27.0 m, maximum lot coverage 40%; accessory uses include Secondary Suite Residential and Detached Garden Suite Residential. These are zone minimums for RS-1, not a claim that every Hammond lot is that size. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Housing pattern the lots carry:** Hammond's stock includes older single-detached houses around a historic commercial area, and the wider sub-area is mainly single-family development east of Hammond, under RS-1. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **Bare-land strata: none verified.** No bare-land strata plan inside this sub-area was confirmed in the research or in the sibling files that exist on disk for this slug (~/workspace/local-notes/condos/southwest-maple-ridge-condos-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/southwest-maple-ridge-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md; ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/southwest-maple-ridge-townhomes-2026-11-01.md). Servicing for any single vacant lot outside that statement: UNCONFIRMED parcel-by-parcel in this file. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 governs; RS-1 s. 605 minimums are in Section 2. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **OCP — CONTESTED citation, flagged, never resolved:** the OCP citation is contested (Bylaw 7060-2014 vs Bylaw 7998-2024, with an update adopted November 25, 2025 noted in the condos sibling). This file cites neither number as settled. The Hammond Area Plan is OCP s. 10.5, adopted, with a Hammond Development Permit Area (heritage) applying to properties within the Hammond Area Plan. https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan and ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/southwest-maple-ridge-condos-2026-11-01.md
- **Lougheed Transit Corridor — ADOPTED Jan 27, 2026:** the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was adopted January 27, 2026 — Official Community Plan Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021 was given third reading and adopted at the January 27, 2026 Council meeting, following the January 20, 2026 public hearing; the corridor runs from the City's western boundary to the Town Centre at 221 Street along Lougheed Highway and Dewdney Trunk Road, with growth directed around future Bus Rapid Transit stations at 203 Street, 207 Street, Laity Street and 216 Street. The City states the plan does not immediately change any property's zoning. Any earlier contrary reading in the research/program record (treating the corridor as still in process) is superseded by this adopted position. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP and https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **SSMUH / suites:** one secondary suite per lot is permitted inside the single detached building, maximum 40% of gross floor area, and not within the floodplain area; eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's small-scale multi-unit housing (SSMUH) bylaws. A suite is inside the principal building — it is not a separate lot. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites and https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the RS-1 minimums in Section 2 (668.0 m² / 18.0 m / 27.0 m) plus City servicing — a municipal servicing-bylaw number for Maple Ridge is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source in the research and is not asserted here; the process position documented is the zone minimums plus the escarpment servicing rules below. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel.
- **Escarpment rules:** the Fraser River Escarpment policy area (City Policies 6.23 and 6.24) is bounded by 207 Street to the west, 124 Avenue to the north, 224 Street to the east and the escarpment crest along the south; all drainage from new construction must connect to the City storm drainage system, properties within 300 m of the crest require a geotechnical report attached to a Section 219 covenant before a building permit, and properties within 100 m of the crest require a detailed geotechnical report addressing potential seismic slope failure; in the policy area septic fields are to be avoided and new residences require sanitary sewers. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Floodplain:** Hammond fronts the Fraser River to the south; the Fraser River flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR rules — general framework, no ALR ground documented in this sub-area:** where a parcel is in the ALR, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); BC Assessment farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. No parcel in this sub-area is documented as ALR in the research, so these rules are carried as the framework to apply only if a parcel check shows ALR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www.equitylawgroup.ca/news/closing-costs-bc-home-purchase
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Southwest Maple Ridge–specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is stated ONLY as the research supports it: the research documents no primary text on the prohibition for this ground, so its application to a lot here is UNCONFIRMED in this file.
- **Property taxes:** Maple Ridge property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies to any outstanding balance on July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% on September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there bare land for sale in Hammond / Southwest Maple Ridge?** No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. What trades is fee-simple house lots in a built single-family area under RS-1 — ask for the specific lot's zone and title rather than a "land program." (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big does a lot have to be to subdivide or build?** RS-1 minimums are 668.0 m² lot area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth, with maximum lot coverage 40% under Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605. Those are zone minimums, not a subdivision promise for a specific parcel. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: Can I put a suite or extra units on a lot here?** One secondary suite per lot inside the house (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain), and eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Can I have a rental suite on a floodplain lot?** No — Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties, and no habitable floor space may sit below the 6.7 m flood construction level on affected lots. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: What does the escarpment mean for building?** Lots within 300 m of the crest need a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant before a building permit; within 100 m, a detailed report on seismic slope failure; drainage must go to the City storm system, septic fields are to be avoided and new residences require sanitary sewers in the policy area. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Q: Is Val Maria a land purchase?** No. You own the manufactured home and rent the pad under "Leasehold not prepaid-NonStrata" ownership — there is no lot, no strata and no fee-simple land. https://www.sellvanhomes.ca/listing/r3098427-21163-lougheed-highway-52-maple-ridge-bc-v2x-2r4/brochure
- **Q: Did the Lougheed corridor plan rezone my lot?** No — the Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was adopted Jan 27, 2026 (Bylaw No. 7781-2021), but the City states the plan does not immediately change any property's zoning. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/maple-ridge-council-approves-plan-guide-transit-oriented-development-around-lougheed-highway
- **Q: Will GST apply on a vacant lot?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — confirm per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Hammond address?** Not confirmed — the SD42 locator returned NO RESULT for 20577 Lorne Avenue. The schools physically in the area (Hammond Elementary, Maple Ridge Elementary, Westview Secondary) are not a catchment answer; run the exact address. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: What are the tax-penalty dates if I hold land here?** Due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and an additional 5% September 2, 2026. https://mapleridge.ca/2329/Due-Dates-and-Penalties-Late-Payments

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **Hammond began as Port Hammond Junction.** The City records that the Hammond brothers, William and John, donated land to the CPR on the condition the first railway station in Maple Ridge be built in Hammond, and the junction served as a supply depot as the community grew around the station, yards and a cedar mill — the origin of the heritage stock and the Hammond Development Permit Area. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/hammond-1st-reading-report and https://www.mapleridge.ca/1754/Hammond-Area-Plan
- **The school locator has a hole on Lorne Avenue.** The SD42 locator returned no result at all for 20577 Lorne Avenue — the address falls between the locator's listed ranges — so a buyer can do everything right and still get no catchment answer from the tool; that gap is documented here rather than papered over with a nearest-school guess. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Two Hammond recreation projects went to an assent vote, outcome unconfirmed.** The Hammond Aquatics and Recreation Centre (Bylaw No. 8123-2026) and Hammond Multi-Use Community Park on the golf course site (Bylaw No. 8124-2026) were put to residents; whether the borrowing passed is UNCONFIRMED — do not price land here on either facility being built. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/what-residents-need-know-oct-17-assent-vote-three-recreation-ready-projects
- **Golden Ears Way is slated to double in width beside the sub-area.** The City states the road will expand from two lanes to four between the Highway 7 interchange and 210 Street, with detailed design underway — relevant to commute and to noise for lots near the corridor. https://www.mapleridge.ca/news/expansion-golden-ears-way-address-major-traffic-bottleneck-maple-ridge
- **No recreation centre or library branch sits in the southwest.** The Maple Ridge Leisure Centre is at 11925 Haney Place in the Town Centre to the east, and Maple Ridge Public Library is at 130 – 22470 Dewdney Trunk Road — land here is bought for the Hammond / river / station position, with a drive for those facilities. https://www.mapleridge.ca/parks-recreation/recreation-facilities/maple-ridge-leisure-centre and https://www.fvrl.bc.ca/locations/maple-ridge-public-library
