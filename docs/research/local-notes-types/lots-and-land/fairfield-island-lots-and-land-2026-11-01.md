## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning and OCP:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 applies, under the 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — including the Schedule B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island Neighbourhoods Plan and the Schedule D development permit areas listed in §1. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **SSMUH / suites / accessory dwellings (Chilliwack specifics):** the SSMUH amendment is ESTABLISHED — Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395 amended Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 and was adopted June 18, 2024, as recorded in s. 14's amendment record in the consolidated Zoning Bylaw 5000 (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, item 6). Suite/ADU figures confirmed in the same verification (item 7f, citing Zoning Bylaw 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04): a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04. Application to any specific parcel remains a per-parcel check.
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision of ALR land may require an ALC application (below); for other land the process, conditions and timeline are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. **The Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** — this area's research opened no City subdivision or servicing page, no number is asserted here, and no other city's number is borrowed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/fairfield-island.md)
- **ALR block — full form (the northern/eastern perimeter where ALR ground exists):** properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development applications, and the City directs owners to check ALR status on the City Webmap; an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment, with ALC applications referred to the City for review. Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm Home Plate: OCP Development Permit Area 4 exists to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and is not used here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — applies in full to a Fairfield Island bare-land strata lot (the §2 form):** Form B (SPA s. 59), provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024 (a bare-land strata's report covers the common property, not the houses on the lots — the documented 6-lot example in §2 sits above the five-lot threshold); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Pure freehold detached purchases have no Form B; diligence runs on title, permits and the flood/ALR checks instead. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Property transfer tax:** the Province's general rates are 1% on the first $200,000 of fair market value, 2% on the portion above $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% on the portion above $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion of value above $3,000,000. First-time-buyer and newly-built exemptions are HOME exemptions — a bare lot's eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
