## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; s.102 defines a coach house as a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. City of Langley parcels sit under the City's separate Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, which uses "carriage home" — the two frames are never blended, and the City detail in this file is queued site copy (§2). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Coach house tenure — Township s.102:** a coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under s.102 — **NEVER strata**. It cannot be sold separately from the parcel, and no Form B exists for it. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Suites — Township, with the LIVE CONFLICT flagged and resolved nowhere:** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at **120 m²** with no percentage cap. The Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states suites are NOT permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the current s.102 definition (July 2025) states a suite may be located in a townhouse or rowhouse — both documents are carried here with their dates; a buyer relying on either point should confirm with the Township. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at ≤280 m² or 4 units at 280–4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Properties that do not qualify are instead eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the SSMUH route and the suite/coach-house route are alternatives, not stackable permissions. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. The page's further R-CL(CH) s.406 detail is queued UNVERIFIED site copy (§2) and is not stated as a rule here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. A coach house does not subdivide its lot — creating a separate lot is a subdivision under this frame. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (where a property sits on ALR ground — parcel status UNVERIFIED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township of Langley?** Under Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.102, a coach house is a dwelling unit on the second storey of a detached garage only. Section 100's own text states no size cap for one, but whether a cap sits in the bylaw's individual zone sections is UNVERIFIED — which is why a ground-level cottage or barn loft marketed as a "coach house" needs its permit checked. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Is a coach house a strata unit I can buy on its own?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on the house's lot under s.102 — never strata, with no strata plan, no Form B and no separate title. You buy the whole parcel or nothing. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Is a carriage home the same as a coach house?** They describe similar buildings under different bylaws: the Township's Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 uses "coach house," while the City of Langley's Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 uses "carriage home" — the live page's detailed City limits are UNVERIFIED in this file because the City bylaw was not opened in this pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-houses-with-coach-houses-for-sale.html
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be in the Township of Langley?** Zoning Bylaw s.102, as amended, caps a Township secondary suite at 120 m² with no percentage cap; note the documented conflict — the Township's January 2025 guide says suites are not permitted in townhouses/rowhouses, while the July 2025 s.102 definition says they may be. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: Can I stack SSMUH units on top of a coach house?** Do not assume so: an eligible Township lot's SSMUH route (3–4 units under Bylaw No. 6020) and its suite/ADU route are alternatives governed by eligibility and zoning — and the page's R-CL(CH) "either/or, not both" detail is itself UNVERIFIED site copy.
