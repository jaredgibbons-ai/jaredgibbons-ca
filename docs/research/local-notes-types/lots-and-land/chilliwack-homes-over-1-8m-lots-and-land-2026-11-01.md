## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning and OCP:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 applies city-wide (s. 6 carries the farm zones), under the 2050 Official Community Plan, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — including Schedule B1 Agricultural Area Plan, Schedule B4 Eastern Hillsides and Schedule B5 Sardis. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

Suite/ADU figures confirmed in the program's rules-gaps verification (item 7f, citing Zoning Bylaw 5000, ss. 2, 4.06, 5.04): a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, one accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot, garden suites are capped at 100 m², and parking rules sit at s. 5.04.
- **Subdivision and servicing:** subdivision of ALR land may require an ALC application (below); for non-ALR land the process, conditions and timeline are confirmed with the City for the specific parcel. **The Chilliwack servicing-bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED** — this slug's research opened no City subdivision or servicing page, no number is asserted here, and no other city's number is borrowed. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-10/chilliwack-homes-over-1-8m.md)
- **ALR block — full form (the band's acreage and farm parcels):** properties in the Agricultural Land Reserve are subject to the Agricultural Land Commission Act and ALR regulations; ALC approval may be required before the City can approve related development, and an ALC application may be required for a new house, coach house or suite, for placing fill or removing soil, for non-farm uses, and for subdivision or a boundary adjustment; check ALR status on the City Webmap. Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area; a secondary suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm Home Plate: OCP Development Permit Area 4 exists to reduce the long-term impact of residential uses on agricultural land, and a Development Permit is required when the Zoning Bylaw's Farm Home Plate regulations are not met. Farm class (BC Assessment) in full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR and is not used here. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=3009 and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block — applies in full to any strata lot in the city, including any bare-land strata lot:** Form B (SPA s. 59), provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
