## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future" — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met** (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950; farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)"). http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future

- **SSMUH / infill — the band's real land rule:** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024. The City states its updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, with Housing Accelerator help for accessory dwelling units like coach houses, garden suites and secondary suites, plus planned parking reductions and pre-zoning of appropriate areas for townhouses or apartments. That is the official frame behind the page's infill section — whether any specific under-$1.8M lot qualifies for a given form is a parcel-level City check, and flood requirements can decide it. The City also reports 1,146 net new units achieved city-wide between October 1, 2023 and September 30, 2025 against the provincial Housing Target Order. http://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Subdivision and servicing standards — status stated precisely:** the Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is cited from civicweb / a January 2024 council report; **final adoption of Bylaw 8288 and the parcel-level servicing standards under it are UNCONFIRMED in this file** — confirm with the City before pricing any subdivision assumption on a band lot.
- **Property transfer tax — the band's worked math, tier by tier.** General PTT: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. (This band's research could not re-open the Province's general PTT page — 429 — so the tiers are carried here as the program's standard block; confirm the current page at offer time.) Worked anchors, showing the tier math: $700,000 → $2,000 + (2% × $500,000) = **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + (2% × $800,000) = **$18,000**; $1,100,000 → **$20,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + (2% × $1,300,000) = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + (2% × $1,600,000) = **$34,000**. Above the band, for reference: $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **First-time buyers' PTT program — mostly out of reach in this band, on the Province's own numbers:** the full exemption requires a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024), exempting the first $500,000 of the purchase price (maximum saving $8,000), with a partial exemption only above $835,000 and below $860,000, plus the program's personal and property conditions (principal residence, 0.5 hectares or smaller, only residential improvements). A detached Ladner home priced between $860,000 and $1,800,000 is therefore outside the program entirely — only the band's lowest tail, if any, could qualify. Do not budget a first-time exemption in this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific band purchase: UNCONFIRMED. Most band purchases are resale homes, where GST does not apply to the resale itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Bare-land strata block — stated for completeness, governing no band stock counted here:** where a bare-land strata lot does exist in BC, it is strata tenure — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block — stated for completeness, for any band parcel where ALR status is confirmed:** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; caps: principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm-class thresholds in the full three-tier form ($2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
