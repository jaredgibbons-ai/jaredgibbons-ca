## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Zoning + the documented page defect.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs. The live page states Clayton has many "RF-12/RF-SD small-lot and coach-house lots" — Surrey's SSMUH page records that the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones were replaced by the new R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD by R6 Semi-Detached in the July 8, 2024 zoning changes, so the page's zone names are pre-2024 names and should not be carried into rebuild copy as current zones. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://jaredgibbons.ca/cloverdale-bc.html
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** Only one secondary suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites.** All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access for a coach house; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing process.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a Cloverdale parcel: UNCONFIRMED — no City figure for this area is stated in the research; where a number is unconfirmed, the process above is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only: a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, and tenants cannot run one. Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's $350/yr plus fees proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase — form only; no Cloverdale instance verified).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR block (applies to Cloverdale's lowland ALR parcels).** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and one additional residence of 90 m² (parcels ≤40 ha) or 186 m² (parcels >40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** $2,500 (farm operation 0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31 for the next assessment and tax year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error pattern and is not used here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** PTT applies unless an exemption applies: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (inputs + tier math): $700,000 → $2,000 + 2% × $500,000 = **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,100,000 → **$20,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 (2% tier) + $45,000 (3% × $1,500,000) + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000** including the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home, not the lot alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Finer points per lot: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; residential owners file the annual declaration and a vacant parcel's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** No primary text was opened in this slug's research lineage for the vacant-land position: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide). https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Foreclosure note.** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure mapping is UNVERIFIED and none is asserted.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy in Cloverdale?** No bare-land subdivision is verified in the research — the purchase is an existing fee-simple house lot for rebuild/added units, an ALR-edge parcel (see the cloverdale-farms file), or a Town Centre plan-area lot. The eleven queued townhouse names in §2 are built strata product counted in the townhomes home file, not lots. (§2 above.)
- **Q: Is The Grove in Clayton the same as The Grove I saw in Walnut Grove?** No — the program queue states expressly that Clayton's The Grove is a DIFFERENT complex from Walnut Grove's condo The Grove. Neither is counted in this land file. (§2 queue note.)
- **Q: How many units can a Cloverdale lot hold?** 3 under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible 281 m²+ lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — subject to zone, servicing and, on ALR parcels, the ALC caps instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Can I add a coach house?** In zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes, yes — 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR), lane access required for a coach house on smaller R3–R6 lots, one extra parking space. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Q: The listing says "RF-12" — is that the lot's zone?** Not currently: RF-12 / RF-12C became R4 and RF-SD became R6 on July 8, 2024. Check the lot's current zone in Bylaw 12000 before quoting small-lot or coach-house potential. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What does the Town Centre plan do to a lot inside its boundary?** The plan area (60 Ave south, 175/175A St west, 64 Ave north, 180 St east, plus 18120 56 Ave; ~80.8 ha, 145 properties) is mid-update around the planned hospital / BC Cancer Centre, with City steps running to spring–summer 2027 — plan upside and plan uncertainty until adoption. The Fairgrounds are outside the plan, under a separate Special Study Area. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/cloverdale-land-use-plans/cloverdale-town-centre-plan
- **Q: Is an edge lot ALR, and what changes if it is?** Check the parcel: ALR parcels are capped at a 500 m² principal residence with any suite inside it and at most one small additional residence (90 m² / 186 m² by parcel size) — do not value an edge lot on urban SSMUH density. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Will GST or PTT relief help on a lot purchase?** GST turns on the seller/subdivision history (CRA GI-003, §3); PTT relief turns on building/occupying a qualifying home; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. None can be assumed on a lot alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will PTT be on a $1.2M lot?** $22,000 on the anchor math in §3 (1% × $200,000 + 2% × $1,000,000), before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a Cloverdale lot?** Ministry-listed schools confirmed for the community include Cloverdale Traditional (17857 56 Ave), A J McLellan Elementary (16545 61 Ave), George Greenaway Elementary (17285 61a Ave), Don Christian Elementary (6256 184 St), Martha Currie Elementary (5811 184 St), Sunrise Ridge Elementary (18690 60 Ave), Katzie Elementary (6887 194a St), Hazelgrove Elementary (7057 191 Street), Regent Road Elementary (18711 74 Ave), Lord Tweedsmuir Secondary (6151 180 St), Clayton Heights Secondary (7003 188 St) and École Salish Secondary (7278 184 St). Catchments are address-based and NO SD36 locator run for a Cloverdale address is on record — run the exact address in the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
