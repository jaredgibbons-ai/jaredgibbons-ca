## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Pad-tenure law (the governing frame for every home in §2):** written Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreements are required in a park, and the MHPTA covers most manufactured home tenancies. Pad-rent increases: only with 3 full months' written notice, only once every 12 months, only up to the yearly limit — **2026: 2.3% plus a proportional amount; 2027: 2.2% plus a proportional amount** (proportional amount = change in local government levies + change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of sites in the park; no rounding up, no carry-forward of unused amounts). These percentages are the ONLY pad-rent figures an official source gives. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Assignment on a sale (MHPTA s.28):** assignment permanently transfers the pad tenancy; it requires the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given), an order of the director — s.28(1)(b) says "an order of the director," NOT arbitrator — or authorization in the agreement. No consent fee can be charged; the buyer's pad rent stays the same and the next increase date does not reset. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Park closure / conversion (MHPTA s.42):** all necessary permits and approvals in place first, then 12 months' notice and $20,000 compensation on or before the effective date, with defined additional-compensation routes where the home cannot be moved or no site is available within a reasonable distance. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and certification:** a manufactured home sale/transfer is only effective if registered with the Manufactured Home Registry (lawyer/notary filing the signed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership); no sale without the original legible CSA decal (Z-240 / A-277); a lost label requires Technical Safety BC re-certification. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a park home:** assessed like other residential owners, but the assessed value excludes the land's value where the home is in a park not on reserve land, or on land owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Township frame (the feed's parks are Township ground):** Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024; 3 units at ≤280 m² / 4 units at 280–4,050 m² eligible lots; NO 6-plex tier); suite cap 120 m² (s.102); coach house = fee-simple second unit under s.102, never strata — live s.102 (July 2025) vs January 2025 guide conflict on townhouse suites flagged, not resolved; R-CL s.403 = 220 m² min / 464.5 m² max LOT area ("355 m²" is NOWHERE in the bylaw); Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. The manufactured-home-park zone name/rules in Bylaw 2500 are UNVERIFIED in this research — the park-zone section was not opened — so no park-zone rule is stated. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **City frame (kept separate; the page spans the City but the feed's parks are Township):** Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 by number (adoption UNVERIFIED); SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (RS1/RS2: 4 units, 6 within 400 m of a frequent bus stop); suite = lesser of 100 m² and 40%, owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4). City park-zone rules likewise UNVERIFIED here. https://www.langleycity.ca/media/file/bylaw-3284
- **Park rules are NOT strata bylaws:** no strata corporation, no Form B, no SPA age-bylaw analysis for a pad-rental purchase (the Pioneer Park co-op sets its own rules as a co-operative — get them in writing). **BC strata block, for any bare-land strata lot met elsewhere in Langley:** Form B diligence (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (for any Langley parcel where reserve status is confirmed — no feed park is documented as ALR in this research):** subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. **Worked examples (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → **$12,000**; $800,000 → **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% over $3M) = **$93,000**. A $78,000 figure for $3.5M is an OPEN reconciliation item where met in siblings — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. (No vacant land is sold through this feed.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs:** Bill C-4 rebate (Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000; Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does buying a home on this page buy me land?** No. In a park you own the home and rent the pad under the MHPTA; at Pioneer Park you own the home plus co-op shares. Neither is a lot purchase, and this file counts none of it as land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes
- **Q: How much is pad rent on a Langley mobile home?** No figure is published as fact here. No official source publishes a Langley pad-rent figure, average or range; sibling pages of the site carry two conflicting UNVERIFIED ranges (flagged in §5), neither adopted. Budget from the specific park's written site tenancy agreement — current rent, last increase date, and what the rent includes. (§3/§5.)
- **Q: How much can pad rent go up each year?** 2.3% plus a proportional amount for 2026; 2.2% plus a proportional amount for 2027 — once every 12 months, on 3 full months' notice. The proportional amount is park-specific; ask the park for its last calculation. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: Does the park raise pad rent when I buy the home?** No — on assignment under MHPTA s.28 the rent stays the same and the increase date does not reset; consent is required (or an order of the director) and no consent fee can be charged. (§3.)
- **Q: Which parks in this feed are verified 55+?** Three pad-rental parks — Cedar Creek Estates, Fernridge Park and Pineridge, all in Brookswood — plus the Pioneer Park co-operative in Aldergrove, all verified in the 55-plus siblings. Langley Grove Estates is expressly all-ages, and Forest Green Estates is an UNCONFIRMED candidate, never counted. (`~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-mobile-homes-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md`)
- **Q: What is different about Pioneer Park?** Tenure: it is a co-operative, not a pad-rental park — you buy the home plus co-op shares, must be accepted by the co-op, and the co-op sets its own occupancy rules. Confirm the current shares, fees and rule in writing with the co-op; figures in listings are that listing's copy. (§2.)
- **Q: A card says "Lease runs to 2072" — what does that mean for me?** It is a leasehold signal in one card that does not fit the standard pad frame; the tenure behind that unit is UNVERIFIED. Verify it against the tenancy agreement, park rules and title before offering — do not price it as a standard pad tenancy. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-mobile-homes.html
- **Q: What happens if the park is converted?** MHPTA s.42: permits first, 12 months' notice, $20,000 compensation, with defined additional routes. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/end-tenancy-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What must be checked before a park home can even be sold?** Its CSA label (Z-240 / A-277) — without the original legible decal, Technical Safety BC re-certification is required — and Manufactured Home Registry registration of the transfer. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are there statistics for Langley mobile homes?** No — SnapStats has no manufactured-home category. The Langley figures in §1 are all-residential context only and do not describe this feed's homes. (Research file above.)
