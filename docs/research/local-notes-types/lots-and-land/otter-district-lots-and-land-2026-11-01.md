## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; minimum lot sizes (s.110.1): RU-1 — 1.7 ha; RU-2 — 8.0 ha; RU-3 — 8.0 ha; SR-2 — 8,094 m²; SR-1 — 3,716 m². City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3–4 units only where eligibility is met — inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to both municipal water and sewer, lot not larger than 4,050 m²; **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. A rural Otter lot that fails those tests falls to one residential unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit, depending on zoning — the frame most rural Otter acreages fall under. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. (Urban-zone reference only; no R-CL ground is documented in this rural district.) https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **ALR block (the governing frame for much of this district — parcel status still parcel-specific):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — creating a bare-land strata on ALR land is itself a subdivision needing the same permission. ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha), where municipal bylaws also permit it. Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, if met outside the ALR):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Water and septic:** the provincial GWELLS registry holds recorded wells (older wells may not appear); domestic groundwater use needs no licence, while non-domestic use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act. A home not connected to a community sewer must use an onsite sewage system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed and installed by an Authorized Person (Fraser Health). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **PTT — worked examples (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,800,000 acreage purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,800,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. On a $3,500,000 purchase — 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + the further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. A townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for the $3.5M case — that figure omits tiers and is an OPEN reconciliation item; this file follows $93,000 and flags the sibling figure wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I subdivide an Otter District acreage?** Only through the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, to the zone minimums (RU-2 and RU-3 need 8.0 ha for a new lot; RU-1 needs 1.7 ha) — and ALR land also needs Agricultural Land Commission approval to subdivide. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Q: Can I build a second house on an ALR acreage here?** Within the ALC frame, one additional detached residence is permitted without an ALC application at up to 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), provided Township bylaws allow it; the principal residence is capped at 500 m² and a suite must be entirely inside the principal residence. Confirm the parcel's ALR status on the ALC maps first — parcel status is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify ALR land into bare-land strata lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)), and filing a strata plan on reserve land is itself a subdivision needing ALC permission. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put four homes on a rural Otter lot?** Almost certainly not on the urban terms: Township eligibility for 3–4 units requires Urban Containment Boundary location, municipal water and sewer, and a lot no larger than 4,050 m²; a rural lot failing those tests falls to one unit plus a suite or accessory unit depending on zoning. There is no 6-plex tier in Bylaw 6020. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: What is a coach house in the Township?** An accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage, under Bylaw 2500 s.102 — a fee-simple second unit, never strata. A ground-floor detached suite is a different accessory unit with different rules. (Research file above, §4.)
- **Q: Is a Langley Grove Estates park home a land purchase?** No — it is a manufactured home on pad rent in a pad-rental park: not owned land, not fee-simple, not strata. Keep it separate from the freehold acreages on the same page; they are different tenure, lending and resale markets. https://jaredgibbons.ca/otter-district.html
- **Q: Why does the page show 26 homes when the statistics show only 2 listings?** The two sources classify differently: the feed's 26 results include pad-rental park homes as well as freehold acreages, while the SnapStats rows count 2 detached and 0 attached listings for September 2026; neither number should be quoted as the other. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.8M Otter acreage?** $34,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,600,000 ($32,000). On $3.5M the full-tier figure is $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant acreage?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which school serves an Otter District parcel?** SD35 School Locator by exact parcel address — catchments follow the address and are UNVERIFIED for Otter District addresses in this program. Ministry-listed names only: the school at 27330 28 Ave is **Shortreed Community School**, not the guide's "Shortreed Community Elementary." https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
