## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / Zoning — Surrey (Grandview ground):** Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is current — never cite 18020 as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH — Surrey:** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; adopted July 8, 2024. This is the frame for fee-simple lots around Grandview — it does not re-zone a strata complex's own ground for an individual unit buyer. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites — Surrey:** secondary suite habitable floor area capped at 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Bylaw 12000. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** licence $358/yr, with the City's $350 proposal conflict disclosed. For a strata unit, the strata's own short-term rental bylaw position must also be checked — the live page states a strata may still have or pass a short-term rental bylaw. https://jaredgibbons.ca/enclave-at-grandview.html
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), verified on City pages. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** due July 2, 5% penalty after midnight July 2, further 5% after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **ALR block (carried for completeness — no ALR ground documented for this project):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (this project's units ARE strata):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ units since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. The 55-plus sibling records Enclave as NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** general tax; a qualifying first-time buyer exempts the first $500,000, saving $8,000, for a net **$6,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. No vacant land is offered by this project. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. A resale Enclave unit is not new housing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
