### Fee-simple land — the default form

- **Urban lots:** the detached stock across the urban part of the west side is fee-simple freehold under the Residential (RS) family of Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 — RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m², RS5 minimum interior lot 420 m², as stated in City rezoning reports. A bare lot in Clearbrook, Peardonville or Townline is a fee-simple parcel: no strata, no Form B, no strata fee; zoning, the OCP and any registered covenants or building scheme on title govern. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/
- **Rural and agricultural parcels (Mt. Lehman Road / YXX edge):** acreage parcels with houses occur on the rural west and southwest edges. The research file's zoning figures are agricultural in scale: Agricultural One Zone (A1) minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha and Agricultural Two Zone (A2) minimum 16.0 ha. Servicing is the dividing line: lots outside the urban boundary in rural residential zones may NOT be connected to the Community Water System or Community Sanitary Sewer System and use wells and onsite sewage instead — "serviced" west-side land and "rural" west-side land are different products at different diligence. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** the Agricultural Land Reserve covers rural parcels on the west and southwest edges, including land near Mt. Lehman Road and around YXX; the urban Clearbrook/Townline ground is non-ALR built subdivision land. The split is parcel-by-parcel, not street-by-street: whether a specific Mt. Lehman-side parcel is in the ALR is UNCONFIRMED until the ALC map and title are checked (Section 3 for what ALR status changes). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Airport overlay:** airport height rules apply to land inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area around YXX, and floodplain boundaries are a schedule to the Zoning Bylaw (Schedule E – Floodplain Boundaries); Fishtrap Creek runs through the Ellwood / Fishtrap Creek Park area with streamside rules at development. Property-level status for any single lot must be checked in the City's WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 governs, at two scales.** Urban: RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m², RS5 420 m². Rural: A1 minimum subdivision lot area 8.0 ha, A2 16.0 ha — the single most important number set for a west-side land buyer, because it decides whether a rural parcel can ever be split. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/52433/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 is CURRENT.** Abbotsford's current Official Community Plan is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025. The research file's "Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated" citation and any live-page "2500-2016" citation are STALE / conflicted — apply 2050 and flag both numbers as a Jerry sweep item. The OCP's transit structure (Secondary Transit Corridors connecting Highstreet and the hospital to the Primary Transit Corridor on South Fraser Way between Clearbrook and McCallum Road) is the plan context for the Highstreet node's land values. https://www.abbotsford.ca/council/your-council-community/blog/making-progress-housing-abbotsford
- **SSMUH / added units on a lot:** June 2024 Council adoption of the SSMUH Zoning Bylaw amendments is City-confirmed; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED ("3560-2024" is asserted in research files and must not be asserted). Small Scale Multi-Unit Housing rules are in Zoning Bylaw s.140, and s.140.3.8 permits one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings, with City-report criteria including minimum lot size 400 m², minimum frontage 12.0 m, not on a cul-de-sac bulb and location on a Collector or Local road. Abbotsford's suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling, and a coach/garden suite is capped at 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m² — Abbotsford's rules ONLY (standing corrections, commissioning brief, Nov 1, 2026). SSMUH does not override the A1/A2 minimums or the ALR on the rural edge. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision and servicing requirements:** subdivision runs through the City's Approving Officer against the zone minimums above and the City's works-and-services (Development Bylaw) requirements, applied file by file through a Works and Services Report and servicing agreement in City council reports. The Development Bylaw's current number/year and full servicing schedule were NOT confirmed on a City page read for this file (a 2021 draft and 2022 references both circulate): detailed servicing requirements for a specific subdivision are UNCONFIRMED until the current Development Bylaw text and the file's report are read. The one servicing rule the research file states outright is the rural one: lots outside the urban boundary in rural residential zones may not connect to the Community Water System or Community Sanitary Sewer System. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58305/ and https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **ALR rules, on the west/southwest edge:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, stated as the ALC states it, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; Strata Property Act s.239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s.21(1)) — which is why no bare-land strata can be created out of ALR ground here. ALC residence caps: principal residence 500 m² or less; suite only within the principal residence; additional residence 90 m² (parcels of 40 ha or less) / 186 m² (over 40 ha). The City's A1 zone mirrors the added-dwelling route: one Accessory Farm Dwelling Unit per lot in accordance with the ALR Use Regulation, B.C. Reg. 30/2019, or as approved by the ALC, on a lot of at least 0.4 ha, at the same 90 m² / 186 m² caps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400210
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the farm operation for farm purposes in excess of 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — Classification of Land as a Farm Regulation, B.C. Reg. 411/95, s.5, applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is asserted in this file and none is documented in this area's research file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **Bare-land strata block (applies to every Downes Road Estates lot):** a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B diligence (the lot's strata fee, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, parking/storage and insurance summary; the strata must provide the Form B within 7 days of request, max $35 plus up to 25¢ per page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (30-year projections, at least three CRF funding models, non-deferrable by vote); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (a directory "rentals allowed" line for Downes is in any event not a restriction); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with legacy-resident, caregiver and the May 1, 2023 spouse/partner and defined-children exemptions) — which is why the "45+" marketing line noted in Section 2 cannot stand as a bylaw and the registered bylaws govern. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Well and septic diligence (rural edge):** on lots outside the urban boundary, check the well record in the provincial GWELLS database and confirm the onsite sewage system was filed under the Sewerage System Regulation by an Authorized Person through Fraser Health — the research file's own buying guidance for the rural edge. A rural lot's price that assumes city services is mispriced ground. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. The exemptions are home-based, with a land route in each: the First-Time Home Buyers' Program can be kept by a buyer who registers a vacant lot and builds, if lot value at registration plus construction cost is $835,000 or less (partial under $860,000), building and moving in by the first anniversary; the Newly Built Home Exemption can refund PTT paid on a vacant lot once a qualifying newly built home is constructed on it ($1,100,000 full / $1,150,000 partial). Land bought to hold gets neither. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/bc-property-transfer-tax-exemptions-guide/ and https://wowa.ca/calculators/bc-property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA source supports:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the example), while an individual's sale can be taxable where the land is capital property used primarily in a business, is sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. A developer's or business's sale of newly subdivided lots sits on the taxable side of that line as a general matter; the treatment of any specific west-side parcel — farm parcels in particular, which the CRA addresses in a separate info sheet — is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and parcel history are established. State the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax:** the Province's taxable-areas list names the City of Abbotsford — owners of residential-class property in Abbotsford must complete the annual declaration. Parcel-level application to vacant or rural land (assessment class, development/under-construction exemptions) is UNCONFIRMED for any single lot until its class and the current exemption list are read. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal prohibition on purchase by non-Canadians:** the March 27, 2023 regulatory amendments repealed the provision that had made vacant land zoned residential or mixed-use inside a census metropolitan area or census agglomeration "residential property" — as amended, vacant land itself can be purchased by a non-Canadian, including for residential development, per the law-firm summaries of the amendments; dwellings remain governed by the prohibition, and application to a specific purchase is UNCONFIRMED at the official-text level in this file. https://www.mross.com/what-we-think/article/update-canadian-government-prohibits-non-canadians-from-purchasing-residential-real-estate
- **First-home programs — the land boundary, stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST/HST Rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent MARCH 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — 100% of GST to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031. A Downes Road Estates resale or a rural parcel attracts no such rebate; the question arises only on a qualifying new home. The GST New Housing Rebate (36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out) is likewise a housing rebate. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty September 16, 2026. A bare-land strata lot adds its strata fee on top; a fee-simple lot has no strata fee but carries its own servicing and maintenance. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Market context, labelled as community-level only:** Abbotsford IS a SnapStats-covered community (September 2026: Abbotsford West detached inventory 138 / solds 12, attached inventory 210 / solds 16; Abbotsford-wide medians detached $949,000, attached $497,500). SnapStats has NO land category — these are housing figures describing the market around the land, never land prices. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kinds of land can I buy in Abbotsford West?** Three: a fee-simple lot in an urban subdivision (Clearbrook / Peardonville / Townline), a bare-land strata lot at Downes Road Estates (32250 Downes Road, LMS2380 — a strata lot that carries a detached rancher, with strata fees and a Form B), or a fee-simple rural/ALR parcel toward Mt. Lehman Road and YXX. No named bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2 above.)
- **Q: What is a bare-land strata lot, in plain terms?** Strata tenure applied to land: you own a strata lot (here, a lot with a rancher on it), you own a share of the common property by unit entitlement, you pay a bare-land strata fee, and the strata's bylaws bind the lot. It is NOT fee-simple — "Freehold Strata" on the title means strata, and the diligence is a Form B, not just a title search. https://www.estateblock.com/abbotsford/16-32250-downes-rd-518712
- **Q: Is Downes Road Estates 55+?** The sibling 55-plus file records listings stating "Age Restricted 55+", while one listing record markets "Age restricted 45+" — a line that cannot be a valid BC age bylaw, since only 55+ bylaws are valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2). Treat the age position as UNCONFIRMED until the registered bylaws attached to the Form B are read, and do not rely on either marketing line. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **Q: Can I split a rural parcel on the Mt. Lehman side?** Almost never at house-lot scale: A1 subdivision minimum is 8.0 ha and A2 is 16.0 ha, and if the parcel is in the ALR, subdivision also requires ALC approval. Price a west-side acreage as one parcel unless the zone math and the ALR map both say otherwise. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a rural lot serviced?** Outside the urban boundary, no city connection is available in rural residential zones — those lots use wells and onsite sewage. Check the GWELLS well record and the Fraser Health sewerage filing before offering, and confirm which side of the urban boundary the parcel sits on; that line changes both servicing and price. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
- **Q: What can I add to a detached lot — a suite, a coach house, more units?** One secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8) subject to the City-report criteria (400 m² lot, 12.0 m frontage, Collector or Local road, no cul-de-sac bulb); Abbotsford caps a suite at under 50% of the principal dwelling and a coach/garden suite at 15% of lot area to 100 m² maximum. SSMUH rules sit in s.140 (bylaw number UNCONFIRMED). On ALR land the ALC caps replace the urban ones: principal 500 m², suite inside the principal only, additional residence 90 m² / 186 m². https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: What taxes hit a lot purchase?** PTT at the general rates, with the first-time/newly-built exemptions reachable only by building and occupying a qualifying home (Section 3). GST depends on the seller: an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt per CRA GI-003; a business sale or a more-than-two-part subdivision sale can be taxable. Abbotsford is a speculation and vacancy tax area for residential-class property (annual declaration). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold land here while I decide?** Property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3, second 5% September 16), insurance, and — on a bare-land strata lot — the strata fee and any levies shown on the Form B; on a rural parcel, well/septic maintenance instead. A fee-simple urban lot has no strata fee at all. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Q: Which schools serve a west-side lot?** Three locator chains are settled: 3614 Clearbrook Road returns Clearbrook Elementary → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 2990 Oriole Crescent returns John MacLure Community School → Colleen and Gordie Howe Middle → W.J. Mouat Secondary; 3433 Firhill Drive returns Blue Jay Elementary → Eugene Reimer Middle → Rick Hansen Secondary (SD34 School Locator, mybaragar.com, run 2026-11-01). Blue Jay Elementary is at 30995 Southern Dr per the Ministry listing. Any other address needs its own run — the west side does not have one catchment. https://www.abbyschools.ca/policy-18-school-catchment-areas
- **Q: Does the airport change what I can build?** It can: airport height rules apply inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area around YXX, and the research file directs buyers near Mt. Lehman Road and YXX to check both ALR status and the airport area before planning additions. Parcel-level height limits: UNCONFIRMED until the regulation area map is read for the lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
