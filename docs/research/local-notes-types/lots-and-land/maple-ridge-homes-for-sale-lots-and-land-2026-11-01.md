- **Floodplain / hazard:** along River Road / Port Haney the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), and floodplain status is per-lot (55-plus sibling, citing the City's floodplain construction document). A specific lot's flood position: UNVERIFIED until read on City records. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **ALR block, in full (rural sub-areas):** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from limited approving-officer cases. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Principal residence 500 m² or less (fill ≤1,000 m²); suite only inside the principal residence; one additional residence at 90 m² or less (≤40 ha) or 186 m² or less (>40 ha), where City bylaws also permit it. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. - **PTT on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully qualifying FTHB); $1.0M → $18,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, max saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Maple Ridge land detail actually filed?** In the sub-area land files (§2 cross-reference) — this CITY file carries no roster and takes no counts. A parcel question starts in its sub-area file and on City records for the PID. (§2 above)
- **Q: How did Maple Ridge sell in September 2026?** Detached: 410 listings, 40 sold, median $1,131,000, 29 days. "Condos & Townhomes" combined: 286 listings, 33 sold, median $525,000, 49 days — the attached median was the low of the 13-month range. Those are combined figures, never land figures; land has no SnapStats category. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a rural Maple Ridge acreage?** If the parcel is in the ALR, stratification is barred and subdivision needs ALC permission; the ALC caps the program at one principal residence (≤500 m², suite inside it only) plus at most one additional residence (90 m² / 186 m²). Parcel ALR status and servicing are UNVERIFIED here — check the PID on the ALC maps and the title. (§3 above)
- **Q: How many units can one City lot hold?** Eligible single-family and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws, with one secondary suite inside the building at a maximum of 40% of gross floor area — but not on floodplain properties, where s.402.8(h) prohibits suites. The count for a specific lot is zone-specific: UNVERIFIED at lot level in this file. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Is The Meadows a townhouse or land option in Northwest Maple Ridge?** Neither in this program's counts: it is QUEUED, counted nowhere — the townhomes position governs over the condos guide sibling's "counted" credit, and the conflict is stated in §2. Do not price it as counted stock or as a lot offering. (§2 above)
- **Q: Are The Maples co-op or Val Maria lots I can buy?** No — The Maples is a housing co-operative (shares and occupancy, not a lot) and Val Maria is a leasehold non-strata park (own the home, rent the pad). Both are tenure records, excluded as lot stock with reasons in §2. (§2 above)
- **Q: What flood rule affects a River Road / Port Haney lot?** The flood construction level there is 6.7 m geodetic including freeboard, floodplain status is per-lot, and suites are prohibited on floodplain properties under s.402.8(h). Read the specific lot's position on City records before promising a suite or addition. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: Which OCP bylaw number should my offer cite?** Neither as settled — the citation is CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024) and this file resolves it nowhere. Quote planning policy for a specific parcel from a current City document, and note the dated adoptions that are not contested: Albion Area Plan Oct 28, 2025; Lougheed Transit Corridor Jan 27, 2026. (§3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve my lot?** SD42's Ministry-listed City schools are in §1 — but catchment assignment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED without an SD42 lookup on record. Confirm the exact address with the district. (§1 above)
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes at the CRA GI-003 level — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
