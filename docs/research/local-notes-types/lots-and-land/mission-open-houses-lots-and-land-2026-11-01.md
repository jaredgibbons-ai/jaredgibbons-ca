## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim (sourcing stated):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that a disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. This file's own research opened that source, and the text above is carried in the form the sibling files that opened the same source record it. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **The regulator:** real estate trading services in BC are regulated by the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA); a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **How open-house dates/times are set and entered, and any visitor sign-in/ID requirement: UNVERIFIED** — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states either. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/mission-open-houses.md)

No OCP-date page defect is documented for this slug and none is asserted. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Zoning / subdivision frame (Mission):** Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020; subdivision/servicing under Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 — carried only from a City source found in an earlier batch, not as a research-file fact for this slug. Mission incorporated SSMUH into its zoning on July 17, 2024 (Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163)); Mission-specific unit counts are UNCONFIRMED here and Surrey's figures are not carried in. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments and https://www.mission.ca/media/file/site-assessment-development
- **Secondary suites (Mission):** all suites must be registered with the City; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes; a new suite goes through the building permit process; an unregistered suite can trigger investigation and a possible decommission permit. One feed card (33480 Blueberry Drive) describes a suite "registered w. City" — listing text only, not verified against the City registry. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **ALR block (Mission's rural fringe includes ALR ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it (none does here). Which feed parcels are ALR: UNVERIFIED, parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (if a lot visited is bare-land strata):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% to $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example:** $800,000 fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 = **$14,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. personal-use land); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Mission property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** on any current balances outstanding on July 3, 2026 (Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; no s.235 alternative scheme). Not the FVRD rural 5% + 5% regime. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes

- **Q: Can I view land at a Mission open house?** The feed's land-adjacent cards are acreage listings with homes on them, held open for one Sunday — that is a viewing of a listing, not a bare-land offering. No vacant-lot roster is verified from this page. (§2 above)
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation while the host is only hosting an advertised open house or giving factual answers — once you share your motivation, finances or needs with the seller's licensee, the exception ends. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** UNVERIFIED as a rule — no BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement; an individual host may still ask on the seller's instructions. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I take the train to a Sunday open house?** No, not on the regular schedule: the West Coast Express is a weekday commuter service with no regular weekend service and Mission City Station is its eastern terminus — weekend open houses are reached by car or local bus (Route 31 serves Mission–Abbotsford). https://www.translink.ca/news/2025/november/all%20aboard%20translink%20celebrates%2030%20years%20of%20west%20coast%20express
- **Q: The home I visited has a suite — what do I check?** Its registration. Mission requires every suite to be registered; suites are not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes at all, and a card saying "registered w. City" is a claim to verify, not a fact. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: What will I pay in tax if I buy the acreage I viewed?** PTT on the general tiers ($800,000, no exemption = $14,000 — §3) and GST only as CRA GI-003 states it for that seller and parcel history. The open-house visit settles neither — both are transaction questions. (§3 above)
- **Q: How did Mission sell in September 2026?** Detached 231 listings / 28 solds / $814,000 median; "Condos & Townhomes" 122 / 10 / $430,000 — community context only, on small attached counts; SnapStats publishes no open-house and no land category. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.

Every other Mission address: UNVERIFIED without an SD75 locator run. (§1 above)
