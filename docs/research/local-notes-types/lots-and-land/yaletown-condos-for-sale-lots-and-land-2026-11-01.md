## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Heritage district — Yaletown Historic Area (HA-3):** the Historic Area District Schedule for Yaletown (HA-3) encourages the conversion and renovation of its buildings and compatible new buildings, to produce a mix of commercial, industrial and residential uses, under the City's heritage-area regulations and companion design guidelines. Exterior change and redevelopment on Yaletown parcels starts with HA-3, not the base district alone. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Downtown districts (DD / CD-1 frame):** Yaletown development parcels sit in the City of Vancouver's downtown district frame (DD / CD-1, per the downtown-ve lots precedent in Section 2); a CD-1 is a site-specific comprehensive district, so the parcel's own CD-1 bylaw — not a generic density figure — governs what its land can hold. Parcel-level district and CD-1 text: confirm on the parcel's bylaw before pricing any redevelopment. (Precedent: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/downtown-ve-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538 (VERIFIED Vancouver frame):** Vancouver subdivision runs under Subdivision By-law No. 5208 with fees under By-law No. 14538; on downtown ground the operative division is airspace subdivision (Section 2), confirmed parcel by parcel with the City. Parcel-level requirements beyond that frame: UNCONFIRMED here.
- **SSMUH / house-lot subdivision potential:** not a Yaletown form — there are no house lots to which small-scale multi-unit subdivision potential would attach in this topic file. No SSMUH figure is asserted for Yaletown ground.
- **Strata block (the operative diligence for Yaletown condo land):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Read the depreciation report, contingency fund and any lease or rental-restriction bylaws off the strata documents — none of that is in the feed. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/yaletown-condos-for-sale.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, 3%:** every Vancouver residential owner must file an annual Empty Homes Tax property status declaration by the 2nd business day in February under Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674; a vacancy tax unpaid as of the 10th business day of April bears a 5% penalty. The EHT rate carried in this program's Vancouver frame is 3% — Vancouver ONLY, never exported. This slug's research pass read the declaration and penalty frame but did not state the rate on the City pages read, so the rate rests on the program's verified Vancouver frame, not on this slug's research alone. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Property taxes (City of Vancouver):** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on unpaid balances after the main-tax deadline. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-Time Home Buyers' exemption: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, out at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Yaletown condo resales are not vacant-land sales; this block is carried for the land file's completeness only. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.60%–4.50% per the condos sibling. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a lot or bare land in Yaletown?** No — there is no house-lot or bare-land form in Yaletown. What trades is strata-condo product on DD/CD-1 downtown parcels, divided by airspace subdivision; the counted stock (10 buildings, verified fresh) lives in the condos sibling only. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Are Yaletown condos freehold or leasehold?** It varies by building, and no official source opened in this research states the tenure building by building — treat any tenure claim, including listing text describing a prepaid Crown lease, as unverified until it is read off the unit's title and strata documents. https://jaredgibbons.ca/yaletown-condos-for-sale.html
- **Q: What is an airspace parcel, and why does it matter downtown?** It is how a downtown development is divided after construction — separate airspace parcels for residential, commercial and parking components — instead of ground lots. Your diligence runs through the strata plan and Form B for your component, not a lot survey. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What does the heritage area stop me doing to a loft?** The HA-3 schedule encourages conversion and renovation of Yaletown's heavy-timber, brick and concrete buildings, but the City's heritage-area regulations and design guidelines govern what can change in the protected area — confirm a renovation plan against the HA-3 district schedule and the strata bylaws before promising it. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: Where exactly is Yaletown?** On the City's own heritage-area description, bordered by Homer and Nelson streets to the north and east, and Cambie, Mainland, and Drake streets to the south and west, on the north shore of False Creek in downtown Vancouver. That is a heritage boundary, not a verified MLS boundary. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: Which elementary school is in Yaletown?** Elsie Roy Elementary, 150 Drake St — confirmed by name and address against the BC Ministry K-12 file in SD39, and Drake Street is one of Yaletown's City-defined boundary streets; catchment assignment for a specific condo address is UNVERIFIED, so check the exact address with SD39. (Section 1.)
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a Yaletown condo I leave empty?** Vancouver's Vacancy Tax By-law No. 11674 requires every owner to file the annual property status declaration by the 2nd business day in February; the EHT rate in the program's Vancouver frame is 3%, Vancouver ONLY. An investor-buyer needs the declaration explained before closing, not after. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/11674c.pdf
- **Q: Can I rent out a Yaletown condo long-term?** Strata rental-restriction bylaws have been void province-wide since November 24, 2022; short-term rentals can still be restricted by bylaw. Read the building's registered bylaws on the Form B — feed amenity lists do not decide value; the depreciation report and contingency fund do. (Section 3.)
- **Q: How much property transfer tax on a $1.0M Yaletown condo?** $18,000 on the general scale (1% / 2% / 3% tiers), before any fact-specific exemption. The Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1.1M, out at $1.15M) is for a new home from a builder, not a resale condo. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Does SnapStats publish a Yaletown median I can price against?** No — SnapStats publishes no Vancouver figures at all and no Yaletown row exists. Pricing support comes from Paragon comparables; the feed's asking prices are listing copy. (Section 1.)
