## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**The land in Matsqui is farm land: fee-simple agricultural parcels on a dyked river prairie, in the A1 Agricultural Zone (subdivision minimum 8.0 ha) and the RR Rural Residential Zone (minimum lot size 4.0 ha). Strata is a structural zero here — no strata development is a documented housing form in this sub-area, there is no bare-land strata, and ALR residences cannot be stratified in any event. No named bare-land subdivision and no presale lot offering exists in the research record. Verified named land developments counted here: 0 — and the program count for this ground lives in the guide lots file named in FILE STATUS; this file claims no separate count.**

- **Fee-simple farm parcels are the land form — and nearly the whole market.** The research file's housing profile is the land profile: single-detached houses are the dominant dwelling type on agricultural and rural parcels, and "the housing stock is freehold farm and rural-residential land; strata development is not a documented housing form in this rural sub-area." A Matsqui purchase is a fee-simple title purchase: no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fee — Zoning Bylaw 2400-2014, ALR rules where the parcel is in the reserve, the dyking levy and any registered covenants govern. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2014%20Matsqui%20Prairie%20Demographic%20Profile_spreads.pdf and (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-11/matsqui.md, §5)
