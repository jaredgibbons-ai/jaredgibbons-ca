## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Dunbar has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community and no presale LOT offering was verified. Land trades as fee-simple house lots on the R1-1 streets, plus one genuinely different form: the Southlands country lots at the south edge, under their own agricultural / limited-agriculture districts with possible ALR status. The area's one new building (West 39th Avenue) is built strata product documented below as a development record — never a lot offering.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form (Dunbar house streets)

- **Established-street house lots:** detached houses on tree-lined streets dominate — the City calls Dunbar-Southlands a mature community of quiet residential streets lined with grand old trees — with low-rise strata product concentrated on the Dunbar Street and West 41st Avenue frontages. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The strata-versus-freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx

These are schedule minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule (house streets) + agricultural / limited-agriculture districts (Southlands):** R1-1 minimum site areas 306 m² (single detached house, duplex, or 3–4-unit multiple dwelling; 10.0 m frontage) / 464 m² (5 units; 13.4 m) / 557 m² (6–8 units; 15.1 m), with secondary suite and laneway house with a house and multiplexes to 6 units or 8 rental units as the schedule states. The Southlands lots sit under their own districts; the exact schedule for a given parcel: UNVERIFIED. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — no separate Dunbar plan + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** Dunbar has no separate adopted community plan confirmed in the sources read: UNVERIFIED; citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a Dunbar house lot must clear both R1-1 and its Subdivision By-law category; a Southlands split additionally clears its own district — and, where the parcel is in the ALR, ALC approval (below). https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework here is the R1-1 schedule itself — house plus secondary suite and laneway house, or a multiplex to 6 strata units / 8 secured rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" on a Dunbar house lot is a per-lot schedule check, not a separate overlay. It does not apply to Southlands parcels under their agricultural districts. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **ALR rules (live for Southlands parcels in the ALR; parcel status UNVERIFIED until checked):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no page stating it is documented in this file's own research. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Floodplain:** the City's designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage at the area's south end, and land in it is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for setbacks and flood construction levels — relevant to Southlands and south-edge purchases, not to the Dunbar Street frontages. Parcel-level status: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata diligence (the frontage strata stock — and any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw — West Pointe's listing field "Rentals Allwd w/Restrctns" is VOID as a long-term restriction on this basis, per the condos sibling); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No Dunbar building is published as 55+ — Fifty-Five 55 Dunbar's "55" is its street number, not an age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED — the exemption to test first on a West 39th Avenue new-build purchase). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Separately, 5% GST applies to new housing (the West 39th Avenue listings state prices including GST) — housing GST, not vacant-land GST. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. The City tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx

The federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position for Dunbar land is UNCONFIRMED in this file: the research cites no prohibition source for Dunbar, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a new lot subdivision in Dunbar?** No verified one — Dunbar is an established house area. Land trades as built house lots and Southlands country lots, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What makes Southlands different from the rest of Dunbar?** Everything that matters for land: Southlands lots are large, semi-rural freehold properties under their own agricultural / limited-agriculture districts beside the Musqueam land, some possibly in the ALR — while the Dunbar streets are R1-1 house lots. Confirm the district and ALR position parcel by parcel before pricing either. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/dunbar-southlands.aspx
- **Q: Can a Dunbar house lot hold more than one home?** Yes, where the R1-1 schedule is met — a single detached house may include a secondary suite and a laneway house, and a multiplex may have up to 6 units or up to 8 rental units, with minimum site areas from 306 m² to 557 m² by form. Southlands lots sit under different districts. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a Dunbar lot into two fee-simple lots?** Only if both resulting parcels clear R1-1 and the Subdivision By-law No. 5208 Schedule A category for the block, through the Approving Officer — and, for a Southlands parcel in the ALR, only with ALC approval as well. A neighbour's earlier split does not grandfather yours. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm
- **Q: Can an ALR lot in Southlands be stratified or subdivided freely?** No — subdivision in the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Residence caps are principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). First confirm the parcel is actually in the ALR — status here is UNVERIFIED parcel by parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is any Dunbar land bare-land strata?** None verified — Dunbar's strata stock is building-form strata on the frontages. If a bare-land strata lot were offered, it would still be strata tenure: Form B, strata fees and bylaws would apply to the lot itself. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Why do listings at 3590 West 39th Avenue say "price includes GST" and "receivership sale"?** Because those units sell as new construction through a receivership, with a disclosure statement and 2-5-10 warranty — a purchase from the developer side, not a homeowner resale and not a land sale. https://www.zolo.ca/vancouver-real-estate/3590-west-39th-avenue/309
- **Q: Does the floodplain touch a Dunbar purchase?** Only at the south edge: the designated floodplain covers the Fraser River frontage, and parcels in it carry Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements. The Dunbar Street buildings are on the retail frontages, not the river edge — confirm the specific parcel in the City dataset. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003); the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not the bare lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Dunbar lot?** By exact address only — three elementaries sit inside Dunbar-Southlands on the VSB list, but no completed locator run is documented for this ground, so every catchment is UNVERIFIED. Run the address in the SD39 locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
