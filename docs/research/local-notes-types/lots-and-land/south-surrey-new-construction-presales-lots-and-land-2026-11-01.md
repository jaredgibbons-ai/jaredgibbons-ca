## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **REDMA block (the presale frame):** under BC's Real Estate Development Marketing Act, the developer must give the buyer a disclosure statement before signing (as the live page states it, attributed there to BCFSA presales information); **s.21 — the buyer may rescind by written notice within 7 days**; **s.18 — deposits are held in trust**. The page describes deposits as "usually 10%–20% total, staged over construction" — that range is page copy, UNVERIFIED as a rule; the disclosure statement for the specific project governs its deposit schedule. Verify current REDMA rules on BCFSA's site and have a lawyer read the disclosure statement inside the rescission window. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Warranty (page copy):** the page lists a 2-5-10 warranty (2 years materials and labour, 5 years building envelope, 10 years structure) — carried as the page's statement; the specific warranty for a project is in its disclosure statement. https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html
- **City plans (Surrey ground):** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) — current; Bylaw 18020 REPLACED, never cite as current. Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. White Rock addresses in the feed answer to White Rock's own bylaws — no Surrey rule is asserted for a White Rock parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH / suites (Surrey, verified in this program and reused in the research):** max 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, under the Jul 8, 2024 changes; a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit, one per unit, never a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision + servicing (Surrey):** Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830, verified on City pages. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside (Surrey):** setbacks in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000, varying by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **STRs (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only, owner must live at the property as primary residence; licence $358/yr carried with the City's $350/yr proposal disclosed as a CONFLICT — check the City licence page before quoting. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **ALR block (completeness only — no ALR parcel documented here):** subdivision needs ALC approval; residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC strata block (the built-form product this page sells is strata; applies in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B (within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; min 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. None of Harlowe, Calais or Rockford carries a 55+ restriction on any source in this program — all explicitly NOT 55+. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT / new-home exemption:** general tiers 1% / 2% to $2M / 3% above, plus further 2% on the residential portion above $3M. Worked examples: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying FTHB); $900K → $16,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier. If a sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M, flag as OPEN reconciliation, follow $93,000. **Newly Built Home Exemption:** full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 (page copy, consistent with the Province frame) — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on new housing / vacant land:** new housing is generally GST-taxable (the presale case); for vacant land itself, CRA GI-003 level ONLY — developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE, an individual's personal-use land generally exempt, finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** applies to new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000/yr. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Regulator:** BCFSA regulates BC real estate trading services; a licensed professional works for either buyer or seller only and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. Registering with your own agent before a first presentation-centre visit matters for representation (page copy). https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-new-construction-presales.html
- **Surrey taxes / registry:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry (foreclosure mapping UNVERIFIED in this program).
