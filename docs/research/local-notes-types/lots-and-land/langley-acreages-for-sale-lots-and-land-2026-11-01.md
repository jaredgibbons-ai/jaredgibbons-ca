## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs Township acreages; rural cards in the feed cite RU-2 and RU-3 — confirm the specific parcel's zone with the Township. City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply to Township parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots up to 280 m² and 4 units on lots under 4,050 m²; lots over 4,050 m² are ineligible, eligibility requires the Urban Containment Boundary and municipal water and sewer, and **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Most acreages fail on size and servicing alone — SSMUH does not rescue a large rural lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Willoughby plan status — Township:** the Willoughby Community Plan (Bylaw 1836) is now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025). The Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 were at first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026 — reading-stage bylaws are never upgraded in this file. (Standing corrections for this program.)
- **ALR block (the frame that governs a large share of Langley acreage — parcel status UNVERIFIED per parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); building more, or larger, requires ALC approval, and local government bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and that error is NOT asserted in this file):** $2,500 in farm-gate sales for a farm operation area of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha for larger operations; $10,000 if the total area is under 0.8 ha; applications must be delivered by October 31. Whether any specific acreage holds farm class is UNVERIFIED per parcel. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **Strata block (where a bare-land strata parcel is met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple acreage carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Well and septic:** the live page advises testing water flow, water quality and the septic system before removing subjects on 1–5 acre properties — adopted here as a tip; per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html

Whether Form B diligence, and how PTT and GST apply, on a court sale is UNCONFIRMED in this file. - **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 acreage purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What counts as an acreage on this page?** The page's own definition is a home on 1 to 10 acres, with land over 10 acres routed to the farms page — but the live feed at reading included a 39.5-acre estate and a bare-land Land listing, so treat the definition as the page's stated intent rather than a verified filter. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Q: How many homes can I build on an ALR acreage in Langley?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), with a secondary suite only inside that residence, plus one additional residence of up to 90 m² on properties 40 ha or less or up to 186 m² on properties over 40 ha; more or larger requires ALC approval and local bylaws may be more restrictive. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What farm income does a Langley acreage need for farm class?** The three-tier thresholds: $2,500 in farm-gate sales for 0.8–4 ha, $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that size, and $10,000 under 0.8 ha, with applications due by October 31. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo89/loo89/411_95_pit
- **Q: Does SSMUH let me put 3–4 units on a Langley acreage?** No for most acreages: Township SSMUH (Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024) requires a lot of 4,050 m² or smaller, inside the Urban Containment Boundary and connected to municipal water and sewer — 3 units at ≤280 m² and 4 units at 280–4,050 m², with no 6-plex tier. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR acreage into lots?** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and depositing a strata plan on ALR land is a subdivision for this purpose — ALR residences cannot be stratified without that permission (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Off-ALR Township subdivision still needs Approving Officer approval and servicing under Bylaw 5382. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Q: What did Langley homes sell for in September 2026?** As board context only — not acreage-segment figures, for which no figure exists in this source: the Langley community detached median was $1,345,000 on 449 listings and 64 sales, and the Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 on 699 listings and 128 sales. (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/stats/)
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M acreage?** $28,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 ($26,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant acreage parcel?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What should I test before removing subjects on an acreage?** Follow the page's own checks, adopted here as tips: test the well and septic, confirm permits for outbuildings, suites and shops, and ask the Township what the zone allows for a second home, a shop or a suite. Per-property well/septic status is UNVERIFIED in this file. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
- **Q: Is the bare-land court-ordered card a normal lot listing?** No — it is a court-ordered Land listing inside a homes-on-acreage feed, documented as feed evidence only. A court sale follows the Supreme Court foreclosure process in §3, and its PTT/GST and Form B position is UNCONFIRMED — price it with legal advice, not as ordinary inventory. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-acreages-for-sale.html
