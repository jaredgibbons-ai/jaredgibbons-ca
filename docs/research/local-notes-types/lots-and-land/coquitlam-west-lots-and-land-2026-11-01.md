## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Coquitlam West. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified on an official or developer source. What trades as "land" here is (1) fee-simple house lots on the established Austin Avenue and interior streets, whose value turns on the R-zone subdivision minimums and the SSMUH unit counts in Section 3, and (2) assembly and redevelopment potential around the two stations under the adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments — planning potential documented as such below, never as for-sale inventory.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 governs — small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with subdivision minimums of 650 m² / 18.5 m width (R-1, R-3) and 465 m² / 12.5 m width (R-2). https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Plan:** the Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, adopted April 27, 2026, supersedes OCP No. 3479. The adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments sit inside that framework for the station areas and Austin Avenue. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review and http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** the provincial SSMUH rules as adopted in Coquitlam allow up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — confirmed bylaw, process stated:** subdivision follows the zone minimums above, and works and services are governed by the City of Coquitlam Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 (confirmed on the City's own bylaw library and the consolidated bylaw text in a fresh check for this file). Parcel-level servicing standards under that bylaw are confirmed with the City for the specific subdivision — no standard is quoted lot-by-lot here. https://coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws
- **Watercourse diligence:** a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse, with Zoning Bylaw s.519 / s.523 setbacks — check slope and creek lots before pricing a split or an added unit. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata rules:** no bare-land strata plan is verified in this area (Section 2), so no strata block is triggered here. For completeness, the BC frame that would apply to such a lot is: Form B diligence; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with the May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Coquitlam West ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm-class thresholds are not documented in this area's own research, so no farm-class figure is asserted here. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Coquitlam West lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Coquitlam West-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Coquitlam West, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Coquitlam property taxes and utilities:** City of Coquitlam property tax payment was due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026 (including unclaimed Home Owner Grants); utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste, and the City's 2026 budget materials record an average-household total property-charges increase of $137. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes and https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17426/2026-Budget-Highlights

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot subdivision I can buy into in Coquitlam West?** No verified one — the area is established station-area strata plus interior house streets. Land trades as built house lots and assemblies, and no presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create here?** It turns on the parcel's zone: 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2, and 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996. Confirm the zone with the City before pricing any split. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: How many homes can a house lot here carry under SSMUH?** Up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes — and only where the lot is connected to municipal water and sewer; unserviced lots fall outside the rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Does a lot near Burquitlam or Lougheed station carry redevelopment value?** Possibly — the adopted Southwest Shoulders and Corridors amendments create medium-density and townhousing opportunities around both stations and along Austin Avenue. Whether the specific lot is inside an amended area is a City confirmation, not a listing claim. http://coquitlam.ca/m/newsflash/Home/Detail/1968
- **Q: Is a Coquitlam West house lot serviced?** The City's framework assumes municipal water and sewer for SSMUH eligibility and bills water, sewer/drainage and solid waste as City utilities — but parcel-level septic or well status is UNVERIFIED in the research, so confirm servicing for the specific lot with the City before writing. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-3/coquitlam-west.md)
- **Q: What servicing bylaw governs a subdivision here?** The Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 — works and services must be provided to the City's standards under that bylaw, confirmed parcel by parcel with the City. https://coquitlam.ca/556/Frequently-Requested-Bylaws
- **Q: Do creeks or ravines affect a lot here?** They can: land within 30 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse can require a Watercourse Protection Development Permit, with Zoning Bylaw s.519 / s.523 flood and riparian setbacks behind it. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I get the first-time buyer GST rebate on the lot itself?** No — the FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land. It becomes relevant when a qualifying new home is built and bought, not at the lot purchase. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Coquitlam West lot?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (575 Poirier Street → Parkland Elementary → École Montgomery Middle → Centennial Secondary) does not transfer to station-area streets. Run the address in the SD43 locator. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
