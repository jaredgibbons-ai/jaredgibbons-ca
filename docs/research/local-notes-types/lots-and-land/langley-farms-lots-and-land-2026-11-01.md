## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Langley farm land IS a real bare-land market — and it is overwhelmingly ALR fee-simple land. That is the centre of this file. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata development, and no presale lot offering was verified for the farm belt on any source in the research: what trades is whole fee-simple farm parcels — working farms with barns and buildings, bare agricultural land, and residential acreages with farm potential. There is no strata land product on ALR ground, for the legal reason stated below.**

### Bare-land strata — its own form, and why it does not exist here

- **Form, stated plainly (for contrast):** a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the lot. No such product is verified on Langley farm land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **The bar on ALR ground:** ALR residences cannot be stratified — ALC Information Bulletin 09 s. 6 (strata plans cause subdivision), Strata Property Act s. 239, and Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1) (subdivision of agricultural land prohibited unless permitted by the Commission after an application or permitted under the ALR General Regulation). A "bare-land strata farm lot" in the ALR is therefore not a product that exists to be counted; any listing presenting strata product on Langley farm land must be checked parcel by parcel, because the parcel may not be in the ALR at all. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Non-ALR bare-land strata:** none was verified for the farm belt on any source in the research — UNCONFIRMED as a category here, and not counted.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **ALR rules — the governing frame:** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Residence caps: land in the ALR may generally have no more than one residence per parcel unless an additional residence qualifies; a principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction, and a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — detached, 90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on parcels larger than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. Local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — the full three-tier thresholds (a flat line is an ERROR):** BC Assessment requires minimum gross income at farm-gate prices of **$2,500 where the farm operation is 0.8 ha to 4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha where the operation is greater than 4 ha; $10,000 where the operation is less than 0.8 ha**, with farm-classification applications due by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Documented page error — asserted for ONE page only:** the Langley horse-properties page's buyer's guide states farm class as a flat "$2,500+ in qualifying annual farm income" rule. That is incomplete against the three-tier schedule above and is an ERROR for that documented page (langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.html), as carried in that slug's sibling files (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §§1, 3; research file ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-16/langley-horse-properties-equestrian-farms-for-sale-mls-listings.md). No flat-line instance is documented on the langley-farms page itself in this file's research — none is asserted for it.
- **Township zoning + SSMUH:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 applies across the Township (RU-1/RU-2, SR-1/SR-2/SR-3 above). SSMUH is Bylaw 6020 — **no 6-plex tier exists in it**; the Township states lots larger than 4,050 m² are eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning, and ALR parcels sit under the ALC caps above regardless. Secondary suite cap (Township): 120 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Coach house — fee-simple, never strata, with a live conflict flagged, not resolved:** Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 102 defines a coach house as an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage on a lot — a fee-simple second unit under s. 102, never strata. LIVE CONFLICT carried in this program: the s. 102 July 2025 text permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says not permitted — flagged here, resolved nowhere; confirm the current Township position for the specific parcel and form. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **R-CL cluster housing (where a Langley page cites it):** Zoning Bylaw 2500 s. 403 (R-CL) carries a 220 m² minimum and a 464.5 m² maximum. Source: program standing corrections, 2026-11-01.
- **Willoughby Community Plan status (Township plan frame):** the Willoughby Community Plan is Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); the Willoughby Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 received first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a public hearing directed for June 15, 2026 — never upgrade reading-stage bylaws to adopted. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is this parcel actually in the ALR?** Check the ALC maps by PID or civic address before anything else — 235 km² of the Township is in the ALR, and reserve status controls residences, subdivision and most use permissions. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Langley farm?** Inside the ALR, only with ALC approval — subdivision (including by strata plan) is prohibited unless the Commission permits it after an application or the ALR General Regulation permits it. Outside the ALR, Township Bylaw 2500 zone rules apply, but the RU-zone minimum parcel size is UNCONFIRMED in this file from direct bylaw text. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Q: Can I buy a bare-land strata lot on a farm?** No verified product exists, and on ALR ground the form is barred: ALR residences cannot be stratified (Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Farm land here is fee-simple. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How big can the house be, and can it have a suite?** Principal residence ≤500 m² without an ALC application (≤1,000 m² of fill for its construction), with a secondary suite permitted only inside that principal residence; the Township suite cap is 120 m². https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional residence without an ALC application where conditions are met — ≤90 m² on parcels ≤40 ha, ≤186 m² above 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. A coach house under Bylaw 2500 s. 102 sits only in the second storey of a detached garage and is fee-simple, never strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income does the farm need to keep farm classification?** The three tiers in full: $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha) — gross income at farm-gate prices, applications by Oct 31. Anyone quoting a flat "$2,500+" is quoting an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: Do I need a licence for the well, and what septic proof should exist?** Domestic groundwater use is exempt; irrigation and other non-domestic use requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act, and the well record is searched in GWELLS (older wells may not appear). For septic, ask for the Authorized Person's record of sewerage system filing and letter of certification — Fraser Health's rules make those the compliance documents. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights/water-licences-approvals/new-requirements-for-groundwater-users and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: A card in the farms feed looks like a subdivision or a townhouse project. Is it farm stock?** No — the feed is a generic Agri-Business filter across Langley sub-areas; a feed card is not a confirmed farm operation, and non-farm product in the feed belongs to its own sub-area file, not to this topic. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-farms.html
- **Q: Will GST apply when I buy bare farm land?** At CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale and a newly subdivided lot are generally taxable, while an individual's personal-use sale is generally exempt. Which side a working-farm sale falls on, and any apportionment, is transaction-specific — UNCONFIRMED beyond GI-003 here. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What soil class is the land, and is it floodplain?** Both are per-parcel checks, UNCONFIRMED at topic level: capability is mapped in the BC Soil Information Finder (Class 1 = no or very slight limitations), and floodplain/watercourse status is checked in the Township's GeoSource — the documented dyke system is at the Salmon River mouth. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/resources/soils-and-agricultural-capability/ and https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/salmon-river-dike-upgrades.aspx

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The 235 km² figure reframes every Langley "farm" search:** most parcels a buyer calls a farm are ALR reserve land first and real estate second — the ALC caps (500 m² principal, suite-inside-principal only, 90/186 m² additional residence) bind before Township zoning is even reached. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/about-the-township.aspx
- **Farm classification is an income test that follows the buyer, not the seller:** if the buyer's plan cannot meet the BC Assessment threshold for the operation's size tier, the classification — and its tax treatment — is at risk from the first assessment cycle. The seller's farm income history and any water licences or ALC approvals in place are the documents a farm buyer's lender and lawyer ask for first. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **The fill rule surprises farmhouse builders:** the ALC's 500 m² principal-residence permission carries a second cap most buyers miss — no more than 1,000 m² of fill may be placed for its construction. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **The Township's SSMUH wording is narrower than buyers expect:** lots larger than 4,050 m² are stated eligible for one unit plus one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit depending on zoning — one added unit, not a multi-unit conversion — and Bylaw 6020 contains no 6-plex tier at all. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **A coach house in Langley is defined by where it sits, not what it looks like:** Bylaw 2500 s. 102 puts it only within the second storey of a detached garage — and the program carries a live, unresolved conflict on townhouse suites under s. 102 (July 2025 text permitting at 120 m² vs the January 2025 guide saying not permitted). https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-100-Administration.pdf
- **Two real gaps for working-farm buyers, carried as gaps:** the minimum lot size for temporary farm labourer accommodation (Bylaw 2500 s. 201.3) could not be confirmed from a Township-hosted bylaw text, and the general RU-zone minimum parcel size and any farm home plate rule are likewise UNCONFIRMED from direct bylaw text — price a parcel's subdivision or second-home potential only after the zone text and the ALC position are both in hand.
