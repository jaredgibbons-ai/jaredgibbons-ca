- London Landing is the waterfront pocket at the east edge of Steveston, at the south foot of No. 2 Road where London Road and Dyke Road meet the South Arm of the Fraser River. It sits inside the Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan, Bylaw 9000); in board listing practice this ground falls inside Steveston South. Boundaries: UNVERIFIED — no City source read publishes a boundary for a "London Landing" neighbourhood; the sourced anchors are London's Wharf Park at 6062 Dyke Road, London Road (The Pier at London Landing, 6160 London Road), Princess Street (London Landing townhouses, 13200 Princess Street) and the No. 2 Road addresses 12771–13131 now in rezoning. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in London Landing. The pocket is a built, strata-led waterfront pocket — mixed-use buildings under site-specific London Landing (Steveston) zones, townhouses, and detached houses in RS1/RS2 at the edges — behind the South Arm dike. The land story is the zone fabric itself, the dike/flood position, and one live No. 2 Road rezoning application documented as a proposal only. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan and no presale lot offering was verified.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, at the edges

- **Detached houses at the pocket's edges:** fee-simple houses in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL added under Bill 44. RS1/RS2 minimum lot areas vary by subzone in Section 8.1.8 — for example RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m². These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **The pocket core is strata land, not lots:** the core product is strata (mixed-use buildings and townhouses on the waterfront and No. 2 Road); strata lots in the ZMU buildings are not governed by the RS1/RS2 minimums. The strata/freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- **Serviced position, as official sources state it:** homes in the pocket are on City water and sewer services; a 2026 utility dollar figure confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. Septic or well use in the pocket: UNVERIFIED. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-5/lower-landing.md, §4)
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** ALR land inside the London Landing pocket: UNVERIFIED. No ALR percentage split is stated; check any parcel on ALC mapping. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/
- **Flood position:** the pocket fronts the South Arm behind the City dike system — Richmond averages 1 metre above sea level, protected by 49 km of dikes and 39 pump stations designed for a 1:500 flood event, with dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m; the City's Steveston Island Dike project addresses the Steveston waterfront and harbour. https://richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw 8500, site-specific London Landing zones:** Section 20.20 is the site-specific "Commercial Mixed Use (ZMU20) – London Landing (Steveston)" zone, created by Bylaw 8818 (September 24, 2012), providing for commercial, residential and industrial uses in the Steveston area, with maximum FAR 1.2 plus 0.1, maximum lot coverage 76% and maximum height 21.0 m. ZMU24 (13040 No. 2 Road, Bylaw 9094, 2014) is the pocket's second site-specific zone. Edge houses sit in RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1). https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/ZMU2033849.pdf
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw 9000 / Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4):** London Landing sits inside the Steveston planning area under Schedule 2.4 of the OCP. Whether any London Landing parcel sits inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area boundary (Heritage Alteration Permit requirements): UNVERIFIED. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states it:** RS1/RS2 edge lots carry secondary suite rules in Section 5.4 and the Bill 44 RSM zones (RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L, RSM/XL) in Bylaw 8500 as amended — eligibility is parcel-specific; the ZMU pocket buildings are governed by their site-specific zones, not the RSM ladder. Confirm with the City for the parcel. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements — VERIFIED bylaws:** the City's own alphabetical bylaw list carries "Subdivision and Development — Bylaw 8751 (2011)" and "Works and Services Cost Recovery Bylaw 8752 (2011)" — Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is Bylaw 8751, with cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (verified, banked in the L3 Richmond batch). Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood Control — Bylaw 8204:** new construction is subject to flood construction levels and dike setbacks under the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 — for waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots in this pocket, confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Strata diligence in the ZMU buildings (the pocket's core tenure):** these are mixed-use strata — read the Form B, depreciation report, contingency fund and commercial-component cost sharing early. The full BC strata block applies: Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no pocket ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
