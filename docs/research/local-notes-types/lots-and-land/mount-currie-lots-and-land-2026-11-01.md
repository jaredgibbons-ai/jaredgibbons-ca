The Nation states the community is home to approximately 1,495 of its more than 2,275 citizens, and is named for Mount Currie, the 2,591 m (8,501 ft) mountain at the centre of Líl̓wat Traditional Territory. https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/ (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-currie.md)

- What a "lot" means here — the Nation's allocation system, documented as the land system itself: a citizen's path runs through the Nation's **"Five Steps to Home Ownership on Reserve"** — (1) meet the Housing Manager; (2) a **land encumbrance check from the Lands Department confirming a serviced lot is available and registered to the person**; (3) a Band Council Resolution (the Band Council acts as guarantor on the loan); (4) possible housing subsidies from Indigenous Services Canada; (5) a Ministerial Loan Guarantee and bank financing. The serviced lot is allocated and registered through that process — it is never listed, priced or sold on an open market. Standard lot dimensions on an official source: UNVERIFIED. https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf
- Tenure: **neither freehold nor strata** — Nation-administered housing on reserve land (band housing, and member home ownership through the process above). Fee-simple lots as a default form do not exist on this ground, and bare-land strata as a form does not exist here either: there is no strata corporation, no Form B, no strata fees and no depreciation report for any home in this community. https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/mount-currie.md)

The Nation's own building-cost guidance: modest new construction at about $250–$300 per square foot including water, sewer and hydro connections, and CSA Z-240 modular/pre-manufactured homes at about $160–$180 per square foot installed and hooked up, with Mount Currie modulars required to meet zone 6 construction standards and a roof snow load rating of 136 lb per square foot. https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Lilwat-Nation-Housing-Plan-2018-2033.pdf and https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a lot — or any home — in Mount Currie?** No. Mount Currie is the main community of the Líl̓wat Nation on reserve land; housing and serviced lots are allocated and financed through the Nation's own process for its citizens — there is no fee-simple open market and no lot inventory. Inquiries go to the Líl̓wat Nation (info@lilwat.ca, 604-894-6115). https://lilwat.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/LWN7610_BuildingCommunitiesFlatsheet.pdf and https://lilwat.ca/our-community/about-us/
- **Q: How does a Líl̓wat citizen get a lot and a home?** Through the Nation's five steps: meet the Housing Manager; get a Lands Department land encumbrance check confirming a serviced lot is available and registered to you; obtain a Band Council Resolution (the Council acts as guarantor); check Indigenous Services Canada housing subsidies and the Ministerial Loan Guarantee; then arrange bank financing. (§2)
- **Q: Is there a Form B, strata fees or a depreciation report on a Mount Currie home?** No — there is no strata corporation on reserve land, so the whole BC strata diligence package does not exist here. That package applies to off-reserve strata product in Pemberton and Squamish, not to this community. (§3)
- **Q: What about property tax and PTT on a Mount Currie home?** Standard municipal/regional property taxes and provincial PTT do not apply to reserve land the way they apply off reserve; the Nation's own taxation laws or per-lot service fees are UNVERIFIED in this program — ask the Nation. No tax figure for this ground is quoted in this file. (§3)
- **Q: I saw a "Mt. Currie View Village" listing — is that here?** No. That project is at 7370 Highway 99 **in the Village of Pemberton** — manufactured homes on rented pads in a Pemberton park, named for the view of Mount Currie. Different municipality, different form, pad-rental tenure — and a home on a rented pad is not land ownership anywhere. (§2)
- **Q: If I want to buy near Mount Currie, where do I look?** The Village of Pemberton — fee-simple municipal housing with standard taxes and MLS® sales. - **Q: Which schools serve Mount Currie?** Xet̓ólacw Community School is the Nation's own school in the community, and under the Local Education Agreement with School District 48, Líl̓wat students also attend Signal Hill Elementary and Pemberton Secondary School in Pemberton, with SD48 bus routes (Bus 5) serving Mount Currie directly.
