## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Poplar is the Paragon sub-area name; the City of Abbotsford's official demographic profile name for the same ground is "South Poplar" (profile area 5, 2020 Demographic Profiles). "Poplar" alone is not a City profile area name. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- Where it sits: south of Highway 1, around Huntingdon Road, King Road and Gladwin Road, west of the Marshall Road/McCallum Road area and east toward Clearbrook Road. Exact Paragon boundary lines are not published in text form — boundaries are shown in the City WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- Nearby areas: Clearbrook to the northwest, the City Centre/South Fraser Way corridor north across Highway 1, and Sumas Prairie to the east/southeast. Highway 1 runs along the north edge with interchanges at McCallum Road and Clearbrook Road; Huntingdon Road leads toward the Huntingdon/Sumas border crossing via Highway 11/Sumas Way. https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf
- Two Poplars in one sub-area: urban lots in the north (King Road/Windsor Street/Marshall Road) and larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge. Sources: Sections 2–3 below.
- Schools physically inside: South Poplar Traditional Elementary (32746 Huntingdon Road), Jackson Elementary (33165 King Road) and Abbotsford Traditional School (2272 Windsor Street). Settled SD34 locator runs (documented in the research) for BOTH 32746 Huntingdon Road and 33165 King Road return the same regular-stream chain: Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary — including for the South Poplar address, where Jackson, not South Poplar Traditional, is the regular catchment elementary; the Traditional pathway (South Poplar → Abbotsford Traditional School, Grades 6–12) is separate. Any other address needs its own run. https://southpoplar.abbyschools.ca/ and https://ats.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/registration/atms
- Transit: BC Transit routes 3 (Clearbrook/Huntingdon), 5 (Clearbrook/Bourquin via Hospital, Marshall Road east edge), 6 (Gladwin corridor) and 12 (UFV/Bourquin via Jackson Street). https://www.bctransit.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cfv_map_21.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata, or presale LOT offering was verified in Poplar on an official or developer source. What trades is individual fee-simple parcels — urban RS lots in the north and rural/A1 lots on the south edge. ### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple detached parcels — the default form.** The established stock is freehold detached housing — on urban lots in the north and on larger rural lots toward Huntingdon Road and the south edge; strata in the sub-area is limited to the townhome complexes along King Road and Windsor Street/Marshall Road. Bare land trades in the same fee-simple form. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** urban minimums are set by the RS zones — RS3 minimum interior lot 540 m² and RS5 minimum interior lot 420 m² in City rezoning reports; rural A1 land subdivides at a minimum of 8.0 ha. These are zone minimums, not surveyed sizes for any individual parcel. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/ and https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **Bare-land STRATA: none verified in Poplar.** No bare-land strata subdivision is documented in the research or siblings for this area. The BC strata block in Section 3 is included only so a buyer who encounters that form elsewhere is not misled — it governs no Poplar lot documented here.
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** rural lots at the south edge toward Huntingdon Road are in or beside the ALR; no Poplar-wide ALR percentage is published, so no split figure is stated — status is parcel-by-parcel. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/content/coa/coabylaws/z/zoning/?xsl=/templates/browse.xsl
- **Servicing:** urban north lots sit in the City's serviced urban fabric (parcel-level confirmation still required); lots at the rural south edge may be on wells and onsite sewage rather than community systems — wells are recorded in GWELLS and onsite systems must comply with the Sewerage System Regulation through Fraser Health. Never assume south-edge servicing matches King Road servicing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Drainage/flood character:** Poplar drains toward the Sumas Prairie system, which the City describes as a low-lying agricultural area subject to Nooksack River overflow flooding — that description is for Sumas Prairie; a floodplain finding for a single Poplar lot is UNVERIFIED until checked against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E in WebMap. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Market context, labelled correctly:** SnapStats recorded Poplar detached inventory 27 / solds 2 (7%) and attached inventory 30 / solds 3 (10%) in September 2026; Abbotsford community-level detached median was $949,000. SnapStats has NO land category (Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes" combined) — no figure here is presented as a land figure. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report (research file above).

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning: Abbotsford Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014.** Urban lots are in the RS family (RS3 540 m² / RS5 420 m² minimum interior lots per City reports); southern lots fall under rural/agricultural zoning, and where A1 applies the minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/bylaw-services/bylaw-listings/zoning-bylaw-2014
- **OCP: Abbotsforward 2050 (Bylaw 2600-2016, as updated) governs; CURRENT form is Abbotsforward 2050, adopted December 2025.** No Poplar-specific neighbourhood plan is listed in the OCP. STALE-CITATION FLAG: bare "2600-2016"/"2500-2016" citations without the Abbotsforward 2050 currency are stale — Jerry sweep item where a live page carries them. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2025bschedulea
- **SSMUH:** City-confirmed June 2024 adoption; the SSMUH bylaw NUMBER is UNCONFIRMED — "3560-2024" must not be asserted; the conflict is recorded, not resolved. Abbotsford suite rules (Abbotsford ONLY): one secondary suite in all single detached dwellings under Zoning Bylaw s.140.3.8, with City-stated criteria including a 400 m² minimum lot and 12.0 m minimum frontage; suite cap under 50% of the principal dwelling; coach/garden suite 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m². Verify the lot meets the suite criteria before pricing suite income into land value. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements beyond zone minimums:** UNCONFIRMED in detail in the research — not filled in. Confirm subdivision servicing and frontage requirements with the City for the specific parcel.
- **ALR rules (south edge):** subdivision and added dwellings on ALR land follow the Agricultural Land Commission Act, the ALR Use Regulation and City zoning; subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval as the ALC states it. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — full three-tier form:** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 (under 0.8 ha); applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Airport diligence:** Poplar is east of YXX; where a lot falls inside the Airport Zoning Regulation area on City drawing M-500, AZR height limits and NAV CANADA/Transport Canada approvals apply before a City permit. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/docs/business-development/Development%20Guidlines%20for%20YXX%202020.pdf
- **Flood diligence:** check the lot against Zoning Bylaw Schedule E Floodplain Boundaries in WebMap before removing subjects — drainage toward Sumas Prairie makes this a live question on south-edge lots, and no lot-level finding is stated here. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/mapping-data-analytics/webmap
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M; FTHB Program removes PTT on the first $500,000 at ≤$835,000 (max $8,000, partial to $860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000 / partial to $1,150,000 is a new-HOME exemption — bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. Tax Rates Bylaw No. 3727-2026 is the current rates bylaw; City taxes due July 2, 2026, 5% penalty July 3, 2026, second 5% September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use, in-the-course-of-business, and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable — developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED per parcel. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Poplar-specific position documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here.
- **Bare-land strata block (none verified in Poplar — form guidance only):** a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B, bylaws and strata fees apply to the lot; 5-year depreciation-report cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/depreciation-reports/depreciation-report-requirements
- **Heritage note:** the Old South Poplar School is on the Abbotsford Community Heritage Register and the community was named for poplar trees planted on the hilltop — a title/heritage check is a diligence item for older south-edge parcels near the school site. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2006%20Commission%20-%20PRC%2028%20Abbotsford%20Community%20Heritage%20Register%20Addition....pdf

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is the smallest urban lot I can create in Poplar?** Zone-dependent: RS3 minimum interior 540 m², RS5 minimum interior 420 m² per City reports — and subdivision approval/servicing for the specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the City confirms it. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/56399/
- **Q: Can I put a suite on a Poplar house lot and count the income?** One suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings under s.140.3.8 if the lot meets the criteria (400 m² minimum, 12.0 m frontage, cap under 50%) — verify before pricing it in. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Is a Huntingdon Road lot in the ALR?** South-edge lots are in or beside the ALR — check the parcel; in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and the A1 minimum is 8.0 ha. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Will a south-edge lot have city water and sewer?** It may be on well and septic instead. Pull the GWELLS record and Fraser Health sewerage filings. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners
- **Q: Is Poplar flood risk real for a building lot?** Drainage runs toward the Sumas Prairie system the City calls low-lying and Nooksack-overflow-prone; your lot's status is a Schedule E/WebMap check, lot by lot — UNVERIFIED until done. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: Does the airport cap my build height?** Only if the lot is inside the AZR area on drawing M-500 — then height surfaces and federal approvals apply before a City permit. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Will I pay GST on a vacant Poplar lot?** Developer/newly subdivided = generally taxable; individual personal-use = generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Confirm the vendor's status in writing. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Are Kings Crossing lots for sale bare?** No — Kings Crossing is sold as 20 finished townhomes (Rockwood Homes, completion expected 2026), documented in the townhomes file. No bare lots are offered there on any source checked. https://thekingscrossing.ca/contact-us/
- **Q: What are holding costs on vacant land here?** City taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalties July 3 and September 16, 2026 — Tax Rates Bylaw 3727-2026) plus servicing maintenance; no strata fees on a fee-simple lot. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools serve a King Road lot?** The settled chain for 33165 King Road is Jackson Elementary → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary. Run any other address yourself in the locator. (Section 1.)

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **"South Poplar" is the City's name; "Poplar" is the board's.** A title, tax, or City-document search under only one name can miss the other — use both when pulling City records for a parcel. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-02/2020%20McKee%20Profile%20-%20spreads%20final.pdf
- **The elementary school on Huntingdon Road is not the regular catchment for its own address.** The locator run for 32746 Huntingdon Road returns Jackson Elementary for the regular stream; South Poplar Traditional feeds the Traditional pathway to Abbotsford Traditional School instead. Land bought "for the school across the road" needs the locator run, not the map. (Section 1.)
- **No shopping centre inside Poplar was verified** on an official source; the nearest verified centre is Sevenoaks on South Fraser Way at Gladwin Road, north of Highway 1 (its civic address is UNVERIFIED in the research). North-edge lots and south-edge lots live different daily lives. (Research file §4.)
