## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), VERBATIM (sourcing as the sibling open-houses files sourced it):** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation frame around that exception:** in BC a licensed real estate professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party, and must disclose at the outset whether they will be able to represent the consumer as a client. Dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018, except in the rarest of circumstances — the host cannot simply "also represent" a buyer who walks in. The regulator is BCFSA. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend a Langley open house?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Q: Which time do I show up — the card says 2–4, the line under it says 1–3?** Neither is verified as authoritative: this slug's research documents exactly that one-hour split on every labelled card on THIS feed, with listing text that sometimes matches the block and sometimes matches neither. Confirm the actual time with the listing brokerage before you drive. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-open-houses.html
- **Q: Does every listing on this page hold an open house?** Not on the evidence of the first page: five of twelve cards carried an open-house time block and seven carried no open-house label or time at all — treat an unlabelled card as a listing to confirm privately, not as an open house. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I have to sign in or show ID?** No BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source states an identification or sign-in requirement — UNVERIFIED as a rule; an individual host may still ask, on the seller's instructions. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Which Langley areas appear on the feed?** On the first page read: Aldergrove, Willoughby Heights and Walnut Grove, across apartment condos, a townhome and detached houses; whether the full 158-listing feed reaches every sub-area is UNVERIFIED, because only the first page was read. https://jaredgibbons.ca/langley-open-houses.html
- **Q: If the home I view is fee-simple, is there still a Form B to ask for?** No — a fee-simple home has NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees; diligence is title, municipal rules (Township Bylaw 2500 or Langley City Bylaw 2025 No. 3300, depending on which municipality the home is in) and any registered covenants. Ask the host which tenure the home is before asking for strata documents that may not exist. (§3.)
- **Q: Can the host also represent me if I want to offer?** Generally no — dual agency was prohibited in BC in 2018 except in the rarest of circumstances, and a licensee must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: What PTT would follow if I buy after viewing?** Worked in §2: $12,000 at $700,000, $22,000 at $1,200,000 and $28,000 at $1,500,000 on the general tiers, before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Does the suite or coach house I saw change the rules?** It depends on the municipality: Township suite cap 120 m² and a coach house is fee-simple under Bylaw 2500 s.102; Langley City suite size is the lesser of 100 m² and 40% under Bylaw 2025 No. 3300. Legality for a specific lot is a municipal check, UNCONFIRMED from the feed. (§3.)
- **Q: Which school serves a home I viewed?** SD35 School Locator by exact address — the one documented sample (7888 200 Street) returns Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, Peter Ewart Middle School and R.E. Mountain Secondary, and that result does not carry to the next street. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
