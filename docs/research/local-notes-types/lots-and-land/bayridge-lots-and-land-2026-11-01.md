## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market exists in Bayridge — stated plainly at the top. The slope trades as fee-simple single-family lots (slope lots above Marine Drive and waterfront lots on it) in the RS zones, with no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, no named condo or townhome complex, and no presale lot offering verified on any official or developer source. The land diligence that matters here is the foreshore/watercourse/wildfire permit frame and the RS minimum-site-area ladder.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default (and only) form

- **Established slope and waterfront lots:** single-family dwellings in the RS zones of Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010, Section 200, on the slope above Marine Drive and on the waterfront streets — predominantly freehold detached in the sources read; any specific strata product in the area is UNVERIFIED. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Lot character from the zone ladder, as stated:** RS minimum site areas run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1), with RS2 at 1,625 m², RS3 and RS10 at 975 m² and RS4 at 731 m². These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size; the zone on a specific lot is shown on the District's Zoning Map (Bylaw Section 852). Secondary suites and a detached secondary suite are permitted uses in the single-family zones, and secondary suites must be registered with the District. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Serviced position, as the District states it:** West Vancouver water and sewer utilities are metered and billed quarterly by the District — the serviced position for District lots as the District states it. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 4662, 2010 (Section 200):** the RS single-family zones state the minimum site areas in Section 2 and list secondary suites and a detached secondary suite among permitted uses; secondary suites must be registered with the District. The bylaw's ground-oriented dwelling rules state 4 dwellings maximum per lot, and 6 maximum if the site is wholly or partly within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop. https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ZONING_BYLAW_4662_SECTION_620_TO_630_0.pdf
- **OCP — Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 4985, 2018:** the District plan covering the area; Council adopted OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 5406, 2025, bringing the OCP into alignment with the Interim Housing Needs Report. A local area plan naming Bayridge is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/housing/official-community-plan-housing-policy-update
- **SSMUH / added units as the District states it:** RS-zone lots carry the suite positions above (secondary suite + detached secondary suite, registration required) and the 4-dwelling / 6-near-bus-stop ground-oriented caps — eligibility and form are parcel-specific under Bylaw 4662; confirm with the District before pricing in suite potential. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots/secondary-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no District of West Vancouver subdivision/servicing bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted and none is invented; the process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's RS zone minimum site area (Section 2), servicing is District metered water and sewer billed quarterly, and hazard development permits (below) gate waterfront and creek-side work. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the District for the specific parcel. https://westvancouver.ca/services/taxes-utility-fees/utility-fees
- **Hazard Development Permits (District frame — the live Bayridge checks):** a Foreshore Development Permit for work within the Foreshore Development Permit Area (waterfront property or other properties at risk of coastal flooding); an Environmental Development Permit for work within 15 metres of the top of any watercourse bank; and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit for work within the Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Area, described as within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. The foreshore area is the live check on Marine Drive waterfront lots. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Strata block (for completeness — no Bayridge strata stock is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Bayridge ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Bayridge-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Bayridge an official West Vancouver neighbourhood?** No — no official District neighbourhood or planning-area name "Bayridge" was confirmed; it is a local area/site name for the West Bay–Westmount slope, covered District-wide by OCP Bylaw No. 4985, 2018. Sell the verifiable anchors, not an official-neighbourhood claim. (FILE STATUS; Section 1.)
- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Bayridge?** No verified one — the slope is established fee-simple houses, and the condos/townhomes siblings verify zero strata complexes here. No presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a lot be subdivided?** By zone: RS minimums run from 488 m² (RS5) to 8,094 m² (RS1) — RS2 1,625 m², RS3/RS10 975 m², RS4 731 m² in between. The parcel's zone is on the District Zoning Map (Section 852). https://westvancouver.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/zoning-bylaw-4662-section-200-single-family-dwelling-zones.pdf
- **Q: What permit does a Marine Drive waterfront lot need?** A Foreshore Development Permit — the Foreshore Development Permit Area covers waterfront property at risk of coastal flooding, and new habitable space must meet the Flood Construction Level where that risk applies. Run the check on every waterfront lot. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: What about creek-side or forest-edge lots?** An Environmental Development Permit within 15 metres of a watercourse bank top, and a Wildfire Hazard Development Permit within 100 metres of an undisturbed forest interface. https://www.westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/building-permits-inspections/development-permits
- **Q: Can a Bayridge lot add suites?** RS zones permit a secondary suite and a detached secondary suite (suites must be registered with the District), and ground-oriented rules cap a lot at 4 dwellings (6 within 400 metres of a prescribed bus stop) — parcel-specific. https://westvancouver.ca/business-development/building-development/development-regulations/single-family-lots/secondary-suites
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** For the one settled run — 3175 Thompson Place → West Bay Elementary + Rockridge Secondary (SD45 locator, run 2026-10-21) — that result is single-address only and non-transferable. Verify every other address in the locator. West Bay Elementary itself (K–7, IB Primary Years Programme) sits in the area at 3175 Thompson Place. https://westvancouverschools.ca/westbay-elementary/
- **Q: Does the additional school tax apply here?** It can: 0.2% on the residential assessed portion between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. Price it into higher-value deals. https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What transit serves the slope?** Route 250 on Marine Drive only — east through Dundarave, Ambleside and Park Royal over the Lions Gate Bridge to downtown Vancouver, west toward Horseshoe Bay. Off Marine Drive the area is car-oriented. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt250.pdf
