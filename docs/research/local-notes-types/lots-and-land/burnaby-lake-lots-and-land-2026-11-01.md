## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026 (No. 14815), adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026** — never cite the repealed 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 as current. The surrounding residential streets follow the R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) rules: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop. Confirm the parcel's district in the 2026 bylaw. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replacing the 1998 OCP, with a parcel-based land use map assigning each lot a specific designation; it takes precedence over older community-plan maps where they conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/official-community-plan
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Burnaby's servicing-bylaw BASE number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program (the bylaw was renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024) — no base number is asserted here. The documented process: new homes and SSMUH projects require Engineering pre-application approval through My Permits Portal before a building permit application, and where offsite servicing requirements are identified in the Pre-Approval Report, a cash-in-lieu contribution to City infrastructure is required, calculated on Engineering unit rates. Confirm subdivision and servicing requirements with the City for the parcel. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/development-permits-construction/new-home-construction
- **Lake-edge diligence:** the lake shore is low, marshy wildlife habitat; a floodplain designation for the sub-area's residential streets was not confirmed in the sources read. For lake-edge and creek-edge lots, check flood, habitat and watercourse-setback conditions early — setback surprises surface late in deals. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Bare-land strata rules (applies to any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever verified here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Burnaby Lake ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Burnaby Lake lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot on Burnaby Lake?** No verified one — the lake itself is a 360-acre regional park, and the surrounding streets are built fee-simple house lots. No bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Does a park-edge home come with lake frontage or dock rights?** No — the park is public land managed by Metro Vancouver under a 25-year agreement; a home borders or overlooks the park, it does not come with lake frontage rights, and the park's wildlife rules are part of living there. http://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-02-05/city-burnaby-and-metro-vancouver-sign-burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Q: How many units can a house lot here hold?** Under the R1 SSMUH rules: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — Sperling–Burnaby Lake Station proximity is the parcel-specific question on the west side. Confirm the parcel's district and transit distance with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Is Savile Row a Burnaby Lake land opportunity?** No — it is a completed 2018 freehold strata townhouse complex on Canada Way, counted in the burnaby-lake townhomes file; listings file it under Burnaby Lake while its marketing says "heart of Deer Lake." A buyer buys a strata townhouse unit there, not a lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What checks does a lake-edge lot need?** Flood, habitat and watercourse-setback checks, early — the shoreline is protected wildlife habitat, and no floodplain designation for the residential streets was confirmed in the research, so the position is parcel-specific. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/burnaby-lake-regional-park
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established single-family homes on municipal water (2026 charge $652.33) with sewer as a parcel tax — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City before writing. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Burnaby Lake lot?** By exact address only — Sperling Elementary, École Seaforth Elementary and Burnaby Central Secondary sit beside the edges, but no catchment for any address in this slug is verified in this file; run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: What is being built at the lake?** A new Burnaby Lake Recreation Centre beside Bill Copeland Sports Centre, past the halfway mark in the City's capital program, plus a delivered pedestrian and cycling overpass across Highway 1 — civic amenity, not lot supply. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2026-05-28/how-burnaby-funding-growth-through-responsible-use-reserves
