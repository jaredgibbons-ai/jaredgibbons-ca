## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Steveston South is the City of Richmond's south-west corner, on the South Arm of the Fraser River. The board sub-area name is "Steveston South" (GVR listing data); the City does not use "Steveston South" as a planning name — the district sits inside the Steveston planning area governed by the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the City Official Community Plan). The board's exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; in listing practice the north/south split of Steveston is generally drawn at Steveston Highway, with the village core, Garry Point and the residential streets south of the highway in Steveston South. Nearby areas: Steveston Village (the historic core, inside this district's south-west), Steveston North (across Steveston Highway), London/Steveston and South Arm (to the east), Seafair (to the north-east). https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- The Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area sits at the district's south-west waterfront, and properties inside it are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- Parks and waterfront: Garry Point Park, a 39-acre waterfront park at the mouth of the Fraser River; Imperial Landing Waterfront Park on the historic BC Packers cannery site; the West Dyke Trail along the west dike; the Steveston Greenways linking the waterfront and historic sites; Steveston Community Park (4011 Moncton Street), a 30-acre park with the Steveston Community Centre, pool and library in the park complex; Richmond Public Library's Steveston Branch (Easthope Hub) is at 4320 Moncton Street. The Gulf of Georgia Cannery at 12138 4th Avenue is a national historic site. https://www.richmond.ca/parks-recreation/parks-trails-cycling/trails-map/steveston.htm and https://parks.canada.ca/culture/designation/lieu-site/gulf-of-georgia
- Main roads: Moncton Street through the village, No. 1 Road north to Richmond centre, Steveston Highway east to Highway 99, Railway Avenue north–south at the east side of Steveston, and No. 2 Road north from the village. Transit: buses serve Steveston Exchange on Chatham Street, including TransLink route 401 via No. 1 Road to Richmond–Brighouse Station for Canada Line connections. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/__shared/assets/CNCL_062209_Bylaw_840323090.pdf and https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/218/tt401.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Steveston South has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. The documented land form is the built RS1/RS2 detached house lot on the residential streets — older fishing-village-era homes near the village core to later subdivision housing further east and north — carrying a permitted secondary suite under measured size rules. Strata product concentrates at the village core and at the London Landing east edge, and is built product documented in the condos/townhomes programs — cross-referenced below, never lot inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the documented form

- **Lot character and minimums, as the research states them:** houses sit in Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500 Single Detached zones RS1 and RS2 (Section 8.1), with minimum lot sizes varying by subzone in Section 8.1.8 — for example RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m², RS1/F 828.0 m² and RS1/K 315.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones. A sourced area-wide typical lot-size range for the district: UNVERIFIED in the research, and none is invented here. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Suite rules on the lot, as the bylaw states them:** a secondary suite in a single-detached house must meet Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500 — at least 33.0 m², and at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m². https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Planning framework — Richmond 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000 + Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4):** the district sits inside the Steveston planning area governed by the Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the OCP. https://richmond.ca/__shared/assets/steveston566.pdf
- **Heritage — Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area:** properties inside the conservation area at the district's south-west waterfront are subject to Heritage Alteration Permit requirements under the Steveston Area Plan; near the village, check heritage status first — it affects renovation plans and timelines on the lot. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1) + suite rules (Section 5.4):** minimum lot sizes by subzone in Section 8.1.8 (RS1/B 360.0 m²; RS1/E 550.0 m²; RS1/F 828.0 m²; RS1/K 315.0 m²; 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones); secondary suites under Section 5.4 (minimum 33.0 m²; maximum the lesser of 40% of the dwelling or 110 m²). Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing zones RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL were added under provincial Bill 44 legislation. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units, as the City states them:** the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Check the suite math before valuing rental income on a lot. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is the Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011), with works and services cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (2011) — verified ledger position for Richmond, confirmed on the City's bylaw list. Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204 + Steveston Island Dike:** the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels; for waterfront and dyke-adjacent lots, confirm both before pricing in a rebuild or addition, and watch the City's Steveston Island Dike project — its named long-term flood project for this waterfront. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf and https://www.letstalkrichmond.ca/StevestonIslandDike
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Steveston South ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat line is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer at that cap); $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Richmond property taxes:** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply in Richmond. No Richmond-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research, and the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a Steveston South lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file — not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Steveston South?** No verified bare-land offering exists — the district is built village and subdivision housing. Land trades as a detached house on its RS1/RS2 lot. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is "Steveston South" a City neighbourhood?** No — it is the board (GVR) listing sub-area name; the City plans this district as part of the Steveston planning area under the Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the OCP. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: How big can a suite on my lot be?** Under Section 5.4 of Bylaw 8500: at least 33.0 m², and at most the lesser of 40% of the dwelling floor area or 110 m²; the City also allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot under its Bill 44 rules. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf and https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Can I add more units under Bill 44?** The City's stated minimums are 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots, with the RSM/S, RSM/M, RSM/L and RSM/XL zones applied under Bill 44 — verify the lot's zone and subzone minimums before promising redevelopment value. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: What heritage rules touch a village-edge lot?** Homes inside the Steveston Village Heritage Conservation Area need a Heritage Alteration Permit for exterior changes — check heritage status before planning renovations on any lot near the village core. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: What flood diligence applies to a waterfront lot?** Confirm dike setbacks and the flood construction level under Bylaw 8204 before pricing in a rebuild or addition; the district sits behind the 1:500-design dike system with an active raising program, and the Steveston Island Dike project is the City's long-term project for this waterfront. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **Q: Which bylaws govern subdividing a Richmond lot?** Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011), with works and services cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (2011); parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Q: Is the London Landing townhouse stock at 13200 Princess Street something I can rely on as strata?** No — it is QUEUED with tenure UNCONFIRMED: City council material from 2002 documents the development by London Landing Development Corp., but no source read states whether it is strata or fee-simple. Settle tenure on title; it is counted nowhere in this program. https://citycouncil.richmond.ca/agendas/archives/dpp/2002/103002_minutes.htm
- **Q: Which schools serve a Steveston South address?** UNVERIFIED by address — Lord Byng, Steves and Westwind elementaries and R.A. McMath Secondary are physically in the district, but SD38 catchments are address-based and no locator run for a district address is documented in the research; run the exact address before relying on a catchment. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Will GST apply if a developer sells me a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such lot offering is verified in Steveston South today. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
