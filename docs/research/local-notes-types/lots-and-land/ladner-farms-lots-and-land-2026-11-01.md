## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: there is no bare-land subdivision market in the Ladner farm belt — no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata farm product, and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source. What trades is whole fee-simple farm and agricultural parcels under the OCP's Agriculture (A) designation and A1 zoning, most of it ALR ground. No named subdivision or bare-land development is listed in this section because none is verified — assemblies, proposals and feed cards are not inventory, and none is presented as such.**

Tenure in the research's own words: farm parcels are overwhelmingly freehold; strata farm product is not a Delta pattern, and no strata farm complex was confirmed on an official source. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** Farm land is designated "Agriculture (A)" (s. 1.5.1: to maintain agricultural lands and promote a thriving agricultural sector), and the Agriculture policies at s. 2.6 protect the ALR and soil-based agriculture. **"Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR wherever met** — the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1 under the OLD Bylaw 3950. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Zoning bylaw number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted.** City pages cite No. 7600, 2017, while an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates; the A1 zone sheet read for this topic is cited as Part 10 as amended by Bylaw 8285, 2023. Neither number is asserted as settled; the ruling is Jared's. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **A1 residential limits (Delta's own numbers, layered over the ALC caps):** one farm house per lot, maximum total floor area 500 m²; farm home plate maximum 3,600 m² for a farm house with or without an accessory farm dwelling unit (5,000 m² where an additional farm house is also on the plate). One accessory farm dwelling unit per lot, maximum 90 m² on lots of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on lots over 40 ha, on the same farm home plate as the farm house; ALC approval is required for more than one. An additional farm house (180 m² maximum on lots under 8 ha; 233 m² on lots of 8 ha or more) requires a Professional Agrologist's report supporting its necessity and written ALC approval. A secondary suite in A1 is one suite accessory to a farm house. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Migrant farm worker housing in A1:** permitted only where, among other criteria, the lot is in the ALR with farming as a permitted use, the lot is classified as a farm under the Assessment Act, written ALC approval has been obtained, the farm employing the workers is at least 8 ha and the lot is at least 4 ha; new buildings for it must be manufactured homes on a temporary foundation or footings. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta adopted its SSMUH zoning amendments as Bylaws 8394 and 8395 on June 24, 2024. In the farm belt the practical added-unit rules are the A1/ALR rules above, not the urban SSMUH frame — SSMUH does not create a subdivision or strata right on ALR ground. (Standing Delta frame for this program.)
- **Subdivision and servicing standards — status stated precisely:** subdivision in A1 additionally requires OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable (§3 ALR block). The Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 is cited from civicweb / a January 2024 council report; **final adoption of Bylaw 8288 and the parcel-level servicing standards under it are UNCONFIRMED in this file** — confirm with the City before pricing any subdivision or servicing assumption.
- **ALR block — full form:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m², with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction without an ALC application; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence — never in a barn or a detached building; one additional residence for farm use or another permitted use, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Local governments may further regulate or restrict residences in the ALR — Delta's A1 numbers above are that layer. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — the full three-tier form:** $2,500 of gross farm income on a farm operation of 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha on operations over 4 ha; $10,000 on operations under 0.8 ha — with applications by October 31 for the next taxation year. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; no such flat line is asserted in this file. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Strata block — stated for completeness, governing no farm-belt stock:** if a strata lot were ever lawfully produced on non-ALR ground, the BC block would apply to the LOT — Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. On ALR parcels the strata form is barred outright (§2). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. First-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are home exemptions; their application to a bare farm parcel purchase is facts-specific: UNCONFIRMED for bare land in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant/farm land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Farm-land sales in particular are treated by the CRA in separate material — the GST treatment of any specific Ladner farm parcel is UNCONFIRMED until the vendor's status and the parcel's history are established; have the contract state it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The older GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in the Ladner farm belt?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering exists here. What trades is whole fee-simple farm and agricultural parcels under Agriculture (A) / A1. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is a listing on the Ladner farms page a confirmed farm operation in that sector?** No. The feed is generic — it spanned Ladner Rural and East Delta Agri-Business cards at reading, and no card is presented in this file as a confirmed operation in any named sector. Sector, quota and board position for any parcel: UNVERIFIED — diligence them from the operation's own records, not the feed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/ladner-farms.html
- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land near Ladner?** A principal residence built without an ALC application must have a total floor area of 500 m² or less, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill placed for its construction; Delta's A1 zone sets the same 500 m² farm house maximum and a 3,600 m² farm home plate maximum. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Ladner-area farm have a second dwelling?** Yes, within limits: Delta's A1 zone permits one accessory farm dwelling unit per lot — 90 m² or less on lots of 40 ha or smaller, 186 m² or less on larger lots — on the same farm home plate as the farm house; more than one needs ALC approval, and an additional farm house needs a Professional Agrologist's necessity report plus written ALC approval. A suite goes only inside the principal residence, never in a barn. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide or stratify a Delta farm parcel?** Only within tight limits: in A1 a new lot created by subdivision needs a minimum of 8 ha, OCP compliance and Agricultural Land Commission Act approval where applicable, with a home site severance exception under ALC policy; and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all without the ALC's permission (ALCA s. 21(1); SPA s. 239; ALC Bulletin 09, s. 6). https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What income does a farm need to keep farm classification?** Three tiers, never a flat figure: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha above that; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — application due by October 31 for the next taxation year. Leased production can qualify where the owner applies, a lease is in place, the land contributes reasonably to the operation, and the lessee-farmer meets the income requirements. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: What water and drainage diligence does a farm parcel need?** Confirm the irrigation source and any water licence position — non-domestic groundwater use requires a licence under the Water Sustainability Act — and get the ditch and drainage position from the City, not from a listing's wording. Parcel-level Flood Construction Levels are property-specific and UNVERIFIED for any parcel in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/water-licensing-rights
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy a farm parcel?** It turns on the seller and the parcel's history, per CRA GI-003: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a sale in the course of a business or of a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable. Farm-land sales have their own CRA treatment — the position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will it cost to hold the parcel?** Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalty after, a further 5% after September 2, 2026), plus insurance, ditch/drainage and field maintenance, and the operating cost of producing the farm income the classification depends on. There is no strata fee: the parcel is fee-simple. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools serve a farm family here?** Catchments are address-based in SD37 and UNVERIFIED for any farm address in this pass — the Ministry-listed Ladner schools are in §1, but no address's catchment is asserted. Run the exact address in the SD37 locator; SD37 runs are on the program's owed queue. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/
