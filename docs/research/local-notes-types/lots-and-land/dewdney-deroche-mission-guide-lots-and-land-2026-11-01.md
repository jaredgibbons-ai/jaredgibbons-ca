- **Jurisdiction — FVRD Electoral Area G (stated expressly):** the FVRD is the local government for this unincorporated ground. City of Mission bylaws (including OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 and Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020), Mission suite rules and Mission tax dates do NOT apply here and are never borrowed. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Document~Library/EA%20Housing%20Needs%20Report.pdf

(Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-18/dewdney-deroche-mission-guide.md)
- **Property taxes — RURAL regime (stated expressly):** a property not located in a city, town, district or village is in a rural area; its owner receives the tax notice from the Province's Surveyor of Taxes Office and pays the Province, not the FVRD or the City of Mission. Under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act, s.11, rural taxes are due July 2, a 5% penalty is added to any portion unpaid on July 2, and a further 5% to any portion unpaid on October 31. **The City of Mission flat 10% penalty does NOT apply to this ground.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Flood:** the FVRD has issued freshet evacuation alerts for Area G ground including Nicomen Island and the Taylor Road area in Deroche, and lists Dewdney Regional Park among flood-related park closures — the lowland corridor is Fraser River floodplain ground. A specific property's flood status, dyke protection and flood-construction requirements are UNVERIFIED at parcel level. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/freshet-update.html
- **ALR block (applies where a parcel is ALR — status per-parcel, UNVERIFIED here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Secondary dwellings, non-ALR:** follows the parcel's FVRD zoning — bylaw numbers and zone names UNVERIFIED (above), so a second-dwelling plan is a per-PID FVRD confirmation, never a Mission "s"-variant rezoning (that is a City process and does not exist here). - **Q: Are Dewdney and Deroche part of Mission?** No. Listings carry Mission mailing addresses, but both are unincorporated FVRD Electoral Area G communities — the FVRD's housing-needs report lists Area G as "Nicomen Island, Deroche, Dewdney, Hatzic Island" — and the City of Mission does not govern them. - **Q: Who do I pay property tax to, and what is the penalty?** The Province (Surveyor of Taxes). Rural taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty on amounts unpaid on July 2 and a further 5% on amounts unpaid on October 31 (Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11). Mission's flat 10% penalty does NOT apply here. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01
- **Q: Is a park home on Lougheed Highway or Taylor Road a land purchase?** No — a manufactured home on a rented pad is a home plus a pad tenancy; you do not buy the land. MHPTA pad-rent limits are Act percentages only (2026 = 2.3% + proportional). Confirm the current pad rent and park rules from the park's own documents; park terms here are UNVERIFIED. (§§2–3.)
- **Q: Can I build a second home on an acreage here?** It depends on the parcel's FVRD zoning and, for farmland, the ALR: ALR parcels are limited to one principal residence of at most 500 m² plus at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha). FVRD bylaw numbers and zone names for these lots are UNVERIFIED in this file — confirm both gates for the specific PID. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How is a lot here serviced?** Possibly by an FVRD community system (a Deroche water system and an Area G Dewdney Community Water System exist in FVRD bylaws), possibly by a private well and septic — parcel servicing is UNVERIFIED in this file, so confirm water source, sewer vs septic and any system fees for the specific parcel. https://www.fvrd.ca/assets/Government/Documents/Bylaws/Sewer~and~Water/CONSOLIDATED%20Water%20Systems%20Regulations%20and%20Fees.pdf
- **Q: Is flood risk real on the lowland lots?** Yes as a diligence item: the FVRD has issued freshet evacuation alerts for Area G ground including Nicomen Island and the Taylor Road area in Deroche. A specific property's flood status and dyke protection are UNVERIFIED at parcel level — check the PID. https://www.fvrd.ca/EN/meta/news/news-archives/2022/freshet-update.html
- **Q: Which schools serve Dewdney and Deroche?** Dewdney Elementary (37151 Hawkins-Pickle Rd B107) and Deroche Elementary (10340 N Deroche Rd), both SD75, are confirmed by name and address in the Ministry file; older students' confirmed schools are Hatzic Middle School and Mission Senior Secondary in Mission — but catchment assignment for a specific address is UNVERIFIED, so confirm with School District 75. ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/education/work/bc_k12_schools.csv
