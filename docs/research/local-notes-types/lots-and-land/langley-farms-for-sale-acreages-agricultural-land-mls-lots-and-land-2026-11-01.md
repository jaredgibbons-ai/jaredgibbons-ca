## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township frame in full:** Zoning Bylaw 2500 applies across the Township. SSMUH Bylaw 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — eligible lots (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units at 280 m² or smaller or 4 units from 280 m² to 4,050 m²; larger lots are not eligible; **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020.** Secondary suite cap 120 m² (s.102); a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under s.102 — an accessory unit only within the second storey of a detached garage — never strata. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 as amended July 2025 lists suite locations including townhouse/rowhouse at the 120 m² cap, while the Township's January 2025 Secondary Suites guide position differs on townhouse suites. - **Township subdivision/servicing:** Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. Subdivision of Township land runs under that bylaw and Bylaw 2500, and, in the ALR, only with ALC approval (below). Per-parcel servicing costs are UNCONFIRMED and none is stated.

- **ALR block (full):** a parcel's reserve status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. Subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). Housing caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only entirely within the principal residence; one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application at 90 m² maximum on properties 40 ha or less, and 186 m² on properties more than 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it; local governments may further regulate or restrict ALR residences. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class — three-tier, in full (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of **$2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha** — farm classification applications are due by **October 31**. This three-tier form is asserted here because THIS file's own research documents it (BC Assessment); it is the same form the horse-properties page owns in this program. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

(Research file above.)
- **Water / septic:** non-domestic groundwater use, including irrigation, requires a water licence under the Water Sustainability Act; domestic use does not. A home not on community sewer needs a Sewerage System Regulation-compliant onsite system, workable only by an Authorized Person. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems

Finer points — including farm-business land — UNCONFIRMED at this level. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

- **Q: How big can a house be on ALR land?** A principal residence permitted without an ALC application must be 500 m² or less total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can a Langley farm have a second dwelling?** On ALR land, one additional detached residence may be permitted without an ALC application — 90 m² maximum at 40 ha or less, 186 m² above 40 ha — and Township bylaws must also permit it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR farm?** Subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). Neither is a bylaw-only question. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What income keeps farm classification?** Three tiers: $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha above 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" line is an error. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification

The Langley figures in §1 are residential community context only. (Research file above.)

Off-sewer homes need a Sewerage System Regulation-compliant onsite system, handled only by an Authorized Person. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/information-for-property-owners/well-records-registration
