## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Port Kells is the one area in this batch where land itself is the market — stated plainly at the top, in two halves that share a name and almost nothing else. North of Highway 1 the land that trades is fee-simple light-industrial (IL) land and buildings. South of Highway 1 it is fee-simple residential and rural land — suburban lots to multi-acre parcels, with ALR land woven through the pocket. What does NOT exist here, on any source checked: a named bare-land subdivision selling lots, a bare-land strata plan offered as lots, or a presale lot offering. Land trades parcel by parcel, not as a lot program.**

### North Port Kells — industrial land

- **Character, as the City states it:** North Port Kells is primarily light industrial zoning (IL) with manufacturing, construction and wholesale/distribution concentrations — roughly 610 businesses across a 781-acre industrial area profiled by the City as one of Surrey's major industrial areas. The stock is warehouses, work yards and industrial buildings on fee-simple industrial parcels; it is employment land, not housing land of any form. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plan and zoning:** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is the Surrey 2050 OCP, **Bylaw No. 21900, adopted June 15, 2026** — the former OCP, Bylaw No. 18020, is replaced and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Area-level guidance is the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan; North Port Kells is primarily light industrial (IL) zoning under **Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000**. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf and https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential (Surrey, as the City states it):** Zoning Bylaw No. 12000's SSMUH framework (in force since July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — permissions that do NOT override ALR status on a farm parcel. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/port-kells-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Secondary suites (Surrey, off-ALR parcels):** one secondary suite may be permitted in a single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling, capped at **40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one per unit, not a separate strata lot, and never inside a coach house or garden suite. ALR parcels follow the ALC rules below instead. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision and servicing — Bylaw No. 8830 (1986), verified ledger:** Surrey's subdivision/servicing instrument is the **Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified on the City's own pages in an earlier batch of this program (that sourcing is stated here because this slug's own research pass opened no servicing page); servicing runs through the City's Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process, confirmed per parcel. Port Kells also carries its own industrial and commercial DCC schedules (§2). (Standing verified ledger.)
- **Streamside setbacks:** Surrey's streamside setbacks sit in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, varying by stream class** — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for a Port Kells parcel; the applicable setback is read off Part 7A for the watercourse class on the specific lot. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R053.pdf
- **ALR block — carried in full because ALR ground is significant in Port Kells on its research:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval. ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal residence **≤500 m²**; a secondary suite **only inside the principal residence**; an additional residence of **90 m² or less on parcels of 40 ha or less, or 186 m² or less on parcels over 40 ha**, under the ALC's rules or with approval. Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR; no flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's own research, so none is asserted for this ground beyond the three-tier frame. Check the ALR boundary on any south-side parcel before pricing. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** STRs are permitted, owner-only — the host must be the property owner living in the home. The City's Short-Term Rentals page states the business licence fee is **$358 per year** — that figure is carried here. **DISCLOSED CONFLICT:** the City's 2024 corporate report on the policy proposed $350 per year plus fees — confirm the current figure on the City's licence page before quoting it. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (Townhomes sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/port-kells-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Bare-land strata frame (no such plan verified here — §2):** a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B under SPA s. 59 on resale; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; at least 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Regulation 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions). On ALR parcels the stratification bar above governs first. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; a page row reading "Over $3M — 5%" is a compression of the 3% tier plus that further 2% — the tier math governs. Worked example, inputs stated: fair market value $1,215,000 (the Surrey community Detached median for September 2026, community context only — no Port Kells median exists), fully residential, no exemption: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $20,300 (2% × $1,015,000) = **$22,300 PTT**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial $835,000–$860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; CRA governs) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself: 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What land can I actually buy in Port Kells?** Two kinds, split by the highway: fee-simple light-industrial (IL) land and buildings north of Highway 1, and fee-simple houses and rural parcels in South Port Kells — suburban lots to multi-acre rural/ALR parcels. There is no lot program: no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata offered as lots, and presale lot offerings verified: zero. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Port Kells really a board sub-area, or just an industrial park name?** Both: it IS a real FVREB sub-area in the board's SnapStats structure AND the City profiles it as a major industrial area; it is not one of Surrey's six town centres, and for community planning it sits within the Guildford area's orbit. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791 (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/port-kells.md)
- **Q: Is Barnston Island part of Port Kells / Surrey?** No. Barnston Island is Metro Vancouver Electoral Area A plus Katzie IR 3 — it only codes to Port Kells for board purposes because the free ferry to it sails from the foot of 104 Avenue in Port Kells, and its own lots file carries its land position. Do not price or describe an island property as Surrey stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/transportation/passenger-travel/water-travel/inland-ferries/barnston-island-ferry
- **Q: Can I subdivide or build a second home on a South Port Kells acreage?** It depends on zoning and, critically, ALR status: the land use plan shows ALR lands inside the residential pocket, subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, and ALR parcels carry the ALC's residence limits — principal ≤500 m², a secondary suite only inside the principal residence, an additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha) — and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all. Off-ALR, Surrey's suite cap is 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area, and subdivision runs under Bylaw No. 8830. Confirm both before pricing in a second dwelling. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: The land use plan mentions cluster housing — is that a complex I can buy into?** No. "Cluster housing" in the adopted South Port Kells Land Use Plan is a plan designation for the residential pocket, not a named strata complex — no complex name, address or strata plan attaches to it on any source checked. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/ADOPTEDSOUTHPORTKELLSLANDUSEPLAN.pdf
- **Q: Are there extra development charges in Port Kells?** Yes — Surrey publishes separate 2025 DCC schedules for Port Kells industrial and commercial development, apart from the city-wide rates. Confirm the schedule early in any development pro forma. https://investsurrey.ca/media/23791
