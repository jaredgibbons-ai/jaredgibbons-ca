- **Q: Is quota included?** UNVERIFIED as a general rule — no marketing-board source was opened in the research. Get the position in writing and verify transfer with the board before subjects are removed. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
- **Q: What residence can an ALR poultry parcel have?** One principal residence ≤500 m² (≤1,000 m² of fill), a suite only inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); local rules may be stricter. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify the farmhouse or create bare-land strata lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval. (§§2–3.)

- **Q: What about barns, floodplain and biosecurity rules?** UNVERIFIED in this file — no official source was opened for barn, Flood Construction Level, environmental-code or biosecurity requirements; confirm the specific parcel's floodplain position and requirements with the City and the applicable authorities before planning. https://jaredgibbons.ca/abbotsford-poultry-farms.html
