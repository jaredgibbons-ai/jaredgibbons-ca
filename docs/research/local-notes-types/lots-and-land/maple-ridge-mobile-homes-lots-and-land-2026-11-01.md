Pad product is therefore excluded as lot stock in full. What little land-form product the research verifies is manufactured/modular homes on OWNED land (fee-simple), documented below as a form only. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **MHPTA pad tenancy — the governing Act for the core product:** manufactured home site tenancies are covered by the Manufactured Home Park Tenancy Act; landlords and tenants must have a written tenancy agreement for the site. Assignment on a sale in a park permanently transfers the pad tenancy to the buyer when the home is sold and remains in the park; under MHPTA s. 28 a tenant may assign only with the landlord's prior written consent (or consent deemed given under the regulations), an arbitrator's order, or where the tenancy agreement authorises it; before asking, the seller must give the proposed buyer written copies of the park rules and the relevant parts of the tenancy agreement; on assignment the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, as does the scheduled date of a subsequent rent increase, and the landlord cannot charge a fee for consent. http://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Pad rent increases — VERIFIED LIVE limits (Act wording):** landlords can only increase rent with 3 full months' notice, can increase rent every 12 months, and the increase cannot exceed the yearly rent increase limit; the landlord may also add a proportional amount — the change in local government levies plus the change in regulated park utility fees, divided by the number of manufactured home sites in the park — and may apply to the Residential Tenancy Branch for an additional increase for expenses or capital expenditures. **The 2026 limit for manufactured home site tenancies is 2.3% plus the proportional amount; the 2027 limit is 2.2% plus the proportional amount** (Province/RTB, verified live in this pass). No dollar pad rent is stated anywhere in this file as fact. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Registration and moving:** the buyer and seller file the original signed and witnessed Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership forms through a qualified supplier (a lawyer or notary); the Province directs buyers to make sure the CSA certification label is attached at the point of sale. A home may not be sold unless it has the original legible CSA decal attached; if the label is removed or lost, the home must be re-certified (Technical Safety BC licensed electrical contractor inspection; the electrical approval label can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home). A permit to move a manufactured home is issued by the Manufactured Home Registry, a tax certificate for transport purposes is required (with exceptions including a move to a different pad within the same park), and the transport permit expires 30 days after issue. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Taxes on a home in a park:** a manufactured home owner is assessed and taxed like other residential property owners, but the assessed value will not include the value of the land if the home is in a manufactured home park not on reserve land, or on land privately owned by someone other than the home owner. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/manufactured-home
- **Zoning:** Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019, Zone RMH (Manufactured Home Park Residential) at s. 626 governs parks; the RMH zone's own rules are UNVERIFIED in this file (consolidated text not opened; fetch 429) and none are asserted. Owned-land manufactured homes sit under the parcel's own zone — confirm per parcel with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/sites/default/files/2024-01/Zoning%20bylaw%20report.pdf
- **OCP citation CONTESTED — flagged wherever met, resolved nowhere:** the Official Community Plan is cited in program material as both Bylaw 7060-2014 and Bylaw 7998-2024. This slug's research cites no OCP number, and neither number is asserted here. (Program frame; research file §1.)
- **Park age rules vs strata age bylaws — do not conflate:** a park's 55+ restriction (Alouette, Val Maria, listing-stated) is a park rule under the MHPTA set in the written park rules — the Strata Property Act exemption list does not govern it. In a BC strata, by contrast, only a 55-or-older residency bylaw is valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions), rental-restriction bylaws are VOID since November 24, 2022, and a "45+" line is NOT a valid age bylaw. Haney Pioneer Village Co-operative (11830 230 St) is 60+, NEVER counted as 55+ — a co-op tenure record, not park or lot stock. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws and https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **BC strata block — applies only if a bare-land strata manufactured home is ever verified (none is, §2):** Form B (SPA s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (before any exemption): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. Anything finer — including the GST treatment of a manufactured home itself — is UNCONFIRMED in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Do I own the land under a Maple Ridge park home?** Generally no: the Province describes park tenancies as owning the manufactured home and renting the site it sits on, under a Manufactured Home Site Tenancy Agreement governed by the MHPTA; the home's assessed value for tax purposes does not include the land value in a park. That is why pad product is not lot stock in this file. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: Can the park raise my pad rent when I buy?** No — on assignment of the site tenancy the pad rent for the new tenant stays the same, and the scheduled date for a subsequent rent increase does not change. Increases otherwise need 3 full months' notice, at most once every 12 months, within the yearly limit plus the proportional levy/utility amount (2026: 2.3% + proportional; 2027: 2.2% + proportional). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
- **Q: What pad rent will I actually pay, in dollars?** UNCONFIRMED in this file — no park's pad rent was confirmed from a park-manager source, and no dollar figure is published here as fact. The live page's $600–$1,050 range is site copy (§5). Get the current rent, in writing, from the tenancy agreement the seller must hand over in the assignment process. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **Q: What is the difference between a mobile home and a modular home in BC?** Both are manufactured homes built off-site, distinguished by their CSA standard: mobile homes under Z-240, modular homes under A-277 — the CSA label on the home states which applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: What if the home has lost its CSA label?** It may not be sold until re-certified: Technical Safety BC arranges a licensed electrical contractor's inspection, and the electrical approval label prepared can be used in place of a CSA label to list the home for sale. Check the label before anything else. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Is any Maple Ridge manufactured-home product on land I would own?** As a form, yes — manufactured/modular homes on fee-simple owned lots (the feed's Calvin Crescent modular is the research's example) are land-plus-home purchases with no pad tenancy. No specific owned-land parcel's title or zone is verified in this research, so verify per parcel. And no bare-land strata manufactured-home community is verified at all. (§2 above)
- **Q: What about The Maples, Alouette or Val Maria — can I buy a lot there?** No lot is sold in any of them: Alouette and Val Maria are pad-rental parks (own the home, rent the pad; Val Maria is leasehold non-strata), and The Maples is a housing co-operative (membership/shares). All three are tenure records, excluded as lot stock with the reason stated in §2. https://www.chf.bc.ca/find-a-co-op/
- **Q: How does closing work in a park?** Through registration, not just the contract: the purchase is only effective once registered, via a lawyer or notary filing the Bill of Sale and Notice to Transfer or Change Ownership with the Manufactured Home Registry — plus the landlord's prior written consent to assign the site tenancy under MHPTA s. 28, with no consent fee. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/owning-a-home/manufactured-home-registry
- **Q: Are park 55+ rules the same as strata 55+ bylaws?** No. A park's age rule is a written park rule under the MHPTA; a strata's 55+ rule is a registered bylaw under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 with provincial exemptions. Verify park rules from the park, in writing, before removing subjects — no Maple Ridge park's rules were confirmed from a manager source in this research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **A transfer does not reset the rent clock.** The feature buyers most often get wrong: on assignment, both the pad rent amount and the next scheduled increase date carry over unchanged — a park office or listing suggesting a transfer triggers a new market rent contradicts the Province's assignment sheet. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/housing-and-tenancy/residential-tenancies/information-sheets/assigment_of_mhp_tenancy_agreements.pdf
- **The proportional amount is the quiet second half of every increase.** Beyond the headline limit (2.3% in 2026, 2.2% in 2027), a park landlord may add the per-site share of changes in local government levies and regulated utility fees — so two parks with the same headline limit can land different dollar increases, which is one more reason no dollar pad rent is quoted without the park's own paperwork. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/residential-tenancies/manufactured-homes/rent-increases-for-manufactured-homes
