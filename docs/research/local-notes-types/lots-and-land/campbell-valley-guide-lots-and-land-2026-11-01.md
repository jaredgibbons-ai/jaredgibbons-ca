- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024):** 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and NO 6-unit tier exists in the bylaw. Most Campbell Valley acreage parcels exceed the 4,050 m² ceiling by a wide margin, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them; eligibility for any specific parcel is UNVERIFIED at parcel level. - **Subdivision and servicing — Township Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED.** Subdivision of a Campbell Valley parcel runs under Bylaw 5382's servicing standards and, where the parcel is ALR, ALC approval (ALR block below). What a specific parcel may build is UNVERIFIED at parcel level — confirm with the Township. (§3.)
- **Q: Do SSMUH unit counts apply to my acreage?** Generally no: Township Bylaw 6020 makes lots over 4,050 m² ineligible and has no 6-unit tier, and most Campbell Valley parcels exceed that ceiling by a wide margin. Parcel-level eligibility is UNVERIFIED. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I keep farm class on a parcel I buy?** Farm classification is property-by-property with BC Assessment against the three-tier income thresholds in §3; a buyer continuing to farm must submit the General Application for Farm Classification by October 31 after purchase. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Q: How much PTT on a $1.5M Campbell Valley acreage?** $28,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $1,300,000 ($26,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a vacant parcel?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale, per CRA GI-003 — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land itself. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
