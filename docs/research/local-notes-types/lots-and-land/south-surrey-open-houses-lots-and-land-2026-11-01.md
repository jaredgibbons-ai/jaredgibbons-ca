## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), carried verbatim as the siblings sourced it:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, provides that a disclosure of representation is **not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate**. Sourcing stated: that is the wording carried in this file's research (reused there from batch-13/open-houses.md) and in the condos and townhomes siblings. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download
- **Representation at an open house:** BCFSA states a licensed professional must only work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional representing another party. The live page's own "Before you walk in" copy is consistent with that frame: it warns the listing agent works for the seller, advises asking for the Form B, depreciation report and minutes at a strata open house, and describes BC's 3-business-day rescission period at 0.25% of the price with leasehold, auction and court-ordered exceptions — recorded as site copy. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://jaredgibbons.ca/south-surrey-open-houses.html

- **Surrey plans:** OCP = **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — parcel zoning confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH (Surrey position):** zoning changes adopted July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** one suite per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; not within a coach house or garden suite; habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**; a suite is not a separate strata lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this program: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside setbacks:** Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 sets streamside setbacks **by stream class** — there is no single riparian figure for Surrey; read the class for the specific watercourse on the parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Surrey short-term rentals:** permitted, **owner-only**; licence figure carried as **$358/yr**, with the $350/yr proposal figure disclosed as a conflict — check the City licence page before quoting either.
- **Strata block — applies in full to any strata lot in this feed, including a bare-land strata lot (Sea Ridge Bays form):** Form B diligence (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, maximum $35 + 25¢/page — fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, budget and depreciation report); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no feed parcel is confirmed ALR here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is asserted here only because this standing ledger states it; no farm-class parcel is documented in this slug's research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → $2,000 + $14,000 = **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → $2,000 + $20,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
