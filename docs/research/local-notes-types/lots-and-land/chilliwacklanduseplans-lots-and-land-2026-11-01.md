## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **City plans:** Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — "Chilliwack 2050" — with Schedule B neighbourhood plans and Schedule D permit areas as in §§1–2. The live page states the OCP was "adopted December 16, 2025"; the City's OCP page read states no adoption date, so that date is UNVERIFIED here and is recorded as the page's own statement only. The live page's "Bylaw No. 5024" citation for the Sardis plan is likewise UNVERIFIED against the current OCP — the City's page names no bylaw number for Schedule B5. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Zoning:** Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000 sets what can be built as-of-right today — the same OCP-vs-zoning distinction the live page is built on: check BOTH layers before valuing a parcel (current zoning under Bylaw 5000; OCP designation under Bylaw 5500 on any rezoning). Farm zones sit at Bylaw 5000 §6. https://chilliwack.com/ocp (research file §4)
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** Chilliwack's SSMUH amendment is Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, amending Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000, adopted June 18, 2024. Within that frame a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling (s.2), only 1 accessory dwelling unit is permitted per lot (s.4.06(1)), garden suites are capped at 100 m² gross floor area, and ADU parking is set at s.5.04. (Research file §4.)
- **Sardis density specifics on the live page** (medium- and high-density growth along the Vedder Road corridor, around Luckakuck Way and Promontory Road, with R1-B/R1-C small-lot and duplex infill on interior streets): the page's own statements — the Schedule B5 Sardis plan document itself was not opened, so they are UNVERIFIED as stated. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwacklanduseplans.html

The live page's "DPA2 Hillside & Upland" name matches neither, and its FAQ claim that DPA2 "requires geotechnical, stormwater, and wildfire-interface engineering before subdivision, land clearing, or building" is UNVERIFIED as stated — the DPA guideline documents were not opened. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Subdivision / servicing process — number HELD UNCONFIRMED:** no Chilliwack servicing bylaw number is asserted in this file. - **ALR block (ALR ground exists in Chilliwack — Schedule B1 is the Agricultural Area Plan):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m² (no more than 1,000 m² of fill), suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it, and no such line is documented for this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata diligence (the BC strata block — applies in full to any bare-land strata lot, if one is met):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Chilliwack property taxes:** notices are mailed annually at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1; a 5% late payment penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026, and an additional 5% penalty to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption, not bare land). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. Where a $78,000 figure for $3.5M is met in program siblings, it omits the further 2% tier and is an OPEN reconciliation item — this file follows **$93,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level ONLY:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Chilliwack's current Official Community Plan?** The Chilliwack 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2025, No. 5500 — the City's long-term vision and growth strategy, including protection of the Agricultural Land Reserve. - **Q: Which neighbourhood plans does the 2050 OCP actually contain?** Seven, all in Schedule B: B1 Agricultural Area, B2 Chilliwack Proper and Fairfield Island, B3 Downtown, B4 Eastern Hillsides, B5 Sardis, B6 South Vedder and B7 Yarrow. Schedule C is the Mountain View Heritage Conservation Area and Schedule D is the Development Permit Areas schedule — the live page's "Sardis (Schedule D)" and "Eastern Hillsides (Schedule C)" labels do not match the current OCP. https://chilliwack.com/ocp

- **Q: How does SSMUH apply in Chilliwack?** Through Bylaw 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024, amending Bylaw 5000): a secondary suite capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the principal dwelling, only 1 accessory dwelling unit per lot, and garden suites capped at 100 m². (§3.)
- **Q: Can ALR parcels in Chilliwack be subdivided for housing?** Not through the City alone — subdivision and non-farm use inside the ALR require Agricultural Land Commission approval, and the residence frame (500 m² principal / suite inside it / 90 m² or 186 m² additional) governs. The live page's related claim that about 67% of Chilliwack is in the ALR is UNVERIFIED on any City or ALC source opened. (§3.)
- **Q: What servicing bylaw governs a Chilliwack subdivision?** No number is asserted in this file — the servicing citation is HELD UNCONFIRMED and the candidate "Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055" is not adopted here. The documented route is the City's Approving Officer / Preliminary Layout Assessment process, confirmed per parcel. (§3.)
- **Q: How do I check the OCP designation and zoning on a specific address?** On the City of Chilliwack Webmap — the City's OCP page points readers there — checking the parcel's zoning, OCP land use designation, Schedule D permit areas and floodplain layers. Per-parcel results are not stated in this file. https://chilliwack.com/ocp
- **Q: What were Chilliwack land or home prices in September 2026?** No figure is stated in this file, deliberately: Chilliwack has no SnapStats coverage and CADREB publishes board-wide statistics only, so any city or plan-area figure would require a Paragon pull, which was not used. (§1.)
- **Q: What does PTT cost on a Chilliwack land purchase?** On the general tiers (fully residential, no exemption): $500,000 → $8,000; $800,000 → $14,000 ($6,000 fully-qualifying first-time buyer); $1,500,000 → $28,000; $1,800,000 → $34,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 including the further 2% tier over $3M. (§3.)
- **Q: When are Chilliwack property taxes due, and what is the penalty?** Notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 — with a 5% penalty on taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
