## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Municipality: Township of Langley, BC — Salmon River is the rural district along the Salmon River in the township's northwest, north of the Otter area and south of Fort Langley. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Page slugs covered by the research: **salmon-river** and **salmon-river-acreages-farms-for-sale** — two pages on the same area, researched once. MLS sub-area name as the board uses it: "Salmon River" (Fraser Valley Real Estate Board data in the SnapStats FVR September 2026 report). (Research file §1; SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- The district follows the Salmon River valley between 232 Street and 264 Street, from the Fraser River side south toward 40 Avenue, with Rawlison Crescent and 56 Avenue on the upland side; no Township-published boundary for the sub-area was confirmed. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- The name is the river's name: the Salmon River is a salmon-bearing watercourse, and Metro Vancouver watershed material covers the Salmon River watershed. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- Nearby areas: Fort Langley to the north, Otter District to the south, Walnut Grove to the west, Aldergrove to the southeast. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- Main roads and commute: 56 Avenue and Rawlison Crescent on the upland, 232 Street and 240 Street as the north–south edges, and River Road / Glover Road toward Fort Langley; Highway 1 access is south via the 200 Street or 232 Street interchanges; there is no SkyTrain or West Coast Express station in the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Transit: TransLink route 562 (Walnut Grove / Langley Centre) runs on Glover Road via 96 Avenue, 88 Avenue and 202 Street to Carvolth Exchange, serving the Fort Langley edge of the district; no TransLink route was confirmed on 56 Avenue, Rawlison Crescent or the valley roads inside the district. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf
- Schools: catchments are address-based in SD35. Poppy Secondary; other addresses may fall in different catchments, and no home is promised a specific school. ## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Salmon River IS a land market — the district's whole stock is land: large fee-simple rural parcels in the valley and on the upland. There is no subdivision roster to list: no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata, and no presale lot offering was verified. Land trades parcel by parcel as farm and acreage freehold, split by the valley floor (floodplain, ALR in places, inside the dyke/pump system) versus the 56 Avenue / Rawlison Crescent upland.**

### Fee-simple parcels — the only verified form

- **Valley-floor parcels:** farm and acreage houses on large rural lots along the river valley, under the Township Rural Plan designations and Zoning Bylaw No. 2500; the district's farm and acreage homes sit in the rural zones, including RU-2. Lot character as stated: large rural parcels — typical lot sizes from direct Bylaw 2500 text were NOT confirmed, and the RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage were not confirmed from a readable bylaw section: both UNCONFIRMED, no size invented here. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **Upland parcels (56 Avenue / Rawlison Crescent side):** the same fee-simple acreage form on the upland side of the district — horse owners using the upland lots are part of the typical-buyer picture in the research. No separate upland zone minimum is stated in the research: UNCONFIRMED lot-by-lot. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Strata vs freehold, as stated:** most Salmon River properties are non-strata freehold acreages; where a strata parcel exists, BC strata law is the Strata Property Act. The housing stock is effectively all detached: SnapStats recorded 22 detached listings against 4 attached listings in the sub-area in September 2026. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.)
- **ALR vs non-ALR split, as stated:** farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve IN PLACES — the research states no percentage split, and none is invented. Confirm a specific parcel on the ALC maps. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Serviced vs unserviced — well/septic ground, as stated:** rural properties may use wells — search GWELLS, the provincial database of registered wells and mapped aquifers, for a parcel's record (well reports due within 90 days of constructing, altering or decommissioning a well). Septic is onsite: Fraser Health requires an authorized person to file a record of sewerage system before construction, alteration or repair of an onsite system, and a letter of certification after construction. No municipal water/sewer position for valley parcels is stated in the research — treat the district as well/septic diligence ground unless the Township confirms otherwise for the parcel. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/resources-for-onsite-sewerage-systems-property-owners-and-authorized-persons
- **Flood character of the land itself:** the Salmon River valley is Fraser River floodplain land — the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station was originally built to help mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet, provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and has flood box gates that close as river levels rise. Valley-floor land value is inseparable from that system. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Riparian character:** the Salmon River is salmon-bearing, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit building envelopes near the river — parcel-level setback distances: UNCONFIRMED; check with the Township before planning a build. Price buildable area, not gross area. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + plans:** rural land is under Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (rural zones, including RU-2; RU-2 minimum lot area/frontage UNCONFIRMED from a readable section; zone assignments for a parcel come from the bylaw Map Schedules through the Township's GeoSource). The Township Official Community Plan and the Rural Plan govern the district. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/ https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/community-planning.aspx
- **SSMUH as the Township states it:** SSMUH = Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024) — NO 6-plex tier in the Township. The Township states its zoning bylaw was updated for the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing rules, and that on lots larger than 4,050 m² the rules allow one secondary suite or accessory dwelling unit in addition to the primary unit depending on zoning; the condos sibling carries the tier detail (3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible for those tiers). Suite cap 120 m². https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/salmon-river-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Coach house — fee-simple, never strata (live conflict flagged, not resolved):** a coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — in s.102 a "coach house" is an accessory dwelling unit located only within the second storey of a detached garage — never a strata unit. The live s.102 position is in conflict on the program record and is flagged here, NOT resolved: do not market a Salmon River coach house as strata, and verify the current s.102 wording for the parcel. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/salmon-river-guide-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3; standing correction for this area.
- **R-CL s.403 (where it applies):** 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.403 — carried as the Township standing position; R-CL is a Willoughby-form zone and its application to any Salmon River parcel is UNCONFIRMED. Willoughby Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (standing position; Willoughby is a different community — do not apply its plan to Salmon River ground). Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/salmon-river-townhomes-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision follows the governing rural zone (RU-2 minimums UNCONFIRMED above) plus ALC approval on ALR parcels (below). A Township servicing-bylaw number could not be confirmed on a primary source in the research — held UNCONFIRMED; the servicing diligence documented here is the well (GWELLS) and septic (Fraser Health filing) trail in Section 2, plus the pump-station/flood position below. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **Flood / pump-station diligence:** check the dyke and pump station file before valuing valley-floor land: the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise, and upgrades are a live Township project — Council authorized staff to proceed with detailed design and tendering for the upgrades. Flood status drives insurance and lending on these parcels. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **ALR rules (valley farm land in the ALR, in places):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha) — the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met, subject to Township bylaws; BC Assessment farm-class thresholds in full three-tier form ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata rules (no such stock verified here; stated so the form is never mis-sold):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2 with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1%/$200K, 2% to $2M, 3% above, +2% on residential portion above $3M — confirm the farm/ALR position for the specific parcel; FTHB Program and Newly Built Home Exemption are home-buyer exemptions (bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). (Program standard frame.)
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points (including farm-business sales) UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36%/$6,300/$350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000/$8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes:** no Salmon River–specific position is documented in the research — none asserted. The Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is VANCOUVER ONLY and does not apply in the Township of Langley. The BC Speculation and Vacancy Tax applies only in designated taxable regions as the Province states them — confirm for the parcel on the provincial source.
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — vacant land:** the research file cites no source on the prohibition's application to vacant land — position UNCONFIRMED in this file. ## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What kind of land actually sells in Salmon River?** Large fee-simple rural parcels — valley-floor farm land and 56 Avenue / Rawlison Crescent upland acreages — parcel by parcel. There is no subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot program to buy into. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is the land in the ALR?** Farm land in the valley is in the Agricultural Land Reserve in places; confirm a specific parcel on the ALC ALR maps before assuming a farm use, a second dwelling or a subdivision is possible. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Is the valley in a floodplain?** Yes — the valley is Fraser River floodplain land; the Township states the Salmon River Pump Station provides flood protection when Fraser River levels rise and was built to mitigate flooding of the surrounding agricultural lands during freshet. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/salmon-river-pump-station-upgrades.aspx
- **Q: Can an acreage add a second dwelling?** On ALR land the ALC permits one additional residence without an ALC application where its size rules are met (up to 90 m² at 40 ha or less, up to 186 m² over 40 ha), subject to Township bylaws; the Township's small-scale rules also allow a suite or accessory unit on lots over 4,050 m² depending on zoning. A coach house is a fee-simple s.102 unit, never strata. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Can I subdivide a big parcel?** UNCONFIRMED from this file — the RU-2 minimum lot area and frontage were not confirmed from a readable Bylaw 2500 section, and any ALR parcel needs ALC approval to subdivide. Get the zone confirmed through the Township's GeoSource Map Schedules before writing on split potential. https://www.tol.ca/at-your-service/bylaw-enforcement/bylaws/
- **Q: Is there water and sewer, or well and septic?** Rural properties may use wells (check the GWELLS record; reports due within 90 days of well work) and onsite sewerage (Fraser Health authorized-person filing before work, letter of certification after). Pull both paper trails before closing. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/environment/air-land-water/water/groundwater-wells-aquifers/understanding-aquifers
- **Q: How close can I build to the river?** The Salmon River is salmon-bearing, so setbacks and riparian rules can limit the building envelope — parcel-level setback distances are UNCONFIRMED; check with the Township before planning a build. https://metrovancouver.org/services/regional-parks/park/campbell-valley-regional-park
- **Q: Which schools serve a Salmon River acreage?** For the sample address 4452 256 Street, the SD35 locator returns North Otter Elementary and D.W. Poppy Secondary (Ministry-listed names as carried in the research), with no middle school shown — run the exact address; no home is promised a specific school. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
- **Q: A listing says "strata" for a Salmon River property. What should I check?** Whether the title is actually a strata lot (including a bare-land strata) or freehold non-strata — listings in the wider valley show both wordings, including "Freehold NonStrata" on Salmon River houses. If it is a strata lot, diligence runs through the Form B. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/salmon-river-condos-2026-11-01.md, §3.
- **Q: Is there transit or shopping in the district?** No route inside the valley roads was confirmed (route 562 runs at the Fort Langley edge), and few or no stores inside the district were confirmed — the nearest store named on a Township page is Lee's Market, 23320 Mavis Avenue, Fort Langley. An acreage here is a car-dependent purchase. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/214/tt562.pdf https://www.tol.ca/en/services/garbage-cart.aspx
