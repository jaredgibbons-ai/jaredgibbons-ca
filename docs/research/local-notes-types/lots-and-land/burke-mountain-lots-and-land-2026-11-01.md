## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**No meaningful bare-land market is verified on Burke Mountain — stated plainly at the top. What trades and is planned here is the lot fabric of a plan-led growth mountain: fee-simple detached lots and strata townhouse lots created under the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhood plans, plus a future village centre. No named bare-land subdivision offered as lots, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified on any official or developer source in the research. The presales that exist are townhouse presales, not lot sales.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form

- **Established and plan-created house lots:** single-detached houses and townhouses are the planned and built forms under the three neighbourhood plans, with apartment form planned at Burke Mountain Village. Lot character, only as the research states it: subdivision minimums under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 are 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, and 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot; the zone and dimensions of a specific lot are UNVERIFIED until checked parcel by parcel. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Plan frame for the remaining land:** the Northwest Burke Vision covers about 400 hectares / 980 acres, described by the City as the last large undeveloped area in Coquitlam, guiding growth over 30+ years. That is a planning frame, documented as such — it is not for-sale inventory, and no lot count, lot size or lot price for undeveloped Burke land is stated in the research. https://www.coquitlam.ca/417/Northwest-Burke-Vision
- **Serviced position, as the City states it:** Coquitlam utility charges cover water, sewer and drainage and solid waste; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules, so property-level septic or well status is UNVERIFIED in the research — confirm servicing for the specific parcel with the City. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/17426/2026-Budget-Highlights
- **ALR vs non-ALR:** parcel-level ALR status for Burke Mountain: UNVERIFIED in the sources read; no ALR percentage split is stated. Check any parcel in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Creek/slope position:** the Watercourse Protection Development Permit Area covers all areas within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam (30 metres in the rest of Coquitlam); Zoning Bylaw s.519 sets flood protection setbacks and s.523 covers riparian areas. Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek, Partington Creek and Burke Mountain Creek are named planning constraints — lot-level creek setback and slope status: UNVERIFIED per lot. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996:** small-scale residential zones R-1, R-2 and R-3, with the subdivision minimums in Section 2; s.519 flood protection setbacks and s.523 riparian areas also sit in this bylaw. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **OCP — Interim Official Community Plan, Bylaws 5511/5512, 2026, adopted April 27, 2026, superseding OCP No. 3479:** the current plan frame, under which the Hyde Creek, Smiling Creek and Partington Creek neighbourhood plans, the Northwest Burke Vision and Burke Mountain Village planning sit. Any Burke document still citing OCP No. 3479 as current is stale. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1436/Official-Community-Plan-Review
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots larger than 280 m² and under 4,050 m², including secondary suites and carriage or laneway homes; lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the SSMUH rules. Confirm zone and services for the parcel before pricing in added units. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Subdivision + servicing — Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 (VERIFIED):** subdivision minimums run off the zone (Section 2), and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003 — the consolidated bylaw requires the owner to grant or acquire statutory rights-of-way for the works and services needed to serve a subdivision or development, in a form acceptable to the City's solicitor, and to pay for traffic-control and street-name signage installed by the City at the owner's expense before subdivision approval or building permit issue. Parcel-level requirements are confirmed with the City. https://publicdocs.coquitlam.ca/coquitlamdoc/getdocIF.asp?doc=6328713
- **Watercourse Development Permit:** within 50 metres of the top of bank of any watercourse in Northeast Coquitlam a Watercourse Protection Development Permit can be required — double the 30-metre reach in the rest of Coquitlam — and the Partington Creek wildlife corridor can also affect creek-edge lots. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Bare-land strata block (for the form — no Burke bare-land strata plan is verified):** Form B diligence (provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Burke ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31). No page stating a flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted here. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Coquitlam property taxes:** due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% penalty after September 15, 2026, including unclaimed Home Owner Grants. https://coquitlam.ca/544/Property-Taxes
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Burke-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is stated ONLY as this file's own cited sources support it: the research cites no prohibition source for this area, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot on Burke Mountain?** No verified lot offering exists — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. What exists is built fee-simple and strata stock under the neighbourhood plans, plus townhouse presales (Versant, Rocklin 1295) that are homes, not lots. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a lot be subdivided?** By zone: 650 m² with 18.5 m width in R-1 and R-3, or 465 m² with 12.5 m width in R-2, under Zoning Bylaw No. 3000, 1996 — and servicing runs under Bylaw No. 3558, 2003. Confirm the parcel's zone first. https://www.coquitlam.ca/DocumentCenter/View/15337/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-Summary-Guide?bidId=
- **Q: How many homes can one lot hold?** Under Coquitlam's SSMUH rules: up to 3 units on lots 280 m² or smaller and up to 4 units on lots under 4,050 m², including suites and carriage/laneway homes — but lots not connected to municipal water or sewer are outside the rules. https://www.coquitlam.ca/1361/Small-Scale-Multi-Unit-Housing-in-Coquit
- **Q: Is Versant a lot development I can buy into now?** No — Versant is a strata townhouse presale, documented in the townhomes home file; the developer price sheet dated April 23, 2026 states completion Fall 2026–Early 2027. It is not a lot sale. ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/burke-mountain-townhomes-2026-11-01.md
- **Q: What creek rule affects a Burke lot?** The Watercourse Protection DPA reaches 50 metres from the top of bank in Northeast Coquitlam — double the rest of Coquitlam — with Zoning ss.519/523 flood and riparian setbacks on top. Check any creek-edge lot before promising additions or suites. https://www.coquitlam.ca/254/Development-Permits
- **Q: Is the Village open yet?** No on the sources read: the 120,000+ sq. ft. retail centre is envisioned, the Discovery Centre at 3537 Princeton Avenue is open, and the community centre broke ground May 12, 2026 with completion UNVERIFIED. Price the Village as a dated future amenity. https://burkevillage.ca/village-timeline/
- **Q: Which schools serve my address?** SD43, address-based, with Smiling Creek Elementary and Coast Salish Elementary named by SD43 as Burke Mountain-area schools — but no Burke address-level locator result is verified, so run the exact address in the SD43 locator before any school claim. https://mybaragar.com/index.cfm?event=page.SchoolLocatorPublic&DistrictCode=bc43
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly subdivided lot bought from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA GI-003, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Is any Burke land in the ALR?** UNVERIFIED parcel by parcel in the research — check the specific parcel in the ALC map finder; if it is ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval and residences cannot be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: How did the mountain trade in September 2026?** Detached sold 3 of 65 (5%) while "Condos & Townhomes" combined sold 11 of 77 (14%) — comp the two markets separately; SnapStats medians exist at Coquitlam community level only, and land has no SnapStats category. (As of September 2026, Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report.)
