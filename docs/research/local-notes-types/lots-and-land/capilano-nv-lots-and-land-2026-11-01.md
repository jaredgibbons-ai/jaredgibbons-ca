## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Capilano in this file is the Capilano Road corridor in the District of North Vancouver's west end: the residential streets on both sides of Capilano Road between the Upper Levels Highway (Highway 1) and the Cleveland Dam, around the Capilano River canyon and the Capilano Suspension Bridge area. The District's Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900, covers this area. Exact boundary streets are UNVERIFIED; the confirmed anchors are Capilano Road itself, Capilano Suspension Bridge Park at 3735 Capilano Road, and the Cleveland Dam at the corridor's north end in Capilano River Regional Park. Paragon (GVR) sub-area match and code: UNVERIFIED. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Naming split buyers meet: District and school sources use "Upper Capilano" for the plateau north of Highway 1 (Canyon Heights, Capilano Highlands, Grousewoods) and "Lower Capilano / Lions Gate" for the Marine Drive end south of the highway — this file covers the Capilano Road corridor itself, not the Lower Capilano village lands south of Highway 1. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Nearby areas: Canyon Heights and Edgemont Village (east, across the Mosquito/Mackay creek ravines), Capilano Highlands and Grousewoods (north), Norgate and Pemberton Heights in the lowlands (south-east, below Highway 1), and — across the Capilano River — West Vancouver's Park Royal / Ambleside side. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/official-community-plan.pdf
- Roads and transit: Capilano Road is the spine — south to Marine Drive and the Lions Gate Bridge, north to the Cleveland Dam and Grouse Mountain (6400 Nancy Greene Way; Capilano Road is the only road up to it) — and the Highway 1 interchange at Capilano Road is the corridor's highway access. Route 236 runs on Capilano Road the length of the corridor toward Grouse Mountain and Lonsdale Quay; route 232 runs via Capilano Road at the east side; route 246 crosses via Capilano Road and Montroyal Boulevard with downtown trips over the Lions Gate Bridge. Commute times in minutes: UNVERIFIED. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
- Anchors: Capilano River Regional Park (Metro Vancouver) runs along the river through and above the corridor (Capilano Pacific Trail, Coho Loop Trail); the Cleveland Dam (completed 1954, 91 m high) holds back Capilano Lake above the corridor; the Capilano River Hatchery (completed 1971) normally draws about 250,000 visitors a year. The Capilano Library, 3045 Highland Boulevard, sits in Edgemont Village east of the corridor; Delbrook Community Recreation Centre is at 851 West Queens Road. No shopping centre or grocery store on the corridor itself is confirmed — UNVERIFIED; Edgemont is one of the District OCP's six Village Centres and is the corridor's retail. ## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — District of North Vancouver Zoning Bylaw 3210 ONLY:** Section 525 (Bylaw 8799) detached zones as stated in Section 2. The CITY of North Vancouver's Zoning Bylaw 6700 never applies to this District corridor. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **OCP — District of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, Bylaw 7900:** covers this area, including its Development Permit Areas in Schedule B; a Capilano-corridor-specific plan is UNVERIFIED (none confirmed). The City's OCP Bylaw 8400 never applies here. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **Added units / SSMUH, as the District and Province state them:** since June 2024, District zoning permits a principal dwelling, a secondary suite and a coach house — three units total — on all single-family zoned properties within the Urban Containment Boundary. The District's GeoWeb SSMUH classification shows non-exempt lots potentially allowing up to 3 units at 280 m² or less, up to 4 units above 280 m², and up to 6 units above 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit bus stop; provincial rules set 3 units on lots of 280 m² or smaller, 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on larger lots within 400 m of a prescribed bus stop. Eligibility is parcel-specific — read the lot's GeoWeb SSMUH classification before pricing density in. https://geoweb.dnv.org/Products/Reports/PHIPO/SSMUH_Classification.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing — District servicing-bylaw number held UNCONFIRMED:** no District of North Vancouver servicing-bylaw number is confirmed on a primary source in this file's research, so none is quoted — and the CITY's Subdivision Bylaw 8014 must never be imported into a District file. The process is documented instead: subdivision potential runs off the parcel's Section 525 zone minimums (Section 2), servicing runs on the District's 2026 single-family utility structure (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; combined $2,571.20), and Schedule B hazard permit areas (below) gate canyon-edge work. Confirm requirements with the District for the parcel. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Hazard Development Permit Areas (OCP Bylaw 7900, Schedule B):** seven Development Permit Areas, including Protection of the Natural Environment, Streamside Protection, Wildfire Hazard, Creek Hazard and Slope Hazard — canyon-edge and ravine lots on this corridor are exactly the lots these areas were written for. Whether a lot is in one is a District GeoWeb map check. https://dnv-docs.simplicitycms.ca/documents/ocp-schedule-b-dpa.pdf
- **Reservoir context — diligence item:** homes below the Cleveland Dam sit downstream of Capilano Lake, the drinking-water reservoir — confirm the parcel's hazard and insurance picture from the District GeoWeb reports rather than assuming from the street. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no corridor ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a corridor lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no corridor-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this corridor's land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Capilano, and the position is not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new subdivision I can buy into on Capilano Road?** No verified one — the corridor is established District housing; land trades as built fee-simple house lots, and no bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a corridor lot?** Only if its Section 525 zone minimum allows it: from R2(S1) at 335 m² / 10 m width up to R1 and R2(S3) at 4,000 m² / 30 m width, with standard R2 at 660 m² / 18 m / 34 m — confirm the lot's zone in the District's GeoWeb before pricing any split. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/zoning-guide-section-525.pdf
- **Q: Can the lot hold a suite and a coach house too?** Yes, as the District states it: since June 2024 three units total (principal, secondary suite, coach house) are permitted on single-family zoned lots within the Urban Containment Boundary, and the GeoWeb SSMUH classification can potentially allow up to 6 units on a non-exempt lot over 280 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — parcel-specific. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/rtc-bylaws-8787-8799.pdf
- **Q: What hazard checks apply to a canyon-side lot?** Schedule B Streamside Protection, Creek Hazard, Slope Hazard and Wildfire Hazard Development Permit Areas can all apply; the District's flood construction level data is modelled to a 2100 horizon plus 0.6 m. Run the GeoWeb reports before pricing a renovation or rebuild. https://geoweb.dnv.org/data/
- **Q: Are the Capilano Crescent strata addresses a land opportunity?** No — 3125 and 3288 Capilano Crescent are built strata addresses in listing traffic whose names, years and unit counts rest solely on a directory this program never cites; they are held UNCONFIRMED in the condos sibling, counted nowhere, and are not lot inventory. (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/capilano-nv-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: Is the lot serviced?** The District's 2026 single-family utility structure (water $932.80; sewer/drainage $1,255.60; combined $2,571.20) covers the corridor's established lots on the District's wording; parcel-level septic/well status is UNVERIFIED — confirm the parcel with the District. https://docs.dnv.org/documents/2026-tax-rates.pdf
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Capilano Road lot?** By exact address only — no corridor catchment is settled in this file; the plateau elementaries beside the corridor are Canyon Heights, Highlands and Cleveland, with Handsworth Secondary the family secondary, and Capilano Elementary is in the City lowlands, not this corridor. Check the address with SD44. https://www.sd44.ca/school/canyonheights/Pages/default.aspx
- **Q: Does Grouse Mountain traffic affect a lot's value?** Capilano Road is the only road up to Grouse Mountain and carries Suspension Bridge traffic past these homes — price road exposure street by street (direct frontage versus the quiet canyon-side crescents), with routes 236 and 246 as the transit offset. https://infomaps.translink.ca/Public_Timetables/213/tt236.pdf
