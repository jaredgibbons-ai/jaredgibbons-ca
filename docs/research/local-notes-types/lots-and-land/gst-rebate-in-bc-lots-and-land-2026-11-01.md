## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **FTHB GST/HST rebate — verified (CRA News No. 122 + CRA tax-tips page, opened in the research pass):** amendments in Bill C-4; Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA governs). Together with the new housing rebate, it provides 100% of the GST (or federal part of HST) on a newly constructed or substantially renovated single unit residential complex or condominium unit valued up to $1 million; between $1 million and $1.5 million the rebate is gradually reduced; no rebate at $1.5 million or more. Maximum $50,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **FTHB eligibility — verified:** at least 18, Canadian citizen or permanent resident, and not have lived in a home — in or outside Canada — that they or their spouse/common-law partner owned as a primary residence in the purchase year or the preceding four calendar years; must meet the new housing rebate criteria (as if its ceiling were $1.5M instead of $450,000); agreement with a builder on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031 (owner-built: construction begins in that window); construction begins before 2031 and is substantially completed before 2036; buyer is first to occupy after substantial completion; buyer has not previously applied for an FTHB rebate. It also generally covers a home on land leased from the builder and a co-op share for a new unit. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Claiming — verified:** usually within two years of taking ownership or finishing construction; a builder can credit the rebate at transfer; otherwise the buyer applies to CRA directly (CRA account or Form GST190 for a builder purchase; Form GST191 for owner-built). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Page statements only — UNVERIFIED:** (a) the straight-line phase-out mechanism and its $1.25M / 50% / $25,000 example; (b) the spousal already-claimed bar; (c) the assignment-sales rule that the original agreement must date from on or after March 20, 2025. None was stated on the CRA pages opened in the research pass. https://jaredgibbons.ca/gst-rebate-in-bc.html
- **New Housing Rebate (the pre-existing rebate) — verified in this pass:** generally 36% of the GST paid, maximum $6,300; gradually reduced for homes priced/valued between $350,000 and $450,000; none at $450,000 or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. This is the tax on the LAND — separate from any rebate on a home later built on it. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **PTT — general tiers (Province, verified):** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000, on fair market value at registration. Worked anchors: $500K → $8,000; $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $2.0M → $38,000. **$3.5M anchor with conflict flagged:** $3,500,000 → **$93,000** including the further 2% tier (1%×$200K $2,000 + 2%×$1.8M $36,000 + 3%×$1.5M $45,000 + further 2%×$500K $10,000 = $93,000); a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M — that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, it omits the further 2% tier, and it is NOT followed here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **BC FTHB PTT exemption — verified:** exempt on the first $500,000 where fair market value is $835,000 or less (effective Apr 1, 2024), property 0.5 ha or smaller, principal-residence use only, with the citizenship/residence and never-owned conditions the Province lists; partial exemption $835,000–$860,000. The page's "up to $8,000" saving is the general-rate tax on $500,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **HBP / tax credit / FHSA — research status stated plainly:** the live page states HBP up to $60,000 (with 90-day, 15-year and five-year-start details) and a $10,000 first-time buyer's tax credit worth up to $1,500 — in the research pass the CRA HBP page failed to load (HTTP 403) and the credit amount was not re-read on a CRA page, so those page figures are UNVERIFIED site copy in the research file; the program anchors carried in this lots-and-land brief are HBP $60,000 and FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year, with FHSA first-year room of $8,000 verified on CRA's FHSA page in the research pass and the FHSA lifetime limit UNVERIFIED there. https://jaredgibbons.ca/gst-rebate-in-bc.html and https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Does the $50,000 GST rebate apply if I just buy a vacant lot?** No. The FTHB rebate is for new HOUSING — a newly built or substantially renovated home — not bare land. GST on the lot itself follows CRA GI-003 (§3). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: If I buy land and build, can I get the rebate on the build?** That is the owner-builder route CRA describes: construction must begin on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, be substantially completed before 2036, and you apply directly on Form GST191 — the rebate attaches to the home, not the land purchase. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Q: How much can a first-time buyer get back on a new home in BC?** Up to $50,000: 100% of the 5% GST up to $1 million, gradually reduced to $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Is the $1.25M / $25,000 example on the page guaranteed?** Its result is consistent with the verified thresholds, but the straight-line mechanism behind it was not stated on the CRA pages opened — CRA says only "gradually reduced": UNVERIFIED as a mechanism. https://jaredgibbons.ca/gst-rebate-in-bc.html
- **Q: What happened to the old new housing rebate?** It still exists: 36% of GST, max $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000, none at $450,000 or more. The FTHB rebate is the top-up to 100% for qualifying first-time buyers. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Who counts as a first-time buyer?** 18+, citizen or permanent resident, and neither you nor your spouse/partner lived in a home either of you owned as a primary residence in the purchase year or the four preceding calendar years. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Will GST be charged on my lot purchase at all?** Generally yes if the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided; generally no for an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I stack the GST rebate with BC's PTT exemption?** They are separate federal and provincial programs and qualifying for both on the same new-home purchase is possible: PTT exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: How and when do I claim?** Usually within two years of ownership or finishing construction — builder credit at closing (Form GST190 jointly), or apply to CRA yourself (GST190; GST191 owner-built). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2026/first-time-buyers-can-save-more-on-new-homes-the-first-time-home-buyers-gst-hst-rebate-is-available-now.html
- **Q: My presale was signed before March 20, 2025 — do I qualify?** No on the verified condition: eligibility turns on an agreement entered into on or after March 20, 2025 and before 2031, no matter the price. The page's separate assignment-sales rule is UNVERIFIED (§3). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
