## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — City of Chilliwack Zoning Bylaw 2020, No. 5000.** Its Section 6 sets the agricultural (farm) zones and Section 7 the rural zones, and Section 12 includes the RS-FHA (Flood Hazard Area) reserve zone. Read the parcel's own zone section before pricing any use; zone minimums for a specific Proper West lot: UNVERIFIED in this file beyond the sections named. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **OCP — Chilliwack 2050, Official Community Plan Bylaw 2025, No. 5500** (frame note at the top of this file; Chilliwack Proper plan = Schedule B-2; Agricultural Plan = B1; Eastern Hillsides = B4; Sardis = B5). https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/attachView.cfm?attachID=5211
- **SSMUH — Zoning Bylaw Amendment Bylaw 2024, No. 5395, adopted June 18, 2024:** allows 3 to 4 dwelling units on parcels within the urban growth boundary zoned for single-detached and duplex housing, per the City's staff report and the amendment record in the consolidated Zoning Bylaw No. 5000. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Suites and ADUs (Bylaw No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06):** only 1 ADU per lot where the zone permits an ADU; a secondary suite is capped at the lesser of 90 m² and 40% of the habitable floor space of the building and must sit within a building containing only 1 principal dwelling unit; garden suites are capped at 100 m². The City's Secondary Suites Policy permits rental of secondary suites within any single detached dwelling regardless of zone, built with a permit and to BC Building and Fire Code and current flood protection requirements; new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf and https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Subdivision / servicing process:** subdivision is the process of changing or creating new lot boundaries; subdivisions must be approved by a municipal Approving Officer, and all subdivisions are subject to the Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055; the Approving Officer evaluates against provincial statutes, the Zoning Bylaw and that bylaw, environmental sensitivity and the OCP, and may refuse a plan considered against the public interest. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- **Flood:** flood hazard zoning exists in the bylaw structure (RS-FHA, s. 12.02) and the City applies flood protection requirements and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw. Parcel-level Flood Construction Levels for Proper West lots: UNVERIFIED — confirm with the City before subjects. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Short-term rentals:** regulated under Short-Term Rental Bylaw 2021, No. 5141 — confirm a lot's STR position under that bylaw before valuing rental income. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/363/BL51411.pdf
- **Property taxes (City of Chilliwack):** notices are mailed at the end of May and taxes are due the first working day after July 1 each year; for 2026, a 5% penalty applies to taxes outstanding after July 2, 2026 and an additional 5% to taxes outstanding after August 4, 2026. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=2522
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (fully residential, no exemption netted): $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,100,000 → $20,000; $1,200,000 → $22,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **PTT exemptions:** the First-Time Home Buyers' Program and the Newly Built Home Exemption are home-buyer exemptions as the Province describes them; bare-land eligibility for either: UNCONFIRMED in this file — do not budget an exemption on a vacant-lot purchase without a parcel-specific ruling. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most individual personal-use sales exempt; business-use / in-the-course-of-business / more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally taxable. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a vacant-lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal purchase prohibition:** no primary prohibition text was opened for this slug in the research — the position for a Chilliwack Proper West lot buyer is UNCONFIRMED here and nothing is conformed from another file.
- **BC strata block (applies in full to any bare-land strata lot, and to the Wolfe Road strata product cross-referenced in §2):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (conditional — applies only where a Webmap check shows the parcel is ALR; no ALR parcel in scope is confirmed, §2):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there vacant land for sale in Chilliwack Proper West?** No meaningful bare-land market is verified for this slug — no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata or presale lot offering was verified. The stock is houses on urban lots; price the house and its infill potential, not a land listing that this file cannot point to. (§2.)
- **Q: How big are lots here?** The live page describes detached homes on 6,000 to 7,500 sq ft lots — page description only, UNVERIFIED as a measured profile. Confirm any single lot from title / the City Webmap, not from the range. https://jaredgibbons.ca/chilliwack-proper-west.html
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Proper West lot?** One ADU per lot where the zone permits — a secondary suite (lesser of 90 m² and 40%), a coach house, or a garden suite (max 100 m²) — under Zoning Bylaw No. 5000, ss. 2 and 4.06. You cannot stack a suite plus a coach house on the one-ADU rule. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/377/ZBA%5F5000%5F%2D%5FZoning%5FBylaw%5F2020%2Epdf
- **Q: What does SSMUH add on top of that?** Bylaw No. 5395 (adopted June 18, 2024) allows 3 to 4 units on eligible single-detached/duplex parcels inside the urban growth boundary. Eligibility is parcel-specific — confirm the parcel's zone and UGB position with the City. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/attachments/Files/3090/Signed%20Staff%20Report%20%2D%20RZ001761%2Epdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a large Proper West lot?** Subdivision needs the municipal Approving Officer's approval under the Land Development Bylaw 2014, No. 3055, tested against the Zoning Bylaw and the OCP — no subdivision outcome can be promised from this file. https://www.chilliwack.com/main/page.cfm?id=653
- **Q: Is my lot in the floodplain?** Flood hazard zoning (RS-FHA) and a Floodplain Regulation Bylaw exist in the City framework, and new suites are not permitted below the flood construction level; your parcel's Flood Construction Level is UNVERIFIED here — get it from the City before subjects. https://www.chilliwack.com/MAIN/page.cfm?id=2724
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a $700,000 / $1,000,000 lot purchase?** $12,000 at $700,000 and $18,000 at $1,000,000 (fully residential anchors, §3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Will GST apply to a newly created lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules (GI-003), unlike an individual's personal-use resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Are the Wolfe Road complexes land I can buy a lot in?** No — they are 4 built strata townhouse complexes counted in this slug's townhomes file, cross-referenced in §2 only. A unit purchase there is a strata purchase (Form B, fees, bylaws), not a lot purchase. (Townhomes sibling, §2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot I'm looking at?** SD33 catchments are address-based and no locator run was done for this area — run the exact address in SD33's School Locator. The page's named schools are Ministry-confirmed as schools (§1), not as your catchment. https://sd33.bc.ca/index.php/find-your-school
