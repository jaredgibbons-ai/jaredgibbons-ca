## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, in effect July 1, 2026:** adopted June 9, 2026, it replaced the former Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 1965 (No. 4742) outright — the 1965 bylaw is NOT current and must never be cited for this area. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025:** it replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; Lougheed is one of Burnaby's town centres, and the town-centre edge is where plan expectations change fastest — review the 2050 land use map before assuming density beyond the R1 district. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing District in June 2024 — R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified; SSMUH lots require a minimum lot width of 10 m and minimum lot area of 281 m², and permitted forms include single-family homes, duplexes, multiplexes, rowhouses and secondary suites. Distance to the Lougheed corridor stops is therefore a priced fact, not a slogan. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Servicing Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. **The base bylaw's own number is held UNCONFIRMED in this file — never filled in**; the rename, the amendment number and the process above are what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Bare-land strata rules (the BC strata block — stated for the form; no Oakdale plan of any strata form is verified):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Oakdale ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — an Oakdale lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** the Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does not apply in Burnaby. No Oakdale-specific speculation-tax or federal non-Canadian purchase-prohibition position is documented in this file's research — none imported, and the prohibition position for vacant land here is UNCONFIRMED rather than conformed from other files.

- **Q: What can an R1 lot here become?** 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — test the multiplex math lot by lot, because distance to the Lougheed corridor stops decides which tier a lot is in. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Can I subdivide, and what servicing will the City require?** Subdivision parcels must conform to the Zoning Bylaw for the zone under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (1971 bylaw renamed by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; base number held UNCONFIRMED in this file) — highways, water, sewage and drainage works to City standards, with the servicing-improvements regime at four or more primary units. - **Q: Is an Oakdale lot serviced?** The City's utility wording puts established homes on annual water and sewer billing (detached water charge $652.33 for 2026, sewer as a parcel tax for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel's connections before writing. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: How big are lots here?** No official source states typical Oakdale lot sizes — UNVERIFIED at sub-area level. Price a specific lot off its own survey, zone and the 400 m frequent-transit test. (Research file, §5.)
- **Q: Is the lot in the ALR or a floodplain?** Neither designation is confirmed at sub-area level in the research — both are property-specific checks (ALC mapping for ALR; parcel-level hazard confirmation with the City). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
