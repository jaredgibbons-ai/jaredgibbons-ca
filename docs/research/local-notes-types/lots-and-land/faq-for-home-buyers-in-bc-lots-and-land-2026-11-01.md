## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

**Province-wide rules only — this topic page has no municipality, so no municipal zoning, OCP, SSMUH or servicing bylaw is stated for it, and none is imported from any area file. A specific parcel's zoning, OCP designation, SSMUH position and servicing bylaw are that parcel's municipality's, checked per parcel: UNVERIFIED in this file.**

- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** general tiers are 1% on the first $200,000, 2% on the portion over $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, on fair market value at registration. On a $500,000 purchase, no exemption: $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: exempt on the first $500,000 at a fair market value of $835,000 or less (effective April 1, 2024; property 0.5 ha or smaller, principal residence, residential improvements only), partial between $835,000 and $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- PTT reference anchors (same tier math): $700K → $12,000; $800K → $14,000 ($6,000 for a qualifying first-time buyer after the $8,000 maximum saving); $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → **$93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M** — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on a purchase — as this slug's research documents it:** the live page states GST of 5% applies to newly built and presale homes and not to resale homes, consistent with the rebate frame below, under which only new or substantially renovated housing carries GST. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED — and a condos sibling's finer GST framing is NOT carried in this file. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate — new HOUSING, not bare land:** Bill C-4 received Royal Assent March 13, 2026 (CRA governs); 100% of the GST on a newly constructed or substantially renovated home valued up to $1 million, gradually reduced between $1 million and $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more, maximum up to $50,000, agreements March 20, 2025–2031. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **New Housing Rebate:** generally 36% of the GST paid, maximum $6,300, gradually reduced for homes priced or valued between $350,000 and $450,000, not available at $450,000 or more. The live FAQ page does not state the old rebate's own numbers; they are recorded here so the rebuild can add them accurately. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Down payment and mortgage insurance (CMHC, verified in the sibling passes):** minimum down payment 5% for homes $500,000 or less; 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion over $500,000 for homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999; 20% at $1,500,000 or more, where insurance is not available; mortgage loan insurance is required below 20% down, and CMHC's published premium range is 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. The page's tiers and its $750,000 worked example ($25,000 + $25,000 = $50,000 minimum) match the verified tiers. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Strata block (bare-land strata lots, where a purchase takes that form):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR wherever met; no flat farm-class figure is documented in this slug's research, so none is asserted for this page. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is a subject in a BC purchase contract?** A condition that must be met before the deal becomes firm — the live page lists financing, inspection, property disclosure statement, title search and strata document review as the common subjects, completed during a negotiable subject-removal period; the page's "typically 7 days" norm is practitioner copy, UNVERIFIED in this file as a stated norm. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **Q: How much PTT will I pay, and what does a first-time buyer save?** At the general rates in §3 — on the $500,000 worked example there, $8,000 with no exemption. A qualifying first-time buyer is exempt on the first $500,000 of the price at a fair market value of $835,000 or less, with a partial exemption between $835,000 and $860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a home purchase in BC?** On a new build or presale, yes — 5% at completion; resale homes are generally exempt. A qualifying first-time buyer can recover up to $50,000 through the FTHB GST/HST rebate (100% to $1 million, gradually reduced to $1.5 million, none at $1.5 million or more), and the pre-existing new housing rebate alone returns 36% of the GST to a maximum of $6,300, phasing out between $350,000 and $450,000. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Do I pay GST if I buy a vacant lot instead of a home?** At the CRA GI-003 level: a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable; an individual's personal-use land sale is generally exempt — and the FTHB GST rebate applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself (§3). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What is the minimum down payment, and when is mortgage insurance required?** 5% on homes of $500,000 or less; 5% on the first $500,000 plus 10% on the portion over $500,000 for homes between $500,000 and $1,499,999; and 20% at $1,500,000 or more. Below 20% down, mortgage loan insurance is required, and CMHC's published premium range is 0.6% to 4.5% of the total mortgage amount. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/observer/2025/cmhc-mortgage-loan-insurance-explained
- **Q: Can I combine the Home Buyers' Plan and the FHSA, and how much can I take out?** The CRA states a person can make a qualifying FHSA withdrawal and also withdraw under the Home Buyers' Plan for the same qualifying home, as long as all conditions are met at the time of each withdrawal (verified in the sibling passes). The amounts are a different matter in this file: the live page's HBP figure ($60,000) and FHSA figure ($40,000) are UNVERIFIED here — the CRA HBP page returned 403 in this program and the CRA FHSA main page verifies only the $8,000 first-year room — so confirm both limits with the CRA or your lender before planning around them. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws apply to the lot itself); a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. On ALR land a residence cannot be stratified at all (§3). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can I rescind an accepted offer if I wrote subject-free?** Do not rely on the page's summary alone: the page states a 3-business-day rescission period with a 0.25% fee, but that statement is UNVERIFIED in this file — confirm the current rescission rules with your REALTOR® or lawyer before writing without subjects. https://jaredgibbons.ca/faq-for-home-buyers-in-bc.html
- **Q: Does PTT go into my mortgage?** No — the page correctly notes PTT cannot be blended into the mortgage; budget it as cash at registration, at the tiers in §3, before any exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
