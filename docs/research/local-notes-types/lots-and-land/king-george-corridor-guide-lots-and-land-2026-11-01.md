## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey:** Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026) — current. The corridor's 1995 concept-plan designations have been superseded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update (via Corporate Report R246, endorsed December 2, 2024); Bylaw No. 18020 is the replaced predecessor and is never cited as current. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans/king-george-corridor-south and https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/council-meetings-documents/corporate-reports/r133-plansurrey-2013-official-community-plan-surrey-2050-official-community-plan-alignment-related-amendments
- **Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Zoning By-law, 1993, No. 12000 governs; every Surrey property carries a zone under it, shown in the City's COSMOS map; zone minimum subdivision lot areas come from the consolidated bylaw text and are confirmed parcel by parcel in COSMOS. No corridor lot-size table is stated in the research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units — Surrey (adopted July 8, 2024):** a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, applying to eligible residential lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including certain hazard lands and properties not connected to community water and sewer. Homes fronting the boulevard's frequent bus service may fall inside a Frequent Bus Stop Area — check the parcel in COSMOS. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites — Surrey:** only one suite per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit, at most 40% of the principal unit's habitable floor area, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and a suite is not a separate strata lot. A "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Subdivision / servicing — Surrey:** the Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830 is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, verified on the City's own pages in this program; the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Floodplain at the north end:** the corridor's north edge is the Nicomekl River, and Surrey's coastal floodplain runs along the Nicomekl and Serpentine Rivers and the Serpentine–Nicomekl lowlands. Parcel-level flood levels: UNVERIFIED — confirmed with the City per parcel. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/serpentine-nicomekl-lowlands
- **Streamside / riparian (Surrey-wide frame):** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and vary by stream class, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process — the setback for a specific parcel is read off Part 7A and COSMOS; no single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** permitted, owner-only — only the property owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required at $358 per year on the City's program page. Licence-figure conflict, disclosed: the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees — this file carries the $358 program figure with the $350 proposal disclosed. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block (provincial frame — no corridor ALR parcel is documented in the research, stated so it is not misapplied):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for this corridor. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $700,000 lot purchase, no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$700,000 ($10,000) = **$12,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Anchor at $3,500,000 for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling in this program carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, and $93,000 is followed here.) First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. **Foreclosure:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — a Surrey foreclosure runs through a registry outside the city; the registry mapping is UNVERIFIED here, the settled court process itself is Rules 21-7/13-5, PD-66, Form 60, and Form B + PTT/GST treatment on court sales is UNCONFIRMED. No foreclosure angle is documented in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
