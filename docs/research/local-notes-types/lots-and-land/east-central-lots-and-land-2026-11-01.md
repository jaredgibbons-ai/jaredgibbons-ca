## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: East Central has no meaningful bare-land market. This is a built-up downtown and established-residential sub-area — land trades as built RS-1 fee-simple house lots, Town Centre strata lots under existing buildings, and occasional redevelopment assemblies under the Town Centre plan, not as vacant subdivision lots. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no named bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified on any source in this slug's research. The full land treatment for this ground is in the guide-variant land home, cross-referenced here with 0 new counted.**

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare building lot in East Central?** There is no lot product to buy — no named bare-land subdivision, named bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering exists here. What trades is built fee-simple house lots, strata units, and occasional redevelopment assemblies; the full treatment is in the land home file. (§2 above)
- **Q: What is the smallest lot I can create by subdividing an RS-1 property?** The RS-1 minimums are 668.0 m² area, 18.0 m width and 27.0 m depth (Zoning Bylaw No. 7600-2019 s. 605) — both the parent-lot remainder and any new lot must meet them, and Town Centre zone minimums are UNVERIFIED in this file. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/2020-07-28-zoning-bylaw-7600-2019-second-reading
- **Q: What does "bare-land strata" mean on a detached-house listing here?** You own a strata lot, pay strata fees, follow the bylaws and diligence through the Form B — but the lot is a detached-house lot, not a unit in a shared building. No named plan or fee is verified for East Central in this file, so pull the title and Form B before quoting anything. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Q: Can an older house lot carry more units without subdividing?** Yes, on the City's wording: one secondary suite per lot (maximum 40% of gross floor area, not in the floodplain) plus up to three, four or six units on eligible single-family and duplex lots under the SSMUH bylaws. Eligibility is lot-specific — confirm with the City. https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-ready/secondary-suites
- **Q: Does the floodplain affect a south-edge lot?** Yes — flood construction level 6.7 m geodetic, no habitable floor space below it on affected lots, a covenant on title, and suites prohibited on floodplain properties under s. 402.8(h). Confirm the covenant and level on title before valuing any lower-level space. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Q: What extra diligence does an escarpment-edge lot need?** Within 300 m of the escarpment crest (east boundary 224 Street), a geotechnical report on a Section 219 covenant is required before a building permit — budget for it on any rebuild or addition plan. http://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/fraser-river-escarpment-information-package
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes at the CRA GI-003 level — developer/business sales and parcels subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable, unlike an individual's personal-use resale, which is generally exempt. Have the contract state the position. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What PTT do I pay on a $1.2M teardown lot?** $22,000 on the stated tiers before any exemption ($200,000 × 1% + $1,000,000 × 2%). The first-time buyers' and newly built home exemptions are home exemptions; bare-land eligibility is UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot I'm looking at?** Catchments are address-based in SD42. The SETTLED sample chain for 11897 227 Street only is Eric Langton Elementary → Thomas Haney Secondary (SD42 School Locator) — single-address only, non-transferable; any other East Central address needs its own locator run. https://www.sd42.ca/school-locator/
- **Q: How do I read East Central's market numbers for land?** As built-stock numbers, not land numbers: 82 detached listings / 5 sales and 120 attached listings / 11 sales in September 2026, with SnapStats publishing no land category at all. The Maple Ridge community medians in §2 are the steadier context; price a lot from the parcel's own comparables and plan designation. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file §2).
