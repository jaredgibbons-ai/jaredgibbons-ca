## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Port Moody Official Community Plan, Bylaw 2026, No. 3522 (adopted April 28, 2026; repeals Bylaw No. 2955 — never cite 2955 as current):** Chapter 14 covers the north shore in Section 14.2 (Pleasantside and April Road), Section 14.3 (the Heritage Mountain grouping) and Section 14.4 (Ioco Lands, the former Imperial Oil lands). https://www.portmoody.ca/media/ctlfdx01/chapter-14-port-moody-2050-official-community-plan.pdf?docnumber=670733
- **Zoning — Port Moody Zoning Bylaw, 2018, No. 2937:** single-detached homes sit in RS zones and multi-residential homes in RT and RM zones; small-scale multi-unit housing is permitted in RS and RT zones outside the transit-oriented areas — up to four units on lots from 280 m² to 4,050 m², and up to six units within 400 m of frequent bus stops (Bylaw Amendment No. 94, 2024, No. 3460, per the siblings). Secondary suites are allowed in all single-detached zones except manufactured-home parks, and a secondary suite may not be stratified. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/zoning/ and https://www.portmoody.ca/en/building-and-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx
- **Small-lot subdivision — the one real subdivision route on this shore:** subdivision from RS1 to RS1-S (minimum 9 m width and 325 m² lot area) is available only in Moody Centre, Seaview and **Pleasantside** where the OCP designates Single Family–Low Density — Pleasantside is the one north shore area on that list. Confirm the specific lot's designation with the City before pricing a split. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/subdivision/
- **Subdivision + servicing — Port Moody number UNCONFIRMED:** the Port Moody servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed. The documented process instead: subdivision runs against the zone minimums and the RS1-S standard above, and servicing is confirmed with the City for the parcel (utilities billed separately; 2026 utility bill due February 27, 2026). https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- **Heritage — Ioco townsite:** the Ioco townsite is a heritage conservation area; a heritage alteration permit can be required for changes, including exterior work on a townsite lot — check heritage status before planning any work there. https://www.portmoody.ca/business-development-and-planning/development/development-application-process/
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no north shore ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat farm-class figure is asserted for this slug because this file's own research does not document one. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met — none verified on this ground):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since November 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. Port Moody's own short-term-rental position: UNVERIFIED in this research — none asserted. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Property taxes:** Port Moody property taxes for 2026 were due July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding amounts after July 2, 2026 and a further 5% after September 15, 2026; utilities are billed separately. https://www.portmoody.ca/home-and-property/property-taxes-and-utility-fees/property-taxes/
- **Property transfer tax — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,000,000 lot-and-house purchase, fully residential, no exemption — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,000,000 ($16,000) = **$18,000**. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
