## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026)** — the governing plan. Bylaw 18020 is the predecessor and is NEVER cited as current in this file. Former neighbourhood plans rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update are no longer in effect; land-use claims run through the OCP and Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. (Condos sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/surrey-homes-for-sale-condos-2026-11-01.md) https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Zoning: Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000.** A new lot must meet its zone's minimum subdivision lot area in Bylaw 12000. Surrey's July 8, 2024 SSMUH changes renamed the former RF-12 / RF-12C zones to the R4 Small Lot Residential Zone and RF-SD to R6 Semi-Detached — quote current zone names, never the retired ones. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH (Jul 8, 2024 frame, as the research states it):** Surrey's zoning allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide and does not create new lots. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (read 2026-11-01)
- **Secondary suites — settled position:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**. A suite is not a separate strata lot. The often-quoted "90 m²" cap is NOT current — no 90 m² suite figure was found in the current consolidated bylaw text, and it is never stated as current here. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite (read 2026-11-01)
- **Coach houses and garden suites:** all Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; the unit must be at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; smaller R3–R6 lots require lane access; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites (read 2026-11-01)
- **Subdivision / servicing: Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** — verified on the City's own Land Development pages in the L10 pass and carried as verified-from-City-source; the City's process runs through its Engineering Land Development process to a Servicing Agreement that defines the off-site works and securities required for final approval. Current DCC rates for a specific parcel: UNVERIFIED in this slug's research — get them from the City in writing. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement (via ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/surrey-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **Streamside / riparian:** Surrey's streamside setbacks are defined in **Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, with different setbacks by stream class**, complemented by the Sensitive Ecosystems Development Permit process; COSMOS shows environmental or riparian permit areas per parcel. **NO single riparian figure is stated for Surrey land generally** — the parcel's stream class and Part 7A govern. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2025-R081.pdf (via the Surrey Lots & Land home)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey):** permitted, owner-only — a short-term rental is the renting of a dwelling unit by its owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host, the owner must live at the property as their primary residence, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence is required before operating, under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, Part 4, s. B.7(d) and Business License By-law No. 13680, s. 60.1. **Licence figure, conflict disclosed:** the City's program page states **$358 per year** (carried here); the City's 2024 corporate report proposing the amendments put the licence at $350/yr plus fees — check the City licence page before quoting either figure to a client. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals (read 2026-11-01) and https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/corporate-reports/CR_2024-R094.pdf
- **ALR block, in full (for Surrey parcels in the reserve):** subdivision of ALR land requires ALC permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where City bylaws also permit it. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Bare-land strata block (where a Surrey lot is a bare-land strata lot):** Form B (SPA s. 59) — fees, amounts owed, approved levies still payable, CRF balance, insurance summary, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page. Depreciation reports every 5 years for 5+ lots since July 1, 2024, non-deferrable. CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023. Rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (long-term; short-term may still be restricted — and Surrey's owner-only STR rule in this section applies at the municipal level regardless). Only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, incl. May 1, 2023 exemptions). **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** general rates are 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, and 3% of the portion over $2,000,000, with a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — new HOME, not bare land. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated:** $800,000 lot, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** — and **$6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer** on that same $800,000 input (the program's worked anchor for the $800K cap); $1,500,000 lot = $2,000 + 2% × $1,300,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**; $3,500,000 purely residential parcel = $2,000 + $36,000 + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + further 2% × $500,000 ($10,000) = **$93,000** (the $93,000 figure governs; a $78,000 figure for this input appearing in a townhomes sibling elsewhere in the program is an OPEN reconciliation item — it was not met in this slug's siblings and is never carried here). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME purchase, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. CMHC premiums 0.6%–4.5% as the published range only; **the CMHC premium-range error is NOT documented for this slug and is NOT asserted here** (mortgage-calculator file only). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Presales (REDMA):** where land is sold as part of a developer project with homes to be built, REDMA s. 18 (disclosure statement, deposits in trust) and s. 21 (7-day rescission) govern the developer sale. No presale LOT project is counted in this file. https://www.paxlaw.ca/blog/real-estate-law/buying-presale-home-bc-legal-guide-redma/
- **Speculation / foreclosure:** Surrey is an SVT taxable area in the provincial framing; per-parcel SVT: UNCONFIRMED. **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition: only as this file's own sources support — none was opened for this slug, so no position is stated or conformed: UNCONFIRMED here.** **Foreclosure:** Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry — a Surrey foreclosure's registry mapping is UNVERIFIED; New Westminster is a working assumption only where the research states it, and this slug's research does not state it, so no registry is asserted here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes (read 2026-11-01)

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Where is Surrey land documented, if not here?** In the sub-area land files and the Surrey Lots & Land topic home cross-referenced in §2. This city-wide file takes no counts of its own, and the condos file's 39 buildings are built stock, cross-referenced only — never land. (§2 above)
- **Q: How many communities does Surrey have, and what is City Centre's board name?** Seven, per the City: City Centre, Cloverdale, Fleetwood, Guildford, Newton, South Surrey and Whalley. The board sub-area covering City Centre product is Whalley — "Surrey City Centre" is not a board name, and a comp set built on it will miss product. (§1 above) https://www.surrey.ca/about-surrey/our-communities
- **Q: What is Serpentine — South Surrey or Cloverdale?** Both filings are real: the board files Serpentine as a Cloverdale sub-area (Jared ruling: it IS a board sub-area), while its feed ground sits in South Surrey. No market claim is made on either filing, and its SnapStats row (0/0/NA) conflicts with its live feed's 16 listings — reconciliation UNVERIFIED. (§1 above)
- **Q: How many units can go on a Surrey lot?** By SSMUH band: 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 on lots larger than 280 m², up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. That is added-unit permission, not subdivision — it creates no new lots. (§3 above)
- **Q: How big can a secondary suite be?** At most 40% of the principal dwelling unit's habitable floor area, one per dwelling unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite, and never a separate strata lot. There is no current 90 m² cap in Bylaw 12000. (§3 above)
- **Q: What setback applies to a lot beside a creek?** It depends on the stream's class under Part 7A of Bylaw 12000 — there is no single Surrey riparian figure, and none is quoted here. Check the parcel's class and any Sensitive Ecosystems permit area on COSMOS with the City. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental from a Surrey house I buy?** Only if you are the owner, live there as your primary residence, and hold the business licence first — tenants cannot host. The program page states $358/yr; a 2024 City corporate report proposed $350/yr plus fees, so confirm the current figure on the City licence page before quoting it. (§3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay on an $800,000 lot?** PTT $14,000 with no exemption ($2,000 at 1% + $12,000 at 2%); $6,000 for a fully qualifying first-time buyer on the program's anchor math. GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Are there market statistics for Surrey land?** No land figure is stated in this file: SnapStats covers Surrey housing (Detached vs Condos & Townhomes combined only) and publishes no vacant-land category, so land sale counts, price per acre and land days on market are UNVERIFIED. The community housing medians in §1 are context only, never land prices. (§1 above)
- **Q: Can an ALR parcel in Surrey be stratified into lots?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)), and subdivision in the ALR needs ALC approval. Check reserve status on the ALC maps before subjects come off. (§3 above) https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
