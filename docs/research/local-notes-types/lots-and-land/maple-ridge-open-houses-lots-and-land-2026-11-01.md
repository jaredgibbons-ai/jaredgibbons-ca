## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

For a topic file, this section carries first the open-house rule that governs a land viewing, then the Maple Ridge land rules a buyer needs if the open house is for a lot or a house lot — because the open house is where the viewing happens, and title, zoning and tax are where a land purchase is actually diligenced.

### The open-house frame

- **Open-house disclosure — BCFSA Rule 5-10(3), verbatim as sourced in the sibling open-houses files:** Rule 5-10(3) of the Real Estate Rules, as set out in the Office of the Superintendent of Real Estate's November 2017 consultation report on the agency and disclosure rule changes, states that the disclosure of representation is not required when a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or providing factual responses to general questions, unless the licensee solicits or receives information from the party about the party's motivation, financial qualifications or needs in respect of real estate. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/surrey-open-houses-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3
- **Representation at an open house:** a licensed real estate professional in BC must work for either the buyer or the seller in a transaction, must inform consumers of the duties and responsibilities owed to clients and non-clients, and must inform unrepresented consumers of the risks of dealing with a professional who is representing another party to the transaction. The regulator is the BC Financial Services Authority (BCFSA), and the Province's Buying and selling a home page directs prospective purchasers to BCFSA for information on purchasing property in British Columbia. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/real-estate-bc/buying-selling/buying-and-selling-home
- **What that means in practice:** chatting factually with the host at an open house does not by itself make you their client — but once you start sharing your motivation, budget or needs with a licensee who represents the seller, the disclosure rules engage. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Identification or sign-in for open-house visitors:** NOT stated in any BC Gov or BCFSA consumer source found — UNVERIFIED as a rule; a host may still ask, as a matter of the seller's instructions, but that practice is not in an official source. (Research file above.)

### The Maple Ridge land frame (after the open house)

- **Zoning — Maple Ridge Zoning Bylaw 7600-2019 governs.** https://www.mapleridge.ca/build-do-business/planning-land-use/community-planning/housing-action/housing-target-order/new
- **OCP citation — CONTESTED (7060-2014 vs 7998-2024):** flagged here and wherever met in this file, resolved nowhere. The **Albion Area Plan was adopted October 28, 2025** and the **Lougheed Transit Corridor Area Plan was ADOPTED January 27, 2026** (OCP Amending Bylaw No. 7781-2021) per the City record — both cited as adopted; this file's research does not address the corridor plan and documents no contrary reading of its adoption. SSMUH: eligible single-detached and duplex lots may have up to three, four or six units under the City's SSMUH bylaws, settled lot by lot. https://engage.mapleridge.ca/LTCAP
- **Floodplain — a Maple Ridge-specific check on any rural-edge or river-edge open house:** properties along the Fraser River frontage sit in a floodplain where the flood construction level is 6.7 m geodetic (including freeboard), a covenant on title identifies construction in the floodplain, no habitable floor space may be located below the flood construction level on affected lots, and Zoning Bylaw s. 402.8(h) prohibits rental accommodation (suites) on floodplain properties. https://www.mapleridge.ca/media/file/floodplain-construction-0
- **Bare-land strata block (applies if the lot viewed is bare-land strata):** a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR block (framework only, no parcel in this file):** where a Maple Ridge parcel is in the ALR, subdivision needs ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, by Oct 31) — flat "$2,500+" is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT:** 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above; further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000 (mixed-class: residential portion only). FTHB exemption: first $500,000 exempt, full at fair market value ≤$835,000 (maximum saving $8,000), partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, phasing to $1.5M, maximum $50,000; agreements March 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Purchase prohibition:** stated ONLY as this file's own research supports it — the research documents no primary text on it for this topic, so the position is UNCONFIRMED here and not conformed. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I see a vacant lot at a Maple Ridge open house?** If a land listing carries an OPEN HOUSE label in a given week, it appears on this feed like any other listing — detached houses, townhomes, condos and rural acreages are the documented feed types, and an open house is an event on a listing, not a land program. This file documents no lot of its own to visit. https://jaredgibbons.ca/maple-ridge-open-houses.html
- **Q: Do I need my own agent to attend an open house for a lot?** No official source requires it. Rule 5-10(3) excuses disclosure of representation where a licensee is only hosting an advertised open house or answering general factual questions — but the moment you share your motivation, financial qualifications or needs, that changes, and BCFSA requires unrepresented consumers to be told the risks of dealing with a licensee who represents the other party. http://bcfsa.ca/media/1815/download and https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: Is the host at the open house the seller's agent?** The host is a licensee connected to the listing side; BCFSA requires a licensee to work for either the buyer or the seller and to inform consumers of the duties owed to clients and non-clients — assume anything non-factual you tell the host can reach the seller. Use the open house for facts; save your story for your own agent. https://www.bcfsa.ca/public-resources/real-estate/protecting-real-estate-consumers
- **Q: How do I know the open house time is current — and is there a time error on this page?** Each listing carries an OPEN HOUSE label with a date and start and end times, and the page states the feed updates every 15 minutes — check the page the day you plan to go.
