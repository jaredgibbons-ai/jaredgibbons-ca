## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no bare-land market in Downtown Vancouver at all — stated plainly at the top, and it is the strongest honest-zero in this batch. There are no house lots: detached houses are not a Downtown housing form, the research states the area is almost entirely strata apartment form, and no named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, no development site offered as land, and no presale lot offering was verified on any City or developer source. Downtown "land" trades only as strata units, as airspace and development rights inside CD-1 and Downtown District parcels, and as whole-building or assembly transactions documented as such — never as lots. Everything below is that record, with nothing dressed up as inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — no such form verified

- **No fee-simple house-lot stock exists to document:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule permits the uses in the Downtown District Official Development Plan — commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — within a district the City describes as the primary business district; the housing form is overwhelmingly strata condominium, with rental apartment buildings and hotel and office strata also in the mix. A sourced unit-count split by tenure: UNVERIFIED. There is no historic house-lot fabric to describe — the peninsula's lot fabric is commercial and strata parcels. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Lot character — district minimums, as the schedules state them:** Downtown parcels are governed by the DD, CD-1 and C-3A districts rather than house-lot minimums — the C-3A District Schedule's overview table shows no minimum site area for the uses in its section 2.1, and a minimum lot size figure for DD or CD-1 parcels: UNVERIFIED. CD-1 districts are site-specific comprehensive development districts — the parcel's own CD-1 bylaw, not a district-wide table, states what its land can hold. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-C-3A.pdf
- **Serviced position:** Downtown homes are on City water and sewer services, as fully urban serviced stock; a 2026 utility dollar figure for a Downtown home confirmed on a City source: UNVERIFIED. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

### Bare-land strata — none verified

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning framework as the research states it:** the Downtown District (DD) schedule of Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575 governs much of the area — the only permitted uses are those in the Downtown District Official Development Plan (commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses), with development permits approved by the Development Permit Board or the Director of Planning as the schedule states; CD-1 comprehensive development districts and C-3A districts also apply in parts of the area. A plan named only for the Downtown local area: UNVERIFIED — the Downtown Eastside Plan provides the vision for the Downtown Eastside, at the east side of Downtown and in Strathcona. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/dtes-local-area-plan.aspx
- **SSMUH position:** the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing framework is a house-lot framework — Downtown has no house-lot stock for it to attach to (Section 2), and no Downtown SSMUH position is documented in the research. Density here is set by the DD/CD-1 position of the specific parcel, not by a lot-size table — never quote an R1-1 multiplex figure for a Downtown parcel. (Research file above.)
- **Subdivision + servicing — VERIFIED City instruments:** subdivision in Vancouver runs under the Subdivision By-law No. 5208, and its 2026 fees are set by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026), which substitutes a new Schedule F: Class I (Major) $174,300, Class II (Intermediate) $87,200, Class III (Minor) $15,020, Class V (Air Space) $125,100 where FSR is greater than 3.0 (the class a downtown tower parcel would meet), and Class VI (Freehold Rowhouses) $15,020 plus $1,950 per freehold lot. The City's major-subdivision process (sites over 10,000 m², or 10,000–40,000 m² likely to require complex legal agreements) requires a preliminary proposal with a current Land Title Office title search and a lawyer's charge summary, submitted to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/major-subdivision.aspx
- **Heritage controls:** the City's heritage areas work gives regulations for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown — Gastown is north of Hastings Street between Richards and Main Streets, Chinatown's boundaries are Hastings, Union, Gore and Carrall Streets, and Yaletown is bordered by Homer and Nelson Streets to the north and east and Cambie, Mainland and Drake Streets to the south and west. Heritage regulations preserve and protect historic fabric and affect renovation, alteration and any redevelopment of a heritage-district parcel from day one. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Floodplain diligence:** the City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, and land in the floodplain is subject to the Vancouver Building By-law Floodplain Standards and Requirements for shoreline setbacks and flood construction levels; the Downtown shoreline on Burrard Inlet and False Creek is the part of the area to check against that dataset. Whether a specific parcel is in the floodplain: UNVERIFIED. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Strata diligence — the operative regime downtown (the BC strata block):** since nearly every Downtown purchase is a strata purchase, the block that governs is: Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page) — read it with the depreciation report, contingency reserve and any special levies before pricing an offer, as the research directs; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw, and Vancouver licences short-term rentals); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. This same block would apply in full to any bare-land strata lot, if one existed here — none is verified. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Downtown ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED, and there is no bare land here in any case). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. Stated for completeness — a Downtown strata resale is not a vacant-land sale, and no vacant-land transaction form is verified in this area. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY, applies here:** the City's Empty Homes Tax is 3% of assessed value for vacant properties (the City's Empty Homes Tax Annual Report 2025 states a consistent and stable rate of 3%, per the townhomes sibling); every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration, and the 2025 Empty Homes Tax payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a standing check on any Downtown purchase, where investor-held units are common. The provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax is a separate filing (per the townhomes sibling); no rate or area-specific position beyond that is documented in this file's research, and none is stated here. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/2025-empty-homes-tax-annual-report.pdf and https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx
- **Additional school tax (documented in this area's own research):** at Downtown price points the Province's additional school tax can apply — 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Purchase prohibition:** the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for Downtown land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Downtown VE, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Vancouver property taxes:** City of Vancouver 2026 advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes were due July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after each due date; the home owner grant was also due July 3, 2026. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot or a house lot downtown?** No — detached houses are not a Downtown housing form and no bare-land offering of any kind was verified. What exists is strata apartment product, rental buildings, and parcel-level development rights under DD/CD-1 zoning. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is this "Downtown West Vancouver"?** No — that archive title is wrong. This is Downtown VANCOUVER, on the downtown peninsula in the City of Vancouver; West Vancouver is a separate municipality across Burrard Inlet with its own bylaws, taxes and market, and nothing here is priced or governed from there. (FILE STATUS block.)
- **Q: What zoning governs a downtown parcel?** Most of the area sits under the Downtown District (DD) schedule of By-law No. 3575, which permits the uses in the Downtown District Official Development Plan — commercial, residential, institutional and light industrial uses, parks and open spaces, and public uses — with CD-1 site-specific districts and C-3A districts in parts of the area. There is no house-lot minimum table to quote; the parcel's own district governs. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-DD.pdf
- **Q: What subdivision form actually happens downtown?** Airspace and strata-plan work, not lot splits: the 2026 fee schedule's Class V (Air Space) fee is $125,100 where FSR exceeds 3.0, and major proposals go through the City's major-subdivision preliminary process with a title search and a lawyer's charge summary to the Approving Officer. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **Q: Are there leasehold condos downtown, like False Creek South?** No named leasehold strata building on the peninsula was verified in the siblings' pass — the documented City leasehold concentrations are False Creek South (south shore, in Fairview) and Champlain Heights, and neither is Downtown ground. Confirm Freehold Strata on title for the specific building. (Section 2; siblings above.)
- **Q: Do heritage rules affect a Gastown or Yaletown purchase?** Yes — the City's heritage areas carry regulations for Gastown, Chinatown and Yaletown that preserve and protect historic fabric, which affects renovation and alteration plans from day one; Gastown is north of Hastings between Richards and Main, and Yaletown is bordered by Homer and Nelson to the north and east and Cambie, Mainland and Drake to the south and west. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: What extra taxes should a downtown buyer budget for?** The annual Empty Homes Tax declaration on every purchase (3% of assessed value if the home is vacant), and — where the residential assessment exceeds $3 million — the Province's additional school tax: 0.2% between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% above $4 million for 2026, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% on January 1, 2027. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Q: What shoreline checks apply to a waterfront-edge building?** The City's designated floodplain includes the False Creek coastal floodplain, with Building By-law setback and flood construction level requirements for parcels in it — read the building's insurance and strata documents against them. https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/designated-floodplain/information/
- **Q: Which schools serve a downtown address?** By exact address only — Elsie Roy and Crosstown elementaries are located in the area, no public secondary school inside the Downtown local area was confirmed, and no Downtown catchment is settled in this file. Do not promise a secondary school; run every address in the VSB locator. https://vsb.bc.ca/catchment-boundaries
