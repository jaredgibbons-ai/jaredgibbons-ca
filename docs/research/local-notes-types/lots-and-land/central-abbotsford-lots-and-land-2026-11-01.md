## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Near-zero bare-land market — stated plainly at the top, as in the guide file for this sub-area. Central Abbotsford has no bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata and no lot-release product verified in this slug's research. The sub-area is built urban core: what trades as "land" is an individual fee-simple infill/teardown parcel on the older detached streets, or a development site assembled for a plan-designated building form. Verified named land developments counted in THIS file: 0 new.**

### Fee-simple infill parcels — the default form

- **The form:** the central area mixes older single detached streets (RS3, RS5 and infill RS3-i/RS7 zones) with the city's deepest strata stock around South Fraser Way and the City Centre. Detached streets are freehold (many with suite potential under s.140.3.8). A vacant or teardown parcel on those streets is a fee-simple lot — the default form: own title, no strata, no Form B, no strata fees; zoning, the two neighbourhood plans and registered covenants govern. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/ and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** RS3 minimum interior lot size 540 m² and RS5 minimum interior lot size 420 m², as compared in a City council document; RS7 (Infill Residential Zone) minimum lot size 300 m² for new interior lots, with minimum lot width 10.0 m and depth 22.5 m; RS3-i (Urban Residential Zone, Infill) is named in City council material for Maple Street in the Historic Downtown plan area. These are zone minimums — among the smallest detached lot minimums in the city — not a claim that every central lot is that size. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a bare lot in Central Abbotsford?** Not from a subdivision or lot release — none is verified. What trades is an individual fee-simple infill/teardown parcel, priced on its zone and plan designation, or assembled development ground. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a new central lot be?** RS7 allows new interior lots down to 300 m² (10.0 m width, 22.5 m depth); RS3 minimum interior lot is 540 m² and RS5 is 420 m². Reaching the infill zone usually takes the rezoning-led process documented in the guide file's Fir Street report. https://abbotsford.civicweb.net/document/58178/
- **Q: What is an older central house worth as land?** Usually its unit potential: s.140 allows 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots over 280 m² in the stated conditions, and one secondary suite is permitted in all single detached dwellings (s.140.3.8) — confirm the zone section and designation for the parcel before paying for potential. The SSMUH bylaw number ("3560-2024") is UNCONFIRMED. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Is the Rail District selling lots?** No — it is a 19-acre developer assembly/master plan selling built homes, documented once in the guide lots file as an assembly, never inventory. No individual lots are offered on any source checked. https://storeys.com/rail-district-the-mason-abbotsford/
- **Q: Do I pay GST on a newly subdivided central lot?** Generally yes where the seller is a developer/business or the lot is newly subdivided — generally taxable under CRA GI-003; an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. State the GST position in the contract. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I add a coach house on a central lot?** Abbotsford's coach/garden suite cap is 15% of lot area to a maximum of 100 m², and the secondary-suite cap is under 50% of the principal dwelling — Abbotsford's rules only, subject to the s.140 conditions for the lot. https://laws.abbotsford.ca/civix/document/id/coa/coabylaws/2014b2400140
- **Q: Is flood risk a central-lot issue?** The core is upland, not Sumas Prairie — but Willband Creek runs through the central area, so a creek-side parcel still needs the City floodplain map check. https://www.abbotsford.ca/floodresponse
- **Q: Which schools serve a central lot?** The two settled locator chains are in Section 1 (Godson and Dormick Park addresses, both → Abbotsford Middle → Abbotsford Senior Secondary). Any other address needs its own SD34 locator run. https://swift.abbyschools.ca/ourschool/kindergartenregistration/swift
- **Q: What are holding costs on a vacant central parcel?** Abbotsford taxes on the City cycle (due July 2, 2026; 5% July 3, 2026; second 5% September 16, 2026). No strata fee — central lots here are fee-simple. (Section 3.)
