## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / Zoning — Surrey:** Surrey Official Community Plan Bylaw 21900 is current — never cite 18020 as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000 governs. The Crescent Beach Local Area Plan guides Crescent Beach parcels within that frame. White Rock's Zoning Bylaw 2024 No. 2506 and OCP 2017 No. 2220 are White Rock ground ONLY and do not apply to this Surrey ground. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH — Surrey (City position as stated):** maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; the City adopted the SSMUH zoning changes on July 8, 2024. (This 6-unit tier is Surrey's own frame — it is not the Township position, which has no 6-plex tier.) https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites — Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000. A "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap and is not used here. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Streamside setbacks — Surrey:** streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey ground. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** licence $358/yr, with the City's $350 proposal conflict disclosed — both figures are carried, neither is silently chosen. (Standing correction for Surrey in this program.)
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing is determined under Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986), verified on City pages. Subdivision itself requires Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/south-surrey-land-use-plans
- **Property taxes — Surrey:** due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists — parcel status here UNCONFIRMED):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, if a parcel is one):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (Worked example at $3.5M for reference: $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; that figure is an OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met, never silently carried.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
