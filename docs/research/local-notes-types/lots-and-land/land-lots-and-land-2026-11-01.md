### ALR block, in full (where ALR ground is in scope — rural examples above)

- Subdivision of ALR land requires Agricultural Land Commission permission, apart from the limited cases in the ALR General Regulation where an approving officer may approve without the Commission. ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). A principal residence built without an ALC application must be **500 m² or less** total floor area, with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction; a secondary suite is permitted only inside the principal residence; one additional detached residence is permitted without an application at **90 m² or less** (parcels of 40 ha or smaller) or **186 m² or less** (larger parcels), where local bylaws also permit it; local governments may regulate further but cannot be more permissive than the ALC's requirements. **Farm class — three-tier, in full:** $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR wherever met; no page stating such a figure is documented in this slug's research, so no such error is asserted for this page. A parcel's ALR status is checked on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/

### Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot)

- Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; CRF minimum 10% since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Fee-simple contrast:** a fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate

### Taxes and rebates (province-wide, stated once — not a municipal frame)

- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000, based on fair market value (land and improvements) on the day of registration at the Land Title Office unless an exemption applies. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** $500,000 fair market value, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + $6,000 (2% × $300,000) = **$8,000**. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000, maximum saving $8,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Whether GST applies to a specific feed parcel: UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel — confirm with the buyer's legal professional before writing. Anything finer: UNCONFIRMED. (The condos sibling's finer GST framing is not carried — see FILE STATUS.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: How many homes can I build on ALR land?** Generally one principal residence of 500 m² or less in total floor area (with no more than 1,000 m² of fill for its construction), with a secondary suite permitted only inside that residence; one additional detached residence may also be permitted without an ALC application, capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels, where local bylaws also permit it — more than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: How do I check whether a lot is in the Agricultural Land Reserve?** On the Agricultural Land Commission's maps, by PID or civic address; subdivision of reserve land requires ALC permission apart from limited approving-officer cases in the ALR General Regulation. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/application-and-notice-process/applications/required-documents/
- **Q: Can I stratify a farm parcel into lots or townhouses?** No — ALR residences cannot be stratified: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) and ALR subdivision needs ALC permission. A bare-land strata project is a non-ALR conversation (§3). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is a strata lot (Form B, fees, bylaws) whose lot is essentially the building lot itself; a fee-simple lot has no strata at all. The feed-wide split is UNVERIFIED — read tenure off title per parcel (§2).
- **Q: What property transfer tax applies on a land purchase?** The general tiers in §3 — on the worked example there ($500,000, no exemption) the bill is $8,000, calculated on fair market value at registration unless an exemption applies. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax

For any specific feed parcel it is UNVERIFIED — confirm with your legal professional before writing (§3). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
