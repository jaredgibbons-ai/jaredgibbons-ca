## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Township:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw 1987 No. 2500 governs; City of Langley bylaws do NOT apply here. The plan layer is the Murrayville Community Plan, Bylaw 1988 No. 2661 (adopted April 17, 1989), as amended including by the 2025 OCP Update, Bylaw No. 6200 (December 1, 2025). https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/zoning-bylaw.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Community-Plan-Bylaws/Murrayville-Community-Plan-(Bylaw-No.-2661).pdf
- **SSMUH — Township Bylaw 6020 (adopted November 18, 2024):** eligible lots (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, on both municipal water and sewer, not heritage-designated, not in a Transit-Oriented Area) may have 3 units if 280 m² or smaller, or 4 units if between 280 m² and 4,050 m²; lots larger than 4,050 m² are not eligible, and **NO 6-plex / 6-unit transit tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Suites and coach houses — Township:** secondary suite cap 120 m². A coach house is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. **LIVE CONFLICT — flagged, not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m², while the Township's January 2025 guide says townhouse suites are not permitted; neither position is adopted here. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **R-CL compact lots — Township s.403:** minimum lot area 220 m² to maximum LOT area 464.5 m². A "355 m²" figure is nowhere in the bylaw and is not used in this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Willoughby Plan note (Township-wide standing correction, carried so it is never misapplied here):** Willoughby Plan 1836 became Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted December 1, 2025); Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 reached first/second reading May 25, 2026 ONLY, with a Public Hearing directed June 15, 2026 — reading-stage bylaws are never upgraded in this file. (Program standing corrections.)
- **Subdivision and servicing — Township:** all subdivisions must be approved by the Approving Officer under the Land Title Act, through application, review, decision letter, conditions, final approval and registration. Servicing is determined under the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx
- **Township taxes:** Township of Langley property taxes were due Thursday, July 2, 2026; a 5% penalty applies at midnight July 2, 2026, and a further 5% at midnight September 3, 2026. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/property-taxes.aspx
- **ALR block (parcel status UNCONFIRMED per parcel here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where they exist):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw — the exact point Cedar Crest Estates' conflict turns on (§2). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 lot purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** (an eligible first-time buyer position on the same value is $6,000 under the FTHB exemption below). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **FTHB PTT exemption / Newly Built Home Exemption:** first $500,000 exempt, full at value ≤$835,000 (max saving $8,000), partial to $860,000; Newly Built Home Exemption full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; a person who paid PTT on vacant land and then has a newly built home on it may be eligible for a refund of the tax paid. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/newly-built-home-exemption
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
