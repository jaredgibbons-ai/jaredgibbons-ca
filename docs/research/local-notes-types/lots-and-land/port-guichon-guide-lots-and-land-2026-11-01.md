## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Honest position, stated at the top: Port Guichon has no meaningful bare-land market. No named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata community, presale lot offering, or serviced vacant-lot development was verified in this sub-area on an official or developer source. What trades here is individual fee-simple parcels — detached-house lots on the RS streets, and farm-scale parcels under A1 Agriculture zoning at the west edge. Any lot count, typical lot size beyond the zoning minimums in Section 3, or lot price would be invented, so none is stated.**

### What land actually trades here

- **Fee-simple detached lots — the default and dominant form.** The confirmed stock is freehold detached housing under the RS zones (RS1, RS5, RS6 and RS7 confirmed in west Ladner). A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees — diligence is title (including flood covenants, Section 3), zoning and municipal flood-construction requirements. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Lot character, as the research states it:** urban minimums frame the east side — RS5 at 550/460 m², RS6 at 390 m², RS7 at 335 m² — with RS1 at 4,000 m² in lower-density parts. These are minimums for creating a lot, not a claim about any individual parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Farm parcels at the west edge — the second land system.** A1 Agriculture zoning is confirmed toward the west edge of the Ladner community, where the A1 zone sets an **8-hectare minimum lot area for parcels created by subdivision**. A west-edge parcel is farm ground with a housing envelope, not a subdividable residential lot — price and plan it on that basis. The ALR status of any specific Port Guichon parcel: UNVERIFIED in the research — check the parcel on the ALC maps by PID or civic address. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Delta Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future" — consolidated, with NO lettered schedules.** The former area plans were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950; farm land under the current OCP is designated "Agriculture (A)". Any source attributing a lettered schedule to Bylaw 8400 is wrong. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Delta zoning number: CONTESTED — flagged, never asserted.** City zone sheets cite the zoning bylaw as No. 7600, 2017, while an internal consolidated title reading "8400, 2025" also circulates. Neither is asserted as settled; the ruling is Jared's. The zone on any parcel is read off the City's zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **Zoning / subdivision minimums (as the research states them):** RS5 550/460 m², RS6 390 m², RS7 335 m² in the urban part; RS1 at 4,000 m² in lower-density parts; and A1 at an 8-hectare minimum for parcels created by subdivision at the west edge. A full per-zone table for this sub-area: UNVERIFIED. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS5.pdf
- **SSMUH: Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024.** Delta's SSMUH amendments allow combinations of single detached dwellings, duplex dwellings, secondary suites, coach houses and garden suites where the zoning applies; the RS1 zone also lists a secondary suite as an accessory use. Added-unit permission is NOT subdivision permission and does not change the A1 8-hectare minimum. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official and https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision in Delta runs under the City's Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw No. 8288, 2024 (repealing Bylaw No. 7162, 2015), made under Local Government Act s. 506 for highways, water, sanitary sewers, drainage and other services on subdivision. Parcel-specific works/frontage requirements beyond that: UNCONFIRMED in the research — confirm with the City for the specific parcel. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/226157/
- **ALR rules (west-edge parcels, where ALR status is confirmed for the parcel):** ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval, as the ALC states it. ALC residence caps: principal residence ≤500 m², a suite within the principal residence only, and an additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Farm class (BC Assessment) — full three-tier form:** $2,500 of farm income on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 on under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR and appears nowhere in this file as a rule. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Flood and dike rules — this sub-area's real diligence layer, for any housing form.** Port Guichon is low land on the Fraser River side of Ladner behind the City's dike system; Delta operates and maintains over 63 kilometres of flood protection infrastructure, 31 drainage pump stations and approximately 538 kilometres of drainage infrastructure, and directs owners to DeltaMap to check streamside areas, diked channels and parcel-level flood information for a specific lot. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and http://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/sanitary-sewer-drainage
- **Flood covenants on title.** In Ladner approvals on Chisholm Street, the City required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and statutory rights-of-way so the dike can be raised to 5.5 metres — the pattern a buyer on river-side lots here should search title for, on a land purchase exactly as on a house purchase. https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Streamside rules.** All properties in Delta within 30 m of a stream, including sloughs and ditches, fall within the Streamside Protection and Enhancement Development Permit Area — a live constraint on adding a suite, coach house or any ground-oriented unit on a Port Guichon lot. https://www.delta.ca/sites/default/files/2023-06/Form%20T1%20-%20SPEA%20DPA%20Application%20Guide%20-%20May%202023.pdf
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief on a new principal-residence HOME up to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a home exemption, not a bare-land exemption. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — stated only at the level the CRA source supports:** CRA Info Sheet GI-003 states most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use), while sales can be taxable where the land was capital property used primarily in a business, sold in the course of a business, or created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts. Sales by developers/businesses and newly subdivided lots are therefore generally TAXABLE and an individual's personal-use sale generally exempt, at that level only. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED — get tax advice. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / HBP / FHSA — land angle stated precisely:** the First-Time Home Buyer GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not to bare land itself. The older New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, phasing out $350,000–$450,000. Home Buyers' Plan: up to $60,000 per buyer. First Home Savings Account: $8,000 per year, $40,000 lifetime. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy taxes + foreign-buyer prohibition — Delta position only:** the Province's Speculation and Vacancy Tax taxable-areas list includes the City of Delta, and states reserve lands, treaty lands and lands of self-governing Indigenous Nations are NOT part of the taxable areas. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply here. Whether SVT tax is payable on a specific vacant parcel, and any federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a specific buyer: UNCONFIRMED in this area's research. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Bare-land strata block (for completeness — none verified in Port Guichon):** where a bare-land strata lot does exist in BC, it is strata tenure — Form B diligence (SPA s. 59; provided within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page), stratas with 5+ lots on a 5-year depreciation-report cycle since July 1, 2024, a minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023, rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022, and only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). This block also governs a River West apartment purchase in the cross-reference above. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Delta property taxes:** 2026 due date July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **CMHC premium range:** 0.60% at 65% loan-to-value or less, to 4.00% at 90.01%–95%, and 4.50% in that band with a non-traditional down payment. https://www.cmhc-schl.gc.ca/professionals/project-funding-and-mortgage-financing/mortgage-loan-insurance/mortgage-loan-insurance-homeownership-programs/second-home

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is Port Guichon in Tsawwassen?** No — it is in Ladner, in west Ladner around River Road West and 46A Street; Ladner and Tsawwassen are separate Delta communities that together make up South Delta. Any page or listing framing it as Tsawwassen is carrying the known geography error. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new lot subdivision I can buy here?** None verified — zero named bare-land developments, zero bare-land strata, zero presale lot offerings. The market is individual resale parcels: RS house lots in the east, A1 farm parcels at the west edge. (Section 2 above)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a west-edge farm parcel into house lots?** No at residential scale: A1 sets an 8-hectare minimum for parcels created by subdivision, and if the parcel is in the ALR, subdivision additionally requires ALC approval and an ALR residence cannot be stratified at all. Confirm ALR status on the ALC maps before pricing any split. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I add a suite or coach house to a Port Guichon house lot instead?** Possibly: a secondary suite is an accessory use in the RS1 zone, and Delta's SSMUH amendments (Bylaws 8394/8395, June 24, 2024) add duplex, coach house and garden suite options where the zoning applies — but any lot within 30 m of a stream, slough or ditch is also in the Streamside DPA. Confirm the parcel's zone and permit areas with the City first. http://www.delta.ca/community-culture/happening-delta/news/city-delta-first-adopt-new-housing-focused-official
- **Q: Is the lot behind a dike, and can a flood covenant sit on title?** Yes to the dike system — low Fraser River-side land protected by Delta's flood protection system — and covenants are a real Ladner pattern: the City has required Section 219 flood-proofing covenants and rights-of-way for future dike raising to 5.5 m in Ladner approvals, so search title and check DeltaMap before subjects come off. https://www.delta.ca/services/water-sewer-drainage/drainage/flood-protection and https://delta.civicweb.net/document/104061/
- **Q: Is the parcel serviced, or will I need a well and septic?** UNCONFIRMED per parcel in the research — the file's own questions for Jared flag wells/septic/drainage as a live issue on older west-Ladner properties. Confirm City connections; for an unserviced parcel check GWELLS and the Fraser Health onsite-sewerage requirements (system complying with the Sewerage System Regulation, designed/installed by an Authorized Person). https://www.fraserhealth.ca/health-topics-a-to-z/onsite-sewerage-systems/regulatory-requirements-for-onsite-sewerage-systems
- **Q: Will I pay GST on a vacant parcel here?** Depends on the seller and history: CRA states most individual personal-use sales are exempt, but business-use, in-the-course-of-business and more-than-two-part subdivision sales can be taxable; a developer's newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Can I get farm-class taxes on a west-edge parcel?** Only if the three-tier income test is met ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha) with an application by October 31 — acreage alone does not qualify. https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/loo87/loo87/42_411_95
- **Q: What are the holding costs while I hold land?** Delta property taxes (due July 2, 2026; 5% penalties after July 2 and again after September 2, 2026), plus drainage/field maintenance on farm parcels; no strata fees exist on a fee-simple parcel. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes
- **Q: Which schools and buses serve a lot here?** SD37 catchments are address-based; the sample address 4381 46A St maps to Port Guichon Elementary and Delta Secondary. Route 618 (Ladner South loop) calls at 46A Street at River Road, and Ladner Exchange is the hub for the 601 to Bridgeport Station. https://www.deltasd.bc.ca/schools/school-locator/ and http://infomaps.translink.ca/system_maps/exchange_maps/ladner.pdf
