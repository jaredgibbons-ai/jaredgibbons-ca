## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Shaughnessy has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. The sub-area is essentially fully built out as estate residential; no named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale lot offering was verified. Land here trades as a large fee-simple estate lot with a house on it — and in First Shaughnessy, the lot's value is governed by heritage and floor-space rules (§3) as much as by its size.**

### Fee-simple estate lots — the only land form

- **Lot character, as the City states it:** First Shaughnessy's subdivision minimums are a 100 ft (30.48 m) parcel width and 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) parcel area under the City's Subdivision By-law, Schedule A — which is why the district's estate lots are among the largest standard residential lots in the city. Housing is large estate homes — Edwardian and interwar mansions in First Shaughnessy, mid-century and modern estates further south — on those lots, many with 100 ft+ frontages and mature landscaping. Almost entirely freehold detached; strata product is minimal, at the edges. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/scheda.pdf
- **Build size is capped, not lot-driven:** the First Shaughnessy District Schedule caps maximum floor space ratio at 0.45 where a protected heritage property is conserved, otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m² to a maximum floor area of 911 m² — so even a very large lot cannot carry an unlimited-size new house. http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf
- **Heritage status sits on the lot:** First Shaughnessy's protected heritage properties (pre-1940) cannot simply be demolished; value often sits in retention-plus-infill/coach-house schemes permitted by the HCA (see §3). South of King Edward Avenue (Second and Third Shaughnessy), no HCA status applies. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf

A specific lot's services are confirmed with the City. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/missing/shaughnessy.md)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Heritage Conservation Area — the defining rule (First Shaughnessy):** on September 29, 2015, Council adopted the HCA Official Development Plan (By-law No. 11349) and designated First Shaughnessy as an HCA. A separate First Shaughnessy District Schedule sits in the Zoning and Development By-law, and protected (pre-1940) heritage properties can only be removed from the protected list through a Council process following a heritage character and value assessment. The HCA framework gives owners of protected homes additional development options — coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill buildings — as incentives to conserve the heritage house. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx and https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf and http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf
- **Subdivision — VERIFIED for Vancouver:** the City's Subdivision By-law is **By-law 5208**, with subdivision fees under **By-law 14538**. First Shaughnessy's Schedule A minimums under that framework are 100 ft (30.48 m) parcel width and 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) parcel area — a lot that cannot produce two parcels meeting both minimums cannot be split, whatever its total area. Servicing standards for a Vancouver subdivision are confirmed with the City for the parcel. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/scheda.pdf
- **Zoning / OCP south of the HCA:** south of King Edward Avenue, the citywide framework applies — citywide land use is now governed by the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. For detached lots outside the HCA, the multiplex by-law (By-law No. 13817) replaced the RS districts with R1-1, allowing up to 6 strata units or 8 secured rental units — the interaction between R1-1 and the First Shaughnessy District Schedule must be confirmed property by property. https://vancouver.ca/files/cov/vancouver-plan.pdf and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/13817.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Shaughnessy ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is not asserted for this ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata frame (for the district's built strata pockets only — no bare-land strata is verified here):** Form B under SPA s. 59; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for strata corporations with 5 or more lots since July 1, 2024; at least 10% of the annual operating budget to the CRF since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws are valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **City of Vancouver taxes:** advance taxes were due February 3, 2026 and main taxes July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty applied to the unpaid balance after the due date. Because most Shaughnessy homes assess above $3 million, BC's additional school tax is also material here: 0.2% on the residential portion of assessed value between $3 million and $4 million and 0.4% on the portion above $4 million, rising to 0.3% and 0.6% effective January 1, 2027. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/deadlines-and-penalties.aspx and https://www2.gov.BC.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/school-tax/additional-school-tax-rate
- **Empty Homes Tax — VANCOUVER ONLY:** the City of Vancouver charges an Empty Homes Tax of **3% of the property's assessed taxable value** on homes declared or deemed vacant. It applies on this Vancouver ground; it does not apply in any other municipality, and it does not apply on UEL / university ground (a different file's ground in any case). https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Property transfer tax:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked example, inputs stated: a $3,500,000 purchase with no exemption = 1% × $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% × $1,800,000 ($36,000) + 3% × $1,500,000 ($45,000) + a further 2% on the $500,000 residential portion above $3 million ($10,000) = **$93,000, INCLUDING the further 2% tier**. A $78,000 figure for the same inputs omits that further tier; where that figure is met (a townhomes sibling carries it), this file follows **$93,000** and flags the conflict rather than silently carrying $78,000 — reconciliation is an open summary-pass item. Exemptions: first-time buyers — exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000; newly built home exemption — full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 (a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% of GST to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Shaughnessy?** No verified bare-land offering exists — the district is built-out estate residential. A land purchase here is an estate house on its fee-simple lot. (§2 above)
- **Q: Can a protected Shaughnessy heritage house be demolished?** Not as of right. A listed property's status is reviewed through a heritage character and value assessment, and removal from the protected list is a legislative act requiring Council approval at a public hearing. https://guidelines.vancouver.ca/bulletins/bulletin-first-shaughnessy-heritage-conservation-area-procedure-for-review-of-protected-heritage.pdf
- **Q: What development options do protected-home owners get?** The HCA zoning provides incentives for conserving pre-1940 homes, including coach house, multiple conversion dwelling and infill building options — price a protected lot with those incentives in view. http://council.vancouver.ca/20150721/documents/phea-QandA.pdf
- **Q: Can I split a Shaughnessy lot?** Only if the result meets First Shaughnessy's subdivision minimums — 100 ft (30.48 m) minimum parcel width and 13,000 sq ft (1,207.7 m²) minimum parcel area under Subdivision By-law 5208, Schedule A — and, in the HCA, survives the heritage process. Most estate lots were platted at or near those minimums. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/scheda.pdf
- **Q: How big a house can I build on a large lot?** The lot does not decide — the District Schedule cap does: 0.45 FSR where a protected heritage property is conserved, otherwise 0.25 plus 139 m² to a maximum floor area of 911 m². http://former.vancouver.ca/commsvcs/BYLAWS/zoning/fsd.pdf
- **Q: What is the difference between First Shaughnessy and the rest of the district, for a lot buyer?** Only the area north of King Edward Avenue, within the City-defined HCA boundaries, is First Shaughnessy — HCA, District Schedule and protected-list rules apply there. South of King Edward the citywide framework and, for detached lots, the R1-1 multiplex rules (By-law 13817) apply instead, confirmed property by property. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/vancouvers-heritage-areas.aspx
- **Q: Does the Empty Homes Tax apply to a Shaughnessy purchase?** If the home will sit vacant or be held as a secondary property, yes — 3% of assessed taxable value on homes declared or deemed vacant (City of Vancouver only). Also model the additional school tax (0.2% on $3–4M, 0.4% above $4M, rising January 1, 2027) into annual carrying costs. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
- **Q: Is Whitford a land opportunity?** No — Whitford is a completed strata townhouse complex on an eight-lot Oak Street assembly, counted in the townhomes sibling on its disclosed basis. It is cross-referenced in §2 only, as an assembly that has already happened. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/shaughnessy-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What about Devonshire Heights?** A townhouse-form name documented in the siblings without a title line or unit count — it is QUEUED program-wide, documented and flagged, and counted nowhere in this file. It is not a lot offering. (§2 above)
- **Q: What are homes worth in Shaughnessy?** UNVERIFIED from any published stats source in this program: the SnapStats GVR report contains no City of Vancouver data at all.
