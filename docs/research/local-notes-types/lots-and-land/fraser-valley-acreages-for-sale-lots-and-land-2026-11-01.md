## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **ALR block (the governing frame for most of this page's ground, applied parcel by parcel):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified without the ALC's permission (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)) — a permission rule, not an absolute ban, and Bulletin 05 contains no stratification statement and is never cited for it. Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local governments may regulate further but not more permissively than the ALC. ALR short-term accommodation: renting the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used for short-term accommodation only as agri-tourism accommodation on a property that is actively farmed and holds farm classification. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/assets/alc/assets/legislation-and-regulation/information-bulletins/ib_09_subdivision_and_plans_that_cause_subdivision.pdf
- **Farm class — THREE tiers (a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR):** a producing farm must meet minimum gross income at farm gate prices of $2,500 if the farm operation is 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area in excess of 4 ha if the operation is greater than 4 ha; and $10,000 if the operation is less than 0.8 ha. New applications must be submitted by the legislated deadline of October 31 for the next assessment and tax year; BC Assessment removes farm classification if the property no longer meets the requirements, and a buyer continuing to farm must apply by October 31. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Municipal frame — stated PER example, never blended:** Township of Langley parcels follow Township Zoning Bylaw 2500 / SSMUH Bylaw 6020 / Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382; Abbotsford, Chilliwack and Mission parcels follow those cities' own bylaws; FVRD electoral-area parcels follow FVRD/Province rules with rural taxes. No one frame is stated as this topic's frame — confirm the parcel's jurisdiction first, then its rules. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-12/fraser-valley-acreages-for-sale.md)
- **Bare-land strata block (off-ALR ground, wherever a bare-land strata lot trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. On ALR ground, ALC permission comes first (ALR block above). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $1,800,000 acreage parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $1,600,000 ($32,000) = **$34,000**. (PTT is provincial — the same tiers apply whichever valley jurisdiction the parcel sits in.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Property taxes follow the parcel's jurisdiction, never blended:** municipal parcels pay their city/district (each with its own due date and penalty); rural electoral-area parcels pay the Province under the Taxation (Rural Area) Act s.11 (due Jul 2; 5% after Jul 2; further 5% after Oct 31). No single tax calendar is stated for this regional topic. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/annual-property-tax/rural-area and https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/complete/statreg/96448_01

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a Fraser Valley acreage price or market statistic?** No — SnapStats publishes no acreage segment: no acreage count, share or median exists for the Fraser Valley or any community in it. Acreages sit inside their own communities' ordinary Detached rows, split only as Detached vs "Condos & Townhomes." Never quote an "average acreage price." (§1 above)
- **Q: Which rules govern an acreage I find on this page?** The rules of the jurisdiction where the parcel actually sits — Township, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Mission or FVRD frames differ and are never blended — plus the ALR frame if the parcel is in the reserve. Confirm the jurisdiction first. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: How many homes can I have on an ALR acreage?** Generally one principal residence (maximum 500 m²), plus, where the property qualifies, one additional residence capped at 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha), with a suite only inside the principal residence; more than that, or a larger residence, requires ALC approval, and local bylaws may restrict further. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Can I stratify or subdivide an ALR acreage into strata lots?** Not without the ALC's permission: depositing a strata plan is a subdivision, and ALCA s.21(1) bars subdividing agricultural land unless the Commission permits it or the ALR General Regulation allows it (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s.6). Do not write "can never be stratified," and do not cite Bulletin 05 for stratification. (§3 above)
- **Q: What farm income does an acreage need to keep farm classification?** The three BC Assessment tiers: $2,500 at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — with new applications due by October 31 for the next tax year. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a specific parcel on this feed in the ALR?** UNVERIFIED until checked parcel by parcel on the ALC maps by PID — only one feed card's ALR statement is even recorded (as listing text) in the research. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: What tax do I pay on an acreage purchase?** PTT on the general tiers — on the §3 worked example ($1,800,000, illustration only) the bill is $34,000 with no exemption; PTT is provincial, so the tiers are the same in every valley jurisdiction. GST depends on the seller and the parcel's history at the CRA GI-003 level — confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I run short-term rentals on an ALR acreage?** Renting the entire principal residence short-term is not permitted outright in the ALR and requires ALC approval; an additional residence may be used only as agri-tourism accommodation on an actively farmed, farm-classified property. (§3 above)
- **Q: What servicing diligence can't this page do for me?** Well and septic records, water licences, floodplain and access are parcel facts — one feed card already shows why, describing an ALR parcel bordered by a park "partially outside the ALR" with buyer-to-verify language on other uses (listing text). (§2 above)
