## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Surrey City Centre has no meaningful bare-land market — stated plainly at the top. This is a built-out, high-density downtown core designated for mixed-use tower development under the City Centre Plan. No named bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata plan, and no presale LOT offering was verified. Land here trades as assembled tower parcels and development applications processed under the City Centre framework — documented below AS development sites/proposals, never as inventory — and, at the core's edges, as built detached house lots in surrounding Whalley blocks.**

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — Surrey Bylaw 21900 (never 18020 as current):** Surrey's current Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 21900; the former OCP Bylaw No. 18020 is replaced and is never cited as current in this file. The downtown's own layer is the City Centre Plan (approved 2017; an update underway adds a defined Central Business District, an Entertainment District, and more transit-oriented development around stations). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/surrey-city-centre-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Zoning — Surrey Zoning Bylaw 12000:** governs the core's high-density mixed-use designations and the fringe lots alike. Streamside setbacks sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — NO single riparian figure is ever stated for Surrey. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Secondary suites — 40% cap:** under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000, one secondary suite may be permitted per single family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; a suite is not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; and a suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit — the "90 m² cap" sometimes quoted is NOT current. Relevant to fringe house lots, not tower parcels. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000 (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/surrey-city-centre-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential:** Surrey's SSMUH framework (in force since July 8, 2024) allows a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop, on eligible lots under 4,050 m² within the Urban Containment Boundary — relevant to the detached fringe blocks around the core, not to the tower parcels. The City states the SSMUH changes do NOT retroactively legalize a secondary suite or coach house built without permits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/surrey-city-centre-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Subdivision + servicing — Bylaw 8830 (1986), verified ledger:** Surrey's subdivision/servicing instrument is carried in this program's verified ledger as Bylaw 8830 (1986). Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City.
- **Density charges — CAC superseded by ACC (June 15, 2026):** City Centre projects seeking bonus density paid Community Amenity Contributions under Surrey's two-tier density-bonus framework; the City states CACs were superseded by its Amenity Cost Charge and bonus density provisions as of June 15, 2026, with the CAC page kept as a historic reference for eligible in-stream applications. Exact per-file CAC/ACC/DCC amounts are application-specific — UNVERIFIED as general figures. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/community-amenity-contributions
- **Short-term rentals — $358/yr, with the $350 conflict disclosed:** in Surrey, only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host a short-term rental, tenants cannot run one, and a business licence at $358 per year is required first, under Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 and the Business License Bylaw. A $350 figure also circulates in program sources for this licence — the conflict is disclosed, not resolved: the siblings carry $358/yr as the stated licence figure, and the $350 variant is flagged wherever met. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/legislation-and-changes/changes-to-legislation (Sibling: ~/workspace/local-notes/condos/surrey-city-centre-condos-2026-11-01.md)
- **ALR rules (for completeness — the core is outside the ALR):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and it is NOT asserted for any Surrey City Centre ground. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general rates 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked examples (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $900,000 → $16,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $1,500,000 → $28,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M (a $78,000 figure carried in one townhomes sibling for this value is NOT followed here — the conflict is flagged and $93,000 is followed per the tier math: $38,000 to $2M + $45,000 at 3% on $1.5M + $10,000 at the further 2% on $500,000). First-Time Home Buyers' Program: full exemption on the first $500,000 at values of $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. For built product the siblings state the companion position: 5% GST applies to new construction, not resales. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122 — a March 12 date in a secondary source is noted in the siblings, CRA governs, never silently overwritten; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. A live page's $1.25M straight-line phase-out row is UNVERIFIED as a mechanism wherever met. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Foreclosure / court-ordered sales:** the settled court process applies (BC Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7/13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60). Form B and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED, kept distinct from GI-003. **Registry ledger: Surrey has NO Supreme Court registry** (Abbotsford is the exception that HAS one); which registry serves a Surrey filing — mapping UNVERIFIED. https://www.bccourts.ca/
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no City Centre-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for this ground is UNCONFIRMED in this file — no primary prohibition text was opened for this file, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Surrey property taxes:** due July 2 (notices sent end of May; if the due date falls on a weekend or statutory holiday, payment the next business day is without penalty), with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% penalty after September 2. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot downtown?** No verified bare-land offering exists — the core is built-out tower ground. Land trades as assembled parcels through the City Centre Plan application process (documented in §2 as applications, never inventory), or as a built house lot on the Whalley fringe. (§2.)
- **Q: What governs what can be built on a downtown parcel?** The City Centre Plan (2017, update underway adding a Central Business District and an Entertainment District) inside Surrey OCP Bylaw 21900, with zoning under Bylaw 12000 — and density charges now under the Amenity Cost Charge regime that superseded CACs on June 15, 2026. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/whalley-land-use-plans/city-centre-plan
- **Q: What can I do with a detached lot on the fringe?** Surrey's SSMUH framework allows 3 units on lots under 280 m², 4 units over 280 m², and up to 6 on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop (eligible lots under 4,050 m² in the Urban Containment Boundary); one secondary suite up to 40% of the principal dwelling may also be permitted, with no suite inside a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: Is downtown land in the ALR or the floodplain?** Neither, on the research record: the core is designated high-density mixed-use (outside the ALR) and is upland, away from the Nicomekl/Serpentine and Little Campbell floodplains. Confirm a specific parcel on COSMOS. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/water-drainage-sewer/flood-control/lowlands-floodplains/surrey-coastal-floodplain
- **Q: Are there bare-land strata lots downtown?** None verified — and remember the form itself: a bare-land strata lot is strata tenure (Form B, fees, bylaws), not fee-simple land. (§2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a fringe address?** By exact address only — the settled SD36 run (13367 97 Ave → A. H. P. Matthew Elementary / Queen Elizabeth Secondary) is a single-address result and does not transfer. A.H.P. Matthew Elementary and École Kwantlen Park Secondary are the in-area schools. https://www.surreyschools.ca/kwantlenpark
- **Q: Can I run a short-term rental on a fringe house lot?** Only as an owner living at the property as a primary residence, with a Surrey business licence at $358/yr first (the $350 variant in circulation is a disclosed conflict, §3); tenants cannot host. (§3.)
- **Q: Will GST apply on a developer's newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable (CRA GI-003); an individual's personal-use resale is generally exempt. The FTHB GST rebate applies to new housing, not bare land. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
