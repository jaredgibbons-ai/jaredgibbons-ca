## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Zoning and Development By-law No. 3575, R1-1 District Schedule:** minimum site areas 306 m² (single detached house, duplex, or 3–4-unit multiple dwelling; 10.0 m frontage) / 464 m² (5 units; 13.4 m) / 557 m² (6–8 units; 15.1 m), with secondary suite and laneway house with a house and multiplexes to 6 units or 8 rental units as the schedule states. This multiplex regime — not any existing subdivision — is how new strata product arrives in the pocket. Parcel-level zone: UNVERIFIED. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Plan — no separate pocket plan + Vancouver Official Development Plan:** MacKenzie Heights has no separate adopted community plan; it falls under Kerrisdale's citywide-policy position — the City's Kerrisdale page notes the one nearby recently completed plan is the Marpole community plan, to the south. Citywide direction comes from the Vancouver Official Development Plan, which replaced the Vancouver Plan on March 31, 2026. There is no local plan document promising future density here. https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/kerrisdale.aspx
- **Subdivision — Subdivision By-law No. 5208, fees by No. 14538:** Vancouver subdivision is governed by the Subdivision By-law (No. 5208), whose Schedule A sets minimum parcel width and area standards by district and sub-area category, administered by the Approving Officer; its fee schedule was most recently amended for 2026 by By-law No. 14538 (enacted December 9, 2025, in force January 1, 2026). A fee-simple split of a pocket lot must clear both R1-1 and its Subdivision By-law category — neither is assumed from a neighbouring lot's history. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/subdiv/subdiv.htm and https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/consolidated/14538.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units as the City states them:** the R1-1 schedule above IS Vancouver's small-scale multi-unit framework for these streets — house plus suite and laneway, duplex, or a multiplex to 6 strata / 8 rental units by lot size — so the "SSMUH question" here is a per-lot schedule check (306 / 464 / 557 m²), not a separate overlay. https://bylaws.vancouver.ca/zoning/zoning-by-law-district-schedule-R1-1.pdf
- **Strata diligence (The Heights — and any bare-land strata lot, if one is ever met here):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (The Heights, at 5 lots on the listing record, sits exactly on that threshold, so the duty applies; a future 3–4 unit multiplex strata sits under it); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw and are separately regulated in Vancouver); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. No counted complex here carries a published 55+ bylaw, and the 55-plus sibling verifies NO 55+ strata ownership complex in MacKenzie Heights. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no pocket ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Vancouver Empty Homes Tax — 3%, Vancouver only:** every Vancouver owner must submit an annual property status declaration; homes declared or deemed vacant for the 2025 reference year are taxed at 3% of assessed taxable value, and the 2025 payment was due April 16, 2026, with a 5% penalty on the unpaid balance after that date — a holding cost on any vacant pocket house bought for a future multiplex project. Confirm the seller's declaration status on any purchase. The City tax is separate from the provincial Speculation and Vacancy Tax, which may also apply. https://vancouver.ca/home-property-development/empty-homes-tax.aspx
