## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township of Langley frame (applies ONLY to Township parcels):** Zoning Bylaw 2500; SSMUH amendments in Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024) allow 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², subject to eligibility (inside the Urban Containment Boundary, connected to municipal water and sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area, not larger than 4,050 m²) — **NO 6-plex tier exists in Bylaw 6020**. Most acreage parcels in this band exceed the 4,050 m² eligibility ceiling, so the SSMUH framing generally does not apply to them. Added-unit permission is NOT permission to subdivide. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township R-CL correction, carried because Willoughby product is in this page's stated scope:** Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.403 (R-CL) = 220 m² minimum LOT area / 464.5 m² maximum LOT area on a fresh re-read of s.403.7 — the "355 m²" figure is NOWHERE in the bylaw and is never carried. Township suite cap: a secondary suite may not exceed 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one suite per principal dwelling unit; on ALR lands a suite must also comply with ALC regulations. A coach house in the Township is a fee-simple second unit under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md)
- **Township subdivision / servicing:** Township of Langley Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 (confirmed in bylaw text) governs subdivision servicing for Township parcels. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — below this band in practice. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land, and below this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
