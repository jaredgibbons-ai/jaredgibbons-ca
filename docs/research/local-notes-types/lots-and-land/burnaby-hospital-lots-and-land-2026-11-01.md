## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Burnaby Zoning Bylaw 2026, No. 14815, adopted June 9, 2026, in effect July 1, 2026.** Never cite the former 1965 Bylaw No. 4742 for a current Burnaby lot. Burnaby consolidated its former single- and two-family districts into one R1 Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (SSMUH) District in June 2024. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **OCP — Burnaby 2050 Official Community Plan, adopted December 9, 2025,** replaces the 1998 OCP and takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict. The Cascade Heights Community Plan, for the neighbourhood at this sub-area's south side, was adopted in March 2025 — read south-side lots against both documents, with the 2050 OCP governing on conflict. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it:** R1 lots may hold 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop; lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. That stratification is how new strata lots are created on a house lot here — it is not a bare-land strata subdivision, and eligibility is parcel-specific. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** subdivision parcel area, shape and dimensions must conform to the zoning bylaw for the parcel's zone, under the Burnaby Subdivision Control and Development Servicing Bylaw (renamed from the Burnaby Subdivision Control Bylaw 1971 by Amendment Bylaw No. 14667, 2024; the City's servicing updates took effect September 30, 2024). Required works and services include highways and lanes, water distribution connected to the City system, sewage collection and drainage collection, built to the City's Engineering Design Criteria Manual standards; the City's Improvements Guide addresses servicing for developments with four or more primary dwelling units on a residential lot. The base bylaw's own number is UNCONFIRMED on a primary source read for this file, so no number is asserted for it — the process above is what is documented. https://pub-burnaby.escribemeetings.com/filestream.ashx?DocumentId=77052 and https://www.burnaby.ca/sites/default/files/acquiadam/2025-08/Servicing-Improvements-Guide.pdf
- **Hazards and campus-edge diligence:** no area-wide hazard designation for this sub-area's residential streets was confirmed in the sources read; parcel-level hazard status is a property-specific check. Fraser Health's July 2025 construction notice covered paving and repainting on Kincaid Street with single-lane alternating traffic and a temporarily relocated bus stop — confirm current construction effects street by street near the campus. https://www.burnaby.ca/explore-outdoors/parks/deer-lake-park and https://www.fraserhealth.ca/news/2025/Jul/Burnaby-Hospital-redevelopment-project-Construction-notice-July-23
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. Check the parcel on ALC mapping before applying any of this. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no position for this sub-area is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Burnaby Hospital, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **Burnaby property taxes and utilities:** 2026 property taxes were due Friday, July 3, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding current taxes (and on an unclaimed home owner grant) after the July due date and a further 5% after the September due date; Council approved a 2.9% property tax increase in the 2026–2030 Financial Plan, about $61 for the average home. The 2026 detached single-family water charge is $652.33 (Section 2). Area-specific levy notes for this sub-area: UNVERIFIED. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/property-taxes/fees-charges-and-penalties

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot near Burnaby Hospital?** No verified one — there is no standing bare-land market here. Land trades as fee-simple house lots on the established streets; no named bare-land subdivision, bare-land strata plan or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What can a house lot here become under the new zoning?** Under the R1 SSMUH district in Zoning Bylaw 2026 No. 14815: 3 units on lots up to 280 m², 4 units on larger lots, and 6 units on lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent-transit stop — and lots with two or more primary units may be stratified. Confirm the parcel's zone and the 400 m test before pricing that potential in. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/projects/zoning-bylaw-rewrite
- **Q: Are these lots serviced?** The established streets sit inside Burnaby's municipal water/sewer system on the City's utility wording (2026 detached water charge $652.33; sewer as a parcel tax on the tax notice for single-family homes) — confirm the specific parcel's connections and any cross-connection device charge with the City. https://www.burnaby.ca/services-and-payments/utility-fees-and-charges/residential-utility-fees
- **Q: Is any of this land in the ALR?** No ALR parcel in the sub-area is confirmed in the research; ALR status is a parcel check through the Agricultural Land Commission, UNVERIFIED lot by lot here. If a parcel were in the ALR, subdivision would need ALC approval and residences could not be stratified. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Does the hospital redevelopment help or hurt a lot purchase?** Both, on the record: the pavilion opened in March 2026 and the Province committed $210 million on September 21, 2026 to emergency and surgical expansion, with cancer care and an acute-care tower to follow — long-term employment support, and years of Kincaid Street construction to disclose. https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2024-2028/2026INF0056-001108.pdf
- **Q: Which plan governs a south-side lot — Cascade Heights or the new OCP?** Both are read, but the Burnaby 2050 OCP (adopted December 9, 2025) takes precedence over older community-plan land use maps where they conflict; the Cascade Heights Community Plan was adopted in March 2025. https://www.burnaby.ca/our-city/news/2025-12-11/city-burnaby-adopts-burnaby-2050-official-community-plan
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Does a lot purchase qualify for the first-time buyer GST rebate?** No — the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) applies to new housing, not bare land itself. It can matter later for the new home built on the lot, within its own rules. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot here?** By exact address only — the one settled locator run in this file (4861 Canada Way → Douglas Road Elementary → Burnaby Central Secondary) does not transfer to other streets. Run the address in the SD41 locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/041
- **Q: How quiet is this market, really?** Very: 1 detached sale on 13 listings (8% ratio) and no attached sales in September 2026 — price a lot or teardown off wider central-Burnaby comparables, not the single local sale. Source: SnapStats GVR September 2026 report (research file).
