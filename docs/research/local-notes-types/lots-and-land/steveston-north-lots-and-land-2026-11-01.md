## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- Steveston North is the City of Richmond's residential district in the city's south-west, north of the historic Steveston village and south of Blundell Road. The board sub-area name is "Steveston North" (GVR listing data); the City does not use "Steveston North" as a planning name — the district spans the northern part of the Steveston planning area (Steveston Area Plan, Schedule 2.4 of the Official Community Plan) and edges into the City's Seafair planning area to the north. The board's exact boundary is UNVERIFIED; in listing practice the split from Steveston South is generally drawn at Steveston Highway. Nearby areas: Steveston South and Steveston Village (south), Seafair and Boyd Park / West Richmond (north), London/Steveston (east). https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Planning framework — Richmond 2041 OCP, Bylaw 9000 + Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4):** the district spans the northern part of the Steveston planning area governed by the Steveston Area Plan (Schedule 2.4 of the OCP), edging into the Seafair planning area to the north. Confirm which plan area a specific lot sits in before relying on plan policy. https://www.richmond.ca/business-development/planning/heritage/village-conservation.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Zoning — Richmond Zoning Bylaw 8500, RS1/RS2 (Section 8.1):** minimum lot areas by subzone (RS1/B 360.0 m²; RS1/E 550.0 m²; RS1/F 828.0 m²; RS1/K 315.0 m²; 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones); a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **SSMUH / added units, as the City states them:** the City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 small-scale multi-unit minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots. Verify the lot's subzone minimums in Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1.8 before promising redevelopment value. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Subdivision + servicing:** Richmond's subdivision/servicing instrument is the Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011), with works and services cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (2011) — verified ledger position for Richmond, confirmed on the City's bylaw list. Parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Flood / dike diligence — Bylaw 8204:** the Flood Plain Designation and Protection Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels across Richmond; the district sits behind the 1:500-design perimeter dike system described in Section 2. https://www.Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/Bylaw_8204_0410201225280.pdf
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Steveston North ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no flat line is asserted for this area. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors (inputs + tier math): $500,000 → $8,000; $700,000 → $12,000; $1,000,000 → $18,000; $2,000,000 → $38,000; $3,500,000 → $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier over $3M. First-Time Home Buyers' Program: first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less (maximum saving $8,000), partial $835,000–$860,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Richmond property taxes:** due once a year on the first business day of July — Thursday, July 2, 2026 — with a 5% penalty on any unpaid balance after that date and a further 5% penalty on balances still outstanding after September 1, 2026. https://richmond.ca/city-hall/finance/rates/duedates-taxes.htm
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY and does NOT apply in Richmond. No Richmond-specific speculation/vacancy position is documented in the research, and the federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for a Steveston North lot is UNCONFIRMED in this file — not conformed from other files.

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Can I buy a vacant lot in Steveston North?** No verified bare-land offering exists — the district is built detached housing. Land trades as a house on its RS1/RS2 lot, carrying suite and SSMUH potential. (Section 2.)
- **Q: How small can a lot here be?** By subzone under Bylaw 8500 Section 8.1: minimum lot areas include RS1/K 315.0 m², RS1/B 360.0 m², RS1/E 550.0 m² and RS1/F 828.0 m², with a 24.0 m minimum depth in most subzones — the lot's own subzone governs, so confirm it before pricing a split. https://Richmond.ca/__shared/assets/RS1-RS2-RS324187.pdf
- **Q: Can I add units to a house lot?** The City allows at least one secondary suite and/or accessory dwelling unit per lot, with Bill 44 minimums of 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less and 4 units on larger lots; a secondary suite is a permitted secondary use in RS1/RS2. https://RICHMOND.CA/business-development/planning/housing-legislation.htm
- **Q: Which bylaws govern subdividing a Richmond lot?** Subdivision and Development Bylaw 8751 (2011), with works and services cost recovery under Bylaw 8752 (2011); parcel-level standards are confirmed with the City. https://RICHMOND.CA/city-hall/bylaws/alpha.htm
- **Q: What flood diligence applies to a lot here?** The district is in the Fraser River floodplain behind a 1:500-design dike system (dikes being raised from 3.5 m to 4.7 m), and Bylaw 8204 sets dike setbacks and flood construction levels for new construction — confirm the lot's level with the City. https://www.Richmond.ca/services/water-sewer-flood/dikes.htm?PageMode=HTML
- **Q: Is Heron a land purchase?** No — Heron (5651 Steveston Highway) is a completed 2022 freehold-strata townhouse development counted in the townhomes file; a purchase there is a strata purchase (Form B diligence applies), not a lot purchase. (~/workspace/local-notes/townhomes/steveston-north-townhomes-2026-11-01.md)
- **Q: What is Innova30's status?** QUEUED and counted nowhere: a 30-home freehold strata townhouse complex at 7580 No. 1 Road whose geography is in conflict — MLS files it under a neighbouring sub-area; the research geography followed here places it in this district. Either way it is built townhouse product, not land. (Townhomes sibling, §2.)
- **Q: Which schools serve a Steveston North address?** By exact address only — the settled locator result (9200 No. 1 Road → Diefenbaker Elementary / Boyd Secondary) is single-address and does not transfer; Hugh Boyd Secondary itself sits at 9200 No. 1 Road and R.M. Grauer Elementary at 4440 Blundell Road. https://sd38.bc.ca/schools/locations-catchments
- **Q: Will GST apply if a developer sells me a newly subdivided lot?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). No such lot offering is verified in Steveston North today. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
