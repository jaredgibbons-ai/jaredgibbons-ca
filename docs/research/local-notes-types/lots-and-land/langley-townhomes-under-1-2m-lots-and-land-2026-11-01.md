## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township frame:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2500. Secondary suites (s.102, as amended July 2025): a suite may be located within a townhouse, rowhouse or manorhome, capped at 120 m² maximum gross floor area, one per principal dwelling unit — the Township's January 2025 secondary-suites guide states the opposite for townhouses/rowhouses; the conflict is reported with dates and not resolved, and the bylaw is the later source. A coach house on fee-simple ground is dealt with under s.102 of the same bylaw. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **Township SSMUH:** Bylaw No. 6020, adopted November 18, 2024 — 3 units on lots of 280 m² or less, 4 units on lots between 280 m² and 4,050 m², lots over 4,050 m² ineligible, and **NO 6-plex tier in the Township**. Strata townhome lots are not the SSMUH shape, and no SSMUH claim is made for any parcel under this slug. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **Township subdivision / servicing:** subdivisions require Approving Officer approval under the Land Title Act; servicing works are determined under the **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — CONFIRMED**. https://www.tol.ca/en/building-development/subdivision.aspx and (~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/langley-lots-land-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md)
- **City frame (kept strictly separate):** Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300, cited by number — its adoption is UNVERIFIED in this program. A City secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, and the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4). City SSMUH Bylaw 3284 (adopted June 17, 2024, per the 55-plus sibling) permits RS1/RS2 lots 4 units, 6 if within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file; ~/workspace/local-notes/55-plus/langley-townhomes-under-1-2m-55-plus-homes-2026-11-01.md)
- **R-CL correction carried in §2:** the "220–355 m²" range that circulated in earlier program research is WRONG — no 355 m² figure exists in s.403; the verified figures are 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **ALR block (applies parcel by parcel, only where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no farm-class claim is asserted for any parcel under this slug. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for a bare-land strata lot, wherever one trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land, worked through the band:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** a $1,200,000 purchase at the band ceiling, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $1,000,000 ($20,000) = **$22,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: What is the median townhome price in Langley?** None is published — the combined Condos & Townhomes median was $611,000 in September 2026, which is condo-weighted context, never "the townhome price." (SnapStats FVR September 2026 report, via the research file)
- **Q: Township or City — which rules apply to my lot?** Whichever municipality the parcel sits in, and the frames are different: Township Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 and SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (no 6-plex tier) versus City Zoning Bylaw 2025, No. 3300 (cited by number, adoption UNVERIFIED) with a City suite capped at the lesser of 100 m² and 40%. Confirm the municipality from the address before applying either. (§3 above)
- **Q: How big can a Willoughby compact lot be?** Under R-CL s.403: minimum lot area 220 m², maximum 464.5 m². The "220–355 m²" range in older summaries is WRONG — no 355 figure exists in s.403. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Can a Langley townhome or lot have a secondary suite or coach house?** Township s.102 (July 2025) permits a suite within a townhouse, capped at 120 m², one per principal unit, and deals with coach houses on fee-simple ground — but the Township's January 2025 guide says the opposite for townhouses, so confirm the specific unit's permits and strata bylaws before valuing income. In the City, the suite cap is the lesser of 100 m² and 40%, with owner-occupancy. (§3 above)
- **Q: What PTT do I pay at the top of this band?** On the §3 worked example — $1,200,000, no exemption — $22,000. A qualifying first-time buyer or newly-built-home position changes that; bare land itself gets no newly-built-home exemption. (§3 above)
- **Q: Does GST apply to a vacant lot here?** At the CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's personal-use land is generally exempt; a developer/business sale or a newly subdivided lot is generally taxable. The FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a bare-land strata lot the same as a fee-simple lot?** No — bare-land strata is strata tenure on the lot itself: Form B, strata fees, bylaws, a CRF and (5+ lots) a five-year depreciation report cycle. A fee-simple lot has none of that structure. (§§2–3 above)
- **Q: Which schools serve a lot I build on?** SD35, by address. The only completed locator run on record is 7888 200 Street → Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary, Peter Ewart Middle School, R.E. Mountain Secondary — one address only; run the exact parcel address before relying on any catchment. (§1 above)
