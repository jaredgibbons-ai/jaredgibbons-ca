## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — current plan only.** Land use now follows the Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan, Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026), and Zoning Bylaw No. 12000. The South Newton plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update and is no longer in effect; development in the area is led by the OCP, and on December 2, 2024 Council endorsed an update to land-use designations for the area. Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. A seller quoting South Newton plan densities is quoting a dead document. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/official-community-plan
- **Zoning.** Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 (R-series, RA at the Colebrook rural edges; R3 minimum subdivision lot area 660 m² as recorded for this plan area — see §2). https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **SSMUH / subdivision potential as the City states it (Jul 8, 2024 framework).** Maximum 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots of at least 281 m² within 400 m of a frequent bus stop; one secondary suite per house; unpermitted existing suites are not legalized by the new rules. Servicing is a condition in practice: properties not connected to community water and sewer are among the exemptions — directly relevant at the Colebrook edge. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Surrey).** Only one per single-family, semi-detached or duplex dwelling unit; not permitted within a coach house or garden suite; habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling unit. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Coach houses and garden suites.** All Surrey residential zones that allow detached houses, duplexes and houseplexes also allow a coach house or garden suite; at least 35 m² and at most 75 m², rising to 120 m² for lots zoned RA, R2, R2-O and R3 where the existing house or duplex is below maximum FAR; one extra parking space is required. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements.** Surrey subdivision proceeds through detailing and a Servicing Agreement defining the off-site works, services and financial securities for final approval. Lot-specific servicing costs, DCC amounts and timelines for a Panorama Ridge parcel: UNCONFIRMED — where a number is unconfirmed, the process is what to follow, not an estimate. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Riparian / creek diligence (the Panorama Ridge land item).** Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries run through the plan area; riparian/setback requirements and any development permit areas can limit building envelopes on creek-side lots — check City mapping and the lot's permit areas before valuing an addition, rebuild or subdivision. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Slope / south-edge diligence.** The research documents the ridge form, Highway 10 at the south edge and the lowlands beyond it: check highway noise exposure and confirm the lot is on the upland side of the floodplain picture before subjects. A parcel-level geotechnical report requirement for a specific Panorama Ridge lot: UNCONFIRMED in the research — obtain the City's requirements for the parcel rather than assuming a slope study is or is not required. (Research file §6.)
- **Short-term rentals (Surrey).** Permitted, owner-only, under licence: only an owner who lives at the property as their primary residence may host and tenants cannot operate one. Licence figure CONFLICT carried openly: $358/yr is the program figure, WITH the City's $350/yr plus fees proposal disclosed — read the City licence page before quoting either. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **BC strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot is the purchase — form only; no Panorama Ridge instance verified).** Form B within 7 days, maximum $35 plus up to 25 cents per page (fees, amounts owed, approved levies, CRF balance, insurance summary); depreciation reports every 5 years for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, May 1, 2023 exemptions). A "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/bylaws-and-rules/age-restriction-bylaws
- **ALR rules (only where a parcel is confirmed ALR — none confirmed in-area).** Residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; SPA s. 239; ALCA s. 21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; principal residence ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **BC Assessment farm class — full three-tier form.** $2,500 (0.8–4 ha); $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha (>4 ha); $10,000 (<0.8 ha); application on or before October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an error pattern and is not used here. https://info.bcassessment.ca/Services-products/property-classes-and-exemptions/farm-land-assessment/farm-classification-in-british-columbia/Apply-for-farm-classification
- **Property transfer tax (PTT) on land.** PTT applies unless an exemption applies: 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. Anchors (inputs + tier math): $700,000 → **$12,000**; $1,000,000 → **$18,000**; $1,100,000 → **$20,000**; $1,200,000 → **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 + $10,000 (further 2% × $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**. First-Time Home Buyers': no PTT on the first $500,000 (maximum $8,000) at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000 — relief turns on a qualifying home, not the lot alone. Newly Built Home Exemption: full relief to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only.** Most individuals' vacant-land sales are exempt (e.g., personal-use land); taxable examples include business capital property, sales in the course of a business, and parcels created by subdividing into more than two parts. Finer points: UNCONFIRMED per lot. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home-on-land programs — stated precisely.** The FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself. New Housing Rebate: 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. Land-plus-build qualification: UNCONFIRMED per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation and vacancy tax.** Surrey IS a designated taxable area; residential owners file the annual declaration and a vacant parcel's position turns on class and exemptions. Vancouver's Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/speculation-vacancy-tax/how-tax-works/taxable-areas
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition — bare land.** No primary text opened in this research lineage for the vacant-land position: UNCONFIRMED — CMHC tool + legal advice. No position asserted.
- **Holding costs / tax dates.** Surrey taxes due July 2, 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2 (city-wide). No Panorama Ridge-specific levy found: UNVERIFIED as an area item. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot I can buy on the ridge?** No bare-land subdivision is verified — the purchase is an existing fee-simple house lot for rebuild/added units. Strata complexes and the Amson Bloc presale are built/presale product, not lots, and the full land treatment sits in the LAND HOME named in the FILE STATUS block. (§2 above.)
- **Q: How much of my creek-side lot can I actually build on?** Riparian setbacks for Archibald Creek headwaters, Sullivan Heights Creek and Hyland Creek tributaries can limit the envelope — get the City's watercourse mapping and permit-area check for the parcel before valuing a rebuild. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Is a Colebrook-edge lot on sewer and water?** UNVERIFIED per lot in the research — and unconnected properties are an SSMUH exemption category. Confirm with the City before counting added units. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: How many units can the lot hold?** 3 under 280 m², 4 over 280 m², up to 6 on eligible 281 m²+ lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop — subject to zone, servicing and any creek/permit-area limits. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Q: What is the minimum lot I can subdivide to?** The research records an R3 minimum subdivision lot area of 660 m² for this plan area (Bylaw 12000, Part 26 Section F) — confirm the parcel's own zone section with the City. https://www.surrey.ca/sites/default/files/bylaws/BYL_Zoning_12000.pdf
- **Q: Can I add a suite or a coach house?** One secondary suite per dwelling unit (at most 40% of the principal unit, never inside a coach house or garden suite), and a coach house or garden suite of 35–75 m² (up to 120 m² on RA, R2, R2-O and R3 lots below maximum FAR). Unpermitted existing suites are not legalized by the new rules. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/residential-building/residential-building-permits/coach-houses-garden-suites
- **Q: Will GST or PTT relief help on a lot purchase?** GST turns on the seller/subdivision history (CRA rules, §3); PTT relief turns on building/occupying a qualifying home; the FTHB GST rebate is for new housing, not bare land. None can be assumed on a lot alone. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What does it cost to hold land here?** Surrey taxes (due July 2; 5% + 5% penalties) plus the speculation and vacancy tax declaration — Surrey is a taxable area. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **Q: Can I trust the South Newton plan densities a seller quotes?** No — the South Newton plan was rescinded by the Surrey 2050 OCP update; the OCP (Bylaw 21900) and Zoning Bylaw 12000 govern. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-use-planning/newton-land-use-plans/south-newton
- **Q: Which schools serve a ridge lot?** Ministry-listed: École Panorama Ridge Secondary (13220 64 Ave), Colebrook Elementary (5404 125a St), McLeod Road Traditional (6325 142 St — the correct name; there is no "McLeod Road Elementary" and no school named "East Newton Elementary" exists), North Ridge Elementary (13460 62 Ave) and W E Kinvig Elementary (13266 70b Ave). A settled SD36 locator result IS documented for one address only — 13220 64 Ave → North Ridge Elementary / École Panorama Ridge Secondary (locator run 2026-11-01) — and transfers to no other address; run any other address in the SD36 School Locator. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/036
