## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Plans and zoning — Surrey:** Surrey 2050 OCP Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted Jun 15, 2026) is current — Bylaw 18020 is never cited as current. Surrey Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 governs; no City land use plan is named for Hazelmere. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **A-1 figures — held UNVERIFIED:** the sibling page's A-1 / Part 10 statements (4 hectares inside the ALR and 2 hectares outside it as minimum lot areas for new A-1 lots; one single-family dwelling which may contain one secondary suite under A-1; RA zoning on some nearby land with a 4,050 m² minimum) were not re-read in Bylaw 12000 in this pass — UNVERIFIED on the bylaw text; the RA 4,050 m² figure is consistent with the City's SSMUH zone table, which carries RA lots at over 4,050 m². Confirm in the current consolidated bylaw before quoting for a parcel. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html and https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **SSMUH caution for this ground — Surrey (adopted Jul 8, 2024):** Surrey's SSMUH allowances apply to eligible residential-zoned properties under 4,050 m² inside the Urban Containment Boundary, with exemptions including lands not connected to community water and sewer — most Hazelmere acreage ground is outside that shape, so the standard 3–4 unit framing must not be applied to Hazelmere lots without a parcel check. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Suites — Surrey:** a secondary suite's habitable floor area may not exceed 40% of the principal dwelling's habitable floor area under Bylaw 12000 (one per unit, not a separate strata lot); a "90 m²" figure is NOT the current Surrey cap. On ALR land a suite is permitted only inside the principal residence (ALR block below). https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Subdivision and servicing — Surrey:** servicing under Surrey Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 8830 (1986, verified on City pages). Streamside setbacks on the Little Campbell River and its tributaries sit in Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw 12000 and vary by stream class — no single riparian figure is ever stated. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **ALR block (ALR ground exists in this watershed — parcel status is parcel-specific and UNVERIFIED for any specific parcel here):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); local governments may further restrict residences through zoning. Farm class three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, and no such line is asserted for this slug because its research does not document one. A separate coach house on an A-1 or ALR acreage is UNVERIFIED parcel-by-parcel. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF since Nov 1, 2023; rental bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions). A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Short-term rentals — Surrey:** renting by the owner for under 90 consecutive days per patron; only the owner may host and must live at the property as their primary residence; a business licence at $358 per year is required (the City's 2024 corporate report proposed $350 + fees — $358 is carried with the $350 conflict disclosed). https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/short-term-rentals
- **Taxes:** City of Surrey property taxes are due July 2, with a 5% penalty if not paid by midnight July 2 and a further 5% after September 2. Whether a specific Hazelmere property carries farm-class taxation is a BC Assessment / parcel question: UNVERIFIED here. https://www.surrey.ca/services-payments/property-taxes/paying-your-property-taxes
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on a $1,500,000 land purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$1,500,000 ($26,000) = **$28,000**. Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. (At $3.5M the total is $93,000 INCLUDING the further 2% tier — a townhomes sibling carries $78,000 for $3.5M; OPEN reconciliation item, flagged wherever met.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). ## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The old name explains the ground (City plan document).** Hall's Prairie became Hazelmere after Henry Thrift's homestead rail station, and the City's plan document says the district kept its rural, agricultural character for a century — the acreage fabric is the historic fabric, not leftover urban lots. https://www.Surrey.ca/sites/default/files/media/documents/SouthCampbellHeightsLocalAreaPlan.pdf
- **Heritage sits on the acreage streets (guide page, site copy attributed to City heritage pages).** Hazelmere United Church, 1614 184 Street, built 1903 in Carpenter Gothic style; Hazelmere Cemetery, 19184 16 Avenue, established when the Hall's Prairie Cemetery Co. incorporated in 1908; and the Hall's Prairie South Building at 18035 8 Avenue, built about 1927 on a site where the first school was built in 1884 — all three on the City's heritage site list per the guide page. https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
- **The river has no dykes here (guide page, site copy).** Unlike the Nicomekl lowlands, the Little Campbell River watershed has no dykes and its floodplain follows the river channel closely — a parcel's setback position is read off Part 7A by stream class, never a single figure (§3). https://jaredgibbons.ca/hazelmere-guide.html
