## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Township frame (Township parcels only):** Zoning Bylaw 2500 — secondary suites under s.102 (as amended July 2025) capped at 120 m², one per principal unit, ALR suites must comply with ALC regulations; a coach house under s.102 is a fee-simple second-storey garage unit, never strata. **SSMUH Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024): NO 6-plex tier** — 3 units on lots ≤280 m², 4 units on lots 280–4,050 m², subject to eligibility (Urban Containment Boundary, municipal water/sewer, no heritage designation, not in a Transit Oriented Area). **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 CONFIRMED.** **R-CL discipline:** s.403 R-CL = 220 m² minimum / 464.5 m² maximum lot area — no "355 m²" figure exists in the bylaw and it is never carried. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file; program frames for this workstream)
- **City frame (City parcels only — kept strictly separate):** Zoning Bylaw 2025 No. 3300 **by number, adoption UNVERIFIED**. A secondary suite may not exceed the lesser of 100 m² and 40% of the building's gross floor area, the registered owner must reside on the lot (s.2.4), and a carriage/garden ground-floor unit is capped at 100 m². City SSMUH Bylaw No. 3284, adopted June 17, 2024, permits RS1/RS2 lots "4; 6 if within 400 m of frequent bus stop," with Plex-Homes of 2–4 units, or 5–6 near frequent bus stops. City short-term rental status was NOT VERIFIED on any City page in the rules-gaps pass — no City STR rule is stated. (~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/rules-gaps-2026-11-01.md, via the research file)
- **ALR block (rural Langley parcels only, where the parcel is in the reserve):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (wherever a bare-land strata lot trades):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022 (short-term may still be restricted); only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. This is the tenure frame behind the band's strata product generally (the condos sibling applies the same void-rental line to the City buildings' directory records). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked example, inputs + tier math stated:** an $800,000 parcel, fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 (1% × $200,000) + 2% × $600,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000**; a fully-qualifying first-time buyer on the same parcel pays the first-time-buyer figure of **$6,000** on the program's anchor table. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. (Separately, GST at 5% applies to new built condos, not resales — the condos sibling's frame for this band's new product.) https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
