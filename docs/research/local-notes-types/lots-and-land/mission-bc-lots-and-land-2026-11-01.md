## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: City of Mission Official Community Plan Bylaw 6405-2026 — ADOPTED APRIL 21, 2026 (standing correction).** The research records the process in its own terms: Bylaw 6405-2026 was put to a public hearing on April 7, 2026 at City Hall, 8645 Stave Lake Street, to replace and repeal OCP Bylaw 6350-2025; the City states the new bylaw is substantively the same OCP — vision, land use framework, mapping and policy direction unchanged — proposed to address a procedural matter and to formally incorporate a Regional Context Statement as required under the Local Government Act. Whether it had since been adopted was UNVERIFIED in the research pass; the standing correction above governs. **No "June 1, 2026" defect is asserted for this page** — this slug's research does not document that date in its own copy. https://www.mission.ca/council-government/news/notice-public-hearing-april-7-2026
- **Zoning: City of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020** (cited in the City's own amending bylaws as "District of Mission Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020"). Parcel zone, minimum lot area and permitted uses are read from the consolidated bylaw per parcel; no city-wide lot-size table is documented in this research — UNVERIFIED as a table here. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **SSMUH / added units (Mission frame):** the City incorporated the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation into Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020 on **July 17, 2024** (Bylaw 6299-2024-5949(163)); as part of that amendment the Urban Residential 930 Secondary Dwelling Zone (R930s) was removed and merged with R930. Specific unit counts per lot size for Mission are **UNCONFIRMED in this research — Surrey's 3/4/6-unit figures are NOT carried into Mission**. Added-unit permission is not permission to subdivide. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/information-package-zoning-bylaw-housekeeping-amendments
- **Secondary suites (Mission's own rule):** all secondary suites in Mission must be **registered with the City**; a suite is a self-contained living space in a single family home forming part of the same real estate entity; suites are **not permitted in townhouses or rowhomes**; a new suite goes through the building permit process, and an unregistered suite can trigger a bylaw investigation and a possible decommission permit. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Subdivision / servicing:** **Development and Subdivision Control Bylaw No. 5650-2017 (DSCB 5650-2017)** — carried here only from a City of Mission source found in an earlier batch (and the bylaw title appears on the City's own site-assessment document in this pass), **not as a research-file fact for this slug**; its schedules govern site assessment for rezoning, development permit and subdivision applications. Process, not a fee/standard table, is documented here — specific servicing standards per parcel are UNCONFIRMED in this research. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/site-assessment-development
- **Development charges (context for a subdivision/build):** the City's Development Cost Charge Bylaw 6348-2025 was adopted May 5, 2025, and DCCs apply to every person who obtains approval of a subdivision (among other triggers); the Amenity Cost Charge Bylaw 6349-2025 was adopted the same day, payable at subdivision approval or building permit issuance. Amounts are in the bylaws — none is quoted here. https://www.mission.ca/planning/development-amenity-cost-charges
- **ALR block (where ALR ground exists):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences **cannot be stratified** (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tier — $2,500 on 0.8–4 ha / $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha / $10,000 under 0.8 ha, applications by Oct 31 — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted only where a file's own research documents it (none does here). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Bare-land strata block (for any bare-land strata lot, wherever one surfaces):** Form B (SPA s.59) within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page, with rules, budget and most recent depreciation report attached; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since Jul 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since Nov 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since Nov 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01 + May 1, 2023 exemptions) — a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **PTT on land:** 1% of fair market value up to and including $200,000, 2% of the portion over $200,000 up to and including $2,000,000, 3% above $2,000,000, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000. First-time buyers: exempt on the first $500,000 at $835,000 or less, partial to $860,000. Newly Built Home Exemption: full to $1,100,000, eliminated at $1,150,000 — a new HOME, not bare land. **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (anchors):** $500,000 fully residential, no exemption = $2,000 + 2% × $300,000 = **$8,000**; $800,000 = $2,000 + 2% × $600,000 = **$14,000**; $1,000,000 = **$18,000**. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (e.g. land kept for personal use); taxable cases include land used primarily in a business, land sold in the course of a business, and a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts — developer/newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE. Anything finer for a specific parcel: UNCONFIRMED — CPA per transaction. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Rebates attach to housing, not bare land:** FTHB GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent **March 13, 2026** per CRA; 100% of GST to $1M, max $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031) — a qualifying new HOME, not raw land; New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 underneath. HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Mission property taxes:** due July 2, with a **FLAT 10% penalty** added to any current balances outstanding (including unclaimed home owner grants) on July 3, 2026 — Community Charter ss.233–234 + Municipal Tax Regulation, B.C. Reg. 426/2003, s.3; Mission has NO s.235 alternative-scheme bylaw. This is **not** Surrey's two-step 5% + 5%, and **not** the FVRD rural 5% + 5% regime. https://www.mission.ca/services/property-taxes and https://www.mission.ca/taxes
- **Floodplain / hazard diligence:** no Mission floodplain zone or figure is documented in this slug's research — none is imported from another file. Parcel-level hazard, riparian and geotechnical items are read from the City's site-assessment process under DSCB 5650-2017 (Schedule J asks, among other things, about septic systems, ditching/ponds and vegetation) per application. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/site-assessment-development

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a bare-land subdivision selling lots in Mission right now?** None was verified on an official or developer source in this research. What trades is the existing fabric — fee-simple house and acreage lots and individual vacant parcels — checked parcel by parcel. (§2 above)
- **Q: Is Montgomery a lot project?** No. Montgomery Condos is a presale apartment project (85 units, Redekop Faye, developer address 7375 Horne Street) documented in the condos file — no lots are offered or counted from it here. https://montgomerycondos.ca/?option=com_content&amp%3Btask=view&amp%3Bid=17&amp%3BItemid=33
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Mission acreage?** Subdivision runs through the City under DSCB 5650-2017 and the zone minimums in Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, with DCCs payable at subdivision approval under Bylaw 6348-2025; if the parcel is in the ALR, ALC approval is also required. No parcel's subdivision potential is stated without its zone and ALR status. (§3 above)
- **Q: Can I add units instead of subdividing?** Mission incorporated SSMUH into its zoning on July 17, 2024, but the specific unit count for a given lot is UNCONFIRMED in this research — do not use Surrey's numbers in Mission — and added units do not create new lots. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a suite on a Mission lot legal?** Only if registered: every Mission suite must be registered with the City, suites sit in single family homes only (never townhouses/rowhomes), and an unregistered suite can be investigated and may need a decommission permit. https://www.mission.ca/business-building/building-development/land-development/secondary-suites-program
- **Q: My lot has a Mission mailing address — do I pay City taxes?** Not necessarily: Hatzic splits three ways (Bench = City; Prairie = FVRD Area F; Island = Area G) and tax follows the jurisdiction, not the mailing address. Inside the City the penalty is a flat 10% after July 2; the rural 5% + 5% regime applies only on FVRD ground. (§1, §3 above)
- **Q: What tax do I pay buying land here?** PTT on the general tiers — $800,000 fully residential with no exemption = $14,000 (see §3). GST depends on the seller and the lot's history at the CRA GI-003 level — most individual sales of personal-use vacant land are exempt; developer/subdivided lots are generally taxable. Confirm with a CPA per transaction. (§3 above)
- **Q: Is a strata lot the same as a freehold lot?** No. A bare-land strata lot is strata tenure — Form B, fees and bylaws apply to the lot itself. No bare-land strata plan city-wide was verified here; tenure is read off title per parcel. (§2, §3 above)
- **Q: How did Mission sell in September 2026?** Detached: 231 listings, 28 solds, $814,000 median, 29 DOM; "Condos & Townhomes": 122 listings, 10 solds, $430,000 median, 61 DOM. Those are all-property community figures; SnapStats publishes no land category. Source: SnapStats FVR September 2026 report.
- **Q: Which school will a home built on my lot feed into?** UNVERIFIED for any address here — SD75 catchments are address-based, the §1 schools are Ministry-listed names by address only, and no locator run for Mission is on record. Run the exact address with SD75 before relying on a school — and note "Mission Elementary School" does not exist in the Ministry file. (§1 above)
