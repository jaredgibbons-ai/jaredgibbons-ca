## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040"** (Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, per the land home's research), covering the whole city including this sub-area, plus the **North Lougheed Area Plan, adopted by OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3060, 2026** (~50 ha, gradual development, further approvals required). https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/official-community-plan-0 and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/homes-development/zoning-land-use/north-lougheed-area-plan
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** A-1 General Agricultural — minimum lot area for subdivision 8 ha, one farm house per lot to a maximum gross floor area of 500 sq m; A-2 Large Lot Agricultural — minimum 16 ha; RR-2 Large Lot Rural Residential — minimum 4 ha; RR-3 — minimum 2 ha; RR-4 — minimum 1 ha (RR zones are for land outside the Agricultural Land Reserve); RS Large Lot Residential — minimum 0.2 ha. Zoning requires residential and agricultural buildings to be sited 15 m from watercourses, key ditches and constructed ditches in the A-1 and A-2 zones. Any specific parcel's zone and ALR status: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED here. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011 - Consolidated Version_0.pdf
- **Second dwellings:** a garden suite is permitted in the A-1, A-2, RR-1 to RR-5 and RS zones, one per lot, 33 sq m to 90 sq m, and a property may have a garden suite or a secondary suite, not both. On ALR land, the ALC allows one additional detached residence without an application where there is one existing residence when construction begins, to a maximum of 90 sq m on properties 40 ha or less. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf and https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
