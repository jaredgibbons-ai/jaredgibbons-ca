## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Property transfer tax — the band's tier math, worked in full:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. A purchase under $1.8M is taxed entirely in the first two tiers (the 3% tier starts above the band's ceiling). Worked anchors: **$700,000 → $12,000** ($2,000 + 2% × $500,000); **$1,000,000 → $18,000** ($2,000 + 2% × $800,000); **$1,100,000 → $20,000**; **$1,200,000 → $22,000**; **$1,500,000 → $28,000**; **$1,800,000 (the band ceiling) → $34,000** ($2,000 + 2% × $1,600,000); $2,000,000 → $38,000 and $3,500,000 → $93,000 (including the further 2% tier) are shown only to mark where the higher tiers begin — both sit above this band. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **Plan, zoning and added units:** OCP Bylaw 6405-2026 (adopted April 21, 2026 — any source stating "June 1, 2026" is WRONG; this band's research does not document that wrong date on this page, so it is carried as the standing correction, not asserted as a defect of this page, §5); zoning under Zoning Bylaw 5949-2020, with parcel-level minimum lot sizes set out in the bylaw itself (rural RU16 at a 1.6 ha minimum in City notices). The City's SSMUH page states that zones previously restricted to single family or duplex dwellings are subject to the provincial small-scale multi-unit housing legislation the City has adopted, and the City's planning pages set out how secondary suites and detached accessory units are handled by zone. https://www.mission.ca/media/file/public-hearing-notice-april-7-2026 and https://www.mission.ca/business-building/land-development-planning and https://www.mission.ca/planning/small-scale-multi-unit-housing-ssmuh
- **ALR block — applies to the band's rural-edge parcels:** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval, and ALR residences cannot be stratified — depositing a strata plan causes a subdivision (ALC Information Bulletin 09, s. 6; Strata Property Act, s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act, s. 21(1)). Residence caps: one principal residence per parcel, maximum gross floor area 500 m²; a secondary suite is permitted **only inside** the principal residence; one additional residence capped at 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less and 186 m² on parcels over 40 ha. Farm class (BC Assessment) runs in the full three-tier form: $2,500 of gross farm income at 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 plus 5% of the actual value of the area over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha — applications by October 31. A flat "$2,500+" statement is an ERROR; none is documented on this band's page in its research, so no page defect is asserted on that point. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/ and https://info.bcassessment.ca/about/Shared%20Documents/Classification_of_Land_as_a_Farm_Regulation.pdf
- **BC strata block — governs any strata parcel in the band, including a bare-land strata lot if one appears:** Form B (SPA s. 59) states the lot's fees, amounts owing, approved special levies, CRF balance and insurance summary; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; sales of newly subdivided lots by a developer/business are generally taxable. Anything finer for a specific band parcel is UNCONFIRMED. Separately, GST on a **new home** in the band (e.g., a developer sale) is a housing question, not a land question — see the programs note below. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, **NOT bare land**, so it does not attach to a vacant-lot purchase in this band. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
