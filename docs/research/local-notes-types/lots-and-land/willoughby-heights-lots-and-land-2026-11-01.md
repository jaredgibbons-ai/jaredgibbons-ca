## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**What land actually trades here is the compact fee-simple lot fabric itself — Willoughby is a newly built-out urban community whose lots were created by subdivision under the R-CL pattern and the neighbourhood plans, and which now trade as individual resale lots (almost always with the house on them). No named bare-land subdivision currently selling vacant lots, and no named bare-land strata community, was verified. The bylaw expressly contemplates bareland strata lots in R-CL (s.403.7, below) — a permitted form, with no named Willoughby example verified. No vacant-lot count or lot price is stated.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form, and the area's defining lot pattern

- **R-CL compact lots (Zoning Bylaw No. 2500, s.403):** the Residential Compact Lot Zone is Willoughby's compact-lot pattern, covering both front-loaded lots and rear-loaded lots with rear lane access. Under s.403.7, lots run from a **minimum lot area of 220 m² to a maximum lot area of 464.5 m²** (5% of lots not fronting a street/cul-de-sac radius may exceed the maximum), with minimum lot frontage of 11 m for front-loaded lots and 8.2 m for rear-loaded lots, minimum corner frontage 13.5 m / 12.5 m, minimum lot depth 18 m / 22 m, and panhandle lots not permitted. Verify which pattern a lot is — front-loaded vs rear-loaded changes garage, parking and lane expectations. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Tenure of the detached streets:** freehold (fee-simple) — detached streets are freehold; condos and townhomes are strata under the Strata Property Act. A fee-simple lot carries NO strata, NO Form B and NO strata fees. The one named fee-simple attached exception, the Westbrooke freehold row homes by Foxridge Homes BC (Qualico) — 21 buildings / 56 units in the builder's completion report, tenure stated expressly as freehold with no strata fees or corporation — is documented and counted in the townhomes sibling (FILE STATUS block), not here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/operating-a-strata/repairs-and-maintenance/paying-for-repair-and-maintenance and http://www.qualico.com/news/foxridge-homes-bc-completes-first-phase-of-freehold-row-homes-in-westbrooke-community
- **Servicing:** Willoughby is an urban growth area inside the Urban Containment Boundary, built out under subdivision servicing standards — all lots created by subdivision in the residential zones must comply with the Township's Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, as amended (stated in Zoning Bylaw ss.401.8, 403.7 and 403.9). No well/septic position is documented for Willoughby lots in the research, and none is asserted — this is serviced urban ground as the bylaws frame it; a specific lot's connections are confirmed with the Township. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **ALR / flood:** Willoughby is an urban growth area inside the Urban Containment Boundary; ALR land inside Willoughby is NOT confirmed in the research, and a floodplain designation for specific Willoughby parcels is not confirmed — both are parcel-level checks, and no ALR split is stated. Yorkson Creek runs through the east side, and Township materials flag hazards around greenways and creeks in the plan area — check creek/greenway setbacks on east-side lots. https://www.tol.ca/en/connect/resources/get-involved/engage-tol/WILL-documents/WILL-Phase-1-BP-paper-pages---December-2018.pdf

### Bare-land strata — a permitted form with no named example verified

- **The form exists in the bylaw:** s.403.7 governs "all fee simple **and bareland strata** lots created by subdivision" in R-CL — bare-land strata subdivision is a contemplated form on Willoughby's compact lots, on the same 220–464.5 m² lot table. Stated plainly, wherever a bare-land strata lot does exist: a bare-land strata lot IS strata tenure — the buyer owns a strata lot, pays strata fees, is bound by the strata bylaws, and diligence runs through the Form B (Section 3). https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Named Willoughby bare-land strata community: none verified.** No complex name with an address was confirmed in the research or the siblings: none found. The area's 8 verified townhome complexes (7 strata + Westbrooke fee-simple) are built housing counted in the townhomes file — none is documented as a bare-land strata.

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning + plan frame:** Township of Langley Zoning Bylaw No. 2500 (R-CL s.403 in Sections 1–2); Willoughby Community Plan = Bylaw 1836 (1979), now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); Plan Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 at first/second reading May 25, 2026 only — never upgrade reading-stage bylaws, and live-page tiles presenting June 15, 2026 repeals by 6202/6203 as done are UNVERIFIED. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **SSMUH (Township): Bylaw 6020, adopted Nov 18, 2024 — NO 6-plex tier exists in it.** (The bylaw's amendments are visible in the Zoning Bylaw text itself, tagged "#6020 18/11/24" through ss.401–404.) Secondary suite cap 120 m². The Township's provincial SSMUH rules apply on eligible Willoughby lots as stated in the Township-wide SSMUH material. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx and https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Coach houses — fee-simple, never strata, with a live conflict flagged:** a coach house is a fee-simple second unit on a detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — never strata, never a townhouse complex. **Live conflict, flagged not resolved:** s.102 (July 2025) permits townhouse suites at 120 m² vs the Township's January 2025 guide saying not permitted. Willoughby's plans carry rear-lane (coach) houses as an alternate housing form on compact R-CL lots. (Townhomes sibling, on disk; Zoning Bylaw s.102.)
- **Subdivision + servicing requirements:** all lots created by subdivision in the residential zones must comply with the **Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382, as amended**, plus the zone table (for R-CL: s.403.7 — 220 m² min / 464.5 m² max lot area, frontage/depth minimums, no panhandles). Exterior Design Control Agreements are secured at subdivision for compact-lot developments as a condition of rezoning (s.403.11). https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Strata block (the area's strata stock, and any bare-land strata lot in full):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (directory "rentals not allowed" lines for Willow Park Estates and Highgate Terrace are void for long-term rentals on exactly this ground); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss.123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions. A listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. **Highgate Terrace (19645 64 Avenue) is age-restricted with the exact age UNCONFIRMED — never state it as 55+.** https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/highgate-terrace-19645-64-avenue
- **ALR rules:** no ALR ground inside Willoughby is confirmed in the research (Section 2), so no ALR rule is applied to a Willoughby parcel here. Standing rules, for completeness only where ALR ground were ever confirmed on a Langley parcel: ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)); subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; residence caps principal ≤500 m², suite within principal only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha); farm class three-tier ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **PTT on land:** general tiers 1% / 2% / 3% + further 2% on the residential portion above $3,000,000; first-time buyer and newly built home exemptions are home-purchase exemptions as BC states them — bare-land eligibility for the newly built home exemption: UNCONFIRMED here. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt. This matters directly for any newly subdivided R-CL lot sold by a developer vs a resale lot from an individual. Anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: How big is a Willoughby lot, really?** Under R-CL s.403.7: minimum lot area 220 m², maximum 464.5 m², frontage at least 11 m front-loaded or 8.2 m rear-loaded, depth at least 18 m / 22 m, no panhandles. Those are the subdivision table figures for lots created in the zone — measure the actual lot against title. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: Front-loaded or rear-loaded — why does it matter?** It changes where the garage and parking sit, whether there is a lane, and the frontage/depth minimums that applied when the lot was created (11 m / 18 m vs 8.2 m / 22 m). Price lane access and parking accordingly. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Is a coach house a strata unit I can buy separately?** No. A coach house is a fee-simple second unit on the detached lot under Zoning Bylaw 2500 s.102 — it is part of the lot's ownership, never strata and never separately titled as a complex. Note the flagged live conflict on townhouse suites (s.102 July 2025 vs the Township's January 2025 guide) before relying on suite potential. (Section 3.)
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Willoughby lot?** Only against the zone table plus the Subdivision and Development Servicing Bylaw 2019 No. 5382 — and on R-CL lots of 220–464.5 m² there is no room left to split. No subdivision promise can be made from this file. https://www.tol.ca/en/services/resources/bylaw-services/bylaws/Zoning-Bylaws/Section-400-Residential-(R,-MH,-FH-and-CRE)-Zones.pdf
- **Q: How many units can I put on a detached lot?** Township SSMUH is Bylaw 6020 (adopted Nov 18, 2024), and NO 6-plex tier exists in it; the secondary suite cap is 120 m². Check the lot's eligibility in the Township's SSMUH material. https://www.tol.ca/en/the-township/small-scale-multi-unit-housing.aspx
- **Q: Did the Willoughby plan update already change what can be built beside me?** Not yet on the verified record: the Community Plan is Bylaw 1836, now Schedule 6 to Bylaw 6200 (adopted Dec 1, 2025); Update Bylaws 6201/6202/6203 are at first/second reading (May 25, 2026) with a public hearing directed June 15, 2026. Treat any page presenting the repeals as done as UNVERIFIED. (FILE STATUS block.)
- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or a bare-land strata I can buy in Willoughby?** None verified: no named vacant-lot subdivision and no named bare-land strata community was confirmed, even though s.403.7 expressly contemplates bareland strata lots in R-CL. What trades is the built lot fabric. (Section 2.)
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What are holding costs on a Willoughby lot?** Township property taxes on the parcel, plus strata fees ONLY if the lot is a strata lot (the area's detached R-CL lots are fee-simple — no strata fees), plus servicing/utility charges as the Township bills them. No tax due-date or rate figure is documented in this file's research — none is stated.
- **Q: Which schools serve a Willoughby lot?** Run the exact address in the SD35 locator — the documented chain for 7888 200 Street is Donna Gabriel Robins Elementary → Peter Ewart Middle → R.E. Mountain Secondary, and other addresses may differ. https://www.sd35.bc.ca/school-locator
