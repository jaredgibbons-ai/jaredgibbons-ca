## 1. STREETS AND BOUNDARIES

- The "area" here is the whole City of Delta — Ladner, Tsawwassen and North Delta — as a planning jurisdiction, not a neighbourhood. The page covers Delta's Official Community Plan, Zoning Bylaw and Small-Scale Multi-Unit Housing (Bill 44) rules for homeowners, builders and acreage buyers across all three communities, with a FAQ section and links out to Delta lot, land and farm listing pages; no listing feed appeared in the page text. https://jaredgibbons.ca/deltalandplans.html
- The governing plan: Delta's current Official Community Plan is Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024 — Delta was the first municipality in the region to complete its OCP review and update. In Delta's own framing, an OCP describes where and what type of housing can be added across the city and contains the long-term objectives and policies guiding land use, development, services, amenities and infrastructure decisions. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- Plan structure: the OCP's Land Use Plan is s. 1.5, and Map 2 is the Land Use Plan for Delta; designations are set out in s. 1.5.1 and applied on Map 2 — including Agriculture (A), whose stated purpose is to maintain agricultural lands. The plan's structure is the municipal-wide plan (s. 1.5, s. 2.0 Policies, Appendix B Development Permit Areas and Guidelines, Appendix D Maps), not a set of community area-plan schedules. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf

## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**Topic position, stated at the top: this page carries no land stock of its own — no lot roster, no named bare-land subdivision for sale, no bare-land strata offering and no presale LOT offering is documented in this file, because the topic is the plan framework itself. What this section documents is what land the Delta plans govern and the lot forms those plans create: fee-simple RS/RD lots as the default, A-zone farm parcels under the Agriculture (A) designation, and the SSMUH infill forms the 2024 framework enables. Named developments, assemblies and proposals are not inventory and none is counted here.**

- **Fee-simple lots — the default form the plans create.** The RS/RD subdivision minimums in Zoning Bylaw No. 7600 (number CONTESTED, §3) run: RS1, RS2, RS3, RD2 and RD3 at 4,000 m²; RS4 at 610 m²; RS5 at 550 m² under the Single Family Residential designation or 460 m² otherwise; RS6 at 390 m²; RS7 at 335 m²; RD1 at 836 m²; RD4 at 504 m²; there is no RD5 zone. These are the minimums for lots created by subdivision, as the research states them — not a survey of existing lot sizes, which are confirmed parcel by parcel on the zoning maps. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Farm land — the second land form.** In the A1 Agriculture zone (s. 10.10.8), subdivision is permitted only where it complies with the OCP and has been approved under the Agricultural Land Commission Act if applicable, and the minimum lot size that may be created by subdivision is 8 ha. The OCP designation for this ground is "Agriculture (A)" — to maintain agricultural lands — applied on Map 2. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf and https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **ALR vs non-ALR split:** no Delta-wide split figure is published in the research and none is stated. The ALR layer applies on top of the OCP designation and zoning where land is in the ALR (§3 ALR block); a specific parcel's status is checked in the ALC's ALR Property and Map Finder and on title. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Bare-land strata as a tenure form — the framework, no offering counted.** Both tenures exist across Delta under the plans, and no Delta-wide tenure split is published: UNVERIFIED. Where a bare-land strata lot is the purchase anywhere in Delta, the lot is a strata lot — Form B, strata fees and bylaws apply to the LOT itself (§3 strata block). No bare-land strata development BY NAME is documented as an offering in this topic file, and none is counted: the program's documented bare-land strata examples live in their own area files (for example Southlands' Beachcomber Cottages is Tsawwassen East ground, filed there — never imported into other Tsawwassen files). (Research file §5)
- **SSMUH infill — plan-enabled, not inventory.** The framework the 2024 OCP provides, implemented by the zoning updates in §3, enables ground-oriented forms the City describes as between single-detached homes and condominiums — suites, duplexes, coach/garden-suite and 3–4 unit infill on single-detached and duplex lots. That is a permission framework, not a list of lots for sale. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Presale LOT offerings: none documented for this topic — counted separately, count 0.** Multi-unit housing in the urban centres and corridors the OCP targets for strategic growth is plan direction, documented AS SUCH, never as inventory. (Research file §5)
- **TFN land — excluded tenure, stated where the plans topic meets it:** land at Boardwalk / Tsawwassen Landing sits on Tsawwassen First Nation land and follows TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws — the Delta OCP and Zoning Bylaw framework in this file does not govern it, and it is documented as excluded tenure wherever it is met, never as Delta land stock. (Program standing note for Tsawwassen files; research file §4 plan scope)
- **Servicing:** Delta's servicing/subdivision standards bylaw number is HELD UNCONFIRMED in this program — Delta Development and Subdivision Standards Bylaw 8288 (2024) adoption is not asserted here, and if a sibling lots file asserts "8288, 2024" that is a conflict to note, never a number to copy. The process is documented instead: OCP designation first, then zone, then DPA, then the City's works-and-services conditions at subdivision through the Approving Officer. (Research file §6)

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP: Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), adopted July 8, 2024** — single consolidated plan, NO lettered schedules. The former Tsawwassen Area Plan (Schedule D.1) and Ladner area plan material belonged to Bylaw No. 3950, which Bylaw 8400 superseded. Any citation of "Schedule D.1 of Bylaw 8400" is an ERROR — correct in-file here and flagged in §5. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Zoning — number CONTESTED, flagged, never asserted:** the OCP's designations are implemented through the Delta Zoning Bylaw cited on City pages as No. 7600, 2017, which provides the parcel-level rules on zoning maps hosted at maps.delta.ca; an internal title in this program says "8400, 2025". This file follows the City pages' citation for zone content and asserts neither number as settled — verify the current consolidated bylaw with the City for a specific parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **SSMUH:** Delta SSMUH Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024 — the City's provincially required zoning updates allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots. SSMUH is not automatic on a given parcel: servicing, flood and setback rules still apply parcel by parcel — confirm with the City before pricing infill potential into a purchase. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **ALR block:** ALR rules apply on top of the OCP designation and zoning where land is in the ALR. The Agricultural Land Commission generally allows no more than one residence per ALR parcel, with a principal residence built without an ALC application capped at 500 m² total floor area and one additional detached residence permitted up to 90 m² on parcels of 40 ha or less or 186 m² on larger parcels. In A1, subdivision additionally needs OCP compliance and ALC Act approval if applicable, with an 8 ha minimum created lot (§2). https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Development Permit Areas:** Appendix B of the OCP carries the DPAs and guidelines, including the Tsawwassen Urban Centre DPA and hazard DPAs SD2 English Bluff (Tsawwassen Escarpment) and SD3 Boundary Bay Foreshore and the Southlands. A use can pass the designation and zone layers and still need a development permit. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Strata block — where a Delta purchase is a bare-land strata lot:** Form B (Strata Property Act s. 59) within 7 days, max $35 plus up to 25¢/page, with the lot's fees, CRF balance, levies, insurance summary, rules, budget and depreciation report; depreciation reports every five years for stratas with five or more lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss. 123.1/123.2 + Reg 7.01, May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Foreclosure note, if met on a Delta land search:** Delta has NO Supreme Court registry — foreclosure/court-ordered-sale mapping for Delta parcels is UNVERIFIED in this program, and no registry-based claim is made in this file. (Program standing note for Delta files)
- **Property transfer tax on land:** 1% on the first $200,000, 2% from $200,000 to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million. Worked anchors: $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.1M → $20,000; $1.2M → $22,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $1.8M → $34,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000 including the further 2% tier. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** most sales of vacant land by individuals are exempt (land kept for personal use is the CRA's example), while a sale can be taxable where the land is used in a business, sold in the course of a business, or is a parcel created by subdividing another parcel into more than two parts; anything finer is UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **First-home programs — the land boundary:** the First-Time Home Buyers' GST rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026) rebates 100% of the GST on new HOUSING up to $1 million (maximum $50,000), phasing out to $1.5 million, agreements March 20, 2025–2031 — new housing, NOT bare land. The GST New Housing Rebate is 36%, maximum $6,300, $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000 per buyer; First Home Savings Account $8,000 per year to $40,000 lifetime. CMHC premiums run 0.6%–4.5%. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition:** no primary text for this prohibition was opened in this slug's research, so its application to a Delta land purchase is UNCONFIRMED in this file — it is not conformed from other files. (Research file: ~/workspace/area-research-2026-11/batch-8/deltalandplans.md)
- **Property taxes / holding cost:** Delta's 2026 property tax due date was July 2, 2026, with a 5% penalty on outstanding balances after that date and a further 5% after September 2, 2026. https://www.delta.ca/services/property-taxes-utilities/property-taxes

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: What is Delta's current Official Community Plan?** Bylaw No. 8400 (2024), "Housing Our Future", adopted July 8, 2024 — a single consolidated plan whose Land Use Plan (s. 1.5, Map 2) sets the designations for the whole city. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: Is there still a Tsawwassen Area Plan or a Ladner Area Plan in the OCP?** No. Those were schedules of the previous OCP, Bylaw No. 3950 (the Tsawwassen Area Plan was Schedule D.1). Bylaw 8400 superseded them — there is no Schedule D.1 in the current OCP, and any document citing one is citing a superseded plan. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/3417/7676/8580/Delta20Official20Community20Plan20202420-20Housing20Our20Future.pdf
- **Q: How do I find what can be built on a Delta lot?** In order: the parcel's OCP designation (s. 1.5.1, Map 2), then its zone on the Zoning Bylaw maps, then whether a Development Permit Area in Appendix B applies — a use can pass one layer and fail the next. https://hdp-ca-prod-app-delta-letstalk-files.s3.ca-central-1.amazonaws.com/8917/7677/0068/OCP_2024_web.pdf
- **Q: What is Delta's zoning bylaw number?** CONTESTED — do not rely on a single citation. City pages cite No. 7600, 2017; an internal title says "8400, 2025". This file follows the City citation for zone content, flags the collision, and asserts neither as settled; confirm the current consolidated bylaw with the City for the specific parcel. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/RS1.pdf
- **Q: What did SSMUH change on Delta's single-family lots?** The provincially required updates (Bylaws 8394 and 8395, adopted June 24, 2024) allow up to four units on all single-detached and duplex lots, within the framework the 2024 OCP provides — subject to servicing, flood and setback rules parcel by parcel. https://www.delta.ca/building-development/housing-our-future
- **Q: How small a lot can be created by subdivision?** From RS7 at 335 m² at the in-town end to 4,000 m² in the RS1/RS2/RS3 and RD2/RD3 zones — and at the farm end, a new A1 lot needs a minimum of 8 ha plus OCP compliance and ALC Act approval if applicable. The range is why the zone lookup comes before any lot-value opinion. https://maps.delta.ca/Utilities/ZoningPDF/A1.pdf
- **Q: What does the Agriculture (A) designation mean for my parcel?** It is the OCP designation applied on Map 2 to maintain agricultural lands. Where the land is also in the ALR, the ALC's residence caps (500 m² principal; 90 m² / 186 m² additional by the 40 ha line) apply on top of the City's A-zone zoning — the City designation alone does not settle what can be built. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: Do Delta's plans govern the Tsawwassen Lands developments (Boardwalk / Tsawwassen Landing)?** No — that ground is Tsawwassen First Nation land under TFN law, not City of Delta bylaws. It is excluded tenure for this topic, never Delta land stock. (Program standing note for Tsawwassen files)
- **Q: Does GST apply when I buy vacant Delta land?** At CRA GI-003 level only: an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt, while a business sale or a parcel subdivided into more than two parts can be taxable; the position for a specific parcel is UNCONFIRMED until vendor status and history are established. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: What will PTT cost on a Delta land purchase?** At the standard tiers (1% / 2% / 3%, plus the further 2% on the residential portion above $3M): $700K → $12,000; $1.0M → $18,000; $1.5M → $28,000; $2.0M → $38,000; $3.5M → $93,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
