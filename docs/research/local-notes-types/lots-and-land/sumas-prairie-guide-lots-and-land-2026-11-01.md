## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Zoning — Abbotsford:** Zoning Bylaw No. 2400-2014 applies city-wide; the City's AgRefresh parcel-size work records a minimum subdivision lot area of 8.0 ha in the Agricultural One Zone (A1) and 16.0 ha in the Agricultural Two Zone (A2). https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **OCP — CORRECTION GOVERNS:** current OCP is **Abbotsforward 2050 (adopted Dec 2025)**. - **SSMUH — number UNCONFIRMED:** the research program asserts "3560-2024"; the standing correction governs and NO number is asserted here. Suite cap is under 50% of the principal unit; garden suites and coach houses may occupy up to 15% of the lot area to a maximum of 100 m² (Abbotsford ONLY) — on ALR land the ALC frame below governs first. (Research file above.)
- **Subdivision and servicing — Abbotsford:** the Development (servicing) Bylaw number is HELD UNCONFIRMED per the verified ledger — the process is documented instead: subdivision approval runs through the City's approving process; no bylaw number is asserted. (Verified servicing ledger, program standing record.)
- **ALR block (Sumas Prairie is ALR farm ground):** subdivision within the ALR requires Agricultural Land Commission approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s.6; SPA s.239; ALCA s.21(1)). Caps: principal residence ≤500 m² total floor area (max 1,000 m² of fill for that residence), suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by Oct 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Strata block (bare-land strata parcels, where met):** Form B within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page; depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022; only 55+ age bylaws valid (SPA ss.123.1/123.2 + May 1, 2023 exemptions); a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. A fee-simple lot carries none of this. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **Taxes:** City of Abbotsford property taxes were due July 2, 2026, with a first 5% penalty on July 3, 2026 and a second 5% penalty on September 16, 2026. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-services/property-taxes/your-current-tax-notice
- **Foreclosure / court sales:** a settled court process (BC Supreme Court Civil Rules 21-7 / 13-5, Practice Direction PD-66, Form 60); Abbotsford HAS a Supreme Court registry. Form B and PTT/GST treatment on court sales: UNCONFIRMED. (Program standing record.)
- **PTT — worked example (inputs + tier math):** on an $800,000 land purchase — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) + 2% on $200,000–$800,000 ($12,000) = **$14,000** ($6,000 for a qualifying first-time buyer under the FTHB exemption math at that value). Tiers: 1% to $200,000, 2% to $2,000,000, 3% above, plus a further 2% on the residential portion over $3,000,000. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
- **GST on vacant land — CRA GI-003 level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land generally exempt. Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate:** applies to new HOUSING, not bare land (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026, CRA governs; 100% to $1M phasing to $1.5M, max $50,000, agreements Mar 20, 2025–2031). New Housing Rebate 36% / max $6,300 / $350K–$450K; HBP $60,000; FHSA $40,000 / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html

- **Q: Is Sumas Prairie farmland in the ALR?** The prairie's documented character is ALR farm ground, but status is parcel by parcel — confirm the exact parcel on the ALC maps before valuing any farm or second-dwelling story. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/alr-maps
- **Q: Can I subdivide a prairie farm parcel?** Subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval first; Abbotsford's A1 minimum subdivision lot area is 8.0 ha and A2 is 16.0 ha per the City's AgRefresh work. https://www.abbotsford.ca/sites/default/files/2021-10/AgRefresh%20St3%20-%20Parcel%20Size.pdf
- **Q: Can I build a second home on a farm parcel?** Under the ALC frame: one principal residence ≤500 m², a suite only inside it, and at most one additional residence of 90 m² (≤40 ha) or 186 m² (>40 ha); Abbotsford's suite cap (under 50%) and coach/garden rule (15% of lot to max 100 m²) apply subject to that frame. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Q: What is being done about flooding?** The Sumas River Watershed Flood Mitigation Planning Initiative (Province, three First Nations, Abbotsford and Chilliwack) was signed in April 2023 after the November 2021 floods; about $220 million has been provided since 2021, including $76.6 million for Barrowtown Pump Station upgrades. https://www.abbotsford.ca/city-hall/news-media/supporting-flood-mitigation-planning-sumas-prairie

- **Q: How much PTT on an $800,000 parcel?** $14,000 — 1% on the first $200,000 ($2,000) plus 2% on the next $600,000 ($12,000). (§3.) https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax
