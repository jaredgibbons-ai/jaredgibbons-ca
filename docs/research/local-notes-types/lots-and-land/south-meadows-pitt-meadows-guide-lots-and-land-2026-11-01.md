## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP — "I See Pitt Meadows 2040," Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 2864, 2020, adopted September 27, 2022** (it repealed OCP Bylaw 2352, 2007), covering the whole city including the South Bonson area; no separate neighbourhood plan for South Bonson was found on the City pages the guide read. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/ocp
- **Zoning — Zoning Bylaw No. 2505, 2011:** houses sit under RS Large Lot Residential, R-3, R-4 and R-6 Urban Residential zones — RS minimum lot area 0.2 ha, with residential buildings sited at least 15.0 m from watercourses and key or constructed ditches; R-3 up to 3 units per lot, R-4 up to 4 units and R-6 up to five units, or six where at least one unit meets the adaptable housing requirements in the BC Building Code on lots close to frequent transit, each with a minimum lot area for subdivision of 1,215 sq m (a subdivision minimum only, per the City's SSMUH implementation update); RM-1 (townhouse) minimum lot area 0.4 ha and RM-2 minimum lot area 3,035 sq m. Any specific parcel's zone: per-parcel, UNVERIFIED. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-04/Zoning%20Bylaw%20No.%202505,%202011%20-%20Consolidated%20Version_0.pdf
- **SSMUH as the City states it:** SSMUH unit counts of 3, 4 or 6 turn on the 280 sq m lot-size threshold and the 400 m frequent-transit test, and the City's OCP Amendment Bylaw No. 3034, 2025 covers 2–6 units through secondary suites, garden suites, duplexes, triplexes, townhomes and small apartment buildings. Suites: one secondary suite per principal dwelling unit, 33 to 90 sq m, inside the same building, where the zone permits it; garden suites one per lot at 33 to 90 sq m in the zones listed in the City garden suite guide, including RS — a lot with a garden suite cannot also have a secondary suite. https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/2025-12/Dec_2_2025_SSMUH_Transit_Oriented_Areas_Implementation_Update.pdf and https://www.pittmeadows.ca/sites/default/files/docs/homes-development/information_sheet_-_planning_-_garden_suitesv4.pdf
- **Subdivision and servicing — Pitt Meadows:** subdivision follows the zone minimums above. The Pitt Meadows servicing bylaw number is UNCONFIRMED — no number is quoted and no other city's bylaw is borrowed; the documented process is zone minimums + the SSMUH update + the drainage/dike position, confirmed with the City for the parcel. Per-property septic/well servicing details: UNVERIFIED. Basis: ~/workspace/local-notes/lots-and-land/south-meadows-pitt-meadows-lots-and-land-2026-11-01.md, §3.
