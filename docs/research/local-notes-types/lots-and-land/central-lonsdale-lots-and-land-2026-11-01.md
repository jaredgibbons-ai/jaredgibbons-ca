## 2. THE LAND ITSELF

**There is no meaningful bare-land market in Central Lonsdale — stated plainly at the top. No named fee-simple bare-land subdivision, no bare-land strata community, and no presale lot offering was verified on any City or developer source. Land here trades as built fee-simple and strata product on an urban grid: avenue apartment sites, side-street house lots whose value now turns on the December 2025 Ground Oriented Zone, and one City-owned development land holding — the Harry Jerome Neighbourhood Lands — documented below as City lands planned for housing, never as for-sale inventory.**

### Fee-simple lots — the default form on the side streets

- **Established side-street house lots:** ground-oriented houses, duplexes and multiplexes on the side streets under the Ground Oriented (GO) Zone, with strata condos and apartments in low-, mid- and high-rise buildings on and beside Lonsdale Avenue — the research calls this the North Shore's most urban mix. The avenue and side-street pattern is historic subdivision fabric — built lots, not a lot offering. The strata/freehold split by street: UNVERIFIED. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Lot character, as the City states it:** GO Zone subdivision minimum lot width is 7.5 m in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation (10.0 m for a corner lot) and 15.0 m in other designations; older bylaw minimum lot areas include 464.5 m² in RS-2 and 334.4 m² in RS-3. These are zone minimums, not a claim about any existing lot's size. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf

## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **OCP / Zoning — CITY rulebook only:** City of North Vancouver Official Community Plan, 2014, Bylaw No. 8400 (amended December 8, 2025, Amendment Bylaw 2025, No. 9114, for provincial housing requirements) and Zoning Bylaw No. 6700 (1995). The District's Bylaws 7900 / 3210 do not apply anywhere in this file. https://www.cnv.org/Community-Environment/Housing/Provincial-Housing-Legislation-Updates
- **GO Zone density (adopted December 8, 2025):** a lot of 280 m² or less allows up to 3 units; a lot over 280 m² allows up to 4 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 designation and up to 6 units in the Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 designations; the adoption increased permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 residential properties and allows up to 6 units per lot on approximately 600 properties. The City sized its December 2025 updates to a 20-year housing need of 21,301 units on top of its existing 29,021 units. Subdivision minimum widths are in Section 2. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **Suites and coach houses:** the City legalized secondary suites in all single-family dwellings in 1993; one suite is permitted per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a suite may be combined with an accessory coach house, which is permitted where the front lot line is at least 10 m and requires a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Subdivision + servicing — the City's own instrument, verified on the City source:** the Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw, 2010, No. 8014 (consolidated June 14, 2021) is "a Bylaw to regulate and require the provision of works and services in respect of the subdivision and development of lands and to establish the standard of services to be provided" — applicants for subdivision and building permits are required to provide the works identified in its Schedule A unless otherwise provided by Development Variance Permit. The City's process runs from a Preliminary Application (which expressly covers new lots, lot-line adjustments, airspace parcels, bare land strata and phased strata plans, submitted to subdivision@cnv.org) to final approval, with final fees calculated by City staff after preliminary review, based on bylaw fees and the actual cost to service the proposed lot configuration. **This is a CITY instrument only — it is never applied to a District lot (see the delbrook file, where the District's own servicing-bylaw number is held UNCONFIRMED).** https://www.cnv.org/-/media/city-of-north-vancouver/documents/bylaws/consolidated/8014-c.pdf and https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/subdivisions
- **Streamside hazard:** the City's Streamside Protection Development Permit Area covers land within 15 metres of the top of bank of a watercourse or within 10 metres of the top of a ravine bank, and a development permit is required before new development there — relevant near the Wagg Creek and Mahon Park ravine lands at the area's west side. Parcel-level floodplain status: UNVERIFIED in the research. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Bare-land strata rules (no plan verified here — the BC strata block that WOULD apply in full to any bare-land strata lot):** Form B diligence (fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary; provided within 7 days, max $35 + 25¢/page); depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024; minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under Strata Property Act ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions (younger spouse/partner, caregivers, legacy residents, defined children); a listing's "45+" line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no Central Lonsdale ALR parcel is confirmed here):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — a new-HOME exemption; bare-land eligibility UNCONFIRMED). https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/property-taxes/property-transfer-tax/exemptions/first-time-home-buyers
- **GST on vacant land — CRA level only:** developer/business sales and newly subdivided lots are generally TAXABLE; an individual's sale of personal-use land is generally exempt (CRA GI-003). Finer points UNCONFIRMED. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **FTHB GST rebate / programs — land angle:** the FTHB rebate (Bill C-4, Royal Assent March 13, 2026 per CRA News No. 122; 100% to $1M, maximum $50,000, phasing to $1.5M; agreements March 20, 2025–2031) applies to new HOUSING, not bare land itself — a Central Lonsdale lot purchase alone earns no rebate. New Housing Rebate 36% / maximum $6,300 / $350,000–$450,000 phase-out. Home Buyers' Plan $60,000; FHSA $40,000 lifetime / $8,000 per year. https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/news122/news122-excise-gst-hst-news-no-122.html
- **Speculation/vacancy + purchase prohibition:** no Central Lonsdale-specific position is documented in the research — none imported. Vancouver Empty Homes Tax (3%) is Vancouver ONLY, not applicable here. The federal non-Canadian purchase prohibition position for vacant land is UNCONFIRMED in this file — the research cites no prohibition source for Central Lonsdale, and the position is not conformed from other files.
- **City property taxes:** City of North Vancouver 2026 property taxes were due July 2, 2026; the 2026 residential municipal-general rate is 1.89202 and the total residential rate is 3.34028 per $1,000 of assessed value, plus a separate residential storm drainage rate of 0.10697 per $1,000; the City illustrates an average residential property assessed at $1,387,684 paying $4,790 in total 2026 property taxes. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/City-Finances/2026/2026_TAX_RATE_SUMMARY_CITY_OF_NORTH_VANCOUVER-2.PDF

## 4. WHAT BUYERS ASK

- **Q: Is there a vacant lot or new subdivision I can buy into in Central Lonsdale?** No verified one — this is a built urban core; land trades as built house lots, strata units and avenue development sites, and no bare-land subdivision or presale lot offering was verified. (Section 2.)
- **Q: What are the Harry Jerome Neighbourhood Lands, and can I buy a lot there?** They are City-owned lands beside the new recreation centre that the City plans for housing in high-rise and ground-oriented forms, with Lonsdale Square and a seniors' residence already built on part of them and further lands to be made available by the City. No lot offering, lot count or sale terms are published in the material read — it is a municipal planning position, not inventory. https://www.cnv.org/Community-Environment/Housing/Provincial-Housing-Legislation-Updates/City-Lands-Planning
- **Q: How many units can a side-street lot hold now?** Under the GO Zone adopted December 8, 2025: up to 3 units on a lot of 280 m² or less, up to 4 units over 280 m² in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1, and up to 6 units over 280 m² in Low Rise Neighbourhood 2 or Residential Level 4 — designation-specific, so confirm the lot's OCP designation first. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/Zoning-Bylaw-1995-No-6700-Amendment-Bylaw-2025-No-9137-City-Initiated-Amendment-Related-to-Provincia.pdf
- **Q: Can I subdivide a Central Lonsdale lot, and what services must be provided?** Subdivision runs under the City's Subdivision and Development Control Bylaw, 2010, No. 8014 — works and services to its Schedule A standard are required unless a Development Variance Permit provides otherwise, the process starts with a Preliminary Application to the City, and final fees are calculated on bylaw fees plus the actual servicing cost of the proposed configuration. GO Zone minimum widths (7.5 m / 10.0 m corner in Low Rise Neighbourhood 1; 15.0 m in other designations) gate whether a split is possible at all. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/city-of-north-vancouver/documents/bylaws/consolidated/8014-c.pdf
- **Q: Is Chesterfield Manor a strata I can finance like a condo?** No — it is a corporation-share purchase, not a strata lot: a current listing states share ownership requiring 35% down. It is excluded program-wide for that reason; confirm the tenure structure and financing consequence from the offering documents, not the listing summary. http://www.bccondosandhomes.com/building/chesterfield-manor-1345-ave
- **Q: Can a house lot add a suite and a coach house?** A suite may combine with an accessory coach house where the lot qualifies — one suite per single-family property, the owner must reside at the property, and a coach house requires a front lot line of at least 10 m plus a Development Permit and a Building Permit. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **Q: What permit applies to a west-side lot near Mahon Park or Wagg Creek?** The Streamside Protection Development Permit Area: land within 15 m of a watercourse bank or 10 m of a ravine bank needs a development permit before new development. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Building/Land-Use-Approvals/Development-Permits/Streamside-Development-Permit-Areas
- **Q: Will GST apply if I buy a newly subdivided lot from a developer?** Generally yes — developer sales and newly subdivided lots are generally taxable under CRA's vacant-land rules, unlike an individual's personal-use resale (CRA GI-003). https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/gi-003/sales-vacant-land-individuals.html
- **Q: Which schools serve a Central Lonsdale lot?** By exact address only — Queen Mary Community Elementary is inside the area and Carson Graham Secondary is at its north end, but no catchment for a corridor address is settled in this file, and the 2026/27 Cloverley catchment review may move Queen Mary addresses east of Lonsdale. Check the address with SD44. https://studentsuccess.gov.bc.ca/schools/in-school-district/044

## 5. LOCAL DETAILS A BUYER WOULD NOT KNOW

- **The City rewrote this area's land math on December 8, 2025:** the GO Zone adopted that day raised permitted units from 3 to 4 on nearly 4,300 City properties and allows up to 6 units on about 600 — a side-street lot's value now turns on its OCP designation (Low Rise Neighbourhood 1 vs 2 / Residential Level 4), not on its old single-family zoning, and the same day's OCP amendment (Bylaw 9114) was made for provincial housing requirements against a stated 20-year housing need of 21,301 units. https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Housing/HTO-Year-2-Progress-Report-July-1,-2025-June-30,-2026.pdf
- **The avenue itself is now a 20-year public-realm project:** Council approved the Lonsdale Great Street Plan on April 20, 2026, funded in its first phase by the Province's Growing Communities Fund — avenue-front lots carry a revitalization program, not just a zoning schedule. https://www.cnv.org/City-Hall/News-Room/News-Releases/2026/7/7/The-City-of-North-Vancouver-launches-next-phase-of-Lonsdale-Great-Street-improvements
- **The City has legalized suites here since 1993:** secondary suites have been permitted in all single-family dwellings in the City since 1993 — the suite position on an older Central Lonsdale house lot is a long-standing right with an owner-residency condition, not a new SSMUH experiment. https://www.cnv.org/Business-Development/Permits-Inspections/building-permits/secondary-suites
- **The density shift is visible in the City's own census backgrounder:** city-wide, single-detached dwellings fell from 20% of total units in 2001 to 11% in 2021, while apartments in buildings of five or more storeys rose from 15% to 29% — and the research notes Central Lonsdale is where that shift is concentrated. The City's 2021 Census population was 58,120 on 11.83 km² (Statistics Canada 2021, via the City's census backgrounder). https://www.cnv.org/-/media/City-of-North-Vancouver/Documents/Zoning-Bylaw-Update/Zoning-for-Diverse-Housing-Needs-Backgrounder.pdf
