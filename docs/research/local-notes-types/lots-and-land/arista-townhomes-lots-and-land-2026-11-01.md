## 3. RULES THAT AFFECT LOTS AND LAND

- **Surrey plans:** OCP = **Surrey 2050 Official Community Plan Bylaw No. 21900 (adopted June 15, 2026)** — Bylaw No. 18020 is REPLACED and is never cited as current. Zoning: **Surrey Zoning By-law No. 12000** — the complex's own zone is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research (no zone for 2955 156 Street was read in the pass); confirm per parcel in COSMOS before stating one. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **SSMUH (Surrey position):** zoning changes adopted July 8, 2024 — a maximum of 3 units on lots smaller than 280 m², 4 units on lots larger than 280 m², and up to 6 units on eligible lots within 400 m of a frequent bus stop. SSMUH is a fee-simple / small-lot frame; it does not re-subdivide an existing strata complex — an Arista strata lot's density is fixed by its strata plan and zoning, not by SSMUH. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/provincial-housing-financial-regulations/zoning-small-scale-multi-unit-housing
- **Secondary suites (Bylaw No. 12000):** the City defines a secondary suite as an accessory dwelling unit whose habitable floor area does not exceed **40% of the principal dwelling unit**, within a single family, semi-detached or duplex building, and not a separate strata lot; only one suite per dwelling unit, and never within a coach house or garden suite. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/building/building-or-legalizing-secondary-suite
- **Suites in townhouses:** Surrey Council approved bylaw changes to permit secondary (lock-off) suites in townhouses in the RM-15, RM-23 and RM-30 zones **for new developments** in transit-oriented areas and frequent bus stop areas, with a suite minimum of 30 sq. m and less than 40% of the habitable floor area of the larger principal unit — those permissions are not a blanket right in a 2011-era complex like Arista. https://www.Surrey.ca/news-events/news/surrey-creating-more-housing-options-secondary-suites-townhouses
- **Subdivision / servicing:** subdivision runs against the zone minimums in Bylaw No. 12000, confirmed per parcel in COSMOS. Verified on the City's own Land Development pages in this program: the **Surrey Subdivision and Development Bylaw, 1986, No. 8830** is the City's subdivision/servicing bylaw, worked through the Engineering Land Development / Servicing Agreement process. A strata lot cannot be "subdivided" by its owner — strata plan amendment is a strata-corporation and Land Title process, not a City subdivision of the lot. https://www.surrey.ca/renovating-building-development/land-planning-development/land-development-application-process/servicing-agreement
- **Streamside setbacks:** Part 7A of Zoning Bylaw No. 12000 sets streamside setbacks **by stream class** — there is no single riparian figure for Surrey; whether any watercourse setback touches this site is UNCONFIRMED in this slug's research. https://www.surrey.ca/city-government/bylaws/12000
- **Surrey short-term rentals:** permitted, **owner-only**; licence figure carried as **$358/yr**, with the $350/yr proposal figure disclosed as a conflict.
- **Strata block — applies to Arista in full (its lot IS a strata lot):** Form B diligence (SPA s. 59; within 7 days, maximum $35 + 25¢/page — fees, amounts owed, levies, CRF balance, insurance summary, rules, current budget, Rental Disclosure Statement and most recent depreciation report attached); the live page itself directs buyers to the Form B, bylaws, depreciation report and minutes, and to confirm what the monthly fees include. Depreciation reports on a 5-year cycle for stratas with 5+ lots since July 1, 2024 (the page-stated 65 homes would put Arista well over the threshold — but the count is UNCONFIRMED in §2, so the threshold position is read off the strata plan, not the page); minimum 10% CRF contribution since November 1, 2023; rental-restriction bylaws VOID since November 24, 2022 (short-term restrictions may still apply by bylaw); only 55+ age bylaws valid under SPA ss. 123.1/123.2, with May 1, 2023 exemptions — Arista carries no verified age bylaw and is NOT a 55+ complex in any source checked; a "45+" listing line is NOT a valid BC age bylaw. Published rental / pet restrictions for Arista: UNCONFIRMED — the live page says only that most strata bylaws at Arista permit pets with size and quantity restrictions and directs buyers to confirm with the strata manager. https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/housing-tenancy/strata-housing/renting-buying-selling/buying-and-selling-strata/paperwork-for-buyers-and-sellers/form-b-information-certificate and https://jaredgibbons.ca/arista-townhomes.html
- **ALR rules (for completeness — no ALR parcel is documented for this site):** subdivision within the ALR requires ALC approval; ALR residences cannot be stratified (ALC Bulletin 09 s. 6; Strata Property Act s. 239; Agricultural Land Commission Act s. 21(1)); ALC residence caps: principal ≤500 m², suite within the principal residence only, additional residence 90 m² (≤40 ha) / 186 m² (>40 ha). Farm class is three-tiered ($2,500 on 0.8–4 ha; $2,500 + 5% of the land's value over 4 ha; $10,000 under 0.8 ha; applications by October 31) — a flat "$2,500+" line is an ERROR, asserted here only as the standing ledger states it. https://www.alc.gov.bc.ca/use-of-alr-land/housing-in-the-alr/
- **Property transfer tax:** general 1% on the first $200,000, 2% to $2 million, 3% above $2 million, plus a further 2% on the residential portion above $3 million; First-Time Home Buyers' Program (first $500,000 exempt at values of $835,000 or less, maximum saving $8,000, partial $835,000–$860,000); Newly Built Home Exemption (full to $1,100,000, partial to $1,150,000 — does not apply to a 2011 resale). **Worked examples, inputs + tier math stated (fully residential, no exemption unless stated):** $500,000 → $2,000 + $6,000 = **$8,000**; $700,000 → $2,000 + $10,000 = **$12,000**; $800,000 → $2,000 + $12,000 = **$14,000** (fully-qualifying first-time buyer: **$6,000**); $900,000 → $2,000 + $14,000 = **$16,000**; $1,000,000 → $2,000 + $16,000 = **$18,000**; $1,200,000 → $2,000 + $20,000 = **$22,000**; $1,500,000 → $2,000 + $26,000 = **$28,000**; $1,800,000 → $2,000 + $32,000 = **$34,000**; $2,000,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 = **$38,000**; $3,500,000 → $2,000 + $36,000 + $45,000 (3% tier) + $10,000 (further 2% on the $500,000 over $3M) = **$93,000**.
